## Derived Synthesis

- `Admetos/Vulcanus = Hades`: enduring severe misfortune, surviving lean conditions, and being forced into tough contact with what is worn down or broken. Main caution: misery under weight: privation deepens until life feels trapped in bitterness, burden, or degradation.
