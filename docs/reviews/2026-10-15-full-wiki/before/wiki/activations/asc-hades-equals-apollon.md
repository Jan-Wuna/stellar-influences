## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Expansion of poverty and need. Dealing with poor or disagreeable customers. An authority on antiques. Nuisance through beggars. Acquaintances with others through old sciences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Extensive environmental pollution, freedom deficiency of others, hazardous environmental freedom, dangerous environmental experiences, lack of environmental knowledge, adverse partner experiences, partner experiences from the past, and knowledge about environmental hazards

## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Hades = Apollon`: a larger field of unpleasant dealings, authority on old or degraded subjects, and the multiplication of difficult contacts. Main caution: widened contamination: what is disagreeable no longer stays local, but circulates widely through trade, knowledge, or social traffic.
