### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Keeping company with mentally mediocre people. Low spiritual or educational standard of the neighborhood.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Findings in environmental crime, dangerous truth of the other, spirit of a past environment, mental disadvantage of others, mental pollution, lack of insight of the partner, and hidden knowledge of the environment

## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Hades = Poseidon`: low standards of thought, weak educational atmosphere, and the disappointment of finding little refinement in the immediate milieu. Main caution: degraded culture: the native may be surrounded by mental mediocrity that narrows aspiration and clarity.
