## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To put oneself in danger of bodily harm from others. To come bodily into contact with dirt.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Disadvantages of other persons, people at risk in the environment, criminal persons in the environment, personal partner deficiency, hidden environment of man, polluted environment of a place, and Centre for Environmental Service

## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Hades = Sun`: embodied vulnerability, heightened physical sensitivity to degraded conditions, and direct confrontation with what is unsafe or dirty nearby. Main caution: personal endangerment: the damaged environment can injure the native in concrete bodily ways.
