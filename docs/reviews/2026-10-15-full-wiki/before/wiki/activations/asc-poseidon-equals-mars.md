## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Separation of minds. To separate from others as a result of their morals.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Insightful actions of the partner, mental activity with the partner, mental activity in the place, ideas impulse of the partner, cultural activity for the environment, active work partner, and truth impulse of the other

## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Poseidon = Mars`: ideological dispute, sharp disagreement, and a refined environment strained by polemics or moral rupture. Main caution: schism: clarity of principle can become hostility or unnecessary severance.
