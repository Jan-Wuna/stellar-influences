## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To enjoy great popularity with others. To feel well in one’s surroundings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joy in a place of strength, peace power in the place, harmony with the energy of space, harmonious partner influence, harmonious local influence, love influence of the partner, strong love partner, and preference for places of strength

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Vulcanus = Venus`: pleasant rapport with powerful or influential people and social comfort in otherwise forceful surroundings. Main caution: enjoying strength without noticing how much pressure it carries.
