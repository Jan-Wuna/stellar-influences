## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To comply with a request or desire of others. To bestow love. To create progeny.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Fiery love for the partner, peace efforts at the place, peaceful environmental goals, harmonious local support, harmonious goal striving with others, creative client as partner, love performance of the other, and love witnessing with the partner

## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Zeus = Venus`: cooperative desire, agreeable productivity, and environments where action serves affection, pleasure, or creation. Main caution: yielding too readily to others' wants.
