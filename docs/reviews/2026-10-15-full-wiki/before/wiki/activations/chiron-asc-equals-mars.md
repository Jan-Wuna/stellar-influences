### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s ability to be effective in one’s actions is inhibited by the limitations one places on one’s identity and self-concept. Conversely one’s actions can become pow erfully fruitful when one aligns one’s identity with spiritual forces.
