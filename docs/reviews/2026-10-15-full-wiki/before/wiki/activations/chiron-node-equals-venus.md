## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A love and appreciation of spiritual connectedness. One has an appreciation of where one has come from and where one is going and of how spiritual forces may be enlisted to enhance the process of evolution.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Chiron/Node = Venus`: relational grace, affectionate karmic healing, and evolution supported by appreciation rather than fear. Main caution: sentimentalizing connection instead of letting it truly change the heart.
