### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The need to take a highly individualistic approach to one’s career. One’s life purpose involves taking a free, open, experimental and independent approach to one’s work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Chiron/Uranus = MC`: liberating vocation, unconventional public purpose, and work that must allow originality. Main caution: mistaking resistance to structure for true calling.
