### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One is aware of the uniqueness of ever different emotion that arises, and even though one may seem to experience the same feelings over and over their subtleties and contexts make them forever new and distinctive. Healing manifests through allow ing oneself to be emotionally free and uninhibited.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Chiron/Uranus = Moon`: emotional liberation, fresh feeling, and healing through not forcing emotion into old scripts. Main caution: confusing emotional unpredictability with deeper freedom.
