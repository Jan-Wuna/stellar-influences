### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Ignorance and mental indifference. To occupy one’s mind with remote antiquity. To be interested in prehistoric things or times. To occupy one’s mind with long- past times.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Depression through truth deficiency, Greek deepening into the past, criminal spiritual depression, inadequate spiritual deepening, hidden mental limitations, hidden spiritual raw material, and deepening into cultures of the past

## Derived Synthesis
