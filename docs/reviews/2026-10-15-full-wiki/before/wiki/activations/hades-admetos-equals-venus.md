## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Captivated or hindered in one’s desire for love. Longing for the past. To be released from duty or freed of service in spite of lack and need of personnel.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Depression through the heart of love, strengthening of love through deprivation, strengthening of peace through renunciation, fervent fondness for the past, poor deepening of love, and deepening into the arts of the past

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Admetos = Venus`: tenderness, pleasure, or relationship being reduced, delayed, or shaped by old lack. Main caution: confusing deprivation-induced longing with actual relational fulfillment.
