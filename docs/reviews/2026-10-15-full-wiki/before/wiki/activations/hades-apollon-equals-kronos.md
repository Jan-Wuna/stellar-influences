#### Witte Entry

Misery of the masses brought on by incompetence of the government. To be an authority in historical science. Abilities in the field of magic and occult science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

authority for hidden knowledge, success of a state through deprivation, dangerous freedom in the state, trade with state secrets, self evident extension of a danger, spread of authoritarian deficiency, and increasing horde crime

## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Apollon = Kronos`: bleak large-scale conditions being tied to power, leadership failure, or official control, while knowledge of the past or hidden arts may also become specialized and authoritative. Main caution: expertise or authority detached from humane responsibility.
