## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

General dependence and suppression. Vices, obstacles, and dirt. General ugly manners and maneuvers. Raw and uncultured people. Terrible acts of violence in public life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

influence of the past in public life, energy deficiency in public, dangerous influence in public life, sorrow through violence in public life, renunciation of public force, and hidden power in public life

## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Vulcanus = Vernal Point`: a whole environment or society being marked by harsh low conditions and overt force. It can show public life saturated with aggression, crudity, and fear.
