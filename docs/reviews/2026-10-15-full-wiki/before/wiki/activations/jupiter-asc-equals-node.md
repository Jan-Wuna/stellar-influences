## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Good relations with other people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful partner connection, successful connection of others, joy at a meeting at the place, joyful partner connection, happy connected in the environment, legal connections at the place, and beneficial environmental connections

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Appreciation of a beautiful and harmonious environment, love of pleasures and social meetings. A festivity, happy associations.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The need to seek out connections with groups or individuals who will help one to expand one’s sense of self and identity, as well as become more selfaware. An enthu siastic, encouraging demeanor when connecting with others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `276`

#### Munkasey Entry

Added ability to attract and meet the right people; a sense of destiny which includes meeting or attracting others who are right for you; presentations which are well received; sexual encounters with social or political hopefuls.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Asc = Node`: good relations, festive or supportive company, and connections that help the native feel more confident, visible, and socially integrated. Main caution: to avoid treating sociability as sufficient in itself; growth still depends on the quality of associations, not just their pleasantness.
