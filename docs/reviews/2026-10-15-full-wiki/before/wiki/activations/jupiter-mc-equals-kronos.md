#### Witte Entry

Fortunate leadership. To be conscious of ones own authority. Self-conscious.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special soul joy, my success in the state, my successful self confidence, my money for the state, my great success, my legal state, my successful independence, my advantages of the state, and important minute of success

## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/MC = Kronos`: public growth through recognized leadership and self-possession. It favors success in elevated roles, while warning against over-identifying purpose with status.
