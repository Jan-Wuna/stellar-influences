## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Mental tranquility and contentment. To enjoy talking.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Thinking of my happiness, animated happiness in youth, I talk about joy, my success in youth, my successful thinking, my money for traffic, my money for the media, my advantages in youth, and talking about soul pleasure

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A wealth of thoughts, prudence, farsightedness, positive outlook on life in general, enjoys exchanging thoughts and social meetings. Successful plans, large-scale projects or ventures, good business associations.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A mind which is enthusiastic about getting to the bottom of things. Thinking strongly influenced by those whom you think have authority. A mind searching for spir itual truth.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `280`

#### Munkasey Entry

Enhanced ability to verbalize and discuss aspects of personal growth in your life; explaining to yourself or others the effect of the inner changes you undergo after studying new philosophies, religions, or ideas of others.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/MC = Mercury`: strong judgment, enjoyable conversation, profitable planning, and a mind drawn to broad projects, truth, or high-minded explanation. Main caution: inflation of thought: big ideas can become excessive talking, mental superiority, or plans that outrun practical follow-through.
