#### Witte Entry

The good fortune which lies in the future. Easily earned money through science or commerce. Much money easily made.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special joy in the air, special happiness through fine tuning, exchange by the financial office, great uncertainty about money, state water law, legal uncertainty of the state, successful water trade, success by water law, weak money increase, uncertain legal knowledge, vie! joy in the water, and spread of uncertainty because of money

## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Neptune = Apollon`: visionary optimism widening into trade, enterprise, and big possibilities. It favors lucrative imagination, but the main caution is believing easy money is more solid than it really is.
