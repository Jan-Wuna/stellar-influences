## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

P Getting acquainted for a fortunate connection. Change of a successful connection.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful development of a connection, successful connection change, Freudc through development of a relationship, changes by kine gleichliche Begegnung, change of a legal connection, and growing financial connection

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Desire to make pleasant and happy contacts with many people.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An extreme need to expand one’s connections with others, sometimes so much so that the other person becomes intimidated and/or the connection weakened. The desire to connect with a lot of people and consequently the possibility of spreading oneself too thinly. As one makes more and more connections a transformation occurs.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `272`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increases the chances that the contacts you make have shady or criminal intentions, and will hold you responsible for whatever help they give you; offers you the possibility of using any contacts to gain enormous benefits.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Node = Pluto`: strong social magnetism, large or strategic circles, and relationships that change the scale or direction of one's growth. Main caution: excess expansion in connection: one can push too hard, intimidate others, or spread oneself thin in trying to turn every contact into a meaningful alliance.
