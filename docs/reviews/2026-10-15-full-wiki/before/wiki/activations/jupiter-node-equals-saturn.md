## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Dissolution of a betrothal or marriage or good connection. Disturbed financial relationship.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Tasks of legal yennistlung, successful maturing connection, money losses in a connection, distanced relationship to money, distanced happiness of a connection, happy connection in old age, and legal problems of a connection

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Desire for solitude or seclusion, urge to be alone. Inhibitions during the initial stage of associations or new contacts, teamwork which is rendered more difficult as time goes on, a retarded venture, difficulties with engagement or marriage, the termination of an association.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To be afraid of connecting with people. The slow and gradual expansion of one’s connectedness. To be frustrated because you feel like you give more than the other person, or like there is too much going on between you and them, and so you feel overwhelmed.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `272`

#### Munkasey Entry

Emphasizes the business aspects of life; contacts with helpful friends or groups; some disappointments thru contacts or associates; delays in receiving help from friends or associates; a lessening of control over others.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Node = Saturn`: fear of reaching out, difficulty sustaining teamwork, and the need to build reliable alliance patiently rather than expecting easy fellowship. Main caution: withdrawal into solitude or discouragement, which can turn potentially helpful connections into endings, missed partnerships, or emotionally heavy social life.
