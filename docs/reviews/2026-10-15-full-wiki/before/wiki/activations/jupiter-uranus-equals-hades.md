## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Luck through things which were found or treasures of the earth such as minerals etc. Good fortune in or through evil activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting financial crime, success in the technical service, success impulses from the past, dangerous legal event, excitement about poor success, new advantages and disadvantages, sudden legal distress, and tensions by giving up money

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Uranus = Hades`: sudden luck emerging from what is hidden, discarded, or morally ambiguous. It can show treasure-finding and underground opportunity, but also the shadow side of risk and gain.
