## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Children without vitality. Agricultural produce. Luck in connection with fuel.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Starting money deficiency, success through renunciation, successful achievements of the past, joy through service, dangerous money generation, secret happiness, criminal legal services, and benefits from past efforts

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Zeus = Hades`: yield from agriculture or fuel and practical material production, but also lowered vitality in children or products.
- Chart expression: gain from basic or hidden materials while warning that fertility, health, or quality can be compromised.
