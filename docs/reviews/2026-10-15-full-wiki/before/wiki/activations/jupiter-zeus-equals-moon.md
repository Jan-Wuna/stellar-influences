## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The procreative woman. Persons happy in their work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful efforts of a people, successful achievements of women, successful objectives of a woman, happy woman through procreation, legal efforts of women, woman's procreation right, and satisfied leadership of a people

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Zeus = Moon`: the procreative woman and people who feel happy in their work or nourished by what they produce.
- Chart expression: fertile responsiveness, emotional satisfaction in labor, and collective identification with productive activity.
