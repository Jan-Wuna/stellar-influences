## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Zeus = Saturn`: hindrance to happy work, blocked productivity, or detachment from what has been built or achieved.
- Chart expression: reality-testing and disciplined limits, but it can also mark painful interruption unless the work is stabilized and preserved.
