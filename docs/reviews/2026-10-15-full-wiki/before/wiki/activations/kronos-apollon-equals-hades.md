### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Spreading of lack and poverty. Great meanness and villainess. Celebrities in the field of sciences of antiquity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of state traditions, great experiences from the past, critical commercial success, lack of self evident experience, poor state success, renunciation of special freedoms, and increase of state criminality

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Apollon = Hades`: large-scale poverty or villainy, but also celebrity in antiquarian, historical, or old sciences.
- Chart expression: expertise applied to degraded conditions or knowledge of neglected, difficult, or decayed fields, while warning that breadth and authority may spread lack as well as understanding.
