Community of labour, blocked Community Arbcit, blocked Community of wills, restricted Community of labour, large Community of action, firm common will, common work on the earth, and joint deepening of work

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Admetos = Cupido`: broken marriages, special work in a limited group, and cooperative effort that may fail, narrow, or collapse into bankruptcy.
- Chart expression: tightly bounded teamwork, but it also warns that shared structures can become brittle, depressed, or financially unsound.
