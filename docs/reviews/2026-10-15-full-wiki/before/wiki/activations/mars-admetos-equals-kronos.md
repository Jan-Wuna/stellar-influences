## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Leading activity in a small circle or special work. Acting independently under limited circumstances. Independent in small circle, in a narrow room or space. Restricted independence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special perseverance at work, blocked self governing activity, limiting handboys of the state, gross work of the authorities, self governing earth work, deepening in self governing work, priority activity with raw material, and resistance to state actions

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Admetos = Kronos`: leading special work in a small circle, acting independently under narrow conditions, and exercising restricted authority.
