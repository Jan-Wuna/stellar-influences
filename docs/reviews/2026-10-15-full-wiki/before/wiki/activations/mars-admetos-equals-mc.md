## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Narrow minded, mean or petty. To confine oneself to a small circle. Activity in a small circle. To experience suppression. Limitation of freedom or movement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My great will, my humble work, my earth work, my profound actions, my blocking work, my restricted activity, mental depressions in arrears, untwisted mental impulses, and deepness in my work

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Admetos = MC`: pettiness, self-confinement, activity in a small circle, and lived experience of suppression or limited freedom of movement.
- Chart expression: a narrowed life direction and work identity shaped by pressure, caution, and restricted scope.
