## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Persons who feel depressed and crowded, who expect the worst. The terrorized public or women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A woman's will, a woman's impulsion in blockade, blocked actions, restricted activities, a people's firm will, a lively deepening of her creativity, a woman's profound work, and a deepening of the work of a people

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Admetos = Moon`: depressed, crowded, pessimistic people and a public or female field that feels terrorized or hemmed in.
- Chart expression: collective discouragement and emotional compression, with expectation turning toward fear or the worst-case outcome.
