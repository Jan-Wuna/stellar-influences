## Identity

## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be a co-worker or to have them. To get a thrashing. To be beaten up.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work centre in the place, for the environment, actions of other persons, corporative work with others, man work for the environment, personal activity for the environment, activity of other persons, and activity of another person

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Excitability, love of fighting, intense teamwork. Excitement, quarrels, mishaps.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One feels that it is important to strongly and assertively project who one is. One’s goals are positively affected by coming across assertively, but if this is too extreme the goals can be negatively affected. One feels self-confident about his ability to do things. One’s self-esteem is strongly based on what one accomplishes.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `248`

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds to the personal belief that you have sufficient energy to succeed in any endeavor; helps you develop your personal or attitudinal strength; enlivens the force of your personality; helps you focus your will power.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Asc = Sun`: energetic teamwork, bold initiative, intense drive, and a personality that wants to be recognized through what it does. Main caution: overheating the self: excitability, quarrels, or mishaps can arise when identity becomes too fused with fighting or proving strength.
