## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be active as a producer. Others exert pressure or compulsion. Injuries by fire or through fire-arms.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Effortful work on the spot, others lead at work, start work on the spot, work design with others, work performance in the environment, actions of local leadership, creative partner work, will direction of others, and procreation will of the partner

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Asc = Zeus` directs the Mars/Asc axis into production, pressure, compulsion, and purposeful activity driven by heat, weapons, or concentrated effort.
- Chart expression: active manufacture, forced effort, and an atmosphere of directed strain, with the harsher side showing injuries through fire, firearms, or other sharply focused force.
