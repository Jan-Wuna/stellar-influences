## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The betrothed person. Satisfaction and contentment in one’s activity. Content with oneself.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful Scelcn Arbcit, good mental action, I work with money, I succeed, my happiness is work, my good will, my right to work, my work joy, my financial activity, my Rchts Action, and psychic GIUcks Impulse

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Joy of living, love of enterprise, creative urge, procreative powers. Becoming engaged or married, ability to make favorable arrangements.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One is capable of playing the role of an authority figure who encourages others to act, but negatively may come across as excessively dominating if one’s fears and insecurities have not been released.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `224`

#### Munkasey Entry

A personal growth and nurturing by using the force or energy available to you; an ability to structure activities in ways that are beneficial; gaining recognition or fame thru using competitive activities as a starting point.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Jupiter = MC`: leadership that energizes others, a public role shaped by initiative and optimism, and the ability to make action serve growth or advancement. Main caution: dominance through certainty: encouraging action can slip into excessive control or inflated belief in one's own correctness.
