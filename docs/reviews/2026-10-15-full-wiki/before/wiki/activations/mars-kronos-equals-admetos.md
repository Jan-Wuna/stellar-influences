## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The state gives order to stop. Heavy pressure by power of state. Tyranny.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Perseverance in self employed work, blockade actions of the state, depressing state activity, containment of state work, end of self employed work, profound creation of the state, heavy work in the state, and resistance to state activity

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Kronos = Admetos` hardens official force into suppression, stoppage, and heavy pressure from above.
- Chart expression: state orders that halt movement, severe restraint imposed by authority, and in extreme form tyranny or crushing pressure exercised through official power.
