## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be active with other people. To give orders to others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

besceltec work on the spot, I work nearby, my work place, my activity Hir the environment, place for psychic treatment, psychic activity of others, and soul work of the partner

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Self-confidence, gains the respect of other people, assumption of leadership, recognition, attainment of success.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One identifies with doing what is right, which could possibly cause conflict with authority figures. To relate to others through encouraging them to act on what is right.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `252`

#### Munkasey Entry

Reflecting on your perceptions of how others change as they grow in life; an emphasis on the importance others have in allowing you to grow and mature as a person and thus gain recognition for your efforts or position.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/MC = Asc`: self-confidence, visible authority, decisive cooperation, and a way of relating that pushes others toward action or right conduct. Main caution: friction with peers or superiors when leadership becomes too forceful, moralizing, or combative.
