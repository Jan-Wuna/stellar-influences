## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Connections through the activity of the body. Sex unions of one’s own body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Working relations f'Lir men, day to day work meetings, contacts at the work centre, activating personal contacts, establishing personal contacts, effective human connections, establishing contact with man, and Centre for work placement

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Comradeship and good fellowship, the will to unite with others. Physical unions, the union of the sexes.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To know that it is important to work together with other people. To help those with whom one is connected to see the meaning and purpose behind their conflicts. One’s goals are furthered and supported by taking the initiative to form contacts with others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `244`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased needs to dominate during meetings where your views are presented for comment or criticism; the personal command and presence you bring before others; enhances your ability to lead others to your ideas.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Node = Sun`: purposeful alliance, confident initiative in relationships, and the sense that one's aims are best served by taking direct action with other people. Main caution: overidentification with the bond itself: union can become too driven by will, desire, or shared struggle.
