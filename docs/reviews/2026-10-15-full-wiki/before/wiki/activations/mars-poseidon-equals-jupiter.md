## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Satisfaction through the feeling of acting ethically and morally right. Satisfaction of acting legally. Happiness through one’s own mental activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success through the production of truth, successful cultural work, creating financial clarity, creating joy through truth, intellectual right to work, ideal financial work, ideal legal action, clarity through legal activity, and active labour law

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Poseidon = Jupiter` finds happiness and justification in acting according to law, ethics, and clear-minded principle.
- Chart expression: satisfaction in doing what feels morally or legally right, and to joy that comes from one's own mental, ethical, or educational activity.
