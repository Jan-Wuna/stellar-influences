## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Disturbances and interruption during instruction. Unpleasantness in such connections. Sickness, separation or death for many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of work tasks, success through hard work, success through concentrated action, mature work experiences, increased activity losses, lots of problems at work, creating knowledge through patience, and increasingly serious work

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Saturn = Apollon` spreads difficulty, interruption, and loss across instruction, wider groups, or larger fields of activity.
- Chart expression: disturbances in learning or organized work, unpleasant conditions in collective settings, and in severe cases sickness, separation, or death affecting many people.
