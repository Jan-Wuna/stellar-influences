## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Energetic work on the day. The man who works goal-oriented. The machinist. Injuries by torch flame or by firearms.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Effortful corporal work, personalizing work, leading work center, man's leadership will, creative personal activity, corporal work performance, personal work performance, and personal leadership work

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Zeus = Sun` embodies the axis in a man of active, goal-directed work.
- Chart expression: a purposeful male figure, technical labor, and daily action centered on making, operating, or firing things into effect, with attendant risk of burns or weapons injury.
