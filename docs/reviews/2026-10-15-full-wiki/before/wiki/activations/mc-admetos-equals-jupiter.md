## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Admetos = Jupiter` tempers the axis with muted fortune and benefit tied to dense or foundational resources.
- Chart expression: luck received without excitement, practical gain from land, minerals, or preservation, and a steady relation to good fortune that remains restrained and unsentimental.
