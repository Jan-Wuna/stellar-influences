## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To disincline tender feminine feelings. To pull oneself together in order to remain steadfast.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

I, as a woman, at the impoverished, my depressed people, my firm feelings, my feelings of restriction, my blocked wife, my limitations by the woman, my firmness by the woman, my spiritual depth, and the emotional strength of the woman

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Admetos = Moon` emotionalizes the axis through suppression of softness in favor of firmness.
- Chart expression: restrained feeling, pulling oneself together, and emotional life hardened so that tenderness is reduced in order to remain composed and steadfast.
