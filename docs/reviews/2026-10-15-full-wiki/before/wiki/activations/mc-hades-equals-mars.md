## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sorrow or damage through one’s own actions. Hesitate to deal, lazy. Little initiative. Wanting to commit evil acts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous soul work, I'm deprived of work, my work service, my secret activity, my past activity, my criminal activity, soul cummer treatment, mental grief at work, and hidden soul work

## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Hades = Mars` turns the axis toward damage through one's own actions and dark initiative.
- Chart expression: sorrow caused by acting badly or not acting at all, little initiative, lazy hesitation, and impulses that can slide toward harmful or destructive deeds.
