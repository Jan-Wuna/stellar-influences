## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Hades = Venus` darkens the axis through grief in love and degraded sensuality.
- Chart expression: sorrow over affection, vulgarized feeling, and a life direction in which desire or pleasure becomes entangled with shame, coarseness, or unhappy attachment.
