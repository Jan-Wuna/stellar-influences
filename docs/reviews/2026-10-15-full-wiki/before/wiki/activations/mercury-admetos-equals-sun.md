#### Witte Entry

Little exercise. To end a motion. The body at a standstill. No bodily perseverance. To be driven in a vehicle. The office-staff.

#### ABC Entry

Blocked young man, restriction of corporal mobility, blocked news center, basic description of a person, personal depression in youth, personal speech deepening, and traffic stau on the day

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Admetos = Sun` embodies the axis in bodily stillness, low motion, and office-like routine.
- Chart expression: minimal exercise, halted movement, being carried rather than moving oneself, and self-expression shaped by sedentary conditions or stalled bodily momentum.
