### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s way of saying things has a healing effect. One helps others to overcome identifying with negative ideas, and to identify more with the thoughts that will most grow one.

- `Mercury/Asc = Chiron` makes the Mercury/Asc axis healing in tone and corrective in effect.
