## Identity

- Astronomicon formula: `T/Asc = S`

## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Exchange of thoughts about the native.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Thinking Centre in the Nahc, Language in the personal environment, intellectual manned partners, personal thoughts about the other, personal description of the partner, writing centre in the place, and Centre for partner language

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inclination to cultivate the exchange of ideas and thoughts, the search for intellectual stimulation, desire to discuss vital questions. The state of defining one's position in relation to one's environment, the act of speaking or negotiating on matters that concern others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One feels it is important to exchange ideas with people. The goal and purpose of one’s life is connecting one’s ideas with people who will benefit from them.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `180`

#### Munkasey Entry

Emphasizing how to speak and relate to others; noticing another’s reactions to your ideas or thoughts; encouragement from others for your ideas; added importance in your communications; determination to get your ideas out.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Asc = Sun`: purposeful dialogue, intellectual self-presentation, and the search for stimulation through contact with others. Main caution: self-centered discussion: the need to state one's position can dominate the exchange and reduce genuine reciprocity.
