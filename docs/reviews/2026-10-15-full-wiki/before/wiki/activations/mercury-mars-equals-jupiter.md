Constructive criticism, constructive thinking, sound judgement. The ability to make one's way successfully, a celebrated orator or speaker, a good critic.

A constructive mind which likes to enlarge on whatever is thinking. An enthusi asm for discussing issues and working out problems. To be generous in supporting and encouraging the ideas of others. A strong faith in the possibility of resolving conflicts.
