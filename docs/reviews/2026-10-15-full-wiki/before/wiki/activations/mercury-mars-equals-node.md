## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Connection through discussion. To interfere suddenly. To be embroiled in disputes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Toughly embezzling language, creating language connections, activating language connections, activity connections to the media, reporting links to work, connection to man's youth, and mediation of youth work

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

The realization of thoughts in association with others. Quarreling within the community.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Seeking out people that one can communicate with openly, and who aren’t afraid to argue. Seeking out people who provide mental stimulation. One’s evolution depends on one’s ability to learn more and more about how to constructively commu nicate and how to make arguing a positive and beneficial experience.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `152`

#### Munkasey Entry

Acquaintances with others who have an irritating style to their speech; how communications with groups of associates can initiate their activity; people with similar interests in the expression or teaching of competitive practices.
