## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To comprehend and to express thoughts. To demonstrate will power in several different fields. To act according to well made plans.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

report about changes in work, changed opinion about work, growing activities of youth, growing media activity, and change of words at work

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A fanatical critic, over-zealous orator, sharp analyst. The misfortune of having to suffer heavy attacks or assaults from others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The relentless urge to communicate, and potential agitation when it isn’t work ing. The need to find better ways of getting one’s ideas across. Attempting to gain power over another through verbal hostility or abuse.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `152`

#### Munkasey Entry

Compulsive or continuous talk which irritates others; discussions about any suspicions which arise on the activities of self or others; angry communications from bosses about your writing, business, or educational capabilities.
