## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sensitive mind. Emotional thinking that is changeable. Moment of public comments. Thoughts about females. Opinions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Description of a woman's skeleton, animated mind, thoughts of my people, feelings language of the soul, I think of the woman, I at the a young woman, I speak as a woman, my wife speaks, my family language, and woman's soul language

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Purposeful thinking dictated by one's inner feelings. Expressing one's opinions about the female sex, communion of thoughts with women.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Being vulnerable to the ideas and thinking of authority figures. The emotional need to find basic ideas that will provide one security.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `184`

#### Munkasey Entry

The uncertainty and vacillation that creeps into your assessment of how life treats you; using intuition and impressions of life rather than study, work, and research to grow as a person; inconsistent thoughts on maturing.
