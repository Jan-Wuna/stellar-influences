## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Person gifted with much imagination. Given to lying. Hours of uncertain news, thoughts or messages.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Thought weakness of a woman, subtle thoughts of the woman, speech about the weak of the woman, young woman in the water, opinions in the people about metaphysics, language of a foreign Y olkes, sensitive woman in the speech, and unsettled youth of a Y olkes

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Pictures and ideas emerging from the subconscious mind, receptivity, a sympathetic understanding of other souls. An impressionable mind, a misguided imagination. A deceived or deceptive woman.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A receptiveness to subtle communications. The emotional need to fantasize, and ideally to share one’s fantasies with others. A person highly impressionable to ide alistic ideas.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `168`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased sensitivity to thoughts or ideas about how the mystical or spiritual realms work; opened receptivity to channeling information from non-physical sources; intuitive thoughts which bring confusion and questions.
