## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Thinking and understanding are one thing. The capability of adjusting one’s thoughts. The mobility of the mind coordinates well with external nimbleness and adaptability.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

I think of change, my change of mind, my movement in the water, my development in the traffic, my development in the youth, my opinion development, my soul development, and the growing language of the soul

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Ability to cope with any situation, good powers of observation, keen powers of judgment or discernment, prudence or circumspection, foresight, ability to arrange matters well.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Creating deeper and truer communication should be a major part of one’s life’s work. Mental battles with employers. A need to allow one’s mind to be mentally receptive to spiritual energy rather than shutting it out due to rigidly and relentlessly held attitudes and ideas.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `172`

#### Munkasey Entry

Added needs to grow and develop thru what you learn about life; receiving recognition from others for your achievements; altering your behavior so others can notice that you have more importance than before.
