## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To see efforts rewarded. To witness many hours of success. Might and power of masses in motion. Great success of nations. To spend many hours with mental themes. Exchange of higher and finer

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

expansion of the power of a people, influence through emotional knowledge, successful power of the woman, influence of an experienced woman, increase of the power of the woman, strength of a respected people, much violence in the people, and increasing violence against the woman
