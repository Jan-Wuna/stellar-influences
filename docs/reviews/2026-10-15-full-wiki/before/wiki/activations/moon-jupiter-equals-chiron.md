### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Your energy helps others to get more fully in touch with their feelings and to accept them. You innately encourage others to feel greater security and faith in the workings of the universe.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Jupiter = Chiron`: Your energy helps others to get more fully in touch with their feelings and to accept them. You innately encourage others to feel greater security and faith in the workings of the universe.
- Practical focus: makes `Moon/Jupiter` healing, reassuring, and quietly faith-restoring. The person tends to help others feel safer inside their own emotions and more trusting of life, so optimism becomes less performative and more therapeutic, grounded in acceptance and emotional permission.
