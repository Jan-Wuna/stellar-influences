### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Your energy helps other people to talk about and think about their feelings, and to be more connected to their emotions. You naturally induce a freer and warmer flow of interchange with others.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Mercury = Chiron`: Your energy helps other people to talk about and think about their feelings, and to be more connected to their emotions. You naturally induce a freer and warmer flow of interchange with others.
- Practical focus: makes `Moon/Mercury` healing, warm, and emotionally articulate. The person tends to help others speak about feelings more honestly and feel safer in emotional exchange, so thought and mood begin to cooperate instead of splitting apart.
