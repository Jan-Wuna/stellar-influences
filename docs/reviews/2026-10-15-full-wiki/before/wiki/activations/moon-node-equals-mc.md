## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sympathy toward women. Moment of contact or connection with a woman.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My relationship with the mother and my relationship with my wife

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Cultivation of individual soul unions. The initiation of a soul union. Moon-Ascendant

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Emotionally connecting with others is a major facet of one’s career, no matter what one’s actual career specifically is. Channeling spiritual energy into one’s emotional connection with others. One’s career involves bring others together on a feeling level.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `136`

#### Munkasey Entry

Achieving success thru meeting or allying with those who share your interests and goals; becoming recognized by influential people; learning and growing from your connections; the security felt thru mastering emotions.
