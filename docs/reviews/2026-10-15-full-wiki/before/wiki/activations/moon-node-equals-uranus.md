## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden surprising union of woman or with women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting connection to women, eventful people, excited women in connection, tense commuting, contact with women, new women's association, and reform links to people

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Restlessness in the presence of others. Sudden experiences with others, suddenly entering into a union or alliance.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A need to be able to freely connect with others on an emotional level. One has a genius for being able to find a way to connect on a feeling level as well as being able to perceive the direction in which emotions are evolving and to support that process.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `136`

#### Munkasey Entry

An unusual approach to meeting people who can help you understand life; meetings with astrologers to obtain counseling; innovative ideas during meetings with inventive or unique people; unusual emotional ties.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Node = Uranus`: awakening through relationship, unconventional kinship, and the ability to form bonds around change and difference. Main caution: instability in belonging: emotional ties can turn erratic, on-off, or difficult to rely on consistently.
