## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Quickly working brain. Mighty thinking activities. Talks and conversations with persons of public influence. One speaks publicly about exciting, sensational scandal stories. The public opinion and talking about it.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

influential love for the woman, force of peace of a people, loving influence of a woman, violence of love against the woman, power of a beautiful woman, strong feelings of love, strong feelings of harmony, strengthening through the love of a woman, and hour of love power

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Vulcanus = Venus` brings mental force, public influence, and strong collective feeling into Venusian themes of attraction, harmony, and affection.
- Practical expression: influential women or public voices, persuasive conversation, strong feelings of love, and emotionally charged public opinion; the caution is that desire or publicity can become forceful, sensational, or intrusive.
