## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Hades = Mars`: To make efforts in vain, useless work. Naval wars. War vessels. Destruction and death by water.
- Practical focus: hidden decline is directed into futile effort, conflict, and destructive struggle, especially where water, vessels, or war turn action toward loss rather than accomplishment.
