## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Ideas on love and affection are not clear and distinct. Wrong perception of love. To pretend love. Moment of deceptive peace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

I love the water, my love ancestors, my joy in the water, my love exchange, my weak for the kiss, my refined love, my love for breathing, my love for the air, and refined soul love

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Feigning or pretending particular feelings, simulation of love. Apparent show of attachment. The art of acting.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An appreciation of high-mindedness and of spiritual idealism. A love of present ing an idealized version of yourself to the world. The need for a love relationship that reinforces one’s spiritual ideals.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `340`

#### Munkasey Entry

Helps you visualize progress or new ideas in love, arts, or music; an increased need to show off your glamour or wealth as you progress in life; using charm to progress from lowly origins to loftier heights.
