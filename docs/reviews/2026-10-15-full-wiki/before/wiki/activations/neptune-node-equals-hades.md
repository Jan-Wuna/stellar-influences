### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To find oneself in a hopeless sorrowful condition. Not knowing a way out. To be separated from, or shunned by others on account of poverty.
