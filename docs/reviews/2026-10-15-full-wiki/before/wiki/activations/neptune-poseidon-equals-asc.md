## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Poseidon = Asc`: Others who follow the wrong faith, who advocate the erroneous faith.
- Practical focus: personalizes false faith through others, bringing one into contact with people whose beliefs are sincerely held but fundamentally misguided.
- Practical expression: clarity through intuition of the partner, cultural place of the future, place of culture by the water, weak reason of the partner, and unclear partner ideas.
