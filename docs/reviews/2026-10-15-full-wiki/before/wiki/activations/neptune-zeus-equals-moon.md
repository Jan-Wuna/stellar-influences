## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Zeus = Moon`: Creative imagination. Night hours sacrificed in vain. Persons who show off. Raving mad. Hour of futile creation.
- Emotional and imaginative output runs high but wastes itself easily, combining creative fantasy, display, exhaustion, and futile expenditure of feeling or effort.
