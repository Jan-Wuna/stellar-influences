## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Changes in the mental attitude towards the public and women. Connections of superficial nature.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

The development of a relationship, the development of contacts with a woman, a different relationship with a woman, growing contacts with women, the transformation of the skills in a relationship, changing skills in a connection, and changing connections of a people

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

The feeling of emotional inhibition in the presence of other people, inability to develop one's inner expression of soul or feeling.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To feel vulnerable to the power of other people. To feel the past as being on the one hand oppressive and yet to be extremely drawn to remaining connected to it. The need to be very careful about who one forms connections with, and to try to seek out others who will truly meet one’s needs to be nurtured in a way that encourages one’s personal growth rather than the development of dependency.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `PLUTO/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `344`

#### Munkasey Entry

The depth of emotions, good and bad, which arise from meetings with other people who arouse sensitive passions; the ups and downs of encounters with others taken to extremes; uncertainties after meeting powerful people.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Pluto/Node = Moon`: intense belonging, transformative emotional bonds, and the ability to experience deep regeneration through others. Main caution: possessive entanglement: relationships can become emotionally coercive, burdened, or hard to leave even when destructive.
