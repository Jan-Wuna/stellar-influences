## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To separate from love, women, or very agreeable things. Love’s sorrow.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Lost Soul Peace, love my duties, my serious skill, my love for patience, my love problems, my love for perseverance, the beauty of a mature soul, psychic harmony in old age, and love for mental tasks

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Sad feelings, renunciation, reserve. Separation from loved ones, grief or suffering.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To desire a love relationship that advances one spiritually. Ethical sincerity in love relationships. To feel that one receives a lack of respect from one’s lover. Art that expresses spirituality.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SATURN/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `304`

#### Munkasey Entry

Softens self-generated critical evaluations of self or others; feeling more worthwhile about your abilities and efforts; a love of music and the arts in special ways; personal pleasure as you age; a love of traditional music or art.
