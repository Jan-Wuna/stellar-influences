## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Suddenly appearing pestilence. A sudden persistent disease.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Excitement due to creeping losses, concentration on metaphysical events, sudden concentration weakness, concentration on technological future, unknown losses in technology, and restlessness due to air problems

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Irritability, excitability, inner unrest, peculiar inclinations. Illness caused through excitement or upset, weakness or illness manifesting suddenly.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One has original and unique insights into problems and their underlying and/or hidden nature, and into the structure of things in general. A genius for finding ways of bringing the products of one’s imagination into physical reality.

#### Munkasey Entry

Uncommon or unexpected recoveries from weaknesses which surface after a dormant period; a new discipline which adds astrology to religious and mystical practices; indifference to decay in older buildings or structures.
