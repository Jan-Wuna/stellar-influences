## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be a model to others mentally. Another person is a model to us. To be mentally separated from others in one’s outlook on life, to be different.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

patience and reason of the partner, spiritual tasks of a place, spiritual maturity of the partner, disturbed environmental culture, problems with the truth of the partner, recognize the difficulties of the other, environmental cultural duties, and rational separation from the partner
