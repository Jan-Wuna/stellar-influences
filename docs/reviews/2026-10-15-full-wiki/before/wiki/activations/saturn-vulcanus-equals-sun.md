## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The custodian, the watchman. Days on which one has to think of the bodily well being. The man who pays much attention to physical events or proceedings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Abandoned centres of power, one rich old centre, energy problems of the body, one sided personal tasks, personal loss of power, personal loss by administration, maturity by the one third of a person, and loss of body force
