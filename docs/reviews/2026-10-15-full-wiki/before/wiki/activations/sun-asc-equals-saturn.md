## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Hindrances through others. Separations from acquaintances. Funerals.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Old man's partner, responsibilities of a local centre, patience with other persons, well established personal environment, concentration on the personal environment, personal environment tasks, separations from other people, and losses in the personal environment

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inhibitions, shyness, difficult attainment of recognition. Difficulties in public life. The sharing of suffering together with others, separation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A fear of coming off as self-important. The slow and gradual attainment of greater levels of self-confidence in terms of how one interacts with others and projects oneself outwardly.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `96`

#### Munkasey Entry

Self-imposed restrictions on being yourself when meeting others; simple expressions which show the nature of your personality; direct but effective ways of demonstrating your personal sense of personhood to others.

## Derived Synthesis
