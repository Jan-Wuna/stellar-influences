## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Fortunate day on earth. Success in general, in daily business and efforts. Payment day in the general public. Well-to-do people. One who is a friend of nature, who feels physically comfortable when surrounded by nature.
