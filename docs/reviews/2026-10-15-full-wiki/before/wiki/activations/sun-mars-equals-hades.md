### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Man's work service, difficult daily work, dangerous personal work, man's criminal activities, personal activity in secret, dirty corporal work, and renunciation of personal hand Boys
