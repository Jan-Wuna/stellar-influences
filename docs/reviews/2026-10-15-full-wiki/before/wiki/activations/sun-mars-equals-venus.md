## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The erotic love of a man. The bed-fellow. A man who is active in love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work on the beauty of the body, work in the peace centre, prefer corporal work, human love acts, personal love acts, personal will for peace, activity in the art, and power of action of a harmonious man

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Urge to beget children, development of creative faculties. Intense love expression.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To express love in a vital and assertive way. To actively show one’s appreciation. To be able to get much done in a smooth and easy way. An appreciation of discipline. The tendency to find work relaxing.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `68`

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds a sensual flavor in your basic appeal to others; the grace of movement you exhibit while in motion; the pleasure you derive from physical activity; energy you put into being successful in any artistic fields of endeavor.
