## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be fortunate through passive physical behavior. Income gained through easy physical work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

success through personal sensitivity, financial weakness of the man, joy of a person in the air, happy person in the water, personal success through sensitivity, personal legal uncertainty, and uncertain legal matters for the person

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Optimism in spite of little vitality. The capacity to enjoy pleasant things in life in spite of weakness or illness, achievement of success with little exertion.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An enthusiasm for helping others and for being self-sacrificing. Having great enthusiasm for mystical experiences and the psychic realms, and possibly going to ward excess where these are concerned.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `84`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increases desires to slip away and live in a world of illusions and fantasies; expands your power to mentally visualize and create; developing a philosophy built around escape; basic and large fundamental errors.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Neptune = Jupiter`: spiritual generosity, success through sympathetic understanding, and the ability to enjoy life or gain help even under weakened conditions. Main caution: excess: idealism, sacrifice, or psychic enthusiasm can become inflated, indulgent, or impractically grand.
