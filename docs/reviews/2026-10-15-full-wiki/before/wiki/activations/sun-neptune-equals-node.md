## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Acquaintance with delicate or fragile people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

In the light of a personal connection, personal relationship with the water, personal contacts in the air, contacts with foreign people, and contact weakness of a person' insecure connection with the man

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Demonstration of a negative or weak attitude in the presence of others. Association with sick, weak persons or with blood relations.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Being connected to people who either drain one psychically, or uplift one through their higher vibrations. Wishful thinking and fantasizing about goals needs to be transformed into a real dedication to fulfilling one’s visions.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `84`

#### Munkasey Entry

Meeting and associating with people who have dreams and desires similar to your own; links to people or groups which have ties to the occult; feeling an attachment to those you meet or associate with; ridding self of bad ideas.
