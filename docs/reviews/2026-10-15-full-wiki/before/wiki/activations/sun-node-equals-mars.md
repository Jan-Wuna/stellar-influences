## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Physical sexual union. Contacts or ties through activity or work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Actions for human connections, man's work connections, day work meetings, convey corporal work, activate corporal relationships, create man's connection, and create personal connections

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Desire to become a person of importance, for recognition and to establish oneself within a community. Associations based solely on physical attraction, cooperation and teamwork.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Consciousness of others’ needs and purposes when one asserts oneself. One’s assertiveness comes off too others as egotistical. Taking action because one is aware of the importance of personal growth, but negatively one takes action with the motive of maintaining the status quo due to one’s ego attachment.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `92`

#### Munkasey Entry

An impatience to meet new friends and allies; the efforts you go thru to locate the kinds of people you need; expending energy to convey your importance to others; meeting effective competitors who can become allies.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Node = Mars`: assertive cooperation, strong group ambition, and the impulse to win recognition through doing rather than merely belonging. Main caution: friction: ego, competitiveness, or physical attraction can make associations unstable, exploitative, or conflict-prone.
