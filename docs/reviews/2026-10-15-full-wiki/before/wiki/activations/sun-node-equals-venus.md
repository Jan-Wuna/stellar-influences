## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Physical love union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Encounter with a gentle man, relations with artistic people, harmonious human Y bonding, love connection with man, personal love V bonding, day of a peaceful connection, and connection to a artificial center

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Interest in artistic performances. Desire for association with artists or people interested in art. Sociable. Beginning of a love relationship.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An appreciation of the need to evolve one’s self-confidence and spiritualize one’s ego. A love of gaining recognition from other people. Complacency caused by connecting with other people who stroke one’s ego in a superficial way.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `92`

#### Munkasey Entry

Affectionate feelings toward those you have recently met; a love for meeting or associating with friends; the calming influences which friends bring; association with artistic individuals; meetings on issues of beautification.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Node = Venus`: pleasant alliances, artistic or cultured company, and the beginning of affectionate or romantic bonds through shared feeling and mutual esteem. Main caution: complacency: vanity, ego-stroking, or superficial charm can weaken the deeper purpose of the connection.
