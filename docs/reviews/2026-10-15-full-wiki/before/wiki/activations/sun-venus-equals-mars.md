## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Love activity of a man.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work at the art, Harmonisch handeltende Person, artistic work of a man ' Love activity in the day, manlike love actions, human affection activate, create shaneness of the carper, and preference for carnal work

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Urge to love, desire to beget children. Creative power. A loving husband.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To assert oneself in a way that is centered, artful and smooth. The desire to create art or beauty. A passion for the arts, as well as well as passion in love usually manifesting in a controlled, centered way. Negatively, a tendency to assert oneself in a selfishly egotistical way. Smugness.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/VENUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `64`

#### Munkasey Entry

Urgency and passion tied to your needs for affection, and also the ways you give comfort or pleasure to others; arguments which arise over how you love, or how you are loved; helps stimulate possible jealous passions.
