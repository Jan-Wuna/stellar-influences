## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Perception of love. The love concept.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

In the case of man, I at the a person, body and soul in one lannony, love of body and body, and in the case of man, my individual, my personal subject, and the day of my love

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

One's own feeling of love, individual inclinations, a beautiful or wonderful human soul. An aesthetic person, art lover, lover of nature, artist. A love union.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To love the world is one’s life path. To channel beauty in the world is one’s life’s work. Inducing others to appreciate the meaning and value of life.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/VENUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `64`

#### Munkasey Entry

Building your reputation for being competent in artistic fields; adds to the respect others give you in return for your appreciation of their efforts; the maturity you bring when expressing love, caring, or affection to others.
