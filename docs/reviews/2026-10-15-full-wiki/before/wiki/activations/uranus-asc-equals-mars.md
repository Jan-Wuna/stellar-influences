## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Surprising deeds of others. To be injured physically by another person.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work in environmental technology, excitement at the place of work, new work partner, excited work partner, rhythmic partner work, tensions with the work partner, technical work at the place, technical activity with others, and restlessness in the environment of work

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Vigorous or active intervention in one's environment, inclination to become physically violent. Practical cooperation in the technological or industrial sphere. Suffering threats, injuries or wounds.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One asserts oneself in a highly independent manner that might shock, surprise, or intimidate other people. A person who is intent on doing things in his own way, and who rebels against being told what to do or how to do it.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `320`

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds a forcefulness to the way you interrupt others and set demands for your needs and requirements; activates your impulses to be different and disruptive; noisy ways of disrupting others; spirited agitation for your needs.
