## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To have to deal with low class people. Obliged to make oneself responsible for ugly deeds of others. Assassination of well- known persons. Others commit wicked deeds, the native has to set matters straight.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

On site activities, night work by Tcchnik on site, adverse partner stresses, new deprivations for the partner, sudden abandonment of the partner, technical deficiencies in the site, troublesome service to the partner, and defective environmental technology

## Derived Synthesis

- `Uranus/Hades = Asc`: To have to deal with degraded or difficult people. Obliged to make oneself responsible for ugly deeds of others. Assassination of well-known persons. Others commit wicked deeds, the native has to set matters straight.
