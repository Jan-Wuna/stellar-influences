## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be thrown into debt or to suffer losses through the deaths of others. Separations because of sudden deaths.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Tasks of a poor technology, exchange over sudden losses, fine tuning for technical problems, metaphysical event in old age, creeping restlessness in old age, technical problems of the future, and surprising losses by the weak

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Depression, instability, pessimism. A painful loss, mourning or bereavement.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To be afraid of exploring other realms, and yet one has a deep inner calling to do so. The need to take other planes of existence as well as one’s intuitive insights seri ously. One delves into all of these things, but tends to do so gradually and slowly, often with much questioning of them, and of oneself.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `308`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased need for solitude and silence to gain inspiration; puts restrictions on your ability to withdraw from unpleasant tasks or surroundings; restrains how you can effect reforms using ideals which are too visionary.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Uranus/Neptune = Saturn`: disciplined vision, reform under constraint, and the capacity to give shape to subtle or unusual insight. Main caution: strained containment: frustration between inspiration and structure can harden into discouragement or breakdown.
