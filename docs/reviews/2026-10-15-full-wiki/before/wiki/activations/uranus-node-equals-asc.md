## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Suddenly meet or make acquaintance. Restless relationships with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Ercignisive contacts with others, dynamic partner relationship, new place of encounter, rhythmic connection with the partner, tensions in connection with others, and communication with environmental reforms

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Tendency to become stimulated through other persons. Sudden and new associations.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One has the ability to freely connect with others. The desire to be able to change the self and to let go of the limits you tend to place on your identity so that you may grow.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `316`

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds a strong mental focus on the need for changing the world so that your ideas on how life operates become more popular; discussions you have with others about your theories and ideas; your need for feedback from others.

## Derived Synthesis
