## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden love or pleasant connection.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting peace connections, exciting love connection, harmonious connection pulses, sudden peace mediation, new love connection, preference for connections to technology, and mediation of peaceful reforms

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Intensified feelings in the presence of others. Experiencing sudden attachments, a person who falls in love quickly. An experience pertaining to the understanding of art.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The need for freedom in your relationships. Love relationships in which the people involved connect in a free and open way. The ability to get others to appreciate the need for change. A love of art that is freed of the past and carries a new and/or ex perimental energy that is meaningful.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `316`

#### Munkasey Entry

Softens the way you introduce your version of change into the world; novel ideas in music or the arts which you introduce others to; the glamour which accompanies the changes you initiate; satisfying sexual ties after encounters.
