## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Pleasantness and success through others. To be admired.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Peace success in the environment, joy in a gentle environment, artistic success in the place, love joy with a partner, love happiness in the place, right wing peace in the place, preference for money in the environment, and advantages of a gentle place

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Cheerfulness, a warm heart, sociable, confident. Acquaintanceship and love, being happy with others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One loves to look good and to get the attention and approval of others. The desire to wear beautiful clothes and jewelry and to be associated with finery. A warmly affectionate disposition.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `192`

#### Munkasey Entry

Concentrating on presenting ideas and opinions about your values or ethics; brings a softness to your personal style which pleases others and adds to your image of being a gentle, cultured, or refined person.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Jupiter = Asc`: likability, graceful optimism, and the ability to attract goodwill through kindness, beauty, and encouragement. Main caution: indulgent self-presentation: charm can become too performative, complacent, or dependent on pleasure and approval.
