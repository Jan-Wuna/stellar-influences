## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Rendezvous. Union of love. Connection with a successful or happy woman.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful peace contacts, success through connection to beauty, joy in a harmonious relationship, joyful love connection, peace through kine legal relationship, artificial success through connections, and preference for money in connections

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Ability to share joy with other people, an unselfish disposition. The state of coming together through unusually strong feelings of love, becoming engaged, a joy shared with others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The need to enlarge one’s ability to love and to appreciate life in this lifetime. Seeking out connections with people who are as enthusiastic about their values as you are. To connect to others in a loving, generous manner.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `192`

#### Munkasey Entry

Making the proper connections with people able to support your ideas or causes; joining groups where you opinions are valued; meetings where you receive feedback or input about your doctrines or ideas.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Jupiter = Node`: joyful companionship, fortunate social ties, and the ability to build goodwill and mutual benefit easily. Main caution: over-social indulgence: belonging can become too pleasure-seeking, wasteful, or dependent on being liked by everyone.
