### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Noble, honorable disposition or world conception. Love for one s fellow-men. Good deeds motivated by conviction. Preference for high spiritual things or matters. High conception of morals and moral behavior.

Special clarity through love, special love for truth, great peace idea, great preference for clarity, artificialness in the cultural state, love for the spiritual hierarchy, self appointed artist ideas, spiritual supremacy of the female principle, and state peace culture
