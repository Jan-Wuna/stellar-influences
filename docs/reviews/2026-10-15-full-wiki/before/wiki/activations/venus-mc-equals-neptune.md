## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Hopeless desire. Love without prospects. Lover’s grief. To renounce love. Sensitive in love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

I love the water, my insecurity in the air, my insecurity in the future, my infirm love, my infirmity in the future, my insecure inner peace, I love the artificial development, I love the changes, and I love to develop within

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A peculiar attitude to the problem of love, shyness, unexplainable dislikes or aversions. Renunciation, the state of being unsatisfied and unhappy (in love), suffering of the soul.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To love authorities in an idealized fashion. To need someone or some thing to worship. An idealistic love nature.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `220`

#### Munkasey Entry

Avoiding commitment in love; enhanced needs for an artificial means of increasing your beauty; self-delusion in what you are able to learn from loving another; a lack of respect for those who love you, or vice-versa.
