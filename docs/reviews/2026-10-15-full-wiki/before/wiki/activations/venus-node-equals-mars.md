## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sexual love union. Impulses, actions and decisions of pleasant relations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joyful contact work, establishing peace contacts, peaceful youth work, harmonious activity in Y bonding, creating harmonious relationships, love activity in relationships, and making connections to the arts

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A passionate disposition, desire for progeny, the procreative urge. Entering into a love union on the basis of physical attraction alone.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To assert oneself with an appreciation of how one’s actions will affect other people. To take the initiative to form loving connections with others, or to increase the harmony of a group.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `212`

#### Munkasey Entry

You encounter others with whom you have sexual attraction; the assertion of your interests to others; added persistence in your efforts to sustain your ideas and interests; demands for immediate gratification or satisfaction.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Node = Mars`: lively rapport, romantic pursuit, and the courage to make connection concrete instead of merely pleasant. Main caution: friction in attraction: desire can turn competitive, impatient, or quarrelsome when harmony and impulse collide.
