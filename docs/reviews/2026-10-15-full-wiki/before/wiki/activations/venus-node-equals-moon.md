## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Woman’s love ties. Ties to loving women. Love ties with many people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Peace mediation of a people, peaceful union of the refugees, fleeting encounter with art, love relations with women, peaceful people connections, harmonious community connection, contact pleasure of the woman, and connection to the woman

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Demonstration of one's feelings, desire to seek a cordial, genuine and complete understanding with others. The love union of a woman.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The emotional need to connect with others in a loving way. To feel vulnerable about expressing one’s love, values and appreciation to groups or individuals. One ‘s ability to connect lovingly is cyclic and depends on moods.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `212`

#### Munkasey Entry

Aids in the assistance or support you receive for your family thru group or personal associations; basic changes in the way you approach others because of advice received; avoiding meetings not needed; popular peace coalitions.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Node = Moon`: nurturing friendship, emotional reciprocity, and supportive domestic or communal ties. Main caution: sentimental attachment: the desire for closeness can become clingy, overly pleasing, or too dependent on mood and reassurance.
