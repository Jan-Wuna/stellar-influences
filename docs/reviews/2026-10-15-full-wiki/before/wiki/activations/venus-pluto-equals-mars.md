## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To create harmony. Sexual sympathy develops. One acts considerately.

#### ABC Entry

Produce peaceful developments, peaceful change of work, want to achieve harmonious change, develop love actions, develop love for work, love of work, love of changing work, growing love activity, and changing love impulses

#### Ebertin Entry

Strongly sensual nature, desire for many children (rape), a brutal and coarse expression of feeling.

#### Sandbach Entry

Intense love expressed with great passion. To feel an overwhelming urge to act. One is impelled to take initiative based on an intense love.
