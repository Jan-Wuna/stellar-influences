## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden interrupted or separated love union. One’s tend to break up.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Bclasted peace reform, stored love event, love impulses in old age, new problems in love, sudden love separation, sudden harmony problems, rhythmic artificial tasks, and tensions through trembled peace

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Controlled sensuality, suppressed tensions in love life, passing sexual and emotional inhibitions. Awakening from love illusions, estrangement, separation. (Difficult birth.)

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Shyness about expressing one’s own individual values. A fear of being open when relating to people. To love the unusual, but to fear being criticized or looked down on for it. Having to know someone a long time before one becomes open in relating.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `200`

#### Munkasey Entry

Moderation shown when demonstrating unusual methods of beautifying or embellishing self; caution taken with innovative forms of gratification; perpetuating new forms of expression involving art, finance, or music.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Uranus = Saturn`: the ability to give unusual bonds form, sustain originality with craft, and build freedom into mature commitments. Main caution: pressure between need and distance can produce inhibition, brittle rules, or abrupt severances when tension can no longer be contained.
