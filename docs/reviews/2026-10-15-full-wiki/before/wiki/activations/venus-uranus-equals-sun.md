## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Delicate feeling. To be loved suddenly. High minded love. Courtesy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dynamic love of a man, harmonious impulses of a man, impulses of a peace center, new artificial center, concrete love experience, personal artificial event, sudden corporal love, and preference of the man for rhythm

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Emotional receptivity, intuition through feeling, a nature quickly aroused. The love adventures of the male.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One feels it is important to relate to others in a free and open way. One long-range objectives are based on one’s own individual values, which may be quite different from mainstream ones.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `200`

#### Munkasey Entry

Effective or reliable ways of showing unusual forms of beauty and appreciation; influencing standards with innovative music or art forms; unusual forms of demonstrating innovation in finance, music, or the arts.
