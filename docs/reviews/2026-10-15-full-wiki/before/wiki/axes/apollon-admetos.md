## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Pair Summary

The feeling of satisfaction. Contentment. Satiety. To be saturated. Stagnation or standstill in trade, handicraft, commerce, industry or science. Science in a small circle, for only a few. Exclusive scientific circle. The few, the small circle. The exclusive circle.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Pair Summary

Blocked science, success through perseverance, consolidation of knowledge, freedom constraint, groindjiche experience, trade restriction, much depression, knowledge deepening, knowledge suppressing, and increasing resistance

## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Admetos` is the axis of contained expansion, satisfied limitation, and broad interests narrowed into stable, enclosed, or highly specialized channels. It combines Apollon's width, science, commerce, and abundance with Admetos's compression, standstill, and saturation.
- Chart expression: contentment, peaceful self-sufficiency, exclusive circles, specialized science or trade, and conditions in which growth has settled into stable sufficiency rather than active striving. Liabilities: complacency, satiety, lack of ambition, narrowed horizons, and stagnation in commerce, knowledge, or development once enough has been attained.
