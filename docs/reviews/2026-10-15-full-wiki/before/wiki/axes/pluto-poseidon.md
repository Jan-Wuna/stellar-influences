### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Pair Summary

Mental, spiritual and educational transformation and development. The development of the spiritual and mental behavior and inclinations. Change of view of the world, of life. Growth and development of elevated mental skills.

#### ABC Pair Summary

insight, evolution knowledge, intellectual development, ideas change, cultural changes, change development, truth development, and growing Kjarheit
