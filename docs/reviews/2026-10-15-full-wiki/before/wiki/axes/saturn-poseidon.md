## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Pair Summary

Education. The pattern, the model. Mental, spiritual or ideological separations. Instructions. Inhibited worldview. To isolate oneself mentally. Focus on directions and following instructions. Separation for logical reasons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Pair Summary

Patience and ability, spiritual tasks, spiritual maturity, mental disturbances, hard truth, courage through patience, culture, problems with truth, and difficulties to recognize
