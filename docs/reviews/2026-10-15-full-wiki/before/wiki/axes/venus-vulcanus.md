## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Pair Summary

The biological urge of man. Great power of attraction. Passion. Mighty love. Ardent desire. Bold passion. Passionate action. Longing. Overblown love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Pair Summary

One flour of beauty, peace power, harmonious force, power of joy, artificial influence, love energy, love violence, power of love, powerful harmony, and strength of the female principle
