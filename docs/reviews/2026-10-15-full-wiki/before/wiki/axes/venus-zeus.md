## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Pair Summary

Devoted activity. Seed. Sperm. Procreation out of love. Increase. To seed. To procreate. To produce love. To force peace. Creative in art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Pair Summary

Start of a love, joy fire, peace effort, design skill, harmony strife, K unt performance, artificial direction, love fire, love divination, and creative arts
