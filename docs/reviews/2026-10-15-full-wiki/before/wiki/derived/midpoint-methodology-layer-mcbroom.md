## Derived Synthesis

McBroom's method layer treats midpoint pictures as chart synthesis problems rather than memorized standalone definitions. A midpoint picture should be identified, organized, prioritized, interpreted through the contributing factors, and then integrated back into the whole chart.

The practical retrieval sequence is:

1. Identify the exact midpoint picture or focal point.
2. Retrieve the canonical activation page first when the formula is explicit.
3. Retrieve the axis page to understand the shared pair meaning.
4. Retrieve factor pages only for repeated factors, personal points, or unclear symbolic weight.
5. Use this methodology layer to decide priority, not to change the formula's source meaning.

## Priority Rules

- Start with focal points and exact midpoint pictures before broad thematic browsing.
- Treat the 90-degree sort as an organization tool for conjunction, square, and opposition contacts, not as a separate doctrine.
- Use orbs as a filtering policy; do not treat every loose symbolic resemblance as an active picture.
- Give special attention to Sun/Moon, Asc/MC, closest midpoint pictures, and Aries Point contacts when they are actually present.
- For unknown birth times, down-rank Moon, Asc, and MC dependent claims unless the method layer supplies a controlled workaround.
- Keep transits, progressions, and solar arcs as activation or timing layers; do not rewrite the natal midpoint meaning from timing evidence alone.
