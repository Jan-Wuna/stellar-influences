## Derived Synthesis

- `Admetos/Poseidon = Vulcanus`: mighty and lasting educational effect, strong formative pressure, and the capacity to impress or be impressed deeply over time.
- `Admetos/Vulcanus = Poseidon`: mental bondage, guardianship, and severe hindrance imposed on intellectual or cultural development.
- `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Admetos`: To enjoy esteem and respect in a small circle.
