## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Poseidon = Vulcanus`: major success achieved through colleagues, comrades, and like-minded cooperation, often with public notice or sensation.
- `Apollon/Vulcanus = Poseidon`: great success in ideas, influential philosophy, and the strong spread of a conception of life.
- `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Apollon`: broadens powerful ideas and spiritual influence into public esteem: being regarded by many as honorable and respected, with success and honor arising because one enjoys high esteem.
