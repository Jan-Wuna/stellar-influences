## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Admetos = Asc`: a tranquil, contented personal field in which breadth has settled into visible peace, sufficiency, and calm enclosure.
- `Asc/Admetos = Apollon`: a fixed or superior personal position before many people, with others feeling smaller, more limited, or inclined to look up to the native.
- `Asc/Apollon = Admetos`: delayed business, a stable but limited customer base, and an outward network that no longer grows freely.
