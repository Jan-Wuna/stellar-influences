## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Apollon = Cupido`: customer flow tied to residence, doorstep commerce, tenancy, and immediate neighborhood relations.
- `Asc/Cupido = Apollon`: enlarged residential or communal life, including many apartments, tenants, urban dwellings, or broad communal-religious structures.
