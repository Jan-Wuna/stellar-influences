## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Cupido = Poseidon`: belonging to groups for cultured, gifted, or highly educated people and a communal field shaped by refinement of mind.
- `Asc/Poseidon = Cupido`: spiritually interested relatives or close associates and belonging with people who share the same worldview.
- `Cupido/Poseidon = Asc`: being among people who represent a faith, body of knowledge, or worldview.
