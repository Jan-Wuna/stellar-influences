## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Apollon = Zeus`: contact with tools, machines, weapons, or technical interests entering the immediate environment from afar.
- `Asc/Zeus = Apollon`: productive activity with others that expands into science, industry, or larger coordinated creation.
- `Zeus/Apollon = Asc`: To receive technical and practical training with or through others. Handling of fireworks, machines, apparatus or other technical things. Technical schools for arms and war.
