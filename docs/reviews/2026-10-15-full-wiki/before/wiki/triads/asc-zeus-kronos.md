## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Kronos = Zeus`: forced activity under command, technical or mechanical employment, and mobilized labor.
- `Asc/Zeus = Kronos`: being stimulated to action by superiors, rank, or official authority.
- `Zeus/Kronos = Asc`: To be connected with great producers. Connection with military leaders. Others take (seize) the lead.
