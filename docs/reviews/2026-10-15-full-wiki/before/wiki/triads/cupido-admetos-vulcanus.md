## Derived Synthesis

- `Admetos/Vulcanus = Cupido`: hard trials within unions, collective burdens, and artistic or social hindrances imposed through group conditions.
- `Cupido/Admetos = Vulcanus`: great hindrance through family or partner, suppression by those conditions, or hard fighting for an artistic or creative work.
- `Cupido/Vulcanus = Admetos`: working alone at a mighty artistic creation, going to the source of things, and fighting heavy resistance under concentrated strain.
