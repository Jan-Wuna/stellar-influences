## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Admetos = Cupido`: satisfaction with one's circle, settled social harmony, and creative or communal life contained within familiar conditions.
- `Cupido/Admetos = Apollon`: satisfaction with a small circle, limited but real cooperation, or a compact scientific or intellectual society.
- `Cupido/Apollon = Admetos`: being recognized in a small circle for artistic or scientific accomplishment, while broader possibilities in those fields are hindered.
