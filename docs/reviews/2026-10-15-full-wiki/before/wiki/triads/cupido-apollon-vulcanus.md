## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Vulcanus = Cupido`: strong collective success, social prosperity, and large creative or artistic achievement achieved with others.
- `Cupido/Apollon = Vulcanus`: great family meetings, major celebrations, organizational conferences, and the greatest economic or artistic success.
- `Cupido/Vulcanus = Apollon`: the expansion of a mighty association, great extended art creations, and art products made for large audiences.
