## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Kronos = Vulcanus`: might and influence through distinguished background, social appearance, state enterprise, and domineering force in art or society.
- `Cupido/Vulcanus = Kronos`: a state-approved society, privileged class status, and ingenious artistic accomplishment backed by formal standing.
- `Kronos/Vulcanus = Cupido`: state-protected or state-subsidized organizations, united great powers, and group structures backed by concentrated authority.
