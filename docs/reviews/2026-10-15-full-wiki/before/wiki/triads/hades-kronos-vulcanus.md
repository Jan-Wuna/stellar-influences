## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Kronos = Vulcanus`: great meanness and evil, devilish government conduct, and murder on a large scale.
- `Hades/Vulcanus = Kronos`: gigantic crimes, vast meanness, and heavy oppression or suppression by governments and ruling powers.
- `Kronos/Vulcanus = Hades`: misery caused by great powers, war-borne killing, and a state force that does not stop at cruelty or degradation.
