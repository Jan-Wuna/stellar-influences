## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Kronos = Zeus`: brutality and meanness deliberately targeted, hate made operational, destructive fire, and the ordering of war atrocities.
- `Hades/Zeus = Kronos`: miserable service conditions, unemployment enlarged by bad government, dissatisfaction with official conditions, and great destructive fire.
- `Zeus/Kronos = Hades`: Great efforts under trying and difficult circumstances. Overworking. Surprising and grave events in war. Fire catastrophes. Ordered war atrocities.
