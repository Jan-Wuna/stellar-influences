## Derived Synthesis

- `Chiron/Asc = Jupiter`: hopeful identity healing, uplifting guidance, and a confidence that grows through spiritual perspective. Main caution: inflation: spiritual encouragement can become overstatement or a grandiose healing identity.
- `Jupiter/Asc = Chiron`: an enthusiastic and generous aura that encourages others to act similarly and heals through sympathetic understanding in relationship.
- `Jupiter/Chiron = Asc`: presence itself being medicine. It can show a person who naturally uplifts, welcomes, and eases others simply by how they meet them.
