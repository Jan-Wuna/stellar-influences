## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Apollon = Jupiter`: happiness in peace, successful fellowship with many, and good fortune through art, science, or commerce.
- `Jupiter/Apollon = Cupido`: enjoyment of arts with many, theatre or concert life, and bank accounts or shared financial holdings.
- `Jupiter/Cupido = Apollon`: married happiness for many, a prosperous and peaceful community, and blessings of peace shared across a broad social or organizational field.
