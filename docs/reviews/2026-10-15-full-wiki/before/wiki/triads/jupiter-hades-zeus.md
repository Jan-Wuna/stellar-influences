## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Zeus = Jupiter`: pleasant relaxation on one side, but also monetary loss through machinery, fraudulent enterprise, and ventures that begin well yet end badly.
- `Jupiter/Hades = Zeus`: innovations not realized because of money shortage, with the source also noting weakened fertility and limited regenerative ability through sickness.
- `Jupiter/Zeus = Hades`: yield from agriculture or fuel and practical material production, but also lowered vitality in children or products.
