## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Apollon = MC`: luck, money handling, and success in commerce or science as a visible calling.
- `Jupiter/MC = Apollon`: luck or money through trade or science and a happily widening life path.
- `MC/Apollon = Jupiter`: blesses the axis with happy experience and success in science or commerce.
