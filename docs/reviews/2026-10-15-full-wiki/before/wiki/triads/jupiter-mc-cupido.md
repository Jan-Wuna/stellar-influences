## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Cupido = MC`: one's own happy marriage or family life, or a public artistic identity linked with successful communal belonging.
- `Jupiter/MC = Cupido`: pleasant social feeling, contentment in married life, and luck through art or communal belonging.
- `MC/Cupido = Jupiter`: blesses the axis with happiness through marriage, art, and community.
