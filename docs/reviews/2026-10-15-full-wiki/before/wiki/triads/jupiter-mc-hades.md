## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Hades = MC`: the shrinkage of fortune, worry over restriction or lack of money, and the possibility of secret investment.
- `Jupiter/MC = Hades`: contentment in modest circumstances.
- `MC/Hades = Jupiter`: clouds the axis through financial or material sorrow.
