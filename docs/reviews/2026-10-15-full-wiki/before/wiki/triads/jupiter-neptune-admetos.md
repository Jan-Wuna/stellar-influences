## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Admetos = Neptune`: wasted money, wrong investment, and lost capital.
- `Jupiter/Neptune = Admetos`: pretended happiness in a small company, difficulty getting on in the world, and change in ownership.
- `Neptune/Admetos = Jupiter`: This activation enlarges `Neptune/Admetos` through fortunate change, relief, and better living conditions. It shows that even a difficult or depleted baseline can evolve toward improvement, abundance, or more hopeful material circumstances.
