## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Cupido = Neptune`: marriage connected with obstacles and mistaken investments by firms or associations.
- `Jupiter/Neptune = Cupido`: contented married life in poor circumstances and a feeling, or imagined feeling, of happiness among associates.
- `Neptune/Cupido = Jupiter`: This activation enlarges troubled union through excess, misplaced optimism, or costly domestic strain, showing marital luck undermined by extravagance and practical instability.
