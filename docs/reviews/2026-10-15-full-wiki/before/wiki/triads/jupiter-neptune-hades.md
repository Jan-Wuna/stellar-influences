## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Hades = Neptune`: shortage of money and money losses caused by deception.
- `Jupiter/Neptune = Hades`: a rough awakening from bliss, bad luck, money through theft or robbery, and losses through speculation.
- `Neptune/Hades = Jupiter`: This activation gives dark or decaying conditions a paradoxical opening through water, liquids, or excess, mixing bodily weakness and wastefulness with a capacity to fare unexpectedly well amid watery danger.
