## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Kronos = Neptune`: the undoing of good luck in leadership, or the attainment of leadership through fraud and subterfuge.
- `Jupiter/Neptune = Kronos`: great carelessness, leading people pretending to be lucky, speculation backed by government, Nobel-level distinction, and governmental pretended luck.
- `Neptune/Kronos = Jupiter`: This activation expands compromised management into easy gain and rapid loss, showing extravagance, inflation, and the shrinking of holdings under poorly bounded authority.
