## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Neptune = Zeus`: money earned through inventions, technical ideas, or work such as patent law.
- `Jupiter/Zeus = Neptune`: effort without success, action that fails to solidify, and productivity weakened by confusion, vagueness, or misdirection.
- `Neptune/Zeus = Jupiter`: This combination points to fortunate returns from subtle or volatile enterprises, including sudden gain or luck through oils, spirits, or related substances.
