## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Cupido = Saturn`: the loss or ending of a happy marriage, withdrawal from an otherwise good community, losses in a successful corporation, or family limits caused by money matters.
- `Jupiter/Saturn = Cupido`: family beginning to separate, marriage settlement, many under one roof, or a union recognized only slowly and later.
