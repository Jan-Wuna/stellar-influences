## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Admetos = Uranus`: sudden disturbance of rest, grave property damage, and abrupt forced spending.
- `Jupiter/Uranus = Admetos`: sudden luck for a few, within a small or exclusive group, corporation, or circle.
