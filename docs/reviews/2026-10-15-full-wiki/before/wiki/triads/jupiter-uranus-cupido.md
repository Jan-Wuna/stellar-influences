## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Cupido = Uranus`: a sudden accomplished marriage, pleasant unexpected connections, or being surprisingly treated as one of the family.
- `Jupiter/Uranus = Cupido`: a sudden fortunate wedding, sudden luck in community, or unexpected gain in business.
