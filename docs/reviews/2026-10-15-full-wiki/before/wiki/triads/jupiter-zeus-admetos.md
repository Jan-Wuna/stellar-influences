## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Admetos = Zeus`: good luck in overcoming a constrained position, restarting movement, and the compulsion to spend money to do it.
- `Jupiter/Zeus = Admetos`: promising productivity that starts under pressure, delay, or heavy conditions before it can show its value.
- `Zeus/Admetos = Jupiter`: Happy and contented activity under different conditions. Satisfactory new situation.
