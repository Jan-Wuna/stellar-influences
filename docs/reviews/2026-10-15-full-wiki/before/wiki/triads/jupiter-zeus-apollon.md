## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Apollon = Zeus`: lucky and cheerful work with many, successful creation in arts and science, stage success, and production favored by high capital.
- `Jupiter/Zeus = Apollon`: successful creation for many, beneficial work in science, commerce, or industry, and prosperity through widening output.
- `Zeus/Apollon = Jupiter`: Happiness and success through learning, studying and searching. Luck and success in the arts and works of peace like science, commerce, trades or industry.
