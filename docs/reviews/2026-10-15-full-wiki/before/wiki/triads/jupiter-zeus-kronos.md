## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Kronos = Zeus`: mastery of productive work, luck with machines, and preparation for large public financing such as an initial public offering.
- `Jupiter/Zeus = Kronos`: successful government aims, major artistic work, and productive activity backed by authority, rank, or large ambition.
- `Zeus/Kronos = Jupiter`: Acting out of power, to enforce implicitly. Brightly blazing conflagration. Sharpshooters. War machines. Armored ships, tanks.
