## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Poseidon = Mars`: division of minds and breaking with others because of principles, morals, or incompatible ideas.
- `Mars/Asc = Poseidon`: refines the Mars/Asc axis through moral conduct, principled behavior, and understanding gained through other people.
- `Mars/Poseidon = Asc`: brings ethics and intellectual action directly into relationships and the immediate environment.
