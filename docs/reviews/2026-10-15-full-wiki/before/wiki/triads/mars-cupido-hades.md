## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Hades = Mars`: quarrels in poor families or associations and being forced to work for a struggling or degraded firm.
- `Mars/Cupido = Hades`: degrades Mars/Cupido through misfortune, sickness, and corruption in communal or married life.
- `Mars/Hades = Cupido`: low communal surroundings, social gatherings touched by death or scandal, and family or marital loss through violence.
