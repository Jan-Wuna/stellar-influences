## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Kronos = Mars`: base acts by bosses, superiors, police, or government, harsh coercion, and mass murder.
- `Mars/Hades = Kronos`: vicious acts by authorities, judicial murder, and harm issued from the top of the hierarchy.
- `Mars/Kronos = Hades`: turns official force destructive, punitive, or loss-producing.
