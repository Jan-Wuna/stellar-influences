## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Cupido = Mars`: betrothal leading to marriage, successful joint action, and productive work inside good firms, financial companies, or legal organizations.
- `Mars/Cupido = Jupiter`: fortunate cooperation, happy marriage, and benefit from joint, artistic, or family enterprise.
- `Mars/Jupiter = Cupido`: brings fortunate action into union, shared undertaking, and artistic or communal fulfillment.
