## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Kronos = Mars`: asserting oneself successfully, acting as a financial manager, operating in high finance, and directing exceptional amounts of money.
- `Mars/Jupiter = Kronos`: elevates successful action into leadership, distinguished accomplishment, and self-directed authority.
- `Mars/Kronos = Jupiter`: links executive power with advantage, money, legal gain, and protected status.
