## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Cupido = Mars`: energizes the axis into marriage intention, collaborative work, and active group effort.
- `Mars/Cupido = MC`: conscious movement toward marriage and committed activity within a community.
- `Mars/MC = Cupido`: brings professional and public action into group, family, and partnership commitments.
