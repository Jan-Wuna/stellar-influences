## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Apollon = Neptune`: breakdown of scientific or commercial activity, but also exploration into unknown fields where outcomes are not yet secure.
- `Mars/Neptune = Apollon`: spreads confusion and harm widely across groups, markets, or large populations.
- `Neptune/Apollon = Mars`: This activation energizes `Neptune/Apollon` through effort spent in uncertain, unproven, or occult fields. It often shows work that pushes into invisible or speculative territory, where activity is real but success is elusive or wasted through diffusion.
