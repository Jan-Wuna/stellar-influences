## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Neptune = Vulcanus`: magnifies weakened or destructive force into major epidemic or large-scale ruin.
- `Mars/Vulcanus = Neptune`: wastes exceptional force through dissipation, futility, or leakage of power.
- `Neptune/Vulcanus = Mars`: Action and force are shown as stalled or turned destructive, with strong energies breaking down values, influence, or existing power.
