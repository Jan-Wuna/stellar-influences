## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Admetos = Node`: association with deeply depressed, pessimistic, or burdened people.
- `Mars/Node = Admetos`: narrows shared action into cliques, small circles, and compressed group effort.
- `Node/Admetos = Mars`: To work together with few. Small working community. Working alone. To reject a working community or to be rejected by a joint-stock company. Connected by fighting for freedom.
