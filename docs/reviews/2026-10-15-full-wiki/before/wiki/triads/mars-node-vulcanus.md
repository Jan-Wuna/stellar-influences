## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Node = Vulcanus`: intensifies common action into powerful, force-backed collective effort.
- `Mars/Vulcanus = Node`: links one with physically strong, forceful, and energetic people.
- `Node/Vulcanus = Mars`: To be busy. Very industrious. To display eager action.
