## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Hades = Saturn`: work conducted at a loss, labor without reward, and death through murder, suicide, or crushing force.
- `Mars/Saturn = Hades`: deepens blocked force into privation, severe sickness, and harsh death conditions.
