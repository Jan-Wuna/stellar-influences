## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Poseidon = Vulcanus`: intensifies doctrine, ethics, and mental effort into forceful conviction and powerful ideology.
- `Mars/Vulcanus = Poseidon`: gives ethical, educational, and intellectual action unusual force and impressiveness.
- `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Mars`: Tremendous mental activity. Bold, honorable and dignified action. To act as a gentleman.
