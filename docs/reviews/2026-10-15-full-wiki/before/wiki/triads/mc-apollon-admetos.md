## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Admetos = MC`: peace of mind, stable satisfaction, and balance so settled that ambition relaxes.
- `MC/Admetos = Apollon`: widens the axis into self-sufficiency while keeping it bounded by necessary restriction.
- `MC/Apollon = Admetos`: compresses the axis into saturation, contentment, and an end to striving.
