## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Zeus = MC`: negativity, idleness, hatred, mean tricks, paralysis, arson, industry, and loss of business by fire.
- `MC/Hades = Zeus`: directs the axis toward active wrongdoing and the making of disharmony.
- `MC/Zeus = Hades`: drags the axis into sorrow, hindrance, dirty work, and service under difficult conditions.
