## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Admetos = Zeus`: directs the axis toward slow-starting but sustained initiative.
- `MC/Zeus = Admetos`: hardens the axis into beginnings that must fight through the strongest obstruction.
- `Zeus/Admetos = MC`: Ready to start, willingness to begin, ability to proceed. To give something shape, image and expression. To feel a new task as a burden.
