## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Apollon = Zeus`: directs the axis toward engineering, technology, fire, and natural science.
- `MC/Zeus = Apollon`: extends the axis into scientific, commercial, and inventive productivity.
- `Zeus/Apollon = MC`: Gifted hands and nimble fingers. To be practical. To solve problems in a practical manner rather than by making a study of them.
