## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Kronos = Zeus`: directs the axis toward command in engineering, machinery, or military settings.
- `MC/Zeus = Kronos`: elevates the axis into independent command, large-scale invention, and technical authority.
- `Zeus/Kronos = MC`: To be energetically active. The will to see it through. Great strategist. Military expert.
