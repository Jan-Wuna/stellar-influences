## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Poseidon = Mercury`: discussion, debate, conference, and exchange among congenial or similarly minded people.
- `Mercury/Apollon = Poseidon`: refines the axis into shared ideas among like-minded thinkers.
- `Mercury/Poseidon = Apollon`: To come into contact with others who have the same ideas.
