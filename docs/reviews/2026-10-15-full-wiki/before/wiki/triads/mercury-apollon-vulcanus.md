## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Vulcanus = Mercury`: forceful correctness in thought, statements that land with impact, and ideas strong enough to be understood and accepted.
- `Mercury/Apollon = Vulcanus`: gives the axis strength, force, and broad understanding through discourse.
- `Mercury/Vulcanus = Apollon`: Thoughts which prove to be correct. Successful thinking.
