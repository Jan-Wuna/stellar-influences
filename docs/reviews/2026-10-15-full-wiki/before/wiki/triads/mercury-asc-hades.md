## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Hades = Mercury`: bad news, talk about ugly or evil matters, and communication entangled with mean intent or degrading conditions.
- `Mercury/Asc = Hades`: darkens the Mercury/Asc axis into gossip, error, and toxic retrospection.
- `Mercury/Hades = Asc`: brings the axis into the immediate environment through defamation and harmful nearby influence.
