## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Admetos = Mercury`: talks about scarcity, old or recycled materials, and a sad or pessimistic mood caused by want.
- `Mercury/Admetos = Hades`: darkens the axis into remorse, brooding, and painful retrospection.
- `Mercury/Hades = Admetos`: compresses the axis into blocked thought and hidden impasse.
