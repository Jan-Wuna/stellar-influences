## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Vulcanus = Mercury`: confident thinking, optimistic speech, large plans, hopeful ideas, and messages that carry persuasive weight.
- `Mercury/Jupiter = Vulcanus`: strengthens the axis into forceful constructive optimism and large-scale transactions.
- `Mercury/Vulcanus = Jupiter`: To concentrate strongly on one’s own fortune. The feeling of bliss.
