## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Zeus = Mercury`: creative thought, cheerful and busy conversation, joy in one's occupation, and messages that announce or stimulate fruitful activity.
- `Mercury/Jupiter = Zeus`: drives the axis into persuasive performance and directed salesmanship.
- `Mercury/Zeus = Jupiter`: Good comprehension. Success or profit through creative ideas. Humorous and witty. Good command. Laws concerning pleasures or money.
