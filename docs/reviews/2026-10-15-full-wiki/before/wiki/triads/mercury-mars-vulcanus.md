## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Vulcanus = Mercury`: intensifies thought and speech into combative, excited, and warlike mental activity.
- `Mercury/Mars = Vulcanus`: strengthens the axis into penetrating speech and rapid decisive force.
- `Mercury/Vulcanus = Mars`: To act fast, to proceed energetically, without noticing resistance.
