## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Neptune = Zeus`: drives the axis into limitless creative ideation without follow-through.
- `Mercury/Zeus = Neptune`: Secret agreements. Secret laws. Destructive or fruidess requests. Unable to command. Commands without effect.
- `Neptune/Zeus = Mercury`: The mind moves quickly toward unusual or covert ideas, favoring spontaneous schemes, secret planning, or thought centered on concealed means.
