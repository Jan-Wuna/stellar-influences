## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Hades = Node`: connects the axis to degraded associations and unhealthy company.
- `Mercury/Node = Hades`: degrades the axis into unclean ties and compromised correspondence.
- `Node/Hades = Mercury`: Pessimism. Sad news. Secret signs. Secret marks. The jargon of the underworld. To plot a crime.
