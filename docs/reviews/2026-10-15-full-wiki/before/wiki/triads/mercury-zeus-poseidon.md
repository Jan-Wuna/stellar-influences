## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Poseidon = Zeus`: To convince others through ideas. The power of conviction of an idea. Ideas which can be proved correct.
- `Mercury/Zeus = Poseidon`: To get new ideas, to create new ideas.
- `Zeus/Poseidon = Mercury`: Ideas, thoughts, and opinions about mental deportment, attitude and behavior.
