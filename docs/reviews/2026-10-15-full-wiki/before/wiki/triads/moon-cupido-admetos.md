## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Admetos = Moon`: lonely, deserted women, passing female association, or solitary hours of rest and relaxation.
- `Moon/Admetos = Cupido`: Village incorporated. Community in crowded circumstances. A community that wants to live by itself.
- `Moon/Cupido = Admetos`: Close community with few in leisure hours.
