## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Apollon = Moon`: women or the public showing interest in art or science and the native being known through those fields.
- `Moon/Apollon = Cupido`: Many hours devoted to art, leisure, relaxation. Common, peaceful competition of the nations. Olympic games.
- `Moon/Cupido = Apollon`: To learn together with many other people, to spend leisure hours together with them.
