## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Hades = Moon`: hoarding women, women from a lower sphere, popular or low art, and the public under the effect of a sad event.
- `Moon/Cupido = Hades`: Poor betrothal. Sad bridal party. Sad leisure hours. No hours of leisure because of grief. Sick wife.
- `Moon/Hades = Cupido`: Poor woman as a widow. Poor womans or a widows leisure hours. Fatal hour for family.
