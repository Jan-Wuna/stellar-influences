## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Apollon = Moon`: mass impoverishment, extensive suffering, epidemics, drought, and collective deficiency.
- `Moon/Apollon = Hades`: To live through many sad hours. Misery in masses. Widespread poverty. People in dire need.
- `Moon/Hades = Apollon`: To have to care for many. Many worries. The mass of the lower people. Need and worries of a multitude of people.
