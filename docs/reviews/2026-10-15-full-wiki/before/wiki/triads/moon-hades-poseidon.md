## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Poseidon = Moon`: odd women or odd public types, and a lack of shared understanding between the native and other people.
- `Moon/Hades = Poseidon`: Sad disposition. Sad mental condition. Stupidity and superstition among the people.
- `Moon/Poseidon = Hades`: Insensible of finer, high spiritual things. Lack of empathy. Men who are unable to perceive or conceive spiritual things.
