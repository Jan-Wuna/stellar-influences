## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Neptune = Vulcanus`: This activation intensifies `Moon/Neptune` into powerful disappointment, strong psychic impression, and deception with force behind it. It can magnify sensitivity into overwhelming reaction, making intuition, scent, or subtle perception unusually potent but also more vulnerable to disillusionment.
- `Moon/Vulcanus = Neptune`: To cause sensation suddenly. Sudden appearing of superiors. To deal with representatives of technique. To have suddenly to do with armed people, with soldiers. To be impressed with soldier like behavior. Fine sense, scent and great sensitivity. To be noticed for weakness, want and unfitness. To withdraw from public influence.
- `Neptune/Vulcanus = Moon`: Public or personal feeling is marked by deep deception, with the native or surrounding people strongly sensing, carrying, or suffering that disillusionment.
