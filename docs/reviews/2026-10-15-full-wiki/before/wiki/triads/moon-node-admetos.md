## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Admetos = Node`: Tied to a cramped space. Coming in contact with death or matters of death.
- `Moon/Node = Admetos`: This activation constricts `Moon/Node` into suppressed association, shrinking community, and emotional belonging cut off or brought to an end. It points to bonds among women or the public being narrowed, stalled, or dissolved under pressure.
- `Node/Admetos = Moon`: Close union with some women. End of the connection with the mother. Little connection with women or the public. The connection of people with their dead. Undeveloped mind. People close together in narrow rooms. Public under strong pressure.
