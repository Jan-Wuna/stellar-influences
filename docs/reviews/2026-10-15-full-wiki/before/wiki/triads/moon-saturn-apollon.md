## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Apollon = Saturn`: Separate from many. To quit an educational project. Separation of nations.
- `Moon/Saturn = Apollon`: This activation expands `Moon/Saturn` through education, instruction, and extended late-hour effort. It suggests emotional seriousness distributed across teaching, study, or long evening obligations, making restraint more useful and socially transmissible.
