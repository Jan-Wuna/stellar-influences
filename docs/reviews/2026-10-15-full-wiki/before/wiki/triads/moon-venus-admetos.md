## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Admetos = Venus`: Death of a beloved person (female). Longing to be loneliness. Fond of meditation. To be content in narrow conditions. A people who are content with their narrow space.
- `Moon/Venus = Admetos`: This activation constricts `Moon/Venus` into deep but unreturned attachment, where tenderness persists under limitation or emotional non-reciprocity. It points to love that is sincere and selfless yet held in a closed or lonely form.
- `Venus/Admetos = Moon`: peace of mind.
