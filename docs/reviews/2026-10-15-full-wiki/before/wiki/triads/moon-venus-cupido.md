## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Cupido = Venus`: Women who marry for love. To prefer the company of women. Leisure hours devoted to love.
- `Moon/Venus = Cupido`: This activation socializes `Moon/Venus` through weddings, happy brides, and collective expressions of loving identity. It ties affection to ceremonial belonging, public sisterhood, and group forms of shared emotional value.
- `Venus/Cupido = Moon`: marital happiness of the woman.
