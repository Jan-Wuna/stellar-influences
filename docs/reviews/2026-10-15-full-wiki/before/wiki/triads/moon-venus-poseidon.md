## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Poseidon = Venus`: Highly sensitive women. Female persons with inclination to occultism.
- `Moon/Venus = Poseidon`: This activation spiritualizes `Moon/Venus` into refined feeling, platonic love, and subtle feminine insight. It favors affection expressed as delicacy, purity, or higher perception rather than raw possession or appetite.
- `Venus/Poseidon = Moon`: strongly and firmly believing persons.
