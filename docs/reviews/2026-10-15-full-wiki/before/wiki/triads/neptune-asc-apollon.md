## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Apollon = Neptune`: bad credit, uncertain business standing, and contacts that look wider than they can actually support.
- `Neptune/Apollon = Asc`: This activation externalizes `Neptune/Apollon` through personal encounters with fraud, deception, or its exposure. It makes large or diffuse falsehoods tangible in the immediate environment, often through others, while also allowing concealed truth to surface.
- `Neptune/Asc = Apollon`: This activation broadens interpersonal deception into repeated patterns, teaching through multiple disappointments, gimmicks, and unstable dealings in trade, study, or exchange.
