## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Zeus = Neptune`: complete relaxation, inability to pull oneself together, impotent hate, futile rage, steam burns, smoke suffocation, smog, and choke damp.
- `Neptune/Hades = Zeus`: This activation directs hidden decline into wasted effort, destructive aftermath, and future-hindering consequences, where the unresolved past burns through action, machinery, or catastrophe.
- `Neptune/Zeus = Hades`: Hidden, dirty, or toxic fire conditions are emphasized here, with destructive gas mixtures or firedamp bringing damage and loss.
