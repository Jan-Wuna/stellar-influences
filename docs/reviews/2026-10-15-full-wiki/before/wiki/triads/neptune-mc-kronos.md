## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Kronos = Neptune`: dissolves the axis through non-recognition, rejection, and uncertain authority.
- `Neptune/Kronos = MC`: This activation internalizes compromised authority as personal incompetence in leadership, showing the managerial role itself weakened by poor judgment, confusion, or inability to direct effectively.
- `Neptune/MC = Kronos`: This activation elevates dubious conduct into independent fraud, manipulation of superiors, or uncertain authority, showing a leadership stance that operates through concealment and questionable means.
