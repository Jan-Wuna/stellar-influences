## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Cupido = Pluto`: This activation transforms communal dissolution at its roots, marking the beginning of separation, shifts in secret organizations, or changing pressure around shared living conditions and collective bonds.
- `Neptune/Pluto = Cupido`: This activation moves hidden transformation into family and home, showing slow domestic changes, secret promises, and private matters that gradually alter belonging without immediate public recognition.
- `Pluto/Cupido = Neptune`: This activation emphasizes dissolution of a family or partnership; Uncertain social and family conditions; To quit a corporation or society.
