## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Pluto = Vulcanus`: This activation intensifies secret development until it pushes for decisive release, forcing hidden processes into visibility through pressure, rupture, or final exposure.
- `Neptune/Vulcanus = Pluto`: Large changes begin quietly and out of sight, with powerful transformations set in motion before they are outwardly recognized.
- `Pluto/Vulcanus = Neptune`: This activation emphasizes to see no way out of a crisis; To deceive oneself about the possibilities of changes and transformations; Conditions that are dragging on.
