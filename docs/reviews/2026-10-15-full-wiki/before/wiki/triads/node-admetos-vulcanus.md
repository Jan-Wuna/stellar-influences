## Derived Synthesis

- `Admetos/Vulcanus = Node`: being tied to conditions of severe handicap, fate pressure, and burdens carried through contact with others.
- `Node/Admetos = Vulcanus`: To display extraordinary activities with few. Undertaking great things with few. To be heavily chained or to burst the chains.
- `Node/Vulcanus = Admetos`: Mighty connection with few. End of a mighty union or connection.
