- `Cupido/Kronos = Node`: wishing to establish a family, witnessing a marriage legally, and connection with great art.
- `Node/Cupido = Kronos`: To go to the marriage official. Distinguished social connection. To mix socially with high minded persons or superiors.
- `Node/Kronos = Cupido`: Civil marriages. Social connections with superiors or official persons.
