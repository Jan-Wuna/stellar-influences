## Derived Synthesis

- `Node/Poseidon = Vulcanus`: To be noticed in mental connections through eagerness and alertness. Influence in or through mental connections.
- `Node/Vulcanus = Poseidon`: Eagerness mentally as well as spiritually. Mighty connection through mental faculties.
- `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Node`: To be connected with people who have pride and dignity . Relationships with the type of people where one can count on honesty and decency. Powerful intellectual connections. To be eager to cultivate the fine intellectual contacts.
