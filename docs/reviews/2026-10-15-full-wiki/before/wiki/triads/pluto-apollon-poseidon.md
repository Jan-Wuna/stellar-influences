## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Poseidon = Pluto`: the beginning or formation of comradeship, association, or a group of like-minded people that gathers force over time.
- `Pluto/Apollon = Poseidon`: This activation emphasizes to experience radical transformations in the concept of the world with or together with friends, colleagues, comrades or like-minded people.
- `Pluto/Poseidon = Apollon`: This activation emphasizes to experience a transformation with many other people which is mental and emotional.
