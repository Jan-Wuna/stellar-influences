## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Apollon = Pluto`: building a new science from small beginnings, large change, and growing or expanding authority through reform.
- `Pluto/Apollon = Kronos`: This activation emphasizes great changes in a state, in politics, in the government.
- `Pluto/Kronos = Apollon`: This activation emphasizes broad advancement; Growth of rank, influence or organized capability.
