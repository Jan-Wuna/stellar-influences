## Derived Synthesis

- `Pluto/Kronos = Zeus`: This activation emphasizes intensified duty and performance in service of advancement or authority.
- `Pluto/Zeus = Kronos`: This activation emphasizes best performance record; To be forced to accomplish the maximum; The boss or the chief or the government demanding the impossible thing, the unrealizable.
- `Zeus/Kronos = Pluto`: The beginning and the development of an expanded work. On the way to great accomplishment. Expansion of a great war.
