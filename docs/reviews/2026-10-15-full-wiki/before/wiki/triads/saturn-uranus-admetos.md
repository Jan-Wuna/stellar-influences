## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Admetos = Uranus`: sudden jarring stop or throttling, to suddenly apply a brake, to suddenly change the course.
- `Saturn/Uranus = Admetos`: to get into or out of a tight spot by surprise, sudden separation of a few.
- `Uranus/Admetos = Saturn`: Enmities which start suddenly and are of long duration. Hindrances and obstacles through the same. Sudden coming of death.
