## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Uranus = Zeus`: losses through conflagrations or acts of war, enforced separation connected with losses.
- `Saturn/Zeus = Uranus`: sudden enforced separation, to be exposed to pressure, loss by fire or lightning.
- `Uranus/Zeus = Saturn`: To obey a force with reluctance. Losses through explosions, also through violence, death through such. Sudden storm. Thunderstorms with damages.
