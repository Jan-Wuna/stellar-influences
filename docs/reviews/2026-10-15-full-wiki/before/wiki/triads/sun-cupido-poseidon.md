## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Poseidon = Sun`: the person of faith or deep thought and active membership in a spiritual, educational, or philosophical community.
- `Sun/Cupido = Poseidon`: This formula emphasizes ideal or spiritual community and to strive for peace.
- `Sun/Poseidon = Cupido`: This formula emphasizes spiritual community and idealistic fellowship.
