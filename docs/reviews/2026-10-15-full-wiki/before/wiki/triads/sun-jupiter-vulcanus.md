## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Vulcanus = Sun`: joy of life, physical well-being, healthy strength, and a person who feels prosperous, vigorous, and supported by strong life force.
- `Sun/Jupiter = Vulcanus`: This formula emphasizes powerful good fortune and strong success.
- `Sun/Vulcanus = Jupiter`: This formula emphasizes powerful good fortune and great success.
