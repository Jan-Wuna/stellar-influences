## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Zeus = Sun`: embodies the axis in a man of active, goal-directed work.
- `Sun/Mars = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes fiery action and the drive to create, fight, and push events forward.
- `Sun/Zeus = Mars`: This formula emphasizes the urge to create or to do something and to lead in a fight and win.
