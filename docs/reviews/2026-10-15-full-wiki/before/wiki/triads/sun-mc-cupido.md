## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Cupido = Sun`: embodies the axis in marriage, membership, and visible artistic participation.
- `Sun/Cupido = MC`: This formula emphasizes domesticity and family.
- `Sun/MC = Cupido`: This formula emphasizes domestic focus and family as a central life direction.
