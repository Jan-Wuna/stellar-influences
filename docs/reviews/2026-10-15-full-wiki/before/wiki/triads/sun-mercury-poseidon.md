## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Poseidon = Sun`: Spiritual conception of being. The spiritualized body. The daily intellectual inspiration.
- `Sun/Mercury = Poseidon`: This formula emphasizes truthful or spiritual thinking and inspired communication.
- `Sun/Poseidon = Mercury`: This formula emphasizes truthful thought and spiritualized communication.
