## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Hades = Sun`: This activation personalizes hidden decline as bodily weakening, infection risk, and exposure to watery danger, making physical vulnerability and self-endangering drift central themes.
- `Sun/Hades = Neptune`: This formula emphasizes the weak person or the sufferer and illnesses difficult to detect.
- `Sun/Neptune = Hades`: This formula emphasizes spiritual suffering and confusion under adverse conditions.
