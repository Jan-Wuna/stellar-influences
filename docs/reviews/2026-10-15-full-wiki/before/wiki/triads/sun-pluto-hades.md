## Derived Synthesis

- `Pluto/Hades = Sun`: This activation emphasizes difficult days; Bodily decline or harmful physical change.
- `Sun/Hades = Pluto`: This formula emphasizes drastic intervention in the case of disease and surgery.
- `Sun/Pluto = Hades`: This formula emphasizes destructive change, decay, or suffering tied to transformation.
