## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Admetos = Saturn`: This formula emphasizes to cut oneself off from connections with others and the inability to make contact with others.
- `Sun/Saturn = Admetos`: This formula emphasizes severance from contact and restricted or isolated conditions.
