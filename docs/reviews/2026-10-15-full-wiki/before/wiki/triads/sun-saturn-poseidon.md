## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Poseidon = Saturn`: This formula emphasizes a burdened soul and emotional inhibition.
- `Sun/Saturn = Poseidon`: This formula emphasizes spiritual sobriety and truth encountered through hardship or restraint.
