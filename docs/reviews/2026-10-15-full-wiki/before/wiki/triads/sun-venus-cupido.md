## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Cupido = Venus`: This formula emphasizes family love and marriage.
- `Sun/Venus = Cupido`: This formula emphasizes family love, marriage, and affectionate belonging.
- `Venus/Cupido = Sun`: marital happiness of the man.
