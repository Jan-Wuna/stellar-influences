## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Kronos = Venus`: This formula emphasizes love of rank and harmony with authority.
- `Sun/Venus = Kronos`: This formula emphasizes love linked with status, authority, or refined dignity.
- `Venus/Kronos = Sun`: readiness for charitable activities.
