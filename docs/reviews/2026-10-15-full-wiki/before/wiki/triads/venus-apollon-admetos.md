## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Admetos = Venus`: self-sufficient peace, inward rest through aloneness, and reduced need for relational disturbance.
- `Venus/Admetos = Apollon`: affectionate and perpetual friendship in a small circle.
- `Venus/Apollon = Admetos`: a few or no friends.
