## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Kronos = Venus`: great artistic taste, accord with leading people or great artists, pleasant intercourse in distinguished circles, and legally permitted love.
- `Venus/Cupido = Kronos`: marital happiness fostered by the authorities.
- `Venus/Kronos = Cupido`: possess the great talent to give someone joy.
