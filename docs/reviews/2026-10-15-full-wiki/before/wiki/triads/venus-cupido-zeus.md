## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Zeus = Venus`: harmonious association and trade dealing in beauty, comfort, or domestic furnishing.
- `Venus/Cupido = Zeus`: conclusion of a marriage, of a sex relationship.
- `Venus/Zeus = Cupido`: devoted artistic creation.
