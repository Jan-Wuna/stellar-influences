## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Admetos = Venus`: joy in giving and money gladly spent.
- `Venus/Admetos = Jupiter`: happy in a close circle, or resting deeply absorbed in thoughts.
- `Venus/Jupiter = Admetos`: to have a few good friends.
