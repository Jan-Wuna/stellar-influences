## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Kronos = Venus`: This activation moves high aspiration into love and value, showing desires that cannot be fulfilled directly and elevation achieved instead through sacrifice, refinement, or renunciation.
- `Venus/Kronos = Neptune`: predestined nurse.
- `Venus/Neptune = Kronos`: sanitariums for blood ailment or for hysterical females.
