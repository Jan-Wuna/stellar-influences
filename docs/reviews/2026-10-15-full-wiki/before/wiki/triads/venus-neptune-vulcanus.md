## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Vulcanus = Venus`: This activation moves hidden force into love, value, and attraction, showing deception in affections and bonds where sympathy, dependence, or vulnerability become the price of approval or attachment. It can mark both loss of magnetism and coercive emotional dynamics masked as care.
- `Venus/Neptune = Vulcanus`: simulated or hidden mighty affection.
- `Venus/Vulcanus = Neptune`: to wield a surprisingly great power of attraction.
