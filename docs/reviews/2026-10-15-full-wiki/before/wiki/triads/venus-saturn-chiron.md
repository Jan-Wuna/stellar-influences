## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Chiron = Venus` brings healing to fear and limitation through love, beauty, and fulfillment. It helps others become more available to affection and pleasure after constriction; the caution is turning love into a therapeutic duty or withholding warmth until someone is "healed.".
- `Venus/Chiron = Saturn`: difficulties encountered in love, as well as lack of gratification in life and lack of prosperity encourage one to recognize the deeper value of all one’s experiences, and eventually to come to see that one is truly rich.
- `Venus/Saturn = Chiron`: one's very vibration helps others to contact the deepest and most profound aspects of love, and to realize the ways they have cut themselves off from love, as well as the fears that caused them to do this.
