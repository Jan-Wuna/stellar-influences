## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Vulcanus = Vernal Point`: exposure to violent, fierce, or superior individuals in the general public.
- `Vernal Point/Asc = Vulcanus`: This formula emphasizes industrial country and to be connected with others through machine work.
- `Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Asc`: This formula emphasizes to have to deal with violent people, others let us feel their power, and to experience violence trough the environment or trough others.
