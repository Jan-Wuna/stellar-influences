## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Zeus = Vernal Point`: the establishment of new societies, general artistic products, and comradeship in arms.
- `Vernal Point/Cupido = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes the founding of a corporation or club.
- `Vernal Point/Zeus = Cupido`: This formula emphasizes a general increase in the number of marriages and an organization with a goal such as trade-unions or political parties.
