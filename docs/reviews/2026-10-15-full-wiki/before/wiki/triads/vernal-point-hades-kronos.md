## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Kronos = Vernal Point`: atrocities, great mortality, sacrifice of human lives, major crimes by or against government, and failed plots.
- `Vernal Point/Hades = Kronos`: This formula emphasizes great general want, great depression, general worries through leading forces, and coal mining.
- `Vernal Point/Kronos = Hades`: This formula emphasizes poverty for many people, great misfortune for many people, unhealthy leader, and secret leadership.
