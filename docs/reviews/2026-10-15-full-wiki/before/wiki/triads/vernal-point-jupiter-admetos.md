## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Admetos = Vernal Point`: rest and seclusion in general, luck that is confined rather than broad, and production of raw materials.
- `Vernal Point/Admetos = Jupiter`: This formula emphasizes the overcoming of difficulties in general, moderate luck, fortunate with property, and fortunate in retirement.
- `Vernal Point/Jupiter = Admetos`: This formula emphasizes general rest and quiet, rich sources of raw materials, and gains from raw materials.
