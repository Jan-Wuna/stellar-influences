## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Asc = Vernal Point`: happy companionship with many.
- `Vernal Point/Asc = Jupiter`: This formula emphasizes jolly company, to be cheerful together, rich places, and fertile areas.
- `Vernal Point/Jupiter = Asc`: This formula emphasizes being fortunate with other persons.
