## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Uranus = Vernal Point`: fortunate circumstances, a stroke of good luck for several people, or lottery-like luck.
- `Vernal Point/Jupiter = Uranus`: This formula emphasizes sudden good fortune and increase in property.
- `Vernal Point/Uranus = Jupiter`: This formula emphasizes sudden luck and increase in property.
