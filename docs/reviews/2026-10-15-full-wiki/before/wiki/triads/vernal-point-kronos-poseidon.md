## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Poseidon = Vernal Point`: high mental influence in the general public, the overall cultural or educational level, and public aspiration toward higher standards.
- `Vernal Point/Kronos = Poseidon`: This formula emphasizes magnitude of intellect and spirit, humanity, philanthropy, the dignity of men, and high culture.
- `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Kronos`: This formula emphasizes mental or intellectual leadership and spiritual standard of the time.
