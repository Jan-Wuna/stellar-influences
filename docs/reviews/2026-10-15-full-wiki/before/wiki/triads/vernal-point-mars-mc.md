## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/MC = Vernal Point`: makes executive action broadly public and socially commanding.
- `Vernal Point/MC = Mars`: This formula emphasizes to work with other people, to let others work, and to look energetic, active and busy.
- `Vernal Point/Mars = MC`: This formula emphasizes to be busy, one who has courage, to feel energy in the public, to be ready for public labor, and moment for start-up.
