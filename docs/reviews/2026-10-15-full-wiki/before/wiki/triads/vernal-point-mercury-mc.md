## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/MC = Vernal Point`: externalizes the axis as opinion, reputation, and public commentary.
- `Vernal Point/MC = Mercury`: This formula emphasizes personal statements in public, conversations in the street, and the minute (moment) of public news.
- `Vernal Point/Mercury = MC`: This formula emphasizes to be interested in general opinion and to be influenced by public opinion.
