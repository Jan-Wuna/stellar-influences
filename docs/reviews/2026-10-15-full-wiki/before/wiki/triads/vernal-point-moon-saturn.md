## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Saturn = Vernal Point`: This activation externalizes `Moon/Saturn` into the suffering public, laboring classes, and general losses carried by a population. It shows emotional deprivation or endurance becoming social fact, especially in the collective tone of endings, work, or late hours.
- `Vernal Point/Moon = Saturn`: This formula emphasizes a separated woman, a forsaken woman, an old woman, a widow, and an old nation with dying out populace.
- `Vernal Point/Saturn = Moon`: This formula emphasizes to take leave of a woman and the parting hours.
