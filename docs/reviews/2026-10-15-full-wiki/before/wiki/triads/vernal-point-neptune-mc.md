## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/MC = Vernal Point`: This activation externalizes false ideas into the public sphere, where many people are drawn into mistaken notions, fraud, or collective misperception.
- `Vernal Point/MC = Neptune`: This formula emphasizes to be deceived with others and uncertain and muddy relations to the public.
- `Vernal Point/Neptune = MC`: This formula emphasizes to be deceived or disappointed by the public.
