## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Poseidon = Vernal Point`: This activation externalizes false faith or confused ideals into the shared atmosphere. It can show public error, collective susceptibility to misleading attitudes, or broad social participation in a mistaken belief pattern.
- `Vernal Point/Neptune = Poseidon`: This formula emphasizes fanaticism, fanatic penetration of mental power, and uncertainties caused by major floods, smoke or vapors.
- `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Neptune`: This formula emphasizes refined mental motions, despondency, and deterioration of a philosophy or belief.
