## Derived Synthesis

- `Node/Apollon = Vernal Point`: Freedom. Business or scientific connections of a general nature. The global internet. Worldwide scientific relationships.
- `Vernal Point/Apollon = Node`: This formula emphasizes connections related to science or commerce and many are connected together.
- `Vernal Point/Node = Apollon`: This formula emphasizes union or connections of scientists or merchants, widespread connections, and freedom to establish relationships.
