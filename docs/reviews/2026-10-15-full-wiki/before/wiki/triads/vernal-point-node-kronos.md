## Derived Synthesis

- `Node/Kronos = Vernal Point`: Connections with the authorities and with the courts.
- `Vernal Point/Kronos = Node`: This formula emphasizes to make contact with people in ruling circles and international contacts between government leaders.
- `Vernal Point/Node = Kronos`: This formula emphasizes connection of people with higher or special rank and connection of states.
