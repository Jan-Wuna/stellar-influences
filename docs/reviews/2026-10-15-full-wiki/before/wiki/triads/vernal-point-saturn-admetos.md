## Derived Synthesis

- `Vernal Point/Admetos = Saturn`: This formula emphasizes endurance, patience, chronic resistance (consistently), and losses and difficulties.
- `Vernal Point/Saturn = Admetos`: This formula emphasizes to be urged into a corner, general restriction and curbing, and obstruction and standstill.
