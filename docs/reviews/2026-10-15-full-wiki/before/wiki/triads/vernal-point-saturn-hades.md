## Derived Synthesis

- `Vernal Point/Hades = Saturn`: This formula emphasizes poor pitied humans, refugees, strangers, decay, and unidentifiable after death.
- `Vernal Point/Saturn = Hades`: This formula emphasizes dismissals of many workers, epidemics, to move away for sad reasons, refugees, and to be a fugitive.
