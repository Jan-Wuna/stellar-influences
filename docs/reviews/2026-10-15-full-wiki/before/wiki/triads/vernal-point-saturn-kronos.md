## Derived Synthesis

- `Vernal Point/Kronos = Saturn`: This formula emphasizes large agricultural estates, forestry, separation and prohibition ordered by the state, and authorities issue orders to evacuate, a curfew or a shut down.
- `Vernal Point/Saturn = Kronos`: This formula emphasizes lockouts, hindrances through officials or superiors, shutout, lockout, and territorial losses.
