## Derived Synthesis

- `Vernal Point/Neptune = Saturn`: This formula emphasizes the end of a romantic affair, of a beautiful dream, losses through fraud, and pestilential diseases.
- `Vernal Point/Saturn = Neptune`: This formula emphasizes separation through sickness, suspected infection, quarantine, to end a state, and to reject something definitively.
