## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Saturn = Vernal Point`: This formula emphasizes isolation or pressure in the immediate environment.
- `Vernal Point/Saturn = Sun`: This formula emphasizes to bid farewell, a day of separation, and dying.
- `Vernal Point/Sun = Saturn`: This formula emphasizes physical sickness, physical separations, sorrow, losses or grief for several, and later years of life.
