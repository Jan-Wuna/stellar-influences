## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Zeus = Vernal Point`: This formula emphasizes activity in the environment and force meeting circumstance directly.
- `Vernal Point/Sun = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes to master the body, capability to lead, person who lead others, and days with fire.
- `Vernal Point/Zeus = Sun`: This formula emphasizes an untiring worker, efforts of the body as regards work, and machine attendants and railroad employees.
