## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Apollon = Vernal Point`: many friendships.
- `Vernal Point/Apollon = Venus`: This formula emphasizes concord and peace, to have many friends, and the science of music.
- `Vernal Point/Venus = Apollon`: This formula emphasizes general peace, concord, unity and friendship.
