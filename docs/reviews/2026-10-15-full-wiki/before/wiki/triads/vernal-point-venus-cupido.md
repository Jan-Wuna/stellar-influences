## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Cupido = Vernal Point`: marital happiness of many people.
- `Vernal Point/Cupido = Venus`: This formula emphasizes a harmonious community and clubs to promote sociability.
- `Vernal Point/Venus = Cupido`: This formula emphasizes marital matters for female persons and a marriage based on love.
