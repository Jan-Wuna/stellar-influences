## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Uranus = Vernal Point`: mating time.
- `Vernal Point/Uranus = Venus`: This formula emphasizes sudden expressions of love and sudden peace.
- `Vernal Point/Venus = Uranus`: This formula emphasizes sudden frenzy of love and sudden events for female persons.
