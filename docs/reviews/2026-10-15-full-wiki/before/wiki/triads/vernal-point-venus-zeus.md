## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Zeus = Vernal Point`: general procreation, propagation.
- `Vernal Point/Venus = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes general procreative frenzy, fortunate sowing time, and the good sower.
- `Vernal Point/Zeus = Venus`: This formula emphasizes natural selection and to produce something beautiful for the general public.
