General Remarks 
The observation and research of illness requires a great deal of care 
and tact. The author has been particularly careful in all of his state- 
ments. No-one should be tempted to give a diagnosis on the basis of 
the cosmic picture alone because it is impossible. So many factors add

the chart. One should always consider that Biological Correspon-

spondences can become effective through various circumstances.

Those who work on cosmobiology must be constantly aware that it is 
nonsense to read the destiny from the stars alone. It is also important

should be conscious of the responsibility and the damaging conse-

The advantages of this method of presentation in terms of struc- 
tural-elements and structural-pictures will be obvious. Looking for 
similarities in charts erected in the usual manner is extremely tedious. 
It is assumed, however, that in converting the chart into its 90° form 
that the 90o-disc is used which enables one to also see the 360° chart. 
The interrelationships of the 360° should not be neglected, whilst an 
even better overall assessment of the chart can be obtained from the 
use of the cosmo-psychogram. However, these work methods will 
only yield their real value when all those interested are working to- 
gether and can assemble really substantial quantities of material. This 
book is only a very modest beginning.
