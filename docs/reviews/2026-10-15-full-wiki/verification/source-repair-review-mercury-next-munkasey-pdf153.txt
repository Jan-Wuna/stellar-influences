MERCURY/MARS with Planets and Points 
 
 
Sun

people; a preference for loud and showy announcements or proclamations; displays

Moon 
An accumulation of reasons for the use of force; defensive tactics against an usually 
communicative person; irregular observations of impatience or irritation; loud talk on 
family or home matters; verbal and active women. 
 
Ven 
An appeasing element amidst talk on the use of force; self satisfaction gained from 
thoughts of stimulation; the intimacy which follows discussions of sexual activity; 
delight received from activity or athletic practices. 
 
Jup 
An abundance of talk about the use of force or might; too much commotion among many 
correspondents; permission granted for talks on the use of energy or force; generosity 
while educating those who have many questions. 
 
Sat 
Avoiding discussions about force or might; frustrations with efforts to compromise 
during negotiations about noise or struggles; negotiations which endure loudly for a very 
long time; an old argument is raised and debated. 
 
Ura 
Unusual approaches to discussing arguments which have irritated people in the past; 
unexpected outcomes from talks involving activity of self or others; insightful gossip 
about energy sources; revision of ideas on noise. 
 
Nep

business or military strategy; neglecting complaints about irritating noises; frustrations at 
not being able to convey hostility toward others. 
 
Plu 
Compulsive or continuous talk which irritates others; discussions about any suspicions 
which arise on the activities of self or others; angry communications from bosses about 
your writing, business, or educational capabilities. 
 
Nod 
Acquaintances with others who have an irritating style to their speech; how 
communications with groups of associates can initiate their activity; people with similar 
interests in the expression or teaching of competitive practices. 
 
ASC 
A strong personal focus on the need to communicate with people who have a decidedly 
angry approach or demeanor; attitudes which show others that you can be limber, shrewd, 
or practiced in the use of your strength or might. 
 
MC 
Fulfilling personal goals to address life’s problems more directly and completely; 
exerting control over others thru the speeches you give; striving to achieve completion 
and competence despite earlier incomplete actions.
