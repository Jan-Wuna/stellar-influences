 
 
Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets 
184
 
MERCURY/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points 
 
 
Sun 
The prominence and renown you achieve thru hard work and your personal will and 
determination to develop into a better person; the confidence you  build in yourself as 
you have matured; a positive picture of self. 
 
Moon 
The uncertainty and vacillation that creeps into your assessment of how life treats you; 
using intuition and impressions of life rather than study, work, and research to grow as a 
person; inconsistent thoughts on maturing. 
 
Ven 
Developing a softness to your personality as you mature; a satisfaction about the person 
you are becoming; helps give self-contentment with your stature and recognition; an 
added deceitful trend creeps into your aims in life. 
 
Mars 
Added energy and activity for the efforts you go thru to mature and develop as a person; 
makes it important for you to work out anger accumulated from situations or people; 
courage to progress toward and work for goals. 
 
Jup 
Adds a successful quality to your writing and speeches and helps you to gain recognition 
for your work; helps you understand and forgive others for deeds done; publicity and 
recognition for work accomplished or completed. 
 
Sat 
Brings a sternness or sense of austerity to your demeanor and self-hood; the sense of 
obligation to work hard and become someone important in life is very real; commitments 
you make to self about completing and finishing. 
 
Ura 
Manipulating position and friends to gain respect and regard for personal attainments; 
sudden or unpredictable events which affect your stature or standing; unique ways to 
show your personal maturity and individuality. 
 
Nep 
Allows personal or self-deceptive practices or habits to confuse your judgment about 
situations or people; a laxity in the attention you give to projects; using education or 
research aptitudes in a very mature way. 
 
Plu 
Urges hard work to correct defects in your personal attitudes; the extremes you go thru to 
reconcile and integrate your principles of work and life; the personal intensity you bring 
toward gaining any recognition due you. 
 
Nod 
Associations with different people who can help you to gain stature and recognition in 
life; the kinship you feel with members of your family; paths which open and allow you 
to make the most of opportunities in life. 
 
ASC 
Observations you make about people and how these help you grow or mature; 
characteristics of personality you develop thru your education, thinking, or writing which 
help you to advance and grow as a person.
