176
 
MERCURY/NODE with Planets and Points 
 
 
Sun 
Devising effective and competent means of communicating to others; influencing groups 
thru communications or the effectiveness of your message; the trust others give to 
adequately prepare and present knowledge. 
 
Moon 
Sensitivity on how information must be presented to those who are able to utilize your 
ideas; a tradition of using associates to research or pass on information and 
communication; locating people to gather information. 
 
Ven 
The rapport you develop thru meetings with people who have interests in banking or the 
arts; appreciation for how friends or allies can help communicate your messages; 
communicating messages of beauty to friends. 
 
Mars 
Spying for secrets by close groups of allied people; struggles to effectively relate ideas to 
those who may be receptive to them; movement while speaking or lecturing; sending 
information to many different people. 
 
Jup 
Reasoning about the ethics of group communications; exhibiting communication 
equipment designed for enhancing exchanges among religious or philosophical 
coalitions; advertising, marketing, or promotional successes. 
 
Sat 
Disciplined thoughts on effectively getting your message across to the bulk of the people; 
the lasting bonds and ties that you have developed thru groups of friends; ties to 
conservative organizations; fated talks on destiny. 
 
Ura 
Manipulating allies to carry your messages to others who need to listen to them; 
indifference to groups who work to communicate your ideas; disruptions or upsetting 
events at sessions where you are lecturing. 
 
Nep 
A weakening of the ties you have established with others thru a lack of proper 
communications; visions of how to improve your communications potentials when 
meeting with others; clever deceptions when passing data. 
 
Plu 
Suspicions about the leakages of data thru spies; the removal of massive amounts of data 
thru groups of closely working companions; drastic measures taken to present your ideas 
to acquaintances; provocative data. 
 
ASC 
Creating conditions where the exchange of data and information is facilitated; 
concentrating on the need for communicating to and thru others; reactions to how 
information is transmitted to or received by groups. 
 
MC 
Development of an understanding of how those who share similar interests are able to 
assist you in enhancing your prestige; the use of groups who can help you grow in stature 
and reputation; your intentions for giving data.
