 
 
Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets 
300
 
SATURN/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points 
 
 
Sun 
Increased need for approval; developing your personality so that you appear friendly, but 
reserved; your need to dominate others is increased, as is your need for identification 
with the real you; effective business practices. 
 
Moon 
One of your parents preferred to remain in the background and taught you a lesson in 
self-sufficiency; your care for others is heightened, although you are reluctant to act 
spontaneously and steal attention from others near you. 
 
Mer 
Developing a shrewd mind and becoming a better analyst; increased ability to correlate 
facts and data; you deal with numbers easily, and can mentally compute or add well; 
increased ability to be articulate with facts or numbers. 
 
Ven 
Enhances the enjoyment received from being in your own mental space; the pleasure 
derived from listening to music or enjoying art by yourself; building appreciation within 
yourself about your abilities and skills. 
 
Mars 
You vacillate between wanting to restrain your impulses and being physically active; you 
have great patience with certain things, and no patience with other activities; you have 
much energy, but prefer to be by yourself. 
 
Jup 
Causes you to inflate perceptions of yourself easily, but are still reticent about sharing 
your views; a silent ego; your comprehension of large amounts of facts and data, 
especially about people or places, is enhanced. 
 
Ura 
You often surprise people with your astute perceptions because you seem to be quiet and 
introspective; you prefer to study others silently, and may use computers, applying 
astrological or statistical methods of analysis to people. 
 
Nep 
Developing an understanding about that side of life which is not easily explained by the 
physical sciences; you spend much time inside of yourself dreaming about what the 
world should be like, as opposed to what it is like. 
 
Plu 
You have a need to accumulate and control data and facts about perceptions of others; 
you easily dismiss and discard information about how life works which does not fit the 
views of the world which you have formulated. 
 
Nod 
You meet others who share the same type of serious need for introspection and analysis 
which you have; you join groups of people who have interests similar to yours; you find 
friends who understand your silence and study. 
 
MC 
The acclaim you receive due to the isolated analysis you performed; a slow start in life 
that included many restrictions; career goals that are achieved very slowly, but steadily; 
personal guarantees that you keep and fulfill.
