SATURN/PLUTO with Planets and Points 
 
 
Sun 
Dominating others thru a strong will and the determination to be better thru competent 
application; leadership based upon effectiveness of plans and thoroughness of 
preparation; important denials of extremes. 
 
Moon 
Identifying and controlling emotions as you push toward goals and objectives; an 
inconsistency in your work, achievements, or records; strong urges to work with women 
who have power; strong control over people’s habits. 
 
Mer 
Bestows a mental shrewdness toward efforts to meet your goals; communicating 
intentions to proceed with dangerous or difficult tasks; adds a curiosity which spurs you 
to research items further; difficult or long studies. 
 
Ven 
Softening excessive restrictions; delays in the destruction of things which have value or 
desired beauty; deriving pleasure from planning or organizing your work; a deep love of 
certain art forms or traditional music. 
 
Mars 
Activating plans for much destruction or upheaval; energy to continue restraining 
activities which contaminate, adulterate, or otherwise poison; a need to use whatever 
means or power is available to continue working. 
 
Jup 
Portends a favorable outcome for the use of large expenditures of resources or energies; 
influences those who have leadership and power to dispense; opinions about how to 
reclaim the use of waste products for other activities. 
 
Ura 
Sudden changes or upsets in plans which have been made concerning very important 
activities; reforms in the way punishment is given for important crimes; the erratic or 
uneven use of retaliatory measures available to you. 
 
Nep 
Defects in the planning for large and important activities; improbable effects from 
excessive activities of the past; neglect of important methods for preserving or caring for 
waste treatment processes; disguised uses of power. 
 
Nod 
Associating with or meeting those who plan and work hard before they proceed with 
important work; identifying paths to those who have power or influence to wield or sell; 
gives a destiny about holding or abusing power. 
 
ASC 
Concentration upon important matters which lie before you; assessments of how to work 
with those who have power or influence; an environment which gives you the impression 
of silent power or continuing influence. 
 
MC 
Brings knowledge of how to acquire, use, and wield the types of power which can make 
significant changes to society; the acknowledgement from others that you have such 
power or prestige in your field of expertise.
