Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Witte Entry

Irritated and angry women. Debates over women. Quarrels over women. Quarrelling persons.

Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

ABC Entry

Thoughts about a woman's one and a half thoughts, a woman's thoughts about work, a woman's ability to describe, talk about work for the people, talk about work with a woman, think about work with a woman, and work with a young woman

Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

Ebertin Entry

Thinking and acting strongly influenced by feeling, "clearing the air" by means of argument and talking it out. A quarrelsome or nagging woman, quarreling on account of the female sex.

John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

Sandbach Entry

The emotional need to be able to say what one thinks, and to let out anger verbally. The urge to argue or to speak passionately is aroused when one is feeling in secure, or very emotional. Difficult emotions generate nervous energy.

An accumulation of reasons for the use of force; defensive tactics against an usually communicative person; irregular observations of impatience or irritation; loud talk on family or home matters; verbal and active women.
