Witte Entry

The activity in the brain increases by leaps and bounds. To suggest and reject many things. Minor issues or ambiguities that cause excitements. Hours in which one is excited for no reason. Energetic women or energetic people who are condemned to inactivity.

ABC Entry

Excitement by unclear fumes, eventful hour in the water, in anticipation of a people reform, restlessness of an exchanged people, uncertainty of a nervous woman, refined feeling impulses, and yes opinion of a reform in the people

Ebertin Entry

Lack of energy, weakness, muddle-headed or confused aspiration. Nervousness, exhaustion. A sudden disappointment.

Intuitive insights that come to one surprisingly or unexpectedly. To hold to the ideal of people being able to freely share their feelings with each other. Psychic insight into the specific type of nurturing that another person needs.
