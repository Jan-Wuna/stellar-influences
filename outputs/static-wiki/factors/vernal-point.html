Robert Hand - Horoscope Symbols

Hand Factor Entry

The first point of Aries, 0° Aries, the vernal point, or the vernal equinoctial point is, as defined in Chapter 2. where the Sun is at the astronomical beginning of spring, about March 21. It, too, is a form of node: the north node of the ecliptic with respect to the equator. Where the Sun crosses the equatorial plane going north is 0° Aries; the south node is 0° Libra. As the beginning of the tropical zodiac, 0° Aides has been used since the ancient Greeks and the dawn of modern horoscopic astrology. Whereas in most astrological systems it has been treated only as the beginning of the sign Aries, however, it was given a new role in the 19203 and 1930s in the Uranian system of Alfred Witte and his followers. According to Uranian usage, 0° Aries represents 0° of all the other

cardinal signs (Cancer, libra, and Capricorn) as well. The reasons for this wll become clear in Chapter 8. I have found the Vernal Point extremely useful. Although it is not an energy source like a true planet, the Vernal Point is treated like a planet in that it receives and gives aspects in the normal manner. Most important, it can be the focus of midpoint combinations (see Chapter 8). According to the Uranian school, the Vernal Point, Moon's nodes, and Ascendant represent a hierarchy of relationships. Whereas the Uranians say that the Ascendant-Descendant axis relates to general social interactions, and the lunar nodes to more intimate kinds of meetings, my o\vn experience indicates that the Ascendant-Descendant axis signifies the more intimate contacts. I do agree, however, that the lunar nodes often signify relationships that come about because the individuals feel they have something in common, a feeling that has its roots in lunar symbolism. Of the three nodal points of relationship, the Vernal Point represents the most impersonal but also the oddest social contacts: one's relationship to the larger world around one. Hence it is associated with fame and greater social significance. If one ever becomes famous or makes a significant impact on society outside one's circle of friends and associates, it is through symbols that relate to the Vernal Point. Nevertheless, a large number of aspects and/or planetary pictures or midpoint combinations to the Vernal Point does not in itself ensure fame. The Vernal Point and the points connected to it only tell what energies are available to one by which to make an impact should one ever wish to try. Involvements concerning the Midheaven and the tenth house are much more likely than an active Vernal Point to indicate that a person ^vill try to become famous. The reason is that an active Midheaven signifies people who have a strong ideal of significance that they must attain. In this respect, the Midheaven is strongly connected to the father complex of symbols, which includes the Sun, Saturn, Capricorn, the tenth house, and, to some extent, Jupiter. Not only should the Vernal Point itself be checked, but also its midpoints ^vdth the personal points: the Midheaven, Ascendant, Sun, and Moon.

Derived Synthesis

  • Vernal Point is the factor of the general public, public life, the world, and the collective field in which a person, event, or influence becomes visible beyond private relationships.
  • Chart expression: publicity, social reach, wider relevance, collective atmosphere, the surrounding world, and the ability to register in public consciousness or move through broad human networks.
  • Its liabilities are superficiality, depersonalized contact, exposure without real rootedness, dependence on public response, and becoming lost in mass currents, fashion, or collective uncertainty.
