## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Reference: [Rules for Planetary Pictures](../sources/alfred-witte-ludwig-rudolph-hermann-lefeldt-rules-for-planetary-pictures.md), one-based PDF p. 310 (entry start).

The feeling of satisfaction. Contentment. Satiety. To be saturated. Stagnation or standstill in trade, handicraft, commerce, industry or science. Science in a small circle, for only a few. Exclusive scientific circle. The few, the small circle. The exclusive circle.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Satisfaction or saturation alongside activity confined to a small circle.
- psychology: Contentment and the feeling of having enough.
- body/health: Satiety is named; the entry gives no specific medical condition.
- social/relationship: Few people and exclusive circles, especially in science.
- events/manifestations: Standstill in trade, handicraft, commerce, industry, or science.
- conflicts/notes: These meanings come from one [Witte entry](#witte-entry); its PDF locator marks the entry start, not a printed page number.

## Contradictions

The single entry places contentment beside commercial or scientific stagnation. These are different expressions of saturation and limited scope, not an established contradiction between sources.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the repeated Admetos pair through satisfaction, saturation, and a narrow range of exchange. A person may feel content with what is available, while scientific work or commerce remains within a few people or an exclusive circle. The same bounded scope can therefore appear as having enough or as a standstill; the entry does not establish that one must develop into the other. This interpretation is limited to the [Witte entry](#witte-entry).
