## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Reference: [Rules for Planetary Pictures](../sources/alfred-witte-ludwig-rudolph-hermann-lefeldt-rules-for-planetary-pictures.md), one-based PDF p. 310 (entry start).

Scantness. Frugality. Great and deep sadness. Pessimism. Coldness. Aversion. Lack of reliability and firmness. Negligence. To stunt or shrivel. Attrition. Worn out materials. Lack of raw materials. Raw materials from old materials or junk. Prehistoric time. The distant or remote past.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Scant resources, attrition, and what remains from the past.
- psychology: Deep sadness, pessimism, coldness, and aversion.
- body/health: Stunting or shriveling is named without a specific medical explanation.
- social/relationship: Negligence and a lack of reliability or firmness.
- events/manifestations: Worn materials, shortages of raw materials, reclaiming material from junk, and the remote past.
- conflicts/notes: Frugality and material depletion should not be reduced to one moral judgment. Only the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) is present.

## Contradictions

Frugality, shortage, and reuse of old material are distinct circumstances, not mutually exclusive claims. The single source supplies no comparative contradiction test.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Hades`, Hades specifies the repeated Admetos pair through scarcity, wear, and remnants. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) brings scantness and frugality together with worn-out materials, shortages, and obtaining raw material from junk; it also extends this backward-looking range to prehistoric or remote times. Its emotional register is deep sadness, pessimism, coldness, and aversion. These are related images of reduction and the past, rather than evidence that every act of thrift entails sorrow or neglect.
