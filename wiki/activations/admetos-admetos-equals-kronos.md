## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Reference: [Rules for Planetary Pictures](../sources/alfred-witte-ludwig-rudolph-hermann-lefeldt-rules-for-planetary-pictures.md), one-based PDF p. 310 (entry start).

Main formula for an odd character. Differences. Odd being. Fall of a ruler or a government. Differences in altitude. Hills. Mountains. Odd character who leads a secluded life. Great depth, cave or cleft. A life apart from others. Fall from great height.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Being markedly apart, alongside differences of height and depth.
- psychology: The source's "odd character" who leads a secluded life.
- body/health: A fall from great height is named; no medical diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Seclusion and a life apart from others.
- events/manifestations: The fall of a ruler or government, hills and mountains, and deep caves or clefts.
- conflicts/notes: Personal difference, political downfall, and physical altitude remain separate applications of the [Witte entry](#witte-entry).

## Contradictions

Height and depth are paired spatial extremes, not contradictory predictions. The entry does not state that a secluded person must be a ruler or suffer a fall.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the repeated Admetos pair through pronounced difference and being apart. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a secluded, unusual person, then gives physical images of altitude and depth: mountains, caves, clefts, and a fall from height. It separately names the fall of a ruler or government. The connecting idea is an exceptional or separated position, but the text does not establish a causal story in which superiority leads to exile or personal isolation causes political downfall.
