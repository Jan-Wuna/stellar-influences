## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Mars`, Mars specifies the repeated Admetos pair through activity confined to special work or constrained by severe hindrance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins specialization with decreasing labor, a sinking market, and the narrowing or ending of action. Its stronger expressions are suppression, terror, and oppression to immobility; its material setting includes rocks, debris, and work in ruins. This range supports restricted effort and harsh working conditions, without promising endurance, technical skill, or recovery.
