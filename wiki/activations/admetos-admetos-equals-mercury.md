## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Reference: [Rules for Planetary Pictures](../sources/alfred-witte-ludwig-rudolph-hermann-lefeldt-rules-for-planetary-pictures.md), one-based PDF p. 309 (entry start).

Serious or Limited thinking. Perseverance in thinking. Profound thinker. Profound concentration of thoughts. Thoughts, news or information of death, grief, standstill, hindrance. Pessimist. End of a motion. Negotiations which are a dead issue. Ponderous, slow and sluggish.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sustained, profound thought or thinking and communication brought to a standstill.
- psychology: Serious or limited thinking, concentration, perseverance, and pessimism.
- body/health: News or thoughts of death and grief are named, without a bodily diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Negotiations that have become a dead issue.
- events/manifestations: Information about hindrance or standstill; an end of motion and sluggish progress.
- conflicts/notes: The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supplies both depth and limitation, without saying one must cause the other.

## Contradictions

Profound concentration and limited or pessimistic thinking are different possibilities within one entry. No independent source comparison is available on this page.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the repeated Admetos pair through thought, information, and negotiation that linger or stop. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes profound concentration and perseverance in thinking, but also serious or restricted thought, pessimism, and ponderous slowness. News may concern death, grief, or hindrance, while negotiations can become a dead issue. The shared feature is persistence or immobility in mental and communicative activity; the entry does not guarantee that slow thought is either insightful or unproductive.
