## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Reference: [Rules for Planetary Pictures](../sources/alfred-witte-ludwig-rudolph-hermann-lefeldt-rules-for-planetary-pictures.md), one-based PDF p. 309 (entry start).

Stillness. Inner contemplation. Narrow-mindedness. Narrow conditions. The closed or locked room. Excluded from the public. Dying nation. Stoic character. Death of a female person. Hour of death. Change in the state of aggregation. Consolidation, freezing, thawing or softening.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Stillness and enclosed conditions, with a separate physical theme of changes in material state.
- psychology: Inner contemplation, stoicism, and narrow-mindedness.
- body/health: Death of a female person and the hour of death are historical source claims, not reliable medical or timing findings.
- social/relationship: Exclusion from the public; the source also uses the collective image of a dying nation.
- events/manifestations: A closed or locked room; consolidation, freezing, thawing, or softening.
- conflicts/notes: Material changes should not automatically become emotional metaphors in this single [Witte entry](#witte-entry).

## Contradictions

Freezing and thawing are opposite changes of state included in one range. Contemplation and exclusion also need not coincide; no second source is present to test agreement.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Moon`, Moon specifies the repeated Admetos pair through stillness, inward contemplation, and closed or narrow conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ranges from a stoic character to exclusion from public life or a locked room. It also names literal changes of material state, including consolidation, freezing, thawing, and softening; these should remain distinct from unsupported claims of emotional hardening. The entry's gendered death wording and "hour of death" are preserved as historical attributions, not individualized predictions.
