#### Witte Entry

Reference: [Rules for Planetary Pictures](../sources/alfred-witte-ludwig-rudolph-hermann-lefeldt-rules-for-planetary-pictures.md), one-based PDF p. 310 (entry start).

The foundation. To go into the depth. To return to the basic origin. Basic changes. Deeply rooted growths (development). Roots. Radical transformations. Deep reaching transformation. Change from the bottom up. From raw material to finished products. From the beginning to the end. The beginning of the end.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development and transformation reaching foundations or origins.
- psychology: Returning to a basic origin is named, without an explicit personality description.
- body/health: No medical meaning is supplied; "growths" is qualified as development.
- social/relationship: No explicit relationship meaning is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Radical changes from the bottom up, roots, and processing raw material into finished products.
- conflicts/notes: Beginning-to-end language in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) does not establish destruction, fate, or eventual rebuilding.

## Contradictions

Origins, development, and the beginning of an end describe positions in a process. The single entry does not make them mutually exclusive or establish a disagreement with another source.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the repeated Admetos pair through change at the foundation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links roots and basic origins with deeply reaching development, bottom-up transformation, and the passage from raw material to finished products. Its beginning-to-end range includes the beginning of an end, but supplies no requirement that transformation be destructive or followed by recovery. The emphasis is the depth and extent of change, rather than a particular emotional reaction to it.
