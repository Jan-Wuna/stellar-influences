Culture. Education. Mental depth. Fine, subtle material. The atom. Radiation. Vibration. Dematerialization. Spirit and matter. The two sides of the same entity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental depth alongside the source's pairing of spirit and subtle matter.
- psychology: Mental depth is explicit; withdrawal or loss of warmth is not.
- body/health: Atom, radiation, and vibration are named without a medical claim.
- social/relationship: Culture and education appear, without a specific interpersonal pattern.

- conflicts/notes: The physical and spiritual language in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) is doctrine, not evidence establishing a scientific mechanism.

## Contradictions

Spirit and matter are explicitly presented as two sides of one entity. That conceptual pairing is not an internal contradiction, and no second source is present for comparison.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the repeated Admetos pair through education, culture, and mental depth, alongside a concern with subtle substance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places the atom, radiation, vibration, and dematerialization beside spirit and matter as two sides of one entity. The bounded throughline is inquiry into depth and the relation of material and spiritual concepts. The entry does not support an added story of isolation, emotional coldness, or impaired ordinary life.
