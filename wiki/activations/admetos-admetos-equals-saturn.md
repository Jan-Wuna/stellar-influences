## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the repeated Admetos pair through prolonged restriction and the patience it demands. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects bottlenecks, stoppage, and being compelled to wait with forbearance and trials of patience. It also includes overcoming a tight spot, so the range permits passage through difficulty without promising that every delay ends that way. Coldness, stiffness, dryness, and depression remain attributed terms, rather than a medical conclusion.
