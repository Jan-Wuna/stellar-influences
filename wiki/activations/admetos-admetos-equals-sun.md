## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Reference: [Rules for Planetary Pictures](../sources/alfred-witte-ludwig-rudolph-hermann-lefeldt-rules-for-planetary-pictures.md), one-based PDF p. 309 (entry start).

Perseverance. Heaviness. Weight. Objects. Ground. Soil. Real estate. Landscape. Homeland. That which is conservative. An object of raw material. An object under pressure. Body without motion. Body in repose. Standstill of the body. Day of death. Landed property.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Perseverance, weight, and material or bodily immobility.
- psychology: Perseverance and conservatism are named; a broader identity theory is not supplied.
- body/health: A body in repose or without motion, and "day of death," are historical source claims, not individual medical or timing findings.
- social/relationship: Homeland is included without an explicit relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: Ground, soil, landscape, real estate, landed property, and raw material under pressure.
- conflicts/notes: The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) contains both ordinary material settings and death language; they should not be treated as equivalent outcomes.

## Contradictions

Property, perseverance, and bodily rest are different applications within one source. No cross-source agreement or contradiction can be established from this page alone.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Sun`, Sun specifies the repeated Admetos pair through weight, persistence, and a body or object that remains in place. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects this material emphasis to soil, landscape, homeland, real estate, and landed property, as well as raw material under pressure. Perseverance and conservatism form its personal range. Its reference to the day of death remains historical doctrine and does not turn bodily rest or a property theme into a prediction of death.
