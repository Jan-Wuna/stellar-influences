## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden separation by rupture, breakage, or tearing apart.
- psychology: Shock and commotion are named, without a personality description.
- body/health: Accidents and homicide are historical event associations, not individual risk findings.
- social/relationship: Separation through a sudden break.
- events/manifestations: Earthquakes, destructive natural events, cracks, fissures, shreds, and rags.
- conflicts/notes: The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) does not establish a preceding deadlock, pressure buildup, or beneficial release.

## Contradictions

The entry consistently concerns rupture across different settings. Its range from torn material to destructive events is variation of scale, not evidence that every break entails catastrophe.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the repeated Admetos pair through abrupt rupture and separation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) repeats breaking off, breaking up, breaking down, and tearing, with cracks, fissures, and shreds as material expressions. Earthquakes, accidents, and homicide are its more severe historical associations. The entry establishes sudden breakage as the throughline; it does not explain that breakage as a release from earlier stagnation or make a personal forecast of disaster.
