## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Reference: [Rules for Planetary Pictures](../sources/alfred-witte-ludwig-rudolph-hermann-lefeldt-rules-for-planetary-pictures.md), one-based PDF p. 309 (entry start).

Connected in love and friendship with a few. Picky, but true and deep in love and friendship. Unselfish. End of a love. Meditation, turning inwards. Death of female persons. Repressed affections. Frigidity in love. Heartlessness. Death of love. Without sensation. Limited feeling.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affection concentrated in a few deep ties, or affection restricted and ended.
- psychology: Selectiveness, unselfishness, meditation, and turning inward.
- body/health: "Frigidity" and death of female persons are historical, gendered source terms, not diagnoses or predictions.
- social/relationship: True, deep love and friendship with a few; repressed affection, heartlessness, or the end of love.
- events/manifestations: An ending of a love is named without a specified cause or timing.
- conflicts/notes: Limited feeling and fidelity both belong to the [Witte entry](#witte-entry); one must not erase the other.

## Contradictions

Deep fidelity and restricted affection are contrasting possibilities in the same entry. It supplies no rule selecting between them and no evidence that one must turn into the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Venus`, Venus specifies the repeated Admetos pair through the concentration or restriction of affection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes being selective yet true, deep, and unselfish in love and friendship with a few people. It also allows inward meditation, repressed feeling, heartlessness, and the ending of love. The limited range of attachment can therefore mean depth or diminished responsiveness; the entry does not settle which applies. Its gendered death language and term "frigidity" remain historical attributions.
