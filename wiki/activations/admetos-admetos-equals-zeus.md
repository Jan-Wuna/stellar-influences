## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Reference: [Rules for Planetary Pictures](../sources/alfred-witte-ludwig-rudolph-hermann-lefeldt-rules-for-planetary-pictures.md), one-based PDF p. 310 (entry start).

First beginning, the act of originating, genesis. All beginning is difficult. To form, to work on, to shape, to form raw materials. Tragedy. Lava, igneous rock. To tackle a problem. Struggling activity. To battle all the way through. Interior of the earth. Raw materials from mines, ore. To process or refining raw materials.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Admetos = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the repeated Admetos pair through originating and forming something under difficulty. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects tackling a problem and struggling through activity with shaping, processing, and refining raw material. Mines, ore, lava, and igneous rock give this theme a concrete material range. The source also names tragedy, but does not make it inevitable or promise that every struggle ends in a usable product.
