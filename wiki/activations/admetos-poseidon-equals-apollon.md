## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312 (entry start).

Educated and trained with many. Satisfactory state of education or training.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 477 (PDF p. 237).

Restricted knowledge of truth, restriction of intellectual freedom, successful consolidation of truth, experiencing thorough clarity, thorough knowledge of truth, increased intellectual suppression, and deepening cultural knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Education and cultural understanding may be consolidated or restricted.
- psychology: Satisfaction with education and the experience of clarity.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Training with many people; restriction of intellectual freedom.
- events/manifestations: A satisfactory state of training, deepening cultural knowledge, or increased intellectual suppression.
- conflicts/notes: The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes satisfactory education; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) retains both clearer knowledge and restriction.

## Contradictions

Satisfactory training and restricted intellectual freedom can coexist. The entries differ in range and emphasis without making mutually exclusive claims; they do not guarantee either success or suppression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through education shared with many and the consolidation of understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a satisfactory state of education or training, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds thorough clarity and deepening cultural knowledge. That intellectual range also includes restricted knowledge, limited freedom, and suppression. The common theme is the formation and scope of understanding; satisfactory training alone does not settle whether intellectual freedom is preserved.
