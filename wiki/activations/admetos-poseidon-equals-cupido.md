## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312 (entry start).

Community’s educational ideals. One’s individual educational ideals. Private teacher. Dissolution of an educational association.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 477 (PDF p. 237).

Shared consolidation of truth, shared intellectual deepening, an intellectually restricted community, intellectual origination of art, thorough clarity through synthesis, suppression of an intellectual community, and deepening involvement in art and culture.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared educational ideals and communal intellectual or cultural formation.
- psychology: Individual ideals and clarity achieved through synthesis.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Educational associations and intellectual communities, including their restriction, suppression, or dissolution.
- events/manifestations: Private teaching, shared pursuit of truth, and deepening involvement in art and culture.
- conflicts/notes: The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes private teaching and dissolved associations; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also preserves constructive shared understanding.

## Contradictions

Communal deepening and a community's suppression or dissolution are alternative conditions, not mutually exclusive doctrines. The entries give no sequence connecting private teaching to dissolution.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through educational ideals and shared intellectual or artistic life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes both a community's and an individual's ideals, private teaching, and the dissolution of an educational association. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds shared consolidation of truth, clarity through synthesis, and deepening art or culture, alongside restricted or suppressed communities. The unifying concern is how understanding is formed together, with both flourishing and interrupted association left possible.
