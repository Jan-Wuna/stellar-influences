## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312 (entry start).

Defects in education and training.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 477 (PDF p. 237).

Consolidation of hidden truth, dangerous suppression of truth, intellectual manifestation of the past, intellectual deepening into the past, criminal suppression of truth, sorrow through intellectual limitation, and lack of intellectual depth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Deficient education, limited understanding, and hidden or suppressed truth.
- psychology: Sorrow through intellectual restriction and a lack of depth.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Dangerous or criminal suppression of truth is a source association, not an allegation about a person.
- events/manifestations: Defects in training; bringing hidden truth or the past into intellectual focus.
- conflicts/notes: Deepening into the past is also present in the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), so its range is broader than the [Witte entry's](#witte-entry) defects alone.

## Contradictions

Depth directed toward the past and a lack of depth are contrasting possibilities within ABC, not a demonstrated disagreement with Witte's brief statement about defective education.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through shortcomings or restrictions in understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives defects in education and training; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds sorrow through intellectual limitation, lack of depth, and dangerous or criminal suppression of truth. It also preserves a different expression: consolidating hidden truth and deepening engagement with the past. The page therefore concerns both deficient formation and concealed knowledge, without turning its criminal wording into a personal accusation or prescribing a diagnosis.
