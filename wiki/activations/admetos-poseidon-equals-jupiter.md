## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312 (entry start).

Joy, happiness and success through education or training.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 476 (PDF p. 237).

Limitation of financial ideas, restricted intellectual knowledge, successful consolidation of truth, successful intellectual deepening, joy over fundamental truth, fundamental legal clarity, and reasonable financial limitation

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Satisfaction or success through education and consolidated understanding, with intellectual or financial limits also possible.
- psychology: Joy in truth and successful intellectual deepening.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a medical meaning.
- social/relationship: No specific relationship pattern is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Educational success, legal clarity, restricted financial ideas, and reasonable financial limitation.
- conflicts/notes: Financial restraint in the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) does not negate the [Witte entry's](#witte-entry) happiness through learning or prove financial gain.

## Contradictions

Successful education and restricted knowledge or finances can coexist. ABC's range adds limits without directly denying Witte's statement; neither supplies an outcome-selection rule.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through joy and success associated with education or deeper understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) directly links happiness and success to training; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful consolidation of truth, intellectual deepening, and legal clarity. Its financial and intellectual restrictions remain part of the same range, including reasonable financial limitation. Reward through learning is supported, while wealth, credential-based overconfidence, or a guaranteed successful outcome are not established.
