## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312 (entry start).

To be subjected to a special education or training. Specially gifted as trainer or teacher.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 477 (PDF p. 237).

Special intellectual depth, blocked culture in the state, firmness through an intellectual hierarchy, intellectual blockage by the state, suppression of intellectual authority, deeper engagement with the intellectual hierarchy, and deepening important insights.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Specialized education or teaching, with intellectual depth and institutional authority.
- psychology: Special depth and important insights; giftedness as a teacher or trainer.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Intellectual hierarchy and authority, including authority suppressed by the state.
- events/manifestations: Special training, cultural blockage in the state, and deeper engagement with intellectual institutions.
- conflicts/notes: The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) concerns special training and teaching talent; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds both firmness through hierarchy and state-imposed restriction.

## Contradictions

Hierarchy can supply firmness in one expression and impose blockage in another. These possibilities do not establish that gifted teaching requires either state control or superiority over others.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair through special education, teaching talent, and the institutions that organize intellectual life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) allows either receiving special training or being specially gifted to teach. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds depth and important insights, firmness through intellectual hierarchy, and a restrictive range of state blockage or suppressed authority. The shared concern is specialized formation in relation to authority, without a claim that hierarchy must be beneficial or oppressive.
