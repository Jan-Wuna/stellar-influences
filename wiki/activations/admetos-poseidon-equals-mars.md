## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312 (entry start).

Courses of instruction with only a few participants, to be part of it, active or passive. Activity connected with education, training.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 476 (PDF p. 237).

Active suppression of truth, deepening knowledge about work, limited cultural activity, restricted intellectual activity, consolidation of intellectual work, creating thorough clarity, thorough cultural creation, and deeper engagement in intellectual activity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Educational or intellectual work conducted within a limited scope.
- psychology: Deepening work-related understanding and creating clarity.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Participation in instruction with only a few people, whether active or passive.
- events/manifestations: Educational activity, consolidated intellectual work, limited cultural activity, or active suppression of truth.
- conflicts/notes: The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) explicitly includes suppression, not support, of truth; this must remain beside its constructive work meanings and the [Witte entry](#witte-entry).

## Contradictions

Creating clarity and suppressing truth are contrasting alternatives within ABC. The page gives no conditions selecting between them; small-group instruction itself does not establish suppression.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through activity devoted to education or intellectual work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes instruction with few participants and either active or passive involvement. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds deepened work knowledge, consolidated intellectual effort, and the creation of clarity, while also retaining restricted activity and active suppression of truth. The connecting theme is work on understanding within limits; neither mechanical drilling nor a necessary failure of insight follows from the entries.
