## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312 (entry start).

One’s own culture and education. To be cultured, educated, trained. To educate or be educated.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 476 (PDF p. 237).

Blocked spirit-soul, the end of my power, consolidation of my power, thorough influence of the soul, my enduring influence, my blocked circulation, my limited reason, my energy and endurance, my blocked energy, and deepening my influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: One's own education and the consolidation or restriction of personal influence.
- psychology: Limited reason, energy, endurance, and a blocked inner life in the source's spirit-soul language.
- body/health: ABC names blocked circulation; it supplies no diagnosis or medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: Educating or being educated; exerting an enduring or deepened influence.
- events/manifestations: Personal culture and training; power consolidated or ended, and energy blocked.
- conflicts/notes: The [ABC entry's](#abc-entry) first-person language concerns the person's own influence, not necessarily vocation; the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) establishes personal education.

## Contradictions

Consolidation and the end of power, like energy and blocked energy, are alternatives within ABC. They do not negate Witte's educational meaning or describe a necessary sequence.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = MC`, MC specifies the pair through one's own education and intellectual formation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes being cultured or trained and either educating or receiving education. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the personal emphasis to influence, power, energy, and endurance, each capable of consolidation or blockage. Its spirit-soul and circulation language remains attributed to that entry. The supported theme is personal formation and its capacity or limits of influence, without an added theory of vocation or dependence on educational status.
