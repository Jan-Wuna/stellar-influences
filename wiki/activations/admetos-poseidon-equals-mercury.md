## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312 (entry start).

Thoughts about education and training. Thoughts on fine, or non-material matters. One’s ideas are rejected.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 476 (PDF p. 237).

Blocked traffic ideas, depressing cultural news, restriction of truth by the media, intellectual deepening in youth, conversations about intellectual suppression, thorough insights in youth, and thorough clarity of thought.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought and communication concerning education, culture, and restricted or clarified understanding.
- psychology: Reflection on fine or non-material matters; clarity and deeper insight in youth.
- body/health: No medical meaning is supplied; depressing news is a description of news.
- social/relationship: Rejected ideas and conversations about intellectual suppression.
- events/manifestations: Educational thought, cultural news, media restrictions on truth, and blocked traffic ideas.
- conflicts/notes: The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) does not explain why ideas are rejected; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds media and youth settings without proving abstract thought causes rejection.

## Contradictions

Clear thought and rejected ideas can coexist. The entries also include depressing news and restricted communication; these are distinct emphases, not a demonstrated contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through ideas, news, and conversation about education or less material concerns. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names thoughts on training and fine or non-material matters, together with rejected ideas. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds intellectual deepening and clarity in youth, cultural news, and discussion of intellectual suppression or media restrictions on truth. The range includes both insight and restricted reception or communication; it does not explain rejected ideas as inherently sterile or too abstract.
