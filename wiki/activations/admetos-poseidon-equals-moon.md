## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312 (entry start).

To be under a certain influence as a medium or unconsciously, to be induced. A nation or people subjected to the influence of a certain upbringing, education or training. The same is valid for women and the general public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 476 (PDF p. 237).

Intellectual restriction of a woman, intellectual depth of a woman, intellectual suppression of a people, thorough clarity of feeling, an hour of intellectual deepening, deeper engagement with a people's culture, suppression of a cultural people, and reasonable restraint of feelings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Educational or cultural influence on people, with depth or restriction of understanding and feeling.
- psychology: Unconscious influence or inducement; clarity and reasonable restraint of feelings.
- body/health: Neither entry establishes a medical diagnosis; mediumistic influence is historical source language.
- social/relationship: Upbringing and education of a people or public; gender-specific claims remain attributed.
- events/manifestations: Cultural deepening, an hour of intellectual deepening, or intellectual suppression of a people.
- conflicts/notes: The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry) use historical gender and collective categories; these do not establish inherent traits of women or nations.

## Contradictions

Intellectual depth and intellectual restriction are alternative possibilities. Educational influence, cultural deepening, and suppression are different qualities of formation, without a rule selecting one for a person or group.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through receptivity to upbringing, education, and cultural influence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes unconscious or mediumistic influence and the formation of a people or public through training. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds intellectual and cultural deepening, clarity of feeling, and reasonable emotional restraint, while also naming intellectual suppression. Its gendered statements remain historical attributions. Together the entries concern how understanding and feeling are formed, without establishing an inherent lack of discrimination in any person or group.
