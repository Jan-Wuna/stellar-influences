## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312 (entry start).

Educational courses in which nothing is accomplished. Wasted education and training.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 477 (PDF p. 237).

Depression through spiritual weakness, spiritual origination with sensitivity, clear water from the depths, consolidation of metaphysical truth, creeping suppression of truth, uncertainty through suppression of truth, and refinement through spiritual depth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Unaccomplished education alongside uncertain, suppressed, or refined spiritual understanding.
- psychology: Sensitivity, spiritual depth, and uncertainty through suppressed truth.
- body/health: Depression through spiritual weakness is ABC's historical association, not a diagnosis or medical explanation.
- social/relationship: Neither entry gives a specific relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: Wasted courses, consolidation of metaphysical truth, creeping suppression, and clear water from the depths.
- conflicts/notes: The [ABC entry's](#abc-entry) refinement is broader than the [Witte entry's](#witte-entry) wasted training; neither makes all spiritual study futile.

## Contradictions

Witte's unaccomplished courses and ABC's spiritual refinement emphasize different manifestations. They need not describe the same course or contradict one another; both remain available without being collapsed into one outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through education that may achieve little and spiritual understanding that may remain uncertain or become more refined. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly names wasted courses and training. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds sensitivity, metaphysical truth, and spiritual depth, but also creeping suppression of truth and the uncertainty it causes. Clear water from the depths is a separate concrete image in that entry. The range preserves both futility and refinement, without deciding that spiritual study must yield either.
