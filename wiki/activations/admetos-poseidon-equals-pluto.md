## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312 (entry start).

New ways of educating. Changes in educational conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 477 (PDF p. 237).

Restricted intellectual development, developing intellectual depth, an intellectual cycle of evolution, developing thorough insight, unfolding and deepening clarity, growing restriction of truth, and changing restriction of truth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Changes in education and in the development or restriction of understanding.
- psychology: Developing depth, insight, and clarity.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: No specific interpersonal pattern is supplied.
- events/manifestations: New educational methods, changed educational conditions, and growing or changing restrictions on truth.
- conflicts/notes: The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) allows both intellectual development and its restriction; the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) does not describe educational reform as necessarily coercive.

## Contradictions

Developing clarity and restricted development are contrasting possibilities, not mutually exclusive source doctrines. Changes in educational conditions alone do not establish either progress or coercion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair through changing educational conditions and developing understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names new ways of educating. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives the intellectual process a wider range: depth, insight, and clarity can develop, while intellectual development or truth can also face growing or changing restrictions. The shared theme is change in how understanding forms and what limits it, without a basis for describing every reform as radical, coercive, or beneficial.
