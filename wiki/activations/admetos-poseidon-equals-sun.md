## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312, Sun row.

Man who undergoes training, finishes schooling, becomes apprentice or helper. To go through schooling. The days when one is attending a training course.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 476 (PDF p. 237), Sun row, checked on the scan.

Limited reason of a man, blockage of mind and body, an intellectually established person, an intellectually suppressed person, thorough clarity of a man, a person with intellectual depth, personal intellectual resistance, and a center for intellectual deepening.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal education and intellectual formation, consolidated or constrained.
- psychology: Depth, clarity, resistance, or limited reason in ABC's historical wording.
- body/health: ABC names blockage of mind and body without defining a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Apprenticeship and the position of a pupil or helper.
- events/manifestations: Finishing schooling and attending a training course.
- conflicts/notes: The gendered examples remain source attributions; no loss of personal autonomy is stated.

## Contradictions

Established understanding and intellectual suppression are alternative expressions in ABC. They do not directly contradict the schooling examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through a person undergoing education: schooling, apprenticeship, or attendance at a course in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds the quality of that person's understanding, including depth and clarity alongside limitation or suppression. The common concern is personal intellectual formation; the entries do not establish that being a pupil or helper prevents autonomy.
