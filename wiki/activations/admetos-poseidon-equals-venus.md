## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312, Venus row.

Harmonious education. Balance of soul, mind and body. Harmony as essence of everything. The harmony of fine material things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 476 (PDF p. 237), Venus row, checked on the scan.

Consolidation of love through truth, deepening insights about peace, intellectual suppression of peace, thorough clarity in love, thorough love of truth, deep intellectual harmony, preference for intellectual deepening, and deepening involvement in art and culture.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Harmonious education and deeper clarity in love, truth, art, and culture.
- psychology: Love of truth and preference for deepened understanding.
- body/health: Witte names balance of soul, mind, and body without a medical claim.
- social/relationship: Love consolidated through truth; harmony and peace.
- events/manifestations: Cultural or artistic deepening and harmonious training.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also names suppression of peace; this does not disappear into the harmony theme.

## Contradictions

Harmony and suppression of peace are contrasting expressions on the page. No conditions select one or explain their relationship.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through harmonious education and clarity in love or understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes balance of soul, mind, and body; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds love consolidated through truth and deeper engagement with art and culture. ABC's separate expression of intellectual suppression of peace limits an entirely harmonious reading. Decorative cultivation or personal fragility is not established.
