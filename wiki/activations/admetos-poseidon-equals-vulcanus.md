## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312, Vulcanus row.

Mighty and lasting influence of education. To be affected that way, or to affect others that way.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 477 (PDF p. 237), Vulcanus row, checked on the scan.

Depressive influence of truth, consolidation of the force of an idea, consolidation of intellectual energies, thorough intellectual influence, restricting cultural influence, consolidation of power through truth, powerful suppression of truth, and condensation of intellectual energies.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Lasting educational influence and the consolidation or restriction of intellectual power.
- psychology: The force of ideas and deep influence on understanding.
- body/health: ABC's depressive influence is an attributed association, without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Affecting others through education or being strongly affected by it.
- events/manifestations: Consolidated ideas, restricted culture, and powerful suppression of truth.
- conflicts/notes: Influence may be lasting without necessarily being coercive; suppression remains an explicit possibility.

## Contradictions

Consolidating ideas and suppressing truth describe different uses or conditions of influence; the entries do not reconcile them into one outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through powerful and lasting educational influence, whether received or exerted on others in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends this to the force of ideas and consolidated intellectual energies, including power strengthened through truth. Restricted cultural influence and powerful suppression of truth remain distinct expressions. The entries support strength of influence without making coercion its necessary mechanism.
