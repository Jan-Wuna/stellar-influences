## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 312, Zeus row.

Enforced education and training. Tests, examinations. Trained in the use of arms.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 477 (PDF p. 237), Zeus row, checked on the scan.

Efforts to consolidate truth, enduring pursuit of truth, blocked cultural achievements, consolidation through intellectual leadership, intellectual shaping of raw material, thorough pursuit of intellectual goals, and deepening involvement in intellectual goals.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed educational effort, testing, and persistent pursuit of understanding.
- psychology: Enduring pursuit of truth and intellectual goals.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Enforced training and intellectual leadership.
- events/manifestations: Examinations, arms training, blocked cultural achievements, and shaping raw material intellectually.
- conflicts/notes: Compulsion is explicit in Witte; ABC's sustained effort does not itself imply exhaustion.

## Contradictions

Enforced training and persistent intellectual pursuit concern different aspects of directed formation; no incompatible assertion is established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Poseidon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair through directed formation and sustained intellectual effort. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives enforced education, examinations, and training in arms; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds enduring pursuit of truth, intellectual leadership, and work toward intellectual goals. Blocked cultural achievements remain possible. Testing and compulsion are sourced, while a general theory of performance-driven schooling would go beyond these entries.
