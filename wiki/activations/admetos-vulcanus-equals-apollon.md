## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 311, Apollon row.

To be exposed with many to a blow of fate.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 475 (one-based PDF p. 236), Apollon row.

Limitation of scientific influence, blocked fullness of power, end of power expansion, success through energy and perseverance, successful violence restriction, successful power strengthening, resistance of the masses against violence, and knowledge of the energy circuit.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Collective hardship and limits on power, alongside successful endurance and resistance.
- psychology: Energy and perseverance are named as means of success.
- body/health: ABC names knowledge of the energy circuit without specifying a bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Many people sharing adversity; mass resistance to violence.
- events/manifestations: Restricted scientific influence, ended power expansion, successful restraint of violence, or strengthened power.
- conflicts/notes: Shared misfortune in Witte does not exhaust ABC's successful resistance and consolidation meanings.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis: collective adversity in Witte, and both constrained power and successful endurance in ABC. They give no conditions selecting an outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Vulcanus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through adversity shared by many and the collective limits or consolidation of power. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes exposure with others to a blow of fate. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also allows success through energy and perseverance, restraint of violence, and mass resistance to it. Collective hardship therefore sits beside effective endurance and resistance, rather than implying that every group must be overwhelmed.
