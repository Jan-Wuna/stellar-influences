## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 311, Hades row.

To get into a period of need and privation through hard blows of fate.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 475 (one-based PDF p. 236), Hades row.

Blocking power of the past, deprivation through restricted energy, dangerous restriction of violence, profound sorrow through violence, lack of strength through depression, renunciation of violent suppression, and resistance against criminal violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Privation and diminished strength amid adversity, with resistance to violence also named.
- psychology: The past's blocking influence and profound sorrow through violence.
- body/health: ABC associates depression with lack of strength; this is source doctrine, without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Renouncing violent suppression and resisting criminal violence.
- events/manifestations: A period of need after hard blows of fate; deprivation through restricted energy.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's dangerous restriction of violence is retained as written; the entry does not explain that qualification.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in privation. ABC adds both harmful violence and resistance to it, without describing these as a single necessary sequence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Vulcanus = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through need, privation, and diminished strength. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects a period of want with hard blows of fate; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds restricted energy, the blocking power of the past, and sorrow through violence. ABC also names renouncing violent suppression and resisting criminal violence, preserving a response to adversity alongside the experience of it.
