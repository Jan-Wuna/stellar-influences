## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 311, Kronos row.

To be noted through something which is extraordinary. To draw attention through unusual events and odd or peculiar fateful occurrences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 475 (one-based PDF p. 236), Kronos row.

Restricted state power, end of state power, energy and perseverance of the state, restrictions on violence by the state, consolidation of state power, state resistance to violence, and resistance to the influence of authority.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Exceptional events drawing attention, alongside the endurance or limits of state power.
- psychology: No individual temperament is specified; ABC names state energy and perseverance.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: State authority and resistance to its influence.
- events/manifestations: Unusual fateful occurrences, ended or consolidated state power, and state action restricting violence.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's personal prominence and ABC's state-level meanings remain distinct; unusual attention does not establish suffering or mastery.

## Contradictions

Restriction, consolidation, and the end of state power are alternatives within ABC. Witte's unusual events do not explain which institutional outcome applies.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Vulcanus = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair through unusual events that draw attention in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places endurance and constraint at the level of state power. Authority may be consolidated, restricted, or ended; the state may resist or restrict violence, and its own influence may encounter resistance. These are two retained scales of manifestation, without a claim that public distinction must arise from suffering.
