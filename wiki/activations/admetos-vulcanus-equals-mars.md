## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 311, Mars row.

Stoppage or grave hindrance at work enforced by fate. End of a battle. Surrender.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 474 (one-based PDF p. 236), Mars row.

Blocked capacity for work, depression through violent activity, end of the exercise of power, consolidation of capacity for work, severe restriction of work, profound influence of action, powerful deepening into work, and resistance activity against violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Work and action stopped or heavily restricted, with consolidated capacity and resistance also possible.
- psychology: Deep engagement in work is named; exhaustion is not explicitly described.
- body/health: ABC's depression through violent activity remains an attributed association.
- social/relationship: Surrender and activity resisting violence.
- events/manifestations: Work stoppage, grave hindrance, a battle's end, or strengthened capacity for work.
- conflicts/notes: ABC retains active resistance and consolidation beside Witte's stoppage and surrender.

## Contradictions

Consolidated working capacity and blocked work are contrasting possibilities in ABC. The entries give no sequence requiring effort to end in surrender.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Vulcanus = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through obstruction in work and the limits of action. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names stoppage, grave hindrance, a battle's end, and surrender. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds severe restriction and an end to the exercise of power, but also consolidated working capacity, deep engagement in work, and resistance to violence. The range includes both halted action and strength directed into work or resistance.
