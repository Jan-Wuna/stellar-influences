## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 311, MC row.

Great perseverance. To stay firm without wilting or bending. Hard in taking it. To be harshly treated by fate.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 474 (one-based PDF p. 236), MC row.

End of my power, profound influence of the soul, my blocked circulation, my profound influence, deepening my influence, my energy and perseverance, my energy blockade, my power strengthening, and the suppressed power of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal perseverance and influence, strengthened or blocked under adversity.
- psychology: Staying firm without bending; the source also names suppressed power of the soul.
- body/health: ABC names blocked circulation without specifying a medical condition.
- social/relationship: One's own influence can be profound or deepened; no specific relationship pattern is given.
- events/manifestations: Harsh treatment by fate, ended power, blocked energy, or strengthened power.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's first-person claims concern personal capacity; a life direction or comparison with others' tolerance is not supplied.

## Contradictions

Energy and blocked energy, like strengthened and ended power, are alternative expressions within ABC. They do not negate the perseverance named by Witte.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Vulcanus = MC`, MC specifies the pair through personal endurance and the capacity to sustain influence under pressure. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes perseverance and remaining firm despite harsh treatment. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) retains personal energy and strengthened power alongside blocked energy, ended power, and suppressed inner force. Its circulation and soul language remains attributed to that entry; firmness alone does not establish emotional dryness or a prescribed life direction.
