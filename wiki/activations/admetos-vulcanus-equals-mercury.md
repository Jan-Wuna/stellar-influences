## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 311, Mercury row.

Mighty, deep thinking. Greatest concentration of thoughts. To stick unyielding to one’s convictions. Unperturbed in one’s thoughts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 474 (one-based PDF p. 236), Mercury row.

Reports of violence restriction, influence of depressive thoughts, energy and perseverance of youth, powerful traffic blockade, news of the end of power, strong movement restriction, strong traffic density, and resistance against youth violence

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Concentrated, steadfast thought alongside reports and concrete restrictions on movement.
- psychology: Deep thinking, fixed convictions, unperturbed thought, or depressive thoughts in ABC.
- body/health: Depressive thoughts are an attributed association; no medical condition is established.
- social/relationship: Youthful endurance and resistance to youth violence.
- events/manifestations: News of ended power or restricted violence; traffic blockages, density, and limited movement.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's mental concentration and ABC's communication/traffic meanings remain distinct; resistance to new information is not stated.

## Contradictions

Unperturbed thought and depressive thoughts describe different possibilities. Neither entry establishes that fixed convictions cause the traffic or social events listed by ABC.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Vulcanus = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through concentrated thought and firmly held convictions in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds reports about restricted violence or ended power, together with traffic blockages and restricted movement; it also names youth's endurance and resistance to violence. These preserve mental, communicative, and concrete forms of constraint without making concentration itself a failure to adapt.
