## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 311, Moon row.

The people, women or several persons who are hard hit by fate.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 474 (one-based PDF p. 236), Moon row.

A woman blocked by violence, limitation of influence through a woman, a woman's energy circulation, consolidation of the power of a people, deepening emotional influence, massive restriction on a woman, violent suppression of a people, and resistance to women's violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Adversity, constrained influence, and power involving groups or women in the sources' historical categories.
- psychology: ABC names deepening emotional influence; a permanently burdened emotional life is not stated.
- body/health: A woman's energy circulation is named without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Influence limited through a woman; suppression of a people and resistance to violence.
- events/manifestations: Several people hard hit by fate, consolidated collective power, or severe restriction.
- conflicts/notes: The gendered and collective claims remain attributed doctrine, not inherent traits of women or peoples.

## Contradictions

Consolidated collective power and violent suppression are different possibilities in ABC. Witte's adversity does not select between them or imply permanent suffering.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Vulcanus = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through adversity affecting a people or several persons in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds the restriction or consolidation of collective power, violent suppression, and resistance to violence, alongside deepening emotional influence. Its statements about women remain historical source attributions. Together the entries describe constrained influence and shared adversity without making emotional life permanently dominated by hardship.
