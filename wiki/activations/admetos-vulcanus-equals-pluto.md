## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 311, Pluto row.

By fate-dictated development. Blows of fate which cause basic change, development.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 475 (one-based PDF p. 236), Pluto row, beginning at “Einschränkung von Gewalt-Entwicklungen”.

Restriction of violent developments, end of the unfolding of power, changes in energy through concentration, thorough development of energy, transformation of raw material energy, growing energy and endurance, and changing depressive influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Basic change under adversity and changes in energy or the expression of power.
- psychology: Growing endurance is named; lasting emotional hardness is not stated.
- body/health: ABC's changing depressive influence is an attributed association, without a medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Restriction of violent developments and an end to the unfolding of power.
- events/manifestations: Fateful events causing basic development; concentration, transformation, or growth of energy.
- conflicts/notes: The opening ABC clauses belong to Pluto, not the preceding Neptune row. Development need not be reduced to trauma.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes change through adversity; ABC includes both limits on power and growing energy. The entries give no rule requiring all development to be harmful.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Vulcanus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair through basic change brought by fateful events in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends that developmental range to concentrated or transformed energy and growing endurance, while retaining restriction of violent developments and an end to the unfolding of power. The connecting theme is a change in capacity and conditions; trauma or lasting hardness is not established as its necessary form.
