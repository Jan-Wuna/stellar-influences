## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 311, Poseidon row.

To be strongly limited and subjected to hindrances and handicaps by fate with regard to mental or intellectual education, development and culture. Mental bondage. To be under guardianship.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 475 (one-based PDF p. 236), Poseidon row.

Depressive influence of truth, strengthening the force of an idea, intellectual energy and perseverance, strengthening cultural influence, strengthening power through truth, concentration of intellectual energies, reasonable energy restriction, and resistance to intellectual violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intellectual and cultural capacity under restriction, with consolidated influence and resistance also represented.
- psychology: Intellectual energy, perseverance, and the force of an idea; mental bondage in Witte's wording.
- body/health: ABC's depressive influence is attributed source language, without a medical explanation.
- social/relationship: Guardianship and resistance to intellectual violence.
- events/manifestations: Hindrances to education or culture, strengthened cultural influence, and reasonable restriction of energy.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's constructive consolidation and reasonable limits remain beside Witte's severe restrictions.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in range: severe intellectual limitation in Witte, and both constrained and strengthened intellectual power in ABC. They do not equate every limit with captivity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Admetos/Vulcanus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through the limits and strength of intellectual or cultural development. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes severe hindrances to education, mental bondage, and guardianship. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also allows intellectual perseverance, strengthened ideas and cultural influence, and resistance to intellectual violence. Reasonable energy restriction is a separate expression in ABC, so the range includes measured limits as well as oppressive ones.
