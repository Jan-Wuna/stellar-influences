## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Slowly forming blows of fate. To be tested by fate for a long time.

Source reference: PDF p. 311 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Enduring energy and patience, profound patience influence, concentrated energy compression, painful circulatory blockade, disturbances by blocked energies, losses by violence restriction, and deepening in power tasks

Source reference: printed p. 475 (PDF p. 236), exact activation entry. The scan confirms Saturn's row and "Ausdauernde Energie und Geduld" (enduring energy and patience), correcting "Exhausting".

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Prolonged testing that calls for endurance under concentrated or blocked force. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Patience and sustained engagement with demanding tasks; the entries do not establish a permanent personality condition. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Body/health:** The ABC entry names painful circulatory blockage and disturbances from blocked energies as historical medical claims, not an established diagnosis. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Power tasks and restrictions of force are named, but no particular intimate relationship is described. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** Slowly forming blows of fate, lengthy ordeals, and losses associated with restriction of force. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** Enduring energy and compressed energy coexist in the ABC list; neither makes lifelong oppression a necessary outcome. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established by these brief entries. Witte describes the duration of an ordeal; the ABC entry adds patience, concentrated energy, and bodily claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies the `Admetos/Vulcanus` pair through prolonged testing: results depend on sustaining energy and patience while force is compressed or obstructed. The slowly forming ordeal in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects with the [ABC entry's](#abc-entry) enduring energy, concentration, and absorption in demanding power tasks. The emphasis is on the duration and effort of resistance; these passages do not establish that hardship defines an entire life.
