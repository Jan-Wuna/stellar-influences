## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Days on which only perseverance leads to results. The man who must show great firmness of character, because he gets that kind of treatment.

Source reference: PDF p. 311 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blocked corporal energy, restricted body force, energy circuit of the body, enormous personal limitation, power strengthening of a person, personal energy and perseverance, deep personal influence, and center for resistance against violence

Source reference: ABC printed p. 474 (PDF p. 236), exact activation entry; Sun's glyph and paragraph were checked on the scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Personal endurance and firmness where bodily or personal force meets strong restriction. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Perseverance and firmness of character are responses to demanding treatment, not evidence of an inherently hardened identity. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry))
- **Body/health:** The ABC entry names restricted bodily energy and the body's energy circuit, without defining a disease. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Harsh treatment and resistance to violence place personal strength in relation to outside pressure. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** Results reached through perseverance; both personal limitation and strengthening of a person's power appear in the source range. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's historical wording refers to "The man"; the entry does not establish a sex-exclusive rule. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry))

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Restricted bodily force and strengthened personal power are different possibilities within the ABC entry, while Witte emphasizes endurance under treatment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies `Admetos/Vulcanus` as a personal and bodily test of endurance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes perseverance and firmness the means by which results are reached under demanding treatment; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends that picture to restricted bodily energy, personal limitation, and resistance to violence. Its simultaneous references to strengthened power and deep personal influence preserve an active response to pressure, rather than making restriction the only possible expression.
