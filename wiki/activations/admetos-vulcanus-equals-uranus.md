## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To suddenly face a hopeless struggle with obstacles, that prove to be fateful.

Source reference: PDF p. 311 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan. A leading ampersand from glyph extraction was removed; the printed row shows two alternative Uranus glyphs, as identified in the source's factor key on PDF p. 28.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Limitation of technical influence, new energy compaction, sudden strong depressions, surprising energy blockade, restlessness in violent restrictions, technical energy compaction, and technical rotational energy

Source reference: ABC printed p. 475 (PDF p. 236), exact activation entry; Uranus's glyph and paragraph were checked on the scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sudden confrontation with heavy obstacles or an unexpected blockage of force. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Restlessness under restrictions and the ABC entry's "sudden strong depressions" describe the source's range, not a clinical diagnosis. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Body/health:** No specific bodily condition is identified in these entries. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Restriction involving force is named, but no particular relationship role is specified. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** An abrupt struggle with fateful obstacles; technical limits, energy compaction, and rotational energy are additional ABC manifestations. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** The technical examples do not establish that every sudden obstruction becomes explosive or disastrous. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is apparent. Witte's struggle and the ABC entry's sudden blockage overlap, while technical rotational energy is an additional application. The Witte row belongs to Uranus; its two glyph variants do not add another activator.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies the `Admetos/Vulcanus` pair through the sudden arrival or discovery of resistance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes abruptly facing a seemingly hopeless struggle with obstacles, and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds unexpected energy blockage and restlessness under restrictions. The latter also gives a distinct technical expression through compaction and rotational energy. These examples support abrupt constraint and concentrated force without requiring an explosive outcome.
