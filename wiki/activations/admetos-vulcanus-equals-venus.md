## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Repulsive character. Mighty hardness and unkindness. Treated harshly.

Source reference: PDF p. 311 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blockage by love violence, blocked love power, depressive influence of art, peaceful energy blockade, profound influence of love, profound peace power, harmonious energy densification, and suppression of love through violence

Source reference: printed p. 474 (PDF p. 236), exact activation entry. Venus's row on the scan confirms "Kunst-Einfluß" and "Unterdrückung der Liebe durch Gewalt", supporting "influence of art" and "suppression of love through violence".

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Strong restraint or force affecting affection, treatment of others, and the capacity for peace. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Witte describes hardness and unkindness; the ABC entry also allows profound love and peaceful strength. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition is specified; "depressive" qualifies the influence of art in the ABC wording. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Harsh treatment, blocked affection, and suppression of love through violence are explicit relational themes. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** The source range includes rejection or unkind treatment, a peaceful energy blockade, and harmonious concentration of energy. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** Peaceful and harmonious expressions must remain visible beside the harsh ones; the passages do not make coldness universal. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

The entries differ in range: Witte is wholly harsh in this passage, while the ABC entry includes profound love, peace, and harmony as well as obstruction. These are alternative expressions, not an explicit logical contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies `Admetos/Vulcanus` through the treatment of affection and peace under powerful restraint. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives hardness, unkindness, and being treated harshly; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) similarly names blocked love and its suppression through violence. Yet its profound influence of love, peaceful strength, and harmonious concentration of energy broaden the reading: force may obstruct affection, but these entries do not limit the formula to lovelessness.
