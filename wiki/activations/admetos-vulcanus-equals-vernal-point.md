## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The general public, treated harshly by fate, must show firmness and power of resistance. Give and take the hard way with reference to the general public.

Source reference: PDF p. 311 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Limitation of violence in the public sphere, end of the public power, strengthening of public power, violence manifestations in the public sphere, profound public influence, public energy blockade, and strongly depressed public sphere

Source reference: ABC printed p. 474 (PDF p. 236), exact activation entry. Vernal Point's row on the scan confirms "öffentlichen Macht", "gründlicher öffentlicher Einfluß", and "öffentliche Energie-Blockade", repairing the damaged public-power, influence, and energy-blockade phrases.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Public life subject to severe pressure, requiring collective firmness and resistance. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Firmness and the power of resistance are attributed to the public response; the ABC entry also describes a depressed public sphere. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Body/health:** No individual bodily condition is identified. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Difficult public give-and-take and force exercised or limited in public are explicit themes. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** Harsh public circumstances, manifestations of violence, and an end to public power appear in the source range. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** Ending public power and strengthening it are alternative ABC expressions; no conditions selecting between them are supplied. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The ABC entry includes both an end to public power and its strengthening, without presenting them as simultaneous outcomes. Witte emphasizes collective resistance under harsh treatment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies `Admetos/Vulcanus` in public life: harsh circumstances require firmness and the ability to resist, while public exchange proceeds with difficulty. That is explicit in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends it to violence and its limitation, blocked public energy, and a depressed public sphere. Its references to both the ending and strengthening of public power keep the political outcome open while retaining the shared emphasis on force and resistance.
