## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Hindered in work by obstacles, a difficult beginning is forcibly aggravated by others.

Source reference: PDF p. 311 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Efforts to strengthen power, perseverance of a leading force, blocked energy performances, depressive leadership influence, constrained procreation force, strengthened leadership, and huge performance limitations

Source reference: printed p. 475 (PDF p. 236), exact activation entry. Zeus's row on the scan confirms "Ausdauer einer Führungs-Kraft" (perseverance of a leading force) and the omitted "gefestigte Führungs-Kraft" (strengthened leadership), repairing the filtration mistranslation and omission.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Effort and leadership exercised through serious restrictions on work or performance. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Perseverance and effort to consolidate power coexist with a depressive leadership influence in the ABC range. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Body/health:** The ABC entry includes constrained procreative power as a historical bodily claim, without establishing an individual condition. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Other people may aggravate an already difficult beginning; leadership is also an explicit setting. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** Work hindered by obstacles, blocked performance, strengthened leadership, and great limits on achievement. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries describe both restricted performance and consolidation of leadership; they do not say every beginning necessarily fails. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Leadership can be strengthened while work remains obstructed; the entries present this range without explaining when each occurs.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies `Admetos/Vulcanus` through obstructed work and the effort to sustain or consolidate leadership. In the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), obstacles impede a difficult beginning and other people aggravate it. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects blocked performance and major limits with perseverance, efforts to strengthen power, and strengthened leadership. The formula therefore holds obstruction and sustained directing effort together; failure of the undertaking is not stated as inevitable.
