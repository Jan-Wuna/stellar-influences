## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To live in a peaceful and tranquil environment. Others express their contentment. The native appears content to others.

Source reference: PDF p. 306 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blocked environmental science, on site depression experiences, strengthening of the partner's success, freedom restrictions on the site, thorough environmental experiences, place for thorough knowledge, environment success by perseverance, and deepening environmental knowledge

Source reference: printed p. 468 (PDF p. 233), exact activation entry. Asc's row on the scan confirms "Partner-Erfolges" and "gründliche"; the malformed partner wording and both "green" mistranslations were repaired.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Contentment in the immediate environment, with an additional range of local restriction and deepened environmental knowledge. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Appearing content to others; the ABC entry separately describes experiences of depression at a place. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Body/health:** Neither entry identifies a bodily condition. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Others' contentment, a partner's consolidated success, and limits on freedom in one's surroundings. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** Peaceful surroundings, local success through perseverance, and deeper knowledge of the environment. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries include both tranquility and restriction; neither establishes that contentment causes stagnation. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

Peaceful contentment and local depression or restricted freedom are different expressions in these entries. Their conditions are not given, so the contrast should remain visible rather than becoming a single guaranteed state.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies `Apollon/Admetos` through one's surroundings and dealings with others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) presents a tranquil environment in which contentment is both expressed and perceived. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a less uniformly easy range: local restrictions and depressive experiences alongside perseverance, a partner's strengthened success, and thorough knowledge of the environment. The common setting is local and relational; the sources leave its degree of ease open.
