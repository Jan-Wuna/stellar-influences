## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To feel happy and content in one’s habits.

Source reference: PDF p. 306 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Limitation of the financial expansion, successful knowledge deepening, joy through thorough knowledge, thorough legal knowledge, extensive money circulation, deepening into trade law, and increasing financial blockade

Source reference: printed p. 468 (PDF p. 233), exact activation entry. Jupiter's row on the scan confirms "gründliches Wissen", "gründliches Rechts-Wissen", and "Geld-Kreislauf", correcting brilliant knowledge and money circle.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Contentment in habits and satisfaction in deeper knowledge, alongside limits to financial expansion. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Happiness with habitual life and joy through thorough knowledge. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition is specified. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Law and trade law are named fields; the entry does not describe personal relationships. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** Deepened knowledge, extensive money circulation, restricted financial expansion, or increasing financial blockage. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** Contentment is not proof of material sufficiency; financial circulation and financial blockage are both in the ABC range. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Contentment in habits is not a financial guarantee, and the ABC entry supplies several financial possibilities without conditions choosing between them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `Apollon/Admetos` through contentment and the rewards of depth. Happiness within one's habits in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects with successful study and joy through thorough knowledge in the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), particularly legal and trade knowledge. The financial examples retain both extensive circulation and restricted expansion or blockage, so the contentment described here does not establish unrestricted material growth.
