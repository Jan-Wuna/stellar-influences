## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Without care and worry. To be very satisfied with one’s special mode of life. Best expression for this formula is: free from worry.

Source reference: PDF p. 306 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special scientific thoroughness, restriction of freedom by the state, restrictions in wholesale trade, success by special perseverance, consolidation of state successes, much depression in the state, and increasing resistance to the state

Source reference: printed p. 469 (PDF p. 233), exact activation entry. Kronos's row on the scan confirms "Gründlichkeit", the omitted "Einschränkungen im Groß-Handel", and "viel Depressionen im Staat"; those damaged or missing phrases were repaired.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Freedom from personal worry; the ABC range adds scientific thoroughness and state-level perseverance or restriction. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Satisfaction with a special way of life is explicit; superiority and aloofness are not. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry))
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition is identified; the ABC depression wording concerns the state. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** State restriction of freedom, wholesale trade limits, and resistance to the state are named. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** Success through exceptional perseverance, consolidated state successes, and scientific thoroughness. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** Personal freedom from worry and state-imposed restriction concern different scopes; neither is explained as the cause of the other. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

The passages differ materially in emphasis: personal carefree satisfaction versus scientific and state affairs, including restriction and depression. They do not supply a rule reconciling those expressions or require them to occur together.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies `Apollon/Admetos` in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) as satisfaction with a particular way of life, expressed most directly as freedom from worry. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives a broader institutional field: thorough scientific work, success through exceptional perseverance, and consolidation of state successes, alongside restrictions on freedom or wholesale trade and resistance to the state. Personal ease and these public restrictions remain separate sourced expressions; neither establishes aloofness or privileged complacency.
