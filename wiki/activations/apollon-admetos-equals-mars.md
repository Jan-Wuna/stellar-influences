## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To find satisfaction in one’s own activity. To be satisfied with one’s results.

Source reference: PDF p. 306 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Spread of depression impulses, blocked scientific work, restriction of trade activity, consolidation of successful efforts, thorough activity experiences, thorough scientific work, and activity success through perseverance

Source reference: printed p. 468 (PDF p. 233), exact activation entry. Mars's row on the scan confirms "erfolgreicher Bemühungen" and both "gründliche" phrases, correcting mitigations and untranslated OCR.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Satisfaction in one's work and results, with achievement sustained through thoroughness and perseverance. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Contentment with one's activity; the ABC entry also names spreading depressive impulses. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Body/health:** No specific bodily condition is given. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Scientific work and trade activity are the explicit settings; an intimate relationship is not described. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** Consolidated successful efforts, thorough work, blocked science, or restricted trade. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** Satisfaction with results does not establish declining initiative; obstruction and perseverance are separately named. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The entries allow satisfaction with activity and success through perseverance while also describing blocked work; they do not state which conditions select each expression.

## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies `Apollon/Admetos` in activity and its results. The satisfaction described by the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects with the [ABC entry's](#abc-entry) consolidation of successful efforts, thorough scientific work, and success through perseverance. Blocked science and restricted trade preserve the possibility of difficult working conditions; the passages do not make contentment a cause of lost initiative.
