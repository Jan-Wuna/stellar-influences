## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The contented man. To have peace of mind. To be mentally balanced. To feel satisfied. Lack of ambition.

Source reference: PDF p. 306 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Limitation of the soul's freedom, my success through perseverance, my blocked freedom, my thorough experiences, my scientific thoroughness, mental depression experiences, and deepening of the knowledge of the soul

Source reference: printed p. 468 (PDF p. 233), exact activation entry. MC's row on the scan confirms "Seelen-Freiheit", "gründlichen Erfahrungen", and "wissenschaftliche Gründlichkeit", correcting the spleen, fundamental, and basicity mistranslations.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Personal peace of mind or satisfaction, with an additional range of restricted inner freedom and perseverance. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Mental balance and lack of ambition; the ABC entry also names mental depressive experiences and knowledge of the soul. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition is specified; the mental language remains the sources' historical description. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Personal freedom is addressed, but no partner or group relationship is specified. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** Personal success through perseverance and thorough scientific experience. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's "man" is retained as historical wording; lack of ambition does not itself establish failed achievement. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry))

## Contradictions

Peace of mind and depressive experiences differ in emotional tone. These short entries give no conditions reconciling them, so both remain in the range rather than being asserted simultaneously.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies `Apollon/Admetos` in one's own mental state and experience. Peace of mind, satisfaction, and lack of ambition are explicit in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal success through perseverance and scientific thoroughness, while also preserving blocked inner freedom and depressive experiences. The shared personal focus is clear; neither contentment nor restriction exhausts the recorded range.
