## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Women or persons express their contentment. People in a state of saturation or peaceful minded. The public is content.

Source reference: PDF p. 306 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blocked knowledge of feelings, restricted freedom of women, success of a woman through perseverance, successful deepening of feelings, thorough emotional experiences, trade restrictions for a people, and suppression of the people's knowledge

Source reference: printed p. 468 (PDF p. 233), exact activation entry. Moon's row on the scan confirms "Gefühle", "gründliche Gefühls-Erfahrungen", and "Wissen des Volkes unterdrücken", repairing flowers, damaged experience wording, and the final clause.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Contentment among people, alongside deepened or restricted emotional knowledge and collective freedom. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Peaceful or saturated feeling, blocked knowledge of feelings, and thorough emotional experience. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Body/health:** No specific bodily condition is named. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** The historical entries speak of women, people, and the public; the ABC entry includes restrictions on a woman's freedom and a people's trade or knowledge. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** Expressed public contentment or a woman's success through perseverance. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** Historical gender assignments remain attributed; no universal rule about women or collective passivity follows. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

Public contentment and restricted collective freedom are different expressions, not a stated causal sequence. The sources give no conditions selecting between them, and their gendered language is historical attribution.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies `Apollon/Admetos` through people and emotional experience. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes women, persons, or the public expressing contentment and peaceful saturation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) preserves deepened feelings and success through perseverance, together with restrictions on freedom, trade, and knowledge. These historical assignments support a collective and emotional field without making tranquility or passivity an inevitable condition.
