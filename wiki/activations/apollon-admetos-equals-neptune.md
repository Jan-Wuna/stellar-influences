## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 306, Neptune row.

To feel uncomfortable, to be disharmonious and dissatisfied. Not to be able to shake off miserable or unpleasant feelings. Dissatisfaction. Under the impression of unpleasant matters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 469 (one-based PDF p. 233), Neptune row.

Blockage of metaphysical knowledge, success in metaphysics through thoroughness, consolidation of success through perseverance, suppression of metaphysical knowledge, deepening into knowledge of the future, much uncertainty through depression, and thorough refinement of knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Dissatisfaction and persistent unpleasant feeling, alongside blocked or refined metaphysical understanding.
- psychology: Difficulty shaking off unpleasant feelings; uncertainty through depression in ABC's historical wording.
- body/health: Neither entry establishes a bodily condition or medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Suppression of metaphysical knowledge is named; no specific relationship role is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Success through thoroughness and perseverance, refined knowledge, or blocked metaphysical inquiry.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's knowledge-of-the-future phrase is retained as doctrine; no predictive ability is established by this review.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes dissatisfaction, while ABC includes both blocked knowledge and success or refinement. The entries provide no conditions reconciling or selecting these expressions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Admetos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through dissatisfaction and unpleasant feelings that persist in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds uncertainty and blocked or suppressed metaphysical knowledge, but also success through thoroughness and perseverance and the refinement of understanding. The page preserves both unsettled feeling and the work of deepening knowledge, without reducing the entire range to chronic malaise.
