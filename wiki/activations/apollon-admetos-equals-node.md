## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be satisfied with the existing circumstances. To have the necessary connections.

Source reference: PDF p. 306 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blocked trade connections, restriction of freedom in a connection, end of a successful connection, successful connection through perseverance, firm connection to science, freedom in a fixed connection, and thorough experiences in connections

Source reference: printed p. 468 (PDF p. 233), exact activation entry. Node's row on the scan confirms "Handels-Verbindungen" and "gründliche Erfahrungen in Verbindungen", repairing the damaged connection and experience phrases.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Necessary or enduring connections and satisfaction with circumstances, with restrictions also possible within a connection. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Satisfaction is explicit; dependency or resistance to growth is not stated. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry))
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition is specified. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Trade connections, a firm connection to science, and freedom or its restriction within a fixed connection. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** A successful connection through perseverance, the end of a successful connection, or blocked trade ties. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** Firm connection does not by itself mean either dependence or confinement; the source lists freedom and restriction separately. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

The ABC entry contains both freedom within a fixed connection and restricted freedom, and both successful continuation and ending. These are alternatives without stated selection conditions, not simultaneous requirements.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies `Apollon/Admetos` through connections and the circumstances they sustain. Necessary ties and satisfaction with existing conditions in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connect with the [ABC entry's](#abc-entry) successful ties through perseverance and firm connection to science. That same entry retains blocked trade ties, an ending, and both freedom and restriction within a connection. Stability therefore remains a possible expression without becoming a claim of inevitable dependence.
