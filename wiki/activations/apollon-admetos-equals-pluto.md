## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The beginning of a development which takes a very satisfactory course.

Source reference: PDF p. 306 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development of increased depression, unfolding thorough experiences, profound change in knowledge, growing barriers to trade, growing change in knowledge, transformation of blocked knowledge, and thorough development of knowledge

Source reference: printed p. 469 (PDF p. 233), exact activation entry. Pluto's row on the scan confirms "gründliche Erfahrungen entfalten" and "Wissen gründlich entwickeln", correcting aggravation and restoring thoroughness.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** A developing process, including a satisfactory course or deep change in knowledge. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Satisfaction is one expression; increased depression is another historical ABC expression. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition is identified. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Growing trade barriers are named; no personal relationship role is specified. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** A favorable beginning, unfolded experience, transformed blocked knowledge, or growing restrictions. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** The favorable course in Witte does not erase the ABC entry's adverse developments or guarantee lasting stability. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

Witte gives a favorable course; the ABC entry includes constructive knowledge development and adverse expansion of depression or trade barriers. The entries offer no conditions for choosing among these possibilities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies `Apollon/Admetos` as a process taking shape. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives a beginning whose development is satisfactory, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) emphasizes unfolding experience, changing or transforming blocked knowledge, and thorough development. Its growing trade barriers and increased depression preserve an adverse range as well. Development is the shared feature; its favorable course is one sourced possibility rather than a guarantee.
