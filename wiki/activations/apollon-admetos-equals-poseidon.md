## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To feel safe at home through outlook on life. Contentment through being in high minded company, with people of similar destiny and outlook on life.

Source reference: PDF p. 306 (one-based), exact entry start, checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blocked mind science, restricted spiritual freedom, successful spiritual origination, thorough spiritual experiences, cultural success through perseverance, strengthening of success by reason, origination of spiritual knowledge, and deepening truth knowledge

Source reference: printed p. 469 (PDF p. 233), exact activation entry. Poseidon's row on the scan confirms "gründliche spirituelle Erfahrungen", "Vernunft", and "Urzeugung geistigen Wissens", correcting grim, Yemunft, and the biological-sounding knowledge phrase.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Security in a shared outlook, with deepened spiritual or truth-oriented knowledge. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Psychology:** Feeling at home through one's outlook and contentment in like-minded company. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry))
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition is specified; origination concerns knowledge, not biological reproduction. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Social/relationship:** Company of similar outlook or destiny and cultural success through perseverance. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Events/manifestations:** Thorough spiritual experience, knowledge origination, reasoned consolidation of success, or restricted spiritual freedom. ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
- **Conflicts/notes:** Shared outlook does not establish social enclosure; restricted freedom is an additional source expression. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte describes belonging through outlook; the ABC entry adds knowledge creation, perseverance, and restriction, without stating that like-minded company causes any restriction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies `Apollon/Admetos` through outlook and spiritual knowledge. Feeling at home among people of similar outlook or destiny in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects with the [ABC entry's](#abc-entry) thorough spiritual experience, deepened truth knowledge, and cultural success through perseverance. The latter also names blocked knowledge and restricted spiritual freedom. Belonging and sustained inquiry are supported, while withdrawal from the wider world is not established.
