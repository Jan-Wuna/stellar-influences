## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 306, Saturn row.

Dissatisfaction, difficult to satisfy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 469 (one-based PDF p. 233), Saturn row.

Problematic limitation of knowledge, success through patience and perseverance, consolidation of success through seriousness, thorough experience of problems, disturbances due to increased depression, and losses due to trade restrictions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Dissatisfaction and limits on knowledge, alongside success through sustained serious effort.
- psychology: Difficulty feeling satisfied; patience, perseverance, and seriousness.
- body/health: ABC's increased depression is an attributed association, without a medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: No specific relationship role is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Thorough experience of problems, consolidated success, or losses through trade restrictions.
- conflicts/notes: Difficulty satisfying oneself does not establish high standards or inevitable failure; ABC explicitly retains success.

## Contradictions

Dissatisfaction and success through patience can coexist. The entries do not state that achievement must resolve dissatisfaction, or that dissatisfaction prevents achievement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Admetos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through dissatisfaction and difficulty being satisfied in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds limits on knowledge and difficult experience, but also success through patience and perseverance and its consolidation through seriousness. Its trade losses and depression language preserve an adverse range without making dissatisfaction a rule that all achievement must feel inadequate.
