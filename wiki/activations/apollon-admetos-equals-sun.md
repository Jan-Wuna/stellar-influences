## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 306, Sun row.

Satisfactory conditions. Days of contentment and rest. The man whose mind is at rest and easy. Well balanced mentally so that nothing can move or scare him.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 468 (one-based PDF p. 233), Sun row.

Blocked personal knowledge, consolidation of personal knowledge, thorough experiences of a man, bodily success through perseverance, personal restriction of freedom, and a scientific center for depression.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal contentment and mental balance, with knowledge consolidated or blocked.
- psychology: Rest and ease of mind; resistance to being moved or frightened in Witte's wording.
- body/health: ABC names bodily success through perseverance and a scientific center for depression without defining either medically.
- social/relationship: Personal freedom is restricted in ABC; no specific partner role is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Days of rest, thorough experience, consolidated knowledge, or blocked knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: The gendered examples remain historical attributions; Witte does not state loss of ambition, and ABC's scientific-center phrase is unexplained.

## Contradictions

Restful contentment and restricted personal knowledge or freedom are different possibilities. The short entries do not explain their relationship or select an outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Admetos = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through satisfactory conditions and a person at mental ease in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds thorough personal experience and knowledge that may be consolidated or blocked, together with bodily success through perseverance and restricted freedom. Personal equilibrium is supported, while inertia or loss of ambition is not stated for this exact formula.
