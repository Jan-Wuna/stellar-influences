## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 306, Uranus row.

To become surprisingly suddenly satisfied. Suddenly content.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 469 (one-based PDF p. 233), Uranus row.

Consolidation of new knowledge, thorough technical reform, suppression of new knowledge, tensions through spreading depression, surprising trade restrictions, deepening into new knowledge, and reform of knowledge concerning primordial matter.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden contentment, with new knowledge consolidated, deepened, or suppressed.
- psychology: Surprising satisfaction; tensions through spreading depression in ABC's historical wording.
- body/health: No bodily condition or medical diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: No specific personal relationship pattern is described.
- events/manifestations: Technical reform, surprising trade restrictions, and changes to knowledge concerning primordial matter.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's Urstoff phrase concerns primordial matter but is not explained further; sudden contentment is not described as shallow or unstable.

## Contradictions

Consolidation and suppression of new knowledge are alternatives in ABC. Witte's sudden satisfaction neither reconciles those alternatives nor guarantees their outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Admetos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through surprising, sudden contentment in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a distinct range of new knowledge and technical reform, including both consolidation and suppression, as well as unexpected trade restrictions. Sudden change is a recurring feature; the entries do not explain the quality or duration of the resulting satisfaction.
