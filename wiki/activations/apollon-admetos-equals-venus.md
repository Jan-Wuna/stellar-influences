## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 306, Venus row.

Self-sufficient. Satisfaction and rest of mind through being single and alone. To be without feeling for love and affection.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 468 (one-based PDF p. 233), Venus row.

Blocked success in peace, restriction of peaceful expansion, successful consolidation of harmony, consolidation of knowledge of art, joy over success through perseverance, thorough experiences in love, and deepening artistic success.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Self-sufficient contentment, with harmony and artistic or loving experience also represented.
- psychology: Rest of mind in solitude; Witte also names absence of feeling for love and affection.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Being single and alone in Witte contrasts with ABC's thorough experiences in love.
- events/manifestations: Consolidated harmony and artistic knowledge, deepened artistic success, or restricted peaceful expansion.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's absence of affection and ABC's loving experience remain distinct expressions; neither is made a universal relationship rule.

## Contradictions

The entries differ materially in their relationship emphasis: Witte permits absence of affection, while ABC includes deep experience in love. No conditions reconcile those expressions or select one.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Admetos = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through self-sufficient satisfaction and rest of mind in solitude in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). That entry also permits absence of feeling for love or affection. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) preserves another range: consolidated harmony, thorough loving experience, and deepening artistic success, alongside limits on peaceful expansion. Solitary contentment is therefore one supported expression, while the full page also retains cultivated love, harmony, and art.
