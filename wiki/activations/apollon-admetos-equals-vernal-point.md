## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 306, Vernal Point row.

General tranquility. To be carefree. General contentment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 468 (one-based PDF p. 233), Vernal Point row.

Blocked knowledge in public life, public success through perseverance, restrictions of freedom in public life, thorough experiences in public, public trade restrictions, and much depression in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public tranquility or contentment, alongside public knowledge, freedom, and trade under restriction.
- psychology: Carefree contentment in Witte; public depression is ABC's historical association.
- body/health: No individual bodily condition or diagnosis is given.
- social/relationship: Public freedom and access to knowledge; no specific personal relationship is described.
- events/manifestations: Public success through perseverance, thorough public experience, or trade restrictions.
- conflicts/notes: The entries retain both general ease and adverse public conditions; complacency and failed reform are not stated.

## Contradictions

The entries have contrasting public tones. Neither supplies conditions explaining when tranquility, restricted freedom, or depression applies.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Admetos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the pair through general tranquility and contentment in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens the public field to success through perseverance and thorough experience, while also naming blocked knowledge and restrictions on freedom or trade. General ease and constrained public conditions remain distinct possibilities, without a claim that calm necessarily discourages action or reform.
