## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 305, Admetos row.

The feeling of satisfaction. Contentment. Satiety. To be saturated. Stagnation or standstill in trade, handicraft, commerce, industry or science. Science in a small circle, for only a few. Exclusive scientific circle. The few, the small circle. The exclusive circle.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Satisfaction and satiety alongside standstill or a small exclusive scope.
- psychology: Contentment and the feeling of having enough.
- body/health: Satiety is named without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Small or exclusive scientific circles.
- events/manifestations: Stagnation in trade, handicraft, commerce, industry, or science.
- conflicts/notes: The entry links a range of satisfaction and limitation; prosperity or arrested personal growth is not stated.

## Contradictions

Contentment and standstill can coexist. This single entry does not specify when a small circle is satisfactory or limiting.
