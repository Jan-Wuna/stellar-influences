#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 305, Hades row.

Lack of experience. Widespread poverty. Mass misery. Garbage and junk dealers. Trade with old objects. Science of the past. Knowledge of history. Occult science. Secret science. Magic. Great meanness, great vileness. Many in need.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Widespread need, old or discarded objects, and study of the past or hidden subjects.
- psychology: Lack of experience and the source's moral terms meanness and vileness.
- body/health: No bodily condition or medical diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Many people in need and mass poverty or misery.
- events/manifestations: Junk dealing, trade in old objects, historical knowledge, occult or secret science, and magic.
- conflicts/notes: The entry does not make historical or occult study the cause of poverty or moral failings.

## Contradictions

Several distinct domains are listed. They are not explained as one causal process or a moral judgment on every person studying the past.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Apollon = Hades`, Hades specifies the repeated pair through widespread need and through dealings with what is old, discarded, or hidden. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places mass poverty and misery beside junk trading, old objects, historical knowledge, and occult or secret study. Lack of experience and its moral language remain separate source associations; they do not establish that these trades or studies produce deprivation or meanness.
