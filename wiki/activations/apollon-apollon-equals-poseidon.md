## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 305, Poseidon row.

People with the same mindset. Congenial. Companions of destiny. Fellow sufferers, comrades. Comradeship. Disposition (way of thinking). Spreading of ideas. Insights through experience. People with similar outlook on life. Spiritual science. Humanities. Theological insights.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared outlook, comradeship, and ideas spread or deepened through experience.
- psychology: Congeniality, disposition, and insight through experience.
- body/health: Fellow sufferers is a social description without a specified medical condition.
- social/relationship: Companions of destiny, comrades, and people of similar mindset.
- events/manifestations: Spreading ideas, spiritual science, humanities, and theological insights.
- conflicts/notes: Common outlook is not stated to prove truth or to cause detachment from practical life.

## Contradictions

No incompatible claim is established within this single entry. Shared suffering, congeniality, and intellectual affinity are retained as different grounds of companionship.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Apollon = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the repeated pair through comradeship and a shared way of thinking. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins people of similar outlook or destiny with the spread of ideas and insight gained through experience. Spiritual science, humanities, and theological understanding give intellectual fields to that shared outlook, while fellow suffering supplies another ground of companionship.
