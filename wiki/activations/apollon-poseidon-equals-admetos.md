## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 308, Admetos row.

Great conviction and steadfastness. Exclusive circle of similar minded people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 473 (one-based PDF p. 235), Admetos row.

Limitation of intellectual freedom, successful concentration of an idea, intellectual knowledge cycle, thorough cultural knowledge, origination of intellectual knowledge, deepening of intellectual knowledge, and resistance to knowledge of the truth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Steadfast conviction and concentrated or deepened knowledge, with restricted freedom also possible.
- psychology: Strong conviction and steadfastness; resistance to knowledge of truth in ABC.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: An exclusive circle of like-minded people.
- events/manifestations: Successful concentration of an idea, thorough cultural knowledge, and origination or deepening of knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: Exclusivity and restricted freedom are explicit, but the entries do not say that all conviction becomes rigid or false.

## Contradictions

Deepening knowledge and resisting knowledge of truth are contrasting possibilities in ABC. No conditions select between them or establish that the exclusive circle causes either.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair through steadfast conviction and a concentrated circle of understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names an exclusive group of like-minded people; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds concentrated ideas and thorough or deepened knowledge. Restricted intellectual freedom and resistance to knowledge of truth remain explicit alternatives within that range, so concentration is not treated as proof of open inquiry.
