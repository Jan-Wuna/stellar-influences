## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 308, Cupido row.

Congeniality in a family or in a community. Same mentality caused by the same social conditions. The same concept of art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 473 (one-based PDF p. 235), Cupido row.

Success of a community through reason, successful intellectual synthesis, a successful artistic idea, shared intellectual freedom, shared cultural success, a community for intellectual knowledge, and cultural knowledge of a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Congenial communal outlook and shared cultural or intellectual achievement.
- psychology: Similar mentality linked by Witte to similar social conditions; reason and synthesis in ABC.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Congeniality in family or community and shared intellectual freedom.
- events/manifestations: A common concept of art, successful artistic ideas, and collective cultural success.
- conflicts/notes: Shared social conditions are a sourced influence; enforced conformity or dependence of belonging on sameness is not stated.

## Contradictions

Shared mentality and shared intellectual freedom are both represented. The entries do not establish that agreement requires conformity or that every community is intellectually free.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through congeniality and shared cultural understanding within a family or community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links a similar mentality to similar social conditions and a common concept of art. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends that shared field to reasoned success, intellectual synthesis and freedom, and successful artistic ideas or cultural work.
