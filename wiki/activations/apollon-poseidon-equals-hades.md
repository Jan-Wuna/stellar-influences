## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Wanting in comradeship. To be disgusted with colleagues comrades or people of the same destiny. To lead a separate life among comrades and colleagues. To withdraw from them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dangers of intellectual freedom, dangerous intellectual knowledge, cultural knowledge of the past, lack of intellectual freedom, hidden intellectual knowledge, abandonment of intellectual knowledge, and increasing criminalization of culture

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Poseidon = Hades`: disappointed fellowship, alienation among colleagues or fellow sufferers, and distaste for the very community one belongs to. Main caution: embittered separation: shared outlook becomes tainted by distaste, decay, or hidden disillusionment.
