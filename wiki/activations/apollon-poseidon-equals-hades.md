## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 308, Hades row.

Wanting in comradeship. To be disgusted with colleagues comrades or people of the same destiny. To lead a separate life among comrades and colleagues. To withdraw from them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 473 (one-based PDF p. 235), Hades row.

Dangers of intellectual freedom, dangerous intellectual knowledge, cultural knowledge of the past, lack of intellectual freedom, hidden intellectual knowledge, abandonment of intellectual knowledge, and increasing criminalization of culture

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strained comradeship and threatened, hidden, or renounced knowledge and freedom.
- psychology: Disgust with colleagues or companions; a desire to withdraw in Witte's wording.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Separate life among comrades or colleagues and withdrawal from them.
- events/manifestations: Cultural knowledge of the past, hidden knowledge, and increasing criminalization of culture.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's dangerous-knowledge and criminalization claims remain source associations; they are not accusations against colleagues or a judgment on historical study.

## Contradictions

Dangers associated with intellectual freedom and lack of that freedom are contrasting conditions in ABC. The entries do not explain either as a necessary cause of withdrawn fellowship.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through strained fellowship and constrained or concealed understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes disgust with comrades or colleagues and withdrawal while among them. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds hidden or renounced knowledge, lack of intellectual freedom, and cultural knowledge of the past. Its danger and criminalization language remains attributed rather than used to explain every separation from like-minded people.
