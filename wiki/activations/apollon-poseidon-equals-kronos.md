## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 308, Kronos row.

To be outstandingly cultured among cultured people and rated as an example by them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 473 (one-based PDF p. 235), Kronos row.

Efforts of the intellectual hierarchy, a special idea for success, a successful intellectual hierarchy, leading intellectual knowledge, great cultural knowledge, independent spiritual science, state cultural achievements, and superior knowledge of truth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Distinction in culture and knowledge, including leadership or hierarchy in intellectual life.
- psychology: Being regarded as an example by cultured people; elitist distance is not stated.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Recognition among cultured peers and an intellectual hierarchy.
- events/manifestations: State cultural achievements, leading knowledge, independent spiritual science, and a special idea for success.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's superior knowledge is attributed source wording; recognition does not establish moral superiority or social detachment.

## Contradictions

The entries give related forms of cultural distinction without a direct disagreement. They do not identify hierarchy as either inherently beneficial or oppressive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair through cultural distinction and leading knowledge. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes being exceptionally cultured and regarded as an example by cultured peers. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) expands this to intellectual hierarchy, extensive cultural knowledge, independent spiritual science, and state cultural achievements. Recognition and the organization of knowledge connect these personal and institutional expressions.
