### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be outstandingly cultured among cultured people and rated as an example by them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Efforts of spiritual hierarchy, special success idea, successful spiritual hierarchy, leading spiritual knowledge, great cultural knowledge, self governing mind science, state cultural achievement, and superior truth knowledge

## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Poseidon = Kronos`: authority in culture, spiritual or philosophical prestige, and recognition as a model within educated circles. Main caution: refined superiority: elevation of mindset can harden into distance, elitism, or self-conscious exemplarity.
