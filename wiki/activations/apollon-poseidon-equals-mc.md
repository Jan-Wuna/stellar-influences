## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 308, MC row.

The personality of the native as an expression of his disposition (way of thinking). To have, with many, the same mental and emotional attitude.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 472 (one-based PDF p. 235), MC row.

Freedom of mind and soul, experiences of the spirit-soul, my intellectual success, my cultural knowledge, my knowledge of truth, my successful ideas, my intellectual freedom, my increasing insights, and increasing clarity of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal disposition and intellectual understanding, shared with many or personally deepened.
- psychology: Personality expressing a way of thinking; increasing inner clarity in ABC's soul language.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Sharing a mental and emotional attitude with many.
- events/manifestations: Successful ideas, cultural knowledge, intellectual success, and freedom.
- conflicts/notes: The personal emphasis does not define a vocation or establish uncritical adoption of consensus; spirit-soul remains source terminology.

## Contradictions

Shared attitude and personal intellectual freedom can coexist. The entries do not establish either conflict between them or a necessary life path.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = MC`, MC specifies the pair through one's own disposition and intellectual understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes personality as an expression of a way of thinking, including a mental and emotional attitude shared with many. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal cultural knowledge, successful ideas, freedom, and increasing clarity, retaining its spirit-soul language for the inward dimension of that understanding.
