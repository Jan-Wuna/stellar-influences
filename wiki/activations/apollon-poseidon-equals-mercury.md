## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Discussion with congenial persons. Conferences, debates.

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 308, entry start; printed page not established here.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful intellectual conversations, successful cultural reports, successful language culture, thoughts on the freedom of the mind, extensive cultural knowledge, truth experiences with the media, and increasing knowledge in youth

Translation check: German `geistige Gespräche` supports conversations rather than the former “spiritual language.” The wording and Mercury glyph were checked on the scan: printed p. 472, one-based PDF p. 235, exact entry.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Congenial discussion and the communication of cultural or intellectual knowledge ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Thoughts about intellectual freedom and increasing understanding in youth are explicit in [ABC](#abc-entry).
- Body/health: Neither entry establishes a bodily or health meaning.
- Social/relationship: Conferences, debates, and conversation among congenial persons ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- Events/manifestations: Cultural reports, language culture, and experiences of truth through media ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: The entries support communication and learning; they do not assert that debate necessarily becomes empty or ungrounded.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident in these entries. Witte names discussion settings; ABC expands the cultural and intellectual content.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through conversation and the exchange of knowledge: congenial people meet in conferences or debates, while language, cultural reporting, and media carry ideas into wider circulation. Thoughts about intellectual freedom and growing understanding in youth extend the same theme of learning through communication. These entries do not establish a separate failure pattern for such discussion ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 472).
