## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A people, the public, women or several persons show their attitude and disposition.

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 308, entry start; printed page not established here.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success through clear feelings, mental experiences of a woman, cultural knowledge of a people, cultural success of a woman, truth knowledge of a woman, knowledge of a culture people, and increasing knowledge of a woman

Translation check: German `klare Gefühle` supports “clear feelings,” not “clear skills.” The wording and Moon glyph were checked on the scan: printed p. 472, one-based PDF p. 235, exact entry.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: The attitudes and cultural understanding expressed by a people, public, or persons ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Clear feelings, mental experience, and increasing understanding appear in [ABC](#abc-entry).
- Body/health: Neither entry establishes a health claim.
- Social/relationship: Witte explicitly includes the public, women, and several persons; ABC includes a people's cultural knowledge.
- Events/manifestations: Showing an attitude or disposition, and the cultural success attributed to a woman ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: References to women are the sources' gendered formulations; they do not establish a universal distinction by gender.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. The entries differ in emphasis between expressed disposition and cultural knowledge. Neither asserts mass suggestibility.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through the disposition and understanding of persons or a public. Witte describes people showing their attitude; ABC develops the cultural side through a people's knowledge and, in its gendered wording, a woman's clear feelings, mental experiences, cultural success, and growing understanding. The supported connection is between expressed outlook and cultural learning, without an asserted pattern of mass suggestibility ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 472).
