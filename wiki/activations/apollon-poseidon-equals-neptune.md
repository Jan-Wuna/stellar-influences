## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Dissolution of a community of like-minded people through fateful influences or internal falsehood, internal insincerity or mutual misunderstanding.

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 308, entry start; printed page not established here.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A fine feeling for true knowledge, foreign cultural knowledge, spiritual experiences with metaphysics, trade in clear water, weak spiritual knowledge, sensitization of science to the spiritual principle, and increasing refinement of knowledge

Source-boundary repair: the scan shows the Neptune entry ending at `zunehmende Verfeinerung der Erkenntnisse`. The subsequent clauses beginning `Erfolgreiche Ideen entwickeln` belong to Pluto and have been restored there. Neptune glyph and paragraph boundary checked on printed p. 473, one-based PDF p. 235.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A divided range: dissolution of congenial association in [Witte](#witte-entry), and refined or weak spiritual knowledge in [ABC](#abc-entry).
- Psychology: Insincerity and mutual misunderstanding contrast with sensitivity to knowledge and metaphysical experience.
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or health interpretation.
- Social/relationship: A like-minded community may dissolve through fateful influences or internal falsehood ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- Events/manifestations: ABC names foreign cultural knowledge and the distinct literal application “trade in clear water.”
- Conflicts/notes: The ABC paragraph boundary was checked on the scan; Pluto's opening clauses were removed from this entry. The corrected Neptune passage retains both fine sensitivity and weak knowledge.

## Contradictions

The entries do not yield one uniform outcome. Witte emphasizes dissolution through falsehood or misunderstanding; ABC includes refinement as well as weak knowledge. This is a substantive difference in emphasis, not proof that either outcome always follows.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair in two distinct ways. Witte describes the breakdown of a like-minded community through external fate, insincerity, or mutual misunderstanding. ABC also allows a finer sensitivity to spiritual or cultural knowledge and metaphysical experience, while explicitly including weak spiritual knowledge. The page therefore supports both vulnerable association and a more receptive pursuit of knowledge; the positive material should not be erased by the dissolution theme ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 473).

ABC additionally names trade in clear water as a concrete application, distinct from the accounts of association and knowledge.
