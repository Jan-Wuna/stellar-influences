## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Dissolution of a community of like-minded people through fateful influences or internal falsehood, internal insincerity or mutual misunderstanding.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A fine feeling for true knowledge, foreign cultural knowledge, spiritual experiences with metaphysics, trade in clear water, weak spiritual knowledge, sensitization of science to the spiritual principle, increasing refinement of knowledge, developing successful ideas, learning evolution through spiritual knowledge, and intellectual freedom

## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Poseidon = Neptune`: confusion inside groups, false spiritual or intellectual unity, and communities eroded by hidden dishonesty or vague projection. Main caution: idealism turned hollow: what seemed congenial can dissolve through internal falseness.
