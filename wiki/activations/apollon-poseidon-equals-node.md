## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be connected with comrades, colleagues, fellow sufferers or congenial persons.

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 308, entry start; printed page not established here.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success through clarity in a connection, combining successful ideas, successful cultural connections, spiritual freedom in a connection, combining spiritual knowledge, and connections to the mind science

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Connection through congeniality, shared ideas, or cultural and spiritual knowledge ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Clarity and spiritual freedom within a connection are explicit in [ABC](#abc-entry).
- Body/health: “Fellow sufferers” identifies a possible bond in Witte, without specifying a disease or health outcome.
- Social/relationship: Comrades, colleagues, fellow sufferers, and congenial persons form the stated range of association ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- Events/manifestations: Combining successful ideas and making successful cultural connections ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's “mind science” renders `Geistes-Wissenschaft`; the entry concerns intellectual or spiritual study, without identifying a particular modern discipline. ABC locator: printed p. 472, PDF p. 235, exact parser-normalized entry.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Witte names kinds of associates; ABC describes clarity, knowledge, and freedom within connection. Neither entry asserts intolerance of disagreement or dependence on conformity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Node`, Node specifies the pair through connection with congenial people: comrades, colleagues, or those linked by shared suffering. ABC gives that association an intellectual and cultural form, joining successful ideas and spiritual knowledge while retaining clarity and freedom within the bond. The evidence supports fellowship and exchange, without making conformity a necessary condition of belonging ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 472).
