## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Development or beginning of a comradeship or an association of congenial people.

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 308, entry start; printed page not established here.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Developing successful ideas, evolution through spiritual knowledge, experiencing intellectual freedom, cultural knowledge unfolding, changes through spiritual or intellectual study, growth in that study, and increasing experiences of reason

Translation and boundary check: the Pluto glyph introduces `Erfolgreiche Ideen entwickeln, Evolution durch geistiges Wissen, geistige Freiheit erfahren` on the scan. These opening clauses were previously attached to Neptune and are restored here. `wachsende Geistes-Wissenschaft` and `zunehmende Vernunft-Erfahrungen` also confirm the repair of “mind csscience.” Printed p. 473, one-based PDF p. 235, exact entry, visually checked.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: The beginning or development of congenial association alongside unfolding cultural knowledge ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Increasing experience of reason, intellectual freedom, and changes through spiritual or intellectual study ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- Social/relationship: A comradeship or association of congenial persons begins or develops ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- Events/manifestations: Successful ideas develop, cultural knowledge unfolds, and spiritual or intellectual study grows ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: The entries establish development without asserting domination by a group.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Association and knowledge are different objects of development here; neither source asserts that shared outlook absorbs individuality.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair through beginnings and development. A comradeship or association of congenial people takes shape in Witte; ABC applies the same movement to successful ideas, unfolding cultural knowledge, changes through spiritual or intellectual study, and growing experience of reason. Intellectual freedom is another explicit experience within that development. The connecting theme is the development of shared association and understanding, without an established claim that the group gains dominance over its members ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 473).
