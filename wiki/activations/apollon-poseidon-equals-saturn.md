## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Persons who share the same educational influence. To separate from comrades, colleagues, or companions.

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 308, entry start; printed page not established here.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful ideas in old age, concentration on spiritual knowledge, maturity through spiritual freedom, problematic cultural outcomes, painful experiences through truth, experiences of reason in old age, and increasing knowledge through patience

Translation check: `Vernunft-Erfahrungen im Alter` supports experiences of reason rather than “communication experiences.” Wording and Saturn glyph checked on the scan: printed p. 473, one-based PDF p. 235, exact entry.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared educational formation, separation from companions, and patient intellectual maturation ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Concentration, patience, and maturity through spiritual freedom; truth can also be painful ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Body/health: Old age is a stated context, without a disease or health outcome.
- Social/relationship: People share an educational influence or separate from comrades and colleagues ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- Events/manifestations: Successful ideas in later life and problematic cultural outcomes ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: Shared upbringing and separation are both preserved; the entries do not establish doctrinal exclusion as their cause.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte gives both shared formation and separation; ABC combines maturation with painful truth and problematic outcomes. These are different possibilities, not a uniform promise of either success or estrangement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through shared educational influence, separation from companions, and the slow acquisition of knowledge. ABC gives this a patient, concentrated form: ideas and reason can mature in old age, while truth may bring pain and cultural outcomes may remain problematic. Shared formation and eventual separation belong to the range without an asserted causal story of exclusion ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 308; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 473).
