## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The physical appearance as an expression of the mental and emotional attitude. The man who reveals his mental and emotional disposition, who shows his attitude. Days which are very exacting concerning the disposition and attitude.

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 308, entry start; printed page not established here.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success of a human being through reason, spiritual freedom of a person, spiritual knowledge centre, personal success idea, spiritual success over the body, clear personal knowledge, clear knowledge of the body, and personal culture success

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal disposition becomes visible, alongside personal success through reason and knowledge ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Revealed mental and emotional attitude, spiritual freedom, and clear personal knowledge.
- Body/health: Physical appearance expresses disposition in Witte; ABC names knowledge of the body and “spiritual success over the body,” without specifying a medical mechanism.
- Social/relationship: The person shows an attitude to others; no particular relationship outcome is supplied.
- Events/manifestations: Exacting days concerning attitude and personal cultural success ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's “man” is preserved as source wording. ABC locator: printed p. 472, PDF p. 235, exact parser-normalized entry; no clinical inference follows from the body language.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Witte emphasizes visible disposition and its demands; ABC emphasizes reason, freedom, and personal knowledge. Visibility alone does not establish harmful overexposure.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through a person's visible mental and emotional disposition. Witte relates physical appearance to that attitude and includes days that demand much of it; ABC adds personal success through reason, freedom, cultural knowledge, and understanding of the body. Together they connect expressed disposition with personal understanding, while the body references remain source claims rather than a medical explanation ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 308; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 472).
