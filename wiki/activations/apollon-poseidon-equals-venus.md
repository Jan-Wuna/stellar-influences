## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Harmony and friendship through congeniality.

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 308, entry start; printed page not established here.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success in arts and culture, successful peace ideas, successful artistic knowledge, peace through culture freedom, spread of a peace culture, meaningful love experience, and preference for spiritual knowledge

Translation check: `Kunst-Wissen` means artistic knowledge, not “artificial knowledge.” Wording and Venus glyph checked on the scan: printed p. 472, one-based PDF p. 235, exact entry.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Congenial harmony and friendship, expressed through culture, art, and peace ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Preference for spiritual knowledge and a meaningful experience of love ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Body/health: Neither entry establishes a bodily or health meaning.
- Social/relationship: Friendship through congeniality and the spread of a culture of peace.
- Events/manifestations: Success in arts and culture, artistic knowledge, and peace ideas ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: The evidence describes affinity without asserting that harmony depends on avoiding disagreement.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. ABC gives artistic, cultural, and peace-related forms to the harmony stated by Witte; neither supplies the former warning about selective sweetness.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair as harmony and friendship through congeniality. ABC carries that affinity into artistic knowledge, cultural success, peace ideas, and a preference for spiritual knowledge; meaningful love and the spread of a culture of peace belong to the same supportive range. The entries connect shared understanding with affection and culture without making sameness a condition of harmony ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 308; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 472).
