## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The dominating world disposition (view). The general mental and emotional attitude.

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 308, entry start; printed page not established here.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful ideas for the public, intellectual freedom in public life, cultural knowledge in the public, public culture success, economic knowledge in the public, and increasing awareness of the public

Translation check: the scan reads `Erfolgreiche Ideen` and `Kultur-Wissen in der Öffentlichkeit`; OCR debris was removed from the English. Wording and Vernal Point glyph checked on printed p. 472, one-based PDF p. 235, exact entry.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A general or prevailing outlook and the public circulation of ideas and knowledge ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: General mental and emotional attitude, and increasing public awareness.
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- Social/relationship: Intellectual freedom in public life and cultural knowledge in the public sphere ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Events/manifestations: Successful public ideas, public cultural success, and economic knowledge.
- Conflicts/notes: A prevailing view is described; the entries do not assert that individuals lose critical judgment.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Witte names the general world disposition; ABC emphasizes knowledge and freedom in public life. Neither entry establishes ideological coercion.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the pair at the level of a general outlook and public life. The prevailing mental and emotional attitude in Witte connects with ABC's successful public ideas, intellectual freedom, cultural knowledge, and growing public awareness. Economic knowledge is a further stated public application; coercion or loss of critical judgment is not established by these entries ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 308; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 472).
