## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To gain great success through colleagues and comrades. To be noted or to create a sensation.

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 308, entry start; printed page not established here.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

experiences of influence through reason, successful cultural power, successful influence of an idea, force of the knowing mind, force through increasing insight, experiences of power through truth, increase of spiritual force, and much spiritual energy

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Strong success and influence through associates, ideas, and knowledge ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Reason, increasing insight, and spiritual energy are named sources of force in [ABC](#abc-entry).
- Body/health: “Spiritual energy” does not establish a bodily or medical outcome.
- Social/relationship: Great success through colleagues and comrades ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- Events/manifestations: Being noted, creating a sensation, cultural influence, and the successful influence of an idea.
- Conflicts/notes: Power is explicit, but coercion or ideological pressure is not. ABC locator: printed p. 473, PDF p. 235, exact parser-normalized entry.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Witte emphasizes conspicuous success through associates; ABC locates influence in reason, truth, and knowledge. Notoriety in a negative sense is not established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through the strength of success and influence. Colleagues and comrades contribute to conspicuous achievement in Witte; ABC develops the power of an idea, cultural influence, and force gained through reason, truth, and increasing insight. The unifying theme is effective association and knowledge, with recognition or a sensation as possible manifestations rather than an asserted pattern of coercion ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 308; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 473).
