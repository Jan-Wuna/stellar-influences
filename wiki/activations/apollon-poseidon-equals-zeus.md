## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be a leader through one’s activity with similar minded people.

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 308, entry start; printed page not established here.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Efforts for knowledge of truth, successful intellectual leadership, successful shaping of ideas, successful cultural achievements, experiencing spiritual fire, clear leadership goals, creative spiritual or intellectual study, and increasing striving for understanding

Translation check: `geistige Führung`, `Führungs-Ziele`, and `Erkenntnis-Streben` support leadership, leadership goals, and striving for understanding; the English “mental movement,” “kl are goal building,” and “cognition strike” were damaged. Wording and Zeus glyph checked on the scan: printed p. 473, one-based PDF p. 235, exact entry.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Leadership through activity with like-minded people, shaping ideas and cultural achievement ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Effort toward truth, spiritual fire, and increasing striving for understanding ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a health interpretation; “spiritual fire” is retained as the source's image.
- Social/relationship: Leadership arises through activity with similarly minded people ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- Events/manifestations: Successful idea formation, clear leadership goals, and cultural achievements ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: The entries support direction and leadership without asserting dominance over followers.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Both entries include leadership; ABC extends it to creative work, cultural achievement, and pursuit of understanding. An inevitable loss of fellowship is unsupported.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Poseidon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair through active leadership among like-minded people. The activity takes intellectual and cultural form in ABC: shaping ideas, clarifying leadership goals, achieving cultural results, and striving for knowledge of truth. Its image of spiritual fire adds intensity to that creative effort, without establishing that leadership must undermine fellowship ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 308; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 473).
