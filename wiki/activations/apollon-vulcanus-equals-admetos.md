## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 307, entry start.

Strongest success on a small scale.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed p. 471, one-based PDF p. 234, exact row; glyph and paragraph checked on the scan.

Blocked energy trade, influence through thorough knowledge, restriction of energy quantities, violent suppression of freedom, thorough influence through knowledge, increase of strength through perseverance, strong spread of depression, and much resistance to violence

Translation repair: grild knowledge and grild influence were repaired from gründliches Wissen and gründlicher Einfluß; Kraft-Vermehrung is increase of strength. Checked against the scan at the locator above.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Strong success within a small scope, alongside restriction and perseverance ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Thorough knowledge and endurance can sustain influence; ABC also names suppression and resistance.
- Body/health: ABC's spread of depression is a historical source claim, not a diagnosis or prediction.
- Social/relationship: Violent suppression of freedom and resistance to violence are explicit in ABC.
- Events/manifestations: Blocked energy trade, restricted quantities, and strength increased through perseverance.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's small-scale success does not erase ABC's adverse political and medical language.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte emphasizes strong results at small scale; ABC adds both endurance and severe restriction. These are distinct emphases within the entries.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies `Apollon/Vulcanus = Admetos` through a restricted scope in which strong results remain possible. Witte's small-scale success connects with ABC's thorough knowledge and strength gained through perseverance. ABC also gives the restriction a harsher range: blocked energy trade, curtailed quantities, suppression of freedom, and resistance to violence. Its depression claim remains historically attributed rather than a clinical conclusion ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 471).
