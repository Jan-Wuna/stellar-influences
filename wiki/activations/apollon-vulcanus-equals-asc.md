## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 307, entry start.

To have success with or through others. Acquaintance with successful persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed p. 470, one-based PDF p. 234, exact row; glyph and paragraph checked on the scan.

On-site energy trade, on-site energy quantities, expansion of the partner's influence, experiences of violence in the environment, enormous success with the partner, increase of strength through partners, expansion of power at the place, much power at the place, and increasing violence at the place

Translation repair: energy handcl was repaired from Energie-Handel; joined English words were separated and Kraft-Vermehrung rendered increase of strength. Checked against the scan at the locator above.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Success with or through others, and strong local or partner influence ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Neither entry specifies an inner attitude or motive.
- Body/health: ABC includes environmental violence but no particular injury or disease.
- Social/relationship: Acquaintance with successful people, success with a partner, and strength through partners.
- Events/manifestations: Energy trade and quantities at a place, expansion of local power, and increasing violence.
- Conflicts/notes: The partner/local setting is explicit; dependence on status is not established.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Witte's successful associations are compatible with ABC's partner success, while ABC additionally records local violence; success does not negate that adverse possibility.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Asc`, Asc specifies the pair through other people and the immediate setting. Success comes with or through others, including successful acquaintances or a partner, while ABC names the partner's expanding influence and added strength. The same entry includes local energy trade and power, together with violence in the environment; these concrete adverse possibilities remain alongside the successful associations ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 470).
