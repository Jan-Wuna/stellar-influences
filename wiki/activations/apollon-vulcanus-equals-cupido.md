## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Success and influence achieved collectively, including economic and artistic activity ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Neither entry provides a particular psychological trait.
- Body/health: ABC mentions violence in a community without a medical consequence.
- Social/relationship: Joint endeavors, social prosperity, an economic community, and a trading community.
- Events/manifestations: Great artistic success, shared energy trade, and expansion of collective power.
- Conflicts/notes: The scan supports an economic-community passage, not the unrelated United Nations wording previously stored here.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Witte stresses shared prosperity and art; ABC adds economic influence, collective power, and violence within the community. The adverse clause should remain visible without being made inevitable.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through collective endeavor. Witte describes social prosperity and artistic success achieved with others; ABC gives the collective a commercial form, with an economic or trading community sharing energy trade, strength, and power. The community can also experience violence, an explicit adverse clause that prevents shared success from becoming the only reading ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 471).
