## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 307, entry start.

To be unsuccessful, defeats and failures. Must accept a bad break or blow of fate. To be defenseless against the evil deeds of others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed p. 471, one-based PDF p. 234, exact row; glyph and paragraph checked on the scan.

Expansion of dangerous violence, influential experiences of the past, dangerous expansion of influence, power through secret knowledge, inadequate experience of power, hidden energy trade, hidden increase of power, and increasing violent crime

Translation repair: unified experiences was repaired from einflußreiche Erfahrungen; the broken crime inalitat from Gewalt-Kriminalität. Checked against the scan at the locator above.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Failure and vulnerability alongside dangerous or hidden forms of influence ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Witte describes defenselessness; ABC includes influential experiences of the past and secret knowledge.
- Body/health: Violence is explicit, but no specific bodily or medical outcome is supplied.
- Social/relationship: Defenselessness against others' evil deeds and dangerous expansion of influence.
- Events/manifestations: Defeat, failure, a bad break, hidden energy trade, and increasing violent crime.
- Conflicts/notes: These are adverse source claims; humiliation and an earlier promised success are not stated.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Both entries contain adverse outcomes, though ABC also names hidden power and knowledge rather than only helplessness.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through failed success and dangerous influence. Witte describes defeats, hard breaks, and defenselessness against others' harmful deeds; ABC adds expanding violence, concealed energy trade, secret knowledge, and violent crime. The range includes both vulnerability to harm and hidden means of power, without requiring a preceding period of prosperity ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 471).
