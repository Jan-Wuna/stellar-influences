## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Great fortune, prosperity, wealth, and successful financial influence ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Joy over increasing energy is explicit in ABC; overconfidence is not.
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- Social/relationship: Money and scientific influence are named, without a particular relationship outcome.
- Events/manifestations: Successful energy trade, expansion of power, legal success, and growing financial influence.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries describe favorable results without asserting excess or eventual reversal.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident in these entries. ABC specifies financial, commercial, scientific, and legal forms of Witte's broad prosperity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair as substantial prosperity and successful influence. Witte names fortune, wealth, and success; ABC makes these concrete through abundant money, growing financial influence, successful energy trade, scientific influence, and legal success. Joy over increasing energy accompanies this favorable range ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 470).
