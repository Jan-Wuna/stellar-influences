## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 307, entry start.

Strongest success in one’s activities. Prosperity and wealth through ones activity. Successful through interfering and being aggressive.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed p. 470, one-based PDF p. 234, exact row; glyph and paragraph checked on the scan.

Work in the energy trade, influence expansion through activity, influential activity of science, powerful expansion of activity, strong work success, strong success impulses, activity with energy quantities, and increasing violence activity

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Powerful activity associated with work success, prosperity, and expanded influence ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Aggressiveness and interference are explicitly named by Witte.
- Body/health: ABC includes violent activity without specifying an injury or disease.
- Social/relationship: Success through interference is a source claim about dealings with others, not advice.
- Events/manifestations: Energy-trade work, scientific activity, expanded work, wealth, and increasing violent activity.
- Conflicts/notes: Backlash and wasted effort are not stated outcomes; aggression and violence already provide the sourced adverse range.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Both entries connect activity with strong results; their aggression and violence clauses remain alongside the success language.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through forceful activity and its results. Witte connects work with prosperity and even success through interference or aggression; ABC adds work in energy trade, influential scientific activity, powerful expansion of work, and strong impulses toward success. Its increasing violent activity is an explicit adverse manifestation, distinct from ordinary productive effort ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 470).
