## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal success, influence, and exercise of power ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC explicitly includes strength of soul, personal energy, and emotional violence.
- Body/health: No clinical condition is specified by the psychological force/violence language.
- Social/relationship: Personal influence on the economy is named; no particular relationship is supplied.
- Events/manifestations: Personal success, increasing energy and strength, and exercise of power.
- Conflicts/notes: The first-person ABC wording does not establish a self-worth mechanism or a career-specific rule.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Witte stresses personal effectiveness; ABC includes inner strength as well as emotional violence. These do not prove that every success is psychologically harmful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = MC`, MC specifies the pair personally: success, great influence, and the exercise of power. ABC links this with influence on the economy and the felt increase of energy and strength. Its account of a strong soul also includes emotional violence, so inner strength and harmful psychological force remain distinct parts of the entry rather than being collapsed into ambition or a theory of self-worth ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 470).
