## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Thoughts that reach their mark and communication with influence ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Successful power of thought and influence through thinking are explicit.
- Body/health: Youthful energy is named without a bodily or health outcome.
- Social/relationship: Being understood and expanding influence through conversation.
- Events/manifestations: Media influence, reports of violence, and increasing youth violence in ABC.
- Conflicts/notes: The entry does not equate being understood with dogmatism; violence reporting and youth violence are separate stated applications.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Witte emphasizes apt thought and understanding; ABC extends this to thought, conversation, media, and adverse youth-related material.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through thought and communication that take effect: opinions reach their mark and the person is understood. ABC extends that effectiveness through conversation, the power of thought, and broad media influence. It separately includes reports of violence, abundant youthful energy, and increasing youth violence; those concrete applications should remain visible without turning all persuasive speech into aggression ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 470).
