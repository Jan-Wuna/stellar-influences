## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Prosperity and expanding influence of a public or people, with gendered examples of women's success ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names a woman's energy without a general psychological profile.
- Body/health: Population growth is stated by Witte; no health outcome is established.
- Social/relationship: A prosperous people, public success, and the source's examples of successful women.
- Events/manifestations: Energy trade, scientific and commercial influence, population spread, and experiences of violence.
- Conflicts/notes: Gendered formulations remain attributed examples; neither entry asserts that growth outruns humane balance.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Public prosperity and women's success are shared emphases; ABC also records collective experiences of violence.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through the success and widening influence of persons, a public, or a people. Witte includes prosperity and population spread; ABC develops scientific influence, commercial power, energy trade, and a people's expanding power. Its references to a woman's energy and success retain the source's gendered framing, while collective experiences of violence remain an additional adverse possibility ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 470).
