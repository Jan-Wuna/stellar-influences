#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed p. 471, one-based PDF p. 234, exact row checked on the scan.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Deteriorating prosperity and uncertain influence, alongside ABC's specific successes with water and air ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names sensitivity and disappointing experiences of violence.
- Body/health: Plagues and infection are historical Witte claims, not diagnoses.
- Social/relationship: Corruption is explicit; no particular relationship outcome is given.
- Events/manifestations: Inflation, destruction of prosperity, uncertain energy trade, and success with water power.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's water/air successes qualify an exclusively adverse reading; invisible decay and concealed causes are not stated.

## Contradictions

Witte's entry is adverse, while ABC also names successful water and air applications. The entries establish a mixed range rather than a single universal outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the axis through prosperity that deteriorates or becomes uncertain: inflation, corruption, and destruction of prosperity are explicit in Witte. ABC adds uncertain energy trade and disappointing experiences of violence, but also influence through sensitivity and concrete successes with water and air power. These favorable applications remain alongside the adverse range; Witte's plague and infection language remains a historical claim ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 471).
