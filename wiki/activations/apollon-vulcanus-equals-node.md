#### Witte Entry

Success through connections. To be right and well connected.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed p. 470, one-based PDF p. 234, exact row checked on the scan.

Influential scientific contacts, connected with energy quantities, connection to energy trade, expansion of power connections, strong success in contacts, much power through connections, and increases in violence in connections.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Success and power through connections ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Neither entry specifies a motive or psychological trait.
- Body/health: Violence in connections is named without a particular medical consequence.
- Social/relationship: Well-placed connections, influential scientific contacts, and expanding power connections.
- Events/manifestations: Success through contacts and links to energy quantities or energy trade.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC includes increasing violence within connections; opportunistic dependency is not established.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Successful connections are common to both entries; ABC additionally supplies scientific, commercial, and violent applications.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Node`, Node specifies the axis through effective connections: being well connected is associated with success, and ABC describes influential scientific contacts and expanding connections of power. Energy trade and energy quantities give those contacts concrete commercial applications. ABC also names increasing violence in connections, retaining an adverse relational possibility alongside successful association ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 470).
