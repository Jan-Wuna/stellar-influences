## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 307, exact row checked on the scan.

Preparation for a great success. To be prepared for a prosperous development.

Transcription repair: the leading E was part of the Pluto glyph, not the sentence; checked on the scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed p. 471, one-based PDF p. 234, exact row checked on the scan.

Develop influential knowledge, successful development of energy, develop fullness of power, extensive unfolding of energy, increased unfolding of strength, growing spread of violence, growing success of power, and unfold much influence.

Translation repair: Kraft-Entfaltung is unfolding of strength; Gewalt-Ausdehnung is spread of violence, distinct from Kraft. Checked against the scan at the locator above.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Preparation for success and the development of energy, knowledge, and influence ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Readiness for prosperous development is explicit; a strategic motive is not specified.
- Body/health: Neither entry gives a specific bodily or health claim.
- Social/relationship: Growing power and influence are named without a particular relationship outcome.
- Events/manifestations: Preparation for success, unfolding energy and strength, and growing spread of violence.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC distinguishes developing strength from expanding violence; an uncontrollable pressure cycle is not stated.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte stresses preparation; ABC describes development, including both successful influence and expanding violence.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the axis through preparation and unfolding development. Readiness for great success connects with developing influential knowledge, energy, strength, and power. ABC also explicitly includes a growing spread of violence, so expanding strength and successful development do not exhaust the passage's range ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 471).
