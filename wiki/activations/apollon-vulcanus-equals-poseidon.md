#### Witte Entry

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 307, exact row checked on the scan.

Great success in the mental realm. Successful philosophical conception of the world. Spreading a philosophy of life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed p. 471, one-based PDF p. 234, exact row checked on the scan.

Influence through spiritual knowledge, expansion of influence through reason, successful cultural influence, insights into increasing violence, experiencing mental violence, power of spiritual science (Geistes-Wissenschaft), power through knowledge of truth, and strong spiritual success.

Translation repair: Kultur-Einfluß and Geistes-Wissenschaft were damaged as cultureInfluence.8 and mindScience; the source compounds are rendered cultural influence and spiritual science. The entry does not define the scope of Geistes-Wissenschaft. Checked against the scan at the locator above.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Successful thought, philosophy, and influence through knowledge ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Reason, insight, and the source's experience of mental violence.
- Body/health: Neither entry names a bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: Spreading a philosophy of life and successful cultural influence.
- Events/manifestations: Mental or spiritual success, cultural influence, power through knowledge of truth, and insight into increasing violence.
- Conflicts/notes: Mental violence is explicit; a theory of ideological domination or lost humility is not supplied.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC's reason, culture, and spiritual knowledge extend the philosophical emphasis while also including mental violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the axis through successful thought and a philosophy capable of spreading. ABC connects this with reason, cultural influence, spiritual knowledge, and knowledge of truth. Its additional references to insight into increasing violence and experiencing mental violence preserve the adverse range without turning philosophical success itself into a claim of ideological domination ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 307; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 471).
