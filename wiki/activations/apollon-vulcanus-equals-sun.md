## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 307, Sun row.

The successful man. The man who has power and influence, who experiences an increase in fortune. Days of prosperity and success.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 470 (one-based PDF p. 234), Sun row.

Energy trading center, expansion of a man's influence, a person's experiences of violence, tremendous personal success, personal quantity of energy, personal influence through knowledge, expansion of personal power, and increasing bodily strength.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal success, growing fortune, and expanded power or influence.
- Psychology: No specific motive or personality trait is stated.
- Body/health: ABC names increasing bodily strength and experiences of violence, without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Personal influence through knowledge; no specific relationship outcome is stated.
- Events/manifestations: Days of prosperity and success, an energy trading center, and expansion of personal power.
- Conflicts/notes: Personal success and experiences of violence both appear. The sources' gendered man wording is retained as attribution, without making it an exclusive application.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in personal success and influence. ABC adds concrete energy, knowledge, bodily-strength, and violence expressions; it does not establish self-inflation or a need for continual triumph as their cause.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through personal success and an increase in fortune, power, or influence. Knowledge gives influence a particular basis, while energy trading and bodily strength make the range concrete. ABC also names a person's experiences of violence; the entries do not explain how that adverse possibility relates to the prosperous one. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
