## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 307, Uranus row; the two adjacent symbols are Uranus glyph variants.

To harbor great hopes and expectations. Passing success.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 471 (one-based PDF p. 234), Uranus row.

Exciting energy trade, influence through new knowledge, tremendous success of technology, new strength through knowledge, new quantities of energy, sudden experiences of violence, strength of a new science, and surprising expansion of power.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Great expectations and passing success, with new knowledge, technology, energy, and unexpectedly expanded power.
- Psychology: Great hopes and expectations; ABC names excitement in energy trade.
- Body/health: Sudden experiences of violence are named without a specific injury or diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: No specific interpersonal outcome is stated.
- Events/manifestations: Technological success, a new science, new energy quantities, and surprising expansion of power.
- Conflicts/notes: Passing success gives Witte's result a duration limit. Overconfidence and a subsequent loss are not stated consequences.

## Contradictions

Witte limits success as passing; ABC adds novel and surprising applications without saying how long they last. This does not establish an alternating cycle of breakthrough and loss.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through great expectations and success that Witte calls passing. New knowledge and technology supply particular ways influence or strength can increase, including energy trade, new energy quantities, and a new science. ABC's surprising expansion of power also sits beside sudden experiences of violence; the entries give no sequence connecting these possibilities.
