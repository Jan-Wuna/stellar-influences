## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 307, Venus row.

To have a great power of attraction. Great success in love or friendship. Peace, prosperity, great joy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 470 (one-based PDF p. 234), Venus row.

Influential art trade, successful power of love, expansion of the influence of peace, joy over increasing influence, experiences of violence in love, tremendous success in love, strong success through beauty, and extensive influence of harmony.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Strong attraction, success in love or friendship, and the influence of beauty, art, peace, and harmony.
- Psychology: Great joy and joy over increasing influence.
- Body/health: ABC names experiences of violence in love without a specific bodily condition or diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Attraction and success in love or friendship; violence in love remains a separate adverse possibility.
- Events/manifestations: Influential art trade, success through beauty, prosperity, and expanded influence of peace or harmony.
- Conflicts/notes: Attraction, harmonious success, and experiences of violence are not explained as one inevitable sequence. Possessiveness or dependence on admiration is not stated.

## Contradictions

The favorable love, friendship, peace, and harmony descriptions stand beside ABC's experiences of violence in love. The entry gives no conditions reconciling or selecting those possibilities; the adverse clause must not be erased by the favorable emphasis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through strong attraction and success in love, friendship, beauty, or art. Joy and prosperity accompany an influence expressed through peace and harmony, with art trade as a concrete commercial application. ABC also retains experiences of violence in love; the passage does not explain their relation to the successful, harmonious range. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
