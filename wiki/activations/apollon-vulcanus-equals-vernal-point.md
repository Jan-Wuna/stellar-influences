## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 307, Vernal Point row.

Great general success. Prosperity and wealth in general. The growth of the general public’s wealth.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 470 (one-based PDF p. 234), Vernal Point row.

Influence of science in public, expansion of influence in public life, experiences of violence in public, tremendous public success, public commercial power, public trade in energy, and strong success in public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: General success, public prosperity, and expanded influence in public life.
- Psychology: No specific psychological trait or motive is stated.
- Body/health: Public experiences of violence are named without a specific medical consequence.
- Social/relationship: The growth of the general public's wealth; public influence of science and commerce.
- Events/manifestations: Public success, commercial power, and public energy trading.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries do not describe the distribution or durability of general wealth. Public violence remains alongside the prosperity claims.

## Contradictions

Both entries place success in a general or public setting. ABC also names experiences of violence; neither passage explains that relation or makes an inflationary or ethical imbalance a necessary result of prosperity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the pair through general prosperity and the growth of public wealth. Expanded influence takes concrete public forms in science, commercial power, and energy trading. ABC also includes public experiences of violence, retaining an adverse expression whose relation to the prosperous range is not specified. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
