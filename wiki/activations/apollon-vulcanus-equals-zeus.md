## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 307, Zeus row.

To overcome a depression. To struggle successfully to the top.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 471 (one-based PDF p. 234), Zeus row.

Strenuous energy trade, successful leadership power, fiery quantity of energy, leading commercial power, leadership influence of the economy, creative power of knowledge, strong leadership success, increased energetic achievements, and increasing capacity for achievement.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Overcoming a depression and achieving a successful rise, with leadership, creative knowledge, and increased achievement.
- Psychology: Witte describes a successful struggle; no combative motive is specified.
- Body/health: Witte's depression is undefined here and does not establish a medical diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Successful leadership and the leading influence of economic or commercial power.
- Events/manifestations: Strenuous energy trading, fiery energy, creative power of knowledge, and increasing energetic achievement or capacity.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's leading commercial power and economic leadership clause are restored. Struggle does not establish resource exhaustion or interpersonal conflict.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize successful effort, leadership, and achievement, with strenuous activity also explicit. Witte does not specify whether depression is emotional, economic, or another condition, and the page retains that limit.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Apollon/Vulcanus = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair through successful effort toward an improved position: Witte describes overcoming a depression and struggling to the top. ABC gives this effort particular forms in leadership, commercial and economic influence, creative knowledge, and increasing energetic achievement. Energy trading can be strenuous, but the entry does not make conflict or exhaustion a necessary part of success; Witte's undefined depression remains an attributed source phrase.
