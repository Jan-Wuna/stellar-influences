## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Vulcanus = Zeus`: determined ascent, energetic recovery, and forceful pursuit of improvement under pressure. Main caution: combative ambition: the climb can become so driven that it burns resources, intensifies conflict, or treats every obstacle as a battle.
