## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Apollon row.

Many people have a feeling of inferiority and look up to the native.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 133 (one-based PDF p. 65), Apollon row.

Expansion of a blockade of the surroundings, restricted freedom in the surroundings, consolidation of the partner's experiences, the partner's thorough success, deepening the partner's experiences, increased depressions in the surroundings, deepening knowledge of the surroundings, and a growing blockade of the surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Broader or growing restrictions in the surroundings, alongside consolidated experience, deeper knowledge, and partner success.
- Psychology: Witte attributes feelings of inferiority to many people who look up to the native; ABC names increased depressions in the surroundings.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition or diagnosis is stated.
- Social/relationship: Many people look up to the native; the partner's experience and success are also represented.
- Events/manifestations: Expanded or growing blockades, restricted freedom, consolidated experiences, and deeper knowledge of the surroundings.
- Conflicts/notes: Increased depressions, rather than deepening depression, is the scan's wording. Witte describes others' feelings without establishing actual inferiority or envy.

## Contradictions

Restrictions and depressed surroundings coexist in ABC's range with partner success and deepened knowledge. Witte's social comparison does not explain those outcomes or establish a cause of the restrictions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through effects extending across the surroundings and other people's experience. Witte describes many people looking up to the native while feeling inferior. ABC adds expanded restrictions and increased depressions in the surroundings, but also consolidated partner experience, deeper knowledge, and partner success. These are distinct possibilities; the entries do not explain the restrictions as a result of social comparison. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
