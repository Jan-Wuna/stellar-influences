#### Witte Entry

The parental home. The place of the family. Art located at a special place. Others are envious. Others feel inferior in the family, in a community or in exercising art.

Community of the environment, restriction of a community by another, first partner community, consolidated local community, joint consolidation of the environment, environmental restriction for of a community, and conclusion of a community in the place

## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Admetos = Cupido`: rooted family structures, artistic or communal life tied to a specific place, and strong belonging mixed with rank or inferiority feelings. Main caution: stifling enclosure: home or group bonds can become narrow, hierarchical, or quietly oppressive.
