## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Jupiter row.

To acclimatize oneself fast. Fortunate home surroundings, to find one’s luck and happiness there. Other people are envious. Rivalry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 132 (one-based PDF p. 65), Jupiter row.

Restricted local finances, consolidation of rights in the surroundings, financial restriction by others, a thorough legal partner, partner success through endurance, restriction of rights in the surroundings, and deepening happiness with the partner.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Finding happiness in home surroundings and with a partner, alongside financial or legal limits involving the surroundings and others.
- Psychology: Quick acclimatization; Witte also attributes envy to others.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition or diagnosis is stated.
- Social/relationship: Rivalry, partner success through endurance, deepening happiness with a partner, and a thorough legal partner.
- Events/manifestations: Fortunate home surroundings, restricted local finances, financial restriction by others, and consolidated or restricted rights.
- Conflicts/notes: Glück is happiness, not glitz. Umwelt-Recht is retained as rights in the surroundings, without assuming a specialized environmental-law claim.

## Contradictions

Happiness at home or with a partner stands alongside restricted finances and rights. The entries give no conditions selecting among those outcomes and do not make comfort the cause of envy or rivalry.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through quickly finding one's footing and happiness in home surroundings, with partner success through endurance and deepened shared happiness as related expressions. The favorable setting remains qualified by restricted local finances, limits imposed by others, and restrictions on rights. Witte also names others' envy and rivalry without making them a necessary consequence of domestic happiness. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
