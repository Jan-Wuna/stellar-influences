## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Mars row.

Unsuccessful actions of others. Others act from a feeling of inferiority.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 132 (one-based PDF p. 65), Mars row.

Others work thoroughly, enduring efforts of the partner, blocked activity in the surroundings, depressing work in the surroundings, restriction of others' efforts, earthworks at the location, and thorough work of the partner.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Other people's work and effort under limits, including both blocked activity and thorough, enduring work.
- Psychology: Witte attributes some actions to others' feelings of inferiority; ABC describes depressing work.
- Body/health: No explicit bodily condition or diagnosis is stated.
- Social/relationship: A partner's persistent efforts and thorough work; restriction of others' efforts.
- Events/manifestations: Unsuccessful actions, blocked activity, work in the surroundings, and local earthworks.
- Conflicts/notes: Gründlich means thorough, not grundly or grunding. The entries do not make all activity aggressive or futile.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes unsuccessful actions and an attributed inferiority motive. ABC also names thorough work and endurance, so the range cannot be reduced to wasted effort; no conditions selecting success are supplied.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through the work and actions of others, especially a partner. Their efforts may be persistent and thorough, or become restricted, blocked, or unsuccessful; earthworks give the activity a concrete local expression. Witte's claim that others act from feelings of inferiority remains attributed, without turning every form of effort into aggression or conflict.
