## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Mercury row.

Unable to follow the thoughts of the native. A lack of logic. Thoughts, opinions, talks about the residence or the place of stay.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 132 (one-based PDF p. 65), Mercury row.

Endurance of youth in the surroundings, reports about restricted surroundings, blocked conversations with the partner, depressed youth in the surroundings, consolidating conversations with the partner, the partner's thorough opinion, and traffic congestion at the location.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Thought and communication concerning residence or restricted surroundings, including both blocked and consolidating partner conversation.
- Psychology: Difficulty following the native's thoughts, Witte's unassigned lack of logic, and ABC's depressed youth or thorough partner opinion.
- Body/health: Youth endurance and depressed youth are source descriptions, without a specific bodily condition or diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Blocked or consolidating conversations with a partner.
- Events/manifestations: Residence-related thought and discussion, reports of restricted surroundings, and local traffic congestion.
- Conflicts/notes: Gründliche Meinung is a thorough opinion, not a Greek opinion; Partner-Gespräche are conversations, not language. Witte does not specify whose logic is lacking.

## Contradictions

Blocked and consolidating partner conversations are alternatives, while thorough opinion prevents reducing the entire entry to mental narrowness. The passages do not state that conversations must be repetitive or that all misunderstanding arises from the native's logic.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through thoughts, opinions, and conversation about where one lives or stays. Reports of restricted surroundings and local traffic congestion give these concerns a practical setting. Partner conversations may be blocked or consolidating, and the partner's opinion can be thorough; Witte also names difficulty following the native's thoughts without explaining every communication problem as mental narrowness. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
