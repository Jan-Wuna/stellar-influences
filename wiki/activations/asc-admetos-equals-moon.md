## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Moon row.

To come into the public through superiority. The settled populace. Country people. The domicile. The territory of a nation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 132 (one-based PDF p. 65), Moon row.

Blocked surroundings of a woman, consolidation of the partner's feelings, restriction of others' feelings, a thorough female partner, congestion in the hour at the location, deep feelings of the partner, and a blockade of a people's surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Settled residence, population, or territory, alongside emotional consolidation and restrictions involving others.
- Psychology: Consolidated or deep partner feelings and restricted feelings of others; Witte's superiority wording remains attributed.
- Body/health: No explicit bodily condition or diagnosis is stated.
- Social/relationship: A thorough female partner and the partner's feelings; women and a people appear in ABC's blocked-surroundings examples.
- Events/manifestations: Settled populace, country people, domicile, national territory, local congestion, and blocked surroundings.
- Conflicts/notes: Restriction of others' feelings and a thorough female partner are separate ABC clauses. The entries do not establish provincialism or a general inferiority pattern.

## Contradictions

Settlement and emotional consolidation occur alongside congestion and restriction. The entries do not give a common cause or establish that attachment to a place narrows perspective.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through settled population and residence, from a domicile or country community to a nation's territory. ABC brings the same surrounding context into a woman's circumstances and the feelings of a partner: feelings may consolidate or deepen, while others' feelings or the surroundings can be restricted. Local congestion is another concrete expression; the entry does not make settlement itself a cause of emotional restriction. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
