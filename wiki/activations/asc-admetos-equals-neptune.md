## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Neptune row.

To have the wrong impression about a place. Others hide their inferiority. Giving up the residence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 133 (one-based PDF p. 65), Neptune row.

Others are restricted and uncertain, dissolution of a blockade of the surroundings, endurance of others in water, consolidation of water in the surroundings (ice), sensitivity and depth of the partner, circulatory weakness of the partner, and the partner's immersion in metaphysics.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Uncertainty about a place or others, alongside release of a surrounding blockade and depth or endurance involving a partner.
- Psychology: Wrong impression about a place, restricted and uncertain others, and partner sensitivity or depth; Witte's concealed-inferiority claim remains attributed.
- Body/health: ABC names the partner's circulatory weakness and others' endurance in water, without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: A sensitive, deep partner and the partner's immersion in metaphysics.
- Events/manifestations: Giving up residence, dissolving a blockade, endurance in water, and water consolidated as ice.
- Conflicts/notes: Ausdauer is endurance, not loss of others in water; Auflösung concerns dissolution of the blockade. The entry does not establish drowning or internal decay.

## Contradictions

Uncertainty and relinquishing residence are not the whole range: dissolution of a blockade, endurance, ice, and partner depth are also explicit. The sources give no sequence making these consequences of deterioration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through uncertainty about a place or other people, including a wrong impression and giving up residence. That uncertainty sits beside dissolution of a surrounding blockade, while endurance in water and water becoming ice provide concrete physical expressions. The partner's sensitivity, depth, and immersion in metaphysics broaden the range beyond displacement; the entry does not explain these as stages of decay. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
