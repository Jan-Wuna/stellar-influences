## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Node row.

The connection with others who are overcome by the native. The connection with the place where one stays.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 132 (one-based PDF p. 65), Node row.

Others restrict the connection, enduring contacts with others, restricted partner connection, firm connections with the surroundings, connection to a restricted partner, and connections in blocked surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Connections with a place or other people that can endure, remain firm, or be restricted.
- Psychology: No motive or psychological trait is specified.
- Body/health: No explicit bodily or health statement in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Enduring contact, restricted partner ties, a restricted partner, and Witte's connection with others overcome by the native.
- Events/manifestations: Connection to the place of stay and contacts within blocked surroundings.
- Conflicts/notes: Restriction by others and connection to a restricted partner have different agency. The entries do not establish dependency as the basis of every connection.

## Contradictions

Firm or enduring contacts coexist with restricted contacts. Witte's overcome by the native phrase is not further explained, and neither passage establishes a necessary hierarchy for all local bonds.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Node`, Node specifies the pair through connections to a place and to other people. Those ties can be firm and enduring, yet also restricted by others, involve a restricted partner, or take place within blocked surroundings. Witte adds connections with others overcome by the native, without defining that relation; the entries do not make dependency a condition of maintaining contact. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
