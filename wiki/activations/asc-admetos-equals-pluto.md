## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Pluto row. The leading E was a second Pluto glyph variant, not prose.

Changes in the place. Others begin to feel inferior.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 133 (one-based PDF p. 65), Pluto row.

Others develop endurance, blocked unfolding of the surroundings, depressing developments in the surroundings, restrictive changes in the surroundings, thorough development of the partner, changes in blocked surroundings, and growing congestion at the location.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Changes at a place or in the surroundings, alongside developing endurance, partner development, and local restrictions.
- Psychology: Witte attributes emerging feelings of inferiority to others; ABC describes depressing developments.
- Body/health: No explicit bodily condition or diagnosis is stated.
- Social/relationship: Others develop endurance and the partner develops thoroughly; others' inferiority feelings remain an attributed possibility.
- Events/manifestations: Local changes, blocked unfolding, restrictive surrounding changes, and growing congestion.
- Conflicts/notes: The removed E is a Pluto glyph artifact. Changes in a place do not by themselves establish relocation or displacement.

## Contradictions

The entries include endurance and partner development as well as blocked or depressing developments. They do not state whether change releases a restriction or perpetuates it, nor that relocation must occur.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair through changes in a place and in the people connected with it. ABC includes developing endurance and thorough partner development, alongside blocked unfolding, restrictive changes, and growing local congestion. Witte adds others beginning to feel inferior; the entries retain that psychological possibility without making every local change a displacement or a repetition of diminished status. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
