## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Poseidon row.

Others feel mentally inferior and want to be instructed by the native.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 133 (one-based PDF p. 65), Poseidon row.

Depressing truths about the surroundings, insights into primordial energies in space, consolidation of culture in the surroundings, intellectual deepening of the partner, thorough culture in the surroundings, blockage of the partner's reason, and suppressed truth about the surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Knowledge, instruction, truth, and culture involving others and the surroundings, including intellectual depth and blocked reason.
- Psychology: Others want instruction and feel mentally inferior in Witte's attributed account; ABC names depressing truths and the partner's blocked reason.
- Body/health: No explicit bodily condition or diagnosis is stated.
- Social/relationship: Others seeking instruction and the partner's intellectual deepening.
- Events/manifestations: Consolidated or thorough surrounding culture, suppressed truth, and the source's insights into primordial energies in space.
- Conflicts/notes: Unterdrückte Umwelt-Wahrheit is suppressed truth, not subordinated prosperity. Primordial energies and thorough culture are source phrases without further definition here.

## Contradictions

Intellectual deepening and desired instruction coexist with blocked reason and suppressed truth. The passages do not establish that teaching causes superiority or distance, nor do they verify the scientific content of the primordial-energy claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through instruction, intellectual depth, and the truth or culture of the surroundings. Witte describes others seeking instruction while feeling mentally inferior; that remains an account of their feelings. ABC preserves both cultural consolidation and a partner's intellectual deepening, alongside depressing or suppressed truth and blocked reason, without making teaching the cause of those restrictions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
