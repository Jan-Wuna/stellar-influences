## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Saturn row.

Others feel conscious of guilt. To feel limited and depressed through the domicile.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 133 (one-based PDF p. 65), Saturn row.

Endurance and patience of the partner, blocked and disturbed surroundings, depressing problems in the surroundings, the partner's tasks requiring thoroughness, problematic restrictions for a place, and disturbances through local congestion.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Residential or local restrictions and disturbances, alongside a partner's endurance and patience.
- Psychology: Feeling limited and depressed through the domicile; others' consciousness of guilt in Witte's account.
- Body/health: The mood descriptions do not specify a bodily condition or diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: A patient, enduring partner with tasks requiring thoroughness; others' guilt feelings remain attributed.
- Events/manifestations: Blocked or disturbed surroundings, depressing local problems, restrictions on a place, and congestion-related disturbance.
- Conflicts/notes: Gestörte means disturbed, not peripheral. The entries do not establish chronic punishment or inferiority as the cause of all restrictions.

## Contradictions

The difficult local conditions coexist with endurance, patience, and thorough tasks. Those capacities are represented without a promise that they resolve the restriction; guilt and restricted domicile are separate Witte statements.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through a place of residence or surroundings experienced as limiting, disturbed, or depressing. ABC makes the obstacles concrete in local restrictions and congestion, while also naming a partner's endurance, patience, and tasks requiring thoroughness. These capacities sit beside the difficult conditions without an assurance of release; Witte's separate guilt statement remains attributed to others. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
