## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Sun row.

To be closely joined to the place (soil or property). The native stands out from his environment, occupies a special position.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 132 (one-based PDF p. 65), Sun row.

Others block the surroundings, endurance of other people, blocked local center, a thorough male partner, a physically restricted partner, personal stabilization of the partner, and a center for stabilizing the surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Close attachment to a place or property and a distinct position there, with stabilization or blockage involving others.
- Psychology: No specific motive or personality trait is stated.
- Body/health: ABC names a physically restricted partner without a condition or diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Other people's endurance, a thorough male partner, and personal stabilization of the partner.
- Events/manifestations: Attachment to soil or property, a special local position, a blocked local center, and a center stabilizing the surroundings.
- Conflicts/notes: Gründlicher männlicher Partner means thorough male partner, not Greek partner. A special position does not by itself establish superiority or overidentification.

## Contradictions

Stabilizing and blocking the surroundings are different possibilities. The entries give no conditions selecting them and do not make attachment to property a cause of a partner's physical restriction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through close attachment to a place, soil, or property and a position that stands out within the surroundings. ABC adds other people's endurance and a partner's thoroughness or personal stabilization, alongside a physically restricted partner and blocked surroundings. The local center may be blocked or serve to stabilize the surroundings; neither role is made an inevitable result of attachment to place. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
