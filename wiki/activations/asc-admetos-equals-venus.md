## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Venus row.

To be in harmony with the place. Others feel their inferiority. Servility.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 132 (one-based PDF p. 65), Venus row.

Blockage of peaceful surroundings, depressions of the love partner, restricted harmony in the surroundings, consolidation of harmonious surroundings, a harmonious cycle in the surroundings, deep love for the partner, and deepening love for the other.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Harmony with a place and deepened love, alongside restrictions on peace or harmony.
- Psychology: ABC names the love partner's depressions; Witte attributes inferiority feelings to others and separately names servility.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition or diagnosis is stated.
- Social/relationship: Deep love for a partner and deepening love for another; servility is an attributed Witte possibility.
- Events/manifestations: Blocked peaceful surroundings, restricted or consolidated harmony, and a harmonious surrounding cycle.
- Conflicts/notes: Harmony and restriction are both represented; the sources do not establish envy or submission as the cause of pleasant surroundings.

## Contradictions

Harmony with a place and deep love stand beside depression, blockage, and servility. These are not reconciled by a claim that harmony necessarily rests on an unequal relationship; the entries leave the conditions unspecified.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through harmony with a place and love that can deepen toward a partner or another person. ABC includes both consolidation of harmonious surroundings and blocked peace or restricted harmony, so the favorable setting is one part of a mixed range. Witte also names servility and others' inferiority feelings; those attributed descriptions do not explain every instance of harmony or deep love. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
