## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be in harmony with the place. Others feel their inferiority. Servility.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blockage of a peaceful environment, depression of the love partner, restricted environmental harmony, consolidation of a harmonious environment, harmonious environmental cycle, deep love for the partner, and deepening of love for the other

## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Admetos = Venus`: aesthetic or emotional fit with a home base, local peace, and social ease shaped by quiet rank. Main caution: unequal harmony: pleasant conditions may rest on submission, envy, or unspoken inferiority rather than mutual reciprocity.
