## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Vulcanus row.

The respect and rating one has at the place. Others are told to keep within bounds. To take one’s chance in a trial of strength or showdown.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 133 (one-based PDF p. 65), Vulcanus row.

A partner depressed by violence, consolidation of the partner's energy, energy and endurance of others, restricted violence at the location, restriction of energy by others, restriction of power in the surroundings, and thorough influence of the partner.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Local standing and trials of strength, with energy or influence that can consolidate or be restricted.
- Psychology: ABC describes a partner depressed by violence; Witte names taking one's chance in a showdown without specifying a motive.
- Body/health: Violence-related depression remains a source claim without a diagnosis; energy and endurance are also named.
- Social/relationship: Others being told to keep within bounds, a partner's energy or influence, and restriction by others.
- Events/manifestations: Local respect or rating, trial of strength, restricted violence, and limits on power or energy.
- Conflicts/notes: The partner's depression through violence and consolidation of the partner's energy are separate clauses. The source does not prescribe strengthening a partner through violence.

## Contradictions

Consolidated energy and restricted energy or power coexist in the range. Witte's local standing and showdown do not establish that respect must be enforced violently or identify who wins a contest.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through local standing, boundaries set for others, and a trial of strength or showdown. ABC adds consolidated partner energy and others' endurance, alongside restrictions on violence, energy, and power. Its partner depressed by violence is a separate adverse statement, not a means of strengthening the partner; the entries give no assured winner or single explanation of local respect.
