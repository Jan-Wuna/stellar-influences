## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 96, Zeus row.

Bad performance of others. To be afraid of a start.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 133 (one-based PDF p. 65), Zeus row. The preceding grief and disadvantage clauses belong to Hades and are excluded here.

Surroundings blocked by fire, restriction of leaders' surroundings, restricted goals for the surroundings, consolidation of goals for the surroundings, thorough efforts of others, thorough performance in the surroundings, deepening the partner's goals, and restricting performance in the surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Starting, performance, and goals involving others or the surroundings, with both thorough effort and restrictions.
- Psychology: Witte names fear of a start; no inferiority motive is stated in this Zeus row.
- Body/health: Fire is a source example of blockage without a specific injury or diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Others' poor performance or thorough efforts, leaders' restricted surroundings, and deepened partner goals.
- Events/manifestations: Fire-related blockage, restricted or consolidated goals, and restricted performance in the surroundings.
- Conflicts/notes: Hades' preceding depression/grief tail is excluded. Gründliche means thorough; neither Greek services nor universal thwarted initiative appears in the source.

## Contradictions

Poor performance and fear of starting coexist with thorough efforts, consolidated goals, and deepened partner goals. The passages do not give a sequence making every attempt fail before action can begin.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Admetos = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair through starts, efforts, and goals involving others and the surroundings. Witte names others' poor performance and fear of a start, while ABC retains thorough efforts, consolidated goals, and deepened partner goals. Fire-related blockage and restrictions on leaders' surroundings or performance give the adverse range concrete forms, without making every initiative unsuccessful. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
