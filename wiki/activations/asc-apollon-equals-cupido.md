## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through a local community's expansion, success, and shared experience. Partner experience and jointly expanded space are particular expressions, with a scientific community as a further local setting. Witte's door-to-door customers and tenant supply compact examples of nearby contacts; they do not exhaust the wider communal range. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
