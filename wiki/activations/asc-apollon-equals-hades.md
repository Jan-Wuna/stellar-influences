## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 95, Hades row.

Poor or unpleasant customers. To do business with matters that are evil. Antique business or dealer in antiques.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 131 (one-based PDF p. 64), Hades row.

Successful service to a partner, dangerous freedom of the partner, dangerous experiences of the surroundings, criminal trading place, disadvantageous expansion of a place, successes in the surroundings through privations, hidden knowledge at the location, and knowledge of a place's past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Difficult or dangerous business and surrounding contacts, with partner service, success through privation, and local historical knowledge also represented.
- Psychology: No specific motive or psychological trait is stated.
- Body/health: Danger is named without a specific bodily condition or diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Poor or unpleasant customers, successful service to a partner, and dangerous partner freedom.
- Events/manifestations: Antique business, criminal trading place, disadvantageous local expansion, and hidden or historical knowledge of a place.
- Conflicts/notes: Antique dealing and historical knowledge are distinct examples; their presence does not make them criminal. Witte's evil wording remains his moral attribution.

## Contradictions

Adverse business, danger, and criminal trade stand alongside successful service and success through privations. The entries do not state that success is possible only through corruption or that all antique business belongs to the adverse range.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through business and surrounding contacts that can be poor, unpleasant, dangerous, or criminal. The range also includes successful service to a partner and success through privations, so difficulty does not erase every constructive outcome. Antique dealing and hidden or historical knowledge of a place form a further thread of contact with the past, without being equated with criminal trade.
