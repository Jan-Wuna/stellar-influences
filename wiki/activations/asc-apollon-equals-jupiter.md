## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Solvent customers. To be fortunate with business people.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Jupiter row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Experienced financial partner, successful rights in the surroundings, successful financial location, joy over successes in the surroundings, happy experience with the partner, a place of legal science, legal successes for the surroundings, and advantageous experiences of the surroundings.

Source reference: printed p. 130 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Jupiter row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Favorable business dealings and beneficial experiences with partners or the surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** The ABC entry names joy at success and happy partner experiences; it does not establish a general temperament.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Solvent customers and an experienced financial partner are the concrete relationship examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Successful dealings, financial settings, and legal or legal-scientific success occur in the entries.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's Umwelt-Recht is retained as rights in the surroundings; a specialized ecological legal field is not established by the entry.

## Contradictions

The entries do not state an opposing claim: Witte gives favorable customer relations, while ABC adds financial and legal examples. This overlap does not establish agreement on every example or a general promise of prosperity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through advantageous dealings with other people: solvent customers, an experienced financial partner, and satisfying experiences of success in the surroundings. The legal and financial examples extend the commercial theme, while the stated joy concerns those successful experiences. These are favorable examples rather than a guarantee of prosperity in every contact. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
