## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Distinguished commercial contacts, specialized knowledge, and participation in institutional or official settings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Study and knowledge are explicit; a need for prestige or superiority is not stated.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Witte names noble customers; ABC names an experienced independent partner and local authorities.
- **Events/manifestations:** Attending a technical institute, being a student, and commercial or state-related achievements are concrete examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's local hierarchy, trading place, and trade authority are distinct clauses. Independent (selbständig) does not by itself specify the partner's employment status.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize different settings—technical study and clientele in Witte, experienced partners and official or commercial surroundings in ABC. No explicit opposition is stated, and no dependence on prestige is specified.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair through distinguished customers, technical education, and dealings involving expertise or authority. Being a student and attending a technical institute sit alongside ABC's experienced independent partner, trade authority, and local hierarchy. Together these describe the settings and people through which knowledge and achievement are encountered; they do not establish social exclusiveness as a personal trait. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
