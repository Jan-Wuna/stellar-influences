## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be a known personality in the near environment. The native as a customer. To be known in the scientific world.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” MC row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My success with others, my successful partner, my knowledge of the partner, my freedom at the place, my successes with partners, my experiences of the surroundings, a place of knowledge about the soul, an experience of a partner at the level of the soul, and the partner's inner breadth.

Source reference: printed p. 130 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” MC row. Translation checked against the actual MC glyph row, including “Ort des Wissens über die Seele.”

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Personal recognition and one's own experience of success, partners, and the surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC includes inner experience and knowledge about the soul; it does not describe a divided or unstable self.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Being known locally or scientifically, being a customer, and knowing a successful partner are explicit examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Local recognition, participation in the scientific world, and personal successes with others are stated.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The ABC translation's former “scene” was corrected to “soul” from the German passage; its experiential language is not a diagnosis or a claim about life direction.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes being known and acting as a customer; ABC uses first-person language for success, freedom, and partner experience. The entries offer different emphases without an explicit opposition. Neither supports a warning about losing one's identity in reputation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = MC`, MC specifies the pair through the person's own place among others: being known in the near surroundings or scientific world, taking the role of a customer, and experiencing success with partners. ABC also gives this contact an inward dimension through knowledge about the soul and personal partner experience. Recognition and experience are the supported throughline; the entries do not turn them into a conflict between reputation and selfhood. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
