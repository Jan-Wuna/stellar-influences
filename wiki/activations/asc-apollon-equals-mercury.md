## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Business agreements. Talks with customers. To serve customers.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Mercury row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reports about successes in the surroundings, successes of young people in the surroundings, successful conversations with those around one, thoughts about the partner's freedom, partner experiences in youth, media successes for the surroundings, extensive knowledge of the surroundings, and expansion of traffic at the place.

Source reference: printed p. 130 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Mercury row. Translation checked against the actual Mercury glyph row, including “Umwelt-Gespräche” and “Medien-Erfolge.”

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Commercial agreements and service through conversation, reports, and knowledge of the surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Thoughts about a partner's freedom and knowledge of the surroundings are explicit; a transactional personality is not.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Talking with and serving customers are central; ABC also names youth and partner experience.
- **Events/manifestations:** Business agreements, reports of success, media successes, and local traffic expansion are given as examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's mixed-language “mediaErfolge” and “environmental language” were repaired from the German passage; the list does not establish that all contacts become commercial.

## Contradictions

No explicit opposition occurs. Witte concentrates on customers and agreements, while ABC adds communication, media, youth, and traffic examples. These are extensions of scope rather than evidence that the sources agree on every detail.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through communication that connects people to business or shared knowledge: agreements, conversations with customers, and customer service. Reports and media successes in the surroundings broaden that theme beyond a single transaction, while ABC separately includes youth, partner experience, and local traffic expansion. The common thread is active exchange with customers or the wider surroundings. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
