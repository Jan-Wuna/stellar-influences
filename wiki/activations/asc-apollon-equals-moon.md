## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To become known to the public. To have women as customers or clients.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Moon row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Experienced partner of a woman, a successful woman at the location, freedom of the female partner, the partner's knowledge of feelings, a people's trading place, expansion of a people's space, a woman's experience of the surroundings, many women at the location, and increasing partner feelings.

Source reference: printed p. 130 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Moon row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Public familiarity and contact with a wider clientele or population ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC names the partner's knowledge of feelings and increasing partner feelings, without specifying their direction or emotional inconsistency.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Women as clients, a woman's partner, and a female partner's freedom are the sources' historically gendered examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Becoming publicly known, a successful woman locally, and a people's trading place or territorial expansion appear in the entries.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The gendered examples are attributed source formulations, not universal rules. Gefühls-Wissen retains emotional knowledge, and Partner-Gefühle does not specify whose feelings are directed toward whom.

## Contradictions

No explicit opposition is stated. Witte's public and customer examples overlap with ABC's broader population and partner examples, but ABC's additional claims are not corroborated individually here.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through becoming familiar to a public and meeting people as clients or members of a wider population. The source examples repeatedly name women, including women customers and a locally successful woman; these remain historically gendered illustrations. ABC adds increasing partner feelings and collective trading or spatial examples, so the page concerns both personal contact and a broader public field, without establishing emotional shallowness or inconsistency. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
