## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To make contact with customers. Many customers. The representative.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Node row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of local connections, successful connections in the surroundings, successful connection to others, connected to an experienced partner, connection to a place of knowledge, connections at a trading place, and many contacts in the area.

Source reference: printed p. 130 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Node row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Numerous or expanding contacts, especially with customers, experienced partners, and places of knowledge or trade ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Neither entry establishes a personal motive for accumulating connections.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Customer contact and representative work are central; ABC adds successful connections with others.
- **Events/manifestations:** Many customers, expansion of local connections, and contact with a place of knowledge are explicit examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The number of contacts is supported; referrals, intimacy, and selectivity are not specified.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe contacts and their extension. ABC supplies additional locations and partner examples without an explicit opposing claim. Their overlap does not establish how close or enduring the contacts are.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Node`, Node specifies the pair through making and extending connections. Witte's representative and many customers give the formula a concrete commercial expression; ABC widens it to local ties, an experienced partner, and places of knowledge or trade. The unifying feature is the reach of contact itself, while the entries leave the intimacy, duration, and motives of those relationships unspecified. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
