## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Changes in customer or business connections. Transient customers.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Pluto row. The leading “B” was an alternate Pluto glyph, not prose; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful development of the partner, successful change of a place, successful development of the surroundings, successful relocation, changed knowledge of the surroundings, growing partner experiences, changing successes in the surroundings, and a changing trading place.

Source reference: printed p. 131 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Pluto row; checked on the actual scan. Veränderung eines Ortes and Orts-Wechsel are separate clauses: change of a place and relocation.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Changing business contacts, developing partnerships, and changes in place or surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Growing partner experience is explicit; restlessness and disloyalty are not.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Witte describes transient customers and changing connections; ABC includes successful partner development.
- **Events/manifestations:** Changes of location, a changing trading place, and developing surroundings are ABC examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Change may involve transient contact or successful development; neither requires continual replacement. A change of a place and relocation are distinct ABC examples; the glyph artifact has been removed.

## Contradictions

Transient customers and successful partner development describe different forms of change rather than an explicit contradiction. The entries do not establish that all change is either beneficial or destabilizing.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair through change in the people and places involved in business or partnership. Customers may be transient and connections may change, while ABC also describes successful development, accumulated partner experience, and a changed location or trading place. The common theme is an evolving field of contact; it does not establish that growth depends on disloyalty, instability, or constant replacement. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
