## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To listen. To have listeners. Scientists of oceanography (marine science) in the near surrounding.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Poseidon row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Extension of the spiritual environment, successful ideas of others, successful environmental culture, successful cultural place, spiritual freedom for the partner, spiritual knowledge in the place, cultural experiences of the partner, and spiritual experiences of the partner.

Source reference: printed p. 131 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Poseidon row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Listening and being heard, with intellectual, cultural, or spiritual contact in the surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Receptive attention and a partner's spiritual freedom or experience are named; detachment is not.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Listeners, nearby marine scientists, and a partner's cultural or spiritual experiences are the stated social examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Nearby oceanographers, successful ideas, and a successful cultural place are concrete examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Marine science is an uncommon explicit example and remains distinct from ABC's spiritual vocabulary; practical disconnection is not stated.

## Contradictions

Witte's listening and marine-science examples differ in emphasis from ABC's cultural and spiritual examples. No explicit opposition is stated, and the latter do not redefine oceanography as spiritual work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through receptive contact: listening, having listeners, and encountering knowledge in the surroundings. Witte's nearby oceanographers make scientific contact one concrete expression, while ABC adds successful ideas, cultural settings, and a partner's spiritual freedom or experience. These examples support a field of attentive intellectual, cultural, or spiritual exchange without making them interchangeable or implying a lack of practical contact. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
