## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Separation from customers. Business connections are severed. To have students. Interruption of schooling.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Saturn row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Spread of problems in the surroundings, an experienced partner in old age, experiences of patience with the partner, concentration on knowledge of the surroundings, painful partner experiences, extensive tasks in the surroundings, and increased losses in the surroundings.

Source reference: printed p. 131 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Saturn row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Interrupted or severed contacts, with demanding experience in partnership, surroundings, or education ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC names patience, concentration on knowledge, and painful partner experience; withdrawal as a trait is not established.
- **Body/health:** Painful experience is not identified as a bodily condition; no specific health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Separation from customers and an experienced partner in old age are explicit examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Severed business connections, interruption of schooling, having students, and extensive tasks or losses are stated.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Having students is retained as a distinct educational example; the entries do not require every relationship to contract.

## Contradictions

The entries share interruption, difficulty, or demanding experience, but also contain educational and experienced-partner examples. Having students and interruption of schooling are separate possibilities, not a stated contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through contacts or learning that meet interruption and demanding experience. Customer relationships may be severed and schooling interrupted; ABC adds extensive tasks, losses, patience, and painful partner experiences. The same material also includes having students and an experienced partner in old age, so the formula's educational and relational range should not be reduced to simple isolation. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
