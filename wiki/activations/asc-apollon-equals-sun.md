## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be familiar to many by appearance. To obtain honor and success through acquaintances.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Sun row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Experienced personal partner, success of other persons, successful environmental center, freedom of the male partner, personal expansion at the location, personal environmental experiences, personal commercial space, and a scientific center at the location.

Source reference: printed p. 130 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Sun row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Personal familiarity and success through acquaintances, with successful people or centers in the surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** The entries describe recognition and experience rather than a need for social validation.
- **Body/health:** Appearance is a recognition cue in Witte, not a health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Acquaintances support honor and success; ABC adds an experienced personal partner and the historically gendered example of a male partner's freedom.
- **Events/manifestations:** Becoming familiar to many, honor through acquaintances, a local scientific center, and personal commercial space are explicit examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Familiarity by appearance does not necessarily mean popularity or approval; the male-partner example remains source-attributed.

## Contradictions

The entries do not state an opposing claim. Witte emphasizes personal recognition and benefit through acquaintances, while ABC adds partners and local settings; those additions are not independently confirmed on this page.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through a recognizable personal presence and success obtained through acquaintances. Being familiar to many by appearance is the concrete form of recognition in Witte; ABC places personal experience alongside successful others, an experienced partner, and local commercial or scientific settings. The entries concern personal participation and recognition among others, without establishing dependence on approval. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
