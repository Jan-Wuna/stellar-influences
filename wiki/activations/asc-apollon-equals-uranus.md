## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden expansion in business. To be in touch with new or revolutionary ideas in the field of science.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Uranus row; checked on the actual scan. The two adjacent symbols are Uranus glyph variants.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success through new knowledge, successful environmental event, successful environmental technology, renewal of partner experiences, new environmental success, new knowledge of the environment, place for new knowledge, reform of environmental knowledge, and technical success of others.

Source reference: printed p. 131 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Uranus row. “Renewal” corrects “emulation” against “Erneuerung” in the actual scanned row.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sudden commercial expansion and contact with new scientific or technical knowledge ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** The entries emphasize new knowledge and ideas; erratic behavior is not stated.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Contact with scientific ideas and others' technical successes is explicit; ABC adds renewed partner experience.
- **Events/manifestations:** Sudden business growth, successful technology, and reform of knowledge about the surroundings are examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Suddenness is not evidence of short duration or instability; ABC's “emulation” was repaired to “renewal” from the scan.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in novelty, knowledge, and successful change, without an explicit opposition. Witte specifically names suddenness; ABC's examples do not establish that every outcome is sudden.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through an opening to new knowledge that can bring sudden business expansion. Revolutionary scientific ideas, successful technology, and others' technical successes make innovation concrete; ABC also includes renewed partner experience and reform of knowledge about the surroundings. These examples support change through discovery and technical contact without establishing that the results are unstable or short-lived. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
