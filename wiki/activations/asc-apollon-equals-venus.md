## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be well liked as a customer. Extensive peace in the near environment.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Venus row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Experienced love partner, successful beautification of a place, successful art place, joy about space expansion, peaceful expansion of a place, peaceful environmental experiences, art trading place, place of peace science, and much harmony at the place.

Source reference: printed p. 130 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Venus row. “Beautification,” “art trading place,” and “much harmony” were checked against “Verschönerung,” “Kunst-Handels-Platz,” and “viel Harmonie” in the actual scanned row.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Being liked in commercial contact and experiencing peace, harmony, or beautification in the surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC names joy at expanded space; superficiality and lack of commitment are not stated.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** A well-liked customer and an experienced love partner are concrete relationship examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Beautifying a place, peaceful expansion, an art trading place, and a place of peace science are explicit examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Peace science is retained as the source's unusual example; it does not establish a specific institution or method.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize agreeable or peaceful surroundings, while ABC adds art, love, and spatial examples. No explicit opposition is stated, and harmony does not by itself establish relationship depth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through welcome, peace, and agreeable surroundings. Witte's well-liked customer connects harmony to commercial contact; ABC extends it to an experienced love partner, beautification, art trade, and joy at the peaceful expansion of a place. The concrete unity is favorable contact within a pleasing or peaceful setting, with no claim that harmony is necessarily superficial. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
