## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be known generally. General superficial acquaintances. Relations with many. Ties with people in the sciences or traders in the international area.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Vernal Point row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Others are successful in public life, expansion of a place in public, an experienced partner in public life, public expansion of a place, a public place of knowledge, public experiences of the surroundings, and successes of the surroundings in public.

Source reference: printed p. 130 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Vernal Point row. The translation was checked against the complete actual glyph row, beginning “Andere sind erfolgreich im öffentlichen Leben.”

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Broad public familiarity and numerous contacts, including international scientific or commercial ties ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Witte describes some acquaintances as superficial; this does not establish a universally shallow character.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Relations with many and ties with scientists, international traders, or publicly experienced partners are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Being generally known, public success, public expansion of a place, and a public place of knowledge occur in the entries.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The repaired ABC row concerns public life throughout; it does not add the generalized loss of substance inferred by the prior interpretation.

## Contradictions

The entries share public reach, but only Witte explicitly calls some acquaintances superficial. ABC's public-success examples neither contradict that qualification nor establish it for every connection.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the pair through public reach: being generally known, relating to many, and having scientific or commercial ties that may extend internationally. ABC keeps success, experienced partners, and places of knowledge in public life. Witte's qualification about superficial acquaintances limits the depth of some contacts; it does not invalidate the practical or intellectual connections described alongside them. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
