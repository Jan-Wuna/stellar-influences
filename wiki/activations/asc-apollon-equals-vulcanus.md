## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Business success, expansive trade, rapidly moving merchandise.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Vulcanus row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Extension of local violence, successful partner influence, successful power expansion, tremendous environmental experiences, power expansion at the site, powerful environmental success, strong expansion of a place, and increasing power of others.

Source reference: printed p. 131 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Vulcanus row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Expansive, successful trade alongside strong growth of influence or power in the surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Neither entry establishes an aggressive temperament or personal ambition.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** ABC names a partner's effective influence and others' increasing power.
- **Events/manifestations:** Rapid merchandise movement, expanding trade, strong local expansion, and extension of local violence are source examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's violence/power vocabulary is broader than commercial success; it does not prove that successful trade is coercive.

## Contradictions

Commercial success and increased influence are compatible examples, but ABC also names violence. These settings should remain distinct: neither entry says that commerce must cause violence or that influence is always beneficial.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through expansion with substantial commercial movement or influence. Witte gives business success, expanding trade, and rapidly moving merchandise; ABC gives effective partner influence, growing power, and strong expansion of a place. ABC's separate reference to local violence preserves a harsher possibility without making force or coercion the necessary cause of commercial success. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
