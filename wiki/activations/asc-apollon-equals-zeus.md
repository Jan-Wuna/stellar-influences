## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Things or objects that come from a distance into our near environment. To deal with military of technical matters. Gun shop. Customers with technical interest.

Source reference: one-based PDF p. 95, “Asc + Apollon,” Zeus row; checked on the actual scan. The printed wording is “military of technical matters.”

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful partner efforts, successful shaping of the surroundings, fire spreading at the location, leading knowledge of the surroundings, creative knowledge at the location, increased goals for the surroundings, procreation success with the partner, and increasing local efforts.

Source reference: printed p. 131 (one-based PDF p. 64), “Aszendent + Apollon,” Zeus row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Technical dealings and successful efforts that shape the surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Goals, creative knowledge, and effort are named; an excessively utilitarian character is not established.
- **Body/health:** ABC's procreation-success example is historical source doctrine, not a fertility assessment.
- **Social/relationship:** Customers with technical interests and successful efforts with a partner are explicit examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Objects arriving from a distance, military or technical matters, a gun shop, local fire expansion, and shaping the surroundings appear in the entries.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's “military of technical” is retained as source/parser wording; the readable military and technical examples are not expanded into a general claim about conflict.

## Contradictions

The entries supply technical, creative, and effort-related examples without a direct opposing claim. Fire expansion and procreation success are separate ABC manifestations, not evidence that every technical contact carries those outcomes.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Apollon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair through technical contact and efforts that produce or shape something in the surroundings. Witte connects arriving objects and technically interested customers with military or technical dealings, including a gun shop; ABC adds successful partner efforts, creative knowledge, and local goals. Its fire-expansion and procreation examples remain distinct possibilities, with the latter a historical source claim rather than a fertility prediction. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
