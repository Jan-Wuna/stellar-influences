#### Witte Entry

Settled. Stationary. Others are pushed in the background or feel inferior. Others retire or withdraw. Other people with a conservative personality. Others are narrow minded or constrain us. Stationary at one place. Narrow or limited surroundings. Few acquaintances.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the repeated Asc pair through a settled or stationary place and a small or limited social field. The entry ranges from few acquaintances to others withdrawing, feeling inferior, or constraining the subject. Those descriptions concern the surroundings and other people as written; they do not establish that the subject has every listed attitude or that settled life must become stagnant. This is a synthesis of one source entry. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
