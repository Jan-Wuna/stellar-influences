## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the repeated Asc pair through the homes and associations that give people a shared setting. Family ties and dwelling conditions belong with business partnerships, common endeavors, artists, and group members: each is a concrete form of being connected with others. The entry also includes the outward appearance of a family or association, without asserting that belonging restricts individual freedom. This range comes from one source entry.
