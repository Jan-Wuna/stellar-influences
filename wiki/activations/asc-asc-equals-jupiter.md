## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Luck with other people; or other people's luck (fortune). To experience good deeds form others. Prospering surroundings. Wealthy friends or companions. Fortunate acquaintances. Happy relations to the surroundings.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 78, “Asc + Asc,” Jupiter row; checked on the actual scan. The apostrophe in people's is restored; the printed form others is retained literally.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Fortune with or among others and favorable surroundings ([Witte entry](#witte-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Happy relations are stated; complacency or dependence is not.
- **Body/health:** No specific body or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Others' good deeds, wealthy companions, and fortunate acquaintances are the concrete examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Prosperous surroundings and experiencing others' good fortune are explicit alternatives.
- **Conflicts/notes:** “Luck with others” and “others' luck” have different subjects; personal enrichment is not guaranteed by either. Only one source is present.

## Contradictions

The single entry offers several favorable relational circumstances. The subject may benefit from others or encounter their good fortune; these are alternatives rather than a contradiction or independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the repeated Asc pair through favorable relations and good fortune in the surroundings. Others may perform good deeds, companions may be wealthy, and the place itself may prosper. The entry carefully includes both luck with others and others' luck, so benefit to the subject and witnessing another person's fortune remain distinct possibilities within this single-source reading.
