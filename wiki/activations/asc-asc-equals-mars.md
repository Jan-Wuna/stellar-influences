## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Actions and deeds of others or with others. Excitement. Arguments with others. To be handled energetically, leading eventually to violence. Fights. Strife. Co worker. To be made angry by others. Excitement or action in the immediate surroundings. Actions and deeds of others. Others are in activity. Place of work. Working environment.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 78, “Asc + Asc,” Mars row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Actions by or with others, from cooperative work to open conflict ([Witte entry](#witte-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Excitement and anger provoked by others are explicit; a constant angry disposition is not.
- **Body/health:** The source includes forceful handling that may lead to violence, but names no particular injury or disease.
- **Social/relationship:** Coworkers, arguments, fights, and strife are different forms of active encounter.
- **Events/manifestations:** Activity in the immediate surroundings and the place of work are explicit settings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The possibility of violence is qualified; ordinary work and shared action must not be collapsed into a required fight. Only one source is present.

## Contradictions

Cooperation and conflict are alternative forms of activity within one entry, not a claim that both must occur together. There is no second source to compare.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Mars`, Mars specifies the repeated Asc pair through what others do and what is done together. Coworkers and a working environment give this activity an ordinary practical form; arguments, provoked anger, strife, and potentially violent handling give it a conflictual range. The single entry holds both forms together as active encounters in the immediate surroundings, without making violence a necessary outcome of shared work. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
