## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

One's relationship, position or attitude towards others. To join with others for a common objective. The social and vocational relations in the closer surroundings. Others influence one’s own emotions. In this minute at this place.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 78, “Asc + Asc,” MC row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** One's own position and relationships among others in the immediate setting ([Witte entry](#witte-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Attitude toward others and their influence on one's emotions are explicit.
- **Body/health:** No specific body or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Joining others for a common objective and local social or vocational relations are central.
- **Events/manifestations:** The entry locates these relations “in this minute at this place,” without establishing a timing method.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Emotional influence is not stated to be excessive or unhealthy. Only one source is present.

## Contradictions

The single entry links personal position, shared objectives, and emotional influence without an explicit opposing claim. It cannot establish cross-source agreement.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = MC`, MC specifies the repeated Asc pair through the person's own relationship, position, and attitude toward nearby people. Shared objectives connect that personal position to social and vocational relationships, while others' influence on emotions gives it an inward consequence. The source's “in this minute at this place” emphasizes the immediate setting; it does not by itself establish overidentification or a method for timing events. This interpretation rests on one entry.
