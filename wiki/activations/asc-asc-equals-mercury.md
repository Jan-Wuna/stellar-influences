## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Talks, conversations and correspondence. To be the theme of conversation with others. Other people think of the native. News from other people. News about the closer environment. Exchange of ideas with acquaintances.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 78, “Asc + Asc,” Mercury row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Conversation, correspondence, news, and exchanged ideas in the nearby social field ([Witte entry](#witte-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Other people thinking of the subject is explicit; mental distraction is not.
- **Body/health:** No specific body or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Acquaintances exchange ideas, while the subject may be discussed by others.
- **Events/manifestations:** News from people or about the close surroundings, talks, and correspondence are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Being discussed is not necessarily gossip, and the entry gives no frequency such as “constant.” Only one source is present.

## Contradictions

The single entry includes sending, receiving, and being the subject of communication. These complementary roles are not a contradiction or independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the repeated Asc pair through communication with and about the people nearby. Conversations, correspondence, local news, and exchanged ideas make acquaintances a source and audience of information; the subject may also be on other people's minds or become their topic of conversation. The entry describes these several directions of exchange without assigning them a constant frequency or a negative intent. This is a single-source reading. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
