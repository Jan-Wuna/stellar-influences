## Comparative Schema

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Moon`, Moon specifies the repeated Asc pair through the people close by and the possibility of being brought into public by others. The source repeatedly names women and female acquaintances, which remain attributed historical examples rather than a rule for every encounter. Its separate phrase about the quality of an hour at a place adds a local situational reference, but supplies no basis here for an emotional temperament or a timing system. This reading rests on one entry. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
