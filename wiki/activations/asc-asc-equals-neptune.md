## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

To be deceived. Wrongly connected. Others are negative, irresolute or prejudiced. Others say “No”. Want of clearness in relations with others. To be deceived or avoided by others. Uncertain, doubtful, precarious, unsteady situations in relation to a location.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 79, “Asc + Asc,” Neptune row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Unclear, mistaken, refused, or unreliable contact with others and uncertain local circumstances ([Witte entry](#witte-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Negativity, irresolution, or prejudice are attributed to others; the subject is not automatically assigned those attitudes.
- **Body/health:** No specific body or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Deception, avoidance, refusal, and lack of clarity in relationships are explicit possibilities.
- **Events/manifestations:** A wrong connection or an uncertain and precarious situation involving a location is named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Saying “No,” being irresolute, and deceiving someone are distinct behaviors rather than equivalent motives. Only one source is present.

## Contradictions

The entry lists several adverse or uncertain forms of contact without saying that they share one intent. No second source is present, so cross-source agreement or contradiction cannot be assessed.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the repeated Asc pair through relations or local situations that lack clarity and dependable connection. The single entry ranges from mistaken connection and indecision to refusal, avoidance, and deception. These are distinct possibilities: a person saying “No” is not necessarily deceiving, and attitudes explicitly assigned to others should not be transferred to the subject. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
