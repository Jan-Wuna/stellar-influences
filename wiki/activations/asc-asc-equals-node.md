## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Node`, Node specifies the repeated Asc pair through making connections and keeping in touch with the close surroundings. The mediator, broker, or agent gives those ties a practical social role, while connection to one place and links between locations extend the meaning to geography. The source also includes limits set by the surroundings, so connection can have boundaries without being inherently unhealthy or repetitive. This is a synthesis of one entry. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
