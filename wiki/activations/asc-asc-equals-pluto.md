## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Other people are changeable. Changes in relation to others. Changes at the place. Moving from residence. Changes in connections with different people. To be transient in a place. Changes of the immediate environment.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 79, “Asc + Asc,” Pluto row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Changes in relationships, residence, and the immediate surroundings ([Witte entry](#witte-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Changeability is attributed to others; personal restlessness is not established.
- **Body/health:** No specific body or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Relations and connections with different people change in the entry.
- **Events/manifestations:** Moving residence, being transient in a place, and local changes are explicit.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entry specifies change without its pace, cause, or beneficial/adverse outcome. Only one source is present.

## Contradictions

The single entry repeats change across people and places without an explicit opposition. Repetition within one source is not independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the repeated Asc pair through changes in the people and places immediately around the subject. Altered relationships and connections sit alongside a move of residence or a transient stay. The entry unifies these as change in the local field, while leaving its speed, cause, and consequences open. This reading is limited to one source entry. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
