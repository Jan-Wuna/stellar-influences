Acquaintances which are high-minded, such as deep thinker or genius. Others are high sensible. Acquaintances to spiritual people. The intellectual-spiritual environmental impressions. Others with mental, cognitive or educational skills. Water masses or oceans in the close surroundings.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the repeated Asc pair through contact with thoughtful, intellectually skilled, or spiritual people and the impressions received from them. The single entry names deep thinkers and people with cognitive or educational abilities, giving the social theme concrete forms. It separately includes large bodies of water or oceans in the close surroundings; that physical example should remain visible without being made a metaphor for the intellectual material.
