## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Separation, refusal, or seriousness in contact with people and place ([Witte entry](#witte-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Estrangement and being alone are stated circumstances; a lasting emotional trait is not established.
- **Body/health:** No specific body or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Refusal, a cold shoulder, farewell, and a serious acquaintance are explicit alternatives.
- **Events/manifestations:** Separation from a place or acquaintances and others bidding farewell are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Serious acquaintance need not entail rejection; the entry supplies no duration for solitude. Only one source is present.

## Contradictions

The entry combines separation with a serious acquaintance as different possible expressions. There is no explicit contradiction and no second source for comparison.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the repeated Asc pair through distance or interruption in relations with people and place. Refusal, estrangement, farewell, and separation give several forms of that distance, while being alone describes one resulting situation. A serious acquaintance remains another explicit possibility, so the single entry is broader than rejection alone and supplies no basis for assuming permanent isolation. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
