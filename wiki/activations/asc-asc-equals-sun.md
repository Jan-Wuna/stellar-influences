## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Personal relationships. Physical relations to other people. To meet or make acquaintances. Personal salutation or greetings. To make acquaintances. To meet acquaintances. Body awareness for the immediate surroundings. The quality of the day (or year) at this location.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 78, “Asc + Asc,” Sun row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Direct personal and physical contact with nearby people ([Witte entry](#witte-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Body awareness of the surroundings is named; emotional reactivity is not.
- **Body/health:** Physical relations and body awareness are explicit, but no illness or health outcome is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** Meeting, greeting, and making or encountering acquaintances are central.
- **Events/manifestations:** Personal meetings and the source's “quality of the day (or year) at this location” are given.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The day/year phrase is preserved without constructing a timing layer. Only one source is present.

## Contradictions

The single entry gives several forms of immediate personal contact and a situational time/place phrase. It offers no opposing claim and cannot establish cross-source agreement.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Sun`, Sun specifies the repeated Asc pair through direct personal encounter: meeting acquaintances, greeting people, and entering physical relations with others. Body awareness connects those meetings to the immediate surroundings. The entry also names the quality of a day or year at a location, but gives no method here for deriving a timing layer from that phrase. This interpretation stays within one source's account of personal presence and contact. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
