## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Excitement, irritation or accidents caused by other people. Sudden acquaintances. Sudden events of other peoples. Tension and excitement by others. Reunions for a special reason. Sudden appearance. Location of an accident. Location for mathematics, technology or inventions. Engineers, restless or excited people in the immediate surroundings. Environmental technology.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 78, “Asc + Asc,” Uranus row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sudden encounters or events and contact with technical people or places ([Witte entry](#witte-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Excitement, irritation, and tension caused by others are explicit; restlessness is also assigned to people nearby.
- **Body/health:** Accidents caused by others are historical source examples; no specific injury is identified.
- **Social/relationship:** Sudden acquaintances, reunions for a special reason, and nearby engineers are named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Sudden appearances, accident locations, and sites of mathematics, technology, or invention are explicit.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Technical settings and accidents are distinct examples, not a claim that engineers or inventions necessarily cause harm. Only one source is present.

## Contradictions

The single entry includes technical contact and disruptive encounters without requiring them to coincide. No cross-source agreement or contradiction can be assessed.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the repeated Asc pair through sudden meetings, appearances, or events involving others. The entry ranges from a reunion for a special reason to irritation, tension, and accidents, and separately locates engineers, mathematics, technology, and inventions nearby. These are several forms of sudden or technical contact in one source; the accident examples do not make every technical setting dangerous or the subject habitually accident-prone. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
