## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Lovable acquaintances. To be treated well by others. Others who long for the native. Personal love connections and love unions. To show evidence of love. Nice appearance. Peace in the immediate vicinity. Harmonious relations to the place. Harmonious surroundings. Peaceful place.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 78, “Asc + Asc,” Venus row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Affectionate contact, good treatment, and harmonious surroundings ([Witte entry](#witte-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Love and longing are named; conflict avoidance or dependence on pleasantness is not.
- **Body/health:** Nice appearance is an explicit aesthetic example, not a health assessment.
- **Social/relationship:** Lovable acquaintances, love unions, and demonstrations of love are central.
- **Events/manifestations:** Being treated well and experiencing peace or harmony at a place are explicit.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Personal affection and peaceful surroundings are both represented, without a claim that they conceal unresolved problems. Only one source is present.

## Contradictions

The single entry's affectionate and peaceful examples present no direct opposition. Repeated harmony language does not supply independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Venus`, Venus specifies the repeated Asc pair through affection in personal contact and harmony in the nearby place. Being treated well, meeting lovable acquaintances, and giving or receiving signs of love connect the relational examples; a nice appearance and peaceful surroundings give the same agreeable quality a visible setting. This single entry supplies no basis for treating its harmony as a concealment of deeper conflict. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
