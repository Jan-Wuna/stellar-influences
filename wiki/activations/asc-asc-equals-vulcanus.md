## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Others are superior. To experience force from others. Powerful, strong or forceful personalities in the close environment. Others act violently. Surroundings occupied by great energies or powerful influences, whether natural or biological.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 79, “Asc + Asc,” Vulcanus row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Strong people, force, or powerful influences encountered in the close surroundings ([Witte entry](#witte-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Forcefulness and superiority are attributed to others; the subject's own disposition is not described.
- **Body/health:** Natural or biological energies and others' violence are source examples, without a specific illness or injury claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Powerful personalities and experiencing force from others are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Others acting violently and surroundings occupied by great energies are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Powerful influence may be natural, biological, or interpersonal; the entry does not equate all strength with violence. Only one source is present.

## Contradictions

The single entry distinguishes powerful personalities, violence, and nonpersonal energies as possible manifestations. They need not occur together; no second source is present.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the repeated Asc pair through force encountered nearby, whether from powerful people or from natural or biological influences. The single entry explicitly includes superiority, forceful personalities, and violence by others, while its broader energy examples need not be interpersonal. Keeping those forms distinct preserves the strength of the source's adverse examples without reducing every powerful setting to coercion. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
