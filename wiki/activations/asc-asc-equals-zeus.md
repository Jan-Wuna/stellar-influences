## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Others are near. Things or matters which are in the immediate environment of the native. To work with others, being together with others. To be under the pressure by others. Working activities in a manufacturing plant. To have something to do with fire or machines. To be the object of disputes. To be threatened. Creating with others.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 79, “Asc + Asc,” Zeus row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Working or creating with others nearby, including manufacturing, fire, or machinery ([Witte entry](#witte-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Pressure and threat are experienced circumstances; no general hostile disposition is assigned to the subject.
- **Body/health:** Fire, machines, and threats are named, but the entry gives no particular injury or disease.
- **Social/relationship:** Being together, joint creation, disputes, and pressure by others are explicit forms of contact.
- **Events/manifestations:** Manufacturing work and involvement with fire or machines are concrete settings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Cooperative creation and threatening contact remain distinct possibilities. Only one source is present.

## Contradictions

Shared work and being the object of disputes occupy different parts of one entry's range. They are alternatives rather than a claim that cooperation must lead to threat; no cross-source comparison is possible.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Asc = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the repeated Asc pair through close involvement with other people in work or creation. A manufacturing plant, fire, and machines give that joint activity concrete settings. The same entry includes pressure from others, disputes, and threats, so its practical togetherness has both cooperative and conflictual forms; it does not say that shared work must become threatening. This reading is limited to one source entry. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry))
