## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

With many people in a community. Urban manner of construction. Many people walk in and out. Many apartments in a house. To have tenants or to be a tenant. Large tenement house. Consecrated places, churches, chapels and musks.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 91, “Asc + Cupido,” Apollon row; checked on the actual scan. “Musks” is the literal printed wording; its intended meaning remains unresolved.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Spread of environmental communities, experienced environmental community, successful local community, successful spatial arts, joint partner experiences, joint environmental success, community blockade on site, and trade community on site

Source reference: printed p. 123 (PDF p. 60), “Aszendent + Cupido,” Apollon glyph row; checked on the scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Numerous people and expanding communities, including dense housing and collective activity ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Neither entry establishes impersonality or lack of intimacy.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Tenants, many community members, shared partner experiences, and a trade community are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Urban construction, many apartments, a large tenement house, consecrated places, and successful spatial art are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC includes a local community blockade alongside success; the unresolved “musks” wording is excluded from interpretation.

## Contradictions

Witte's many-person settings and ABC's spreading communities overlap. ABC's blockade example qualifies a uniformly expansive reading but does not contradict growth under other circumstances.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through communities and places that bring many people together. Urban buildings with multiple apartments, tenants, and people moving in and out give Witte's examples a concrete residential scale; consecrated places extend the range beyond housing. ABC adds spreading or successful communities, spatial art, shared experience, and trade, while retaining a separate possibility of community blockade. The number of people does not by itself establish the quality of their intimacy. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
