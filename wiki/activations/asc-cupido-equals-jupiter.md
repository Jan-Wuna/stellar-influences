## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Home comfort. To be satisfied with the apartment. To be jolly with other people.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 91, “Asc + Cupido,” Jupiter row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A successful art place, a local financial community, shared joy at the place, a happy partner community, the partner's success in art, a local legal community, a community's successes in the surroundings, and a satisfied local community.

Source reference: printed p. 122 (PDF p. 60), “Aszendent + Cupido,” Jupiter glyph row; the complete translation and printed page were checked on the scan. The parser locator's p. 121 is incorrect for this row.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Comfort and satisfaction in home or community life, with shared joy and successful collective activity ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Satisfaction with the apartment and shared joy are explicit; complacency is not.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Being jolly with others and a happy partner community are central relationship examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** A successful art place, a partner's artistic success, and financial or legal communities are named by ABC.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The formerly fragmented ABC translation is restored from its own row; comfort is not evidence that arrangements need changing.

## Contradictions

The entries share satisfaction and enjoyable togetherness, while ABC adds financial, legal, and artistic settings. No explicit opposition is stated, and those additional settings are not each independently corroborated here.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through satisfaction in the home and enjoyment with other people. Comfort in an apartment and convivial company give the theme an immediate domestic form. ABC adds shared joy, a happy partner community, success in art, and financial or legal communities. These are concrete forms of contentment and collective activity, without a stated inference of complacency. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
