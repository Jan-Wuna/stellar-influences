## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Wanting to marry. Common activities with family members. Housekeeping work. Homework.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 91, “Asc + Cupido,” Mars row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A work community at the place, efforts of an art place, joint activity in the surroundings, actions of a local community, a partner's will toward the whole, cooperative work in partnership, and a work community in the surroundings.

Source reference: printed p. 122 (PDF p. 60), “Aszendent + Cupido,” Mars glyph row; “Bemühungen” (efforts), the complete translation, and printed page were checked on the scan. The parser's p. 121 is incorrect here.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Shared work and activity in family, partnership, or a local community ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Wanting to marry and will directed toward a whole are explicit; resentment of duty is not.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Activities with family members and cooperative partnership work are central.
- **Events/manifestations:** Housekeeping, homework, a work community, and efforts involving an art place are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Shared labor is supported; arguments, pressure, and domestic friction are not stated in these entries.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe joint activity, while Witte also names wanting to marry. No explicit opposition occurs, and the wish for marriage is not independently corroborated by ABC here.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through doing work together. Family activities, housekeeping, and homework give shared effort a domestic form; ABC broadens it to local work communities, art-related effort, and cooperative partnership. Witte's wish to marry connects purposeful action with forming a union, without claiming that marriage necessarily occurs. The entries support joint effort rather than an inferred pattern of domestic conflict. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
