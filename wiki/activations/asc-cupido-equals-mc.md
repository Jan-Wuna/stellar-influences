## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

One’s own mental attitude toward the apartment, the own home. One’s own parental home.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 91, “Asc + Cupido,” MC row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

ensouled place of art, ensouled partner community, art of my partner, my other communities, my art on the spot, my environment with the partner, my community in the environment, my local community, and my partner community

Source reference: printed p. 122 (PDF p. 60), “Aszendent + Cupido,” MC glyph row; checked on the scan. The parser's p. 121 is incorrect here.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** One's own attitude to home and personal participation in partner, local, or artistic communities ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Mental attitude to the apartment or home is explicit; ABC's “ensouled” marks inner involvement rather than a defined psychological condition.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** One's parental home, partner community, and other communities are central.
- **Events/manifestations:** Personal art, a partner's art, and an art place are ABC examples; no specific external event is supplied by Witte.
- **Conflicts/notes:** “Ensouled” translates the source's “beseelt”; no mechanism of soul influence or life-direction rule is established.

## Contradictions

Witte's home-focused mental attitude and ABC's first-person community and art examples have different scope without an explicit opposition. Neither establishes that family patterns determine the person's whole identity.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = MC`, MC specifies the pair through the person's own inward relationship to home and community. Witte names mental attitude to the apartment or home and one's parental home; ABC repeatedly says “my” partner, community, surroundings, and art, while describing some places or unions as “ensouled.” The supported connection is personal involvement in those shared settings, without making home history a rule for the whole life direction. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
