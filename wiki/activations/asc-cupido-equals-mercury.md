Other youth communities, conversations within a partner community, a young art community, the youth community of a place, opinions of a local community, local news for the community, and a community's conversations about the surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Thought and communication concerning home, partners, and local communities ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Thinking about home or the parental home and local opinions are explicit; repetitive preoccupation is not.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Receiving a social invitation and conversations within partner, youth, or art communities are concrete examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Invitations and local news for a community are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Conversation and thought are supported; household logistics and planning are not specifically supplied.

## Contradictions

The entries connect home or community with thought and exchange, but ABC adds youth and art settings. There is no explicit opposition and no basis for treating every conversation as domestic planning.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through thoughts of home and communication within shared settings. Witte joins the home or parental home to receiving a social invitation; ABC adds partner conversations, local opinions and news, and youth or art communities. The common thread is the exchange of thought and information around belonging, without the entries establishing constant household planning or repetitive worry. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
