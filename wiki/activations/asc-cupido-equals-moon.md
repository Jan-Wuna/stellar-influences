## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 91, Moon row.

Social relations with female persons. Together with many people. Houses and buildings for service to the public, such as railroad stations, city hall, post offices or libraries.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 122 (one-based PDF p. 60), Moon row. The parser's printed p. 121 locator is incorrect; the page footer reads 122.

Other women's communities, a women's community in the surroundings, shared surroundings of a people, community with other women, a sense of community at the location, a communal hour at the location, a place of folk art, and a local community of women.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Social gathering and community within a local or public setting.
- Psychology: ABC names a local sense of community, without a separate personality claim.
- Body/health: No explicit bodily or health statement in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Social relations with women, being together with many people, and local women's communities.
- Events/manifestations: Public-service buildings such as stations, city hall, post offices, and libraries; local communal occasions and places of folk art.
- Conflicts/notes: Gemeinschafts-Gefühl means a sense of community, not community community; Volks-Kunst means folk art. Communal hour is not further defined in the entry.

## Contradictions

Both entries concern people sharing a local setting, while Witte adds public-service buildings and ABC adds folk art and community feeling. No passage establishes a loss of domestic privacy or a specifically nurturing function.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through social relations with women, gathering with many people, and places that support communal life. Stations, city hall, post offices, and libraries give that public function concrete form. ABC adds women's local communities, a sense of community at a place, and folk art, linking shared surroundings with social and cultural participation.
