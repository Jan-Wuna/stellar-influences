## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Dissolved betrothal or society. Divorce. The parents disappoint. Dissolution of the parental home. Shortage of living quarters.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 91, “Asc + Cupido,” Neptune row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Disappointed community in the surroundings, sensitivity within a partner community, a community sensitive to its surroundings, sensitivity of a local community, an uncertain community place, water as shared surroundings, and a future partner community.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 123, one-based PDF p. 60, “Aszendent + Cupido,” Neptune glyph row; checked on the actual scan. These seven clauses replace the unrelated Community of the Regions artifact. The following Pluto row beginning “Entfaltung einer Gemeinschaft am Ort” is excluded.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Disappointment or dissolution in shared life, alongside sensitivity within a community ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Disappointment and communal or partner sensitivity are stated; a personal tendency to dissolve every bond is not.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a specific body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Dissolved engagement or association, divorce, parental disappointment, and a future partner community are separate examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Dissolution of the parental home, housing shortage, an uncertain community place, and water as shared surroundings are explicit.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's sensitivity and water examples broaden Witte's adverse range; the future-partnership phrase gives no prediction method.

## Contradictions

Both entries include disappointment or uncertainty in shared settings. ABC also names sensitivity, shared water, and future partnership; these are additional possibilities, without a stated contradiction or a claim that every community dissolves.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through disappointed or uncertain shared arrangements. Witte makes this concrete in dissolved engagements or associations, divorce, the parental home, and housing shortage. ABC adds sensitivity within a partnership or local community, water as shared surroundings, and a future partner community. The combined range therefore includes receptive communal life as well as dissolution, while leaving the circumstances of each expression open. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
