## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Acquaintances through family members. To establish sociable connections. Partner in a society or to partake in sociability. Dealing with matters concerning flats and apartments.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 91, “Asc + Cupido,” Node row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Meetings at art places, shared partnership connections, shared connections in the surroundings, contacts of communities at the place, connecting partner communities, connection with other communities, and connections to local communities.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 122, one-based PDF p. 60, “Aszendent + Cupido,” Node glyph row; checked on the actual scan. The parser's printed p. 121 is incorrect; “Partner-Gemeinschaften verbinden” means connecting partner communities.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Making social connections through family, partnership, and local communities ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Neither entry establishes dependence on inherited ties or a particular motive for sociability.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Family introductions, sociability, shared partnerships, and connections among communities are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Art-place meetings and dealings with flats or apartments are concrete settings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Connections within and between communities are represented; neither source says those contacts must remain confined to family channels.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe establishing social ties. Witte's family and housing examples and ABC's art-place and intercommunity contacts differ in setting without an explicit opposition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Node`, Node specifies the pair through connections made within shared life and between communities. Family members introduce acquaintances, sociability brings people together, and flats or apartments provide a practical area of dealings. ABC extends the contact to art places and links among partner or local communities. The common thread is establishing connections, with no stated requirement that they depend on inherited or socially expected ties. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
