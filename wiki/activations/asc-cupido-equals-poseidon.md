## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Members of associations or groups for highly educated or exceptionally intelligent people.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 91, “Asc + Cupido,” Poseidon row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Insights of other communities, an insightful local community, an intellectual or spiritual partner community, clarity in shared surroundings, the art and culture of others, a place for art and culture, the place of a cultural community, and a reasonable local community.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 123, one-based PDF p. 60, “Aszendent + Cupido,” Poseidon glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Einsichtige” means insightful, and “vernünftige” means reasonable, not future. “Geistige” retains its intellectual/spiritual range.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Intellectual or cultural participation in groups and shared places ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC names insight, clarity, and reasonableness; Witte assigns education or intelligence to the people in its group example.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Membership in educated groups, partner community, and others' communities are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Places for art, culture, or a cultural community give the theme a local setting.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Intellectual/spiritual scope is retained for “geistige”; neither entry establishes exclusion, elitism, or detachment.

## Contradictions

Witte's educated-group example and ABC's insights, clarity, and cultural places overlap without an explicit opposition. ABC supplies additional cultural and partner-community forms rather than independent support for every Witte qualification.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through intellectual or cultural life shared with others. Witte's example is membership in associations for highly educated or exceptionally intelligent people. ABC extends the range to insights, clarity in shared surroundings, intellectual or spiritual partnership, and places for art and culture. The organizing theme is participation in those collective settings; the entries do not supply a social hierarchy or a pattern of exclusion. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
