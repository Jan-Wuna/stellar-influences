## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Dissolution of betrothal and marriage. Separation from the family, the apartment or a union.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 91, “Asc + Cupido,” Saturn row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Old partner community, tasks of a community in the surroundings, patience in a partner community, local community problems, shared surroundings in old age, a place for serious art, disturbances through other communities, and losses of a local community.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 123, one-based PDF p. 60, “Aszendent + Cupido,” Saturn glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Ort für ernste Kunst” means a place for serious art, not serious pleasure.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Separation or loss in shared life, with tasks and patience within continuing communities also represented ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Patience is explicit; loneliness or emotional barrenness is not established.
- **Body/health:** Old age is a setting in ABC, without a specific health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Dissolved engagement or marriage, separation from family or union, and an old partner community are distinct examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Separation from an apartment, local community losses or disturbances, and a place for serious art are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC includes continuing shared surroundings and communal tasks alongside losses; not every example implies breakup.

## Contradictions

Separation and loss overlap across the entries. ABC also includes patience, communal tasks, and shared surroundings in old age; these broaden the range without contradicting separation under other circumstances.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through separation or difficulty in shared life. Witte names dissolved engagements or marriages and departure from family, apartment, or union; ABC adds community problems, disturbances, and losses. Its tasks, patience, old partner community, and shared surroundings in old age also allow continuing association under demanding conditions. A place for serious art is a separate explicit setting within that range. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
