## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Personal participation in household, partnership, or local community settings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Neither entry establishes overidentification with a domestic or relationship role.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a health condition; people and personal participation are the stated scope.
- **Social/relationship:** Bridegroom, landlord, tenant, invited person, and partner community are explicit examples; men's communities retain ABC's historical gender wording.
- **Events/manifestations:** Community or art centers, local groups of people, and personal spatial art are ABC settings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The listed roles are distinct; invitation does not establish hosting, and personal participation does not determine the whole identity.

## Contradictions

Witte supplies domestic and social roles, while ABC supplies personal communities and local centers. These differ in detail without a stated opposition; neither source independently repeats every example of the other.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through a person's place in shared domestic or community life. Witte gives the concrete roles of bridegroom, landlord, tenant, and invited person. ABC adds local communities of people, community and art centers, personal partnership, and spatial art. Together these describe personal participation in shared settings, without establishing that a household role controls the person's identity. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
