## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A surprise marriage. Sudden event in a marriage or at a wedding. Excitement in the family. To surprisingly obtain an apartment. Modern home. Excitement in or through a community.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 91, “Asc + Cupido,” Uranus row; checked on the actual scan. Two Uranus glyph variants precede the printed “A surprise marriage.”

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Excited local community, dynamic partner community, shared renewal of the surroundings, new art at the place, a local reform community, tensions in shared surroundings, technical communities nearby, and a restless community place.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 123, one-based PDF p. 60, “Aszendent + Cupido,” Uranus glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Erneuerung” is renewal, “neue Kunst” is new art, and “Gemeinschafts-Ort” is community place.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sudden developments or excitement in family and community life, including renewal and technical settings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Excitement, tension, or restlessness appears in shared settings; repeated personal instability is not established.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Surprise marriage, wedding events, a dynamic partner community, and communal excitement are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Unexpectedly obtaining an apartment, a modern home, local new art, renewal, and technical or reform communities are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Renewal and tension are distinct possibilities; the source does not say every modern home or community repeatedly breaks continuity.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe excited or dynamic shared life. ABC adds renewal, new art, reform, and technical communities; these extend the range without opposing Witte's sudden domestic events.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden or dynamic developments in shared life. Witte gives surprise marriage, a wedding event, an unexpectedly obtained apartment, and a modern home as concrete examples. ABC adds shared renewal, new local art, and reform or technical communities, alongside tension and a restless community place. The common theme is excitement or renewal within family and community settings, with the outcome left open. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
