## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Love acquaintances lead to marriage. Harmonious community with others. To beautify or to decorate the home. To keep the home in good order.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 91, “Asc + Cupido,” Venus row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Other partner communities, a peaceful community of a place, a harmonious partner community, a local art community, a beautiful community place, beautification of a place's surroundings, preference for an art place, and being together with the love partner.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 122, one-based PDF p. 60, “Aszendent + Cupido,” Venus glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Kunst-Gemeinschaft” is art community, “schöner” is beautiful, and “Verschönerung” is beautification. The parser's printed p. 121 is incorrect here.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Affectionate or harmonious shared life and beautification of home or local surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Preference for an art place is explicit; dependency or conflict avoidance is not established.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a body or health claim; beauty here concerns places and surroundings.
- **Social/relationship:** Love acquaintances leading to marriage, partner togetherness, and peaceful or harmonious communities are explicit source examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Decorating or ordering the home, local art community, and beautification of shared places are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's marriage statement remains attributed source doctrine; the entries do not make harmonious appearance proof of hidden conflict.

## Contradictions

Both entries connect shared life with harmony or beauty. ABC adds local art and community places, while Witte includes marriage and home order; no explicit opposition is stated.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through affection, harmonious community, and care for shared surroundings. Witte links love acquaintances with marriage and gives decorating or ordering the home as practical expressions. ABC adds peaceful partnership, an art community, a beautiful community place, and being together with a love partner. These entries connect relationship harmony with the appearance and enjoyment of shared places, without supplying a hidden pattern of dependence or conflict avoidance. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
