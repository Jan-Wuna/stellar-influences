## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Family, community, and artistic places participate in public life.
- **Psychology:** Enjoyment of social life is explicit in the Witte entry.
- **Body/health:** These entries make no distinct body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Families and partner communities appear in the wider public environment.
- **Events/manifestations:** Dwellings and public community or art venues are concrete settings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Public participation is supported; pressure from public expectations is not stated.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction appears in these entries. Dwellings and sociability in Witte sit alongside the ABC emphasis on public community and art settings.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Asc/Cupido axis as family and community life taking a public form. Dwellings, sociability, and families among the general public connect with community venues, art venues, and partner groups in public surroundings. The common theme is participation and shared place; neither entry establishes a loss of privacy or pressure to conform. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 122.)
