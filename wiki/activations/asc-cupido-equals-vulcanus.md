## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Groups exert strength through coordinated will or shared local power.
- **Psychology:** Alignment of will is explicit; individual motives are not described.
- **Body/health:** No distinct body or health claim appears.
- **Social/relationship:** Strong partner groups and communities can exercise power or face violence.
- **Events/manifestations:** An influential art venue and violence against a local community are specific ABC examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Collective strength and violence against a group are different possibilities, not a single compulsory outcome.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte describes coordinated will, while ABC includes both a community's strength and force directed against it; the direction of power matters.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Cupido = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Asc/Cupido axis through the power of people acting together. Witte locates that power in a common direction of will; ABC extends it to strong local and partner communities and influential art venues. ABC also includes violence against a community, so collective strength and a group's exposure to force must remain distinct readings. Internal domination or demands for sameness are not stated. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 123.)
