## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 92, entry start (parser evidence; not visually checked).

Expansion of poverty and need. Dealing with poor or disagreeable customers. An authority on antiques. Nuisance through beggars. Acquaintances with others through old sciences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Extensive environmental pollution, others' lack of freedom, endangered freedom in the surroundings, dangerous experiences in the surroundings, lack of knowledge about the surroundings, adverse partner experiences, partner experiences from the past, and knowledge of environmental dangers.

Translation repaired against the German entry under Aszendent + Hades, printed p. 125, one-based PDF p. 61; wording and Apollon row verified against the scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Wider contact with need or harmful conditions coexists with knowledge of old subjects and local dangers.
- **Psychology:** These entries do not state a distinct psychological disposition.
- **Body/health:** Environmental pollution and dangers are named without a specific illness.
- **Social/relationship:** Poor customers, beggars, acquaintances through old sciences, and difficult partner experiences are source examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Expansion of poverty, extensive pollution, expertise in antiques, and knowledge of environmental hazards are distinct manifestations.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Antiquarian expertise is not itself degradation; knowing dangers differs from lacking local knowledge.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC presents both lack of environmental knowledge and knowledge of hazards as possibilities, while Witte includes constructive expertise in antiques alongside distressing dealings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Hades = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the Asc/Hades axis through an expanded field of need, difficult contacts, and knowledge. Poverty may spread and dealings may involve poor customers or dangerous surroundings; the same entry also supports expertise in antiques and acquaintances formed through old sciences. These older subjects need not be degraded. ABC adds extensive pollution and the contrast between lacking local knowledge and knowing environmental dangers, so exposure and informed understanding remain separate possibilities. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 125.)
