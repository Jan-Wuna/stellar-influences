## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 92, entry start (parser evidence; not visually checked).

Great lack and damage in the near environment. To cause damage to the state or to independent persons in company with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Particular disadvantaging of others, service in an environmental authority, great endangerment of the surroundings, lack of independent partners, inadequate local authority, state-related environmental disadvantages, hidden direction of the surroundings, and important environmental service.

Translation repaired against the German entry under Aszendent + Hades, printed p. 125, one-based PDF p. 61; wording and Kronos row verified against the scan. “Mangel an selbständigen Partnern” concerns a lack of independent partners, not “self indigenous parts.”

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Serious local disadvantage or damage involves authorities, the state, or independent people.
- **Psychology:** No distinct psychological claim appears.
- **Body/health:** Environmental endangerment is named without a specific health outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Others may join in causing damage; ABC also names a lack of independent partners.
- **Events/manifestations:** State damage, inadequate local authority, environmental administration, and important service are supported examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Official environmental service is not equated with wrongdoing; the entries include both service and harm.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte emphasizes major damage and collective involvement, while ABC also names administrative service. A role in an authority does not by itself establish corruption.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Hades = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the Asc/Hades axis as serious local disadvantage involving authority or independent people. Witte describes major lack and damage nearby, including damage to the state caused with others. ABC broadens the setting to inadequate local authority, state-related environmental disadvantages, and work in environmental administration. Important service remains a supported possibility alongside harm; the formula does not make every official role destructive. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 125.)
