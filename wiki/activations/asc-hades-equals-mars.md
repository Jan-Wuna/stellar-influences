## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Action in difficult surroundings includes assistance, mistakes, threats, and harmful activity.
- **Psychology:** Being threatened is explicit; a general defensive disposition is not stated.
- **Body/health:** Witte includes assisting others after accidents without specifying injuries or outcomes.
- **Social/relationship:** Others can need assistance, act faultily, or pose threats.
- **Events/manifestations:** Accident assistance, ineffective deeds, pollution, dirty workplaces, and concealed local activity are source examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Helping after an accident differs from causing damage; the entries do not assign one fixed role.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction appears. The entries describe several roles in difficult activity, including helper, threatened person, and faulty actor; these should not be collapsed into a single hostile identity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Hades = Mars`, Mars specifies the Asc/Hades axis through what people do in difficult or hazardous surroundings. Activity may mean assisting others after accidents, encountering faulty actions, or facing threats. ABC adds dangerous or criminal environmental activity, pollution, dirty workplaces, and concealed work. The shared theme is action under adverse conditions, with the important distinction that responding to harm and producing it are separate possibilities. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 124.)
