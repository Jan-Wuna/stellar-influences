### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

I lack the partner; the partner's endangered inner life; my secret partner; my partner from the past; my service in the surroundings; my criminal surroundings; criminal souls in the surroundings; a moment of danger at a place; emotional sorrow with the partner.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Adverse surroundings and troubled partner experiences affect one's inner life.
- **Psychology:** Witte explicitly links others' poverty with psychic influence and disgust toward surroundings.
- **Body/health:** Emotional sorrow is described; no specific illness is established.
- **Social/relationship:** ABC includes an absent, secret, or past partner and sorrow with a partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** Environmental service, criminal surroundings, and a moment of local danger are distinct ABC examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries do not establish a permanent identity or lasting psychological outcome; ABC's moral language remains attributed.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction appears after the unrelated ABC translation is removed. Witte emphasizes the inward effect of poverty and surroundings; ABC adds several personal partner and environmental situations without making them one inevitable biography.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Hades = MC`, MC specifies the Asc/Hades axis as the personal inward impact of difficult surroundings. Witte makes the connection explicit: others' poverty affects the psyche, and surroundings can evoke disgust. ABC adds emotional sorrow with a partner, a missing or secret partner, and personal involvement in dangerous or criminal surroundings. These examples support distress and involvement, without proving a permanent identity or inevitable life course. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 124.)
