## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 92, Ascendant + Hades, Pluto row; checked on the actual scan. The two leading Pluto glyph variants are labels, not prose.

To fail together with others. Change in a sad surroundings, to good or to worse (depends on the quality of Pluto in the chart).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous development of the surroundings, secret change of partner, inadequate development of the partner, adverse local change, adverse change of partner, hidden local transformations, growing environmental crime, and transformation of environmental pollution.

Translation and complete Pluto row restored from the scan, printed p. 125, one-based PDF p. 61. Its opening clauses had been attached to the preceding Neptune entry by the parser. The former repeated quality-of-life wording was unrelated translation output.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Changes in unhappy surroundings can go better or worse; shared failure is also named.
- **Psychology:** The entries establish no distinct psychological trait or compulsory crisis process.
- **Body/health:** No specific illness or bodily outcome is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** Shared failure, secret or adverse partner change, and inadequate partner development are explicit possibilities.
- **Events/manifestations:** Adverse local change, hidden transformations, growing environmental crime, and changing pollution are ABC examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte expressly makes improvement or worsening conditional on Pluto's chart quality; that condition must remain visible.

## Contradictions

Witte allows change toward either better or worse conditions, while ABC emphasizes adverse changes. This is a difference in range, not proof that improvement is impossible.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Hades = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the Asc/Hades axis as change within unhappy surroundings and possible failure shared with others. Witte explicitly leaves the direction open: conditions can improve or worsen, depending on the source's stated qualification about Pluto in the chart. ABC emphasizes adverse local and partner changes, hidden transformations, and growing environmental crime. Those adverse examples qualify the range without replacing Witte's conditional allowance for improvement. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 125.)
