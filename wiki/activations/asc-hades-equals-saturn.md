## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 92, entry start (parser evidence).

Big disagreeableness. To be separated from others by accident, poverty or illness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous losses in the surroundings, a partner's troubled past, a partner's grief and pain, lack of a partner in old age, problematic environmental crime, hidden disturbances in the surroundings, and environmental pollution involving losses.

Translation repaired against the German Saturn row, printed p. 125, one-based PDF p. 61; verified against the scan. “Partner-Mangel im Alter” means lack of a partner in old age, not a partner's poverty; “Störungen” means disturbances.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Hardship separates people and brings losses or disturbances to their surroundings.
- **Psychology:** A partner's grief and troubled past are ABC examples; social coldness is not expressly stated.
- **Body/health:** Accident or illness is a historical Witte example of separation, without a diagnosis or inevitable outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Separation through poverty and lack of a partner in old age are explicit possibilities.
- **Events/manifestations:** Environmental losses, hidden disturbances, crime, and costly pollution appear in ABC.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries do not establish that all support disappears or that isolation is permanent.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction appears. Witte emphasizes separation through hardship, while ABC adds partner sorrow, old age without a partner, and environmental losses.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Hades = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the Asc/Hades axis as separation and loss arising within difficult surroundings. Witte names accident, poverty, or illness as reasons for being separated from others. ABC adds a partner's grief and troubled past, lack of a partner in old age, and losses or concealed disturbances in the environment. These examples connect hardship with disrupted companionship without establishing permanent isolation or the disappearance of every form of support. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 125.)
