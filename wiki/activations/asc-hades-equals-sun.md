## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 92, entry start (parser evidence).

To put oneself in danger of bodily harm from others. To come bodily into contact with dirt.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Disadvantaging other people, endangered people in the surroundings, criminal people in the surroundings, disadvantages through other people, personal lack of a partner, a person's hidden surroundings, a place's polluted environment, and a center for environmental service.

Translation repaired against the German Sun row, printed p. 124, one-based PDF p. 61; wording and all eight clauses verified against the scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Adverse surroundings directly involve people, their bodies, and their local conditions.
- **Psychology:** Heightened physical sensitivity is not established by these entries.
- **Body/health:** Witte names danger of bodily harm from others and bodily contact with dirt; neither is an inevitable outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Endangered people, criminal people, disadvantage through others, and personal lack of a partner appear in ABC.
- **Events/manifestations:** Contact with dirt, polluted surroundings, and a center for environmental service are concrete settings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Environmental service and causing disadvantage are distinct roles; involvement does not itself establish guilt or injury.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction appears. Witte focuses on bodily contact and danger, while ABC includes wider personal circumstances and environmental service.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Hades = Sun`, Sun specifies the Asc/Hades axis as direct personal and bodily involvement in adverse surroundings. Witte describes risk of bodily harm from others and physical contact with dirt; ABC adds endangered people, disadvantage through other people, and polluted places. A center for environmental service is another supported setting. The concrete contact matters more than an inferred trait of heightened sensitivity, and the historical harm statement is not an inevitable prediction. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 124.)
