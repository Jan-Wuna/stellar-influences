## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: one-based PDF p. 92, entry start (parser evidence).

To be tied to others by vulgar love. Sympathy with people who live in the slums.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Endangered peace in the surroundings, a partner's dangerous preference, lack of harmony nearby, harmony with an old partner, secret love for another, sorrow with a love partner, crime involving love at a place, and preference for hidden surroundings.

Translation repaired against the German Venus row, printed p. 124, one-based PDF p. 61; verified against the scan. “Gefährdeter Umwelt-Friede” means endangered peace, not dangerous peace.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Affection and sympathy occur in troubled surroundings, with both harmony and relationship sorrow possible.
- **Psychology:** Sympathy for people living in slums and attraction to hidden surroundings are explicit source themes.
- **Body/health:** No distinct medical claim appears.
- **Social/relationship:** Secret love, harmony with an old partner, love-related sorrow, and Witte's value-laden phrase vulgar love are retained as source claims.
- **Events/manifestations:** Threatened local peace, lack of harmony, and crime involving love are ABC examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Sympathy for poverty is not itself a harmful bond; harmony with an old partner remains a supported alternative.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction appears. Affection, sympathy, threatened peace, and harmony with an old partner describe different expressions rather than a universal pattern of unhealthy attachment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Hades = Venus`, Venus specifies the Asc/Hades axis through affection and sympathy within difficult surroundings. Witte includes sympathy for people in slums and uses the value-laden phrase vulgar love for another form of attachment. ABC adds secret love, sorrow with a love partner, and threatened local harmony, while preserving harmony with an old partner as a separate possibility. Compassion is therefore supported without turning it into an inevitable unhealthy bond. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 124.)
