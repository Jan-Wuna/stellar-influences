## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 92, “Asc + Hades,” Vernal Point row; checked on the actual scan.

To come into contact with poverty.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Others are disadvantaged in public, endangered or dangerous public surroundings, disadvantages through others in the surroundings, public environmental pollution, others' traditions in public, and polluted surroundings in public life.

Translation repaired against the German entry, printed p. 124, one-based PDF p. 61; wording and the vernal-point row verified against the scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Contact with poverty and environmental disadvantage enters public life.
- **Psychology:** No claim about conscience or becoming accustomed to hardship is stated.
- **Body/health:** Pollution and danger are named without a specific medical outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Public disadvantage and disadvantage through other people are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Public pollution, dangerous public surroundings, and others' traditions in public are examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Tradition is not itself pollution; exposure does not establish indifference.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction appears. Witte's contact with poverty is narrower than ABC's public environmental examples; neither states that hardship becomes morally normalized.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Hades = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Asc/Hades axis through public contact with poverty and adverse surroundings. ABC makes the public setting explicit in others' disadvantage, endangered or dangerous environments, and visible pollution. Others' traditions in public form a separate cultural example. The entry describes what enters public experience without establishing indifference to it. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 124.)
