## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 92, “Asc + Hades,” Vulcanus row; checked on the actual scan.

To live in dangerous surroundings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous violence in the surroundings, renunciation of violence locally, enormous disadvantages through the partner, criminal local power, extensive environmental pollution, disadvantages through local violence, the partner's hidden influence, and hidden influence in the surroundings.

Translation repaired against the German entry, printed p. 125, one-based PDF p. 61; wording and the vulcanus row verified against the scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Dangerous surroundings involve force, power, or concealed influence.
- **Psychology:** Hidden influence is named without a specific motive or psychological trait.
- **Body/health:** Danger and violence are source examples without a specified bodily outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** A partner may bring severe disadvantage or exert concealed influence.
- **Events/manifestations:** Criminal local power, violence, and major pollution are concrete examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Violence and its renunciation are distinct local possibilities; no sequence between them is stated.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC includes both violence and renouncing violence in the local environment. They are different possibilities and should not be reduced to a uniformly coercive outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Hades = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Asc/Hades axis through force and influence in dangerous surroundings. Witte names living amid danger; ABC details criminal local power, violence, severe disadvantage through a partner, and concealed influence. ABC also names renunciation of violence as a distinct local possibility, without stating a sequence between these examples. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 125.)
