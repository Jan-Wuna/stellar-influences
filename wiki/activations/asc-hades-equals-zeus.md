## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 92, “Asc + Hades,” Zeus row; checked on the actual scan.

Constraint. Participate in arson, or to be threatened by such. To be exposed to oppression.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Efforts against environmental crime, beginning disadvantage to others, dangerous environmental goals, dangerous fire locally, inadequate environmental efforts, adverse creativity by others, and environmental achievements from the past.

Translation repaired against the German entry, printed p. 125, one-based PDF p. 61; wording and the zeus row verified against the scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Constraint and harmful or inadequate efforts occur in immediate surroundings.
- **Psychology:** The entries specify goals and oppression without establishing an aggressive personality.
- **Body/health:** Fire and arson are historical danger examples, not inevitable injury predictions.
- **Social/relationship:** One may participate in arson or be threatened by it; the source distinguishes those roles.
- **Events/manifestations:** Efforts against environmental crime, dangerous fire, and past environmental achievements are explicit.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Oppression and counter-crime effort coexist; harmful intent is not the only form of action.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction appears. Witte distinguishes participating in arson from being threatened by it, and ABC includes efforts against crime alongside dangerous or inadequate efforts.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Hades = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the Asc/Hades axis through constrained or hazardous efforts in one's surroundings. Witte includes oppression and either participating in arson or being threatened by it. ABC extends the range to dangerous goals and fire, inadequate efforts, and adverse creativity, but also efforts against environmental crime and achievements from the past. The exact entry therefore preserves both harmful action and action against harm. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 125.)
