## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Kronos = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the Asc/Kronos axis through scientific work, knowledgeable direction, and the growth of respected places. Witte's scientific employee or assistant gives a concrete working role; ABC adds successful local authority, trading and economic settings, and a partner's superior knowledge. Increasing partner independence is part of that range, so wider expertise need not mean greater dependence. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 129.)
