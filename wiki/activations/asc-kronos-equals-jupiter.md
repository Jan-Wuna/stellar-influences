## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Financial responsibility, legal authority, and success take a local or organizational form.
- **Psychology:** Joy with others is explicit; approval-seeking and self-worth claims are not.
- **Body/health:** No distinct body or health claim appears.
- **Social/relationship:** Trust over money and success with an independent partner are supported.
- **Events/manifestations:** Trustee, cashier, legal venue, financial center, and successful local hierarchy are concrete examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Independent partnership and favorable direction are stated without a promise of guaranteed security.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction appears. Witte's financial trustee or cashier is a narrow occupational example within ABC's wider financial, legal, and successful organizational range.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Kronos = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the Asc/Kronos axis through trusted financial roles and favorable authority. Witte's trustee or cashier handles money for others; ABC adds legal venues, financial centers, local financial authority, and a successful hierarchy. Joy with others and success with an independent partner extend the picture beyond office alone. The entry supports responsibility and favorable direction without an added claim that self-worth depends on approval. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 128.)
