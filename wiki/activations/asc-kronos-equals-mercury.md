## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Orders by superiors. To carry out orders. To direct one’s thoughts according to others. To have no right to an opinion of one’s own. Language courses. Arbitrated decision.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 94, “Asc + Kronos,” Mercury row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Others direct conversations, a respected media place, extensive surroundings for young people, a large traffic place, young people of other states, independent young people locally, a self-confident conversation partner, local traffic direction, important local news, and an important conversation place.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 128, one-based PDF p. 63, “Aszendent + Kronos,” Mercury glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Gespräche” means conversations, “selbständige” independent, and “selbstbewußter” self-confident.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Thought, communication, and local information are shaped or directed by others ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Witte names thought directed according to others and lack of a right to one's opinion; ABC also names a self-confident conversation partner.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Superiors giving orders, independent young people, and a conversation partner are explicit roles.
- **Events/manifestations:** Language courses, an arbitrated decision, media and conversation places, traffic direction, and local news are concrete examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's constrained voice and ABC's confidence or independence have different subjects and settings; neither permits a universal rule of intellectual submission.

## Contradictions

The entries share direction of communication or thought. Witte stresses orders and restricted opinion, while ABC adds independent youth and a self-confident conversation partner; this variation in agency remains visible without treating every speaker as both constrained and independent.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Kronos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through directed thought and communication. Witte emphasizes superiors' orders and limits on one's own opinion, alongside language courses and an arbitrated decision. ABC gives this a wider local setting in media, traffic direction, news, and conversation places, while also naming independent young people and a self-confident conversation partner. The entry therefore preserves several roles within directed exchange, with different degrees of agency. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
