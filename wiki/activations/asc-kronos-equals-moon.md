## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The husband. To be the master of a female. Employed in public service.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 94, “Asc + Kronos,” Moon row; checked on the actual scan. The gender hierarchy is retained as historical source wording.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A leading woman locally, great partner feelings, a large place of the people, an independent woman as partner, the state as partner of the people, superiority of other women, the partner's feeling of superiority, precedence of the woman as partner, and an important woman locally.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 128, one-based PDF p. 63, “Aszendent + Kronos,” Moon glyph row; checked on the actual scan. The translation restores “führende,” “Partner-Gefühle,” and “Überlegenheits-Gefühl”; “am Ort” does not specify a village.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Authority, prominence, or independence in gendered partnership and public roles ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC names great partner feelings and a partner's feeling of superiority, without defining a general emotional temperament.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Witte lists husband and a historically hierarchical male role; ABC includes a leading or independent woman and her precedence as partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** Public-service employment, a large place of the people, and the state as the people's partner are explicit examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The sources' gendered allocations of authority differ; neither is adopted as a rule about actual partners or women.

## Contradictions

Witte's male-mastery wording and ABC's leading or independent woman give materially different gendered examples of authority. They remain separately attributed rather than merged into a universal relationship hierarchy.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Kronos = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through gendered partnership and public roles involving authority or prominence. Witte lists husband, male mastery, and public-service employment in its historical terms. ABC instead includes a leading or independent woman, her precedence as partner, and a partner's feeling of superiority, alongside the state as partner of the people. These examples make authority in relationship and collective life visible while preserving the sources' different allocations of it. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
