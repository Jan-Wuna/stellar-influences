## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To give up employment. Quitting a business by the principal. Bankruptcy of the employer. To be deceived by the state.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 94, “Asc + Kronos,” Neptune row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A respected place by the water, a particularly sensitive partner, special water at the place, rejecting others' direction, authority as an uncertain partner, weak local authority, a sensitive local hierarchy, weak direction of the surroundings, and rejection of local authority.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 129, one-based PDF p. 63, “Aszendent + Kronos,” Neptune glyph row; checked on the actual scan. The nine clauses end with “Verneinung der Autorität am Ort.” The following Pluto row beginning “Andere lenken die Entwicklung” is excluded. “Lenkung anderer verneinen” is retained as rejection of others' direction, not steering vehicles.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Authority or employment may be uncertain, weak, rejected, or relinquished, with sensitivity and water settings also represented ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC names sensitivity in a partner or hierarchy; neither entry establishes habitual credulity or a particular motive for rejection.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** An uncertain authority as partner, employer bankruptcy, and state deception are distinct source examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Giving up employment, a principal quitting a business, and a respected place by water are concrete settings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Water and sensitivity remain separate possibilities; the source does not say every authority makes false promises or collapses suddenly.

## Contradictions

Both entries include unreliable or relinquished authority. ABC adds sensitive partnership and water-related places, which broaden Witte's employment and state-deception examples without contradicting them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Kronos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through uncertainty or relinquishment in authority and employment. Witte gives leaving work, a principal quitting a business, employer bankruptcy, and deception by the state as concrete examples. ABC adds weak or rejected authority and a sensitive partner or hierarchy. Its respected place by water and special local water remain additional physical settings, so the range extends beyond institutional failure alone. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
