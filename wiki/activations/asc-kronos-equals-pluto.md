## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be an apprentice. Change in the relation to the chief.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 94, “Asc + Kronos,” Pluto row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Others direct development, development in a large place, independent unfolding of a partner, directing development of the surroundings, a changed local hierarchy, a priority change of place, growing local authority, growing reputation of a place, and an important change of partner.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 129, one-based PDF p. 63, “Aszendent + Kronos,” Pluto glyph row; checked on the actual scan. All nine clauses begin after Neptune's “Verneinung der Autorität am Ort”; the first three were misassigned to Neptune. “Orts-Wechsel” is change of place and “Orts-Ansehen” reputation of a place.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Development or change affects authority, working relations, and local standing ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Neither entry specifies an emotional response to change or a fixed disposition.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Apprenticeship, changed relations with a chief, independent partner development, and a change of partner are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** A changed local hierarchy, change of place, growing authority, and a place's growing reputation are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Growth and change are represented without a stated pace or necessary disruption; the directions of influence vary across clauses.

## Contradictions

Witte's apprenticeship and changed relation to a chief are narrower than ABC's development, hierarchy, and place examples. They share change in authority-related settings without an explicit opposition or a rule of unstable dependence.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Kronos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair through development or change in authority-related relations and places. Witte makes this concrete in apprenticeship and an altered relation to a chief. ABC adds changed hierarchy, growing local authority or reputation, change of place, and independent development or replacement of a partner. These entries describe changes in role and standing while leaving their speed and emotional consequences open. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
