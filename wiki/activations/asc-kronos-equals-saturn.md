## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

As official or employee severely handicapped. To reject the boss or the government. To get notice. Change of employment.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 94, “Asc + Kronos,” Saturn row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Local authority in old age, special partner tasks, special losses of the partner, an inhibited local hierarchy, major problems of a place, great losses through others, a slow local administrative office, the state as a hard partner, and important tasks of a place.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 129, one-based PDF p. 63, “Aszendent + Kronos,” Saturn glyph row; checked on the actual scan. The omitted “große Verluste durch andere” is restored; “Orts-Behörde” denotes a local administrative office.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Authority or employment involves impediments, demanding tasks, or loss ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Rejection of a boss or government is explicit; neither entry gives a permanent attitude toward all authority.
- **Body/health:** Witte's “severely handicapped” is retained in its employment context without a specified condition; ABC's old-age setting supplies no diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** A hard state partner, partner tasks or losses, and losses through others are stated.
- **Events/manifestations:** Employment change, getting notice, inhibited hierarchy, and a slow local office are explicit examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The direction of rejection in Witte is toward the boss or government; the source does not say authority always punishes or dismisses the subject.

## Contradictions

Witte's impeded employment and ABC's inhibited hierarchy, slow office, and losses overlap. ABC also names authority in old age and important tasks; these broaden the range without making every official role punitive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Kronos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through impediments or demanding conditions in authority-related life. Witte names severe difficulty as an employee or official, rejection of a boss or government, notice, and employment change. ABC adds partner tasks and losses, inhibited hierarchy, a slow local office, and the state as a hard partner. Authority in old age and important local tasks remain further examples, without a stated rule that every difficulty is punishment from above. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
