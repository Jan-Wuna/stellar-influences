## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden scenes with superiors.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 94, “Asc + Kronos,” Uranus row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A respected place of technology, special tensions with the partner, dynamic local authority, an excited local hierarchy, major local reform, great unrest of the partner, new local authority, reform in other states, and an independent partner rhythm.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 129, one-based PDF p. 63, “Aszendent + Kronos,” Uranus glyph row; checked on the actual scan. The omitted “große Orts-Reform” is restored, along with “neue Orts-Autorität” and “selbständiger Partner-Rhythmus.”

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sudden or dynamic relations with authority, including tension, reform, and technical settings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Partner tension and unrest are explicit; no general rebellious disposition is supplied.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Sudden scenes with superiors, dynamic authority, and an independent partner rhythm are distinct examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** A respected technical place, major local reform, new authority, and reform in other states are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Excitement and reform do not establish humiliation or a necessary collapse of order; the partner-rhythm phrase is not further defined.

## Contradictions

Witte's sudden scenes with superiors and ABC's tensions or excited hierarchy overlap. ABC adds technical places, reform, and independence, broadening the range without a stated opposition.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Kronos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden or dynamic relations with authority. Witte gives sudden scenes with superiors; ABC adds partner tension, excited hierarchy, new authority, and local or interstate reform. A respected technical place and an independent partner rhythm remain further examples. Together these entries describe movement and excitement in authority-related settings while leaving their consequences open. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
