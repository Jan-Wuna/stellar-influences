## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be liked in the capacity of an official or an employee. To be the favorite.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 94, “Asc + Kronos,” Venus row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A respected art place, special joy with the partner, special beauty of a place, local authority in art, harmonious authorities of a place, harmonious direction of the surroundings, an independent love partner, a superior love partner, and an important place of peace.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 128, one-based PDF p. 63, “Aszendent + Kronos,” Venus glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Angesehener” is respected, “Kunst-Autorität” authority in art, “selbständiger” independent, and “überlegener” superior, not Tibetan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Favor or harmony in authority-related roles, with joy, art, and attractive places ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Joy with a partner is explicit; approval dependence is not established.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a body or health claim; beauty is assigned to a place.
- **Social/relationship:** A liked employee or official, favorite, and independent or superior love partner are explicit source roles.
- **Events/manifestations:** A respected art place, art authority, harmonious direction, and an important peace place are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Being liked does not identify who grants favor or prove dependency; superior love partner is retained as the source's comparative wording.

## Contradictions

Witte's favor in an employee role and ABC's harmony, art, or partner examples overlap in favorable relations. ABC also explicitly includes independence, with no direct opposition or universal need for approval.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Kronos = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through favor and harmony in authority-related relations. Witte names being liked as an official or employee and being the favorite. ABC extends the range to joy with a partner, art and beautiful places, harmonious authorities or direction, and an independent or superior love partner. These entries connect favorable relations with shared places and roles without establishing dependence on approval from a superior. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
