## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be in public service. To execute public tasks. To give orders to many. To be a subject. To bring problems of general interest to the public notice. Connections of superiors.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 94, “Asc + Kronos,” Vernal Point row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

respected public place, public authority on the spot, public environmental guidance, independent partner in public, public state place, precedence of the partner in public, and important place in public life

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 128, one-based PDF p. 63, “Aszendent + Kronos,” Vernal Point glyph row; checked on the actual scan.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Authority, service, and direction have a public setting ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Neither entry supplies a psychological trait or a stated feeling about public expectations.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Giving orders, being a subject, superiors' connections, and an independent partner in public are explicit roles.
- **Events/manifestations:** Public service or tasks, public notice of general-interest problems, public authority, and significant state or public places are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Exercising authority and being subject to it are both represented; public visibility does not establish excessive exposure.

## Contradictions

Both entries explicitly place authority in public life. Witte includes both giving orders and being a subject, while ABC adds independence and public precedence; these are different roles without a stated direct opposition.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Kronos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the pair through authority and service in public life. Witte names public tasks, giving orders to many, being a subject, and bringing matters of general interest to public notice. ABC adds respected public or state places, public authority, and an independent partner with public precedence. The shared setting is explicit, while the person's position within authority varies across the examples. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
