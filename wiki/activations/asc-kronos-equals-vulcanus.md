## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The competency of an official or employee. Official or employee with unrestricted power. Ambassador or secretary of state.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 94, “Asc + Kronos,” Vulcanus row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influencing others through direction, influence of a place's hierarchy, influential local authority, an influential state site, great partner influence, the other's superiority in power, local state power, the partner's superior strength, and an important place of power.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 129, one-based PDF p. 63, “Aszendent + Kronos,” Vulcanus glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Andere lenkend beeinflussen” is active influence through direction; the omitted “großer Partner-Einfluß” is restored.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Official power and the influence of hierarchy, state, or partner are explicit ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Neither entry supplies a motive for exercising power or a habit of domination.
- **Body/health:** Partner strength is stated without a specific physical or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Officials or employees with unrestricted power, ambassadors, secretaries of state, and influential partners are source examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Influential state sites, local state power, and a place of power give authority concrete settings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's “competency” is not further defined; unrestricted power and comparative strength do not alone establish abuse.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize power or influence in authority-related settings. Witte supplies official roles; ABC adds local hierarchy, state sites, and partner influence without an explicit opposition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Kronos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through the power and influence attached to authority. Witte names official competency, an official or employee with unrestricted power, and high state roles such as ambassador or secretary of state. ABC adds the influence of local hierarchy, state sites, and a strong or influential partner. These examples describe the reach of authority without supplying a necessary motive of domination or an inevitable abusive outcome. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
