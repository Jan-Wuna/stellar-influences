## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be forced to increased activity as employee. Compulsory work. Railroad official. To be employed in a mechanical establishment. To be called in a case of war.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 94, “Asc + Kronos,” Zeus row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Leading and directing others, efforts of an authority for the surroundings, a particularly purposeful partner, dominant goals for the surroundings, leadership of a respected place, a major local fire, a creative local hierarchy, independent partner shaping, and important partner achievements.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 129, one-based PDF p. 63, “Aszendent + Kronos,” Zeus glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Andere führen und lenken” means leading and directing others; the omitted “Groß-Feuer am Ort” is restored. “Partner-Gestaltung” retains its broad shaping sense without assigning an actor.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Work or purposeful activity occurs under authority, with both compulsion and leadership represented ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC names a purposeful partner and dominant goals; neither entry makes every employee aggressive or unwilling.
- **Body/health:** Fire and wartime calling are source circumstances, without a specific injury claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Compulsory employee activity, railway or mechanical employment, directing others, and partner achievements are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Increased work, wartime calling, a major local fire, and leadership of a respected place are concrete examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Forced labor and active direction are different roles; the broad partner-shaping clause does not establish coercion of a partner.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes compelled work and specific occupations, while ABC includes leading others, purposeful partnership, and creative hierarchy. Their agency differs, so compulsory service cannot absorb every expression on the page.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/Kronos = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair through work and purposeful activity within authority-related settings. Witte includes compelled employee activity, railway or mechanical work, and being called in wartime. ABC also names leading and directing others, purposeful partnership, and creative hierarchy, with a major local fire as a separate concrete event. The range preserves both imposed service and active direction rather than treating every form of work as compulsion. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
