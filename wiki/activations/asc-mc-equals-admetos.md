## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To have firmly established relations to other people.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 58, “Meridian + Ascendant,” Admetos row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Others restrict me, the partner's blocked soul, I stabilize others, I go on strike with others, my partner is thorough, my depressions at the place, my deepening into the surroundings, inner blockage through others, and resistance in my surroundings.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 61, one-based PDF p. 29, “Meridian + Aszendent,” Admetos glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Ich streike” means going on strike, “gründlich” thorough, and “seelische Blockade” inner blockage.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Firmly established relations, stabilization, and restriction or resistance within personal surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC names a blocked inner life, deepening, and a thorough partner; these remain attributed source descriptions.
- **Body/health:** ABC's “my depressions” is historical source wording without a defined clinical condition or diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Firm ties, others restricting the person, stabilizing others, and going on strike together are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** A joint strike and resistance in one's surroundings are concrete examples; Witte gives no specific event beyond established relations.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Established ties and obstruction are distinct possibilities; neither entry makes every durable relationship stagnant.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses firm relations, while ABC includes stabilization alongside restriction, blockage, and resistance. These differ in valence without establishing that stability necessarily causes obstruction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair through established or constrained relations with other people. Witte gives firmly established ties; ABC adds stabilizing others, a thorough partner, and deepening into one's surroundings, alongside restriction, inner blockage, and resistance. Going on strike with others gives the resistant side a specific collective form. These entries retain both consolidation and obstruction without making stagnation the inevitable result of a lasting tie. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
