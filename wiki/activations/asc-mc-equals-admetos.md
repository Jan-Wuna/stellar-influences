## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/MC = Admetos`: stable contact patterns, entrenched commitments, and a life direction shaped by persistence, rooted obligations, or long-standing association with particular people. Main caution: stagnation: what is secure here can also become rigid, immobile, or too compressed to adapt.
