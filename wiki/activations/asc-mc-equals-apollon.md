## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Professional success with others. Dealings with many people.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 58, “Meridian + Ascendant,” Apollon row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My success in the environment, my successful partner, my environmental knowledge, my knowledge of the partner, my experiences with others, my partner experiences, my environmental experience, and minute of experience on site

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 61, one-based PDF p. 29, “Meridian + Aszendent,” Apollon glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Umwelt” refers broadly to the surroundings here; no environmental-science specialty is specified.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Success, knowledge, and experience in relations with other people and one's surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Knowledge of a partner and experiences with others are explicit; scattered attention or overcommitment is not stated.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Dealings with many people, a successful partner, and partner experiences are named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Professional success with others is Witte's concrete manifestation; ABC also gives a minute of experience at a place.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The time/place phrase has no method here; the entries do not require wider audiences or larger systems to explain success.

## Contradictions

Both entries include success with other people. ABC adds knowledge and experience of partner and surroundings, without a stated opposition or independent support for every particular form of professional success.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through success and experience involving others. Witte makes this concrete in professional success with others and dealings with many people. ABC adds a successful partner and knowledge or experience of the partner and surroundings. The shared theme is successful or experienced participation in relations with others, without an added claim that the person must expand into larger systems or become overextended. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
