## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s vibration helps others to subtly align their identity more closely to their spiritual self. Chiron heals on this midpoint by helping others (to the extent they are ready and can) see beyond the limits of their own identity into the greater spiritual realities beyond.

Source reference: John Sandbach, one-based PDF p. 122, Chiron paragraph in “Planets activating the Ascendant/Midheaven midpoint”; checked on the actual page. The chapter begins on PDF p. 121, which is not the exact Chiron entry page. The split word “real ities” is joined.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sandbach describes subtle help aligning another person's identity with a spiritual self ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Seeing beyond limits of one's current identity is the passage's stated change in self-understanding.
- **Body/health:** “Heals” belongs to Sandbach's spiritual account here; no bodily condition, treatment, or clinical result is specified.
- **Social/relationship:** The described influence helps others, explicitly only to the extent they are ready and able.
- **Events/manifestations:** The entry gives no concrete event or professional mentoring role.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The readiness and capacity qualification limits the claim; a personal mission or tendency to impose help is not stated.

## Contradictions

Only one entry is present. Its claimed help and its readiness/capacity limit are compatible; no independent corroboration or opposing source claim appears here.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Chiron`, Chiron specifies the pair through what Sandbach describes as subtle help with another person's sense of identity. Within his spiritual account, that help brings identity closer to the spiritual self and opens a view beyond its present limits. The passage expressly qualifies this by the recipient's readiness and capacity, so the synthesis preserves a conditional influence rather than a guaranteed healing result or a prescribed mentoring role. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry))
