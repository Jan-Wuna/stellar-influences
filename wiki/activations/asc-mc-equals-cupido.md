## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be active in society. To conclude a marriage. Professional associates.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 58, “Meridian + Ascendant,” Cupido row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Ensouled artistic surroundings, the ensouled space of a community, I and others in the community, my place in the community, my local community, my partner community, my community in the surroundings, and an inner partner community.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 61, one-based PDF p. 29, “Meridian + Aszendent,” Cupido glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Beseelt” retains the source's ensouled wording; “seelische” refers to inner or soul life and is not narrowed to religious spirituality.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Personal participation in society, partnership, and community ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC describes shared artistic or communal space as ensouled, without defining a psychological condition or need for approval.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Social activity, marriage, professional associates, and one's place among others in a community are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Concluding a marriage and participation in local, partner, or artistic communities are concrete examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The source's inner-life language remains broad; conformity or loss of individuality is not stated.

## Contradictions

Witte's social, marital, and professional associations and ABC's personal-community examples share participation with others. Their different settings supply no explicit contradiction or rule of group dependence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through one's participation in shared life. Witte names social activity, marriage, and professional associates; ABC repeatedly identifies one's own place among others in local or partner communities. Its ensouled artistic surroundings and communal space add an inward quality to those shared settings. The organizing theme is personal involvement in community, without an added claim of conformity or dependence on approval. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
