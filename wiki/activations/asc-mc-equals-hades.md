## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be in a sad mood with others. In profession to be in contact with ugly and dirty things.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 58, “Meridian + Ascendant,” Hades row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Danger to me and others, a dangerous minute at the place, the partner's endangered soul, grief of my partner, my partner is criminal, my place is in danger, my mysterious surroundings, my criminal surroundings, and inner distress nearby.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 61, one-based PDF p. 29, “Meridian + Aszendent,” Hades glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Gefährdete Partner-Seele” means endangered, not dangerous; “seelische Not” retains inner distress rather than a defined diagnosis. The criminality claims remain historical source assertions.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sadness, danger, or distress in personal relations and surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Sad mood, partner grief, and inner distress are named without establishing a permanent personality condition.
- **Body/health:** Dirt and danger are source circumstances; no specific illness, injury, or clinical outcome is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Being sad with others and concern for an endangered or grieving partner are explicit; ABC's criminal-partner statement remains attributed doctrine.
- **Events/manifestations:** Professional contact with ugly or dirty things, a place in danger, and mysterious or criminal surroundings are examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Endangered does not mean dangerous; mysterious surroundings are not themselves proof of criminality, and the local-minute phrase gives no timing method.

## Contradictions

Witte's sadness and difficult work materials overlap with ABC's grief or distress. ABC adds danger and criminality as separate assertions, without establishing that sadness, dirt, and crime necessarily coincide.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through sadness or difficult conditions involving others and one's surroundings. Witte gives shared sad mood and professional contact with ugly or dirty things. ABC adds a grieving or endangered partner, danger to a place, and inner distress, alongside separate mysterious or criminal settings. These attributed examples establish an adverse range without turning it into a permanent life path, a diagnosis, or a judgment about an actual partner. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
