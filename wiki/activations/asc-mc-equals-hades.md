## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/MC = Hades`: a life path entangled with hardship, unattractive labor, or relationships colored by sadness, wear, or contamination. Main caution: immersion in decay: this can produce realism and endurance, but it can also trap the personality in discouraging, degrading, or unhealthy atmospheres.
