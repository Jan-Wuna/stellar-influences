## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be bossy. Self-confident towards a close contact in the near surroundings.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 58, “Meridian + Ascendant,” Kronos row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A special minute at the place, my reputation in the surroundings, my independent partner, my self-confidence in the surroundings, my authority in the surroundings, the partner's inner authority, precedence of my partner, my boss at the place, and my independence at the place.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 61, one-based PDF p. 29, “Meridian + Aszendent,” Kronos glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Selbständiger/Selbständigkeit” means independent/independence, “Selbstbewußtsein” self-confidence, and “Vorrang” precedence. “Seelische Autorität” retains broad inner-life scope.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Authority, standing, confidence, and independence in one's immediate relations ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Witte explicitly includes bossiness and self-confidence; ABC adds personal self-confidence without making either a universal character judgment.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** A close contact, independent partner, partner's precedence or inner authority, and one's local boss are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Reputation, authority, and independence in the surroundings or at a place are named; the special-minute phrase gives no timing method.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Authority may be one's own or another person's; the entries do not establish emotional distance or an inevitable assumption of superiority.

## Contradictions

Witte's bossiness and confidence overlap with ABC's authority and standing. ABC also distributes authority between the person, partner, and boss and includes independence; these are different roles rather than a claim that everyone holds precedence at once.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair through authority and confidence within immediate relations. Witte explicitly names bossiness and self-confidence toward a close contact. ABC adds one's own reputation, authority, and independence, alongside an independent partner, the partner's precedence, and one's boss. The interpretation therefore preserves both exercising authority and encountering it in others, without requiring a formal public role or an inevitable pattern of emotional distance. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
