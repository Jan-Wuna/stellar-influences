## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Vocational or business conversations. Talks about the business.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 58, “Meridian + Ascendant,” Mercury row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Ensouled youth at the place, thoughts about my partner, conversations in ensouled surroundings, youth in my surroundings, my conversation partner, my young partner, my partner reports, my partner in youth, and soul language of the partner.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 60, one-based PDF p. 29, “Meridian + Aszendent,” Mercury glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Gedanken an meinen Partner” means thoughts about the partner, not the partner's thoughts; “Gesprächs-Partner” means conversation partner. Soul/ensouled wording remains at the source's broad level.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Exchange of thoughts, use of one's critical faculties. A frank discussion between people, cooperation in the scientific or commercial sphere.

Source reference: Reinhold Ebertin, printed p. 307, one-based PDF p. 309, coded Mercury entry 1109 under “Ascendant/Medium Coeli”; checked on the actual scan.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

How one thinks about things and the ideas that one adopts either serve to enhance or frustrate personal growth. Enhancement happens when one is willing to let go of ideas that no longer serve one, and remain flexible in one’s thinking. Otherwise this combination might degenerate into a mere cleverness at avoiding personal growth.

Source reference: John Sandbach, one-based PDF p. 122, Mercury paragraph under “Planets activating the Ascendant/Midheaven midpoint”; checked on the actual page. PDF p. 121 is the chapter start.

### Don McBroom - Midpoints

- Source heading: `Mercury = Asc/Mc`

#### McBroom Entry

Thoughts, ideas, and communication come naturally. It is easy to talk with people - and to have them listen attentively. The power of communication is emphasized and is often highly developed and refined. Somehow people are as drawn to the communication style in the delivery of the message as with the songs of Homer's Sirens. There may also be deep analytical introspection in an attempt to figure out where (or if) one belongs.

Source reference: Don McBroom, printed pp. 62–63, one-based PDF pp. 73–74, Mercury = Asc/Mc paragraph and continuation; checked on both actual pages. The page-break “Some- / how” is joined as Somehow. The famous-person examples following the paragraph are outside this represented excerpt.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `364`

#### Munkasey Entry

The thinking or analysis you do about the effects of the daily contacts you have with the people in your life who are meaningful to you; the versatility you show when adapting to the examples of those who influence you.

Source reference: Michael Munkasey, printed p. 364, one-based PDF p. 365, “ASCENDANT/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points,” Mer row; checked on the actual page.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Thought, conversation, and analysis connect personal development with daily or professional relations ([Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Critical faculties, flexible thinking, adaptation to influential people, and introspection about belonging are explicit ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [McBroom](#mcbroom-entry)).
- **Body/health:** These entries supply no specific body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Partner conversations or reports, youth, frank exchange, and attentive listeners are concrete examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Business discussion and scientific or commercial cooperation are explicit; McBroom also describes a compelling communication style.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Fluent or refined communication does not guarantee personal growth; Sandbach expressly allows cleverness that avoids it. ABC's soul language is not further defined.

## Contradictions

Ease and refinement of communication coexist with the possibility of avoiding growth through cleverness. These claims address different aspects of thought and speech rather than directly contradicting one another. McBroom's outward communication also sits alongside inward questions of belonging.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through thought and communication involving other people. Business conversations, frank exchange, and scientific or commercial cooperation give this a practical setting. Partner conversations or reports and a refined style that draws listeners extend the same theme into personal exchange. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [McBroom](#mcbroom-entry))

The entries also turn communication inward. Munkasey describes analyzing meaningful daily contacts and adapting to influential examples; McBroom includes introspection about where one belongs. Sandbach makes growth depend on flexible thinking and releasing unhelpful ideas, with cleverness that avoids growth as an alternative. The common thread is how exchange and reflection shape one's relation to others and oneself, with fluency distinct from guaranteed development. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [McBroom](#mcbroom-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
