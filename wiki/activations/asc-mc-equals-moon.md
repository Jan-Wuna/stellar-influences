## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

One's behavior toward women. Professional relations to the public or women.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 58, “Meridian + Ascendant,” Moon row; checked on the actual scan. The possessive apostrophe is restored.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

An ensouled woman as partner, a woman in my surroundings, my warmhearted partner, my place among the people, my wife as partner, the place of my wife, the space of my people, hour and minute at the place, surroundings of my wife, and soulful feelings of the partner.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 60, one-based PDF p. 29, “Meridian + Aszendent,” Moon glyph row; checked on the actual scan. The first two “Frau” clauses mean woman, while “meine Frau” later permits wife. “Gemütvoller” is warmhearted, and “seelenvolle Gefühle” means soulful feelings, not stuff.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

One's personal attitude to life as governed by feeling, seeking a cordial understanding with others, a person guided primarily by instinct. Attitude to the female principle in general. Association with the female sex.

Source reference: Reinhold Ebertin, printed p. 307, one-based PDF p. 309, coded Moon entry 1108 under “Ascendant/Medium Coeli”; checked on the actual scan. Gendered terminology remains the source's own.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Staying in tune with one’s emotions and maintaining awareness of what is going on in one’s inner life enhances positive personality changes and promotes personal growth. Spiritual progress may come through leading a simple life or through the carrying out of ordinary tasks, or through nurturing and emotionally supporting other people. One’s feelings about one’s place in the world may fluctuate continually.

Source reference: John Sandbach, one-based PDF p. 122, Moon paragraph under “Planets activating the Ascendant/Midheaven midpoint”; checked on the actual page. PDF p. 121 is the chapter start. The split word “carry ing” is joined.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `364`

#### Munkasey Entry

A strong sense of attachment to the rhythms of life; sensitivity about people you encounter and how you use these impressions to recognize yourself for what you are and what you contribute to your own progress in life.

Source reference: Michael Munkasey, printed p. 364, one-based PDF p. 365, “ASCENDANT/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points,” Moon row; checked on the actual page.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Feeling and sensitivity shape relations with others and one's sense of place among them ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Instinct, cordial understanding, awareness of inner life, and sensitivity to impressions of people are explicit.
- **Body/health:** These entries supply no specific body or health claim; rhythms of life are not a defined physical condition.
- **Social/relationship:** Emotional support, a warmhearted partner, and professional relations with women or the public are source examples; gendered claims remain attributed.
- **Events/manifestations:** Ordinary tasks, simple living, nurturing others, and public or professional contact are concrete settings; hour/minute clauses give no timing method.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Sandbach allows fluctuating feelings about one's place while also describing constructive emotional awareness; fluctuation is not automatically failure or pathology.

## Contradictions

The entries combine feeling-based contact with opportunities for self-understanding or growth. Sandbach's fluctuating sense of place qualifies a fixed emotional reading without contradicting his constructive examples. Historical women/female-principle clauses remain distinct from a universal claim about gender or temperament.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through felt relations with others and one's sense of place among them. Cordial understanding, instinct, and sensitivity to people connect the emotional and social examples. Witte's professional contact with women or the public and ABC's woman or wife as partner remain particular historical source forms of that relational range. ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

Emotional awareness also becomes a means of self-understanding. Sandbach describes spiritual progress through simple living, ordinary tasks, or nurturing and supporting others; Munkasey describes using impressions of people to recognize oneself and one's progress. Sandbach's possible fluctuation in feelings about one's place remains part of the range, without an added judgment that such feeling necessarily weakens purpose. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))
