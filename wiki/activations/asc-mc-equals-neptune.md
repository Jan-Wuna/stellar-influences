## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be deceived by others. Experiencing deceptions with others. Unstable professional conditions or associates.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My swapped partner, my fine partner, my partner in the air, my weak partner, my unknown partner, minute in the air, psychic weakness of the andre, and water in my environment

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Feeling ill at ease in one's circle, emotional suffering, need to hide one's true nature by feigning, simulating or pretending. The misfortune to be surrounded by deceitful or bad people, experiencing disappointments.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Tuning in to subtle energies and developing one’s psychic sensing can greatly further the spiritualizing of one’s personality. As one sees subtler and subtler aspects of one’s identity one is able to let go of limiting self-notions and rise above the restrictions of one’s personality. Identifying more and more with cosmic realities serves to further the spiritual work one is engaging in in this lifetime.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `364`

#### Munkasey Entry

Gives you both intuitive insight into others, and yet at the same time clouds your assessment of their intentions; brings an inability to plan or foresee the consequences of your daily activities on the personal growth you encounter.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/MC = Neptune`: a porous personality, aspiration toward subtler realities, and a life direction shaped by vision, impressionability, or sacrifice. Main caution: deception and loss of footing: false appearances, unsuitable associates, emotional suffering, and confusion about who one is or where one belongs.
