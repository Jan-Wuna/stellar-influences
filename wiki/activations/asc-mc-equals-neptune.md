## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be deceived by others. Experiencing deceptions with others. Unstable professional conditions or associates.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 58, “Meridian + Ascendant,” Neptune row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My disappointed partner, my sensitive partner, my partner in the air, my weak partner, my unknown partner, my uncertain partner, my future partner, a minute at the place in the air, inner weakness of the other, and water in my surroundings.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 61, one-based PDF p. 29, “Meridian + Aszendent,” Neptune glyph row; checked on the actual scan. All ten clauses are retained, including uncertain and future partner. “Enttäuschter” means disappointed, “feinfühliger” sensitive, and “seelische Schwäche” inner weakness.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Feeling ill at ease in one's circle, emotional suffering, need to hide one's true nature by feigning, simulating or pretending. The misfortune to be surrounded by deceitful or bad people, experiencing disappointments.

Source reference: Reinhold Ebertin, printed pp. 307–308, one-based PDF pp. 309–310, coded Neptune entry 1115 and its final word's continuation; checked on both actual pages.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Tuning in to subtle energies and developing one’s psychic sensing can greatly further the spiritualizing of one’s personality. As one sees subtler and subtler aspects of one’s identity one is able to let go of limiting self-notions and rise above the restrictions of one’s personality. Identifying more and more with cosmic realities serves to further the spiritual work one is engaging in in this lifetime.

Source reference: John Sandbach, one-based PDF pp. 122–123, Neptune paragraph and continuation; checked on both actual pages. PDF p. 121 is the Ascendant/Midheaven chapter start.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `364`

#### Munkasey Entry

Gives you both intuitive insight into others, and yet at the same time clouds your assessment of their intentions; brings an inability to plan or foresee the consequences of your daily activities on the personal growth you encounter.

Source reference: Michael Munkasey, printed p. 364, one-based PDF p. 365, “ASCENDANT/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points,” Nep row; checked on the actual page.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Uncertainty in relations or professional conditions, alongside subtle perception and spiritual self-understanding ([Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Emotional discomfort and concealment appear in Ebertin; Munkasey combines intuition with clouded judgment, while Sandbach describes releasing limiting self-notions.
- **Body/health:** Inner weakness and emotional suffering are source descriptions without a specific clinical condition.
- **Social/relationship:** Disappointed, sensitive, unknown, uncertain, or future partners are distinct ABC examples; deception is not asserted of every partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** Unstable professional conditions or associates and water in one's surroundings are explicit; the air/minute phrases give no method or definite event.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The constructive spiritual account and adverse social accounts differ materially in emphasis. Intuitive insight does not guarantee accurate judgment of intentions.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize deception, unstable conditions, or emotional suffering. Sandbach instead describes spiritual development through subtle perception, while Munkasey explicitly combines insight and clouded assessment. These differences remain visible; none proves that every intuition is true or every associate deceptive.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through uncertain relations, impressions, and professional conditions. Deception, discomfort within one's circle, and disappointed or uncertain partners give this an adverse social form. ABC also retains sensitive or future partners and literal water nearby as separate examples. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

Perception has a constructive possibility as well as limits. Munkasey explicitly joins intuitive insight to clouded assessment of intentions and difficulty foreseeing consequences. Sandbach describes subtle sensing as a way to release limiting self-notions within his spiritual framework. The synthesis therefore preserves spiritual openness alongside uncertainty, without equating intuition with reliable knowledge or deception with every relationship. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
