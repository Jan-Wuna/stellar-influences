## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Developments or changes in the professional life or in relation to others. Professional changes effect the native’s personality.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development of an animated environment, development with my partner, my growing environment, my changing partners, spiritual development of the partner, changes between me and other, growing partner souls, and changes in my environment

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

An unusual person in unusual surroundings, a fascinating personality. The power to exercise a strong influence upon the people in one's environment.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One has extremely strong personality traits which can cause one to resist spir itual growth, if one is fearful, but which can help one to grow if one is willing to delve into the depths of one’s obsessions to understand and release them at a very deep level.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `364`

#### Munkasey Entry

Heightens your resolve to use others and their talents before you drop them and proceed with your life; brings depression over your lack of recognition from others; firms up your intentions to gain notoriety at any price.

## Derived Synthesis
