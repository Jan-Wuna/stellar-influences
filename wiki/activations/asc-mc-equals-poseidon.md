## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

One own’s intellectual agreement with others.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 58, “Meridian + Ascendant,” Poseidon row; checked on the actual scan. The awkward printed “One own’s” is retained, with its apostrophe restored.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Ensouled spiritualized surroundings, the partner's spirit-soul, my insight for the surroundings, my cultivated surroundings, a minute of truth at the place, inner clarity of the partner, a spiritualized soul partner, and truth between me and the partner.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 61, one-based PDF p. 29, “Meridian + Aszendent,” Poseidon glyph row; checked on the actual scan. Soul/spirit terms are retained broadly; “seelische Klarheit” is inner clarity, not necessarily religious spirituality.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Intellectual agreement, insight, clarity, and truth in relation with others ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC names one's insight and the partner's inner clarity; no fixed teaching or counseling disposition is stated.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Agreement with others and truth between person and partner are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Cultivated or spiritualized surroundings and a local minute of truth are source examples without a specified event or timing method.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Soul/spirit wording remains undefined; the entries do not establish impracticality, moral superiority, or a professional advisory role.

## Contradictions

Witte's intellectual agreement and ABC's clarity or truth overlap without a stated opposition. ABC's spiritualized and cultivated settings add scope but do not independently define the awkward Witte sentence or every soul-related term.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through intellectual agreement and clarity in relations with others. Witte names intellectual agreement; ABC adds insight concerning the surroundings, the partner's inner clarity, and truth between person and partner. Cultivated or spiritualized surroundings give further settings for those claims. The synthesis preserves that intellectual and inner-life range without inferring teaching, counseling, or a necessary loss of practical grounding. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
