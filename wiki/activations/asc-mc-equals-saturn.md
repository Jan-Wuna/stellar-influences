## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Professional difficulties. To separate from others.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 58, “Meridian + Ascendant,” Saturn row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Patience of my partner, patience with my partner, concentration on my partner, my inhibited partner, my partner in old age, my tasks in the surroundings, my problems in the surroundings, disturbances in my surroundings, and separation from my partner.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 61, one-based PDF p. 29, “Meridian + Aszendent,” Saturn glyph row; checked on the actual scan. The first two clauses distinguish the partner's patience from patience with the partner.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

The inhibited type, feelings of inferiority, moods of depression. Separation, mourning or bereavement.

Source reference: Reinhold Ebertin, printed p. 307, one-based PDF p. 309, coded Saturn entry 1113; checked on the actual scan. Psychological wording remains attributed source description.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A feeling that there is an unbridgeable rift between who one is versus who one wants to be and could be. Spiritual progress wants to be slow, steady, and grounded. Identifying with negativity cuts one off from spiritual progress.

Source reference: John Sandbach, one-based PDF p. 122, Saturn paragraph under “Planets activating the Ascendant/Midheaven midpoint”; checked on the actual page. PDF p. 121 is the chapter start.

This makes it more difficult for you to recognize the motives and intentions of the people you encounter daily; sensing disappointment from others about the way you have conducted your life; simple plans for self growth.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Difficulty or separation in relations, with patience and steady personal development also represented ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Inhibition, inferiority, a felt gap between actual and hoped-for self, and difficulty reading others are attributed source possibilities ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
- **Body/health:** Ebertin's moods of depression and ABC's old-age setting do not specify a clinical condition.
- **Social/relationship:** Patience of or with a partner, concentration on the partner, and separation from others or partner are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Professional difficulties, disturbances nearby, separation, mourning, and bereavement are source examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Simple plans and slow, steady spiritual progress remain constructive possibilities; sensed disappointment does not prove others' intentions or make estrangement inevitable.

## Contradictions

Difficulty, inhibition, or separation recur, while ABC includes patience and Munkasey includes simple growth plans. Sandbach pairs the felt identity gap with slow, grounded spiritual progress. These qualify an exclusively blocked reading without directly contradicting the adverse examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through difficulty, inhibition, or separation involving others. Professional problems, partner disturbances, and loss give this concrete relational forms, while patience of or with a partner remains another explicit possibility. Ebertin's inferiority and depressed moods describe an inward aspect of the adverse range. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry))

Personal development is qualified rather than excluded. Sandbach describes a felt gap between actual and hoped-for identity, alongside slow, steady spiritual progress; Munkasey includes difficulty reading people and sensed disappointment, but also simple growth plans. These entries preserve a measured route for development without turning every difficulty into permanent estrangement or a clinical diagnosis. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))
