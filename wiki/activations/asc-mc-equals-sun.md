## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To have close professional contact with others. Working together.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 58, “Meridian + Ascendant,” Sun row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Ensouled human surroundings, personal surroundings, I and other people, a man in my surroundings, my local center, my personal partner, my personal surroundings, a partner for body and soul, day and minute at the place, and surroundings of my husband.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 60, one-based PDF p. 29, “Meridian + Aszendent,” Sun glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Beseelte” is ensouled; the two personal-surroundings clauses are retained separately, with their different possessive wording.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

The relationship between body and soul, a person's attitude to other persons. The man in his sphere of life and activity. Going together, living together, attitude to the male principle in general.

Source reference: Reinhold Ebertin, printed p. 307, one-based PDF p. 309, coded Sun entry 1107; checked on the actual scan. Gendered terminology remains source-native.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The ability to be conscious and objective about one’s own evolution. The need for one’s major goals in life to be one’s that further one’s development rather than just safely and easily reachable ones. Egotism standing in the way of personal progress.

Source reference: John Sandbach, one-based PDF pp. 121–122, Sun paragraph and continuation under “Planets activating the Ascendant/Midheaven midpoint”; checked on both actual pages.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `364`

#### Munkasey Entry

Acclaim and recognition received for efforts at improving the quality of your personality and awareness; your contributions to life and the world as gained from personal experiences and interaction with other people.

Source reference: Michael Munkasey, printed p. 364, one-based PDF p. 365, “ASCENDANT/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points,” Sun row; checked on the actual page.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Personal contact, living or working with others, and awareness of one's development ([Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Conscious assessment of development and goals is explicit; Sandbach includes egotism as a possible obstacle.
- **Body/health:** Body-and-soul relationship or partnership is named, without a specific medical condition.
- **Social/relationship:** Close professional contact, working or living together, and a personal partner are concrete examples; gendered male/husband wording remains attributed.
- **Events/manifestations:** Recognition for efforts at improving personality or awareness is Munkasey's example; ABC's day/minute clause gives no timing method.
- **Conflicts/notes:** External recognition and inner development are distinct claims; Sandbach's qualified progress does not establish a guaranteed outcome from every interaction.

## Contradictions

The entries connect personal relations with activity or development, but emphasize different aspects. Munkasey supplies recognition for efforts at improvement, while Sandbach qualifies progress by the goals chosen and possible egotism. These are not a guarantee that recognition and inward progress always coincide.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through personal involvement with others: close professional contact, working or living together, and a partner for body and soul. Ebertin also describes one's attitude to others within the sphere of life and activity. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry))

Personal experience becomes material for development in the other entries. Munkasey names contributions and recognition arising from interaction and efforts at greater awareness. Sandbach emphasizes conscious assessment of one's development and goals that advance it, with egotism as a possible obstacle. Together these passages connect shared life with self-development while preserving its qualifications. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
