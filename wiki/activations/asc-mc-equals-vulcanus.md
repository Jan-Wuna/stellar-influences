## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To exercise power with other people. Joint proceedings.

Source reference: Witte, Rudolph & Lefeldt, one-based PDF p. 58, “Meridian + Ascendant,” Vulcanus row; checked on the actual scan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Others influence me, energy of my partner, power of my partner, my influence on the partner, my influence on the surroundings, my strength for the surroundings, psychological violence of the partner, psychological strength of the partner, and psychological partner influence.

Source reference: Udo Rudolph, printed p. 61, one-based PDF p. 29, “Meridian + Aszendent,” Vulcanus glyph row; checked on the actual scan. “Kraft” retains strength, distinct from “Macht” power; “seelisch” concerns inner or psychological life here, without specifying a paranormal power.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Power or influence is exercised with others and moves between person, partner, and surroundings ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC explicitly includes psychological strength, influence, and violence as separate possibilities; no fixed motive is supplied.
- **Body/health:** Strength and partner energy are named without a specific physical condition or injury claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Others' influence on the person and the person's influence on the partner are distinct directions.
- **Events/manifestations:** Exercising power with others and joint proceedings are Witte's concrete examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Joint power and psychological violence must both remain visible; neither source states that every collaboration becomes coercive or publicly prominent.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe power or influence involving others. ABC adds different directions of influence and psychological violence, which qualifies a wholly benign reading without turning all joint proceedings into abuse.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Asc/MC = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through power and influence involving others. Witte gives exercising power together and joint proceedings; ABC distinguishes others influencing the person, the person's influence on the partner, and influence or strength in the surroundings. It also explicitly includes psychological violence alongside psychological strength. The interpretation retains those different directions and qualities of influence without assigning every joint action a coercive motive or a public role. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
