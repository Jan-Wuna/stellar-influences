## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/MC = Vulcanus`: commanding group operations, strong public impact, and relationships organized around force, stamina, or the ability to move matters through with others. Main caution: overpoweringness: the same collective strength that makes this effective can become coercive or too heavy-handed if not controlled.
