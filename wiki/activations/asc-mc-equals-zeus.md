## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To incite others. To incentive others. Aspirations that one shares with a partner.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 58, Meridian + Ascendant, Zeus row. The awkward printed wording “To incentive others” is retained.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Fire in my surroundings, a fiery partner soul, direction of my partner, my striving at the place, my purposeful partner, my efforts for the other, my partner achievement, my goals for the surroundings, and inner partner direction.

Source reference: printed page 61, one-based PDF page 29, Meridian + Aszendent, Zeus row. “Führung meines Partners” is direction or leadership of the partner; the genitive does not settle who leads whom. “Seelische Partner-Führung” concerns inner or psychological partner direction, without an explained method.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Inciting others and sharing aspirations with a partner, alongside effort, purpose and direction in one's surroundings.
- **Psychology:** A fiery or purposeful partner and inner partner direction; the entry does not define a leadership style.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Shared aspirations and efforts for another person; the direction of leadership remains partly ambiguous.
- **Events/manifestations:** Witte names incitement; ABC adds partner achievement, local striving and literal fire in the surroundings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries overlap in purposeful engagement with others. They do not establish that this necessarily causes pressure, strain or conflict; fire remains a distinct literal ABC clause.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/MC = Zeus` describes inciting others and pursuing aspirations shared with a partner. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes that shared purpose explicit; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a purposeful partner, effort for the other person, partner achievement and goals in one's surroundings. Together these support an interpretation centered on initiative and effort in relation to another person. ABC's leadership wording leaves the direction of influence partly open, while its literal fire clause supplies a separate possible manifestation.
