## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To go with others very deep into the bottom of a matter.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Admetos row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blocked ideas of the partner, a restricted cultural place, consolidation of the culture of the surroundings, a thorough idea of the partner, a reasonable cycle of the surroundings, deepening the truth of the other, and resistance to insights concerning the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 137, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Admetos row. “Gründliche” means thorough; “vernünftiger Umwelt-Kreislauf” is retained as a reasonable cycle of the surroundings, whose mechanism is not explained. “Umwelt-Kultur” concerns culture in the surroundings without an explicit ecological meaning.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Going deeply into a matter with others, with thoroughness and cultural consolidation alongside blocked ideas or restriction.
- **Psychology:** Thorough thinking and deepening another's truth; resistance to insights is also represented.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Joint investigation and the partner's ideas, rather than a specified scholarly profession or relationship type.
- **Events/manifestations:** Restriction of a cultural place and consolidation of surrounding culture; ABC also names an undefined reasonable cycle.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Depth and consolidation coexist with blockage and resistance. The entries do not make every deep inquiry mentally immobile or describe these alternatives as a sequence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Admetos` centers on going deeply into a matter with other people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states the joint investigation directly; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds thorough ideas, deepening another's truth and consolidating culture in the surroundings. Its blocked partner ideas, restricted cultural place and resistance to insights keep the restrictive side visible alongside depth. These are distinct possibilities in the entry, rather than a claim that thorough investigation necessarily becomes fixation.
