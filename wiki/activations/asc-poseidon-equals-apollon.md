## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To enlarge and widen the mental horizon together with many others.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Apollon row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of the culture of the surroundings, a successful cultural place, mental or spiritual freedom of the partner, cultural knowledge of the partner, a reasonable trading partner, reasonable science concerning the surroundings, and experiences of truth for the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 137, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Apollon row. Both “vernünftiger” and “vernünftige” mean reasonable, not impoverished. “Geistige” permits mental or spiritual scope; “Umwelt-Wissenschaft” does not name a specific scientific discipline here.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Widening the mental horizon with many others and expanding cultural experience in the surroundings.
- **Psychology:** Partner freedom, cultural knowledge and experiences of truth.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Many people participate in the widening of outlook; ABC also names a reasonable trading partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** Cultural expansion and a successful cultural place, with science concerning the surroundings among the ABC examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries share an expansive intellectual or cultural emphasis. Neither states that expansion necessarily disperses attention; ABC does not describe poverty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Apollon` describes widening one's outlook through involvement with many others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names the shared enlargement of the mental horizon; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places cultural expansion, partner freedom and knowledge, reasonable trade and experiences of truth in the surroundings. The supported theme is a broadening of intellectual or cultural experience with other people, without specifying an educational institution or making loss of focus an inevitable cost.
