## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Spiritually interested members in the family. Together with men of the same world-concept.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Cupido row. The gendered wording is retained as printed.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Shared local culture, shared ideas concerning the surroundings, shared cultural space, an ideal-based partner community, art and culture in the surroundings, a cultural community at the place, synthesis for the culture of the surroundings, and a reasonable partner community.

Source reference: printed page 137, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Cupido row. “Ideelle” concerns ideas or ideals; “Synthese” is synthesis. The final “vernünftige Partner-Gemeinschaft” clause is restored.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Shared outlook expressed through family, partnership and cultural community.
- **Psychology:** Spiritual interests, common ideas or ideals and a shared world-concept.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Spiritually interested family members and people sharing a worldview; ABC adds a reasonable community of partners.
- **Events/manifestations:** Shared local culture, cultural space, art and a cultural community at the place.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte emphasizes family and shared worldview, while ABC extends into local cultural activity. Neither specifies how agreement is maintained or how disagreement is handled.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Cupido` describes association through shared spiritual interests, ideas and culture. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places spiritually interested family members alongside people with the same world-concept; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the theme into shared cultural space, art, local community and an ideal-based or reasonable partnership community. The common thread is a shared outlook taking communal form, with no stated requirement that the group becomes insular.
