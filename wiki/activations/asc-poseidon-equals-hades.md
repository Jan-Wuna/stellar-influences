## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be with others who are mentally below the native, who are mediocre. Together with stupid men.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Hades row. These derogatory assessments and gendered wording are the source's judgments, not an objective measure of anyone's intelligence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous culture in the surroundings, a historical cultural place, local culture of the past, cultural criminality of others, lack of reason in the partner, disadvantageous truth concerning the surroundings, and renunciation of the truth of the other.

Source reference: printed page 137, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Hades row. “Mangelnde Vernunft” is lack of reason; “Verzicht” is renunciation. The compound “Kultur-Kriminalität” is retained broadly because no particular offense is specified.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Adverse judgments of others' reasoning and of surrounding culture, alongside historical places and culture of the past.
- **Psychology:** Witte's disparaging intellectual comparisons and ABC's lack-of-reason clause are attributed assessments.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Company judged mentally inferior by Witte; ABC adds a partner's lack of reason and renunciation of another's truth.
- **Events/manifestations:** Historical cultural places, past local culture, dangerous surrounding culture and broadly stated cultural criminality.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Historical culture is a separate clause, with no stated implication that it is criminal. The source does not establish contamination, cynicism or contempt as consequences for the person.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Hades` brings together adverse assessments of reasoning, truth and culture in one's associations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) expresses this through derogatory comparisons of other people with the native; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds lack of reason in a partner, disadvantageous truth and renunciation of another's truth. ABC also names historical cultural places and culture of the past as distinct manifestations. These passages support an attributed account of troubled intellectual or cultural relations, while providing no objective basis for ranking the intelligence of actual people.
