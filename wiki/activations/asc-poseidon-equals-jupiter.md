## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Friends who get advice, counsel, understanding and wisdom from the native.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Jupiter row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success through the truth of the other, a successful partner idea, a successful cultural place, the financial culture of a place, joy in the insight of the other, joy in cultivated surroundings, a place for legal truth, and advantageous culture in the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 136, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Jupiter row. “Rechts-Wahrheit” is legal truth; the compounds financial culture and legal truth have no more specific examples in this entry.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Advice and understanding among friends, alongside success or joy through another's truth and insight.
- **Psychology:** Wisdom offered to friends and appreciation of another person's insight or cultivated surroundings.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Witte directs counsel from the native to friends; ABC also values truth and ideas from the other person.
- **Events/manifestations:** A successful cultural place or partner idea, advantageous surrounding culture and broadly stated financial culture or legal truth.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The direction of contribution differs across the entries. The favorable language does not specify a professional counseling role or guaranteed success.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Jupiter` describes helpful understanding in one's associations. In the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), friends receive advice, counsel and wisdom from the native; in the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), another person's truth, insight or ideas can be a source of success and joy. The shared theme is benefit through intellectual or cultural exchange, with ABC extending it to successful cultural places, financial culture and legal truth. The two directions of contribution remain visible rather than making the native the sole source of understanding.
