## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Separation of minds. To separate from others as a result of their morals.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Mars row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Insightful actions of the partner, mental or spiritual activity with the partner, mental or spiritual work at the place, impulses of ideas from the partner, cultural activity for the surroundings, a reasonable work partner, and impulses of truth from the other.

Source reference: printed page 136, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Mars row. “Vernünftiger Arbeits-Partner” is a reasonable work partner; “geistige” permits mental or spiritual scope.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Acting on ideas and moral judgments in one's associations, with separation in Witte and shared activity in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Moral differences can prompt separation in Witte; ABC emphasizes insight, ideas and truth impulses.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Separation because of others' morals stands beside mental or spiritual activity with a reasonable work partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** Separation of minds or people, shared intellectual work and cultural activity for the surroundings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The emphases differ materially. ABC's cooperative activity is not described as a stage leading to Witte's separation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Mars` concerns ideas and judgments expressed through action in one's associations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes separation of minds and parting because of others' morals. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) instead supplies insightful partner actions, shared mental or spiritual activity, cultural work and a reasonable work partner. The interpretation therefore retains both separation over moral differences and constructive activity around ideas; the entries provide no condition that would select one outcome or turn their contrast into a necessary sequence.
