## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Union of ideas with others. To be brought together with others for the same idea.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Mercury row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reports about a cultural place, conversations with the partner about ideas or ideals, youth as a cultural partner, clear reports concerning the surroundings, cultural media at the place, cultural news of a place, young people's insights concerning the surroundings, and words of truth from the partner.

Source reference: printed page 136, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Mercury row. “Ideelle Partner-Gespräche” concerns partner conversations about ideas or ideals; “Erkenntnisse” retains insights rather than a specified body of environmental science.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Being brought together by the same idea and communicating cultural information or insight.
- **Psychology:** Shared ideas, clarity in reports and words of truth from a partner.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Union around an idea, partner conversations and youth as a cultural partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** Reports, cultural media and local cultural news, including young people's insights about their surroundings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries connect shared ideas with communication. They do not specify a teaching profession or explain whether agreement is open to revision.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Mercury` describes association through a shared idea and its communication. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names being brought together with others for the same idea; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops this through partner conversations, cultural reports, local media and news, young people's insights and words of truth from the partner. Common ideas therefore provide both a reason for meeting and material for exchange, with the source leaving the group's form and handling of disagreement unspecified.
