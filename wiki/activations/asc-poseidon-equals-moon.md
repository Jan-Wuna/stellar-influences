## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The spiritual susceptibility of other people. To become publicly known through spiritual relations with others. Spiritualized women.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Moon row. The spiritual and gendered descriptions remain source-native.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Insight of other women, feelingful ideas of the partner, clear feelings of the other, a cultural place of a people, spiritualized surroundings of the woman, a spiritualized female partner, a reasonable woman as partner, and culture of another people.

Source reference: printed page 136, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Moon row. “Gefühlvolle Ideen” concerns ideas imbued with feeling; “klare Gefühle” is clear feelings, and “vernünftige Frau” retains reasonable woman. The entry's gender categories are historical source wording.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Spiritual relations with others, alongside women's insight, a partner's feelings and cultural surroundings.
- **Psychology:** Spiritual susceptibility belongs to other people in Witte; ABC names feelingful ideas and clear feelings of the other.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Spiritual relations, spiritually described women or female partners, and a reasonable woman as partner in the source's gendered examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Becoming publicly known through spiritual relations; cultural places and the culture of another people.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Public recognition is a specific Witte possibility, not evidence of universal popularity. Neither source establishes nurturing behavior or inevitable emotional overexposure.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Moon` describes spiritual relations and insight or feeling encountered through others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places spiritual susceptibility in other people and includes becoming publicly known through spiritual relations. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds women's insight, a partner's feelingful ideas, the other's clear feelings and cultural settings. These gendered examples remain attributed to the source; the supported interpretation centers on others' spiritual or emotional qualities and the cultural relationships through which they are encountered.
