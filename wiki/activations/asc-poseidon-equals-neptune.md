## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others are mentally or spiritually led astray or they are characterized by the higher and finer qualities of the mind.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Neptune row. The alternatives and evaluative “higher and finer” wording are retained as stated.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Disappointing culture in the surroundings, insight into the subtle surroundings, fine sensitivity to the culture of the surroundings, a cultural place by the water, weak local culture, unclear partner ideas, uncertainty at the place of truth, and spiritualized subtle surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 137, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Neptune row. “Enttäuschende” means disappointing; the omitted “Feingefühl für Umwelt-Kultur” clause is restored. “Feinstofflich” is retained as subtle, with no physical or perceptual mechanism explained.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others' mental or spiritual qualities can involve being led astray or the refinement Witte describes; ABC joins sensitivity with cultural uncertainty.
- **Psychology:** Fine sensitivity and insight into subtle surroundings coexist with unclear partner ideas and uncertainty.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health delineation; subtle surroundings are undefined source terminology.
- **Social/relationship:** The mental or spiritual condition belongs to others in Witte; ABC specifically names partner ideas.
- **Events/manifestations:** Disappointing or weak culture and a cultural place by water, alongside spiritualized subtle surroundings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte offers two alternatives without a selection rule. ABC's sensitivity clause must remain alongside disappointment and lack of clarity.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Neptune` describes contrasting mental or spiritual qualities in one's associations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) allows others to be led astray or to possess what it calls higher and finer qualities of mind. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) likewise keeps fine sensitivity and subtle insight beside disappointing culture, unclear partner ideas and uncertainty. Sensitivity and uncertainty both belong in the interpretation; the entries give no condition that selects one of Witte's alternatives. ABC's cultural place by water remains a separate literal example.
