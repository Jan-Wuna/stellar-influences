## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be with people who go through mental or spiritual transformations.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Pluto row. The two Pluto glyph variants precede this single sentence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Unfolding of a local culture, development of a cultural place, development in spiritualized surroundings, mental or spiritual unfolding of the partner, reasonable partner development, growing insight of the other, and changing truth of the other.

Source reference: printed page 137, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Pluto row. “Vernünftige” means reasonable, not poor; “vergeistigter” retains spiritualized and “geistige” permits mental or spiritual scope.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Association with people undergoing mental or spiritual transformation, alongside cultural and partner development.
- **Psychology:** The partner's unfolding and another person's growing insight or changing truth.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Transformation is occurring in the people one is with; ABC also names reasonable partner development.
- **Events/manifestations:** Unfolding local culture, development of a cultural place and development within spiritualized surroundings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries share a developmental emphasis without specifying a crisis, coercion or conversion process. “Changing truth” has no further explanation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Pluto` describes contact with people whose mental or spiritual life is changing. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states that association directly; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends development to the partner, local culture and a cultural place, with growing insight and changing truth in the other person. The supported theme is intellectual, spiritual or cultural development encountered through others. Its scale and process remain open, so the passage does not require a radical crisis or pressure to convert.
