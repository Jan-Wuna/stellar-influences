## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others mentally draw the dividing line. Others hinder mentally. They are trying to teach you.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Saturn row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Tasks of a cultural place, a serious reasonable partner, disturbed culture in the surroundings, clarity about partner duties, concentration on the culture of the surroundings, a problematic partner idea, painful truth of the partner, and a spiritualized older partner.

Source reference: printed page 137, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Saturn row. The scan reads “ernster vernünftiger,” “gestörte” and “vergeistigter älterer,” restoring serious/reasonable, disturbed and spiritualized older rather than the former damaged wording.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Mental limits or hindrance from others, alongside cultural tasks, concentration and partner duties.
- **Psychology:** Serious reasoning, problematic ideas and painful truth; others' attempts to teach are explicit in Witte.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a bodily or health delineation; older partner is an age description.
- **Social/relationship:** Others draw a dividing line or hinder mentally; ABC also includes a reasonable partner and clarity about duties.
- **Events/manifestations:** Tasks of a cultural place, disturbed surrounding culture, concentration on culture and a spiritualized older partner.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Hindrance and instruction coexist with reasonable seriousness and concentration. The entries do not establish punitive teaching or a successful learning outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Saturn` describes mental limits encountered through others and serious attention to culture or partnership. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names others drawing a dividing line, hindering mentally and trying to teach. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds cultural tasks, partner duties, concentration and a serious reasonable partner, while retaining disturbed culture, problematic ideas and painful truth. The interpretation therefore includes both restriction and focused responsibility; the source does not say that hindrance is necessarily beneficial instruction.
