## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Association with mentally wide awake people. To be tutored and enlightened by others.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Sun row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Insight of another man, body culture at the place, the cultural center of a place, spiritualized surroundings of a person, a spiritualized man as partner, a reasonable man as partner, truth of another person, and a center for culture in the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 136, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Sun row. The opening “Einsicht eines anderen Mannes” is restored; “vernünftiger” means reasonable and “eines Menschen” means of a person. The male-partner examples remain source-native. “Körper-Kultur” is body culture without a specified practice or health outcome.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Association with mentally alert people and receiving instruction or enlightenment from others.
- **Psychology:** Another's insight or truth and the source's mentally awake, spiritualized or reasonable descriptions.
- **Body/health:** ABC names body culture at the place, but supplies no practice, diagnosis or bodily outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Others tutor or enlighten the native; ABC includes male-partner examples with spiritualized or reasonable qualities.
- **Events/manifestations:** A cultural center, a center for surrounding culture and spiritualized surroundings of a person.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries emphasize learning through others and cultural settings. They do not establish strengthened identity or excessive dependence as effects.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Sun` describes receiving insight or instruction through one's associations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places the native with mentally awake people who tutor or enlighten them; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds another person's insight or truth, reasonable or spiritualized male partners and cultural centers. The supported emphasis is learning and cultural contact through others. ABC's body-culture clause remains a distinct local example, while its gendered partner descriptions remain attributed to the source.
