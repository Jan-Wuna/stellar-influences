## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others suddenly come to an understanding, realization.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Uranus row. Both leading glyphs are Uranus variants.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A dynamic cultural place, influential reform of the surroundings, mental or spiritual impulses of the partner, new ideas of the other, new insights concerning the surroundings, sudden clarity at the place, technical ideas from the partner, and spiritualized technology in the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 137, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Uranus row. “Einflußreiche” means influential, not integrated. “Geistige” permits mental or spiritual scope; “Umwelt-Technik” is technology in the surroundings without a specified technical field.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sudden realization in others, new ideas and influential reform in the surroundings.
- **Psychology:** Partner impulses, new insights and sudden clarity.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Witte places understanding in others; ABC includes ideas and technical ideas received from the partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** A dynamic cultural place, reform, sudden local clarity and spiritualized surrounding technology.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries share a theme of new understanding. They do not establish disruption, instability or a frequency of sudden insight.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Uranus` describes sudden understanding and new ideas encountered through others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) puts the realization in other people; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds partner impulses and technical ideas, new insights into the surroundings and sudden clarity at the place. This new understanding has a cultural or practical setting in ABC's dynamic cultural place, influential reform and technology. The passages support novelty and clarity while leaving their consequences and frequency unspecified.
