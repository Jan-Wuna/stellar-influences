## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Religious acquaintances. To prefer people who are religiously inclined.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Venus row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joy in the insight of the other, harmony with a spiritualized partner, a place of art and culture, love culture of the partner, a place for harmony and truth, spiritualized love for the partner, and reasonable harmony with the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 136, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Venus row. “Vernünftige” means reasonable, not intense. “Liebes-Kultur” is retained as love culture without a specified practice or relationship form.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Religious preference in acquaintances, alongside joy, harmony, love and cultural surroundings.
- **Psychology:** Preference for religiously inclined people in Witte; joy in another's insight and spiritualized love in ABC.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Religious acquaintances, harmony with a spiritualized partner and reasonable harmony with the surroundings.
- **Events/manifestations:** A place of art and culture or a place for harmony and truth; partner love culture remains broadly stated.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's explicitly religious setting is narrower than ABC's artistic and cultural examples. Neither asserts sentimental idealization or attachment based only on piety.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Venus` describes valued associations through religion, insight and culture. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) specifically names religious acquaintances and preference for religiously inclined people. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens the setting to joy in another's insight, harmony and spiritualized love with a partner, and places of art, culture or truth. These support an interpretation of affinity and harmonious cultural or spiritual contact, while preserving Witte's narrower religious emphasis.
