## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others associate with the generality for mental or spiritual reasons.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Vernal Point row. “Generality” is retained as the printed wording for the general public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A public cultural place, public culture in the surroundings, ideas concerning the surroundings in public, spiritualized public surroundings, insights concerning the surroundings in public, and truth of the other in public life.

Source reference: printed page 136, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Vernal Point row. “Öffentliche” is public, not often; “vergeistigte öffentliche Umwelt” retains spiritualized public surroundings. The final clause keeps the other person as the subject of truth.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others' association with the general public for mental or spiritual reasons, in a setting of public culture and ideas.
- **Psychology:** Mental or spiritual motives and publicly expressed insight or truth.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Witte places public association in others; ABC retains the other's truth in public life.
- **Events/manifestations:** Public cultural places, surrounding culture, ideas and insights in public, and spiritualized public surroundings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Both entries share a public intellectual or cultural setting. A teaching role, audience size and loss of personal specificity are unstated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Vernal Point` describes mental, spiritual or cultural association in public life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places the motive for public association in others; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) makes the setting concrete through a public cultural place, public ideas and insights concerning the surroundings, and another person's truth in public life. The supported theme is the public expression and setting of those associations, without assigning the native a teaching role or a particular audience size.
