## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A intense mental influence of another person. Others are subject to influence.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Vulcanus row. The awkward printed “A intense” is retained.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

An influential cultural place, the partner's power of insight, mental or spiritual strength of the other, mental or spiritual partner influence, clear partner influence, powerful local culture, strengthening of culture in the surroundings, and reasonable influencing of the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 137, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Vulcanus row. “Vernünftige Beeinflussung der Umwelt” is reasonable influencing of the surroundings, not powerful influence from them. “Geistig” permits mental or spiritual scope; the former /3 was a misread ß.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Intense mental influence involving other people, with partner insight and cultural influence in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Power of insight, mental or spiritual strength and clear influence.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a bodily or health delineation; the named strength is mental or spiritual.
- **Social/relationship:** Another person's intense mental influence and others' susceptibility to influence; ABC also names partner influence.
- **Events/manifestations:** An influential cultural place, powerful local culture, strengthening surrounding culture and reasonable influence upon the surroundings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Influence is explicit, but teaching, coercion and indoctrination are not specified. The final ABC clause directs influence toward the surroundings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Vulcanus` describes strong mental or spiritual influence in one's associations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names another person's intense mental influence and others being subject to influence. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a partner's power of insight, clear partner influence and strong cultural surroundings, while also naming reasonable influence upon those surroundings. The supported emphasis is the strength and direction of influence; the entries leave its method open rather than identifying it with coercive teaching.
