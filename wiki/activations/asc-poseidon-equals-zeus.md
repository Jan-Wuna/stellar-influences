## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Purposeful spiritual guidance by others.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 98, Ascendant + Poseidon, Zeus row. Others provide the guidance.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Leading culture in the surroundings, mental or spiritual striving toward goals with others, ideas for shaping the surroundings, creative spiritualized surroundings, shaping culture with others, achievements of a cultural place, a creative spiritualized partner, and recognizing goals concerning the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 137, one-based PDF page 67, Aszendent + Poseidon, Zeus row. “Führende” is leading; “geistiges” permits mental or spiritual scope, and both “vergeistigte” qualifiers retain spiritualized. “Gestaltung” is shaping without a specified design profession or method.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Purposeful spiritual guidance from others and shared striving or cultural shaping around goals.
- **Psychology:** Mental or spiritual goals, creative ideas and recognition of goals concerning the surroundings.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Guidance comes from others in Witte; ABC also names striving and shaping culture with others and a creative partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** Leading surrounding culture, shaping one's surroundings and achievements of a cultural place.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Both entries emphasize direction and purpose. Their wording does not establish slogans, a fixed program or a necessarily restrictive agenda.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Poseidon = Zeus` describes purposeful guidance and shared intellectual, spiritual or cultural effort. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) directs spiritual guidance from others toward the native; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds striving toward goals with others, shaping culture, creative surroundings or partnership and cultural achievement. Purpose links guidance with joint creative activity, while the practical method and content of the goals remain unspecified.
