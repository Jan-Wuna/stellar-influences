## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Desire to dominate another meets with heavy resistance.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Admetos row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Restriction of influence involving the surroundings, energy and endurance of the partner, consolidation of partner influence, a violence blockade at the place, thorough violation of the surroundings, concentration of force at the place, strong depressions of the partner, and resistance to violence in the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 135, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Admetos row. “Gründliche Vergewaltigung der Umwelt” uses the literal rape/violation term for the surroundings; no specific act is explained. “Gewalt-Blockade” leaves the form of the blockade open. “Depressionen” remains attributed source terminology, without a clinical diagnosis or an established cause.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** A desire to dominate meeting heavy resistance, alongside restricted influence and consolidated partner strength.
- **Psychology:** Domination and resistance in Witte; ABC separately names the partner's endurance and strong depressions.
- **Body/health:** ABC's depression wording is an attributed claim without diagnostic criteria or a stated causal link to the other clauses.
- **Social/relationship:** Resistance to domination coexists with energy, endurance and consolidation of partner influence.
- **Events/manifestations:** A local violence blockade, violation of the surroundings, concentration of force and resistance to surrounding violence.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Restriction and resistance stand beside endurance and consolidation. The entries give no rule that every situation becomes a deadlock or accumulates pressure without release.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Admetos` describes a limit encountered when trying to dominate another person: the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly names heavy resistance. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds restricted influence, a local blockade and resistance to surrounding violence, while retaining partner endurance, consolidation of influence and concentrated force. The shared theme is force meeting restraint or resistance, with enduring strength also represented. ABC's depression and violation clauses remain specific attributed possibilities rather than necessary consequences of that encounter.
