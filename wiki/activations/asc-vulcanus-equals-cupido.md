## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others show their allied power.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Cupido row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

An influential local community, shared partner energy, joint violation of the surroundings, a violent community at the place, strength of a partner community, the place of a community's power, and a strong partner of a community.

Source reference: printed page 135, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Cupido row. All seven actual clauses replace unrelated energy-policy translation output. “Gemeinsame Vergewaltigung der Umwelt” uses the rape/violation term for the surroundings, with no specific act explained; the separate violent-community clause remains explicit.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others' allied power and the influence or strength of a community.
- **Psychology:** No separate individual psychological trait is stated; the emphasis is collective strength.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Shared partner energy, a partner community and a strong partner within a community.
- **Events/manifestations:** An influential local community, a place of communal power, joint violation of the surroundings and a violent local community.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte does not specify the alliance's conduct. ABC explicitly includes violent manifestations as well as community strength; no particular family, faction or institution is named.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Cupido` describes the power other people display through association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) calls it allied power; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives it local and relational form through an influential community, shared partner energy, communal strength and a strong community partner. ABC also names a violent community and joint violation of the surroundings, so collective strength cannot be read as uniformly favorable. The type of community and the particular act remain unspecified.
