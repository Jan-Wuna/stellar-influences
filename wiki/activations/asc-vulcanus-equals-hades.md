## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Meanness and baseness in actions of others.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Hades row. The moral assessments belong to the source.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous influence at the place, lack of energy in the partner, enormous criminality in the surroundings, criminal violence of others, inadequate spatial strength, disadvantageous partner influence, distress through violence at the place, a strong partner in distress, and hidden strength at the place.

Source reference: printed page 135, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Hades row. “Not” is distress or need; “Umwelt-Kriminalität” concerns criminality in the surroundings without naming a specific offense. Spatial strength and lack of energy are broad source terms, not diagnostic measurements.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others' actions judged mean or base, with dangerous influence, violence, distress and inadequate strength in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Witte's moral judgments remain attributed; ABC names lack of partner energy without defining a psychological condition.
- **Body/health:** Violence and energy lack are represented, but no injury, illness or diagnosis is specified.
- **Social/relationship:** Criminal violence of others and adverse partner influence; a strong partner in distress is a separate clause.
- **Events/manifestations:** Dangerous local influence, surrounding criminality, distress through violence and hidden local strength.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries strongly emphasize adversity, while hidden strength and a strong partner in distress preserve a wider range than uniformly malicious power. No motive is supplied for the violence clauses.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Hades` describes adverse actions and influence encountered through others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) characterizes others' actions as mean or base; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) makes the adverse range more concrete through dangerous influence, criminal violence, surrounding criminality and distress. ABC also retains a partner's lack of energy, inadequate spatial strength, hidden strength and a strong partner in distress. These remain distinct possibilities, without assigning every manifestation a malicious motive or turning energy lack into a diagnosis.
