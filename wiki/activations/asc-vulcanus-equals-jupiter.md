## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others use all their power for the best of the native. To be infected by optimism of others.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Jupiter row. “Infected by optimism” is the source's figurative wording.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful power in the surroundings, successful partner influence, financial strength of the partner, joy in the partner's strength, joy in partner influence, legal power at the place, legal authority for the surroundings, and advantage through spatial energy.

Source reference: printed page 134, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Jupiter row. “Rechts-Macht” and “Rechts-Gewalt” concern legal power or authority, not a judgment that violence is right. “Kraft” retains strength, distinct from power; spatial energy has no further mechanism here.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others' power directed to the native's benefit, with successful influence and advantage in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Optimism received through others and joy in a partner's strength or influence.
- **Body/health:** Witte's optimism metaphor supplies no infection or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Others act for the native's best interests; ABC names successful partner influence and financial strength.
- **Events/manifestations:** Successful surrounding power, local legal power or authority and advantage through spatial energy.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Both entries emphasize favorable uses of power. They do not guarantee permanent support or make legal authority an endorsement of violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Jupiter` describes benefit and encouragement through other people's power. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly directs their power toward the native's best interests and includes being affected by their optimism. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful partner influence, financial strength, joy in that influence or strength, and legal power or authority in the surroundings. The shared emphasis is favorable influence and advantage, with no stated duration or guarantee of support.
