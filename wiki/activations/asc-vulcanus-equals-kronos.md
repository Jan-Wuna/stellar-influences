## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To have to deal with people who exercise the power of the state, to feel their power.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Kronos row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special energy of the partner, influence of an independent partner, enormous energies in the surroundings, great force or violence at the place, power of the local hierarchy, a place of state power, strong authority of the partner, state influence upon the surroundings, and important spatial energy.

Source reference: printed page 135, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Kronos row. “Selbständigen” means independent; “Gewalt” can denote force or violence. The state and local-hierarchy clauses are explicit, while spatial energy remains undefined.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Experiencing the power of people acting for the state, with local hierarchy and partner authority in ABC.
- **Psychology:** No distinct emotional response or personality trait is stated; the partner is described as independent and authoritative.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** People exercising state power and a partner's influence or strong authority.
- **Events/manifestations:** A place of state power, local hierarchy, state influence, enormous energies and local force or violence.
- **Conflicts/notes:** State power and authority are explicit; coldness, rigidity and an executive profession are not specified. Independent partner influence remains a separate example.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Kronos` describes encountering authority through people who exercise substantial power. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) specifically names dealing with people who wield state power and feeling that power. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds local hierarchy, a place of state power, state influence and a partner's strong authority or independence. These support a governmental, hierarchical or partnership setting for powerful influence, with force or violence also present among ABC's possibilities; the source does not define the authority's temperament.
