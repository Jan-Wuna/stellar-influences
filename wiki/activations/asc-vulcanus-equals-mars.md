## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others have the power of disposal. “A tough guy”. Acrobats.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Mars row. The terse examples and “power of disposal” wording are retained without a more specific definition.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influential activities in the surroundings, energy impulses of the partner, violent activity of the partner, activity exercising power at the place, shared work involving strength, spatial-energy impulses, strong impulses of the partner, and vigorous partner influence.

Source reference: printed page 134, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Mars row. “Partnerschaftliche Kraft-Arbeit” concerns joint work involving strength; “tatkräftiger” retains vigorous or energetic action rather than guaranteeing effectiveness. Spatial-energy impulses have no stated mechanism.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others' power and forceful activity, including partner impulses and shared work involving strength.
- **Psychology:** Witte's “tough guy” is a terse source characterization; no universal motive or competitive trait is given.
- **Body/health:** Witte names acrobats; ABC includes work involving strength and violent activity, without a specified injury or health outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Others have the power of disposal; ABC names vigorous partner influence and shared exertion.
- **Events/manifestations:** Influential surrounding activities, local exercise of power, strong impulses and partner violence.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Physical skill and joint work remain beside violent activity. The entries do not make every exertion a fight or imply inevitable escalation.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Mars` describes power expressed through others' actions and strength. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places the power of disposal in others and gives the distinct examples of a “tough guy” and acrobats. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds strong partner impulses, vigorous influence and shared work involving strength, while explicitly including violent partner activity. Applied strength is the organizing theme, with skill, joint exertion and violence retained as different manifestations rather than an inevitable progression toward injury.
