## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be taxed, to be drawn upon heavily by others.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, MC row. The second phrase clarifies being taxed as being heavily drawn upon.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My influence at the place, my influence involving the surroundings, my energy for the surroundings, my spatial energy, my strength with the partner, psychological violence in the closer surroundings, psychological power of the partner, psychological partner influence, and a strong partner soul.

Source reference: printed page 134, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, MC row. All nine clauses are retained, including the formerly omitted surrounding-influence and psychological partner-influence clauses. “Meine Stärke beim Partner” is strength with the partner; “seelisch” concerns inner or psychological life without specifying a paranormal mechanism.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Being heavily drawn upon by others, alongside one's own influence and strength in the surroundings or with a partner.
- **Psychology:** ABC names psychological violence, partner power and influence, and a strong partner soul.
- **Body/health:** The entries give no specific bodily condition or health outcome; heavy demands do not establish exhaustion as a diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Others draw on the native in Witte; ABC also names my strength with the partner and the partner's psychological influence.
- **Events/manifestations:** Personal local influence, energy for the surroundings and psychological violence in the closer surroundings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's demands from others and ABC's own or partner influence have different directions. No public role, responsibility or source of the demands is specified.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = MC` describes personal involvement in strong demands and influence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places the native under heavy demands from others. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also names my influence, energy and strength with a partner, alongside the partner's psychological power or influence and psychological violence in the surroundings. The interpretation therefore preserves both being drawn upon and exerting influence; the entries do not make either direction universal or specify a resulting health condition.
