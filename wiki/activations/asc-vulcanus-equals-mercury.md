## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others are superior in thinking. To be strongly influenced by the opinion of others. Others don’t permit the native to have a free opinion.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Mercury row. The superiority comparison and denial of free opinion are the source's stated assessments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reports about violence at the place, influential conversations with the partner, energy of a young partner, conversations about spatial energy, youth violence at the place, news about violence against the surroundings, heavy traffic at the place, and a strong conversation partner.

Source reference: printed page 134, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Mercury row. “Gespräche” means conversations and “Gesprächs-Partner” is a singular conversation partner, without an added local qualifier. Spatial energy has no technical explanation in the entry.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Strong influence through others' opinions and conversations, with restricted free opinion in Witte.
- **Psychology:** Witte describes others as superior in thinking and the native as strongly influenced; these remain attributed assessments.
- **Body/health:** ABC mentions violence and youth violence but no specified bodily injury or diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Influential partner conversations, a strong conversation partner and the energy of a young partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** Reports or news of violence, conversations about spatial energy and heavy local traffic.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte explicitly includes denial of free opinion. ABC's conversational and traffic examples are broader and are not all described as coercive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Mercury` describes strong intellectual or conversational influence in one's associations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly includes others' opinions strongly influencing the native and denying free opinion. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds influential partner conversations and a strong conversation partner, while also naming reports of violence, youth energy and heavy traffic. Pressure on independent opinion is therefore a specific Witte claim within a wider set of communication and local manifestations, rather than a condition imposed on every ABC example.
