## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Women or persons in public who tend to violence or bring their power and influence into bearing.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Moon row. The alternatives and gendered examples are the source's wording, not a universal description of women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Energy of the female partner, a violent people at the place, a place of strength of a people, power of the people at the place, a place of power for a people, spatial energy of a people, strong partner feelings, a strong woman as partner, a strong woman at the place, and strength of a people in the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 134, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Moon row. “Volk” denotes a people collectively; “Kraft” retains strength, distinct from “Macht” power. “Partner-Gefühle” is retained without specifying whose feelings are directed toward whom. The source's gender and collective categories remain attributed.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Power and influence expressed by women or people in public, with collective strength and partner energy in ABC.
- **Psychology:** ABC states strong partner feelings without a defined direction or unstable mood pattern.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a specific bodily or health outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Female-partner energy, strong women as partners and the power of a people are source-native examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Public exercise of influence or violence in Witte; places of collective strength or power and a violent people at the place in ABC.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte allows either tending to violence or bringing power and influence to bear. Strength and public influence are not all equated with violence or emotional coercion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Moon` describes power and influence encountered through women, public persons or a people collectively in the source's examples. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives alternatives of tending to violence or bringing power and influence to bear. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds female-partner energy, strong partner feelings and places of collective strength or power, retaining a violent collective as one distinct possibility. These attributed examples support interpersonal and collective strength without establishing family pressure, unstable moods or a universal trait of women.
