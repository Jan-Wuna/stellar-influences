## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others waste their efforts, forced to learn a lesson. Others succumb by a trial of strength.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Neptune row. The wasted effort and defeat are attributed to others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

An influential partner in the air, subtle energy of the partner, a subtle place of strength, foreign force or violence at the place, weak local power, refined partner influence, refinement of spatial energy, violation by environmental poisons, and water power at the place.

Source reference: printed page 135, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Neptune row. Both “verfeinerter Partner-Einfluß” and “Verfeinerung der Raum-Energie” are restored. “Vergewaltigung durch Umwelt-Gifte” uses the rape/violation term for an effect of environmental poisons; it gives no named substance or clinical outcome. “Wasser-Kraft” is water power. Subtle and spatial energy, foreign force and the partner in the air remain broad source wording.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others' wasted effort or defeat in a trial of strength, alongside weak power, subtle energy and refinement in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Witte says others are forced to learn a lesson, without specifying confusion, misjudgment or a personality trait.
- **Body/health:** ABC names violation by environmental poisons but no particular poison, exposure, illness or diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Others undergo the trial; ABC also names an influential partner and refined partner influence.
- **Events/manifestations:** Air and water examples, foreign force or violence, weak local power, subtle places of strength and refinement of spatial energy.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Refinement is a distinct ABC possibility and is not equated with defeat or depletion. No cause is supplied for the wasted effort.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Neptune` describes others' effort failing to prevail in a test of strength. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states wasted effort and defeat; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds weak local power but also subtle energy, refined partner influence and refinement of spatial energy. The interpretation retains limits on force alongside refinement, without assuming that refinement is another form of failure. ABC's air, water-power and environmental-poison clauses remain separate source examples rather than an explanation of why others lose the trial.
