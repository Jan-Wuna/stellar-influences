## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To use violent people, to keep in touch with them.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Node row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Encounter with force or violence at the place, an influential partner connection, influential relationships in the surroundings, energy contacts with the partner, contacts with local power, influence involving the surroundings through connections, connection to a place of strength, and connection to the energy of the space.

Source reference: printed page 134, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Node row. The former “Y binding” artifacts are “Verbindungen” and “Verbindung,” connections or connection. “Gewalt” retains force or violence; energy contacts and spatial energy are not further defined.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Contact with force, power and influential relationships, with violent people explicitly named by Witte.
- **Psychology:** No separate individual psychological trait is given; the entries emphasize the nature of contacts.
- **Body/health:** Violence is represented, but no injury or health outcome is specified.
- **Social/relationship:** Keeping contact with violent people in Witte; ABC adds influential partner connections and surrounding relationships.
- **Events/manifestations:** Encounters with local force or violence, contacts with local power, and connections to a place of strength or spatial energy.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's violent-contact example must remain explicit. ABC's broader connections are not all characterized as violent, dependent or formal alliances.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Node` describes connections through which power, force or influence is encountered. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly names using violent people and keeping in touch with them as a source delineation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens the field to influential partner relationships, contacts with local power and influence through connections, while retaining an encounter with local force or violence. Connection is the organizing theme; the entries do not make every contact a violent alliance or establish dependence on it.
