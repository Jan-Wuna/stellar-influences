## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Changes through violent interference of others. To experience a quick change through the influence of others.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Pluto row. Both Pluto glyph variants label these two sentences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development of energy for the surroundings, development of force or violence at the place, developing spatial energy, strong unfolding of the partner, change of power at the place, change of spatial energy, growing strength of the space, and growing partner influence.

Source reference: printed page 135, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Pluto row. “Veränderung” is change, not improvement; “Entfaltung” retains unfolding. “Kraft” is strength, distinct from “Macht” power, and spatial energy is not technically defined.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Change through others' force or influence, with development and growth of power, energy or partner influence in ABC.
- **Psychology:** The partner's strong unfolding is named without a particular inner process or motive.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a bodily or health outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Others interfere violently or bring quick change through their influence in Witte; ABC adds growing partner influence.
- **Events/manifestations:** Quick change, developing local force or violence, altered local power or spatial energy and growing spatial strength.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Violent interference is explicit but does not exhaust the ABC development clauses. Change of spatial energy has no stated favorable direction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Pluto` describes change brought through others' influence or force. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly includes violent interference and quick change through others. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the range to developing energy or force, unfolding in the partner, changed local power and growing partner influence. The shared theme is changing power and influence in one's surroundings, with violent intervention preserved as a specific possibility and no assumption that every development is either coercive or an improvement.
