## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be under the mighty influence of others. Occurring forces of tidal waves.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Poseidon row. The tidal-wave forces are a separate literal example.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influence of a reasonable partner, influential ideas of the partner, influential culture in the surroundings, insight into spatial energy, mental or spiritual energy of the partner, mental or spiritual power at the place, a place of mental or spiritual strength, cultural influence at the place, and power of truth concerning the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 135, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Poseidon row. “Vernünftigen” means reasonable, not strong; “geistig” permits mental or spiritual scope. “Kraft” retains strength, distinct from “Macht” power. Spatial energy and surrounding truth are not further explained.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Mighty influence from others, with a reasonable partner's ideas and intellectual, spiritual or cultural influence in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Partner ideas and insight into spatial energy; no specified loss of control or inability to manage influence.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a health outcome. Witte's tidal-wave forces remain a natural-force example.
- **Social/relationship:** Others exert mighty influence; ABC specifies a reasonable partner and the partner's ideas or mental/spiritual energy.
- **Events/manifestations:** Tidal-wave forces, influential surrounding culture, local mental/spiritual power or strength and cultural influence.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Human influence and natural forces are distinct examples. Strong influence is explicit, while being overwhelmed or unable to cope is not stated.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Poseidon` describes being under strong influence from others, with mental, spiritual and cultural forms in the [ABC entry](#abc-entry). Its reasonable partner, influential ideas and local cultural influence give specificity to the [Witte entry's](#witte-entry) mighty influence. Witte also names tidal-wave forces as a distinct natural manifestation. The interpretation retains both interpersonal influence and literal natural force, without assuming that either necessarily exceeds the person's ability to cope.
