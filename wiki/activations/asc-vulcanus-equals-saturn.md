## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others are in dire need, are restrained or restrain the native or meet with powerful resistance.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Saturn row. The alternative directions of restraint remain explicit.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Enduring partner energy, enormous damage to the surroundings, concentrated energy of the space, power problems at the place, a place for patience and strength, problematic partner influence, strong concentration on the partner, and pain through violence at the place.

Source reference: printed page 135, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Saturn row. “Ausdauernde” means enduring, not exhausting. “Schmerzen durch Gewalt” retains pain through violence without a specified injury or clinical condition; spatial energy has no technical mechanism in the entry.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Need, restraint or powerful resistance involving others, alongside endurance, concentration and patience.
- **Psychology:** Strong concentration on the partner and patience, without a specified frustration pattern.
- **Body/health:** ABC names pain through violence but no particular injury, illness or diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Others may be restrained, restrain the native or meet resistance; problematic partner influence is another ABC example.
- **Events/manifestations:** Damage to the surroundings, power problems, concentrated spatial energy and a place for patience and strength.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Endurance and patience remain alongside adverse manifestations. The entries specify neither immovable resistance nor a duration of hardship.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Saturn` describes restraint or resistance within powerful associations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) keeps several directions open: others may be in need, be restrained, restrain the native or meet powerful resistance. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds damage, power problems and pain through violence, but also enduring partner energy, patience, strength and concentration. The interpretation therefore preserves endurance beside restriction, with no stated duration or rule that resistance is immovable.
