## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To have to deal with bodily stronger people. Others are bodily superior. To be kept in check.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Sun row. Superiority is specifically bodily strength in this entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influence of a male partner, force or violence during the day at the place, a physically strong partner, physical violence at the place, a center of strength of a place, a center of power at the place, personal spatial energy, personal influence involving the surroundings, and a center for influence involving the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 134, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Sun row. The final two influence clauses are restored. “Männlichen” means male, not manly; “am Tag am Ort” retains day and place. “Kraft” is strength, distinct from “Macht” power. The male-partner example remains source-native.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Encountering physically stronger people and being kept in check, alongside personal, partner and local power or influence.
- **Psychology:** No distinct identity process or psychological trait is stated.
- **Body/health:** Bodily strength and physical violence are explicit; no particular injury or health outcome is given.
- **Social/relationship:** Others are bodily stronger in Witte; ABC names a male partner's influence and a physically strong partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** Local force or violence, centers of strength or power, personal spatial energy and centers of surrounding influence.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Bodily superiority is a limited comparison, not overall human superiority. Personal influence remains beside being kept in check; a permanent loss of autonomy is unstated.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Sun` describes encounters with bodily strength and power in one's associations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) specifically names physically stronger people and being kept in check. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a physically strong partner, physical violence, local centers of strength or power and personal influence in the surroundings. Bodily force and its interpersonal or local setting organize the interpretation, while the entries leave identity formation and the duration of restraint unspecified.
