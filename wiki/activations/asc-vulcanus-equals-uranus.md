## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others become suddenly engaged in close fights, come to blows, act suddenly, explosively, abruptly.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Uranus row. Both leading glyphs are Uranus variants; others are the subject of the actions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Agitation through violation of the surroundings, dynamic strength of the place, an influential partner event, reform of power at the place, new force or violence at the place, sudden partner influence, strong nervousness of the partner, technical energies at the place, and technical strength for the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 135, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Uranus row. “Kraft” means strength, not crash; “einflußreiches Partner-Ereignis” is an influential partner event. “Neue Gewalt” retains force or violence rather than specifying a new administration. “Vergewaltigung” uses the rape/violation term for the surroundings without identifying a particular act.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others' sudden or explosive action, with dynamic strength, sudden partner influence and changes in local power.
- **Psychology:** Agitation through surrounding violation and strong nervousness of the partner in ABC.
- **Body/health:** Fights and blows are explicit in Witte, but no particular injury or clinical condition is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** Others fight or act abruptly; ABC adds an influential partner event and sudden partner influence.
- **Events/manifestations:** Reform of power, new local force or violence, dynamic local strength and technical energies or strength.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Violence remains explicit alongside technical and local-power examples. The entry gives no accident forecast or rule that sudden action is uncontainable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Uranus` describes sudden forceful action in one's associations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places fights, blows and abrupt or explosive action in others. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds sudden partner influence, agitation, partner nervousness and new local force or violence, while retaining power reform and technical energies as further manifestations. Suddenness and force organize the interpretation, without making every technical or influential event a fight or predicting an accident.
