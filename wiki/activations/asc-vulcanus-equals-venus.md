## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To enjoy great popularity with others. To feel well in one’s surroundings.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Venus row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joy in a place of strength, peace power in the place, harmony with the energy of space, harmonious partner influence, harmonious local influence, love influence of the partner, strong love partner, and preference for places of strength

Source reference: printed page 134, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Venus row. All eight clauses match the actual row. “Friedens-Macht” is retained as peace power; the entry defines no particular institution or practice. Spatial energy and places of strength remain broad source wording.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Popularity and feeling well among others, with harmonious influence and affinity for strength in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Joy, preference for places of strength and harmony with one's surroundings or partner.
- **Body/health:** Feeling well in the surroundings is stated without a particular bodily condition or health outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Great popularity, harmonious partner influence, the partner's love influence and a strong love partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** A place of strength, local peace power and harmonious local influence.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Both entries are favorable in emphasis. No hidden pressure or failure to recognize it is stated, and the source leaves peace power undefined.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Venus` describes popularity and ease in one's surroundings, joined to harmonious expressions of influence or strength. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states great popularity and feeling well; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds joy in a place of strength, peace power, harmonious local or partner influence and a strong love partner. Strength is therefore represented through harmony, affection and enjoyment in these entries, without a stated hidden-pressure condition.
