## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others carry their point and direct their power and superiority at a certain aim.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 97, Ascendant + Vulcanus, Zeus row. Others direct their power toward an aim; the aim is not identified as the native.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Drawing energy from the space, fiery spatial energy, leadership power at the place, leadership strength of the partner, shaping strength at the place, tremendous achievements in the surroundings, the partner's capacity for achievement, creative energy of the partner, and procreative strength of the partner.

Source reference: printed page 135, one-based PDF page 66, Aszendent + Vulkanus, Zeus row. “Führungs-Macht” and “Führungs-Kraft” concern leadership, not movement. “Leistungs-Kraft” concerns capacity for achievement, while “Zeugungs-Kraft” retains procreative strength without a clinical or fertility assessment. Spatial energy has no technical mechanism here.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others direct their power to an aim, with leadership, shaping and achievement in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Purposeful assertion of an aim; no specified hostile agenda or manipulation of the native.
- **Body/health:** ABC names partner procreative strength as source terminology without a fertility prediction or health outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Others carry their point; ABC adds partner leadership strength, achievement capacity and creative energy.
- **Events/manifestations:** Local leadership and shaping strength, tremendous surrounding achievements and drawing upon fiery spatial energy.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Directed power can be represented through leadership or creative achievement. The entries do not specify who or what the aim targets or make it inherently hostile.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Vulcanus = Zeus` describes others' power directed toward a particular aim. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states that they carry their point and direct their power; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops this through leadership, shaping strength, achievement capacity and creative partner energy. Purposeful use of strength is the shared theme, with productive and procreative examples retained. The entries leave the aim's object open and do not establish that the native is being singled out or manipulated.
