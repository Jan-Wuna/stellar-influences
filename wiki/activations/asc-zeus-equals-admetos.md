## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be forced to conquer obstacles and opposition. To be forced to start, commence, or to take up. Others are confronted with obstacles and strong resistance.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Admetos row. The forced beginning remains alongside obstacles and opposition.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Enduring shaping of the surroundings, beginning depressions in the partner, restricted goals concerning the surroundings, thorough local leadership, thorough partner goals, restriction of achievement by others, blocking efforts concerning the surroundings, and resistance to the local leadership.

Source reference: printed page 127, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Admetos row. “Ausdauernde” retains enduring; “gründliche” means thorough. “Umwelt-Anstrengungen blockieren” names blocking efforts without specifying the agent. “Beginnende Depressionen” is retained as attributed source wording, without a clinical diagnosis or a stated cause.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Being forced to begin or overcome opposition, with restriction and thorough, enduring effort in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Thorough goals and beginning depressions in the partner are separate source clauses.
- **Body/health:** The depression wording has no diagnostic criteria, health outcome or stated causal link to the restrictions.
- **Social/relationship:** Others face resistance or restrict achievement; local leadership can also encounter resistance.
- **Events/manifestations:** A forced start, obstacles, restricted surrounding goals, blocked efforts and enduring shaping of the surroundings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Beginning and endurance remain visible beside restriction. The source does not say that work only moves through endurance or inevitably accumulates pressure.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Admetos` describes compelled beginnings or effort in the face of opposition. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes both being forced to start and being forced to overcome obstacles, while others also confront strong resistance. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds restricted goals, blocked efforts and limits on achievement, alongside enduring shaping and thorough leadership or goals. The supported theme is effort and initiative meeting resistance; the partner's depression clause remains an attributed possibility without an established causal connection to that effort.
