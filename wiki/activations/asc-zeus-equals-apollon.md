## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Scientific activity together with others, also industrial. Scientific creations with others.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Apollon row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Extensive efforts concerning the surroundings, experienced local leadership, successful partner direction, extension of goals with the partner, commercial goals of others, achievements of business in the surroundings, extensive local shaping, and procreative success with the partner.

Source reference: printed page 127, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Apollon row. “Orts-Führung” retains local leadership; “Partner-Führung” does not identify who leads whom. “Handels-Ziele” is commercial goals and “Umwelt-Gewerbe” business or trades in the surroundings. “Zeugungs-Erfolg” is source-native procreative success without a clinical fertility assessment.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Shared scientific or industrial creation, with extensive effort, experienced leadership and expanded goals in ABC.
- **Psychology:** No separate trait or loss-of-focus pattern is given; the entries emphasize activity and goals.
- **Body/health:** ABC names procreative success with a partner as a source claim, without clinical evidence or a fertility prediction.
- **Social/relationship:** Scientific work and creation with others, successful partner direction and extending goals with the partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** Scientific or industrial activity, surrounding business achievements, commercial goals and extensive local shaping.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries share a productive emphasis. Partner-direction agency remains open, and extensive effort is not explicitly described as overextension.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Apollon` describes shared productive activity with a scientific or industrial form in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens this into extensive efforts, experienced local leadership, partner direction, expanded goals and business achievements in the surroundings. Joint creation and a wider scope of effort connect the entries, while ABC also retains procreative success as a distinct source example. The passage does not state that expansion necessarily loses focus or exceeds capacity.
