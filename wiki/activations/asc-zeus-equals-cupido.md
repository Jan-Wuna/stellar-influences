## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Unity is power. Community work in art.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Cupido row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Efforts of a partner community, fire in shared surroundings, leadership of a local community, shared local shaping, a creative partner community, achievements of a community in the surroundings, and goals of other communities.

Source reference: printed page 127, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Cupido row. “Orts-Gestaltung” retains local shaping without a specified design profession; the shared-surroundings fire is a literal separate clause.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Unity expressed through communal effort and creative work.
- **Psychology:** No separate individual trait is stated; the emphasis is shared purpose and creativity.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health outcome; ABC's fire clause supplies no injury claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Community work, partner-community effort and local community leadership.
- **Events/manifestations:** Collective art, shared local shaping, community achievements, other communities' goals and fire in shared surroundings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries overlap in communal creation. They do not specify family or workshop membership or a conflict between cooperation and conformity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Cupido` describes shared effort and creation through community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects unity with power and names communal work in art; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds partner-community effort, local leadership, shared shaping and community achievements or goals. Communal organization is the supported form of productive activity here, without a stated requirement that cohesion turns into conformity. ABC's fire in shared surroundings remains a distinct literal example.
