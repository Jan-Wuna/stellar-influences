## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Spiteful or vulgar deeds of others. Damage through fire by other persons.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Hades row. The moral descriptions are the source's judgments; no intent or specific mechanism is given for the fire damage caused by others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Strenuous service for the surroundings, impeded creativity in the surroundings, fire danger nearby, criminal goals concerning the surroundings, inadequate local shaping, disadvantageous renunciation concerning the partner, hidden local leadership, renunciation of efforts for the surroundings, and serving the goals of others.

Source reference: printed page 127, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Hades row. “Partner-Verzicht” retains renunciation involving the partner without settling its agent; “verborgene Orts-Führung” is hidden local leadership. The final clause is serving others' goals, not targeting others.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others' adverse deeds and fire damage, with burdens or restrictions affecting effort, creativity and goals in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Spitefulness or vulgarity are Witte's attributed assessments; no negligence or sabotage motive is established.
- **Body/health:** Fire danger and damage are named without a specific bodily injury or health outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Other people cause fire damage; partner renunciation and serving others' goals are separate ABC clauses.
- **Events/manifestations:** Strenuous service, impeded creativity, criminal goals, inadequate shaping and hidden local leadership.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The sources emphasize adversity but give different manifestations. Fire damage is not expressly arson, and partner-renunciation agency remains open.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Hades` describes adverse activity encountered through others and burdens affecting effort in one's surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names others' spiteful or vulgar deeds and fire damage caused by other people. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds strenuous service, impeded creativity, criminal goals, inadequate local shaping and renunciation, while retaining hidden leadership and service to others' goals. These passages support difficult or damaging forms of activity without establishing sabotage, negligence or a single motive behind every example.
