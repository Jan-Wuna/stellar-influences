## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Good things are forced upon us. To be forced by others into fortune or luck. To create pleasure with others.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Jupiter row. The favorable result and being compelled by others are both explicit.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful partner direction, successful local leadership, financial efforts concerning the surroundings, financial performance of the partner, fire of joy at the place, fortunate procreation at the place, shaping local law, legal goals of others, and procreative success with the partner.

Source reference: printed page 126, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Jupiter row. All nine clauses are restored, including the opening partner-direction and others' legal-goals clauses. “Führung” is direction or leadership, not welfare; “glückliche” is fortunate, not glial. “Orts-Recht-Gestaltung” and “Rechts-Ziele” retain legal scope. The procreative clauses are source claims without a clinical assessment.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others compel favorable circumstances, with shared pleasure and successful direction or effort in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Pleasure and fortune are named without a specific growth or judgment process.
- **Body/health:** ABC's fortunate procreation and procreative success remain attributed claims without a fertility or pregnancy-outcome prediction.
- **Social/relationship:** Others bring the native into luck; creating pleasure together and successful partner direction are also represented.
- **Events/manifestations:** Successful local leadership, financial efforts or performance, fire of joy, shaping local law and others' legal goals.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte combines compulsion with favorable outcomes. The entry gives no general rule for evaluating every pressure or for guaranteeing success.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Jupiter` describes favorable circumstances brought through others, even in a compelled form. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly says good things or luck are forced upon the person and also names creating pleasure together. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful partner or local direction, financial efforts and performance, legal goals and procreative success. The supported theme is benefit and shared achievement through directed activity; the specific legal, fire and procreative clauses retain their own source scope.
