## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be stimulated to action by superiors.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Kronos row. The wording is stimulated to action, without an explicit command or coercion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special achievements in the surroundings, leading local authority, a major fire nearby, independent shaping of the surroundings, a self-confident creative partner, state achievements concerning the surroundings, state goals concerning the surroundings, important efforts for the surroundings, and goals of other states.

Source reference: printed page 127, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Kronos row. The omitted “Groß-Feuer im Umfeld” is restored. “Selbständige” means independent and “selbstbewußter” self-confident; “Leistungen” concerns achievements or performance, not standards. States refer to governments or countries, not mental states.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Action stimulated by superiors, with leading authority, special achievements and independent or state-directed goals in ABC.
- **Psychology:** ABC names a self-confident creative partner, distinct from independent shaping of the surroundings.
- **Body/health:** A major fire is named without a specific bodily injury or health outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Superiors stimulate action; a leading local authority or creative partner supplies further ABC examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Major fire, special or state achievements, independent local shaping and goals of other states.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Stimulation by superiors coexists with independent activity. The entries do not reduce every action to hierarchical command or compulsory subordination.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Kronos` describes action prompted by superiors and activity involving authority or significant achievement. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly names stimulation to action; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds leading local authority, state achievements and goals, independent shaping and a self-confident creative partner. Authority is therefore one source of initiative alongside independent and creative effort, rather than an instruction to read every activity as subordination. ABC's major fire remains a separate literal manifestation.
