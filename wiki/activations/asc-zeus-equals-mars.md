## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be forced to a deed. Others enforce a decision. To receive burns. To be injured by somebody.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Mars row. Burns and injury remain attributed historical delineations, not predictions about a particular person.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Strenuous work at the place, work with fire nearby, work achievements at the place, leading activity with the partner, actions of the local leadership, creative partner actions, creative activity in the surroundings, and vigorous efforts concerning the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 126, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Mars row. “Führende Tätigkeit” is leading activity, not driving, and “Orts-Führung” local leadership, not support. “Tatkräftige” retains vigorous effort without guaranteeing effectiveness.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Compelled action or enforced decisions in Witte, with strenuous, leading and creative work in ABC.
- **Psychology:** The entries name action and effort without a specific aggression trait or escalation process.
- **Body/health:** Witte explicitly names burns and injury by somebody as historical claims; ABC names work with fire but supplies no specific injury.
- **Social/relationship:** Others compel a deed or enforce a decision; ABC also includes leading activity with a partner and creative partner actions.
- **Events/manifestations:** Work with fire, local work achievements, local leadership actions and vigorous surrounding efforts.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's coercion and injury examples remain explicit beside ABC's productive activity. The latter is not all described as injurious or coerced.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Mars` describes forceful or strenuous action in one's associations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly includes being forced to act, an enforced decision, burns and injury caused by someone. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also gives productive forms: strenuous work, work with fire, leading activity with a partner and creative actions or efforts. Both compulsion and active creation belong in the interpretation; the historical injury claims remain attributed, without making every work or leadership example a harmful event.
