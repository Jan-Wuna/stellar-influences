## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The native’s way is shown to him. The thoughts are forced to follow a fixed trend. To be under the influence of other’s opinions. To be compelled to much conversation. Talks about machines.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Mercury row. The printed possessive and gendered wording are retained.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A report about fire nearby, thoughts about local shaping, creative partners in youth, news about achievements in the surroundings, language creations with others, young people's goals concerning the surroundings, traffic guidance of a place, and word shaping at the place.

Source reference: printed page 126, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Mercury row. “Sprach-Schöpfungen” is language creations, not voice recordings; “Verkehrs-Führung” is traffic guidance, not transport support. “Wort-Gestaltung” retains shaping words without a specified medium or profession.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Directed thinking and compelled conversation in Witte, with creative language, reports and local guidance in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Thoughts follow a fixed trend and others' opinions influence the native in Witte; ABC adds thoughts about local shaping.
- **Body/health:** Reports of fire and talks about machines are communication subjects, with no bodily or health outcome stated.
- **Social/relationship:** Others show the native's way or influence opinions; ABC names language creation with others and creative partners in youth.
- **Events/manifestations:** Much conversation, machine talk, fire reports, news of achievements, youth goals, traffic guidance and word shaping.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's constrained thought and conversation coexist with ABC's creative language examples. A particular task, recording medium or planning profession is not specified.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Mercury` describes thought and communication shaped through others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly includes a shown path, thoughts forced into a fixed trend, influence from others' opinions and compelled conversation, with machines as one topic. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds creative language with others, reports, local shaping, youth goals and traffic guidance. The interpretation keeps constraint and creative communication visible together rather than making every conversation compelled or technically focused.
