## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To accomplish a task with others. Making plans with others. The working crew or shift in a plant.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Moon row. The actual scan reads “The working crew”; the initial I was an extraction error.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A strenuous hour with others, fiery feelings of the partner, a leading woman at the place, a leading people in the surroundings, feelingful partner direction, a creative woman as partner, achievements of other women, achievements of a people in the surroundings, goals of a people concerning the surroundings, and goals of other peoples.

Source reference: printed page 126, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Moon row. “Gefühle” means feelings, not customs; “führende” means leading, not driving. “Volk/Völker” retains a people/peoples collectively. “Gefühlvolle Partner-Führung” concerns direction imbued with feeling without identifying who leads whom. The gendered and collective examples remain source-native.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Accomplishing tasks and making plans with others, including shared work, leadership and collective goals.
- **Psychology:** Fiery partner feelings and feelingful direction, without a stated loss of personal space or individual identity.
- **Body/health:** The strenuous hour is an effort description; no bodily condition or health outcome is specified.
- **Social/relationship:** A working crew or plant shift, creative women as partners, women's achievements and peoples' collective goals are the source examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Shared tasks or planning, local leadership, achievements and goals in the surroundings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's work setting is one explicit example, while ABC extends to gendered and collective leadership or creativity. Neither makes routine or group absorption a universal condition.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Moon` describes tasks and plans carried out with others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives a working crew or plant shift as a concrete example; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds shared effort, partner feelings, leadership, creativity and collective achievements or goals. The common theme is activity organized with other people, with ABC's women and peoples retained as attributed source examples. The entries leave personal belonging, routine and the amount of individual space unspecified.
