## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be without progeny due to marriage partner. Others incite you without success.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Neptune row. The scan reads marriage partner; parmer was an extraction error. The childlessness attribution is historical source doctrine, not a fertility diagnosis or evidence of a partner's responsibility.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Sensitive partner efforts, creative intuition of the partner, creeping fire nearby, weak local leadership, goals concerning the surroundings for air and water, unclear partner goals, uncertain partner direction, and future goals concerning the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 127, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Neptune row. These eight clauses run from “Feinfühlige Partner-Anstrengungen” through “zukünftige Umwelt-Ziele.” The following Pluto glyph starts “Anstrengende Partner-Entwicklung”; its development and fire-development clauses are excluded here. The source does not explain the air/water goals or identify the leader in partner direction.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Unsuccessful incitement by others in Witte; sensitive effort, creative intuition and uncertain goals or leadership in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Partner intuition and sensitive efforts remain alongside unclear goals, without a stated illusion or deception process.
- **Body/health:** Witte's childlessness-due-to-partner clause remains historical attribution, with no clinical assessment or justified assignment of responsibility.
- **Social/relationship:** Others try to incite the native without success; ABC names partner efforts, intuition, goals and uncertain direction.
- **Events/manifestations:** Creeping fire, weak local leadership, air/water goals and future surrounding goals.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's lack of success does not erase ABC's intuitive or future-oriented clauses. Neighboring Pluto development belongs to its own orientation.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Neptune` describes another person's incitement failing to produce success, alongside sensitivity and uncertainty in effort or direction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states unsuccessful incitement and separately makes a historical childlessness attribution. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds sensitive partner efforts, creative intuition, unclear goals and weak or uncertain leadership, while retaining future goals and literal fire or air/water examples. These support a mixed account of sensitive initiative and uncertain effectiveness, without treating intuition as failure or inferring deception, infertility or blame.
