## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To accomplish something with others.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Node row.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Strenuous partner mediation, connection with the goals of others, connection with the leadership of a place, connection with fire nearby, creative partner connections, a meeting by the fire at the place, and procreation in a partner connection.

Source reference: printed page 126, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Node row. “Führung eines Ortes” is leadership of a place, not delivery; “Zielen” concerns goals. The entry does not specify the form of partner mediation. The procreation clause remains attributed source wording without a clinical assessment.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Accomplishment with others through connections, shared goals and creative partnership.
- **Psychology:** No separate individual motive or trait is stated; the entry does not say connection is valued only for its output.
- **Body/health:** ABC names procreation in a partner connection without a fertility or health outcome assessment.
- **Social/relationship:** Partner mediation, others' goals, local leadership links and creative partner connections.
- **Events/manifestations:** Joint accomplishment, connection with fire nearby and a meeting by the fire at the place.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries overlap in shared activity. A formal alliance, exploitative motive or specified mediation process is not established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Node` describes accomplishing something with other people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives that shared activity directly; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds connections with others' goals or local leadership, partner mediation and creative partner links. Connection provides the setting for accomplishment without a stated judgment that relationships are merely instrumental. ABC's fire meeting and procreation clauses remain distinct source examples of those connections.
