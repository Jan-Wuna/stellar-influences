## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be forced by others to work or accomplish more.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Pluto row. Both leading Pluto glyph variants label this sentence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Demanding partner development, development of fire nearby, creative partner development, shaping a change of place, unfolding goals concerning the surroundings, growing creativity of the partner, changing local leadership, and changing partner striving.

Source reference: printed page 127, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Pluto row. The first three clauses, beginning “Anstrengende Partner-Entwicklung,” follow the actual Pluto glyph; the parser had attached them to Neptune and left only the word ending “lung” here. “Wechselnde Orts-Führung” is changing local leadership and “wechselndes Partner-Streben” changing partner striving, not strikes.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others force increased work or accomplishment in Witte; ABC adds demanding or creative development and changing goals or leadership.
- **Psychology:** Growing partner creativity and changing striving, without a specified burnout or domination process.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health outcome; fire development supplies no injury claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Increased demands come from others in Witte; partner development, creativity and striving also change in ABC.
- **Events/manifestations:** Developing fire, shaping a change of place, unfolding surrounding goals and changing local leadership.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Compulsion to do more is explicit, while ABC also represents creative development. The entry gives no condition making all growth coerced or harmful.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Pluto` describes increased demands from others alongside development and change in shared activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly says others force the person to work or accomplish more. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds demanding and creative partner development, growing creativity, unfolding goals and changing local leadership or partner striving. The interpretation retains compelled effort without reducing every developmental clause to coercion. Fire development and shaping a change of place remain separate concrete source examples.
