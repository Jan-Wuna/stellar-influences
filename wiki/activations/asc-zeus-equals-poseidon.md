## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be forced to a mental attitude. To be lead mentally or spiritually by other people.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Poseidon row. The scan reads spiritually; the awkward printed “To be lead” is retained.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Mental or spiritual partner direction, clear testimony of the partner, clarity about goals concerning the surroundings, cultural shaping at the place, a place of attesting truth, creative ideas of the partner, creative culture in the surroundings, spiritual local leadership, and reasonable goals concerning the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 127, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Poseidon row. “Führung” means direction or leadership, not welfare; “geistige” permits mental or spiritual scope, while “spirituelle” is explicitly spiritual. “Vernünftige” means reasonable, not future. Partner-direction agency and the form of truth testimony are unspecified.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** A mental attitude compelled or guided by others, with direction, clarity, creative ideas and cultural shaping in ABC.
- **Psychology:** External mental or spiritual guidance and a forced attitude in Witte; clear goals and reasonable aims in ABC.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Other people guide the native in Witte; ABC names partner direction, ideas and testimony without identifying who leads whom.
- **Events/manifestations:** Cultural shaping, a place of attesting truth, creative surrounding culture and spiritual local leadership.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Compulsion is explicit in Witte. ABC's creative and reasonable-goal clauses are broader; no particular doctrine, institution or imposed belief is named.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Poseidon` describes mental or spiritual direction involving others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly includes a forced mental attitude and guidance by other people. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds partner direction, clear testimony, creative ideas, reasonable goals and cultural shaping. The interpretation retains Witte's compulsion alongside these creative or clarifying forms of direction, while leaving the doctrine, practical method and exact partner-guidance agency open.
