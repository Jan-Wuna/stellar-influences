## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Hindrances, obstacles forced upon us by others. Other people enforce a discharge (job). Discharged from the army. Losses by fire or machinery.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Saturn row. Job discharge, army discharge and fire or machinery losses remain distinct source examples.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Strenuous duties at the place, serious goals of others, fire losses nearby, patient striving concerning the surroundings, concentrated local leadership, problematic goals concerning the surroundings, disturbances in partner direction, and local shaping entailing losses.

Source reference: printed page 127, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Saturn row. “Ernste Ziele” is serious goals; “Streben” is striving, not stress. Both “Führung” clauses concern leadership or direction, not transportation, and “Störungen” disturbances. The source does not identify who leads whom in partner direction.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Hindrances or discharge imposed through others, with duties, loss and problematic goals alongside patience and concentration.
- **Psychology:** Serious goals and patient striving are explicit without a defined despair or stress disorder.
- **Body/health:** Fire or machinery losses are named without a particular bodily injury or health outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Others impose obstacles or job discharge; ABC adds disturbed partner direction and local leadership.
- **Events/manifestations:** Job or army discharge, fire or machinery losses, strenuous duties and loss-entailing local shaping.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The adverse examples remain explicit, while patient striving and concentrated leadership preserve constructive effort. Machinery losses do not specify a mechanical failure mechanism.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Saturn` describes obstacles and losses affecting activity with others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly includes imposed hindrances, job discharge, army discharge and losses by fire or machinery. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds strenuous duties, serious or problematic goals, disturbed partner direction and loss-entailing local shaping, while retaining patience and concentrated leadership. Restriction and loss therefore coexist with sustained effort; the source does not provide a sequence in which productive pressure necessarily becomes dismissal or failure.
