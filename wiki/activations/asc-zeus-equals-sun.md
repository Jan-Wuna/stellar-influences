## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be forced to become a leader. To travel by train. Railroad passenger. To associate with railroad employees.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Sun row. Leadership and the three railroad examples remain separate statements.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Fire during the day nearby, a leadership center at the place, shaping a local center, a creative male partner, an achievement center at the place, personal partner direction, personal achievements concerning the surroundings, personal goals of others, and a man's goals concerning the surroundings.

Source reference: printed page 126, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Sun row. “Kreativer männlicher Partner” is one creative male partner; “Führungs-Zentrum” is a leadership center and “Partner-Führung” partner direction. “Leistungen” retains achievements or performance. The source's male examples remain attributed, and partner-direction agency is unstated.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Becoming a leader under compulsion, with personal direction, achievements and local centers in ABC.
- **Psychology:** No particular leadership temperament or response to necessity is specified.
- **Body/health:** ABC names nearby fire during the day but no injury or bodily outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Railroad employees, a creative male partner and others' personal goals are distinct source examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Train travel or railroad passenger, local leadership or achievement centers, local shaping and personal surrounding achievements.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Compulsion applies explicitly to becoming a leader; the railroad and creative-partner examples are not all described as forced. A visible public role or general transport doctrine is not stated.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Sun` describes compelled leadership and activity involving personal direction or achievement. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly names being forced to become a leader, then gives train travel, railroad passengers and railroad employees as separate examples. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds leadership or achievement centers, local shaping, a creative male partner and personal goals or achievements. These specific settings remain visible without merging railroad contact into a general theory of collective movement or making every example a forced role.
