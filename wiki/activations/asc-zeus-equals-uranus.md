## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others are in excitement because of or through the native. Danger to the native from explosions, lightning or machines.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Uranus row. Both leading glyphs are Uranus variants. Excitement belongs to others and is attributed to the native; the danger clause remains a historical source claim.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Demanding events in the surroundings, excitement through fire nearby, impulses of a creative partner, nervous local leadership, new goals concerning the surroundings, new goals with the partner, technical achievements in the surroundings, shaping reforms of the surroundings, and local unrest through fire.

Source reference: printed page 127, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Uranus row. “Ereignisse” means events, “Ziele” goals, “Führung” leadership and “Leistungen” achievements or performance. These replace the former achievement/cicle/transport/licence artifacts. Shaping reforms and fire-caused unrest are distinct final clauses.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Others' excitement through the native, with demanding events, new goals and reform in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Excitement and nervous leadership are stated, alongside creative partner impulses; no particular conflict motive is named.
- **Body/health:** Witte names danger from explosions, lightning or machines as historical delineation, without specifying an injury or clinical outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** The native excites others in Witte; ABC also names partner impulses and new goals with the partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** Fire-related excitement or unrest, technical achievements, reform and new surrounding goals.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Hazard and agitation remain explicit beside creative and technical activity. These entries do not make every reform an accident, rupture or explosive dispute.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Uranus` describes excitement and demanding activity in one's associations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) specifically places others in excitement because of the native and separately names danger from explosions, lightning or machines. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds fire-related agitation, nervous leadership, new goals, creative impulses, technical achievements and reform. The interpretation retains both hazard and constructive change, with the historical danger clause attributed rather than turned into a prediction that every technical or creative event is harmful.
