## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To comply with a request or desire of others. To bestow love. To create progeny.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Venus row. The progeny statement remains a source claim without a clinical assessment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Fiery love for the partner, peace efforts at the place, peaceful goals concerning the surroundings, harmonious local leadership, harmonious striving toward goals with others, a creative artist as partner, achievement in love by the other, and procreation in love with the partner.

Source reference: printed page 126, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Venus row. “Künstler” means artist, not client; “Orts-Führung” is local leadership. “Liebes-Zeugung” is procreation in love, not witnessing, and “Liebes-Leistung” remains broad achievement or performance in love without a specific measure.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Responding to others' desires, bestowing love and harmonious shared effort or creation.
- **Psychology:** Fiery love, peaceful goals and harmonious striving, without a stated excess of compliance.
- **Body/health:** Progeny or procreation is named by both sources as doctrine, without a fertility or pregnancy-outcome assessment.
- **Social/relationship:** Compliance with others' requests, a creative artist as partner and affection expressed toward a partner.
- **Events/manifestations:** Peace efforts, harmonious local leadership, joint goals, achievement in love and procreation in love.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Affection, peace and harmony overlap across the entries. The source does not specify submissiveness, excessive yielding or the form of love performance.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Venus` describes action responsive to others' desires and expressed through affection or harmonious creation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names complying with a request, bestowing love and creating progeny. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds fiery partner love, peace efforts, harmonious leadership and joint goals, with an artist partner and achievement in love among its examples. The shared emphasis is affectionate or peaceful activity with others; the procreative statements remain attributed source doctrine rather than a clinical prediction.
