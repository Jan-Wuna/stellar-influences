## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Machine operator. Train crew.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Vernal Point row. These are the complete two examples in this entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Fire in the surroundings of public life, partner direction in public, public achievements concerning the surroundings, public local shaping, public goals concerning the surroundings, goals of others in public life, and local leadership in public.

Source reference: printed page 126, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Vernal Point row. “Umwelt” retains surroundings without a specifically ecological meaning; “Orts-Gestaltung” is local shaping and “Partner-Führung” leaves who leads whom unspecified.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Concrete machine or train work roles in Witte, with public direction, achievement and goals in ABC.
- **Psychology:** Neither entry states an individual trait, work identity or attitude toward routine.
- **Body/health:** ABC names fire in public-life surroundings without an injury or health outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** A train crew, public partner direction and others' goals in public life.
- **Events/manifestations:** Machine operation, train work, public local shaping and leadership, public goals or achievements and fire.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte gives two occupational examples; ABC gives a broader public setting. They do not establish that every example is mechanical, routine or impersonal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Vernal Point` has concrete work roles and public activity as its represented settings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) consists of machine operator and train crew; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds public partner direction, local shaping or leadership, achievements and goals in public life. Together they support activity organized through work or public direction, while the precise occupation and psychological meaning remain open. ABC's fire clause is a separate literal example, not a claim that all public work involves machinery.
