## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others enforce something. To deal with bodily superior men. To handle mechanical powers.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 93, Ascendant + Zeus, Vulcanus row. The bodily comparison and male example remain source-native; handling mechanical powers is a distinct statement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influential partner direction, force of fire at the place, leadership strength at the place, creative partner energy, exertions of strength by the other, power goals of the partner, creative partner influence, energy output in the surroundings, and procreative strength of the partner.

Source reference: printed page 127, one-based PDF page 62, Aszendent + Zeus, Vulcanus row. The omitted “schöpferischer Partner-Einfluß” is restored. “Führung” means direction, not welfare; “Kraft” retains strength or force, distinct from “Macht” power. Partner-direction agency and the form of surrounding energy output are unstated. Procreative strength is a source claim without a clinical assessment.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Enforcement by others and handling bodily or mechanical power, with leadership, exertion and creative influence in ABC.
- **Psychology:** The entries state power goals and creative energy without a particular intimidation motive.
- **Body/health:** Witte names bodily stronger men; ABC names partner procreative strength. Neither supplies a clinical or fertility assessment.
- **Social/relationship:** Others enforce something; ABC also names influential partner direction, power goals and creative partner influence.
- **Events/manifestations:** Handling mechanical powers, local fire force or leadership strength, exertion and surrounding energy output.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Enforcement remains explicit beside handling and creative activity. The source does not make every machine overpowering or every use of strength coercive.

## Derived Synthesis

`Asc/Zeus = Vulcanus` describes activity involving substantial force or strength. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names others enforcing something, bodily stronger men and handling mechanical powers as distinct examples. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds influential partner direction, leadership strength, exertion, power goals and creative partner energy or influence. The interpretation preserves enforcement alongside practical handling and creative effort, with no stated requirement that every expression of power is intimidating or overpowering.
