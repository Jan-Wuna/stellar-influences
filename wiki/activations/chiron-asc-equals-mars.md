## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s ability to be effective in one’s actions is inhibited by the limitations one places on one’s identity and self-concept. Conversely one’s actions can become powerfully fruitful when one aligns one’s identity with spiritual forces.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 102, Mars paragraph under “Planets activating the Chiron/Ascendant midpoint.” PDF page 101 is the chapter start. The source's typographic split “pow erfully” is joined; both conditional statements remain intact.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Effectiveness in action connected to the limits of self-concept or alignment with spiritual forces in Sandbach's account.
- **Psychology:** Self-imposed definitions can inhibit action; spiritual identification is presented as an alternative basis for fruitful action.
- **Body/health:** The paragraph gives no bodily injury, wound or clinical healing claim.
- **Social/relationship:** No separate interpersonal relationship or partner outcome is stated.
- **Events/manifestations:** Inhibited effectiveness or potentially fruitful action, without a named event or profession.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entry gives two conditional forms of action. It does not identify every inhibition with a wound or defensive behavior, or explain the spiritual mechanism.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Asc = Mars` connects action with the way one defines oneself. In the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry), self-imposed identity limits inhibit effectiveness, while alignment with spiritual forces can make action fruitful. The interpretation preserves this contrast within the source's spiritual account: the issue is the basis from which action proceeds, without an added claim that the person is wounded, defensive or necessarily ineffective.
