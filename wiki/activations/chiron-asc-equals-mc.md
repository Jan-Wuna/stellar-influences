## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One's life purpose is to continue to let go of the limits of one's identity until it is perfectly spiritualized. One may be unhappy and unfilled in one's career until one is able to give up old and restrictive ways of seeing oneself.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 102, Midheaven paragraph under “Planets activating the Chiron/Ascendant midpoint.” PDF page 101 is the chapter start. The actual wording “unfilled” and the conditional “may” are retained.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Life purpose framed as releasing identity limits and spiritualizing the self, with possible career dissatisfaction tied to restrictive self-views.
- **Psychology:** Letting go of old definitions of oneself, within Sandbach's spiritual account of identity.
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition or clinical healing process is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** Career is named, but no distinct interpersonal or partner outcome is given.
- **Events/manifestations:** Possible unhappiness in one's career while restrictive self-views remain; no specific job change or result is promised.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The paragraph says career dissatisfaction may occur. It does not assign every vocational difficulty this cause or guarantee fulfillment after a change of self-view.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Asc = MC` connects life purpose and career experience with the limits of self-definition. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) presents ongoing release of those limits as a spiritual purpose and says career unhappiness may persist while old, restrictive self-views remain. The supported interpretation is a possible relationship between vocational dissatisfaction and identity limits within that spiritual account, without making it a universal explanation or promising a particular career result.
