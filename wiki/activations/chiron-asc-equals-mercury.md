### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A way of communicating that carries a healing aura, no matter what is said. The ability to couch things in terms that will most foster spiritual openings.

## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Asc = Mercury` describes communication whose manner and framing are understood to encourage spiritual openness. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) places a healing aura in the way of communicating, regardless of what is said, and links it with choosing terms that foster spiritual openings. The supported interpretation concerns the quality of expression within that spiritual account, without assigning a therapeutic profession, a clinical result or an additional identity-work requirement.
