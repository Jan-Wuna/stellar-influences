## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The emotional need to clear negative elements from one’s identity. The need to open oneself to those forces that can elevate, refine and spiritualize one’s identity.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 102, Moon paragraph under “Planets activating the Chiron/Ascendant midpoint.” PDF page 101 is the chapter start. Negative elements and elevating forces are the source's spiritual descriptions, not defined conditions or mechanisms.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** An emotional need to clear identity and open to its spiritual refinement.
- **Psychology:** The felt need to release what the source calls negative identity elements and become receptive to elevating forces.
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition, wound or clinical healing process is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** The paragraph gives no specific interpersonal or family role.
- **Events/manifestations:** Inner clearing and receptivity are the described activities; no concrete event is named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entry describes needs and forces that can refine identity, not a guaranteed result. It does not define nurturance or identification with wounds as part of this formula.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Asc = Moon` describes an emotionally felt need to change and refine one's sense of self. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) links clearing negative elements of identity with opening to forces that can elevate and spiritualize it. Within this spiritual account, emotion supplies the motive for clearing and receptivity; the passage does not add a family role, a wound diagnosis or a guaranteed transformation.
