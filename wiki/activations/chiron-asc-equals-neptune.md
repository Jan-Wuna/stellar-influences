## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

As one’s intuition becomes more and more attuned to the spiritual realms one’s personality becomes cleared and healed, so that it is a transparent vehicle through which cosmic forces may work.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 102, Neptune paragraph under “Planets activating the Chiron/Ascendant midpoint.” PDF page 101 is the chapter start. Personality healing, spiritual realms and cosmic forces are the source's spiritual account, without a clinical mechanism or outcome.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Increasing intuitive attunement associated with a spiritually cleared personality through which cosmic forces may work.
- **Psychology:** Intuition and personality are linked through the source's image of becoming a transparent vehicle.
- **Body/health:** The healing described concerns personality in a spiritual account, not a specified bodily condition or treatment.
- **Social/relationship:** No particular relationship, recipient of healing or professional role is stated.
- **Events/manifestations:** Progressive attunement and personality clearing, without a concrete external event.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The paragraph presents a constructive spiritual process and retains “may” for cosmic forces working through it. Confusion, boundary collapse and a pace of destabilization are not stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Asc = Neptune` describes intuition and personality changing together through spiritual attunement. In the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry), greater attunement clears and heals the personality so that it can serve as a transparent vehicle for cosmic forces. This is the source's spiritual image of receptivity; the paragraph supplies neither a clinical process nor an adverse scenario of confusion or dissolving boundaries.
