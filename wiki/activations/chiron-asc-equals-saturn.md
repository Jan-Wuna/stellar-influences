## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To feel cut off from spiritual forces, and/or unable to identify with them. The need to overcome the fear of losing one’s identity, so that it may expand into alignment with spiritual realities.

Source reference: one-based PDF page 102, Saturn paragraph under “Planets activating the Chiron/Ascendant midpoint.” PDF page 101 is the chapter start. The felt disconnection, fear and possible expansion are retained as the source's spiritual account.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Feeling spiritually disconnected and fearing identity loss, with a need to allow identity to expand toward spiritual alignment.
- **Psychology:** Fear of losing the self and difficulty identifying with spiritual forces.
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition, wound or clinical healing process is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** Spiritual disconnection is described without a specific social separation or partner outcome.
- **Events/manifestations:** An inward difficulty and possible expansion of self-understanding, without a named external event.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Identity is said to expand, not necessarily disappear. The source does not equate every form of caution or separation with harm or a preserved wound.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Asc = Saturn` describes a felt distance from spiritual forces tied to fear of losing one's identity. In the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry), overcoming that fear opens the possibility for identity to expand into spiritual alignment. The interpretation preserves the tension between retaining a sense of self and allowing a wider spiritual identification, without adding rigidity, a wound diagnosis or a claim that ordinary caution is harmful.
