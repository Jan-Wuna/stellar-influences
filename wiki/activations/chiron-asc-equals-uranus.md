### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An experimental approach to allowing spiritual forces to work through one’s identity. This may manifest as role playing, acting, and the putting on and taking off of identities at will. A highly independent and even inspired way of expressing spiritual realities.

## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Asc = Uranus` describes an experimental, independent approach to expressing spiritual realities through identity. Sandbach suggests role playing, acting, or freely trying on and setting aside identities as possible forms of that expression, which may also be inspired. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
