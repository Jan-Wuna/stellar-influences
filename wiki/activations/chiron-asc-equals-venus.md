## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s love relationships are harmed or blocked by negative elements in one’s identity, and, by the same token can be healed when one identifies more and more strongly with spiritual forces.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 102, Venus paragraph under Chiron/Asc; PDF page 101 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF page.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Identity affects love relationships, with negative elements obstructing them and stronger spiritual identification offering healing.
- **Psychology:** Sandbach links a change in identification with the possibility of relationship healing.
- **Body/health:** Healing concerns love relationships here; no bodily diagnosis or treatment is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** Love relationships may be harmed or blocked by negative elements in identity.
- **Events/manifestations:** Relationship obstruction and possible healing through increasing identification with spiritual forces are the explicit alternatives.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The source says relationships can be healed; it gives no guaranteed result, timetable, or practical method for spiritual identification.

## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Asc = Venus` connects the state of love relationships with identity. Sandbach describes negative elements in identity as harming or blocking love, while stronger identification with spiritual forces can allow those relationships to heal. The proposed healing is a possibility within his spiritual account. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
