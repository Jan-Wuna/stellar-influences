## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One may clearly receive spiritual messages when one believes one is capable of doing so, and when one let’s go of the negative and limiting aspects of their identity.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 104, Ascendant paragraph under Chiron/Midheaven; PDF page 102 is the chapter start. Actual PDF checked; the printed “let’s” is retained.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Possible clarity in receiving spiritual messages through confidence and release of limiting identity.
- **Psychology:** Believing oneself capable of receiving messages and letting go of negative self-definitions are the stated conditions.
- **Body/health:** No specific bodily or clinical claim is made in this paragraph.
- **Social/relationship:** No particular interpersonal outcome is stated.
- **Events/manifestations:** One may receive spiritual messages clearly under the described conditions.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Sandbach's “may” qualifies the claim; the paragraph does not specify a career, public position, or practical test of a message.

## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/MC = Asc` concerns openness to receiving spiritual messages. Sandbach links possible clarity with believing oneself capable of receiving them and releasing negative or limiting aspects of identity. Both conditions belong to his spiritual interpretation, and the result remains qualified by “may.” ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
