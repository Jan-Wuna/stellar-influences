## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/MC = Jupiter` describes an ability to encourage people to strengthen their connection with spiritual forces and follow their true path in life. Sandbach's emphasis is on widening and supporting that connection; the paragraph leaves the content of each person's path open. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
