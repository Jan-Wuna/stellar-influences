### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

Action that may carry much spiritual authority. The ability to act on the direction of spiritual messages.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Acting on spiritual messages, with action that may carry spiritual authority.
- **Psychology:** An ability to translate perceived spiritual direction into action.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or clinical healing claim is stated in this paragraph.

## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/MC = Mars` links spiritual direction with the ability to act on it. Sandbach describes action that may carry substantial spiritual authority, keeping that authority a possibility rather than an assured result of acting. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
