## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The emotional need to play a part in healing the world and fostering the entrance of spiritual energy into physical reality. The ability to make others feel that they are a necessary and important part in the world and that they have a limitless potential to change it.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 103, exact activation paragraph; PDF page 102 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF. The visible internal space in “en trance” is normalized to “entrance.”

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** An emotional need to help heal the world and bring spiritual energy into physical reality.
- **Psychology:** Feeling a need to contribute, with an ability to strengthen others' sense of importance and potential.
- **Body/health:** World healing and spiritual energy are Sandbach's broad terms; no bodily diagnosis or treatment is specified.
- **Social/relationship:** Helping others feel necessary and important in the world.
- **Events/manifestations:** Fostering others' sense that they can change the world expresses the stated ability.
- **Conflicts/notes:** “Limitless potential” describes what others are made to feel; it is not a specified material result or a parenting claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/MC = Moon` joins an emotional need to contribute to the world's healing with an ability to strengthen other people's sense of belonging and significance. Sandbach links this to fostering spiritual energy in physical reality and helping others feel that their potential to change the world is limitless. The passage describes that felt potential within his spiritual account. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
