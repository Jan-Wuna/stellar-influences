## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Through one’s connections with others one discovers at deeper and deeper levels what one’s life purpose is. Negatively one seeks out connections with others who reinforce one’s negative image of parent and authority figures, which serves to disempower rather than empower one.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 104, exact activation paragraph; PDF page 102 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF. The visible internal space in “disem power” is normalized to “disempower.”

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Discovering life purpose through connections, with a disempowering alternative when those connections reinforce negative authority images.
- **Psychology:** Understanding of purpose deepens through contact; negative images of parents and authority can also be reinforced.
- **Body/health:** No bodily diagnosis or clinical treatment is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** One's choice of connections can support discovery or strengthen a negative image of parent and authority figures.
- **Events/manifestations:** Deepening life-purpose discovery or disempowerment through reinforcing connections are the explicit alternatives.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The negative case concerns reinforced images of parents and authority, rather than every difficult relationship; no specific vocation is named.

## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/MC = Node` describes connections through which understanding of life purpose grows deeper. Sandbach also gives a negative alternative: seeking people who reinforce one's negative image of parents and authority figures can disempower the person. The distinction turns on what the connections reinforce. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
