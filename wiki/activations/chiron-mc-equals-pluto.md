## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One feels a tremendous inner pressure to find and fulfill one’s life purpose, but will not be able to alleviate this pressure until one allows oneself to become completely receptive to spiritual energies.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 103, exact activation paragraph; PDF page 102 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Intense inner pressure to discover and fulfill life purpose, linked to complete receptivity to spiritual energies.
- **Psychology:** Sandbach describes the pressure as tremendous and makes receptivity a condition for relieving it.
- **Body/health:** Inner pressure is described without a bodily diagnosis or clinical treatment.
- **Social/relationship:** The paragraph specifies no interpersonal or public-role outcome.
- **Events/manifestations:** Seeking and fulfilling life purpose is the focus; relief is said to remain unavailable until spiritual receptivity is allowed.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The strong “until” condition is the source's claim; spiritual energies and complete receptivity are not operationally defined.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/MC = Pluto` describes tremendous inner pressure to find and fulfill life purpose. In Sandbach's account, that pressure cannot be relieved until the person allows complete receptivity to spiritual energies. The emphasis is on this condition for relief, with life purpose left broader than a particular career. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
