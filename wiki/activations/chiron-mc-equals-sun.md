## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s sense of self-importance is based on one’s career and place in the world. When this is expressed with selfishness and pettiness suffering is the result. When one sees one’s career ultimately as a healer and sharer of spiritual energies (no matter what the actual worldly career may be) one fulfills one’s purpose in life and is a bringer of light.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 103, exact activation paragraph; PDF page 102 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Self-importance tied to career and place in the world, with suffering or spiritual purpose depending on how it is expressed.
- **Psychology:** Sandbach contrasts selfishness and pettiness with seeing one's work as healing and sharing spiritual energies.
- **Body/health:** Healing is a spiritual characterization of one's work here; no medical profession or clinical result is specified.
- **Social/relationship:** Sharing spiritual energies gives the career an outward contribution in the source's account.
- **Events/manifestations:** Selfish expression brings suffering; seeing any worldly career as healing and spiritual sharing is said to fulfill life purpose.
- **Conflicts/notes:** These are Sandbach's moral and spiritual judgments. His explicit “no matter what” qualification leaves the actual occupation open.

## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/MC = Sun` ties self-importance to career and one's place in the world. Sandbach contrasts suffering through selfishness and pettiness with fulfillment through seeing one's work as healing and sharing spiritual energies. He applies the latter perspective to any worldly career, calling its expression a fulfillment of purpose and a bringing of light. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
