### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

In love relationships one helps the other person to attain to greater and truer self-respect. Love that channels spiritual forces.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 103, exact activation paragraph; PDF page 102 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Love that channels spiritual forces while helping the other person develop greater, truer self-respect.
- **Psychology:** The stated development concerns the other person's self-respect.
- **Body/health:** No bodily diagnosis or clinical healing claim is made.
- **Social/relationship:** Helping the other person occurs within love relationships.
- **Events/manifestations:** Support for greater self-respect and love as a channel for spiritual forces are the explicit expressions.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Sandbach specifies the direction of help toward the other person; he gives no vocation, rescuing or over-giving claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/MC = Venus` describes love that helps the other person attain greater and truer self-respect. Sandbach places this help within love relationships and describes the love itself as a channel for spiritual forces. The emphasis is on the other person's self-respect and the spiritual quality of the relationship. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
