## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Healing visions shared with the world. One brings a therapeutically imaginative energy to one’s career. One’s mission in life is to clear away confusion and bring light to that which is hidden, as well as to listen to and follow the messages received from one's intuition.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 98–99, exact activation paragraph; PDF page 97 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF. The MC paragraph begins at the foot of PDF98 and continues at the top of99. The visible internal space in “imagi native” is normalized to “imaginative.”

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sharing healing visions and bringing therapeutic imagination to career and life mission.
- **Psychology:** Listening to and following intuitive messages accompanies the wish to clear confusion and illuminate what is hidden.
- **Body/health:** Healing and therapeutic energy are broad source descriptions, without a diagnosis, treatment or specified profession.
- **Social/relationship:** Healing visions are shared with the world.
- **Events/manifestations:** Imaginative career expression, clearing confusion, revealing the hidden and following intuition are the stated forms.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The source says “that which is hidden,” without restricting it to suffering; no savior-fantasy, drift or discipline warning is supplied.

## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Neptune = MC` describes sharing healing visions with the world and bringing what Sandbach calls therapeutically imaginative energy to a career. He characterizes the life mission as clearing confusion, illuminating what is hidden, and listening to and following intuitive messages. These are the entry's linked aims, with the actual occupation left open. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
