### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A mind capable of communicating deeply healing subtleties. Communicating that can heal confusion.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 98, exact activation paragraph; PDF page 97 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Communicating subtleties that Sandbach describes as deeply healing, including communication that can heal confusion.
- **Psychology:** The mind's capacity for subtle communication is the stated ability.
- **Body/health:** Healing confusion is the specific claim; no bodily diagnosis or clinical treatment is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Communication is the means of influence, without a specified recipient or relationship.
- **Events/manifestations:** Conveying healing subtleties and possible relief of confusion are the explicit expressions.
- **Conflicts/notes:** “Can” preserves possibility; the paragraph gives no fear-healing claim or warning about vague language.

## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Neptune = Mercury` describes an ability to communicate subtleties that Sandbach calls deeply healing. He specifically includes communication that can heal confusion, leaving the recipients and practical form of the communication open. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
