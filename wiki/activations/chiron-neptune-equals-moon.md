## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A tremendous refinement of feeling that has the potential of healing others at subtle, hidden levels. To be able to nurture others in a delicate, subtle manner.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 98, exact activation paragraph; PDF page 97 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Refined feeling with a potential for healing others at subtle, hidden levels.
- **Psychology:** Tremendous refinement of feeling is the stated emotional quality.
- **Body/health:** Healing is presented as potential and its hidden levels are unspecified; no clinical diagnosis or treatment is given.
- **Social/relationship:** An ability to nurture others delicately and subtly.
- **Events/manifestations:** Subtle nurturing and potential healing of others are the stated expressions.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The paragraph gives no nonverbal-only method, emotional-flooding claim or burden of absorbing others' pain.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Neptune = Moon` describes highly refined feeling expressed through delicate, subtle nurturing of others. Sandbach gives that feeling the potential to heal at subtle, hidden levels, without specifying a method or promising a result. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
