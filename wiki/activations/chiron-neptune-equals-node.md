## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The ability to be aware of the subtle influences at work in all your connections. When you allow them to guide you, healing is the result.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 98, exact activation paragraph; PDF page 97 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Awareness of subtle influences throughout one's connections, with healing attributed to allowing their guidance.
- **Psychology:** Becoming aware of those influences and allowing them to guide are distinct parts of the description.
- **Body/health:** Sandbach states healing as the result of following the influences, without defining a bodily or clinical mechanism.
- **Social/relationship:** The influences are said to operate in all one's connections.
- **Events/manifestations:** Allowing the perceived subtle influences to guide one's course is the condition attached to healing.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The healing claim is Sandbach's; he supplies no practical test of the influences or wishful-interpretation warning.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Neptune = Node` describes awareness of subtle influences at work throughout one's connections. Sandbach states that allowing those influences to guide the person results in healing. The claim depends on accepting that guidance, while the nature of the influences and healing remains unspecified. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
