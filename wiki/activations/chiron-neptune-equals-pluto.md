## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To have intense intuitions that will not go away until they are listened to and acted on.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 98, exact activation paragraph; PDF page 97 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Intense intuitions that persist until attended to and acted upon.
- **Psychology:** The stated intensity and persistence keep the intuitions present.
- **Body/health:** This sentence states no healing outcome or bodily diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** No particular relationship or other person is specified.
- **Events/manifestations:** Listening and acting are both attached to the point at which the intuitions cease persisting.
- **Conflicts/notes:** This is Sandbach's description of persistence; he supplies no test of intuition, clinical obsession claim or specific action.

## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Neptune = Pluto` describes intense intuitions that continue to press for attention. Sandbach says they persist until they are both listened to and acted on, leaving their content and the action involved unspecified. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
