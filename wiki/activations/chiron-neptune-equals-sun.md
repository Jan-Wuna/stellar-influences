### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To be highly aware of the healing potentials of the imagination, together with the desire to use them in a meaningful way. The ability to bring to light those subtleties that are the ones that will most heal a situation.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 98, exact activation paragraph; PDF page 97 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Awareness of imagination's healing potential and a wish to use it meaningfully.
- **Psychology:** Sensitivity to the subtleties described as most healing for a situation.
- **Body/health:** Healing refers to a situation and imagination's potential; no specific bodily diagnosis or cure is named.
- **Social/relationship:** The situation and people involved remain unspecified.
- **Events/manifestations:** Bringing helpful subtleties to light is the stated ability.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Sandbach's healing language is retained as his claim; the paragraph does not discuss identity strengthening or an ego-investment warning.

## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Neptune = Sun` combines awareness of imagination's healing potential with a desire to put it to meaningful use. Sandbach also describes an ability to reveal the subtleties he regards as most healing for a situation. His focus is on imagination and situational insight, with the specific situation left open.
