## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Suffering due to one’s personality causing one to be cut off from spiritual forces, or joy due to one’s personality being open and unattached enough to allow one to experience spiritual connectedness.

Source locator: one-based PDF page 101, exact activation paragraph; PDF page 100 is the chapter start. Checked against the actual PDF.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Personality linked to suffering through spiritual disconnection or joy through open, unattached connectedness.
- **Psychology:** Openness and nonattachment are the personality qualities associated with joy in Sandbach's account.
- **Body/health:** Suffering and joy are broad experiences here, without a bodily diagnosis or clinical treatment.
- **Social/relationship:** The connectedness is explicitly spiritual; no particular human relationship is named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Being cut off from spiritual forces or experiencing spiritual connection are the stated alternatives.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The alternatives are attributed to Sandbach; the sentence does not specify a self-presentation practice or an unmet longing.

## Derived Synthesis

`Chiron/Node = Asc` presents two ways personality can affect spiritual connectedness. Sandbach links suffering with personality cutting the person off from spiritual forces, and joy with a personality open and unattached enough to experience connection. The distinction concerns spiritual openness and its stated emotional consequences. ([Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry))
