## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One is able to connect with spiritual forces in a full and rich way, on many levels. An understanding of spiritual connections and an ability to enhance and further them.

Source locator: PDF p. 101 (one-based; exact Jupiter entry in the Chiron/Node chapter).

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Rich spiritual connection on several levels.
- **Psychology:** Understanding connections and how to develop them.
- **Body/health:** Spiritual forces are discussed; no bodily or clinical claim is made.
- **Social/relationship:** Connections are spiritual; a particular partnership is not specified.
- **Events/manifestations:** Enhancing and furthering spiritual connections.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The account is spiritual; its breadth is not a measure of specific worldly outcomes.

## Contradictions

One entry presents mutually compatible aspects of spiritual connection; it gives no competing account.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `Chiron/Node` as a full, varied experience of spiritual connection. Understanding those connections supports the ability to enhance and develop them, so the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) joins rich experience with active cultivation. The scope remains spiritual connection on many levels, without a particular worldly outcome being named.
