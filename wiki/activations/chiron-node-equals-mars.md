## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To build spiritual connections and to carry out actions in a spiritually connected way. The striving to connect with spiritual forces. Engaging in conflicts that have a spir itual healing effect on one and which advance one’s evolution.

Source locator: PDF p. 101 (one-based; exact Mars entry in the Chiron/Node chapter).

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Building spiritual connections through action and striving.
- **Psychology:** A wish to connect with spiritual forces.
- **Body/health:** Healing is spiritual in this entry, without a bodily treatment claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Connection is pursued through action; no particular relationship is named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Actions performed with spiritual connection, including conflicts described as healing.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The healing effect qualifies the conflicts described; it does not endorse every conflict.

## Contradictions

The entry links action and qualified healing conflict; no separate contradictory account is present.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies `Chiron/Node` through efforts to build spiritual connections and act from them. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) includes conflicts that have a spiritual healing effect and advance evolution. That qualification belongs to the interpretation: the passage does not make conflict itself evidence of healing.
