## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s life purpose involves fostering spiritual connectedness between people. Healing one’s attachment to the past is a key to being able to fulfill one’s life purpose.

Source locator: PDF p. 101 (one-based; exact Midheaven entry in the Chiron/Node chapter).

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Life purpose centered on spiritual connectedness between people.
- **Psychology:** Healing attachment to the past is presented as a key to purpose.
- **Body/health:** Healing refers to past attachment; no physical condition is specified.
- **Social/relationship:** Helping people connect spiritually.
- **Events/manifestations:** Pursuing a purpose whose fulfillment involves healing past attachment.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entry supplies a condition for fulfillment, not a named profession.

## Contradictions

The entry makes past attachment relevant to fulfillment; it supplies no competing explanation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies `Chiron/Node` as a life purpose involving spiritual connectedness between people. In the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry), healing attachment to the past is a key to fulfilling that purpose, linking work on one's own history with the connections one helps create. A particular vocation or role as a guide is not established.
