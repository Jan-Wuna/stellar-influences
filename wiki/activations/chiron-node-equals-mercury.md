## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One is able to communicate ideas that come directly from spiritual sources. Through communicating one’s helps others to connect more to spiritual forces. Think ing and communicating that help to heal the past.

Source locator: PDF p. 100 (one-based; exact Mercury entry in the Chiron/Node chapter).

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Communication of ideas understood as coming from spiritual sources.
- **Psychology:** Thinking that helps heal the past.
- **Body/health:** Healing is described spiritually; no medical claim is made.
- **Social/relationship:** Communication helps others connect with spiritual forces.
- **Events/manifestations:** Sharing spiritually sourced ideas and thinking through the past.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The awkward phrase "Through communicating one’s helps" is retained; it does not establish hypocrisy or a teaching role.

## Contradictions

Only one entry is present; its communication and past-healing themes do not supply a competing account.

## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies `Chiron/Node` through thinking and communication. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) connects ideas attributed to spiritual sources with helping others make spiritual connections, while thought and expression also help heal the past. Its awkward middle sentence is retained as a textual limitation; the surrounding statements keep the communicative and past-healing focus clear.
