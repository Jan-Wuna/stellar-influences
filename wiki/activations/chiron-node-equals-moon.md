## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An emotional need to overcome the past and to seek out those experiences which will enhance one’s evolution. To instinctively feel what areas and aspects of one’s past need healing.

Source locator: PDF p. 100 (one-based; exact Moon entry in the Chiron/Node chapter).

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** An emotional need to move beyond the past toward further evolution.
- **Psychology:** Instinctively sensing which aspects of the past need healing.
- **Body/health:** The passage gives emotional and spiritual language, without a bodily diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** No particular relationship is specified.
- **Events/manifestations:** Seeking experiences that enhance evolution.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The past is the object of healing, not proof that the person remains trapped in it.

## Contradictions

The emotional need and instinctive sensitivity are complementary; there is no competing entry.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies `Chiron/Node` as an emotional need to overcome the past and seek experiences that support evolution. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) locates guidance in an instinctive sense of which past areas need healing. Its focus is the relationship to one's history; a separate interpersonal story or inevitable emotional bondage is not stated.
