## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One may have visions of how everything is spiritually connected. Subtle awareness of the way in which the force of evolution is working. The inability to let go of the past creates confusion and delusion in one’s life.

Source locator: PDF p. 101 (one-based; exact Neptune entry in the Chiron/Node chapter).

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Visions of spiritual connectedness and subtle awareness of evolution.
- **Psychology:** Failure to release the past is linked to confusion and delusion.
- **Body/health:** The psychological wording is the source's description, not a clinical finding.
- **Social/relationship:** Connection concerns reality as a whole; no particular partner is named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Experiencing visions or recognizing how evolution is working.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The confusion claim is conditional on inability to let go of the past.

## Contradictions

The visionary and confused possibilities have different conditions; the entry does not state that every vision is delusion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `Chiron/Node` through visions of spiritual connectedness and subtle awareness of evolution. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) also states a conditional difficulty: inability to release the past creates confusion and delusion. Keeping that condition intact preserves both the visionary possibility and its stated limit without adding a general claim about fantasy.
