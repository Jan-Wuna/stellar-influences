## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An intense desire to let go of the past and to experience spiritual connectedness. The seeking of outer power to serve the ego because one is unable to let go of the past and take in those new experiences which would feed the soul.

Source locator: PDF p. 101 (one-based; exact Pluto entry in the Chiron/Node chapter).

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Intense desire to release the past and experience spiritual connectedness.
- **Psychology:** Ego-serving pursuit of outer power when the past cannot be released.
- **Body/health:** The entry's soul language does not specify a physical condition.
- **Social/relationship:** No specific partner or group is identified.
- **Events/manifestations:** Seeking new experiences that feed the soul, or seeking outer power when blocked.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The ego-serving power motive is conditional, not a universal trait.

## Contradictions

The entry contrasts releasing the past with a conditional ego-serving response to being unable to do so.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies `Chiron/Node` as an intense desire to let go of the past and experience spiritual connectedness. In the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry), inability to make that release can instead direct the search toward outer power serving the ego, excluding new experiences that would feed the soul. The contrast concerns these stated motives; it does not establish inevitable obsession or control of other people.
