## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To feel cut off from one’s spiritual connections, or unable to make or find them. The frustration concerning this nags at one until the connections are formed. Difficulty in connecting with healing forces, and a need for patience, persistence and focus in doing so. Connecting to healing forces that operate gradually or slowly in one’s life.

Source locator: PDF p. 101 (one-based; exact Saturn entry in the Chiron/Node chapter).

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Difficulty finding spiritual connections and gradual access to healing forces.
- **Psychology:** Frustration, with patience, persistence and focus required.
- **Body/health:** Healing forces are spiritual in context; no bodily treatment is specified.
- **Social/relationship:** Spiritual connections are sought; particular interpersonal bonds are not named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Connections forming slowly or gradually.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Feeling cut off is not presented as permanent inability to connect.

## Contradictions

The feeling of disconnection and eventual formation of connections describe a process, not a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies `Chiron/Node` through the frustration of feeling cut off from spiritual connections or struggling to find them. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) makes patience, persistence and focus relevant to forming those connections, including healing forces that work gradually. Difficulty and slow development belong together here; the passage does not declare connection impossible.
