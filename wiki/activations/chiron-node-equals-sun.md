### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s life is given meaning and purpose by connecting spiritually with others. To be aware of and vitalized by one’s spiritual connections to the whole of reality.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Chiron/Node = Sun`: identity and purpose being nourished by real connectedness. Main caution: making belonging into a source of self-importance instead of living it as service.
