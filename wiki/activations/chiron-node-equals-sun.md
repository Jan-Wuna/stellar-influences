## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s life is given meaning and purpose by connecting spiritually with others. To be aware of and vitalized by one’s spiritual connections to the whole of reality.

Source locator: PDF p. 100 (one-based; exact Sun entry in the Chiron/Node chapter).

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Meaning and purpose arising through spiritual connection.
- **Psychology:** Awareness of spiritual connections that vitalize life.
- **Body/health:** Vitalization is stated, without a physical diagnosis or health mechanism.
- **Social/relationship:** Connecting spiritually with others.
- **Events/manifestations:** Experiencing connection to the whole of reality.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entry makes no claim about self-importance or an obligation to serve.

## Contradictions

The entry's interpersonal and wider spiritual connections are compatible; no competing account is present.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies `Chiron/Node` as spiritual connection that gives life meaning and purpose. Connecting with others belongs to the wider awareness of connection with reality as a whole, which the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) describes as vitalizing. The interpretation rests on that source of purpose; it does not add a moral warning about self-importance.
