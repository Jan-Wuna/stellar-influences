## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A love and appreciation of spiritual connectedness. One has an appreciation of where one has come from and where one is going and of how spiritual forces may be enlisted to enhance the process of evolution.

Source locator: PDF p. 101 (one-based; exact Venus entry in the Chiron/Node chapter).

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Love and appreciation of spiritual connectedness.
- **Psychology:** Appreciating one's origins and direction.
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition or treatment is specified.
- **Social/relationship:** Spiritual connectedness is valued; romantic partnership is not specified.
- **Events/manifestations:** Enlisting spiritual forces to enhance evolution.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entry supplies no opposition between appreciation and fear, or warning about sentimentality.

## Contradictions

The entry presents compatible forms of appreciation; it contains no competing account.

## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies `Chiron/Node` as love and appreciation of spiritual connectedness. Appreciation extends to where one has come from and where one is going, while spiritual forces may support evolution. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) therefore links valuing connection with a sense of one's path, without specifying romance or a failure of sincerity.
