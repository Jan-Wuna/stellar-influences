## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 100 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

A personality that emanates an extremely healing energy, which may be overt or covert. One is pushed in this lifetime to let go of the strongest and deepest at tachments that have formed one’s identity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A healing presence and pressure to release deep attachments that define identity.
- psychology: Strong identification and the need to let its deepest attachments go.
- body/health: Healing energy is spiritual source language; no physical condition or treatment is identified.
- social/relationship: The personality's influence may be overt or covert.
- events/manifestations: A change in attachments forming the identity; no concrete event is specified.
- conflicts/notes: The entry does not name identity wounds or an attraction to intensity for its own sake.

## Contradictions

The overt and covert forms describe visibility of the same attributed influence; they are not conflicting accounts.

## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Chiron/Pluto through the personality and the attachments shaping identity. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) describes an overt or covert healing influence together with strong pressure to release the deepest of those attachments. The connection is between how the person manifests and what must change in their self-definition; the healing-energy language remains a spiritual claim rather than a clinical mechanism.
