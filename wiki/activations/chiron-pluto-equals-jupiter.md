## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 99 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

An enthusiasm so tremendous that it can create profound transformations. Being obsessively excessive eventually has the effect of spiritually transmuting one’s being.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Tremendous enthusiasm or obsessive excess associated with profound spiritual transformation.
- psychology: Enthusiasm, intensity, and obsessive excess.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: No particular relationship is identified.
- events/manifestations: Profound transformation and spiritual transmutation are the stated possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: The source positively links excess to eventual transmutation; it does not separately diagnose grandiosity.

## Contradictions

The entry includes both enthusiasm and obsessive excess within its transformative account; it supplies no competing warning that excess necessarily prevents change.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Chiron/Pluto through enthusiasm strong enough, in the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry), to produce profound transformation. The source extends this to obsessive excess eventually bringing spiritual transmutation. That unusual positive claim about excess remains attributed to the entry; the brief passage does not explain its mechanism or establish a general rule that excess is beneficial.
