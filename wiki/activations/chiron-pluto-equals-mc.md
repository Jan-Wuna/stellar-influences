## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An intense need to fulfill one’s life purpose and to be open to spiritual guidance, though one often finds that any pursuit of this may seem to get nowhere. The key here is to learn to harmonize one’s will with the will of the Spiritual Light and then to let everything flow.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Chiron/Pluto = MC`: vocation under deep pressure and to a purpose that matures through surrender rather than sheer force. Main caution: mistaking spiritual purpose for another project of willpower.
