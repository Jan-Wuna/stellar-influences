## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 100 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

An intense need to fulfill one’s life purpose and to be open to spiritual guidance, though one often finds that any pursuit of this may seem to get nowhere. The key here is to learn to harmonize one’s will with the will of the Spiritual Light and then to let everything flow.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: An intense sense of life purpose whose pursuit may seem unproductive.
- psychology: Need for purpose and guidance, with apparent lack of progress.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: No specific relationship or public position is named.
- events/manifestations: Harmonizing personal will with what the source calls Spiritual Light.
- conflicts/notes: Life purpose is explicit; a particular profession or public role is not.

## Contradictions

Intense pursuit and seeming to get nowhere coexist; the entry proposes spiritual alignment without giving a timetable or guaranteed career outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Chiron/Pluto through an intense need to fulfill a life purpose and receive spiritual guidance. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) retains the frustration that pursuing this can seem to get nowhere. Its proposed resolution is to harmonize personal will with the will of Spiritual Light and allow events to flow. This remains the source's spiritual account of purpose, without a specified profession or assured external achievement.
