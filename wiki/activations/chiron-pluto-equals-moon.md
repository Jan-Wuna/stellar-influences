## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 99 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

To experience feelings of great intensity, which, if given into, can lead to pro found healing.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intense feeling that may lead to healing when given into.
- psychology: Great emotional intensity and receptivity to feeling.
- body/health: The source names healing without identifying a physical condition, treatment, or mechanism.
- social/relationship: No relationship setting is supplied.
- events/manifestations: An inner change through feeling; no external event is named.
- conflicts/notes: If given into and can lead are material qualifications.

## Contradictions

The single sentence offers a conditional possibility, with no separate claim about emotional drama or inevitable transformation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Chiron/Pluto through feelings of great intensity. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) makes its healing possibility conditional on giving into those feelings and says that this can lead to profound healing. The scope is an attributed emotional and spiritual process; it supplies neither a diagnosis nor a certainty that intense feeling produces the stated result.
