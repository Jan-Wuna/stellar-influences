## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 100 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

To be drawn to connections with others with whom profound and intense healing may be experienced. To deeply and fully come to terms so completely with the past that one is freed to evolve.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections involving intense healing and coming to terms with the past.
- psychology: Deep acceptance or resolution of the past as a condition of further development.
- body/health: Healing is spiritual language here, with no stated physical condition or treatment.
- social/relationship: Being drawn to people with whom healing may be experienced.
- events/manifestations: Release from past attachments to allow development.
- conflicts/notes: The entry does not identify karmic ties, trauma reenactment, or a compulsory crisis.

## Contradictions

Healing with others and coming to terms with the past are linked possibilities, not a claim that every intense bond heals.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Chiron/Pluto through connections with people alongside whom profound healing may be experienced. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) connects this with coming so fully to terms with the past that further development becomes possible. The passage supports relational and past-oriented change without calling every bond karmic or making intensity a guarantee of healing.
