## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 99 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

One is highly aware of persistent healing forces which are always looking for a way to manifest. The pressure to let go of agendas and give in to the force of evolution. The relentless search for what is truly important.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Persistent awareness of healing forces and a search for what matters most.
- psychology: Pressure to release agendas and relentless concern with importance.
- body/health: The source names healing forces without a bodily condition or treatment.
- social/relationship: No particular relationship setting is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Releasing agendas and yielding to development are the stated expressions.
- conflicts/notes: Purified identity, self-importance, and egoic intensity are not independently stated.

## Contradictions

Persistent searching and yielding personal agendas are compatible emphases in this single entry.

## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Chiron/Pluto through awareness of persistent healing forces and a relentless search for what is truly important. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) ties that search to pressure to let go of personal agendas and yield to what it calls evolution. The organizing issue is purpose within the source's spiritual process, without an added claim about self-importance or a medical healing result.
