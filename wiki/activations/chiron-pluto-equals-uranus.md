## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF pages 99–100 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

One is guided by spiritual forces that push one to break away from old forms, be experimental, and tune into one’s inner genius. Inspiration that has a profoundly transforming effect.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Experimentation and transformative inspiration that breaks away from old forms.
- psychology: Receptivity to inspiration and what the source calls inner genius.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: No particular relationship is delineated.
- events/manifestations: Experimental departures from old forms.
- conflicts/notes: The source describes guidance and inspiration, without indiscriminate destruction.

## Contradictions

Breaking from old forms and following inspiration are linked in the entry; no separate destructive outcome is specified.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies Chiron/Pluto through inspiration that prompts experimentation and a break with old forms. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) explains this as spiritual guidance toward one's inner genius and attributes a profoundly transforming effect to inspiration. It does not extend that departure into an instruction to destroy existing structures or establish a particular external breakthrough.
