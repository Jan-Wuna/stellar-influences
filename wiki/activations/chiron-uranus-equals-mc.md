## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 97 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

The need to take a highly individualistic approach to one’s career. One’s life purpose involves taking a free, open, experimental and independent approach to one’s work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: An individual, open, experimental, and independent approach to work.
- psychology: A need to approach career in a highly individual way.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Work and career are explicit, without a particular public role.
- events/manifestations: An experimental approach to one's work and life purpose.
- conflicts/notes: Independence does not by itself establish opposition to all structure.

## Contradictions

The entry consistently emphasizes an individual approach; it supplies no conflict between true calling and structure.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Chiron/Uranus through the way work and career express individuality. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) joins freedom, openness, experimentation, and independence as qualities of that approach, describing them as part of life purpose. The passage identifies a manner of working without naming a profession or making resistance to structure a test of vocation.
