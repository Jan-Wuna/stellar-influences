## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 97 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

One is aware of the uniqueness of ever different emotion that arises, and even though one may seem to experience the same feelings over and over their subtleties and contexts make them forever new and distinctive. Healing manifests through allow ing oneself to be emotionally free and uninhibited.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Recognizing each feeling's distinctive context and allowing emotional freedom.
- psychology: Attention to subtle differences even when feelings seem repetitive.
- body/health: The source's healing claim concerns emotional openness; no physical treatment is given.
- social/relationship: No particular relationship is delineated.
- events/manifestations: Experiencing feelings as new through their subtleties and context.
- conflicts/notes: Emotional freedom is stated; habitual unpredictability is not.

## Contradictions

Apparently repeated feelings and their distinctive contexts are complementary parts of the account.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Chiron/Uranus through the distinctiveness of emotional experience. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) explains that even apparently repeated feelings differ through subtleties and context, and connects healing with allowing emotional freedom. The emphasis is receptive attention to feeling, without making unpredictability itself a measure of freedom.
