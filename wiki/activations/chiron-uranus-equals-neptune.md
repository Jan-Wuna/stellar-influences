## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 97 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

Insights into the ways that anything and everything can be used for healing purposes. An intuition that receives messages from beyond that can come in many forms, such as portents and synchronistic occurrences.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intuitive recognition of varied healing possibilities and symbolic messages.
- psychology: Intuition receiving what the source calls messages from beyond.
- body/health: No particular condition or treatment follows from the broad healing language.
- social/relationship: No particular relationship setting is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Portents and synchronistic occurrences are named forms of messages.
- conflicts/notes: Portents means perceived signs; the entry treats them as spiritual messages, without independently verifying them.

## Contradictions

Only the source's intuitive account is supplied; it does not give a separate test for discerning signs.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Chiron/Uranus through intuition receptive to many forms of meaning. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) describes insight into varied healing possibilities and messages from beyond appearing as portents, or perceived signs, and synchronistic occurrences. These remain the source's spiritual interpretation of experience, without establishing a medical use or independently confirming the messages.
