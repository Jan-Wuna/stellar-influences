## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 97 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

Seeking out connections with others who help you explore and affirm your own individual uniqueness and its gifts. A need in this lifetime to let go of the fear of being different and/or alienated.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections that affirm individual uniqueness and help release fear of difference.
- psychology: Fear of being different or alienated and affirmation of one's gifts.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Seeking people who help explore and value one's distinctive qualities.
- events/manifestations: Developing supportive connections around individuality.
- conflicts/notes: The entry does not require difference to be pursued as an end in itself.

## Contradictions

Connection and individuality are linked rather than opposed in the single entry.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Chiron/Uranus through seeking people who help explore and affirm one's uniqueness and gifts. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) joins that support with releasing fear of being different or alienated. Individuality develops through connection in this account, without a requirement that every meaningful bond emphasize difference.
