## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 97 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

One may find it difficult to be free and open but feels the persistent need to do so. You are caught off guard by unexpected happenings and this can help you to free yourself of needless restrictions and limitations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficulty being open alongside persistent need for freedom.
- psychology: Resistance or limitation meeting the surprise of unexpected events.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: No particular relationship is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Being caught off guard can help release needless restrictions.
- conflicts/notes: The possible release is conditional; slow progress is not explicitly stated.

## Contradictions

Difficulty and need coexist, and unexpected events can help without guaranteeing release.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Chiron/Uranus through difficulty being free and open while still feeling a persistent need for it. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) gives unexpected events a possible opening role: being caught off guard can help loosen needless restrictions. The possibility remains conditional and does not establish that every disruption heals or that the process must be slow.
