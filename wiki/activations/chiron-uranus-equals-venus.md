## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An appreciation and love of the uniqueness of each individual, and even each situation that arises in life, and to see that healing may arise out of this uniqueness in infinite and ever-changing ways. Knowing the value of letting go of expectations and appreciating all the surprises and unforeseen events that cross one’s path.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Chiron/Uranus = Venus`: love that frees, aesthetics of difference, and healing through appreciative openness to the unexpected. Main caution: idealizing uniqueness without learning how to sustain relationship.
