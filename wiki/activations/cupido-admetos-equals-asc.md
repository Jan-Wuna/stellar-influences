## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 271 (printed 275).

#### Witte Entry

The members of an association drift apart, they seclude themselves.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 215 (printed 432).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Endurance of an environmental community; blocking environmental community; restricted local community; consolidated local community; shared deepening into the environment; thorough partner community; suppression within the partner community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Local or partner communities may become restricted, consolidated or divided.
- psychology: Withdrawal and seclusion in an association.
- body/health: No specific physical or medical claim.
- social/relationship: Members drifting apart; endurance and suppression within partner or local communities.
- events/manifestations: Restricted or consolidated local association; shared deepening into the surroundings.
- conflicts/notes: Drifting apart is one source possibility, alongside consolidated association and endurance.

## Contradictions And Differences

Drifting apart is one source possibility, alongside consolidated association and endurance. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Admetos = Asc` concerns the condition of an association in its surroundings and partnerships. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes members drifting apart and withdrawing; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also allows endurance and a consolidated local community, alongside restriction or suppression within partnership. The range therefore includes both seclusion and a more firmly established association, without making either the inevitable outcome.
