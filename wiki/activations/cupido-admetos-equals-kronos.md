## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 272 (printed 276).

#### Witte Entry

An association dissolved by the state or the authorities. The state causes obstacles and difficulties during the dissolution of a corporation. Dissolution or breaking up of treaties, of the constitution, or form of government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 215 (printed 433).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Reputation of a community through perseverance; blocked state community; depressed authority of a community; consolidation of an independent community; consolidated hierarchical community; thorough superiority of a community.

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Admetos = Kronos` concerns the standing and institutional conditions of an association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes state action dissolving an association or obstructing a corporation's dissolution, extending the range to treaties and constitutional forms. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes blocked state association and depressed authority, but also reputation through perseverance and consolidation of an independent or hierarchical community. These clauses support both institutional obstruction and firmer organization, without making dissolution universal.
