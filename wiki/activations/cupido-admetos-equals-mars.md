## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 271 (printed 275).

#### Witte Entry

Marriage under aggravating circumstances. To separate immediately after marriage. To separate from a partnership or community in connection with the activity. To be alone with one’s work. The efforts against wasting the forces or establishing cooperative relations are in vain.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 215 (printed 432).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Restriction of shared actions; blocked action community; depressed work community; shared endurance at work; thorough communal work; deepening in shared activity; resistance of a work community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Common activity under hindrance, with separation or sustained concentrated work.
- psychology: Depressed work community; perseverance in shared work.
- body/health: No specific physical or medical claim.
- social/relationship: Separation from a community through work or marriage under aggravating conditions.
- events/manifestations: Blocked action, thorough communal work and resistance by a work community.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's failed cooperative efforts coexist with ABC's perseverance; neither applies to every effort.

## Contradictions And Differences

Witte's failed cooperative efforts coexist with ABC's perseverance; neither applies to every effort. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Admetos = Mars` brings communal restriction into action and work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes separation, working alone and unsuccessful attempts to establish cooperation, including marriage under aggravating circumstances. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) retains blocked or depressed work association but also names shared endurance, thorough communal work and deepening in joint activity. The common thread is effort under constraint, with both separation and sustained shared work represented.
