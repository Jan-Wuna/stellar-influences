## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Marriage under aggravating circumstances. To separate immediately after marriage. To separate from a partnership or community in connection with the activity. To be alone with one’s work. The efforts against wasting the forces or establishing cooperative relations are in vain.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Limitation of common actions, blocked community of action, depressed community of work, joint perseverance at work, fundamental community work, deepening in joint activity, and resistance of a community of work

## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Admetos = Mars`: relational strain around work, action, or conflict and to the frustration of trying to force cooperation where it no longer holds. Main caution: harsh practical pressure breaking the bond faster than either party admits.
