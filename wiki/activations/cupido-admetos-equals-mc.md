## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 271 (printed 275).

#### Witte Entry

The native has few but close contacts. To be lonely, to become isolated in a community or partnership. To take part in the dissolution of a partnership or community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 215 (printed 432).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Blocked community of souls; soul depressed through art; shared depth of soul; I strengthen the community; my resistance against a community; my restrictions within the community; inner suppression within the community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The person's position within a close, isolated or inwardly constrained community.
- psychology: Loneliness, shared inner depth and inner suppression in community.
- body/health: No specific physical or medical claim; soul language is not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Few close contacts; isolation in partnership or involvement in dissolution.
- events/manifestations: Strengthening a community, resistance to it, restriction within it and depressing effects of art.
- conflicts/notes: Inner depth, strengthening and suppression are separate clauses; isolation need not define every contact.

## Contradictions And Differences

Inner depth, strengthening and suppression are separate clauses; isolation need not define every contact. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Admetos = MC` describes the person's own place in a community: a few close contacts can coexist with loneliness or isolation, and the person may participate in a partnership's dissolution. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supplies those relational circumstances; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds shared inner depth, strengthening the community, resistance to it and inner suppression within it. Its soul and art language remains a description of inner experience, without establishing a medical condition.
