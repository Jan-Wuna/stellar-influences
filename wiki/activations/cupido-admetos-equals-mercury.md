## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: printed p. 275 (one-based PDF p. 271), Mercury row.

To be per se with the thoughts. To think of the dissolution of the family or partnership. Farewell speeches, talks. Artistic thoughts in solitude.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 432 (one-based PDF p. 215), Mercury row.

Reports about shared depressions, blocked youth community, blockage of thought within the community, end of a news community, conversations about depressing art, and thorough conversations about art.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Thought and conversation concerning separation or communal blocks, alongside solitary artistic thought and thorough art discussion.
- Psychology: Thoughts about the dissolution of a family or partnership; ABC names blocked thinking and shared depressions without a diagnosis.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition is stated; depression remains the source's unqualified wording.
- Social/relationship: Family or partnership dissolution, farewell talks, blocked youth community, and the end of a news community.
- Events/manifestations: Farewell speeches, reports about shared depressions, and discussions about depressing art or art in depth.
- Conflicts/notes: The source's awkward per se with the thoughts is retained. News community is not further defined; Tibetan language is a translation artifact absent from the German row.

## Contradictions

Separation and blocked communication are prominent, but artistic thought in solitude and thorough art discussion give the entry a range beyond endings. Neither source states that reflection prevents a person from experiencing or accepting a separation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Admetos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through thought and speech about separation: family or partnership dissolution, farewell talks, and reports of shared difficulties. ABC adds blocked thinking within a community and the end of a news community. Art forms another thread, from solitary artistic thought to discussion of depressing art and thorough conversation about art; solitude is therefore a setting for reflection as well as a sign of separation. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
