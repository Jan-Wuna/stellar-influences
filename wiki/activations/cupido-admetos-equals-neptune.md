## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 271 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Changed conditions in a partnership or community. New moral standards and customs. New fashions. Revolting against old manners. Uncertain social position. Unreliable allies or partners.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 433; PDF page 215 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Blocked water circulation, restricted air circulation, consolidation of a shared future, joint dissolution of a blockage, shared endurance in the air, uncertainty in a suppressed community, water restrictions for the community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertain or changed community conditions, with restrictions and possible release of blockage.
- psychology: Uncertainty of social position and unreliable allies in Witte.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Changing partnership customs and the uncertainty of a suppressed community.
- events/manifestations: New moral standards or fashions, restricted water or air circulation, and joint removal of a blockage.
- conflicts/notes: The air and water clauses are literal source expressions without an explained mechanism.

## Contradictions

Witte's changed customs and unreliable partners differ from ABC's circulation restrictions and possible release of blockage. Neither requires the community to dissolve.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Cupido/Admetos through uncertain or altered conditions within a partnership or community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes new customs, fashions, resistance to old manners, and unreliable partners or an uncertain social position. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives a different concrete range: restricted water or air circulation, uncertainty in a suppressed community, but also consolidating a shared future and jointly dissolving a blockage. These possibilities preserve restriction and possible release without assuming that the bond must dissolve.
