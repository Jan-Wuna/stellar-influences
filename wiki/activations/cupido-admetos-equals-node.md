## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 271 (printed 275).

#### Witte Entry

In connection with people who are lonely and avoided. A small or limited union with shunned or avoided persons. To be a witness of the breaking up or dissolution of a partnership or community. To partake. Clique.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 215 (printed 432).

#### ABC Entry

Perseverance in common connections, end of common connections, firm connections in the community, consolidation of a community through contacts, connection to common resistance, and deepening of common relations

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restricted connections alongside firm ties, endurance and shared resistance.
- psychology: The source describes avoidance and loneliness without explaining their cause.
- body/health: No specific physical or medical claim.
- social/relationship: Connection with lonely or avoided people; a small union or clique.
- events/manifestations: Witnessing or taking part in dissolution; strengthening community through contacts and deepening relations.
- conflicts/notes: The source's labels for avoided people do not establish their character or why others avoid them.

## Contradictions And Differences

The source's labels for avoided people do not establish their character or why others avoid them. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Admetos = Node` specifies the bonds through which a community is limited, sustained or brought to an end. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes a small union with lonely or avoided people, a clique and participation in or witnessing dissolution. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also names enduring connections, firm ties, community consolidated through contacts and deepened relations. These are differing conditions of connection, without evidence for why particular people are avoided.
