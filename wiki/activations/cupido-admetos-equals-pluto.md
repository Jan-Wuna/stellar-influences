## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 271 (printed 275).

#### Witte Entry

Deep reaching changes or transformations in family or partnership. Passing reunion in family or passing changes in a community or partnership.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 215 (printed 433).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Depressing artistic development; restricted development of a community; consolidation through artistic developments; developing shared endurance; thorough changes within a community; growing resistance of a community.

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Admetos = Pluto` describes changes reaching into family, partnership or the community's development. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes deep transformation but also passing reunion or temporary communal change. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds restricted development and growing resistance alongside consolidation through artistic development and the growth of shared endurance. Change can therefore deepen or strengthen association without being uniformly lasting or welcome.
