## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 271 (printed 275).

#### Witte Entry

To face grave obstacles in one’s own family, to be rejected by the family. A family or partnership is in a fix, in a tight spot. To separate definitely from a partnership or community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 215 (printed 433).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Endurance and concentration of a community; depressions of a disturbed community; end of a community through separation; shared losses through blockage; thorough losses of a community; deepening in shared tasks.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A community under severe obstacles, separation or sustained concentration.
- psychology: Depression in a disturbed community, retained as the source's description.
- body/health: No specific physical or medical claim; communal depression is not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Family rejection, partnership in a tight spot and definite separation.
- events/manifestations: Loss through blockage; communal endurance, concentration and deepening in shared tasks.
- conflicts/notes: Concentration and shared tasks are present alongside loss; they do not promise that a partnership survives.

## Contradictions And Differences

Concentration and shared tasks are present alongside loss; they do not promise that a partnership survives. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Admetos = Saturn` concerns a community facing obstacles that may become separation or loss. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes family rejection, a partnership in difficulty and definite separation; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a disturbed community, losses through blockage, endurance and concentration in common tasks. Shared work can deepen under these conditions, but the entries do not turn that endurance into a guarantee of continued association.
