## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 271 (printed 275).

#### Witte Entry

The man who experiences the dissolution of his family, who is part of the dissolution of a partnership or community and experiences it immediately.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 215 (printed 432).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Blocked art centre; depressed community of people; restricted community of men; end of a community of people; shared physical endurance; centre of a resistance community; centre for shared deepening.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A person's direct involvement in communal ending, restriction or endurance.
- psychology: Depressed community of people.
- body/health: Shared physical endurance is explicit; no illness or diagnosis is specified.
- social/relationship: Witte's historical male example directly experiences family or partnership dissolution.
- events/manifestations: Blocked art centre, restricted community of men, ending an association and centres of resistance or deepening.
- conflicts/notes: The male example stays attributed; ABC's physical endurance is distinct from a health diagnosis.

## Contradictions And Differences

The male example stays attributed; ABC's physical endurance is distinct from a health diagnosis. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Admetos = Sun` concerns direct personal involvement in the community's limits or endurance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives the historical example of a man immediately experiencing the dissolution of family or partnership. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes a blocked art centre, restricted association and communal ending, but also shared physical endurance and a centre for resistance or common deepening. These concrete clauses do not establish a universal male fate or a medical condition.
