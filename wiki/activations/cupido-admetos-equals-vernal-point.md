## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 271 (printed 275).

#### Witte Entry

To get into strange surroundings, to be without connections. To feel lost.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 215 (printed 432).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Blocked community in public; depressed community in public life; depressing art in public; consolidated community in public life; shared restrictions in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public association under blockage or consolidation, alongside loss of connection.
- psychology: Feeling lost in unfamiliar surroundings.
- body/health: No specific physical or medical claim.
- social/relationship: Being without connections in unfamiliar surroundings; public communal life.
- events/manifestations: Blocked or depressed public community, depressing public art, consolidated public association and shared restrictions.

## Contradictions And Differences

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Admetos = Vernal Point` describes communal conditions in public life and the experience of entering unfamiliar surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes being without connections and feeling lost. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) specifies blocked or depressed public community, depressing public art and shared public restrictions, but also a consolidated community in public life. That fuller range allows established public association alongside disconnection.
