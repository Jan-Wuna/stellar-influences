## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 271 (printed 275).

#### Witte Entry

To tackle and work on a problem. Hindrance and delay in the newly established firm. The start of a great artistic creation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 215 (printed 433).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Enduring accomplishments of a community; restricted accomplishments of a community; consolidated leadership community; consolidating shared accomplishments; thorough shared efforts; resistance against a leadership community.

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Admetos = Zeus` concerns accomplishment pursued through obstacles. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names tackling a problem, delay in a newly established firm and the beginning of a great artistic creation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds enduring or restricted communal achievement, consolidation of a leadership community and thorough shared efforts, alongside resistance to that leadership association. The emphasis is on communal work and its constraints, with no stated research or technology programme.
