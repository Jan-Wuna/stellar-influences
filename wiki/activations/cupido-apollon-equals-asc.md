## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 270 (printed 274).

#### Witte Entry

To be connected with other people through art, science or family relations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 214 (printed 430).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Other communities are successful; expansion of a local community; successful partner community; successful environmental community; environmental experiences of a community; artistic successes at the location; the partner's artistic knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections through art, science, family and successful local or partner association.
- psychology: No separate personality or motive is given.
- body/health: No specific physical or medical claim.
- social/relationship: Relations with others through art, science or family.
- events/manifestations: Expansion of a local community; successful partner and environmental communities; artistic success at the location.
- conflicts/notes: The connection and success clauses do not establish dependence on a social milieu.

## Contradictions And Differences

The connection and success clauses do not establish dependence on a social milieu. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Apollon = Asc` describes connection with other people through art, science or family relations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supplies that direct relational meaning, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds expanding local association, successful partner and environmental communities, local artistic success and the partner's artistic knowledge. These are concrete forms of successful association rather than a claim about how a person defines the self.
