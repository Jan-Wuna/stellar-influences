## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 270 (printed 274).

#### Witte Entry

Scientific or artistic activity. Business management of a firm, an association or an alliance.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 214 (printed 430).

#### ABC Entry

Achievements in the arts, successful community work, creating common knowledge, joint work success, joint business activity, activity of a trade community, and scientific work community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Artistic, scientific and commercial work carried out in association.
- psychology: No distinct inner disposition is specified.
- body/health: No specific physical or medical claim; overwork is not stated.
- social/relationship: Management of a firm, association or alliance; successful communal work.
- events/manifestations: Creating common knowledge, artistic achievement, shared work success and trade activity.
- conflicts/notes: The entries describe productive activity without specifying friction or exhaustion.

## Contradictions And Differences

The entries describe productive activity without specifying friction or exhaustion. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Apollon = Mars` concerns the work through which an association operates and achieves results. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names artistic or scientific activity and business management of a firm, association or alliance. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful communal work, the creation of shared knowledge, joint business activity and scientific work association. This supports productive cooperation without supplying a separate claim about overwork or conflict.
