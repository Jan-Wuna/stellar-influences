## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 270 (printed 274).

#### Witte Entry

To belong to a great family or association. To have success as an artist or scientist.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 214 (printed 430).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Shared inner experiences; I am successful in art; my success in the community; my artistic knowledge; my experiences in the community; my freedom within the community; my trade community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The person's success and experience within a large family or association.
- psychology: Shared inner experiences and personal freedom within community.
- body/health: No specific physical or medical claim.
- social/relationship: Belonging to a great family or association.
- events/manifestations: Success as artist or scientist; personal artistic knowledge and trade association.
- conflicts/notes: Belonging and success do not by themselves establish public vocation or prestige dependence.

## Contradictions And Differences

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Apollon = MC` describes the person's belonging and success within a large family or association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly includes success as an artist or scientist; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds shared inner experiences, personal knowledge of art, experience and freedom within the community, and a trade community. The interpretation concerns participation and accomplishment rather than an inferred life destiny.
