## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Apollon = Mercury` concerns the exchanges that accompany broad association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names commercial talks, entertainment with many and discussion of art and science. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds shared language knowledge, reports of artistic success, youth association, news dissemination and an expanding transport community. These are distinct channels of contact and knowledge; the entries do not establish that talk displaces useful work.
