## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 270 (printed 274).

#### Witte Entry

Women or persons who show an interest in art or science. To be known to the public through arts or science. The public that is interested in art and science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 214 (printed 430).

#### ABC Entry

expansion of a community of the people, experiences of a community of the people, successful women's community, successful folk art, freedom of a community of the people, shared emotional knowledge, and hour of shared experience

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public interest and recognition in art or science, with communal experience.
- psychology: Shared emotional knowledge and experience.
- body/health: No specific physical or medical claim.
- social/relationship: An interested public and successful women's association; Witte's wording includes women or persons.
- events/manifestations: Public recognition, folk art, communal expansion and freedom.
- conflicts/notes: Public interest is explicit; chasing approval is not a stated motive.

## Contradictions And Differences

Public interest is explicit; chasing approval is not a stated motive. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Apollon = Moon` concerns a public that takes an interest in art or science and people becoming known through those fields. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes both the audience and recognition. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful women's community, folk art, collective freedom and shared emotional knowledge or experience. Its communal emphasis does not establish a motive of seeking approval, and Witte's historical reference to women does not limit all the meanings to women.
