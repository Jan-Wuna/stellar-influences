## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 270 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Dissolution of a union with many. Dissolution of a family association. Distrust among the members of he family. Not understood futurism (art).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 431; PDF page 214 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Success of a community through sensitivity, a successful community in water, shared experiences in the air, shared knowledge of the future, a community for knowledge of the future, great sensitivity of a community, an economic community involving water.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Collective sensitivity alongside possible dissolution or distrust in a large association.
- psychology: Community sensitivity and distrust among family members are distinct source expressions.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: A union with many, a family association, and communities sharing experience or knowledge.
- events/manifestations: Dissolution, misunderstood futurist art, and success through sensitivity are specific possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: Water, air, and future-knowledge language remains attributed; it supplies no physical mechanism or timing layer.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes dissolution and distrust; ABC explicitly includes community success through sensitivity. These different outcomes must remain side by side.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Cupido/Apollon through a community whose sensitivity and cohesion can take different courses. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes dissolution of a large union or family association, distrust within the family, and futurist art that is not understood. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also permits success through sensitivity, shared experience, and knowledge of the future, with air and water settings stated literally. This constructive range does not cancel dissolution or distrust, and the source does not make either outcome inevitable.
