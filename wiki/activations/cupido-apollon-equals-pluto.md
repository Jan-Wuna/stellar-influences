Source locator: PDF 270 (printed 274).

#### Witte Entry

To build-up an artistic or scientific work community from a small beginning.

Source locator: PDF 214 (printed 431).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Expansion of a shared development; successful development of a community; developing shared knowledge; unfolding artistic freedom; growing trade community; growing artistic success; growing economic community.

## Contradictions And Differences

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Apollon = Pluto` concerns the development of a community from small beginnings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) specifies building an artistic or scientific work community; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends this to successful shared development, developing knowledge, unfolding artistic freedom and growing trade or economic association. Growth and accomplishment connect the entries, without an additional claim that the association becomes centralized or controlling.
