## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 270 (printed 274).

#### Witte Entry

To be joined with others of the same faith. Artistic or scientific union by the same world outlook

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 214 (printed 431).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Expansion of a spiritual community; successful cultural community; successful artistic idea; spiritual knowledge of a community; shared spiritual experiences; knowledge of art and culture; increasing shared insights.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Association through shared faith, worldview and spiritual or cultural knowledge.

- body/health: No specific physical or medical claim; spiritual experience remains source language.
- social/relationship: Union with others of the same faith or world outlook.

## Contradictions And Differences

Shared worldview is explicit; exclusion or ideological coercion is not. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Apollon = Poseidon` concerns association through a common faith or world outlook. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) specifies artistic or scientific union on that basis; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds spiritual knowledge and experience, successful cultural community, artistic ideas and increasing shared insights. These are forms of common understanding, without a stated claim that agreement requires excluding other people.
