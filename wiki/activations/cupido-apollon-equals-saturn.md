## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 270 (printed 274).

#### Witte Entry

Success in arts or science through practice and efforts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 214 (printed 431).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Success through shared patience; seriousness of a scientific community; shared concentration on knowledge; shared experiences of pain; problematic artistic successes; losses of a trade community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Artistic or scientific success through practice, effort and shared patience, with losses also represented.
- psychology: Seriousness, concentration and shared experiences of pain.
- body/health: Shared pain experiences are named without specifying a physical condition or treatment.
- social/relationship: A serious scientific community and communal concentration on knowledge.
- events/manifestations: Problematic artistic success and losses in a trade community.
- conflicts/notes: Witte stresses practiced success; ABC also retains pain, difficulty and commercial loss.

## Contradictions And Differences

Witte stresses practiced success; ABC also retains pain, difficulty and commercial loss. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Apollon = Saturn` describes success in art or science achieved through practice and effort. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supplies that work-based success; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds shared patience, serious scientific association and concentration on knowledge. Shared pain, problematic artistic success and trade-community losses remain in the range too. The entries therefore describe effort with possible success and difficulty, without guaranteeing that an association lasts.
