## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 270 (printed 274).

#### Witte Entry

The man who is a member of a great family or a wide spread community. To be successful as an artist or a scientist, to become known and recognized as such. To be a member of a research society or group. Days devoted to art and science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 214 (printed 430).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Expansion of a community of people; successful men's community; commercial centre of a community; personal artistic successes; personal success in the community; scientific community centre.

- psychology: No distinct psychological disposition is specified.

- social/relationship: Witte's historical male example belongs to a great family, broad community or research society.

## Contradictions And Differences

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Apollon = Sun` concerns personal participation and recognition within a broad community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives a historical male example of membership in a great family or research group and becoming known as an artist or scientist. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds expanding association, a successful men's community, commercial and scientific centres and personal artistic success. The shared emphasis is recognized accomplishment in association, without establishing a need for group approval.
