## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Apollon = Uranus` concerns an association's sudden beginning and new accomplishments or knowledge. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names the surprising foundation of an artistic or scientific association. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds dynamic trade, successful technical community, new knowledge and scientific reform, while retaining tension within trade association. Innovation and tension remain distinct possibilities without a claim that novelty necessarily undermines the organization.
