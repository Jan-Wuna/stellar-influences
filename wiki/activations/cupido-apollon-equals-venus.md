## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 270 (printed 274).

#### Witte Entry

Harmonious union with many. Friendship and familiar harmony through art or science. Art of singing. Vocal art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 214 (printed 430).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Success through peace in the community; freedom and peace within a community; joy in shared success; increasing peace within the community; shared artistic knowledge; harmonious expansion of a community; preference for the scholarly study of art (Kunst-Wissenschaft).

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Harmonious broad association through art or science, with peace and shared success.
- psychology: Friendship and joy in shared accomplishment.
- body/health: No specific physical or medical claim.
- social/relationship: Harmonious union with many and familiar harmony through art or science.
- events/manifestations: Singing and vocal art; increasing communal peace; shared artistic knowledge and harmonious expansion.
- conflicts/notes: Harmony and peace are explicit; hidden lack of rigor or commitment is not.

## Contradictions And Differences

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Apollon = Venus` concerns harmonious union with many people through art or science. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects friendship and familiar harmony with singing and vocal art; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds peace, joy in shared success, shared artistic knowledge and harmonious communal expansion. The common theme is a successful and agreeable association, without an implied deficiency concealed by its pleasantness.
