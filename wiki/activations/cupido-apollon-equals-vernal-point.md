## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 270 (printed 274).

#### Witte Entry

General peaceful efforts. General scientific or artistic associations. To become known in wide circles through art or science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 214 (printed 430).

#### ABC Entry

expansion of a community in public, success of a community in public life, public trading community, public economic community, artistic enterprise in public, and artistic freedom in public life

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Apollon = Vernal Point` concerns association reaching public life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names general peaceful efforts, artistic or scientific associations and becoming widely known through those fields. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds public community expansion and success, public trade and economic association, artistic enterprise and artistic freedom. The interpretation stays with those public forms of participation and recognition.
