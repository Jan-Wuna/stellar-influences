## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Cupido = Admetos` describes separation from communal life through avoiding others, being avoided or the dissolution of an association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) extends solitude to lonely places, hermitage, monasticism and a monastery. These examples connect withdrawal and the end of being together without explaining why the withdrawal occurs.
