## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Cupido = Sun` describes the person as a member of family, association, community or group. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives historical male family and marriage roles, sociability, family meetings and club sessions, while also naming the artist, an art association and an object of art. Wedding and meeting days are listed manifestations rather than a timing procedure.
