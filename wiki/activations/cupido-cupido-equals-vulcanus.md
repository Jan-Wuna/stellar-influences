## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Cupido = Vulcanus` describes power and influence achieved in or through association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names collective power, a great organization, a political party and might through unity. Strong artistic ability, great art and the influence of an art form belong to the same range of communal and artistic influence.
