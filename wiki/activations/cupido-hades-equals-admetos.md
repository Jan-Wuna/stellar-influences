## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 267 (one-based; exact activator row).

#### Witte Entry

Grand public spirit under adverse conditions. To be avoided by others, proscribed. Raw material from used material. To stand together under sorrowful conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 211 (printed 425).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Experiences of a community from the past; consolidation of a community through deprivation; dangerous depressions within a community; shared deepening into the past; disadvantages within a community through restrictions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Solidarity, restriction and exclusion under adverse communal conditions.
- psychology: Dangerous communal depression is source wording without a clinical account.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or treatment is supplied.
- social/relationship: Standing together in sorrow or being avoided and proscribed.
- events/manifestations: Raw material from used material; consolidation through deprivation and deepening in a shared past.
- conflicts/notes: Solidarity and exclusion are alternatives; neither source says suffering is the only basis of belonging.

## Contradictions And Differences

Solidarity and exclusion are alternatives; neither source says suffering is the only basis of belonging. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Hades = Admetos` concerns association under adverse conditions, where public spirit and standing together can coexist with avoidance or proscription. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) also names raw material obtained from used material. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds experience from the past, consolidation through deprivation, restriction and communal depression. These clauses preserve both solidarity and exclusion without making hardship a necessary foundation of every bond.
