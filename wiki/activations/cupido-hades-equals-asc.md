## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 267 (one-based; exact activator row).

#### Witte Entry

Miserable marriage bond. To be unhappy in one’s own home. Disharmony through home conditions. The home life is not social. Storage room. Sickness in the family.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 211 (printed 424).

#### ABC Entry

old place of art, disadvantaged community in the environment, endangered local community, dangerous community in the environment, shared secret with the partner, criminal local community, and renunciation of a partner community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult home or partner conditions, alongside old art and shared secrets.
- psychology: Unhappiness at home and disharmony through domestic conditions.
- body/health: Witte names sickness in the family without specifying illness, diagnosis or treatment.
- social/relationship: A troubled marriage bond, endangered or dangerous local association and a shared partner secret.
- events/manifestations: Storage room, an old art location, local criminal community and partner-community renunciation.
- conflicts/notes: A storage room and old art location are separate concrete clauses; hardship does not establish criminality.

## Contradictions And Differences

A storage room and old art location are separate concrete clauses; hardship does not establish criminality. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Hades = Asc` concerns the conditions of home, partnership and local association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes domestic unhappiness, disharmony, a storage room and sickness in the family. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds an old art location, disadvantaged or endangered surroundings, a shared partner secret and criminal association as separate possibilities. Those clauses describe a varied range of difficult circumstances without identifying every troubled household as criminal.
