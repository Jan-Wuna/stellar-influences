## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 267, Jupiter row.

Decreasing substance. To be and feel happy in sad surroundings. Money through popular or vulgar art. Windfalls in miss-mated marriage.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 424 (one-based PDF p. 211), Jupiter row.

A community's success in danger, successful community service, joy in old art, money for a disadvantaged community, shared financial crime, a shared secret of success, and justice for a disadvantaged community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Happiness, benefit, or support within adverse shared conditions, with communal success also endangered.
- psychology: Feeling happy in sad surroundings and joy in old art.
- body/health: Decreasing substance is not explained as a medical condition.
- social/relationship: A mismatched marriage in Witte; service or justice for a disadvantaged community in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Money through popular or vulgar art, marital windfalls, communal funds, and shared financial crime.
- conflicts/notes: The historical marriage/art labels and financial-crime claim remain attributed; the entries do not say a windfall resolves an unhappy bond.

## Contradictions

Happiness amid sad surroundings can coexist with disadvantage. ABC adds both communal support and financial wrongdoing; no conditions select between those expressions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Hades = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through happiness or material benefit within difficult shared conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names happiness in sad surroundings, income from popular art, and windfalls in a mismatched marriage. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds service, money, and justice for a disadvantaged community, together with joy in old art. Endangered communal success and shared financial crime remain separate parts of that range, rather than a judgment that all benefit conceals an unhealthy bond.
