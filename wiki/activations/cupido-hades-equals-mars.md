## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 267, Mars row.

Quarrels and dissension in poor families or associations. To be forced to work for a poor firm or company.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 424 (one-based PDF p. 211), Mars row.

Activities of a disadvantaged community, lack of work in a community, dangerous work of a community, actions of a criminal community, secret actions of a community, and impulses from a shared past.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Communal activity under deprivation, conflict, or difficult working conditions.
- psychology: Impulses from a shared past are named; exhaustion and blame are not stated.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Quarrels in poor families or associations; shared disadvantaged, secret, or criminal activity in ABC's attributed wording.
- events/manifestations: Forced work for a poor company, lack of work, and dangerous communal work.
- conflicts/notes: Criminal and secret actions are separate source associations, not descriptions of every disadvantaged group.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in deprived work and communal difficulty. Their additional secret or historical expressions are not presented as consequences of every quarrel or shortage.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Hades = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through work and activity in disadvantaged shared conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names quarrels in poor families or associations and being forced to work for a poor firm. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds lack of work, dangerous communal work, and impulses from a shared past. Its separate secret or criminal actions remain attributed possibilities, while the recurring theme is difficult communal action and employment.
