## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 267, MC row.

Egoist. Individualist. To be part of a tragedy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 424 (one-based PDF p. 211), MC row.

Old art imbued with soul, an endangered soul within a community, I lack the community, my service within the community, my disadvantage through the community, a community of souls from the past, and emotional distress of a community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: One's own involvement with community under disadvantage or distress, alongside old art and an inner connection with the past.
- Psychology: Witte's attributed labels are egoist and individualist; ABC names an endangered soul, emotional distress, and the felt lack of community.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition or diagnosis is stated.
- Social/relationship: Service within a community, disadvantage through it, absence of community, and a community of souls from the past.
- Events/manifestations: Participation in a tragedy; old art imbued with soul.
- Conflicts/notes: The restored ABC wording says I lack the community, rather than I have the community. Historical moral labels do not establish a person's character or a cause of misfortune.

## Contradictions

Witte's individualist labels and ABC's service or lack of community give different aspects of personal involvement, without explaining their relation. Neither passage establishes grievance or self-protection as the motive; ABC also preserves an artistic and historical range beyond distress.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Hades = MC`, MC specifies the pair through one's own experience of community: serving it, lacking it, or being disadvantaged within it. The distress can be personal or shared, with Witte giving participation in a tragedy as a concrete example. His egoist and individualist labels remain attributed descriptions rather than an explanation of why the difficulty occurs. ABC also retains a quieter historical and artistic range—old art imbued with soul and a community of souls from the past—whose precise scope the entry leaves undefined.
