## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Hades = Mercury` concerns the realization of artistic ideas and communication about difficult communal circumstances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states a fight for artistic realization; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds reports on communities in need, conversations about old art or a shared past, opinions of an endangered community and news of dangerous communities. Its criminal youth association is a separate example, rather than a description of all youth or artistic effort.
