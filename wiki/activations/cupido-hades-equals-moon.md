## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Women's or collective experience of need, sorrow, old art and emotional lack.
- psychology: Public response to a sad event; emotional deficiency as source description.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or treatment is supplied; emotional lack is not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Historical examples of women who hoard; service to community, women in need and disadvantaged collective life.
- events/manifestations: Popular art, old art of a people and distinct criminal-association examples.
- conflicts/notes: The source's class and gender judgments stay attributed; neither poverty nor womanhood establishes criminality.

## Contradictions And Differences

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Hades = Moon` concerns women and collective experience under difficult or sorrowful conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names a woman who hoards, popular art and a public affected by a sad event, using a historical class judgment about women that remains attributed. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds old art, service to the community, women in need and emotional lack. Its criminal-community examples remain separate possibilities, without implying that disadvantaged women or communities are inherently criminal.
