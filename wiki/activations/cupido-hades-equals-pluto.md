## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 267 (one-based; exact activator row).

#### Witte Entry

Change in sad surroundings. Changes of bad social conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 211 (printed 425).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Development of past art; development from a shared past; dangerous shared development; deficient shared development; adverse changes within a community; a community's renunciation of change.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Change in difficult communal conditions and development from a shared past.
- psychology: No distinct inner disposition is specified.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or treatment is supplied.
- social/relationship: Communal change may be disadvantageous or renounced.
- events/manifestations: Development of past art, dangerous or deficient shared development and changes in sad surroundings.
- conflicts/notes: Change is explicit but its improvement, necessity or inevitability is not.

## Contradictions And Differences

Change is explicit but its improvement, necessity or inevitability is not. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Hades = Pluto` concerns changes in sad surroundings or difficult social conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states change without specifying that it improves the situation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds development of past art and from a shared past, but also dangerous or deficient shared development, adverse communal changes and renunciation of change. The range therefore includes both change and resistance to it, without a required progression toward relief.
