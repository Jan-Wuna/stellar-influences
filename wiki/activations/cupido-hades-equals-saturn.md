## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 267 (one-based; exact activator row).

#### Witte Entry

Family depressed through poverty. Grief and sorrow in or separation of a poor family.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source locator: PDF 211 (printed 425).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial English translation, checked against the German scan:

Shared renunciation in old age; disadvantages through separation from a community; concentration on a shared past; worry and difficulties within a community; problems through a dangerous community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Poverty, sorrow and separation in communal or family life.
- psychology: Grief, sorrow, worry and concentration on a shared past.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or treatment is supplied; depressed family is a source description.
- social/relationship: Separation from a poor family or community; shared renunciation in old age.
- events/manifestations: Disadvantages through separation and problems through a dangerous community.
- conflicts/notes: The sources name sorrow and difficulty without a rule of chronicity or permanent resignation.

## Contradictions And Differences

The sources name sorrow and difficulty without a rule of chronicity or permanent resignation. Comparison is limited to the exact entries above.

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Hades = Saturn` describes grief and sorrow in a family affected by poverty, including separation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supplies that family setting; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds shared renunciation in old age, disadvantages from leaving a community, concentration on a shared past and communal worries or difficulties. These are specific forms of hardship without a claim that sorrow becomes a permanent emotional condition.
