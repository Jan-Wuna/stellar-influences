## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden events or reform affect communal life marked by danger, shortage, or a shared past.
- Psychology: No distinct psychological state is specified beyond tension.
- Body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim appears in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Matrimonial scandal and dangerous communal tensions are named.
- Events/manifestations: Sudden street-artist offerings, unexpected shortage, reforms, or refusal of reform appear as separate examples.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC includes both reform and renunciation of reform; neither is an inevitable outcome.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in scale, from matrimony and street performance to communal reform. These are different manifestations, not a direct disagreement.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Hades = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the Cupido/Hades pair through sudden events and tension in communal life. [Witte](#witte-entry) names matrimonial scandal and sudden offerings by street artists; [ABC](#abc-entry) names communal danger, unexpected shortage, and events arising from a common past. ABC also allows either reforms within a traditional community or its renunciation of reforms, so the common thread is disruption or a contested change rather than one fixed outcome.
