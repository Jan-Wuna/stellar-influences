### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 424 (printed); PDF page: 211.

Common love secret, harmony deficiency in of a community, criminal arts community, criminal love community, love service for of a community, preference for old arts, and affection for a disadvantaged community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Affection, love, or artistic preference occurs within disadvantaged, secret, or troubled associations.
- Psychology: Compelled satisfaction with poor circumstances and affection for a disadvantaged community are distinct themes.
- Body/health: No specific medical claim appears; prostitution is a social example in Witte.
- Social/relationship: Unhappy love associations, shared love secrets, lack of harmony, and service to a community are named.
- Events/manifestations: Poor housing, preference for old art, and criminal artistic or love associations appear as separate examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Affection and service coexist with troubled examples; the entries do not equate all disadvantaged bonds with degraded love.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes hardship and constraint; ABC also includes sympathetic attachment and artistic preference. This widens the range rather than establishing a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Hades = Venus`, Venus specifies the Cupido/Hades pair through affection, harmony, and artistic preference within difficult or disadvantaged associations. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes unhappy love, poor housing, and being compelled to accept what is available. [ABC](#abc-entry) also includes shared secrets, lack of harmony, and criminal associations, but preserves another possibility: loving service or affection for a disadvantaged community and preference for old art. Hardship is therefore a setting for attachment, not proof that every attachment is degrading.
