### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 424 (printed); PDF page: 211.

Old art in public; disadvantaged communities in public life; communities in need in public; endangered communities in public life; dangerous communities in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Communal disadvantage, danger, or old art takes a public form.
- Psychology: No individual psychological state is specified.
- Body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim appears in these entries.
- Social/relationship: A poor populace and disadvantaged, endangered, or dangerous communities are named.
- Events/manifestations: Struggling stock companies and old art in public are concrete examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The public setting is shared, but old art, poverty, and danger remain different examples.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes economic hardship; ABC includes cultural and dangerous communal settings. The entries do not directly oppose one another.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Hades = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Cupido/Hades pair through its presence in public life. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes an indigent populace and stock companies struggling to survive; [ABC](#abc-entry) includes disadvantaged or endangered communities, dangerous communities, and old art in public. The formula thus has both social hardship and cultural expressions, without establishing that every public manifestation is an emergency.
