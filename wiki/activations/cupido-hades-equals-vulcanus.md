## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 267 (one-based PDF numbering).

Concerted attacks to overcome poverty. Great obstacles to a community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 425 (printed); PDF page: 211.

Community disadvantaged by violence, influence of old art, dangerous violent community, a community's renunciation of violence, criminal violence by a community, lack of power in a community, and past shared influence.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Communal hardship is expressed through power, force, or their limitation.
- Psychology: No distinct psychological state is specified.
- Body/health: Violence is named as a disadvantage; no specific injury or medical diagnosis is given.
- Social/relationship: Collective efforts against poverty coexist with dangerous or criminal communal violence and renunciation of violence.
- Events/manifestations: Great obstacles, lack of power, past shared influence, and the influence of old art are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Force and renunciation of force are alternative examples, not a single combative outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes collective effort and obstacles; ABC includes both violence and its renunciation. These alternatives should remain visible rather than become one claim of inevitable conflict.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Hades = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Cupido/Hades pair through the power available to a community facing hardship. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes concerted attempts to overcome poverty and great communal obstacles. [ABC](#abc-entry) ranges from lack of power and disadvantage through violence to a community's renunciation of violence; it also preserves the influence of old art and past shared influence. The entries concern the use, absence, or refusal of force, rather than requiring a permanently combative group.
