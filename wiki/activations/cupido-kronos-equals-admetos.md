## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

Clumsy behavior and hermit-like life. To be noted unfavorably through dull manners which cause disadvantages and losses. To exclude oneself from a social group. To be socially banned. To make oneself impossible.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 429; PDF p. 213, Admetos row.

German: "Ansehen durch Ausdauer einer Gemeinschaft, besondere Festigkeit in einer Gemeinschaft, große Einschränkungen in einer Gemeinschaft, gründliche Hierarchie-Gemeinschaft, selbständige Vertiefung in die Kunst, Widerstand einer Hierarchie-Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Standing through a community's perseverance; particular firmness within a community; major restrictions within a community; a thoroughgoing hierarchical community; independent deepening in art; resistance by a hierarchical community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Firmness and restriction within a community, including social exclusion and sustained engagement in art ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Witte describes clumsy or dull manners and withdrawal; ABC adds perseverance and deepening in art.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Exclusion, social banning, and resistance within a hierarchical community are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Disadvantages and losses through manners, a hermit-like life, communal restrictions, and sustained artistic engagement are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Perseverance and firmness may support standing in ABC; they are not equivalent to Witte's unfavorable reception.

## Contradictions

ABC includes standing gained through perseverance, whereas Witte describes social disadvantage and exclusion. These different outcomes must remain alternatives rather than a single uniformly negative judgment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Admetos`, Admetos specifies collective standing and hierarchy through firmness, restriction, and separation. The social examples include withdrawal, exclusion, and disadvantage associated with manners; the entry does not establish a wish to enter elite circles or a motive of self-sabotage ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

Perseverance provides another outcome within this exact formula: it can sustain a community's standing and accompany independent deepening in art. ABC's firmness, restrictions, and hierarchical resistance therefore belong alongside, rather than erase, that constructive possibility ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
