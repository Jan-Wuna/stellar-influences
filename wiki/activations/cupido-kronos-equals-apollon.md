## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

Great distinguished social gatherings. Extensive artistic or scientific associations. To be or to become known to many through great art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 429; PDF p. 213, Apollon row.

German: "Ansehen einer wissenschaftlichen Gemeinschaft, Ausdehnung der gemeinsamen Selbständigkeit, besondere Kunst-Erfolge, erfolgreiche staatliche Gemeinschaft, große Überlegenheit einer Gemeinschaft, großer gemeinsamer Erfolg, Lenkung einer Handels-Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Standing of a scientific community; expansion of shared independence; particular artistic successes; a successful state community; great superiority of a community; great shared success; direction of a commercial community.

The former policy/economic-union wording was a translation artifact.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Recognition, expansion, and success through organized social, artistic, scientific, or commercial association ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names shared independence and superiority; no account of individual motivation is given.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Distinguished gatherings and extensive artistic or scientific associations are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Becoming widely known through art, artistic successes, shared success, and directing a commercial community are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's superiority is retained as source language; neither entry supplies a caution about superficial prestige.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe recognition and successful association. Their different institutional examples do not establish a disagreement or independent corroboration of each particular claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Apollon`, Apollon specifies collective standing through wider association and recognized accomplishment. Distinguished gatherings, extensive artistic or scientific groups, and becoming known to many through art connect social reach with achievement ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

Shared independence and success extend this theme into scientific, state, and commercial communities. The source names superiority as well as success, but does not infer that recognition outpaces substance or cohesion ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
