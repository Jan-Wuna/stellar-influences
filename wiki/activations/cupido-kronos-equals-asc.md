## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

To meet with distinguished persons. To be connected with great artists. To take part in weddings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 428; PDF p. 213, Asc row.

German: "Ansehen einer Orts-Gemeinschaft, Dominanz einer Partner-Gemeinschaft, Leitung einer Orts-Gemeinschaft, Ort für gemeinsame Kunst, selbständige Partner-Gemeinschaft, staatliche Umwelt-Gemeinschaft, wichtige Orts-Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Standing of a local community; dominance of a partner community; leadership of a local community; a place for shared art; an independent partner community; a state community in one's surroundings; an important local community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Contact with distinguished people and the standing or leadership of local and partner communities ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No individual motive or emotional pattern is described; ABC names dominance and independence in a community.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Meetings with distinguished people or great artists, participation in weddings, and partner communities are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Weddings, local leadership, and a place for shared art are concrete examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The former cathedral, performance, and common-interest wording mistranslated dominance, leadership, and shared art.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes personal meetings and weddings; ABC emphasizes local and partner communities. These are different settings within the formula, with no direct opposing claim stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Asc`, Asc specifies collective standing through encounters and the communities in one's surroundings. Meeting distinguished people, being connected with great artists, and attending weddings give this formula a concrete social setting ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

The same emphasis on situated association extends to the standing and leadership of a local community, a place for shared art, and dominant or independent partner communities. These examples describe forms of contact and organization without establishing a personal need for status ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
