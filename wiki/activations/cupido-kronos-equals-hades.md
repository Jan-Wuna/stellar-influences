### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 429; PDF p. 213, Hades row.

special danger for of a community, special past of a community, shared service in the state, great artistic criminality, criminality in a state community, artistic authority of the past, and deficiency of a state community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Disadvantage, decline, or danger affecting a prominent community, with a separate emphasis on its past ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No individual attachment to prestige or motive is described.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named; impoverishment concerns society and institutions.
- Social/relationship: Impoverished noble society, state communities, and shared service in the state are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Corporate collapse, misfortune to a large concern, dormant art, and criminality are named.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC also retains artistic authority from the past; a past association is not automatically a claim of decay or crime.

## Contradictions

Witte concentrates on decline and institutional misfortune. ABC adds shared service and the past alongside danger and deficiency; those additions do not contradict the adverse examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Hades`, Hades specifies collective prominence through impoverishment, institutional misfortune, or danger. Noble society without resources, a collapsing corporation, and dormant art place the loss within social and artistic institutions ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

The exact formula also retains communities' past and artistic authority from the past, as well as shared service in the state. These meanings belong beside criminality and deficiency rather than being reduced to an unsupported judgment about clinging to prestige ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
