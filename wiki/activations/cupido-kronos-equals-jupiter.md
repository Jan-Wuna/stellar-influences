## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

Good leadership and good times concerning a large community. Marriage license. To enjoy the company of cultured people. The signing of a document. Financiers. National banks. Directory of the mint.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 428; PDF p. 213, Jupiter row.

reputation of a legal community, special success of a community, success of a state community, successful authority of a community, successful leadership of a community, and leadership of a financial community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Successful communal leadership and recognized legal or financial organization ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Enjoyment of cultured company is explicit; no general emotional or ethical character is established.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: A large community, cultured company, and legally recognized marriage are examples.
- Events/manifestations: A marriage license, signing a document, financiers, national banks, and leadership of financial or state communities are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Success and legal authorization do not establish fairness; the entries do not make that further judgment.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize successful leadership and institutional standing. ABC extends the communal examples without opposing Witte's specific legal and financial forms.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies collective standing through successful leadership and formal recognition. The marriage license and signed document make authorization concrete, while national banks and financiers place the same institutional emphasis in financial life ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

Successful authority, state communities, and leadership of a financial community reinforce that organizing theme. Enjoyment of cultured company remains a social expression of the formula; neither institutional success nor sociability establishes a general judgment about personal ethics ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
