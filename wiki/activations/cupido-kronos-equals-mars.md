Marriage ceremony. To officiate. Completion of an official activity. Official function. Action of great communities. Objects of art made of metals.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 428 (one-based PDF p. 213), Mars row.

Work of a hierarchical community, great artistic creation, directing work of a community, independent working community, independent artistic activity, state working community, and important communal work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Official or organized collective activity, alongside substantial artistic work.
- Psychology: No separate psychological description; ABC names independence in work and art.
- Body/health: No explicit health statement in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Officiating at a marriage and work within hierarchical, independent, or state communities.
- Events/manifestations: Marriage ceremony, official function or completed official activity, communal work, artistic creation, and metal artworks.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's full seven-clause work/art row replaces an unrelated policy-translation artifact. Neither entry gives enforcement or loss of human meaning as a required consequence.

## Contradictions

Witte's specific ceremonies, official functions, and metal artworks sit alongside ABC's broader organizational work and artistic independence. These are differences of scope, with no direct contradiction stated.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through work that gives collective organization a concrete function: officiating at a marriage, completing an official activity, or directing communal work. Hierarchical and state organizations appear alongside independent working groups and artistic activity. The artistic branch includes great creative work and, specifically in Witte, objects made of metal. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
