## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: printed p. 273 (one-based PDF p. 269), MC row.

Marriages. Great family celebration. To be present at family gatherings. One's own social position. Great artistic talent.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 428 (one-based PDF p. 213), MC row.

Special community of souls, the inner nature of a state community, I lead a community, my standing in the community, my artistic independence, and my independence within the community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: One's own position, leadership, and independence within a community, with artistic talent also represented.
- Psychology: ABC names a community of souls and the inner nature of a state community without further defining those phrases.
- Body/health: No explicit health statement in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Marriage, family celebration or gathering, standing within a community, and communal leadership.
- Events/manifestations: Marriages and family gatherings; artistic talent and independent artistic expression.
- Conflicts/notes: The corrected six-clause ABC row concerns community, leadership, and independence. It does not establish dependence of self-worth on public honor.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes marriage, family occasions, social position, and talent; ABC adds personal leadership and independence. These emphases are compatible, but neither entry explains the relation between independence and communal standing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = MC`, MC specifies the pair through one's own place in a community: social standing, leadership, and the independence one holds within it. Marriage and family gatherings make this communal position concrete, while artistic talent and artistic independence form another expression of it. The entries support participation and distinction within a group without specifying a conflict between belonging and independence. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
