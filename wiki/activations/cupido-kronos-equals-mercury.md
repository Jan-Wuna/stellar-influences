### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

To arrange a wedding. Artistic thinking. Joint conversations covering great ideas.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 428; PDF p. 213, Mercury row.

German: "Autorität der Medien-Gemeinschaft, besondere Gedanken einer Gemeinschaft, leitende Gemeinschaft im Gespräch, gemeinsame Sprache der Hierarchie, selbständige Nachrichten-Gemeinschaft, staatliche Verkehrs-Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Authority of a media community; special ideas of a community; a leading community in conversation; the hierarchy's shared language; an independent news community; a state transport community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Communal standing expressed through ideas, conversation, and organized communication ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Artistic thinking and special communal ideas are explicit.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Joint conversations and a hierarchy's shared language connect people within organized groups.
- Events/manifestations: Arranging a wedding, media/news communities and a state transport community are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries name communication and arrangement without assessing the depth of a bond.

## Contradictions

Witte describes wedding arrangements and artistic conversation; ABC extends communication into media, news and transport communities. No direct opposing claim is stated.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies communal standing through thought, conversation, and arrangements. Artistic thinking, shared discussion of great ideas, and arranging a wedding connect cultural exchange with the coordination of social life ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

Authority in a media community, a hierarchy's shared language, and independent news or state transport communities extend this organizing role into communication networks. The formula's evidence concerns these activities without judging whether they replace a lived bond ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
