## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

Civil Marriage (for the bride). Artist (female). Art of female artists, coming from the people. Popular art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 428; PDF p. 213, Moon row.

German: "Ansehen einer Volks-Gemeinschaft, besondere Kunst eines Volkes, leitende Frau einer Gemeinschaft, Leitung einer Volks-Gemeinschaft, Gefühl gemeinsamer Größe, Hierarchie einer Volks-Gemeinschaft, selbstbewußte Frauen-Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Standing of a people's community; a people's distinctive art; a woman leading a community; leadership of a people's community; a feeling of shared greatness; hierarchy in a people's community; a self-confident women's community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Communal standing and artistic recognition expressed through women and a people's collective life ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names a feeling of shared greatness and a self-confident women's community.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is stated; the gendered examples are historical source formulations.
- Social/relationship: A bride's civil marriage, women artists and a woman leading a community are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Popular art, distinctive art of a people, and communal leadership are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The historical association with women does not establish a universal gender role.

## Contradictions

The entries connect women, art and public community at different scales. Their gendered examples require historical attribution rather than a general claim about women.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Moon`, Moon specifies collective standing through women, public community, and art arising from a people. The bride's civil marriage and female artists are historically gendered examples; popular art connects their particular setting with a wider public ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

A woman leading a community, shared greatness, and a self-confident women's community extend the same theme into leadership and collective feeling. These are source-specific examples, not universal expectations of women's conduct ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
