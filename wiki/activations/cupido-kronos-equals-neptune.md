## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

Shifting of the social classes. Decline of distinguished families or of privileged classes. Dissolution of old culture, tradition or old habits.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 429; PDF p. 213, Neptune row.

German: "Auflösung einer selbständigen Gemeinschaft, Feingefühl einer Hierarchie-Gemeinschaft, Lenkung einer Gemeinschaft in der Luft, Führungs-Schwäche einer Gemeinschaft, schwache staatliche Gemeinschaft, Sensibilität einer Kunst-Autorität".

English translation: Dissolution of an independent community; sensitivity of a hierarchical community; direction of a community in the air; weakness in a community's leadership; a weak state community; sensitivity of an artistic authority.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Changes or dissolution in established communal order, with sensitivity also named ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC describes sensitivity in a hierarchy and an artistic authority.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named; weakness refers to leadership or a state community.
- Social/relationship: Distinguished families, privileged classes, independent communities and hierarchies are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Shifts in social classes and dissolution of old culture, traditions or habits are named.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's sensitivity and air phrase are not explained as causes of institutional decline.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes social decline and dissolution, while ABC also includes sensitivity. The broader ABC range should not be reduced to failure alone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies collective standing through shifts in established social order and the weakening or dissolution of communal structures. Decline of distinguished families or privileged classes and dissolution of old culture, tradition, or habits make this institutional and cultural change concrete ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

Sensitivity within a hierarchy or artistic authority remains another part of this exact formula. ABC also names an independent community's dissolution and weak leadership, but does not explain the phrase about directing a community in the air or make sensitivity a cause of decline ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
