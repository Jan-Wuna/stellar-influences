## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

To wish to establish a family. Legal witness of a marriage. Connection with great art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 428; PDF p. 213, Node row.

Specially linked together, contacts with national communities, meetings of an important community, contacts in a leading community, connection with a special community, and important links in of a community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Connections with recognized communities, family formation and art ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Witte names the wish to establish a family without a wider personality account.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Legal marriage witnesses and contacts with important, leading or state communities are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Meetings of an important community and connection with great art are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Formal recognition is one named setting, not a measure of a bond's quality.

## Contradictions

Witte gives family and artistic examples; ABC describes communal contacts more broadly. Neither entry directly opposes the other's claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Node`, Node specifies collective standing through connection and participation. The wish to establish a family, serving as a legal marriage witness, and connection with great art bring family and cultural association into the formula ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

Contacts with state, leading, important, or distinctive communities extend that pattern into organized networks and meetings. The source describes those connections without judging their emotional quality ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
