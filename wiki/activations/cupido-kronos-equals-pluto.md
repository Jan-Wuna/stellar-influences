## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

Changes in social forms. To develop one’s social forms, social manners. Establishment of new offices. Development of a great art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 429; PDF p. 213, Pluto row.

German: "Besondere Entfaltung einer Gemeinschaft, große Kunst-Entfaltung, Leitungs-Wechsel in einer Gemeinschaft, selbständige Kunst-Entwicklung, staatliche Entwicklungs-Gemeinschaft, wachsendes Ansehen einer Gemeinschaft, wichtige Synthese-Entwicklung".

English translation: Particular unfolding of a community; major artistic unfolding; a change of leadership in a community; independent artistic development; a state development community; a community's growing standing; important development of synthesis.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Development and change in communal standing, leadership, social forms and art ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Witte names development of social manners; no deeper motive is stated.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Changing social forms and communal leadership place development within organized association.
- Events/manifestations: New offices, great artistic development and growing communal standing are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Development of synthesis is an ABC phrase without a further definition here.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe development and institutional or artistic change. They do not distinguish superficial restructuring from genuine renewal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies collective standing through development and changed forms. Evolving social manners, establishment of new offices, and development of a great art connect social organization with artistic growth ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

A change of communal leadership and growing standing give the same theme an institutional expression. ABC also retains independent artistic development and development of synthesis, without imposing a further test of authentic renewal ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
