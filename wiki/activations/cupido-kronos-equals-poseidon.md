## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

State protected spiritual community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 429; PDF p. 213, Poseidon row.

German: "Ansehen einer Kultur-Gemeinschaft, Autorität einer geistigen Synthese, besondere Klarheit in einer Gemeinschaft, große Erkenntnisse einer Gemeinschaft, große geistige Gemeinschaft, Vorrang in Kunst und Kultur, wichtige Synthese der Wahrheit".

English translation: Standing of a cultural community; authority of an intellectual or spiritual synthesis; particular clarity in a community; great insights of a community; a great intellectual or spiritual community; precedence in art and culture; important synthesis of truth.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Recognized spiritual or cultural association, including state protection, clarity and synthesis ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names communal clarity and insights without describing individual motives.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: A state-protected spiritual community and a prominent cultural or intellectual community are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Precedence in art and culture and synthesis of truth are named expressions.
- Conflicts/notes: State protection does not itself establish ideological coercion or official doctrine.

## Contradictions

Witte provides the compact example of state protection; ABC describes cultural standing, insight and synthesis. The entries do not state a conflict between protection and truth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies collective standing through spiritual and cultural association. A spiritual community protected by the state gives a concrete institutional form to that recognition ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

Cultural standing, communal clarity and insights, and authority in intellectual or spiritual synthesis broaden the account. Precedence in art and culture belongs to the same range; neither entry makes the protected community an example of ideological coercion ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
