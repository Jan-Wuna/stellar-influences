## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

To have to obey. Deprived of freedom. Rural, country like surroundings. Artistic gardening. Forestry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 429; PDF p. 213, Saturn row.

German: "Aufgaben einer hierarchischen Gemeinschaft, besondere Gemeinschaft im Alter, gemeinsame Konzentration auf den Staat, Probleme einer leitenden Gemeinschaft, gestörte selbständige Gemeinschaft, Verluste einer staatlichen Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Tasks of a hierarchical community; a distinctive community in old age; shared concentration on the state; problems in a leading community; a disturbed independent community; losses of a state community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Constraint and tasks within communal order, alongside rural and cultivated surroundings ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Obedience and shared concentration are named.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is stated; old age is a communal setting in ABC.
- Social/relationship: A hierarchy, a leading community and an independent community under disturbance are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Loss of freedom, state-community losses, artistic gardening and forestry are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Rural surroundings, gardening and forestry remain distinct from the adverse institutional examples.

## Contradictions

Witte's rural and artistic-gardening examples broaden the range beyond institutional constraint; ABC's communal tasks and problems do not explain those examples away.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies communal order through obligations, obedience, and restricted freedom. ABC adds tasks within hierarchy, problems in leadership, and losses or disturbance affecting state and independent communities ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).

The formula also includes a community in old age, rural surroundings, artistic gardening, and forestry. These retained settings give it a wider range than a general judgment about the burden of status ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
