## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

To proceed to the license bureau (registry office). Reunion with the family. To rejoin a former connection or union. Professional artist.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 428; PDF p. 213, Sun row.

German: "Ansehen einer Personen-Gemeinschaft, besonderer Tag für eine Gemeinschaft, leitende Personen einer Gemeinschaft, männliche Dominanz in einer Gemeinschaft, selbständiges Kunst-Zentrum, staatliches Gemeinschafts-Zentrum".

English translation: Standing of a community of people; a special day for a community; leading people in a community; male dominance within a community; an independent art centre; a state community centre.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Communal standing expressed through people, recognized roles, reunion and centres of association ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No account of personal identity or need for acknowledgment is supplied.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is stated; male dominance is a historical source formulation.
- Social/relationship: Family reunion, rejoining a former union, and leading people in a community are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: A registry-office visit, professional artistic work, an art centre and a state community centre are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's male dominance is retained as an attributed example rather than a universal social rule.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes reunion, registration and professional art; ABC adds community leadership and centres. These settings give different forms to communal standing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Sun`, Sun specifies communal standing through people and their recognized participation. Reuniting with family or a former union and proceeding to a registry office connect belonging with concrete social acts; the professional artist supplies a cultural expression ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

Leading people, a special communal day, and art or state community centres extend that participation into organized collective life. ABC also names male dominance, which remains a historical source example rather than a general requirement of leadership ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
