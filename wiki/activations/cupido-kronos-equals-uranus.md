## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

To be married. To find oneself suddenly in distinguished society. Great musicians and technicians. Sudden artistic product. An event of allied nations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 429; PDF p. 213, Uranus row.

German: "Autorität einer technischen Gemeinschaft, besondere Rhythmus-Kunst, Leitung einer rhythmischen Gemeinschaft, neues Ansehen einer Gemeinschaft, staatliche Reform-Gemeinschaft, Unruhe in einer Führungs-Gemeinschaft, wichtige Reformen für die Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Authority of a technical community; distinctive rhythmic art; leadership of a rhythmic community; new standing of a community; a state reform community; unrest in a leadership community; important reforms for the community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden social or artistic developments and reform within recognized communities ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names unrest in a leadership community.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Marriage, sudden contact with distinguished society and allied nations are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Great musicians and technicians, a sudden artistic product, technical authority and communal reforms are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Suddenness is explicit in Witte; failure to integrate prestige is not stated.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sudden events and artistic or technical accomplishment; ABC adds reform and leadership unrest. The examples include both accomplishment and disturbance.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies communal standing through sudden encounters and developments. Marriage, unexpectedly finding oneself in distinguished society, a sudden artistic product, and an event of allied nations place abrupt change within social and institutional settings ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

Musicians, technicians, technical authority and rhythmic art connect this range with artistic and technical accomplishment. New communal standing and reforms add organizational change, while unrest within leadership retains its possible disturbance ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
