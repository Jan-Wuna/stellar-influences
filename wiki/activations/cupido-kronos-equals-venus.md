## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

A great sense for beauty. To harmonize and agree with great artists or leading people. Pleasant social intercourse in distinguished circles. Legally permitted love. Great artistic talent.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 428; PDF p. 213, Venus row.

German: "Besondere Schönheit der Kunst, Dominanz der Freude in einer Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame künstlerische Leitung, gemeinsame Vorliebe für die Kunst, Harmonie in einer staatlichen Gemeinschaft, Kunst-Autorität für eine Gemeinschaft, wichtige Friedens-Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Particular beauty of art; predominance of joy in a community; shared artistic direction; a shared preference for art; harmony in a state community; artistic authority for a community; an important peace community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Beauty, artistic affinity and harmony within recognized communal life ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: A sense of beauty, joy and shared artistic preference are explicit.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Agreement with artists or leading people, pleasant company and legally permitted love are named.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic talent, shared artistic direction, state-community harmony and a peace community are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Love's legal permission is a source expression; neither entry infers dependence on rank.

## Contradictions

Witte's personal artistic affinity and sociability overlap with ABC's communal harmony and artistic direction. The peace-community example adds a collective setting.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Venus`, Venus specifies communal standing through beauty, artistic agreement, and pleasant association. Great artistic talent and a sense of beauty connect with harmony among artists or leading people and with love described as legally permitted ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

Shared artistic direction, preference for art, and joy give this affinity a collective form. Harmony within a state community and an important peace community extend the same theme into organized public association ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
