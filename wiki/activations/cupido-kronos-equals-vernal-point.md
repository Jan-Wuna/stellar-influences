## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public expression of communal standing, leadership and artistic ability ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No account of inward experience is supplied.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Distinctive and state communities appear in public life.
- Events/manifestations: Demonstrating abilities, artistic activity, shared public leadership and independent art are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's demonstrated ability and ABC's public communal standing supply different scales of visibility.

## Contradictions

Witte concentrates on showing ability and artistic activity; ABC places communities and leadership in public life. No opposing claim is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies communal standing through public expression. Demonstrating or exhibiting one's abilities and artistic activity make that visibility concrete ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

ABC extends the public setting to shared leadership, communal reputation, independent art, and a state community. Their common thread is the visible presence of communal or artistic standing ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
