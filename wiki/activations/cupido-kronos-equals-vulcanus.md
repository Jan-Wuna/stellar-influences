## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

Might and influence through personal appearance or the distinguished social background to which one belongs. To be very dominant or domineering in art or society. Great state enterprises.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 429; PDF p. 213, Vulcanus row.

German: "Ansehen einer einflußreichen Gemeinschaft, besondere Macht einer Gemeinschaft, Einfluß einer wichtigen Gemeinschaft, einflußreiche Kunst-Autorität, hierarchische Macht-Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame staatliche Macht, Gewalt einer großen Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Standing of an influential community; particular power of a community; influence of an important community; influential artistic authority; a hierarchical power community; shared state power; the force or violence of a large community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Power and influence exercised through social standing, art and state organization ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Dominant or domineering conduct is explicit in Witte.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Distinguished social background and influential or hierarchical communities are named.
- Events/manifestations: Great state enterprises, shared state power and influential artistic authority are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: German Gewalt retains a range of force or violence; no specific justification for its use is stated.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe power and influence. Witte's domineering conduct and ABC's Gewalt retain the more coercive end without reducing every institutional example to it.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies communal standing through power and influence. Distinguished social background and personal appearance can carry social force, while dominant or domineering conduct and great state enterprises express its personal and institutional range ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

Influential artistic authority, hierarchical power, and shared state power extend that range across cultural and political association. ABC's final Gewalt retains force or violence as a source meaning; the passage supplies no further account of how that force is justified ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
