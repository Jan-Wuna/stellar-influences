## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 273; PDF p. 269.

Major accomplishments through cooperation. Orders and decorations from the authority. The art of mechanical engineering. Brilliant successes of the army. The military union of several nations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 429; PDF p. 213, Zeus row.

German: "Ansehen einer selbständigen Gemeinschaft, besondere Anstrengungen einer Gemeinschaft, Führungs-Autorität einer Gemeinschaft, selbständige Leistungs-Gemeinschaft, staatliche Führungs-Gemeinschaft, wichtige Zielsetzung einer Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Standing of an independent community; particular efforts of a community; a community's leadership authority; an independent achievement community; a state leadership community; important goal-setting by a community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Recognized collective achievement through cooperation, leadership and shared goals ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names communal effort and goal-setting without an account of individual motives.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Cooperation, military union of several nations and independent or state leadership communities are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Official decorations, mechanical engineering and army successes are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Military success is a historical source example; no judgment of a goal's ethical worth is supplied.

## Contradictions

Witte supplies concrete artistic, engineering and military achievements; ABC describes effort, independence and leadership more generally. These are differences of scope.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Kronos = Zeus`, Zeus specifies communal standing through coordinated accomplishment. Cooperation, mechanical engineering, official decorations, and the successes or union of military forces show achievement within organized collective action ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

Communal effort, leadership authority, independence, and important goal-setting give that action its shared direction. These source examples retain their particular institutional and military settings ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
