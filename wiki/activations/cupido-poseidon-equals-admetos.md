## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

Knowledge through the faith. To quit a spiritual faith. To stay outside of the faith. No inclinations to join, disadvantages and oppression through it.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 437; PDF p. 217, Admetos row.

German: "Eingeschränkte Kultur-Gemeinschaft, Festigung einer geistigen Gemeinschaft, geistig blockierte Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Vertiefung in die Wahrheit, gründliche Ideen einer Gemeinschaft, unterdrückte geistige Gemeinschaft, Vertiefung in Kunst und Kultur".

English translation: A restricted cultural community; consolidation of an intellectual or spiritual community; an intellectually or spiritually blocked community; shared deepening in truth; thorough ideas of a community; an oppressed intellectual or spiritual community; deepening in art and culture.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Restriction or consolidation in shared faith, ideas and culture, alongside deepening knowledge ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Lack of inclination to join a faith is explicit in Witte.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Leaving or staying outside a faith and an oppressed community are named.
- Events/manifestations: Knowledge through faith, communal consolidation and deeper artistic/cultural engagement are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: Depth and consolidation coexist with restriction and oppression; no purity motive is stated.

## Contradictions

The entries include knowledge and deepening as well as withdrawal, blocking and oppression. Those branches cannot be reduced to estrangement alone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies shared outlook through restriction, separation or sustained depth. Witte names knowledge through faith alongside leaving or staying outside a faith, lack of inclination to join, disadvantage and oppression ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds consolidation, thorough ideas and shared deepening in truth, art and culture, while also retaining restricted, blocked or oppressed communities. These source meanings preserve the difference between deepening a shared outlook and being constrained by a community ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
