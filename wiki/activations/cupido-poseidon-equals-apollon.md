## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

The spreading of a spiritual cult, the joining up of people of the same faith.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 437; PDF p. 217, Apollon row.

Expansion of arts and culture, successful intellectual community, successful trade community, shared cultural experiences, community of intellectual science, science of arts and culture, and increasing knowledge in the community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Expansion and success of associations sharing faith, ideas or culture ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No individual motive or emotional style is stated.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: People of the same faith joining and successful intellectual or commercial communities are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Spread of a spiritual cult, expansion of art/culture and increasing communal knowledge are named.
- Conflicts/notes: The source's cult wording is retained; expansion does not itself evaluate the truth of the shared outlook.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes spread and shared faith; ABC adds cultural, scientific and commercial success. The entries provide different settings for expansion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Apollon`, Apollon specifies shared outlook through expansion and wider association. Witte names the spread of a spiritual cult and people of the same faith joining together ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC connects expansion with art and culture, successful intellectual and commercial communities, shared cultural experience and increasing knowledge. The formula therefore includes the widening of both belief-based and cultural or intellectual association ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
