## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

In the company of people who represent a faith, a knowledge or a worldview.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 436; PDF p. 217, Asc row.

German: "Andere geistige Gemeinschaften, Erkenntnisse einer Umwelt-Gemeinschaft, geistige Partner-Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Umwelt-Ideen, Klarheit einer Partner-Gemeinschaft, Kultur-Gemeinschaft am Ort, Partner für Kunst und Kultur, vernünftige Orts-Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Other intellectual or spiritual communities; insights of a community in one's surroundings; an intellectual or spiritual partner community; shared ideas concerning the surroundings; clarity within a partner community; a cultural community at the locality; partners for art and culture; a reasonable local community.

The preceding MC row ends "meine Klarheit in der Kunst". Asc begins "Andere geistige Gemeinschaften" and ends "vernünftige Orts-Gemeinschaft".

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Encounters and partnership within shared intellectual, spiritual and cultural life ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names shared ideas, insight, clarity and reason within communities.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Company of people representing a faith, knowledge or worldview and partners for art/culture are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Local cultural community and insight or clarity in partner communities are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The former ABC opening belonged to MC; the Asc row begins Andere geistige Gemeinschaften and has eight clauses.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes companionship; ABC develops local and partner communities. Neither entry describes an echo chamber or intellectual closure.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Asc`, Asc specifies shared outlook through the people and communities one encounters. Being in the company of people representing a faith, knowledge or worldview gives the formula a concrete social setting ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC extends that setting to local cultural communities, intellectual or spiritual partners, shared ideas and communal clarity, with partners for art and culture among its examples ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
