## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

Deterioration of sects. Associations which represent a certain philosophy of life or an ideology, on the stupidity and ignorance of their members. Spreading of erroneous beliefs or superstition.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 437; PDF p. 217, Hades row.

German: "Gemeinsame Klarheit aus der Vergangenheit, gemeinsamer Verzicht einer Gemeinschaft, geistig gefährdete Gemeinschaft, Ideen-Mangel einer Gemeinschaft, Kriminalität einer geistigen Gemeinschaft, Kultur einer Traditions-Gemeinschaft, Kunst und Kultur der Vergangenheit".

English translation: Shared clarity from the past; shared renunciation by a community; an intellectually or spiritually endangered community; a community's lack of ideas; criminality of an intellectual or spiritual community; the culture of a traditional community; art and culture of the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Deterioration or danger within shared belief and ideas, alongside past clarity and culture ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Witte's stupidity/ignorance language is an attributed judgment about members.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Endangered or criminal intellectual communities and a traditional cultural community are named.
- Events/manifestations: Spread of erroneous beliefs or superstition, lack of ideas, shared renunciation and past art/culture are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's phrase connecting associations to members' ignorance is grammatically incomplete in the scan and is retained without reconstruction.

## Contradictions

Witte concentrates on deterioration and erroneous belief; ABC also retains shared past clarity and culture. Past material is not automatically treated as degraded.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Hades`, Hades specifies shared outlook through deterioration, intellectual danger or lack of ideas. Witte describes deteriorating sects and spread of erroneous beliefs or superstition, using evaluative language for their members ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC also retains shared clarity from the past and the art and culture of traditional or past communities. These historical meanings remain alongside endangered or criminal communities, rather than being turned into a general judgment that older culture is backward ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
