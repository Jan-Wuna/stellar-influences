## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

To feel happy in a spiritual, cultural or philosophical community. Luck and success with or through such associations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 436; PDF p. 217, Jupiter row.

German: "Erkenntnisse einer Finanz-Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Finanz-Kultur, gemeinsame Freude an der Wahrheit, gemeinsame Rechts-Klarheit, gemeinsames Kultur-Recht, geistige Erfolge einer Gemeinschaft, Kultur-Erfolge einer Gemeinschaft, Rechts-Kultur einer Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Insights of a financial community; shared financial culture; shared joy in truth; shared legal clarity; shared cultural law; intellectual or spiritual successes of a community; cultural successes of a community; the legal culture of a community.

The preceding Mars row ends "vernünftige Gemeinschafts-Handlungen". Jupiter begins "Erkenntnisse einer Finanz-Gemeinschaft" and ends "Rechts-Kultur einer Gemeinschaft".

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Happiness and success through shared cultural, spiritual or philosophical association ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Feeling happy in a community and shared joy in truth are explicit.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Financial, legal and cultural communities are named forms of association.
- Events/manifestations: Communal intellectual/cultural successes, financial insights and shared legal clarity are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Jupiter begins Erkenntnisse einer Finanz-Gemeinschaft; the preceding cultural-activity clauses belong to Mars.

## Contradictions

Witte gives the general happiness and success of association; ABC provides financial, legal and cultural expressions. No direct opposing claim is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies shared outlook through happiness, good fortune and success within a community ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC gives the shared life of thought a financial and legal range: insights within a financial community, shared financial culture, legal clarity and legal culture appear beside joy in truth and intellectual or cultural successes. These concrete settings connect communal understanding with beneficial participation ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
