### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To accept the leadership and management of a cultural organization. State-run spiritual communities. The dominant philosophical politics of the state.

#### ABC Entry

Authority of a spiritual community, special ideas of a community, hierarchy for arts and culture, self conscious knowledge community, state cultural community, superiority through common clarity, and a state community
