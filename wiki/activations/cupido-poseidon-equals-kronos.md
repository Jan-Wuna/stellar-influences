## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

To accept the leadership and management of a cultural organization. State-run spiritual communities. The dominant philosophical politics of the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 437; PDF p. 217, Kronos row.

German: "Autorität einer geistigen Gemeinschaft, besondere Ideen einer Gemeinschaft, Hierarchie für Kunst und Kultur, selbstbewußte Erkenntnis-Gemeinschaft, staatliche Kultur-Gemeinschaft, Überlegenheit durch gemeinsame Klarheit, Vernunft einer staatlichen Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Authority of an intellectual or spiritual community; particular ideas of a community; hierarchy for art and culture; a self-confident community of insight; a state cultural community; superiority through shared clarity; reason within a state community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Leadership and authority within communities of ideas, culture and shared outlook ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names self-confidence, shared clarity and reason.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Cultural management, state-run spiritual communities and a hierarchy for art/culture are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Accepting organizational leadership and the state's dominant philosophical politics are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Official organization is stated without a further claim of coercion or sanctification.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes management and state organization; ABC adds communal insight, clarity and reason. No direct opposing claim is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Kronos`, Kronos specifies shared outlook through leadership and formal organization. Managing a cultural organization, state-run spiritual communities and the state's dominant philosophical politics give the formula concrete institutional settings ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds authority and hierarchy for art and culture, a self-confident community of insight, shared clarity and reason within the state community ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
