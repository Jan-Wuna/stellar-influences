## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

To fight for or against a faith or worldview.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 436; PDF p. 217, Mars row.

German: "Arbeit einer Kultur-Gemeinschaft, Erkenntnis-Handlungen einer Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Kultur-Tätigkeit, geistige Synthese herstellen, Klarheit gemeinsam erarbeiten, Kunst- und Kultur-Tätigkeit, vernünftige Gemeinschafts-Handlungen".

English translation: Work of a cultural community; a community's acts of insight; shared cultural activity; producing an intellectual or spiritual synthesis; working together to achieve clarity; artistic and cultural activity; reasonable communal actions.

Mars begins "Arbeit einer Kultur-Gemeinschaft" after the Venus harmony row and ends "vernünftige Gemeinschafts-Handlungen" before Jupiter.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Action on shared outlook, through cultural work or conflict over faith ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names communal work toward clarity and reasonable action.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Fighting for or against a worldview and cooperative cultural work are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Producing synthesis and artistic/cultural activity are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's fight phrase does not require physical violence; ABC retains cooperative work.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes conflict for or against a worldview; ABC emphasizes cultural work and shared clarity. Both forms of activity remain present.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Mars`, Mars specifies shared outlook through action. Witte's fighting for or against a faith or worldview supplies a conflict-oriented expression ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC gives a cooperative range as well: cultural work, producing intellectual or spiritual synthesis, working together toward clarity, and reasonable communal actions. The exact formula therefore retains both contest over outlook and practical cultural collaboration ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
