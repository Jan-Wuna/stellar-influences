## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

To occupy one’s mind mentally during the hours of relaxation. Women or people who represent a worldview.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 436; PDF p. 217, Moon row.

German: "Frau in geistiger Gemeinschaft, Gefühl für Kunst und Kultur, gemeinsames Gefühl für Wahrheit, Gemeinschafts-Geist eines Volkes, Kultur-Gemeinschaft eines Volkes, Kunst eines Kultur-Volkes, Stunde gemeinsamer Klarheit, vernünftige Frauen in einer Gemeinschaft".

English translation: A woman in an intellectual or spiritual community; feeling for art and culture; shared feeling for truth; a people's communal spirit; a people's cultural community; the art of a cultured people; an hour of shared clarity; reasonable women in a community.

Moon begins "Frau in geistiger Gemeinschaft" after Sun's vergeistigte Person clause and ends "vernünftige Frauen in einer Gemeinschaft" before Node.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared outlook expressed through feeling, a people's cultural life and mental activity in leisure ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Feeling for art/culture and truth is explicit.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named; women are historical source examples.
- Social/relationship: Women or people representing a worldview and a people's cultural community are named.
- Events/manifestations: Mental occupation during relaxation, communal art and an hour of shared clarity are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The source does not establish passive absorption of doctrine or a nurturing role.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes leisure and people representing a worldview; ABC adds shared feeling and collective culture. The gendered examples remain attributed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Moon`, Moon specifies shared outlook through feeling and the people who carry a worldview. Witte connects mental occupation with hours of relaxation and names women or people representing an outlook ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC extends the range to feeling for art, culture and truth, a people's communal spirit and culture, and shared clarity. Its references to women remain historical examples within the same communal field ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
