## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Dissolution or false doctrine in a group, alongside subtle clarity and communities concerned with water, air and future culture ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names clarity through subtle feeling.
- Body/health: Clear air and water culture are source subjects without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Spiritual, cultural, philosophical and metaphysical communities are named.
- Events/manifestations: Group dissolution/decay, noticeable false doctrine, insights about water and future art/culture are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's sensitivity and future subjects must remain distinct from Witte's adverse examples.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes dissolution and false doctrine; ABC includes subtle clarity and water, air or future culture. These are materially different ranges, not a uniform account of confusion.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies shared outlook through a range that includes both group dissolution and subtle clarity. Witte describes decay in spiritual, cultural or philosophical groups and false doctrine becoming noticeable ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC retains clarity through subtle feeling, insights within metaphysical communities, and communal concerns with water, clear air and future culture. These meanings broaden the exact formula beyond a single story of deception ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
