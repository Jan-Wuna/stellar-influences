## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

To deal with matters of faith. In connection with spiritual, educational, cultural or philosophical groups.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 436; PDF p. 217, Node row.

German: "Beziehungen zu einer geistigen Gemeinschaft, durch gemeinsame Ideen verbunden, gemeinsame Kultur-Verbindungen, klare Beziehungen zur Gemeinschaft, klare gemeinsame Verbindung, Verbindung durch gemeinsame Wahrheit, Verbindung zu Kunst und Kultur".

English translation: Relationships with an intellectual or spiritual community; connected through shared ideas; shared cultural connections; clear relations with the community; a clear shared connection; connection through shared truth; connection with art and culture.

Node begins "Beziehungen zu einer geistigen Gemeinschaft" and ends "Verbindung zu Kunst und Kultur"; prior Moon and following Mercury tails are excluded.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Connections formed through faith, ideas, shared truth and culture ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Shared ideas are named without an individual personality profile.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Relations with spiritual, educational, cultural or philosophical groups are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural ties and connection through shared truth are named expressions.
- Conflicts/notes: The final truth/art/culture clauses were missing from the cached translation.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe connection through faith or ideas; ABC develops the cultural and shared-truth expressions without an opposing claim.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Node`, Node specifies shared outlook through connection. Dealing with faith and being connected with spiritual, educational, cultural or philosophical groups provide Witte's broad forms ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC makes the ties explicit as shared ideas, cultural connections, clear relations with the community and connection through shared truth, art and culture ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
