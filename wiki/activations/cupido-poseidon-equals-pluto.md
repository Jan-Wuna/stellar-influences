## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

Institution, development and transformation of spiritual, cultural or a philosophical community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 437; PDF p. 217, Pluto row.

German: "Entwicklung einer spirituellen Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Kultur-Evolution, gemeinsame geistige Entwicklung, spirituelle Kunst-Entwicklung, Vernunft-Entwicklung in der Gemeinschaft, wachsende Vernunft in der Gemeinschaft, Wandlung von Kunst und Kultur".

English translation: Development of a spiritual community; shared cultural evolution; shared intellectual or spiritual development; spiritual artistic development; development of reason in the community; growing reason in the community; transformation of art and culture.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Founding, development and transformation of shared cultural or spiritual life ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names development and growth of reason within a community.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Spiritual, cultural and philosophical communities are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Institution-building, cultural evolution and spiritual artistic development are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Development is stated without a doctrine-survival purpose or a test of genuine renewal.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize institutional or cultural development; ABC adds the growth of communal reason. No direct opposing claim is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies shared outlook through establishment, development and transformation. Witte applies this to spiritual, cultural or philosophical communities ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC extends the process into shared cultural evolution, intellectual or spiritual development, spiritual art and growing reason within the community. Transformation therefore concerns the shared life of thought and culture as well as its institutional form ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
