## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

The man who has faith or is a deep thinker. To be a member of a spiritual, educational, cultural or philosophical community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 436; PDF p. 217, Sun row.

German: "Erkenntnisse einer Personen-Gemeinschaft, geistiges Gemeinschafts-Zentrum, Gemeinschaft für Körper-Kultur, Klarheit für die menschliche Gemeinschaft, Kunst- und Kultur-Zentrum, persönliche Ideen für die Gemeinschaft, vergeistigte Person einer Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Insights of a community of people; an intellectual or spiritual community centre; a community for physical culture; clarity for the human community; an art and culture centre; personal ideas for the community; an intellectually or spiritually oriented person within a community.

Sun begins "Erkenntnisse einer Personen-Gemeinschaft" and ends "vergeistigte Person einer Gemeinschaft"; preceding Asc local-community clauses are excluded.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal membership and centres of shared intellectual, spiritual or cultural life ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Faith, deep thought and personal ideas for the community are explicit.
- Body/health: Physical culture is a communal subject without a medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Membership in spiritual, educational, cultural or philosophical community is named.
- Events/manifestations: An intellectual community centre and art/culture centre are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The Sun row begins insights of a community of people; the preceding local-community clauses belong to Asc.

## Contradictions

Witte gives personal faith and membership; ABC adds community centres, physical culture and ideas. These are different concrete expressions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Sun`, Sun specifies shared outlook through a person and their membership in a community. Witte describes a person with faith or deep thought who belongs to spiritual, educational, cultural or philosophical association ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds personal ideas and communal insight or clarity, alongside intellectual, art/culture and physical-culture centres or communities. These examples connect the individual participant with organized cultural life ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
