### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 437; PDF p. 217, Uranus row.

German: "Aufregende Ideen einer Gemeinschaft, dynamische geistige Gemeinschaft, geistige Reform-Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Unruhe wegen Wahrheit, Impulse einer geistigen Gemeinschaft, neue Kunst und Kultur, Vernunft einer technischen Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Exciting ideas of a community; a dynamic intellectual or spiritual community; an intellectual or spiritual reform community; shared unrest over truth; impulses of an intellectual or spiritual community; new art and culture; reason within a technical community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Excitement, argument, reform and new cultural expression within shared outlook ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Exciting ideas and shared unrest over truth are explicit.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Arguments over faith/worldview, a reform community and a technical community are named.
- Events/manifestations: New art/culture and intellectual or spiritual communal impulses are examples.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes excitement and argument; ABC adds reform, new culture and technical reason. The dynamic range includes more than factional conflict.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies shared outlook through excitement and argument over faith or worldview ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC broadens that movement into exciting communal ideas, reform, new art and culture, and reason in a technical community. Shared unrest about truth and impulses within intellectual or spiritual association connect those changes with the concerns of a community ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
