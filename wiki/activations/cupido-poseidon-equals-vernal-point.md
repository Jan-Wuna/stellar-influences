## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

General spiritual, educational, cultural or philosophical communities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 436; PDF p. 217, Vernal Point row.

German: "Gemeinsame Erkenntnisse für die Öffentlichkeit, gemeinsame Ideen im öffentlichen Leben, geistige Gemeinschaft in der Öffentlichkeit, Klarheit für eine öffentliche Gemeinschaft, öffentliche Kultur-Gemeinschaft, Kunst und Kultur im öffentlichen Leben".

English translation: Shared insights for the public; shared ideas in public life; an intellectual or spiritual community in public; clarity for a public community; a public cultural community; art and culture in public life.

Vernal Point ends "Kunst und Kultur im öffentlichen Leben" before MC's "Beseelte Kultur-Gemeinschaft".

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared cultural, intellectual and spiritual life expressed publicly ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Shared insight, ideas and clarity are named.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: General spiritual, educational, cultural or philosophical communities are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Public cultural communities and art/culture in public life are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The final public-cultural clauses were lost at a parser boundary with MC.

## Contradictions

Witte gives general communities; ABC locates ideas, clarity and culture in public life. No direct opposing claim is stated.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies shared outlook at a general or public level. Witte lists spiritual, educational, cultural and philosophical communities ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC makes the public expression concrete through shared insights and ideas, public cultural communities, clarity for a public community, and art and culture in public life ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
