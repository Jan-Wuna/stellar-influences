## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 278; PDF p. 274.

To take part in the leadership and the institution of a spiritual, cultural or philosophical community. To be the cofounder.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 437; PDF p. 217, Zeus row.

Efforts of a spiritual community, leading knowledge community, spiritual leadership community, common truth witnessing, common culture creation, shaping of arts and culture, and clear objectives of a community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Founding and directing communities of shared outlook through effort and cultural creation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Clear communal objectives are explicit.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Cofounding and leadership of spiritual, cultural or philosophical community are named.
- Events/manifestations: Shared witnessing of truth, cultural creation and shaping art/culture are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The source gives goal-setting and leadership without an account of zeal, mission or humility.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes cofounding and leadership; ABC adds effort, shared testimony and cultural creation. These are related roles within communal work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Poseidon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies shared outlook through founding, leadership and directed effort. Taking part in the establishment or leadership of a spiritual, cultural or philosophical community, including being its cofounder, supplies Witte's concrete role ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds communal effort, shared witnessing of truth, cultural creation and clear objectives, linking organized leadership with making art and culture together ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
