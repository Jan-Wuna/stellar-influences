## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 277; PDF p. 273.

To go to the bottom of things, to the source of things. To be alone (single) at a mighty artistic creation and fighting against heavy resistance, under pressure.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 435; PDF p. 216, Admetos row.

German: "Ausdauer und Energie einer Gemeinschaft, blockierte Gewalt-Gemeinschaft, deprimierender Kunst-Einfluß, gemeinsame Macht-Einschränkung, gründlicher Einfluß einer Gemeinschaft, Widerstand gegen Gewalt-Gemeinschaften, Macht-Festigung in einer Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Perseverance and energy of a community; a blocked community of force or violence; depressing artistic influence; shared restriction of power; thoroughgoing influence of a community; resistance to communities of force or violence; consolidation of power within a community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Depth, resistance and consolidation within collective strength or artistic creation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Perseverance and depressing artistic influence are source descriptions without a diagnosis.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Working alone under pressure and resistance to violent communities are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Going to the source of things, major artistic creation and restricted or consolidated communal power are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Perseverance and consolidation coexist with restriction; isolation does not imply loss of all support.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes solitary artistic work under resistance; ABC adds communal perseverance, power consolidation and resistance to violence. These are different positions within the formula.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies collective or artistic strength through depth, pressure and resistance. Going to the source of things and working alone on a major artistic creation make that sustained effort concrete ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds communal perseverance and energy, restriction or consolidation of power, and resistance to communities of force or violence. The exact formula retains both constrained effort and the strengthening of communal power ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
