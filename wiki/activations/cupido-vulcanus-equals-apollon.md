## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 277; PDF p. 273.

Expansion of a mighty association. Greatly extended art creations. Art products for the masses.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 435; PDF p. 216, Apollon row.

German: "Energie-Handels-Gemeinschaft, erfolgreicher Kunst-Einfluß, gemeinsame Macht-Erfahrungen, Gewalt-Vermehrung in der Gemeinschaft, Macht-Ausdehnung einer Gemeinschaft, mächtiger Erfolg einer Gemeinschaft, zunehmende Gewalt in der Gemeinschaft".

English translation: An energy-trading community; successful artistic influence; shared experiences of power; proliferation of force or violence within a community; expansion of a community's power; powerful success of a community; increasing force or violence within the community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Expansion of collective power, artistic reach and communal success ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Shared experiences of power are named without a fuller personality account.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: A powerful association and an energy-trading community are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Art products for the masses, extended artistic creation and growing communal power or violence are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC retains increasing Gewalt beside success; scale does not by itself imply loss of craft or intimacy.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes artistic and organizational expansion; ABC adds power experience and increasing force or violence. The latter is a substantive additional range.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies collective strength through expansion and reach. A mighty association grows, artistic creations extend, and art products reach the masses ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC connects this range with successful artistic influence, an energy-trading community, shared power experiences and expanded communal power, while also retaining proliferation of force or violence. Success and that coercive possibility remain distinct source expressions ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
