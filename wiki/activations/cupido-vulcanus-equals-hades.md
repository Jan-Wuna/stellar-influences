## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 277; PDF p. 273.

Great misfortune or tragedy in a family. Evil deeds of a mighty association. Great want in artistic accomplishment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 435; PDF p. 216, Hades row.

German: "Energie-Mangel einer Gemeinschaft, gefährlicher Einfluß einer Gemeinschaft, gemeinsamer Kräfte-Mangel, kriminelle Gewalt-Gemeinschaft, Kunst-Einfluß aus der Vergangenheit, Stärke aus gemeinsamer Vergangenheit, verborgene Gewalt in einer Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Lack of energy in a community; dangerous influence of a community; shared lack of strength; a criminal community of force or violence; artistic influence from the past; strength from a shared past; hidden force or violence within a community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Misfortune, deficient strength or harmful communal force, alongside influence from the past ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No individual psychological pattern is described.
- Body/health: Lack of energy/strength concerns a community rather than a medical diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Family tragedy and harmful deeds of a powerful association are named.
- Events/manifestations: Deficient artistic accomplishment, criminal or hidden violence and past artistic influence are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Strength from a shared past remains a substantive branch beside deficit and harm.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses tragedy, harmful deeds and artistic deficiency; ABC also retains strength from the shared past and past artistic influence. These are not all destructive meanings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = Hades`, Hades specifies collective strength through misfortune, deficit or harmful influence. Family tragedy, harmful deeds of a mighty association and a lack of artistic accomplishment form Witte's examples ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds communal energy or strength deficits and criminal or hidden force, but also artistic influence from the past and strength from a shared past. Those historical resources remain part of the exact formula alongside its adverse expressions ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
