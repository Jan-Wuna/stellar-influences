## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 277; PDF p. 273.

Double family celebrations, mass (numerous) weddings. To prove one’s great artistic ability by facts. Working on some great artistic creation. Power demonstration of a community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 434; PDF p. 216, Mars row.

German: "Handlungen einer mächtigen Gemeinschaft, Energie-Herstellung für die Gemeinschaft, einflußreiche Arbeits-Gemeinschaft, einflußreiche Kunst-Impulse, gemeinsame Energie-Impulse, Gewalt-Handlungen einer Gemeinschaft, kraftvolle gemeinsame Tätigkeit".

English translation: Actions of a powerful community; energy production for the community; an influential work community; influential artistic impulses; shared energy impulses; acts of force or violence by a community; powerful shared activity.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Collective strength enacted through work, artistic creation and public demonstration ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No individual emotional or motivational pattern is described.
- Body/health: Energy production is communal activity without a medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Double family celebrations and numerous weddings are explicit social settings.
- Events/manifestations: Proving artistic ability through work and a communal power demonstration are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Acts of violence coexist with artistic and celebratory work; spectacle replacing meaning is not stated.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes artistic accomplishment, celebrations and demonstration; ABC adds energy production, work and violent acts. These activities remain distinct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = Mars`, Mars specifies collective strength through action and accomplishment. Working on a major artistic creation and proving ability by facts sit beside family celebrations, numerous weddings and a community's demonstration of power ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds energy production, influential work and artistic impulses, powerful shared activity and acts of force or violence. The formula retains constructive work and celebration alongside that coercive branch ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
