## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 277; PDF p. 273.

Great talent for organizing. To be an outstanding artist. Mighty art creation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 434; PDF p. 216, MC row.

German: "Gemeinsamer seelischer Einfluß, ich bin stark in der Gemeinschaft, mein Einfluß in der Gemeinschaft, meine mächtige Gemeinschaft, psychische Energie einer Gemeinschaft, seelische Kraft einer Gemeinschaft, starke Seelen-Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Shared influence of the soul or inner life; I am strong in the community; my influence within the community; my powerful community; psychic energy of a community; inner or soulful strength of a community; a strong community of souls.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Organizing or artistic capacity and personal strength within a community ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC's first-person strength and communal psychic/inner energy are explicit source formulations.
- Body/health: No bodily condition or diagnostic mechanism is named.
- Social/relationship: Personal influence within a powerful community is named.
- Events/manifestations: Outstanding artistry, major artistic creation and organizing talent are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Soul and psychic vocabulary is retained without an added mechanism or control motive.

## Contradictions

Witte describes capacity and artistic accomplishment; ABC describes inner and personal participation in communal strength. They have different emphases.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = MC`, MC specifies collective strength through personal organizing and artistic capacity. Talent for organization, outstanding artistry and major artistic creation give Witte's concrete expressions ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC emphasizes my influence and strength within the community, alongside shared inner influence, psychic energy and a strong community of souls. These source phrases connect personal participation with communal strength ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
