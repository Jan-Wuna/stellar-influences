## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 277; PDF p. 273.

Mighty artistic mental projects. Sketch or plan of a great artistic creation. Mighty artistic plans.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 434; PDF p. 216, Mercury row.

German: "Berichte über Gewalt in der Gemeinschaft, Einfluß der Jugend in der Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Gedanken-Kraft, Gewalt gegen Jugend-Gemeinschaften, Macht der Medien-Gemeinschaft, Nachrichten-Einfluß auf die Gemeinschaft, starke Verkehrs-Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Reports about force or violence in a community; influence of youth within a community; shared power of thought; force or violence against youth communities; power of a media community; influence of news on the community; a strong transport community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Collective strength expressed through artistic plans, thought, reporting and media ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Artistic mental projects and shared power of thought are explicit.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Youth communities, media and transport communities are named settings.
- Events/manifestations: Sketches/plans of major art, news influence and reports on communal violence are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Reports about violence and violence against youth communities are different source roles.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes artistic planning; ABC adds media, youth, news and transport. The exact formula includes more than a blueprint for art.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = Mercury`, Mercury specifies collective strength through thought and communication. Major artistic projects, sketches and plans give that mental activity an artistic form ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC broadens it to shared power of thought, youthful influence, media power, news influence and a strong transport community. Reports about communal force or violence and violence directed against youth communities remain distinct examples ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
