## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed p. 277 (PDF p. 273).

Great family celebrations, betrothal, weddings etc. To be a partner of an influential person. Influential public associations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed p. 434 (PDF p. 216).

influential women's community, shared force against a woman, shared emotional influence, shared power of the people, violent community of the people, power of the woman in the community, strong shared feelings, and strong community of the people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Collective influence expressed through shared feelings, families, women, and public associations.
- psychology: Strong common feelings and shared emotional influence.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a specific bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Family celebrations, influential partners, women's communities, and the power of women within a community; the ABC entry also names collective violence against a woman.
- events/manifestations: Betrothals, weddings, large family celebrations, and influential public associations.
- conflicts/notes: The gendered examples belong to the source wording. Collective feeling includes both solidarity and coercion; emotional dependence is not established by these entries.

## Contradictions

Celebration and collective violence are different manifestations in the entries, not a direct disagreement about one defined situation. Their overlap on communal influence does not establish independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = Moon`, Moon specifies communal power through shared feelings and the people who carry them. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives this a visible social setting in family celebrations, betrothals, weddings, and influential partnerships or public associations. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds women's communities, women's influence within a group, and strong collective feelings, while also retaining collective violence against a woman as a harsher possibility. The entries describe a range from communal celebration to coercion; they do not establish emotional dependence or make the gendered examples universal.
