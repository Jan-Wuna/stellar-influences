## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed p. 277 (PDF p. 273).

The beginning of the dissolution of an association or family. To pretend to belong to a mighty association.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed p. 435 (PDF p. 216). The English passage below was corrected against the German scan; the former economic-and-monetary-union wording did not represent this entry.

Dissolution of a power community, shared weakness of energy, shared influence through sensitivity, violence from the air against the community, great uncertainty within a community, a water-energy community, wind energy for the community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A powerful association loses cohesion or energy, with uncertainty about its strength or membership.
- psychology: Shared sensitivity and great communal uncertainty are named in the ABC entry.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical meaning is given; weakness refers to shared energy in the community.
- social/relationship: Beginning dissolution of a family or association and pretended membership in a powerful association.
- events/manifestations: Dissolution, violence from the air against a community, and shared water or wind energy are explicit examples.
- conflicts/notes: Sensitivity and water or wind energy broaden the entry beyond dissolution. No inevitable collapse or particular cause of dissolution is supplied.

## Contradictions

Both entries include dissolution. The ABC entry's sensitivity and energy examples add different manifestations rather than contradicting that theme; no conditions select among them.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies communal power through weakening cohesion, uncertainty, or an uncertain claim to membership. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names the beginning dissolution of a family or association and pretending to belong to a powerful group. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) likewise includes dissolution and shared weakness, but also gives shared sensitivity, violence from the air, and communal water or wind energy. These concrete alternatives should remain visible: the formula is not confined to deception, and the entries supply no rule making every association dissolve.
