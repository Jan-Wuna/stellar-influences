## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed p. 277 (PDF p. 273).

To be connected with a mighty society, family or great art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed p. 434 (PDF p. 216). Energy linkage corrects the former "energy relief" against the German scan.

Encounter with communities of violence, relations with influential communities, energy linkage within a community, connecting energies together, joint transmission of strength, and connections to power communities.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections join people or energies to influential, powerful, or violent groups.
- psychology: Neither entry establishes a motive for seeking these connections.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical meaning is given.
- social/relationship: Ties to a mighty society, family, great art, or an influential community; encounters with violent groups are also named.
- events/manifestations: Combining energies and transmitting strength jointly are the ABC entry's practical examples.
- conflicts/notes: Connection does not establish endorsement, rank, or moral purpose. "Energy relief" was a translation error for energy linkage.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap on connection with powerful communities. Artistic and violent associations are distinct examples, not a direct contradiction or evidence that all such connections are coercive.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = Node`, Node specifies communal power as a connection to a powerful group or a joining of energies within it. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names society, family, and great art; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds influential communities, violent groups, and jointly linking or transmitting strength. This is a statement about association and connection. It does not establish why someone enters the group, whether the connection is welcome, or what purpose its power serves.
