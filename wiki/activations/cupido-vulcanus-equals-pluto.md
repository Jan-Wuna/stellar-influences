## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed p. 277 (PDF p. 273).

Great family changes. Great transformation in the concept of art. To work on the building up of a great society. Great art creation in the making.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed p. 435 (PDF p. 216). The English wording and omitted final clause were checked against the German scan.

Influential development of a community, developing energy together, collective unfolding of power, development of violence within the community, change through common strength, growing violence within the community, and growing artistic influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Communal strength develops or transforms families, associations, and artistic creation.
- psychology: Neither entry gives a distinct personality or motivational description.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical meaning is supplied.
- social/relationship: Great family changes and the building of a great society.
- events/manifestations: Transformation of the concept of art, artistic creation in progress, growing artistic influence, and development of communal energy or violence.
- conflicts/notes: Constructive development and growing violence are both represented. No claim says that transformation must destroy what it develops.

## Contradictions

Building, artistic development, and growing violence are different possible directions of communal change. The entries give no conditions for selecting among them and do not establish a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies communal power as development and transformation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places this in great family changes, building a society, and changing or creating art; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends it to jointly developing energy and power, change through common strength, and growing artistic influence. That development can also take the form of increasing violence within a community. The entries preserve both possibilities without making destruction the necessary result of collective strength.
