## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed p. 277 (PDF p. 273).

To exercise enormous influence in the mental and spiritual as well as the philosophical sphere. Important society in the humanities field.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed p. 435 (PDF p. 216). Influence, shared truth, and the final clause were checked against the German scan.

Influence of art and culture, an influential community of ideas, an influential cultural community, shared intellectual energy, shared cultural power, strength from shared truth, and rational collective influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Collective influence operates through ideas, culture, philosophy, and shared intellectual energy.
- psychology: Shared truth and rational influence are ABC themes; no individual personality is described.
- body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is supplied.
- social/relationship: Influential communities of ideas or culture and an important society in the humanities.
- events/manifestations: Great mental, spiritual, or philosophical influence and the influence of art and culture.
- conflicts/notes: "Shared truth" is attributed source wording, not proof that a group's beliefs are true. The entries do not establish ideological domination.

## Contradictions

Both entries place communal influence in intellectual and cultural life. This overlap is not proof of independent corroboration, and neither entry sets out an opposing claim.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies communal power through ideas, culture, and intellectual or spiritual influence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names philosophy and an important society in the humanities; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds communities of ideas and culture, shared intellectual energy, and strength drawn from what a group regards as shared truth. The organizing theme is the influence of a community's thought and culture. The entries do not by themselves establish domination or validate the truth of its beliefs.
