## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed p. 277 (PDF p. 273).

Obstacles from a mighty society. Interruption of artistic activities. Projects of art which ripen slowly because of great hindrances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed p. 435 (PDF p. 216). English wording checked against the German scan.

Energy and perseverance of a community, shared energy losses, concentration on common strength, artistic influence in old age, concentration of a community's power, power problems within a community, and disturbances of a community through violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Collective strength persists or concentrates while meeting hindrance, loss, and interruption.
- psychology: Perseverance and concentration on common strength are explicit ABC themes.
- body/health: No specific medical condition is given; the ABC entry mentions artistic influence in old age.
- social/relationship: Obstacles imposed by a powerful society and power problems within a community.
- events/manifestations: Interrupted artistic activity, art projects ripening slowly, communal energy losses, and disturbance through violence.
- conflicts/notes: Endurance and delay coexist; neither entry promises eventual completion of a project.

## Contradictions

The entries' obstacles and perseverance are compatible aspects of constrained collective effort. They do not specify when persistence succeeds or establish a direct disagreement.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies communal strength as effort maintained or concentrated under restriction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives obstacles from a powerful society, interrupted artistic work, and projects that mature slowly under hindrance. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds perseverance, shared energy losses, power problems, and violence disrupting a community, while retaining artistic influence in old age as a separate example. The common thread is strength encountering limits; eventual success is not assured.
