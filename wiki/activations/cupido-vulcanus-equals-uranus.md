## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed p. 277 (PDF p. 273).

Trial of strength by a community. To find oneself suddenly in a great society. Objects of art or organizations created by superhuman efforts in a short time.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed p. 435 (PDF p. 216). English wording checked against the German scan.

Influence of a reform community, an influential artistic event, shared energy reform, the power of a technical community, technical energy for the community, surprising violence against the community, and unrest caused by a violent community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Communal strength appears in sudden events, concentrated effort, reform, or technical change.
- psychology: Neither entry establishes burnout or a lasting psychological trait.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical meaning is given.
- social/relationship: A sudden place in a great society, reform communities, and technical communities.
- events/manifestations: Trials of strength, art or organizations created quickly through extraordinary effort, artistic events, and unexpected violence or unrest.
- conflicts/notes: Rapid accomplishment and disruptive events are both represented; the entries do not make one the necessary consequence of the other.

## Contradictions

Constructive reform and unexpected violence are alternatives in the entries, not a direct contradiction. No conditions select between them.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Vulcanus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies communal strength through sudden participation, concentrated exertion, and reform. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names trials of strength, suddenly finding oneself in a great society, and creating art or organizations rapidly through exceptional effort. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds reform and technical energy alongside influential artistic events, unexpected violence, and unrest. The sudden or concentrated character connects these examples; a medical claim about burnout is not supplied.
