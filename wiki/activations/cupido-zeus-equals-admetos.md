### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Obstacles and perseverance in concerted effort or communal founding ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Perseverance and goal-directed effort are explicit.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: A leadership community and a young firm are named.
- Events/manifestations: Blocked efforts, a difficult business beginning and fire restriction are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Blocking by fire and restriction of fire by a community are different ABC expressions.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes obstacles; ABC also includes consolidation and perseverance. Neither source makes blockage proof of a sound foundation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies shared founding and effort through obstacles and persistence. Concerted effort meets grave resistance, and a young firm has a difficult beginning ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC retains blocked communal efforts alongside perseverance and consolidation of a goal-directed community; its fire-blocked and fire-restricting examples describe separate conditions ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
