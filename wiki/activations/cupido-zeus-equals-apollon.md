## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

To unite for common efforts. Foundation of a corporation for trade or science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 427; PDF p. 212, Apollon row.

German: "Erfolgreiche Führungs-Gemeinschaft, erfolgreiche gemeinsame Anstrengungen, Leistungen einer Handels-Gemeinschaft, schöpferische Kunst-Erfahrungen, wissenschaftliche Führungs-Gemeinschaft, Ziele einer freien Gemeinschaft".

English translation: A successful leadership community; successful shared efforts; achievements of a commercial community; creative artistic experiences; a scientific leadership community; goals of a free community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Successful cooperation and founding in trade, science and creative work ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Communal goals are named without an individual motive.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Uniting for shared effort and a free community are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Founding a trading/scientific corporation and communal artistic experiences are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Scientific leadership replaces the corrupt filtration wording; speed of organizational growth is unstated.

## Contradictions

Witte names cooperation and trade/science founding; ABC adds success, artistic experience and free-community goals. These are different settings within the same range.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies communal founding through united effort and success. Founding a corporation for trade or science makes the cooperative purpose concrete ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds successful leadership and shared effort, commercial achievement, creative artistic experience, scientific leadership and the goals of a free community ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
