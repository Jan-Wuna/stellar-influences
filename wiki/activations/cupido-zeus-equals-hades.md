## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

Foundation of a concern dealing in minerals and coal. Destruction through nihilistic instincts. Disintegration of the existing social order.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 427; PDF p. 212, Hades row.

German: "Gefährliche Ziele einer Gemeinschaft, heimliche Anstrengungen einer Gemeinschaft, kriminelle Führungs-Gemeinschaft, Kunst-Schöpfungen aus der Vergangenheit, mangelhafte Leistungen einer Gemeinschaft, Nachteile für eine Gemeinschaft durch Feuer".

English translation: Dangerous goals of a community; secret efforts of a community; a criminal leadership community; artistic creations from the past; deficient achievements of a community; disadvantages to a community caused by fire.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Adverse communal aims or achievement, alongside mineral enterprise and art from the past ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Nihilistic instincts are an attributed source expression.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: A criminal leadership community and disintegration of social order are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Founding a mineral/coal concern, secret effort, fire disadvantage and past artistic creations are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Mineral enterprise and past art are separate source meanings, not automatically exploitative or destructive.

## Contradictions

Witte's mineral enterprise and ABC's past artistic creations broaden the range beyond destructive activity. Neither is reduced to the adverse goals listed beside it.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Hades`, Hades specifies shared founding and effort through dangerous aims, deficient achievement or destructive social activity. Witte names destruction through nihilistic instincts and disintegration of social order, while ABC adds secret effort, criminal leadership and disadvantage through fire ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)). Founding a mineral or coal concern and artistic creations from the past remain separate, concrete branches of the exact formula.
