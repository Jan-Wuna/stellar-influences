## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

Great creations in art. Great artistic creations in connection with others. Establishment of a company protected by the state. Comradeship. United troops. Army organization. Military organizations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 427; PDF p. 212, Kronos row.

special efforts of a community, leadership authority of a community, great achievements of a community, state leadership community, important goals of a community, and goals of a hierarchical community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Communal creation and effort expressed through leadership, state protection and major artistic work ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Important goals are named without a personal motive.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Comradeship, united troops, hierarchical goals and a state leadership community are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: State-protected company founding and major collaborative artistic creations are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Military organization and artistic creation remain distinct expressions; neither is ethically endorsed by this list.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes major creations, state protection and military organization; ABC adds leadership authority and communal goals. No direct opposing claim is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Kronos`, Kronos specifies communal founding and effort through recognized leadership and organized accomplishment. Major artistic creation with others, a state-protected company, comradeship and united troops give the institutional and artistic range ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds leadership authority, great communal achievements and important goals within state or hierarchical communities ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
