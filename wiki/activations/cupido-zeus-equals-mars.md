## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 426; PDF p. 212, Mars row.

Efforts of a leadership community, fiery impulses of a community, joint work performances, joint procreation actions, will efforts of a community, and determined work of a community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared founding and effort made active through work, decisions and coordinated exertion ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Will effort and goal-directed work are explicit.
- Body/health: Procreative actions are historical source wording without a physiological finding.
- Social/relationship: Cooperative work, war allies and a leadership community are named.
- Events/manifestations: Preparing for work, an organizational decision and trading metal products are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Procreative activity, commercial work and military alliance retain their separate settings.

## Contradictions

Witte supplies practical, commercial and military examples; ABC adds will, work and procreative action. No direct opposing claim is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Mars`, Mars specifies shared founding and effort through coordinated action. Preparing and working energetically with others, making an organizational decision, trading metal products and war alliance supply Witte's concrete forms ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds communal will effort, goal-directed work, fiery impulses and its historical procreative-action phrase, connecting the formula with exertion toward a shared result ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
