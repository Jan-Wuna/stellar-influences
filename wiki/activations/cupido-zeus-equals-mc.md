## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

Founder of a corporation. To create artistically. To be a member of a military organization.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 426; PDF p. 212, MC row.

German: "Gemeinsame seelische Anstrengungen, ich führe eine Gemeinschaft, mein Zielstreben in der Gemeinschaft, meine Leistungen für die Gemeinschaft, schöpferische Seelen-Gemeinschaft, seelische Führung einer Gemeinschaft, Ziele einer Seelen-Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Shared inner or soulful efforts; I lead a community; my pursuit of goals within the community; my achievements for the community; a creative community of souls; inner or soulful leadership of a community; goals of a community of souls.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal participation in communal founding, leadership and creative effort ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: First-person goal pursuit and shared inner effort are explicit.
- Body/health: Soul vocabulary is source language without a bodily claim.
- Social/relationship: A corporate founder, military membership and leadership of a community are named.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic creation and personal achievements for the community are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The omitted shared-inner-effort clause is restored; a general life-purpose mechanism is not supplied.

## Contradictions

Witte identifies founding, artistic creation and membership; ABC adds first-person leadership and inner effort. These related roles retain different emphases.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = MC`, MC specifies shared founding and effort through personal participation. Being a corporate founder, creating artistically or belonging to a military organization give Witte's concrete roles ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC's first-person leadership, pursuit of goals and achievements for the community connect those roles with inner effort and a creative community of souls, using the source's own vocabulary ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
