## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

A corporation instituted by the people, presided over by the people. Foundation of a national community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 426; PDF p. 212, Moon row.

German: "Führung einer Volks-Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Anstrengungen eines Volkes, Gemüts-Anstrengungen einer Gemeinschaft, Stunde für gemeinsame Zeugung, weibliche Führungs-Gemeinschaft, zielstrebige Frauen-Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Leadership of a people's community; shared efforts of a people; emotional efforts of a community; an hour for shared procreation; a female leadership community; a goal-directed women's community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Popular or women's communal founding, leadership and shared effort ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names communal emotional effort.
- Body/health: The procreative-hour phrase is historical source language without a timing model or fertility finding.
- Social/relationship: A corporation instituted/presided over by people and a women's leadership community are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Founding a national community and popular shared efforts are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Leadership and emotional-effort modifiers were lost in the former generic Community wording.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes a people-led corporation and national founding; ABC adds women-led communities and emotional effort. The gendered examples remain source-specific.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Moon`, Moon specifies shared founding and effort through a people and its communal leadership. A corporation instituted and presided over by the people, and founding a national community, are Witte's expressions ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds shared popular effort, communal emotional effort and female or goal-directed women's leadership, while retaining its historical procreative-hour phrase without adding a timing model ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
