## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

Bankruptcy of a newly established concern. Secret military organizations. Disarmed military units. Parts of an army forced into inactivity. Dissolved armies.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 427; PDF p. 212, Neptune row.

German: "Feingefühl einer Führungs-Gemeinschaft, Führung einer metaphysischen Gemeinschaft, Führungs-Gemeinschaft in der Luft, gemeinsame Anstrengungen im Wasser, Leistungs-Schwäche einer Gemeinschaft, zukünftige Richtung einer Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Sensitivity of a leadership community; leadership of a metaphysical community; a leadership community in the air; shared efforts in water; weakness in a community's performance; a community's future direction.

The earlier English policy/cohesion/funds wording was a translation artifact. The following Pluto row begins "Entfaltung einer Leistungs-Gemeinschaft" and is excluded here.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Collective leadership and organized effort appear in forms ranging from sensitivity and future direction to inactivity or dissolution ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names sensitivity; neither entry establishes deception as a motive.
- Body/health: No bodily condition or diagnosis is stated; weakness refers to communal performance.
- Social/relationship: Secret military organizations, disarmed units, and a metaphysical community are explicit forms of association.
- Events/manifestations: Bankruptcy of a new concern, forced inactivity, dissolved armies, efforts in water, and a leadership community in the air are named.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC retains sensitivity and future direction alongside performance weakness; Witte emphasizes institutional failure or suspended force. The air, water, and metaphysical phrases receive no further explanation here.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in range rather than directly contradicting one another: Witte lists bankruptcy, secrecy, disarmament and dissolution, while ABC also names sensitivity and future direction. These alternatives do not establish that every shared undertaking fails.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the axis of collective founding and effort through weakened performance, suspended activity, or dissolution. Bankruptcy of a new concern and disarmed or inactive military units make that loss of effective action concrete; secrecy is also named without establishing deception as its cause ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).

The formula's range also includes sensitivity within leadership, a metaphysical community, and consideration of a community's future direction. These ABC examples broaden the account beyond failure; its references to air and water remain literal source phrases without an explanation supplied by this entry ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
