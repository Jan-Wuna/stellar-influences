## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

Family increase. To establish branches. Growing concern. Increase of membership. Reinforcement of units. Increasing army.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 427; PDF p. 212, Pluto row.

German: "Entfaltung einer Leistungs-Gemeinschaft, Entwicklung einer Zeugungs-Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Anstrengungen entwickeln, Veränderungen in einer Führungs-Gemeinschaft, wachsendes Synthese-Streben, zielstrebige Entwicklung einer Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Unfolding of an achievement community; development of a procreative community; developing shared efforts; changes within a leadership community; growing striving for synthesis; goal-directed development of a community.

Pluto begins "Entfaltung einer Leistungs-Gemeinschaft" after Neptune ends "zukünftige Richtung einer Gemeinschaft". The first two Pluto clauses and the start of the third were previously in the Neptune parser record.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Growth and development of communal founding, membership, effort and leadership ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Goal-directed development and growing striving for synthesis are named.
- Body/health: Family increase and procreative-community development are historical source phrases without a fertility diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Increasing membership, branches, reinforced units and a growing army are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: A growing concern and changes in communal leadership are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Pluto begins Entfaltung einer Leistungs-Gemeinschaft; its first clauses were formerly absorbed by Neptune. Witte's P was an alternate glyph.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe growth and development. The source does not state an inevitable self-justifying expansion or a rate of increase.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies shared founding through growth, extension and changed organization. Family increase, new branches, growing membership and reinforced units or an increasing army show the concrete expansion of an established body ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds developing communal efforts, changes in leadership and goal-directed development, while distinguishing procreative-community development and growing striving for synthesis ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
