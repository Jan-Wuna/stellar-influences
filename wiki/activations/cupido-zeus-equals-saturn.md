## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

A disturbing factor in an organization. Losses of a corporation, an association, or a military organization.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 427; PDF p. 212, Saturn row.

German: "Anstrengende Aufgaben für eine Gemeinschaft, gestörtes Zielstreben einer Gemeinschaft, Konzentration auf gemeinsame Anstrengungen, Probleme in einer Führungs-Gemeinschaft, reife Leistungen einer Gemeinschaft, Zielstreben einer Gemeinschaft im Alter".

English translation: Demanding tasks for a community; disturbed pursuit of goals by a community; concentration on shared efforts; problems in a leadership community; mature achievements of a community; a community's pursuit of goals in old age.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Tasks, disturbance and losses in organized effort, with concentration and mature achievement also named ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Concentration on shared effort and disturbed goal pursuit are explicit.
- Body/health: Old age is a source setting without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Corporations, associations, military organizations and leadership communities are named.
- Events/manifestations: Organizational losses, demanding tasks and mature achievements are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The actual ABC range includes achievement and concentration; no response of pressing harder is prescribed.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes disturbance and loss; ABC also retains mature achievement, tasks and concentration. These broaden the formula beyond obstructed cohesion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies shared founding and effort through organizational disturbance, tasks and loss. A disturbing factor and losses within a corporation, association or military organization supply Witte's examples ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds demanding tasks, disturbed goal pursuit and leadership problems, alongside concentration on shared effort and mature achievement, including goal pursuit in old age ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
