## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

The man who assists to draw up new units or who cooperates with reference to new foundations of clubs and corporations. The head of a new foundation. Comrade (military).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 426; PDF p. 212, Sun row.

leadership center of a community, founding of a community of persons, male leadership community, performance center of a community, center for shared efforts, and goal directed environmental community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: People participating in founding and directing communal activity ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No general personality or identity pattern is described.
- Body/health: Male leadership is a historical source example without a bodily claim.
- Social/relationship: A new foundation's head, military comrades and a community of persons are named.
- Events/manifestations: Forming new units, clubs or corporations and centres for leadership/performance/shared effort are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Cooperating in founding and heading a foundation are distinct roles retained by the entry.

## Contradictions

Witte describes individual founding roles; ABC supplies communal centres and shared direction. No direct opposing claim is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Sun`, Sun specifies shared founding and effort through the people who establish or lead a collective body. Assisting with new units, clubs or corporations, heading a foundation and military comradeship give Witte's roles ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds founding a community of people, centres for leadership or shared achievement, and goal-directed communal surroundings, retaining male leadership as its historical example ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
