## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

Corporation stepping suddenly into existence. The sudden foundation of a technical establishment. Military organizations. An uproar.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 427; PDF p. 212, Uranus row.

dynamic leadership community, shared technical goals, new goals of a community, new leadership of a community, rhythm of a performance community, sudden coming together, and tensions in a leadership community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden founding, new direction and tension in organized communal effort ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Tensions and dynamic leadership are named.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Corporations, military bodies and leadership communities are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: A suddenly founded technical establishment, new communal goals and sudden coming together are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Sudden arrival is stated without a corresponding prediction of sudden rupture.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sudden appearance and uproar; ABC also gives new technical goals, leadership and rhythm. The range includes purposeful change and tension.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies shared founding and effort through sudden appearance and new direction. A corporation or technical establishment comes into being abruptly; military organization and uproar are also named ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC connects dynamic leadership and sudden coming together with new or shared technical goals, a new leader and tensions within leadership ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
