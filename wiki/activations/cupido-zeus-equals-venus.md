## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

Harmonious community. Congeniality. Trading company dealing in cosmetics, furniture or articles to furnish a home.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 426; PDF p. 212, Venus row.

German: "Freude bei gemeinsamer Anstrengung, gemeinsame Friedens-Anstrengungen, gemeinsame Liebes-Zeugung, Harmonie einer Führungs-Gemeinschaft, Harmonie-Streben einer Gemeinschaft, harmonische Führung einer Gemeinschaft, künstlerische Leistungs-Gemeinschaft".

English translation: Joy in shared effort; shared peace efforts; shared procreation through love; harmony of a leadership community; a community's striving for harmony; harmonious leadership of a community; an artistic achievement community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Harmonious communal effort, shared joy and peaceful or artistic cooperation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Joy and striving for harmony are explicit.
- Body/health: Procreation through love is historical source wording without a fertility assessment.
- Social/relationship: Congeniality and harmonious communal leadership are named.
- Events/manifestations: Peace efforts, artistic achievement and a trading company in cosmetics/furniture/home articles are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The source says procreation through love, not love denial; harmonious leadership replaces filtration.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize harmony and congeniality. ABC adds peace effort, leadership and artistic achievement; Witte retains a concrete trading example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Venus`, Venus specifies shared founding and effort through harmony and congeniality. A harmonious community and a trading company dealing in cosmetics, furniture or home articles give Witte's social and commercial expressions ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds joy in shared effort, peace efforts, harmonious leadership and artistic achievement, while retaining its historical phrase for shared procreation through love ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
