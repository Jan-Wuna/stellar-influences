## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 272; PDF p. 268.

To establish new societies (corporations). General artistic products. Comrade in arms.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 426; PDF p. 212, Vernal Point row.

German: "Führende Gemeinschaft in der Öffentlichkeit, gemeinsame Anstrengungen im öffentlichen Leben, Gründung einer Gemeinschaft in der Öffentlichkeit, Leistungs-Gemeinschaft in der Öffentlichkeit, zielstrebige Gemeinschaft in der Öffentlichkeit".

English translation: A leading community in public; shared efforts in public life; founding a community publicly; an achievement community in public; a goal-directed community in public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public communal founding, shared effort and creation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Goal-directedness is named without an individual profile.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: New societies/corporations, public leadership and comrades in arms are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Public founding, general artistic products and public shared achievement are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The former single public-community phrase lost five distinct qualified clauses.

## Contradictions

Witte gives corporate, artistic and military expressions; ABC places leadership, effort and achievement in public life. No opposing claim is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Cupido/Zeus = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies shared founding and effort publicly. Establishing new societies or corporations, general artistic products and comrades in arms supply Witte's expressions ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC brings leadership, shared effort, founding, achievement and goal-directed community into public life, making the collective scope explicit ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
