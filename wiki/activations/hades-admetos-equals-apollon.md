## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 282, Apollon row, ending at “Economic downturn” before the Vulcanus glyph.

Beginning of hardship for many people. Satisfactory revelations in history science. Economic downturn.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 445 (one-based PDF p. 221), Apollon row.

Spread of depression and grief, restriction of commercial crime, success through perseverance and deprivation, dangerous experiences of depression, thorough knowledge of the past, adverse limitation of freedom, and deepening into old knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Hardship affecting many, with economic decline and understanding of the past also represented.
- psychology: Spread of grief and depression in ABC's historical wording.
- body/health: Depression is an attributed association; neither entry supplies a bodily diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Many people facing hardship and freedom adversely limited.
- events/manifestations: Economic downturn, satisfactory historical revelations, restricted commercial crime, or success through perseverance and deprivation.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's following Vulcanus row about excessive frugality and starvation is not part of this orientation. Study of the past is not stated to require decline.

## Contradictions

Economic hardship and successful perseverance or historical insight are different expressions. The entries supply no conditions making them either mutually exclusive or a necessary sequence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through hardship on a shared or economic scale and through knowledge of the past. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places the beginning of hardship for many beside economic downturn and satisfactory historical revelations. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds deeper old knowledge and success through perseverance and deprivation, while retaining grief and restricted freedom. These preserve insight and endurance alongside collective difficulty.
