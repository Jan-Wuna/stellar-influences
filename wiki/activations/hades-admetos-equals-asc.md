## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

Connected with people who are in need. To suffer weather change or cold.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 444; PDF p. 221, Asc row.

German: "Einschränkung der Umwelt-Nachteile, gefährdeter Kreislauf des Partners, gründlicher Partner-Verzicht, kriminelle Unterdrückung eines Ortes, Mangel an Gründlichkeit mit dem Partner, Nachteile durch Depressionen des Partners, Vertiefung in die Vergangenheit des anderen".

English translation: Restriction of disadvantages in the surroundings; a partner's endangered circulation; thoroughgoing renunciation involving a partner; criminal oppression of a locality; lack of thoroughness with a partner; disadvantages through a partner's depressions; deeper engagement with another person's past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Contact with need, adverse surroundings and partners, alongside attention to another's past ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Depressions are historical source language, not a diagnosis.
- Body/health: Witte names suffering from cold/weather change; ABC's circulation claim remains attributed historical doctrine.
- Social/relationship: Connection with people in need and partner renunciation or lack of thoroughness are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Local criminal oppression and deeper engagement with another person's past are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Partner renunciation does not specify death or loss; restricting surrounding disadvantages also remains present.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes contact with need and weather; ABC adds partners, local oppression and past inquiry. These different settings do not establish a permanent life atmosphere.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Asc`, Asc specifies scarcity and adversity through contact with people and surrounding conditions. Connection with people in need and suffering from weather change or cold are Witte's expressions ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds partner-related renunciation, lack of thoroughness and local criminal oppression, while also naming restriction of surrounding disadvantages and deeper engagement with another's past. Its circulation and depression terms remain historical claims rather than diagnoses ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
