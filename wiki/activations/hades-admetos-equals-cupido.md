## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

A small association which suffers from different kinds of lack. Discord and meanness in partnerships and associations. Decrease of membership.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 445; PDF p. 221, Cupido row.

German: "Entbehrungen und Einschränkungen in einer Gemeinschaft, Festigung einer Gemeinschaft durch Entbehrungen, gründlicher Verzicht einer Gemeinschaft, Mangel an gemeinsamer Ausdauer, mangelhafte gemeinsame Vertiefung, Vertiefung in gemeinsame Vergangenheit".

English translation: Privations and restrictions within a community; consolidation of a community through privation; thoroughgoing renunciation by a community; lack of shared perseverance; deficient shared deepening; deeper engagement with a shared past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Communal privation, restriction and contraction, with consolidation and shared historical depth ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Discord and lack of shared perseverance are named.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: A small association, partnerships and declining membership are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Consolidation through privation, communal renunciation and shared past inquiry are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Scarcity and consolidation coexist; the source does not infer exhausted warmth or goodwill as a cause.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes lack, discord and membership loss; ABC also includes consolidation and deeper shared historical engagement. The formula is not wholly diminishing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Cupido`, Cupido specifies scarcity and constraint within association. Witte's small group suffers various lacks, partnership discord and declining membership ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds privation, restriction, renunciation and limited shared perseverance, but also consolidation through privation and deeper engagement with a shared past. These meanings retain both contraction and the possibility of sustained communal depth ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
