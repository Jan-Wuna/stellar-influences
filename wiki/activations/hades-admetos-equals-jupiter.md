## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

To have the absolutely necessary essentials in spite of lack of raw materials and scanty conditions. Not to feel the existing needs.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 444; PDF p. 221, Jupiter row.

German: "Depressionen durch Rechts-Nachteile, Gefahr für den Finanz-Kreislauf, Gefährdung für festgelegte Gelder, gründliche Zufriedenheit durch Verzicht, Mangel durch blockiertes Geld, verborgener Geld-Kreislauf, Vertiefung in das Recht der Vergangenheit".

English translation: Depressions through legal disadvantages; danger to financial circulation; danger to allocated funds; deep satisfaction through renunciation; scarcity through blocked money; hidden money circulation; deeper engagement with the law of the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Having essentials or satisfaction amid scarcity, alongside financial blockage and legal disadvantage ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Not feeling existing needs and satisfaction through renunciation are explicit; depression remains historical terminology.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition is named; financial circulation concerns money.
- Social/relationship: Legal disadvantage is named without a developed relationship scenario.
- Events/manifestations: Allocated funds at risk, blocked or hidden money flows and study of past law are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The source retains sufficiency and satisfaction rather than merely equating them with damaged recovery.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes essentials despite scarcity and not feeling need; ABC adds satisfaction alongside legal/financial difficulty. These are different experiences of limited conditions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies scarcity through sufficiency or satisfaction as well as financial difficulty. Having necessary essentials despite scant resources and not feeling existing needs provide Witte's account ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds satisfaction through renunciation alongside blocked money, endangered financial circulation or allocated funds, and legal disadvantages. Hidden money flows and deeper engagement with past law preserve the formula's financial and historical subjects ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
