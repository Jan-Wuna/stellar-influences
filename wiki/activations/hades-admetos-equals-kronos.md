## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

Great negligence. Great lack of raw materials. Meanness and villainess which is unsurpassed hitherto. Strange methods of the government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 445; PDF p. 221, Kronos row.

German: "Besonderer Mangel an Ausdauer, Einschränkungen und Nachteile durch den Staat, dominierende Kreislauf-Gefahr, Festigung der Autorität durch Verzicht, staatliche Einschränkungen aus der Vergangenheit, Vertiefung in die Vergangenheit des Staates".

English translation: Particular lack of perseverance; restrictions and disadvantages caused by the state; dominant circulatory danger; consolidation of authority through renunciation; state restrictions from the past; deeper engagement with the state's past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Scarcity and negligence in institutional settings, with renunciation and historical state inquiry ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Lack of perseverance is explicit.
- Body/health: ABC's circulatory-danger phrase remains historical doctrine without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: State-imposed restrictions and authority consolidated through renunciation are named.
- Events/manifestations: Major raw-material lack, unusual governmental methods and past state restrictions are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's unusual villainess wording is retained as printed; no specific female agent is inferred.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes negligence, material shortage and governmental methods; ABC adds authority consolidation and state history. These institutional subjects have different emphases.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Kronos`, Kronos specifies scarcity and constraint through institutional authority. Great negligence, raw-material shortage and governmental methods described as strange are Witte's concrete expressions ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds state restrictions, lack of perseverance and authority consolidated through renunciation, while retaining inquiry into the state's past. Its circulatory-danger phrase remains an attributed historical claim ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
