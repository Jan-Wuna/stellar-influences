## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

To be pressured to perform menial service. To be terrorized in or through one’s work. To make up for neglect and omissions through one’s work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 444; PDF p. 221, Mars row.

German: "Ausdauer im Arbeits-Dienst, Festigung des Willens durch Verzicht, geheime Blockade-Tätigkeit, gründliche Bemühungen im Verborgenen, Mangel an Ausdauer im Bemühen, nachteilige Depressions-Impulse, Vertiefung in Geheim-Tätigkeit".

English translation: Perseverance in work service; strengthening the will through renunciation; secret blockade activity; thorough efforts in concealment; lack of perseverance in effort; adverse depressive impulses; deeper engagement in secret activity.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Work and effort under pressure, with corrective labor, perseverance and concealed activity ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Strengthening will through renunciation and deficient perseverance coexist in ABC.
- Body/health: Depressive impulses are historical source terms without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Pressure into menial service and terror through work are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Making up neglect/omissions through work, secret blockade and thorough hidden efforts are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Correcting omissions is a named work function; punitive intent is not a general source explanation.

## Contradictions

Witte includes coercive work and repairing omissions; ABC includes perseverance, its lack and concealed effort. These different kinds of work are not interchangeable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Mars`, Mars specifies scarcity and constraint through work and effort. Menial service under pressure and terror through work sit beside making up neglect or omissions through one's work ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds perseverance, will strengthened by renunciation, and thorough concealed efforts, while also retaining lack of perseverance, secret blockade and depressive impulses as historical terms. The formula therefore distinguishes pressured work from corrective or sustained effort ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
