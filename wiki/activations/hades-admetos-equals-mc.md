## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

Coldness, hard to approach. To stay alone and lonely all through life. To live in scant circumstances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 444; PDF p. 221, MC row.

German: "Gefährliche seelische Unterdrückung, mein gefährdeter Kreislauf, mein gründlicher Verzicht, mein Mangel an Ausdauer, meine gefährlichen Depressionen, meine Festigung durch Entbehrung, meine Vertiefung in Verborgenes, seelische Festigkeit in der Not".

English translation: Dangerous inner oppression; my endangered circulation; my thoroughgoing renunciation; my lack of perseverance; my dangerous depressions; my consolidation through privation; my deeper engagement with hidden things; inner firmness in need.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal isolation and scant circumstances, alongside inner consolidation through privation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Coldness, difficulty of approach and inner firmness in need are attributed descriptions.
- Body/health: Endangered circulation and depressions remain historical source claims, not diagnoses.
- Social/relationship: The source describes lifelong solitude as one historical example.
- Events/manifestations: Renunciation, consolidation through privation and engagement with hidden things are named.
- Conflicts/notes: The inspected Witte passage is PDF p. 284; the same entry also appears on PDF p. 282. Lifelong solitude is not made a universal outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes coldness and solitude; ABC also retains inner firmness and consolidation through privation. These different possibilities remain side by side.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = MC`, MC specifies scarcity and constraint through personal experience of scant circumstances and isolation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). Renunciation and limited perseverance appear beside inner firmness in need, consolidation through privation and deeper engagement with hidden things. These ABC meanings preserve resources within hardship as well as its pressure; its circulation and depression terms remain historical claims ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
