## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 282, Mercury row; the same chapter and row also appear on PDF p. 284.

Scantiness. Sad and pessimistic mood caused by lack. Talks about raw materials or old materials.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 444 (one-based PDF p. 221), Mercury row.

Lack of endurance in youth, reports about danger to circulation, dangerous traffic blockades, criminal oppression of youth, inadequate depth of thought, disadvantages through blocked thinking, adverse traffic congestion, and immersion in old writings.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Scarcity and impeded thought or communication, with discussion of raw or old materials and study of old writings.
- Psychology: Witte attributes a sad, pessimistic mood to lack; ABC names inadequate depth and blocked thinking.
- Body/health: ABC names lack of endurance in youth and reports of danger to circulation, without a specific diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Criminal oppression of youth is an explicit source possibility; no particular family or partnership outcome is stated.
- Events/manifestations: Talks about raw or old materials, circulation-danger reports, dangerous traffic blockades or congestion, and immersion in old writings.
- Conflicts/notes: Verkehrs-Stau means traffic congestion, not a thaw. Sadness and blocked thought are source claims, not proof of an inescapable scarcity mindset.

## Contradictions

Blocked or inadequate thought and immersion in old writings are different possibilities, not a stated causal chain. The passages do not establish that studying old material creates pessimism or that every named limitation has the same cause.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through thought and communication concerned with lack or impediments. Witte links a sad, pessimistic mood to scarcity and names discussion of raw or old materials; ABC adds blocked thinking, reports of danger to circulation, and practical traffic obstructions. Immersion in old writings preserves a distinct form of sustained attention to the past, without making that study a cause of the adverse claims.
