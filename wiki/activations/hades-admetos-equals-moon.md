## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 282, Moon row. The same chapter and row also appear on PDF p. 284.

Diseased, oppressed frame of mind. Women or other persons who are in dire need. Deficiency symptoms in the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 444 (one-based PDF p. 221), Moon row.

Lack of endurance of a woman, blockage through lack of feeling, endangered circulation of a woman, dangerous suppression of the woman, a woman's thorough renunciation, lack of emotional firmness, and immersion in the past of a people.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Dire need, deficiency, and emotional or physical restriction in the sources' attributed account, alongside attention to a people's past.
- Psychology: Oppressed frame of mind, lack of feeling or emotional firmness, and a woman's renunciation.
- Body/health: Witte names deficiency symptoms in the public; ABC names a woman's endurance deficit and endangered circulation. These historical claims do not specify a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Women or other people in dire need, and dangerous suppression of a woman.
- Events/manifestations: Blockage through lack of feeling, public deficiency symptoms, and immersion in a people's past.
- Conflicts/notes: A woman's renunciation is distinct from renouncing a woman. The source's health and gender wording remains attributed, without adding domestic conditions or a chronic adaptation mechanism.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in need and deficiency. ABC adds suppression, renunciation, and a people's past without explaining their causal relationship or establishing that deficiency becomes a person's expected emotional climate.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through need and deficiency experienced in mood, feeling, and the public. Witte describes an oppressed frame of mind and people in dire need; ABC adds lack of emotional firmness, a woman's renunciation, and suppression. Its endurance and circulation language remains a historical health claim without a diagnosis. Immersion in a people's past is a further explicit expression, whose relation to deprivation is not explained. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
