## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

A change of the present condition is retarded or stopped because of a lack of clarity, deception or a mistake.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 445; PDF p. 221, Neptune row.

German: "Auflösung der Streik-Gefahr, Festigung einer unbekannten Vergangenheit, gefährdeter Luft- und Wasser-Kreislauf, geheimnisvolles tiefes Wasser, gründliche Intuitionen aus der Vergangenheit, Mangel an Ausdauer in Luft oder Wasser, Verborgenes im tiefen Wasser".

English translation: Dissolution of the danger of a strike; consolidation of an unknown past; endangered air and water circulation; mysterious deep water; thorough intuitions from the past; lack of perseverance in air or water; hidden things in deep water.

Neptune ends "Verborgenes im tiefen Wasser". The following Pluto glyph begins "Gefährliche Kreislauf-Entwicklung".

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Delayed change through unclear conditions, alongside past inquiry and hidden or endangered water/air conditions ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Past intuitions and lack of clarity are named.
- Body/health: Air and water circulation are source environmental subjects without a personal diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Dissolution of strike danger is a collective source example.
- Events/manifestations: Retarded change, mysterious deep water and hidden things in deep water are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: Neptune ends Verborgenes im tiefen Wasser; the following dangerous-circulation-development clause belongs to Pluto. The cited Witte passage is on PDF p. 284 and also appears on PDF p. 282.

## Contradictions

Witte describes change delayed by error, deception or lack of clarity; ABC adds ending strike danger, past intuition and water subjects. These are not all frozen bad conditions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies constraint through change delayed or stopped by lack of clarity, deception or mistake ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). The exact formula also includes ending a strike danger, unknown past material and intuitions from the past. Endangered air/water circulation, mysterious deep water and hidden things there give the passage a separate environmental and concealed-material range ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
