### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

Increasing lack of raw materials. Repeated lowering of the standards of living. Scantness and frugality. Regeneration.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 445; PDF p. 221, Pluto row.

German: "Gefährliche Kreislauf-Entwicklung, gründliche Vertiefung in die Vergangenheit, gründlicher Verzicht auf Veränderungen, nachteilige Entwicklung durch Depression, Transformation durch verborgenen Urstoff, wachsender Widerstand gegen Kriminalität".

English translation: Dangerous development of circulation; thorough deepening into the past; thoroughgoing renunciation of changes; adverse development through depression; transformation through hidden primordial material; growing resistance to criminality.

Pluto begins "Gefährliche Kreislauf-Entwicklung" and "gründliche Vertiefung in die Vergangenheit" before "gründlicher Verzicht auf Veränderungen"; the preceding parser had absorbed this opening.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies scarcity through development and change, including increasing material lack and lower living standards, while explicitly retaining regeneration ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). Deeper engagement with the past, renunciation of changes, transformation through hidden primordial material and growing resistance to crime extend the range. ABC's circulation and depression phrases remain historical claims, without making worsening conditions an inevitable route to renewal ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
