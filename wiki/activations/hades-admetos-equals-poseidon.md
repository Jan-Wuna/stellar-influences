## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

Ignorance and mental indifference. To occupy one’s mind with remote antiquity. To be interested in prehistoric things or times. To occupy one’s mind with long- past times.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 445; PDF p. 221, Poseidon row.

German: "Depression durch Wahrheits-Mangel, gründliche Vertiefung in die Vergangenheit, kriminelle geistige Unterdrückung, mangelhafte geistige Vertiefung, verborgene geistige Einschränkungen, verborgener geistiger Urstoff, Vertiefung in Kulturen der Vergangenheit".

English translation: Depression through lack of truth; thorough deepening into the past; criminal intellectual or spiritual oppression; deficient intellectual or spiritual deepening; hidden intellectual or spiritual restrictions; hidden intellectual or spiritual primordial material; deeper engagement with cultures of the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Intellectual limitation and inquiry into remote history or hidden material ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Ignorance and mental indifference are attributed judgments, distinct from historical interest.
- Body/health: Depression is historical source language without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Criminal intellectual or spiritual oppression is a distinct source expression.
- Events/manifestations: Interest in prehistory, remote antiquity, past cultures and hidden primordial material are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Historical inquiry is not made evidence of withdrawal from living reality.

## Contradictions

Witte names ignorance/indifference alongside remote historical study; ABC adds oppression and hidden material. The historical branch is retained without equating it to defect.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies scarcity and constraint in intellectual life, while giving sustained attention to the past a substantial place. Remote antiquity, prehistory and long-past times are explicit interests beside the source's judgments of ignorance and indifference ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds deeper engagement with past cultures and hidden primordial material, alongside deficient depth, hidden restrictions and criminal intellectual or spiritual oppression. These meanings preserve historical inquiry as a subject in its own right ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
