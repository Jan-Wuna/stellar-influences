## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

To get into a tight spot through separation. Losses because of enmity. To be locked tight without any way out. To be at one’s wit’s end.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 445; PDF p. 221, Saturn row.

German: "Ernste Gefahr für die Erde, Festigung durch geduldiges Dienen, gefährliche Schmerz-Verdichtung, gründliche Konzentration auf die Vergangenheit, harte Entbehrungen und Einschränkungen, Nachteile und Probleme durch Depression, Verluste durch Mangel an Ausdauer".

English translation: Serious danger to the earth; consolidation through patient service; dangerous concentration of pain; thorough concentration on the past; harsh privations and restrictions; disadvantages and problems through depression; losses through lack of perseverance.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Separation, restriction and loss, with consolidation through patient service ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Being at one's wit's end and lack of perseverance are source descriptions.
- Body/health: Pain and depression are historical claims without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Loss through enmity and difficult separation are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Being locked in a tight situation, earth danger and concentration on the past are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Patient service and past concentration remain present; no universal closed-system mechanism is given.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes entrapment, separation and loss; ABC also retains consolidation through patient service. The difficult examples do not establish an inevitable lifelong dead end.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies scarcity and constraint through separation, enmity and a tightly restricted situation. Witte's being at one's wit's end conveys the pressure of those examples ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds harsh privation and losses through limited perseverance, while retaining consolidation through patient service and thorough concentration on the past. Its pain and depression phrases remain historical claims rather than diagnoses ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
