## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Days pass in an oppressed mood. The man who suffers from several lacks and has to scrape along. The man with very few needs. The ugly man.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Depression heartache a day, danger to the body circuit, serious personal renunciation, criminal subjugation of man, lack of corporal perseverance, personal consolidation through deprivation, and personal deepening into the past

## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Admetos = Sun`: identity being narrowed by scarcity, meagerness, and emotional pressure. Main caution: reducing the person to the deprivations they have had to survive.
