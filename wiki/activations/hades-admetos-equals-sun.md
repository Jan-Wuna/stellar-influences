## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

Days pass in an oppressed mood. The man who suffers from several lacks and has to scrape along. The man with very few needs. The ugly man.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 444; PDF p. 221, Sun row.

German: "Depressions-Kummer am Tag, Gefahr für den Körper-Kreislauf, gründlicher persönlicher Verzicht, kriminelle Unterdrückung des Menschen, Mangel an körperlicher Ausdauer, persönliche Festigung durch Entbehrungen, persönliche Vertiefung in die Vergangenheit".

English translation: Depressive sorrow during the day; danger to bodily circulation; thoroughgoing personal renunciation; criminal oppression of a person; lack of physical perseverance; personal consolidation through privations; personal deepening into the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal scarcity or few needs, with renunciation and consolidation through privation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: An oppressed mood and historical depressive sorrow are attributed source descriptions.
- Body/health: Circulation risk and limited physical perseverance are historical claims without diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Criminal oppression of a person is named.
- Events/manifestations: Scraping along, having very few needs and personal engagement with the past are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The source's ugly-man judgment is retained as historical evaluation rather than a judgment of the reader.

## Contradictions

Lack and an oppressed mood coexist with few needs and consolidation through privation. The text does not reduce personal identity to scarcity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Sun`, Sun specifies scarcity and constraint through a person's circumstances. Scraping along under several lacks and an oppressed mood coexist with the distinct example of having very few needs ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). Personal renunciation, consolidation through privation and deeper engagement with the past extend that range, alongside criminal oppression. ABC's bodily and depression claims remain attributed historical language ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
