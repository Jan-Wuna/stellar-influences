## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

Grave event. Suddenly appearing want, need or poverty. Sudden serious enmity. Sudden slide of the earth or of firm things. To be buried under something.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 445; PDF p. 221, Uranus row.

German: "Gefährdeter technischer Kreislauf, Kummer durch neue Einschränkungen, plötzliche Kreislauf-Gefahr, Nervosität wegen mangelhafter Gründlichkeit, Verdichtung gefährlicher Spannungen, Vertiefung in vergangene Rhythmen".

English translation: An endangered technical cycle; sorrow through new restrictions; sudden circulatory danger; nervousness because of insufficient thoroughness; concentration of dangerous tensions; deeper engagement with past rhythms.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden need or serious events, with new restriction and concentrated tension ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Nervousness through insufficient thoroughness and sorrow over new restrictions are named.
- Body/health: Sudden circulatory danger is a historical source claim, not an individual prediction.
- Social/relationship: Sudden serious enmity is an explicit social expression.
- Events/manifestations: Earth or solid-material slides, being buried, technical-cycle danger and past rhythms are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Insufficient thoroughness replaces poor working conditions; past rhythms remain a distinct subject.

## Contradictions

Witte's sudden severe events coexist with ABC's technical cycles and historical rhythms. Chronicity or an inevitable breakdown is not established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies scarcity and constraint through sudden events. Sudden need or poverty, serious enmity, a slide of earth or solid material and burial are Witte's severe examples ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds new restrictions, concentrated tensions and endangered technical cycles, while retaining deeper engagement with past rhythms. Its nervousness and circulation phrases remain historical source claims ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
