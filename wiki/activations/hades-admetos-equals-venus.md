## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

Captivated or hindered in one’s desire for love. Longing for the past. To be released from duty or freed of service in spite of lack and need of personnel.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 444; PDF p. 221, Venus row.

German: "Depression durch Liebes-Kummer, Festigung der Liebe durch Entbehrungen, Friedens-Festigung durch Verzicht, gründliche Vorliebe für die Vergangenheit, mangelhafte Vertiefung in die Liebe, Vertiefung in die Kunst der Vergangenheit".

English translation: Depression through sorrow in love; consolidation of love through privations; consolidation of peace through renunciation; a profound preference for the past; deficient deepening in love; deeper engagement with art of the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Restricted desire or longing, with love/peace consolidated through privation and renunciation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Longing for the past and love-sorrow are explicit source themes.
- Body/health: Depression remains historical source terminology without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Hindered desire for love and release from duty despite personnel need are distinct examples.
- Events/manifestations: Strengthened love or peace and engagement with past art are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Past art and release from service remain substantive meanings, not merely forms of emotional deprivation.

## Contradictions

The entries include hindered love and insufficient depth alongside strengthened love, peace and release from service. They do not define every bond by unfulfilled longing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Venus`, Venus specifies scarcity and constraint through love, longing and release. Hindered desire and longing for the past appear beside release from duty or service despite personnel need ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC also names love consolidated through privation, peace consolidated through renunciation and deeper engagement with past art, while retaining sorrow and insufficient depth in love. This range includes sustained affection and cultural interest as well as restriction ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
