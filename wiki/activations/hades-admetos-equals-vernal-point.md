## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

General lack of raw materials. Sagging economies, sagging living standards. General pessimism.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 444; PDF p. 221, Vernal Point row.

German: "Begrenzung der Kriminalität in der Öffentlichkeit, Ende der Entbehrung im öffentlichen Leben, gefährdeter öffentlicher Kreislauf, nachteilige Blockaden in der Öffentlichkeit, öffentliche Vertiefung in die Vergangenheit, Unterdrückung der öffentlichen Kriminalität".

English translation: Limitation of criminality in public; an end to privation in public life; endangered public circulation; harmful public blockades; public deepening into the past; suppression of public criminality.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public scarcity and declining conditions, alongside limits on crime and an end to privation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: General pessimism is an attributed source description.
- Body/health: Public circulation is an undefined collective source term, not a medical prediction.
- Social/relationship: Public restriction or suppression of criminality is explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Raw-material lack, sagging economies/living standards, public blockades and past inquiry are named.
- Conflicts/notes: The source says suppression of public crime, not promotion; danger-to-life wording was not in the actual row.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes economic decline and pessimism; ABC also names ending privation and curbing crime. These material differences remain side by side.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies scarcity and constraint at a public level. Raw-material shortage, sagging economies and living standards, and general pessimism form one source range ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). The exact formula also retains ending public privation, limiting or suppressing public crime, public blockades and deeper public engagement with the past. Those ABC meanings preserve both adverse conditions and their restriction or ending ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
