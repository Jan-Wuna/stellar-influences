## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

To overdo frugality. Under heavy pressure through need, starvation or enmity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 445; PDF p. 221, Vulcanus row.

German: "Einschränkung gefährlicher Gewalt, gewaltige Nachteile durch Depression, gründlicher Einfluß der Vergangenheit, gründlicher Macht-Verzicht, heimliche Macht-Verdichtung, Mangel an Energie und Ausdauer, stabilisierende Kraft der Vergangenheit".

English translation: Restriction of dangerous force or violence; enormous disadvantages through depression; profound influence of the past; thoroughgoing relinquishment of power; secret concentration of power; lack of energy and perseverance; stabilizing strength of the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Heavy pressure and excessive frugality, with power relinquishment and stabilizing past influence ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Historical depression language and lack of perseverance are source expressions.
- Body/health: Starvation is a severe source circumstance, not a diagnosis from the formula.
- Social/relationship: Enmity and restriction of dangerous violence are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Relinquished or secretly concentrated power and stabilizing strength from the past are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Relinquishment of power replaces power absorption; pressure does not automatically produce brutality.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes pressure and overdone frugality; ABC also names restricting violence, relinquishing power and stabilization. These are different relationships to force.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies scarcity and constraint through heavy pressure and frugality carried too far. Need, starvation or enmity supply Witte's circumstances ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). Restricting dangerous violence and relinquishing power are distinct ABC meanings beside secret concentration of power and energy deficits. Profound influence and stabilizing strength from the past preserve another resource within the formula ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
