## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 284.

To relieve or get rid of poor living conditions or lack of raw materials through great energetic efforts. To go ahead with a fanatical eagerness while carrying on a hateful fight.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 445; PDF p. 221, Zeus row.

German: "Blockade durch gefährliches Feuer, Einschränkung krimineller Führung, eingeschränkte Dienst-Leistungen, feurige Urenergie im Verborgenen, mangelhafte Ausdauer der Führung, Verdichtung einer Feuer-Gefahr, Vertiefung in Leistungen der Vergangenheit".

English translation: Blockade through dangerous fire; restriction of criminal leadership; restricted services; fiery primordial energy in concealment; insufficient perseverance of leadership; concentration of a fire danger; deeper engagement with achievements of the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Energetic effort against scarcity, with restricted leadership/services and fire danger ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Fanatical eagerness in a hateful fight is one attributed source branch.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Restriction of criminal leadership and limited services are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Relief of poor living conditions/raw-material lack, fire blockade and past achievements are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Corrective effort and hateful fighting remain different expressions; neither is presented as the inevitable cause of the other.

## Contradictions

Witte includes relieving deprivation and hateful fighting; ABC adds fire, service limits and restrictions of criminal leadership. The remedy branch is retained without a general hostility mechanism.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Admetos = Zeus`, Zeus specifies scarcity and constraint through directed effort. Great energetic effort can relieve poor living conditions or material lack, while fanatical eagerness in a hateful fight remains a separate source expression ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds restrictions on criminal leadership and services, insufficient leadership perseverance, hidden primordial energy and fire danger. Deeper engagement with past achievements gives the formula a further historical subject ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
