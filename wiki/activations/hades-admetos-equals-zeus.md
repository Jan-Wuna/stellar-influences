### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To relieve or get rid of poor living conditions or lack of raw materials through great energetic efforts. To go ahead with a fanatical eagerness while carrying on a hateful fight.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blockage by dangerous fire, restriction of criminal activity, restricted service performances, fiery primordial energy in secret, inadequate perseverance of operation, compaction of a fire danger, and deepening in achievements of the past

## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Admetos = Zeus`: scarcity provoking sharp initiative and aggressive attempts at remedy. Main caution: fighting deprivation with so much hostility that the cure reproduces the same harshness.
