## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 285; PDF p. 281.

Poverty and need of a few. To be part of these few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 443; PDF p. 220, Admetos row.

German: "Ausbreitung von Kummer durch Depression, Einschränkung der Handels-Kriminalität, Erfolg durch Ausdauer und Entbehrungen, gefährliche Depressions-Erfahrungen, gründliches Wissen über die Vergangenheit, Vertiefung in altes Wissen, viel Not durch Unterdrückung".

English translation: Spread of sorrow through depression; restriction of commercial criminality; success through perseverance and privations; dangerous depressive experiences; thorough knowledge of the past; deepening into old knowledge; much need through oppression.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Need affecting a few, alongside success through endurance, crime restriction and historical depth ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Depression/sorrow are historical source descriptions, not diagnoses.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Being among a few in poverty and oppression-related need are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Commercial crime restricted, success through privation and thorough past knowledge are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The final ABC word is oppression, not underpressure; permanent resignation is not stated.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes poverty among a few; ABC also retains endurance-based success and historical knowledge. The formula's range is broader than enclosed exclusion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies widespread need and knowledge of the past within a narrower or sustained field. Being among a few in poverty gives one concrete social expression ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). Success through perseverance and privation, restriction of commercial crime, and thorough engagement with old knowledge retain distinct possibilities beside sorrow and need through oppression. ABC's depression terminology remains historical source language ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
