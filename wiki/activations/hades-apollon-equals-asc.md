## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 284; PDF p. 280.

Others are poverty-stricken. To experience mass misery in the environment. Others blame the misery on the past. Ugly things of the past are pointed out to the native.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 442; PDF p. 220, Asc row.

German: "Erfolg in der Umwelt durch Entbehrungen, gefährdete Freiheit eines Ortes, gefährliche Erfahrungen mit dem Partner, heimlicher Handel in der Umwelt, Mangel an Wissen über den Partner, vermehrter Kummer am Ort, zunehmende Gefahren am Ort".

English translation: Success in one's surroundings through privations; endangered freedom of a locality; dangerous experiences with a partner; secret trade in the surroundings; lack of knowledge about a partner; increased sorrow at the locality; increasing local dangers.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Contact with widespread need and local or partner difficulty, with success through privation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Increased sorrow is an attributed local source expression.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Impoverished others, partner experiences and local freedom at risk are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Mass misery in the surroundings, secret trade and reminders of adverse past events are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Endangered freedom is not dangerous freedom; success through privation remains present.

## Contradictions

Witte describes surrounding misery and others' references to the past; ABC also gives success through privation. No direct opposing claim is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = Asc`, Asc specifies widespread need and historical material through others and one's surroundings. Poverty in other people, mass misery nearby and adverse things from the past brought to attention form the social setting ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds partner difficulty, hidden local trade, increased sorrow or danger and threatened local freedom, while retaining success through privation ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
