## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 284; PDF p. 280.

To be well taken care of in hard times. Income or money through collecting rags and junk. Success in secret sciences. Luck and success in antiquity or history science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 442; PDF p. 220, Jupiter row.

German: "Erfolgreicher Rechts-Dienst, gefährdete Rechts-Freiheit, gefährliche Geld-Vermehrung, Geld-Handels-Dienst, Glück und Erfolg durch Entbehrungen, heimlicher Geld-Handel, Kummer durch viel Geld, nachteilige Rechts-Erfahrungen".

English translation: Successful legal service; endangered legal freedom; dangerous increase of money; money-trading service; good fortune and success through privations; secret money trading; sorrow through much money; adverse legal experiences.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Support and success amid hardship, with old-material trade and historical or secret knowledge ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Sorrow associated with much money is named without a general motive.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Being cared for in hard times and legal service are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Income from rags/junk, successful historical/secret studies and money trading are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Endangered legal freedom is distinct from dangerous freedom; success is retained without an invented deeper-misery diagnosis.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes care and successful trade/study; ABC includes success through privation alongside legal/financial danger. These different outcomes remain side by side.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies hardship and old or hidden knowledge through support and successful activity. Being cared for in hard times, earning through rags or junk and success in secret, antiquarian or historical studies give concrete forms ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds successful legal service and fortune through privation, alongside endangered legal freedom, risky money growth and secret trading. Financial success and financial trouble remain distinct possibilities ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
