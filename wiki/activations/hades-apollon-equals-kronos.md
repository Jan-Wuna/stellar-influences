## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 285; PDF p. 281.

Misery of the masses brought on by incompetence of the government. To be an authority in historical science. Abilities in the field of magic and occult science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 443; PDF p. 220, Kronos row.

German: "Autorität für verborgenes Wissen, Erfolg eines Staates durch Entbehrungen, gefährdete Freiheit im Staat, Handel mit Staats-Geheimnissen, selbständige Ausdehnung einer Gefahr, Verbreitung von Autoritäts-Mangel, zunehmende Behörden-Kriminalität".

English translation: Authority for hidden knowledge; success of a state through privation; endangered freedom in the state; trade in state secrets; independent spread of a danger; spread of a lack of authority; increasing criminality among authorities.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Authority and government specify the pair's large-scale hardship and knowledge of what is old or hidden.
- **Psychology:** Expertise in history or occult study is represented; the entries do not establish a general personality profile.
- **Body/health:** Mass misery is named, but no distinct illness or bodily mechanism is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Government incompetence, state secrets, endangered freedom in the state, and criminality among authorities concern public institutions.
- **Events/manifestations:** Historical scholarship, occult expertise, state deprivation, spreading danger, and lack of authority are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Authority can appear as expertise or as institutional failure. Behörden-Kriminalität concerns criminality among authorities; it does not mean horde crime.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Authority in historical or hidden knowledge differs from the listed lack of authority and institutional criminality; the entries do not specify when each occurs.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = Kronos`, Kronos locates the pair's hardship, historical knowledge, and secrecy in authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives two distinct expressions: government incompetence producing mass misery, and recognized ability in historical or occult study. The unifying question is how authority handles difficult collective conditions or specialized knowledge, rather than a claim that expertise itself causes suffering.

The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds success through state privation and dealings in state secrets, alongside endangered freedom, independently spreading danger, lack of authority and increasing criminality among authorities. These preserve the institutional failures as well as the scholarly expertise, without making them necessary consequences of each other.
