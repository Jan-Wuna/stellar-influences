## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 284; PDF p. 280.

To come through bad experience to knowledge. To be under the influence and impression of great poverty. To know the past. To be a historian. Knowledge of a secret doctrine.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 442; PDF p. 220, MC row.

German: "Mein altes Wissen, mein Erfolg durch Entbehrungen, mein geheimes Wissen, mein Mangel an Wissen, meine Erfahrungen mit der Kriminalität, meine gefährlichen Erfahrungen, meine gefährdete Freiheit, Zunahme des Seelen-Kummers".

English translation: My old knowledge; my success through privation; my secret knowledge; my lack of knowledge; my experiences with criminality; my dangerous experiences; my endangered freedom; increase in sorrow of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Knowledge of hardship, the past, or concealed doctrine becomes a personal concern.
- **Psychology:** Being deeply impressed by poverty, learning through bad experience, and increasing inner sorrow are explicit.
- **Body/health:** The entries do not name a specific illness. ABC's Seelen-Kummer describes inner sorrow, without a medical heart claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Poverty and experience of criminality are named contexts, without a distinct partnership claim.
- **Events/manifestations:** Historical study, knowledge of a secret doctrine, and learning through difficult experience are represented.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Knowledge and lack of knowledge both occur in the ABC list. They are possible states, not proof of a logical contradiction.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC includes both knowledge and a lack of knowledge, success through privation and increasing inner sorrow, without explaining when each alternative applies.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = MC`, MC makes the pair's difficult experience and historical or hidden knowledge personally consequential. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects learning with bad experience, the impression made by great poverty, historical understanding, and secret doctrine. It supports a person encountering and trying to understand these subjects, without requiring a life defined entirely by bleakness.

The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) reinforces old or secret knowledge and success through privation, while also naming lack of knowledge, experience of criminality, endangered freedom and increasing inner sorrow. These alternatives keep the personal cost visible alongside learning; the sorrow is not a medical heart condition.
