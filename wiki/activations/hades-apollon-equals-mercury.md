## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Talks, opinions and thoughts about epidemics, lack, poverty. To blab secrets. Debates or scientific thoughts about the past, antiquity on historical science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reports about successful past, language about old knowledge, secret trade traffic, opinions about poor knowledge, news about successful deprivations, writings about secret knowledge, and multiplication of traffic risk

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Reports, thought, and discussion take hardship, history, or secret knowledge as their subject.
- **Psychology:** Concern with these topics is explicit; habitual pessimism or inability to act is not established.
- **Body/health:** Epidemics are a subject of discussion in Witte, not a diagnosis of the speaker.
- **Social/relationship:** Disclosure of secrets and concealed commercial dealings concern how information circulates between people.
- **Events/manifestations:** Historical debate, scientific thought, writing about hidden knowledge, and reports concerning deprivation are represented.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The ABC entry adds traffic risk. This is an additional manifestation, not a reason to reduce every communication theme to danger.

## Contradictions

The entries supply different subjects for communication and do not establish a direct contradiction. No causal claim about analysis displacing remedy is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies how the pair becomes thought, speech, or written information. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins discussions of poverty and epidemics with historical scholarship and the disclosure of secrets. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends this to reports about the past, writings on concealed knowledge, and secret commercial traffic (printed p. 442).

The connection is the circulation and examination of difficult, old, or hidden information. Traffic risk remains a separate ABC detail; neither that detail nor the difficult subjects justify diagnosing a pessimistic personality or predicting an epidemic.
