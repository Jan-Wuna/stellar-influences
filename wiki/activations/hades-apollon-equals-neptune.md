## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 284; PDF p. 280.

Wanting to scientifically disclose or predict the future from events of the past. Great humidity. To deal with masses of water. Water systems. Drainage.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 443; PDF p. 220, Neptune row.

German: "Ausbreitung einer gefährlichen Schwäche, Feingefühl für geheimes Wissen, Mangel an Wissen über Feinstoff, metaphysisches Wissen der Vergangenheit, nachteilige Erfahrungen im Wasser, Sensibilität für Wissen über die Vergangenheit".

English translation: Spread of a dangerous weakness; sensitivity to secret knowledge; lack of knowledge about subtle matter; metaphysical knowledge of the past; adverse experiences in water; sensitivity to knowledge about the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Historical inference and sensitivity to concealed knowledge, with a concrete water-management branch ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Wanting to infer the future from past events is an aim, not demonstrated predictive success.
- Body/health: Dangerous weakness is a historical source phrase without a specified diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: No distinct relationship pattern is supplied.
- Events/manifestations: Humidity, large quantities of water, water systems, drainage and past metaphysical knowledge are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's water continuation belongs to Neptune. ABC ends sensitivity to past knowledge; the following successful-development clauses belong to Pluto.

## Contradictions

Water management and historical or metaphysical inquiry are distinct applications. Neither source supplies a single causal mechanism joining them.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through historical inference, sensitivity to hidden knowledge and concrete water subjects. The source's wish to disclose or predict the future from past events states an aim; humidity, masses of water, water systems and drainage form its separate practical branch ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds sensitivity to secret or past knowledge, past metaphysical knowledge and adverse experience in water, retaining dangerous weakness as historical language without a diagnosis ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
