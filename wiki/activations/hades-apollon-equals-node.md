## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 284; PDF p. 280.

Stoppage of trade. Decline of economics. In connection with science of antiquity or science of history. Accomplice to hidden things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 442; PDF p. 220, Node row.

German: "Beziehung zu altem Wissen, erfolgreiche Geheim-Verbindungen, gefährdete Freiheit durch eine Bindung, gefährliche Erfahrungen in einer Verbindung, heimliche Handels-Verbindungen, Kummer-Vermehrung durch eine Verbindung".

English translation: Relationship to old knowledge; successful secret connections; freedom endangered through a bond; dangerous experiences in a connection; clandestine trade connections; increased sorrow through a connection.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Connections link people to historical knowledge, concealed dealings, or economic decline.
- **Psychology:** Grief within a bond is named; a general need to bond through suffering is not established.
- **Body/health:** No distinct bodily condition is described.
- **Social/relationship:** Historical associations, secret connections, freedom endangered through a bond, and complicity in hidden things are explicit settings.
- **Events/manifestations:** Stoppage of trade, economic decline, secret commercial links, and difficult experiences within a connection occur in the entries.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Successful secret connections and troubled connections are alternatives. ABC describes freedom endangered through a bond, rather than freedom itself as dangerous.

## Contradictions

The entries do not establish a direct contradiction or make every connection harmful. Successful secret connections coexist with danger to freedom, difficult experiences and sorrow through a connection.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = Node`, Node places the pair in connections and participation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins associations with historical study to hidden involvement, interrupted trade, and economic decline (PDF entry start p. 280). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds relationships to old knowledge and secret commerce, alongside both successful connections and grief, dangerous experience or endangered freedom through a bond. The connection is the relationship's subject and circumstances; these alternatives do not establish that all such bonds are complicit or destructive.
