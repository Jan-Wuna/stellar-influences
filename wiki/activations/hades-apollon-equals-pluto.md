## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 284; PDF p. 280.

To begin or to end the time of great emergency. The development of occult abilities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 443; PDF p. 220, Pluto row.

German: "Erfolgreiche Entwicklung durch Entbehrungen, Evolution durch altes Wissen, mangelhafte Wissens-Entfaltung, verborgene Handels-Entwicklungen, wachsende Wissenschafts-Kriminalität, Wandlung durch gefährliche Erfahrungen".

English translation: Successful development through privations; evolution through old knowledge; deficient unfolding of knowledge; hidden commercial developments; growing scientific criminality; transformation through dangerous experiences.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Beginning or ending emergency, and development through deprivation, old knowledge or dangerous experience ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No individual personality pattern is stated.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Hidden trade and scientific criminality are institutional source settings.
- Events/manifestations: Development of occult abilities, successful development through privation and evolution through old knowledge are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte water text belongs to Neptune, while emergency/occult development belongs to Pluto. ABC's first clauses were previously absorbed by Neptune.

## Contradictions

Beginning or ending emergency and developing occult abilities have a different scope from ABC's knowledge/commerce developments. These are distinct manifestations without a stated common mechanism.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair through a change in an emergency condition or development of abilities. Beginning or ending a great emergency and developing occult abilities are the actual Witte expressions ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds successful development through privation, evolution through old knowledge and transformation through dangerous experience, alongside deficient knowledge growth, hidden commerce and scientific criminality. These meanings retain different directions of development rather than treating water as this orientation's doctrine ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
