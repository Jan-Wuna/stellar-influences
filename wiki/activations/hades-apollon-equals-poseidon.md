## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 285; PDF p. 281.

Share and share alike. To establish connections with people who are like minded in the social and political respect. The view of the world corresponds with the social situation. To find adherents and like minded people for a science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 443; PDF p. 220, Poseidon row.

German: "Altes Kultur-Wissen, Erfolg durch vernünftige Entbehrungen, gefährdete geistige Freiheit, gefährliche Verbreitung einer Idee, geheimes geistiges Wissen, heimlicher Kultur-Handel, Mangel an geistigem Wissen, verborgene Wahrheits-Erfahrung".

English translation: Old cultural knowledge; success through reasonable privations; endangered intellectual freedom; dangerous spread of an idea; secret intellectual or spiritual knowledge; clandestine cultural trade; lack of intellectual or spiritual knowledge; hidden experience of truth.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Shared outlook and cultural or concealed knowledge give the pair an intellectual and social expression.
- **Psychology:** A worldview corresponding to social circumstances is explicit; grievance-driven ideology is not.
- **Body/health:** No distinct bodily or medical claim is present.
- **Social/relationship:** Like-minded social or political associates and adherents to a science are the clearest shared settings.
- **Events/manifestations:** Intellectual fellowship, older cultural knowledge, concealed cultural trade, reasonable privation, endangered intellectual freedom and dangerous spread of an idea are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's vernünftige Entbehrungen means reasonable privations; gefährdete geistige Freiheit means endangered intellectual freedom. Geistig can concern intellectual or spiritual matters here.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The entries differ in emphasis between shared outlook and concealed cultural knowledge; that does not establish a harmful ideology.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through outlook, knowledge, and intellectual association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes sharing, finding socially or politically like-minded people, and gaining adherents to a science, with worldview related to social situation (PDF entry start p. 281). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds older cultural knowledge and concealed intellectual or cultural dealings, with success through reasonable privations alongside endangered intellectual freedom and the dangerous spread of an idea. The evidence supports fellowship around knowledge and circumstances, without showing that the shared ideas must arise from grievance or that the associates necessarily agree on every doctrine.
