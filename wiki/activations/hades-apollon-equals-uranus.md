## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 284; PDF p. 280.

Sudden mean acts as consequence of previous mistakes. Old sins and failings react. The past mistakes and evil deeds are rubbed in. Astrology of the long past ages.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 443; PDF p. 220, Uranus row.

German: "Konzentration auf neues Wissen, nachteilige Überraschungen erfahren, Reform-Erfolge durch Entbehrungen, Spannungen über verborgenes Wissen, viel Kummer und Nervosität, Wissens-Erneuerung aus der Vergangenheit, Zunahme von Rhythmus-Störungen".

English translation: Concentration on new knowledge; experiencing adverse surprises; reform successes through privation; tensions over hidden knowledge; much sorrow and nervousness; renewal of knowledge from the past; increase in disturbances of rhythm.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Earlier failures or knowledge reappear through surprise, tension, or reform.
- **Psychology:** Nervousness and grief are named in ABC; the entries do not establish a general character diagnosis.
- **Body/health:** ABC names increasing disturbances of rhythm without specifying a bodily system or clinical condition.
- **Social/relationship:** Reproaches about old mistakes and tensions over concealed knowledge are the explicit interpersonal settings.
- **Events/manifestations:** Sudden adverse acts, reminders of past wrongdoing, ancient astrology, reform through privation, and renewal of knowledge from the past are represented.
- **Conflicts/notes:** New knowledge and old knowledge both matter. Renewal should not be lost by reducing the formula to the return of past evil.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC includes both concentration on new knowledge and renewal of knowledge from the past. Its unspecified disturbances of rhythm do not establish a medical diagnosis.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden consequences and changed relations to past knowledge. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes earlier mistakes returning as adverse acts or reproaches, and separately names astrology of distant ages (PDF entry start p. 280). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds adverse surprises, tensions over hidden knowledge, new knowledge, reform through privation, and renewal of knowledge from the past. Sorrow, nervousness and unspecified disturbances of rhythm preserve the disruptive side of that range. Together these support disruption involving the past, with reform also represented; they do not establish that every renewed interest in old knowledge is an eruption of wrongdoing.
