## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 284; PDF p. 280.

General poverty and general misery. Dealing with rags. Second hand clothing. The attitude toward the past.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 442; PDF p. 220, Vernal Point row.

German: "Ausbreitung der Kriminalität in der Öffentlichkeit, gefährdete öffentliche Freiheit, nachteilige Erfahrungen im öffentlichen Leben, verborgener Handel in der Öffentlichkeit, vermehrte Verschmutzung im öffentlichen Leben".

English translation: Spread of criminality in public; endangered public freedom; adverse experiences in public life; hidden trade in public; increased pollution in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Poverty, old material, and attitudes to the past appear in public life.
- **Psychology:** An attitude toward the past is named; no personal temperament is supplied.
- **Body/health:** Public pollution is an ABC theme, without a specific illness claim.
- **Social/relationship:** General misery, endangered public freedom, and public commercial or criminal activity concern collective circumstances.
- **Events/manifestations:** Trade in rags or second-hand clothes, hidden trade, and public pollution are represented.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC describes endangered public freedom and adverse experiences in public life. Neither phrase characterizes freedom itself as dangerous.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Public hardship, commerce in old material, and an attitude toward history are different recorded expressions, not proof that the whole public sphere has one fixed outlook.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point gives the pair a general or public setting. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects general poverty and misery with second-hand clothing, rags, and the attitude toward the past (PDF entry start p. 280). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds concealed trade, spreading criminality, endangered public freedom, adverse public experience and pollution. These specify public circumstances alongside the older goods and collective hardship, without establishing a personal temperament.
