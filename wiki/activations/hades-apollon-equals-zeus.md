## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: printed p. 285; PDF p. 281.

Mass misery and large destruction caused by war hate. Mass destruction. Creative activity in historical research.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 443; PDF p. 220, Zeus row.

German: "Erfolg durch Anstrengungen und Entbehrungen, gefährdeter Zeugungs-Erfolg, gefährliche Feuer-Ausbreitung, heimliche Handels-Anstrengungen, kriminelle Ziele der Wissenschaft, Leistungen der Wissenschaft im Verborgenen, Mangel an Führungs-Wissen".

English translation: Success through efforts and privations; endangered success in procreation; dangerous spread of fire; clandestine trade efforts; criminal aims of science; scientific achievements in secret; lack of leadership knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Purposeful effort engages historical work or hidden activity, with destructive collective action also represented.
- **Psychology:** A motive of using history to fuel hostility is not stated; the entries name activities and outcomes instead.
- **Body/health:** ABC names endangered success in procreation as a historical source expression, without specifying a diagnosis or a necessary reproductive outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** War hatred and clandestine trade or scientific efforts are the social settings; family and societal unpleasantness belongs to the preceding Cupido row.
- **Events/manifestations:** Creative historical research, war destruction, dangerous fire spread, criminal scientific aims, and concealed scientific achievements occur in the entries.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Creative research and destruction are distinct branches. Witte's Zeus row starts on printed p. 285/PDF p. 281; the preceding Cupido row's family, society and old-material clauses were removed from this entry. Führungs-Wissen means leadership knowledge.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Creative historical research differs from mass destruction through war hatred, and scientific achievement differs from criminal scientific aims. The entries do not make scholarship itself a cause of warfare.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Apollon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair through directed effort and production. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives creative historical research alongside mass misery and destruction through war hatred. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds effort through privation, endangered success in procreation, clandestine trade efforts, criminal scientific aims, secret scientific achievements, dangerous fire spread and lack of leadership knowledge. These branches should remain distinct: the text does not establish that scholarship itself produces warfare or that old knowledge must be used for hostility.
