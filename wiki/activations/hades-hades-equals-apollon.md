## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Lack of experience. Widespread poverty. Mass misery. Garbage and junk dealers. Trade with old objects. Science of the past. Knowledge of history. Occult science. Secret science. Magic. Great meanness, great vileness. Many in need.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Widespread need, trade in old objects, and knowledge of the past are the principal branches.
- **Psychology:** Lack of experience is explicit; moral judgments such as “vileness” remain attributed source language.
- **Body/health:** Mass misery is named without a distinct illness or medical mechanism.
- **Social/relationship:** Many people in need and junk dealers provide collective and occupational settings.
- **Events/manifestations:** Trade in discarded objects, historical science, occult study, secret science, and magic are listed.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Scholarship and commerce should not be labeled degraded merely because the entry also includes moralized negative terms.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Hades = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the repeated Hades pair in collective, commercial, and intellectual settings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes widespread poverty and many people in need, then trade in junk or old objects, history, and concealed or occult study (PDF entry start p. 276). These branches share need or contact with older and hidden material, without making scholarship or second-hand trade morally inferior. The source's judgments about meanness remain its own terms, not a character verdict derived from the formula.
