#### Witte Entry

Others resign, refuse or are unable. The relation to servants and the socially inferior. To come into contact with disagreeable things. Acquaintance with disagreeable people. Difficulties at the close surroundings. Contaminated environment. Ugly or old surroundings. To be in acquaintance with sick, lonely or old persons. Others are in need. Unpleasantness with other people. Formula for a physician (others are sick).

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** The repeated pair is located in immediate surroundings and contact with other people's difficulty or need.
- **Psychology:** Refusal, resignation, or inability are attributed to others; they do not establish the subject's character.
- **Body/health:** Contact with sick people and a physician example are explicit; they do not diagnose the subject or determine a profession.
- **Social/relationship:** Difficult acquaintances, lonely or older people, and people in need are the entry's social field.
- **Events/manifestations:** Contaminated or old surroundings, interpersonal unpleasantness, and medical contact are listed.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The source's class judgments, including “socially inferior,” are historical language rather than an endorsed ranking of people.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present. Its physician example does not contradict difficult contacts; it is one specific setting in which contact with illness occurs.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Hades = Asc`, Asc specifies the repeated Hades pair through surroundings and encounters. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes difficult immediate conditions and contact with people who are ill, isolated, older, or in need, including its explicit physician example (PDF entry start p. 275). The organizing theme is proximity to these circumstances, rather than proof of personal illness or a fixed burdened life. Class-based and derogatory descriptions in the historical entry should not be transferred into a judgment of the people encountered.
