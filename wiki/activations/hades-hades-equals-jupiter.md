## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 276 (printed p. 280).

Saddened disturbed or hidden happiness. Lack of money. To be lucky with raw materials, antiquities, old materials, junk or on the black market. Clouded happiness. Money losses. Bargaining. Dirty financial transactions. Money laundering. Poor cash flow.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Money and happiness meet shortage, loss, concealment, or gain through older goods.
- **Psychology:** Clouded or hidden happiness is explicit; no fixed personality judgment follows.
- **Body/health:** No distinct medical or bodily claim is present.
- **Social/relationship:** Bargaining and financial dealings are the principal exchanges described.
- **Events/manifestations:** Poor cash flow, money losses, trade in antiquities or junk, black-market activity, and money laundering are listed.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Legitimate trade in old goods and criminal finance are separate examples; one does not imply the other.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present. It includes both gain and loss without establishing that either is inevitable or independently corroborated.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Hades = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the repeated Hades pair through money and the experience of happiness. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins financial shortage or loss with opportunities in raw materials, antiquities, and junk, while also naming concealed or illicit finance (PDF entry start p. 276). The connection is prosperity under difficult or hidden circumstances. Trade in older goods remains distinct from money laundering or black-market dealings; the entry does not make every such occupation morally compromised.
