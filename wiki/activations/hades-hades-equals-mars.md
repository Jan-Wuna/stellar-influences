## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Work, service, fatigue, inactivity, and damaging actions specify the repeated pair.
- **Psychology:** The entry names harmful acts but does not establish a causal path from poverty or fatigue to violence.
- **Body/health:** Fatigue and serious sickness are historical source claims, not diagnoses or necessary outcomes.
- **Social/relationship:** Difficult working conditions, service, solitary work, and coerced labor are distinct social settings.
- **Events/manifestations:** Recycling, waste handling, work with old material, damaging acts, and homicide are listed as separate possibilities.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Necessary waste work is not morally equivalent to violent action. The entry also includes lack of activity.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present. Activity and inactivity are alternative conditions, not an established contradiction; the severe examples are not a forecast.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Hades = Mars`, Mars specifies the repeated Hades pair through action, labor, and the ability to act. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives difficult or solitary work, recycling and waste handling, service, fatigue, and lack of activity, while retaining violence and serious sickness as adverse examples (PDF entry start p. 275). The useful connection is action under difficult circumstances. These distinct entries do not show that poverty, fatigue, or working with refuse causes criminal behavior, nor that all work described here is harmful.
