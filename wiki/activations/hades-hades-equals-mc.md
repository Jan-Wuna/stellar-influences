## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Hades = MC`, MC makes the repeated Hades pair a personal concern in experience, goals, and occupation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes grief and deprivation, business losses, service to others, waste management, and historical work (PDF entry start p. 275). The source also supplies emotional judgments, but these do not establish that a historian or waste worker necessarily has misguided goals, shame, or a lifelong inferior position.
