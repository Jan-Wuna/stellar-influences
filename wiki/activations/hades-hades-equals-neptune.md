## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Error, lack of clarity, decay, scarcity, and water-related damage specify the repeated pair.
- **Psychology:** Deception and lack of lucidity are named without establishing a permanent personality trait.
- **Body/health:** Dropsy and grave epidemics are historical source claims; “dropsy” is retained as its older medical term rather than a diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Deception is represented, but no developed partnership scenario is supplied.
- **Events/manifestations:** Water shortage, drowning, cloudbursts, tidal waves, cyclones, and lack of air are listed.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Water scarcity and excess are different harmful conditions; the entry supplies no single invisible mechanism joining every item.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present. Shortage and water disaster are alternative manifestations rather than a contradiction or a forecast of catastrophe.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Hades = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the repeated Hades pair through uncertainty, deterioration, and damaging water conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) begins with error, deception, and lack of lucidity, then lists scarcity and concrete environmental disasters, including water shortage as well as destructive water (PDF entry start p. 276). Its epidemic and older medical terminology remain attributed possibilities. The range supports several difficult conditions without proving that invisible contamination, corruption, or any one cause explains them all.
