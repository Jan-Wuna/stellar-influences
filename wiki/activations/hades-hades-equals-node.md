## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 275 (printed p. 279).

A mean act committed jointly. Connections started under difficulties or with reluctance. Unpleasant connections or contacts. Poor connections or associations. Contact with disease, evil, poverty or old affairs.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** The repeated pair is specified in difficult, reluctant, or unpleasant connections.
- **Psychology:** Reluctance is explicit; a general preference for harmful bonds is not established.
- **Body/health:** Contact with disease is named, without diagnosing the people involved.
- **Social/relationship:** Poor associations and connections formed under difficulty are central.
- **Events/manifestations:** Jointly committed wrongdoing and contact with poverty, disease, or old affairs are included.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Contact with need or illness is not moral complicity; the adverse joint-act example must remain distinct.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present. Difficult contact and joint wrongdoing are different situations, not equivalent judgments about every relationship.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Hades = Node`, Node specifies the repeated Hades pair through bonds and encounters. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes connections begun with difficulty or reluctance, unpleasant associations, and contact with poverty, disease, or old affairs, alongside a separate example of a jointly committed mean act (PDF entry start p. 275). The relationship is the setting in which difficulty is met. Contact with people in need or with illness does not itself imply wrongdoing, an unhealthy attachment, or an unwillingness to form constructive bonds.
