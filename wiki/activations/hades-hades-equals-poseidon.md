- **Core meaning:** Intellectual, cultural, or spiritual isolation accompanies older or secret doctrine.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Hades = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the repeated Hades pair in intellectual, cultural, and spiritual life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places isolation and deficiency alongside secret sciences, hidden doctrine, and the spirit of the past (PDF entry start p. 276). The supported connection is a constrained or secluded field of knowledge and belief. Its evaluative terms do not establish that antiquarian study is barren, that every secret teaching is false, or that the reader has a mental disorder.
