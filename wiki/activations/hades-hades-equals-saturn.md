## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Prolonged hardship, serious obstacles, separation, and restriction specify the repeated pair.
- **Psychology:** Melancholy and sadness are explicit source terms, not diagnoses or necessary permanent states.
- **Body/health:** Severe sickness, illness restrictions, and isolation or impoverishment through illness remain attributed historical claims.
- **Social/relationship:** Refugees, unemployment, imprisonment, enmity, and separation are distinct social settings.
- **Events/manifestations:** Long poverty, serious loss, crop damage, and separation through accidents are listed.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The list supports duration in unemployment and poverty, but does not define a whole life solely by duty or survival.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present. Its varied forms of restriction are not independently corroborated and do not establish a universal life course.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Hades = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the repeated Hades pair through serious restriction and separation, sometimes of long duration. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives prolonged unemployment or poverty, refugee experience, imprisonment, losses, and isolation through sickness (PDF entry start p. 276). These concrete settings connect hardship with reduced freedom or participation. The medical and psychological terms remain historical possibilities; the entry does not establish that endurance becomes the reader's entire life or that relief is impossible.
