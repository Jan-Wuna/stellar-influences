## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Hades = Venus`, Venus specifies the repeated Hades pair through love, beauty, and service. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes secret affection, love worries, and domestic or hospitality work, but also aging beautifully, old beautiful things, and old love (PDF entry start p. 275). Keeping those positive older forms visible gives the reading more range than a list of sexual judgments. The source's reproductive claims and moralized wording remain attributed historical examples; they do not diagnose fertility, rank occupations, or define a person's character.
