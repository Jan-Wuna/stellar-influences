## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Purposeful activity is paused, prevented, or expressed destructively, with fire and industry prominent.
- **Psychology:** Lack of aim and hatred are source examples; voluntary rest is also present and should not be treated as vice.
- **Body/health:** Burns are named; “paralyzed” is expressly qualified as inability to act rather than a diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Industrial strikes and lockouts are explicit collective settings.
- **Events/manifestations:** Vacation, recreation, compelled inactivity, coal shortage, destructive fire, and war atrocities are listed.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Relaxation and enforced inactivity are distinct. The source does not show that depleted conditions cause destructive anger.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present. Recreation, industrial interruption, and destructive fire are different branches, not proof that rest leads to catastrophe.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Hades = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the repeated Hades pair through purpose, activity, and fire. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes ordinary relaxation or vacation as well as prevented action, strikes, lockouts, coal shortage, destructive fire, and hatred (PDF entry start p. 276). The shared issue is how activity is absent, restricted, or directed; voluntary recreation must remain distinct from forced inactivity and destructive acts. The source does not establish that poverty causes violence, and its qualified action-related “paralyzed” should not be turned into a medical diagnosis.
