## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

Great mean acts committed in a limited scope. Mean acts stay hidden. Evil deeds or acts which are not discovered.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 441; PDF p. 219, Admetos row.

German: "Besondere Nachteile durch Depressionen, Dienst-Ende beim Staat, Einschränkungen und Nachteile für den Staat, Festigung der Selbständigkeit durch Entbehrungen, Unterdrückung durch gelenkte Kriminalität, Widerstand gegen kriminelle Überlegenheit".

English translation: Particular disadvantages through depressions; the end of state service; restrictions and disadvantages for the state; strengthening independence through privation; oppression through directed criminality; resistance to criminal superiority.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Hidden wrongdoing within a limited scope, restriction and resistance amid state or criminal authority ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names "depressions" as historical source language without a diagnosis.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: State service ending, strengthened independence through privation and resistance to criminal superiority are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Undiscovered harmful acts and oppression through directed crime are named.
- Conflicts/notes: The entry does not state that an institution structurally protects the hidden acts.

## Contradictions

Witte describes concealed harmful acts; ABC also includes strengthened independence and resistance. These distinct positions must remain visible.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Admetos`, Admetos specifies adverse authority through restriction and limited or concealed action. Harmful acts remain hidden or undiscovered in Witte's examples, while ABC names directed criminal oppression and disadvantages affecting the state ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)). ABC also retains resistance to criminal superiority and independence strengthened through privation, so the formula includes responses to constraint as well as its harmful forms.
