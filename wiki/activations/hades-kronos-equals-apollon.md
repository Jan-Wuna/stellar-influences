## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

Grand scale meanness. To detect large corruption. Great lack in the government becomes known to the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 441; PDF p. 219, Apollon row.

German: "Benachteiligungen im selbständigen Gewerbe, besonderes Wissen der Vergangenheit, erfolgreicher Dienst der Hierarchie, Kriminal-Wissenschaft des Staates, Mangel an staatlichen Erfolgen, wichtige Erfahrungen der Vergangenheit, wichtiges Geheim-Wissen".

English translation: Disadvantages in independent business; particular knowledge of the past; successful service to hierarchy; the state's criminal science; lack of state successes; important experiences of the past; important secret knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Large-scale wrongdoing and its detection, alongside institutional service and knowledge of the past ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No individual psychological pattern is described.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Independent business, successful service to hierarchy and the state are explicit settings.
- Events/manifestations: Detection of major corruption and public discovery of government deficiencies are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Criminal science is retained as the source compound; investigation is distinct from criminal conduct.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes scale and public discovery; ABC adds successful service and historical or secret knowledge alongside disadvantage. These additions broaden the formula.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Apollon`, Apollon specifies adverse authority at a broad scale, including detection of corruption and government deficiencies becoming public ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). Knowledge is another branch: ABC includes knowledge and experience of the past, secret knowledge, and the state's criminal science, alongside disadvantages in independent business and successful service to hierarchy. The exact formula therefore retains investigation and institutional knowledge as well as exposed failure ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
