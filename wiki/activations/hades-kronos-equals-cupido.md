## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

Mean deeds of a mob. Gang of criminals. Vulgar or obscene art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 441; PDF p. 219, Cupido row.

German: "Angesehene Gemeinschaft aus der Vergangenheit, besonders benachteiligte Gemeinschaften, gemeinsame Nachteile durch den Staat, gemeinsamer Staats-Dienst, kriminelle Gemeinschaften im Staat, Mangel an selbständigen Gemeinschaften".

English translation: A respected community from the past; particularly disadvantaged communities; shared disadvantages caused by the state; shared state service; criminal communities within the state; a lack of independent communities.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Collective wrongdoing and disadvantage, with past community standing and shared state service also represented ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No individual emotional or motivational account is given.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: A criminal gang, disadvantaged communities and shared state service are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Harmful mob deeds, art judged vulgar or obscene, and a lack of independent communities are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Disadvantaged communities and service are distinct from criminal association; the art judgments remain attributed.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes collective harmful acts and evaluative art language; ABC also names respected past communities and shared service. These branches should remain distinct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Cupido`, Cupido specifies adverse authority in group and community settings. Harmful mob deeds, a criminal gang, and art judged vulgar or obscene give Witte's branch its collective form ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC also includes respected communities from the past, disadvantaged communities and shared state service. Shared disadvantage through the state and lack of independent communities broaden the formula beyond participation in crime ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
