## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

Base acts of the boss, the superior or the government. To be in the hands of the detective force. Mass murder.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 440; PDF p. 219, Mars row.

German: "Bemühungen um Staats-Geheimnisse, besondere Nachteile behandeln, gefährliche selbständige Tätigkeit, gefahrvolle staatliche Handlungen, gelenkte kriminelle Tätigkeit, große Nachteile in der Arbeit, Tätigkeit im Staats-Dienst".

English translation: Efforts concerning state secrets; addressing particular disadvantages; dangerous independent activity; hazardous state actions; directed criminal activity; major disadvantages at work; activity in state service.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Adverse authority expressed in actions, work and investigation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No individual motive or temperament is described.
- Body/health: Mass murder is an extreme event example, not a medical diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Bosses, superiors, government, detective authorities and state service are named.
- Events/manifestations: Harmful official acts, dangerous work, directed crime and addressing disadvantages are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: Being in detectives' hands and performing state service are distinct from committing criminal acts.

## Contradictions

The entries contain adverse acts and dangerous work as well as investigative contact and addressing disadvantages. Their different roles should remain distinct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Mars`, Mars specifies adverse authority through action and work. Witte names harmful acts by a boss, superior or government, contact with detectives, and mass murder ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds dangerous independent or state activity and directed crime, while also retaining work in state service and efforts to address particular disadvantages. The source describes several roles within that difficult field, rather than assigning all activity to criminal enforcement ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
