## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 279, MC row.

To experience great meanness, to suffer from it or to commit it. Loss of business or profession as a result of mismanagement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 440 (one-based PDF p. 219), MC row.

Special sorrow of the soul, great danger to the soul, my standing in the past, my great secret, my lack of authority, my renunciation of independence, my disadvantages through the boss, and my hidden authority.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal distress or disadvantage involving authority, independence, and professional position.
- Psychology: ABC's sorrow and danger to the soul; Witte includes suffering or committing great meanness, as attributed moral descriptions.
- Body/health: No specific bodily illness or diagnosis is stated.
- Social/relationship: Disadvantage through a boss, lack of authority, renounced independence, and hidden authority.
- Events/manifestations: Loss of business or profession through mismanagement; personal secrecy and standing in the past.
- Conflicts/notes: Hidden authority and past standing remain alongside present lack or disadvantage. The entries do not reduce every expression to corrupt governance.

## Contradictions

The entries do not explain when a person suffers from harmful conduct or commits it, nor how hidden authority relates to a stated lack of authority. Preserve these alternatives without inferring a single role or motive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = MC`, MC specifies the pair through one's own distress, authority, and independence. Disadvantage through a boss and relinquished independence connect the personal difficulty to an unequal professional relationship; Witte gives loss of a business or profession through mismanagement as a concrete outcome. His account also allows either suffering from or committing great meanness, so the person's role is not fixed. ABC broadens the range with personal secrecy, past standing, and hidden authority, whose relationship to the stated lack of authority remains unexplained.
