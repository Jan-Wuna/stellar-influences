## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

Writings about great poverty and misery. Thinking about great mean deeds. Leader among criminals.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 440; PDF p. 219, Mercury row.

German: "Besondere Berichte über die Vergangenheit, besondere Nachteile in der Jugend, gefährliches Selbstbewußtsein der Medien, Geheim-Gespräche der Obrigkeit, Mangel an selbständigen Gedanken, Nachteile für einen jungen Chef, verborgene Medien-Hierarchie".

English translation: Particular reports about the past; particular disadvantages in youth; dangerous self-confidence of the media; secret conversations of the authorities; lack of independent thoughts; disadvantages for a young boss; a hidden media hierarchy.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Thought, reporting and concealed communication within adverse authority ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Thoughts about harmful deeds, dangerous media self-confidence and lack of independent thought are explicit.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Criminal leadership, authorities' secret conversations and a hidden media hierarchy are named.
- Events/manifestations: Writing about poverty and misery, reports about the past and disadvantages in youth are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Reporting on misery is distinct from propaganda or serving corrupt power; neither is inferred merely from the subject.

## Contradictions

Witte includes criminal leadership as well as writing about poverty; ABC adds historical reports and hidden institutional communication. These different activities do not share a stated motive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies adverse authority through thought, reports and conversation. Writings about poverty and misery and thinking about harmful acts appear alongside the separate example of leadership among criminals ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). Reports about the past, secret conversations of authorities and a hidden media hierarchy broaden that communication field. ABC's lack of independent thoughts and disadvantages affecting youth or a young boss remain additional meanings ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
