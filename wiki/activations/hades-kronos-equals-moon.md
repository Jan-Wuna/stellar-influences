## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

Great misfortune for the woman or for the people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 440; PDF p. 219, Moon row.

German: "Ansehen eines Volkes in der Vergangenheit, besonders benachteiligte Frau, Entbehrungen eines großen Volkes, Geheimnis einer besonderen Frau, gelenkte Kriminalität gegen ein Volk, großer Gefühls-Mangel, Stunde besonderer Nachteile".

English translation: A people's standing in the past; a particularly disadvantaged woman; privations of a great people; a distinctive woman's secret; directed criminality against a people; a great lack of feeling; an hour of particular disadvantages.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Misfortune affecting women or a people, with privation and historical communal standing ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names a great lack of feeling, without a diagnosis.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: A disadvantaged woman, a woman's secret and a people facing directed crime are explicit historical examples.
- Events/manifestations: Collective privation and an hour of particular disadvantages are named.
- Conflicts/notes: The source says secret rather than imprisonment, and lack of feeling rather than a thirteen-year-old person.

## Contradictions

Witte gives the broad adversity of a woman or a people; ABC supplies varied examples and past standing. The gendered terms remain source-specific.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Moon`, Moon specifies adverse authority through hardship affecting a woman or a people ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC makes that range more concrete with privation, a disadvantaged woman, and directed crime against a people, while also retaining a people's past standing and a woman's secret. Its lack-of-feeling and unfortunate-hour examples remain attributed descriptions, without a general explanation of family life or a diagnosis ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
