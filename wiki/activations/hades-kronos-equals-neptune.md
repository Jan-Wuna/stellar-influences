## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

Fraud developed by corruption. The criminal police is wrong.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 441; PDF p. 219, Neptune row.

German: "Besonderer Luft- und Wasser-Mangel, Entbehrungen durch staatliche Schwäche, gefährliche Zukunfts-Lenkung, große Gefahr im Wasser, große Luft-Verschmutzung, selbständiger Dienst in der Luft, Staats-Geheimnisse für die Zukunft, Unsicherheit durch gelenkte Kriminalität".

English translation: Particular shortage of air and water; privations caused by state weakness; dangerous direction of the future; great danger in water; major air pollution; independent service in the air; state secrets for the future; uncertainty through directed criminality.

The Neptune row ends with "Unsicherheit durch gelenkte Kriminalität". The following "Ansehen aus der Vergangenheit entwickeln" begins the Pluto row.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Fraud or mistaken investigation alongside uncertainty, state weakness and environmental shortage ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names uncertainty through directed criminality.
- Body/health: Air/water shortage and air pollution are environmental source claims without an individual diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Criminal police, state weakness, future state secrets and independent service in the air are named.
- Events/manifestations: Fraud through corruption, danger in water and dangerous future direction are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: The Neptune row ends with uncertainty through directed crime; the following development clauses belong to Pluto.

## Contradictions

Witte focuses on fraud and mistaken criminal police; ABC adds environmental shortage and state weakness. Error in investigation does not establish intentional complicity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies adverse authority through fraud, uncertainty and weakness. Witte names fraud developed by corruption and criminal police being wrong ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC broadens the range to privation caused by state weakness, air and water shortage, pollution, danger in water, future state secrets and uncertainty through directed crime. Independent service in the air remains a separate named expression ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
