## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

To be witness to a great crime or great accident. To be a party to doubtful things. To be active as a spy. To be hired. Connection with the criminal investigation department.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 440; PDF p. 219, Node row.

German: "Besondere Verbindungen zur Vergangenheit, Kontakte zu einer verborgenen Hierarchie, Mangel an Selbstbewußtsein in einer Verbindung, mit besonderen Nachteilen verbunden, Verbindung zu einem armen Staat, Verbindung mit Staats-Geheimnissen".

English translation: Particular connections to the past; contacts with a hidden hierarchy; lack of self-confidence in a connection; association with particular disadvantages; connection to a poor state; connection with state secrets.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Connections with secrecy, disadvantage, the past and criminal investigation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Lack of self-confidence within a connection is explicit.
- Body/health: Witnessing a serious accident is an event example, without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Witnesses, spies, hired people and criminal investigators have distinct source roles.
- Events/manifestations: Witnessing a great crime or accident and contact with a poor state or state secrets are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Being a witness or investigator does not itself establish complicity.

## Contradictions

Witte includes witnesses, questionable involvement and investigators; ABC adds hidden hierarchy and disadvantaged connections. These relationships occupy different roles.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Node`, Node specifies adverse authority through connection and encounter. Witnessing a great crime or accident, questionable involvement, espionage, being hired, and connection with criminal investigators are separate source roles ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds connections with the past, hidden hierarchy, state secrets or a poor state, and lack of self-confidence in a connection. These examples retain the distinction between contact with difficulty and participation in wrongdoing ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
