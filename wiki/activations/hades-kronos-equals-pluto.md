## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

Great evil deeds are developed. The growing great evil. Large failure.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 441; PDF p. 219, Pluto row.

German: "Ansehen aus der Vergangenheit entwickeln, Armuts-Entwicklung im Staat, Entwicklung von Autoritäts-Mangel, kriminelle Entwicklungen im Staat, Veränderungen im Staats-Dienst, wachsende Überlegenheit der Kriminalität, Wandlungen durch verborgene Hierarchie".

English translation: Developing standing from the past; development of poverty in the state; development of a lack of authority; criminal developments in the state; changes in state service; growing superiority of criminality; transformations through a hidden hierarchy.

The Pluto glyph begins "Ansehen aus der Vergangenheit entwickeln". Its first two clauses and the start of "Entwicklung von Autoritäts-Mangel" were previously absorbed into Neptune.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Development of adverse authority, poverty or crime, alongside changes in service and past standing ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: No individual psychological pattern is described.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: State service and hidden hierarchy are explicit institutional settings.
- Events/manifestations: Growing harmful acts, major failure, developing state poverty and increasing criminal superiority are named.
- Conflicts/notes: The first two ABC clauses and development of authority lack were split into the preceding Neptune parser entry.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes harmful growth and failure; ABC also names past standing and changes in state service. Neither entry states inevitable collapse or exposure.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies adverse authority through development and increase. Witte's growing harmful deeds and major failure overlap with ABC's developing poverty, authority deficits and increasing criminal superiority in the state ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)). Development of standing from the past, changes in state service, and transformations through hidden hierarchy remain part of the exact formula's institutional range. The entries give no endpoint at which collapse or exposure becomes inevitable.
