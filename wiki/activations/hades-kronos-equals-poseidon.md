## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

Base or mediocre way of seeing life and the world. Wickedness, lies or distortion in the field of ideology or world outlook.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 441; PDF p. 219, Poseidon row.

German: "Alte geistige Hierarchie, große Gefahr für die Wahrheit, großer Mangel an Vernunft, kulturelle Vergangenheit eines Staates, Klarheit über Kriminalität im Staat, nachteiliger Verzicht auf Selbständigkeit, verborgene geistige Überlegenheit".

English translation: An old intellectual or spiritual hierarchy; great danger to truth; a great lack of reason; a state's cultural past; clarity about criminality within the state; disadvantageous relinquishment of independence; hidden intellectual or spiritual superiority.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Adverse authority in worldview and intellectual life, with historical culture and clarity about crime also represented ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Lack of reason is a source judgment rather than a diagnosis.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Old intellectual hierarchy and relinquished independence are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Distortion or lies in ideology, cultural history and clarity about state crime are named.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC Vernunft means reason, not courage; clarity and cultural history remain distinct from distorted ideology.

## Contradictions

Witte's adverse ideological judgments coexist with ABC's cultural history, hidden intellectual superiority and clarity about crime. The formula is broader than falsehood alone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies adverse authority in the field of ideas and worldview. Witte names lies, distortion and an outlook it judges base or mediocre ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC retains danger to truth and lack of reason, but also a state's cultural past, old intellectual hierarchy, hidden intellectual superiority and clarity about crime. These meanings preserve both obscured thought and the separate possibility of understanding institutional criminality ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
