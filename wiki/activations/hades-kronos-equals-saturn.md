## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

Losses by mismanagement of the government. The government is not keeping its promise. To be deserted by the government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 441; PDF p. 219, Saturn row.

German: "Belastende Staats-Aufgaben, besondere Nachteile im Alter, Konzentration auf staatliche Tradition, Nachteile durch Ansehens-Verlust, problematische Staats-Geheimnisse, verborgene staatliche Verluste, Verluste durch gelenkte Kriminalität".

English translation: Burdensome state tasks; particular disadvantages in old age; concentration on state tradition; disadvantages through loss of standing; problematic state secrets; hidden state losses; losses through directed criminality.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Burdens and losses involving government, failed support and diminished standing ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Concentration on state tradition is named.
- Body/health: Old age is a source setting without a medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Government abandonment and unkept promises concern failed institutional support.
- Events/manifestations: Loss through governmental mismanagement, problematic state secrets and losses through crime are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: Belastende means burdensome rather than contaminating; the entry leaves the broader cause of institutional failure unspecified.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe institutional burden or loss. ABC adds tradition, old age and hidden losses without establishing a separate theory of structural betrayal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies adverse authority through burden, loss and failed institutional support. Government mismanagement, promises not kept and being deserted by government form Witte's concrete examples ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds burdensome state tasks, lost standing, problematic secrets and hidden or crime-related state losses; concentration on state tradition and disadvantages in old age remain further settings ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
