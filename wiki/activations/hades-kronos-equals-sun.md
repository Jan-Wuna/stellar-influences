## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

Days of ill fortune. A run of bad luck The man who is subjected to great baseness, who has to defend himself against a vile action. The government does not keep its promise to him. Much ill fate or death by grave events.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 440; PDF p. 219, Sun row.

German: "Besondere körperliche Mängel, großes Geheimnis eines Mannes, Mangel an persönlicher Autorität, mangelhafte staatliche Hierarchie, persönliches Ansehen in der Vergangenheit, Verzicht einer führenden Person, Zentrum für gelenkte Kriminalität".

English translation: Particular bodily deficiencies; a man's great secret; lack of personal authority; a deficient state hierarchy; personal standing in the past; relinquishment by a leading person; a centre for directed criminality.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal adversity, deficient authority and past standing within adverse institutional conditions ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: The entries do not describe a general identity or vitality pattern.
- Body/health: Bodily deficiencies and death through grave events are historical source claims without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: A person defending against harmful action, unkept government promises and a leading person's relinquishment are named.
- Events/manifestations: Runs of bad fortune, a man's secret and a centre for directed crime are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's bodily opening was missing; historical gender/medical examples remain attributed.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes personal misfortune and defense; ABC adds bodily deficiency, secrets, past standing and authority deficits. These are different aspects without a developed opposing claim.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Sun`, Sun specifies adverse authority through personal circumstances. Witte describes a person defending against harmful acts and a government failing to keep its promise, alongside grave misfortune ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds personal authority deficits, a man's secret and past standing, a leading person's relinquishment, and a centre for directed crime. Its bodily-deficiency phrase remains a historical medical claim rather than a diagnosis ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
