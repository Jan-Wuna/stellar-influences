## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

Sudden great misfortune with loss of life. Sudden murder where the government is involved.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 441; PDF p. 219, Uranus row.

Dangerous state event, great technical disadvantages, clandestine tensions in the state, new danger to the state, sudden great danger, tensions over state secrets, reforms in the state service, and unrest due to controlled criminality

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden serious adversity and tension involving government or the state ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: ABC names hidden tensions and unrest.
- Body/health: Loss of life and murder are extreme event examples, not individual predictions.
- Social/relationship: Government involvement, state secrets and state-service reform are explicit settings.
- Events/manifestations: Sudden misfortune, dangerous state events, technical disadvantages and directed-crime unrest are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Reform in state service remains part of the range alongside danger; assassination is not the exact source term.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sudden fatal events; ABC also lists technical disadvantage, tension and reform. These broader expressions should remain visible.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies adverse authority through sudden events and tension. Witte's severe examples involve sudden loss of life or murder with government involvement ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC includes dangerous state events, technical disadvantages, hidden tension, state secrets and unrest through directed crime, while retaining reforms in state service. The formula's range therefore includes institutional disturbance and change as well as its extreme events ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
