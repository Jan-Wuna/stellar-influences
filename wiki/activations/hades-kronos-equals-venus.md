## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: PDF p. 279.

To find help and support from the boss, the superior or the government to enable defending oneself against evil matters. Leading (setting the pace) in purchasable love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: printed p. 440; PDF p. 219, Venus row.

German: "Besonderer Liebes-Dienst, Freude am Staats-Dienst, großes Liebes-Geheimnis, Harmonie-Mangel im Staat, Kunst-Autorität aus der Vergangenheit, nachteilige Liebe zum Chef, Vorliebe für Autoritäten der Vergangenheit, wichtiger Friedens-Dienst".

English translation: Particular service through love; joy in state service; a great love secret; lack of harmony within the state; artistic authority from the past; disadvantageous love for the boss; preference for authorities of the past; important service for peace.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Support against adversity and love or service within institutional authority ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), [ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Psychology: Joy, affection for the boss and preference for past authorities are named.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- Social/relationship: Help from a boss, superior or government and separate transactional-love examples are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: State service, peace service, past artistic authority and lack of state harmony are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Protection from harmful matters is distinct from participation in them; love-service remains an undefined source phrase.

## Contradictions

Help and service coexist with secret or disadvantageous love and lack of harmony. The formula does not make every benefit morally compromised.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Venus`, Venus specifies adverse authority through help, affection and service. Support from a boss, superior or government enables defense against harmful matters; Witte separately retains leadership in purchasable love ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)). ABC adds joy in state service, peace service and artistic authority from the past, alongside a love secret, disadvantageous love for a boss and lack of state harmony. These different settings preserve the distinction between receiving protection and participating in harm ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
