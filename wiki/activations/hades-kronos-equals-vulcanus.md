## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 279, Hades + Kronos, Vulcanus row; glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Great brutality, meanness and evil. The government proceeds in a devilish way. Murders on a large scale.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 441; one-based PDF p. 219, Hades/Kronos, Vulcanus row.

Special energy deficiency, great violence through criminality, dangerous authoritarian influence, criminal power superiority, criminal violence against the state, power deficiency of the state, strong dominance of the past, and hidden power of a hierarchy

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Concentrated authority and criminal coercion, alongside the state's lack of power ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 441).
- Psychology: Dominance is represented through imposed power; no separate account of personal motives is established.
- Body/health: Violence and large-scale killing occur in the historical Witte wording; these are attributed claims, not medical predictions.
- Social/relationship: The entries concern government, hierarchy, and those subject to their power.
- Events/manifestations: Criminal influence, violence against the state, state weakness, and mass violence are distinct possibilities in the entries.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte describes violence committed by government; ABC also names criminal violence against the state. The direction of violence must remain distinct, alongside the state's possible lack of power.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize coercive power and lack of state power respectively. These are alternative conditions, not evidence that every manifestation combines both.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Hades/Kronos pair through the scale and imbalance of power: criminal dominance, dangerous authority, or a state unable to exercise sufficient power. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) gives the extreme example of brutality, government violence and mass killing; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also retains energy deficiency and the hidden power of hierarchy. ABC's criminal violence against the state differs from Witte's violence by government. These are distinct expressions of coercion or weakness, rather than a claim that every manifestation combines them.
