## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Brutality and meanness well aimed, or directed. Meanness born out of hate. To start a large destructive fire. To order war atrocities.

Source: PDF p. 279, Zeus entry under Hades/Kronos.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

English translation: Special achievements through deprivation, danger from a large fire, deficient state leadership, lack of independent achievement, state achievements from the past, a hidden leadership hierarchy, and important leadership service.

Source: printed p. 441 (PDF p. 219), Zeus entry under Hades/Kronos.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Directed destructive action or leadership and achievement under deprivation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 441).
- Psychology: Witte expressly connects aimed brutality with hatred; ABC's service language does not establish the same motive.
- Body/health: The destructive fire and war atrocities are historical source examples of harm, not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Authority appears through orders, state service, and a hidden leadership hierarchy.
- Events/manifestations: Witte names starting a destructive fire and ordering atrocities; ABC adds danger from a large fire, deficient state leadership, and lack of independent achievement alongside achievements and leadership service.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's seven clauses are verified against printed p. 441 (PDF p. 219). Its danger from a large fire does not itself say that the person starts the fire; Witte expressly names starting one. Witte's entry is verified on PDF p. 279.

## Contradictions

Witte's destructive commands and ABC's service under deprivation are materially different emphases. The service terms cannot all be read as evidence of cruelty.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Kronos = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the Hades/Kronos pair through directed action and leadership. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) makes the direction destructive: hatred becomes aimed brutality, starting a large destructive fire, or an order for wartime atrocities. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 441) includes danger from a large fire, deficient state leadership, and lack of independent achievement, but also special achievements through deprivation, state achievements from the past, a hidden leadership hierarchy, and important leadership service. That broader achievement and service strand remains beside the destructive examples; it does not establish a uniformly malicious purpose.
