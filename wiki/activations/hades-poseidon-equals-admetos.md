## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

To hold a special position among a few. To be outstanding.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 449; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, Admetos row.

Mysterious intellectual or spiritual restriction, intellectual or spiritual consolidation through service, lack of clarity through depressions, reasonable restriction through renunciation, deepening into old cultures, and deepening into hidden truths.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A restricted or specialized mental position, including deepening into old cultures and hidden truths ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 449).
- Psychology: ABC describes mental limitation and consolidation through service or renunciation.
- Body/health: ABC uses depression in connection with lack of clarity; this is an attributed historical claim, not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Witte locates distinction among a few, without describing the quality of those relationships.
- Events/manifestations: Specialized standing, concentrated study, and voluntary restriction are the stated range.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC gives reasonable restriction through renunciation, not a statement of severe restriction. Its depression language remains attributed, and Witte's outstanding position among a few is a separate social example.

## Contradictions

Being outstanding among a few and experiencing mental restriction describe different sides of a narrow position. Neither entry establishes that distinction requires exclusion by others.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through narrowing and deepening. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes an outstanding position among a few; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 449) adds mental restriction, consolidation through service, and immersion in old cultures or hidden truths. A specialized place can therefore carry depth as well as limitation, without the entries establishing that social exclusion is its necessary cause.
