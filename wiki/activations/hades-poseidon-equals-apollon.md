## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To separate or isolate from many or to have a special position among many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Old culture knowledge, spread of hidden knowledge, expansion of dangerous ideas, successful spiritual service, successful cultural past, spiritual success through renunciation, cultural success in old age, and increasing cultural danger

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A special position among many, or the wider circulation of old and hidden knowledge ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 449).
- Psychology: The entry supports intellectual distinctiveness, without specifying an individual temperament.
- Body/health: Neither entry gives a specific bodily or medical manifestation.
- Social/relationship: Witte describes distinction or isolation within a larger population.
- Events/manifestations: ABC includes cultural knowledge, successful spiritual service, and the spread of dangerous ideas.
- Conflicts/notes: Greater circulation can involve cultural success or danger; it does not itself establish mainstream acceptance. Witte's parser locator is one-based PDF p. 286 (entry start), not a verified printed-page reference.

## Contradictions

Witte's separation among many and ABC's spread of knowledge are different emphases, compatible without proving that isolation produces recognition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through its relation to a wider field: a special position among many in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), and the spread of old or hidden knowledge in the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 449). This can involve cultural achievement and spiritual service, but also the expansion of dangerous ideas. Wide reach and distinction are supported; public approval or broad recognition is not assured by these passages.
