## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

The native lives his own life mentally in an environment which does not understand him. Others isolate themselves mentally from the native. The native seems to be incomprehensible, mentally not understood.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 448; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, Asc row.

The old spirit of a place, endangered local culture, a partner's intellectual or spiritual distress, a partner's clear renunciation, the cultural past of a place, a partner's lack of clarity, hidden truth in the surroundings, a partner's lack of reason, and reasonable service in the surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Mental incomprehension between a person and the surrounding people ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Witte describes living an inward mental life that others do not understand; ABC also names a partner's distress and lack of clarity.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition is established; mental distress remains ABC's attributed wording.
- Social/relationship: Partners and the local environment are the explicit setting.
- Events/manifestations: Others' withdrawal and difficulty making oneself understood are stated; ABC also retains local cultural and service motifs.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC distinguishes endangered local culture and the cultural past of a place. It gives lack of reason rather than lack of communication, and reasonable service rather than poor service; these are separate examples without a stated causal sequence.

## Contradictions

The entries do not establish an explicit contradiction. Witte emphasizes mental incomprehension and separation from others; ABC adds partner distress or unclear understanding alongside local cultural history, endangered culture and reasonable service.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Asc`, Asc specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through partners and the immediate environment. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes a person whose mental life is not understood by others, with withdrawal making the separation more visible. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds a partner's distress and lack of clarity or reason, as well as local cultural history, endangered culture and reasonable service. Difficulty in mutual understanding is one strand beside these cultural and service examples.
