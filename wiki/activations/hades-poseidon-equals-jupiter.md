## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Happiness through mental isolation. Losses of money through no understanding.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 448; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, Jupiter row.

Success through clear renunciation, joy in intellectual or spiritual service, endangered legal culture, dangerous financial culture, dangerous legal truth, intellectual or spiritual success of the past, intellectual or spiritual success through deprivation, and lack of legal clarity.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Satisfaction or success through mental apartness and renunciation, with financial or legal difficulty also represented ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 448).
- Psychology: Witte names happiness in mental isolation; ABC names joy in spiritual service.
- Body/health: Neither entry gives a specific bodily or medical manifestation.
- Social/relationship: Misunderstanding has practical consequences; service supplies another relation to others.
- Events/manifestations: Witte includes monetary loss through lack of understanding; ABC includes intellectual achievement and unclear legal circumstances.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's legal culture is endangered, while its financial culture and legal truth are described as dangerous. These different directions of danger remain distinct; the source does not specify their practical mechanism.

## Contradictions

Inward happiness and practical loss can coexist. The entries do not promise material success merely because renunciation or spiritual service is satisfying.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through happiness, achievement, and their practical limits. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) places happiness in mental isolation beside monetary loss through lack of understanding. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 448) adds success through renunciation or deprivation and joy in spiritual service, alongside legal and financial difficulty. Inner satisfaction and worldly results therefore need separate consideration; the text does not make one a reliable sign of the other.
