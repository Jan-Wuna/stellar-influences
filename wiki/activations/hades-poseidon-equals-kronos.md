## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

To be subjected to great meanness and losses, particularly by the authorities or higher ups because of ones special position.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 449; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, Kronos row.

A particularly dangerous idea, intellectual or spiritual authority in old age, great lack of clarity, great hidden culture, lack of independent reason, lack of clarity in the authority, a hidden intellectual or spiritual hierarchy, and important hidden truth.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A special intellectual position brought into contact with authority, hidden hierarchy, or serious lack of clarity ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 449).
- Psychology: ABC names a dangerous idea and lack of clarity; it does not establish a fixed personal character.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily or medical manifestation.
- Social/relationship: Witte describes harm from authorities or superiors because of a special position.
- Events/manifestations: Losses imposed from above, concealed spiritual hierarchy, and important hidden truth are the stated range.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC gives intellectual or spiritual authority in old age, not authoritarianism; lack of independent reason is distinct from lack of self-reliance. Its hidden hierarchy and truth clauses are complete, readable examples.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses adverse treatment by authorities; ABC also includes cultural and hidden-truth themes. They do not establish that every intellectual distinction leads to punishment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through authority and exceptional standing. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes meanness and losses inflicted by higher authorities because of a person's special position. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also includes intellectual or spiritual authority in old age, hidden hierarchy and important hidden truth beside a marked lack of clarity and independent reason. Pressure from above and concealed cultural authority remain distinct expressions.
