## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Isolation through one’s own activities or moral behavior. To receive special treatment. To be isolated.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 448; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, Mars row.

Deprivation through intellectual or spiritual activity, intellectual or spiritual activity in secret, endangered cultural work, clear actions in service, inadequate efforts toward reason, deficient intellectual or spiritual activity, hidden cultural activity, and renunciation of clear actions.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Action produces isolation or special treatment; mental and cultural work may occur in secret ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 448).
- Psychology: Witte links the position to one's conduct or moral behavior, without defining a fixed motive.
- Body/health: No specific bodily or medical manifestation is established.
- Social/relationship: Others' special treatment or isolation is the explicit relational result.
- Events/manifestations: Secret intellectual work, service, and renunciation of clear action appear in ABC.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC gives deprivation through activity and inadequate efforts toward reason; it does not give weapons or interruptions in these clauses. Endangered cultural work remains distinct from dangerous work.

## Contradictions

Isolation through conduct and hidden service are different emphases. The entries do not identify all secret work as morally questionable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Mars`, Mars specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through what is done. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) makes isolation or special treatment a consequence of one's activities or moral behavior. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds deprivation through intellectual or spiritual activity, secret cultural work and clear actions in service, alongside inadequate efforts toward reason and renunciation of clear action. The connection concerns conduct and a concealed or separate intellectual position.
