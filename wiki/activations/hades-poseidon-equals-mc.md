## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

To be poor mentally or spiritually, to show defects. To be mentally an odd fellow. To represent a secret science, or secret wisdom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 448; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, MC row.

Danger to soul and mind, I serve truth, I hide truth, my old spirit, my intellectual or spiritual distress, my clear past, my cultural past, my lack of clarity, and my reasonable renunciation.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: An unusual or concealed mental position experienced as one's own ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 448).
- Psychology: Witte includes mental or spiritual inadequacy as well as representing secret wisdom; ABC names personal adversity and unclear understanding.
- Body/health: Witte's evaluative language and ABC's danger to soul and mind are attributed historical wording without a specified bodily condition or medical diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Representing secret knowledge is stated, but a public profession or teaching role is not.
- Events/manifestations: Concealing truth and relating to one's cultural past are ABC's personal expressions.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's complete nine-clause row includes serving and hiding truth, danger to soul and mind, and reasonable renunciation. These alternatives do not establish one moral assessment or a medical mechanism.

## Contradictions

Secret wisdom and spiritual inadequacy are alternative evaluations in Witte. The entry does not make them equivalent or identify an unusual thinker as deficient.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = MC`, MC makes the Hades/Poseidon pair a personal mental or spiritual condition. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) ranges from what it calls mental poverty or oddness to representing secret science or wisdom. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) speaks in the first person of serving or hiding truth, intellectual or spiritual distress, cultural inheritance, lack of clarity and reasonable renunciation. These passages support a private relation to unusual or hidden knowledge, with their evaluative and danger language remaining attributed.
