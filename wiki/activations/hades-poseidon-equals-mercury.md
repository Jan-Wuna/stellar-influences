## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

To have peculiar ideas and to hang on to them. To be immersed in unusual thoughts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 448; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, Mercury row.

Reports about old cultures, dangerous media culture, conversations about cultural dangers, criminal ideas of youth, lack of clarity in youth, speaking about hidden truths, reasonable conversations about the past, and reasonable renunciation of conversations.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Persistent unusual ideas and communication about old cultures or hidden truths ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 448).
- Psychology: Witte describes immersion in peculiar thoughts and holding on to them.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily or medical manifestation.
- Social/relationship: Reports, conversations, and media carry the intellectual content.
- Events/manifestations: ABC names discussion of cultural dangers, hidden truths, and the past, with youth-related difficulties also listed.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC includes both reasonable conversations about the past and reasonable renunciation of conversations. The latter does not name an indomitable entity; the source leaves the circumstances of foregoing conversation unspecified.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in focus between persistence of thought and subjects of communication. Neither establishes originality, depth, or inability to listen as a necessary character trait.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through thought and its expression. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes unusual ideas held persistently and thoughts in which one becomes immersed. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) gives communication concrete subjects: old cultures, hidden truths, the past and cultural dangers. It also allows reasonable renunciation of conversation as a separate possibility, without specifying the circumstances.
