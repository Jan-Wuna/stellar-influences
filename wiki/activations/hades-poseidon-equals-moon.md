## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Odd female persons. To stand out from other persons and the public in relation to mentality and understanding of the world, The native, as a rule, has no understanding for the public or other people in this respect.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 448; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, Moon row.

An ancient cultural people, the dangerous spirit of a people, hiding clear feelings, the cultural past of a people, lack of clear feelings, a woman's lack of clarity, hidden culture of a people, a woman's reasonable renunciation, and a people's culture in danger.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Difference from public understanding and difficulty with clear feelings or collective culture ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 448).
- Psychology: Witte describes limited understanding of other people's worldview; ABC names unclear feelings.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition is given. The gendered judgments are historical source claims, not diagnoses.
- Social/relationship: The public, a people, and women are the sources' stated settings; the gender associations remain attributed.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural inheritance, concealed collective culture, and danger to that culture appear in ABC.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC distinguishes hiding clear feelings from lacking clear feelings, and gives a woman's reasonable renunciation rather than poor renunciation. The historical collective and gendered examples remain attributed.

## Contradictions

Witte says the person lacks understanding of the public; this is not identical to others failing to understand the person. ABC adds collective and emotional material without resolving the direction of misunderstanding.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Moon`, Moon specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through public understanding, feelings and collective culture. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes standing apart mentally and having difficulty understanding the public. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds both hiding clear feelings and lacking them, a people's cultural past or concealed culture, and a woman's reasonable renunciation. The gendered judgments remain historical attributions, and Witte's direction of misunderstanding is preserved.
