## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To appear to others as unclear and intricate through the mental behavior and by isolating of oneself. Not to be understood.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 449; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades/Poseidon, Neptune row; the following Pluto row is excluded.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Dissolution of the cultural past, sensitivity to hidden truth, dangerous cultural weakness, disadvantages through falsification of truth, disadvantages through weakness of mind or spirit, unknown mental or spiritual danger, and uncertainty through lack of clarity.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Mental separation experienced as unclear or hard to understand ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)); sensitivity to hidden truth coexists with uncertainty and falsification in [ABC Entry](#abc-entry).
- Psychology: Witte describes how isolation and mental behavior appear to others; it does not establish deliberate evasion.
- Body/health: ABC's weakness of mind or spirit is attributed source vocabulary, not a specific medical diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Others encounter the person as intricate or incomprehensible.
- Events/manifestations: Dissolution of a cultural past, dangerous cultural weakness, disadvantages from falsified truth, and an unknown mental or spiritual danger.
- Conflicts/notes: The checked Neptune row ends with lack of clarity; the following cultural-development clauses belong to Pluto. Witte's retained parser locator is one-based PDF p. 286 (entry start), not a verified printed-page reference.

## Contradictions

ABC includes sensitivity to hidden truth beside uncertainty. That range does not prove that an unclear presentation is deceptive.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through obscurity and uncertainty. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes mental behavior and self-isolation that others find intricate and difficult to understand. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds sensitivity to hidden truth alongside dissolution of a cultural past, cultural weakness, and disadvantages from falsification or weakness of mind or spirit. Its unknown mental or spiritual danger and lack of clarity retain the difficulty of knowing what is involved. Difficulty understanding and actual falsification remain distinct possibilities; an unclear presentation does not by itself establish deception or a motive.
