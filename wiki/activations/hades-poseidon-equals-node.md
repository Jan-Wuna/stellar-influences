## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

To be associated with odd people. Contact with intellectual inadequacies.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 448; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, Node row.

Encounter with ancient cultures, intellectual or spiritual deficiency in a connection, lack of clarity in a connection, association with dangerous ideas, lack of reason in a connection, and reasonable renunciation of a connection.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Associations involving unusual thought, unclear understanding, or contact with ancient cultures ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 448).
- Psychology: Intellectual inadequacy is Witte's evaluation of a contact, not an established diagnosis of either person.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: Connections and encounters are the explicit setting.
- Events/manifestations: ABC lists meeting ancient cultures and being connected with dangerous ideas.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's lack of reason in a connection and reasonable renunciation of a connection are two separate clauses, not a communication failure. The source does not specify whether the latter means foregoing or ending a particular bond.

## Contradictions

Contact with ancient cultures is not inherently a deficient bond. The entries offer different possible contents of an association without establishing how every connection functions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Node`, Node specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through encounters and associations. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes association with people it calls odd or intellectually inadequate. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also includes contact with ancient cultures, unclear understanding or lack of reason within a connection, and association with dangerous ideas. Reasonable renunciation of a connection is a separate source possibility; unusual companions do not necessarily prevent mutual understanding.
