## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Adaptability to occurring difficulties. Separation and special position develop.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 449; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, complete Pluto row after the Neptune glyph row.

Endangered cultural development, dangerous intellectual or spiritual transformation, cultural development from the past, deficient intellectual or spiritual development, hidden cultural development, growing insights into criminality, growing cultural danger, and growing lack of reason.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Development of a separate mental position, with adaptation and cultural difficulties both represented ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 449).
- Psychology: Witte explicitly names adaptability to difficulties; resilience beyond that is not established.
- Body/health: ABC's mental-development language is an attributed evaluation, not a medical diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Separation and a special position develop in Witte.
- Events/manifestations: ABC names hidden cultural development, growing knowledge of criminality, and increasing cultural danger.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's initial E was a misread alternate Pluto glyph. The complete ABC Pluto row starts with endangered cultural development and includes development from the past; those clauses belong here rather than to the preceding Neptune row.

## Contradictions

Adaptation and deficient development are different possible courses. The entries do not support a uniformly strengthening or deteriorating process.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair as a developing condition. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) links adaptation to difficulties with growing separation or a special position. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds endangered cultural development, dangerous intellectual or spiritual transformation, development from the past and hidden development, with increasing insights into criminality or cultural danger. Change connects these possibilities, while their quality remains varied.
