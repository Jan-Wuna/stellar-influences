## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Deficiencies in education. As a consequence of isolation one feels depressed or is oppressed. To be persecuted as a result of one’s different mental attitude or belief. Mental disorder.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 449; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, Saturn row.

Danger through problematic ideas, intellectual or spiritual maturity through deprivation, concentration on old cultures, cultural losses of the past, painful truth of the past, separations through lack of reason, and hidden intellectual or spiritual tasks.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Mental separation carries restriction, loss, or pressure, while attention may concentrate on old cultures ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 449).
- Psychology: Witte describes depression following isolation and oppression because of belief; these are source claims.
- Body/health: Witte's `Mental disorder` is historical diagnostic language and cannot diagnose a reader or subject.
- Social/relationship: Persecution for a different attitude or belief is an explicit Witte example.
- Events/manifestations: Educational deficiencies, cultural losses, painful past truths, and hidden mental tasks appear.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC gives maturity through deprivation and separations through lack of reason, rather than mental traits or communication failure. These clauses remain distinct from Witte's historical disorder and persecution examples.

## Contradictions

Restriction and concentrated study are distinct expressions. The historical disorder claim must not be used to explain away a different belief or persecution.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through restriction and the cost of mental separation. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) names deficient education, depression or oppression following isolation, and persecution for belief. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds intellectual or spiritual maturity through deprivation, concentration on old cultures, cultural losses and painful past truths, alongside separation through lack of reason. Burden and maturity remain distinct possibilities; Witte's medical wording stays an attributed historical claim.
