## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

To see through mean actions. Sudden heavy grief.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 449; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, Uranus row.

Agitation over lack of reason, hindered intellectual or spiritual renewal, endangered cultural renewal, a new cultural danger, new truths from the past, sudden renunciation of truth, unrest through lack of clarity, and reasonable renunciation of technology.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden recognition or disturbance in relation to truth and culture ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 449).
- Psychology: Witte names heavy grief; ABC names restlessness arising from lack of clarity.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily diagnosis; grief is an emotional source claim.
- Social/relationship: Seeing through mean actions is stated, but loss of trust or exclusion is not specified as its consequence.
- Events/manifestations: New truths from the past and sudden abandonment of truth appear in ABC.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC distinguishes lack of reason, hindered renewal and endangered cultural renewal. Its final technology renunciation is reasonable rather than urgent; the source does not specify a technology or setting.

## Contradictions

Recognizing meanness and abandoning truth are different possible expressions. The entries do not establish that grief always results from insight.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through sudden recognition and disturbance. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) places seeing through mean actions beside sudden heavy grief, without making one the necessary cause of the other. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds hindered or endangered renewal, new truths from the past, sudden renunciation of truth and unrest through lack of clarity. Reasonable renunciation of technology is another separate example whose setting is unspecified.
