## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Loving devotedness to a work. To be put on special duty. To be rejected for ideological reasons. Ideologically rooted in the past.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Endangered peace culture, spiritual love service, clear renunciation of peace, arts and culture of the past, deficient love culture, adverse arts and culture, lack of love, and preference for old cultures

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Poseidon = Venus`: attachment flowing toward a rare doctrine, cause, or work rather than toward easy social harmony. It can show dedicated service and aesthetic loyalty, but also exclusion through belief.
