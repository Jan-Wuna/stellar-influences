## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Loving devotedness to a work. To be put on special duty. To be rejected for ideological reasons. Ideologically rooted in the past.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 448; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, Venus row.

Endangered peace culture, intellectual or spiritual service of love, clear renunciation of peace, art and culture of the past, deficient love culture, disadvantageous art and culture, lack of reason in love, and a preference for old cultures.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Devotion to work, culture, or inherited belief, with ideological rejection also possible ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Loving dedication and preference for old cultures are stated.
- Body/health: Neither entry gives a specific bodily or medical manifestation.
- Social/relationship: Special duty, rejection for beliefs, and troubled love or peace culture are the relational range.
- Events/manifestations: ABC includes art from the past and spiritual service (printed p. 448).
- Conflicts/notes: ABC gives lack of reason in love rather than lack of love. Its service, cultural attachment and renunciation examples remain distinct, without identifying a necessary relationship form.

## Contradictions

Devotion and rejection describe different relations to the work or belief. Their coexistence does not prove that dedication prevents social harmony.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Venus`, Venus specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through devotion and cultural attachment. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) links loving commitment to work with special duty, while also naming rejection for ideology and beliefs rooted in the past. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds intellectual or spiritual service of love, older art and culture, endangered peace and lack of reason in love. Devotion and troubled cultural or relational conditions remain distinct possibilities.
