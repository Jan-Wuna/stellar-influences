## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 286, Hades + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

To be conscious of ones isolated and exceptional position. To become very noted or notorious. To experience refusal and rejection in a special position.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 449; one-based PDF p. 223, Hades + Poseidon, Vulcanus row.

Influence of past cultures, influence of hidden truths, insights into violent crime, power of insight in distress, intellectual or spiritual strength through deprivation, lack of intellectual or spiritual energy, hidden intellectual or spiritual force, and reasonable influence through renunciation.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Exceptional standing becomes conspicuous, influential, or subject to rejection ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Awareness of one's isolated position is explicit; ABC also describes mental strength through deprivation.
- Body/health: Lack of spiritual energy in ABC is not a specific bodily diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Notoriety, refusal, and rejection relate the special position to others.
- Events/manifestations: ABC names influence from past cultures or hidden truths (printed p. 449).
- Conflicts/notes: ABC gives insights into violent crime and reasonable influence through renunciation. Its hidden geistige Gewalt is retained as intellectual or spiritual force without assigning a concrete method or equating insight with criminal conduct.

## Contradictions

ABC includes both mental strength and lack of energy. Neither source establishes that increasing recognition necessarily intensifies rejection.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Poseidon = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Hades/Poseidon pair through prominence and influence. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes awareness of an isolated position, becoming noted or notorious, and experiencing rejection in that position. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds the influence of past cultures and hidden truths, insights into violent crime and reasonable influence through renunciation, with strength through deprivation beside lack of energy. These remain distinct forms of influence and limitation.
