## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Admetos row. The community paragraph previously attached here belongs to the Cupido row.

Wickedness and evil acts in a limited scope.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 447 (one-based PDF p. 222), Admetos row.

Limitation of dangerous violence, enormous disadvantages due to depression, profound influence of the past, thorough renunciation of power, lack of energy and endurance, stabilizing force of the past, and suppression of criminal violence.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A limited scope of harmful action, and restriction or renunciation of dangerous force.
- Psychology: Depression appears in ABC as a source claim; the text does not establish its cause or a diagnosis.
- Body/health: ABC names lack of energy; no specific bodily disease is established.
- Social/relationship: Suppression of criminal violence and renunciation of power are named; the community paragraph belongs to another activation.
- Events/manifestations: Dangerous violence limited, power thoroughly renounced, and stabilizing influence from the past.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's moral wording remains attributed, not an accusation. ABC's influence of the past and endurance clauses are restored from the scan.

## Contradictions

Witte describes harmful acts limited in scope; ABC also describes dangerous violence restrained or power renounced. These are different directions of limitation, without a rule selecting which applies.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair through the scope and restriction of harmful force. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) confines its moral description to acts in a limited scope. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds limitation of dangerous violence, thorough renunciation of power, and suppression of criminal violence. Lack of energy and endurance remains beside the stabilizing influence of the past, preserving depleted capacity as well as deliberate restraint.
