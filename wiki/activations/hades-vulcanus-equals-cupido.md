## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Cupido row. The long-lasting-development sentence previously attached here belongs to Pluto.

Oppressed or decimated families and great communities. The community that has been weakened through great losses or that is in constant danger. Gang of criminals.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 447 (one-based PDF p. 222), Cupido row.

Influence of the past on the community, an energy-service community, dangerous influence of a community, enormous disadvantages for a community, grief in a community through violence, and lack of power in a community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A community bears the influence of past power, disadvantage, or violence ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 447).
- Psychology: Grief through violence is ABC's explicit emotional expression.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition is established.
- Social/relationship: Families and communities may be oppressed, depleted, endangered, or themselves a source of dangerous influence; Witte also names a criminal gang.
- Events/manifestations: Great communal losses, constant danger, past influence, major disadvantage, and lack of power.
- Conflicts/notes: Source boundaries and dangerous influence are restored from the scans. ABC's energy-service community is not defined further; criminal wording remains attributed rather than applied to every affected group.

## Contradictions

The entries include communities suffering harm and communities exerting dangerous influence. These are different positions, without a claim that every endangered group is itself criminal.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through force and danger situated in communal life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes oppressed or depleted families and communities weakened by losses or constant danger, while separately naming a criminal gang. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds the influence of the past, grief through violence, disadvantage, and lack of power, alongside dangerous influence exerted by a community. Shared exposure to force and its communal exercise remain distinct parts of the range.
