## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Jupiter row. The injustice/activity paragraph previously attached here belongs to Mars.

To be happy despite a state of dependence. To find the necessary protection to avert danger. To escape evil attempts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 446 (one-based PDF p. 222), Jupiter row.

Successful influence from the past, dangerous financial influence, enormous legal disadvantages, criminal financial force, criminal legal power, powerful joy in secret, inadequate legal influence, and legal power in the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Happiness or protection within dependence, alongside adverse financial and legal influence.
- Psychology: Happiness despite dependence and powerful private joy are named.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily or medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Finding necessary protection and escaping harmful attempts in Witte.
- Events/manifestations: Successful past influence, financial danger, legal disadvantage, and inadequate legal influence.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's protective strand and ABC's criminal force/power remain distinct source associations; the foreign Mars paragraph is excluded.

## Contradictions

Witte's protective escape and ABC's adverse legal or financial conditions differ in emphasis. The entries supply no selection rule or claim that protection removes every disadvantage.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through possible happiness and protection within difficult power relations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives happiness despite dependence, protection that averts danger, and escape from harmful attempts. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also retains successful past influence and private joy, while describing financial danger, legal disadvantage, and criminal force or power. Relief remains possible without erasing the source's adverse material and legal range.
