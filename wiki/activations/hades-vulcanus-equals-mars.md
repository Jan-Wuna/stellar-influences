## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Mars row. The love/sexual-strength paragraph previously attached here belongs to Venus.

Unjust and unfair treatment. To be exposed to viciousness, meanness and hostility on account of an activity. Great destruction. Mighty acts of war. Rebelling against oppression.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 446 (one-based PDF p. 222), Mars row.

Influential activity in the past, dangerous violent actions, secret energy impulses, violent activity in secret, enormous disadvantages at work, lack of working energy, and inadequate working strength.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Activity encounters injustice, destructive force, resistance, or insufficient working energy.
- Psychology: Hostility is encountered in connection with activity; no fixed motive for violence is established.
- Body/health: Working-energy deficiency is not a specific medical diagnosis; sexual weakness belongs to the neighboring Venus row.
- Social/relationship: Unfair treatment and rebellion against oppression.
- Events/manifestations: Dangerous actions, hidden energy impulses, and major work disadvantages are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: Destructive action, suffering injustice, and rebellion are distinct positions; none establishes a person's guilt by itself.

## Contradictions

The entries include harmful force exercised, injustice suffered, and resistance to oppression. These alternatives do not describe one inevitable course of action.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through action under injustice or harmful force. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names unfair treatment, hostility arising around activity, destruction or war, and rebellion against oppression. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds concealed violent action, work disadvantages, and deficient working energy. Harmful action, exposure to it, and resistance remain separate expressions of this difficult field of activity.
