## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 285.

Feeling of dependence. To be dependent on natural forces.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 446; PDF p. 222.

My influence from the past, my lack of energy, my dangerous influence, my lack of strength, my lack of power, secret power of the soul, my enormous disadvantages, psychological influence of the past, psychological sorrow through violence.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A personal feeling of dependence and insufficient power ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Experienced dependence, the psychological influence of the past, sorrow through violence, and secret power of the soul.
- Body/health: No specific bodily disease is established.
- Social/relationship: Witte specifies dependence on natural forces rather than a particular social hierarchy.
- Events/manifestations: Subjection to natural conditions, personal lack of power, dangerous influence, and enormous disadvantages.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's energy deficiency and disadvantages have been checked against its German row; neither abundant energy nor night work is stated.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize dependence and lack of power, while ABC also names secret psychological power and personal dangerous influence. They do not explain how these alternatives relate in an individual case.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = MC`, MC specifies the Hades/Vulcanus axis as personally experienced dependence and limited agency. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) expressly names dependence on natural forces; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds deficient energy, strength and power, enormous disadvantages, and psychological sorrow through violence. Its references to past influence and secret power of the soul also give the formula an inward dimension: the experience of power can remain hidden even where outward capacity is restricted. The passages do not establish a particular career or predetermined destiny.
