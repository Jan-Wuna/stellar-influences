## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Moon row. The bodily contamination paragraph previously attached here belongs to Sun. “Viscous” is printed in the source's character list.

Woman weakened by sickness. Weakened people without courage. Low, fiendish, brutal, viscous and uncultured people. People inclined toward violence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 446 (one-based PDF p. 222), Moon row.

Influence of a criminal woman, influence of the past on a people, lack of energy of a woman, enormous disadvantages for a woman, grief for a people through violence, power of hidden feelings, lack of emotional strength, and hidden power of a woman.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Weakened people, collective grief, and the influence or force of hidden feeling in the sources' historical assignments.
- Psychology: ABC names a people's grief through violence, not a universal emotional pattern.
- Body/health: Witte's woman weakened by sickness and ABC's energy deficiency are attributed associations, not diagnoses or rules about women.
- Social/relationship: The entries assign weakness, violence, disadvantage, or hidden influence to people or women without establishing inherent traits.
- Events/manifestations: Past influence on a people, grief through violence, hidden feeling or power, and deficient emotional strength.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's printed “viscous” remains unexplained. Moral and criminal labels are attributed; the former Sun paragraph is excluded.

## Contradictions

Weakness, deficient emotional strength, and inclination toward violence are different descriptions. Neither source establishes that illness causes brutality or that its gendered claims apply to all women.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through the condition and influence of people or women in the historical entries. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes weakened or discouraged people alongside moral descriptions of violence. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds collective grief, energy or emotional deficiency, and hidden feeling or power. Vulnerability and harmful influence remain distinct source possibilities; neither illness nor gender establishes a person's character.
