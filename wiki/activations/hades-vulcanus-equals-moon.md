## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be physically dirty, to have lice, vermin. The body that is infected and weakened from within. Days filled with obstacles. To be compelled to a life of dependency.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influence of a criminal woman, influence of a people, lack of energy of a woman, enormous disadvantages for a woman, grief for a people through violence, power of hidden refugees, lack of power of a woman, and lost power of a woman

## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Vulcanus = Moon`: the body and emotional life being worn down by harsh conditions. It can show contamination, weakness, and a daily rhythm governed by obstacles and dependency.
