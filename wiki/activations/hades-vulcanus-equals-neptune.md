## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Neptune row. The sudden oppression/murder paragraph previously attached here belongs to Uranus.

To be powerless against meanness, wickedness and other evil things. Dissolution, the end of brute force.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 447 (one-based PDF p. 222), Neptune row.

Influence of polluted air, disappointing influence from the past, dangerous force of air and water, great danger from the air, disadvantageous weakness of power, uncertainty through violent crime, and hidden influence through sensitivity.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Powerlessness, weakening or dissolution of brute force, and uncertain or environmental danger.
- Psychology: Uncertainty is present in ABC; no enduring personality trait is established.
- Body/health: Polluted air is a source example of exposure, not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Being powerless against harmful acts; uncertainty through violent crime in ABC.
- Events/manifestations: Danger from air or water and hidden influence are ABC's additional settings.
- Conflicts/notes: The sudden murder example belongs to Uranus. ABC's disappointing past influence, physical force, disadvantageous weakness, and violent crime are restored from the scan.

## Contradictions

Powerlessness against harmful force and the end of brute force are different positions or outcomes. The entries do not establish that every danger is sudden or that every harmful power dissolves.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through powerlessness or the weakening and dissolution of force. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places inability to oppose harmful acts beside the end of brute force. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds weak power, uncertainty through violent crime, and hidden influence through sensitivity, while also giving polluted air and the dangerous force of air or water as concrete environmental expressions. Human power and environmental danger remain distinct parts of the range.
