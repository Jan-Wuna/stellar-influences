## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Node row. The weakened-people paragraph previously attached here belongs to Moon.

To face a danger directly. To have dealings with coarse and uncouth people. To be harassed by them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 446 (one-based PDF p. 222), Node row.

Bound up with the influence of the past, energy deficiency in connections, dangerous influence of a relationship, secret connections to power, tremendous disadvantages of a relationship, and sorrow through a connection to violence.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Connections expose people to harmful influence, disadvantage, or depleted energy ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 446).
- Psychology: Sorrow through a connection to violence; no fixed courage or character trait is supplied.
- Body/health: Energy deficiency is named without a specific bodily or medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Direct danger or harassment in dealings with others, and secret connections to power.
- Events/manifestations: Past influence, deficient energy in bonds, and major relational disadvantages are listed.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's coarse/uncouth descriptions remain attributed; the misplaced Moon paragraph and its gender/illness claims are excluded.

## Contradictions

Direct exposure to danger and secret links to power are different positions in a relationship. The entries do not establish that every difficult connection involves voluntary wrongdoing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Node`, Node specifies the pair through relationships that expose one to force or danger. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes directly facing danger and being harassed in dealings with others. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds dangerous relational influence, secret links to power, energy deficiency, and sorrow through violence. The common concern is the effect and risk of a connection, with past influence another explicit setting.
