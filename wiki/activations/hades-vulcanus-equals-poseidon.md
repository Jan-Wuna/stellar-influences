## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Poseidon row. The preceding community, crime, government, trade, and limited-scope paragraphs belong to Cupido, Zeus, Kronos, Apollon, and Admetos respectively.

To have a guilty conscience. To be depressed through ruling world outlook. Meditation on the past.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 447 (one-based PDF p. 222), Poseidon row.

Influence of past cultures, influence of hidden truths, spiritual strength through privation, spiritual power of the past, lack of spiritual energy, hidden force of an idea, reasonable renunciation of violence, and renunciation of violent ideas.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: The inward force of outlook, conscience, cultural inheritance, and hidden truth.
- Psychology: Guilty conscience, meditation on the past, spiritual strength through privation, and lack of spiritual energy.
- Body/health: Witte's depression through ruling world outlook is an attributed association, without a medical diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Past cultures and ideas carry influence; reasonable renunciation of violence is possible.
- Events/manifestations: Hidden force within an idea and relinquishing violent ideas.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's actual Poseidon row has three sentences. The preceding community and crime narratives are foreign to this orientation and have been excluded.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in the influence of outlook and the past. ABC preserves spiritual strength beside deficient energy and force beside its renunciation, without prescribing one outcome or explaining all guilt through violence.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through the inward influence of ideas, conscience, and the past. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names guilty conscience, depression through a ruling outlook, and meditation on the past. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds the influence of past cultures and hidden truths, spiritual strength through privation, and hidden force within an idea. Deficient spiritual energy and reasonable renunciation of violence remain beside those forms of influence, preserving both vulnerability and restraint.
