## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Saturn row. The happiness/protection paragraph previously attached here belongs to Jupiter.

To be separated through viciousness and meanness. To be hindered through same. To be under suspicion and spied upon. War services.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 447 (one-based PDF p. 222), Saturn row.

Dangerous problems of violence, concentration on hidden power, lack of strength through separation, grief and losses through violence, disadvantages due to loss of power, problematic energy deficiency, and disturbing influence from the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Separation, obstruction, scrutiny, and lost strength amid harmful force.
- Psychology: Grief and concentration on hidden power; suspicion is a circumstance rather than a diagnosis.
- Body/health: ABC's energy deficiency is not a specific medical diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Separation through harmful conduct, hindrance, suspicion, and spying.
- Events/manifestations: War services, losses through violence, loss of power, and disturbing past influence.
- Conflicts/notes: Protection and happiness belong to Jupiter's row; the actual Saturn and ABC qualifications are restored from the scans.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize related forms of impediment and depleted strength. They do not identify every separation as punitive or establish a necessary duration of endurance.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through separation and impediment under harmful conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names obstruction through vicious conduct, suspicion or spying, and war services. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds strength lost through separation, grief and losses through violence, lost power, and disturbing influence from the past. These connect restrictions in contact or activity with diminished capacity, while leaving their precise circumstances unspecified.
