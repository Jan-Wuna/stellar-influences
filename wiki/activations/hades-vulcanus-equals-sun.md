## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Sun row. The implication-through-others paragraph previously attached here completes Asc's row.

To be physically dirty, to have lice, vermin. The body that is infected and weakened from within. Days filled with obstacles. To be compelled to a life of dependency.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 446 (one-based PDF p. 222), Sun row.

Influence of a criminal man, dangerous personal influence, enormous bodily disadvantages, bodily energy deficiency, a person's grief through violence, lack of bodily strength, a hidden power center, and a center for the influence of the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Bodily weakness, difficult personal conditions, dependency, and personal influence under danger.
- Psychology: ABC names personal grief through violence; no motive for initiating harm is established.
- Body/health: Lice, infection, internal weakness, and low bodily energy are historical source associations, not diagnoses or predictions.
- Social/relationship: Compelled dependency; ABC's criminal-man wording is attributed, not an allegation about a person.
- Events/manifestations: Obstructed days, dangerous personal influence, and a hidden center of power or past influence.
- Conflicts/notes: The actual Sun row and all ABC qualifiers are restored from scans. The entries give no mechanism linking moral status with bodily illness.

## Contradictions

The entries include suffering weakness or dependency and exercising dangerous influence as different positions. They do not establish that an ill or dependent person is criminal.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through difficult personal and bodily conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names obstructed days and compelled dependency alongside its historical contamination and weakness claims. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds bodily energy deficiency, grief through violence, and dangerous personal or hidden power. These preserve both vulnerability and harmful influence as source possibilities, without making illness evidence of personal guilt.
