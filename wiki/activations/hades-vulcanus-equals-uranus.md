## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Uranus row. Its two symbols are alternative Uranus glyphs; the separation/suspicion paragraph previously attached here belongs to Saturn.

Sudden heavy oppression. Sudden terrible murder.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 447 (one-based PDF p. 222), Uranus row.

Influential impulses from the past, dangerous renewal of power, criminal reform of power, grief through surprising violence, surprising criminal influence, restlessness through lack of energy, and hidden technical energy.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden oppression or violent danger, with surprising changes in power or influence.
- Psychology: Grief through surprising violence and restlessness through deficient energy in ABC.
- Body/health: Neither usable passage gives a specific medical manifestation.
- Social/relationship: Sudden heavy oppression and surprising criminal influence are attributed source associations.
- Events/manifestations: Witte's extreme murder example, dangerous power renewal, criminal reform, and hidden technical energy.
- Conflicts/notes: Extreme violence remains historical doctrine, not a prediction or allegation. The Saturn paragraph is excluded and ABC's surprise/energy clauses restored.

## Contradictions

The entries share sudden or surprising harmful influence, while technical energy is a separate expression. They do not require every unexpected change to involve personal violence.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden harmful force or surprising changes in power. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives extreme historical examples of sudden oppression and murder. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens the range to dangerous power renewal, surprising criminal influence, grief through unexpected violence, and restlessness through lack of energy. Hidden technical energy remains a distinct nonpersonal expression in that entry.
