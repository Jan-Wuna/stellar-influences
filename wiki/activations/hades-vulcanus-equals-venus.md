## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Venus row. The news/writings paragraph previously attached here belongs to Mercury.

Repulsiveness in love. Weakness in sexual strength.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 446 (one-based PDF p. 222), Venus row.

Influential service in love, mysterious strength of love, sorrow through violence in love, artistic influence from the past, influence of love in adversity, lack of harmonious influence, hidden influence of art, and renunciation of violence in love.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Difficulty or force within love, alongside the influence of love and art and renunciation of violence.
- Psychology: ABC names sorrow through force in love, alongside love's mysterious strength.
- Body/health: Witte's sexual weakness is historical source language, not a diagnosis or judgment on a person's sexuality.
- Social/relationship: Renunciation of violence in love is an explicit ABC possibility.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic influence from the past, love in adversity, and hidden artistic influence.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's evaluative love claim remains attributed. ABC's influential service and omitted love/art clauses are restored from the scan.

## Contradictions

Sexual weakness and the strength of love are different expressions of capacity. ABC also includes violence renounced as well as sorrow through it, without selecting one relationship outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through the difficulty, strength, or influence of love and art. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives its historical claims of repulsiveness in love and sexual weakness. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds love's mysterious strength and influence in adversity, artistic influence from the past, and lack of harmony. Sorrow through violence and renunciation of violence remain distinct possibilities within that relational range.
