## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 285, Zeus row. The community paragraph previously attached here belongs to Cupido.

Meanness, malignancy and evil deeds born out of hate or a weak will.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 447 (one-based PDF p. 222), Zeus row.

Beginning of enormous disadvantages, dangerous violence of fire, criminal force of fire, grief through enormous fire, lack of energetic achievement, adverse efforts of power, hidden creative force, past capacity for achievement, and renunciation of leadership power.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Harmful deeds and dangerous force, with deficient achievement and renunciation of leadership power also represented.
- Psychology: Hate or weak will in Witte's attributed moral account; grief through fire in ABC.
- Body/health: Fire is a source example of harm, without a medical diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Renunciation of leadership power; no specific family or communal unit is named in the corrected Witte row.
- Events/manifestations: Dangerous or criminal fire, hidden creative force, past capacity for achievement, and adverse power efforts.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's moral language is historical attribution, not an assessment of a person. ABC's creation and past-performance clauses are restored from the scan.

## Contradictions

Harmful deeds, deficient capacity, hidden creative force, and renunciation of power are different expressions. The entries give no conditions selecting among them or establishing planned intimidation as their common motive.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Vulcanus = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair through action and force under adverse conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) attributes harmful deeds to hate or weak will. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds dangerous fire, grief, deficient energetic achievement, and adverse power efforts, while retaining hidden creative force, past capacity, and renunciation of leadership power. The range includes both harmful exertion and a limit or relinquishment of power.
