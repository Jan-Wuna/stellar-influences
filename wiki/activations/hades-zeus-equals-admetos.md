## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 282; PDF p. 278.

Trying to find recreation together with few. Small groups fighting each other. Coal mines. Deep seated hate. Irreconcilable hate. Hate among a few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 439; PDF p. 218.

Strenuous deepening into what is hidden, disadvantage through restricted performance, consolidation through hidden leadership, ending leadership service, mysterious restriction of performance, and resistance to fire-related crime.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Restricted performance and a small, enclosed field of activity; deep-rooted hostility is one expression.
- Psychology: Witte names deep-seated, irreconcilable hate; ABC adds strenuous exploration of what is hidden.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: Recreation among a few, conflict between small groups, and hidden leadership.
- Events/manifestations: Coal mines, limited performance, ending leadership service, and resistance to fire-related crime.
- Conflicts/notes: The mining and recreation meanings remain alongside conflict; they are not evidence that every small group is hostile.

## Contradictions

Recreation among a few and conflict among a few are different possibilities in Witte's entry. ABC emphasizes restriction and hidden leadership; it does not resolve when the hostile expression applies.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Zeus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the Hades/Zeus axis through a narrowed field of activity and restricted performance. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) places recreation and fighting among small groups beside coal mines and deeply rooted hate. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds strenuous exploration of what is hidden, consolidation through hidden leadership, and the ending or restriction of leadership service. The common thread is concentrated activity under limits; hostility is a distinctive possibility, while recreation, mining, and resistance to fire-related crime retain their separate meanings.
