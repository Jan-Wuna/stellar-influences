## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 282; PDF p. 278.

Destructive fire of a company, or a fire involving many people. In enmity with unions or associations, to suffer from their antagonism. To attack or hate communities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 439; PDF p. 218.

Strenuous community service, a community's dangerous objectives, shared leadership service, community achievements from the past, joint renunciation of procreation, a community's deficient performance, and a criminal leadership community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: The work, aims, deficiencies, or antagonisms of a community or association.
- Psychology: Witte names hatred of communities and suffering through their antagonism.
- Body/health: Fire involving many is named; no specific bodily condition is established.
- Social/relationship: Unions, associations, common leadership service, and joint renunciation of procreation.
- Events/manifestations: Company fire, collective fire damage, strenuous community service, past achievements, and deficient or criminal leadership.
- Conflicts/notes: Community service and collective hostility remain separate possibilities; neither source makes every association harmful.

## Contradictions

ABC includes service and past community achievements alongside dangerous objectives and deficient leadership. Witte emphasizes antagonism and fire; the difference of emphasis does not erase the service meanings.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Zeus = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the Hades/Zeus axis through communities, companies, and associations. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes fire involving a company or many people, together with antagonism between a person and an organized group. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also gives the community a working role: strenuous service, shared leadership, and achievements inherited from the past. Dangerous objectives and deficient or criminal leadership are adverse expressions within that collective field; joint renunciation of procreation remains a separate listed meaning.
