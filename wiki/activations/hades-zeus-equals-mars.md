## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 281; PDF p. 277.

End of vacation. Starting new laborious work. Very dangerous acute illness or bad injury. A deed born out of hate. Strikers (work). Target practice. Battle actions. Fires, conflagrations. Fire taking toll of lives. To die by premeditate homicide.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 438; PDF p. 218.

Active service at a fire, leadership activity in the past, dangerous work with fire, inadequate leadership impulses, disadvantageous leadership actions, activity in leadership service, hidden leadership impulses, and renunciation of efforts toward procreation.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Laborious work, hazardous action, and leadership service.
- Psychology: Witte explicitly includes deeds born of hate.
- Body/health: Witte lists acute illness, injury and fatal violence as historical possibilities, not diagnoses.
- Social/relationship: Strikers, battle actions, and leadership activity.
- Events/manifestations: End of vacation, new hard work, target practice, fire service, and conflagration.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC renounces procreative efforts; it does not assert infertility.

## Contradictions


Returning to hard work and serving at a fire are practical meanings distinct from the hateful or violent actions in Witte's entry.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Zeus = Mars`, Mars specifies the Hades/Zeus axis through work, action, and direct engagement with fire or conflict. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) ranges from returning to hard work and labor strikes to target practice, battle, and destructive fire. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also describes active service at a fire and leadership actions, including inadequate or hidden impulses. Effort and hazardous service unify the practical meanings; Witte's severe illness, injury and homicide claims remain attributed possibilities rather than inevitable outcomes.
