## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 281; PDF p. 277.

Scoffing or hateful remarks. Hateful way of thinking. Defamations, calumniation. Denouncing. Propaganda with lies. Arson. To catch fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 438; PDF p. 218.

Strenuous transport service, reports about achievements of the past, fire-related crime among young people, leading language of the past, discussing dangerous goals, secret leadership conversations, deficient guidance of thought, and news about dangerous fire.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Communication concerning hostile aims, leadership, past achievements, or dangerous fire.
- Psychology: Hateful thinking and deficient guidance of thought.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: Scoffing, defamation, denunciation, propaganda, and secret leadership conversations.
- Events/manifestations: Transport service, historical reports, fire news, arson, and catching fire.
- Conflicts/notes: Reporting danger differs from producing hateful propaganda or causing fire.

## Contradictions


ABC's reports and transport service are broader than Witte's malicious speech; communication about harm is not equivalent to causing it.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Zeus = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the Hades/Zeus axis through speech, thought, reporting, and transport service. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes hostile remarks, defamation, denunciation and lying propaganda, while the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) includes transport service, reports about past achievements, discussions of dangerous goals and secret leadership conversations. Communication is the common field, but a report about fire or a difficult service task should not be conflated with malicious speech or arson.
