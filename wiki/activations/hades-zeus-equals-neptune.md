## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 281; PDF p. 277.

Complete relaxation. Not able to pull oneself together. Impotent rage and hate. Futile outbreak of hatred. To be scalded by steam. To be asphyxiated by smoke. Smog. Choke damp.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 439; PDF p. 218.

Efforts against air-related crime, fire danger from gas, sorrow through weakness in procreation, producing air pollution, inadequate efforts for the future, disadvantages from weak performance, weak services, and unknown criminal leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Relaxation, weak performance, and ineffective hostile activity.
- Psychology: Inability to pull oneself together, impotent rage, and futile hate in Witte.
- Body/health: Witte names steam scalding and smoke asphyxiation; ABC adds procreative weakness, as historical claims.
- Social/relationship: Weak services and unknown criminal leadership.
- Events/manifestations: Gas fire danger, air pollution, smog, choke damp, and efforts against air-related crime.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's final developing-services/fire clauses belonged to Pluto and have been removed from this row.

## Contradictions


Complete relaxation and ineffective rage are different states in Witte; the entry does not explain their relation to its atmospheric hazards.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Zeus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the Hades/Zeus axis through relaxation or ineffective action, with gas, smoke, and polluted air among its hazards. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) places complete relaxation beside inability to pull oneself together and futile rage. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) similarly emphasizes weak service and deficient effort, while both entries bring the danger into an atmospheric field: gas fire, polluted air, smoke, steam and choke damp. These are several listed expressions, not a claim that relaxation causes toxic exposure. The severe bodily and reproductive statements remain historical source claims.
