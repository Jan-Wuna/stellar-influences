## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 281; PDF p. 277.

To participate in large fires or damages. Connection with the industry or industrial class (industrial workers). Connection with strikes. To witness a great destructive fire. To be connected with meanness and hatefulness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 438; PDF p. 218.

A dangerous fire connection, dangerous goals of a connection, criminal links to leadership, a creative connection to the past, an inadequate connection to leadership, a hidden procreative connection, and renunciation of a procreative connection.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Participation or association with difficult work, dangerous aims, or fire.
- Psychology: Connections with meanness and hatefulness in Witte's wording.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: Industrial workers, strikes, links to leadership, and procreative connections.
- Events/manifestations: Witnessing or participating in major fire or damage; creative connection to the past.
- Conflicts/notes: Witnessing an event does not establish causing it; renunciation differs from a hidden connection.

## Contradictions


ABC adds creative and reproductive connections to Witte's industrial and fire associations; these are additional domains, not interchangeable relations.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Zeus = Node`, Node specifies the Hades/Zeus axis through connections to industry, collective action, leadership, and dangerous events. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes connections with industrial workers and strikes, as well as witnessing or participating in destructive fire and damage. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds dangerous goals, criminal or inadequate links to leadership, and a creative connection to the past. Involvement is the organizing theme; it can mean association or witnessing rather than responsibility for harm. Hidden and renounced procreative connections remain distinct ABC possibilities.
