## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 282; PDF p. 278.

Want of intellectual endeavors. Indifferent in mental sphere. Hate in an ideological way.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 439; PDF p. 218.

Insightful leadership service, insights into fire-related crime, leading cultural service, intellectual achievements of the past, lack of intellectual goal striving, lack of intellectual leadership, hidden intellectual leadership, and past cultural achievements.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Intellectual or cultural effort, deficient direction, and ideological hostility.
- Psychology: Mental indifference or ideological hate in Witte; insight in ABC.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: Intellectual leadership and leading cultural service.
- Events/manifestations: Insights into fire-related crime and intellectual or cultural achievements from the past.
- Conflicts/notes: Insight and cultural service remain alongside indifference; rationalized contempt is not explicitly stated.

## Contradictions


ABC's insightful and cultural service extends beyond Witte's mental indifference and ideological hate; neither emphasis cancels the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Zeus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the Hades/Zeus axis through intellectual aims, ideas, cultural work, and their leadership. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) names lack of intellectual endeavor, mental indifference and ideological hate. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) retains a broader range: insightful leadership, cultural service, past intellectual achievements, and leadership that may be deficient or hidden. The formula concerns the direction and quality of intellectual work; it includes constructive insight as well as disengagement or hostility, without establishing a motive of rationalization.
