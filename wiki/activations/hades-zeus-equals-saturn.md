## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 281; PDF p. 277.

Hate and enmity for a long time. Losses and destructions through fire, arson or war. Slow destructive hate.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 439; PDF p. 218.

Shaping tasks from the past, more difficult efforts in old age, concentration on hidden goals, problematic leadership service, a hidden leadership task, and losses through lack of goal striving.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Enduring enmity and difficult leadership or task conditions.
- Psychology: Long-lasting hate and concentration on hidden goals.
- Body/health: ABC mentions harder effort in old age; no specific diagnosis is stated.
- Social/relationship: Problematic leadership service and a hidden leadership task.
- Events/manifestations: Loss through fire, arson, war, or insufficient goal striving.
- Conflicts/notes: Past task experience and hidden goals add scope beyond Witte's prolonged enmity.

## Contradictions


ABC's tasks, hidden goals and difficult effort in old age are broader than Witte's long enmity and destruction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Zeus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the Hades/Zeus axis through persistent hostility, difficult tasks, and impaired goal-directed effort. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes long-standing enmity and destruction through fire, arson or war. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds difficult effort in old age, tasks shaped by the past, hidden goals, and problematic leadership. The organizing theme is sustained difficulty in action or purpose; the losses may be connected to hostile events or to a lack of goal striving, without making every difficult task hateful.
