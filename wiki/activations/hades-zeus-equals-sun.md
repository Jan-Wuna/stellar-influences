## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 281; PDF p. 277.

Injury to the body by burns. To be hindered in work on account of physical defects, sickness or being crippled. The man who is subject to hate, who is detested. Burnt objects. Remainders from a fire such as soot, carbonization, ashes. Dirt through reconstruction.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 438; PDF p. 218.

Dangerous personal objectives, a dangerous fire center, disadvantages through leading persons, personal leadership service, deficient personal performance, a hidden leadership center, and a man's renunciation of procreation.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal performance, leadership service, and impediments to work.
- Psychology: Witte describes a man who is hated; ABC names dangerous personal goals.
- Body/health: Witte's burns, sickness and physical defects remain historical medical statements.
- Social/relationship: Disadvantages through leading persons, personal service, and a man's renunciation of procreation.
- Events/manifestations: Burnt objects, soot, ashes, reconstruction dirt, and hidden leadership centers.
- Conflicts/notes: The German procreation renunciation does not mean general forbearance or infertility.

## Contradictions


ABC emphasizes personal leadership and performance, while Witte emphasizes physical impediment and material fire aftermath.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Zeus = Sun`, Sun specifies the Hades/Zeus axis through personal activity, bodily impediments, and the material aftermath of fire. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) places bodily burns and impeded work beside burnt objects, soot, ashes and reconstruction dirt. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds personal leadership service, deficient achievement, dangerous objectives and disadvantages through leading persons. The formula therefore concerns personal participation and its practical or bodily limits, with literal fire aftermath retained as a distinctive meaning. The historical health and gender statements do not establish a diagnosis or universal male experience.
