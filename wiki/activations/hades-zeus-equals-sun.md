## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Injury to the body by burns. To be hindered in work on account of physical defects, sickness or being crippled. The man who is subject to hate, who is detested. Burnt objects. Remainders from a fire such as soot, carbonization, ashes. Dirt through reconstruction.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous objective, dangerous fire centre, disadvantages caused by guidance persons, personal guidance service, lack of personal performance, hidden guidance centre, and man's forbearance
