## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 281; PDF p. 277.

Suddenly befalling heavy sufferings and harm. Horrors and murderous acts. Crimes. To be surprised by fire. Conflagration. Sudden outbreaks of fanatic hate.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 439; PDF p. 218.

Agitation over inadequate leadership, inadequate technical services, disadvantages through new leadership, sudden fire danger, reform efforts against crime, an unexpected lack of performance, and unrest over dangerous objectives.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Abrupt danger or deficient performance, with reform also possible.
- Psychology: Agitation, unrest, and sudden outbreaks of fanatic hate.
- Body/health: Witte names severe suffering and murderous acts as historical possibilities.
- Social/relationship: Inadequate or new leadership and efforts to reform crime.
- Events/manifestations: Sudden fire, conflagration, deficient technical service, and unexpected performance failure.
- Conflicts/notes: Reform against crime differs from criminal action; sudden danger is not an inevitable event.

## Contradictions


ABC's reform efforts against crime are constructive activity alongside the sudden harm and criminal acts emphasized by Witte.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Zeus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the Hades/Zeus axis through sudden harm, fire danger, and disruption in leadership or performance. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes sudden suffering, fire and outbreaks of fanatic hate. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) makes the working conditions more specific through inadequate technical service, new or deficient leadership, unexpected performance failure and agitation over dangerous objectives. It also names reform efforts against crime. Sudden disruption unifies the range, while participation in reform remains distinct from causing the harm.
