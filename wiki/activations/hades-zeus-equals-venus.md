## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 281; PDF p. 277.

To be forced into menial service. Ugly way of acting by female persons. Injury to female person by fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 438; PDF p. 218.

Strenuous service in love, the beginning of a dangerous love, beginning sorrow in love, secret love goals, lack of striving for harmony, traditional celebratory fire, renunciation of peace efforts, and a preference for achievements of the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Difficult love or service and deficient efforts toward harmony.
- Psychology: Sorrow in love, secret love goals, and preference for past achievements.
- Body/health: Witte names fire injury to a woman as historical doctrine.
- Social/relationship: Menial service, dangerous love, and renunciation of peace efforts.
- Events/manifestations: The beginning of love troubles and traditional celebratory fire.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's judgment of female conduct remains attributed; celebratory fire is distinct from injury.

## Contradictions


ABC's celebratory fire and preference for past achievements differ from Witte's injury and compulsory service.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Zeus = Venus`, Venus specifies the Hades/Zeus axis through love, harmony, service, and specific fire imagery. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes compulsory menial service and historical gendered claims about conduct and fire injury. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) specifies strenuous service in love, dangerous or secret aims, sorrow, and a failure to strive for harmony or peace. Its traditional celebratory fire and preference for past achievements preserve meanings beyond conflict. The formula connects difficult service and affection without making every relationship coercive.
