## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 282; PDF p. 278.

Decrease of activity. Mighty efforts to do away with unemployment. Increase of lowness and vulgarity. Violent outbreak of hate.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 439; PDF p. 218.

efforts against violent criminality, serving leadership force, dangerous leadership influence, dangerous power goals, tremendous fire danger, lack of energetic performance, lack of leadership influence, strong influence of the past, and hidden leadership power

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Powerful effort can confront unemployment or violent crime, while activity may also decrease.
- Psychology: Witte names violent outbreaks of hate.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: Serving leadership strength, dangerous leadership influence, and hidden leadership power.
- Events/manifestations: Efforts against unemployment and violent crime; major fire danger and deficient energy performance.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's decrease of activity and ABC's lack of power prevent a uniformly forceful reading.

## Contradictions


Reduced activity and energy deficiency coexist in the entries with powerful efforts against unemployment or crime; a uniformly strong expression is not established.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Hades/Zeus = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Hades/Zeus axis through forceful efforts, reduced activity, and the use or deficiency of leadership power. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) contrasts decreased activity with mighty efforts against unemployment, alongside a violent outbreak of hate. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) similarly includes efforts against violent crime and serving leadership strength, as well as dangerous power goals, fire danger and deficient energy or influence. The formula concerns the effort and power brought to difficult conditions, including their insufficiency; strength need not be expressed as hatred.
