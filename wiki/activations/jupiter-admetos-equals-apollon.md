## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 220; PDF p. 216.

Lucky through contentment. Many people close together, but happy with each other.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 343; PDF p. 170.

Continued increase of money, restricted money trading, restricted legal success, success through thorough knowledge, consolidation of commercial law, abundance of money in circulation, thorough legal knowledge, and increasing blockage of money.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Contentment in close company; financial circulation under limits.
- Psychology: Happiness with one another and contentment.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Many people close together; trade and commercial law.
- Events/manifestations: Money growth or blockage, restricted trade, and success through knowledge.
- Conflicts/notes: The financial range is mixed; togetherness does not establish material prosperity.

## Contradictions

Witte's collective contentment and ABC's financial restrictions are compatible but distinct themes; neither supplies conditions for choosing a financial outcome.


<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the Jupiter/Admetos axis through contentment among many and the expansion or restriction of financial and legal activity. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes contented people gathered closely together. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds a practical range in which money may increase or remain blocked, trade and legal success may be restricted, and thorough knowledge can secure success. The formula holds happiness within close conditions alongside the management of resources and rights.
