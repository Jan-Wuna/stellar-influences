## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 220; PDF p. 216.

Small closed community, small art circle. Art understood only by a few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 343; PDF p. 170.

Blocked community rights, success of a confined community, successful consolidation of a community, fixed funds of a community, consolidation of shared rights, financial restriction in communities, and shared circulation of money.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Limited groups can consolidate successfully despite restricted finances.
- Psychology: Neither entry gives a separate psychological description.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Small communities and art circles, shared rights and money.
- Events/manifestations: Art understood by a few, fixed community funds, and financial restriction.
- Conflicts/notes: A small audience is not evidence of artistic failure or superiority.

## Contradictions

ABC's legal and financial restrictions add practical limits to Witte's small-group art meanings.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the Jupiter/Admetos axis through a small or restricted community, including its art, resources, and rights. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) specifies a closed community or small art circle whose art is understood by few. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds successful consolidation, shared funds and rights, and financial restriction. The unifying field is a limited group sustaining its activity and resources; the sources preserve both success and constraint without requiring broad popularity.
