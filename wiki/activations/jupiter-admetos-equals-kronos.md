## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 220; PDF p. 216.

Expenses. Great spending of money. High financial burden. Raw material deposit.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 343; PDF p. 170.

Blocked legal authority, blocked state funds, a restricted state governed by law, consolidation of the state governed by law, a major money blockade, state circulation of money, deepening into constitutional law, and important success through perseverance.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Large expenditure and legal or state-resource restrictions.
- Psychology: Neither entry describes a distinct psychological state.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Legal authority and the constitutional or law-governed state.
- Events/manifestations: High expenses, raw-material deposits, blocked state funds, and success through perseverance.
- Conflicts/notes: A raw-material deposit is a distinct source meaning; wealth or steadiness is not assured.

## Contradictions

Witte's expenses and raw-material holding are more concrete than ABC's state-law range; the entries do not establish comfortable wealth.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the Jupiter/Admetos axis through substantial financial burdens and the state's legal or financial structures. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) names great spending, a high financial burden, and a raw-material deposit. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) specifies blocked state funds and legal authority, but also consolidation of the law-governed state and important success through perseverance. The formula concerns substantial resources and their legal constraints, preserving both burdens and the possibility of consolidation.
