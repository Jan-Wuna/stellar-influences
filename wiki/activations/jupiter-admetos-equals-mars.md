## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: printed p. 220 (one-based PDF p. 216), Mars row.

Successful work in a special sphere. Very active.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 342 (one-based PDF p. 170), Mars row. The previously attached “See above” belongs to the following Jupiter row.

Work with fixed funds, blocked financial activity, depression through legal work, restriction of successful work, successful immersion in work, consolidation of joy through work, joy in earthwork, and thorough joy in work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Active, successful work in a special sphere, with both deep engagement and restrictions represented.
- Psychology: Joy consolidated through work, joy in earthwork, and thorough enjoyment of work; ABC also uses depression through legal work.
- Body/health: No specific bodily illness or diagnosis. ABC does not clarify whether depression denotes an emotional or economic condition here.
- Social/relationship: No explicit interpersonal statement; legal and financial work provide occupational contexts.
- Events/manifestations: Work with fixed funds, blocked financial activity, restrictions on successful work, and successful immersion in work.
- Conflicts/notes: The eight-clause ABC Mars row is substantive; the neighboring Jupiter cross-reference supplies no Mars meaning. Success and enjoyment are not the only outcomes named.

## Contradictions

Witte's successful, vigorous specialized work stands beside ABC's blocks and restrictions as well as its success and joy. The entries give no conditions selecting among these possibilities, and ABC's depression phrase remains undefined.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through vigorous work in a special sphere and a capacity to become deeply engaged in it. Work can consolidate joy, including the specific example of earthwork. The financial and legal branch also includes fixed funds, blocked financial activity, and restrictions on otherwise successful work, so specialized effort does not imply an unrestricted outcome. ABC's phrase depression through legal work remains attributed and does not establish a diagnosis. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
