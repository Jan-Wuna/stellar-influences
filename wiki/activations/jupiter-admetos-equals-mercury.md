## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: printed p. 220 (one-based PDF p. 216), Mercury row.

Quiet and profound thinking. Talking about the limited financial situation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 342 (one-based PDF p. 170), Mercury row. The love, art, and harmony paragraph previously attached here belongs to the following Venus row.

Restricted traffic rights, successful deepening of conversation, thoughts about fixed funds, thorough reports of success, thorough legal conversations, conversations about financial restrictions, and news about a legal blockade.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Quiet, profound thought and sustained conversation, particularly about limited finances and legal matters.
- Psychology: Quiet and profound thinking; successful deepening of conversation.
- Body/health: No explicit bodily or health statement in these Mercury entries.
- Social/relationship: Conversation rather than a specific relationship outcome is named.
- Events/manifestations: Reports of success, legal discussions, thoughts about fixed funds, discussion of financial restrictions, and news of legal blocks.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's actual Mercury row concerns thought, reports, conversation, and legal or financial limits. The neighboring Venus love/harmony row is excluded.

## Contradictions

The two entries overlap in thought and discussion of financial limits. ABC also gives successful deepening of conversation and reports of success; the entries do not state that limits are accepted or that stable sufficiency has been achieved.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through quiet, profound thinking and conversation that goes into a matter thoroughly. Financial limits and fixed funds are concrete subjects, joined in ABC by legal discussion, reports of success, and news of legal blocks. The depth of thought and conversation is supported; an attitude of accepting the limits is not specified. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
