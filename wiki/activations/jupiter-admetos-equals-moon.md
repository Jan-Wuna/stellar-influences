## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: printed p. 220 (one-based PDF p. 216), Moon row.

Women who feel happy in their seclusion. Happiness through composure and meditation. Small closed circle in the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 342 (one-based PDF p. 170), Moon row. The connection/funds paragraph previously attached here belongs to the following Node row.

Blocked emotional joy, success of a people through endurance, successes of a woman through thoroughness, successful deepening of feeling, financial restrictions for a people, circulation of money within a people, and thorough success of a woman.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Happiness in seclusion, composure, or a small circle, with success through endurance or thoroughness and possible emotional or financial restriction.
- Psychology: Happiness through composure and meditation, blocked emotional joy, and successful deepening of feeling.
- Body/health: No explicit bodily condition or diagnosis is stated.
- Social/relationship: Women happy in seclusion, a small closed circle in the public, and the source's collective success of a people.
- Events/manifestations: Success through endurance or thoroughness, financial restrictions for a people, and circulation of money within a people.
- Conflicts/notes: The restored seven-clause ABC Moon row excludes the following Node financial-connection paragraph. Quiet contentment and blocked joy are both represented.

## Contradictions

Witte's happiness in seclusion and ABC's blocked emotional joy are different possibilities. ABC also names successful deepening of feeling and collective financial restrictions; the entries do not give conditions selecting among them or state that a small space is protective.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through happiness found in composure, meditation, seclusion, or a small closed circle. ABC connects success with endurance and thoroughness and includes deepened feeling, while also retaining blocked emotional joy. Its collective range includes financial restrictions and money circulating within a people, so quiet contentment does not exhaust the entry. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), [ABC entry](#abc-entry))
