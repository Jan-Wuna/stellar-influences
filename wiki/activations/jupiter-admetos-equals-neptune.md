## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 220; PDF p. 216.

Wasted money. Wrong investment. Lost investment capital.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 343; PDF p. 170.

Disappointing blockage of rights, successful deepening into metaphysics, consolidation of success through sensitivity, happiness in deep water, thorough legal uncertainty, creeping financial restriction, uncertainty through blocked money, and deepening into foreign law.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Financial loss or uncertainty alongside metaphysical depth and sensitivity.
- Psychology: Disappointment and uncertainty about blocked money or rights.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Foreign law and blocked rights.
- Events/manifestations: Wrong investment, lost capital, gradual restriction, metaphysical study, and happiness in deep water.
- Conflicts/notes: Deep water and metaphysics are explicit meanings; imagined financial security is not stated.

## Contradictions

ABC's metaphysical success and sensitivity extend beyond Witte's exclusively adverse financial entry.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the Jupiter/Admetos axis through uncertain or restricted resources, with depth and sensitivity as additional routes to success. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) warns of wasted money, wrong investment and lost capital. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) similarly names disappointing legal blockage and creeping financial restriction, yet also gives success through metaphysical depth or sensitivity and happiness in deep water. Material uncertainty is central, but those uncommon constructive meanings remain separate supported possibilities.
