## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 220; PDF p. 216.

Feeling lucky through connection with few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 342; PDF p. 170.

Relationships to fixed funds, blocked legal connections, firm financial connections, restricted money contacts, successful thorough contacts, thorough financial connections, and connections to the circulation of money.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Benefit from select connections, with firmness or restriction in financial ties.
- Psychology: Feeling lucky through connection with a few in Witte.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Firm financial ties, blocked legal links, and thorough contacts.
- Events/manifestations: Connections to fixed funds or the circulation of money.
- Conflicts/notes: Few connections do not establish trust; financial ties may be restricted or successful.

## Contradictions

ABC's restricted legal or money contacts qualify the favorable experience of select ties described by Witte.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Node`, Node specifies the Jupiter/Admetos axis through connections with a few people and defined financial or legal ties. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) locates happiness in connection with a few. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) specifies financial and legal relations: fixed funds, firm or restricted contacts, and thorough connections that may succeed. The formula concerns the value and limits of a defined set of ties, without assuming that a small network is automatically trustworthy.
