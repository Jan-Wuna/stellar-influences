## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 220; PDF p. 216.

Deep reaching education. High standard of reason.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 343; PDF p. 170.

Restricted cultural rights, successful intellectual deepening, consolidation of cultural successes, consolidation of law and reason, joy in intellectual deepening, intellectual success through thoroughness, happiness through intellectual steadfastness, and thorough legal clarity.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Depth of education and thorough intellectual or legal understanding.
- Psychology: Joy in intellectual depth and happiness through steadfastness.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Cultural rights and consolidation of law and reason.
- Events/manifestations: Intellectual success, cultural achievements, and legal clarity.
- Conflicts/notes: Thoroughness is supported; age, patience and quick brilliance are not compared.

## Contradictions

ABC adds restricted cultural rights to Witte's favorable educational statement without denying the value of intellectual depth.


<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the Jupiter/Admetos axis through deep education, reason, and intellectual or cultural consolidation. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) joins deep-reaching education to a high standard of reason. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) develops that theme through thorough intellectual work, cultural achievement, legal clarity and happiness in mental steadfastness, while retaining restricted cultural rights. The formula emphasizes depth and reasoned consolidation rather than asserting a particular age or speed of learning.
