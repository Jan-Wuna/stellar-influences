## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 220; PDF p. 216.

To become involved in difficulties through spending money.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 343; PDF p. 170.

Depression through loss of rights, successful deepening through patience, disturbed circulation of money, restriction of profit through losses, painful financial restriction, loss of fixed funds, deepening into legal problems, and benefits in old age through perseverance.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Financial restriction and loss alongside success through patience.
- Psychology: Painful restriction and depression through lost rights are source wording.
- Body/health: Depression and old-age benefit remain attributed; no diagnosis is established.
- Social/relationship: Loss of rights and sustained engagement with legal problems.
- Events/manifestations: Spending difficulties, lost fixed funds, disturbed money circulation, and benefit through perseverance.
- Conflicts/notes: Patient success coexists with loss; the price of stability is not the stated cause.

## Contradictions

ABC's patience and perseverance provide constructive possibilities absent from Witte's short spending warning.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the Jupiter/Admetos axis through spending difficulties, financial loss, and patient work with legal limits. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) links difficulty directly to spending money. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds lost rights or fixed funds, reduced profits and disturbed money circulation, while preserving success through patience and benefits in old age through perseverance. The formula concerns constrained resources and the effort required to work through them, rather than a general rule that stability becomes costly.
