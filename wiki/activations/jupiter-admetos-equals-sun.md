## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 220; PDF p. 216.

Every day is proportioned with symmetry. The man who lives his days in tranquility on a retiring pension, annuity or his private income or old age security.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 342; PDF p. 170.

A blocked money center, a restricted legal center, success through bodily endurance, a successful strike day, financial restriction of a center, thorough success of a man, thorough personal rights, and personal circulation of money.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Regular, tranquil living alongside personal success and financial restriction.
- Psychology: Tranquility is explicit in Witte.
- Body/health: Bodily endurance is explicit; no disease is stated.
- Social/relationship: Witte's man on retirement income is a historical example.
- Events/manifestations: Pension, annuity or private income; successful strike day; blocked money center.
- Conflicts/notes: A quiet routine does not establish modest means or universal preference for peace.

## Contradictions

ABC's strike and blocked financial center extend beyond Witte's quiet retirement example.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Sun`, Sun specifies the Jupiter/Admetos axis through ordered daily living, bodily endurance, and personal financial or legal conditions. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes a proportioned daily routine and a man living tranquilly on pension, annuity or private income. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds bodily endurance, personal rights and success, a successful strike day, and restricted financial centers. The formula brings orderly personal activity together with its material provisions and limits; the retirement example remains one specific manifestation.
