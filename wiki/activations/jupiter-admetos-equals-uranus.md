## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 220; PDF p. 216.

Suddenly disturbed rest. Sudden grave property damage. Suddenly forced to spend much money.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 343; PDF p. 170.

Agitating blockage of rights, depression through a legal event, restrictive impulses affecting success, sudden deepening into law, reform for fixed funds, tensions through fixed funds, unexpected restriction of success, and unrest through blocked money.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Abrupt material disruption and restrictions on success.
- Psychology: Agitation, tension, unrest, and historically described depression.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition is established.
- Social/relationship: Legal events, blocked rights, and reform of fixed funds.
- Events/manifestations: Sudden property damage, forced large spending, and unexpected success restrictions.
- Conflicts/notes: Reform is listed alongside disruption; an explosive legal breakthrough is not stated.

## Contradictions

The sudden material costs in Witte are broader than ABC's legal and fixed-fund tensions; neither makes every reform damaging.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the Jupiter/Admetos axis through sudden disruption of rest, property, and financial or legal arrangements. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes rest suddenly interrupted by grave property damage or forced expenditure. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) places agitation and tension around blocked money or rights, unexpected limits on success, and reform of fixed funds. The common thread is disruption of an established material arrangement, with legal reform also retained as a possible response.
