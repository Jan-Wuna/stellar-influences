## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 220; PDF p. 216.

Rest and seclusion. Luck in general is confined. Production of raw materials.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 342; PDF p. 170.

Restriction of rights in public, financial restriction in public life, blocked money in public, circulation of money in public, successful strikes in public, and stabilization of public rights.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public restriction and stabilization, with rest or seclusion.
- Psychology: No separate psychological state is described.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Public rights, financial restrictions, and strike success.
- Events/manifestations: Raw-material production, public money blockage or circulation, and stabilization of rights.
- Conflicts/notes: Public sufficiency and reserves are not established by these entries.

## Contradictions

ABC adds public-rights and strike meanings to Witte's rest and raw-material range; neither establishes general prosperity.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Jupiter/Admetos axis through public financial or legal limits, rest, and raw-material production. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes rest and seclusion, confined luck, and raw-material production. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) specifies restrictions on public rights and finances, money blockage or circulation, successful strikes and stabilization of public rights. The formula concerns general conditions under limits, including both material production and legal or financial organization.
