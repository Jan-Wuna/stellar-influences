## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 220; PDF p. 216.

Mighty limitation of the luck. Great purchases.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 343; PDF p. 170.

Blocked financial power, depression through legal power, successful consolidation of power, consolidated legal power, thorough legal influence, beneficial influence of circulation, thorough advantage in power, consolidation of power through money, and advantage through energy restriction.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Strong limitation of luck alongside significant transactions and power consolidation.
- Psychology: ABC's depression through legal power remains attributed wording.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Legal power and influence strengthened by money.
- Events/manifestations: Great purchases, blocked financial power, successful consolidation, and benefit through restricted energy.
- Conflicts/notes: Money can consolidate power while luck remains limited; wealth is not guaranteed.

## Contradictions

Power consolidation in ABC can coexist with Witte's limited luck, but the entries do not explain when each expression predominates.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Admetos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Jupiter/Admetos axis through major purchases and the restriction or consolidation of financial and legal power. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) gives a strong limitation of luck and great purchases. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) sets blocked financial power beside successful consolidation of legal or monetary power and benefit through energy restriction. The formula concerns substantial material or legal force operating within limits; large transactions need not imply expanding freedom or uncomplicated prosperity.
