## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

Rest and contentment. Luck through science in small circle. Successful produce of raw material. Trade or sale in raw materials, natural products. Luck through raw material quantities. Decrease of money in circulation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 341; PDF p. 169.

Continued increase of money, limited money trading, blocked financial activity, restricted joy in legal matters, success through thorough knowledge, consolidation of commercial law, thorough legal knowledge, and deepening into financial science.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Contentment and specialized success alongside restricted circulation.
- Psychology: Rest and contentment.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Science pursued within a small circle.
- Events/manifestations: Raw-material production/trade, blocked financial activity, and thorough legal knowledge.
- Conflicts/notes: Limited circulation coexists with continued money increase; neither is universal.

## Contradictions

Witte's reduced circulation and ABC's continued money increase are different expressions; the entries do not say they must occur together.


<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through a limited working field, raw materials, and concentrated financial or scientific activity. Rest and contentment accompany work in a small scientific circle and successful production or trade in raw materials. The financial range includes both continuing increase and reduced circulation or blocked activity; thorough knowledge and deeper financial study provide a specific route to success. The organizing idea is productive concentration within limits, with actual raw materials retained rather than reduced to a metaphor. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
