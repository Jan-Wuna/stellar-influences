### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 340; PDF p. 169.

successful expansion of a place, expansion of the rights of a place, financial success of a place, money business at a place, legal success of the partner, legal knowledge at a place, increased environmental success, much money of the partner, and knowledge of the law of the partner

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Success or expansion encountered through place and other people.
- Psychology: Neither entry gives a separate psychological trait.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Ties through science or commerce; partner's rights and resources.
- Events/manifestations: Local money business, expanded local rights, and legal knowledge.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries establish connections and success, not a general degree of public visibility.

## Contradictions

Witte specifies the tie through science or commerce; ABC supplies broader local and partner-rights examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Asc`, Asc specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through commercial or scientific ties, local expansion, and a partner's legal or financial success. Scientific and commercial ties connect the person to others, while the local setting may expand in financial activity, rights or legal knowledge. The partner's money and legal success are explicit expressions of this outward-facing field. The interpretation centers on successful association and local opportunity, without requiring a public reputation or a particular kind of partnership. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
