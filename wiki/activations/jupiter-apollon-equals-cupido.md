## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Community success and enjoyment shared with many.
- Psychology: Much shared joy.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Trading and legal communities; shared legal advantages.
- Events/manifestations: Theatre/concert visits, bank accounts, and joint increase of money.
- Conflicts/notes: A community's benefit does not determine each member's finances.

## Contradictions

ABC's legal and trading communities broaden Witte's arts and bank-account examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through shared enjoyment, community enterprise, and collective financial or legal benefit. Shared enjoyment joins practical community success: visiting theatre or concerts, maintaining bank accounts, and cooperating through trading or legal groups. Joint financial increase and common legal advantages extend the same theme into resources and rights. The formula describes benefit and pleasure organized through a group, with art and law retained as specific fields. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
