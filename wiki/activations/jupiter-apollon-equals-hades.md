## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

Wrong financial speculation. Great losses through bad investments. Great amounts of money which are lost.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 341; PDF p. 169.

Old commercial rights, success through knowledge of the past, happiness and success through renunciation, happiness and success in secret, criminal money trading, increased financial disadvantages, knowledge and experience from the past, and increasing financial crime.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Loss and financial disadvantage alongside success through older knowledge or renunciation.
- Psychology: Happiness through renunciation or in secret is stated.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Old commercial rights and criminal money trading.
- Events/manifestations: Failed speculation, bad investments, large losses, and increasing financial crime.
- Conflicts/notes: The sources do not attribute loss to insufficient grounding or prove criminal guilt.

## Contradictions

Witte gives only adverse financial outcomes; ABC also admits concealed success and success through renunciation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Hades`, Hades specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through large financial losses, older knowledge or rights, and hidden or renounced success. Large losses through wrong speculation or bad investments form the adverse financial expression. The entry also preserves older commercial rights, knowledge from the past, and happiness or success through renunciation or in secret. These alternatives keep the formula broader than financial collapse: scale can involve substantial disadvantage, while earlier knowledge or withdrawal can still support success.
