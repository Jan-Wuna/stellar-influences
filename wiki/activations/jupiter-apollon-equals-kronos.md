## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

Fortunate leader, or fortunate leading in science. Captain of industry. Principal of a business. Director of a bank.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 341; PDF p. 169.

Reputation and success through money, successful scientific authority, a successful state governed by law, major financial successes, state financial activity, increase of state funds, superior legal knowledge, and advantages through important knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Success in directing scientific, industrial or financial activity.
- Psychology: Reputation through money is explicit; no further personality trait is given.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Scientific authority, business principals, bank directors, and the state.
- Events/manifestations: Major financial success, increased state funds, and superior legal knowledge.
- Conflicts/notes: Leadership is specified; its ethical quality is not assessed.

## Contradictions

ABC's state-law and public-finance themes extend Witte's business and scientific leadership examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through leadership, scientific authority, and substantial institutional or state finances. The formula places success in positions that direct or authorize wider activity: scientific leadership, industry, business and banking. State finances, legal knowledge and reputation through money extend the institutional range. Its distinctive theme is successful oversight backed by knowledge or resources, rather than a general claim that rank alone guarantees prosperity. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
