## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

Successful activities in commerce or science. Interest payments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 340; PDF p. 169.

Work in the money trade, successful actions for money, successful legal actions, successful financial activity, activity in law science, effective financial economy, economic success through work, and increasing work pleasure

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Success through activity and work in financial or scientific fields.
- Psychology: Increasing pleasure in work.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Legal actions and work in jurisprudence.
- Events/manifestations: Interest payments, financial activity, and efforts to obtain money.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries name successful work; they do not explain why effort is well directed.

## Contradictions

ABC adds legal activity and work pleasure to Witte's commerce/science and interest-payment meanings.


<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Mars`, Mars specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through successful practical work in commerce, science, finance, and law. Commercial and scientific success appears as practical activity: working with money, taking legal action, engaging in jurisprudence, and obtaining economic results through work. Interest payments give the formula a precise financial manifestation, while increasing work pleasure supplies its experiential dimension. Productive action unifies the range without requiring a particular occupation. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
