## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

To be lucky. To handle money. To be fortunate in commerce or science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 340; PDF p. 169.

Expansion of joy in the soul, joy in psychological experience, my happiness and success, my success through freedom, my financial success, my legal success, my legal knowledge, my experience of happiness, my increasing joy, and increase of my money.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal success and handling money.
- Psychology: Increasing joy, psychological experience, and success through freedom.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Personal legal success and legal knowledge.
- Events/manifestations: Handling money and success in commerce, science or finance.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries concern personal participation; they do not prescribe a vocation or management ability.

## Contradictions

ABC adds inward joy, freedom and legal knowledge to Witte's personal luck and money handling.


<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = MC`, MC specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through one's experience of happiness, freedom, and commercial, scientific, financial or legal success. Handling money and succeeding in commerce or science join a personal experience of increasing joy and freedom. Financial and legal success, legal knowledge, and growth of one's money make the practical side explicit. The formula centers on the person's experience and involvement in this favorable field; it does not establish an inevitable career path. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
