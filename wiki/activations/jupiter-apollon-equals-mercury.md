## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

Successful scientific thinking. Fortunate scientific debates.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 340; PDF p. 169.

Reports about commercial successes, successful legal conversations, thoughts about money trading, conversations about much money, news about legal successes, legal success in transport, much money in youth, knowledge of juvenile law, and increasing media success.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Scientific thinking and communication about trade, money or rights.
- Psychology: Successful scientific thought is explicit.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Scientific debate, legal conversations, and juvenile law.
- Events/manifestations: Success reports, transport-law success, media success, and money in youth.
- Conflicts/notes: Persuasiveness or personal profit is not established merely by successful discussion.

## Contradictions

ABC specifies money, transport, youth-law and media contexts beyond Witte's scientific debate.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through successful thought, discussion, reporting, and legal or commercial communication. Successful scientific thinking and debate connect with practical communication about trade, money and legal results. Reports, conversations and media activity give knowledge a public or transactional expression, while juvenile law and money in youth remain specific additional meanings. The common field is useful intellectual and communicative activity, rather than a general judgment of intelligence. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
