## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

The successful business woman. Persons who are successful in commerce or science. Many cheerful people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 340; PDF p. 169.

expansive joy of a people, success through emotional knowledge, successful trade among the people, financial success of the woman, woman with much money, increase of money among the people, legal success for women, legal knowledge of a woman, and increased emotional joy

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Successful people and collective financial or legal benefit.
- Psychology: Cheerfulness, emotional knowledge, and increased emotional joy.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Businesswomen, successful people in commerce/science, and women's legal success.
- Events/manifestations: Popular trade, collective money increase, and a woman's financial or legal knowledge.
- Conflicts/notes: Gendered examples remain source-specific, not universal traits of women.

## Contradictions

ABC supplies emotional, collective and legal detail alongside Witte's successful and cheerful people.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Moon`, Moon specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through cheerful people, emotional knowledge, and women's or collective commercial success. Commercial or scientific success is embodied in cheerful people and the source's example of a successful businesswoman. Emotional knowledge and increased joy join collective trade and financial increase, while women's legal success and knowledge remain explicit. The formula unifies successful participation with shared feeling and resources, preserving the historical gendered examples without generalizing them to everyone. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
