## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

Great money transaction. Great amounts of money are changing hands. The money in circulation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 341; PDF p. 169.

Developments in commercial law, experience with development funds, successful legal development, extensive financial change, changes in money trading, much money for development, growing legal experience, and developing the science of money.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Money in circulation and extensive financial or legal development.
- Psychology: Neither entry specifies a distinct psychological state.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Commercial law and growing legal experience.
- Events/manifestations: Large transactions, development funds, changing money trading, and financial study.
- Conflicts/notes: Money changing hands does not establish personal gain for every participant.

## Contradictions

ABC's legal and developmental range is broader than Witte's direct description of large transactions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through large money transfers and changes in financial or legal activity. Large amounts of money change hands, and their circulation accompanies wider development in finance, money trading and commercial law. Development funds, increasing legal experience and study of money give the process specific institutional and intellectual forms. The formula concerns substantial movement and change in resources, rather than guaranteeing that each transaction produces wealth. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
