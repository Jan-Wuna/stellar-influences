## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

Success through methodicalness in educational science. Educational successes. Success in trading things which are sold or disposed of very slowly.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 341; PDF p. 169.

Expansion of legal problems, success through experience of patience, financial success in old age, disturbed money trading, profit and loss in trade and business, jurisprudence as a task, extensive financial losses, and advantages of knowledge in old age.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Success through method or patience alongside substantial losses or legal problems.
- Psychology: Patience is explicit in ABC.
- Body/health: Old age is a source context; no disease is stated.
- Social/relationship: Education and jurisprudence as work.
- Events/manifestations: Slow-selling goods, financial success in old age, disturbed trade, and large losses.
- Conflicts/notes: Patient success does not eliminate the adverse financial range.

## Contradictions

ABC's extensive losses and disturbed trade qualify Witte's favorable account of methodical or slow success.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through methodical education, patience, slow trade, and financial or legal difficulties. Methodical educational work and trading goods that sell slowly are specific routes to success. Experience and patience extend this theme, including financial or knowledge advantages in old age. Yet expanding legal problems, disturbed trade and extensive losses also remain explicit. The formula holds measured work together with its possible burdens, without promising that persistence always produces profit. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
