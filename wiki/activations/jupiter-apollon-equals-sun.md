## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

The many lucky days. To be successful in science or commerce. The capitalist.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 340; PDF p. 169.

Expansion of a financial center, a man's experiences of success, a successful legal center, a person's financial success, a money-trading center, a man of jurisprudence, personal legal success, and much money belonging to a man.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Success represented by a person or financial/legal center.
- Psychology: Experiences of success are stated without a broader personality account.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: The capitalist and male legal/financial examples remain historical source descriptions.
- Events/manifestations: Many lucky days, financial centers, money trading, and personal legal success.
- Conflicts/notes: The formula does not establish health, vitality or entitlement to wealth.

## Contradictions

ABC gives legal and financial-center detail alongside Witte's commerce/science success and capitalist example.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Sun`, Sun specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through personal commercial, scientific, financial, and legal success. Success appears in the person's daily experience, scientific or commercial work, and financial or legal involvement. The source's capitalist and man of jurisprudence are specific examples; expanding financial centers and personal legal success broaden the practical range. The formula makes achievement personally concrete, without deriving a health claim from the Sun's identity. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
