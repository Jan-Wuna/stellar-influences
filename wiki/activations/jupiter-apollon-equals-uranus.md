## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

Sudden commencing of great gains and successes in trade or science. Good luck and success in astrology or technology.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 341; PDF p. 169.

Dynamic commercial law, success through new knowledge, successful financial reform, joy in technical successes, new legal experiences, new legal knowledge, sudden legal success, tensions in money trading, and restless financial business.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Rapid gains and novelty in scientific, commercial or legal activity.
- Psychology: Joy in technical success; tension and restlessness in money trading.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: New legal knowledge and dynamic commercial law.
- Events/manifestations: Sudden trade/science gains, astrology or technology success, and financial reform.
- Conflicts/notes: Novelty may bring tension as well as benefit; an already fertile setting is not required.

## Contradictions

ABC's financial tension and restlessness qualify the favorable sudden gains emphasized by Witte.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through sudden success, new knowledge, financial reform, and technical achievement. Sudden gains in commerce or science join success in astrology or technology, new knowledge and financial reform. New legal experience and technical achievement give the change a specific practical direction. Tension and restlessness in money trading remain part of the range, so the formula describes an opening through novelty without assuring an effortless result. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
