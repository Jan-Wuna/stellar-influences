## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

Much luck, joy and friendship through science or commerce.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 340; PDF p. 169.

Successful peace studies, successful art trade, experiencing joy in love, dealing with money and love, increased artistic success, much joy and harmony, preference for legal knowledge, and increasing advantages through experience.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Joy and friendship through science or commerce.
- Psychology: Joy in love, harmony, and pleasure in legal knowledge.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Friendship, love and peaceful exchange.
- Events/manifestations: Art trade, peace studies, artistic success, and benefits from experience.
- Conflicts/notes: Pleasurable exchange is stated; every friendship is not necessarily profitable.

## Contradictions

ABC adds art, peace studies and love to Witte's commerce/science friendships.


<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Venus`, Venus specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through friendship, enjoyment, harmony, art, and successful exchange. Joy and friendship arise through scientific or commercial exchange, while art trading, peace studies and experience provide more specific fields of success. Love and harmony are present alongside money and legal knowledge. The formula joins favorable practical exchange to enjoyment and relationship, without making material profit the measure of each friendship. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
