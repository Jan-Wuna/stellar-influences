## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

To be happy with many. Expansion of good fortune in general.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 340; PDF p. 169.

successful expansion in public, money trade in public, increase of money in public life, happiness and success in public life, public legal success, and commercial advantages in public

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Good fortune expands in general and public life.
- Psychology: Happiness shared with many.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Public participation in favorable conditions.
- Events/manifestations: Public money trading/increase, legal success, and commercial advantages.
- Conflicts/notes: Public benefit does not establish equal benefit for everyone.

## Contradictions

ABC specifies financial, commercial and legal public outcomes within Witte's general expansion of fortune.


<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through public success, shared happiness, and expansion in money or trade. Happiness with many accompanies an expansion of favorable conditions in public life. Money trading and increase, legal success and commercial advantages give that general good fortune concrete expressions. The formula's scale is shared and public, while the entries leave the distribution of those advantages unspecified. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
