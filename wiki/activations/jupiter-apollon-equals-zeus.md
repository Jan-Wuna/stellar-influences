## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 215.

Lucky and cheerfully working with many. Successful creation work in arts and science. Stage success. The influence of high capital. Production favored by high capital.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 341; PDF p. 169.

Strenuous money trading, successful scientific efforts, successful legal efforts, happiness through successful goal setting, achievements through knowledge, achievements in jurisprudence, and creative legal knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Successful production and achievement supported by knowledge or capital.
- Psychology: Cheerful work with many and happiness through successful goals.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Collective work, science and jurisprudence.
- Events/manifestations: Stage success, artistic creation, capital-backed production, and strenuous money trading.
- Conflicts/notes: Capital support is one listed factor; the entries do not say success is effortless.

## Contradictions

ABC's knowledge and legal effort supplement Witte's artistic production and capital support.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Apollon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the Jupiter/Apollon axis through creative production, collective work, and purposeful scientific or legal effort. Cheerful work with many takes productive form in art, science, stage performance and jurisprudence. High capital can favor production, while knowledge and successful goal setting support achievement through effort. Strenuous money trading remains part of this working range. The formula therefore joins collective and creative output to the resources and purpose that sustain it. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
