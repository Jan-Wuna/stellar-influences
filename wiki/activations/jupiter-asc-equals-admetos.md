## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 86.

Luck and fortune through being settled. To seek one’s happiness in a peaceful environment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 113; PDF p. 55.

Blocked place of money, consolidation of the environmental success, permanently satisfied partner, blocked environmental success, restriction of the local right, restricted joy in the environment, money circle in the environment, and deepening the success with the partner


## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Happiness through settlement, with environmental success consolidated or blocked.
- Psychology: Seeking peace, enduring partner contentment, or restricted local joy.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Partner's success and restricted local rights.
- Events/manifestations: Blocked money location and circulation in the environment.
- Conflicts/notes: Settlement is favorable in Witte; ABC retains restrictions alongside contentment.

## Contradictions

ABC's blocked success and restricted joy qualify Witte's favorable settlement meaning.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Asc = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the Jupiter/Asc axis through settled surroundings, enduring contentment, and local financial or legal limits. A peaceful, settled setting can support happiness, while partner contentment and consolidated local success give that stability a relational form. Yet local money, rights or success can also be blocked or restricted. The formula therefore concerns what is sustained within a settled environment, preserving contentment without assuming unlimited opportunity. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
