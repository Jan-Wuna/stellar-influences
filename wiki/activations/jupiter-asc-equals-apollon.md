## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 86.

To be fortunate with many. Good relations with business people or men of science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 113; PDF p. 55.

Extensive joy in the place, success with money of the partner, successful local economy, successful trade in the place, place of money increase, place of law science, place of peace and joy, legal success of the partner, and increasing success of others


## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Good fortune with many and success in the local setting.
- Psychology: Extensive local joy and peace.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Relations with business or scientific people; partner's money and legal success.
- Events/manifestations: Local trade, money increase, economic success, and jurisprudence.
- Conflicts/notes: Wide connections do not by themselves establish popularity.

## Contradictions

ABC's local legal and financial examples extend Witte's good scientific or commercial relations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Asc = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the Jupiter/Asc axis through many favorable connections, successful local commerce, and a partner's resources. Relations with business or scientific people place favorable association in a practical field. Successful local trade, money increase, legal knowledge and the partner's financial or legal success extend that field through the surroundings. The formula brings benefit through many people and a productive local setting, with peace and joy retained as explicit experiences. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
