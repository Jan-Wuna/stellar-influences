## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 85, exact Chiron paragraph.

One has an enthusiastic, generous aura about them which induces others to act the same way. One heals others through being understanding and sympathetic in relating to them.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: An enthusiastic, generous presence encourages a reciprocal response.
- Psychology: Enthusiasm, sympathy and understanding.
- Body/health: Sandbach uses healing language without naming a disease or treatment.
- Social/relationship: Others are encouraged to respond with similar generosity.
- Events/manifestations: Supportive relating through sympathy and understanding.
- Conflicts/notes: This single short entry does not establish clinical healing or a broader Chiron doctrine.

## Contradictions

The single source gives mutually reinforcing generosity and understanding, without a second account for comparison.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Asc = Chiron`, Chiron specifies the Jupiter/Asc axis through generosity that encourages similar behavior and sympathetic understanding in relationships. The [Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry) describes a generous and enthusiastic presence that encourages others to respond similarly. Understanding and sympathy supply the relational means of what Sandbach calls healing. The formula's focus is the effect of a supportive manner on others; the entry offers no specific medical claim or treatment.
