## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 86.

Friendly relations to single women. Money losses.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 113; PDF p. 55.

The partner's secret of success, successful service in the environment, a place's successful past, others' financial crime, the partner's lack of money, deficient local success, the partner's legal sorrow, and dirty money in the surroundings.


## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Money loss and deficient resources alongside a partner's secret of success or past local success.
- Psychology: The partner's legal sorrow is explicit.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Friendly relations with single women; partner's lack of money.
- Events/manifestations: Successful environmental service, past local success, financial crime and dirty money.
- Conflicts/notes: Friendly contact does not prove financial harm; the entries do not identify one causal chain.

## Contradictions

ABC preserves a partner's secret of success and a place's successful past as well as financial difficulties; Witte does not explain how its friendly relations and losses relate.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Asc = Hades`, Hades specifies the Jupiter/Asc axis through mixed relationships and local financial conditions, including loss or a partner's secret of success. Friendly relations with single women sit beside money losses in the brief Witte entry. The wider range includes successful local service or a place's past, the partner's secret of success, but also lack of money, legal sorrow and financial wrongdoing in the environment. These are mixed forms of involvement with others and local resources; the sources do not say that friendliness itself causes loss. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
