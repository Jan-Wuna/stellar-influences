## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 86.

Harmonious relations with the father, with the boss or with the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 113; PDF p. 55.

Special partner pleasure, success with other states, successful independence of the partner, financial office at the place, money of the state for the environment, great luck of the partner, place for state funds, place of law of the state, and advantages for others by the state


## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Benefit in relations with father, boss, state or institutions.
- Psychology: Special joy with the partner and great partner happiness.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Harmonious authority relations; partner's successful independence.
- Events/manifestations: Local finance office, state funds, state legal venue and advantages for others.
- Conflicts/notes: Protection is not explicitly stated; authority relations and material advantages are.

## Contradictions

ABC adds public funds and partner independence to Witte's harmonious authority relations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Asc = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the Jupiter/Asc axis through harmonious relations with authority and state or partner-related advantages. Harmonious relations with father, boss or state connect favorable association to authority. State funds and legal or financial offices provide concrete institutional expressions, while partner happiness and successful independence preserve a personal range. The formula concerns benefit and accord within these relations without establishing that all authority figures offer protection. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
