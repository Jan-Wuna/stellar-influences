## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To receive proposals or offers. To gain a willing ear. Betrothal. To receive orders.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work pleasure of the partner, successful environmental activity, financial activity on site, successful partner activity, joyful work for the environment, legal activity on site, advantages of work partners, and successful work on site

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Pleasant cooperation, an optimistic or generous partner or colleague. Desire to establish a beautiful home, getting engaged, a favorable business association.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One takes the initiative to try to enlarge upon and enrich one’s relationships. Anger expressed through an overcompensating personality.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds a sharpness and loudness to the way you greet people; greeting others with hail and heartiness; much activity and motion when meeting or introducing others; stimulates your desire to be around and with others.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Asc = Mars`: constructive action with others, favorable associations, direct pursuit of partnership or shared plans, and enthusiasm translated into doing. Main caution: heated overcompensation: impatience, pushiness, or anger can distort an otherwise generous and promising relational field.
