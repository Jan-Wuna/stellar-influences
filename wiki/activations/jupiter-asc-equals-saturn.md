## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To experience refusal. Not being accepted. Separations from friends.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success through patience with the partner, successful concentration on the environment, successful separation from the partner, loss of money by the partner, concentration on the local law, loss of rights in the place, and separation from the partner because of money

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inhibitions in personal development, inconstancy in associations. Estrangement and alienation, separation, departure.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One desire’s to grow in one’s identity, but is possibly afraid to do so, or un certain as to how to do it, and/or may take a long time doing it. Stress caused by the holding back of one’s enthusiasm.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds reserve and caution to an otherwise affable personality; cautious when meeting others at first, but then sincerely opens up; shows an interest in people but will hold back impressions until these have been evaluated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Asc = Saturn`: slowed growth, strained associations, and the need to mature beyond fear, uncertainty, or withheld enthusiasm in relationships. Main caution: contraction into alienation: discouragement can turn a naturally expansive social field into rejection, estrangement, or loss of confidence.
