## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To get help and assistance. To be loved by many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joy in the partner's art, harmony and joy in the environment, artistic success in the place, love happiness with the partner, right to peace in the place, advantages through harmony in the place, environmental benefits through beauty, and preference for the partner's money

- Translation note: ABC PDF p. 55, printed p. 112, has `Freude an der Kunst des Partners` and `Kunst-Erfolg`; these concern art, rather than an artist. See the [review proof](../../docs/reviews/2026-10-15-full-wiki/verification/activations-015-017-source-proof.md).

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Affectionate relationship with people in one's personal environment, desire to enjoy the company of others, artistic interests, a sense for harmonious or artistic decoration. A happy love union.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One loves to relate to many different things. Other people admire and appreci ate your exuberant personality. Love enlarges one’s identity.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `276`

#### Munkasey Entry

A relaxing and calm mannerism which inspires others thru your ability to mix good humor and wise sayings; music or art which arouses the emotions and spirits of others; helps others like you for the true person you are.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Favorable contact becomes affectionate, aesthetically pleasing, and personally encouraging.
- psychology: Sandbach describes admiration for an exuberant personality and identity enlarged through love.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified; Munkasey's calm manner concerns social presentation.
- social/relationship: Help, affection, enjoyment of company, and a happy love union recur across the entries.
- events/manifestations: Art, music, harmonious decoration, assistance, and romantic satisfaction are concrete manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's checked art wording supports the aesthetic theme; its preference for a partner's money is a specific additional motif.

## Contradictions

- The entries emphasize affection and enjoyment without an explicit disagreement. ABC's interest in a partner's money adds a material preference rather than negating the affectionate examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Asc = Venus`, Venus specifies the Jupiter/Asc pair as benefit through affection, shared enjoyment, and beauty. [Witte](#witte-entry) names help and being loved, while [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) links affectionate surroundings with artistic interests and a happy love union. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) makes love a way of enlarging identity.

[Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 276, explains how this can reach others: a calm, humorous manner or art that moves their feelings helps them appreciate the person. [ABC](#abc-entry) also connects harmony and beauty with advantages in one's surroundings, while retaining the more material preference for a partner's money.
