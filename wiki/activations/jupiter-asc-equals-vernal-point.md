## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Happy companionship with many.

Source: PDF p. 86, Vernal Point entry under Asc/Jupiter.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

English translation: Success in public surroundings, a money place in public, others' happiness in public, a money place in public, a public place of law, a place of public joy, partner joy in public life, and a contented partner in public.

Source: printed p. 112 (PDF p. 55), Vernal Point entry under Asc/Jupiter. The printed row repeats `Geld-Ort in der Öffentlichkeit` (a money place in public).

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Happy companionship is situated among many people and in public surroundings.
- psychology: ABC supports satisfaction with a public setting; no detailed personality account is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or medical manifestation is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte names happy company with many, and ABC describes partner satisfaction in public life.
- events/manifestations: Public joy, local public finance, and public legal settings are listed by ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC repeats its money-place clause in the printed eight-clause row. Its public places of money and law remain broad source terms.

## Contradictions

- Witte's brief companionship statement and ABC's public examples differ in detail rather than directly conflict.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Asc = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Jupiter/Asc pair by placing favorable companionship in a wider public setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives the concise image of happy company with many; [ABC](#abc-entry) develops public joy and a satisfied partner in public life, alongside financial and legal places.

This supports an agreeable social environment or public gathering. The entries do not by themselves establish lasting popularity, public status, or the success of every public encounter.
