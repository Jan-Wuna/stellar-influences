## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Luck through mighty enterprises, or through enterprising and jolly people.

Source: PDF p. 86, Vulcanus entry under Asc/Jupiter.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

English translation: Success through the partner's energy, influence of the partner's finances, tremendous success with others, fortunate partner influence, power at the place through money, powerful joy with others, a place of legal power, legal authority in the surroundings, and others' legal advantages.

Source: printed p. 113 (PDF p. 55), Vulcanus entry under Asc/Jupiter.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Opportunity with others is linked to energetic partners and large enterprises.
- psychology: Witte's enterprising, cheerful company is the clearest personal description; no fuller psychological mechanism is stated.
- body/health: ABC's energy refers to the partner's effectiveness, not a diagnosed bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Strong joint endeavors, a partner's energy and finances, and fortunate partner influence can support success.
- events/manifestations: Large enterprises, financial power, and legal power appear in the entries.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's nine-clause row includes the influence of a partner's finances and fortunate partner influence. Its `Rechts-Gewalt` is rendered as legal authority in this legal-power context; the term does not by itself establish physical violence or coercion.

## Contradictions

- Energetic cooperation and financial or legal power are different emphases, without a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Asc = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Jupiter/Asc pair through the scale and energy of what others undertake. [Witte](#witte-entry) names luck through large enterprises and enterprising, cheerful people; [ABC](#abc-entry) links success with a partner's energy, the influence of that partner's finances, and fortunate partner influence, alongside power associated with money or law.

The bounded reading is opportunity through vigorous people or substantial joint undertakings. ABC's legal-power terms do not by themselves establish a separate claim of physical violence or coercion.
