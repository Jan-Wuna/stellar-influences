## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Fatherhood. To advance through patronage in one’s own work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful efforts of a place, successful partner leadership, financial goals of a place, design of partner law, place of money production, legal efforts of a place, creative joy at the place, and procreation success with the partner

- Translation note: ABC PDF p. 55, printed p. 113, has `erfolgreiche Partner-Führung`: successful partner leadership, rather than delivery. See the [review proof](../../docs/reviews/2026-10-15-full-wiki/verification/activations-015-017-source-proof.md).

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Another person's backing helps purposeful work advance.
- psychology: The entries describe directed effort and creative satisfaction without a detailed personality account.
- body/health: Witte's fatherhood and ABC's procreation success are historical symbolic associations, not fertility assessments.
- social/relationship: Witte makes patronage in one's work explicit; ABC includes productive effort with a partner.
- events/manifestations: Work advancement, creative production, financial goals, and fatherhood are named examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's checked leadership phrase concerns guidance of a partner; it does not add an obstetric meaning to the separate procreation motif.

## Contradictions

- Work patronage, partner leadership, and procreative imagery describe separate manifestations rather than competing claims about one event.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Asc = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the Jupiter/Asc pair through support that advances an undertaking. [Witte](#witte-entry) makes the relationship concrete as patronage in one's own work; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful effort, creative satisfaction, financial aims, and production.

Witte also names fatherhood, and ABC includes successful procreation with a partner. These remain attributed symbolic manifestations, with no fertility or outcome guarantee. ABC's separate partner-leadership phrase reinforces the guidance of an undertaking rather than describing childbirth.
