## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 80, exact Asc paragraph.

One emanates an aura of generosity and understanding which can be deeply healing to the world around one.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Generosity and understanding are conveyed through personal presence.
- psychology: The entry emphasizes an emanated quality of understanding rather than an explicit method or belief system.
- body/health: Sandbach calls the effect healing; the short entry supplies no medical condition or treatment claim.
- social/relationship: The immediate world around the person receives the generous, understanding presence.
- events/manifestations: The stated manifestation is a deeply supportive effect on one's surroundings, without a named event.
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach is represented; the generous, understanding presence is the stated channel of the supportive effect.

## Contradictions

- Only one short entry is represented; no internal opposition is stated, and no cross-source agreement can be assessed.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Chiron = Asc`, Asc specifies the Jupiter/Chiron pair through the quality a person brings into contact with the surrounding world. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) describes generosity and understanding as an emanated presence that can have a deeply healing effect on others.

Here healing names the source's supportive or spiritual effect of this presence. The one-sentence entry does not establish a medical intervention, universal ability, or a particular occupation.
