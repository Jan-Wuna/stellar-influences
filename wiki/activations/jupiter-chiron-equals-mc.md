## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 80, exact MC paragraph.

One’s career involves the expression of deep spiritual understanding. One’s mission in life is to promote profound optimism and hope, which ultimately heals sorrow and depression.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Career and life mission become channels for spiritual understanding and hope.
- psychology: Profound optimism is the explicit inner quality expressed through one's work.
- body/health: Sandbach's claim of healing sorrow and depression is spiritual language here, not clinical evidence of treatment efficacy.
- social/relationship: The mission is described as promoting hope; no particular relationship role or profession is specified.
- events/manifestations: Expression of spiritual understanding through a career is the stated manifestation.
- conflicts/notes: The sole entry leaves the particular occupation and actual outcome unspecified.

## Contradictions

- One source entry is represented. Its career and spiritual-purpose statements form one account, with no internal conflict stated.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Chiron = MC`, MC specifies the Jupiter/Chiron pair as a calling expressed through work and life purpose. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) joins deep spiritual understanding with a mission to promote profound optimism and hope.

Sandbach describes this hope as healing sorrow and depression. That remains an attributed spiritual interpretation, not a clinical finding or a promise of recovery; the entry also leaves the specific occupation open.
