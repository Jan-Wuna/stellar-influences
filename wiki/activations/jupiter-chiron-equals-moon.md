## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 79, exact Moon paragraph.

An emotional makeup whose openness and generosity has a deeply healing effect on the world around it. An instinct for knowing how to spontaneously overcome all limitations and obstructions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Emotional generosity and openness are linked with an instinctive response to obstacles.
- psychology: The entry describes a spontaneous confidence in moving beyond limits rather than a deliberate technique.
- body/health: Sandbach calls the emotional effect healing; no bodily condition, treatment, or medical outcome is established.
- social/relationship: The open emotional makeup is said to affect the surrounding world deeply.
- events/manifestations: Spontaneously overcoming limitations is the named manifestation, without a concrete event or setting.
- conflicts/notes: The source's 'all limitations' is expansive spiritual language, not a warranted guarantee of unrestricted success.

## Contradictions

- The sole entry links emotional openness with overcoming obstacles. It states no counterclaim, and its breadth cannot be independently assessed from this page.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Chiron = Moon`, Moon specifies the Jupiter/Chiron pair through emotional openness and generosity. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) connects their supportive effect on the surrounding world with an instinct for spontaneously finding a way past obstructions.

The emphasis is on emotional disposition and spontaneous response. Sandbach's language of healing and overcoming all limitations remains an attributed spiritual claim; this brief entry supplies neither a medical mechanism nor a guarantee that every obstacle disappears.
