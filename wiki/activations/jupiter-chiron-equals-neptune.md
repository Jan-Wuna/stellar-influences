## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Chiron = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the Jupiter/Chiron pair as a subtle, intuitive mode of spiritual understanding. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) places depth of perception at the center: things are understood from a spiritual perspective through intuition.

The sentence does not describe a particular relationship, healing practice, or external result. Its narrow claim is about how understanding is experienced, so those further meanings are left open.
