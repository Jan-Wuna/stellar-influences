## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Chiron = Node`, Node specifies the Jupiter/Chiron pair through connection with others. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) makes relationship the channel that promotes spiritual understanding, rather than presenting understanding as a solely private experience.

The sentence leaves the kind of bond and the process of learning unspecified. It does not itself promise healing or renewed hope, so the interpretation stays with understanding developed through contact.
