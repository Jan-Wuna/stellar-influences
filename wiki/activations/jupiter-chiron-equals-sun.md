## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 79, exact Sun paragraph.

One’s vitality and sense of meaning and purpose in life is powered by an optimism based on attunement to one’s spiritual self. The whole purpose of one’s life centers around opening up to higher forces and allowing them to flow through your every action.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Spiritual attunement supplies optimism, vitality, and a sense of purpose.
- psychology: The source describes a life orientation toward allowing higher forces to inform action.
- body/health: Vitality is mentioned as an experience of purpose and optimism, without a bodily assessment or medical outcome.
- social/relationship: The entry does not identify a partner, group, or relationship role.
- events/manifestations: The stated manifestation is purposeful action informed by the spiritual self, rather than a named external event.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's 'whole purpose' and 'every action' are the scope of his spiritual account, not universal facts established about a person.

## Contradictions

- The sole entry presents optimism and spiritual openness as one organizing account, without a competing explanation on this page.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Chiron = Sun`, Sun specifies the Jupiter/Chiron pair as the source of vitality and life purpose. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) describes optimism grounded in attunement to the spiritual self, with a wish to let higher forces inform action.

This makes spiritual openness an organizing principle of purposeful living within Sandbach's account. The expansive language does not establish the actual course of a life or a medical benefit; the entry gives no particular event or relationship setting.
