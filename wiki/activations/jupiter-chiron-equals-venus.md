### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 80, exact Venus paragraph.

An appreciation of spiritual understanding and through relating this outwardly bringing optimism and hope to others.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Chiron = Venus`, Venus specifies the Jupiter/Chiron pair through appreciation that is expressed to others. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) moves from valuing spiritual understanding inwardly to relating it outwardly, where it brings optimism and hope.

This is an account of encouragement through shared appreciation. It does not itself specify romantic love, artistic beauty, or a healing practice, so those familiar Venus associations are not added to this orientation.
