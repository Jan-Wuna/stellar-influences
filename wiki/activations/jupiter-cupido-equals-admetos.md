## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 215; PDF p. 211.

A few being in happy company. End of a happy marriage, partnership or art work

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 333; PDF p. 165.

Blocked money of a community, a restricted legal community, the end of a happy community, a successful thorough community, financial restriction of a community, a consolidated legal community, and suppression of community rights.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared happiness is restricted to a small circle or meets an ending.
- psychology: The entries do not supply a psychological account; small company is a social condition, not evidence of introversion.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte contrasts a few in happy company with the end of a happy marriage or partnership.
- events/manifestations: Ending an artistic work, blocked shared money, financial restriction, and a consolidated legal community are listed.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's unusual phrase 'successful thorough community' is literal to the scanned row; suppressed community rights are also explicit.

## Contradictions

A small happy group, a consolidated legal community and the ending of a union are different possibilities; none makes the others inevitable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through a small circle, restricted resources, or an association reaching its end. A few can remain in happy company, while a happy marriage, partnership or artwork may also end. Consolidated legal community and successful thorough community remain explicit alongside blocked money and suppressed community rights. The entries preserve consolidation and closure as different expressions. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
