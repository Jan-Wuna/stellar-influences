## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 215; PDF p. 211.

Married happiness for many. Happy community with many. Happiness, peace, prosperity. Blessings of peace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 333; PDF p. 165.

Extension of common joy, successful trade community, common money proliferation, money trade community, common legal success, multiplication of common money, advantage through common knowledge, and scientific legal community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared happiness and prosperity extend to many people.
- psychology: Peace and joy are named conditions, without a developed personality theory.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Witte describes happy marriage and community benefiting many.
- events/manifestations: ABC gives trade communities, increased common money, shared legal success, and benefit through common knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: The entries support a widening communal benefit; they do not establish that every large organization is prosperous or peaceful.

## Contradictions

- The social peace of Witte and ABC's commercial and knowledge-based examples differ in emphasis. No direct opposition is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through shared benefit extending to many. Marriage and community can express happiness, peace and prosperity, while trade, shared funds, legal success and common knowledge give the benefit practical forms. The unifying theme is communal enjoyment and advantage on a wider scale, with social peace and commercial activity retained as distinct expressions. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
