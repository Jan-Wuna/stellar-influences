## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 215; PDF p. 211.

Happy married life or partnership in modest circumstances or with sad experiences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 333; PDF p. 165.

success through shared renunciation, successful past of a community, financial criminality of a community, dangerous legal community, financial distress of a community, happiness of a community in distress, and legal criminality of a community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared happiness may coexist with modest means, sacrifice, or distress.
- psychology: The entries do not explicitly establish loyalty, resilience, or a psychological mechanism for enduring hardship.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte preserves happy marriage or partnership despite modest circumstances or sad experiences.
- events/manifestations: ABC adds shared renunciation, communal financial distress, past success, and financial or legal criminality.
- conflicts/notes: The favorable and criminality motifs must stay separate; an association under hardship is not thereby criminal.

## Contradictions

- Happiness amid distress and harmful financial conduct are distinct possibilities. The sources do not equate poverty, sadness, and criminality.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Hades`, Hades specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through happiness sustained within modest or painful circumstances. A happy marriage or partnership can coexist with limited means, sad experiences or shared renunciation. Past community success and happiness in distress remain explicit, while financial or legal criminality is a separate adverse expression. Hardship alone does not establish wrongdoing. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
