- Source reference: printed p. 333; PDF p. 165.

Special joy for a community, a special legal art (Rechts-Kunst), dominance of financial communities, success of a state community, money of the state community, shared artistic successes, a legal community of the state, and a state financial community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared success is expressed through prominence, leadership, and large or official associations.
- psychology: The entries describe social position rather than an individual temperament.
- body/health: No bodily or medical manifestation is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte names a large family meeting and pleasant connections with prominent artists.
- events/manifestations: Leading artists, a renowned firm's chief, state communities, and shared artistic achievement are named.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's 'Rechts-Kunst' is retained as its unusual legal-art expression; prominence does not establish moral superiority.

## Contradictions

- Family, artistic, commercial, and state examples broaden the setting without making contradictory claims about one institution.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through prominent people, substantial associations and official communities. Leading artists, pleasant ties to prominent artists, a large family meeting and the head of a renowned firm share this leading social scale. State or financial communities and shared artistic achievements extend it into public resources and collective standing. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
