## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 215; PDF p. 211.

One’s own fortunate marriage or partnership. Publicly appearing artist. One’s own happy family life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 332; PDF p. 165.

shared joy of the soul, happy soul community, my community is successful, my happiness in the community, my advantage through the community, my financial community, and my rights in the community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared happiness is experienced as one's own partnership, family life, or community benefit.
- psychology: ABC emphasizes personal happiness and shared joy of the soul, without a broader personality account.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte names one's own fortunate marriage or partnership and happy family life.
- events/manifestations: Public appearance as an artist and personal benefit, finances, or rights in a community are listed.
- conflicts/notes: The artist's public appearance is not itself proof of recognition built on domestic happiness; that causal link is not stated.

## Contradictions

- Domestic happiness and public artistic appearance are separate examples. The source does not make one the cause of the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = MC`, MC specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through one's own fortunate union and personal participation in communal happiness. Marriage, partnership and family life join shared joy, personal benefit, community finances and rights. A publicly appearing artist is another specific expression. The formula makes shared happiness personally one's own without making domestic harmony the cause of public artistic achievement. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
