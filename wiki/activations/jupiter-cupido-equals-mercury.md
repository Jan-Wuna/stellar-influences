## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 215; PDF p. 211.

Thoughts about a happy marriage, or partnership. Cheerful around the table talks. Information or plans on the status of family or company finances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 332; PDF p. 165.

Reports from a legal community, success through shared conversations, news of a community's success, success of a media community, successful thinking toward synthesis, joy in a youth community, and conversations about shared money.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared happiness and resources are discussed, reported, or planned.
- psychology: Witte describes thought about a happy union and cheerful conversation; no fixed personality trait is established.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Family, partnership, company, and community provide the setting for exchange.
- events/manifestations: Table talk, financial information and plans, success reports, media activity, and shared conversation are listed.
- conflicts/notes: The entries support coordination through speech and thought, without guaranteeing that a plan or report is accurate or successful.

## Contradictions

- Convivial discussion and financial planning differ in purpose but can belong to the same association; no direct conflict is stated.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through discussion, information and planning about a shared life. Cheerful table talk and thoughts of marriage sit alongside family or company financial plans, community success reports, media activity and conversations about common money. Social enjoyment and practical coordination meet in the same communicative field. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
