## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 215; PDF p. 211.

To close an acquaintance that leads to happy marriage. Connections with good corporations. Contact with associations in judicial or financial matters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 332; PDF p. 165.

Successful Community relations, successful links with the arts, joy over joint meetings, joint money contacts, legal relations of a community, and links with the financial community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Favorable union and communal benefit emerge through acquaintances and organizational contacts.
- psychology: The entries do not describe a personality trait; their emphasis is on connecting.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte describes acquaintance leading to happy marriage and connections with good corporations.
- events/manifestations: Legal or financial associations, arts connections, joint meetings, and common money contacts are named.
- conflicts/notes: The entries support useful links but do not specify how long they last or guarantee institutional support.

## Contradictions

- Marital acquaintance and corporate, artistic, or legal contacts are different forms of connection, with no explicit opposition stated.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Node`, Node specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through acquaintances and affiliations that open favorable shared possibilities. An acquaintance may lead to happy marriage; other contacts link the person with good corporations, art, judicial affairs or finances. Joint meetings and common money contacts give the connection a practical setting. The emphasis is on the tie through which a favorable association can form. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
