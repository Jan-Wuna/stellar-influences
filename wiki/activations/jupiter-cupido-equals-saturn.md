## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 215; PDF p. 211.

Separation of a happy marriage or death of one partner. To turn away from an otherwise happy community, not wanting or not being able to join. Losses of an otherwise successful corporation. Restriction due to financial matters in the family.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 333; PDF p. 165.

Tasks of a legal community, success through shared patience, disturbances of success in a community, a community's money problems, shared concentration on money, a happy community in old age, and advantage to a community through separation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: An otherwise happy union or successful organization meets separation, inability to join, or financial restriction.
- psychology: Witte describes turning away or not wanting or being able to join; ABC adds patience and financial concentration.
- body/health: Witte names a partner's death as one historical manifestation; this is not a medical or mortality forecast.
- social/relationship: Happy marriage and community are the relationships whose participation or continuation is restricted.
- events/manifestations: Separation, corporate losses, financial limits, success through shared patience, a happy community in old age and benefit through separation.
- conflicts/notes: The scanned ABC row confirms separation (Trennung), not decision, and a happy community in old age.

## Contradictions

Separation can restrict an otherwise happy association in Witte or benefit a community in ABC. The sources do not give conditions for choosing between these outcomes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through limits on continuing or participating in an otherwise happy association. Separation, withdrawal, inability to join, corporate losses and family financial restrictions are explicit. Shared patience can also succeed, and a community may be happy in old age or benefit through separation. Witte's partner-death example remains historical doctrine. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
