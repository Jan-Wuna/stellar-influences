## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 215; PDF p. 211.

Daily happiness of the family. Fortunate associates of the day. With luck and success appearing artists or companies. The man who is made happy through his own art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 332; PDF p. 165.

successful center of art, day of joy in of a community, shared financial center, artistic success of the man, personal rights in the community, personal success in the community, and center of a legal community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared happiness is expressed in everyday family life and personal artistic or communal success.
- psychology: The entries state joy and success rather than a mechanism of strengthened identity or vitality.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte names daily family happiness and fortunate associates encountered that day.
- events/manifestations: Artists or companies appearing successfully, pleasure through one's art, and common financial centers are named.
- conflicts/notes: Witte and ABC's man-specific artistic examples remain attributed historical wording; family support and loyal allies are not stated causes of success.

## Contradictions

- Domestic, artistic, and financial examples broaden the setting. The entries do not state a causal dependence of public success on family support.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Sun`, Sun specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through everyday family happiness and personal participation in successful art or community. Fortunate associates, artists or companies appearing successfully, and pleasure in one's own art give the experience concrete form. Shared financial or legal centers and personal rights extend the communal range. The man-specific artistic wording remains a historical example. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
