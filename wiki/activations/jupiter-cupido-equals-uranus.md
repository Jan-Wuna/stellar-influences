## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 211 (printed p. 215), Uranus row.

Sudden accomplished marriage or partnership. Pleasant connections. To contact surprisingly people where one is treated as one of the family. Artist’s marriage.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 333 (one-based PDF p. 165), Uranus row.

Excitement over shared money, a successful technical community, financial reform for the community, a joyful event in a community, impulses of joy in a community, a nervous financial community, and tensions in a community because of money.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden favorable association and communal excitement, joy, or tension around shared money.
- psychology: Surprise and welcome in Witte; excitement, nervousness, and impulses of joy in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Pleasant connections and a family-like welcome; a technical or financial community.
- events/manifestations: Sudden marriage or partnership, an artist's marriage, financial reform, or a joyful communal event.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's money-related tension remains beside Witte's favorable associations; suddenness does not guarantee communal harmony.

## Contradictions

Witte's unexpected welcome and ABC's communal joy overlap in tone. ABC also includes financial nervousness and tension without denying the possibility of a favorable union.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden or exciting developments in shared life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives sudden marriage or partnership and unexpected contacts with a family-like welcome. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds joyful communal events, a successful technical community, and reform of shared finances, while retaining nervousness or tension about money. The recurring feature is the quickening of association, with both welcome and financial strain left possible.
