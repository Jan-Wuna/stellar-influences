## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 215; PDF p. 211.

Love’s happiness connected with married life. Harmony in company. Balanced financial condition of the family or company. No money debts in the family.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 332; PDF p. 165.

Joy in a successful community, shared joy and harmony, shared fondness for money, a happy love community, an artistically successful community, preference for community rights, and goodwill from a financial community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared life is expressed through affection, harmony, and balanced finances.
- psychology: The entries describe enjoyment and shared preferences, without establishing personal grace or a stabilizing mechanism.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte joins love in married life with harmony in company; ABC adds communal joy and artistic success.
- events/manifestations: Balanced family or company finances and absence of family debt are Witte's concrete financial examples.
- conflicts/notes: The scanned ABC row confirms a happy love community; debt-free family finances remain a particular favorable source example.

## Contradictions

- Love and financial balance are adjacent favorable examples, without a stated claim that one causes the other.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Venus`, Venus specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through affection and harmony within shared life. Happiness in love, harmonious company and artistic success join common enjoyment and shared preferences. Balanced family or company finances, absence of family debt and goodwill from a financial community supply specific material expressions; the entries do not make affection their necessary cause. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
