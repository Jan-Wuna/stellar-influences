## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 215; PDF p. 211.

To find oneself happily related to the public. Contented body of artists.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 332; PDF p. 165.

Shared joy in public, artistic success in public, a public financial community, a public legal community, advantages for a community in public, and a contented public community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared happiness and communal benefit appear in public life.
- psychology: The usable entry gives contentment rather than a developed personality account.
- body/health: Witte's 'body of artists' means a group; it is not a bodily or medical statement.
- social/relationship: Witte describes feeling happily related to the public and a contented group of artists.
- events/manifestations: Public rapport, artistic success, financial and legal communities, and public communal contentment.
- conflicts/notes: All six ABC public-life clauses are verified from the actual row; Witte's body of artists means a group.

## Contradictions

ABC's financial and legal communities extend Witte's public rapport and artistic-group contentment; the entries do not establish universal popularity.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through shared happiness in public life. Happy rapport with the public and a contented body of artists join public artistic success, financial or legal communities, and advantages experienced by a community publicly. Communal satisfaction takes an outward social setting, with art and shared resources retained as distinct forms. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
