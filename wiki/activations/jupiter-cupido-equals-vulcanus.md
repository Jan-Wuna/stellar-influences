## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 215; PDF p. 211.

Power and influence in or on a community through artistic abilities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 333; PDF p. 165.

influence of a legal community, influential money community, successful power of the community, joy over shared strength, money power of a community, shared success of power, strong financial community, and advantageous artistic influence

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Communal success is specified as concentrated power or influence.
- psychology: The entries describe effectiveness of a group rather than an individual temperament.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated; shared strength is communal capacity.
- social/relationship: Witte places artistic ability at the source of influence in or on a community.
- events/manifestations: ABC lists legal or financial influence, strong financial communities, and joy in shared strength.
- conflicts/notes: Artistic influence and financial power are different examples; family authority or coercive intent is not established.

## Contradictions

- Artistic, financial, and legal influence differ in medium rather than contradicting one another.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through an association's power to exert influence. Artistic ability can supply influence in or on a community, while legal and financial communities express shared power, resources and enjoyment of collective strength. The formula emphasizes effective communal capacity in these fields; a coercive purpose is not specified. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
