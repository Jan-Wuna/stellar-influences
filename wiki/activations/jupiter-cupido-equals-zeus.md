## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 215; PDF p. 211.

Happy family blessed with children. Successful activity in the family or community. Success in good firms. Military service in a good regiment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 333; PDF p. 165.

Beginning success for a community, successful artistic creation, a successfully led community, a successful performance community, shared leadership success, legal services for the community, and an advantageous common beginning.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared happiness is expressed through productive activity and a successful common beginning.
- psychology: The entries emphasize achievement and direction without a developed personality account.
- body/health: Witte's family blessed with children is an attributed symbolic example, not a fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: Family, community, firm, and regiment provide distinct settings for successful participation.
- events/manifestations: Witte names successful communal activity and military service in a good regiment; ABC adds artistic creation, legal service, and an advantageous shared start.
- conflicts/notes: Children, organized work, and military service are separate examples; none is a required manifestation of the formula.

## Contradictions

- Family, artistic, commercial, legal, and military examples describe different settings for shared achievement rather than mutually exclusive meanings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Cupido = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the Jupiter/Cupido axis through productive shared activity, leadership and a favorable beginning. Family or communal work, successful firms, art and legal service provide distinct settings. A good regiment gives an organized military expression, while a happy family with children supplies a generative one. These remain source examples of shared achievement rather than required outcomes. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
