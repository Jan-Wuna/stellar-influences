## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 216; PDF p. 212.

Shrinkage of money. Deflation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 335; PDF p. 166.

Continuing financial sorrow, deprivation through blocked money, success through thorough renunciation, a successful end to hardship, consolidation of hidden happiness, criminal circulation of money, sorrow through blocked rights, and deepening into past law.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Monetary contraction or blockage alongside possible relief from hardship.
- psychology: Continuing financial sorrow and hidden happiness.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Blocked rights, past law, and criminal money circulation are particular settings.
- events/manifestations: Deflation, blocked money, success through renunciation, and hardship ending.
- conflicts/notes: Money contraction and hidden happiness or relief remain distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte's monetary shrinkage and ABC's successful end to hardship differ in outcome; the entries do not explain the transition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Hades = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the Jupiter/Hades axis through contraction or blockage of money. Financial sorrow, deprivation and blocked rights give this a practical burden, while success through thorough renunciation, hidden happiness and an end to hardship retain a constructive range. The formula concerns constrained resources and the possible resolution of need; it does not prescribe a financial strategy. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
