## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 216; PDF p. 212.

Many people are unfortunate, out of luck. Science hindered through lack.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 335; PDF p. 166.

Experiences of joy from the past, renunciation of success in trade, dangerous increase of money, hardship through money trading, deficient legal successes, renunciation of money increase, advantages and disadvantages of money, and growing financial crime.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Lack or misfortune affects many, science, commerce or finance.
- psychology: Past experiences of joy remain explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Many unfortunate people and science hindered by lack.
- events/manifestations: Dangerous money increase, hardship through trading, deficient legal results, and financial crime.
- conflicts/notes: Renunciation of trade success or money increase is stated, without a recommended policy.

## Contradictions

Past joy and renunciation add scope to the adverse collective or commercial conditions; no source explains one as the cause of another.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Hades = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the Jupiter/Hades axis through lack or misfortune extending across people, science or commerce. Science can be hindered by shortage, while money trading may bring hardship, dangerous increase or deficient legal results. Past joy and renunciation of trade success or money increase keep the range broader than loss alone. The entries describe circumstances and choices without recommending a response. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
