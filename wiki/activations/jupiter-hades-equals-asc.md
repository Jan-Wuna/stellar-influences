## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 216; PDF p. 212.

To be impoverished by others. Other people are the cause of less income.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 334; PDF p. 166.

Old rights of the partner, a place's successful past, joy with the partner in old age, financial crime in the surroundings, local rights from the past, advantages through the partner in hardship, hidden happiness at the location, and past success with a partner.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Others or the local setting can bring reduced income or past and hidden benefit.
- psychology: Joy with the partner in old age and hidden local happiness.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Old partner rights and advantages through a partner in hardship.
- events/manifestations: Reduced income through others, past local success, and environmental financial crime.
- conflicts/notes: The adverse Witte account and favorable ABC possibilities remain distinct.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes impoverishment through others; ABC admits partner-related benefit and hidden local happiness, without explaining which outcome applies.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Hades = Asc`, Asc specifies the Jupiter/Hades axis through other people and the local setting. Income may fall through others, but the entry also preserves older partner or local rights, past success, joy with a partner in old age, and advantages in hardship. Hidden local happiness and financial wrongdoing are further separate meanings. Financial harm and limited or past benefit therefore remain side by side. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
