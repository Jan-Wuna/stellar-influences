## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 212 (printed p. 216), Cupido row.

Poor married life. Money losses of a corporation. Poorly paid art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 335 (one-based PDF p. 166), Cupido row.

Success through shared renunciation, a community's successful past, a dangerous legal association, a community's fortune in danger, a criminal financial association, sorrow in a happy community, and legal disadvantages of a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared disadvantage or insufficient reward, with success through renunciation and a successful communal past also represented.
- psychology: Sorrow within a happy community; loyalty or dependence is not stated.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Poor married life in Witte; shared renunciation, community history, and legal or financial associations in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Corporate money losses, poorly paid art, endangered communal fortune, and legal disadvantages.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's dangerous or criminal associations remain source claims, not allegations about a particular community.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes poor conditions and insufficient reward. ABC also allows success through shared renunciation and a successful past, while retaining danger and sorrow. No conditions select between these expressions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Hades = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through the fortunes and disadvantages of shared life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives poor married life, corporate money losses, and poorly paid art. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds communal danger, sorrow, and legal disadvantage, but also success through shared renunciation and a successful communal past. Shared conditions and resources connect these expressions, with no claim about the quality of affection or loyalty.
