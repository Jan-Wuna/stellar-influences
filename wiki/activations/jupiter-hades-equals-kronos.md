## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 216; PDF p. 212.

To play the fine gentleman while living in poverty. Poverty-stricken government. Shortage of money or losses through the authorities. Superiors or representatives of the government who do not appear in their capacity as such. Welfare departments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 335; PDF p. 166.

Special joy through renunciation, lack of success of the state, financial crime of the state, dangers to the state governed by law, artistic success in the past, legal hardship of the state, state financial distress, and state rights from the past.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Material shortage in official settings or behind social appearance.
- psychology: Maintaining a fine-gentleman appearance while poor is Witte's specific example.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Authorities, government representatives, superiors and welfare departments.
- events/manifestations: Government poverty, authority-related losses, state financial or legal hardship, and past art success.
- conflicts/notes: Joy through renunciation and older rights broaden the institutional shortage range.

## Contradictions

Official shortage, maintaining appearances and state wrongdoing are different meanings; neither source makes every authority-related loss corrupt.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Hades = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the Jupiter/Hades axis through rank, authority and public institutions. The appearance of gentility can coexist with poverty, while government shortage, losses through authorities and welfare departments make the official setting concrete. State financial or legal hardship, older rights and past artistic success extend the range. Representatives acting outside their official capacity remain a distinct example with no stated motive. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
