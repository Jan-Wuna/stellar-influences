### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To play the fine gentleman while living in poverty. Poverty-stricken government. Shortage of money or losses through the authorities. Superiors or representatives of the government who do not appear in their capacity as such. Welfare departments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special joy by renunciation, failure of the state to succeed, financial crime of the state, dangers of the legal state, artistic success in the past, legal necessity of the state, state money emergency, and state rights from the Y passed

## Derived Synthesis
