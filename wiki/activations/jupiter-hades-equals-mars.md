## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reduced success of one’s activities due to lack of money. Aggravated service. Disturbances and unpleasantness with servants or as a servant.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work success through deprivation, successful work in secret, successful dangerous work, difficult legal work, joy in the work service, dangerous work with money, mysterious work success, and working with legal disadvantages

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Hades = Mars`: effort being frustrated by scarcity. It can show labor under strain, service made harsher by want, or action that cannot succeed fully because resources are inadequate.
