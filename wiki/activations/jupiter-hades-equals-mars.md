## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 216; PDF p. 212.

Reduced success of one’s activities due to lack of money. Aggravated service. Disturbances and unpleasantness with servants or as a servant.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 334; PDF p. 166.

Success at work through deprivation, successful work in secret, successful dangerous work, difficult legal work, joy in work service, dangerous work with money, mysterious work successes, and working through legal disadvantages.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Work under scarce resources, difficult service, secrecy or danger.
- psychology: Joy in service is explicit.
- body/health: Dangerous work is a context; no bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Difficulties in service relations, whether serving or employing servants.
- events/manifestations: Reduced results through lack of money; work may also succeed under deprivation.
- conflicts/notes: Adverse work conditions do not establish uniform failure; servant wording remains historical.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses impaired results; ABC also gives successful work under deprivation, with no rule selecting one outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Hades = Mars`, Mars specifies the Jupiter/Hades axis through the conditions and results of work. Inadequate money can reduce success and complicate service relationships, while work may also succeed through deprivation, in secret or in dangerous circumstances. Difficult legal work, service pleasure and work on legal disadvantages provide specific expressions. Restricted conditions unify the range, with both frustrated and successful effort retained. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
