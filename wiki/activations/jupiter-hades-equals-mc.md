## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 216; PDF p. 212.

Shrinkage and decrease of fortune. Worried by restrictions or lack of money. Moment of a secret investment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 334; PDF p. 166.

I renounce advantages, lack of joy in the soul, my lack of money, my happiness in danger, my success through deprivation, my joy in serving, my secret happiness, my legal disadvantages, and my advantage from the past.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personally experienced restriction and concern about money.
- psychology: Financial worry, deficient inner joy, secret happiness and joy in serving.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Joy in serving is named without a particular role.
- events/manifestations: Reduced fortune, secret investment, legal disadvantage, and benefit from the past.
- conflicts/notes: Renouncing advantages differs from fearing them; secret investment is not stated as the cause of lack.

## Contradictions

Financial worry and reduced inner joy coexist in the range with secret happiness or success through deprivation; no transition is specified.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Hades = MC`, MC specifies the Jupiter/Hades axis through one's own restricted resources and response to them. Decreasing fortune and worry over money coexist with renunciation of advantages, success through deprivation, joy in serving, secret happiness and benefits from the past. A moment of secret investment is separately listed. The account concerns personal experience rather than a necessary vocational or lifelong course. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry))
