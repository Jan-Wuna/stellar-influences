## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The disturbed happiness of a woman. People or female persons lack money. Happiness in the world of prostitutes. Women in economic poverty.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Old people right, success of a woman by renunciation, success of a woman in old age, courageCummer for money, poor right of the woman, flawed right of the woman, hidden happiness of a woman, and satisfaction by service to the people

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Hades = Moon`: poverty being felt intimately and shared collectively. It can show emotional unhappiness tied to lack, especially in feminine, domestic, or public conditions.
