## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. `212`, printed p. `216`, Neptune row; checked against the scan.

Shortage of money because of fraud. Money losses through fraud.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `335`, one-based PDF p. `166`, Neptune row; English translation checked against the German scan.

Success through sensitivity and renunciation, successful intuition in old age, successful service in the air, secret happiness in the air, disadvantages through counterfeit money, resolving legal disadvantages, uncertainty through lack of money, and the advantages and disadvantages of water.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Financial shortage or loss through deception, beside distinct ABC forms of sensitivity and service.
- psychology: ABC names intuition in old age and uncertainty through lack of money.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is stated.
- social/relationship: Fraudulent money dealings are named; no particular relationship or perpetrator is specified.
- events/manifestations: Counterfeit-money disadvantages, resolution of legal disadvantages, air-related service and water's advantages/disadvantages are separate examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's fine-tuning, legal-disadvantage and water clauses were repaired from the scan; no causal connection among every example is stated.

## Contradictions

Witte specifies fraud-related shortage, while ABC also allows success through sensitivity or renunciation. These are different possibilities, not evidence that every loss is deceptive or that sensitivity prevents fraud. The air and water examples remain unexplained settings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Hades = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair's material difficulty through deception and uncertainty. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names shortage and loss of money through fraud; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds counterfeit money and uncertainty from insufficient funds, while also allowing legal disadvantages to be resolved. ABC's success through sensitivity, renunciation or service, and its air/water examples, remain distinct possibilities rather than evidence that every loss has a hidden manipulator.
