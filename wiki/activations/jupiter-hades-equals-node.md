## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. `212`, printed p. `216`, Node row; checked against the scan.

To be entangled in money difficulties. Connected with people who have hard luck.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `334`, one-based PDF p. `166`, Node row; wording and orientation checked against the German scan.

Successful contacts from the past, joy over encounters in need, secret money connections, happiness through connections in need, lack of successful relationships, adverse legal connections, and connection to dirty money

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections formed amid financial difficulty, including both adverse and helpful encounters.
- psychology: These entries do not specify a separate psychological mechanism.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte names ties with people who have hard luck; ABC includes successful past contacts and happiness through connections in need.
- events/manifestations: Money difficulties, secret money ties, adverse legal connections and a connection to dirty money are attributed examples.
- conflicts/notes: The Node row is intact; debt and a universal harmful influence of relationships are not stated.

## Contradictions

The difficult ties in Witte do not exclude ABC's joy or help through encounters in need. Connections to dirty money and unsuccessful relationships are adverse alternatives, not a judgment about every association with hardship.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Hades = Node`, Node specifies the pair through connections made under conditions of need. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes entanglement in money difficulties and ties to people who have hard luck. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also retains joy or happiness through encounters in need and successful contacts from the past, alongside secret money ties and adverse legal connections. The common setting is relationship under material constraint; the entries allow those ties to help as well as burden.
