## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 216; one-based PDF page 212, Jupiter/Hades section, Pluto row.

To practice moderation. One has to live thriftily.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 335; one-based PDF page 166, Jupiter/Hades section, Pluto row.

Successful development from the past, difficult legal development, developing joy in old age, developing pleasure in serving, dangerous financial development, favorable development despite danger, and growing financial crime

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Moderation and thrift in Witte; ABC describes development involving the past, service, old age, law, money, or danger.
- psychology: Witte calls for living thriftily; ABC includes developing joy in old age and pleasure in serving.
- body/health: Neither entry names a bodily condition.
- social/relationship: ABC connects development with service and legal or financial circumstances; financial crime is one listed manifestation.
- events/manifestations: Poor financial conditions can call for moderation; ABC also allows favorable development despite danger.
- conflicts/notes: The sources differ in emphasis. Growth, financial crime, and favorable development are alternatives in ABC, not a single inevitable sequence.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes moderation, while ABC lists several kinds of development, including favorable change and financial danger. These emphases can coexist; neither entry makes financial crime an inevitable result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For Jupiter/Hades = Pluto, [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes moderation and the need to live thriftily. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds development shaped by the past, service, old age, legal difficulty, or financial danger. Favorable development despite danger belongs alongside the harder possibilities, including growing financial crime. The shared reading is of development under constrained financial conditions, with thrift as one response; the entries do not establish a universal story of crisis or transformation.
