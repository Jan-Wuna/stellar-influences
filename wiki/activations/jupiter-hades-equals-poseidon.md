## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 216; one-based PDF page 212, Jupiter/Hades section, Poseidon row.

To be depressed. Sad over suffered injustice. To be forced to suffer injustice.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 335; one-based PDF page 166, Jupiter/Hades section, Poseidon row.

Success through service to the truth, successful cultivation of the past, insights into financial crime, joy over the truth about the past, money for an old culture, inadequate legal clarity, renunciation of rights for reasons of prudence, and hidden legal culture

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Witte describes distress over injustice and being forced to endure it; ABC concerns truth, the past, culture and legal clarity.
- psychology: Sadness over suffered injustice in Witte; joy over truth about the past in ABC.
- body/health: Witte's word 'depressed' is an emotional description here; the entry supplies no clinical criteria or named bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Suffered injustice, service to truth, legal understanding and renunciation of rights appear in the entries.
- events/manifestations: ABC includes support for an old culture, insight into financial crime and inadequate legal clarity.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's distress and ABC's cultural or legal possibilities remain distinct; service to truth does not promise that injustice will be corrected.

## Contradictions

Witte centers on distress and endured injustice. ABC includes successful service to truth and joy as well as inadequate legal clarity. The entries supply different possibilities rather than evidence that distress necessarily leads to insight or justice.

## Derived Synthesis

For Jupiter/Hades = Poseidon, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes sadness over injustice and the burden of having to endure it. [ABC](#abc-entry) develops a separate set of possibilities around truth about the past, cultural work, legal clarity and financial crime. Service to truth or support for an old culture can be successful, while legal understanding can also be inadequate or rights relinquished for prudent reasons. These entries support an ethical and legal focus, without making suffering a necessary path to wisdom or resolution.
