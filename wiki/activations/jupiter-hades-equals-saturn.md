## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 216; one-based PDF page 212, Jupiter/Hades section, Saturn row.

Unnecessary or wrong spending of money or poor investments. To be impoverished by shortage of money.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 335; one-based PDF page 166, Jupiter/Hades section, Saturn row.

Burden through financial crime, success through patience in danger, concealing money problems, happiness through deprivations in old age, disadvantages through financial loss, disadvantages through legal problems, and problems through lack of money

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Witte concerns wrong or unnecessary spending, poor investment and impoverishment; ABC adds financial or legal burdens and success through patience in danger.
- psychology: Patience and concealment of money problems occur in ABC; Witte emphasizes spending and investment decisions.
- body/health: Neither entry names a bodily condition.
- social/relationship: ABC includes financial crime and legal problems; the entries do not specify a relationship role.
- events/manifestations: Money shortage, loss, poor investment and concealment are possible manifestations; ABC also lists happiness through deprivations in old age.
- conflicts/notes: Financial difficulty need not prove poor judgment: Witte names such decisions, while ABC also lists external burdens and successful patience.

## Contradictions

The predominantly difficult financial indications coexist with ABC's success through patience and happiness through deprivations in old age. The sources do not explain those favorable clauses further, so they should remain explicit alternatives.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For Jupiter/Hades = Saturn, [Witte](#witte-entry) gives concrete financial difficulties: unnecessary or wrong spending, poor investments and impoverishment through lack of money. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds burdens from financial crime or legal problems and the concealment of money difficulties. Its favorable alternatives—success through patience in danger and happiness through deprivations in old age—qualify the picture. The orientation concerns constrained finances and their consequences; it does not establish that every shortage results from bad judgment or carries a moral lesson.
