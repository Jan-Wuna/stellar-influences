## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 216; one-based PDF page 212, Jupiter/Hades section, Sun row.

Daily worries. A man with worries. To be or to become poor. Persons with lung trouble.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 334; one-based PDF page 166, Jupiter/Hades section, Sun row.

A man's lack of success, a successful center in the past, danger for people with money, crime in the financial center, disadvantages for a financial center, personal success through deprivation, and personal joy from the past

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal worry or poverty in Witte; ABC includes financial centers, danger and success through deprivation.
- psychology: Witte names daily worries; ABC also names personal joy from the past.
- body/health: Witte lists persons with lung trouble; it supplies no mechanism, diagnosis or claim that worry causes lung illness.
- social/relationship: A worried man and people with money appear as distinct source examples; financial crime is also listed.
- events/manifestations: Poverty, lack of success and disadvantages for a financial center coexist with personal success through deprivation.
- conflicts/notes: The source's bodily statement and ABC's financial-center examples should not be compressed into a general claim about life force or chronic strain.

## Contradictions

Witte's worry and poverty coexist with ABC's personal success and joy. These are alternative manifestations. The reference to lung trouble supplies no causal link between finances, worry and illness.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Hades = Sun`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) centers on daily worries, poverty and a worried man, with lung trouble separately listed. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a man's lack of success and difficult circumstances involving people with money or a financial center, but also personal success through deprivation and joy from the past. These particulars support a reading of personal and financial hardship qualified by possible success; the physical example does not establish that hardship causes illness.
