## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 216; one-based PDF page 212, Jupiter/Hades section, Uranus row.

Sudden appearing shortage of money or money losses. To strike, lockout.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 335; one-based PDF page 166, Jupiter/Hades section, Uranus row.

Deprivations through financial reform, a successful technical past, secret impulses of happiness, nervousness through lack of money, sudden renunciation of rights, tensions over advantages from the past, and surprising joy through serving

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden money shortage or loss in Witte; ABC adds deprivation through financial reform and surprising joy through service.
- psychology: Nervousness from lack of money, secret impulses of happiness and tension over past advantages are ABC examples.
- body/health: No bodily condition is named; ABC's nervousness is not supplied as a clinical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Witte lists strike and lockout; ABC names service and renunciation of rights.
- events/manifestations: Abrupt financial loss, financial reform and labor conflict are concrete possibilities, alongside a successful technical past.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's positive possibilities qualify the difficult financial indications; its first clause concerns deprivation, not merely disappointment.

## Contradictions

Financial loss and deprivation coexist with ABC's successful technical past and surprising joy through serving. A strike or lockout is a Witte example, not a necessary outcome of all sudden money difficulties.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Hades = Uranus`, [Witte](#witte-entry) makes the financial difficulty sudden and includes strike or lockout. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds deprivation through financial reform, nervousness over lack of money, tensions over past advantages and sudden relinquishment of rights. It also preserves a successful technical past, secret happiness and surprising joy through service. Abrupt change is therefore central to several examples, while the sources allow both disruption and favorable experiences.
