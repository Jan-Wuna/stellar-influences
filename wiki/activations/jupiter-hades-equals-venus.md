## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 216; one-based PDF page 212, Jupiter/Hades section, Venus row.

Love orgies. To receive favors while on duty. Fine arts with lack of success.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 334; one-based PDF page 166, Jupiter/Hades section, Venus row.

Joy in the service of love, dangerous happiness in love, the mystery of joy and harmony, secret joy in love, artistic success from the past, service of love for money, past happiness in love, and advantages and disadvantages of beauty

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Love, favors and fine arts in Witte; ABC includes hidden or past happiness, paid love service and beauty's advantages and disadvantages.
- psychology: ABC names joy, harmony and secret happiness, as well as danger in happiness in love.
- body/health: Neither entry names a bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Witte lists love orgies and favors received while on duty; ABC includes service of love for money.
- events/manifestations: Lack of success in fine arts in Witte contrasts with ABC's artistic success from the past.
- conflicts/notes: The sources do not establish a universal judgment of impurity or ethical compromise; artistic success was mistranslated as artificial success.

## Contradictions

Witte's lack of success in fine arts differs from ABC's artistic success from the past. The time qualification allows both to remain without forcing a single verdict on artistic ability or success.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Hades = Venus`, [Witte](#witte-entry) connects love, favors received while on duty and fine arts without success. [ABC](#abc-entry) extends the picture to secret or past happiness in love, joy and harmony, service of love for money and the mixed effects of beauty. Its artistic success from the past must remain beside Witte's unsuccessful arts example. The formula therefore holds pleasure and artistic or relational difficulty together without making an unsourced judgment about purity.
