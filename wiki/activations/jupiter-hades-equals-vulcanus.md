## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 212, Jupiter/Hades section, Vulcanus row.

Not solvent. Crimes caused by lack of money.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 335; one-based PDF page 166, Jupiter/Hades section, Vulcanus row.

Disadvantaged financial power, successful influence of the past, dangerous influence of money, strength and joy through renunciation, powerful happiness from the past, disadvantageous legal influence, hidden financial power, and advantage through renouncing power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Insolvency and crimes caused by lack of money in Witte; financial power and influence in ABC.
- psychology: ABC names strength and joy through renunciation and happiness from the past.
- body/health: No health claim is stated.
- social/relationship: Financial influence can be dangerous, hidden or disadvantaged in ABC; Witte specifically lists money-related crime.
- events/manifestations: Insolvency, disadvantageous legal influence and benefit through renouncing power are separate possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: Witte states a cause for its crime example; this does not establish that all people lacking money become criminals.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes insolvency and crime, while ABC includes successful influence, joy and advantage through relinquishing power. Coercion is not the only possible expression supported by these entries.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Hades = Vulcanus`, [Witte](#witte-entry) lists insolvency and crimes caused by lack of money. [ABC](#abc-entry) focuses on financial power: its dangerous or hidden influence, legal disadvantage, and the past's influence. It also names strength and joy through renunciation and advantage through giving up power. The formula therefore concerns difficult financial power as well as withdrawal from it; it does not reduce every expression to coercion or criminal action.
