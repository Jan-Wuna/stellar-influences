## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Innovations not realized because of a shortage of money. Fertility weakened through sickness. Limited regenerative ability.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success through renunciation, successful efforts in need, joy through service benefits, dangerous money production, criminal money production, criminal finance handling, legal services in need, and traditional joy fire

## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Hades = Zeus`: creative or productive drive being cut back by lack, illness, or insufficient means. It can show plans that cannot ignite because the material basis is too compromised.
