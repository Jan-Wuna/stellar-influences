## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 212, Jupiter/Hades section, Zeus row.

Innovations not realized because of a shortage of money. Fertility weakened through sickness. Limited regenerative ability.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 335; one-based PDF page 166, Jupiter/Hades section, Zeus row.

Success through acts of renunciation, successful efforts in need, joy through services rendered, dangerous money production, criminal money production, criminal financial management, legal services in need, and traditional celebratory fire

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Witte describes unrealized innovations, weakened fertility and limited regeneration; ABC adds effort, service and money production.
- psychology: ABC includes joy through services and success through acts of renunciation.
- body/health: Witte lists fertility weakened through sickness and limited regenerative ability, without diagnostic criteria.
- social/relationship: Service and legal assistance in need occur in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Unfunded innovations, successful effort in need, dangerous or criminal money production and celebratory fire are distinct examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's successful efforts qualify Witte's blocked innovations; the health examples do not establish outcomes for a particular person.

## Contradictions

Witte's unrealized innovations and limited regenerative ability coexist with ABC's successful efforts in need. They describe different possibilities rather than an inevitable failure of productive activity.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Hades = Zeus`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes innovations prevented by lack of money and separately lists weakened fertility through sickness and limited regeneration. [ABC](#abc-entry) allows successful efforts in need, joy through service and success through renunciation, alongside dangerous or criminal money production and financial management. Productive activity under difficult conditions is a shared theme, but the cited material includes both frustrated projects and effective work.
