## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Jupiter = Admetos`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) holds contentment in restricted circumstances beside a slowing or stopping of financial flow. Raw materials can bring gains, and money may be gladly spent, while income can cease and losses or expenses occur. Rest and a stationary currency market specify the restrictive side. The strong optimism, financial power and excellent credit formerly copied here belong to the adjacent Vulcanus entry and do not support this orientation.
