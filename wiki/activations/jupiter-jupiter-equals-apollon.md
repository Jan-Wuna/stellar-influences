## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Jupiter = Apollon`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects success and happy experiences with growth, economic increase and successful enterprises. Science, trades and law are concrete routes to money in the entry; capital, high finance and money in circulation describe its larger scale. Luck and happiness can extend to many people.
