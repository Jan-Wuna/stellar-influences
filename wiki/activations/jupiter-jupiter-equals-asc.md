## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 205, Jupiter/Jupiter section, Asc row; printed 'form others' is retained as a source typo for 'from others'.

Luck with other people; or other peoples luck (fortune). To experience good deeds form others. Prospering surroundings. Wealthy friends or companions. Fortunate acquaintances. Happy relations to the surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Good fortune involving other people and prosperous surroundings.
- psychology: Happy relations to one's surroundings.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Beneficial deeds from others, wealthy friends or companions and fortunate acquaintances.
- events/manifestations: Prospering surroundings and encounters with fortunate people are the explicit examples.
- conflicts/notes: Single-source repeated-pair activation; the source typo 'form others' does not change the social meaning.

## Contradictions

The row gives favorable social examples and includes both one's luck with others and other people's fortune. No second-source disagreement is available.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Jupiter = Asc`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) locates good fortune in contact with other people: fortunate acquaintances, wealthy friends or companions, helpful deeds and prosperous surroundings. It can describe either luck in those relationships or the fortune of the people encountered. Happy relations to the environment are the stated emphasis; no broader health or achievement claim is needed to explain this orientation.
