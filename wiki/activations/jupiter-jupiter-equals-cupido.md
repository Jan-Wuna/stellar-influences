## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Jupiter = Cupido`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places happiness and success in shared life: family, marriage, partnerships, communities and associations. The arts can bring both success and money, while companies and financial services give organizational examples. Money through marriage or relatives is also explicit. The focus is successful participation and association; the source does not attach Neptune's speculation or Pluto's fluctuations to this row.
