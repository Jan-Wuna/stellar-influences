## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 206, Jupiter/Jupiter section, Hades row; glyph and neighboring row boundaries visually checked.

Saddened disturbed or hidden happiness. Lack of money. To be lucky with raw materials, antiquities, old materials, junk or on the black market. Clouded happiness. Money losses. Bargaining. Dirty financial transactions. Money laundering. Poor cash flow.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Disturbed or hidden happiness and shortage of money.
- psychology: Sadness and clouded happiness.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Bargaining and financial transactions appear; no specific relationship role is assigned.
- events/manifestations: Luck with raw materials, antiquities, old materials or junk; losses, money laundering and poor cash flow.
- conflicts/notes: Ordinary dealings in old materials and illicit financial examples are separate possibilities, not equivalent moral categories.

## Contradictions

The row permits luck with old materials alongside lack of money and losses. That range does not make dealing in antiques or raw materials evidence of wrongdoing.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Jupiter = Hades`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) puts disturbed or hidden happiness beside money shortage, losses and poor cash flow. It also allows luck with raw materials, antiquities, old materials and junk. Black-market dealings, dirty transactions and money laundering form a separate illicit group within the examples. The shared theme is fortune under difficult or obscured financial conditions, without treating every trade in old materials as criminal.
