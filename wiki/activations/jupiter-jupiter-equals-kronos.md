### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 206, Jupiter/Jupiter section, Kronos row; glyph and neighboring row boundaries visually checked.

Formula of a wealthy man, or lucky and successful manager. Bankers. Independence in money matters. Philanthropist. Great luck and fortune. Government regulations for the monetary system and for the national banks. State justice.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Jupiter = Kronos`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links wealth and good fortune with successful management, banking and financial independence. Philanthropy is another named role. Government regulation of money and national banks, together with state justice, extends the formula into public financial authority. These concrete roles explain its administrative emphasis without assuming that all expressions require high social rank.
