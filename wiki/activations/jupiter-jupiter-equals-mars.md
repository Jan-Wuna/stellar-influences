## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 205, Jupiter/Jupiter section, Mars row; glyph and neighboring row boundaries visually checked.

Joyous happenings. Betrothal. Fortunate deed. To create something. To produce. Successful propagation. Pregnancy. Generation. Children. Fruits. Lucky deed. Joy of work. Fortunate accomplishments. Much to do. Work or activity for justice or money.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Fortunate deeds, creation, production and successful activity.
- psychology: Joy in work and accomplishment.
- body/health: Pregnancy and successful propagation are historical source examples, not a fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: Betrothal and children are named alongside activity for justice or money.
- events/manifestations: Production, much work and fortunate accomplishments.
- conflicts/notes: The former love-bliss, goldsmith and plump-woman passage belonged to Venus; this page now uses the Mars row.

## Contradictions

The correct Mars row is favorable. The former material about lovers' bliss and balanced finances belonged to Venus and does not qualify the Mars entry.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Jupiter = Mars`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes fortune active through deeds, work, production and accomplishment. Joy in work and having much to do sit beside activity for money or justice. Betrothal, pregnancy, children and successful propagation are additional source examples of productive or generative results. They should remain examples rather than promises about work success or fertility.
