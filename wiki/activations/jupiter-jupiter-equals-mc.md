## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 205, Jupiter/Jupiter section, MC row; glyph and neighboring row boundaries visually checked.

Blissful feelings. To be positive of one’s own direction in life. An optimist. To be well-wishing and benevolent. Harmless, good natured or contented. To feel happy. Reach ones own goals with luck and success. The own attitude to money. Moment of happiness.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Blissful feelings, optimism and confidence in one's direction.
- psychology: Benevolence, contentment and feeling happy are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Well-wishing and a good-natured attitude.
- events/manifestations: Reaching one's goals with luck and success; a moment of happiness.
- conflicts/notes: The row concerns personal direction and attitude to money; vocation is not specifically named.

## Contradictions

The entry is consistently favorable and supplies no second-source disagreement. Its momentary happiness and goal-reaching examples need not describe an entire life course.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Jupiter = MC`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) centers on a happy personal outlook: optimism, benevolence, contentment and confidence in one's direction. It also names reaching one's own goals with luck and success, one's attitude to money and a moment of happiness. This is a personal orientation toward good fortune; the text does not restrict it to vocation or guarantee lifelong success.
