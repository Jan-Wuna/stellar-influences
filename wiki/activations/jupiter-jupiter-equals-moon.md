## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Jupiter = Moon`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins faith and happy hours with contentment, using women and a nation as its historical examples. Its public scope also includes luck in public life and national justice or jurisdiction. Happiness can therefore be personal or collective in this row, without requiring a claim that life is universally supportive.
