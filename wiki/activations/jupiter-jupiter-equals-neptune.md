## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Jupiter = Neptune`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) combines a lucky mood and easily acquired money with uncertain possessions, speculation, shortage and losses. Financial transactions can carry this mixture of apparent ease and uncertainty. Imagined happiness adds a subjective qualification.
