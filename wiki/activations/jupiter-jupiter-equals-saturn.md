## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 205, Jupiter/Jupiter section, Saturn row; factor glyph and neighboring row boundaries visually checked.

“Ripe Old Age”. Inconstant success. Successful separations. Separates from luck. Buildings. Change of residence. To have no luck. Money losses. Contentment in being alone. Separation from happiness or happy separation. Basic separation formula for birth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Inconstant success, separation and contentment in being alone.
- psychology: Solitary contentment or separation from happiness.
- body/health: The row names ripe old age and a separation formula for birth; it gives no diagnosis or timing instruction.
- social/relationship: Successful or happy separation is possible.
- events/manifestations: Buildings, change of residence, money loss and separation.
- conflicts/notes: The former Venus continuation about love and enjoying money is excluded; the birth formula remains an attributed source phrase.

## Contradictions

Witte includes both separation from happiness and happy separation, as well as contentment alone and loss of luck. These are explicit alternatives rather than one uniformly unfavorable outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Jupiter = Saturn`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) qualifies success through change, loss and separation. A separation may be successful or happy, and being alone may bring contentment, while other examples involve loss of money or happiness. Buildings and a change of residence give concrete settings. The row also lists ripe old age and calls this a basic separation formula for birth, without supplying timing procedures.
