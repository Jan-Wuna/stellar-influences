Lovers bliss. Rapture of joy. Goldsmiths. Well-balanced finances. Plump woman. Happiness in love. To enjoy money. Legal peace and justice.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Happiness in love, enjoyment and well-balanced finances.
- psychology: Lovers' bliss and rapture of joy.
- body/health: The source's 'plump woman' is a historical bodily example without diagnostic detail.
- social/relationship: Happiness in love and legal peace.
- events/manifestations: Goldsmiths, enjoyment of money and balanced finances.
- conflicts/notes: The prior speech/legal-correspondence passage belonged to Mercury; Venus's correct row is restored.

## Contradictions

The row is favorable and joins love, money and legal peace. Its bodily example does not define health or explain the other indications.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Jupiter = Venus`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins happiness in love and strong joy with enjoyment of money and balanced finances. Goldsmiths provide a craft or occupation example, while legal peace and justice extend the favorable picture beyond pleasure. The historical description of a plump woman is retained separately, without turning it into a health judgment. Speech talent and financial correspondence belong to a different row.
