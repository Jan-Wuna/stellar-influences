### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 205, Jupiter/Jupiter section, Vernal Point row; factor glyph and neighboring row boundaries visually checked.

Good fortune in general. Success in general. General contentment or possessions. Money, law, enrichment in general public. Success, luck, joy and happiness in general life or in the world. International justice and jurisdiction.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: General fortune, success, contentment and enrichment.
- psychology: Joy and happiness at a general or public level.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: The public, the world and international justice are explicit scopes.
- events/manifestations: Money, law, general possessions and international jurisdiction.
- conflicts/notes: The row broadens fortune into public life; visible recognition is not specifically required.

## Contradictions

The row is favorable and provides no contrary claim or second-source comparison. Its public scope does not imply that every individual shares the same circumstances.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Jupiter = Vernal Point`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) extends good fortune, success and contentment into general life and the public world. Money, possessions and enrichment supply material examples, while law, international justice and jurisdiction supply legal ones. The orientation concerns a broad public scope rather than a particular private relationship or a guarantee of universally favorable conditions.
