## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 206, Jupiter/Jupiter section, Vulcanus row; factor glyph and neighboring row boundaries visually checked.

Optimism. Confidence. Huge luck. Success by force. The power of money and finances. Very influential financial power. Benefice energy. Benevolent power. Successful power. The great optimist. Excellent credit.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Great luck, financial power and successful or benevolent power.
- psychology: Optimism and confidence.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Influential financial power and credit are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Success by force, power of money and excellent credit.
- conflicts/notes: The printed phrase 'Benefice energy' is retained without silently normalizing it; the former wisdom/conscience fragment belonged to Poseidon.

## Contradictions

The row combines benevolent power with success by force. It does not explain how those examples relate, and they need not characterize the same action.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Jupiter = Vulcanus`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes optimism, confidence and great luck together with the force of money and influential financial power. Excellent credit is a concrete financial example. Benevolent and successful power appear alongside success by force, so the account retains both uses of strength rather than assuming a uniformly gentle expression. Wisdom and conscience are not supplied by this row.
