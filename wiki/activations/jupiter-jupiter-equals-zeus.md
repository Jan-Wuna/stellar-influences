## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 206, Jupiter/Jupiter section, Zeus row; factor glyph and neighboring row boundaries visually checked.

Successful creation. Fortunate activity. To bring good luck and benefits. Beneficial acts. To be compelled to give. Time of fortunate activity. To be fortunate in connection with machines. To produce something. Money through creativity. Successful procreation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful creation, production and fortunate activity.
- psychology: The row does not isolate a psychological trait.
- body/health: Successful procreation is listed without diagnostic or predictive detail.
- social/relationship: Beneficial acts and being compelled to give both appear.
- events/manifestations: Machines, money through creativity and producing something.
- conflicts/notes: Giving can be compelled rather than freely chosen; constructive force is an inference rather than a separate source phrase.

## Contradictions

The favorable activity includes being compelled to give. That specific qualification should remain rather than turning every beneficial act into voluntary generosity.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Jupiter = Zeus`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects fortune with creating, producing and doing something beneficial. Machines and money through creativity give that activity concrete forms, and successful procreation is another listed example. The entry also says one can be compelled to give, which qualifies an entirely voluntary or generous reading. Its common focus is productive activity with favorable results or benefits.
