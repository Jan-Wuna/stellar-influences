## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 218; one-based PDF page 214, Jupiter/Kronos section, Admetos row.

Spending money, one likes to do. Luck for a few in a small way. To be a leader of a few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 339; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Admetos row; neighboring boundaries visually checked.

Special financial circulation, a large financial blockade, restricted state law, restriction of state funds, success through particular thoroughness, consolidation of the constitutional state, thorough legal authority, and superiority through legal knowledge

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Witte limits leadership or luck to a few; ABC adds restricted state funds and success through thoroughness.
- psychology: Witte describes spending money gladly; ABC emphasizes thoroughness.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Leadership of a few in Witte; legal authority and the constitutional state in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Financial blockade, restricted public funds, consolidation and legal knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: Small scale is explicit in Witte, but exclusivity and selective influence should not replace ABC's legal and financial particulars.

## Contradictions

Witte's luck for a few coexists with ABC's large blockade and restrictions, while thoroughness and legal knowledge can bring success. The entry does not make concentration uniformly favorable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Kronos = Admetos`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes gladly spending money, luck on a small scale and leadership of a few. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds financial circulation and blockade, restrictions on state law or funds, and success through thoroughness or legal knowledge. The shared picture involves constrained financial or authoritative scope, with competent, detailed work as a favorable possibility rather than a guarantee of unrestricted power.
