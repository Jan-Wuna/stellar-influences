## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 218; one-based PDF page 214, Jupiter/Kronos section, Apollon row.

Expansive luck for many. Progress in trade and science leading to prospering developments. Monetary income through trades or industry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 339; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Apollon row; neighboring boundaries visually checked.

Prestige and success through money, successful scientific authority, a successful constitutional state, great financial successes, great economic success, growth of state funds, superior legal knowledge, and advantages through important knowledge

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Prospering development through trade and science; economic and financial success.
- psychology: The entries do not specify a separate psychological trait.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Luck for many in Witte; scientific authority and the constitutional state in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Trade or industrial income, growth of state funds and benefits through knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: ABC concerns scientific authority and growth of public funds, not authorship or money administration.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize favorable development. Their science, trade, legal and state examples differ in setting without providing an explicit contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Kronos = Apollon`, [Witte](#witte-entry) extends good fortune to many through trade, science and industry. [ABC](#abc-entry) reinforces the economic scale with financial success and growing state funds, while scientific authority and legal knowledge add forms of expertise. Prestige through money is one example, not the whole meaning. The orientation concerns broad prosperity supported by knowledge and organized economic activity.
