### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 338; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Asc row; neighboring boundaries visually checked.

Special success of a place, special local law, successful independence of the partner, financial authority of a place, joy over the partner's prestige, legal authority at the place, an important financial location, successes of the state in its surroundings, and important partner rights

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Social contact with leading people in Witte; successful partners and places in ABC.
- psychology: Joy over the partner's prestige in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Leading persons, a partner's independence and partner rights.
- events/manifestations: Local financial or legal authority, an important financial location and state successes in its surroundings.
- conflicts/notes: The former opening contained the end of MC's row; the complete Asc row concerns partner/place, not first-person financial superiority.

## Contradictions

Witte's brief leading-person contact and ABC's partner/place details are complementary. Neither says social contact necessarily creates prosperity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Kronos = Asc`, [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies contact with leading people. [ABC](#abc-entry) gives more specific settings: a partner's independence and prestige, local success, financial or legal authority and an important financial location. Partner rights and the state's successes in its surroundings extend that relational or environmental scope. The common focus is significant people and places, without assuming that access to them automatically produces wealth.
