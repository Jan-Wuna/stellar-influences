## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 218; one-based PDF page 214, Jupiter/Kronos section, Cupido row.

Leader of master in art. Organizations dealing with exceptional amounts of money, e.g. pension funds, the stock market, state banks.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 339; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Cupido row; neighboring boundaries visually checked.

prestige of a legal community, authority of a financial community, special success of a community, successful state community, great happiness of a community, legal community of the state, and state financial community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Leadership in art and organizations handling exceptional sums in Witte; successful legal or financial communities in ABC.
- psychology: ABC includes great happiness of a community.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Legal communities, financial communities and state communities are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Pension funds, stock market, state banks and organizational financial authority.
- conflicts/notes: Witte prints the awkward phrase 'Leader of master in art'; it is preserved rather than silently rewritten.

## Contradictions

The entries share organizational and financial authority, while Witte also names art. Neither requires that every group possess both cultural prestige and large capital.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Kronos = Cupido`, [Witte](#witte-entry) places leadership in the arts beside organizations dealing with unusually large sums, such as pension funds, stock markets and state banks. [ABC](#abc-entry) describes the prestige, authority, success or happiness of legal, financial and state communities. The orientation therefore concerns successful collective institutions and their authority; the artistic and financial examples need not describe the same organization.
