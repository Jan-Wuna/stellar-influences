## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 218; one-based PDF page 214, Jupiter/Kronos section, Hades row.

To accomplish much with little. Thriftiness. Gain in money from things which are taken out of the earth. Owner of mines.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 339; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Hades row; full row and neighbors checked against the scan.

Special success through renunciation, successes of a criminal authority, joy in state service, lack of money for the state, great joy from the past, great happiness in secret, criminal state law, a constitutional state in distress, and state financial crime

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Economy and gains from the earth in Witte; state financial hardship, hidden happiness and criminal authority in ABC.
- psychology: Thrift in Witte; joy in service, the past or secrecy in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Mine ownership in Witte; state service, law and authority in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Accomplishing much with little, extraction-related gains, public money shortage and financial crime.
- conflicts/notes: Thrift and lawful service remain distinct from criminal authority; ABC does not equate all state activity or mining with wrongdoing.

## Contradictions

Witte's effective economy and ABC's success or joy coexist with ABC's distressed state and criminal examples. The entries do not support an exclusively profitable or virtuous picture.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Kronos = Hades`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes accomplishing much with little, thrift and financial gains from things taken out of the earth, including mine ownership. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds success through renunciation and joy in state service or the past, alongside public money shortage, criminal authority and financial crime. The synthesis holds resource economy beside difficult or concealed public finances, keeping lawful service and criminal examples distinct.
