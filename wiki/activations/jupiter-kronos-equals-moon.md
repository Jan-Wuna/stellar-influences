## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 218; one-based PDF page 214, Jupiter/Kronos section, Moon row.

Woman who’s a successful manager. Persons or a people under the impression of great luck.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 338; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Moon row; complete row checked between neighboring glyphs.

A particularly successful woman, dominant emotional joy, a successful people's state, a woman's successful authority, a woman as legal authority, the financial hierarchy of a people, a strong sense of justice, and a happy independent woman

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful management in Witte; female authority, joy and public law in ABC.
- psychology: Great luck as an impression in Witte; strong joy and sense of justice in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: The historical examples name a successful female manager, a woman with legal authority and a people's state.
- events/manifestations: Success, independence and a people's financial hierarchy.
- conflicts/notes: The former ABC opening copied Sun's financial-center continuation; protective or reassuring authority is not explicitly stated.

## Contradictions

The sources share success, authority and collective fortune. Witte's impression of great luck is an experience rather than proof of universal prosperity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Kronos = Moon`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a woman who manages successfully and people impressed by great luck. [ABC](#abc-entry) likewise names successful women, independence, emotional joy and legal authority, then extends the examples to a people's state and its financial hierarchy. A strong sense of justice is also explicit. The shared emphasis is successful personal or collective authority, without adding an unsupported protective or reassuring role.
