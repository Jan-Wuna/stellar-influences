## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 218; one-based PDF page 214, Jupiter/Kronos section, Neptune row.

Dissolving of good luck. A fortunate leadership dissolves through discord. To attain leadership through fraud and subterfuge.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 339; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Neptune row; complete row checked between neighboring glyphs.

Special joy in water, special expectations of happiness, disappointment over state law, great successes through intuition, legal uncertainty in the state, uncertainty through state funds, important advantage through sensitivity, and important air/water law

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Dissolving luck or leadership in Witte; legal/financial uncertainty and intuition in ABC.
- psychology: Discord, disappointed expectations and intuitive success are different source possibilities.
- body/health: Joy in water is a stated setting, not a bodily or medical claim.
- social/relationship: Leadership attained by fraud in Witte; state law and funds in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Dissolution of leadership, legal uncertainty, intuitive success and air/water law.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's former ending belonged to Pluto; sensitivity is Feingefühl, not fine tuning.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes dissolution and deception, while ABC also gives intuitive success, joy and advantage through sensitivity. These favorable examples must remain beside uncertainty rather than being flattened into inevitable loss.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Kronos = Neptune`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes good luck or leadership dissolving through discord, and leadership obtained through fraud. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointment over state law and uncertainty involving law or state funds, but also intuition, sensitivity and joy in water. Its air/water-law example gives a specific legal context. The common concern is uncertain authority or fortune, qualified by the source's favorable intuitive possibilities.
