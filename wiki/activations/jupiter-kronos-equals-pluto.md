## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 218; one-based PDF page 214, Jupiter/Kronos section, Pluto row.

A great fortune is on the way. Practice makes perfect. Changes in the high finance sector.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 339; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Pluto row; complete row checked between neighboring glyphs.

Special financial development, development of state finances, successful unfolding of authority, successful development in the state, great successful changes, development of state law, growing success through independence, and important legal development

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Great fortune ahead and practice in Witte; financial, legal and authoritative development in ABC.
- psychology: Witte's practice-makes-perfect phrase supports developing proficiency.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Authority and state institutions are explicit ABC settings.
- events/manifestations: Changes in high finance, state funds and law; increasing success through independence.
- conflicts/notes: The former wrinkling garble was a broken unfolding-of-authority clause, and the opening financial-development clauses were missing.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize favorable development. They provide examples of change rather than a promise that every transformation or financial development succeeds.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Kronos = Pluto`, [Witte](#witte-entry) places great fortune ahead, links improvement with practice and names change in high finance. [ABC](#abc-entry) extends that developmental theme to state finances, authority and law, with increasing success through independence. The shared emphasis is favorable growth or improvement in financial and authoritative settings; its concrete examples are more specific than a generic breakthrough narrative.
