## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 218; one-based PDF page 214, Jupiter/Kronos section, Poseidon row.

Happiness and good luck through intelligence and reason of the superiors, the chief or the government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 339; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Poseidon row.

Success through intellectual dominance, a successful cultural state, the goodness of an intellectual authority, joy over state culture, the state's legal culture, state funds for culture, superior legal understanding, advantage through intellectual greatness, and important cultural law

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Fortune through intelligent, reasonable superiors in Witte; intellectual and cultural authority in ABC.
- psychology: Intelligence and reason; ABC also names goodness of an intellectual authority.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Superiors, a chief or government in Witte; cultural and legal institutions in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Cultural funding, legal understanding and successful cultural administration.
- conflicts/notes: ABC says superior legal understanding, not illegitimate knowledge; goodness belongs to authority, not authorship.

## Contradictions

The sources are favorable but differ in scope: Witte attributes benefit to reasonable superiors, while ABC includes intellectual dominance, culture and legal understanding. They do not make every authority benevolent.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Kronos = Poseidon`, [Witte](#witte-entry) attributes happiness and luck to the intelligence and reason of superiors, a chief or government. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds intellectual authority, its goodness, cultural success and legal understanding. Public cultural funding and legal culture give this institutional settings. The shared emphasis is benefit from capable thought and authority, with the source's specific cultural and legal examples preserved.
