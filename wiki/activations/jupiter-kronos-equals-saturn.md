## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 218; one-based PDF page 214, Jupiter/Kronos section, Saturn row.

To lose the leadership. To tumble down from on high.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 339; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Saturn row.

Special success through patience, special benefits in old age, great money losses, great financial losses, problems of the legal state, state money problems, priority financial problems, important legal tasks, and loss of state funds

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Loss of leadership in Witte; substantial financial loss and state problems in ABC.
- psychology: ABC allows success through patience.
- body/health: Benefits in old age are named, without a bodily condition or medical claim.
- social/relationship: Leadership loss and problems of the legal state.
- events/manifestations: State money problems, loss of state funds and important legal tasks.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's patience and old-age benefits qualify the difficult examples; weight, duty and decline were added by the former synthesis.

## Contradictions

Witte is adverse, while ABC also includes success through patience and benefits in old age. These favorable possibilities should remain beside leadership and financial losses.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Kronos = Saturn`, [Witte](#witte-entry) gives loss of leadership and a fall from an elevated position. [ABC](#abc-entry) extends the difficult side to major financial losses, state money problems and important legal tasks. It also allows special success through patience and advantages in old age. The orientation therefore includes limits on authority or public finances, with patience as a favorable alternative rather than an inevitable fall.
