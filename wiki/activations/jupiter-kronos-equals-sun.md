## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 218; one-based PDF page 214, Jupiter/Kronos section, Sun row.

The happy passed days. High personage. Successful leader. The man who is happy in his capacity as leader.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 338; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Sun row.

Special success of the man, a successful state center, great personal success, personal success through independence, a state legal center, personal legal superiority, the center of a financial hierarchy, and the center of a constitutional state

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful leadership in Witte; personal success and important state or financial centers in ABC.
- psychology: Happiness in one's role as leader.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte's historical high personage or male leader; state legal and financial centers in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Personal success through independence and legal superiority.
- conflicts/notes: The former ABC opening copied Asc's place/partner tail; vitality and confidence rising with recognition are not explicit claims.

## Contradictions

Both entries are favorable and use leadership or centrality. Happiness as a leader does not by itself establish a physical or psychological mechanism.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Kronos = Sun`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes successful leadership and happiness in the role of a leader, using a high personage as an example. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds personal success through independence and central positions in state, legal or financial structures. Its personal legal superiority is another form of prominence. The common emphasis is successful personal standing or central responsibility, without adding a claim about bodily vitality.
