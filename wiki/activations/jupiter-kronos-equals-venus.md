## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 218; one-based PDF page 214, Jupiter/Kronos section, Venus row.

To be able to inflame with love. To be favored by the authorities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 338; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Venus row. Two obscured qualifiers are marked in the translation; readable clauses were checked in the high-resolution raw crop.

Prestige through artistic success, authority for law and peace, special joy through harmony, [modifier obscured in the scan] artistic success, great joy through love, great success in peace, preference of the [subject obscured in the scan] for money, and preference for state funds.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Love and favor from authorities in Witte; artistic success, harmony and peace in the legible ABC clauses.
- psychology: Joy through harmony and love.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Official favor, law and peace are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Artistic success and preference for state funds are legible ABC possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: Two ABC qualifiers remain physically obscured on printed338/PDF168; no unverified reconstruction is used. State funds are not state ownership.

## Contradictions

Witte's official favor and ABC's artistic or peace-related success can coexist. The damaged ABC art-success modifier and money-preference subject remain unknown and cannot support a further comparison.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Kronos = Venus`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes kindling love and receiving favor from authorities. The readable [ABC clauses](#abc-entry) add prestige through artistic success, joy through harmony or love, authority for law and peace, and success in peace. A preference for state funds is also legible. Two qualifiers in the ABC scan remain obscured, so the synthesis does not assign them a subject or infer a particular form of patronage.
