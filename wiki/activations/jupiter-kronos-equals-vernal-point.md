#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 338; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Vernal Point row; complete row visually checked.

Special advantages for the public, success of the state in public life, great happiness in public life, a public constitutional state, public prestige through money, public state finances, and state law in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Financial authority and the global financial system in Witte; public state success and finance in ABC.
- psychology: ABC lists great happiness in public life.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Tax or financial authorities, the public and the state are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Public advantages, public prestige through money and state law.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's former European Parliament phrase had no basis in the seven-clause row; no specific parliament is named.

## Contradictions

Both sources give a broad public scope, but Witte's global finance and ABC's state-law examples do not identify a particular institution.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Kronos = Vernal Point`, [Witte](#witte-entry) gives financial giants, the global financial system and general tax or financial authority. [ABC](#abc-entry) likewise moves the formula into public life through state success, public finances, legal structures and prestige through money. Public advantage and happiness are also listed. The emphasis is public financial and legal authority; no particular parliament is identified.
