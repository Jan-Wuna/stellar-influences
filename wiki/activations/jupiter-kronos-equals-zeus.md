## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 214; printed page 218, Jupiter/Kronos section, Zeus row.

Master of productive work. Luck and good fortune through or with machines. Preparation for a initial public offering.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 339; one-based PDF page 168, Jupiter/Kronos section, Zeus row; complete row visually checked.

Efforts within the constitutional state, special financial accomplishments, successful state leadership, great efforts toward success, a creative financial hierarchy, a creative constitutional state, successful state achievements, and important legal goals

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Productive mastery and machine-related fortune in Witte; effective state or financial work in ABC.
- psychology: Great efforts toward success are explicit in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: State leadership, financial hierarchy and legal goals.
- events/manifestations: Machine-related fortune, preparation for an initial public offering and successful state achievements.
- conflicts/notes: Large-scale output and technological profit are broader inferences than the listed work and machine examples.

## Contradictions

The entries are favorable but distinguish private productive or financial preparation from state leadership and legal goals. They do not require these to occur together.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Kronos = Zeus`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes mastery of productive work, fortune with machines and preparation for an initial public offering. [ABC](#abc-entry) extends effective effort into state leadership, financial accomplishment and legal goals. Creativity appears in its financial hierarchy and constitutional-state examples. The shared focus is productive, organized work, with both industrial and public-administration settings.
