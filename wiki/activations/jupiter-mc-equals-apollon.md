## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 65, Jupiter/MC section, Apollon row.

Luck or money through science or commerce. Happy expansion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 75; one-based PDF page 36, Meridian/Jupiter section, Apollon row; complete row visually checked.

Expansion of my joy, I spread joy, my success with money, my knowledge of success, increase of my money, my luck in trade, my much money, my increasing happiness, experiencing inner joy, and increase of inner peace

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Science or commerce bringing luck and expansion in Witte; increasing personal joy, money and peace in ABC.
- psychology: Spreading joy, increasing happiness and inner peace.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Trade is explicit; larger networks or public status are not specifically named.
- events/manifestations: Money through science or commerce, financial increase and knowledge of success.
- conflicts/notes: The source's repeated first-person and inner-joy wording should not be reduced to public achievement.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize increase and favorable expansion. Their financial and inward examples can coexist without implying that money necessarily produces inner peace.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/MC = Apollon`, [Witte](#witte-entry) connects happy expansion and money with science or commerce. [ABC](#abc-entry) makes increase personal: more joy, money, happiness and inner peace, together with spreading joy and knowledge of success. Trade remains one concrete setting. The common theme is favorable expansion experienced inwardly as well as materially, rather than an exclusively public or network-based success.
