## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A pleasant feeling in society. Contented with married life. Luck through art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success Minute of a community, shared soul joy, happiness of an animated community, my successful community, all my joy, all my money, my artistic success, and my right in the community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal happiness and success are expressed within a community, marriage or art.
- psychology: Contentment and shared inner joy.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim is made.
- social/relationship: Pleasant social participation and satisfaction in married or communal life.
- events/manifestations: Artistic success and a community's successful moment.
- conflicts/notes: The entries join personal and collective satisfaction; they do not establish public advancement as a necessary outcome.

## Contradictions

Social contentment and communal or artistic success are compatible emphases. The two entries do not establish every listed outcome, or independent corroboration merely through repeated themes.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/MC = Cupido`, Cupido gives personal happiness and success a shared setting: society, marriage, community and art. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects social ease and marital contentment with luck through art; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 75 (PDF p. 36), extends this to shared joy and artistic or communal success. The organizing theme is satisfaction through belonging and creative participation.
