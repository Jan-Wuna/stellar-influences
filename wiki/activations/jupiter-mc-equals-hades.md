## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Contentment in modest circumstances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joy and sorrow of the soul, my secret of success, my lack of success, my dangerous success, my money is in danger, my hidden happiness, my past happiness, my successful past, and my financial crime

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal satisfaction in modest circumstances coexists with hidden, past or endangered happiness.
- psychology: Contentment and acceptance in Witte; joy mixed with sorrow in ABC.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: The entries do not specify a relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: Modest conditions, threatened money or success, and hidden or past happiness; ABC also lists financial crime.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's contentment should not erase ABC's loss, danger and concealment motifs; crime remains a source-listed possibility, not an accusation.

## Contradictions

Contentment in modest circumstances does not contradict the possibility of hidden or endangered happiness, but the second emphasis materially broadens the first. ABC's “my financial crime” is a historical source formulation, not a factual allegation.

Source locators in the review cache: ABC printed p. 75 (PDF p. 36); Witte PDF p. 65 (entry start). These parser-derived locators have not been visually verified in this review.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/MC = Hades`, Hades qualifies personal happiness and success through modest conditions, concealment or vulnerability. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supports being content with little; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 75 (PDF p. 36), also gives sorrow, endangered money and hidden or past happiness. A balanced reading therefore preserves both satisfaction under reduced circumstances and anxiety around success. ABC's financial-crime phrase remains attributed rather than defining the person.
