## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Kronos row.

Fortunate leadership. To be conscious of ones own authority. Self-conscious.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 75; one-based PDF p. 36, MC + Jupiter, Kronos row.

Special joy of the soul, my success with the state, my successful self-confidence, my money for the state, my great success, my state governed by law (mein Rechts-Staat), my successful independence, my advantages from the state, and an important moment of success.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal success is expressed as leadership, authority or independence.
- psychology: Awareness of one's own authority and confidence.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Relations with the state or public authority are emphasized in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Fortunate leadership, substantial success and advantages associated with the state.
- conflicts/notes: The entries support authority and independence without specifying a necessary cost or failure.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in scale, from self-awareness to success involving the state, without establishing a direct contradiction. ABC's legible mein Rechts-Staat means a state governed by law; the first-person phrase does not identify a particular state or political role.

Visually verified source rows: ABC printed p. 75, one-based PDF p. 36, MC + Jupiter, Kronos row; Witte one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Kronos row.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/MC = Kronos`, Kronos specifies personal success through authority, leadership and independence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins fortunate leadership with awareness of one's authority, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 75 (PDF p. 36), adds success and advantages in relation to the state. The common thread is effective standing supported by self-confidence.
