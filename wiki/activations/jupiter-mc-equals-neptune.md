## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Neptune row.

The loss of happiness. Expected luck ends in a disappointment. A premonition of loss.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 75; one-based PDF p. 36, MC + Jupiter, Neptune row.

Blissful joy in the air, joy of a sensitive soul, my money for the future, my disappointment over the law, my fortunate intuitions, my future happiness, my weakness for money, a moment of joy in the water, and unknown joy of the soul.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Dreaming of good fortune and success without the right sense or appreciation of reality, desire to gain success without effort. Luck established upon a weak basis, temporary and passing good fortune, disappointments, losses, being angry.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 87, Planets activating the Jupiter/Midheaven midpoint, Neptune paragraph.

To feel an obligation to educate the world, or uplift it in some other way. To have career plans that are unrealistic in that they are too big. Confusion caused by being overwhelmed by too many things at once. You need to use your intuition to know how to grow in your career work.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `280`

#### Munkasey Entry

Illusions and deceptions about your abilities to cope with life’s problems; a lack of progress in expanding your horizons thru study or travel; a need for poetry and music; motivation gained from studying the occult.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Hopes for personal success meet uncertainty, disappointment or an insecure basis.
- psychology: Unrealistic plans, confusion and deceptive confidence coexist with intuition and a wish to uplift others.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Sandbach describes educating or uplifting the world; no defined intimate relationship pattern is given.
- events/manifestations: Passing good fortune, losses, stalled development and motivation through poetry, music or occult study.
- conflicts/notes: ABC retains both future happiness and disappointment over the law, alongside fortunate intuitions and weakness for money. These do not establish that every hope fails.

## Contradictions

Intuitive or uplifting aspirations do not cancel the loss and confusion emphasized in other entries. ABC gives disappointment over the law, future happiness and weakness for money. Its air and water examples remain literal; the source does not explain their setting.

Visually verified source rows: ABC printed p. 75, one-based PDF p. 36, Neptune row; Witte one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Neptune row; Sandbach one-based PDF p. 87, Neptune paragraph under Jupiter/Midheaven. Existing parser locators retained: Ebertin printed p. 244, entry 0862; Munkasey printed p. 280; those two source images were not reopened for this bounded correction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/MC = Neptune`, Neptune specifies personal hopes and success through uncertainty about what is achievable. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin entries](#ebertin-entry) emphasize disappointment or good fortune with a weak foundation; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) connects this with oversized career plans, overwhelm and the wish to uplift others. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 280, adds deceptive assessments of one's abilities alongside an attraction to music, poetry and occult study. Imaginative aspiration is therefore present, but the passages require care over whether confidence has a workable basis.
