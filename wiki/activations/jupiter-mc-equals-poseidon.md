## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Poseidon row.

Mental happiness and health. Good luck through a sensible attitude.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 75; one-based PDF p. 36, MC + Jupiter, Poseidon row.

Intellectual or spiritual happiness and happiness of the soul, my joy in understanding, my financial clarity, my joy in culture, my intellectual or spiritual joy, my intellectual or spiritual right, my success in ideals, my spiritual happiness, clarity through joy of the soul, and a spiritual moment of happiness.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal happiness is associated with understanding, clarity and a sensible attitude.
- psychology: Joy in cognition, culture and spiritual or ideal aims.
- body/health: Witte historically associates the formula with mental health; this is an astrological claim, not a clinical assessment.
- social/relationship: ABC mentions cultural enjoyment without defining an intimate relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: Good fortune through a sensible attitude; ABC also lists financial clarity and ideal success.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's happiness and moment of happiness are legible. The unspecified intellectual or spiritual right is retained without a concrete legal inference.

## Contradictions

Witte's sensible attitude and ABC's cognitive or spiritual joy are compatible emphases, without proving independent corroboration. ABC's Glück and Glücks-Minute are legible as happiness and a moment of happiness. Its broad geistig wording permits intellectual or spiritual meaning; no particular intellectual right is defined.

Visually verified source rows: ABC printed p. 75, one-based PDF p. 36, Poseidon row; Witte one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Poseidon row.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/MC = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies personal happiness through understanding and clarity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects good fortune with a sensible attitude; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 75 (PDF p. 36), adds joy in knowledge, culture and ideal aims. The supported center is satisfaction through an intelligible outlook. Witte's mental-health association remains a historical source claim rather than evidence of anyone's health.
