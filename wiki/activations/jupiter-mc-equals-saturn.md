## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Parting without sorrow. To be glad over a separation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My success through patience, my pain money, my advantage through seriousness, my successful separation, my successful concentration, my financial losses, my joy in old age, concentration on the right, and maturing soul pleasure

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inhibitions coupled with an otherwise happy disposition, the non-utilization of good opportunities offered, the occasional missing of the right moment, unrecognized talent, discontent. A liking for separation from others, a happy change.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Progress in your career happens slowly and may cause frustration. High hopes for one’s career results in feeling dejected. Learning one’s career work requires pa tience and focus.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Increases your personal restraint and tolerance when accepting various pitfalls in life; meticulous and exacting experiences for you to examine and thus learn more about your inner self; a loss of personal prestige or morals.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/MC = Saturn`: slow professional progress, missed openings, and the tension between high hopes and constricting circumstance. Main caution: discontent and underuse of opportunity: talent or promise may be present, but frustration or caution can keep it from being fully realized.
