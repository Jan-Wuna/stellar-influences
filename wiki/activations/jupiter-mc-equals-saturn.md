## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Saturn row.

Parting without sorrow. To be glad over a separation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 75; one-based PDF p. 36, MC + Jupiter, Saturn row.

My success through patience, my compensation for pain and suffering (Schmerzens-Geld), my advantage through seriousness, my successful separation, my successful concentration, my financial losses, my joy in old age, concentration on the law, and maturing joy of the soul.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inhibitions coupled with an otherwise happy disposition, the non-utilization of good opportunities offered, the occasional missing of the right moment, unrecognized talent, discontent. A liking for separation from others, a happy change.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF pp. 86–87, Planets activating the Jupiter/Midheaven midpoint, Saturn paragraph.

Progress in your career happens slowly and may cause frustration. High hopes for one’s career results in feeling dejected. Learning one’s career work requires patience and focus.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `280`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increases your personal restraint and tolerance when accepting various pitfalls in life; meticulous and exacting experiences for you to examine and thus learn more about your inner self; a loss of personal prestige or morals.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal success and happiness are qualified by delay, restraint and separation.
- psychology: Patience and focus coexist with frustration, inhibition and discontent.
- body/health: ABC names compensation for pain and suffering without identifying an injury or medical condition.
- social/relationship: Separation may be welcome or bring relief in Witte and Ebertin.
- events/manifestations: Slow career progress, missed opportunities, loss of prestige or money, and beneficial separation.
- conflicts/notes: The favorable separation motif must remain alongside restricted progress; the entries do not describe every outcome as a loss.

## Contradictions

Welcome separation and discontent over blocked progress describe different circumstances, not a direct contradiction. ABC names compensation for pain and suffering (Schmerzens-Geld), without identifying a particular injury or legal case. Sandbach's Saturn paragraph continues from one-based PDF p. 86 onto p. 87; the word patience is restored across the extraction break.

Visually verified source rows: ABC printed p. 75, one-based PDF p. 36, Saturn row; Witte one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Saturn row. Sandbach one-based PDF pp. 86–87, Saturn paragraph under Jupiter/Midheaven. Existing parser locators retained: Ebertin printed p. 244, entry 0860; Munkasey printed p. 280; those two source images were not reopened for this bounded correction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/MC = Saturn`, Saturn gives personal growth a demanding pace: patience, concentration and restraint are needed, while missed openings or slow progress may bring frustration. The [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) and [Munkasey entries](#munkasey-entry) support that tension between promise and restriction. Yet the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly gives gladness at a separation, and Ebertin includes a happy change. The formula therefore also allows relief through parting or reducing commitments, rather than only disappointment.
