## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

(J) Sudden happiness. Sudden personal luck.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting soul joy, happy event minute, my exciting success, my new happiness, my excitement about money, my joy in technology, my beneficial reform, my successes in technology, and surprising mental satisfaction

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Optimism, a lucky hand in enterprise or new ventures, ability to utilize the right moment. Sudden successes, recognition, fortunate adjustment to new circumstances or conditions.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A talent for finding just the right spiritual wisdom that one needs. Sudden good fortune in one’s career work, Progress in that realm happens erratically.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Growing and maturing despite upsets or surprises which offer new insight into people or self; personal growth and insight achieved thru the study or application of science or astrology; disruptions to your needs for security.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/MC = Uranus`: sudden recognition, fortunate enterprise, and the ability to seize the right moment when circumstances shift. Main caution: irregularity: progress may come erratically, and the same suddenness that brings success can also destabilize continuity or judgment.
