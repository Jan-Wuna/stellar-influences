## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Vernal Point row.

The state of mind effects the general world. General contentment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 74; one-based PDF p. 36, MC + Jupiter, Vernal Point row.

My success in public, my joy in public, my happiness in public life, my public right (mein öffentliches Recht), my advantage in public, my finances in public life, and joy of the soul in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal happiness and success are expressed in the public sphere.
- psychology: General contentment and a state of mind that affects the wider world.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: The setting is public life rather than a specified intimate relationship.
- events/manifestations: Public joy, success, advantage and finances in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's legible mein öffentliches Recht means my public right, without identifying a particular entitlement; it does not say right to life.

## Contradictions

The public-life emphasis is shared without establishing that every listed financial or social outcome follows. ABC's complete seven-clause row includes joy of the soul in public and public right; the particular legal context is unspecified.

Visually verified source rows: ABC printed p. 74, one-based PDF p. 36, MC + Jupiter, Vernal Point row; Witte one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Vernal Point row.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/MC = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point places personal happiness and success in the wider public setting. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects a person's state of mind with the general world, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 74 (PDF p. 36), lists joy, advantage and success in public life. The formula therefore concerns how private contentment is expressed beyond the individual, with public finances as an additional source-listed setting.
