## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Vulcanus row.

To have great optimism.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 75; one-based PDF p. 36, MC + Jupiter, Vulcanus row.

Influence of a happy moment, successful influence of the soul, my influence through money, my advantages in energy, my energy for success, my financial power, my tremendous happiness, my strength from joy, my legal advantage, and strong joy of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal happiness and success are expressed with strong optimism, energy or influence.
- psychology: Great optimism and strength drawn from joy.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: ABC describes influence through money without defining a relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: Energetic success, financial influence and a sense of powerful happiness.
- conflicts/notes: The entries state intensity and optimism; they do not themselves give an overreach warning.

## Contradictions

ABC broadens Witte's brief optimism statement to influence and money. The scan clearly gives a happy moment, tremendous happiness and legal advantage; no particular legal circumstance or scale of success is stated.

Visually verified source rows: ABC printed p. 75, one-based PDF p. 36, MC + Jupiter, Vulcanus row; Witte one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Vulcanus row.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/MC = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus intensifies personal happiness and success into strong optimism and an ability to exert influence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supplies great optimism; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 75 (PDF p. 36), adds energy toward success, influence through money and strength arising from joy. This supports a forceful experience of confidence, while leaving the actual scale of success unspecified.
