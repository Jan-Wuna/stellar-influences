## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Zeus row.

Happiness over accomplishments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 75; one-based PDF p. 36, MC + Jupiter, Zeus row.

Successful guidance of the soul, joy of a creative soul, my success in procreation, my successful leadership, my financial efforts, my successful accomplishments, my legal goals, and creative joy of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal happiness is linked with accomplishment and creative achievement.
- psychology: Joy in making or completing something.
- body/health: ABC historically lists procreative success; the page does not establish a fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: No specific relationship pattern is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Achievements, financial efforts and legal goals in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's Seelen-Führung and erfolgreiche Führung give guidance of the soul and successful leadership. No particular leadership office or institution is named.

## Contradictions

The entries share an accomplishment theme without establishing that success follows every effort. ABC adds successful guidance of the soul and leadership beside creativity, financial efforts and legal goals; it names no particular office.

Visually verified source rows: ABC printed p. 75, one-based PDF p. 36, MC + Jupiter, Zeus row; Witte one-based PDF p. 65, Meridian + Jupiter, Zeus row.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/MC = Zeus`, Zeus specifies personal happiness through accomplishment. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states joy over what has been achieved, and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 75 (PDF p. 36), adds creative joy, efforts and successful performance. The result is a bounded reading of satisfaction in productive achievement. ABC also lists procreation as a historical manifestation, without establishing any claim about an individual's fertility.
