## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 213; one-based PDF p. 209, Jupiter + Neptune, Admetos row.

Pretending happiness and luck in small company. Not to get on in the world. Change in ownership.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 329; one-based PDF p. 163, Jupiter + Neptune, Admetos row.

Restriction through financial uncertainty, end of happiness on/in/with water, disappointing legal restrictions, success in deep water, successful removal of a blockade, consolidation of happiness through metaphysics, and beneficial accumulation of water (Wasser-Stau).

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Apparent good fortune is qualified by limited circumstances, financial uncertainty or stalled progress.
- psychology: Pretended contentment within a small circle in Witte.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: The social setting is a small company whose apparent happiness may conceal restriction.
- events/manifestations: Failure to advance and change of ownership in Witte; ABC also lists financial limitation and deep-water success.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's water, metaphysical and blockade motifs are distinct source-listed possibilities. Wasser-Stau names accumulated or backed-up water without a specified technical setting.

## Contradictions

Restriction and the separate successful-water or metaphysical possibilities are different contexts, not proof that the formula is uniformly adverse. The scan gives disappointing legal restrictions, removal of a blockade and beneficial Wasser-Stau; the practical water setting is unspecified.

Visually verified source rows: ABC printed p. 329, one-based PDF p. 163, Jupiter + Neptune, Admetos row; Witte printed p. 213, one-based PDF p. 209, Jupiter + Neptune, Admetos row.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Neptune = Admetos`, Admetos specifies uncertain or apparent good fortune as a condition in which movement is restricted. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes seeming happy in a small company while failing to advance, and also names change of ownership. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 329 (PDF p. 163), adds limitation through financial uncertainty. ABC separately lists success in deep water and a metaphysical consolidation of happiness; those uncommon motifs remain source-specific rather than explaining away the restriction.
