## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 213; one-based PDF p. 209, Jupiter + Neptune, Apollon row.

The good fortune which lies in the future. Easily earned money through science or commerce. Much money easily made.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 329; one-based PDF p. 163, Jupiter + Neptune, complete Apollon row after Kronos.

Extensive joy in water, successful water trade, success through water law, weak increase in money, uncertain legal knowledge, much joy in water, spreading uncertainty because of money, advantage through unknown knowledge, and increasing weakness in money matters.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Anticipated gains through science or commerce stand beside uncertain money growth and water-related success.
- psychology: Expectation of future benefit in Witte; ABC also names uncertain legal knowledge and advantage through unknown knowledge.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Commerce provides an economic setting; no intimate relationship pattern is established.
- events/manifestations: Witte names money made through science or trade; ABC adds water trade, water law, extensive enjoyment of water and increasing weakness in money matters.
- conflicts/notes: The complete ABC Apollon row begins with extensive joy in water after the separate Kronos row. Its water examples and unknown knowledge have no specified practical setting.

## Contradictions

Witte's favorable commerce statement and ABC's weak money growth or increasing financial weakness differ materially in emphasis. ABC also names successful water trade and legal advantage, so its uncertainty should remain beside its favorable examples. The preceding state/finance-office clauses belong to Kronos and are excluded from this orientation.

Visually verified source rows: ABC printed p. 329, one-based PDF p. 163, Jupiter + Neptune, Apollon row; Witte printed p. 213, one-based PDF p. 209, Jupiter + Neptune, Apollon row.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Neptune = Apollon`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) specifies anticipated good fortune in a scientific or commercial setting, describing money earned with ease and benefit lying ahead. The complete [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful water trade, success through water law and extensive joy in water, beside weak money growth and increasing weakness in money matters. Favorable prospects and financial uncertainty remain visible together; the water and unknown-knowledge examples keep their literal source scope.
