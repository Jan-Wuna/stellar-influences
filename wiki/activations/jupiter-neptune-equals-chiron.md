## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Neptune = Chiron`, Chiron specifies imaginative and ideal aspiration through its effect on other people. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) describes encouraging imaginative exploration and enthusiasm for ideals. It also speaks of harmonizing “subtle energies,” an esoteric explanation within that source. The supported practical reading is an encouraging, imaginative presence; the paragraph does not establish therapeutic efficacy.
