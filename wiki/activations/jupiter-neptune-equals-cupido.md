## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 213; one-based PDF p. 209, Jupiter + Neptune, cupido row.

Contented married life in poor circumstances. Feeling of happiness or imagined happiness of associates.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 329; one-based PDF p. 163, Jupiter + Neptune, cupido row and adjacent glyph boundaries.

A successful community in the air, foreign law for a community, joy of a community in water, shared uncertainty about money, shared water law, shared success through sensitivity, and legal uncertainty of a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Marital or communal happiness can coexist with poor circumstances, financial uncertainty or uncertain rights.
- psychology: A sense of happiness may be sincere or imagined.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Witte names marriage and associates; ABC explicitly concerns a community and shared success or uncertainty.
- events/manifestations: Contented married life despite limited resources; communal success, water law and shared financial or legal uncertainty.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's complete Cupido row is communal. The preceding Pluto development/metaphysics row is separate; the community's air, water and foreign-law settings are not explained further.

## Contradictions

Witte's material poverty and emotional contentment can coexist. ABC likewise keeps shared success beside money and legal uncertainty; neither entry guarantees that a community's sense of happiness reflects material security.

Visually verified source rows: ABC printed p. 329, one-based PDF p. 163, complete Cupido row between Pluto and Hades; Witte printed p. 213, one-based PDF p. 209, Cupido row.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Neptune = Cupido`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) specifies happiness in marriage or association despite poor material circumstances, while allowing that associates' happiness may be imagined. The complete [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds communal success through sensitivity alongside shared uncertainty about money and law. Cupido places the question of real or apparent contentment in shared life; ABC's air, water and foreign-law examples retain their literal, unspecified settings.
