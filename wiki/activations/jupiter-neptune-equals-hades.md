## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 213; one-based PDF p. 209, Jupiter + Neptune, hades row.

Rough awakening from blissful feeling. Unlucky person. Money acquired through theft or robbery. Losses through speculations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 329; one-based PDF p. 163, Jupiter + Neptune, hades row and adjacent glyph boundaries.

Sorrow through expectations of happiness, dangerous legal uncertainty, insufficient sensitivity to the law, disadvantages through water law, uncertainty through financial crime, advantages for the future through renunciation, and future success from the past.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Disillusionment and legal or financial uncertainty accompany expectations of happiness; ABC also allows future advantage through renunciation.
- psychology: Disillusionment after a blissful feeling, sorrow over expectations and insufficient sensitivity to the law.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: No definite intimate relationship pattern is established; ABC's actual row concerns law, financial crime and renunciation.
- events/manifestations: Speculative loss, legal uncertainty and water-law disadvantages; future advantage through renunciation or success from the past. Witte also lists money obtained through theft or robbery.
- conflicts/notes: The preceding community paragraph belongs to Cupido. Hades has its own complete row; crime wording remains attributed and cannot identify a person's conduct.

## Contradictions

Witte's disillusionment overlaps ABC's sorrow over expectations and financial or legal uncertainty. ABC additionally names future advantage through renunciation and future success from the past; those possibilities remain beside its adverse examples.

Visually verified source rows: ABC printed p. 329, one-based PDF p. 163, complete Hades row between Cupido and Zeus; Witte printed p. 213, one-based PDF p. 209, Hades row.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Neptune = Hades`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) specifies apparent good fortune as a rough awakening: a blissful feeling can give way to speculative loss. The complete [ABC entry](#abc-entry) likewise names sorrow over expectations and financial or legal uncertainty, but also future advantage through renunciation and future success from the past. Witte's theft or robbery and ABC's financial-crime examples remain attributed manifestations, not allegations about anyone.
