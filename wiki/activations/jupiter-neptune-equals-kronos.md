## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Great carelessness. Leading people pretend to be lucky or happy. Speculations promoted by government. Nobel prizes. Pretended luck by the government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Efforts for poor luck, starting money weakness, exchangeable money production, successful handling through fine tuning, successful water performance, water law design, money through fire water, and weak legal services

## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Neptune = Kronos`: idealism and speculation moving into elite or official space. It can show high recognition or prestigious dream-images, but also public authority built on exaggeration, carelessness, or false appearances.
