## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 213; one-based PDF p. 209, Jupiter + Neptune, kronos row.

Great carelessness. Leading people pretend to be lucky or happy. Speculations promoted by government. Nobel prizes. Pretended luck by the government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 329; one-based PDF p. 163, Jupiter + Neptune, kronos row and adjacent glyph boundaries.

Special joy in the air, special happiness through sensitivity, disappointment through the tax office, great expectations of happiness, great uncertainty about money, state water law, legal uncertainty of the state, and uncertainty about state funds.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Hopes and apparent good fortune take an official or state setting, alongside uncertainty about money and law.
- psychology: Carelessness and the presentation of happiness by leading people.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Leaders, public authority and government supply the social setting.
- events/manifestations: Government-promoted speculation, pretended official luck and Nobel prizes in Witte; tax-office disappointment, state water law and uncertain state funds in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's complete Kronos row follows the separate Zeus effort/production row. Its state water law and joy in the air remain literal examples without a specified technical setting.

## Contradictions

Witte's recognition example and its warnings about pretended luck are different possible manifestations. ABC also gives special happiness and great expectations beside tax-office disappointment and uncertain state finances or law; the favorable and uncertain examples remain side by side.

Visually verified source rows: ABC printed p. 329, one-based PDF p. 163, complete Kronos row between Zeus and Apollon; Witte printed p. 213, one-based PDF p. 209, Kronos row.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Neptune = Kronos`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) specifies apparent good fortune through leading people or government. It describes official promotion of speculation and displays of happiness, with carelessness as a possible weakness. Nobel prizes also appear as a concrete source-listed recognition motif; they should not be silently reduced to false prestige. The complete [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds special happiness and great expectations beside disappointment through the tax office, state water law and uncertainty about state funds. These official financial/legal examples broaden the range without turning every recognition into false prestige.
