### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Jupiter + Neptune`
- Source page: `209`
#### Witte Entry

Betrothal slowly cooling off. Beautiful outer appearance, but disappointing on the inside. Activities with negative results, ending with losses. Actions based on speculation.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Jupiter/Neptune`
- Source page: `232`
- Entry: `0811`

#### Ebertin Entry

Unreliable, prone to irresponsibility, tendency to rely on others (with the consequence of being let down), speculation. Desire to realize plans without prospect.
