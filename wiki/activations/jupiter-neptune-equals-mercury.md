## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Imaginative, inspired or sympathetic communication, with illusion and misdirection as alternatives.
- psychology: Rich visualization and idealism in Ebertin/Sandbach; raising hopes or putting on an act in Ebertin.
- body/health: No source entry names a bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Sympathetic understanding in Sandbach and speaking for the deprived in Munkasey contrast with misleading others.
- events/manifestations: Talking, legal judgments, uncertain finances, mysticism and discussion of the supernatural.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's air/water and youth clauses remain concrete source examples; they do not independently prove a psychic or technological mechanism.

## Contradictions

Imaginative and sympathetic communication in Sandbach and Ebertin coexists with Witte's illusions and Munkasey's deceptive practices. Inspiration and misleading performance are distinct possibilities, not interchangeable descriptions of every communication.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Neptune = Mercury`, imagination becomes communication: rich perception and inspiration can support sympathetic speech and the envisioning of possibilities. The [source entries](#source-entries) also qualify this fluency with illusions, excessive idealism, raised hopes, acting a part or deliberately misdirecting attention. Munkasey's speaking for the deprived gives a constructive social use, while ABC supplies legal, financial, air/water and youth examples. The central distinction is between communicating an imaginative possibility and presenting it as established reality.
