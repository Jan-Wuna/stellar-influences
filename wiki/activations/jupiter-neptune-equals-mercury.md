## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To talk much and coherently. Illusions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exchangeable legal talk, success through linguistic fine tuning, successful language in the air, successful traffic in the water, foreign money in the youth, joy of youth in the water, happiness's expectations in the youth, and talk about uncertain finances

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Active and intense imagination, rich powers of perception and visualization, the gift of inspiration. Raising of hopes, tendency to talk a lot and to put on an act.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A very imaginative way of communicating. One who delights in envisioning potentials. A sympathetic and understanding way of communicating. A mind that is ex cessively idealistic.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Analysis and discussion about the effect of the supernatural in reality; skills with deceptive practices; misleading or misdirecting the attention of others; self study in areas of magic or mysticism; speaking for the deprived.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Neptune = Mercury`: poetic or idealistic language, rich perception, and communication that can raise hopes or convey subtle insight. Main caution: illusion in words: talking too much, acting a part, or mistaking inspired impression for clear fact can blur meaning and credibility.
