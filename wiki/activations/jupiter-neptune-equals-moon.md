## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 213; one-based PDF page 209, Jupiter/Neptune section, Moon row.

Serene. Careless hours. Extravagant (wasteful) women. Speculating people. Many people pretend to be happy and fortunate.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 328; one-based PDF page 163, Jupiter/Neptun section, Moon row; full row checked against the German scan.

Success of a people in the air, joy for a woman in water, a happy woman in the air, uncertain women's rights, an uncertain sense of justice, a woman's uncertainty through money, water rights of a people, and future happiness of a people

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed page 232, coded activation entry 0808; retained entry-specific locator.

Little sense of reality, desire to dream, tendency to lose oneself in plans, becoming involved in speculation, instability, wastefulness.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 81, Moon entry; its complete five-sentence passage ends before Mercury.

Much richness of feeling. One who is very nurturing and sympathetic. Dreams and imaginings bring one security. A person who gets into moods of cosmic exaltation. One who can be easily taken advantage of when their emotions are brought into play.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `264`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: printed source page 264, Jupiter/Neptune with Planets and Points; retained source-page locator, not a new PDF-page claim.

Instincts which lead you to seek a mystical explanation for all that occurs in life; fundamental assumptions about how the supernatural pervades all areas of life; uncertainty about what is real and what is non-real in life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Rich feeling and dream-based security alongside speculative or uncertain happiness.
- psychology: Sandbach emphasizes nurturing sympathy and emotional vulnerability; Ebertin emphasizes dreaming, instability and loss of realism.
- body/health: No entry gives a named bodily condition.
- social/relationship: The historical Witte/ABC examples name women and a people; emotional susceptibility can expose a person to exploitation in Sandbach.
- events/manifestations: Speculation, wastefulness, imagined happiness, financial/legal uncertainty and mystical explanations.
- conflicts/notes: Dreams bringing security in Sandbach need not establish their factual truth; ABC's missing legal/financial and future-happiness clauses are restored.

## Contradictions

Sandbach's nurturing sympathy and dream-based security differ in emphasis from Ebertin's poor reality sense and Witte's pretended happiness. These remain alternative expressions, while Munkasey explicitly questions the boundary between real and non-real.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Neptune = Moon`, the [source entries](#source-entries) place emotional richness, sympathy and security found in dreams beside difficulty judging reality. Imagination may comfort and nurture, while exaggerated hopes, speculation or wastefulness can weaken practical judgment. Sandbach makes susceptibility to emotional exploitation explicit; Munkasey describes a tendency to seek mystical explanations for everything. ABC adds uncertainty involving rights and money as well as future happiness, preserving both difficult and favorable possibilities.
