## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Lucky feeling through connections. Connections with deceptive luck. Participants in speculation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Closure of a connection for money, exchange of legal connection, success by fine tuning in connections, successful connections in the air, joy by refining a connection, unclear money connections, and uncertain legal connection

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Tendency to place false hopes upon one's associations, association with naive persons, swindlers and speculators.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One may have unrealistic and/or impractical expectations about any individuals or groups one connects with. To associate with people who encourage one’s imagin ings. A need to find a grounded way to express one imagination. A possible tendency to fall back on wishful thinking.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `264`

#### Munkasey Entry

Affiliation among people who share a mystical rapport; meetings where the subject matter turns to aspects of non-physical reality; encounters which cause people to give up on a mystical or magic approach to explanations.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Neptune = Node`: inspirational or spiritual associations, groups that encourage vision, and relationships formed around possibility more than proof. Main caution: false hope in company: wishful social thinking, deceptive luck, or ungrounded expectations about allies can turn connection into confusion.
