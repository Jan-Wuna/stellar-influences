## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 213; one-based PDF page 209, Jupiter/Neptune section, Node row.

Lucky feeling through connections. Connections with deceptive luck. Participants in speculation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 328; one-based PDF page 163, Jupiter/Neptun section, Node row; full row checked against the German scan.

Dissolution of a connection because of money, a disappointing legal connection, success through sensitivity in connections, successful connections in the air, joy through refinement of a connection, unclear financial connections, and an uncertain legal connection

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed page 232, coded activation entry 0815; retained entry-specific locator.

Tendency to place false hopes upon one's associations, association with naive persons, swindlers and speculators.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 82, Node entry; chapter begins on page 81, which is not the entry page.

One may have unrealistic and/or impractical expectations about any individuals or groups one connects with. To associate with people who encourage one’s imagin ings. A need to find a grounded way to express one imagination. A possible tendency to fall back on wishful thinking.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `264`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: printed source page 264, Jupiter/Neptune with Planets and Points; retained source-page locator, not a new PDF-page claim.

Affiliation among people who share a mystical rapport; meetings where the subject matter turns to aspects of non-physical reality; encounters which cause people to give up on a mystical or magic approach to explanations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Associations carrying shared imagination, mystical rapport or uncertain hopes.
- psychology: Wishful expectations and the need to ground imagination in Sandbach; false hopes in Ebertin.
- body/health: No bodily condition is named.
- social/relationship: Connections with speculators, naive people or swindlers in Ebertin coexist with Munkasey's mystical affiliation.
- events/manifestations: Speculative partnerships, financially unclear ties, disappointing legal connections and meetings about non-physical reality.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey also allows encounters that lead people to abandon mystical explanations; spiritual associations are not uniformly affirmed.

## Contradictions

Shared imagination can encourage connection, but the same sources describe false expectations, deception and dissolution. Munkasey's encounters can either sustain mystical rapport or cause people to give up that explanatory approach.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Neptune = Node`, the [source entries](#source-entries) connect people through imagination, possibility or mystical rapport, while repeatedly warning of false hopes and unclear financial or legal ties. A group can encourage a person's imaginings; it can also encourage impractical expectations or expose them to speculation and deception. Sandbach calls for a grounded expression of imagination. Munkasey preserves another outcome: an encounter may lead someone to abandon a mystical explanation rather than strengthen it.
