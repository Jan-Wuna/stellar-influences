## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 213; one-based PDF page 209, Jupiter/Neptune section, Poseidon row.

Luck and happiness in highly mental matters. Happiness through right, reason, wisdom, or intelligence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 329; one-based PDF page 163, Jupiter/Neptun section, Poseidon row; checked against the complete German row.

Successful refinement of culture, joy over ideas of the future, joy over clear air and clear water, legal clarity through sensitivity, legal culture of the future, reasonable water law, truth about the weakness of money, and future intellectual or spiritual happiness

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental happiness through reason or wisdom in Witte; refined culture and legal clarity in ABC.
- psychology: Joy in future ideas, clear air/water and intellectual or spiritual matters.
- body/health: Clear air and water are source settings, not a clinical claim.
- social/relationship: Law, legal culture and culture of the future are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Cultural refinement, reasonable water law and understanding money's weakness.
- conflicts/notes: ABC says reasonable water law and future happiness, not impoverished law or poor happiness; geistig can cover intellectual or spiritual scope.

## Contradictions

Both entries are favorable about reason and understanding. ABC's weakness of money is a specific qualification, not a general denial of happiness or a claim of impoverished law.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Neptune = Poseidon`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes happiness through right, reason, wisdom or intelligence. [ABC](#abc-entry) develops culture, future ideas and legal clarity through sensitivity, with clear air and water as concrete examples of joy. Reasonable water law and truth about money's weakness give that clarity practical subjects. Its future geistig happiness allows an intellectual or spiritual scope, without requiring a broader philosophical doctrine.
