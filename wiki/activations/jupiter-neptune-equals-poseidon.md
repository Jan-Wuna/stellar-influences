## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Luck and happiness in highly mental matters. Happiness through right, reason, wisdom, or intelligence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful refinement of culture, joy over ideas of the future, joy over clear air and clear water, right clarity through fine feeling, right culture of the future, impoverished water right, truth about the weak of money, and poor spiritual happiness

## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Neptune = Poseidon`: idealism being clarified rather than merely fantasized. It favors spiritual or philosophical luck grounded in insight, truth-seeking, and lucid understanding.
