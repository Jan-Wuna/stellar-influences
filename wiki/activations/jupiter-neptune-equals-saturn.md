## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 213; one-based PDF page 209, Jupiter/Neptune section, Saturn row.

To worry about money matters. Separation caused by sickness. To awaken from carelessness. Losses through speculation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 329; one-based PDF page 163, Jupiter/Neptun section, Saturn row; checked against the complete German row.

Successful concentration on unknowns, happiness in old age through metaphysics, concentration on water law, creeping money problems, losses due to legal uncertainty, and satisfaction about dissolution and separation

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed page 232, coded activation entry 0812; retained printed coded-entry locator.

Pessimism, lack of confidence, feeling abandoned by luck. Experiencing the consequences issuing from false hopes, plans which come to nothing, disappointments, losses.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 82, Saturn entry; exact row checked, chapter begins on page 81.

To be excessively idealistic and then to be let down. Fear that one’s ideals will not be realized. The need to be patient, careful and circumspect if one is to attain one’s ideals.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `264`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: printed source page 264, Jupiter/Neptune with Planets and Points; retained source-page locator, not a newly verified PDF page.

Looking at both the mystical and real side of life; combining the real with the non-real in your activities; disappointment with a mystical explanations of reality; curtails occult practices which have little or no scientific basis.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Disappointment and limits on hopes, plans or finances, with careful concentration as a constructive response.
- psychology: Pessimism and lost confidence in Ebertin; patience and circumspection in Sandbach.
- body/health: Witte lists separation caused by sickness without naming a condition or giving diagnostic criteria.
- social/relationship: Separation and questioning mystical practices are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Speculative loss, failed plans, legal uncertainty and satisfaction in dissolution or separation.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's successful concentration and metaphysical happiness in old age coexist with Munkasey's disappointment in mystical explanations.

## Contradictions

ABC allows happiness through metaphysics and satisfaction over separation, while Munkasey includes curtailing occult practices without scientific basis. The pages preserve this difference rather than prescribing one attitude toward metaphysics.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Neptune = Saturn`, the [source entries](#source-entries) bring hopes and speculation into contact with disappointment, financial concern and loss. Witte adds an awakening from carelessness, while Sandbach gives patience, care and circumspection as ways of working toward ideals. ABC also permits successful concentration and satisfaction in separation, even happiness through metaphysics in old age. Munkasey's critical approach to mystical explanations remains alongside that possibility. The common theme is the testing and limiting of expectations, without inventing a universal lesson or inevitable despair.
