## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 213; one-based PDF page 209, Jupiter/Neptune section, Sun row.

Bodily well-being. Wastage of sustenance (the body). The man who makes himself believe he is lucky and happy. Speculators. Spendthrifts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 328; one-based PDF page 163, Jupiter/Neptun section, Sun row; full row checked against the German scan.

A man's success through sensitivity, joy during the day in the air, a man's happiness in the air, personal joy in water, personal uncertainty about money, legal uncertainty during the day, water money per person, and a weak financial center

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed page 232, coded activation entry 0807; retained printed coded-entry locator.

Lack of self-control, irresponsibility, speculation, squandering of physical strength. The act of deceiving or the misfortune of being deceived.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 81, Sun entry; exact entry visually checked, chapter begins on page 81.

Fantasies and imaginings give one a lot of energy and bring meaning to one’s life. Much envisioned about the future, but a great deal of it may be unrealistic and/or unattainable. A wealth of intuitive insights brought to light.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `264`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: printed source page 264, Jupiter/Neptune with Planets and Points; retained printed-page locator, not a new PDF-page claim.

Emphasizes the use of daydreams and visions to enhance personal capability; brings purpose and confidence to areas where confusion or deception exists; the prestige you lend to efforts which can helping others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Imagination giving meaning, energy or capability alongside self-deception and speculation.
- psychology: Intuitive insight in Sandbach, confidence/purpose in Munkasey and lack of self-control in Ebertin.
- body/health: Witte lists bodily wellbeing and wastage of bodily sustenance; Ebertin lists squandering physical strength without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Munkasey includes lending prestige to efforts helping others; Ebertin includes deceiving or being deceived.
- events/manifestations: Daydreams, speculation, financial uncertainty and imagined good fortune.
- conflicts/notes: The sources allow both useful vision and unrealistic plans; they do not define all imaginative energy as artistic.

## Contradictions

Witte's bodily wellbeing and Sandbach's energizing fantasies differ from Ebertin's squandered strength and irresponsibility. Munkasey supplies a constructive role for visions. These possibilities must remain conditional rather than describing a single inevitable decline.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Neptune = Sun`, the [source entries](#source-entries) describe imagination that can energize life, give meaning and support personal capability. Sandbach's intuitive insights and Munkasey's purposeful use of visions sit beside Witte's imagined good fortune and Ebertin's self-deception, speculation or wasted strength. ABC adds concrete financial uncertainty and air/water examples. The useful vision and the unrealistic expectation belong side by side; neither should erase the other.
