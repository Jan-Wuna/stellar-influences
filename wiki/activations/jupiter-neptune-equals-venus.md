#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 213; one-based PDF page 209, Jupiter/Neptune section, Venus row.

Rapturous. Romantic love or unreliable happiness in love. Lyric poet.

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed page 232, coded activation entry 0810; retained printed coded-entry locator.

Erotic imagination, a reveling and idolizing disposition. Love dreams, love poetry and writing. Optimistic attitude in love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 82, Venus entry; exact entry visually checked, chapter begins on page 81.

One takes great pleasure in dreaming and speculating. An appreciation of richly imaginative art. A relationship in which the two people stimulate each other’s imagina tion, but which may be impractical.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `264`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: printed source page 264, Jupiter/Neptune with Planets and Points; retained printed-page locator, not a new PDF-page claim.

Pleasant feelings obtained by escape from reality; delight or pleasure thru using substances or methods to allow an escape from mental or physical pain; satisfaction from attempts to understand the mystical side of life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Romantic or aesthetic rapture alongside unreliable or impractical happiness.
- psychology: Erotic imagination, idolization, love dreams and pleasure in imaginative art.
- body/health: Munkasey describes escape from mental or physical pain through substances or methods, without a diagnosis or recommended treatment.
- social/relationship: Partners stimulating one another's imagination, with possible impracticality in Sandbach.
- events/manifestations: Love poetry, imaginative art, romantic optimism and pleasure in mystical understanding.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's dissolved happiness and Wasser-Kunst remain literal source phrases; no specific breakup or technical meaning is inferred.

## Contradictions

The entries join romantic optimism and artistic pleasure with unreliable happiness, impractical relationships or escape from reality. These are meaningful tensions rather than proof that imaginative love is necessarily false.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Neptune = Venus`, the [source entries](#source-entries) join romantic rapture, love poetry and pleasure in imaginative art. Partners may enrich one another's imagination while still finding their relationship impractical. Witte's unreliable happiness and ABC's uncertain or dissolved happiness qualify Ebertin's optimism and idolization. Munkasey adds pleasure gained through escape from reality or through exploring mystical ideas. The shared theme is richly imagined pleasure, whose emotional appeal does not by itself establish practical reliability.
