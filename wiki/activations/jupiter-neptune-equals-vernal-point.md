## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public speculation, pretended good fortune and carelessness in Witte; public law and funds in ABC.
- psychology: General carelessness and expectations concerning law.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: The public is the explicit scope of ABC's legal and water examples.
- events/manifestations: Public water law, legal uncertainty and unclear public money.
- conflicts/notes: The sources do not explicitly call the public atmosphere dreamy or inflated; ABC also includes good water for the public.

## Contradictions

Witte's short adverse description differs from ABC's mixture of public benefit, expectations and uncertainty. Public water provision is a concrete example rather than proof of universal deception.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Neptune = Vernal Point`, [Witte](#witte-entry) places speculation, pretended luck and carelessness on a general level. [ABC](#abc-entry) specifies public matters: foreign law, good water for the public, water law, legal expectations and uncertainty over public money. The orientation therefore brings uncertain or apparent fortune into public financial and legal conditions, while retaining ABC's favorable water example.
