## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 213; one-based PDF page 209, Jupiter/Neptune section, Vulcanus row.

Great sums of money easily made and just as easily lost.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 329; one-based PDF page 163, Jupiter/Neptun section, Vulcanus row; complete row and adjacent factor glyphs checked.

Success through water energy, successful influence through metaphysics, foreign legal influence, foreign money power, money through water energy, powerful success expectations, enormous legal uncertainty, and beneficial influence of water

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Large sums easily gained and lost in Witte; influential money, expectations and uncertainty in ABC.
- psychology: Powerful expectations of success.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Foreign legal influence and financial power are ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: Water energy, metaphysical influence, substantial financial gains/losses and enormous legal uncertainty.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's water-energy and metaphysics examples are source terms, not explained mechanisms; dramatic opportunity is not separately stated.

## Contradictions

Witte gives an explicit ease-of-gain/ease-of-loss tension. ABC adds favorable influence and success alongside enormous legal uncertainty, without resolving them into one outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Neptune = Vulcanus`, [Witte](#witte-entry) makes financial scale prominent: large sums can be earned easily and lost just as easily. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds financial power, strong expectations of success and substantial legal uncertainty, with water energy and metaphysical influence among its stated contexts. The common emphasis is consequential financial or authoritative uncertainty, qualified by favorable influences rather than reduced to inevitable loss.
