## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 144, Nodes/Jupiter section, Admetos row; actual row and glyph checked.

The end of a happy union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 215; one-based PDF page 106, Mondknoten/Jupiter section, Admetos row.

Limitation of success in a connection, end of a good connection, success in a relationship by perseverance, consolidation of a relationship by money, firm legal connection, financial limitation in a connection, and happiness in a fixed connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: End or limitation of a fortunate union, with perseverance and consolidation in ABC.
- psychology: ABC allows happiness within a firm connection.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: A good connection can end; a firm legal relationship can also persist.
- events/manifestations: Financial limitation, money consolidating a relationship and success through perseverance.
- conflicts/notes: Cooling or reducing a bond's size is not stated; ABC's durable connection is a real alternative to Witte's ending.

## Contradictions

Witte gives the end of a happy union. ABC repeats an ending but also gives successful perseverance, consolidation and happiness in a fixed connection. These alternatives should not be collapsed into inevitable separation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Node = Admetos`, [Witte](#witte-entry) gives the end of a happy union. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds limits on success or money within a connection, while also allowing perseverance, consolidation and happiness in a firm bond. Money may restrict a relationship or help stabilize it. The orientation therefore concerns both the boundary and durability of a fortunate connection, rather than requiring that every bond cool or end.
