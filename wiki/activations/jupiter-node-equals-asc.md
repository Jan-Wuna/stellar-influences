## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 144, Nodes/Jupiter section, Asc row; actual row and glyph checked.

Good or successful connections with others. Contact with others involved with success, justice or money.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 214; one-based PDF page 106, Mondknoten/Jupiter section, Asc row.

successful connections with others, financial connections at the place, joyful encounter with others, lucky partner relationship, legal partner relationship, legal connection to a place, legal mediation at the place, and mediation of money for the environment

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed page 238, Jupiter/Dragon's Head, coded activation entry 0840; retained printed coded-entry locator.

Ability to come into quick personal contact with other people through a pleasant and friendly disposition, a harmonious relationship to the environment.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 85, Jupiter/Node Ascendant entry; chapter begins on page 84, which is not the entry page.

A person who is capable of connecting with people in many different ways. One who relates to others through encouraging progress.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `272`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: printed source page 272, Jupiter/Node with Planets and Points; retained printed-page locator, not a new PDF-page claim.

Focusing on the reality of what other people offer you, or can do for you; insight into another’s psychological needs or purposes; increased ability to find and locate people; responses to those who have separated from you.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful personal contacts and a harmonious relationship to the surroundings.
- psychology: A pleasant, friendly disposition in Ebertin; insight into others' needs or purposes in Munkasey.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Connecting in varied ways, encouraging progress and mutually relevant offers or help.
- events/manifestations: Local financial/legal links, finding people and responding to those who have separated.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's attention to what people actually offer provides a practical qualification; social overreach is not stated by these entries.

## Contradictions

The entries are largely favorable, with Munkasey adding practical assessment and responses to separated people. No source here supplies the former warning about excessive friendliness, so no such contradiction is manufactured.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Node = Asc`, the [source entries](#source-entries) place successful connection in direct dealings with other people. A pleasant disposition supports quick rapport, while encouraging progress and recognizing others' needs broaden the ways a connection can develop. Witte and ABC add financial, legal and local contexts. Munkasey keeps the assessment practical by focusing on what others actually offer, finding people and responding to separation. The synthesis concerns effective contact without an invented warning about social overreach.
