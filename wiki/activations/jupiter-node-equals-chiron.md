### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page `84`, Jupiter/Node Chiron entry; complete paragraph checked in the raw source.

Your energy helps other people to enter more wholeheartedly, richly and fully into connections. You also can give them a lot of hope for the possibility of growth and confidence in taking in new experiences.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Helping others participate more fully in connections.
- psychology: Hope for growth and confidence in new experiences.
- body/health: Not represented in this entry.
- social/relationship: Encouraging wholehearted involvement with others.
- events/manifestations: Entering new experiences with greater confidence.
- conflicts/notes: One entry supports encouragement; it does not establish a medical healing claim.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Node = Chiron`, Chiron specifies the pair's growth through connection as encouragement offered to other people: helping them enter relationships more wholeheartedly and see new experiences as possible. The emphasis is on giving others hope and confidence to participate, rather than on a stated bodily or medical effect. [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry)
