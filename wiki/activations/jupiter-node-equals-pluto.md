## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 144, Nodes/Jupiter section, Pluto row. The P beside the Pluto glyph is an alternate factor identifier, excluded from the prose.

Getting acquainted for a fortunate connection. Change of a successful connection.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 215; one-based PDF page 106, Mondknoten/Jupiter section, Pluto row; full German row checked.

Successful unfolding of a connection, successful change of connection, joy through development of a relationship, changes through a happy encounter, change of a legal connection, and a growing financial connection

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed page 238, Jupiter/Dragon's Head, coded entry 0839; retained printed coded-entry locator.

Desire to make pleasant and happy contacts with many people.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF pages 84–85, Pluto entry crossing the page boundary; exact full paragraph checked.

An extreme need to expand one’s connections with others, sometimes so much so that the other person becomes intimidated and/or the connection weakened. The desire to connect with a lot of people and consequently the possibility of spreading oneself too thinly. As one makes more and more connections a transformation occurs.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `272`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: printed source page 272, Jupiter/Node with Planets and Points; retained printed locator, not a newly verified PDF page.

Increases the chances that the contacts you make have shady or criminal intentions, and will hold you responsible for whatever help they give you; offers you the possibility of using any contacts to gain enormous benefits.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Expansion or change of fortunate connections, with potentially costly intensity.
- psychology: A desire for many happy contacts in Ebertin; extreme expansion and thinly spread attention in Sandbach.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: New acquaintances, changing ties, intimidation or weakened connections, and Munkasey's conditional risky contacts.
- events/manifestations: Successful legal/financial changes and large benefits, with possible obligations or dubious help in Munkasey.
- conflicts/notes: The prefixed P is the source's alternate Pluto identifier, not prose; the source's criminal-intentions warning is conditional, not a judgment on every contact.

## Contradictions

Witte, ABC and Ebertin emphasize favorable contacts, while Sandbach warns of intimidation or weakened ties and Munkasey of shady or criminal intentions. These are material differences in risk, not grounds to assume every expanding circle is beneficial or criminal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Node = Pluto`, the [source entries](#source-entries) give connections a strong impulse toward expansion and change. New acquaintances, happy encounters and developing financial or legal ties can bring substantial benefit. Sandbach qualifies that drive: too much expansion may intimidate another person, weaken a tie or spread attention too thinly. Munkasey adds a separate warning about contacts whose help carries dubious intentions or obligations. The synthesis therefore holds social opportunity beside the consequences of how a connection grows.
