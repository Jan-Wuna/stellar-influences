## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 144, Nodes/Jupiter section, Saturn row.

Dissolution of a betrothal or marriage or good connection. Disturbed financial relationship.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 215; one-based PDF page 106, Mondknoten/Jupiter section, Saturn row; full German row checked.

Tasks of legal mediation, a successfully maturing connection, financial losses in a connection, a disturbed relationship to money, disturbed happiness in a connection, a happy connection in old age, and legal problems of a connection

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed page 238, Jupiter/Dragon's Head, coded entry 0836; retained printed coded-entry locator.

Desire for solitude or seclusion, urge to be alone. Inhibitions during the initial stage of associations or new contacts, teamwork which is rendered more difficult as time goes on, a retarded venture, difficulties with engagement or marriage, the termination of an association.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 84, Saturn entry; exact full paragraph checked.

To be afraid of connecting with people. The slow and gradual expansion of one’s connectedness. To be frustrated because you feel like you give more than the other person, or like there is too much going on between you and them, and so you feel overwhelmed.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `272`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: printed source page 272, Jupiter/Node with Planets and Points; retained printed locator, not a newly verified PDF page.

Emphasizes the business aspects of life; contacts with helpful friends or groups; some disappointments thru contacts or associates; delays in receiving help from friends or associates; a lessening of control over others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficulty, delay or ending in associations, with maturation and later happiness as alternatives.
- psychology: Fear of contact, desire for solitude, frustration or overwhelm in Sandbach/Ebertin.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Difficult teamwork, delayed help, business contacts and engagement/marriage problems.
- events/manifestations: Financial loss, legal mediation, slow expansion, termination or successful maturation.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's disturbed relationship is not merely distant; its maturing/later-life connection qualifies a purely ending-oriented reading.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin give dissolution or difficult association, while ABC allows successful maturation and happiness in old age. Munkasey also retains helpful friends despite delays or disappointment. These alternatives are not erased by the sources' difficult emphasis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Node = Saturn`, the [source entries](#source-entries) describe connections that may be inhibited, delayed, financially troubled or ended. Sandbach adds fear of reaching out and the strain of unequal giving or too much relational demand. Yet expansion can be slow rather than absent: ABC permits a successfully maturing connection and happiness in old age, while Munkasey keeps helpful business contacts beside delayed assistance. The orientation concerns limits and the pace of connection, without making withdrawal the proven cause of every ending.
