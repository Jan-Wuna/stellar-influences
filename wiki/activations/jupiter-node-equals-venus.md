## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 144, Nodes/Jupiter section, Venus row; full row checked.

Fortunate (happy) love union. Pleasant and harmonious connections. Preference for financial relations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 214; one-based PDF page 106, Mondknoten/Jupiter section, Venus row; full German row and neighbors checked.

A successful connection to art, successful conveying of wishes, joy over harmonious connections, happiness and harmony in a connection, a happy love connection, legal peace within a connection, and preference for financial relationships

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed page 238, Jupiter/Dragon's Head, coded entry 0834; retained printed coded-entry locator.

Friendly and pleasant conduct in the presence of others, making oneself popular. Affectionate care of another person, a love union.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 84, Jupiter/Node Venus entry; exact paragraph checked.

A love of art that feels like it has many interconnections within it. The desire in relationships to feel a fullness and richness of connection. In one’s relationships one al ways wants greater closeness and fullness.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `272`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: printed source page 272, Jupiter/Node with Planets and Points; retained printed-page locator, not a new PDF-page claim.

Added charm and grace with manners or movements; others see you as a more gentle and refined person in social capacities; increased contacts among those who have authority in the artistic, financial, or musical fields.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affectionate, pleasant and harmonious connections, with art and financial ties.
- psychology: Desire for fuller and closer connection in Sandbach; charm and grace in Munkasey.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated; manners or movements describe social presentation.
- social/relationship: Love union, affectionate care and contacts in artistic, musical or financial fields.
- events/manifestations: Artistic links, conveying wishes, legal peace and popularity.
- conflicts/notes: ABC says art, not an artist, and conveying wishes, not veneration; no entry supplies a pleasant-versus-supportive deception warning.

## Contradictions

The entries are favorable and complementary. Sandbach's wish for greater closeness is a desire rather than evidence that harmonious ties are superficial or inadequate.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Node = Venus`, the [source entries](#source-entries) describe affectionate care, love union and pleasant, harmonious contact. Sandbach emphasizes richness and greater closeness in relationships, alongside art with many interconnections. ABC adds conveying wishes and legal peace, while Witte and Munkasey retain financial relationships and social access to artistic, musical or financial circles. The focus is connection experienced through affection, aesthetic appreciation and social ease, without an invented warning that pleasant ties conceal a lack of support.
