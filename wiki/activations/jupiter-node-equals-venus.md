## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Fortunate (happy) love union. Pleasant and harmonious connections. Preference for financial relations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful connection to the artist, successful veneration of wunschen, joy over harmonious connections, G_IUck and harmony in one connection, right peace in one connection, and preference for financial relations

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Friendly and pleasant conduct in the presence of others, making oneself popular. Affectionate care of another person, a love union.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A love of art that feels like it has many interconnections within it. The desire in relationships to feel a fullness and richness of connection. In one’s relationships one al ways wants greater closeness and fullness.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Added charm and grace with manners or movements; others see you as a more gentle and refined person in social capacities; increased contacts among those who have authority in the artistic, financial, or musical fields.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Node = Venus`: warm social charm, harmonious alliances, and the desire for relationships that feel full, rich, and mutually pleasing. Main caution: overvaluing pleasant connection for its own sake, which can blur the difference between genuinely supportive ties and merely agreeable ones.
