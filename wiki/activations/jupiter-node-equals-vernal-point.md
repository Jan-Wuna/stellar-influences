## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 144, Nodes/Jupiter section, Vernal Point row; full entry and factor glyph checked.

Good relationships in the public life. Relations with public funds. Relations with public justice.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 214; one-based PDF page 106, Mondknoten/Jupiter section, Vernal Point row; full German entry and neighbors checked.

relationships to public funds, successful connection to the public, joyful encounter in public, mediation of money in public life, luck through public connections, public legal mediation, and connection to public justice

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Beneficial connections in public life, especially public finance and justice.
- psychology: Joy in public encounters in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: The public, public funds and public justice are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Public financial mediation, successful public contact and public legal mediation.
- conflicts/notes: The sources supply public scope without a separate claim of social recognition or goodwill.

## Contradictions

The entries are favorable and closely aligned. ABC expands Witte's brief public-finance/justice statement through encounters and mediation, without an explicit disagreement.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Node = Vernal Point`, [Witte](#witte-entry) places good relationships in public life, public funds and public justice. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful public contacts, joyful encounters and financial or legal mediation. The shared emphasis is beneficial connection within public financial and legal settings. Visibility or social recognition is not needed as an additional condition.
