## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 144, Nodes/Jupiter section, Vulcanus row; full entry and factor glyph checked.

Influence and might through fortunate connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 215; one-based PDF page 106, Mondknoten/Jupiter section, Vulcanus row; full German entry and neighbors checked.

An influential connection to art, the successful strength of a connection, power through financial intermediation, powerful good fortune in a relationship, an influential financial connection, strong attachment to money, and the strength of a legal connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Influence and power arising through fortunate connections.
- psychology: Strong attachment to money is explicitly named in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Influential artistic, financial and legal connections.
- events/manifestations: Power through financial intermediation and a relationship's strength or good fortune.
- conflicts/notes: The source names art, not artificiality, and financial intermediation, not a money entity; strength does not by itself specify how it is used.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize strong or influential connections. ABC's attachment to money adds a particular motivation without establishing that every powerful relationship has that motive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Node = Vulcanus`, [Witte](#witte-entry) makes fortunate connections a source of influence and might. [ABC](#abc-entry) gives that influence artistic, financial and legal settings, including power through financial intermediation and strength within a relationship. A strong attachment to money is another stated possibility. The orientation concerns the force and leverage of connections without assigning every use of that strength the same purpose.
