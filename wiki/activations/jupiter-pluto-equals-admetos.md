## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 214; one-based PDF page 210, Jupiter/Pluto section, Admetos row; full row visually checked.

To remain calm in spite of lucky changes, to ignore them, to deny them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 331; one-based PDF page 164, Jupiter/Pluto section, Admetos row; complete German row checked.

Restriction in financial development, the end of a happy development, successful growth through thoroughness, successful rotational development, thorough legal development, growing financial restriction, and thorough change through money

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Calm, ignoring or denying fortunate changes in Witte; restriction and thorough development in ABC.
- psychology: Witte distinguishes calm from ignoring or denying favorable changes.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: ABC includes legal development; no specific relationship role is named.
- events/manifestations: Financial restriction, an ending of happy development and growth through thoroughness.
- conflicts/notes: Rotations-Entwicklung remains literal rotational development because the source gives no explanation; calm does not establish mastery under pressure.

## Contradictions

Witte's fortunate changes may be ignored or denied, while ABC includes an actual end or restriction of favorable development as well as successful thorough growth. These are different circumstances, not one inevitable account of resistance.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Pluto = Admetos`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes remaining calm despite fortunate change, or ignoring or denying it. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds financial restriction and an ending of happy development, but also growth and legal development through thoroughness. The common focus is favorable development meeting restraint or a limited response. Its unexplained rotational-development phrase remains a source example, without supplying a general mechanism of transformation.
