## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 214; one-based PDF page 210, Jupiter/Pluto section, Apollon row; full row visually checked.

Agreeable change for many. Fortunate development in trade and science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 331; one-based PDF page 164, Jupiter/Pluto section, Apollon row; complete German row checked.

Successful financial development, successful legal development, changes in legal knowledge, increased money transactions, beneficial change through knowledge, growing trade development, developing increasing joy, and increasing money trade

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Agreeable change for many, especially trade, science, finance and law.
- psychology: ABC includes developing increasing joy.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Many people and commercial/scientific settings in Witte; legal knowledge and trade in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Increasing transactions, financial/legal development and favorable change through knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: The sources state breadth and development without a separate doctrine of broad networks or reform.

## Contradictions

The entries are favorable and complementary. Their trade, science, knowledge and legal contexts remain distinct examples rather than a single necessary institutional structure.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Pluto = Apollon`, [Witte](#witte-entry) extends agreeable change to many through trade and science. [ABC](#abc-entry) gives the developmental theme concrete financial and legal forms: growing transactions, changing legal knowledge and favorable change through knowledge. Increasing joy and trade are also stated. The orientation concerns broad favorable development with commercial and intellectual settings, without requiring a separate network or reform explanation.
