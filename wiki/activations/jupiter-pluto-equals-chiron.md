## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 83, Jupiter/Pluto Chiron entry; exact paragraph checked, chapter begins on page 82. The source's printed 'intensity' is retained.

Your vibration stimulates other people to be more enthusiastic and to take what they are doing far enough that they experience a breakthrough or transformation. You can also heal them of obsessive excesses by inducing them to see what it is that they really want. You can also intensity and transform their understanding of life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Encouraging others toward breakthrough or transformed understanding.
- psychology: Enthusiasm and recognizing what one really wants, rather than obsessive excess.
- body/health: Sandbach uses healing language for obsessive excesses, without a medical condition, diagnostic standard or treatment procedure.
- social/relationship: Helping others sustain effort and clarify desire.
- events/manifestations: Breakthrough, transformation and a changed understanding of life.
- conflicts/notes: Single-source entry; crisis and a therapeutic practice are not explicitly stated.

## Contradictions

The entry promotes taking effort far enough for a breakthrough while also reducing obsessive excess. The source connects these through recognizing what is really wanted, without prescribing a clinical method.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Pluto = Chiron`, the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) places the emphasis on helping others direct their enthusiasm. Effort can be sustained far enough for a breakthrough, while recognizing a person's actual desires can reduce obsessive excess. Transformation also concerns understanding life. The source's healing vocabulary belongs to that interpretive account; it does not establish a medical treatment or require a crisis before growth.
