## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 214; one-based PDF page 210, Jupiter/Pluto Cupido row.

Family happily reunited continuing its former life. Fortunate development in an art, in the family, or among associates.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 331; one-based PDF page 164, Jupiter/Pluto Cupido row; all six clauses checked before Hades begins.

Developments involving shared money, developing community law, happy development of a community, changes through shared happiness, changes in a legal community, and transformation of community law

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Fortunate development in family, art or associations; community money and law in ABC.
- psychology: Shared happiness in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: A reunited family continuing its former life; successful development of a community.
- events/manifestations: Changes involving shared funds, legal community and community law.
- conflicts/notes: Reunion is one Witte example; broad rebuilding or regeneration is not an additional source claim.

## Contradictions

Both entries are favorable, with Witte emphasizing reunion and family/art while ABC supplies community-finance and legal changes. These settings do not require a prior rupture in every case.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Pluto = Cupido`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a family happily reunited and continuing its former life, together with fortunate development in art, family or associations. [ABC](#abc-entry) extends community development into shared money and changes in community law. Shared happiness is another explicit link between them. The orientation concerns favorable development in collective life, with reunion as one example rather than a necessary starting point.
