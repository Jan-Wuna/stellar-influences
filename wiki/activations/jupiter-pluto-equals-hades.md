## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 214; one-based PDF page 210, Jupiter/Pluto Hades row.

A fortunate development is denied through want.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 331; one-based PDF page 164, Jupiter/Pluto Hades row; all seven clauses checked between Cupido and Zeus.

Successful changes from the past, dangerous legal development, happiness within a dangerous development, sorrow within a happy development, inadequate legal development, a criminal financial transaction, and advantage through renouncing growth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Fortunate development blocked by want in Witte; mixed happiness, danger and legal/financial development in ABC.
- psychology: Happiness in danger and sorrow within a happy development are explicit ABC alternatives.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Legal development and criminal financial transaction are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Want preventing development, successful changes from the past and benefit through renouncing growth.
- conflicts/notes: The row allows benefit from foregoing growth; it does not prescribe persistence under pressure.

## Contradictions

ABC explicitly puts happiness in a dangerous development and sorrow in a happy one, while also allowing advantage through renouncing growth. Those reversals complicate a simple growth-good/blockage-bad account.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Pluto = Hades`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a fortunate development prevented by want. [ABC](#abc-entry) makes the picture more mixed: past changes can be successful, danger can contain happiness, and a happy development can contain sorrow. Legal difficulty and a criminal financial transaction are separate adverse examples. Advantage through renouncing growth qualifies the drive to expand. The formula therefore concerns the conditions and costs of development, without an unsupported prescription to persist at all costs.
