## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 214; one-based PDF page 210, Jupiter/Pluto Moon row.

Happy passing hours. Happy change for people, for women or several persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 330; one-based PDF page 164, Jupiter/Pluto Moon row; complete German wording checked.

Successful development of a people, developing a feeling of joy, developing women's rights, a woman's financial transactions, a people's right to development, change in the sense of justice, change in a woman through money, and growing happiness of a people

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed page 235, Jupiter/Pluto coded entry 0820; retained printed coded-entry locator.

Richness of feeling, desire to bring about social aspirations and improvements, ability to influence the masses or the public through an appeal to the feelings. A good psychologist.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 83, Jupiter/Pluto Moon entry; exact full paragraph checked, chapter begins on page 82.

The emotional desire for some form of grandiosity. One who has extremely strong emotions and who desires to see change happen in the world. To be impres sionable to big ideas.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `268`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: printed source page 268, Jupiter/Pluto with Planets and Points; retained printed locator, not a newly verified PDF page.

Your quest to reach the top of your society may take many turns; an inconsistency in pursuing power goals; a powerful female influence in your life; insecurity about self or family; a powerful woman with increased influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Happy personal/collective changes and strong feeling directed toward social change.
- psychology: Rich feeling and public emotional appeal in Ebertin; grandiosity or impressionability to big ideas in Sandbach.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated; Ebertin's psychologist example does not supply a diagnostic claim.
- social/relationship: Women, a people, social aspirations and powerful female influence are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Women's rights, financial transactions, public improvement, changing power goals and insecurity about self/family.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's sense of justice is not merely law itself; Munkasey's insecurity and inconsistent power aims qualify unbroken emotional influence.

## Contradictions

Witte/ABC emphasize happy change and public development, while Sandbach adds emotional grandiosity and Munkasey allows insecurity or inconsistency in power goals. These remain source-specific qualifications rather than a single confident public role.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Pluto = Moon`, the [source entries](#source-entries) give change a strong emotional and collective dimension. Happy changes for people or women sit beside social aspirations, women's rights and a changing sense of justice. Ebertin includes influencing the public through feeling; Sandbach adds intense emotion and responsiveness to big ideas. Munkasey contributes powerful female influence, insecurity about self or family and an uneven pursuit of social power. The synthesis therefore includes both emotional influence and the uncertainty surrounding its direction.
