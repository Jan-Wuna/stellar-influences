## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Happy passing hours. Happy change for people, for women or several persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

developing joy feeling, developing women's rights, money transactions of a woman, right of a people to development, change in law, change of women through money, and growing happiness of a people

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Richness of feeling, desire to bring about social aspirations and improvements, ability to influence the masses or the public through an appeal to the feelings. A good psychologist.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The emotional desire for some form of grandiosity. One who has extremely strong emotions and who desires to see change happen in the world. To be impres sionable to big ideas.

- Source heading: `JUPITER/PLUTO with Planets and Points`

Your quest to reach the top of your society may take many turns; an inconsistency in pursuing power goals; a powerful female influence in your life; insecurity about self or family; a powerful woman with increased influence.
