## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 214; one-based PDF page 210, Jupiter/Pluto Poseidon row.

Successful development in the field of education. Happy spiritual development.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 331; one-based PDF page 164, Jupiter/Pluto Poseidon row; full eight-clause German row checked.

Successful cultural development, joy over ethical developments, joyful unfolding of truth, happiness through spiritual growth, clear legal development, reasonable financial development, change in legal culture, and growing legal clarity

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful education and spiritual development in Witte; culture, truth, ethics and legal clarity in ABC.
- psychology: Joy in ethical development and unfolding truth.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Education, cultural development and legal culture are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Reasonable financial development, growing legal clarity and happiness through geistig growth.
- conflicts/notes: ABC says reasonable, not simply smooth, financial development; wisdom as the transformation of power is not a separate stated mechanism.

## Contradictions

The entries are favorable and complementary. Their spiritual, educational, financial and legal forms remain separate settings rather than a claim that growth in one necessarily produces all the others.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Pluto = Poseidon`, [Witte](#witte-entry) locates successful development in education and spiritual life. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds culture, ethical development, truth and growing legal clarity, together with reasonable financial development. Its geistig growth allows an intellectual or spiritual emphasis. The shared focus is favorable development through understanding and culture, without requiring an additional story in which power must first be transformed into wisdom.
