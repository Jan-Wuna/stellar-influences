## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 214; one-based PDF page 210, Jupiter/Pluto Uranus row.

A surprisingly fortunate change, transformation or development.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 331; one-based PDF page 164, Jupiter/Pluto Uranus row; complete German row checked.

Successful technical development, joy over sudden changes, impulses of joy unfold, sudden development for the good, surprising legal development, unrest through money transactions, and changes through financial reform

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed page 235; one-based PDF page 237, Jupiter/Pluto coded entry 0825; actual factor glyph and paragraph visually checked.

Fanatical striving for improvement, quick exploitation of every situation. Sudden reform, quick development, adjustment to new circumstances.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 83, Jupiter/Pluto Uranus entry; exact full paragraph checked.

Having inspirations about how one can use a situation for personal expansion, or as a learning experience, or as a way of furthering evolution. A genius for knowing how to make something grow or evolve. The possibility that one’s experiments will be wildly successful.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `268`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: printed source page 268, Jupiter/Pluto with Planets and Points; retained printed locator, not a newly verified PDF page.

Sudden and unexpected interference to plans for advancing in society; the exceptional and unusual situation blocks your way and upsets you; you rebel against society and seek to start your own clique of influential friends.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden favorable change and rapid development, with possible interference or blockage.
- psychology: Inspired experimentation in Sandbach; fanatical improvement in Ebertin; upset or rebellion in Munkasey.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Technological connections and a possible clique of influential friends.
- events/manifestations: Unexpected reform, successful experiments, financial unrest and disrupted advancement plans.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's sudden obstruction differs from the other sources' favorable surprise; its outcome is not reduced to acceleration alone.

## Contradictions

Witte and Sandbach give favorable surprise or successful experiments, while Munkasey gives unexpected interference and blocked advancement. ABC also includes unrest from transactions. These are material differences in outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Pluto = Uranus`, the [source entries](#source-entries) make development rapid or surprising. Witte and ABC allow fortunate change, technological progress and reform; Sandbach describes inspiration and experiments with considerable potential. Ebertin adds an intense drive to exploit changing circumstances. Munkasey supplies the opposing possibility of interference or blockage, followed by rebellion or a new influential circle. Sudden change therefore can open or obstruct advancement, with financial unrest also retained.
