## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Beginning of a game of love. Beginning of a pleasant change.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

development of joy and harmony, development of love happiness, successful artistic development, joy through artificial development, preference for money transactions, preference for change of law, and growing love happiness

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Creative power. Craving for indulgence. Giving one's love to many.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To be tremendously enthusiastic when in love and when relating to others. To desire relationships that are expansive and which grow and can be enriched. The tendency to be very free or even wasteful with money. The desire for a great amount of money. One is attracted to things that are big, overpowering, and grandiose.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds to your social charm, and the attention you are willing to give to other people you want to impress with your manners and sophistication; pleasure from music and the arts; enjoyment of artistic notoriety at some level.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Pluto = Venus`: strong attractiveness, creative potency, and love or money matters tied to major change or growth. Main caution: indulgence and excess: craving can become wasteful, possessive, or too enamored of what is grand and overpowering.
