## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 214; one-based PDF page 210, Jupiter/Pluto Venus row.

Beginning of a game of love. Beginning of a pleasant change.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 330; one-based PDF page 164, Jupiter/Pluto Venus row; complete German row checked.

Unfolding joy and harmony, development of happiness in love, successful artistic development, joy through artistic unfolding, preference for financial transactions, preference for changes in law, and growing happiness in love

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed page 235; one-based PDF page 237, Jupiter/Pluto coded Venus entry0822; paragraph and glyph checked.

Creative power. Craving for indulgence. Giving one's love to many.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF page 83, Jupiter/Pluto Venus entry; exact full paragraph checked.

To be tremendously enthusiastic when in love and when relating to others. To desire relationships that are expansive and which grow and can be enriched. The tendency to be very free or even wasteful with money. The desire for a great amount of money. One is attracted to things that are big, overpowering, and grandiose.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `268`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: printed source page 268, Jupiter/Pluto with Planets and Points; retained printed locator, not a new PDF-page claim.

Adds to your social charm, and the attention you are willing to give to other people you want to impress with your manners and sophistication; pleasure from music and the arts; enjoyment of artistic notoriety at some level.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Pleasant change in love and art, with strong desire or indulgence.
- psychology: Enthusiasm in love, attraction to grand things and desire for money in Sandbach.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Expansive relationships, giving love to many and social attention.
- events/manifestations: Creative power, artistic recognition, financial transactions and possible wastefulness.
- conflicts/notes: Artistic unfolding is not artificial development; possessiveness was an added claim rather than an explicit source example.

## Contradictions

The entries favor pleasure, love and creative development while Ebertin/Sandbach add indulgence or wastefulness. Munkasey's artistic notoriety is a concrete social outcome, not proof that every expansive relationship becomes possessive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Pluto = Venus`, the [source entries](#source-entries) describe love, art and pleasure becoming expansive. Witte gives the beginning of a love game or pleasant change; ABC adds growing harmony and artistic development. Ebertin and Sandbach qualify the attraction with indulgence, abundant desire and possible financial waste. Munkasey adds charm, attention and enjoyment of artistic recognition. The common theme is enriched pleasure and relationship, with the scale of desire and spending left visible.
