#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 330; one-based PDF page 164, Jupiter/Pluto Vernal Point row; complete German row checked.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Fortunate general change and public financial/legal development.
- psychology: Joy over public development in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: The public and public funds/law are the explicit scope.
- events/manifestations: Financial transactions, changed public law and growing public funds.
- conflicts/notes: The former single-letter ABC text was an extraction failure; all six clauses are substantive.

## Contradictions

The entries are favorable and complementary. ABC specifies public finance and law within Witte's general changes, without requiring a particular reform program.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Pluto = Vernal Point`, [Witte](#witte-entry) describes fortunate transformations and changes in general. [ABC](#abc-entry) specifies that scope through public development, financial transactions, public law and growing public funds. Joy and advantageous change remain explicit. The shared emphasis is favorable development affecting public conditions, with concrete financial and legal forms rather than an unspecified renewal narrative.
