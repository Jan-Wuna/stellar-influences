## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed page 214; one-based PDF page 210, Jupiter/Pluto Vulcanus row.

Fortunate changes and transformations starting with great force.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed page 331; one-based PDF page 164, Jupiter/Pluto Vulcanus row; complete German row checked.

Influential financial transactions, successful development of energy, successful unfolding of power, powerful financial development, transformation of power through money, changes through legal force, an advantageous change of power, and growing financial influence

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Fortunate change starting with great force and financial or legal power.
- psychology: The sources do not isolate a psychological trait.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Financial influence, changes of power and legal force.
- events/manifestations: Influential transactions, developing energy and power, and advantageous power changes.
- conflicts/notes: The entry states forceful beginnings, not a necessary breakthrough or a universal preference for abrupt reform.

## Contradictions

The entries are favorable about strong change. Their financial influence and legal-force examples do not specify how force is exercised or guarantee that every change of power is advantageous.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Jupiter/Pluto = Vulcanus`, [Witte](#witte-entry) gives fortunate changes that begin with great force. [ABC](#abc-entry) identifies financial transactions, developing energy and power, legal force and growing financial influence as settings for that change. An advantageous transfer of power is one explicit possibility. The common emphasis is forceful development with financial or authoritative consequences, without adding a specific reform mechanism.
