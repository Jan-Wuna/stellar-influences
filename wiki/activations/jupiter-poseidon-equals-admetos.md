## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 222; one-based PDF p. 218, Jupiter + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Education, profound insight and wisdom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 347; one-based PDF p. 172, Jupiter/Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Success through thorough ideas, successful consolidation of truth, consolidated legal culture, thorough legal clarity, thorough intellectual/spiritual success, reasonable restriction of energy, deepening in law and truth, and truth about tied-up funds.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Education, profound insight and wisdom; thorough ideas consolidate truth and legal understanding.
- psychology: Depth of understanding and reasonable restraint of energy are explicit motifs.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Consolidated legal culture places thorough understanding in a shared legal setting.
- events/manifestations: Successful learning and clarification of truth, law and tied-up funds.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's reasonable restriction of energy and truth about tied-up funds do not support poor energy or uncertainty; neither entry specifies a slow timetable.

## Contradictions

The entries are compatible: Witte emphasizes education and wisdom, while ABC specifies thorough ideas, consolidated truth and legal culture. The financial clause concerns truth about tied-up funds; it does not state prosperity or uncertainty.

## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Poseidon = Admetos` centers on education that deepens into insight and wisdom. Thorough ideas help establish truth and clarify law; restraint of energy belongs to the same emphasis on considered understanding. The financial expression is specific: gaining truth about funds that are tied up. These themes connect [Witte's education and wisdom](#witte-entry) with [ABC's thoroughness and consolidation](#abc-entry).
