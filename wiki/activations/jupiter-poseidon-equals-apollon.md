## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 222; one-based PDF p. 218, Jupiter + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Many have the same opinion or judgement. Many are sensible.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 347; one-based PDF p. 172, Jupiter/Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Special legal success, experience in cultural law, successful intellectual/spiritual knowledge, successful cultural dissemination, trade in financial ideas, clear legal knowledge, truth about increase of money, knowledge of law and truth, and increase of law and clarity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared sensible judgment and successful dissemination of cultural and legal knowledge.
- psychology: Many people share an opinion or judgment; sense and clear knowledge are emphasized.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Agreement among many and cultural dissemination are explicit collective expressions.
- events/manifestations: Legal success, experience in cultural law, trade in financial ideas and truth about increasing money.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's agreement among many is not proof of unanimity or correctness; ABC gives knowledge and financial clauses without a network or education mechanism.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes many people sharing a sensible judgment; ABC specifies legal, cultural and financial knowledge and its dissemination. No direct contradiction is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Poseidon = Apollon` brings reasoned judgment into a wider collective setting: many share an opinion, and sensible understanding accompanies it. The practical expressions include spreading culture, knowing law and truth, and trading in financial ideas. Clear knowledge also extends to the increase of money. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies the shared judgment, while [ABC](#abc-entry) identifies the legal, cultural and financial fields in which clarity can grow.
