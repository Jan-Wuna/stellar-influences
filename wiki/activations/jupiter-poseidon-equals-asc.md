## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 222; one-based PDF p. 218, Jupiter + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

To deal with reasonable and intelligent people. The rights of others. Treated fair by others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 346; one-based PDF p. 172, Jupiter/Poseidon, Asc row.

Successful local culture, successful ideas for the surroundings, a successful cultural partner, a successful cultural place, financial ideas of others, joy in an intellectually lively partner, money for the culture of the surroundings, the legal culture of others, and law and reason at the place.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Reasonable, intelligent people and fair treatment meet successful local and shared cultural conditions.
- psychology: Joy in an intellectually lively partner accompanies respect for reason.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: The rights of others, fair treatment and a cultural partner are explicit relationship themes.
- events/manifestations: Successful cultural places, others' financial ideas, money for the surrounding culture and local law and reason.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's surroundings wording need not mean ecology; geistvoll describes an intellectually lively partner, and the omitted legal-culture-of-others clause is restored.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in reasonable dealings with other people. Witte states their rights and fair treatment; ABC adds local cultural, partner and financial contexts. No direct contradiction is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Poseidon = Asc` describes dealings with intelligent, reasonable people in which others' rights and fair treatment matter. Shared surroundings can support this through successful cultural places, an intellectually lively partner and clear local law. Others' financial ideas and funding for that culture give the picture a practical setting. [Witte's interpersonal account](#witte-entry) and [ABC's local and cultural clauses](#abc-entry) support these connected expressions.
