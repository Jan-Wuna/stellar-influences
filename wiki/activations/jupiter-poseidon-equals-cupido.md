## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 222; one-based PDF p. 218, Jupiter + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Relations and associations with intelligent and reasonable people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 347; one-based PDF p. 172, Jupiter/Poseidon, oriented activator row.

A successful community of ideas, a successful cultural community, intellectual/spiritual joy within a community, shared intellectual/spiritual success, clear finances within the community, legal clarity within a community, and advantage through clarity within a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful communities of ideas and culture associate intelligent and reasonable people.
- psychology: Shared intellectual/spiritual joy and success give the community a positive mental life.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Relations and associations rest on intelligent, reasonable company and shared ideas.
- events/manifestations: Clear community finances, legal clarity and advantage through clarity are concrete collective expressions.
- conflicts/notes: The entries specify community and culture without naming family or an artistic practice; legal clarity must remain explicit.

## Contradictions

Witte identifies intelligent and reasonable associates; ABC develops successful communities of ideas and culture with financial and legal clarity. These are complementary rather than contradictory.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Poseidon = Cupido` places intelligent, reasonable fellowship within a successful community of ideas or culture. Shared intellectual or spiritual joy can accompany common success, while clear finances and clear legal relations give that fellowship a practical foundation. The emphasis on useful clarity joins [Witte's associations](#witte-entry) to [ABC's community clauses](#abc-entry).
