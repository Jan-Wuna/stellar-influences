## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 222; one-based PDF p. 218, Jupiter + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Unpleasantness and difficulties through lack of reason and intelligence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 347; one-based PDF p. 172, Jupiter/Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Insightful financial renunciation, a successful spirit of the past, joy of discovery in what is hidden, joy in insight into the past, criminal legal culture, lack of law and reason, disadvantages through media culture, and outdated legal culture.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficulties through lack of reason coexist with insight into hidden matters and the past.
- psychology: Insight, discovery and considered financial renunciation give ABC favorable alternatives beside deficiencies of understanding.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Criminal or outdated legal culture and lack of law and reason affect the collective setting.
- events/manifestations: Insight into the past, financial renunciation and disadvantages through media culture.
- conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes deficient reason; ABC also names favorable insight. Its past and hidden-matter clauses should not be reduced to folly alone.

## Contradictions

Witte gives a critical account of difficulties caused by deficient reason and intelligence. ABC retains critical legal and media clauses but also gives successful engagement with the past, discovery and insightful financial renunciation. Those favorable and difficult alternatives remain distinct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Poseidon = Hades` can show understanding encountering deficits: lack of reason, damaged legal culture or disadvantages through media can create difficulty. It also has an inward or retrospective expression, finding joy in hidden knowledge and insight into the past, or making an informed financial renunciation. [Witte's critical account](#witte-entry) and [ABC's mixed alternatives](#abc-entry) support both expressions without treating every encounter with the past as a failure of intelligence.
