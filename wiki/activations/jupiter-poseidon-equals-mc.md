## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 218, Jupiter + Poseidon, MC row.

To have wisdom and intelligence. To listen to the voice of conscience and reason. The ability to judge what is right and wrong.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 346; one-based PDF p. 172, Jupiter + Poseidon, MC row.

Successful spirit-soul (Geist-Seele), clear joy of the soul, my intellectual or spiritual happiness, my idea of money, my cultural success, my right to clarity, my right to culture, my advantage through clarity, my joy in insights, and my legal insights.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal judgment guided by reason and conscience ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Intelligence includes the ability to distinguish right from wrong.
- Body/health: Neither entry establishes a bodily or medical meaning.
- Social/relationship: Moral judgment is explicit; a particular relationship outcome is not.
- Events/manifestations: The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds personal cultural success and legal understanding.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's full row gives personal joy, insights, culture and rights to clarity and culture. Its unusual Geist-Seele compound is retained explicitly without adding a separate psychological doctrine.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis rather than directly contradicting one another. ABC adds personal cultural success and rights to clarity or culture beside Witte's conscience and judgment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Poseidon = MC`, MC makes the pair's reason and conscience a matter of personal judgment: listening to conscience and deciding what is right or wrong are the explicit focus of the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds personal spiritual happiness, cultural success, and legal understanding. Together these support an inwardly owned standard of understanding; they do not establish a particular career or guarantee success.
