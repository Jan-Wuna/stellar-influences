## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 218, Jupiter + Poseidon, Mercury row.

Thoughts about right and conscience. Sensible thoughts. Conversations with intelligent and reasonable people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 346; one-based PDF p. 172, Jupiter + Poseidon, Mercury row.

Reports on cultural successes, success through clear thoughts, conversations about money culture, successful language culture, joy in understanding in youth, reasonable conversations about the law, advantages of media culture, and discussing advantageous ideas.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Reason and conscience expressed through thought and conversation ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Sensible thinking and clear ideas organize the interpretation.
- Body/health: The entries supply no specific bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Conversation with reasonable people is explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Reports, language, media, and discussion of useful ideas extend the theme ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: ABC explicitly gives reasonable conversations about the law; the earlier poor-language rendering was a mistranslation.

## Contradictions

Witte's sensible thought and ABC's reasonable legal conversation are compatible. Reporting, language and media provide more particular settings without establishing a disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Poseidon = Mercury`, Mercury gives the pair's reason and conscience a verbal and intellectual form. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes sensible thought and conversation with intelligent people; the complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) extends this to clear ideas, cultural reporting, language, and media. The unifying theme is understanding made communicable, including reasonable conversation about the law.
