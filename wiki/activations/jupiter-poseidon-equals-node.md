## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Connections with intelligent and reasonable people. Happiness through intellectual and spiritual connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Encounters with right and truth, success in spiritual relationships, joy through ethics in connections, joy through spiritual contacts, good cultural relationships, clear relationships with money, and clarity in financial connections

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Benefit through intellectual, spiritual, and ethical connections ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Enjoyment of shared understanding and spiritual contact is explicit.
- Body/health: Neither entry establishes a medical or bodily meaning.
- Social/relationship: Reasonable associates and ethical relationships carry the central theme.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural relationships and clarity in financial connections broaden the examples ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: ABC locator: printed p. 346 (PDF p. 172). No direct conflict is apparent, but repetition is not independent corroboration. Witte entry starts on PDF p. 218; its printed page is unconfirmed.

## Contradictions

The entries offer compatible examples of beneficial connection; they do not establish that every association is beneficial.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Poseidon = Node`, Node makes the pair's reason and spiritual understanding relational. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) locates happiness in connections with intelligent, reasonable people; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds ethical contact, cultural relationships, and clarity about money within connections. The shared thread is benefit arising through the quality of understanding between people, rather than a promise attached to association alone.
