## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 218, Jupiter + Poseidon, Saturn row.

To have to switch off reason and intelligence. Persons with whom reason and intelligence first must grow to maturity. To educate others to reason and intelligence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 347; one-based PDF p. 172, Jupiter + Poseidon, Saturn row.

Intellectual or spiritual success through patience, intellectual or spiritual advantages in old age, disturbed money culture, gain in ideals through losses, problematic legal culture, problematic financial truth, imbuing legal problems with intellectual or spiritual meaning (Rechts-Probleme durchgeistigen), and loss of clarity about money.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Reason and spiritual understanding meet impediment, maturity, or patience ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Judgment may need development; educating others is a separate explicit possibility.
- Body/health: ABC's old-age example supplies no diagnosis or bodily mechanism.
- Social/relationship: Teaching reason is explicit; ABC adds problematic legal and financial clarity.
- Events/manifestations: Patient success, gain through loss, and loss of clarity about money occur in the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry).
- Conflicts/notes: ABC gives disturbed money culture and Rechts-Probleme durchgeistigen, retained as imbuing legal problems with intellectual or spiritual meaning. That unusual verb does not specify a legal method.

## Contradictions

Blocked reason and success through patience are contrasting possibilities, not one assured progression from difficulty to wisdom.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Poseidon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies a difficulty or maturation in the pair's reason and understanding. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) ranges from switching off reason to cultivating it through education. The complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds patient spiritual success alongside loss and financial or legal uncertainty. These are alternative expressions of the formula: patience may support understanding, but neither the duration of a blockage nor its eventual resolution is established.
