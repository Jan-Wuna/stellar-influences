## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 218, Jupiter + Poseidon, Sun row.

Days devoted to meditation. The voice of the conscience. The man who follows the voice of his conscience, who knows what is right, who listens to reason.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 346; one-based PDF p. 172, Jupiter + Poseidon, Sun row.

Financial ideas of a man, intellectual or spiritual advantage of a person, personal legal clarity, personal cultural success, personal success concerning truth, a person's right to clarity, advantages of a cultured person, and a center for law and reason.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A person follows reason and conscience ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Meditation and attention to what is right are explicit.
- Body/health: No particular bodily or medical meaning is established.
- Social/relationship: The historical male example belongs to the source's language, not a universal gender rule.
- Events/manifestations: ABC adds personal cultural success, legal clarity, and financial ideas ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: ABC gives intellectual or spiritual advantage, not common advantage, and separately names a person's right to clarity and the advantages of a cultured person. The historical male example remains source-specific.

## Contradictions

Personal conscience and cultural or legal success are distinct emphases, not proof that right conduct always produces success.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Poseidon = Sun`, Sun gives the pair's reason and conscience a personal expression: the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes meditation and a person listening to conscience. The complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) broadens this to personal legal clarity, cultural success, and financial ideas. Reflective conduct unifies the clearest material; neither the historical male wording nor the success examples establish a gender restriction or a guaranteed reward.
