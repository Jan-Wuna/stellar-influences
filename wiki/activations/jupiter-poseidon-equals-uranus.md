## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 218, Jupiter + Poseidon, Uranus row.

Sudden understandings. Wisdom and understanding come as a surprise.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 347; one-based PDF p. 172, Jupiter + Poseidon, Uranus row.

Exciting legal culture, intellectual or spiritual impulses of happiness, a clear legal event, successful technical ideas, joy over a cultural event, new cultural successes, impulses of law and reason, and advantage through surprising clarity.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Understanding arrives unexpectedly ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Surprise and sudden clarity specify the manner of insight.
- Body/health: Neither entry establishes a medical or bodily claim.
- Social/relationship: ABC gives cultural and legal contexts, without a specific relationship outcome.
- Events/manifestations: Technical ideas, cultural events, and new cultural successes are explicit ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: ABC gives impulses of law and reason as a complete clause, beside technical ideas and surprising clarity; it does not name a particular legal event.

## Contradictions

Sudden insight and cultural or technical examples differ in scope; no direct conflict is established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Poseidon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair's understanding as sudden and surprising. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) states this directly, while the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) places surprising clarity alongside successful technical ideas and cultural events. The common thread is a useful realization or new idea arriving abruptly; the entries do not claim that every surprise provides complete understanding.
