## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 218, Jupiter + Poseidon, Venus row.

To be happy in one’s religion, faith, attitude towards life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 346; one-based PDF p. 172, Jupiter + Poseidon, Venus row.

Joy in intellectual or spiritual harmony, success in culture and art, successful peace culture, successful artistic ideas, joy through intellectual or spiritual love, joy and harmony through culture, harmony between law and truth, and preference for legal clarity.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Happiness in faith or one's outlook on life ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Spiritual harmony and satisfaction link understanding with enjoyment.
- Body/health: The entries do not establish a bodily or medical meaning.
- Social/relationship: Spiritual love, peace, and harmony are explicit in ABC.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural and artistic success broaden the religious emphasis ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: ABC separately names successful peace culture and successful artistic ideas; they are distinct clauses rather than a run-together phrase.

## Contradictions

Faith, art, and legal harmony are different contexts of satisfaction; the entries establish no direct conflict between them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Poseidon = Venus`, Venus makes the pair's spiritual understanding a source of happiness and harmony. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) locates this in religion, faith, or one's attitude to life; the complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) extends it to spiritual love, art, culture, and harmony between law and truth. Satisfaction in a meaningful outlook is central, with cultural expression as a further possibility.
