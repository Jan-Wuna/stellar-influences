## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 218, Jupiter + Poseidon, Zeus row.

To follow the better instinct. Success through correct perception of things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 347; one-based PDF p. 172, Jupiter + Poseidon, Zeus row.

Success through intellectual or spiritual effort, successful intellectual or spiritual leadership, successful legal clarity, successful cultural shaping, generation of successful ideas, joy in striving for insight, money for cultural goals, clear legal guidance, and shaping legal culture.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Success through sound perception and mental effort ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Following the better instinct connects discernment with purpose.
- Body/health: Neither entry establishes a bodily or medical manifestation.
- Social/relationship: Cultural design and legal guidance are explicit in ABC.
- Events/manifestations: Successful ideas and money for cultural goals are supported examples ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: ABC gives intellectual or spiritual leadership and joy in striving for insight, not movement or struggle; shaping legal culture is an active final clause.

## Contradictions

Sound instinct and deliberate mental effort are complementary emphases here, not opposing doctrines.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Poseidon = Zeus`, Zeus gives the pair's understanding a purposeful expression. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) makes correct perception and the better instinct the grounds of success; the complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds intellectual or spiritual leadership and effort, cultural shaping, generation of ideas, striving for insight and resources for cultural goals. The connection is discernment directed toward making or guiding something, without assuming that every undertaking succeeds.
