## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 207, Jupiter + Saturn, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

Happy separation of many or for many. Building for trade, commerce or science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 325; one-based PDF p. 161, Jupiter/Saturn, oriented activator row.

Success with legal problems, disruptions of success in trade, financial losses in the economy, concentration on legal knowledge, problematic increase of money, disturbed joy in peace, loss of much money, much joy in old age, and increasing legal problems.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Separation, building, and constraint operate across commerce or many people ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Concentration on legal knowledge is present in ABC; a general temperament is not established.
- Body/health: No specific bodily or medical meaning is supplied.
- Social/relationship: Witte explicitly describes separation involving or benefiting many.
- Events/manifestations: Buildings for trade or science coexist with commercial and financial losses, success with legal problems and much joy in old age ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: The raw ABC row states disturbed joy in peace, much money and much joy in old age. Its favorable legal/old-age clauses remain beside commercial losses.

## Contradictions

Useful construction and commercial losses are different possible manifestations; a uniformly constructive expansion would flatten the evidence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Saturn = Apollon`, Apollon gives the pair's building and separation themes a wider commercial, scientific, or collective setting. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) includes useful separation and buildings for trade or science, whereas the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also names economic losses and increasing legal problems. Success with legal problems and much joy in old age provide favorable alternatives. Practical organization is a possible expression, but the same orientation includes limits and losses across a broader field.
