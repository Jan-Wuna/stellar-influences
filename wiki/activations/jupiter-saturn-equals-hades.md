## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 207, Jupiter + Saturn, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

Content with little. Consequences of former errors show. Misfortune in or with buildings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 325; one-based PDF p. 161, Jupiter/Saturn, oriented activator row.

The secret of success in aging, disruptions of success in service, dangerous legal problems, lack of money in old age, financial problems through renunciation, successful concentration on the past, inadequate success through harshness, and losses through financial crime.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Limited means and consequences of earlier errors ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Contentment with little and successful concentration on the past coexist with inadequate success through harshness.
- Body/health: References to old age do not establish a medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Financial crime is an ABC example, not grounds to label a person criminal.
- Events/manifestations: Building misfortune, shortages, and financial losses are concrete examples ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: ABC names a secret of success in aging without explaining it; disruptions in service and success through concentration on the past remain separate clauses.

## Contradictions

Contentment and misfortune can describe different aspects of limitation; the entries do not promise that adversity brings wisdom.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Saturn = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair's limitation through scarcity and the aftermath of earlier errors. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) ranges from contentment with little to misfortune involving buildings. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds shortage, disruptions in service, legal problems and losses involving financial crime. It also allows successful concentration on the past, while harshness yields inadequate success. Restricted resources and difficult consequences therefore coexist with contentment and a potentially useful retrospective focus.
