#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: retained printed p. 258, JUPITER/SATURN With Itself; passage unchanged in this review.

Increased need to apply principles of hard work and dedication toward goals set; added generosity shown toward others; difficult assignments performed with ease; additional delays when producing publications; many obstacles.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Greater application of hard work and dedication to goals ([Munkasey Entry](#munkasey-entry), p. 258).
- Psychology: The need for sustained effort increases, even when difficult tasks feel easier.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is given.
- Social/relationship: Added generosity toward others is explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Publication delays and many obstacles coexist with ease in difficult assignments.
- Conflicts/notes: This repeated-factor formula has one source and no distinct triad hub; ease and obstruction must both remain visible.

## Contradictions

Ease in demanding assignments and delays in publication are different manifestations, not a guarantee that effort removes every obstacle.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Saturn = Jupiter`, repeated Jupiter specifies the pair's effort toward goals through increased generosity and the capacity to handle demanding assignments. The [Munkasey Entry](#munkasey-entry), p. 258, keeps this alongside additional publication delays and many obstacles. The result is a mixed picture of greater engagement and continuing constraint: difficult work may become easier while delays and obstacles remain.
