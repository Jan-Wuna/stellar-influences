## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 207, Jupiter + Saturn, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

Sudden separation favored by good fortunate. Surprising quick development of a matter. To arrive at a decision. Sudden material loss or damaged buildings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 325; one-based PDF p. 161, Jupiter/Saturn, Uranus row.

Agitating disruptions of success, an exciting legal event, successful concentration on technology, joyful excitement despite pain, a happy event in old age, sudden financial problems, difficult financial reform, and surprising success through separation.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 226; one-based PDF p. 228, coded entry 0788.

Strong emotional tension, frequent change of mood, irritability. Sudden loss of consciousness (through illness, shock or death), sudden change in circumstance, making great efforts, change of residence, change of occupation. Damage to a building, motor stoppage, car breakdown.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 79, Jupiter/Saturn, Uranus paragraph; chapter begins on p. 78.

You need to come to one’s own decisions about what is right and wrong for you. A genius for seeing what kind of discipline is needed and for justifying why.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `256`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: retained printed p. 256, JUPITER/SATURN with Planets and Points; passage unchanged in this review.

Creating a lifestyle where you have more freedom to choose your activities; freedom from the shackles of a demanding job to pursue more leisurely and personal activities; quick changes abruptly introduced to your lifestyle.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Change, separation, or decision becomes abrupt ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Independent moral decisions coexist with tension and irritability.
- Body/health: ABC mentions joyful excitement despite pain; Ebertin's loss-of-consciousness and death examples remain historical claims, not medical predictions.
- Social/relationship: Freedom from a demanding job and increased choice are explicit in Munkasey.
- Events/manifestations: Rapid development, material loss, relocation, and breakdowns remain alternative examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Raw ABC inspection confirms separation rather than infancy and pain rather than faltering, with a happy event in old age and sudden financial problems. Sandbach's exact Uranus paragraph is on PDF p. 79.

## Contradictions

Liberating change and disruptive loss are contrasting outcomes. The entries do not establish tension as the cause of the listed material events.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Saturn = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair's changes as sudden decisions, breaks, or rapid developments. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) holds fortunate separation alongside material loss, and the [Munkasey Entry](#munkasey-entry) describes abruptly increased freedom of activity. The [Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry) adds deciding one's own standards; the [Ebertin Entry](#ebertin-entry) retains tension and disruption. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds technological concentration, financial reform, joy despite pain and surprising success through separation. The shared concern is abrupt restructuring, whose consequences can be liberating or difficult.
