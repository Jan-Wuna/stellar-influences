## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 207, Jupiter + Saturn, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

Secluded buildings. Buildings with are guarded. Armories.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 325; one-based PDF p. 161, Jupiter/Saturn, oriented activator row.

Successful concentration of energy, tremendous success through perseverance, a happy influence of separation, strength and joy through patience, massive financial loss, problematic power of money, separation through legal force, and loss of legal influence.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Seclusion and guarded structures give limitation a concrete form ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Concentrated energy, patience, and perseverance are ABC themes.
- Body/health: Energy language supplies no medical or bodily finding.
- Social/relationship: Separation through legal force and loss of legal influence occur in ABC.
- Events/manifestations: Guarded buildings and armories coexist with financial-power problems ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's printed with are is retained as an awkward phrase. Raw ABC inspection confirms a happy influence of separation and massive financial loss; neither clause is damaged.

## Contradictions

Persevering success, guarded enclosure, and loss of influence are distinct manifestations; they do not establish that enclosure protects growth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Saturn = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair's limitation through concentrated force and guarded enclosure. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) gives secluded buildings, guards, and armories; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds perseverance, patience, a happy influence of separation, massive financial loss, legal force and problems involving financial power. The connection is strong concentration or enforcement within limits, without assuming that a guarded setting is beneficial.
