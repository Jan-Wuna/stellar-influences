## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 212; one-based PDF p. 208, Jupiter + Uranus, oriented activator row.

Sudden luck for a few, in a small circle, in a closed corporation, in an exclusive circle.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 327; one-based PDF p. 162, Jupiter/Uranus, oriented activator row.

A restrictive legal event, successful rotational rhythm, successful deepening in technology, concentration on technical successes, establishing new law, sudden consolidation of happiness, sudden legal restriction, and a surprising end of happiness.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden luck is confined to a small or exclusive circle ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Concentration on technical success is explicit in ABC.
- Body/health: No specific bodily or medical meaning is supplied.
- Social/relationship: A few people or a closed corporation share the fortunate opening.
- Events/manifestations: Legal restriction and technical specialization broaden the range ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: Raw ABC inspection resolves establishing new law and both happiness clauses. Rotational rhythm is retained literally; no particular process or machine is specified.

## Contradictions

Selective luck and legal restriction are contrasting possibilities. ABC also explicitly gives both sudden consolidation and a surprising end of happiness, so its favorable and adverse endings remain distinct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Uranus = Admetos`, Admetos narrows the pair's sudden luck to a few people or a bounded setting, as the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) explicitly states. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds technical concentration, establishing new law and legal limitation, together with sudden consolidation or a surprising end of happiness. The connection is opportunity within a restricted field, with restriction retained as a possible difficulty rather than assumed to be a privilege.
