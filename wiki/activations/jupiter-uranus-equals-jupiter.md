#### Munkasey Entry

A restlessness about the status of ideas and concepts and a need to change these for more modern doctrines; increased ability or need to boast or give opinions where you have little skill or expertise; many forms of peculiarity.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Uranus = Jupiter`, repeated Jupiter specifies the pair's novelty through restless revision of ideas and doctrines. The [Munkasey Entry](#munkasey-entry), p. 262, couples this with boasting or giving opinions beyond one's competence. The connecting issue is the urge to advance a new outlook, which may exceed its evidential or practical foundation; the entry does not provide a separate promise of fortune.
