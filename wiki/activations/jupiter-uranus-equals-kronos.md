## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 212; one-based PDF p. 208, Jupiter + Uranus, oriented activator row.

Good luck through the authorities, for the state, or for the father.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 327; one-based PDF p. 162, Jupiter/Uranus, oriented activator row.

Special impulses of joy, a particularly gratifying event, impulses of good fortune from the boss, a successful state event, excitement over state funds, sudden state success, state advantages through technology, unrest in the state under the rule of law, and important financial reform.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Fortune involves authorities, the state, or a father ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Joy over a notable event appears in ABC; no fixed temperament is established.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim is given.
- Social/relationship: Authority and paternal figures define the supported relational setting.
- Events/manifestations: State success, technical benefits, financial reform, and legal unrest are ABC examples ([ABC Entry](#abc-entry)).
- Conflicts/notes: Raw ABC inspection confirms impulses of good fortune from the boss. Its Rechts-Staat means the state under the rule of law; unrest remains beside favorable state success.

## Contradictions

State benefit and legal unrest are distinct possibilities; official involvement does not assure support in every case.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Uranus = Kronos`, Kronos places the pair's fortune in an official or paternal context. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes luck through authorities, for the state, or for a father. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds impulses of good fortune from the boss, sudden state success, technology and financial reform, while retaining unrest in the state under the rule of law. Authority is the setting of the opportunity, not an assurance that institutions always confer benefit.
