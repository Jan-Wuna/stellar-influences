## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 212; one-based PDF p. 208, Jupiter + Uranus, oriented activator row.

Pleasure over a stroke of luck or good fortune.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 326; one-based PDF p. 162, Jupiter/Uranus, oriented activator row.

A joyful event for the soul, impulses of happiness in the soul, my sudden success, my technical advantage, my financial tension, my joy in rhythm, my surprising joy, and sudden joy of the soul.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 230; one-based PDF p. 232, coded entry 0805.

Optimism, fortunate powers of adaptability, love of change and variety, attainment of good knowledge and good understanding of things. A fortunate turn in life.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 81, Jupiter/Uranus, Midheaven paragraph; chapter begins on p. 80.

A person who encourages all people to be free and to value their individuality. One who helps others to accept their own uniqueness.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `260`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: retained printed p. 260, JUPITER/URANUS with Planets and Points; passage unchanged in this review.

Receiving recognition for the improvements you created because you were dissatisfied with parts of life; the personal rewards you gain from seeing the effects of changes you have initiated or promoted in life.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Good fortune and beneficial change become a personal source of pleasure ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Optimism, adaptability, and enjoyment of variety are explicit in Ebertin.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical manifestation is established.
- Social/relationship: Encouraging others to accept their individuality appears in Sandbach.
- Events/manifestations: Recognition and rewards for improvements are concrete Munkasey examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Raw ABC inspection resolves all eight inward/first-person clauses, including technical advantage and sudden joy of the soul. Financial tension remains beside joy. Sandbach's exact Midheaven paragraph is on PDF p. 81; individuality is normalized across a typographical split.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize personal pleasure, adaptation and encouragement of individuality. ABC also includes personal financial tension, which remains distinct from its joy and technical advantage.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Uranus = MC`, MC makes the pair's fortunate change a personally experienced pleasure and direction. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) names enjoyment of luck; the [Ebertin Entry](#ebertin-entry) adds optimistic adaptability and a fortunate turn in life. The [Munkasey Entry](#munkasey-entry) connects reward with improvements one has promoted, while the [Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry) adds encouragement of individuality. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds inward joy, sudden personal success and technical advantage beside financial tension. Personal benefit and support for change connect these themes without requiring a public leadership role.
