## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 212; one-based PDF p. 208, Jupiter + Uranus, oriented activator row.

Cheerfulness. Mockery and wit combined. Quick at repartee. News or information about sudden money or judgment. Reports or talks on technological success.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 326; one-based PDF p. 162, Jupiter/Uranus, oriented activator row.

Exciting discussions among young people, a successful media event, joy in rhythmic movement, discussing joyful events, conversations about new money, reform of youth law, opinions about financial reform, and news about legal events.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 229; one-based PDF p. 231, coded entry 0797.

Abundance of thoughts, a person with many ideas, ready-wittedness. Gathering of ample knowledge, a fortunate turn in one's occupation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 80, Jupiter/Uranus, Mercury paragraph.

Communicating with an unbridled enthusiasm. Sudden bouts of excessive talking or thinking. A person who thinks independently and who has many ideas about many things. A love of free-ranging thinking and speculating.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `JUPITER/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `260`

#### Munkasey Entry

- Source reference: retained printed p. 260, JUPITER/URANUS with Planets and Points; passage unchanged in this review.

Recognizing the improvements that change can bring, and your dexterity with inventing and creating mechanical objects to effect this change; thinking on the need for change in your life, and how you can effect this.

### Aren Ober - Midpoint Interpretation Simplified

- Source heading: `Jupiter/Uranus Interpretation`
- PDF page(s): `127`

#### Ober Entry

- Source reference: retained one-based PDF p. 127, Jupiter/Uranus Interpretation, Mercury example; paraphrase unchanged in this review.

If Mercury is at Jupiter/Uranus, Ober gives sudden creative ideas or thoughts that can lead the native beneficially forward.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Novel possibilities arrive through ideas, information, and invention ([Ober Entry](#ober-entry), PDF p. 127).
- Psychology: Ready wit and independent thought coexist with excessive thinking or talking.
- Body/health: No medical or bodily meaning is established.
- Social/relationship: Repartee, mockery, and enthusiastic communication give the interpersonal range.
- Events/manifestations: Technical reporting, mechanical invention, media events, and occupational change are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: Raw ABC inspection confirms reform of youth law and financial reform, not forums. Sandbach's Mercury paragraph is on PDF p. 80 itself, so this is an exact entry locator as well as the chapter-start page.

## Contradictions

Inventive insight and mental excess are different manifestations, not evidence that every idea is beneficial or every enthusiastic speaker persuasive.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Uranus = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair's new possibilities through thought and communication. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) gives wit and technological news; the [Ebertin Entry](#ebertin-entry) and [Ober Entry](#ober-entry) add abundant or sudden creative ideas. The [Munkasey Entry](#munkasey-entry) makes invention practical through mechanical objects. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) places discussion in media, youth law, new money and financial reform. Free-ranging thinking is central, but the [Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry) explicitly retains excessive talking or thinking alongside enthusiasm.
