## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 212; one-based PDF p. 208, Jupiter + Uranus, oriented activator row.

Good fortune on mental or spiritual plane, i.e. through intelligence, reason, wisdom, the way you view the world.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 327; one-based PDF p. 162, Jupiter/Uranus, oriented activator row.

Successful cultural impulses, joy in new insights, joy in technical ideas, a new money culture, new legal clarity, spiritual impulses of success, dynamic legal culture, tensions over the truth about money, and reasonable legal impulses.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Fortune through reason, wisdom, or one's outlook ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry)).
- Psychology: Joy in new insights is explicit in ABC.
- Body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- Social/relationship: Culture and legal clarity provide social settings; a particular relationship outcome is not specified.
- Events/manifestations: Technical ideas, cultural impulses, and financial truth tensions appear in the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry).
- Conflicts/notes: Raw ABC inspection confirms reasonable legal impulses, not strong right impulses. Tensions over the truth about money remain alongside legal clarity and intellectual benefit.

## Contradictions

New understanding and tension about money concern different aspects of the entry; insight is not an assurance of clarity in every matter.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Uranus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair's fortune through mental or spiritual understanding. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) explicitly locates benefit in intelligence, reason, wisdom, and worldview; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds new insights, technical ideas, culture, legal clarity and reasonable legal impulses. Fresh understanding is the connecting theme, with tensions about financial truth retained as a limit to an entirely favorable reading.
