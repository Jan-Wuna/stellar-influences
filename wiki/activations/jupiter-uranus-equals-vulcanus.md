## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 212; one-based PDF p. 208, Jupiter + Uranus, Vulcanus row. The awkward opening Sudden might luck is printed as transcribed.

Sudden might luck Optimism through sudden luck.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 327; one-based PDF p. 162, Jupiter/Uranus, Vulcanus row.

Excitement over legal force, dynamic financial power, successful impulses of energy, successful influence through rhythm, joy in technical energy, tremendous technical success, powerful tension through money, and sudden legal influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Optimism through sudden luck meets forceful financial, technical and legal influence.
- psychology: Successful energy impulses and joy in technical energy accompany strong money-related tension.
- body/health: Energy language here does not establish a bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Legal force and sudden legal influence provide an institutional or social expression.
- events/manifestations: Dynamic financial power, technical success and successful influence through rhythm.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's opening Sudden might luck is awkward as printed and is not expanded; its clear optimism clause remains usable. ABC supplies eight clauses, including rhythm and legal influence.

## Contradictions

Witte's clear clause concerns optimism through sudden luck. ABC describes technical success and financial or legal power but also powerful tension through money. Favorable events and tension remain distinct rather than implying an assured gain in strength.

## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Uranus = Vulcanus` combines optimism after sudden luck with strong technical, financial or legal effects. Successful energy impulses and technical achievement can be accompanied by financial power, influence through rhythm or sudden legal influence. Money can also create powerful tension. [Witte's optimism clause](#witte-entry) and [ABC's full set of influence and energy clauses](#abc-entry) support this range; the malformed opening in Witte is left without further interpretation.
