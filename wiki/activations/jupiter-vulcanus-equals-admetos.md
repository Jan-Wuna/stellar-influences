## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 217, Jupiter + Vulcanus, Admetos row; printed page unconfirmed.

To be left in peace. Not to be troubled by anybody. To be happy with a few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 345; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, Admetos row.

Blocked financial power, consolidated legal power, thorough legal influence, a good influence of circulation, consolidating success in power, a thorough power advantage, consolidation of power through money, and advantage through restriction of energy.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Peace with a few and freedom from disturbance coexist with consolidated or blocked power.
- psychology: Being left in peace is explicit; the source does not equate happiness with possessing little.
- body/health: Energy restriction and circulation are named without specifying a bodily system or medical effect.
- social/relationship: Happiness with a few provides a limited social setting, while legal influence and power concern wider relations.
- events/manifestations: Consolidating legal or financial power, blocked financial power and advantages through restricted energy.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's gründlich means thorough, not Greek. Circulation and thorough power advantage remain literal without a specified mechanism.

## Contradictions

Witte describes undisturbed happiness with a few. ABC describes blocked financial power alongside consolidation, thorough influence and benefit through restricted energy. The limited setting and power clauses are different emphases; neither establishes that all restriction is favorable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Admetos` can express satisfaction in being left in peace and happy with a few people. Its power-related expression involves concentration and consolidation: legal influence may become established, money may consolidate power, and restricted energy may offer an advantage. Blocked financial power remains a different possibility. [Witte's peaceful small circle](#witte-entry) and [ABC's consolidation clauses](#abc-entry) support these bounded expressions without equating seclusion with poverty or guaranteed security.
