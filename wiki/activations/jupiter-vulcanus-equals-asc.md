## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To instill hope and trust in others, to give them courage to go on. To be influenced by others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

influential partner rights, influential financial place, successful influence of a place, financial influence of a place, money power at a place, money for strengthening the environment, enormous advantage of the partner, success of power at a place, and legal influence of the partner

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Vulcanus = Asc`: a presence that gives others courage, confidence, and trust, while also showing how strongly the environment can shape or impress the native.
- Chart expression: personal encouragement, influential reassurance, and the ability to transmit hope with weight and conviction.
