## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 217, Jupiter + Vulcanus, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

To instill hope and trust in others, to give them courage to go on. To be influenced by others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 344; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, Asc row.

influential partner rights, influential financial place, successful influence of a place, financial influence of a place, money power at a place, money for strengthening the environment, enormous advantage of the partner, success of power at a place, and legal influence of the partner

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Encouraging hope and trust in others meets local, partner and financial influence.
- psychology: Giving others courage and being influenced by others are both explicit.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Partner rights and legal influence accompany mutual influence and encouragement.
- events/manifestations: An influential financial place, local financial power, funding to strengthen the surroundings and a partner's substantial advantage.
- conflicts/notes: Witte allows receiving influence as well as giving it; ABC's surroundings need not mean an ecological project.

## Contradictions

Witte describes the interpersonal effect of hope, trust and influence. ABC specifies legal, financial and local forms of influence. No direct contradiction is stated, and the entries do not establish that influence always operates in one direction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Asc` joins the ability to give hope and courage with the experience of being influenced by other people. That influence can have a practical local form: a partner's rights or advantage, a financial center or money used to strengthen the surroundings. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies the interpersonal encouragement, while [ABC](#abc-entry) identifies the partner, legal and financial settings in which influence operates.
