## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 217, Jupiter + Vulcanus, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

To escape treachery, or to be successful with treacherous deeds.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 345; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, oriented activator row.

Successful influence of the past, success through renunciation of power, danger through legal power, dangerous financial influence, an enormous lack of money, criminal financial power, great joy in what is hidden, and disadvantages through legal force.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Escape from treachery or success through treacherous deeds meets difficult financial and legal power.
- psychology: Great joy in hidden matters and influence from the past are stated without a fixed personality judgment.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Treachery and criminal financial power are source examples; renouncing power is a separate route to success.
- events/manifestations: Legal-power dangers, financial influence, severe money shortage and disadvantage through legal force.
- conflicts/notes: ABC says renunciation of power, reversing the former absorption wording. Witte does not name superior strength as the means of escape.

## Contradictions

Witte distinguishes escaping treachery from succeeding through it. ABC adds success through renouncing power beside dangers from legal and financial power. These alternatives should remain distinct; they do not establish that force is always the route to advantage.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Hades` brings advantage into contact with compromised or dangerous power. One expression is escape from treachery; another is success through deeds the source calls treacherous. Success may also come through renouncing power, while financial shortage and legal danger remain possible. [Witte's two treachery alternatives](#witte-entry) and [ABC's renunciation and power clauses](#abc-entry) keep the source's different routes to benefit visible.
