## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 217, Jupiter + Vulcanus, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

To have the confidence of the state. Mighty fortune or gains of money through the power of the state. Successful use of the power of the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 345; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, oriented activator row.

Particularly successful influence, dominant financial influence, influential legal authority, a successful hierarchy of power, successful state power, great strength through joy, power of the state under the rule of law, state financial power, and state legal force.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Confidence from the state and gains through state power meet dominant financial and legal influence.
- psychology: Great strength through joy is explicit, without a general temperament claim.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: State confidence, legal authority and hierarchy define the institutional setting.
- events/manifestations: Financial gains through state power, successful use of state power and state financial/legal force.
- conflicts/notes: ABC describes influential legal authority, not monolithic authority. The entries do not specify personal protection or patronage.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in successful state power and finance. Witte emphasizes state confidence and gains, while ABC specifies hierarchy and legal authority. No direct contradiction is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Kronos` places successful influence in a state or institutional setting. Having the state's confidence, gaining money through its power and using that power successfully are explicit expressions. Financial influence, legal authority and hierarchy give the picture its formal reach. [Witte's state-power account](#witte-entry) and [ABC's institutional clauses](#abc-entry) support this interpretation without specifying a protective patron or personal rank.
