## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 217, Jupiter + Vulcanus, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

Great business success through one’s own accomplishment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 344; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, oriented activator row.

Working with the power of money, working with legal force, influential legal activity, successful capacity for work, successful actions of power, joy through influential work, the influence of power at work, and great joy in work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Great business success through one's own accomplishment meets influential work with money or legal force.
- psychology: Joy in influential work and great work-related joy are explicit.
- body/health: Capacity for work is stated without a bodily condition or medical claim.
- social/relationship: Legal activity and the influence of power at work provide a social setting.
- events/manifestations: Business accomplishment, work with financial power and successful actions involving power.
- conflicts/notes: Rechts-Gewalt is legal force here; the entry does not establish personal violence or competitive action as the means of success.

## Contradictions

Witte's business success through personal accomplishment and ABC's successful, influential work overlap. ABC specifies financial power and legal force; no direct contradiction is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Mars` links accomplishment with effective and influential work. Business success may arise through one's own achievement, while work involving money's power, legal force or other forms of influence brings joy and visible effectiveness. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies the personal business accomplishment and [ABC](#abc-entry) its work, legal and financial expressions.
