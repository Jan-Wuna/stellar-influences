## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: one-based PDF p. 217, MC row.

To be an optimist. To trust to others, and to be trusted. To have good luck.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 344 (one-based PDF p. 171), MC row.

Successful influence of the soul, joy in psychic energy, strength of inner joy, my influence through money, my successful influence, my great advantage, my legal influence, my financial strength, and the influence of money on the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal optimism, trust, and effective influence or advantage.
- psychology: Inner joy and psychic energy in ABC's source terminology; optimism in Witte.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily condition or medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Trusting others and being trusted; one's own successful influence.
- events/manifestations: Good luck, financial strength, monetary influence, and legal influence.
- conflicts/notes: MC's personal influence is explicit; vocation, public status, or guaranteed financial gain is not supplied.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize compatible personal capacities without a direct disagreement. Trust, good luck, and financial or legal influence are not stated to occur together in every case.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Jupiter/Vulcanus = MC`, MC specifies the pair through personal optimism and the strength of one's influence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives trust in others, being trusted, and good luck. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds inner joy, successful influence, and personal financial or legal capacity, retaining its soul language for the inward effect of money and joy. The common focus is confidence and effective personal capacity.
