## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 217, Jupiter + Vulcanus, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

Confident thinking. Optimistic conversations. Great wishes. Hopes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 344; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, oriented activator row.

Reports of successes in power, influential financial conversations, influential legal conversations, successful power of thought, successful media influence, power-related advantages of the media, news from a financial power, and beneficial influence of young people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Confident thinking and optimistic conversation meet influential communication in money, law and media.
- psychology: Hope and great wishes are explicit.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Influential conversations and the beneficial influence of young people give thought an interpersonal expression.
- events/manifestations: Reports of power successes, media influence and news from a financial power.
- conflicts/notes: Gespräche means conversations, not a separate financial or legal language; the sources do not state that speech itself produces success.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes confidence, optimism and hope; ABC supplies financial, legal and media influence. These are compatible emphases without a stated mechanism by which confidence guarantees influence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Mercury` gives confidence and hope a communicative expression. Optimistic conversation can meet influential discussion of financial or legal matters, reports of successful power and the reach of media. The favorable influence of young people is another stated channel. [Witte's confident thought](#witte-entry) and [ABC's communication clauses](#abc-entry) support these expressions without claiming that words alone create the desired result.
