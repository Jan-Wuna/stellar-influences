#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 344; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, oriented activator row.

A woman's successful power, successful power of a people, a woman's financial strength, a feeling of joy through power, a woman's great joy, a woman's legal influence, a woman's legal success, strong inner joy, and the beneficial influence of a people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Optimism and confidence in women or the public accompany joy, financial strength and influence.
- psychology: Feelings of joy through power and strong inner joy are explicit.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: A woman's legal influence and a people's beneficial influence are source-native social examples.
- events/manifestations: Successful public or female power, financial strength and legal success.
- conflicts/notes: The gendered examples are historical source wording; neither entry specifies motherhood, maternal reassurance or universal emotional trust.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes optimism and confidence among women and the public. ABC adds financial and legal strength and joy through power. No direct contradiction is stated.

## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Moon` expresses confidence and joy in a personal or public setting. The specific examples include a woman's financial and legal strength, successful power of a people and the feeling of joy that power can bring. [Witte's optimism](#witte-entry) and [ABC's nine clauses](#abc-entry) support this mix of feeling and influence, with the gendered examples retained as the sources' historical wording.
