## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 217, Jupiter + Vulcanus, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

Mighty good luck or fortune which is not immediately recognized, but reveals itself in the future. The beginning of earning or making larger sums of money easily. Also to lose large sums of money.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 345; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, Neptune row.

Successful water power, successful influence of metaphysics, joy in subtle energies, enormous legal uncertainty, great success through sensitivity, great joy in water, weak legal force, and advantage through water energy.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Fortune may reveal itself later; easy large earnings and large losses coexist with sensitivity, metaphysics and water power.
- psychology: Joy in subtle energies and sensitivity are ABC motifs, without an explained physical mechanism.
- body/health: The source's subtle-energy and water language does not identify a bodily condition or medical effect.
- social/relationship: Enormous legal uncertainty and weak legal force are explicit practical contexts.
- events/manifestations: Later-recognized fortune, beginning to earn large sums easily, losing large sums and advantages through water energy.
- conflicts/notes: ABC says subtle energies, not fossil energies, and includes great success through sensitivity. Neither source gives blurred valuation or speculation as a causal explanation.

## Contradictions

Witte explicitly gives both large earnings and large losses. ABC combines successful sensitivity, metaphysical influence and water power with legal uncertainty and weak legal force. The favorable and adverse possibilities remain distinct; no mechanism connects them.

## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Neptune` can describe fortune that becomes clear only later, including the beginning of easy large earnings, while large losses remain a separate possibility. Sensitivity, metaphysical interests and water-related energy give [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) a further range, alongside substantial legal uncertainty. [Witte's financial alternatives](#witte-entry) and those ABC clauses support delayed recognition and mixed practical outcomes without specifying why a gain or loss occurs.
