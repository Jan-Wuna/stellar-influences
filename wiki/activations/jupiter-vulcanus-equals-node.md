## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 217, Jupiter + Vulcanus, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

To be connected with trustworthy people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 344; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, Node row.

Relationships with financial power, the influence of good relationships, influential financial connections, successful relationships with power, enormous advantage through connections, great joy within a connection, joining strength and joy, and connections with legal power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections with trustworthy people meet beneficial, influential financial or legal relationships.
- psychology: Great joy within a connection and joining strength with joy are explicit.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Trustworthy people, good relationships and influential connections define the relational setting.
- events/manifestations: Advantages through connections and contacts with financial or legal power.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's trustworthiness and ABC's influence are different qualities; financial power alone is not stated to establish trust or protection.

## Contradictions

Witte identifies trustworthy contacts; ABC describes their possible influence, advantage and financial/legal settings. No direct contradiction is stated, but power and trustworthiness should not be treated as identical.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Node` places benefit in connections. Trustworthy people and good relationships can bring joy and substantial advantage, while influential financial or legal contacts give the picture a practical setting. Joining strength and joy is one explicit expression of the bond. [Witte's trustworthy people](#witte-entry) and [ABC's connection clauses](#abc-entry) support this range without making influence itself proof of dependability.
