## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 217, Jupiter + Vulcanus, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

Great luck begins to develop. A turnaround that is very successful.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 345; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, oriented activator row.

Successful transformation of energy, successful development of power, unfolding financial influence, developing legal power, unfolding great joy, powerful legal influence, strong success in evolution, strong change in finance, and changed financial strength.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Great luck begins to develop through a successful turnaround, with transformed energy and growing influence.
- psychology: Unfolding great joy accompanies successful development.
- body/health: Energy transformation and evolution are source terms without a specified bodily system or medical effect.
- social/relationship: Developing legal power and financial influence provide practical social settings.
- events/manifestations: Successful turnaround, changed finances and financial strength, and unfolding influence.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's evolution clause is retained broadly; it does not identify a biological mechanism. The entries do not specifically state recovery from an earlier failure.

## Contradictions

The entries share successful development and turnaround. ABC adds financial and legal change; no direct contradiction is stated, and no precise sequence of reform or recovery is supplied.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Pluto` describes fortune beginning to develop through a successful turnaround. Energy and power can develop with it, while financial influence, legal power and joy unfold in parallel. Changes in finance and financial strength make the transformation concrete. [Witte's turnaround](#witte-entry) and [ABC's nine development clauses](#abc-entry) support this account without specifying an earlier crisis or a guaranteed path of recovery.
