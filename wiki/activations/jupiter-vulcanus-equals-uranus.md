## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 217, Jupiter + Vulcanus, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

Sudden commencing of great fortune. Sudden reason for great optimism.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 345; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, Uranus row.

Agitating legal force, influential financial reform, an influential legal event, joy in rhythmic energy, powerful impulses of success, sudden strong success, enormous unrest because of money, advantage through technical energies, and beneficial legal influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Great fortune or strong success can begin suddenly, bringing a reason for optimism.
- psychology: Joy in rhythmic energy and sudden optimism coexist with enormous money-related unrest.
- body/health: Rhythmic and technical energy language does not specify a bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Legal force, influential legal events and beneficial legal influence define practical collective settings.
- events/manifestations: Sudden success, financial reform and advantages through technical energies.
- conflicts/notes: ABC says sudden rather than explosive success and beneficial rather than proactive legal influence; invention or liberation is not stated in these rows.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes the sudden beginning of fortune and optimism. ABC adds legal and technical success but also enormous unrest because of money. Those favorable and difficult possibilities remain distinct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Uranus` brings the possibility of a sudden favorable beginning and a strong reason for optimism. Financial reform, legal events and technical energies can give that change practical reach, while money may also bring substantial unrest. [Witte's sudden fortune](#witte-entry) and [ABC's legal, financial and technical clauses](#abc-entry) support the abrupt change without specifying invention or liberation as its mechanism.
