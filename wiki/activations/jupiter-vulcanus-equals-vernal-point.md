## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 217, Jupiter + Vulcanus, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

Optimism. General confidence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 344; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, oriented activator row.

Financial influence in public, an enormous advantage in public, the strength of public law, advantages of power in public, great joy in public, public financial power, and legal influence in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: General confidence and optimism take public financial, legal and power-related forms.
- psychology: Great joy in public accompanies Witte's general confidence.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Public law and financial power provide a collective institutional setting.
- events/manifestations: Public financial influence, substantial advantages and legal influence.
- conflicts/notes: ABC gives seven distinct public clauses, not three repeated power phrases. The sources do not state that prosperity is widely distributed.

## Contradictions

Witte supplies optimism and general confidence; ABC specifies public money, law, power and joy. No direct contradiction is stated, and general confidence is not itself a claim about equal distribution of benefit.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Vernal Point` gives optimism and general confidence a public setting. Financial influence, the strength of public law, advantages of power and public joy are the concrete expressions supplied here. [Witte's confidence](#witte-entry) and [ABC's seven public clauses](#abc-entry) support collective visibility without specifying how the advantages are distributed.
