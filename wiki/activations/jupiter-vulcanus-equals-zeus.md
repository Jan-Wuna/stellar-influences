## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 217, Jupiter + Vulcanus, oriented activator row; printed page unconfirmed.

Great happiness through successful creation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 345; one-based PDF p. 171, Jupiter + Vulkanus, Zeus row.

The beginning of great joy, influential shaping of law, success through powerful efforts, successful leadership power, successful legal force, leading financial influence, a tremendous fire of joy, great joy in creation, and beneficial legal influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Great happiness through successful creation meets strong effort, leadership and legal influence.
- psychology: The beginning of great joy and joy in creation are explicit.
- body/health: The fire-of-joy phrase supplies no bodily condition or medical claim.
- social/relationship: Leadership power and shaping law give creation a collective or institutional scope.
- events/manifestations: Successful effort, legal force, leading financial influence and beneficial legal influence.
- conflicts/notes: ABC says leadership power, not financial power, in its fourth clause. Fire of joy is retained without a specified ritual or physical process.

## Contradictions

Witte's successful creation and ABC's effort and creative joy overlap. ABC specifies leadership, finance and law; no direct contradiction is stated, and the source does not explain a universal release-of-will mechanism.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Vulcanus = Zeus` links successful creation with substantial happiness. Powerful effort can be expressed through leadership, shaping law and financial or legal influence, while the joy of creating remains central. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives the concise creative result and [ABC](#abc-entry) identifies its effort and institutional settings without prescribing a particular way to produce success.
