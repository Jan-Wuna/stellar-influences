## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

A fortune promising, but difficult beginning.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 337; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Continuing successes in accomplishment, restricted legal leadership, success through thorough pursuit of goals, successful limitation of fire, creative circulation of money, purposeful deepening in law, and resistance to financial leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A promising but difficult beginning meets continuing achievement and thorough pursuit of goals.
- psychology: Purposeful concentration on goals and deeper legal understanding are explicit.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Restricted legal leadership and resistance to financial leadership provide practical contexts.
- events/manifestations: Continuing accomplishments, successful limitation of fire and creative circulation of money.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's gründlich is thorough rather than natural; Witte's difficult beginning does not specify a slow timetable or guarantee eventual visible success.

## Contradictions

Witte describes the difficulty of a promising beginning. ABC allows continuing accomplishment and thorough goal pursuit alongside restricted leadership and resistance. They can describe different aspects of the effort without establishing a necessary delay-then-success sequence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Admetos` describes a beginning that holds promise while presenting difficulty. Continued accomplishment and thorough pursuit of a goal remain possible, but legal leadership may be restricted and financial leadership resisted. [Witte's difficult opening](#witte-entry) and [ABC's goal, law, fire-limitation and money-circulation clauses](#abc-entry) support concentrated work within limits without prescribing how long it takes or how it must end.
