## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Successful creation of products for many or with many. Beneficial creation work in science, commerce and industry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 337; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Successful leadership through money, successful legal efforts, financial success through creativity, joy experienced through goal striving, performance benefits through knowledge, creative legal knowledge, increased money production, and increasing performance pleasure

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creating products for or with many people meets beneficial work in science, commerce and industry.
- psychology: Joy in pursuing goals and increasing enjoyment of accomplishment are explicit in ABC.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Many people may participate in or benefit from creation; leadership and legal efforts are additional contexts.
- events/manifestations: Financial success through creativity, increased money generation, performance advantages through knowledge and creative legal knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: The entries specify collective creation and knowledge but do not establish an output-scaling mechanism or guarantee commercial growth.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in successful creative work. Witte specifies collective science, commerce and industry; ABC adds finance, legal effort, knowledge and goal pursuit. No direct contradiction is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Apollon` places successful creation in work done for or with many people. Science, commerce and industry are explicit fields, while knowledge and creative legal understanding can support accomplishment. Financial success through creativity and joy in pursuing goals extend that productive range. [Witte's collective work](#witte-entry) and [ABC's eight creative, knowledge and financial clauses](#abc-entry) support these concrete settings.
