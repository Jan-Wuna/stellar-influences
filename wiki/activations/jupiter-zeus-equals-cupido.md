## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Successful activities of a community. Lucky establishment of a firm. The fortunate beginning in a new firm. Artistic activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 337; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Efforts of a legal community, success of a creative community, a community's leadership successes, shared advantages through effort, happiness through a community's pursuit of goals, and accomplishments of a legal community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful community activity and a fortunate firm beginning meet shared effort and creative work.
- psychology: Happiness through collective goal pursuit is explicit.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Creative and legal communities, a firm and shared effort define the group setting.
- events/manifestations: Establishing a firm, a favorable beginning in a new firm, artistic activity and communal leadership success.
- conflicts/notes: ABC names leadership success and the community's goal pursuit; belonging itself is not stated to cause productive strength.

## Contradictions

Witte gives successful community activities, firm beginnings and art. ABC specifies collective effort, leadership and legal-community accomplishment. No direct contradiction is stated.

## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Cupido` brings successful effort into community, business and artistic activity. Establishing a firm or beginning work in a new one can be fortunate, while a creative or legal community gains through shared effort and leadership. [Witte's communal and firm examples](#witte-entry) and [ABC's six group clauses](#abc-entry) connect practical achievement with happiness in pursuing common goals.
