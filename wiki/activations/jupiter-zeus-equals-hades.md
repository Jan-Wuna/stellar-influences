## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Children without vitality. Agricultural produce. Luck in connection with fuel.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 337; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

An emerging lack of money, success through an act of renunciation, successful accomplishments of the past, joy through service, dangerous generation of money, shaping secret happiness, criminal legal services, and advantages from past efforts.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Agricultural produce and benefit through fuel coexist with service, past efforts and financial or legal difficulty.
- psychology: Joy through service and shaping secret happiness are explicit possibilities.
- body/health: Witte's children without vitality is a historical bodily claim; the entry does not establish a present medical condition, infertility or product quality.
- social/relationship: Service and criminal legal services are distinct examples, not grounds to label a person criminal.
- events/manifestations: Agriculture, fuel-related luck, emerging money shortage, dangerous money generation and benefits from past effort.
- conflicts/notes: Witte does not extend its vitality phrase to products, and ABC does not identify what is renounced or how secret happiness is shaped.

## Contradictions

The entries mix material benefit and difficult examples. Witte's fuel or agricultural clauses and ABC's service or past-effort benefits remain distinct from the vitality, financial and legal concerns; no common causal chain is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Hades` brings productive benefit into basic material or service settings. Agriculture and luck connected with fuel are explicit, while past effort and an act of renunciation may also yield advantage. Emerging money shortage and dangerous or criminal financial/legal activity remain separate possibilities. [Witte's material examples](#witte-entry) and [ABC's service, past-effort and constraint clauses](#abc-entry) support this range; Witte's historical vitality claim stays bounded to its wording above.
