## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Luck through energetic working. Happy procreation. Sexual intercourse with consequences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 336; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, Mars row.

Successful work efforts, leadership activity in finance, joy in creative work, leading legal efforts, joy in accomplishment at work, the will of financial leadership, and the will to shape law.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Luck through energetic work meets successful effort, creative work and financial or legal leadership.
- psychology: Joy in creative work and accomplishment accompanies directed will.
- body/health: Witte explicitly names happy procreation and sexual intercourse with consequences as historical source claims; no clinical or fertility outcome is established here.
- social/relationship: Financial leadership and shaping law provide practical collective settings; Witte also supplies a sexual/procreative example.
- events/manifestations: Successful work effort and leading legal efforts are stated without a fixed speed of result.
- conflicts/notes: The will of financial leadership is distinct from generic financial work; the source does not say creation necessarily becomes concrete quickly.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize successful effort. ABC specifies creative, financial and legal work, while Witte also names sexual and procreative consequences. These are distinct applications, without a stated universal process or timetable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Mars` joins energetic work with the satisfaction of accomplishing something. Creative effort, leadership in finance and the will to shape law give the activity concrete directions. [Witte](#witte-entry) also includes procreation and sexual consequences within its historical account; [ABC](#abc-entry) focuses on successful work and leadership, without specifying how quickly results appear.
