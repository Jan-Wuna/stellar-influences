## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Delight in creating. Moment of successful procreation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 336; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, MC row.

Success of a creative soul, successful guidance of the soul, joy of a fiery soul, my successful leadership, my financial efforts, my joy in creation, my legal efforts, and my successful procreation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Delight in creating and successful inner guidance meet personal leadership and effort.
- psychology: Joy of a fiery or creative soul and personal joy in creation are explicit.
- body/health: Both entries mention successful procreation as historical source doctrine rather than a clinical finding or promised fertility outcome.
- social/relationship: Personal leadership and legal efforts are stated without a public career role.
- events/manifestations: Financial efforts, legal efforts and successful creation are practical expressions.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's full eight clauses are inward and first-person; MC does not justify adding a calling or a life-path claim absent from these entries.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in creative joy and procreation. ABC adds inward guidance and first-person financial, legal and leadership efforts. No direct contradiction is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = MC` makes the delight of creating personally and inwardly felt. Successful guidance, leadership and financial or legal effort can accompany that creative joy. Procreation is another explicit historical source example. [Witte's delight](#witte-entry) and [ABC's eight first-person and inward clauses](#abc-entry) support personal satisfaction in creation without assigning a particular career or life calling.
