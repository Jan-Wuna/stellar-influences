## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

The procreative woman. Persons happy in their work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 336; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Successful efforts of a people, a woman's successful accomplishments, a woman's successful setting of goals, a woman happy through procreation, a woman's legal efforts, a woman's right to procreation, and satisfied leadership of a people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Happiness in work and successful effort coexist with historical procreative examples.
- psychology: Satisfaction in work and goal setting are explicit.
- body/health: Both entries use historical procreative language about a woman; this does not establish a clinical fertility outcome.
- social/relationship: A woman's legal efforts and procreative right coexist with a people's effort and leadership.
- events/manifestations: Successful accomplishments and goal setting, happiness in work and satisfied leadership.
- conflicts/notes: The gendered source examples do not establish fertile responsiveness or nourishment by production as a mechanism.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes procreation and people happy at work; ABC adds individual and collective accomplishment, goal setting and legal efforts. No direct contradiction is stated, and the historical gendered examples remain attributed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Moon` connects happiness in work with successful effort and goal setting. Its examples include a woman's accomplishments and legal efforts, as well as the effort and leadership of a people. Procreation is another explicit historical source expression. [Witte's concise work and procreation account](#witte-entry) and [ABC's seven clauses](#abc-entry) support these personal and collective forms of satisfaction.
