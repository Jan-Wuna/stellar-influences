## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

To work without success.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 337; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, Neptune row.

Emerging legal uncertainty, disappointed joy in procreation, successful efforts in the air, successful refinement of accomplishments, joy in creating in water, happiness through intuitive leadership, and weak legal accomplishment.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Unsuccessful work in Witte contrasts with ABC's mixed refinement, intuitive leadership and legal uncertainty.
- psychology: Disappointment coexists with creative joy and intuitive leadership.
- body/health: ABC's disappointed procreative joy is an attributed historical phrase without a specified bodily cause or clinical outcome.
- social/relationship: Weak legal accomplishment and emerging legal uncertainty provide practical collective contexts.
- events/manifestations: Successful refinement, successful efforts in the air and creation in water remain specific ABC expressions.
- conflicts/notes: The entries materially differ in their allowance of success; no source says confusion causes failure or that grounding the work prevents it.

## Contradictions

Witte gives work without success. ABC explicitly includes successful effort and refinement and happiness through intuitive leadership beside disappointment and weak legal accomplishment. Its favorable alternatives cannot be reduced to Witte's uniformly adverse statement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Neptune` has a divided source account of productive effort. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes work without success; [ABC](#abc-entry) also allows successful refinement and happiness through intuitive leadership, while retaining legal uncertainty and disappointment. Creative joy in water and successful effort in the air are further literal ABC examples whose setting is not specified. These possibilities remain side by side without an invented cause of failure or remedy.
