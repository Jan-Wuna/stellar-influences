## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Connected with work or with production. To undertake successful projects with cooperation. Making contact for pregnancy (successful procreation).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 336; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

The beginning of successful relationships, success through creative connections, successful shaping of contacts, a successful procreative connection, joy through connection with fire, joy in beginning connections, and good connections to leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Cooperation in work or production meets successful creative and new relationships.
- psychology: Joy in beginning connections is explicit.
- body/health: Witte's contact for pregnancy and ABC's procreative connection are historical source examples without a clinical fertility conclusion.
- social/relationship: Successful projects with cooperation, creative contacts and good connections to leadership define the relational setting.
- events/manifestations: Beginning successful relationships and joint production or projects.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's leadership-connection clause is restored; connection with fire is retained without a specified activity.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize successful cooperation and productive contact. Witte also specifies pregnancy, while ABC adds leadership connections and joy in new bonds. No direct contradiction is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Node` connects successful work with other people. Cooperation can support a project or production, while new creative contacts and connections to leadership extend the range of useful relationships. The sources also give procreative contact as a historical example. [Witte's cooperative projects](#witte-entry) and [ABC's seven connection clauses](#abc-entry) support productive association and joy in a beginning bond.
