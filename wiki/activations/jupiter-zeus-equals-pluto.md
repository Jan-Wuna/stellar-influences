## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Successful, increase of accomplishment. Joy and satisfaction in doing one’s work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 337; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Developing successful leadership, successful development of accomplishment, developing joy in leadership, happy development of procreation, change in the performance of money, developing legal accomplishments, and growing financial efforts.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Increasing accomplishment and successful development of leadership and work.
- psychology: Joy and satisfaction in work, and developing joy in leadership.
- body/health: ABC gives happy development of procreation as a historical source example without a clinical conclusion.
- social/relationship: Leadership, legal accomplishments and financial efforts extend the productive theme.
- events/manifestations: Development of accomplishments and increasing financial effort.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's initial stray E was a misread alternate Pluto glyph. ABC's change in the performance of money is retained broadly because the financial mechanism is unspecified.

## Contradictions

Both entries favor development and accomplishment. ABC adds leadership, legal and financial settings and a procreative example; these extend Witte's brief account rather than contradict it.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Pluto` links increasing accomplishment with enjoyment of the work itself. Successful leadership can develop alongside legal accomplishments and growing financial efforts. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes joy and satisfaction in work; [ABC](#abc-entry) extends development to leadership and finance.
