## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Fortunate mental creations. To convince by reason and wisdom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 337; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Efforts toward legal clarity, insightful financial leadership, success through reasonable leadership, successful intellectual or spiritual leadership, joy in cultural accomplishments, joy in pursuing understanding, money for cultural accomplishments, and shaping legal culture.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Fortunate mental creation and success through reasonable, insightful leadership.
- psychology: Reason, wisdom and joy in pursuing understanding.
- body/health: The entries concern thought, leadership and culture rather than a specified bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Financial leadership, cultural accomplishments and shaping legal culture provide collective settings.
- events/manifestations: Convincing by reason and wisdom, supporting cultural accomplishments with money, and seeking legal clarity.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's geistige leadership is kept open to intellectual or spiritual meaning; it does not mean mental control.

## Contradictions

The entries agree on productive thought and reason. ABC extends this to leadership, legal clarity, finance and culture. No direct contradiction is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Poseidon` joins fortunate mental creation with reasoned persuasion and thoughtful leadership. Joy can come through pursuing understanding or cultural accomplishments, while practical expressions include financial leadership and support for culture. [Witte's reason and wisdom](#witte-entry) and [ABC's complete eight clauses](#abc-entry) give the shared emphasis on understanding directed toward productive action.
