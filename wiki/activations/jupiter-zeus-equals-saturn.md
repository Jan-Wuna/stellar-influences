## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Interruption and hindrance of happy workings. Separation from the achieved status or a created product.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 337; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Beginning money problems, success through patient pursuit of a goal, leadership tasks in the legal system, the financial burden of leadership, joy through serious striving, patient financial leadership, financial leadership involving losses, and benefits through creativity in old age.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Interrupted accomplishment and financial burdens coexist with success through patient effort.
- psychology: Joy through serious striving and patient pursuit of a goal.
- body/health: ABC specifies benefits from creativity in old age, without defining a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Leadership tasks in the legal system and the financial burden of leadership.
- events/manifestations: Interruption of happy work, separation from achieved status or a created product, and financial losses.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's adverse account and ABC's patient success remain distinct; the entries do not offer stabilization as a remedy.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes hindrance and separation from what has been achieved. ABC includes money problems and losses, but also successful patient effort, serious joy and the benefits of creativity in old age. Its favorable alternatives must remain visible.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Saturn` can describe interruption of satisfying work or separation from an achievement. Financial problems and the burden of leadership belong to the same account, yet patient pursuit of a goal can also succeed, and serious effort can bring joy. [Witte's hindrance and separation](#witte-entry) remain beside [ABC's mixed eight-clause account](#abc-entry), including benefits through creativity in old age. Neither entry establishes a remedy that prevents interruption.
