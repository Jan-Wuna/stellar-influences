## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Days of cheerful work. Successful physical work. The man who is happy through his successful work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 336; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

A man's striving for success, a successful center of accomplishment, successful personal accomplishment, a leading financial center, a leading legal center, a person's joy in creating, good daily accomplishment, and personal success through effort.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Cheerful work, successful personal accomplishment and striving for success.
- psychology: Happiness through successful work and joy in creating.
- body/health: Witte explicitly includes successful physical work.
- social/relationship: ABC extends accomplishment to leading financial and legal centers.
- events/manifestations: Days of cheerful work and good daily accomplishment through effort.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's man and ABC's man's striving are source wording; ABC also speaks of a person. No broader claim about vitality or public visibility is supplied.

## Contradictions

Both entries connect effort with accomplishment and happiness. ABC adds financial and legal centers, while Witte specifies physical work. These are different settings without a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Sun` emphasizes happiness in successful work and the joy of creating. Personal effort and striving for success can lead to good daily accomplishment; physical work is explicit in [Witte](#witte-entry), while leading financial and legal centers appear in [ABC](#abc-entry). The shared account concerns cheerful productive activity and personal achievement.
