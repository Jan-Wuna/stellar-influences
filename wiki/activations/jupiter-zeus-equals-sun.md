## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Days of cheerful work. Successful physical work. The man who is happy through his successful work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

The success of the man, the successful performance centre, the successful performance of the person, the leading legal centre, the creative pleasure of a man, the good day, and the personal success through effort

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Zeus = Sun`: cheerful work, successful physical effort, and a person whose sense of life is strengthened by productive accomplishment.
- Chart expression: visible competence, bodily engagement in work, and happiness that comes from doing something successfully.
