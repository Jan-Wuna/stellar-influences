## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

General prosperous working and accomplishing. Products, creations in general.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 336; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, Vernal Point row.

Successful pursuit of goals in public, financial efforts for the public, good accomplishments in public life, a public fire of joy, good accomplishments for the public, and shaping law in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Prosperous work and accomplishment in general, with public goals and service.
- psychology: ABC's public fire of joy adds a collective expression of pleasure.
- body/health: The former good-health phrase is absent from the actual row; no specific bodily condition is given.
- social/relationship: Financial efforts and accomplishments for the public, and shaping law in public life.
- events/manifestations: Public pursuit of goals, products and creations in general.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's general scope and ABC's public-life examples are retained separately; no fame or recognition mechanism is stated.

## Contradictions

Witte speaks generally of prosperous work and creations. ABC specifies public goals, financial effort and law. These scopes complement one another without a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Vernal Point` describes prosperous work and accomplishment with a public or general scope. Products and creations belong to [Witte's concise entry](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry) adds pursuit of public goals, financial efforts for the public and shaping law in public life. Public joy accompanies these examples without requiring personal fame or recognition.
