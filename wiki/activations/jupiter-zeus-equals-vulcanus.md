## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 213, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Optimistic and positive accomplishment. To see the accomplishments of one’s work. Increase of fortune through successful work

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 337; one-based PDF p. 167, Jupiter + Zeus, oriented activator row.

Influential legal leadership, successful power of leadership, successful shaping of power, joy in energetic accomplishments, leading financial power, good energetic accomplishment, powerful financial effort, powerful procreative happiness, and advantages of power through accomplishment.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Optimistic accomplishment, successful leadership power and benefits from effective work.
- psychology: Optimism and joy in energetic accomplishments.
- body/health: ABC's procreative happiness is an attributed historical expression; energetic accomplishment does not specify a health outcome.
- social/relationship: Influential legal leadership and leading financial power provide institutional settings.
- events/manifestations: Seeing the accomplishments of one's work and increased fortune through successful work.
- conflicts/notes: The full nine-clause ABC row restores its two opening leadership clauses; the sources do not add stamina or a specific execution method.

## Contradictions

Both entries connect accomplishment with benefit. ABC adds legal, financial and leadership power to Witte's optimistic account. No direct contradiction is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Jupiter/Zeus = Vulcanus` emphasizes effective accomplishment and the satisfaction of seeing work succeed. Leadership and financial power provide further settings for the benefits of productive effort. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives optimism and increased fortune through successful work; [ABC](#abc-entry) extends that account to influential legal leadership, energetic accomplishment and power gained through accomplishment.
