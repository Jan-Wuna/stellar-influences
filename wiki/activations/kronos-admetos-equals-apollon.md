## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 300, Kronos + Admetos, oriented activator row.

Many must lead an odd life, under unusual circumstances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 463; one-based PDF p. 230, Kronos + Admetos, oriented activator row.

Special success through perseverance, consolidation of important knowledge, consolidated authority of knowledge, an industrial strike against the state, thorough superiority through knowledge, state trade restrictions, and increasing resistance to the state.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Unusual circumstances for many people coexist with perseverance, knowledge and state restrictions.
- psychology: Perseverance is explicit, while Witte describes an unusual way of life.
- body/health: Neither oriented entry specifies a bodily condition; odd life is a description of circumstances.
- social/relationship: Authority of knowledge, industrial strike, trade restrictions and resistance to the state.
- events/manifestations: Many living unusually, special success through perseverance and consolidation of knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's collective unusual circumstances and ABC's knowledge/state account remain separate; authority is not authorship.

## Contradictions

Witte concerns many people living under unusual circumstances. ABC concerns knowledge, perseverance and state restrictions, including both success and resistance. The overlap is limited, and neither account establishes elite networks or a ranked collective status.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Kronos/Admetos = Apollon` has two distinct emphases: unusual circumstances shared by many, and the consolidation of knowledge or authority through perseverance. State trade restrictions, industrial strike and increasing resistance supply concrete collective expressions in [ABC](#abc-entry). [Witte](#witte-entry) gives the broader example of many people leading an unusual life. These meanings remain side by side.
