## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 300, Kronos + Admetos, oriented activator row.

To be noted through one’s odd character. To seclude oneself from others or to be secluded. Acquaintance with odd persons. Also to lead a secluded odd life together with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 462; one-based PDF p. 230, Kronos + Admetos, Asc row.

A partner's special perseverance, blocked independence in the surroundings, the end of independence at a place, a partner's consolidated authority, a partner's great depressions, the other's thorough superiority, state restrictions on the surroundings, and superior resistance by others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Seclusion or unusual company meets restricted independence and consolidated authority in the surroundings.
- psychology: Being noticed for an unusual character and a partner's perseverance or depression are explicit historical expressions.
- body/health: ABC's great depressions of a partner are source language, without a clinical diagnosis or specified cause.
- social/relationship: Seclusion with others, acquaintances described as odd, partner authority and others' resistance.
- events/manifestations: Withdrawing or being secluded, the end of independence at a place and state restrictions on the surroundings.
- conflicts/notes: Selbständigkeit means independence; Ausdauer means perseverance. The sources do not require an exceptional character to cause state restrictions.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes seclusion and unusual acquaintances. ABC focuses on partner perseverance, authority, restricted independence and resistance. These settings may overlap, but no causal chain between personal peculiarity and institutional restrictions is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Kronos/Admetos = Asc` concerns relationships and surroundings marked by separation, unusual company or limits on independence. A partner's perseverance and consolidated authority can coexist with resistance from others or state restrictions. [Witte's seclusion and acquaintances](#witte-entry) and [ABC's partner and local conditions](#abc-entry) supply the distinct personal and institutional settings.
