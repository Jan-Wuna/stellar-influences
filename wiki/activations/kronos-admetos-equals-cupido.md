## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 300, Kronos + Admetos, oriented activator row.

To hold an exclusive position in art, in society, or in the family. Activities in an unusual art, an unusual artist.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 463; one-based PDF p. 230, Kronos + Admetos, oriented activator row.

Special perseverance of a community, blocked independence of a community, consolidated authority of a community, joint direction of circulation, joint resistance to the boss, and a community blocked by the state.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A special or exclusive group position meets community perseverance and restricted independence.
- psychology: Perseverance and resistance within a community are explicit.
- body/health: ABC's joint direction of circulation is not assigned a bodily or medical setting.
- social/relationship: An exclusive position in art, society or family; community authority and resistance to a boss.
- events/manifestations: Unusual artistic activity and a community blocked by the state.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's exclusive position does not necessarily imply wealth, elite membership or a closed social circle. ABC leaves Kreislauf-Lenkung context unspecified.

## Contradictions

Witte describes an exclusive social, familial or artistic position. ABC gives perseverance, authority and blocked independence within a community. These are distinct emphases rather than a uniformly privileged group setting.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Kronos/Admetos = Cupido` places special standing or unusual artistic activity within a group, family or society. Community perseverance and consolidated authority form another expression, alongside blocked independence and joint resistance. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies the exclusive position and unusual art; [ABC](#abc-entry) supplies the community's institutional conditions, including blockage by the state.
