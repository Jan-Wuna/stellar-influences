## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 300, Kronos + Admetos, oriented activator row.

To be or to feel happy in a secluded or special position.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 462; one-based PDF p. 230, Kronos + Admetos, oriented activator row.

Success through special perseverance, restricted financial independence, consolidated financial authority, consolidated rule of law, great success in a strike, legal restrictions on the state, a state financial blockade, and deep study of state law.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Happiness in a secluded or special position and success through special perseverance.
- psychology: Feeling happy in seclusion is explicit.
- body/health: The entries concern circumstances and perseverance without a specified bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Financial independence and authority, rule of law, strike success and state restrictions.
- events/manifestations: Success through perseverance, financial blockade and deep study of state law.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes restrictions alongside success; authority is not authorship. Witte's happy seclusion does not establish privilege or dignity.

## Contradictions

Witte gives happiness in a secluded or special position. ABC includes success and consolidated authority but also restricted financial independence and a state financial blockade. The favorable Witte statement must not erase these restrictions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Kronos/Admetos = Jupiter` allows happiness in a secluded or special position and success through unusual perseverance. Its institutional examples include consolidated financial authority and rule of law, alongside restricted financial independence and blockade. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies the personal happiness in seclusion; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives the mixed legal and financial conditions.
