## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 300, Kronos + Admetos, oriented activator row.

To receive an unusual order. Decrease of work and duties to carry out a special commission.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 462; one-based PDF p. 230, Kronos + Admetos, oriented activator row.

Work with important primordial energy, especially enduring active effort, blocked state efforts, restriction of an important activity, the end of independent work, major blockade activity, major resistance activity, and deep involvement in special work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A special commission and sustained effort coexist with restrictions on independent work.
- psychology: Enduring active effort and deep involvement in special work are explicit.
- body/health: ABC's primordial energy is source terminology without a specified bodily mechanism.
- social/relationship: An unusual order, state efforts, blockade and resistance activities.
- events/manifestations: Reduced work and duties to carry out a special commission, or an end to independent work.
- conflicts/notes: ABC says enduring effort rather than exhausting power and state efforts rather than relief. Reduced ordinary duties are not equivalent to loss of all work.

## Contradictions

Witte gives reduced ordinary work for a special commission. ABC includes enduring effort and immersion in special work but also blocked state efforts and an end to independent work. The restrictive and purposeful alternatives remain distinct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Kronos/Admetos = Mars` focuses activity on unusual or special work. A commission can reduce ordinary duties, while enduring effort and deep involvement characterize another expression. [Witte's special order](#witte-entry) remains beside [ABC's restrictions, blockade and resistance activities](#abc-entry), which give a less favorable institutional setting.
