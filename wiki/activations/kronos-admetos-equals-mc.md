## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 300, Kronos + Admetos, oriented activator row.

To be noted for a certain peculiarity. To be different. To be an odd character and noted even among many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 462; one-based PDF p. 230, Kronos + Admetos, oriented activator row.

Blocked self-confidence of the soul, the end of my independence, consolidation of my independence, my special perseverance, my blocked independence, my consolidated authority, my great depressions, and my thorough superiority.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal difference and special perseverance meet both consolidated and blocked independence.
- psychology: Blocked self-confidence, a sense of personal difference and the source's great depressions.
- body/health: ABC's depression language is historical source wording without a clinical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Being noticed even among many and one's own consolidated authority.
- events/manifestations: The end or consolidation of personal independence; being noted for peculiarity.
- conflicts/notes: ABC speaks in the first person about the soul and independence. MC must not be reduced to career or public office, and independence is not self-abandonment.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes being different and noticed for peculiarity. ABC adds an inward first-person account with both consolidated and blocked independence and authority. These alternatives remain visible without turning the formula into a career-only description.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Kronos/Admetos = MC` connects personal difference with a strong focus on one's own independence and authority. Being noticed for peculiarity is explicit in [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry) also gives special perseverance, blocked self-confidence and both the consolidation and ending of independence. The source's inward first-person language keeps this account broader than vocation or public status.
