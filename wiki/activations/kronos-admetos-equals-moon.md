#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special perseverance of a woman, blocked independence of a people, end of a woman's selfishness, firm authority of a woman, great depression of a people, large scale depression, grand superiority of a woman, and superior amelioration resistance

## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Admetos = Moon`: misunderstood or peculiar women and people whose special public position makes their behavior seem different from the norm.
