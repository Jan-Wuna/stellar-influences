## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 300, Kronos + Admetos, Node row.

Connected with persons who lead an odd life. To be connected with odd characters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 462; one-based PDF p. 230, Kronos + Admetos, Node row.

Special perseverance in a connection, blocked independence of a connection, end of an independent connection, great depression in a connection, independence in firm connections, and connections restricted by the state.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Association with people living unusually, with endurance or restriction within the bond.
- **Psychology:** Perseverance and discouragement within a connection are explicit ABC themes.
- **Body/health:** ABC's great depression in a connection is historical source language without a clinical diagnosis of either person.
- **Social/relationship:** Witte names connections with unusual people; ABC addresses independence and state constraints on connections.
- **Events/manifestations:** Maintaining a bond, ending an independent connection, or a connection subject to state limits.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Independence within a firm bond and blocked independence are alternative conditions in ABC's list.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 300 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 462.

## Contradictions

The entries concern unusual associates and the conditions of a connection; they do not establish seclusion or a particular kind of organization. ABC includes both independence in firm connections and restrictions on it, without specifying when each applies.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Admetos = Node`, Node places the pair's exceptional or restricted position within a connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes association with people who lead an unusual life, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds perseverance, constrained independence, and state-bound connections. The relationship is the focus; a claim about withdrawal or niche membership would go beyond these entries.
