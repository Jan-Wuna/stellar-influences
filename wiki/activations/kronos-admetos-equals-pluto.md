## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 300, Kronos + Admetos, Pluto row.

The beginning and the development of an exclusive position. The development is connected either with change of authorities or other transformations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 463; one-based PDF p. 230, Kronos + Admetos, Pluto row.

Developing special perseverance, unfolding an established authority, thorough changes in the state, deep involvement in an important change, growing resistance to the authorities, and transformations through state restriction.

The Pluto paragraph begins "Besondere Ausdauer entwickeln" on the same spread; the previous extraction misplaced its opening under Neptune and split "Veränderungen" across the boundary.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** The emergence and transformation of an exclusive position.
- **Psychology:** ABC names deep involvement in an important change and growing resistance to authority.
- **Body/health:** No body or health meaning is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Changing authorities and resistance to them shape the position.
- **Events/manifestations:** An exclusive position or established authority develops amid transformations or state restrictions.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Development of authority and resistance to it are both represented; their sequence is unspecified.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 300 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 463.

## Contradictions

Witte's development of a special position and ABC's development of authority or resistance to it are compatible possibilities, not a guaranteed sequence.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Admetos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair as the beginning and development of an exclusive position through changes in authority or other transformations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supports that process; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds developing perseverance and authority, involvement in important change, and growing resistance under state restriction. Authority can develop or be challenged within this range; the entries do not prescribe one outcome.
