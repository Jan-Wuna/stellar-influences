## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 300, Kronos + Admetos, Saturn row.

To be in a difficult position because of separation or seclusion. To be unjustly treated, suppressed or set back for special reasons. Embittered by being unjustly treated.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 463; one-based PDF p. 230, Kronos + Admetos, Saturn row.

Special concentration and perseverance, restrictions and losses through the state, blocked independence in old age, restrictions and losses through the state, serious established authority, great patience and thoroughness, and great disturbances through depression.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** A difficult secluded or restricted position, with endurance under limitation.
- **Psychology:** Embitterment after unjust treatment; ABC adds concentration, perseverance, and patience.
- **Body/health:** ABC mentions depression historically; neither entry establishes a diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Suppression, exclusion, or state-imposed losses affect the person's position.
- **Events/manifestations:** Separation, setbacks for special reasons, and limitations or losses caused by the state.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC repeats state restrictions and losses and adds an old-age example; these remain its particular attributions.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 300 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 463.

## Contradictions

Endurance does not cancel the burdens Witte describes. ABC's concentration, patience, and established authority coexist with restrictions and losses; no sequence or guaranteed compensation is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Admetos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair as the burden of a separated or restricted position. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ties the difficulty to unjust treatment, suppression, and resulting bitterness; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds perseverance, concentration, patience, and losses through the state. The common thread is endurance under constraint, without treating historical depression language as a clinical conclusion.
