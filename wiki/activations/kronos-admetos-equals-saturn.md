## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be in a difficult position because of separation or seclusion. To be unjustly treated, suppressed or set back for special reasons. Embittered by being unjustly treated.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special concentration and perseverance, limitations and losses by the state, bloked self sandiness in old age, serious strengthened authority, great patience and clarity, and great disturbances by depression

## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Admetos = Saturn`: a difficult position caused by separation, unjust treatment, embitterment, and being pushed back or held down for special reasons.
- Chart expression: constriction, exclusion, and painful delay, with seclusion turning into burden rather than chosen difference.
