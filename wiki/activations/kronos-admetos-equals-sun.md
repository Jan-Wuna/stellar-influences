## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The man who has to lead the life as an odd being, is all alone, who is different through his position, who is an exception, and who receives special treatment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special perseverance of a person, blocked a man's self governance, end of personal self governance, strengthened personal authority, great depression of a man, great superiority of a man, and personal limitations by the state

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Admetos = Sun`: a person who lives as an exception, alone, different through position, and subject to special treatment because of singular status.
- Chart expression: strongly marked individuality, but also the loneliness and burden of being set apart.
