## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 300, Kronos + Admetos, Venus row.

To be contented with a secluded life or a secluded position.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 462; one-based PDF p. 230, Kronos + Admetos, Venus row.

Special preference for thoroughness, restricted goodwill from the state, end of a great love, joy of an established authority, established artistic authority, consolidation of peace by the state, and suppression of a great affection.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Contentment in a secluded position, alongside pleasure or affection subject to restriction.
- **Psychology:** Satisfaction in seclusion is explicit; ABC also names joy and the end of a great love.
- **Body/health:** No physical or health meaning is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Restricted goodwill, a love ending, suppressed affection, and peace consolidation are ABC's social examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** A secluded life experienced contentedly; artistic authority and state peace appear separately in ABC.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's suppression of affection must remain distinct from Witte's contentment in seclusion.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 300 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 462.

## Contradictions

Witte's contentment and ABC's restriction of affection are different possibilities. The entries do not make seclusion either universally satisfying or necessarily a loss of love.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Admetos = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through satisfaction with a secluded life or position, the complete emphasis of the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places enjoyment of established or artistic authority beside restricted goodwill, the end of a great love, and suppressed affection. The range includes both contentment within limits and limits on affection; the sources do not collapse these into a promise of harmony.
