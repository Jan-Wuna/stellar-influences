## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 300, Kronos + Admetos, Vulcanus row.

To create a sensation as an odd character. To call attention to oneself. In this position one must count on grave obstacles.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 463; one-based PDF p. 230, Kronos + Admetos, Vulcanus row.

Restriction of state power, consolidation of state power, an established power hierarchy, state restriction of power, great influence of depression, thorough superiority of power, important primordial-material energy, and suppression of violence by the state.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** A forceful exceptional position that draws attention and encounters serious obstacles.
- **Psychology:** Calling attention to oneself is explicit; motives or temperament are not specified.
- **Body/health:** ABC's great influence of depression is historical source wording without an individual clinical diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** ABC describes both the consolidation and limitation of state power.
- **Events/manifestations:** A sensation caused by an unusual person; obstacles, power hierarchy, and raw-material energy.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Consolidation and restriction are alternative power conditions, not a prediction of inevitable conflict.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 300 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 463.

## Contradictions

Witte's personal sensation and ABC's state-power examples operate at different scales. Neither establishes that resistance is caused by the person's character.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Admetos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair as a conspicuous exceptional position accompanied by grave obstacles, as the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives the institutional counterpart in consolidated or restricted state power and power hierarchy. The shared concern is the strength and limits of a special position; the entries do not explain the cause of every obstacle.
