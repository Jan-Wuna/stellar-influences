## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

The specialist. Authority in a special field. Authority for a few in a small circle. Dealing with special products.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 461; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Special successes through thoroughness, the end of great abundance, thorough successes of the hierarchy, successful resistance to the state, independent deepening of knowledge, state restrictions on freedom, and increasing restriction of independence.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Specialist authority concentrated in a field, small circle, or special product.
- **Psychology:** The entries establish expertise and thoroughness, not a broader personality profile.
- **Body/health:** No body or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Authority for a few and resistance to state restrictions appear at different scales.
- **Events/manifestations:** Specialist work, dealing in special products, or restrictions on freedom.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC gives thorough successes of the hierarchy and independent deepening of knowledge; those readable clauses do not concern Greek affairs or a defensive discipline.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 461.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes specialist authority within a small circle. ABC includes successful thoroughness and independent study but also the end of abundance and restrictions on freedom. These are distinct favorable and restrictive alternatives.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair as concentrated specialist authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) confines its reach to a particular field, a few people, or special products. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds success through thoroughness and independent deepening of knowledge beside the end of abundance and restrictions on freedom. Specialist competence and institutional restriction remain distinct possibilities.
