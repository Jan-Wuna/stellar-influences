## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

To be superior to others in ability, knowledge, or in experience. To be higher rated than others. To be in the company of great scientific authorities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 460; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, Asc row.

The reputation of science at a place, a partner's special successes, a partner's special freedom, a successful local hierarchy, a partner's great experiences, great expansion of a place, state successes at the place, and important trade at the place.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Recognition for superior ability, knowledge, or experience in relation to others.
- **Psychology:** Competence is recognized; the passages do not establish pride or a stable personality trait.
- **Body/health:** No health or bodily condition is specified.
- **Social/relationship:** Company with scientific authorities and the partner's success or experience are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Higher rating, local scientific reputation, and important local trade.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The successful local hierarchy and expansion of a place are readable source clauses; Asc retains its partner and surroundings focus.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 460.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize recognition through others and place. ABC extends Witte's scientific company and higher rating to partner freedom, local hierarchy and expansion of a place. No direct contradiction is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Asc`, Asc specifies the pair through one's standing among others and contact with recognized expertise. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes being highly rated for ability, knowledge, or experience and associating with scientific authorities. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds the partner's successes and freedom, local scientific reputation and expansion of a place, keeping the focus on relationships and surroundings.
