## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

The great artist. Great expansion of a community or society. Great peace movement. Reconciliation. Prosperity of all peaceful matters. Dealing with art products. Important art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 461; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Expansion of independence in the community, special knowledge of a community, success of a state community, successful synthesis of the hierarchy, great successes of a community, and great shared experiences.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Recognized art and expansion of a community or peaceful undertaking.
- **Psychology:** Shared experience is represented; no individual temperament is specified.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry offers a body or health delineation.
- **Social/relationship:** Community growth, reconciliation, and a peace movement are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Important art, art products, and prosperity in peaceful collective matters.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Collective success and art are supported examples, not evidence of inevitable cultural leadership.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 461.

## Contradictions

The entries place success in community and art; they do not establish that every expanding community is peaceful or artistic.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through collective accomplishment and art. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins the great artist and art products with expanding societies, reconciliation, and peaceful affairs; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds shared knowledge, experience, and community success. Their overlap is accomplishment carried by a community or cultural work, without assuming that its leader or organization must have a particular character.
