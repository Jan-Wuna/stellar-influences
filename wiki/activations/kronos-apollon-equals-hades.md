## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Spreading of lack and poverty. Great meanness and villainess. Celebrities in the field of sciences of antiquity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 461; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Expansion of state traditions, great experiences from the past, criminal commercial successes, lack of independent experience, deficient state successes, renunciation of special freedoms, and increase of state criminality.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Expansion involving deprivation or wrongdoing, alongside expertise concerning antiquity.
- **Psychology:** Witte names meanness and villainy; no general personality diagnosis follows.
- **Body/health:** Poverty is a social condition here; no specific illness is established.
- **Social/relationship:** Spreading lack, reduced freedoms, poor state success, and state criminality are listed.
- **Events/manifestations:** Celebrities in sciences of antiquity; experience from the past and expansion of state traditions.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC explicitly says criminal commercial successes and lack of independent experience; these clauses are distinct from its past-experience examples.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 461.

## Contradictions

Historical scholarship and the spread of poverty or wrongdoing are distinct branches without a causal connection. ABC's criminal commercial success is consistent with its state-criminality clause; experience from the past remains a separate example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through two distinct ranges preserved in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry): spreading deprivation or wrongdoing, and prominence in sciences of antiquity. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds traditions and past experience beside criminal commercial successes, lack of independent experience and deficient state results. The link to the past does not imply moral degradation.
