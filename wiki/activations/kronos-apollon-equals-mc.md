## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Great knowledge and ability or to have or to gain great experience. To be an authority for many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 460; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

The reputation of my science, experiences of a great soul, my special success, my independent knowledge, my successes in the state, my freedom as boss, my great experiences, my increasing independence, and my economic independence.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** One's knowledge, ability, and experience expressed as authority for many.
- **Psychology:** ABC's first-person account emphasizes personal success, independent knowledge and increasing independence.
- **Body/health:** No health condition is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Witte explicitly places the person as an authority for many; ABC includes freedom as a boss.
- **Events/manifestations:** Gaining great experience, personal success, and success within the state.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC distinguishes increasing independence from economic independence, while its opening concerns the reputation of one's science rather than awareness.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 460.

## Contradictions

Both entries concern one's own knowledge, experience and standing. ABC adds first-person success, freedom as boss and economic independence. No direct contradiction or predetermined life direction is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = MC`, MC specifies the pair through one's own knowledge, ability and accumulated experience. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes these the basis of being an authority for many, and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds the reputation of one's science, personal success, freedom as a boss, and increasing or economic independence. The emphasis remains personal competence, experience and standing.
