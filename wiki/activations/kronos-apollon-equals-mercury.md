## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

The thinker. The thinking is concentrated on scientific matters. Spreading of great or scientific thoughts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 460; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Reports on the reputation of science, a special abundance of thoughts, special freedom of youth, a successful media hierarchy, great experience with language, news of state successes, increased independence in thinking, and important successes of youth.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Scientific thinking and the communication or spread of important thoughts.
- **Psychology:** Concentration on scientific matters is Witte's explicit psychological emphasis.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry supplies a body or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** ABC includes media hierarchy, reports on science, and success of youth.
- **Events/manifestations:** Spreading scientific thoughts, language experience, and reporting state achievements.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC adds abundance of thought and independence in thinking; the latter does not mean self-satisfaction.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 460.

## Contradictions

Scientific thought and its dissemination overlap. ABC adds abundant thought, independence in thinking and freedom or success of youth. These extend the account without a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through thinking about scientific matters and distributing those thoughts. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins the thinker with the spread of great or scientific ideas, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds abundant and independent thought, reports on science, language experience and media. Youthful freedom and success form further explicit examples.
