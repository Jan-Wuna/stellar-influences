## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Female scientist. To be a scientific authority for others. Representing science. Judges and lawyers. Science about population. Science of the layman.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 460; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Expansion of the national economy, special success of a woman, experiences of a people with the state, successful women in the state, great expansion of a people, great experiences of a woman, state freedom for a people, and increasing independence of a woman.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Scientific or legal authority represented through women and collective life in the sources.
- **Psychology:** The entries establish experience and success, not collective trust or emotional identification.
- **Body/health:** Population science is a field of study here, not a medical diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Witte lists female scientists, judges, lawyers, and scientific authority for others.
- **Events/manifestations:** Representing science, population study, women's success, and a people's experience with the state.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Historical gender categories remain attributed. ABC's Volks-Wirtschaft means the national economy, and its final clause concerns increasing independence.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 460.

## Contradictions

Witte's scientific and legal representatives and ABC's economic and collective examples concern different settings. Their historical female categories do not establish a universal gender rule or guarantee that expertise earns public trust.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through the sources' female and collective representatives of knowledge. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) lists female scientists, judges and lawyers, authority for others, and population study; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds women's success and a people's experience with the state. These historical categories support represented expertise and collective experience, without inventing emotional identification or public trust.
