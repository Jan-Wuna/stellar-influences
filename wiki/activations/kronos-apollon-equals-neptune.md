## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The searcher. Science proceeds into unknown fields. Expansion and spreading which cannot accurately be defined, and is overlooked. Uncertainty in economic life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special knowledge of the future, success through great finesse, success in the air, great experience with water, large quantities of water, weak state successes, important experiences through metaphysics, and proliferation of large quantities of water

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Apollon = Neptune`: the searcher, science moving into unknown fields, poorly defined expansion, and uncertainty in economic life.
- Chart expression: pioneering inquiry and subtle discovery, but it also warns that large systems of knowledge or commerce can spread beyond what is clearly grasped or controlled.
