## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

The searcher. Science proceeds into unknown fields. Expansion and spreading which cannot accurately be defined, and is overlooked. Uncertainty in economic life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 461; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, Neptune row.

Special knowledge of the future, success through great sensitivity, successful independence in the air, great experiences with water, large quantities of water, weak state successes, important experiences through metaphysics, and the spread of great uncertainty.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Inquiry into unknown fields and expansion whose extent cannot be clearly defined.
- **Psychology:** Searching is explicit; ABC also names success through great sensitivity.
- **Body/health:** Water and air are contexts in ABC; no bodily diagnosis is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Witte locates uncertainty in economic life; ABC mentions weak state successes.
- **Events/manifestations:** Scientific exploration, overlooked expansion, water experience, and metaphysical experience.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's final clause concerns the spread of great uncertainty, while its water examples and successful independence in the air remain separate.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 461.

## Contradictions

Witte's unknown scientific field and economic uncertainty overlap with ABC's spread of uncertainty. ABC also allows success through sensitivity and independence in the air, alongside weak state successes. These favorable and uncertain alternatives remain visible.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through uncertain extent and knowledge still being sought. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes science entering unknown fields, expansion that escapes definition and uncertainty in economic life. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds success through sensitivity, water and metaphysical experiences, weak state successes and the spread of great uncertainty. Favorable inquiry and uncertain conditions remain distinct possibilities.
