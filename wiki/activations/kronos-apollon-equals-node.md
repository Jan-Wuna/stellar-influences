## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

To have or to make connections with scientists. Extremely far expanded limits.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 460; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Expansion of successful connections, special success of a connection, dominant relations with science, successful state connections, a successful connection to the hierarchy, great experiences through contacts, and increased freedom in connections.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Connections with scientists and the expansion of successful associations.
- **Psychology:** Experience gained through contacts; no individual temperament is specified.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health meaning is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Scientific, state, and hierarchical connections are explicit ABC examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Making scientific contacts or expanding the reach of connections.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's final clause gives increased freedom in connections; expanded limits do not establish a particular network structure.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 460.

## Contradictions

Both entries concern the reach of connections. ABC extends scientific contacts to state and hierarchical connections, with experience and increased freedom. No direct contradiction is stated.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Node`, Node specifies the pair through connections and their reach. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly names contacts with scientists and greatly expanded limits; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful associations and experience through contacts. The interpretation is widened access through relationships, with no need to invent a formal expert network.
