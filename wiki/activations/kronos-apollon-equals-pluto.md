## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

To develop a new science from a small beginning. Great and large changes and transformations. Increasing and expanding.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 461; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, Pluto row.

Particularly successful development, successful unfolding of the hierarchy, development of state successes, developing independent knowledge, change in the reputation of science, growing dominance of the economy, and increasing unfolding of independence.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Developing new science from small beginnings and expanding transformations.
- **Psychology:** The focus is development of knowledge, not a defined personality.
- **Body/health:** No physical or health condition is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** ABC adds development within hierarchy and growing economic dominance.
- **Events/manifestations:** Large changes, scientific development, increasing expansion, and growing independence.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC explicitly gives development of state successes and change in the reputation of science; reform and breakthrough are not specified mechanisms.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 461.

## Contradictions

Witte's new science and ABC's economic and hierarchical examples concern development at different scales, not a single required sequence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair as development and expansion. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) traces a new science from a small beginning and names large transformations; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds developing knowledge, hierarchy, economic dominance, and independence. Growth and change unite the examples, without proving that every change is a breakthrough or reform.
