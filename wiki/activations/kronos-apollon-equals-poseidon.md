## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Recognized authority among people who are scientifically and ideologically of the same opinion. Great scientific perceptions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 461; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

The reputation of intellectual or spiritual studies, special spiritual successes, success through intellectual or spiritual independence, state cultural successes, increased intellectual or spiritual independence, important experiences with truth, and increasing insight of the state.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Recognized authority within a shared scientific or ideological outlook.
- **Psychology:** Scientific perception and independent thought are represented, not infallible clarity.
- **Body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Authority among like-minded people; ABC adds state cultural achievement.
- **Events/manifestations:** Scientific perceptions, spiritual success, and experience with truth.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Recognition within agreement does not establish universal authority or truth.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 461.

## Contradictions

Shared opinion and recognition are linked in Witte; they do not verify the correctness of the opinion. ABC's spiritual and cultural examples broaden the context.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through understanding recognized within a shared outlook. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places scientific authority among people of the same scientific or ideological opinion; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds spiritual independence, cultural achievement, and truth-related experience. This is recognition within an intellectual setting, without equating acceptance with proof.
