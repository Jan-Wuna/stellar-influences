## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Master of method. Stoppage in science or commerce. A general and extended great hindrance of long duration, especially for the economic life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 461; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Special tasks of science, special successes through concentration, dominance through maturity and experience, success through independence in old age, great trade losses, great concentration on knowledge, and important problems of science.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Mastery of method alongside long-lasting hindrance in science or commerce.
- **Psychology:** Concentration, maturity, and experience are ABC's explicit resources.
- **Body/health:** Old age is an ABC context, not a diagnosis or a universal decline claim.
- **Social/relationship:** Extended economic hindrance and trade losses affect collective activity.
- **Events/manifestations:** Stoppage in science or commerce; difficult scientific tasks and problems.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Methodological mastery and obstruction are separate possibilities, not a necessary causal relation.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 461.

## Contradictions

The entries include both capability and prolonged obstruction; neither cancels the other or proves that rigor causes stagnation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through method and limits. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) preserves mastery of method beside a broad, long-lasting stoppage in scientific or economic activity. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds concentration, maturity, difficult scientific problems, and trade losses, supporting a reading that keeps competence and obstruction in view without turning one into the cause of the other.
