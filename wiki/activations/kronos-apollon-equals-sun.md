## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

The man who is an authority through his knowledge, through his experience or through his ability. Days devoted to science, days which bring important understanding, discovery or experiences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 460; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, Sun row.

Dominance of personal freedom, success of an independent man, a successful day for the state, great bodily fullness, personal prestige through knowledge, personal commercial experiences, important experiences of a man, and a center for political science.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** A person's authority through knowledge, ability, or experience.
- **Psychology:** Understanding and experience are stated; dignity or stable character traits are not.
- **Body/health:** ABC gives great bodily fullness as historical source wording, without specifying a medical condition.
- **Social/relationship:** Personal prestige through knowledge and commercial experience are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Scientific activity, discovery, important understanding, and state-success days are source examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The male categories are historical; references to days do not authorize a timing model.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 460.

## Contradictions

ABC adds personal freedom, independent success and bodily fullness to Witte's scientific authority. Its male categories remain attributed, and the day references remain examples rather than a method for selecting dates.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through a person's authority based on knowledge, experience or ability, as the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states. Its scientific and discovery examples align with the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) on prestige through knowledge and personal experience. ABC also emphasizes personal freedom and independent success. The male language and bodily-fullness phrase remain attributed historical examples.
