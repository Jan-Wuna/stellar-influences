## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Astrologer. Large bills and figuring, calculating, measuring, counting. To become suddenly interested in a new field of science. Suddenly gained knowledge, understanding or experiences. To suddenly become a scientific authority. Suddenly rising stock market.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 461; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

A respected place of technology, special tensions with the partner, dynamic authority at the place, an agitated local hierarchy, great unrest of the partner, new authority at the place, the partner's independent rhythm, and important new knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Suddenly acquired knowledge, scientific authority, or interest in a new field.
- **Psychology:** Abrupt understanding and new interest are explicit; ABC adds partner unrest.
- **Body/health:** No body or health interpretation is given.
- **Social/relationship:** Scientific authority is gained suddenly; ABC separately lists partner tensions.
- **Events/manifestations:** Astrology, calculation, measurement, counting, and a sudden stock-market rise are Witte examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC gives local authority and important new knowledge. Witte's calculation examples do not specify fast calculation.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 461.

## Contradictions

Scientific changes, calculating, partner tension, and a stock-market rise are distinct examples. None establishes a forecast or a promise of innovation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden knowledge or standing: the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names new scientific interest, abruptly gained understanding and becoming an authority suddenly. It also preserves astrology, measurement, calculation and a rising stock market as separate examples. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds partner tension, local authority and important new knowledge; these remain distinct expressions.
