## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Expanded friendships, harmony and willingness to help with or through scientists. The peacemaker. Appointment ratified by the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 460; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Special expansion of harmony, great freedom in love, experiencing great affection, independent knowledge of art, state success in peace, a preference for the authority of science, much joy through independence, and important experiences of love.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Harmony and expanding friendship associated with scientific people.
- **Psychology:** Willingness to help and experienced affection are explicit.
- **Body/health:** No physical or health condition is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** Peacemaking, friendship, love experience, and state peace success are listed.
- **Events/manifestations:** An appointment ratified by the state is a distinct Witte example.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC gives independent knowledge of art, the authority of science and joy through independence; these readable clauses extend its harmony and affection examples.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 460.

## Contradictions

Friendship and peaceful relations overlap; the state-ratified appointment is a separate institutional example, not proof of diplomatic office.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through friendship, harmony and help associated with scientists. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes peacemaking and a state-ratified appointment; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds affection, freedom in love, independent artistic knowledge and joy through independence. These are distinct agreeable relations around knowledge, love and official recognition.
