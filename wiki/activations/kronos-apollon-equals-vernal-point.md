## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

The rise of authority in general public. Things which are expanding. Growing authoritative attitude in public. The public understands a matter.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 460; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

The public reputation of science, special public successes, expansion of the state in public, state successes in public life, and increased independence or growing self-confidence in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Authority rising in public and matters expanding into wider public life.
- **Psychology:** Public understanding is explicit; acceptance or trust is not assured.
- **Body/health:** No individual physical or health meaning is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** An increasingly authoritative public attitude and public success are represented.
- **Events/manifestations:** Publicly expanding matters and an audience understanding a subject.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC concerns the public reputation of science and expansion of the state; its final clause distinguishes independence from self-confidence.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 460.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes rising public authority and public understanding. ABC adds the reputation of science, state successes and increased independence or self-confidence in public life. These extend the public setting without a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the pair through public authority and expanding matters. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins a rising authoritative attitude with the public understanding a subject; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds the public reputation of science, state successes and increased independence or self-confidence. Authority and understanding remain the focus in public life.
