## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

The mighty recognized leader in science, commerce or trade. The great scientific success.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 461; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, Vulcanus row.

Authoritative influence of science, special successes of power, influence through special knowledge, successful influence of the hierarchy, great influence of experience, power successes of the state, the state's fullness of power, and an important increase of power.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Recognized leadership and strong influence through knowledge or experience.
- **Psychology:** The entries establish influence, not a temperament or a manner of exercising it.
- **Body/health:** No body or health condition is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** Leadership in science, commerce, or trade and influence within hierarchy are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Great scientific success, state power gains, and increasing power are ABC/Witte examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Influence is supported; inevitability, decisiveness, or ethical quality is not.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 461.

## Contradictions

Scientific leadership and institutional power are different scales of influence; the entries do not establish independent proof that one produces the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through the influence carried by recognized expertise. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a powerful recognized leader in science, commerce, or trade and great scientific success; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds influence through knowledge, experience, and hierarchy. The focus is effective standing and power, without a claim about how that power is used.
