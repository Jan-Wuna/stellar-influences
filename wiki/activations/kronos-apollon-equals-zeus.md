## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 299, Kronos + Apollon, oriented activator row.

Enlarging of the field of operation. Expanding economic life. New branches in the economic system. To specialize in science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 461; one-based PDF p. 229, Kronos + Apollon, Zeus row.

Special goal setting in the economy, successful efforts of the hierarchy, successful state leadership, great successes in accomplishment, the spread of a large fire, striving for independent experience, important leadership experience, and an increase in independent accomplishments.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Expansion of operations or economic activity, including scientific specialization.
- **Psychology:** Directed objectives and striving for experience are ABC themes.
- **Body/health:** No physical or health interpretation is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Economic branches and state leadership are institutional examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** A wider field of operation, new economic branches, and scientific specialization.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC explicitly includes the spread of a large fire as a separate example; its activity or setting is not specified. Independent accomplishments do not mean self-sustaining ones.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 299 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 461.

## Contradictions

Expansion and specialization concern different dimensions of an undertaking. ABC extends the economic and leadership account and separately gives the spread of a large fire; no source makes fire a necessary setting for specialization.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Apollon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair as the enlargement of a field of operation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places this in economic expansion, new branches of a system and scientific specialization; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds goal setting, independent accomplishments and leadership experience. ABC's separate large-fire example remains distinct from these work and economic expressions.
