- **Psychology:** The source's unusual person lives apart; no rigidity or personality diagnosis is established.
- **Body/health:** Fall from height is an event example, not an asserted medical outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** A secluded life and the fall of a ruler or government are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Mountains, hills, caves, clefts, depth, and differences in altitude are preserved concrete examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Political downfall and physical height are parallel examples; one is not proof of the other.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 298 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions
