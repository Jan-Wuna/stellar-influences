## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 297, Kronos + Kronos, Asc row.

Things which come to the native from above. Workman. Employee. Official. Others are superiors. The influence of the state on the environment. Officials in the near. To come in touch with employees, officials or authorities. Things upon which one is dependent. Orders emanating from the authorities. Extraordinary place.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Authority encountered through surroundings, superiors, and dependency.
- **Psychology:** The entry describes conditions of dependency, not feelings about them.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health condition is named.
- **Social/relationship:** Contact with employees, officials, authorities, and people in superior positions.
- **Events/manifestations:** Orders from authorities, state influence on the environment, and an extraordinary place.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Being an employee, meeting an official, and receiving orders are distinct situations.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 297 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions

This single entry describes authority in one's surroundings, not proof that every environment is coercive or outside one's control.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Kronos = Asc`, Asc specifies the repeated pair through authority encountered around the person. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names superiors, officials, employees, orders from authorities, and things on which one depends. State influence on the environment and an extraordinary place extend that same outward setting; the passage does not define a response to those conditions.
