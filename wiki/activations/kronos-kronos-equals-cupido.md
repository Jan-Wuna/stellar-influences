## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Kronos = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the repeated pair through group prominence and the representation or support of art. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ranges from the family head and distinguished company to large enterprises, meetings of government heads, patrons, and official registries. Leadership or notable standing within a collective unifies the range, while each concrete institution remains an example rather than a required outcome.
