## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 298, Kronos + Kronos, Hades row.

Great baseness and meanness, poverty or want. Poor government. Plain clothes police. The criminal on a large scale. Organized crime. Detective force. Criminal police. To be obliged to give up independence. Death and ruin in ruling circles.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Deprivation, wrongdoing, or ruin associated with government and ruling circles.
- **Psychology:** Meanness is the source's evaluative language; no clinical character judgment follows.
- **Body/health:** Death is mentioned in ruling circles without a medical mechanism.
- **Social/relationship:** Organized crime, criminal police, poor government, and loss of independence.
- **Events/manifestations:** Poverty, large-scale criminal activity, detective work, death, and ruin are listed.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Police work and criminal conduct are separate examples; justice or its failure is not an inferred cause.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 298 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes both crime and investigation. It does not justify treating investigators as criminals or deriving a moral theory of power.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Kronos = Hades`, Hades specifies the repeated pair through deprivation, wrongdoing, and destructive conditions in ruling circles. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) also includes detective and criminal-police functions, so investigation must remain distinct from the crimes it addresses. The range includes lost independence and ruin, without adding the unsupported claim that these occur because authority no longer serves justice.
