### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 297, Kronos + Kronos, Jupiter row.

Formula of a wealthy man, or lucky and successful manager. Bankers. Independence in money matters. Philanthropist. Great luck and fortune. Government regulations for the monetary system and for the national banks. State justice.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Wealth, monetary independence, and success in managerial or state settings.
- **Psychology:** Luck and success are source themes; the manager's disposition is unspecified.
- **Body/health:** No body or health condition is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** Bankers, philanthropists, monetary regulators, and state justice are represented.
- **Events/manifestations:** Financial independence and government rules for money and national banks.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The source's wealthy-man category is historical; regulations are not said to protect or expand fortune.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 297 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions

This single entry joins private financial success with public monetary administration. It does not establish that regulation guarantees prosperity.
