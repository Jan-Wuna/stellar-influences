## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 297, Kronos + Kronos, Mars row.

To act independently. Autocratic actions. Main formula for the great leader and organizer. Master of the house. Police. Power of the state. Procedure indicated by power of government. Police authority. Penal law. Work in areas that go beyond the ordinary.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Independent or autocratic action expressed through leadership and organization.
- **Psychology:** Autocratic conduct is explicit; the source does not diagnose an overbearing personality.
- **Body/health:** No physical or health meaning is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** The house master, police, and state authority are concrete social roles.
- **Events/manifestations:** Government procedures, police authority, penal law, and work beyond the ordinary.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Leadership and penal authority are examples, not proof that all independent action is coercive.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 297 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions

This single entry places independence beside government procedure. It does not establish that exceptional work requires punishment or domination.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Kronos = Mars`, Mars specifies the repeated pair through action and organization. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names independent and autocratic acts, the great leader or organizer, and police or state power exercised through procedures and penal law. Work beyond the ordinary broadens the range, but a judgment that every such actor is coercive would exceed the passage.
