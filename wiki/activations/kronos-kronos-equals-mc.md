To be independent or outstanding in profession. To direct and accomplish something. Authority. Feeling of self-confident or grandeur. The own authority, greatness and skills. ## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** One's professional independence, authority, and ability to direct and accomplish.
- **Psychology:** Self-confidence, grandeur, and rejecting the average are explicit source descriptions.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health condition is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Holding an official position or receiving authority from the state.
- **Events/manifestations:** Professional distinction, accomplishment, and an outstanding moment are source examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The moment reference is retained as doctrine, not converted into a timing method.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 297 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions

This single entry combines felt authority with official authority; it does not establish that confidence alone confers public rank.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Kronos = MC`, MC specifies the repeated pair through one's own authority, skills, and independent professional position. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins directing and accomplishing with self-confidence, an official post, and authority bestowed by the state. These describe personal standing and its possible official expression, without turning the source's moment reference into a timing technique or inferring an entire life direction.
