### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 298, Kronos + Kronos, Neptune row.

Diplomacy. The great diplomat. Master of deception. Incompetent management. The great uncertainty. Unlimited uncertainty. Vaporized air. Misfortune to one’s superiors. Misfortune to the father. Confusion of the boss or the authorities. Annulment of decrees.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Diplomacy, deception, or uncertainty at the level of management and authorities.
- **Psychology:** Confusion of the boss or authorities is explicit; no clinical impairment is diagnosed.
- **Body/health:** Misfortune to a father or superior gives no specified medical cause.
- **Social/relationship:** Diplomatic roles, management, and relations with superiors are represented.
- **Events/manifestations:** Incompetent management, annulled decrees, great uncertainty, and vaporized air.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Diplomacy is not identified with deceit in every case; vaporized air remains a separate literal example.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 298 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes skilled diplomacy and incompetent management as alternatives. It does not prove that diplomacy is inherently deceptive.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Kronos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the repeated pair through uncertain or obscured authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) preserves the diplomat and the deceiver alongside incompetent management, confused authorities, and annulled decrees. These are different expressions of the relationship, so diplomatic skill should not be equated with deception, and misfortune to a father or superior supplies no medical diagnosis.
