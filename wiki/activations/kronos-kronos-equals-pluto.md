## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Development and proof of ability accompanying ascent in rank or changed authority.
- **Psychology:** Proving ability and developing skills are explicit; ambition is not separately asserted.
- **Body/health:** No physical or health condition is established.
- **Social/relationship:** Apprentice, journeyman, master, and a changed boss are concrete roles.
- **Events/manifestations:** Examination, certification, upward development, new wealth, and changes in government.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The self-made-man wording is historical; tests and rising status are not a guaranteed causal sequence.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 298 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes skill development and institutional change. It does not establish that every examination produces promotion or new wealth.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Kronos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the repeated pair as developing and demonstrating ability while standing changes. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links apprenticeship, testing, certification, and mastery with ascent in rank, a new boss, and changes of government. Its new-rich and self-made examples broaden the range of upward change; they do not guarantee that skill or a passed test will create wealth.
