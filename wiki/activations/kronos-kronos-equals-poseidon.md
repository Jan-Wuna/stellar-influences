## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Intellectual or moral stature, with education and culture as institutional expressions.
- **Psychology:** The sage, genius, and high-mindedness are the source's idealized descriptions.
- **Body/health:** No physical or health condition is named.
- **Social/relationship:** Education and culture departments are explicit official settings.
- **Events/manifestations:** Revelation, cultural activity, and educational administration are listed.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Spiritual praise and official office are parallel examples, not proof of an official's wisdom or morality.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 298 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions

This single entry's devotional and evaluative language remains attributed. It does not establish that educational institutions possess superior moral knowledge.
