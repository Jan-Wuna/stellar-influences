## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 298, Kronos + Kronos, Saturn row.

Old gentleman. High age. Late fulfillment. Long time or duration. Great hindrance. Ruling and independent persons impeded. Loss of sovereign persons. Losses suffered by the authorities. The great land owner or the person urgently requiring help. Losses or separations through the authorities. Separations of long duration. Long time. Late. Separation from the father. Shutdown of government structures.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Long duration, delayed fulfillment, and hindrance or loss affecting independent authorities.
- **Psychology:** The entry describes impediment and need for help; it does not establish a serious temperament.
- **Body/health:** High age is a source category; no specific illness is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Separation from the father, losses through authorities, and impeded rulers.
- **Events/manifestations:** Long separations, official losses, government shutdown, and a great landowner.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Age, delay, and institutional loss are distinct examples; endurance is not asserted as a trait.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 298 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes both those in authority and someone urgently needing help. It does not establish that old age necessarily brings loss.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Kronos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the repeated pair through duration, delay, and restrictions on independent or ruling people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes late fulfillment, prolonged separation, loss through authorities, and government shutdown, while retaining the great landowner and a person urgently needing help as concrete examples. The throughline is prolonged limitation around standing or independence, without turning age into a prediction of decline.
