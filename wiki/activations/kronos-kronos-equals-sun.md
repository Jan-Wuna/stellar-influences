### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 297, Kronos + Kronos, Sun row.

The father. The master. The boss (male or female). To be superior. Capabilities of the body. Independence. Power of the state. The king. The ruling prince. President. Disposition to leadership. To deal with officials. Official requests, summons. To be in service. One must appear before the authorities. Day of independence. (In the solar cycle: an extraordinary period.)

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Personal leadership, independence, and direct involvement with official authority.
- **Psychology:** Disposition to leadership is explicit; an identity theory is not supplied.
- **Body/health:** Capabilities of the body are named without a particular condition or diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** The father, boss of either gender, ruler, official, and person in service are examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Requests, summons, appearing before authorities, and an independence day are listed.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Leadership and being summoned are different positions; the solar-cycle note stays outside timing models.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 297 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Kronos = Sun`, Sun specifies the repeated pair through the person who leads, acts independently, or deals directly with authorities. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes the father, boss of either gender, ruler, official service, and summons to appear.
