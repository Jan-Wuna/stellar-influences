### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 298, Kronos + Kronos, Uranus row.

Emphatic expressions, words of command. Authority appearing suddenly. Great tension and collected power. Events of the state. Governmental intervention. To be ordered. Great excitement. Sudden decrees of the government or chief. Outstanding innovation, science or technology -or— outstanding skills in one of these fields.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sudden or emphatic authority, with tension and outstanding technical capability.
- **Psychology:** Excitement and great tension are explicit; no stable personality trait is given.
- **Body/health:** No physical or medical condition is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** Commands, government intervention, and sudden decrees define official relationships.
- **Events/manifestations:** Sudden authority, state events, innovation, and scientific or technological skill.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Technical skill and abrupt decrees are parallel examples; a breakthrough is not guaranteed.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 298 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes both sudden official action and outstanding skill. It does not establish that either causes the other.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Kronos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the repeated pair through sudden authority and concentrated tension. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names words of command, government intervention, sudden decrees, and excitement, while separately preserving innovation or outstanding scientific and technical skill. The exact reading joins abrupt official expression with exceptional capability, without turning all such skill into a decisive breakthrough.
