## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 298, Kronos + Kronos, Vulcanus row.

The top authority. The referee. The despot. A great statesman. Politics. Power of the state. Great force. Great power (nation). Supreme or executive power.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Supreme or executive authority, great force, and state power.
- **Psychology:** The despot is one source example; no universal disposition follows.
- **Body/health:** Great force is not a specific bodily or medical condition.
- **Social/relationship:** The referee, statesman, top authority, and great power are distinct roles.
- **Events/manifestations:** Politics and the exercise of supreme or executive power are explicit.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Supreme authority may be described as statesmanship or despotism; its moral character is not fixed.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 298 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes a referee, a statesman, and a despot. It does not establish that all high office is despotic or force is maximal.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Kronos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the repeated pair through supreme authority and the power to decide or govern. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ranges from the referee and great statesman to the despot, state force, and executive power. Power and rank unify these examples, while their different roles and moral implications remain distinct rather than becoming a universal warning about domination.
