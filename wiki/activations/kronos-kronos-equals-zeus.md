#### Witte Entry

Great and energetic activities. Energetic leadership. To assert oneself at all costs. The machine industry. Conflagrations. Warfare. Armament. The great commander-in-chief. ## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Energetic leadership and goal achievement through competence, including forceful assertion.
- **Psychology:** Self-assertion at all costs and lording over others are explicit source behaviors.
- **Body/health:** The entry names fires and warfare without a specified injury or medical condition.
- **Social/relationship:** Commanders, government intervention, industrial work, and authoritarian conduct are listed.
- **Events/manifestations:** Machine industry, armament, conflagrations, fire protection, and government fire insurance.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Fire prevention and destructive fire are separate branches; leadership need not be domineering.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 298 (entry start); this page has one source entry.

## Contradictions

This single entry preserves constructive competence, domination, warfare, and fire protection together. They are alternatives, not a required sequence.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Kronos = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the repeated pair through energetic direction and the pursuit of a goal. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes competent achievement, command, industrial and military activity, and forceful self-assertion. Its fire range includes both conflagration and official protection or insurance, so the interpretation preserves constructive administration alongside destructive or domineering examples.
