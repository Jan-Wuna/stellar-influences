## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 303, continuation of Kronos + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Highly spiritual and cultural endeavors intended for a few only. The spiritual and cultural elite.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 467; one-based PDF p. 232, Kronos + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Particular suppression of truth, restriction of an important idea, consolidation of a state's culture, consolidated intellectual or spiritual authority, great intellectual or spiritual blockage, a thorough hierarchy of the mind, independent intellectual or spiritual primordial generation, and important intellectual or spiritual deepening.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Spiritual or cultural endeavors confined to a few, with depth or restriction.
- **Psychology:** Spiritual deepening and mental blockade are ABC themes; special wisdom is not assured.
- **Body/health:** No body or health interpretation is given.
- **Social/relationship:** A small cultural or spiritual elite in Witte; cultural consolidation in ABC.
- **Events/manifestations:** A limited important idea, consolidated culture, and restricted spiritual work.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC explicitly gives suppression of truth, consolidated authority and independent primordial generation. The latter is retained literally without an asserted mechanism; elite remains Witte's term.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 303 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 467.

## Contradictions

Witte's selective cultural work and ABC's suppression of truth or mental blockage are distinct conditions. ABC also allows consolidated authority and independent origination; no entry establishes that an elite necessarily possesses truth.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Poseidon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair through spiritual and cultural work intended for only a few. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) calls this a spiritual or cultural elite; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds restricted ideas, suppression of truth and mental blockage beside consolidated cultural authority and independent origination. The range concerns a narrow or restricted sphere of thought and culture, without certifying its truth.
