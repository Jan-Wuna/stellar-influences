## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 303, continuation of Kronos + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

To be the sage for many. General enlightenment, widely spread culture. Great knowledge and wisdom together. Culture as the force of destiny, through the same cultural attitude joined. The science of culture.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 467; one-based PDF p. 232, Kronos + Poseidon, oriented activator row.

Expansion of the hierarchy of the mind, authority of intellectual or spiritual studies, success of a special idea, success of a cultural state, successful cultural reputation, independent intellectual or spiritual experiences, important intellectual or spiritual knowledge, and growing intellectual or spiritual greatness.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Knowledge and wisdom addressed to many, with widely spread culture.
- **Psychology:** Enlightenment and spiritual knowledge are source themes, not proven personal wisdom.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health condition is specified.
- **Social/relationship:** People joined by a shared cultural attitude; a sage for many is Witte's image.
- **Events/manifestations:** Cultural diffusion, the science of culture, and cultural reputation are represented.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC gives authority, independent experiences and growing intellectual or spiritual greatness; cultural destiny remains Witte's source language rather than an established causal theory.

Evidence: [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 303 (entry start); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 467.

## Contradictions

Shared culture and wide diffusion overlap, but no formal network or universal enlightenment is established by the entries.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Kronos/Poseidon = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through the wider reach of knowledge and culture. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes the sage for many, general enlightenment, cultural study and people joined by the same cultural attitude; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful ideas, cultural reputation, independent experiences and growing intellectual or spiritual greatness. Breadth of cultural understanding is the throughline, while the source's destiny language remains attributed.
