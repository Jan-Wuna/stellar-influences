## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 302 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Asc entry.

#### Witte Entry

To be connected with others through high minded subjects. Others are intellectuals or the native appears mentally superior. To be under the mental influence of others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 466; PDF p. 232 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Asc entry.

#### ABC Entry

Guide others intellectually; distinctive culture of the surroundings; a partner's dominant idea; an intellectually independent partner; intellectual authority locally; a place's cultural standing; state culture in the surroundings; important clarity locally; important truth from others.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Intellectual influence passes through partners, other people, and the immediate cultural environment.
- Psychology: Perceived mental stature and an independent partner's ideas shape the encounter.
- Body/health: Neither entry develops a body or health claim.
- Social/relationship: Shared elevated subjects coexist with influencing others and being influenced by them.
- Events/manifestations: Local intellectual authorities and a place's cultural standing give the theme a public setting.
- Conflicts/notes: Descriptions of mental superiority are source evaluations, not objective rankings.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Descriptions of mental superiority are source evaluations, not objective rankings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Asc, the intellectual and cultural distinction of Kronos/Poseidon takes shape through other people and the local environment. Shared elevated subjects can connect people, but influence runs both ways: one may guide others or come under their mental influence. An intellectually independent partner, local authority, or a place's cultural standing specifies this relational setting. The sources' language of superiority describes perceived stature; it does not establish anyone's intrinsic worth. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
