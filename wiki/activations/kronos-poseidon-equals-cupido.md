## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 302 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Cupido entry.

#### Witte Entry

Community with an elevated spiritual and cultural attitude. Cultural and mental groups. Political party. A major educational institution.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 467; PDF p. 232 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Cupido entry.

#### ABC Entry

A dominant cultural community; a shared intellectual hierarchy; a community of cultural states; a community's intellectual independence; a great intellectual community; a state's culture and art; a community's cultural standing.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Intellectual and cultural distinction belongs to a community or institution.
- Psychology: Shared attitudes and collective intellectual independence organize group identity.
- Body/health: The entries supply no specific body or health meaning.
- Social/relationship: Cultural groups, political parties, educational institutions, and associations of states carry the theme.
- Events/manifestations: A major educational institution or a community's cultural standing are explicit manifestations.
- Conflicts/notes: Intellectual hierarchy and elevated culture are evaluative source terms.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Intellectual hierarchy and elevated culture are evaluative source terms.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Cupido, Kronos/Poseidon concerns a community's cultural identity and intellectual standing. Shared attitudes support cultural and mental groups, while the named political party and major educational institution make the institutional range explicit. ABC extends this to art, state culture, and communities of cultural states, retaining both a shared hierarchy and collective independence. The emphasis is on the organized group through which cultural authority operates; the source's elevated language is an evaluation of that group. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
