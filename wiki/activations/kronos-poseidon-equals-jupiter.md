## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 302 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Jupiter entry.

#### Witte Entry

Happiness and success through agreement in high minded and cultural matters with superiors, the boss or the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 466; PDF p. 232 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Jupiter entry.

#### ABC Entry

Success of a dominant idea; joy in great clarity; joy in cultural standing; a state's money culture; goodness of a great intellectual figure; the state's legal success; state legal culture; rational financial independence.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Success, happiness, and favorable cultural or intellectual standing specify the pair.
- Psychology: Joy in clarity and cultural standing accompanies the success of a dominant idea.
- Body/health: The entries give no specific body or health claim.
- Social/relationship: Agreement with superiors, an employer, or the state supports Witte's favorable outcome.
- Events/manifestations: ABC adds legal success, state legal and money culture, and rational financial independence.
- Conflicts/notes: Agreement with authority and financial or legal success are explicit motifs, not guarantees.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Agreement with authority and financial or legal success are explicit motifs, not guarantees.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Jupiter, Kronos/Poseidon links cultural or intellectual standing to happiness and success. Witte locates this in agreement with superiors, an employer, or the state over elevated and cultural matters. ABC gives a wider institutional range: the success of a dominant idea, joy in clarity and standing, legal success, and financial independence. These entries make favorable recognition and agreement central while retaining law and finance as distinct stated manifestations. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
