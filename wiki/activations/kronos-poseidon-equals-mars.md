## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 302 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Mars entry.

#### Witte Entry

One’s own high moral standards. Activity in cultural or educational fields. To raise the cultural standard and the educational standards.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 466; PDF p. 232 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Mars entry.

#### ABC Entry

Create great clarity; impulses of the intellectual hierarchy; the state's cultural work; establish cultural standing; independent intellectual activity; work in the cultural authority; priority actions toward clarity; important impulses toward truth.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Active cultural or educational work seeks higher standards and greater clarity.
- Psychology: Personal moral standards and independent intellectual activity motivate action.
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a medical or bodily interpretation.
- Social/relationship: Cultural authorities and an intellectual hierarchy supply institutional settings for the work.
- Events/manifestations: Raising educational standards, establishing cultural standing, and impulses toward truth are explicit activities.
- Conflicts/notes: High moral standards are attributed ideals; the entries do not establish moral superiority.

## Contradictions And Tensions

High moral standards are attributed ideals; the entries do not establish moral superiority.

## Derived Synthesis

With Mars, the cultural and intellectual distinction of Kronos/Poseidon becomes work undertaken: raising educational standards, establishing cultural standing, and creating clarity. Witte emphasizes one's own moral standards; ABC adds independent intellectual activity, state cultural work, and work within a cultural authority. The shared theme is purposeful action in these fields, with impulses toward truth as an explicit aim. This does not by itself certify the actor's morality or the truth of a particular position. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
