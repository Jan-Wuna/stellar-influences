## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 302 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, MC entry.

#### Witte Entry

High or independent spiritual, mental or educational attitude. To have a revelation. To strive for wisdom and understanding. Averse to material things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 466; PDF p. 232 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, MC entry.

#### ABC Entry

Special insights into the soul; great insight into inner life; my insights into the state; my intellectual hierarchy; my intellectual independence; my great ideas; my cultural standing; truth is important to me; guidance of inner life through the mind.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A personal spiritual, intellectual, or educational attitude seeks insight, independence, and truth.
- Psychology: Wisdom, understanding, inner insight, and aversion to material things describe Witte's personal orientation.
- Body/health: ABC's soul and inner-life language does not supply a clinical claim.
- Social/relationship: Personal cultural standing and one's relation to intellectual hierarchy or the state appear in ABC.
- Events/manifestations: Revelation and insights into inner life or the state are named experiences.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries concern personal attitude and insight; they do not establish a particular career.

## Contradictions And Tensions

The entries concern personal attitude and insight; they do not establish a particular career.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With MC, Kronos/Poseidon becomes a personally held orientation toward wisdom, understanding, and intellectual or spiritual independence. Witte includes revelation and an aversion to material things; ABC connects insight into inner life with one's ideas, cultural standing, and conviction that truth matters. ABC also retains a relation to the state and intellectual hierarchy. These themes describe how meaning and authority are taken personally, without requiring an occupational interpretation. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
