- Source reference: PDF p. 302 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Mercury entry.

#### Witte Entry

High ideals, spiritualized thinking. Talks, conversations and correspondence about high minded or cultural concerns.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 466; PDF p. 232 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Mercury entry.

#### ABC Entry

Distinctive youth culture; conversations about intellectual hierarchy; great clarity of an idea; the media's cultural standing; news about great intellectual figures; self-confident youth culture; the state's insights concerning transport; consider important truths.

## Derived Synthesis

With Mercury, Kronos/Poseidon centers on articulating and exchanging elevated ideas. Witte names thinking, talks, and correspondence about cultural concerns; ABC adds clarity of an idea, news of intellectual figures, and the cultural standing of media. Youth culture is another explicit setting, including its self-confidence. The unusual reference to the state's insights concerning transport remains a separate stated motif; it does not justify adding a particular technical application. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
