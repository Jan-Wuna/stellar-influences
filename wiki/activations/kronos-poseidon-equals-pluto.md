## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 302 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Pluto entry.

#### Witte Entry

Cultural transformation. Highly spiritual or intellectual advancement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 467; PDF p. 232 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Pluto entry.

#### ABC Entry

Develop dominant ideas; develop intellectual independence; develop great clarity; cultural transformations in the state; rational changes in the state; growing intellectual independence; growing cultural standing; important unfolding of truth.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Cultural transformation develops ideas, intellectual independence, clarity, and standing.
- Psychology: The emphasis is on developing rather than merely possessing dominant ideas or insight.
- Body/health: Neither entry develops a health or bodily claim.
- Social/relationship: Cultural transformation and rational changes in the state give development a collective setting.
- Events/manifestations: Growing cultural standing, intellectual advancement, and unfolding truth are explicit processes.
- Conflicts/notes: Advancement is the source's evaluation; the entry does not guarantee beneficial political change.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Advancement is the source's evaluation; the entry does not guarantee beneficial political change.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Pluto, Kronos/Poseidon describes cultural and intellectual development as a process of change. Witte names cultural transformation and spiritual or intellectual advancement; ABC specifies developing dominant ideas, independence, and clarity, together with growing cultural standing. Cultural transformations and rational changes in the state supply the institutional range. The growth and advancement language belongs to the sources and does not certify every change as progress. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
