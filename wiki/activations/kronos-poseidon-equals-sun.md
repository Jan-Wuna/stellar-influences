## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 302 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Sun entry.

#### Witte Entry

The spiritualized and cultured man. The man who shows his mental ability and is far above the average. The body as expression of the mind and of spiritualization. Days devoted to high minded projects or subjects.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 466; PDF p. 232 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Sun entry.

#### ABC Entry

A person's dominant idea; an intellectually independent man; great personal clarity; a place's cultural center; a rational authority figure; important personal truth; important truth about a person; the center of an intellectual hierarchy.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Cultural and intellectual distinction is embodied in a person, personal clarity, or an authority figure.
- Psychology: A dominant idea and intellectual independence organize the personal emphasis.
- Body/health: Witte calls the body an expression of mind and spiritualization; this is symbolic source doctrine, not a health finding.
- Social/relationship: A rational authority figure or a person at the center of intellectual hierarchy are ABC examples.
- Events/manifestations: A cultural center and days devoted to elevated subjects are explicitly named.
- Conflicts/notes: The sources' masculine and above-average language remains historical evaluation.

## Contradictions And Tensions

The sources' masculine and above-average language remains historical evaluation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Sun, Kronos/Poseidon gives intellectual and cultural distinction a personal embodiment. A dominant idea, independence, and clarity characterize the person, while ABC also names a rational authority figure and the center of an intellectual hierarchy. Witte's body-as-expression-of-mind motif and days devoted to elevated projects retain their stated symbolic scope. His cultured man and above-average ability are historical, gendered evaluations, not objective judgments about a native's ability or worth. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
