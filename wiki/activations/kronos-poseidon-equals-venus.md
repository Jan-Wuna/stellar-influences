## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 302 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Venus entry.

#### Witte Entry

Highly religious attitude. Esoteric. Joy and harmony through the same highly mental or cultural matters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 466; PDF p. 232 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Venus entry.

#### ABC Entry

Authority for art and culture; special clarity in love; a distinctive culture of love; joy in special insights; a hierarchy's peaceful spirit; a peaceful cultural state; great intellectual harmony; an important love of truth.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Joy and harmony arise through shared cultural or spiritual concerns, love, and valued insight.
- Psychology: Religious or esoteric attitudes coexist with love of truth and clarity in love.
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a specific medical or bodily claim.
- Social/relationship: Shared elevated interests and a peaceful hierarchy or cultural state provide relational and collective settings.
- Events/manifestations: An authority for art and culture and joy in special insights are ABC manifestations.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's religious emphasis is narrower than ABC's institutional and love-related range.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Witte's religious emphasis is narrower than ABC's institutional and love-related range.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Venus, Kronos/Poseidon joins cultural and spiritual affinity to joy, harmony, and love of truth. Witte specifically names religious and esoteric attitudes, with harmony through shared elevated concerns. ABC adds clarity and a distinctive culture in love, joy in insight, and an authority for art and culture. Its peaceful hierarchy and cultural state extend harmony into institutional life. The range includes both intimate valuation and public culture without requiring a particular religious belief. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
