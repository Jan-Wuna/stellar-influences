## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 302 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Vernal Point entry.

#### Witte Entry

Mental high forces or the highly spiritual influence in the general public. The general spirituality or education, the respective cultural level. General cultural aspirations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 466; PDF p. 232 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Vernal Point entry.

#### ABC Entry

A dominant idea in public; intellectual independence in public life; great public clarity; a public cultural authority; state culture in public; important truth for the public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Intellectual or spiritual distinction becomes public influence, cultural aspiration, and public clarity.
- Psychology: Intellectual independence in public life is explicitly retained.
- Body/health: The entries develop no health or bodily meaning.
- Social/relationship: General education, cultural level, and a public cultural authority supply collective settings.
- Events/manifestations: A dominant public idea, state culture in public, and truth important to the public are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Descriptions of high mental or spiritual influence are historical evaluations of public culture.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Descriptions of high mental or spiritual influence are historical evaluations of public culture.

## Derived Synthesis

With Vernal Point, Kronos/Poseidon concerns the public reach of ideas, education, and culture. Witte names general cultural aspirations and intellectual or spiritual influence in the public; ABC adds intellectual independence in public life, clarity, and a dominant public idea. A public cultural authority and the public expression of state culture make the institutional setting explicit. Truth important to the public remains the stated concern, without treating public prominence as proof of intellectual superiority. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
