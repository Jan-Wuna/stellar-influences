## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 303 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Vulcanus entry.

#### Witte Entry

Special cultural influence. Mighty influence of a truth. Tremendous spirituality. A great outstanding culture.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 467; PDF p. 232 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Vulcanus entry.

#### ABC Entry

Special cultural influence; a dominant intellectual force; an influential cultural authority; insight into intellectual guidance; great cultural power; great intellectual influence; spiritual influence of the hierarchy; superior power of the mind; important influence of truth.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Intellectual, spiritual, and cultural authority is described through strong influence and power.
- Psychology: Insight into intellectual guidance accompanies the superior power attributed to mind.
- Body/health: No specific health or bodily claim appears.
- Social/relationship: Influential cultural authority and spiritual influence of a hierarchy are explicit social forms.
- Events/manifestations: Great cultural power, outstanding culture, and the influence of truth are named manifestations.
- Conflicts/notes: Power and prominence do not independently establish truth or cultural worth.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Power and prominence do not independently establish truth or cultural worth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Vulcanus, Kronos/Poseidon describes the force with which intellectual, spiritual, or cultural authority operates. Both entries emphasize cultural influence and the influence of truth; ABC also names dominant intellectual force, cultural power, and spiritual influence through hierarchy. Witte's tremendous spirituality and outstanding culture mark the same evaluative intensity. The synthesis concerns the strength of influence, while keeping a source's claim to truth distinct from independent verification. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
