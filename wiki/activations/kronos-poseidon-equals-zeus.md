## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 303 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Zeus entry.

#### Witte Entry

Highly mental creation. To have one’s mental ability tested. To take the lead in highly mental things, to be a leader. Great cultural accomplishment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 467; PDF p. 232 (one-based), Kronos + Poseidon, Zeus entry.

#### ABC Entry

A special culture of achievement; shaping dominant ideas; a leading cultural state; great intellectual efforts; the state's cultural achievements; a creative intellectual hierarchy; shape state culture; rational state leadership; important testimony to truth.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Intellectual creation and cultural achievement take form through effort and leadership.
- Psychology: Mental ability is tested and dominant ideas are shaped.
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a specific medical or bodily meaning.
- Social/relationship: Creative intellectual hierarchy and leadership in cultural affairs are explicit social forms.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural achievements, shaping state culture, rational state leadership, and testimony to truth are ABC motifs.
- Conflicts/notes: Achievement and leadership are source themes; a particular office or outcome is not established.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Achievement and leadership are source themes; a particular office or outcome is not established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Zeus, Kronos/Poseidon places intellectual and cultural distinction in the act of creating, shaping, and leading. Witte joins mental creation to tested ability and cultural accomplishment; ABC adds great intellectual efforts, the shaping of dominant ideas, and the formation of state culture. Rational state leadership and testimony to truth retain the institutional and declarative range. The focus is on producing and directing cultural work, rather than cultural standing alone. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
