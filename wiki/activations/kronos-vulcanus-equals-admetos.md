## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Vulcanus = Admetos`: a frozen or obsolete political order, suspended state power, and government force that has become rigid, peculiar, or inert.
- Chart expression: heavy, entrenched authority, but it also warns of dead systems, hardening bureaucracy, and power that no longer adapts or moves.
