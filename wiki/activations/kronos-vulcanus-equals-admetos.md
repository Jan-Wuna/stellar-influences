## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 301 (one-based), Kronos + Vulcanus, Admetos entry.

#### Witte Entry

Past and outdated political system of a government. Isolation of a great power. States which are considered peculiar by other states. The end of the power of a state. The dead or suspended state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 465; PDF p. 231 (one-based), Kronos + Vulkanus, Admetos entry.

#### ABC Entry

Restriction of state power; consolidation of state power; a consolidated hierarchy of power; restriction of force by the state; thoroughgoing superiority of power; stabilization of a state's power; important primordial-matter energy; resistance to state authority.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: State power is restricted, consolidated, isolated, or brought to an end.
- Psychology: Resistance to state authority is explicit; no general personality profile is supplied.
- Body/health: ABC's primordial-matter energy is a material motif, not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: An isolated great power and states judged peculiar by others are Witte's political examples.
- Events/manifestations: An outdated political system, a suspended state, stabilization, and restriction of force occur.
- Conflicts/notes: Restriction and consolidation coexist; this is not solely a picture of collapse.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Restriction and consolidation coexist; this is not solely a picture of collapse.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Admetos, Kronos/Vulcanus describes the limits and consolidation of state power. Witte emphasizes outdated government, isolation, and the end or suspension of a state's power. ABC preserves another possibility: stabilization and a firmly established hierarchy, alongside restriction of force and resistance to authority. The range therefore includes power becoming fixed as well as power ceasing to operate. Primordial-matter energy remains a distinct material motif rather than a political explanation. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
