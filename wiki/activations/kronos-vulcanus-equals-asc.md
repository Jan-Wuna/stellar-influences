## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 301 (one-based), Kronos + Vulcanus, Asc entry.

#### Witte Entry

To be exposed to great powers and great forces. Others make their power felt or are themselves subject to the effect of the power of the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 464; PDF p. 231 (one-based), Kronos + Vulkanus, Asc entry.

#### ABC Entry

The influence of others' authority; a partner's special power; dominance of a strong partner; the state's influence on the surroundings; an influential local hierarchy; a place of great power; state authority locally; an important partner influence.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Great power is encountered through others, a partner, and the immediate environment.
- Psychology: The entry stresses experiencing another's dominance rather than an inferred character trait.
- Body/health: No medical meaning is specified.
- Social/relationship: A strong partner and influential local hierarchy make power relational.
- Events/manifestations: Exposure to great forces and local state authority are explicit manifestations.
- Conflicts/notes: Others may exert power or themselves be subject to it.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Others may exert power or themselves be subject to it.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Asc, Kronos/Vulcanus concerns encountering power through other people and the surroundings. Witte preserves two positions: others make their power felt, or they are themselves affected by state power. ABC locates the encounter in a strong partner, local hierarchy, or a place with substantial power. The orientation therefore concerns the relational and local setting of authority, without assuming that every person involved occupies the commanding position. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
