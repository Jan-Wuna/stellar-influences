- Source reference: PDF p. 301 (one-based), Kronos + Vulcanus, Cupido entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 465; PDF p. 231 (one-based), Kronos + Vulkanus, Cupido entry.

#### ABC Entry

A community's special influence; influence of shared strength; a great community of energy; hierarchical influence on the community; the strength of a great community; a powerful community of states; a community's strong dominance.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Power and influence belong to a community, association, or union of states.
- Psychology: Shared strength is explicit; a particular collective intention is not specified.
- Body/health: No specific bodily or health claim appears.
- Social/relationship: State protection and subsidy support associations; great powers can unite.
- Events/manifestations: A powerful community of states and hierarchical influence on a community are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Collective strength and state support are different forms of influence.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Collective strength and state support are different forms of influence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Cupido, Kronos/Vulcanus describes power organized through a community. Witte names associations protected and subsidized by the state and united great powers. ABC adds the influence of shared strength, a powerful community of states, and a hierarchy's influence on a group. A community may thus possess power collectively or receive support from an authority. These stated forms do not require an invented organizational structure or political purpose. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
