## Derived Synthesis

With Mercury, Kronos/Vulcanus concerns thinking and speaking about power, especially the state and politics. ABC makes communication itself a source of power through media authority and dominance, conversations about hierarchy, and strength of thought. It also names youth, including self-confidence and state force directed against young people, alongside traffic and energies in motion. These are distinct stated manifestations; strategic planning or a specific administrative function need not be added. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
