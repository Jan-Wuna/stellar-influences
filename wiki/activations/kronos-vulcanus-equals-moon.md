## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 301 (one-based), Kronos + Vulcanus, Moon entry.

#### Witte Entry

Persons (especially females) with executive power. High government authorities, offices. Mighty sensation in the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 464; PDF p. 231 (one-based), Kronos + Vulkanus, Moon entry.

#### ABC Entry

Special influence of a people; special emotional strength; influence of a great people; influence of an independent woman; a woman with state authority; great emotional strength; strong dominance of a woman; an hour of special strength; important energies of a people.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Executive power and public impact coexist with the influence and emotional strength of a people.
- Psychology: ABC explicitly names emotional strength and dominance in historically gendered terms.
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a medical claim.
- Social/relationship: People with executive power, especially women in Witte's wording, and government offices are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: A powerful public sensation and a people's energies are named; ABC also states an hour of special strength.
- Conflicts/notes: Gendered descriptions remain historical claims, not universal differences in ability.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Gendered descriptions remain historical claims, not universal differences in ability.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Moon, Kronos/Vulcanus describes executive authority together with its public and emotional reach. Witte names government authorities and offices, particularly women in executive positions, and a powerful public sensation. ABC adds an independent woman's influence, emotional strength, and the influence or energies of a people. The hour-of-strength phrase remains a stated source image. The gendered emphasis is preserved as historical wording rather than a general rule about who can hold power. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
