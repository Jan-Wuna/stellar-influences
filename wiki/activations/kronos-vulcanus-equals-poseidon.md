## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 301 (one-based), Kronos + Vulcanus, Poseidon entry.

#### Witte Entry

Great mental or spiritual influence. Might in spiritual fields or through spiritual influence. Great powers through spirituality or through the outlook on life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 465; PDF p. 231 (one-based), Kronos + Vulkanus, Poseidon entry.

#### ABC Entry

Special cultural influence; a dominant intellectual force; an influential cultural authority; great cultural power; spiritual influence of the hierarchy; superior power of the mind; rational state power; important insights into energy; important influence of truth.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Power operates through intellectual, spiritual, cultural, or worldview influence.
- Psychology: Outlook on life and the power attributed to mind are explicit.
- Body/health: Insights into energy are stated without a medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Cultural authority and a hierarchy's spiritual influence are named.
- Events/manifestations: Great cultural power, rational state power, and the influence of truth form ABC's range.
- Conflicts/notes: A claim to truth or rational authority remains a source theme, not independent verification.

## Contradictions And Tensions

A claim to truth or rational authority remains a source theme, not independent verification.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Poseidon, Kronos/Vulcanus locates power in intellectual and spiritual influence. Witte explicitly includes spirituality and outlook on life as ways that great power operates. ABC broadens this to cultural authority, cultural power, rational state power, and truth's influence, retaining insights into energy as another stated theme. The emphasis is on ideas and worldview as sources of influence; a powerful belief is not thereby established as true. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
