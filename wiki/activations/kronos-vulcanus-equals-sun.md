### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Days and times which bring great events. The politician, the statesman. The man who exercises great physical influence, who appears as the representative of a power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

influence of a man, great body power, great self being of a man, strong dominance of a man, superior corporal violence, important personal influence, and center of state power
