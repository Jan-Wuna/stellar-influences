## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 301 (one-based), Kronos + Vulcanus, Sun entry.

#### Witte Entry

Days and times which bring great events. The politician, the statesman. The man who exercises great physical influence, who appears as the representative of a power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 464; PDF p. 231 (one-based), Kronos + Vulkanus, Sun entry.

#### ABC Entry

A man's special power; a person's influential authority; great bodily strength; a man's great self-confidence; a man's strong dominance; superior physical force; important personal influence; the center of state power.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Substantial power is embodied or represented by a person.
- Psychology: Self-confidence and dominance are explicit in ABC's historically masculine wording.
- Body/health: Bodily strength and physical force are stated without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: The politician, statesman, and representative of a power are Witte's explicit figures.
- Events/manifestations: The center of state power and times bringing great events are named.
- Conflicts/notes: The masculine wording is historical; representing power does not itself establish a particular office.

## Contradictions And Tensions

The masculine wording is historical; representing power does not itself establish a particular office.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Sun, Kronos/Vulcanus gives substantial power a personal embodiment. Witte names the politician, statesman, and person representing a power; ABC adds influential personal authority, bodily strength, self-confidence, dominance, and the center of state power. Witte's times bringing great events remain part of the source's stated range. Its masculine wording is historical, and the entry does not establish either a person's actual office or a medical conclusion from physical strength. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
