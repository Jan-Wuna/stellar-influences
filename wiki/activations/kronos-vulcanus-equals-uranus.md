## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 301 (one-based), Kronos + Vulcanus, Uranus entry.

#### Witte Entry

The accurate and punctual working of the bureaucracy of the state. Surprising interference by the state. The suddenly appearing great power (=nation).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 465; PDF p. 231 (one-based), Kronos + Vulkanus, Uranus entry.

#### ABC Entry

Special energy impulses; special power of technology; influence of state reforms; excitement over state force; renewal of power in the state; tensions over power in the state; surprising influence of the hierarchy; important technical energies.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: State power acts with precision or appears through surprising intervention and renewal.
- Psychology: Excitement over state force and tensions over power are explicit ABC motifs.
- Body/health: No specific bodily or health claim occurs.
- Social/relationship: Bureaucracy and a hierarchy's surprising influence are institutional settings.
- Events/manifestations: Accurate administration, state reforms, technical power, and sudden emergence of a great power are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Administrative regularity and surprising intervention are both present.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Administrative regularity and surprising intervention are both present.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Uranus, Kronos/Vulcanus joins the precise operation of state machinery to unexpected assertions of power. Witte names accurate and punctual bureaucracy, surprising interference, and a great power suddenly appearing. ABC adds state reforms, renewal and tensions over power, technical energies, and excitement over state force. The range thus includes both regular administrative operation and disruptive intervention; speed alone does not exhaust the meaning. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
