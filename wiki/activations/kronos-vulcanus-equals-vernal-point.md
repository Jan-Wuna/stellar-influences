## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 301 (one-based), Kronos + Vulcanus, Vernal Point entry.

#### Witte Entry

Great powers. Powers of States. To be under the impression or influence of the power of the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 464; PDF p. 231 (one-based), Kronos + Vulkanus, Vernal Point entry.

#### ABC Entry

Special public power; dominance of strength in public; influential public authority; great force in public life; state authority in public; important energies for the public.

## Derived Synthesis

With Vernal Point, Kronos/Vulcanus concerns substantial power in public life. Witte names great powers and the impression or influence of state power; ABC adds dominant strength, influential public authority, and energies directed toward the public. The orientation emphasizes how power is publicly present and experienced. It does not require a particular conflict or political event to make that influence meaningful. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
