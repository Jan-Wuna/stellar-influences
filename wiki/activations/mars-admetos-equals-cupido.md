## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Cupido entry.

#### Witte Entry

Broken marriages. Special work in a small community. Cooperative work in a small circle. Unsuccessful cooperative work. Bankruptcies of firms.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 319; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Cupido entry.

#### ABC Entry

A persevering work community; blocked communal work; a blocked community of will; a restricted work community; a thorough community of action; a firm common will; shared earth work; shared immersion in work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Specialized or restricted communal work combines with a firm shared will.
- Psychology: Perseverance and shared immersion in work are explicit ABC themes.
- Body/health: No specific medical or bodily claim is stated.
- Social/relationship: Small working communities and cooperative effort coexist with Witte's broken-marriage motif.
- Events/manifestations: Unsuccessful cooperation, firm bankruptcies, shared earth work, and thorough collective action occur.
- Conflicts/notes: Small-scale cooperation need not fail; adverse relationship and business outcomes remain source possibilities.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Small-scale cooperation need not fail; adverse relationship and business outcomes remain source possibilities.

## Derived Synthesis

With Cupido, Mars/Admetos places concentrated and restricted effort in a community. Witte names special work and cooperation in a small circle, alongside unsuccessful cooperation, broken marriages, and firms' bankruptcies. ABC preserves the working group's perseverance, firm common will, thorough action, and shared immersion, but also blocked communal work and will. The group can sustain specialized effort or experience serious obstruction; the entry does not require either outcome in every case. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
