## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Hades entry.

#### Witte Entry

To be humiliated and oppressed in one’s work. Little services of a low kind. Humiliated, abased and wronged through services.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 319; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Hades entry.

#### ABC Entry

Privations through blocked activity; consolidation through labor service; lack through work restrictions; dangerous depression impulses; hidden earth works; immersion in dangerous work; resistance to criminal acts.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Work is associated with privation, oppression, danger, and resistance to wrongdoing.
- Psychology: Humiliation and dangerous depression impulses are attributed source themes.
- Body/health: Depression-related wording remains historical doctrine, not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Witte describes being wronged through service; ABC adds labor service and resistance to criminal acts.
- Events/manifestations: Hidden earth works and immersion in dangerous work are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: Historical judgments about low services do not establish a worker's personal worth.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Historical judgments about low services do not establish a worker's personal worth.

## Derived Synthesis

With Hades, Mars/Admetos describes adverse working conditions and the restriction of agency within them. Witte names humiliation, oppression, and being wronged through service; ABC adds privation and lack caused by blocked or restricted activity, labor service, hidden earth works, and dangerous work. Resistance to criminal acts remains a distinct stated response. The historical language about low services and depression is attributed to the sources, without turning occupation into a judgment of worth or a clinical conclusion. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
