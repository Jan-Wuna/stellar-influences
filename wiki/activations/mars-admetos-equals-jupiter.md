## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Jupiter entry.

#### Witte Entry

Fortunate work accomplished in a small circle. Joy through decreasing one’s work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 318; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Jupiter entry.

#### ABC Entry

Persevering actions toward success; limitation through legal activity; blocked joy in work; a restricted right to work; restriction of financial activity; joy through working with earth; thorough financial activity; immersion in labor law.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A small field or reduced workload can bring satisfaction, while persistent work seeks success.
- Psychology: Joy in decreased work or in earth work contrasts with blocked joy in work.
- Body/health: Neither entry makes a specific medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Restrictions on the right to work and legal activity give limitation a social form.
- Events/manifestations: Financial restrictions, thorough financial work, and labor law are ABC manifestations.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries preserve benefits of reduction alongside real legal and financial constraints.

## Contradictions And Tensions

The entries preserve benefits of reduction alongside real legal and financial constraints.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Jupiter, Mars/Admetos gives limitation a potentially favorable expression: fortunate work in a small circle and joy in reducing one's work. ABC adds persevering actions toward success and joy in earth work, without omitting blocked enjoyment, a restricted right to work, or limits on financial activity. Thorough financial work and immersion in labor law give the entry concrete fields. The benefit lies in particular stated forms of small-scale work or reduction, not in a rule that every restriction brings luck. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
