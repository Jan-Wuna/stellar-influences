## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Kronos entry.

#### Witte Entry

Leading activity in a small circle or special work. Acting independently under limited circumstances. Independent in small circle, in a narrow room or space. Restricted independence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 319; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Kronos entry.

#### ABC Entry

Special perseverance at work; blocked independent activity; restrictive actions of the state; thorough work of authority; independent earth work; immersion in independent work; priority work with primordial matter; resistance to state actions.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Independent or leading work is exercised in a narrow field under restrictions.
- Psychology: Special perseverance and immersion in independent work are explicit.
- Body/health: No specific medical or bodily claim is supplied.
- Social/relationship: Leadership within a small circle and restrictive state actions are distinct settings.
- Events/manifestations: Thorough work by authority, independent earth work, and priority work with primordial matter occur.
- Conflicts/notes: Restricted independence is still independence; resistance to state action is separately retained.

## Derived Synthesis

With Kronos, Mars/Admetos describes independence or leadership exercised within a small and restricted field. Witte explicitly retains the ability to act independently under limited circumstances. ABC adds perseverance, thorough work by authority, independent earth work, and work with primordial matter. Blocked independence, restrictive state actions, and resistance to those actions keep the adverse side visible. The formula thus concerns the scope and limits of independent activity rather than unrestricted command. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
