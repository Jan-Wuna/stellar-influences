## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, MC entry.

#### Witte Entry

Narrow minded, mean or petty. To confine oneself to a small circle. Activity in a small circle. To experience suppression. Limitation of freedom or movement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 318; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, MC entry.

#### ABC Entry

My consolidated will; my persevering work; my earth work; my thorough actions; my blocked work; my restricted activity; emotional depressions at work; suppressed inner impulses; immersion in my work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A personally narrowed field of work combines with blocked activity, consolidated will, and immersion.
- Psychology: Suppressed inner impulses and emotional depression at work coexist with perseverance.
- Body/health: Depression language is source doctrine; it does not establish a clinical condition.
- Social/relationship: Self-confinement, suppression, and limited freedom or movement appear in Witte.
- Events/manifestations: Personal earth work, thorough actions, and work in a small circle are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's petty and narrow-minded character judgments are attributed, not automatic traits.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Witte's petty and narrow-minded character judgments are attributed, not automatic traits.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With MC, Mars/Admetos concerns restriction as personally experienced in work and freedom of action. Witte includes self-confinement, a small circle, suppression, and limited movement, along with evaluative character language. ABC adds a consolidated will, persevering work, thorough actions, and immersion in one's work, while retaining blocked activity and suppressed inner impulses. The personal range therefore includes concentration as well as oppression; the source's depression terms and character judgments should not become a diagnosis or a fixed identity. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
