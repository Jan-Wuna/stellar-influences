## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Moon entry.

#### Witte Entry

Persons who feel depressed and crowded, who expect the worst. The terrorized public or women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 318; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Moon entry.

#### ABC Entry

A woman's persevering will; a woman's impulses toward blockage; blocked actions of feeling; restricted emotional activity; a people's consolidated will; feeling-filled immersion in creative work; a woman's thorough work; immersion in a people's creative work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Emotional constriction and pessimism coexist with persevering will and immersion in creative work.
- Psychology: Expecting the worst, feeling crowded, and restricted emotional activity are explicit source themes.
- Body/health: Depressed feelings are historical source language, not a clinical diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Witte names a terrorized public or women; ABC includes a people's consolidated will.
- Events/manifestations: Thorough work and immersion in a people's creative work add a sustained productive expression.
- Conflicts/notes: Gendered claims remain attributed; pessimism does not exhaust the entry.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Gendered claims remain attributed; pessimism does not exhaust the entry.

## Derived Synthesis

With Moon, Mars/Admetos places restriction in emotional and public experience. Witte describes feeling depressed and crowded, expecting the worst, and a terrorized public or women. ABC preserves blocked feeling and restricted emotional activity, but also a woman's persevering will, a people's consolidated will, and immersion in creative work. These sustained forms of effort prevent a wholly pessimistic reading. The gendered and depression-related language remains historical source material, not a general claim about women or a clinical finding. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
