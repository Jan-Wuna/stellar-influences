## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Neptune entry.

#### Witte Entry

Important hindrances. Great failures. Very great disturbances. Ruin.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 319; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Neptune entry.

#### ABC Entry

Depressive weakness of will; disappointing restriction of work; consolidation of unclear work; thorough activity in air or water; immersion in hidden work; work involving backed-up water; future work with primordial matter.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Serious hindrance and failure coexist with unclear, hidden, or materially specific work.
- Psychology: Depressive weakness of will and disappointing work restrictions are explicit source themes.
- Body/health: The depression wording is not a diagnosis; air, water, and backed-up water are material motifs.
- Social/relationship: The entries concern activity and work rather than a distinct relationship pattern.
- Events/manifestations: Thorough activity in air or water, hidden work, and future work with primordial matter occur.
- Conflicts/notes: Thorough work remains in ABC alongside Witte's ruin and failures; the entry does not supply a remedy.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Thorough work remains in ABC alongside Witte's ruin and failures; the entry does not supply a remedy.

## Derived Synthesis

With Neptune, Mars/Admetos includes significant hindrance, disturbance, failure, and ruin in Witte's entry. ABC retains weakness of will and disappointing work restrictions, while giving a more particular range: unclear work becoming established, thorough activity in air or water, hidden work, backed-up water, and future work with primordial matter. These concrete motifs remain alongside the adverse outcome language. Neither the depression terminology nor the image of ruin should be converted into a clinical diagnosis or an inevitable result. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
