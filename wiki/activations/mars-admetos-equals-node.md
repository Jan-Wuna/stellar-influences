## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Node entry.

#### Witte Entry

Connected with strongly depressed or pessimistic persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 318; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Node entry.

#### ABC Entry

Consolidate work connections; persevering activity in connections; blocked work contacts; depression impulses in connections; restricted mediation of work; thorough contact activities; connected with earth works; wanting to end connections.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Work connections can be consolidated, blocked, sustained, or deliberately ended.
- Psychology: Perseverance in connections coexists with depression impulses and pessimistic company.
- Body/health: The sources' depression terms do not establish a clinical condition.
- Social/relationship: Witte describes connection with depressed or pessimistic people; ABC describes working contacts and mediation.
- Events/manifestations: Earth works, thorough contact activity, and restricted mediation of work are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries concern the connection; they do not establish that every participant shares the same condition.

## Contradictions And Tensions

The entries concern the connection; they do not establish that every participant shares the same condition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Node, Mars/Admetos describes work and limitation through connections. Witte emphasizes association with strongly depressed or pessimistic people; ABC adds blocked work contacts and depression impulses within ties. Yet it also names consolidating work connections, persevering activity, thorough contact, and a wish to end connections. Earth works and restricted mediation of work give the theme concrete settings. The condition of a contact should remain distinct from a diagnosis of the person connected to them. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
