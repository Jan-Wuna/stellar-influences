## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Pluto entry.

#### Witte Entry

Discarded plans. Dwindling development. The stock market shows sinking tendencies. Profound planning in a special domain. Breaking down of present conditions changes things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 319; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Pluto entry.

#### ABC Entry

Develop perseverance in work; blocked developmental work; develop consolidation of will; activate fluid circulation; thorough changes in work; transformative activity with primordial matter; growing depression impulses.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Development and plans can contract or change while perseverance and will are deliberately developed.
- Psychology: Developing endurance and consolidation of will coexist with growing depression impulses.
- Body/health: ABC's fluid circulation is a stated motif without a medical mechanism or diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: The entries emphasize work and development rather than a separate relationship meaning.
- Events/manifestations: Discarded plans, a sinking stock market, special-domain planning, and transformation of primordial matter are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Contraction and thorough change do not establish that breakdown is necessary for future progress.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Contraction and thorough change do not establish that breakdown is necessary for future progress.

## Derived Synthesis

With Pluto, Mars/Admetos concerns development under constraint and the thorough changes that follow. Witte names discarded plans, dwindling development, a sinking market, and profound planning in a special domain. ABC adds developing perseverance and a consolidated will, alongside blocked development, thorough changes in work, fluid circulation, and transformation involving primordial matter. Growing depression impulses retain the adverse range. The entries allow contraction and deliberate development together, without making breakdown a necessary step toward improvement. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
