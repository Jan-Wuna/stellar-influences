## Source Entries

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Saturn entry.

#### Witte Entry

Decreased periodic work. Spreading work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 319; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Saturn entry.

#### ABC Entry

Interruption of work through depressions; perseverance and patience at work; serious thorough activity; depression through hard work; painful blockage of work; difficult earth work; blockade activity involving losses; immersion in work tasks.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Work is reduced or interrupted, while patience and thoroughness can sustain attention to tasks.
- Psychology: Perseverance, patience, and immersion coexist with painful blockage and depression language.
- Body/health: Depression through hard work is attributed doctrine, not a clinical causal finding.
- Social/relationship: The entries focus on work conditions rather than a distinct intimate relationship meaning.
- Events/manifestations: Difficult earth work, blockade activity involving losses, and decreased periodic work are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's exact phrase Spreading work is unexplained; no specific distribution method is inferred.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Witte's exact phrase Spreading work is unexplained; no specific distribution method is inferred.

## Derived Synthesis

With Saturn, Mars/Admetos combines decreased or interrupted work with the effort to persist. ABC explicitly names patience, serious thorough activity, and immersion in tasks alongside painful blockage, difficult earth work, losses, and depression-related language. Witte's decreased periodic work fits the restricted field, while his unexplained phrase Spreading work is retained without an invented account of its meaning. The entries support endurance and difficulty together; they do not establish chronic exhaustion or a medical cause. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
