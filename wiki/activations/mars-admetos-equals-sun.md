## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Sun entry.

#### Witte Entry

The little daily tasks. Activity confined to small space. The man who works with loss, who narrows, reduces his field of work. Clumsy man.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 318; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Sun entry.

#### ABC Entry

A blocked work center; a person's consolidated will; a man's thorough actions; the body's circulatory work; a person working with earth; thorough personal activity; restriction of personal will; the center of a work cycle.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Daily activity is confined to a small field, with consolidated will and thorough work also possible.
- Psychology: Personal restriction of will coexists with firm intention.
- Body/health: The body's circulatory work is an explicit ABC motif, not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Witte uses historical masculine and evaluative wording about the working person.
- Events/manifestations: Small daily tasks, work in limited space, earth work, and a work-cycle center are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Thorough activity and restricted scope do not by themselves imply lack of ability.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Thorough activity and restricted scope do not by themselves imply lack of ability.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Sun, Mars/Admetos concerns the person engaged in a small or narrowed field of work. Witte names daily tasks, limited space, reduced scope, and work with loss. ABC adds consolidated will, thorough personal activity, earth work, the body's circulatory work, and the center of a work cycle. These statements retain both constriction and sustained practical attention. Witte's masculine language and judgment of clumsiness remain attributed evaluations, not conclusions about an individual's ability or health. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
