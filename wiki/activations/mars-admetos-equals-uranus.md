## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Uranus entry.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden restriction in the field of work. Enemies made suddenly. To rip or tear something suddenly. Fragments and rags.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 319; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Uranus entry.

#### ABC Entry

Blocked reform activity; restrictive reform activity; new activity with primordial matter; sudden depression impulses; strike and unrest at work; activity in rotational technology; unrest through blockage of work; immersion in new work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Work is suddenly restricted or disrupted, while reform and new work remain part of the range.
- Psychology: Sudden depression impulses and unrest are explicit historical source terms.
- Body/health: No particular clinical claim is established by the depression language.
- Social/relationship: Strikes, work unrest, and suddenly made enemies are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Tearing, fragments, blocked reforms, rotational technology, and new primordial-matter work are named.
- Conflicts/notes: New work and reform can be pursued or restricted; abrupt rupture is not the sole expression.

## Contradictions And Tensions

New work and reform can be pursued or restricted; abrupt rupture is not the sole expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Uranus, Mars/Admetos includes sudden restriction and disruption in work. Witte names abruptly made enemies, tearing, and fragments. ABC adds strikes, unrest, blocked or restrictive reform, and sudden depression impulses, while retaining immersion in new work. Rotational technology and new activity with primordial matter give specific technical or material expressions. The range is therefore wider than an image of compression breaking apart, and its adverse terms do not make disruption inevitable. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
