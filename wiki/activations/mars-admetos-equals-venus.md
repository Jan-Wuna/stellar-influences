## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 202 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Venus entry.

#### Witte Entry

An intimate small circle of friends or acquaintances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 318; PDF p. 158 (one-based), Mars + Admetos, Venus entry.

#### ABC Entry

Persevering artistic activity; limited peace at work; blocked love activity; restricted joy in work; thorough work for peace; consolidate harmony at work; deepen artistic work; preference for earth work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A small intimate circle coexists with persevering art, restricted enjoyment, and efforts toward harmony.
- Psychology: Preference for earth work and deepening artistic work are explicit.
- Body/health: No specific body or health claim is given.
- Social/relationship: Friends and acquaintances define Witte's social scope; ABC also names blocked love activity.
- Events/manifestations: Workplace peace, thorough work for peace, and consolidation of harmony are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: Intimacy, limitation, and artistic perseverance are stated; loyalty is not independently established.

## Contradictions And Tensions

Intimacy, limitation, and artistic perseverance are stated; loyalty is not independently established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

With Venus, Mars/Admetos combines an intimate small circle of friends or acquaintances with concentrated artistic and relational activity. ABC names persevering art and deepening artistic work, but also blocked love activity and restricted joy at work. Limited workplace peace can sit alongside deliberate efforts to consolidate harmony and work thoroughly for peace. A preference for earth work remains a distinct practical motif. The small circle is explicit; broader claims of loyalty or emotional warmth need not be supplied. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
