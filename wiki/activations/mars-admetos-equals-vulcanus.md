## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To experience mighty suppression. To become terrorized.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Working with primordial energies, blocked labor force, depressive will influence, restricted will force, curtailment of violence actions, enormous deepening into work, powerful blockage of work, and resistance to violence activity

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 319 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 202 (entry start).

- Core meaning: Force encounters blockage, suppression, or concentrated application.
- Psychology: Intimidation and a restricted will coexist with resistance to coercion.
- Body/health: Blocked working power is named, but no distinct bodily condition is established.
- Social/relationship: Witte's terrorization concerns coercive relations; ABC also allows resistance to violent activity.
- Events/manifestations: Powerful obstruction and intensive work with primordial energies are alternative expressions.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes suffering suppression; ABC also describes limiting violence and deepening work, without establishing that either must occur.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies the Mars/Admetos axis through the strength of pressure and resistance. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes overwhelming suppression and terrorization, while [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) extends the picture to blocked working power, curtailing violent action, and an enormous deepening of work. The organizing theme is force constrained or concentrated; the evidence does not reduce every expression to domination.
