## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Admetos = Zeus`: moving old things out of the way, destruction used to prepare for something new, and becoming the cause of removal or wreckage.
- Chart expression: forceful clearing and destructive preparation, where ending something is treated as necessary space-making for the next phase.
