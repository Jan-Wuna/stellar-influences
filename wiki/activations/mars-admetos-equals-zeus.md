## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To want to make room. To move old things out of the way to make room for new things. To wreck things. To be the cause of destruction.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

enduring creative work, strenuous strike activity, work performance deepening, blocked will performances, fire blocked work, restriction of a leading activity, creative work with primordial material, and determined earth work

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 319 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 202 (entry start).

- Core meaning: Work is cleared, concentrated, or obstructed in a creative undertaking.
- Psychology: Determined effort appears alongside blocked performance of the will.
- Body/health: These entries concern work and destruction without specifying a bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: ABC includes strike activity and restriction of a leading role.
- Events/manifestations: Witte describes removing old things or wrecking them; ABC adds earthwork, persistent creation, and work blocked by fire.
- Conflicts/notes: Destructive clearing and sustained creation are different manifestations, not proof that destruction is always necessary for renewal.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies the Mars/Admetos axis as concentrated creative effort that may require clearing material away or may itself be blocked. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) supplies the image of removing old things to make room, including wreckage; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also preserves sustained creative work, determined earthwork, and restrictions on performance. These entries support both building through persistence and destructive clearance, without making destruction a universal prerequisite.
