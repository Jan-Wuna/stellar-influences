## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Finish of a scientific dissertation. Satisfaction over concluded business transactions or about activities with a few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Limitation of increasing work, restricted scientific work, end of successful work, success through sustained work, creating thorough knowledge, creating a trade blockade, and increased work against depression.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 317, PDF p. 157 (exact activator row); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 201 (exact activator row).

- Core meaning: Expanding scientific or commercial work reaches a limit, conclusion, or result through persistence.
- Psychology: Satisfaction in completion accompanies perseverance under restriction.
- Body/health: ABC mentions work against depression; it does not establish a clinical condition or treatment effect.
- Social/relationship: Witte includes completed transactions and activity with a small number of people.
- Events/manifestations: A finished dissertation, a concluded deal, and a trade blockade illustrate different kinds of closure.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's thorough knowledge and sustained work qualify restriction with productive concentration; its depression phrase remains an attributed claim.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies the Mars/Apollon axis by bringing broad scientific or commercial activity to a boundary. That boundary may be a satisfactory completion, such as a dissertation or transaction in [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), or a restriction on further work in [ABC Entry](#abc-entry). Persistence can still produce success within those limits; the page therefore supports productive closure and obstruction as distinct possibilities.
