## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Independent work in science and commerce. Leading activity in science and trade.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of self employed work, special impulses of science, successful work of the state, success will of the authorities, self employed scientific work, self confidence through economic success, and primary scientific activity

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 317 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 201 (entry start).

- Core meaning: Scientific and commercial activity becomes independent, leading, or connected with state work.
- Psychology: ABC ties self-confidence to economic success and records authorities' will to succeed.
- Body/health: The entries concern leadership and work, without a distinct bodily claim.
- Social/relationship: Independent work and successful work by the state identify different institutional settings.
- Events/manifestations: Leading trade, self-employed science, and expansion of independent work are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries support leadership and independence; they do not establish unrestricted command over productive systems.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies the Mars/Apollon axis as independent or leading activity in commerce and science. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) supplies that central role, and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) extends it to expanding self-employed work, successful state work, and confidence arising from economic achievement. Authority is expressed through the conduct and position of the work, without an added claim of total control.
