## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Bold, open minded, cheerful. To unfold large, peaceful scientific or trade activity. Generous character. Ready to act. Comprehensive activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful treatment of the soul, my will for peace, my will for freedom, my work in the economy, my work expands, my successful work, my activity in trade, my activity in science, and my increasing drive.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 316, PDF p. 157 (exact activator row); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 201 (exact activator row).

- Core meaning: Broad scientific and commercial activity is experienced as one's own successful work.
- Psychology: Witte describes boldness, cheerfulness, generosity, and readiness to act; ABC includes a wish for freedom.
- Body/health: ABC 'successful soul treatment' is a historical source phrase, not a demonstrated therapeutic effect.
- Social/relationship: Peaceful trade and scientific activity provide the social setting.
- Events/manifestations: Expansion of one's work and comprehensive activity are explicit manifestations.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's treatment-of-the-soul phrase remains a historical claim; the work, peace and freedom clauses do not establish therapeutic efficacy.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies the Mars/Apollon axis as one's own broad and successful activity. Readiness to act, openness, and generosity in [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) connect with expanding personal work in the economy, trade or science and a will for peace in [ABC Entry](#abc-entry). The central picture is cheerful engagement in a wide undertaking; the isolated treatment phrase does not establish a clinical outcome.
