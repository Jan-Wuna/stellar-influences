## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Dissolution or failures of scientific or commercial activities. To explore unknown fields in science and trade.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Extended activity in air or water, disappointing work experience, creating metaphysical knowledge, uncertain peace work, hidden scientific work, working on future knowledge, and increasing weakness of will.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 201 (exact activator row); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 317, PDF p. 157 (exact activator row).

- Core meaning: Scientific or commercial activity can disperse or fail, or explore an unknown field.
- Psychology: ABC names disappointing work experience and increasing weakness of will.
- Body/health: ABC includes activity in air or water; this is a work setting, not a bodily diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Uncertain peace work is named without a specific relationship scenario.
- Events/manifestations: Witte gives commercial or scientific failure and exploration; ABC adds hidden scientific and metaphysical work.
- Conflicts/notes: Neptune's seven ABC clauses end with increasing weakness of will. The following Pluto row concerns development and supplies no evidence for this formula.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies the Mars/Apollon axis through work whose outcome or field is uncertain. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) connects failed or dissolving undertakings with exploration of unknown areas of science and trade. [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also names hidden scientific work, metaphysical or future knowledge, disappointing experience and weakening will. The uncertain field supports inquiry as well as a loss of coherence; the separate Pluto development row adds no meaning to this orientation.
