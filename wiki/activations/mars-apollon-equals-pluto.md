## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To plan the beginning of a peaceful or comprehensive activity in the industrial and scientific field. Rising or falling trend of the market.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Economic activity, development of scientific activity, evolution work of science, increasing work change, and creating growing knowledge

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 201 (entry start); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 317 (entry).

- Core meaning: Scientific and industrial work develops, changes, or is planned at its beginning.
- Psychology: Planning is explicit; a claim of extraordinary personal power is not.
- Body/health: These entries address economic and scientific change, without a bodily claim.
- Social/relationship: Industrial and economic undertakings supply the collective work setting.
- Events/manifestations: New undertakings, changing work, growing knowledge, and rising or falling markets are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Both directions of market movement are retained; 'decisive restructuring' went beyond the entry.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies the Mars/Apollon axis through the development and changing scope of scientific, industrial, or economic work. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) names planning the beginning of a comprehensive undertaking and markets moving either up or down; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds unfolding scientific activity, changes in work, and growing knowledge. The theme is work in development, without assuming that the change must be dramatic or financially favourable.
