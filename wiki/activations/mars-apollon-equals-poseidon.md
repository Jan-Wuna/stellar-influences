## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Business practices, business customs, business moral in trade, commerce or industry. Mental property. Mental freedom. Scientific colleagues.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work in the mind science, expansion of mental work, successful spiritual activity, successful proliferation of truth, cognition work of science, commercial reason acts, clear knowledge impulses, and scientific culture work

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 201 (entry start); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 317 (entry).

- Core meaning: Scientific and commercial activity concerns knowledge, intellectual freedom, and standards of conduct.
- Psychology: Clear understanding and freedom of thought recur.
- Body/health: The entries describe mental or spiritual work without establishing a health effect.
- Social/relationship: Scientific colleagues and business customs place understanding within working relationships.
- Events/manifestations: Intellectual property, cultural scientific work, spiritual activity, and business practices are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: Business morality is a topic in Witte, not proof that every action is ethical; spiritual success remains a source claim.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies the Mars/Apollon axis through the ideas and standards that guide intellectual and commercial work. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) joins business customs and morality to intellectual property, freedom, and scientific colleagues; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds the expansion of mental work, knowledge, and cultural or spiritual activity. The shared question is how work expresses understanding and principle, without certifying the truth or virtue of a particular undertaking.
