## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Merchant or scientist. The extensive daily activity. The peacefully working man. The man who widens or enlarges his field of work, who goes eagerly and cheerfully to work, and accomplishes much.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful actions of the man, the partner's will for freedom, a person's will for peace, a person active in trade, personal freedom of will, increased personal drive, much physical work, and a scientific work centre.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 201 (exact activator row); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 316, PDF p. 157 (exact activator row).

- Core meaning: Broad scientific or commercial activity becomes a person's extensive daily work.
- Psychology: Witte describes eagerness, cheerfulness, and peaceful work; ABC includes personal freedom of will.
- Body/health: ABC names much physical work; this describes activity without establishing a bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: A merchant, scientist, or partner appears in historical, sometimes male-specific wording.
- Events/manifestations: Widening a field of work and accomplishing much are Witte's concrete examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Gendered 'man' wording belongs to the sources; the formula does not establish an exclusive male capacity.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies the Mars/Apollon axis as a person's extensive and effective daily activity. The merchant or scientist in [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) works eagerly, peacefully, and cheerfully while widening the field of work; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds personal initiative, freedom of will, and much physical work. These occupational examples support productive breadth without making the historical male wording a universal rule or turning physical work into a health claim.
