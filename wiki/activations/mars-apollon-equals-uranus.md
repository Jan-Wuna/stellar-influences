## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden disturbance, surprises or excitements in trade or science. Sudden extended activity in these fields.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting scientific activity, dynamic will for freedom, successful work rhythm, successful will for reform, creating new knowledge, sudden peace activity, restless commercial work, increased technical work, and much new work.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 201 (exact activator row); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 317, PDF p. 157 (exact activator row).

- Core meaning: Scientific or commercial activity changes suddenly or expands with excitement.
- Psychology: ABC includes restlessness, a will to reform, and a wish for freedom.
- Body/health: The entries describe work rhythm and activity without specifying a bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: Trade, science, and sudden peace activity are the stated social fields.
- Events/manifestations: Surprises, sudden expansion, new knowledge, and increased technical work are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: Much new work and reform in ABC coexist with Witte's disturbance; surprise does not by itself ensure a favorable result.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies the Mars/Apollon axis through sudden changes in the tempo and scope of work. Surprises, excitement, and abrupt extension in [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) connect with technical work, new knowledge, and reform in [ABC Entry](#abc-entry). The page supports an unsettled or rapidly changing undertaking, without assuming either a breakthrough or a market shock in every case.
