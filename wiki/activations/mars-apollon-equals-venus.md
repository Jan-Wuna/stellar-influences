## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Close acquaintance and friendship with many or with persons from trade, industry and commerce.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Efforts toward knowledge of art, successful peace work, artistic work experience, artistic work success, experiencing acts of love, increased activity in love, will for free love, and scientific work on love.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 201 (exact activator row); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 316, PDF p. 157 (exact activator row).

- Core meaning: Active scientific or commercial life is connected with friendship, art, love, or peace work.
- Psychology: Enjoyment of artistic work and a wish for free love appear in ABC.
- Body/health: The entries do not establish a bodily or medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Close acquaintances and friendships with many, including business people, are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic achievement, peace work, and scientific study of love extend the occupational setting.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's effort to gain knowledge of art adds a learning motif to artistic achievement; love and friendship remain distinct examples.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies the Mars/Apollon axis through friendly and creative participation in a broad field of work. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes close acquaintances and friendships in trade and industry; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also includes artistic work, peace work, and activity or study concerning love. The connection is social and creative engagement within enterprise, with those distinct examples preserved rather than reduced to pleasant business manners.
