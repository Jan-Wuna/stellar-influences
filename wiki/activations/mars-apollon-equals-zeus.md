## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Scientific creations. Technicians. Technical products.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Strenuous scientific activity, successful procreative activity, leading commercial activity, leadership work in the economy, creative scientific work, extensive work performance, increased performance of the will, and purposeful peace work.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 201 (exact activator row); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 317, PDF p. 157 (exact activator row).

- Core meaning: Scientific work takes a creative, productive, or technical form.
- Psychology: Strenuous effort and directed will accompany creation in ABC.
- Body/health: ABC's procreation example is an attributed historical claim, not evidence of fertility.
- Social/relationship: ABC includes leading business activity and determined peace work.
- Events/manifestations: Scientific creations, technicians, technical products, and extensive work performance are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC includes leadership work in the economy alongside scientific creation; its procreative example remains an attributed historical claim.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies the Mars/Apollon axis through creative work that produces scientific or technical results. The creations, technicians, and products in [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) connect with strenuous scientific work and extensive performance in [ABC Entry](#abc-entry). Leadership work in the economy and purposeful peace work broaden the examples beyond technical production.
