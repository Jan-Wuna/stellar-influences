## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be depressed or oppressed by others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work blockade in the environment, long term partner work, depressive partner impulses, limited activity at the site, consolidation of the partner's will, thorough work partner, restricted work for the environment, and deepening in environmental work

- Source reference: printed p. 111 (PDF p. 54), Ascendant + Mars, Admetos row; English checked against the scanned German.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 85 (entry start); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 111 (entry).

- Core meaning: Work with others is blocked, restricted, or sustained through concentration.
- Psychology: Oppression and depressive impulses coexist with a consolidated partner's will.
- Body/health: Depression is historical source wording; no clinical diagnosis is established.
- Social/relationship: Partners can obstruct work or persevere together in it.
- Events/manifestations: Local work blockades, limited activity, and deepened work in one's surroundings are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: The scan confirms a thorough work partner; endurance and concentration qualify a solely oppressive reading.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established for this exact orientation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies the Mars/Asc axis through the limits placed on activity with others. Being oppressed by others in [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) is one expression, but [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also preserves sustained partner work, a strengthened will, and deepening work in the surroundings. The picture therefore includes both interpersonal obstruction and enduring concentration within a restricted setting.
