## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Your energy helps others to take charge of their identity more, so that they are induced to identify with positive constructiveness, as well as realizing and changing those aspects of their identity which are self-destructive and non life-supporting.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 75 (chapter start only).

- Core meaning: One's energy helps others take greater charge of identity.
- Psychology: Sandbach describes recognizing and changing self-destructive identifications.
- Body/health: The entry concerns identity and constructive conduct, not a medical healing claim.
- Social/relationship: An influence on others encourages their own constructive self-definition.
- Events/manifestations: Realizing and changing damaging aspects of identity is the stated process.
- Conflicts/notes: This single Sandbach passage does not establish clinical healing or a right to force others to change; its locator is only a chapter start.

## Contradictions

Only one source passage is present; no comparison between independent sources can be made.

## Derived Synthesis

Chiron specifies the Mars/Asc axis as energy that encourages others to assume responsibility for their identity. In [Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry), they recognize damaging identifications and move toward constructive, life-supporting expression. The claim concerns an interpersonal influence and the other's change; the one-source passage does not establish a medical effect or justify coercive self-correction.
