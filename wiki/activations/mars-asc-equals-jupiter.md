## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Asc + Mars`
- Source page: `85`
#### Witte Entry

To be forced by others into one’s own good fortune. To become engaged or betrothed. Other people are successful. Advantages and profits through the action, deeds or accomplishments of others.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Mars/Ascendant`
- Source page: `220`
- Entry: `0763`

#### Ebertin Entry

Harmonious teamwork or cooperation, ability to lead others, good organizer, agreement with others on the pursuit of the same objectives. Engagement, marriage.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Mars/Ascendant midpoint`
- Source page: `75`
- Activator: `Jupiter`

#### Sandbach Entry

Enthusiasm expressed with assertiveness. Confidence in one’s ability to get things done. A forceful identity may be expressed with so much energy that it over whelms other people
