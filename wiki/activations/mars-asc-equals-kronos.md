## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be punished by the authorities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special force for the environment, special will of the partner, actions of the state in the place, steering work for the environment, partner work with the boss, independent actions of the partner, and important environmental actions

- Source reference: printed p. 111 (PDF p. 54), Ascendant + Mars, Kronos row; English checked against the scanned German.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 85 (entry start); [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 111 (entry).

- Core meaning: Actions involving others take place through authority, direction, or a leading role.
- Psychology: ABC names the partner's particular will and independent actions.
- Body/health: These entries do not specify a bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: State action, work with a boss, and punishment by authorities are distinct examples.
- Events/manifestations: Directing work for the surroundings and important local actions are explicit.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes punishment; ABC also allows work with authority and independent partner action. The scan verifies 'selbständige', repairing 'self same'.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis; the different manifestations do not establish a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies the Mars/Asc axis through authority and direction in activity with others. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) gives punishment by authorities as one manifestation, while the scan-checked [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also includes state action, work with a boss, and independent actions of a partner. The evidence includes both being subject to authority and working through a directing role.
