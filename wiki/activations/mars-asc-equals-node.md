## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To exert charm of sex upon a woman. To enter into a sex union. To be called upon, to be asked. In connection with others under compulsion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work connections for the environment, connections to the other through work, contact work of the partner, contact actions in the environment, effective connections for the environment, connecting partner activity, and linking with the work partner

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Comradeship and good fellowship, teamwork, the guidance of others. A union based on physical attraction.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The need to develop more effective ways of asserting the self, and to find con nections with others wherein one can be productive.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Prompts you to seek those who are similarly active and energetic; brings urges for you to find friends who share your attitudes and opinions and will work with you to develop these; brings a need to understand motives.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Asc = Node`: comradeship, productive partnership, strong mutual pull, and the search for people who can match one's energy and support decisive action. Main caution: compulsion in relating: connection can become driven by pressure, utility, or raw attraction more than emotional balance.
