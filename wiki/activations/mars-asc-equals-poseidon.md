## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The moral behavior of other people. To experience comprehensive through others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Spiritual or mental work for the surroundings, recognizing the actions of others, ideal actions at the place, wanting clarity for the surroundings, the partner's cultural work, cultural activity at the place, sensible impulses from the partner, and rational actions for the surroundings.

- Source reference: printed p. 111, PDF p. 54, Poseidon row; English translation checked against the German scan.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity with others is considered through moral behavior, clarity and cultural or mental work.
- psychology: Recognizing others' actions and seeking clarity are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is stated.
- social/relationship: The partner's cultural work and sensible impulses are named.
- events/manifestations: Local cultural activity and rational action for the surroundings are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's phrase 'To experience comprehensive through others' is printed as shown on PDF p. 85 and remains linguistically unclear.

## Contradictions

Moral conduct and cultural or rational activity are different emphases. Witte's unclear final phrase cannot establish an additional shared meaning.

## Derived Synthesis

`Mars/Asc = Poseidon` specifies action with others through its moral and mental quality. [Witte](#witte-entry) names other people's moral behavior; the scan-checked [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives this a practical setting in clarity, sensible partner impulses, cultural work and rational action for the surroundings. The formula concerns recognizing and carrying out those kinds of activity without making every action by another person an ethical lesson.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 85; ABC printed p. 111, PDF p. 54. Witte's final printed phrase remains unclear and is not used to infer understanding or instruction.
