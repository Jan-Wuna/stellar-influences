## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be a co-worker or to have them. To get a thrashing. To be beaten up.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work centre at the place, a person active for the surroundings, actions of other people, physical work with others, men's work for the surroundings, personal activity for the surroundings, activity of other people, and another person's energy for action.

- Source reference: printed p. 110, PDF p. 54, Sun row; English checked against the German scan, including `körperliche Arbeit` (physical work).

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Excitability, love of fighting, intense teamwork. Excitement, quarrels, mishaps.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: “Planets activating the Mars/Ascendant midpoint,” PDF p. 76 (complete entry).

One feels that it is important to strongly and assertively project who one is. One’s goals are positively affected by coming across assertively, but if this is too extreme the goals can be negatively affected. One feels self-confident about his ability to do things. One’s self-esteem is strongly based on what one accomplishes.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `248`

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds to the personal belief that you have sufficient energy to succeed in any endeavor; helps you develop your personal or attitudinal strength; enlivens the force of your personality; helps you focus your will power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal energy and accomplishment take shape through action with others.
- psychology: Confidence and self-esteem depend on what one can do; excessive assertion can hinder goals.
- body/health: ABC names physical work; Witte includes being beaten, without establishing a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Co-workers and intense teamwork coexist with quarrels and fighting.
- events/manifestations: Work for the surroundings, excitement and mishaps are explicit examples.
- conflicts/notes: Helpful assertion and excessive assertion are conditional expressions; ABC's gendered work example remains historical source wording.

## Contradictions

The entries include both effective assertion and conflict. Sandbach explicitly distinguishes assertion that helps one's goals from excess that harms them; this is a qualification rather than a contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies the Mars/Asc axis through personal energy invested in action with others. Co-workers in [Witte](#witte-entry), physical work in [ABC](#abc-entry), and intense teamwork in [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) give this a practical setting. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) connects activity with confidence and focused will, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) makes accomplishment central to self-esteem and distinguishes useful assertion from excess that undermines one's goals. The entries also retain fighting, quarrels and mishaps; confidence alone does not establish a favorable result.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 85; ABC printed p. 110, PDF p. 54; Ebertin printed p. 220, code 0759; Sandbach PDF p. 76 (complete entry); Munkasey printed p. 248.
