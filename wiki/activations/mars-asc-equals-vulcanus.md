## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others act on their own authority. Violent deeds of others. To be exposed to grave threats of others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work capacity for the surroundings, energy impulses from the surroundings, violent activity at the place, enormous influence of others, powerful actions for the surroundings, powerful energy for action at the place, powerful actions of others, and strong efforts for the surroundings.

- Source reference: printed p. 111, PDF p. 54, Vulcanus row; the scan reads `starke Umwelt-Bemühungen`, strong efforts for the surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action involving others carries great force or influence.
- psychology: The entries emphasize forceful action without describing a distinct inner attitude.
- body/health: Violence and grave threats are named, without a specific medical condition.
- social/relationship: Others' independent authority, influence and powerful actions are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Strong work efforts coexist with violent local deeds and threats.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's forceful work extends the range beyond threat; strong effort is not necessarily violence.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes threats and violent deeds; ABC also names work capacity and strong efforts. This is a difference of range rather than a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies the Mars/Asc axis through the force of action involving other people and one's surroundings. [ABC](#abc-entry) includes powerful work capacity, influence and strong efforts, while [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes others acting on their own authority, violent deeds and grave threats. The common theme is forceful action; the entries retain both work and danger without equating all strength with coercion.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 85; ABC printed p. 111, PDF p. 54.
