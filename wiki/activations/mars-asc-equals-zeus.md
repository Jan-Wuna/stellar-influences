## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be active as a producer. Others exert pressure or compulsion. Injuries by fire or through fire-arms.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Effortful work on the spot, others lead at work, start work on the spot, work design with others, work performance in the environment, actions of local leadership, creative partner work, will direction of others, and procreation will of the partner

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action with others is directed toward production, work and creation.
- psychology: Others' direction of will and the partner's will to procreate are ABC examples.
- body/health: Witte names injuries by fire or firearms as historical source possibilities.
- social/relationship: Work leadership, creative partnership and pressure from others are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Beginning work, joint work design and acting as a producer are named.
- conflicts/notes: Production and creative work need not involve compulsion or injury; these remain distinct manifestations.

## Contradictions

The entries combine productive and creative activity with pressure and injury. They do not claim that productive cooperation must involve force.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies the Mars/Asc axis through directed production and work with others. Acting as a producer in [Witte](#witte-entry) connects with beginning work, joint work design, leadership and creative partnership in [ABC](#abc-entry). Witte also names pressure or compulsion and injuries through fire or firearms. These retain a difficult part of the entry's range without turning every productive undertaking into forced work or danger.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 85; ABC printed p. 111, PDF p. 54.
