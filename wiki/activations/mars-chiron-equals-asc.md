## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One emanates an active, stimulating aura that can help others to be more assertive and deal with life in a more constructive manner.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: One's active presence can encourage constructive action in others.
- psychology: Greater assertiveness is the stated response.
- body/health: No bodily condition or medical healing is specified.
- social/relationship: The entry concerns the effect of one's stimulating presence on others.
- events/manifestations: Helping others deal with life constructively is stated without a concrete event.
- conflicts/notes: The source says this can help; it does not establish a universal effect or a harmful excess.

## Contradictions

Only one short source entry is present; it offers a possibility, not a comparison of independent accounts.

## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies the Mars/Chiron axis through the effect of one's presence on others. In the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry), an active, stimulating presence can encourage greater assertiveness and a more constructive response to life. This short passage supports encouragement through contact, without specifying medical healing or a negative consequence of being too forceful.

Source locator: Sandbach PDF p. 69 is the Mars/Chiron chapter start; the exact entry page has not been verified.
