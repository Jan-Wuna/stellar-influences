## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s mission in this life is simply to do what needs to be done, and to base this on deeply listening to one’s spiritual self and then acting on its guidance.

- core meaning: Needed action is guided by listening to one's spiritual self.

## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies the Mars/Chiron axis through a sense of mission expressed in needed action. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) places deep listening to one's spiritual self before acting on its guidance. Its emphasis is practical response to that guidance; the passage does not identify a particular occupation or public role.
