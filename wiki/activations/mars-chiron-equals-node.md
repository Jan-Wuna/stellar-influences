## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The need to tune in to one’s personal spiritual guidance so that one will know what one needs to do to overcome one’s limiting ties to the past and assertively move forward.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Spiritual guidance informs action beyond limiting ties to the past.
- psychology: Tuning in to personal guidance is the stated need.
- body/health: No bodily condition or medical process is specified.
- social/relationship: Ties are described as limiting, without identifying a person or relationship.
- events/manifestations: Assertive forward movement is named without a concrete event.
- conflicts/notes: Overcoming limiting ties does not by itself specify separation or a new relationship.

## Contradictions

One source entry is present; it connects guidance with moving beyond past limitations without a competing claim.

## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies the Mars/Chiron axis through action that overcomes limiting ties to the past. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) makes personal spiritual guidance the basis for knowing what to do and moving forward assertively. It identifies the limitation and direction of action without specifying a separation, conflict or new connection.

Source locator: Sandbach PDF p. 69 is the Mars/Chiron chapter start; the exact entry page has not been verified.
