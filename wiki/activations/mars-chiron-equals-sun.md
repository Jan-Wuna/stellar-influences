#### Sandbach Entry

One is inspired by the Spiritual Light to act, and needs to not repress those impulses when felt. The ability to bring to light the real and true nature of conflicts and what they have to teach us.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Acting on spiritual inspiration and illuminating conflict.
- Psychology: Recognizing and expressing an impulse to act rather than repressing it.
- Body/health: Not addressed in this entry.
- Social/relationship: Conflicts can reveal something worth learning.
- Events/manifestations: Bringing the nature and lesson of a conflict into view.
- Conflicts/notes: The spiritual framing belongs to Sandbach's account.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Chiron = Sun`, Sun specifies action inspired by what Sandbach calls the Spiritual Light. The emphasis is on allowing that impulse expression and bringing the true nature of a conflict into view, so that its lesson can be understood. The entry does not say that suppressed impulses inevitably return as stronger conflict. [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry)
