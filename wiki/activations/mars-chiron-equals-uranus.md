#### Sandbach Entry

A unique and inspired way of taking spiritual action. One need to be free and independent in carrying out the actions dictated by one’s High Self.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Chiron = Uranus`, Uranus specifies a unique and inspired manner of spiritual action. Freedom matters because the person needs room to carry out the guidance of what Sandbach calls the High Self. The passage supports independent action, without describing rebellion or damage to established structures. [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry)
