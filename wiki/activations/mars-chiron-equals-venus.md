### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. `70` (complete oriented entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `69`; raw scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

One sees the deeper meaning and purpose of conflicts in relationships, and is able to bring healing to them. An appreciation of how conflicts serve to advance us spiritually.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Understanding and healing conflict in relationships.
- Psychology: Appreciating the deeper purpose of a conflict.
- Body/health: Healing is used in a relational and spiritual sense; no physical condition is given.
- Social/relationship: Bringing insight and repair to relationship difficulties.
- Events/manifestations: A conflict becomes an occasion for spiritual development.
- Conflicts/notes: The entry describes a capacity, not a guarantee that every conflict is beneficial.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present; it does not establish agreement across sources.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Chiron = Venus`, Venus specifies the ability to understand and heal conflict within relationships. Seeing a difficulty's deeper purpose can make it part of spiritual development. This is the capacity described by the entry, rather than a claim that every troubled relationship necessarily improves through conflict. [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry)
