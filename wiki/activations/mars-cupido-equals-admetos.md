## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 197.

Going one’s own way in a community, but keeping connections alive for the sake of pretence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 309; PDF page: 153.

Perseverance in common work, blockade of common actions, depressed work community, restriction of the work of a community, consolidation of a common will, thorough actions for the whole, and deepening of joint activity

Translation checked against the Admetos row: “Festigung eines gemeinsamen Willens” means consolidation of a common will.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Restricted or deepened participation in shared work.
- Psychology: Perseverance, discouragement, or maintaining appearances.
- Body/health: No physical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Remaining connected while going one's own way.
- Events/manifestations: Blocked joint action, sustained common work, or a firmer shared will.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes pretence; ABC also allows constructive endurance and depth.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis: outward connection without shared direction in Witte, restriction and perseverance in ABC. These are distinct possibilities, not a single inevitable outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Cupido = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the limits and persistence of shared activity. Cooperation can become blocked or discouraged; a person may remain outwardly connected while following a separate course. The same entry set also allows perseverance, a consolidated common will, and deeper joint work, so restriction does not exhaust its meaning. [Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry)
