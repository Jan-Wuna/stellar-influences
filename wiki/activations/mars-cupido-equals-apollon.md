## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Extensive cooperation and growth in shared work.
- Psychology: Learning through joint effort and commitment to knowledge.
- Body/health: Not addressed by these entries.
- Social/relationship: Work partnerships with many people; Witte also gives mass marriage.
- Events/manifestations: Expansion of a working community and scientific collaboration.
- Conflicts/notes: Public or corporate status is not required by the passages.

## Contradictions

The entries connect many participants with expanded joint work; ABC develops its educational and scientific forms while Witte adds mass marriage.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Cupido = Apollon`, Apollon specifies shared work involving many people or an expanding community. Experience and knowledge grow through cooperation, with scientific and scholarly work among the stated examples. Witte's separate example of mass marriage extends the collective setting to marriage without making every collaboration a marital one. [Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry)
