## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source page: 308; PDF page: 153.

shared workplace, community work at the place, shared actions of others, production of a partner community, activity of a local community, environmental work of a community, and will of a local community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared activity with other people in an immediate community.
- Psychology: Common interests; no fuller psychological account is supplied.
- Body/health: Not addressed by these entries.
- Social/relationship: Association, partnership, and the marriages of others.
- Events/manifestations: A shared workplace, local community work, or a connection leading to marriage.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries do not promise immediate rapport or an inevitable marriage.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes personal associations and marriage; ABC emphasizes the local setting of shared work. These are compatible but distinct examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Cupido = Asc`, Asc specifies shared activity through nearby people, common interests, and a local working community. Personal association can lead to marriage in Witte's examples, while ABC also places the formula in shared workplaces and collective work for the surrounding community. These passages describe possible forms of association, without promising effortless rapport. [Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry)
