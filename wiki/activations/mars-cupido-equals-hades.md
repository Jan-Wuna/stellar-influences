## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 197.

Common work with an evil end. End and misfortune in working with others. Misfortune in a community or family. Sickness and misery in married life. End of matrimony. Misfortune through unusual death.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 309; PDF page: 153.

Community action in need, dangerous actions of a community, common secret activity, collective renunciation of will, criminal work community, deficient community will, and deficient work of a community

Translation checked against the Hades row: “gemeinsamer Willens-Verzicht” denotes collective renunciation of will, not abuse of will.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared work or communal life under harmful or deficient conditions.
- Psychology: Renunciation of shared will appears in ABC.
- Body/health: Witte historically lists sickness and unusual death; these are attributed examples, not diagnoses.
- Social/relationship: Misfortune in family or community, including the ending of marriage.
- Events/manifestations: Dangerous, secret, criminal, or unsuccessful joint activity.
- Conflicts/notes: Distress affecting a community and wrongdoing by that community remain different possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses misfortune and endings; ABC includes communal need, secrecy, and deficient or dangerous activity. Neither entry requires all of these to occur together.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Cupido = Hades`, Hades specifies shared activity burdened by need, poor conditions, or harm. A community may suffer misfortune or carry out dangerous and secret actions; in family and marriage, Witte gives misery and endings as historical manifestations. The formula's range includes both hardship suffered together and destructive cooperation, which should remain distinguishable. [Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry)
