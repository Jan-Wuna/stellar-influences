## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 197.

Study group or community working under protection of the state. Rifle or artillery practice. The married life of the father.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 309; PDF page: 153.

A distinctive working community, actions of the state for the community, independent work within the community, an independent artistic community, a state working community, the energy of a self-confident community, and important communal activity.

Translation repaired from the Kronos row, beginning “Besondere Arbeits-Gemeinschaft”; the previous repetitive European Communities wording did not represent this passage.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Important, independent, or state-supported shared work.
- Psychology: Self-confidence and initiative within a community.
- Body/health: Not addressed by these entries.
- Social/relationship: A protected study or working community; Witte also mentions the father's married life.
- Events/manifestations: State action for a community, independent artistic work, and military practice.
- Conflicts/notes: State protection and independence are different examples within the source range.

## Contradictions

Both entries include the state in communal work. ABC also stresses independent work, while Witte adds specific family and military examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Cupido = Kronos`, Kronos specifies a working community through importance, independence, or support from the state. The examples include protected study groups, independent artistic cooperation, and official training. Witte also names the father's married life, a separate family example rather than evidence that every group must be hierarchical. [Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry)
