## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Joint activity with others in public life. Woman’s marriage. Mother’s remarriage.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Actions of a women's community, emotional impulses of a community, shared emotional actions, actions of a people's community, artistic activity of a woman, a people's will toward community, a community of shared will among a people, and working together among the people.

- Source reference: printed p. 308, PDF p. 153, Moon row; the scan explicitly includes `Gefühls-Impulse` and `Gefühls-Handlungen`.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared action takes a public and communal form, including emotional participation.
- psychology: ABC names emotional impulses and shared will.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Public joint activity, women's community and marriage are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Witte names a woman's marriage and a mother's remarriage; ABC includes artistic activity.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered examples remain historical source claims, not a rule assigning communal roles by gender.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes public activity and marriage; ABC emphasizes communal feeling and will. These are different examples rather than a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies the Mars/Cupido axis through public and emotionally shared activity. [Witte](#witte-entry) names joint action in public life, while [ABC](#abc-entry) adds community impulses, emotional actions and shared will. Witte's marriage and remarriage examples and ABC's women's-community and artistic examples remain particular historical formulations; the common thread is participation in a collective undertaking.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 197; ABC printed p. 308, PDF p. 153.
