## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

End or dissolution of a joint or artistic activity. Marriage intentions have faded away.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A work community in the air or in water, a disappointed work community, shared actions in the air or in water, shared activities in metaphysics, uncertainty in a work community, and a community's work for the future.

- Source reference: printed p. 309, PDF p. 153, Neptune row; the scan supplies six distinct phrases rather than repeated “working conditions.”

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared activity may become uncertain, disappointed or dissolved.
- psychology: Disappointment and uncertainty within a work community are explicit.
- body/health: No medical or bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Joint work and faded marriage intentions are named.
- events/manifestations: Dissolution of artistic activity coexists with ABC's air, water, metaphysical and future-oriented work.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's fields of work extend the range beyond dissolution; the repeated cached “working conditions” wording was corrupt.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes activity ending; ABC also supplies settings for continuing shared work. The entries do not make dissolution inevitable in those settings.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies the Mars/Cupido axis through uncertainty or disappointment in shared work. [Witte](#witte-entry) names the ending of joint or artistic activity and faded marriage intentions; [ABC](#abc-entry) retains uncertainty and disappointment while also naming shared work in air, water, metaphysics or preparation for the future. These practical settings broaden the entry beyond dissolution without establishing success for a particular undertaking.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 197; ABC printed p. 309, PDF p. 153.
