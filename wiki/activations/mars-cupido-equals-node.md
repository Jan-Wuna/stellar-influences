## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Connection through common activity. Marital bonds.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Working contacts in the arts, actions for common encounters, joint contact work, establishing a common connection, connecting through common impulses, and establishing connections to the whole

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared activity creates connections and bonds.
- psychology: Common impulses support connection in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Marital bonds, artistic contacts and shared encounters are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Establishing a common connection is the stated action.
- conflicts/notes: These entries describe connection through activity without asserting its duration or emotional quality.

## Contradictions

The brief entries share a connection-through-activity theme; their different examples do not establish a disagreement.

## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies the Mars/Cupido axis through the bonds formed by shared activity. [Witte](#witte-entry) names connection through common action and marital bonds; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds artistic work contacts, shared encounters and connecting through common impulses. The evidence centers on making a connection, without specifying how long it lasts or excluding an emotional basis.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 197; ABC printed p. 308, PDF p. 153.
