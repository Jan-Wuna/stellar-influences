## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Building up of cooperative work. Plans for community work. Change in work for the purpose of art. Change in cooperative work. Marriage or wedding plans.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development work of a community, developing shared action, a changing work community, developing synthesis work, growing artistic activity, a growing will toward the whole, and transformation of a work community.

- Source reference: printed p. 309, PDF p. 153, Pluto row; the scan contains no “organization of markets” wording.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Cooperative work develops, grows or changes.
- psychology: ABC names a growing will toward the whole.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Community work and marriage or wedding plans are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Building cooperation, changing artistic work and developing shared action are named.
- conflicts/notes: Change is specified without a guarantee of success or a claim of coercion.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe development and change in shared activity; their particular examples do not establish a disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies the Mars/Cupido axis through the development and reorganization of cooperative activity. Building shared work and planning community work in [Witte](#witte-entry) connect with the changing work community and growing artistic activity in [ABC](#abc-entry). Witte also names marriage or wedding plans. The emphasis is on forming and changing a common undertaking, without specifying a necessary outcome for it.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 197; ABC printed p. 309, PDF p. 153.
