## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Passing, short or periodic working together. End of a collaboration. End of a working community. Death terminates marriage. A broken promise to marry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Tasks of a work community, burdensome cooperation, serious shared creation, a separated work community, a hard community of will, concentrated work of a community, painful cooperation, and a community's work that brings losses.

- Source reference: printed p. 309, PDF p. 153, Saturn row; `ernstes gemeinsames Schaffen` means serious shared creation, not first joint work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Cooperation is limited, burdensome, concentrated or brought to an end.
- psychology: Seriousness and a hard shared will are ABC themes.
- body/health: Witte names death ending marriage; the entry does not establish a medical prediction.
- social/relationship: Short collaboration, separated work groups and broken marriage promises are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Ending a collaboration, difficult shared tasks and losses through work are named.
- conflicts/notes: Concentrated work remains part of the range; limitation does not mean every cooperation ends in loss.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes brief or ended cooperation; ABC also includes serious, concentrated work. These describe different forms of limited or difficult joint activity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies the Mars/Cupido axis through limits and burdens in shared activity. Brief or ended collaboration in [Witte](#witte-entry) sits beside serious creation, concentrated work and painful cooperation in [ABC](#abc-entry). Witte also retains broken marriage promises and death terminating marriage as particular source examples. The range includes both sustained difficult work and separation, rather than one inevitable ending.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 197; ABC printed p. 309, PDF p. 153.
