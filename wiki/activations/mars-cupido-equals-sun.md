## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The daily joint work. Community work during the day. Man active in community, family or art. One’s own marriage (a man).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work centre of one community, common arts creation, man's will community, personal impulses to the whole, personal community action, activity in a arts centre, and center of a will community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal action participates in daily collective work.
- psychology: Personal impulses toward the whole and shared will are ABC themes.
- body/health: No bodily condition or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Community, family, artistic activity and a man's marriage are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Daily joint work and activity in an arts centre are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Historical male-specific language remains attributed; recognition or social status is not stated.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize daily participation and personal contribution to shared work. Their examples differ without a direct contradictory claim.

## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies the Mars/Cupido axis through personal participation in collective work. [Witte](#witte-entry) names daily joint work and activity in community, family or art; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds personal impulses and action toward the whole, including artistic work. Witte's male-specific marriage example remains historical source wording. Participation is explicit here, while public recognition is not.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 197; ABC printed p. 308, PDF p. 153.
