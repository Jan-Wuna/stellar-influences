## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Marriages. Working with others in general.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Community of labour in the public sphere, creation in a common public sphere, joint actions in the public sphere, public sphere of work together, will to the whole in the public sphere, and will community in the public sphere

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Cooperative action takes place publicly or involves others in general.
- psychology: A public shared will is an ABC theme.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Marriage and working with others are Witte's brief examples.
- events/manifestations: Public joint work, creation and action are explicit in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: Public activity is established; recognition, status and success are not.

## Contradictions

Witte supplies broad examples and ABC develops the public setting. The brief entries contain no direct disagreement.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies the Mars/Cupido axis through shared activity in the public sphere. [ABC](#abc-entry) repeatedly locates joint work, creation, action and shared will in that setting; [Witte](#witte-entry) names marriage and working with others in general. The entry supports public cooperation without establishing a particular social standing or result.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 197; ABC printed p. 308, PDF p. 153.
