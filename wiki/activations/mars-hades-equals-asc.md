## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Vulgar and evil acts of others. To have to set right the mistakes of others. Danger of being murdered, also danger of other people being murdered.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Lack of work at the place, dangerous impulses from the partner, a dangerous workplace, mysterious activity at the place, addressing local deficiencies, adverse actions of others, dirty work for the surroundings, and working on others' past.

- Source reference: printed p. 310, PDF p. 154, Ascendant row; the scan distinguishes addressing deficiencies from the separate phrase `nachteilige Handlungen anderer`.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult work and harmful actions involve one's surroundings or other people.
- psychology: No distinct inner attitude is established.
- body/health: Witte names danger of murder as a source possibility, not a predicted event.
- social/relationship: Correcting others' mistakes and dangerous partner impulses are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Work shortage, a dangerous workplace and addressing local deficiencies are ABC settings.
- conflicts/notes: Working on deficiencies or the past is distinct from committing wrongdoing; identity formation is not stated.

## Contradictions

The entries include both harmful acts and corrective work. Their shared setting among others does not make every act in that setting harmful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies the Mars/Hades axis through difficult work and adverse actions in one's immediate surroundings. Setting right others' mistakes in [Witte](#witte-entry) connects with addressing local deficiencies and working on others' past in [ABC](#abc-entry). Both retain danger involving others, including Witte's explicit murder warning. Corrective work and danger remain separate examples; the warning is an attributed source claim, not a forecast or a statement about personal identity.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 198; ABC printed p. 310, PDF p. 154.
