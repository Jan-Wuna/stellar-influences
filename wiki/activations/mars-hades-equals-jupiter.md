## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Bad results of work. Killing. Sentence. Capital punishment. Quick, unusual death.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Past work successes, successful will in need, joy in service work, money through criminal actions, dirty money trading, disadvantages through legal actions, and hidden success of an action.

- Source reference: printed p. 310, PDF p. 154, Jupiter row; the line break in `Rechts-Handlungen` means legal actions, not “right hand boys.”

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Outcomes of difficult or concealed action range from success to severe adverse consequences.
- psychology: ABC includes successful will in need and joy in service work.
- body/health: Witte's killing and death statements are historical claims, not medical predictions.
- social/relationship: Legal action, sentencing and criminal financial activity are explicit examples.
- events/manifestations: Past or hidden work success coexists with bad work results and capital punishment.
- conflicts/notes: Witte stresses bad results; ABC includes success as well as corrupt gain. The favorable examples must not be erased.

## Contradictions

Witte's adverse results and ABC's work successes differ materially in emphasis. The entries support a mixed range of outcomes; neither establishes the result of every action.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies the Mars/Hades axis through the results and gains of difficult or concealed action. [ABC](#abc-entry) includes past successes, effective will in need, joy in service and hidden success, alongside criminal money and disadvantages through legal action. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes bad work results, sentencing and fatal outcomes. This material difference prevents reducing the formula either to reward or to punishment; the severe examples remain source claims rather than forecasts.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 198; ABC printed p. 310, PDF p. 154.
