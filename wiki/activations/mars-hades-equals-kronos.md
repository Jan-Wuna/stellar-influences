## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Vicious and mean acts committed by leading personages, by the state, by the chief or by the government. Unusual death of the father. Murder instigated by the authorities. Judicial murder.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special energy for action in need, great lack of work, addressing state criminality, criminal actions of the state, lack of independent actions, state emergency actions, state service work, and important work in secret.

- Source reference: printed p. 311, PDF p. 154, Kronos row; `Mangel an selbständigen Handlungen` means lack of independent actions, not lack of self-defence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult or harmful action involves leadership, the state or important work.
- psychology: No distinct individual attitude is specified.
- body/health: Witte includes fatal events involving the father and authorities as source claims, not predictions.
- social/relationship: State service, emergency action and wrongdoing by leaders are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Work shortage, addressing state criminality and important secret work are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Addressing criminality is distinct from committing it; lack of independent action is not self-defence.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes abuse by leaders; ABC also includes emergency service and work addressing state crime. These different roles must remain distinct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies the Mars/Hades axis through difficult action involving leaders or the state. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes wrongdoing by authorities and severe fatal examples. [ABC](#abc-entry) retains state criminal acts but also names addressing state criminality, emergency service and important hidden work. The entry therefore includes both wrongdoing and work responding to difficult public conditions; its fatal statements remain attributed source claims.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 198 (printed p. 202); ABC printed p. 311, PDF p. 154.
