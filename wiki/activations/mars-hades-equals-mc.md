## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To suffer through mean acts of others. Quickly annoyed and weary. To be fed up and to have one’s fill. To be separated mentally through the mean actions of others. To want to revenge oneself. An eye for an eye. To be disposed to atrocities or murder. Ugly deeds which bounce back.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

I lack work, I want to renounce, I hide my will, my work in the past, my dangerous actions, my mysterious activity, my hidden work, my renunciation of activity, and hidden soul work.

- Source reference: printed p. 310, PDF p. 154, MC row; `Ich entbehre die Arbeit` means I lack work, and the final hidden soul work is not possessively qualified in the German.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: One personally experiences difficult actions, deprivation or concealed work.
- psychology: Weariness, annoyance and revenge are Witte themes; ABC includes hidden will and renunciation.
- body/health: Weariness is stated without a specific medical condition.
- social/relationship: Suffering through others' mean acts and mental separation from them are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Lack of work, hidden activity and dangerous personal actions are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's violent dispositions are historical source assertions; vocation and moral contamination are not established here.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize personal suffering and retaliation on one hand, concealment and renunciation on the other. These are different responses within the stated range.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies the Mars/Hades axis through one's own involvement in difficult or concealed action. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes suffering from others' mean acts, weariness and a desire for retaliation. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds lacking work, hiding one's will, renunciation and hidden activity. These entries distinguish inward withdrawal from retaliatory action; they do not identify a necessary occupation or make the severe violent examples a personal forecast.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 198 (printed p. 202); ABC printed p. 310, PDF p. 154.
