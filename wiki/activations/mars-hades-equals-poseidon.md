## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The moral behavior of lowbrow people. Services which demand little mental ability.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Clear impulses from the past, cultural work service, cultural creations in secret, defective cultural work, disadvantages by producing the truth, lack of mental power, hidden spiritual work, and working on the truth of the past

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult or hidden work concerns culture, truth, spiritual activity or mental demands.
- psychology: ABC includes clear impulses from the past and diminished mental drive.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte's class judgment remains attributed, while services with low mental demands are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Hidden cultural work and disadvantages from bringing forth truth are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: The entries include cultural and spiritual effort; absence of conscience or refinement is not established.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes class-based moral description and low-demand service; ABC adds hidden cultural and spiritual work. These emphases differ materially and should not be flattened into intellectual degradation.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies the Mars/Hades axis through the moral, mental and cultural dimensions of difficult work. [Witte](#witte-entry) names services requiring little mental ability and uses a historical class label. [ABC](#abc-entry) preserves a wider range: hidden cultural or spiritual work, truth concerning the past, and disadvantages in bringing truth forth. The entry therefore cannot be reduced to absent conscience or shallow thought; some of its work is concealed or disadvantaged rather than intellectually empty.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 198 (printed p. 202); ABC printed p. 311, PDF p. 154.
