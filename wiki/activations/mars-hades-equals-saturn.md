## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Activities conducted at a loss. Work without pay. Death by murder or suicide, or by crushing.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Burdening work in need, difficult work problems, difficult separation actions, danger in hard actions, grief due to loss of work, problems due to work shortage, painful work service, and losses due to criminal acts

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult work brings burdens, loss or lack of return.
- psychology: Grief through losing work is explicit in ABC.
- body/health: Witte's fatal examples remain historical source claims, not predicted outcomes.
- social/relationship: Work without pay and losses through criminal acts are named.
- events/manifestations: Work shortage, difficult separation and painful service are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: The sources specify hardship; they do not establish an inevitable progression toward death or exclude recovery.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize burdens and loss, while Witte adds severe fatal examples. The different severity does not establish a necessary sequence of events.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies the Mars/Hades axis through burdens and losses in difficult work. Work at a loss or without pay in [Witte](#witte-entry) connects with shortage, painful service and grief at losing work in [ABC](#abc-entry). Witte also retains fatal events as severe historical examples. The shared theme is costly or unrewarded activity; the entries do not establish that such hardship progresses toward a fatal outcome.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 198 (printed p. 202); ABC printed p. 311, PDF p. 154.
