## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Daily dirty work. The man who tires easily while working. The man who has to do low, dirty or unfamiliar work. Grave illness or injury through sudden attack.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work on the personal past, a secret work centre, dangerous actions of a person, bodily deficiency through work, detrimental work of a person, personal danger at work, and a personal will to serve.

- Source reference: printed p. 310, PDF p. 154, Sun row; `körperlicher Mangel durch Arbeit` means bodily deficiency through work, not lack of work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: One personally performs or is affected by difficult, hidden or dangerous work.
- psychology: A personal will to serve is explicit in ABC.
- body/health: Fatigue and work-related bodily deficiency are source claims; Witte also names grave illness or injury without establishing a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Service and unfamiliar or low-status tasks are historical Witte examples.
- events/manifestations: Daily dirty work, a secret work centre and work on one's past are named.
- conflicts/notes: Work-related bodily deficiency differs from unemployment; repeated testing of vitality or identity formation is not stated.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes daily taxing work; ABC also includes service, personal history and hidden work. These different settings do not establish one necessary bodily result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies the Mars/Hades axis through personal involvement in difficult work. Daily taxing or unfamiliar tasks in [Witte](#witte-entry) connect with personal service, hidden work and work on one's past in [ABC](#abc-entry). Both retain danger or bodily consequences as historical source assertions. The practical theme is the person's participation in this work, without turning the medical examples into diagnoses or asserting an identity formed by hardship.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 198 (printed p. 202); ABC printed p. 310, PDF p. 154.
