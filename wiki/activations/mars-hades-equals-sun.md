## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Daily dirty work. The man who tires easily while working. The man who has to do low, dirty or unfamiliar work. Grave illness or injury through sudden attack.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work on the personal past, secret work centre, dangerous actions of a person, corporal lack of work, detrimental work of a person, personal danger at work, and personal will to serve

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Hades = Sun`: the self worn down by grim tasks, identity shaped by difficult service, and vitality repeatedly tested by harsh or dangerous conditions.
