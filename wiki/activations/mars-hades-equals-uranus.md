## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Excitement, or loss producing activities. War atrocities. Murder. Suicide.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Lack of work reform, inadequate technical work, lack of new work, disadvantages through excitement at work, sudden actions out of need, a surprising criminal action, and hidden impulses through technology.

- Source reference: printed p. 311, PDF p. 154, Uranus row; `mangelhafte technische Arbeit` means inadequate technical work, not its absence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult activity involves excitement, abrupt action or deficiencies in renewal and technical work.
- psychology: Excitement at work can have disadvantages in ABC.
- body/health: Witte names fatal acts as historical doctrine, not predictions.
- social/relationship: Criminal or war-related activity appears as severe source examples.
- events/manifestations: Sudden actions from need, inadequate technical work and hidden technical impulses are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: The practical work deficiencies must not be erased by Witte's extreme examples; no causal pathway from excitement to killing is established.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes loss-producing and severe destructive activity; ABC also includes technical and work-reform deficiencies. These differ in scale and do not establish one inevitable event.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies the Mars/Hades axis through excitement, abrupt action and deficient renewal in difficult work. [ABC](#abc-entry) names inadequate technical work, missing reform or new work, and sudden actions out of need. [Witte](#witte-entry) retains loss-producing activity and severe destructive examples. Those examples remain attributed source claims; the broader work difficulties do not establish a fatal event or a causal progression to violence.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 198 (printed p. 202); ABC printed p. 311, PDF p. 154.
