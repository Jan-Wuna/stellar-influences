## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Serving position which leads to close acquaintance or close ties to blood relations. Abortion (female fetus). Immoral assault with injuries.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Old artistic work, difficult peace activity, a will for peace in need, a dangerous act of love, lack of harmonious activity, activation of art-related criminality, hidden love impulses, and a preference for dangerous work.

- Source reference: printed p. 310, PDF p. 154, Venus row; `Kunst-Arbeit` and `Kunst-Kriminalität` concern art, not artificial work or crime.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult work involves intimacy, art, peace or impaired harmony.
- psychology: Hidden love impulses, peace-seeking in need and preference for dangerous work are ABC themes.
- body/health: Witte includes abortion and assault injury as historical claims, not diagnoses or forecasts.
- social/relationship: Service leading to close acquaintance or ties with blood relations is explicit.
- events/manifestations: Old art work, art-related criminality and difficult peace work are retained.
- conflicts/notes: The entry does not establish every close tie as unequal or every artistic activity as criminal.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes service-related closeness and severe bodily events; ABC adds art, peace and hidden affection. These different settings should remain visible rather than being reduced to violated intimacy.

## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies the Mars/Hades axis through intimate, artistic and peace-related concerns within difficult work. Service leading to closeness in [Witte](#witte-entry) sits beside old art work, difficult peace activity and hidden affection in [ABC](#abc-entry). The entries also retain dangerous love action, art-related crime and severe bodily examples. Those particular claims do not make every relationship unequal or every form of closeness harmful.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 198 (printed p. 202); ABC printed p. 310, PDF p. 154.
