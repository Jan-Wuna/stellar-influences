## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Actions under aggravating circumstances. Serious misfortunes. Fatal crimes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Lack of work in public life, service work for the public, difficult creation in public, dangerous actions in public life, criminal actions in public, and public actions out of need.

- Source reference: printed p. 310, PDF p. 154, Vernal Point row; `Arbeits-Dienst für die Öffentlichkeit` means service work for the public, not employment dictation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult action and work occur in a public setting.
- psychology: No distinct individual attitude is specified.
- body/health: Witte names fatal crimes as historical source examples, not predicted outcomes.
- social/relationship: Service for the public and actions arising from public need are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Work shortage, difficult public creation and dangerous or criminal public actions are retained.
- conflicts/notes: Public service is distinct from crime; the entries do not define public life as generally degraded.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes misfortune and crime; ABC also includes service and difficult work for the public. These different manifestations should remain visible.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies the Mars/Hades axis through difficult activity in public life. [ABC](#abc-entry) names service work, shortage, difficult creation and actions out of need in that setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) adds aggravating circumstances, serious misfortune and fatal crime. The public setting unifies the entries; their severe examples do not make all public work criminal or establish a general condition for society.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 198 (printed p. 202); ABC printed p. 310, PDF p. 154.
