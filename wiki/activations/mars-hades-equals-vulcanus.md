## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Dangerous missions or assignments. Warlike activities. Murderous deeds.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influential secret activity, dangerous violent acts, enormous lack of work, secret exercise of power, impulses to renounce violence, criminal violent acts, powerful actions of the past, and adverse energies of will.

- Source reference: printed p. 311, PDF p. 154, Vulcanus row; `Einflußreiche` means influential. The scan also retains `Impulse zum Gewalt-Verzicht`, impulses toward renouncing violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult or secret action carries force, danger or influence.
- psychology: Adverse will energies coexist with impulses to renounce violence.
- body/health: Witte's murderous deeds are historical source examples, not predicted outcomes.
- social/relationship: Secret power and dangerous assignments are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Violent acts, major work shortage and powerful actions from the past are retained.
- conflicts/notes: Renouncing violence is part of the entry and prevents equating every forceful action with destruction.

## Contradictions

ABC includes both violent action and impulses to renounce violence. These opposing directions are distinct possibilities, while Witte emphasizes the dangerous end of the range.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies the Mars/Hades axis through force and influence in difficult or concealed action. Dangerous assignments in [Witte](#witte-entry) connect with secret power and violent acts in [ABC](#abc-entry). ABC also explicitly includes impulses to renounce violence and enormous work shortage. This wider range retains both the use of destructive force and movement away from it; the severe historical examples do not predict an individual's conduct.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 198 (printed p. 202); ABC printed p. 311, PDF p. 154.
