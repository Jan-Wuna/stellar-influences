## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Your vibration helps others to experience the most exalted and spiritual aspect of the beauty of everything around them. You also have the ability to help them appre ciate the foundation of their life, that is, all that creates security for them, as well as to recognize and be at peace with whatever their heart’s desire is.

- psychology: Recognition and acceptance of one's desires are explicit.
- body/health: No medical condition or healing treatment is specified.
- social/relationship: The passage describes helping others through one's “vibration,” its own spiritual term for influence.
- events/manifestations: No concrete external event is stated.
- conflicts/notes: This unusual beauty-and-security emphasis is stated under Chiron for this exact midpoint; it must not be replaced with generic healing doctrine.

## Derived Synthesis

Chiron specifies the Mars/Jupiter axis here through an influence that helps others appreciate their surroundings and the foundations of their lives. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) explicitly names spiritual beauty, sources of security and peace with heartfelt desire. Its distinctive emphasis is appreciative and supportive; the passage does not itself specify medical healing or a practical event.
