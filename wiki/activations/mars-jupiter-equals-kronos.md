## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Good fruits, fine accomplishments. Leadership through one’s own fortunate activities. Through happy activities to independence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work within the money hierarchy, special legal actions, a particular will to succeed, successful state activity, legal actions of the state, independent legal actions, state money trading, and important impulses toward success.

- Source reference: printed p. 299, PDF p. 148, Kronos row; `selbständige Rechts-Handlungen` means independent legal actions, not self-legislative acts.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful action leads toward leadership, independence or important state activity.
- psychology: A particular will to succeed is explicit in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Leadership through one's own activity and independent legal action are named.
- events/manifestations: Fine accomplishments, state finance and successful state activity are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Rank and public visibility are not necessary outcomes of the stated leadership and independence.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes accomplishment and independence; ABC adds state, financial and legal settings. These different examples do not establish a disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies the Mars/Jupiter axis through accomplishment that supports leadership or independence. [Witte](#witte-entry) explicitly links these with one's own fortunate activity; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful state activity, independent legal action and financial hierarchy. The common thread is success expressed through a leading or independent capacity, without a necessary promise of rank or public recognition.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 192; ABC printed p. 299, PDF p. 148.
