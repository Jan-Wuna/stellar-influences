## Source Entries

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/JUPITER With Itself`
- Source page: `226`

#### Munkasey Entry

An abrasiveness and roughness to the way you use your energy; gives you a competitive edge in contact sports; adds to your need to bully others and threaten them with your strength or physical ability; hasty expansion plans.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Energy is applied roughly, competitively or in haste.
- psychology: Abrasiveness and a wish to threaten others with strength are explicit.
- body/health: Contact sports and physical ability appear without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Competitive contact and bullying are distinct uses of strength.
- events/manifestations: A competitive edge in contact sports and hasty expansion plans are named.
- conflicts/notes: The entry does not equate sporting competition with justified intimidation.

## Contradictions

Only one “With Itself” entry is present. Its competitive and threatening examples are different applications of energy, not independent conflicting accounts.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In this repeated-factor formula, Mars specifies the Mars/Jupiter axis through rough or hurried use of energy. The [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry) includes a competitive edge in contact sports, but also bullying, physical threats and hasty expansion plans. The shared emphasis is forceful application; sporting advantage and intimidation remain distinct manifestations rather than equivalent or equally justified conduct.

Source locator: Munkasey, “MARS/JUPITER With Itself,” printed p. 226.
