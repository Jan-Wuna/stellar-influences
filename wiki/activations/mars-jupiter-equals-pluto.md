## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Inception of betrothal or fortunate activity. Successful build-up. Several competitors.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Developing joy in work, development of a legal activity, successful development work, successful unfolding of will, wanting good development, creating legal changes, an advantageous change of work, and change in labor law.

- Source reference: printed p. 299, PDF p. 148, Pluto row. It begins `Arbeits-Freude entwickeln`; the extraction had attached its first two phrases and `erfolgreiche Entwicklungs-` to Neptune. `Arbeit` continues the same Pluto phrase.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

extraordinary or unusual spirit of enterprise, great creative powers, brilliant success. People capable of tackling big projects or enterprises.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Difficulty in stopping oneself when one is going too far or doing too much. The compulsion to engage in destructive excess. Through one’s ability to keep working and to maintain a positive attitude as to the outcome of one’s actions one can experience breakthroughs and transformations leading to a higher state of consciousness.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `224`

#### Munkasey Entry

An extreme or fanatical use of the forces under your control; rebellious or vigilante activities which provoke legal action; added intensity within your activities; increased powers or forces for use against others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intensified enterprise can support major undertakings and successful development, with destructive excess as a serious alternative.
- psychology: Ebertin describes unusual enterprise and creative power; Sandbach adds persistence, difficulty stopping and compulsive excess.
- body/health: The entries do not establish a specific bodily or medical implication.
- social/relationship: Witte includes betrothal and competitors; Munkasey adds force directed against others and vigilante action provoking legal response.
- events/manifestations: Large projects, successful build-up and breakthroughs coexist with legal conflict from extreme activity.
- conflicts/notes: Success-oriented statements and warnings about fanaticism describe the range of intensified effort; neither erases the other.

## Contradictions

The contrast is between constructive sustained enterprise and compulsive or coercive excess. The source entries do not require one outcome to cancel the other.

## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies the Mars/Jupiter axis through unusually large or persistent effort. Major projects in [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) and successful build-up in [Witte](#witte-entry) connect with developing work enjoyment, legal activity and will in the scan-checked [ABC entry](#abc-entry). [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds breakthroughs through continued work and a positive attitude.

The same entry also describes difficulty stopping. [Munkasey's](#munkasey-entry) extreme force and vigilante examples make moderation a material limit: expansion or determination alone does not establish that the objective, method or effect on others is beneficial.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 299, PDF p. 148; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 192; [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), printed p. 203, entry 0694; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 68, Pluto paragraph checked directly; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 224.
