## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Inception of betrothal or fortunate activity. Successful build-up. Several competitors.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work, successful will development, good development, creating legal changes, beneficial change of work, and change of work right

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

extraordinary or unusual spirit of enterprise, great creative powers, brilliant success. People capable of tackling big projects or enterprises.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Difficulty in stopping oneself when one is going too far or doing too much. The compulsion to engage in destructive excess. Through one’s ability to keep working and to maintain a positive attitude as to the outcome of one’s actions one can experience breakthroughs and transformations leading to a higher state of consciousness.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

An extreme or fanatical use of the forces under your control; rebellious or vigilante activities which provoke legal action; added intensity within your activities; increased powers or forces for use against others.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Jupiter = Pluto`: big undertakings, breakthrough effort, strategic confidence, and success that comes from sustained force joined to belief in the objective. Main caution: compulsion: the desire to win or grow can become excessive, destructive, or impossible to moderate once momentum builds.
