## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Way of acting dictated by morality and reason.

- Source reference: PDF p. 192, Poseidon row; the scan reads `morality`, repairing extracted `morahty`.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success through ideal activity, successful intellectual work, insights into labor law, joy in cultural work, clear legal action, rational legal actions, advantage through rational actions, and truthful work successes.

- Source reference: printed p. 299, PDF p. 148, Poseidon row; `vernünftige Rechts-Handlungen` means rational legal actions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Enterprise is directed through reason, principle and intellectual or cultural work.
- psychology: The entries emphasize reasoned action and clarity of purpose, without establishing a detailed personality profile.
- body/health: Neither brief entry states a bodily or medical implication.
- social/relationship: ABC places the formula in cultural work and labor-law activity; no specific intimate relationship is described.
- events/manifestations: Successful intellectual work, cultural activity and clear legal action are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: The short Witte statement and ABC examples support principled work, not guaranteed moral correctness or legal success.

## Contradictions

The entries share an emphasis on reasoned action. Their brevity does not establish agreement on every possible application or an inevitable successful result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies the Mars/Jupiter axis as enterprise guided by reason and principle. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies the compact organizing statement; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives it practical settings in intellectual and cultural work, labor law and clear, rational legal action.

These are examples of the kind of work and judgment described by the entries. They do not establish that a person's beliefs are correct or that a legal undertaking must succeed.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 299, PDF p. 148; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 192 (entry start).
