## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The ripe fruit. Confinement or end of pregnancy. The birth. Annulment of an engagement. Separated liaisons. To finish a successful activity. Unpleasant decision with good results.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joy in work in old age, burdensome legal work, successful patient work, processing financial losses, disturbed joy in work, good will in old age, concentrated legal work, problems with labor law, and loss-making money trading.

- Source reference: printed p. 299, PDF p. 148, Saturn row; `gestörte Arbeits-Freude` means disturbed joy in work, not storage work pleasure.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inhibitions and difficulties in decision-making, great difficulty in making up one's mind, tendency to let good opportunities slip away. Termination of a relationship, a difficult birth.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One want’s to act but is held back by insecurities or fears. To do too much or go too far because of one’s fears and/or feelings of inadequacy. The slow development of confidence in one’s ability to act.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `224`

#### Munkasey Entry

Desires which build to use your strength for personal progress; gives a more realistic view toward using restraint vs. force; helps to sap your energy; despair when you meet forces which you perceive to be stronger than you.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Initiative reaches a limit, completion or difficult decision; confidence may develop slowly.
- psychology: Ebertin and Sandbach describe indecision, fear and inadequacy; Sandbach also allows gradual confidence, and Munkasey considers restraint against stronger forces.
- body/health: Witte and Ebertin include historical birth and pregnancy associations; these are not medical forecasts.
- social/relationship: Witte and Ebertin name ended engagements or relationships.
- events/manifestations: Finishing successful work and an unpleasant decision with good results coexist with missed opportunities, loss processing and demanding legal work.
- conflicts/notes: Completion can be fruitful in Witte, whereas Ebertin emphasizes inhibition; neither makes every ending a failure. ABC retains successful patience and concentrated legal work alongside loss.

## Contradictions

The sources differ in emphasis between fruitful completion and inhibited action. These possibilities should remain distinct; repeated relationship-ending examples do not establish inevitable separation.

## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies the Mars/Jupiter axis through restraint, completion and the difficulty of deciding when or how to act. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) and [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describe hesitation and fear that can waste opportunities; Sandbach's slow development of confidence and [Munkasey's](#munkasey-entry) realistic restraint preserve a constructive response to limits.

[Witte's](#witte-entry) finished successful activity and unpleasant decision with good results matter here: an ending is not automatically a defeat. The scan-checked [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also retains successful patient work. The pregnancy and birth statements remain historical source associations rather than bodily predictions.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 299, PDF p. 148; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 192; [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), printed p. 202, entry 0691; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 67, Saturn paragraph checked directly; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 224.
