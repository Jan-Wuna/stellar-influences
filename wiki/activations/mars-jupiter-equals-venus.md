## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Pregnancy. Birth of a girl. Love’s happiness connected with sexual intercourse. Successful pleasant work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work on peace right, successful peace activity, g!Exceptional love activity, joy over Harrnonian impulses, joy in legal activity, joy in artistic work, love activity for money, and preference for work with money

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Harmonious sex expression, richly perceptive faculties and feelings, creative faculties, creative artistic activity, urge to beget children. Procreation caused by love, birth. Fortunate decisions in love and marriage.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Love expressed through doing things for others, and maybe doing too much or going too far. A love of doing many things. People are attracted to you because you are passionately positive. The joy of having a good time. In a careless way to go ahead and do things without considering the ramifications of one’s actions.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Admiring the ways you use your strength or force in general; adds a conflict between wanting to use guile or force when competing; the use of social energies to stimulate expression in others of fine arts or music.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Jupiter = Venus`: harmonious desire, fertile or procreative themes, artistic productivity, and the ability to act for love, enjoyment, or relational good results. Main caution: indulgent excess: pleasure and positive feeling can encourage carelessness, overdoing, or acting before considering consequences.
