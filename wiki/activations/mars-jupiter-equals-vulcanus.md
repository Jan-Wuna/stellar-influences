## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Mighty promise of results. Enforced, increased optimism. Power through successful accomplishments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

influential labor law, successful energy impulses, successful activity of power, enormous legal influence, happiness through strength of will, powerful joy in work, powerful legal actions, and mighty money trade

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Forceful enterprise, heightened optimism and power associated with accomplishment.
- psychology: Witte describes increased or enforced optimism; ABC adds strength of will and powerful joy in work.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is stated; the references to strength and energy do not establish a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: ABC includes legal influence and labor law, rather than a specific intimate relationship.
- events/manifestations: Successful accomplishments, powerful legal action and money trading are the explicit examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's 'promise of results' is distinct from a guaranteed outcome; increased optimism can be imposed as well as felt.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte's promise and ABC's successful examples have different scopes and should not be turned into an inevitable result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies the Mars/Jupiter axis through strong will, intensified optimism and the power associated with successful accomplishment. [ABC](#abc-entry) gives that force concrete settings in work, labor law, legal action and money trading.

The narrow wording matters: [Witte](#witte-entry) speaks both of the promise of results and of enforced optimism. The entry supports a forceful expectation of accomplishment, without establishing that confidence alone secures it.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 299, PDF p. 148; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 192 (entry start).
