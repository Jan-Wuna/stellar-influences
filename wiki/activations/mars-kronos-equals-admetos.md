## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The state gives order to stop. Heavy pressure by power of state. Tyranny.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Perseverance in independent work, blocking actions of the state, depressing state activity, restriction of state work, the end of independent work, thorough creation by the state, heavy work for the state, and resistance to state activity.

- Source reference: printed p. 315, PDF p. 156, Admetos row; `gründliches Schaffen` is thorough creation and `für den Staat` means for the state.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Official activity meets stoppage, pressure or resistance; ABC also retains perseverance in independent work.
- psychology: ABC mentions perseverance and a depressing experience of state activity, without providing a detailed personality account.
- body/health: Neither entry states a specific bodily condition.
- social/relationship: State power can constrain activity or encounter resistance; Witte names tyranny as an extreme example.
- events/manifestations: Stop orders, blocked state activity, curtailed work and the end of independent work are explicit manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: Witte concentrates on repression; ABC also allows persistent and thorough work. The constructive alternatives should not disappear behind tyranny.

## Contradictions

The dominant restrictive theme is not the whole entry: ABC's perseverance is an additional possibility rather than a denial of Witte's coercive examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies the Mars/Kronos axis through limitation and resistance in official or independent action. [Witte's](#witte-entry) state order to stop and pressure exerted by state power give the restrictive extreme; [ABC](#abc-entry) also describes the end of independent work and resistance to state activity.

ABC preserves a quieter alternative in perseverance and thorough work. That keeps this formula from being reduced to tyranny alone, while retaining the central question of action that is narrowed, halted or carried on under pressure.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 315, PDF p. 156; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 200 (entry start).
