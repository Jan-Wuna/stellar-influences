## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Independent activities in science or in trade. Leading business manager. Pleasant official orders. Extensive precautionary action by the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Extension of self employed work, successful work of the state, extension of special actions, self employed scientific work, achievements of the boss, priority work of science, important work experiences, and increase of important work

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independent or official work extends into science, trade and management.
- psychology: The entries emphasize professional independence and experience; no detailed emotional profile is supplied.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical implication is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte names a leading business manager; ABC includes a chief's successful activity and work for the state.
- events/manifestations: Scientific and commercial activity, expanded important work and extensive state precautions are explicit examples.
- conflicts/notes: The examples concern breadth and professional scope; they do not establish a particular office or guarantee success.

## Contradictions

The entries share expanded professional activity but provide different examples. No direct conflict is established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies the Mars/Kronos axis through a wider reach for independent or executive activity. [Witte](#witte-entry) locates it in science, trade and business management; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds the expansion of important work, scientific independence and successful activity by a chief or the state.

The state precautions in Witte are another form of extensive organized activity. They should remain a concrete example rather than being expanded into an unsupported list of regulatory occupations.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 315, PDF p. 156; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 200 (entry start).
