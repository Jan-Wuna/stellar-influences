## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To bring about autocratic deeds with others. Interaction with people who are independent or act autocratically. To be threatened by the police or government. To be arrested.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Others act self employed, special place of work, dominant force in the environment, self governing actions of the partner, state work in the place, priority environmental work, important work in the place, and will of the authority in the place

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Kronos = Asc` brings official or autocratic force directly into one's dealings with other people and the immediate environment.
- Chart expression: contact with powerful or self-directing people, acting forcefully with others, and in harder cases threat, arrest, or direct intervention by police or government.
