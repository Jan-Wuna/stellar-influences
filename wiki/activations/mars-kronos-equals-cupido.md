## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Artists with their own style. Great original art. Activity in the marriage-office.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

work of a state community, special actions of a community, leader's work for the community, independent work community, state will for the community, priority activity of a community, and important community work

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Distinctive creative work and organized activity within a community or formal union.
- psychology: Witte identifies artistic originality and personal style; the entries provide no broader emotional profile.
- body/health: No bodily or medical implication is stated.
- social/relationship: ABC emphasizes a community, its leadership and work; Witte adds activity in a marriage office.
- events/manifestations: Original art, independent work groups, community projects and marriage administration are explicit manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's art and marriage-office examples differ from ABC's state/community work; neither establishes that artistic style must be backed by authority or discipline.

## Contradictions

The entries offer different settings rather than contradictory claims. Community leadership, marriage administration and individual artistic style are not interchangeable occupations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies the Mars/Kronos axis through a collective or formal social setting. [ABC](#abc-entry) centers on important community work, a chief working for a group and independent working communities; [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies the distinct examples of a marriage office and original art.

The artistic strand should retain its own meaning: Witte emphasizes artists with a style of their own. The page does not establish that the originality must come from state authority or imposed discipline.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 315, PDF p. 156; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 200 (entry start).
