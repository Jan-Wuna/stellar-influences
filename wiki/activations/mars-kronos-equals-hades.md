## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Losses caused by the authorities. To be reprimanded or destroyed by power of state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 315, PDF p. 156; English translation checked against the original German scan.

#### ABC Entry

Work in the service of the state; particular disadvantages at work; dangerous state activity; dangers in independent work; extensive work-related crime; dealing with crime by authorities; a lack of state action; state labor service.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Official or independent activity involves loss, danger or shortcomings.
- psychology: Neither entry supplies a detailed emotional profile; the focus is on disadvantages and harmful exercise of authority.
- body/health: Danger is mentioned, but no particular bodily condition or medical conclusion is established.
- social/relationship: Witte describes losses and reprimand through authorities; ABC includes criminal activity involving authority.
- events/manifestations: State-caused loss, dangerous state or independent work, insufficient state action and state labor service are explicit settings.
- conflicts/notes: The punitive Witte statements do not exhaust ABC's wider labor and administrative examples.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in disadvantages around authority, while ABC also includes service and administrative tasks. No universal claim about state actors follows from the examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies the Mars/Kronos axis through disadvantages, danger and loss in official or independent action. [Witte's](#witte-entry) account centers on harm inflicted by authorities; the scan-checked [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens the setting to dangerous work, shortcomings in state action and criminal activity involving authority.

The common thread is a problematic exercise or condition of work and authority. The references to labor service and dealing with official crime remain concrete settings rather than proof that every state worker is coercive or criminal.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 315, PDF p. 156; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 200 (entry start).
