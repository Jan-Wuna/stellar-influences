## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Giver of money. Advantages through the government. Money through lawsuits. Security police.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work of the financial office, work pleasure of the hierarchy, special financial activity, successful state activity, money through self employed work, good work of the state, state legal actions, and important labour law

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Financial or legal advantage connected with official activity and independent work.
- psychology: ABC includes satisfaction in hierarchical work; no broader personality account is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or medical implication is stated.
- social/relationship: Government assistance and official financial or legal institutions are central settings; Witte also lists security police.
- events/manifestations: Money given, lawsuit proceeds, finance-office work and earnings from independent work are explicit examples.
- conflicts/notes: The brief security-police reference is not a general promise of protection; benefits through institutions are source examples, not guaranteed legal outcomes.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict is established. The favorable financial examples have a narrower scope than a guarantee of winning a lawsuit or receiving state assistance.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies the Mars/Kronos axis through financial and legal advantage in independent or official action. [Witte](#witte-entry) names money given, government benefits and money through lawsuits; [ABC](#abc-entry) locates this activity in a finance office, state legal work and earnings from independent work.

Witte also lists security police, but the brief term does not justify expanding the whole formula into protected status or inevitable institutional support. The strongest shared theme remains money and benefit connected with work and official channels.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 314, PDF p. 156; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 200 (entry start).
