## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The ability to act independently. Craving for power. To wish to assert oneself.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 314, PDF p. 156; English translation checked against the original German scan.

#### ABC Entry

My particular will; my work as a chief; my work for the state; my work for the hierarchy; my independent work; my important work; independent work on the soul; the active force of a great soul; important psychological treatment.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal independence, self-assertion and the wish to exercise power through work.
- psychology: Witte explicitly names craving for power and wishing to assert oneself; ABC locates important work in a first-person inner perspective.
- body/health: ABC's psychological-treatment example is an attributed source setting, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: ABC names work as a chief, for the state and for a hierarchy.
- events/manifestations: Independent work, important work and work on the inner life are explicit examples.
- conflicts/notes: Independent agency and craving for power are distinct emphases; the formula does not establish that authority is attained.

## Contradictions

The ability to act independently should be distinguished from wanting power. The entries do not state that ambition must result in authority.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies the Mars/Kronos axis through a personal concern with independent action and self-assertion. [Witte](#witte-entry) names both an ability to act independently and a craving for power; the scan-checked [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places that concern in one's own work as a chief, for the state or within a hierarchy.

ABC also retains independent work on the inner life and psychological treatment as source examples. They broaden the first-person work theme without establishing an occupation, a diagnosis or a guaranteed position of command.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 314, PDF p. 156; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 200 (entry start).
