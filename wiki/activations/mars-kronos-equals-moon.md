## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Mistress of the house. Independently acting official staff. Policeman.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 314, PDF p. 156; English translation checked against the original German scan.

#### ABC Entry

Special emotional impulses; particularly sensitive work; the particular drive of a people; the dominant will of a people; women's leadership work; a woman's independent actions; a woman's superior will; important emotional impulses.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independent or leading activity appears in household, staff, collective and historically gendered examples.
- psychology: ABC emphasizes emotional impulses and sensitive work, without establishing a stable personality type.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte lists household leadership and independently acting official staff; ABC includes a people's dominant will and women's leadership.
- events/manifestations: Household management, official staff activity and Witte's policeman example are the concrete roles.
- conflicts/notes: Historical female/male role labels remain attributed; they do not establish that leadership or police work belongs to one gender.

## Contradictions

The sources supply different social settings rather than a gender rule. Their roles and emotional emphases are examples, not exhaustive categories.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies the Mars/Kronos axis through people acting in household, staff and collective settings. [Witte's](#witte-entry) household head and independently acting officials connect with the scan-checked [ABC entry's](#abc-entry) leading activity by women and the active will of a people.

ABC adds a feeling-oriented dimension through emotional impulses and sensitive work. These examples should retain their historical wording and range, without converting the role labels into universal rules about women, men or occupations.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 314, PDF p. 156; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 200 (entry start).
