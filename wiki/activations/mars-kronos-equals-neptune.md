## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Police in distress. Power of state not enforced. Ineffective power of the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 315, PDF p. 156; English translation checked against the original German scan.

- Row boundary: Neptune ends with `wichtige Zukunfts-Arbeit`. The next glyph starts Pluto's `Besondere Aktionen zur Veränderung`; `Entfaltung selbständiger Tätigkeit` is wholly within Pluto's paragraph.

#### ABC Entry

Special work in air or water; disappointing state work; expecting great work; weak actions of the chief; uncertain efforts of the authorities; hidden state activities; important work in metaphysics; important work for the future.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Official activity becomes weak, uncertain, hidden or ineffective; ABC also gives water, air and future-oriented work.
- psychology: ABC speaks of expectation and uncertainty, rather than a detailed personality profile.
- body/health: No bodily or medical implication is stated.
- social/relationship: The chief, authorities and police are the represented actors.
- events/manifestations: Witte names unenforced state power and police in distress; ABC adds hidden state activity, metaphysics and work for the future.
- conflicts/notes: The ABC paragraph boundary was checked against the original scan: change/development clauses belong to Pluto, not this Neptune entry.

## Contradictions

Practical weakness and future-oriented or less visible work are different source emphases. The page does not infer that all hidden or future work is ineffective.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies the Mars/Kronos axis through uncertain or ineffective official action. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes state power that is not enforced and police in distress; the scan-checked [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds weak action by a chief, uncertain efforts by authorities and hidden state activity.

ABC also includes work in air or water, metaphysics and work for the future. These specific settings keep the entry broader than failed enforcement alone. The change-and-development clauses previously attached to this entry have been restored to the Pluto glyph row after checking the German scan.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 315, PDF p. 156; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 200 (entry start).
