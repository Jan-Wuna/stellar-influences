## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Mental and spiritual independence. Moral actions are of a high standard. The ultimate intellectual work. Education on the basis of state regulations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 315, PDF p. 156; English translation checked against the original German scan.

#### ABC Entry

Efforts toward clarity in the state; the influence of state actions; clear impulses of the chief; independent intellectual work; state cultural work; activity of a spiritual authority; superior spiritual drive; important reasoned actions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independent intellectual or spiritual activity, reasoned action and official education or culture.
- psychology: Witte emphasizes mental and spiritual independence and a high moral standard; ABC adds clarity and reason.
- body/health: No bodily or medical implication is stated.
- social/relationship: A spiritual authority, state education and cultural work are explicit institutional settings.
- events/manifestations: Intellectual work, education under state regulations and state cultural activity are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: The moral ideal is attributed source language, not proof that official or spiritual authority is inherently right.

## Contradictions

The entries place independence beside institutional education and culture. Those settings can coexist; neither is evidence of inherent moral superiority.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies the Mars/Kronos axis through intellect, spiritual authority and reasoned judgment in independent or official action. [Witte's](#witte-entry) mental and spiritual independence joins the scan-checked [ABC entry's](#abc-entry) efforts toward clarity, independent intellectual work and actions guided by reason.

State-regulated education and cultural work give the theme institutional settings. Witte's high moral standard remains a source ideal; the formula does not establish that a state regulation or spiritual leader is correct by virtue of authority.

Evidence: [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 315, PDF p. 156; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 200 (entry start).
