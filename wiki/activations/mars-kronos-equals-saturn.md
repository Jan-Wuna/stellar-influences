## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Passing service order (official order) of the state. Separation through the power of the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special work tasks, particularly difficult work, concentrated state actions, problematic state work, painful independent activity, losing independent work, state separation actions, and state activity that brings losses.

- Source reference: printed p. 315, PDF p. 156, Saturn row; `verlustreiche Tätigkeit` is activity bringing losses, not simply loss of activity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Official or independent work encounters difficulty, concentration or separation.
- psychology: No distinct individual attitude is specified.
- body/health: Painful activity is named without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Official orders and separation through state power are Witte examples.
- events/manifestations: Difficult state tasks, lost independent work and state activity bringing losses are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Concentrated work is part of the range; the entries do not reduce every official order to punishment or exclusion.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes orders and separation; ABC includes difficult and concentrated work as well as loss. These are different manifestations of limitation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies the Mars/Kronos axis through difficult tasks and limits on official or independent action. Orders and state-imposed separation in [Witte](#witte-entry) connect with concentrated state work, painful independent activity and loss in [ABC](#abc-entry). The entry concerns both demanding work and separation; it does not establish that every official action is punitive.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 200; ABC printed p. 315, PDF p. 156.
