## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To make one’s self independent through force. The police and the state employ violent measures.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Actions of state power, energetic drive of the chief, influential activity of the authorities, violent state actions, great work energy, forceful independent activity, and self-confident work capacity.

- Source reference: printed p. 315, PDF p. 156, Vulcanus row; `selbständige Tätigkeit` and `selbstbewußte Arbeits-Kraft` concern independent activity and self-confident work capacity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independent or state activity carries force, influence and strong work capacity.
- psychology: Energetic drive and self-confidence are ABC themes.
- body/health: Work energy is stated without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Witte names independence through force and violent police/state measures.
- events/manifestations: Powerful independent work and influential official actions are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Work capacity is distinct from violence; the entry must retain both rather than reducing all force to coercion.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes force and violence; ABC also includes ordinary work energy and independence. These different applications of strength remain separate possibilities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies the Mars/Kronos axis through strong work capacity and the force of independent or official action. [ABC](#abc-entry) names energetic leadership, influence and self-confident work, while [Witte](#witte-entry) retains independence gained through force and violent state measures. Strength unifies the range without making every independent action coercive.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 200; ABC printed p. 315, PDF p. 156.
