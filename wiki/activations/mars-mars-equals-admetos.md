## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 191, Mars + Mars, Admetos row.

#### Witte Entry

Specialism. Special work. The sinking trend of the market. The decrease of work. To throttle. Suppression. Terror. Rawness, crudeness. Rocks, fragments, debris, to wreck. The decrease of labor and activity. Narrowing of the field of activity. End of an action. Grave hindrances in the activity. Oppression to the point of immobility. Oppression. To work in wreckage and ruins. Very cold.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action is narrowed, specialized, obstructed or stopped.
- psychology: No distinct personal attitude is specified.
- body/health: Immobility and very cold conditions are named without a specific diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Suppression, terror and oppression are severe source examples.
- events/manifestations: Special work, market decline, reduced labor and work in ruins are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Specialization is part of the range and need not mean oppression; the harsh examples remain attributed.

## Contradictions

One source entry supplies both specialization and severe restriction. These are distinct forms of narrowed activity, without an independent comparison.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through a narrowed field of activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes specialization and special work alongside reduced labor, market decline, obstruction and ended action. Its severe examples include oppression, wreckage and immobility; these do not define every form of concentrated or specialized work.
