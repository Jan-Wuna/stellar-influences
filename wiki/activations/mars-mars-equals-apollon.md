## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 191, Mars + Mars, Apollon row.

#### Witte Entry

Generous way of acting. Peaceful, scientific or commercial activities. Successful activity. An activity is expanded and enlarged. Rising of the market. Activity in handicraft or industry. The forces of the trading market. Trading activity. Peacemaker. Scientist. Merchant.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity expands through peaceful, scientific or commercial work.
- psychology: A generous way of acting is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Peacemaker, scientist and merchant are source roles.
- events/manifestations: Successful work, market rise, industry, handicraft and trading are named.
- conflicts/notes: The entry gives favorable examples without guaranteeing profit or identifying every expanded activity as scientific.

## Contradictions

Only one source passage is present; its several fields and roles are examples, not independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through expanded and generous activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places this in peaceful work, science, commerce, industry and handicraft, with successful activity and a rising market among its examples. The range includes several practical fields rather than a promise that every enlarged undertaking is profitable.
