## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 190, Mars + Mars, Ascendant row.

#### Witte Entry

Actions and deeds of others or with others. Excitement. Arguments with others. To be handled energetically, leading eventually to violence. Fights. Strife. Co worker. To be made angry by others. Excitement or action in the immediate surroundings. Actions and deeds of others. Others are in activity. Place of work. Working environment.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action involves other people and one's immediate surroundings.
- psychology: Excitement and anger prompted by others are explicit.
- body/health: Violence is a source possibility without a specific bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Co-workers, joint action, arguments and fights are represented.
- events/manifestations: Workplaces and active surroundings are concrete settings.
- conflicts/notes: Shared work is retained alongside conflict; not every interaction is described as a fight.

## Contradictions

One source entry contains cooperative and conflictual examples. These are different interactions within its range, not independent contradictory accounts.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through action with other people and activity in the immediate surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes co-workers and workplaces as well as excitement, arguments, anger and possible violence. The organizing theme is interpersonal or local activity; the conflict examples do not characterize every shared task.
