## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 191, Mars + Mars, Cupido row.

#### Witte Entry

Work partnership. Marriage. Marriage partnership. Artistic activities. To establish married life. To be active in a community. Work community. Labor unions. The active family. Working for the family, association or a joint-stock company. Social work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity takes a shared, artistic, family or organized group form.
- psychology: No distinct individual attitude is specified.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Work partnership, marriage, family and labor unions are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Establishing married life, artistic activity and social work are named.
- conflicts/notes: These settings identify collective activity without establishing harmony or duration.

## Contradictions

The single source supplies several forms of shared work and life; it provides no independent comparison.

## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through activity shared in partnership or a group. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) spans marriage, family, art, labor unions, associations and companies, with social work as another practical form. Collective involvement unifies these settings without stating that every collaboration is harmonious or permanent.
