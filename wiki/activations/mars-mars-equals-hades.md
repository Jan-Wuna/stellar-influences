## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 191, Mars + Mars, Hades row.

#### Witte Entry

Fatigue. Weariness. Atrocity, vulgar acts. To cause damages. Base services. Male servant. Slave labor. Murder. Homicide. Unusual death. Serious sickness. Faulty or bad actions. Working in solitude. Working in poverty. Preparation for recycling. Working under difficult conditions. Working in outflow channels. Dealing with old things, historical things, waste or refuse. To serve. A serious act. Evil deed. Lack of activity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity involves difficult service, deficiencies, old material or harmful acts.
- psychology: Weariness is stated without a detailed attitude profile.
- body/health: Fatigue, sickness and fatal examples are historical source claims, not diagnoses or forecasts.
- social/relationship: Service, solitude, poverty and coerced labor are represented.
- events/manifestations: Recycling, historical material, waste work and faulty actions are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Practical work with waste or old material is distinct from wrongdoing; severe examples must not define every service task.

## Contradictions

The single entry spans useful difficult work and harmful action. These distinct roles should not be flattened into moral degradation.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through difficult conditions of work and service. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes fatigue, poverty and solitude alongside practical work with recycling, waste and historical material. It also retains damaging acts, coerced labor and severe bodily or fatal examples. Those historical claims remain attributed, while ordinary service or handling old material is not itself wrongdoing.
