- Source reference: PDF p. 190, Mars + Mars, Jupiter row.

#### Witte Entry

Joyous happenings. Betrothal. Fortunate deed. To create something. To produce. Successful propagation. Pregnancy. Generation. Children. Fruits. Lucky deed. Joy of work. Fortunate accomplishments. Much to do. Work or activity for justice or money.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity is productive, fortunate or enjoyable.
- psychology: Joy in work is explicit.
- body/health: Pregnancy and propagation are historical source associations, not reproductive predictions.
- social/relationship: Betrothal and children are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Creating, producing and work for justice or money are named.
- conflicts/notes: A justice-related activity is not proof of moral correctness; favorable examples do not guarantee results.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present. Its favorable work and social examples have no independent comparison on this page.

## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through productive activity and enjoyment of work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names creation, fortunate accomplishment and work for justice or money, while retaining betrothal and reproductive examples. These supply the range of favorable action without guaranteeing success or making every achievement morally justified; reproductive claims remain historical source wording.
