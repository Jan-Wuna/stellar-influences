## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 191, Mars + Mars, Kronos row.

#### Witte Entry

To act independently. Autocratic actions. Main formula for the great leader and organizer. Master of the house. Police. Power of the state. Procedure indicated by power of government. Police authority. Penal law. Work in areas that go beyond the ordinary.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action is independent, leading, official or beyond the ordinary.
- psychology: Independent and autocratic conduct are distinct source expressions.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Leadership, household authority, police and state power are named.
- events/manifestations: Organizing, government-directed procedure and penal law are concrete settings.
- conflicts/notes: Independent work does not itself establish office, autocracy or coercion.

## Contradictions

One source entry spans personal independence and official authority; these are different roles without an independent conflicting account.

## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through independent or leading action. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) moves from personal independence to organizing, household leadership, police, state power and penal law. These are different scopes of authority, while work beyond the ordinary broadens the range beyond a formal position of command.
