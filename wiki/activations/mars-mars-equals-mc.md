## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 190, Mars + Mars, MC row.

#### Witte Entry

Making arrangements. Giving orders. One’s own action. A agitated mind. Self will. To feel energy. To pull oneself together. The urge to be active. Minute of action. Personal goals at work. Psychically active.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: One's own initiative, arrangements and work goals become active.
- psychology: Agitation, self-will, felt energy and pulling oneself together are explicit.
- body/health: Feeling energy is stated without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Giving orders and making arrangements are the interpersonal examples.
- events/manifestations: The moment of action and personal work goals are named.
- conflicts/notes: Psychic activity is source wording; no clinical state or particular vocation is established.

## Contradictions

The single entry includes agitation and deliberate arrangement as different forms of one's own activity, without an independent comparison.

MC specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through one's own action and work goals. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects making arrangements and giving orders with self-will, felt energy and the urge to act, while also retaining mental agitation. The theme is personally directed activity; the brief passage does not identify a particular vocation or clinical state.
