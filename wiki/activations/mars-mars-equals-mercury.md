## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 190, Mars + Mars, Mercury row.

#### Witte Entry

Energetic thought. Hasty and energetic reaction. Malice. Quarrelsome thoughts and debates. Critics. Criticism. To excite oneself mentally. Argument. Activity of the mind. Power of thought. Clear, sharp thinking. Disputes.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought and reaction are energetic, sharp or argumentative.
- psychology: Haste, mental excitement and malice coexist with clear thinking.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Criticism, debate, argument and disputes are explicit.
- events/manifestations: No concrete event is supplied beyond reaction and verbal or mental activity.
- conflicts/notes: Clear thinking and quarrelsomeness are distinct expressions; mental energy does not prove either accuracy or malice.

## Contradictions

The single entry places clarity beside hostile and hasty thought. These are different uses of mental activity, not independent contradictory accounts.

## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through energetic thought and quick reaction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes clear, sharp thinking as well as mental excitement, criticism, debate and malice. The common theme is active thought and expression; clarity and hostile argument remain different possible applications rather than interchangeable qualities.
