## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 191, Mars + Mars, Neptune row.

#### Witte Entry

Rejection. Denial. Destruction. Infection. Failures. Future employment. Causing poisoning. To act with poor results. To be hindered, prevented. Inability to carry out the plans. Unclear or incomprehensible actions. Working under insecure or unknown conditions. Activities or labor in connection with water or air.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action meets uncertainty, prevention, poor results or unclear conditions.
- psychology: Incomprehensible actions are named without a distinct mental diagnosis.
- body/health: Infection and poisoning are historical source claims, not predicted conditions.
- social/relationship: Rejection and denial are stated without a particular relationship.
- events/manifestations: Unexecuted plans, future employment and water/air work are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Future work and water/air settings broaden the range beyond failure; these settings do not imply poisoning or infection.

## Contradictions

The single entry includes uncertain outcomes and particular work settings. No necessary harmful result follows from those settings.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through action whose execution or conditions are uncertain. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names hindrance, poor results and unclear activity, but also future employment and work connected with water or air. Its infection, poisoning and destruction examples remain historical source claims; the practical settings do not establish those outcomes.
