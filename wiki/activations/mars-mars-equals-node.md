## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 190, Mars + Mars, Node row.

#### Witte Entry

Working community. Concerted action. Betrothal. Sexual union. Erotic unions. Common action. Connected through work or activities. Joint activities.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity connects people through common work or union.
- psychology: No distinct individual attitude is specified.
- body/health: Sexual union is named without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Work community, betrothal and erotic union are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Concerted and joint activity are the practical forms stated.
- conflicts/notes: Connection through activity does not establish harmony, permanence or one motive for every union.

## Contradictions

One source passage provides work and intimate forms of joint activity; no independent comparison is present.

## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through connection formed in common action. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes work communities and concerted tasks alongside betrothal and sexual or erotic union. Shared activity unifies the range, without specifying that every connection has the same intimacy, motive or duration.
