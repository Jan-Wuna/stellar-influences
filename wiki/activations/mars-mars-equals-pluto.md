## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 191, Mars + Mars, Pluto row.

#### Witte Entry

Organizing. Planning. Plans. Duplicate, to copy, to repeat, to multiply. To lead, to supervise or to perform several activities at the same time. To divide, to split, to branch off. To work alternately at different tasks. To do different kinds of work. To work on schedule. Transformation and change in the activity in one’s work and in the working conditions. Actively bring about change.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity is organized, multiplied, divided or changed.
- psychology: Planning is stated without a detailed attitude profile.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Leadership and supervision are explicit work roles.
- events/manifestations: Copying, repeated tasks, schedules, multitasking and changed working conditions are named.
- conflicts/notes: Active change is established without a guarantee of improvement, control or success.

## Contradictions

The single entry lists several ways of arranging and changing activity. Repetition and transformation are different work processes rather than contradictory outcomes.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through the organization and transformation of work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects planning, copying and repetition with divided tasks, supervision, schedules and changing working conditions. The common theme is arranging how activity proceeds, whether by repeating a process or actively changing it; beneficial results are not guaranteed by the entry.
