## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 190, Mars + Mars, Saturn row.

#### Witte Entry

Periodic work. Interruption of work. Sickness. Acute sicknesses. Current affairs. Interruption. The act of separation. Forced separation. A general interruption. Activities leading to separation or which are periodically interrupted. End of action. The main formula for death.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity is periodic, interrupted, separated or ended.
- psychology: No distinct individual attitude is specified.
- body/health: Sickness and the source's “main formula for death” label remain historical claims, not diagnoses or forecasts.
- social/relationship: Separation and forced separation are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Periodic work, interrupted labor and the end of action are named.
- conflicts/notes: Ordinary work intervals are part of the range and must not be equated with fatality.

## Contradictions

The single passage spans routine interruptions and severe endings. These differ in scale without establishing a necessary progression from one to the other.

## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through intervals and endings in activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes periodic work and interrupted tasks alongside separation and ended action. Its sickness references and severe death label remain historical doctrine; they do not make a routine work interruption a medical or fatal prediction.
