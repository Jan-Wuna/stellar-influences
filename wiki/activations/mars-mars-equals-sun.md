- Source reference: PDF p. 190, Mars + Mars, Sun row.

#### Witte Entry

The man. The husband. The active man. The soldier. The action of the living body. Work with physical strength. The working day. Men at work. Daily action. Handyman. Energy level of the body. (In the solar cycle: period concerning to work.)

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal bodily activity is expressed in daily work.
- psychology: No distinct individual attitude is specified.
- body/health: Bodily action, strength and energy level are stated without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Husband, soldier and handyman are historical source roles.
- events/manifestations: Physical work, daily action and the working day are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Male-specific labels do not establish that bodily work is exclusive to men; the retained solar-cycle note is not developed into a timing model here.

## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through the person's bodily action and daily work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names physical strength, the working day and bodily energy alongside husband, soldier and handyman roles. These historical male-specific examples do not establish an exclusive gender principle or a personal identity defined by exertion.
