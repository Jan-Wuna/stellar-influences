## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 190, Mars + Mars, Uranus row.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden event. Exciting action. Energetic act. Fighting. Wounds. Bloody injury. Surgical procedure. Over impulsive or sudden action. Surprising action. Unexpected or new work. Impulsive movements or action. Actions in the field of technology or mathematics.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action is sudden, surprising, impulsive or connected with new work.
- psychology: Impulsiveness and excitement are explicit.
- body/health: Wounds, injury and surgery are historical source examples, not medical predictions.
- social/relationship: Fighting is named without defining every interaction.
- events/manifestations: New work and activity in technology or mathematics are specific applications.
- conflicts/notes: Technical or mathematical activity does not itself imply injury; “explosive” movement was not stated.

## Contradictions

One passage includes sudden harmful events and practical new work. The shared sudden-action theme does not establish the same result in each setting.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through sudden or surprising activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) spans impulsive movement, unexpected work and technological or mathematical action, while retaining fighting, injury and surgery as severe historical examples. Newness and suddenness unite the range without making technical work a predictor of harm.
