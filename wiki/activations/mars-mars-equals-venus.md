## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 190, Mars + Mars, Venus row.

#### Witte Entry

The blood-relationship. Intimate friendship. Sex love. To act with devotion, out of love for the cause. To become intimate. Sex activity. Joy of action. Acting like a maternal female. Passionate activity. Kinship.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action expresses affection, devotion, intimacy or kinship.
- psychology: Passion, devotion and joy in action are explicit.
- body/health: Sexual activity is named without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Blood relations, intimate friendship and sexual love are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Becoming intimate and acting for a loved cause are stated forms.
- conflicts/notes: The maternal-female example remains historical wording; affection does not establish a general reduction of force.

## Contradictions

One entry spans familial, friendly, erotic and cause-related devotion. These are different relationships rather than conflicting accounts.

## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through action motivated by affection or devotion. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects kinship and intimate friendship with sexual love, passionate activity and work for a loved cause. Its maternal example remains particular historical wording; intimacy and devotion can take several forms within this range.
