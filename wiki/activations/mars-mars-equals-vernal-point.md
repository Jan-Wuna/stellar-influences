## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 190, Mars + Mars, Vernal Point row.

#### Witte Entry

Labor, the forces of nature. Injuries. Nature’s power in a general sense. Work and activity in public life. Working energy. Energy in general. The world of labor. To show courage.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity concerns public work, labor and forces in general.
- psychology: Showing courage is explicit.
- body/health: Injuries are historical source examples, not predicted outcomes.
- social/relationship: Public life and the world of labor are the shared settings.
- events/manifestations: Work energy and the forces of nature are named without a particular event.
- conflicts/notes: Visibility or acclaim is not stated; public activity does not by itself imply injury.

## Contradictions

Only one broad entry is present; public work and natural forces are different examples without an independent comparison.

## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through work and energy in a broad public setting. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins public activity and the world of labor with natural forces and showing courage. Injury remains one historical source example; the wider theme does not establish harm or recognition for a particular public action.
