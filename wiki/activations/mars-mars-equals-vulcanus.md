- Source reference: PDF p. 191, Mars + Mars, Vulcanus row.

#### Witte Entry

Great effort. Exercise. Mighty activity. Great power. Acts of violence. Mechanical energies. Unleash power. Energetic forces in action. Muscular strength. Working in the field of high energies.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity involves great effort, strength or released power.
- psychology: No distinct individual attitude is specified.
- body/health: Exercise and muscular strength are stated without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Violent acts are one source possibility.
- events/manifestations: Mechanical energy and work with high energies are explicit fields.
- conflicts/notes: Physical exercise and mechanical work are distinct from violence; great power does not establish its purpose.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through the scale of effort and power in action. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes exercise, muscular strength, mechanical energy and high-energy work alongside violent acts. Force is the shared theme; its bodily or technical use is distinct from using it against others.
