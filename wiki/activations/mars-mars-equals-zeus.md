## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 191, Mars + Mars, Zeus row.

#### Witte Entry

To heat, to fire, to burn. Shooting flames. Danger of fire. Incentive creative work, or forced creative work. Creative activity. Leading activity. Procreation. Engineering. Machines. Military engineering. Officers. To handle firearms. To act goal-oriented. An act followed by consequences. Fires, conflagrations. Directed activity. The urge for deeds. Ignition of a rocket.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity is directed toward creation, ignition or a goal.
- psychology: The urge to act and goal orientation are explicit.
- body/health: Procreation is a historical association without a medical prediction.
- social/relationship: Leading activity, military officers and forced creative work are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Engineering, machines, firearms, fire and rocket ignition are retained.
- conflicts/notes: Fire danger and coercion are particular forms; creative or engineering work does not necessarily cause destruction.

## Contradictions

The single entry combines creative work and directed technical activity with danger or force. These are different uses and conditions of purposeful action.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies the repeated Mars/Mars axis through directed action that creates, ignites or pursues a goal. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) spans creative work, engineering, leadership and procreation, with machines, firearms and rocket ignition as concrete examples. It retains forced work and fire danger without making those conditions necessary to every purposeful undertaking.
