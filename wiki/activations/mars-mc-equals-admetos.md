## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A limited sphere of work. Actions meet with grave resistance. Condemned to inactivity. Operating in or with debris or ruins.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Restricted psychic activity, strengthening of my will, I work with perseverance, I delve into work, my work is restricted, my work with primordial material, my depressive impulses, my actions are blocked, and deep soul work

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: One's work is restricted, blocked or concentrated through perseverance.
- psychology: Will strengthening, delving into work and depressive impulses are ABC themes; the latter is attributed historical language.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte names grave resistance to action without identifying a particular relationship.
- events/manifestations: A limited work field, debris/ruins and work with primordial material are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: Perseverance and deep inner work remain beside restriction; public visibility or a fixed vocation is not stated.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes limited activity; ABC also includes perseverance, strengthened will and depth. These preserve a concentrated response as well as obstruction.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies the Mars/MC axis through limits and concentration in one's own work. A narrowed field and grave resistance in [Witte](#witte-entry) connect with restricted or blocked action in [ABC](#abc-entry), which also retains perseverance, strengthened will and deep soul work. Work in ruins or with primordial material gives concrete settings; the entry does not make every concentrated undertaking unpromising or establish a clinical state.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 64; ABC printed p. 73, PDF p. 35.
