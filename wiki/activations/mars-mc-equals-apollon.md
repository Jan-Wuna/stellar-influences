## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Being generous. To be prudent. Engaged in science or commerce. Activity of a economic expert.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful soul impulses, successful treatment of the soul, I work a lot, I want freedom, my work experiences, my scientific work, my work is successful, much psychological energy for action, and scientific soul work.

- Source reference: printed p. 73, PDF p. 35, Apollon row; the scan reads `ich arbeite viel`, I work a lot, and `psychische Tatkraft`, psychological energy for action.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: One's activity extends into scientific, commercial or experienced work.
- psychology: Generosity, prudence and a wish for freedom are explicit.
- body/health: ABC's successful soul treatment remains a historical claim without a diagnosis or medical outcome specified.
- social/relationship: Witte names an economic expert; no intimate relationship is described.
- events/manifestations: Scientific work, commerce, extensive work and work experience are source examples.
- conflicts/notes: Economic expertise is one role; management and guaranteed professional competence are not established.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes science, commerce and prudence; ABC adds personal work experience and inner-life work. These are different settings rather than a direct conflict.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies the Mars/MC axis through the breadth and experience of one's activity. Scientific or commercial work and prudence in [Witte](#witte-entry) connect with scientific work, extensive effort, experience and a wish for freedom in [ABC](#abc-entry). ABC's soul-work and treatment language remains a particular historical strand, without establishing a medical result or one required occupation.

Evidence: Witte PDF p. 64; ABC printed p. 73, PDF p. 35.
