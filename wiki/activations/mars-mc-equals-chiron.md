## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Your energy helps others to actively and assertively align themselves with higher truths, which has the effect of spiritualizing their ability to confront difficulties and problems, and to assert themselves. You also help them, when necessary, to come into conflict with authorities and the part of them that is telling them what to do.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/MC = Chiron` channels action and vocation into spiritually aligned assertion and constructive conflict with authority.
- Chart expression: helping others act from higher truth, confront problems more consciously, and assert themselves in ways that are both spiritualized and effective, even when this means challenging what tries to control them.
