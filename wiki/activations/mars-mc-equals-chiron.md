## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: “Planets activating the Mars/Midheaven midpoint,” PDF p. 77.

Your energy helps others to actively and assertively align themselves with higher truths, which has the effect of spiritualizing their ability to confront difficulties and problems, and to assert themselves. You also help them, when necessary, to come into conflict with authorities and the part of them that is telling them what to do.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/MC = Chiron`, Chiron specifies the pair through helping others act assertively from what they regard as higher truth. The emphasis is on facing difficulties and questioning both external authorities and internal commands when they obstruct that purpose. This is Sandbach's spiritual interpretation of the exact formula, with no additional health or career outcome established. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry))
