## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “MC + Mars,” PDF p. 64.

To be ready to act in a group, family or association. Decision to marry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Mars,” printed p. 73 (PDF p. 35).

Soulful work community, shared soul impulses, an active soul community, I work in the community, my will toward community, my work community, and my actions in the community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Readiness to act within a community, family, or association.
- psychology: A will to participate and work with others.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a body or health statement.
- social/relationship: Group and family commitments; Witte also names deciding to marry.
- events/manifestations: Community work and a decision toward marriage.
- conflicts/notes: The entries share communal activity; their related tradition is not independent proof, and marriage is a specific example.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears: ABC develops shared inner impulses and work, while Witte also gives the specific decision to marry.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/MC = Cupido`, Cupido specifies personal action through participation in a group: work becomes something undertaken with a community, family, or association. ABC connects this participation with shared inner impulses, while Witte emphasizes readiness to act. His decision to marry is a particular form of that commitment, not a promised outcome. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 64; [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 73 / PDF p. 35)
