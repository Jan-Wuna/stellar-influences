#### Munkasey Entry

Fighting for what you want in life and also taking pains to protect your gains from those who covet them; adds to your anger when others question your status or gains; helps excite you about competitive activities.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/MC = Mars`, repeated Mars specifies the pair as active striving and protection of what has been won.
