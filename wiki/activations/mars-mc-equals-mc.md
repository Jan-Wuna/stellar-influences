## Source Entries

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/MIDHEAVEN With Itself`
- Source page: `254`

#### Munkasey Entry

Brings more energy and emphasis to attaining career status than personal status; working very hard and long to achieve expertise and recognition for your efforts; a tendency to fight to defend your code of ethics, or family.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sustained effort toward career standing, expertise, and recognition.
- psychology: Persistence and a readiness to defend one's ethics.
- body/health: Long, hard work is mentioned without a medical consequence.
- social/relationship: Defense of family and one's code of conduct.
- events/manifestations: Building professional expertise and recognition through prolonged effort.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's single 'With Itself' passage distinguishes career standing from personal status; repeated MC is not a second source.

## Contradictions

The single entry contrasts career standing with personal status while also naming defense of ethics or family; these are different concerns within its account of effort.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/MC = MC`, repeated MC specifies the pair through the effort to attain career standing and recognized expertise. Long work and the defense of one's ethics or family belong to the same account of purposeful striving. Munkasey gives career status more emphasis than personal status in this single passage; it does not establish a medical consequence of that effort. ([Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 254)
