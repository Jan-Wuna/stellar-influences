## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “MC + Mars,” PDF p. 64.

One’s moral mode of action. Mentally active. High-minded insights based on a professional action.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Mars,” printed p. 73 (PDF p. 35).

Actions of the spirit soul, work on spirit and soul, I want the truth, clear soul impulses, my clear actions, my mental activity, my ideal actions, my culture work, and reason impulses of the soul

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action guided by moral judgment, thought, and a desire for truth.
- psychology: Clarity of intention and work on one's mental or spiritual life.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is described.
- social/relationship: Ethical conduct rather than a particular relationship outcome.
- events/manifestations: Mental activity, cultural work, and insight through professional action.
- conflicts/notes: The entries share ethical and intellectual activity; they do not establish moral superiority or a guaranteed vocation.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears: Witte emphasizes moral conduct and insight through work, while ABC expands truth-seeking, clarity, and cultural activity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/MC = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies personal action through ethical judgment and the search for clarity or truth. Mental activity and cultural work belong to this effort to bring ideals into conduct; Witte also describes insight arising from professional action. The reading concerns the orientation of effort, not proof that a person's conduct is morally superior. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 64; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 73 / PDF p. 35)
