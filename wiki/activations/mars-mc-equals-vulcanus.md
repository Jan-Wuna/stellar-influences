## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “MC + Mars,” PDF p. 64.

Energetic. A mighty willpower. Personal usage of force.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Mars,” printed p. 73 (PDF p. 35).

Influence of a psychic treatment, my action influence, my work power, my violence activity, my will to power, my strong will, my will influence, powerful psychic action power, my powerful actions, and strong soul impulses

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong will, energetic action, and the personal use of force.
- psychology: A drive for power and influence.
- body/health: ABC names the influence of a psychological treatment without establishing a medical result.
- social/relationship: Forceful influence on others; violence is one source term, not an inevitable behavior.
- events/manifestations: Powerful activity and concentrated working effort.
- conflicts/notes: The entries support intensity, not automatic public dominance or a particular career outcome.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears: Witte emphasizes personal force and willpower, while ABC extends influence to work, inner impulses, and psychological treatment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/MC = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies personal action through strong will and the capacity to exert force or influence. Energetic effort, a will to power, and powerful actions connect the entries. ABC also names the influence of psychological treatment and violent activity as distinct examples; neither establishes that strength must produce violence. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 64; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 73 / PDF p. 35)
