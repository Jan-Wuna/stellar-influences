## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “MC + Mars,” PDF p. 64.

To make arrangements that have to be carried out immediately. To force a matter through. No opposition tolerated.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Mars,” printed p. 73 (PDF p. 35).

I work strenuously, I lead at work, my work beginning, my will to procreate, my shaping of work, my creative work, my work achievements, the direction of my will, my procreative actions, and psychological leadership work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed effort, work initiation, and forcing a matter into execution.
- psychology: Urgency and an unwillingness to tolerate opposition in Witte.
- body/health: ABC names procreative activity, without a health claim.
- social/relationship: Leading work and imposing immediate execution.
- events/manifestations: Creative work, beginnings, performance, and arrangements to be carried out at once.
- conflicts/notes: Immediate execution without opposition is Witte's emphasis; ABC's creative and psychological leadership need not carry that coercive demand.

## Contradictions

The entries share direction of work, but Witte's demand for immediate compliance is stronger than ABC's descriptions of creative effort and leadership.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/MC = Zeus`, Zeus specifies personal action through direction and execution. ABC names work beginnings, creative effort, work achievements, and psychological leadership; Witte demands that arrangements be carried out immediately and allows no opposition. That forceful demand is his emphasis rather than a necessary feature of all leadership. ABC also names procreative action as a separate example. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 64; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 73 / PDF p. 35)
