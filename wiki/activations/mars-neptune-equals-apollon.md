## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptune,” PDF p. 195.

Many are under the spell of false hopes. Infection and destruction of many people. Extensive destruction.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptun,” printed p. 305 (PDF p. 151).

Expansion of work on/in or with water, successful work in the air, successful future activity, processing metaphysical knowledge, increased uncertainty at work, increasing activity of water, and increasing metaphysical activity

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity or uncertainty expanded in scope, with sharply different favorable and destructive examples.
- psychology: False hopes in Witte; increased work uncertainty in ABC.
- body/health: Infection of many people is Witte's historical medical claim.
- social/relationship: Witte places the effects among many people; ABC does not describe a specific relationship.
- events/manifestations: Extensive destruction in Witte; expanded water work, successful air or future work, and metaphysical activity in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's destructive mass effects differ materially from ABC's successful air and future work; expansion is not uniformly harmful here.

## Contradictions

The sources differ in their range: Witte describes widespread harm, while ABC includes successful activity as well as uncertainty. These claims cannot be flattened into one exclusively harmful outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Neptune = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through expanded activity or effects involving many. Witte names widespread false hopes and destruction, while ABC includes successful work in air or for the future as well as increased uncertainty and water-related or metaphysical activity. The common increase in scope therefore covers different directions of activity; Witte's mass-infection statement remains an attributed historical claim. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 195; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 305 / PDF p. 151)
