### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: “Planets activating the Mars/Neptune midpoint,” PDF p. 73.

Your vibration naturally helps others to align their desire for action with their own intuition, so that their actions become not only more refined and subtle, but more effective. You also help them to deal assertively with the psychic forces at work around them, so that they can align themselves with the positive ones as well as overcome the draining and confusing effects of the negative ones.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Neptune = Chiron`, Chiron specifies the pair through helping others bring action into alignment with intuition. Sandbach connects subtlety with effectiveness: assertion is refined while people learn to respond to influences he describes as supportive, draining, or confusing. This is the single entry's spiritual account, not a separate medical claim. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 73)
