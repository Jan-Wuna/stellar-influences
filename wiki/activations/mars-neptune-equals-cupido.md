## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptune,” PDF p. 195.

Destruction of family happiness. Dissolved community. A grave misunderstanding in the society. Accident. Divorce.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptun,” printed p. 305 (PDF p. 151).

Refusal to work by a community, dissolution of a work community, mistaken actions by a community, joint work on/in or with water, weak will of a community, an unclear community of action, and cooperation in the air.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Unclear or weakened joint action and disruption of communal bonds.
- psychology: Grave misunderstanding, weak collective will, and uncertain joint action.
- body/health: Witte lists an accident but gives no specific medical condition.
- social/relationship: Family unhappiness, dissolved community, work-community difficulties, and divorce.
- events/manifestations: Work refusal, communal dissolution, mistaken action, and joint work in air or water.
- conflicts/notes: Joint air or water work is a distinct example from disrupted community; divorce is one Witte possibility rather than a necessary result.

## Contradictions

Both sources describe communal difficulty, but ABC also names ordinary shared work in air or water. The narrower family-breakdown reading does not exhaust the entries.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Neptune = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through shared activity that may become unclear, weakened, or disrupted. ABC's refusal to work, mistakes, and dissolution of a work community connect with Witte's grave misunderstanding and dissolved community. ABC also names joint water-related work and cooperation in the air, so the formula is not restricted to family breakdown. Witte's damaged family happiness, divorce, and accident remain specific possibilities. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 195; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 305 / PDF p. 151)
