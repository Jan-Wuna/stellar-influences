## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptune,” PDF p. 195.

Happiness combined with great disappointments. Denial of happiness. Weak lungs. Loss of property.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptun,” printed p. 304 (PDF p. 151).

Success through sensitive actions, successful work in or by water or in the air, incorrect financial actions, limited successes of the will, unclear legal activity, uncertain labour law, uncertain work successes, and work on water law.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source: “Mars/Neptune,” printed p. 211, entry 0727.

Rich and colorful imagination, sensitivity, inspiration, spiritual perception despite a weak vitality. A fortunate turn or break after suffering disappointments or a disappointment under otherwise fortunate circumstances, unhappiness or ill luck in the midst of wealth. Pulmonary diseases.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: “Planets activating the Mars/Neptune midpoint,” PDF pp. 72–73.

An eagerness and enthusiasm to engage in passionate idealism. The ability to understand subtle conflicts that are going on. To have good luck with knowing intuitively what to do.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `236`

#### Munkasey Entry

Amplification of your dreams and visions and the power or force you use to bring them to reality; excessive or impractical planning for how you should spend your energy resources; theories about the energies that mystics use.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Inspired or subtle activity with fortune mixed with disappointment.
- psychology: Imagination, spiritual perception, idealistic enthusiasm, and potentially impractical plans.
- body/health: Weak lungs and pulmonary disease are historical claims in Witte and Ebertin.
- social/relationship: The entries do not establish a particular relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: Successful air or water work, water-law work, financial errors, uncertain legal activity, property loss, and reversals of fortune.
- conflicts/notes: Inspiration and disappointment coexist; the medical claims do not establish a causal effect of optimism.

## Contradictions

Success and disappointment occur together in Witte and Ebertin. ABC and Sandbach also retain successful sensitive or intuitive action, while Munkasey warns of impractical plans.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Neptune = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through enlarged imagination, idealistic enthusiasm, and intuitive possibility. Fortune remains mixed: disappointment can accompany wealth or be followed by a fortunate turn, and Munkasey warns of impractical energy plans. ABC includes successful sensitive, water, or air work alongside financial errors and uncertain legal activity, with water law as a particular field. ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), printed p. 211, entry 0727; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF pp. 72–73; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 236; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 304 / PDF p. 151) Witte's weak lungs and Ebertin's pulmonary disease remain historical medical claims, not consequences inferred from optimism. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 195)
