## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptune,” PDF p. 195.

Rejection of unions. Connection to contaminated or mentally depraved persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptun,” printed p. 304 (PDF p. 151).

Establishing relationships with water, mistaken actions in connections, contact activity in the air, weak impulses in a connection, energetic contacts by or in water, uncertain work connections, and wanting connections with metaphysics.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source: “Mars/Neptune,” printed p. 212 (PDF p. 214), entry 0731 and its footnote 43.

Undermining of associations through lack of stability, unreliability. Weakness of will or negative attitude. Association with weak or sick persons.

Footnote 43: “Node = Mars/Neptune has frequently been found in the case of hospitalization.”

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: “Planets activating the Mars/Neptune midpoint,” PDF p. 73.

To seek out people one can psychically dominate, or who psychically dominate you. To want connections with others with whom one can work toward an ideal. One is learning in this lifetime how to refine one’s ability to act, and how to actively and constructively work with psychic energy.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `236`

#### Munkasey Entry

Meeting or encountering people who have access to subtle or mystical energy forces; meetings with new people who try to deceive you about their stamina or competitive capabilities; draws you into punishing romances.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections affected by unstable assertion, or formed around subtle activity and shared ideals.
- psychology: Weak will, unreliability, and the wish either to dominate or to refine action.
- body/health: Ebertin names sick associates and reports a hospitalization association in footnote43; these are historical claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Rejection of unions, unreliable ties, spiritual collaboration, deception, and punishing romance are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Air- or water-related contacts and encounters with people involved in mystical practices.
- conflicts/notes: Ideal cooperation and domination are different possibilities. Witte's stigmatizing labels remain historical wording, not judgments adopted by the synthesis.

## Contradictions

Sandbach explicitly includes ideal cooperation and psychic domination, while Ebertin emphasizes unstable associations. The different possibilities prevent treating every connection as either helpful or harmful.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Neptune = Node`, Node specifies the pair through connections that can support a shared ideal or become uncertain, unreliable, or coercive. Sandbach includes psychic domination and constructive work toward an ideal; Ebertin emphasizes unstable associations, while Munkasey adds deceptive encounters and punishing romance. ABC's contacts involving air or water and wish for metaphysical connections preserve a range beyond broken unions. Witte's stigmatizing labels and Ebertin's hospitalization observation remain attributed historical statements.
