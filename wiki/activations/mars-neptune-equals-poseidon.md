## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptune,” PDF p. 195.

Ingenious and high minded disposition or mental sickness. Madness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptun,” printed p. 305 (PDF p. 151).

Insight through intuitional impulses, insights from metaphysical activity, subtle spiritual force, creating metaphysical insights, unclear ideas in activity, weak spiritual will, spiritual future work, and refined cultural work

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intuitive or metaphysical insight brought into activity, with possible lack of clarity.
- psychology: High-minded thinking, unclear ideas, and weak spiritual will are separate source terms.
- body/health: Witte's mental sickness and 'madness' are attributed historical language, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: No specific relationship outcome is described.
- events/manifestations: Spiritual future work and refined cultural work in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC supplies constructive activity as well as uncertainty; Witte's mental-health alternatives do not establish a course of deterioration.

## Contradictions

Witte gives ingenuity and mental sickness as alternatives. ABC expands the activity into insight, uncertainty, future work, and culture, without specifying a progression between them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Neptune = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through intuition, metaphysical understanding, and the attempt to make spiritual insight active. ABC also names unclear ideas and weak spiritual will, so insight is not equated with certain direction. Its future and cultural work broaden the range beyond Witte's short contrast between high-minded ingenuity and mental sickness. Witte's “madness” remains historical source language and does not establish an inevitable decline under psychic strain. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 195; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 305 / PDF p. 151)
