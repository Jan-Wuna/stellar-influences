## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptune,” PDF p. 195.

Lasting periodic work which cause destructions. Articular rheumatism. Slow death as a result of a pestilential diseases.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptun,” printed p. 305 (PDF p. 151).

Serious work for the future, hard work in the air, inhibitions through weakness of will, concentrated work in metaphysics, problematic work on/with or in water, a gradually emerging will to separate, and dissolution actions involving losses.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source: “Mars/Neptune,” printed p. 211, entry 0728.

Instability, feelings of inferiority, misdirected energy. Unfortunate consequences as a result of a weak will, lasting weakness or illness. (Malfunctioning of the skin, weak bones, toxins in the body, an epidemic.)

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: “Planets activating the Mars/Neptune midpoint,” PDF p. 73.

To feel unable or afraid of acting on one’s intuition. To be afraid of psychic warfare and attacks happening on subtle and energetic planes. Confused or chaotic action that happens over and over and that one is likely to overcome only slowly.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `236`

#### Munkasey Entry

Frustrations encountered when punishing those who deceive others; helps block the anger you show toward people who avoid reality; pessimism over the arguments received from clerics or mystics; a yogi who shows discipline.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Persistent difficulty, inhibited action, and slowly overcome confusion.
- psychology: Fear of acting on intuition, inferiority, frustration, or disciplined restraint.
- body/health: Witte's rheumatism and epidemic death and Ebertin's skin, bone, toxin, and illness statements remain historical claims.
- social/relationship: Blocked anger toward deceivers or people avoiding reality; pessimism about mystics.
- events/manifestations: Serious future work, hard air work, concentrated metaphysical work, recurring confusion, and dissolution involving losses.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's disciplined yogi and ABC's concentrated work remain constructive possibilities alongside difficulty and loss.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize restraint and difficulty but include disciplined practice and concentrated work. These constructive examples prevent equating persistence with disease or failure.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Neptune = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through persistent difficulty or restraint in putting subtle intentions into action. Sandbach describes recurring confusion overcome slowly; ABC includes serious future work and concentrated metaphysical activity alongside inhibited will and dissolution involving losses. Munkasey's disciplined yogi provides a constructive example beside frustration and blocked anger. Witte's and Ebertin's disease, toxin, and death statements remain attributed historical claims, not a causal explanation of discouragement.
