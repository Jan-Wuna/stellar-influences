## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptune,” PDF p. 195.

Sudden destruction. Accidents. Attempted assassination. Emergency slaughter due to accident or pestilential diseases.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptun,” printed p. 305 (PDF p. 151).

Work technique with water, work with metaphysical experiences, disappointing work events, sensitivity to rhythmic impulses, technical activity in the air, technical work for the future, weak impulses of will, and sensitive rhythmic impulses.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source: “Mars/Neptune,” printed p. 211, entry 0729.

Changing energy levels, states of weakness emerging suddenly. Sudden disadvantages caused through lack of energy, a life crisis, an illness or accident.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: “Planets activating the Mars/Neptune midpoint,” PDF p. 73.

To be suddenly inspired to act intuitively. One has a gift for picking up on subtle energies and the conflicts they may be creating, and then finding ways of liberating oneself from them. To be able to help others in unique or unsuspected ways.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `236`

#### Munkasey Entry

An unusual stamina to speed up the outcome of activities; an unconventional approach to forcing ideas on idealism or socialism on others; a noisy rebellion against the aggravating dreams or visions forced by others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden changes in action or energy and unexpectedly intuitive responses.
- psychology: Abrupt inspiration, sensitivity to subtle conflict, rebellion, or unconventional drive.
- body/health: Ebertin's sudden weakness and illness and Witte's disease-related destruction are historical claims.
- social/relationship: Unusual ways of helping others or resisting imposed visions.
- events/manifestations: Crises, accidents, sudden destruction, disappointing work events, and technical water, air, or future work.
- conflicts/notes: Ebertin's sudden weakness contrasts with Munkasey's unusual stamina; intuitive release remains another supported expression.

## Contradictions

Sudden weakness in Ebertin and unusual stamina in Munkasey are opposing energy possibilities. Sandbach's helpful intuition also differs from Witte's emphasis on destruction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Neptune = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through abrupt shifts: Ebertin describes changing energy and sudden weakness, while Sandbach describes intuitive inspiration and unexpected ways to help or escape subtle conflict. Munkasey's unusual stamina and rebellion provide another active expression. ABC includes disappointing work events, technical work with water or air and for the future, and sensitivity to rhythm. Witte's destruction, assassination, accident, and disease examples remain source-specific possibilities rather than a necessary outcome of sudden inspiration.
