## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptune,” PDF p. 195.

Great destruction. Great weakness that is caused by an epidemic.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Neptun,” printed p. 305 (PDF p. 151).

Work with water or wind energy, influential activity on/in or with water, disappointing power actions, weakened strength of will, rejecting violent actions, metaphysical energies in activity, and unsettling a trading power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful activity or major destruction, with force that may be weakened or uncertain.
- psychology: Weakened strength of will and disappointment in power actions.
- body/health: Great weakness from an epidemic is Witte's historical claim.
- social/relationship: Influence through water activity, rejecting violence, and unsettling a trading power.
- events/manifestations: Work with water or wind energy, metaphysical energies in action, and great destruction are distinct examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC explicitly names rejection of violent actions; force or scale does not imply destructive intent in every expression.

## Contradictions

Witte's great destruction contrasts with ABC's energy work and rejection of violence. Weakened will and influential activity are also distinct possibilities in ABC.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Neptune = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through the scale or force of activity. ABC includes work with water or wind energy, influential water activity, and metaphysical energies in action, alongside disappointing power actions and weakened will. It explicitly names rejecting violence. Witte emphasizes great destruction and epidemic-related weakness; that historical claim and the contrasting ABC examples do not make every powerful expression catastrophic. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 195; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 305 / PDF p. 151)
