## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Node + Mars,” PDF p. 143.

Working community in a small circle. To be a participant in or witness to terrorist acts. Clique.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mondknoten + Mars,” printed p. 213 (PDF p. 105).

Perseverance in a relationship through energy for action, blocking actions in connections, creating firm connections, thorough mediation activity, establishing firm connections, and oppressive actions within a connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Persistent or fixed connections and work within a small circle.
- psychology: Perseverance in maintaining a relationship; blocking activity is also named.
- body/health: No medical effect is described.
- social/relationship: Small working communities, cliques, mediation, or oppressive relations; Witte separately includes participating in or witnessing terrorism.
- events/manifestations: Establishing durable links, thorough mediation, and blockages in connections.
- conflicts/notes: Witte distinguishes participant from witness; his exceptional terrorist-act example does not characterize every small group.

## Contradictions

Firm connections and thorough mediation in ABC can support continuity, while its blockages and oppression restrict it. Witte's small circle gives a scale of cooperation without deciding which possibility applies.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Node = Admetos`, Admetos specifies shared action through persistence, firm links, and a small circle of cooperation. ABC's durable connections and thorough mediation support continuity, while blockages and oppressive actions describe restriction. Witte's small working community or clique gives a concrete social scale; he separately names either participating in or witnessing terrorist acts. That exceptional example does not imply that every small group is oppressive or that a witness is a perpetrator. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 143; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 213 / PDF p. 105)
