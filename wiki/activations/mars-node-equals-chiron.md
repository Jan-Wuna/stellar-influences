## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: “Planets activating the Mars/Node midpoint,” PDF p. 75.

Your vibration can help other people to reach out and connect with those individuals and groups with whom they need to connect, as well as to find new and more effective ways of asserting themselves. You also help them sometimes to realize that some of their old ways of asserting themselves are no longer effective. You also have the innate power to heal conflict in groups so that they can get more accomplished and be more dynamic.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Helping people connect and renew effective assertion in groups.
- psychology: Recognizing that former ways of asserting oneself no longer work.
- body/health: Healing group conflict is social and spiritual language here, not a medical claim.
- social/relationship: Connecting people with needed individuals or groups and resolving conflict.
- events/manifestations: More effective collective action after changes in assertion or group dynamics.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's healing language refers to group conflict and effectiveness; no separate medical claim is established.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Node = Chiron`, Chiron specifies shared action through helping people make needed connections and find more effective ways to assert themselves. Sandbach links this with recognizing ineffective older patterns and healing conflict so a group can accomplish more. The healing language concerns group dynamics in this single spiritual account, without establishing a medical effect. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 75)
