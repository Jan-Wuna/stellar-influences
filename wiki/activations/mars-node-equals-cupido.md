## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “NODES + MARS,” PDF p. 143.

To plan mutual union. To enter into matrimony.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mondknoten + Mars,” printed p. 213 (PDF p. 105).

Encounters in a work community, relations with a community of action, a joint work meeting, shared willing within a connection, contacts in a work community, and a community that creates connections.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Joint action that forms or sustains community and partnership.
- psychology: Shared willingness to work together.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Work communities and cooperation; Witte adds planned union and marriage.
- events/manifestations: Joint work meetings, contacts within a community, and entering matrimony.
- conflicts/notes: Marriage is Witte's specific formal example; ABC describes a broader community of work and shared intention.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap on intentional union and shared activity. Work communities in ABC do not contradict Witte's marriage example or make all communal ties matrimonial.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Node = Cupido`, Cupido specifies shared action through a community or partnership formed around doing things together. ABC's joint work meeting, shared willing, and community contacts connect with Witte's planned mutual union. Marriage is Witte's particular formal example, while ABC retains the broader communal setting: people meet, act together, and form further connections. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 143; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 213 / PDF p. 105)
