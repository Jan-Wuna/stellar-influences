## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “NODES + MARS,” PDF p. 143. “Free purchasable love” is printed as retained; its intended meaning is unclear.

Unpleasantness during working hours. Free purchasable love. To join together to perpetrate evil activities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mondknoten + Mars,” printed p. 213 (PDF p. 105).

actions in a dangerous connection, activating relationships in distress, dangerous work connection, establishment of criminal contacts, disadvantageous change in a relationship, activity of a criminal connection, and activating connections from the past

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult or dangerous connections, work unpleasantness, and criminal cooperation in particular examples.
- psychology: Activating contacts in distress or returning to past connections.
- body/health: No medical effect is specified.
- social/relationship: Dangerous work ties, criminal contacts, and disadvantageous changes in relationships.
- events/manifestations: Work difficulties, harmful joint activity, and renewed past connections.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's printed “Free purchasable love” remains unclear and supplies no confident sexuality or exploitation claim.

## Contradictions

ABC includes activating contacts in need or from the past alongside dangerous or criminal ties. These different contexts do not make every difficult connection criminal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Node = Hades`, Hades specifies shared action through difficult or dangerous contacts and work circumstances. Witte's work unpleasantness and harmful joint activity meet ABC's dangerous work ties or criminal contacts, but ABC also names activating relationships in distress and reconnecting with the past. These contexts do not all imply vice. Witte's printed “Free purchasable love” remains unclear and supplies no confident sexuality or exploitation claim. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 143; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 213 / PDF p. 105)
