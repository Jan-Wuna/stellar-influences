## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page: `143` (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Inclinations to flirtations. Mutual activities with others. Common working interests with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source page (printed): `212` · PDF page: `105`

#### ABC Entry

My relations with work, my contact activity, my energy for action in relationships, my mediation activity, inner or emotional activities in a relationship, an inner or emotional link with work, wanting inner or emotional contacts.

Translation checked against “Mondknoten + Mars,” MC row, printed p. 212 / PDF p. 105.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source page (printed): `218` · Entry code: `0757`

#### Ebertin Entry

Integration of one's own individuality into the community, good teamworker or partner in work. Entering into individual relationships, a sex-union, (marriage).

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- PDF page: `75` (complete Midheaven entry; chapter starts on p. 74).

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s highest spiritual attainment is to come together with others for the purpose of constructively working together and resolving problems.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page (printed): `244`

#### Munkasey Entry

The recognition you achieve with your ideas, and the help you have from groups and associates in achieving distinction; your experience and maturity at being able to control and direct the will of the collective masses.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal involvement in shared work and collective purpose.
- psychology: Integration of individuality into cooperation, problem-solving, and inner or emotional contact.
- body/health: No distinct health claim is stated.
- social/relationship: Work partnerships, flirtation and intimate union.
- events/manifestations: Contact and mediation activity, recognition aided by groups, and directing collective effort.
- conflicts/notes: ABC connects personal work with inner or emotional involvement; group recognition and intimacy remain different settings.

## Contradictions

The entries move between intimate partnership and public group work. These are different settings for personal participation, not opposing claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Node = MC`, MC specifies the Mars/Node axis through one's own place in joint action: bringing individual effort into a partnership, resolving problems with others and gaining recognition through associates. ABC places this involvement in one's relation to work, contact and mediation activity, and inner or emotional connections. The personal involvement can be intimate, including flirtation or marriage, or collective, including directing a group's will. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 143; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 212 / PDF p. 105; [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), entry 0757; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 75; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 244)
