## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Thoughts concerned with union with the other sex. Intentions of concerted action.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Working meetings with youth, activating relations with youth, connecting thought and action, creating media connections, writing work mediation, establishing links in youth, and communicating youth work

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

One's personal attitude toward the community in general, inclination to form associations to serve one's own ends, intended cooperation or collaboration. Inclination to think about a union based entirely on physical attraction.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To desire one’s ideas and communicating to have a positive effect on groups and individuals. What one says can cause conflict, but this can be a good thing. One is developing in this lifetime the ability to say what one thinks.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `244`

#### Munkasey Entry

Articulating your needs and views to others; adds mental agility to field questions and present forceful, direct answers which close debate on the issues involved; life-long friends who meet during sport activities at school.
