## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “NODES + MARS,” PDF p. 143.

Mighty work-community or common activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mondknoten + Mars,” printed p. 213 (PDF p. 105).

Efforts toward influential contacts, influential activity as a mediator, establishing energy connections, violent activity in a connection, creating powerful contacts, activating forces in a relationship, and establishing connections to power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful joint activity and influential connections.
- psychology: Effort directed toward influence and activating strength within a relationship.
- body/health: No bodily or medical consequence is supplied.
- social/relationship: Influential mediation, powerful contacts, and connections to authority or power.
- events/manifestations: Strong work communities, energy connections, and forceful activity within ties.
- conflicts/notes: Violent activity is explicitly one ABC example; it does not define every powerful community or connection.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap on strong common activity. ABC adds specific channels of influence and a violent possibility without making violence the only expression of strength.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Node = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies shared action through powerful communities and influential contacts. ABC makes the channels concrete: mediation, energy connections, activating forces within a relationship, and forming ties to power. Its violent activity remains one named possibility rather than the necessary meaning of strong cooperation. The organizing theme is the strength brought into a connection through mediation or direct action. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 143; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 213 / PDF p. 105)
