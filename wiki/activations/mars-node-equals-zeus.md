## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “NODES + MARS,” PDF p. 143.

Determined, energetic work in a community. Sex union with consequences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mondknoten + Mars,” printed p. 213 (PDF p. 105).

Strenuous connecting activity, beginning an active connection, leadership activity within a connection, creating contacts for leadership, wanting creative contacts, procreative efforts within a connection, and purposeful action within a connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Determined shared action, initiation, and leadership within connections.
- psychology: Purposeful effort and a wish for creative contacts.
- body/health: Procreative efforts and consequential sexual union are named without a medical outcome.
- social/relationship: Community work, active partnerships, and contacts for leadership.
- events/manifestations: Beginning connections, energetic community work, and procreative activity.
- conflicts/notes: Witte does not specify the consequences of sexual union; neither timing nor a particular result can be supplied from this phrase.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize purposeful activity in connection. ABC broadens Witte's community and sexual examples with leadership and creative contacts.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Node = Zeus`, Zeus specifies shared action through determined effort, beginning active connections, and leadership within them. ABC adds creative contacts and procreative effort; Witte names energetic work in a community and a sexual union with consequences. Those consequences remain unspecified, so the entry supports purposeful involvement without establishing an immediate or far-reaching result in every case. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 143; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 213 / PDF p. 105)
