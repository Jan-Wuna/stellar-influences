## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Pluto = Apollon`, Apollon specifies developing work and plans through expansion and the pursuit of knowledge. Witte names elaborate plans and scientific work; ABC adds growing efforts toward knowledge, unfolding scientific activity, and many changes as work develops. Successful development is explicitly included, without making complexity or scale a guarantee of success. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 196; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 307 / PDF p. 152)
