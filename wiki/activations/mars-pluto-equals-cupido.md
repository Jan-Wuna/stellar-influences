## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Pluto,” PDF p. 196.

Marriage plans. Community planning. Artistic plans.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Pluto,” printed p. 307 (PDF p. 152).

Development work of a community, a changing work community, developing a synthesis of work, growing artistic activity, transformation of a work community, unfolding the will of a community, and changes within a work community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Developing or planning communal and artistic activity.
- psychology: Unfolding collective will toward change.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Changing work communities and plans for marriage or shared life.
- events/manifestations: Community planning, artistic plans, growing art activity, and changes in a work group.
- conflicts/notes: Marriage is Witte's particular example; communal development is broader than a change in marital status.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears: Witte names plans, while ABC describes development and change in communities or artistic activity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Pluto = Cupido`, Cupido specifies developing work and plans through a community or artistic activity. Witte names marriage, community, and art plans; ABC develops the communal side through changing work groups, collective will, and growing artistic work. The connection is planned or developing shared activity, with marriage as one particular form rather than the necessary outcome of every community change. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 196; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 307 / PDF p. 152)
