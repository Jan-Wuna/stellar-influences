## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Pluto,” PDF p. 196.

Collateral government. Plans of the government. The power of the government is split or divided among several. Great plans. Plans of leading persons. Great organizer.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Pluto,” printed p. 307 (PDF p. 152).

Developing special work, development toward independent activity, state development work, changing actions of the state, growing activities of the state, important change of activity, and unfolding the will of the boss.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Important or independent development and governmental or leadership plans.
- psychology: Unfolding a leader's will and developing independence.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: State activity, leading people, and government power divided among several.
- events/manifestations: Government plans, major organization, special work, and important activity changes.
- conflicts/notes: Divided governmental power is Witte's particular arrangement; independent activity in ABC is a broader work possibility.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears: Witte emphasizes government and leading planners, while ABC extends important development to independent activity and changes of work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Pluto = Kronos`, Kronos specifies developing work and plans through independence, important activity, and public authority. ABC includes development toward independent work and changes in state activity or a boss's will. Witte adds major plans and organization, with governmental power divided among several as a specific arrangement. The entries therefore include both independent development and plans carried by leading people or institutions. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 196; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 307 / PDF p. 152)
