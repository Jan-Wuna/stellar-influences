## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Pluto,” PDF p. 196.

Plans and projects which concern the generality. Organizing, supervising the general work. General administration. Civil Service.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Pluto,” printed p. 306 (PDF p. 152).

developmental actions in public, change of public will, changing actions in public, growing public work, change of a public activity, and unfolding of will in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public development, planning, and organization of common work.
- psychology: Changes and unfolding of will in public life.
- body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public administration, civil service, and supervision of general work.
- events/manifestations: Public projects, growing public activity, and changes in public work.
- conflicts/notes: Civil service is a stated setting; the entries do not require a particular governmental office or project scale.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears: Witte emphasizes organizing public work, while ABC describes changes and growth in that public field.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Pluto = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies developing action and plans through public work. Witte names administration, supervision, and projects concerning the public; ABC adds growing activity and changes in public will or work. The shared theme is organizing or changing activity in a collective field, with civil service as one explicit setting. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 196; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 306 / PDF p. 152)
