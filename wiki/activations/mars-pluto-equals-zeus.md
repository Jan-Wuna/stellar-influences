## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Pluto,” PDF p. 196.

Plan to provide work. Plans for increased production. War planning, work distribution.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Pluto,” printed p. 307 (PDF p. 152).

A strenuous beginning of work, developing leadership activity, changing work goals, unfolding creative work, changed work performance, growing impulses toward achievement, change of work leadership, and developing the will to procreate.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Plans and development directed toward work provision, production, and leadership.
- psychology: Growing achievement impulses and a developing procreative will.
- body/health: Procreative will is named without a medical claim or reproductive outcome.
- social/relationship: Work distribution and changes in leadership.
- events/manifestations: Increased production plans, creative work, changed goals, and war planning as Witte's specific example.
- conflicts/notes: War planning and procreative will are distinct source examples; neither defines all productive or creative work.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears: Witte emphasizes work provision and allocation, while ABC develops leadership, goals, creativity, and achievement within work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Pluto = Zeus`, Zeus specifies developing action and plans through production, work allocation, and leadership. Witte names providing work, increasing output, and distributing tasks, with war planning as a particular example. ABC adds strenuous beginnings, changed goals or leadership, creative work, and growing achievement impulses; its developing procreative will is another distinct expression. The common thread is directed development of work rather than one necessary kind of project. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 196; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 307 / PDF p. 152)
