## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Poseidon,” PDF p. 204.

Moral attitude of others. Intellectual activity with others. To be under the intellectual influence of others or to influence others intellectually, ethically or morally.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Poseidon,” printed p. 322 (PDF p. 160).

Others create clarity, work with others' insights, work with a thoughtful partner, clear will of a partner, cultural work at the location, a partner who creates culture, spiritual work with a partner, activity at a cultural place, and rational work in the surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intellectual or moral activity expressed through partners and immediate surroundings.
- psychology: Clarity gained with others and a clear partner's will.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Mutual intellectual, ethical, or moral influence and shared spiritual work.
- events/manifestations: Local cultural work, work with others' insights, and rational activity in the surroundings.
- conflicts/notes: Influence runs in both directions in Witte; the entries do not place one partner automatically above the other.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears: Witte describes reciprocal influence, while ABC supplies partner and cultural-work settings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Asc`, Asc specifies intellectual and ethical activity through encounters, partners, and the local environment. Witte explicitly allows both influencing others and being influenced by them; ABC gives clarity from others' insights, shared spiritual work, and cultural activity at a place. The emphasis is reciprocal exchange and concrete work with others rather than an automatic claim of moral authority. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 204; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 322 / PDF p. 160)
