## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Poseidon,” PDF p. 204.

Marriages or associations resulting from like attitudes. Work or combat together with others for a certain ideology in economy, politics or religion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Poseidon,” printed p. 323 (PDF p. 160).

Work of a cultural community, an intellectual work community, intellectual synthesis work, creating clarity together, producing the whole truth, a community of action around ideals, clarity within a community of will, and rational activity by a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intellectual or cultural activity organized through shared attitudes and ideals.
- psychology: Collective clarity, shared will, and a concern with truth.
- body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is supplied.
- social/relationship: Like-minded marriages or associations and communities working or struggling for an ideology.
- events/manifestations: Cultural work, intellectual synthesis, and joint economic, political, or religious activity.
- conflicts/notes: Shared claims to truth describe a group's aim; they do not establish that its ideology is correct or that every association involves combat.

## Contradictions

The entries share activity grounded in common attitudes. Witte includes ideological combat, while ABC emphasizes cultural work, synthesis, and clarity; neither exhausts the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies intellectual and ethical activity through a community joined by like attitudes. Witte includes marriages or associations and joint work or struggle for an economic, political, or religious ideology. ABC adds cultural work, intellectual synthesis, and creating clarity together. These describe shared conviction and collective inquiry, without proving the group's truth claims or making every such community combative. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 204; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 323 / PDF p. 160)
