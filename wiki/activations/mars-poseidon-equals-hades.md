## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Poseidon,” PDF p. 204.

Morally base acts. Lack of ethics or morality.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Poseidon,” printed p. 323 (PDF p. 160).

Insights into work-related criminality, dangerous activity involving ideals, creating dangerous truths, clear renunciation of the will, cultural activity in the past, criminalization of work with ideals, deficient mental or spiritual work, and hidden mental or spiritual will.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intellectual or ethical activity associated with deficiency, danger, or moral failure in different examples.
- psychology: Renunciation or concealment of will and difficult engagement with truth or ideals.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Work-related criminality or criminalization of ideal work; Witte separately names lack of morality.
- events/manifestations: Dangerous truths, deficient mental work, and cultural activity in the past.
- conflicts/notes: Criminalization of ideal work and past cultural activity do not by themselves describe immoral intent; Witte's judgment remains attributed.

## Contradictions

Witte gives moral condemnation, while ABC includes dangerous truths, criminalized ideal work, and past cultural activity as well as deficiency. The broader ABC range cannot all be reduced to personal moral failure.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Hades`, Hades specifies intellectual and ethical activity through difficult, dangerous, concealed, or deficient expressions. Witte calls the acts morally base; ABC also includes insight into work-related criminality, dangerous truths, and criminalization of work with ideals. Those contexts are not equivalent to the same immoral motive. Past cultural work and renunciation of will further broaden the examples beyond Witte's brief judgment. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 204; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 323 / PDF p. 160)
