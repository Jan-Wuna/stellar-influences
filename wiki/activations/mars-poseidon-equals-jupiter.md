## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Poseidon,” PDF p. 204.

Satisfaction through the feeling of acting ethically and morally right. Satisfaction of acting legally. Happiness through one’s own mental activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Poseidon,” printed p. 322 (PDF p. 160).

Success through producing truth, successful cultural work, creating financial clarity, creating joy through truth, a mental or spiritual right to work, financial work with ideals, legal actions guided by ideals, clarity through legal activity, and rational labour law.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Satisfaction through ethical, legal, intellectual, or cultural activity.
- psychology: Joy in truth and the feeling of acting morally or legally right.
- body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is supplied.
- social/relationship: Work with legal or financial ideals and clarity, without a specific relationship outcome.
- events/manifestations: Successful culture work, financial clarity, rational labour law, and happiness through mental activity.
- conflicts/notes: Feeling morally right is Witte's wording; satisfaction does not establish objective moral or legal correctness.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears: Witte emphasizes satisfaction, while ABC develops successful cultural, truth-oriented, legal, and financial activity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies intellectual and ethical activity through satisfaction, joy, and successful work. Witte emphasizes feeling that one's action is morally right, satisfaction in legal action, and happiness through mental activity. ABC adds cultural success, financial clarity, truth, and ideals expressed in law or finance. The entries describe felt satisfaction and a constructive range of work without establishing that every such conviction is correct. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 204; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 322 / PDF p. 160)
