## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Poseidon,” PDF p. 204.

Leading because of mental activity. Outstanding for morals, ethics or intellectual activity. Morals of the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Poseidon,” printed p. 323 (PDF p. 160).

Special work culture, clear shaping of work, clear will of the state, independent mental work, state work culture, superior mental activity, rational actions of the state, creating important insights, and important cultural creation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Important or leading intellectual and moral activity, including state culture.
- psychology: Clarity of will and independent mental work.
- body/health: No bodily or medical statement is supplied.
- social/relationship: Leadership through thought or ethics and morals attributed to the state.
- events/manifestations: Important insights, cultural creation, rational state actions, and clear work design.
- conflicts/notes: Outstanding or superior activity is source language; office or intellectual distinction does not by itself prove moral correctness.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears: Witte emphasizes leadership or outstanding activity, while ABC includes independent work and state or cultural settings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Kronos`, Kronos specifies intellectual and ethical activity through leadership, importance, and independence. Witte names leading through mental activity and being outstanding in morals, ethics, or intellect; ABC adds independent thought, clear work design, important insights, and cultural creation. State will and state culture are explicit settings, without making official standing proof of moral authority. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 204; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 323 / PDF p. 160)
