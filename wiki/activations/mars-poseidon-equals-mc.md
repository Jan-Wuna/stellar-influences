## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Poseidon,” PDF p. 204.

The personal and moral behavior. Exposed to moral influences. To be an ethical or moral example. Mental activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Poseidon,” printed p. 322 (PDF p. 160).

Soulful spiritual activity, soulful cultural work, influence of the spirit-soul, my clear will, my mental or spiritual work, my activity guided by ideals, my cultural work, my rational actions, my spiritual activity, and strength of soul and spirit.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal moral conduct and intellectual, spiritual, or cultural activity.
- psychology: Clear will and rational action guided by ideals.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is described.
- social/relationship: Receiving moral influence or serving as an ethical example.
- events/manifestations: Personal mental work, spiritual activity, and cultural creation.
- conflicts/notes: Being an ethical example is a source possibility; moral influence and a clear will do not establish infallibility.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears: Witte emphasizes one's conduct and moral influence, while ABC develops the personal inner, intellectual, and cultural work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = MC`, MC specifies intellectual and ethical activity through one's own conduct and inner purpose. Witte names personal behavior, receiving moral influences, and being an ethical example. ABC connects clear will and rational action with spiritual and cultural work, extending the reading from outward example to the activity one regards as personally meaningful. These are source descriptions of principled effort rather than proof of moral correctness. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 204; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 322 / PDF p. 160)
