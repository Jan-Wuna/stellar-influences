## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Unsuccessful mental or spiritual activity. Doubtful morals, giving rise to objections. Pretended morals. The moral attitude cannot be seen through.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Working with clear water, exchangeable efforts for truth, spiritual work in the unknown, ideal future activity, clear intuition impulses, cultural future work, weak will energy, refinement of mental work development of a clear will, mental transformation activity, and ideal development

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Poseidon = Neptune` clouds mental or spiritual effort so that morality becomes doubtful, pretended, or hard to discern.
- Chart expression: unsuccessful spiritual or intellectual work, dubious ethics, false moral postures, and principles that cannot be clearly seen through.
