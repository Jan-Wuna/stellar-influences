## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: one-based PDF p. 204, Neptune row under Mars/Poseidon.

Unsuccessful mental or spiritual activity. Doubtful morals, giving rise to objections. Pretended morals. The moral attitude cannot be seen through.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

English translation: Work with clear water; disappointing efforts toward truth; mental or spiritual work in the unknown; ideal activity directed toward the future; clear intuition impulses; cultural work directed toward the future; weak will-energy; refinement of mental or spiritual work.

Source: printed p. 323 (one-based PDF p. 160), exact activator row under Mars/Poseidon.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Mental or spiritual effort meets uncertainty, weak will, or disappointing results.
- Psychology: Moral intentions may be difficult to discern; ABC also retains intuition and refinement of mental work.
- Body/health: Neither entry establishes a health condition; work with clear water is an activity, not a treatment.
- Social/relationship: Witte describes doubtful or pretended morality provoking objections.
- Events/manifestations: Unsuccessful intellectual work, work in the unknown, and cultural work directed toward the future.
- Conflicts/notes: The verified eight-clause Neptune row ends with refinement of mental or spiritual work. Development of a clear will begins the following Pluto row and is excluded here; disappointing efforts toward truth does not mean exchangeable efforts.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes failure and suspect morality; ABC also allows intuitive and refining work. These emphases do not establish that every uncertain effort is dishonest.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies mental or spiritual activity through uncertainty about its results or intentions. Effort toward truth may disappoint, while intuition and refinement remain possible; Witte's warning about pretended morality is one expression, not a verdict on every instance. This reading uses the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 204) and the complete [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 323). Its refinement of mental or spiritual work remains distinct from the following Pluto row's development of clear will.
