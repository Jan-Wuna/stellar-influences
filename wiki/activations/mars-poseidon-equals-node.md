## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Connections through ethics, morals and intellectual activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Activation of a cultural connection, mental activity in a connection, creating spiritual connections, wanting clarity in a connection, creating cultural connections, cultural work connection, and will for an ideal connection

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Connections formed through intellectual, ethical, spiritual, or cultural activity.
- Psychology: A wish for clarity and a shared ideal gives the connection direction.
- Body/health: The entries give no specific bodily or medical manifestation.
- Social/relationship: Shared mental or moral activity is the explicit basis of association.
- Events/manifestations: Creating cultural links and working together on spiritual or intellectual concerns.
- Conflicts/notes: Shared ideals do not establish the connection's quality, permanence, or exclusivity.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in connection through mental activity; ABC elaborates cultural work and the wish for clarity without contradicting Witte's brief statement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Node`, Node specifies mental or spiritual activity as a connection with others. Ethical interests, intellectual work, and cultural projects supply the meeting ground, while the wish for clarity or an ideal connection can motivate cooperation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 204) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 322) support this shared activity, but do not establish lasting agreement or a particular relationship form.
