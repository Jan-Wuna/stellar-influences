## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: one-based PDF p. 204, Pluto row under Mars/Poseidon.

Developments, changes or transformations concerning ethics or morals. Planning, beginning or development of an intellectual (mental) activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

English translation: Development of a clear will; mental or spiritual transformation activity; ideal development activity; unfolding cultural work; cultural evolutionary impulses; developing a will toward truth; reasonable development work; growing work culture.

Source: printed p. 323 (one-based PDF p. 160), exact activator row under Mars/Poseidon.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Development or transformation of ethical, intellectual, and cultural activity.
- Psychology: Convictions and the will to seek truth develop rather than remain fixed.
- Body/health: No specific bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Cultural work can grow; no particular relationship outcome is stated.
- Events/manifestations: Planning or beginning mental work, changing moral outlook, and developing a working culture.
- Conflicts/notes: The eight-clause Pluto row begins with development of a clear will, previously attached to Neptune. Developing a will toward truth and reasonable development work do not assert prediction or hidden knowledge.

## Contradictions

The entries share development of mental or cultural work. Neither establishes that a change of convictions is necessarily improvement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies mental, ethical, or cultural effort as a developing process: work is planned or begun, convictions change, and a culture of work takes shape. The connection is development through activity, rather than an assumed crisis or compulsory transformation. This bounded reading follows the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 204) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 323).
