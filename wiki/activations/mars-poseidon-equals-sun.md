## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: one-based PDF p. 204, Sun row under Mars/Poseidon.

Mental work of the day. Daily mental work. Moral behavior of man.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

English translation: A mentally or spiritually working man; mental and bodily activity; clear personal will; ideal actions of a man; personal actions based on insight; personal cultural creation; energetic physical culture; a center for cultural work.

Source: printed p. 322 (one-based PDF p. 160), exact activator row under Mars/Poseidon.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Mental or cultural activity expressed through personal daily action.
- Psychology: ABC emphasizes clear personal will and action informed by understanding.
- Body/health: ABC connects mental and bodily activity and physical culture; it gives no diagnosis or health guarantee.
- Social/relationship: Witte names moral conduct; ABC includes a center for cultural work.
- Events/manifestations: Daily mental work and personally undertaken cultural activity.
- Conflicts/notes: The printed `persönliches Kultur-Schaffen` means personal cultural creation. The source's references to a man remain historical wording; the bodily-culture clause provides no health guarantee.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in personal mental work. ABC's physical and cultural examples broaden the range without proving a separate bodily condition.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Sun`, Sun specifies the Mars/Poseidon activity as personally enacted daily work. Intellectual effort and moral conduct are joined to clear intention, with ABC also connecting mental and physical activity. This supports an emphasis on what a person does with an idea, rather than an assertion of superior character. See the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 204) and the complete [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 322), including personal cultural creation.
