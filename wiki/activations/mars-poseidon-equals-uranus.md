## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Surprising actions which bring forth clarity in regard to moral behavior.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Eventful culture creating, mental activity in technology, clear reform work, creating new insights, unrest through ideal activity, surprising cognition work, meaningful technical work, and surprising truth

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Surprising activity brings clarity, new insight, or reform to intellectual and cultural work.
- Psychology: Insight can arrive with unrest rather than quiet certainty.
- Body/health: Neither entry identifies a specific health manifestation.
- Social/relationship: Witte concerns clarification of moral conduct; ABC includes reform and cultural work.
- Events/manifestations: Unexpected actions, technical work, new insights, and surprising disclosures of truth.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries support surprise and clarification, not a guaranteed correction or an inherently accurate revelation.

## Contradictions

The moral emphasis in Witte and technical or cultural examples in ABC are differences of emphasis, not opposing claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies intellectual or cultural activity through surprise and new insight. An unexpected action may clarify moral behavior, while reform or technical work can provide a practical setting for the same search for clarity. Unrest remains possible rather than being erased by the promise of insight. This follows the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 204) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 323).
