## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: one-based PDF p. 204, Venus row under Mars/Poseidon.

Intimate acquaintance with others of like mental or moral outlook.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

English translation: Joy in mental or spiritual work; mental artistic activity; ideal artistic activity; clear peace work; creating clarity in love; harmonious cultural work; artistic and cultural creation; energetic peace ideas; preference for mental or spiritual work.

Source: printed p. 322 (one-based PDF p. 160), exact activator row under Mars/Poseidon.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Mental or cultural activity becomes a source of pleasure, harmony, or closeness.
- Psychology: Enjoyment of intellectual work and a preference for it are explicit in ABC.
- Body/health: No specific medical or bodily claim is made.
- Social/relationship: Witte describes intimacy with people of similar mental or moral outlook; ABC adds clarity in love and peace work.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic activity, harmonious cultural work, and practical peace ideas.
- Conflicts/notes: The printed `Kunst- und Kultur-Schaffen` means artistic and cultural creation, rather than artificial creation. The entry states no permanence or specific romantic form for Witte's intimate acquaintance.

## Contradictions

Affinity with like-minded people and enjoyment of art or cultural work are compatible examples; neither guarantees a romantic relationship.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Venus`, Venus specifies mental or spiritual activity through enjoyment and affinity. Shared outlook can support intimate acquaintance, while art, cultural work, and efforts toward peace give that affinity something to do. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 204) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 322) connect pleasure with shared activity; they do not establish that every such bond is romantic or permanent.
