## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: one-based PDF p. 204, Vernal Point row under Mars/Poseidon.

The moral outlook, the ethical behavior of the public. The general mental or moral activities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

English translation: Mental or spiritual activity in public; ideal activity in public; clear public will; creating clarity in public; cultural work for the public; actions of reason by the public.

Source: printed p. 322 (one-based PDF p. 160), exact activator row under Mars/Poseidon.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Ethical, mental, or cultural activity takes a public setting.
- Psychology: Witte identifies public moral outlook, without specifying an individual's motives.
- Body/health: No bodily or health claim is established.
- Social/relationship: Public conduct and activity for the public are the explicit social focus.
- Events/manifestations: General mental activity and cultural work in public life.
- Conflicts/notes: The complete six-clause Vernal Point row is readable on the scan. Its public will, clarity and actions of reason do not establish that public conduct is always rational or ethical.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in public mental or cultural activity. ABC adds clear public will and actions of reason without making those universal public qualities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies ethical or intellectual activity through its public setting: the public's moral behavior, general mental activity, and work undertaken for public cultural life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 204) names public moral outlook and activity; the complete [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 322) adds clear public will, creating public clarity, cultural work and actions of reason.
