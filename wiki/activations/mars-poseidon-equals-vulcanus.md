## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: one-based PDF p. 204, Vulcanus row under Mars/Poseidon.

Mighty mental activity. Power of a certain doctrine, ethic or moral code.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

English translation: Work with mental or spiritual energy; influence through mental or spiritual work; working on influential ideas; influential spiritual work; exercising mental or spiritual power; tremendous cultural work; clear actions of reason; powerful actions based on insight; mighty spiritual impulses.

Source: printed p. 323 (one-based PDF p. 160), exact activator row under Mars/Poseidon.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Great mental effort and the influence of doctrine, ethics, or cultural work.
- Psychology: Forceful ideas and powerful spiritual impulses are emphasized, without proving their truth.
- Body/health: The entries identify mental energy, not a specific bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: A doctrine or moral code can exercise influence.
- Events/manifestations: Substantial cultural work and influential intellectual or spiritual activity.
- Conflicts/notes: The printed `geistige Macht ausüben` means exercising mental or spiritual power, rather than power from above. The row provides no supernatural origin or guarantee of truth for that power.

## Contradictions

The entries share mental power and influence. They do not establish that an influential code is necessarily beneficial or coercive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies mental or spiritual activity through strength and influence. A doctrine or ethical code can become a powerful organizing force, while substantial cultural work expresses that force in action. This is power exercised through ideas, without an implied supernatural source or guarantee of moral worth. See the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 204) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 323).
