## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Other people make up your mind or you make up their mind. Leading in an ideological battle, to put through a conviction, a belief or a moral idea. To impress through one’s example or as a model.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Effortful mental activity, fiery mental activity, leading mental activity, spiritual leadership work, clear procreation will, creating clarity through creativity, cultural work performance, cultural leadership work, and creative culture creation

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Directed mental activity and leadership in expressing or advancing convictions.
- Psychology: Minds can be influenced in either direction; ABC adds strenuous and creative intellectual effort.
- Body/health: ABC mentions procreative will, but neither entry establishes fertility or a medical outcome.
- Social/relationship: Leading an ideological struggle and influencing others by example are explicit Witte meanings.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural leadership, creative work, and efforts to put a belief or moral idea into practice.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's ideological struggle does not make all creative or principled leadership coercive; the previous “weaponizes” wording overstated this.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes influence and ideological struggle; ABC emphasizes cultural and creative leadership. These are distinct emphases, not a demonstrated disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Poseidon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies intellectual or spiritual activity as directed leadership: advancing a conviction, influencing minds, or setting an example. Cultural and creative work can carry this purpose, while Witte also allows ideological struggle and being influenced by others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 204) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 323) support purposeful influence without making coercion its universal form.
