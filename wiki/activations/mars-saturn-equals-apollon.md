## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Disturbances and interruption during instruction. Unpleasantness in such connections. Sickness, separation or death for many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of work tasks, success through hard work, success through concentrated action, mature work experiences, increased activity losses, lots of problems at work, creating knowledge through patience, and increasingly serious work

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Difficulty or concentrated effort across expanded tasks, instruction, or groups.
- Psychology: Patient learning and mature work experience accompany the possibility of many work problems.
- Body/health: Witte's sickness or death affecting many is a historical source claim, not a collective medical prediction.
- Social/relationship: Instruction and connections involving many people can be disturbed.
- Events/manifestations: Interrupted instruction, increased responsibilities or losses, and success through concentrated work.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC explicitly includes success through hard work; a synthesis of loss alone omits an important part of this orientation.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes disruption and collective loss; ABC also allows successful expansion through patience. These different outcomes must remain visible.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Saturn = Apollon`, Apollon specifies demanding effort on a wider scale: more tasks, many work problems, or disturbed instruction. Expansion can increase strain, but patience and concentrated action can also produce knowledge and success. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 193) gives severe collective-loss examples; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 301) preserves the constructive range. Neither outcome is inevitable.
