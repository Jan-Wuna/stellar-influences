## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Actions of others which bring sorrow. Cases of illness. Bereavement. Death in the family, or of acquaintances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Saturn,” printed p. 300 (PDF p. 149).

An older work partner, serious activity for the surroundings, serious efforts involving a partner, patient efforts at the location, problematic work in the surroundings, difficult work at the location, others' acts of separation, work in the surroundings in old age, and loss of the workplace.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A person whose advancement in life takes place with great difficulty, obstacles or inhibitions caused by other people. The need to economize, illness, separation, mourning and bereavement.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: “Planets activating the Mars/Saturn midpoint,” Ascendant entry, PDF p. 69.

One comes across to others as a carefully assertive person. One may be touchy and overly self-conscious about one’s identity, as, for instance, how one ap pears to others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `228`

#### Munkasey Entry

Concentrating on bringing discipline or restraint to the various activities or contests you engage in; important military people or athletes who are close to you; your attitudes about disciplining yourself, and promoting practice.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Assertion or advancement constrained by other people, appearances, or a need for discipline.
- Psychology: Careful assertion and self-consciousness in Sandbach coexist with Munkasey's deliberate practice and restraint.
- Body/health: Witte and Ebertin historically include illness and bereavement; they do not establish a causal sequence from inhibition to illness.
- Social/relationship: Others can impede advancement or bring sorrow; close military or athletic figures are a separate Munkasey example.
- Events/manifestations: Economizing, disciplined activity, patient local work, loss of a workplace, separation, and mourning appear as distinct possibilities.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC includes serious or patient partner work alongside difficulty and loss. Sandbach's appearance concerns and Munkasey's practiced restraint must not be reduced to loss alone.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize different expressions of constrained assertion, including patient work, careful presentation, and loss. None establishes a causal progression from inhibition to illness or bereavement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Saturn = Asc`, Asc specifies restrained effort through encounters with others, the local work setting, and the way one presents oneself. Progress may meet interpersonal obstacles, while careful assertion, practice, and restraint offer a more deliberate expression. ABC adds serious or patient partner and local work, older partners, and difficult work or loss of a workplace. ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), printed p. 206, entry0708; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 69; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 228; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 300 / PDF p. 149)

[Witte's](#witte-entry) loss and illness examples (PDF p. 193) remain historical claims rather than consequences of self-consciousness.
