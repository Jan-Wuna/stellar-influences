## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Your vibrations spontaneously help others to see their own pent-up energy, as well as the need to find a positive way of releasing it. You help others to realize where they have repressed themselves, and why. You then instinctively assist them in focus ing their energy in a meaningful way, so that it has the greatest life-supporting effect.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Helping others recognize pent-up energy and direct it toward meaningful release.
- Psychology: Recognition of self-repression and its reasons is central to Sandbach's account.
- Body/health: 'Life-supporting' describes constructive use of energy here; the entry supplies no medical outcome.
- Social/relationship: The helping role is directed toward others rather than an assertion that the person has no blocked energy.
- Events/manifestations: Assisting recognition and focused release of previously restrained energy.
- Conflicts/notes: This is one Sandbach interpretation; 'vibrations' is source language, not evidence of a demonstrated mechanism.

## Contradictions

Only one source is represented, so no inter-source agreement or disagreement can be established.

## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's single-source reading of `Mars/Saturn = Chiron`, Chiron specifies restrained energy as an opportunity to help others recognize self-repression and find a meaningful release. Understanding where energy has been held back leads into focusing it constructively. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) (chapter starts at PDF p. 68; exact entry page unresolved) supports this helping function, without establishing a medical effect or validating a mechanism of 'vibrations'.
