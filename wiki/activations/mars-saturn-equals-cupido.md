## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Quarrel in a community. Interruption or an end of activities. Separation of married life, divorce. Death in the family. Death in the company of others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

serious work community, disturbed trade community, shared work problems, shared activity in old age, painful activity of a community, cooperation involving loss, and separating actions of a community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Strain, painful work, or interruption within a community or shared activity.
- Psychology: The entries concern shared difficulties rather than a specific individual personality.
- Body/health: Witte historically includes death in the family or company of others; no health prognosis follows.
- Social/relationship: Quarrels, cooperation involving loss, marital separation, and divorce are distinct source examples.
- Events/manifestations: Work problems in communities and the interruption or ending of joint activities.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC also includes serious communal work and shared activity in old age; difficulty does not invariably mean dissolution.

## Contradictions

Witte's severe separation examples and ABC's shared work problems overlap in communal strain; continued cooperation remains possible.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Saturn = Cupido`, Cupido specifies difficult or interrupted effort within shared work, community, or married life. Cooperation can become painful or troubled, and some source examples extend to quarrels and separation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 193) also lists bereavement, which remains an attributed historical possibility. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 301) retains serious joint work and activity in old age, so the formula does not establish an unavoidable ending.
