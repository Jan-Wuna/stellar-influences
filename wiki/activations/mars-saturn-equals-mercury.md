## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Thoughts or news about sickness, separation and death. Sick-calls. Violent words bring sorrow.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Discuss work tasks, think actions in old age, patient work language, hard youth work, slow movements at work, problematic speech activity, discuss separation actions, and lossy actions in youth

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Thoughtlessness, hopelessness. Thoughts on separation, illness, death or the next world. News of mourning and bereavement (murderers).

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One communicates in an assertively logical fashion. To speak with a concen trated aggressiveness. The fear of acting creates nervousness.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Communication with athletic or military personnel; angry talk in a hushed tone; education which slows development of your might or ability; loud shouting or noises at formal speeches; expressions which show regret.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Saturn = Mercury`: disciplined logic, tough-minded speech, and the ability to communicate with force and focus under pressure. Main caution: mental constriction: fear, hopelessness, or concentrated aggressiveness can turn thought and speech into a vehicle for strain and sorrow.
