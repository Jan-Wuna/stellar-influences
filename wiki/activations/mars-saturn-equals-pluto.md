## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 197; one-based PDF p. 193, Mars + Saturn, Pluto row.

Interruption of a development. To quit one of several activities. Inception of a later separation or interruption of an activity. Cause for a later sickness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 301; one-based PDF p. 149, Mars + Saturn, Pluto row.

Developing activities in old age, development of a serious activity, patient developmental work, inhibited unfolding of the will, concentrated developmental work, problematic developmental work, painful change of work, and changing work tasks.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed pp. 205–206; one-based PDF pp. 207–208, coded entry 0706 and its complete footnote 38.

Brutality, the rage or fury of destruction. Intervening of a higher power, bodily injury or harm (murder, death of many people).

Footnote 38 to entry 0706, beginning on printed p. 205 and continuing on p. 206: These statements relate also to world events, natural catastrophes, road accidents, etc. In some charts it has been found that a combination such as Pluto = Mars/Saturn has only related to accidents or some kind of suffering if the native was in a position and an environment and conditions which made such serious consequences possible.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 69, Planets activating the Mars/Saturn midpoint, Pluto paragraph.

Pent up energy can create drastic pressure within oneself, with a consequent need for it to find release. To discipline one’s energy can create massive transformation.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `228`

#### Munkasey Entry

Extreme measures taken to apply force or might directly to those who are no longer able to defend themselves adequately; an intensification of applying might or energy toward your goals; corrupts respected activities or efforts.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Intensified pressure, disrupted development, or force applied under restraint.
- Psychology: Sandbach describes pent-up energy needing release and the possibility of transformation through discipline.
- Body/health: Ebertin's historical injury and mass-death examples are qualified by footnote 38's requirement for a position, environment and conditions making serious consequences possible; no medical prediction follows.
- Social/relationship: Munkasey warns of extreme force against people unable to defend themselves.
- Events/manifestations: Quitting an activity, painful work changes, interrupted development, and intensified pursuit of goals.
- Conflicts/notes: Ebertin's body sentence continues with higher power on printed p. 206. Footnote 38 separately continues with Pluto = Mars/Saturn and its circumstances qualification; the following MC footnote 39 is a different note.

## Contradictions

Sandbach's disciplined transformation and Munkasey's coercive force are different expressions. The formula does not establish that internal pressure necessarily erupts violently.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Saturn = Pluto`, Pluto specifies restrained effort through intensified pressure and consequential changes in activity. Development can be interrupted or work changed painfully; discipline may redirect pent-up energy, while extreme force remains a warning in the sources. See [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 193), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 301), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) (exact Pluto paragraph at PDF p. 69), and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (printed p. 228). [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) (printed pp. 205–206, code 0706 and footnote 38) qualifies accidents or suffering by the person's position, environment and conditions; its severe examples do not stand without that qualification.
