## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 197; one-based PDF p. 193, Mars + Saturn, Poseidon row; or is printed.

Interruption or a mental activity. Mental sickness. Mania.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 301; one-based PDF p. 149, Mars + Saturn, Poseidon row.

Burdensome work of understanding, intellectual or spiritual work in old age, actions in pursuit of ideals, serious clear volition, hard intellectual or spiritual work, concentration on intellectual or spiritual work, cultural work tasks, serious cultural activity, disturbed rational actions, and reasonable separating actions.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Demanding or interrupted mental and cultural work.
- Psychology: Concentration and serious intention appear in ABC alongside disturbed reasoning.
- Body/health: Witte's 'mental sickness' and 'mania' remain attributed historical labels, not diagnoses.
- Social/relationship: Cultural tasks are represented; a particular relationship outcome is not.
- Events/manifestations: Hard intellectual work and cultural activity, including work in old age.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte actually prints “Interruption or a mental activity”; the awkward or is retained as printed. ABC gives burdensome understanding, serious clear volition, disturbed rational actions and reasonable separating actions; its geistig wording remains broad.

## Contradictions

Witte's pathology emphasis differs materially from ABC's serious and concentrated work. The entries do not support a progression from difficult study to mental illness.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Saturn = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies demanding effort as mental or cultural work. ABC supports concentration and serious engagement even where understanding is burdensome or reasoning disturbed. The complete [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 301) supports this range and also names reasonable separating actions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 193) retains historical psychiatric labels and the awkward printed opening; neither supplies a diagnosis or an inevitable result of intellectual difficulty.
