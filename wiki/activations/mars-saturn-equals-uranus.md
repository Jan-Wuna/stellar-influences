### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Extraordinary and unusual powers of resistance, ability to give as well as to take under provocation, inclination to apply brute force. A test of nervous strength, intervention by a higher power or providence, sudden illness, sudden accident, separation or case of death.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To be very protective of one’s independence. To be highly sensitive to any criticism received concerning one’s uniqueness. To suddenly become mean or cruel. One may have a genius for organizing how something is to be done, or for finding the most energy-saving way of doing something.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Sudden and unexpected interruptions carried out in a loud and brash way at or in formal settings; indifference to slow and arduous activity; impulsive behavior controlled by friends; astrological efforts or actions which persist.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Saturn = Uranus`: unusual endurance, technical problem-solving, and abrupt intervention when pressure can no longer be held back. Main caution: crisis by rupture: concentrated strain can become violent separation, severe injury, or sudden destructive action.
