## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 197; one-based PDF p. 193, Mars + Saturn, Uranus row.

Sudden actions which separate. Violent separation and death. Sudden death.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 301; one-based PDF p. 149, Mars + Saturn, Uranus row.

Agitating disturbance impulses, serious reform efforts, hard dynamic work, concentration on technical work, new work tasks, sudden separation from work, surprising work problems, and reform work involving loss.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 205; one-based PDF p. 207, coded entry 0704.

Extraordinary and unusual powers of resistance, ability to give as well as to take under provocation, inclination to apply brute force. A test of nervous strength, intervention by a higher power or providence, sudden illness, sudden accident, separation or case of death.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 69, Planets activating the Mars/Saturn midpoint, Uranus paragraph.

To be very protective of one’s independence. To be highly sensitive to any criticism received concerning one’s uniqueness. To suddenly become mean or cruel. One may have a genius for organizing how something is to be done, or for finding the most energy-saving way of doing something.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `228`

#### Munkasey Entry

Sudden and unexpected interruptions carried out in a loud and brash way at or in formal settings; indifference to slow and arduous activity; impulsive behavior controlled by friends; astrological efforts or actions which persist.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Abrupt interruption or unusual resistance within constrained effort.
- Psychology: Sensitivity about independence can coexist with skill in organizing efficient action.
- Body/health: Witte and Ebertin's sudden illness, accident, and death examples remain attributed historical claims.
- Social/relationship: Provocation, brash interruption, and friends controlling impulsive behavior are distinct social examples.
- Events/manifestations: Technical work, new tasks, sudden separation from work, and persistent astrological activity.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC names disturbance impulses, serious reform efforts and reform work involving loss beside technical concentration and new tasks; these examples do not require a violent outcome.

## Contradictions

The sources preserve both efficient organization and abrupt or forceful disruption. Pressure is not shown to culminate inevitably in violent rupture.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Saturn = Uranus`, Uranus specifies restrained effort through abrupt changes, unusual resistance, and the search for efficient ways to act. Sudden interruption and sensitivity to interference can accompany technical concentration or organizing ability. See [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 301), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) (exact Uranus paragraph at PDF p. 69), and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (printed p. 228). [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 193) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) (printed p. 205, code 0704) retain severe historical examples without establishing inevitable injury or death.
