### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Mars/Saturn`
- Source page: `205`
- Entry: `0704`

#### Ebertin Entry

Extraordinary and unusual powers of resistance, ability to give as well as to take under provocation, inclination to apply brute force. A test of nervous strength, intervention by a higher power or providence, sudden illness, sudden accident, separation or case of death.

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Mars/Saturn midpoint`

#### Sandbach Entry

To be very protective of one’s independence. To be highly sensitive to any criticism received concerning one’s uniqueness. To suddenly become mean or cruel. One may have a genius for organizing how something is to be done, or for finding the most energy-saving way of doing something.
