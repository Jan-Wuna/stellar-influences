## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 197; one-based PDF p. 193, Mars + Saturn, Venus row.

Insensibility. Ingratitude for kind action. Actions of separation by female persons. Death of female persons; also of mother, blood-relatives, or close acquaintances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 300; one-based PDF p. 149, Mars + Saturn, Venus row.

Enduring peace work, joy in serious work, patient peace work, disturbed peace at work, disturbed activity in love, harmonious work in old age, loving hard work, activity in love in old age, impulses of pain in love, and loss of joy in work.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 205; one-based PDF p. 207, coded entry 0702.

Coldness of feeling and frigidity, absence of feeling and sensitive perception, loveless disposition. Inability to love and express love. Illness, separation, loss (death) of female persons.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 69, Planets activating the Mars/Saturn midpoint, Venus paragraph.

To fear arguing with others, and with one’s lover in particular. To hold on to resentments toward one’s partner, or toward anyone with whom one is relating. To be attracted to people who are sincere in their actions.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `228`

#### Munkasey Entry

Softens the hard edge of anger and irritation shown during active moments or in haste; added admiration for those who train or practice well; a love of might or muscle developed thru work and application.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Relating and enjoyment shaped by restrained action, resentment, or sustained effort.
- Psychology: Fear of argument and held resentment coexist with attraction to sincerity and disciplined practice.
- Body/health: Ebertin's 'frigidity' and inability-to-love labels are historical; no sexual or medical diagnosis follows.
- Social/relationship: Coldness and ingratitude are source possibilities, while Munkasey explicitly allows softened anger.
- Events/manifestations: Patient peace work, harmonious activity in old age, and admiration for strength developed through work.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's enduring and patient peace work remains beside disturbed peace at work, disturbed activity in love and loss of work enjoyment. Historical female-loss claims remain attributed.

## Contradictions

Munkasey's softening of anger and ABC's enjoyment of serious work materially broaden Ebertin's unqualified coldness language; neither should erase the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Saturn = Venus`, Venus specifies restrained effort through affection, enjoyment, and the handling of anger in relationship. Resentment or fear of conflict can inhibit closeness, while sincerity, patient peace work, and admiration for disciplined practice offer another expression. See [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 300), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) (exact Venus paragraph at PDF p. 69), and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (printed p. 228). [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 193) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) (printed p. 205, code 0702) retain historical coldness and loss claims, not universal judgments about a person's capacity to love.
