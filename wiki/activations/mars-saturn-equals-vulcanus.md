## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 197; one-based PDF p. 193, Mars + Saturn, Vulcanus row.

Conflicts about competence. Death in captivity or prison.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 301; one-based PDF p. 149, Mars + Saturn, Vulcanus row.

Influential activity in old age, disturbed energy impulses, concentrated strength of will, establishing a concentration of power, powerful work problems, pain through violent acts, heavy trade losses, disturbing influence during creative work, and loss of will energy.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Concentrated power meets restricted activity, major work problems, or disputed competence.
- Psychology: ABC includes concentrated strength of will and loss or disturbance of energy.
- Body/health: Pain through violent acts is an ABC example; Witte's prison-death claim remains historical.
- Social/relationship: Conflict over competence and interference in work are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Concentration of power, major trade losses, and influential activity in old age.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's concentrated strength of will stands beside disturbed energy impulses, disturbing influence during creative work and loss of will energy; the row states distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

Power concentration and depleted will are different possibilities, not a universal progression to imprisonment or death.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Saturn = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies constrained effort through concentrated power and substantial work problems. Questions of competence or interference can become central, while the will may be strengthened or depleted. See the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 193) and the complete [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 301). Witte's death-in-captivity example remains an attributed historical claim, not the necessary outcome of conflict.
