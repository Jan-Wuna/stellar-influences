## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 197; one-based PDF p. 193, Mars + Saturn, Zeus row.

Quarrels and grave enmities which in most cases are never forgotten. Death through fire-arms, fire, assassination.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 301; one-based PDF p. 149, Mars + Saturn, Zeus row.

Strenuous patient work, shaping work tasks, work accomplishments in old age, beginning trade losses, serious will to procreate, patient leadership activity, concentration on achievement-oriented work, and creative concentration on work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Directed effort sustained through patience, creative concentration, or lasting conflict.
- Psychology: Concentration on achievement coexists with remembered hostility.
- Body/health: Witte's firearms, fire, and assassination claims remain historical examples; ABC's procreative will proves no bodily outcome.
- Social/relationship: Witte describes enduring enmity; ABC includes patient leadership activity.
- Events/manifestations: Designing tasks, work achievement in old age, and concentrated creative effort.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC explicitly names patient leadership activity and creative concentration. Neither entry makes all directed effort violent.

## Contradictions

The constructive work and leadership emphasis in ABC materially broadens Witte's hostility and death emphasis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Saturn = Zeus`, Zeus specifies difficult effort through purpose and concentrated application. ABC supports patient leadership, creative work, and achievement, while Witte describes the severe alternative of enduring enmity. These emphases should remain visible together: the formula does not make purposeful work an act of hostility. See the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 193) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 301); Witte's death examples remain attributed historical claims.
