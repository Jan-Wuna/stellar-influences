## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: one-based PDF p. 194, Admetos row under Mars/Uranus.

Sudden grave or serious suppression. Sudden great enmity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

English translation: Perseverance in rhythmic work; blocked work rhythm; depressions through restless activity; consolidated dynamic will; tension through restricted activities; spontaneous efforts toward thoroughness; deeper engagement with new work.

Source: printed p. 303 (one-based PDF p. 150), Admetos row under Mars/Uranus.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden action meets suppression, blocked rhythm, or restricted activity.
- Psychology: ABC includes tension and strengthened will; its depression language remains an attributed description.
- Body/health: No specific bodily condition is established.
- Social/relationship: Witte includes sudden serious enmity, without establishing its duration or resolution.
- Events/manifestations: Interrupted work rhythm and sustained engagement with new work.
- Conflicts/notes: The printed `spontane Bemühungen um Gründlichkeit` means spontaneous efforts toward thoroughness, rather than a reaction to thoroughness. Depression remains attributed source language without a clinical definition.

## Contradictions

Suppression and enmity coexist with perseverance and deepening work; neither source proves an irreversible blockage.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Uranus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies sudden or restless activity through restriction of its rhythm. Suppression and tension are possible, but perseverance and deeper engagement with new work remain within the source range. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 194) supplies abrupt suppression or enmity; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 303) adds blocked rhythm and sustained will without establishing an inevitable or permanent impasse.
