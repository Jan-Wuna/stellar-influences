## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden grave or serious suppression. Sudden great enmity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Perseverance in rhythmic work, blocked work rhythm, depression due to restless activity, strengthened dynamic will, tension due to restricted activity, spontaneous reaction to grtindlichkeit, and deepening into new work

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Uranus = Admetos` turns sudden force into grave suppression, hardened hostility, and abrupt blockage.
- Chart expression: sudden serious repression and to enmity that becomes heavy, extreme, and difficult to release or resolve.
