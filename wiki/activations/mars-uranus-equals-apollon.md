## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Surprising actions of many. Sudden astrological occupation. Many explosions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Extension of rhythmic work, work with new knowledge, success through technical work, creating new knowledge, spontaneous expansion of work, surprising work experiences, surprising success in trade, and increased unrest at work

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden activity expands across many people, new knowledge, or technical work.
- Psychology: Surprise and increased restlessness can accompany the expansion.
- Body/health: No specific medical outcome is stated; Witte's explosions are event examples.
- Social/relationship: Witte includes surprising actions by many; ABC includes trade and wider work.
- Events/manifestations: Technical success, creation of new knowledge, spontaneous work expansion, astrology, and multiple explosions.
- Conflicts/notes: The presence of astrology or technology does not mean these activities must be disruptive or explosive.

## Contradictions

ABC explicitly allows success through technical work; Witte's explosive examples are not the sole outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Uranus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies sudden activity through wider participation, expanded work, or new knowledge. Technical work and trade can bring surprising success, while expansion may also increase unrest. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 194) retains astrology and many explosions as distinct examples; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 303) supports a broader range than collective disruption alone.
