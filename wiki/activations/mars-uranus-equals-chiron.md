## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Your vibration encourages others to find they own way of doing things, and to assert themselves more individually and independently. You intuitively induce them to act on inspiration, and through doing so often surprise themselves.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Encouraging others to act independently and find their own methods.
- Psychology: Inspiration and individual assertion are central to Sandbach's account.
- Body/health: No bodily healing or medical effect is stated.
- Social/relationship: The helping influence is directed toward others' independence.
- Events/manifestations: Others may surprise themselves by acting on inspiration.
- Conflicts/notes: This is a single Sandbach interpretation; 'vibration' is attributed source language, not a demonstrated mechanism.

## Contradictions

No second source is represented, so no independent corroboration or contradiction is established.

## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's single-source `Mars/Uranus = Chiron`, Chiron specifies spontaneous action as encouragement of others' individuality. The emphasis is on helping people find their own way to act, trust inspiration, and discover unexpected possibilities in their assertion. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) (chapter starts at PDF p. 71; exact entry page unresolved) supports that interpersonal function without establishing a medical effect or a verified mechanism of 'vibration'.
