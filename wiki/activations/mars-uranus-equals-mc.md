## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: one-based PDF p. 194, MC row under Mars/Uranus.

To get excited while working. Dare-devil. To be very violent. To suffer violence from others. Personal acts of violence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

English translation: I work in technology; I work on events; I am restless at work; I create something new; my work rhythm; my activity is exciting; my will is dynamic; psychological will-impulses; inner or emotional excitement at work.

Source: printed p. 302 (one-based PDF p. 150), MC row under Mars/Uranus.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source: printed p. 209 (one-based PDF p. 211), entry 0721.

An inflexible character, desire for independence, a person "putting the pistol to someone's head," act of violence, extraordinary achievements. The execution of drastic and violent measures, injury, accident, operation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: one-based PDF p. 72, exact Midheaven paragraph under Mars/Uranus.

To rebel against authority figures. To act freely and openly based on the dictates of the higher self. One needs an employer who will allow one to act out of one’s own creative and/or independent urges, and so dislikes an employer who is overly controlling. One derives much security from being able to do one’s own thing.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `232`

#### Munkasey Entry

Becoming more philosophical and mature in your ways; new approaches to effecting social changes or lifestyles; recognition for work you have done thru persisting for modernization; recognition for scientific achievements.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Independent action and reform expressed through personal work or direction.
- Psychology: Excitement and security in autonomy can coexist with inflexibility or rebellion against control.
- Body/health: Witte and Ebertin's violence, injury, and operation examples remain historical possibilities, not a prognosis.
- Social/relationship: Controlling employers can provoke conflict; recognition for modernization is another social outcome.
- Events/manifestations: Technical work, scientific achievements, modernization, and drastic action.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's nine-clause MC row is first-person throughout where possessives or pronouns appear; `meine Tätigkeit ist spannend` means my activity is exciting. Its psychological and inner-excitement terms supply no paranormal mechanism.

## Contradictions

Munkasey's mature recognition and Sandbach's need for autonomy broaden Witte's violence emphasis. Independence does not establish a propensity to violence.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Uranus = MC`, MC specifies spontaneous or forceful activity through personal work, independence, and reform. Freedom to act can support modernization or scientific achievement, while controlling authority may provoke resistance. See [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) (exact Midheaven paragraph, PDF p. 72) and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (printed p. 232). [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 194) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) (printed p. 209, code 0721) retain severe force and injury examples; these do not make autonomy itself violent. [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 302, adds personally experienced technical work, restlessness, new creation and dynamic will.
