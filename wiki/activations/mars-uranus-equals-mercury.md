## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden surprising and alarming news. Sudden violent debates.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work rhythm of youth, exciting news activity, dynamic work meetings, talk about exciting actions, talk about new work, rhythmic movement work, exciting actions in youth, and restless traffic activity

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

The inclination to act independently and to draw heavily on one's nervous energy reserves. A test of strength or power carried through calculatingly, an achievement (also an invention) thought out well and in great detail, injury (operation). An excessive irritation of the nerves.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s communicating can be excitingly refreshing, and imbued with an unusual passion. To surprise others with one’s unique and lively way of saying things. Sudden overstimulation agitates the nervous system.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

You find working with metal and mechanical objects interesting; you devote much energy to research, and are careful to present facts clearly and correctly; urges you to action to have your views debated and not set aside.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Uranus = Mercury`: inventive ideas, lively speech, and the ability to act mentally with boldness and technical precision. Main caution: over-stimulation: nerves can be overtaxed and speech can turn into shocking argument, abrupt debate, or mental agitation.
