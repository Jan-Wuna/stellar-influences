## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: one-based PDF p. 194, Neptune row under Mars/Uranus.

Wasted energy. Energetic action in the wrong direction. Injured by treachery. Fight or accident with death resulting.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

English translation: Work technique with water; disappointing work events; sensitivity to rhythmic impulses; creating metaphysical experiences; technical activity in the air; technical work directed toward the future; weak will-impulses; uncertainty through nervous actions.

Source: printed p. 303 (one-based PDF p. 150), Neptune row under Mars/Uranus.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source: printed p. 208 (one-based PDF p. 210), entry 0717 and footnote 41, explicitly concerning Neptune = Mars/Uranus and both car and aircraft engines.

Cunning and deceit, a low and mean way of acting, desire to harm others, a person with bad intentions. Swooning or fainting when overtaxing one's strength, a fit of rage or frenzy, raving madness, (a car-accident). 41 It frequently has been found that Neptune = Mars/Uranus is present with engine defects. This applies equally to car and aircraft engines.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: one-based PDF p. 71, exact Neptune paragraph under Mars/Uranus.

Sudden actions that are confusion or misconstrued. If one is clearly in tune with one’s intuition, one may engage in sudden, inspired actions that even display ge nius or brilliance.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `232`

#### Munkasey Entry

You may be pushy and impetuous, but you have a dreamy and intuitive side to you which will abandon fights or confrontations when you become bored; you want to push for change but you do not always understand why.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden action affected by unclear direction, misinterpretation, or intuition.
- Psychology: A wish for change may lack a clear reason; inspired action is conditional in Sandbach.
- Body/health: Ebertin's fainting, frenzy, and accident claims remain historical, not diagnostic.
- Social/relationship: Witte includes treachery; Munkasey includes abandoning confrontations when bored.
- Events/manifestations: Wasted energy, action in the wrong direction, and Ebertin's attributed engine-defect observation.
- Conflicts/notes: The eight-clause ABC Neptune row ends with uncertainty through nervous actions; agitating development work starts Pluto's separate row. Sandbach actually prints “actions that are confusion or misconstrued”; that awkward wording is retained. Ebertin's note 41 explicitly concerns this Neptune formula.

## Contradictions

Sandbach allows inspired brilliance when intuition is clear, contrasting with confusion and hostile character judgments elsewhere. Neither inspiration nor bad intent is established in every case.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Uranus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies abrupt action through uncertain direction or interpretation. Energy can be misapplied, yet Sandbach conditionally allows inspired action when intuition is clear. Munkasey adds the wish for change without a fully understood motive. See [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 194), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) (exact Neptune paragraph, PDF p. 71), and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (printed p. 232). [Ebertin's](#ebertin-entry) medical and engine-defect claims (printed p. 208, code 0717) remain attributed observations. [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 303, adds disappointing work events, weak will-impulses and uncertainty through nervous actions, alongside water/air technical work and sensitivity to rhythm.
