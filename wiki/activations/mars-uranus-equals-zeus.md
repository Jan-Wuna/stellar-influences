## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Uranus,” PDF p. 194.

Injury through exploding forces, through lightening, through sudden fire or fire arms. To be occupied with internal combustion engines.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Uranus,” printed p. 303 (PDF p. 150).

Effortful technical work, exciting work start, dynamic procreation will, creative work rhythm, spontaneous work design, technical work performance, and surprising work effort

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Dynamic activity through technical performance, creative effort, and sudden ignition.
- psychology: Excitement at a new undertaking and dynamic procreative will in ABC's terms.
- body/health: Witte historically associates injury with explosive forces, fire, lightning, or firearms.
- social/relationship: No distinct relationship scenario is supplied beyond ABC's procreative wording.
- events/manifestations: Internal combustion engines, demanding technical work, spontaneous work design, and creative rhythm.
- conflicts/notes: Work and creation remain distinct from the dangerous events; occupation with engines does not itself imply injury.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes combustion and injury, while ABC emphasizes technical and creative effort. The difference is one of expression and emphasis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Uranus = Zeus`, Zeus specifies sudden and dynamic activity through technical performance and concentrated creative effort. ABC describes an exciting beginning, demanding technical work, spontaneous work design, creative rhythm, and procreative will. Witte supplies a particular technical setting—internal combustion engines—and also lists injury through explosive forces, lightning, fire, or firearms. The productive and dangerous expressions retain their source scope; the entry does not establish that technical work necessarily ends in an accident. ([ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 303 / PDF p. 150; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 194)
