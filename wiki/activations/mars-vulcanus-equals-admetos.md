## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulcanus,” PDF p. 203.

To stop or finish a mighty activity. Great energies used up. Fatigue. End of fight. Armistice.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulkanus,” printed p. 321 (PDF p. 159).

Blocked working strength, depressive influence on the will, restricted willpower, restriction of violent actions, powerful strike activity, tremendous suppression activity, tremendous absorption in work, and working with primordial-material energies.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful activity stopped, restricted, exhausted, or concentrated in depth.
- psychology: Restricted willpower or deep absorption in work.
- body/health: Witte names fatigue after great expenditure of energy.
- social/relationship: Ending a fight, armistice, strike action, suppression, or restriction of violence.
- events/manifestations: A mighty activity ends; ABC also includes work with primordial-material energies.
- conflicts/notes: Stopping force, suppressing others, and concentrating on work are distinct source possibilities.

## Contradictions

The entries share restriction or concentration of force. Witte emphasizes cessation and fatigue; ABC also includes strikes, suppression, and deep work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Vulcanus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies powerful effort through restriction, cessation, or deep concentration. Witte describes used-up energy, fatigue, the end of a fight, and armistice. ABC includes restricted willpower and violent actions being curtailed, but also powerful strikes, suppression, and intense absorption in work. Its unusual primordial-material energy example remains a separate work setting; the common theme does not make every restriction either peaceful or coercive. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 203; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 321 / PDF p. 159)
