## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulcanus,” PDF p. 203.

Powerful activity of others. Powerful activity with others. Others are head and shoulders above the rest. Possibility of becoming violent to others and of receiving violence from them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulkanus,” printed p. 320 (PDF p. 159).

Working strength at the location, working with influence from the surroundings, an influential workplace, power of the work partner, violent activity at the location, tremendous work in the surroundings, local energies in action, strong partner activities, and others' willpower.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful activity with others or in the immediate work setting.
- psychology: Encountering others' willpower and strong activity.
- body/health: No medical effect is described; Witte names giving or receiving violence as a possibility.
- social/relationship: Powerful partners, joint effort, and possible violent contact.
- events/manifestations: An influential workplace, local energies in action, and tremendous work in one's surroundings.
- conflicts/notes: Power in contact includes productive effort; the entry does not establish that a strong partner is violent.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap on others' power and activity. ABC adds workplace and local-energy settings to Witte's interpersonal emphasis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Vulcanus = Asc`, Asc specifies powerful effort through other people and the immediate surroundings. Witte describes activity with or from strong people; ABC makes the work setting concrete through an influential workplace, local energies, and a partner's activity or willpower. Both entries include violence as a possible difficult expression, alongside substantial joint or local work, without identifying strength itself with violent conduct. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 203; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 320 / PDF p. 159)
