## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulcanus,” PDF p. 203.

To direct the mobilization of labor. Mobilization of labor under supervision of the state. Enforced interference through the state. Use of energies directed and controlled by the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulkanus,” printed p. 321 (PDF p. 159).

Special working energy, influential shaping of work, violent actions by the state, exercise of state power, powerful creation by authority, independent working strength, foremost willpower, and important influence of work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful work directed by authority or expressed through independent working strength.
- psychology: Foremost willpower and special work energy in ABC's terms.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is described.
- social/relationship: State supervision, enforced intervention, and authority's use of labour and energy.
- events/manifestations: Mobilization of labour, influential work design, and state-directed activity.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's independent working strength is retained alongside Witte's state-control emphasis; authority is not always described as benign.

## Contradictions

Witte concentrates on state control, while ABC also names independent working strength. The entries support both without making independence identical to official direction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Vulcanus = Kronos`, Kronos specifies powerful effort through authority, direction of work, and exceptional or independent working strength. Witte's concrete setting is labour mobilized and energy controlled by the state, including enforced intervention. ABC adds influential work design and creation by authority, but also independent working strength. The reading therefore retains personal independence as well as official control, with violent state action among ABC's difficult expressions. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 203; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 321 / PDF p. 159)
