## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulcanus,” PDF p. 203.

Great efforts. To expect a fight. To express courage. To take and give violence. To develop great energies. Ability to perform powerful deeds.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulkanus,” printed p. 320 (PDF p. 159).

My strong will, my working strength, my energetic actions, my influential work, my powerful activity, producing psychological energy, psychological energies in action, inner or soul influence at work, and work involving inner or soul strength.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal effort, courage, and capacity for powerful deeds.
- psychology: Strong will, energetic action, and inner strength applied to work.
- body/health: No diagnosis is supplied; Witte includes taking or giving violence.
- social/relationship: Expecting a fight and exerting influence through one's work.
- events/manifestations: Developing great energies and carrying out substantial personal activity.
- conflicts/notes: Capacity and courage do not establish that conflict or violence must occur; ABC's psychological wording need not imply paranormal action.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap on personal strength and effort. ABC develops inner energy and work, while Witte includes courage, fighting, and violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Vulcanus = MC`, MC specifies powerful effort as one's own will, energy, and capacity to act. Witte names courage and the ability to perform powerful deeds, alongside expecting a fight and giving or taking violence. ABC emphasizes personal energetic actions, influential work, and psychological or inner strength brought into activity. Their overlap is personally mobilized energy, with conflict among the possible expressions rather than the required purpose of that strength. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 203; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 320 / PDF p. 159)
