## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulcanus,” PDF p. 203.

To express ethical and moral ideas with great energy. To fight for moral or ethical ideas. Mental energies. Impressive activity in the fields of education and intellectuality. To be proud of intellectual achievement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulkanus,” printed p. 321 (PDF p. 159).

Work with mental or spiritual energy, influence of mental or spiritual work, working with influential ideas, influential spiritual activity, mental violence, tremendous cultural work, powerful spiritual impulses, a powerful will for truth, and creating the influence of reason.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Great energy applied to ideas, education, culture, and ethical or intellectual activity.
- psychology: Commitment to truth or principles and pride in intellectual achievement.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Fighting for moral ideas and influencing others through education or ideas.
- events/manifestations: Impressive educational activity, cultural work, and energetic expression of principles.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's mental violence remains a difficult expression; force of conviction does not establish an idea's truth or moral correctness.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap on mental energy and influential ideas. ABC includes cultural or spiritual activity and mental violence alongside Witte's educational and ethical emphasis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Vulcanus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies powerful effort through ideas, education, culture, and ethical or spiritual work. Witte describes energetic expression or defense of principles and pride in intellectual achievement; ABC adds influential ideas, cultural work, and a strong will for truth. ABC's mental-violence wording also preserves a difficult expression of intellectual power. Forceful advocacy and sincere conviction do not themselves establish the correctness of a principle. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 203; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 321 / PDF p. 159)
