### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To express ethical and moral ideas with great energy. To fight for moral or ethical ideas. Mental energies. Impressive activity in the fields of education and intellectuality. To be proud of intellectual achievement.

Working with spiritual energy, influence of spiritual work, working with influential ideas, influential spiritual activity, mental violence activity, tremendous cultural work, powerful spiritual impulses, powerful truth will, and creating the influence of reason
