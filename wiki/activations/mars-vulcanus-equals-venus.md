#### Witte Entry

Passionate sexual love. Great readiness for intimate acquaintances. Close ties with artists. Intense harmony in regard to activity with intimate acquaintances or relatives.

#### ABC Entry

Influence of joy at work, immense artistic power, powerful peace work, love influence at work, powerful peace activity, strong harmony impulses, strong through harmonious work, and preference for violence activity
