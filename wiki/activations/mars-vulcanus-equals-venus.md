## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulcanus,” PDF p. 203.

Passionate sexual love. Great readiness for intimate acquaintances. Close ties with artists. Intense harmony in regard to activity with intimate acquaintances or relatives.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulkanus,” printed p. 320 (PDF p. 159).

Influence of joy at work, immense artistic power, powerful peace work, love influence at work, powerful peace activity, strong harmony impulses, strong through harmonious work, and preference for violence activity

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful affection, artistic activity, and harmonious or peace-oriented work.
- psychology: Passion, joy in work, and strong impulses toward harmony.
- body/health: Sexual passion is named, without a medical effect.
- social/relationship: Intimate acquaintances, artists, relatives, and intense harmony in shared activity.
- events/manifestations: Artistic power, peace work, and strength through harmonious work.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also names a preference for violent activity; it remains distinct from its peace and harmony expressions.

## Contradictions

The dominant themes are passion and harmony, but ABC's preference-for-violence clause preserves a difficult possibility that the favorable phrases do not erase.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Vulcanus = Venus`, Venus specifies powerful effort through passionate intimacy, artistic activity, and harmony with others. Witte connects close ties with artists or relatives to intense harmony in shared activity; ABC adds joy in work, peace work, and strength through harmonious effort. ABC also retains a preference for violent activity as a separate difficult expression, so the page preserves the tension rather than presenting all powerful affection as harmonious. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 203; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 320 / PDF p. 159)
