## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulcanus,” PDF p. 203.

General energies, efforts or violent acts. Efforts in the field of climate, because of extremes heat or cold.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Vulkanus,” printed p. 320 (PDF p. 159).

work force for the public, influence of public will, influential activity in public, violent activity in public, actions of public power, power of action of the public, and strong public work

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful effort and public activity on a general or collective scale.
- psychology: Influence of public will in ABC's entry.
- body/health: No medical effect is supplied; Witte names work prompted by extreme heat or cold.
- social/relationship: Public power, influential public activity, and violent acts among the source possibilities.
- events/manifestations: Strong public work and efforts in the field of climate.
- conflicts/notes: Climate-related effort and public violence are distinct examples; neither is required for all public exertion.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap on general or public effort. Witte adds the particular circumstance of climate extremes, while ABC develops public work and power.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Vulcanus = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies powerful effort through general energies and public life. ABC describes influential public work, public will, and the exercise of public power. Witte also supplies a concrete uncommon setting: efforts prompted by extremes of heat or cold. Violent acts remain one expression in both entries, alongside work and exertion that need not be violent. ([ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 320 / PDF p. 159; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 203)
