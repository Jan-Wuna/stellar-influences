## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” PDF p. 199.

Work is limited and suppressed. To work or to start working under difficult circumstances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” printed p. 313 (PDF p. 155).

Persistent work with fire, beginning of a blockage of will, depressing leadership activity, restriction of creative work, ending strenuous work, thorough leadership work, resistance to leadership work, and the goal of blockade activity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed or creative work restricted, suppressed, or pursued under difficult conditions.
- psychology: Blocked will and depressing leadership activity in ABC's terms.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is described.
- social/relationship: Resistance to leadership and blockade activity.
- events/manifestations: Difficult work beginnings, ending strenuous work, persistent fire work, and thorough leadership work.
- conflicts/notes: Thorough or persistent work remains alongside obstruction; the entry does not describe only failure.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes restriction and difficult circumstances. ABC adds persistence and thoroughness as well as blockade and resistance.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Zeus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies directed and creative effort through limitation, suppression, or difficult working conditions. Witte names both working and beginning work under those conditions. ABC adds blocked will, resistance to leadership, and ending strenuous work, but also persistent work with fire and thorough leadership work. The reading retains sustained effort within difficulty rather than treating obstruction as an inevitable end to activity. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 199; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 313 / PDF p. 155)
