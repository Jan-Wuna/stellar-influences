## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” PDF p. 199.

Creative, fiery soul. Minute of rocket ignition.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” printed p. 312 (PDF p. 155).

I want to begin, my will to lead, my will to achieve, my procreative will, my work goals, my work with fire, my leadership activity, my strenuous activity, inner or soul leadership work, and creative work of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal initiative and inner creative drive expressed through goals, leadership, and beginning work.
- psychology: Will to begin, lead, achieve, or procreate in ABC's terms.
- body/health: No medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Personal leadership activity, without a specific relationship scenario.
- events/manifestations: Strenuous work, work with fire, and the precise rocket-ignition moment in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: A rocket-ignition example does not establish a vocation or a general timing method.

## Contradictions

Witte's creative, fiery inner description overlaps with ABC's personal will and creative soul work; the rocket-ignition example remains a particular source expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Zeus = MC`, MC specifies directed and creative effort as one's own initiative, goals, and will to act. ABC names beginning, leadership, achievement, procreative will, and strenuous or fire-related work, along with creative work of the soul. Witte's “Creative, fiery soul” overlaps with that inner emphasis, while the minute of rocket ignition remains a precise particular example rather than proof of a calling or a general timing rule. ([ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 312 / PDF p. 155; [Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 199)
