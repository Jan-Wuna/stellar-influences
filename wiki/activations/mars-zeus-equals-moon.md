## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” PDF p. 199.

Strong emotion. Excited populace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” printed p. 312 (PDF p. 155).

A strenuous working hour, a woman's work goals, a people's work achievements, a leading woman in action, a woman's will to lead, creative impulses of feeling, a people's will to achieve, creative emotional actions, and activity in a people's leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed or creative effort expressed through feeling, leadership, and collective activity.
- psychology: Strong emotion and creative impulses of feeling.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: An excited populace, collective leadership, and historically gendered examples of women's work and leadership.
- events/manifestations: A strenuous working hour, work achievements, and creative emotional action.
- conflicts/notes: Collective excitement need not imply unrest; women's roles remain source-specific historical examples.

## Contradictions

Witte's short emotion/populace entry is broader in tone, while ABC specifies creative action, work, and leadership. Neither requires every emotional expression to be disruptive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Zeus = Moon`, Moon specifies directed and creative effort through feelings and collective activity. Witte names strong emotion and an excited populace; ABC adds creative emotional action, a people's achievements and will to achieve, and historically gendered examples of a woman's goals or leadership. Work and creative feeling therefore remain as central as collective excitement, without adding an inevitable state of unrest or reactivity. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 199; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 312 / PDF p. 155)
