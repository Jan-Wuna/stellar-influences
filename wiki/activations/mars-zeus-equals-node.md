## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” PDF p. 199.

To unite oneself with producers. Rifle club. A goal-oriented cooperation or partnership in work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” printed p. 312 (PDF p. 155).

Mediating work achievements, leadership activity within a connection, wanting creative connections, will to achieve within a connection, creative activity within a connection, procreative will within a connection, and goal-directed contact work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Goal-directed cooperation and connections organized around productive or creative work.
- psychology: Shared will to achieve or form creative connections.
- body/health: Procreative will is named, without a medical effect.
- social/relationship: Partnership in work, association with producers, and leadership within a connection.
- events/manifestations: Mediation of work achievements, purposeful contacts, and a rifle club as a specific Witte example.
- conflicts/notes: A rifle club is one association named; the entry does not make all cooperation tactical or armed.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap on purposeful work connections. ABC develops leadership, creativity, achievement, and procreative will within them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Zeus = Node`, Node specifies directed and creative effort through goal-oriented work connections. Witte names partnership with producers and a rifle club; ABC develops mediation of achievement, leadership within a connection, creative activity, and shared will to achieve. The common thread is purposeful association, with the armed-club example retaining its particular scope rather than defining every cooperative aim. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 199; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 312 / PDF p. 155)
