## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” PDF p. 199.

Planning and bringing about of energetic work. To do several jobs at the same time. To bring about changes by energetic work. Development in technique, especially with regard to firearms.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” printed p. 313 (PDF p. 155).

Strenuous developmental work, beginning developmental work, creative development of work, unfolding procreative will, creative change in work, changed work goals, and growing leadership activity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed and energetic work bringing development, changed goals, and technical change.
- psychology: Planning and unfolding procreative will in the source terms.
- body/health: No medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Growing leadership activity in ABC's entry.
- events/manifestations: Several jobs at once, developmental work, creative work change, and technical development involving firearms.
- conflicts/notes: Firearms are a particular technical example; development does not imply a single prescribed outcome or method.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe development through work. Witte emphasizes planning, simultaneous jobs, and technique; ABC adds changed goals and growing leadership.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Zeus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies directed and creative effort through planning, development, and changes in work. Witte describes doing several jobs at once and bringing about technical change, especially involving firearms. ABC adds beginning developmental work, changed goals, creative work transformation, and growing leadership activity, with unfolding procreative will as a distinct expression. The overlap is work that develops or changes conditions, without adding a universal claim of sustained pressure or innovation. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 199; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 313 / PDF p. 155)
