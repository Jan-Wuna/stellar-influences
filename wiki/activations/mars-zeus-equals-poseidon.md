## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” PDF p. 199.

To submit one's mental faculties to proof, to demonstrate them. To be under pressure. To be influenced morally. Acts, deeds under moral or similar circumstances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” printed p. 313 (PDF p. 155).

Strenuous work toward understanding, mental or spiritual leadership work, ideal work goals, clear directional impulses, creative cultural activity, spiritual direction of work, efforts of will toward truth, and goal-directed rational actions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed activity through demonstrated mental ability, ideals, truth-seeking, and cultural work.
- psychology: Mental faculties tested under pressure and effort toward understanding or truth.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Moral influence and mental or spiritual leadership.
- events/manifestations: Creative cultural work, clear direction, and deeds performed under moral circumstances.
- conflicts/notes: Demonstrating ability and seeking truth do not establish moral or intellectual infallibility.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes proof, pressure, and moral influence; ABC also includes constructive cultural activity and rationally directed work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Zeus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies directed and creative effort through mental ability, ideals, and the search for truth. Witte describes demonstrating one's faculties under pressure or moral influence; ABC adds clear direction, mental or spiritual leadership, rational action, and creative cultural work. The reading therefore includes constructive principled effort as well as testing circumstances, without treating conviction or demonstrated ability as infallibility. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 199; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 313 / PDF p. 155)
