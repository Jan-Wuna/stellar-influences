## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” PDF p. 199.

Unsuccessful action. Loss to machinery. Obstacles to military activities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” printed p. 313 (PDF p. 155).

Strenuous acts of separation, losing work through fire, beginning serious creative work, leadership work in old age, concentration on creative actions, creative work tasks, loss of performance at work, and a difficult beginning of work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed work meeting loss, obstacles, difficult beginnings, or serious creative tasks.
- psychology: Concentration on creative action in ABC's entry.
- body/health: Old age appears as a leadership-work setting, without a medical claim.
- social/relationship: Strenuous separation and leadership work in later life.
- events/manifestations: Unsuccessful action, machinery losses, work lost through fire, and obstacles to military activity.
- conflicts/notes: Serious creation and concentration remain alongside failure and loss; difficulty does not make all work futile.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes unsuccessful action and obstacles. ABC also includes serious creation, creative tasks, and concentrated leadership in old age.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Zeus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies directed and creative effort through obstacles, losses, and difficult work beginnings. Witte gives unsuccessful action and machinery or military losses; ABC adds work lost through fire and strenuous separation. ABC also retains beginning serious creative work, concentrated creative tasks, and leadership in old age, so its range includes demanding sustained work as well as failure. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 199; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 313 / PDF p. 155)
