## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” PDF p. 199.

Sudden energetic action, which cannot be stopped. Fast assault. Quick-firing weapons. Ignition. Inventive creative work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” printed p. 313 (PDF p. 155).

Effortful leadership activity, exciting fire activity, creative work rhythm, new work goals, start reform activity, technical performance will, surprising performance by action force, and determined technical trade

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden energetic or inventive work, new goals, and reform activity.
- psychology: Technical will to achieve and creative work rhythm.
- body/health: No medical effect is supplied; Witte names assault, ignition, and quick-firing weapons.
- social/relationship: Demanding leadership and reform among ABC's work settings.
- events/manifestations: Sudden unstoppable action, inventive creation, surprising achievement, and purposeful technical trade.
- conflicts/notes: Inventive work and violent or rapid action are distinct possibilities; the formula does not establish a single inevitable eruption.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes abrupt action and ignition, while ABC adds new work goals, creative rhythm, and reform. Both retain productive as well as forceful expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Zeus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies directed and creative effort through sudden action, invention, and new working goals. Witte includes unstoppable energetic action, ignition, and quick-firing weapons, but also inventive creative work. ABC develops the productive range through reform, creative rhythm, technical will to achieve, and surprising accomplishments. These settings retain rapid or forceful action without reducing every expression to assault or eruption. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 199; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 313 / PDF p. 155)
