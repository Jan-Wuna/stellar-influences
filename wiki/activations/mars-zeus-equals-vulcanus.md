## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” PDF p. 199.

Thirst for action cannot be stifled. Great creative power. Explosive manner of working. Mighty accomplishment. Natural forces actively at work

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Mars + Zeus,” printed p. 313 (PDF p. 155).

Strenuous strength-demanding work, beginning violent activity, influential leadership activity, fiery energies in action, tremendous work achievement, creative working energy, a powerful will to lead, creative energy of will, and activity of the leading power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed or creative effort expressed with great working power and accomplishment.
- psychology: An unstifled urge to act and powerful will to lead.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Influential leadership and activity of a leading power.
- events/manifestations: Explosive work, tremendous achievement, natural forces in action, and beginning violent activity.
- conflicts/notes: Creative energy and violence are separate source expressions; great accomplishment need not be violent.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes creative power and natural forces; ABC adds leadership, energy of will, and the possible beginning of violent activity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mars/Zeus = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies directed and creative effort through great working power, leadership, and accomplishment. Witte describes a thirst for action that cannot be stifled, explosive work, and natural forces actively at work; ABC adds influential leadership, creative working energy, and a powerful will to lead. Beginning violent activity remains a particular difficult expression alongside creative achievement rather than its required means. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 199; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 313 / PDF p. 155)
