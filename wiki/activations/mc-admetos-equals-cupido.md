## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Admetos,” PDF p. 75.

Wanting to be alone. To reject to be with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Admetos,” printed p. 95 (PDF p. 46).

A firm inner or soul community, I stabilize the community, my suppression within the community, my stabilized circulation or cycle, my restrictions within the community, my artistic perseverance, and inner or soul suppression within a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal firmness or restriction expressed through community, artistic perseverance, or withdrawal.
- psychology: Wanting solitude and maintaining endurance in art.
- body/health: ABC names a stabilized circulation or cycle, without supplying a medical conclusion.
- social/relationship: Stabilizing a community, firm inner bonds, restrictions, or suppression within a group.
- events/manifestations: Rejecting company, community stabilization, and persistent artistic work.
- conflicts/notes: Withdrawal, stabilizing a group, and being suppressed by it are distinct possibilities; suppression is not support.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes refusing company. ABC also includes a firm community and stabilizing it, alongside restriction or suppression within that community.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Admetos = Cupido`, Cupido specifies personal firmness and restriction through community or the choice to withdraw from it. Witte describes wanting to be alone and rejecting company; ABC also names a firm inner community, stabilizing the group, and artistic perseverance. Its suppression and restriction clauses preserve the difficult side of group membership. The entries therefore allow both steadfast participation and withdrawal, without treating suppression as supportive belonging. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 75; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 95 / PDF p. 46)
