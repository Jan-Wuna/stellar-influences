## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Admetos,” PDF p. 75.

To be and to remain poor. Indifferent to poverty or misfortune.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Admetos,” printed p. 95 (PDF p. 46).

Depression through inner or soul criminality, lack of inner depth, my thorough renunciation, my lack of perseverance, my deficient circulation or cycle, inner firmness through renunciation, an inner blockage from the past, and suppression of my past.

The source's “seelische Kriminalität” is retained literally as “inner or soul criminality”; the entry does not explain that expression further.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal restriction or deficiency in poverty, endurance, inner depth, or relation to the past.
- psychology: Indifference to misfortune, inner blockage, and firmness through renunciation.
- body/health: ABC names depression and deficient circulation or cycle as historical associations, without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Poverty and renunciation, without a particular relationship scenario.
- events/manifestations: Lack of perseverance, blocked inner life from the past, and suppression of one's past.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's criminality wording remains unexplained and is excluded from confident interpretation; firm renunciation differs from lack of endurance.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes remaining poor and indifference to misfortune. ABC includes lack of endurance but also firmness through renunciation, so unbroken stoic persistence is not the sole reading.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Admetos = Hades`, Hades specifies personal firmness or restriction through scarcity, deficiency, and the burden of the past. Witte describes poverty and indifference to misfortune; ABC adds lack of endurance or inner depth and an inner blockage from the past, but also firmness through renunciation. Those differences prevent a single portrait of numb, unwavering endurance. ABC's depression and circulation wording remains attributed historical material, and its unexplained criminality phrase supports no further inference. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 75; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 95 / PDF p. 46)
