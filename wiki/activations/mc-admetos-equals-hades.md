## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Admetos = Hades` lowers the axis into poverty, misfortune, and numb endurance of difficult conditions.
- Chart expression: a life hardened by scarcity, indifference to degraded circumstances, and persistence through misery without much visible emotional response.
