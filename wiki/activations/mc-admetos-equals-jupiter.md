## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Admetos,” PDF p. 75.

Insensible or indifferent towards good luck. To be fortunate in mineral resources or soil conservation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Admetos,” printed p. 94 (PDF p. 46).

My financial circulation, my money is tied up, my good circulation or cycle, my success through perseverance, my financial restriction, my joy is restricted, my legal restriction, inner blockage because of money, and deep inner joy.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal firmness or restriction expressed through fortune, money, perseverance, and joy.
- psychology: Indifference to good luck, restricted joy, or deep inner joy are distinct source expressions.
- body/health: ABC names good circulation or a cycle, without a medical conclusion.
- social/relationship: Legal or financial restrictions, without a specific relationship scenario.
- events/manifestations: Tied-up money, success through perseverance, and fortune in mineral resources or soil conservation.
- conflicts/notes: Restraint and deep joy coexist; the source does not require emotional numbness toward every favorable outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte includes indifference to luck, while ABC also names deep inner joy. Both retain favorable possibilities alongside restriction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Admetos = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies personal firmness and restriction through money, fortune, and joy. Witte includes indifference to good luck and a particular favorable setting in mineral resources or soil conservation. ABC adds tied-up money, financial or legal restriction, and success through perseverance, while retaining deep inner joy alongside restricted joy. The reading therefore preserves both restraint and satisfaction, rather than treating every favorable experience as emotionally muted. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 75; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 94 / PDF p. 46)
