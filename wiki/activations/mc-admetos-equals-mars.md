## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Admetos,” PDF p. 75.

Persistent activity or inactivity. A paralysed procedure or activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Admetos,” printed p. 94 (PDF p. 46).

Thorough inner or soul work, I block work, I stabilize activity, my cycle of activity, deepening my work, my perseverance at work, my depressing work, my restricted activity, and suppression of loving activity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal firmness expressed through persistent, stabilized, blocked, or restricted activity.
- psychology: Thorough inner work and perseverance, with depressing work among the examples.
- body/health: Witte's paralysed activity describes a procedure; no medical diagnosis is established.
- social/relationship: Suppression of loving activity in ABC's entry.
- events/manifestations: Deepening work, stabilizing activity, or a procedure coming to a standstill.
- conflicts/notes: Persistent activity and persistent inactivity are explicitly distinct; the source does not say effort always continues through blockage.

## Contradictions

Both entries include persistence and blockage. ABC adds stabilizing and deepening work, retaining more than inactivity alone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Admetos = Mars`, Mars specifies personal firmness and restriction through the course of activity itself. Witte explicitly allows persistent activity or inactivity and a paralysed procedure; ABC adds stabilizing activity, deepening work, and perseverance, alongside blocked or restricted work. Their range includes sustained effort and genuine standstill, with suppression of loving activity as a particular relational expression. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 75; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 94 / PDF p. 46)
