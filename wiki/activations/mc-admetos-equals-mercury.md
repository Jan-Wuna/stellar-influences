## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Admetos,” PDF p. 75.

Slow thinking. Immobile personal attitude in conversations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Admetos,” printed p. 94 (PDF p. 46).

Deep, soulful conversations, conversations about inner restriction, I block traffic, my blocked thinking, stabilizing my thinking, my perseverance in youth, my depressed thoughts, my thorough thoughts, and suppression in my youth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal firmness or restriction expressed in thought, conversation, and communication.
- psychology: Slow, blocked, stabilized, thorough, or depressed thinking among the source expressions.
- body/health: No medical diagnosis is supplied; depressed thoughts remain source wording.
- social/relationship: Deep conversation, fixed conversational attitudes, and suppression in youth.
- events/manifestations: Discussing inner restriction, perseverance in youth, and blocking traffic.
- conflicts/notes: Thorough or deep thinking differs from immobility; the literal traffic example does not require a cognitive interpretation.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes slow thinking and immobility in conversation. ABC also includes deep conversation, thorough thoughts, and stabilizing thought alongside blockage.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Admetos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies personal firmness and restriction through thinking and conversation. Witte describes slowness and an immobile conversational attitude; ABC adds blocked or stabilized thought, but also thorough thinking and deep, soulful conversations. Youthful perseverance or suppression and the literal blocking of traffic are further concrete examples. The range includes depth and steadiness as well as mental or communicative obstruction. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 75; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 94 / PDF p. 46)
