## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Admetos,” PDF p. 75.

To disincline tender feminine feelings. To pull oneself together in order to remain steadfast.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Admetos,” printed p. 94 (PDF p. 46).

I as a woman am consolidated or firm, my depressed people, my consolidated feelings, my restriction of feeling, my blocked woman or wife, my restriction through the woman, my firmness through the woman, my inner depth, and the woman's inner firmness.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal firmness and restriction expressed through feeling, inner depth, and women's roles in the historical entries.
- psychology: Consolidated or restricted feelings and pulling oneself together to remain steadfast.
- body/health: No bodily mechanism is supplied; ABC's depressed people remains attributed source language.
- social/relationship: A woman as a source of firmness or restriction, and a blocked woman or wife.
- events/manifestations: Emotional consolidation, inner depth, and an affected collective mood.
- conflicts/notes: Historical feminine-tenderness language is not a universal gender claim; firmness need not mean the absence of depth or feeling.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes rejecting tenderness to remain steadfast; ABC includes both emotional restriction and consolidated feelings or inner depth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Admetos = Moon`, Moon specifies personal firmness and restriction through feeling and inner depth. Witte describes pulling oneself together to remain steadfast using historical language about feminine tenderness. ABC adds consolidated feelings, inner depth, and a woman's firmness or restrictive influence, alongside blocked feeling and a depressed collective mood. Both emotional strength and restriction remain visible; steadiness is not reduced to emotional hardness alone. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 75; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 94 / PDF p. 46)
