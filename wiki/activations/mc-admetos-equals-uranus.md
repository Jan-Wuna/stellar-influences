## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Being suddenly startled from a rest. To feel shock. To be suddenly upset.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Admetos`; printed p. `95`; one-based PDF page `46` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Consolidation of inner tensions, my stabilized rhythm, my restless circulation, my thorough renewal, my sudden restriction, my surprising depressions, sudden inner blockage, surprising inner deepening.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Abrupt disturbance of rest and a sudden constraint.
- **Psychology:** Shock, upset, and persistent inner tension are represented.
- **Body/health:** ABC's circulation and depression wording is historical source association; it establishes no diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** The entries supply no particular relationship outcome.
- **Events/manifestations:** Being startled from rest or experiencing sudden blockage are explicit.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's renewal and surprising inner deepening differ from its separate depression clause; circulation and depression expressions remain historical associations without a diagnosis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies `MC/Admetos` as rest or fixity interrupted by a sudden shock. Being startled, upset, or abruptly constrained gives this orientation its distinctive movement; ABC adds tensions and blockage, so the interruption need not resolve the underlying difficulty. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 75, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 95.)
