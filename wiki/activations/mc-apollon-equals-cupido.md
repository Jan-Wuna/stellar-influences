## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

An experienced art expert. Many connections in the field of art. A friend of peace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `MC + Apollon`; printed p. `93`; one-based PDF page `45` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Successful community of souls, shared inner experiences, shared knowledge of the soul, my success in the community, my experiences in the community, inner experiences in the community, growing inner community.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Experience and knowledge shared in artistic or communal settings.
- **Psychology:** A disposition toward peace and shared inner experience is represented.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Artistic networks and community participation are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Art expertise and success within a community are named possibilities.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's community of souls and shared inner knowledge are spiritual formulations. They do not add a separate promise of pleasure, and Witte's art expertise remains a distinct cultural example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies `MC/Apollon` through artistic connections and shared experience. Art expertise, many contacts in the arts, and a wish for peace join ABC's success and experience in community; shared knowledge of the soul and a growing inner community give that participation a spiritual as well as social form. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 74, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 93.)
