## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Making experiences with evil or old things. Historical scientist. To have knowledge of antiquity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `MC + Apollon`; printed p. `93`; one-based PDF page `45` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

My successful renunciation, my secret of success, my hidden knowledge, my successful past, my dangerous experiences, adverse inner experiences, increased sorrow of the soul, increasing inner criminality.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Experience of old, hidden, or adverse material.
- **Psychology:** Dangerous experiences, renunciation and increased sorrow of the soul are represented.
- **Body/health:** No specific medical condition is established.
- **Social/relationship:** ABC's criminality language is an attributed historical claim, not a judgment about a person.
- **Events/manifestations:** Historical research, knowledge of antiquity, and a successful past are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC retains increased sorrow and inner criminality as severe source examples; they do not equate historical research or knowledge of antiquity with criminal conduct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies `MC/Apollon` through knowledge of difficult, hidden, or old material. Historical inquiry and antiquity stand beside dangerous or adverse experiences and concealed knowledge; the common thread is learning from what is past or troubling, without equating historical scholarship with wrongdoing. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 74, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 93.)
