## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Happy experiences. Luck and success in commerce and science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `MC + Apollon`; printed p. `92`; one-based PDF page `45` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Joy in my success, joy and success in the minute, my financial success, my financial knowledge, my legal knowledge, my financial freedom, my legal experiences, experiencing joy of the soul, increase of my money.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Happy experience and success in commerce or science.
- **Psychology:** Joy in achievement is explicit.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health interpretation is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Legal experience is named, but no particular relationship outcome is established.
- **Events/manifestations:** Financial success, increase of money, financial freedom, and scientific or commercial achievement are represented.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC includes increase of money as a favorable possibility, not veneration of money. Its minute wording remains source-native language and supplies no independent forecasting rule.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `MC/Apollon` through satisfying achievement in commerce and science. Joy in success connects with ABC's financial and legal knowledge and financial freedom; the entries describe favorable possibilities for experience and accomplishment, rather than a guarantee of prosperity. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 74, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 92.)
