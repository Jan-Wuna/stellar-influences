## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

One who has an intellectually high standing. To be authority for many. To pursue the political sciences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special knowledge of the soul, great spiritual experiences, my special success, my great knowledge, my experiences with the state, my achievements for the state, my great experiences, my important successes, and my economic independence

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Distinguished knowledge and important achievement.
- **Psychology:** Extensive knowledge and significant experience are represented.
- **Body/health:** The entries establish no specific bodily meaning.
- **Social/relationship:** Authority for many and dealings with the state are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Political science, work for the state, and economic independence are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries emphasize standing and achievement without documenting a conflict; their repetition is not independent proof of authority.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies `MC/Apollon` through knowledge that gives a person standing and authority. Intellectual distinction and political science meet ABC's experience of the state, achievements for it, and economic independence; the entry concerns recognized scope of knowledge and accomplishment, without guaranteeing rank. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 74, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 93.)
