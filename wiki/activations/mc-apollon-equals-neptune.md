## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Peculiar experiences. To penetrate unknown fields of science. To work in a new field of science. Engaged in sciences of the future.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `MC + Apollon`; printed p. `93`; one-based PDF page `45` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Expansion of an inner weakness, my success in water, my metaphysical knowledge, my experiences in the air, my freedom in water, my increasing uncertainty, increase of my uncertainty, refinement of knowledge of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Unusual experience and inquiry into unfamiliar knowledge.
- **Psychology:** Increasing uncertainty accompanies the exploratory theme, alongside refinement of knowledge.
- **Body/health:** ABC's inner weakness is a historical description and does not establish a medical condition.
- **Social/relationship:** No specific relationship outcome is supplied.
- **Events/manifestations:** New scientific fields, metaphysical knowledge, and experience involving water or air are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC retains both increasing uncertainty and increase of uncertainty as separate clauses, beside metaphysical knowledge and refinement of knowledge of the soul. Its inner weakness is historical descriptive language rather than a diagnosis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `MC/Apollon` through unusual experience and exploration beyond established scientific fields. Witte's inquiry into unfamiliar or future sciences stands beside ABC's metaphysical knowledge and insecurity; openness to the unknown therefore includes uncertainty, not assured insight. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 74, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 93.)
