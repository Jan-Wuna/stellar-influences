## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To congregate with many people. Meeting scientific people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `MC + Apollon`; printed p. `92`; one-based PDF page `45` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Successful inner connections, my knowledge about connections, my successful connections, my experiences of contact, my connection to knowledge, connection to the science of the soul, increased inner connections.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Connections built through gatherings and knowledge.
- **Psychology:** Experience of contact and interest in knowledge are represented.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health meaning is established.
- **Social/relationship:** Many people and scientific associates are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Congregating and forming successful connections are named possibilities.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's final clause describes increased connections, rather than affection. Its science-of-the-soul language is a source-specific formulation; the entry does not determine the duration of an association.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies `MC/Apollon` through gathering and connections around knowledge. Meeting many people and scientific people joins ABC's successful and increasing contacts and connection to knowledge; the orientation concerns association and shared inquiry, without determining how lasting those contacts will be. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 74, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 92.)
