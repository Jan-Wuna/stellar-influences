## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The correct evaluation of own experiences. Developing scientific abilities. To experience an extensive change, or many alterations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `MC + Apollon`; printed p. `93`; one-based PDF page `45` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Development of my knowledge, successful evolution of the soul, my successful development, my extensive changes, my growing freedom, unfolding breadth of soul, science of the growth of the soul, changing experiences of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Development of knowledge and ability through changing experience.
- **Psychology:** Evaluation of one's own experience is explicit.
- **Body/health:** No distinct bodily or medical association is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** No defined relationship outcome occurs in the entries.
- **Events/manifestations:** Scientific ability, extensive change, growing freedom and unfolding inner breadth are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's breadth of soul is a spiritual description of unfolding inner scope, not a separate technical or medical concept. Successful development remains a possibility rather than a guarantee that every change is beneficial.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies `MC/Apollon` through development informed by experience. Evaluating what one has lived through can accompany growing scientific ability, extensive changes, and increasing freedom; the entries support a developmental process rather than a claim that every alteration is constructive. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 74, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 93.)
