## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be a student of commerce or science. Successful education. To be fortunate through following the correct methods and in the area of education.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `MC + Apollon`; printed p. `93`; one-based PDF page `45` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

My serious knowledge, my successful concentration, my problems with knowledge, my extensive inhibitions, my scientific tasks, maturity through inner experiences, spread of inner disturbances, loss of my freedom.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Methodical learning alongside limitations and problems.
- **Psychology:** Concentration and maturity through experience are represented.
- **Body/health:** ABC's mental-disturbance language is historical and does not establish a diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Loss of freedom is named without a specific relationship setting.
- **Events/manifestations:** Commerce or science education, scientific tasks and extensive inhibitions are explicit.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Successful education and extensive inhibitions are different source possibilities. ABC's inner-disturbance language remains an attributed historical association, not a clinical diagnosis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies `MC/Apollon` through concentration and method in learning. Witte describes successful education in commerce or science, while ABC adds problems with knowledge, scientific tasks, and loss of freedom; disciplined study can be productive without removing constraint or difficulty. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 74, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 93.)
