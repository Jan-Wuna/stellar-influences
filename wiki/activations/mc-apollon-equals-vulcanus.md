## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Broad mental horizon. Great success in personal experiences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `MC + Apollon`; printed p. `93`; one-based PDF page `45` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Expansion of inner force or violence (seelische Gewalt), successful power of the soul, successful inner influence, my influence through knowledge, my experiences with power, my abundance of strength, experiencing inner strength, increase of inner strength, increasing inner influence.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Broad understanding, personal success, and influence through knowledge.
- **Psychology:** Experience of inner strength is represented.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or medical finding is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** ABC includes influence, power, and inner force or violence as historical descriptions.
- **Events/manifestations:** Great success in personal experience and increasing influence are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's Kraft-Fülle is abundance of strength, and Vermehrung is increase. Its seelische Gewalt retains a force or violence range; influence and successful experience do not erase that severe possibility.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies `MC/Apollon` through a broad mental horizon and influence gained through knowledge. Personal success meets ABC's experience of power and increasing inner influence, but its reference to inner force or violence must remain distinct from successful achievement rather than being absorbed into an entirely favorable picture. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 74, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 93.)
