## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Experiences in the field of fire, technology and engineering. An interest in the natural sciences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `MC + Apollon`; printed p. `93`; one-based PDF page `45` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Expansion of my achievements, success of a fiery soul, successful guidance of the soul (Seelen-Führung), my successful striving toward goals, my creative knowledge, my successful leadership, creative science of the soul, inner experiences of guidance.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Technical experience joined to purposeful achievement.
- **Psychology:** Goal striving and a creative orientation toward knowledge are explicit.
- **Body/health:** The entries establish no specific bodily or health claim.
- **Social/relationship:** ABC includes successful leadership and guidance of the soul as distinct source expressions.
- **Events/manifestations:** Fire, engineering, technology, and natural science are explicit fields.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC distinguishes guidance of the soul, successful personal leadership and inner experiences of guidance. These source formulations do not establish a particular leadership office or a therapeutic method.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies `MC/Apollon` through experience in fire, technology, engineering, and natural science. ABC adds expanding achievement, purposeful striving, creative knowledge and guidance; these connect technical experience with directed accomplishment without establishing invention or success as inevitable. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 74, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 93.)
