## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Being in community with others. Relationships with art-lovers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Cupido`; printed p. `84`; one-based PDF page `41` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Ensouled local community, ensouled place of art, ensouled spatial art, community of my partner, art of my partner, my artistic surroundings, my other community, my partner community, inner community with the partner.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Community experienced through other people and art.
- **Psychology:** The entries do not develop a separate psychological profile.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Companionship, art lovers, and community with a partner are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Artistic surroundings and participation with others are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC distinguishes local community, place of art and spatial art; ensouled remains its attributed vocabulary.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies `MC/Cupido` through immediate companionship and shared artistic surroundings. Being in community with others and relating to art lovers joins ABC's partner-community theme; the evidence supports association and affinity without establishing the quality or permanence of a partnership. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 70, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 84.)
