## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Contentment and happiness through occupations dealing with art, marriage or community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Cupido`; printed p. `84`; one-based PDF page `41` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Ensouled legal community, joy in ensouled art, shared joy of the soul, happy soul community, my financial community, my joy within the community, my artistic successes, my legal community.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Happiness and contentment in shared or artistic life.
- **Psychology:** Joy in community and art is explicit.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health meaning is established.
- **Social/relationship:** Marriage and financial or legal communities are named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Occupations involving art, marriage, or community may bring satisfaction.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's Rechts-Gemeinschaft means legal community, and Kunst-Erfolge means artistic successes; happiness is source language rather than a guarantee.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `MC/Cupido` through contentment in art, marriage, or communal occupation. Shared joy and enjoyment of community connect those settings with ABC's financial and legal communities, without turning satisfaction into a promise of good fortune in every partnership. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 70, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 84.)
