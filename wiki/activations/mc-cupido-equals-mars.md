## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Intending to marry. Working together with others. Belonging to a military union. Works of art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work community, the inner world of common work, my work for the community, my work community, my efforts for the arts, my synthesis activity, and the psychic impulses of a community

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Joint activity and communal effort.
- **Psychology:** An intention to marry is explicit; no broader motivation is established.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Working together, community service, and a military union are named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Works of art and collaborative work are explicit manifestations.
- **Conflicts/notes:** “Synthesis activity” remains an undefined source phrase, not a warrant to invent a specialized practice.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies `MC/Cupido` through doing things together. Collaborative work, work for the community, and efforts in art unite the entries, while Witte also names an intention to marry and membership in a military union as distinct forms of organized commitment. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 70, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 84.)
