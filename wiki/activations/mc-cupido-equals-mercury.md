## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Artistic and social thinking. Lover of the arts. The amateur. To be entertaining.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blessed young community, animated thoughts of the arts, common language of the soul, inner community of the youth, kiss of an animated youth, my intercourse in the community, my thoughts for synthesis, and my community in the youth

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Social and artistic thought communicated in community.
- **Psychology:** Art interest and an entertaining disposition are explicit.
- **Body/health:** No particular health meaning is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Shared language and youthful community are named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Amateur artistic participation and entertaining others are represented.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's “kiss of an animated youth” mistranslates Kunst einer beseelten Jugend; “intercourse” does not by itself establish sexual content.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies `MC/Cupido` through artistic and social thought expressed with others. An interest in the arts and entertaining company joins ABC's common language and youthful community, allowing shared communication and artistic participation without assuming professional expertise. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 70, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 84.)
