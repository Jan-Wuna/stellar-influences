## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To experience deception in social interaction. To be party to dissolving an organization. To remain a bungler in the arts. To quit art. A magician.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Cupido`; printed p. `85`; one-based PDF page `41` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Foreign souls within the community, my sensitivity within the community, my expectations of the community, my community in water, my uncertainty within the community, my future community, inner sensitivity within the community.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Community experienced through sensitivity, uncertainty or unreliable social dealings.
- **Psychology:** Sensitivity and expectations coexist with uncertainty about community.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health meaning is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Deception in social interaction and dissolving an organization are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Quitting art, remaining inept in it, and the magician are Witte's stated examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's water and future-community examples remain literal source motifs without an explained mechanism or timing rule; Witte's ineptitude is an attributed judgment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `MC/Cupido` through unreliable social dealings and the possible dissolution of an organization. Witte also names unsuccessful or abandoned artistic work and the magician, so the orientation includes both social and artistic expressions. ABC adds sensitivity and expectations alongside uncertainty within community, giving an inner counterpart to Witte's troubled social and artistic examples. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 70, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 85.)
