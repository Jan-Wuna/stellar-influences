## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The family tradition. Social changes. The sense for the community or art develops.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Inspired artistic development, development of a soul community, inner growth of a community, my development in the community, my artistic development, psychic unfolding in the community, and growing soul community

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Development of art, community, and their traditions.
- **Psychology:** A growing sense of community or art is explicit.
- **Body/health:** No specific bodily or health claim is established.
- **Social/relationship:** Family tradition and growth within a community are named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Social changes and artistic development are represented.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Family tradition and social change are separate themes in the entry; neither establishes the scale of a cultural transformation or a guaranteed beneficial outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies `MC/Cupido` through the growth of communal and artistic life. Family tradition exists alongside social change and a developing sense for art or community; ABC adds development within a group, so continuity and change are both part of the orientation. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 70, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 85.)
