## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be member with a group of gifted individuals. Spiritual communities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Insight of an inner community, insights of an animated community, common clarity of souls, ideas of a soul community, my clarity for the community, my culture and arts, my artistic ideas, and synthesis between spirit and soul

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Community organized around insight, gifts, or spiritual interests.
- **Psychology:** Clarity and artistic ideas are represented.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or medical meaning is established.
- **Social/relationship:** Gifted associates and spiritual communities are explicit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Cultural and artistic participation are named.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's synthesis of spirit and soul is a broad source expression, not a defined technique or a basis for ranking communities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies `MC/Cupido` through shared insight and spiritual association. Membership among gifted individuals joins ABC's communal clarity, cultural interests, and artistic ideas; the connection rests on shared understanding, without assuming that every such group is ideal or superior. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 70, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 85.)
