## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A sudden marriage. To be original and enterprising in society. Sense for rhythmic art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Cupido`; printed p. `85`; one-based PDF page `41` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Shared rhythm of the soul, my new art, my rhythm community, my reform community, my tensions within the community, my technical synthesis, new soul within the community, restless soul community.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sudden or original expression within social and artistic life.
- **Psychology:** Tension in community accompanies the initiative theme.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Sudden marriage and reform-oriented communities are named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Rhythmic art and enterprising social participation are explicit.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC names new art and a restless soul community; rhythmic art and sudden marriage remain distinct source examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies `MC/Cupido` through sudden union, social initiative, and rhythmic art. ABC adds reforming or tense communities, so novelty can energize shared life while also making it restless; the formula does not establish that every group change is liberating. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 70, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 85.)
