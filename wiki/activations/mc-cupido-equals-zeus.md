## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A desire to produce something artistic with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Cupido`; printed p. `85`; one-based PDF page `41` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Fiery soul community, shared inner efforts, I lead a community, my striving for the community, my artistic efforts, my achievements for the community, my goals within the community.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Collaborative creation and purposeful communal effort.
- **Psychology:** A desire for artistic production with others is explicit.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health meaning is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Shared efforts and leadership of a community are named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Artistic efforts and achievements for a group are represented.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC explicitly says I lead a community (ich führe eine Gemeinschaft); artistic creation is a shared effort rather than a guaranteed achievement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies `MC/Cupido` through a desire to produce art with others. ABC extends this into shared effort, leadership, and goals for the community; creative participation and communal accomplishment are linked, without assuming every group undertaking is artistic. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 70, entry start; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 85.)
