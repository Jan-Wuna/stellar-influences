## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be sad on account of money or other losses. Clouded happiness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Hades`; printed p. `86`; one-based PDF page `42` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Joy of an old soul, my lack of money, my endangered happiness, my joy in serving, my happiness in the past, my secret joy, my legal disadvantages, inner disadvantages through criminality, hidden joy of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Happiness troubled by loss, alongside private joy and pleasure in service.
- **Psychology:** Sadness about money; concealed or remembered happiness.
- **Body/health:** No specific bodily claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Service and the personal consequences of criminality appear in ABC.
- **Events/manifestations:** Financial losses or shortages.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's Rechts-Nachteile means legal disadvantages; endangered happiness coexists with private and remembered joy.

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 86, PDF p. 42.

## Contradictions

The entries allow both clouded happiness and private joy; these are different circumstances, not an established contradiction. ABC adds legal disadvantages as a distinct clause.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `MC/Hades` through happiness affected by financial or other losses in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also preserves pleasure in service, secret joy and happiness remembered from the past. The shared theme is the coexistence of deprivation and personal sources of happiness, rather than the absence of all joy.
