## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” PDF p. 71.

Sorrow or damage through one’s own actions. Hesitate to deal, lazy. Little initiative. Wanting to commit evil acts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” printed p. 86 (PDF p. 42).

Dangerous inner or soul work, I lack work, my labour service, my secret activity, my activity in the past, my criminal activity, addressing inner or soul grief, inner grief at work, and hidden inner or soul work.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sorrow involving one's actions, work or failure to act.
- **Psychology:** Hesitation, little initiative and harmful intentions in Witte's account.
- **Body/health:** No specific bodily claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Service, concealed activity and addressing grief in ABC.
- **Events/manifestations:** Damage through one's actions; distress at work or lack of work.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC includes addressing grief as well as experiencing it; no specific treatment method is supplied.

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 86, PDF p. 42.

## Contradictions

Hesitation and harmful action describe different possibilities. ABC's service and addressing grief also preserve activity that is not harmful in intention.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies `MC/Hades` through personal sorrow, deficiency, and service in acting or working. Witte describes damage caused by one's deeds, hesitation, and little initiative, as well as harmful intentions. ABC brings the theme into lack of work, service, concealed activity, grief at work, and addressing grief. These remain alternatives within the entries; they do not establish that every action is harmful or supply a particular treatment method. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 86 / PDF p. 42)
