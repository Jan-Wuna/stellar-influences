## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Ponderous thoughts. Stupidity. To keep bad things to oneself. To meditate about miserable things. Wishing evil on others. A bad moment for plans or decisions. Problematic attitude in ideas, plans, talking or writing.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

I at the thinking of the past, my sorrow in youth, my language of privation, my flawed writing, my hidden thoughts, and my psychic youth crime

## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Hades = Mercury` mentalizes the axis into heavy thought, bad intentions, and problematic planning.
- Chart expression: ponderous thinking, miserable reflection, harmful wishes, and ideas, speech, or decisions shaped by gloom, secrecy, or mental distortion.
