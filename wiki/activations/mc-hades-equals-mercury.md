## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” PDF p. 71.

Ponderous thoughts. Stupidity. To keep bad things to oneself. To meditate about miserable things. Wishing evil on others. A bad moment for plans or decisions. Problematic attitude in ideas, plans, talking or writing.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” printed p. 86 (PDF p. 42).

Soulful connections in youth, reports about dangers to the inner life or soul, I think of the past, my grief in youth, my conversations about privations, my deficient writing, my hidden thoughts, and inner or soul-related youth criminality.

The final phrase retains the source's “seelische Jugend-Kriminalität,” which the entry does not explain further.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Burdened thought, speech, writing and decisions.
- **Psychology:** Brooding on misery, keeping troubles private or wishing harm in Witte.
- **Body/health:** No specific bodily claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Soulful youth connections and conversations about deprivation.
- **Events/manifestations:** Problematic plans, reports about inner danger, hidden thoughts and deficient writing.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's youth-criminality expression remains unexplained and supports no individual criminal or clinical judgment.

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 86, PDF p. 42.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes burdened or hostile thinking. ABC also includes soulful connections, reporting danger, and discussing privation, so communication is not uniformly hostile.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies `MC/Hades` through thought and expression occupied by personal sorrow, privation, danger, or the past. Witte links brooding, secrecy, and problematic decisions with planning, speech, or writing difficulties; ABC adds soulful youth connections, reports about inner danger, conversations about privation, and hidden thoughts. The organizing theme is difficult material being thought about or communicated, with hostile wishes retained as a Witte possibility and ABC's unexplained youth-criminality phrase excluded from further inference. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 86 / PDF p. 42)
