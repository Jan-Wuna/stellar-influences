## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” PDF p. 71.

To get into a tight spot on account of a woman. Being worried about a woman. Dissatisfied with public conditions. The moment of a grieving populace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” printed p. 86 (PDF p. 42).

Deprivation regarding the woman, endangered soul of the woman, endangered soul of the people, my service to the people, my lack of feeling, minute of danger for the woman, soul sorrow of the woman, soul sorrow in the hour, hidden soul of the woman, and past of my people

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Personal worry and collective grief.
- **Psychology:** Sorrow, dissatisfaction or a reported lack of feeling.
- **Body/health:** No specific bodily condition is named.
- **Social/relationship:** The sources associate distress with women and the populace.
- **Events/manifestations:** Worry about a woman, dissatisfaction with public conditions, service to the people, and a people's past.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Gender associations belong to the sources' historical doctrine.

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 86, PDF p. 42.

## Contradictions

No direct disagreement is established. Personal concern for a woman and public sorrow are different scales of the same entry, not interchangeable claims about all women.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies `MC/Hades` through worry and grief that extend from personal relationships to public feeling. Witte names concern about a woman, difficult circumstances involving her, and dissatisfaction with public conditions; ABC adds service to the people, lack of feeling, a woman's hidden inner life, and a people's past. The references to women and a grieving populace remain historical source associations, without becoming universal gender claims. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 86 / PDF p. 42)
