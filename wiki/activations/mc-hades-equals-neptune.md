## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” PDF p. 71.

Grief because of falsehood, deceit or want of firmness. To give up the last hope. To deal with contaminated air or water.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” printed p. 87 (PDF p. 42).

Sensitivity of an old soul, lack of inner sensitivity, my service in the air, my shortage of water, my endangerment in water, my hidden weakness, a minute of danger in water, and unknown inner or soul grief.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Grief involving deceit, instability or lost hope.
- **Psychology:** Lost hope, sensitivity or its lack, hidden weakness and unknown grief.
- **Body/health:** Contaminated air or water is an environmental image, not a diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Falsehood and deceit.
- **Events/manifestations:** Giving up hope; service in the air, water shortage or danger in water.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Sensitivity and its lack are separate ABC possibilities; air/water work is not itself a diagnosis or proof of contamination.

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 87, PDF p. 42.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes deceit, lost hope, and contaminated air or water. ABC also includes service in the air, sensitivity, and hidden weakness; not every air or water setting is described as contaminated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `MC/Hades` through grief when trust, firmness, or hope fails, alongside sensitivity, hidden weakness, and air or water settings. Witte names deceit and contaminated air or water; ABC includes service in the air, shortage or danger in water, and unknown inner grief. These are distinct emotional and environmental expressions, with sensitivity and its lack both retained, rather than evidence of a medical condition or one universal hidden cause. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 87 / PDF p. 42)
