## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” PDF p. 71.

Grief and sorrow in one’s own relationships. Selecting bad relationships. To serve in a relationship. Disloyal attitude towards contacts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” printed p. 86 (PDF p. 42).

A mysterious inner or soul connection, my encounter with need, my disadvantageous connections, my criminal connections, my connections in service, my connection to the past, and a hidden inner or soul connection.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sorrow, service or adverse choices within relationships.
- **Psychology:** Grief and disloyal attitudes in Witte's account.
- **Body/health:** No specific bodily claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Difficult contacts, service ties and connections to the past.
- **Events/manifestations:** Choosing harmful relationships or encountering need.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Service and ties to the past remain distinct from criminal or disadvantageous connections.

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 86, PDF p. 42.

## Contradictions

The entries describe several kinds of connection, including service, secrecy, disadvantage, and ties to the past. Service does not by itself imply degradation or disloyalty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies `MC/Hades` through personal sorrow, service, or disadvantage within relationships. Witte includes poor choices of contacts and disloyal attitudes, while ABC adds encounters with need, concealed connections, and ties to the past. The common theme is what one's connections carry, with service, criminal contacts, and historical ties retained as distinct possibilities rather than one uniform kind of relationship. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 86 / PDF p. 42)
