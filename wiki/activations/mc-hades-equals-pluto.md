## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” PDF p. 71.

To grieve over one’s decline. Sad transformations. Changes within worse psychological situations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” printed p. 87 (PDF p. 42).

Development of inner grief, dangerous inner or soul unfolding, my secret development, my criminal development, my change of service, change in my distress or need, hidden inner development, and growing danger to the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sorrow accompanying decline or difficult change.
- **Psychology:** Grieving over one's decline; growing emotional distress.
- **Body/health:** No specific bodily condition is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Concealed or criminal developments are listed in ABC.
- **Events/manifestations:** Sad transformations, changes in service, and changes in distress or need.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Change in service and concealed development retain their own scope; the source does not establish inevitable lifelong deterioration.

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 87, PDF p. 42.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sorrow and decline. ABC adds changes in service or need and concealed development, while retaining dangerous or criminal development as particular difficult expressions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies `MC/Hades` through personal sorrow or difficult circumstances undergoing development and change. Witte describes grieving over decline and sad transformations; ABC adds changes in service or need, concealed development, and growing inner danger, including criminal development as one difficult possibility. The entries support changes within a burdened situation without establishing a clinical condition or an inevitable lifelong course. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 87 / PDF p. 42)
