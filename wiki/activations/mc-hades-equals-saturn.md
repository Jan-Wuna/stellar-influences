## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” PDF p. 71.

Homeless. The stranger. Suffering through one’s own actions. Grief through sickness and separation. To be unemployed. To feel set back because of a wrong attitude.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” printed p. 87 (PDF p. 42).

Grief and pain of the soul, my dangerous past, my hard service, my criminal past, my disadvantages through losses, my hidden problems, and hidden inner or soul disturbances.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Estrangement, loss and personal setback.
- **Psychology:** Grief, inner pain, hidden disturbances and feeling set back.
- **Body/health:** Witte associates grief with sickness; no diagnosis follows.
- **Social/relationship:** Homelessness, being a stranger and separation.
- **Events/manifestations:** Unemployment, hard service, losses and a dangerous or criminal past.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Historical sickness and inner-disturbance claims remain attributed; hardship is not uniformly described as self-caused.

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 87, PDF p. 42.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize different forms of hardship. Witte includes homelessness and unemployment, while ABC develops past danger, hard service, losses, and hidden inner problems.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies `MC/Hades` through personal hardship, estrangement, and setback. Witte names homelessness, being a stranger, unemployment, and grief through sickness or separation; ABC adds inner pain, hard service, losses, a dangerous past, and hidden problems. These are related difficult circumstances, while Witte's reference to one's actions or attitude does not establish that all such hardship is self-caused. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 87 / PDF p. 42)
