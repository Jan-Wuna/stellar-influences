## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” PDF p. 71.

Ill humored on account of sickness. To grieve over the poor condition of one’s own body. Grief through male persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” printed p. 86 (PDF p. 42).

My bodily deficiency, my personal secret, my personal past, a minute of personal danger, a man's inner or soul grief, inner or soul service to people, a man's hidden soul, and a centre for inner or soul grief.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal sorrow or deficiency expressed through the body, one's past, secrets, and service.
- psychology: Ill humor, personal grief, and concealed inner life.
- body/health: Witte historically associates illness or poor bodily condition with grief; no individual diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Grief involving men and service to people in the source's historical examples.
- events/manifestations: A moment of personal danger and a centre for inner grief.
- conflicts/notes: Service, personal secrecy, and a moment of danger remain distinct from bodily illness.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes illness and male-linked grief. ABC also supplies personal secrets, the past, danger, and service, extending the range beyond bodily condition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Hades = Sun`, Sun specifies personal sorrow and deficiency through the body, one's history, and individual circumstances. Witte names grief over poor bodily condition and male persons; ABC adds secrets, the personal past, a moment of danger, and service to people involving inner grief. Bodily difficulty is one historical association, while service, concealment, and the past retain their own scope. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 86 / PDF p. 42)
