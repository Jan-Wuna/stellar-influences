## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” PDF p. 71.

Sorrow through dependence and poverty.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Hades,” printed p. 87 (PDF p. 42).

Influential service of the soul, danger to the soul through violence, dangerous inner or soul influence, my lack of influence, my secret influence, my hidden influence, my renunciation of violence, hidden inner or soul strength, and criminal violence in the minute.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal sorrow or service expressed through dependence, influence, powerlessness, and force.
- psychology: Hidden strength, secret influence, or lack of influence in ABC's entry.
- body/health: No medical effect is supplied; danger through violence is a source possibility.
- social/relationship: Dependence, poverty, influential service, and renunciation of violence.
- events/manifestations: Dangerous influence and criminal violence in a particular moment among ABC's examples.
- conflicts/notes: Renouncing violence and experiencing it are distinct expressions; the minute phrase is not a rate of violence or a supplied timing method.

## Contradictions

Witte focuses on dependence and poverty. ABC also preserves influential service, hidden strength, and renunciation of violence, alongside dangerous force.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Hades = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies personal sorrow and service through influence, dependence, and force. Witte names sorrow through dependence and poverty; ABC includes both lack of influence and influential service, as well as secret influence and hidden inner strength. Danger through violence remains alongside explicit renunciation of violence, so the range is not uniformly powerless or violent. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 71; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 87 / PDF p. 42)
