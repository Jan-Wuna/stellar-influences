## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” PDF p. 73.

Socially leading others. A great artist. Artistic genius.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” printed p. 91 (PDF p. 44).

A special inner or soul community, my authority in the community, my community with the chief, my great art, my independence within the community, a self-confident inner or soul community, and an important minute for a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal authority or independence through community, social leadership, and art.
- psychology: Artistic stature and communal self-confidence.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Leading socially, belonging with a chief, and remaining independent within a group.
- events/manifestations: Great art or artistic genius and an important community moment.
- conflicts/notes: Artistic genius remains a source possibility, not a guaranteed personal ability; independence and group confidence are distinct clauses.

## Contradictions

Both entries support social or artistic stature. ABC also develops community membership, self-confidence, and independence within the group.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Kronos = Cupido`, Cupido specifies personal authority and independence through community and art. Witte describes social leadership and artistic greatness; ABC adds authority within a group, community with a chief, and independence within that community. Its communal self-confidence retains belonging as well as leadership, while artistic genius remains a particular source possibility rather than a promised ability. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 73; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 91 / PDF p. 44)
