## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” PDF p. 73.

To have a great and high trend of thoughts. To make arrangements. Words of the native, which people pay attention.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” printed p. 90 (PDF p. 44).

Especially soulful conversations, thoughts of a great soul, my thoughts about authority, my conversations with the hierarchy, my independence in youth, my important conversations, and an important discussion in this minute.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal authority and independence expressed through thought, arrangements, and consequential conversation.
- psychology: Elevated or soulful thought and youthful independence.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Conversations with hierarchy and words receiving others' attention.
- events/manifestations: Making arrangements and important discussions.
- conflicts/notes: Being heard does not establish compulsory agreement; youthful independence and conversations with hierarchy are distinct examples.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize significant thought or speech. ABC adds dialogue with hierarchy, youthful independence, and soulful conversation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Kronos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies personal authority and independence through thought, arrangements, and conversation. Witte describes elevated thoughts and words that receive attention; ABC adds thoughts about authority, conversations with hierarchy, soulful dialogue, and independence in youth. The emphasis is consequential communication, without requiring that listeners agree or obey. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 73; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 90 / PDF p. 44)
