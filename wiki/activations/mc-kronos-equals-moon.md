## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” PDF p. 73.

To fill a public office. Deputies. General overseers or managers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” printed p. 90 (PDF p. 44).

An especially feelingful soul, the great soul of a people, my authority as a woman, my woman or wife dominates, my great feelings, my independent woman or wife, my people's self-confidence, my woman or wife's self-confidence, and an important minute of an hour.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal authority and independence through public responsibility, feelings, and women's roles in the historical entry.
- psychology: Strong feelings and individual or collective self-confidence.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public office, deputies or managers, and a woman's authority or independence.
- events/manifestations: General oversight and significant collective or emotional moments.
- conflicts/notes: Historical gender examples are not universal claims; strong feeling does not establish appointment to public office.

## Contradictions

Witte supplies public-office and management roles. ABC also includes emotional depth, women's independence, and collective self-confidence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Kronos = Moon`, Moon specifies personal authority and independence through public responsibility, collective confidence, and feelings. Witte names public office, deputies, and managers; ABC adds strong feeling, a people's self-confidence, and historical examples of a woman's authority, dominance, or independence. The emotional and relational range remains alongside official roles, which are possibilities rather than promised appointments. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 73; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 90 / PDF p. 44)
