## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” PDF p. 73.

To be unrecognized, rejected, overlooked. Failure, in particular cases, as a manager. Captains in air or water.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” printed p. 91 (PDF p. 44).

Special inner sensitivity, I reject authority, my weak superior, my dominance in water, my great intuition, my weak authority, my future independence, and refinement of my self-confidence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal authority and independence expressed through non-recognition, weakness, intuition, or air and water command.
- psychology: Inner sensitivity, intuition, and refined self-confidence.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Rejection, overlooked leadership, weak superiors, or rejecting authority.
- events/manifestations: Managerial failure in particular cases, captains in air or water, and future independence.
- conflicts/notes: Failure is explicitly conditional in Witte; sensitivity and future independence keep the range broader than failed leadership.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes non-recognition and conditional managerial failure, but also captains in air or water. ABC adds intuition, sensitivity, and future independence alongside weak authority.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Kronos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies personal authority and independence through non-recognition or weakness, alongside intuition, sensitivity, and air or water settings. Witte describes rejection and managerial failure in particular cases, while separately naming captains in air or water. ABC retains weak authority but also great intuition, refinement of self-confidence, and future independence. Those favorable or neutral expressions prevent non-recognition from becoming the sole outcome. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 73; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 91 / PDF p. 44)
