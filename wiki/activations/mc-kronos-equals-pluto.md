## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” PDF p. 73.

The desire to dominate. To be or to become a master. To develop skills over the average.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” printed p. 91 (PDF p. 44).

Special inner or soul unfolding, development of my self-confidence, my development with the chief, my independent development, my growing independence, developing inner or soul greatness, and growing dominance of the inner being.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal authority and independence undergoing development toward mastery or greater inner stature.
- psychology: Desire to dominate, developing self-confidence, and inner unfolding.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Development with a chief alongside independent development.
- events/manifestations: Becoming a master, developing above-average skills, and growing independence.
- conflicts/notes: Mastery, self-confidence, and domination are distinct expressions; no necessary method of force is supplied.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes domination and mastery. ABC also includes self-confidence and independent inner development, broadening the range beyond ruling others.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Kronos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies personal authority and independence through development of mastery, self-confidence, and inner stature. Witte names the desire to dominate and skills above the average; ABC adds development with a chief, independent development, and growing independence. Their overlap is authority or capacity taking shape, with domination retained as one expression rather than the necessary means of all development. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 73; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 91 / PDF p. 44)
