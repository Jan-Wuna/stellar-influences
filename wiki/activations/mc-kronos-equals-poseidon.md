## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” PDF p. 73.

Higher education. Intellectual leader. Spiritus rector.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” printed p. 91 (PDF p. 44).

Insight of a great soul, my cultural state, my special clarity, my insight into authority, my mental or spiritual authority, my mental or spiritual guidance, my intellectual or spiritual independence, a self-confident mind-soul, and an important minute of truth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal authority and independence through higher education, intellectual guidance, clarity, and insight.
- psychology: Self-confidence, independent thought, and insight into authority.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Intellectual leadership and mental or spiritual guidance.
- events/manifestations: Higher learning, cultural state, and a significant moment of truth in ABC's terms.
- conflicts/notes: Spiritus rector denotes the directing or guiding mind in Witte's intellectual-leadership context; no infallibility is established.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize intellectual guidance. ABC adds independent thought, clarity, and insight into authority without requiring formal educational status in every expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Kronos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies personal authority and independence through learning, insight, and intellectual or spiritual guidance. Witte names higher education, an intellectual leader, and the directing mind; ABC adds clarity, insight into authority, independent thought, and a self-confident mind-soul. Their overlap is the capacity to guide through understanding, without turning intellectual authority into infallibility or requiring one formal role. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 73; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 91 / PDF p. 44)
