## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” PDF p. 73.

Decrease and losses through one’s own initiative. Owner of an estate. Dealing in agricultural produce. An authority on the soil.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” printed p. 91 (PDF p. 44).

Reputation of a mature soul, special inner problems, great inner or soul pain, my disturbed independence, my great difficulties, my concentration on what is special, inner or soul greatness in old age, and loss of a great soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal authority or independence meeting loss and difficulty, with land-related authority as a separate setting.
- psychology: Concentration, mature reputation, inner pain, and disturbed independence.
- body/health: Old age and inner pain are source examples, without a medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Estate ownership, agricultural trade, and authority on the soil.
- events/manifestations: Losses through one's initiative and loss of a great soul among the examples.
- conflicts/notes: Agricultural or land authority is not inherently a loss; mature stature remains alongside difficulty.

## Contradictions

Witte includes losses but also land and agricultural authority. ABC adds mature reputation and inner greatness in old age alongside disturbed independence and pain.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Kronos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies personal authority and independence through difficult conditions, losses, and mature or land-related settings. Witte names losses through one's initiative but also estate ownership and agricultural or soil expertise. ABC adds disturbed independence, inner pain, and difficulty alongside a mature soul's reputation and inner stature in old age. The range preserves competence and maturity as well as setback, without treating all land-related activity as a loss. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 73; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 91 / PDF p. 44)
