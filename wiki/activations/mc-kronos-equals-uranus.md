## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” PDF p. 73.

Wanting to create something on an important scale. Suddenly show leadership, which causes excitement and surprise. Manager of a technical establishment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” printed p. 91 (PDF p. 44).

Great inner excitement, my new self-confidence, my independent rhythm, my particular restlessness, my great excitement, impulses through my authority, my technical independence, and an important event in the minute.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal authority or independence expressed suddenly, technically, or on an important scale.
- psychology: New self-confidence, excitement, restlessness, and an independent rhythm.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Sudden leadership that excites or surprises others.
- events/manifestations: Managing a technical establishment, technical independence, and important creation or events.
- conflicts/notes: Technical management is a particular role alongside broader independent activity; surprise does not itself establish forceful command.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sudden leadership and technical management. ABC adds new self-confidence, independent rhythm, and restlessness alongside technical independence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Kronos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies personal authority and independence through sudden leadership, technical activity, and renewed self-confidence. Witte describes important creation, surprising leadership, and management of a technical establishment; ABC adds independent rhythm, technical independence, and inner excitement or restlessness. The entries connect authority with change and surprise without making every such event an act of forceful command. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 73; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 91 / PDF p. 44)
