## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” PDF p. 73.

A superior to other people. An authority for others. The expert.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” printed p. 90 (PDF p. 44).

An especially soulful public, my chief in public, my precedence in public, my authority in public, my agency in public, my independence in public life, and an important minute in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal authority and independence expressed publicly through expertise or precedence.
- psychology: No distinct psychological state is supplied beyond ABC's soulful-public wording.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Being an authority or expert for others and a chief's public presence.
- events/manifestations: Public authority, institutional presence, and independent public activity.
- conflicts/notes: Expertise and a superior role do not establish intrinsic superiority over other people.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes expertise or a superior role. ABC develops public authority, a chief, institutions, and public independence without stating a conflict.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Kronos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies personal authority and independence through public life. Witte names an authority or expert for others; ABC adds public precedence, a chief's presence, an authority or agency, and independence in public activity. The common emphasis is publicly expressed standing or competence, without making a superior role a judgment of intrinsic human worth. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 73; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 90 / PDF p. 44)
