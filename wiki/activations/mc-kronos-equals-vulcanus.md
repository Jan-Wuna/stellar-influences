## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” PDF p. 73.

To have the power of attorney. A mighty authority. To exercise the power of the state. Executive powers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” printed p. 91 (PDF p. 44).

Special inner or soul influence, great inner strength, my powerful state, my influential authority, my strength from independence, my power through the chief, my strong self-confidence, and strong dominance of the inner being.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal authority and independence expressed through power, influence, and executive responsibility.
- psychology: Strong self-confidence, inner strength, and inner dominance.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Power through a chief, power of attorney, and exercise of state authority.
- events/manifestations: Executive powers, influential authority, and strength from independence.
- conflicts/notes: Formal authority and inner strength are distinct expressions; the entries do not establish compulsory submission by others.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes legal or executive powers. ABC adds inner strength and independence alongside power through the chief or state.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Kronos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies personal authority and independence through power, influence, and inner strength. Witte names power of attorney, executive powers, and state authority; ABC adds self-confidence, power through a chief, and strength from independence. Their range includes formally granted authority and personal inner capacity, without establishing that others must submit or that all influence is governmental. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 73; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 91 / PDF p. 44)
