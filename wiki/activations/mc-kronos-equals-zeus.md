## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” PDF p. 73.

A foreman or manager of a machine works. Army officers. To lead without falling.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Kronos,” printed p. 91 (PDF p. 44).

Efforts of a great soul, beginning of my independence, special inner effort, my independent striving toward goals, leadership and direction of the soul, goals of my state, creative dominance of the soul, and important effort in the minute.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal authority and independence expressed through purposeful effort, leadership, and technical or military work.
- psychology: Independent goal-striving, inner effort, and creative inner dominance.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Foremen, managers of machine works, and army officers are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Beginning independence, pursuing state goals, and leading without falling in Witte's wording.
- conflicts/notes: Technical or military roles are examples rather than required occupations; a particular minute is not a rate of work.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes technical or military leadership. ABC adds independent goal-striving, inner direction, and the beginning of independence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Kronos = Zeus`, Zeus specifies personal authority and independence through purposeful effort and leadership. Witte gives foremen, machine-works managers, and army officers as examples of leading without falling. ABC adds the beginning of independence, independent goal-striving, inner direction, creative dominance, and state goals. These retain inner initiative and development alongside technical or military roles, without prescribing a single occupation. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 73; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 91 / PDF p. 44)
