## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Admetos entry, PDF p. 57.

One’s firmness, perseverance and steadfastness. To be insensible, apathetic, hard hearted. A conservative attitude. Standing on firm ground. Going back to the primary cause of things. To feel paralyzed. A dead soul. The moment (minute) of death.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal firmness, perseverance, resistance, and possible standstill.
- psychology: Steadfast or conservative attitude; apathy and hardness are also named.
- body/health: Feeling paralyzed and death language are historical associations, not an individual prognosis.
- social/relationship: Hard-heartedness is named, without a particular relationship scenario.
- events/manifestations: Standing firm and returning to primary causes; the source also names a moment of death.
- conflicts/notes: Firmness and apathy are distinct expressions; no cross-source comparison is possible here.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes both steadfastness and deadened feeling. They describe a range rather than proving that perseverance must become apathy.

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through firmness, perseverance, and resistance. Witte includes standing on firm ground and returning to primary causes, alongside apathy, hardness, and feeling paralyzed. The range preserves grounded persistence as well as standstill; the severe soul and death language remains historical source doctrine, without establishing an individual's outcome. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 57)
