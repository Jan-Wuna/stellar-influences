## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Ascendant entry, PDF p. 56.

One's relationship, position or attitude towards others. To join with others for a common objective. The social and vocational relations in the closer surroundings. Others influence one’s own emotions. In this minute at this place.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal orientation expressed through relationships and the immediate social or vocational setting.
- psychology: Others' influence on one's emotions and one's attitude toward them.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Joining others for a common objective.
- events/manifestations: Local social and vocational contacts and a particular time at a place.
- conflicts/notes: Mutual influence does not erase personal agency; the entry supplies no separate timing method.

## Contradictions

Only one entry is represented. Personal attitude, others' emotional influence, and cooperation describe related directions of contact.

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Asc`, Asc specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through others and the immediate surroundings. Witte includes one's attitude toward people, their influence on one's emotions, and joining them for a common objective, especially in local social or vocational relations. The entry connects inner experience with actual contact, while its particular time-and-place phrase supplies no further timing procedure. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 56)
