#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Hades entry, PDF p. 57.

To have secrets. Ill humored. Bereavement. Grief. To do without. To be easily offended. Grief through deficiencies causes the feeling of inferiority. Business losses. Pursued by misfortune. Poor profession or wrong goals of life. Profession in waste management. Misguided emotions. Inclined toward serving others. Historian. The attitude towards the past. A bad moment (minute).

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal sorrow, deprivation, secrecy, service, and attitudes toward the past.
- psychology: Grief, ill humor, feeling inferior, and being easily offended among the source expressions.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is supplied.

- events/manifestations: Business losses, vocational difficulty, waste management, and historical work.
- conflicts/notes: Service, history, and waste management are not inherently shameful; the source's difficult expressions do not define every occupation or personal outcome.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Hades`, Hades specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through sorrow, deprivation, secrets, service, and the past. Witte includes grief, business or vocational losses, and feelings of inferiority, but also serving others, historical work, and waste management. The common thread is engagement with difficult or overlooked personal material and circumstances, while the listed occupations and service need not be read as shameful or uniformly unfortunate. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 57)
