## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Jupiter entry, PDF p. 56.

Blissful feelings. To be positive of one’s own direction in life. An optimist. To be well-wishing and benevolent. Harmless, good natured or contented. To feel happy. Reach one's own goals with luck and success. The own attitude to money. Moment of happiness.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal contentment, optimism, benevolence, and favorable direction in life.
- psychology: Blissful feelings, confidence in one's direction, and happiness.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Well-wishing, benevolence, and good nature.
- events/manifestations: Reaching goals with luck or success and one's attitude to money.
- conflicts/notes: A favorable moment and successful goals are source possibilities, not guaranteed outcomes or a financial prescription.

## Contradictions

Only Witte's entry is represented. Positive disposition and favorable results coexist, without an explicit claim that one necessarily causes the other.

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through optimism, contentment, and goodwill. Witte connects positive direction in life with benevolence and blissful feelings, while also naming goals reached through luck and success and one's attitude to money. The entry retains favorable inner experience and outcomes as possibilities without making happiness a guarantee of success. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 56)
