## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Kronos entry, PDF p. 57.

To be independent or outstanding in profession. To direct and accomplish something. Authority. Feeling of self-confident or grandeur. The own authority, greatness and skills. To fill an official position. An independent attitude. To reject the average. Authority bestowed by the state. An outstanding moment (minute).

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal authority, independence, skills, and professional distinction.
- psychology: Self-confidence or grandeur and rejection of the average.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Directing activity, official position, and authority bestowed by the state.
- events/manifestations: Accomplishment, independent professional activity, and a distinguished moment.
- conflicts/notes: Independent authority and state-granted authority are distinct expressions; neither requires every person to hold rank.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes professional independence as well as official authority. Those routes are not interchangeable requirements.

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through authority, independence, skill, and accomplishment. Witte includes an independent or outstanding professional position and confidence in one's own capacities, alongside official or state-granted authority. The range therefore includes independent competence and formal standing, rather than making rank the necessary centre of identity. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 57)
