## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Mars entry, PDF p. 56.

Making arrangements. Giving orders. One’s own action. A agitated mind. Self will. To feel energy. To pull oneself together. The urge to be active. Minute of action. Personal goals at work. Psychically active.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal initiative, energy, self-will, and activity directed toward work goals.
- psychology: An agitated or active mind, feeling energy, and pulling oneself together.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Giving orders and making arrangements.
- events/manifestations: One's actions, personal work goals, and a moment of action.
- conflicts/notes: Initiative and agitation are distinct aspects; the entry does not establish successful completion or an external conflict.

## Contradictions

One entry is represented. It includes deliberate arrangements and work goals alongside agitation and the urge to act.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Mars`, Mars specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through initiative, self-will, and the urge to act. Witte connects feeling energy and pulling oneself together with arrangements, orders, and personal goals at work. Agitation and deliberate direction both remain visible, without a separate promise that the intended work is completed. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 56)
