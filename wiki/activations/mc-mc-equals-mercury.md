### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Mercury entry, PDF p. 56.

One’s own observation. One’s own thinking. To meditate. Monologues. Self talks. Flexibility of the soul. Business thoughts. Mental attitude. The mobility of thinking. The faculty of thinking. The minute of news. Individual thoughts. Thoughts or news in profession. Media professionals.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal observation, thought, and mental flexibility.
- psychology: Meditation, self-talk, mental attitude, and the mobility of thinking.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Professional information and media work; no distinct intimate relationship claim is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Business thoughts, professional news, and a moment of receiving news.
- conflicts/notes: Private reflection and professional communication are both represented; neither requires a fixed profession or personality.

## Contradictions

This single entry ranges from private self-talk to professional news and media activity. These are different settings for thought, not conflicting claims.

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through observation, thinking, and mental flexibility. Witte connects meditation and self-talk with business thoughts, professional news, and media activity: attention may turn inward to one's own thinking or outward to information in one's work. The entry supports these settings without making communication a compulsory profession or the whole of personal identity. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 56)
