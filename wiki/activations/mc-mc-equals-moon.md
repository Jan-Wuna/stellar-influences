## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Moon entry, PDF p. 56.

The psychological influence of women. One’s own mind. The own attitude towards females, a people or to the public. The state of the mind and soul. Personal relations with females and to the public. Emotional hour. Emotions in a people. The start of an hour. The feeling or attitude of being a woman.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: One's state of mind and emotional relations with women or the public.
- psychology: Personal attitude, emotional experience, and the source's feeling of being a woman.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Women's psychological influence, relations with women, and attitudes toward a people or the public.
- events/manifestations: Public emotions, an emotional hour, and the start of an hour.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered associations belong to this historical entry; its hour references do not supply a timing method.

## Contradictions

One entry is represented. It moves between one's own emotional state and relations with women or a public, without establishing that these settings always coincide.

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Moon`, Moon specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through one's state of mind and feelings in relation to women or the public. Witte links private attitude with personal relations and emotions within a people, so the entry spans individual feeling and a wider social setting. Its associations with women and the feeling of being a woman remain attributed historical language; the hour expressions supply no separate timing procedure. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 56)
