## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Neptune entry, PDF p. 57.

To decline. To refuse something. To be unsure, unable to support. Unintelligible attitude. To follow wrong ideas. “I deceive”. Untruth. Falsehood. Swindle. To be intuitive. To concentrate on the future. To follow the unknown, the doubtful. To be disappointed. To appear unreliable or mysterious. To feel uncomfortable. Moment (minute) of uncertainty.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertainty, refusal, intuition, and attention to the future or unknown.
- psychology: Doubt, disappointment, discomfort, and following wrong ideas.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Inability to support, an unintelligible attitude, and appearing unreliable or mysterious.
- events/manifestations: Refusal, deception or swindle, and a moment of uncertainty.
- conflicts/notes: Intuition and future-directed attention coexist with deception and doubt; the entry does not equate them or establish a person's honesty.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes intuition as well as wrong ideas and deception. Those distinct possibilities should not become one verdict about character.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through uncertainty and attention to what is unknown or still ahead. Witte includes intuition and concentration on the future alongside refusal, disappointment, wrong ideas, and deception. The connection is an unsettled or difficult-to-understand attitude, but the separate possibilities remain open: an intuitive interest in the future is not evidence that someone deceives others. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 57)
