## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Node entry, PDF p. 56.

Soul mates. One’s close personal connections. Contacts in profession. Inner or emotional bonds. The moment (minute) of contact. The moment of connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Close personal connections and inner or emotional bonds.
- psychology: Felt affinity expressed in the source's “soul mates” language.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Personal bonds and professional contacts.
- events/manifestations: Contact, connection, and the moment of making contact.
- conflicts/notes: The entry supplies affinity and contact without guaranteeing a predestined partner, durable union, or a timing method.

## Contradictions

One entry is represented. Close emotional bonds and professional contacts are distinct settings for connection; not every contact is described as intimate.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Node`, Node specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through close connections and inner bonds. Witte places the intimate language of “soul mates” beside contacts in one's profession and moments of connection, joining felt affinity with the practical occurrence of contact. These settings need not have the same depth or duration, and the entry does not establish a predestined relationship. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 56)
