## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Pluto entry, PDF p. 57.

One’s own intellectual capacity, as well as the ability and possibility of development. One’s capacity to apprehend. One’s own transformation and development. Inner transformation. The developmental stages of the own mind. Metamorphosis. To appear to be a completely different person. Moment (minute) of change.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal development, inner transformation, and capacity to understand.
- psychology: Intellectual capacity and developmental stages of one's mind.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Appearing to be a completely different person; no specific relationship is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Metamorphosis and a moment of change.
- conflicts/notes: Capacity and possibility of development are not promises of improvement, repeated crises, or increased power.

## Contradictions

One entry is represented. Intellectual development and a changed outward impression are different aspects of transformation, not mutually exclusive results.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through development and inner transformation. Witte connects the capacity to apprehend with stages of mental development and the possibility of appearing to be a completely different person. The entry thus joins a change within one's understanding to a changed impression of the person, without specifying that change is repeated, beneficial, or an increase in power. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 57)
