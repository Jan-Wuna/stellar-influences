## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Poseidon entry, PDF p. 57.

The divine spark in men. The own intellectual aspiration for knowledge and education. Deep thinker, or seeking to be one. One’s own intellectual knowledge, education or mental skills. One’s own higher self-image or aspiration. To give life to a project or plan. The moment of a large flooding.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intellectual aspiration, knowledge, education, and a higher self-image.
- psychology: Deep thinking or the wish to become a deep thinker; the source's “divine spark” language.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: No specific relationship is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Giving life to a project or plan; a separately stated moment of large flooding.
- conflicts/notes: Aspiration and attained knowledge differ. Flooding is explicit but unexplained and is not evidence of intellectual attainment or a flood prediction.

## Contradictions

This single entry combines intellectual and spiritual aspiration with a separate flooding clause. It gives no explanation connecting the latter to the personal themes.

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through intellectual aspiration, education, and a higher self-image. Witte distinguishes deep thinking from seeking to become a deep thinker and connects mental skills with giving life to a project or plan. Its “divine spark” remains the source's spiritual language, while the separate large-flooding clause is retained without an explanation that the entry does not provide. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 57)
