## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Saturn entry, PDF p. 56.

To feel a burden. Inhibition or loneliness. To take over a duty. Depression. Dejection. To separate. To feel deserted. Being ponderous. Personal sorrows. Mournful states or afflictions. Serious minded. Concentrated attitude. To be restrained. To slow down. Losing life goals. The moment (minute) of separation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Felt burden, restraint, duty, and separation.
- psychology: Loneliness, sorrow, seriousness, concentration, and dejection.
- body/health: “Depression” is historical source wording, not a clinical assessment.
- social/relationship: Taking over a duty, separating, and feeling deserted.
- events/manifestations: Slowing down, loss of life goals, and a moment of separation.
- conflicts/notes: Concentration and accepted duty remain visible alongside distress; no diagnosis or inevitable loss follows from the formula.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes concentrated seriousness and taking up duty as well as sorrow and inhibition. These emphases should not collapse into a clinical or uniformly helpless portrait.

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through duty, restraint, and a felt burden. Witte connects serious concentration and taking over responsibility with loneliness, sorrow, slowing down, or separation; the range includes a focused attitude as well as distress. Its word “depression” remains historical delineation language and does not establish a clinical condition or an inevitable loss of goals. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 56)
