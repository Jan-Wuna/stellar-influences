## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Sun entry, PDF p. 56.

Body and soul. The appearance of the “I”. The body’s capacity to react to the mind. The interaction between the body and the soul. One’s own living body. One’s life and goals of life. One moment (minute) at the day. Daily cycle.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The living body and its interaction with the mind or soul.
- psychology: Appearance of the “I” and one's goals of life.
- body/health: The source describes bodily response to the mind, without a particular illness or health outcome.
- social/relationship: No specific relationship is supplied.
- events/manifestations: One's life, a moment in the day, and the daily cycle.
- conflicts/notes: Body–mind language belongs to the entry's doctrine; the daily references do not establish a timing technique.

## Contradictions

One entry is represented. It links bodily presence, inner experience, and life goals without supplying a separate claim about vitality or achievement.

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Sun`, Sun specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through one's living body and the source's interaction of body and soul. Witte connects the appearance of the “I” and bodily response to the mind with one's life and life goals, placing inner experience in bodily presence. Its daily-cycle expressions remain part of the entry, without adding a promise of vitality, accomplishment, or a timing procedure. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 56)
