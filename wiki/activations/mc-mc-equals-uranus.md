## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Uranus entry, PDF p. 56.

To have a mind to do something. Personal tension. To be excited. Vibrations. One inclined to sudden decisions. An explosive manner. To show ambitions. Impatient. The moment (minute) of tenseness.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal tension, excitement, and readiness to decide or act.
- psychology: Ambition, impatience, and having a mind to do something.
- body/health: “Vibrations” is unspecified; no bodily condition is established.
- social/relationship: An explosive manner; no specific relationship is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Sudden decisions and a moment of tension.
- conflicts/notes: Intent and ambition are represented without assurance that action or accomplishment follows.

## Contradictions

One entry is represented. Its urge to do something and tendency toward sudden decisions concern readiness and manner, rather than a guaranteed result.

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through tension, excitement, and an impatient readiness to do something. Witte connects ambition and intention with sudden decisions or an explosive manner, describing how an impulse may be experienced and expressed. The unspecified “vibrations” adds no particular medical claim, and readiness does not establish that a goal is achieved. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 56)
