## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Venus entry, PDF p. 56.

One’s own personal affections. Love sentiments. One’s wishes and hopes. One’s own personal attraction. To have the power of attraction. To be attractive. To be fond of something. Peaceful minded. Feeling balanced. Sense of attraction. Sympathies.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal affection, attraction, wishes, and hopes.
- psychology: Fondness, peaceful-mindedness, and feeling balanced.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Love sentiments, sympathies, and one's personal attraction.
- events/manifestations: Being attracted or attractive; no particular event is specified.
- conflicts/notes: The entry includes inward fondness and outward attraction without guaranteeing reciprocal love or lasting harmony.

## Contradictions

One entry is represented. Attraction and peaceful feeling are related emphases here, but neither makes every relationship reciprocal or enduring.

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Venus`, Venus specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through affection, wishes, and attraction. Witte connects being fond of something and feeling sympathy with one's own attractiveness, while also naming peaceful-mindedness and balance. The range joins what a person feels drawn toward with how attraction is expressed, without establishing that another person reciprocates or that harmony persists. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 56)
