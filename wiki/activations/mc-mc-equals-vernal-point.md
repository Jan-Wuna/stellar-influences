## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Meridian,” Vernal Point entry, PDF p. 56.

The relationship, the emotional or the mental attitude, of the native toward the world in general. The “I” as embodied on the earth. Particularities of the time (moment) for several in one place.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: One's emotional or mental relationship to the world in general.
- psychology: Personal attitude toward the world and the source's embodied “I.”
- body/health: Embodiment is named, without a medical condition or outcome.
- social/relationship: Relation to the wider world and circumstances shared by several people in one place.
- events/manifestations: Particularities of a shared moment and place; no particular event is specified.
- conflicts/notes: Individual attitude and shared circumstances are both present; the entry supplies no timing procedure or universal collective outcome.

## Contradictions

One brief entry is represented. It places an individual attitude within a moment and place shared with others without making their inner responses identical.

## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/MC = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the repeated MC pair's personal and emotional orientation through one's relationship to the world in general. Witte joins the individual's emotional or mental attitude and embodied “I” with the particular circumstances of a moment shared by several people in one place. The connection places personal experience in a wider setting without claiming that everyone present has the same response. ([Witte](#witte-entry), “Meridian + Meridian,” PDF p. 56)
