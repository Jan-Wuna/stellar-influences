## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

One’ own creative abilities. The thirst for action. The urge to work. Passion for work. The urge to accomplish great feats. Leadership. The want to lead. Being goal-oriented. Consciously controlled energy. The urge to create something. To assert oneself successfully. The moment (minute) of creation or fathering.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Purposeful creation and work.
- **Psychology:** Passion for accomplishment and consciously controlled energy.
- **Body/health:** Witte's fathering reference is reproductive symbolism, not a fertility assessment.
- **Social/relationship:** The wish to lead and assert oneself.
- **Events/manifestations:** Work, creative accomplishment, and the named moment of creation or fathering.
- **Conflicts/notes:** One Witte entry; leadership is an expression, not an assured status.

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 57 (entry start).

## Contradictions

The entries do not establish a direct contradiction; the stated range is not proof of independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies `MC/MC` through purposeful creation: the urge to work and accomplish something is linked with leadership, controlled energy and successful self-assertion. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) also names the moment of creation or fathering; that historical reproductive wording is one particular expression of the creative theme.
