## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be educated. Profundity of thought. To go back to the original cause. Deep thinking.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Poseidon`; printed p. `99`; one-based PDF page `48` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Blockage of soul and spirit, restricted culture of the soul, manifestation of my spirit, my spiritual deepening, my thorough ideas (gründlichen Ideen), my deepening into culture, condensation of soul and spirit, stabilizing inner clarity, resistance of a spirit-soul.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Depth of understanding and spiritual concentration.
- **Psychology:** Deep thinking, with possible inner restriction.
- **Body/health:** The entries do not establish a bodily condition.
- **Social/relationship:** Education and cultural engagement are named; no specific relationship outcome is given.
- **Events/manifestations:** Study and inquiry into first causes.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC names thorough ideas and stabilizing inner clarity alongside restriction and deepening. Its condensation and spirit-soul language remains an attributed spiritual formulation, not a bodily mechanism.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 99 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 77 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Profundity and thoroughness coexist with inner blockage, while stabilizing clarity is a distinct possible expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies `MC/Poseidon` through depth of thought and inquiry into original causes. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) connects education with this search for fundamentals; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds spiritual deepening alongside restriction and blockage. Depth can therefore coexist with a felt narrowing of inner life, rather than implying effortless clarity.
