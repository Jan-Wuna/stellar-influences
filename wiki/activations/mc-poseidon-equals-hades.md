## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be living in the past. One’s mental baseness or stupidity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Old spirit soul, mysterious soul culture, criminal spirit soul, lack of psychic clarity, my mental deficiency, my abandonment of truth, my dangerous ideas, my spiritual past, and my criminal insights

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Past-bound thought and compromised clarity.
- **Psychology:** Preoccupation with the past or a felt lack of understanding.
- **Body/health:** The language about mental deficiency is not a clinical assessment.
- **Social/relationship:** ABC associates the formula with dangerous or criminal ideas; it does not establish conduct.
- **Events/manifestations:** Returning mentally to the past and, in ABC, relinquishing truth.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The sources' derogatory judgments must not become factual labels for a person.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 99 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 77 (entry start).

## Contradictions

The entries do not establish a direct contradiction; the stated range is not proof of independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies `MC/Poseidon` through past-bound thinking and difficulty attaining inner clarity. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) uses disparaging judgments about mentality; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) extends this to abandoning truth and dangerous ideas. Those historical moral and criminal labels remain attributed source language, not a finding about intelligence, character or conduct.
