## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Lucky mental disposition. To search for spiritual contentment. Longing for wisdom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joy of animated culture, happiness through psychic clarity, my success through truth, my spiritual happiness, my successful idea, my financial clarity, my joy at the reason, my right clarity, and true soul joy

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Wisdom, contentment and joy through clarity.
- **Psychology:** A fortunate disposition and longing for understanding.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Enjoyment of cultural life; no specific relationship outcome is stated.
- **Events/manifestations:** Successful ideas and financial clarity are ABC motifs.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's 'my right clarity' is awkward translation of 'Rechts-Klarheit'; its exact sense is not used in synthesis.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 98 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 77 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC 'my right clarity' does not securely convey German 'meine Rechts-Klarheit'; retained but excluded.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `MC/Poseidon` through the search for wisdom and spiritual contentment. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes a fortunate mental disposition, and the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) links joy with inner clarity, culture and successful ideas. Its financial-clarity motif broadens that range, but neither entry guarantees happiness or financial success.
