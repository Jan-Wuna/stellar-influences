## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Mental independence. To differ mentally from others. Intellectual activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Cultivated activity, spiritual impulses to the soul, creating psychic clarity, clear action, my actions for truth, my knowledge work, my spiritual activity, and the power of soul and spirit

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Active and independent understanding.
- **Psychology:** Willingness to think differently from others.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health condition is stated.
- **Social/relationship:** Intellectual difference from others is named, not hostility.
- **Events/manifestations:** Work toward understanding and clarity in action.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Different emphasis on independence and constructive effort is not a contradiction.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 98 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 77 (entry start).

## Contradictions

The entries do not establish a direct contradiction; the stated range is not proof of independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies `MC/Poseidon` through active, independent thought. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) explicitly allows thinking differently from others; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) links mental activity with efforts toward truth, clear action and the work of understanding. Independence here concerns the activity of thought, without implying inevitable conflict.
