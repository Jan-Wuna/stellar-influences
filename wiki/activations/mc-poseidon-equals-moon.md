## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Highly sensitive. Comprehensive mental perception. To influence women and the public. To possess emotional intelligence or perceptive faculty.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Poseidon`; printed p. `98`; one-based PDF page `48` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Ensouled hour of clarity, ensouled culture of a people, a woman's spirit-soul, clear feelings of the soul, culture of my people, my insights as a woman, my spiritualized wife, my reasonable wife, my true feelings.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sensitive and comprehensive perception.
- **Psychology:** Emotional intelligence and perceptiveness.
- **Body/health:** Sensitivity is not a diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Witte names influence on women and the public; the gender framing is attributed.
- **Events/manifestations:** Participation in a people's culture is an ABC motif.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's reasonable wife and true feelings are distinct from the damaged poor-wife and idol renderings. These gendered source examples remain attributed, and the hour phrase supplies no independent forecasting rule.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 98 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 77 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte's sensitivity and perception overlap ABC's clarity of feeling, with a reasonable or spiritualized wife and a woman's insight retained as specific historical examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies `MC/Poseidon` through sensitivity, broad mental perception and emotional intelligence in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). Its public-influence motif connects perception with response to others. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds cultural and emotional associations, including true feelings and a reasonable or spiritualized wife. Its gendered examples remain historical source language rather than universal relationship rules.
