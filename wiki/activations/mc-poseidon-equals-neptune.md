### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Poseidon`; printed p. `99`; one-based PDF page `48` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Disappointed spirit-soul, sensitive spirit-soul, my disappointing truth, my spiritual uncertainty, my cultural expectations, my unclear ideas, inner clarity through sensitivity, refinement of soul and spirit, future inner clarity.

- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's disappointment and uncertainty coexist with sensitivity and refinement; these attributed spiritual descriptions do not establish a clinical condition.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `MC/Poseidon` through mental delicacy, enthusiasm and a non-material inclination in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds sensitivity and refinement of soul and spirit alongside disappointment, unclear ideas and uncertainty. Its reference to future inner clarity expresses the source's expectation; it does not supply a timing rule. Sensitivity and lack of clarity can therefore coexist within this attributed account.
