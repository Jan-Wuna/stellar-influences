## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To form a mental partnership with other people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Poseidon`; printed p. `98`; one-based PDF page `48` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Ensouled spiritual connection, clear relationships of the soul, clarity of my feelings, my spiritual connections, my clarity in connections, my contact ideas, my cultural connections, my connection to truth, linking of soul and spirit.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Connection through ideas and understanding.
- **Psychology:** Clarity experienced within connections.
- **Body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Mental partnerships and cultural connections.
- **Events/manifestations:** Forming contacts and sharing ideas.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's Verknüpfung means linking, while Gefühle means feelings; neither establishes constriction or gestures.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 98 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 77 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC's Verknüpfung means linking, while Gefühle means feelings; neither establishes constriction or gestures.

## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies `MC/Poseidon` through mental partnership. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) explicitly joins one's outlook with other people, while the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds cultural connections, contact ideas and clarity within connections. The emphasis is on bonds formed through understanding and the linking of soul and spirit, with clarity also expressed through feelings.
