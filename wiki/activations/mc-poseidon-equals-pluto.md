## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A transformation in one’s thought processes. Ones personal mental and spiritual development. To change one’s own attitude to mental insights.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Poseidon`; printed p. `99`; one-based PDF page `48` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Unfolding of my ideas, development of soul and spirit, my development toward reason, my spiritual unfolding, my cultural development, my increasing spiritualization, developing soul culture, growing clarity of the soul.

- **Body/health:** No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Cultural development is named; no particular relationship outcome is given.
- **Events/manifestations:** Mental and spiritual development.

## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies `MC/Poseidon` through changes in thought and personal spiritual development. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) connects altered attitudes with mental insight; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds cultural development and growing inner clarity. The common thread is an evolving understanding rather than a guaranteed improvement or a claim of repeated crises.
