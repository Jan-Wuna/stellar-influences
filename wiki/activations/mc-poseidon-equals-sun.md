## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The mind of the native stipulates bodily form and appearance.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Poseidon`; printed p. `98`; one-based PDF page `48` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Body + soul + spirit (personality point according to Carl Perch), my cultural center, my spiritualized body, my personal idea, my personal truth, my body culture, personal inner clarity, soul and spirit of a person.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Inner life expressed in personal and bodily form.
- **Psychology:** Personal ideas and personal truth.
- **Body/health:** Witte's claim about mind determining appearance remains attributed; no health diagnosis follows.
- **Social/relationship:** A person's cultural expression is named; no specific bond is established.
- **Events/manifestations:** Bodily appearance and personal cultural expression.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC explicitly attributes the body + soul + spirit personality-point description to Carl Perch; this historical attribution and Witte's appearance claim do not establish a physical mechanism.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 98 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 77 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC explicitly attributes the body + soul + spirit personality-point description to Carl Perch; this historical attribution and Witte's appearance claim do not establish a physical mechanism.

## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies `MC/Poseidon` through a relationship between inner life and bodily expression. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) explicitly claims that mind shapes appearance; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) joins personal truth and culture with the body, soul and spirit of a person. This is an attributed symbolic account of embodiment, not an established physical mechanism.
