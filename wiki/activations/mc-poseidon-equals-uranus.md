## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To inform. Mental clarity. Sudden realization of the truth. Recognizing ones own path to the goal of live.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Poseidon`; printed p. `99`; one-based PDF page `48` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Excited spirit-soul, clear impulses of the soul, my exciting truth, my spiritual restlessness, my spiritual impulses, my cultural impulses, my new ideas, my sudden insights, soul and spirit in tension, surprising inner clarity.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sudden insight and recognition of direction.
- **Psychology:** Restlessness, inner tension and new ideas.
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition is established.
- **Social/relationship:** Informing others is explicitly named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Sudden realization and recognition of one's path.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's überraschende means surprising; it agrees with sudden insight rather than reversing it.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 99 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 77 (entry start).

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies `MC/Poseidon` through sudden realization and recognition of one's direction in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds new ideas, restlessness and inner tension. The emphasis is on clarity arriving abruptly amid mental activity: ABC explicitly names surprising inner clarity as well as tension of soul and spirit.
