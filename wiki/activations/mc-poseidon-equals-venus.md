## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Inclination or attitude toward freedom, religion or the beauty. Ones own disposition.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Poseidon`; printed p. `98`; one-based PDF page `48` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Ensouled art and culture, joy in inner clarity, spirit and soul in harmony, clarity through inner harmony, my joy in clarity, my idea of peace, my love of truth, my preference for reason, benevolent influence on the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Affinity for beauty, freedom and harmony.
- **Psychology:** Joy in clarity and harmony of inner life.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Benevolent influence and a peace idea are ABC motifs.
- **Events/manifestations:** Artistic and cultural engagement.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's Vorliebe für Vernunft is a preference for reason; its aesthetic and spiritual vocabulary remains attributed.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 98 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 77 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC's Vorliebe für Vernunft is a preference for reason; its aesthetic and spiritual vocabulary remains attributed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies `MC/Poseidon` through one's disposition toward freedom, religion and beauty in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) links enjoyment of clarity with inner harmony, art and love of truth. Together these support an affinity between understanding and aesthetic or harmonious experience, without establishing elevated taste as a fact about a person.
