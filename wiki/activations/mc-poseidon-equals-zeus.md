## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To enforce mentally. To mentally prove or compel something. To affirm (testify) the truth. To demonstrate something.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Poseidon`; printed p. `99`; one-based PDF page `48` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

My spiritual goal, my reasonable striving, my efforts toward inner clarity, my spiritual leadership, my cultural shaping, my creative ideas, my testimony to truth, achievements of soul and spirit, striving for clarity of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Purposeful demonstration and assertion of ideas.
- **Psychology:** Effort toward clarity and a defined mental goal.
- **Body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Testimony, demonstration and mental persuasion.
- **Events/manifestations:** Creative ideas and cultural work directed toward a goal.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's vernünftiges Streben means reasonable striving; testimony and mental compulsion do not prove the truth of an assertion.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 99 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 77 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC's vernünftiges Streben means reasonable striving; testimony and mental compulsion do not prove the truth of an assertion.

## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies `MC/Poseidon` through purposeful demonstration and assertion of an idea. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) names proof, testimony and mental compulsion; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds spiritual goals, creative ideas and effort toward clarity. The theme joins intellectual purpose with active expression, without proving that an asserted proposition is true.
