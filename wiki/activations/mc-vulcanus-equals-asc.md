## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To let other people feel one’s power and influence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Others influence me, violence of others against me, my influence at the place, my environmental influence, my energy for the environment, my violence against others, my strength with the partner, psychic power of the partner, psychic partner influence, and strong partner soul

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Power and influence in direct contact.
- **Psychology:** Awareness of one's strength and another's influence.
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition is established.
- **Social/relationship:** Reciprocal influence, partner strength and source examples of violence.
- **Events/manifestations:** Making one's power felt in the immediate environment.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte emphasizes outward influence; ABC adds inward influence without contradicting it.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 96 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 76 (entry start).

## Contradictions

The entries do not establish a direct contradiction; related wording is not proof of independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies `MC/Vulcanus` through power felt in direct contact with others. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes making others feel one's influence; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also includes others influencing oneself, a partner's strength and violence in either direction. The relationship is reciprocal in scope, and the violent examples are possibilities in the source rather than predictions.
