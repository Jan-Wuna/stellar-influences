## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Might and influence through family or community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Vulkanus`; printed p. `97`; one-based PDF page `47` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Shared psychic influence, my influence in the community, my artistic strength, my strong community, psychic energy of a community, inner influence of art, inner strength of a community, strong community of souls.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Influence through communal and artistic life.
- **Psychology:** A sense of strength associated with community.
- **Body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Family, community and shared influence.
- **Events/manifestations:** Artistic strength and exercising influence within a group.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC names the inner influence of art, not a specific artist. Communal strength and artistic influence remain distinct examples within the shared setting.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 97 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 76 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte's family or community influence is broadened by ABC's communal strength and influence of art.

## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies `MC/Vulcanus` through power connected with family or community. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) locates influence in these bonds, while the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also names one's influence within a community, artistic strength and the inner influence of art. The common theme is personal force participating in collective or artistic life; inherited loyalty is not established by these entries.
