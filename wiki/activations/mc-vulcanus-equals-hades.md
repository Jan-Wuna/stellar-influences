## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Encircled by danger. Ignoring danger. To deliberately commit oneself to danger. To take up the fight against something which is repulsive.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Vulkanus`; printed p. `97`; one-based PDF page `47` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Danger through psychic violence, power of a serving soul, lack of psychic energy, my hidden influence, my energy deficiency, my endangered power, my power in the past, crime involving psychic violence, strong emotional grief.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Power encountered under dangerous or difficult conditions.
- **Psychology:** Ignoring danger, confronting it, or experiencing grief.
- **Body/health:** ABC's depleted energy and grief do not establish illness.
- **Social/relationship:** Hidden influence and the source's psychological-violence examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Exposure to danger or deliberate confrontation.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's gefährdete Macht is endangered power. Danger faced by the person is distinct from danger caused by the person's power; criminality remains an attributed severe source example.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 97 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 76 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte distinguishes ignoring danger from deliberately entering it, while ABC also permits the person's own power to be endangered.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies `MC/Vulcanus` through danger and the use of strength in confronting something repulsive. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) distinguishes being surrounded by danger, ignoring it and deliberately entering it. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds hidden influence, endangered power, depleted energy and grief. These form a range of difficult encounters with power, without proving a dangerous or criminal character.
