## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Luck and success through the exercise of personal power. The optimist.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Vulkanus`; printed p. `96`; one-based PDF page `47` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Success through psychic energy, successful psychic influence, joy in psychic violence, joy of a strong soul, my successful influence, my strength through joy, my legal influence (Rechts-Einfluß), my financial power, psychic influence through money.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Optimism and success through personal influence.
- **Psychology:** Strength and joy associated with effective action.
- **Body/health:** No health condition is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Influence through money; ABC also preserves a troubling violence motif.
- **Events/manifestations:** Financial power, legal influence and successful influence.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's Rechts-Einfluß denotes legal influence in this row alongside financial power; it provides no right-wing political identification. The joy-in-violence example remains a separate severe claim.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 96 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 76 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Favorable influence and optimism coexist with ABC's troubling enjoyment-of-violence example; legal influence and financial power are distinct manifestations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `MC/Vulcanus` through optimism and success associated with exercising personal power. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) supplies that theme, and the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds strength through joy and influence through money. ABC also includes enjoyment of psychological violence, so success cannot be equated with constructive conduct. Its legal-influence example remains distinct from financial power and supplies no political affiliation.
