## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

An intense urge for action. Activity based on one’s own strong expectations (willpower).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Vulkanus`; printed p. `96`; one-based PDF page `47` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Efforts toward psychic energy, influence of my activity, influential work of the soul, actions of a strong soul, my work energy, my powerful activity, my willpower, activity involving psychic violence, strong impulses of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Intense action and willpower.
- **Psychology:** Strong expectations and the urge to act.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Influence exerted through one's activity.
- **Events/manifestations:** Forceful work and purposeful action.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's opening concerns efforts toward psychic energy rather than imitation. It retains a separate activity-involving-violence example alongside work, willpower and strong impulses.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 96 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 76 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Purposeful activity and powerful work coexist with ABC's separate psychic-violence activity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies `MC/Vulcanus` through an intense urge to act, with activity driven by one's strong expectations or willpower in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds work energy, powerful activity and the influence of one's actions. The violent-activity example remains part of ABC's range, while the entries do not make every concentrated drive unstoppable or aggressive.
