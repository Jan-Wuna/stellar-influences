## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Strong thinking activity. Great agility and activity of thoughts. Psychically impressed by mental power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Vulkanus`; printed p. `96`; one-based PDF page `47` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Influence of my thoughts, force of media language (Gewalt der Medien-Sprache), conversations about psychic energy, my movement energy, my energy in youth, my verbal power (Sprach-Gewalt), my strong thoughts, news about psychic violence, inner strength in youth.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Active thought and the influence of communication.
- **Psychology:** Mental agility and being impressed by intellectual power.
- **Body/health:** Movement energy is an ABC motif, not a health assessment.
- **Social/relationship:** Influence through speech and media.
- **Events/manifestations:** Reports about psychological violence and energetic communication.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Sprach-Gewalt concerns force or power of expression; it does not itself specify physical violence. ABC separately names news about psychic violence, so that severe motif remains distinct from verbal force.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 96 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 76 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte's agile thinking and mental power connect with ABC's force of expression, while reports of psychic violence remain a separate example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies `MC/Vulcanus` through vigorous, agile thinking and the impression made by mental power in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) extends influence into thoughts, speech, media and youthful energy. These connect mental activity with its effects on others, without requiring every strong expression to be violent.
