## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To strongly influence women and the public. To make a great stir. Executive powers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Energy of my woman, influence of my woman, power of my woman, my emotional influence, powerful people, my influence as a woman, my energetic woman, my power as a woman, my strong feelings, my strong hour, and strong soul of the people

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Public and emotional influence.
- **Psychology:** Strong feelings and forceful emotional expression.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health condition is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** The sources name women, a partner and the public in gendered examples.
- **Events/manifestations:** Making a public stir and exercising executive power.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The gendered range must not become a claim that gender determines influence.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 96 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 76 (entry start).

## Contradictions

The entries do not establish a direct contradiction; related wording is not proof of independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies `MC/Vulcanus` through public impact, emotional influence and executive power. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) names making a great stir; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds strong feelings and the influence or strength of a woman. The gendered examples remain attributed historical language rather than a universal claim about women or an assured public role.
