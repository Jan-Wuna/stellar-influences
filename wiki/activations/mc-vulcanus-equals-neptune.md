## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To miss completely. Dissolution of one’s own power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Vulkanus`; printed p. `97`; one-based PDF page `47` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

My intuitive influence, my weak influence, my strength through metaphysics, my strong uncertainty, my refined energy, weak psychic energy, weak inner influence, psychic energy of water, unknown inner influence.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Dissipation of power and subtle or weak influence.
- **Psychology:** Uncertainty and an intuitive mode of influence.
- **Body/health:** Low inner energy is source language, not a diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Weak or unknown influence on others.
- **Events/manifestations:** Missing an aim or losing effectiveness.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC explicitly retains strength through metaphysics alongside weak influence and strong uncertainty. These are different possibilities, and its water-energy terminology is a source claim rather than a medical mechanism.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 97 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 76 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte's complete miss and dissolution of power are more adverse than ABC's intuitive influence, refined energy and strength through metaphysics; ABC also retains weak influence and uncertainty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `MC/Vulcanus` through an aim missed and personal power dissolving in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds weak influence and uncertainty alongside intuitive influence, refined energy and strength through metaphysics. The range is therefore broader than total collapse: reduced effectiveness can coexist with subtle influence, without the entries explaining when either occurs.
