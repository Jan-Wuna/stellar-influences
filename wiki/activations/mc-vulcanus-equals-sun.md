## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To have great physical strength and vigor and to use it.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Vulkanus`; printed p. `96`; one-based PDF page `47` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Influence of body and soul, my personal influence, my masculine strength (Mannes-Kraft), my bodily strength, personal psychic energy, personal inner influence, a person's inner strength, center for psychic energy.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Physical vigor expressed as personal power.
- **Psychology:** An emphasis on one's experienced strength.
- **Body/health:** Witte names physical strength; this is not a measured health or fitness assessment.
- **Social/relationship:** Personal influence is named; no particular relationship result is supplied.
- **Events/manifestations:** Using physical strength and vigor.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's Mannes-Kraft is explicitly masculine source wording, while the remaining personal-strength clauses are not restricted by gender. The center for psychic energy is a source expression rather than an established medical mechanism.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 96 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 76 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte's physical vigor is joined by ABC's personal influence and inner energy; its center for psychic energy remains a source expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies `MC/Vulcanus` through physical strength, vigor and their use in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) joins bodily strength with personal inner energy and influence. This connects bodily capacity with expression of personal power, while ABC's masculine wording remains a historical example rather than a restriction by gender or proof of anyone's fitness.
