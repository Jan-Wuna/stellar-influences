## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Great passion in love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Vulkanus`; printed p. `96`; one-based PDF page `47` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Influence of a beautiful soul, peaceful influence of the soul, power of my beauty, my influence in love, my harmonious influence, my power in love, harmony through psychic energy, my artistic influence, strong harmony of souls.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Passionate affection and harmonious influence.
- **Psychology:** Intensity in love and a sense of inner harmony.
- **Body/health:** No health or sexual-function claim is established.
- **Social/relationship:** Passion in love and peaceful influence.
- **Events/manifestations:** Artistic influence and the power associated with beauty.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's schöne Seele is a beautiful soul; peaceful and harmonious influence are separate clauses. These examples do not establish an objective measure of beauty or a universal temperament.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 96 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 76 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte's great passion and ABC's peaceful or harmonious influence describe different possibilities rather than mutually exclusive outcomes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies `MC/Vulcanus` through great passion in love in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) expands the range to harmonious or peaceful influence, beauty and artistic influence. Intensity and harmony can thus be associated with the same formula; this does not establish an inevitable erotic behavior or exceptional attractiveness.
