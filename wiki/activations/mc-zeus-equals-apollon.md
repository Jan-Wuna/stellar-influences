## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be a scientific researcher. Inventor in trade or commerce. To force to peace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Zeus`; printed p. `89`; one-based PDF page `43` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Expansion of my goals, successes of a fiery soul, leading science of the soul, my efforts for freedom, my leadership experiences, my commercial achievements, my abundance of achievement, my increasing efforts.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Directed effort in research, commerce and expanded goals.
- **Psychology:** Increasing effort and a drive toward wider achievement.
- **Body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Witte's forcing-peace example retains a coercive dimension.
- **Events/manifestations:** Scientific research and commercial invention.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's leadership experiences and abundance of achievement are source expressions; its science-of-the-soul wording supplies no empirical validation.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 89 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC's leadership experiences and abundance of achievement are source expressions; its science-of-the-soul wording supplies no empirical validation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies `MC/Zeus` through research, invention in trade or commerce and, as a separate example, forcing peace in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds expanding goals, commercial achievement and increasing effort. The common theme is directed effort reaching into a wider field; negotiation is not supplied as the mechanism of peace.
