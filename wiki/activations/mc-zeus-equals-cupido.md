## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Need to work in community. Artistic creativity. To bring something new in art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Zeus`; printed p. `89`; one-based PDF page `43` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Fiery soul community, shared guidance of a soul, shared inner goals, I lead a community, inner fire of a community, my goal for the community, my artistic creations, my achievements for the community.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Communal work and artistic creation.
- **Psychology:** A drive to contribute creatively to shared goals.
- **Body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Community work and group leadership.
- **Events/manifestations:** Creating something new in art and achieving goals for a community.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's Kunst-Schöpfungen means artistic creations; communal leadership and artistic work remain distinct expressions.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 89 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC's Kunst-Schöpfungen means artistic creations; communal leadership and artistic work remain distinct expressions.

## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies `MC/Zeus` through creative work in community. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) connects that need with artistic creativity and bringing something new into art; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds communal goals, leadership and achievements for the group. The theme is contribution and direction within collective creation, without requiring a particular profession.
