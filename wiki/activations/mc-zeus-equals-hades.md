## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To wish to help the sick. Sorrow and hindrances at work. To be obliged to fight strong resistance. To work with coals, soot or dirt. To produce lowbrow art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous inner fire, I begin to serve, I form from the past, my dangerous goals, my strenuous past, my achievements from the past, my hidden efforts, and spiritual leadership service

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Work and service under difficult conditions.
- **Psychology:** Sorrow at work and persistence against resistance.
- **Body/health:** Helping sick people is a service motif, not a diagnosis of the native.
- **Social/relationship:** Service and encountering opposition.
- **Events/manifestations:** Work with coal, soot or dirt; Witte also names 'lowbrow art'.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's artistic value judgment remains attributed; no universal standard is inferred.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 89 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72 (entry start).

## Contradictions

The entries do not establish a direct contradiction; related wording is not proof of independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies `MC/Zeus` through work confronted by sorrow, obstacles and strong resistance. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) also preserves the wish to help the sick, work with coal or dirt, and its evaluative phrase 'lowbrow art'. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds service, past experience and hidden effort. Difficult labor and service coexist here; the entries do not establish that the person is ill.
