## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Creative thoughts. Wanting always to start something new. To make proposals.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reports with inner fire, fiery soul of youth, I design reports, my efforts in traffic, my youth goals, my achievements in youth, my media performances, my words are creative, and language of a fiery soul

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Creative thought expressed in proposals and communication.
- **Psychology:** Interest in new beginnings.
- **Body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Communicating through reports, words and media.
- **Events/manifestations:** Making proposals and producing reports or media work.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's traffic phrase is one specific rendering of 'Verkehr'; no broader travel claim is added.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 88 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72 (entry start).

## Contradictions

The entries do not establish a direct contradiction; related wording is not proof of independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies `MC/Zeus` through creative thoughts, proposals and the wish to start something new in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) extends this into reports, creative words, media activity and youthful goals. Initiative takes a communicative form here; the sources do not establish that ideas demand immediate action.
