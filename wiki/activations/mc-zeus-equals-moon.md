## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To own work in connection with women or the public. To be stimulated to work by women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Zeus`; printed p. `88`; one-based PDF page `43` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Efforts of my people, fiery soul of a woman, I lead as a woman, I generate feelings, my creative temperament, my fiery wife, my wife is goal-oriented, my young wife, my achievements in the hour, goals of my people.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Work stimulated through personal and public connections.
- **Psychology:** A creative temperament and determined activity.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** The sources' gendered examples connect women, partners and public activity.
- **Events/manifestations:** Work involving the public and shared goals.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC explicitly says generating feelings (ich erzeuge Gefühle); gendered wife/woman and hour expressions remain historical source language without a timing rule.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 88 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72 (entry start).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC explicitly says generating feelings (ich erzeuge Gefühle); gendered wife/woman and hour expressions remain historical source language without a timing rule.

## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies `MC/Zeus` through work connected with women or the public, and stimulation to work by women in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds a creative temperament and goals or efforts of a people. The gendered examples remain attributed to these entries; changing emotional states are not established as the cause of work.
