## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Weakness of will. Incompetence. Taking on too much. Abandoning plans.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Meridian + Zeus`; printed p. `89`; one-based PDF page `43` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Sensitivity of a fiery soul, I shape the formless, my goal is sensitivity, my efforts in water, my beginning refinement, my creative intuition, my achievements in the air, my weak efforts, creeping inner fire.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Dispersed effort with a subtler creative range.
- **Psychology:** Weakened resolve, overreach and sensitivity.
- **Body/health:** No health diagnosis is established.
- **Social/relationship:** The entries give no specific relationship outcome.
- **Events/manifestations:** Abandoning plans, shaping the formless, and ABC's water or air examples.
- **Conflicts/notes:** ABC's creative intuition and achievements in air are printed expressions; the water/air settings have no further explained mechanism, and Witte's incompetence is an attributed judgment.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 89 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72 (entry start).

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `MC/Zeus` through overextension, weakened will and abandoned plans in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds refinement and shaping what is formless alongside weak efforts. The range includes both difficulty carrying plans through and a subtler creative activity; Witte's judgment of incompetence does not become a fixed label for a person.
