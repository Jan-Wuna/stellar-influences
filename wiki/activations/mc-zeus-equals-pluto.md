## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To thrust oneself forward. Increased urge to accomplish something. To bring on a transformation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Beginning of my transformation, development of the inner fire, fiery soul development, I develop achievements, I grow through change, my goal is development, my creative development, and my growing efforts

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Increased effort toward accomplishment and change.
- **Psychology:** A stronger urge to advance and develop.
- **Body/health:** No bodily or health condition is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Self-advancement is named; no specific bond is established.
- **Events/manifestations:** Creative development and bringing about transformation.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries emphasize development without proving a particular result.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 89 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72 (entry start).

## Contradictions

The entries do not establish a direct contradiction; related wording is not proof of independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies `MC/Zeus` through an increased urge to accomplish something and bring about change. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) names thrusting oneself forward; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) connects growing effort with creative development and growth through change. Accomplishment and development reinforce the theme, without establishing crisis, coercion or a necessary transformation of every circumstance.
