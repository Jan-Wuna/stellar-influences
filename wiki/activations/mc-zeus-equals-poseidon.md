## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Zeus,” Poseidon entry, PDF p. 72.

Mental work or creations. To be a witness. To bear witness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Zeus,” Poseidon entry, printed p. 89 / PDF p. 43. Translation checked against the German passage.

Fiery spirit and soul, leadership of soul and spirit, I shape ideas, my spiritual fire, my clear striving toward a goal, my creative spirit, my striving for truth, my mental efforts, my cultural achievements, my rational goals.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Mental creation and bearing witness.
- **Psychology:** Clear goals, creative thought, and striving for truth.
- **Body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Testimony and the communication of ideas.
- **Events/manifestations:** Mental work and cultural achievement.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Inner leadership and spiritual fire are ABC's doctrinal language; bearing witness is Witte's distinct example.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 89 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72 (entry start).

## Contradictions

The entries share mental creation, while ABC emphasizes inner direction and truth-seeking and Witte separately names bearing witness. The checked German reads leadership of soul and spirit, striving for truth, and rational goals; it supplies no prosperity claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Zeus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair's personal creative effort and goal-directed activity through mental work and the shaping of ideas. Witte includes bearing witness, while ABC connects creative thought and cultural achievement with clear, rational goals and striving for truth. The shared direction is purposeful intellectual production; testimony remains a particular Witte example, and ABC's spiritual language describes inner direction without supplying a financial motive. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 89 / PDF p. 43)
