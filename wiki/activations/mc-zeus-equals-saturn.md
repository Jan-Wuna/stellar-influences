## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Zeus,” Saturn entry, PDF p. 72.

Activities combined with hindrances, sorrows, money losses. Losses from inventions. Thefts to patents (infringements).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Zeus,” Saturn entry, printed p. 89 / PDF p. 43. Translation checked against the German passage.

I create problems, I shape seriously, inner fire through perseverance, my goal is patience, my creativity in old age, my concentration on accomplishments, my procreative maturity, mature inner leadership, pain of a fiery soul.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Creative activity under constraint.
- **Psychology:** Seriousness, patience, perseverance, mature inner direction, and sorrow.
- **Body/health:** ABC's pain and reproductive-maturity wording is historical symbolism, not a health finding.
- **Social/relationship:** Patent infringement names a specific conflict over creative work.
- **Events/manifestations:** Money losses, invention losses and continued creative effort.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Continued effort and maturity coexist with loss and difficulty; the entries do not promise compensation or success after suffering.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 89 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes hindrances and losses, including patent infringement. ABC also gives serious shaping, endurance, patience and mature inner leadership. These are different emphases within constrained activity, without a promised sequence from loss to success.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Zeus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair's personal creative effort through constraint, serious shaping, and endurance. Witte's hindrances, sorrow and financial loss include losses from inventions and patent infringement; ABC places patience, concentrated accomplishment and creativity in old age beside problems and inner pain. Mature direction and continued effort therefore remain visible alongside loss, without making suffering a necessary stage before success. The reproductive-maturity and pain expressions remain historical source language. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 89 / PDF p. 43)
