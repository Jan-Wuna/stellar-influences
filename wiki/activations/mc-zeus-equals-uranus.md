## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Zeus,” Uranus entry, PDF p. 72.

Suddenly wanting to be creatively active. Sudden urge for great deeds. To force one’s will on someone. Personal creative ability for innovation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Zeus,” Uranus entry, printed p. 89 / PDF p. 43. Translation checked against the German passage.

Beginning of psychic tensions, excited fiery soul, I start suddenly, I create nervousness, my leadership impulses, my new goals, my reform efforts, my rhythmic achievements, and my technical efforts

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sudden initiative and innovation.
- **Psychology:** Excitement, tension and nervousness.
- **Body/health:** Nervousness is a source motif, not a diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Leadership impulses and possible imposition of will.
- **Events/manifestations:** New goals, reform efforts and technical work.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Creative innovation and coercive assertion are separate possibilities within the range.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 89 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72 (entry start).

## Contradictions

The entries share sudden creative initiative. Witte also gives imposing one's will, whereas ABC specifies leadership impulses, reform, rhythm and technical effort; these are related but distinct expressions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Zeus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair's personal creative effort through sudden initiative and innovation. Witte joins an abrupt urge toward great deeds with the possibility of forcing one's will on someone; ABC places tension and nervousness beside leadership impulses, new goals, and reform, rhythmic or technical effort. A sudden beginning may thus seek a directed change, while the coercive example remains one possibility rather than the necessary form of creative action. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 89 / PDF p. 43)
