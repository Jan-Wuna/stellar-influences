## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Zeus,” Venus entry, PDF p. 72.

Wishing to produce something beautiful. Producing with love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Zeus,” Venus entry, printed p. 88 / PDF p. 43. Translation checked against the German passage.

Harmony with a fiery soul, harmonious soul guidance, inner love fire, love efforts of the soul, my peace goal, my love efforts, my achievements in the arts, my procreation in love, my preference to lead.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Loving creation and the production of beauty.
- **Psychology:** Affection and harmonious intention in creative work.
- **Body/health:** ABC names procreation in love, without a fertility or health outcome.
- **Social/relationship:** Efforts in love and harmonious guidance.
- **Events/manifestations:** Artistic achievement and making something beautiful.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Producing beauty, artistic work and procreation are different source expressions of creation with love.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 88 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes producing beauty with love. ABC adds harmonious inner guidance, artistic achievement and the explicit procreation-in-love clause; this is not a claim about testimony or guaranteed fertility.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Zeus = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair's personal creative effort through affection and the wish to produce beauty. Witte's creating with love connects with ABC's artistic achievement, harmonious inner guidance and preference to lead; ABC also names procreation in love as a particular expression. Affection gives direction to creation across these settings, without promising reciprocal love, fertility, or lasting harmony. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 88 / PDF p. 43)
