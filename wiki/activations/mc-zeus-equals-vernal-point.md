## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Meridian + Zeus,” Vernal Point entry, PDF p. 72.

To contribute something through an urge to action.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Meridian + Zeus,” Vernal Point entry, printed p. 88 / PDF p. 43. Translation checked against the German passage.

I produce in public, I shape public life, I strive to enter public life, my start in public, my goals in public, my efforts in public, my creations in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Contribution and creative effort in public life.
- **Psychology:** An urge to contribute and goals directed toward public life.
- **Body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Entering and shaping public life.
- **Events/manifestations:** A public start, public efforts, and creations in public life.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Public orientation and contribution do not guarantee recognition, influence, or success.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 88 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte gives a brief urge toward contribution, while ABC names seven distinct public-life clauses. The checked German resolves the collapsed translation; its repeated public setting is one source's emphasis, not multiple corroborating claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `MC/Zeus = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the pair's personal creative effort through contribution in public life. Witte describes contributing from an urge to act; ABC places production, a beginning, goals, efforts and creations in the public sphere. The connection is taking purposeful activity into a wider setting, while striving to enter that setting remains distinct from attaining public recognition or success. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 72; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 88 / PDF p. 43)
