## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Admetos,” Cupido entry, PDF p. 171.

To feel alone, to be isolated. Gloomy thoughts in company or in the family. Faux pas or social blunders in a community. Concentration in a certain field in art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Admetos,” Cupido entry, printed p. 265 / PDF p. 131. Translation checked against the German passage.

Depressed youth community, restricted media community, strengthening of a young community, deepening conversations about art, a thorough discussion community, a news blackout for a community, a transport community in a traffic jam.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Isolation within groups and focused artistic attention.
- **Psychology:** Loneliness and gloomy thinking.
- **Body/health:** Depressed-group wording is attributed, without diagnosing a member.
- **Social/relationship:** Family or community awkwardness, alongside strengthening a young group.
- **Events/manifestations:** Specialized artistic concentration, deep art discussions, restricted news circulation, and a group caught in traffic.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Isolation within company and strengthening a discussion community are different possibilities, not an assurance of social exclusion.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 265 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte includes isolation within company and social blunders, but also artistic specialization. ABC adds strengthening a young group and thorough discussion alongside media restrictions and congestion. The entries retain constructive group attention as well as social difficulty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Admetos = Cupido`, Cupido specifies concentrated or restricted thought and communication through family, groups, and artistic activity. Witte describes feeling alone in company and making social blunders, alongside concentration in a particular field of art. ABC carries both sides into group communication: restricted media or news and a group in traffic coexist with strengthening a young community and deepening conversations about art. Focused communal work therefore remains visible beside isolation, without treating depressed-group language as a diagnosis of its members. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 265 / PDF p. 131)
