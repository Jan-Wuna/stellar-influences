## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Admetos,” Jupiter entry, PDF p. 171.

To concentrate one’s own thoughts successfully. Fortunate of happy concentration of thoughts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Admetos,” Jupiter entry, printed p. 264 / PDF p. 131. Translation checked against the German passage.

Restriction through youth law, successful consolidation of youth, successful earth movements, thorough financial discussions, thorough legal opinions, reports about financial restrictions, suppression of joy in youth.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Fruitful concentration amid topics of restriction.
- **Psychology:** Satisfaction or success in sustained thought.
- **Body/health:** The entries do not establish a health condition.
- **Social/relationship:** Youth law and legal opinions are ABC contexts, not legal advice.
- **Events/manifestations:** Reports about financial restrictions; ABC separately names earth movements.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Successful concentration or consolidation coexists with restrictions and suppressed joy; no financial gain is guaranteed.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 264 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte stresses successful or happy concentration. ABC includes successful consolidation and thorough financial/legal discussion alongside restrictions and suppressed joy. The subject of concentration and its felt or external outcome need not be the same.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Admetos = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies concentrated or restricted thought and communication through successful focus and sustained legal or financial consideration. Witte gives successful or happy concentration; ABC adds successful consolidation in youth and thorough discussions or opinions, while retaining financial restrictions and suppression of youthful joy. The capacity to concentrate can therefore be fruitful even when its subject concerns limits. ABC's separate successful-earth-movement clause supplies no general financial or physical-event forecast. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 264 / PDF p. 131)
