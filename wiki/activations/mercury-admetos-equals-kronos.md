## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Admetos,” Kronos entry, PDF p. 171.

Great ability to concentrate on something. To prepare for future successes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Admetos,” Kronos entry, printed p. 265 / PDF p. 131. Translation checked against the German passage.

Restriction of media independence, restriction of a young chief, thorough conversations with the chief, circulation of news within a hierarchy, news about thoroughness by the state, deepening important conversations.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Concentration and preparation in institutional settings.
- **Psychology:** A capacity for sustained focus.
- **Body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Restrictions involving a young boss, media independence and hierarchy.
- **Events/manifestations:** Preparation for future success, important conversations, and news circulating within hierarchy or concerning state thoroughness.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Concentration and preparation do not guarantee authority or success; institutional restrictions are separate examples.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 265 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte describes ability and preparation, whereas ABC supplies conversations with a chief and circulation within a hierarchy alongside restricted media independence. Those institutional settings do not require every instance of concentration to involve authority.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Admetos = Kronos`, Kronos specifies concentrated or restricted thought and communication through strong focus, preparation, and dealings within authority structures. Witte emphasizes the ability to concentrate and prepare for future successes; ABC places thorough or important conversations beside a chief, hierarchical news circulation and limits on media independence. These institutional examples give settings for sustained communication, while preparation remains distinct from attaining authority or success. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 265 / PDF p. 131)
