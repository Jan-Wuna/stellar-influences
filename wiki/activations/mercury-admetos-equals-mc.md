## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Admetos,” MC entry, PDF p. 171.

To think about a matter thoroughly. To be serious or sad, to grieve, to feel depressed by an unpleasant condition. Pessimist.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Admetos,” MC entry, printed p. 264 / PDF p. 131. Translation checked against the German passage.

Thorough description of the soul, I write thoroughly, my circulation of thought, my depression in youth, my restricted movements, my thoughts are blocked, my depth of thought, suppression of soulful thoughts.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Thorough reflection that may feel blocked or sorrowful.
- **Psychology:** Seriousness, grief and sustained thought.
- **Body/health:** The sources' depressive language remains attributed and non-diagnostic.
- **Social/relationship:** No specific relationship outcome is supplied.
- **Events/manifestations:** Thorough writing or description of inner life, and restricted movement.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The checked ABC opening describes the soul rather than criticizing it; depth and personal distress remain distinct emphases.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 264 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte includes thorough consideration and distress in an unpleasant condition. ABC adds thorough writing and description of the soul alongside blocked or suppressed thought. The source supports sustained inner description as well as heaviness, without a fixed pessimistic identity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Admetos = MC`, MC specifies concentrated or restricted thought and communication through one's personal inner experience. Witte combines thorough consideration with seriousness, grief and feeling depressed by an unpleasant condition; ABC adds thorough writing, description of the soul, depth of thought and blocked or suppressed thoughts. The range joins sustained self-description with felt mental heaviness, without making pessimism or depression a permanent identity or diagnosis. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 264 / PDF p. 131)
