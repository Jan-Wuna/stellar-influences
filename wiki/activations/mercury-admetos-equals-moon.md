## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Admetos,” Moon entry, PDF p. 171.

Persons with sedentary life. Drive, ride, journey. Circumstances of driving or riding. People on a vehicle. Travelling people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Admetos,” Moon entry, printed p. 264 / PDF p. 131. Translation checked against the German passage.

Reports about a depressed people, blocked thinking of a woman, depth of feeling in youth, media restriction within a people, speech restriction of a woman, deep thoughts of a woman, a people in traffic congestion, oppressed female youth.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sedentary or travelling circumstances with restricted expression.
- **Psychology:** ABC names depth of feeling in youth, deep thoughts, and blocked thinking in gendered examples.
- **Body/health:** Sedentary life is named, without a health diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Travelling people and source examples involving women or a people.
- **Events/manifestations:** Driving, riding and journeys; media restriction.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Emotional depth and oppression differ from depressed mood; historical gender and collective examples do not diagnose a person or population.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 264 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte gives sedentary life and travel, while ABC describes deep feeling, restricted expression and oppression in gendered or collective examples. Their coexistence does not make transport circumstances the cause of emotional or social restriction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Admetos = Moon`, Moon specifies concentrated or restricted thought and communication through people's feelings, expression, and travel circumstances. Witte names sedentary life, driving, riding and journeys; ABC adds emotional depth in youth alongside blocked thought, restricted speech or media, and oppressed female youth. These are parallel personal and collective settings, with historical gendered language retained and no claim that travel determines emotional life. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 264 / PDF p. 131)
