## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Admetos,” Node entry, PDF p. 171.

Being driven in a vehicle with others. Connected with people who drive or ride. Limited or suppressed freedom of thought.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Admetos,” Node entry, printed p. 264 / PDF p. 131. Translation checked against the German passage.

Blocked thinking in connections, depressing contacts with youth, restricted links in youth, fixed media connection, talk about depression in connections, and deepening the connection with youth

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Connections involving shared travel or restricted thought.
- **Psychology:** Blocked thinking and limited mental freedom.
- **Body/health:** Discussion of depression is attributed, without diagnosing anyone.
- **Social/relationship:** Contacts with drivers, riders or youth.
- **Events/manifestations:** Being driven with others and deepening a connection.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The entries do not establish that shared travel causes mental restriction.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 264 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte includes shared travel and limited freedom of thought. ABC adds fixed media links and deepening contact with youth alongside restricted connections. A firm link and mental restriction need not describe the same outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Admetos = Node`, Node specifies concentrated or restricted thought and communication through connections with others. Witte includes shared vehicle travel and contacts with drivers or riders, alongside limited freedom of thought; ABC places blocked thinking and restricted youthful links beside a fixed media connection and deeper contact with youth. The range includes both firm connection and constraint, without making shared travel the cause of mental restriction or depressive wording a diagnosis. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 264 / PDF p. 131)
