## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Admetos,” Pluto entry, PDF p. 171.

To develop one’s ability to concentrate. To change the mental attitude radically.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Admetos,” Pluto entry, printed p. 265 / PDF p. 131. Translation checked against the German passage.

Blocked development of youth, unfolding of a news blackout, deepening the development of thinking, development of resistance in youth, deepening thoughts about development, thorough development in youth, growing media successes.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Developing concentration and changing mental attitude.
- **Psychology:** Radical shifts in outlook and deeper attention to development.
- **Body/health:** No health condition is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Youthful resistance and blocked youth development are ABC contexts.
- **Events/manifestations:** Development of news restrictions or growing media success.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Opposed outcomes are listed possibilities; the text does not specify which occurs.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 265 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Developing concentration and a radically changed attitude in Witte coexist with ABC's blocked development, growing resistance and growing media successes. Development describes change here without guaranteeing that restrictions dissolve.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Admetos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies concentrated or restricted thought and communication through development and a changed mental attitude. Witte connects developing the ability to concentrate with radical change of outlook; ABC extends development into thinking, youthful resistance and news restrictions, while also naming thorough youthful development and growing media success. Both obstruction and increased effectiveness remain within the range, without requiring pressure as their cause or a favorable ending. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 265 / PDF p. 131)
