## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Admetos,” Poseidon entry, PDF p. 171.

Concentrating on mental or spiritual things, or ideas.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Admetos,” Poseidon entry, printed p. 265 / PDF p. 131. Translation checked against the German passage.

Blocked influence of an idea, restriction of clear thoughts, consolidation of youth through insight, intellectual or spiritual deepening of youth, thorough insights of youth, reports about cultural deepening, deep intellectual or spiritual conversations, suppressed reason in traffic.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Concentration on mental and spiritual matters.
- **Psychology:** Insight, intellectual or spiritual deepening, and restrictions on clear thought.
- **Body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Cultural communication and insight associated with youth.
- **Events/manifestations:** Deep conversations and reports about cultural deepening; blocked influence of an idea.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Suppressed reason in traffic is an explicit, unexplained source example; depth does not establish privileged access to truth.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 265 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte names mental or spiritual concentration; ABC retains both thorough insight and restrictions on clear thought or an idea's influence. Focus and its expression need not have the same outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Admetos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies concentrated or restricted thought and communication through mental or spiritual ideas. Witte emphasizes concentration on that material; ABC adds thorough insight, deep conversations and cultural deepening alongside restricted clarity and the blocked influence of an idea. Focused understanding and difficulty giving it expression remain distinct possibilities, without promising purification or privileged truth. ABC's separate suppressed-reason-in-traffic example is retained without an added explanation. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 265 / PDF p. 131)
