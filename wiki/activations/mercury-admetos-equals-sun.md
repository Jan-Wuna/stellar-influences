## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Admetos,” Sun entry, PDF p. 171.

Little exercise. To end a motion. The body at a standstill. No bodily perseverance. To be driven in a vehicle. The office-staff.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Admetos,” Sun entry, printed p. 264 / PDF p. 131. Translation checked against the German passage.

Blocked young man, restriction of bodily mobility, blocked news centre, thorough description of a person, personal depression in youth, deepening personal conversation, traffic congestion during the day.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Bodily stillness and restricted mobility.
- **Psychology:** ABC names depression in youth, without establishing a general temperament.
- **Body/health:** Little exercise and lack of bodily perseverance are Witte's claims, not measured fitness findings.
- **Social/relationship:** Office staff and a gendered young-man example are named.
- **Events/manifestations:** Ending a motion, being driven, a blocked news centre, thorough personal description, and daytime traffic congestion.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Personal description and deep conversation remain alongside bodily restriction; no actual endurance, routine, or gender restriction is established.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 264 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes minimal movement and seated work or travel. ABC also names thorough personal description and deeper personal conversation, alongside mobility restrictions and congestion. These are different personal settings rather than one compulsory daily routine.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Admetos = Sun`, Sun specifies concentrated or restricted thought and communication through the person's bodily circumstances and personal description. Witte gives stillness, little exercise, being driven and office staff; ABC adds restricted mobility and daytime congestion, while also naming thorough description and deepening personal conversation. Bodily stillness and sustained attention thus remain distinct possibilities. The historical young-man and depressive expressions establish neither a gender restriction nor an individual's measured health or endurance. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 264 / PDF p. 131)
