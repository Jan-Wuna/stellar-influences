## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Admetos,” Vernal Point entry, PDF p. 171.

Serious thinking of the general public. The mood is generally depressed.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Admetos,” Vernal Point entry, printed p. 264 / PDF p. 131. Translation checked against the German passage.

Depressive thoughts in public, earth movements in public, thorough news for the public, a public news blackout, suppressed opinion in public, a public traffic blockade.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Serious or restricted public thought.
- **Psychology:** A depressed collective mood, as source language.
- **Body/health:** Collective depressed-mood wording is not a population health finding.
- **Social/relationship:** Suppressed opinion in public.
- **Events/manifestations:** Thorough public news, a public news blackout, traffic blockade, and earth movements.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Public thoroughness and restriction are different possibilities; mood wording does not establish a population's mental health.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 264 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes serious public thinking and depressed mood. ABC also includes thorough public news beside suppressed opinion, news blackout and traffic blockade. These clauses concern distinct forms of public attention or restriction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Admetos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies concentrated or restricted thought and communication in public life. Witte names serious public thinking and generally depressed mood; ABC adds thorough public news alongside suppressed opinion, a news blackout and traffic blockade. Sustained public attention and public restriction both remain visible. The mood language is historical attribution, and the separately named earth movements supply no population-health finding or physical-event forecast. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 171; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 264 / PDF p. 131)
