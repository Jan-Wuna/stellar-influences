## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Apollon,” Admetos entry, PDF p. 170.

Scientific thoughts for a few. Not being understood or unable to make oneself understood. Not understanding a foreign language.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Apollon,” Admetos entry, printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130. Translation checked against the German passage.

Continuing trade discussions, spread of depressive thoughts, blocked media freedom, successful deepening of thought, consolidation of scientific thoughts, suppressed freedom of youth, increasing restrictions in traffic.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Specialized thought with limited reception or understanding.
- **Psychology:** Concentrated deepening of thought.
- **Body/health:** ABC's depressive-thought language is attributed, not diagnostic.
- **Social/relationship:** Difficulty making oneself understood and restricted media freedom.
- **Events/manifestations:** Scientific thinking for a few, continuing trade discussion, and foreign-language incomprehension.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Successful deepening and consolidation can coexist with limited access or understanding; they do not establish broad acceptance.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 263 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes limited understanding or reception. ABC includes successful thought-deepening and scientific consolidation beside blocked media freedom and suppressed youthful freedom. Depth and access are distinct outcomes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Apollon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies scientific thought and extensive communication through limited access, reception and understanding. Witte names scientific thoughts for a few, inability to make oneself understood and foreign-language incomprehension; ABC retains successful thought-deepening, scientific consolidation and continuing trade discussions beside restrictions on media, youthful freedom and traffic. Sustained thought may deepen without broad access or comprehension. Difficulty understanding remains distinct from the other orientation's refusal of ideas. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130)
