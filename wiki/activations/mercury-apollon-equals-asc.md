## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Apollon,” Asc entry, PDF p. 170.

Many talks with others. Acquaintanceship with scientists.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Apollon,” Asc entry, printed p. 262 / PDF p. 130. Translation checked against the German passage.

Extensive discussions with the partner, success reports about a place, successful youth in the surroundings, reports about experiences in the surroundings, young people's knowledge of the surroundings, many opinions about the surroundings, increasing local traffic.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Extensive conversation and scientific acquaintance.
- **Psychology:** Varied views, experiences and knowledge concerning the surroundings.
- **Body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **Social/relationship:** Many talks with others, including a partner or scientists.
- **Events/manifestations:** Reports about a place and increased local activity.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The specific scientist example does not establish broad expertise or a profession.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 262 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte's many conversations and scientific acquaintances meet ABC's extensive partner discussions and local reports. The named scientist is a particular acquaintance, not a claim that every participant holds expertise.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Apollon = Asc`, Asc specifies scientific thought and extensive communication through other people and the immediate surroundings. Witte gives many conversations and acquaintance with scientists; ABC adds extensive partner discussion, local success reports, experiences and varied opinions, along with increasing local traffic. The relation is active exchange in a particular social setting, without expanding acquaintance with scientists into universal expertise or a required profession. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 262 / PDF p. 130)
