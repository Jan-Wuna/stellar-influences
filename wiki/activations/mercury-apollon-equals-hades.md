## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Apollon,” Hades entry, PDF p. 170.

To speak with many about the past. Many feel oppressed who do not dare to express their opinion, who hide their feelings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Apollon,” Hades entry, printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130. Translation checked against the German passage.

Successful confidential conversations, successful conversations about the past, dangers of freedom for youth, endangered media freedom, conversations about lack of success, adverse experiences of youth, a call for help from trade, dissemination of old languages.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Communication concerning the past or concealed experience.
- **Psychology:** Feeling oppressed and hiding feelings or opinions.
- **Body/health:** No health diagnosis is established.
- **Social/relationship:** Fear of speaking openly and confidential discussion.
- **Events/manifestations:** Successful past/confidential discussions, a call for help from trade, and circulating old languages.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Endangered media freedom differs from freedom itself being dangerous. ABC separately states dangers of freedom for youth, without an explanation of that clause.

Evidence: [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 263 (entry); [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170 (entry start).

## Contradictions

Witte describes suppressed opinion and hidden feelings. ABC adds successful confidential or historical conversation as well as endangered media freedom and adverse youthful experiences. Its separate dangers-of-freedom clause remains source wording, without a general argument against freedom.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Apollon = Hades`, Hades specifies scientific thought and extensive communication through past, concealed, or difficult material. Witte connects talking with many about the past with people who hide feelings or fear stating an opinion; ABC retains successful confidential and historical discussions, old-language dissemination, adverse youthful experiences and endangered media freedom. Exchange can succeed even around difficult subjects, while the trade call for help and dangers-of-freedom clause remain particular source examples without a broader causal explanation. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130)
