## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Apollon,” Mars entry, PDF p. 170.

Keen debates with many. The astrological or scientific work or activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Apollon,” Mars entry, printed p. 262 / PDF p. 130. Translation checked against the German passage.

Extension of news activity, experienced media work, successful work meetings, successful work in youth, successful action through reflection, talk about scientific activity, and increasing thought work

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Active debate and scientific or astrological work.
- psychology: Reflection contributing to effective action and increasing mental work.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Keen debate with many and successful work meetings.
- events/manifestations: Expanded news activity, experienced media work, and scientific activity.
- conflicts/notes: Keen debate does not establish hostility; the named successes do not guarantee every project succeeds.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes debate and scientific or astrological activity, while ABC gives media work, reflection and work meetings. They describe different active uses of thought without requiring conflict.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Apollon = Mars`, Mars specifies scientific thought and extensive communication through active work and debate. Witte names keen debate with many and scientific or astrological activity; ABC adds media experience, expanded news work, successful meetings and action through reflection. The shared relation is thought put to work in exchange and practical activity, without treating sharp debate as hostility or assuming that discussion must always lead to a completed project. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 262 / PDF p. 130)
