## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Apollon,” MC entry, PDF p. 170.

Let one’s thoughts wander far. Much thinking. To be capable of scientific thinking. To be capable as an astrologer, technician or mathematician in the profession.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Apollon,” MC entry, printed p. 262 / PDF p. 130. Translation checked against the German passage.

Expansion of my movement, soulful experiences in youth, I write much, my successful youth, my thoughts of freedom, my abundance of thoughts, my conversational successes, extensive description of the soul, science of the soul of youth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal range of thought, writing, and scientific capacity.
- psychology: Abundant thought, freedom of thought, and meaningful youthful experience.
- body/health: Expansion of movement is named without a medical condition or outcome.
- social/relationship: Conversational success and the source's professional examples.
- events/manifestations: Much writing, extensive inner description, and scientific, technical, mathematical or astrological work.
- conflicts/notes: Capacity and examples of professions do not certify expertise or require a particular career; soul terminology remains source doctrine.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses far-ranging thought and professional capability. ABC adds abundant thought, writing, freedom and inner description, without making all those capacities one compulsory occupation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Apollon = MC`, MC specifies scientific thought and extensive communication through one's own mental range and inner experience. Witte gives much and far-reaching thought, with scientific, technical, mathematical and astrological capability as professional examples. ABC connects abundant thought and writing with freedom, conversational success and extensive description of the soul. These expressions join personal intellectual activity with inner description, while the listed capacities do not establish credentials or a required career. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 262 / PDF p. 130)
