## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Apollon,” Pluto entry, PDF p. 170.

Many converse about an alteration and change their views. Speaking with many about a change.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Apollon,” Pluto entry, printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130. Translation and paragraph boundary checked against the German passage.

Reports about the development of science, successful development of youth, news about developmental successes, growing freedom of youth, growth through trade discussions, developing scientific thinking.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Developing scientific thought and changing views through discussion.
- psychology: Changing one's outlook and growing youthful freedom.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Many discussing change and growth through trade conversations.
- events/manifestations: Reports of scientific development, youthful development and developmental successes.
- conflicts/notes: The complete development row belongs to Pluto, beginning with scientific-development reports; change is not a promise of universal agreement or progress.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes discussion of change and altered views, while ABC gives scientific, youthful and commercial development. These settings do not require a conversation to transform public opinion as a whole.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Apollon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies scientific thought and extensive communication through development and changed views. Witte describes many discussing an alteration and changing their outlook; ABC places scientific development, youthful development and freedom alongside growth through trade discussions. Communication concerns change at both intellectual and practical levels, without requiring mass persuasion or making every development beneficial. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130)
