## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Apollon,” Poseidon entry, PDF p. 170.

To debate ideas or problems with others who think and feel similar as oneself.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Apollon,” Poseidon entry, printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130. Translation checked against the German passage.

Success through spiritualized thinking, successful youth culture, free media culture, thoughts about the science of the mind or spirit, conversations about cultural science, conversational success through clarity, clear words of science, scientific thoughts about truth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Debate among people of similar outlook and clarity in intellectual exchange.
- psychology: Spiritualized thought and reflection on mind, spirit or truth.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Debate with like-minded people and free media culture.
- events/manifestations: Cultural discussion, youthful cultural success, and conversational success through clarity.
- conflicts/notes: Similarity of outlook and clear speech do not verify the truth of an idea; spiritual terms remain source doctrine.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes a shared outlook, while ABC adds clarity, cultural discussion and thought about truth. Similarity and clarity are conditions of exchange here, not proof that a conclusion is correct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Apollon = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies scientific thought and extensive communication through shared outlook and clarity. Witte gives debate with people who think and feel similarly; ABC adds cultural conversation, spiritualized thought and success through clear expression. The connection supports exchange around ideas and problems within a receptive intellectual setting, while clear or shared thinking does not establish that its claims about truth are correct. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130)
