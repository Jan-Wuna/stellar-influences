## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Apollon,” Saturn entry, PDF p. 170.

To separate from studying. To part from many. Serious scientific plans or ideas.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Apollon,” Saturn entry, printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130. Translation checked against the German passage.

Hindrance of trade discussions, reports about increasing losses, disturbed freedom in youth, discussing problems of science, reflecting on the tasks of trade, thinking much about separations, scientific tasks for youth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Serious scientific thought alongside separation or restricted exchange.
- psychology: Reflection on scientific problems, commercial tasks, and separation.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied; hindrance of talks is not disability.
- social/relationship: Parting from many, disturbed youthful freedom, and obstructed trade discussions.
- events/manifestations: Separation from study, loss reports, and scientific tasks for youth.
- conflicts/notes: Serious plans remain alongside withdrawal; the entry does not guarantee discipline or a completed scientific task.

## Contradictions

Witte includes separation from study as well as serious scientific plans. ABC also gives scientific problems and tasks alongside hindered trade discussion. Restriction and sustained intellectual work remain different possibilities.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Apollon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies scientific thought and extensive communication through seriousness, separation and limits on exchange. Witte names parting from many or from study beside serious scientific plans; ABC adds hindered trade discussions, loss reports and disturbed youthful freedom, while retaining reflection on scientific or commercial tasks. The range includes both withdrawal and sustained attention to difficult work, without making restriction a guarantee of disciplined achievement. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130)
