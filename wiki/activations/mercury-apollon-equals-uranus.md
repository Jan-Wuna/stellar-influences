## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Apollon,” Uranus entry, PDF p. 170.

Surprising news for many. Many speak excitedly. New or revolutionary scientific thinking.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Apollon,” Uranus entry, printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130. Translation checked against the German passage.

disturbing reports of science, dynamic media successes, successful events in youth, successful youth reform, experience impulses in conversation, exciting reports of science, and technical reports of experience

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Surprising news, excited exchange, and new scientific thinking.
- psychology: Excitement, disturbance, and impulses experienced in conversation.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Many speaking excitedly and reports reaching a wider audience.
- events/manifestations: Dynamic media success, youthful reform, and scientific/technical reports.
- conflicts/notes: Exciting or disturbing news and successful reform differ in effect; novelty is not proof of scientific validity.

## Contradictions

Witte gives surprising news and revolutionary thought; ABC includes disturbing or exciting reports as well as successful reform. New information need not be either uniformly welcome or disruptive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Apollon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies scientific thought and extensive communication through surprising information and excited exchange. Witte includes new or revolutionary scientific thinking and many speaking excitedly; ABC adds dynamic media success, youthful reform, and scientific or technical reports that may excite or disturb. The connection is exchange stimulated by novelty or change, without making every report disruptive or every new idea scientifically valid. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130)
