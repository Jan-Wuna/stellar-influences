## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Apollon,” Vernal Point entry, PDF p. 170.

Thinking of the masses. Spreading of an idea generally. Scientific thoughts in general.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Apollon,” Vernal Point entry, printed p. 262 / PDF p. 130. Translation checked against the German passage.

Expansion of youth in public, media successes in public life, trade news for the public, scientific opinions in public, much youth in public life, increasing public traffic.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Scientific thought and ideas in general public circulation.
- psychology: Thinking of a wider public; no distinct private mental state is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Youth, media and scientific opinion in public life.
- events/manifestations: General spread of ideas, trade news, media success and increasing public traffic.
- conflicts/notes: Public circulation does not guarantee acceptance, truth or personal recognition.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes general dissemination, while ABC gives public media, science, trade and youth settings. Their range does not specify a single audience or level of influence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Apollon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies scientific thought and extensive communication through public circulation. Witte gives thinking of the masses and the general spread of ideas; ABC names public media success, trade news, scientific opinion and youthful presence, alongside increasing public traffic. The connection places information and exchange in a wider public setting, without making circulation equivalent to acceptance, truth or personal fame. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 262 / PDF p. 130)
