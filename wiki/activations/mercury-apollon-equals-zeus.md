## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Mercury + Apollon,” Zeus entry, PDF p. 170.

To direct the discourses of many. To lead scientific debates.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Merkur + Apollon,” Zeus entry, printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130. Translation checked against the German passage.

Reports about trade goals, success through creative thinking, scientific achievement reports, free speech of the media, leading scientific opinion, conversations about successful efforts, successful achievements of youth, discussing scientific goals.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directing discussion and leading scientific debate.
- psychology: Creative thinking and attention to goals or successful effort.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Guiding many speakers, leading scientific opinion, and free media speech.
- events/manifestations: Trade-goal reports, scientific achievement reports, and youthful achievements.
- conflicts/notes: Leading a discussion does not establish command over its participants or correctness of the leading opinion.

## Contradictions

Witte describes leadership of discourse; ABC adds creativity, media freedom and scientific or commercial goals. These settings need not involve coercion or unanimity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Apollon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies scientific thought and extensive communication through direction and purposeful discussion. Witte gives leading scientific debate and directing the discourse of many; ABC links creative thinking and successful effort with media speech, scientific goals and trade-goal reports. The connection is organizing exchange around aims and achievements, without turning leadership into compulsory agreement or assuming that the leading opinion is correct. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 170; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 263 / PDF p. 130)
