## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: “Planets activating the Mercury/Ascendant midpoint,” Chiron entry, PDF p. 53 (complete entry; chapter starts on p. 52).

One’s way of saying things has a healing effect. One helps others to overcome identifying with negative ideas, and to identify more with the thoughts that will most grow one.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Speech that helps others reconsider identification with negative ideas.
- psychology: Moving toward thoughts the source associates with growth.
- body/health: “Healing” is Sandbach's interpretive language; no medical treatment or outcome is established.
- social/relationship: Helping others through one's way of saying things.
- events/manifestations: A helpful exchange or change in the ideas with which someone identifies; no specific event is supplied.
- conflicts/notes: Growth concerns identification with ideas here; the entry does not supply a therapeutic method or guaranteed benefit.

## Contradictions

One brief entry is represented. It links the manner of speaking with changing identification, without adding a separate claim about diagnosis, treatment or universal effectiveness.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Asc = Chiron`, Chiron specifies communication with others through the effect Sandbach attributes to one's way of saying things. The source connects helpful speech with loosening identification with negative ideas and moving toward thoughts associated with growth. The emphasis is a change in how ideas are taken into the sense of self, while “healing” remains the author's interpretation rather than a demonstrated treatment effect or a method supplied by this entry. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 53)
