## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source: “Ascendant + Mercury,” Cupido entry, PDF p. 83.

Intentions of marriage. Domestic themes of conversation or conversations at home. Talks about marriage, social conditions or associations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: “Aszendent + Merkur,” Cupido entry, printed p. 107 / PDF p. 52. Translation checked against the German passage.

Mobile community at the place, others' community conversations, intellectual partnership community, young partners in the community, media art at the place, discussing shared matters with others, a community's conversations about the surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Conversation in domestic, marital and communal settings.
- psychology: Attention to shared matters and intellectual partnership.
- body/health: No bodily or medical effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: Marriage intentions, associations, and young partners within a community.
- events/manifestations: Talks at home, local media art, and community discussion of surroundings.
- conflicts/notes: Discussing marriage or belonging to a group does not establish that a union forms or the group agrees.

## Contradictions

Witte gives home and marriage themes, while ABC adds local community mobility, art and shared discussion. Domestic and wider communal exchange remain distinct settings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Asc = Cupido`, Cupido specifies communication with others and the immediate surroundings through home, marriage and community. Witte names domestic conversation, marriage intentions and association themes; ABC adds intellectual partnership, local media art and discussing shared matters or the surroundings with a group. The relation places exchange within shared social settings, without treating talk of marriage as a completed union or communal discussion as agreement. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 83; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 107 / PDF p. 52)
