## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT + MERCURY`
- Source locator: PDF p. `83` (oriented entry; scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

The opinion of others about the native. To receive news and information. To converse with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Aszendent + Merkur`
- Source locator: printed p. `106`, PDF p. `52` (entry).
- Text basis: translation checked against the raw scan on PDF p. `52`; supplied parser-normalized OCR was not itself visually verified.

#### ABC Entry

Soulful thinking of the partner, soulful local youth, I think about the environment, I write for others, my conversation partner, my partner in youth, my words for the partner, the partner's soul-language, and emotional movement in the environment.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. `171`, PDF p. `173`, entry `0565` (scan checked).

#### Ebertin Entry

An individual attitude toward others, one's criticism of other people. The act of speaking or negotiating with others, revealing one's opinion.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. `53` (complete Midheaven entry; chapter starts on p. `52`; scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

One has the ability to see the spiritual implications of all ideas, and to put them together in such a way that their spiritual basis is revealed and enhanced.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source locator: printed p. `180`, PDF p. `181` (MC entry; scan checked).

#### Munkasey Entry

Realizing the role and contributions of others in your life, especially as you mature; coping with the difficulties you find, and placing these into a better perspective on life, its turns, and meanings; education as a step to fame.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Conversation and other people's opinions bring one's own position into view ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry)).
- psychology: Personal judgments, perspective on life's difficulties, and spiritual interpretation of ideas are separate emphases.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is stated in the legible entries.
- social/relationship: Speaking, negotiating, receiving information, and recognizing others' contributions are described.
- events/manifestations: News received, opinions revealed, and education as a step toward fame are named ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 180).
- conflicts/notes: The ABC opening was repaired from the legible scan, including its soul-language vocabulary; it is a source-specific emphasis.

## Contradictions

Sandbach's spiritual integration, ABC's soul-language, and the other entries' interpersonal judgments differ in emphasis; no direct contradiction is established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Asc = MC`, MC specifies how exchanges with others are personally experienced: receiving their opinions and information accompanies stating one's own attitude through conversation or negotiation. Recognizing what others contribute can also place one's difficulties in perspective ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 180). [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) separately emphasizes revealing the spiritual basis of ideas, while [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 106) describes personal and soulful conversation; these do not establish that every conversation has a spiritual result.
