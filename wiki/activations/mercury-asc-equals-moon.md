## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Talks about female persons. Vehicles and transport facilities in the neighborhood.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

The woman as a language partner, language of the local people, language with other women, young women in the environment, partnership thinking of the woman, language of another people, environmental ideas of a woman, and people's movement in the place

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

The search for inner contact with others, judgements based on one's personal feelings. Conversations or negotiations with or about women.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To have strong feelings about the conflict between new and old ideas. A need for intimacy expressed through communicating with others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Sensitivity and awareness when judging the reactions of others and their responses to your ideas or thoughts; sensitive or moody reactions when stimulated by certain people; irregular communication attempts or patterns.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Asc = Moon`: responsive speech, feeling-based judgments, intimate or domestic discussion, and a strong need to be understood by one's environment. Main caution: mood-driven thinking: sensitivity, inconsistency, and personal reactions can override objectivity in communication.
