## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT + MERCURY`
- Source locator: PDF p. `83` (oriented entry; scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Talks about female persons. Vehicles and transport facilities in the neighborhood.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Aszendent + Merkur`
- Source locator: printed p. `106`, PDF p. `52` (entry).
- Text basis: translation checked against the raw scan on PDF p. `52`; supplied parser-normalized OCR was not itself visually verified.

#### ABC Entry

The woman as a conversation partner, conversations of the local people, conversations with other women, young women in the surroundings, the woman's thoughts about partnership, the language of another people, a woman's thoughts about her surroundings, and a people's movement in the locality.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. `170`, PDF p. `172`, entry `0556` (scan checked).

#### Ebertin Entry

The search for inner contact with others, judgements based on one's personal feelings. Conversations or negotiations with or about women.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF pp. `52–53` (Moon entry spans the page break; scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

To have strong feelings about the conflict between new and old ideas. A need for intimacy expressed through communicating with others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source locator: printed p. `180`, PDF p. `181` (Moon entry; scan checked).

#### Munkasey Entry

Sensitivity and awareness when judging the reactions of others and their responses to your ideas or thoughts; sensitive or moody reactions when stimulated by certain people; irregular communication attempts or patterns.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Interpersonal communication carries feelings, sensitivity, and a wish for inner contact ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- psychology: Feeling-based judgments, sensitivity to reactions, and tension between old and new ideas are described.
- body/health: Mood and sensitivity are psychological descriptions here; no medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Intimacy through communication and historical associations with conversations with or about women are recorded.
- events/manifestations: Irregular communication patterns, conversations about women, and nearby transport facilities are named.
- conflicts/notes: The women-specific meanings belong to Witte, Udo Rudolph, and Ebertin's entries; they are not universal claims about women.

## Contradictions

The transport detail in Witte and the intimacy emphasis in Sandbach concern different manifestations; they do not contradict each other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Asc = Moon`, Moon specifies exchange with others through emotional response: communication seeks inner contact, and the reception of ideas can be sensitive or moody. A need for intimacy through talking and irregular patterns of contact belong to this range ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 180). The older entries also associate the formula with conversations with or about women and local transport ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 106); these remain attributed details rather than a domestic or gender rule.
