## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT + MERCURY`
- Source locator: PDF p. `83` (oriented entry; scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

To be connected with other people by thoughts and ideas.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Aszendent + Merkur`
- Source locator: printed p. `107`, PDF p. `52` (entry).
- Text basis: translation checked against the raw scan on PDF p. `52`; supplied parser-normalized OCR was not itself visually verified.

#### ABC Entry

Describing others' ideas, the partner's clear thinking, partner conversations about truth, clear conversations with young people, cultural conversations in the locality, sensible young partners, speaking about a clean environment, and spiritually oriented conversation partners.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connection through thoughts and ideas is made explicit ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: Clear thinking and describing others' ideas are named in the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 107.
- body/health: No bodily or health statement is supplied; a clean environment is a topic of conversation.
- social/relationship: Conversations with partners or young people may concern truth or culture; ABC also names spiritually oriented conversation partners.
- events/manifestations: Describing ideas and discussing truth, culture, or the environment are the activities recorded.
- conflicts/notes: The ABC phrase for sensible young partners was mistranslated as "strong"; the German scan on PDF p. 52 reads "vernünftige junge Partner." Shared principles are not asserted as a necessary condition.

## Contradictions

The entries are compatible in connecting people through ideas, but their proximity does not establish independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Asc = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the link with others as a connection through ideas. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states that link directly; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 107) makes its content more concrete through clear thinking, describing others' ideas, conversations about truth or culture, and spiritually oriented conversation partners. The evidence supports these modes of exchange without establishing that the people necessarily share every principle.
