## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT + MERCURY`
- Source locator: PDF p. `83` (oriented entry; scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Farewell visits to depart. To be denied, refused.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Aszendent + Merkur`
- Source locator: printed p. `107`, PDF p. `52` (entry).
- Text basis: translation checked against the raw scan on PDF p. `52`; supplied parser-normalized OCR was not itself visually verified.

#### ABC Entry

Thinking of separation from a place, discussing tasks with the partner, the other's serious thoughts, serious conversations in old age, the partner's disturbed thoughts, harsh conversations in the surrounding area, youth as a partner of old age, and discussing others' problems.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. `170`, PDF p. `172`, entry `0560` (scan checked).

#### Ebertin Entry

Inability to discuss vital matters within the family circle, suffering from inhibitions. Sadness, talking about separation, the moment of saying good-bye.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. `53` (complete Saturn entry; chapter starts on p. `52`; scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

Shyness in sharing ideas with others. Feeling limited or inadequate when one attempts to share thoughts. Sharing ideas of a depressing, sorrowful or otherwise negative nature.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source locator: printed p. `180`, PDF p. `181` (Saturn entry; scan checked).

#### Munkasey Entry

Disciplined observations which help you shorten how you classify and categorize the reactions of others; added realism to your view of what motivates others; shortens the time you need for communicating with others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Communication is restrained or serious, with both inhibition and disciplined observation represented.
- psychology: Shyness, inadequacy, sadness, and realism about others' motives are named.
- body/health: The entries describe inhibition and sadness but state no medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Difficulty discussing vital family matters contrasts in emphasis with concise, realistic communication.
- events/manifestations: Farewells, refusals, discussion of separation, tasks, and others' problems are recorded.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's disciplined economy of communication should remain alongside the more difficult expressions in Ebertin and Sandbach.

## Contradictions

Inhibited conversation and effective discipline are different possible expressions, not a direct contradiction requiring one to be discarded.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Asc = Saturn`, Saturn specifies exchange with others through limitation and seriousness. This can appear as shyness, a sense of inadequacy, difficult family discussions, or words of separation ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)); [Witte](#witte-entry) adds farewell visits and refusal. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (p. 180) preserves a different expression: disciplined observation that makes communication more concise and realistic. The page therefore supports restraint as a range, not inevitable isolation.
