## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Farewell visits to depart. To be denied, refused.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Thinking of separation from the place, discussing tasks with the partner, serious thoughts of the other, serious language in old age, disturbance thoughts the Partners, hard language in the environment, youth as partner of the age, and discussing problems of others

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inability to discuss vital matters within the family circle, suffering from inhibitions. Sadness, talking about separation, the moment of saying good-bye.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Shyness in sharing ideas with others. Feeling limited or inadequate when one attempts to share thoughts. Sharing ideas of a depressing, sorrowful or otherwise neg ative nature.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Disciplined observations which help you shorten how you classify and categorize the reactions of others; added realism to your view of what motivates others; shortens the time you need for communicating with others.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Asc = Saturn`: reserve, shyness, sobriety, and the need to weigh speech before exposing oneself to others. Main caution: isolation: fear of judgment, difficulty discussing vital matters, or communication that reinforces sadness, distance, or withdrawal.
