## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT + MERCURY`
- Source locator: PDF p. `83` (oriented entry; scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Others get excited and talk excited. To be involved in an argument. Altercation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Aszendent + Merkur`
- Source locator: printed p. `107`, PDF p. `52` (entry).
- Text basis: translation checked against the raw scan on PDF p. `52`; supplied parser-normalized OCR was not itself visually verified.

#### ABC Entry

Excitement about local youth, dynamic youth in the surroundings, the other's thought impulses, local youth reform, news about environmental technology, new language in the locality, new environmental news, and sudden local movement.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. `170`, PDF p. `172`, entry `0561` (scan checked).

#### Ebertin Entry

Irritability, tendency to lose one's temper quickly in the presence of others, becoming upset or heated about other people. Frank talks or discussions brought about suddenly.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. `53` (complete Uranus entry; chapter starts on p. `52`; scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

Originality when it comes to connecting ideas together. The need to share ideas with others in a context where one can be one’s own unique self and be free.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source locator: printed p. `180`, PDF p. `181` (Uranus entry; scan checked).

#### Munkasey Entry

Introduces you to the potential astrology or science has in helping you to understand other people and how they live; gives others the idea that you have eccentric or singularly focused ideas or views on life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden, excited, or original exchanges with others are represented.
- psychology: Quick irritation coexists in the recorded range with originality and a need for freedom in sharing ideas.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is stated; nervous overstimulation is not an explicit claim.
- social/relationship: Frank discussion, altercation, freedom of self-expression, and being perceived as eccentric are named.
- events/manifestations: Sudden discussions, local youth reform, news about environmental technology, and new language are recorded.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey adds astrology or science as ways of understanding people (p. 180); this is a particular example, not a requirement.

## Contradictions

The excited arguments in Witte and Ebertin and the originality in Sandbach are distinct possibilities, not mutually exclusive conclusions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Asc = Uranus`, Uranus specifies contact through ideas as sudden, excited, or unusual. A frank conversation may arise quickly, with irritation or argument among the adverse possibilities ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry)). The same page also preserves originality and freedom in sharing ideas, and scientific or astrological approaches to understanding people ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 180). These entries do not establish that every original exchange is disruptive.
