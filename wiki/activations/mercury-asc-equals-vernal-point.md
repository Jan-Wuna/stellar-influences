## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Asc = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies communication in the environment as public circulation. Conversations, local media, or reports can take forms intended for general notice, including announcements, advertisements, newspapers, and a public memorandum ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 106). The formula's recorded range concerns the audience and channels of communication, not a guaranteed public reputation.
