## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT + MERCURY`
- Source locator: PDF p. `83` (oriented entry; scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

To give commands or orders. To have to obey. Talks about machines or technical matters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Aszendent + Merkur`
- Source locator: printed p. `107`, PDF p. `52` (entry).
- Text basis: translation checked against the raw scan on PDF p. `52`; supplied parser-normalized OCR was not itself visually verified.

#### ABC Entry

Demanding conversations with others, reports about achievements for the environment, the partner's fiery language, the direction of others' thoughts, creative partner conversations, creative conversation partners, the partner's linguistic performance, and others' word coinages.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Conversation concerns direction, commands, technical work, or creative verbal effort.
- psychology: The entries describe demanding or creative speech, not a general inclination to command.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Giving orders and having to obey identify different positions within directed communication.
- events/manifestations: Commands, technical discussions about machines, reports of achievements, and word coinages are recorded.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's "word chops" is repaired to word coinages; the German scan on PDF p. 52 reads "Wort-Schöpfungen." No wider technical talent is inferred.

## Contradictions

The commanded and commanding positions are both recorded. They are relational alternatives, not a contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Asc = Zeus`, Zeus specifies exchange with others through direction and productive effort. [Witte](#witte-entry) names giving orders, having to obey, and discussing machines or technical matters; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 107) adds demanding conversations, creative partners, verbal performance, and word coinages. The combined range concerns how speech directs or produces something, without establishing that the individual always holds authority.
