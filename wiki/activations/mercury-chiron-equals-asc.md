#### Sandbach Entry

One’s personality communicates healing energy to others. One’s essential sense of being has a way of making others realize how deeply connected they are to everything.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A person's presence conveys a sense of connection.
- psychology: Personality and the essential sense of being are the means of communication.
- body/health: Healing is Sandbach's spiritual language here; no disease, treatment, or physical outcome is stated.
- social/relationship: Others are described as recognizing their connection with everything.
- events/manifestations: The entry describes an effect of personal presence rather than a dated event.
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach is represented; the complete oriented entry is verified on PDF p. 47, following the chapter opening on p. 46.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Chiron = Asc`, Asc specifies Mercury/Chiron through the person who communicates: the manner of being itself carries what Sandbach calls healing energy. The same presence is said to help others recognize their connection with everything. This [single entry](#sandbach-entry) concerns communicated connectedness; it does not establish a therapeutic outcome.
