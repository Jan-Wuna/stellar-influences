### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The building of new lines of communication as well as the repairing of old ones. The ability to guide any and all conflicts toward something more meaningful and productive. Actions based on guidance by higher forces.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Communication is built or repaired through action, including redirecting conflict.
- psychology: The entry associates action with guidance by higher forces.
- body/health: Repair concerns communication; no physical healing claim is stated.
- social/relationship: Conflicts can be directed toward more meaningful and productive contact.
- events/manifestations: Building new lines of communication and repairing old ones are explicit activities.
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach is represented; the complete oriented entry is verified on PDF p. 47, following the chapter opening on p. 46.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Chiron = Mars`, Mars specifies Mercury/Chiron through the work of making and repairing connections. In the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry), this includes guiding conflict toward something meaningful and productive, with action understood as guided by higher forces. The spiritual account remains attributed to this entry; it does not guarantee that every conflict can be resolved.
