### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Mercury/Chiron midpoint`

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s place in the world is as a healer of communicating, meaning that you help others to make new and needed connections with the world around them, as well as with themselves.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A place in the world is defined through helping communication.
- psychology: The entry concerns a sense of role rather than a separate psychological trait.
- body/health: The healer language concerns connection; no medical qualification or treatment is stated.
- social/relationship: Helping others reconnect with themselves and their surroundings is central.
- events/manifestations: Making new and needed connections is named; no particular profession is specified.
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach is represented; the complete oriented entry is verified on PDF p. 47, following the chapter opening on p. 46.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Chiron = MC`, MC specifies Mercury/Chiron as a role in the world: helping others form needed connections with their surroundings and with themselves. Sandbach calls this being a healer of communication in the [single entry](#sandbach-entry). It supports a sense of role without establishing a public career, credential, or particular occupation.
