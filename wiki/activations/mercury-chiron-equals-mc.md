### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s place in the world is as a healer of communicating, meaning that you help others to make new and needed connections with the world around them, as well as with themselves.

- `Mercury/Chiron = MC` makes the axis vocational and public in its healing purpose.
