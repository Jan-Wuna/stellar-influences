#### Sandbach Entry

One possesses an imagination which communicates much healing energy. The ability to subtly, covertly and/or intuitively foster deeper levels of communication and to heal communication rifts.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Imagination and subtle communication are used to repair rifts.
- psychology: Intuition, imagination, and covert approaches are named.
- body/health: Healing energy is Sandbach's language; no medical mechanism or outcome is stated.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Chiron = Neptune`, Neptune specifies Mercury/Chiron through imagination and less overt ways of communicating. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) links intuitive, subtle, or covert approaches with deeper communication and repair of rifts. This supports those modes of connection without adding a promise of spiritual attunement or effortless reconciliation.
