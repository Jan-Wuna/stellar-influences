### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One is automatically drawn to connecting with others with whom one can have healing communications, and who will help one to overcome the limits that one has placed on one’s thinking.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Chiron = Node` socializes the axis through relationships that heal and widen thought.
- Chart expression: being drawn toward people with whom restorative communication is possible, and to connections that help loosen the limits one has placed on one's own thinking.
