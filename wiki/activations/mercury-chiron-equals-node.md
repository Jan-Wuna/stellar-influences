### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Mercury/Chiron midpoint`
- Source locator: PDF p. `47` (complete oriented entry; chapter starts on p. `46`; scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

One is automatically drawn to connecting with others with whom one can have healing communications, and who will help one to overcome the limits that one has placed on one’s thinking.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections are sought with people who enable healing communication.
- psychology: The person's own restrictions on thinking may be loosened through these connections.
- body/health: Healing concerns communication and thinking here; no bodily condition is given.
- social/relationship: The attraction is toward people who support exchange and challenge self-imposed limits.
- events/manifestations: Being drawn into such connections is the only manifestation described.
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach is represented; the complete oriented entry is verified on PDF p. 47, following the chapter opening on p. 46.

## Contradictions

A single passage supplies the evidence, so cross-source agreement or contradiction cannot be assessed.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Chiron = Node`, Node specifies Mercury/Chiron as attraction to connections in which healing communication can occur. In the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry), these people also help overcome limits imposed on one's own thinking. The connection and the widening of thought belong together here; the single passage does not establish a benefit from every relationship.
