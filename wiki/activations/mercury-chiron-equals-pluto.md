## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Mercury/Chiron midpoint`
- Source locator: PDF p. `47` (complete oriented entry; chapter starts on p. `46`; scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

A driving need and urge to heal all breaks in communication and to be understood and understand on deeper and deeper levels. Transformation which happens through the various aspects of oneself coming into deeper communication with each other.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A strong need for deeper understanding drives communication repair.
- psychology: Different aspects of the self are described as entering deeper communication.
- body/health: The healing language concerns breaks in understanding, not a medical claim.
- social/relationship: The desire to understand and be understood extends to deeper levels.
- events/manifestations: Transformation is described through internal communication as well as repair of external breaks.
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach is represented; the complete oriented entry is verified on PDF p. 47, following the chapter opening on p. 46.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Chiron = Pluto`, Pluto specifies Mercury/Chiron as a driving need to understand, be understood, and repair breaks in communication. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) also turns this inward: transformation occurs as different aspects of the self communicate more deeply with one another. It describes a strong impetus, not a guarantee that every break is repaired.
