### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Frustrations and limitations overcome by connecting to spiritual sources which provide one with the exact information and ideas needed and hence further one’s evolution. The ability to focus with depth and clarity on those thoughts and ideas which will heal a situation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Focused ideas and spiritual guidance are linked with overcoming limitations.
- psychology: Depth, clarity, frustration, and the search for needed information are named.
- body/health: Healing refers to a situation and personal evolution; no bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: No particular relationship is named; the scope is a situation requiring focused understanding.
- events/manifestations: Obtaining needed ideas and focusing on them are the actions described.
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach is represented; the complete oriented entry is verified on PDF p. 47.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Chiron = Saturn`, Saturn specifies Mercury/Chiron through a precise focus on ideas needed to work through frustration or limitation. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) attributes access to this information to spiritual sources and relates it to personal evolution. That attribution matters: the entry does not establish that limitation alone produces insight or that focus guarantees a cure.
