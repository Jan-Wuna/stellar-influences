### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The ability to appreciate how communication happens in many different ways, and on many different levels. The ability to intuitively find subtle ways of communicating love. One’s peace and calmness creates an atmosphere in which healing communication can happen.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Appreciation of many forms of communication accompanies subtle expressions of love.
- psychology: Intuition, peace, and calmness support the communicative atmosphere.
- body/health: Healing communication is described; no disease or medical effect is named.
- social/relationship: Love can be conveyed subtly, and a calm atmosphere can support exchange.
- events/manifestations: Creating an atmosphere for communication and expressing love are named.
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach is represented; the complete oriented entry is verified on PDF p. 47.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Chiron = Venus`, Venus specifies Mercury/Chiron through appreciation and affection: recognizing many ways and levels of communication enables subtle expressions of love. Peace and calmness create an atmosphere in which what Sandbach calls healing communication can occur. The [single entry](#sandbach-entry) describes that supportive setting without guaranteeing reconciliation or a therapeutic effect.
