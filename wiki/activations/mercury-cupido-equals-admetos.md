## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Close or restricted conversation can turn toward solitude or depth.
- psychology: Melancholy, loneliness in company, and meditation are explicit in Witte.
- body/health: ABC names restricted communal movement; it does not state a medical condition.
- social/relationship: A few people or a close family circle replace the larger social setting.
- events/manifestations: Laconic conversation, solitary meditation, blocked group thought, and sustained art discussion are named.
- conflicts/notes: Restriction and deepening are both recorded; the entry does not make solitude inherently harmful.

## Contradictions

The entries supply different examples within this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes are not proof of independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Cupido = Admetos`, Admetos specifies Mercury/Cupido through a narrowing or sustained concentration of shared exchange. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes serious conversation with a few people, loneliness in company, brief speech, and meditation alone; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds blocked common thought but also joint deepening of thought and art discussion. The range includes both felt isolation and sustained attention, without treating them as the same experience.
