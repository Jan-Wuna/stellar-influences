## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Cupido = Asc`, Asc specifies Mercury/Cupido as contact with people in the immediate social setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) names social conversation and company with artists, while [ABC](#abc-entry) situates art, partners, youth, and community discussion locally. The entries support participation in artistic and group exchange without establishing that it is always effortless.
