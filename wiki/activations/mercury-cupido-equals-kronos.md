## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Cupido = Kronos`, Kronos specifies Mercury/Cupido through artistic mastery or a speaking role within a group. [Witte](#witte-entry) joins rich poetic thought with a society's leading spokesperson; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds media authority, state communities, and the act of gathering important thoughts. This supports leadership and distinguished artistic thought without assigning authority in every setting.
