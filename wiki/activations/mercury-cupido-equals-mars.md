- core meaning: Artistic or communal thought is put to work through making and organized activity.
- psychology: Wanting to produce something beautiful is explicit in Witte.
- body/health: No health claim is supplied; metalwork is a craft example.
- social/relationship: Work discussions, shared youth work, and an active media group are named.
- events/manifestations: Artistic activity, metal art, wrought-iron work, and forming a young community are recorded.
- conflicts/notes: Craft examples do not establish a universal occupation or skill.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Cupido = Mars`, Mars specifies Mercury/Cupido as shared or artistic thought put into activity. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives making something beautiful, including metal art and wrought-iron work; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives work discussions, collective thought-work, youth work, and active media groups. Making and coordinating are the link, without implying that each expression involves metalworking.
