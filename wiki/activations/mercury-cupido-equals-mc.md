## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Cupido = MC`, MC specifies Mercury/Cupido through personal artistic inspiration and involvement with community. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes inspiration by the muse, artistic giftedness, and social inclination; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives first-person thought about the whole, writing about art, and a soulful shared language. The entries support that personal orientation, not a guaranteed artistic vocation.
