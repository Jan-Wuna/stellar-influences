## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared conversation carries feeling and communal or women-specific themes.
- psychology: ABC describes feeling-filled thoughts of a community, not necessarily happy thoughts.
- body/health: Witte's feminine beauty is an aesthetic association, not a health assessment.
- social/relationship: Women in a conversation group, public discussion, and a people's shared language are named.
- events/manifestations: Public discussion groups and communal opinions are recorded.
- conflicts/notes: The gender associations belong to the sources; repeated common-language wording in ABC is one source statement.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Cupido = Moon`, Moon specifies Mercury/Cupido through feeling in shared conversation and through the groups described in the entries. [ABC](#abc-entry) names women in a conversation community, emotional group thoughts, a people's common language, and communal opinions. [Witte](#witte-entry) adds a public discussion group and historical associations with an artistically gifted woman and feminine beauty. Those gendered examples remain attributed rather than universal.
