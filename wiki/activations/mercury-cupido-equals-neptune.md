## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Cupido = Neptune`, Neptune specifies Mercury/Cupido through shared ideas whose realization remains uncertain. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives mutual plans not accomplished alongside artistic fairy-tale fiction; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds foreign-language and metaphysical discussion, thoughts about future communities or art, and weak shared thinking. Imagination and unrealized plans are both recorded, but artistic fiction is not itself proof of impracticality.
