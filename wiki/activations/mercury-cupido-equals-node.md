## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Cupido = Node`, Node specifies Mercury/Cupido as connection made through a community's exchange of ideas. [Witte](#witte-entry) names conversation with several people, artistic ideas, and an art agency; [ABC](#abc-entry) extends the examples to shared media relations, language, youth connections, and thought that links the group. The point is the connecting process, not a guaranteed lasting alliance.
