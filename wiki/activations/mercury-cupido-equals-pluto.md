## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Cupido = Pluto`, Pluto specifies Mercury/Cupido through the development and alteration of shared expression. [Witte](#witte-entry) names transformation in social conversation, art, or language; [ABC](#abc-entry) makes this concrete as developing thoughts together, changing communal opinions, and changes in a media community. These describe change in exchange without establishing that it is always deep, coercive, or society-wide.
