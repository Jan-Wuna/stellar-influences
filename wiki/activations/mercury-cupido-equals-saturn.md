## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Cupido = Saturn`, Saturn specifies Mercury/Cupido through constraint and concentrated effort in shared exchange. [Witte](#witte-entry) names hindrance to social discussion, travel to talk a matter over, sculpture, and wood carving. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds patient discussion, shared concentration, youth tasks, problematic conversations, and thoughts of separation. These support both difficulty and patient attention; craft does not prove a universal talent or a solution to every obstacle.
