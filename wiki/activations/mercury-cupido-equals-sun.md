## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A personal center in communal or artistic activity is represented.
- psychology: Personal opinions about the whole and thoughts about community are ABC emphases.
- body/health: Witte associates the formula with physical beauty and a youthful appearance; these are historical aesthetic claims.
- social/relationship: Communities of people, youth, and media provide the ABC setting.
- events/manifestations: A professional artist and centers of youth or media communities are named.
- conflicts/notes: Artistic vocation, appearance, and communal thought are separate source associations, not a necessary causal sequence.

## Contradictions

The entries supply different examples within this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes are not proof of independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Cupido = Sun`, Sun specifies Mercury/Cupido through personal participation in artistic or communal life. [ABC](#abc-entry) names personal opinions about the whole and a center for youth or media activity; [Witte](#witte-entry) names a professional artist and a youthful, beautiful appearance. The vocational and aesthetic examples remain attributed, without implying that communal intelligence causes physical attractiveness.
