## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thinking about hidden difficulty becomes blocked or sustained.
- psychology: Confusion and not seeing a way out are Witte's explicit descriptions.
- body/health: ABC's dangerous depressions of youth are historical source language, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: A blocked emergency call and disadvantages of a restricted youth are ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: Repeated talk about distress, depressing past news, and deepening secret thoughts are recorded.
- conflicts/notes: No solution seen in Witte does not prove an actual problem has no possible solution.

## Contradictions

The entries describe different settings or expressions rather than a direct contradiction. Historical clinical and moral terms remain attributed; thematic overlap does not establish independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Hades = Admetos`, Admetos specifies Mercury/Hades through a sense of mental impasse. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes difficulty thinking clearly and seeing no way out of a hidden problem; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds sustained distress-talk, a blocked emergency call, and thoughts deepening around the past. These describe the recorded experience of blockage, not an objective absence of solutions or a diagnosis of depression.
