## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficulty in shared thinking sits alongside specialized knowledge of old languages.
- psychology: Witte describes many being mistaken; ABC also names secret knowledge and dangerous experience.
- body/health: Witte's large association for mental illness is a historical institutional association, not a judgment of a group or individual.
- social/relationship: ABC names danger through freedom of speech, freedom of opinion under threat, and many people in distress as separate examples.
- events/manifestations: Old-language success, accounts of danger, and news of many in need are recorded.
- conflicts/notes: The successful old-language example remains alongside adverse collective examples. ABC's literal successful privations and distress of youth in freedom are unexplained; they support no further general inference.

## Contradictions

The entries describe different settings or expressions rather than a direct contradiction. Historical clinical and moral terms remain attributed; thematic overlap does not establish independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Hades = Apollon`, Apollon specifies Mercury/Hades through a wider field of thought, knowledge, and reported difficulty. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives many people being mistaken and an association connected with mental illness; [ABC](#abc-entry) also gives success through old languages, discussion of secret knowledge, accounts of danger, and freedom of opinion at risk. The page therefore supports both specialized past knowledge and collective difficulties; it does not establish a generally disturbed public mentality.
