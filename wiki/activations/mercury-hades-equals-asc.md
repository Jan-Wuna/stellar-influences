## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Adverse communication is placed in the immediate environment.
- psychology: Witte describes nearby ideas judged wrong or directed toward the past.
- body/health: The entries do not establish illness in a nearby person; Witte's 'ill ideas' remains its wording.
- social/relationship: Threats, defamation, others' disadvantageous opinions, and a partner's emergency call are named.
- events/manifestations: Old writings, discussion of local deficiencies, dangerous local traffic, and news from danger are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: The bullet-only ABC corruption was restored from the raw scan. Reports and others' opinions are distinct from verified conduct.

## Contradictions

The entries describe different settings or expressions rather than a direct contradiction. Historical clinical and moral terms remain attributed; thematic overlap does not establish independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Hades = Asc`, Asc specifies Mercury/Hades through people and communication nearby. [Witte](#witte-entry) names defamation, threats, and someone whose ideas are judged wrong or oriented toward the past; the restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds old conversation partners or writings, local deficiencies, others' disadvantageous opinions, and a partner's emergency call. These remain attributed possibilities, not evidence that a particular neighbor is sick or dangerous.
