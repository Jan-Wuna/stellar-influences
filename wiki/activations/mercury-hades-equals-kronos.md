## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Adverse communication concerns authority, government, or important past writings.
- psychology: The young leader's distress is an ABC example rather than a general mental trait.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Ruling persons, media authority, the state, and grievances about official disadvantage are named.
- events/manifestations: Satire, calumny, secret state talks, state emergency calls, and reports of threats are recorded.
- conflicts/notes: Satire and calumny are distinct source terms; the former is not automatically proven false or malicious.

## Contradictions

The entries describe different settings or expressions rather than a direct contradiction. Historical clinical and moral terms remain attributed; thematic overlap does not establish independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Hades = Kronos`, Kronos specifies Mercury/Hades through adverse or concealed communication around authority. [Witte](#witte-entry) names calumny and satire concerning rulers; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds media authority, complaints of disadvantage by officials, danger to the state, secret state talks, and important past writings. These preserve several authority-related settings without equating every satire or complaint with slander.
