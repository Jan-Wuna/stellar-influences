## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Concealed or adverse thinking is expressed in a personal voice.
- psychology: Pessimism, an immovable attitude, and hidden thoughts are Witte's descriptions.
- body/health: ABC's emotional distress is a report topic; it supplies no diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Personal reports about deprivation and youth disadvantages are recorded.
- events/manifestations: An emergency call and reports about the past or poverty are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's garbled soul-language opening was restored from the scan. Concealment does not establish a motive beyond what is stated.

## Contradictions

The entries describe different settings or expressions rather than a direct contradiction. Historical clinical and moral terms remain attributed; thematic overlap does not establish independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Hades = MC`, MC specifies Mercury/Hades as personally held or expressed thought about difficulty. [Witte](#witte-entry) names hidden bad thoughts, pessimism, and an immovable attitude; the restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) uses a first-person voice for reports about the past, poverty, deprivation, disadvantages in youth, and an emergency call. Its emotional-distress and secret soul-language phrases remain source descriptions, without adding an inevitable refusal to change or a medical judgment.
