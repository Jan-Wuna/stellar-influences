## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `167`.

#### Witte Entry

To be connected with thieves, receivers of stolen goods, vulgar people. Connection with dealer in rags. To be in touch with the topic of mental illness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `256`; PDF page: `127`.

#### ABC Entry

Talking about connections in need, lack of language contacts, secret news connections, criminal connections in youth, associated with emergency calls, emergency calls in traffic, and connection to writings of the past

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections involving difficult communication, need, or concealed material ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: No distinct inner disposition is established by these association lists.
- body/health: Witte names contact with the topic of mental illness, not illness in the person.
- social/relationship: The entries list contact with thieves, receivers of stolen goods, rag dealers, or people linked by emergency communication.
- events/manifestations: Emergency calls, traffic distress, secret messages, and earlier writings are explicit examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's class-based and pejorative labels remain attributed; dealing in castoffs is not evidence of criminality.

## Contradictions

The sources list several kinds of contact rather than one morally uniform circle. Witte's rag dealer and contact with mental illness must not be collapsed into criminal or supposedly unhealthy company.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Hades = Node`, Node makes the difficult or concealed Mercury/Hades material a matter of connection: whom one contacts, what information passes, and what brings people together. The source range includes criminal contacts, but also trade in castoffs, contact with the topic of mental illness, emergency calls, and writings from the past. These distinct examples preserve a wider field than the original moral judgment about one's company ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
