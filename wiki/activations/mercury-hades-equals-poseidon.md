Old language culture, reports of cultural adversity, language about mental deficiency, language about poor renunciation, criminal youth culture, lack of poor languages, lack of spiritual agility, and lack of clarity of youth

- `Mercury/Hades = Poseidon` exposes the axis by making ignorance and falsity unmistakable.
