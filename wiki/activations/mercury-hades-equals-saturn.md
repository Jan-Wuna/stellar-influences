## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `167`.

#### Witte Entry

To be shunned or to shun others. Loss through sharpers or through slanders. Being taken advantage of. Tramp. Vagabond. Long-term mental illness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `257`; PDF page: `127`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `127`, printed page `257`):

> An Verzicht und Trennung denken, gefährliche Jugend-Probleme, gefahrvolle Aufgaben besprechen, Konzentration der Gedanken auf die Vergangenheit, mangelnde Konzentration der Gedanken, problematische Geheim-Gespräche, Störungen durch Jugend-Kriminalität

Editorial translation from the German passage: Thinking about renunciation and separation; dangerous youth problems; discussing dangerous tasks; concentration of thought on the past; lack of concentration; problematic secret conversations; disturbances through youth criminality.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Exclusion, losses, and burdensome or constrained thought ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Thinking about separation or the past appears alongside concentration difficulties.
- body/health: Witte lists long-term mental illness as historical doctrine; it is not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Shunning, being shunned, exploitation, and slander are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Loss through cheats, separation, discussion of dangerous tasks, and troubling secret conversations are named.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes concentration on the past and lack of concentration as separate possibilities.

## Contradictions

The entry includes withdrawal by oneself and exclusion by others. ABC's concentration on the past and lack of concentration are alternatives, not an assertion that both are always present. Witte's medical and social labels remain attributed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Hades = Saturn`, Saturn specifies difficult thought and exchange through separation, exclusion, or sustained burden. Being shunned, avoiding others, and suffering loss through cheats or slander are different ways the exchange can contract. ABC adds attention to separation and the past, sometimes with difficulty concentrating. Witte's additional reference to long-term mental illness remains a historical source association; the formula alone establishes no condition ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
