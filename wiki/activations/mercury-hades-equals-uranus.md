## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `167`.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden or surprising news about the past. Sudden miserable thoughts. Thoughts of murder or homicide.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `257`; PDF page: `127`.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting news of the past, report of technical deficiency, excited talk about deprivation, lack of movement rhythm, dangerous thought impulses, new youth crime, sudden emergency call, and report about dangerous events

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Abrupt troubling thought or news, including news about the past ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Sudden miserable thoughts are explicit; Witte additionally names thoughts of homicide.
- body/health: ABC mentions movement rhythm but no diagnosis.
- social/relationship: ABC lists youth crime and discussion of deprivation.
- events/manifestations: Surprising news, technical-deficiency reports, and a sudden emergency call are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Thinking about violence does not establish violent action.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in sudden or disturbing information. Witte's homicide wording is one listed thought content; ABC includes practical technical reports and emergency messages without asserting violence in every case.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Hades = Uranus`, Uranus specifies troubled thought and communication through abruptness: unexpected news about the past, sudden miserable thoughts, or an urgent call. Reports of technical deficiencies or dangerous events preserve a practical reporting strand alongside Witte's more extreme example of thoughts about homicide. The entry concerns the content or arrival of thoughts and messages; it does not establish that a violent act occurs ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
