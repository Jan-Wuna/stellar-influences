- PDF page (entry start): `167`.

#### Witte Entry

Sad thoughts of love. Weakness of the sexual nerves. Acquaintances who cause slander and bad reputation. Art in the dives. Dirty jokes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `256`; PDF page: `127`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `127`, printed page `256`):

> Gefährdete Friedens-Gespräche, heimliche Liebes-Gedanken, liebevolle Gedanken an die Vergangenheit, kummervolle Liebes-Kriminalität, schmutzige Liebes-Gedanken, heimliche Jugend-Liebe, Verkehrs-Nachrichten-Dienst, Vorliebe für alte Sprachen

Editorial translation from the German passage: Endangered peace talks; secret thoughts of love; affectionate thoughts about the past; sorrowful love-related criminality; dirty thoughts of love; secret youthful love; a traffic-news service; fondness for old languages.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Troubled or concealed love thoughts and communication affecting reputation ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Sad love thoughts and affectionate recollection of the past are distinct.
- body/health: Witte's “weakness of the sexual nerves” remains historical medical language.
- social/relationship: Acquaintances can cause slander; ABC adds secret youthful love.
- events/manifestations: Art in low-status venues, dirty jokes, traffic-news service, and old-language interests are separate examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's sorrowful love-related criminality is a distinct, attributed historical expression.

## Contradictions

Sadness, secrecy, affectionate memory, and vulgar expression remain separate motifs. The unusual ABC phrase “sorrowful love-related criminality” is retained as a labeled literal translation, without elaborating a specific crime.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Hades = Venus`, Venus specifies difficult communication through love, acquaintance, and art. Sad or secret love thoughts sit beside affectionate recollection of the past; social difficulty appears where acquaintances damage a reputation through slander. Witte also lists art in dives and dirty jokes, while ABC preserves interest in old languages and a traffic-news service. Those unusual examples should remain distinct from the relationship theme, and the historical bodily phrase does not establish a condition ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
