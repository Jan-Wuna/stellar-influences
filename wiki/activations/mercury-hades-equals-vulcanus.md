## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `167`.

#### Witte Entry

Criminal thoughts. Vicious and inimical evil gossip or slander.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `257`; PDF page: `127`.

#### ABC Entry

Reports about renunciation of energy, influence of old languages, dangerous influence of the media, dangerous youth violence, talk about violence renunciation, news about danger by violence, news about power renunciation, and hidden thought power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Hostile communication and the influence of troubling messages ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Witte names criminal thoughts and malice.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears.
- social/relationship: Gossip, slander, dangerous media influence, and discussion of violence are explicit.
- events/manifestations: ABC includes news about relinquishing power or energy and concealed thought power.
- conflicts/notes: Reports about violence and discussion of renouncing it are not equivalent.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses hostility; ABC includes both dangerous influence and discussion of giving up violence or power. The latter must not be rewritten as an endorsement or exercise of violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Hades = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies difficult thought and speech through hostility or influence. Witte emphasizes vicious gossip, slander, and criminal thoughts; ABC adds dangerous media influence and messages concerning violence. ABC also names discussion of relinquishing violence or power, so the page includes communication about force and its renunciation as well as hostile uses of speech ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
