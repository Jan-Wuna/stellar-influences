## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `167`.

#### Witte Entry

To mock military persons, or guards. Vulgar, hateful thoughts and talks. Insults, threats and slander. To create strange thoughts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `257`; PDF page: `127`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `127`, printed page `257`):

> Anstrengende Gespräche über Vergangenheit, benachteiligte Jugend-Leistungen, Feuer-Not-Ruf, gefährliche Führungs-Gespräche, Geheim-Gespräche führen, Medien-Leistungen durch Entbehrungen, Sprach-Schöpfungen der Vergangenheit, Verzicht der Jugend auf Zeugung

Editorial translation from the German passage: Strenuous conversations about the past; disadvantaged achievements of young people; a fire emergency call; dangerous leadership discussions; conducting secret conversations; media achievements through deprivation; linguistic creations of the past; young people forgoing procreation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Threatening or hateful speech and difficult communicative effort ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Witte lists hateful thoughts and the creation of strange thoughts.
- body/health: ABC's procreation reference is attributed, not a medical finding.
- social/relationship: Mocking military persons or guards is Witte's specific social example.
- events/manifestations: Insults, threats, slander, secret discussions, and a fire emergency call are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: ABC distinguishes leadership discussions, secret discussions, and earlier linguistic creations.

## Contradictions

Witte's example concerns military persons or guards. ABC separately includes leadership discussions, secret conversations, and linguistic creations of the past; they do not establish that every unusual idea is hateful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Hades = Zeus`, Zeus specifies difficult communication through insults, threats, slander, and the production of strange thoughts. Witte's concrete example is mockery of military persons or guards. ABC adds effortful discussion of the past, secret discussion, and a fire emergency call; those practical communication settings broaden the entry without making every unusual idea hostile ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
