## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `161`.

#### Witte Entry

Fortunate entertainment with many. Success through knowledge of languages. Pleasant scientific discussions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `245`; PDF page: `121`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `121`, printed page `245`):

> Berichte zur Rechts-Wissenschaft, Erfolg durch rechtes Denken, Erfolgs-Nachrichten der Wissenschaft, erfolgreiche Handels-Gespräche, erfolgreiche Finanz-Gespräche, erfolgreiche Recht-Sprechung, viel Gespräche über Geld, zunehmende Rechte der Jugend

Editorial translation from the German passage: Reports concerning legal scholarship; success through sound thinking; news of scientific successes; successful trade discussions; successful financial discussions; successful administration of justice; much conversation about money; increasing rights for young people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful exchange with many, including linguistic, scientific, commercial, and legal subjects ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Knowledge of languages and sound thinking are named resources.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte describes fortunate entertainment with many people.
- events/manifestations: Scientific discussion, finance and trade conversations, legal reports, and youth rights are listed.
- conflicts/notes: The legal terms in ABC concern law or justice, rather than merely correct speech.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes enjoyable company and language knowledge; ABC expands the subject matter to science, trade, finance, justice, and youth rights. These are complementary settings, without guaranteeing success in all of them.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Jupiter = Apollon`, Apollon specifies expansive communication through exchange with many people and through useful knowledge. Language skill and pleasant scientific discussion are Witte's concrete examples; ABC adds successful trade and finance conversations, scientific reports, and legal matters. The organizing theme is effective exchange across a wider field of people or knowledge ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
