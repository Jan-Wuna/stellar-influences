## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `161`.

#### Witte Entry

Obscene jokes, Low jolly entertainment. To become excited about atrocities. Babble. To chaffer about second-hand things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `245`; PDF page: `121`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `121`, printed page `245`):

> Bericht über Glück in Gefahr, erfolgreiche Gespräche über Verzicht, erfolgreiche Gespräche über die Vergangenheit, Freude der Jugend an Kriminalität, Freude über alte Schriften, Gedanken an Geld-Mangel, mangelhafte Recht-Sprechung, Medien-Erfolg durch Verzicht

Editorial translation from the German passage: A report about happiness in danger; successful conversations about renunciation; successful conversations about the past; young people's pleasure in criminality; pleasure in old writings; thoughts of money shortage; deficient administration of justice; media success through renunciation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Pleasure or communication involving coarse subjects, earlier material, or deprivation ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Excitement about atrocities and thoughts of money shortage are distinct source motifs.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Obscene jokes, low entertainment, and second-hand bargaining are Witte's examples.
- events/manifestations: ABC adds old writings, discussion of renunciation, and deficient administration of justice.
- conflicts/notes: Interest in old writings or second-hand goods is not itself criminal or degraded.

## Contradictions

The entries include both coarse entertainment and successful discussion of the past or renunciation. Those strands should remain distinct; the material does not support a general judgment that second-hand dealings debase a person.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Jupiter = Hades`, Hades specifies favorable or animated communication through difficult, coarse, or earlier material. Witte lists obscene jokes, excitement about atrocities, babble, and bargaining over second-hand goods. ABC adds satisfaction in old writings and successful conversations about the past or renunciation, alongside financial shortage and deficient justice. Enjoyment of earlier material therefore has its own place beside the troubling subjects ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
