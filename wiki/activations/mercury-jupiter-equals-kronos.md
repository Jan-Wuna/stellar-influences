## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Eloquent or prominent communication, including official celebration ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Witte names artistry with words and talkative rulers.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears.
- social/relationship: Courts, national celebrations, hierarchy, and legal authority are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Especially good news, notable youth success, and significant media success are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Official prominence and verbal skill are listed associations, not evidence of correctness.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes verbal artistry and celebration; ABC adds hierarchy, legal authority, and notable success. These describe different expressions of prominence, without establishing the merit of an authority's views.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Jupiter = Kronos`, Kronos specifies favorable communication through verbal distinction and official prominence. Artistry with words and talkative rulers connect the personal skill to Witte's court and national celebrations; ABC adds especially good news, legal authority, hierarchy, and significant media success. The concrete range joins eloquence and recognized public occasions without assuming that official speech is necessarily wise ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
