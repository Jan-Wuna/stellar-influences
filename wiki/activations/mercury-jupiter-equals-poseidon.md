## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `161`.

#### Witte Entry

Happiness through spiritual conversation (entertainment). To debate ideas with prudent and philosophically minded people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `245`; PDF page: `121`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `121`, printed page `245`):

> Berichte über Rechts-Kultur, Erfolg durch Klarheit der Gedanken, erfolgreiche Kultur-Gespräche, erfolgreiche Medien-Kultur, Gedanken zu Recht und Wahrheit, Gespräche über geistige Erfolge, klare Recht-Sprechung, vernünftiges Jugend-Recht

Editorial translation from the German passage: Reports about legal culture; success through clarity of thought; successful cultural conversations; successful media culture; thoughts about law and truth; conversations about intellectual successes; clear administration of justice; reasonable youth law.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Joy through thoughtful spiritual or philosophical conversation ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Clarity of thought and prudent debate are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears.
- social/relationship: Exchange with philosophically minded people is Witte's setting.
- events/manifestations: Cultural discussion, intellectual successes, and legal culture are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC describes reasonable youth law, not poor youth law.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses happiness in conversation; ABC adds clarity, culture, law, and truth. The entries do not establish a separate adverse category for this formula.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Jupiter = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies expansive communication through clear thought and spiritual or philosophical exchange. Witte describes happiness in conversation with prudent, philosophically minded people; ABC adds successful cultural discussion, thought about law and truth, and clear administration of justice. The common thread is satisfaction in ideas made intelligible and thoughtfully discussed ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
