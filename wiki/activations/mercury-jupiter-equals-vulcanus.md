## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `161`.

#### Witte Entry

Constructive optimistic thinking. Huge financial transactions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `245`; PDF page: `121`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `121`, printed page `245`):

> Energie durch glückliches Denken, einflußreiche Rechts-Gespräche, erfolgreiche Macht des Denkens, Freude der Jugend an Gewalt, Freude über Wort-Gewalt, Nachrichten über Recht und Gewalt, über den Einfluß des Geldes sprechen

Editorial translation from the German passage: Energy through happy thinking; influential legal discussions; successful power of thought; young people's pleasure in violence; joy in verbal force; news about law and violence; talking about the influence of money.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Constructive optimism and influential thought or finance ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Happy thinking is connected with energy in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears.
- social/relationship: ABC separately names pleasure in physical violence and joy in verbal force; the latter is rhetorical power.
- events/manifestations: Large financial transactions and discussion of money's influence are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Constructive thinking and the troubling violence examples remain distinct.

## Contradictions

Witte's constructive optimism differs from ABC's pleasure in violence. ABC also names joy in verbal force, a separate rhetorical clause which must not be treated as physical violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Jupiter = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies favorable thought through effectiveness and scale: constructive optimism, successful mental effort, influential legal discussion, and large financial transactions. ABC distinguishes pleasure in violence from joy in verbal force, so rhetorical power and physical force remain separate examples. Discussion of money's influence supplies another concrete expression ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
