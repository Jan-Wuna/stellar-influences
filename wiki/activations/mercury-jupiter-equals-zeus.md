## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `161`.

#### Witte Entry

A jester who carries one away by his speech and gestures. To be forced to buy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `245`; PDF page: `121`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `121`, printed page `245`):

> Anstrengende Rechts-Gespräche, erfolgreiche Führungs-Gedanken, erfolgreiche Medien-Leistungen, Geld-Bewegungen bedenken, Gespräche über Zeugungs-Erfolge, Leistungs-Erfolge der Jugend, schöpferische Recht-Sprechung, über Rechts-Erfolge berichten

Editorial translation from the German passage: Strenuous legal discussions; successful leadership thoughts; successful media achievements; considering money movements; conversations about procreative successes; achievements of young people; creative administration of justice; reporting on legal successes.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Speech and gesture that carry an audience along, with directed communicative effort ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: The entries emphasize persuasive performance and leadership thought rather than a separate mood.
- body/health: ABC's procreation-success wording remains an attributed example.
- social/relationship: Witte explicitly includes being forced to buy.
- events/manifestations: Media achievement, legal discussion, money movements, and youth achievements are listed.
- conflicts/notes: Being carried along and being compelled are distinct; neither should be softened into free agreement.

## Contradictions

Witte names both a compelling performer and being forced to buy. ABC emphasizes successful effort in leadership, media, and law; it does not cancel Witte's coercive example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Jupiter = Zeus`, Zeus specifies expansive communication through a performance or effort that directs response. Witte's jester carries people along through speech and gesture, but the separate phrase “forced to buy” makes pressure explicit. ABC adds leadership thought, media achievements, legal discussions, and attention to money movements. The range therefore includes accomplished expression and compelled response, rather than persuasion understood only as willing agreement ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
