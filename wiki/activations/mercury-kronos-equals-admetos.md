#### Witte Entry

Good foundation of theory of thoughts. Origin and beginning of a great idea. Great ideas for few. Thoughts which are only understood by few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `261`; PDF page: `129`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `129`, printed page `261`):

> Besonderer Tiefgang der Gedanken, deprimierende Berichte des Staates, gefestigter junger Chef, großer Verkehrs-Stau, gründliche Behörden-Gespräche, staatliche Nachrichten-Sperre, unterdrücktes Selbstbewußtsein der Jugend, Vertiefung selbständiger Gedanken

Editorial translation from the German passage: Particular depth of thought; depressing state reports; an established young boss; major traffic congestion; thorough discussions with authorities; a state news blackout; suppressed self-confidence among young people; deepening independent thoughts.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Foundational or deeply concentrated ideas ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Independent thought is deepened; ABC also names suppressed youthful self-confidence.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Witte's ideas are understood by only a few people.
- events/manifestations: Theory foundations, official discussions, state news restrictions, and traffic congestion are named.
- conflicts/notes: The restored ABC row belongs to Admetos; Apollon's success paragraph was previously merged into it.

## Contradictions

Witte's foundational thinking and ABC's depth overlap. ABC additionally includes depressing state reports and suppression; these constraints do not make every deep idea incomprehensible or unsuccessful.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Kronos = Admetos`, Admetos specifies important thought through foundations and depth. Witte describes the beginning of a great idea or a theory understood by only a few; ABC adds deepening independent thought and thorough official discussion. Its state news restrictions, depressing reports, and suppressed youthful self-confidence preserve a separate limiting strand. The entry concerns concentrated understanding and constrained communication, rather than a guarantee of intellectual superiority ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
