### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `169`.

#### Witte Entry

Artistically active thinker. Great artistic ability.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `261`; PDF page: `129`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `129`, printed page `261`):

> Ansehen einer Jugend-Gemeinschaft, Berichte einer staatlichen Gemeinschaft, große Verkehrs-Gemeinschaft, selbstbewußte Meinung einer Gemeinschaft, selbständige Jugend-Gemeinschaft, staatliche Medien-Gemeinschaft, wichtige Gespräche über Kunst

Editorial translation from the German passage: The standing of a youth community; reports from a state community; a large transport community; a community's self-confident opinion; an independent youth community; a state media community; important conversations about art.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Artistic thought and the standing or voice of a community ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: A community's self-confident opinion is explicit in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears.
- social/relationship: Youth, state, transport, and media communities are named.
- events/manifestations: Artistic activity, great artistic ability, and important conversations about art are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Self-confident and independent community activity differs from the old mistranslations “self evident” and “self employed”.

## Contradictions

Witte focuses on artistic ability while ABC includes community reputation and communication. These are different settings, not a claim that every community has artistic distinction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Kronos = Cupido`, Cupido specifies important thought through artistic activity and community expression. Witte's artistically active thinker connects with ABC's important conversations about art. ABC also describes a community's standing and self-confident voice, including youth, state, transport, and media groups. The page therefore retains both artistic ability and significant collective communication ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
