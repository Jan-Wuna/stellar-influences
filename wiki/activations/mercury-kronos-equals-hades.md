### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `169`.

#### Witte Entry

Great ideas or thoughts of the past. Great thoughts about the antique. Perished powers of states. Swindle eluding the law. King of crooks.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `261`; PDF page: `129`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `129`, printed page `261`):

> Gefährliche Autorität der Medien, Dominanz einer alten Sprache, Not-Ruf des Staates, Mangel an selbständigem Denken, nachteilige Meinung vom Staat, Selbstbewußtsein einer kriminellen Jugend, staatliche Geheim-Gespräche, über besondere Nachteile sprechen

Editorial translation from the German passage: Dangerous media authority; dominance of an old language; a state emergency call; lack of independent thinking; an adverse opinion of the state; self-confidence of criminal young people; secret state conversations; speaking about particular disadvantages.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Important thought concerning earlier power, or troubled and concealed authority ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: ABC names lack of independent thought and criminal youths' self-confidence as separate examples.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears.
- social/relationship: State authority, media authority, and evasion of law are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: Antiquity, vanished state powers, state emergency calls, and secret discussions are named.
- conflicts/notes: Historical interest is distinct from fraud; lack of independent thinking is not lack of self-esteem.

## Contradictions

Witte places great ideas of the past beside fraud that evades law. Those strands remain distinct; ABC's dangers and secrecy do not establish that historical study is deceptive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Kronos = Hades`, Hades specifies important thought through the past and through problematic authority. Great earlier ideas, antiquity, and vanished state powers form a historical strand; evading law, dangerous media authority, and secret state conversations form another. ABC also adds lack of independent thought. These examples describe different relations to authority and earlier material, without turning interest in the past into evidence of fraud ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
