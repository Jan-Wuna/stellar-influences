## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `169`.

#### Witte Entry

Good mental ability. Moment of government news, ideas or plans. Striving for justice.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `260`; PDF page: `129`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `129`, printed page `260`):

> Besondere Sprache der Seele, die Sprache meines Chefs, Dominanz einer beseelten Sprache, Gedanken einer großen Seele, mein junger Chef, mein selbständiges Denken, meine Meinung als Chef, meine Meinung vom Staat, Selbstbewußtsein der Jugend, Vorrang meiner Worte

Editorial translation from the German passage: A particular language of the soul; my boss's language; dominance of soulful language; thoughts of a great soul; my young boss; my independent thinking; my opinion as a boss; my opinion of the state; young people's self-confidence; precedence of my words.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: One's intellectual ability, independent judgment, and concern with state or justice ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Independent thinking and youthful self-confidence are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears.
- social/relationship: One's boss, one's role as boss, and one's opinion of the state are named.
- events/manifestations: Government news, plans, and striving for justice are Witte's examples.
- conflicts/notes: Selbständiges Denken means independent thinking; the former selfishness claim is unsupported.

## Contradictions

Witte's pursuit of justice and ABC's own opinions describe personal orientations, without showing that one's opinions or official plans are just.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Kronos = MC`, MC specifies important thought as one's own intellectual work and judgment. Witte describes mental ability, government news or plans, and striving for justice; ABC adds independent thinking, one's opinion as a boss, and one's view of the state. The common field is a personal relation to consequential ideas and authority, while the source's “language of the soul” remains a distinct expression of inward meaning ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
