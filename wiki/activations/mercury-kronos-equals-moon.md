## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `169`.

#### Witte Entry

Good comprehension. Able to learn easily. High minded active women. Great ideas in the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `260`; PDF page: `129`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `129`, printed page `260`):

> Besonders gefühlvolle Gespräche, selbständige junge Frau, Dominanz der Frau im Gespräch, Gedanken einer großen Frau, großes Ansehen eines Volkes, Meinungs-Dominanz des Volkes, Jugend eines großen Volkes, selbstbewußte weibliche Jugend

Editorial translation from the German passage: Especially emotional conversations; an independent young woman; a woman's dominance in conversation; a great woman's thoughts; a people's high standing; dominance of the people's opinion; the youth of a great people; self-confident female youth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Comprehension, learning, and important ideas expressed through women or the public ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Emotional conversation and self-confidence appear in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Independent young women, active high-minded women, and public standing are named.
- events/manifestations: Learning easily and great ideas in public are Witte's examples.
- conflicts/notes: Historical gender and national labels remain attributed; memory is not specified.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes comprehension and public ideas; ABC adds emotional conversation and social standing. Their historical female examples do not define a universal feminine trait.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Kronos = Moon`, Moon specifies important thought through comprehension and its expression in women or the public. Witte joins ease of learning with active, high-minded women and public ideas; ABC adds emotional conversation, an independent young woman, and a people's standing or dominant opinion. The page concerns understanding and a significant voice, without importing memory as an additional mechanism ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
