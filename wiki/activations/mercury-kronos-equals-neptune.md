## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `169`.

#### Witte Entry

Rhetoric gift. Slam poet. Unclear relations to the power of the state. Uncertainties about the future mode of acting of the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `261`; PDF page: `129`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `129`, printed page `261`):

> Besondere Gedanken-Schwäche, besondere Luft-Bewegung, dominanter Fremden-Verkehr, Gedanken über die Zukunft des Staates, Gespräche über die Wichtigkeit des Wassers, schwache Autorität des Chefs, unsicherer junger Chef, wichtige Gespräche über Metaphysik

Editorial translation from the German passage: Particular weakness of thought; particular air movement; dominant foreign traffic; thoughts about the state's future; conversations about the importance of water; a boss's weak authority; an insecure young boss; important conversations about metaphysics.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Rhetorical ability beside uncertainty about authority or state action ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: ABC names particular weakness of thought and an insecure young boss.
- body/health: No bodily illness is established.
- social/relationship: Relations with state power and a boss's weak authority are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Poetic performance, foreign traffic, air movement, water discussion, and metaphysics are separate examples.
- conflicts/notes: Neptune's verified ABC row ends at metaphysics; the development paragraph is separate.

## Contradictions

Rhetorical talent and uncertainty about state power are distinct motifs. ABC's weak authority does not imply that skillful speech resolves the uncertainty.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Kronos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies important speech through rhetorical or poetic ability while leaving relations to authority uncertain. Witte explicitly separates the gift of rhetoric from unclear state relations and uncertainty about future state action. ABC adds a boss's weak authority and an insecure young boss, alongside distinct examples involving air movement, foreign traffic, water, and metaphysical discussion. Effective expression therefore coexists with uncertain authority in this exact entry ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
