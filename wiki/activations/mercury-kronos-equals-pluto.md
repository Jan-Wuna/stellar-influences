## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `169`.

#### Witte Entry

To develop great ideas. To grow up into a great mental or spiritual leader.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `261`; PDF page: `129`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `129`, printed page `261`):

> Entwicklungs-Gedanken des Staates, Gespräche über große Veränderungen, große Gedanken entwickeln, Nachrichten über Veränderungen im Staat, selbstbewußte Entwicklung der Jugend, staatliche Entwicklungs-Gespräche, wachsende Autorität der Jugend

Editorial translation from the German passage: The state's thoughts about development; conversations about major changes; developing great thoughts; news of changes in the state; self-confident development of young people; state discussions about development; young people's growing authority.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development of great ideas or intellectual leadership ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Self-confident development of young people is an ABC example.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears.
- social/relationship: Growing youthful authority and a mental or spiritual leader are named.
- events/manifestations: Discussion of major changes and state development are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Authority does not mean authoritarianism; the complete Pluto paragraph is restored to its own glyph.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe development, with Witte emphasizing ideas and leadership and ABC adding state changes and youth. They do not specify a rate of growth or guarantee office.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Kronos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies important thought through its development: great ideas take shape, or someone grows into intellectual or spiritual leadership. ABC extends this to discussion of major changes, news of state changes, and the increasing authority of young people. The shared theme is developing ideas and standing, without equating authority with authoritarianism or prescribing how quickly development occurs ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
