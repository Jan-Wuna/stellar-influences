## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `169`.

#### Witte Entry

To conceive great thoughts. A body ennobled through the mind. Fathers word. Government head.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `260`; PDF page: `129`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `129`, printed page `260`):

> Berichte über menschliche Größe, großer Personen-Verkehr, junge Autoritäts-Person, Nachrichten-Zentrum des Staates, persönliche Autorität durch die Sprache, persönliche Gespräche mit dem Chef, staatliches Jugend-Zentrum, wichtige persönliche Gespräche

Editorial translation from the German passage: Reports about human greatness; substantial movement of people; a young authority figure; the state's news center; personal authority through language; personal conversations with the boss; a state youth center; important personal conversations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Conceiving great ideas and personal authority through expression ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: The entries emphasize thought and standing rather than a specific mood.
- body/health: Witte's body ennobled through mind is an attributed symbolic claim.
- social/relationship: A father's word, a head of government, and one's boss are named.
- events/manifestations: State news centers, reports of human greatness, and personal conversations are examples.
- conflicts/notes: The governing or paternal examples do not establish the person's office or gender.

## Contradictions

Witte's symbolic bodily language and ABC's practical communication settings remain distinct. Neither makes a government position or paternal role necessary.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Kronos = Sun`, Sun specifies important thought through a person and the authority of their words. Conceiving great ideas belongs beside Witte's father's word and head of government; ABC adds personal authority through speech and conversations with a boss. State news and youth centers supply institutional settings. The bodily phrase remains Witte's symbolic association, without extending it into a medical effect ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
