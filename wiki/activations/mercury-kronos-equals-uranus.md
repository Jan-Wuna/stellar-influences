## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `169`.

#### Witte Entry

Good mathematician. Ingenious formation of words, phrases. Announcements by the state. Creator of new things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `261`; PDF page: `129`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `129`, printed page `261`):

> Aufregende staatliche Nachrichten, besonderer Rhythmus der Jugend, beunruhigende Meldungen über die Obrigkeit, dynamische Autorität der Jugend, große Spannungen mit der Jugend, staatliche Verkehrs-Reform, wichtige Denk-Impulse

Editorial translation from the German passage: Exciting state news; a particular rhythm among young people; disturbing reports about the authorities; dynamic youthful authority; major tensions with young people; state transport reform; important impulses of thought.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Inventive expression and consequential announcements ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Ingenious word formation and new thought impulses are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears.

- conflicts/notes: Innovation and alarming official news remain distinct manifestations.

## Contradictions

Witte's inventive ability differs from ABC's disturbing official reports and youth tension. These are separate settings of consequential new thought or communication.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Kronos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies important thought through invention and new expression. Witte names mathematics, ingenious phrasing, and creating new things, alongside announcements by the state. ABC adds reform, thought impulses, and disturbing reports about authorities. Ingenuity and official disruption are distinct expressions of the entry, rather than evidence that every innovation has public stature ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
