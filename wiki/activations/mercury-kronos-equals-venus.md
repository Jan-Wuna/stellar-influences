## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `169`.

#### Witte Entry

Acquaintance through great thoughts or ideas. Soft artistic way of expression. Pleasant acquaintance with experts or the power of the state, and thereby enjoy advantages. Artistic form of literature.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `260`; PDF page: `129`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `129`, printed page `260`):

> Ansehen der Medien-Kunst, besondere Sprach-Kunst, dominierende künstlerische Meinung, Friedens-Gespräche des Staates, große Jugend-Liebe, große Liebe zur Jugend, selbstbewußte junge Liebe, wichtige Worte des Wohlwollens, wohlwollende Worte des Chefs

Editorial translation from the German passage: The standing of media art; particular language artistry; a dominant artistic opinion; state peace talks; great youthful love; great love for young people; self-confident young love; important words of goodwill; a boss's benevolent words.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Artistic expression and pleasant ties through significant ideas ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Goodwill and self-confident young love are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears.
- social/relationship: Acquaintance with experts or state power may bring advantages.
- events/manifestations: Literary form, language art, state peace talks, and a boss's benevolent words are named.
- conflicts/notes: An advantage from acquaintance is not a guaranteed benefit or proof of merit.

## Contradictions

The artistic and social themes overlap in expressive form and agreeable contact. ABC also includes state peace talks and youthful love, without making all such ties romantic.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Kronos = Venus`, Venus specifies important thought through artistic expression and agreeable acquaintance. Great ideas can bring people together; Witte adds literature and benefits from pleasant contact with experts or state power. ABC's language art, peace talks, and benevolent words from a boss connect that social ease with significant speech, while youthful love remains a separate named example ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
