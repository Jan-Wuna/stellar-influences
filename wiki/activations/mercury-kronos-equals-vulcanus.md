## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `169`.

#### Witte Entry

Power and influence of great ideas. Powerful spiritual leader. High concept of the idea of the state, or great ideas.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `261`; PDF page: `129`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `129`, printed page `261`):

> Besondere Jugend-Gewalt, Dominanz durch Wort-Gewalt, große Kraft der Gedanken, große Medien-Macht, großer Einfluß der Jugend, machtvolle Worte des Staates, Meinungs-Einfluß des Staates, staatliche Energie-Berichte, starker Autoritäts-Einfluß

Editorial translation from the German passage: Particular youth violence; dominance through forceful words; great strength of thought; great media power; great influence of young people; powerful words of the state; the state's influence on opinion; state energy reports; strong authoritative influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The power and influence of important ideas or authoritative words ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Strong thought is explicit without a separate emotional claim.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears.
- social/relationship: Powerful spiritual leadership, media power, and state influence on opinion are named.
- events/manifestations: State words, energy reports, and youthful influence or violence are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Influence may be forceful or troubling; intellectual importance does not establish ethical value.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes the influence of great ideas; ABC adds forceful words and youth violence. These difficult examples remain alongside leadership rather than being recast as uniformly elevated thought.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Kronos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies important ideas through their force and influence. Witte's powerful spiritual leader and concept of the state connect with ABC's media power, authoritative words, and state influence on opinion. ABC also includes youth violence and dominance through forceful words. The entry therefore concerns consequential thought and speech whose power is not itself a guarantee of constructive use ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
