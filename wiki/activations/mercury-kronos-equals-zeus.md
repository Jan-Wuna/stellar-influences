## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `169`.

#### Witte Entry

To realize great thoughts systematically. War laws and commands issuing from the highest authority. Binding promise.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `261`; PDF page: `129`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `129`, printed page `261`):

> Besondere Leistungen der Medien, besondere Meinung zur Zeugung, führende Meinung im Staate, Gespräche über besondere Anstrengungen, Meinungen über die Staats-Führung, Nachrichten über Ziele der Obrigkeit, wichtige Gespräche mit Autoritäten

Editorial translation from the German passage: Particular media achievements; a particular opinion on procreation; a leading opinion in the state; conversations about particular efforts; opinions about state leadership; news about the authorities' goals; important conversations with authorities.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Systematic realization of important ideas and binding expression ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: The entry emphasizes organized effort rather than a separate mood.
- body/health: ABC's procreation reference remains an attributed subject of opinion.
- social/relationship: Highest authority, state leadership, and conversations with authorities are explicit.
- events/manifestations: War laws, commands, binding promises, media achievements, and official goals are named.
- conflicts/notes: Binding does not establish that a promise is fulfilled or that every command is unavoidable.

## Contradictions

Witte specifies war laws and commands as well as a binding promise. ABC's official discussion and media achievements supply further settings, without making all planned activity military.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Kronos = Zeus`, Zeus specifies important thought through systematic realization and words that bind or direct action. Witte joins carrying out great ideas with commands from the highest authority, war laws, and a binding promise. ABC adds discussion of leadership, official goals, special effort, and media achievements. The connection is between formulated ideas and directed implementation, while military command remains one specific source example ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
