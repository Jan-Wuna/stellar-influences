## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Dumb. Taciturn. Slow in expression. Debates in a small circle. The activities are suspended or hindered. Not to be able to act.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Persistent work meetings, blocked thought activity, depressing news work, activating thought circuit, grim thought activity, underriding youth actions, and resistance to media work

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Mars = Admetos` compresses the axis into blocked speech and hindered action.
- Chart expression: taciturn or dull expression, debate kept to a small circle, and forceful thought slowed down so much that action stalls or cannot be carried out.
