## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 160, one-based, entry start (supplied source-evidence record; not a printed-page number).

Dumb. Taciturn. Slow in expression. Debates in a small circle. The activities are suspended or hindered. Not to be able to act.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `120`, printed page `243`):

> Andauernde Arbeits-Besprechungen, blockierte Gedanken-Tätigkeit, deprimierende Nachrichten-Arbeit, Gedanken-Kreislauf aktivieren, gründliche Gedanken-Tätigkeit, Unterdrückung von Aktionen der Jugend, Widerstand gegen Medien-Arbeit

Editorial translation from the German passage: Continuing work discussions; blocked mental activity; depressing news work; activating a cycle of thought; thorough mental activity; suppression of young people's actions; resistance to media work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Speech and action meet restriction or suspension.
- psychology: Taciturnity and slow expression in Witte; blocked thinking in ABC.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established; Witte's historical word “Dumb” is retained as source wording.
- social/relationship: Debate confined to a small circle; persistent work meetings and resistance to media work.
- events/manifestations: Hindered activity and difficulty acting or expressing oneself.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes thorough mental work as well as blockage and suppression; these are different forms of concentrated or constrained activity.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes slowed or hindered action. ABC also includes thorough mental activity and activating a cycle of thought; restriction does not exclude depth of work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies `Mercury/Mars = Admetos` through slowing, narrowing, or hindering expression and action. Debate may remain within a small circle, and activity may be suspended. ABC adds sustained work discussions and blocked thought, but also thorough mental activity and activating a cycle of thought. The distinction is between concentrated effort and effort that cannot proceed, while suppression of young people's actions and resistance to media work supply concrete social settings ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
