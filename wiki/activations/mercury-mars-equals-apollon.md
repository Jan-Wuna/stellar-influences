## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 160, one-based, entry start (supplied source-evidence record; not a printed-page number).

To have much to speak. Good in expression. Fatiguing scientific efforts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `120`, printed page `243`):

> Ausgedehnte Arbeits-Besprechungen, erfolgreiche Gedanken-Tätigkeit, erfolgreiche Gespräche über die Arbeit, erfolgreiche Medien-Tätigkeit, Freiheit der männlichen Jugend, Gedanken-Arbeit der Wissenschaft, zunehmende Verkehrs-Aktivität

Editorial translation from the German passage: Extensive work discussions; successful mental activity; successful conversations about work; successful media activity; freedom of male youth; the mental work of science; increasing transport activity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Abundant expression and extensive intellectual work.
- psychology: Productive thinking can demand tiring effort.
- body/health: Witte mentions fatigue from scientific effort, without a medical claim.
- social/relationship: Extended work discussions and successful communication or media activity.
- events/manifestations: Scientific work, much speaking, and increasing traffic activity in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's freedom of male youth is a separate historical social example; it does not establish freedom from every work restriction.

## Contradictions

Extensive and successful communication coexists with Witte's tiring scientific effort. The separate ABC example of freedom for male youth does not remove that cost.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies `Mercury/Mars = Apollon` through extensive expression and intellectual work: having much to say, expressing it well, and making demanding scientific efforts. ABC adds extended work meetings, successful media activity, and increasing transport activity, with freedom of male youth retained as a separate social example. Productive breadth and tiring effort therefore remain side by side ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
