#### Sandbach Entry

To be guided spiritually when arguing, and because of this to use the conflict of ideas as a healing device. To say pointed things of a healing nature which carry a po tent impact, often without knowing one is doing so.

## Derived Synthesis

Chiron specifies Mercury/Mars as the use of argument and pointed speech for healing, within [Sandbach's single entry](#sandbach-entry). The conflict of ideas is not simply removed: it becomes the means through which a forceful statement may help, sometimes without the speaker recognizing its impact. “Guided spiritually” and “healing” belong to this source's interpretation; the passage supplies no medical mechanism or assurance that an argument will be beneficial.
