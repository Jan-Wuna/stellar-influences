## Derived Synthesis

Cupido locates Mercury/Mars in collective activity: people consult, give lectures, discuss art or marriage, and act as an association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins organized speech to an association's powerful action, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the setting to shared youth work, work reports, and community media activity. The interpretation is therefore about communication doing work within a group; the entries do not establish a general claim about every family relationship.
