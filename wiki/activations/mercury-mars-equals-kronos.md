## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 160, one-based, entry start (supplied source-evidence record; not a printed-page number).

To be particular in the mode of expression, to well weigh or consider the words. Debate, dispute, argument about ideas and great thoughts. Controversy among authorities. Through a decree of the government. Government order. Dictator.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `120`, printed page `243`):

> Aktivitäten zwischen Medien und Staat, Autorität in Wort und Tat, besondere Arbeit besprechen, selbständige Medien-Tätigkeit, junger männlicher Chef, vorrangige Gedanken-Tätigkeit, wichtige Arbeits-Besprechung, wichtige Jugend-Arbeit

Editorial translation from the German passage: Activities between media and state; authority in word and deed; discussing particular work; independent media activity; a young male boss; priority mental activity; an important work discussion; important youth work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Carefully weighed speech with authority or official consequence.
- psychology: Deliberate expression and attention to important ideas.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears.
- social/relationship: Controversy among authorities and debate about major ideas.
- events/manifestations: Government decrees or orders in Witte; media-state activity and important meetings in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: Witte ranges from considered speech to dictatorship; these are different expressions of authority.

## Contradictions

Weighed words, official orders and dictatorship are distinct possibilities. ABC's independent media activity and young male boss do not imply dictatorial behavior.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos gives Mercury/Mars an authoritative setting: words are weighed carefully, major ideas become subjects of dispute, and speech may take the form of an official order. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ranges from deliberate expression to controversy among authorities, government decrees, and the extreme example of a dictator. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds media-state activity and important work meetings. Authority is the connecting theme; the extreme example does not turn every firm statement into dictatorial behavior.
