## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 160, one-based, entry start (supplied source-evidence record; not a printed-page number).

Irritated and angry women. Debates over women. Quarrels over women. Quarrelling persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `120`, printed page `242`):

> Gedanken über die Handlungen einer Frau, Gedanken einer Frau über die Arbeit, Gemüts-Tätigkeit beschreiben, Gespräche über Arbeit für das Volk, mit einer Frau über die Arbeit sprechen, Gefühls-Handlungen bedenken, Tätigkeit einer jungen Frau

Editorial translation from the German passage: Thoughts about a woman's actions; a woman's thoughts about work; describing emotional activity; conversations about work for the people; speaking with a woman about work; considering actions prompted by feeling; a young woman's activity.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Printed source page: `149`; entry code: `0472`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Thinking and acting strongly influenced by feeling, "clearing the air" by means of argument and talking it out. A quarrelsome or nagging woman, quarreling on account of the female sex.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- PDF chapter start: `42` (chapter locator; not an exact activation-page reference).

#### Sandbach Entry

The emotional need to be able to say what one thinks, and to let out anger verbally. The urge to argue or to speak passionately is aroused when one is feeling in secure, or very emotional. Difficult emotions generate nervous energy.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `152`

#### Munkasey Entry

An accumulation of reasons for the use of force; defensive tactics against an usually communicative person; irregular observations of impatience or irritation; loud talk on family or home matters; verbal and active women.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Feeling strongly colors thought, speech, and action.
- psychology: Emotional insecurity or anger can prompt passionate expression and argument.
- body/health: Sandbach describes nervous energy arising from difficult emotions, without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Talking out conflict, family discussion, and historical gendered examples of quarrelling.
- events/manifestations: Loud talk about home matters and argument used to clear the air.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered quarrel examples remain attributed; ABC separately includes women's actions, work and emotional activity.

## Contradictions

The sources include talking out conflict as well as reactive quarrelling. The restored ABC row adds work and emotional activity; it does not support the former “one and a half thoughts” phrase.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Mercury/Mars through the pressure of feeling on communication: the need to say what one thinks or let anger out can make debate passionate and reactive. The [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) and [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) entries connect feeling to argument, including the possibility of talking a conflict through; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (p. 152) adds loud discussion of home or family matters. Witte's and Ebertin's gendered quarrel descriptions remain historical source claims, not a general characterization of women.
