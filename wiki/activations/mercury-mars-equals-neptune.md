## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 160, one-based, entry start (supplied source-evidence record; not a printed-page number).

To speak badly. To lisp. A defect in speech. Strong imagination. To commit blunders. To be excited over nothing. Will to destroy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `120`, printed page `243`):

> Arbeit in fremder Sprache, Arbeits-Gespräche über Wasser, enttäuschende Jugend-Arbeit, metaphysische Arbeit besprechen, Gespräche über Intuition bei der Arbeit, zukünftige Jugend-Arbeit, Verfeinerung der Gespräche über die Arbeit

Editorial translation from the German passage: Work in a foreign language; work conversations about water; disappointing youth work; discussing metaphysical work; conversations about intuition at work; future youth work; refinement of conversations about work.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Printed source page: `149`; entry code: `0477`.

#### Ebertin Entry

The pursuit of fantastic plans, speaking without acting, failure to keep promises. Hopeless plans, tendency to harm others through falsehood or underhanded behavior, undermining another person's position.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- PDF chapter start: `42` (chapter locator; not an exact activation-page reference).

#### Sandbach Entry

Arguing in a vague or unformed manner that confuses other people. A subtle and/or covert aggressiveness when speaking. One expresses one’s idealism with forcefulness. Arguing about ideals.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `152`

#### Munkasey Entry

Expressions of anger which do little to clarify situations; a misuse of clever ideas in business or military strategy; neglecting complaints about irritating noises; frustrations at not being able to convey hostility toward others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Forceful speech loses clarity or separates from effective action.
- psychology: Imagination, idealism, and vague or covert aggression.
- body/health: Witte's lisp and speech-defect statements are historical attributions, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Misleading promises, confusing argument, or anger that fails to clarify a situation.
- events/manifestations: Fantastic plans, strategic misuse of ideas, and difficulty conveying hostility.
- conflicts/notes: The ABC Neptune row ends with refinement of work conversations; youth-development work belongs to the distinct Pluto row.

## Contradictions

Strong imagination and forceful idealism coexist with vague speech, unfulfilled promises or covert aggression. ABC also names foreign-language work and refinement of work conversations as distinct practical examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Mercury/Mars as forceful thought or speech whose direction becomes unclear: imagination and ideals may be strongly expressed, yet discussion can confuse others or fail to produce action. The [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) entries support that connection through unfulfilled promises, vague argument, covert aggression, and anger that explains little. Witte also lists speech difficulties and destructive intent; these remain attributed possibilities, with no diagnostic inference.
