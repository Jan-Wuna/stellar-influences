## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 160, one-based, entry start (supplied source-evidence record; not a printed-page number).

Connection through discussion. To interfere suddenly. To be embroiled in disputes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `120`, printed page `242`):

> Gespräche tatkräftig vermitteln, Gesprächs-Verbindungen schaffen, Sprach-Verbindungen aktivieren, Tätigkeits-Verbindungen zu den Medien, über Verbindungen zur Arbeit berichten, Verbindung zur männlichen Jugend, Vermittlung von Jugend-Arbeit

Editorial translation from the German passage: Energetically facilitating conversations; creating conversational connections; activating language connections; activity connections to the media; reporting on connections to work; a connection to male youth; mediation of youth work.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Printed source page: `150`; entry code: `0479`.

#### Ebertin Entry

The realization of thoughts in association with others. Quarreling within the community.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- PDF chapter start: `42` (chapter locator; not an exact activation-page reference).

#### Sandbach Entry

Seeking out people that one can communicate with openly, and who aren’t afraid to argue. Seeking out people who provide mental stimulation. One’s evolution depends on one’s ability to learn more and more about how to constructively commu nicate and how to make arguing a positive and beneficial experience.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `152`

#### Munkasey Entry

Acquaintances with others who have an irritating style to their speech; how communications with groups of associates can initiate their activity; people with similar interests in the expression or teaching of competitive practices.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Discussion creates connections and activates shared ideas.
- psychology: Seeking mental stimulation and learning constructive disagreement.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears.
- social/relationship: Associates can realize ideas together, provoke irritation, or quarrel.
- events/manifestations: Discussion networks, group activity, and teaching competitive practices.
- conflicts/notes: Constructive exchange and community disputes are alternative outcomes; ABC's active mediation does not imply embezzlement.

## Contradictions

The entries permit shared realization of ideas and community quarrels. ABC's active mediation and Sandbach's constructive disagreement add practical ways the connection can work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Mercury/Mars through the connections people form in discussion. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) entries place shared realization of ideas beside interference and community quarrels; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) identifies the constructive task as making open disagreement beneficial. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (p. 152) adds associates whose communication initiates activity or whose interests meet in competitive teaching. The common ground is an active network of exchange, with both stimulation and friction represented.
