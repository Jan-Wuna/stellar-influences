## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 160, one-based, entry start (supplied source-evidence record; not a printed-page number).

Hasty and overly-hurried action so that one can be criticized morally. Subjectivity in telling the truth.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `120`, printed page `243`):

> Bearbeitung von Kultur-Berichten, Gesprächs-Arbeit über die Wahrheit, klare Beschreibung einer Tätigkeit, klärende Arbeits-Gespräche, Kultur-Arbeit der Medien, Kultur-Handlungen der Jugend, Meinungen über geistige Arbeit

Editorial translation from the German passage: Working on cultural reports; conversational work about truth; clear description of an activity; clarifying work conversations; cultural work by the media; cultural actions of young people; opinions about intellectual or spiritual work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action and speech assessed in relation to truth, clarity, and moral judgement.
- psychology: Subjective truth-telling and excessive haste in Witte.
- body/health: No specific body or health claim appears.
- social/relationship: Clarifying work discussions in ABC; exposure to moral criticism in Witte.
- events/manifestations: Cultural reports, media work, and descriptions of activity.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's clarity and Witte's subjectivity are materially different emphases; neither cancels the other.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis: ABC foregrounds clarity, Witte subjective truth and moral criticism. This is not evidence that either condition must occur.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Mercury/Mars through the way words and action relate to truth and judgement. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) emphasizes clear descriptions, clarifying work discussions, and cultural or intellectual work; the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) instead stresses hurried action open to moral criticism and subjectivity in telling the truth. The supported range includes an effort to clarify and the risk that haste or a partial account compromises that effort.
