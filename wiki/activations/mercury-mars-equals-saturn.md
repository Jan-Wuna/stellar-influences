## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 160, one-based, entry start (supplied source-evidence record; not a printed-page number).

Serious discussion. Depressed conversation. Quarrel resulting in separation. Death by assassination. Contemplated murder.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `120`, printed page `243`):

> Arbeits-Aufgaben besprechen, Arbeit in alten Sprachen, belastende Jugend-Arbeit, Berichte über Arbeits-Verlust, ernste Gespräche über die Arbeit, Gedanken auf Medien konzentrieren, Gespräche über Probleme in der Arbeit, verlustreiche Arbeits-Besprechungen

Editorial translation from the German passage: Discussing work tasks; work in old languages; burdensome youth work; reports of work loss; serious conversations about work; concentrating thought on media; conversations about problems at work; work discussions involving losses.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Printed source page: `149`; entry code: `0475`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Deep thinking, good powers of concentration. Inhibited powers of decision, a slow or sluggish mode of thinking. The desire to harm others, tendency to say bad things, inclination to bring harm to oneself by exaggerating, separation caused through quarreling.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- PDF chapter start: `42` (chapter locator; not an exact activation-page reference).

#### Sandbach Entry

To express one’s anger in a slow, careful manner. To fear arguments. Holding back from saying what one thinks. One needs to express one’s spiritual truth directly and openly and without fear that it may generate conflict. Assertive communication ex pressed in a cautious and/or grounded manner.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `152`

#### Munkasey Entry

Avoiding discussions about force or might; frustrations with efforts to compromise during negotiations about noise or struggles; negotiations which endure loudly for a very long time; an old argument is raised and debated.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Serious thought and argument constrained by caution or difficulty.
- psychology: Concentration can coexist with slow decisions, fear of arguments, or withholding speech.
- body/health: Witte's assassination and Ebertin's harm statements are attributed extreme historical claims, not predictions.
- social/relationship: Long negotiations, old arguments, and separation through quarrelling.
- events/manifestations: Work problems, reports of work loss, and prolonged discussion.
- conflicts/notes: Careful assertion and inhibited or harmful speech are different possibilities. Extreme violence must not dominate the general reading.

## Contradictions

Deep concentration and inhibited expression are different possibilities. ABC adds concentration on media and work discussions involving loss; the exact available Ebertin locator is supplied, while Sandbach retains a chapter-start locator.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Mercury/Mars as speech and decision made serious, slow, or difficult. The [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry) preserves the constructive side in concentration and deep thinking, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) connects caution to fear of arguments and the need to speak directly. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (p. 152) add depressed conversation, prolonged negotiations, old disputes, and separation through quarrelling. Witte's extreme violence examples remain historical source assertions, not an expected outcome of this formula.
