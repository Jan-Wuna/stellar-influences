#### ABC Entry

## Contradictions

Witte names speaking and rapid action; ABC supplies public settings in youth, media and news activity. The entries do not specify whether every quick action is effective.

## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies `Mercury/Mars = Vernal Point` through speaking and energetic action in public life. Witte supplies quick execution; ABC places discussion of activities, youth work, male youth, media creation, news work, and language activity in public settings. The shared emphasis is active communication reaching a public field, without a claim that speed itself ensures effectiveness ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
