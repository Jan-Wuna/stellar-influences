## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 160, one-based, entry start (supplied source-evidence record; not a printed-page number).

Hasty and fast action, to conceive something fast. A penetrating voice heard loud and clear. Impressive speeches.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `120`, printed page `243`):

> Einfluß der Gedanken-Tätigkeit, einflußreiche Jugend-Arbeit, kraftvolle Gedanken-Arbeit, Gedanken-Energien aktivieren, Gewalt-Aktionen der Jugend, Nachrichten über Gewalt-Handlungen, schriftliche Arbeit verstärken, Wort-Gewalt aktivieren

Editorial translation from the German passage: Influence of mental activity; influential youth work; powerful mental work; activating mental energies; violent actions by young people; news about violent acts; strengthening written work; activating verbal force.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought and expression acquire force, speed, and impact.
- psychology: Quick conception and activating mental energy.
- body/health: A penetrating voice is described; no health condition is asserted.
- social/relationship: Impressive speeches and influential youth work.
- events/manifestations: Fast action, strengthened writing, and reporting violent acts in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC distinguishes activating verbal force from separate examples of physical violence.

## Contradictions

A penetrating voice and effective writing are distinct from actual violence. ABC's Wort-Gewalt means verbal force here.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Mercury/Mars through the force with which thinking, speech, and action reach others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives rapid conception, fast action, and a penetrating voice that makes speeches impressive; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds powerful thought, influential youth work, and strengthened writing. ABC also lists youth violence and news of violent acts. Those are particular manifestations in the entry, not a reason to equate every strong message with violence.
