## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 160, one-based, entry start (supplied source-evidence record; not a printed-page number).

Exactly formulated thoughts. Tone of authority. Anger leading to violence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `120`, printed page `243`):

> Anstrengende Arbeits-Gespräche, feurige Gedanken-Tätigkeit, führende Nachrichten-Tätigkeit, Gespräche über den Willen zur Zeugung, kreative Medien-Impulse, führende Medien-Arbeit, schöpferische Jugend-Arbeit, tatkräftige Gesprächs-Führung

Editorial translation from the German passage: Strenuous work conversations; fiery mental activity; leading news activity; conversations about the will to procreate; creative media impulses; leading media work; creative youth work; energetic guidance of conversation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Precisely formulated, forceful thought and directed speech.
- psychology: An authoritative tone; anger escalating to violence in Witte.
- body/health: Procreation is a topic of discussion in ABC, not a health assertion.
- social/relationship: Leading conversation and media work; authority conveyed through words.
- events/manifestations: Creative media impulses, youth work, and strenuous work discussions.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's violence example and ABC's creative work differ in severity; neither exhausts the entry.

## Contradictions

Creative direction and the extreme anger example differ in severity. ABC specifies leading media work and energetic guidance of conversation, without implying that leadership requires violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Mercury/Mars through directed, forceful formulation: the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins exact thoughts to a tone of authority, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives creative media impulses and leadership in conversation or media work. The same Witte passage includes anger leading to violence. Precision and creative direction therefore belong beside an attributed escalation risk, without making violence an inevitable consequence of authoritative speech.
