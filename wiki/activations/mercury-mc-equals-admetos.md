## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 62, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Grave hindrances due to the own thinking. To be a blockhead. To keep serious thoughts. Melancholy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `33`, printed page `69`):

> Festigung meines Denkens, gründliche Beschreibung der Seele, ich habe Ausdauer im Denken, Kreislauf beseelter Gedanken, mein blockiertes Denken, meine Gedanken-Tiefe, meine depressiven Gedanken, Seelen-Sprache verdichten

Editorial translation from the German passage: Consolidation of my thinking; thorough description of the soul; I have endurance in thinking; a cycle of soulful thoughts; my blocked thinking; my depth of thought; my depressive thoughts; condensing the language of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: One's own thinking becomes an obstacle or a sustained inward effort.
- psychology: Serious thoughts, blocked thinking, and perseverance.
- body/health: Witte's melancholy and ABC's depressive thoughts are historical descriptions, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: No distinct relationship claim is established.
- events/manifestations: Hindrances attributed to one's thinking; consolidation and depth of thought in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: Perseverance, depth, and blocked thought are separate examples; Witte's pejorative label remains attributed.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes hindrance and melancholy; ABC also includes endurance and depth. These differ in outcome without requiring a fixed judgment of intelligence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Mercury/MC through sustained engagement with one's own thinking. Witte emphasizes serious thought that becomes an obstacle and names melancholy; ABC also preserves endurance, depth, and a cycle of soulful thoughts beside blockage and depressive thoughts. The passage therefore includes inward concentration and difficulty moving beyond one's thinking, without establishing a clinical condition or fixed intellectual capacity ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
