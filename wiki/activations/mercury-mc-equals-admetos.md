#### Witte Entry

Grave hindrances due to the own thinking. To be a blockhead. To keep serious thoughts. Melancholy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Consolidation of my thinking, grild description of the soul, I have perseverance in thinking, circularly inspired thoughts, my blocked thinking, my thought depth, my depressive thoughts, and soul language condensing

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/MC = Admetos` narrows the axis into blocked self-direction and heavy thought.
- Chart expression: grave hindrance arising from one's own thinking, melancholy, and an inward seriousness that can become dull, stubborn, or mentally immobilizing.
