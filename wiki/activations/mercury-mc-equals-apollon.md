## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 62, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Thinking about trade and traffic. Scientific chinking. Talkative. To speak several languages.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `69`; PDF page: `33`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `33`, printed page `69`):

> Erweiterung meiner Gedanken, ich denke an Ausdehnung, mein Erfolg durch Schreiben, meine Gedanken-Fülle, meine Erfahrung im Denken, meine Erfolge in der Jugend, meine Gedanken-Freiheit, wissenschaftliche Beschreibung der Seele

Editorial translation from the German passage: Expansion of my thoughts; I think of expansion; my success through writing; my abundance of thoughts; my experience in thinking; my successes in youth; my freedom of thought; scientific description of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A broad personal field of communication, commerce, and inquiry.
- psychology: Thinking about expansion, experience, and success through writing in ABC.
- body/health: No specific bodily or health statement appears.
- social/relationship: Talkativeness and speaking several languages in Witte.
- events/manifestations: Trade, traffic, writing, freedom of thought, and ABC's scientific description of the soul.
- conflicts/notes: Actual Witte PDF62 appears to print “Scientific chinking”; the phrase is retained and not silently corrected.

## Contradictions

The Witte scan retains the apparent printed typo “Scientific chinking,” which is not used as a confident new aptitude. ABC independently names a scientific description of the soul, with no further research method specified.

## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Mercury/MC through an expanded field of thought and communication. Witte names trade, traffic, talkativeness, and speaking several languages; ABC adds success through writing, abundant thoughts, experience, and freedom of thought. Its scientific description of the soul is a distinct listed subject. The supported connection is breadth of personal inquiry and expression ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
