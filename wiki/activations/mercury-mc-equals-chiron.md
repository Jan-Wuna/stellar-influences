## Derived Synthesis

Chiron specifies Mercury/MC through communication that [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes as spiritualizing ideas and helping others connect to their higher self. The organizing action is the exchange of information itself, experienced in this account as increasingly aligned with “cosmic realities.” This is a bounded summary of one metaphysical interpretation; the passage does not separately establish a medical healing claim or a career outcome.
