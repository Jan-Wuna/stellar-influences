## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 62, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Thinking about marriage or arts. Moment of news or thoughts, about art, society or family.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `33`, printed page `69`):

> Gemeinsame Seelen-Sprache, ich denke an das Ganze, ich schreibe kunstvoll, ich spreche über Gemeinschaft, mein Gemeinschafts-Denken, mein Synthese-Denken, meine Jugend-Gemeinschaft, meine Sprach-Gemeinschaft, seelisch bewegte Gemeinschaft

Editorial translation from the German passage: Shared language of the soul; I think of the whole; I write artfully; I speak about community; my community thinking; my synthesis thinking; my youth community; my language community; an inwardly moved community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal attention directed toward art, marriage, family, or community.
- psychology: Thinking about the whole and synthesis appear in the intelligible ABC phrases.
- body/health: No body or health claim appears.
- social/relationship: Thoughts and news concerning society, family, and marriage.
- events/manifestations: Art-related news and reflection; talking about community in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: The ABC row names artful writing and synthesis alongside community; it does not restrict the formula to domestic life.

## Contradictions

Artistic expression and shared community thinking are complementary settings here. The sources do not establish a particular relationship's success.

## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Mercury/MC by directing personal thought and news toward art, marriage, society, and family. ABC adds thinking about the whole, synthesis, artful writing, and a shared language of the soul. These entries connect a personal outlook with shared cultural or community activity, while leaving the outcome of any particular relationship unspecified ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
