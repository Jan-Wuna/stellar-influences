## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 62, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Sensitive mind. Emotional thinking that is changeable. Moment of public comments. Thoughts about females. Opinions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `33`, printed page `68`):

> Beschreibung der Seele einer Frau, beseeltes gemütvolles Denken, Gedanken an mein Volk, Gefühls-Sprache der Seele, ich denke an die Frau, ich bin eine junge Frau, ich spreche als Frau, meine Frau spricht, meine Gefühls-Sprache, Seelen-Sprache der Frau

Editorial translation from the German passage: Description of a woman's soul; soulful emotional thinking; thoughts of my people; the soul's language of feeling; I think of the woman; I am a young woman; I speak as a woman; my wife speaks; my language of feeling; the woman's language of the soul.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source locator: printed p. 173, coded entry 0568.

Purposeful thinking dictated by one's inner feelings. Expressing one's opinions about the female sex, communion of thoughts with women.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 53, one-based, chapter start only; exact entry page unverified.

Being vulnerable to the ideas and thinking of authority figures. The emotional need to find basic ideas that will provide one security.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `184`

#### Munkasey Entry

The uncertainty and vacillation that creeps into your assessment of how life treats you; using intuition and impressions of life rather than study, work, and research to grow as a person; inconsistent thoughts on maturing.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal judgement and direction strongly shaped by feeling.
- psychology: Sensitivity, changeability, and a need for ideas that provide security.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears; ABC describes a woman's soul, not anatomy.
- social/relationship: Receptivity to authority figures and historical examples involving women.
- events/manifestations: Public comments and shifting assessments of personal growth.
- conflicts/notes: The historical female examples remain attributed; feelings and personal direction form the common theme.

## Contradictions

Purposeful feeling-based thought differs from Munkasey's vacillation and reliance on impressions. ABC's soul-language examples add no anatomical claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Mercury/MC through feeling as a basis for personal thought and direction. Witte and Ebertin describe sensitivity and purposeful thinking guided by inner feeling; Sandbach adds vulnerability to authority and a search for secure ideas. Munkasey makes vacillation and reliance on impressions explicit. ABC preserves the inner and historically female language of the entry, including a description of a woman's soul, without supplying a bodily condition ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
