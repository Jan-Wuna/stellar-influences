## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 62, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Physical agility. Thoughts about one’s own body. To philosophize.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source locator: printed p. 68, PDF p. 33, entry (supplied German OCR; not visually verified).

Thoughts about body and soul, talk to my husband, I think of my husband, my personal thinking, my husband speaks, my thinking center, my personal youth, personal soul description, and language of body and soul

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source locator: printed p. 173, coded entry 0567.

A harmonious relationship between body, soul and spirit; tackling life's problems, forming one's mental attitude and philosophy of life. Reflecting on one's health, the study of human nature.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 54, exact oriented entry checked against the actual raw scan; chapter starts at PDF p. 53.

To develop a command or mastery of communicating in your career, or of com municating spiritual ideas. The success of one’s goals depend on one’s ability to be flexible and open to the reception of spiritual ideas.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `184`

#### Munkasey Entry

The prominence and renown you achieve thru hard work and your personal will and determination to develop into a better person; the confidence you build in yourself as you have matured; a positive picture of self.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal outlook connected to body, philosophy, and self-development.
- psychology: Confidence and a coherent attitude to life's problems.
- body/health: Witte mentions agility; Ebertin describes reflection on health and body-soul-spirit harmony, not a clinical result.
- social/relationship: Communicating in a career or expressing spiritual ideas in Sandbach.
- events/manifestations: Personal development and recognition through work in Munkasey.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's husband examples are historically gendered; no source here establishes excessive self-reference.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC's references to a husband remain source-specific examples; Sandbach's exact Sun entry is verified at PDF page 54.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Mercury/MC through a personal outlook that includes the body and a philosophy of life. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) entries connect bodily reflection to thinking about life's problems; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds mastery in career or spiritual communication, with openness to ideas as a condition. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (p. 184) gives the theme a developmental expression in confidence and recognition through hard work. These are the entry's stated possibilities, not a guarantee of health or renown.
