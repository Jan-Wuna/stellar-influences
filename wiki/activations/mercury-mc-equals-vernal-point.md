## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 62, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

The personal opinion about the world in general. Judgement about common things. Opinion. Reputation. The minute of news for the general world.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `33`, printed page `68`):

> Beseelte Jugend in der Öffentlichkeit, ich im öffentlichen Verkehr, ich schreibe für die Öffentlichkeit, ich spreche in der Öffentlichkeit, meine Jugend in der Öffentlichkeit, seelische Bewegung in der Öffentlichkeit

Editorial translation from the German passage: Soulful youth in public; I am in public traffic; I write for the public; I speak in public; my youth in public; inner movement in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal opinion expressed about the world at large.
- psychology: Judgement about common things in Witte.
- body/health: No body or health claim appears.
- social/relationship: Public writing and reputation.
- events/manifestations: News reaching the general world and public commentary.
- conflicts/notes: The sources specify a public subject or audience without measuring the influence or speed of distribution.

## Contradictions

Witte's opinion and reputation themes and ABC's public writing or speaking are compatible settings. Neither specifies how influential the message becomes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Mercury/MC through one's opinions about the wider world. Witte connects judgment about common things with reputation and news for the general public; ABC adds writing, speaking, youth, and inward movement in public life. The entry establishes a public subject or audience for personal thought while leaving its influence and speed of circulation unspecified ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
