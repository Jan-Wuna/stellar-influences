## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 62, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Good comprehension. A quick mind. An abundance of ideas. One’s own power of thought. To make strong comments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source locator: printed p. 69, PDF p. 33, entry (supplied German OCR; not visually verified).

Influence of animated thoughts, influence of my thoughts, I write about violence, I think of violence, powerful language of the soul, my energetic speaking, my thought energy, my thoughts are strong, and my strength in youth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Good comprehension, a quick mind, abundant ideas, and emphatic personal comment.
- psychology: Mental energy and strong thinking.
- body/health: No body or health claim appears.
- social/relationship: Strong comments and energetic speaking.
- events/manifestations: Influential thoughts; writing or thinking about violence in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: Writing or thinking about violence is a named subject, not evidence of a violent act.

## Contradictions

Good comprehension and quick thinking are distinct Witte clauses. ABC's thought about violence supplies subject matter rather than a behavioral prediction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Mercury/MC through the strength and speed of personal thinking. Witte describes good comprehension, a quick mind, abundant ideas, one's power of thought, and strong comments; ABC adds energetic speech and influential thoughts. Writing or thinking about violence is a separate named subject, so it does not establish an act of violence ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
