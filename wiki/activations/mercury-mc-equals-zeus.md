## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 62, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Self control. To exercise self-restraint. Forethought.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `33`, printed page `69`):

> Feurige Seelen-Sprache, ich beginne zu schreiben, ich führe meine Gedanken, ich führe die Jugend, ich denke kreativ, meine Anstrengung beim Denken, meine Gedanken-Richtung, meine Leistungen in der Jugend, meine sprachliche Leistung, meine Wort-Schöpfungen

Editorial translation from the German passage: Fiery language of the soul; I begin to write; I direct my thoughts; I lead young people; I think creatively; my effort in thinking; my direction of thought; my achievements in youth; my linguistic achievement; my word creations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal thinking directed through forethought and self-restraint.
- psychology: Self-control alongside effortful, creative thinking in ABC.
- body/health: No body or health claim appears.
- social/relationship: ABC names leading young people, alongside directing one's own thoughts.
- events/manifestations: Beginning to write, directing thoughts, and creative expression.
- conflicts/notes: Self-restraint, creative thinking and linguistic creation are distinct but compatible themes.

## Contradictions

Witte's self-restraint and ABC's fiery or creative expression are compatible possibilities. Leading young people is explicit, but the passage does not assign a formal office.

## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Mercury/MC through forethought and deliberate mental direction. Witte is explicit about self-control and restraint; ABC adds beginning to write, directing one's thoughts, creative thinking, and word creations. Leading young people is another stated example. The connected theme is giving thought and expression a direction, while intensity and restraint remain distinct qualities within the entry ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
