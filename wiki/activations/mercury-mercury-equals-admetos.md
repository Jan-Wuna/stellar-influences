## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 158, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Serious or limited thinking. Perseverance in thinking. Profound thinker. Profound concentration of thoughts. Thoughts, news or information of death, grief, standstill, hindrance. Pessimist. End of a motion. Negotiations which are a dead issue. Ponderous, slow and sluggish.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought concentrated or limited to the point of standstill.
- psychology: Perseverance, profound concentration, pessimism, and sluggishness.
- body/health: Death and grief occur as subjects of information, not a health prediction.
- social/relationship: Negotiations that have become a dead issue.
- events/manifestations: News about hindrance or standstill and the end of a motion.
- conflicts/notes: Depth of concentration and restricted thinking are both stated; one source supplies the range.

## Contradictions

Only the Witte entry is represented. Its listed alternatives do not establish independent corroboration or a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Mercury/Mercury through thought that persists but may scarcely move. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) puts profound concentration and perseverance beside limited, pessimistic, or sluggish thinking, then carries the same stoppage into negotiations that have become a dead issue. Information about death or grief is also listed as subject matter. This one entry supports both depth and standstill; neither should erase the other.
