## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 158, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

High flight of thought. Scientific thinking. Many talks. Much speaking. Knowledge of languages. Scientific languages. Business talks. Thoughts concentrated on commerce and earnings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Extensive thought and communication in scientific or commercial fields.
- psychology: High flights of thought and concentration on commerce or earnings.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Many talks and business discussions.
- events/manifestations: Languages, scientific language, and ideas concerning trade.
- conflicts/notes: The entry offers a range of intellectual subjects, without asserting equal mastery of them all.

## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Mercury/Mercury through the breadth and subject matter of thinking and speaking. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins many talks and knowledge of languages to scientific thought and commercial discussion, including earnings. The connecting theme is extended intellectual exchange across specialized fields, within one source rather than a claim of universal expertise.
