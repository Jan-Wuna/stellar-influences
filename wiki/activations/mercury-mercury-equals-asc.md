## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 157, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Talks, conversations and correspondence. To be the theme of conversation with others. Other people think of the native. News from other people. News about the closer environment. Exchange of ideas with acquaintances.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought and news exchanged with the immediate environment.
- psychology: Being the object of others' thoughts as well as thinking with them.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Conversation, correspondence, and exchange with acquaintances.
- events/manifestations: News from other people and about nearby surroundings.
- conflicts/notes: The entry includes being talked about, without specifying praise, criticism, or reputation.

## Contradictions

Only the Witte entry is represented. Its listed alternatives do not establish independent corroboration or a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Mercury/Mercury through exchange with people nearby. In the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), conversation and correspondence carry ideas and news between acquaintances, while the person can also become the subject of someone else's conversation. This two-way movement is the specific relation supported here; the passage does not say whether the attention is favorable.
