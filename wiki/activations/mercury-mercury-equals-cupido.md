## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Mercury/Mercury through the subjects and settings of shared cultural and family life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects social conversation and speech with music, artistic taste, family concerns, and marriage intentions. It supports thinking and talking about these matters, without establishing that an intention becomes a marriage or that taste becomes artistic achievement.
