## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 158, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

To err or make mistakes. To be silent with shame. To be sad or laconic. Hidden thoughts. Ugly thinking. Stupid, malicious, drastic way of expression. Old plans, information or conversations. Old movements. The worried mind. Negative dialogues. Bad or sad news.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thinking and communication burdened by error, worry, or painful information.
- psychology: Shame, hidden thoughts, sadness, and a worried mind.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Silence, malicious expression, and negative dialogue.
- events/manifestations: Bad or sad news, old plans, and earlier conversations.
- conflicts/notes: Old material is not necessarily malicious; pejorative source language remains attributed.

## Contradictions

Only the Witte entry is represented. Its listed alternatives do not establish independent corroboration or a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Mercury/Mercury through difficulty in thought and expression. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ranges from mistakes, worried thinking, and shame-related silence to malicious speech and sad news. Old plans or conversations form a separate strand concerning earlier material. This single-source range should not be compressed into a verdict that all private thought, silence, or attention to the past is malign.
