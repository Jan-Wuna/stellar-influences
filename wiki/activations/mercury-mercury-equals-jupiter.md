## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 157, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Cheerful mood. Liking to speak. Fluent speech. Talent for speech. Talkativeness. Joyful thinking or speech. Positive dialogues, thoughts or news. The legal mind. Financial reports. Legal correspondence. Thoughts or news about money.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Fluent and cheerful communication, including legal and financial subjects.
- psychology: Joyful thought, a liking for speaking, and verbal talent.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Positive dialogues and correspondence.
- events/manifestations: Financial reports, legal letters, and news about money.
- conflicts/notes: A favorable tone does not establish financial gain or a successful legal result.

## Contradictions

Only the Witte entry is represented. Its listed alternatives do not establish independent corroboration or a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Mercury/Mercury through a cheerful, fluent mode of thought and speech in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). Positive dialogue and verbal talent sit beside concrete subjects: law, financial reports, legal correspondence, and money news. The entry names a style of exchange and its possible content; it does not turn a report about money into a promise of profit.
