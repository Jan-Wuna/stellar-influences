## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Mercury/Mercury through thought carrying elevated or official authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links honorable thinking and great ideas to spiritual leadership, then gives institutional forms in decrees, official letters, and court decisions. The organizing relation is authority expressed in ideas or documents, with Witte's leadership claim kept within this single-source account.
