## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 157, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Energetic thought. Hasty and energetic reaction. Malice. Quarrelsome thoughts and debates. Critics. Criticism. To excite oneself mentally. Argument. Activity of the mind. Power of thought. Clear, sharp thinking. Disputes.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Energetic, sharp thought expressed as criticism or argument.
- psychology: Hasty reactions, mental excitement, and power of thought.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Debates, criticism, disputes, and the source possibility of malice.
- events/manifestations: Quick mental reaction and active argument.
- conflicts/notes: Clear thinking and quarrelsome thought are both present; neither is inevitable.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Mercury/Mercury through energetic mental activity that can become sharp analysis or conflict. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places clear thinking and mental power beside haste, criticism, disputes, and malice. The shared feature is an active, forceful response, while the quality of that response ranges from clarity to quarrelling in this one entry.
