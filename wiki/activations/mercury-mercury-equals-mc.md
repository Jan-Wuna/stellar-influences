## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Mercury/Mercury through the ownership and direction of thinking. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) begins with one's own observation, meditation, and self-talk, and also names business thoughts, professional news, and media work. These strands connect personal mental activity to its possible professional setting, without making private reflection and public communication interchangeable.
