### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 157, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Motion of the brain. Thinking capacity. The memory. The imaginative power. Superficial thinking. The young generation. Young girl. Young woman. Several hours. Office hours. Vehicles. Shifted hour. Lively and changeable train of thoughts. The general opinion in relation to the hour and female persons.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Memory, imagination, and a lively, changeable train of thought.
- psychology: Thinking capacity and the source possibility of superficial thinking.
- body/health: “Motion of the brain” is historical source wording, not a neurological account.
- social/relationship: Young generations and female persons are historical associations.
- events/manifestations: Office hours, shifted hours, vehicles, and general opinion in relation to the hour.
- conflicts/notes: The source lists temporal and gendered associations without explaining a mechanism; do not invent one.

## Contradictions

Only the Witte entry is represented. Its listed alternatives do not establish independent corroboration or a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Mercury/Mercury through memory, imaginative power, and changeable thinking in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The same passage lists office hours, shifted hours, vehicles, and general opinion in relation to the hour, preserving a concrete time-and-movement strand without explaining its mechanism. Youth and female persons remain historical source associations, rather than universal gender claims or a medical theory of the brain.
