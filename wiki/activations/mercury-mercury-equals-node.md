#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 157, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Connections of thoughts. Correspondence. To make new connection. Intellectual or communications ties. Contacts or connections for communication.

## Comparative Schema

- psychology: No separate personality claim is supplied.
- body/health: No body or health claim is supplied.

- events/manifestations: Establishing channels or connections for exchange.

## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Mercury/Mercury through the ties that allow thought to be exchanged. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names correspondence, new connections, and intellectual or communication contacts. Its limited claim is that communication connects; it does not specify the closeness, harmony, or permanence of the resulting relationship.
