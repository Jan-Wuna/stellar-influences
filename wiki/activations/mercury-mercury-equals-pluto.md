## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 158, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

The function of the nerves. External and internal motion. The development of the thinking. From thinking to understanding. To develop or transform a thought. Thinking and perception is one thing. The development or change of the opinion, of the outlook on life, or of the understanding.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thinking develops into understanding or a changed outlook.
- psychology: Developing, transforming, or revising a thought or opinion.
- body/health: Witte's nerve-function statement is historical doctrine, not a physiological conclusion.
- social/relationship: No specific interpersonal claim is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Internal or external motion and change in one's understanding of life.
- conflicts/notes: The passage does not explicitly add pressure, trauma, or compulsion to that development.

## Contradictions

Only the Witte entry is represented. Its listed alternatives do not establish independent corroboration or a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies Mercury/Mercury through the development of a thought into understanding and the possibility of revising an opinion or outlook. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) also names internal and external motion and the function of the nerves, which remain attributed source associations. The connective interpretation is change in thinking and perception; it does not require an invented story of pressure or nervous reorganization.
