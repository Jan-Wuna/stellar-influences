## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 157, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Logic. Philosophical thinking. Competence to form an opinion, to pass judgement. Thoughts of separation. Parting to travel. To ponder. Deliberation. Impaired thinking. Serious conversations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Deliberate judgement and serious conversation.
- psychology: Logic, philosophical thought, pondering, and the source possibility of impaired thinking.
- body/health: “Impaired thinking” is historical wording without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Serious conversations and thoughts of separation.
- events/manifestations: Parting to travel and forming or passing judgement.
- conflicts/notes: Competence and impairment are both listed possibilities; the source does not resolve them into one fixed condition.

## Contradictions

Only the Witte entry is represented. Its listed alternatives do not establish independent corroboration or a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Mercury/Mercury through the effort to form a considered opinion. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins logic and philosophical thinking to deliberation and serious conversation, while also retaining impaired thinking as a different possibility. Separation and departing to travel are concrete subjects in the same entry, without proving that every serious conversation ends in parting.
