### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 157, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

A body in motion. Young body. The head. The nerves. Consciousness. Thoughts in general. Youth. The thinking faculty. The mobility of the body. News of the day.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thinking and consciousness associated with bodily motion and youth.
- psychology: The thinking faculty and thoughts in general.
- body/health: Head, nerves, and a moving or young body are historical source associations, not clinical findings.
- social/relationship: Youth is named, without a distinct relationship claim.
- events/manifestations: Bodily mobility and news of the day.
- conflicts/notes: Nerves are listed; the entry does not establish nervous sensitivity or a physiological mechanism.

## Contradictions

Only the Witte entry is represented. Its listed alternatives do not establish independent corroboration or a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Mercury/Mercury through consciousness and thought in association with the moving body. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names the head, nerves, thinking faculty, youth, and bodily mobility, and separately includes news of the day. These remain the scope of one source: the entry does not explain a medical mechanism or establish the extra claim of nervous sensitivity.
