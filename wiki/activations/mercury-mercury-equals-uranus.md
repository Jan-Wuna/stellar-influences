## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 157, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Nerves. Practical thinking. Logic. Good mathematician. Revolutionary thoughts. Original. Rhythm. Telegrams. New information. Sudden inspiration. Surprising mails. Sudden news or information. Excited way of speaking. Technological mindset. The main axis for astrologers, astronomers, technicians or mathematicians.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Practical, original, or technical thinking and sudden information.
- psychology: Logic, mathematical thinking, inspiration, and excited speech.
- body/health: Nerves are a historical association, not a described nervous-system condition.
- social/relationship: Surprising mail and sudden news.
- events/manifestations: Technology, rhythm, telegrams, and the source's occupational associations.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's “main axis” claim for occupations is attributed doctrine, not a guarantee of profession.

## Contradictions

Only the Witte entry is represented. Its listed alternatives do not establish independent corroboration or a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies Mercury/Mercury through practical and original thought combined with unexpected information. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects logic, mathematics, and a technological mindset to sudden inspiration, surprising mail, and excited speech. It also names astrologers, astronomers, technicians, and mathematicians as occupational associations. These are Witte's claims within one entry, without an added account of how a nervous system is “tuned” for insight.
