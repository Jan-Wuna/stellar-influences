## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 157, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

General thinking. General news. General opinion. General motion. Media and information in the general public. The youth in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thinking, news, and information in public life.
- psychology: General opinion is named; no individual personality profile is supplied.
- body/health: No body or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: A general public audience and youth in public life.
- events/manifestations: Public media, information, and general motion.
- conflicts/notes: The source does not establish the speed, credibility, or reception of public information.

## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Mercury/Mercury at the level of public thought and information. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names general opinion, news, motion, media, and youth in public life. Its bounded theme is the public field in which thought and information circulate; it does not establish that a particular message is influential, accurate, or rapidly received.
