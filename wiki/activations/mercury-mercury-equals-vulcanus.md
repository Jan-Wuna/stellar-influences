## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 158, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Mighty exerted thinking. The force of thought. The power of thoughts. Great speed. Mighty and fast motion. Sports that require speed. The dominant mind. Strong opinion. Very important news, ideas, plans or messages. Forceful words.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful mental effort and forceful communication.
- psychology: Strong opinion and a dominant mind.
- body/health: Sports requiring speed appear; no health condition is asserted.
- social/relationship: Forceful words and dominant opinion.
- events/manifestations: Great speed, fast motion, and important news, plans, or messages.
- conflicts/notes: Important content and force of delivery are stated; successful persuasion is not guaranteed.

## Contradictions

Only the Witte entry is represented. Its listed alternatives do not establish independent corroboration or a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Mercury/Mercury through force and speed in thought, motion, and messages. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins exerted thinking and strong opinion to forceful words and important news or plans, while separately naming sports that require speed. The connected theme is intensity of effort and delivery, rather than an assurance that a strong opinion is correct or convincing.
