## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source locator: PDF p. 158, one-based, entry start (not a printed-page number).

Creative thinking. To command. To threaten. Dictatorial. Inventions. Thinking that aims at something. Creative inventions. An order that was obeyed. Single minded thinking. To issue decrees or laws. To pass sentence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Purposeful, creative thought expressed through invention or command.
- psychology: Single-minded thinking and an aim.
- body/health: No body or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Commands, threats, and dictatorial expression in Witte.
- events/manifestations: Inventions, obeyed orders, decrees, laws, and sentences.
- conflicts/notes: Creative invention and coercive command are distinct manifestations, not moral equivalents.

## Contradictions

Only the Witte entry is represented. Its listed alternatives do not establish independent corroboration or a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Mercury/Mercury through thinking directed at an aim. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) carries that purpose into invention and into commands that are obeyed, including decrees, laws, and sentences. Threats and dictatorial behavior are also listed. Direction and effect connect the examples, while creative production and coercion remain separate possibilities within this one source.
