## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `164`.

#### Witte Entry

To adopt one’s thinking to changed conditions. Elimination of intuition or to search into special fields through intuition.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `251`; PDF page: `124`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `124`, printed page `251`):

> Auflösung von Depressionen der Jugend, Ausdauer der Jugend im Wasser, Blockade des Luft-Verkehrs, deprimierende Berichte vom Wasser, fremde Sprachen vertiefen, Gedanken-Blockade auflösen, Grund-Wasser-Bewegung, schwache Gedanken festigen

Editorial translation from the German passage: Dissolution of depression among young people; young people's endurance in water; blockage of air traffic; depressing reports from water; deepening knowledge of foreign languages; releasing a mental blockage; groundwater movement; consolidating weak thoughts.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Adapting thought, restricting intuition, or directing it into a particular field ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: ABC includes deepening language study and releasing mental blockage.
- body/health: ABC's depression language remains attributed historical material.
- social/relationship: No distinct interpersonal claim is established.
- events/manifestations: Blocked air travel, water reports, groundwater movement, and sustained activity in water are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Eliminating intuition and specializing through intuition are alternatives.

## Contradictions

Witte permits either eliminating intuition or using it in a special field. ABC likewise includes blockage and its release; these do not support a uniformly restrictive interpretation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Neptune = Admetos`, Admetos specifies intuitive or uncertain thinking through adjustment and a narrower field of work. Witte permits both setting intuition aside and investigating a special field through it. ABC adds deepening foreign-language knowledge, releasing blocked thought, and consolidating weak thoughts; blocked air transport and groundwater movement remain separate practical examples. Concentration and release therefore belong beside restriction ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
