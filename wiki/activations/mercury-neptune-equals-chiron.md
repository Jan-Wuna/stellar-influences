#### Sandbach Entry

To help others to become more expansive and open in their thinking, and especially more open to the wisdom and knowledge that is everywhere around them and always ready to be accessed. To help others to overcome their mental confusion by transmuting it into clear intuition.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Helping thought move from confusion toward clear intuition ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- psychology: Openness to surrounding knowledge is emphasized.
- body/health: No bodily condition or medical treatment is specified.
- social/relationship: Helping other people become more receptive is the stated relationship.
- events/manifestations: The passage names clearer intuitive understanding as the intended change.
- conflicts/notes: This is one source's interpretive process, not a guarantee of reliable intuition.

## Contradictions

Only Sandbach is represented. The passage describes help with confused thinking, without specifying a medical healing process or independent test of intuitive accuracy.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Neptune = Chiron`, Chiron specifies intuitive or confused thinking through helping others become more open and expansive. Sandbach describes access to surrounding wisdom and a transformation of confusion into clear intuition. The connected process is opening understanding so that what was unclear becomes usable within the source's intuitive account; no bodily cure or guaranteed accuracy is established ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
