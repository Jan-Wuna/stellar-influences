### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `164`.

#### Witte Entry

Unfortunate thoughts of marriage. Sad thoughts for dissolution of a family. Society swindle. Marriage swindler.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `251`; PDF page: `124`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `124`, printed page `251`):

> Bewegung einer Gemeinschaft in der Luft, feinfühlende Gesprächs-Gemeinschaft, Gedanken einer Gemeinschaft zur Zukunft, gemeinsame Gedanken zur Metaphysik, Luft-Verkehrs-Gemeinschaft, unklare Gespräche in der Gemeinschaft, verunsicherte Jugend-Gemeinschaft

Editorial translation from the German passage: Movement of a community through the air; a sensitive conversational community; a community's thoughts about the future; shared thoughts about metaphysics; an air-transport community; unclear conversations within a community; an unsettled youth community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertain or sensitive communication in family and community ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Sad thoughts about marriage or family dissolution are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition appears.
- social/relationship: Witte names social or marriage fraud; ABC also names a sensitive conversation group.
- events/manifestations: Community future plans, metaphysical ideas, and air-transport groups are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Sensitivity, uncertainty and fraud are distinct; not every group is deceptive.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes unfortunate marriage thoughts and fraud. ABC also permits a sensitive conversational community and shared metaphysical ideas, alongside unclear conversation and insecurity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Neptune = Cupido`, Cupido specifies intuitive or uncertain exchange within family or community. Witte's sad marriage thoughts, family dissolution, and fraud form a difficult strand; ABC adds sensitive group conversation and common ideas about metaphysics or the future. These sources therefore include shared sensitivity and uncertain or deceptive collective exchange, without equating the two ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
