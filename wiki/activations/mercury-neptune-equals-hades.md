## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `164`.

#### Witte Entry

Unclear thoughts cause obstacles and unpleasantness. To plot dirty tricks.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `251`; PDF page: `124`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `124`, printed page `251`):

> An Gefahr in der Luft denken, Bewegungs-Mangel in der Luft, Gedanken über zukünftige Entbehrungen, fremde kriminelle Jugend, fremde Not beschreiben, gefährlicher Verkehr auf dem Wasser, Meinungen zu Vergangenheit und Zukunft, Nachteile durch unklares Denken

Editorial translation from the German passage: Thinking of danger in the air; lack of movement in the air; thoughts about future deprivation; foreign criminal youth; describing foreign need; dangerous traffic on water; opinions about past and future; disadvantages through unclear thinking.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Confused thought producing obstacles or underhanded plans ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: Witte describes plotting dirty tricks; ABC adds disadvantage through unclear thinking and thoughts of future deprivation.
- body/health: ABC names danger and deprivation but establishes no distinct medical claim.
- social/relationship: ABC includes foreign criminal youth and describing need experienced elsewhere.
- events/manifestations: Danger in air or water transport and opinions about past and future are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Obstruction through confusion and deliberate dirty plotting are distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap on difficulty from unclear thinking. Witte additionally describes deliberate plotting; ABC's danger, deprivation and transport examples do not by themselves establish that intention.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Neptune = Hades`, Hades specifies the axis through the consequences of unclear thought: obstacles, unpleasantness and disadvantage. Witte also allows deliberate dirty plotting, which should remain distinct from confusion. ABC broadens the picture to future deprivation, foreign need and danger in air or water transport; these concrete examples do not imply that every uncertain thought is malicious ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
