## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Lucky (fortunate) imagination. To build “castles in the air”.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Movement joy in the water, successful air traffic, success reports about water, successful speech about metaphysics, thoughts about the future of money, weak youth law, and uncertainty through speech about money

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A rich imagination and great powers of fantasy, stepped-up powers of imagination. Poet, actor. The inclination to harbor great hopes.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One who has an abundant and varied imagination. A great enthusiasm for ide alistic concepts. Success through being guided by spiritual forces and spirit guides.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased dreams and visions on how the future should be; insight into using meditative faculties to understand various problems which trouble you; elusive thoughts about an ideal society; new patterns of metaphysics.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Neptune = Jupiter`: mythic or inspirational language, charitable ideas, and the ability to move others through vision rather than literal argument. Main caution: inflated ambiguity: ideals can become grand, credulous, or too diffuse to test carefully.
