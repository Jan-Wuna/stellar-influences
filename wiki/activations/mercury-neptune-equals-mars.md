## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To materialize inventive ideas. Genius.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work in air traffic, media work on metaphysics, news activity in the air, weak thought work, activity in foreign language, activity in the W asser Y return, insecure actions in the youth, and discuss inconvenient actions

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Thinking with a purpose, great powers of imagination (the disposition of a genius). The realization of plans or inventions, acting according to deliberation and experience.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Taking action to realize one’s vision. Actions that are ineffective due to wavering, lack of focus, or confusion. To act with subtlety, and/or the ability act gently or quietly, with a lightness of touch.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Energizes your dreams and ideas, and breathes real life into them; added confusion about how to mentally prepare self for exercise or combat; anxieties which may reflect themselves in dreams about violence or anger.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Neptune = Mars`: artistic initiative, courageous sensitivity, and the ability to act on subtle perception or symbolic insight. Main caution: misdirected force: thought and action can combine into confusion, evasive struggle, or argument driven more by impression than clarity.
