## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Neptune = Neptune`, Neptune increases dreaminess in reasoning while opening an unfamiliar route to a problem. Munkasey's central distinction is between how impractical a solution first appears and the underlying basis it may contain. This repeated-pair activation therefore supports considering a new approach without treating every dreamy idea as a sound solution ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
