## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To develop an intuitive or mendacious disposition. Subconscious development. To recognize that one has made an error. To see through false pretences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development of language, growing expectations of young people, change of youth through fine tuning, and poor news development

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Unusual actions originating in the subconscious mind, tendency to draw highly upon nervous energy.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One is relentlessly pursued by thoughts coming from the spiritual realms, and which need to be listened to and heeded - or if they are not, stress will be the result. A tremendous ability to read between the lines when one hears what others have to say. An exceptionally psychic person.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

An obsession with using of psychic or dream material as a means for determining physical reality; a termination of contacts with the spiritual realms; learning and practicing purification rituals to rid self of mystical influences.
