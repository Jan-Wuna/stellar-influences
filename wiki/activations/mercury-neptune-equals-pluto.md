## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `164`.

#### Witte Entry

To develop an intuitive or mendacious disposition. Subconscious development. To recognize that one has made an error. To see through false pretences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `251`; PDF page: `124`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `124`, printed page `251`):

> Enttäuschender Wort-Wechsel, Entwicklung in einer fremden Sprache, Meinungen über Metaphysik entwickeln, unklare Gesprächs-Entwicklung, wachsende Erwartungen der Jugend, Wandlung der Jugend durch Feingefühl, zukünftige Nachrichten-Entwicklung

Editorial translation from the German passage: A disappointing exchange of words; development in a foreign language; developing opinions about metaphysics; unclear development of a conversation; growing expectations of youth; transformation of youth through sensitivity; future development of news.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Printed source page: `161`; entry code: `0526`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Unusual actions originating in the subconscious mind, tendency to draw highly upon nervous energy.

Source footnote 29 (attached to entry `0526`): Mercury/Neptune = Pluto has been found in the case of exceptional actors.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- PDF chapter start: `49` (chapter locator; not an exact activation-page reference).

#### Sandbach Entry

One is relentlessly pursued by thoughts coming from the spiritual realms, and which need to be listened to and heeded - or if they are not, stress will be the result. A tremendous ability to read between the lines when one hears what others have to say. An exceptionally psychic person.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `168`

#### Munkasey Entry

An obsession with using of psychic or dream material as a means for determining physical reality; a termination of contacts with the spiritual realms; learning and practicing purification rituals to rid self of mystical influences.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Developing intuition or falsehood, with pressure from subconscious material.
- psychology: Witte includes recognizing error and seeing through pretence; Munkasey includes obsession with psychic or dream material.
- body/health: Ebertin's high use of nervous energy and Sandbach's stress are attributed historical claims.
- social/relationship: Sandbach describes reading between the lines of others' speech.
- events/manifestations: ABC includes foreign-language development and changing youth expectations; Ebertin's footnote mentions exceptional actors.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's sustained spiritual contact differs from Munkasey's termination of that contact.

## Contradictions

Intuitive development does not have a uniformly affirmative direction. Witte permits a mendacious disposition, Sandbach describes persistent spiritual impressions, and Munkasey also permits ending spiritual contact or seeking release from it.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Neptune = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the axis through development and pressure from subconscious or psychic material. Witte permits either intuition or a mendacious disposition, but also recognizing an error and seeing through false pretences. Sandbach's persistent impressions and ability to read between the lines coexist with Munkasey's obsession or wish to end spiritual contact. The distinction is between developing discernment and becoming governed by the material; therapeutic communication and coercion are not required claims ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)). ABC adds changing conversation, language and youth expectations ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
