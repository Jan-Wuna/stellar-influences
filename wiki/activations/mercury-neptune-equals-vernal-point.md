### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `250`; PDF page: `124`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `124`, printed page `250`):

> Fremde Sprachen im öffentlichen Leben, Gespräche über Wasser in der Öffentlichkeit, öffentlicher Luft-Verkehr, unklare Meinungen in der Öffentlichkeit, Zukunft der Jugend im öffentlichen Leben, Zukunfts-Gespräche in der Öffentlichkeit

Editorial translation from the German passage: Foreign languages in public life; public conversations about water; public air transport; unclear public opinions; youth's future in public life; public conversations about the future.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: General receptivity, uncertainty or nonsense ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: ABC includes unclear opinions in public.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is established.
- social/relationship: Foreign languages and public discussion of water or the future are ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: Public air transport and youth's future in public life are separate manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: Receptivity and nonsense are alternatives, not proof of accurate perception.

## Contradictions

Witte allows receptivity alongside uncertainty and nonsense. ABC's public settings specify where these themes appear without resolving their accuracy.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Neptune = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point gives the axis a general or public setting. Witte retains receptivity beside uncertainty and nonsense; ABC makes the setting concrete through foreign languages, unclear public opinions, public air transport and conversations about water or the future. The central distinction is between openness to impressions and clarity of what is publicly understood or said ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
