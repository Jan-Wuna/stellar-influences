## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `164`.

#### Witte Entry

Mighty intuitive accomplishments magnified by ingeniousness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `251`; PDF page: `124`.

#### ABC Entry

Reports of the violence of water, reports of air power, influence of metaphysics in thought, influential thoughts on the future, violence in air traffic, insecurity through youth violence, refined thought force, and strong air traffic

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful intuitive accomplishment joined with ingenuity ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: ABC includes refined mental force and influential thoughts about the future.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is established.
- social/relationship: ABC includes insecurity caused by youth violence, distinct from mental strength.
- events/manifestations: Water's force, air power and heavy air traffic are separate ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Intellectual power must not be equated with the source's literal violence or physical-force examples.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes accomplishment. ABC separately includes destructive force and insecurity through youth violence; these remain beside the constructive mental expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Neptune = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the axis through intuitive accomplishment made powerful by ingenuity. ABC supports a mental expression through refined thought force, metaphysical influence and influential ideas about the future. Its reports of water's force, air power and youth violence describe separate concrete manifestations. Mental effectiveness and literal physical force therefore remain distinct ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
