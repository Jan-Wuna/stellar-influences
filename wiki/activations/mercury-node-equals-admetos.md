## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `141`.

#### Witte Entry

Deep, sad thoughts. Definite severing of connections, of a trend of thoughts. Something that is definitely ended.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `209`; PDF page: `103`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `103`, printed page `209`):

> Blockierte Verkehrs-Verbindungen, depressive Gesprächs-Verbindung, eingeschränkte Nachrichten-Verbindung, Festigung der Meinung in Verbindungen, Gedanken-Blockade in einer Verbindung, gründliche Medien-Kontakte, Vertiefung der Beziehung zur Jugend

Editorial translation from the German passage: Blocked transport connections; a depressive conversational connection; a restricted news connection; consolidation of opinion in connections; a mental blockage in a connection; thorough media contacts; deepening the relationship with youth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Deep or sad thought and a definite ending of a connection ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: ABC adds mental blockage and consolidation of opinion.
- body/health: ABC's depressive wording remains attributed; no diagnosis is established.
- social/relationship: Severed ties coexist with deepening relations to youth and thorough media contacts.
- events/manifestations: Blocked transport links and restricted news connections are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC permits consolidation or deepening as well as restriction.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes conclusive ending. ABC also allows consolidating opinion and deepening a youth relationship, so not every expression is severance.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Node = Admetos`, Admetos specifies intellectual connection through depth, restriction and definite closure. Witte describes sad thought and a conclusive break in a connection or line of thinking. ABC adds blocked transport or news links and mental blockage, but also thorough media contacts, consolidated opinion and deeper relations to youth. The distinction is between an exchange that ends and one that becomes narrower or more deeply established ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
