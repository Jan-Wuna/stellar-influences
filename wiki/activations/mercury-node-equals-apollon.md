## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `141`.

#### Witte Entry

Exchange of thoughts with many, with scientists or representatives of industry and commerce.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `209`; PDF page: `103`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `103`, printed page `209`):

> Erfolgreiche Medien-Verbindungen, erfolgreiche Beziehungen durch Gespräche, Gesprächs-Kontakte mit Wissenschaftlern, schriftliche Handels-Verbindungen, viele Verbindungen zu Intellektuellen, zunehmende Verbindung zur Jugend

Editorial translation from the German passage: Successful media connections; successful relationships through conversation; conversational contacts with scientists; written trade connections; many connections to intellectuals; an increasing connection to youth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Wide intellectual exchange with many people, scientists, industry or commerce.
- psychology: The emphasis is on exchange and contact rather than a distinct private psychological trait.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is established.
- social/relationship: Successful relationships through conversation and contacts with intellectuals recur.
- events/manifestations: Media connections, scientific conversation and written trade links are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: The constructive statements do not establish universal success for every network.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict is stated. Witte's exchange with many and ABC's successful, increasing connections reinforce the same social emphasis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Node = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the axis through a widening exchange of ideas and useful intellectual or commercial contacts. Witte names scientists and representatives of industry and commerce; ABC adds media links, scientific conversation and written trade connections. The connection is therefore a network for exchanging thought and information, with success and growth stated in ABC rather than an unrestricted promise for every association ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
