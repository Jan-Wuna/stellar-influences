#### Sandbach Entry

One helps others to perceive the ideas they need to move away from the one’s they need to move toward. One helps others to find a new way of looking at things. Sharing one’s ideas with groups and/or individuals can have a profoundly healing effect.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Node = Chiron`, Chiron specifies the exchange of ideas as a way to help others see things differently. Sandbach's clear statements connect a new perspective with sharing ideas among individuals or groups, which he describes as potentially healing. The damaged opening sentence does not justify a more precise account of which ideas should be abandoned or adopted ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
