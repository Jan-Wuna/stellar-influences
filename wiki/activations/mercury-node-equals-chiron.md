### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One helps others to perceive the ideas they need to move away from the one’s they need to move toward. One helps others to find a new way of looking at things. Sharing one’s ideas with groups and/or individuals can have a profoundly healing effect.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Node = Chiron` heals the axis through shared reframing and helpful ideas.
- Chart expression: guiding others toward better ways of thinking, helping them leave old ideas behind, and creating exchanges or group conversations that have a strongly healing effect.
