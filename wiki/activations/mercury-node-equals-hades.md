## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `141`.

#### Witte Entry

To be tied up with unclean affairs. Correspondence for such reasons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `209`; PDF page: `103`.

#### ABC Entry

Hide thoughts in connections, dangerous thoughts connections, thought connections from the past, criminal youth connection, disadvantages in connections by speech, and hidden news relations

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intellectual or written ties involving unclean affairs ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: ABC includes concealing thoughts and dangerous connections of thought.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is established.
- social/relationship: Hidden news relationships and criminal youth connections are ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: Correspondence concerning unclean affairs and disadvantage through conversation recur.
- conflicts/notes: A connection from the past is not by itself proof of criminality.

## Contradictions

The entries share hidden or compromised communication. ABC's past connections are an additional example and should not all be equated with Witte's unclean affairs.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Node = Hades`, Hades specifies the axis through compromised ties and the correspondence that maintains them. Witte names involvement in unclean affairs; ABC adds hidden thoughts, dangerous mental connections, concealed news relationships and disadvantage through conversation. These give a concrete basis for secrecy or compromised exchange. ABC's connection to the past remains a separate possibility, rather than evidence that every old tie is corrupt ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
