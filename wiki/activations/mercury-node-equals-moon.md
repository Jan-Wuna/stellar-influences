## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Connection of thoughts with females, or with numbers of people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Facilitating language connections, language about feelings in relationships, contactLanguage with women, media connections between people, writing about encounters with women, connection with young women, and people connection through youth

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Desire to tell other people one's troubles, communion of thoughts with an emotional accent, desire to exchange ideas and thoughts with females.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To have strong feelings about the conflict between new and old ideas. A need for intimacy expressed through communicating with others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Sensitivity on how information must be presented to those who are able to utilize your ideas; a tradition of using associates to research or pass on information and communication; locating people to gather information.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Node = Moon`: connective speech, emotionally aware teamwork, and belonging created through listening, reflection, and mental rapport. Main caution: impressionable connection: communication can drift too much with mood, familiarity, or emotional dependency.
