## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `141`.

#### Witte Entry

Connection of thoughts with females, or with numbers of people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `208`; PDF page: `103`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `103`, printed page `208`):

> Gefühlvolle Gesprächs-Verbindungen, Gespräche über Gefühle in Beziehungen, Kontakt-Gespräche mit Frauen, Medien-Verbindungen zwischen den Völkern, über Begegnung mit Frauen schreiben, Verbindung zu jungen Frauen, Völker-Verbindung durch die Jugend

Editorial translation from the German passage: Emotional conversational connections; conversations about feelings in relationships; contact conversations with women; media connections among peoples; writing about an encounter with women; a connection to young women; connections among peoples through youth.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Printed source page: `167`; entry code: `0544`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Desire to tell other people one's troubles, communion of thoughts with an emotional accent, desire to exchange ideas and thoughts with females.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- PDF chapter start: `51` (chapter locator; not an exact activation-page reference).

#### Sandbach Entry

To have strong feelings about the conflict between new and old ideas. A need for intimacy expressed through communicating with others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `176`

#### Munkasey Entry

Sensitivity on how information must be presented to those who are able to utilize your ideas; a tradition of using associates to research or pass on information and communication; locating people to gather information.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Exchange of ideas with an emotional accent and a wish for intimacy.
- psychology: Sandbach describes strong feelings about the conflict of old and new ideas.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is established.
- social/relationship: Sharing troubles, conversation with women and communication among peoples appear in the sources.
- events/manifestations: Research through associates and locating people to gather information are Munkasey's examples.
- conflicts/notes: Emotional connection is stated; emotional dependency and teamwork are not necessary claims.

## Contradictions

The sources overlap on communication shaped by feeling. Munkasey's information-gathering emphasis adds a practical social use without cancelling the wish for intimacy in Sandbach.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Node = Moon`, Moon specifies intellectual connection through feeling: people share troubles, seek intimacy through communication and respond strongly to old and new ideas. Witte and Ebertin also name exchange with women; ABC adds emotional conversations and links among peoples through youth. Munkasey's attention to presenting and gathering information gives a practical use of this sensitivity. The distinction is between exchanging facts and the emotional connection carried by that exchange, without inferring dependency ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
