## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `141`.

#### Witte Entry

Disappointing correspondence or exchange of thoughts. Fruitless waiting, to leave someone in the lurch.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `209`; PDF page: `103`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `103`, printed page `209`):

> Auflösung schriftlicher Verbindungen, Feingefühl durch Begegnung mit Intellektuellen, Gespräche mit Fremden anknüpfen, sich mit der Zukunft der Jugend verbinden, unsichere Verbindungen in der Jugend, Vermittlung metaphysischer Gedanken

Editorial translation from the German passage: Dissolution of written connections; sensitivity through encounters with intellectuals; starting conversations with strangers; connecting with the future of young people; uncertain connections in youth; communicating metaphysical thoughts.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Printed source page: `167`; entry code: `0550`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Difficult adjustment to community life. An interrupted contact, shared disappointments.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- PDF chapter start: `51` (chapter locator; not an exact activation-page reference).

#### Sandbach Entry

To perceive subtle connections between ideas. To feel a spiritual obligation to share one’s ideas with others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `176`

#### Munkasey Entry

A weakening of the ties you have established with others thru a lack of proper communications; visions of how to improve your communications potentials when meeting with others; clever deceptions when passing data.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertain or interrupted communication, with subtle understanding as another possibility ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- psychology: Disappointment, sensitivity, and perception of subtle connections are distinct strands.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition appears.
- social/relationship: Ties can weaken through poor communication; ABC also names contact with strangers and intellectuals.
- events/manifestations: Interrupted correspondence, fruitless waiting, deceptive data, and sharing metaphysical ideas are named.
- conflicts/notes: Failed communication and subtle perception are alternatives; neither erases the other.

## Contradictions

The emphasis differs materially: Witte and Ebertin stress disappointment or interrupted contact, whereas Sandbach describes subtle understanding and an obligation to share ideas. Munkasey includes both improving communication and deception. The scan-verified ABC passage likewise includes dissolution and uncertainty alongside sensitive or metaphysical exchange.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Node = Neptune`, Neptune specifies connections made through ideas by making their clarity and continuity uncertain. Correspondence may disappoint, contact may be interrupted, or ties may weaken because communication fails; clever deception is a distinct further possibility ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).

The same exact orientation also has a perceptive strand: noticing subtle connections and feeling obliged to share ideas, sensitivity in intellectual encounters, and communicating metaphysical thoughts. Those entries concern what can be sensed or conveyed beyond straightforward exchange. They do not establish artistic circles or guarantee that an uncertain connection becomes spiritually meaningful ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
