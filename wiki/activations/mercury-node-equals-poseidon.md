## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Node = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair as an exchange seeking shared understanding. Conversation and correspondence concern mental or spiritual matters, while clarity, insight, and cultural encounters extend the theme into youth and media connections. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry). The entries support the subject and quality of this communication without establishing that every exchange produces insight.
