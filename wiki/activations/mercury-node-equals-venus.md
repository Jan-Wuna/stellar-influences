## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Node + Mercury`
- Source page: `141`
#### Witte Entry

Fond to exchange ideas and thoughts, from love for the cause. Mind games about a love connection. To flirt. Acquaintances through correspondence.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Mercury/Dragon's Head`
- Source page: `167`
- Entry: `0545`

#### Ebertin Entry

Urge to mix with people expressing the same feelings and having the same interests, shared artistic interests with other people, an art association, commencement of a love relationship.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Mercury/Node midpoint`
- Source page: `51`
- Activator: `Venus`

#### Sandbach Entry

Seeking out people who will appreciate one’s ideas. The artist who is searching for a community of fellow artists. To seek a lover who will introduce one to new ideas, and who will push one to develop greater communication skills. To appreciate art that connects lots of ideas together.
