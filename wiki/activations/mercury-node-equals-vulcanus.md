## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Node + Mercury`
- Source reference: PDF p. 141 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Mighty, extraordinary thought activity. Special abilities in coordination of thoughts. Mighty connections of thoughts, correspondence, conversations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Mondknoten + Merkur`
- Source reference: printed p. 209; PDF p. 103 (one-based) (entry).

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `103`, printed page `209`):

> Einfluß einer Jugend-Verbindung, einflußreiche Medien-Verbindungen, Gewalt gegen Jugend-Verbindungen, machtvolle Gedanken-Verbindungen, Nachrichten über Gewalt in Verbindungen, Sprach-Gewalt in Beziehungen, starke Verbindung zur Jugend

Editorial translation from the German passage: Influence of a youth connection; influential media connections; violence against youth connections; powerful connections of thought; news about violence in connections; verbal force in relationships; a strong connection to youth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intense thought coordination and influential communication links. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Extraordinary mental activity and special coordination abilities. [Witte](#witte-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health claim appears; ABC's references to violence describe relationship situations.
- social/relationship: Strong youth connections and influential media relationships, alongside verbal force. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Powerful correspondence and conversations; news concerning violence in connections. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Capacity to coordinate and coercive speech are different expressions; neither implies a fixed group size.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified; ABC adds coercive manifestations to Witte's emphasis on mental capacity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Node = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies connections through the force and concentration of thought. Correspondence and conversation may carry unusual influence, and coordinating ideas is a central ability in [Witte](#witte-entry). [ABC](#abc-entry) extends this to strong youth and media links, while also naming verbal force and violence in relationships. That range keeps influence distinct from a guarantee of constructive coordination.
