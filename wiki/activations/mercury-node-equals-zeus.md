## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Node = Zeus`, Zeus specifies communication through creating and directing a connection toward a goal. Shared inventions and the patent-attorney example give this a concrete collaborative form; youth and media relationships broaden the settings in [ABC](#abc-entry). Conversation may begin or shape the association, with effort as well as creativity involved. [Witte](#witte-entry).
