## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Change in thinking at a fundamental or deepened level. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Thought development may deepen or be blocked; ABC also names changed thinking through depression.
- body/health: Restricted development of movement is an ABC motif; it establishes no diagnosis.
- social/relationship: The entries emphasize thought, conversation, and movement rather than a specific relationship type.
- events/manifestations: Continuing discussions of development and evolution, and deepening language. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Depression remains ABC's attributed language; it is not an inferred medical condition.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified; fundamental change and blocked development describe different possible conditions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Admetos`, Admetos specifies changing thought through depth, persistence, or blockage. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a transformation from the foundations, while [ABC](#abc-entry) includes sustained developmental discussions and a deepening of language alongside inhibited thought and movement. These entries support a range from thorough revision to difficulty developing ideas; ABC's depression reference remains an attributed possibility.
