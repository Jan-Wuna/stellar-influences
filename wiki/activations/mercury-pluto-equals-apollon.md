## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Developing thought across many subjects, including scientific language. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Freedom of thought and changing opinions. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Witte refers to many people changing their minds; ABC includes growth of knowledge among youth.
- events/manifestations: Scientific thinking, language development, and accounts of transformation experiences. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Multiplicity and successful development are different emphases; the entries do not guarantee expertise.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified; Witte emphasizes multiplicity, while ABC emphasizes development and knowledge.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Apollon`, Apollon specifies developing thought through breadth: many matters require attention, and opinions may change. [Witte](#witte-entry). [ABC](#abc-entry) gives that breadth a more specific expression in scientific thinking, expanded language, freedom of thought, and growing knowledge among youth. Describing transformation experiences belongs to this range without proving that all changes of mind are successful.
