## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thinking and communication change through dealings with others. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Persistence and the desire to control information. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 172.
- body/health: No specific bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Influence on the environment, persuasion by others, and possible attempts to dominate relationships. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- events/manifestations: Discussions of changed circumstances and reports on local developments. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Influencing others and being persuaded by them both occur; domination is a possible strain, not the whole interpretation.

## Contradictions

Witte includes being persuaded by others, while Ebertin emphasizes influence over them. These are opposite directions of influence, not proof of incompatibility.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Asc`, Asc specifies changing thought through contact with other people and the immediate environment. Views can be revised in discussion, but the same exchange can become an effort to dominate or control information. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 172. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) makes the relational distinction explicit: intense communication can help both parties grow, whereas domination weakens the connection.
