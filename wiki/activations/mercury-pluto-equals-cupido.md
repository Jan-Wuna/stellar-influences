## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Cupido`, Cupido specifies changing thought within family and community settings. Reflection on personal status in a family meets the development of conversation groups, shared opinions, and media communities. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry). Games are a separate concrete motif in Witte; the damaged final clause is preserved for checking and does not support a confident claim about persuasion.
