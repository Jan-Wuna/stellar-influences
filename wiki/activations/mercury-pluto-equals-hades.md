- Source heading: `Mercury + Pluto`

#### Witte Entry

To recognize vile things too late. To recognize evil. Conservative ideas or plans in change.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Merkur + Pluto`
- Source reference: printed p. 253; PDF p. 125 (one-based) (entry).
- Translation review: corrected against the German scan at the reference above; original factor orientation retained.

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous verbal exchange, conversations about growing dangers, inadequate development of thoughts, changing opinions about the past, changes through inadequate thinking, growing criminality in youth, and an increasing lack of movement

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Recognition of troubling developments and changes in thought about danger or the past. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Belated recognition and inadequate development of thought. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: ABC mentions an increasing lack of movement; it supplies no diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Dangerous verbal exchanges and ABC's claim about growing youth criminality remain source-specific examples.
- events/manifestations: Discussions of increasing dangers and changes to opinions about the past. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Witte's moral language and ABC's youth-crime statement are attributed possibilities, not assessments of a person or group.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified; recognition and deficient development describe different aspects of the range.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Hades`, Hades specifies changing thought through the recognition of harmful or neglected matters. Recognition may come late, while conversations can concern growing dangers or revisions to views of the past. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry). Inadequate thought development and dangerous exchanges describe difficulties within that process; the moral and criminal language in the entries remains attributed, rather than a verdict about someone's character.
