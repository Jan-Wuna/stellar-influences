- Source heading: `Mercury + Pluto`
- Source reference: PDF p. 165 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Fortunate development of the trend of thoughts. To come to recognizing happy changes of conditions. Successful persuasion through talking.

Developing enjoyment of movement, successful development of youth, gratifying changes in thinking, discussing financial developments, talking about the development of money, fast traffic development, and change of youth law

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Mercury/Pluto`
- Source reference: printed p. 164; entry `0535` (coded entry).

#### Ebertin Entry

The art of persuasion, ability to teach, diplomacy. Recognition by the masses or the public, great success. A crook or swindler.

enthusiasm for convincing other people. A teacher who can be highly com pelling when presenting ideas. Success through impressing other people with one’s ideas, though if the person lacks a moral sense this might imply one who has a great ability to dupe others.

- Source reference: printed p. 172 (entry page).

Increased need to control publishing sources and the way that material and information is presented to the public; increased public relations or advertising expertise; a bias about what information you release to others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Persuasion, teaching, and favorable developments in thought. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- psychology: Enthusiasm for convincing others; presentation may become biased or deceptive. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 172.
- body/health: ABC mentions developing enjoyment of movement; no illness is specified.
- social/relationship: Compelling teaching, diplomacy, and public recognition. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- events/manifestations: Publishing, advertising, financial discussions, and changes in youth law. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Successful persuasion and swindling are alternatives in the entries; influence alone does not establish dishonesty.

## Contradictions

Success and deception are conditional alternatives, not a contradiction or a basis for calling someone a swindler.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies changing thought through persuasive success. Teaching, diplomacy and compelling presentation can win recognition and help others see changed conditions. Ebertin and Sandbach also explicitly retain deception as an adverse possibility ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)). Munkasey adds control of publishing and bias in releasing information; that is a distinct limit on presentation ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 172). ABC's financial and youth-law discussions provide concrete subjects for developmental communication ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
