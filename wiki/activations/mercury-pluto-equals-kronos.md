## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Kronos`, Kronos specifies developing thought through the scope of understanding and authority. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes cognition, while [ABC](#abc-entry) includes independent thinking, a young leader's development, and reports on the state. Authoritarian opinion is also present, so intellectual development should not be equated with unquestionable judgment.
