## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Mercury + Pluto`
- Source reference: PDF p. 165 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Function of the glands and nerve activities of the female body. Change in public thinking. To adopt oneself to public opinions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Merkur + Pluto`
- Source reference: printed p. 252; PDF p. 125 (one-based) (entry).
- Translation review: corrected against the German scan at the reference above; original factor orientation retained.

#### ABC Entry

Development of the language of a people, development into a young woman, developing feeling-filled thoughts, development of the woman in conversation, media development in the people, change of opinion of the woman, and change of opinion in the people's opinion

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Mercury/Pluto`
- Source reference: printed p. 164; entry `0532` (coded entry).

#### Ebertin Entry

The power to speak with heart and soul and consequently to convince people, adaptable and changeable in thinking and speaking. A woman who can speak convincingly. Also a restless woman.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Mercury/Pluto midpoint`
- Source reference: PDF p. 50 (one-based; chapter start only). This is a chapter start, not a verified exact entry page.

#### Sandbach Entry

Insecurities manifesting as mental obsessiveness. Intensity of communication stemming from one’s imbuing one’s words with tremendous feeling. An emotional need to communicate deeply.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source reference: printed p. 172 (entry page).

- Source heading: `MERCURY/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `172`

#### Munkasey Entry

Diminished capacity for enjoying sex with one partner; mood swings when you can not obtain your way with others; added sensitivity to the sexual and intellectual needs of others; obsessive needs for privacy or power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Emotion gives communication intensity and persuasive force. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- psychology: Insecurity, obsessive thought, mood changes, and the need for deep communication. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 172.
- body/health: Witte's female gland and nerve statement and Munkasey's sexual themes are historical attributed claims, not medical findings.
- social/relationship: Convincing speech, sensitivity to others' needs, and wishes for privacy or power. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- events/manifestations: Changes in public opinion and the development of a people's language or media. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Gendered examples remain attributed; the entries do not establish a universal sex-specific trait.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified; personal feeling and public opinion are different scopes. Historical health and gender claims need attribution.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Moon`, Moon specifies changing thought through feeling and emotional need. Words can carry unusual conviction, while insecurity can intensify communication into recurring or obsessive thought. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry). Changes in public opinion and a people's language extend the scope beyond private feeling. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry). The historical gendered and bodily statements remain source claims and do not ground a diagnosis or a universal description of women.
