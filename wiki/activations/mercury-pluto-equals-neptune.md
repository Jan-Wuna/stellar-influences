## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Uncertainty regarding the near future. Sneaky, uncertain conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exchangeable exchange of words, development in a foreign language, language about water change, developing opinions about metaphysics, language about poor evolution, unclear development of youth, and uncertain development of movement see above

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Overly receptive, nervous sensitivity, pursuit of peculiar plans, cunning, falsehood, libel, slander and defamation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To be extremely receptive to the ideas of others. To be mentally obsessive about one’s ideals. Nagging and relentless thoughts sap one’s energy.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Mystical insight into where and how to get the information you need; the use of deception and concealing information; an inefficient or messy researcher; the disappearance or falsification of experimental data.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Pluto = Neptune`: intuitive research, sensitivity to hidden patterns, and speech that works indirectly or below the surface. Main caution: obscured force: insight can slip into paranoia, confusion, or covert manipulation.
