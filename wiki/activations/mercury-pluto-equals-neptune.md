Uncertainty regarding the near future. Sneaky, uncertain conditions.

- Source heading: `Merkur + Pluto`

- Source heading: `Mercury/Pluto`

Overly receptive, nervous sensitivity, pursuit of peculiar plans, cunning, falsehood, libel, slander and defamation.

#### Sandbach Entry

To be extremely receptive to the ideas of others. To be mentally obsessive about one’s ideals. Nagging and relentless thoughts sap one’s energy.

Mystical insight into where and how to get the information you need; the use of deception and concealing information; an inefficient or messy researcher; the disappearance or falsification of experimental data.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertain conditions and highly receptive or obscured thinking. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- psychology: Absorbing others' ideas, fixation on ideals, and relentless thoughts. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- body/health: Ebertin mentions nervous sensitivity; Sandbach says recurring thoughts sap energy. Neither establishes a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Communication may involve concealment, falsehood, or defamation in the attributed examples. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- events/manifestations: Uncertain future developments, metaphysical discussion, and missing or falsified research data. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 172.
- conflicts/notes: The trailing 'see above' belonged to the following Pluto line in the scan and has been removed from this translation.

## Contradictions

Receptivity and deceptive presentation are different possible expressions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Neptune`, Neptune specifies developing thought through uncertainty and receptivity. Ideas from others may enter deeply, but relentless reflection or fixation on an ideal can drain attention and energy. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry). Munkasey's mystical information-seeking belongs beside his examples of concealed or falsified data, so intuitive access should not be confused with reliable evidence. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 172. Ebertin's accusations remain attributed possibilities, not conclusions about a person.
