- Source heading: `Mercury + Pluto`
- Source reference: PDF p. 165 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To come to the conclusion that separation is compulsory.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Merkur + Pluto`

Discussing development tasks, developing concentration of thought, slow development of traffic, problematic changes of opinion, developing thoughts of separation, talking about developments in pain, and increasing traffic burdens

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Mercury/Pluto`
- Source reference: printed p. 164; entry `0536` (coded entry).

#### Ebertin Entry

Quarrelsome nature, nagging character. Irritability, skepticism. Nervous irritation through over-work, exposure to heavy and bitter attacks, separation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Mercury/Pluto midpoint`
- Source reference: PDF p. 50 (one-based; chapter start only). This is a chapter start, not a verified exact entry page.

#### Sandbach Entry

A need to be sensitive to the ideas of others rather than solely focused on one’s own. Negative reactions encountered when one tries to impress one’s ideas on others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source reference: printed p. 172 (entry page).

- Source heading: `MERCURY/PLUTO with Planets and Points`

#### Munkasey Entry

Disappointment when releasing information which you feel is secret; delays in your plans to exert control over knowledge; added efficiency to your ruthlessness in pursuing sources of data or rumors.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought meets resistance, delay, or a conclusion that separation is necessary. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- psychology: Skepticism, irritability, and insistence on one's ideas. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- body/health: Ebertin attributes nervous irritation to overwork; ABC mentions discussions of developing pain. These are not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Negative reactions to imposed ideas, bitter attacks, and separation. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- events/manifestations: Delayed information-control plans, disappointing disclosures, and discussions of development tasks. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 172; [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Concentration appears in ABC, but the entries do not establish research stamina or possession of difficult truths.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified; the entries emphasize different forms of resistance.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Saturn`, Saturn specifies developing thought through limits and resistance. Concentration can grow, yet attempts to press ideas on others may bring rejection, quarrels, or separation. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry). [Witte](#witte-entry) gives the specific conclusion that separation is necessary; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 172, adds delayed plans and disappointment over information. The range concerns difficulty handling thought and disclosure, not proof that the person sees a harsher truth than others.
