## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Mercury + Pluto`
- Source reference: PDF p. 165 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To desire people, to wish for people in whom one can trust, and get along with harmoniously. New harmonious acquaintances. New love acquaintances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Merkur + Pluto`
- Source reference: printed p. 252; PDF p. 125 (one-based) (entry).
- Translation review: corrected against the German scan at the reference above; original factor orientation retained.

#### ABC Entry

Evolution of media arts, harmonious language development, developing thoughts of love, developing opinions about love, changes through peace talks, growing young love, and changing opinions about art

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Mercury/Pluto`
- Source reference: printed p. 164; entry `0533` (coded entry).

#### Ebertin Entry

Interest in unusual and specialized artistic fields and the inclination to produce propaganda for the latter. An unrestrained sex expression, preoccupation with peculiar problems of love. A marriage swindler.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Mercury/Pluto midpoint`
- Source reference: PDF p. 50 (one-based; chapter start only). This is a chapter start, not a verified exact entry page.

#### Sandbach Entry

One appreciates extreme ideas. Intense communications with the loved one. Intense verbal expressions of love.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source reference: printed p. 172 (entry page).

- Source heading: `MERCURY/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `172`

#### Munkasey Entry

Added ruthlessness to desires to control others; seeking more control over sources of artistic information and using these to your advantage or to increase your position; a desire for intellectual gratification from the arts.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Developing love, artistic interest, and intense appreciative communication. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- psychology: Appreciation of extreme ideas and intellectual gratification through art. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- body/health: Ebertin's sexual language remains attributed and does not establish a medical condition.
- social/relationship: New harmonious or romantic acquaintances and intense exchanges with a loved one. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- events/manifestations: Specialized artistic activity, media arts, and control over artistic information. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 172.
- conflicts/notes: The desire for trust differs from Munkasey's control theme; neither should displace the other.

## Contradictions

Harmony and control are contrasting possible expressions. Ebertin's marriage-swindler example does not establish a general relationship pattern.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Venus`, Venus specifies developing thought through love and artistic appreciation. The wish for trusted, harmonious contact can lead to new acquaintances and intense verbal expressions of love. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry). Unusual artistic interests and attempts to control artistic information broaden the range, while keeping appreciation distinct from manipulation. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), p. 172. The fraud example is an attributed possibility, not a judgment about a relationship.
