## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies developing thought at the public level. General opinion changes and adapts, while conversations make public development and youth development subjects of exchange. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry). The entries describe changing shared discourse, without identifying a particular person as its leader or predicting which view prevails.
