## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Pluto = Zeus`, Zeus specifies developing thought through effort and achievement. A shared conversation or undertaking can begin, while directing the exchange and developing language give the effort a concrete form. [ABC](#abc-entry). [Witte](#witte-entry) adds exertion and recognition of one's increased accomplishment, keeping the interpretation focused on the work of development rather than an assumed technical vocation.
