- Source heading: `Mercury + Poseidon`

#### Witte Entry

Ideas which are understood only by few people. To create enemies through one’s ideas.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Merkur + Poseidon`
- Source reference: printed p. 269; PDF p. 133 (one-based) (entry).
- Translation review: corrected against the German scan at the reference above; original factor orientation retained.

#### ABC Entry

Limited understanding among youth, consolidation of insight among youth, intellectual or spiritual deepening of youth, intellectual or spiritual resistance among youth, thorough conversations about reason, suppression of thoughtful conversations, and youth deepening their engagement with truth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Ideas understood by few, alongside deepened or restricted understanding. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Consolidating insight, resistance, and intellectual or spiritual deepening. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: One's ideas may create enemies or meet a limited audience. [Witte](#witte-entry).
- events/manifestations: Thorough conversations about reason and suppression of thoughtful conversation. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Limited reception is not proof of superior truth; deepening and suppression are different possibilities.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified; understanding, resistance, and suppression describe different conditions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies ideas through depth and limited reception. An idea may be understood by few or provoke hostility, while understanding can become more settled and thorough. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry). ABC also retains intellectual resistance and suppressed conversation, so an inward deepening should not be confused with assurance that the idea is correct.
