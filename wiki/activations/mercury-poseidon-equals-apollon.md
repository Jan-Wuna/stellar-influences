- core meaning: Meeting like-minded people and expanding understanding. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified; social contact and intellectual expansion are different emphases.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Apollon`, Apollon specifies ideas through wider understanding and contact with people who share them. [Witte](#witte-entry). Thoughtful conversation, cultural achievement, and expanding insight among youth give this contact an educational and cultural range in [ABC](#abc-entry). The entries describe the circulation and development of ideas, while leaving their truth to be established separately.
