## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Mercury + Poseidon`
- Source reference: PDF p. 173 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To meet or to have dealings with people who represent an idea.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Merkur + Poseidon`
- Source reference: printed p. 268; PDF p. 133 (one-based) (entry).
- Translation review: corrected against the German scan at the reference above; original factor orientation retained.

#### ABC Entry

Thoughtful conversations locally, conversations about local culture, local cultural activity, clear reports about the environment, local conversations with others, reasonable conversations with a partner, a reasonable conversation partner, and the partner's true thoughts

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Ideas represented in direct dealings with other people. [Witte](#witte-entry).
- psychology: Clear reporting and reasonable conversation, rather than a distinct temperament, are emphasized. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: People who represent an idea and a thoughtful conversation partner. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Local cultural discussion, reports on the environment, and conversations with a partner. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: ABC's truncated local-language wording was repaired from the scan; it does not describe language proficiency.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified; ABC expands direct contact into local and partner conversation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Asc`, Asc specifies ideas through the people and conversations encountered nearby. An idea is met through someone who represents it, while local culture and clear reports about the environment make the setting concrete. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry). Reasonable conversation with a partner belongs to the same direct exchange; the entries do not establish that every partner's thoughts are known with certainty.
