## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies ideas through community: people speak about them together, and a shared idea can hold a society together. [Witte](#witte-entry). Insightful synthesis in conversation, youth culture, and community cultural reports extend that collective setting in [ABC](#abc-entry). Shared conviction explains cohesion here, without proving that a community's belief is correct.
