## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Hades`, Hades specifies ideas through memory and perceived limits in understanding or culture. [Witte](#witte-entry) includes reminiscence as well as derogatory judgments about people encountered; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds unclear thought, cultural dangers, and concealed thoughts. These entries do not authorize labeling a person unintelligent or criminal. ABC's reasonable renunciation is a separate motif, not another claim of deficiency.
