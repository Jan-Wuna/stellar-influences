## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies ideas through enjoyment and happy conversation about ideals. [Witte](#witte-entry). [ABC](#abc-entry) gives this a practical range in lucid financial and legal discussion and benefits from clear thinking. The emphasis is on appreciation and understanding, without promising a favorable legal or financial result.
