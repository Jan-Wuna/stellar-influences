## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Kronos`, Kronos specifies ideas through prominence and intellectual leadership. High ideals and a leading philosophical thinker are Witte's central examples. [Witte](#witte-entry). [ABC](#abc-entry) adds insight in leadership, dominance in conversation, and important cultural reporting. The range concerns the standing and influence of thought, without establishing that a prominent thinker is correct.
