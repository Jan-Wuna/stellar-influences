## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Active engagement with ideals, including disputes over morals. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Anger over moral behavior is a Witte possibility; ABC emphasizes thoughtful work.
- body/health: Swimming in an ocean is a concrete Witte activity, not a medical claim.
- social/relationship: Moral quarrels and cultural or ideal youth work. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Cultural work reports, intellectual work, and media activity. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: The happy-conversation clause belonged to Jupiter and was removed after checking the scan.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified after correcting Witte's Mars/Jupiter boundary.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Mars`, Mars specifies ideas through activity and engagement. Work can involve culture, media, and discussion of intellectual tasks; moral concerns can also provoke quarrels or anger. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry). Swimming in an ocean is a separate, literal activity retained by Witte, not a basis for merging the whole interpretation into a metaphor about ideals.
