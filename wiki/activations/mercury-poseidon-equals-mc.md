## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = MC`, MC specifies ideas through one's own thinking and expression. A person may carry or present an idea, write about it, or speak about truth as a personal concern. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry). Clear and reasonable thought is the supported theme; it describes an orientation toward understanding, without certifying the content of any belief.
