## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Moon`, Moon specifies ideas through the people who embody and communicate them, with feeling and culture prominent. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes spiritual attitudes and strong attachment to an idea, while [ABC](#abc-entry) includes women's cultural activity, youth culture, and conversation about emotional clarity. The historical gendered examples remain attributed rather than becoming a general rule about who can carry or understand an idea.
