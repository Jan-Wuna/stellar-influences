## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies ideas through receptivity: new ideas may be accepted intuitively as true while the person remains passive. [Witte](#witte-entry). Foreign cultures, metaphysical conversation, unknown ideas, and uncertainty through truthful words broaden the setting in [ABC](#abc-entry). Intuitive acceptance is the reported experience, not independent proof of truth; movement in clear water remains a separate concrete motif.
