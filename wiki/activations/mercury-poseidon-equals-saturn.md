## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies ideas through patient formulation and the difficulty of communicating them. A logical idea can instruct others, but it may also be refused or produce mental distance. [Witte](#witte-entry). [ABC](#abc-entry) adds serious words, hard truths, and cultural losses, making clarity something worked toward under difficult conditions rather than an automatic revelation.
