## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal intellectual or spiritual inspiration. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Mental mobility and clarity in personal conversation. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: Witte's 'spiritualized body' is doctrinal language, not a physiological finding.
- social/relationship: A spiritual center and understanding among young people are ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: Reports on a spiritual center and clear descriptions of a person. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: ABC's reasonable young people were mistranslated as impoverished; the gendered example remains attributed.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified after repairing ABC's mistranslations.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Sun`, Sun specifies ideas through personal inspiration and a spiritual conception of being. [Witte](#witte-entry). Clarity in personal conversation, mentally active people, and reports on a spiritual center make the theme concrete in [ABC](#abc-entry). The language of a spiritualized body remains a source doctrine; it does not support an anatomical or medical conclusion.
