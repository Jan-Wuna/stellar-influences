## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies ideas through sudden recognition and creative activation. A person may be taken by an idea or experience it as inner enlightenment. [Witte](#witte-entry). [ABC](#abc-entry) adds new youth ideas, media culture, reform, and surprising words, alongside tension during spiritual discussions. The emphasis is on the arrival and use of an idea, not independent confirmation of a revelation.
