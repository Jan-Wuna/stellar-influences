## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Venus`, Venus specifies ideas through appreciation and conversation that joins love, insight, and culture. Clear words of love and discussion of artistic ideas are ABC's concrete forms of exchange. [ABC](#abc-entry). [Witte](#witte-entry) separately names contact with ministers and preachers, preserving a religious setting without turning all appreciative discussion into religious teaching.
