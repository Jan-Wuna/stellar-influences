## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies ideas through public life: general ideals are voiced in speeches, cultural reports, and clear reporting for a public audience. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry). Youth understanding and traffic ideas are included as subjects of public expression. The entries establish a public scope without guaranteeing that the public accepts an idea.
