## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Mercury + Poseidon`
- Source reference: PDF p. 173 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To convince others through ideas. The power of conviction of an idea. Ideas which can be proved correct.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Merkur + Poseidon`
- Source reference: printed p. 269; PDF p. 133 (one-based) (entry).
- Translation review: corrected against the German scan at the reference above; original factor orientation retained.

#### ABC Entry

Strenuous spiritual conversations, knowledgeable word creations, discussing spiritual goals, speaking about cultural goals, clear youth leadership, creative youth culture, creative language about truth, and shaping language culture

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Conviction and the creative formulation of ideas. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Effort in spiritual conversation and commitment to intellectual or cultural aims. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Convincing others and guiding youth through clear aims. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Creative verbal expression and discussions of cultural or spiritual goals. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Witte says ideas can be proved correct; it does not certify any particular idea in advance.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified; conviction and creative effort are different aspects of purposeful ideas.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Poseidon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies ideas through purposeful, creative expression and conviction. Conversation can formulate spiritual or cultural goals and shape a culture of speech. [ABC](#abc-entry). [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes convincing others with an idea whose correctness can be shown; persuasion therefore remains distinct from a claim that every strongly held idea has been proved.
