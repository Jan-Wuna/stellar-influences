## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Deep, sustained thought and thorough discussion. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Patience and concentration, with depression linked to separation discussions in ABC.
- body/health: ABC's restricted movement in old age and depression language remain attributed claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Conversations about separation and resistance among troubled youth. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Thorough examination of tasks and slow consolidation among youth. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: The scan supports thorough concentration and resistance among troubled youth; neither is a claim about a locality.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified; philosophical depth and restriction are different emphases.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Admetos`, Admetos specifies serious thought through depth and sustained attention. Witte's philosophical thinking meets ABC's patience in conversation and thorough discussion of tasks. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry). The same entry includes restriction and separation-related depression, which remain attributed possibilities rather than the necessary price or result of deep thinking.
