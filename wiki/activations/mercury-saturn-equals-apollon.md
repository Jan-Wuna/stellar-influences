## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Logical, scientific, and educational thought under serious conditions. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Patience and mature thinking in the pursuit of knowledge. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Talking with many people about separation. [Witte](#witte-entry).
- events/manifestations: Scientific tasks, educational activity or its cessation, and slow language success. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Scientific and educational activity coexists with cessation and loss; success is neither immediate nor assured.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is identified; educational work and its cessation are alternatives.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Apollon`, Apollon specifies serious thought through knowledge, science, and education. Logical inquiry and discussion of scientific tasks are joined with patience and gradual success in language. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry). Witte also retains stopping teaching and discussing separation with many people; those less expansive outcomes qualify an interpretation based only on intellectual growth.
