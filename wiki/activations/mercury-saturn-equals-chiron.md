## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Mercury/Saturn midpoint`; one-based PDF pages `45–46` (exact oriented passage checked in the actual PDF text).

An instinctive ability to communicate with a profound clarity. To be able to perceive the most important ideas., which leads to greater organization in thinking. A deep sensitivity to problems with communication, and an intuitive ability to heal them.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Chiron`, Chiron specifies organized thought as an instinctive sensitivity to what matters in communication. Perceiving essential ideas supports clear expression, while noticing communication problems opens an intuitive capacity to help repair them. The entry's healing language concerns communication and supplies no medical claim. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
