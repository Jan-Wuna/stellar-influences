## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Serious shared conversations and collective journeys.
- psychology: Seriousness is explicit; no separate inner account is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Family travel, shared responsibilities and separation discussions are group settings.
- events/manifestations: Honeymoon or artistic tours, rail networks and disrupted community traffic are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's journeys and ABC's communal losses remain distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes shared travel, while ABC adds serious tasks, disruption and separation. Different settings do not establish a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Cupido`, Cupido places serious communication and movement in a shared setting. Families or groups travel together, and communities discuss common tasks or separations; roads and railway networks retain the concrete collective-movement meaning. ABC adds a hard or disturbed youth community, disrupted transport and losses within a young community, so shared organization can also carry difficulty. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
