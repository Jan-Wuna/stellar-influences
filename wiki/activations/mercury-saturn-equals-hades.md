## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `162` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Unpleasantness while traveling. Enmity and slander in that connection. Journey into imprisonment. Journey into the past.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Saturn`; printed p. `247`; one-based PDF page `122` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Reports about losses and deprivation, lack of movement and pain, concentration of thought on the past, criminal problems among young people, lack of serious conversations, reports about dangerous tasks, considering the disadvantages of separation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult travel and reports of loss concentrate thought on deprivation and the past.
- psychology: Thinking about the past and weighing separation's disadvantages are explicit.
- body/health: ABC names lack of movement and pain as attributed associations.
- social/relationship: Enmity, slander and criminal youth problems are specific settings.
- events/manifestations: Travel into imprisonment or the past and reports of dangerous tasks are distinct examples.
- conflicts/notes: The scan supports lack of serious conversations, replacing the former 'first rate speech'.

## Contradictions

The entries share difficult movement and communication while retaining separate examples. A journey into the past does not itself establish imprisonment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Hades`, Hades specifies serious thought and constrained movement through loss, deprivation or an encounter with the past. Unpleasant travel and slander sit beside reports about losses or dangerous tasks, while thought concentrates on the past and weighs the disadvantages of separation. Witte's journey into imprisonment is a severe example within this range; ABC's lack of movement and pain remain attributed bodily associations. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
