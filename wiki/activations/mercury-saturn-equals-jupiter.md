## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Vacation trip. Pleasure trip. Fortunate and happy trip.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reports of successes in old age, success in youth through patience, serious legal language, joy in old languages, inhibited movement pleasure, joy in separation language, successful concentration in old age, and talk about money losses

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Good powers of concentration, philosophical thinking, the application of method, thoroughness, industriousness. Success with difficult or irksome work, fortunate separation, glad farewell, short journeys.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An enthusiasm which takes into account the drawbacks of any situation and which tries to work around them. Success in organizing thoughts. Taking a good atti tude toward difficult work.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Additional ideas which form the basis for serious discussions or exchanges; generosity shown with helping others align their thoughts or thinking; idealistic but simple reasoning about philosophical or religious matters.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Saturn = Jupiter`: mature reasoning, practical wisdom, and the capacity to build ideas patiently over time. Main caution: heavy-minded doctrine: realism can become pessimistic preaching, inflexible judgment, or fear of broader possibilities.
