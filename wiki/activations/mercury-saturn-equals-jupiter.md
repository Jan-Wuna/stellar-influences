## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `162` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Vacation trip. Pleasure trip. Fortunate and happy trip.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Saturn`; printed p. `246`; one-based PDF page `122` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Reports about success in old age, success in youth through patience, serious legal conversations, enjoyment of old languages, inhibited enjoyment of movement, joy about separation conversations, successful concentration in old age, talking about monetary losses.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury/Saturn`; printed p. `155`; entry code `0499` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Good powers of concentration, philosophical thinking, the application of method, thoroughness, industriousness. Success with difficult or irksome work, fortunate separation, glad farewell, short journeys.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Mercury/Saturn midpoint`; one-based PDF page `45` (exact oriented passage checked in the actual PDF text).

An enthusiasm which takes into account the drawbacks of any situation and which tries to work around them. Success in organizing thoughts. Taking a good atti tude toward difficult work.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `160`

#### Munkasey Entry

Additional ideas which form the basis for serious discussions or exchanges; generosity shown with helping others align their thoughts or thinking; idealistic but simple reasoning about philosophical or religious matters.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Patient, organized thinking supports success with difficult work.
- psychology: Enthusiasm acknowledges drawbacks and seeks workable approaches.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Generosity helps others organize thoughts; serious philosophical discussions are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Pleasure trips, glad farewells and fortunate separations are stated possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: Positive concentration does not erase ABC's inhibited enjoyment of movement or discussions of money losses.

## Contradictions

The sources emphasize constructive method and fortunate departures. ABC also retains inhibited enjoyment and financial losses; these qualify an exclusively fortunate reading.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies serious thought as patient, constructive reasoning that can succeed with difficult work. Enthusiasm recognizes obstacles and works around them; organized ideas, philosophical reflection and generosity in helping others think provide a practical expression. ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))

Pleasure travel, glad farewells and fortunate separation preserve a concrete release from difficulty. ABC also names inhibited enjoyment of movement and discussion of monetary losses, so the entry does not promise that every departure or undertaking succeeds. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
