## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `162` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

A journey caused by the authorities, or a great journey. Pensioned.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Saturn`; printed p. `247`; one-based PDF page `122` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Especially problematic conversations, discussing special tasks, describing great losses, concentration on the chief's thoughts, concentration on the importance of youth, priority media tasks, speaking about losses of the state.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Serious problems and tasks are discussed at a large or authoritative scale.
- psychology: Concentration on a chief's thoughts or the importance of youth is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Authority, leadership and the state provide the stated institutional settings.
- events/manifestations: An officially caused or great journey, pensioning, media priorities and state losses are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Retirement and travel remain Witte examples; ABC concerns conversations and concentration.

## Contradictions

The sources offer different institutional expressions without a direct disagreement: journeys or pensioning in Witte and exceptional tasks or losses in ABC.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Kronos`, Kronos specifies serious communication through exceptional tasks, leadership or affairs of the state. Conversations address especially difficult problems or major losses, with attention concentrated on a chief's thinking, youth's importance or priority media work. Witte gives a journey caused by authorities, a great journey and pensioning as concrete institutional expressions. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
