## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `162` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

To be separated through quarrel or dissension. A trip that has to be made.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Saturn`; printed p. `246`; one-based PDF page `122` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Discussing work problems, reports about loss of work, serious work by young people, thoughts about work in old age, disrupted media activity, hard transport work, problematic media activity, painful reports about work, discussing actions of separation.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury/Saturn`; printed p. `155`; entry code `0498` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Incompatibility, unrest, desire to quarrel. Tendency to treat others badly, separations caused by disputes and arguments, tendency to bring about a separation by force, a change through force of circumstances.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Mercury/Saturn midpoint`; one-based PDF page `45` (exact oriented passage checked in the actual PDF text).

Anger arising from difficulties in communication. An aggressive expression of rigid ideas. Anger manifesting as careful, pointed speech. Uncertainty of thought in hibits action. After much deep thought, and/or soul searching, action is taken.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `160`

#### Munkasey Entry

Tough-minded negotiations in business or personal matters; energy used in the production of exacting or serious information; hasty yet simple communication; information which is simple or sparse, yet troubling.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Forceful or difficult communication brings work, conflict or separation into action.
- psychology: Anger, rigid ideas and uncertainty can inhibit action; deep reflection can precede action.
- body/health: ABC names painful reports about work; no specific medical condition is given.
- social/relationship: Tough negotiations and quarrels are stated interpersonal expressions.
- events/manifestations: Compulsory travel, work-loss reports and separation through dispute are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: The former ABC passage absorbed the Venus row and omitted part of the actual Mars row; both are corrected from the scan.

## Contradictions

The entries allow both inhibited action and action after reflection. Exacting information and tough negotiation do not require the abusive treatment also named by Ebertin.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Mars`, Mars specifies serious or constrained thought as forceful communication and consequential action. Difficult negotiations and exacting information can demand concentrated energy; rigid ideas or communication failures can instead provoke anger, pointed speech and disputes that force a separation. Sandbach also allows thought to delay action until deep reflection has taken place. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry))

The practical setting includes compulsory travel and discussion of work problems, lost employment, media difficulties and separative actions. These are separate examples, not a requirement that purposeful effort become quarrelsome or harmful. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
