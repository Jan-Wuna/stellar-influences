## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `162` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Preparations for travel. Travelling companion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Saturn`; printed p. `246`; one-based PDF page `122` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Discussing tasks within connections, serious conversations in a relationship, disturbed conversational connections, a disturbed connection to young people, contacts between youth and age, discussing problems in relationships, connecting conversations about separation.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury/Saturn`; printed p. `156`; entry code `0503` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Desire to have philosophical discussions, making plans, a short journey in company.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Mercury/Saturn midpoint`; one-based PDF page `46` (exact oriented passage checked in the actual PDF text).

The desire to seek out people with whom one can have deep and/or profound communication. One gets involved with people who one has communication problems with. A need to get out of old patterns of thinking and to find new systems of thought. One has come into this incarnation to learn how to think spiritually and to see the spiri tual implications behind all thoughts.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `160`

#### Munkasey Entry

Others who come to assist you with serious communication secrets or tasks; connections to groups which are able to provide scientific or thoroughly researched information on areas of interest to you; attracting deep thinkers.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections center on serious communication, joint thought or travel.
- psychology: Seeking depth and leaving old thought patterns are explicit needs.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Deep thinkers and research groups can assist communication, while some ties contain communication problems.
- events/manifestations: Planning, shared journeys and discussions about tasks or separation are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's spiritual-incarnation account remains his doctrinal claim; it is not implied by the other sources.

## Contradictions

Supportive intellectual connections and troubled communication both occur. Sandbach's incarnation language is a distinct spiritual explanation rather than shared cross-source evidence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Node`, Node specifies serious thought through connections with other people. Deep thinkers or groups with thoroughly researched information can assist demanding communication tasks; philosophical discussion and shared planning express the same search for substance. Some relationships instead expose communication problems or a need to leave established patterns of thought. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))

Travel preparations and companions preserve a practical shared undertaking. ABC adds links between youth and age, relationship problems and conversations about separation. Sandbach's call to think spiritually, including his incarnation framework, remains a source-specific explanation of this orientation. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
