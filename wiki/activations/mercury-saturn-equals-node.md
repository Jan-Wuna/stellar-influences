## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Preparations for travel. Travelling companion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Discuss tasks in connections, discuss serious language in a connection, spatial language connections, distance connection to youth, contacts between youth and age, discuss problems in connections, and connect language about separation

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Desire to have philosophical discussions, making plans, a short journey in company.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The desire to seek out people with whom one can have deep and/or profound communication. One gets involved with people who one has communication problems with. A need to get out of old patterns of thinking and to find new systems of thought. One has come into this incarnation to learn how to think spiritually and to see the spiri tual implications behind all thoughts.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Others who come to assist you with serious communication secrets or tasks; connections to groups which are able to provide scientific or thoroughly researched information on areas of interest to you; attracting deep thinkers.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Saturn = Node`: dependable dialogue, loyal exchange, and the ability to sustain ties through steadiness and restraint. Main caution: guarded distance: relating can become sparse, heavy, or too constrained by caution and doubt.
