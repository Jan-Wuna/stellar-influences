## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Break away. Move away. Give something up. Uncertain about near future. Sorrow of parting.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Young people, hard exchange of words, slow development of young people, slow development of languages, loss of traffic development, and growing seriousness in languages

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Tendency to toil or grapple with unusual and special problems, inclination to brood over one's destiny, plans determine a person's destiny.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Relentless narrow-mindedness. An obsessive need to organize ideas. An overpowering fear of communication. The need to continue to try to communicate until there is a breakthrough. An intense desire to figure something out. Mysteries or prob lems which one will not let go of until they are solved.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Serious incidents which provoke a questioning of official communications; compulsion in ensuring that data or information is correct; the ending of business with others due to serious breakdowns in your communications.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Saturn = Pluto`: formidable focus, hard-won insight, and the capacity to persist through intellectual or psychological extremes. Main caution: obsessive severity: thought can become ruthless, fear-driven, or locked into mental struggle.
