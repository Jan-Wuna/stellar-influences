## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `162` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Break away. Move away. Give something up. Uncertain about near future. Sorrow of parting.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Saturn`; printed p. `247`; one-based PDF page `122` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Discussing developmental tasks, disrupted development of transport, disturbed development in youth, harsh exchange of words, slow development of young people, slow development of language, loss-laden development of transport, growing seriousness in conversations.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury/Saturn`; printed p. `155`; entry code `0502` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Tendency to toil or grapple with unusual and special problems, inclination to brood over one's destiny, plans determine a person's destiny.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Mercury/Saturn midpoint`; one-based PDF page `46` (exact oriented passage checked in the actual PDF text).

Relentless narrow-mindedness. An obsessive need to organize ideas. An overpowering fear of communication. The need to continue to try to communicate until there is a breakthrough. An intense desire to figure something out. Mysteries or prob lems which one will not let go of until they are solved.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `160`

#### Munkasey Entry

Serious incidents which provoke a questioning of official communications; compulsion in ensuring that data or information is correct; the ending of business with others due to serious breakdowns in your communications.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Persistent thought grapples with difficult problems, communication and parting.
- psychology: Intense curiosity can become obsession, fear of speaking or brooding over destiny.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Communication breakdown may end business ties; correctness of official information is questioned.
- events/manifestations: Moving away, giving something up, slow development and difficult exchanges are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Persistence can seek a breakthrough or remain narrow; neither intellectual success nor disaster is guaranteed.

## Contradictions

Relentless problem-solving and narrow-mindedness are distinct possibilities within the entries, not a single necessary result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Pluto`, Pluto specifies serious thought as an intense struggle to solve difficult problems or make communication break through. The effort to organize ideas and ensure information is correct can become obsessive, fearful or narrowly fixed; official messages may be questioned after serious incidents. ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))

Breaking away, moving or ending business after failed communication gives the struggle a concrete consequence. ABC adds developmental tasks, slow or disrupted development and increasingly serious conversations. Sorrow at parting and uncertainty about the near future remain possibilities rather than an inevitable ending. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
