## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `162` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Logic in an idea. Educational ideas which cause travel. To separate in thought from mental or spiritual things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Saturn`; printed p. `247`; one-based PDF page `122` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Reports about cultural tasks, serious spiritual conversations, clarity through concentration of thought, conversations about cultural loss, difficult insights in youth, difficult conversations about truth, mature thoughts about truth, conversations about spiritual problems.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Concentrated thought addresses logic, truth, culture or spiritual questions.
- psychology: Clarity, mature reflection and difficult insights are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Serious spiritual conversations and discussions about cultural tasks or loss are stated settings.
- events/manifestations: Educational ideas may prompt travel; thought may separate from mental or spiritual things.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's former 'haughty knowledge' mistranslated difficult insights in youth.

## Contradictions

Logical or spiritual clarification and mental separation are different expressions; the page does not establish an inherently harmonious spiritual outlook.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies concentrated thought through logic, truth and cultural or spiritual questions. Serious conversation can seek clarity, mature understanding or difficult insights, while cultural loss and spiritual problems remain subjects that demand thought. Witte adds educational ideas that prompt travel and separating in thought from mental or spiritual things, preserving movement and disengagement beside clarification. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
