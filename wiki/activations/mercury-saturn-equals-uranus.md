## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `162` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Sudden departure. To disunite. To separate suddenly. To start differences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Saturn`; printed p. `247`; one-based PDF page `122` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Tasks of media technology, excitement about difficult conversations, disrupted communications technology, sudden problems in conversation, problematic reform of youth, disturbances in the rhythm of movement, tension through separation conversations, restlessness caused by serious words.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury/Saturn`; printed p. `155`; entry code `0500` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Great inner tension, excitability, haste, incompatibility. A sudden release from tension, quick decision, quick departure, car ride, inclination to go one's own way. A disease of the nerves.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Mercury/Saturn midpoint`; one-based PDF page `46` (exact oriented passage checked in the actual PDF text).

A genius for organizing ideas in an original and/or unique manner. The desire to overcome communication difficulties. The sudden arising of problems in communi cation. Negative thoughts about how one is different from other people, and a need to overcome those.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `160`

#### Munkasey Entry

Innovation from scientific research irregular communication or disruptions in any forms of information processing; indifference to formal ideas and the ways they are presented; messages delayed by mechanical failures.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Original thinking and communication confront sudden disruption or separation.
- psychology: Inner tension, haste and negative feelings about difference coexist with a drive to overcome communication problems.
- body/health: Ebertin's nerve-disease association is historical, not diagnostic.
- social/relationship: Differences can arise suddenly; formal ideas may meet indifference.
- events/manifestations: Quick decisions, departures, mechanical message delays and media-technology problems are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Original organization is supported; the entries do not establish consistently careful modernization.

## Contradictions

Innovation and abrupt breakdown both appear. Quick release of tension and communication problems need not occur together.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Uranus`, Uranus specifies disciplined thought through originality and sudden disturbance. Ideas can be organized in a unique way or advanced through scientific research, while communication problems, mechanical failures or abrupt differences interrupt their exchange. A wish to overcome those difficulties coexists with tension, haste or negative feelings about being different. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

Quick decisions, departures and sudden separations give this release or disruption a concrete form. Ebertin's association with nerve disease remains attributed historical doctrine rather than a diagnosis. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry))
