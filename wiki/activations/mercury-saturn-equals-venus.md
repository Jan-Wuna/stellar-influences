## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `162` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

New friends one separates from. Separations from or through acquaintances. To become unfaithful. To propose divorce.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Saturn`; printed p. `246`; one-based PDF page `122` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Thinking about disturbed harmony, conversations about the beauty of age, disturbed harmony in youth, difficult peace conversations, discussing artistic tasks, problems of youthful love, mature love conversations, speaking about love problems.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury/Saturn`; printed p. `155`; entry code `0497` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Instability in matters of love and affection, inclination to be unfaithful. A separation in love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Mercury/Saturn midpoint`; one-based PDF page `45` (exact oriented passage checked in the actual PDF text).

A love of mentally organizing information in a beautiful and/or pleasing way. One greatly appreciates the beauty of clearly expressed thoughts. Laziness supported by negative thinking. To enjoy the company of people who don’t say much. Practical cleverness in dealing with money.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `160`

#### Munkasey Entry

Appreciating how others think and/or show logic; pleasures derived from rigorous thinking or problem solving; simple ideas about how to charm others with your thoughts or views; placing melody or word to music.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affection and appreciation engage clear thought, reserved company or separation.
- psychology: Pleasure in well-organized ideas coexists with negative thinking that can support laziness.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Quiet companions and appreciation of others' logic sit beside love instability or proposed divorce.
- events/manifestations: Musical words or melody, financial cleverness and discussions of love problems are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte and Ebertin emphasize separation; Sandbach and Munkasey retain intellectual or artistic enjoyment.

## Contradictions

Separation and unfaithfulness are historical source examples, not necessary character traits. They do not erase the other entries' appreciation of clarity and quiet company.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Venus`, Venus specifies serious thought through appreciation of clear expression, pleasing organization and the logic of others. Pleasure may come from rigorous problem-solving, quiet company or setting words and melody to music; practical cleverness with money remains another stated expression. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))

The relationship range also includes disturbed harmony, difficult peace talks and problems in youthful love. Witte and Ebertin's separation or unfaithfulness examples remain attributed possibilities, alongside ABC's mature love conversations and reflection on the beauty of age. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
