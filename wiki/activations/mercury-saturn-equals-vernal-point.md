### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Saturn`; printed p. `246`; one-based PDF page `122` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Difficult conversations in public, disrupted transport in public, tasks of youth for the public, discussing important tasks, problems of young people in public, thoughts of separation in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Travel and serious communication enter public life.
- psychology: Thoughts of separation are explicit in the public setting.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Public youth tasks and important shared concerns provide social settings.
- events/manifestations: Hiking, excursions, voyages, location changes and disrupted public transport are examples.
- conflicts/notes: The former urban-transport phrase mistranslated disrupted transport and omitted three public clauses.

## Contradictions

Witte's journeys and ABC's public conversations are different expressions; the entries do not establish that all travel is troubled.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies movement and serious communication in a wider public setting. Hiking, excursions, voyages and changes of location give concrete forms of travel. ABC places difficult conversations, disrupted transport, youth tasks and problems, important discussions and separation thoughts in public life. The range joins outward movement with publicly expressed responsibilities or difficulties. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
