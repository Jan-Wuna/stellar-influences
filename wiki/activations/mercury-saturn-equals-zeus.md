## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `162` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

To be refused. Ejected. Enforced journey. Order of punishment. Severe criticism, castigation. Fire alarm.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Saturn`; printed p. `247`; one-based PDF page `122` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Strenuous movement in old age, serious conversations about procreation, leadership tasks in youth, concentrating thoughts on a goal, inhibited performance-oriented thinking, difficult creative conversations, news about losses through fire, problematic media performance.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought and communication concentrate on goals, performance or imposed action.
- psychology: Goal-focused concentration coexists with inhibited achievement-oriented thought.
- body/health: Strenuous movement in old age is named; no diagnosis is given.
- social/relationship: Refusal, ejection, punishment and severe criticism are stated difficult interactions.
- events/manifestations: Forced journeys, fire alarms, fire-loss news and problematic media performance are examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's leadership tasks and strenuous movement replace the former camouflage and exercising mistranslations.

## Contradictions

Focused performance and punitive or disruptive events are distinct examples rather than one unavoidable sequence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Saturn = Zeus`, Zeus specifies serious thought through goals, leadership tasks and creative or media performance. Concentration can be directed toward a goal while thinking about achievement remains inhibited. Witte's refusal, ejection, imposed journey or severe criticism gives a forceful external form to difficulty; fire alarms and reports of losses through fire preserve a separate concrete manifestation. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
