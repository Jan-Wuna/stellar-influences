## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Uranus`; one-based PDF page `163` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

To feel very depressed. Experiencing a catastrophe suddenly. To stay for some time under the impression of a sorrowful event.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Uranus`; printed p. `249`; one-based PDF page `123` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Ongoing technical conversations, excitement among blocked young people, excitement about traffic blockage, dynamic condensation of thoughts, news of depressing events, discussing new things thoroughly, sudden restriction of movement.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden stimulation meets blockage, prolonged impact or concentrated thought.
- psychology: Depression and lingering impressions of sorrow are attributed source themes.
- body/health: Restricted movement is named; depression is not established as a clinical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Technical conversations may continue while a young generation is blocked.
- events/manifestations: Traffic blockage, sudden catastrophe and news of depressing events are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Thorough discussion of new things remains alongside severe and restrictive examples.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize different forms of interruption or lasting impact; they do not establish that every blockage becomes a catastrophe.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Uranus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies sudden thought or events through restriction, concentration and an impression that lasts. Traffic blockage or sudden restriction of movement gives the interruption a concrete form; ongoing technical conversation and discussing new things thoroughly preserve a concentrated intellectual expression. News of depressing events and Witte's lingering sorrow after a sudden catastrophe mark the severe range, without predicting an event or diagnosing depression. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
