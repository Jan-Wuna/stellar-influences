## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Uranus = Chiron`, Chiron specifies original communication as helping others open their minds and recognize thoughts of their own. The entry links that assistance with moving beyond limiting ideas and the patterns of mass consciousness. Its focus is intellectual freedom through communication, without a medical claim or a guarantee that every unconventional idea is sound. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
