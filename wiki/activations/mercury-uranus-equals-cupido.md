## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden ideas, news and technical activity take communal or artistic form.
- psychology: An agitated young community and sudden artistic thought are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Wedding messages, shared technology and community conversations are social settings.
- events/manifestations: Technical and dramatic arts, group movement and media communities are examples.
- conflicts/notes: The entries support communal stimulation without assigning the same meaning to other triad orientations.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes artistic and wedding messages; ABC emphasizes communal movement and technology. These settings do not establish a disagreement.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Uranus = Cupido`, Cupido specifies sudden communication and technical ideas through shared life and the arts. A wedding telegram, sudden artistic train of thought or technical and dramatic art gives Witte's concrete forms; ABC extends the range to lively community conversations, dynamic group movement and shared transport or media technology. New ideas circulate in a communal setting, with agitation also possible in a young group. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
