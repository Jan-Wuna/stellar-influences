### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Uranus`; printed p. `249`; one-based PDF page `123` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Exciting youth crime, lack of movement through technology, talk about technical dangers, talk about events of the past, lack of impulses for the youth, consider technical disadvantages, and describe technical innovations

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden news and technical thought encounter disturbing events, disadvantages or the past.
- psychology: Thinking about technical dangers or disadvantages is explicit.
- body/health: Witte's vegetative and digestive nerve associations remain historical, not diagnostic.
- social/relationship: Youth crime and instigation of robbery or murder are severe source examples, not character judgments.
- events/manifestations: Terrible messages, restricted movement through technology and descriptions of innovations are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Technical innovation remains present beside danger; neither criminality nor illness follows from the formula alone.

## Contradictions

Technical innovation and disadvantage coexist in ABC. Witte's severe criminal and nerve examples do not define every expression of this orientation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Uranus = Hades`, Hades specifies sudden communication through disturbing news, technical disadvantages or events from the past. Discussion can identify technical dangers and lack of movement or impulses, while describing technical innovations remains part of the same entry. Witte's terrible messages and criminal instigation give severe examples; his vegetative and digestive nerve associations remain attributed historical doctrine rather than diagnoses or predictions.
