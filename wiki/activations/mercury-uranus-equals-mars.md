## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Uranus`; one-based PDF page `163` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

To incite to violence. Suddenly executed action. The work as an astrologer. A message about a fact.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Uranus`; printed p. `248`; one-based PDF page `123` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Exciting youth work, renewal of thinking and acting, excitement of youth about work, new language connections, technical thought work, technical work reports, technical media work, and technical actions discuss

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury/Uranus`; printed p. `158`; one-based PDF page `160`; entry code `0510` (actual PDF passage checked).

Summing up situations quickly and acting accordingly, courage, determination, sudden success and advancement in life. A sudden realization of ideas, assaults, nervousness.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Mercury/Uranus midpoint`; one-based PDF page `48` (exact oriented passage checked in the actual PDF text).

To act with inspired cleverness. To have one’s own way of doing things. To find it difficult to fit in with the agendas of others, or to be told what to do. Swift assessment of a situation plus the ability to take quick action. Anger can upset the nervous system.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MERCURY/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `164`

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds quickness and sharpness to your mind and wit; an abrasiveness when addressing others; you may be very restless but do need to be active; balks at allowing others to commit time or resources; hastens insightful thinking.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Quick assessment of ideas becomes immediate or independent action.
- psychology: Courage, determination and inspired cleverness coexist with restlessness or anger.
- body/health: Nervousness and anger upsetting the nervous system are attributed associations, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Abrasive address and difficulty following others' agendas are stated risks.
- events/manifestations: Sudden realization, technical work and astrological work are examples beside incitement or assault.
- conflicts/notes: Rapid action may be constructive or violent; the formula alone does not determine which.

## Contradictions

The entries allow swift insight and useful technical action while retaining abrasive or violent examples. These should not be merged into a claim that speed requires harm.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Uranus = Mars`, Mars specifies quick, original thought as action taken after a rapid assessment. Inspired cleverness, determination and sharp wit support an independent way of working, including technical thinking, reports and astrological work. ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry))

The same speed can make address abrasive or create difficulty with others' agendas. Incitement and assault are severe source examples, while restlessness, nervousness and anger's effect on the nervous system remain attributed associations rather than predictions or diagnoses. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry))
