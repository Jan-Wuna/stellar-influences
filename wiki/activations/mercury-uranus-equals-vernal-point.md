## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Uranus = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies sudden communication in public life. Exciting news, agitated conversations, media technology and reports about public events carry the immediate message outward; reform among youth and new public thinking give it a social and intellectual setting. The entry identifies public communication rather than a particular private relationship. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
