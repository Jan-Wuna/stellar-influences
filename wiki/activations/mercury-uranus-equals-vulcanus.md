## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Uranus`; one-based PDF page `163` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

To comprehend (grasp) fast and good. Fast thinking. Potent original thoughts. Sudden news creating, causing great excitement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Uranus`; printed p. `249`; one-based PDF page `123` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Excitement about youth violence, reports about tremendous events, dynamic youth power, excited talk about violence, strong impulses of youth, tension through verbal force, tension by powerful thinking, and report about technical energies

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Fast comprehension and original thought carry powerful excitement.
- psychology: Tension accompanies powerful thinking and strong impulses.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Reports and conversations about youth violence or verbal force are severe social examples.
- events/manifestations: Sudden news, tremendous events and technical energies are concrete manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: Quick comprehension does not require violent expression; the entries retain both capacities and difficult examples.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses potent thought and exciting news; ABC includes violence and tension. These are different expressions rather than evidence that all powerful thought is harmful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Uranus = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies fast comprehension through potent original thought and news that creates great excitement. ABC extends that force to reports of tremendous events, technical energies and strong impulses among youth. Conversations about violence, verbal force and tension from powerful thinking preserve the difficult range beside the intellectual capacity. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
