## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Venus`; one-based PDF page `159` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

To have few acquaintances. Cold behavior towards new friends. New friends given the cold shoulder. Disorderly conduct.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Venus`; printed p. `241`; one-based PDF page `119` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Ongoing peace conversations, depressing art reports, consolidation through love conversations, harmony through depth of thought, thorough description of art, suppression of love in youth, resistance from young women.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affectionate or artistic communication meets depth, persistence or social restriction.
- psychology: Deep thought can support harmony, while coldness or depressing reports remain possible.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Few acquaintances, cold treatment of new friends and young women's resistance are examples.
- events/manifestations: Ongoing peace conversations and thorough artistic description sit beside suppressed youthful love.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's former support of love reversed the actual suppression clause.

## Contradictions

Witte's cold social treatment and ABC's harmony through depth are distinct possibilities; neither should erase the other. Suppression, not support, is the difficult youth-love clause.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies affectionate or artistic communication through persistence, depth and restriction. Ongoing peace conversations, stabilizing love conversations and harmony through deep thought preserve a constructive concentrated expression, alongside thorough description of art. ([ABC](#abc-entry))

The social range also includes few acquaintances, cold behavior toward new friends and disorderly conduct. Depressing artistic reports, suppression of love in youth and resistance from young women retain difficulty rather than turning persistence into an automatic promise of harmony. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
