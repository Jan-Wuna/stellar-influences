## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Mercury + Venus`; one-based PDF page `159` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

Many new relations, very popular, sure and good manners.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Merkur + Venus`; printed p. `241`; one-based PDF page `119` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Expansion of media art, success through harmonious speaking, successful peace conversations, freedom of thought in art, love experiences in youth, description of art through science, speaking much about love, many beautiful words.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Harmonious speech and artistic thought extend into many relationships or wider success.
- psychology: Freedom of thought in art and affection are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Popularity, good manners, many new ties and love experiences in youth are examples.
- events/manifestations: Expanding media art, successful peace conversations and scientific description of art are manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's final clause is many beautiful words, not many nice places.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize expansion and harmonious communication without recording a direct disagreement among their examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies harmonious communication through many new relationships, popularity and assured manners. The same widening reaches artistic thought: media art expands, free thought works within art, and harmonious speech or peace conversations can succeed. Scientific description of art, youthful love experiences, much talk of love and beautiful words preserve specific expressions of this range. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
