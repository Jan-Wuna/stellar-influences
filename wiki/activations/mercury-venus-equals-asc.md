## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 159 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Venus`.

#### Witte Entry

To meet with friends and new people. The art of being adaptable.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 240; one-based PDF p. 119 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Venus`.

#### ABC Entry

Peace conversations in the environment, peaceful youth at the place, harmonious conversations with a partner, a young female partner, description of art at the place, media art at the place, a place for the art of language, conversations with a partner about love.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Evidence locator: printed p. 147 (coded entry 0468); source heading: `Mercury/Venus`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Appreciation of pleasing manners or proper behavior when in contact with others, talent for establishing social contacts or the gift to entertain people well, a sense of tact or discretion, adaptable, ability to create a beautiful environment.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 42 (exact oriented passage checked in actual PDF text); source heading: `Planets activating the Mercury/Venus midpoint`.

#### Sandbach Entry

One comes across to others as a smooth and flexible person, an artful communicator. The ability to project oneself in an accommodating, diplomatic and/or persuasive manner. Playing the role of conciliator.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Evidence locator: printed p. 148 (entry page); source heading: `MERCURY/VENUS with Planets and Points`.

- Source heading: `MERCURY/VENUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `148`

#### Munkasey Entry

Assessing the sentiments of others and judging their ability to express their love and feelings for you in positive ways; emphasized responses received from others when discussing affections; a setting showing love and beauty.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Tactful expression and appreciation of beauty shape encounters with others.
- psychology: Adaptability, diplomacy and an accommodating presentation are explicit.
- body/health: These entries make no specific health claim.
- social/relationship: Friendship, new acquaintances, returned affection and conversations with a partner about love recur.
- events/manifestations: Conciliation and a beautiful local setting unite social and aesthetic examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's omitted love-conversation clause and generic partner wording are repaired from the actual row.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize adaptable contact and reciprocal affection without stating directly opposing claims. Their descriptions of tact do not establish loss of candor.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Asc`, Asc specifies affectionate or artistic communication through the way one meets and responds to others. Tact, adaptable manners and artful expression help create contact and an agreeable setting; assessing how love is returned adds a reciprocal dimension. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))

ABC makes the local setting concrete through peace conversations, art and media activity, a young female partner and conversations with a partner about love. These examples preserve aesthetic surroundings alongside personal exchange. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
