## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 159 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Venus`.

#### Witte Entry

New acquaintances through social, family relations or through art work. Social manners. Master of ceremonies. Virtuoso in playing solos (violin etc.).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 241; one-based PDF p. 119 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Venus`.

#### ABC Entry

Reports from a peace community, a mobile community of artists, an intellectual community of artists, a young community of love, a community of media artists, shared conversations about love, shared description of art.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Artistic and agreeable expression takes a shared social or family form.
- psychology: Social manners and skilled performance are prominent in Witte.
- body/health: No bodily or health correspondence is stated.
- social/relationship: New acquaintances, peace or artistic communities and shared love conversations are examples.
- events/manifestations: Masters of ceremonies, solo virtuosity and shared description of art are concrete manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: Individual performance and communal art are related settings rather than mutually exclusive claims.

## Contradictions

The examples span individual performance within social life and collective art; they are not mutually exclusive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Cupido`, Cupido gives agreeable expression and artistic skill a social setting: people meet through family ties, shared occasions or art. The master of ceremonies and solo virtuoso make that participation concrete, while artistic and media communities, shared conversations about love and shared description of art extend it into collective exchange. These are related forms of social participation rather than a promise of an occupation. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
