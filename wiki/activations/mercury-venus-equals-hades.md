## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 159 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Venus`.

#### Witte Entry

Acquaintance with vulgar things or base men. Bad manners. Rudeness. Unethical artistic occupation. Immoral performances. Preference for riches of the earth.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 241; one-based PDF p. 119 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Venus`.

#### ABC Entry

Old art of writing, reports about criminality in art, conversations about renunciation of love, thinking about love sorrow, criminal young love, sorrowful youthful love, inadequate art of language, dirty conversations about love, young people's preference for danger.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affectionate or artistic expression encounters sorrow, renunciation or ethically contested contact.
- psychology: Love sorrow, rough manners and inadequate language are source themes.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Difficult acquaintances, troubled youthful love and crude conversations are examples.
- events/manifestations: Old writing arts, crime reports in art and interest in earthly riches are manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: Historical criminal and moral labels remain attributed; the scan repairs former love-forgottenness and hearty-love mistranslations.

## Contradictions

Witte's moral judgments and ABC's love sorrow or renunciation are different emphases. Neither supplies facts about a person's character merely from the formula.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Hades`, Hades specifies affectionate or artistic communication through troubled contact, sorrow or renunciation. Witte emphasizes rough manners and artistic activity judged unethical in that source's vocabulary; ABC adds old writing arts, reports about crime in art, thoughts of love sorrow and painful youthful love. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

Inadequate expression, crude love conversations and attraction to danger remain difficult possibilities, beside Witte's preference for earthly riches. The source's moral and criminal labels remain attributed rather than facts about a person's character.
