- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 159 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Venus`.

High social connections. Promotion through acquaintance. Polite. Artistic taste of highest degree. Distinguished forms of behavior. Art critic.

- Evidence locator: printed p. 241; one-based PDF p. 119 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Venus`.

#### ABC Entry

Thinking about great love, special peace conversations, conversations with an authority on art, a young female chief, independent media art, state and media in harmony, goodwill from the youth authority, a benevolent media authority.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Tasteful expression and acquaintanceship meet distinction, promotion or artistic authority.
- psychology: Politeness and highly developed taste are Witte's explicit emphases.
- body/health: No bodily or health association is given.
- social/relationship: High social connections and promotion through acquaintances appear in [Witte](#witte-entry).
- events/manifestations: The art critic, conversations with an authority on art and independent media art are specific examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's former authorship wording mistranslated authority; the scan preserves youth and media authorities.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes distinction and promotion through contacts; ABC extends the institutional range to art, media and youth authorities. Different examples do not establish disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Kronos`, Kronos specifies artistic and social expression through distinction, authority and standing. Refined conduct and highly developed taste sit beside influential acquaintances or promotion; the art critic is one concrete example. ABC extends this range to conversations with an authority on art, a young female chief, independent media art and benevolent youth or media authorities, keeping thought of great love and special peace conversations alongside institutional settings. These are possible forms of elevated contact rather than guaranteed advancement. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
