## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 159 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Venus`.

#### Witte Entry

Youthful beauty. Female acquaintance. To be fond of flat artwork (paintings etc.)

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 240; one-based PDF p. 119 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Venus`.

#### ABC Entry

Peace conversations for a people, a woman's thoughts about art, a woman's harmonious thoughts, a young beautiful woman, a woman's artful language, a woman's thoughts of love, a woman's beautiful words, conversations about feelings of love, describing folk art.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Evidence locator: printed p. 146 (coded entry 0460); source heading: `Mercury/Venus`.

#### Ebertin Entry

The ability to shape plain surfaces, a talent for painting or drawing (Sun = living body, Moon = plain surface, Mercury = a drawn outline of a shape), a perception and feeling of beauty. A beautiful young person, a person who is susceptible to beauty, a love contact.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 41 (exact oriented passage checked in actual PDF text); source heading: `Planets activating the Mercury/Venus midpoint`.

#### Sandbach Entry

The emotional need to communicate about what one loves. Feeling uncertain or vulnerable about the value of one’s own ideas. Harmonious and peaceful communica tion gives one a feeling of security.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Evidence locator: printed p. 148 (entry page); source heading: `MERCURY/VENUS with Planets and Points`.

- Source heading: `MERCURY/VENUS with Planets and Points`

#### Munkasey Entry

Expressions of (so-called) “feminine” values in life; feelings for what forms of pleasure another will accept; increased sensitivity toward the affection and pleasure others want and desire; discussions on love and romance.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Feeling for beauty and emotional security shape artistic or affectionate expression.
- psychology: Sandbach emphasizes vulnerability about one's ideas and reassurance through peaceful communication.
- body/health: Youthful beauty is mentioned, but no medical condition is established.
- social/relationship: Love contacts and sensitivity to others' desired affection occur in the sources.
- events/manifestations: Drawing, painting and shaping flat surfaces are specific Witte/Ebertin examples.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered language belongs to the sources; it is not a universal claim about women or men.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Moon`, Moon specifies affectionate or artistic expression through feeling and receptivity to beauty. Painting, drawing and shaping flat surfaces give that sensitivity a concrete outlet. The emotional need to speak about what one loves can coexist with vulnerability about one's ideas; peaceful communication provides a sense of security. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))

Sensitivity to another's desired affection connects with conversations about love's feelings, beautiful words and folk art. The sources' examples involving women and Munkasey's explicitly qualified 'feminine' values remain attributed, rather than universal claims about gender. ([ABC](#abc-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))
