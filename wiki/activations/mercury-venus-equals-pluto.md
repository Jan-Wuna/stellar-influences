## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 159 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Venus`.

#### Witte Entry

Deep reaching change in acquaintances. Development of beauty, art, love. Development of greeting or personal behavior.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 241; one-based PDF p. 119 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Venus`.

#### ABC Entry

Unfolding of opinions in art, development of media art, development of young love, conversations about development of the feminine principle, harmonious development of thought, developing the art of language, liking an exchange of words.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Evidence locator: printed p. 147 (coded entry 0466); source heading: `Mercury/Venus`.

#### Ebertin Entry

A tremendous creative power, recognition of art as a mission, one-sided or too specialized artistic objectives. Peculiar contacts or acquaintances.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 42 (exact oriented passage checked in actual PDF text); source heading: `Planets activating the Mercury/Venus midpoint`.

#### Sandbach Entry

An intensely powerful manner of artful communication. The ability to be seduc tive with words. Smooth and/or polished communication that has a deep impact on others. A profound involvement with the beauty of language. To be obsessed with thoughts of love.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Evidence locator: printed p. 148 (entry page); source heading: `MERCURY/VENUS with Planets and Points`.

- Source heading: `MERCURY/VENUS with Planets and Points`

#### Munkasey Entry

Intensified desires to use others for the pleasures they may give; going to extremes with the thoughts of pleasure you may derive from another; verbalizing and sharing ideas with another person on what love can be.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Artistic and affectionate expression becomes intense or undergoes deep development.
- psychology: Creative absorption, specialized aims and obsession with love occur in the sources.
- body/health: No specific health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Acquaintances may change deeply; words may have a strong seductive impact.
- events/manifestations: Art as a mission and development of beauty or language are stated examples.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's use of others for pleasure is a particular risk, not proof that all charm is manipulative.

## Contradictions

Munkasey's use of others for pleasure is a particular risk, not proof that all charm is manipulative.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies affectionate or artistic expression through intensity and deep development. Acquaintances, personal manners, beauty and love can undergo change; creative power can make art a mission or narrow its aims into one-sided specialization. Development of media art, language and youthful love preserves this movement within communication and relationships. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

Polished or seductive words may have a deep impact, with profound involvement in language or preoccupation with love. Munkasey also allows extremes of pleasure-seeking that use another person, placing an ethical difficulty beside the creative intensity. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))
