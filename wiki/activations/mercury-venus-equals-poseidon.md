## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies affectionate or artistic communication through ideas, insight and spiritual matters that can bring people into friendship. Discussion of spiritual harmony, a culture of peace and the beauty of language links understanding with agreeable expression; clear artistic description and speaking about love of truth preserve more specific intellectual settings. These examples support thoughtful artistic and social exchange without making a spiritual conclusion compulsory. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
