## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies affectionate or artistic expression through seriousness, concentration and restraint. Quiet conditions, thorough work and organized thought support demanding creation, including wood carving or work in hard materials. Finding diplomatic words may be difficult even when expression can be clear, simple and focused. ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry))

Older acquaintances, distance from friends and caution about romantic commitment give that restraint a relational setting. ABC adds harmonious separation, disturbed or serious peace conversations, inhibited young love and young love in old age. Munkasey's shortened talk and long discussions both remain possible, rather than silence defining every expression. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))
