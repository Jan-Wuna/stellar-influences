## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Acquaintance with older or more serious people. Travelling-acquaintance. Falling out with friends, quarrelling with them, to separate. Retiring disposition. To be surly. Wood carver.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Thinking of harmonious separation, serious peace language, inhibited young love, stewed peace language, young love in old age, concentration on media arts, discussing artistic tasks, discussing love problems, and fondness of old languages

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Thoroughness, a serious attitude toward life, desire to contemplate or meditate deeply in retirement and solitude, creative activity under quiet conditions. Difficult art work, creative design or form shaped from hard or heavy material, a difficult artistic career.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Practicality manifesting through cleverness. An ability to organize thoughts in a beautiful, simple and/or profound manner. Difficulty in finding the right words to put an idea across diplomatically or persuasively, but an ability to do so in a clear, simple, fo cused way.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Shortened talk when discussing your affections and love for another; accepting rejections of your love calmly; caution in your expressions of love; you do not obligate yourself to romance easily; long discussions on love.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Venus = Saturn`: dependable courtesy, thoughtful affection, and the ability to communicate care without excess. Main caution: inhibited charm: expression can become too formal, withheld, or afraid to risk genuine softness.
