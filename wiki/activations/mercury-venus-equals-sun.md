## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Personal meeting or greeting of friends or new acquaintances. Love of one’s own body. Vanity. Taste for plastic arts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joy in the youth center, talk to a gentle man, love of a young man, media arts center, personal thoughts on the arts, personal opinion on love, gentle words of the man, and beauty of human movement

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

An appreciation and keen sense of physical form, sculpture, architecture, a sense of beauty, vanity or conceit, self-glorification. Sympathy and compassion, thoughts of love, creative activity, an art which can be of practical use, recognition.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A potential master of beautiful communication. The ability to be very persuasive when one speaks or writes. Goals attained through the use of diplomacy and artful communication. An egotist in love with his or her own ideas.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Your determination to effectively use your physical assets to appear more attractive to others; the convictions with which you convey love or affection; satisfaction with the person you are; humorous forms of expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Venus = Sun`: attractive speech, likable selfhood, and the ability to lead through warmth, tact, and verbal ease. Main caution: vanity of expression: identity can become too dependent on approval, polish, or being well received.
