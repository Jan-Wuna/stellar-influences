## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Pleasant expression, appearance and acquaintanceship take a public form.
- psychology: ABC mentions harmonious thoughts addressed to public life.
- body/health: Appearance is mentioned; no medical claim is made.
- social/relationship: Witte describes becoming acquainted with many people.
- events/manifestations: Fashion, public art description and media art in public life are the concrete settings.
- conflicts/notes: Public scope is supported; the sources do not establish fame or lasting popularity.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies affectionate or artistic expression through public encounters. Becoming acquainted with many people, fashion and attractive appearance connect with peace conversations, beautiful words and harmonious thoughts in public life. Public description of art, media art and young women appearing in public retain particular settings of this outward expression, without guaranteeing recognition or lasting popularity. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
