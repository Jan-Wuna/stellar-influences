### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 241; one-based PDF p. 119 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Venus`.

#### ABC Entry

influence of media art, influential conversations about art, strength and joy of youth, forceful thoughts of peace, harmony through the strength of thoughts, harmonious influence of speech, news about violence in love, and beauty as moving energy

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Artistic or agreeable expression has influence and strong social reception.
- psychology: Forceful thoughts of peace and the influence of speech appear in ABC.
- body/health: Youthful strength is an ABC example, not a medical assertion.
- social/relationship: Witte emphasizes firm new friendships, welcome and friends' favor.
- events/manifestations: Influential art discussion and media art are specific ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's report of violence in love must remain distinct from the favorable friendship examples.

## Contradictions

ABC's report of violence in love must remain distinct from the favorable friendship examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies affectionate or artistic expression through influence and strong social reception. A welcome, firm new friendships and friends' favor sit beside influential art discussion, media art, powerful peace thoughts and harmony through thought or speech. Youthful strength and joy, and beauty as moving energy, preserve further ABC examples. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also names news about violence in love. That adverse association remains separate from favorable friendship and harmonious influence; it cannot be recast as merely stronger affection. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
