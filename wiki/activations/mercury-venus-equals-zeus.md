## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 159 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Venus`.

#### Witte Entry

Acquaintances by order or work. To be forced to greet the superiors. Incendiary mania. Pyrotechnist. Artist with fireworks.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 241; one-based PDF p. 119 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Venus`.

#### ABC Entry

Strenuous peace conversations, a leading opinion on art, the beauty of creative language, harmonious conversations about procreation, powerful media art, procreation through love in youth, creative art of writing, female youth leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Artistic or social expression meets directed work, obligation or creative production.
- psychology: The entries do not supply a general character description.
- body/health: Witte's historical "incendiary mania" is an attributed claim, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Witte describes work-based acquaintances and obligatory greetings to superiors.
- events/manifestations: Pyrotechnics, fireworks art, creative writing, powerful media art and conversations about procreation are explicit examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's art opinion and procreation clauses are restored from the scan; historical medical and sexual associations remain attributed.

## Contradictions

Directed work, imposed greetings, creative production and procreation discussions are different examples. The historical incendiary-mania phrase does not define the entire orientation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Venus = Zeus`, Zeus specifies affectionate or artistic expression through directed creation, work or imposed social forms. Acquaintances can arise through work or orders, and greetings may be required by hierarchy; pyrotechnics and fireworks are concrete artistic examples. ABC adds creative writing and language, powerful media art, a leading opinion on art and strenuous peace conversations. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

Conversations about procreation, procreation through love in youth and female youth leadership remain specific ABC associations. Witte's separate historical phrase 'incendiary mania' is retained as an attributed claim rather than a diagnosis or a conclusion about the person. ([ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry))
