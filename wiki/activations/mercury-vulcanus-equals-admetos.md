## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

Important thoughts, which at the beginning are only partly successful, or not at all.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 267; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

End of violence among youth, reports about restrictions on power, deepening conversations about power, thorough media power, news about energy limits, strong deepening of thought, heavy traffic congestion, suppressed power of young people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong thought or influence meets restriction or incomplete initial success.
- psychology: ABC includes concentrated or deepened thinking.
- body/health: No specific health correspondence is supplied.
- social/relationship: Youth and media appear as settings of restricted power in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Partial initial success, energy limits and traffic congestion are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte limits incomplete success to the beginning. ABC's literal 'thorough media power' is not further explained by the entry.

## Contradictions

Witte limits incomplete success to the beginning; this is not lifelong failure. ABC adds restrictions and suppressed power as well as the ending of youth violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies forceful thought through limits and concentrated depth. Important ideas may at first succeed only partly or not at all; power or energy can be restricted, while conversations about power and thought itself deepen. Heavy traffic congestion and suppressed youthful power provide concrete restrictive settings. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also names the ending of violence among youth. This distinct example and Witte's explicit qualification about the beginning prevent the orientation from being reduced to permanent failure or unending obstruction. ([ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry))
