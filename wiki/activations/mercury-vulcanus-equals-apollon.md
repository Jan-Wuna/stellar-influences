## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

Thoughts which prove to be correct. Successful thinking.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 267; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of violence among youth, reports about quantities of energy, influence of scientific opinions, influential trade conversations, successful influence of young people, increased media power, knowledge of the power of thoughts, increasing energy of youth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful thinking and expanding influence are the main themes.
- psychology: Witte describes thoughts that prove correct, rather than a general guarantee of infallibility.
- body/health: Youthful energy in ABC is not a health diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Youth, trade and scientific communication supply collective settings.
- events/manifestations: Media power and reports about energy quantities are specific ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes expansion of violence as well as successful influence; expansion is not necessarily beneficial.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies strong thought through successful results and wider influence. Thinking that proves correct sits beside influential scientific opinions, trade conversations, increased media power and knowledge of thought's power. Reports about energy quantities and growing youthful energy preserve concrete forms of expansion. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also lists expansion of violence among youth. That adverse collective example remains distinct from successful thinking, so increased influence is not necessarily beneficial and correctness is not a promise of infallibility. ([ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry))
