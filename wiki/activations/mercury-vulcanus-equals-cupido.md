## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

The thoughts are concentrated on the family, the arts or a big organization.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 267; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

Influence of a young community, shared thought energy, shared conversations about power, conversations about the influence of art, violence among youth in the community, strength of a young community, power of the media community, a strong transport community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought and influence are directed toward a family, art or organization.
- psychology: Concentration on collective concerns is explicit in Witte.
- body/health: No specific bodily implication is stated.
- social/relationship: Shared thought and community influence are ABC themes.
- events/manifestations: Family, arts, large organizations, media communities and a strong transport community are concrete settings.
- conflicts/notes: Shared influence includes artistic and organizational strength alongside a distinct youth-violence example.

## Contradictions

Concentration on family or art, communal strength and youth violence are different examples. Collective influence does not itself establish violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Cupido`, Cupido specifies concentrated thought through family, art and organizational life. Shared mental energy and conversations about power or art's influence connect with the strength of young, media or transport communities. The social focus is therefore both what occupies thought and how groups exert influence. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

Violence among youth within a community remains one adverse ABC setting, alongside family concerns and artistic or organizational strength. It does not redefine every collective use of influence as violence. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
