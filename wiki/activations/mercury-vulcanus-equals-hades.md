## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

The thoughts are directed toward evil deeds. Important news about the past.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 267; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

Reports about dangers of power, dangerous media power, conversations about renunciation of power, talk about hidden power, enormous deprivations in youth, criminal power of youth, defective thinking power, and hidden power of youth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful thought and reporting concern danger, hidden power or the past.
- psychology: Witte's direction toward evil deeds is a historical source judgment.
- body/health: ABC's "defective thinking power" does not establish a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Youthful deprivation and concealed influence occur in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Important news about the past is a distinct Witte example.
- conflicts/notes: ABC explicitly includes renunciation of power, replacing the former power-abuse mistranslation; its criminal and moral examples remain attributed.

## Contradictions

Renouncing power, hidden influence and dangerous power are distinct ABC examples. Witte's moral judgment about evil deeds does not define every expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Hades`, Hades specifies powerful thought or news through danger, hidden influence and the past. Reports about dangerous power or media influence sit beside conversations about renouncing power and youthful deprivation. Witte's important news about the past provides a separate historical focus. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

The sources also give evil deeds, criminal youthful power and deficient thinking power as difficult examples. These remain attributed associations rather than a judgment that the person is inherently criminal or a diagnosis of intellectual capacity. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
