## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

To concentrate strongly on one’s own fortune. The feeling of bliss.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 266; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

Influence through the administration of justice, influence of joyful thoughts, successful media power, financial strength of youth, joy through the power of thought, conversations about the power of money, powerful movements of money, reports about legal force.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong thought centers on fortune, joy or successful influence.
- psychology: Concentration on one's own fortune and bliss are Witte's exact emphases.
- body/health: No health outcome is stated.
- social/relationship: Youth, media and the administration of justice supply ABC's collective settings.
- events/manifestations: Money movements and discussion of money's power are specific ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Fortune and financial or legal influence are associations, not promised results; the scan restores Recht-Sprechung as administration of justice.

## Contradictions

Concentration on fortune, joyful thought and financial or legal influence are different examples rather than guaranteed wealth or legal success.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies forceful thinking through one's fortune, joy and favorable influence. Strong concentration on personal fortune and a feeling of bliss sit beside joy through thought, successful media power and the influence of joyful ideas. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

Financial strength among youth, powerful money movements, conversations about money's power and influence through the administration of justice provide concrete collective settings. These are stated associations rather than a promise of wealth or a particular legal outcome. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
