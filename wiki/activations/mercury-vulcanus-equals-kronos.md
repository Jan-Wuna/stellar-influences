## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Kronos`, Kronos specifies forceful thought and communication through affairs of state. Political thinking, debates and conversations connect with reports about state force, the power of authorities and the influence of youth or traffic. These examples identify the subject and institutional setting of communication without establishing that the speaker holds office. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC retains violent or forceful collective examples. The German power/force vocabulary has contextual range, so the source's state and traffic phrases do not establish that every exercise of authority is physical violence. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
