## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong, rapid thought is expressed personally.
- psychology: Thinking ability and speed are Witte's main claims.
- body/health: Personal energy is mentioned, but no health correspondence is given.
- social/relationship: Influence on opinion is an ABC theme.
- events/manifestations: Writing about power, speaking of violence and reporting on strength of soul are distinct personal statements.
- conflicts/notes: Mental strength is not a claim that every thought is correct; no direct incompatible statement is present.

## Contradictions

Mental strength is not a claim that every thought is correct; no direct incompatible statement is present.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = MC`, MC specifies forceful thought through one's own mental capacity and expression. Strong, rapid thinking connects with personal statements about writing on power, influencing opinion, movement energy and strength in youth. ABC also gives soul-strength reports and the emotional influence of language, preserving an inner-life dimension beside outward impact. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

Speaking about violence is a separate ABC example; it does not establish violent conduct. Mental strength likewise does not guarantee that every thought is correct or every expression effective. ([ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry))
