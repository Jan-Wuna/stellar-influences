## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

The public under the impact of a mighty or great idea or an enormous might. To get on and off, to ride back and forth. Riding people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 266; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

A woman's influential words, influence of young women, emotional strength in conversation, conversations about the power of a people, power of young women, media influence among a people, opinions of an influential woman, a strong women's movement, speaking about emotional strength.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful ideas or force affect a public; movement is a distinct Witte motif.
- psychology: A public's reception of a great idea and emotional strength in conversation are explicit.
- body/health: The entries do not establish a specific health condition.
- social/relationship: Women, youth and a public appear in ABC's social examples.
- events/manifestations: Riding back and forth and media influence are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's emotional-strength phrases are restored; its gendered examples remain attributed rather than universal properties.

## Contradictions

Public influence, emotional strength, movement and women's collective examples are different manifestations rather than universal traits of women.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Moon`, Moon specifies powerful thought through its impact on a public and the force of feeling in communication. A great idea or enormous power can affect a public; influential words, media influence, a strong women's movement and conversations about a people's power give collective examples. Emotional strength in conversation and speaking about that strength connect feeling with expression. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))

Witte separately gives getting on and off and riding back and forth as movement examples. ABC's recurring women and young women remain source-specific settings, not universal properties assigned to gender. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
