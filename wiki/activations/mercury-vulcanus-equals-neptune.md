## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

Great intuition. Great ability for sympathetic understanding. Mighty thoughts for the future. Great entanglement or confusion. Illusion and deception.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 267; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

Reports about water energy, influence of thinking on the future, foreign power of thought, conversations about the influence of air, conversations about unknown forces, power through foreign languages, powerful air movement, young people made insecure by violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intuitive or future-oriented thought coexists with illusion and confusion.
- psychology: Strong sympathetic understanding and entanglement are both Witte possibilities.
- body/health: No particular health outcome is stated.
- social/relationship: Foreign languages and young people made insecure by violence are intact ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: Water energy, powerful air movement, unknown forces and thought about the future are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: The actual eight-clause Neptune row is restored; the former tail absorbed the following Pluto row.

## Contradictions

Witte preserves intuitive understanding beside confusion or deception. ABC's physical, foreign-language and youth examples do not establish that either psychological expression is universal.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies powerful thought through intuition, sympathetic understanding and the future, while also allowing confusion, illusion or deception. The strength of an impression therefore belongs beside uncertainty about it rather than establishing its truth. ([Witte](#witte-entry))

ABC gives reports about water energy, powerful air movement, conversations about unknown forces and power through foreign languages as distinct settings. Its final example concerns young people made insecure by violence. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
