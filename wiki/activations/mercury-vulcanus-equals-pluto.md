## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

To be very agile. To develop great speed. To recognize a mighty change.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 267; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

Energetic exchanges of words, conversations about changes in power, development of violence among youth, developing powerful thoughts, reports about developments in power, strong development of traffic, growing media power, changing influence of thought.

Editorial note: The actual scan assigns the complete eight-clause row to Pluto. Its opening clauses had been absorbed into the preceding Neptune extraction; the split word `Ju-gend` belongs to this Pluto row.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Developing force in speech and thought accompanies agility, acceleration and recognition of major change.
- psychology: Powerful ideas develop while influence over thought can change.
- body/health: Agility and speed of movement are explicit; no illness is specified.
- social/relationship: Exchanges about changing power and growing media influence share this orientation; youth violence remains a separate source example.
- events/manifestations: Reports of power developments, increasing traffic and energetic exchanges provide concrete settings.
- conflicts/notes: The scan restores the complete Pluto row across a broken extraction boundary; changes in power do not themselves imply violence.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Agility, changing influence and youth violence are distinct examples within the same oriented row; the violent example does not define every form of development.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Pluto`, forceful thought and movement develop through change: quickness and agility accompany recognition of a major shift, while strong ideas, exchanges about changing power and reports of power developments give that shift a communicative form. Growing media influence, increasing traffic and changing influence over thought extend the theme into collective channels. The source also names developing violence among youth as a particular manifestation, which must remain distinct from energetic discussion or rapid movement. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
