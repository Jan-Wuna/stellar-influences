## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

The thoughts are concentrated on mental matters. To develop great ideas.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 267; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

Spiritual energies of youth, conversations about the power of truth, thorough cultural reports, clarity of strong youth, powerful ideas among youth, cultural influence of youth, power of spiritualized thoughts, reports of cultural deepening.

Editorial note: The scan reads `gründliche Kultur-Berichte`, translated here as “thorough cultural reports.”

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Concentrated mental energy develops large ideas and cultural or intellectual influence.
- psychology: Strong ideas, clarity and attention to truth are linked with sustained thought.
- body/health: No health correspondence is supplied by these entries.
- social/relationship: Youth's cultural influence and intellectual energies provide the collective setting.
- events/manifestations: Conversations about the power of truth, thorough cultural reports and news of cultural deepening are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: The source's association with truth does not certify a particular belief or claim.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Mental concentration and cultural influence overlap; the language of truth remains a source association, not a guarantee that an idea is true.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Poseidon`, concentrated thought gives force to substantial ideas and intellectual or cultural expression. The emphasis on great ideas takes a collective form in the energies, clarity and cultural influence attributed to youth; conversations about the power of truth, thorough cultural reporting and news of cultural deepening are specific ways that influence is expressed. The association with truth describes the subject and aspiration of thought, without validating any particular belief. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
