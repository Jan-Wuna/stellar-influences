## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

To be physically much in motion. A life or mode of living which demands much moving around. Great speed of a body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 266; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

Report by an influential man, description of a violent person, influence of young people, energy-reporting center, bodily strength in youth, powerful bodily movements, powerful youth center, strength in personal conversation.

Editorial note: The scan reads `Bericht eines einflußreichen Mannes`, `Energie-Melde-Zentrale` and `stark im persönlichen Gespräch`; the translated phrases retain the distinct report, center and personal-conversation examples.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Force and speed take bodily and personal forms, alongside influential reporting and conversation.
- psychology: Strength in personal conversation is explicit; no broader mental disposition is established.
- body/health: Much physical movement, bodily speed and youthful strength are named without a medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: The source names an influential man, young people's influence and a powerful youth center.
- events/manifestations: A mobile way of life, powerful bodily movements and an energy-reporting center are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: The violent-person example is distinct from bodily vigor; the gendered man example is preserved as source wording.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Physical vigor, personal influence and a description of a violent person are separate examples; the violence example does not turn bodily speed into a diagnosis.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Sun`, forceful movement becomes bodily and personal: much moving around can characterize a way of life, with physical speed, youthful strength and powerful bodily movements as explicit examples. Influence also appears through a man's report, young people, a youth center and strength in personal conversation; the energy-reporting center gives the reporting theme an institutional form. The separate description of a violent person should retain its source-specific meaning without equating vigor or influence with violence. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
