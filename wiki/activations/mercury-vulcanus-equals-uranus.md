## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

To get suddenly excited or into motion through surprising news or telegrams.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 267; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

Influence through exciting conversations, reports of tremendous events, dynamic energy of youth, reform of the power media, strong nervousness in traffic, new reports about violence, strong impulses of youth, conversations about powerful events.

Editorial note: The scan reads `Reform der Macht-Medien`, translated here as “reform of the power media.”

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Surprising news and exciting conversation suddenly stir forceful thought or movement.
- psychology: Excitement, strong impulses and nervousness are explicit.
- body/health: Strong nervousness in traffic is named without a clinical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Youthful energy and reform of powerful media are social settings.
- events/manifestations: News of violence, reports of tremendous events and telegrams can provoke a sudden response.
- conflicts/notes: Reform of the media is distinct from violent events; excitement does not imply that every news report is violent.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The entries connect sudden excitation with news and conversation while retaining separate examples of media reform, traffic nervousness and violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Uranus`, powerful news or exciting conversation abruptly stirs thought and movement: a surprising message can produce excitement or set someone in motion. Dynamic youthful energy and strong impulses extend that response into collective life; reform of powerful media and nervousness in traffic are specific settings. Reports of major events and violence retain their separate subjects, without making violence the meaning of every sudden reaction. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
