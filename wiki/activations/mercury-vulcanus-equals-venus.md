## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

New acquaintances through powerful thoughts. New acquaintances who become important. To think intensively of new acquaintances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 266; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

influence of media art, influence of peaceful thoughts, thoughts about the influence of beauty, conversations about the power of love, harmonious conversational influence, strength of a young love, strength and beauty of youth, and powerful thoughts of peace

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful thought and conversation focus on new acquaintances, affection, beauty and peace.
- psychology: Intense thought about new acquaintances and influential peaceful thoughts are explicit.
- body/health: Youthful strength and beauty are examples; no health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: New acquaintances can become important, while young love and harmonious talk carry influence.
- events/manifestations: Media art and conversations about the power of love provide concrete expressions.
- conflicts/notes: This oriented entry concerns influential thought and acquaintances; it supplies no guarantee of lasting love or approval.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Important new acquaintances, young love, beauty and peaceful expression overlap without making every acquaintance romantic.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Venus`, intense thought gives new acquaintances particular importance, while affection, beauty and harmonious conversation become means of influence. Thinking strongly about a new person is one expression; art in the media, peaceful thoughts and conversations about love broaden the theme beyond a single relationship. Youthful strength and beauty and the strength of young love remain specific examples of this attractive influence. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
