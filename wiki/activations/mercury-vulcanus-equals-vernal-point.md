## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

Great thinking faculty of the general public. Generality under the impact of a mighty idea, of significant news.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 266; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

reports of energy for the public, influence of youth in public life, youth violence in public, power of public opinion, strong movement in public, and strong traffic in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful ideas and significant news affect thought and movement in public life.
- psychology: The general public's thinking capacity and the impact of a mighty idea are explicit.
- body/health: No health condition is supplied; public movement and traffic are collective examples.
- social/relationship: Public opinion and the influence of youth provide the social scope.
- events/manifestations: Energy reports, youth violence in public, strong public movement and traffic are named.
- conflicts/notes: Public influence does not establish unanimity or make all public activity violent.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The entries share a public scope while distinguishing public thinking and influence from the particular example of youth violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Vernal Point`, a powerful idea or significant news reaches the general public and can shape public thought. Public opinion, the influence of youth and energy reporting show communicative influence; strong movement and traffic give it visible collective expressions. The separate example of youth violence in public remains part of the source without defining every form of public influence or implying unanimous agreement. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
