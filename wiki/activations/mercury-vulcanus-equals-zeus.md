## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 172 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Vulcanus`.

#### Witte Entry

Orders are issued energetically. Ruthlessness, without regard or consideration.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 267; one-based PDF p. 132 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Vulkanus`.

#### ABC Entry

Reports of tremendous achievements, fire power in motion, leadership influence of youth, enormous exertions of youth, creative power of thought, powerful achievements of youth, reports of striving for violence, strong achievement-oriented thinking.

Editorial note: The scan reads `Nachrichten über Gewalt-Streben`, translated here as “reports of striving for violence”; this remains distinct from ordinary effort or achievement.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Forceful thought takes the form of energetic commands, creative effort and achievement.
- psychology: Strong achievement-oriented thinking coexists with disregard for others in the command example.
- body/health: Great exertion among youth is named without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Youth leadership and issuing orders provide social settings.
- events/manifestations: Reports of tremendous achievements, fire power in motion and reports of striving for violence are distinct examples.
- conflicts/notes: Creative achievement does not erase ruthlessness or the separate striving-for-violence example.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Productive effort and ruthless command are different expressions; achievement supplies no moral endorsement of disregard or violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Vulcanus = Zeus`, forceful thought becomes energetic direction and an emphasis on achievement: commands are issued strongly, while creative thinking, great effort and youth leadership give mental force a productive form. The same source material also includes ruthlessness, fire power in motion and reports of striving for violence. Those particular expressions must remain visible alongside achievement rather than being softened into ordinary ambition. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
