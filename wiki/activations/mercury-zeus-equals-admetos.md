## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Purposeful thought faces a hard beginning, a small scope and initial restrictions.
- psychology: Effortful deepening and consolidation of creative ideas coexist with blocked youthful fire and depression associated with achievement thinking.
- body/health: The depression association is historical source language; no clinical diagnosis is established.
- social/relationship: Orders for few and thorough guidance of youth retain small-group and guiding roles.
- events/manifestations: Blockage through a fire report and discussion of beginning restrictions are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: Restricted beginnings do not establish eventual failure; consolidation and careful guidance remain positive forms within the same entry.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Blockage and depression appear alongside consolidation and thorough guidance, so difficulty does not exhaust the orientation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Admetos`, purposeful thinking meets a difficult beginning or a restricted field: orders reach only a few, while creative thought is deepened and consolidated under limitations. Blocked youthful fire and the source's depression-through-achievement-thinking phrase describe a more difficult side of that concentrated effort. Thorough guidance of youth remains an active expression, and the particular blockage through a fire report should retain its literal setting. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
