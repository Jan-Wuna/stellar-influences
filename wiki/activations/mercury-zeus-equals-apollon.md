## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Purposeful thought and instruction gain wider scientific, creative and collective expression.
- psychology: Creative thinking, experienced conversation leadership and fiery free speech are named.
- body/health: Scientific conversations about procreation are a subject of discussion, not a health outcome.
- social/relationship: Orders for many and successful guidance of youth give the collective scope.
- events/manifestations: Reports of scientific achievements, youthful achievement successes and increasing shaping of language are examples.
- conflicts/notes: The entries name successful forms without guaranteeing success or validating a scientific claim.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Scientific invention, a wider audience and successful guidance are distinct expressions of directed thinking.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Apollon`, creative direction reaches a wider field through new scientific ideas and orders addressed to many. Success through creative thought extends into scientific achievement reports and successful guidance of youth, while experienced leadership of conversation, fiery free speech and the shaping of language give that direction expressive forms. Scientific discussion of procreation is a specific subject within the entry, without supplying a reproductive prediction. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
