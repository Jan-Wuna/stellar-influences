- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 168 (exact oriented entry checked against the scan); source heading: `Mercury + Zeus`.

#### Witte Entry

Marriage laws. An ordered marriage. Registry office. Wanting to carry through a plan jointly. Creative thoughts in art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 259; one-based PDF p. 128 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Zeus`.

#### ABC Entry

Efforts of the media community, reports of an achievement community, leading media art, joint guidance of youth, describing common goals, common goals of youth, a young leadership community, creative conversations in a community.

Editorial note: Witte reads `jointly` in the actual PDF. ABC reads `gemeinsame Jugend-Führung` and `kreative Gespräche in einer Gemeinschaft`, translated as joint guidance of youth and creative conversations in a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creative direction takes collective, marital and artistic forms through shared plans and goals.
- psychology: Wanting to carry a plan through jointly supplies an explicit shared intention.
- body/health: Marriage institutions and youth guidance are examples; no medical claim is given.
- social/relationship: Marriage laws, ordered marriage and community leadership retain institutional and collective settings.
- events/manifestations: A registry office, leading media art and reports of an achievement community are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: An ordered marriage is a particular source example, not a description of every union; shared guidance is not translated as training.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Legal organization, communal goals and artistic creation share coordination while retaining their distinct settings.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Cupido`, directed thought becomes a shared undertaking: a plan is carried through jointly, common goals are described and a community gives creative conversation a collective setting. Marriage laws, an ordered marriage and the registry office provide institutional examples, while creative thought in art and leading media art provide artistic ones. Collective effort and joint youth guidance connect these settings through coordinated purpose without requiring that every union be arranged by command. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
