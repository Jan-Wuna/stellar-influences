## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 168 (exact oriented entry checked against the scan); source heading: `Mercury + Zeus`.

#### Witte Entry

Vile demands or requests. Orders and rules born out of villainy or baseness. Instigation to robbery, murder or theft.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 259; one-based PDF p. 128 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Zeus`.

#### ABC Entry

Demanding conversations about the past, efforts in an old language, fire emergency call, criminal efforts of youth, deficient achievement-oriented thinking, media achievements through deprivation, purposeful secret conversations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Purposeful requests and commands occur in adverse, secret or explicitly harmful settings.
- psychology: The source attributes baseness to some demands and deficient achievement-oriented thinking to another example.
- body/health: No specific health condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Secret conversations and difficult discussions of the past or in an old language are named.
- events/manifestations: Instigation to robbery, murder or theft, criminal efforts of youth, a fire emergency call and media achievements through deprivation are examples.
- conflicts/notes: The severe criminal and moral language remains attributed and cannot establish an individual's conduct or character.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Criminal instigation, emergency communication and difficult speech about the past are distinct examples; adversity alone does not make a conversation criminal.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Hades`, directed speech enters difficult or concealed circumstances: demanding conversations about the past, effort in an old language and purposeful secret conversations place strain or secrecy around communication. A fire emergency call and media achievements through deprivation provide specific adverse settings. The most severe examples are base demands and instigation to robbery, murder or theft; these must remain explicit source examples rather than become a character verdict about someone. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
