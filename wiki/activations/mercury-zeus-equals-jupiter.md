## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 168 (exact oriented entry checked against the scan); source heading: `Mercury + Zeus`.

#### Witte Entry

Good comprehension. Success or profit through creative ideas. Humorous and witty. Good command. Laws concerning pleasures or money.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 258; one-based PDF p. 128 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Zeus`.

#### ABC Entry

Successful creative youth, successful media achievements, discussing financial goals, enjoyment of fiery conversations, leading legal thoughts, the right of leadership among youth, conversations about financial achievements, shaping youth law.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Good comprehension and creative ideas take successful, profitable or effectively directive forms.
- psychology: Humor, wit and enjoyment of fiery conversation are explicit.
- body/health: No health correspondence is supplied.
- social/relationship: Successful creative youth, leadership rights among youth and media achievements provide social settings.
- events/manifestations: Financial goals, financial achievements and laws concerning pleasure or money are named.
- conflicts/notes: The source's legal examples and favorable associations do not guarantee a financial or legal outcome.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Creative success, humorous conversation and legal or financial subjects are related examples, without making profit the only expression.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Jupiter`, creative direction works through good comprehension, wit and effective command, with success or profit through ideas as a favorable expression. Enjoyment of fiery conversation and successful creative youth give this a lively communicative form; media achievements, financial goals and financial achievements give it practical subjects. Laws about pleasures or money, legal thinking and shaping youth law retain the entry's institutional range. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
