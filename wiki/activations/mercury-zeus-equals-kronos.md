## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creative thought and directed speech operate through superior authority and state or leadership aims.
- psychology: Great creative thoughts, leading opinion and self-confident guidance of youth are named.
- body/health: No particular health condition is given.
- social/relationship: Supreme orders, authority in youth guidance and important conversations with a boss establish hierarchical settings.
- events/manifestations: State objectives, special youthful achievements and the public prosecutor are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: The prosecutor is a source example; authority and high aims do not establish a particular profession.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Creative thought and superior command overlap while retaining the distinct roles of state objectives, youth guidance and public prosecution.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Kronos`, purposeful thought and speech take an authoritative form: great creative ideas stand beside supreme orders, leading opinion and discussion of state objectives. Authority and self-confidence in guiding youth, special achievements and important conversation with a boss show leadership in concrete relationships. The public prosecutor preserves a specific official expression without turning that example into an occupational assignment. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
