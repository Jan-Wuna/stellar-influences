### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 258; one-based PDF p. 128 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Zeus`.

#### ABC Entry

Inspired shaping of language, fiery language of the soul, my goal is writing, my direction of thought, my creative conversations, my strenuous movements, my striving in youth, my leadership of conversation, my fiery words.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed thought and effort become personal aims, expression and response to command.
- psychology: Outdoing oneself, personal direction of thought and striving in youth are explicit.
- body/health: Strenuous personal movements are named without a health diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Heeding a command places personal effort in a relationship to direction from another.
- events/manifestations: Writing as a goal, creative conversations, leading a conversation and fiery words are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Personal striving and obeying a command are different expressions; neither defines a permanent identity.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Outdoing oneself and heeding a command can both express directed personal effort, without implying permanent obedience or leadership.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = MC`, purposeful thought is owned personally: directing one's thoughts, creative conversations and fiery words express an individual aim, including writing. Outdoing oneself and strenuous movement give that aim an effortful side, while heeding a command preserves the possibility of responding to someone else's direction. The first-person examples connect personal striving and expression without making either command or obedience the person's fixed role. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
