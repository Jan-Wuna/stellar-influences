## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Original thought and directed speech gain public expression through edicts, publicity and gendered source examples.
- psychology: Achievement-oriented thinking and feeling in leading conversation are explicitly associated with women in the source.
- body/health: A woman's thoughts of procreation are a stated subject, not a reproductive outcome or diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Women who command, laws for women and the speech of a leading people preserve the entry's historical social examples.
- events/manifestations: Publicity for original thoughts, mustering, recruiting and reports of the people's achievements are named.
- conflicts/notes: The female and collective associations remain historical source examples rather than universal gender traits.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Public edicts and women's creative or feelingful expression are distinct settings; neither warrants a universal claim about women or a people.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Moon`, original ideas become public through publicity, edicts for the populace and recruitment. The source also gives women active expressive and directive roles: commanding, fiery speech, leading conversation with feeling, achievement-oriented thought and creative youthful effort. Laws for women and a woman's thoughts of procreation retain their specific institutional and personal subjects. These gendered examples remain attributed historical associations, without becoming general claims about how women think or act. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
