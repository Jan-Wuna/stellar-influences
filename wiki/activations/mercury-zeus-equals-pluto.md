## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 168 (exact oriented entry checked against the scan); source heading: `Mercury + Zeus`.

#### Witte Entry

Development of creative thinking. To learn to command and to obey.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 259; one-based PDF p. 128 (oriented row checked against the scan); source heading: `Merkur + Zeus`.

#### ABC Entry

Unfolding of a fiery conversation, creative development of youth, developing achievement-oriented thinking, reports of changes in leadership, discussing changes of direction, growing leadership thinking, purposeful development of youth.

Editorial note: The scan assigns the complete seven-clause developmental row to Pluto. It reads `wachsendes Führungs-Denken`, translated here as “growing leadership thinking”; the opening clauses had been absorbed into the Neptune extraction.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creative thinking and directed conversation develop through learning, leadership change and purposeful youthful growth.
- psychology: Learning to command and to obey accompanies developing achievement-oriented and leadership thinking.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is given.
- social/relationship: Changes in leadership and creative development of youth provide social settings.
- events/manifestations: Fiery conversation unfolds while reports and discussions address changes of direction.
- conflicts/notes: The complete seven-clause ABC row belongs to Pluto; growing leadership thinking is not the inherited 'filthy thinking' mistranslation.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Learning command and obedience describes two sides of direction, while the developmental row remains distinct from Neptune's uncertainty and ineffective commands.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Pluto`, purposeful thought develops through learning both to give direction and to respond to it. Creative thinking, achievement-oriented thought and leadership thinking grow alongside the unfolding of a fiery conversation. Reports of leadership changes, discussions of changed direction and purposeful youthful development give that learning concrete social forms. The complete row supports development and changing direction without importing Neptune's separate secrecy or ineffectiveness. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
