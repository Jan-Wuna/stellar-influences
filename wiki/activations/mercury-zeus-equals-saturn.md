## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 168 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Zeus`.

#### Witte Entry

Demands which are harmful, cause damage and are a hindrance. Laws for agriculture. Edicts concerning lands or building codes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 259; one-based PDF p. 128 (entry); source heading: `Merkur + Zeus`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `128`, printed page `259`):

> Aufgaben-Gestaltung für die Jugend, Behinderung kreativer Jugend, Führungs-Aufgaben besprechen, geduldige Jugend-Führung, gestörte Führungs-Gespräche, gestörte Gedanken-Leistung, problematische Gesprächs-Ziele, schöpferische Gedanken-Konzentration

Editorial translation from the German passage: Designing tasks for youth; hindrance of creative youth; discussing leadership tasks; patient youth leadership; disrupted leadership conversations; disrupted mental performance; problematic conversational aims; creative concentration of thought.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Demands, rules and creative effort meet restriction or formal requirements.
- psychology: Creative concentration and problematic conversational goals appear in ABC.
- body/health: ABC describes hindrance of creative youth, not a diagnosed disability.
- social/relationship: Leadership tasks and demands placed on others are social settings.
- events/manifestations: Agricultural laws, land edicts and building codes are specific Witte examples.
- conflicts/notes: Harmful demands, neutral regulations, and patient leadership are distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte's harmful demands coexist with neutral laws and codes. ABC also permits patient youth leadership beside disrupted leadership conversations and mental performance.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies command and directed effort through hindrance, difficult demands and regulation. [Witte](#witte-entry) includes harmful demands alongside agricultural law, land edicts and building codes. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds leadership tasks and concentrated creative thought; those administrative examples should not all be interpreted as harmful simply because they share this orientation.
