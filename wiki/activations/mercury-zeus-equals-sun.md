## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 168 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Zeus`.

#### Witte Entry

Decrees of the day. The justice (judge). The superior in person. To be condemned.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 258; one-based PDF p. 128 (entry); source heading: `Merkur + Zeus`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `128`, printed page `258`):

> Berichte über menschliche Leistungen, Feuer-Melde-Zentrale, führendes Nachrichten-Zentrum, körperliche Leistungen der Jugend, persönliche Führung der Jugend, persönliche Sprach-Leistungen, persönliches Leistungs-Denken, schöpferischer junger Mann

Editorial translation from the German passage: Reports about human achievements; a fire-reporting centre; a leading news centre; physical achievements of youth; personal leadership of youth; personal linguistic achievements; personal achievement-oriented thinking; a creative young man.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Command and achievement take a personal or embodied form.
- psychology: Personal performance thinking is an ABC theme.
- body/health: Bodily achievements are mentioned without a health condition.
- social/relationship: A judge or superior in person is Witte's explicit setting.
- events/manifestations: Daily decrees, condemnation and reports of achievements are source examples.
- conflicts/notes: Being the superior and being condemned are alternative roles; a leading news centre is an additional ABC example.

## Contradictions

Holding authority and being subject to judgment are alternative roles, not one necessary sequence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Sun`, Sun specifies commands and decrees through persons and direct encounters with authority. [Witte](#witte-entry) names the judge, the superior in person and being condemned; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds personal achievements and direction of youth. These are distinct roles and settings, not a prediction that the person must either judge others or face condemnation.
