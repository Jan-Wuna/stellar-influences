## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the axis through abrupt or emphatic direction. [Witte](#witte-entry) names calling a halt, emergency decrees and martial law; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds technical aims, media technology and reform of traffic management. The examples span urgent command and technical innovation without making emergency rule the meaning of every inventive thought.
