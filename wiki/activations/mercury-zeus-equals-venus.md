## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 168 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Zeus`.

#### Witte Entry

Well meaning arrangements, dispositions or orders. Decrees about moral matters and customs. Rules about greeting.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 258; one-based PDF p. 128 (entry); source heading: `Merkur + Zeus`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `128`, printed page `258`):

> An Liebes-Zeugung denken, Beginn einer jungen Liebe, feurige Jugend-Liebe, harmonische Gesprächs-Führung, Leistungs-Wünsche der Jugend, Liebes-Gespräche führen, Nachrichten über Friedens-Ziele, kreative Gespräche über Kunst, wohlwollende Jugend-Führung

Editorial translation from the German passage: Thinking of procreation in love; the beginning of a young love; fiery young love; harmonious direction of conversation; young people's wishes for achievement; conducting conversations about love; news about peace aims; creative conversations about art; benevolent youth leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed expression concerns goodwill, customs, affection or art.
- psychology: Benevolent direction and wishes for achievement appear in ABC.
- body/health: ABC explicitly mentions thoughts of procreation in love, without a bodily outcome.
- social/relationship: Rules for greeting and the beginning of young love are separate source examples.
- events/manifestations: Peace goals, art conversation and customary decrees are concrete settings.
- conflicts/notes: Historical moral rules remain attributed; benevolent leadership is distinct from a guaranteed romantic outcome.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict is stated. Well-meaning orders and greeting rules are distinct from ABC's love, procreation, peace and art examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Venus`, Venus specifies command or arrangement through goodwill, manners and affectionate or artistic aims. [Witte](#witte-entry) includes well-meaning orders and rules about greeting; [ABC](#abc-entry) wording adds young love, peace goals and creative art conversation. The moral and customary rules remain source examples rather than universal standards of conduct.
