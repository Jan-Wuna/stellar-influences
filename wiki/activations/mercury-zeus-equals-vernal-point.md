German passage (transcribed from PDF page `128`, printed page `258`):

> Anstrengung der Jugend in der Öffentlichkeit, Feuer-Meldung in der Öffentlichkeit, Gespräche der öffentlichen Führung, Leistungs-Denken im öffentlichen Leben, öffentliche Medien-Kreativität, öffentliche Meinungs-Gestaltung, zielstrebige Jugend in der Öffentlichkeit

Editorial translation from the German passage: Young people's effort in public; a public fire report; conversations of public leadership; achievement-oriented thinking in public life; public media creativity; shaping public opinion; purposeful youth in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed expression and command take public form.
- psychology: Public opinion is an ABC theme, without an individual character claim.
- body/health: No medical or bodily association is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public leadership and youth in public life are source settings.
- events/manifestations: Edicts, laws, decrees and fire reports are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: The restored ABC compounds concern achievement-oriented thought and public media creativity.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict is stated. General decrees and laws coexist with ABC's public leadership conversations, media creativity and youth effort.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the axis in public direction. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives edicts, laws and decrees in general, while [ABC](#abc-entry) examples add public opinion and public youth effort. The formula therefore concerns commands and purposeful expression in public life, without assigning a specific law or political outcome.
