## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 168 (entry start); source heading: `Mercury + Zeus`.

#### Witte Entry

The enraged superior. Scene with superiors. To be handled roughly or severely. Commands issued without regard for anybody or anything.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 259; one-based PDF p. 128 (entry); source heading: `Merkur + Zeus`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `128`, printed page `259`):

> Berichte über feurige Energien, einflußreiche Gesprächs-Führung, führender Einfluß der Medien, Gedanken einer Führungs-Kraft, jugendliches Gewalt-Streben, Macht schöpferischer Gedanken, mächtige Leistungen der Jugend, über gewaltige Anstrengungen berichten

Editorial translation from the German passage: Reports about fiery energies; influential direction of conversation; leading influence of the media; thoughts of a leader; youthful striving for power or force; the power of creative thoughts; mighty achievements of youth; reporting on tremendous efforts.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Command and creative thought become forceful or severe.
- psychology: Anger and disregard in command are explicit Witte emphases.
- body/health: Rough treatment is a source example; no medical diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Scenes with superiors and being handled severely are Witte's social settings.
- events/manifestations: Direction of conversation, thoughts of a leader, creative power and tremendous efforts are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Youthful striving for power or force is distinct from Witte's rough treatment by a superior.

## Contradictions

Witte's harsh interpersonal commands coexist with ABC's creative thought and mighty achievement; neither erases the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Mercury/Zeus = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies command through force and intensity. [Witte](#witte-entry) explicitly depicts an enraged superior, rough treatment and orders without regard for others; [ABC](#abc-entry) material adds powerful creative thoughts and achievements. Creative force does not cancel the harsh interpersonal possibilities, which remain possibilities rather than a verdict on every authority figure.
