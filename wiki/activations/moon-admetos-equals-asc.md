## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 135 (entry start); source heading: `Moon + Admetos`.

#### Witte Entry

Death of acquaintances or in nearer surroundings, emotionally connected with them. To express sorrow, condolence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 202; one-based PDF p. 100 (entry); source heading: `Mond + Admetos`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `100`, printed page `202`):

> Bedrücktes Gemüt des Partners, blockierte Gefühle im Umfeld, deprimierte Frauen in der Nähe, Einschränkung anderer Völker, Frauen mit Ausdauer im Umfeld, gefestigte Frau als Partner, Gefühls-Tiefe des Partners, Kreislauf anderer Frauen

Editorial translation from the German passage: A partner's depressed mood; blocked feelings in the surroundings; depressed women nearby; restriction of other peoples; women with endurance in the surroundings; a steadfast woman as partner; a partner's depth of feeling; circulation of other women.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Emotional restriction or depth is placed in nearby relationships.
- psychology: Blocked feelings and a depressed partner are ABC associations.
- body/health: ABC explicitly names circulation of other women, without a specific diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Sorrow and condolence after a nearby loss are Witte's relational examples.
- events/manifestations: Endurance and emotional depth of a partner extend the range beyond bereavement.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered examples and death claims remain attributed rather than universal or predictive.

## Contradictions

Gendered examples and death claims remain attributed rather than universal or predictive.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Admetos = Asc`, Asc specifies the axis in emotionally significant relationships and nearby circumstances. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives bereavement among acquaintances and the expression of condolence; [ABC](#abc-entry) examples add a partner's emotional depth, restricted feelings and endurance. The death association remains a historical source statement, not a prediction about an acquaintance.
