### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Village incorporated. Community in crowded circumstances. A community that wants to live by itself.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 203; one-based PDF p. 100 (entry); source heading: `Mond + Admetos`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `100`, printed page `203`):

> Ausdauer einer Frauen-Gemeinschaft, deprimierte Frauen einer Gemeinschaft, eingeschränkte Gemüts-Gemeinschaft, gründliche Frauen-Gemeinschaft, Stunde der gemeinsamen Vertiefung, tiefe Gefühle einer Gemeinschaft, Widerstand einer Frauen-Gemeinschaft

Editorial translation from the German passage: Endurance of a women's community; depressed women in a community; a restricted community of feeling; a thorough women's community; an hour of shared deepening; deep feelings of a community; resistance of a women's community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Constricted circumstances and endurance occur collectively.
- psychology: ABC includes deep shared feelings as well as depressed women in a community.
- body/health: No verified specific bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: A community may seek to live by itself in Witte's account.
- events/manifestations: Crowding and an incorporated village are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: Deep feelings do not mean divisions; both endurance and emotional restriction are present.

## Contradictions

Community endurance, deep shared feeling and restriction coexist. The actual German does not support the former internal-divisions translation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Admetos = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the axis through a community living in constricted circumstances. Witte names crowding and a wish for separate existence, with an incorporated village as an example. ABC adds endurance, resistance, deep shared feelings and shared deepening within a women's community. Collective restriction therefore remains distinct from either internal division or an absence of emotional depth ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
