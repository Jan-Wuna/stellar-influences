## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 135 (entry start); source heading: `Moon + Admetos`.

#### Witte Entry

Offices. Red tape. Bureaucracy. Narrow minded bureaucratic attitude. A crowded great nation. A great nation without political influence. A great nation that has lost its independence and lives in the past.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 203; one-based PDF p. 100 (entry); source heading: `Mond + Admetos`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `100`, printed page `203`):

> Besondere Gefühls-Tiefe, blockierte Autorität eines Volkes, Depressionen einer angesehenen Frau, deprimierte Obrigkeit eines Volkes, eingeschränkte Volks-Hierarchie, Festigung der Selbständigkeit einer Frau, Vorrang einer gründlichen Frau

Editorial translation from the German passage: Exceptional depth of feeling; blocked authority of a people; depression of a respected woman; depressed authorities of a people; a restricted national hierarchy; consolidation of a woman's independence; priority for a thorough woman.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restriction appears in bureaucracy and public authority.
- psychology: Witte's narrow bureaucratic attitude is an attributed characterization.
- body/health: ABC's depression reference does not diagnose a respected woman.
- social/relationship: A nation's constrained influence and authority are major social examples.
- events/manifestations: Offices, red tape and lost independence are concrete Witte settings.
- conflicts/notes: The German names independence rather than self-glorification; authority restriction and personal consolidation differ.

## Contradictions

Collective authority may be blocked while a woman's independence consolidates; the sources do not make these the same outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Admetos = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the axis through constrained authority and bureaucracy. Witte describes red tape and a nation with little political influence or lost independence. ABC also names blocked authority, but separately permits exceptional depth of feeling, a woman's consolidated independence and priority for thoroughness. Collective restriction therefore does not erase personal firmness ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
