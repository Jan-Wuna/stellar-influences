Forced to work in crowded places. Prison labor. Slave labor.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 202; one-based PDF p. 100 (entry); source heading: `Mond + Admetos`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `100`, printed page `202`):

> Arbeits-Streik in der Stunde, blockierte Gefühls-Handlungen, depressive Gemüts-Impulse, eingeschränkte Arbeit der Frau, gründliche Frauen-Arbeit, Unterdrückung der Gefühle bei der Arbeit, Vertiefung einer Frau in die Arbeit, Willens-Beschränkungen eines Volkes

Editorial translation from the German passage: A work strike in the hour; blocked actions prompted by feeling; depressive emotional impulses; restricted work of a woman; thorough women's work; suppression of feelings at work; a woman's absorption in work; restrictions on a people's will.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Work and action occur under restriction.
- psychology: ABC includes blocked feeling-led actions, depressive impulses and suppression of feelings at work.
- body/health: No specific bodily disorder is established by the labor examples.
- social/relationship: Forced labor and a people's restricted will are different source settings.
- events/manifestations: Prison labor, slave labor and work stoppage are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: The severe labor examples remain explicit without being generalized to every work difficulty.

## Contradictions

The severe labor examples remain explicit without being generalized to every work difficulty.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Admetos = Mars`, Mars specifies the axis through work under constraint. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives the severe examples of crowded forced labor, prison labor and slave labor; [ABC](#abc-entry) material adds restricted work and work stoppage. These remain attributed source associations, without treating every restriction or labor difficulty as enslavement.
