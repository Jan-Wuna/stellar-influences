## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 135 (entry start); source heading: `Moon + Admetos`.

#### Witte Entry

Deeply absorbed in thought. To keep sad thoughts. To feel crowded. To be depressed. Narrow thinking.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 202; one-based PDF p. 100 (entry); source heading: `Mond + Admetos`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `100`, printed page `202`):

> Beschränkung der Medien eines Volkes, blockierte Gefühle der Jugend, eingeschränkte junge Frau, Gefühls-Tiefe der Jugend, Gespräche über blockierte Gefühle, gründliche Gespräche unter Frauen, gründliche Medien eines Volkes

Editorial translation from the German passage: Restriction of a people's media; blocked feelings of youth; a restricted young woman; young people's depth of feeling; conversations about blocked feelings; thorough conversations among women; thorough media of a people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought is absorbed, sad or narrowed by a sense of constriction.
- psychology: Deep absorption and keeping sad thoughts are Witte's explicit emphases.
- body/health: Witte's depressed description is interpretive rather than a clinical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Limits on a people's media appear in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Conversations about blocked feeling and thorough conversations among women are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: The German concerns feelings and thoroughness, not skills or deficient ability.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sad and narrow thought; ABC also permits depth of feeling and thorough conversation. These do not establish deficient ability.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Admetos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the axis through thought absorbed in sadness or constriction. Witte names deep absorption, sad thoughts and feeling crowded. ABC adds conversations about blocked feelings and limits on a people's media, while also allowing emotional depth and thorough conversation. Restriction in thought or exchange is therefore distinct from a lack of intellectual skill ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
