## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 135 (entry start); source heading: `Moon + Admetos`.

#### Witte Entry

Nation facing destruction and misfortune. Uncertain and black future. Hour of desperation. Deceived by the personal conditions. Deluded.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 203; one-based PDF p. 100 (entry); source heading: `Mond + Admetos`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `100`, printed page `203`):

> Ahnungen einer deprimierten Frau, Ausdauer einer Frau im Wasser, Feingefühl einer gefestigten Frau, Gefühls-Täuschung durch Depressionen, Unsicherheit einer Frau durch Depressionen, Vertiefung eines Volkes in Metaphysik, Widerstand eines Volkes gegen Wasser

Editorial translation from the German passage: Intimations of a depressed woman; a woman's endurance in water; sensitivity of a steadfast woman; emotional deception through depression; a woman's uncertainty through depression; a people's absorption in metaphysics; a people's resistance to water.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Constricted circumstances are associated with uncertainty or desperation.
- psychology: Witte explicitly includes delusion about personal conditions.
- body/health: ABC's depression wording remains an attributed historical association, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Witte uses a nation facing misfortune as a collective example.
- events/manifestations: ABC separately gives endurance in water, sensitivity and metaphysical absorption.
- conflicts/notes: The complete Neptune row ends with resistance to water; the following development clauses belong to Pluto.

## Contradictions

Neptune's uncertainty and sensitivity coexist with endurance and metaphysical absorption. The previously merged development fragment belongs to the next Pluto glyph.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Admetos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the axis through uncertainty, desperation or a deceptive view of one's conditions. ABC gives an emotional form through intimations, sensitivity, uncertainty and emotional deception, while retaining endurance in water and absorption in metaphysics as separate examples. Witte's national-destruction wording remains an attributed historical association; these sources do not predict a nation's or person's fate ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
