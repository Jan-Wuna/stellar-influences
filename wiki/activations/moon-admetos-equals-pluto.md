## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 135 (entry start); source heading: `Moon + Admetos`.

#### Witte Entry

Modified conditions. To get out of crowded circumstances. To gain more freedom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 203; one-based PDF p. 100 (entry); source heading: `Mond + Admetos`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `100`, printed page `203`):

> Eingeschränkte Entwicklung einer Frau, gründliche Entwicklung einer Frau, Stillstand in der Entwicklung eines Volkes, tiefe Gefühle entwickeln, wechselnde Gefühls-Blockade, Wachstum einer Frau durch Ausdauer, Wandlung zur gefestigten Frau

Editorial translation from the German passage: Restricted development of a woman; thorough development of a woman; a standstill in a people's development; developing deep feelings; changing emotional blockage; a woman's growth through endurance; transformation into a steadfast woman.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Constricted conditions can change and allow more freedom.
- psychology: Development of deep feelings and perseverance appear in ABC.
- body/health: The German names emotional blockage, not a verified bodily-flow condition.
- social/relationship: A people's development and a woman's growth are source-specific examples.
- events/manifestations: Leaving crowded circumstances is Witte's concrete favorable example.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes restricted development and standstill as well as growth; freedom is not guaranteed.

## Contradictions

Witte allows escape from crowding and greater freedom. ABC also retains restricted development and a people's standstill, so release is not the sole expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Admetos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the axis through changing conditions and possible release from crowding. Witte explicitly describes gaining freedom; ABC adds growth through endurance, deeper feeling and becoming steadfast, but also restricted development and a people's standstill. Change can therefore deepen or loosen a restriction without guaranteeing that every confinement ends ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
