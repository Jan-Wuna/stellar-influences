## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 135 (entry start); source heading: `Moon + Admetos`.

#### Witte Entry

Restriction of mental and spiritual freedom. Mentally enslaved. Spiritual endeavors under exclusion of the public or with only a few people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 203; one-based PDF p. 100 (entry); source heading: `Mond + Admetos`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `100`, printed page `203`):

> Geistige Vertiefung einer Frau, durch Ideen blockiertes Volk, gründliche Erleuchtung einer Frau, Klarheit durch Gefühls-Tiefe, Stunde der geistigen Vertiefung, tiefe Einsicht einer Frau, unterdrückte Kultur eines Volkes, Wahrheit einer gründlichen Frau

Editorial translation from the German passage: Intellectual or spiritual deepening of a woman; a people blocked by ideas; thorough enlightenment of a woman; clarity through depth of feeling; an hour of intellectual or spiritual deepening; a woman's deep insight; suppressed culture of a people; the truth of a thorough woman.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental or spiritual activity occurs under limited freedom or in a small circle.
- psychology: ABC includes clarity through depth of feeling, deep insight and spiritual deepening.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Small private groups and a people's suppressed culture are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Spiritual endeavors outside public view are an explicit Witte setting.
- conflicts/notes: The source's truth wording is retained without certifying it; privacy and enforced suppression remain distinct.

## Contradictions

Chosen privacy and enforced restriction are different conditions and must not be flattened.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Admetos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the axis through intellectual or spiritual life under restriction. [Witte](#witte-entry) includes curtailed freedom and spiritual work with few people or away from the public; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds deep insight and suppressed culture. Private study and imposed suppression remain distinct possibilities, without equating every small group with enslavement.
