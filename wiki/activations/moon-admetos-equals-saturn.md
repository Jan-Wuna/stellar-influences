## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 135 (entry start); source heading: `Moon + Admetos`.

#### Witte Entry

Oppressed or suppressed. To feel oppressed. Feeling abandoned. A suppressed bad mood.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 203; one-based PDF p. 100 (entry); source heading: `Mond + Admetos`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `100`, printed page `203`):

> Depressionen einer Frau im Alter, Einschränkungen und Verluste für ein Volk, Ende der Gemüts-Störungen, Festigung der Probleme einer Frau, Geduld und Ausdauer einer Frau, gestörter Kreislauf der Frau, Stunde der Konzentration und Vertiefung

Editorial translation from the German passage: Depression of a woman in old age; restrictions and losses for a people; the end of mood disturbances; consolidation of a woman's problems; a woman's patience and endurance; disturbed circulation of a woman; an hour of concentration and deepening.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restriction is felt as oppression or abandonment.
- psychology: A suppressed bad mood is Witte's explicit psychological example.
- body/health: ABC's depression and disturbed circulation remain historical claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Losses for a people and an older woman's experience are ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: Patience and endurance are constructive ABC associations alongside limitation.
- conflicts/notes: ABC permits the end of mood disturbance beside persistent problems and endurance.

## Contradictions

Oppression and persistent problems coexist with patience and an end to mood disturbance. No necessary clinical course is established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Admetos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the axis through oppression, abandonment or a held-in bad mood. ABC adds restrictions and losses beside patience, endurance and concentration, and also permits an end to mood disturbances. These are alternative emotional or social expressions; the circulation and depression statements remain historical source claims rather than a diagnosis or fixed course of illness ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
