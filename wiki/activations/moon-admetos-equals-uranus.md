## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 135 (entry start); source heading: `Moon + Admetos`.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden death of a woman. Sudden appearance of stress and need of people. Futile uproar among people connected with murder and revolts, death.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 203; one-based PDF p. 100 (entry); source heading: `Mond + Admetos`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `100`, printed page `203`):

> Ausdauer einer dynamischen Frau, eingeschränkte Gefühls-Impulse, Erregung durch unterdrückte Gefühle, Impulse einer gefestigten Frau, neue Depressionen einer Frau, technische Reform eines Volkes, Widerstand einer Frau gegen die Technik

Editorial translation from the German passage: Endurance of a dynamic woman; restricted emotional impulses; agitation through suppressed feelings; impulses of a steadfast woman; new depression in a woman; technical reform of a people; a woman's resistance to technology.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restriction is associated with abrupt distress, impulses or upheaval.
- psychology: ABC includes restricted emotional impulses and agitation caused by suppressed feelings.
- body/health: Witte's sudden-death claim is attributed and not a diagnosis or forecast.
- social/relationship: Public stress and resistance to technology are different social examples.
- events/manifestations: Revolt and technical reform are concrete source settings.
- conflicts/notes: The entries span severe upheaval and technical change; these possibilities are not equivalent.

## Contradictions

The entries span severe upheaval and technical change; these possibilities are not equivalent.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Admetos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the axis through abrupt distress or upheaval in [Witte](#witte-entry). That source's sudden-death and revolt examples remain historical associations rather than predictions. [ABC](#abc-entry) broadens the range to endurance, impulses and technical reform, so its technical examples should not be absorbed into a claim that every sudden change is fatal.
