## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: one-based PDF p. 135 (entry start); source heading: `Moon + Admetos`.

#### Witte Entry

Death of a beloved person (female). Longing to be loneliness. Fond of meditation. To be content in narrow conditions. A people who are content with their narrow space.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Evidence locator: printed p. 202; one-based PDF p. 100 (entry); source heading: `Mond + Admetos`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `100`, printed page `202`):

> Ausdauernde Liebe zur Frau, Depressionen einer schönen Frau, eingeschränkte Frauen im Volk, Ende der Liebe zur Frau, harmonische Vertiefung der Gefühle, harmonischer Kreislauf der Frau, Stabilisierung des Friedens im Volke, unterdrückte Liebes-Gefühle

Editorial translation from the German passage: Enduring love for a woman; depression of a beautiful woman; restricted women among the people; the end of love for a woman; harmonious deepening of feelings; harmonious circulation of a woman; stabilization of peace among the people; suppressed feelings of love.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affection and attachment are placed beside solitude or narrow circumstances.
- psychology: Meditation and contentment are explicit Witte possibilities.
- body/health: ABC's circulation and depression remain historical attributed claims.
- social/relationship: Loss of a beloved female person and ending love are separate source examples.
- events/manifestations: Stabilizing peace is an ABC theme distinct from bereavement.
- conflicts/notes: Enduring affection, its ending, meditation and bereavement are distinct possibilities; actual Witte's awkward wording is retained.

## Contradictions

Enduring love and its ending coexist. Actual WittePDF135 prints 'Longing to be loneliness.'; the grammatical defect is retained without supplying a corrected sentence.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Admetos = Venus`, Venus specifies the axis through affection alongside solitude or contentment with little space. [Witte](#witte-entry) includes meditation and, separately, loss of a beloved female person; [ABC](#abc-entry) material adds ending love and stabilizing peace. Bereavement is one historical source example, without making every quiet or restricted relationship a sign of death.
