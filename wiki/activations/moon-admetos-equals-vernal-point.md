- Evidence locator: printed p. 202; one-based PDF p. 100 (entry); source heading: `Mond + Admetos`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `100`, printed page `202`):

> Ausdauer einer Frau in der Öffentlichkeit, bedrückte Gemüter in der Öffentlichkeit, deprimiertes Volk in der Öffentlichkeit, eingeengte Frauen in der Öffentlichkeit, öffentliche Einschränkungen für ein Volk, Stunde der Blockade in der Öffentlichkeit

Editorial translation from the German passage: A woman's endurance in public; depressed moods in public; a depressed people in public; confined women in public; public restrictions for a people; an hour of public blockage.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Crowding or national decline is the public-level source theme.
- psychology: ABC adds depressed public moods beside a woman's public endurance.
- body/health: Witte's dying-nation wording is collective imagery, not a medical assertion.
- social/relationship: Crowded populations are Witte's social setting.
- events/manifestations: Public restrictions and an hour of blockage are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: The restored public row contains no repeated public-opinion claim; national decline remains attributed.

## Contradictions

Witte's severe national-decline language coexists with ABC's public endurance and restriction; these are not a forecast for any present nation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Admetos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the axis through public or collective constriction. Witte names crowded people and dying or dead nations; ABC adds depressed public moods, confined women and public restrictions, while retaining a woman's endurance. The range therefore includes perseverance within public limitation; the severe historical national claims remain attributed ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
