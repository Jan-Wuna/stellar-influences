## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

Many hours in contentment. To feel depressed or limited by the public. The masses of people see their goals attained.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `201`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

endurance of a successful woman, expansion of an emotional depression, blocked economy of a people, success through the thoroughness of a woman, trade blockade for a people, business restrictions for the people, and condensation of emotional knowledge

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Contentment or attained collective aims alongside restriction by the public.
- psychology: Witte permits both contentment and feeling depressed or limited by others.
- body/health: ABC's expanding emotional depression remains an attributed historical claim.
- social/relationship: The public and a people subject to economic restrictions are distinct settings.
- events/manifestations: Trade blockade, business restrictions and success through thoroughness appear in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: Restriction and collective attainment are alternatives, not one inevitable outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte explicitly permits contentment and attained goals beside feeling limited by the public. ABC adds economic blockages while also allowing endurance and success through thoroughness.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair's broad experience through concentration or limitation. Many hours can be contented, and collective aims may be attained; yet the public may also feel limiting or depressing. ABC gives restriction an economic form through blocked commerce and business limits, while retaining endurance and thoroughness. Attainment within a narrower field therefore remains distinct from an absence of restriction ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
