## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

To spend many hours together with many people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `200`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `99`, printed page `200`):

> Erfolg anderer Völker, erfolgreiche Frau als Partner, Gefühls-Freiheit in der Umwelt, Handel mit anderen Völkern, viele Frauen in der Nähe, Gefühls-Erfahrungen mit dem Partner, Gefühls-Wissen im Umfeld, Stunde des Erfolgs mit dem Partner

Editorial translation from the German passage: Success of other peoples; a successful woman as partner; emotional freedom in the surroundings; trade with other peoples; many women nearby; emotional experiences with the partner; emotional knowledge in the surroundings; an hour of success with the partner.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Spending many hours with many people ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: ABC adds emotional freedom, experience and knowledge in one's surroundings.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: A successful woman as partner and many nearby women are ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: Trade with other peoples and shared success are additional settings.
- conflicts/notes: Emotional freedom is not freedom of enterprise.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict is stated. Witte emphasizes duration and company; ABC adds emotional and commercial examples without making all company successful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Asc`, Asc places breadth of experience among nearby people and partners. Witte emphasizes many hours spent together with many people; ABC adds emotional freedom and learning through the partner, alongside a successful woman and trade with other peoples. The distinctive connection is extended social experience in one's environment, with success as one stated example rather than a universal result ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
