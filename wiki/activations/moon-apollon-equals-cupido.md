## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

Many hours devoted to art, leisure, relaxation. Common, peaceful competition of the nations. Olympic games.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `201`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `99`, printed page `201`):

> Erfahrungen einer Gemüts-Gemeinschaft, erfolgreiche Frauen-Gemeinschaft, gemeinsames Frauen-Wissen, gemeinsames Gefühls-Wissen, viele Frauen einer Gemeinschaft, Wirtschaft einer Volks-Gemeinschaft, wissenschaftliche Frauen-Gemeinschaft

Editorial translation from the German passage: Experiences of a community of feeling; a successful women's community; shared knowledge of women; shared emotional knowledge; many women in a community; the economy of a people's community; a scientific women's community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared leisure, art and peaceful competition among communities or nations.
- psychology: ABC adds shared knowledge of women and shared emotional knowledge.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: A successful women's community and many women in a community are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Olympic games, art, relaxation and a community's economy are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Peaceful competition is distinct from a promise that every community interaction is harmonious.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict is stated. Witte emphasizes shared recreation and peaceful competition; ABC adds community experience, knowledge and economic life.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair's many hours and broad participation through shared community life. Witte gives art, leisure, relaxation and peaceful competition, with the Olympic games as a concrete example. ABC adds shared emotional knowledge, women's communities and a community's economy. The collective setting connects these expressions without making success or harmony a required outcome for every group ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
