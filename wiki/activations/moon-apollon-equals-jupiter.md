## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

Many happy hours. Many happy hours spent together with other people. Well being of nations. Prosperity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `200`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `99`, printed page `200`):

> Erfahrungen im Frauen-Recht, Erfolg bei vielen Frauen, erfolgreiches Gefühls-Wissen, Finanz-Erfolg der Frau, Finanz-Wirtschaft für das Volk, Handels-Erfolge eines Volkes, Rechts-Erfolg einer Frau, vorteilhafte Ausdehnung eines Volkes

Editorial translation from the German passage: Experiences in women's rights; success with many women; successful emotional knowledge; a woman's financial success; financial economy for the people; a people's trade successes; a woman's legal success; advantageous expansion of a people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Many happy hours and shared prosperity ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: ABC adds successful use of emotional knowledge.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Shared happiness, success with many women and women's rights appear in the sources.
- events/manifestations: Financial success, trade success and advantageous collective expansion are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Prosperity is an attributed possible expression, not a financial guarantee.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict is stated. Both sources emphasize favorable experiences at personal and collective scales.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies broad experience through happy hours shared with other people and prosperity at a collective level. ABC makes success concrete through women's rights, emotional knowledge, finance and trade. The recurring link is favorable participation and benefit across a wider field, while the particular economic or legal examples remain possibilities rather than promised results ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
