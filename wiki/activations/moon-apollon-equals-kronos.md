## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

To lead great masses and be a model leader. Expanding people. Great leading people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `201`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `99`, printed page `201`):

> Ansehen der Wissenschaft im Volk, besonderer Erfolg einer Frau, Erfahrungen eines großen Volkes, Erfolg einer angesehenen Frau, Handels-Überlegenheit eines Volkes, Hierarchie der Wissenschaft im Volk, wichtiges Gefühls-Wissen, Wirtschafts-Erfolge eines Volkes

Editorial translation from the German passage: The standing of science among a people; a woman's particular success; experiences of a great people; the success of a respected woman; a people's commercial superiority; the hierarchy of science among a people; important emotional knowledge; a people's economic successes.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Leadership of large populations and distinguished collective achievement.
- psychology: Witte describes being a model leader; ABC links success with reputation and experience.
- body/health: No distinct bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Leading people, respected women and the standing of science within a population are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Commercial superiority, economic success and hierarchy in science are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Public distinction and model leadership do not establish an individual's rank or qualifications.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses leading great masses; ABC broadens the picture to distinguished women and the scientific or economic standing of a people. These are related expressions of prominence, not identical offices.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair's broad population and experience themes through leadership and recognized standing. Witte's model leader of great masses is the clearest expression; ABC extends prominence to respected or successful women and to a people's science, commerce and economy. The connection is achievement with collective visibility, while the entry's specific claims of superiority remain attributed examples rather than an automatic judgment of personal excellence ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
