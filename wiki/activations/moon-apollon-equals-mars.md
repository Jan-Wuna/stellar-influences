## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

Busy for many hours in trade, industry, or science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `200`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `99`, printed page `200`):

> Arbeits-Erfahrungen mit Frauen, Freiheits-Wille eines Volkes, erfolgreiche Arbeits-Stunde, erfolgreich handelnde Frau, Gefühls-Wissen aktivieren, Tätigkeit in der Volks-Wirtschaft, wissenschaftliche Arbeit der Frau, zunehmende Gefühls-Handlungen

Editorial translation from the German passage: Work experiences with women; a people's will for freedom; a successful working hour; a woman acting successfully; activating emotional knowledge; activity in the national economy; a woman's scientific work; increasing actions arising from feeling.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Extended activity in trade, industry or science, with successful work as an ABC possibility.
- psychology: ABC names activating emotional knowledge and increasing actions arising from feeling.
- body/health: No distinct bodily or medical condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Work experiences with women and a people's will for freedom are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Many hours of work, a successful working hour, economic activity and women's scientific work are examples.
- conflicts/notes: The corrected Gefühls-Handlungen means actions arising from feeling, without a melancholy qualifier.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes prolonged busyness; ABC includes success, collective freedom and feeling translated into action. Long duration alone does not guarantee the successful outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Mars`, Mars gives the pair's duration and wider commercial or scientific activity a working expression: many hours spent busy in those fields. ABC adds work with women, economic activity and the activation of emotional knowledge, so the link is sustained engagement that puts experience and feeling into action. Its successful working hour and a people's will for freedom are particular expressions, not promises attached to every period of effort ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
