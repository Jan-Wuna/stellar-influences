## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

Greatness of soul. Generously predisposed. To be in touch with several nations, to be impressed by them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `200`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

ensouled emotional knowledge, experiences of a sensitive soul, freedom of my people, my success with women, my emotional knowledge, my experienced woman, my successful woman, my many women, soulful experiences of the woman, and hour of my success

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Generosity of soul and personal experience of several nations.
- psychology: A sensitive soul and personally felt emotional knowledge are ABC themes.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim is given.
- social/relationship: Contact with nations and personal relationships with experienced or successful women are explicit.
- events/manifestations: A personally successful hour and the freedom of one's people occur in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: The first-person examples locate personal involvement; they do not establish a required number of partners.

## Contradictions

Witte's generous disposition and encounters with nations differ in scale from ABC's intimate first-person examples. Both are retained without treating them as a single literal event.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = MC`, MC makes the pair's breadth of experience personally felt. Witte describes generosity of soul and impressions received through contact with several nations; ABC brings that scope into one's own emotional knowledge, successful hour and relationships with experienced or successful women. The common thread is personal participation in a wider field of experience, while the source's first-person relationship examples remain alternatives rather than a prescribed biography ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
