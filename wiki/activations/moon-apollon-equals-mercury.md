## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

Many hours of the youth. To be many hours on the move. Conversation for many hours. Languages and study of languages. Wider horizons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `200`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `99`, printed page `200`):

> Erfolg durch Sprach-Gefühl, Freiheit der weiblichen Jugend, Handels-Gespräche für ein Volk, Gefühls-Wissen in der Jugend, Medien-Freiheit im Volk, Nachrichten über Frauen-Handel, viele junge Frauen, viele Meinungen der Frau, wissenschaftlich denkende Frau

Editorial translation from the German passage: Success through a feel for language; freedom of young women; trade discussions for a people; emotional knowledge in youth; media freedom among a people; news about trade in women; many young women; a woman's many opinions; a scientifically thinking woman.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Extended conversation, movement and language study broaden experience.
- psychology: Linguistic sensitivity, emotional knowledge in youth and a woman's many opinions are ABC themes.
- body/health: No distinct bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Young women, freedom of young women and a people's media freedom appear in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Trade discussions, news about trade in women, and scientific thinking are specific examples.
- conflicts/notes: Sprach-Gefühl means a feel for language, not language fiction; the news example is retained as a source association.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes time spent moving, conversing and learning languages; ABC adds social freedom, trade discussions and women thinking scientifically. The wider horizons motif does not erase the troubling trade-in-women example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Mercury`, Mercury expresses the pair's many hours and wider horizons through conversation, movement and learning languages. ABC makes linguistic sensitivity a possible route to success and connects communication with youth, trade discussions and media freedom. This supports an interpretation of experience widening through language and exchange; the specific news about trade in women remains an attributed example, not a definition of all communication under the formula ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
