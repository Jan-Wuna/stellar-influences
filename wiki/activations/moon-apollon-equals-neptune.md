## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

Many feel themselves deceived, cheated out of their future. Misled masses. Mistrust between nations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `201`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `99`, printed page `201`):

> Ahnungen einer erfolgreichen Frau, Erfolg einer Frau im Wasser, erfolgreiche Stunde in der Luft, Freiheit eines fremden Volkes, metaphysisches Wissen einer Frau, Täuschungen durch Gefühls-Wissen, Unsicherheit vieler Frauen

Editorial translation from the German passage: A successful woman's premonitions; a woman's success in water; a successful hour in the air; the freedom of a foreign people; a woman's metaphysical knowledge; deceptions through emotional knowledge; uncertainty among many women.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Widespread deception, misleading and uncertainty about the future.
- psychology: Feeling cheated, premonitions and deception through emotional knowledge are explicit.
- body/health: Water and air occur as settings; no medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Misled masses, mistrust between nations and many uncertain women are social expressions.
- events/manifestations: ABC also names a woman's success in water, a successful hour in air and a foreign people's freedom.
- conflicts/notes: Ahnungen means premonitions; Täuschungen means deceptions, not an exchange of knowledge.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes deception and mistrust; ABC also retains successful or liberating examples. Those positive possibilities do not remove the shared uncertainty theme.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Neptune`, Neptune qualifies the pair's broad population and future prospects with uncertainty: many people feel deceived or cheated, and mistrust can spread between nations. ABC parallels this through uncertain women and deception arising through emotional knowledge, while also allowing premonitions and successful experiences in water or air. The source supports a tension between sensed possibilities and dependable expectations; it does not make every intuition false or every collective prospect deceptive ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
