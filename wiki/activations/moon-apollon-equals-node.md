## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

To be in touch with many. Union of nations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `200`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `99`, printed page `200`):

> Beziehung zu erfolgreichen Frauen, erfolgreiche Kontakte zum Volk, Gefühls-Erfahrungen in Verbindungen, Handels-Verbindungen eines Volkes, Kontakte zu erfahrenen Frauen, mit vielen Frauen verbunden, Verbindung zur Wissenschaft eines Volkes

Editorial translation from the German passage: Relationship with successful women; successful contacts with a people; emotional experiences in connections; a people's trade connections; contacts with experienced women; being connected with many women; a connection to a people's science.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Contact with many people and association among nations.
- psychology: Emotional experiences within connections are ABC's experiential expression.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Successful or experienced women, many women and contacts with a population are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Trade connections and connections to a people's science are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: A union of nations is one collective expression; no particular institution or political outcome is named.

## Contradictions

Witte gives broad contact and union; ABC specifies relationships with women and commercial or scientific connections. The examples vary in scale without requiring one literal organization.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Node`, Node turns the pair's multitude and breadth of experience into connections: contact with many people and, at a collective scale, union among nations. ABC supplies more specific relationships with successful or experienced women, emotional experiences in associations, and trade or scientific connections. The common meaning is participation in an extended field of contacts, while each named relationship remains a possible setting rather than a mandatory social network ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
