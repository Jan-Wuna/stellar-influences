## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

Changes in hourly schedule. Changes or transformations in public develop from small beginnings. Changes or transformations in the masses of people, or in the structure of nations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `201`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `99`, printed page `201`):

> Erfolgreiche Gemüts-Entwicklung, Entfaltung vieler Frauen, Entwicklung der Volks-Wirtschaft, Freiheits-Gefühle entfalten, Gefühls-Wissen entwickeln, wachsender Frauen-Handel, Wandlungen einer erfahrenen Frau, zunehmende Entwicklung der Frau

Editorial translation from the German passage: Successful emotional development; the unfolding of many women; development of the national economy; unfolding feelings of freedom; developing emotional knowledge; growing trade in women; changes in an experienced woman; increasing development of women.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Changes in schedules, public life and national structures can develop from small beginnings.
- psychology: ABC adds development of the emotions, feelings of freedom and emotional knowledge.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: The development of many women and transformations among populations are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Economic development and growing trade in women are separate ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: The former leading E was an alternate Pluto glyph; the troubling trade example remains attributed.

## Contradictions

Witte describes changes of scale and public structure without prescribing an outcome. ABC adds successful emotional development alongside growing trade in women; the positive and harmful examples must remain distinct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Pluto`, Pluto gives the pair's many hours and broad population a developing form: schedules change, and small beginnings can grow into public or national transformations. ABC also describes emotional development, women's development and change in the economy. The shared emphasis is change unfolding across time or many people; its success language does not turn every such development into a benefit, particularly the separate trade-in-women example ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
