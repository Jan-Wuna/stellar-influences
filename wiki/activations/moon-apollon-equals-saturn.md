## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

Separate from many. To quit an educational project. Separation of nations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `201`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `99`, printed page `201`):

> Aufgaben der Wissenschaft eines Volkes, Erfahrungen einer alten Frau, Erfolg einer Frau im Alter, Geduld eines großen Volkes, Gemüts-Störungen erfahren, Handels-Probleme eines Volkes, verlustreiche Volks-Wirtschaft, Schadens-Ausweitung für ein Volk

Editorial translation from the German passage: Tasks of a people's science; experiences of an old woman; a woman's success in old age; the patience of a great people; experiencing disturbances of mood; a people's trade problems; a national economy suffering losses; widening damage to a people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Separation from many people, withdrawal from education and division among nations.
- psychology: Patience and disturbances of mood are ABC themes.
- body/health: ABC names disturbances of mood without a specific diagnosis.
- social/relationship: An old woman's experience and a woman's success in old age are retained alongside collective difficulties.
- events/manifestations: Trade problems, economic losses and widening damage to a people are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: The success-in-old-age example coexists with separation and loss; neither is universal.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes separation and abandoning an educational project; ABC includes patience, experience and success in old age as well as loss. Those constructive possibilities are preserved beside the difficulties.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Saturn`, Saturn restricts the pair's multitude and extended experience through separation: people or nations part, or an educational undertaking is left behind. ABC gives the collective cost in trade problems, economic losses and widening damage, but also retains patience and success in old age. The connection is breadth brought under limits, with experienced endurance and loss as distinct possibilities; its mood language is not a clinical diagnosis ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
