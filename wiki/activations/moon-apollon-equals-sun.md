## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

The man who strives for freedom of action. The daily one and the same. Daily and hourly encounter many people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `200`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

Experiences between woman and man, experiences of woman with man, success hour of a man, successful woman of a man, feelings Knowledge of a man, commercial center of a people, many women of a man, and knowledge of man about the woman

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A man's freedom of action and repeated encounters with many people.
- psychology: ABC includes a man's emotional knowledge and knowledge about a woman.
- body/health: No distinct bodily or medical condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Experiences between women and men and a man's relationships with women are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Daily and hourly meetings, a successful hour and a people's commercial center are examples.
- conflicts/notes: The source's repeated daily wording is awkward but does not authorize an invented meaning.

## Contradictions

Witte's daily encounters and freedom of action differ from ABC's relationship and commercial examples. They remain possible expressions rather than one required personal biography.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Sun`, Sun brings the pair's many hours and multitude into a man's daily activity: repeated encounters with people and a striving for freedom of action. ABC adds experience between women and men, emotional knowledge and a successful hour, with a commercial center as a separate collective example. The shared theme is active participation in a broad field of encounters; the relationship wording does not establish a fixed number or kind of partners ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
