## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

Favorite everywhere. To get many friends. Many hours of love. Peace of nations. Peaceful spreading out of a people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `200`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `99`, printed page `200`):

> Ausdehnung der Harmonie mit der Frau, erfahrene Frau in der Liebe, erfolgreiche Frauen eines Volkes, Gefühls-Wissen in der Liebe, Kunst-Wissen einer Frau, Schönheits-Erfolg einer Frau, viel Harmonie-Gefühl, zunehmende Liebe zur Frau

Editorial translation from the German passage: Expansion of harmony with a woman; a woman experienced in love; successful women of a people; emotional knowledge in love; a woman's knowledge of art; a woman's success in beauty; much feeling of harmony; increasing love for a woman.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Many friends, hours of love and peaceful association among peoples.
- psychology: Harmony, emotional knowledge in love and growing love for a woman are ABC themes.
- body/health: No distinct bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Popularity, friendship and peace among nations are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Knowledge of art, success in beauty and peaceful expansion of a people are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Popularity and success are source possibilities rather than guarantees of affection or peace.

## Contradictions

Witte moves between personal popularity and peace among nations; ABC focuses more on women, love, harmony and art. The scales differ without requiring a single literal setting.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Venus`, Venus gives the pair's multitude and extended experience an affectionate, peaceful expression: many friends, many hours of love and peace among nations. ABC connects this with harmony with a woman, emotional knowledge in love and knowledge of art. The common thread is breadth sustained through enjoyment and concord, while the named successes in beauty or public affection remain particular possibilities rather than assured results ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
