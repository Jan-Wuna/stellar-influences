## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

To see efforts rewarded. To witness many hours of success. Might and power of masses in motion. Great success of nations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `201`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

expansion of the power of a people, influence through emotional knowledge, successful power of the woman, influence of an experienced woman, increase of the power of the woman, strength of a respected people, much violence in the people, and increasing violence against the woman

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Rewarded effort and success through the power of masses in motion.
- psychology: ABC names influence through emotional knowledge and the influence of an experienced woman.
- body/health: ABC includes violence against women without specifying an injury or medical condition.
- social/relationship: The power of nations and increasing power of women are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Many hours of success and national success coexist with much violence among a people.
- conflicts/notes: The Poseidon opening formerly attached to this Witte row has been restored to its own glyph; success and violence remain distinct.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses success and rewarded effort. ABC includes power and influence alongside violence against women; force cannot be reduced to a uniformly favorable meaning.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus gives the pair's many hours and collective scale forceful effect: effort is rewarded, and masses in motion can bring great success. ABC extends that effect through the power of a people and the influence or increasing power of women, but also names violence within a population and against women. The connection is collective force with substantial consequences; success and coercive harm remain distinct possibilities rather than interchangeable meanings ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
