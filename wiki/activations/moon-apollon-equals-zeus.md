### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `134`.

#### Witte Entry

Many creative hours. Scientific research, political survey. Leadership in politics.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `201`; PDF page: `99`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `99`, printed page `201`):

> Ansehen einer führenden Frau, Erfahrungen im führenden Volk, erfolgreiche Anstrengungen einer Frau, erfolgreicher Volks-Führer, Gefühls-Wissen erzeugen, Leistungs-Erfolge einer Frau, Selbständigkeit einer kreativen Frau, Stunde des schöpferischen Erfolges

Editorial translation from the German passage: The standing of a leading woman; experiences among a leading people; a woman's successful efforts; a successful leader of a people; creating emotional knowledge; a woman's achievements; a creative woman's independence; an hour of creative success.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Many creative hours, scientific research and political leadership.
- psychology: ABC names creating emotional knowledge and a creative woman's independence.
- body/health: No distinct bodily or medical condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: A leading woman's standing and successful leadership of a people are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Successful effort, achievement and an hour of creative success are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: The political survey phrase is retained as printed; no specific survey method or office is inferred.

## Contradictions

Witte links creativity with research and politics; ABC adds women's leadership and creative independence. These are related productive expressions, not a single required career.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Apollon = Zeus`, Zeus gives the pair's extended experience and public breadth a creative or leading task. Witte's many creative hours include scientific research and political leadership; ABC adds successful effort, a leading woman and the independence of a creative woman. The common thread is sustained creative work with potential public consequence, while success, research and leadership remain source examples rather than qualifications automatically granted by the formula ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
