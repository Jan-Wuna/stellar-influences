## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `81`.

#### Witte Entry

Grave hindrances and resistances in public. Deaths among the resident population.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `103`; PDF page: `50`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `50`, printed page `103`):

> Andere Völker sind deprimiert, blockierte Gefühle der Frau, Einengung durch Frauen als Partner, Gemüts-Depression des Partners, gründliche Frau als Partner, Stau in der Stunde am Ort, Umwelt-Widerstand durch Frauen, unterdrückte Partner-Gefühle

Editorial translation from the German passage: Other peoples are depressed; a woman's blocked feelings; restriction through women as partners; a partner's depressed mood; a thorough woman as partner; congestion at a place and hour; resistance in the surroundings through women; suppressed partner feelings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Serious local or public hindrance, resistance and blocked feeling.
- psychology: ABC explicitly names depressed mood, blocked feelings and suppressed partner feelings.
- body/health: Witte includes deaths among the resident population; ABC's depression wording is not a clinical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Restrictions through women as partners and resistance in the surroundings are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Congestion at a place and hour appears beside public resistance; a thorough woman as partner is a separate ABC example.
- conflicts/notes: Deaths and depression are attributed possibilities, not inevitable consequences of meeting resistance.

## Contradictions

Witte's grave public obstacles and deaths are stronger than ABC's range from depressed feelings to a thorough partner. The thoroughness example is retained without converting the entire entry into a favorable or fatal meaning.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Asc = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair's immediate people and emotional surroundings through obstruction: grave public resistance, blocked feeling and restriction in partnership. ABC carries this into suppressed partner feelings and congestion at a place and hour, while also retaining a thorough woman as partner. Witte's deaths among residents is a separate severe association, not an outcome inferred from every emotional blockage. The connection is local or relational life meeting a limit, with the severity kept specific to each source example ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
