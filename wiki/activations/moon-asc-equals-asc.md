#### Munkasey Entry

An enhanced ability to read the mood and emotions of those around you; the other than verbal convictions you receive about the moods and attitudes of people you contact; an emphasis on emotional responses.

## Comparative Schema

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Asc = Asc`, the repeated Asc closes Munkasey's `Moon/Asc` pair on immediate emotional encounters. His With Itself entry describes an enhanced ability to read nearby people's moods and receive impressions beyond words, with emotional response itself emphasized. This makes sensitivity to the contacted person's attitude the central meaning; it does not establish that every impression is accurate or add a separate medical or event claim ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
