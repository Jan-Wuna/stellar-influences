#### Sandbach Entry

Your energy helps others to be more aware of how they feel about themselves and their relationships, and to allow emotional energy to flow more freely when they re late. You help people to see how their feelings conditions their identity.

Editorial note: The raw PDF itself prints the split word `re late` and the grammatical form `feelings conditions`; the passage is retained. The synthesis below paraphrases the clear relationship between feelings and identity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Helping others recognize their feelings about themselves and their relationships.
- psychology: Awareness of how feelings shape identity and freer emotional flow are central.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition or therapeutic procedure is specified.
- social/relationship: The help is directed toward other people as they relate.
- events/manifestations: Recognizing feelings and allowing more emotional expression are the stated changes.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's malformed words are retained as printed; the clear meaning is paraphrased rather than silently repaired.

## Contradictions

Only Sandbach's Chiron entry is present. Its help with emotional awareness does not establish a general healing role or clinical treatment.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Asc = Chiron`, Chiron directs the pair's sensitivity in relationships toward helping other people notice their own feelings. Sandbach connects this help with freer emotional exchange and recognition of how feeling shapes identity. The distinguishing emphasis is assistance with awareness during relating, rather than merely absorbing another person's mood; the entry does not specify a healing technique or promise a therapeutic result ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
