## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `81`.

#### Witte Entry

Social gatherings with women. Getting acquainted with the wife. The quiet hours in the home. To become a public person through unions or parties.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `103`; PDF page: `50`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `50`, printed page `103`):

> Andere Volks-Gemeinschaften, Frauen-Gemeinschaften für die Umwelt, Frauen-Kunst am Ort, gefühlvolle Partner-Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Nähe zur Mutter, gemeinsame Stunden am Ort, Gemeinschafts-Ort für Frauen, Kunst-Ort einer Gemeinschaft

Editorial translation from the German passage: Communities of other peoples; women's communities for the surroundings; women's art at the place; an emotionally responsive partnership; shared closeness to the mother; shared hours at the place; a community place for women; a community's place for art.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Gatherings, quiet domestic hours and shared local or public association.
- psychology: ABC retains emotional partnership and shared closeness to the mother.
- body/health: No distinct bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Female gatherings, meeting a wife and affiliation with unions or parties are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Shared hours at a place, women's community spaces and art within a community are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Domestic quiet and becoming public through organizations are distinct settings; neither is a required social identity.

## Contradictions

Witte combines quiet home life with public recognition through groups; ABC adds local women's communities and art. The private and public expressions are retained without reducing the entry to family life alone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Asc = Cupido`, Cupido gives the pair's immediate relationships a shared setting: gatherings with women, quiet hours at home or public affiliation through unions and parties. ABC adds emotional partnership, shared closeness to the mother and community places for women or art. The connection is feeling and acquaintance held within a common place or association; domestic intimacy and organized public belonging remain different expressions of that connection ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
