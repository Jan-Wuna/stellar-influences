## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page (entry start): `81`.

#### Witte Entry

Ailments of female persons. Unpleasant things through women. Oppressive events in public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `103`; PDF page: `50`.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (transcribed from PDF page `50`, printed page `103`):

> Andere Völker in Not, bekümmertes Gemüt des Partners, Dienst der Mutter am Ort, gefährdete Umwelt der Mutter, gefährlicher Ort für die Frau, Kriminalität eines anderen Volkes, mangelndes Gefühl einer Frau, Stunde der Gefahr am Ort

Editorial translation from the German passage: Other peoples in need; a partner's sorrowful mood; the mother's service at the place; the mother's threatened surroundings; a dangerous place for a woman; criminality of another people; a woman's lack of feeling; an hour of danger at the place.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficulty, need and oppressive events in immediate or public surroundings.
- psychology: ABC names a partner's sorrowful mood and a woman's lack of feeling.
- body/health: Witte names ailments of women without a particular diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Other peoples in need, maternal service and a threatened environment around the mother are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Danger to a woman at a place and a local hour of danger are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Crime is an attributed collective example, not a judgment about a people or nearby woman.

## Contradictions

Witte's ailments and oppressive events overlap with ABC's danger and need. ABC's service by the mother is a separate role, and its criminality example does not describe every person in the environment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Asc = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair's nearby people and public encounters through need, ailment or oppression. ABC adds a sorrowful partner, a threatened environment around the mother and danger at a place; Witte keeps unpleasant encounters and women's ailments. These entries connect difficult feeling with concrete local relationships and conditions. Their crime and danger examples remain attributed possibilities, without making a nearby woman or another people inherently harmful ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
