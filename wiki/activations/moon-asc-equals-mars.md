## Identity

## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The wife. The mother. Quarrels with female persons. Common activity with others in public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

woman's place of work, women acting in the environment, partner's impulse, melodious actions of others, hour of activity in the place, woman's activity in the place, the power of other women, and the will of other peoples

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The tendency to do things on the spur of the moment, or when the mood strikes one. The need to act out or share how one is feeling in a relationship. The effectiveness of one’s actions depends on one’s feelings about oneself.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

An irritation and impatience with those who interfere with your procedures; quickly receiving impressions or feelings from others close to you; activates you to develop a unique personal style of caring and concern.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Asc = Mars`: protective courage, direct emotional honesty, and the ability to act on instinct without delay. Main caution: reactivity: mood and temper can surface too fast and turn closeness into conflict or strain.
