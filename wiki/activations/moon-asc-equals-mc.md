## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Asc + Moon`; one-based PDF page `81` (entry starts here; supplied parser locator, not visually verified).

Concerning ones own mother. Acquaintances with women. The psychological influence of a woman. To be brought into public by others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Aszendent + Mond`; printed p. `102`; PDF page `50` (complete oriented row checked against actual scan).

Other women and me, soulful hour at the place, feelingful partner-soul, my wife as partner, my mother nearby, my partner feelings, maternal soul in the surroundings, soulful woman at the place, hour and minute at the place.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Moon/Ascendant midpoint`; exact Midheaven entry at one-based PDF page `40` (checked in actual raw PDF text).

One’s career involves helping others to realize how their emotions and subconscious form their identity, and how clearing out the emotional body can release unneeded limitations surrounding one’s self-concept. A career having to do with relat ing to others on a feeling and/or instinctual level.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `140`

#### Munkasey Entry

Attaining positions or power due to your ability to read and satisfy the needs of others; the experience you gain in understanding the emotional side of the many people you work or have day-to-day interactions with.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Emotional contact with nearby people enters personal identity, public exposure and vocational direction.
- psychology: Understanding how feelings and subconscious patterns shape self-concept joins sensitivity to others' needs.
- body/health: Sandbach's emotional-body language concerns his spiritual process, not a physical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Mother, wife, female acquaintances and a partner's feelings retain their historical source settings.
- events/manifestations: Being brought into public by others and advancement through reading needs are stated possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: Public and vocational emphases overlap without equating a family relationship with a career; the ABC first-person clauses remain distinct.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Personal relationships, the experience of a place and vocational care are different settings for emotional contact; source-specific gender and spiritual language remain attributed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Asc = MC`, contact with others' feelings becomes personally consequential: responding to needs can contribute to advancement, while helping people recognize how emotion shapes identity can become vocational work. That reflective role connects one's own self-concept with emotional and instinctual relating. The traditional mother, wife and female-acquaintance examples, psychological influence of a woman and public exposure through others retain more specific personal settings; ABC also preserves one's partner feelings and a soulful or maternal local atmosphere. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey, p. 140](#munkasey-entry).)
