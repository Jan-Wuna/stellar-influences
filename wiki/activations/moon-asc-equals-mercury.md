## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Asc = Mercury`, emotional contact becomes conversation, correspondence and thoughtful attention to care. Professional exchanges with many people and feelingful partner conversations give that communication social forms, while reflection on how to protect or nurture others deepens understanding of the motives behind mutual support. ABC's young woman or mother, women of other peoples and opinions about the environment retain specific gendered and local examples. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Munkasey, p. 140](#munkasey-entry).)
