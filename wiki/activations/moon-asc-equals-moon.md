- Source heading: `MOON/ASCENDANT With Itself`

#### Munkasey Entry

The personal sensitivity you have about picking up the emotional state of people near to you; your instinct for judging the mood of people from their body language and postures; the support you get from being with others.

## Comparative Schema

- social/relationship: Companionship provides support while proximity makes others' feelings perceptible.

## Derived Synthesis

Munkasey links this sensitivity to observing posture and body language and to receiving support from companionship.
