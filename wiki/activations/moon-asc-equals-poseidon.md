## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Contact with others is specified through insight, knowledge and cultural or spiritual refinement.
- psychology: Receptivity to intellectual and spiritual influence; a partner's insight is an ABC example.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Witte emphasizes communication with high-minded women; ABC includes knowledgeable partners and cultural surroundings.
- events/manifestations: Appearing spiritualized in public and an occasion of insight at a place are source examples.
- conflicts/notes: The scan's feelingful insightful partner joins emotional receptivity and insight; truth remains a source subject rather than certification of a belief.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Intellectual or spiritual refinement and feelingful insight overlap; the source's gendered and cultural examples remain specific.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Asc = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies contact with others through insight and intellectual or spiritual influence. Witte describes conversation with high-minded women and a spiritualized public appearance; ABC broadens the setting to an insightful partner, knowledge and cultural surroundings. The shared emphasis is refinement in contact and receptivity to insight. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC, printed p. 103](#abc-entry))
