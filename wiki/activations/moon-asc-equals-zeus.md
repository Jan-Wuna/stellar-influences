## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creative activity, initiative and leadership specify contact with others.
- psychology: Purposeful effort and emotionally charged partnership appear in ABC.
- body/health: Pregnancy and procreation are traditional examples, not fertility diagnoses or predictions.
- social/relationship: Creative or leading women, stimulating acquaintances and people who issue orders.
- events/manifestations: Public emergence through one's activity and military contacts are Witte's stated manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: The scan's achievements of other mothers is a distinct clause; pregnancy and procreation retain historical source status.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The local, public and relational examples retain their distinct source settings and do not imply universal outcomes.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Asc = Zeus`, Zeus specifies contact with others through creative effort, stimulation or command. Witte links it to stimulating female acquaintances, military or ordering figures and becoming public through one's own activity; ABC adds creative partners and purposeful women. Pregnancy and procreation remain specific traditional examples within this wider range, rather than a universal fertility claim. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC, printed p. 103](#abc-entry))
