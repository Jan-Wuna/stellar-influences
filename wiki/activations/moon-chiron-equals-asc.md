### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Moon/Chiron midpoint`; exact Ascendant entry at one-based PDF page `33` (checked in actual raw PDF text).

The ability to heal and cleanse negative feelings about the self, and to let go of identifying with one’s insecurities by attuning to a very deep level of spiritual security.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Release of negative self-feelings through a deeper sense of spiritual security.
- psychology: Loosening identification with insecurity rather than treating insecurity as the whole self.
- body/health: Sandbach's healing language concerns feelings and spiritual security; no bodily mechanism is supplied.
- social/relationship: The entry concerns self-feeling; it does not specify a partner or interpersonal event.
- events/manifestations: An internal shift in self-identification is described, without a concrete external event.
- conflicts/notes: This is one Sandbach entry; spiritual security is its stated frame, not a verified treatment claim.

## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's `Moon/Chiron = Asc`, Asc specifies emotional clearing through one's relationship to oneself. A deeper sense of spiritual security permits negative self-feelings and insecurity to lose their hold on identity. The entry supports that inward change, without supplying a separate relationship scenario or physical healing claim. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
