### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Moon/Chiron midpoint`; exact Jupiter entry at one-based PDF pages `32–33` (checked in actual raw PDF text).

An enthusiasm and optimism that nurtures others in a profoundly healing way. A generous way of nurturing others that helps them to overcome emotional blocks and encourages them to be more aligned with their instincts.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Generous, optimistic nurture is the form of emotional healing described.
- psychology: Enthusiasm helps others approach emotional blocks and trust their instincts.
- body/health: The healing claim concerns emotional blocks; no medical condition or intervention is specified.
- social/relationship: Care is offered generously to others, with encouragement rather than a named relationship role.
- events/manifestations: Support that helps another person move beyond emotional blocks is the stated manifestation.
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach supplies this orientation; optimism does not guarantee a healing outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's `Moon/Chiron = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies emotional healing as generous encouragement. Enthusiasm and optimism make nurturing expansive enough to help others approach emotional blocks and reconnect with their instincts. This is a claim about the quality of care in the entry, not a promise that positivity resolves every difficulty. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
