## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Moon/Chiron midpoint`; exact Midheaven entry at one-based PDF page `33` (checked in actual raw PDF text).

One’s path in this lifetime has to do with bringing the warmth of nurturing and love to others in a very pure form, and creating a sense of security wherever one goes.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Nurturing warmth and creating security are presented as a life direction.
- psychology: A sense of purpose is connected to giving love and emotional reassurance.
- body/health: No physical health condition or treatment is stated.
- social/relationship: The stated concern is carrying warmth to others wherever one goes.
- events/manifestations: Creating a secure atmosphere is the entry's manifestation; no career title is named.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's life-path language is a single source's interpretation, not an established destiny.

## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's `Moon/Chiron = MC`, MC specifies a life direction organized around bringing warmth, nurture and love to others. Creating a sense of security is the practical expression of that direction in the entry. The passage does not name an occupation or establish that this must be a person's destiny.
