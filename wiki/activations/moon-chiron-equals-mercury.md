## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's `Moon/Chiron = Mercury`, Mercury specifies emotional nurture through thinking and communication. What is communicated carries the healing emotional quality itself, so that thought and speech become the means of deeply affecting others. The short entry supports that connection without identifying a profession or treatment. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
