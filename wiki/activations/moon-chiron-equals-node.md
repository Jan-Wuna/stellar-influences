## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Moon/Chiron midpoint`; exact Node entry at one-based PDF page `33` (checked in actual raw PDF text).

When connecting with others one naturally helps them to get in touch with their feelings and to allow them to flow in a cleansing, healing manner.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connection with others is the setting in which emotional clearing occurs.
- psychology: Helping people recognize their feelings and allow them to move freely.
- body/health: The healing claim concerns emotion; no physical illness or treatment is named.
- social/relationship: Contact itself is described as naturally helping another person access feelings.
- events/manifestations: An encounter that lets another person's feelings flow is the stated manifestation.
- conflicts/notes: Only one source supplies this claim; no particular kind or duration of bond is specified.

## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's `Moon/Chiron = Node`, Node specifies emotional clearing through connection. The relationship or encounter helps another person get in touch with feelings and let them flow, so that contact becomes the vehicle of nurture. The passage does not require a particular relationship type or promise a permanent bond. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
