## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Moon/Chiron midpoint`; exact Saturn entry at one-based PDF page `33` (checked in actual raw PDF text).

A fear of clearing the emotional body, but one knows at a very deep level that this must be done. Many of the limitations and difficulties in one’s life can be cleared by allowing one’s emotions to flow and by knowing that one is ultimately safe and there fore need not hold on to or store feelings that need to be let go of.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Emotional clearing meets fear and the need for a deeper sense of safety.
- psychology: Holding onto feelings is linked to fear, while trust makes release conceivable.
- body/health: 'Emotional body' describes Sandbach's spiritual account of feeling; no physical mechanism is given.
- social/relationship: The entry addresses one's own limitations without naming a partner or helper.
- events/manifestations: Letting stored feelings flow is presented as a way to ease some life difficulties.
- conflicts/notes: The source's broad language about clearing limitations remains attributed; it does not promise universal resolution.

## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's `Moon/Chiron = Saturn`, Saturn specifies emotional clearing through the obstacle of fear: feelings are retained even while the need to release them is recognized. A deep sense of safety is the condition Sandbach gives for allowing those feelings to flow and easing limitations. His account concerns emotional and spiritual experience, without establishing a medical treatment or a guarantee that all difficulties disappear. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
