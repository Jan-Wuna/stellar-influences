## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Moon/Chiron midpoint`; exact Sun entry at one-based PDF page `32` (checked in actual raw PDF text).

The goal of one’s life is dependent on one’s ability to allow emotions to flow openly and freely so that the emotional body may be cleansed, vitalized and purified. The ego is healed by deep attunement to and acceptance of one’s feelings as well as the feelings of others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Life purpose and ego renewal are connected to open acceptance of feeling.
- psychology: Attunement to one's own feelings and those of others is the stated path.
- body/health: Cleansing and vitalizing the 'emotional body' are Sandbach's spiritual terms, not physical treatment claims.
- social/relationship: Acceptance extends to other people's feelings as well as one's own.
- events/manifestations: Freer emotional flow is the process described; no concrete external event is forecast.
- conflicts/notes: The strong life-goal claim belongs to this single source and is not universal necessity.

## Contradictions

Only Sandbach's entry is displayed, so this page cannot establish agreement or disagreement between sources.

## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's `Moon/Chiron = Sun`, Sun specifies emotional clearing in relation to purpose and the ego. Allowing feelings to flow and accepting both one's own feelings and other people's are presented as renewing the self. This is Sandbach's spiritual interpretation of emotional acceptance, rather than proof that a person's life goal or physical health depends on it. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
