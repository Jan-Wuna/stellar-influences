## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Moon/Chiron midpoint`; exact Uranus entry at one-based PDF page `33` (checked in actual raw PDF text).

When you allow your originality and uniqueness as an individual to shine forth it has an emotionally cleansing and clearing effect on others, which helps them to re ceive the nurturing from the universe that they need.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Expressing individuality is described as helping emotional clearing in others.
- psychology: Originality is allowed to emerge rather than being concealed.
- body/health: Cleansing language concerns emotional experience; no bodily health result is supplied.
- social/relationship: Another person's expression of uniqueness may help others receive nurture.
- events/manifestations: Showing originality is the stated action; no sudden event is required by this passage.
- conflicts/notes: 'Nurturing from the universe' remains Sandbach's spiritual claim, not independently established causation.

## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's `Moon/Chiron = Uranus`, Uranus specifies emotional nurture through the open expression of individuality. Allowing originality to show is described as emotionally clearing for others and helping them receive the nurture they need. The passage frames that nurture spiritually, as coming from the universe, and does not establish a separate event or physical mechanism. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
