### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Moon/Chiron midpoint`; exact Venus entry at one-based PDF page `32` (checked in actual raw PDF text).

One relates to others in an emotionally healing way. When one relates emotions flow in cleansing fashion.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Relating to others becomes a vehicle for emotional release.
- psychology: Feelings are allowed to flow in the course of interaction.
- body/health: The healing language concerns emotion; no bodily condition or technique is stated.
- social/relationship: The entry describes the quality of relating without specifying romance or partnership.
- events/manifestations: Emotional flow during contact is the only stated manifestation.
- conflicts/notes: This sparse Sandbach entry does not establish romantic success, attraction or a clinical outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's `Moon/Chiron = Venus`, Venus specifies emotional clearing in the way a person relates to others. Interaction allows feelings to flow and is described as nurturing through that release. The brief entry supports this relational quality without making a separate claim about romance, attraction or medical healing. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
