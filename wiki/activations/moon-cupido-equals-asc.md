## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = Asc`, community becomes direct company in shared surroundings: visitors, leisure at home and brief attendance at a meeting make the encounter concrete. Emotional community and a shared feeling of belonging with a partner connect that local company to intimacy, while women's communities and art at a place give it collective and artistic settings. Witte's wife and fiancée examples retain their specific betrothal context. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
