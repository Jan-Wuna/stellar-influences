## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Moon + Cupido`; one-based PDF page `130` (exact oriented entry checked against actual scan).

Willingly marrying women. Happy bride or wife. Happy leisure hours. Women happy in their associations. Men happily associated with women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Cupido`; printed p. `192`; PDF page `95` (complete oriented row checked against actual scan).

Successful woman in art, finances of a people's community, women's rights in the community, shared right or law for a people, a community's sense of right, a woman's art of law (Rechts-Kunst einer Frau), satisfied people's community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Satisfaction and favorable association specify community and marriage.
- psychology: Happiness in shared company is Witte's explicit emotional emphasis.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Happy brides, wives and association with women are traditional examples; ABC adds women's rights.
- events/manifestations: Enjoyable leisure, artistic success, community finances and common law are distinct listed manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's Rechts-Kunst einer Frau is retained literally as a woman's art of law; its precise compound meaning is unresolved and no expanded role is inferred.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The legal and financial examples do not promise prosperity; the literal Rechts-Kunst compound remains unexpanded.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies community through satisfaction and favorable shared life. Witte emphasizes happiness in marriage, company and leisure; ABC adds communal finances, women's rights, common law and artistic success. The resulting range joins enjoyable belonging with favorable communal arrangements, while leaving each legal or financial outcome a possibility rather than a promise. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
