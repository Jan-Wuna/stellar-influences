## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community and marriage are specified through authority, distinction or independence.
- psychology: ABC names strong community feeling without a fuller psychological account.
- body/health: No bodily or medical manifestation is stated.
- social/relationship: Authorities affecting marriage and leaders or respected women within a community.
- events/manifestations: State-ordered free time and leisure devoted to great things are Witte's examples.
- conflicts/notes: Authority over a group and the group's independence are different ABC examples, not a single guaranteed hierarchy.

## Contradictions

The displayed examples differ in range rather than making mutually exclusive claims. Shared traditional vocabulary does not establish independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = Kronos`, Kronos specifies community through authority and distinction. Witte connects marriage and leisure to official influence, including state-ordered free time; ABC names leaders, respected women and an independent community. These examples concern the standing and organization of shared life without establishing that every association is either controlled by the state or socially eminent. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
