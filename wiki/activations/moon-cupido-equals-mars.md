- Source passage: `Mond + Cupido`; printed p. `192`; PDF page `95` (complete oriented row checked against actual scan).

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community is expressed through joint action, sociability and leisure activity.
- psychology: A desire for marriage or company appears in Witte; ABC names communal will.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Sociable women and men who prefer women's company are traditional examples.
- events/manifestations: Amateur pursuits, shared activities and a woman's artistic work are explicit manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered marriage examples are historically attributed; joint activity does not imply conflict.

## Contradictions

The displayed examples differ in range rather than making mutually exclusive claims. Shared traditional vocabulary does not establish independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = Mars`, community is enacted through participation: shared emotional activity, a women's working community and collective will give belonging an active form. Leisure pursuits, the amateur and a woman's artistic activity connect sociability with doing something together. Witte's wedding bride and people seeking marriage or company retain their historical relationship examples without making joint activity inherently conflictual.
