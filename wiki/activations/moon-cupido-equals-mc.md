## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community becomes personally felt and part of one's own social experience.
- psychology: Social inclination and an inwardly felt sense of belonging are explicit themes.
- body/health: ABC's soul vocabulary is spiritual description, not a bodily claim.
- social/relationship: One's fiancée and company with women are Witte's traditional relational examples.
- events/manifestations: Clubs, balls and dancing are concrete settings; ABC also names personal involvement in art.
- conflicts/notes: The source's unusual One own’s wording is preserved from the scan; personal community feeling does not independently specify profession or public status.

## Contradictions

The displayed examples differ in range rather than making mutually exclusive claims. Shared traditional vocabulary does not establish independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = MC`, MC specifies community as one's own lived and inwardly felt involvement. Witte describes personal social inclination, engagement and enjoyment of clubs, balls and dancing; ABC emphasizes one's own feeling of community and connection with art. The interpretation centers on personal belonging, while preserving betrothal as a traditional example. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
