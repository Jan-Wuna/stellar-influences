## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Moon + Cupido`; one-based PDF page `130` (exact oriented entry checked against actual scan).

Hours of play in youth. Correspondence with or of the bride. Literary or social entertainment during leisure hours. To think of women or the community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Cupido`; printed p. `192`; PDF page `95` (complete oriented row checked against actual scan).

Feelingful community of conversation, community of a people's youth, community of young women, artistic feeling for language, women speaking about art, opinions about the art of a people, hour of a young community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community and leisure take the form of conversation, correspondence and literary activity.
- psychology: Thinking about women or the community is Witte's stated mental emphasis.
- body/health: No bodily or medical manifestation is stated.

- events/manifestations: Play in youth, bridal correspondence and literary or social entertainment.
- conflicts/notes: The scan reads feelingful conversation community and artistic feeling for language; it contains no French-language claim.
