## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Trust in community or betrothal is clouded by deception, disappointment or uncertainty.
- psychology: False or weak feelings of belonging and uncertainty within a group.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Betrayed fiancées, deceived people and social intrigues are Witte's examples.
- events/manifestations: Denial of marriage or being deprived of leisure are stated possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: The complete seven-clause Neptune row ends with refined art of a woman; the subsequent developmental clauses belong to Pluto.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Communicative, social and emotional examples retain their distinct scope within this orientation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = Neptune`, shared life is marked by uncertainty or disappointment in trust: a wronged fiancé, denied marriage, lost leisure and social intrigue give the theme concrete forms. False or weak community feeling and an uncertain woman within the community describe a fragile sense of belonging. A foreign people's community and refined art of a woman remain distinct examples within the row, while the developmental clauses belong to the separate Pluto orientation. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
