## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Moon + Cupido`; one-based PDF page `130` (exact oriented entry checked against actual scan).

Leisure hours devoted to spiritual enjoyment. To stay in company with people who ideologically favor the higher and finer qualities of the mind.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Cupido`; printed p. `193`; PDF page `95` (complete oriented row checked against actual scan).

Women in a cultural community, intellectual or spiritual community of a people, clarity in a women's community, art and culture of a people, hour of truth in a community, spiritualized art of a woman, reasonable woman in the community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community and leisure are oriented toward spiritual enjoyment and culture.
- psychology: Preference for elevated intellectual or spiritual company is Witte's emphasis.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Cultural communities, women's associations and spiritually minded companions.
- events/manifestations: Leisure devoted to spiritual enjoyment and a people's arts and culture.
- conflicts/notes: The scan reads hour of truth, spiritualized art and a reasonable woman; these cultural/spiritual examples do not certify moral or factual superiority.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The specific emotional, social and artistic settings remain side by side rather than becoming universal outcomes.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = Poseidon`, shared life centers on intellectual, spiritual and cultural enjoyment. Leisure with people who value the higher qualities of mind connects companionship to a spiritual community, clarity within a women's community and a people's art and culture. The hour of truth, a woman's spiritualized art and a reasonable woman are distinct examples of this orientation toward understanding, without making the group's claims automatically true. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
