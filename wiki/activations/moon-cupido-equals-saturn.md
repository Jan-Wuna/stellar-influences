## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community and marriage are specified through separation, burden or loss.
- psychology: Loneliness in free time and shared painful feelings.
- body/health: Widowhood is Witte's historical example; it is not an individual mortality prediction.
- social/relationship: Desertion, separation and older women's communities are distinct examples.
- events/manifestations: Solitary leisure, pensioning, communal tasks and losses are listed manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: The scan distinguishes burdened feelings, shared pain and disturbed feelings; the historical widowhood example remains separate.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The specific emotional, social and artistic settings remain side by side rather than becoming universal outcomes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = Saturn`, belonging is constrained by separation, loss or emotional burden. Desertion, a separated wife and solitary leisure give this a personal form, while serious communal tasks, shared pain, disturbed feelings and patient endurance give it collective expressions. Women's community in old age and pensioning retain age-related settings; widowhood remains a specific historical example rather than a forecast. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
