## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community is embodied in family and in people sharing life.
- psychology: The man's feeling of community and personal community feelings are explicit ABC clauses.
- body/health: No bodily or medical manifestation is stated.
- social/relationship: Bride, wife, family home and community of woman and man retain concrete relational settings.
- events/manifestations: Recurring leisure and centers of a people's community, folk art and women's community are listed.
- conflicts/notes: The scan restores all seven ABC clauses; personal feelings and artistic centers remain distinct from a generic list of communities.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The specific emotional, social and artistic settings remain side by side rather than becoming universal outcomes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = Sun`, shared life has a personal and concrete setting in the bride, wife, family home and recurring leisure. Community between woman and man is experienced through the man's and the person's own feelings of belonging. Centers of a people's community, folk art and a women's community extend that shared life into collective places without replacing the personal emotional connection. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
