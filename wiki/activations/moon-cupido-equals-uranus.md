## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community and leisure are marked by excitement, interruption or sudden change.
- psychology: Irritation, excitement and unconventional interests appear in Witte.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Sudden marriage, new women entering a community and reform of shared life.
- events/manifestations: Unexpected free time and leisure devoted to eccentric interests are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: The scan restores folk-art impulses and tensions in an emotional community; the extracted leading marker is absent from the printed prose.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The specific emotional, social and artistic settings remain side by side rather than becoming universal outcomes.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = Uranus`, shared life becomes excited, surprising or unsettled. Sudden marriage and new women entering a community alter relationships, while reforms, unrest and tensions change the collective emotional setting. Folk-art impulses and eccentric leisure interests retain creative or unconventional forms of this excitement; unexpected free time gives it a practical leisure expression.
