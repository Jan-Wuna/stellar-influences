## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Moon + Cupido`; one-based PDF page `130` (exact oriented entry checked against actual scan).

Women who marry for love. To prefer the company of women. Leisure hours devoted to love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Cupido`; printed p. `192`; PDF page `95` (complete oriented row checked against actual scan).

Joy in a woman's art, shared love for the mother, harmonious emotional community, harmony of a women's community, the woman's art of love, peaceful people's community, the woman's loving community, fondness for folk art.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affection and harmony specify community and shared leisure.
- psychology: Preference for women's company and enjoyment of love are Witte's emphasis.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Marriage for love, shared love for a mother and harmony in women's communities.
- events/manifestations: Leisure devoted to love and a peaceful community are clear examples.
- conflicts/notes: The scan gives a woman's art and harmonious emotional community, not a kiss or animal community; affection and art remain distinct linked themes.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Love, artistic enjoyment and harmonious shared feeling coexist without making every association romantic.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = Venus`, affection and artistic enjoyment make shared life pleasant: marriage for love, preferred company and leisure devoted to love give intimacy a concrete place. Shared love for a mother, harmonious emotional or women's communities and a peaceful people's community broaden that affection into belonging. Joy in a woman's art and fondness for folk art preserve aesthetic enjoyment alongside love. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
