## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community and its activities appear in public.
- psychology: The entries describe public settings, without a separate inner psychological account.
- body/health: No bodily or medical manifestation is stated.
- social/relationship: Women proceeding to marriage and women's communities in public life.
- events/manifestations: Weddings, general gatherings, public halls and public presentation of art.
- conflicts/notes: The public setting does not by itself establish fame or artistic success.

## Contradictions

The displayed examples differ in range rather than making mutually exclusive claims. Shared traditional vocabulary does not establish independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies community as publicly shared activity. Witte gives weddings, general social gatherings and public halls; ABC includes women's communities, shared feeling and art in public life. The unifying meaning is public participation in communal life, without a separate promise of recognition or success. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
