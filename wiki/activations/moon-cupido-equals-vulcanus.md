## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Moon + Cupido`; one-based PDF page `130` (exact oriented entry checked against actual scan).

To utilize free time to the utmost. To be in a mighty community. To come into public through a community. Successful amateur work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Cupido`; printed p. `193`; PDF page `95` (complete oriented row checked against actual scan).

Emotional influence of a people, shared emotional strength, violence in a women's community, influential people's community, unfolding of a people's strength, strong women's community, strong community feeling.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community is specified through strength, influence and concentrated use of leisure.
- psychology: Strong community feeling is a clear ABC example.
- body/health: ABC lists violence in a women's community; this is not a prediction for any group.
- social/relationship: Belonging to a mighty or influential community is central.
- events/manifestations: Public emergence through a community and successful amateur work are Witte's examples.
- conflicts/notes: The scan names shared emotional strength and a people's emotional influence, not power of faith; violence remains a separate example.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Collective strength, emotional influence and the separate violence example must retain their different scopes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Cupido = Vulcanus`, shared feeling gains collective strength and influence: a mighty community can help someone become public, while strong women's communities and a people's emotional influence give belonging social power. Shared emotional strength and fully used leisure connect that power with successful amateur work. Violence within a women's community remains a distinct severe example rather than a necessary feature of strong belonging. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
