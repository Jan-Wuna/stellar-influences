#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Hades`; printed p. `195`; PDF page `96` (complete oriented row checked against actual scan).

Endurance of an old woman, blockage through emotional grief, danger of depression for a woman, depressed woman in need, endangered circulation of a woman, sorrowful mental depression, thorough renunciation by a woman, danger of oppression during the hour.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Severe hardship is depicted as enduring or blocked.
- psychology: ABC names depression and renunciation in women; these remain attributed descriptions.
- body/health: Witte names death from misery and plague; ABC names endangered circulation and depression. These are historical associations, not diagnoses or individual risk estimates.
- social/relationship: The entries concern distressed people and older women rather than a defined partnership.
- events/manifestations: Prolonged or blocked hardship, renunciation and danger of oppression are distinct examples; Witte gives extreme collective mortality.
- conflicts/notes: The scan gives emotional grief, endangered circulation and danger of oppression; it supplies neither the inherited heart wording nor a second final depression-risk clause.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The entries' severe and less severe examples retain their separate scopes and historical source status.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Hades = Admetos`, distress is endured or held in a blocked state: an old woman's endurance, emotional grief and renunciation give hardship a sustained quality. Depression, endangered circulation and danger of oppression are specific severe ABC examples, while Witte supplies the extreme collective image of people dying from misery or plague. Those medical and mortality associations remain historical source claims and do not determine an individual's condition or outcome. ([ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry).)
