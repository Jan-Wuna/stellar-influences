## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Moon + Hades`; one-based PDF page `131` (exact oriented entry checked against actual scan).

Connection with worried, depressed persons. Acquaintance with prostitutes, or with bad women. Worries caused by others, or which one causes others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Hades`; printed p. `194`; PDF page `96` (complete oriented row checked against actual scan).

old woman at the place, other peoples in distress, emotional sorrow with the partner, danger for women in the environment, emotional deficiency of the partner, lacking feeling for the environment, hour of danger for the environment, past of other peoples, and polluted environment of a people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Worry and deprivation enter direct contact with nearby people or a partner.
- psychology: Emotional sorrow with a partner and worry passed between people.
- body/health: Witte's 'depressed persons' is historical description, not a clinical assessment.
- social/relationship: Acquaintance with distressed people and mutual causation of worry are central.
- events/manifestations: Local danger or polluted surroundings are ABC's stated environmental examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's moralized bad-women and prostitution examples remain historical source language, without a character inference about acquaintances.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Worry can pass in either direction; the moralized examples and environmental danger do not establish an individual's conduct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Hades = Asc`, hardship enters encounters with nearby people: worry may be caused by others or caused to them, while grief with a partner and lack of feeling complicate close contact. An old woman at the place, other peoples in need, environmental danger and pollution give the burden specific local and collective settings. Witte's prostitution and bad-women examples remain moralized historical claims rather than conclusions about the people encountered. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
