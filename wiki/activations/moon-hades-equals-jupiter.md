## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Moon + Hades`; one-based PDF page `131` (exact oriented entry checked against actual scan).

Happy widowhood. The merry widow. Food or money worries. Earning money in a base, immoral way. Hour of great wastage.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Hades`; printed p. `194`; PDF page `96` (complete oriented row checked against actual scan).

Financial crime among a people, financial disadvantages of a woman, joy and grief of a people, good fortune through a woman from the past, lack of a sense of right, legal disadvantages for a people, advantage for a people in need, satisfied woman in service.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Material or legal concerns coexist with favorable experience within hardship.
- psychology: Witte's happy widowhood and ABC's joy and sorrow preserve a mixed emotional range.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is supplied; widowhood remains a historical life circumstance.
- social/relationship: A satisfied woman in service and a woman from the past are ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: Food or money worries, financial disadvantage, waste and legal disadvantage are stated possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: The scan restores good fortune through a woman from the past; favorable examples coexist with financial crime, disadvantage and waste.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The entries' severe and less severe examples retain their separate scopes and historical source status.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Hades = Jupiter`, resources and satisfaction appear within a difficult setting: food or money worries, financial or legal disadvantage and waste coexist with advantage for a people in need and satisfaction in service. Good fortune through a woman from the past and happy widowhood preserve favorable experiences alongside collective joy and grief. The source also names morally condemned earnings and financial crime as separate examples, without making either fortune or hardship an exclusive verdict. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
