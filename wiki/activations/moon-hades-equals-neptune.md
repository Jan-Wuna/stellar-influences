## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: one-based PDF page `131`, `MOON + HADES`, Neptune row; checked against the actual source text.

Poor abandoned wife. Widow. Worries for the future, not seeing a way out. To be in low water, without resources.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `195`, PDF page `96`, `Mond + Hades`, Neptune row; all eight clauses checked against the scan.

Source wording: "Durch Not enttäuschte Frau, enttäuschende Vergangenheit eines Volkes, Feingefühl einer alten Frau, fremdes Volk in Not, Mangel einer Frau an Feingefühl, unbekannte Gefahr für die Frau, Unsicherheit durch Gemüts-Kummer, Wasser-Mangel eines Volkes".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A woman disappointed through hardship; a people's disappointing past; an older woman's sensitivity; a foreign people in need; a woman's lack of sensitivity; unknown danger for a woman; uncertainty through emotional grief; a people's shortage of water.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Deprivation is accompanied by uncertainty and a perceived absence of a way forward.
- psychology: Worry about the future, disappointment, and uncertainty through emotional grief; ABC also includes sensitivity and its lack as distinct expressions.
- body/health: ABC's water shortage is collective material deprivation, not a bodily diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Abandonment and widowhood are traditional relational examples; ABC also includes an older woman's sensitivity and foreign people in need.
- events/manifestations: Lacking resources, a people's disappointing past, an unknown danger, and collective water shortage.
- conflicts/notes: Sensitivity remains part of the ABC range alongside its lack. Witte's "low water" is paired with lack of resources; ABC's separate water-shortage clause does not establish that the two expressions have identical scope.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in worry and deprivation, while ABC broadens the range to sensitivity, the past, and collective need. The scan resolves the former "exchanged", "exchangeable", "F" and "feelings Kummer" wording as disappointment, sensitivity, and emotional grief. These were translation errors rather than conflicting doctrine.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Hades = Neptune`, the [pair's hardship and emotional deprivation](../axes/moon-hades.md#derived-synthesis) are experienced through disappointment, uncertainty, and difficulty seeing a way forward. Witte describes future worries and a lack of resources; ABC connects uncertainty with emotional grief and includes unknown danger. The abandoned wife and widow are Witte's specific historical examples. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also preserves a wider range: an older woman's sensitivity, a woman's lack of sensitivity, a people's disappointing past, foreign people in need, and collective water shortage. These clauses keep emotional responsiveness and material circumstances visible alongside the difficult psychological themes, without claiming that all these expressions must occur together. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
