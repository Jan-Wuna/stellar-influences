## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Moon + Hades`; one-based PDF page `131` (entry starts here; supplied parser locator, not visually verified).

Prospect of becoming a widow. Connections which cause worries. Connection to old women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Hades`; printed p. `194`; PDF page `96` (entry-level locator in supplied parser-normalized German OCR; not visually verified).

relationships to the past of a people, emotional deficiency in connections, emotional connection from the past, emotional sorrow in a connection, contact with an old woman, contact with women in distress, and renunciation of the bond to women

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Worry and deprivation are experienced in connections.
- psychology: Emotional sorrow or lack within a bond is ABC's emphasis.
- body/health: Witte's prospect of widowhood is a historical example, not an individual mortality estimate.
- social/relationship: Contact with older women, women in distress and bonds from the past.
- events/manifestations: Connections causing worry and relinquishing a bond to women are stated possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: The widowhood example does not make all difficult connections portents of bereavement.

## Contradictions

Witte gives widowhood as well as worrying connections; ABC extends the range to emotional lack, women in distress and past ties. These are different examples of burdensome bonds, with no direct disagreement established by the entries.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Hades = Node`, Node specifies hardship through the bonds one has or forms. Witte gives connections that cause worry and contact with older women; ABC adds emotional sorrow in a connection, women in distress and ties from the past. Witte's prospect of widowhood remains one attributed historical example, rather than the necessary outcome of a burdensome bond. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
