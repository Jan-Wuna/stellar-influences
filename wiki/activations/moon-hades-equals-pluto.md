## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Moon + Hades`; one-based PDF page `131` (exact oriented entry checked against the actual raw scan).

Sterility of the wife. Paralyzed by fear. Failing people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Hades`; printed p. `195`; one-based PDF page `96` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Developments from the past of a people, dangerous development for a woman, developing a lack of feeling, criminal developments within a people, a woman's renunciation of change, growing need of a people, change of a serving woman.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Developments within hardship may worsen need or evoke fear.
- psychology: Witte explicitly describes being paralyzed by fear.
- body/health: Witte's sterility claim is historical and must not be read as a fertility diagnosis.
- social/relationship: ABC refers to changes affecting women or a people; a specific partnership pattern is not established.
- events/manifestations: Growing collective need and developments rooted in the past are clear ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's P is an alternate Pluto glyph, not prose. ABC's Gefühls-Mangel means a lack of feeling; crime and historical sterility remain separate attributed examples.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes fear and failing people; ABC adds a growing lack of feeling, need and developments rooted in a people's past. These are distinct expressions of hardship, without a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Hades = Pluto`, Pluto specifies hardship through difficult developments and intensified need. ABC's clearer phrases concern developments from a people's past and growing distress; Witte adds paralysis by fear and people failing. The developing lack of feeling adds an emotional form of deprivation; Witte's sterility statement remains an attributed historical medical claim. ([ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry))
