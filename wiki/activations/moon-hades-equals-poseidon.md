## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Moon + Hades`; one-based PDF page `131` (exact oriented entry checked against the actual raw scan).

Sad disposition. Sad mental condition. Stupidity and superstition among the people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Hades`; printed p. `195`; one-based PDF page `96` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Women from a past culture, mental danger for the woman, mental distress of a people, clarity of an old woman, a people of a past culture, disadvantages for spirit and temperament, hidden insights of a woman, a woman's reason through renunciation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Hardship is expressed in mental disposition, culture and the relation to the past.
- psychology: Witte describes sadness; ABC includes hidden insights and an older woman's clarity.
- body/health: Witte's 'sad mental condition' is historical language, not a psychiatric diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Women from an earlier culture and a people's cultural past are ABC settings.
- events/manifestations: Sad disposition and collective superstition are Witte's stated examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's Geist und Gemüt distinguishes spirit and temperament, and Vernunft means reason; Witte's derogatory judgments and sad mental condition remain historical attribution.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sadness and superstition, while ABC also allows clarity, hidden insight and reason through renunciation. That is a material difference in range; Witte's derogatory language remains attributed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Hades = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies hardship through mental and cultural experience. Witte emphasizes sad disposition and collective superstition; ABC also preserves clarity in an older woman, hidden insight and links with past cultures. Those latter examples keep the range wider than unrelieved mental decline, while its reason-through-renunciation phrase remains a source association and Witte's derogatory labels are not adopted as conclusions. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
