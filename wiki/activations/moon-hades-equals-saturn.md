## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Moon + Hades`; one-based PDF page `131` (exact oriented entry checked against the actual raw scan).

Hostility. Worries through enmities. Separation. Loss. Disappearance of vile things. Operative cut in the womb.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Hades`; printed p. `195`; one-based PDF page `96` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Tasks from the past of a people, a woman burdened by the past, a woman maturing through sorrow, patience and renunciation of a woman, emotional sorrow in old age, hardness of an old woman, inhibitions through a lack of feeling, sorrow and pain of a woman.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Hardship takes the form of loss, separation and burdens carried from the past.
- psychology: Hostility, grief, patience and emotional inhibition are stated themes.
- body/health: Witte's operation on the womb is a historical medical example, not a recommendation or prediction.
- social/relationship: Enmities and women's experiences of grief are traditional relational settings.
- events/manifestations: Separation, loss and disappearance of harmful things are Witte's distinct examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC explicitly names sorrow in old age, hardness and inhibition through a lack of feeling; gendered characterizations and Witte's surgical example remain attributed historical claims.

## Contradictions

Witte's loss and separation coexist with the disappearance of unwanted things; ABC emphasizes sorrow, patience and emotional inhibition. These are distinct outcomes and responses, not a single forecast.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Hades = Saturn`, Saturn specifies hardship through separation, loss and burdens that endure from the past. Witte gives worry through enmity as well as the disappearance of harmful things; ABC adds patience, renunciation and grief. The range includes both enduring loss and something unwanted ending, while Witte's surgical example remains an attributed historical claim. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
