## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Hardship enters personal and everyday life.
- psychology: Daily recurring worry is Witte's direct psychological statement.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Service and deprivation between women and men are ABC's traditional examples.
- events/manifestations: Repeated daily concerns and personal renunciation are stated possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's 'people of a people' is awkward but not used as a character inference; gendered service remains historically attributed.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes daily worries; ABC gives mutual service, deprivation and emotional sorrow between women and men. These differ in emphasis without establishing a direct disagreement. The gendered examples remain historically attributed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Hades = Sun`, Sun specifies hardship in personal and everyday circumstances. Witte gives a poor wife and worries that repeat daily; ABC widens the relationship picture to service in both directions, deprivation between women and men and emotional sorrow. The supported theme is personally lived concern, without assigning service or suffering as a universal gender role. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
