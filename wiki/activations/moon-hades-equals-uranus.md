## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: one-based PDF page `131`, `MOON + HADES`, Uranus row; checked against the actual source text.

Becoming a widow through accident. Sudden worries. To be impoverished suddenly.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `195`, PDF page `96`, `Mond + Hades`, Uranus row; all seven clauses checked against the scan.

Source wording: "Erregung einer bekümmerten Frau, plötzlicher Gemüts-Kummer, gefährliche Verluste für die Frau, Probleme im Volk durch die Vergangenheit, Reformen für ein Volk in Not, Spannungen durch Gefühls-Mangel, Stunde einer plötzlichen Gefahr".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Agitation of a worried woman; sudden emotional grief; dangerous losses for a woman; problems among a people because of the past; reforms for a people in need; tensions arising from a lack of feeling; an hour of sudden danger.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden distress, worry, or loss within the pair's emotional and collective hardship.
- psychology: Agitation, sudden grief, and tensions arising from a lack of feeling.
- body/health: Witte gives widowhood through accident as a severe historical example. ABC's actual row names emotional grief, not a heart attack.
- social/relationship: A worried woman and dangerous losses affecting women; tension through a lack of feeling also has an interpersonal expression.
- events/manifestations: Sudden impoverishment, problems connected with a people's past, reforms for people in need, and an hour of sudden danger.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's reform clause adds a collective response to need; it does not establish that every sudden loss produces a beneficial reform.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize distress, with sudden worry, grief, loss, or danger. ABC also includes a people's past and reforms for people in need. The scan resolves the former translation errors: "heart attack" was sudden emotional grief, "remorning" was reforms, and "shortage of fish" was a lack of feeling. These were wording errors rather than competing source claims.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Hades = Uranus`, the [pair's emotional or collective hardship](../axes/moon-hades.md#derived-synthesis) takes a sudden, agitated, or tense form. Witte names abrupt worry and impoverishment; ABC adds sudden emotional grief, a worried woman's agitation, and tensions arising from a lack of feeling. These passages connect the abrupt event with the distressed emotional experience. Witte's widowhood through accident remains a severe historical example. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also extends the range to problems connected with a people's past and reforms for people in need. This collective application sits beside the personal examples without establishing that reforms necessarily follow a loss or relieve distress. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
