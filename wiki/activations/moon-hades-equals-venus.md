## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Moon + Hades`; one-based PDF page `131` (exact oriented entry checked against the actual raw scan).

A loving poor woman. Street-walker. Venal love. Worries caused by love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mond + Hades`; printed p. `194`; one-based PDF page `96` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Dangerous love of a woman, lack of harmony with the woman, sorrow through a lack of feeling, love for a woman from the past, love sorrow of a woman, love service of the woman, a woman's renunciation of love, preference for the past of a people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affection is accompanied by worry, lack of harmony or renunciation.
- psychology: Love-related worry and difficulty finding harmony are explicit themes.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: A loving poor woman, love for a woman from the past and giving up love are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Trouble caused by love and commercialized love are Witte's traditional manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: ABC distinguishes sorrow through a lack of feeling from a woman's love sorrow; Witte's commercial-sex labels remain historical source judgments.

## Contradictions

Witte's worry caused by love overlaps with ABC's lack of harmony, sorrow through emotional lack and renunciation. Commercial sex and love for someone from the past are separate examples, not equivalent relationships.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Hades = Venus`, Venus specifies hardship through love and the difficulty of finding harmony. Witte describes a loving poor woman and worries caused by love; ABC adds a relationship with someone from the past, emotional lack and renouncing love. Witte's commercial-sex examples stay historically attributed rather than becoming a judgment about the person or relationship. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
