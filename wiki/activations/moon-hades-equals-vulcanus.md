#### ABC Entry

energy of an old woman, secret power of a woman, tremendous distress of a people, criminal violence against women, powerful sorrow of a woman, deficient emotional influence, hidden power of the woman, and hidden emotional influence

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Hardship is intensified through force, powerful sorrow or concealed influence.
- psychology: Powerful grief is an ABC example; no separate diagnosis is established.
- body/health: Violence against women is an attributed ABC scenario, not an individual risk estimate.
- social/relationship: Criminal gangs and people endangered by them are Witte's examples.
- events/manifestations: Severe public conditions, tremendous collective distress and hidden power.
- conflicts/notes: Older women's energy and criminal violence are distinct examples; the entries do not equate strength with wrongdoing.

## Contradictions

Witte concentrates on dangerous public conditions and gangs; ABC includes powerful sorrow, concealed influence and an older woman's energy. That wider range does not equate strength or hidden influence with criminality.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Hades = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies hardship through force and concentrated distress. Witte describes deplorable public conditions and danger from criminal gangs; ABC adds tremendous collective need, powerful sorrow and hidden influence. Its example of an older woman's energy remains a separate possibility, so strength itself is not equated with violence or criminality. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry))
