#### Witte Entry

Restful, happy hours in a small circle and through being left alone. For women it may mean end of good fortune.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blocked rights of a people, depressions of a successful woman, consolidation of women's rights, financially restricted woman, happiness of a woman through perseverance, great glilk's oath, hour of glulk's intensification, and suppression of the rights of a people

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Jupiter = Admetos`: Restful, happy hours in a small circle and through being left alone. For women it may mean end of good fortune.
- Practical focus: narrows `Moon/Jupiter` into quiet containment, reduced social scale, or happiness found in withdrawal. It can show contentment in a small protected circle, but it can also mark fortune that contracts, especially when emotional abundance gives way to isolation, limitation, or the end of an easier period.
- Practical expression: blocked rights of a people, depressions of a successful woman, consolidation of women's rights, financially restricted woman, and happiness of a woman through perseverance.
