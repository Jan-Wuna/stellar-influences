## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Admetos row.

Restful, happy hours in a small circle and through being left alone. For women it may mean end of good fortune.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `183`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Admetos row.

Source wording: "Blockierte Rechte eines Volkes, Depressionen einer erfolgreichen Frau, Festigung des Frauen-Rechts, finanziell eingeschränkte Frau, Glück einer Frau durch Ausdauer, gründliches Glücks-Gefühl, Stunde der glücklichen Vertiefung, Unterdrückung der Rechte eines Volkes".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Blocked rights of a people; depressions of a successful woman; strengthening women's rights; a financially restricted woman; a woman's happiness through perseverance; a thorough feeling of happiness; an hour of happy deepening; suppression of a people's rights.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Quiet or deepened happiness alongside limits on fortune, finances, and rights.
- psychology: Restfulness, happiness through perseverance, and deepened happy feeling occur alongside ABC's attributed depression wording.
- body/health: ABC names "depressions" without establishing a clinical condition; no specific bodily correspondence is supplied.
- social/relationship: Happy hours in a small circle or alone; women's rights may be strengthened, while a people's rights may be blocked or suppressed.
- events/manifestations: Financial restriction and a possible end of good fortune coexist with contentment in a smaller setting.
- conflicts/notes: Privacy and persistence need not mean loss; restriction need not produce contentment. The source's different possibilities remain separate.

## Contradictions

Witte explicitly allows both restful happiness and an end of good fortune, the latter framed historically "for women." ABC likewise includes deepened happiness and perseverance alongside restrictions, depression, and suppressed rights. These are contrasting expressions within the entries, not a reason to turn all solitude into deprivation or all limits into a benefit. The damaged "glilk's oath" wording was replaced from the complete ABC row.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Admetos`, the happiness and contentment of the [Moon/Jupiter axis](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) find a quieter, more contained setting: restful hours with a small circle or through being left alone. Happiness through perseverance and a thorough or deepened happy feeling extend this theme beyond withdrawal into persistence. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Restriction is also a distinct possibility. Financial limits, blocked or suppressed rights, and Witte's possible end of good fortune prevent a uniformly reassuring reading. ABC separately includes strengthening women's rights, so limitation and consolidation must remain distinguishable. The material spans private contentment and collective or legal conditions without making one cause the other; its historical gender and depression language supplies neither a universal outcome nor a diagnosis. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
