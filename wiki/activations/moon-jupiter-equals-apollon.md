## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Apollon row.

The happy hours for many. Many happy women. Spreading of good fortune.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `183`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Apollon row.

Source wording: "Ausdehnung eines glücklichen Volkes, Frau mit viel Geld, Geld-Handel im Volk, Handels-Rechte eines Volkes, Rechts-Erfolg einer Frau, Rechts-Wissen einer Frau, Staats-Finanzen eines Volkes, Wirtschafts-Geld der Frau, zunehmendes Glücks-Gefühl".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Expansion of a happy people; a woman with much money; financial trading among the people; a people's trading rights; a woman's legal success; a woman's legal knowledge; a people's state finances; a woman's money in economic affairs; an increasing feeling of happiness. The compound "Wirtschafts-Geld" is retained above rather than treated as a separately defined financial instrument.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Happiness and good fortune extending to many, with a distinct financial, commercial, and legal range.
- psychology: ABC names an increasing feeling of happiness; the entries give no detailed personality account.
- body/health: No bodily condition or health claim is specified.
- social/relationship: Shared happy hours and many happy women are explicit; private financial resources and collective rights are additional settings.
- events/manifestations: Spreading good fortune, financial trading, state finances, trading rights, and legal success or knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: Shared happiness and financial or legal circumstances are distinct applications. No negative profile is supplied by these two brief entries.

## Contradictions

No direct disagreement appears in these two passages. Witte emphasizes happy hours for many; ABC adds commercial, financial, and legal applications. Those additions broaden the scope without establishing that every happy group is wealthy. The OCR fragment "vie!" reads "viel" on the scan, and the old "money trade in people" translation has been corrected.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Apollon`, the happiness and fortunate circumstances of the [Moon/Jupiter axis](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) extend to many people. Shared happy hours, many happy women, and the spreading of good fortune give this orientation its breadth; ABC also names an increasing feeling of happiness. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

That breadth has a practical economic and legal range as well. Personal money, a people's state finances, financial trading, and trading rights extend the orientation from felt happiness to public economic affairs. Legal success and knowledge remain explicit applications alongside shared joy; the favorable vocabulary does not guarantee wealth. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
