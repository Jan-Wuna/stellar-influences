## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Ascendant row.

Acquaintance with cheerful and serene women. Happy and cheerful surroundings. Success of a female person in the immediate vicinity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `182`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Ascendant row.

Source wording: "Erfolgreiche Frau als Partner, glückliche Umwelt eines Volkes, glückliche Stunde mit dem Partner, Glücks-Gefühl an einem Ort, Glücks-Gefühl des Partners, glückliche Frau als Partner, Mutter-Glück am Ort, Rechts-Gefühl des anderen, Stunde des Glücks für den Partner".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A successful woman as partner; happy surroundings of a people; a happy hour with the partner; a feeling of happiness at a place; the partner's feeling of happiness; a happy woman as partner; maternal happiness at the place; the other's sense of justice; an hour of happiness for the partner.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source reference: printed page `124`, PDF page `126`, entry `0372`.

Cheerful, confident and happy in the company of other people, the creation of a harmonious environment. Fortunate contacts.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source reference: PDF page `30`, Ascendant under `Planets activating the Moon/Jupiter midpoint`; the chapter begins on PDF page `29`.

One’s personality displays a wealth and abundance of feeling. One plays the role of a nurturer who encourages, uplifts, and offers approval. The potential of feel ing overwhelmed in a relationship.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `116`, PDF page `117`, `ASC` row.

#### Munkasey Entry

Inconsistent ideas about the role that others play in helping you in life; old memories of experiences during travels which you find pleasant; an added need to study the ideas of others and how these can be further developed.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Abundant feeling expressed in company, encouraging contacts, and a happy immediate environment.
- psychology: Cheerfulness and confidence, encouragement and approval, but also possible overwhelm and inconsistent ideas about others' help.
- body/health: ABC's maternal-happiness example supplies no medical condition or health finding.
- social/relationship: Nurturing, fortunate contacts, happy partners, and attention to the role others play in one's life.
- events/manifestations: Creating a harmonious environment, pleasant travel memories, and studying or developing others' ideas.
- conflicts/notes: Offering encouragement and receiving help are different sides of the relationship. Feeling overwhelmed remains possible alongside happy company.

## Contradictions

The cheerful surroundings and contacts in Witte and Ebertin overlap with Sandbach's encouraging personality, while Sandbach also allows relationship overwhelm. Munkasey's inconsistent ideas about others' help qualify a uniformly reassuring account without denying the possibility of pleasant contact. ABC's gendered partnership and maternal examples remain attributed to the source. Its missing happiness-at-a-place clause and damaged happiness/justice words have been restored from the scan.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Asc`, Asc (Ascendant) brings the [pair's abundant feeling and contentment](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) into immediate company and surroundings. Cheerful acquaintances, confidence with other people, and creating a harmonious environment connect the felt happiness with a social setting. Sandbach describes a personality that nurtures, encourages, uplifts, and offers approval. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The entries also raise questions about others' help and ideas. Munkasey includes pleasant travel memories, studying others' ideas, and inconsistent views of their role in helping; Sandbach allows feeling overwhelmed in a relationship. Encouraging company is therefore part of a wider range that includes uncertainty about support and difficulty managing abundant feeling in a relationship. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
