## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source reference: PDF page `30`, Chiron under `Planets activating the Moon/Jupiter midpoint`; the chapter begins on PDF page `29`.

Your energy helps others to get more fully in touch with their feelings and to accept them. You innately encourage others to feel greater security and faith in the workings of the universe.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Encouragement that helps others recognize and accept feelings, with greater security and trust.
- psychology: Contact with one's feelings and acceptance of them are the stated concerns.
- body/health: No bodily condition, clinical treatment, or physical healing is described.
- social/relationship: Helping others feel more secure and encouraging their emotional acceptance are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Reassuring exchanges are described; no specific external event is predicted.
- conflicts/notes: This is one short Sandbach entry. Its affirmative description supplies no independently supported negative profile or comparison with another source.

## Contradictions

No source comparison is possible from this single passage. Its language of emotional encouragement and faith does not establish a therapeutic method, a clinical benefit, or a general healing meaning for every Chiron formula.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Chiron`, the emotional richness and willingness to share feelings of the [Moon/Jupiter axis](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) take the form of encouragement: helping others become more fully aware of their feelings and accept them. Sandbach connects that acceptance with greater security and faith in the workings of the universe. These phrases describe reassurance through emotional contact; they do not identify a clinical treatment or bodily healing process. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 30)
