## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Kronos row.

To be in good mood through ones own ability. Gain through lottery by the wife. A woman having become well-to-do through the state. The populace made happy by the government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `183`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Kronos row.

Source wording: "Besondere Vorteile für die Frau, Erfolg einer Frau als Chef, erfolgreiche Autorität der Frau, finanziell selbständige Frau, großes Glücks-Gefühl, Selbstbewußtsein einer glücklichen Mutter, staatliches Frauen-Recht, wichtige Rechte der Frau".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Special advantages for the woman; a woman's success as boss; a woman's successful authority; a financially independent woman; a great feeling of happiness; a happy mother's self-confidence; state women's rights; important rights of women.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Happiness and advantage associated with ability, authority, financial independence, or the state.
- psychology: Good mood through one's own ability, a great feeling of happiness, and a happy mother's self-confidence are explicit.
- body/health: The maternal example supplies no medical condition or health finding.
- social/relationship: A successful woman as boss, female authority, financial independence, and women's rights are historical source examples.
- events/manifestations: Witte names a wife's lottery gain, wealth through the state, and a populace made happy by government; ABC adds important rights and advantages.
- conflicts/notes: Personal ability and independence remain distinct from fortune through state institutions. The claims do not establish universal government support or a lottery prediction.

## Contradictions

The passages overlap in advantage, happiness, and authority, while Witte's own-ability meaning prevents a reading confined to institutional assistance. ABC reads "Autorität" (authority), not authorship, and "finanziell selbständige" (financially independent), not necessarily self-employed. Those repairs materially change the emphasis. Witte's "ones" is retained as printed.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Kronos`, the [pair's happiness and fortunate circumstances](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) take a form associated with ability, standing, and authority. Good mood through one's own ability, a woman's success as boss, financial independence, and self-confidence connect satisfaction with competence and autonomy. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The public and state sphere is another explicit setting: Witte names wealth through the state and a populace made happy by government, while ABC includes state women's rights and important rights for women. Witte's wife's lottery gain is a separate named example of advantage. These are attributed possibilities, not a promise of official protection, state assistance, or a financial result. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
