## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Deeds which are caused by happiness. Serene and happy hours while working. The betrothed woman. To feel approaching motherhood.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Actions of a satisfied people, work right for the woman, happy actions of a woman, successful work hours, success actions of a woman, financial work of a woman, happy love activity, legal actions of a woman, and activity in women rights

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A healthy love relationship, love of enterprise, striving for success. The happy wife, cheerful mother, success of a woman.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

In expressing anger one may be excessively emotional. Asserting oneself with a wealth of feeling. The person is at their best when they act at a time that feels right to do so. Taking the initiative to reach out to others on a feeling level.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

An added element of rashness and temper; increased dimensions for your opinions; further harshness to your religious or philosophical ideas; increased energy and activity for projects done together as or for the family.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Jupiter = Mars`: brave caring, productive optimism, and the ability to act decisively when encouragement or protection is needed. Main caution: overreaction: expansive feeling can become pushy, wasteful, or combative when urgency outruns judgment.
