## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Mars row.

Deeds which are caused by happiness. Serene and happy hours while working. The betrothed woman. To feel approaching motherhood.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `182`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Mars row.

Source wording: "Aktionen eines zufriedenen Volkes, Arbeits-Recht für die Frau, beglückende Gefühls-Handlungen, erfolgreiche Arbeits-Stunde, Erfolgs-Handlungen einer Frau, Finanz-Arbeit einer Frau, glückliche Liebes-Tätigkeit, Rechts-Handlungen einer Frau, Tätigkeit im Frauen-Recht".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Actions of a contented people; labor rights for women; actions that bring emotional happiness; a successful work hour; a woman's successful actions; a woman's financial work; happy loving activity; a woman's legal actions; activity in women's rights.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source reference: printed page `123`, PDF page `125`, entry `0366`.

A healthy love relationship, love of enterprise, striving for success. The happy wife, cheerful mother, success of a woman.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source reference: PDF page `30`, Mars under `Planets activating the Moon/Jupiter midpoint`; the chapter begins on PDF page `29`.

In expressing anger one may be excessively emotional. Asserting oneself with a wealth of feeling. The person is at their best when they act at a time that feels right to do so. Taking the initiative to reach out to others on a feeling level.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `116`, PDF page `117`, Mars row.

#### Munkasey Entry

An added element of rashness and temper; increased dimensions for your opinions; further harshness to your religious or philosophical ideas; increased energy and activity for projects done together as or for the family.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Abundant feeling expressed in initiative, work, and striving for success, with rashness and temper also represented.
- psychology: Enterprise and emotional assertiveness may coexist with excessive emotion in anger or harsher religious and philosophical opinions.
- body/health: Approaching motherhood and a cheerful mother are attributed historical examples. Ebertin's "healthy love relationship" does not establish a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Emotional outreach, loving activity, and energy for projects together as or for the family.
- events/manifestations: Happy work, successful actions, financial work, labor or women's rights activity, and family projects.
- conflicts/notes: The favorable work and relationship meanings coexist with Sandbach's anger and Munkasey's rashness, temper, and harsh opinions; neither side should erase the other.

## Contradictions

Witte, ABC, and Ebertin emphasize happy or successful activity, while Sandbach and Munkasey retain emotional excess, rashness, and temper. This is a supported range of action, not a direct denial of happy work or loving relationships. ABC's "beglückende Gefühls-Handlungen" refers to emotionally happy actions, not specifically a woman's actions; its separate women's work and rights clauses remain intact.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Mars`, the [Moon/Jupiter wealth of feeling and contentment](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) becomes active: deeds prompted by happiness, enjoyment of work, enterprise, and striving for success. Reaching out emotionally and working together as or for the family connect initiative with shared feeling. ABC's labor, financial-work, and women's-rights clauses give this activity a legal and economic range as well. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The same intensity of expression can include excessive emotion in anger, rashness, temper, and harsher religious or philosophical opinions. Those tensions qualify the favorable activity without making every initiative confrontational. The precise issue is how strongly felt conviction enters action and contact with others; the passages support both productive engagement and an emotionally excessive expression. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))
