## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Midheaven row.

Ones own contentment. Many happy hours in the life-time. Being made happy by women. To be pregnant. Personal success in the public. Emotions in the hour of success.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `182`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Midheaven row.

Source wording: "Finanzen meines Volkes, Geld meiner Frau, Glück meiner Mutter, ich als Frau bin glücklich, ich fühle mich glücklich, mein Rechts-Gefühl, meine Frau ist erfolgreich, meine Frau hat Geld, meine Frau hat Recht, meine Glücks-Stunde, Rechte meines Volkes".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: My people's finances; my wife's money; my mother's happiness; as a woman I am happy; I feel happy; my sense of justice; my wife is successful; my wife has money; my wife is right; my happy hour; my people's rights.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source reference: printed page `124`, PDF page `126`, entry `0373`.

A kind-hearted, social-minded or religious person. Popularity, confidence, the joy of expectation. The happy wife, fiancee, bride, mother.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source reference: PDF pages `30–31`, Midheaven under `Planets activating the Moon/Jupiter midpoint`; the chapter begins on PDF page `29`.

One’s work involves helping other people to come together harmoniously and to share their feelings with each other. A career that involves the successful nurtur ing of people.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `116`, PDF page `117`, `MC` row.

#### Munkasey Entry

Family or family activities move into a more central focus as you grow and mature in life; recognition from others for your ideas and work in expanding knowledge in areas such as philosophy, foreign affairs, etc.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal contentment and confidence, with nurturing work, public success, and family life as distinct expressions.
- psychology: Kind-heartedness, social or religious interests, confidence, and the joy of expectation are explicit.
- body/health: Witte names pregnancy; this remains an attributed astrological example, not a medical finding or personal prediction.
- social/relationship: Family activities, helping people share feelings, and the source's historical wife, bride, and mother examples.
- events/manifestations: Personal public success, nurturing work, and recognition for expanding knowledge in philosophy or foreign affairs; ABC adds personal and collective finances and rights.
- conflicts/notes: The entries include own contentment and family concerns as well as vocation. They supply no independent negative profile of approval dependence or inflated public feeling.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis rather than directly disagreeing: personal contentment, public recognition, nurturing work, and family focus need not refer to the same situation. The historical gender and pregnancy examples remain attributed. ABC's eleven first-person clauses are now separated correctly; "ich fühle mich glücklich" means feeling happy, and "mein Rechts-Gefühl" is one's sense of justice, not a right over one's wife.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = MC`, MC (Midheaven) specifies the [pair's happiness and abundance of feeling](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) through personal satisfaction and a socially meaningful direction. Own contentment, confidence, kind-heartedness, and joyful expectation connect inner well-being with personal success in public life. ABC's first-person examples also include family happiness, money, a sense of justice, and the rights of one's people, so the orientation reaches beyond vocation alone. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Work may involve nurturing people and helping them come together and share feelings. Munkasey adds a more central family focus and recognition for ideas or work that expands knowledge, including philosophy and foreign affairs. These give the emotional and social breadth a concrete role in how one works and relates to others, without requiring that public success, family happiness, and every historical example occur together. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))
