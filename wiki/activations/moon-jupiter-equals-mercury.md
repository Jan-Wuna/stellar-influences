## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Mercury row.

To think of happy hours. Happy hours with cheerful conversations. Thoughts of a happy woman. News that make a people happy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `182`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Mercury row.

Source wording: "Gespräche über Frauen-Recht, gute Nachrichten für das Volk, Bericht einer Finanz-Frau, Freude einer jungen Frau, Glück einer jungen Mutter, Jugend-Recht eines Volkes, Recht-Sprechung für die Frau, Rechts-Gefühl der Jugend, Stunde des Verkehrs-Rechtes".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Conversations about women's rights; good news for the people; a report from a woman in finance; a young woman's joy; a young mother's happiness; a people's youth law; administration of justice for women; young people's sense of justice; a time of traffic law.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source reference: printed page `123`, PDF page `125`, entry `0364`.

Far-reaching plans, a large sphere of thinking, consciousness of objective, urge to learn and study. Union with a wealthy or happy woman. Successes through speaking or writing.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source reference: PDF page `30`, Mercury under `Planets activating the Moon/Jupiter midpoint`; the chapter begins on PDF page `29`.

One communicates with a wealth of feeling and has an abundance of insights and perceptions. One things about many things and has a great number of ideas. The ability to speak in an enthusiastically emotional manner.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `116`, PDF page `117`, `Mer` row.

#### Munkasey Entry

Added needs for further study and education; publishing opinions and thus increase your influence among people; a lot of verbiage in your writing and speeches, especially when expounding; ventings of great emotional impact.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Broad thought, learning, and communication carrying abundant feeling, from cheerful conversation to public or legal news.
- psychology: Many ideas and perceptions, far-reaching plans, a sense of objective, and enthusiastic emotional speech.
- body/health: No medical condition or bodily correspondence is specified.
- social/relationship: Cheerful conversations and influential opinions; Ebertin separately names a union with a wealthy or happy woman.
- events/manifestations: Study, publication, success through speaking or writing, emotionally forceful expression, and ABC's financial reports or legal concerns.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's verbiage and emotional venting qualify an exclusively agreeable communication profile. ABC distinguishes judicial activity, legal rights, and a sense of justice.

## Contradictions

Cheerful speech and successful study coexist with Munkasey's wordiness and emotionally forceful venting; these are differences within the range, not a denial of happy conversation. Sandbach's "One things about many things" is actually printed on PDF page `30` and is retained as written. The synthesis relies on his clear abundance-of-ideas and emotional-speech clauses. ABC's "Recht-Sprechung" concerns administration of justice, not merely "right talk."

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Mercury`, the [pair's richness of feeling and social reach](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) enter thought and communication. Many ideas, broad plans, study, enthusiastic speech, cheerful conversation, and good news connect emotional abundance with a wide field of interests and expression. Success through speaking or writing and the wish to publish opinions give that expression a public reach. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))

The range includes more than agreeable conversation. Munkasey names verbiage and emotional venting, while ABC gives communication a financial and legal setting through reports, women's rights, youth law, judicial activity, and traffic law. The common thread is thought and speech enlarged by feeling and interest; its expression may be cheerful, learned, or emotionally forceful. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
