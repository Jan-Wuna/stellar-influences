## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To think of happy hours. Happy hours with cheerful conversations. Thoughts of a happy woman. News that make a people happy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Talk about women's right, good news filr the people, report of a financial woman, joy of a young woman, happiness of a young mother, youth law of a people, right talk filr the woman, right feeling of youth, and hour of traffic right

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Far-reaching plans, a large sphere of thinking, consciousness of objective, urge to learn and study. Union with a wealthy or happy woman. Successes through speaking or writing.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One communicates with a wealth of feeling and has an abundance of insights and perceptions. One things about many things and has a great number of ideas. The ability to speak in an enthusiastically emotional manner.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Added needs for further study and education; publishing opinions and thus increase your influence among people; a lot of verbiage in your writing and speeches, especially when expounding; ventings of great emotional impact.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Jupiter = Mercury`: supportive communication, moral or educational feeling, and a mind that links emotion to meaning naturally. Main caution: overstatement: emotional confidence can turn into exaggeration, preaching, or loose proportion in judgment.
