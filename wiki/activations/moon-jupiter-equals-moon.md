### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/JUPITER With Itself`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased needs for contacts with people as well as needs for understanding their motivations and inspirations; a feeling for the psychological side of life, and a need to study and understand the role of people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Contact with people accompanied by a wish to understand their motivations and inspirations.
- psychology: Sensitivity to life's psychological side and an interest in studying people's roles.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is described.
- social/relationship: An increased need for contact and for understanding the people one meets.
- events/manifestations: Studying and trying to understand people; no specific external event is predicted.

## Derived Synthesis

`Moon/Jupiter = Moon` repeats Moon within the [Moon/Jupiter axis](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis). Its represented "With Itself" entry joins a need for contact with a wish to understand what moves people: their motivations, inspirations, and roles in life. Psychological curiosity accompanies social contact, so the emphasis is both meeting people and trying to understand them. This is a stated interest and need, without an assurance of accurate insight into every person. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 118 / PDF p. 119)
