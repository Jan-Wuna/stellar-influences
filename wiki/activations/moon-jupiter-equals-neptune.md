## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Neptune row.

The departing happiness of a woman. Girl suffering through lover’s unfaithfulness. Happiness diminished through falsehood or error. Prodigality.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `183`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Neptune row; the following Pluto row is separate.

Source wording: "Auflösung der Rechte eines Volkes, Erfolge eines Volkes am/im Wasser, glückliche Frau im Wasser, Glücks-Gefühl in der Luft, Glücks-Erwartungen eines Volkes, Rechts-Unsicherheit der Frau, Stunde der Freude im Wasser, zukünftiges Glück der Frau".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Dissolution of a people's rights; a people's successes by or in water; a happy woman in water; a feeling of happiness in the air; a people's expectations of happiness; a woman's legal uncertainty; an hour of joy in water; a woman's future happiness.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source reference: printed page `123`, PDF page `125`, entry `0369`.

A faint notion of happiness, tendency to abandon hope easily, bad social conditions. Dwindling happiness, losses and disadvantages through speculation or wastefulness.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source reference: PDF page `30`, Neptune under `Planets activating the Moon/Jupiter midpoint`; the chapter begins on PDF page `29`.

To become psychically drained by too many incoming impressions. Idealism expressed with a wealth of feeling. The possibility of enjoying being confused. Being pulled in many different ways causes confusion.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `116`, PDF page `117`, `Nep` row.

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased needs for drugs to escape from reality and the pressures of life; added confusion about your plans for your future; your plans for the family may be unclear; you may deceive or delude yourself about emotional states.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertain, diminished, or dispersed happiness, with idealism, confusion, and expectations of happiness also represented.
- psychology: Easily abandoned hope, too many impressions, emotional confusion, and self-deception; Sandbach also names idealism with abundant feeling.
- body/health: Munkasey's drug-escape claim and Sandbach's psychic-drain language remain attributed astrological interpretations, not diagnoses or established medical causes.
- social/relationship: Witte's unfaithful-lover example and Munkasey's unclear family plans; ABC adds legal uncertainty affecting a woman.
- events/manifestations: Losses through falsehood, speculation, or wastefulness; ABC separately includes dissolution of rights and happy or successful water settings.
- conflicts/notes: The adverse meanings do not exhaust the ABC range, which includes happiness in water, happiness in the air, and future happiness. These settings are not explained as causes of emotional confusion.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize diminished happiness and loss; Sandbach includes idealism as well as confusion, and ABC retains both legal uncertainty and happy water or air settings. These differences broaden the range without proving mutually exclusive doctrines. The complete eight-clause ABC Neptune row replaces a damaged translation; the development clauses absorbed by the parser belong to the following [Pluto orientation](moon-jupiter-equals-pluto.md#abc-entry).

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Neptune`, the [pair's happiness, hope, and abundance of feeling](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) take an uncertain or dispersed form. Happiness may be faint, diminish through falsehood or error, or give way to easily abandoned hope. Sandbach describes abundant feeling and idealism alongside too many impressions and confusion; Munkasey adds uncertainty about future or family plans and about one's own emotional state. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))

The difficult range includes a lover's unfaithfulness, wastefulness, and losses through speculation. ABC also retains water and air references, legal uncertainty or dissolution of rights, and expectations of future happiness. Those applications extend the scope beyond psychological disappointment without establishing a single mechanism that connects them all. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
