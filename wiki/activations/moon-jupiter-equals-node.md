## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Node row.

To be joyful and cheerful with several persons. Connection with cheerful and joyous women. To become pregnant.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `182`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Node row.

Source wording: "Glück in den Beziehungen zu Frauen, Beziehungen zum Frauen-Recht, glückliche Verbindung in der Stunde, Mutter-Glück begegnen, Rechts-Gefühl in einer Verbindung, Verbindungen zu erfolgreichen Frauen, verbunden mit den Freuden des Volkes".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Happiness in relationships with women; connections with women's rights; a happy connection in the hour; encountering maternal happiness; a sense of justice within a connection; connections with successful women; connected with the people's joys.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source reference: printed pages `123–124`, PDF pages `125–126`, entry `0371`; the word "successful" crosses the page break.

Sociable, desire to join associations. Success with other persons, successful women. An association or an alliance, a fruitful marriage.

Editorial boundary note: footnote `21` on printed page `123` explicitly belongs to [Moon/Jupiter = Pluto](moon-jupiter-equals-pluto.md#ebertin-entry), entry `0370`. Its text was interleaved into this Node paragraph during extraction and is retained on the Pluto page.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source reference: PDF page `30`, Node under `Planets activating the Moon/Jupiter midpoint`; the chapter begins on PDF page `29`.

The ability to generously share one’s feelings when one connects to other people. An excess of emotional attachment to the past. The need to enthusiastically embrace new experiences.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `116`, PDF page `117`, `Nod` row.

#### Munkasey Entry

Fortunate results from activities with friends and associates; enhanced attraction to the right types of people and influences; increased ability to judge people and their needs; selecting the right friends or associates.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared happiness, sociability, and fortunate association, with emotional generosity in contact.
- psychology: Sharing feelings and judging people's needs; Sandbach also names excessive attachment to the past and a need for new experiences.
- body/health: Witte names becoming pregnant. This remains his historical astrological example, not a medical prediction.
- social/relationship: Associations, alliances, a fruitful marriage, cheerful or successful women, and selecting friends or associates.
- events/manifestations: Joy with others, fortunate joint activities, and connections with women's rights or a people's joys.
- conflicts/notes: Familiar attachments and openness to new experience form a stated tension. Ebertin's footnote about wanting popularity belongs to Pluto, not this Node entry.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in sociability and fortunate contact, while Sandbach qualifies this range with excessive attachment to the past. Munkasey's "right types" of people remains his wording without a separately defined criterion. Ebertin's popularity concern was a misplaced Pluto footnote, not a disagreement about the Node orientation; the correct paragraph is now joined across printed pages `123–124`.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Node`, the [pair's happiness and emotional breadth](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) are shared through contact and association. Joyful company, joining groups, alliances, and successful activity with friends connect sociability with fortunate relationships. Sandbach's generous sharing of feeling supplies an explicitly emotional form of that connection; ABC also includes women's rights and a people's shared joys. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The range includes choosing associates and considering their needs, rather than treating every contact as equally helpful. Sandbach also places openness to new experiences beside an excessive attachment to the past. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
