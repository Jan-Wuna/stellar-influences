## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Pluto row. The leading `P` in the old extraction was the alternative Pluto glyph, identified in the source key on PDF page `28`, not part of the prose.

Increased fortune and good luck. The transformation into a fortunate nation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `183`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Pluto row. Its opening clauses were previously absorbed into the parser's Neptune entry.

Source wording: "Erfolgreiche Entwicklung einer Frau, finanzielle Entwicklungen in einem Volk, Frauen-Rechte entwickeln, Recht eines Volkes auf Entwicklung, Veränderungen im Recht eines Volkes, wachsendes Glücks-Gefühl, wachsendes Recht für die Frau, wechselndes Glücks-Gefühl".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A woman's successful development; financial developments among a people; developing women's rights; a people's right to development; changes in a people's law; a growing feeling of happiness; growing rights for women; a changing feeling of happiness.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source reference: printed page `123`, PDF page `125`, entry `0370` and footnote `21`.

The desire to start great enterprises, an extraordinary and unusual striving for possessions and wealth.

Source footnote `21`, explicitly attached to Pluto:

> It should be added to Moon/Jupiter = Pluto that this indicates a person who wants to be popular and who will be anxious to be well thought of by others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source reference: PDF page `30`, Pluto under `Planets activating the Moon/Jupiter midpoint`; the chapter begins on PDF page `29`.

To feel many things very intensely which at worst can make it difficult to focus. The need to guard against going to emotional excesses without repressing one’s emo tions. To feel pressurized by one’s daily life being filled with too many things, and hence a pressing need to prioritize.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `116`, PDF page `117`, `Plu` row.

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased ability to think about family and social implications on a very large scale; purging and cleaning out unwanted items from home or work; rejection or denunciation of your ideas or opinions about life in general.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Growth or transformation of fortunate conditions, large ambitions, and feeling experienced with great intensity.
- psychology: Striving for wealth or great enterprises; wanting to be well thought of; many intense feelings that may make focus difficult.
- body/health: Sandbach's pressure and emotional excess are descriptions of experience, not an established medical condition.
- social/relationship: Thinking about family and social implications on a large scale, with a wish for popularity and the possibility of rejection of one's ideas.
- events/manifestations: Increased fortune, national transformation, financial and legal development, starting large enterprises, and clearing unwanted items from home or work.
- conflicts/notes: Growth and changing happiness coexist with difficulty focusing or prioritizing. Ebertin's footnote `21` belongs here; it must not be transferred to Node.

## Contradictions

The favorable growth and development vocabulary does not erase Sandbach's pressure or Munkasey's rejection of ideas. These are different expressions and emphases, not proof that growth must produce distress. ABC's eight-clause Pluto row and Ebertin's explicit Pluto footnote are restored here; the latter's placement inside the extracted Node paragraph was a boundary error. Witte's prefixed `P` was a glyph rather than a word.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Pluto`, the [pair's emotional and material abundance](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) takes a larger or changing form. Increased fortune, a nation's transformation into fortunate conditions, and financial or legal development describe growth in circumstances; great enterprises and an unusual striving for wealth describe ambitious aims. ABC also includes changing happiness, so development does not mean a single unbroken increase. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry))

The enlarged scale has an intensive emotional expression as well. Sandbach describes many strong feelings, difficulty focusing, and a need to prioritize without repressing emotion. Ebertin's footnote adds the wish to be popular and well thought of; Munkasey includes large-scale thinking about family and society, but also rejection of one's opinions. The range therefore includes growth, intensity, and concern with others' reception, without reducing the whole formula to success or to distress. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry))
