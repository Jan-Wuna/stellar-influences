## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Venus row.

To love happy and cheerful hours. A woman made happy by love. Harmonious nation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `182`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Venus row.

Source wording: "Erfolg einer schönen Frau, Friedens-Erfolg eines Volkes, Glück und Freude einer Mutter, harmonisches Glücks-Gefühl, Kunst-Erfolg einer Frau, Liebes-Freude in der Stunde, Liebes-Glück einer Frau, Recht der Frau auf Liebe, Recht eines Volkes auf Frieden".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A beautiful woman's success; a people's success in peace; a mother's happiness and joy; a harmonious feeling of happiness; a woman's artistic success; joy in love in the hour; a woman's happiness in love; a woman's right to love; a people's right to peace.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source reference: printed page `123`, PDF page `125`, entry `0365`.

An abundance of feeling, a harmonious nature radiating happiness, an artistic sense. A female artist. A happily loving woman.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source reference: PDF page `30`, Venus under `Planets activating the Moon/Jupiter midpoint`; the chapter begins on PDF page `29`.

An enjoyment and appreciation of anything that displays a wealth of feeling. The love of exposing oneself to many different types of things. To like being flooded by im pressions or stimuli. The tendency to freely share one’s feelings in relationships.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `116`, PDF page `117`, `Ven` row.

#### Munkasey Entry

Charm and politeness in an already caring and sensitive person; increased comfort and pleasure in a home setting; a preference for relaxation and good music; developing artistic talent; increasing value or monetary resources.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Enjoying and sharing abundant feeling through love, harmony, art, and comfort.
- psychology: Appreciation of emotional richness and diverse impressions, charm, sensitivity, and a harmonious disposition.
- body/health: These entries do not supply a distinct medical claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: Happiness in love, freely shared feelings, politeness, maternal joy, and ABC's right to love and collective peace.
- events/manifestations: Artistic development or success, pleasure at home, relaxation and good music, increasing monetary resources, and a people's success in peace.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's liking for many stimuli is explicit. The entries do not independently establish the former generic claims of complacency or excessive indulgence.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in happy feeling, love, harmony, or artistic enjoyment. Munkasey's comfort and monetary-resource examples and ABC's peace and rights clauses broaden the range without establishing a disagreement. ABC names a beautiful woman, not a gentle one, and includes artistic success omitted from the former translation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Venus`, the [pair's abundance of feeling and happiness](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) becomes something to enjoy, share, and express through love or art. Witte names happiness in love and cheerful hours; Ebertin connects abundant feeling with harmony and artistic sense. Sandbach emphasizes appreciation of emotionally rich experiences and freely sharing feelings in relationships. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))

Munkasey's comfort at home, music, relaxation, and artistic development give this enjoyment practical forms, while his monetary-resource clause retains a material application. ABC adds maternal joy, artistic success, a woman's right to love, and a people's right to peace. Those civic and material meanings extend the scope beyond affectionate temperament. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
