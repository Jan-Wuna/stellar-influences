## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To love happy and cheerful hours. A woman made happy by love. Harmonious nation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success of a gentle woman, peace success of a people, happiness and joy of a mother, harmonious GlUcks filial, love joy in the hour, love happiness of a woman, right of woman to love, and right of a people to peace

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

An abundance of feeling, a harmonious nature radiating happiness, an artistic sense. A female artist. A happily loving woman.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An enjoyment and appreciation of anything that displays a wealth of feeling. The love of exposing oneself to many different types of things. To like being flooded by im pressions or stimuli. The tendency to freely share one’s feelings in relationships.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Charm and politeness in an already caring and sensitive person; increased comfort and pleasure in a home setting; a preference for relaxation and good music; developing artistic talent; increasing value or monetary resources.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Jupiter = Venus`: hospitable love, emotional sweetness, and the ability to create a richly supportive or aesthetically nurturing atmosphere. Main caution: indulgent feeling: comfort and affection can become excessive, complacent, or too desirous of easy harmony.
