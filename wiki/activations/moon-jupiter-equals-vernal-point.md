## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Vernal Point row.

Happy hours. Fortunate change in general. Happy and contented nation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `182`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Vernal Point row.

Source wording: "Erfolgreiche Frau im öffentlichen Leben, Finanz-Frau in der Öffentlichkeit, Finanzen des Volkes in der Öffentlichkeit, glückliche Stunde im öffentlichen Leben, glückliches Volk in der Öffentlichkeit, öffentliche Freude des Volkes, Volks-Recht in der Öffentlichkeit".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A successful woman in public life; a woman involved in finance in public; a people's finances in public; a happy hour in public life; a happy people in public; a people's public joy; a people's rights in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Happiness, fortunate change, and contentment in a general or public setting.
- psychology: Happiness and contentment are named; neither entry develops a separate account of inner motives.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: Publicly successful women and a happy or contented people, rather than a specific private relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: Happy hours, fortunate changes, public joy, and financial or rights matters in public life.
- conflicts/notes: The financial and legal applications are part of ABC's explicit range; their public setting alone does not establish a particular financial or legal outcome.

## Contradictions

Both entries place happiness or contentment on a general or collective scale. ABC explicitly names public settings and adds financial and rights applications; these are differences of detail and scope.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Vernal Point`, the [pair's happiness and collective prosperity](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) take a general or public form. Witte names happy hours, fortunate change, and a contented nation; ABC repeatedly places happiness and joy in public life. Its successful women, public finances, and a people's rights also keep civic applications visible. The common theme is a public or collective setting for the pair's favorable meanings, with the financial and legal clauses retaining their specific scope. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
