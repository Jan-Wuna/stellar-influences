## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Vulcanus row.

Happy hours through mighty successful creation. Growing of the own optimism.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `183`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Vulcanus row.

Source wording: "Einfluß der Frau durch Geld, Erfolg bei Frauen durch Stärke, Erfolg gegen ein Volk durch Gewalt, erfolgreicher Einfluß einer Frau, glücklicher Einfluß einer Stunde, Finanz-Macht eines Volkes, Macht einer Frau durch Geld, vorteilhafter Gemüts-Einfluß".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A woman's influence through money; success with women through strength; success against a people through violence; a woman's successful influence; a happy influence of an hour; a people's financial power; a woman's power through money; a favorable emotional influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Happiness or optimism connected with strong accomplishment and effective influence, including financial power.
- psychology: Growing optimism and a favorable emotional influence.
- body/health: Neither entry establishes a distinct medical claim. ABC's strength and violence remain its stated means of influence or success.
- social/relationship: A woman's influence through money and success with women through strength; these are separate clauses with different subjects.
- events/manifestations: Powerful successful creation, financial power, and influence through strength or money; ABC also includes success against a people through violence.
- conflicts/notes: The coercive collective clause is a significant part of the range, not an endorsement of violence or proof that success benefits everyone affected.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes happiness and growing optimism through successful creation. ABC broadens effective influence to money, strength, and violence against a people. The last clause changes whose interests a "success" serves, so the favorable language should not be flattened into a universally beneficial outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Vulcanus`, the [pair's happiness and success](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) are connected with forceful accomplishment and influence. Witte links happy hours with powerful successful creation and growing optimism; ABC adds effective emotional influence and power exercised through money or strength. The financial clauses preserve a concrete material expression of that influence. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also names success against a people through violence. This makes the perspective of "success" consequential: an effective action may advantage one party while harming another. That severe collective example remains distinct from Witte's personal optimism and the favorable emotional-influence clause. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
