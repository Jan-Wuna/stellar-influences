## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `129`, PDF page `125`, `MOON + JUPITER`, Zeus row.

Happiness in creating. To love to become a mother. A happy motherhood.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `183`, PDF page `90`, `Mond + Jupiter`, Zeus row.

Source wording: "Erfolg einer kreativen Frau, Frauen-Recht gestalten, Leistungs-Erfolge einer Frau, Recht eines schöpferischen Volkes, Stunde eines glücklichen Beginns, weibliche Finanz-Führung, Zeugungs-Freude der Frau, Rechts-Ziele einer Frau".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A creative woman's success; shaping women's rights; a woman's successful achievements; a creative people's rights; an hour of a happy beginning; female financial management; a woman's joy in procreation; a woman's legal goals.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Happiness through creating, achieving, or beginning, with motherhood as an explicit source expression.
- psychology: Enjoyment of creation, desire for motherhood, and joy connected with procreation.
- body/health: Motherhood and procreation are attributed source themes; the entries do not establish fertility or a medical outcome.
- social/relationship: Maternal happiness and women's creative, financial, or rights-related activity.
- events/manifestations: Creative achievements, a happy beginning, financial management, shaping women's rights, and legal goals.
- conflicts/notes: The entries are favorable in tone but do not describe every form of creation or establish that all creative efforts succeed.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in happiness through creation and motherhood. ABC extends the range to achievements, financial management, rights, and legal goals; no opposing claim is present in these two short entries.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Jupiter = Zeus`, the [pair's happiness and success](../axes/moon-jupiter.md#derived-synthesis) find expression through creation, achievement, and beginnings. Witte states happiness in creating and happy motherhood; ABC adds a creative woman's successes, an hour of a happy beginning, and joy in procreation. These connect the enjoyment of creating with specific generative or productive expressions. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also names shaping women's rights, female financial management, a creative people's rights, and a woman's legal goals. This preserves civic and financial activity alongside the maternal examples, rather than making motherhood the formula's only meaning. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
