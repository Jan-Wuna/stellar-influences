## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Admetos row.

Decreased rendering of service. Reduction of officials.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `199`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Admetos row.

Source wording: "Beschränkung einer selbstbewußten Frau, besondere Gründlichkeit einer Frau, Blockade eines großen Volkes, Depressionen einer selbständigen Frau, Festigung des Ansehens einer Frau, Unterdrückung der Führung eines Volkes, Widerstand einer selbständigen Frau".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Restriction of a self-confident woman; a woman's particular thoroughness; blockade of a great people; an independent woman's depression; consolidation of a woman's standing; suppression of a people's leadership; an independent woman's resistance.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Reduction or restriction in service, independence, or authority, alongside thoroughness and consolidation of standing.
- psychology: Thoroughness and resistance are explicit. Self-confidence and independence were mistranslated as self-consciousness or self-defense.
- body/health: ABC's depression clause is an attributed astrological association, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Restriction or resistance involving independent women, and suppression of collective leadership.
- events/manifestations: Reduced service or official numbers, a blockade, and consolidation of a woman's standing.
- conflicts/notes: Consolidation and thoroughness prevent the range from being reduced to loss alone. The source does not explain a necessary progression from restriction to stronger standing.

## Contradictions

Witte's two clauses concern reduced service and officials. ABC adds both restriction and consolidation in personal or collective standing; those are distinct expressions, rather than a promise that one produces the other. The former strictness, promotion, and filtration wording was translation damage.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Admetos`, the [pair's independence, standing, and official authority](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) encounter reduction or restriction. Witte names reduced service and fewer officials; ABC adds restrictions on a self-confident woman, blockade, and suppression of leadership. An independent woman's resistance gives a personal response to constraint. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also names particular thoroughness and consolidation of a woman's standing. These retain a more settled expression alongside the restrictive ones, without making consolidation an assured result of hardship. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
