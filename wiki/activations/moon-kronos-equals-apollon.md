## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Apollon row.

Greater demands in the office. More office hours. To hold several offices at same time.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `199`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Apollon row.

Source wording: "Ansehen der Wissenschaft im Volk, besondere Gefühls-Erfahrungen, Erfahrungen einer großen Frau, Erfolg einer Frau beim Chef, Erfolg einer selbstbewußten Frau, erfolgreiche Obrigkeit eines Volkes, selbständige Handels-Frau, zunehmendes Selbstbewußtsein der Frau".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: The standing of science among a people; distinctive emotional experiences; a great woman's experiences; a woman's success with the boss; a self-confident woman's success; a people's successful authorities; an independent woman in trade; a woman's increasing self-confidence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A wider or more demanding official role, with experience, success, and growing confidence also represented.
- psychology: Distinctive emotional experiences and increasing self-confidence.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: Holding several offices, success with a boss, and an independent woman in trade; these retain their distinct roles and subjects.
- events/manifestations: More office hours or demands, successful authorities, a woman's successes, and the standing of science among a people.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's increased demands are not simply praise or a promise of advancement. ABC's experience and confidence clauses broaden the range without explaining a causal link to longer office hours.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes the quantity or demands of official work; ABC emphasizes standing, experience, and success. These are different emphases. The scan resolves the former "self conscious third woman" and "in the boss" damage as self-confidence and success with the boss.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Apollon`, the [pair's independence and official standing](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) have a wider or more demanding field of expression. Witte specifies more office hours, greater demands, and several offices held together. ABC retains successful authorities, an independent woman in trade, and success with a boss as related social applications. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also preserves distinctive emotional experiences, the standing of science, and a woman's increasing confidence. These add experiential and intellectual meanings alongside the administrative ones; the entries do not establish that increased duties necessarily produce success or confidence. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
