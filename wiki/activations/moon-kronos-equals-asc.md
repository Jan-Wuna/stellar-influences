## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Asc row.

Acquaintance with independent women. Dealing with officials or governments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `198`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Asc row.

Source wording: "Angesehene Frau im Umfeld, Dominanz anderer Völker, Hierarchie eines anderen Volkes, Partner-Gefühle lenken, selbständige Frau als Partner, Überlegenheits-Gefühl des anderen, vorrangige Gefühle des Partners, wichtige Stunde am Ort".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A respected woman in the surroundings; dominance of other peoples; another people's hierarchy; guiding a partner's feelings; an independent woman as partner; another person's feeling of superiority; a partner's feelings taking precedence; an important hour at the place.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Encountering independence, recognized standing, or authority through partners, contacts, and surroundings.
- psychology: Another person's feeling of superiority and the guidance or precedence of a partner's feelings.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: Acquaintances with independent women, an independent woman as partner, and dealings with officials or governments.
- events/manifestations: Official contacts, a respected local woman, another people's hierarchy or dominance, and an important hour at a place.
- conflicts/notes: Recognized standing and feelings of superiority are distinct claims; the entries do not establish that authority is deserved, harmful, or benevolent in every case.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in contacts with independent women or authorities. ABC adds the partner's emotional position and collective hierarchy. Its former garbled partner wording was a translation problem, not a different activation orientation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Asc`, the [pair's independence, standing, and authority](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) appear through encounters, partners, and surroundings. Witte names acquaintances with independent women and dealings with officials or governments. ABC makes the setting more specific through a respected woman nearby and an independent woman as partner, while also naming the hierarchy or dominance of other peoples. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The relationship range includes guiding a partner's feelings, another person's feeling of superiority, and a partner's feelings taking precedence. These preserve an emotional dimension alongside formal rank; they do not establish a single pattern for every partnership. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
