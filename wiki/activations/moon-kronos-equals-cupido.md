## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Cupido row.

Independent authoritative housewife. Woman leads in art. In community with officials. Leisure hours of the housewife.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `199`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Cupido row.

Source wording: "Ansehen einer Frauen-Gemeinschaft, besondere Gemüts-Gemeinschaft, Frau als Chef einer Gemeinschaft, große Volks-Gemeinschaft, selbständige Frauen einer Gemeinschaft, Obrigkeits-Gemeinschaft eines Volkes, wichtige Stunde für die Frau".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: The standing of a women's community; a distinctive emotional community; a woman as head of a community; a large community of a people; independent women of a community; a people's community of authorities; an important hour for a woman.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independence, standing, or authority expressed within a community, household, or artistic setting.
- psychology: ABC includes a distinctive emotional community; the entries do not give a separate account of personal motives.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: An authoritative housewife, women leading or belonging to communities, and association with officials.
- events/manifestations: A woman leading in art, the standing of a women's community, household leisure hours, and an important hour for a woman.
- conflicts/notes: Household, art, emotional community, and official association are separate supported settings. Independent women are not necessarily self-employed, as the former translation implied.

## Contradictions

Both entries place independence or authority in a shared setting. Witte includes household and artistic examples; ABC adds community standing and emotional community. The range does not require every community to be formal officialdom.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Cupido`, the [pair's independence and recognized standing](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) are expressed within shared or communal life. Witte places an authoritative woman in household and artistic roles and names community with officials. ABC adds women heading or belonging to communities and the standing of a women's community. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The scope also includes a distinctive emotional community, household leisure hours, and a people's community of authorities. Those examples retain emotional, domestic, artistic, and administrative forms of association without collapsing them into one institutional model. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
