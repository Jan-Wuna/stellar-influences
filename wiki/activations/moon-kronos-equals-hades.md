## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Hades row.

Grief through independent women or officials. Poor dismissed officials.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `199`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Hades row.

Source wording: "Ansehen einer alten Frau, Benachteiligung selbstbewußter Frauen, Entbehrungen einer selbständigen Frau, kriminelle Obrigkeit eines Volkes, kriminelle Überlegenheit einer Frau, Mangel an Selbständigkeit einer Frau, wichtige Vergangenheit einer Frau".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: An older woman's standing; disadvantage affecting self-confident women; an independent woman's privations; a people's criminal authorities; a woman's criminal superiority; a woman's lack of independence; a woman's important past.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Grief, privation, or disadvantage involving independence and authority, with age and the past also represented.
- psychology: Self-confidence and lack of independence are explicit positions; the entries do not explain an individual's motives for criminal conduct.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: Grief through independent women or officials, disadvantage affecting self-confident women, and dismissed officials in poverty.
- events/manifestations: Dismissal, deprivation, an older woman's standing or important past, and ABC's explicitly criminal authority or superiority.
- conflicts/notes: The criminal clauses are particular source judgments. They do not make independence, official status, or old age evidence of criminality.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in disadvantage or grief involving independent women and officials. ABC also preserves standing in old age and an important past. Its former self-wound, self-indigenous, self-indulgence, and misdemeanour wording was translation damage; the scan names confidence, independence, and the past.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Hades`, the [pair's independence, standing, and official authority](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) are involved in grief, disadvantage, or deprivation. Witte names grief through independent women or officials and poor dismissed officials. ABC adds disadvantage affecting self-confident women, an independent woman's privations, and a woman's lack of independence. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also retains an older woman's standing and a woman's important past, alongside its severe criminal-authority and criminal-superiority clauses. These are different applications of the formula; neither age nor independence itself establishes criminality. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
