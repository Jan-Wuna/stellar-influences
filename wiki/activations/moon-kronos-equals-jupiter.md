## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Jupiter row.

Successful leading lady. Beneficent independent women. Successful officialdom. The board under successful direction.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `198`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Jupiter row.

Source wording: "Besonderes Glück einer Frau, erfolgreiche selbständige Frau, finanzielle Überlegenheit einer Frau, Finanz-Hierarchie eines Volkes, großes Freude-Gefühl, Rechts-Hierarchie eines Volkes, Vorteile für ein großes Volk, wichtiges Frauen-Recht".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A woman's particular happiness; a successful independent woman; a woman's financial superiority; a people's financial hierarchy; a great feeling of joy; a people's legal hierarchy; advantages for a great people; an important women's right.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Success or benefit in independence, leadership, or official standing, with happiness and financial or legal applications.
- psychology: Particular happiness and a great feeling of joy are explicit.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: Beneficent independent women, a successful leading woman, and boards under successful direction.
- events/manifestations: Successful officialdom, financial superiority or hierarchy, legal hierarchy, collective advantages, and an important women's right.
- conflicts/notes: A financial or legal hierarchy is an explicit application; its presence alone does not establish that every official action is fair or beneficial to everyone.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in successful independence or authority. ABC also preserves personal joy and financial or legal structures. Its "right hierarchy" was a legal hierarchy, rather than a statement that a hierarchy is morally right.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Jupiter`, the [pair's independence and recognized authority](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) have successful or beneficial expressions. Witte names beneficent independent women, a successful leading woman, and official bodies under successful direction. ABC adds personal happiness and joy alongside the successful independent woman. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Financial superiority, financial and legal hierarchy, collective advantages, and an important women's right keep the material and civic range visible. These are particular applications of success and standing, rather than evidence that every hierarchy has the same effects. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
