## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Mars row.

Independent female manager acting with authority. Independent or despotic acting officials. Striving for official position.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `198`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Mars row.

Source wording: "Autorität des Willens einer Frau, Behörden-Arbeit in einem Volk, besondere Impulse einer Frau, Lenkung der Gefühls-Handlungen, selbständig handelnde Frau, vorrangige Behandlung der Frau, wichtige Tätigkeit einer Frau, Wille eines selbstbewußten Volkes".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Authority of a woman's will; administrative work among a people; a woman's distinctive impulses; directing emotionally motivated actions; a woman acting independently; priority treatment for a woman; a woman's important activity; the will of a self-confident people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independence or authority exercised through action, will, administration, or pursuit of office.
- psychology: Will, distinctive impulses, and direction of emotionally motivated actions.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: An authoritative female manager and officials acting independently or despotically; ABC adds priority treatment for a woman.
- events/manifestations: Administrative work, striving for official position, independent action, and important activity.
- conflicts/notes: Witte explicitly keeps independent and despotic official action side by side. Effective action does not by itself settle how authority is exercised.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in independent or authoritative action. Witte's despotic officials are a significant adverse expression within that range; they should remain distinct from an assumption that all independent management is coercive.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Mars`, the [pair's independence and authority](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) become active through will, management, administration, or pursuit of office. Witte's female manager acts with authority; ABC adds a woman acting independently and direction of emotionally motivated actions. These connect standing with the exercise of agency. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The manner of acting remains consequential: Witte names both independent and despotic officials. ABC's public administrative work, priority treatment, and a self-confident people's will preserve collective and interpersonal applications alongside individual action. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
