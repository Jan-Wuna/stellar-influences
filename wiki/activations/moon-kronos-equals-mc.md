## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, MC row.

Nobility of soul. Mental influence by independent women. To belong to the leading people. Civil servants.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `198`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, MC row.

Source wording: "Ansehen meiner Frau, beseeltes selbstbewußtes Volk, besonders gefühlvolle Seele, Hierarchie meines Volkes, mein Dominanz-Gefühl, meine Frau als Chef, meine Frau hat Vorrang, meine selbstbewußte Frau, seelische Größe einer Frau, wichtige Gefühle der Seele".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: My wife's standing; an inspired, self-confident people; a soul particularly rich in feeling; my people's hierarchy; my feeling of dominance; my wife as chief; my wife takes precedence; my self-confident wife; a woman's inner greatness; important feelings of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Authority or independence has a personal and inward expression, including noble-mindedness, emotional richness, and identification with standing or hierarchy.
- psychology: Nobility of soul, important feelings, a feeling of dominance, and influence by independent women.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: A wife's standing, confidence, leadership, or precedence, alongside belonging to a leading people or being a civil servant.
- events/manifestations: Civil service and participation in hierarchy are named settings; no single career event is selected by the entries.
- conflicts/notes: Inner nobility, personal dominance, and formal hierarchy are distinct expressions. ABC's language of soul does not specify a metaphysical mechanism.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes inner nobility and influence as well as official belonging; ABC repeatedly uses first-person relations and feelings. These overlap without reducing the formula to a job title or equating noble-mindedness with dominance.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = MC`, the [pair's independence and recognized standing](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) have a personal and inward dimension. Witte names nobility of soul and mental influence by independent women; ABC adds emotional richness, important feelings, and a woman's inner greatness. Its first-person clauses also locate standing in one's wife, one's people, or one's own feeling of dominance. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Belonging to a leading people, civil service, and collective hierarchy preserve an official or public application. They sit beside personal feelings and relationships; the entries do not make formal rank a necessary measure of inner nobility. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
