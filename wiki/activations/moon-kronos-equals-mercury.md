## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Mercury row.

Independent thinking. Decrees. Official style in writing. Autocratic.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `198`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Mercury row.

Source wording: "Besonderes Sprach-Gefühl, Frau als Medien-Chef, Gespräche mit angesehenen Frauen, Jugend-Führung eines Volkes, Nachrichten über die Obrigkeit eines Volkes, selbstbewußte junge Frau, Vorrang der Jugend eines Volkes, wichtige Worte einer Frau".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A distinctive feel for language; a woman as media chief; conversations with respected women; leadership of a people's youth; news about a people's authorities; a self-confident young woman; precedence of a people's youth; a woman's important words.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independence or authority expressed through thought, language, writing, media, or youth leadership.
- psychology: Independent thinking, a feel for language, and a young woman's self-confidence; Witte also names an autocratic expression.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: Conversations with respected women, a woman leading media, and leadership or precedence of youth.
- events/manifestations: Decrees, an official style of writing, news about authorities, and important words.
- conflicts/notes: Independent thought and autocratic expression are not identical. ABC's youth leadership was mistranslated as youth future; the entry does not supply a timing forecast.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in independent or authoritative communication. ABC adds media and youth applications. Witte's autocratic expression remains a possible manner within this range, rather than a judgment about every independent thinker.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Mercury`, the [pair's independence and authority](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) find expression in thinking and communication. Witte names independent thought, decrees, and an official writing style; ABC adds a feel for language, important words, conversations with respected women, and news about authorities. These connect personal expression with the language of recognized standing or office. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also includes a woman as media chief, a self-confident young woman, and leadership or precedence of youth. Witte's autocratic clause supplies a distinct adverse manner of expression without making authority or independent thinking inherently autocratic. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
