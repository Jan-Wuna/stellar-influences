## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Neptune row.

Independent women unfit as managers. Leading women cause intrigues, failures. Officials and boards interfere in vain.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `199`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Neptune row; the following development clauses belong to Pluto.

Source wording: "Ahnungen einer selbständigen Frau, Ansehen eines fremden Volkes, Autoritäts-Schwäche einer Frau, Enttäuschung durch die Obrigkeit eines Volkes, Feingefühl einer weiblichen Autorität, große Zukunft einer Frau, Überlegenheit einer Frau im Wasser".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: An independent woman's premonitions; a foreign people's standing; a woman's weakness of authority; disappointment through a people's authorities; the sensitivity of a female authority; a woman's great future; a woman's superiority in water.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Authority or management may be weak or ineffective, while intuition, sensitivity, and foreign or water-related applications also remain represented.
- psychology: Premonitions, sensitivity, and disappointment are ABC's explicit themes.
- body/health: The actual ABC row has no health clause. The former repeated health wording was translation and row-boundary damage.
- social/relationship: Witte's critical judgments about leading women and officials remain attributed historical wording; ABC also names a sensitive female authority.
- events/manifestations: Failed management or vain official interference, a foreign people's standing, a woman's future, and superiority in water.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's adverse emphasis does not exhaust ABC's range. The water clause does not specify a sport, occupation, or medical condition.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes unsuitable management, intrigue, failure, or ineffective interference. ABC adds weakness and disappointment but also premonitions, sensitivity, standing, and a great future. These differences materially broaden the reading. The source does not turn its gendered management judgments into a general assessment of women's ability. The full seven-clause Neptune row is now distinct from the following Pluto development row.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Neptune`, the [pair's independence and authority](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) include weakened or ineffective forms. Witte describes failures of management and vain official interference; ABC adds weakness of authority and disappointment through authorities. These passages concern the effectiveness or experience of leadership, with Witte's gendered judgments retained as his historical wording. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also preserves an independent woman's premonitions, a female authority's sensitivity, a foreign people's standing, a woman's great future, and superiority in water. These retain receptive and wider situational applications alongside the failures. The short water clause has no more specific setting in the entry and supplies no health claim. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
