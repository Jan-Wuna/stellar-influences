## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Node row.

Connections with independent women or officials.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `198`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Node row.

Source wording: "Beziehung zu selbstbewußten Frauen, große Gefühls-Verbindung, Kontakte zu angesehenen Frauen, Stunde wichtiger Verbindungen, Verbindung zur Obrigkeit eines Volkes, Verbindungen zu einem dominierenden Volk, Vorrang der Frau bei Kontakten".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A relationship with self-confident women; a great emotional bond; contacts with respected women; an hour of important connections; connection with a people's authorities; connections with a dominant people; a woman's precedence in contacts.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections involving independent or respected women, official authority, or collective standing.
- psychology: ABC names a great emotional bond and self-confidence; it does not describe a separate hidden motive for forming contacts.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: Relationships with self-confident women, official connections, and a woman's precedence in contacts.
- events/manifestations: Important connections and contact with a people's authorities or a dominant people.
- conflicts/notes: An emotional bond and an official connection are separate supported forms of association; neither must be inferred from the other.

## Contradictions

Both entries concern connections with independence or authority. ABC makes the emotional bond, respected standing, and collective contact more explicit. The former "self grown" wording was a mistranslation of self-confident women.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Node`, the [pair's independence and recognized standing](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) are encountered through connections. Witte gives the short formula-specific meaning as contact with independent women or officials; ABC adds respected women, important connections, and contact with collective authorities. The emphasis is on association with standing or independence, rather than holding office oneself. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC's great emotional bond and a woman's precedence in contacts preserve personal feeling and relational position alongside the formal connections. These add distinct meanings without requiring every official association to be intimate. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
