## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Pluto row. The former leading `P` is an alternative Pluto glyph, identified in the source key on PDF page `28`.

Institution and organization of a civil service.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `199`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Pluto row; its first clauses had been absorbed into the parser's Neptune entry.

Source wording: "Besondere Gemüts-Entwicklungen, Entwicklung einer Frau zur Selbständigkeit, große Gefühls-Entwicklung, Obrigkeits-Veränderungen für ein Volk, wachsender Vorrang der Frau, Wandlungen einer selbständigen Frau, wichtige Entwicklungen in der Stunde".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Distinctive inner developments; a woman's development toward independence; great emotional development; changes in a people's authorities; a woman's growing precedence; changes in an independent woman; important developments in the hour.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Developing or reorganizing independence, emotional life, or official authority.
- psychology: Distinctive inner or emotional development is explicit in ABC's restored opening clauses.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: A woman's development toward independence and growing precedence; changes in an already independent woman.
- events/manifestations: Institution and organization of a civil service, changes in collective authorities, and important developments.
- conflicts/notes: Personal emotional development and civil-service organization are different scales of application. The sources do not say every institutional change increases independence.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes creating or organizing a civil service; ABC also names inner development and women's independence. The full seven-clause Pluto row restores that personal range. Its earlier missing opening was a Neptune/Pluto extraction boundary error, and Witte's prefixed P was a glyph.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Pluto`, the [pair's independence and official standing](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) undergo development or organization. Witte names the institution and organization of a civil service; ABC adds changes in a people's authorities and important developments. These give the theme a concrete institutional form. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also makes the personal range explicit: inner and emotional development, a woman's movement toward independence, her growing precedence, and changes in an independent woman. Development therefore concerns both the person and the structures of authority, without requiring the same outcome in every case. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
