## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Poseidon row.

Officials of universities. Ecclesiastical administrative authority. The spiritual authorities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `199`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Poseidon row.

Source wording: "Autorität einer Frau durch Wahrheit, besondere Einsicht der Frau, geistige Überlegenheit einer Frau, geistige Hierarchie eines Volkes, große Ideen einer Frau, Kultur-Autorität eines Volkes, Vernunft einer selbständigen Frau, wichtige Erkenntnis einer Frau".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A woman's authority through truth; a woman's distinctive insight; a woman's mental or spiritual superiority; a people's mental or spiritual hierarchy; a woman's great ideas; a people's cultural authority; an independent woman's reason; a woman's important insight.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Authority or recognized standing in intellectual, spiritual, educational, or cultural life.
- psychology: Insight, great ideas, and reason; ABC explicitly connects a woman's authority with truth.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: University officials, church administrators, spiritual authorities, and a woman's authority or independence.
- events/manifestations: Educational or ecclesiastical administration, cultural authority, and important insights.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's `geistig` retains mental and spiritual senses. Formal office, truth, and reason are named applications rather than proof that every office-holder possesses them.

## Contradictions

Witte names institutions and spiritual authorities; ABC adds truth, insight, reason, and cultural authority. These overlap without equating institutional position with wisdom. The former "cultural authorship" and "munft" were mistranslations of authority and reason.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Poseidon`, the [pair's independence and recognized authority](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) take intellectual, spiritual, or cultural forms. Witte's university officials and church administrators give this a concrete institutional setting; ABC connects authority with truth, insight, great ideas, and reason. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also retains mental or spiritual hierarchy and a people's cultural authority. Those collective applications sit beside an independent woman's reason or insight; they do not make institutional standing a guarantee of truth. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
