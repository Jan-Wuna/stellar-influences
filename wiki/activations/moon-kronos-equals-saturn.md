## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Saturn row.

Hindrances through officials or leading women, losses therefrom. Hindrances through officials, commissions or boards.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `199`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Saturn row.

Source wording: "Ansehen einer Frau im Alter, Aufgaben einer selbständigen Frau, Autoritäts-Verlust im Volk, besondere Gemüts-Probleme, Geduld eines großen Volkes, reife Leistungen einer Frau, Schaden durch die Obrigkeit eines Volkes, wichtige Aufgaben der Frau".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A woman's standing in old age; an independent woman's tasks; loss of authority among a people; distinctive emotional problems; a great people's patience; a woman's mature achievements; harm through a people's authorities; a woman's important tasks.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independence or authority involves tasks, hindrances, losses, or maturity; patience and achievement remain part of the range.
- psychology: Emotional problems and patience are explicit; the former poverty wording was not the actual ABC clause.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct medical claim; ABC's emotional problems are not a clinical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Obstruction through leading women, officials, commissions, or boards, and an independent woman's tasks.
- events/manifestations: Losses through official hindrance, loss of authority, harm through authorities, standing in old age, and mature achievements.
- conflicts/notes: Maturity and patience coexist with obstruction or damage; the entries do not promise that endurance necessarily produces success.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes hindrances and resulting losses; ABC also names standing in old age, patience, and mature achievements. These add a less adverse range without cancelling the loss clauses. The former self-defense and poverty wording mistranslated independence and emotional problems.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Saturn`, the [pair's independence and official standing](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) encounter tasks, hindrances, or losses. Witte locates obstruction in officials, leading women, commissions, or boards; ABC adds an independent woman's tasks, emotional problems, loss of authority, and harm through authorities. These retain both the experience of responsibility and the difficulties associated with official power. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also names standing in old age, a people's patience, and mature achievements. Those clauses preserve maturity and accomplishment as specific expressions alongside obstruction, without making them inevitable rewards for difficulty. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
