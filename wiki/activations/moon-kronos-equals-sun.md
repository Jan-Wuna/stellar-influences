## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Sun row.

The independent wife. Daily office and consultation hours.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `198`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Sun row.

Source wording: "Mann einer angesehenen Frau, Menschen eines angesehen Volkes, Personen aus der Hierarchie eines Volkes, selbständige Frau eines Mannes, Selbstbewußtsein zwischen Mann und Frau, Überlegenheits-Gefühl des Mannes, wichtige Stunde am Tag".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A respected woman's husband; members of a respected people; persons from a people's hierarchy; a man's independent wife; self-confidence between man and woman; a man's feeling of superiority; an important hour in the day.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independence and standing expressed through persons, partnership, hierarchy, or everyday official hours.
- psychology: Self-confidence between partners and a man's feeling of superiority are distinct ABC clauses.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: An independent wife, a respected woman's husband, and persons belonging to a respected people or hierarchy.
- events/manifestations: Daily office or consultation hours and an important hour in the day.
- conflicts/notes: The source names a feeling of superiority, not proof of actual superiority. The independent-wife clause had been mistranslated as a man's self-esteem.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in an independent wife and retain official or hierarchical settings. ABC's confidence and superiority clauses add personal feelings; they do not contradict the daily-office application, which has a different scope.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Sun`, the [pair's independence and standing](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) are located in persons and their everyday relations. Witte names the independent wife; ABC adds her husband's position, membership in a respected people or hierarchy, and confidence between woman and man. A man's feeling of superiority is a separate subjective expression within that range. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Daily office and consultation hours, together with ABC's important hour in the day, preserve a concrete administrative application. These ordinary settings sit beside the personal meanings rather than making the whole formula a statement about temperament or rank. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
