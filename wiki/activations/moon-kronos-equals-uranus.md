## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Uranus row.

Leading women act suddenly. Officials or boards interfere surprisingly.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `199`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Uranus row.

Source wording: "Ansehen eines Volkes in der Technik, besonderes Ereignis für eine Frau, Erregung über die Frau als Chef, Impulse einer selbständigen Frau, staatliche Frauen-Reform, Unruhe in der Obrigkeit des Volkes, wichtige Gemüts-Impulse".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A people's standing in technology; a distinctive event for a woman; excitement over a woman as chief; an independent woman's impulses; state reform concerning women; unrest among a people's authorities; important emotional impulses.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden action or surprising intervention involving independent women or official authority, alongside excitement, reform, or unrest.
- psychology: Excitement and important emotional impulses are explicit.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: Leading or independent women acting, excitement over a woman as chief, and unsettled authorities.
- events/manifestations: Surprising official interference, a distinctive event, a people's technical standing, and state reform concerning women.
- conflicts/notes: Surprise, reform, and unrest are different expressions. The entries do not select a uniformly favorable or adverse result.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sudden acts or surprising intervention; ABC adds emotional excitement, technical standing, and public reform or unrest. These broaden the range without establishing that every unexpected intervention is a reform.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Uranus`, the [pair's independence and official authority](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) are expressed through sudden action or surprising intervention. Witte names leading women acting suddenly and unexpected interference by officials or boards; ABC adds excitement over a woman as chief and an independent woman's impulses. The connection is between authority and an abrupt or agitated expression of it. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also retains a people's technical standing, state reform concerning women, unrest among authorities, and important emotional impulses. These preserve public and emotional applications without making every surprising event either progress or disruption. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
