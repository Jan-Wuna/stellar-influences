## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Venus row.

Charitable. Treated well by officials. Philanthropist (female). Female officials. Pleasant connections with officials and boards.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `198`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Venus row.

Source wording: "Besondere Harmonie mit der Frau, dominierende Schönheit einer Frau, große Liebe einer Frau, große Wünsche einer Frau, Selbständigkeit der Frau in der Liebe, vorrangiger Frieden eines Volkes, Wohlwollen einer angesehenen Frau".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Distinctive harmony with a woman; a woman's dominant beauty; a woman's great love; a woman's great wishes; a woman's independence in love; priority given to a people's peace; a respected woman's goodwill.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independence or standing expressed through goodwill, harmony, love, or pleasant official relations.
- psychology: Great love, great wishes, and goodwill are explicit.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: Charitable women, pleasant official connections, favorable treatment by officials, and independence in love.
- events/manifestations: Philanthropy, female official roles, a respected woman's goodwill, and priority given to collective peace.
- conflicts/notes: Beauty and wishes are separate ABC clauses; independence in love was mistranslated as self-confidence. No adverse counterpart is specified in these two entries.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in goodwill and agreeable relations involving women or officials. ABC adds love, beauty, wishes, and collective peace. Its second beauty clause in the former translation was actually great wishes.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Venus`, the [pair's independence and recognized standing](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) have agreeable or benevolent expressions. Witte names charity, favorable official treatment, and pleasant connections with officials or boards; ABC adds harmony and a respected woman's goodwill. These connect authority or standing with the quality of relations around it. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also preserves great love, great wishes, independence in love, dominant beauty, and priority given to a people's peace. Those personal and collective applications extend the range beyond official favor while retaining independence within affectionate relations. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
