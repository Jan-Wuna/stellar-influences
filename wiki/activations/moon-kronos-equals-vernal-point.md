## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Vernal Point row.

Independent women. Officials. Great people. The people of a leading nation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `198`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Vernal Point row.

Source wording: "In der Öffentlichkeit angesehene Frau, Gefühl der Überlegenheit im öffentlichen Leben, Hierarchie eines Volkes in der Öffentlichkeit, selbständige Frau in der Öffentlichkeit, Vorrang der Frau im öffentlichen Leben, wichtige Stunde in der Öffentlichkeit".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A woman respected in public; a feeling of superiority in public life; a people's hierarchy in public; an independent woman in public; a woman's precedence in public life; an important hour in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independence, standing, or hierarchy in public and collective life.
- psychology: ABC includes a feeling of superiority in public, distinct from proof of actual superiority.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct body or health claim for this orientation.
- social/relationship: Publicly respected or independent women, officials, and members of a leading nation.
- events/manifestations: Hierarchy or female precedence in public and an important public hour.
- conflicts/notes: Public standing, personal independence, and a subjective feeling of superiority are different applications. The entries do not establish how the standing was gained.

## Contradictions

Witte names independent women, officials, and leading peoples; ABC explicitly places the pair's standing or hierarchy in public life. The former "women of public opinion" wording had replaced the independent-woman clause and supplies no separate claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Vernal Point`, the [pair's independence, standing, and authority](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) are expressed publicly or collectively. Witte names officials, independent women, and people of a leading nation; ABC makes the public setting explicit through respected women, hierarchy, and female independence or precedence. The source's feeling of superiority adds a subjective experience of public standing, while an important public hour retains a concrete occasion rather than a character trait. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
