## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `133`, `MOON + KRONOS`, Vulcanus row.

Smooth function of office machinery. Officials and office work at maximum. High and mighty officials. Ministry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `199`, PDF page `98`, `Mond + Kronos`, Vulcanus row.

Source wording: "Besondere Energie einer Frau, Einfluß einer überlegenen Frau, kulturelles Ansehen einer Frau, große Gewalt gegen Frauen, Macht einer selbständigen Frau, hierarchische Macht eines Volkes, Staats-Gewalt einer Frau, starke Gefühle einer Autorität".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A woman's particular energy; a superior woman's influence; a woman's cultural standing; great violence against women; an independent woman's power; a people's hierarchical power; a woman's state authority or state power; an authority's strong feelings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong influence, power, or full official functioning, with coercive applications also explicitly represented.
- psychology: Particular energy and an authority's strong feelings; the latter was mistranslated as virtues.
- body/health: The entry names violence against women, not a distinct medical correspondence or diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Powerful independent women, influential officials, and hierarchical or state power; violence against women remains a separate severe example.
- events/manifestations: Smooth office machinery, maximum official work, a ministry, cultural standing, and the exercise of power.
- conflicts/notes: `Staats-Gewalt` can denote state authority or power; it should not be collapsed into the separately explicit violence-against-women clause. Effective administration and coercion remain distinct expressions.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes full or smooth official functioning and powerful officials. ABC also names energy and influence but retains severe violence and hierarchical power. These are materially different expressions of power; neither smooth functioning nor rank establishes benevolent use.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Kronos = Vulcanus`, the [pair's independence and official authority](../axes/moon-kronos.md#derived-synthesis) are expressed with power, energy, or full administrative operation. Witte names smooth office machinery, officials working at maximum, and a ministry; ABC adds influential or powerful independent women, cultural standing, and hierarchical or state power. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also explicitly names great violence against women and an authority's strong feelings. Those clauses preserve the severe and emotional range alongside effective administration. Its state-power expression is broader than violence alone, and neither should be inferred from the other without further evidence. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
