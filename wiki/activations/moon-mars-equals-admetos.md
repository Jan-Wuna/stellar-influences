## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: printed page `128`, PDF page `124`, `MOON + MARS`, Admetos row. The scan begins "Limited", correcting the extracted lowercase initial.

Limited work. Special work. To concentrate on one’s work. Difficulties in establishing a home. A modest or small home.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `181`, PDF page `89`, `Mond + Mars`, Admetos row.

Source wording: "Arbeit in einem unterdrückten Volk, begrenzte Frauen-Arbeit, Einschränkung der Gefühls-Handlungen, Gefühls-Handlungen vertiefen, gründliche Tatkraft der Frau, Handlungen der Frau blockieren, Stunde eines Arbeits-Streiks, Unterdrückung des Volks-Willens".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Work among an oppressed people; limited women's work; restriction of emotionally motivated actions; deepening emotionally motivated actions; a woman's thorough energy for action; blocking a woman's actions; an hour of a work strike; suppression of a people's will.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Limited or concentrated work and emotionally motivated action, with blocking and deepening as distinct expressions.
- psychology: Concentration and deepened action are explicit; the entries do not specify a separate inner motive for the restriction.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a distinct medical claim. The woman's energy for action is not a health diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Women's restricted or blocked work and collective suppression; Witte separately names difficulties establishing a home and a modest or small home.
- events/manifestations: Limited or special work, concentrated effort, work stoppage, a small home, or difficulty establishing one.
- conflicts/notes: The actual ABC row concerns feeling-directed actions, not a general freedom-of-action doctrine. Thoroughness and concentration do not make every imposed restriction beneficial.

## Contradictions

The sources overlap in limited or concentrated work. ABC also names deepening emotional action, blocking it, and suppression of collective will. These retain focused effort and external constraint as distinct expressions. Its former freedom, fundamental-power, and submission wording was translation damage.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Admetos`, the [pair's work and feeling-directed action](../axes/moon-mars.md#derived-synthesis) are limited, concentrated, blocked, or deepened. Witte names limited or special work and concentration; ABC adds both restriction and deepening of emotionally motivated actions. These distinguish focused effort from imposed obstruction rather than treating them as the same experience. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The concrete range includes work among an oppressed people, blocked women's activity, a work strike, and suppression of collective will. Witte separately retains difficulty establishing a home and a modest or small home. These preserve labor and domestic applications without assuming that every small home is unwanted or every concentrated task is coercive. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
