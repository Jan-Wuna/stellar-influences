## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Moon + Mars`

#### Witte Entry

Procurement of work. Extension of working time, many working hours. Improvement of marriage possibility. Improving. Redecorating or refurbishing the house.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Mond + Mars`
- Source locator: printed p. 181; one-based PDF p. 89.
- Editorial translation: checked against the original German scan, including the activation glyph and paragraph boundary.

#### ABC Entry

Successful collective will; a woman acting successfully; a people's desire for freedom; a people's peace actions; knowledge impulses informed by feeling; an hour of preparation for work; a woman's activity in state service; increasing collective will.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Expansion and improvement of work, with collective freedom and peace in the ABC entry. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: The ABC entry names a desire for freedom and knowledge informed by feeling; neither entry establishes a general personality profile. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health claim occurs in these entries.
- social/relationship: Witte includes improved marriage prospects and refurbishment of the home; ABC uses gendered examples of successful work and state service. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Obtaining work, longer working hours, work preparation, home improvement, and collective peace actions. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Longer hours and preparation are different aspects of work, not a promise of effortless success. The two entries do not establish independent corroboration.

## Contradictions

No mutually exclusive claim is apparent in these entries. Witte emphasizes practical improvement; ABC adds collective freedom, peace, and state service.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair's activity through an enlarged or improving field of work: obtaining work, extending its hours, preparing for it, or refurbishing a home. The same orientation includes improved marriage prospects in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends successful action to collective freedom and peace. These are the entry's particular applications; they do not establish that every increase in effort is beneficial.
