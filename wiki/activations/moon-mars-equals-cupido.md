## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Moon + Mars`

#### Witte Entry

Works in common. Hours of work in a community. Art workers (female). To keep house together. The natural (free or common law) marriage.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Mond + Mars`
- Source locator: printed p. 181; one-based PDF p. 89.
- Editorial translation: checked against the original German scan, including the activation glyph and paragraph boundary.

#### ABC Entry

A people's community of action; a community's working hours; shared feelingful action; a people's common will; a community of women acting together; women's artistic work; the will of a national community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Work and action organized through a community or shared household. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: The ABC entry names common will and shared action informed by feeling; neither entry defines a general cooperative personality. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health claim occurs in these entries.
- social/relationship: Keeping house together, common-law marriage, community work, and women's artistic work are the sources' particular social settings. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Joint working hours, collective action, shared housekeeping, and artistic work. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: The examples concern joint activity and social form; they do not establish that cooperation is harmonious or free of conflict.

## Contradictions

No explicit contradiction is apparent. Household and common-law marriage examples in Witte sit beside collective and national-community examples in ABC; these are different scales of shared action.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair's work and action as something carried out together. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) moves from common working hours to keeping house and common-law marriage; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the same orientation to communities of action and shared will. Joint labor and shared living are the connecting themes, without a claim that every such arrangement is harmonious.
