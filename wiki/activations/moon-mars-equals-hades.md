## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Moon + Mars`
- Source locator: printed p. 128; one-based PDF p. 124, Hades row checked on the actual scan.

#### Witte Entry

Bad working conditions. Defective work. Lack and disorder in the home. Women occupied with dirty work. Coarse and vulgar women or man. The low and mean acting populace. People occupied with coal or military service. The slain wife.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Mond + Mars`
- Source locator: printed p. 181; one-based PDF p. 89.
- Editorial translation: checked against the original German scan, including the activation glyph and paragraph boundary.

#### ABC Entry

Work-related crime within a people; dangerous women's work; perilous actions arising from feeling; mysterious actions during the hour; a people's actions in the past; a woman's criminal actions; a people's traditional actions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Work and action under adverse or dangerous conditions, alongside past and traditional collective action. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: The sources use evaluative language about conduct; they do not establish a psychological mechanism linking this formula to criminality. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: Witte includes the violent image of a slain wife, and ABC names dangerous work. These are attributed historical claims, not an injury prediction. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- social/relationship: Domestic lack and disorder, dirty work, coal work, and military service occur in Witte. Its judgments about women and the populace remain source language. [Witte](#witte-entry).
- events/manifestations: Defective work and poor working conditions are concrete examples; ABC also names crime, past actions, and traditions. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: The source's moral judgments and extreme violence must not be generalized into a person's character or treated as inevitable events.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in adverse work conditions, while ABC also includes historical and traditional actions without necessarily calling them adverse. Those additional meanings should not be flattened into a single claim of degradation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair's work and action through poor conditions, defective results, danger, or domestic disorder. Those concrete themes are supported by the [Witte](#witte-entry) and [ABC](#abc-entry) entries. ABC also retains action rooted in a people's past and traditions, which need not carry the same negative judgment. Witte's moralizing descriptions and image of a slain wife remain attributed historical examples; this synthesis does not turn them into character judgments or predictions.
