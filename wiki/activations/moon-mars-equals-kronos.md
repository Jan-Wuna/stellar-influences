## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Leadership, supervision, or special importance attached to work and action. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: The ABC entry names self-assurance and independence in its example of a woman acting; no general executive temperament is established. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health claim occurs in these entries.
- social/relationship: Witte's foreman, female executives, and woman in command of a home are source examples of authority; ABC adds collective leadership. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Supervising hourly work, independent work, priority treatment, and important collective actions. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Authority and prominence are stated; discipline, fairness, and effective management are not guaranteed by either entry.

## Contradictions

No explicit mutually exclusive claim is apparent. Witte's household and workplace examples and ABC's national leadership examples place authority at different scales.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair's labor and action through supervision, authority, or special importance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes this concrete in a foreman, female executive, or woman commanding the household; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes independent work and collective leadership. The shared theme is who directs or has priority in the activity, without assuming that prominence ensures competent or fair leadership.
