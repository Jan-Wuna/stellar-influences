## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 128; one-based PDF p. 124, Neptune row checked.

#### Witte Entry

To be asleep or awake regardless of day or night. Woman infected with venereal diseases. Irregular working time. To do without.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 181; one-based PDF p. 89, Neptune row checked through its final work-uncertainty clause, before Pluto.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: Sensitive women's work; women's work in the air or in water; a woman's emotional impulses; a people working by water; an hour of negating work; unclear collective will; uncertain emotional action; a feeling of uncertainty at work.

The following Pluto row beginning “Entfaltung des Volks-Willens” is excluded. Arbeits-Verneinung is retained literally as negating work; its actor or form is not specified.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 120; one-based PDF p. 122, entry 0357 (Neptune), checked.

#### Ebertin Entry

A negative attitude or character, weakness of will, tendency to worry a lot, the state of fretfulness and of grief, misdirection of energy. A misdirected emotional life, diseased procreative organs (infection), renunciation. Undermining circumstances affecting the family life detrimentally.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 29, exact Neptune paragraph checked; chapter begins on p. 28.

#### Sandbach Entry

To become emotionally fired up by one’s imaginings. Idealism expressed with an emotional forcefulness. A tendency to psychically pick up on the strong emotions of other people and possibly to experience emotional agitation from that. Assertively nur turing others in a selfless manner.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `112` (printed); one-based PDF p. 113, Nep paragraph checked.

#### Munkasey Entry

Disguising the causes of common complaints; becoming adept at soothing the animosity family members exhibit; enlightening others who may panic or become angry in unfamiliar situations; anger at deceitful psychics.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sensitive or ideal-driven action, with uncertainty, irregular work and possible misdirection. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- psychology: Imagination and others' strong emotions can stir action; Ebertin also gives worry, grief and weakened will. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- body/health: Witte names altered waking/sleeping rhythms and venereal disease; Ebertin names infection of procreative organs. These are attributed historical correspondences, not diagnoses. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- social/relationship: Selfless nurturing and soothing family animosity coexist with detrimental family circumstances. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- events/manifestations: Irregular hours, work in air or water, uncertain collective action, disguising complaints and responding to panic or deceit. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- conflicts/notes: The corrected ABC has eight Neptune clauses; the next Pluto development row supplies no Neptune claim. Work-negation remains literal and unspecified.

## Contradictions

Ebertin's weak or misdirected energy does not exhaust the range: Sandbach permits forceful idealism and selfless nurturing, while Munkasey permits soothing others' anger. These possibilities remain beside the difficult expressions. ABC's work-negation clause does not identify who refuses or negates the work.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Neptune`, Neptune specifies emotionally charged action through sensitivity, imagination and uncertainty about where effort leads. One may become stirred by ideals or other people's feelings, act in selfless care, or help soothe family anger; the same entries also allow worry, misdirected energy, irregular work and unclear collective will. These possibilities are grounded in the [ABC](#abc-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) passages.

The orientation therefore includes both responsive involvement and difficulty keeping action clearly directed. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supplies concrete irregular working and waking hours; ABC additionally retains literal work in air or water. Neither setting should be replaced by a purely psychological reading.
