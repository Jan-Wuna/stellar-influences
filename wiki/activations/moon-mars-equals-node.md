## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 128; one-based PDF p. 124, Node row checked.

#### Witte Entry

Connections though the same work relations. Connections with women or soldiers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 180; one-based PDF p. 89, complete Node row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: Job placement for women; connections with a people's productive activity; emotionally engaged relationships at work; establishing connections among women; connected with a people's will; mediation of working hours; establishing connections between peoples.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 121; one-based PDF p. 123, entry 0359 (Node), checked.

#### Ebertin Entry

Energetic or vigorous cooperation with others. An association of women, an organization consisting only of female members.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 29, exact Node paragraph checked; chapter begins on p. 28.

#### Sandbach Entry

The need to grow in one’s ability to let out one’s feelings in a positive and lifesupporting manner. Anger pertaining to the past and old situations. Growing and evolving through emotional conflicts.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `112` (printed); one-based PDF p. 113, Nod paragraph checked.

#### Munkasey Entry

You meet people who express anger thru their emotions; you shy away from those who are emotionally assertive; encountering kind words given in haste from others; meeting people who come to give complaints.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Emotionally engaged connections, shared work and vigorous cooperation. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- psychology: Positive expression of feeling develops alongside anger from past situations and reluctance around emotionally assertive people. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health outcome is stated in these activation entries.
- social/relationship: Work contacts, connections with women or soldiers, a women's association, and ties between peoples are explicit source settings. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- events/manifestations: Job placement, mediation of work hours, cooperative activity, complaints and kind words spoken hastily. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Contact need not mean harmonious closeness: cooperation, anger, retreat and brief kindness remain different expressions.

## Contradictions

Energetic cooperation in Ebertin and withdrawal from emotionally assertive people in Munkasey describe different relational responses. Sandbach adds learning through emotional conflict. ABC does not explain the precise arrangement meant by mediation of working hours; that phrase remains literal.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Node`, Node specifies emotionally charged activity through work contacts and acting with other people. Shared work can organize vigorous feeling into cooperation, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the connections to job placement for women, mediation of working hours and ties between peoples. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) retain work relations and particular associations as concrete settings.

These contacts also bring emotional demands. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) includes anger, complaints and retreat from assertive people, but also kind words given hastily. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes developing a more positive expression of feeling and working through anger from old situations. Cooperation and conflict therefore both belong to the orientation.
