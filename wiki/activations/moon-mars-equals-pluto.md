## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 128; one-based PDF p. 124, Pluto row checked.

#### Witte Entry

Hourly schedule. Work schedule. Changes in working conditions. To establish the home by and by.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 181; one-based PDF p. 89, full seven-clause Pluto row checked from “Entfaltung des Volks-Willens” through “Willens-Wechsel der Frau”, before Cupido.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: Unfolding a people's will; development of the masculine principle in a woman; developing feelingful action; changes in a woman's activity; changes to working hours; a woman's increasing activities; changes in a woman's will.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed pp. 120–121; one-based PDF pp. 122–123, entry 0358 (Pluto) crossing the page boundary; complete entry checked before Node0359.

#### Ebertin Entry

One-sided outlook, fanaticism, moodiness. A woman who stands alone in the world, periodic disturbances within the female organism.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 29, exact Pluto paragraph checked; chapter begins on p. 28.

#### Sandbach Entry

An intimidating emotional forcefulness which may have drastic results. An intensely passionate nature that may intimidate others. The need to channel one’s neg ative feelings into constructive channels. The relentless and irresistible urge to act on instinct.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `112` (printed); one-based PDF p. 113, Plu paragraph checked.

#### Munkasey Entry

Eliminating the anger or irritation which accompanies complaints; intensified defiance during periods of uncertainty; troubles which arise when your imagination is overstimulated; wasting common sources of energy.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Changing patterns of work and emotionally driven action, alongside intense or compelling feeling. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- psychology: Intimidating passion and a relentless urge to act coexist with one-sidedness, moodiness, defiance and overstimulated imagination. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- body/health: Ebertin names periodic disturbances within the female organism; this remains an attributed historical correspondence, not a diagnosis. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- social/relationship: The entries include intimidating others, handling complaints, a woman standing alone, and gradually establishing a home. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry).
- events/manifestations: Changed hours and working conditions, increasing activity, unfolding collective will, eliminating irritation or wasting shared energy. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Practical change does not necessarily imply a crisis. The complete seven-clause ABC Pluto row is distinct from Neptune's preceding paragraph.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes schedules, changed work and gradual home establishment, while Sandbach and Ebertin describe intense or difficult emotional expressions. Munkasey includes both eliminating irritation and intensified defiance. These different possibilities should remain visible without making every change destructive.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair through changes in activity and the force with which feeling presses toward action. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [ABC](#abc-entry) entries make change concrete in working hours, a woman's developing activity, collective will and the gradual establishment of a home. The formula therefore retains ordinary practical development alongside its more intense emotional expressions.

[Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes passion that may intimidate others and an insistent urge to act on instinct, while also naming the need for constructive channels for negative feelings. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) adds one-sidedness and moodiness; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) allows both removing irritation and intensifying defiance or wasting energy. The sources leave the direction of that force open.
