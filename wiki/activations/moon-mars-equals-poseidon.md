## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 128; one-based PDF p. 124, complete Poseidon row checked.

#### Witte Entry

The working hours or evening hours are devoted to mental matters. Working times in spiritual profession.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 181; one-based PDF p. 89, complete eight-clause Poseidon row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: An insightful working hour; feelingful, insightful action; women's intellectual work; a reasonable woman's actions; clear collective will; a woman's cultural work; creating a people's culture; an hour of activity concerned with understanding.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Work and emotionally informed action directed toward understanding, mental matters or culture. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Insight, reason and clarity of collective will are explicit in ABC; no broader personality profile is supplied. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health condition occurs in either entry.
- social/relationship: ABC names women's intellectual and cultural activity and the creation of a people's culture. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Working or evening hours devoted to mental matters, work in a spiritual profession, and cultural creation. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: The entries describe the subject of work and action. Disciplined service or a general elevation of instinct is not stated.

## Contradictions

No mutually exclusive claim is apparent. Witte emphasizes working time devoted to mental or spiritual matters; ABC adds reason, insight, clear collective will and cultural creation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair's work and emotionally informed action through thought, insight and cultural activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places mental matters in working or evening hours and names work in a spiritual profession. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds insightful action, reason, a clear collective will, and cultural work by a woman or a people. The connecting theme is activity directed toward understanding and cultural expression, with both individual and collective applications.
