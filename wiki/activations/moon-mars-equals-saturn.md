## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 128; one-based PDF p. 124, Saturn row checked.

#### Witte Entry

The interrupted, disturbed working time which causes anger and disgust. The sick wife. The separated home. The separated wife. The dead wife.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 181; one-based PDF p. 89, complete nine-clause Saturn row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: A woman's loss of work; dealing with women's problems; disturbed emotional actions; concentrated collective will; a hard working hour; a woman's pain at work; difficult women's work; an older woman's activity; a woman's acts of separation.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 120; one-based PDF p. 122, entry 0355 (Saturn), checked.

#### Ebertin Entry

Inhibitions, suppressed feelings, controlled passion, a state of dissatisfaction or of unsatisfied desire, a lack of interest with regard to one's work, a quarrel. The suffering or separated wife, separation from the wife (or mother).

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 29, exact Saturn paragraph checked; chapter begins on p. 28.

#### Sandbach Entry

Repressing one’s passionate feelings, and/or being cautious in the expressing of them. If this is done to excess it could pressurize one feelings and cause them to come out in an explosive manner. A slow, controlled expression of anger.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `112` (printed); one-based PDF p. 113, Sat paragraph checked.

#### Munkasey Entry

Maternal panic felt and exhibited in isolation; the responsibility you undertake for correcting uncertainties about products or activities; obligations you incur toward activities involving showing caring or concern for others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Emotional action constrained by interruption, restraint, separation or responsibility. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- psychology: Suppressed passion, dissatisfaction, caution, slow anger and isolated maternal panic are stated; Sandbach makes explosive release conditional on excessive repression. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- body/health: Witte names a sick or dead wife, and ABC names pain at work. These remain attributed historical examples, not diagnoses or predictions. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- social/relationship: Separation from a wife or mother and obligations to care for others are distinct source settings. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- events/manifestations: Interrupted working hours, loss or difficulty of work, concentrated collective will, an older woman's activity and correcting uncertain products or activities. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Controlled expression and concentrated action remain beside disruption and loss; restraint is not automatically either effective discipline or destructive repression.

## Contradictions

Sandbach distinguishes cautious or slow expression from repression taken to an explosive extreme. ABC also retains concentrated will and an older woman's activity, which do not by themselves assert harm. Those possibilities remain beside the entries' difficult work and separation examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Saturn`, Saturn specifies emotionally charged action through interruption, restraint and demands of responsibility. Working time may be disturbed, interest in work may weaken, or passionate feeling may be withheld. [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) give concrete work and separation settings; they also retain concentrated action and controlled passion.

How feeling is held matters in [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry): caution or slow anger differs from repression carried so far that it builds pressure toward an outburst. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds the obligations of caring and correcting uncertain products or activities, alongside maternal panic felt in isolation. The orientation can therefore involve effort under emotional constraint without reducing every expression to either endurance or frustration.
