## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 128; one-based PDF p. 124, Sun row checked.

#### Witte Entry

The time of work during the day. The daily work. An active man. The man's wife.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 180; one-based PDF p. 89, full seven-clause Sun row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: A working hour during the day; a man's efforts toward a woman; a man's feelingful actions; a woman's actions for a man; an impulse-giving center for the people; the masculine principle in female persons; productive members of a people.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 120; one-based PDF p. 122, entry 0351 (Sun), checked.

#### Ebertin Entry

A strong feeling of solidarity between husband and wife, particularly felt by the latter. The qualities of a homemaker, the power to establish oneself in the world, striving for independence, consciousness of an objective. The union between husband and wife. Prosperity through harmonious cooperation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 28, exact Sun paragraph checked; the chapter begins on this page.

#### Sandbach Entry

The need and desire to channel one’s passions into meaningful endeavors. One feels passionate about any goals one adopts and can have strong feelings about what ever one thinks is important. Strong emotional energy fuels the pursuit of one’s goals.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `112` (printed); one-based PDF p. 113, Sun paragraph checked.

#### Munkasey Entry

Violent influences which may lead to rioting; the importance of placing energy into imaginative enterprises; determination to end conflicts about your initiatives or intentions; intentions to control nagging uncertainties.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Emotional energy directed into daily work, conscious aims and shared activity. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- psychology: Passion for meaningful goals, striving for independence and determination to resolve conflict or uncertainty. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: Ebertin names solidarity and harmonious cooperation between husband and wife; ABC gives actions directed between a man and a woman. These are the sources' gendered settings. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Daytime work, homemaking, imaginative enterprise and prosperity through cooperation occur beside Munkasey's conditional violent influence that may lead to rioting. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Harmonious cooperation and destructive collective action are different source possibilities; neither defines every expression of this formula.

## Contradictions

Ebertin's solidarity and harmonious cooperation are favorable expressions, while Munkasey retains conflicts and a possibility of rioting. Sandbach describes emotional energy directed toward whatever goals are adopted, so the goal's value is not established merely by the strength of feeling.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through purposeful activity: daily work, establishing oneself, pursuing an objective and investing strong feeling in meaningful endeavors. [Witte](#witte-entry) keeps the daily work setting explicit; [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) adds homemaking, independence and marital solidarity, and [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes passion fueling adopted goals. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends action between people to a productive collective setting.

The force of that commitment can enter imaginative enterprises or efforts to end conflicts, but [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) also retains violent influences that may lead to rioting and concern about uncertain intentions. Cooperative purpose and conflict therefore remain possible directions of the same emotionally invested activity.
