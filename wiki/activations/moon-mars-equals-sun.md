## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Moon + Mars`
- Source page: `124`
#### Witte Entry

The time of work during the day. The daily work. An active man. The mans wife.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Moon/Mars`
- Source page: `120`
- Entry: `0351`

#### Ebertin Entry

A strong feeling of solidarity between husband and wife, particularly felt by the latter. The qualities of a homemaker, the power to establish oneself in the world, striving for independence, consciousness of an objective. The union between husband and wife. Prosperity through harmonious cooperation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Moon/Mars midpoint`
- Source page: `28`
- Activator: `Sun`

#### Sandbach Entry

The need and desire to channel one’s passions into meaningful endeavors. One feels passionate about any goals one adopts and can have strong feelings about what ever one thinks is important. Strong emotional energy fuels the pursuit of one’s goals.
