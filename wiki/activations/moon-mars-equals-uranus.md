## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden changes in working schedules. Unusual working time. Sudden interference in the working time conditions. The willful woman. The hot tempered woman. Excitement about the keeping of the home. The armed, rebellious or uproarious populace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

work reform for women, exciting emotional actions, dynamic will of a woman, eventful work hour, excited will of the people, sudden actions of a people, technical women's work, and reform actions for a people

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Quick irritability, a quick and violent temper, inclination to give back as well as to take or to hit back at once under provocation, an irascible nature prone to sudden anger. A sudden experience in a woman's life.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A sudden, surprising and/or unexpected display or forceful or passionate feeling. A genius for stirring up others emotionally. To suddenly act on inspiration. To be inspired by a gut feeling about what needs to be done.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Appearing dispassionate during angry emotional outbursts; detachment of self from suspected animosity or irritation; the emotional energy you use to practice astrology; irregular attempts at or with home repairs.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Mars = Uranus`: rapid mobilization, emotional breakthrough, and the ability to act instantly when old patterns break open. Main caution: explosive reactivity: upset and force can become erratic, accident-prone, or too volatile for sustained trust.
