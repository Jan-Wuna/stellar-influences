## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 128; one-based PDF p. 124, Venus row checked.

#### Witte Entry

Agreeable working time and conditions without time clock. The passionate wife. Motherhood. A beautiful home that is taken care of.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 180; one-based PDF p. 89, full eight-clause Venus row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: A woman's joy in work; a people's will for peace; activating harmonious feelings; a woman's harmonious actions; a woman's artistic work; a woman's loving actions; the masculine principle in a beautiful woman; a beautiful woman's energy for action.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 120; one-based PDF p. 122, entry 0353 (Venus), checked.

#### Ebertin Entry

A passionate nature, sex desire, desire for children. The wish to become a mother, creative activity, urge to create, shape or mold something.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 28, exact Venus paragraph checked; the chapter begins on this page.

#### Sandbach Entry

The tendency to be frank and direct in the expression of love. The ability to express forceful emotions in a smooth and even alluring way. Love expressed in an impulsive and/or fiery and passionate manner. A love of emotional conflict.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `112` (printed); one-based PDF p. 113, Ven paragraph checked.

#### Munkasey Entry

Softening the angry emotional feelings you sometimes feel; increased stimulation of your imaginative side for producing art or musical works; appreciating or consoling the emotional nature or ups and downs of others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affection, pleasure and creative expression within emotionally driven action. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- psychology: Direct or alluring passion, creative desire, softened anger and a possible enjoyment of emotional conflict. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- body/health: Ebertin names sexual desire and wanting children or motherhood; Witte names motherhood. These are attributed examples and wishes, not proof of fertility or pregnancy. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry).
- social/relationship: A passionate wife, a cared-for home, loving action and consoling other people's emotional ups and downs are explicit settings. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- events/manifestations: Agreeable working conditions without a time clock, art or music, creating and shaping, and a collective will for peace. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Harmony and affection remain beside Sandbach's love of emotional conflict; intensity does not establish jealousy.

## Contradictions

ABC names peace and harmonious feeling, while Sandbach retains a love of emotional conflict. Munkasey supplies soothing and consoling expressions. These different possibilities prevent a reading of either unbroken harmony or inevitable friction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Venus`, Venus specifies emotionally charged action through affection, pleasure and the wish to create. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [ABC](#abc-entry) entries connect this with agreeable work, loving or artistic activity and a home that is cared for. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) retains both sexual or parental wishes and the broader urge to shape or make something.

The emotional force can be softened into art, music or consolation in [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), yet it need not become placid. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes direct, fiery or alluring expressions of love and even enjoyment of emotional conflict. Tenderness, creative work and passionate tension therefore remain within the source range.
