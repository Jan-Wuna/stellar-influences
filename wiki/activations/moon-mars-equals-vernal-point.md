## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Work and emotionally driven action in public life. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: ABC explicitly places the activation of feeling and collective will in public; no fuller personality account is supplied. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health condition occurs in either entry.
- social/relationship: Female workers, a publicly active woman and collective activity are named source settings. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Public works, general daily working hours and conditions, and publicly expressed activity or will. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: The entries identify a public setting without specifying a political cause, rank or degree of fame.

## Contradictions

No mutually exclusive claim is apparent. Witte emphasizes public work, workers and daily conditions; ABC also makes feeling and collective will publicly active.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the pair's work and action in public life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names public works, workers, daily working conditions and the force of the people. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds publicly activated feelings, women acting in public and a people's will expressed there. The common thread is the public setting of daily effort and collective emotional activity.
