## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 128; one-based PDF p. 124, complete Vulcanus row checked.

#### Witte Entry

Heavy demands on the working time. Overtime. The wife who is in command at home. The big household. Manifestation of power of a nation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 181; one-based PDF p. 89, complete Vulcanus row checked; wording retained.

#### ABC Entry

influence through emotional actions, influence of the will of the people, energetic actions of the woman, active influence of the woman, strong masculine principle in the woman, strength of action of an energetic woman, power of a creative people, and strength of will of a people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Heavy work demands and forceful influence through emotional action or collective will. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Willpower and influence through feeling are named; the entries do not supply a general personality or stamina assessment. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health condition is stated; overtime alone does not establish exhaustion or endurance.
- social/relationship: Witte names a wife commanding the home and a large household; both entries include national or collective power. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Overtime, heavy demands on working time, energetic activity and the influence of collective will. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Greater demands and stronger influence do not establish an unlimited ability to carry work or control its outcome.

## Contradictions

No explicit mutually exclusive claim is apparent. Witte gives heavy working-time demands and commanding household or national examples; ABC emphasizes influence and energetic action at individual and collective scales.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mars = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through heavy demands on activity and the power of feeling or will to influence others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes the work burden concrete in overtime and a large household, and retains commanding domestic and national examples. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds energetic action, influence through feeling and collective willpower. The shared emphasis is the weight and reach of action, with demands on effort kept distinct from any assurance that they can be met.
