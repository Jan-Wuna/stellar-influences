## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 60, Admetos row under Meridian + Moon; complete row checked.

#### Witte Entry

To be closed, refusing or narrow-minded. Immobile in personal emotions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 65; one-based PDF p. 31, complete eight-clause Admetos row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: Blockage of the soul and emotional life; my wife's depressions; I restrict my feelings; my people are restricted; my wife is thorough; my deep feelings; deepening of the feminine soul; inner depth of feeling.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restricted or immobile personal feeling, with emotional depth also represented. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Closed, refusing or narrow-minded attitudes occur beside deliberate restriction of feeling, thoroughness and deep feeling. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: ABC names a wife's depressions; this remains an attributed source example, not a clinical diagnosis. No bodily condition is specified. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- social/relationship: The wife and a restricted people are the entry's particular relational and collective examples. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Restricting one's feelings and deepening inner emotional experience are stated; no specific occupation or life event is established. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Depth and thoroughness should not automatically be recast as narrow-mindedness or illness.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes closedness and immobility, while ABC also retains thoroughness and depth. These additional meanings prevent an exclusively negative reading. The source gives no basis for treating emotional depth itself as clinical depression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/MC = Admetos`, Admetos specifies inner feeling through restriction, fixedness and depth. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) stresses being closed or refusing and immobile in personal emotions. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes restricting one's own feelings, but also thoroughness, deep feeling and a deepening of the feminine soul. The orientation therefore retains a distinction between a narrowed emotional response and inward depth; the entries do not make those the same condition.
