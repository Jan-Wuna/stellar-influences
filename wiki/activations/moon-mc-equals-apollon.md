## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 60, complete Apollon row under Meridian + Moon checked.

#### Witte Entry

To be broad-minded and big-hearted. Frank. One’s emotional attitude towards peace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 65; one-based PDF p. 31, complete nine-clause Apollon row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: Expansion of a woman's feelings; my success as a woman; my success with a woman; my knowledge through feeling; my people are successful; my knowledge about a woman; my successful wife; my feelings of freedom; knowledge of the feminine psyche.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Breadth and generosity of personal feeling, with freedom, knowledge and success also represented. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Broad-mindedness, frankness, a peaceful emotional attitude and knowledge through feeling are stated. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition or diagnosis.
- social/relationship: ABC gives particular first-person examples involving a woman, wife and a successful people; these remain the source's examples. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Personal and relational success, expansion of a woman's feelings and feelings of freedom appear; no specific occupation or public role is established. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: The entries express possibilities and attitudes, without making success inevitable or identifying knowledge through feeling with a special ability.

## Contradictions

The entries are compatible in their emphasis on emotional breadth. ABC adds knowledge, freedom and first-person examples of success. Its gendered examples remain attributed and do not define a universal role for women or wives.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/MC = Apollon`, Apollon gives personal emotional life breadth, openness and room to expand. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes broad-mindedness, a big heart, frankness and an emotional attitude toward peace. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds expansion of feeling, knowledge through feeling and feelings of freedom, alongside particular examples of personal, relational and collective success. Together these support generous and expansive inner feeling; the success examples do not establish a guaranteed outcome or a specific public vocation.
