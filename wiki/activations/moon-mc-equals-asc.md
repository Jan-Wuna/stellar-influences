## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 60, complete Asc row under Meridian + Moon checked.

#### Witte Entry

One’s mental attitude toward others. The psychological or emotional influence of women in the near environment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 64; one-based PDF p. 31, complete nine-clause Asc row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: Soul-filled feelings of the partner; a soul-filled hour with the partner; the soul of other peoples; the surroundings of my people; feelings in a soulful environment; the emotional nature and soul of others; my place among the people; my wife as partner; the feminine soul at a place.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 144; one-based PDF p. 146, coded entry `0457`, complete entry checked.

#### Ebertin Entry

An emotional attitude to one's environment, comprehension of other people's spiritual needs.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 41, complete Ascendant paragraph under the Moon/Midheaven chapter beginning on PDF p. 40 checked.

#### Sandbach Entry

One identifies with exalted emotions. One plays the role of nurturing parent in life. In relationships one has a need for the other person to acknowledge and re spect one’s feelings. The strength and health of one’s identity depends on one's emo tional security.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `144`; one-based PDF p. 145, complete `ASC` row checked.

#### Munkasey Entry

Personal motivations to be like (or opposite to) mother, and to adopt (or discard) the family values acquired earlier; increases your drive to understand others thru the role models your mother provided you about life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal feeling in relation to other people and the immediate environment. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- psychology: Understanding others' spiritual needs, identifying with exalted emotion and seeking emotional security are stated. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- body/health: Sandbach's strength and health of identity concerns emotional security; no bodily diagnosis is specified. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- social/relationship: A partner, the surrounding people, nurturing relationships and the need for feelings to be respected appear. Maternal role models can be adopted or rejected. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- events/manifestations: Playing a nurturing-parent role and choosing how to relate to inherited family values are source examples. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Nurturing and emotional understanding do not establish that others can read one's life path or that approval must control self-presentation.

## Contradictions

The entries share an interpersonal emotional focus. Munkasey explicitly allows both likeness and opposition to the mother, and both adoption and rejection of family values. The gendered and maternal examples are attributed possibilities rather than required identities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/MC = Asc`, personal emotional life takes shape through encounters with others and the surrounding environment. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) entries join an emotional attitude toward others with understanding their spiritual needs; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes partner feelings and a place among a people. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) develops nurturing, emotional security and the wish for feelings to be acknowledged. The [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry) adds a choice about inherited maternal models: understanding others can involve taking up those family values or departing from them. These strands support attentive emotional relating without making family conformity the only outcome.
