## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 60, complete Cupido row under Meridian + Moon checked.

#### Witte Entry

The community sense is directed by the mind. Marital soul harmony.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 65; one-based PDF p. 31, complete eight-clause Cupido row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: Soulful art by a woman; a soulful community of a people; an emotional community of souls; together with my wife; the art of my people; my feelings in the community; my people's community; a feminine community of souls.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal feeling in community and marital emotional harmony. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: The mind directs community feeling, while personal feelings enter a shared emotional life. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition or diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Marriage, being together with one's wife and communities of people or souls are stated examples. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: A woman's art and the art of one's people connect emotional life with shared artistic expression. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: The wife and feminine-community examples remain attributed; they do not require marriage or a prescribed family role.

## Contradictions

Both entries connect personal feeling with community. ABC adds artistic and collective examples beyond Witte's marital harmony. Neither entry establishes an inevitable life direction or a universal family role.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/MC = Cupido`, inner feeling is expressed through togetherness, community and shared art. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a community sense directed by the mind and harmony within marriage. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens the setting to personal feelings in community, a people's art and emotionally connected groups. These support an emotional life that finds expression in shared belonging and artistic participation, with marriage as one particular source example.
