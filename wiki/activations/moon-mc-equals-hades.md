## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 60, complete Hades row under Meridian + Moon checked.

#### Witte Entry

Vileness of women. A low mentality. Trouble with women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 65; one-based PDF p. 31, complete nine-clause Hades row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: A woman in psychological danger; lack of feeling in the soul; feelings from my past; I renounce feelings; my troubled emotional nature; my sorrow involving the woman; my wife is criminal; my criminal feelings; my hidden feelings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Troubled, withheld or hidden personal feeling. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Feelings from the past, renouncing feelings, emotional lack and a troubled inner state are distinct examples. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: ABC mentions psychological danger but provides no diagnosis; emotional difficulty is not itself evidence of illness. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- social/relationship: The entries include sorrow or trouble involving women. Their pejorative and criminal descriptions remain attributed historical claims, not judgments about a person. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Emotional renunciation and concealed feelings are stated; criminality is a particular source example and cannot be inferred from this formula. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Emotional lack, concealment, sorrow and the source's moral judgments should not be treated as interchangeable.

## Contradictions

Witte uses strongly pejorative language about women. ABC also contains criminal claims but gives broader examples of past, hidden and renounced feelings. These are preserved as attributed source claims; they do not justify a general judgment about women or criminal behavior.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/MC = Hades`, the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes personal feeling through emotional lack, memories of the past, renunciation, sorrow and concealment. These give a more differentiated account of inner difficulty than a single label of melancholy. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) adds trouble involving women but frames it in pejorative historical language. The usable shared focus is troubled emotional life; the sources' moral and criminal claims remain attributed examples and do not establish character, diagnosis or conduct.
