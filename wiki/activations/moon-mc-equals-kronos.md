## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 60, complete Kronos row under Meridian + Moon checked.

#### Witte Entry

One who possesses good mental qualities. Magnanimity. Generosity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 65; one-based PDF p. 31, complete nine-clause Kronos row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: The distinctive quality of the feminine psyche; I feel independent; my great people; my people possess standing; my wife possesses authority; my great feelings; my independent wife; my foremost feelings; a woman's inner greatness.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Quality and largeness of inner life, with independence, standing and authority also represented. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Good mental qualities, magnanimity, generosity, feeling independent and giving certain feelings precedence are stated. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition or diagnosis.
- social/relationship: ABC gives examples of an independent wife with authority and a people with standing. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Personal independence and collective standing are examples; no particular profession, office or leadership event is specified. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Independence and standing are distinct from the qualities of generosity and magnanimity; neither guarantees the other.

## Contradictions

The entries are compatible but differ in emphasis: Witte gives qualities of mind, while ABC adds independence, authority and collective standing. The gendered and collective examples remain attributed rather than universal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/MC = Kronos`, the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes personal inner quality through magnanimity and generosity. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds independence, large or foremost feelings and particular examples of authority and standing. The orientation therefore brings together a generous inner attitude and a sense of emotional independence or importance. It does not establish a public office or prove that authority is always exercised with good judgment.
