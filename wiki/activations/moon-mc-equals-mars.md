## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 60, complete Mars row under Meridian + Moon checked.

#### Witte Entry

Sexual stimulation of women. One who is able to be firm. Made up mind. The masculinity. The attitude of a soldier.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 64; one-based PDF p. 31, complete nine-clause Mars row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: My wife's work; soulful creative work by women; emotional soul-work; my actions arising from feeling; my will as a woman; my activity as a woman; inner emotional impulses; the vigor of my people; feminine soul-work.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 143; one-based PDF p. 145, coded entry `0450`, complete entry checked.

#### Ebertin Entry

Devotion to a task or cause both in body and soul, acting on emotion or according to feeling, industrious, spirit of endeavor. Longing of the soul for union with a partner, man's desire to possess a woman's soul.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 40, complete Mars paragraph under the Moon/Midheaven chapter beginning on PDF p. 40 checked.

#### Sandbach Entry

To feel anger if one’s feelings are not acknowledged or respected. One is assertive about playing a nurturing role in the life of another person and/or people in general. The desire to take action to alleviate suffering and to bring greater comfort and security to other people.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `144`; one-based PDF p. 145, complete `Mars` row checked.

#### Munkasey Entry

Anger you experience due to pressures to conform to maternal rules which others impose; insistence that you adopt maternal roles in life; persistence that others show in pushing on you their ideas of how you should develop.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Feeling translated into will, effort and action. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- psychology: Firmness, a decided mind, devotion to a task and anger when feelings are disregarded are distinct possibilities. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- body/health: Witte names sexual stimulation; no bodily diagnosis is established. [Witte](#witte-entry).
- social/relationship: Nurturing and bringing comfort occur alongside pressure to conform to maternal roles. Ebertin's possessive gendered example remains attributed. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- events/manifestations: Work, committed endeavor and acting to relieve suffering are stated. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Freely chosen care differs from an imposed caring role; neither general public success nor a combative career follows from the entries.

## Contradictions

Sandbach describes assertive nurturing, while Munkasey describes anger at pressure to adopt maternal roles and others' expectations. Ebertin's longing for union and possessive gendered language are retained as source examples, without equating closeness with ownership.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/MC = Mars`, personal feeling becomes a motive for work, resolve and action. The [ABC](#abc-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) entries emphasize emotional impulses, effort and devotion, while the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) adds firmness and a made-up mind. This energy can go toward the [Sandbach entry's](#sandbach-entry) active nurturing and relief of suffering, but anger also appears when feelings are disregarded. The [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry) gives a different relational pressure: others may insist on maternal roles or dictate how one should develop. The explanation therefore keeps committed care alongside resistance to an imposed role.
