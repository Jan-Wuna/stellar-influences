## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sexual stimulation of women. One who is able to be firm. Made up mind. The masculinity. The attitude of a soldier.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My wife's work, a woman's creation, a soul's work, my actions, my will as a woman, my work as a woman, my soul's impulses, the power of action of my people, and my female soul's work

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Devotion to a task or cause both in body and soul, acting on emotion or according to feeling, industrious, spirit of endeavor. Longing of the soul for union with apartner, man's desire to possess a woman's soul,

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To feel anger if one’s feelings are not acknowledged or respected. One is assertive about playing a nurturing role in the life of another person and/or people in general. The desire to take action to alleviate suffering and to bring greater comfort and security to other people.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Anger you experience due to pressures to conform to maternal rules which others impose; insistence that you adopt maternal roles in life; persistence that others show in pushing on you their ideas of how you should develop.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/MC = Mars`: energetic public engagement, decisive caring, and the ability to mobilize from gut-level conviction. Main caution: reactive direction: career and status can become too combative, hurried, or mood-charged.
