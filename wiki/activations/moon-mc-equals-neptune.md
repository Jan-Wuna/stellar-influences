## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 60, complete Neptune row under Meridian + Moon checked.

#### Witte Entry

To be annoyed by women. Women who are deceitful. Straying fancy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 65; one-based PDF p. 31, complete eight-clause Neptune row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: A disappointed soul of a people; sensitivity of the feminine soul; I feel unsure; my uncertain people; my disappointment as a woman; a sensitive feminine psyche; weaknesses of a feelingful soul; a refined feelingful soul.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 143; one-based PDF p. 145, coded entry `0454`, complete entry checked.

#### Ebertin Entry

False emotional conceptions or pictures, inclination to torment oneself and to worry unnecessarily, tendency to lose hope easily. Experiencing falsehood and deceitfulness, vexations or troubles through members of the female sex.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 41, complete Neptune paragraph under the Moon/Midheaven chapter beginning on PDF p. 40 checked.

#### Sandbach Entry

Feelings concerning one’s relationship to the world are confusing, overly idealistic, or unrealistic, often involving fantasies of fulfillment that ignore problems and challenges. Or, one has profound intuitive insight due to one’s being receptive to higher spiritual energies. Emotional security comes through holding steadily to exalted ideals.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `144`; one-based PDF p. 145, complete `Nep` row checked.

#### Munkasey Entry

Idealistic or unclear images about who you are or how you can grow; brings confusion over your role for supporting others in life; questions about where your identity begins and where the person mother wants ends.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sensitive personal feeling alongside uncertainty, idealization and disappointment. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- psychology: Misleading emotional pictures and worry coexist with refined sensitivity; Sandbach also describes intuitive spiritual receptivity. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- body/health: The entries describe emotional states without establishing a bodily diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Confusion can concern supporting others or distinguishing personal identity from maternal expectations. Deceitful-women claims remain attributed historical examples. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry).
- events/manifestations: Disappointment, uncertainty about growth and holding steadily to ideals are stated possibilities; no artistic or caring occupation is specified. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Sensitivity is not automatically deception or weakness; Sandbach explicitly presents intuitive insight as an alternative to unrealistic fantasy.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize misleading pictures and trouble, including gendered claims about deceit. ABC also gives sensitivity and refinement, while Sandbach preserves intuitive spiritual insight alongside unrealistic fantasy. These distinctions prevent either an exclusively negative account or an assured spiritual ability.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/MC = Neptune`, personal emotional orientation may become uncertain, overly idealized or disappointed. The [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) entries describe unreliable inner pictures and questions about identity, growth and supporting others. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also retains sensitivity and refinement, and the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) explicitly offers intuitive spiritual receptivity as an alternative to fantasy that overlooks difficulties. The orientation therefore holds emotional uncertainty beside finer receptivity, with each remaining a source possibility. Historical accusations about women are attributed rather than adopted as a general judgment.
