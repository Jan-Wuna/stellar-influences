- Source heading: `Moon/Medium Coeli`

#### Ebertin Entry

False emotional conceptions or pictures, inclination to torment oneself and to worry unnecessarily, tendency to lose hope easily. Experiencing falsehood and deceitfulness, vexations or troubles through members of the female sex.

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Moon/Midheaven midpoint`
