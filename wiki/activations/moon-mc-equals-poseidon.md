### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 65; one-based PDF p. 31, complete eleven-clause Poseidon row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Inspired folk culture, feeling for spirit and soul, emotional spirit soul, spiritual female soul, spirit of my woman, I possess clarity as a woman, I feel the truth, clarity of my woman, culture of my people, reason of my woman, and truth about the soul of the woman

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A spiritual or mental orientation within personal emotional life, with clarity, truth and culture. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Feeling for spirit and soul, a sense of truth and clarity are stated. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition or diagnosis.
- social/relationship: ABC gives a woman's or wife's spirit, clarity and reason, alongside the culture of one's people. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Inspired collective culture is represented. Witte's gifted-medium claim remains an attributed spiritual example. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Feeling that something is true is a source theme, not independent proof of truth or a demonstrated special ability.

## Contradictions

The entries share spiritual and mental themes. ABC also includes reason, clarity and collective culture; these should not be reduced to Witte's mediumship example. Gendered examples remain source-specific.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/MC = Poseidon`, personal feeling is related to spirit, understanding and clarity. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) moves between feeling for spirit and soul, a sense of truth, reason and the culture of a people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a spiritual-mental attitude and gives a gifted medium as one particular example. These support a spiritual or reflective emotional orientation without establishing unseen guidance, a particular life direction or an objectively verified gift.
