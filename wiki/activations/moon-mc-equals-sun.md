## Identity

- Formula: `Moon/MC = Sun`
- Astronomicon formula: `R/MC = S`
- Axis page: [Moon/MC](../axes/moon-mc.md)

## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The mental influence on the body. The glandular system. Daily mental and emotional influences. The body condition is influenced by the state of mind.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

The soul of a man

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A masculine soul, a positive attitude to life, vigor, enterprising spirit, tie or attachment of the ego both in body and soul, a strong soul link with the mother or wife.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s purpose in life centers on clearing the emotional body so that exalted feelings may shine forth. The ability to remain constant and relatively undisturbed by the ever-changing conditions that occur in one’s career.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `144`

#### Munkasey Entry

Defining your own character and growing beyond early family influences; the purpose and determination you set for yourself in defining who you are and what you will do with your life; the importance of family identification.
