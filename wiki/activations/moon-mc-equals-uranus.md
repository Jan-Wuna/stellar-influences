- Source heading: `Moon/Medium Coeli`
- Source page: `143`
- Entry: `0453`

#### Ebertin Entry

An unusual degree of emotional excitability, irritability, nervousness. Emotional crises, upsets through members of the female sex, an unusual emotional state, motherhood, birth.

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Moon/Midheaven midpoint`
