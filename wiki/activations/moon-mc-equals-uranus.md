## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 60, complete Uranus row under Meridian + Moon checked.

#### Witte Entry

Excitement in general. Women in the state of excitement. Labor-pains.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 65; one-based PDF p. 31, complete ten-clause Uranus row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: Excitement through my wife; renewal among my people; an excited feminine psyche; I feel tensions; emotional nature and soul in excitement; my sense of rhythm; my new wife; reforms for my people; hour and minute of an event; unrest among my people.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 143; one-based PDF p. 145, coded entry `0453`, complete entry checked.

#### Ebertin Entry

An unusual degree of emotional excitability, irritability, nervousness. Emotional crises, upsets through members of the female sex, an unusual emotional state, motherhood, birth.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: one-based PDF pp. 40–41, complete split Uranus paragraph under the Moon/Midheaven chapter beginning on PDF p. 40 checked.

#### Sandbach Entry

One has the ability to experience and project exalted feelings in a highly uninhibited fashion. Emotional growth comes through realizing one’s own individual and unique feelings as apart from the generalized emotions of other people or the mass consciousness.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `144`; one-based PDF p. 145, complete `Ura` row checked.

#### Munkasey Entry

Sudden or unexpected disruptions in your affiliation with maternal imaging; peculiar ideas from mother about what you should be or how you should develop in life; a strong inner interest in computers or astrology.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Excited or changing personal feeling, with individual emotional expression and renewal. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- psychology: Emotional excitability, tension, irritability and uninhibited feeling are represented; Sandbach distinguishes individual feeling from mass emotion. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).
- body/health: Labor pains, motherhood and birth are attributed source examples, not predictions or a diagnosis. [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).
- social/relationship: Excitement involving a wife and disruptions in maternal expectations appear alongside collective renewal or unrest. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- events/manifestations: Reform, a sense of rhythm, event-hour/minute examples and an inner interest in computers or astrology are stated. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Emotional individuality and renewal differ from crisis or instability; no loss of public trust is established.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize excitement and emotional upset. Sandbach also presents uninhibited individual feeling as a route to growth, while ABC includes renewal and reform. Munkasey's disruptions concern maternal imaging rather than an inevitable breakdown of career continuity.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/MC = Uranus`, personal emotional life is stirred into excitement, tension or renewal. The [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) entries include both heightened feeling and unsettling states. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) adds emotional individuality: growth can involve recognizing one's own feelings apart from collective emotion. The [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry) describes changes in maternal expectations and also names an inner interest in computers or astrology. The orientation therefore retains emotional disturbance beside freer individual expression, without making every change a threat to trust or continuity.
