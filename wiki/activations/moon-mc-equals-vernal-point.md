## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 60, complete Vernal Point row under Meridian + Moon checked.

#### Witte Entry

To be influenced by the female persons. The mental attitude toward the world in general.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 64; one-based PDF p. 31, complete seven-clause Vernal Point row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: Soulful hours in public; the feminine soul in public; myself as a woman in public life; my feeling in public; my people in the world; my wife in public; the soul of a people in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal feeling and mental attitude in relation to the public and the wider world. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: Attitude toward the world and feelings experienced in public are stated. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition or diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Influence through women and public examples involving a woman, wife or people are represented. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Public hours, a woman's public life and a people in the world are examples; no specific public office or event is named. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Public exposure and influence do not establish fame, a fixed life direction or unavoidable dependence on collective feeling.

## Contradictions

The entries share a public/world setting and give no explicit conflict. Witte emphasizes influence and mental attitude; ABC adds particular personal and collective emotional examples. Gendered examples remain attributed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/MC = Vernal Point`, personal feeling meets the public sphere and a broader attitude toward the world. The complete [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes influence through women and one's mental attitude toward the world in general. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places feelings, a woman or wife, and the soul of a people in public settings. This supports an outward context for emotional experience without turning public participation into a prescribed vocation or assured visibility.
