## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. 60, complete Vulcanus row under Meridian + Moon checked.

#### Witte Entry

The power of the mind. One who is powerfully influenced by women. Strong personal connections with the public. Influenced by the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 65; one-based PDF p. 31, complete ten-clause Vulcanus row checked.

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation checked against the German scan: Energy of a feelingful soul; emotional influence on the soul; I am a strong woman; strength of the soul in the hour; my feeling of strength; my people are powerful; my wife is energetic; my strong feelings; a woman's psychological energy; a woman's inner influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong personal feeling, mental force and emotional influence. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: A sense of strength, strong feelings and energy within the inner life are stated. [ABC](#abc-entry).
- body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition, diagnosis or measure of physical strength.
- social/relationship: Strong public connections and influence by women or the public occur beside an energetic wife and a powerful people. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- events/manifestations: Inner strength and public connection are represented; no particular achievement, office or coercive act is specified. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- conflicts/notes: Possessing inner force and being strongly influenced are distinct directions; neither guarantees control over other people.

## Contradictions

The entries are compatible in their emphasis on strength and influence. Witte explicitly includes being influenced by women and the public, which should remain visible beside ABC's stronger-feeling and energy examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/MC = Vulcanus`, personal emotional life is described through strength, energy and influence. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives strong feelings, a sense of strength and psychological energy. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins mental force and strong public connections with the possibility of being powerfully influenced by women or the public. The orientation therefore includes both inner force and responsiveness to outside influence; it does not establish domination, physical capacity or an inevitable inability to separate personal feeling from collective pressure.
