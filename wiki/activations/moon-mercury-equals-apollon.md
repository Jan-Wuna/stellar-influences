## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Moon + Mercury`
- Source reference: PDF p. `122` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Meeting of youth. Convention of youth of different nations. Thoughts about the expansion of people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Mond + Merkur`
- Source reference: printed p. `177`, PDF p. `87` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Reports on women's freedom, mobile experienced woman, success of a young woman, successful women's conversations, successful youth of a people, a woman who speaks a great deal, and increasing conversations about feelings

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: widening the field of conversation, youth association, and public participation ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: the ABC includes experience and increasing discussion of feelings; it does not describe a general personality type.
- body/health: neither entry states a specific bodily or health implication.
- social/relationship: meetings of youth from different nations and women's freedom place communication in a wider social setting.
- events/manifestations: conventions, reports on freedom, successful conversations, and the success of youth or a young woman are named examples.
- conflicts/notes: the original German says conversations about feelings, not children; the source's gendered examples remain examples rather than universal rules.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated in these two entries. International youth gatherings and successful or more extensive conversation differ in emphasis, but can share an expanding social field without being identical claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mercury = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the Moon/Mercury pair through a widening field of people and conversation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) puts youth into meetings across nations and names thoughts about a people's expansion. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful conversations, reports on women's freedom, and increasing discussion of feelings (printed p. 177, PDF p. 87).

The shared emphasis is scope and participation: ideas and feelings circulate among more people or find a more successful social expression. The entries retain particular youth and women's examples; they do not establish that every instance produces success or social freedom.
