## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Acquaintance with the young, especially with young girls. To be with other people during business hours. To work with vehicles. Associations of youth.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

mobile women at the place, women's conversations in the environment, emotional conversations with the partner, youth of other peoples, young woman as partner, opinion of the woman about the environment, news about other peoples, language of other peoples, and hour of the media at the place

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Love of conversation or talk. An active exchange of thoughts. An acquaintance dating from one's youth.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One seems outwardly to be emotionally flexible, and/or plays the role of being a person with whom one might have an intimate exchange of ideas. The need to communicate one’s feelings in relationships.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Concentrating on the need to convey emotion when speaking or talking with others; perceptions of the emotional level of others thru their speech and discourse patterns and information; identifying thru discussion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Mercury = Asc`: social quickness, intuitive communication, and the ability to make inner states immediately legible to others. Main caution: nervous subjectivity: presence can become too reactive, chatty, or mood-driven to stay proportionate.
