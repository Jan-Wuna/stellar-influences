## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `26` (entry; one-based).

#### Sandbach Entry

Your energy helps other people to talk about and think about their feelings, and to be more connected to their emotions. You naturally induce a freer and warmer flow of interchange with others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: facilitating warmer, freer communication about feelings ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- psychology: talking and thinking about emotion are linked to becoming more connected to it.
- body/health: the entry states no bodily condition or therapeutic outcome.
- social/relationship: the emphasis is on helping other people speak and exchange more freely.
- events/manifestations: conversations in which others become more able to discuss and reflect on feelings are the described manifestation.
- conflicts/notes: this is one source's account of the exact orientation; it does not establish clinical healing or a general Chiron doctrine.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present, so no comparison between sources is possible. It states a consistent facilitating role and supplies no opposing claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Mercury = Chiron`, Chiron specifies the Moon/Mercury pair through a facilitating influence on other people's emotional communication. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) connects helping others talk and think about their feelings with a warmer, freer interchange. The supported interpretation is increased contact with emotion through conversation; the entry does not promise healing or a therapeutic result.
