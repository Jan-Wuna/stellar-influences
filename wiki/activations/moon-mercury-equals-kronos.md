## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: conversation and opinion concerning authority, leadership, and public affairs ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: ABC names self-confidence, important thoughts imbued with feeling, and great emotional stirring.
- body/health: no specific bodily or health implication is stated.
- social/relationship: discussion with magistrates and opinions about the state are explicit institutional contacts.
- events/manifestations: conferences, official correspondence, and public opinions on authorities are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: leadership and self-confidence are examples; the entries do not promise recognition or status.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte focuses on communication with magistrates; the ABC also includes emotional intensity and confident youth. These additional emphases do not establish that every expression is official or administrative.

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Mercury = Kronos, Kronos specifies the Moon/Mercury pair through consequential conversation and opinion, particularly in relation to authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names discussions, conferences, and correspondence with magistrates. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds opinions on the state and authorities, a woman in a leading position, self-confident young women, and thoughts carrying strong feeling (printed p. 177, PDF p. 87).

The institutional and emotional examples meet in communication that concerns leadership or matters regarded as important. They do not establish that the speaker necessarily gains status or a dignified manner.
