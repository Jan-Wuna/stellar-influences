## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The growing youth. Time of puberty. Training of youth. Irresponsible. Inconsistent. Change in conception of one’s pattern of existence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

unfolding of the youth of a people, development of the language of a people, women's conversations about evolution, developing emotional thinking, conversations about the development of the woman, changes of opinion among the people, and altered thinking of the woman

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

The necessity to adjust one's thinking to new conditions or circumstances, reorientation of thinking caused by special events or experiences coupled with destiny. Tragic realizations.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The obsessive need to communicate one’s feelings. The transforming power of sharing one’s emotions. Nurturing others through communicating, and doing so in such a way that is extremely regenerative.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Communicating emotionally powerful feelings; the transition from analytical reasoning to emotional insight; investigative research; situations which allow you to feel or make powerful moving responses.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Mercury = Pluto`: psychological acuity, penetrating speech, and the ability to understand what lies beneath emotional surfaces. Main caution: mental compulsion: thought can become suspicious, obsessive, or too influenced by buried emotional pressure.
