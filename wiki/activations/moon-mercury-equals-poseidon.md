## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Moon + Mercury`
- Source reference: PDF p. `122` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Spiritually minded youth. Mental susceptibility in the youth. Enthusiasm. Young woman or girl with high education.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Mond + Merkur`
- Source reference: printed p. `177`, PDF p. `87` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Reports on a people's culture, an insightful movement of the people, insights of a young woman, rational thinking imbued with feeling, youth culture among the people, clear public opinion, spiritualized women in conversation, and a woman's rational opinion

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: spiritually directed or educated thought and culturally informed communication ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: enthusiasm, mental susceptibility in youth, insight, and rational thought with feeling are named.
- body/health: no specific bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: educated young women and a people's culture are particular social examples.
- events/manifestations: cultural reports, youth culture, conversation, and clear public opinion are described.
- conflicts/notes: the German final phrase is a woman's rational opinion, not a poor opinion; the entries do not establish universal idealism or suggestibility.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte's mental susceptibility and enthusiasm do not contradict the ABC's rational or clear opinion, but neither source says that receptivity always produces sound judgment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Mercury = Poseidon, Poseidon specifies the Moon/Mercury pair through spiritually or culturally directed thought and communication. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names spiritually minded youth, enthusiasm, mental susceptibility, and higher education. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds reports on a people's culture, youth culture, insight, rational thinking imbued with feeling, and clear public opinion (printed p. 177, PDF p. 87).

The supported connection is between receptivity, understanding, and their expression in conversation or culture. Education and clarity are particular source possibilities, not a promise that every receptive or enthusiastic opinion is well founded.
