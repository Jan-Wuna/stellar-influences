## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `122` (entry; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Young persons. Young women. The thinking populace. Memory for general things. The thinking nation. General travelling connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `176`, PDF p. `87` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

women's conversations in public, youth of a people in public, opinion of the woman in public, opinions about women in public life, hour of the public media, and opinion of the people in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: public thought, opinion, and communication ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: memory for general matters and collective thinking are named.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: young people, women, and a thinking populace appear in public settings.
- events/manifestations: public conversation, media, and general travelling connections are examples.
- conflicts/notes: the public setting does not establish unusually high emotional intelligence.

## Contradictions

The entries share a public setting without stating a direct disagreement. Witte adds general memory and travel; ABC concentrates on public discussion, opinion, and media.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Mercury = Vernal Point, Vernal Point specifies the Moon/Mercury pair as thought and communication circulating in public. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names the thinking populace, general memory, youth, and travelling connections; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places conversations, opinions about women, and media activity in public life (printed p. 176, PDF p. 87). Together these entries describe collective attention and exchange, without establishing a particular quality of intelligence.
