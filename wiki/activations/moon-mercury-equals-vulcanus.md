## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `122` (entry; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Powerful memory. To think fast. Fast vehicle.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `177`, PDF p. `87` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Influence of the youth of a people, influential young woman, emotional influence on thinking, conversations about women's power, news of violence for the people, opinions about women's power, strong emotional movement, and strong public opinion

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: force and speed in thought, memory, and public opinion ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: powerful recall, fast thinking, and strong emotional movement are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: influential youth and women, and discussions of women's power, are examples.
- events/manifestations: fast vehicles and news of violence appear alongside strong public opinion.
- conflicts/notes: quick thinking does not by itself establish sound judgment or decisive action.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte emphasizes memory, thinking speed, and vehicles; ABC emphasizes influence, emotional intensity, and public power.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Mercury = Vulcanus, Vulcanus specifies the Moon/Mercury pair through mental speed and forceful influence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives powerful memory, fast thought, and a fast vehicle. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends that force into influential youth or women, emotional influence on thinking, strong public opinion, and news or discussion about power and violence (printed p. 177, PDF p. 87). The shared thread is strength of thought or expression; the entries do not guarantee that speed produces good judgment.
