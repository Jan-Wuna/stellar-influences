#### Witte Entry

Stillness. Inner contemplation. Narrow-mindedness. Narrow conditions. The closed or locked room. Excluded from the public. Dying nation. Stoic character. Death of a female person. Hour of death. Change in the state of aggregation. Consolidation, freezing, thawing or softening.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: stillness, narrowed conditions, exclusion, and changes of physical state ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: inner contemplation, a stoic character, and narrow-mindedness are distinct possibilities.
- body/health: the historical entry names death and its hour; it does not establish a medical prognosis.
- social/relationship: exclusion from the public and a dying nation are explicit collective examples.
- events/manifestations: closed rooms, consolidation, freezing, thawing, and softening retain the entry's literal range.
- conflicts/notes: contemplation and narrow-mindedness are different qualities of limitation; physical phase changes should not automatically become emotional metaphors.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present. Its stillness and later thawing or softening describe a range of states; no independent comparison is available.

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = Admetos, Admetos specifies the repeated Moon pair through stillness and narrowed or enclosed conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) moves from inner contemplation and a stoic character to narrow-mindedness, closed rooms, and exclusion from public life. The common thread is confinement or reduced scope, whose psychological quality is not fixed by the formula alone.

The entry also names literal physical changes—consolidation, freezing, thawing, and softening—and historical death associations. These preserve a material and collective range beyond private feeling; they do not justify predicting an individual's death or translating every physical state into an emotion.
