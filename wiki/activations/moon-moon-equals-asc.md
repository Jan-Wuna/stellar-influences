## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = Asc, Asc specifies the repeated Moon pair through people in the immediate surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes female acquaintances and women nearby, then extends contact into being brought before the public by others. Its reference to the quality of an hour at a place preserves a local, situational meaning; the entry does not describe a fixed emotional response to those contacts.
