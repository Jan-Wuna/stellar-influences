## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: hardship, worry, solitude, and misfortune ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: resignation, worry, and anguish are explicit.
- body/health: Witte historically associates uterine disease, stomach trouble, and ailments of women; these are not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: poverty, widowhood, solitary women, and a people affected by misfortune are examples; a caring woman is also named.
- events/manifestations: difficult or unfortunate hours and collective misfortune are stated.
- conflicts/notes: the source's demeaning descriptions of women and people remain historical language, not an assessment of their worth.

## Contradictions

This is a single-source entry. Its caring woman and derogatory social labels describe different examples, rather than establishing one character type or a direct disagreement.

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = Hades, Hades specifies the repeated Moon pair through hardship experienced privately or collectively. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) brings resignation, worry, and anguish together with poverty, widowhood, solitude, and a people affected by misfortune. It also names a caring woman, so the range should not be reduced to a single negative personality.

The bodily associations and demeaning descriptions of women or social groups belong to this historical source. They preserve its doctrine but do not justify a diagnosis, a judgment of personal worth, or an inevitable fate.
