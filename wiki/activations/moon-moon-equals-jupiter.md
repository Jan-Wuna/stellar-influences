## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = Jupiter, Jupiter specifies the repeated Moon pair through happy or fortunate conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) carries faith and contentment from women and particular hours into national and public life. National justice and jurisdiction add an institutional dimension to this favorable range; the entry does not establish generosity or guaranteed success as a universal character trait.
