## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: independence, rank, administration, and relations to authority ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: greatness, nobility, and independence of mind are stated.
- body/health: no bodily or health implication is specified.
- social/relationship: independent women, household roles, administrators, officials, and leading peoples are examples.
- events/manifestations: office hours and contact with authorities give concrete settings.
- conflicts/notes: the source's gendered occupational examples do not establish that authority ensures noble conduct.

## Contradictions

The single entry includes both independence and relations with authorities. These are different positions within its range, not a stated contradiction or independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = Kronos, Kronos specifies the repeated Moon pair through independence and positions of authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins greatness or independence of mind to women administering households or holding official roles, and to leading nations or peoples. Office hours, rank, and dealings with authorities make this a concrete administrative as well as psychological range; noble-mindedness is a named possibility rather than a guarantee attached to office.
