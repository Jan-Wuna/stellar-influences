## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: conscious activity, acts of will, and work ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: consciousness and volition are explicitly linked to activity.
- body/health: the active brain and its working processes are source associations, without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: active women, household roles, the sweetheart, wife or mistress, and laboring people are historical examples.
- events/manifestations: working hours, household activity, and collective labor are named.
- conflicts/notes: energetic work does not establish emotional impulsiveness or a necessary transition from mood to action.

## Contradictions

Only one entry is present. Brain activity, household work, and collective labor connect different scales of activity without an independent comparison.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = Mars, Mars specifies the repeated Moon pair through active consciousness and work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects the working brain and acts of volition with active women, households, working hours, and the laboring population. The shared emphasis is activity at individual and collective levels. Its historical relationship examples remain part of the range, but the entry does not say that the activity must be impulsive or emotionally charged.
