## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = MC, MC specifies the repeated Moon pair through one's own mental and emotional state, particularly the attitude toward women, a people, or the public. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links personal relations and women's psychological influence with an emotional hour and emotions within a people. This connects an individual standpoint to a social setting while retaining the source's distinct references to the start of an hour and the feeling of being a woman.
