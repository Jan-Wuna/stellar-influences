- body/health: brain activity is a historical source association; no clinical condition is established.
- social/relationship: the young generation, young women, and opinion about women are examples.
- events/manifestations: vehicles, office hours, several hours, and a shifted hour preserve practical meanings.
- conflicts/notes: mental mobility does not establish rapid emotional reactions.

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = Mercury, Mercury specifies the repeated Moon pair through mental activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects memory and imagination with lively, changeable thought, while retaining superficial thinking as another possibility. Youth, vehicles, office hours, and shifted hours extend the range beyond private thought into people, movement, and schedules.
