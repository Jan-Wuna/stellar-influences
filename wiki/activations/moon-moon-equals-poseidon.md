#### Witte Entry

The influence of the spirit upon the soul. A people that is receptive to spiritual or intellectual issues. The medium. The hour of realization. Subconscious mind. Mentally elevated woman. Large sea tides.

## Comparative Schema

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = Poseidon, Poseidon specifies the repeated Moon pair through receptivity to spiritual or intellectual matters. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links the influence of spirit upon soul with the subconscious mind, a medium, and an hour of realization, and extends receptivity to a whole people. Large sea tides remain a separate physical example rather than a metaphor that proves an emotional trait.
