#### Witte Entry

Restrain. Separate. Despair. Depressed mind. Old, single, separated or unhappy woman. Widow. The hours one has to be alone. Evening hours. To be separated from the public. The hour of separation. Separation of female persons. To avoid women. Discontentment trough separation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: restraint, separation, solitude, and their distress ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: despair, a depressed mind, and discontent through separation are named.
- body/health: a depressed mind is the historical source's wording; no clinical diagnosis is established.
- social/relationship: widowhood, separated women, avoidance of women, and exclusion from the public are examples.
- events/manifestations: hours alone, evening hours, and an hour of separation are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: age, singleness, and widowhood do not by themselves establish unhappiness in real people.

## Contradictions

This page contains one Witte entry. Its several examples do not supply an independent comparison or require every manifestation to occur together.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = Saturn, Saturn specifies the repeated Moon pair through restraint and separation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places hours alone and separation from women or the public beside despair, a depressed mind, and discontent through separation. Its evening-hour and historical social examples preserve concrete settings, without turning age, singleness, or widowhood into an inevitable emotional condition.
