#### Witte Entry

Awake. Rouse. Excite. Vibrating motion. Interest. Attention. Restlessness of the body and the mind. Womens ambition. Energetic woman. Hour of tension. Eventful hour. Uproar of the populace. Excitement. Nervousness of female persons. Uproars caused by women. Excited persons and people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: wakefulness, arousal, excitement, and tension ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: interest, attention, ambition, and mental restlessness are named.
- body/health: bodily restlessness and nervousness are historical associations, without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: energetic women, excited people, and uproar among the populace are examples.
- events/manifestations: an eventful or tense hour, vibrating motion, and public uproar are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: interest and attention are not identical with disruptive unrest; the entry includes both.

## Contradictions

This page contains one Witte entry. Its several examples do not supply an independent comparison or require every manifestation to occur together.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = Uranus, Uranus specifies the repeated Moon pair through heightened arousal. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects waking, interest, and attention with bodily or mental restlessness and nervous excitement. At a collective scale it names uproar, while an eventful or tense hour gives a situational expression. The range runs from attention to disruption, rather than requiring every expression to be an upheaval.
