#### Witte Entry

The feeling. The love. The loving woman. Hours of love. Pregnancy. Motherhood. Peaceful hour. The love or affection of women. The love of a people. The people’s peace or harmony.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: love, affection, and peaceful or harmonious conditions ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: feeling and love are explicitly named.
- body/health: pregnancy and motherhood are historical associations, not a fertility prediction.
- social/relationship: a loving woman, women's affection, and love of a people are examples.
- events/manifestations: hours of love, a peaceful hour, and a people's peace or harmony are stated.
- conflicts/notes: the entry does not establish that tenderness or attachment is universal.

## Contradictions

This page contains one Witte entry. Its several examples do not supply an independent comparison or require every manifestation to occur together.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = Venus, Venus specifies the repeated Moon pair through love and affection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) carries this from a loving woman and hours of love to a people's peace or harmony. Pregnancy and motherhood belong to its historical range, but they do not establish a medical conclusion or make one family role the definition of this formula.
