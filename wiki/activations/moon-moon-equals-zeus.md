#### Witte Entry

A creative mind. The hours of creative work. Discipline. Hot blooded woman. The martial nation. A woman who has children. Procreative faculty of female person. Institutions of general benefit created by the state. Inflammable. A people lined up.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creative work, discipline, procreation, and organized collective activity ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: a creative mind and discipline are stated.
- body/health: procreative faculty and a woman with children are historical associations, not a fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: a martial nation, a people lined up, and state-created public institutions are examples.
- events/manifestations: hours of creative work and inflammability retain productive and material meanings.
- conflicts/notes: inflammability should not automatically be translated into emotional drive.

## Contradictions

This page contains one Witte entry. Its several examples do not supply an independent comparison or require every manifestation to occur together.

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Moon = Zeus, Zeus specifies the repeated Moon pair through creation and organized activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links a creative mind and hours of work with discipline, then extends the range to procreation, state-created institutions, and a martial or assembled people. Inflammability and the historical description of a hot-blooded woman remain particular examples, rather than proof that feeling causes every form of production.
