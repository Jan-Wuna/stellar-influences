## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `128` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Short time of relaxation. Little rest during the night.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `189`, PDF p. `93` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A woman's blocked sensitivity, resolving emotional blockages, stabilizing weak feelings, a difficult future for a people, refinement and deepening of feelings, a woman's immersion in metaphysics, and a woman's insecurity through depression

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: limited rest and sensitivity that may be blocked, stabilized, or deepened ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: ABC names emotional blockages, refinement, deepening, and insecurity through depression.
- body/health: Witte names little rest at night; ABC's depression association remains historical doctrine, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: a woman's sensitivity and a people's difficult future are particular examples.
- events/manifestations: brief relaxation and engagement with metaphysics are named.
- conflicts/notes: resolving blockages and stabilizing feelings should not be omitted in favor of restriction alone.

## Contradictions

Witte concentrates on limited rest; ABC includes both blocked sensitivity and its resolution or deepening. These are different possibilities, not a claim that all sensitivity remains obstructed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Neptune = Admetos, Admetos specifies the pair through restriction or concentration within rest and sensitivity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives a short period of relaxation and little rest at night. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds blocked sensitivity but also resolving emotional blockages, stabilizing weak feelings, and deepening feeling or engagement with metaphysics (printed p. 189, PDF p. 93). The range therefore includes both difficulty and consolidation; it does not establish an inevitable failure of emotional recovery.
