### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `35` (entry; one-based).

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s energy helps others to perceive the subtleties of their own feelings, and to see how their feelings are being affected by subtle energies and psychic influences. You also help people to feel higher and exalted emotions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: helping others notice subtleties in their feelings ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- psychology: the entry describes awareness of influences on feeling and access to exalted emotions.
- body/health: no bodily condition, treatment, or clinical outcome is stated.
- social/relationship: the emphasis is assistance to other people in understanding their own feelings.
- events/manifestations: helping someone perceive emotional subtleties is the described activity.
- conflicts/notes: subtle energies and psychic influences are Sandbach's explanatory terms; clinical healing is not established.

## Contradictions

Only one Sandbach entry is present. Its account of awareness and exalted feeling supplies no independent comparison or clinical outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Neptune = Chiron, Chiron specifies the pair through helping other people perceive subtleties in their feelings. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) describes recognizing what it calls subtle energies and psychic influences, and helping others experience higher or exalted emotions. This supports an interpretation of emotional awareness and refinement within the source's terms; the entry does not describe medical treatment or establish healing outcomes.
