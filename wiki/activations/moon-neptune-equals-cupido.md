## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `128` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Women who are not understood. Socially deceiving or disappointing women. Nightly leisure hours.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `189`, PDF p. `93` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disappointed women in a community, an emotionally sensitive community, foreign women in a community, foreign folk art, a weak national community, an hour of shared disappointment, and an association of insecure women

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sensitivity, misunderstanding, or disappointment in social groups ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: being misunderstood and insecurity are named, without establishing one personality.
- body/health: no specific bodily or health condition appears.
- social/relationship: communities, associations of women, and foreign women in a community are explicit.
- events/manifestations: nightly leisure, shared disappointment, and foreign folk art are examples.
- conflicts/notes: the historical gendered descriptions do not establish alluring charm or deception in every social setting.

## Contradictions

Both entries include social disappointment; ABC also preserves sensitive community and foreign folk art. These additional meanings do not erase Witte's misunderstanding or demonstrate a direct disagreement.

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Neptune = Cupido, Cupido specifies the pair through sensitivity and uncertainty within social belonging. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names women who are misunderstood, socially disappointing or deceiving women, and nightly leisure. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places disappointment and insecurity in communities while retaining an emotionally sensitive community and foreign folk art (printed p. 189, PDF p. 93). The supported range concerns how people share a social setting; attraction or charm need not be added to explain it.
