## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A woman made happy by sensitiveness. Undisturbed night rest. Sleep well. Pleasant dreams.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

success by alien people, successful woman in metaphysics, financial weakness of a people, joy of a woman in the water, legal uncertainty of the woman, refined legal filial, and future advantages for a people

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Abundant powers of imagination, fantasy, good understanding of others through a sympathetic and compassionate attitude, far-reaching wishes. Artistic successes.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To relish the subtleties of emotion. To encourage others to experience the fineness and subtlety of their feelings. Success through one’s understanding and sym pathetic nature. To be overwhelmed by chaotic feelings.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `128`

#### Munkasey Entry

Enhanced ability to read people’s motives and put these into a political context; enlarges upon needs to dream and fantasize; increased needs for stimulants or drugs to enable functioning in the physical world.
