## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `128` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Girls protected by the state. Refuge for young girls. A woman who losses the lead. Night-rest or hours of rest under control of the state. To be very susceptible and sensible of something during nightly hours. To have a sensitive attention.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `189`, PDF p. `93` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

An especially sensitive emotional nature, dominance of a woman in the future, great insecurity among the people, an independent woman in metaphysics, a woman's state-related insecurity, superiority of a people in water or in the air, and the future of a great people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sensitivity within protection, authority, independence, or insecurity ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: sensitive attention and an especially sensitive emotional nature are explicit.
- body/health: Witte names rest controlled by the state; no bodily condition or treatment is established.
- social/relationship: state protection of girls, independent women, and a great people are examples.
- events/manifestations: refuge, regulated rest, loss of a woman's lead, and collective activity in water or air are named.
- conflicts/notes: institutional protection and state-related insecurity are distinct source possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte includes both protection and loss of a woman's lead; ABC includes both dominance and insecurity. This is a range of relationships to authority rather than a promise that institutional control is protective.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Neptune = Kronos, Kronos specifies the pair through sensitive attention placed in relation to authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names state protection, refuge, and controlled hours of rest, but also a woman losing the lead. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds independence, dominance, and collective stature alongside state-related or public insecurity (printed p. 189, PDF p. 93). The organizing issue is the relation between sensitivity and formal power; shelter and insecurity remain different possibilities within it.
