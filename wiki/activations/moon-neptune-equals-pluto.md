## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: PDF p. `128`.

#### Witte Entry

Changeable fearsome mind. Hypersensitive. Unnoticeable changes in the night hours. Hidden changes of the glandular function.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: printed p. `189`; PDF p. `93`.

#### ABC Entry

A sensitive change in a woman, creeping changes of emotional state, weak development of feelings, uncertain development of a people, uncertainty through changing feelings, change in a disappointed woman, growing refinement of feelings.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source passage: printed p. `132`, entry `0406`.

#### Ebertin Entry

A high degree of sensitivity coupled with an inclination to be easily influenced by others. An emotional shock or upheaval.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source passage: PDF p. `35` (entry checked directly).

#### Sandbach Entry

A feeling of extreme oppression caused by one’s psychic sensitivity. The pur pose of this oppression is to induce one to clear and elevate the emotional body so that the opposite extreme is eventually attained: tremendous empowerment rooted in one’s psychic sensitivity.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `128`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased needs for revenge against those who injure or hurt you in any way; added ferment and seething to emotions which you create within your dreams for escape from reality; a need to escape from confining ways.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Pluto specifies the pair through intensified sensitivity and emotional change or upheaval.
- psychology: fear, oppression, seething feelings, and a desire to escape confinement are represented; Sandbach also describes empowerment through refining sensitivity.
- body/health: Witte's hidden glandular changes are a historical source claim, not a medical inference from the formula.
- social/relationship: susceptibility to others and revenge against those who cause hurt are distinct interpersonal expressions.
- events/manifestations: emotional shock, gradual changes of feeling, and unnoticed nighttime changes appear in the entries.
- conflicts/notes: healing and hidden insight are not established by these entries; ABC specifically describes changes and refinement of feelings.

## Contradictions

- Sandbach proposes a movement from oppression toward empowerment; Ebertin and Munkasey emphasize shock, influence, and resentment. That proposed development is not a denial of their difficult expressions, and the entries do not establish that it necessarily occurs.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Neptune = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair as sensitivity undergoing pressure and change. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describes emotional shock and susceptibility to others; [Munkasey, p. 128](#munkasey-entry) adds seething feelings, revenge, and escape from confinement. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) gives the distinct possibility of turning oppressive sensitivity into empowerment through emotional refinement. This is a conditional development within the entry, not a promise of healing or a reason to erase the more troubled expressions.
