## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: PDF p. `128`.

#### Witte Entry

The relaxed or sleeping body. Physical sensitivity. Mediumistic state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: printed p. `188`; PDF p. `93`.

#### ABC Entry

Sensitive body of a woman, sensitive body of a man, persons of a foreign people, weak ccirper of a woman, sensitivity of a woman, unclear feelings of the man, future of woman and man, and uncertainty between woman and man

- Translation check: on printed p. `188` (PDF p. `93`, visually checked), the second phrase describes a man's sensitive mind or disposition (`feinfühlendes Gemüt`), not his body. `schwacher Körper` means a weak body. The imported English is retained above; its gendered examples remain attributed to this entry.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source passage: printed p. `132`, entry `0399`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Sensitivity, receptive power, a delicate and sensitive body or physique. A sensitive wife. Illusions and deceptions.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source passage: PDF p. `35` (entry checked directly).

#### Sandbach Entry

One feels it is important to take care of other people, often in subtle and poten tially profound ways. Emotional confusion and/or the taking on of negative psychic energy from other people can drain one’s vitality, and this often occurs without one recognizing the source of the problem. One is aware of many feelings that are affecting situations in hidden ways. One’s energy is fed and maintained by focusing on exalted emotions of love and joy, and by connecting with spiritual forces.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `128`

#### Munkasey Entry

Strengthened inner devotion; authority to your nurturing instincts; original and creative ways of showing how you care for others; the commitment you bring to mothering; impressive insights into people or events in life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sun specifies the pair through embodied sensitivity and an active commitment to caring.
- psychology: devotion, receptive insight, and awareness of subtle feelings coexist with emotional confusion or illusion.
- body/health: Witte describes the relaxed or sleeping body; Ebertin names a sensitive physique. Sandbach attributes vitality changes to emotional experience, without establishing a medical explanation.
- social/relationship: creative nurturing and care for others are explicit, alongside uncertain feelings between people.
- events/manifestations: relaxation, sleep, mothering, and original ways of showing care appear in the entries.
- conflicts/notes: the checked ABC entry distinguishes bodily sensitivity from a sensitive mind; its historical gendered wording is not a universal claim.

## Contradictions

- Devotion and receptive care differ in emphasis from confusion and depletion. Sandbach describes both sides within one entry, so they are a range of possible expressions rather than a demonstrated contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Neptune = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through sensitive embodiment and commitment to care: [Witte](#witte-entry) describes relaxation and physical sensitivity, while [Munkasey, p. 128](#munkasey-entry) describes devotion and creative nurturing. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) connects subtle awareness of others' feelings with both caring and emotional depletion. The orientation thus places receptivity in the person's lived energy and care for others, without making every sensitive impression reliable or turning the historical bodily descriptions into diagnoses.
