## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Anemic girls. Maidens. Nuns. Also, secret love of females. Tactful.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Fine feeling love for a woman, peace expectations of a people, foreign people art, love for a future woman, love exchange of a woman, unclear love feelings, hour of love exchange, and preference of a woman for metaphysics

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Over-enthusiastic love and adoration, peculiar and strange tendencies in the expression of love, odd or perverse inclinations. Spiritual and unearthly love or compassionate love, renunciation, disappointment in love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Exalted feelings of love. A love which is boundless. One who appreciates sensi tive, delicate and subtle emotions. In love one deeply takes in the feelings of the other person.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `128`

#### Munkasey Entry

Femininity and charm added to your devotion and ideal feelings of others; an enjoyment when sharing feelings of mystical contact; heightened needs to show love to others; increased needs to share love with another.
