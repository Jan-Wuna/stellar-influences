## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: PDF p. `128`.

#### Witte Entry

Anemic girls. Maidens. Nuns. Also, secret love of females. Tactful.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: printed p. `188`; PDF p. `93`.

#### ABC Entry

Fine feeling love for a woman, peace expectations of a people, foreign people art, love for a future woman, love exchange of a woman, unclear love feelings, hour of love exchange, and preference of a woman for metaphysics

- Translation check: `Liebes-Enttäuschung` on printed p. `188` (PDF p. `93`, visually checked) means disappointment in love, not an exchange of love. `fremde Volks-Kunst` concerns foreign folk art. The imported English above is retained for traceability.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source passage: printed p. `132`, entry `0401`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Over-enthusiastic love and adoration, peculiar and strange tendencies in the expression of love, odd or perverse inclinations. Spiritual and unearthly love or compassionate love, renunciation, disappointment in love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source passage: PDF p. `35` (entry checked directly).

#### Sandbach Entry

Exalted feelings of love. A love which is boundless. One who appreciates sensi tive, delicate and subtle emotions. In love one deeply takes in the feelings of the other person.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `128`

#### Munkasey Entry

Femininity and charm added to your devotion and ideal feelings of others; an enjoyment when sharing feelings of mystical contact; heightened needs to show love to others; increased needs to share love with another.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Venus specifies the pair through delicate, idealized, or compassionate affection.
- psychology: exalted love and appreciation of subtle emotion coexist with over-enthusiastic adoration and disappointment.
- body/health: Witte's anemia reference remains an attributed historical claim, not a diagnosis or a defining trait of women.
- social/relationship: sharing love, mystical rapport, secret love, and renunciation are represented.
- events/manifestations: expressions of devotion, disappointment in love, and ABC's folk-art and peace expectations are specific manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: Ebertin's evaluative terms for unusual love remain his wording; the corrected ABC phrase concerns disappointment rather than exchange.

## Contradictions

- Boundless love in Sandbach and renunciation or disappointment in Ebertin are differing possibilities. The entries do not resolve Ebertin's evaluative language into a shared judgment about forms of love.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Neptune = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through love that values delicate feelings and may be experienced as mystical or boundless. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) emphasizes taking in the beloved's feelings, and [Munkasey, p. 128](#munkasey-entry) emphasizes sharing devotion and affection. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) and the checked [ABC entry, p. 188](#abc-entry) retain disappointment in love alongside idealization. The common thread is heightened emotional investment in love, with neither fulfilment nor renunciation guaranteed.
