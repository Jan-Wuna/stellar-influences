## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: PDF p. `128`.

#### Witte Entry

A mighty sense of scent. Mighty disappointment of the people. Deception.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: printed p. `189`; PDF p. `93`.

#### ABC Entry

Exchange through violence against women, confiscation of a sensitive woman, influence of foreign folk, influence by refinement of the feelings, weakened energy of a people, enormous exchange of a woman, and future influence of women

- Translation check: printed p. `189` (PDF p. `93`, visually checked) says disappointment through violence against a woman, influence on a sensitive woman (`Beeinflussung`), and great disappointment of a woman. The imported words "exchange" and "confiscation" do not convey those phrases and are excluded from interpretation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Vulcanus specifies the pair through powerful disappointment, deception, or influence on sensitive feeling.
- psychology: the entries emphasize the magnitude of disappointment and the influence that refined feelings can exert.
- body/health: Witte names a strong sense of smell; neither entry establishes a health diagnosis.
- social/relationship: ABC includes violence against a woman and influence involving foreign peoples; these remain attributed examples.
- events/manifestations: collective disappointment, deception, weakened collective energy, and future influence appear in the entries.
- conflicts/notes: the original German supports influence and disappointment, not confiscation or exchange; a strong sense of smell does not establish general psychic accuracy.

## Contradictions

- Power and weakened energy refer to different expressions in the entries, not a direct assertion that the same condition is simultaneously strong and weak. No explicit contradiction is established; the translation defects are documented above.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Neptune = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through the force of disappointment, deception, and emotional influence. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes collective disappointment; the checked [ABC entry, p. 189](#abc-entry) connects sensitive feeling with influence, violence, and weakened collective energy. Witte's strong sense of smell is a separate specific expression. It does not justify extending the formula into a general claim of heightened psychic perception.
