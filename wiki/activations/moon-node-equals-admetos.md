## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: PDF p. `121`.

#### Witte Entry

Suppression or the end of women’s associations. The end of a people’s community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: printed p. `175`; PDF p. `86` (entry visually checked).

#### ABC Entry

blockade in emotional relationships, depressions of a woman in a connection, restriction of the connection to the mother, restricted connection to women, firmly connected with the woman, thorough connection to the mother, and deepening of contacts with the woman

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Admetos specifies the pair through restricted association or a bond becoming firmly rooted.
- psychology: ABC includes emotional blockage and deepened attachment; its depression wording remains attributed, not diagnostic.
- body/health: no bodily condition is established; the historical emotional wording does not justify a clinical inference.
- social/relationship: ties with a mother or woman may be restricted, firm, or deepened; Witte also names the end of associations.
- events/manifestations: suppression or dissolution of women's associations and a people's community are Witte's collective examples.
- conflicts/notes: restriction and depth are different possibilities within ABC; a bond's endurance does not establish its emotional quality.

## Contradictions

- Witte emphasizes suppression and endings, while ABC also includes firm and deepened connection. This is a material difference in range, not grounds to turn every restricted association into a lasting bond or every deep bond into an ending.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Node = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair through limits on association and the depth or fixity of a bond. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes suppression and the end of communities; [ABC, p. 175](#abc-entry) includes restricted emotional or maternal connections but also firm attachment and deepened contact. The common issue is how tightly connection is held or constrained. The entries leave open whether that produces enduring depth, blockage, or an end to association.
