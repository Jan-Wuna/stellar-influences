## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: PDF p. `121`.

#### Witte Entry

Far spread women’s association. Expansion of a people’s society. National assembly.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: printed p. `175`; PDF p. `86`.

#### ABC Entry

Extensive connections in the people, successful mother relationship, successful people relationships, freedom of feelings in relationships, trade relationships in people, connected with many women, and mediation of women in business and trade

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Apollon specifies the pair through extensive associations and expanding collective contact.
- psychology: the checked ABC wording permits freedom of feeling in relationships, not a claim about freedom of thought.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a specific bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: numerous connections with women or peoples and a successful maternal relationship are explicit examples.
- events/manifestations: national assembly, expanded associations, trade connections, and mediation in business appear in the entries.
- conflicts/notes: the examples span personal and collective relationships; their breadth does not make every association successful.

## Contradictions

- Expansion in Witte and extensive or successful connections in ABC are compatible emphases. Their overlap alone does not establish independent corroboration, and neither resolves every outcome of a large network.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Node = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair as connection spreading through larger associations and numerous contacts. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives expanding societies and national assembly; [ABC, p. 175](#abc-entry) adds trade, mediation, and freedom of feeling within relationships. The connecting theme is wider participation, from personal ties to collective networks, with successful relationships as stated possibilities rather than a guaranteed result of scale.
