#### Sandbach Entry

Your energy helps others to be aware of the emotional aspects of their connec tion with others, as well as what they need for their own personal emotional growth. You trigger others to work through their feelings, and often do this with no conscious effort to do so.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Chiron specifies the pair through stimulating awareness of feelings within relationships.
- psychology: another person's presence can prompt emotional reflection and recognition of growth needs, even without deliberate effort.
- body/health: the entry supplies no bodily or medical-healing claim.
- social/relationship: the effect concerns how others understand the emotional side of their own connections.
- events/manifestations: becoming aware of relational feelings and working through them are the stated processes; no specific external event is named.
- conflicts/notes: this single entry supports emotional growth language, not a guaranteed therapeutic result.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Node = Chiron`, Chiron specifies the pair as a stimulus to emotional awareness within relationships. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes helping others recognize both what they feel in connection and what they need for emotional growth; this prompting may occur without conscious intention. The bounded interpretation is an impetus to work through relational feelings, not an established medical or therapeutic effect.
