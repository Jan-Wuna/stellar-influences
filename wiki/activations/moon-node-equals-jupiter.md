## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: PDF p. `121`.

#### Witte Entry

Happy (fortunate) union of a woman, also union with women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: printed p. `174`; PDF p. `86` (entry visually checked).

#### ABC Entry

financial relationships to peoples, joy through connection to the people, joy in an emotional bond, happily connected with the woman, joyful emotional contacts, legal connection to the woman, and advantage through connections among peoples

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source passage: printed p. `137`, entry `0427`.

#### Ebertin Entry

A generous behavior in associations, an atmosphere of confidence in teamwork. Successful unions or associations, happy relations with a woman.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source passage: PDF p. `38` (entry checked directly).

#### Sandbach Entry

An expansive and enthusiastic sharing of one’s feelings with others. Being generous in terms of one’s willingness to nurture others. Being willing and enthusiastic to evolve new ways of feeling and to let go of feeling which no longer serve the self.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `136`

#### Munkasey Entry

Meeting others who share a similar philosophical sense about how life works; increases the conviviality and joviality during meetings with others; studies of social theory or social psychology; sales meetings or presentations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Jupiter specifies the pair through joyful association, generosity, and confidence in shared feeling.
- psychology: enthusiasm for emotional exchange and willingness to develop new ways of feeling are explicit.
- body/health: no entry supplies a specific bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: happy unions, confident teamwork, nurturing generosity, and convivial meetings are repeated motifs.
- events/manifestations: financial or legal connections, sales meetings, presentations, and study of social theory are stated examples.
- conflicts/notes: the entries emphasize generous association without specifying excess as its outcome.

## Contradictions

- The entries share favorable social emphases while covering different activities. No explicit contradiction is present on this page, but repeated favorable wording does not guarantee success in every association.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Node = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair as generous and enthusiastic participation in relationships. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) emphasizes confidence in teamwork and successful unions; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) connects nurturing generosity with willingness to develop new feelings. [Munkasey, p. 136](#munkasey-entry) makes this concrete in convivial meetings, shared philosophies, and presentations. Confidence, generosity, and shared emotional interests connect the personal and public examples.
