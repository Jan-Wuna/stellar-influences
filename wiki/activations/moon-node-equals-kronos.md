## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: PDF p. `121`.

#### Witte Entry

Union of the woman with the husband. The state sponsored women’s association. Association of housewives. Great nations unite.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: printed p. `175`; PDF p. `86`.

#### ABC Entry

Special emotional connections, dominance of a woman in relationships, a woman with special contacts, great attachment to the people, a woman's self-confident relationships, priority of feelings in a connection, and a woman's connection with the boss

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Kronos specifies the pair through prominent, authoritative, or institutionally supported associations.
- psychology: ABC includes confidence and the priority of feelings within a connection, rather than priority of skills.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a specific bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: special contacts, dominance within a relationship, marriage, and connection with a superior are represented.
- events/manifestations: state-sponsored associations, housewives' associations, and the union of great nations are Witte's examples.
- conflicts/notes: institutional support or prominence does not establish dignity, benefit, or equality in every relationship.

## Contradictions

- Witte's institutional examples and ABC's prominence or dominance differ in scale and emphasis. No explicit incompatible claim is established; official status alone does not resolve the quality of an association.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Node = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair through associations that carry prominence or authority. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives state-sponsored groups and unions of nations; the checked [ABC entry, p. 175](#abc-entry) adds special contacts, confidence, and connections with a superior. The theme extends from institutional ties to the standing or dominance of a person within a relationship. Neither official support nor prominence establishes that the bond is beneficial or equal.
