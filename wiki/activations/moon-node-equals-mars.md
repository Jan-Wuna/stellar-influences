## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: PDF p. `121`.

#### Witte Entry

Union of a woman with a man.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: printed p. `174`; PDF p. `86`.

#### ABC Entry

Active connections within the people, job placement for women, establishing emotional relationships, impulses from women within connections, connection to the working people, establishing a connection to the mother, and creating connections among peoples

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source passage: printed p. `137`, entry `0426`.

#### Ebertin Entry

A vigorous advocacy of unions or associations, energetic teamwork or cooperation. A union based entirely on physical attraction, the desire or urge to get married.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source passage: PDF p. `38` (entry checked directly).

#### Sandbach Entry

The need to actively share one’s feelings with others. One’s actions only serve to support and maintain old insecurities. One takes the initiative to form connections with others that will support one’s emotional needs.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `136`

#### Munkasey Entry

The energy you put into finding others and soliciting their advice and opinions; increased sexual stimulation and excitement for meeting others; business acquaintances joining together for their own enhancement.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mars specifies the pair through initiative and effort directed toward making connections.
- psychology: actively sharing feelings and seeking support can coexist with actions that maintain old insecurities.
- body/health: Ebertin names physical attraction and Munkasey sexual stimulation; neither supplies a health diagnosis.
- social/relationship: energetic teamwork, advocacy of associations, seeking advice, and business cooperation are explicit.
- events/manifestations: forming emotional contacts, soliciting opinions, and the desire to marry are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: the entries do not establish fierce loyalty or quarrelsomeness; ABC's corrected wording concerns emotional relationships and peoples.

## Contradictions

- Initiative is not presented as uniformly constructive: Sandbach explicitly includes actions that maintain insecurities alongside supportive contact. That tension qualifies the energetic cooperation elsewhere without contradicting its possibility.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Moon/Node = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through active efforts to form, support, or advocate connections. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describes energetic cooperation, while [Munkasey, p. 136](#munkasey-entry) describes seeking others' advice and joining business acquaintances. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) brings the emotional purpose into focus: initiative may obtain needed support, yet action can also keep old insecurities in place. The entries support activity in association without requiring either loyalty or conflict as its outcome.
