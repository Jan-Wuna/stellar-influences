## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `121` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The idea or the spirit out of which is founded or established a women’s or people’s association.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `175`, PDF p. `86` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Insights into a connection with a woman, intellectually rich connections with women, cultural connections among peoples, connected with rational women, an hour of truth in a connection, connection to spiritually minded women, and connections among peoples through ideas

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: ideas, understanding, and intellectual or spiritual foundations of association.
- psychology: insight and rational understanding are named.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition appears.
- social/relationship: women's or people's associations, cultural ties, and intellectually or spiritually minded women are examples.
- events/manifestations: founding an association and an hour of truth in a connection are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: shared ideas do not by themselves establish conscience, superiority, or the truth of a group's beliefs.

## Contradictions

Both entries connect association with ideas or intellectual and spiritual life. ABC adds cultural contact and truth within a bond; this overlap does not independently establish the validity of any particular belief.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Node = Poseidon, Poseidon specifies the pair through the ideas or spirit on which association rests. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes this foundational: an idea gives rise to a women's or people's association. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) carries the theme into cultural ties, rational or spiritually minded women, insight, and connections among peoples through ideas (printed p. 175, PDF p. 86). The common thread is understanding or shared thought as a basis of connection, without assuming that a founding vision is necessarily true.
