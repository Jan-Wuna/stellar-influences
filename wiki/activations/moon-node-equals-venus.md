## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `121` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The love-union of a woman.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `174`, PDF p. `86` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Peaceful contacts among peoples, harmonious connections with women, a harmonious connection of feelings, a love connection with a woman, connected with feminine beauty, the hour of a harmonious connection, and connections with folk art

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `137`, entry `0425`.

#### Ebertin Entry

An affectionate behavior in associations. Tendency to form an attachment to other persons. Entering into a love union. Contacts or connections with the arts.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `38` (entry; one-based).

#### Sandbach Entry

A love of emotionally connecting with others. A love of witnessing emotional progress. One finds it easy to connect on a feeling level with people. To appreciate the process of getting more in touch with and working through one’s emotions.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `136`

#### Munkasey Entry

The intimacy and rapport you develop thru meeting others; people who bring their artistic talents to you or who come to you for your artistic talents; the charm and manners you show when meeting and greeting others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: affectionate connection, rapport, and appreciation of emotional exchange.
- psychology: enjoyment of emotional progress and getting in touch with feelings are stated.
- body/health: no bodily or clinical condition appears; harmony refers to feelings in the ABC passage.
- social/relationship: love unions, attachment, charm, and intimacy in meetings are examples.
- events/manifestations: meeting around artistic talents, connections to the arts, and peaceful contact among peoples are named.
- conflicts/notes: the entries do not establish clinging, sentimentality, or avoidance of tension.

## Contradictions

The entries share affectionate and artistic themes, with different personal and collective examples. No explicit incompatible claim appears, and pleasure in contact does not by itself describe how conflict is handled.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Node = Venus, Venus specifies the pair through affectionate and enjoyable connection. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) joins attachment and love unions with the arts; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) emphasizes pleasure in emotional contact and progress. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) makes this concrete through rapport, charm in greeting others, and meetings around artistic talent (p. 136).

The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends harmony into peaceful contacts among peoples and folk art (printed p. 174, PDF p. 86). The connecting thread is affection or aesthetic appreciation in association; the entries do not supply a necessary pattern of clinging or conflict avoidance.
