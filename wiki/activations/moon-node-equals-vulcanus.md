## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `121` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Mighty and influential women’s or people's association.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `175`, PDF p. `86` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Influential connections among peoples, influential connections with women, emotional influence in relationships, contact with an energetic woman, a woman's power in a connection, powerful connections among peoples, a strong connection with a woman, and a strong maternal bond

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: powerful, influential, or strong associations.
- psychology: ABC names emotional influence within relationships without defining its quality.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: women's associations, connections among peoples, maternal bonds, and energetic women are examples.
- events/manifestations: the exercise of influence within a connection is explicit.
- conflicts/notes: power does not itself establish organization, benefit, or coercion.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize strength and influence. ABC extends the collective association into personal and maternal ties, without establishing how the power is used.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Node = Vulcanus, Vulcanus specifies the pair through the strength and influence of association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names powerful women's or people's groups; [ABC](#abc-entry) carries influence into relationships, an energetic woman, and a strong maternal bond (printed p. 175, PDF p. 86). The common issue is the force a connection can carry, while its purpose and emotional quality remain unspecified.
