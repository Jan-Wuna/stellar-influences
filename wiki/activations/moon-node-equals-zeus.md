## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `121` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Energetic connections with women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `175`, PDF p. `86` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A demanding connection with a woman, shaping the connection to the mother, a creative woman in a connection, achievements of a connection with women, a creative emotional connection, a procreative connection with a woman, and connection with a highly productive people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: energetic, demanding, or creative connections.
- psychology: creative emotional connection is stated without a general personality description.
- body/health: ABC's procreative connection is historical doctrine, not a fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: maternal ties, a creative woman, and connections with productive people are examples.
- events/manifestations: shaping connections and achievements within women's associations are named.
- conflicts/notes: energy and achievement do not establish decisive pursuit or a universally deliberate bond.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes energetic contact; ABC includes demanding ties and creative or productive expressions. These qualify the range without stating that every demanding connection becomes productive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Node = Zeus, Zeus specifies the pair through energetic contact that may take a demanding or creative form. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names energetic connections with women, while [ABC](#abc-entry) adds shaping a maternal connection, creative emotional bonds, and achievements or productivity in association (printed p. 175, PDF p. 86). Its procreative example remains a particular historical association, rather than a conclusion about fertility or the purpose of every bond.
