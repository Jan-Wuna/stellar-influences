## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `129` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Changes in the schedule of the hours for many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `191`, PDF p. `94` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Extensive unfolding of feelings, development of knowledge through feeling, successful development of a people, a woman's knowledge of evolution, developing knowledge through feeling, a woman's experiences of transformation, and a woman's increasing development

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: change affecting many people and extensive emotional or collective development.
- psychology: ABC includes knowledge through feeling and experience of transformation.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: a people's successful development and a woman's increasing development are examples.
- events/manifestations: schedule changes for many people are Witte's specific manifestation.
- conflicts/notes: schedules and emotional knowledge are distinct examples; the entries do not state that one causes the other.

## Contradictions

Witte supplies a concise collective scheduling example; ABC supplies emotional, experiential, and collective development. They differ in specificity without directly negating one another.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Pluto = Apollon, Apollon specifies the pair through change with wider extent. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) is concrete: schedules change for many people. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends development through feelings, knowledge gained through feeling, transformative experience, and the growth of a people (printed p. 191, PDF p. 94). Breadth connects the examples, while their different settings remain distinct.
