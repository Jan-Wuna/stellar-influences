## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `129` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Uncertain attitude toward others and the place. Coming and going. Change of place in hourly rhythm. Change of shift.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `190`, PDF p. `94` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Development with a woman as partner, development of other peoples, emotional changes in the partner, the hour of the partner's transformation, changed feelings toward the partner, transformation of feelings in a place, and transformation of a woman in the surroundings

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `135`, entry `0420`.

#### Ebertin Entry

A violent reaction to environment influences, the tendency to cause upsets in one's environment through one's conduct. Many upsets.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `37` (entry; one-based).

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s personality is powerfully impacted by certain energies from the outside which set up a resonance with energies deep within oneself. One picks up on and take on powerful projections coming from others, and needs to learn how to trans form and assimilate these.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `132`

#### Munkasey Entry

A strong personal focus on the need to eliminate habits or moods that interfere with life; contrasts ideals within against the reality of the world; many private moods and feelings; keeps thinking of another’s problems.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: strong responses to the environment and changes in relations or surroundings.
- psychology: powerful impressions, private moods, and attention to obstructive habits are explicit.
- body/health: no bodily or clinical condition is specified.
- social/relationship: a partner's changes, other people's projections, and concern with another's problems are examples.
- events/manifestations: upsets, coming and going, shifts, and changes of place are named.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's energetic resonance is the source's explanatory model; penetrating intuition and controlling protectiveness are not established.

## Contradictions

Witte describes movement and uncertain attitudes; Ebertin includes forceful reactions and upsets, while Sandbach and Munkasey emphasize assimilating impressions or examining habits. These are different responses to changing surroundings, not mutually exclusive claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Pluto = Asc, Asc specifies the pair through changes in one's surroundings and the force of one's response to them. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) names strong reactions and conduct that causes upsets; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes outside impressions resonating with deeper feelings and needing to be assimilated. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds the effort to remove habits or moods that interfere with life (p. 132).

The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) retains coming and going, shift changes, and uncertainty toward people or place. [ABC](#abc-entry) makes the relational range explicit through changing partners, feelings, and local surroundings (printed p. 190, PDF p. 94). Environmental impact is supported without assuming silent intuition or a controlling character.
