### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `37` (entry; one-based).

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s energy helps others to get in touch with intense feelings, and to experi ence emotional transformation. You automatically stir up energies latent in the subcon scious of others and which need to be released.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: prompting intense feelings and emotional transformation in others.
- psychology: the entry describes latent subconscious material being stirred toward release.
- body/health: no medical treatment or bodily outcome is stated.
- social/relationship: the described effect concerns other people's contact with their feelings.
- events/manifestations: automatically stirring latent energies and experiencing emotional change are the named processes.
- conflicts/notes: the source supports emotional transformation language without establishing a healing result.

## Contradictions

Only one Sandbach entry is present. It describes a process of stirring and release without an independent comparison or a clinical outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Pluto = Chiron, Chiron specifies the pair through prompting other people to encounter intense feelings. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) describes automatically stirring latent subconscious energies toward release and emotional transformation (PDF p. 37). This gives a specific interpersonal process; renewal or therapeutic success is not stated as its necessary outcome.
