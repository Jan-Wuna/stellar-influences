### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `191`, PDF p. `94` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

development of an emotional community, development of a community of women, shared unfolding of feelings, changes through the art of the people, growth of a people's community, and union of women for a development

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: change within family or community, including collective emotional development.
- psychology: shared unfolding of feelings is explicit in ABC.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: families, visitors, women's communities, and a people's community are examples.
- events/manifestations: passing family changes, communal growth, and changes through folk art are named.
- conflicts/notes: visitors and community growth need not represent the same scale or duration of change.

## Contradictions

Witte's passing family changes and visitors are narrower than ABC's developing communities. The broader collective range adds possibilities without showing that every small domestic change produces wider growth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Pluto = Cupido, Cupido specifies the pair through change within shared life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives passing family changes and visitors; [ABC](#abc-entry) describes developing emotional or women's communities, shared unfolding of feelings, and growth through a people's community or art (printed p. 191, PDF p. 94). Family movement and collective development share a social setting, while their scale and duration remain open.
