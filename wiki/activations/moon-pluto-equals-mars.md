## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `129` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Plans through inspiration of the moment. To make quick plans and carry them out immediately. Plan public works.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `190`, PDF p. `94` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Developmental impulses for a people, changes in a woman's will, developing emotionally motivated actions, actions that change the emotional state, unfolding activity of a woman, energetic development of a people, and growing productive activity with a woman

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `135`, entry `0414`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Great changes of mood, easy excitability, inclination to outbursts of anger. Quarrels, injuries.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `36` (entry; one-based).

#### Sandbach Entry

To assert oneself with extreme emotional intensity. The tendency to externalize and/or project onto others one’s deepest emotional conflicts. The overpowering urge to externalize one’s feelings.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `132`

#### Munkasey Entry

Uses force to achieve changes in corrupt practices; active in assuring that the protection of self or family promised is delivered; participates in any clamor as a part of emotional intensity; deep irritation from delays in any form.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: forceful emotional expression and action directed toward change.
- psychology: excitability, anger, emotional intensity, and irritation at delay are explicit.
- body/health: Ebertin includes injuries as a historical manifestation, without establishing a prognosis.
- social/relationship: externalizing conflicts, protecting self or family, and force against corrupt practices are examples.
- events/manifestations: rapid plans, immediate execution, public works, quarrels, and collective development are named.
- conflicts/notes: the entries do not establish resilience without collapse or make vengeance a necessary motive.

## Contradictions

Witte's prompt planning and public works differ from Ebertin's quarrels and Sandbach's projected conflicts. Purposeful activity and conflict are different possible expressions; one does not guarantee the other.

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Pluto = Mars, Mars specifies the pair through strong emotional impulses carried into action. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives quick plans put into immediate practice and public works, while [ABC](#abc-entry) describes developing activity and actions that alter emotional states (printed p. 190, PDF p. 94). [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) retains force used to change corrupt practices or secure promised protection (p. 132).

The emotional force can also become conflict: [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) names excitability, anger, quarrels, and injuries; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes projecting deep emotional conflicts onto others. Action is the shared thread, while its purpose and outcome differ across the entries.
