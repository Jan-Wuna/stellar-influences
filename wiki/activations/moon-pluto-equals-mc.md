## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be exposed to fast or hourly changes. Continuous readjusting. To be “Johnny on the spot”. Likes to attend to his affairs without delay.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Implication of women, development of a fine tuning soul, my growing people, my GcmUts ycrung, scelic change of a woman, hour of my change, hour and minute of change, transformation of a people soul, and evolution of my people

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A highly strained emotional life, one-sidedness, the inclination to remain and to act alone and never in accord with other people.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A career involving the nurturing of people on a mass scale. At best, a powerful openness to messages coming from the spiritual realms. One’s spiritual path involves being attentive to impulses coming from their subconscious and supporting their process of outworking until all stress and karma is cleared.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Attaining positive psychological growth thru the analysis and evaluation of your varying moods; fulfilling your personal inner aims when conquering emotionally based tendencies; successful handling of the moods of women.
