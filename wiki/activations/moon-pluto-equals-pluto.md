## Source Entries

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/PLUTO With Itself`
- Source page: `134`

#### Munkasey Entry

Added feelings that you must fight against whatever evils there are in life, even if you must drastically alter or rearrange your living circumstances to do this; feelings which are deep and personal; drastic mood variations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: deep personal feelings intensify an urge to oppose what is experienced as evil.
- psychology: emotional conviction and drastic mood variation accompany that urge.
- body/health: no specific bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: the entry does not identify a particular partner or social outcome.
- events/manifestations: a willingness to alter living circumstances drastically in order to pursue the struggle.
- conflicts/notes: "evils" expresses the entry's felt moral urgency; it does not establish that the perceived threat is objectively evil.

## Contradictions

- Only Munkasey's entry is present. Its moral urgency, deep feelings, and mood variation are different features of one account, not competing claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies the Moon/Pluto pair as a deeply personal emotional imperative to fight what one experiences as evil, potentially strong enough to prompt drastic changes in living circumstances. Munkasey joins this conviction to marked mood variation, so intensity does not imply emotional steadiness ([Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry), p. 134).
