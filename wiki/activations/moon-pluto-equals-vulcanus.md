## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Moon + Pluto`, PDF p. `129` (entry start; printed page not supplied).

#### Witte Entry

Mighty changes in the public, in the executive power, for persons or people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Mond + Pluto`, printed p. `191`, PDF p. `94` (row checked against the German scan).

#### ABC Entry

influential hour of transformation, developing violence against the woman, violent changes among the people, woman developing influence, unfolding of strength of a woman, strong changes in the emotional nature, changed emotional influence, and growing influence of the woman

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: changes carry unusual force or alter influence and power.
- psychology: ABC includes strong changes in emotional nature and emotional influence.
- body/health: strength is mentioned, but neither entry identifies a specific medical condition.
- social/relationship: changes in executive power and women's influence appear alongside the distinct possibility of violence against a woman.
- events/manifestations: powerful public changes, violent changes among people, and growing influence.
- conflicts/notes: empowerment and violence are different possibilities; the source's gendered examples are not universal claims about women.

## Contradictions

- The entries share forceful change as a theme. ABC's growing influence and violence describe different forms that force may take, not evidence that increased influence must involve violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies the Moon/Pluto pair through forceful change in public conditions, executive power, and personal influence. ABC extends this to strong emotional change and a woman's growing strength or influence, while also retaining violence as a separate, adverse possibility ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 191). The common thread is the force carried by change; increased power and harmful coercion must not be treated as the same outcome.
