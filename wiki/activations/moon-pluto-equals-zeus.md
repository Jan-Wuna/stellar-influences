## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Moon + Pluto`, PDF p. `129` (entry start; printed page not supplied).

#### Witte Entry

To enter new service. Something new. New obligations. Increased production.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Mond + Pluto`, printed p. `191`, PDF p. `94` (row and English translation checked against the German scan).

#### ABC Entry

Beginning development of feelings, development with a demanding woman, a woman developing achievement, creative transformation of a woman, increase of a people through procreation, growing achievements of a people, and transformation of a woman through procreation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: development takes the form of new activity, obligations, creation, or production.
- psychology: ABC includes the beginning development of feelings and the demands of creative change.
- body/health: ABC's procreation examples are source-specific possibilities, not fertility predictions; the German entry does not say "ailment."
- social/relationship: new service and obligations can involve others; ABC includes development with a demanding woman.
- events/manifestations: entering new service, increased production, growing achievements, and creative or procreative change.
- conflicts/notes: new duties and procreation are distinct examples of productive development; neither should be inferred in every case.

## Contradictions

- No direct contradiction is stated. Witte describes new duties and production; ABC adds emotional, creative, and procreative forms of development without claiming that all must occur together.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies the Moon/Pluto pair through development that becomes productive or creates fresh demands. Witte names new service, obligations, and increased production; ABC includes feelings beginning to develop, creative change, growing achievement, and procreation ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 191). These are particular ways that something new takes shape, not grounds for predicting an illness or treating every new duty as a birth.
