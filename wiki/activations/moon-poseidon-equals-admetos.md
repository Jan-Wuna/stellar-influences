## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Moon + Poseidon`, PDF p. `138` (entry start; printed page not supplied).

#### Witte Entry

To go to the bottom with the help of tact and fine delicate feeling. Journalist.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Mond + Poseidon`, printed p. `207`, PDF p. `102` (row and English translation checked against the German scan).

#### ABC Entry

Blockage of mind and mood, depression in a woman through truth, insight of a steadfast woman, strengthening of true feelings, a mentally blocked people, mental oppression of a woman, mental deepening among the people, and mental deepening in a woman.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sensitive understanding goes deeper or encounters restriction.
- psychology: tact, fine feeling, steadfast insight, and strengthening true feelings coexist with mental blockage.
- body/health: ABC's depression is an attributed description connected to confronting truth, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: ABC includes mental oppression; Witte names journalism as a particular example of probing understanding.
- events/manifestations: getting to the bottom of a matter, mental deepening, or restricted intellectual and emotional expression.
- conflicts/notes: deepening and blockage are different possibilities; the entry does not make one a necessary consequence of the other.

## Contradictions

- Witte emphasizes the capacity to penetrate a matter sensitively, while ABC includes both deepening and inhibition. These directions remain distinct; the page does not establish whether depth or restriction will prevail.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies the Moon/Poseidon pair through the depth or restriction of sensitive understanding. Witte describes getting to the bottom of a matter through tact and delicate feeling, with journalism as a concrete example; ABC also includes steadfast insight, true feelings becoming firmer, and mental deepening ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 207).

ABC retains a more difficult side: mind and mood can be blocked, truth can be burdensome, and mental oppression can occur. The capacity for depth therefore should not be read as proof of unrestricted insight or emotional ease.
