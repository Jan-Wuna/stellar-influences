## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Moon + Poseidon`, PDF p. `138` (entry start; printed page not supplied).

#### Witte Entry

Congeniality in higher spiritual thinking. Sense or flair for new sciences. Feeling ones way into new fields of knowledge.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Mond + Poseidon`, printed p. `207`, PDF p. `102` (row and English translation checked against the German scan).

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of a people's culture, success of a sensible woman, successful ideas of a woman, freedom of mind among the people, mental or spiritual experiences of a woman, intellectual culture of a people, clarity in emotional knowledge, and an hour of experiencing truth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: intuitive understanding opens into wider knowledge and cultural development.
- psychology: a flair for new sciences and feeling one's way into unfamiliar knowledge are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health manifestation.
- social/relationship: congeniality in higher thinking and cultural freedom are represented.
- events/manifestations: successful ideas, expansion of culture, new fields of knowledge, and experiences of truth.
- conflicts/notes: the entries describe receptivity and possible success, not scientific proof of an intuition or guaranteed achievement.

## Contradictions

- No direct contradiction is stated. Witte concentrates on entering new knowledge; ABC gives cultural expansion, intellectual freedom, and successful ideas as related but separate manifestations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies the Moon/Poseidon pair through an opening of sensitive understanding toward new knowledge. A flair for unfamiliar sciences and congeniality in higher thinking can become exploration of new fields; ABC broadens the range to successful ideas, cultural expansion, and freedom of mind ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 207). The orientation describes receptivity and widening experience, while leaving the validity and success of any particular idea to be established.
