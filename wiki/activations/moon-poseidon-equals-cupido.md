## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Moon + Poseidon`, PDF p. `138` (entry start; printed page not supplied).

#### Witte Entry

Spiritual community. To be a member of a spiritual community. The audience.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Mond + Poseidon`, printed p. `207`, PDF p. `102` (row checked against the German scan).

#### ABC Entry

woman for art and culture, spirit of a people's community, community of ideas of a people, clarity of an emotional community, cultural women's community, hour of truth for of a community, and reasonable women of a community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: shared spiritual, intellectual, or cultural life takes communal form.
- psychology: shared ideas and clarity within an emotional community are represented.
- body/health: neither entry gives a bodily or health manifestation.
- social/relationship: membership in a spiritual community, a cultural women's group, or an audience.
- events/manifestations: participation in art and culture and an hour of truth for a community.
- conflicts/notes: a shared community of ideas does not by itself establish unanimity, harmony, or the truth of those ideas.

## Contradictions

- No direct conflict is stated. Witte's spiritual community and audience are broader examples; ABC specifies cultural, intellectual, and emotional forms of community.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies the Moon/Poseidon pair through shared participation in spiritual, intellectual, and cultural life. Witte names membership in a spiritual community and an audience; ABC includes a community of ideas, cultural groups, and clarity within an emotional community ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 207). Understanding becomes something held or experienced together, while the entries leave the group's degree of agreement and the truth of its beliefs open.
