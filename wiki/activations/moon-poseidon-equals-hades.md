## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Moon + Poseidon`, PDF p. `138` (entry start; printed page not supplied).

#### Witte Entry

Insensible of finer, high spiritual things. Lack of empathy. Men who are unable to perceive or conceive spiritual things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Mond + Poseidon`, printed p. `207`, PDF p. `102` (row and English translation checked against the German scan).

#### ABC Entry

Insight of an old woman, insight into a woman's secret, cultural lack among a people, privations of a sensible woman, criminal ideas of a woman, an hour of dangerous truth, a people's past culture, and hiding true feelings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: understanding is limited, concealed, or directed toward difficult and hidden material.
- psychology: Witte describes deficient empathy or spiritual receptivity; ABC also allows insight into secrets and concealed feelings.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a specific bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: cultural deprivation, secrecy, and lack of empathy can affect relations; ABC's gendered examples remain attributed.
- events/manifestations: encountering dangerous truth, cultural lack, the past culture of a people, or hidden feelings.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's inability to perceive spiritual things and ABC's examples of insight are a real difference in emphasis; do not turn the whole page into either wisdom or incapacity.

## Contradictions

- Witte emphasizes deficient perception, while ABC explicitly permits insight into age, secrets, and difficult truth. The entries do not explain how these capacities coexist; preserve the tension rather than treating every hidden matter as proof of deeper wisdom.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies the Moon/Poseidon pair through a troubled relation to understanding: perception may be limited, or its subject may be concealed and difficult. Witte stresses lack of empathy and inability to perceive spiritual matters, whereas ABC includes insight into secrets, dangerous truth, cultural deprivation, and hidden feelings ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 207).

The entries therefore leave a genuine range between deficient receptivity and selective insight into unwelcome material. Neither a general claim of wisdom nor a universal judgment about a person's empathy captures that range.
