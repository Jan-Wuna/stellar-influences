## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Moon + Poseidon`, PDF p. `138` (Kronos row checked against the scan; printed page not supplied).

#### Witte Entry

Mentally highly attuned, to associate with such people. A major Tsunami.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Mond + Poseidon`, printed p. `207`, PDF p. `102` (row and English translation checked against the German scan).

#### ABC Entry

Reputation of a cultured people, special clarity of feelings, mental or spiritual guidance of a woman, cultural hierarchy among the people, reason of a great woman, an independent cultured people, clarity of a respected woman, and mental or spiritual hierarchy of a people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: mental attunement and clarity are associated with respected people, guidance, or cultural standing.
- psychology: heightened attunement, reason, and clear feelings are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a medical claim.
- social/relationship: association with highly attuned people, cultural hierarchy, and respected women are represented.
- events/manifestations: cultural reputation or independence; Witte also separately lists "A major Tsunami."
- conflicts/notes: the tsunami is a verified literal source example, but the entry gives no explanation connecting it to the psychological meanings.

## Contradictions

- The mental and cultural examples do not directly conflict. Witte's tsunami statement occupies a separate descriptive register and should remain a discrete source claim rather than a consequence inferred from mental attunement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies the Moon/Poseidon pair through mental attunement linked with respected people and cultural standing. Witte describes being highly attuned or associating with such people; ABC adds clarity of feeling, reason, guidance, reputation, and cultural hierarchy ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 207).

Witte also lists a major tsunami. No explanation is supplied that would allow it to be derived from the psychological reading or predicted from it.
