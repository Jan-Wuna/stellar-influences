## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Moon + Poseidon`, PDF p. `138` (entry start; printed page not supplied).

#### Witte Entry

High intellectual activity. To follow unconsciously inspired guidance in one’s intellectual activity. To act on instinct. To battle for spiritual things. Activity in field of occultism.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Mond + Poseidon`, printed p. `206`, PDF p. `102` (row and English translation checked against the German scan).

#### ABC Entry

Mental or spiritual creation of a woman, mental activity of a people, actions of a woman arising from insight, actions with clear feelings, cultural work of a woman, sensible impulses of mood, clarity of a woman's will, and a people's will for truth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: insight and inspiration become intellectual, cultural, or spiritual activity.
- psychology: instinctive guidance, clarity of will, and acting from insight are represented.
- body/health: neither entry states a bodily or health manifestation.
- social/relationship: cultural work and efforts on behalf of spiritual matters can involve a wider people or community.
- events/manifestations: intellectual work, cultural creation, activity in occultism, and a struggle for spiritual aims.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's battle for spiritual things does not make the ABC mood impulses military; the German clause describes sensible impulses.

## Contradictions

- No direct conflict is stated. Unconsciously inspired guidance and deliberate clarity of will are different modes of action in the entries, without a claim that one excludes the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies the Moon/Poseidon pair by turning mental and spiritual receptivity into activity. Witte describes intellectual work guided by instinct or unconscious inspiration, including efforts for spiritual matters and occult interests; ABC adds action arising from insight, cultural work, and clarity of will ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 206). The unifying movement is from impression to action, with both instinctive and more explicitly reasoned forms represented.
