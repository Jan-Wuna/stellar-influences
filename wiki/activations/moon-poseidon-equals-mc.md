## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Moon + Poseidon`, PDF p. `138` (entry start; printed page not supplied).

#### Witte Entry

Mentally prepared. Susceptible to spiritual things. To be influenced by spiritualized people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Mond + Poseidon`, printed p. `206`, PDF p. `102` (row and English translation checked against the German scan).

#### ABC Entry

Soulful culture of a people, the mind and soul of a woman, ideas of my wife, clear feelings of the soul, my feeling for culture, my spiritualized wife, my sensible wife, my clear feelings, the hour of my insight, and truth about my people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal receptivity to spiritual influence and inner clarity.
- psychology: mental preparedness, susceptibility, clear feelings, and a moment of insight are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry gives a bodily or medical manifestation.
- social/relationship: being influenced by spiritualized people; ABC also gives first-person examples concerning a wife.
- events/manifestations: personal realization, a feeling for culture, and insight concerning one's people.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's first-person spouse examples are source-native illustrations, not required identities for every reader.

## Contradictions

- The entries place receptive influence beside personal clarity, without saying that influence must either improve or diminish that clarity. No direct incompatibility is established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies the Moon/Poseidon pair as personally felt receptivity and realization. Witte describes mental preparedness and openness to spiritual matters or spiritualized people; ABC gives the experience an inward vocabulary of clear feelings, a feeling for culture, and the hour of one's own insight ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 206). Its spouse and people-related examples show where that awareness may be directed, without making them prerequisites for the reading.
