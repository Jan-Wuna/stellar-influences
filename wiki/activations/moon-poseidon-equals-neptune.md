## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Moon + Poseidon`, PDF p. `138` (entry start; printed page not supplied).

#### Witte Entry

Highly attuned. Delicacy of feeling. Intuition, instinct, flair. A very fine and highly sensitive ability to find the way.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source passage: `Mond + Poseidon`, printed p. `207`, PDF p. `102` (row checked against the German scan).

#### ABC Entry

disappointment through a spiritual woman, weakness of insight of a woman, sensitivity of an insightful woman, foreign spirit in the people, ideas for the future of a people, culture of a foreign people, weak spirit of a woman, and deceptive emotional influence

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: unusually delicate perception can guide or mislead.
- psychology: intuition, instinct, flair, sensitivity, weak insight, and disappointment are represented.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a specific health manifestation.
- social/relationship: ABC includes disappointment through a woman and deceptive emotional influence; these remain attributed possibilities.
- events/manifestations: finding one's way sensitively, ideas for the future, and encounters with another people's culture.
- conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes effective intuition, while ABC explicitly admits weak insight and deception; sensitivity is not a sufficient test of accuracy.

## Contradictions

- There is a material difference between Witte's ability to find the way and ABC's weak insight or deceptive influence. The entries support both possibilities without giving a rule for deciding which applies.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies the Moon/Poseidon pair through very delicate perception whose reliability remains open. Witte emphasizes intuition, instinct, and a sensitive ability to find the way; ABC preserves sensitivity but also names weak insight, disappointment, and deceptive emotional influence ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 207). The useful reading retains both guidance and susceptibility to error, rather than treating the strength of an impression as proof of its truth.
