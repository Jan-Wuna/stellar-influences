## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `138` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To see and recognize new mental or spiritual development. Transformation of the state of mind.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `207`, PDF p. `102` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

development of a cultivated woman, spiritual changes in a people, spiritual growth of a woman, emotional change through ideas, change of ideas of a woman, cultural development of a woman, and growing insight of a woman

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: recognizing new mental or spiritual development and a changing state of mind.
- psychology: growing insight and emotional change through ideas are explicit.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: a cultured woman's development and spiritual changes among a people are examples.
- events/manifestations: changes of ideas and cultural development are named.
- conflicts/notes: recognizing development does not establish that every new idea is true or beneficial.

## Contradictions

Both entries concern development of mind or spirit. ABC adds cultural and emotional examples without supplying a rule that every change produces clearer understanding.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Poseidon = Pluto, Pluto specifies the pair through recognition of new mental or spiritual development. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links seeing that development with transformation of the state of mind. [ABC](#abc-entry) makes the process more particular through changing ideas, emotional change arising from ideas, cultural growth, and increasing insight (printed p. 207, PDF p. 102). Understanding develops through change, while the quality of any particular new idea remains open.
