## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `138` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The daily repeating hours of susceptibility. To be in company with such people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `206`, PDF p. `102` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A mental or spiritual centre of a people, clarity between woman and man, clarity of personal feelings, an hour of truth for a man, a day of enlightenment for a woman, a woman's rational man, a man's spiritually minded woman, and a centre of folk culture

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: recurring receptivity and personal or collective clarity.
- psychology: clarity of personal feelings and enlightenment are explicit.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: companionship with receptive people and clarity between a woman and a man are examples.
- events/manifestations: repeated hours of susceptibility and cultural or spiritual centres are named.
- conflicts/notes: the temporal examples do not establish a method for predicting when insight will occur.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes recurring receptivity and company; ABC emphasizes clarity, truth, and culture. These describe different expressions without establishing that receptivity always becomes insight.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Poseidon = Sun, Sun specifies the pair through receptivity expressed in daily company and personal clarity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names repeating hours of susceptibility and association with receptive people. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds clarity between women and men, clear personal feelings, moments of truth or enlightenment, and a people's cultural centre (printed p. 206, PDF p. 102). The connection is awareness in personal and shared life, without making every receptive moment a realization.
