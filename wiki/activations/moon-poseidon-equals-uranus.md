## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `138` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be exposed to new, unknown mental or spiritual influences. Suddenly to see through something, understand if and know it. To get sudden mental or spiritual contact. Capable of empathy for new and revolutionary perceptions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `207`, PDF p. `102` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Exciting truths of a woman, renewal of a people's ideas, mental or spiritual reform among the people, a woman's sudden clarity, technology of a cultured people, a surprising truth for a woman, rational impulses of a woman, and true emotional impulses

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sudden understanding, new mental influences, and intellectual or spiritual renewal.
- psychology: openness to unfamiliar perceptions, sudden clarity, and rational or emotional impulses are named.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: new mental or spiritual contact and reform among a people are examples.
- events/manifestations: renewed ideas, sudden recognition, surprising truth, and cultural technology are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: the sources call some perceptions true without giving a practical test for the truth of every sudden impression.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize new or sudden perceptions. Their favorable language does not establish that every unfamiliar influence is accurate; no direct incompatible claim appears.

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Poseidon = Uranus, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden contact with new ideas and unfamiliar mental or spiritual influences. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes rapid recognition and receptivity to revolutionary perceptions. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds renewed ideas, reform, sudden clarity, and surprising truth (printed p. 207, PDF p. 102). The shared pattern is a break into new understanding, with the particular cultural and technological examples retained as possibilities.
