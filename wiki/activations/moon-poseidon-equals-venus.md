## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `138` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Highly sensitive women. Female persons with inclination to occultism.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `206`, PDF p. `102` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A people's culture of peace, harmony with cultured women, clear feelings of love, love of a rational woman, spiritualized love for a woman, spiritualization of a beautiful woman, rational women of a people, and rational harmony of feelings

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sensitivity, harmonious feeling, and love in a cultured or spiritual setting.
- psychology: clear love feelings and rational harmony of feeling are explicit.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is specified.
- social/relationship: cultured or rational women, love, and a people's culture of peace are examples.
- events/manifestations: Witte names inclination to occultism; ABC names spiritualized love and harmony.
- conflicts/notes: the historical gendered examples do not establish a universal temperament of women.

## Contradictions

Witte's highly sensitive women and occult inclination differ from ABC's peace, reason, and harmonious love. The examples can share a refined setting without becoming equivalent practices or character traits.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Poseidon = Venus, Venus specifies the pair through sensitivity and harmonious or spiritualized affection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives highly sensitive women and an inclination to occultism; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds clear love feelings, cultured or rational women, and a culture of peace (printed p. 206, PDF p. 102). The supported connection is between sensitive awareness and the quality of affection or culture, without reducing all the examples to one gendered personality.
