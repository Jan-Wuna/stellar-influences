## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `138` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Spiritualized men. Media.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `206`, PDF p. `102` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

An insightful people in public, insights of the mothers of this world, the spirit of a people in public life, cultured women in public, an hour of truth in public, and a people's culture in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: spiritual or cultural awareness in the public sphere.
- psychology: ABC names insight and truth; Witte names spiritualized people.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: peoples in public life, cultured women, and the world's mothers are examples.
- events/manifestations: public culture and an hour of truth in public are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's Media remains its source wording; the entry does not explain a modern media occupation or a private setting.

## Contradictions

Witte's brief mention of people and Media differs in detail from ABC's public cultural examples. No direct disagreement appears, but the brevity does not establish that every example denotes a public broadcast.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Poseidon = Vernal Point, Vernal Point specifies the pair through people and spiritual or cultural awareness in public life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) briefly names spiritualized men and Media. [ABC](#abc-entry) makes the public setting explicit through an insightful people, cultured women, a people's culture, and an hour of truth (printed p. 206, PDF p. 102). Witte's brief Media reference remains distinct from an assumed modern occupation.
