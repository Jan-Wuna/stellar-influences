## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `138` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Great sensitivity for men and things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `207`, PDF p. `102` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Mental or spiritual violence against a people, mental or spiritual power of a people, influence of a spiritually minded woman, cultural power among the people, strength through clarity of feelings, cultural influence of a woman, strength of a truthful woman, and power of truth in the hour

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: strong sensitivity and mental, spiritual, or cultural influence.
- psychology: clarity of feeling is associated with strength in ABC.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is specified.
- social/relationship: a spiritually minded or truthful woman and collective cultural power are examples.
- events/manifestations: mental or spiritual violence and the power of truth are explicitly distinct manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: sensitivity and influence do not establish that power is beneficial or harmless.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sensitivity to people and things; ABC ranges from clarity and cultural influence to violence against a people. This wider range prevents treating sensitivity as proof of benevolent influence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Poseidon = Vulcanus, Vulcanus specifies the pair through strong sensitivity and influence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives sensitivity to people and things; [ABC](#abc-entry) extends strength into clarity of feelings, cultural influence, and collective mental or spiritual power (printed p. 207, PDF p. 102). ABC also names violence against a people, so the force of an influence leaves its purpose and effects open.
