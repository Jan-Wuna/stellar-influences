## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `138` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Creative mental accomplishments. To mentally force circumstances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `207`, PDF p. `102` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A demanding hour of truth, the spirit of a leading people, mental or spiritual goals of a woman, ideas of a leading woman, a clear direction of feelings, cultural goals of a woman, cultural efforts of a people, and a rational leader of the people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creative mental achievement and effort directed by ideas or goals.
- psychology: clear direction of feelings and purposeful ideas are named.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: a leading woman, leading people, and a rational public leader are examples.
- events/manifestations: creative accomplishments, cultural efforts, and a demanding hour of truth are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: mentally forcing circumstances is a source example, without establishing that every achievement requires coercion.

## Contradictions

Witte's creative achievement and forcing circumstances differ in tone from ABC's rational leadership and cultural aims. These are distinct modes of directed effort, without an explicit statement that one excludes the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Poseidon = Zeus, Zeus specifies the pair through creative mental effort and directed aims. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names intellectual accomplishment and mentally forcing circumstances. [ABC](#abc-entry) places ideas, cultural goals, clear direction of feelings, and rational leadership within the range (printed p. 207, PDF p. 102). The connecting theme is bringing an idea or aim into effect, with effort and force retained as possibilities rather than a required method.
