## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `126` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Female teachers. Hours of lessons. The many evening hours.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `185`, PDF p. `91` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Experiences of a mature woman, success through separation from a woman, patience of an experienced woman, disturbed trade of a people, expanding problems for a woman, an increase in disturbances of feeling, much patience in a woman, and a woman's scientific tasks

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: teaching, experience, extended time, and a wider range of tasks or problems.
- psychology: mature experience and patience coexist with disturbances of feeling.
- body/health: ABC's disturbed feelings are historical descriptions without a clinical condition being established.
- social/relationship: female teachers, an experienced woman, and collective trade difficulties are examples.
- events/manifestations: lessons, many evening hours, scientific tasks, and success through separation are named.
- conflicts/notes: greater extent may concern patience or problems; neither is the automatic result of education.

## Contradictions

Witte supplies teachers, lessons, and many evening hours. ABC adds experience and scientific tasks but also expanding problems and disturbed trade. The educational range does not make all the expressions productive or successful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Saturn = Apollon, Apollon specifies the pair through experience, instruction, and extended demands on time or patience. [Witte](#witte-entry) names female teachers, lessons, and many evening hours. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds mature experience and scientific tasks, while retaining disturbed trade, expanding problems, and emotional disturbances (printed p. 185, PDF p. 91). The common range is sustained or extensive engagement, whose outcome may include learning and patience as well as difficulty.
