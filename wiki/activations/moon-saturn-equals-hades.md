## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `126` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Severe sickness of an elderly female. Saddening obstacles and difficulties. A nation under foreign rule.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `185`, PDF p. `91` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A people burdened by criminality, an old woman's sorrow, a woman matured through privations, a woman's patient service, danger to an old woman, disadvantages through disturbances of feeling, a people's distress through losses, and problems of a criminal woman

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: hardship, sorrow, and disadvantage under difficult personal or collective conditions.
- psychology: sorrow, emotional disturbance, patience, and maturity through privation are named.
- body/health: Witte's severe illness in an elderly woman is a historical association, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: patient service, older women, a nation under foreign rule, and criminal burdens are examples.
- events/manifestations: obstacles, collective losses, and danger are stated.
- conflicts/notes: the entry includes service and maturity as well as distress; it does not establish inevitable defeat.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses severe illness, obstacles, and foreign rule; ABC also preserves patient service and maturity through privation. These broaden the range without promising that hardship is either wholly defeating or beneficial.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Saturn = Hades, Hades specifies the pair through hardship and sorrow that may concern an individual or a people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives difficult obstacles, a nation under foreign rule, and a historical illness association. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds losses, emotional disturbance, and criminal burdens, but also patient service and maturity through privation (printed p. 185, PDF p. 91). The range therefore includes ways of enduring difficult conditions without making defeat or illness a necessary outcome.
