## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `126` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Wife separated or divorced by law. Resistances through self-consciousness. Hindrances through administrative authorities. Head mistress of a charitable institution.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `185`, PDF p. `91` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Reputation of an old lady, special evening hour, women's problems of the state, great pain of a woman, problems of a respected people, difficulties of a woman with the boss, superiority of a patient woman, and woman separated from the state

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: separation, resistance, or responsibility in relation to formal authority.
- psychology: self-conscious resistance and patience are named.
- body/health: ABC's pain example remains historical doctrine, without a medical finding.
- social/relationship: legal divorce, administrative obstacles, institutional leadership, and difficulties with a superior are examples.
- events/manifestations: official separation and a special evening hour are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: authority may obstruct as well as confer responsibility; the entries do not say institutions resolve grief.

## Contradictions

Witte includes both administrative hindrance and leadership of a charitable institution; ABC includes respect, difficulty, and separation from the state. These are different positions within authority rather than a uniform benefit or harm.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Saturn = Kronos, Kronos specifies the pair through the formal conditions of responsibility, separation, and resistance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places legal divorce and administrative obstacles alongside leadership of a charitable institution. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds an older woman's standing, difficulties with a superior, state-related problems, and patience (printed p. 185, PDF p. 91). The common issue is a person's relation to authority; official involvement need not imply that the burden is successfully managed.
