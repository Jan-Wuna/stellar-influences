## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Moon + Saturn`
- Source page: `126`
#### Witte Entry

Connection with old, sick or unfortunate women or widows. Separation of connections, of unions, affecting the mind and female persons for a long time. Connections, which overwhelmingly dominate the evening hours.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Moon/Saturn`
- Source page: `127`
- Entry: `0383`

#### Ebertin Entry

Lack of adaptability and self-confidence, tendency to shun other people. Association with uncommunicative, lonely or sick women.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Moon/Saturn midpoint`
- Source page: `31`
- Activator: `Node`

#### Sandbach Entry

Feeling emotionally vulnerable in groups. The need to seek out contacts with others that will acknowledge one’s deepest feelings and share their own. Sad, heavy or depressing feelings about the past. The need to let go of negative emotions connected to the past.
