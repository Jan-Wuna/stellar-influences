## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `126` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Unfortunate mother. All alone in marriage. Wishes which come not through. Unmarried mother.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `184`, PDF p. `91` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disturbed peace of a people, disturbances of a woman's harmony, sorrow of a beautiful woman, art of a mature woman, love as a woman's task, love of a mature woman, problematic love of a woman, and beauty of a mature woman

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `126`, entry `0377`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Changes of mood, inhibitions in love expression, lack of self-confidence, the capability to renounce. Resignation, inclination to stand alone in love or marriage, illegitimate associations, disappointment in love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `31` (entry; one-based).

#### Sandbach Entry

Feeling emotionally vulnerable in relationships. A love of sharing deep emotions. A love of sadness or crying. Difficulty in taking care of the loved one. Having a love who lacks feeling and/or who is emotionally repressed.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `120`

#### Munkasey Entry

The pleasant feelings you learned about parenting from various role models in your life; the satisfaction you get by attuning to inner feelings of caring, parenting, or concern; internal musical rhythms or beats you create or use.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: vulnerability and inhibition in affection alongside satisfaction in caring and artistic feeling.
- psychology: lack of confidence, renunciation, sadness, and attunement to caring feelings are named.
- body/health: none of these entries supplies a specific bodily condition.
- social/relationship: loneliness in marriage, difficult care of a loved one, emotional sharing, and parenting models are examples.
- events/manifestations: disappointed wishes, problematic love, mature art or beauty, and internal musical rhythms are listed.
- conflicts/notes: pleasant caring experiences and relational disappointment are separate possibilities; lasting affection is not guaranteed.

## Contradictions

Munkasey's satisfaction in caring and Sandbach's enjoyment of emotional sharing broaden the range beyond the older entries' loneliness and disappointment. They do not cancel those difficulties or establish marital fidelity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Saturn = Venus, Venus specifies the pair through affection that may expose vulnerability, restraint, or loneliness. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) names inhibition in expressing love and disappointment; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) includes both deep emotional sharing and difficulty caring for a loved one. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) retains unfulfilled wishes and loneliness in marriage as historical examples.

The [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry) also describes satisfaction in caring, learned parenting models, and internal musical rhythms (p. 120), while [ABC](#abc-entry) includes the love, art, and beauty of a mature woman alongside disturbed harmony (printed p. 184, PDF p. 91). The entry range permits warmth and creative feeling without establishing enduring or uncomplicated relationships.
