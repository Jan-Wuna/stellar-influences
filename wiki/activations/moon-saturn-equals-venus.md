## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Unfortunate mother. All alone in marriage. Wishes which come not through. Unmarried mother.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Lost peace of a people, harmonies of a woman, sorrow of a gentle woman, kiss of a mature woman, love as a woman's task, love of a mature woman, problematic love of a woman, and beauty of a mature woman

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Changes of mood, inhibitions in love expression, lack of self-confidence, the capability to renounce. Resignation, inclination to stand alone in love or marriage, illegitimate associations, disappointment in love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Feeling emotionally vulnerable in relationships. A love of sharing deep emotions. A love of sadness or crying. Difficulty in taking care of the loved one. Having a love who lacks feeling and/or who is emotionally repressed.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

The pleasant feelings you learned about parenting from various role models in your life; the satisfaction you get by attuning to inner feelings of caring, parenting, or concern; internal musical rhythms or beats you create or use.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Saturn = Venus`: enduring care, faithful warmth, and the ability to make affection dependable and concrete. Main caution: inhibited softness: fear of loss or disappointment can make love feel careful, sparse, or slow to relax.
