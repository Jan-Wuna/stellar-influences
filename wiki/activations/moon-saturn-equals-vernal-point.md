## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Laboring people (classes), suffering, suppressed populace. Losses in the populace. General happenings in the evening hours.

Source locator: PDF p. 126.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Old woman in public, patience of a people in public, emotional disturbance in the public, public problems of a people, woman separated from public life, losses of a people in public.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 184, PDF p. 91. The former wording about public-service termination was a translation error.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Public suffering, restraint, patience, and loss ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** ABC describes patience and disturbed feeling in a public setting; Witte emphasizes suffering and suppression.
- **Body/health:** These entries do not identify a bodily condition; suffering is not a diagnosis.
- **Social/relationship:** Laboring classes and a suppressed populace appear in Witte; ABC includes an older woman in public and a woman cut off from public life.
- **Events/manifestations:** Collective losses and public problems; Witte also records general evening happenings.
- **Conflicts/notes:** The gendered examples belong to the historical source wording. The evening association is a separate, less specific motif.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in collective loss and hardship. Public visibility and exclusion from public life are alternative examples, not a claim that both occur together; no direct contradiction is established here.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies the Moon/Saturn pair through hardship, patience, or loss experienced in public life. The emphasis falls on a population or social group carrying a burden: laboring classes, a suppressed populace, or people enduring public problems and losses. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and the scan-checked [ABC entry](#abc-entry) support this collective setting without making suffering inevitable.

Witte also associates this orientation with general evening happenings. ABC's older woman in public and woman separated from public life remain particular historical examples of visibility and exclusion, rather than universal statements about women.
