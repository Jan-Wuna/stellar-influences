## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Very inhibited in the public. Men on guard duty. People in a state of alertness. The high point of the day is in the evening.

Source locator: PDF p. 126.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influential old woman, influence through a woman's patience, woman separating by force, forcible separation from a woman, problematic influence of a woman, enormous problems of a people, painful violence against a woman, strong emotional burden.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 185, PDF p. 91. “Strong militia load” mistranslated “starke Gemüts-Belastung.”

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Heavy pressure, public inhibition, vigilance, and forceful separation ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Alertness and inhibition in Witte; a strong emotional burden and patience under influence in ABC.
- **Body/health:** ABC names painful violence, but neither entry establishes a medical condition.
- **Social/relationship:** Guard duty and a watchful population; ABC adds an influential older woman and coercive separation involving a woman.
- **Events/manifestations:** Public restraint, forcible separation, violence, and large collective problems; Witte separately emphasizes the evening.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Witte's guard-duty example does not turn ABC's emotional burden into a military burden. Historical gendered examples remain attributed.

## Contradictions

The entries describe different forms of pressure and restraint without a direct opposition. ABC's forceful influence and Witte's inhibition are distinct examples; the evening motif is additional rather than contradictory.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Moon/Saturn through considerable pressure: public inhibition and vigilance in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), and heavy emotional burden, coercive separation, or powerful influence in the [ABC entry](#abc-entry). The connected theme is strain experienced under force or restraint, whether by a person or a population.

Guard duty and violence are concrete source examples, not requirements for every reading. Witte's evening emphasis and ABC's influential older woman extend the range without reducing the whole orientation to either example.
