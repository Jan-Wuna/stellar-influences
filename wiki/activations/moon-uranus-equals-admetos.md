## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Small events. Excitement in a little circle. Small revolts (revolutions), nipped in the bud. Squelching or localizing bigger revolts.

Source locator: PDF p. 127.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blocked dynamism of a woman, eventful hour in a traffic jam, agitated resistance of a woman, new depressions of a woman, sudden restriction of feeling, tensions in an oppressed people, suppressed emotional impulses.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 187, PDF p. 92. “Gemüt,” “unterdrückten Volk,” and “unterdrückte Gefühls-Impulse” refer to feeling, an oppressed people, and suppressed emotional impulses.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Excitement or resistance confined, blocked, or suppressed ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Restricted feeling and suppressed impulses; ABC also names agitated resistance and depressions in its historical wording.
- **Body/health:** ABC's reference to depressions is an attributed source claim, not a clinical diagnosis or prediction.
- **Social/relationship:** Excitement in a small circle, contained revolts, and tensions in an oppressed population.
- **Events/manifestations:** Small disturbances, suppression or localization of larger revolts, and ABC's traffic-jam example.
- **Conflicts/notes:** “Small” concerns the scope of Witte's events; it does not establish that distress is minor.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe agitation meeting limits. Small-circle excitement and tensions in a larger oppressed population differ in scale, but Witte explicitly allows the localization of larger revolts; no direct contradiction follows.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Moon/Uranus through agitation that encounters a boundary: excitement remains within a small circle, a revolt is contained, or emotional impulses are suppressed. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes limited or localized disturbance; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) supplies blocked dynamism, resistance, restricted feeling, and tension under oppression.

The common pattern is interrupted expression rather than an absence of excitement. ABC's traffic jam is a concrete image of blockage, while its historical depression language remains an attributed claim rather than a diagnosis.
