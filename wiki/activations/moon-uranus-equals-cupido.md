## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A woman pressing marriage. Exciting events and hours while attending parties, socials, club meetings, and so on. Eccentric art.

Source locator: PDF p. 127.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Agitated community of a people, eventful hour for a community, excited women's community, women's reform for the community, new sense of community, impulses of a women's community, unrest in a community of a people.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 187, PDF p. 92, Cupido row. “Neues Gemeinschafts-Gefühl” means a new sense of community.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Excitement, change, or fresh feeling within a social group ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Agitation and a new sense of belonging are both present in ABC.
- **Body/health:** Neither entry specifies a bodily condition.
- **Social/relationship:** Parties, clubs, communities, and pressure toward marriage; ABC emphasizes women's groups and community reform.
- **Events/manifestations:** Exciting gatherings, community unrest or renewal, and eccentric art.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Pressure toward marriage is a particular Witte example, not the definition of every group relationship.

## Contradictions

Social excitement and community unrest overlap without having the same tone. New belonging, reform, and eccentric art broaden the range; the entries establish no direct contradiction or required progression from one to another.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Moon/Uranus through shared social life: excitement at a gathering, agitation in a community, or a new sense of belonging. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) locates the excitement in parties and clubs, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds community impulses, reform, and unrest.

Witte's eccentric art and pressure toward marriage are distinct examples of this socially charged setting. The entries allow novelty and renewal as well as disruption; they do not make every gathering unconventional or every relationship unstable.
