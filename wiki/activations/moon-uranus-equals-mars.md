## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Hours of energetic activities. Excited through actions. To act in the state of excitement. Women who are inclined to violence. Hurts caused by a woman. Acts of an insurgent people.

Source locator: PDF p. 127.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work in an hourly rhythm, dynamic will of a people, excited actions of a woman, new impulses for a people, sudden emotional impulses, technical creation by a people, surprising emotionally driven actions, restless actions of a woman.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 186, PDF p. 92, Mars row. “Überraschende Gefühls-Handlungen” concerns surprising emotional actions, not specifically a woman's actions.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Excessive ambition, the craving for sensation, lack of self-control, the tendency to act rashly. Acts of violence. Injury.

Source locator: printed p. 129, entry 0390.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To assert oneself with unexpected emotionality. To act freely based on one’s instincts of the moment and on sudden feelings which arise. To have exaggerated or seemingly inappropriate reactions to experiences.

Source locator: PDF p. 34, Mars entry; checked in the PDF text.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `124`

#### Munkasey Entry

Actively creating labor saving objects for the household; added efforts put into unconventional approaches to solving ordinary problems; agitation for alterations and improvements in cars, motors, etc.; mechanical skills.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Sudden feeling or excitement expressed in action ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Rashness, sensation-seeking, and exaggerated reactions are explicit cautions ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- **Body/health:** Ebertin names injury and Witte names hurts; these remain attributed possibilities, not diagnoses or predictions.
- **Social/relationship:** Emotionally forceful assertion and collective insurgent action; Witte's gendered violence examples remain attributed.
- **Events/manifestations:** Energetic activity, technical creation, mechanical improvements, and household problem-solving ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
- **Conflicts/notes:** Inventive practical effort and impulsive harm are distinct expressions; neither defines every activation.

## Contradictions

The emphasis ranges from rash or violent action to useful mechanical invention. These are different possible channels of activity, not proof that either must occur; no single outcome reconciles them automatically.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Moon/Uranus through action taken in excitement or in response to sudden feeling. [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry), and [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) all describe feeling becoming movement or assertion; [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) makes rashness and poor self-control explicit risks.

Action also has a constructive practical expression: [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) describes mechanical skills, labor-saving objects, and unconventional solutions to ordinary problems. This keeps invention beside the historical violence and injury examples, without treating either result as inevitable.
