## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Moon + Uranus`
- Source page: `127`
#### Witte Entry

Hours of energetic activities. Excited through actions. To act in the state of excitement. Women who are inclined to violence. Hurts caused by a woman. Acts of an insurgent people.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Moon/Uranus`
- Source page: `129`
- Entry: `0390`

#### Ebertin Entry

Excessive ambition, the craving for sensation, lack of self-control, the tendency to act rashly. Acts of violence. Injury.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Moon/Uranus midpoint`
- Source page: `33`
- Activator: `Mars`

#### Sandbach Entry

To assert oneself with unexpected emotionality. To act freely based on one’s instincts of the moment and on sudden feelings which arise. To have exaggerated or seemingly inappropriate reactions to experiences.
