## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Attentive. Capable of quick action. To show interest. Wanting to take part. To be under steady mental tenseness. Irritable mind or attitude. Moment of a sudden event in public.

Source locator: PDF p. 127.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Soulful nervous woman, hour and minute of an event, renewal among my people, my feelings are aroused, my hourly rhythm, my excited wife, my new wife, my nervous wife, my surprised wife, tensions in the collective soul, restless collective soul.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 186, PDF p. 92, MC row. “Beseelte” and “Erneuerung” were mistranslated as serving and emulation; “Volks-Seele” is the source's collective-soul language.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Prone to states of excitement and great energy. The pursuit of ambitious aims, determination, readiness for action. Tendency to take a hand or to suddenly interfere.

Source locator: printed p. 130, entry 0397.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The goal of life is to be creative in the moment, and to be uninhibited in the expression of one’s instincts. One’s career may involve nurturing the creativity and ge nius of others.

Source locator: PDF p. 34, Midheaven entry; checked in the PDF text.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `124`

#### Munkasey Entry

Growth of self as a person and recognition for creating things for others; the stature you gain from your inventiveness and ingenuity; the expertise and notoriety you are able to acquire thru application of your practical skills.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Personal readiness to act, participate, or create in an excited moment ([Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Attention, determination, mental tension, and irritability; ABC adds personally experienced emotional arousal ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- **Body/health:** No bodily condition is specified in these entries.
- **Social/relationship:** Participation or sudden interference; nurturing others' creativity ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- **Events/manifestations:** Sudden public events, creative work, recognition for invention, and practical expertise ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
- **Conflicts/notes:** Career and recognition are supported emphases, but a repeatedly changing career or public role is not established by these entries.

## Contradictions

The entries range from tense readiness and interference to creative purpose and recognition. These are different expressions of active engagement, not direct contradictions or evidence that creativity removes tension.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Moon/Uranus through personal involvement in an excited moment: attention, readiness to act, and the wish to take part. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) entries also preserve the difficult side of this readiness as mental tension, irritability, or sudden interference; [ABC](#abc-entry) makes emotional arousal personally immediate.

The creative expression is supported by [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), who names spontaneous creativity and fostering it in others, and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), who links recognition to invention and practical skill. These entries support active creative engagement without establishing an erratic life course.
