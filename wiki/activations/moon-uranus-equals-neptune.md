## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The activity in the brain increases by leaps and bounds. To suggest and reject many things. Minor issues or ambiguities that cause excitements. Hours in which one is excited for no reason. Energetic women or energetic people who are condemned to inactivity.

Source locator: PDF p. 127.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Agitation through unclear feelings, eventful hour in water, awaiting a reform affecting the people, unrest among a disappointed people, uncertainty of a nervous woman, refined emotional impulses, rejection of a reform among the people.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 187, PDF p. 92, Neptune row. “Unklare Gefühle,” “enttäuschten Volkes,” and “Verneinung” concern unclear feelings, a disappointed people, and rejection.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Lack of energy, weakness, muddle-headed or confused aspiration. Nervousness, exhaustion. A sudden disappointment.

Source locator: printed p. 129, entry 0393.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Intuitive insights that come to one surprisingly or unexpectedly. To hold to the ideal of people being able to freely share their feelings with each other. Psychic insight into the specific type of nurturing that another person needs.

Source locator: PDF p. 34, Neptune entry; checked in the PDF text.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `124`

#### Munkasey Entry

Creativity and insight added to an already budding but unconventional genius; the use of questionable methods to produce results; others who steal your ideas or products; deceitful people who drain you of ideas or time.

## Comparative Schema

- **Core meaning:** Excitement shaped by ambiguity, uncertain feeling, or unexpected insight ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- **Psychology:** Confused aims and disappointment in Ebertin; intuition, creative insight, and an ideal of freely shared feeling elsewhere ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
- **Body/health:** Weakness, nervousness, and exhaustion are Ebertin's historical claims. Witte's brain-activity wording is not a verified physiological mechanism.
- **Social/relationship:** Insight into another's nurturing needs contrasts with Munkasey's warnings about deceit and appropriation of ideas.
- **Events/manifestations:** Sudden disappointment, forced inactivity, disputed reform, questionable methods, and stolen ideas or products.
- **Conflicts/notes:** Creative insight and confusion are both represented; neither warrants certainty about unseen motives or psychic abilities.

## Contradictions

The entries differ materially in evaluation: Sandbach emphasizes intuitive care, Ebertin confusion and depletion, and Munkasey both creativity and deception. They are preserved as distinct possibilities; the presence of insight does not establish immunity to error or exploitation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Moon/Uranus through excitement whose meaning is uncertain: ambiguous issues, unclear feelings, or anticipation can arouse a person or group without producing clear action. [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry), and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) support this difficult side through inactivity, disappointment, confused aims, and exhaustion.

There is also an explicitly intuitive and creative strand: [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes unexpected insight into another's need for care, and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds creative insight while warning about questionable methods and stolen ideas. These remain different expressions of the orientation; intuition alone does not settle questions of accuracy or trust.
