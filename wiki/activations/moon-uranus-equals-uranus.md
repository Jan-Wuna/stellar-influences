## Source Entries

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/URANUS With Itself`
- Source page: `126`

#### Munkasey Entry

Seeing that an extreme approach to solving sticky everyday problems may be the only practical solution; a restlessness about the current state of affairs involving your family, home, or personal life; unexpected family roles.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: restlessness in home or personal affairs and consideration of an extreme practical response.
- psychology: dissatisfaction with current family, home, or personal conditions is stated.
- body/health: the single entry supplies no bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: unexpected family roles are a specific example.
- events/manifestations: a difficult everyday problem may prompt an extreme approach regarded as practical.
- conflicts/notes: may be the only practical solution is conditional source wording, not a general endorsement of drastic action.

## Contradictions

The single entry links restlessness, a possible extreme response, and unexpected family roles without asserting that they must occur together. It offers no separate source assessment of whether that response works.

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Uranus = Uranus, the repeated Uranus specifies the pair through restlessness about family, home, or personal affairs and the possibility of an unexpected family role. The [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry) also describes seeing an extreme approach as potentially the only practical answer to a stubborn everyday problem (p. 126). This is a conditional perception within the entry; it does not establish that a drastic response is necessary or successful.
