## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `127` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

General tension (excitement). Eventful hours. To be surprised. Uproar, rebellion of the people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `186`, PDF p. `92` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

excited women in public, eventful hours in public life, excited emotional natures in public, women's reform in public, women's impulses in public life, and restless people in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: surprise and emotional excitement in public or collective settings.
- psychology: excited emotional states are described without an individual personality claim.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: public expressions involving women, shared unrest, and popular rebellion are examples.
- events/manifestations: eventful public hours, women's reform, and collective uproar are stated.
- conflicts/notes: general tension does not establish that every public event is violent or that reform succeeds.

## Contradictions

Witte includes rebellion and general surprise; ABC specifies public emotional expression and reform. These overlap in collective excitement without making every listed event an uprising.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Uranus = Vernal Point, Vernal Point specifies the pair through public excitement, surprise, and eventful hours. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes collective uproar and rebellion; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds excited women and emotional states in public life, women's reform, and a restless people (printed p. 186, PDF p. 92). The common public setting unifies these examples while leaving the event's scale and outcome unspecified.
