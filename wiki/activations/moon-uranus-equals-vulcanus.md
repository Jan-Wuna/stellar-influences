## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `127` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Mighty events. Populace and women under great excitement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `187`, PDF p. `92` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

dynamic power of a woman, influence of a nervous woman, influential event of an hour, tremendous unrest among the people, power reform of a people, powerfully excited emotional nature, renewal of energy of the woman, sudden violence against the woman, and strong emotional impulses

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: powerful excitement and influential events affecting women or a populace.
- psychology: strong emotional impulses and a powerfully excited emotional state are named.
- body/health: ABC's sudden violence against a woman is a historical event association, not a bodily diagnosis.
- social/relationship: a nervous woman's influence, collective unrest, and reform of power are examples.
- events/manifestations: mighty events, renewed energy, and sudden violence appear alongside dynamic power.
- conflicts/notes: intensity does not determine whether influence is constructive or harmful.

## Contradictions

Dynamic influence and renewed energy coexist with tremendous unrest and violence in ABC. The latter are specific source examples, not necessary outcomes of the intensity described by Witte.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Uranus = Vulcanus, Vulcanus specifies the pair through powerful excitement and consequential influence. [Witte](#witte-entry) names mighty events and a greatly excited populace or women; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds dynamic power, a nervous woman's influence, strong emotional impulses, and collective unrest (printed p. 187, PDF p. 92). ABC also retains energy renewal, reform of power, and sudden violence against a woman. These distinguish constructive and harmful possibilities without making force or violence inevitable.
