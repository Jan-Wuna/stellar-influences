## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `123` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Deep, unselfish love of a woman. The woman whose love is not returned.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `179`, PDF p. `88` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A woman's limited joy, a beautiful woman's depression, a woman's deep love, disturbances of a woman's harmony, harmonious deepening of feelings, suppression of a peaceful people, suppression of love for a woman, and deepening love for a woman

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: deep or unselfish affection alongside limitation, suppression, or love that is not returned.
- psychology: limited joy, disturbed harmony, and deepening feelings are named.
- body/health: ABC's depression is an attributed historical association, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: a woman's unreturned love and suppression of love for a woman are explicit examples.
- events/manifestations: ABC separately includes suppression of a peaceful people.
- conflicts/notes: depth, harmony, and non-reciprocity remain distinct possibilities; deep love does not prove mutual attachment.

## Contradictions

Witte's unselfish love and unreturned love are separate descriptions. ABC similarly retains both harmonious deepening and disturbed harmony, without requiring that every deep attachment be unhappy.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Venus = Admetos, Admetos specifies the pair through deep affection whose expression or return may be limited. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names unselfish love and a woman whose love is not returned; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds limited joy, suppression of love, and disturbed harmony alongside harmonious deepening of feeling (printed p. 179, PDF p. 88). These entries distinguish depth from reciprocity. ABC's depression and suppression of a peaceful people remain specific historical associations rather than necessary results of loving deeply.
