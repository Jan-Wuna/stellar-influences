## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `123` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Faithful womans love. The love of many women. Peace and friendship among nations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `179`, PDF p. `88` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of love for a woman, expansion of the feeling of harmony, a beautiful woman's success, successful folk art, a people's successes in peace, a woman's experience of art, experiencing feelings of love, an hour of freedom in love, and a woman's increasing love

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: expanding affection or harmony, with artistic success and peace among peoples.
- psychology: growing love and experiencing loving feelings are stated.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: Witte includes faithful love, the love of many women, and friendship among nations.
- events/manifestations: folk-art success, experience of art, peace successes, and freedom in love are named.
- conflicts/notes: the entries do not establish that faithful love, many women's love, and freedom in love describe one relationship arrangement.

## Contradictions

Witte's faithful love and love of many women are not explained as a single relationship pattern. ABC's freedom in love and increasing affection add possibilities without resolving that ambiguity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Venus = Apollon, Apollon specifies the pair through an expanded range of affection and harmony. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives faithful love, the love of many women, and peace or friendship among nations. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds increasing love, experience of loving feelings, freedom in love, and success in folk art or peace (printed p. 179, PDF p. 88). These personal, artistic, and collective examples share a widening or flourishing quality, while leaving the form of any particular relationship unspecified.
