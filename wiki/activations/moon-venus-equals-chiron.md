## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `27` (entry; one-based).

#### Sandbach Entry

Your energy helps others to tap into the love surrounding them in the moment, and to love their own past, even the most difficult parts of it. You also have the effect of helping them to be more vulnerable and open in relating.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: helping others recognize present love and approach their past with love.
- psychology: openness to difficult memories and greater vulnerability in relating are named.
- body/health: the single entry supplies no bodily condition or clinical treatment claim.
- social/relationship: the described effect is helping other people become more open in relationships.
- events/manifestations: recognizing surrounding love and relating differently to one's past are the examples.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach describes a helping influence; the entry does not establish guaranteed healing or erase the difficulty of the past.

## Contradictions

This single entry provides one account of a helping influence without an independent contrasting assessment. Its mention of difficult parts of the past does not establish any particular history or diagnosis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Venus = Chiron, Chiron specifies the pair through helping others notice love available in the present and approach even difficult parts of their past with love. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) also describes encouraging greater vulnerability and openness in relating (PDF p. 27). This is the entry's specific account of supportive influence; broader promises of emotional redemption or clinical healing exceed what it establishes.
