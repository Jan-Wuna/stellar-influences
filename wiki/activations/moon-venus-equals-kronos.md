### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `179`, PDF p. `88` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A woman's special joy, a special hour of harmony, a beautiful woman's dominance, a great peaceful people, a woman in harmony with the boss, the art of a self-confident people, a woman's direction of love, and a self-confident beautiful woman

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: affection or harmony in settings of prominence, leadership, or authority.
- psychology: self-confidence, special joy, and direction of love are named.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a specific bodily condition; motherhood is a historical role example.
- social/relationship: a landlord's wife, marital motherhood, a leading feminist, and harmony with a boss are listed.
- events/manifestations: a special hour of harmony, dominant influence, and a self-confident people's art are examples.
- conflicts/notes: prominence and harmony are stated, but exemplary virtue or superior care is not established.

## Contradictions

Witte's household and feminist roles differ from ABC's authority, self-confidence, and artistic examples. They do not establish a single social rank or a moral standard for affection.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Venus = Kronos, Kronos specifies the pair through affection and harmony in prominent or authoritative settings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives a landlord's wife, marital motherhood, and a leading feminist; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a woman's dominance, self-confidence, harmony with a boss, and direction of love (printed p. 179, PDF p. 88). ABC also includes a special hour of harmony and a self-confident people's art. These examples support prominence and influence without making status evidence of superior care.
