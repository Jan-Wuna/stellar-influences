## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Moon + Venus`
- Source page: `123`
#### Witte Entry

To be adored by women. To have others mother oneself. To be a mother. The attitude or feelings of a feminine woman. To experience a harmonic hour.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Moon/Venus`
- Source page: `118`
- Entry: `0349`

#### Ebertin Entry

A heart filled with love. A loving wife or mother. The state of becoming a wife or mother. 20 Moon/Venus = Pluto often gives similar indications as under 0344.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Moon/Venus midpoint`
- Source page: `27`
- Activator: `Midheaven`

#### Sandbach Entry

A career which involves soothing or nurturing other people. One’s life path has to do with taking care of other people, and expressing loving feelings to the world. A career that involves the beautification of the home, or bring artistry to common and everyday sorts of things, such as food.
