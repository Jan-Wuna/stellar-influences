## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `127`, PDF p. `123` (entry).

#### Witte Entry

The thoughts of a loving woman. Acquaintanceship with loving or pregnant women. Thoughts of motherhood.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `178`, PDF p. `88` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Reports about folk art, young women's joy, a people's peace talks, a beautiful woman's thoughts, emotionally harmonious conversations, a woman's harmonious youth, love for a young woman, and an hour of harmonious conversation

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `117`, PDF p. `119`, entry `0340`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Longing for love, erotic thoughts. Reflecting on love problems.

<a id="ebertin-source-note-18"></a>
#### Ebertin Source Note 18

- Source reference: printed p. `117`, PDF p. `119`, footnote `18`.

Although its marker is attached to Mars entry `0341`, Ebertin's note 18 explicitly names `Moon/Venus = Mercury` among artistically creative people. It also permits replacing love by art in many cases; see the [marker-owning Mars note](moon-venus-equals-mars.md#ebertin-source-note-18). This is the author's specific Mercury reference, without transferring Mars's other statements.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `27` (entry; one-based).

#### Sandbach Entry

One communicating nurtures the loved one. One’s communicating is charac terized by much persuasive feeling. A smooth and flowing way of communicating that is simple, natural and graceful. One’s thinking is constantly oriented toward emotional satisfaction and gratification.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/VENUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `108`

#### Munkasey Entry

Your need to talk about how you want to care and nurture others; additional ideas and thinking on how you can best support people you care about; the care and concern you show for your neighborhood.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: thought and communication oriented toward love, emotional satisfaction, or practical care.
- psychology: longing for love, erotic thoughts, persuasion through feeling, and reflection on love problems are named.
- body/health: Witte mentions pregnant women as acquaintances; no bodily condition is established.
- social/relationship: nurturing conversation and ideas for supporting loved people or a neighborhood are examples.
- events/manifestations: peace talks, reports on folk art, and thoughts of motherhood are stated.
- conflicts/notes: smooth or affectionate speech does not establish accurate thought, avoidance of conflict, or manipulative intent.

## Contradictions

Ebertin's reflection on love problems adds a difficult subject to the otherwise affectionate communication examples. The entries do not establish that graceful expression eliminates such problems.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Venus = Mercury, Mercury specifies the pair through thinking and speaking about affection and care. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) names longing, erotic thoughts, and reflection on love problems; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes nurturing communication, persuasive feeling, and thought directed toward emotional satisfaction. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds discussion and planning of support for loved people and concern for a neighborhood (p. 108).

The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) retains thoughts of motherhood and acquaintances with loving or pregnant women, while [ABC](#abc-entry) adds harmonious conversation, peace talks, and folk-art reports (printed p. 178, PDF p. 88). These examples support affectionate or care-oriented communication without establishing that its tone ensures sound judgment.
