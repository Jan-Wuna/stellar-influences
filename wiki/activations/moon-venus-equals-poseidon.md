## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `127`, PDF p. `123` (entry).

#### Witte Entry

Refined love-feeling. Spiritual or platonic love of a woman. The hour of female insights.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `179`, PDF p. `88` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A woman's joy in insight, a people's peace and culture, harmony through clear feelings, a people's cultural beauties, a lovable spiritualized woman, a woman's love of truth, an hour of spiritual harmony, and spiritualized love of women

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: refined or spiritual affection, clear feelings, and joy in insight.
- psychology: clarity of feeling, love of truth, and spiritual harmony are named.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: Witte gives spiritual or platonic love of a woman; ABC adds peace and culture among a people.
- events/manifestations: an hour of insight, cultural beauty, and an hour of spiritual harmony are listed.
- conflicts/notes: refinement does not establish moral purity or certify the truth of an insight.

## Contradictions

Witte's refined or platonic love and ABC's truth, clarity, and cultural examples differ in setting without an explicit conflict. Their favorable wording does not validate a claim of higher perception.

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Venus = Poseidon, Poseidon specifies the pair through refined affection and insight. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names spiritual or platonic love and an hour of female insight; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds harmony through clear feelings, joy in insight, and love of truth (printed p. 179, PDF p. 88). ABC's peace, culture, and cultural beauty broaden the setting beyond a personal bond. These descriptions support refinement without establishing moral purity or the accuracy of any particular insight.
