## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: PDF p. 123.

Sudden love of a woman. To become a mother suddenly. Suddenly acting mother.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: printed p. 179; PDF p. 88.

Joyfully excited woman, peaceful reform of a people, harmonious emotional impulses, a love event for the woman, new love for a woman, new folk art, technology of a peaceful people, surprising love for a woman, and surprising wishes of the woman.

Editorial translation checked against the German entry and Uranus row symbol in the scan, printed p. 179 (PDF p. 88).

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: printed p. `117`, PDF p. `119`, entry `0344`.

A periodic and sudden manifestation of sexual desire (in connection with a disturbance of the glandular function, the tendency to act suddenly under the dictates of feeling, with the exclusion of ego consciousness), fits of emotion. (Epilepsy and other convulsive states.) Sudden experience in a woman's life, sudden motherhood.

<a id="ebertin-source-note-19"></a>
#### Ebertin Source Note 19

- Source reference: printed p. `117`, PDF p. `119`, footnote `19`.

Ebertin reports a historical association in female charts with early love relationships, seduction and rape. This is an attributed claim by the author; it establishes neither causation nor an individual risk or inevitable experience.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: PDF pp. 27–28; the Uranus entry begins on p. 27 and continues on p. 28.

A sudden manifesting of feelings when one is relating. The need to be able to share feelings freely in a relationship. An unusual or inspired way of nurturing others by being able to tap into their unique needs.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/VENUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `108`

#### Munkasey Entry

Care and concern shown in unusual or different ways; demonstrations of impersonal or detached affections; showing creativity in the ways you care for others; unexpected or sudden reversals between friends or neighbors.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sudden feeling and unusual expressions of care specify this Moon/Venus activation ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
- psychology: feelings may emerge abruptly; the need to share them freely can inspire attention to another person's unique needs ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- body/health: [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) includes historical glandular and convulsive associations. These remain attributed source claims, not diagnoses established by this formula.
- social/relationship: creative or detached affection and unexpected reversals between friends or neighbors occur alongside sudden love ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry)).
- events/manifestations: sudden motherhood, an abrupt emotional response, or an inventive act of nurturing are distinct examples in the entries ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- conflicts/notes: ABC also includes collective reform, folk art, and technology, extending beyond the personal examples. Historical references to women and motherhood retain their source-specific scope.

## Contradictions

- The entries differ in emphasis between excited feeling, detached care, and sudden events; none states that its example excludes the others. Ebertin's medical associations are not established by the other entries. ABC's collective reform and folk-art examples broaden the setting without contradicting the personal accounts.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies the Moon/Venus pair through sudden feeling and departures from familiar ways of caring. Affection may surface unexpectedly, seek free expression, or find an inspired response to someone's particular needs. This connects [Sandbach's account of relating and nurturing](#sandbach-entry) with [Munkasey's creative or detached care](#munkasey-entry), while retaining the possibility of abrupt changes between friends or neighbors (printed p. 108).

[Witte's sudden love and motherhood](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin's sudden emotional experiences](#ebertin-entry) give concrete historical examples of this abruptness. They do not make motherhood or Ebertin's medical associations universal consequences of the activation.
