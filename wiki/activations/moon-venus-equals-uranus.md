## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden love of a woman. To become a mother suddenly. Suddenly acting mother.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joyfully excited woman, peaceful reform of a people, harmonious flow impulses, love event for the woman, new love for the woman, new people skill, technique of a peaceful people, surprising love for the woman, and surprising wonder of the woman

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A periodic and sudden manifestation of sexual desire (in connection with a disturbance of the glandular function, the tendency to act suddenly under the dictates of feeling, with the exclusion of ego consciousness), fits of emotion. (Epilepsy and other convulsive states.) Sudden experience in a woman's life, sudden motherhood.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A sudden manifesting of feelings when one is relating. The need to be able to share feelings freely in a relationship. An unusual or inspired way of nurturing others by being able to tap into their unique needs.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Care and concern shown in unusual or different ways; demonstrations of impersonal or detached affections; showing creativity in the ways you care for others; unexpected or sudden reversals between friends or neighbors.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Venus = Uranus` channels the tender, receptive love of the Moon/Venus axis through Uranus, making affection unusual, liberating, and emotionally changeable. It points to tenderness that wants space, novelty, and honesty more than conventional steadiness.
- Chart expression: refreshing attachment, unconventional closeness, and emotional warmth that comes alive through difference and spontaneity. Main caution: instability in affection, because the need for freedom can make comfort and closeness feel vivid but intermittent.
