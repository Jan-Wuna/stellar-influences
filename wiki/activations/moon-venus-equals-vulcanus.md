## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: PDF p. 123.

The passionate love of a woman. Matters and things to which one pays attention in the public. Strong in the public eye.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: printed p. 179; PDF p. 88.

Joyful influence of a woman, harmonious emotional energy, strength of a loving woman, artistic influence of a woman, love-related violence against a woman, a woman's power in love, a people's power for peace, strong harmonious feelings, and strong love for a woman.

Editorial translation checked against the German entry and Vulcanus row symbol in the scan, printed p. 179 (PDF p. 88). The source's adverse phrase is `Liebes-Gewalt gegen die Frau`.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: forceful affection, influence, and public prominence characterize this activation ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: passionate love and strong harmonious feeling are the emotional emphases ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- body/health: neither entry supplies a medical claim. ABC's love-related violence is an attributed harmful manifestation, not a diagnosis or an inevitable outcome.
- social/relationship: the power of a loving woman and strong love for a woman are attributed ABC examples; Witte describes passionate love and public attention.
- events/manifestations: artistic influence and being strongly in the public eye are concrete examples ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry)).
- conflicts/notes: ABC juxtaposes harmonious emotional energy with love-related violence; the latter must remain visible alongside its constructive examples. Its gendered wording does not establish universal roles.

## Contradictions

Passionate love and public influence are different emphases, not mutually exclusive claims. ABC expressly includes love-related violence alongside harmony and influence; this is a range of manifestations rather than a claim that all occur together.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies the Moon/Venus pair through the strength and reach of affection. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) links passionate love with public attention, while [ABC](#abc-entry) adds the influence of a loving woman and artistic influence. The connecting theme is affection that carries force or becomes conspicuous. ABC also includes love-related violence against a woman, so intensity cannot be treated as necessarily harmonious or beneficial.
