## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To cause a sensation in a small way or in a small circle. Grave hindrances and obstacles through persons of public power. To be completely without influence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

blocked power of the people, influence through the consolidation of feelings, influence of a thorough woman, energy restrictions in the people, limitation of power for a woman, strong depressions of a woman, strength of an enduring people, and hour of the suppression of feelings
