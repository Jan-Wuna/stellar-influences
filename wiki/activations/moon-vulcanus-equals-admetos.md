## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: PDF p. 137.

To cause a sensation in a small way or in a small circle. Grave hindrances and obstacles through persons of public power. To be completely without influence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: printed p. 205; PDF p. 101.

blocked power of the people, influence through the consolidation of feelings, influence of a thorough woman, energy restrictions in the people, limitation of power for a woman, strong depressions of a woman, strength of an enduring people, and hour of the suppression of feelings

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: influence is narrowed, obstructed, or suppressed ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: consolidated feelings and endurance coexist with suppressed feeling in ABC's range of examples.
- body/health: ABC mentions strong depression in a woman; this is an attributed association, not a clinical conclusion.
- social/relationship: persons with public power can impose obstacles; influence may remain within a small circle ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- events/manifestations: restricted energy, limited power, or a small-scale sensation illustrate the constraint.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also retains endurance and firmness as possible strengths; restriction does not imply that all capacity disappears.

## Contradictions

Witte's complete lack of influence and ABC's enduring strength describe different ends of a range. The entries do not state that both must occur together; neither establishes a universal loss of agency.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Moon/Vulcanus by limiting the reach or expression of power. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes influence confined to a small circle or obstructed by powerful people, while [ABC](#abc-entry) adds restricted energy and suppressed feelings. Its firmness and endurance examples retain a more sustained form of strength within this constraint; its depression wording does not establish a diagnosis.
