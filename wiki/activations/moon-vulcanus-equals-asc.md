## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: PDF p. 136; Asc row checked visually.

To cause public interest through others, or others cause public interest for the native. To experience great events with others. Being connected with others through great events and having together great influence on the public, to meet these people socially.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: printed p. 204; PDF p. 101; Asc row checked visually.

Other peoples are powerful, an influential woman nearby, the partner's emotional influence, violence against another people, a woman's power in the surroundings, strong feelings in the surroundings, a strong woman as partner, and an hour of strength for the surroundings.

Editorial translation checked against the German scan and Asc row symbol.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: power and public interest arise through contact with others ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: ABC names a partner's emotional influence and strong feelings nearby.
- body/health: no medical claim is supplied; ABC's violence is a harmful social example.
- social/relationship: influential companions and shared public impact organize the entries.
- events/manifestations: shared major events, social meetings, and public interest generated through others are Witte's examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also includes violence against another people; its collective and gendered examples remain attributed, without making all encounters cooperative.

## Contradictions

Shared public influence in Witte and ABC's forceful or violent encounters differ in tone, not logical scope. The entries describe possible relations with others rather than one uniformly beneficial relationship.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Moon/Vulcanus through other people and the immediate social setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes shared events and connections that draw public interest or produce influence together. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds the emotional force of a partner or an influential woman nearby, while retaining collective violence as an adverse possibility. The theme is power encountered and exercised in relation to others, with cooperation and coercion kept distinct.
