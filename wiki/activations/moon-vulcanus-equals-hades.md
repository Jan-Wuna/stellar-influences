## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: PDF p. 137.

Abominable, vile deeds. Great disgust and loathe. Great events with great damage and people killed. Shabbiness and baseness of the representatives of public power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: printed p. 205; PDF p. 101.

Lack of energy in a woman, privations of a strong people, criminal influence among a people, power of an old woman, disadvantages through emotional influences, harmful violence against a woman, an hour of danger through violence, and an hour of relinquishing power.

Editorial translation checked against the German scan and Hades row symbol; the omitted criminal-influence clause is restored.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: harmful influence, deprivation, and dangerous uses of power dominate the entries ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: disgust in Witte and disadvantage through emotional influence in ABC are explicit responses or mechanisms.
- body/health: Witte names deaths and ABC low energy and violence; these remain source examples of harm, not medical predictions.
- social/relationship: corrupt public representatives, criminal influence, and violence against a woman are attributed examples.
- events/manifestations: damaging major events, privation, danger, and relinquishing power illustrate the range.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also names an old woman's power without defining it as harmful; age alone must not be made the cause of the adverse themes.

## Contradictions

Both entries contain harmful manifestations, but ABC's old-woman example is not expressly criminal or destructive. That distinct example should not be forced into a uniformly malign account.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Moon/Vulcanus where power is experienced through deprivation, harmful influence, or danger. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives destructive public events and corrupt representatives; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds criminal influence, emotional disadvantage, violence, and relinquished power. These establish an adverse range without predicting a particular disaster. ABC's separate reference to an old woman's power does not justify treating age or women as intrinsically harmful.
