## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: PDF p. 137.

Great admiration and surprise. Might and influence of a high state official, cabinet member or diplomat.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: printed p. 205; PDF p. 101.

Renown of a strong people, exceptional power of feelings, an influential hierarchy of a people, energy of a great woman, state violence against a people, power of a respected woman, an hour of a strong woman, and superior force against a woman.

Editorial translation checked against the German scan and Kronos row symbol.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: influence occupies a high, respected, or commanding position ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: admiration and surprise accompany authority in Witte; ABC adds exceptional power of feelings.
- body/health: no medical claim is present; ABC's violence and superior force describe social harm.
- social/relationship: officials, diplomats, hierarchy, and a respected woman's power are explicit examples.
- events/manifestations: prominent public authority and the exercise of state power are central settings.
- conflicts/notes: status can command admiration or impose overwhelming force; respectability does not establish benevolence.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes admiration and high office; ABC adds state violence and superior force against a woman. These distinguish constructive or prestigious influence from coercive manifestations without declaring every authority violent.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Moon/Vulcanus as influence backed by prominence, rank, or a commanding position. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies high officials and diplomats who inspire admiration; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds hierarchy and a respected woman's power. Its examples of state violence and superior force preserve the danger of unequal power alongside public esteem.
