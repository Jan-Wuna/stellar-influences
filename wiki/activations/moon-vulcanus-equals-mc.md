## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: PDF p. 136; MC row checked visually.

The native is the cause of a great surprise to the public. To deal with people of public influence, and be impressed by them. There is a powerful mutual influence and interest between the public and the native.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: printed p. 204; PDF p. 101.

Influence of my woman, violence against my people, strength of my people, my emotional influence, my energetic woman, my feelings of power, my strong woman, my strong feelings, a psychologically strong woman, an hour of my strength, and an hour of psychological violence.

Editorial translation checked against the German scan and MC row symbol; `seelisch` is rendered psychologically here rather than as a claim about spiritual practice.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: power and public influence are experienced as personally involving the subject ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: personal feelings of power, strong feelings, and psychological strength or violence are ABC examples.
- body/health: no medical claim is present; psychological violence remains an attributed harmful manifestation.
- social/relationship: mutual influence between the person and the public, and being impressed by influential people, are explicit Witte themes.
- events/manifestations: causing public surprise and encounters with publicly influential people are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's first-person wording personalizes collective and relationship examples; it does not make them universal personal experiences.

## Contradictions

The public-personal reciprocity in Witte and ABC's first-person feelings of power do not directly conflict. ABC includes psychological violence, preventing an exclusively empowering interpretation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Moon/Vulcanus as a personal involvement in powerful feeling and public influence. In [Witte](#witte-entry), the person can surprise the public, be impressed by influential people, and participate in a reciprocal public response. [ABC](#abc-entry) brings power into first-person emotional and relationship experiences, including psychological strength and violence. Personal impact therefore does not imply personal control over every encounter.
