## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: PDF p. 136; Node row checked visually.

The connections cause public interest. To be in contact with people of public interest. To be partner in public influence. To be connected with powerful people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: printed p. 204; PDF p. 101.

Encounter with a strong woman, influence of a women's association, contact with influential women, a strong emotional bond, strong feelings in a connection, an hour of strength in a connection, and connection to the power of a people.

Editorial translation checked against the German scan and Node row symbol.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: connections carry emotional strength and public influence ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: a strong emotional bond and strong feelings in a connection are ABC's explicit themes.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a health claim.
- social/relationship: contact with powerful people and influential women is central.
- events/manifestations: meetings, associations, and partnerships attracting public interest illustrate the activation.
- conflicts/notes: connection to power does not itself establish control of that power; the women's-association example remains particular to ABC.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize public contact and emotional bonding without an explicit conflict. Neither states that every strong bond brings public influence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Moon/Vulcanus through the connections that carry strength and influence. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes contact with powerful people and partnership in public influence; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds strong emotional bonds and encounters with influential women. This can mean power experienced through an association, without establishing that every emotionally strong connection is publicly prominent.
