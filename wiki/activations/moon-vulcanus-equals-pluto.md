## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: PDF p. 137.

Forceful events are developing. Development is stormy and noisy. Changes among representatives of the public power start to show. Others are prepared to take over power. Great changes in a nation relating to power and might.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: printed p. 205; PDF p. 101.

Develop emotional strength, tremendous changes among a people, unfolding violence against women, unfolding feelings of power, a change of power within the hour, strong emotional development, changed emotional influence, and a woman's growing power.

Editorial translation checked against the German scan and Pluto row symbol; the first three clauses formerly attached to the preceding Neptune parser record belong to this Pluto row.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: power and strong feeling develop or change hands ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: growing emotional strength and unfolding feelings of power are ABC's themes.
- body/health: no medical claim is present; ABC includes violence as a harmful manifestation.
- social/relationship: changes among public representatives and preparations to take power are explicit Witte examples.
- events/manifestations: stormy developments, national power changes, and a woman's growing influence illustrate the range.
- conflicts/notes: development includes emotional growth and the spread of violence; growth alone does not establish improvement.

## Contradictions

The entries share a theme of changing power without directly opposing claims. Constructive emotional development and developing violence are distinct possibilities, not interchangeable descriptions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies Moon/Vulcanus through the development and redistribution of power. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes stormy public changes and others preparing to take authority; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds growing emotional strength and a woman's increasing influence. Its violence example keeps this process open to harmful development as well as growing capacity: a change in power is not necessarily an improvement.
