## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: PDF p. 136; Venus row checked visually.

Mighty care. Welfare departments, which cause lots of interest. To have great influence on female persons, to be very impressed by them. Doing well with female persons and people of public interest and influence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: printed p. 204; PDF p. 101; Venus row checked visually. The adverse phrase is `Liebes-Gewalt gegen die Frau`.

influential love for the woman, force of peace of a people, loving influence of a woman, violence of love against the woman, power of a beautiful woman, strong feelings of love, strong feelings of harmony, strengthening through the love of a woman, and hour of love power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: power takes the form of care, affection, and influential attachment ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: strong love and harmonious feelings appear in ABC; Witte includes being impressed by women.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a medical claim; love-related violence remains an adverse source example.
- social/relationship: welfare activity, influence involving women, and strengthening through a woman's love are explicit themes.
- events/manifestations: welfare institutions attracting attention and successful dealings with influential people are Witte's examples.
- conflicts/notes: affectionate strength and violence in love must remain distinct; care does not justify coercion.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes care and favorable dealings; ABC includes love-related violence alongside love and harmony. The entries therefore support a range that cannot be reduced to benevolent affection alone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies Moon/Vulcanus through care and the influence of affection. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes forceful care, welfare institutions, and dealings with influential women; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds strong love, harmony, and strength received through love. ABC also retains violence in love, so powerful attachment cannot automatically be treated as caring or consensual.
