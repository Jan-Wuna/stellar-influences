## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: PDF p. 137; Zeus row checked visually.

Events of importance prescribed by natural law. Startling creations, accomplishments and deeds. Great help by the representatives of public power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: printed p. 205; PDF p. 101.

Influential creative shaping by a woman, a woman as a leader, a leading woman's power, powerful efforts of a people, creativity through emotional strength, a woman's capacity for achievement, an hour of procreative strength, and an influential woman's goals.

Editorial translation checked against the German scan and Zeus row symbol.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: strength is directed toward creation, achievement, and leadership ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: emotional strength supports creativity and goals in ABC.
- body/health: ABC names procreative strength without supplying a medical account or fertility prediction.
- social/relationship: a leading woman and assistance from public representatives are explicit examples.
- events/manifestations: striking creations, accomplishments, and collective efforts illustrate the theme.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's reference to natural law is left at the source's stated level; it does not establish a causal explanation for every event.

## Contradictions

Witte's accomplishments and ABC's leadership and creative effort do not directly conflict. The natural-law and procreative examples are separate emphases, not proof that all achievements are biological or inevitable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Moon/Vulcanus by directing strength toward goals, creation, and achievement. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives striking accomplishments and help from public authorities; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds leadership, collective effort, and creativity supported by emotional strength. Its procreative example is one form of productive power within this range, not a requirement for every expression of the activation.
