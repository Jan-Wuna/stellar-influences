## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Decrease in working hours. Shrinking of accomplishment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `197`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A creative woman's perseverance, the beginning of depression among a people, limitation of women's goals, a blocked creative disposition, a people's restricted creativity, a woman's thorough accomplishments, and suppression of leading women

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: reduced activity or accomplishment and limits on creative goals, alongside perseverance.
- psychology: a blocked creative disposition and perseverance are named.
- body/health: ABC's depression among a people is collective historical wording, not an individual diagnosis.
- social/relationship: limitations on women's goals and suppression of leading women are examples.
- events/manifestations: decreased working hours, shrinking accomplishment, and thorough work are stated.
- conflicts/notes: perseverance and thoroughness coexist with restriction; reduced output does not establish lack of effort.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes shrinking accomplishment, while ABC includes perseverance and thorough work within a restricted range. These can concern different aspects of work and do not imply that effort inevitably restores production.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Admetos, Admetos specifies the pair through restricted creative activity or reduced accomplishment. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names fewer working hours and shrinking output; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds limited goals, blocked creativity, and suppression of leading women, while also retaining perseverance and thorough accomplishments (printed p. 197, PDF p. 97). The range distinguishes reduced scope from absent effort and leaves the eventual result open.
