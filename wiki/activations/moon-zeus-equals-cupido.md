## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Woman fit to have progeny. Love of sociability. Hours of leisure crowded with creative activities. Strongly led community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `197`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Shared efforts with a woman, a community of leaders of the people, a creative community of women, leadership of a community of women, achievements in folk art, goals of an emotional community, and leadership of a community of a people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: shared creative effort, sociability, and directed group activity.
- psychology: love of sociability is named without a general personality account.
- body/health: Witte's fitness for progeny is a historical reproductive assertion, not a fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: creative communities of women and strongly led groups are examples.
- events/manifestations: leisure filled with creative activity, shared efforts, and folk-art achievements are stated.
- conflicts/notes: sociability and strong leadership are distinct emphases; group creativity does not establish consensual leadership.

## Contradictions

Witte's reproductive example is separate from its sociability and creative-leisure examples. ABC supplies communal goals and leadership, without making group participation evidence of reproductive capacity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Cupido, Cupido specifies the pair through creative activity undertaken with others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names sociability, creatively busy leisure, and a strongly led community; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds shared efforts, creative communities of women, communal goals, and folk-art achievements (printed p. 197, PDF p. 97). Witte's reproductive assertion remains an attributed historical example. The shared activity does not determine how leadership is exercised or whether every member shares its aims.
