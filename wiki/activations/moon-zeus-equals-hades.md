## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

People working in the industry. To incite to misdeeds. Reproduction under evil or bad circumstances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `197`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

An old woman's efforts, disadvantages for women in leadership, criminal leadership of a people, criminal goals of women, deficient leadership of a people, a woman's hidden accomplishments, leaders of the people in need, and a goal-directed woman in the past

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creative or directed activity in adverse, deficient, or concealed circumstances.
- psychology: goal-directedness is named without a broader personality claim.
- body/health: Witte's reproduction under bad circumstances is an attributed historical example.
- social/relationship: leadership disadvantage, criminal leadership, and leaders in need are examples.
- events/manifestations: industrial work, incitement to misdeeds, and hidden accomplishments are stated.
- conflicts/notes: industry and hidden work are not equated with crime merely because these occur in the same entry.

## Contradictions

Witte's industrial work and adverse reproduction examples differ from ABC's leadership problems and hidden achievements. Neither entry establishes that productive activity is inherently criminal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Hades, Hades specifies the pair through directed effort under adverse or obscured conditions. [ABC](#abc-entry) names deficient or criminal leadership, disadvantage for women in leadership, leaders in need, and hidden accomplishments (printed p. 197, PDF p. 97). The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) separately gives industrial work, incitement to misdeeds, and reproduction under bad circumstances. These examples retain productive effort as well as harmful action, without treating work or reproduction as inherently corrupt.
