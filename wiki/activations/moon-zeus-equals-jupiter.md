## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Cheerful disposition. Happy hours of creation. Fortune or happiness through creative achievement. Light. Bonfire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `196`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A woman's financial accomplishments, joyful feelings through creativity, financial leadership by a woman, shaping rights for women, the happy birth of a girl, happy leadership of a people, and advantages of the leading people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: joy or favorable results linked with creative achievement.
- psychology: cheerfulness and joyful feeling through creativity are named.
- body/health: ABC's happy birth of a girl is a historical event association, not a reproductive forecast.
- social/relationship: women's financial leadership, rights, and favorable collective leadership are examples.
- events/manifestations: happy creative hours, financial accomplishment, light, and a bonfire are stated.
- conflicts/notes: financial leadership is distinct from financial support, and creative happiness does not establish universal success.

## Contradictions

Witte's light and bonfire examples are concrete manifestations alongside creative happiness. ABC's financial, rights, leadership, and childbirth examples do not establish one common cause.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Jupiter, Jupiter specifies the pair through joy in creation and favorable achievement. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names cheerfulness, happy creative hours, and fortune or happiness through accomplishment; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds financial achievement, women's financial leadership, and favorable collective leadership (printed p. 196, PDF p. 97). Light, a bonfire, rights, and the birth of a girl remain particular source examples, without turning creative enthusiasm into a guarantee of prosperity or a reproductive outcome.
