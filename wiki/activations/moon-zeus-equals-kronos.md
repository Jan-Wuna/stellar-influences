## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Great creative power. Great accomplishments. The population is protected by the authorities. Military. Soldiers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `197`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A leading woman's standing, a demanding hour for the boss, a people's special efforts, a woman's dominant leadership, a woman's great accomplishments, a people's hierarchical leadership, an independent creative woman, and precedence of the leading people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: large creative accomplishments and effort in authoritative or hierarchical settings.
- psychology: independence is named for a creative woman; no general emotional pattern is supplied.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: authorities protecting a population, military personnel, and dominant or hierarchical leadership are examples.
- events/manifestations: great achievements, special collective efforts, and a demanding hour for a boss are stated.
- conflicts/notes: authority and scale of achievement do not establish that every leader is protective.

## Contradictions

Witte includes protection by authorities, while ABC emphasizes standing, hierarchy, and precedence. These differing descriptions do not make dominance proof of protection.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Kronos, Kronos specifies the pair through prominent accomplishment and organized authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names great creative power and achievement, protection by authorities, and military personnel. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a leading woman's standing, dominant or hierarchical leadership, special collective effort, and independent creativity (printed p. 197, PDF p. 97). These examples connect achievement with authority while leaving the quality and effects of that authority open.
