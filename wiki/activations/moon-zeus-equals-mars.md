## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Women acting with a goal in mind. To realize a creative thought. To be at hand quickly. Fire outbreak.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `196`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Work performances of the woman, fiery feelings of a woman, peace objectives of a woman, leadership impulses of the woman, actions of the leading people, creative feelings impulses, creative women work, and will efforts of a people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: goal-directed action that realizes creative thought.
- psychology: fiery feelings and creative emotional impulses are named.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily condition; fire outbreak is an event example.
- social/relationship: women acting toward goals and leadership impulses are stated.
- events/manifestations: work performance, creative work, peace objectives, prompt action, and fire outbreak are listed.
- conflicts/notes: goal-directed effort and a fire outbreak are distinct examples rather than a necessary sequence.

## Contradictions

Witte's fire outbreak and ABC's peace objectives differ in setting. Neither entry asserts that creative action must become destructive or that purposeful work always succeeds.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Mars, Mars specifies the pair through acting toward a goal and putting creative thought into practice. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives prompt action and realization of an idea; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds work performance, leadership impulses, creative emotional impulses, and peace objectives (printed p. 196, PDF p. 97). Witte's fire outbreak remains a separate concrete association, without making destructive fire a necessary result of purposeful effort.
