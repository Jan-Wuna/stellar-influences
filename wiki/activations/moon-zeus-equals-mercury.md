## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Live mind. Creative thoughts. Very temperamental. Exchange of information on female conception.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `196`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A fiery young woman, leadership of female youth, thoughts of a creative disposition, emotionally expressive conversations about procreation, a creative woman's opinions, news about a people's accomplishments, and leaders of the people in the media

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: lively creative thought and communication about directed activity.
- psychology: a lively mind, temperament, opinions, and creative thoughts are named.
- body/health: information about conception is a subject of conversation, not a bodily assessment.
- social/relationship: female youth leadership and leaders appearing in media are examples.
- events/manifestations: creative opinions, achievement news, and conversations about procreation are stated.
- conflicts/notes: emotionally expressive conversation is not the same as guaranteed agreement or accuracy.

## Contradictions

The entries converge on thought and communication while supplying different subjects. News, leadership, and conception conversations do not establish a single purpose for every exchange.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Mercury, Mercury specifies the pair through lively creative thought and the exchange of information. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names a lively mind, creative ideas, and discussion of conception; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds opinions, news of accomplishments, leaders in media, and emotionally expressive procreation conversations (printed p. 196, PDF p. 97). These examples support active communication without establishing that its ideas or reports are accurate.
