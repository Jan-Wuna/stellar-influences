## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Efforts wrongly directed. Fiery, fantastic ideas. Delusions. Inflammable liquids. Uncertainties about female procreation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `197`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A creative woman's presentiments, a people's disappointment caused by leadership, sensitivity of a creative disposition, a woman's accomplishments in water, weak leadership of a people, and a people's insecurity caused by leadership

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creative ideas or efforts marked by uncertain direction, sensitivity, or disappointing leadership.
- psychology: presentiments, sensitivity, fantastic ideas, and delusions are attributed source terms.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a specific bodily condition; procreation uncertainty is a historical concern.
- social/relationship: weak leadership and a people's disappointment or insecurity caused by leadership are examples.
- events/manifestations: misdirected effort, accomplishments in water, and inflammable liquids are listed.
- conflicts/notes: presentiment and sensitivity do not establish reliable foresight; liquid and water examples remain distinct.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses misdirected efforts and uncertainty, while ABC also retains sensitivity of a creative disposition. Neither entry establishes that sensitivity resolves mistaken direction.

## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Neptune, Neptune specifies the pair through uncertainty in the direction of creative effort or leadership. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names wrongly directed efforts, fantastic ideas, and uncertainty about procreation; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds weak leadership and a people's disappointment or insecurity caused by it (printed p. 197, PDF p. 97). ABC also retains presentiments and sensitivity, while the entries separately name water and inflammable liquids. These examples leave insight and effectiveness uncertain rather than treating imaginative intensity as proof of sound direction.
