## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Creative accomplishments in new fields. To start new paths of research. Increase work. Temporary change in working.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `197`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

unfolding of a fiery woman, development of creative feelings, change of leadership in the people, creative development of a woman, development of the achievements of the woman, growing leadership through women, and goal oriented development of a people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creative work develops into new fields or changes its form.
- psychology: developing creative feelings are named without a broader psychological mechanism.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: changing collective leadership and growing leadership through women are examples.
- events/manifestations: new research paths, increased work, temporary work changes, and developing accomplishment are stated.
- conflicts/notes: development and change are supported; permanent improvement or a successful breakthrough is not assured.

## Contradictions

Witte's temporary work change and ABC's growing leadership concern different scales of development. Neither requires that every change be lasting or beneficial.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Pluto, Pluto specifies the pair through developing creative activity and changes in its field or direction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names new fields of accomplishment, new research paths, increased work, and temporary changes in working; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds developing creative feelings, achievements, and changes in collective leadership (printed p. 197, PDF p. 97). The common developmental theme leaves the permanence and value of a particular change open.
