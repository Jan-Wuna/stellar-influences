## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Continuous pursuit of ones goal on the mental or spiritual level.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `197`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Insightful leadership by women, a leading cultured people, a woman's mental or spiritual efforts, a woman's clear goal-setting, striving for clarity of feelings, a people's mental or spiritual accomplishments, a woman's cultural accomplishments, and a woman's striving for truth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: persistent pursuit of a mental or spiritual goal with attention to clarity and truth.
- psychology: clear goal-setting, striving for emotional clarity, and mental effort are named.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: insightful leadership by women and a leading cultured people are examples.
- events/manifestations: continuous goal pursuit and mental or cultural accomplishments are stated.
- conflicts/notes: striving for truth does not establish that a conclusion or leadership claim is true.

## Contradictions

Witte's mental or spiritual goal pursuit and ABC's leadership, clarity, and cultural accomplishments overlap without asserting a shared doctrine or guaranteed insight.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Poseidon, Poseidon specifies the pair through sustained mental or spiritual goal pursuit. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states continuous pursuit at that level; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds clear goal-setting, efforts toward clarity of feeling, cultural or mental accomplishments, and striving for truth (printed p. 197, PDF p. 97). Its leadership examples give that effort a collective setting without certifying the truth or quality of any particular outlook.
