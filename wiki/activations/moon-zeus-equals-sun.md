## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To master ones body. A sense for discipline and order. The daily repeated working hours. Wanting to assist. Energetic interference. Easily inflammable substance. The hour of the commencement of a fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `196`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A woman's fiery man, a man's fiery feelings, a woman's day of childbirth, a man's creative woman, a man's creative disposition, persons of the leading people, a day of effort for a woman, and a center of the leading people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: disciplined personal effort and repeated work alongside fiery creative feeling.
- psychology: discipline, order, willingness to assist, and a creative disposition are named.
- body/health: mastering the body is Witte's wording; ABC's day of childbirth is a historical event association.
- social/relationship: energetic assistance or intervention and people in a leading collective are examples.
- events/manifestations: repeated working hours, an effortful day, inflammable substance, and the start of a fire are stated.
- conflicts/notes: discipline, assistance, and fire are distinct source examples, without a demonstrated causal sequence.

## Contradictions

Witte combines orderly work with energetic intervention and literal fire examples; ABC adds creative people and childbirth. These settings should not be reduced to one bodily or occupational event.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Sun, Sun specifies the pair through personal discipline and energetic engagement with work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names bodily self-command, order, repeated working hours, assistance, and energetic intervention. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds fiery feelings, creative disposition, and an effortful day (printed p. 196, PDF p. 97). Childbirth and the commencement of a fire remain separate historical event examples; neither follows necessarily from disciplined activity.
