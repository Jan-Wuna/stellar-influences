## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Sudden emotion. To incite. Ring leader. Ignite (motor). Procreation with technical support.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `197`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

birth event for a woman, impulses of the leadership of a people, creativity of a dynamic woman, new goals of a woman, reform efforts of a people, creative emotional impulses, technical goals of a people, and unrest in the leading people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sudden emotional or creative impetus, incitement, and new goals.
- psychology: sudden emotion and creative emotional impulses are named.
- body/health: technical support for procreation and a birth event remain historical associations.
- social/relationship: a ringleader, collective leadership impulses, and reform efforts are examples.
- events/manifestations: motor ignition, technical goals, new aims, and unrest in a leading people are stated.
- conflicts/notes: new goals and unrest do not establish whether a reform is beneficial or technically successful.

## Contradictions

Witte's ignition and procreation examples differ from ABC's reform and technical goals. The common sudden impetus does not make these manifestations interchangeable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Uranus, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden emotion and an impulse to initiate or incite. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives a ringleader and motor ignition; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds new goals, creative emotional impulses, reform efforts, and technical aims (printed p. 197, PDF p. 97). The reproductive examples remain attributed historical associations. Initiation and unrest describe the impetus, while leaving its success and effects unspecified.
