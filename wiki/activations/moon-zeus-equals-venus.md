## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Fiery love nature. Hot blooded. Hour of progeny.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `196`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Beginning love for a woman, a woman's fiery feelings, a people's efforts for peace, a woman's procreation through love, a woman's artistic accomplishments, a fiery woman's beauty, a woman's creative art, and the wishes of a people's leadership

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: fiery affection and creative or artistic expression.
- psychology: fiery loving feeling and beginning love are named.
- body/health: Witte's hot-blooded wording and procreation examples are historical descriptions, not physiological or fertility findings.
- social/relationship: love for a woman and collective peace efforts are examples.
- events/manifestations: artistic accomplishments, creative art, beauty, and leadership wishes are stated.
- conflicts/notes: passion, art, and peace efforts are different manifestations rather than proof of one relationship outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses fiery love and procreation; ABC also gives art and peace efforts. The entries do not assert that all intense love is reproductive or that artistic activity ensures harmony.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Venus, Venus specifies the pair through fiery affection and creative expression. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names an ardent love nature; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds beginning love, fiery feelings, artistic accomplishments, creative art, and collective peace efforts (printed p. 196, PDF p. 97). Their procreation examples remain historical associations alongside these other settings, without defining the outcome of a particular love or creative effort.
