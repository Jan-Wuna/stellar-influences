## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Inflammability. Spontaneous combustion. General discipline and order. Attendants of machines driven by fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `196`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

efforts of a woman in public, fiery woman in public life, women's goals in public life, creative woman in public, achievements of a woman in public life, and leader of the people in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: discipline, technical fire activity, and creative effort in a general or public setting.
- psychology: neither entry supplies a detailed individual psychological account.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily condition; combustion is an event example.
- social/relationship: machine attendants, publicly active women, and public leaders are examples.
- events/manifestations: inflammability, spontaneous combustion, public accomplishments, and public goals are stated.
- conflicts/notes: public creativity and literal combustion are distinct examples, with no common mechanism established.

## Contradictions

Witte's mechanical and combustion examples differ from ABC's public creative effort. They share the orientation but do not require that public achievement involve fire.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Vernal Point, Vernal Point specifies the pair through general discipline and publicly situated activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) retains machinery driven by fire, inflammability, and spontaneous combustion; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives women's public effort, goals, creative activity, and leadership (printed p. 196, PDF p. 97). These technical and public manifestations remain distinct, without interpreting every visible creative effort as a fire event.
