## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `132` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Every minute taken up. To exert oneself under expenditure of all one’s force. Record accomplishment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `197`, PDF p. `97` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A woman's leadership influence, a woman's childbearing strength, a woman's tremendous efforts, a woman's striving for power, the influence of a creative disposition, energetic leadership by women, a leading woman's strength, powerful leaders of a people, and strength of a leading people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: intense expenditure of effort, leadership strength, and exceptional accomplishment.
- psychology: striving for power and a creative disposition are named.
- body/health: ABC's childbearing strength is a historical assertion, not a fertility finding.
- social/relationship: energetic women leaders and powerful collective leaders are examples.
- events/manifestations: fully occupied time, expenditure of all one's force, and record accomplishment are stated.
- conflicts/notes: intense effort and record results are listed together without proving that every exhausting effort succeeds.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes total exertion and record accomplishment; ABC adds leadership and procreative strength. These do not establish that achievement requires a particular gender or reproductive role.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Moon/Zeus = Vulcanus, Vulcanus specifies the pair through concentrated expenditure of strength toward achievement. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes every minute occupied, exertion of all one's force, and record accomplishment; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds leadership influence, tremendous efforts, striving for power, and the influence of a creative disposition (printed p. 197, PDF p. 97). Its childbearing-strength example remains an attributed historical assertion. The intensity of effort does not guarantee an exceptional result.
