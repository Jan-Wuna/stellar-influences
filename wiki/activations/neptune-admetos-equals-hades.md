## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `252` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Deterioration of the living conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `403`, PDF p. `200` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a dangerous blockade, disappointment and restriction and renunciation, danger in deep water, dangerous circulatory weakness, sorrow through thorough deception, creeping hidden depression, and hidden deepening in metaphysics

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: deteriorating living conditions and adverse restrictions, with a separate blockade-release example.
- psychology: disappointment, sorrow through deception, and hidden depression are attributed source themes.
- body/health: dangerous circulatory weakness and depression remain historical claims, not clinical findings.
- social/relationship: neither entry supplies a specific relationship role; living circumstances are Witte's focus.
- events/manifestations: danger in deep water, renunciation, dissolution of a dangerous blockade, and hidden metaphysical involvement are stated.
- conflicts/notes: removal of a dangerous blockade differs in direction from deteriorating conditions and is not flattened into decline.

## Contradictions

Witte's deterioration and ABC's dissolution of a dangerous blockade differ in direction. They remain side by side; the other adverse ABC examples do not erase the release example.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Admetos = Hades, Hades specifies the pair through deteriorating conditions and adverse limitations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states deterioration of living conditions; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointment, restriction, renunciation, and sorrow caused by deception (printed p. 403, PDF p. 200). ABC also names dissolution of a dangerous blockade, so relief remains a distinct possibility within its range. Its deep-water danger, circulatory weakness, and hidden depression remain attributed historical associations.
