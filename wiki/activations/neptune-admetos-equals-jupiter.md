## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `252` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Happy and fortunate changes in relation to living conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `402`, PDF p. `200` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a financial blockade, restricted joy in water, success through thorough sensitivity, successful dissolution of a blockade, consolidation of financial weakness (Finanz-Schwäche festigen), joy in deep water, thorough deception concerning law or rights (Rechts-Täuschung), and advantage through deepening involvement in metaphysics

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: fortunate living-condition changes and relief from blockades, qualified by adverse ABC examples.
- psychology: sensitivity and joy in deep water are named without a general personality claim.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a specific bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Witte describes living conditions; ABC includes financial obstruction and deception concerning law or rights.
- events/manifestations: successful blockade dissolution and advantage through metaphysical deepening are examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC literally says Finanz-Schwäche festigen; this is not rewritten as strengthening finances or assumed improvement.

## Contradictions

Witte's favorable changes and ABC's successful releases coexist with ABC's financial-weakness consolidation and Rechts-Täuschung. These materially adverse phrases prevent an unqualified reading of abundance or improvement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Admetos = Jupiter, Jupiter specifies the pair through favorable changes in living conditions and successful release of obstructions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives fortunate change; [ABC](#abc-entry) names financial-blockade dissolution, success through sensitivity, and joy in deep water (printed p. 402, PDF p. 200).

ABC also retains the literal phrase Finanz-Schwäche festigen, or consolidating financial weakness, and deception concerning law or rights. Those adverse examples are not converted into financial strength. The favorable strand therefore does not establish universal material improvement or validate metaphysical claims.
