## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `252` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The government sets up new standards of living. Changes ordered by the authorities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `403`, PDF p. `200` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a large blockade, special circulatory weakness, blocked sensitivity of the state, the end of a state's weakness, great water depth, independent deepening in metaphysics, and important fluid circulation

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: authority-ordered changes in living standards, with state weakness or blockade release among ABC's examples.
- psychology: ABC's blocked sensitivity of the state is retained as its own wording.
- body/health: special circulatory weakness and fluid circulation are historical associations, not individual findings.
- social/relationship: government, authorities, and the state are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: new living standards, ordered changes, large-blockade dissolution, and deep water are stated.
- conflicts/notes: ordered change does not establish deprivation or improvement in every case; metaphysical deepening is a separate example.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes official changes, while ABC includes the end of state weakness and release of a large blockade. The entries do not establish that authority-directed change is inherently harmful or beneficial.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Admetos = Kronos, Kronos specifies the pair through changes directed by authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names government-established living standards and changes ordered by officials; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds state weakness, its ending, and dissolution of a large blockade (printed p. 403, PDF p. 200). ABC's deep-water, circulation, and metaphysical examples remain separate associations. Authority sets the context of change without determining whether every resulting condition is better or worse.
