## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `252` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To work for changes and improvements, for better living conditions. Changes of conditions in relation to work. To work under changed conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `402`, PDF p. `200` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Actions to dissolve blockades, work in deep water, work on water circulation, dissolution of a labor strike, limited work in the air, disappointment through restriction of work, treating circulatory weakness, and resistance to Fremd-Arbeit (outside or foreign work)

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: working for improved conditions or operating under changed work conditions.
- psychology: disappointment when work is restricted is ABC's explicit feeling theme.
- body/health: treating circulatory weakness is a historical activity example, not treatment advice.
- social/relationship: labor strikes and resistance to Fremd-Arbeit are listed without specifying the latter's social setting.
- events/manifestations: blockade dissolution, deep-water work, water-circulation work, and limited aerial work are stated.
- conflicts/notes: effort aims at improvement but does not guarantee it; Fremd-Arbeit remains contextually unexplained.

## Contradictions

Witte's effort toward better conditions is an aim, while ABC retains restricted work and disappointment. These do not establish that work necessarily resolves every changed circumstance.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Admetos = Mars, Mars specifies the pair through work responding to changed conditions or seeking improvement. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names both working for better living conditions and working under altered conditions; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds actions to dissolve blockades, ending a labor strike, and work involving deep water or water circulation (printed p. 402, PDF p. 200). ABC also retains restricted work and disappointment. The effort is explicit, while its success remains open; the unexplained Fremd-Arbeit phrase is not assigned a particular labor or political setting.
