## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `252` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

People, the public, several persons or women experience changes in their form of being.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `402`, PDF p. `200` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A disappointed woman's depression, restriction of water for a people, consolidation of unclear feelings, a woman's fluid circulation, thorough deception of feelings, a woman's circulatory weakness, release of an emotional blockade, and deepening and refinement of feelings

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: changes in the state of a public, group, or women, alongside altered or released feelings.
- psychology: disappointment, unclear feeling, emotional deception, and refinement of feeling are named.
- body/health: depression, fluid circulation, and circulatory weakness are ABC's historical claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Witte names several persons, women, or the public; ABC includes water restriction for a people.
- events/manifestations: release of an emotional blockade and deepening feelings are explicit examples.
- conflicts/notes: collective change does not establish deprivation or freezing; unclear feelings and their refinement are distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte gives group or public change without judging its direction. ABC retains both unclear or deceived feelings and release or refinement, so neither decline nor improvement describes every example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Admetos = Moon, Moon specifies the pair through changes affecting a public, several people, or women. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives that collective range; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointment, unclear feelings, and emotional deception, but also release of an emotional blockade and deepening or refinement of feeling (printed p. 402, PDF p. 200). Its water restriction and bodily claims remain separate historical associations. The shared change does not require a uniform movement toward deprivation or recovery.
