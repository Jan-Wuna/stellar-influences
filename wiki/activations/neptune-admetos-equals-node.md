## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `252` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be part or to suffer through changes in new conditions of living.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `402`, PDF p. `200` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of blocked connections, a relationship to deep water, disappointment over a suppressed connection, deepening subtle connections, thorough deception in a connection, and creeping oppression in a connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: participation in changed living conditions and connections that may be obstructed, released, or oppressive.
- psychology: disappointment, deception, and creeping oppression in a connection are ABC themes.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a specific bodily condition.
- social/relationship: suppressed or blocked connections and participation in shared conditions are explicit.
- events/manifestations: blocked-connection dissolution, deep-water association, and deepening subtle connections are stated.
- conflicts/notes: participation does not guarantee solidarity; subtle connections are source language without an established mechanism.

## Contradictions

Witte allows participation or suffering in new conditions. ABC similarly includes release of a connection alongside suppression and oppression, without requiring a single direction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Admetos = Node, Node specifies the pair through participation in changed living conditions and the state of connections. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) allows being part of change or suffering through it; [ABC](#abc-entry) names blocked connections dissolving, but also disappointment, deception, and creeping oppression within a connection (printed p. 402, PDF p. 200). Its deep-water and subtle-connection examples remain specific source associations, without making shared circumstances evidence of a supportive bond.
