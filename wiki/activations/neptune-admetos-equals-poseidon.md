## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `252` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Changes in the mental or spiritual concept of being.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `403`, PDF p. `200` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a mental or spiritual blockade, perseverance in clear air, depression through mental or spiritual weakness, disappointment through restricted truth, thorough clarity through sensitivity, clear deep water, spiritual deepening in metaphysics, and deepening in future culture

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: change in a mental or spiritual conception of being, with blockade release or deepening.
- psychology: sensitivity, clarity, disappointment, and depression are ABC's attributed terms.
- body/health: mental weakness and depression remain historical claims; clear air and water are separate settings.
- social/relationship: neither entry specifies a relationship role; ABC names restricted truth.
- events/manifestations: mental-blockade dissolution, metaphysical deepening, and future culture are listed.
- conflicts/notes: clarity is a source possibility, not proof that a metaphysical belief or future claim is true.

## Contradictions

ABC combines mental-blockade release and clarity with weakness and disappointment. Witte's conceptual change does not settle which of these expressions occurs.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Admetos = Poseidon, Poseidon specifies the pair through a changed mental or spiritual conception of existence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives that conceptual shift; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds mental-blockade release, clarity through sensitivity, and metaphysical deepening, alongside disappointment through restricted truth (printed p. 403, PDF p. 200). The air, water, and depressed-state examples remain attributed associations. Change in outlook does not certify its truth or establish that deprivation forces it.
