## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `252` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The condition of the body changes. The man who enters different living conditions. Days when the weather changes. To change to a different aggregate condition. To freeze or to thaw.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `402`, PDF p. `200` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a personal blockade, a blocked air center, a person's fluid circulation, profound personal weakness, creeping personal depression, weak bodily resistance, and stabilization of bodily weakness

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: changes of bodily or living conditions and changes of physical state.
- psychology: creeping personal depression is ABC's historical wording.
- body/health: weakness, bodily resistance, and fluid circulation are attributed source claims, not findings about a reader.
- social/relationship: Witte names a man entering different living conditions without a specific relationship role.
- events/manifestations: weather changes, freezing or thawing, personal-blockade dissolution, and stabilization of weakness are listed.
- conflicts/notes: bodily change and material phase change are different examples; stabilization of weakness is not rewritten as restored strength.

## Contradictions

Witte's bodily, weather, and aggregate-state changes differ in subject. ABC's release of a blockade coexists with stabilized weakness, so change is not uniformly improvement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Admetos = Sun, Sun specifies the pair through altered bodily or living conditions and changes of physical state. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes weather change, freezing, and thawing; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds personal-blockade release alongside weakness, fluid circulation, and stabilization of bodily weakness (printed p. 402, PDF p. 200). These remain distinct historical associations. Material phase change does not supply a mechanism for the bodily claims, and stabilized weakness is not equated with recovery.
