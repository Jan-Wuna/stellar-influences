## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `252` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To change the form of being in general. To enter new conditions of existence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `402`, PDF p. `200` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of congestion in public, public life blocked by water, restrictions and disappointments in public life, thorough deception of the public, circulatory weakness in public, and water circulation in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: general changes of existence expressed through public conditions.
- psychology: public disappointment and deception are ABC themes without an individual personality claim.
- body/health: circulatory weakness in public is historical source wording, not a population-health finding.
- social/relationship: the public and public life are the explicit collective settings.
- events/manifestations: public congestion dissolving, water-blocked public life, and water circulation are listed.
- conflicts/notes: public change need not be either universal progress or decline; relief and restriction coexist.

## Contradictions

Witte gives new conditions of existence in general. ABC adds both congestion release and public restrictions, without asserting that all members of a public experience the same change.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Admetos = Vernal Point, Vernal Point specifies the pair through general changes of existence and public conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names entering new conditions; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives public congestion dissolving, public life blocked by water, and restrictions, disappointments, or deception in public life (printed p. 402, PDF p. 200). Its circulatory wording remains attributed historical doctrine. The collective setting does not establish a single direction or outcome for everyone.
