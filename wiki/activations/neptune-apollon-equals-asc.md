## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `251` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Others try to deceive the native. Detection of deception or embezzlement. To experience embezzlement, money frauds or deception. The truth is brought to light.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `400`, PDF p. `199` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Others pretend to have knowledge, spread of uncertainty at the place, freedom in airspace, success through a sensitive partner, metaphysical experiences with the partner, others' metaphysical knowledge, uncertain freedom at the place, and quantities of water at the place

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: deception or its exposure in encounters, alongside local uncertainty and partnership experiences.
- psychology: uncertainty and a partner's sensitivity are ABC themes.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: others pretending knowledge, a sensitive partner, and attempted deception are examples.
- events/manifestations: fraud detection, embezzlement, truth coming to light, airspace freedom, and local water are listed.
- conflicts/notes: experiencing deception and exposing it are different possibilities; uncertainty alone does not prove fraud.

## Contradictions

Witte includes both being deceived and discovering deception; ABC also allows success through a sensitive partner. These do not justify treating every associate as deceitful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Apollon = Asc, Asc specifies the pair through encounters in which deception may occur or be exposed. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names attempted deception, embezzlement, fraud, and truth coming to light; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds others pretending knowledge and uncertainty at a place, but also success through a sensitive partner (printed p. 400, PDF p. 199). Its airspace, water, and metaphysical examples remain distinct settings. Uncertainty does not by itself establish another person's dishonesty.
