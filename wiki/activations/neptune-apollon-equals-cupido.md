## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `251` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Many dissolutions of marriages and partnerships.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `401`, PDF p. `199` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Spread of uncertainty in a community, successful dissolution of a community, shared spread of metaphysics, shared pretence of knowledge, a community's metaphysical experiences, and a community's knowledge of the future

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: dissolution of partnerships or communities, with shared uncertainty or claimed knowledge.
- psychology: collective uncertainty and pretended knowledge are ABC themes.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: marriages, partnerships, and communities are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: many marital or partnership dissolutions, shared metaphysical activity, and community dissolution are stated.
- conflicts/notes: ABC calls dissolution successful; neither entry defines whose interest it serves or guarantees the truth of shared claims.

## Contradictions

Witte gives many dissolutions and ABC calls one communal dissolution successful. Their different evaluations remain visible without making separation necessarily harmful or beneficial to every participant.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Apollon = Cupido, Cupido specifies the pair through partnerships or communities that may dissolve or share uncertainty. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names many marital and partnership dissolutions; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds community dissolution, spreading uncertainty, and shared metaphysical activity or pretended knowledge (printed p. 401, PDF p. 199). A group's shared claims do not establish their truth, and ABC's favorable evaluation of dissolution does not determine its effect on every member.
