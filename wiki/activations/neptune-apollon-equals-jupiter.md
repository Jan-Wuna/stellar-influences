## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `251` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To live under a fortunate illusion. To penetrate and absorb a new science successfully. To seek one’s happiness in occult science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `400`, PDF p. `199` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disappointing financial activity, success through metaphysical knowledge, joy in metaphysical experiences, deception in knowledge of law or rights, increasing uncertainty about law or rights, advantage through knowledge of the future, and increasing financial weakness

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: a favorable illusion or successful learning alongside financial and legal uncertainty.
- psychology: joy in metaphysical experiences and happiness sought in occult science are source themes.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: financial activity and knowledge of law or rights are ABC settings.
- events/manifestations: absorbing a new science, claimed advantage through future knowledge, and increasing financial weakness are stated.
- conflicts/notes: the favorable learning strand does not erase financial disappointment or establish that occult knowledge is reliable.

## Contradictions

Witte's fortunate illusion and successful learning coexist with ABC's financial weakness and legal uncertainty. These differing evaluations prevent an unqualified account of optimism or prosperity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Apollon = Jupiter, Jupiter specifies the pair through a favorable illusion or satisfaction in learning, including occult interests. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names successful absorption of a new science and happiness sought in occult science; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds joy in metaphysical experience but also disappointing finances, deception concerning law or rights, and increasing financial weakness (printed p. 400, PDF p. 199). These contrasts keep claimed success separate from verified knowledge or guaranteed prosperity.
