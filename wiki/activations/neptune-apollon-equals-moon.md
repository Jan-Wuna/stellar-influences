## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `251` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be deceived by female persons, to experience disappointments with them. The public or persons who feel themselves deceived.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `400`, PDF p. `199` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Spread of uncertainty among a people, a woman's intuitive experiences, metaphysical emotional knowledge, deceptive emotional knowledge, uncertain freedom for a people, spread of metaphysics among a people, and a people's knowledge of the future

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: deception or felt deception involving women, people, or a public.
- psychology: disappointment, collective uncertainty, and intuitive emotional knowledge are source themes.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: women and a public feeling deceived are Witte's examples, not universal gender traits.
- events/manifestations: uncertain freedom and spreading metaphysics among a people are stated.
- conflicts/notes: feeling deceived is not identical to proving deception; intuition does not establish the truth of emotional knowledge.

## Contradictions

Witte distinguishes experienced deception from people feeling themselves deceived. ABC's intuitive and deceptive emotional knowledge similarly retains different evaluations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Apollon = Moon, Moon specifies the pair through experienced or felt deception in a social setting. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes disappointment involving women and a public that feels deceived; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds collective uncertainty, intuitive experiences, and both metaphysical and deceptive emotional knowledge (printed p. 400, PDF p. 199). Its gendered examples remain particular historical claims. Neither shared feeling nor intuition alone proves that a deception or future claim is real.
