## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `251` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be connected with people who distort the truth, who are insincere.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `400`, PDF p. `199` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Encounter with quantities of water, successful connection through intuition, metaphysical experience in connections, deceiving oneself about the freedom of a connection, connection to metaphysical knowledge, and knowledge about future connections

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: connections involving insincerity, uncertain freedom, or claimed intuitive knowledge.
- psychology: self-deception about a connection's freedom is an explicit ABC theme.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: people who distort truth and metaphysically oriented connections are examples.
- events/manifestations: successful intuitive connection, water encounters, and claims about future connections are stated.
- conflicts/notes: insincerity is one source example; the entries do not make every association fraudulent.

## Contradictions

Witte's insincere associates differ from ABC's successful intuitive connection, while ABC itself adds self-deception. These possibilities remain separate rather than defining a single deceptive network.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Apollon = Node, Node specifies the pair through connections whose truthfulness or freedom may be uncertain. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names insincere people who distort truth; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds self-deception about a connection's freedom but also successful connection through intuition and shared metaphysical experience (printed p. 400, PDF p. 199). Those intuitive or future-oriented claims remain attributed, and no entry establishes that every connection is deceptive.
