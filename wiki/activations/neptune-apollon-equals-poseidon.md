## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `251` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To experience lack of understanding in highly mental or spiritual things with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `401`, PDF p. `199` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Spread of uncertainty through truth, trade in clear water, clarity through successful intuition, metaphysics as culture knowledge, uncertain spiritual freedom, refined spiritual knowledge, and true future knowledge

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: difficulty sharing mental or spiritual understanding, alongside claims of intuition and refined knowledge.
- psychology: uncertain spiritual freedom and claimed intuitive clarity appear together.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: Witte explicitly places the lack of understanding between people.
- events/manifestations: ABC includes clear-water trade, metaphysics as cultural knowledge, and claimed future knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: truth and future knowledge are source descriptions, not independent confirmation of their accuracy.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses failed mutual understanding; ABC includes both uncertainty and claimed clarity. Neither passage establishes that spiritual claims are true or that disagreement proves another person's failure.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Apollon = Poseidon, Poseidon specifies the pair through difficulty making mental or spiritual matters understood between people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states this interpersonal difficulty directly; [ABC](#abc-entry) places uncertainty beside successful intuition, refined spiritual knowledge, and metaphysics as cultural knowledge (printed p. 401, PDF p. 199). Clear-water trade is a separate concrete example. These passages support a tension between claimed understanding and its communication, while leaving the truth of the claims unverified.
