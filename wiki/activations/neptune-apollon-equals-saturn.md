## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `251` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Unclean methods of deceiving of many. Deception of or for many of long duration.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `401`, PDF p. `199` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Spreading of uncertainty through separation, breathing results through concentration, experiences with creeping pain, water mass damage, extensive future tasks, and increasing losses due to uncertainty

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: prolonged deception affecting many, with separation and growing uncertainty in ABC.
- psychology: concentration and uncertainty are explicit, without an explanation of how either operates.
- body/health: ABC's breathing results and creeping pain are historical source claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Witte describes deception of or for many; ABC adds separation.
- events/manifestations: long deception, water-mass damage, extensive future tasks, and increasing losses are stated examples.
- conflicts/notes: the concentration example does not make every entry fraudulent, and uncertainty alone does not establish deceit.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes long-lasting deceptive methods. ABC also includes concentration and future tasks, so those examples cannot all be reduced to deliberate corruption.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Apollon = Saturn, Saturn specifies the pair through prolonged deception and uncertainty associated with separation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) concerns deception of or for many over a long period; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds increasing losses, water-mass damage, and extensive future tasks (printed p. 401, PDF p. 199). Its concentration, breathing, and pain examples remain distinct historical claims. The combined emphasis is on duration and adverse uncertainty, without assuming that every separation, task, or bodily complaint entails fraud.
