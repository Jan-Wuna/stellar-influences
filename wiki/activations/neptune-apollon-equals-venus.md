## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `251` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Insincere or feigned love and friendship. To be deceived about an affection (sympathy). Dislike turns into affection and vise versa.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `400`, PDF p. `199` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disappointing love experiences, joy over freedom in water, harmony through metaphysical knowledge, metaphysical experiences in the arts, insecurity through freedom in love, preference for metaphysical experiences, and increasing disappointment in love

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: misleading affection and disappointment in love, alongside harmony through metaphysical or artistic experience.
- psychology: changing sympathy and insecurity concerning freedom in love are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: feigned love or friendship and deception about affection are Witte's relationship themes.
- events/manifestations: disappointing love experiences, metaphysical experience in art, and joy in freedom in water are examples.
- conflicts/notes: the entries do not predict the duration or outcome of a particular relationship.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes insincerity and changes in affection. ABC retains disappointment but also harmony, art, and preferred metaphysical experiences; these do not establish deception in every affectionate bond.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Apollon = Venus, Venus specifies the pair through affection whose sincerity or stability is uncertain. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes feigned friendship, deception about sympathy, and shifts between liking and dislike. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointment in love and insecurity through freedom in love, while retaining harmony through metaphysical knowledge and experience in the arts (printed p. 400, PDF p. 199). This supports a distinction between misleading affection and valued experiences of harmony, without predicting that a relationship must fail.
