## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `251` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Corruption. The general deception about the future. The general uncertainty about the near future.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `400`, PDF p. `199` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Spread of uncertainty in public, successful intuitions in public life, metaphysical knowledge in public life, public water management, much uncertainty in public, and masses of water in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: public uncertainty and deception concerning the future.
- psychology: uncertainty about the near future is Witte's stated outlook.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: both entries explicitly concern general or public life.
- events/manifestations: corruption, public metaphysical claims, water management, and masses of water are named settings.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's successful intuitions remain source claims; public uncertainty does not prove corruption.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses general deception and corruption. ABC also describes successful intuition and public water settings, which should not automatically be classified as deception.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Apollon = Vernal Point, Vernal Point specifies the pair through public life and the general outlook on the near future. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names corruption, deception, and uncertainty; [ABC](#abc-entry) repeats public uncertainty while adding claimed successful intuitions and metaphysical knowledge in public life (printed p. 400, PDF p. 199). Its public water-management and water-mass examples are concrete settings in the same row. They do not establish a shared cause, and neither uncertainty nor a public knowledge claim alone demonstrates corruption.
