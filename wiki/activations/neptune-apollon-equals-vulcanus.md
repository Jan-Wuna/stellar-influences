## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `251` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To experience a great disappointment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `401`, PDF p. `199` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

expansion of water power, influential knowledge in metaphysics, successful influence through intuition, successful air power, uncertainty through increase of force, spread of strong uncertainty, and feign knowledge and power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: great disappointment, with power, influence, and strong uncertainty in ABC.
- psychology: disappointment and pretended knowledge or power are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: ABC describes influential metaphysical knowledge and influence through intuition.
- events/manifestations: water power, air power, increased force, and spreading strong uncertainty are source examples.
- conflicts/notes: the entries do not explain disappointment as a necessary collapse of hope or validate claims of power.

## Contradictions

Witte gives only great disappointment. ABC includes both successful influence and feigned knowledge or power, so disappointment cannot be used to invalidate every favorable example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Apollon = Vulcanus, Vulcanus specifies the pair through great disappointment and questions of influence or power. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states the disappointment directly; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful intuitive influence, influential metaphysical knowledge, increased force, and feigned knowledge and power (printed p. 401, PDF p. 199). Strong uncertainty accompanies these alternatives. Water power and air power remain separate practical examples; the passages do not supply a mechanism that makes disappointment inevitable whenever influence is exercised.
