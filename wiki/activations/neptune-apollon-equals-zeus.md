## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `251` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Wasted efforts. Useless activities. Creative activities in occult science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `401`, PDF p. `199` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Effortful metaphysical experiences, beginning of an uncertain freedom, experience with fire-water (Feuer-Wasser), pretending leadership successes, performance successes in water, shaping metaphysical knowledge, determined dissemination of metaphysics, and shaping future knowledge

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creative occult or metaphysical activity, with wasted effort and pretended success among the examples.
- psychology: effortful experience and determined dissemination are explicit without a motive being supplied.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: ABC's leadership successes are pretended; they must not be reported as actual leadership achievement.
- events/manifestations: creative occult activity, shaping metaphysical or future knowledge, and performance successes in water are stated.
- conflicts/notes: Feuer-Wasser is retained literally because its intended referent is unexplained; it is excluded from confident synthesis.

## Contradictions

Witte places creative occult activity beside useless effort. ABC distinguishes pretended leadership success from performance success in water, so neither universal futility nor general success is warranted.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Apollon = Zeus, Zeus specifies the pair through effort directed toward occult or metaphysical activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names both creative activity and wasted effort; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds determined dissemination and shaping claimed metaphysical or future knowledge (printed p. 401, PDF p. 199). Its pretended leadership successes differ from the separately stated performance successes in water. The combined reading concerns the activity and the status of its claimed results, without assuming that all such work succeeds or is useless.
