## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `89` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be deceived many times. To gain ample experience through fraud and tricks. Duped by a gimmick. Uncertain, unstable behavior of the counterpart in trade, commerce or science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `119`, PDF p. `58` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Extension of the subtle environment, experiences in a foreign place, success of the partner through sensitivity, success in an uncertain environment, success with others in water, successful trade with water, much water nearby, and increasing sensitivity of the environment

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: repeated experience of deception, alongside successful sensitive dealings and wider experience.
- psychology: being duped and increasing sensitivity are separate explicit themes.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: counterparts in trade, commerce or science and success with others are the stated settings.
- events/manifestations: foreign-place experience, water trade, and an uncertain environment appear alongside repeated fraud.
- conflicts/notes: repeated deception does not establish that the person learns a lesson, and uncertainty is not equivalent to fraud.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses deception and unstable counterparts; ABC retains success in uncertain surroundings and a partner's success through sensitivity. Neither outcome cancels the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Asc = Apollon, Apollon specifies the pair through repeated or extended experience with others in uncertain surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes repeated deception and unstable counterparts in trade, commerce, or science. [ABC](#abc-entry) also includes a partner's success through sensitivity, success in an uncertain environment, foreign-place experience, and water trade (printed p. 119, PDF p. 58). These examples preserve the difference between being misled and acting successfully amid uncertainty, without promising that deception necessarily produces learning.
