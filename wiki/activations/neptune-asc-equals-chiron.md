## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `113` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `112`).

#### Sandbach Entry

To help others to heal by intuitively making them aware of other and deeper levels of their personality, so that their identity is not so tightly or narrowly focused.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: helping another person loosen a narrow identity through intuitive awareness of deeper personality levels.
- psychology: a less tightly focused identity is Sandbach's explicit intended change.
- body/health: healing is the source's language; no bodily condition or treatment is specified.
- social/relationship: one person helps others become aware of deeper personality levels.
- events/manifestations: an intuitive helping encounter is described without a concrete event prediction.
- conflicts/notes: only Sandbach is represented; intuitive healing is an attributed interpretation, not established therapeutic efficacy.

## Contradictions

Only one source is represented, so there is no inter-source disagreement to compare. The passage concerns widening self-awareness, without a stated physical healing mechanism.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Asc = Chiron, Chiron specifies the pair through helping others recognize deeper levels of personality so that identity becomes less narrowly focused. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) places intuitive awareness at the center of this helping relationship (PDF p. 113). Its healing language concerns this broader understanding of self; the passage supplies neither a bodily diagnosis nor a treatment. The supported emphasis is on loosening a limiting self-definition through awareness, rather than assuming a general healing capacity.
