## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `89` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be united in unhappy marriage. To wish to quit a union. Secrecies in the home. To be deceived about the true conditions in the home.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `119`, PDF p. `58` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a shared environment, disappointed partner-community, sensitive artist as partner, together with others in the air, shared surroundings by the water, weak local community, and uncertain partner for the community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: unhappy or uncertain union and shared surroundings, including domestic secrecy.
- psychology: the wish to leave a union and deception about home conditions are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: marriage, the home, partner-community, and a sensitive artist as partner are the stated settings.
- events/manifestations: dissolution of shared surroundings and a weak local community accompany water and air settings.
- conflicts/notes: an unhappy union and an uncertain community partner are examples, without a universal separation forecast.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes unhappy marriage and concealed home conditions. ABC adds artistic sensitivity and shared settings, which need not themselves be unhappy or deceptive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Asc = Cupido, Cupido specifies the pair through the condition of a union, home, or shared environment. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes unhappy marriage, wishing to leave a union, and secrecy about home conditions; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a disappointed partner-community and dissolution of shared surroundings (printed p. 119, PDF p. 58). A sensitive artist as partner and shared water or air settings remain distinct examples. The common concern is uncertainty within belonging, without making every shared setting evidence of deception.
