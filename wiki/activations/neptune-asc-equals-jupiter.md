## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `89` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To commit money frauds.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `118`, PDF p. `58` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Partner disappointed by the law, success of the partner through sensitivity, joy in subtle surroundings, joy with the partner in water, legal uncertainty of the other person, uncertain place for money, and uncertain legal surroundings

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `287`, entry code `1028`.

#### Ebertin Entry

The misfortune to lose one's good luck, good name, possessions or money through others. The urge to cheat others or the tendency to be cheated by others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `113` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `112`).

#### Sandbach Entry

To enthusiastically identify with one’s ideals and to eagerly share them with others. To be overly self-sacrificing. Success through taking a delicate, subtle approach to situations.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source reference: printed p. `336` (entry).

#### Munkasey Entry

An increased religious philosophy or outlook; added need for legal actions or responses; a need to be generous to those for whom you feel compassionate; relating your ideas persuasively; ideas for publicity or public relations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: financial and legal vulnerability through others, alongside generosity, shared ideals, and subtle success.
- psychology: enthusiasm for ideals and compassion coexist with the urge to cheat or vulnerability to being cheated.
- body/health: none of the entries supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: generosity, persuasive communication, and excessive self-sacrifice qualify dealings with others.
- events/manifestations: money fraud, losses of reputation or possessions, legal responses, and public relations are named examples.
- conflicts/notes: the favorable idealistic reading does not erase the explicit financial-fraud claims or establish every loss as fraud.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize fraud and loss through others; Sandbach and Munkasey include successful subtle approaches and generosity. ABC preserves both success through sensitivity and legal uncertainty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Asc = Jupiter, Jupiter specifies the pair through dealings where generosity and shared ideals coexist with financial or legal uncertainty. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) passages explicitly name fraud and losses through others, while [ABC](#abc-entry) places legal uncertainty beside a partner's success through sensitivity (printed p. 118, PDF p. 58). [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds subtle success and excessive self-sacrifice; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds compassion, persuasion, and legal responses. The reading therefore retains both constructive outreach and the possibility of being misled, without treating either as inevitable.
