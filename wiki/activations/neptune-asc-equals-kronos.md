## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To reject authorities because one feels oneself deceived. To be the leader or to follow a leader in fraudulent enterprises.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Authoritats Schwach the Partners, particularly delicate partner, dominance of others in the air, large place on the water, weak state as partner, weak self confidence of the partner, superior foreign partner, and opinion of the authoritat of others

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Asc = Kronos`: To reject authorities because one feels oneself deceived. To be the leader or to follow a leader in fraudulent enterprises.
- Practical focus: elevates deception into questions of authority and leadership, showing distrust of power, rejection of superiors, or participation in fraudulent enterprises directed from above.
- Practical expression: particularly delicate partner, dominance of others in the air, large place on the water, weak state as partner, and weak self confidence of the partner.
