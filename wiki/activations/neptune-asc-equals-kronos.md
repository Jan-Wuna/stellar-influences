## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `89` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To reject authorities because one feels oneself deceived. To be the leader or to follow a leader in fraudulent enterprises.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `119`, PDF p. `58` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Weakness of the partner's authority, particularly sensitive partner, dominance of others in the air, large place by the water, weak state as partner, weak self-confidence of the partner, superior foreign partner, and denial of others' authority

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: uncertainty involving authority, leadership, and the partner's standing.
- psychology: feeling deceived and weak self-confidence are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: rejecting authority or leading or following fraudulent enterprises are Witte's stated alternatives.
- events/manifestations: a weak state as partner, a superior foreign partner, and a large place by water are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: authority weakness does not establish fraudulent leadership, and rejection of authority need not prove actual deception.

## Contradictions

Witte links rejection of authorities to feeling deceived and includes fraud. ABC also includes sensitivity and differing strengths of partners, without making every superior or weak partner fraudulent.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Asc = Kronos, Kronos specifies the pair through authority and the standing of those with whom one deals. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes rejecting authorities after feeling deceived, as well as leading or following fraudulent enterprises. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds weak authority and self-confidence in a partner, a superior foreign partner, and denial of others' authority (printed p. 119, PDF p. 58). These passages distinguish uncertainty about authority from demonstrated fraud; sensitivity and weakness are also retained without turning them into evidence of wrongdoing.
