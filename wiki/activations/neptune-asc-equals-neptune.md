## Source Entries

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/ASCENDANT With Itself`
- Source reference: printed p. `338` (entry).

#### Munkasey Entry

Abandoning situations or people when your attitudes toward them change; eases your concerns about being deceived or tricked when you are with untrustworthy people; an enhanced ability to read future trends.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: leaving people or situations as attitudes change, with reduced concern about deception.
- psychology: concern about being tricked is eased even in the company of untrustworthy people.
- body/health: the entry supplies no bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: abandoning people and being among untrustworthy people are the stated relationship settings.
- events/manifestations: changing involvement and reading future trends are Munkasey's examples.
- conflicts/notes: reduced concern is not proof of safety, and claimed trend-reading is not validated here; this is a single-source repeated pair.

## Contradictions

Only Munkasey is represented. Ease about deception is not the same as a reduction in actual deception; the entry makes the former claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Asc = Neptune, the repeated Neptune specifies the pair through changing involvement with people and a change in concern about being deceived. The [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry) describes leaving people or situations when attitudes change, easing concern even around untrustworthy people, and an enhanced ability to read future trends (printed p. 338). The useful distinction is between feeling less concerned and being less exposed to deception. The passage does not establish the latter or verify the claimed ability to anticipate trends.
