## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `89` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To have to deal with visionary, deluded or mentally blind people. Large floods or water masses at a particular place cause uncertainties.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `119`, PDF p. `58` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disappointment about the partner's truth, understanding of a subtle environment, foreign cultural place, intuitions in a spiritualized environment, clear air in the surroundings, clear water at the location, mental sensitivity of the partner, and refinement of the surrounding culture

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: dealing with uncertain vision or understanding in others, alongside sensitivity and cultural refinement.
- psychology: Witte's deluded or mentally blind wording is historical; ABC includes intuition and mental sensitivity.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily diagnosis; flood and water-mass examples concern local conditions.
- social/relationship: dealing with visionary people and disappointment about a partner's truth are explicit.
- events/manifestations: local flooding, clear water or air, and a foreign cultural place are named settings.
- conflicts/notes: claimed intuition or understanding is not independently verified; flooding need not be read as a psychological metaphor.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes delusion and uncertainty, while ABC also names clear conditions and refined culture. These alternatives should remain distinct from an assumption of universal spiritual insight.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Asc = Poseidon, Poseidon specifies the pair through dealings with another person's vision, understanding, or claim to truth. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes visionary or deluded people; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointment about a partner's truth, intuition, mental sensitivity, and cultural refinement (printed p. 119, PDF p. 58). The local flood and clear-water examples concern the surroundings in their own right. Together the passages distinguish claimed understanding from disappointment in it, without proving spiritual insight or using environmental events as its cause.
