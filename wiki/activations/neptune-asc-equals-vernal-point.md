## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `89` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Being involved in fraud. To be deceived by the general public at the door or upon the open street.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `118`, PDF p. `58` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disappointed public environment, sensitive partner in public life, subtle public environment, public life of a foreign place, weak partner in public life, uncertain public environment, and water in the public environment

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: public involvement in fraud or uncertainty involving the surroundings.
- psychology: disappointment and uncertainty are explicit public-setting descriptions.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: public life and encounters at a door or in a street are the stated settings.
- events/manifestations: fraudulent involvement, public life in a foreign place, and water in public surroundings are examples.
- conflicts/notes: the public setting does not establish a generally deceptive atmosphere or implicate every encounter.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses fraud in public encounters. ABC also includes sensitive or weak partners and subtle surroundings, which are broader than fraudulent conduct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Asc = Vernal Point, Vernal Point specifies the pair through public encounters and the surroundings of public life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives involvement in fraud and deception at a door or in a street; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds an uncertain public environment, a sensitive or weak partner, and public life in a foreign place (printed p. 118, PDF p. 58). Water in the public surroundings is a separate setting. These examples locate uncertainty publicly without making every public contact deceptive.
