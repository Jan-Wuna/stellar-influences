## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `89` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Mighty deception and disappointment through others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `119`, PDF p. `58` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Energy weakness of the partner, powerful subtle environment, strong weakening of others, uncertainty through violence at the location, refined environmental energy, refined partner influence, force of water in the surroundings, and wind energy in the surroundings

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: forceful deception or disappointment through others, with power and weakening in ABC.
- psychology: disappointment is explicit, without a supplied motive or emotional mechanism.
- body/health: ABC's energy weakness and weakening are source descriptions without a specified medical condition.
- social/relationship: influence of a partner and weakening of others are stated interpersonal themes.
- events/manifestations: local violence, force of water, wind energy, and a powerful subtle environment are distinct examples.
- conflicts/notes: water force is a physical example; it does not establish interpersonal violence or an inevitable overwhelming impact.

## Contradictions

Witte centers strong deception and disappointment. ABC also names environmental forces and refined influence, so these should not all be made evidence of hidden motives.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Asc = Vulcanus, Vulcanus specifies the pair through forceful deception or disappointment involving others and through differences in influence or strength. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states the interpersonal disappointment; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a partner's energy weakness, strong weakening of others, and refined influence (printed p. 119, PDF p. 58). Local violence, water force, and wind energy are separate examples in the row. The passages support force and vulnerability as concerns without explaining them through hidden motives or predicting a particular emotional aftermath.
