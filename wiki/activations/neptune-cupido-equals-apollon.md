## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `247` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Breaking-up of many families. Dissolution of large associations. Housing shortage for many. The expansion of a secret society or secret agency.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `393`, PDF p. `195` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a successful community, shared knowledge of the future, metaphysical knowledge of a community, weak trading community, weakness of an economic community, uncertainty in a successful community, and a community pretending knowledge

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: dissolution or uncertainty across larger associations, with expansion of secret groups as a distinct possibility.
- psychology: claims of shared future or metaphysical knowledge coexist with pretended knowledge.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: families, large associations, trading or economic communities, and secret societies are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: housing shortage for many, association dissolution, and expansion of a secret agency are stated.
- conflicts/notes: weakness or uncertainty in a community does not by itself establish general social breakdown or pretence.

## Contradictions

Witte includes both dissolution of large associations and expansion of secret groups. ABC's knowledge claims and pretended knowledge likewise remain distinct, rather than one uniform process of collapse.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Cupido = Apollon, Apollon specifies the pair through the scope and condition of associations involving many people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes family breakups, large-association dissolution, housing shortage, and expansion of a secret society. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds weak trading or economic communities and uncertainty in a successful community, alongside shared knowledge claims and a community pretending knowledge (printed p. 393, PDF p. 195). The reading retains dissolution, weakness, and secret expansion as different possibilities, without making them a single inevitable social collapse.
