## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `247` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be unmarried. To enter into a precarious marital union. A partnership which soon again is dissolved. Unpleasant housing facilities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `392`, PDF p. `195` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a community at the location, sensitive partner-community, shared future with the partner, partner-community in the air, weak partner of a community, uncertain partner-community, and rejection of community with others

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: uncertain partnership or local community, with dissolution and unfavorable housing among the examples.
- psychology: ABC includes sensitivity and rejection of community without a stated motive.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: unmarried status, precarious union, and a shared future with a partner are distinct possibilities.
- events/manifestations: a quickly dissolved partnership, unpleasant housing, and local community dissolution are stated.
- conflicts/notes: unmarried status is a historical source example, not evidence of a defect or unhappy outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes precarious union and housing; ABC also describes sensitivity and a shared future. These should not all be collapsed into inevitable instability.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Cupido = Asc, Asc specifies the pair through a partner and the condition of local or shared surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes precarious or quickly dissolved unions and unpleasant housing; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds local community dissolution and rejection of community, while retaining a sensitive partner-community and shared future (printed p. 392, PDF p. 195). These examples concern the reliability or continuance of association, without treating unmarried status as a defect or predicting dissolution for every partnership.
