## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `247` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Disgusting conditions in poor marital state. Difficulties or trouble with or through a community or about housing matters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `393`, PDF p. `195` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a dangerous community, shared weaknesses from the past, shared past and future, deficiencies of an uncertain community, disadvantages for a weak community, and uncertainty through art-related crime (Kunst-Kriminalität)

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: troubled community or marital conditions, with deficiencies, disadvantages, and harmful collective circumstances.
- psychology: shared weaknesses from the past are stated without a theory of inherited emotional burdens.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: marriage, community, and housing troubles are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: dissolving a dangerous community and uncertainty through Kunst-Kriminalität are particular examples.
- conflicts/notes: Kunst-Kriminalität is retained as art-related crime; the source specifies no offense, actors, or current legal classification.

## Contradictions

Witte's adverse marital and housing conditions overlap with ABC's community deficiencies. ABC's dissolution concerns a dangerous community, so ending it is not necessarily another harmful outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Cupido = Hades, Hades specifies the pair through adverse conditions within marriage, community, or housing. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes troubled marital conditions and community or housing difficulties; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds shared past weaknesses, community deficiencies, and disadvantages for a weak group (printed p. 393, PDF p. 195). Its dissolution of a dangerous community remains distinct from those deficiencies. The art-related crime phrase is retained without inventing a particular offense, and the passages do not establish an emotional cause for the material difficulties.
