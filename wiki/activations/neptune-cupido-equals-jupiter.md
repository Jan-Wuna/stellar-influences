## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `247` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Extravagance, prodigality in an unhappy marriage. Worried marital luck through housing shortage.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `392`, PDF p. `195` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a financial community, successful sensitivity of a community, joy about a shared future, shared financial weakness, shared happiness in the air, legal uncertainty of a community, and weak financial community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: financial weakness or excess in union, alongside joy and successful sensitivity in a community.
- psychology: joy about a shared future is stated, without necessarily being misplaced optimism.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: unhappy marriage and financial community are the explicit relationship settings.
- events/manifestations: extravagance, housing shortage, financial-community dissolution, and legal uncertainty are examples.
- conflicts/notes: favorable shared feeling does not cancel explicit financial weakness or imply prosperity.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes extravagance and worried marital luck, whereas ABC includes both shared joy and financial weakness. The favorable feeling and adverse material conditions can remain distinct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Cupido = Jupiter, Jupiter specifies the pair through the financial condition and shared expectations of a union or community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) associates unhappy marriage with extravagance and housing shortage. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds financial-community dissolution, shared financial weakness, and legal uncertainty, while also naming successful sensitivity and joy about a shared future (printed p. 392, PDF p. 195). These distinguish favorable communal feeling from material security; joy is neither proof of prosperity nor necessarily an illusion.
