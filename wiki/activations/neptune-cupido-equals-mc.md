## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `247` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Grief through marriage. To be part of dissolution of a union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `392`, PDF p. `195` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of my community, sensitivity of a soul-community, I am disappointed by the community, I am uncertain in the community, my subtle community, my art in water, and my future in the community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: one's personal participation in a union or community that may disappoint or dissolve.
- psychology: grief, disappointment, and uncertainty are explicit alongside sensitivity.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: marriage and one's own community are the stated relationships.
- events/manifestations: participating in union dissolution, personal art in water, and a future in community are examples.
- conflicts/notes: the soul-community phrase remains source language without an invented psychological or spiritual mechanism.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes grief and dissolution. ABC also retains sensitivity, art, and a future within community; these are not necessarily stages of the same ending.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Cupido = MC, MC specifies the pair through one's own involvement in the condition of a union or community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes grief through marriage and participation in dissolution; [ABC](#abc-entry) explicitly states disappointment and uncertainty within one's community (printed p. 392, PDF p. 195). It also includes sensitivity, personal art in water, and one's future in community. The personal emphasis does not establish that grief must dissolve identity or that every form of belonging ends.
