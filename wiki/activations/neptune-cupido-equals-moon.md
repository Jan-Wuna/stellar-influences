## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `247` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Unfortunate (unhappy) wife. Unhappy populace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `392`, PDF p. `195` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Sensitivity in a community of women, community of women in the air, community of women in water, emotional weakness of a community, weakened people's community, weak women in a community, and uncertain people's community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: unhappiness or weakness in communal feeling, alongside sensitivity in a community of women.
- psychology: sensitivity and emotional weakness are distinct ABC themes.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health diagnosis; source weakness remains unspecified.
- social/relationship: Witte's wife and populace examples and ABC's communities of women are historical gendered settings.
- events/manifestations: a weakened or uncertain people's community and communities in water or air are stated.
- conflicts/notes: the gendered examples do not establish that all women or all communal life are unhappy.

## Contradictions

Witte gives unhappiness, while ABC also retains sensitivity and environmental settings. Sensitivity alone does not establish sorrow or communal failure.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Cupido = Moon, Moon specifies the pair through feelings and vulnerability within a community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes an unhappy wife or populace; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds sensitivity among women, emotional weakness, and a weakened or uncertain people's community (printed p. 392, PDF p. 195). Its air and water settings remain concrete source examples. The supported connection is between collective association and its felt condition, without extending the historical gendered examples into a claim that women or communal life are inherently unhappy.
