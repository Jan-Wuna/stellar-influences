## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `247` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To break off connections. To make connections which turn out to be unsatisfactory.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `392`, PDF p. `195` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a shared connection, disappointing art connections, connected together for the future, connected together in water, weak connections to the community, and uncertain connections to a community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: connections to a community that may be weak, disappointing, or discontinued.
- psychology: dissatisfaction is explicit, without a supplied explanation for when a connection ends.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: community ties and art connections are the stated forms of association.
- events/manifestations: breaking connections, shared future ties, and connections in water are examples.
- conflicts/notes: the entries do not say that weak promises create the bond or that recognition of instability necessarily causes separation.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes unsatisfactory or broken connections. ABC also retains common future ties and shared settings, so discontinuation is not the sole possibility.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Cupido = Node, Node specifies the pair through ties to a community and whether those connections continue satisfactorily. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes ending ties or forming unsatisfactory ones; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointing art connections and weak or uncertain community links (printed p. 392, PDF p. 195). Shared future connections and connections in water remain alongside dissolution. The combined reading concerns the condition of association, without inventing a sequence in which unstable promises inevitably become broken ties.
