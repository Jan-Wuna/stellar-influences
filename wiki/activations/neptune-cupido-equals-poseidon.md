## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `247` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Dissolution of a community that serves for entertainment or relaxation. Loss of a community that pursues cultural or political goals. Unsuccessful establishment of an cultural or political community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `393`, PDF p. `195` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a community of ideas, foreign cultural community, clarity in an art community, metaphysical insights of a community, synthesis art of the future, unknown truth in the community, and refinement of a spiritual community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: dissolution or unsuccessful establishment of cultural, political, recreational, or idea-based communities.
- psychology: claimed clarity, metaphysical insight, and unknown truth are ABC themes.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: recreational, artistic, cultural, political, and spiritual communities are distinct stated settings.
- events/manifestations: losing a community, unsuccessful group establishment, and refinement of a spiritual community are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Synthese-Kunst remains a literal synthesis-art phrase without a specified technique or future event.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes loss or unsuccessful establishment, while ABC also retains clarity and refinement. The latter are source claims and do not prove that a community succeeds or possesses truth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Cupido = Poseidon, Poseidon specifies the pair through communities gathered around recreation, culture, politics, or ideas. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes dissolution, loss, or unsuccessful establishment; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds dissolution of an ideas community alongside clarity in art and refinement of a spiritual community (printed p. 393, PDF p. 195). These distinguish the condition of an association from its claimed insight. Neither unclear ideals as a cause of failure nor the truth of a group's metaphysical claims is established.
