## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `247` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

A sickness beclouds a marriage or organization. Separation of a mismarriage. Dissolution of associations. Disagreeableness, unpleasantness and misunderstandings in a community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `393`, PDF p. `195` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Serious community with sensitivity, serious art of the future, disturbed community in water, gradual separation of a community, disturbance of a weak community, disturbances and uncertainty in a community, and losses through dissolution of a community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: separation, disturbance, and misunderstanding in unions or communities.
- psychology: seriousness and sensitivity are ABC examples beside communal uncertainty.
- body/health: Witte's sickness affecting marriage or organization is an attributed historical claim, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: marriage, organizations, and weak communities are the stated settings.
- events/manifestations: gradual separation, losses through dissolution, and disturbed community in water are examples.
- conflicts/notes: the entries do not state that associations inevitably decay or cannot recover.

## Contradictions

Separation and disturbance recur, but ABC's serious sensitivity and future art do not themselves imply decline. Duration and ultimate recovery are not established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Cupido = Saturn, Saturn specifies the pair through separation and disturbance in a union or community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes sickness, misunderstanding, and association dissolution; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds gradual separation, uncertainty, and losses through dissolution (printed p. 393, PDF p. 195). Serious sensitivity and art of the future are retained as distinct examples. The passages support difficult collective conditions without establishing that every association decays permanently or that sickness is their necessary cause.
