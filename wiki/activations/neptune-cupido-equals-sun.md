## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `247` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Unfortunate (unhappy) husband. A day with dissonances in associations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `392`, PDF p. `195` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a community of people, sensitivity of a community of people, shared future with a man, art center by the water, men's community by or in water, community of people in the air, and weak community of people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal unhappiness or discord in association, alongside communal sensitivity and a shared future.
- psychology: unhappiness and sensitivity are explicit, without a permanent personality description.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: Witte's husband and ABC's communities of people or men are historical settings.
- events/manifestations: association dissonance, community dissolution, and an art center by water are stated.
- conflicts/notes: the gendered examples do not make unhappiness inherent in men or every association.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes an unhappy husband and daily discord; ABC also includes sensitivity, art, and a shared future. These do not all indicate dissolution.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Cupido = Sun, Sun specifies the pair through a person's participation in association and its discord or sensitivity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names an unhappy husband and a day of association dissonance; [ABC](#abc-entry) places community dissolution and weakness beside sensitivity, a shared future, and an art center by water (printed p. 392, PDF p. 195). These preserve both adverse and other communal examples, without making unhappiness central to every person's identity or every group encounter.
