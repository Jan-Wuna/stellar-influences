## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `247` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Sudden rupture in married life. Sudden grief in married life through death or accident. Sudden dissolution of a community under revolutionary aspects. Sudden events in a secret organization.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `393`, PDF p. `195` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a technical community, disappointing peace event, shared rhythms in water, shared tensions in the air, nervous weakness of a community, and refined rhythms of a community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sudden disruption of unions or groups, alongside collective tension and changing rhythms.
- psychology: nervous weakness and disappointment are ABC themes without a diagnosis.
- body/health: Witte's death or accident examples are historical possibilities, not forecasts of a particular event.
- social/relationship: marriage, secret organizations, and technical communities are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: sudden rupture, revolutionary community dissolution, and a disappointing peace event are stated.
- conflicts/notes: shared or refined rhythms do not necessarily imply abrupt dissolution; the severe examples are not universal.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sudden events, while ABC includes community rhythms and tensions without specifying suddenness in every clause. Those examples should not all become predictions of rupture.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Cupido = Uranus, Uranus specifies the pair through abrupt developments in a union or collective setting. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes sudden marital rupture and events in secret or revolutionary groups; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds technical-community dissolution, a disappointing peace event, and shared tensions (printed p. 393, PDF p. 195). Shared and refined rhythms remain distinct possibilities. Witte's death or accident examples retain historical attribution and do not make severe loss the necessary outcome of every group change.
