## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `247` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Many children in poor family. The “black sheep” of the family. Lack of shelter due to fire. Military camps, barracks, shelters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `393`, PDF p. `195` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Effort of a community in water, dissolution of a leadership-community, shared future efforts, leadership-community of the future, creative art of the future, performance-community in the air, and weak leadership-community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: communal effort and leadership, with shelter and difficult family circumstances among Witte's examples.
- psychology: the black-sheep phrase is a historical family description, without a specified scapegoating process.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: a poor family, military camps, and leadership-communities are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: loss of shelter through fire, barracks, future collective effort, and creative art are stated.
- conflicts/notes: the entries do not establish frequent displacement or say that every camp is an emergency shelter.

## Contradictions

Witte's family and shelter examples differ from ABC's leadership and future effort. They are not presented as a causal sequence from unstable belonging to emergency organization.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Cupido = Zeus, Zeus specifies the pair through collective effort, leadership, and particular conditions of family or shelter. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives a poor family, its black sheep, fire-related loss of shelter, camps, and barracks. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds dissolved or weak leadership-community beside shared future efforts and creative art (printed p. 393, PDF p. 195). These remain separate examples within the orientation, without assuming frequent displacement, scapegoating, or a necessary progression from family strain to emergency shelter.
