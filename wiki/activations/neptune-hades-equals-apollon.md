## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `248` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Hopes for the future, which are never realized. Many are under the influence of alcohol. Chemical science. Evil fumes and vapors. Quantities of liquids. Water system, creeks, rivers, streams, oceans.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `395`, PDF p. `196` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of air pollution, experiences with weaknesses of the past, dangerous masses of water, mysterious knowledge of the future, extensive water damage, unknown hidden knowledge, much dirt in water, and increasing air pollution

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: large quantities or wider environmental conditions, alongside unrealized hopes and hidden knowledge claims.
- psychology: hopes never realized and mysterious knowledge of the future are distinct source themes.
- body/health: alcohol influence and harmful fumes are historical Witte examples without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Witte's many people under alcohol's influence is the explicit collective setting.
- events/manifestations: chemical science, water systems, rivers or oceans, pollution, and extensive water damage are named.
- conflicts/notes: chemical science and water systems are not intrinsically pathological or evidence of concealed business trouble.

## Contradictions

Witte includes neutral scientific and water-system examples alongside adverse hopes and fumes. ABC emphasizes pollution and damage, without turning all extensive systems into harmful networks.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Hades = Apollon, Apollon specifies the pair through quantity and extent in water, air, and experience. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives unrealized hopes, chemical science, fumes, and large water systems; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds spreading pollution, dangerous water masses, and extensive damage (printed p. 395, PDF p. 196). Hidden or future knowledge remains a source claim. The shared scale does not make every scientific or environmental system pathological, nor does it establish hidden business trouble.
