## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `248` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Obsolete form of government. To experience evil through or with ruling persons. Death and ruin in ruling circles or in the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `395`, PDF p. `196` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disadvantage through weak self-confidence, particular air pollution, great lack of air, great water damage, weaknesses of a state from the past, and unknown hidden hierarchy

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: adverse conditions involving government, rulers, or hierarchy, with past state weaknesses in ABC.
- psychology: disadvantage through weak self-confidence is ABC's specific psychological claim.
- body/health: Witte's death in ruling circles is a historical event example, not a health assessment.
- social/relationship: rulers, the state, and an unknown hierarchy are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: obsolete government, ruin in the state, major water damage, and air pollution are named.
- conflicts/notes: the entries do not establish corruption as the cause of every adverse government condition.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses ruling circles and obsolete government. ABC also includes self-confidence and environmental damage, without placing all of those examples in the same political event.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Hades = Kronos, Kronos specifies the pair through authority, hierarchy, and adverse conditions at the level of rulers or the state. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives obsolete government and harm involving rulers; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds past state weaknesses and an unknown hierarchy, alongside self-confidence and environmental examples (printed p. 395, PDF p. 196). These support a concern with weakness or harm in authority without establishing corruption as its cause. Witte's death and ruin examples remain particular historical claims.
