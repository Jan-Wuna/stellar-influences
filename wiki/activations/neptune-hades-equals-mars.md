## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `248` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To make efforts in vain, useless work. Naval wars. War vessels. Destruction and death by water.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `394`, PDF p. `196` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disappointing lack of work, dangerous work in water, working on weaknesses of the past, deception through criminal acts, dealing with unknown and hidden matters, uncertainty through secret work, and working on water damage

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: futile or uncertain effort, with water work, concealment, or conflict among the settings.
- psychology: disappointment and uncertainty about secret work are explicit.
- body/health: Witte's destruction and death by water are historical claims, not forecasts.
- social/relationship: naval conflict and criminal acts are particular source settings rather than universal conduct.
- events/manifestations: lack of work, dangerous water work, addressing past weaknesses, and working on damage are named.
- conflicts/notes: the criminal-deception example does not make all hidden or remedial work criminal or useless.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes useless effort and naval war. ABC also includes addressing weaknesses or water damage, so remedial action should not automatically be classed as futile destruction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Hades = Mars, Mars specifies the pair through effort that may be futile, dangerous, or uncertain. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes useless work and naval conflict; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointing lack of work, dangerous water work, and uncertainty through secret activity (printed p. 394, PDF p. 196). It also retains working on past weaknesses or water damage as distinct examples. Neither remedial work nor dealing with hidden matters is necessarily criminal or useless; the severe destruction examples remain historical source claims.
