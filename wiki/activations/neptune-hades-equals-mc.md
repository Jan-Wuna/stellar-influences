## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `248` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To commit oneself to great danger. Subjected to grave deceptions. To drown. Seafarer. Suffocation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `394`, PDF p. `196` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Troubled weak soul, danger for me in water, I have water damage, my lack of air, my dirty water, my dangerous poisoning, my weaknesses from the past, minute of danger in water, and creeping distress of the soul

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal exposure to deception, danger, or distress, with water and air among the explicit settings.
- psychology: a troubled soul and gradually developing distress are source language, without an identity defined by decline.
- body/health: drowning, suffocation, and poisoning are attributed historical examples, not diagnoses or individual predictions.
- social/relationship: no specific relationship or group setting is supplied.
- events/manifestations: seafaring, personal water damage, and past weaknesses are explicit examples.
- conflicts/notes: the minute-of-danger wording is retained as a source phrase without calculating a timing layer.

## Contradictions

Witte combines danger and seafaring; ABC adds first-person distress and material conditions. The entry does not imply that every seafarer experiences the severe outcomes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Hades = MC, MC specifies the pair through personally experienced danger, deception, or distress. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names grave deception, seafaring, drowning, and suffocation; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a troubled soul, personal water damage, lack of air, and past weaknesses (printed p. 394, PDF p. 196). These remain distinct historical and first-person examples, without making danger a permanent identity or predicting it for a seafarer. The minute-of-danger phrase supplies no calculated timing or necessary event.
