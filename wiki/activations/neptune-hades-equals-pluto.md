## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `248` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Mistakes that are detected too late. The beginning of decay or rotting.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `395`, PDF p. `196` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Development of sorrow through lack of clarity, development of sensitivity through renunciation, developing sensitivity through privations, dangerous change in water, developing the future from the past, and changes through water shortage

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: late recognition of mistakes or beginning decay, alongside development through difficulty.
- psychology: sorrow through unclear circumstances and sensitivity through renunciation or privation are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health diagnosis.
- social/relationship: no particular relationship or group is specified.
- events/manifestations: beginning decay, dangerous changes in water, and change through water shortage are named.
- conflicts/notes: the source does not establish irreversible transformation or guarantee that deprivation improves sensitivity.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes mistakes detected too late and decay; ABC also includes developing sensitivity and a future from the past. These do not all imply an irreversible adverse outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Hades = Pluto, Pluto specifies the pair through development that can involve late recognition, sorrow, or difficult conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names mistakes detected too late and the beginning of decay; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds sorrow through lack of clarity and sensitivity developed through renunciation or privations (printed p. 395, PDF p. 196). It also includes changes in water and through shortage. These retain both deterioration and other developmental possibilities without claiming that every change is irreversible or that hardship necessarily produces insight.
