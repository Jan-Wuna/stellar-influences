## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `248` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Mistakes through lack of mental lucidity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `395`, PDF p. `196` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disadvantage through mental weakness, disappointing lack of reason, mental air pollution, lack of clear water, weakness through lack of truth, unknown culture from the past, and pollution of clear water

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: mistakes associated with lack of mental clarity, alongside deficient truth or clear-water conditions in ABC.
- psychology: lack of reason and disadvantage through mental weakness are attributed source descriptions.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a specific bodily diagnosis.
- social/relationship: an unknown culture from the past is ABC's cultural example; no particular relationship is specified.
- events/manifestations: mistakes, lack of clear water, and pollution of clear water are stated.
- conflicts/notes: mental air pollution is literal source language without a defined mechanism; no general inability to distinguish reality is established.

## Contradictions

Witte's lack of lucidity overlaps with ABC's lack of reason. ABC also gives literal water conditions and culture, without making them effects of mental confusion.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Hades = Poseidon, Poseidon specifies the pair through the problem of clarity and the mistakes associated with its lack. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states that relation directly; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointing lack of reason and weakness through lack of truth (printed p. 395, PDF p. 196). Its clear-water shortage, pollution, and past-culture examples remain distinct. The passage supports a qualified concern with judgment, without establishing a permanent inability to distinguish reality or a psychological cause for water conditions.
