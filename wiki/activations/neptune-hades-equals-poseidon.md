## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Hades = Poseidon`: Mistakes through lack of mental lucidity.
- Practical focus: spiritualizes confusion in the negative sense, showing mistakes born from lack of lucidity, poor discrimination, or a mind unable to keep reality and distortion clearly apart.
- Practical expression: disadvantaged by spiritual weak, exchangeable lack of Y rational, mental airpollution, lack of clear water, and weak by truth deficiency.
