## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `248` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Serious sickness. To experience hindrances through deceptions, mistakes or other vile circumstances. Losses through inundations or water. To overturn or fall into the water. Shipwreck. To give up seafaring. Evil smelling liquids, dung water, excrements, natural manure.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `395`, PDF p. `196` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Concentration on hidden and subtle matters, sorrow through creeping disturbances, adverse problems with water, lack of patience and sensitivity, problems through air pollution, disturbances through lack of air, and unknown hidden problems

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: hindrance, loss, or difficulty with hidden and material conditions.
- psychology: concentration, sorrow, and lack of patience or sensitivity are explicit.
- body/health: Witte's serious sickness is historical doctrine, not a diagnosis; accident examples are not individual forecasts.
- social/relationship: no particular relationship or group is specified.
- events/manifestations: shipwreck, abandoning seafaring, water losses, pollution, and manure are distinct source examples.
- conflicts/notes: age, duty, dependency, and a demand for endurance are not stated by these entries.

## Contradictions

Witte's severe losses and ordinary material examples differ from ABC's concentration and hidden problems. The source does not make all foul liquids or hidden matters evidence of illness.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Hades = Saturn, Saturn specifies the pair through hindrance, loss, or persistent difficulty in unclear or material circumstances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives hindrance through errors and deception, water losses, shipwreck, and giving up seafaring; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds creeping disturbances, sorrow, and lack of patience or sensitivity (printed p. 395, PDF p. 196). Foul liquids and manure remain concrete examples. The severe sickness and accident claims retain historical attribution, without inferring age, duty, dependency, or prescribed endurance.
