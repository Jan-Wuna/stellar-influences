## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `248` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Murder in connection with revolutionary activities. Death and ruin through water catastrophes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `395`, PDF p. `196` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Alarming lack of air, air pollution through technology, nervousness through dangerous liquid, new danger in water, sudden water shortage, unrest through weaknesses of the past, and surprising water damage

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sudden or alarming danger and environmental disruption.
- psychology: nervousness and unrest through past weaknesses are explicit.
- body/health: Witte's death and violence examples are historical event claims, not health or individual risk assessments.
- social/relationship: revolutionary activities are Witte's specific collective setting.
- events/manifestations: technology-related air pollution, sudden water shortage, and surprising water damage are named.
- conflicts/notes: the source does not identify concealed decay as the cause of the sudden events.

## Contradictions

Witte gives severe revolutionary and water catastrophes; ABC includes varied environmental disturbances. The severe examples should not be generalized to every sudden shortage or upset.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Hades = Uranus, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden or alarming disturbances involving water, air, or collective circumstances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives revolutionary violence and water catastrophe; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds technology-related pollution, dangerous liquid, sudden water shortage, and surprising damage (printed p. 395, PDF p. 196). Nervousness and unrest are stated responses. These historical examples do not establish hidden decay as their cause or make every unexpected environmental problem a forecast of ruin.
