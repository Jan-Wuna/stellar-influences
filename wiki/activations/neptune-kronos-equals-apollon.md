## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `250` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Great failure of management. Many feel themselves deceived by their leadership.

Source-mark note: The scan has a tiny unidentified printed mark after the final period. It is not transcribed as legible characters or used in synthesis.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `399`, PDF p. `198` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of air supremacy, special experiences in metaphysics, success through great intuition, successful direction of the future, great uncertainty in science, large quantities of water, and importance of metaphysical knowledge

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: large-scale management failure or perceived deception, alongside successful guidance and intuitive claims.
- psychology: feeling deceived differs from demonstrated deception; ABC also states success through intuition.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: many people and their leadership are Witte's explicit collective setting.
- events/manifestations: failed management, expanded air supremacy, scientific uncertainty, and large quantities of water are named.
- conflicts/notes: the tiny unidentified Witte mark is recorded in the source note without a guessed character transcription; claimed intuition is not independently verified.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes failed leadership, whereas ABC also includes successful future direction. These are alternatives, not evidence that every successful guidance claim conceals managerial failure.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Kronos = Apollon, Apollon specifies the pair through broad consequences of leadership or guidance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes great management failure and many feeling deceived; [ABC](#abc-entry) also includes successful direction of the future and success through intuition, alongside scientific uncertainty and expanded air supremacy (printed p. 399, PDF p. 198). The favorable claims do not establish accurate foresight, and the feeling of deception is not itself proof of fraud. Large quantities of water remain a separate example.
