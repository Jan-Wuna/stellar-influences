## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `250` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be involved in the dissolution of an establishment. In touch with unpleasantness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `398`, PDF p. `198` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Others pretend superiority, special expectations of the surroundings, particularly sensitive partner, great sensitivity to the environment, great disappointment through the partner, direction of the future for a place, and unknown hierarchy at the location

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: involvement with an establishment's dissolution, alongside expectations and disappointment involving a partner or place.
- psychology: sensitivity to surroundings and great disappointment are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: a sensitive partner, pretended superiority, and local hierarchy are stated settings.
- events/manifestations: institutional dissolution, unpleasant contact, and direction of a place's future are named.
- conflicts/notes: the entries do not make every formal institution a failing establishment.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes involvement in dissolution; ABC also retains sensitivity and future direction. Pretended superiority and sensitivity are distinct examples rather than a single deceptive partner profile.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Kronos = Asc, Asc specifies the pair through a partner, immediate surroundings, or an establishment with which one is involved. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names dissolution and unpleasant contact; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds pretended superiority, sensitivity to surroundings, and disappointment through a partner (printed p. 398, PDF p. 198). Its local hierarchy and direction of a place's future are separate examples. The reading concerns those contacts and expectations without assuming that every institution is failing or every sensitive partner is deceptive.
