## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `250` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Marriages that are clouded through bad laws (poor laws). Guidance of a secret community. To give up the management of an association or corporation. Hidden or secret abilities, kept secret underground movement. Art, realized secretly.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `399`, PDF p. `198` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a large community, special expectations of a community, special sensitivity of a community, shared air superiority, shared direction of the future, independent community in the air, and unknown hierarchical community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: community management and concealed or official conditions affecting association.
- psychology: collective expectations and sensitivity are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: marriage under poor laws, secret communities, associations, and corporations are Witte's settings.
- events/manifestations: giving up management, secret art or abilities, large-community dissolution, and shared future direction are named.
- conflicts/notes: secrecy is a setting and does not establish bad law or deception in every communal activity.

## Contradictions

Witte includes both adverse law and hidden abilities or art; ABC adds sensitivity and independent community. These should not all be reduced to compromised authority.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Kronos = Cupido, Cupido specifies the pair through association, management, and the conditions under which communal activity is directed. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes marriages affected by poor laws, secret-community guidance, relinquished management, and concealed art or abilities. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds large-community dissolution, collective sensitivity, and shared future direction (printed p. 399, PDF p. 198). These retain adverse conditions and independent or hidden collective activity as distinct possibilities; secrecy alone does not establish deception or bad governance.
