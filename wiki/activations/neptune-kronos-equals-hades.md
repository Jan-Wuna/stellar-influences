## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `250` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

A firm or a leader ruined by misfortune. Catastrophe in the government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `399`, PDF p. `198` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Special expectations from the past, special danger in water, great privations for the future, great unknown danger, great water shortage, disadvantages through a weak state, weak state servant, and concealed direction of the future

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: misfortune involving a firm, leader, or state, with danger and scarcity in ABC.
- psychology: expectations from the past are stated without a cause in deception or failed control.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health diagnosis.
- social/relationship: a firm, leader, government, weak state, and state servant are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: ruin by misfortune, water danger or shortage, and concealed future direction are named.
- conflicts/notes: the source does not establish that ruin is preceded by deception or that concealed direction offers relief.

## Contradictions

Witte gives catastrophic misfortune; ABC includes expectation and concealed future direction alongside weakness and scarcity. These do not prove a single sequence of political decline.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Kronos = Hades, Hades specifies the pair through misfortune or weakness affecting leadership and institutions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a firm or leader ruined by misfortune and government catastrophe; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a weak state or servant, privations, water danger, and concealed direction of the future (printed p. 399, PDF p. 198). Expectations from the past remain part of the row. The passages do not identify prior deception as the cause of ruin or promise relief through the concealed direction.
