## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `250` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To earn money easily and to spend it again. Great extravagance. Shrinking of property or holdings. Inflation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `398`, PDF p. `198` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Special financial expectations, success through special sensitivity, successful air supremacy, financial weakness of the state, great legal uncertainty, good direction of the future, weaknesses of the financial hierarchy, and advantageous subtle guidance

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: financial expectations and spending or shrinkage, alongside successful sensitivity and guidance.
- psychology: financial expectations and great legal uncertainty are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: state finances and a financial hierarchy are ABC's stated institutional settings.
- events/manifestations: easy earnings and renewed spending, extravagance, shrinking holdings, and inflation are named.
- conflicts/notes: the source does not specify rapid loss or poor authority as the cause of every financial change.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes spending and shrinking assets; ABC includes favorable guidance and sensitivity beside state financial weakness. Favorable direction does not cancel material uncertainty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Kronos = Jupiter, Jupiter specifies the pair through financial expectations and the management of resources. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives easy earnings followed by spending, extravagance, shrinking holdings, and inflation. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds state financial weakness and legal uncertainty, but also success through sensitivity and advantageous guidance (printed p. 398, PDF p. 198). These retain favorable direction and adverse financial conditions as separate possibilities, without imposing rapid loss or incompetent authority as the explanation for every change.
