## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `250` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Great deeds without success. Fruitless attacks. Accidents in aeronautics. Breakage.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `398`, PDF p. `198` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Expecting special actions, particularly sensitive action, great disappointment at work, actions to direct the future, establishment of air supremacy, state work by/in/with water, activity of the air authority, and important work in the air

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: action or effort that can disappoint or fail, alongside sensitive and official work.
- psychology: expectations of special actions and disappointment at work are explicit.
- body/health: Witte's aeronautic accidents are historical examples, not predictions of an individual injury.
- social/relationship: state work and the air authority are ABC's specific institutional settings.
- events/manifestations: fruitless attacks, breakage, air supremacy, and work involving water or air are named.
- conflicts/notes: the source does not make every substantial effort or technical activity end in accident.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes unsuccessful action, while ABC includes sensitive action and important official work. The latter are not stated to be futile.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Kronos = Mars, Mars specifies the pair through the execution and results of significant activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives unsuccessful deeds, fruitless attacks, and breakage; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointment at work beside sensitive action and official water or air activity (printed p. 398, PDF p. 198). Establishing air supremacy and work by the air authority are concrete examples. The historical accident claim does not make every technical effort a failure or identify compromised authority as its cause.
