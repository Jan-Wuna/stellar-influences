## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `250` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Great accomplishment through sensitiveness. Persons who try to deceive, who are not sincere in their opinions. Aviation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `398`, PDF p. `198` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Special expectations of a woman, special sensitivity of a woman, great disappointment of a woman, great emotional weakness, large people by the water, weak state of a people, uncertain guidance of feelings, and uncertain leadership of a people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: accomplishment through sensitivity alongside disappointment, insincerity, or uncertain guidance.
- psychology: sensitivity, disappointment, and emotional weakness are explicit alternatives.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Witte's insincere people and ABC's woman, people, and weak state are specific settings.
- events/manifestations: aviation, accomplishment, uncertain leadership, and a large people by water are named.
- conflicts/notes: sensitivity does not prove sincerity, and neither gender nor aviation establishes illusion.

## Contradictions

Witte explicitly includes both accomplishment and attempted deception. ABC adds emotional and collective uncertainty without making all sensitive accomplishment deceptive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Kronos = Moon, Moon specifies the pair through sensitivity, expectations, and the condition of feelings or collective guidance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places great accomplishment through sensitivity beside insincerity; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a woman's disappointment, emotional weakness, and uncertain leadership of a people (printed p. 398, PDF p. 198). Aviation and a people by water remain separate examples. These distinguish accomplishment from deceptive conduct without inferring unstable high leadership or illusion from sensitivity alone.
