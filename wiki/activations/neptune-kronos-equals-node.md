## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `250` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Secret machinations. Compelled to have dealings with insincere people. To experience maneuvers of deception. Connection with inefficient management or managers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `398`, PDF p. `198` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Special expectations in a connection, great disappointment in a connection, conveying great sensitivity, weak connections of a state, connection to the water authority, important connection for the future, and direction of the future in a connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: dealings involving managerial inefficiency or deception, alongside sensitivity and future-oriented connections.
- psychology: expectations and disappointment in a tie are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: insincere contacts, managers, state connections, and the water authority are stated settings.
- events/manifestations: secret maneuvers, conveying sensitivity, and an important future connection are named.
- conflicts/notes: not every official or future-oriented connection is described as a deceptive alliance.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes insincere or inefficient dealings. ABC also retains sensitivity and an important future tie, without specifying deceptive conduct in all connections.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Kronos = Node, Node specifies the pair through dealings whose sincerity or effectiveness may be uncertain. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names secret machinations, insincere people, and inefficient managers; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds expectations and disappointment in a tie, but also conveying sensitivity and a connection important for the future (printed p. 398, PDF p. 198). State and water-authority connections are concrete examples. These retain adverse dealings and useful possibilities without making every official connection a covert alliance.
