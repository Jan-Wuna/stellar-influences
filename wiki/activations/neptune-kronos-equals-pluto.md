## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `250` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To develop political and diplomatic abilities. To lead something from the background. To detect and publicize incompetent leadership.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `399`, PDF p. `198` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Expecting special development, agitation over a weak state, evolutionary impulses of the hierarchy, developing great sensitivity, impulses for shaping the future, transformation of an unknown energy, growing foreign authority, and increasing great disappointment

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: development of political or diplomatic ability, background direction, and exposure of incompetent leadership.
- psychology: expectations, agitation about a weak state, developing sensitivity, and disappointment are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: leadership, state, hierarchy, and foreign authority are stated settings.
- events/manifestations: publicizing incompetence, shaping the future, and growing authority are named.
- conflicts/notes: unknown energy is literal source wording without a specified mechanism or measurable process.

## Contradictions

Witte includes useful diplomatic development and exposure of incompetence. ABC adds both sensitivity development and increasing disappointment, so change is neither uniformly empowering nor uniformly adverse.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Kronos = Pluto, Pluto specifies the pair through development and changing influence in leadership or hierarchy. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes diplomatic ability, direction from the background, and exposing incompetent leadership; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds developing sensitivity, agitation over a weak state, foreign authority, and increasing disappointment (printed p. 399, PDF p. 198). These retain constructive political development and adverse expectations as different possibilities. The unknown-energy phrase does not supply a mechanism for behind-the-scenes influence.
