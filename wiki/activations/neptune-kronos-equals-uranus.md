## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `250` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Sudden intervention of a governmental power without success. A revolution from above. A revolution supported by the government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `399`, PDF p. `198` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dynamics of an unknown hierarchy, disappointing reforms of the state, development of special technology, agitation over state weaknesses, new great disappointments, uncertainty through a new boss, sudden superiority in water, and important subtle impulses

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sudden governmental action or reform, with disappointment and uncertainty in ABC.
- psychology: agitation over state weaknesses and new disappointment are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: government, a new boss, and an unknown hierarchy are stated settings.
- events/manifestations: unsuccessful intervention, revolution from above, state-supported revolution, and technological development are named.
- conflicts/notes: government-supported change is not necessarily described as destabilizing the government's own order.

## Contradictions

Witte states unsuccessful intervention but separately names revolution from above. ABC includes disappointing reforms and technical development, without assigning the same outcome to every change.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Kronos = Uranus, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden government action, reform, and changing hierarchy. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes an unsuccessful intervention as well as revolution from above or supported by government; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointing reforms, agitation over state weaknesses, and uncertainty through a new boss (printed p. 399, PDF p. 198). Technical development and sudden superiority in water remain separate examples. The entries do not establish that every intervention destabilizes its own government or that every innovation disappoints.
