## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `250` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Wishes and desires in matters of love which cannot be fulfilled or accomplished. Great through sacrificing love. The abbess.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `398`, PDF p. `198` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Special expectations of peace, special sensitivity in love, peaceful direction of the future, great disappointment in love, weak self-confidence in love, unknown peace hierarchy, and uncertainty of a peaceful state

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: unfulfilled love wishes and sacrifice, alongside sensitivity and expectations of peace.
- psychology: disappointment and weak self-confidence in love are ABC themes.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: love, the abbess, a peaceful state, and an unknown peace hierarchy are specific settings.
- events/manifestations: unfulfilled desires, sacrificing love, and peaceful future direction are stated.
- conflicts/notes: the source does not prescribe sacrifice as a route to elevation or promise fulfillment after renunciation.

## Contradictions

Witte's unfulfilled wishes and sacrificing love differ from ABC's sensitive love and peace expectations. The latter do not guarantee fulfillment or reconciliation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Kronos = Venus, Venus specifies the pair through wishes in love, disappointment, and sensitive or peaceful direction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives unfulfilled desires and greatness through sacrificing love, with the abbess as a particular example. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds sensitivity, great disappointment, weak self-confidence, and expectations of peace (printed p. 398, PDF p. 198). These retain sacrifice and peaceful aspiration as source possibilities without prescribing renunciation, promising elevation, or treating every unfulfilled wish as a necessary spiritual path.
