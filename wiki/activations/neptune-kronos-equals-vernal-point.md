## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `250` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The general public lives under the wrong impression about the administration. Incompetence in ruling circles.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `398`, PDF p. `198` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Special expectations in public, great sensitivity for public life, great disappointments for the public, weak authorities in public, unknown hierarchy in public life, and direction of the future for the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: public impressions and expectations concerning administration or authority.
- psychology: wrong impressions, sensitivity, and public disappointment are explicit.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: the public, administration, ruling circles, and public authorities are stated settings.
- events/manifestations: incompetence in ruling circles, unknown public hierarchy, and future direction for the public are named.
- conflicts/notes: Witte does not say the public correctly senses the incompetence it describes.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes mistaken public impressions and incompetence. ABC also retains sensitivity and future direction, without establishing that the public accurately recognizes managerial failure.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Kronos = Vernal Point, Vernal Point specifies the pair through public expectations and impressions of authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a wrong impression about administration and incompetence in ruling circles; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds public disappointment, weak authorities, and an unknown public hierarchy (printed p. 398, PDF p. 198). Sensitivity to public life and future direction remain alongside them. The passages distinguish actual or stated incompetence from what the public perceives, without assuming that public feeling correctly identifies it.
