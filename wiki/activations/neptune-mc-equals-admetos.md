## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be repulsive. To radiate a cold nature. Situations that are injurious to ones own tactful nature. To experience changes of conditions from the bottom up. Experiencing a thorough change of circumstances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `81`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of my blockade, consolidation of my uncertainty, my thorough intuitive sense, my endurance in water, my circulatory weakness, my deepening in metaphysics, and suppression of my premonitions

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: thorough change of circumstances, with personal blockage, uncertainty, and sensitivity in ABC.
- psychology: uncertainty may be consolidated; intuitive sense and suppressed premonitions are also explicit.
- body/health: ABC's circulatory weakness is a historical source claim, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Witte describes cold or repulsive presentation and circumstances injuring one's tact; these are source judgments, not a general personality verdict.
- events/manifestations: blockade dissolution, endurance in water, and bottom-up changes of conditions are named.
- conflicts/notes: release of a blockade does not guarantee relief from uncertainty, which ABC also describes as consolidated.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes coldness and thorough circumstantial change; ABC gives both release and consolidation of uncertainty. Neither requires a single progression toward greater warmth or stability.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Admetos, Admetos specifies the pair through thorough changes of condition and one's experience of blockage or uncertainty. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes changes from the bottom up and difficulty with tactful presentation; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds blockade dissolution, consolidated uncertainty, intuitive sense, and suppressed premonitions (printed p. 81, PDF p. 39). These retain release and restriction as different possibilities. Witte's adverse judgments about coldness are not a universal personality description, and circulatory weakness remains an attributed historical claim.
