## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Extensive deceptions and frauds.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `81`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

I have success in the air, my success in metaphysics, my metaphysical knowledge, my uncertain success, my knowledge of the future, my successful intuitions, my freedom in water, my many disappointments, and increasing sensitivity of the soul

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: extensive deception or fraud alongside personal claims of success, intuition, and disappointment.
- psychology: many disappointments and increasing sensitivity are ABC themes.
- body/health: neither entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: fraud is Witte's explicit conduct setting; no particular relationship is specified.
- events/manifestations: success in air or metaphysics, uncertain success, and freedom in water are named.
- conflicts/notes: the source does not verify future knowledge or equate every claimed success with fraud.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes extensive fraud. ABC also includes successful intuition and metaphysical work beside uncertain success and disappointment, without establishing that all of those activities are deceptive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Apollon, Apollon specifies the pair through the extent and claimed results of personal activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names extensive deception and fraud; [ABC](#abc-entry) places success in air or metaphysics beside uncertain success, many disappointments, and increasing sensitivity (printed p. 81, PDF p. 39). Claims of future knowledge and successful intuition remain attributed. The passages distinguish claimed accomplishment from deception without proving either fraud in every success or reliable foresight in every intuitive claim.
