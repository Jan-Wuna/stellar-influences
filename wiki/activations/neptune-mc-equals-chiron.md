### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `114` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `113`).

#### Sandbach Entry

Your energy naturally and spontaneously helps others to be more intuitive about what they need for their own spiritual development, and to perceive at subtler levels their spiritual place in the world.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: helping others intuit what supports their spiritual development and sense of place.
- psychology: subtler self-understanding and intuition are the specific intended changes.
- body/health: the entry supplies no bodily condition, healing claim, or treatment.
- social/relationship: one person's influence on others' spiritual self-understanding is the stated helping relationship.
- events/manifestations: spontaneous help with intuitive orientation is described without a concrete event prediction.
- conflicts/notes: this is Sandbach's single-source spiritual interpretation, without independent confirmation of an energy mechanism.

## Contradictions

Only Sandbach is represented. The passage describes spiritual orientation, without a second account to compare or a medical healing claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Chiron, Chiron specifies the pair through helping others become more intuitive about their spiritual development and place in the world. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) describes this help as spontaneous and directed toward subtler perception (PDF p. 114). The supported emphasis is another person's understanding of spiritual direction; the passage does not establish a medical healing effect, a porous identity, or a defined mechanism by which the stated energy operates.
