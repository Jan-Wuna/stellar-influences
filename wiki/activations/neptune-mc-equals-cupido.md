## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Jointly to follow the unknown. False pretences. Marriage swindler. Forger in the arts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `81`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

My disappointment concerning the community, my intuitive sense for the whole, my premonitions concerning the whole, my art in water, my artistic intuition, my sensitivity in the community, and my uncertainty in the community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: shared pursuit of the unknown, with communal sensitivity, uncertainty, and deceptive pretences among the stated expressions.
- psychology: intuition concerning the whole and disappointment with a community are named.
- body/health: no health claim is stated; art in water is a separate literal example.
- social/relationship: shared exploration, community sensitivity, marriage fraud, and forgery in art occur in the entries.
- events/manifestations: following the unknown together and art in water are explicit settings.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's fraud examples do not make all collaboration fraudulent; ABC distinguishes art in water from artistic intuition.

## Contradictions

Witte's shared exploration is followed by specific fraud examples. ABC also describes communal sensitivity and artistic intuition, without asserting that every community or artwork deceives.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Cupido, shared involvement gives personal uncertainty a communal setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) joins following the unknown together with false pretences, marriage fraud, and forgery; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointment, intuition concerning the whole, artistic intuition, and sensitivity within a community (printed p. 81, PDF p. 39). The shared theme is uncertainty within collective or artistic involvement; the fraud examples remain particular expressions, while art in water is retained as its own literal example.
