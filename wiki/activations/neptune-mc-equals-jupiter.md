## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Imaginary luck. To imagine or pretend luck. A money swindler. A speculator.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `80`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

My sense for money, my water right, my joy in the water, my breathing successes, my disappointments through the law, my successful premonitions, my good intuitions, my legal uncertainty, and refined happiness of the soul

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `290`, entry code `1040`.

#### Ebertin Entry

The joy of expectation, tendency to dream of happiness and good fortune, pretense of happiness and good luck. Inclination to put all one's eggs in one basket, speculation, gambling.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `114` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `113`).

#### Sandbach Entry

Enthusiasm that is ineffective because it is based on an unrealistic and/or ungrounded approach to one’s career work. Success through humanitarian work. The ability to trust one’s intuitions about how to proceed in one’s work.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source reference: printed p. `340` (entry).

#### Munkasey Entry

Proceeding anyhow despite poorly defined goals; an increased awareness and perception of how other people think or what their needs are; makes it easier for you to fall into selfdelusion or deception; expands vistas of life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: expectation of happiness or success, ranging from trusting intuition to imagined luck and speculation.
- psychology: dreaming of good fortune, self-delusion, and sensitivity to others' needs are explicit.
- body/health: ABC's breathing successes are a historical claim; joy in water does not itself establish a health effect.
- social/relationship: pretended luck, money swindling, and perception of others' needs are distinct social expressions.
- events/manifestations: gambling, speculation, humanitarian work, and proceeding despite poorly defined goals are named.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach allows humanitarian success and trusted intuition while warning against unrealistic career enthusiasm; no guaranteed success follows.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize imagined or pretended luck and speculation. Sandbach also allows effective humanitarian work and intuitive guidance; Munkasey pairs expanded possibilities with self-delusion.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Jupiter, hopeful expectations about direction or success can enlarge possibilities while obscuring what supports them. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) emphasize imagined luck and speculation; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) includes proceeding despite vague goals and greater susceptibility to self-delusion. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) distinguishes unrealistic career enthusiasm from success through humanitarian work and trust in intuition. [ABC](#abc-entry) likewise places successful premonitions beside disappointment through law and legal uncertainty, so the optimistic expressions do not promise a successful outcome.
