## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To do something independently which is dubious, unknown or uncertain. Chief instigator of frauds. Deception of higher-ups.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `81`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

I am independent in the air, I deny authority, my direction of breathing, my special intuitive sense, my disappointment about the state, my dominating sensitivity, my great uncertainty, my weak authority, and greatness of the soul through sensitivity

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: independent activity under uncertain conditions, with sensitivity and authority as stated concerns.
- psychology: special intuitive sense, dominating sensitivity, and great uncertainty are named.
- body/health: ABC's direction of breathing is a historical source claim without a stated mechanism.
- social/relationship: denying authority, weak authority, and deception of higher-ups are distinct relationships to power.
- events/manifestations: Witte names dubious independent activity and instigating fraud; ABC adds disappointment concerning the state.
- conflicts/notes: independence and sensitivity do not establish fraud; greatness of soul is an attributed qualitative expression.

## Contradictions

Witte concentrates on uncertain independent action and fraud involving higher-ups. ABC includes both weakened authority and sensitive personal capacities, without making these equivalent to deception.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Kronos, independent action and authority are qualified by uncertainty. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes acting independently in dubious or unknown matters and particular forms of fraud; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds denial or weakness of authority, disappointment about the state, and a special intuitive sense (printed p. 81, PDF p. 39). Sensitivity and uncertainty can therefore be described together here, while fraud remains an explicit source example rather than a conclusion about every independent undertaking.
