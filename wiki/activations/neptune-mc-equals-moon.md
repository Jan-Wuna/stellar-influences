## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Sensitive to beams (radiated energy). Sleepwalker. To deceive females, or the people. To be very imaginative.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `80`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Blissful bodily feelings, my wife's premonitions, my wife's sensitivity, my wife's intuitions, my disappointed wife, my uncertainty of feeling, my weak wife, my sensitive feeling, and the future of my people

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `290`, entry code `1036`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Tendency to revel in fanciful imaginings, active and intense imagination, misinterpretation of observations and perceptions, dreamy nature, peculiar state of feeling.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `114` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `113`).

#### Sandbach Entry

The emotional need to have a career you can really feel dedicated to. Your ability to intuit how to spiritually advance yourself comes and goes, and you need to be sensitive to these cycles, so that you know that even though you sometimes feel not so spiritually connected you will experience a deeper connection soon. When you feel emotionally down you may get into negative imaginings about your career.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source reference: printed p. `340` (entry).

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased receptivity about your plans and direction in life; brings a sensitivity to how others can or will deceive you about the help they give in fulfilling your dreams and visions; prompts hesitations about your goals.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: emotional imagination and receptivity around personal direction, ideals, or career.
- psychology: intense imagining, misread perceptions, uncertain feelings, and hesitation about goals are explicit.
- body/health: Witte's radiated-energy sensitivity and sleepwalking are historical claims; ABC adds blissful bodily feelings without a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: deceiving women or the people and uncertainty about promised help are stated; ABC's wife language remains source-specific.
- events/manifestations: career dedication and negative imaginings about a career are named by Sandbach.
- conflicts/notes: spiritual connectedness is Sandbach's account, not an established timing rule; nurturing service or dependence on approval is not demonstrated.

## Contradictions

Ebertin and Witte foreground imagination and misperception. Sandbach also describes career dedication and fluctuating spiritual connection; Munkasey emphasizes receptivity and uncertain help from others.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Moon, emotional receptivity intensifies imagination about one's direction and ideals. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) pairs vivid imagining with mistaken perceptions; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes the need for a meaningful career and negative career imaginings when emotionally low. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds hesitation about goals and sensitivity to misleading promises of help, while [ABC](#abc-entry) preserves uncertainty of feeling and disappointment in its wife-specific wording. The historical bodily claims in [Witte](#witte-entry) remain attributed and do not establish a diagnosis or a general vocation of nurturing service.
