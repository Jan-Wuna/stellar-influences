#### Munkasey Entry

Adds to your neglect of or inability to see the need for thoroughly planning thru any work you want to do; adds to pretenses you have about your own self-importance and the recognition which you feel others should give you.

## Derived Synthesis

For Neptune/MC = Neptune, [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) connects inadequate planning with inflated expectations of personal importance and recognition (printed p. 342). The entry concerns both the preparation work receives and the attention one expects from others; it does not establish that either problem must occur.
