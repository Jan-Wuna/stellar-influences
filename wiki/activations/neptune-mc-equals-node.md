## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To induce others to commit fraudulent acts. Errors and deception in relationships.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `80`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Premonitions in my contacts, contact with a sensitive soul, my disappointing connections, my contacts in the air, my sensitive relationships, my uncertainty in relationships, and my future connections

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `291`, entry code `1044`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Unpleasant associations, bad company, sharing of bad intentions with other people.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `114` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `113`).

#### Sandbach Entry

The need to connect with people who share your same career ideals and aspira tions. Working for others who are working for humanity.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source reference: printed p. `340` (entry).

#### Munkasey Entry

Finding others who share your dreams and visions for the future and who will work with you; acquiring help from support groups; people who listen to your dreams of the future; friends who create reality from your visions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: connections formed around future aims, with support and deceptive association as contrasting possibilities.
- psychology: shared dreams, sensitivity, premonitions, and uncertainty in relationships are named.
- body/health: no health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: help from friends or support groups contrasts with misleading relationships and shared bad intentions.
- events/manifestations: collaborating on visions and working for humanitarian workers are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: bad company in Ebertin is a distinct severe possibility; it does not describe every group sharing an ideal.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize deceptive or harmful association, while Sandbach and Munkasey describe constructive connections around career ideals and future visions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Node, relationships gather around visions of what one's work or future could become. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes shared career ideals and humanitarian service, and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds friends and support groups who help realize visions. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) preserve the contrasting possibility of deception or harmful shared intentions; [ABC](#abc-entry) includes sensitivity, disappointment, and uncertainty in connections. Shared ideals therefore describe the basis of association without proving that the association is helpful or deceptive.
