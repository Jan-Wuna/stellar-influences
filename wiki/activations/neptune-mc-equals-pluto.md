## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be cunning. Swindles which are not uncovered. Moment of an uncertain development.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `81`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Inner transformation through metaphysics, my development of breathing, my unfolding in the air, my growing sensitivity, my growing disappointment, my growing intuition, my future development, and growing uncertainty of the soul

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `291`, entry code `1043`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Pursuit of peculiar objectives, supernatural experiences. Adventurous enterprises, criminal offences.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `114` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `113`).

#### Sandbach Entry

A tremendous idealism about one’s career. The potential of sharing one’s pow erful visions with the world. One’s life is transformed by being open and receptive to spiritual messages that come to one.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source reference: printed p. `340` (entry).

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds intensity and depth to the reality you feel is coming in the future; little abnormalities which create big problems in reaching your goals; concealing your plans from others while you scheme and plan about future directions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: intensified future aims and development under uncertain conditions.
- psychology: powerful career ideals, concealment of plans, growing intuition, and disappointment are explicit.
- body/health: ABC's development of breathing is a historical bodily claim without a stated mechanism.
- social/relationship: sharing visions and concealing plans are contrasting social expressions; criminal offences are Ebertin's severe example.
- events/manifestations: uncertain development, adventurous enterprises, and small irregularities obstructing goals are named.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's spiritual transformation is attributed; the sources do not establish a universal transformative calling or scandal.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize cunning, uncertain enterprises, and offences. Sandbach describes powerful idealism and spiritual receptivity; Munkasey adds concealed planning and obstacles.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Pluto, future plans and ideals acquire intensity while their development remains uncertain. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) names depth of expectation, concealed plans, and small problems that obstruct goals; [ABC](#abc-entry) places growing intuition beside growing disappointment and uncertainty of soul. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes powerful career idealism and receptivity to spiritual messages. The cunning and offences in [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) remain specific darker expressions, without making every strong vision manipulative.
