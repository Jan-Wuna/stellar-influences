## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To simulate brains. The bluffer.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `81`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Recognition of weakness of the soul, my weak reason, my mental sensitivity, my sense for truth, my ideas for the future, my clarity through metaphysics, my spiritual uncertainty, and refinement of the spirit-soul

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: claimed insight or intelligence contrasted with bluffing and spiritual uncertainty.
- psychology: weak reasoning, mental sensitivity, and recognition of weakness are explicit.
- body/health: weakness of the soul is historical qualitative language, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Witte's bluffer and simulated intelligence concern the presentation of understanding.
- events/manifestations: ideas for the future and clarity through metaphysics are ABC's examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's sense for truth does not prove truth, and Witte's bluffing does not invalidate every stated idea.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes simulated intelligence. ABC places sensitivity and metaphysical clarity beside weak reasoning and spiritual uncertainty, without identifying them all as bluffing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Poseidon, the question of insight includes both its substance and its appearance. [Witte](#witte-entry) names simulated intelligence and bluffing; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a sense for truth, ideas for the future, weak reasoning, and spiritual uncertainty (printed p. 81, PDF p. 39). The entries support a distinction between claiming understanding and having clarity, while the metaphysical expressions remain attributed source claims.
