## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To suffer or separate through having the wrong conception (notion) of the situation. Losses or sickness caused by weakness or neglect.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My patience in the air, my disappointing losses, my intuitions in old age, my concentration on breathing, my weak perseverance, my sensitivity to disturbances, my unknown losses, and my water problems

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Lack of clarity, insecurity and uncertainty, tendency to worry. Suffering from the consequences of wrong actions, the stage of getting ill. Inability to get over one's losses.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To be cautious and careful about not being too unrealistic and idealistic about one’s career goals. The need to take seriously the intuitions one’s has about one’s work and one’s own spiritual development. To suffer restrictions or letdowns concerning one’s career ideals.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Brings reality to what might otherwise be fruitless planning or a frivolous use of resources; planing more thoroughly for your goals in life, but success comes slowly; lessens your ability to idly dream about future possibilities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/MC = Saturn`: disciplined compassion, long endurance in difficult callings, and the capacity to make intuition serve concrete responsibility. Main caution: disappointed purpose: pressure can reduce vision to discouragement, confusion, or a career path that never fully takes shape.
