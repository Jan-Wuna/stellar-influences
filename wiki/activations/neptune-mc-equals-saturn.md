## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To suffer or separate through having the wrong conception (notion) of the situation. Losses or sickness caused by weakness or neglect.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `81`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

My patience in the air, my disappointing losses, my intuitions in old age, my concentration on breathing, my weak perseverance, my sensitivity to disturbances, my unknown losses, and my water problems

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `290`, entry code `1041`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Lack of clarity, insecurity and uncertainty, tendency to worry. Suffering from the consequences of wrong actions, the stage of getting ill. Inability to get over one's losses.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `114` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `113`).

#### Sandbach Entry

To be cautious and careful about not being too unrealistic and idealistic about one’s career goals. The need to take seriously the intuitions one’s has about one’s work and one’s own spiritual development. To suffer restrictions or letdowns concerning one’s career ideals.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source reference: printed p. `340` (entry).

#### Munkasey Entry

Brings reality to what might otherwise be fruitless planning or a frivolous use of resources; planing more thoroughly for your goals in life, but success comes slowly; lessens your ability to idly dream about future possibilities.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: restriction and realism applied to uncertain aims or ideals.
- psychology: worry, insecurity, cautious career expectations, and difficulty recovering from loss are stated.
- body/health: Witte and Ebertin associate weakness or wrong action with illness; ABC mentions concentration on breathing. These are historical claims.
- social/relationship: Witte includes separation through a mistaken conception of the situation.
- events/manifestations: disappointing losses, career restrictions, and slower but more thorough planning are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's practical realism differs from the other entries' loss emphasis; disciplined compassion and endless endurance are not supplied.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin foreground loss, uncertainty, and illness; Sandbach warns against unrealistic career ideals. Munkasey instead emphasizes more realistic and thorough planning, with slow success.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Saturn, uncertain aims encounter limits that can bring either discouragement or more realistic planning. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describe losses and the consequences of mistaken action; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds restrictions or letdowns around career ideals. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) supplies the constructive counterpart of planning more thoroughly and using resources less frivolously. The historical illness claims remain attributed, while the shared practical issue is how ideals meet constraint.
