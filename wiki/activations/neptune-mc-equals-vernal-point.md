## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To arouse false notions. To deceive (defraud) many people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `80`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

My uncertainty in public, my premonitions for the public, my intuition in public life, my sensitivity in public, my deceptions in public life, and psychic weakness in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: uncertainty, sensitivity, and deception in public life.
- psychology: public premonitions, intuition, and uncertainty are explicit.
- body/health: ABC's weakness of soul in public is historical qualitative language, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Witte specifically names deceiving many people and arousing false notions.
- events/manifestations: public-life deception and sensitivity are the named settings.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's public sensitivity is not automatically deliberate fraud; collective misperception beyond the stated false notions is not established.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes deliberate public deception; ABC also lists public intuition, sensitivity, and uncertainty without making every public appearance fraudulent.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Vernal Point, the uncertainty and sensitivity of the pair enter public life. [ABC](#abc-entry) names public premonitions, intuition, uncertainty, and deception (printed p. 80, PDF p. 39), while [Witte](#witte-entry) specifically names arousing false notions and deceiving many people. Those latter examples preserve the possibility of public fraud without turning all public sensitivity into deceptive conduct.
