## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Pronounced refinement of feeling. Enormous imagination. To deceive or be deceived on a grand scale. To pretend to great power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `81`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of my power, influence of my breath, energy of a sensitive soul, my influence through sensitivity, my influence in the air, my weak influence, my subtle-material energy, my weaknesses and strengths, and my strong intuition

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: great imagination and sensitivity alongside uncertain or pretended power.
- psychology: refinement of feeling and strong intuition are explicit.
- body/health: ABC's breath influence and subtle-material energy are historical claims without a demonstrated mechanism.
- social/relationship: deceiving or being deceived on a grand scale and pretending power are Witte's examples.
- events/manifestations: dissolving power, weak influence, and influence through sensitivity are named.
- conflicts/notes: the entries do not say that refined feeling inevitably grows into deception or exaggerated power.

## Contradictions

Witte places great imagination beside grand deception and pretended power. ABC also describes weak influence, dissolved power, and sensitivity; these are not a single causal progression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Vulcanus, pronounced sensitivity and imagination are considered alongside the extent and reliability of personal influence. [Witte](#witte-entry) names both refinement of feeling and deception on a grand scale; [ABC](#abc-entry) includes strong intuition, influence through sensitivity, weak influence, and dissolution of power (printed p. 81, PDF p. 39). These remain alternative expressions rather than a claim that heightened imagination necessarily becomes deceit.
