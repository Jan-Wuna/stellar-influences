## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `68` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To gravely offend, or to scald, someone. Enterprises that disappoint.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `81`, PDF p. `39` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Creativity of a sensitive soul, my directed breathing, my achievements in water, my creative intuitions, my weak efforts, my uncertain leadership, my refined shaping, my weakness of procreation, and my shaping of the future

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creative initiatives and leadership whose effort or results may disappoint.
- psychology: creative intuition and uncertainty in leadership are explicit.
- body/health: directed breathing and weakness of procreation are ABC's historical claims; Witte's scalding is a literal harm example, not a forecast.
- social/relationship: gravely offending someone and uncertain leadership are stated interpersonal expressions.
- events/manifestations: disappointing enterprises, achievements in water, and shaping the future are named.
- conflicts/notes: creative sensitivity does not establish harmful conduct or a guaranteed failure; Witte's offence and scalding examples remain particular.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes offence, scalding, and disappointment in enterprises. ABC also includes creative intuition and achievements, without stating that every initiative fails or harms someone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/MC = Zeus, the attempt to create or direct something is qualified by uncertain leadership and weak effort. [ABC](#abc-entry) also preserves creative intuition, achievements in water, and shaping the future (printed p. 81, PDF p. 39). [Witte](#witte-entry) adds disappointing enterprises and particular examples of offence or scalding. The sources connect initiative with uncertainty without making harm or failure inevitable.
