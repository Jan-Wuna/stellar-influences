#### Witte Entry

Embezzlement. Great corruption. Deception on a large scale. Dissolution on a large scale. Chemistry. Science of the future. Occult science. Masses of air, gas or water. The deception of many. Large deception, frauds.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Neptune = Apollon gives scale to dissolution and deception while also naming chemistry and masses of air, gas, or water. [Witte](#witte-entry) explicitly includes embezzlement, corruption, and deceiving many people, but places future and occult science beside them as separate examples (PDF p. 245, entry start). The passage does not establish that its scientific or material examples are inherently fraudulent.
