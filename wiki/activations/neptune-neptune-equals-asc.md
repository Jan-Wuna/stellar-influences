#### Witte Entry

To be deceived. Wrongly connected. Others are negative, irresolute or prejudiced. Others say “No”. Want of clearness in relations with others. To be deceived or avoided by others. Uncertain, doubtful, precarious, unsteady situations in relation to a location.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Neptune = Asc places uncertainty in relations with others and in one's situation at a location. [Witte](#witte-entry) includes refusal, avoidance, deception, and lack of clarity in relationships, together with precarious or unsteady local circumstances (PDF p. 244, entry start). The entry describes possible forms of unreliable contact without establishing that all other people or all surroundings are unreliable.
