## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Neptune = Cupido concerns dissonance and loss in shared life. [Witte](#witte-entry) names grief in marriage or societies, dissolution of a partnership, and unfortunate parents; housing shortage and dwindling art provide separate material and cultural examples (PDF p. 245, entry start). This supports a bounded reading of weakened or dissolving associations, without predicting the fate of any particular family.
