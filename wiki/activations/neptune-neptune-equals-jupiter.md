#### Witte Entry

Lucky mood. Easily acquired money. Shortage of money. Money losses. Easily made money. Imagined happiness. Uncertain possession. Speculations. Financial transactions. The future life.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Neptune = Jupiter joins the feeling or prospect of good fortune with uncertainty over possession. [Witte](#witte-entry) lists lucky mood, imagined happiness, easy money, shortage, financial loss, and speculation (PDF p. 244, entry start). The connection is between expectation and insecure fortune; the passage does not promise either gain or a gain-then-loss sequence.
