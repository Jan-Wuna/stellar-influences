## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Neptune = Mars concerns action whose conditions or results are uncertain. [Witte](#witte-entry) names hindered plans, poor results, and unclear actions, while also listing future employment and work with water or air (PDF p. 244, entry start). Infection and poisoning remain separate historical hazard examples; they do not define every form of work described here.
