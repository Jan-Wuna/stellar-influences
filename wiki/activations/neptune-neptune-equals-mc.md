#### Witte Entry

To decline. To refuse something. To be unsure, unable to support. Unintelligible attitude. To follow wrong ideas. “I deceive”. Untruth. Falsehood. Swindle. To be intuitive. To concentrate on the future. To follow the unknown, the doubtful. To be disappointed. To appear unreliable or mysterious. To feel uncomfortable. Moment (minute) of uncertainty.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal attitude toward uncertainty, intuition, and the unknown.
- psychology: following wrong ideas, disappointment, discomfort, and intuitive orientation are explicit.
- body/health: no specific health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: deceiving and appearing unreliable or mysterious are stated.
- events/manifestations: refusing, declining support, concentrating on the future, and a moment of uncertainty are named.
- conflicts/notes: intuition and deliberate falsehood are separate possibilities; the entry does not make all intuition deceptive.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Neptune = MC places the uncertainty of the pair in personal attitude and direction. [Witte](#witte-entry) combines intuition and concentration on the future with following doubtful ideas, disappointment, refusal, and explicit deception (PDF p. 244, entry start). The common concern is how one meets what is unclear; intuitive interest, uncertainty, and deliberate falsehood remain distinct expressions.
