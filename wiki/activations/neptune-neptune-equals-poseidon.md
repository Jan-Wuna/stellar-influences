## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Neptune = Poseidon connects subtle and intellectual matters with reflection and the reliability of ideas. [Witte](#witte-entry) places metaphysics and psychology beside misrepresented facts, false views, enthusiasm, and fanaticism; mirrors, refraction, and steam are separate literal examples (PDF p. 245, entry start). The entry does not establish that spiritual or intellectual interests necessarily produce false belief.
