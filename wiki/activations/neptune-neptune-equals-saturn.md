#### Witte Entry

Habit. Things of long duration. Sickness. Treacherous diseases. Chronic ill health. Plague. Chronic conditions. Theft. Losses or separation through deception or fraud. Patience in an uncertain situation. Intuition in old age. Creeping losses. Problems through or with gas, air or water.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: long duration, patience, and gradual loss in uncertain conditions.
- psychology: habit, patience, and intuition in old age are named.
- body/health: chronic ill health, treacherous disease, and plague are historical medical claims.
- social/relationship: theft and separation through deception or fraud are explicit.
- events/manifestations: creeping losses and problems with gas, air, or water are listed.
- conflicts/notes: patience is a response to uncertainty, not evidence that deception itself persists patiently.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Neptune = Saturn emphasizes duration in uncertain or difficult circumstances. [Witte](#witte-entry) links long-standing conditions with patience, creeping losses, and separation through deception or fraud (PDF p. 245, entry start). Its chronic-illness language remains a historical claim; the shared emphasis on persistence does not give every loss or physical condition the same cause.
