#### Witte Entry

Suddenly incapacitated. Unconsciousness. Checkmated. The transition into the beyond. Crisis. Dead persons. Revolution. Sudden disagreeable events. To cause sudden confusion and deception of a revolutionary kind. To give up something. To bring an end to something. Futile excitement. Sudden uncertainty.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Neptune = Uranus concerns abrupt uncertainty and interruption. [Witte](#witte-entry) names crisis, sudden confusion, futile excitement, and bringing something to an end (PDF p. 245, entry start). Incapacity and death are severe historical examples retained in the source entry; they do not follow inevitably from ordinary confusion or establish an individual forecast.
