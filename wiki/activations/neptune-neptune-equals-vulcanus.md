## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Sensibility. Power one cannot hear or see. The might of the fine material forces. To resign. To be without power, might, strength or influence. Mighty deception or dissolution. Power comes from hidden sources. Powerlessness.

## Comparative Schema

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Neptune = Vulcanus places hidden influence beside its absence. [Witte](#witte-entry) lists power that cannot be heard or seen, fine material forces, and hidden sources of power, but also resignation, powerlessness, and great deception or dissolution (PDF p. 245, entry start). The entry supports this contrast without supplying a mechanism for the unseen forces or ranking them above visible influence.
