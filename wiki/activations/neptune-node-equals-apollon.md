## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `147` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Dissolution of union with many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `221`, PDF p. `109` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of trade relations, successful connection to metaphysics, creeping expansion of a community, uncertain success in relationships, connection to knowledge of metaphysics, and increasing connections with trade

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: wider or trade-related connections, with dissolution and uncertain success alongside expansion.
- psychology: no distinct psychological motive is supplied.
- body/health: no health claim is stated.
- social/relationship: unions with many and expanding community connections are explicit.
- events/manifestations: dissolving trade relations and connections with metaphysical knowledge are named.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes increasing trade links and success, so Witte's dissolution does not define every extensive connection.

## Contradictions

Witte's short entry concerns dissolution with many. ABC also describes expansion, increasing trade links, and successful connection with metaphysics.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Node = Apollon, the scale or extent of connection is prominent. [Witte](#witte-entry) names dissolution of a union with many; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds dissolving trade relations, expanding community, increasing trade links, and uncertain success in relationships (printed p. 221, PDF p. 109). The shared setting is a wider field of association, with expansion and dissolution retained as different possibilities.
