## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `147` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To find oneself in a hopeless sorrowful condition. Not knowing a way out. To be separated from, or shunned by others on account of poverty.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `221`, PDF p. `109` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Secret connection to metaphysics, lack of sensitivity in a relationship, weakness in a criminal connection, unknown connections from the past, uncertain relationship in distress, and hidden weakness in encounters

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: connection under distress, deprivation, or hidden weakness.
- psychology: hopelessness, sorrow, and not knowing a way out are explicit.
- body/health: weakness is unspecified; the entries supply no medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: poverty-related exclusion, deficient sensitivity, and uncertain relationships in distress are named.
- events/manifestations: past unknown ties and a secret metaphysical connection are distinct settings.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's criminal connection is unspecified; neither poverty nor metaphysical contact establishes criminality.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses sorrow and exclusion through poverty; ABC includes hidden or past connections and deficient sensitivity without specifying one cause for every difficulty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Node = Hades, uncertain connection is described in settings of distress and exclusion. [Witte](#witte-entry) names sorrow, not knowing a way out, and being shunned through poverty; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds deficient sensitivity, hidden weakness, and an uncertain relationship in distress (printed p. 221, PDF p. 109). Its criminal-connection example remains unspecified and is not inferred from poverty, secrecy, or metaphysical interest.
