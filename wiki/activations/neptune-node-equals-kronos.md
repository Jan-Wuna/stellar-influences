## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `147` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Bad contact with the state. Through power of the state dissolved unions. The deserted leader.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `221`, PDF p. `109` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of an important connection, disappointing connection to the state, hopeful relationships with authorities, great sensitivity in a connection, uncertainty in connection with authority, connection to the water authority, and important air connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: uncertain or dissolving connections involving authority or importance.
- psychology: hopeful expectations and sensitivity in a connection are explicit.
- body/health: no health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: state contact, relationships with authorities, and a deserted leader are named.
- events/manifestations: state dissolution of unions and contact with a water authority are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: hopeful or sensitive relations are also stated; the source does not make all official contact hostile or every leader abandoned.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes poor state contact and unions dissolved by state power. ABC also includes expectations of authorities, sensitivity, and specific water or air connections.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Node = Kronos, uncertainty in ties is specified through authority or an important connection. [Witte](#witte-entry) names bad state contact, unions dissolved by state power, and a deserted leader; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointing state connections, hopeful relations with authorities, and uncertainty concerning authority (printed p. 221, PDF p. 109). The emphasis is on the reliability of consequential ties, with sensitivity and ordinary institutional contact retained alongside dissolution.
