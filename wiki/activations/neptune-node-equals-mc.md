## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `147` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To see oneself disappointed and deceived in one’s own connections. To feel deserted by all.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `220`, PDF p. `109` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Sensitive soul-connection, my sensitivity in encounters, my uncertainty in contact, my future connection, moment of dissolution of a relationship, psychic sensitivity of a connection, and unclear relationship of the soul

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `285`, entry code `1021`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Inability to realize one's own objectives with the help of others. Emotional suffering, preference for life in seclusion and away from other people.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `112` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `111`).

#### Sandbach Entry

To be duped by authority figures, or to idealize them in a way that retards one’s personal growth. In one’s career one makes subtle, intuitive connections with others. One’s spiritual path in this life involves the development of one’s intuition.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source reference: printed p. `332` (entry).

#### Munkasey Entry

Doing your best self-evaluation and cope with your problems and worries when alone and away from people’s influences and distractions; increased enjoyment in gaining inspiration from the works of others as you mature.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal experience of uncertain ties, disappointment, and the role of solitude.
- psychology: emotional suffering, self-evaluation, worries, and inspiration are explicit.
- body/health: no specific medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: feeling deserted, lack of help toward goals, and idealizing or being deceived by authorities are stated.
- events/manifestations: future connections, dissolution of a relationship, and intuitive career contacts are named.
- conflicts/notes: solitude is both a preference in Ebertin and a useful setting for self-evaluation in Munkasey; a humanitarian calling is not established.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize disappointment, desertion, and lack of help. Sandbach also includes intuitive career contacts; Munkasey presents solitude as useful for self-evaluation and inspiration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Node = MC, uncertainty in ties becomes a personal question of disappointment, help, and time apart. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes feeling deceived or deserted, and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) adds difficulty realizing aims through others. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) gives solitude a constructive use in self-evaluation and coping with worries; [ABC](#abc-entry) includes sensitivity and uncertain contact. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) distinguishes idealizing authority from making subtle career connections, without establishing a fixed humanitarian vocation.
