## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `147` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Untenable connections or unions. Divorce. To be deserted. Insecurity in personal relationships.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `220`, PDF p. `109` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Sensitive personal relationship, contacts with a center of metaphysics, personal connection to water, connections between people in the air, meeting sensitive people, unclear contacts with a man, and uncertainty in personal relationships

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `284`, entry code `1011`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Difficulty in establishing contacts with other people, inability to explain one's ideas to others and to come to an understanding with other people. The misfortune to be tormented, disappointed or let down by others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `111` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `111`).

#### Sandbach Entry

One is aware of subtle and hidden connections, and is prone to them influencing one’s vitality. This will manifest as either becoming drained of energy or energized by positive spiritual connections.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source reference: printed p. `332` (entry).

#### Munkasey Entry

Encourages you to be your own person in deciding what influences you will or will not accept from other people; adds to your determination to be basic and original in your own interpretation of which paths to travel in life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal relationships and choice of outside influences under uncertain conditions.
- psychology: difficulty explaining ideas and determination to choose one's path are explicit.
- body/health: Sandbach's energized or drained vitality is an attributed spiritual claim, not a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: desertion, untenable ties, sensitive encounters, and difficulty reaching understanding are stated.
- events/manifestations: divorce, contact with a metaphysical center, and connection to water are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey emphasizes deciding which influences to accept; no universal depletion or fellowship is established.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin foreground insecurity and difficult contact. ABC also includes sensitivity; Sandbach describes vitality in spiritual connection, while Munkasey stresses personal choice of influence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Node = Sun, uncertainty in relationships bears on personal direction and the influences one accepts. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describe untenable ties, desertion, and difficulty reaching understanding; [ABC](#abc-entry) also preserves sensitive relationships and encounters. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) gives the person a deciding role in accepting influence. [Sandbach's](#sandbach-entry) account of vitality through spiritual connection remains attributed, without making every relationship energizing or depleting.
