## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Unfortunate love unions. Unions which seems to be harmonious at first, but later become untenable.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Autlosung of a love relationship, encounter with future art, joy through connections in the air, harmonious connection through fine feeling, unsafe peace contacts, unsafe love connection, and preference for connection to the water

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Misconceptions with regard to other people's relationships and associations. Wrong liaisons, unfaithfulness.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A love of experiencing the subtlest levels of one’s connections to others. An appreciation of the delicate or fragile nature of connections. Laziness here may express itself as falling into daydreaming, idle fantasy and/or wishful thinking, though love may inspire one to evolve one’s sensitivity and idealism.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `332`

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds to the contentment and satisfaction when you find time to be alone with yourself; increased needs to pamper and indulge your personal retreats with comfortable and tasteful surroundings, and music, art, etc.
