## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `147` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Dissolutions and separations in general.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `220`, PDF p. `109` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of connections in public, relationship to public water, disappointing connections in public life, public connections through the air, uncertainty in public connections, and mediation of water for the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: dissolution or uncertainty in public connections.
- psychology: disappointment in public life is explicit.
- body/health: public water is named, without a medical claim.
- social/relationship: public ties and separations in general are stated.
- events/manifestations: air connections and mediation of water for the public are literal examples.
- conflicts/notes: the entry supplies no particular mass event or claim that all separations happen collectively.

## Contradictions

Witte gives a broad separation statement; ABC supplies public settings and particular water or air connections without making each one a separation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Node = Vernal Point, connection is considered in a public setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) names dissolutions and separations in general, while [ABC](#abc-entry) specifies disappointing or uncertain public ties, public water, and connections through the air (printed p. 220, PDF p. 109). Public setting does not itself establish a widespread collective breakup.
