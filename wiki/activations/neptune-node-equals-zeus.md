## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `147` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The end of a creative period. The end of a fight. Settling an argument.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `221`, PDF p. `109` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Efforts in a weak connection, dissolution of a creative connection, sensitivity of a procreative connection, unclear striving toward a goal in a relationship, uncertainty at the beginning of a contact, and connected with a start into the air

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: ending of a creative or conflict phase, alongside uncertain efforts within a connection.
- psychology: unclear aims and uncertainty at first contact are explicit.
- body/health: procreative connection is source language without a particular health claim.
- social/relationship: settling an argument and weak or creative ties are named.
- events/manifestations: ending a fight, dissolving a creative connection, and a start into the air are stated.
- conflicts/notes: ending a fight need not mean exhaustion or failure; the procreative phrase is not further explained.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes closure and settlement. ABC also includes effort, uncertain beginnings, and unclear aims within an ongoing relationship.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Node = Zeus, a connection may reach an ending or require clarification of its purpose. [Witte](#witte-entry) names the end of a creative period or fight and settling an argument; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds weak effort, dissolution of a creative tie, unclear aims, and uncertainty at first contact (printed p. 221, PDF p. 109). Closure can therefore mean settlement as well as dissolution, without an added claim that the struggle has exhausted itself.
