## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `246` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Slow changes in the family or home, like being secretly promised or engaged. Departing of members of the family through slow sickness, or secret family matters, which are hidden from the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `391`, PDF p. `194` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disappointed development-community, developing shared sensitivity, metaphysical development-community, creeping changes in a community, uncertain development in a community, and growing expectations in a community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: slow or hidden development within family, home, or community.
- psychology: disappointment, shared sensitivity, and growing expectations are explicit.
- body/health: departure through slow sickness is Witte's historical claim, not an individual forecast.
- social/relationship: private family matters, secret engagement, and community development are named.
- events/manifestations: slow domestic changes and uncertain or creeping community change are stated.
- conflicts/notes: the examples do not define all private family matters as harmful or prescribe a single community outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes family and home, including secrecy and loss. ABC adds community sensitivity, expectations, and uncertain development.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Pluto = Cupido, hidden or gradual development takes place within shared life. [Witte](#witte-entry) names slow family changes, private matters, and secret engagement; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds shared sensitivity, disappointed community development, and growing expectations (printed p. 391, PDF p. 194). The family and community settings overlap without making every private change a loss; Witte's sickness-related example remains attributed.
