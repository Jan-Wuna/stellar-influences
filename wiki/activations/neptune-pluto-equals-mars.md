## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To work without planning, without using the head. An undertaking which has been rushed and set going without consideration.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development work in the air, development of fine rules at work, impulses to change the weather, create air change, develop activity in the water, expect unsafe work development, change in work, and growing work in the air

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Lack of energy, the misfortune to be used as a tool for other people's interests, lack of resistance and stamina. Tendency to succumb to external powers, being utilized as a medium.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The ability to assert oneself with extreme subtlety, possibly in a hidden or covert way. To act on the basis of an extreme idealism. The potential of being subtly destructive.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased potential to use of drugs or artificial devices to speed up or alter your life; adds a compulsion to use violence to achieve your dreams; hostility emphasized by any imminent destructive or punishing activity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Pluto = Mars`: sacrificial effort, fierce work in unseen conditions, and the ability to act inside deep transformation or crisis. Main caution: covert destructiveness: force can become compulsive, misdirected, draining, or driven by motives that remain obscured.
