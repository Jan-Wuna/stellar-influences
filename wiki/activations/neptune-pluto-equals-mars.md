## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `246` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To work without planning, without using the head. An undertaking which has been rushed and set going without consideration.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `390`, PDF p. `194` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Development work in the air, developing sensitivity at work, impulses to change the water, creating a change of air, developing activity in water, uncertain development of work, expecting changes in work, and growing work in the air

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `281`, entry code `1003`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Lack of energy, the misfortune to be used as a tool for other people's interests, lack of resistance and stamina. Tendency to succumb to external powers, being utilized as a medium.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `110` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `110`).

#### Sandbach Entry

The ability to assert oneself with extreme subtlety, possibly in a hidden or covert way. To act on the basis of an extreme idealism. The potential of being subtly destructive.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source reference: printed p. `328` (entry).

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased potential to use of drugs or artificial devices to speed up or alter your life; adds a compulsion to use violence to achieve your dreams; hostility emphasized by any imminent destructive or punishing activity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: action or work affected by unclear planning, subtle motives, or outside influence.
- psychology: extreme idealism, hostility, and rushed undertaking are explicit.
- body/health: lack of energy and stamina and drug-related behavior are historical claims, not diagnoses or recommendations.
- social/relationship: being used for others' interests and possible covert destructiveness are stated.
- events/manifestations: unplanned work, uncertain work development, and activity involving water or air are named.
- conflicts/notes: the source examples of violence and destructiveness do not define every subtle or idealistic action.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses rushed, unplanned undertaking; Ebertin emphasizes low resistance and external influence. Sandbach includes subtle assertion and possible destructiveness, while Munkasey supplies severe drug and violence examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Pluto = Mars, action may proceed without sufficient planning or be shaped by subtle and external influences. [Witte](#witte-entry) names a rushed undertaking; [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describes being used for others' interests and lacking resistance. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds subtle assertion and extreme idealism, while [ABC](#abc-entry) preserves sensitive work and uncertain development. [Munkasey's](#munkasey-entry) severe examples remain attributed possibilities, without turning all idealistic effort into sacrifice or violence.
