## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `246` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Perception gained through intuition. The ability to hide the real thoughts and keep secrets. News or knowledge where the real meaning is only partly conceived. The beginning of a slow transformation in perception and thinking.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `390`, PDF p. `194` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Reports of uncertain developments, creeping traffic development, talking about changes in the water, discussing uncertain developments, growing changes in traffic, changing air movements, and discussing future developments

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `281`, entry code `1001`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Inability to think independently, thinking and acting while under strange influences, sensitive nerves, many plans incapable of realization. Nervous weakness.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `110` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `110`).

#### Sandbach Entry

One who communicates with great subtlety, which, of course, may be lost on other people. The communication of intense and powerful fantasies. Thinking which is extremely confused.

- Source artifact: actual PDF p. 110 visibly places the smaller isolated label `3 Highlighters-` between `on` and `other people`. It is preserved here as an unexplained document artifact, not interpretive wording.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NEPTUNE/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source reference: printed p. `328` (entry).

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased needs to educate or enlighten yourself about processes which cause change; learning how to create ways to gain control or power; helps you gather thoughts and information about transforming your lifestyle.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: intuitive perception, hidden thought, and changing or confused understanding.
- psychology: powerful fantasies, influenced thinking, and learning about change are explicit.
- body/health: Ebertin's sensitive nerves and nervous weakness are historical medical language, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: keeping secrets and communication too subtle for others are stated.
- events/manifestations: partly understood news, slow shifts in thinking, and gathering information about lifestyle change are named.
- conflicts/notes: the source's isolated 3 Highlighters- label is a document artifact; investigative diagnosis and conspiratorial thinking are not established.

## Contradictions

Witte includes intuition and secrecy; Ebertin emphasizes influenced or unrealizable thinking. Sandbach pairs subtle communication with confusion, and Munkasey emphasizes learning about change and power.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Pluto = Mercury, perception and communication concern change that may be subtle, hidden, or only partly understood. [Witte](#witte-entry) names intuition, secrets, and a slow transformation in thought; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes subtle communication and powerful but confused fantasies. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) adds thinking under outside influence, while [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) describes gathering information about change. [ABC](#abc-entry) supplies discussion of uncertain and future developments; the entry does not establish diagnostic skill or conspiratorial thinking.
