## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Mysterious persons. Persons who slowly and unnoticeable change their attitude towards the native. It can also mean the public or especially women who change their attitude.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

dissolving developments of a people, feigned emotional change, creeping emotional changes, weak emotional unfolding, woman developing sensitivity, uncertain development of a people, and growing expectations of a woman

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Strange soul experiences, highly sensitive, changes of mood, an immediate response to every influence. A peculiar or strange woman, supernatural experiences.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One who is extremely emotionally sensitive and extremely impressionable to subtle energies, which could cause strange health problems, asthma and allergic reac tions being among them. A person who derives security from extreme or profound fan tasies, or who feels emotionally attached to intense ideals.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Increases the care you bring to transforming the lives of self or others for their emotional betterment; adds to your need to purchase devices, drugs, or other means to transform your present reality ideas; needs for short escapes.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Pluto = Moon`: profound intuition, healing depth, and the capacity to feel collective or ancestral undercurrents strongly. Main caution: psychic overwhelm: the emotional life can become compulsive, flooded, or quietly eroded by invisible pressures.
