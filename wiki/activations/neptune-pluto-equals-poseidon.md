## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `246` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Unnoticeable spiritual transformation and development. Secret wisdom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `391`, PDF p. `194` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disappointing cultural development, developing insights about the future, pretending growth of mind, cultural development of the future, weak unfolding of reason, refined development of mind, and developing increasing clarity

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: unnoticed spiritual or mental development, with growing clarity and pretended growth among the possibilities.
- psychology: weak unfolding of reason and refinement of mind are explicit.
- body/health: no health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: cultural development is named without a defined relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: secret wisdom, future cultural development, and disappointing cultural change are stated.
- conflicts/notes: the source does not specify ideological influence, suggestion, or an unconscious mechanism.

## Contradictions

Witte describes unnoticed spiritual transformation and secret wisdom. ABC also includes pretended growth, weak reasoning, and disappointing cultural development alongside greater clarity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Pluto = Poseidon, subtle development is considered through understanding, mind, and culture. [Witte](#witte-entry) names unnoticed spiritual transformation and secret wisdom; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds refinement and increasing clarity, but also pretended growth of mind and weak unfolding of reason (printed p. 391, PDF p. 194). The entry distinguishes development from its appearance without supplying a mechanism of suggestion or ideological influence.
