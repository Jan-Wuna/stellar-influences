## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `246` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

A secret development which by force strives for a decision and thus becomes public. In therapy: bursting of an abscess. In sport: the final spurt.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `391`, PDF p. `194` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Unfolding influence through sensitivity, developing strength from intuitions, unrest through developing violence, transformation of water-energy, uncertainty through growing violence, changes through the influence of water, and pretending growing power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: hidden development pressing toward a decision or public manifestation.
- psychology: intuition and unrest are explicit.
- body/health: Witte's abscess example is historical medical analogy, not treatment guidance or an individual forecast.
- social/relationship: influence through sensitivity, developing violence, and pretended power are named.
- events/manifestations: a secret development becoming public, a final sporting spurt, and water-energy transformation are stated.
- conflicts/notes: the medical, sporting, and power examples are distinct; the source does not make all hidden development end in violent rupture.

## Contradictions

Witte describes pressure toward a decision and supplies medical and sporting examples. ABC includes sensitivity, uncertainty through violence, water, and pretended growing power.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Pluto = Vulcanus, hidden development can press toward a decision or visible result. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives this theme through a development becoming public and a final sporting spurt, alongside a historical medical example. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds influence through sensitivity, strength from intuition, uncertainty through growing violence, and pretended power (printed p. 391, PDF p. 194). The entries preserve pressure and influence without making every change a violent rupture.
