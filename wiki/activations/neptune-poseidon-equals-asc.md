## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `254` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Others who follow the wrong faith, who advocate the erroneous faith.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `406`, PDF p. `202` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Clear water at the location, clarity through the partner's intuition, cultural place of the future, place of culture by the water, weak reason of the partner, unclear partner ideas, refinement of the partner's culture, and spiritualization of the surroundings through metaphysics

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: belief, reasoning, or clarity encountered through other people and surroundings.
- psychology: partner intuition, unclear ideas, and weak reasoning are explicit.
- body/health: clear water at a place is a literal example, without a medical claim.
- social/relationship: a partner's ideas and culture are named; Witte judges others' faith erroneous.
- events/manifestations: cultural settings by water and metaphysically framed surroundings are stated.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's wrong-faith judgement is historical attribution, not a basis for declaring a person's beliefs false or sincere.

## Contradictions

Witte supplies a negative judgement of others' faith. ABC also includes clarity, intuition, and cultural refinement alongside unclear ideas.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Poseidon = Asc, questions of belief and understanding arise through the partner or surroundings. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes others advocating what he calls erroneous faith; [ABC](#abc-entry) also includes clarity through a partner's intuition, weak reasoning, and unclear ideas (printed p. 406, PDF p. 202). The contrast is between clarity and uncertain understanding in contact, without adopting the source's historical judgement of another person's faith.
