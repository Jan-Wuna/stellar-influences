## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `254` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Associations of people with refined mentality or occur interests. Highly spiritual art. Attending theatres. Motion pictures about art and artists.

- Source wording note: actual PDF p. 254 prints `occur interests`. The intended word is not established; it is retained without silently substituting `occult`.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `407`, PDF p. `202` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Refined mental community, community of mind of the future, shared pretence of truth, clear water for a community, clarity through shared sensitivity, weakness of reason in a community, and unclear community of ideas

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: shared refined interests, spiritual art, and community ideas that may be clear or pretended.
- psychology: shared sensitivity and weak reasoning are explicit.
- body/health: clear water for a community is a literal example without a health claim.
- social/relationship: associations, theatrical attendance, and a community of ideas are named.
- events/manifestations: art films, spiritual art, and shared pretence of truth are stated.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's printed occur interests is unresolved; it does not support confidently substituting occult interests.

## Contradictions

Witte describes associations and spiritual art. ABC adds both shared clarity and collective pretence or unclear ideas, without making all artistic communities deceptive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Poseidon = Cupido, refinement and shared ideas receive a communal or artistic setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) names associations, spiritual art, theatre, and art films; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds shared sensitivity and clarity alongside pretended truth and weak reasoning in a community (printed p. 407, PDF p. 202). These distinguish shared refinement from the reliability of the ideas it supports; the unresolved printed phrase occur interests is not expanded into doctrine.
