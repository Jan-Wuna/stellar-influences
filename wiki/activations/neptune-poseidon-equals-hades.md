## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `254` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

False concept of the world caused by stupidity or stubbornness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `407`, PDF p. `202` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disadvantage through weakness of mind, danger through renouncing reason, lack of clear water, sorrow through denial of truth, unclear ideas from the past, hidden metaphysical truth, and refinement of culture from the past

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: unclear or denied understanding, with disadvantage and sorrow among the expressions.
- psychology: stubbornness, weak reasoning, and denial of truth are source themes.
- body/health: weakness of mind is historical evaluative language, not a clinical finding.
- social/relationship: no specific relationship pattern is supplied.
- events/manifestations: past ideas, hidden metaphysical truth, and lack of clear water are named.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's stupidity label is attributed; the passage does not justify assessing a person's intelligence or define all past culture as erroneous.

## Contradictions

Witte assigns false worldview to stupidity or stubbornness. ABC also includes hidden truth and refined past culture alongside unclear inherited ideas and denied reason.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Poseidon = Hades, the sources concern difficulties in reasoning and facing truth. [Witte](#witte-entry) uses the historical judgements of stupidity or stubbornness; [ABC](#abc-entry) names renouncing reason, denying truth, and unclear ideas from the past, but also hidden metaphysical truth and cultural refinement (printed p. 407, PDF p. 202). The entry can describe this tension without endorsing a judgement of a person's intelligence.
