## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `254` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be fortunate in highly placed mental matters. To remain hidden. To successfully pretend.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `406`, PDF p. `202` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disappointing legal truth, disappointing financial truths, successful cultural refinement, false money-culture, joy through metaphysical truths, success of mind through intuition, happiness of mind through sensitivity, and clarity through successful intuition

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: success or happiness in refined understanding alongside pretence and disappointing truths.
- psychology: joy, intuition, sensitivity, and clarity are explicit.
- body/health: no health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: successful pretence and false money-culture are source examples, without a defined relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: cultural refinement and disappointing legal or financial truth are named.
- conflicts/notes: success and concealment are separate Witte expressions; intuitive clarity is a source claim rather than proof that a belief is correct.

## Contradictions

Witte includes fortune, concealment, and successful pretence. ABC also includes intuitive success and cultural refinement alongside disappointing legal or financial truth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Poseidon = Jupiter, refinement or intuitive understanding may be associated with satisfaction and success, while truth remains a separate question. [Witte](#witte-entry) names both fortune in mental matters and successful pretence; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds cultural refinement and clarity alongside disappointing legal or financial truths (printed p. 406, PDF p. 202). Prosperity or happiness therefore does not itself verify what is claimed.
