## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `254` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

A great pretender who appears or wants to give the impression of great importance.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `407`, PDF p. `202` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Special clarity through intuition, expectations of the geistige Hierarchie (hierarchy of mind), authority of mind of the future, weak reason of state, self-confidence through metaphysical insights, denial of state ideas, and important foreign culture

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: claimed importance or authority considered alongside clarity, confidence, and uncertain reasoning.
- psychology: self-confidence through metaphysical insight and special intuitive clarity are explicit.
- body/health: no health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: state ideas, reason of state, authority, and foreign culture are named.
- events/manifestations: pretended importance and future mental authority are stated.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's geistige Hierarchie is unexplained and retained literally outside confident synthesis; asserted importance is not proof of insight.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes pretending importance. ABC also describes confidence and clarity, while retaining weak state reasoning and denied state ideas.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Poseidon = Kronos, the question of understanding is expressed through importance or authority. [Witte](#witte-entry) names a pretender seeking to appear important; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds self-confidence through metaphysical insight, special clarity, weak reason of state, and authority (printed p. 407, PDF p. 202). These distinguish a claim to importance from the clarity of its content; the unexplained hierarchy phrase is not developed into a system.
