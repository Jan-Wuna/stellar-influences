## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `254` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To refuse a doctrine, a faith, an idea, or the concept of the world for moral reasons. To withdraw from something under false pretext.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `406`, PDF p. `202` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Work in clear water, efforts toward metaphysical truth, future work of mind, refining actions from insight, weak activity of mind, weak impulses of reason, refined cultural efforts, and future cultural work

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: action, refusal, or withdrawal in relation to ideas and understanding.
- psychology: moral reasons, false pretext, and weak reasoning are explicit.
- body/health: work in clear water is a literal example without a health claim.
- social/relationship: refusing doctrine or withdrawing under a pretext are stated.
- events/manifestations: metaphysical efforts, refining action from insight, and future cultural work are named.
- conflicts/notes: moral refusal and false pretext are separate possibilities; the source does not make every principled withdrawal insincere.

## Contradictions

Witte pairs moral refusal with withdrawal under a false pretext. ABC also describes constructive cultural efforts and refinement alongside weak mental activity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Poseidon = Mars, ideas and beliefs become matters for action or refusal. [Witte](#witte-entry) names rejecting a doctrine for moral reasons and withdrawing under a false pretext as distinct expressions. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds efforts toward metaphysical truth, refining action from insight, weak reasoning, and future cultural work (printed p. 406, PDF p. 202). The entry therefore preserves both purposeful effort and uncertain or insincere action without equating them.
