## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `254` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To look into the mirror, to regard the reflected image, knowledge of oneself. Introspection. To be under the influence of a wrong view of life. To be considered a follower of the wrong faith.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `406`, PDF p. `202` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Soulful insights into the future, my mind in foreign lands, my insights about water, my future of mind, my unknown ideas, my clarity through intuition, my reason is weak, refined culture of the soul, and refinement of my insights

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: self-reflection and understanding of one's beliefs, with clarity and error both present.
- psychology: introspection, self-knowledge, unknown ideas, and weak reasoning are explicit.
- body/health: no specific health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: being regarded as following a wrong faith concerns others' perception of the person.
- events/manifestations: looking in a mirror, insights about water, and refinement of understanding are named.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's wrong-faith language is a historical judgement; introspection does not prove that an idea is true.

## Contradictions

Witte pairs introspection with mistaken views or others' religious judgement. ABC adds intuitive clarity, refined insights, and weak reason without resolving that tension.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Poseidon = MC, reflection on oneself includes the beliefs through which life is understood. [Witte](#witte-entry) names mirrors, self-knowledge, and introspection alongside influence by a view he calls wrong; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds intuitive clarity, unknown ideas, and weak reason (printed p. 406, PDF p. 202). The entry concerns the relation between self-understanding and clarity, without adopting a judgement of the person's faith.
