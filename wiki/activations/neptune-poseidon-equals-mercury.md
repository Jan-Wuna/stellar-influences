## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `254` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Thoughts about fine mental things, debates about them. To accept thoughts intuitively. Wrong information or insights.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `406`, PDF p. `202` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Movement in clear water, insight and sensitivity of youth, disappointing truth in youth, thoughts about the mind of the future, meeting in clear air, refinement of conversational culture, weakness of reason in Verkehr, and news pretending truth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: intuitive thought and discussion of subtle matters alongside misleading information.
- psychology: accepting thoughts intuitively and disappointing truth in youth are explicit.
- body/health: movement in clear water and meeting in clear air are literal examples, not health claims.
- social/relationship: debate, conversational refinement, and news pretending truth are named.
- events/manifestations: thinking about the future and receiving wrong information are stated.
- conflicts/notes: Verkehr remains ambiguous between traffic and interaction; intuition is not established as a test of accuracy.

## Contradictions

Witte names subtle debate and intuitive acceptance alongside wrong insights. ABC adds refined conversation and sensitivity, but also news that only pretends to be true.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Poseidon = Mercury, subtle ideas may be discussed or accepted intuitively while their accuracy remains uncertain. [Witte](#witte-entry) names debate, intuitive acceptance, and wrong information; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds conversational refinement, disappointing truth, and news pretending truth (printed p. 406, PDF p. 202). The common issue is the relation between intuitive understanding and reliable communication, without assuming that refinement protects against error.
