## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `254` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Persons or the public who labor under a delusion, who are under the influence of erroneous beliefs.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `406`, PDF p. `202` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

disappointing hour of truth, emotional clarity through intuition, culture of a people by the water, unclear ideas of a woman, uncertain future of a people, refinement of emotional clarity, refinement of a cultured people, and weakness of reason of a woman

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: emotional clarity or uncertainty of belief in persons or the public.
- psychology: disappointing truth, intuitive emotional clarity, and unclear ideas are explicit.
- body/health: the source's delusion and weak-reason labels are historical descriptions, not clinical findings.
- social/relationship: woman-specific and people-specific descriptions remain attributed.
- events/manifestations: a culture by water, uncertain collective future, and refinement of emotional clarity are named.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's clarity contrasts with Witte's erroneous beliefs; the source does not state that people experience all errors as meaningful truth.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes delusion and erroneous belief. ABC also describes intuitive emotional clarity and refinement alongside unclear ideas and weak reasoning.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Poseidon = Moon, belief and clarity are considered through feeling and collective or woman-specific experience. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes people or a public influenced by erroneous beliefs; [ABC](#abc-entry) also names intuitive emotional clarity, disappointing truth, and refined feeling (printed p. 406, PDF p. 202). Both clarity and error remain available in the comparison, while the historical labels do not establish a clinical judgement.
