## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `254` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

One’s own inner revolution. To rid oneself of false concepts and to turn to the truth. To suddenly realize the facts. To gain surprising new knowledge of highly mental occult connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `407`, PDF p. `202` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

excitement through unclear ideas, dynamic culture of the future, disappointing cultural event, clarity through intuitive impulses, new ideas through sensitivity, surprising pretense of truth, restless weak mind, and uncertainty through weakness of reason

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sudden revision of belief or insight alongside unclear ideas and unrest.
- psychology: inner revolution, new ideas, and surprising pretence of truth are explicit.
- body/health: weak-mind language is historical, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: no distinct relationship pattern is supplied.
- events/manifestations: sudden recognition of facts and a disappointing cultural event are named.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's turn toward truth contrasts with ABC's pretended truth; an apparent revelation does not prove accuracy.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes leaving false concepts and recognizing facts. ABC also retains unclear ideas, a surprising pretence of truth, and uncertainty through weak reasoning.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Poseidon = Uranus, understanding may change suddenly. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes an inner revolution, abandoning false concepts, and recognizing facts; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds new ideas and intuitive clarity but also unclear ideas, unrest, and surprising pretence of truth (printed p. 407, PDF p. 202). The comparison preserves the difference between a sudden insight and an appearance of truth.
