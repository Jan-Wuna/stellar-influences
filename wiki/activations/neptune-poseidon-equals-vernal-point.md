## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `254` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The general false faith. The generality has the wrong attitude, it succumbs to great errors.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `406`, PDF p. `202` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disappointing truth in public, mind of the future in public life, clear water for the public, public truth about metaphysics, weak reason in public, and unclear ideas in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: public belief, reasoning, and disappointing or unclear truth claims.
- psychology: public error and unclear ideas are explicit.
- body/health: clear water for the public is a literal example without a health claim.
- social/relationship: collective attitudes and public metaphysical claims are named.
- events/manifestations: public-life ideas and disappointing truth are stated.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's general false-faith judgement is attributed; no specific public belief or social group is identified.

## Contradictions

Witte judges general attitudes mistaken. ABC specifies public reasoning and truth claims, together with a separate clear-water example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Poseidon = Vernal Point, belief and understanding are considered in public life. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a general attitude he calls erroneous; [ABC](#abc-entry) names disappointing truth, weak public reasoning, and unclear ideas (printed p. 406, PDF p. 202). These preserve the theme of public uncertainty without identifying a particular community's beliefs as false; clear water for the public remains a distinct literal example.
