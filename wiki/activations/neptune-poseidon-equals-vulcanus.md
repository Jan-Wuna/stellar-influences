## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `254` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Mighty delusion or great accomplishments in highly spiritual or occult fields.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `407`, PDF p. `202` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Influence of metaphysical insights, influential idea of the future, immense weakness of mind, subtle-material power of mind, clarity about the energy of water, powerful clarity through intuition, unknown energies of mind, and cultural power of the future

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: great spiritual or occult accomplishment contrasted with great delusion.
- psychology: intuition, clarity, and weakness of mind are explicit.
- body/health: mental power and unknown energies are historical source terms, without a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: influence of metaphysical insights and an influential future idea are named.
- events/manifestations: cultural power of the future and clarity about water-energy are stated.
- conflicts/notes: Witte presents delusion and accomplishment as alternatives; strength of belief does not distinguish them.

## Contradictions

Witte directly contrasts delusion with accomplishment. ABC adds both influential clarity and immense weakness, without an independent criterion for judging either.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Poseidon = Vulcanus, the scale of a spiritual or occult claim does not settle its quality. [Witte](#witte-entry) contrasts mighty delusion with great accomplishment; [ABC](#abc-entry) likewise places powerful intuitive clarity and influence beside immense weakness of mind (printed p. 407, PDF p. 202). Its unknown-energy language remains attributed, without supplying a mechanism or a way to infer truth from intensity.
