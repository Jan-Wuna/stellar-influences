## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Mighty changes of the present forms of living or being.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `405`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

dissolution of blocking energies, depression and disappointment through force, restriction of unknown force, consolidation of subtle energies, thorough influence of metaphysics, rotational energy of the wind, and solidification of fluid energy

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: major changes in forms of living or being, alongside constrained or consolidated energy.
- psychology: depression and disappointment through force are named.
- body/health: depression is a historical claim here, not an individual diagnosis or a causal finding.
- social/relationship: no specific relationship pattern is supplied.
- events/manifestations: dissolving blocking energies, wind rotation, and solidifying fluid energy are literal examples.
- conflicts/notes: unknown force and subtle energies are unexplained source terms; defensive withdrawal or fragility is not established.

## Contradictions

Witte's general changes of condition are broader than ABC's particular force and energy examples; the entries do not provide one psychological cause.

## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Admetos, the stated concern is a major change of condition. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes changes in present forms of living or being; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds dissolution, restriction or consolidation of energy, together with wind and fluid-energy examples (printed p. 405, PDF p. 201). These literal and historical expressions do not establish an inner pressure mechanism or a characteristic response of defensive withdrawal.
