## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To experience through others a great deception or disappointment. To be deprived through others of influence and power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `404`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

disappointing influence of the partner, subtle energies in the environment, negation of force by the partner, tremendous deception in the partner, place of power in the water, weak strength of the partner, strong intuition of the partner, and water power at a place

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: influence or disappointment experienced through another person or the surroundings.
- psychology: great deception and strong partner intuition are explicit.
- body/health: the partner's weak strength is historical descriptive language, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: loss of power through others and disappointing partner influence are named.
- events/manifestations: water power at a place and subtle energies in surroundings are stated.
- conflicts/notes: loss of influence is a source possibility; external pressure or deceptive relationships are not a universal explanation.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes deception and deprivation of power through others. ABC also gives strong intuition and literal place or water examples alongside weakness.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Asc, another person or the surroundings becomes the setting for uncertain influence. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes deception, disappointment, and power taken away through others; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointing partner influence, weak strength, and strong intuition (printed p. 404, PDF p. 201). This supports a comparison of influence and disappointment in contact, without assuming every relationship undermines the person or explaining the literal water-power examples psychologically.
