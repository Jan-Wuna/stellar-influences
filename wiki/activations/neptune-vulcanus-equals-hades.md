## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be powerless in the face of misery, worries and lack. To be unable to cope with dangers and bad forces.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `405`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

dissolution of criminal force, danger through water force, dangerous subtle influence, mysterious dissolution of energy, deficient energy of water, disadvantageous unknown influence, unknown influence from the past, and renunciation of wind power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: powerlessness before hardship or danger, with adverse or unknown influence among the examples.
- psychology: worries and inability to cope are explicit.
- body/health: danger through water force and energy dissolution are historical hazard language, not an individual diagnosis.
- social/relationship: misery, lack, and criminal force are source settings without a specific relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: unknown past influence, water-energy deficiency, and renouncing wind power are named.
- conflicts/notes: the source does not establish an invisible corrosive mechanism or a loss of confidence in every case.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes helplessness in distress. ABC also lists literal energy and water examples as well as criminal or unknown influence, without supplying a common cause.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Hades, the sources describe difficulty meeting hardship or danger with sufficient power. [Witte](#witte-entry) names misery, worry, lack, and inability to cope; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds dangerous or unknown influence and water-related force (printed p. 405, PDF p. 201). These remain bounded expressions of vulnerability, while the energy language is not expanded into a physical or psychological mechanism.
