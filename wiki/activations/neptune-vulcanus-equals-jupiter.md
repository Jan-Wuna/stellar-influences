## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be pleasantly disappointed. Unexpected easily earned money, also money losses.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `404`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

successful influence of metaphysics, successful dissolution of force, successful air power, money through water power, powerful joy in the air, weak financial power, weak legal power, strong legal uncertainty, and uncertain money power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: unexpected financial gain or loss, with successful influence and uncertainty both present.
- psychology: pleasant surprise and powerful joy are explicit.
- body/health: no medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: weak financial or legal power is stated without a relationship type.
- events/manifestations: easy money, money loss, water power, and successful air power are examples.
- conflicts/notes: gain and loss are alternatives; the source does not establish rapid reversal or a universal pattern of unreliable benefit.

## Contradictions

Witte includes both pleasant surprise and financial loss. ABC likewise places successful influence beside weak financial power and legal uncertainty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Jupiter, favorable influence or unexpected gain coexists with financial uncertainty. [Witte](#witte-entry) names pleasant disappointment, easy money, and money loss; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful influence, money through water power, weak financial power, and strong legal uncertainty (printed p. 404, PDF p. 201). These are contrasting possibilities, without a prescribed gain-and-reversal sequence.
