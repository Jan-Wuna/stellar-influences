## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The authorities fail in their leadership. Powerlessness of the boss or the government. The authorities are unable to carry on.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `405`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

special subtle energy, great influence of the water, dissolutions of power in the state, powerful uncertainty in the state, weak state power, state air power, unknown influence of the hierarchy, and important influence of metaphysics

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: authority whose effective power is weak, dissolving, or uncertain.
- psychology: great uncertainty in the state is explicit.
- body/health: subtle-energy language supplies no medical claim.
- social/relationship: bosses, government, state power, and hierarchy are named.
- events/manifestations: failed leadership, state air power, and influence of water or metaphysics are stated.
- conflicts/notes: the unspecified hierarchy and energy examples do not explain a hidden cause for every leadership failure.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes inability to lead or continue. ABC adds state uncertainty and power dissolution alongside specific water, air, and metaphysical influences.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Kronos, effective authority is the central concern. [Witte](#witte-entry) names failed leadership and powerlessness of a boss or government; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds weak state power, dissolution of power, and great state uncertainty (printed p. 405, PDF p. 201). Its hierarchy and metaphysical-influence language remains unspecified rather than becoming an explanation for an actual government's condition.
