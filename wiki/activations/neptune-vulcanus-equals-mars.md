## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Suspension of activity, might or influence. Destruction of great values. Forces, which can be very destructive.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `404`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Actions of an air power, work with subtle energy, work with water force, influential activity in the air, weak labor force, strong intuition impulses, activity with fluid energy, and uncertainty of labor force

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: activity or influence that may be suspended or destructive, with energy-related work also present.
- psychology: strong intuition impulses are explicit.
- body/health: weak labor force or work power is descriptive source language, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: an air power and influential work are named without a relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: suspended activity, destruction of values, and work with water or fluid energy are stated.
- conflicts/notes: ordinary work examples are not all destructive; the entry does not say every strong energy breaks down existing power.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes suspended or destructive force. ABC also includes influential activity and work involving subtle, fluid, or water energy.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Mars, the effectiveness and direction of action are in question. [Witte](#witte-entry) names suspended activity and destructive force; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds weak work power and uncertainty alongside influential activity, intuition impulses, and water-related work (printed p. 404, PDF p. 201). The comparison keeps destruction distinct from ordinary work with the listed forms of energy.
