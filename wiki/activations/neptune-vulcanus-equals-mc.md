## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Easy to deceive or defraud. To experience or feel a great disappointment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `404`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of my influence, I release or dissolve energies, I reject violence, my breathing strength, my subtle-material energies, my strength in water, my intuitive strength, my weak strength, my unknown influence, and refinement of the soul's strength

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal susceptibility to deception or disappointment alongside uncertain influence and strength.
- psychology: great disappointment and intuitive strength are explicit.
- body/health: breathing strength and strength of soul are historical descriptions without a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: being deceived or defrauded and rejecting violence are stated.
- events/manifestations: releasing or dissolving energies and dissolution of personal influence are named.
- conflicts/notes: the energy terms are unexplained; the source does not establish a life-direction mechanism.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes being deceived or disappointed. ABC also gives personal rejection of violence and intuitive strength alongside weak strength and dissolved influence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = MC, uncertain influence and strength are experienced personally. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes susceptibility to deception or fraud and great disappointment; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds dissolution of influence, weak strength, intuitive strength, and rejection of violence (printed p. 404, PDF p. 201). Its energy and soul-strength language remains attributed rather than becoming a mechanism for the person's life direction.
