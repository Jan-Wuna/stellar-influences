## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Mighty intuitive thinking. Also, to be greatly mistaken in opinions and remarks.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `404`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

dissolution of youthful violence, influence of metaphysics on youth, influence of sensitive thoughts, subtle energy exchange, forceful movement in water, strong air traffic, strong insecurity of youth, and movements of water forces

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: strong intuitive thinking that may nevertheless produce seriously mistaken opinions.
- psychology: intuition, sensitive thoughts, and insecurity of youth are explicit.
- body/health: the energy and water-force examples supply no individual health claim.
- social/relationship: opinions, remarks, and influences on youth are named.
- events/manifestations: dissolution of youthful violence, air traffic, and forceful water movement are stated.
- conflicts/notes: strength of an intuitive impression does not establish its accuracy; no probability of error is given.

## Contradictions

Witte explicitly joins powerful intuition with potentially great error. ABC supplies sensitive thinking and youth or traffic examples without resolving their accuracy.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Mercury, the force of intuitive thinking is separate from its reliability. [Witte](#witte-entry) places mighty intuitive thought beside serious mistakes in opinions or remarks; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds sensitive thoughts and insecurity of youth, with literal traffic and water-force examples (printed p. 404, PDF p. 201). The passage does not provide a rule for inferring truth or the likelihood of error from the strength of an impression.
