## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Women, the public or persons who are greatly deceived and feel the deception.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `404`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

influence of metaphysics on a people, subtle energies of the woman, woman denying force, air power of a people, weakness of power of a people, powerful uncertainty of a woman, strong emotional deceptions, and unknown emotional influence

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: felt deception or uncertainty in persons, women, or the public.
- psychology: emotional deception and great uncertainty are explicit.
- body/health: subtle energy of the woman is historical language without a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: woman-specific and collective descriptions remain source attributions.
- events/manifestations: weak public power, air power of a people, and unknown emotional influence are named.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's people who feel deception are not a universal description of every surrounding person or the native.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes people who are deceived and feel it. ABC also supplies collective power and woman-specific examples alongside unknown emotional influence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Moon, deception is described through its felt impact on people. [Witte](#witte-entry) names women, a public, or persons who experience and feel great deception; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds emotional deception and uncertainty alongside collective and woman-specific power expressions (printed p. 404, PDF p. 201). These are attributed possibilities, without assigning the same disillusionment to every person involved.
