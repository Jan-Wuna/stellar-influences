## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Maneuvers of deception. To deceive others successfully.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `404`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a strong connection, subtle-material energy connection, connecting fluid energies, rejection of violence in a relationship, connection with wind energies, connection to water force, and great uncertainty in a connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: connections involving deceptive maneuvers, dissolution, or great uncertainty.
- psychology: no separate motive is supplied beyond Witte's deliberate deception.
- body/health: energy connections are historical source terms without a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: successfully deceiving others and rejecting violence within a relationship are named.
- events/manifestations: dissolution of a strong tie and wind or water-force connections are stated.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's rejection of violence and energy examples do not make every connection a calculated deception.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes deceptive maneuvers. ABC also includes rejecting violence, strong ties dissolving, and literal energy connections.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Node, influence enters the terms and reliability of a connection. [Witte](#witte-entry) names deceptive maneuvers and successfully misleading others; [ABC](#abc-entry) also includes dissolution of a strong tie, great uncertainty, and rejection of violence in a relationship (printed p. 404, PDF p. 201). Deliberate deception is therefore one stated expression rather than the assumed purpose of every connection.
