## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Unnoticeable initiation of great changes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `405`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Influential development through metaphysics, unfolding air power, unfolding water force, developing subtle-material energies, rejecting the development of violence, change through tremendous deceptions, and growing influence of intuition

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: great changes beginning unnoticed, with developing influence and power among the expressions.
- psychology: growing intuition and great deception are explicit.
- body/health: subtle-material energy is historical language without a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: rejecting developing violence is stated without a particular relationship type.
- events/manifestations: development through metaphysics, air power, and water force are named.
- conflicts/notes: the entry does not specify a timetable or a universal mechanism of hidden transformation.

## Contradictions

Witte names the unnoticed start of great changes. ABC supplies varied forms of development, rejection, and deceptive change rather than one necessary progression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Pluto, important changes may begin without being noticed. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies that emphasis; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds developing influence, air and water power, rejection of developing violence, and change through great deception (printed p. 405, PDF p. 201). Its growing intuition and metaphysical-development language remains attributed, without making every powerful development deceptive or defining its timetable.
