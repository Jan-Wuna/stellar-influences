## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be separated or secluded for a long and indefinite duration. Long prison term. Indefinite captivity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `405`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

energy loss of the water, concentration on subtle energies, powerful uncertainty in old age, problematic influence of metaphysics, problems through weakness of power, creeping loss of power, painful negation of force, and strong loss of water

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: prolonged restriction, separation, or loss of effective power.
- psychology: uncertainty in old age and concentration are explicit.
- body/health: water loss and pain are source examples, without a medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: long captivity or seclusion is Witte's severe relationship-to-society example.
- events/manifestations: creeping power loss and problems concerning metaphysical influence are named.
- conflicts/notes: indefinite captivity is a historical possibility, not a forecast of imprisonment or a defined duration.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes long separation or captivity. ABC describes declining power, uncertainty, and water or energy loss without identifying one cause for confinement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Saturn, restriction or loss is described as enduring rather than momentary. [Witte](#witte-entry) names prolonged separation, seclusion, and indefinite captivity; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds creeping power loss, weakness of power, and great uncertainty (printed p. 405, PDF p. 201). The literal water-loss examples remain separate, and the severe confinement wording does not establish an individual prison prediction.
