## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Great physical delicacy (of feelings). To be physically without strength, to succumb. The man who feels weak and without spirit, who does not want to or cannot pull himself together.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `404`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

influential person in the air, subtle energy of a person, tremendous personal deception, powerful uncertainty of a person, personal negation of force, strong personal intuition, and unknown personal influence

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal weakness or delicacy alongside uncertain influence and strong intuition.
- psychology: feeling weak and without spirit is Witte's explicit description.
- body/health: Witte's physical weakness and ABC's subtle energy are historical claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: the gendered man-specific wording remains attributed; no relationship pattern is supplied.
- events/manifestations: personal deception, uncertainty, and unknown influence are named.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also includes strong intuition and influential presence; weakness is not a universal assessment of vitality or morale.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes weakness and inability or unwillingness to rally. ABC also gives influential presence and strong intuition alongside uncertainty and deception.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Sun, personal strength is considered alongside uncertainty and influence. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes physical delicacy and a man who feels unable to rally; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds personal deception, uncertainty, strong intuition, and unknown influence (printed p. 404, PDF p. 201). These historical descriptions remain attributed rather than becoming a universal assessment of the person's health or morale.
