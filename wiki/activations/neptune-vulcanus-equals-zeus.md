## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `253` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To revolt in vain against force and necessities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `405`, PDF p. `201` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

beginning influence of metaphysics, fiery fluid energy, shaping of subtle energy, performance power of the water, weak energy performance, weak leadership influence, strong intuitive performance, and uncertainty through fire power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: effort against force or necessity that may prove ineffective, alongside varying achievement.
- psychology: revolt and intuitive performance are explicit.
- body/health: fiery-fluid energy and fire power are source terms without an individual medical claim.
- social/relationship: weak leadership influence is stated.
- events/manifestations: futile revolt, shaping energy, and the performance power of water are named.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also includes strong intuitive achievement; the source does not make every effort rebellious or ineffective.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes vain revolt against force and necessity. ABC also includes shaping energy and strong intuitive performance beside weak achievement and uncertainty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Neptune/Vulcanus = Zeus, effort is described in relation to force, necessity, and achievement. [Witte](#witte-entry) names a vain revolt; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds weak energy performance and leadership influence, but also strong intuitive performance and shaping subtle energy (printed p. 405, PDF p. 201). These are differing expressions of effectiveness, without making all effort futile or providing a mechanism for the energy terms.
