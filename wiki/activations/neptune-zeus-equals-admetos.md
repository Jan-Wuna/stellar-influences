## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `249` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To work unsuccessfully on a small scale or in a confined situation. To be forced to produce under very difficult circumstances and under grave pressure.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `397`, PDF p. `197` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Enduring shaping of the future, beginning dissolution of a blockade, restricted future goals, disappointing restriction of performance, consolidation through unknown guidance, deepening into metaphysical goals, and creating a backlog of water

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: production under confinement or grave pressure, with endurance and restricted goals.
- psychology: persistence and deepening into metaphysical goals are named.
- body/health: no specific diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Witte describes being forced to produce; ABC names unknown guidance.
- events/manifestations: unsuccessful small-scale work, a loosening blockade and backed-up water are separate expressions.
- conflicts/notes: endurance and the start of dissolution prevent treating confinement as necessarily permanent.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses unsuccessful, pressured production. ABC adds endurance and the beginning dissolution of a blockade alongside restrictions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Zeus = Admetos places sustained effort within narrow or difficult conditions. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes pressured production and unsuccessful small-scale work; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds enduring future plans, restricted goals, and a blockade beginning to dissolve (printed p. 397, PDF p. 197). The emphasis can therefore include persistence and a changing restriction, without promising either success or permanent confinement. Backed-up water and metaphysical goals remain distinct source examples.
