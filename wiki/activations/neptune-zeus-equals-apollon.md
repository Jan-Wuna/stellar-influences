## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `249` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Creative accomplishments in a new science. Quantities of liquid fuels. Quantities of gas.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `397`, PDF p. `197` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of leadership weakness, successful design through intuition, trade in fire water, extensive leadership weakness, dissemination of procreation weakness, knowledge of an unknown leadership, and future goals of science

ABC's chapter heading glosses Feuer-Wasser as alcohol (printed p. 396, PDF p. 197); the entry's trade clause retains that source term.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creative scientific accomplishment alongside broad weakness in leadership.
- psychology: successful intuitive design and scientific future goals are explicit.
- body/health: ABC's procreation-weakness clause is historical source language, not a medical assessment.
- social/relationship: trade and widely extended leadership concerns are named.
- events/manifestations: new science, quantities of liquid fuel and gas, and trade in Feuer-Wasser appear.
- conflicts/notes: productive scientific work and leadership weakness coexist; one does not negate the other.

## Contradictions

Witte gives scientific accomplishment and fuel quantities. ABC combines successful intuitive design with extensive leadership weakness and historical reproductive language.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Zeus = Apollon joins creative scientific work with questions of scale and direction. [Witte](#witte-entry) names accomplishment in a new science and quantities of liquid fuel or gas; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful intuitive design and scientific goals alongside extensive leadership weakness (printed p. 397, PDF p. 197). These passages support both productive and impaired expressions. ABC's Feuer-Wasser trade clause uses a term its chapter heading glosses as alcohol; it does not make profit or failure inevitable.
