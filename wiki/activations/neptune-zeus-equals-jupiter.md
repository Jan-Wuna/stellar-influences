## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `249` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Unexpected gain. Productive oil wells. Luck in alcohol.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `396`, PDF p. `197` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Disappointing legal performance, successful leadership in the air, successful performance in water, joy over future goals, a bonfire of celebration by the water, good results in metaphysics, weak performance of money, and unclear financial leadership

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: unexpected gain and successful performance coexist with weak financial direction.
- psychology: joy in future goals is explicit.
- body/health: alcohol is an attributed historical example without medical guidance.
- social/relationship: legal performance and financial leadership can disappoint.
- events/manifestations: productive oil wells, work in air or water, and a celebratory fire by water are named.
- conflicts/notes: the entry does not extend luck to every volatile or subtle enterprise.

## Contradictions

Witte lists gain, oil and alcohol. ABC includes successful performance but also disappointing legal results and unclear financial leadership.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Zeus = Jupiter can describe gain or successful accomplishment without making every undertaking fortunate. [Witte](#witte-entry) names unexpected gain, productive oil wells and luck in alcohol. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful activity in air or water and joy in future goals, while retaining disappointing legal performance and unclear financial leadership (printed p. 396, PDF p. 197). The supported theme is mixed opportunity and direction, with the concrete oil, alcohol and water examples kept within their stated scope.
