## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `249` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Hot tempered. Pompous person. Moment of an unfortunate conception.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `396`, PDF p. `197` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

My efforts in water, my disappointing achievements, my leadership weakness, my intuitive shaping, my creative sensitivity, my unknown guidance, unknown soul guidance, and my future goals

The literal phrase 'unknown soul guidance' (unbekannte Seelen-Führung) has no further explanation in this entry.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal achievement, direction and intuitive creation, with possible disappointment.
- psychology: creative sensitivity stands beside Witte's hot-tempered and pompous descriptions.
- body/health: Witte's unfortunate-conception phrase is historical reproductive language, not a medical forecast.
- social/relationship: the personal leadership emphasis does not establish a public-facing role.
- events/manifestations: efforts in water and future goals are ABC's examples.
- conflicts/notes: unknown soul guidance remains an unexplained source phrase.

## Contradictions

Witte's temper and conception examples differ from ABC's personal sensitivity, leadership and future aims. Neither warrants a general judgement of ambition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Zeus = MC centers on one's own direction and accomplishments. [ABC](#abc-entry) places intuitive shaping and creative sensitivity beside disappointing achievements and weak leadership (printed p. 396, PDF p. 197). [Witte](#witte-entry) adds hot temper, pompousness and an unfortunate-conception example. Those attributed descriptions do not establish that the person's entire outward stance or ambition is misdirected; ABC's unexplained soul-guidance phrase remains outside the confident interpretation.
