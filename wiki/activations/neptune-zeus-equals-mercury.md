## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `249` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Spontaneous execution of peculiar ideas. To think of secret or concealed weapons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `396`, PDF p. `197` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Strenuous conversations about the future, reports on performance in water, shaping water traffic, news about weak performance, weak media performance, speaking about future goals, and discussing procreative weakness

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: unusual ideas and communication about performance or future plans.
- psychology: spontaneous execution of peculiar ideas is explicit.
- body/health: ABC discusses reproductive weakness; it does not diagnose the speaker.
- social/relationship: reports, conversation and media performance provide the communication setting.
- events/manifestations: water traffic and thoughts about concealed weapons are specific examples.
- conflicts/notes: thinking of secret weapons does not turn every future conversation into covert planning.

## Contradictions

Witte gives spontaneous unusual ideas and concealed weapons. ABC adds ordinary reports, media weakness and future conversations; the secrecy motif has a limited scope.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Zeus = Mercury concerns ideas put into action and the communication of plans or performance. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes spontaneous execution of peculiar ideas and thoughts of concealed weapons. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds strenuous future conversations, reports about work in water, water traffic and weak media performance (printed p. 396, PDF p. 197). These support unusual thinking and practical communication without making every discussion a secret scheme. Its reproductive clause concerns a topic of conversation, not a diagnosis.
