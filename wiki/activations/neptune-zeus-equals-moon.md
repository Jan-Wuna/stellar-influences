## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `249` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Creative imagination. Night hours sacrificed in vain. Persons who show off. Raving mad. Hour of futile creation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `396`, PDF p. `197` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Efforts of a people on or with water, beginning weakness of feeling, disappointing achievements of a woman, creative sensitivity of the woman, unclear aims of a people, refined emotional striving, and procreative weakness of a woman

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creative imagination and sensitivity alongside effort that may disappoint.
- psychology: refined emotional striving and beginning weakness of feeling both appear.
- body/health: Witte's stigmatizing madness phrase and ABC's reproductive claim are historical, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: the sources use women and a people as particular traditional referents.
- events/manifestations: night hours spent in vain and collective effort involving water are named.
- conflicts/notes: the passages do not establish that heightened imagination inevitably exhausts itself.

## Contradictions

Both creativity and futility are stated. ABC's refined feeling prevents reducing the entry to waste, while its gendered and reproductive wording requires attribution.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Zeus = Moon holds creative imagination beside effort that may not fulfil its aim. [Witte](#witte-entry) lists imagination and night hours spent in vain; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds creative sensitivity and refined emotional striving alongside disappointing achievements and unclear collective aims (printed p. 396, PDF p. 197). This supports a mixed picture of creation and uncertainty. The gendered, reproductive and stigmatizing mental-health phrases remain historical claims, without a diagnosis or an assumption that feeling must be exhausted.
