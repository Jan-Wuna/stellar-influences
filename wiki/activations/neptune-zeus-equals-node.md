## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `249` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Spontaneous union with consequent separation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `396`, PDF p. `197` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Strenuous connections in water, beginning dissolution of a connection, leadership weakness in a relationship, intuitive shaping of a connection, a creative connection to metaphysics, and connection with Feuer-Wasser

The chapter heading glosses Feuer-Wasser as alcohol (printed p. 396, PDF p. 197).

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: a connection forming spontaneously or beginning to dissolve.
- psychology: intuitive shaping and metaphysical creativity are explicit.
- body/health: the alcohol connection is a source example without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Witte's union followed by separation overlaps with ABC's dissolving connection.
- events/manifestations: connections involving water and weak relational leadership are named.
- conflicts/notes: the entry gives no speed or rule that every spontaneous union will fail.

## Contradictions

Witte links spontaneous union to separation. ABC includes dissolution but also intuitive creation, with no specified timetable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Zeus = Node concerns connections whose formation or direction may be uncertain. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes spontaneous union followed by separation; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a connection beginning to dissolve and weak leadership within a relationship, alongside intuitive shaping and metaphysical creation (printed p. 396, PDF p. 197). The passages supply no timetable and do not make every sudden association short-lived. ABC's Feuer-Wasser clause refers to the chapter's alcohol gloss.
