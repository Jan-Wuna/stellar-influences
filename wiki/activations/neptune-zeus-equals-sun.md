## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `249` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Days of unsuccessful work. Fruitless work. The man who makes efforts in vain. Scald-burns.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `396`, PDF p. `197` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Efforts of a man in water, disappointing human achievements, a fiery body in the air, creative sensitivity of a man, personal leadership weakness, personal procreative weakness, and unclear personal aims

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal work may disappoint or lack clear direction.
- psychology: creative sensitivity and unclear aims coexist.
- body/health: scald burns and reproductive weakness are historical examples, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: the sources use a man as a traditional referent and mention personal leadership.
- events/manifestations: work involving water and a fiery body in the air are particular source images.
- conflicts/notes: scalding does not justify a general claim of scorched vitality.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses unsuccessful effort. ABC also includes creative sensitivity and concrete water or air images; it does not reduce the person to wasted energy.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Zeus = Sun emphasizes personal effort whose result or direction may disappoint. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes unsuccessful work and effort in vain; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds unclear personal aims and weak leadership alongside creative sensitivity (printed p. 396, PDF p. 197). The concrete water and fiery-body images stay within the source's wording. Scald burns and reproductive weakness remain attributed historical examples, without turning them into a diagnosis or a general claim about vitality.
