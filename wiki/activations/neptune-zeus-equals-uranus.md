## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `249` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Revolutionary activities, to want to force a revolution. Gas and gasoline motors. Explosion of burnable gas.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `397`, PDF p. `197` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Efforts in air technology, subtle-material creative impulses, intuitive technical performance, new future goals, sudden leadership weakness, tension through subtle-material guidance, surprising uncertainty of leadership, and unclear leadership impulses

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sudden or technical activity with uncertain leadership.
- psychology: intuitive technical performance and new future goals are explicit.
- body/health: subtle-material language is attributed without a bodily mechanism.
- social/relationship: Witte names revolutionary activity and wanting to force a revolution.
- events/manifestations: gas or gasoline motors and gas explosion are specific physical examples.
- conflicts/notes: technical intuition and leadership uncertainty should not be flattened into universal explosive force.

## Contradictions

Witte includes revolution, motors and explosion. ABC adds intuitive technology and uncertain leadership; no common physical mechanism is stated for every example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Zeus = Uranus brings sudden activity and technical invention into a setting of uncertain direction. [Witte](#witte-entry) lists revolutionary action, gas or gasoline motors and exploding gas. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds intuitive technical performance and new goals alongside sudden leadership weakness (printed p. 397, PDF p. 197). This supports both technical creation and disruption, while keeping explosion a specific example and the source's subtle-material guidance language attributed.
