## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `249` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The beginning of dissolution and destruction of progeny. Made unhappy through love without consequences. Sterilization of a woman.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `396`, PDF p. `197` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Beginning dissolution of a love, sensitive shaping of love, sensitivity for artistic achievement, liking Feuer-Wasser, joy through striving for refinement, secret efforts in love, unclear aims in love, and a preference for fiery liquids

The chapter heading glosses Feuer-Wasser as alcohol (printed p. 396, PDF p. 197).

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: uncertain or dissolving affection alongside sensitive love and artistic refinement.
- psychology: joy in refinement and sensitivity to art are explicit.
- body/health: Witte's reproductive loss and sterilization claims are historical, not medical predictions.
- social/relationship: unhappy love, secret efforts and unclear aims are named.
- events/manifestations: artistic achievement and liking Feuer-Wasser appear as separate expressions.
- conflicts/notes: reproductive loss is not a necessary meaning of artistic pleasure or uncertain love.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes unhappy love and reproductive claims. ABC includes sensitive affection and artistic joy as well as dissolution; its fuller range must remain visible.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Zeus = Venus holds sensitive affection and artistic refinement beside uncertainty in love. [ABC](#abc-entry) describes both a love beginning to dissolve and joy in refinement, with secret efforts and unclear aims (printed p. 396, PDF p. 197). [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes unhappy love and historical reproductive-loss or sterilization claims. Those claims stay attributed, without making damage to reproduction a necessary outcome of love or artistic pleasure. Feuer-Wasser uses the chapter's alcohol gloss.
