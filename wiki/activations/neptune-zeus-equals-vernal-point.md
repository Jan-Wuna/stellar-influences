## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `249` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Exploding gases. Destroyed coal mines. Destroyed common property as, for example, by war. Defeats in war. Unsuccessful fight. Gas war.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `396`, PDF p. `197` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Efforts for public waters, disappointing performance in public life, weak public leadership, public future goals, unknown leadership in public life, and unclear public aims

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: public activity may lack clear leadership or fail to reach its aims.
- psychology: unclear public goals are explicit; no individual temperament is supplied.
- body/health: Witte's gas-war and explosion examples concern historical harms, not personal diagnoses.
- social/relationship: public leadership and common property are named.
- events/manifestations: public waters, destroyed coal mines and wartime losses are particular examples.
- conflicts/notes: war destruction is not the necessary expression of every public disappointment.

## Contradictions

Witte gives destructive collective examples. ABC describes broader public effort, goals and leadership weakness without requiring warfare.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Zeus = Vernal Point concerns public effort and direction that may prove ineffective or unclear. [ABC](#abc-entry) names efforts for public waters, disappointing public performance and weak leadership (printed p. 396, PDF p. 197). [Witte](#witte-entry) gives the more severe historical examples of exploding gas, destroyed mines or common property, and unsuccessful warfare. Those specific losses do not turn every uncertain public aim into a prediction of collective devastation.
