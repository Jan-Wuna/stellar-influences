## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `249` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Mighty but useless and wasted efforts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `397`, PDF p. `197` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Beginning dissolution of violence, influence of Feuer-Wasser, influence of future goals, disappointing energy performance, strength of unknown leadership, power weakness of leadership, strength through striving for refinement, and water against the force of fire

The chapter heading glosses Feuer-Wasser as alcohol (printed p. 396, PDF p. 197). The final water/fire-force clause is retained literally without a broader mechanism.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: great effort may be wasted, while force or leadership may weaken.
- psychology: future goals and striving for refinement are also named.
- body/health: the alcohol example is historical source language without a medical claim.
- social/relationship: dissolving violence and weak leadership are explicit.
- events/manifestations: disappointing energy performance and water against fire-force are particular examples.
- conflicts/notes: strength through refinement prevents treating all force as necessarily useless.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses mighty wasted effort. ABC adds strength through refinement and dissolving violence as well as disappointing energy performance.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune/Zeus = Vulcanus can describe substantial effort with disappointing results. [Witte](#witte-entry) calls the effort mighty but wasted; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds weak leadership and disappointing energy performance, yet also strength through refinement and violence beginning to dissolve (printed p. 397, PDF p. 197). The contrast supports impaired force and possible refinement without a guaranteed outcome. Its water/fire-force image remains literal, and Feuer-Wasser follows the chapter's alcohol gloss.
