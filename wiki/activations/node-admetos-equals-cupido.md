## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `154` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Few members of family. Reduction of family. To retain only a few social connections. Loneliness in connection with a few. Heavily oppressed communities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `235`, PDF p. `116` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Deepening a connection to the whole, connected with one another through depressions, restricted community connections, a firmly connected community, a thorough community of mediation, and strengthening the connection to art

Depressionen is retained as 'depressions'; this entry does not specify a clinical or economic sense.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: a reduced circle or restricted community can coexist with firm and deepened ties.
- psychology: loneliness is explicit in Witte; no universal response to a small family is stated.
- body/health: ABC's depressions phrase has no specified clinical or economic sense.
- social/relationship: few family or social connections and heavily oppressed communities are Witte's examples.
- events/manifestations: a firmly linked community, mediation and a strengthened connection to art broaden the picture.
- conflicts/notes: small family size is not inherently a negative condition or proof of oppression.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses reduction, loneliness and oppression. ABC adds firm communal ties and connection to art, without making either depend on family size.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Admetos = Cupido concerns the strength and limits of communal ties. [Witte](#witte-entry) lists reduced family or social connections, loneliness and oppressed communities. [ABC](#abc-entry) places restricted contacts beside a firmly connected community, deeper connection to the whole and stronger ties to art (printed p. 235, PDF p. 116). A small circle is not by itself a difficulty; the supported distinction is between firm connection and loneliness or oppression. ABC's unexplained depressions phrase stays qualified.
