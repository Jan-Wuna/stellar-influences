## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `154` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be modest. To be happy and fortunate with few. Content with little. To have a modest stroke of fortune. Free from bonds.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `234`, PDF p. `116` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Restrictions through legal mediation, restricted financial connection, end of a favorable relationship, success through a thorough connection, successful connection to the earth, fixed relationship to money, and joy over deepening a connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: contentment with little and success through a deepened connection coexist with legal or financial limits.
- psychology: modesty, contentment and joy are explicit.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: being happy with few contacts and free from bonds are Witte's positive expressions.
- events/manifestations: a modest stroke of fortune, restricted finance and ending an advantageous relationship are named.
- conflicts/notes: happiness with a few is not a difficult expression in itself.

## Contradictions

Witte's positive modesty and freedom stand beside ABC's mixture of success and restriction. Neither warrants treating a small circle as deprivation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Admetos = Jupiter emphasizes satisfaction that does not require many connections or abundant possessions. [Witte](#witte-entry) names modesty, happiness with a few, contentment with little and freedom from bonds. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds success through a thorough connection and joy in deepening it, alongside legal or financial restrictions and the ending of an advantageous tie (printed p. 234, PDF p. 116). The distinction is between contented simplicity and the specifically stated limits, rather than between large and small circles.
