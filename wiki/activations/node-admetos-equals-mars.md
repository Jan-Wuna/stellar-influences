## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `154` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To work together with few. Small working community. Working alone. To reject a working community or to be rejected by a joint-stock company. Connected by fighting for freedom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `234`, PDF p. `116` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Enduring work in a relationship, blocked actions within a connection, depressive impulses within a connection, restricted work mediation, thorough mediation activity, and suppressed mediation activity

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: working alone or with a few, with endurance or obstruction within collaboration.
- psychology: ABC's depressive impulses are attributed historical descriptions.
- body/health: no diagnosis follows from the source's depressive wording.
- social/relationship: a small working community, rejection of one and joint struggle for freedom are named.
- events/manifestations: work mediation may be restricted, thorough or suppressed.
- conflicts/notes: working with few people is not inherently a negative expression.

## Contradictions

Witte gives small-scale work, solitary work and rejection of a group. ABC adds enduring activity as well as blockage; group size alone does not explain the result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Admetos = Mars concerns how work proceeds within a limited circle. [Witte](#witte-entry) names working alone or with a few, rejection of a working community and connection through a fight for freedom. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds enduring work, blocked action and thorough but possibly suppressed mediation (printed p. 234, PDF p. 116). A small team can therefore be a straightforward setting; the difficulties lie in the stated rejection or obstruction. The depressive phrase remains a historical description rather than a diagnosis.
