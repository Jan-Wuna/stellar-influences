## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `154` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Dissolving connection with few. To free oneself from bonds, to untie.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `235`, PDF p. `116` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Restricted connections to air, disappointment through blocked contacts, deepening subtle-material connections, a firm connection to water, uncertainty in a connection through depressions, and consolidating future connections

ABC's subtle-material and depressions phrases are retained literally; this entry does not explain a mechanism or specify a clinical or economic sense of Depressionen.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: release from bonds in Witte stands beside restricted and consolidated connections in ABC.
- psychology: disappointment through blocking contacts and uncertainty are explicit.
- body/health: depressions and subtle-material connections remain unexplained historical wording.
- social/relationship: Witte names dissolving ties with a few; ABC also names firm or future ties.
- events/manifestations: air and water connections are source examples with no defined application.
- conflicts/notes: release is explicit in Witte; ABC also retains firm and future ties.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes untying bonds. ABC also includes restrictions, firm water connections and consolidation of future ties; their emphasis should not be forced into agreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Admetos = Neptune brings release and uncertain contact into view. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a connection with a few dissolving and freeing oneself from bonds. [ABC](#abc-entry) gives a different range: blocked contacts and disappointment, but also deepening or consolidating connections, including firm ties to water (printed p. 235, PDF p. 116). Dissolution is therefore Witte's explicit emphasis rather than a universal result shared by every clause. ABC's subtle-material and depressions wording stays qualified.
