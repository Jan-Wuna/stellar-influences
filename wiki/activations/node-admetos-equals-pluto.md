## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `154` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Fundamental transformation in connection with a few. Weakening or strengthening the bonds.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `235`, PDF p. `116` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Developing enduring relationships, blocked development of a connection, consolidation of a growing connection, thorough transformation within a connection, development of manifestation within a connection, and change in a relationship through depressions

Manifestations-Entwicklung is rendered literally as 'development of manifestation'; its mechanism is not explained. Depressionen has no specified clinical or economic sense here.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: transformation may strengthen, weaken, consolidate or block a connection.
- psychology: the sources give no required personal motive for the change.
- body/health: depressions is historical wording with an unspecified sense.
- social/relationship: a few people and developing enduring relationships are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: growth, blocked development and thorough transformation are named alternatives.
- conflicts/notes: manifestation development is an unexplained source phrase, not a guaranteed process.

## Contradictions

Both sources describe changes in bonds. Witte explicitly allows weakening or strengthening; ABC adds blocked development as well as growth, without a single prescribed direction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Admetos = Pluto concerns bonds undergoing substantial change. [Witte](#witte-entry) explicitly allows either weakening or strengthening; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds enduring relationships developing, a growing connection consolidating, and development that is blocked (printed p. 235, PDF p. 116). The common emphasis is transformation of connection, with its direction left open. ABC's manifestation-development and depressions phrases remain attributed and unexplained rather than supplying a confident causal mechanism.
