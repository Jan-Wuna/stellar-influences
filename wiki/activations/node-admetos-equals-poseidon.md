## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `154` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be in mental or spiritual harmony with a few. Cultural work in a small circle. During education to be subjected to severe restrictions and limitations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `235`, PDF p. `116` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Enduring cultural mediation, restricted reason within a connection, consolidation through intellectual encounters, a mental blockade within a connection, deepening intellectual contacts, deepening cultural connections, and restricting truth within a connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: mental or cultural harmony with a few beside restricted reason or truth.
- psychology: mental blockage and deeper intellectual contact are both named.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: a small cultural circle can be harmonious; educational restrictions are separate.
- events/manifestations: enduring cultural mediation and consolidation through intellectual encounters appear.
- conflicts/notes: harmony with a few is not inherently a difficult expression.

## Contradictions

Witte gives small-circle harmony and educational restriction. ABC adds cultural depth alongside blocked reason or restricted truth, without equating the size of the circle with its limits.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Admetos = Poseidon concerns intellectual and cultural connection within a limited circle. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes harmony with a few and cultural work in a small group, while also naming severe educational restrictions. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds deeper intellectual or cultural contacts beside mental blockage and restriction of truth (printed p. 235, PDF p. 116). The supported distinction is between depth of connection and restrictions within it; a small circle need not itself be the difficulty.
