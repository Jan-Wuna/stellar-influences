## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `154` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

A few people come together for a particular reason. To be captivated in bonds.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `234`, PDF p. `116` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A blocked encounter with people, the end of relationships to a man, earth-connected people, firm contact with a man, consolidation of the connection to a man, deepening personal contacts, and connection to a depressed person

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: a few people gather for a purpose, with firm or restricted personal contact.
- psychology: feeling captivated is Witte's description without a specified motive.
- body/health: ABC's depressed-person example is historical source language rather than a diagnosis of the reader.
- social/relationship: contact with a man and deeper personal contacts are explicit.
- events/manifestations: a blocked encounter or ended relationship sits beside consolidation.
- conflicts/notes: a few gathering for a reason is not inherently a difficult expression.

## Contradictions

Witte gives both purposeful gathering and captivity in bonds. ABC adds firm, deepened, blocked or ended contact, without one necessary result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Admetos = Sun concerns purposeful connection among a few people. [Witte](#witte-entry) names gathering for a particular reason and being captivated in bonds. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds firm or deepened personal contacts alongside a blocked encounter and the ending of a relationship to a man (printed p. 234, PDF p. 116). Gathering in a small circle is therefore a setting rather than an inherent difficulty. The depressed-person example remains attributed historical language.
