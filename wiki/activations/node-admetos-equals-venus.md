## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `154` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be harmonious with very few. To find little or no response. Bonds which do not hurt. Bonds of love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `234`, PDF p. `116` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Enduring peace mediation, blocked peace contacts, the end of a love relationship, thorough art mediation, suppressing love within a relationship, suppressed wishes within a relationship, and deepening a love relationship

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: harmonious love among a few beside limited response or suppressed wishes.
- psychology: love, wishes and harmony are named.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Witte explicitly includes bonds that do not hurt and bonds of love.
- events/manifestations: enduring peace mediation, art mediation and ending or deepening a love tie appear.
- conflicts/notes: harmony with very few is a positive expression rather than an inherent difficulty.

## Contradictions

Witte allows harmony and painless bonds as well as little response. ABC adds suppressed wishes and endings but also deepened love and enduring peace work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Admetos = Venus concerns the quality of affection within a small or limited circle. [Witte](#witte-entry) names harmony with very few, bonds of love and bonds that do not hurt, beside little response. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds deepened love and enduring peace mediation alongside suppressed wishes, blocked contacts and a love relationship ending (printed p. 234, PDF p. 116). The limits concern response or suppression; they do not make a small harmonious bond deficient.
