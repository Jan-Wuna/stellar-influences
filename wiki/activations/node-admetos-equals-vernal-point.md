## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `154` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Connected with a few through matters of death or mourning. A few come together. To be without freedom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `234`, PDF p. `116` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

enduring public connections, limited contacts in public, blocked connections in public life, end of a public connection, firm connections to the public, and contacts in public congestion

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: limited or firm public connections, with few people coming together.
- psychology: mourning is an attributed source setting rather than an assumed emotional state.
- body/health: death is a historical example, not a forecast.
- social/relationship: restricted freedom, public contacts and enduring ties are explicit.
- events/manifestations: public congestion and the end of a public connection are specific examples.
- conflicts/notes: gathering among a few does not by itself imply loss of freedom.

## Contradictions

Witte gives mourning and lack of freedom. ABC describes broader public contact, including firm or enduring ties as well as blockage and ending.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Admetos = Vernal Point concerns a limited circle within public life. [Witte](#witte-entry) names a few people gathering, with mourning and lack of freedom among its examples. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds enduring or firm public ties alongside blocked, limited or ended contacts and public congestion (printed p. 234, PDF p. 116). This supports both durable contact and restriction. The death or mourning example remains attributed rather than a prediction about a public gathering.
