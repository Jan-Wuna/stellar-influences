## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

From freedom into lack of freedom. Small exclusive economic or scientific communities. To be content with freedom or lack of freedom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `233`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Blockage in a mass meeting, blocked trade connections, restricted relations with the economy, consolidation of knowledge about relationships, increased restriction within connections, and thorough relationships to knowledge

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: economic, scientific or broad contacts become restricted while knowledge may consolidate.
- psychology: contentment with freedom or lack of it is Witte's stated response, not a required attitude.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: small exclusive economic or scientific communities are named.
- events/manifestations: blocked trade and a blockade at a mass meeting are specific examples.
- conflicts/notes: concentration on knowledge can coexist with restriction; no moral judgement about a small community follows.

## Contradictions

Both sources describe restrictions. Witte additionally names contentment; ABC adds consolidation of relationship knowledge without promising freedom.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = Admetos concerns the narrowing of economic, scientific or numerous contacts. [Witte](#witte-entry) names small exclusive communities and movement from freedom into lack of freedom. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds blocked trade or mass contact, while also describing consolidated knowledge about relationships and thorough engagement with knowledge (printed p. 233, PDF p. 115). Restriction and concentration coexist in the passages; Witte's contentment is one attributed response rather than a requirement to accept constraint.
