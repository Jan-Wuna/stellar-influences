## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Free or many social connections. Commercial or scientific connections with many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `233`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Successful connection to the whole, sharing common knowledge, contacts within a trading community, joining art and knowledge, connection to a successful community, and connection through shared experiences

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: free or numerous communal connections built around commerce, science or shared knowledge.
- psychology: shared experiences are explicit without a stated individual motive.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: a trading or successful community provides the group setting.
- events/manifestations: joining art and knowledge broadens the commercial and scientific examples.
- conflicts/notes: breadth of social contact does not guarantee that every group succeeds.

## Contradictions

Witte's many social or scientific contacts overlap with ABC's trading community and shared knowledge; ABC adds art and shared experience.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = Cupido concerns broad participation in a community through knowledge and experience. [Witte](#witte-entry) names free or many social ties and commercial or scientific connections with many. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a trading community, common knowledge, shared experiences and the joining of art with knowledge (printed p. 233, PDF p. 115). The emphasis is on a group's connections and exchanges, with successful community as a source expression rather than a promise about every association.
