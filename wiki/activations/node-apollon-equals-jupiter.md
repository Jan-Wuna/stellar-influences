## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Luck of freedom. Successfully connected in the field of economy or science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `232`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Relationships with legal knowledge, successful money mediation, successful contacts with the economy, joy over knowledge mediation, successful law mediation, connection to much money, and advantageous trade relations

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: successful economic, scientific, legal or financial connections, with freedom.
- psychology: joy in sharing knowledge is explicit.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: business and legal contacts provide the relational setting.
- events/manifestations: money mediation, advantageous trade and connection to much money are source examples.
- conflicts/notes: the financial examples are attributed possibilities rather than guaranteed gains.

## Contradictions

Both sources emphasize successful connections. ABC specifies money, law and knowledge-sharing without supplying an inevitable financial result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = Jupiter emphasizes opportunity through free and successful connections. [Witte](#witte-entry) names success in the economy or science and the good fortune of freedom. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds legal and financial mediation, advantageous trade and joy in sharing knowledge (printed p. 232, PDF p. 115). The common theme is benefit through connection and exchange. Its references to much money remain source examples, without a guaranteed level of wealth or gain.
