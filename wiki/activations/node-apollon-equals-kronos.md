## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Great and free or proud and free. Connected with authorities, courts, officials.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `233`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Special success of a connection, successful chief connections, successful connection to the state, great success in connections, state trade relations, state knowledge transfer, and connection to authorities of knowledge

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: successful contact with authorities, the state or recognized knowledge.
- psychology: Witte's pride and freedom are attributed descriptions.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: chiefs, courts, officials and authorities of knowledge are explicit.
- events/manifestations: state trade and knowledge transfer are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: status and institutional connection do not establish personal superiority or guaranteed success.

## Contradictions

Witte's authority contacts and freedom overlap with ABC's successful chief or state ties. ABC adds authorities of knowledge without turning authority into proof of truth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = Kronos concerns influential or institutional connections in commerce and knowledge. [Witte](#witte-entry) names authorities, courts and officials beside pride and freedom. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful contact with chiefs or the state, state trade and knowledge transfer, and authorities of knowledge (printed p. 233, PDF p. 115). The emphasis is on the reach and setting of connection; institutional status does not itself establish personal superiority, factual truth or an inevitable result.
