## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Free commercial activity. Far reaching connections through scientific activities or business matters or with representatives of commerce and science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `232`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Activities in knowledge mediation, labor mediation in trade, extension of activity in a relationship, successful actions in a connection, establishing contacts with the economy, and combining knowledge impulses

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: active commercial or scientific connection and the practical exchange of knowledge.
- psychology: no fixed motive or temperament is supplied.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: commercial or scientific representatives and work relationships are explicit.
- events/manifestations: labor mediation, forming business contacts and combining knowledge impulses are named.
- conflicts/notes: wide-ranging activity does not guarantee that every action succeeds.

## Contradictions

Witte's free commercial activity and far-reaching contacts overlap with ABC's knowledge and labor mediation. Their successful examples remain bounded.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = Mars concerns active work that establishes or extends connections. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes free commercial activity and far-reaching ties through business or science. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds knowledge mediation, labor mediation in trade and forming economic contacts, with successful action among its expressions (printed p. 232, PDF p. 115). The focus is practical exchange and expanding activity within relationships, without a guarantee that every undertaking succeeds.
