## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To feel free. Mental and emotional experience with many. To be connected with many in science or business.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `232`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Success of a soulful relationship, my successful connections, my contact experiences, my connections to trade, my connections to knowledge, soul-related experiences within a connection, increase of my contacts, and sharing my knowledge

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal freedom and one's own widening scientific, commercial or knowledge connections.
- psychology: mental, emotional and soul-related experiences are attributed descriptions.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: many contacts and a successful soulful relationship are explicit.
- events/manifestations: trade connections and sharing one's knowledge are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: soul-related experience is not proof of a special psychic ability or guaranteed success.

## Contradictions

Witte's felt freedom and experience with many overlap with ABC's personal contacts and knowledge sharing. ABC's soulful language remains attributed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = MC centers on personal experience of freedom and many connections. [Witte](#witte-entry) names mental or emotional experience with many in science or business. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds one's successful ties, trade and knowledge contacts, growing contacts and sharing knowledge (printed p. 232, PDF p. 115). The emphasis is on the individual's participation and experience. Its soulful-relationship language remains an attributed description without implying an unusual psychic faculty.
