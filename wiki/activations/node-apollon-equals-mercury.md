## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To think of freedom. Free or far reaching thinking. Trade connections. Connections with talkative or foreign people. Language courses.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `232`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Reports on trade connections, conveying reports of experience, successful connections to youth, binding trade conversations, joining knowledge and opinions, sharing knowledge through media, and increasing connection to youth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: free or far-reaching thought with practical communication and trade contacts.
- psychology: thinking of freedom and joining knowledge with opinions are explicit.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: youth, talkative or foreign people and trade partners are named.
- events/manifestations: reports, media knowledge sharing and language courses are specific examples.
- conflicts/notes: joining opinions and knowledge does not establish that every opinion is true.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes freedom of thought and social or language contact. ABC supplies trade conversations, reports and knowledge-sharing media, with no material contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = Mercury concerns broad thought made social through conversation and information. [Witte](#witte-entry) names free or far-reaching thinking, trade contacts and language courses. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds reports of experience, media knowledge sharing and contacts with youth (printed p. 232, PDF p. 115). The common emphasis is on exchange across people and subjects. Joining knowledge with opinions is a source expression, without making the opinions reliable merely because they circulate widely.
