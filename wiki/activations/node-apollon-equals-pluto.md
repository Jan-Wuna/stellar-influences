## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Increasing desire for liberty. Increase of authority. Increasing scientific and economic connections. Great volume of new connections of this kind.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `233`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Developing relations with the economy, successful development in business, successful development of an encounter, changes in trade relations, growing trade connections, and sharing knowledge and experience

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: growing or changing scientific and economic contacts.
- psychology: an increasing desire for liberty is explicit.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: new encounters and trade relationships provide the social setting.
- events/manifestations: increased authority, business development and sharing experience are named.
- conflicts/notes: more authority and a desire for freedom are stated together without an explanation of their relationship.

## Contradictions

Both sources emphasize development and increasing contacts. Witte separately names increased authority and desire for liberty; neither source resolves a necessary tension between them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = Pluto emphasizes connections developing in number or scope. [Witte](#witte-entry) names increasing scientific and economic ties, authority and desire for liberty. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds business development, encounters and changing or growing trade relations, with knowledge and experience being shared (printed p. 233, PDF p. 115). The common emphasis is expansion through developing contact. Authority and freedom remain distinct source themes rather than a claimed causal sequence.
