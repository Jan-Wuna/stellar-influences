## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The feeling of freedom among one’s equals. To be connected with many through similar world concept or same philosophy or views.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `233`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of cultural connections, encounter with intellectual or spiritual knowledge, success through intellectual or spiritual connections, joining successful ideas, clear economic connections, sharing cultural knowledge, increased intellectual or spiritual contacts, and sharing knowledge and truth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: cultural or philosophical connection with many who share views.
- psychology: feeling free among equals is Witte's explicit description.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: similar philosophies and intellectual or spiritual contacts are named.
- events/manifestations: sharing cultural knowledge and joining ideas are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: shared views and a source's truth language do not establish the truth of a group belief.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes freedom among peers with shared views. ABC adds cultural, intellectual and economic exchanges; its truth language remains attributed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = Poseidon concerns freedom and affinity through shared thought. [Witte](#witte-entry) names equals connected by a similar philosophy or worldview. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds widening cultural contacts, joined ideas and the sharing of knowledge (printed p. 233, PDF p. 115). The common theme is a broad circle sustained by intellectual or spiritual exchange. Its references to success and truth are source claims, without making shared conviction evidence that a belief is correct.
