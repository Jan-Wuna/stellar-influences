## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Limited freedom. Connected with many through methods and systems. Hindrances and separations in economic and scientific connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `233`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Tasks within a scientific connection, a successful connection in old age, serious contacts with the economy, conveying experiences of pain, losses within trade connections, many problems within a connection, and increasing separations within connections

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: systematic or constrained broad ties, with tasks and possible separations.
- psychology: serious contact and conveying painful experience are explicit.
- body/health: pain and old age are historical examples, not diagnoses or inevitable outcomes.
- social/relationship: many people may be connected through methods and systems.
- events/manifestations: economic or scientific hindrances, trade losses and successful later-life contact are named.
- conflicts/notes: methods and systems do not automatically mean isolation or failure.

## Contradictions

Both sources allow hindrance and separation. ABC also includes successful contact in old age and serious economic ties, without one uniform outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = Saturn concerns broad connections organized through methods, systems or enduring tasks. [Witte](#witte-entry) names limited freedom and hindrances in economy or science. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds scientific duties, serious contacts and trade losses, but also a successful connection in old age (printed p. 233, PDF p. 115). The range includes useful structure and restriction; painful experience and separation remain specific source expressions rather than a necessary result of systematic work.
