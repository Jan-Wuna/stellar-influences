## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Success due to personal freedom. To evolve unhindered. Connected with business people, scientists or many persons, promotion through these. Free people among each other.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `232`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of personal contacts, encounters with successful people, relations with people in the economy, a successful mediation center, personal success within connections, and connection to a center of knowledge

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal freedom and widening contacts with opportunities for success.
- psychology: unhindered development is a stated source expression.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: business people, scientists and successful people provide the social setting.
- events/manifestations: promotion through contacts and mediation or knowledge centers are named.
- conflicts/notes: success and promotion remain source possibilities rather than guaranteed results.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes personal freedom and promotion through others. ABC supplies personal expansion and centers of mediation or knowledge, with overlapping opportunity themes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = Sun emphasizes personal freedom expressed through widening contact. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes unhindered development and promotion through business people, scientists or many others. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds encounters with successful people, economic ties and connection to a knowledge center (printed p. 232, PDF p. 115). The common emphasis is personal opportunity through participation in wider networks, with success remaining an attributed expression rather than an assured outcome.
