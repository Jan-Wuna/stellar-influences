## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Sudden expansion of the freedom or of the sphere of activity. Connected suddenly with scientists and economists. Connections through scientific predictions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `233`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

expansion of tensions in a connection, dynamic business relationships, new trade connections, sudden experience in a connection, connection to technical science, and increasing reform in connections

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sudden widening of freedom or activity, with dynamic or newly formed connections.
- psychology: sudden experience and increasing tension are explicit.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: scientists, economists and technical science are named.
- events/manifestations: new trade ties, reforms and scientific predictions provide particular examples.
- conflicts/notes: sudden expansion can coexist with tension; scientific predictions are not guaranteed accurate.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sudden expansion and scientific contact. ABC also includes widening tension and reform, so expansion should not be read as uniformly easy.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = Uranus concerns sudden expansion or change in the field of contact. [Witte](#witte-entry) names wider freedom or activity and unexpected ties to scientists or economists. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds dynamic trade, new connections and technical science alongside increasing tension and reform (printed p. 233, PDF p. 115). These support opportunity and disruption together. Witte's scientific-prediction example stays within its stated scope, without a claim that forecasts must be accurate.
