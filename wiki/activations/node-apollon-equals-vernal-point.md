## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `153` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Freedom. Business or scientific connections of a general nature. The global internet. Worldwide scientific relationships.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `232`, PDF p. `115` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of connections in public, successful encounters in public, many public contacts, public trade relations, many connections in public life, and increasing connections to the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: free and expanding public, commercial or scientific connections.
- psychology: no particular individual motive is supplied.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: many public encounters and worldwide scientific ties are explicit.
- events/manifestations: the global internet and public trade relations are specific source examples.
- conflicts/notes: wide reach is not a guarantee of success in every encounter or reliable information.

## Contradictions

Witte gives general and worldwide networks. ABC adds public encounters and trade; their broad scope does not establish the quality of every connection.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Apollon = Vernal Point concerns publicly accessible or far-reaching connections. [Witte](#witte-entry) names freedom, general business or scientific ties, the global internet and worldwide scientific relationships. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds many public contacts, public trade and increasing connections to the public (printed p. 232, PDF p. 115). The shared emphasis is breadth of contact and exchange, while successful encounters remain examples rather than a guarantee about every public network.
