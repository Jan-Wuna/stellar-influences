## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `82` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Hindrances and obstacles through acquaintances. A small circle of acquaintances which always remains the same.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `105`, PDF p. `51` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Blocked connections to the surroundings, restricted local connections, consolidation of the connection to the partner, thorough contacts at the place, an oppressed partner within a connection, connection with congestion at the place, and resistance within a partner connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: a stable small circle may be constrained by acquaintances or local limits.
- psychology: no necessary motive for keeping the same acquaintances is supplied.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: partner ties may consolidate or contain oppression and resistance.
- events/manifestations: local congestion and thorough contacts at a place are named.
- conflicts/notes: a small unchanged circle is not itself proof of oppression.

## Contradictions

Witte gives enduring small-circle contact and hindrance. ABC adds consolidation and thorough contact as well as restriction; the partner's oppression is a particular difficult example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Asc = Admetos concerns the stability and limits of nearby connections. [Witte](#witte-entry) names a small circle that stays the same and obstacles through acquaintances. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds restricted local contacts, but also a firmer partner connection and thorough contact at a place (printed p. 105, PDF p. 51). Resistance, oppression and congestion are particular difficulties in the entry, rather than necessary qualities of a small stable circle.
