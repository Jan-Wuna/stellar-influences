## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `82` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Relations with business people and trades people in the near environment. General business connections or relations, also acquaintances with scientists and customers. Expanded limits of the immediate environment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `105`, PDF p. `51` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Spread of environmental connections, successful partner connection, trade connections at the site, link to environmental science, many environmental connections, economic connections of a place, and increasing connections at the site

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: expansion of nearby business, scientific and partner connections.
- psychology: no particular motive or temperament is supplied.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: business or trades people, scientists and customers are explicit.
- events/manifestations: trade at a place, environmental science and more local connections are named.
- conflicts/notes: the sources' success and expansion examples do not guarantee every partner connection succeeds.

## Contradictions

Both sources widen the local field of contact. Witte specifies business people, scientists and customers; ABC adds partner success and environmental science.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Asc = Apollon concerns a local circle becoming broader through commerce, science and contact with others. [Witte](#witte-entry) names nearby business people, scientists and customers and expanded limits of the immediate environment. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds trade at a place, environmental science and increasing local connections (printed p. 105, PDF p. 51). The supported theme is a wider practical network, with partner success retained as an attributed possibility rather than a guarantee.
