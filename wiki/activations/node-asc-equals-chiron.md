#### Sandbach Entry

One’s vibration spontaneously helps others to relate to their own past in a more healing manner and to identify more strongly with those energies which will most help them to evolve.

## Derived Synthesis

Node/Asc = Chiron is described by [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) as helping others approach their past in a more healing way and identify with what supports their evolution (PDF p. 119; chapter starts on p. 118). The interpretive focus is supportive reorientation of identity and past experience. His explanation through vibration and energies remains a spiritual claim, without an established healing mechanism or a promise that another person will change.
