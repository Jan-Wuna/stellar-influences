## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `82` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Connections with artists, with people who are in the same house, with the family, with clubs, unions etc.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `105`, PDF p. `51` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Relations with other communities, community connections at a place, shared relations with the surroundings, shared local encounters, mediating partner communities, connections within a local community, and connections within shared surroundings

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: nearby ties organized through family, shared residence or communities.
- psychology: the entry supplies no necessary emotional response to group membership.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: artists, family, clubs, unions and other communities are explicit.
- events/manifestations: shared local encounters and mediation between partner communities are named.
- conflicts/notes: sharing a place or group does not itself establish harmony or conflict.

## Contradictions

Both sources emphasize communal or shared-local connections. Witte specifies family, residence and organizations; ABC adds community mediation and shared surroundings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Asc = Cupido concerns the communities formed through a shared place or circle. [Witte](#witte-entry) names family, people in the same house, artists, clubs and unions. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds relations between communities, shared local encounters and partner-community mediation (printed p. 105, PDF p. 51). The common theme is belonging and contact through a local or organized group, without a stated guarantee of harmony or a necessary conflict.
