## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `82` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Disagreeable acquaintances. Misunderstandings through them. To avoid certain acquaintances and certain places. Closely connected persons suffer from sorrow and illness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `105`, PDF p. `51` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Connections to crime in the surroundings, disadvantages in partner connections, traditional connections at the place, meetings in historical places, connections with partners from the past, hidden partner connections, and waiver of partner connections

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: difficult or avoided acquaintances alongside historical, past or hidden connections.
- psychology: misunderstanding and sorrow are particular source descriptions.
- body/health: illness among close persons is historical wording, not a prediction.
- social/relationship: partner disadvantages and renouncing connections are named.
- events/manifestations: historic meeting places and ties from the past are specific examples.
- conflicts/notes: old, hidden or traditional contact is not by itself proof of crime or illness.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes disagreeable or avoided contact. ABC also includes traditional and historical links, which should not automatically inherit the negative examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Asc = Hades concerns difficulties in nearby or partner connections, including misunderstanding and avoidance. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives those themes, while [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disadvantages, hidden ties and renunciation alongside historical places and partners from the past (printed p. 105, PDF p. 51). The past-related examples remain distinct from crime in the surroundings. Sorrow and illness are attributed historical claims rather than necessary consequences of an acquaintance.
