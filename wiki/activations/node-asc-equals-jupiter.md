## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `82` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Fortunate acquaintances. Useful, supportive connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `104`, PDF p. `51` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Successfully connected with the partner, financial relations with the partner, joy by meeting others, money for partner mediation, legal connection at the place, connection to a financial place, and beneficial environmental contacts

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. `301`, entry code `1088`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Sociable. Amusements and entertainments. A liking for festivities and festivals, sharing of enjoyments and joyful occasions with other people. Happy unions and fortunate associations.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. `119` (entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `118`).

#### Sandbach Entry

Enthusiasm for allowing one’s personality and self-concept to grow. Negatively to overcompensate for problems which arise in one’s life by trying to hold on more and more tightly to old ways of seeing and defining oneself.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NODE/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source reference: printed p. `356` (entry).

#### Munkasey Entry

Expanding your participation and acceptance within groups or among friends with whom you share efforts; promises made to support and work with groups or friends; the politics behind guiding or shaping opinions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: supportive acquaintances, shared enjoyment and wider acceptance in groups.
- psychology: enthusiasm for growth can contrast with holding tightly to an old self-concept in Sandbach.
- body/health: no specific bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: happy unions, useful connections and promises of group support are explicit.
- events/manifestations: festivities, financial or legal partner ties, and shaping group opinions are named.
- conflicts/notes: group participation and self-concept are distinct emphases rather than one prescribed motive.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize support and enjoyment. Sandbach adds growth or defensive attachment to an old self-concept; Munkasey adds group promises and opinion politics.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Asc = Jupiter emphasizes supportive contact and shared enjoyment. [Witte](#witte-entry) names useful acquaintances, while [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describes sociability, festivities and happy associations. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds advantageous partner or local ties; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) places this in group participation and promises of support. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds enthusiasm for a growing self-concept, but also possible attachment to old self-definitions (PDF p. 119). Support and enjoyment can therefore accompany either openness to growth or a more fixed self-definition.
