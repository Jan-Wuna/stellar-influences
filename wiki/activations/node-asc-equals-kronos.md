## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `82` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Having connections with people higher up or very self-confident persons. Personal relations to members of the government. To be an adherent.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `105`, PDF p. `51` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

A special connection to others, relations with other states, directing a connection to the partner, a self-confident partner connection, superiority within a partner connection, local connections to authority, and important relations with the surroundings

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: connections with authority, confident people or significant partners.
- psychology: self-confidence is explicitly named; superiority is attributed source language.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: government members, higher-ranking people and other states are particular settings.
- events/manifestations: being an adherent and directing a partner connection are named.
- conflicts/notes: status or confidence does not establish actual superiority or benevolent leadership.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes high-ranking or self-confident acquaintances. ABC adds partner direction and relations with states, retaining superiority as a historical description rather than a judgement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Asc = Kronos concerns nearby or personal contact with authority and confident people. [Witte](#witte-entry) names higher-ranking acquaintances and government members. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds special partner ties, directing a connection and local links to authority, with relations to other states among its examples (printed p. 105, PDF p. 51). The emphasis is on the standing and direction of the connection. Its superiority language remains attributed, without establishing that status makes a person superior.
