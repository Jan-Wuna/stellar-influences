## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Local telephone calls. Discussion and mental association with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Local relations with youth, thoughts about partner connections, conversations with other veins, young partner connections, opinions about human relationships, Y connect to a young partner, and traffic connections at the place

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Desire and the inclination to seek and receive mental stimuli, demonstration of interest. Sharing the same thoughts with others, studies and research with other people.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A mind strongly influenced by identifying with the past on the one hand, but which which is, on the other, aware of the need for growth and progress. A mind limited and restricted by clinging to old and worn-out concepts about the self, but which can become clear, powerful when it opens to the possibilities of growth and change of one’s self-concept.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NODE/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `356`

#### Munkasey Entry

The influence wielded by an effective communicator; expressing your ideas to others; developing skills to gather advice or support from friends; educating others to your methods; your ability to get ideas across to groups.
