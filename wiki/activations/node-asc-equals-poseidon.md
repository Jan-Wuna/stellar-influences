## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. `82` (entry start; one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Close connections to people who have spiritual, mental, cognitive or educational experiences – to gain a growing understanding through such acquaintances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. `105`, PDF p. `51` (entry).

#### ABC Entry

Connected to others through ideas, insightful connections with others, conveying insights to others, an insightful encounter with others, a spiritual partner connection, clarity within a partner connection, and cultural relations at the place

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: understanding grows through intellectual, educational or spiritual acquaintances.
- psychology: ideas, insight and clarity are explicit.
- body/health: no distinct bodily claim or therapeutic effect is supplied.
- social/relationship: partner ties and contacts with experienced people are named.
- events/manifestations: conveying insights and local cultural relations are specific examples.
- conflicts/notes: source claims of insight do not guarantee the truth of every shared idea.

## Contradictions

Both sources emphasize understanding through acquaintance. Witte specifies experienced people; ABC adds partner clarity and cultural relations at a place.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node/Asc = Poseidon concerns learning and understanding through contact with others. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes close ties with people who have spiritual, cognitive or educational experience. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds shared ideas, insights conveyed to others and clarity within a partner connection, with local cultural relations among its examples (printed p. 105, PDF p. 51). The emphasis is on the exchange and growth of understanding, while the source's insight language does not itself establish that every shared idea is true.
