## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden acquaintances, and to experience excitement through them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Excited encounter with others, impulses in a partnership, new partner partnerships, reconnection of a partner link, tensions in connection with the environment, technical environmental connections, and Surprising encounter at the site

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Perpetual desire for company, sudden seeking of contacts. Sudden experiences with one's associates.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Allowing oneself to be free, open, and uninhibited helps one to let go of the past and to see oneself in new and life-supporting ways. An intense desire to free one self from the past, but often rebellion only attaches one to it more closely. Sudden and surprising changes in the growth of one’s personality, and the more one allows these to happen the greater the benefit one will receive from them.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Focusing upon sudden outbursts at meetings; interruptions at gatherings; meetings which focus upon astrological or computing methods; remaining impartial from the ideas and emotional issues under discussion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Node/Asc = Uranus`: unconventional friendships, stimulating contact, and the ability to reinvent oneself through social change. Main caution: estrangement: identity can become erratic, reactive against closeness, or too unsettled to sustain stable belonging.
