## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Nice acquaintances and connections. Love-acquaintances. Sensual connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Encounters at a place of peace, relations with the arts, peace contacts at the place, harmonious connections with the environment, love connection with the partner, preference for connections in the environment, and wishes for partner mediation

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Agreeable, affectionate or tactful treatment of other persons. The establishment of a union between people through the expression of the same interests and feelings. A love union.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Relationships that encourage one to grow, or which may encourage one to be tray oneself by avoiding evolution. The latter type will always eventually feel more and more unfulfilling, which will encourage one to seek the former. The ability to appreciate how one’s identity has been formed by one’s past, as well as perceiving the need to allow one’s identity to grow. True love enlarges the identity.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Receiving admiration and satisfaction from your associates; focusing on the values received from mutual participation; how others perceive your personal charm and manners in social situations; groups interested in the arts.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Node/Asc = Venus`: tact, popularity, and the ability to build closeness through kindness, style, and diplomacy. Main caution: people-pleasing: identity can become too dependent on being liked, too accommodating, or too polished to risk necessary friction.
