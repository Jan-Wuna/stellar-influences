## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Known persons get excited, they deal with or work with fire. Working with others. Connections with creative persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Others lead in a connection, encounter with fire in the environment, beginning of a partner relationship, fiery connection to the other, creative relationships in the environment, procreation connection with the partner, and determined partner in a connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Joint work, creative connections, and the beginning or direction of a partnership.
- psychology: Determination and leadership appear in ABC's partner descriptions.
- body/health: ABC includes procreative connection; no health inference follows.
- social/relationship: Working with creative people or with a determined partner.
- events/manifestations: Work with fire, encounters with fire, and the start of a partner relationship.
- conflicts/notes: The entries retain literal fire alongside creative or purposeful cooperation.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is evident. Fire, procreation, and collaborative creation are separate stated manifestations; none should replace the others.

## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Node/Asc through doing or beginning something with others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links the formula to joint work, creative people, and work with fire; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a partnership's beginning, a determined or leading partner, and procreative connection. These entries support purposeful or creative cooperation while retaining literal fire as a specific manifestation.
