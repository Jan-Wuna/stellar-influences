## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Many social connections with scientists, commercial people, industry and craftsmen.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful connection to of a community, joint contact extension, joint success of an encounter, successful connection to the whole, contacts to trade communities, knowledge sharing community, and knowledge sharing

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Expansion of community contacts through knowledge, trade, and practical occupations.
- psychology: No distinct individual psychological disposition is stated.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is provided.
- social/relationship: Connections with scientists, business people, industry, and craftspeople.
- events/manifestations: Shared success, widening contacts, trade communities, and knowledge exchange.
- conflicts/notes: The entry supports expansion and shared activity, not guaranteed commercial success.

## Contradictions

The entries develop a shared theme of many or expanding contacts; neither establishes that all such contacts succeed.

## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Node/Cupido through a widening network of communal and professional contacts. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names science, commerce, industry, and crafts; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects expansion with shared success, trade communities, and the exchange of knowledge. The unifying theme is participation in a larger field of useful contacts rather than a promise of success in every association.
