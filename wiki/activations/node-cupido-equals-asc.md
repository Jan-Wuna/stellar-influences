## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Betrothal. To be invited. To live with other people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mondknoten + Cupido`; printed p. `224`; one-based PDF page `111` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Encounters with other artists, relationships in a local community, shared contacts in the surroundings, artistic partner relationships, partner relationships in the community, partner connection to the whole, environmental mediation community (Umwelt-Vermittlungs-Gemeinschaft).

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community ties take form through nearby people and shared living.
- psychology: No independent psychological claim is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Invitation, betrothal, cohabitation, and relationships in a local community.
- events/manifestations: Joining others socially or living with them; artistic partnerships in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: The opening explicitly names other artists. The final Umwelt-Vermittlungs-Gemeinschaft is retained as a source compound for a mediating community in one's surroundings, without specifying a formal institution.

## Contradictions

Witte supplies specific social arrangements while ABC broadens the local-community setting. Neither says that every local association becomes a betrothal or shared household.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Node/Cupido through the people with whom communal life is directly shared. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes this concrete as invitation, betrothal, or living with others; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds encounters with other artists, local community contacts and artistic partner relationships. These are related ways of entering a shared social setting, not interchangeable predictions.
