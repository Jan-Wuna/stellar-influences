## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Death in the family. Funeral rites. A weakly founded poor firm.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mondknoten + Cupido`; printed p. `225`; one-based PDF page `111` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Danger in a shared connection, secret connection to a community, contacts with a community in need (Not-Gemeinschaft), disadvantageous connection to a community, connection through a shared past, connection to a criminal community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community or family ties encountered through adversity, loss, or a shared past.
- psychology: No separate psychological trait is established.
- body/health: Death in a family is a historical source example, not a medical prediction.
- social/relationship: Funeral participation, communities in need, and disadvantageous group ties.
- events/manifestations: Funeral rites and a poorly founded firm in Witte; danger and criminal associations in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: The secret connection and community in need are distinct clauses; criminal community is a separate severe historical example, not a judgment about every community tie.

## Contradictions

Family mourning, a weak firm, secret ties and harmful associations are distinct adverse manifestations. A community in need is not thereby identified as criminal.

## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Node/Cupido through community ties burdened by loss or disadvantage. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names family bereavement, funeral rites, and a poorly founded firm; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds secret ties, a community in need, disadvantageous associations and links through a common past. These describe different contexts in which hardship binds people together. The references to death and criminal groups remain attributed examples, not predictions about a person or group.
