## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Proposal of love. Promise to marry. To join with others for common activities. Concerted activities in a firm.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mondknoten + Cupido`; printed p. `224`; one-based PDF page `111` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Actions connecting the whole, joint action in a connection, community for employment placement (Arbeits-Vermittlung), activities of an artistic community, establishing a connection to art, mediation activity for art.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: People join through a common action or undertaking.
- psychology: An intention to join or cooperate is shown by the proposals and shared activities.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Marriage proposals, promises to marry, and coordinated work with others.
- events/manifestations: Concerted activities in a firm, employment placement and the work of an artistic community.
- conflicts/notes: Arbeits-Vermittlung concerns employment placement, not transport. ABC separately names connections to art and mediation for art, rather than a particular artist.

## Contradictions

Marriage proposals and collective work are different settings for an act that joins people. They are not competing meanings or evidence that every joint task concerns marriage.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Node/Cupido through action that brings people together. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives a proposal or promise to marry alongside concerted activity in a firm. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds joint action, employment placement and the activities of an artistic community. The shared theme is undertaking something together, while each concrete example retains its own setting.
