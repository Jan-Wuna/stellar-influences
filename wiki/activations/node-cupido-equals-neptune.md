## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Postponed engagement. Liquidated corporation. Tie-ups or to join with a secret society or organization.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mondknoten + Cupido`; printed p. `225`; one-based PDF page `111` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Dissolution of shared bonds, relationships with a metaphysical community, sensitivity of an artists' community, jointly connected in the air, shared connection to water, uncertainty in a shared relationship.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertain, dissolving, or less publicly visible communal bonds.
- psychology: Uncertainty in a shared relationship is explicit in ABC.
- body/health: No medical condition is supplied; water and air are stated settings.
- social/relationship: Postponed engagement and joining a secret or metaphysical society.
- events/manifestations: Corporate liquidation, dissolution of ties, and shared water-related connections.
- conflicts/notes: The Neptune row ends with uncertainty in a shared relationship. The following encounter that changes a community and development of relations belong to the separately marked Pluto row.

## Contradictions

Dissolution and joining a secret or metaphysical group are different stated outcomes. They do not establish that every spiritual association must dissolve; ABC's air and water clauses remain literal source examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Node/Cupido through communal connections whose form is uncertain, dissolving, or concealed. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives postponed engagement, corporate liquidation, and joining a secret organization. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds uncertainty, dissolution of common ties, and metaphysical community contacts. These support several kinds of elusive or changing bond, with ABC's sensitivity in an artists' community retained alongside uncertainty.
