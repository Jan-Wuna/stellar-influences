## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `NODES + CUPIDO`; one-based PDF page `149` (Pluto row and alternate Pluto glyph checked against the raw scan).

Previous connections are started all over. Newly arising community. Assembly of a corporation. Inauguration (opening) of parliament.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mondknoten + Cupido`; printed p. `225`; one-based PDF page `111` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

An encounter that changes a community, developing relationships with a community, changed community relationships, growing connections within a community, changing connections within a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Renewed, growing, or newly assembled communal connections.
- psychology: No separate psychological disposition is stated.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Old contacts restart and new communities form.
- events/manifestations: Assembly of a corporation or opening of parliament in Witte; growing contacts in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's encounter that changes a community starts the separately marked Pluto row. Witte's renewed connections and formal assemblies remain specific examples rather than a general claim of control.

## Contradictions

Restarting an old connection, changing a community and assembling a new one are compatible possibilities of renewal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies Node/Cupido through the renewal and development of communal ties. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes restarting previous connections, a newly arising community, and formal assemblies such as a corporation or parliament. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds an encounter that changes a community, developing relationships and growing or changing connections within it. This supports a process of re-formation rather than a generic claim of domination.
