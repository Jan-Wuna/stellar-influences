## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

In mental and spiritual union joined with others. To come into contact with religious or similar sects.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Relations with a culture community, contacts of ideas community, encounters in a spiritual community, connection through common knowledge, connection to spiritual synthesis, and connections in a culture community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental, spiritual, or cultural union with others.
- psychology: Shared ideas and knowledge provide the basis of connection.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Religious groups and communities united by ideas, knowledge, or culture.
- events/manifestations: Encounters in spiritual or cultural communities.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's term “sects” is an attributed example, not a judgment about all such groups.

## Contradictions

The spiritual and cultural examples overlap without making all mental union religious. No direct contradiction is evident.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Node/Cupido through a community formed around shared ideas, knowledge, or spiritual understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names mental and spiritual union and contact with religious groups; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens this to cultural communities and common knowledge. The connecting element is a shared field of understanding rather than a particular required belief.
