## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Dissolution of a betrothal. Breach of promise to marry. Loss in the family. Separation of a familiar or social connection.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Mondknoten + Cupido`; printed p. `225`; one-based PDF page `111` (complete oriented row checked against the raw scan).

Burdensome linkage of a community, patient encounter within a community, concentration on connection to the whole, problematic relationships with the community, separation of connections within a community, connection to serious art.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Burdens, difficulty, or separation in communal and family ties.
- psychology: Patience and concentration on a shared connection in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is established; the burden concerns a communal linkage.
- social/relationship: Loss in the family, a broken betrothal, or a separated social connection.
- events/manifestations: Dissolution of engagement and problematic community relations.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's burdensome linkage is not bodily contamination, and its closing phrase names serious art rather than a particular artist. Patience and separation remain distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes separation while ABC also includes patience and concentration. These allow difficulty within a continuing tie as well as its ending; they are not a simple contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Node/Cupido through difficulty in maintaining a shared bond. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives a broken betrothal, family loss, and separation from a familiar or social connection. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds problematic relations alongside patience and concentration, so the interpretation includes enduring a burden as well as a tie ending. Connection to serious art remains a separate ABC example.
