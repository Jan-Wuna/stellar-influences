## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Family convention. Mighty influential social connections. Social unions or ties to reinforce common power and influence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Encounter with a violent community, powerful connections of a community, strong mediation community, connecting energies in a relationship, and connection with a powerful community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Social union that concentrates shared power or influence.
- psychology: No separate psychological trait is stated.
- body/health: ABC includes a violent community; no medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Influential social ties and a powerful community.
- events/manifestations: A family convention or union formed to reinforce common influence.
- conflicts/notes: Powerful and violent are distinct possibilities in the entries.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses reinforced influence; ABC adds violence as one adverse form. The latter does not describe every strong community.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Node/Cupido through people joining to reinforce collective power. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives a family convention and influential social unions; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds strong communal links and encounters with a powerful group. ABC's separate violent-community example keeps this power theme from being uniformly favourable.
