## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Disagreeableness in a small circle. Junk dealer.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source reference: printed p. `227`, one-based PDF p. `112`; exact activating-factor row.

#### ABC Entry

Consolidating old relationships, ending a connection from the past, depression in a criminal connection, restriction of a connection through lack, dangerous suppression within a connection, thorough relationships in distress.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficulty within a small circle and restricted or consolidated old ties.
- psychology: Disagreeableness in the circle; ABC's clinical-sounding depression example stays attributed.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is established.
- social/relationship: A small social circle and old relationships under restriction.
- events/manifestations: Junk dealing is a separate occupational example in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes both consolidating old ties and ending a connection from the past; depression remains attributed source wording.

## Contradictions

Witte's small-circle difficulty and ABC's consolidation or restriction overlap. ABC also names ending a past connection and thorough relationships in distress; these are different expressions rather than a single outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Node/Hades through the restriction and difficulty of a close circle. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes disagreeableness within a small group, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds consolidating old relationships, ending a tie from the past, and restriction through lack. Witte also names a junk dealer as a separate concrete example; this must not be turned into a judgment about everyone in such work.
