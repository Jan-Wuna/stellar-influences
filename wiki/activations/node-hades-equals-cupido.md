## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Poverty, lack or other circumstances strain family relations. To experience something evil in company with others. Sociability in criminal circles.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source reference: printed p. `227`, one-based PDF p. `112`; exact activating-factor row.

#### ABC Entry

Danger in a shared connection, a secret connection with a community, contacts with a community in need, a disadvantageous connection with a community, a connection through a shared past, a connection with a criminal community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Family or group relations strained by poverty, lack, or adversity.
- psychology: No separate psychological disposition is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is established.
- social/relationship: Family strain, an emergency community, or connection through a shared past.
- events/manifestations: Experiencing adversity with others; criminal social circles in the attributed entries.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also names a secret group connection; its criminal examples are not universalized.

## Contradictions

The shared-adversity theme is compatible across entries. A common past or an emergency group does not itself imply criminal activity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Node/Hades through hardship shared within a family or group. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly describes poverty or lack straining family relations and experiencing adversity in company. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds emergency or disadvantageous group ties and a common past. Both also retain criminal-circle examples, which are particular adverse settings rather than a label for every group under strain.
