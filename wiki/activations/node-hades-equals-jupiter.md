## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be prompted or to prompt others to misappropriate or to extort something. Unpleasantness in money affairs. To be a witness to thefts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success in dangerous connection, money deficiency in a connection, happiness in dangerous connection, criminal financial connection, poor relation to money, adverse legal mediation, and advantage through contacts from the past

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Financial or legal entanglement in a difficult or dangerous connection.
- psychology: Inducement by or of others appears in Witte; no general moral character is established.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Money-related difficulty involving associates, or advantage through earlier contacts.
- events/manifestations: Misappropriation, extortion, or witnessing theft in Witte; criminal finance and adverse legal mediation in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: Receiving an advantage is not equivalent to being free of danger or loss.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses unpleasant financial events; ABC also includes success or happiness within danger and benefit from past contacts. These qualify the adverse theme without removing its context.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Node/Hades through money, advantage, or legal matters within a troublesome connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives financial unpleasantness, inducement to misappropriate or extort, and witnessing theft. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) retains money shortage and dangerous financial links but also benefit through past contacts. The range includes both involvement in an adverse situation and advantage within it; it does not establish personal criminality.
