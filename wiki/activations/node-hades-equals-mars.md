## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To call together or be together for the purpose of committing mean acts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Establishing relationships with the past, mediating dangerous work, criminal connection actions, grim actions in connections, activities of an emergency community, and hidden actions of a connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Actions undertaken with others in dangerous, concealed, or distressing circumstances.
- psychology: An adverse shared purpose is explicit in Witte; no fixed character is asserted.
- body/health: Dangerous work appears in ABC; no medical condition is specified.
- social/relationship: People gather for harmful acts in Witte, or work within an emergency community in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Hidden actions, dangerous-work mediation, and re-establishing past contacts.
- conflicts/notes: Emergency work and criminal action are distinct source examples.

## Contradictions

ABC broadens Witte's harmful-purpose example to activity in need and relations with the past. These are different contexts, not evidence that emergency cooperation is criminal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Node/Hades through what people do together in difficult or concealed circumstances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names gathering for mean acts. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds dangerous work, hidden actions, emergency-community activity, and establishing links with the past. Shared action unifies these settings, while their purpose and social character remain distinct.
