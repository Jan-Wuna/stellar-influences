## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Pessimism. Sad news. Secret signs. Secret marks. The jargon of the underworld. To plot a crime.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reports about secret connections, thoughts about the need of a relationship, thoughts hide in a connection, talk about relationships from the past, criminal youth connections, and inadequate news connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sad, concealed, or troubled communication within a connection.
- psychology: Pessimism and thoughts about a relationship's difficulties.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Secret connections and discussion of earlier relationships.
- events/manifestations: Sad news, secret signs or marks, underworld jargon, and plotting in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: The criminal-language examples are specific; not every secret message concerns crime.

## Contradictions

Sad news and concealed communication are different forms of the stated theme. Neither establishes that every past relationship or private discussion is harmful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies Node/Hades through the messages, thoughts, and signs carried by a difficult or hidden connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) moves from pessimism and sad news to secret marks and criminal jargon; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds reports about secret ties, concealed thoughts, and discussion of relationships from the past. This supports both troubled communication and deliberate concealment without treating all privacy as criminal.
