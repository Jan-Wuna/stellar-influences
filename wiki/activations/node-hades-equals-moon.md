## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Women with bad company. To be disgusted with the public conditions. To be slandered in public. To be exposed to such things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source reference: printed p. `226`, one-based PDF p. `112`; exact activating-factor row.

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous relationships with a woman, hiding feelings within a connection, a lack of feeling in a connection, an emotional connection with the past, a disadvantageous connection involving women, hidden contacts with a woman, a connection with a people in need.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult, hidden, or publicly exposed relations, with women and collective conditions as source examples.
- psychology: Disgust at public conditions in Witte; ABC describes hidden feelings, a lack of feeling in a connection and emotional ties to the past.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Hidden or disadvantageous connections involving women, as historically phrased.
- events/manifestations: Public slander and association with a people in need.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered examples remain attributed; the restored emotional clauses do not define every woman or relationship.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses public exposure while ABC also includes hidden contacts, concealed feelings and emotional ties to the past. These are different experiences, not a contradiction that requires choosing one.

## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Node/Hades through relationships experienced as hidden, disadvantageous, or exposed to public harm. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes disgust at public conditions and public slander, alongside its gendered bad-company example. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds concealed feelings, a lack of feeling within a connection, emotional ties to the past, and connection with a people in need. These historical gender and public examples retain their source context rather than defining every woman or relationship.
