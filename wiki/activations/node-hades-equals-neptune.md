## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Passing connection with evil things. To have to do with water. To experience water accidents. Danger of drowning.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 227; one-based PDF p. 112, complete Neptune row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Feingefühl einer alten Verbindung, in schmutziger Luft verbunden, Nachteile in Verbindung mit Wasser, unklare Beziehungen aus der Vergangenheit, unsichere Geheim-Verbindung, verborgene Verbindungen zur Metaphysik, Verneinung einer kriminellen Verbindung

Editorial translation: Sensitivity in an old connection; connected in dirty air; disadvantages connected with water; unclear relationships from the past; an uncertain secret connection; hidden connections with metaphysics; negation of a criminal connection.

The final phrase does not specify whether a connection is denied or rejected. The metaphysical wording remains an attributed source association.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Unclear or hidden difficult connections, including literal water-related circumstances. [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry).
- psychology: Sensitivity within an old connection and uncertainty about past relationships are ABC themes.
- body/health: Witte lists water accidents and drowning as historical examples, without establishing a diagnosis or individual outcome.
- social/relationship: Old, secret and past relationships occur alongside a negated criminal connection in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Connections in dirty air and disadvantages connected with water are literal ABC settings.
- conflicts/notes: ABC does not explain the act of negating a criminal connection; no specific legal action or finding is inferred.

## Contradictions

Witte's passing adverse connection and ABC's old or concealed ties describe different circumstances. Both retain water-related examples, but ABC's disadvantages do not establish Witte's extreme drowning example as a necessary outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Node/Hades = Neptune`, Neptune specifies difficult connections through uncertainty, sensitivity and concealed ties. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes sensitivity in an old connection, unclear relationships from the past and an insecure secret connection. Its hidden links with metaphysics remain a source-specific possibility; the negation of a criminal connection is not explained further.

The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a passing connection with adversity and contact with water. ABC also names disadvantages connected with water and connections in dirty air, keeping the physical settings distinct from the relational uncertainty. Witte's water accidents and drowning remain attributed historical examples, not predictions of harm.
