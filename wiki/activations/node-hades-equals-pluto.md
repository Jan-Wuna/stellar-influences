## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Promises that are not kept. Connections that fail.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source reference: printed p. `227`, one-based PDF p. `112`; exact activating-factor row.

#### ABC Entry

Developing relationships from the past, unfolding criminal connections, change in a relationship through privations, developing hidden contacts, growing distress within connections, transformation of distress through connections.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A difficult connection develops, changes, or fails.
- psychology: Growing grief is described in ABC; no general psychological profile is stated.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Broken promises, failed connections, and ties shaped by deprivation.
- events/manifestations: Development of past or criminal connections and hidden contacts; transformation of distress through connections in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes developing hidden contacts; development or transformation does not necessarily mean improvement.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses failure, while ABC describes development and change, including change of need through relationships. Development need not be improvement, and failure is not the only stated form.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies Node/Hades through the development or alteration of a difficult relationship. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives unkept promises and failed connections. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens the process to developing past ties, deprivation changing a relationship, growing grief, and a change of need through connection. This preserves change in several directions rather than reducing the whole entry to failure.
