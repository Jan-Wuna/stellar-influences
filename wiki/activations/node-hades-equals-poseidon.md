## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Wisdom of the antiquity. Secret or occult society. To be connected through forbidden ideological conceptions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source reference: printed p. `227`, one-based PDF p. `112`; exact activating-factor row.

#### ABC Entry

Relationships with an old culture, insightful connections from the past, going without spiritual encounters, spiritual hardship within a connection, dangerous spiritual contacts, sorrow through truth in a connection, a lack of cultural connections.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections through antiquity, concealed beliefs, or difficult spiritual and cultural relations.
- psychology: Insight from past connections, or sorrow through truth, in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Secret or occult societies and contact with an old culture.
- events/manifestations: Association through forbidden ideological ideas in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: Ancient wisdom and cultural or spiritual deprivation are separate possibilities; an old culture is not inherently adverse.

## Contradictions

The wisdom-of-antiquity example is not adverse in itself. ABC adds cultural or spiritual deprivation and dangerous contacts, so the entries do not support a uniformly negative view of old culture or occult groups.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Node/Hades through ideas and cultural or spiritual connections shaped by the past or by concealment. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes wisdom from antiquity, secret societies, and forbidden ideological conceptions. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds old cultures, insight from past contacts, sorrow through truth, and lack of cultural connection. The range includes inherited understanding as well as difficulty in finding or sharing it.
