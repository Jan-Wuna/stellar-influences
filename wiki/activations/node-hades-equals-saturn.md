## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be tied to hostile people. Loss by theft or through poor company.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Old problems in a connection, patient contacts in need, dangerous tasks in a connection, concentration on the lack of a connection, disadvantages due to problematic relationships, and separation of an old connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: An old or hostile connection carries burdens, loss, or separation.
- psychology: Patience in need and concentration on a relationship's lack in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Being tied to hostile people or disadvantaged by problematic relations.
- events/manifestations: Loss through theft or poor company; ending an old connection.
- conflicts/notes: Continuing under difficulty and separation are both present; neither is inevitable.

## Contradictions

Witte's unwanted tie and ABC's separation describe different possible stages or outcomes. ABC also includes patient contact in need, which should not be flattened into hostility.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Node/Hades through the burden of a difficult or longstanding tie. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives being bound to hostile people and loss through theft or poor company. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds old problems, patient contacts in need, disadvantage, and separation from an old relationship. The entry therefore includes both enduring difficulty and ending a connection.
