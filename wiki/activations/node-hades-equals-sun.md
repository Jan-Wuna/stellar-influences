## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To meet invalids. To be bodily disgusted and fed up with something. Bodily displeasure. To be robbed. To unite with thieves.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Relations with people of the past, encounter with men in need, contacts with a secret centre, adverse personal relations, personal deprivation in connections, personal secret connections, and association with criminal persons

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal contact with deprivation, adverse company, or people from the past.
- psychology: Bodily disgust and displeasure are named in Witte.
- body/health: Witte includes meeting ill people and bodily displeasure; no diagnosis is established.
- social/relationship: Secret personal ties, encounters with men in need, and past associates in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Being robbed or uniting with thieves in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: Historical health and gender examples remain attributed; victimization and participation are distinct.

## Contradictions

Witte includes both being robbed and joining thieves. These opposite positions in a criminal event must not be collapsed into a character judgment; ABC also includes non-criminal past and need-related ties.

## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Node/Hades through direct personal involvement in an adverse or deprived connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes bodily displeasure, meeting ill people, being robbed, and joining thieves as distinct examples. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds people from the past, men in need, and secret personal ties. The common focus is personal exposure to a difficult setting, while the health and criminal examples remain source-attributed possibilities.
