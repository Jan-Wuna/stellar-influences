## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Suddenly connected with evil things. To be present at accidents or murder.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 227; one-based PDF p. 112, complete Uranus row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Erschwerte Kontakte zur Technik, mit gefährlichen Ereignissen verbunden, Kummer durch neue Beziehungen, Nervosität in einer benachteiligten Verbindung, Spannungen in einer Geheim-Verbindung, verbindende Impulse in der Not

Editorial translation: Difficult contacts with technology; connected with dangerous events; sorrow through new relationships; nervousness in a disadvantaged connection; tensions in a secret connection; unifying impulses in distress.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden adverse contact and strain within new, disadvantaged or secret connections. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: ABC names nervousness, sorrow and tension in particular connections, without establishing a permanent disposition.
- body/health: Witte gives accidents and murder as events one may witness; these do not establish a diagnosis or inevitable harm.
- social/relationship: New relationships can bring sorrow; ABC also allows unifying impulses in distress.
- events/manifestations: Dangerous events and difficult contacts with technology are specific source examples.
- conflicts/notes: Presence at an adverse event remains distinct from causing it; the distress-related impulse is not limited to violence.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sudden involvement in dangerous events, while ABC also describes relational strain, difficult technical contacts and impulses toward connection in distress. These are different expressions of the exact orientation; neither establishes that a person causes an event merely by being present.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Node/Hades = Uranus`, Uranus specifies difficult connections through sudden involvement and strained encounters. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives sudden adverse contact and being present at an accident or murder. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds sorrow through new relationships, nervousness in a disadvantaged connection and tension in a secret one, as well as difficult contacts with technology.

ABC also includes unifying impulses in distress, so the range permits movement toward connection amid difficulty. The entries do not make every new tie dangerous, and Witte's presence at an adverse event must remain distinct from causing it.
