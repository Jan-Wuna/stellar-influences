## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Vulgar acquaintances. Vulgar love. Street love. Dealing with servants.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source reference: printed p. `226`, one-based PDF p. `112`; exact activating-factor row.

#### ABC Entry

Harmonious development in secret, a criminal love connection, inadequate peace mediation, connections with art from the past, preference for dangerous connections, benevolent connections in need.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Love or social contact described in adverse or historically stigmatizing terms, with benevolence in need also present.
- psychology: ABC explicitly includes preference for dangerous connections.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Love connections and dealings with servants in Witte's historical wording.
- events/manifestations: Benevolent connections in need in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's classed language remains attributed; ABC also includes concealed harmony, art from the past and inadequate peace mediation.

## Contradictions

Witte uses derogatory social and sexual descriptions, while ABC also includes benevolence in need. The latter is a meaningful difference of emphasis; adversity is not the whole range of affection in the entry.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies Node/Hades through affection or social connection within an adverse setting. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) uses historically stigmatizing language about love and acquaintances and separately mentions dealing with servants. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes concealed harmonious development and connections with art from the past, alongside attraction to dangerous connections and benevolent relations in need. This preserves the source's social range without adopting its class judgments or making every such bond harmful.
