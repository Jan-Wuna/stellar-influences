## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Official meeting with a few. Close connections with the chief or the authorities. Latent connections with authorities. Connection with dethroned persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blocked relations of the state, end of a special connection, restricted self confidence in a connection, state blockage of connections, self governing contact deepening, and strengthening state connections

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Close, limited, latent, or blocked connections with authority.
- psychology: ABC includes restricted self-confidence in a connection.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: A small official circle or close tie to a chief; contact with dethroned persons in Witte.
- events/manifestations: An official meeting with few people, blocked state relations, or an ended special tie.
- conflicts/notes: Consolidating official contacts is also present; small meetings are not inherently adverse.

## Contradictions

Restriction and strengthening are both stated. A few close official ties may be firm rather than unsuccessful, so the number of participants does not determine the outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Node/Kronos through the limited or concentrated form of an authority connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a small official meeting, close or latent ties to authorities, and contact with dethroned people. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds blockage or ending of ties alongside strengthening state connections. This supports both restriction and consolidation, without treating every small official circle as a difficulty.
