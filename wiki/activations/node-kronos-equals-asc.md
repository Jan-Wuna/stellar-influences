## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Employment-contract. Others have difficulties with the authorities. To become entangled with the authorities through others. The beginning or end of a lawsuit.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Renown of a partner relationship, special relations with the environment, local connection with the authority, partner relationship with the boss, state environmental relations, links with public places, and priority partner link

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Authority connections encountered through a partner, other people, or the local environment.
- psychology: ABC mentions the standing of a partnership; no broader personality trait is stated.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: A tie with a boss or local authority; becoming involved through others.
- events/manifestations: An employment contract or the beginning or end of a lawsuit in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: Official entanglement is one possibility; ABC also supplies neutral local and partner settings.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes employment and legal difficulty, while ABC includes status and local official contact. These differ in emphasis without requiring all authority ties to be contentious.

## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Node/Kronos through the people and places that bring an authority connection into immediate experience. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives an employment contract, entanglement with authorities through others, and the beginning or end of a lawsuit. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a partner relationship with a boss and local official contacts. The common focus is authority encountered through a concrete interpersonal or local tie.
