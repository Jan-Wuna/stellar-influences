## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Node/Kronos through the social group or formal union involved in an authority connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names civil marriage and social ties with officials or superiors. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds shared state connections, community leaders, and reputation through art. These place the connection within a communal or socially recognized setting.
