## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Deplorable conditions in leading circles. Corruption. Sorrow or loss through connection with authorities. Connection with authorities on account of shameful actions, crimes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

Source reference: printed p. `231`, one-based PDF p. `114`; exact activating-factor row.

#### ABC Entry

Forgoing a special connection, a special danger to a connection, being bound to the state through need, dangerous relationships of the state, a lack of authority in encounters, a criminal connection with the state, important contacts from the past.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Authority connections burdened by corruption, danger, sorrow, or need.
- psychology: Sorrow through official connections is explicit in Witte.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Troubled leading circles and being bound to the state by necessity.
- events/manifestations: Loss through authorities or official contact because of crime in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: ABC adds forgoing a special tie and lack of authority during encounters; its state-dependence examples need not all imply corruption.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes corruption and loss; ABC also includes state dependence in need and contacts from the past. Those additional settings should not all be characterized as corruption.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Node/Kronos through disadvantage within a connection to authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes corruption in leading circles, sorrow or loss through authorities, and official contact arising from crime. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds danger, dependence on the state in need, forgoing a special connection, and important contacts from the past. The interpretation retains both compromised authority and hardship within an official tie.
