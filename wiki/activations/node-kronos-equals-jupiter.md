## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 152 (one-based).

Court action. Good connections with authorities. Reward. To receive money.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 230 (printed); PDF page: 114 (one-based).

Special connection to money, successful independence within a connection, joy in a special encounter, independent financial connections, state mediation in legal matters, a favorable connection to the state, and important relationships with the law.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Advantage, reward or financial benefit through official and legal connections.
- Psychology: Joy in an important encounter; successful independence within a connection.
- Body/health: Not addressed by these entries.
- Social/relationship: Favorable relations with authorities and the state.
- Events/manifestations: Court action, reward, receiving money and legal mediation.
- Conflicts/notes: Court action is not itself a guarantee of a favorable judgment.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize compatible benefits from official connections; Witte names court action and payment, while ABC adds independence and legal mediation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `Node/Kronos` as a connection with authorities or law that brings advantage, recognition or money. The relationship matters because it provides access to reward or favorable official dealings, rather than merely indicating contact with a powerful person. [Witte](#witte-entry) explicitly names court action, reward and receiving money; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful independence, financial connections and joy in a significant encounter. These are the entry's possible expressions, not a promise that every legal proceeding succeeds.
