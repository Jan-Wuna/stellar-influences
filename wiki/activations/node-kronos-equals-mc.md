## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 152 (one-based).

A leader among leading men. Connected with leading men. Professional intercourse with the authorities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 230 (printed); PDF page: 114 (one-based).

A special inner connection, my self-confidence within a connection, my respected connection, my contacts with authorities, my dominance in contacts, my connection to the boss, my connections to the state, and inner greatness within a relationship.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal involvement and standing among leaders or authorities.
- Psychology: Self-confidence, dominance and inner stature within a relationship.
- Body/health: Not addressed by these entries.
- Social/relationship: Respected personal ties with leaders, a boss or state authorities.
- Events/manifestations: Leadership among leaders and professional dealings with authorities.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries describe standing within connections, not a universal leadership role.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes professional leadership; ABC also describes the personal confidence and inner dimension of these connections. No direct disagreement is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies `Node/Kronos` through one's own place in a relationship with leaders and authorities. Professional contact can involve leading among other leaders, while the personal experience centers on confidence, standing and the wish to dominate a contact. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies the professional and leadership expressions; [ABC](#abc-entry) brings them into one's own connections with a boss, the state and respected people, including the inner stature experienced within a relationship.
