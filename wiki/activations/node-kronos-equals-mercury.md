## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 152 (one-based).

Great association of ideas. Correspondence with authorities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 230 (printed); PDF page: 114 (one-based).

Meeting with self-confident young people, the dominance of youth within connections, a state news connection, conveying state opinions, binding state discussions, important conversational connections, and an important connection with young people.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Official connections formed or maintained through ideas and communication.
- Psychology: Association of ideas and self-confidence in the youth-related examples.
- Body/health: Not addressed by these entries.
- Social/relationship: Correspondence with authorities and significant contacts with young people.
- Events/manifestations: State news, conveying official opinions and consequential discussions.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's youth motif is additional to Witte's official correspondence.

## Contradictions

Both entries support communication in important connections; ABC adds youth-related contacts without contradicting Witte's narrower statement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies `Node/Kronos` through the exchange of ideas, information and consequential speech within important connections. Official correspondence, state news and conveying state opinions make communication the means by which authority is contacted or represented. [Witte](#witte-entry) also names a great association of ideas; [ABC](#abc-entry) extends the social range to significant encounters with self-confident young people and their prominence within connections.
