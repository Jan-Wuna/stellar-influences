## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 152 (one-based).

Acquaintance with the future wife. Acquaintance or friendship with females of official character, in official position.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 230 (printed); PDF page: 114 (one-based).

Meeting with an independent woman, an especially emotional connection, the dominance of feelings within a connection, a meeting of the leaders of a people, a superior woman within a connection, and connections with the leading people.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Emotional or woman-centered connections involving standing and leadership.
- Psychology: Strong feelings that may dominate a connection.
- Body/health: Not addressed by these entries.
- Social/relationship: An independent or officially placed woman; Witte also names a future wife.
- Events/manifestations: Acquaintance, friendship and meetings involving a people's leaders.
- Conflicts/notes: Wife and female-role statements belong to the sources' historical framing.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes acquaintance with women in official positions and a future wife; ABC adds emotional intensity, independence and leaders' meetings. These are differing emphases rather than a stated disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies `Node/Kronos` through a significant connection with a woman or through feelings that become prominent within a relationship. The contact may involve an independent woman, someone in an official position, or the leaders of a people; [ABC](#abc-entry) also stresses emotional intensity and the dominance of feelings. [Witte's](#witte-entry) future-wife example retains its historical gender framing and should be read as one particular relationship claim, not a rule about every person with this formula.
