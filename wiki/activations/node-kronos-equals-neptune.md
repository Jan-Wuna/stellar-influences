- Source page: 231 (printed); PDF page: 114 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: disappointment or dissolution in connections with authority.
- psychology: uncertainty about a superior or official connection.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: unclear ties to the state and dissolving ties among leading people.
- events/manifestations: respite, release from prison, divorce, and unsuccessful official contacts; ABC also names water, air and metaphysics.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's release or respite can be welcome while its official connection fails; ABC adds uncertainty and nonlegal settings. These possibilities do not constitute a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `Node/Kronos` through connections with authority that become uncertain, disappointing or dissolved. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives both release from a binding situation and failed ties with officials or leading people; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) emphasizes lack of clarity and uncertainty with a superior. A release may therefore be welcome without making the connection itself successful.

ABC also names connections with water, air and metaphysics. These remain distinct possibilities within this entry, rather than reasons to treat every uncertain official relationship as spiritual or to promise divorce or release from custody.
