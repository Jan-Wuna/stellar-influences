### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 152 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

Desire to dominate. Developments, changes in connection with the authorities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 231 (printed); PDF page: 114 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

Develop special relationships, develop self-confidence in relationships, unfold superiority within a connection, develop connections to the state, a growing connection with the boss, important change through an encounter.

## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies `Node/Kronos` as the development or alteration of a significant connection with authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins changes involving officials to a desire to dominate, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes growing state or workplace ties and the development of confidence within relationships. The distinctive tension is between building a connection and seeking superiority through it; growth alone does not establish a mutually beneficial relationship.
