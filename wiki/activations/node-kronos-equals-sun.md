#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 230 (printed); PDF page: 114 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

Special meetings among men, dominant personal relationship, contacts with respected people, relations with people of the state, personal contacts with the boss, meetings in the local center, and centre for state meetings

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal involvement with officials or prominent people.
- psychology: dominance within a personal relationship is named in ABC.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: personal contacts with a superior, respected people or state representatives; ABC also uses historical male-specific wording.
- events/manifestations: being summoned, official entanglements, meetings at a local center or a center for state encounters.
- conflicts/notes: The entries differ in range and emphasis without establishing a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies `Node/Kronos` through direct personal contact with officials, superiors or respected people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes this involvement concrete as being entangled with officials or summoned; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also allows ordinary personal contacts and meeting places. The shared concern is one's actual involvement in the connection, with dominance as one possible relationship dynamic. ABC's meetings among men remain its historical gendered example, not a restriction on who can experience the formula.
