#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 231 (printed); PDF page: 114 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

Special contacts with technology, new contacts with leaders, sudden connection with the state, mediation of state reform, self-confidence within a tense connection, tensions in the connection with the boss, connection with an event of state.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sudden or tense contact with authority.
- psychology: self-confidence within tension and strain in the relationship with a superior.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: new contacts with leaders and sudden ties with the state.
- events/manifestations: technical contacts, mediation of state reform, and connection with an event of state.
- conflicts/notes: The entries differ in range and emphasis without establishing a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies `Node/Kronos` through an abrupt or tense connection with authorities. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives the compact theme of sudden official contact, and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops new ties with leaders, tensions with a superior and confidence within a strained connection. Technology, reform and events of state are named settings for that contact; their presence does not make every sudden encounter a political reform.
