## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 152 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

Acquaintance with the future husband. Harmonious connections with the authorities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 230 (printed); PDF page: 114 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

An especially harmonious connection, a great love connection, state mediation of peace, connections to the hierarchy of art, preference for a relationship with the boss, a benevolent connection to the state, desire for independence within a connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: harmony, affection or goodwill within a prominent or official connection.
- psychology: preference for the relationship and a desire for independence within it.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: a great love connection, harmonious state ties, and the historically phrased acquaintance with a future husband.
- events/manifestations: peace mediation by the state and connections to an artistic hierarchy.
- conflicts/notes: The entries differ in range and emphasis without establishing a direct contradiction.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in range and emphasis without establishing a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies `Node/Kronos` through harmony, affection or goodwill in a connection distinguished by authority or standing. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) pairs harmonious official ties with acquaintance with a future husband; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens the range to love, peace mediation and an artistic hierarchy. ABC also retains a wish for independence within the relationship, so harmony need not imply the surrender of autonomy. Witte's future-husband statement remains a historical relationship example rather than a marriage prediction.
