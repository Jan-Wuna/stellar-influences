## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies `Node/Kronos` through public contact with authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names authorities and courts, and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) emphasizes state meetings and significant relationships in public life. The organizing meaning is the public setting and standing of the connection; the evidence does not require a legal dispute, and ABC also allows independence within these public ties.
