## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies `Node/Kronos` through the power and influence carried by official connections. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes state power explicit, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds standing gained through those ties, powerful links with leaders and the influence of an independent connection. State mediation of energy is another concrete ABC expression. The common thread is the force available through the association, without establishing how that force will be used.
