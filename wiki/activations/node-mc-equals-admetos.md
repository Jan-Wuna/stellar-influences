## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 61 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

Difficulty to get connections. Sterile relationships. Feeling trapped in connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 67 (printed); PDF page: 32 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

Perseverance in my connections, depression within a soul connection, the end of my connection, a thorough connection to the soul, I strengthen connections, my restricted connections, inner resistance within a connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal bonds experienced as difficult, fixed or restricted.
- psychology: feeling trapped, inner resistance and perseverance; ABC's depression remains historical source language.
- body/health: no physical condition is stated; the references to sterility and depression describe the sources' relationship and psychological vocabulary, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: difficulty forming connections and relationships experienced as unproductive.
- events/manifestations: ending a connection or strengthening an existing one are both named possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: Perseverance and strengthening coexist with restriction and endings; these are different possibilities within a fixed connection, not a promised outcome.

## Contradictions

Perseverance and strengthening coexist with restriction and endings; these are different possibilities within a fixed connection, not a promised outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies `Node/MC` through a personally significant connection that feels fixed, difficult to establish or hard to leave. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) stresses unproductive relationships and feeling trapped, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes both inner resistance and the perseverance that strengthens a bond. The distinctive question is whether the connection is being deepened or merely endured; its ending is another stated possibility. The sources' sterility and depression wording establishes no bodily or clinical diagnosis.
