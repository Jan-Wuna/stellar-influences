## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 61 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

Far reaching connections. The free choice of personal connections. Personal relationships to scientists, commerce, trade or religion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 67 (printed); PDF page: 32 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

Expansion of my connections, successful soul contacts, my experiences in connections, my trading connections, my connections to science, contacts with experienced souls, inner experiences within connections.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: expansion, experience and freedom of choice in personal connections.
- psychology: learning through relationships and inner experiences.
- body/health: no bodily or medical condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: far-reaching contacts, freely chosen relationships and contacts with experienced people.
- events/manifestations: relationships involving science, commerce, trade or religion.
- conflicts/notes: Witte names freedom of choice and religion; ABC emphasizes successful contacts and experience. The entries establish no direct disagreement.

## Contradictions

Witte names freedom of choice and religion; ABC emphasizes successful contacts and experience. The entries establish no direct disagreement.

## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies `Node/MC` through the widening of personally meaningful connections and the experience gained through them. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes far-reaching ties and freedom to choose one's associates; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful contacts, trade and science, and encounters with experienced souls. Relationships can therefore broaden both a person's network and inner experience. Religion is an additional Witte setting, without making every broad association religious or commercially successful.
