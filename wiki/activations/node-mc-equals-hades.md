## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 61 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

To be linked with evil things, old or historical matters. To suffer a mental depression in a relationship.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 67 (printed); PDF page: 32 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

My sorrow within connections, my secret contacts, my criminal connections, my connections from the past, my connections in need, disadvantages of an inner relationship, a connection of old souls, a hidden soul connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal ties burdened by sorrow, secrecy, hardship or the past.
- psychology: sorrow and the historical statement of mental depression within a relationship.
- body/health: Witte's mental-depression wording is an attributed historical claim; the formula supplies no clinical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: secret, disadvantaged or criminal associations are possibilities named alongside old or hidden soul connections.
- events/manifestations: connections involving past or historical matters and relationships formed or experienced in need.
- conflicts/notes: The source's evaluative evil and criminal wording must not be applied as a moral verdict on a person; historical interests and old-soul ties remain distinct possibilities.

## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies `Node/MC` through personally significant bonds that carry sorrow, secrecy, hardship or the weight of the past. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes old and historical matters alongside distress in a relationship; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the range to hidden contacts, need and connections of old souls. These ties may concern history or an inward bond as well as the sources' more difficult associations. Witte's evil and mental-depression language and ABC's criminal wording remain attributed possibilities, not moral judgments or diagnoses of the people involved.
