## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 61 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

Happiness through ones own connections. Blissful feelings in relationships.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 66 (printed); PDF page: 32 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

Joy in a soul connection, happiness in a soul connection, a happy connection in the minute, my good fortune in mediation, my successful connections, my financial connections, my legal connections, my joy in contact.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 304 (printed); PDF page: 306 (one-based); entry code: 1100 (direct source text checked).

Love of jolly parties and entertainment. A liking of social meetings with others in a cozy atmosphere. A happy communion of souls.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page(s): 121 (one-based; exact activation located in direct source text).

To be enthusiastic about connecting to the world. Success in evolving toward a stronger connection to one’s spiritual path. A rich, full manner of connecting to all of re ality. The encouragement of progress in the world.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NODE/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `360`

#### Munkasey Entry

Meeting with groups or people who share familiar ideas about philosophy, religion, or law to yours; large numbers of people who have similar origins or expertise; acquaintances with others who share similar beliefs and ideas.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: joy, encouragement and success through personally meaningful associations.
- psychology: enthusiasm about relating to the world and encouragement of spiritual progress.
- body/health: no bodily or medical condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: happy companionship, convivial meetings and associations with shared beliefs or origins.
- events/manifestations: social entertainment and contacts involving finance, law, philosophy or religion.
- conflicts/notes: The entries are broadly favorable; Sandbach supplies a spiritual-growth extension and Munkasey shared-belief settings. No direct disagreement is established.

## Contradictions

The entries are broadly favorable; Sandbach supplies a spiritual-growth extension and Munkasey shared-belief settings. No direct disagreement is established.

## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `Node/MC` through the joy and encouragement found in personal connections. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) emphasize happy companionship and convivial gatherings; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful, financial and legal ties, and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) describes people joined by familiar beliefs, origins or expertise. The bond is rewarding because it brings pleasure, affinity or useful opportunity.

[Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) extends this enthusiasm into a fuller relationship with the world and progress on a spiritual path. Shared pleasure, affinity and encouragement therefore sit alongside this specifically spiritual form of growth.
