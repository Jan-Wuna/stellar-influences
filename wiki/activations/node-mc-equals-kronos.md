## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies `Node/MC` as a personally significant connection marked by authority, leadership or unusual inner stature. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names contact with authorities and mentally elevated relationships; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes one's own authority within a connection, a superior and a great soul. The meaning extends from an official encounter to a bond experienced as inwardly important, while keeping dominance as a possible relationship dynamic.
