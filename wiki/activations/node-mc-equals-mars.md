## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 61 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

An erotic soul union. To seek fellowship in one’s work. Emotional bonding to one’s own work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 66 (printed); PDF page: 32 (one-based PDF numbering; exact row checked).

I create connections, my activity in contacts, my work connections, the minute of establishing a connection, soul work within a connection, wanting a soul encounter, establishing inner contacts, inner impulses in contacts.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 304 (printed); PDF page: 306 (one-based); entry code: 1099 (direct source text checked).

Comradeship and team spirit, intensely working with one's colleagues, an association or organization fighting to uphold the common ideas and principles. Inner attachment and physical attraction.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page(s): 120–121 (one-based; exact activation located in direct source text).

Working hard to advance one’s position in the world. To be conflicted about clinging to old ways of being in the world versus new more progressive and evolution ary ways. The desire to align one’s efforts with progressive spiritual values.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NODE/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `360`

#### Munkasey Entry

Immediate or rapid meetings with people who have principles similar to yours; aggressive people or groups who try to force their attitudes upon you; noisy encounters or meetings with others who have similar ideas to yours.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: active engagement in personally meaningful bonds, shared work or common aims.
- psychology: team spirit and emotional attachment, with a conflict between familiar security and progressive values.
- body/health: Ebertin names physical attraction and Witte an erotic soul union; neither establishes a medical condition.
- social/relationship: comradeship and close cooperation can coexist with aggressive people who force their attitudes on others.
- events/manifestations: intense joint work, associations defending shared principles, and rapid or noisy encounters.
- conflicts/notes: Cooperative effort and coercive encounters are conditional alternatives. Sandbach's spiritual conflict adds a specific inner dimension.

## Contradictions

Cooperative effort and coercive encounters are conditional alternatives. Sandbach's spiritual conflict adds a specific inner dimension.

## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies `Node/MC` through taking action to form a meaningful bond or pursue shared work. [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) connect initiative with fellowship, emotional attachment and team effort; erotic or physical attraction is also explicit. The connection becomes something actively sought and worked through.

The same force can press on others: [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) includes aggressive groups and noisy encounters, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes effort to advance alongside conflict between old security and progressive spiritual values. The distinction is between energy serving a shared purpose and energy imposing an attitude on the relationship.
