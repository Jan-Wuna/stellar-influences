## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 61 (one-based; exact row checked).

Dissolution of personal connections. Unreliable associations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 67 (printed); PDF page: 32 (one-based; exact row checked).

Dissolution of psychic contacts, sensitivity of a soul connection, I connect with the future, my sensitivity in connections, my encounter with metaphysics, my disappointing connections, and sensitivity in my connections

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 304 (printed); PDF page(s): 306–307 (one-based); entry code: 1103 (direct source text checked).

A lack of adaptability in individual relationships, experiencing difficulties in maintaining contacts and associations with other people. Separation caused through the process of sobering and "coming down to earth again" and through disappointment or deceit. Mourning and bereavement, a grievous loss.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 121 (one-based; exact activation located in direct source text).

Confusion as to how to progress in one’s work or one’s spiritual path, and possibility to have unrealistic or impractical notions about these things.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NODE/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `360`

#### Munkasey Entry

Deceit encountered from well meaning people who offer to help you meet your goals or increase your stature; illusive goals set at meetings; encountering others whose ambitions are vague but which sound familiar.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: unclear, disappointing or dissolving personal connections.
- psychology: sensitivity and difficulty adapting, together with confusion about work or spiritual progress.
- body/health: no bodily or medical condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: unreliable associations and apparently helpful people whose intentions or goals prove misleading.
- events/manifestations: separation, disillusionment, mourning or bereavement are named possibilities; ABC adds metaphysical contacts and connecting with the future.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's sensitivity and metaphysics broaden the range; they do not cancel the other entries' disappointment. Bereavement is a historical source possibility, not a prediction.

## Contradictions

ABC's sensitivity and metaphysics broaden the range; they do not cancel the other entries' disappointment. Bereavement is a historical source possibility, not a prediction.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `Node/MC` through a personally meaningful connection whose reliability or direction becomes uncertain. [Witte](#witte-entry) names unreliable associations and dissolution; [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describes difficulty sustaining contacts and separation through disillusionment, disappointment or deceit. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) makes the ambiguity concrete in people who offer help but bring vague goals or deception.

Sensitivity remains part of the range in [ABC](#abc-entry), alongside metaphysical contact and connecting with the future. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds confusion about progress in work or a spiritual path. The distinction is between a subtle inner connection and a clear, workable association; the sources do not make one proof of the other. Ebertin's bereavement language remains an attributed possibility, not a forecast.
