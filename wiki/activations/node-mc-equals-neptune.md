## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Dissolution of personal connections. Unreliable associations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of psychic contacts, sensitivity of a soul connection, I connect with the future, my sensitivity in connections, my encounter with metaphysics, my disappointing connections, and sensitivity in my connections

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A lack of adaptability in individual relationships, experiencing difficulties in maintaining contacts and associations with other people. Separation caused through the process of sobering and "coming down to earth again" and through disappointment or deceit. Mourning and bereavement, a grievous loss.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Confusion as to how to progress in one’s work or one’s spiritual path, and possibility to have unrealistic or impractical notions about these things.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Deceit encountered from well meaning people who offer to help you meet your goals or increase your stature; illusive goals set at meetings; encountering others whose ambitions are vague but which sound familiar.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Node/MC = Neptune`: humanitarian or artistic association, symbolic belonging, and the ability to move toward purpose through subtle communal feeling. Main caution: misleading alliance: direction can be clouded by projection, vague loyalties, or groups that drain rather than guide.
