## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 61 (one-based; exact row checked).

To have and to seek for mental connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 67 (printed); PDF page: 32 (one-based; exact row checked).

Insights from my connections, clarity within my connections, my relationships with culture, my intellectual or spiritual connections, my connection to truth, the minute of an intellectual or spiritual connection, connection between spirit and soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: seeking mental or spiritual connection, clarity and insight through relationships.
- psychology: insight, clarity and a relationship to truth.
- body/health: no bodily or medical condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: mental or spiritual associations and connections with culture.
- events/manifestations: an encounter experienced as a connection between spirit and soul.
- conflicts/notes: Witte is brief; ABC adds culture, clarity and truth without establishing a direct disagreement.

## Contradictions

Witte is brief; ABC adds culture, clarity and truth without establishing a direct disagreement.

## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies `Node/MC` through a connection of mind or spirit that brings understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supports both having and seeking mental ties; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops insight, clarity, truth and cultural relationships. The personally meaningful bond is therefore a setting for understanding or an experienced connection between spirit and soul, without requiring every intellectual acquaintance to carry all of those meanings.
