## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 61 (one-based; exact row checked).

The own devotion in connections. Personal love connections. Soulful love union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 66 (printed); PDF page: 32 (one-based; exact row checked).

A soulful love connection, joy through a soul connection, a peaceful soul encounter, harmonious soul contact, my relationships with art, beauty of the soul within a connection, desiring a soul connection, preference for soul connections.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 304 (printed); PDF page(s): 306 (one-based); entry code: 1098 (direct source text checked).

Urge to reveal one's innermost feelings and inclinations in personal associations. The capacity to love with one's whole heart.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 120 (one-based; exact activation located in direct source text).

Love causes one to grow in terms of how one relates to the world. A desire for comfort and superficial gratification causes one to hold on to old ways of looking at the world. An appreciation of how the world needs to grow and evolve. Smooth and posi tive connections with authority figures.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `NODE/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `360`

#### Munkasey Entry

Intimacy formed from friendships with those you met thru groups or associations which perform for the arts, music, or sculpture; garden or beautification clubs with those who have interests similar to your own.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: love, devotion and harmony in personally meaningful connections.
- psychology: revealing intimate feelings and loving wholeheartedly; comfort can also preserve limiting ways of relating.
- body/health: no bodily or medical condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: personal love bonds, intimacy arising from friendship and positive relations with authorities.
- events/manifestations: connections through art, music, sculpture, gardens or beautification groups.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach qualifies love's growth with attachment to comfort and superficial gratification; the source entries do not reduce all harmony to shallowness.

## Contradictions

Sandbach qualifies love's growth with attachment to comfort and superficial gratification; the source entries do not reduce all harmony to shallowness.

## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies `Node/MC` through the affection and devotion that make a bond inwardly fulfilling. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes soulful love, [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) opening one's innermost feelings, and [ABC](#abc-entry) joy, peace and beauty within connection. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) gives concrete settings in friendships formed through art, music, gardens and similar interests.

Love may also change how a person relates to the wider world. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) presents that growth beside a desire for comfort or superficial gratification that keeps old views intact, while retaining positive ties with authorities. The tension concerns growth within affection, not a requirement that a harmonious bond must lack substance.
