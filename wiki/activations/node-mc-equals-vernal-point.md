## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 61 (one-based; exact row checked).

Mental connections with many. Personal relationships in public life or to the world in general.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 66 (printed); PDF page: 32 (one-based; exact row checked).

Soulful connections in public, bonds with my public, my relationships in public life, my connections with the public, inwardly connected with the public, inner contacts in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personally meaningful connections in public life or with the wider world.
- psychology: inner involvement with the public and mental connection with many people.
- body/health: no bodily or medical condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: personal bonds with one's public or a broad circle of people.
- events/manifestations: public encounters and relationships extending into the world at large.
- conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes many people and the wider world; ABC emphasizes one's own inward bond with a public. No direct disagreement is established.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes many people and the wider world; ABC emphasizes one's own inward bond with a public. No direct disagreement is established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies `Node/MC` through the extension of personally meaningful contact into public life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins mental connection with many people to relationships with the world at large; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes one's own public and the inner bond with it. The formula concerns personal participation in that wider field of relationships, without by itself establishing fame, office or a particular public event.
