## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 61 (one-based; exact row checked).

To strive for powerful and influential connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 67 (printed); PDF page: 32 (one-based; exact row checked).

Power of the soul within a connection, my energy within connections, my influential connections, my connective force, the minute of force within a connection, inner strength of a relationship, a strong soul connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: the strength and influence of personally important connections.
- psychology: striving for influential ties and experiencing inner strength within a bond.
- body/health: no bodily or medical condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: powerful personal associations and strong inner relationships.
- events/manifestations: no particular external event is specified beyond seeking influential contacts.
- conflicts/notes: ABC names Gewalt as well as Kraft and Macht; force and inner strength are related possibilities without establishing that every powerful tie is violent.

## Contradictions

ABC names Gewalt as well as Kraft and Macht; force and inner strength are related possibilities without establishing that every powerful tie is violent.

## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies `Node/MC` through the strength and influence of a personally meaningful bond. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes seeking powerful connections; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) turns to one's energy in relationships, their inward strength and the force of connection itself. The range therefore includes both an influential association and a bond experienced as inwardly strong. ABC's force wording does not require violence in every such relationship.
