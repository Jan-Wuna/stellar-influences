## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 61 (one-based; exact row checked).

To be incited. To be aroused mentally. Want to achieve the personal goal in a relationship.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 67 (printed); PDF page: 32 (one-based; exact row checked).

Beginning of an inner connection, a fiery soul connection, my efforts within connections, my goals within the connection, the minute of a procreative connection, leadership within an inner connection, striving for a soul connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: arousal, initiative and the pursuit of a personal goal within a relationship.
- psychology: mental stimulation, desire, effort and goal-directed striving.
- body/health: ABC names a procreative connection; it does not supply a fertility or medical prediction.
- social/relationship: a passionate inner bond and leadership within a connection.
- events/manifestations: beginning a connection and directing effort toward a personal relationship goal.
- conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes being incited and pursuing one's goal; ABC adds passion, leadership and procreation. These are distinct expressions of initiative, not a direct contradiction.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes being incited and pursuing one's goal; ABC adds passion, leadership and procreation. These are distinct expressions of initiative, not a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies `Node/MC` through the impulse to begin a meaningful connection or achieve something within it. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes mental arousal and a personal relationship goal; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) joins effort and striving to a fiery soul bond and leadership within the connection. Procreative union is another explicit ABC possibility, while the wider meaning remains the desire and directed effort invested in the bond.
