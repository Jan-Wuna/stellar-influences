### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 140 (one-based numbering; exact row checked).

Small exclusive circle. Connections with a few. Shackles. Connections which are oppressive and are a burden. Few connections. Reliable or very close connections. Static or unchangeable connections. Connections which are of great hindrance. Joining raw materials.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: few, close or fixed connections that can be reliable or restrictive.
- psychology: feeling burdened or oppressed is supported, but a complete personality profile is not.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is given in this entry.
- social/relationship: a small exclusive circle and very close or reliable relationships.
- events/manifestations: hindering or unchangeable ties, shackles, and joining raw materials.
- conflicts/notes: Closeness and reliability coexist with burden and hindrance as alternatives; few connections are not inherently difficult.

## Contradictions

Closeness and reliability coexist with burden and hindrance as alternatives; few connections are not inherently difficult. Only the Witte entry is represented here.

## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies `Node/Node` through a limited or fixed connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) spans a small reliable circle and very close ties as well as oppressive, static relationships that become a burden. The distinction is between the security of a firm bond and the hindrance of one that cannot change. Joining raw materials is a separate material expression of the same entry, not evidence that every close human connection is restrictive.
