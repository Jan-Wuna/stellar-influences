#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 139 (one-based numbering; exact row checked).

Acquaintances. To establish connections. To keep in touch. Contact with the closer environment. Limits set by the environment. The mediator, broker or an agent. Being connected to one place. Connected locations. Links to the surroundings.

## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies `Node/Node` through the people and places in one's immediate surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins making acquaintances and keeping in touch to mediation, brokerage and links between locations. Connection is therefore both interpersonal and local. The same surroundings can set limits or bind someone to one place, so the entry includes the boundary of contact as well as the means of establishing it.
