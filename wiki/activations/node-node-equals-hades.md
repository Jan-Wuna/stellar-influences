## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 140 (one-based numbering; exact row checked).

A mean act committed jointly. Connections started under difficulties or with reluctance. Unpleasant connections or contacts. Poor connections or associations. Contact with disease, evil, poverty or old affairs.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: connections marked by difficulty, reluctance, hardship or old affairs.
- psychology: reluctance and an unpleasant experience of contact are explicit.
- body/health: contact with disease is a source possibility, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: poor or difficult associations and a mean act committed jointly.
- events/manifestations: contacts involving poverty, disease or old matters.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's evil and mean-act language is historical evaluation, not a verdict on every person in a difficult relationship.

## Contradictions

Witte's evil and mean-act language is historical evaluation, not a verdict on every person in a difficult relationship. Only the Witte entry is represented here.

## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies `Node/Node` through connections formed or experienced under difficulty. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins reluctance and unpleasant associations to contact with poverty, disease or old affairs. The supported range includes adverse circumstances as well as the past; it does not make every old connection harmful. The source's mean-act and evil wording remains its evaluative language, and contact with disease establishes no diagnosis.
