## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies `Node/Node` through a connection experienced as personally or inwardly significant. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins soul mates and emotional bonds to one's close contacts and professional relationships. Its moment or minute of contact also emphasizes the making of the link itself. This preserves both personal intimacy and professional connection without making every contact a soul-mate relationship.
