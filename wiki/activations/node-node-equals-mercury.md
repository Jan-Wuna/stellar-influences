### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 139 (one-based numbering; exact row checked).

Connections of thoughts. Correspondence. To make new connection. Intellectual or communications ties. Contacts or connections for communication.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: connection through thought, correspondence or communication.
- psychology: intellectual links are stated without a wider psychological profile.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is given in this entry.
- social/relationship: contacts made for communication and new connections.
- events/manifestations: correspondence and establishing a new link through communication.
- conflicts/notes: An intellectual or communicative tie does not by itself establish emotional closeness or agreement.

## Contradictions

An intellectual or communicative tie does not by itself establish emotional closeness or agreement. Only the Witte entry is represented here.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies `Node/Node` through thought and communication as the link between people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes correspondence, intellectual ties and making a new connection. The passage therefore supports both exchanging ideas within a relationship and communication that establishes the relationship itself. It does not state that shared contact entails agreement or emotional intimacy.
