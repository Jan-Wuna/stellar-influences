## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 140 (one-based numbering; exact row checked).

New connections. Changes through connections. Acquaintances who experience changes. Relationships that can change events or who expect changes. Development or transformation in relationships. Renewing a relationship.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: development, renewal or change through connections.
- psychology: expectation of change is named, without a broader personality claim.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is given in this entry.
- social/relationship: new contacts, changing acquaintances and renewed relationships.
- events/manifestations: changes in events through relationships and transformation within a bond.
- conflicts/notes: The entry includes change within a relationship and change brought by it; it does not determine whether either is beneficial.

## Contradictions

The entry includes change within a relationship and change brought by it; it does not determine whether either is beneficial. Only the Witte entry is represented here.

## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies `Node/Node` through a connection that develops, renews or alters circumstances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) covers new ties, acquaintances undergoing change and relationships capable of changing events. The association can therefore be the subject of transformation or a means through which change occurs. Renewal is explicit, while the passage leaves the value and direction of the change open.
