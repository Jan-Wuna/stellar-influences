## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 139 (one-based numbering; exact row checked).

Breaking off of connections or other relationships. Difficulties in relationships or with contacts. To participate in separations. End of a connection. Long-term and serious connections. Separations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: serious or long-term connections alongside difficulty and separation.
- psychology: the entry does not specify a motive for remaining or separating.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is given in this entry.
- social/relationship: difficult contacts and serious relationships.
- events/manifestations: breaking off a connection, participating in separation or maintaining a long-term tie.
- conflicts/notes: Endings and long duration are alternative possibilities within the same single-source entry, not a contradiction to flatten.

## Contradictions

Endings and long duration are alternative possibilities within the same single-source entry, not a contradiction to flatten. Only the Witte entry is represented here.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies `Node/Node` through the seriousness, difficulty or limit of a connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives breaking off and separation a prominent place, but also preserves long-term and serious ties. These are different possible conditions of the bond, so the formula cannot be reduced either to inevitable separation or to guaranteed endurance.
