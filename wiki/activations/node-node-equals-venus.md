## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies `Node/Node` through the pleasure, affection or harmony of a connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places love ties and caring contacts beside relationships formed around peace, beauty, music and the arts. Its references to women and a motherly figure remain historical examples within that range. The unifying meaning is a pleasant or loving link, without reducing all artistic contacts to romance.
