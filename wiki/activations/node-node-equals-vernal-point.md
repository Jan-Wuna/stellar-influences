## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies `Node/Node` through a connection extending into the general public or a wider network. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes alliances of many people and international relationships. The formula therefore emphasizes the breadth and public field of association, while leaving the intimacy, duration and quality of each link unstated.
