## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies `Node/Node` through the force and strength of a connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes a stable tie and influential association alongside eagerness, command and the application of force. The distinction is between strength that maintains the bond and power exercised through it. The passage does not establish whether the relationship is mutual or how its influence is used.
