## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 140 (one-based numbering; exact row checked).

To elect. To select. To join together with others for creative accomplishment. Union for procreation. Union of fire. Work in blast furnaces. Procreation. Strive for a connection. Connections in matters of stoves, fire, firearms or machines. To form a relationship for creative accomplishment. Impelled by curiosity for connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: joining for creative accomplishment, procreation or purposeful action.
- psychology: curiosity, choice and striving to connect are explicit.
- body/health: procreation is named without a fertility prediction.
- social/relationship: forming a relationship to create or accomplish something together.
- events/manifestations: election or selection, procreation, and connections involving fire, furnaces, firearms or machines.
- conflicts/notes: The creative, reproductive and technical meanings are separate supported possibilities, not a single compulsory event.

## Contradictions

The creative, reproductive and technical meanings are separate supported possibilities, not a single compulsory event. Only the Witte entry is represented here.

## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies `Node/Node` through a connection chosen or sought for accomplishment. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links selection, curiosity and striving with joining others to create, while also naming procreation. Fire, furnaces, firearms and machines preserve a distinct technical range. These expressions share directed joining or production, without turning every creative association into a reproductive or hazardous one.
