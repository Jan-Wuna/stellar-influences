## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be mentally connected with many. Connections which occur on account of similar views in relation to world affairs or science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Extension of mental connections, relationships with culture knowledge, knowledge proliferation in one connection, conveying spiritual knowledge, clear trade connections, conveying culture knowledge, connection to spirit science, and many ideas in connections

## Comparative Schema

The categories below summarize the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 156) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 239; PDF p. 118).

- Core meaning: An expanding network linked by knowledge and shared outlook.
- Psychology: Interest in ideas and the circulation of understanding.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim appears in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Associations around science, world affairs and culture.
- Events/manifestations: Sharing knowledge, extending contacts and cultural or trade exchange.
- Conflicts/notes: The examples describe kinds of association, not a guarantee of reach or success.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in breadth: Witte stresses shared views, while ABC includes cultural and trade mediation. These emphases can coexist; they do not establish independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies `Node/Poseidon` as a widening network of people joined by ideas, learning or views of the world. The emphasis falls on extending and sharing knowledge through connections: scientific or philosophical association in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), and cultural knowledge, intellectual exchange and trade contacts in the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry). The range is broader than a single friendship, but the entries do not establish how large or successful a particular network will be.
