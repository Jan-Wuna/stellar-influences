## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be mentally associated with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Insightful mediation between partners, mental connection with one's surroundings, an ideal connection with a partner, clarity in a partnership, connection with the culture of one's surroundings, sharing understanding of one's surroundings, reasonable contacts with one's surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

The categories below summarize the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 156) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 238; PDF p. 118).

- Core meaning: Mental or ideal association with others.
- Psychology: Clarity and understanding within a connection.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim appears in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Partnership and contact with one's surroundings.
- Events/manifestations: Mediating between partners and sharing cultural understanding.
- Conflicts/notes: The former ABC bullet-only translation concealed a substantive German entry; it has been replaced from the scan.

## Contradictions

No incompatible claim is apparent in the two short entries. Witte gives a general association; ABC supplies partnership and environmental examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies `Node/Poseidon` through an intellectual or ideal connection with another person and the immediate surroundings. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) states the association briefly; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) develops it as understanding between partners, clarity within a relationship and sharing cultural understanding with one's environment. The passage supports a quality of contact, without specifying a particular relationship event.
