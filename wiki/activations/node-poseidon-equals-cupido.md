## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Connections through mental associations, high IQ societies, sects etc. Member of such unions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

encounter with of a community of ideas, spiritual connections in of a community, shared idea connections, culture mediating community, connected with culture and art, meeting of a spiritual community, and connection to the spiritual whole

## Comparative Schema

The categories below summarize the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 156) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 239; PDF p. 118).

- Core meaning: Community formed through shared ideas or culture.
- Psychology: Identification with a shared intellectual or spiritual outlook.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim appears in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Membership in associations, cultural groups or spiritual communities.
- Events/manifestations: Meetings and participation in common cultural or artistic activity.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's high-IQ and sect examples are source-specific, not universal descriptions of members.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize different examples of idea-based community rather than mutually exclusive claims. Their similarity should not be counted as independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies `Node/Poseidon` as belonging to a community organized around shared ideas, intellectual interests or cultural activity. Membership and meeting others are central: the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) names mental associations and sects, while the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) includes artistic and cultural communities. These examples describe the basis of affiliation; they do not establish the intelligence, merit or beliefs of every member.
