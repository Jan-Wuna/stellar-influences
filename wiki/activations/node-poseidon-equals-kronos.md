## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Connections with mentally, superior people. To let others influence oneself.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Meeting with the state culture, clear connections with the state, contacts with the spiritual hierarchy, mediation of special ideas, connection with a spiritual great, connection with intellectual authoritates, communication of special ideas, and important cultural relationship

## Comparative Schema

The categories below summarize the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 156) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 239; PDF p. 118).

- Core meaning: Intellectual or cultural connection involving authority or prominence.
- Psychology: Receptivity to another person's intellectual influence.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim appears in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Contacts with intellectual authorities, cultural leaders or the state.
- Events/manifestations: Sharing special ideas and forming significant cultural relationships.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's superiority language is an attributed evaluation; authority does not establish correctness.

## Contradictions

Personal influence in Witte and institutional contacts in ABC are different scales of the same stated theme, not a demonstrated contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies `Node/Poseidon` through relationships with intellectual authorities, cultural leadership or state institutions. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) stresses allowing oneself to be influenced by people judged mentally superior; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) places contacts with notable thinkers and the exchange of special ideas alongside state culture. Read together, the entries concern authority operating through a connection, without making rank a measure of a person's worth or assuring sound guidance.
