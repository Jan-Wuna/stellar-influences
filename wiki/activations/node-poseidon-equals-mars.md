## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Connections gained through one’s own mental accomplishments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Efforts to establish mental contacts, convey mental impulses, ideal actions in a connection, clear actions in a connection, establish clarity in a connection, mediate cultural work, and create cultural contacts

## Comparative Schema

The categories below summarize the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 156) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 238; PDF p. 118).

- Core meaning: Active effort creates or clarifies intellectual connections.
- Psychology: Initiative in communicating ideas and seeking understanding.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim appears in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Contacts made through mental accomplishment or cultural work.
- Events/manifestations: Mediating cultural work and establishing clear exchanges.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's former 'Impediments' mistranslated Bemühungen, which means efforts in this passage.

## Contradictions

The apparent obstruction in the old English ABC wording was a translation error. The scanned German supports effort, consistent with Witte's emphasis on accomplishment.

## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies `Node/Poseidon` as actively making intellectual or cultural connections through one's own work. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) ties the relationship to mental accomplishment, and the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) describes efforts to make contacts, communicate ideas, bring clarity into a connection and mediate cultural work. The emphasis is on doing something that creates or clarifies the relationship, rather than assuming that contact arrives without effort.
