## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The native has mental and spiritual connections. The mental connections affect the native.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Soulful cultural connection, clarity in an emotional relationship, my cultural connections, my mental connections, my ideal relationships, my connection with truth, connection of mind and soul, reason in an emotional connection.

## Comparative Schema

The categories below summarize the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 156) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 238; PDF p. 118).

- Core meaning: The personal significance of mental, cultural or spiritual ties.
- Psychology: Connections with ideals and truth, and their effect on inner life.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim appears in these entries.
- Social/relationship: One's own cultural and intellectual relationships.
- Events/manifestations: The entries describe involvement and influence rather than a specific event.
- Conflicts/notes: The damaged ABC references to 'sea' were translation errors for Seele, soul.

## Contradictions

The corrected ABC passage elaborates the personal influence stated in Witte. No explicit incompatibility appears in these entries, but this does not prove independent agreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies `Node/Poseidon` as the personal, inward significance of intellectual or spiritual relationships. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) says these connections affect the individual; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) develops connections with culture, ideals and truth, together with the relation of mind and soul. The organizing theme is how a connection becomes part of one's inner life, without identifying a particular event or psychological condition.
