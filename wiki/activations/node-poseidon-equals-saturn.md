## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To have to deal with educators and students. Serious or separated mental connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Spiritual connections in old age, mental problems in one connection, clarity of a mature relationship, concentration on clarity in a relationship, cultural tasks in one connection, and painful contacts with truth

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 156; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 239, PDF p. 118.

- Core meaning: Seriousness and limits within intellectual or cultural ties.
- Psychology: Concentration on clarity and difficult encounters with truth.
- Body/health: The entries do not establish a medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Educator-student relationships and mental connections in later life.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural tasks, mature relationships and possible separation.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's 'one direction' has been corrected to 'a relationship' from Beziehung.

## Contradictions

Teaching, mature connection and separation describe different manifestations. The passages do not state that serious relationships necessarily end.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies `Node/Poseidon` through seriousness, responsibility or separation in intellectual relationships. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) includes educators and students, while the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds mature relationships, cultural tasks and the difficult work of facing truth. The relationship may demand concentration or encounter limits; maturity, pain and separation are distinct examples rather than a single inevitable sequence.
