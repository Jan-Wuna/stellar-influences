## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To hold mental leadership. To be outstanding in mental connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Efforts in a cultural connection, relationships with intellectual leadership, intellectual striving in a relationship, clarity in a procreation connection, connection with a fiery spirit, connection with intellectual leaders.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 156; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 239, PDF p. 118.

- Core meaning: Intellectual leadership and effort within a connection.
- Psychology: Striving and a fiery intellectual impulse.
- Body/health: ABC mentions procreation connections without a clinical claim.
- Social/relationship: Relationships with intellectual leaders.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural effort and taking a leading intellectual role.
- Conflicts/notes: Leadership and procreation are separate source examples.

## Contradictions

The corrected German concerns Führung and Führenden, leadership and leaders, rather than generic movement or progress.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies `Node/Poseidon` as purposeful intellectual leadership within a connection. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes standing out and holding mental leadership; the corrected [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) includes effort in cultural relationships, contact with intellectual leaders and a fiery spirit. Its procreation example is retained as a separate source expression, without turning leadership or enthusiasm into a claim about fertility.
