## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Mighty connection with few. End of a mighty union or connection.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Persistent violence in connections, blocked transmission of energy, restriction of influential connections, end of a powerful connection, consolidation of a powerful connection, deepening of influence in a connection.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 155; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 237, PDF p. 117.

- Core meaning: Concentration, restriction or ending of powerful ties.
- Psychology: No distinct inner trait is established by the entries.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim is stated.
- Social/relationship: A powerful connection with a few people.
- Events/manifestations: Blocked energy exchange, consolidation or the end of a union.
- Conflicts/notes: A small circle is not inherently a difficult or violent expression.

## Contradictions

The entries allow both consolidation and ending. The former synthesis wrongly labeled a connection with few as necessarily difficult.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies `Node/Vulcanus` as a powerful connection narrowed, blocked or brought to an end. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) places strong ties with few people beside the ending of a mighty union; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also includes consolidation and deepened influence. A restricted circle can concentrate a connection without itself being harmful, while blockage, persistent violence and dissolution remain separate possibilities.
