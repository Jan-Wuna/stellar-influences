## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Success and promotion through mighty connection. To cause much excitement, excitation. Many get excited.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of violence in connections, combining influence with knowledge, influential relations with the economy, powerful trade connections, communicating knowledge energetically, combining knowledge and power, and increasing influence in connections

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 155; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 237, PDF p. 117.

- Core meaning: Expansion of influential connections and their effects.
- Psychology: Excitement or energetic communication of knowledge.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim is stated.
- Social/relationship: Influential economic and trade relationships.
- Events/manifestations: Promotion, wider influence and many people becoming excited.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC includes expanding violence as well as combining knowledge and power.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes advancement and excitement; ABC also names coercive expansion. These possibilities should remain distinguishable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies `Node/Vulcanus` as influential connections that spread, mobilize many people or join power with knowledge. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) includes advancement through a powerful connection and widespread excitement; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds commerce, economic relations and energetic sharing of knowledge. Expansion may bring opportunity or extend coercion, so breadth of influence alone does not determine its value.
