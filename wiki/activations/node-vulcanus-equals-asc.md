## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

One’s counterpart is eager. The will to jointly accomplish something. To become enthusiastic about something together with others. To be in eagerness and excitement together with others. Others are very excited.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Encounter with violence in one's surroundings, transmission of energy to a partner, influential partnership, violence in a partnership, the other's power in a connection, strong connection with one's surroundings, connection with local force.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 155; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 236, PDF p. 117.

- Core meaning: Energy and power in a relationship or immediate setting.
- Psychology: Shared eagerness and enthusiasm.
- Body/health: No clinical condition is established; violence is a source example.
- Social/relationship: Joint activity and an influential partner.
- Events/manifestations: Accomplishing something together or encountering local force.
- Conflicts/notes: Shared enthusiasm must be distinguished from domination or violence.

## Contradictions

Witte's willing cooperation and ABC's power or violence differ materially in emphasis. Neither cancels the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies `Node/Vulcanus` through an energized or powerful relationship with another person. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) stresses joint enthusiasm and the will to accomplish something together; the restored [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also describes a partner's influence, strong environmental ties and encounters with violence. The relationship can support shared action or place one person under another's power; the entries do not make those conditions interchangeable.
