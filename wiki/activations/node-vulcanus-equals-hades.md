## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Vile acts by mighty persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influential connections in distress, danger through violence in a relationship, relinquishment of power in a connection, adverse influence through a connection, strong secret connection, strong connection from the past, connection with violent crime.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 155; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 237, PDF p. 117.

- Core meaning: Harmful or compromised influence in a connection.
- Psychology: The entry does not establish a specific personality trait.
- Body/health: Danger through violence is mentioned without a clinical claim.
- Social/relationship: Powerful people, secret ties and connections in distress.
- Events/manifestations: Adverse influence, relinquishment of power and violent wrongdoing.
- Conflicts/notes: Historical or secret connections are not inherently criminal.

## Contradictions

Witte focuses on wrongdoing; ABC has a wider range, including ties in distress and relinquishing power. Those examples should not be flattened into criminal conduct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies `Node/Vulcanus` through harmful or compromised uses of influence within a connection. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) names wrongdoing by powerful people; the corrected [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds danger through violence, adverse influence, secret ties and relinquishing power. Connections from the past and help in distress also appear, so secrecy, history and power should not automatically be equated with criminality.
