## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Happily tied to influential persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influential financial connections, successful strengthening of a connection, financial links in a connection, money through energy mediation, legal mediation against violence, connection with legal power, advantages through connection with power.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 155; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 236, PDF p. 117.

- Core meaning: Benefit through influential relationships.
- Psychology: Happiness in a strong or influential connection.
- Body/health: Legal mediation against violence is not a medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Financial ties and relationships with influential people.
- Events/manifestations: Strengthening a connection and gaining advantages through power.
- Conflicts/notes: Financial links are not the 'constraints' asserted by the old translation.

## Contradictions

No incompatible claim is apparent. ABC supplies financial and legal examples beyond Witte's brief statement of a happy connection.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `Node/Vulcanus` as benefit or strengthening through influential connections. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes a happy tie to influential people; the corrected [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) gives financial relationships, energy-related income and legal mediation against violence as examples. The connecting thread is an advantage gained through a relationship with power, without promising wealth, protection or a favorable legal result.
