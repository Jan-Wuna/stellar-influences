## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be busy. Very industrious. To display eager action.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Place workers, influential actions in a connection, influential acts of mediation, energy-transmission activity, violent activity in a connection, coercive mediation activity, establishment of influential contacts.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 155; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 236, PDF p. 117.

- Core meaning: Energetic action through influential connections.
- Psychology: Industry and eagerness to act.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim is stated.
- Social/relationship: Worker placement and influential working contacts.
- Events/manifestations: Energy transmission, mediation and possible coercive action.
- Conflicts/notes: Industriousness and violence are distinct source expressions.

## Contradictions

Witte's productive eagerness and ABC's coercive examples differ in emphasis. Neither makes every vigorous activity coercive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies `Node/Vulcanus` as eager, effective activity within influential connections. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes industry and being busy; the corrected [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) includes placing workers, transmitting energy and establishing influential contacts. ABC also names violent or coercive activity, which is a distinct expression rather than an implication that productive effort is itself violent.
