## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Written complaints. Indignant, enraged in speech and writing.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influential media reports, power of thought in relationships, violent meetings of young people, encountering verbal force, powerful thinking in connections, transmission of power through media, news about violence in connections, strong youth connections.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 155; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 236, PDF p. 117.

- Core meaning: Power expressed or conveyed through communication.
- Psychology: Indignation, anger and forceful thinking.
- Body/health: No clinical condition is established.
- Social/relationship: Media relations and strong youth connections.
- Events/manifestations: Written complaints, influential reports and news about violence.
- Conflicts/notes: Verbal force and power mediation should not automatically be translated as violence.

## Contradictions

Witte's complaints are narrower than ABC's media and youth examples. The source separates reports about violence from the exercise of communicative power.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies `Node/Vulcanus` through the force exerted by speech, writing and media in relationships. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) centers on complaints, indignation and angry expression; the corrected [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also includes influential reporting, powerful thought and media transmission of power. The theme includes both the use of communication to exert influence and reports about violence; reporting an event is distinct from causing it.
