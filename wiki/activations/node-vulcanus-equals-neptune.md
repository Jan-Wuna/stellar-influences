## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Difficult to be caught through application of force. Connected with confused power. Tainted mighty connections. Dissolution of mighty connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Encounter with the force of water, disappointing connection with power, strength through a connection with metaphysics, unknown influence of a connection, connection with water power, transmission of water energy, future influence of a connection.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 155; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 237, PDF p. 117.

- Core meaning: Unclear, disappointing or dissolving relations to power.
- Psychology: Disappointment in a connection with power.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- Social/relationship: Connections with confused power or metaphysical interests.
- Events/manifestations: Dissolution, unknown influence and water-energy mediation.
- Conflicts/notes: The unusual water examples remain separate from the interpersonal possibilities.

## Contradictions

The passages range from dissolution to water power and metaphysics. These are multiple applications, not evidence of a single necessary outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `Node/Vulcanus` as a powerful connection whose influence may be unclear, disappointing or dissolving. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) includes confused or tainted power and difficulty being caught by force; the restored [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also gives water power, metaphysical ties and future influence as distinct examples. Water-energy mediation is a literal source application, without making every unclear relationship a water-related event.
