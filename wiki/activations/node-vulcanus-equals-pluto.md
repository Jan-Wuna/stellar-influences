## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 155 (one-based; exact row checked).

To develop eagerness. Forced changes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 237 (printed); PDF page: 117 (one-based; exact row checked).

develop influential relationships, mediate experiences of energy, development of a strong connection, unfolding influence in a relationship, development of violence in a connection, linkage with a change of power, and growing power in connections

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: development or forced change in powerful connections.
- psychology: growing eagerness and unfolding influence.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: developing influential relationships and a strong connection.
- events/manifestations: a change of power, growing power within connections and the source's development of violence.
- conflicts/notes: Influence can grow while change is forced; the entry does not establish that every strong connection becomes violent.

## Contradictions

Influence can grow while change is forced; the entry does not establish that every strong connection becomes violent.

## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies `Node/Vulcanus` through the development or alteration of power within a connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins growing eagerness to forced changes; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes influential relationships developing, a strong tie growing and a connection to a change of power. The passage also retains the development of violence as a difficult possibility. The distinctive theme is power becoming more active or changing form within the association, rather than strength remaining fixed.
