## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 155 (one-based; exact row checked).

To be eager. To exert great powerful influence by personal appearance or participation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 236 (printed); PDF page: 117 (one-based; exact row checked).

Encounter with human power, contact with a man of influence, personal mediation of energy, personal influence in mediation, a strong personal relationship, connections to a center of power, connection to a violent person.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal presence or participation carrying strong influence.
- psychology: eagerness and personal influence in mediation.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: strong personal relationships, contacts with influential people and a center of power, and contact with a violent person.
- events/manifestations: exerting influence by appearing or participating and mediating energy personally.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's man of influence is historical gendered wording; its final gewalttätige Person explicitly means a violent person, a distinct adverse example within this row.

## Contradictions

ABC's man of influence is historical gendered wording; its final gewalttätige Person explicitly means a violent person. This adverse example stands alongside strong relationships and influential contacts without making every instance violent.

## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies `Node/Vulcanus` through the influence brought by a person's own presence or participation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes eagerness and personal impact explicit; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a strong personal relationship, mediation and contacts with influential people or a center of power. Its final example is a connection to a violent person, preserving an adverse expression of power encountered through direct human involvement. ABC's male-specific example remains historical wording, and its violent-person clause is one stated possibility within this row.
