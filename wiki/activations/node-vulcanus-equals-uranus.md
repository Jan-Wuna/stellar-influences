## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 155 (one-based; exact row checked).

To show great zeal quite suddenly. To be suddenly, severely checked over. Sudden powerful union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 237 (printed); PDF page: 117 (one-based; exact row checked).

Influential contacts with technology, enormous tensions within a relationship, nervousness within an influential connection, encountering sudden violence, reforms of a linkage of power, technical transmission of energy, restless relationships with power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sudden or tense activation of a powerful connection.
- psychology: abrupt zeal, nervousness and restlessness.
- body/health: nervousness is stated as a relationship response; no medical diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: a sudden powerful union and tense influential ties.
- events/manifestations: sudden checks, encounters with violence, technical energy transmission and reform of power links.
- conflicts/notes: Sudden zeal and sudden checking are different possible responses; the sources also preserve a technical range.

## Contradictions

Sudden zeal and sudden checking are different possible responses; the sources also preserve a technical range.

## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies `Node/Vulcanus` through a powerful connection that becomes suddenly active, tense or disruptive. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places abrupt zeal beside being suddenly checked and a sudden strong union; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds nervousness, tension and encounters with sudden violence. Technology, energy transmission and reform of power links are also explicit. The common thread is an abrupt expression or alteration of force, rather than a requirement that every technical or influential contact be violent.
