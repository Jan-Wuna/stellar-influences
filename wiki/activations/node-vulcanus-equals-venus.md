## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: strength expressed through favor, affection, art or peace.
- psychology: the force of love and harmony in mediation.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: charitable favors, a strong love bond and powerful peace contacts.
- events/manifestations: doing favors, mediating energy harmoniously and art exerting a connecting influence.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's charitable favors are a narrow concrete expression; ABC broadens the range to love, art and peace without establishing a direct disagreement.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies `Node/Vulcanus` through influence that connects by affection, harmony or goodwill. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes doing favors and charity explicit; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends this to the force of love, peace contacts, art and harmonious mediation of energy. The strong tie can thus support or reconcile people as well as carry affection. These are supported uses of influence within the connection, without requiring every loving relationship to involve charity or public peace work.
