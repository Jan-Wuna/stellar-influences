## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 151 (one-based; exact row checked).

A work-community fighting against odds, great obstacles and opposition. To have to overcome all obstacles at the beginning.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 229 (printed); PDF page: 113 (one-based; exact row checked).

Ending a strenuous connection, beginning of a fundamental relationship, beginning of a contact limitation, blocking in a creative connection, deepening contact by striving for goals, and strengthening creative relationships

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: a creative connection meeting obstacles, restriction or the need to deepen.
- psychology: goal-directed perseverance and concentration through difficulty.
- body/health: no bodily or medical condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: a working group facing opposition and creative relationships being strengthened.
- events/manifestations: ending a strenuous tie, beginning a fundamental relationship, or restricting contact.
- conflicts/notes: Deepening and strengthening coexist with blockage and endings; the small entry does not guarantee that overcoming obstacles will succeed.

## Contradictions

Deepening and strengthening coexist with blockage and endings; the small entry does not guarantee that overcoming obstacles will succeed.

## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies `Node/Zeus` through a creative or purposeful connection that has to contend with obstacles. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a working group meeting opposition from the outset, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes both blocked contact and deepening a relationship through persistent aims. A tie may become more firmly established, limited or ended. The distinction is between effort that consolidates the connection and resistance that prevents its intended work.
