## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 151 (one-based; exact row checked).

To commit something with several others. To be armed together with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 228 (printed); PDF page: 113 (one-based; exact row checked).

Strenuous encounters in the surroundings, beginning of a partner relationship, a creative partner relationship, being connected with partners through achievements, creative relationships with the surroundings, connection with local leadership, a goal-directed partner connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: joint action or a purposeful relationship with partners and nearby people.
- psychology: goal-directed effort in partnership is named without a full character profile.
- body/health: no bodily or medical condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: creative partner ties, local relationships and association with several others.
- events/manifestations: beginning a partnership, shared achievements, contact with local leadership and being armed with others.
- conflicts/notes: The armed association is Witte's particular possibility; ABC also gives ordinary creative and local partnership settings.

## Contradictions

The armed association is Witte's particular possibility; ABC also gives ordinary creative and local partnership settings.

## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies `Node/Zeus` through action shared with partners or people nearby. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes acting with several others concrete, including an armed association; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens the range to beginning a partnership, shared achievements and purposeful local ties. A connection to local leadership is also explicit. The bond takes shape through what the people do or seek to accomplish together, without making every partnership an armed group.
