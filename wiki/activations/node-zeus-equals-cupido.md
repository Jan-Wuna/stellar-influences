## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 151 (one-based; exact row checked).

To found associations (corporations). To be forced into a union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 229 (printed); PDF page: 113 (one-based; exact row checked).

Effort through a connection with art, beginning contacts with a community, a shared bond through procreation, artistic creations within a connection, connection with a creative community, a goal-directed community of mediation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creating or entering a group connection through shared productive activity.
- psychology: effort and directed purpose within a creative community.
- body/health: ABC names a bond through procreation without establishing a medical or fertility outcome.
- social/relationship: founding associations, creative communities and being compelled into a union.
- events/manifestations: artistic creations, new community contacts and a shared procreative bond.
- conflicts/notes: Founding a group and being forced into one are different positions within association; creative belonging is not automatically voluntary.

## Contradictions

Founding a group and being forced into one are different positions within association; creative belonging is not automatically voluntary.

## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies `Node/Zeus` through bringing a creative association into being or entering its shared work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives founding an association beside being forced into a union; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes community contacts, art and goal-directed mediation. A bond formed through procreation is another named possibility. The central tension is between creating an association together and having participation imposed on oneself.
