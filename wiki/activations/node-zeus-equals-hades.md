## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 151 (one-based; exact row checked).

Instigation of trouble by several people. To be impoverished by fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 229 (printed); PDF page: 113 (one-based; exact row checked).

Renouncing a procreative connection, connected in need through striving for a goal, criminal linkages of leadership, insufficient effort within a connection, renunciation of a procreative connection, goal-directed contacts from the past.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: trouble, deprivation or inadequate effort within a purposeful connection.
- psychology: insufficient effort and renunciation are explicit possibilities.
- body/health: renunciation of a procreative connection is named; no fertility diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: joint instigation of trouble, ties formed in need and the source's criminal leadership links.
- events/manifestations: impoverishment by fire and contacts from the past directed toward a goal.
- conflicts/notes: The repeated renunciation clause belongs to the source; criminal and trouble-making claims remain attributed possibilities, not character verdicts.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies `Node/Zeus` through the difficult or deprived side of a purposeful association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names trouble instigated by several people and impoverishment through fire; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds insufficient effort, ties made in need and renunciation of a procreative bond. Goal-directed contacts from the past remain within the range. The source's criminal-leadership wording is a particular difficult possibility, not a judgment on everyone in such a connection.
