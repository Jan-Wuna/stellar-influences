## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 151 (one-based; exact row checked).

Favorable choice. Joyful cooperation, Fortunate working together. Working with jewelry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 228 (printed); PDF page: 113 (one-based; exact row checked).

Joy beginning within a connection, a successful connection with fire, joyful striving for contact, good fortune through pursuing a goal within a connection, a creative connection with money, creative legal mediation, goals of a financial connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: a favorable choice or rewarding creative cooperation.
- psychology: joy in cooperation and in pursuing contact or shared goals.
- body/health: no bodily or medical condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: fortunate working together and beneficial creative, legal or financial ties.
- events/manifestations: working with jewelry, a successful fire-related connection and creative legal mediation.
- conflicts/notes: Jewelry, fire, law and finance are specific source settings; favorable choice does not establish a guaranteed financial or legal outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `Node/Zeus` through the reward and enjoyment of choosing or working with others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes a favorable choice and joyful cooperation; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops joy in contact and successful goal pursuit. Jewelry, finance, legal mediation and a successful connection with fire are concrete fields in the source range. The common meaning is constructive reward within directed association, without requiring all these settings to occur together.
