## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 151 (one-based; exact row checked).

To meet in order to produce something great. The machine-master.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 229 (printed); PDF page: 113 (one-based; exact row checked).

Beginning of important contacts, special achievements through a connection, creative relationships with the authorities, independence within a leading connection, superior mediation of achievement, connections to state leadership, connection with a major fire.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creative cooperation directed toward exceptional achievement or leadership.
- psychology: independence within a leading connection.
- body/health: no bodily or medical condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: important contacts, creative official relationships and ties with state leadership.
- events/manifestations: meeting to produce something great, the machine-master role and connection with a major fire.
- conflicts/notes: ABC adds official standing, independence and a large-fire setting to Witte's exceptional production; these do not establish a direct contradiction.

## Contradictions

ABC adds official standing, independence and a large-fire setting to Witte's exceptional production; these do not establish a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies `Node/Zeus` through a connection directed toward important or exceptional accomplishment. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names meeting to produce something great and the machine-master; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends this to special achievements, creative official ties and state leadership. Independence within a leading connection remains explicit. A major fire is a separate concrete ABC manifestation, rather than an implication of every high-level creative association.
