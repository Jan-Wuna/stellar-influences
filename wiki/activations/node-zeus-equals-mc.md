## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 151 (one-based; exact row checked).

To be elected or selected. The driver. Wishing to produce something with several others. To force oneself or to submit to force.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 228 (printed); PDF page: 113 (one-based; exact row checked).

Beginning of an inner relationship, a fiery soul relationship, a creative inner connection, my efforts within a connection, my connection to leadership, my goals within connections, inner leadership within the connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal involvement, direction or compulsion within a productive connection.
- psychology: wanting to produce something with others, self-pressure and personal goals.
- body/health: no bodily or medical condition is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: being elected or selected and an inwardly important creative bond.
- events/manifestations: taking the driver role, beginning a relationship and directing or submitting to force.
- conflicts/notes: The driver, self-forcing and submission are source alternatives; leadership does not necessarily mean freedom from pressure.

## Contradictions

The driver, self-forcing and submission are source alternatives; leadership does not necessarily mean freedom from pressure.

## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies `Node/Zeus` through one's personal place in a connection aimed at producing something. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) spans being chosen, acting as the driver and wishing to work with several others; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal efforts, goals and inner leadership in a creative bond. The same connection may involve forcing oneself or submitting to force. The organizing tension is between directing the undertaking and being driven by its demands.
