## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 151 (one-based; exact row checked).

Concentration. Employed in thought. Sketches for an intellectual work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 228 (printed); PDF page: 113 (one-based; exact row checked).

Efforts in connection with the media, leading news mediation, language about creative connections, creative language contacts, creative contacts with youth, and procreation thoughts in one connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: thought and communication directed toward creative accomplishment.
- psychology: concentration, intellectual work and creative ideas.
- body/health: procreative thoughts are stated in ABC without a bodily or medical claim.
- social/relationship: creative conversational contacts and contacts with youth.
- events/manifestations: sketching an intellectual work, media efforts and leading news mediation.
- conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes concentrated thought; ABC emphasizes communication and transmission. These are related activities without a demonstrated disagreement.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes concentrated thought; ABC emphasizes communication and transmission. These are related activities without a demonstrated disagreement.

## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies `Node/Zeus` through the thinking and communication that prepare or carry a creative undertaking. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes concentration and sketches for intellectual work; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes media effort, creative conversation and contact with youth. Procreative thoughts are another explicit ABC possibility. The common thread is directed mental activity within connection, ranging from forming a work in thought to communicating its aims.
