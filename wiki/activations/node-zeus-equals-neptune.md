## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 151 (one-based; exact row checked).

Useless effort of several people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 229 (printed); PDF page: 113 (one-based; exact row checked).

Beginning of a metaphysical connection, an encounter at takeoff, sensitivity within a creative connection, an unclear procreative connection, uncertainty at the beginning of a connection, fire in connection with water, goal-setting in connection with water.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: uncertain or ineffective beginnings and efforts within a creative connection.
- psychology: sensitivity and uncertainty at the start of a bond.
- body/health: an unclear procreative connection is source wording, not a fertility or medical assessment.
- social/relationship: several people making an effort that proves useless in Witte; a metaphysical connection begins in ABC.
- events/manifestations: an encounter at an air departure, fire meeting water and water-related goal-setting.
- conflicts/notes: Witte gives ineffective shared effort; ABC also preserves sensitivity, metaphysics and literal air/water settings. They should not be collapsed into guaranteed failure.

## Contradictions

Witte gives ineffective shared effort; ABC also preserves sensitivity, metaphysics and literal air/water settings. They should not be collapsed into guaranteed failure.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `Node/Zeus` through a creative connection or shared effort whose beginning, direction or usefulness is uncertain. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives the compact difficult expression of several people making a useless effort; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds sensitivity, an uncertain start and a metaphysical connection. Air departure, fire with water and water-related aims remain its concrete source range. The formula can therefore concern an indistinct undertaking without making every sensitive or metaphysical bond futile.
