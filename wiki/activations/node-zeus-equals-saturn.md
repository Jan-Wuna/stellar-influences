## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 151 (one-based; exact row checked).

To drop a matter.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 229 (printed); PDF page: 113 (one-based; exact row checked).

Tasks of a creative connection, the beginning of a separation, a burdensome fire connection, patient pursuit of goals within a relationship, concentration on a goal within a connection, striving for contact in old age, problems within a procreative connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: tasks, burdens or limits within a purposeful connection.
- psychology: patient goal pursuit and concentration alongside the possibility of dropping the matter.
- body/health: problems within a procreative connection remain historical source wording, not a fertility diagnosis.
- social/relationship: creative ties facing separation or contact sought in old age.
- events/manifestations: abandoning a matter, the beginning of separation and a burdensome fire connection.
- conflicts/notes: Patient concentration is supported alongside abandonment; the entries do not force a choice between guaranteed endurance and inevitable failure.

## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies `Node/Zeus` through the burden or limit encountered in a creative or goal-directed connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names dropping a matter, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) preserves both the start of separation and patient, concentrated pursuit of a goal. Creative tasks, contact-seeking in old age, fire-related burden and problems in a procreative tie remain specific expressions. The bond may require sustained effort or be relinquished; neither outcome is automatic.
