## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 151 (one-based; exact row checked).

To call forth love. To find sympathy. Congeniality. Natural selection. To meet for some charitable work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 228 (printed); PDF page: 113 (one-based; exact row checked).

Beginning of peace mediation, a fiery love connection, harmonious development of achievement, connection with creative art, preference for creative relationships, preference for a connection with fire, procreation within a love connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creative contact made harmonious, sympathetic or loving.
- psychology: congeniality, affection and preference for creative ties.
- body/health: ABC names procreation within a love bond without a fertility prediction.
- social/relationship: calling forth love, finding sympathy and meeting for charitable work.
- events/manifestations: peace mediation, artistic connections, harmonious achievement and fire-related interest.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's natural selection remains unexplained source wording; the other statements do not justify a new biological claim from it.

## Contradictions

Witte's natural selection remains unexplained source wording; the other statements do not justify a new biological claim from it.

## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies `Node/Zeus` through the affection and sympathy that help a creative connection form. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names congeniality, finding sympathy and charitable cooperation; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops peace mediation, artistic ties and harmonious achievement. Fiery love and procreation within a love bond remain explicit possibilities. The connection is directed toward something people create or support together, with affection rather than mere selection giving it its distinctive quality.
