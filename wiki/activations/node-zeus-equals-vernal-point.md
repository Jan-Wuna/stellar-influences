## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 151 (one-based; exact row checked).

To elect. To select. Mating. To create (produce) something with several other people. To be with others in an armed group.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 228 (printed); PDF page: 113 (one-based; exact row checked).

Strenuous encounters in public, beginning of a connection in public, creative contacts in public life, mediation of achievement in public, connection with fire in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: public initiation or selection of a connection for shared production.
- psychology: the passage supplies no distinct inner profile beyond shared productive purpose.
- body/health: mating is named in Witte, without a medical or fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: creating with several people, selection and armed groups.
- events/manifestations: public beginnings, creative public contacts, election or selection and public contact with fire.
- conflicts/notes: The public, armed and reproductive settings are separate source expressions rather than requirements that must occur together.

## Contradictions

The public, armed and reproductive settings are separate source expressions rather than requirements that must occur together.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies `Node/Zeus` through a connection initiated or selected in a public field of shared activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes election, selection and producing something with several people; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds creative contacts and achievement made public. Armed association, mating and fire in public preserve a concrete additional range. They are alternative manifestations within the entry, not a single event that the formula requires.
