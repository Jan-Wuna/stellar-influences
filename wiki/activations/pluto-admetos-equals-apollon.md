## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 262 (one-based; exact row checked).

The change that brings happiness, contentment and is satisfactory.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 419 (printed); PDF page: 208 (one-based; exact row checked).

Successful development through perseverance, successful cyclical/circulatory development, successful transformation of primal material, thorough changes in knowledge, profound changes in science, developing much firmness, developing increasing perseverance.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: satisfying change and successful development through perseverance or thorough work.
- psychology: contentment, firmness and increasing perseverance.
- body/health: ABC's Kreislauf wording can mean a cycle or circulation; it identifies no bodily mechanism or diagnosis.
- social/relationship: the entries do not directly describe a particular relationship or group.
- events/manifestations: changes in knowledge or science and transformation of primal material.
- conflicts/notes: Witte gives satisfaction in the change; ABC specifies endurance, knowledge and material transformation without establishing a direct disagreement.

## Contradictions

Witte gives satisfaction in the change; ABC specifies endurance, knowledge and material transformation without establishing a direct disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies `Pluto/Admetos` through a deep change that becomes satisfying or productive. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes happiness and contentment with the result, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) links successful development to perseverance, firmness and thorough work in knowledge or science. Transformation of primal material is a distinct concrete expression. The emphasis is on what sustained, thorough change can accomplish; ABC's cyclical/circulatory wording remains a source correspondence rather than a medical conclusion.
