## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 262 (one-based; exact row checked).

The native goes through deep reaching changes in relation to the surroundings and other people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 418 (printed); PDF page: 208 (one-based; exact row checked).

Blocked development of a partner, restricted development at a place, consolidation of a partner's development, thorough changes to a place, profound change of the partner, transformation through the partner's depressions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: deep change in relation to partners, other people or one's surroundings.
- psychology: the sources connect restriction and consolidation with change; ABC also names a partner's depressions.
- body/health: ABC's depression wording remains an attributed historical claim, not a diagnosis of a partner.
- social/relationship: a partner may be changing, restricted or becoming more firmly established.
- events/manifestations: thorough changes to a place and altered relations with the surrounding environment.
- conflicts/notes: The sources do not equate deep change with improvement; ABC includes both blocked and consolidated development.

## Contradictions

The sources do not equate deep change with improvement; ABC includes both blocked and consolidated development.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies `Pluto/Admetos` through profound changes in one's relation to people and place. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) locates transformation in the surroundings and other people; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes both blocked partner development and its consolidation, as well as thorough changes to a place. The result need not be improvement simply because it is deep. ABC's reference to change through a partner's depressions remains historical source language, not an assessment of that person's health.
