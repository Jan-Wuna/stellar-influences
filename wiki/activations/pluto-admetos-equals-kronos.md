## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 262 (one-based; exact row checked).

In the life of nations great changes in the existing forms of governments. In personal horoscopes, to live through such changes, or changed attitude of the native toward superiors, government, or the form of government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 419 (printed); PDF page: 208 (one-based; exact row checked).

Developing exceptional perseverance, consolidation of important developments, great cyclical/circulatory changes, thorough unfolding of the hierarchy, thorough changes within the state, profound transformation of authority, growing superiority through thoroughness.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: fundamental change involving government, hierarchy or authority.
- psychology: exceptional perseverance and changed attitudes toward superiors or government.
- body/health: ABC's Kreislauf wording can mean a cycle or circulation; it identifies no bodily mechanism or diagnosis.
- social/relationship: relations to superiors, governing structures and authority are affected.
- events/manifestations: changes in forms of government, important developments becoming established and transformation of authority.
- conflicts/notes: Witte explicitly includes a person's altered attitude to authority as well as changes in nations; these must remain distinct scales.

## Contradictions

Witte explicitly includes a person's altered attitude to authority as well as changes in nations; these must remain distinct scales.

## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies `Pluto/Admetos` through deep change in the structures or experience of authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes changes in government and the individual living through them, but also an altered attitude toward superiors or government. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds consolidation, thorough transformation of hierarchy and exceptional perseverance. The formula thus spans structural change and a changed personal relation to authority; one does not require the other to occur at the same scale.
