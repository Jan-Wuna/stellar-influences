## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 262 (one-based; exact row checked).

Fundamental change of working conditions. (This constellation is predominantly unpleasant, because Admetos and Mars together spell suppression.)

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 418 (printed); PDF page: 208 (one-based; exact row checked).

Develop perseverance in activity, blocked developmental activity, depressive developmental impulses, the end of developmental work, thorough developmental efforts, thorough changes in work, growing cyclical/circulatory impulses.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: fundamental change in work under restriction or suppression.
- psychology: perseverance and the source's depressive developmental impulses.
- body/health: ABC's Kreislauf wording can mean a cycle or circulation; it identifies no bodily mechanism or diagnosis; its depressive terms also remain historical source language.
- social/relationship: working conditions are named, but no specific group or partner is delineated.
- events/manifestations: blocked developmental activity, the ending of developmental work and thorough work changes.
- conflicts/notes: Witte explicitly calls the combination predominantly unpleasant through suppression; perseverance does not erase that qualification.

## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies `Pluto/Admetos` through fundamental change in work and the effort needed to develop it. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly qualifies the change as predominantly unpleasant because of suppression; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes blocked activity, the end of developmental work and perseverance alongside thorough changes in work. Effort may continue under restriction rather than producing immediate progress. The depressive and cyclical/circulatory terms remain historical source correspondences, not medical conclusions.
