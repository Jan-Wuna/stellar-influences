## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 262 (one-based; exact row checked).

Able to change. To face completely changed circumstances. To find one’s way under completely changed conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 418 (printed); PDF page: 208 (one-based; exact row checked).

Restricted inner development, thorough inner transformation, I develop perseverance, my growing resistance, my blocked development, my cyclical/circulatory change, developing inner firmness, profound inner change.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: finding one's way through completely changed conditions and inner development.
- psychology: perseverance, inner firmness, growing resistance and blocked growth.
- body/health: ABC's Kreislauf wording can mean a cycle or circulation; it identifies no bodily mechanism or diagnosis.
- social/relationship: no particular relationship or group is specified.
- events/manifestations: facing completely changed circumstances and undergoing profound inner transformation.
- conflicts/notes: Ability to adapt in Witte coexists with resistance or blockage in ABC; they describe possible responses rather than incompatible claims.

## Contradictions

Ability to adapt in Witte coexists with resistance or blockage in ABC; they describe possible responses rather than incompatible claims.

## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies `Pluto/Admetos` through one's personal adaptation to deeply altered circumstances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes being able to change and find a way under completely new conditions; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) brings perseverance and inner firmness beside growing resistance and blocked development. The distinctive tension is between becoming capable of inhabiting the changed situation and resisting the change inwardly. ABC's cyclical/circulatory wording adds no medical conclusion.
