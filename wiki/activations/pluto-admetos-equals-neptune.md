## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 262 (one-based; exact row checked).

One experiences deep changes in the near future. Coming events develop below the surface and can be noticed in small symptoms.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 419 (printed); PDF page: 208 (one-based; exact row checked).

Blocked future development, development of a buildup of liquid, restricted development in air or water, thorough change through sensitivity, changes through backed-up water, transition between solid and liquid.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `Pluto/Admetos` through deep changes whose development is initially concealed or only faintly noticeable. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes events forming below the surface and showing small signs; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds sensitivity and restricted development in air or water. Buildup of liquid, backed-up water and transition between solid and liquid preserve a literal material range. The passage concerns how change develops, without fixing its date or identifying a bodily condition.
