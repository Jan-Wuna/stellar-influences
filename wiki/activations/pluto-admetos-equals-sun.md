## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 262 (one-based; exact row checked).

Profound change in the body-mechanism. The glandular system of the body, changes noticeable. The man who experiences a deep reaching transformation in relation to the exterior conditions of his existence. Changed course of the day, therefore also changed meals or food-intake.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 418 (printed); PDF page: 208 (one-based; exact row checked).

Blocked bodily development, a blocked center of development, restricted bodily development, depressive development of a man, thorough change of a person, developing personal perseverance, deepening bodily development.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: profound personal change involving bodily development or external living conditions.
- psychology: personal perseverance and the source's depressive development.
- body/health: Witte names bodily and glandular changes; ABC names restricted bodily development. These remain historical medical correspondences, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: a person's relation to external conditions changes; male-specific wording remains source attribution.
- events/manifestations: changes in daily routine, meals and food intake are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: The bodily and daily-life statements form a specific historical range; they do not establish a disease or a universal male pattern.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies `Pluto/Admetos` through a profound change in personal existence, including its bodily and everyday conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names bodily and glandular changes but also altered external circumstances, daily routine and meals; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds restricted bodily development and personal perseverance. The passage therefore concerns concrete personal adjustment as well as historical bodily correspondences. Its medical and male-specific formulations remain attributed source claims, without diagnosing a condition.
