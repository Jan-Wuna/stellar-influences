## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 262 (one-based; exact row checked).

Changes in the existing conditions that are brought about through sudden breaks or separations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 419 (printed); PDF page: 208 (one-based; exact row checked).

Blocked reform development, restricted technical development, sudden depressive development, sudden resistance to development, stress development through limitations, and profound changes in technology

## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies `Pluto/Admetos` through a sudden break that alters established conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes abrupt separation; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds resistance to development, tension arising from limitation and blocked reform or technical progress. Profound technological change is also supported. The distinctive tension is between an attempted development and the resistance or break that changes its course, with ABC's depressive language retained as historical attribution.
