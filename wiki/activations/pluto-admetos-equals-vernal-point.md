## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 262 (one-based; exact row checked).

Changed general conditions affect the native. Making the best of it.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 418 (printed); PDF page: 208 (one-based; exact row checked).

Blocked developments in public, depressive development in public, restrictive changes in public, thorough changes in public life, profound changes in the public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: deep changes in public or general conditions that affect the individual.
- psychology: making the best of altered circumstances is explicit in Witte.
- body/health: ABC's depressive development describes public conditions; it supplies no individual diagnosis.
- social/relationship: the public or public life is the field of change.
- events/manifestations: blocked developments and thorough or restrictive changes in public life.
- conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes the individual's response; ABC describes the altered public conditions. Neither guarantees an easy adjustment.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes the individual's response; ABC describes the altered public conditions. Neither guarantees an easy adjustment.

## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies `Pluto/Admetos` through changed general conditions that reach into an individual's life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) pairs that impact with making the best of it; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes public development becoming blocked or restricted, as well as thorough changes in public life. The formula therefore keeps the wider change and the person's adaptation in view. ABC's depressive wording remains a characterization of the public process, not an individual diagnosis.
