## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 262 (one-based; exact row checked).

Mighty changes under grave hindrances and obstacles.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 419 (printed); PDF page: 208 (one-based; exact row checked).

blocked unfolding of strength, depressions through violent unfolding, developing influence through endurance, restrictions through shifts of power, forceful cyclical/circulatory development, profound changes through force, and resistance against growing force

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: mighty change confronting serious obstacles, power or resistance.
- psychology: endurance can build influence; depression through violence remains a source possibility.
- body/health: ABC's Kreislauf wording can mean a cycle or circulation; it identifies no bodily mechanism or diagnosis; its depressive terms also remain historical source language.
- social/relationship: shifts of power can impose restrictions and growing force can meet resistance.
- events/manifestations: blocked strength, profound changes through force and mighty changes under hindrance.
- conflicts/notes: The entry preserves both exerted force and resistance to it; developing influence is not synonymous with domination.

## Contradictions

The entry preserves both exerted force and resistance to it; developing influence is not synonymous with domination.

## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies `Pluto/Admetos` through mighty changes forced to contend with serious resistance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places grave hindrances at the center; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) joins blocked strength and power shifts to influence developed through endurance. It also names profound changes through force and resistance to growing force. The distinctive tension concerns what power can change and what prevents or opposes it, while the depressive and cyclical/circulatory terms remain attributed source language.
