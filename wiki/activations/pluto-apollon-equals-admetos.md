## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 261 (one-based; exact row checked).

Stagnation of development in a small circle. To be satisfied about it. Radical change for a few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 417 (printed); PDF page: 207 (one-based; exact row checked).

Blocked trade developments, developing success through perseverance, thorough development of knowledge, extensive changes through restrictions, deepening scientific developments, growing consolidation of knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: expansion becoming limited, concentrated or deepened.
- psychology: satisfaction with a small circle and perseverance.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: stagnation within a small circle and radical change for a few.
- events/manifestations: blocked trade development and knowledge consolidated through restricted or deepened development.
- conflicts/notes: Witte permits satisfaction with limited growth; ABC also supports success through perseverance. Restriction is not automatically failure.

## Contradictions

Witte permits satisfaction with limited growth; ABC also supports success through perseverance. Restriction is not automatically failure.

## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies `Pluto/Apollon` through an expanding development that becomes limited or concentrated. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names stagnation in a small circle but also satisfaction with it and radical change for a few. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) joins blocked trade development to success through perseverance and deeper, firmer knowledge. The distinctive tension is between breadth of growth and depth or consolidation within limits.
