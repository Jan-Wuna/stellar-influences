## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 261 (one-based; exact row checked).

Others cause a large change in the life of the native. Beginning of a big change at the place.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 416 (printed); PDF page: 207 (one-based; exact row checked).

Extensive developments in the surroundings, developmental successes with others, a successful change of place, successful changes in the surroundings, expanded knowledge of the surroundings, trade developments at a place, growing trading partners, growing success of a partner.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: large change through other people or in one's surroundings.
- psychology: expanded understanding of the surroundings is named without a wider psychological profile.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: development with others, growing trading partners and a partner's growing success.
- events/manifestations: a major change at a place, a successful move and local trade development.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's large change need not carry every favorable ABC expression; other people and place are distinct routes of change.

## Contradictions

Witte's large change need not carry every favorable ABC expression; other people and place are distinct routes of change.

## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies `Pluto/Apollon` through a development enlarged or redirected by other people and by one's surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names others causing a large life change or a major change beginning at a place; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds success with others, a change of place and growing trading partners. The common theme is extensive change encountered through one's local or relational environment, without making every move or partnership successful.
