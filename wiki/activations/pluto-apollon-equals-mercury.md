## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 261 (one-based; exact row checked).

Thoughts, opinions or debates about transformations and changes. To be under the influence of a great change.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 416 (printed); PDF page: 207 (one-based; exact row checked).

Successful development of young people, successful traffic developments, language about expansion of development, news about successful changes, extensive changes in traffic, and changes in trade traffic

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: thought, debate or communication shaped by extensive change.
- psychology: opinions and thought occupied by transformation.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: the development of youth and discussions of expanding development are named.
- events/manifestations: news of successful change and extensive changes in traffic or commercial exchange.
- conflicts/notes: Witte focuses on thought under a great change; ABC supplies youth, traffic and news as distinct fields of expression.

## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies `Pluto/Apollon` through thought and communication about a development whose reach is expanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names opinions and debates about transformation, including being mentally influenced by a great change; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds news, youthful development and changes in traffic or commercial exchange. The formula therefore spans discussing a change and the channels through which development itself spreads.
