## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies `Pluto/Apollon` through connections that develop in scope or establish something new. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names creating precedent cases, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) follows experience and successful development within relationships into trading, business and economic ties.
