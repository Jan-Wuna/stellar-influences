## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 261 (one-based; exact row checked).

Occurrences happening but once, which are without consequences, the expected consequences do not come forward.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 417 (printed); PDF page: 207 (one-based; exact row checked).

Successful changes through patience, disrupted trade developments, slow development of success, concentration on developing knowledge, unfolding mature experiences, growing problems in science, developing knowledge of pain.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: development whose expected consequences may fail or emerge only slowly through patience.
- psychology: patience, concentration and mature experience.
- body/health: knowledge of pain is stated without identifying an illness or treatment.
- social/relationship: trade is named without a particular partner or group.
- events/manifestations: a one-time occurrence lacking expected consequences, disrupted trade and slow success.
- conflicts/notes: Witte gives absent expected consequences; ABC also allows successful change through patience. This material difference in outcome remains visible.

## Contradictions

Witte gives absent expected consequences; ABC also allows successful change through patience. This material difference in outcome remains visible.

## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies `Pluto/Apollon` through the limits placed on a development's expected consequences. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a one-time occurrence whose anticipated effects do not follow. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also names disrupted trade and problems in science, but retains success through patience, slow progress and mature experience. These outcomes should remain side by side: the formula supplies neither a promise that waiting will bring success nor a rule that every limited development is fruitless.
