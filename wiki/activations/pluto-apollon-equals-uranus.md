## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies `Pluto/Apollon` through a sudden or newly developing change whose consequences reach beyond its immediate setting. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes an incisive alteration of the situation; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) supplies dynamic knowledge, new science, technical transformation and reform. Surprising success coexists with restless trade development. The common thread is extensive development taking a new or abrupt course, rather than a guarantee that the disruption is welcome.
