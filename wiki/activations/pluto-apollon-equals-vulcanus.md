## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 261 (one-based; exact row checked).

Great transformation with extraordinary consequences for the future.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 417 (printed); PDF page: 207 (one-based; exact row checked).

Successful energy transformation, successful changes of power, strong trade developments, unfolding much force, growing experience of violence, growing influence through perseverance, scientific development of energy, developing increasing power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: extensive transformation carrying extraordinary consequences and growing power.
- psychology: perseverance builds influence while force and experienced violence remain source possibilities.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: strong trade development and changes of power are named.
- events/manifestations: energy transformation, scientific development of energy and increasing power.
- conflicts/notes: The source distinguishes influence, power and experience of violence; consequential growth should not be read as automatically benign.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies `Pluto/Apollon` through a transformation whose scale and force produce extraordinary consequences. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes that reach; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops successful energy transformation, changes of power and growing influence through perseverance. Experience of violence remains part of the difficult range alongside strong trade and scientific energy development. The formula concerns consequential force in development, without deciding whether its effects are beneficial.
