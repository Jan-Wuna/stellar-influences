## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 261 (one-based; exact row checked).

Relief in employment relationships. Easier work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 417 (printed); PDF page: 207 (one-based; exact row checked).

Expansion of fire development, successful transformation efforts, directing trade developments, creative development of knowledge, an extensive change of direction, growing success in leadership, goal-directed development of knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: easier work or directed effort accompanying extensive development.
- psychology: goal-directed knowledge and successful transformation efforts.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: relief in employment relationships and directing trade developments.
- events/manifestations: expanded fire development, a large change of direction and growing leadership success.
- conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes relief and easier work; ABC specifies effort, direction and leadership. Effort does not itself contradict relief in working conditions.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes relief and easier work; ABC specifies effort, direction and leadership. Effort does not itself contradict relief in working conditions.

## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies `Pluto/Apollon` through a development whose effort becomes directed or whose working conditions become easier. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives relief in employment relationships and easier work; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful transformation effort, directing trade and growing leadership success. Extensive changes of direction, creative knowledge and fire development remain concrete possibilities. The shared field is productive change and its organization, with easier conditions distinct from an absence of effort.
