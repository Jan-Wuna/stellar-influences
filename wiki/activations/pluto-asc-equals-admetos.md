## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 90 (one-based; exact row checked).

Deep reaching change in relation to others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 121 (printed); PDF page: 59 (one-based; exact row checked).

Depression after a change of partner, restricted development of a place, consolidation through a partner's development, thorough transformations in the surroundings, suppression of a partner's development, deepening into the development of the surroundings, growing resistance from the other person.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: deep change in relation to others meeting consolidation, suppression or resistance.
- psychology: resistance and the source's depression after a partner change.
- body/health: depression is attributed historical source wording, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: a partner's development can consolidate a situation or be suppressed.
- events/manifestations: restricted local development and thorough transformation of the surroundings.
- conflicts/notes: Consolidation through a partner's development coexists with suppression of that development and resistance from the other person; these remain different possible conditions.

## Contradictions

Consolidation through a partner's development coexists with suppression of that development and resistance from the other person; these remain different possible conditions.

## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies `Pluto/Asc` through deep relational change that becomes firmly established or meets restriction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives profound change in relation to others; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds consolidation through a partner's development beside suppression of that development and growing resistance from the other person. Place and surroundings can also become restricted or thoroughly transformed. ABC's depression after a partner change remains historical source language, not a clinical conclusion.
