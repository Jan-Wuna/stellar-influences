## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 90 (one-based; exact row checked).

Many changes in the location. Elaborate changes in the place. Change of location for many. Many travel.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 121 (printed); PDF page: 59 (one-based; exact row checked).

Expansion of change in the surroundings, successful development of a partner, a partner's development in freedom, many changes in the surroundings, a place of transformation for knowledge, changing experiences with partners, increasing development of the partner.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: extensive or numerous changes of place, surroundings or partner development.
- psychology: knowledge is a field of transformation in ABC; no distinct psychological trait is supplied.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: many people changing place and a partner's development in freedom.
- events/manifestations: travel, elaborate local changes and changing experiences with partners.
- conflicts/notes: Many changes and favorable partner development are distinct source expressions; breadth of change does not make every move beneficial.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies `Pluto/Asc` through change becoming extensive or affecting many people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes numerous or elaborate local changes, travel and changes of place for many; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds expanded change in the surroundings and a partner developing successfully or freely. ABC also names a place in which knowledge is transformed. The common theme is enlarged scope of change, without treating every move as an assured improvement.
