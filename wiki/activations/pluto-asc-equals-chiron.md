#### Sandbach Entry

One’s energy spontaneously brings out intensity in others, and can help them to see themselves in a new way and to let go of no longer needed personality traits.

## Derived Synthesis

Chiron specifies `Pluto/Asc` through an encounter that brings out another person's intensity and can change their self-understanding. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) connects that new view of the self with letting go of personality traits no longer needed. Its claim is potential and relational: one's energy can help the other person make this change. The passage supplies no further medical or guaranteed healing result.
