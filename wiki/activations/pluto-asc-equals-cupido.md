## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies `Pluto/Asc` through change in shared home or community life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names changes in or of the home and in social relations; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends that to shared relocation, partner changes, local community development and art at a place. The surroundings change through collective participation as well as through a particular relationship, without every change of home implying a change of partner.
