## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 90 (one-based; exact row checked).

Deficiency prevents change of location. Degradation of the local situations. Insufficient changes of others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 121 (printed); PDF page: 59 (one-based; exact row checked).

Difficult change of location, dangerous environmental development, criminal developments in the environment, grief caused by partner change, environmental changes caused by deprivation, hidden environmental development, renunciation of partner change, and growing environmental crime

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: difficult or deprived changes in place, surroundings or partner relations.
- psychology: grief after partner change and relinquishment of such a change are explicit.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: deprivation and the source's criminal or dangerous environmental developments.
- events/manifestations: a prevented or difficult move, deteriorating local conditions and hidden environmental change.
- conflicts/notes: The source's crime and danger examples remain particular possibilities; deficiency can also prevent change rather than cause it.

## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies `Pluto/Asc` through change in surroundings or relationships burdened by deficiency, danger or loss. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes a move prevented by lack, degraded local conditions and insufficient change in others. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds grief after a partner change, deprivation and hidden environmental development. Its criminal examples remain specific source possibilities, while giving up a partner change is another supported outcome.
