## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 90 (one-based; exact row checked).

Fortunate changes at the place, or of the place.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 120 (printed); PDF page: 59 (one-based; exact row checked).

Fortunate development of a partner, development of environmental law, a successful change of place, successful development of the surroundings, financial growth nearby, local legal developments, an advantageous change of partner, changeable success of a partner.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 295 (printed); PDF page: 297 (one-based); entry code: 1064 (direct source text checked).

An imposing and powerful personality, generosity. A large gain, the good fortune to receive a present, a favorable turn of circumstances in life.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 117 (one-based; exact activation located in direct source text).

Enthusiasm which can manifest in an overpowering and/or intimidating way. To be eager and optimistic about the positive outcome of intense encounters with others, and about the potential of positively changing oneself.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `PLUTO/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `348`

#### Munkasey Entry

Your love of politics and the law is enhanced, and you use your knowledge of people and their motives to push ahead in life; adds excessive urgency to control the feelings and lives of others; an increased aura of sincerity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: favorable changes in circumstances or partnerships accompanied by powerful personal influence.
- psychology: generosity and optimism can coexist with overpowering enthusiasm or an urgent wish to control others.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: an imposing personality, partner development and knowledge of people's motives.
- events/manifestations: fortunate local changes, a large gain or present, and legal or financial developments.
- conflicts/notes: The favorable circumstances in Witte and Ebertin do not cancel Sandbach's intimidating enthusiasm or Munkasey's excessive control.

## Contradictions

The favorable circumstances in Witte and Ebertin do not cancel Sandbach's intimidating enthusiasm or Munkasey's excessive control.

## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `Pluto/Asc` through a favorable turn in circumstances or a powerful, optimistic encounter. [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) include beneficial local or partner changes, generosity and a large gain or present. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds enthusiasm about changing oneself or the outcome of an intense meeting.

The same confidence may overwhelm another person: Sandbach retains intimidating enthusiasm, and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) names urgency to control feelings and lives even while understanding motives or appearing sincere. The tension is between using influence to take up an opportunity and letting optimism become overbearing.
