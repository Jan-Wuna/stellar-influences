## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 90 (one-based; exact row checked).

Change of place for the purpose of training or educating pupils. Change of students. Increasing demands.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 121 (printed); PDF page: 59 (one-based; exact row checked).

Special developments in the surroundings, great changes at a place, self-confident development of a partner, state-directed changes in the surroundings, priority development of a partner, growing standing of others, an important change of partner.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: significant local or partner changes involving education, demands or standing.
- psychology: a partner's self-confidence is explicitly named in ABC.
- body/health: no bodily or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: pupils, students, partners and others whose standing grows.
- events/manifestations: a move for training or education, changing students and state-related environmental changes.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's educational move is a specific setting; ABC also supplies partner development and standing without making every change a school event.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies `Pluto/Asc` through a change of place or relationship carrying increased demands or significance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives an educational setting: moving to train pupils, changing students and rising demands. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a partner's confident development, state-related environmental changes and the growing standing of others. Education is a concrete expression within this wider significant change, rather than its only possible setting.
