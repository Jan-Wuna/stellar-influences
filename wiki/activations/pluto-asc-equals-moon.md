## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 90 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

The influences of the immediate environment. The influence of a change in place on the mind. The native appears in public places or is brought into public by others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 120; PDF page 59.

#### ABC Entry

Development of other peoples, developments in the environment through a woman, change in the partner's feelings, transforming the partner's feelings, the hour of development for a place, changes in the environment of a people, changes through other women.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: Printed page 295, entry 1060.

#### Ebertin Entry

Strong emotional response to the influence coming from one's environment or, alternately, the wielding of a powerful psychic influence upon others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. `116`, exact complete entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `116`. No printed folio is visible.

#### Sandbach Entry

The emotional need for intense connections with other people. To feel extremely vulnerable about all you dislike about yourself. One goes through cycles of very intense and possibly stressful interactions with others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `PLUTO/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `348`

#### Munkasey Entry

An instinct for avoiding powers or people which can be destructive to self or the environment; a sensitivity about reading and understanding the motives of others; difficulties with abusive females; intense mood swings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Emotional exchange with a changing environment.
- psychology: Vulnerability, intense attachments, and sensitivity to others' motives.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: Powerful emotional influence, stressful contacts, and public exposure.
- events/manifestations: Changes of place affect mood; close interactions may recur intensely.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's gendered abuse example is an attributed possibility, not a rule.

## Contradictions

Environmental influence and emotional intensity are compatible emphases. ABC specifies changes in the partner's feelings and transforming those feelings, rather than two repeated claims of changing partners.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Pluto/Asc through the emotional impact of encounters and surroundings: changes outside oneself can be felt deeply, while one's own feelings also influence others. The need for intense connection can expose vulnerability and make interactions stressful; sensitivity to motives may also help someone avoid destructive contacts. These themes connect the [Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), and [Munkasey entries](#munkasey-entry).
