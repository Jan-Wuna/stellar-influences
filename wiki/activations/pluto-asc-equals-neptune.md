## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 90 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To have to give up something through change of the surroundings. Change in the environment, where the results or consequences are only noticed much later, or not at all.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 121; PDF page 59.

#### ABC Entry

A disappointing change of partner, developing the partner's sensitivity, changes of place in the air (flying), partner development through metaphysics, creeping changes in the environment, weak environmental changes, changes of a place through water.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: Printed page 295, entry 1067.

#### Ebertin Entry

The misfortune to suffer from the actions of bad, malicious and deceitful people within one's environment. Experiencing a most awkward and unfortunate situation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: one-based PDF p. `117`, exact complete entry; chapter starts on PDF p. `116`. No printed folio is visible.

#### Sandbach Entry

Intense and even obsessive identification with one’s ideals and/or imaginings. Subtle transformations occurring in one’s personality.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `PLUTO/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `348`

#### Munkasey Entry

Lends an appearance of being a dreamer and visionary; a need to use drugs to heighten your experiences and reactions to the world; allows you to consider fraud and deception as a personal policy and deliberate activity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Subtle or uncertain changes in surroundings and personal presentation.
- psychology: Identification with ideals or imaginings; a visionary appearance.
- body/health: Munkasey's drug-use example is historical interpretation, not a need or recommendation.
- social/relationship: Deception may be suffered from others or adopted as conduct.
- events/manifestations: Relocation, delayed recognition of environmental consequences, and awkward situations.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes disappointment, developing a partner's sensitivity and metaphysical development; the following Pluto cross-reference is not part of this row.

## Contradictions

Sandbach's inward transformation differs from Ebertin's harmful environment. Munkasey describes deceptive agency, Ebertin victimization; neither makes deceit inevitable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Pluto/Asc as change whose effects or motives can be hard to see. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes consequences noticed late, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) places subtle transformation in personality and ideals. This uncertainty can appear socially as exposure to deceit or deceptive self-presentation in [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); these are distinct possibilities, not a judgment about a person's character.
