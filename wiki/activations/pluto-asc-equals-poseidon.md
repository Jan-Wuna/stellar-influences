## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 90 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Changes of place for the purpose of intellectual and spiritual development and education. Waves in the surrounding area.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 121; PDF page 59.

#### ABC Entry

Development of a cultural place, intellectual partner development, development of the partner's ideas, clear changes in the environment, cultural development of the partner, a reasonable change of partner, developing partner culture, growing insight of the partner.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intellectual, spiritual, and cultural development through place or partnership.
- psychology: Development of ideas and growing insight.
- body/health: No health interpretation is stated.
- social/relationship: A partner's intellectual and cultural development.
- events/manifestations: Moving for education; development of cultural places.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's 'Waves in the surrounding area' is genuinely printed but undefined; ABC separately names a reasonable change of partner.

## Contradictions

The education and cultural themes fit together. Witte's printed 'waves' remains unexplained; ABC's reasonable change of partner is a separate explicit example.

## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Pluto/Asc through changes in place or partnership that serve learning, ideas, and cultural development. Moving to pursue intellectual or spiritual education in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects with the growth of a cultural place, a partner's ideas, and insight in the [ABC entry](#abc-entry). ABC also names a reasonable change of partner. Witte's unexplained 'waves' remains outside the synthesis.
