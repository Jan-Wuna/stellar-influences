## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden surprising changes of the place.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting partner development, dynamic environmental changes, new environmental developments, sudden partner change, technical transformations on site, changes of a place through technology, and growing environmental stresses

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A frequent change of environment, the misfortune to live in constant anxiety and restlessness. Extraordinary incidents and upsets, accidents.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s unique characteristics manifest in a highly intense manner. Strange and unexpected transformations in one’s personality. An intense identification with some thing of an unusual or bizarre nature. A genius for coming across to others in a vivid, powerful or potent manner.

- Source heading: `PLUTO/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`

#### Munkasey Entry

An increased need to be disruptive toward practices of which you do not approve; a need to study the psychological ways of other people; prompts an interest in astrology and using astrology to understand others and life.
