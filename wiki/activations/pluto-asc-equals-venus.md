## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To desire a change in the place. Nice change in the surroundings. To think of the beautification of the environment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Others develop harmoniously, development of a art place, peaceful change in one place, harmony with the partner develop, preference for environmental changes, growing partner love, and change of the partner through love

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Ability to wield an influence upon others by means of an extremely attractive personality. A strong love union.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A love of coming across with a lot of intensity, and of people who also do this. To appreciate pressures in one’s own and others’ personalities, as well as appreciating the process of one’s personality transforming.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds softness to an intense demeanor and helps others relate better to the inner you; an increased need for affection, and to give and receive both love and sexual pleasure from others; adds grace and charm to your disposition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Pluto/Asc = Venus`: seductive charisma, regenerative love experience, and the ability to change relationships through emotional and aesthetic intensity. Main caution: possessive attraction: charm can become strategic, manipulative, or too entangled with control and fixation.
