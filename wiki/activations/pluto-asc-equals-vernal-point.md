## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 90 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

New acquaintances. Inconstant life. To learn to know other places. Uncertain situations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 120; PDF page 59.

#### ABC Entry

Unfolding of a partner in public, a public change of partner, a place of transformation in public, changes of a place in public, changes at public places, growing public surroundings, transformations in the public environment.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Change in public surroundings and the people encountered there.
- psychology: Uncertainty associated with an inconstant situation.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears.
- social/relationship: New acquaintances and publicly visible partner changes.
- events/manifestations: Learning about other places and changes in public locations.
- conflicts/notes: ABC distinguishes changes of a place in public from changes at public places.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes uncertainty and acquaintance; ABC emphasizes public development and changes of public places. These are compatible emphases.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Pluto/Asc by placing change in public surroundings and encounters. New acquaintances and learning about other places in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connect with publicly visible partner and environmental changes in the [ABC entry](#abc-entry). Witte also retains instability and uncertain situations, so the interpretation is broader than straightforward public growth. ABC distinguishes publicly visible changes of a place from changes at public places.
