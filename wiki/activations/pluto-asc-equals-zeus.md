## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 90 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

New duties. Change of place for enlargement of sphere of activities or changes at the place which enable expansion of work facilities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 121; PDF page 59.

#### ABC Entry

Starting environmental change, development goals of a place, directional change for the environment, changing partner goals, changing a place through fire, growing environmental design, and changing partner services

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Changes of place or partnership organized around work and goals.
- psychology: A directed effort to shape surrounding conditions.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is given.
- social/relationship: Changing partner goals and contributions.
- events/manifestations: New duties, expanded work facilities, and local change through fire.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's fire example is specific; it does not make every work expansion destructive.

## Contradictions

Witte's work expansion and ABC's changing goals are complementary. Fire is a separate listed manifestation, not the explanation of all changes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Pluto/Asc through changes that redirect activity or enlarge its scope. New duties and a move that expands work facilities in [Witte](#witte-entry) connect with the local development goals and environmental shaping described by [ABC](#abc-entry). Partner goals and contributions may change as part of this process. ABC's reference to change through fire is a particular example alongside these productive meanings.
