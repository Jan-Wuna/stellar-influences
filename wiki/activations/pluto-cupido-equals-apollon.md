## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 257 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To build up a scientific community from small beginnings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 409; PDF page 203.

#### ABC Entry

Extension of joint development, experience of a development community, successful developments in of a community, developing common knowledge, growing trade community, and knowledge development of a community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Expansion of collective knowledge and organization.
- psychology: Learning through shared experience.
- body/health: No bodily or health meaning is given.
- social/relationship: Building scientific or trading communities.
- events/manifestations: Growth from small beginnings and successful shared development.
- conflicts/notes: The scientific example is specific, not a description of every community.

## Contradictions

The scientific and commercial examples extend collective learning into different settings; no direct contradiction is established.

## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Pluto/Cupido as the expansion of a shared undertaking through knowledge and experience. Building a scientific community from small beginnings in [Witte](#witte-entry) connects with shared knowledge, growing trade, and successful collective development in [ABC](#abc-entry). The common thread is a community becoming more extensive and capable, with science and trade as particular examples.
