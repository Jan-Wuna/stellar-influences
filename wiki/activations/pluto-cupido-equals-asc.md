## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 257 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Change in social conditions, relations. Transformation, changes in the social environment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 408; PDF page 203.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (checked against the scan):

> Entwicklung einer Orts-Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Entfaltung mit dem Partner, gemeinsame Veränderung eines Ortes, Kunst-Entwicklungen in einem Ort, Umwelt-Veränderungen durch eine Gemeinschaft, zusammenhängende Veränderungen in einem Ort

Editorial translation: Development of a local community, shared development with a partner, joint change of a place, artistic developments in a place, environmental changes through a community, interconnected changes in a place.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Transformation of social conditions, local community, and immediate relations.
- psychology: No specific inner psychological state is described.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Changes in one's social environment and shared development with a partner or community.
- events/manifestations: Joint changes of a place, local artistic development, and community effects on the environment.
- conflicts/notes: The scan supports local and shared changes, without specifying an economic policy or their desirability.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in changes to social surroundings. ABC adds local community, partnership, and artistic settings rather than an incompatible claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Pluto/Cupido by locating shared transformation in the social environment, immediate relations, and a particular place. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes changed social conditions and surroundings; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) makes this more concrete through a developing local community, shared development with a partner, and joint changes to a place. Local art and changes to the environment are included in that shared development. Neither entry determines whether these changes are welcome or lasting.
