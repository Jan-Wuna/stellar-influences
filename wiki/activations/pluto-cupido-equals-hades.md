## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 257 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Sad social and family conditions. Welfare department.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 409; PDF page 203.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (checked against the scan):

> Entbehrungen einer Entwicklungs-Gemeinschaft, gefährliche Entwicklungen in der Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Entwicklungen aus der Vergangenheit, kriminelle Entwicklung einer Gemeinschaft, mangelhafte Entwicklung einer Gemeinschaft

Editorial translation: Privations in a community undergoing development, dangerous developments in the community, shared developments from the past, criminal development of a community, deficient development of a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Distressed social or family circumstances and deficient or dangerous community development.
- psychology: Sadness belongs to the stated circumstances, not a clinical diagnosis.
- body/health: No specific disease or bodily condition is given.
- social/relationship: Family hardship, community privation, and welfare assistance.
- events/manifestations: Contact with a welfare department; shared developments from the past, dangerous changes, and criminal development are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Criminal development is one attributed possibility, not a judgment about every struggling community.

## Contradictions

Social distress and deficient community development overlap. ABC's references to the past, danger, and criminality are distinct possibilities; Witte's welfare department does not establish that every case involves crime.

## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Pluto/Cupido through hardship affecting a family or community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects distressed social and family conditions with a welfare department, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes privation and deficient or dangerous community development. Shared developments from the past broaden the setting beyond immediate hardship. Criminal development is a narrower attributed possibility, not a necessary explanation for the distress or for welfare contact.
