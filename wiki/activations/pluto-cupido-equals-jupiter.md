## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 257 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Happy and fortunate family, Happy and fortunate development of familiar and social relations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 408; PDF page 203.

#### ABC Entry

Development of a financial community, successful development community, gratifying changes in the arts, joint financial developments, changes in a legal community, and beneficial growth of the whole

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Beneficial development of family or collective life.
- psychology: Happiness and gratification within shared development.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Fortunate family and social relations.
- events/manifestations: Joint financial growth, artistic improvement, or legal-community change.
- conflicts/notes: Positive examples do not guarantee success in every group.

## Contradictions

The family emphasis and financial, legal, or artistic examples differ in setting, without a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Pluto/Cupido through developments experienced as fortunate or beneficial within a family or community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes happy social relations, while [ABC](#abc-entry) gives joint financial development and pleasing artistic change as examples. These belong to a broader reading of shared improvement; they do not establish guaranteed financial gain.
