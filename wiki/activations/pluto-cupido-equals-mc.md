## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 257 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Taking part in the transformation of a community. To become a member of another club. Change in the social surroundings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 408; PDF page 203.

#### ABC Entry

Unfolding the soul of a community, shared emotional development, I change a community, my development in the community, my artistic development, emotional change in the community, a growing community of souls.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal participation in community transformation.
- psychology: Personal and shared emotional development within a community.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Changing club membership or social surroundings.
- events/manifestations: Taking part in a community's transformation.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes personal artistic development as well as changing a community.

## Contradictions

Witte supports participation and changing membership. ABC adds personal artistic development and shared emotional change, extending the community theme inward.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Pluto/Cupido by making the community's transformation personally involving. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes participation, changed social surroundings, and becoming a member of another club. Personal artistic development and shared emotional change in [ABC](#abc-entry) add an inward dimension, alongside the explicit possibility of changing the community oneself.
