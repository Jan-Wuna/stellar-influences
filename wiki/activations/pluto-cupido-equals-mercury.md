## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 257 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

The thoughts are on the changes in the family, or on general social conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 408; PDF page 203.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (checked against the scan):

> Entwicklung der Jugend in der Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Verkehrs-Entwicklung, gemeinsame Sprach-Entwicklung, Gespräche über gemeinsame Entwicklungen, Jugend-Gemeinschaften im Wandel, wachsende Verkehrs-Gemeinschaft

Editorial translation: Development of young people in the community, shared development of traffic or transport, shared language development, conversations about shared developments, youth communities undergoing change, a growing transport community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thinking and talking about shared change, alongside youth, language, and transport development in a community.
- psychology: Attention directed to changing family or collective circumstances.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is present.
- social/relationship: Family and general social conditions, youth communities, and shared language or transport activity.
- events/manifestations: Conversations about common developments, changes in youth communities, and growth of a transport community.
- conflicts/notes: Thinking about change does not establish that the thinker causes it; ABC's transport and language clauses are distinct examples.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes the subject of thought; ABC includes conversation and concrete community developments. These emphases do not conflict or require a technological-research interpretation.

## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies Pluto/Cupido through thinking and talking about change shared by a family or community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) directs attention to family changes and general social conditions; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes conversations about common developments and change within youth communities. Shared language development and the development or growth of transport activity give the formula additional concrete settings. Attention and discussion alone do not establish who causes the change or how it is received.
