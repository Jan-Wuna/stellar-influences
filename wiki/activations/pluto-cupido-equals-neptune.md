## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 257 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Dissolution of a family or partnership. Uncertain social and family conditions. To quit a corporation or society.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 409; PDF page 203.

#### ABC Entry

Disappointing developments in communities, development within metaphysical communities, subtle-material evolutionary communities, shared development of the future, weak development toward the whole, synthesis development through intuition.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertain or dissolving collective and family conditions.
- psychology: ABC includes disappointing development alongside synthesis through intuition.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Leaving a society, corporation, family, or partnership.
- events/manifestations: Dissolution and uncertain membership; metaphysical communities in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: The source's metaphysical and subtle-material community vocabulary is retained without adding a mechanism.

## Contradictions

Witte's dissolution and ABC's metaphysical or intuitive development are different emphases. The positive and difficult examples are not interchangeable.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Pluto/Cupido through uncertain group ties or a community whose direction is difficult to define. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives dissolution and leaving a corporation or society as concrete possibilities. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also places development in metaphysical communities and intuition, alongside disappointing developments; these examples retain both intuitive and difficult expressions.
