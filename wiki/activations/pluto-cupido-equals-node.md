## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 257 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To be connected with changed conditions of being.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 408; PDF page 203.

#### ABC Entry

Developing common links, different links in of a community, links to a development community, growing links to the whole, and changes in common links

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development and alteration of shared connections.
- psychology: No specific emotional state is stated.
- body/health: No bodily or health interpretation appears.
- social/relationship: Links within a community and connections to a developing group.
- events/manifestations: Changed affiliations as collective circumstances change.
- conflicts/notes: The entries describe connection without prescribing its quality.

## Contradictions

Witte's broad changed conditions and ABC's explicit shared links fit together; neither establishes coercion or a guaranteed beneficial bond.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Pluto/Cupido through connections within, or to, a changing community. The broad involvement in altered conditions in [Witte](#witte-entry) becomes more concrete in [ABC](#abc-entry): developing shared links, changing existing connections, and becoming connected to a collective undertaking. The evidence does not determine whether those changes strengthen every relationship.
