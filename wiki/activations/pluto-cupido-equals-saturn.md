## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 257 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Parting from the family through marriage, divorce, moving or death. Leaving the civil service or a corporation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 409; PDF page 203.

#### ABC Entry

serious developmental community, serious artistic development, inhibited community developments, shared developments in old age, concentration of a community on development, and problematic community developments

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Separation from a collective or constrained shared development.
- psychology: Seriousness and concentration on development.
- body/health: Death is one listed reason for family separation, not a prediction.
- social/relationship: Parting from family or leaving institutional membership.
- events/manifestations: Marriage, divorce, relocation, or departure from civil service.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also includes development in old age and artistic seriousness.

## Contradictions

Witte describes leaving, while ABC describes constrained or concentrated development within a community. These are different expressions, not mutually exclusive rules.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Pluto/Cupido through limits on collective participation and the work of serious shared development. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes departure from family or institutional membership, with several distinct possible causes. [ABC](#abc-entry) also retains development within a group through concentration, seriousness, or old age, even when progress is inhibited. Separation is therefore one expression rather than the whole reading.
