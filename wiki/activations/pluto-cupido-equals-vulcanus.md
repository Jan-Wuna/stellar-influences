## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 257 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Great increase in families and communities. For a nation or a country it signifies increase of the population and land or territory. The social surroundings experience a drastic change.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 409; PDF page 203.

#### ABC Entry

influential developmental community, energy transformation for the community, community of energy transformation, forceful developments in the community, changes of power in of a community, and strength of a shared development

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Large-scale growth or forceful change in a collective.
- psychology: No particular inner state is supplied.
- body/health: Population increase is a collective example, not a bodily diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Growing families and communities; shifts in collective power.
- events/manifestations: Drastic social change and territorial or population increase in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: National growth is a scale-specific historical example.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes increase; ABC adds force and power changes, which need not always enlarge a community. No direct contradiction is established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Pluto/Cupido through the scale and force of collective development. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes expanding families or communities and drastic changes in social surroundings, extending the example to population or territory for a country. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds influence, energy transformation, and changes of power; these specify forceful collective change without making expansion its only form.
