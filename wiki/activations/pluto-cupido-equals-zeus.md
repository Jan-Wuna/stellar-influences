## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 257 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Family increase. Members of a family who are soldiers. Army unites in the state of development. Military tradition.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 409; PDF page 203.

#### ABC Entry

Strenuous development within a community, leading artistic developments, founding a development community, development of a community's achievements, changes through shared shaping, aims of a development community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Collective development directed toward organized aims.
- psychology: No specific internal state is established.
- body/health: Family increase does not establish a reproductive or medical prediction.
- social/relationship: Family military traditions and organized collective activity.
- events/manifestations: Developing army units, shared design, and community goals.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes leading artistic developments and founding a development community, beyond the military examples.

## Contradictions

Witte's family and military examples are more specific than ABC's shared goals. Their difference does not justify treating every community as military.

## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Pluto/Cupido through the development of a collective organized around aims and coordinated activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives family increase, soldiers within a family, and developing army units as particular examples. The shared shaping, community goals and artistic developments in [ABC](#abc-entry) extend the reading to collective projects.
