## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

First symptoms of a deep seated transformation. Changes which first are called off a few times, until they succeed.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 411; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Blocked development from the past, depression due to deficient developments, restriction through secret changes, a dangerous developmental cycle, thorough changes in old age, deepening into developments from the past.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Deep change that encounters blockage or repeated postponement.
- psychology: ABC names depression after poor development; Witte describes early signs of transformation.
- body/health: These historical psychological terms establish no clinical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Hidden changes may restrict circumstances; no specific relationship outcome is stated.
- events/manifestations: Changes called off repeatedly before succeeding in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also retains resistance and a dangerous developmental cycle.

## Contradictions

Witte explicitly allows eventual success after postponements; ABC does not promise that its blocked cycle resolves. These conditional ranges should remain separate.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Pluto/Hades through difficult change that begins below the surface or fails to proceed directly. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes repeated cancellations before a transformation succeeds. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds blocked development rooted in the past and restrictions through hidden changes; its depressive language describes the source's difficult range, not a diagnosis or proof that every blockage ends successfully.
