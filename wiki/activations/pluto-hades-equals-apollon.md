## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

An evil development which starts very small, grows and grows bigger.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 411; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Successful developments from the past, deficient development of knowledge, adverse changes in trade, extensive changes in old age, many changes under privations, transformations through experiences from the past.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: An expanding harmful development, alongside changes informed by the past.
- psychology: Learning from past experience is included in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is given.
- social/relationship: Trade and knowledge are particular settings for adverse change.
- events/manifestations: A small harmful beginning grows larger in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also preserves successful developments from the past.

## Contradictions

Witte's harmful growth and ABC's successful developments are a real difference in valence; the positive examples must not be flattened into universal decline.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Pluto/Hades through a development that extends in scale or through accumulated experience. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) is specifically adverse: a small harmful beginning grows larger. [ABC](#abc-entry) includes adverse trade and knowledge developments but also successful developments from the past and change through experience. The latter leaves a constructive range that Witte's short statement does not describe.
