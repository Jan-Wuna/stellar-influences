## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Incapable of marriage. Marriages and partnerships of short duration. Marriage prevented through poverty and shortage. Decrease of members in clubs.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 411; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Developments from a common past, common dangerous development, common change in old age, secret change in of a community, poor common development, and adverse change in of a community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Collective ties strained by deprivation or adverse change.
- psychology: The entries do not establish a specific internal psychological state.
- body/health: 'Incapable of marriage' is historical relationship wording, not a medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Short partnerships, obstacles to marriage, or declining club membership.
- events/manifestations: Poverty or shortage may prevent marriage in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: Shared history and secret collective changes are additional ABC settings.

## Contradictions

Witte's categorical opening is broader than its specific poverty and short-duration examples. It is retained as source language rather than universal incapacity.

## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Pluto/Hades through the effects of deprivation and difficult development on shared life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives short partnerships, marriage obstructed by poverty, and fewer club members. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a common past and adverse or hidden changes within a community. These are circumstances that strain participation; the historical label 'incapable of marriage' is not a verdict on anyone's lasting capacity for relationship.
