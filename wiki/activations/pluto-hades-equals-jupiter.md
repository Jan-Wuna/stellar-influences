## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Fictitious luck. Evil things are palliated. Decline of fortune and welfare.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 410; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Successful developments from the past, joy about hidden changes, mysterious money development, happiness development in old age, poor money growth, adverse legal development, and growing financial crime

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Apparent benefit or declining welfare, with some constructive past-based developments.
- psychology: Joy about hidden changes and happiness in old age appear in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily or clinical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Adverse legal development and financial wrongdoing are ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: Fictitious luck, poor money growth, or a decline of fortune.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's successful and happy examples retain a positive range.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes apparent luck that masks decline; ABC also gives success and happiness. Those different evaluations cannot be synthesized as uniformly good or bad.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Pluto/Hades through the quality of apparent benefit and changing welfare. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) warns that luck can be fictitious and harmful conditions minimized while fortune declines. [ABC](#abc-entry) includes financial and legal difficulties, but also success rooted in the past, joy about hidden changes, and happiness in old age. These positive examples remain distinct from Witte's more consistently adverse reading.
