## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

The incompetent chief or boss. Failure of leadership. Incompetent government. Growing dissatisfaction with the government. Bad relation to the chief or to the government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 411; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Special development from the past, great change in old age, poor development of the state, adverse development of reputation, adverse changes for self employed, self conscious development in old age, and hidden changes in the state

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Troubled leadership, state development, or reputation.
- psychology: Dissatisfaction with leadership in Witte.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is stated.
- social/relationship: Poor relations with a chief or government.
- events/manifestations: Leadership failure and hidden changes in the state.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also includes developments from the past and in old age.

## Contradictions

Witte judges leadership harshly; ABC gives a wider set of state, reputation, and age-related developments. These are emphases, not proof every authority is incompetent.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Pluto/Hades through difficult developments in leadership and public standing. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) centers on failed leadership, dissatisfaction, and strained relations with a chief or government. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds poor state development, adverse reputation changes, and hidden changes within the state, while retaining age-related and past-based examples. The adverse leadership theme does not exhaust the entry's range.
