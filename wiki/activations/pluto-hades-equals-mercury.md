## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

In a melancholy mood, depressed. To think about grievances. Talk about the situation which is changing for the worse. Pessimism. Error.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 410; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Reports of dangerous developments, language about developments of the past, difficult youth development, secret thoughts of changes, adverse traffic development, news about hidden changes, and growing youth crime

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought and communication concerning grievance or adverse change.
- psychology: Melancholy, pessimism, preoccupation with grievances, or error in Witte.
- body/health: Historical 'depressed' wording is not a clinical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Discussion and reporting of difficult conditions.
- events/manifestations: News of dangerous or hidden developments; youth and traffic difficulties in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's reports do not imply the speaker causes the reported harm.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes mood and thought; ABC emphasizes reports and particular settings. Reporting a danger is distinct from thinking falsely or causing it.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies Pluto/Hades through thought and communication about difficult developments. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes grievances, pessimism, and talk of worsening conditions. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds reports of danger or hidden change, alongside youth and traffic examples. The distinction matters: concern about a real adverse situation is not equivalent to error, and a report does not make its speaker responsible for the harm.
