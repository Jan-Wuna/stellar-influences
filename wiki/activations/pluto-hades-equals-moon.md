## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Persons unreliable. Decline of people. People without discipline and order, excited about a nuisance, rebelling against corruption.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 410; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

German passage (checked against the scan):

> Gefährliche Veränderung einer Frau, Entwicklungen aus der Vergangenheit eines Volkes, mangelhafte Gefühls-Entwicklung, Veränderungen für eine alte Frau, verborgene Gemüts-Veränderungen, wachsender Gefühls-Mangel

Editorial translation: Dangerous change involving a woman, developments from the past of a people, deficient emotional development, changes for an old woman, hidden changes of mood, growing lack of feeling.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult collective conditions, reactions against corruption, and troubled emotional development.
- psychology: Agitation about grievances; deficient emotional development, hidden mood changes, and growing lack of feeling in ABC.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is stated; ABC's reference to an old woman is an age-related example.
- social/relationship: Public disorder or rebellion against corruption; women are specific ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: A people's decline and developments connected with its past.
- conflicts/notes: Emotional and gendered descriptions are attributed source claims, not diagnoses or general judgments about women.

## Contradictions

Witte's public response to corruption is distinct from its judgment of unreliability. ABC adds emotional and gendered examples, which do not establish that public disorder and deficient feeling must occur together.

## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Pluto/Hades through difficult public or emotional developments. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes collective decline and disorder as well as agitation and rebellion against corruption. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds developments from a people's past and a more inward range: deficient emotional development, hidden changes of mood, and a growing lack of feeling. Its examples involving a woman or an old woman remain source-specific possibilities. These public and emotional expressions need not occur together, and the entry supplies no clinical diagnosis.
