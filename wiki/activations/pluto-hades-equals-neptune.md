## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To get slowly, unnoticeable and irresistibly into an evil situation. Sneaking crisis of the most evil kind. Going down. Break down. Deterioration.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 411; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Risk of deception within a development, deficient exchange of air, adverse changes in water, changing dirty water, uncertain development from the past, changes through age-related weakness, a growing risk of weakness.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Gradual, difficult-to-notice deterioration.
- psychology: Uncertainty about developments connected with the past in ABC.
- body/health: ABC mentions age-related weakness historically, without establishing a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: No specific relationship outcome is stated.
- events/manifestations: A creeping crisis or worsening conditions; adverse water changes in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC adds deception and water/air settings; the following Pluto cross-reference is not part of this row.

## Contradictions

Witte strongly emphasizes deterioration; ABC adds deception risk and environmental examples, including changing dirty water. Those examples do not establish a universal medical outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Pluto/Hades through deterioration that can proceed slowly and escape notice. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a creeping crisis and breakdown; the uncertainty, deception risk and water-related phrases in [ABC](#abc-entry) add particular settings. The historical age-related weakness wording does not supply a diagnosis.
