## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Connection with persons who disappoint, who fall short of expectations. The same with things. To be connected with untenable things. Eager for or thinking about remedial measures.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 410; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Development of a connection from the past, dangerous development in a connection, development of secret connections, alteration of a connection under deprivation, and renunciation of connection changes

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections burdened by disappointment, danger, or untenable circumstances.
- psychology: Considering remedial measures in Witte.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim appears.
- social/relationship: Disappointing contacts, secret ties, or relationships under deprivation.
- events/manifestations: Changes to old connections or refraining from changing them.
- conflicts/notes: Association with difficulty is distinct from moral condemnation of every contact.

## Contradictions

Witte includes efforts to remedy a situation as well as disappointment; ABC's secret and past-based ties do not themselves establish wrongdoing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Pluto/Hades through connections that disappoint or become difficult to sustain. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes untenable circumstances and thinking about remedies, preserving an active response to the problem. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds ties from the past, secret connections, and changes under deprivation. The shared theme is difficulty within connection, rather than a blanket claim that the people involved are bad.
