## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To despair as far as belief and good are concerned. To be without a backbone in worlds concept. Deterioration of faith.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 411; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Old cultural development, spiritual change under deprivation, dangerous change in truth, defective spiritual development, adverse cultural development, hidden spiritual development, and renunciation of a clear development

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strained faith, worldview, or cultural development.
- psychology: Despair concerning belief and goodness in Witte.
- body/health: No bodily or clinical condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Cultural difficulties and spiritual change under deprivation.
- events/manifestations: Deterioration of faith or hidden spiritual development.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's 'without a backbone' is historical evaluative language.

## Contradictions

Witte's loss of faith and ABC's older or hidden spiritual development are not identical. Hidden development alone does not establish deception.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Pluto/Hades through difficulties in belief, understanding, or cultural development. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes despair and deteriorating faith, while [ABC](#abc-entry) includes spiritual change under deprivation and adverse cultural development. ABC also preserves older and hidden spiritual developments. The evidence supports strain in these areas without equating every private spiritual process with falsehood.
