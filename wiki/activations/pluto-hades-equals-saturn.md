## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Grievances which cannot be removed for a long time. A bad situation grows worse.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 411; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Developments under deprivation and pain, patience despite lack of development, developing dangerous tasks, slow developments in old age, problems caused by secret changes, and losses caused by growing criminality

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Persistent hardship and slow or obstructed development.
- psychology: Patience despite a lack of progress in ABC.
- body/health: Pain is a historical listed experience, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Losses through growing crime are a particular ABC example.
- events/manifestations: Long-standing grievances, slow changes in old age, or worsening conditions.
- conflicts/notes: The entries do not establish separation as the sole outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes duration and worsening; ABC adds patience and specific causes of difficulty. These fit without proving that hardship is permanent.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Pluto/Hades through difficult conditions that resist quick resolution. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) stresses grievances lasting a long time and worsening situations. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds deprivation, pain, slow developments in old age, and patience despite little progress. The interpretation centers on persistence under limitation, with crime-related losses and dangerous tasks as particular examples.
