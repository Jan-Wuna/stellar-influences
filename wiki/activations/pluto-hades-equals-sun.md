## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

The man who physically fails, whose body is deteriorating. Chronic disease.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 410; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous development of a man, corporal changes in old age, defective corporal development, adverse personal changes, hidden corporal changes, hidden center development, and growing personal danger

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Adverse physical or personal development.
- psychology: The entries do not supply a distinct psychological state.
- body/health: Witte historically associates this formula with bodily decline and chronic disease; this is not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Male examples are source language, not an exclusive restriction.
- events/manifestations: Bodily changes in old age and hidden physical changes in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: No named disease, clinical mechanism, or inevitable outcome is established.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in adverse bodily change. Their historical medical and gendered descriptions remain attributed rather than universalized.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Pluto/Hades through physical and personal deterioration in these historical entries. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names bodily decline and chronic disease; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds adverse or hidden physical changes and changes in old age. This is an attributed astrological association, not evidence of disease in a person or a prognosis. Neither entry identifies a specific condition or establishes that the examples apply only to men.
