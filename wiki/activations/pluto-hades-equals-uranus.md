## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Suddenly experiencing difficulties and hindrances. To be suddenly hard pressed. To get surprisingly into a bad situation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 411; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting development of criminality, secret reform developments, lack of dynamism of development, new adverse development, sudden development from the past, surprising development in old age, and renunciation of sudden change

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden difficulty or adverse change.
- psychology: Being surprised or hard pressed in Witte.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Secret reform developments and criminality are particular ABC settings.
- events/manifestations: Unexpected hindrances or developments emerging from the past.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also includes absent dynamism and refraining from sudden change.

## Contradictions

Witte describes abrupt difficulties; ABC includes both sudden development and lack of dynamism. The formula should not be reduced to perpetual activity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies Pluto/Hades through difficulty that arrives abruptly or unexpectedly. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes sudden pressure and hindrance, while [ABC](#abc-entry) adds new adverse developments, secret reforms, and changes emerging from the past. ABC also retains a lack of dynamism and refraining from sudden change, so its range includes obstructed or withheld change as well as disruption.
