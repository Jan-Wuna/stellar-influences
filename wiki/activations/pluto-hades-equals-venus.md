## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Unreliable in love. Faked affections. Decline of morals.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 410; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Harmonic development from the past, joy in love service, poor peace development, adverse development of love, growing harmony in old age, growing love in secret, and growing beauty through deprivation

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult affection alongside harmony developing under limitation.
- psychology: Joy in loving service and growing harmony appear in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Unreliable or feigned affection in Witte; secret love in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Adverse love or peace development, with harmony in old age also retained.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's moral-decline language is an attributed judgment.

## Contradictions

Witte is consistently adverse about affection, whereas ABC includes service, harmony, and growing love. This is a substantive difference in valence, not uniform deterioration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies Pluto/Hades through changes in affection and harmony under difficult or hidden circumstances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes unreliable or feigned affection. [ABC](#abc-entry) preserves adverse love development but also joy in loving service, harmony in old age, and love growing in secret. Those constructive examples prevent a synthesis that treats all such affection as false or morally deficient.
