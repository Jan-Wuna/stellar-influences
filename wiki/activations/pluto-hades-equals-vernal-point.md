## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Symptoms of deterioration and decadence. General decline. General break down.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 410; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous developments in public life, adverse changes for the public, hidden transformations in public life, changes in public life arising from the past, growing criminality in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Deterioration in wider public conditions.
- psychology: No particular psychological state is described.
- body/health: No specific disease or bodily condition is given.
- social/relationship: Adverse developments affecting the public.
- events/manifestations: Public danger, general breakdown, or increasing crime.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes hidden transformations in public life; hidden does not specify the scale of a change.

## Contradictions

Witte's general decline and ABC's public examples are compatible; they do not establish that every large public change is harmful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Pluto/Hades through adverse developments in public life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes general decline and breakdown; [ABC](#abc-entry) supplies public danger, disadvantage, and increasing crime as examples. ABC also includes hidden transformations in public life, without specifying their scale.
