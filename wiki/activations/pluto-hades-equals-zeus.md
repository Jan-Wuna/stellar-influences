## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 258 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To make efforts in vain. Useless efforts, wasted strength.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 411; PDF page 204.

#### ABC Entry

Beginning dangerous developments, secret changes of direction, criminal developments in leadership, deficient efforts toward development, changes of direction in old age, creative developments from the past.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Misapplied or fruitless effort within difficult development.
- psychology: No particular emotional state is described.
- body/health: Wasted strength is an activity description, not a bodily diagnosis.
- social/relationship: ABC includes criminal developments in leadership as a distinct adverse example.
- events/manifestations: Poor developmental efforts, hidden changes of direction, and creative developments from the past.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's leadership example does not make every leader or change of direction criminal.

## Contradictions

Witte describes effort in vain; ABC also retains creative developments from the past. Fruitless effort is therefore not the only recorded expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Pluto/Hades through the direction and effectiveness of effort. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes useless effort and wasted strength; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds poor developmental efforts, dangerous beginnings, and hidden changes of direction. Its creative developments from the past remain a different possibility; criminal developments in leadership are a separate adverse example.
