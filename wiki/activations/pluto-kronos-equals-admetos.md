## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 260 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Transformation in a small circle. Deep reaching change for a few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 415; PDF page 206.

#### ABC Entry

Especially thorough development, blocked development toward independence, restricted unfolding for the state, development toward independence through endurance, thorough changes in the state, resistance to priority changes.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Deep change concentrated within a limited circle.
- psychology: ABC includes developing independence through endurance.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is given.
- social/relationship: A few people are affected by a deep-reaching transformation.
- events/manifestations: Restricted state development and resistance to priority changes in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC pairs thorough development and independence with blockage and resistance.

## Contradictions

Witte's small-circle transformation concerns scope; ABC adds restriction and resistance alongside thorough development and independence through endurance.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Pluto/Kronos through a transformation whose reach is concentrated rather than broad. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly describes deep change for a few people. The thorough development and independence through endurance in [ABC](#abc-entry) add a constructive range alongside its restricted state development and resistance.
