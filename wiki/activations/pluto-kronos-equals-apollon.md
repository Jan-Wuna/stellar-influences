## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 260 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

From a tiny start developing great transformation. Many are examined, tested. A great change for many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 415; PDF page 206.

#### ABC Entry

Especially successful development, unfolding within the hierarchy of knowledge, successful development of authority, major changes in trade, independent unfolding of knowledge, extensive development of authority, increasing unfolding of self-confidence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Extensive transformation growing from a small beginning.
- psychology: ABC includes independent development of knowledge and growing self-confidence.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Great change involving many people.
- events/manifestations: Many people examined or tested; major trade changes in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's authority development is distinct from an assertion of authoritarian politics.

## Contradictions

Witte's examinations and broad change differ from ABC's successful development; neither requires that all testing produces advancement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Pluto/Kronos through a change that grows in reach from a small beginning. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes major transformation for many people and many being examined or tested. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful development, knowledge hierarchies, and major trade changes. ABC also includes independent knowledge development and growing self-confidence; its authority vocabulary does not establish authoritarian rule.
