## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 260 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To experience through others or with others a great change. Great changes at the place. To attend an educational school with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 414; PDF page 206.

#### ABC Entry

Others develop self-confidence, special developments at the location, independent partner development, state-related environmental changes, change in a place's hierarchy, growing authority of the partner, an important change of partner.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Major local change experienced through or with others.
- psychology: Others developing self-confidence is an ABC example.
- body/health: No bodily or health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Shared experience and educational attendance with others.
- events/manifestations: Large changes at a place or changes in its hierarchy.
- conflicts/notes: ABC names growing authority of the partner, alongside self-confidence and local change.

## Contradictions

Witte includes education and shared change; ABC adds independent partner development, growing authority and local hierarchy.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Pluto/Kronos by placing major change in shared experience and the immediate environment. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes change through or with other people, large local changes, and attending an educational school together. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds self-confidence in others, growing authority of the partner and changes in a place's hierarchy.
