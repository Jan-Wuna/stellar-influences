## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 260 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Evil changes caused by meanness and treachery.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 415; PDF page 206 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Impeded development toward independence, dangerous developments in the state, great developments from the past, criminal developments in the state, directing hidden developments, growing criminal superiority, growing lack of authority.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Adverse changes involving treachery, impeded independence, or dangerous state developments.
- psychology: Meanness and treachery are attributed moral descriptions.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Dangerous or criminal state developments and deficient authority are ABC settings.
- events/manifestations: Major developments from the past and direction of hidden developments.
- conflicts/notes: Criminality is a specific possibility; hidden activity and lack of authority are not equivalent to it.

## Contradictions

Witte gives a moral cause for adverse change; ABC lists broader settings. Neither supports a generic failed-promotion reading.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Pluto/Kronos through adverse changes rather than assured advancement. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) attributes such changes to meanness and treachery; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds impeded independence, dangerous or criminal developments in the state, and increasing lack of authority. Developments from the past and the direction of hidden changes are additional settings. These specific claims do not establish that every difficulty in authority is criminal.
