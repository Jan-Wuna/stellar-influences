### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 260 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

The beginning of a fortunate development. Favors through the boss or superiors.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 414; PDF page 206 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Particularly successful development, development to legal independence, joy over growing prestige, joy over important changes, state financial developments, state legal changes, and growing state achievements

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A fortunate beginning and favorable development supported by superiors.
- psychology: Joy in growing prestige or important changes.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Favor from a boss or superiors; legal independence and state achievements.
- events/manifestations: Successful development, state financial development, and legal changes.
- conflicts/notes: A favorable beginning does not guarantee promotion or wealth.

## Contradictions

Witte's favor from superiors and ABC's legal independence are compatible possibilities at different scales.

## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Pluto/Kronos through the beginning of a fortunate development. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects this with favors from a boss or superiors; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds particularly successful development, legal independence, and joy in growing prestige or important changes. State financial and legal developments broaden the setting beyond personal advancement, while neither entry guarantees a particular promotion or financial result.
