## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 260 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Women who succeed. Dealing with persons under the impression of great changes and transformations. Women, the nation or the public experience great changes and transformations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 414; PDF page 206 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Special emotional development, development of a people toward greatness, directing emotional development, great changes for a people, self-confident development of a woman, growing authority of a woman, growing self-confidence of a people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Great changes involving women, a people, or public conditions.
- psychology: Emotional development and growing self-confidence are ABC themes.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Women, a nation, and the public are explicitly named; family is not stated.
- events/manifestations: Women's success, growing authority, and major changes affecting a people.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered success and authority examples remain attributed possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte's successful women and public transformation coexist with ABC's emotional development. These do not imply a universal female trait.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Pluto/Kronos through changes affecting women, a people, or the public. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes women who succeed and people affected by major transformations; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds directed emotional development, a woman's growing authority, and a people's growing self-confidence. The public and gendered examples remain distinct possibilities, with no basis for adding a family setting or assuming universal success.
