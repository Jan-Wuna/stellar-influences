## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 260 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

To fail an examination. Great changes caused by separation. The fall of a government. The end of a form of government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 415; PDF page 206 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Particularly slow developments, disturbed unfolding of independence, problematic development within hierarchy, great changes in old age, concentration on state changes, growing disturbances in the state.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Failed tests, separation, slow development, or disruption in hierarchy and government.
- psychology: Concentration on state changes; no fuller emotional profile is given.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Problematic hierarchy and disturbed independence.
- events/manifestations: Failed examination, fall or end of government, major changes in old age.
- conflicts/notes: Age-related change is not itself proof of failure.

## Contradictions

Witte's failures and endings are narrower than ABC's slow or age-related change; these should not be equated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Pluto/Kronos through development meeting a test, separation, or structural difficulty. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes a failed examination, major changes caused by separation, and the fall or end of a government. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds slow development, disturbed independence, and problems within hierarchy, while major changes in old age remain a separate setting. The reading therefore preserves concrete setbacks without making every slow change a failure.
