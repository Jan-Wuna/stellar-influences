## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 260 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Days which bring drastic changes. The man who experiences a great change of his physical functions. To change one’s diet.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 414; PDF page 206 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Special development of a person, personal development within a hierarchy, development of a person toward independence, great changes for a person, unfolding personal authority, a state development centre, growing personal authority.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Drastic personal or bodily change, with independence and authority as ABC settings.
- psychology: Personal independence and growing authority in ABC.
- body/health: Witte names changes of physical functions and diet; no diagnosis or recommended diet is supplied.
- social/relationship: Personal development within hierarchy and a state development centre.
- events/manifestations: Great personal changes, changed physical functions, or changed diet are source examples.
- conflicts/notes: Bodily change is not attributed to increased responsibility or rank by these entries.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes bodily and dietary changes; ABC emphasizes independence and authority. The entries do not state a causal link between them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Pluto/Kronos through drastic changes affecting the person. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) specifically names altered physical functions and a changed diet; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal development in hierarchy, independence, and growing authority. These are different settings of personal change, not evidence that responsibility or rank causes a bodily adjustment. Witte's bodily language remains an attributed statement without a diagnosis or dietary recommendation.
