## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 260 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

To put themselves at the top. Mighty changes enforced by the power of the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 415; PDF page 206 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Considerable development of power, special development of violence, influential unfolding of a hierarchy, great changes of power, self-confident unfolding of power, state power changes, growing force or violence of authority, important energy development.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Taking a top position or enforcing mighty changes through state power.
- psychology: Self-confident exercise of power in ABC.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: The state, hierarchy, authority, and violence are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: Large power shifts, influential hierarchy development, and energy development.
- conflicts/notes: Power and violence remain distinct source expressions; neither proves merit.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize power; Witte's enforcement qualifies any reading that treats advancement as automatically favorable.

## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Pluto/Kronos through power sufficient to enforce major change. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes taking a place at the top and mighty changes enforced by the state. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds influential hierarchy development, large power shifts, self-confident power, and authority backed by force or violence. The strength of this position does not establish its merit or benefit to those affected.
