## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 69 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Development of persistence and firmness. Standstill of development.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 83; PDF page 40 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Blocked inner development, my development is restricted, my thorough development, developing inner firmness, profound inner changes, growing inner firmness, change through inner repression.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Inner development meeting restriction or standstill while persistence and firmness develop.
- psychology: Inner firmness, depth, and the effects of repression.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: No specific relationship setting is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Blocked development, profound inner changes, and greater firmness.
- conflicts/notes: Persistence and standstill coexist; adaptation to altered circumstances is not expressly stated.

## Contradictions

Firmness and standstill are different expressions, not a guarantee that restriction yields depth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Pluto/MC through personal development that can become firm or come to a standstill. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) holds persistence and halted development together; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds restriction, thorough inner development, deep changes, and repression. The common issue is how development becomes constrained or consolidated, without assuming that every blockage produces growth.
