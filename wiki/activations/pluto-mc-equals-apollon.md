## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 69 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Free, uninhibited mental development. Ones own development in professions of trading or sciences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 83; PDF page 40 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

My development in trade, my successful development, my knowledge development, my knowledge of transformation, soul growth experience, psychic freedom develop, knowledge of the soul develop, and increasing psychic change

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Free mental development through knowledge, trade, or science.
- psychology: Inner freedom, experience of growth, and knowledge of transformation.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Professions in trading or science are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Successful development and increasing inner change.
- conflicts/notes: Success is a source possibility, not a guaranteed career result.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in free development and knowledge; no incompatible claim is explicit.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Pluto/MC through personal development that can proceed freely and expand knowledge. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly connects uninhibited mental development with trading or scientific professions; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful development, knowledge of transformation, inner freedom, and experience of growth. These concrete settings give the development scope without establishing that every expansion brings professional success.
