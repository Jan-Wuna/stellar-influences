## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 69 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

To influence the development of others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 82; PDF page 40 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Development of my partner, I change partners, my changes of place, my changes with others, my surroundings change, the inner development of the other, inner development with or through partners, inner development in the surroundings.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: Printed page 299; PDF page 301 (one-based; coded activator entry checked).

#### Ebertin Entry

Procurement of an important position and of recognition under any condition. A person of fame.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 118 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

One has a strong need to be respected and/or to be seen as a powerful person. A personality fraught with intense insecurities. One’s limited sense of who one is may stand in the way of being able to allow spiritual forces in to one’s life. The need to let go of basic assumptions about the self that are restrictive and hence prevent one’s following one’s spiritual path.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `PLUTO/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `352`

#### Munkasey Entry

Concentrating more on your goals; increased ability to seek out practical ways of meeting success; added ability to persuade others to your ideas; an extreme intensity and power when addressing others; personal power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal development expressed through influence, recognition, and relations with others.
- psychology: Need for respect or power may coexist with intense insecurity and restrictive assumptions about identity.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Influencing others or a partner; persuading people and seeking recognition.
- events/manifestations: An important position, fame, practical pursuit of goals, and changes in surroundings.
- conflicts/notes: Recognition and power can express development while leaving inner security unsettled.

## Contradictions

Ebertin's recognition contrasts in emphasis with Sandbach's insecure self-concept; outward standing does not establish inner security.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Pluto/MC through how personal development acts upon and is seen by other people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names influencing others, and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places change in partnership and surroundings. An important position or recognition in [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) and persuasive, intense pursuit of goals in [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) give this influence a public expression. The need to be respected can nevertheless coexist with intense insecurity: [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes restrictive assumptions about identity that obstruct openness to a spiritual path. Outward recognition therefore does not settle the inner question of security.
