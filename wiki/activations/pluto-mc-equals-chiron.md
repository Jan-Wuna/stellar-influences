## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 118 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s vibration helps others to see the true source of their security and hence to let go of their insecurities. The ability to heal through helping others to become more attuned to spiritual realities.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Helping others release insecurity by recognizing a spiritual source of security.
- psychology: Attunement to spiritual realities and less attachment to insecurity.
- body/health: Sandbach uses healing language but identifies no physical condition, medical mechanism, or treatment.
- social/relationship: Helping others discover security is the explicit interpersonal role.
- events/manifestations: A shift in others' understanding of security; no external event is specified.
- conflicts/notes: The single entry makes a spiritual healing claim, not a clinical finding.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present. It supplies no competing account or basis for a preparatory sequence of healing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Chiron specifies Pluto/MC in the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) through helping other people recognize what it calls the true source of their security. Attunement to spiritual realities is presented as enabling them to release insecurity, and the source calls this healing. The entry does not describe a medical intervention or establish an order in which defenses must be addressed, so its scope remains the stated spiritual and interpersonal change.
