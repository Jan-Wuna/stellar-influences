## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 69 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

To grow worse. To be on the decline, downfall. Moment of changes in condition of a disease, for better or worse. The attitude to developments of the far past.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 83; PDF page 40 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Unfolding of an old soul, developments from my past, my dangerous change, my deficient development, my development in secret, adverse inner development, growth of the soul in secret.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Adverse personal development and changes linked with the past or hidden inner life.
- psychology: Deficient or concealed inner development and growth in secret.
- body/health: Witte includes a change in a disease's condition for better or worse; no disease or prognosis is specified.
- social/relationship: No specific interpersonal setting is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Decline, dangerous change, hidden development, and engagement with the far past.
- conflicts/notes: The disease clause explicitly allows improvement as well as deterioration.

## Contradictions

Witte's general decline does not cancel its for-better-or-worse disease qualification; the past and hidden growth have no necessary adverse outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Pluto/MC through personal development associated with decline, danger, or what remains hidden. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) also connects the attitude with developments of the far past, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes one's past, deficient development, and inner growth in secret. Witte's disease example expressly allows a change for better or worse; it cannot support a uniformly worsening prognosis or any diagnosis.
