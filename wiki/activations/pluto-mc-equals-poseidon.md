## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 69 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Perceptive change. The turning from materialism to spiritualism.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 83; PDF page 40 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Development of my ideas, development of spirit and soul, realization of a psychic change, understanding of the soul, developing my spiritual development, developing my cultural development, soul culture, and psychic development through truth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Perceptive inner change involving ideas, understanding, and spiritual outlook.
- psychology: Recognition of inner transformation and development of ideas or understanding of the soul.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Cultural development is stated; no specific relationship is described.
- events/manifestations: A turn from materialism to spiritualism is Witte's specific example.
- conflicts/notes: Spiritual truth and soul language remain attributed source vocabulary.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in inner and spiritual development; no incompatible claim is explicit.

## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Pluto/MC through a change in understanding of oneself and one's outlook. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives the specific example of turning from materialism to spiritualism; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds ideas, recognition of inner transformation, knowledge of the soul, and cultural development. These expressions establish the source's mental and spiritual range without requiring a particular education or verifying a spiritual belief.
