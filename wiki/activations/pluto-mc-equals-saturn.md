## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The own development runs under inhibitions or slows down. The moment when the change or transformation is in delay.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Tempered psychic connection, slow psychic development, my development is problematic, my concentration on growth, my alternations in old age, my changing losses, soul maturity unfold, and growing soul pains

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Self-sacrifice. Attainment of one's objectives through sacrifices. A toilsome struggle of existence, a death or bereavement.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A fear of power struggles with authority figures. Frustration concerning the difficulties one encounters when trying to align oneself with one’s spiritual path. The ability to effect changes in the world through being cautious and focused.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `PLUTO/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `352`

#### Munkasey Entry

Early frustrations which help you work hard to achieve your desires; increased desire to work hard to add to your recognition in life; questioning the dedication of others helping you achieve or gain your ends.
