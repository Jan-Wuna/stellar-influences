## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 69 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

To concentrate on the condition and development of the own body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 82; PDF page 40 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Development of my husband, body and soul undergoing change, my development as a man, personal changes, my bodily development, personal inner transformations, unfolding of the soul in the body, inner development of a man.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: Printed page 298; PDF page 300 (one-based; coded activator entry checked).

#### Ebertin Entry

Desire to obtain power and authority. Ability to stand on one's own feet, the power of one's personality in attaining success in life.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 117 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

To realize the importance of pursuing one’s spiritual path at all costs. One’s ego may be caught up in an obsessive desire to achieve worldly success. One’s goal and purpose is to transform the world.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `PLUTO/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `352`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased determination to become a better person thru the accumulation power, wealth, or fame; seeking leadership and notoriety as a means of power; tremendous pride in your accomplishments; a rise from obscurity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal development directed toward bodily condition, independence, purpose, or worldly standing.
- psychology: Determination, pride, ambition, and possible obsession with success.
- body/health: Witte emphasizes the condition and development of one's own body; ABC joins bodily and inner change without specifying a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Power, authority, leadership, fame, or notoriety are source settings.
- events/manifestations: Standing on one's own feet, rise from obscurity, and personal or bodily change.
- conflicts/notes: A spiritual purpose and a search for worldly success are distinct source possibilities.

## Contradictions

Attention to the body and ambitions for status are different emphases; no necessary causal link between them is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Pluto/MC through development centered on the person and the purpose pursued. Attention to one's own body in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and joined bodily and inner change in [ABC](#abc-entry) sit beside independence, personality, and authority in [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry). [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) preserves a tension between a spiritual path and obsessive worldly success; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds pride, power, wealth, fame, and a rise from obscurity. These are distinct expressions of personal striving, without a promised rise or a medical conclusion.
