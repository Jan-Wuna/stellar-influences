## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `MC + Pluto`
- Source page: `69`
#### Witte Entry

One’s development is uneven, it leaps. Sudden changes of the mind. The revolutionary moment of change. To create a surprising change of mood or attitude.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Pluto/Medium Coeli`
- Source page: `298`
- Entry: `1078`

#### Ebertin Entry

Restless, indefatigable, enduring, prudent, organizing ability. Sudden attainment of one's aim or aspiration with irresistible power, sudden occupational changes.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Pluto/Midheaven midpoint`
- Source page: `117`
- Activator: `Uranus`

#### Sandbach Entry

An unexpected and maybe surprising opening up to spiritual realities. The freeing up of circumstances that leads to a transformation in one’s career. Sudden episodes of extreme insecurity, or opening up to finding much deeper security. Unique and/or strange experiences that completely alter the basis of one’s security and maybe change how one views the world.
