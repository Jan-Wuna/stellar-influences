## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 69 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

One’s development depends on the conditions of the general world.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 82; PDF page 40 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): I change the public, my development in public, my changes in public life, my move into public life, unfolding of the soul in public, inner development in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal development in relation to public life and general world conditions.
- psychology: No specific emotional or personality profile is supplied.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: The public is the explicit setting.
- events/manifestations: Changes in public life, a move into public life, and the person's participation in changing the public.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's dependence on world conditions and ABC's active public change are distinct directions of influence.

## Contradictions

The person may be affected by general conditions or act within public change; the entries do not prescribe a necessary reorientation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Pluto/MC through the relationship between personal development and public conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes one's development dependent on the general world; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also includes changing the public, moving into public life, and developing inwardly in public life. Influence can thus run from the larger setting toward the person or from personal development into public activity, without a specified outcome.
