## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF page 115 (one-based; exact activator entry checked in raw PDF text).

#### Sandbach Entry

One helps others to get go of the past and to embrace the future, often in ways and to an extent that they never thought they could. The ability to spontaneously evoke in others memories of the past that need to be released.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Helping others release memories or attachments to the past and embrace a future.
- psychology: Memories may arise spontaneously as material to be released.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: The explicit role is helping other people.
- events/manifestations: A changed relationship to the past and greater openness to future possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: The single entry supplies no clinical healing claim or required crisis process.

## Contradictions

Only one source entry is present. It does not establish that every connection triggers a purge or crisis.

## Derived Synthesis

Chiron specifies Pluto/Node in the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) through helping others release the past and become open to a future they had not thought possible. Memories that need release may arise spontaneously in this process. The entry defines an interpersonal change in relation to past and future, without a necessary crisis, a medical healing claim, or a rule that every bond must serve this purpose.
