## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 148 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Changes in the family, of the members of the family, in number. Social changes. Old connections are taken up again.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 223; PDF page 110 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Developing relationships with a community, changing shared relationships, shared development within a connection, changed community relationships, changes in relationships within a community, growing ties within a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Changes or renewals of family and community connections.
- psychology: No specific individual inner state is described.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Family membership, community relationships, and renewed old connections.
- events/manifestations: Changes in family numbers, social change, and growing community ties.
- conflicts/notes: The entries include renewal of old ties as well as changing existing relationships.

## Contradictions

Old connections being resumed and community relations changing are compatible; the formula does not require wholly new ties.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Pluto/Node through connections that change within a family or community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names changes in family membership or numbers, social changes, and old connections taken up again. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds developing relations with a community, shared development, and growing community ties. Renewal is therefore as explicit as change, and the entries do not restrict this orientation to new associations.
