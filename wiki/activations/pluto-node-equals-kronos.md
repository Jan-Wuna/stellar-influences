## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 148 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Connections in which one is the student, the beginner.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 223; PDF page 110 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Development of an important connection, changes in connections with the state, a growing relationship to independence, great changes in a connection, developing connections with authorities, transformations in a connection with authorities.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections involving learning, beginnings, authority, or independence.
- psychology: No detailed inner state is supplied.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: A student or beginner within a connection; links to authority and the state.
- events/manifestations: Important connections, major changes in ties, and development toward independence.
- conflicts/notes: Being a beginner is an explicit Witte expression, not tested mastery.

## Contradictions

Witte's student position and ABC's authority contacts have different roles; a connection to authority does not mean holding it.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Pluto/Node through a connection in which position or development matters. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) specifically places the person as student or beginner; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds important ties, links with the state or authorities, and a growing relationship to independence. This preserves a learning position alongside contact with authority without turning the formula into a claim of tested mastery or higher rank.
