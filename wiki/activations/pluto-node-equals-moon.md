- Source heading: `Planets activating the Pluto/Node midpoint`
- Source page: `114`
- Activator: `Moon`

#### Sandbach Entry

To feel vulnerable to the power of other people. To feel the past as being on the one hand oppressive and yet to be extremely drawn to remaining connected to it. The need to be very careful about who one forms connections with, and to try to seek out others who will truly meet one’s needs to be nurtured in a way that encourages one’s personal growth rather than the development of dependency.
