## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Connections of a very passing nature. Development of sensitivity in a relationship.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development of precision in a compound, contact development in the air, slipping changes in a compound, change in development in a compound, change in a relation by V c cn, and see above

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Attainment of success within a community by means of lies and fraud. Disadvantages and losses.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To have vivid sometimes overwhelming imaginings about one’s past, and po tentially about the traumatic situations one has been through. One is intuitively aware of pressures and intensities going on between themselves and those with whom they connect. To feel confused about the power struggles that go on between you and other people. The possibility of obsessively idealizing the past. Your intuition can guide you toward making far-reaching changes that will help you to grow.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Increases the potential for you to be deceived by others, or for you to deceive them with your intentions; seeing that people are not always what they seem to be; added doubts raised about the effectiveness of friends.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Pluto/Node = Neptune`: psychic or karmic-feeling ties, subtle group influence, and the ability to encounter major change through hidden relational processes. Main caution: covert entanglement: associations can become deceptive, draining, or too saturated with fantasy and manipulation.
