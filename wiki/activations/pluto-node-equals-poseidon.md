## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 148 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

New connections which are of importance for the mental and spiritual development.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed p. 223 (PDF p. 110).

#### ABC Entry

Development of cultural connections, development of spiritual connections, change of spiritual connections, development of sensible relationships, growing knowledge in one connection, growing connection to truth, and clear development of a connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections develop through knowledge, culture and spiritual understanding.
- psychology: Growing recognition and a connection to truth.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Cultural and spiritual relationships change or develop.
- events/manifestations: New contacts matter for mental or spiritual development.
- conflicts/notes: The German scan supports sensible relationships, rather than the previous translation's future relationships.

## Contradictions

The entries share a developmental theme, but repeated wording is not proof of independent confirmation. Neither entry establishes a specific future relationship.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Node = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies changing connections through mental and spiritual development: a relationship becomes a setting for greater knowledge, cultural contact or a clearer relationship to truth. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes the importance of new connections explicit, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the theme to cultural and spiritual ties and the development of sensible relationships.
