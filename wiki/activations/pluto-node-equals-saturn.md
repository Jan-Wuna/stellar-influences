## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

New connections by separating from the old ones. New connections which become effective in old age, but not at present. Change of connections at a later age.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Task interaction in a connection, stressful development of a relationship, developing patience in a connection, lossful change in a connection, growth by separation of a relationship, and changes in an emergency connection

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Lack of adaptability, rebelliousness or truculence, revolt, fighting against oppression. Violent termination of relationships.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A fear of powerful connections with other people, and a tendency to feel oppressed or restricted by them. The feeling that there is no way to avoid the changes that need to be made in one’s life, and the tendency to feel great stress and pressure until those change are under way.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Lessens desires to use and discard others; allows you realize that personal trust must be built up in people over time and that relationships with others are not to be rushed or used to extremes; helps you read people.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Pluto/Node = Saturn`: serious loyalty, enduring but transformative bonds, and the ability to withstand difficult group conditions. Main caution: oppressive connection: ties can become isolating, punitive, or too fused with fear, burden, and control.
