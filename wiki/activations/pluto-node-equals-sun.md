## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 148 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Much change in the personal unions and connections. Ties in connection with foods. To get new connections while buying and selling food.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed p. 222 (PDF p. 110).

#### ABC Entry

Development of personal relations, developing contacts with a man, personal development through links, changes in personal relations, growing personal ties, and changing relationships in one centre

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: Printed p. 293, entry 1047.

#### Ebertin Entry

The urge to ruthlessly impose one's will upon others, and vice versa.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: Chapter starts at PDF p. 114 (one-based); exact activation page is not established.

#### Sandbach Entry

One feels it is meaningful and important to connect with people who have much power, and/or to have intense connections with others. The need to not allow the past to get in the way of one’s fulfilling one’s goals.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `PLUTO/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `344`

#### Munkasey Entry

Evaluating and making use of the people you meet or encounter thru your activities; the importance you attach to such meetings or encounters; the confidence you feel from others during meetings; respect you thus gain.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal connections become important to development, goals and influence.
- psychology: Confidence, respect and the will to influence others.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified; food contacts are an economic/social motif.
- social/relationship: Important encounters may involve power, mutual influence or coercion.
- events/manifestations: Changing personal unions and contacts through buying or selling food.
- conflicts/notes: Ebertin's coercion is stronger than the other entries' accounts of useful encounters.

## Contradictions

Useful meetings and personal development do not remove Ebertin's explicit possibility of ruthless imposition of will. Witte's food-trade detail is a separate concrete manifestation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Node = Sun`, Sun specifies developing connections as a personal matter: encounters can shape goals, confidence and the respect one receives. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) emphasizes the importance attached to powerful or intense contacts, and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 344, describes evaluating and making use of meetings. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) gives the sharper possibility of will imposed in either direction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) also preserves the unusual, concrete motif of contacts made through food buying and selling.
