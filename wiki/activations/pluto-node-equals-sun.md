## Identity

- Formula: `Pluto/Node = Sun`
- Astronomicon formula: `Z/g = S`

## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Much change in the personal unions and connections. Ties in connection with foods. To get new connections while buying and selling food.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development of personal relations, man made contacts, personal development through links, changes in personal relations, growing personal ties, and changing relationships in one centre

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

The urge to ruthlessly impose one's will upon others, and vice versa.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One feels it is meaningful and important to connect with people who have much power, and/or to have intense connections with others. The need to not allow the past to get in the way of one’s fulfilling one’s goals.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Evaluating and making use of the people you meet or encounter thru your activities; the importance you attach to such meetings or encounters; the confidence you feel from others during meetings; respect you thus gain.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Pluto/Node = Sun`: commanding alliance, socially amplified power, and the ability to define one's path through major encounters with others. Main caution: ego invested in influence: identity can become too dependent on control, status within the group, or intense relational power.
