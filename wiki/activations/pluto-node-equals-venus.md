## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

New, pleasant connections with women, or representatives of theatres or art. Harmonious development of these connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development of a love relationship, harmonious development of a connection, love relationship change, changes in a artistic connection, preference for contactY changes, and growing peace mediation

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

An unusual power of attraction to other people, a tragic love union.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A love of connecting intensely with other people. To have a love relationship with someone who tries to dominate you, or whom you try to dominate.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Influential artists or bankers you meet thru contacts or groups; accumulating friendships for wealth or power potentials; added intimacy felt from people meet; intimate questions about the role of love in attraction or sex.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Pluto/Node = Venus`: magnetic companionship, regenerative love bonds, and the ability to create strong social loyalty through warmth and value. Main caution: possessive attachment: affection can become manipulative, consuming, or too entangled with power and control.
