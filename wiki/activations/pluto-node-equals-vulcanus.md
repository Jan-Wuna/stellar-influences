## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 148 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

New connections of great importance for the native.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed p. 223 (PDF p. 110).

#### ABC Entry

Developing energies in a relationship, developing a strong relationship, developing power in a relationship, powerful change in the connection, a strong reciprocal relationship, growing violence in a connection, and changing influence of a connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections grow in strength, power or importance.
- psychology: Power and influence within a relationship are emphasized.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: A strong tie may also carry coercion or violence.
- events/manifestations: New important connections and changes in relational influence.
- conflicts/notes: Importance and strength do not themselves establish a beneficial relationship.

## Contradictions

Witte states importance without a favorable or unfavorable outcome. ABC also includes violence; a powerful bond is not necessarily a helpful one.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Node = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies developing connections through strength, power and influence. A new tie can become highly important to the person, as the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes developing relational power and growing violence, so the strength of a connection must be distinguished from its quality.
