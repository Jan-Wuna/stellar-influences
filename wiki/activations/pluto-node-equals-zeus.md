## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 148 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To conclude new connections for the increase of one’s accomplishment. One’s increased accomplishments bring new connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed p. 223 (PDF p. 110).

#### ABC Entry

Developing efforts in a connection, beginning of the development of contacts, development of a procreative connection, alterations in a creative connection, growing achievements in connections, and changes in a relationship through goal striving

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Contacts and accomplishment develop through one another.
- psychology: Effort and striving toward a goal shape the relationship.
- body/health: ABC names a procreative connection without specifying a bodily condition or outcome.
- social/relationship: Connections can support creative or goal-directed activity.
- events/manifestations: New contacts increase accomplishment; accomplishment brings new contacts.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes procreation as well as creative work; neither establishes an obligatory bond.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in effort and relational development. ABC's procreative connection is a distinct manifestation alongside creative effort.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Node = Zeus`, Zeus specifies developing connections through effort, creativity and pursuit of a goal. The relationship is reciprocal in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry): new contacts increase accomplishment, while increased accomplishment brings new contacts. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds growing effort and achievements within connections, with procreation retained as a distinct relational manifestation. The common thread is a connection that develops through purposeful activity.
