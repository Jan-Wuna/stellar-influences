## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Pluto = Admetos`, Admetos specifies transformation at its foundations: change reaches the roots or returns to the basic origin. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links this depth with a process from raw material to finished product and from beginning to end. The organizing theme is thorough restructuring, with the beginning of an ending retained as one expression rather than a universal outcome.
