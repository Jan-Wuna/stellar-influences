## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 256 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

From a small staff to great importance. Occurrences with great consequences. Business enlargement, extension. Development of a science. Far reaching and expanded transformations. To grow or extend in all directions. Repetitions. To start small and to grow big. A large change. Events taking place for the first time with great consequences. Starting a commerce or trade on a small scale and developing it into a large scale.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Change expands from a small beginning to wide consequences.
- psychology: No separate inner disposition is specified.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: A small staff or enterprise can gain importance.
- events/manifestations: Business expansion, scientific development, repetition and far-reaching events.
- conflicts/notes: Expansion and consequences are described without guaranteeing benefit.

## Contradictions

The entry combines scale, repetition and consequence; it does not say that growth is invariably fortunate.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Pluto = Apollon`, Apollon specifies transformation through expansion: a small beginning develops into something larger and more consequential. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects business growth and scientific development with repetition and change spreading in many directions. What unifies these examples is increasing scale and reach, rather than a guaranteed favorable result.
