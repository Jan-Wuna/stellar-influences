## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 255 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Other people are changeable. Changes in relation to others. Changes at the place. Moving from residence. Changes in connections with different people. To be transient in a place. Changes of the immediate environment.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Change concerns other people and the immediate environment.
- psychology: Other people are described as changeable; no fixed personal trait follows.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Relations with different people change.
- events/manifestations: Moving residence or being transient in a place.
- conflicts/notes: The entry names surroundings and relationships, not a promised personal reinvention.

## Contradictions

The social and residential examples share changing surroundings; neither requires the other to occur.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Pluto = Asc`, Asc specifies transformation in relations with other people and in one's immediate surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) moves between changeable people, altered contacts, moving residence and temporary residence. These are linked by a changing social or local environment; the entry does not make every altered relationship a relocation.
