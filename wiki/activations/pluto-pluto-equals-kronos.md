## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Pluto = Kronos`, Kronos specifies transformation through developing and proving ability. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) traces apprenticeship, examination and certification toward mastery or higher rank, and also includes a change of boss or form of government. The common thread is a changed level of competence or authority; the historical social labels do not guarantee wealth or promotion.
