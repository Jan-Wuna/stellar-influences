## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 255 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Organizing. Planning. Plans. Duplicate, to copy, to repeat, to multiply. To lead, to supervise or to perform several activities at the same time. To divide, to split, to branch off. To work alternately at different tasks. To do different kinds of work. To work on schedule. Transformation and change in the activity in ones work and in the working conditions. Actively bring about change.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Transformation is actively organized and carried out.
- psychology: Planning and managing several tasks.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Leading or supervising work is included.
- events/manifestations: Copying, multiplying, dividing and changing working conditions.
- conflicts/notes: The row concerns work activity; it does not establish conflict or violence.

## Contradictions

The multiple-task and repetition motifs describe ways of organizing change, not conflicting outcomes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Pluto = Mars`, Mars specifies transformation as something actively brought about through work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins planning and supervision with copying, repetition, division and alternating between tasks. The practical unity is the organization of activity and working conditions to produce change, rather than change merely happening to the person.
