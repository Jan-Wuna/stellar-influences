## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 255 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

One's own intellectual capacity, as well as the ability and possibility of development. One’s capacity to apprehend. One’s own transformation and development. Inner transformation. The developmental stages of the own mind. Metamorphosis. To appear to be a completely different person. Moment (minute) of change.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal and inner development.
- psychology: Intellectual capacity, apprehension and stages of mental development.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: A person may appear markedly different to others.
- events/manifestations: Inner transformation and a moment of change.
- conflicts/notes: The reference to a minute of change supplies no timing method.

## Contradictions

One account links capacity and inner development; it gives no test for measuring either.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Pluto = MC`, MC specifies transformation as one's own inner development. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) relates intellectual capacity and apprehension to changing stages of mind, so that the person may appear markedly different. Its moment-of-change phrase describes the occurrence of change without supplying a timing technique.
