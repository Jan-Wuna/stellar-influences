## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 256 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Secrecies. Changes which are not noticed externally. Sneaking, unnoticeable transformation. Changes only recognized in the future. Developments below the radar screen. Sneaking crisis. Hidden alteration. An uncertain turnaround.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Change proceeds unnoticed or remains uncertain.
- psychology: Awareness may lag behind the change.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Secrecy can conceal what is changing; no specific bond is named.
- events/manifestations: Hidden alteration, a developing crisis or an uncertain turnaround.
- conflicts/notes: Later recognition does not establish a future prediction.

## Contradictions

The examples share limited visibility; the presence of hidden change does not mean every instance is a crisis.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Pluto = Neptune`, Neptune specifies transformation that is difficult to observe while it happens. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects secrecy and unnoticed alteration with changes recognized only later. A concealed crisis is one possible expression, while an uncertain turnaround preserves the more general limit: neither the course nor its outcome is yet clear.
