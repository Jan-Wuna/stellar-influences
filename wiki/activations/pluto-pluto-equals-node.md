## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 255 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

New connections. Changes through connections. Acquaintances who experience changes. Relationships that can change events or who expect changes. Development or transformation in relationships. Renewing a relationship.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Transformation develops in and through connections.
- psychology: Expectations of change can be shared with acquaintances.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: New, renewed or changing relationships.
- events/manifestations: Contacts experience change or participate in changing events.
- conflicts/notes: This repeated-pair orientation stays distinct from Pluto/Node activations.

## Contradictions

The entry spans changes to contacts and changes through contacts; these need not occur together.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Pluto = Node`, Node specifies transformation through connections: a relationship may be renewed, a new acquaintance may arrive, or people connected with one another may undergo change. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) also allows relationships to participate in changing events. This ties transformation to the relational setting without assigning the meaning of any differently oriented Pluto/Node formula.
