## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 256 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Hindered or slow development. Difficult to apply oneself. Differentiation. Slow, gradual separation or divorce. Hindrance and slowing up. Interruption of the development. Transformation through separation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development slows, is obstructed or changes through separation.
- psychology: Difficulty applying oneself is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Gradual separation or divorce is one manifestation.
- events/manifestations: Interruption, differentiation and a slower course of change.
- conflicts/notes: Separation is a possibility, not a necessary outcome.

## Contradictions

The entry links hindrance with differentiation; it does not say that delay always ends a relationship.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Pluto = Saturn`, Saturn specifies transformation through slowing, obstruction or separation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes difficulty applying oneself and interrupted development, while gradual separation or divorce gives the relational form of that process. What unifies these examples is development constrained or redirected by a limit.
