## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 256 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Mutations. Jumps. To leap. Explosions. Changes and transformations which start suddenly. The development starts with a shock. Inventions or changes in the field of technology. Surprising, unexpected turnaround or development.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Transformation begins suddenly or takes an unexpected turn.
- psychology: Shock and surprise mark the start of development.
- body/health: Mutations are named without a specific bodily claim.
- social/relationship: No specific relationship pattern is described.
- events/manifestations: Leaps, explosions, inventions and technological change.
- conflicts/notes: The examples describe sudden change without fixing its value.

## Contradictions

Shock, invention and explosion share suddenness; they are not interchangeable predictions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Pluto = Uranus`, Uranus specifies transformation by a sudden start, leap or unexpected reversal. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ranges from shock and explosion to invention and technological change. Their common feature is discontinuity in the course of development, rather than a requirement that every change be destructive.
