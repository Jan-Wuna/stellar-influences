## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 256 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Mighty changes or transformations that occurs with great speed. Unhealthy, rushed development. The changes (growth) inside the greenhouse. Quick transformation. Mighty change in circumstances. Transformations and developments which are too rapid, too swift in their course.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Transformation is forceful and excessively rapid.
- psychology: No distinct inner disposition is specified.
- body/health: Unhealthy rushed growth is the source's description, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: No specific relationship pattern is described.
- events/manifestations: Major shifts in circumstances, quick transformation and greenhouse growth.
- conflicts/notes: Speed is central; force does not imply that growth is beneficial.

## Contradictions

The entry explicitly qualifies forceful development as potentially too rapid rather than simply successful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Pluto = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies transformation through power and great speed. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes major changes in circumstances but also development that is rushed or too swift. Greenhouse growth is its concrete image of rapid development; the scale of the change should therefore be distinguished from its soundness.
