- Source locator: PDF p. 256 (one-based; entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To start something new. Attending to duty. Increase of duties. Increase of efforts. Transformation in the immediate vicinity. Duty bound. Increase in accomplishment. Increase of creative power. Development of creative power. To begin something new.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: New beginnings develop through effort, duty and creative power.
- psychology: Attention to duty and increased effort.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Obligations are named without a specific relationship.

## Contradictions

Increased duties and creative power are complementary emphases; the entry gives no necessary sequence between them.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Pluto = Zeus`, Zeus specifies transformation through beginning something and developing the effort or creative power to carry it forward. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places increased accomplishment beside increased duties and obligations. The theme is productive change taking shape through activity, with the burden of duty retained alongside creative growth.
