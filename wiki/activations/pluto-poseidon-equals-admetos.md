## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

To experience a mental and emotional transformation in a small circle.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 423; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Restricted mental/spiritual development, consolidation of a clear development, developing spiritual depth, thorough unfolding of truth, thorough change of ideas, developing deep insights, growing spiritual deepening, growing clarity through perseverance.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental or emotional transformation within a small circle, with restricted yet deepening development.
- psychology: Depth, insight, and clarity through perseverance.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: A small circle is the explicit setting.
- events/manifestations: Consolidation, thorough changes of ideas, and unfolding truth.
- conflicts/notes: Restriction and deepening coexist; neither guarantees a favorable outcome.

## Contradictions

A small social circle and inward depth are compatible settings. Restriction need not prevent all development.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Pluto/Poseidon as a concentrated transformation: the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places mental and emotional change within a small circle, and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes restricted development that can also become deeper and more firmly established. Thorough changes of ideas, deep insights, and clarity through perseverance preserve a range beyond blockage alone. The entries do not say that every restriction produces insight.
