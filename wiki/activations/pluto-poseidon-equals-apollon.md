## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

To experience a transformation with many other people which is mental and emotional.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 423; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of cultural changes, successful spiritual development, developing spiritual knowledge, development of cultural science, evolutionary knowledge of science, growing spiritual experiences, and increasing truth unfolding

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental and emotional transformation shared with many people.
- psychology: Growing mental/spiritual knowledge and experience.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Many people participate in the transformation; culture and science are additional settings.
- events/manifestations: Expansion of cultural change and development of knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: Source language about success and truth does not independently verify a belief.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes the number of participants; ABC emphasizes expansion and knowledge. No incompatible claim is explicit.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Pluto/Poseidon through mental and emotional transformation experienced with many other people in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens that shared development through expanding cultural change, knowledge, science, and growing spiritual experience. Its language of success and unfolding truth describes the source's interpretation without proving that a shared worldview is correct.
