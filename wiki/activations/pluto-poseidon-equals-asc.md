## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

The people around the native change and develop mentally.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 422; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Insightful development of a partner, development of a cultural place, mental/spiritual development with a partner, a place of mental/spiritual transformation, a place for the development of truth, change of a cultural place, growing clarity in the surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental development in surrounding people, partnership, or a cultural place.
- psychology: Growing insight and clarity in the environment.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Development with a partner and changes among nearby people.
- events/manifestations: Development or transformation of a cultural place.
- conflicts/notes: A place for truth-development is source vocabulary, not verification of its claims.

## Contradictions

Witte's surrounding people and ABC's partner or cultural place are distinct local settings for development.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Pluto/Poseidon through mental development in one's surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) locates change in the people nearby; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds development with a partner and changes to a cultural place. Insight and growing clarity connect the personal surroundings with a setting in which ideas or cultural understanding develop, without establishing that every local change is an improvement.
