## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Reason an good judgement lead to a very fortunate change or transformation in the spiritual attitude as well as in the attitude towards the world.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 422; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Development of law and truth, successful mental/spiritual development, successful unfolding of truth, joy in rational developments, developing legal culture, beneficial cultural developments, growing clarity in finance.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Favorable development of understanding, judgment, and outlook.
- psychology: Reason, good judgment, and joy in rational developments.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Legal and cultural development are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: Beneficial cultural changes and growing clarity in finance.
- conflicts/notes: Success and truth remain the source's characterizations, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Contradictions

Witte's favorable change and ABC's successful development overlap; legal and financial examples add settings.

## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Pluto/Poseidon through an outlook changing favorably through reason and good judgment in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends this constructive emphasis to intellectual development, law, cultural development, and clarity in finance. Joy in rational developments belongs to this pattern of understanding becoming more useful or coherent, without guaranteeing a legal, financial, or spiritual success.
