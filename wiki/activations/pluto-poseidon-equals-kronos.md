## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

An independent attitude in mental and emotional matters. An acknowledged leader in terms of a concept or the meaning of life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 423; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Special mental/spiritual development, development of the spiritual hierarchy, development of mental/spiritual independence, development of important insights, unfolding of spiritual authority, growing reputation through clarity, important changes through truth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independent understanding and recognized mental or spiritual leadership.
- psychology: Important insights, independence, and clarity.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Spiritual authority, hierarchy, and reputation are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: Development of authority or important change through truth.
- conflicts/notes: Recognized authority does not prove a concept true or superior.

## Contradictions

Independence and hierarchy are different source expressions, not a requirement that every independent thinker holds office.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Pluto/Poseidon through independence and authority in matters of understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes an independent mental or emotional attitude and an acknowledged leader in a concept or meaning of life. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds important insights, spiritual independence, hierarchy, and reputation through clarity. These expressions connect a developed outlook with a recognized position without validating the outlook itself.
