## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

To bring order into mental matters. To follow a plan in the mental development.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 422; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Developing work culture, mental/spiritual development work, creating clarity through change, cultural activity undergoing change, developing cultural activity, rational development work, growing mental/spiritual activity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Planned work that orders and develops mental or cultural activity.
- psychology: Mental order, planning, and increasing mental activity.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Work culture and cultural activity are the stated collective settings.
- events/manifestations: Development work, changing cultural activity, and clarity through change.
- conflicts/notes: The entries describe directed work without guaranteeing its success.

## Contradictions

Planning in Witte and work or cultural activity in ABC are compatible emphases.

## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Pluto/Poseidon through active, organized development. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) brings order and a plan to mental matters; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places that work in mental activity, work culture, and changing cultural practice. Clarity through change and rational development work connect the plan with its execution, while the entries leave the result open.
