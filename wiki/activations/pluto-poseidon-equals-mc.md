## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

The ability to mentally change and develop. To fit oneself mentally into a different environment. If the native is mentally or spiritually in a higher state of development, this configuration takes him higher and higher. In a state of low development it brings inconsistency in his belief. Fickleness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 422; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Unfolding of the spiritual soul, insight-rich development of the soul, inner mental/spiritual transformation, my development toward clarity, my mental/spiritual development, my cultural transformation, my growing reason, growing inner clarity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal mental or spiritual transformation and adaptation.
- psychology: Inner development, reason, and clarity; Witte conditionally includes inconsistent belief and fickleness.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Mental adaptation to a different environment is explicit.
- events/manifestations: Personal cultural transformation and development of understanding.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's higher/lower-development distinction is an attributed hierarchy, not an objective ranking of a person.

## Contradictions

Witte qualifies development: growth under its higher-development condition, inconsistent belief under its lower-development condition. ABC's constructive inner-development wording does not cancel that qualification.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Pluto/Poseidon as personal transformation in understanding and outlook. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes the ability to change mentally and adapt to a different environment; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds inner transformation, insight, reason, and increasing clarity. Witte makes its favorable reading conditional: what it calls a higher state develops further, whereas its lower state brings inconsistent belief and fickleness. That historical hierarchy remains attributed to the entry rather than a judgment to impose on the person.
