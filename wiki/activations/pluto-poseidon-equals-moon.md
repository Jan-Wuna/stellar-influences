## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

The emotional and metal (psychic) contact with women. The mental influence of female persons. Political, spiritual or worldly transformation of the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 422; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Unfolding of truth among a people, mental/spiritual development of a woman, change of ideas among a people, developing clear feelings, cultural developments of a people, development of reason in a woman, growing insight of a woman, growing clarity of feeling.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Changes in public outlook, feeling, and mental or spiritual influence.
- psychology: Clearer feeling, growing insight, and reason are ABC themes.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: The public, a people, and women are the source's specific settings.
- events/manifestations: Political, spiritual, cultural, or worldly public transformation.
- conflicts/notes: Witte genuinely prints 'metal (psychic)' on PDF p. 264 (printed p. 268). The intended word is uncertain; no metallic meaning is inferred. Gendered descriptions and psychic contact remain attributed source language.

## Contradictions

Public transformation and the source's female examples are distinct expressions; neither establishes a universal quality of women.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Pluto/Poseidon through changes of feeling and understanding in a public or interpersonal setting. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes women's mental influence and political, spiritual, or worldly transformation of the public. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds changing ideas and cultural development among a people, together with clear feelings, insight, and reason in its female examples. Those examples preserve the source's range without making a general claim about women or verifying its psychic vocabulary.
