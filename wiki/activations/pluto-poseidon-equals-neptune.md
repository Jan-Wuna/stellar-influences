## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

The slow unnoticeable beginning of a highly mental transformation or development. Creations of the imagination.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 423; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

disappointing cultural development, developing sensitivity for truth, spiritual development through sensitivity, clear future development, changes through metaphysical truths, and growing clarity through refinement

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Subtle beginnings of mental transformation, imagination, and sensitive development.
- psychology: Imagination, sensitivity to truth, and refinement.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Cultural development is named, including disappointment.
- events/manifestations: Slow, initially unnoticed development and changes through metaphysical beliefs described as truths by the source.
- conflicts/notes: Metaphysical truth is the source's term; refinement and disappointment are both included.

## Contradictions

ABC includes disappointing culture as well as growing clarity; these are alternative expressions, not proof of inevitable improvement.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Pluto/Poseidon through a transformation that begins slowly and may initially pass unnoticed. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links this subtle development with creations of imagination; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds sensitivity and refinement as routes toward greater clarity. Disappointing cultural development remains part of the range. Its references to metaphysical truths describe the source's spiritual vocabulary without independently establishing those beliefs.
