## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

The change of mind or heart of others forces the native to change his own mind or heart, and vice versa. Mental development is influenced by connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 422; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Developing insight in a connection, developing mental/spiritual connections, change of ideas in a connection, clear development in a connection, cultural changes through a connection, growing clarity in a connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental development and changes of outlook through connections.
- psychology: Reciprocal influence on mind, heart, insight, and clarity.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: A connection or relationship is the explicit setting.
- events/manifestations: Changing ideas and cultural changes through a connection.
- conflicts/notes: Influence runs in both directions in Witte; it is not necessarily voluntary.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes mutual pressure to change; ABC emphasizes development within a connection. These emphases can coexist.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Pluto/Poseidon through connections that alter understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a reciprocal process: another person's change of mind or heart presses one to change, and one's own change affects the other. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds insight, changing ideas, cultural change, and growing clarity within a connection. The connection is the setting of development; neither entry guarantees agreement or equal freedom to change.
