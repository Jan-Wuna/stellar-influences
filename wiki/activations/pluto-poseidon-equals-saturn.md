## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Termination of an education. To interrupt the mental growth. Not able to keep up the pace in mental development with others. To be separated because of differing convictions. To be left behind, which causes isolation and resentment in the attitude toward the world.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 423; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Developing insight and patience, disturbed mental/spiritual development, clear development in old age, concentration on mental/spiritual development, slow growth of an idea, painful unfolding of truth, growing clarity through patience, growing cultural tasks.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Interrupted or slow mental development, with patience and concentration as additional expressions.
- psychology: Isolation, resentment, differing convictions, and clarity through patience.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Separation over beliefs or falling behind others in development.
- events/manifestations: Ending an education, interrupted growth, slow development of an idea, and cultural tasks.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes disturbed development as well as clearer development in old age; neither removes Witte's setbacks.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes interruption and exclusion; ABC also permits gradual clarity through patience. The constructive possibility does not guarantee recovery from a setback.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Pluto/Poseidon through limits on mental development. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes interrupted education or growth, separation over convictions, and being left behind with isolation and resentment. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also allows a slower course in which concentration and patience accompany an idea's growth or clearer development in old age. Painful unfolding of truth and disturbed development remain alongside that possibility, so patience is not presented as an assured remedy.
