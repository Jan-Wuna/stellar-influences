## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Days very favorable for mental development. The man who has an open mind and inclines to reform his life, who will influence his body through his mind, who declines poisonous drugs.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 422; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Unfolding of a cultural centre, development of a mental/spiritual centre, mental/spiritual development of a person, developing personal ideas, development of reason in a man, a growing spiritual centre, growing personal clarity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal mental or spiritual development and reform of one's life.
- psychology: An open mind, personal ideas, reason, and growing clarity.
- body/health: Witte attributes mind-to-body influence and refusal of poisonous drugs to its example; this supplies no medical mechanism or treatment advice.
- social/relationship: Cultural or spiritual centres are ABC settings.
- events/manifestations: Development of personal ideas and cultural or spiritual centres.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's favorable days and gendered man example remain source wording; no timing layer or health prediction is established.

## Contradictions

Personal and cultural-centre development are different settings. Witte's bodily and drug language is a historical qualification rather than a medical claim to adopt.

## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Pluto/Poseidon through personal development that can change how life is approached. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes an open mind inclined to reform life, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal ideas, reason, clarity, and the development of cultural or spiritual centres. Witte also associates its example with influencing the body through the mind and declining poisonous drugs; these remain attributed historical statements and supply no medical recommendation or promised bodily result.
