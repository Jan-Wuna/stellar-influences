## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Surprising, suddenly gained understanding asks for a push in the mental and emotional attitude. A theoretical development with revolutionary results.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 423; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Disturbing unfolding of ideas, development of spiritual dynamism, mental/spiritual development impulses, clear development of reform, developing new ideas, sudden clarity through changes, surprising unfolding of truth, growing mental/spiritual reform.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden understanding and new ideas that change mental or emotional attitudes.
- psychology: Surprise, disturbance, spiritual impulses, and sudden clarity.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Theoretical developments may have revolutionary results; no specific relationship is delineated.
- events/manifestations: New ideas, reform, and surprising unfolding of truth.
- conflicts/notes: Unexpected clarity is a source characterization, not proof that a new idea is true.

## Contradictions

Disturbing ideas and clarifying reform are distinct possibilities; the sources do not require every surprise to be beneficial.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies Pluto/Poseidon through understanding that arrives suddenly and demands a changed attitude. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects this with theoretical development having revolutionary results; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds new ideas, spiritual impulses, reform, and sudden clarity through change. Disturbance remains alongside discovery, so the novelty or force of an insight does not establish its truth or benefit.
