## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Harmonious transformation and development of the outlook on life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 422; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Joy through mental/spiritual development, developing spiritual harmony, insightful development in love, developing love of reason, spiritual artistic development, changes in art and culture, growing clarity through harmony.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Harmonious change of outlook, with joy, love, art, and culture.
- psychology: Spiritual harmony, reason, insight, and clarity through harmony.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Insightful development in love is explicit.
- events/manifestations: Spiritual artistic development and changes in art or culture.
- conflicts/notes: Harmony does not validate a worldview or guarantee a relationship outcome.

## Contradictions

The entries share harmony; love, reason, and artistic development are additional settings rather than contradictions.

## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies Pluto/Poseidon through a change of outlook experienced as harmonious. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states that organizing theme directly; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects it with joy in spiritual development, insight in love, affection for reason, and changes in art or culture. Clarity through harmony unites these expressions without proving that a pleasing belief is true or that a relationship will succeed.
