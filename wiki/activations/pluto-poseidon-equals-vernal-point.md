## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Changes in general worldview and ideas in public life.
- psychology: The entries describe public ideas and clarity rather than an individual psychological profile.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: The public is the explicit setting.
- events/manifestations: Cultural changes and rational developments in public life.
- conflicts/notes: Growing clarity is the source's characterization, not proof that public belief becomes true.

## Contradictions

Witte's general world concept and ABC's public ideas or culture are compatible levels of description.

## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Pluto/Poseidon through changes in worldview at a general or public level. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names transformation of world concept, and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places changing ideas and culture in public life. Rational development and growing clarity describe the source's constructive range without establishing that every public change improves understanding.
