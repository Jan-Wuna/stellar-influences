## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

A tremendous transformation in the mental or emotional attitude.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 423; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Restricted truth development, influential cultural development, tremendous mental changes, violent change of truth, powerful spiritual development, transformation of spiritual energies, altered cultural influence, and growing spiritual influence

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A tremendous mental or emotional transformation with powerful cultural or spiritual influence.
- psychology: Great changes of attitude and transformation of spiritual energies.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Cultural influence and changing influence are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: Powerful development, restricted unfolding of truth, and violent alteration of truth are source expressions.
- conflicts/notes: Intensity and influence do not establish truth, benefit, or literal violence in every expression.

## Contradictions

The entries agree on magnitude; ABC also includes restriction and violent alteration of truth. These prevent an automatically favorable reading.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Pluto/Poseidon through the magnitude of a mental or emotional transformation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) calls the change tremendous, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects powerful spiritual development with changing cultural and spiritual influence. Its restricted unfolding of truth and violent alteration of truth qualify that force: a powerful change of outlook need not make an idea clearer, truer, or more beneficial.
