## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 264 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

To be in part leading (by inner conviction) in the development of a new outlook in life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 423; PDF page 210 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Beginning mental/spiritual development, development of a creative spirit, unfolding mental/spiritual leadership, developing cultural achievements, growing cultural efforts, growing insight into leadership, growing spiritual goal striving, changing spiritual goals.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Initiating or helping lead the development of a new outlook.
- psychology: Inner conviction, creative spirit, growing insight, and goal striving.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Spiritual leadership and cultural efforts are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Beginning mental/spiritual development, cultural achievements, and changing spiritual goals.
- conflicts/notes: Leadership and conviction do not establish the truth or superiority of the outlook.

## Contradictions

Witte's part in leadership and ABC's changing goals are compatible; neither guarantees a fixed destination.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Pluto/Poseidon through initiating or helping lead the development of a new outlook. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) grounds that role in inner conviction, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds creative spirit, cultural achievement, and growing insight into leadership. Goal striving gives the development direction, but changing spiritual goals remain part of the same entry, so conviction need not imply a fixed or verified destination.
