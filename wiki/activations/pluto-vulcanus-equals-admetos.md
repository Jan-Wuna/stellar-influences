## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 263 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

The breakdown of a mighty development. Transformation or change caused by too great a resistance.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 421; PDF page 209 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Developing perseverance and energy, blockage against the development of violence, blocked change of power, restricted development of power, developing firmness and strength, thorough unfolding of energy, changes through depressive influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful development meeting resistance or breaking down.
- psychology: Perseverance, energy, firmness, and depressive influence are ABC expressions.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Blocked changes of power are named; no intimate relationship is delineated.
- events/manifestations: Restricted power development, resistance to violence, and thorough development of energy.
- conflicts/notes: Perseverance is present beside breakdown; no guaranteed overcoming of resistance is stated.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses breakdown under excessive resistance; ABC adds endurance and firmness as well as blockage. These are different possibilities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Pluto/Vulcanus through powerful development encountering resistance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes breakdown or a transformation caused by resistance becoming too great; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds blocked power shifts and restricted development alongside perseverance, energy, firmness, and strength. This preserves both obstruction and sustained force without promising that endurance will overcome the obstacle.
