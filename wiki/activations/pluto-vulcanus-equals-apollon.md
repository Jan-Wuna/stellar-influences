### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 421; PDF page 209 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of a power development, influential trade developments, successful energy transformation, powerful knowledge development, spread of violence developments, growing power through knowledge, and increasing force development

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A large change beginning to show itself, with expanding power or influence.
- psychology: No specific inner state is supplied.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Trade and knowledge are ABC settings for growing power.
- events/manifestations: Expansion, successful energy transformation, spread of violence, and increasing force.
- conflicts/notes: The spread of violence is one source possibility, not a necessary consequence of expansion.

## Contradictions

Witte describes first indications; ABC describes expansion. The former is a source sequence, not a calculated timing layer.

## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Pluto/Vulcanus through a development whose larger scale becomes apparent. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes first indications of a later great change; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds expanding power, influence in trade, energy transformation, and power through knowledge. Its spread-of-violence example qualifies the constructive language of success, so increasing scale alone does not determine the value of the change.
