## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 263 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Very great changes in family or society.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 421; PDF page 209 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

German passage (checked against the scan):

> Einfluß einer Entwicklungs-Gemeinschaft, Energie-Entwicklung einer Gemeinschaft, gemeinsame Stärke entwickeln, Macht-Wechsel in einer Gemeinschaft, Veränderungen in einer Gemeinschaft durch Gewalt, wachsende Gewalt in der Gemeinschaft

Editorial translation (checked against the scan): Influence of a development community, energy development of a community, developing shared strength, change of power in a community, changes in a community through violence, growing violence in the community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Very great changes affecting family, society, or a community.
- psychology: The entries describe collective change without a specific inner state.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Family, society, and communities are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: Community energy development, shared strength, changes of power, and changes through violence.
- conflicts/notes: Violence and shared strength are distinct source possibilities; no economic-policy interpretation is established.

## Contradictions

Family and social change overlap with ABC's community emphasis; violence does not characterize every collective change.

## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Pluto/Vulcanus through powerful change within a family or collective whole. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names very great changes in family or society; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a community developing energy and shared strength, with changes of power and influence. Changes through violence and growing violence are also explicit possibilities, so collective strength is not automatically harmonious.
