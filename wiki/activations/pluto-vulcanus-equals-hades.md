## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF page 263 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### Witte Entry

Great increase of evil things. The situation is getting worse. Increased dangers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: Printed page 421; PDF page 209 (one-based; exact activator row checked).

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous power developments, adverse energy development, grief caused by violent changes, deficient force development, changes caused by criminal violence, growing force in danger, and growing power in secret

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Worsening conditions and increasingly dangerous power development.
- psychology: Grief associated with violent changes.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Criminal violence and power growing in secret are specific ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: Increased danger, adverse energy development, and deficient unfolding of force.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's evil is a historical moral term; danger does not establish criminality in every case.

## Contradictions

Worsening conditions and growing danger overlap. Deficient force and hidden power are different ABC expressions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Pluto/Vulcanus through a powerful development taking an adverse or dangerous course. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes worsening conditions and increased danger; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds grief after violent changes, criminal violence, and power growing in secret. Deficient force development is also included, so the adverse result may involve impaired force as well as threatening power. The moral and criminal descriptions remain specific source claims.
