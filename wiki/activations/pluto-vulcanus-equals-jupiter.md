## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 263.

To experience a great pleasant change.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 420 (printed); PDF page: 209.

Successful unfolding of strength, successful change of power, developing financial strength, joy in growing influence, the unfolding of the power of money, advantageous changes of power, beneficial energy developments, growing financial strength, growing legal influence.

Translation checked against the exact glyph row in the German scan.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A welcome and successful increase in power or resources.
- Psychology: Joy in growing influence.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is established by these entries.
- Social/relationship: Financial and legal influence rather than an explicit relationship claim.
- Events/manifestations: Pleasant change, growing financial strength, and beneficial shifts in power.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte gives a broad pleasant change; ABC specifies financial, legal, and energetic forms.

## Contradictions

Witte gives a broad pleasant change; ABC specifies financial, legal, and energetic forms.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Vulcanus = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies `Pluto/Vulcanus` as a welcome increase in strength or influence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a great pleasant change; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) makes that benefit concrete through growing financial resources, legal influence, and successful changes of power. These are possible forms of beneficial development, not a guarantee of profit.
