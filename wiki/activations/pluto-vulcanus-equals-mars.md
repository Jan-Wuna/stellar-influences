## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 263.

A great change in activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 420 (printed); PDF page: 209.

Influential development work, unfolding working strength, developing powerful activity, developing activity involving power, bringing about a change of power, strong impulses toward development, active development of violence, growing development of energy.

- Core meaning: Forceful development expressed through a change in activity and work.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Vulcanus = Mars`, Mars specifies `Pluto/Vulcanus` through activity: the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names a great change in what is being done, and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds the development of working strength, powerful action, and strong impulses toward development. ABC also includes active violence; this preserves the forceful end of the range without making productive effort inherently violent.
