## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 263.

To be under the impression and influence of a great change. Either our mental attitude is changed through outside events, or a change of our mental attitude makes us see the outside world differently.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 420 (printed); PDF page: 209.

The influence of inner development, unfolding an inner change, developing inner energies, my unfolding of power, developing strength of soul, influencing inner developments, growing psychological strength, growing inner influence.

Translation checked against the exact glyph row in the German scan.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A powerful change in inner attitude or personal influence.
- Psychology: Outside events can change one's attitude; a changed attitude can also change how the world appears.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is established by these entries.
- Social/relationship: No specific relationship outcome is supplied.
- Events/manifestations: Inner development and growing psychological strength or influence.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte explicitly allows both directions between events and attitude.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Vulcanus = MC`, MC specifies `Pluto/Vulcanus` as the inner experience of a powerful transformation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes two directions: outside events can alter one's attitude, and an altered attitude can change how the outside world is seen. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds developing inner energy and psychological strength, keeping the emphasis on the person's experience rather than a predetermined external event.
