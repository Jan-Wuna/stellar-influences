## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 263.

A people changes its entity. Great changes in the general public conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 420 (printed); PDF page: 209.

A woman developing influence, evolution of a strong people, developing emotional strength, changes of power among the people, growing violence against women, growing influence of women, growing influence of feelings, changing strength of feelings.

Translation checked against the exact glyph row in the German scan.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Powerful change in public conditions and in the influence of feelings or women.
- Psychology: Developing emotional strength and changing intensity of feeling.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is established by these entries.
- Social/relationship: A people and women's influence are explicit collective subjects.
- Events/manifestations: Major public changes and, at the adverse end, growing violence against women.
- Conflicts/notes: The historical gender categories and violence claim are attributed source associations, not necessary outcomes.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Vulcanus = Moon`, Moon specifies `Pluto/Vulcanus` through changes in a people and its public conditions, as the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also includes developing emotional strength, changing intensity of feeling, and growing influence of women. Its separate reference to violence against women preserves an adverse collective possibility; it is not a prediction or a universal description of women.
