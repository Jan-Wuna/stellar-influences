## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 263.

To see no way out of a crisis. To deceive oneself about the possibilities of changes and transformations. Conditions that are dragging on.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 421 (printed); PDF page: 209.

Disappointing developments of violence, the developmental influence of metaphysics, development of subtle energy, changes through unknown influences, changes through the force of water, energy development through water, growing influences of air.

Translation checked against the exact glyph row in the German scan.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A powerful transformation experienced as a prolonged crisis or a misleading possibility.
- Psychology: Seeing no way out and deceiving oneself about what can change.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is established by these entries.

- Events/manifestations: Prolonged conditions; ABC also names water, air, unknown influences, and metaphysical development.
- Conflicts/notes: The physical and metaphysical examples remain distinct from Witte's account of crisis.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Vulcanus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies `Pluto/Vulcanus` through uncertainty about change. In the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), conditions drag on while a person sees no way out or misjudges the possibilities of transformation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes disappointing developments of force and changes through unknown influences, alongside water, air, and metaphysical energy examples. Those examples broaden the source range without resolving the crisis or establishing physical causation.
