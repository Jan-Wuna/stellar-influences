## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 263.

Retarding a mighty transformation. Obstacles and hindrances which occur counter to a change. To keep something below the boiling point, to simmer.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 421 (printed); PDF page: 209.

Influential changes in old age, concentration on energy development, problematic change of power, painful development of violence, strong disturbances in a development, losses through violent changes, growing strength through seriousness.

Translation checked against the exact glyph row in the German scan.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Powerful change meets resistance, delay, or painful disruption.
- Psychology: Concentration and seriousness can accompany developing strength.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is established by these entries.
- Social/relationship: Problematic changes of power and losses through violent change.
- Events/manifestations: Retarded transformation, strong disturbances of development, and changes in old age.
- Conflicts/notes: Growing strength through seriousness does not erase the stated obstacles and losses.

## Contradictions

Growing strength through seriousness does not erase the stated obstacles and losses.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Vulcanus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies `Pluto/Vulcanus` through resistance to a mighty transformation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes hindrances and keeping something below boiling point, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds painful disruption and losses during changes of power. ABC also retains concentration on energy development and growing strength through seriousness, so difficulty is not the entry's only emphasis.
