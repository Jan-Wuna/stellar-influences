## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 263.

Days which bring mighty changes. Life reformer. The man whose body has an unusual capacity to regenerate, who changes to new ways or different ways to nourish the body. The reformer of life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 420 (printed); PDF page: 209.

Development of a center of influence, developing bodily strength, personal unfolding of power, changes in personal influence, growing bodily strength, a growing energy center, growing personal influence, changing influence of power.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Major personal change and the development of bodily strength or influence.
- Psychology: Personal power and influence develop; no detailed emotional profile is supplied.
- Body/health: The historical Witte entry describes bodily regeneration and changed nourishment; neither establishes medical efficacy.
- Social/relationship: No explicit relationship outcome is supplied.
- Events/manifestations: Life reform, changed nourishment, and shifts in personal influence.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's unusual bodily regeneration is historical doctrine, not an established physiological capacity.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Vulcanus = Sun`, Sun specifies `Pluto/Vulcanus` through personal and bodily transformation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a reformer of life who changes ways of nourishing the body; its claim of unusual regeneration remains historical source doctrine. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds growing bodily strength, personal influence, and a changing center of power. These meanings do not establish a medical effect or prescribe a diet.
