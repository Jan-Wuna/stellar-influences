## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Vulcanus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies `Pluto/Vulcanus` through sudden changes of conditions in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives this a more concrete range: influential technical changes, new energy transformation, and sudden unfolding of strength or changes of power. Its unrest through growing violence marks a possible adverse expression alongside dynamic development.
