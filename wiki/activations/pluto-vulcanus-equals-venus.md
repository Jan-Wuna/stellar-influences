## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 263.

Fulfilment of a wish, which one had for a long time. A change which was desired for a long time.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 420 (printed); PDF page: 209.

Influential artistic developments, development of a power for peace, harmonious change of power, strength through growing beauty, powerful development in love, growing strength through harmony, growing strength of love.

Translation checked against the exact glyph row in the German scan.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Powerful development takes the form of a desired or harmonious change.
- Psychology: Fulfilment of a long-held wish and growing love.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is established by these entries.
- Social/relationship: Development in love, harmony, and a power serving peace.
- Events/manifestations: Desired change, influential artistic development, and a harmonious change of power.
- Conflicts/notes: Wish fulfilment and peace or art are distinct possible expressions, not promised outcomes.

## Contradictions

Wish fulfilment and peace or art are distinct possible expressions, not promised outcomes.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Vulcanus = Venus`, Venus specifies `Pluto/Vulcanus` as a change that has long been wanted. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes wish fulfilment; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the range to love, artistic development, harmony, and a power serving peace. These passages join the force of development with attraction and harmony without guaranteeing a wish's fulfilment.
