## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 263.

Situation improves due to great increase of accomplishments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 421 (printed); PDF page: 209.

Beginning of a mighty change, developing leadership strength, developing performance capacity, change of power in leadership, change through the force of fire, growing exertions of strength, transformations through energetic leadership.

Translation checked against the exact glyph row in the German scan.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Powerful change expressed through accomplishment and leadership.
- Psychology: Growing exertion and energetic leadership.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is established by these entries.
- Social/relationship: Changes of power in leadership.
- Events/manifestations: Improvement through increased accomplishments, with fire-driven change also named.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's improvement and ABC's force or fire examples are different parts of the range.

## Contradictions

Witte's improvement and ABC's force or fire examples are different parts of the range.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Vulcanus = Zeus`, Zeus specifies `Pluto/Vulcanus` through increased accomplishment: the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects that increase with an improving situation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds developing leadership and performance, changes of power in leadership, and growing exertion. Its fire example remains a separate forceful manifestation rather than a necessary condition of achievement.
