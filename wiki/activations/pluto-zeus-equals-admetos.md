## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 259.

To have to overcome the greatest difficulties at the start. Performance of duty under difficult or aggravating circumstances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 413 (printed); PDF page: 205.

Developing sustained performance, beginning of a thorough development, blocked change of leadership, restriction of creative development, thorough unfolding of performance, suppressed striving toward development, growing restrictions through leadership.

Translation checked against the exact glyph row in the German scan.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Difficult beginnings and restricted performance.
- Psychology: Suppressed striving toward development.
- Body/health: No medical correspondence is supplied in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Leadership changes can be blocked or restrictive.
- Events/manifestations: Duty under aggravating conditions; thorough and sustained performance.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC includes endurance and thorough development as well as restriction; Witte stresses difficulty.

## Contradictions

ABC includes endurance and thorough development as well as restriction; Witte stresses difficulty.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Zeus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair through a difficult beginning and duties performed under aggravating conditions in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds blocked leadership change and restricted creativity, while retaining sustained performance and thorough development. Persistence and obstruction remain distinct possibilities within the same demanding undertaking.
