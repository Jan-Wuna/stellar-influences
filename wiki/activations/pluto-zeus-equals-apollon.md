## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 259.

Easy work. Performing one’s duty under pleasant circumstances or conditions. Success incites.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 413 (printed); PDF page: 205.

Expansion of a fire development, beginning of an extensive change, successful change of leadership, creative development of knowledge, extensive development of performance, growing achievements of science, unfolding economic goals.

Translation checked against the exact glyph row in the German scan.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Expanding development and achievement under favorable working conditions.
- Psychology: Success stimulates further effort.
- Body/health: No medical correspondence is supplied in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Scientific, economic, and leadership developments are named.
- Events/manifestations: Easy work, extensive performance development, and growing scientific achievements.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's spreading fire and Witte's pleasant work are separate manifestations.

## Contradictions

ABC's spreading fire and Witte's pleasant work are separate manifestations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Zeus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through work made easier by favorable conditions: the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) says success itself stimulates effort. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds extensive development of performance, knowledge, and economic goals. Its separate spreading-fire example preserves a material possibility rather than making expansion uniformly beneficial.
