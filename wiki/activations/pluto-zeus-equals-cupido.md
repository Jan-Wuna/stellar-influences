## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 259.

The duty to begin a family. Increase of marriage. To increase power and accomplishment by uniting or coordinating. Founding of a club.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 413 (printed); PDF page: 205.

Beginning of a shared development, development of a leadership community, change of leadership in art, shared change of goals, creative artistic developments, growing performance of a community, changing goals of a community.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Zeus = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through coordinated effort and new collective undertakings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins increased accomplishment to uniting, beginning a family, and founding a club. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds shared development, community performance, and changing artistic or communal goals; these preserve the communal emphasis without prescribing family duties.
