### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 259.

Best performance record. To be forced to accomplish the maximum. The boss or the chief or the government demanding the impossible thing, the unrealizable.

Beginning of an important development, unfolding special achievements, unfolding leadership authority, change of leadership in the state, developing independent goals, state development efforts, growing reputation through performance, beginning important developments.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: High performance and developing authority, potentially under excessive demands.
- Psychology: Pressure to accomplish the maximum; developing self-directed goals.
- Body/health: No medical correspondence is supplied in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Superiors or government may demand what cannot be achieved.
- Events/manifestations: Important developments, special achievements, leadership change, and reputation through performance.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's authority and reputation do not remove Witte's warning about impossible demands.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Zeus = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair through exceptional performance and the demands attached to authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes a best performance record but also being pushed toward the impossible by a superior or government. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds self-directed goals and reputation through achievement, preserving both accomplishment and the limits of imposed expectations.
