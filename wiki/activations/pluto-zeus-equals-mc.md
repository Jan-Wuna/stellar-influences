## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 259.

One’s personal impulses. Sense of duty. To be conscientious. Eagerness to produce. To take over a duty.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 412 (printed); PDF page: 205.

Development of a fiery soul, I change leadership, shaping my evolution, my fiery development, my changed achievements, my creative development, my goals change, changed inner leadership, growing inner exertions.

- Core meaning: Personal initiative, conscientiousness, and creative development.
- Psychology: Sense of duty, eagerness to produce, and changing personal goals.
- Body/health: No medical correspondence is supplied in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Taking on a duty; no specific relationship outcome is supplied.
- Events/manifestations: Personal leadership change and increasing inner effort.
- Conflicts/notes: The fiery-soul expression is the source's inner-development language, not a bodily claim.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Zeus = MC`, MC specifies the pair through an inward impulse to produce and take on responsibility. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names conscientiousness and a sense of duty; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal creative development, changing goals, and increasing inner effort. Its fiery-soul language describes the source's inner emphasis without establishing a physical process.
