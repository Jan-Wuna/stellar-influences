Demanding change in a connection, beginning of contact development, development of a creative connection, development of performance in a connection, change in a leading connection, growing performance in a connection.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Beginning work or developing an undertaking within a connection.
- Psychology: Readiness to tackle a problem.
- Body/health: No medical correspondence is supplied in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Creative and leading connections whose performance develops.
- Events/manifestations: Starting work, developing contacts, and demanding changes within a connection.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's general beginning is narrower than ABC's detailed relational settings.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Zeus = Node`, Node specifies the pair through beginning work and tackling a problem in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) situates that beginning within contacts and creative or leading connections, where performance develops and relationships change through effort. The connection is the setting of an undertaking, without establishing a particular intimate relationship outcome.
