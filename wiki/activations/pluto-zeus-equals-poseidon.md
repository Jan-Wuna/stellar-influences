## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 259.

To develop a new way to see the world. To show the duties to be derived from such doctrine.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 413 (printed); PDF page: 205.

Unfolding spiritual fire, leading cultural evolution, developing intellectual or spiritual striving toward goals, creative cultural development, changed cultural shaping, rational change of leadership, growing spiritual leadership, change in cultural leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A new worldview and the duties or cultural goals derived from it.
- Psychology: Spiritual or intellectual striving toward a goal.
- Body/health: No medical correspondence is supplied in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Cultural evolution and changes in cultural leadership.
- Events/manifestations: Creative cultural development and changed cultural shaping.
- Conflicts/notes: A doctrine's associated duties do not establish that doctrine as true or binding.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Zeus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through developing a new way of seeing the world and identifying duties that follow from it in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds cultural creativity, intellectual goals, and changing cultural leadership. This connects an outlook with directed creative work without certifying the resulting doctrine as true or universally binding.
