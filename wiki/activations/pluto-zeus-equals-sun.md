### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 259.

Daily obligations. The man who increases, or is asked to increase physical efforts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 412 (printed); PDF page: 205.

Demanding personal changes, changes in a man's performance, personal creative development, change of direction of a person, changes in a leadership center, growing physical performance, change of a leading person.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal change and increased physical effort in daily obligations.
- Psychology: Personal creative development and changing direction.
- Body/health: The entries name physical effort and performance, without specifying a disease or medical effect.
- Social/relationship: Changes involving a leader; the historical entry also uses male language.
- Events/manifestations: Growing physical performance and changes at a leadership center.
- Conflicts/notes: The physical-effort claim supplies no diagnosis; gendered wording is attributed source language.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Zeus = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through the personal and physical demands of daily obligations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes increased bodily effort, willingly or on demand; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal creativity, changed direction, and growing physical performance. Its leadership examples broaden the setting without turning physical effort into a health prediction or fixed male role.
