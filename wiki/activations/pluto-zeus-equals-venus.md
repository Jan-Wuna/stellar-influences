## Derived Synthesis

In `Pluto/Zeus = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through duties experienced as pleasant and efforts directed toward love, peace, or art. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives marital, maternal, and household examples; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds artistic achievement and harmony through changing leadership. Its preference for unfolding fire is a separate source association, while the domestic language remains historical rather than prescriptive.
