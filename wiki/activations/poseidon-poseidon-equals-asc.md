## Derived Synthesis

In `Poseidon/Poseidon = Asc`, Asc specifies the repeated pair through other people and the impressions of an intellectual or spiritual environment. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names acquaintances with deep thought, knowledge, or educational skills. It separately includes nearby oceans and water masses; that material association remains literal source vocabulary without predicting a water event.
