## Derived Synthesis

In `Poseidon/Poseidon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the repeated pair through models, instructions, and education, while the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) also names ideological separation and mental isolation. Separation can be made for logical reasons, and attention can concentrate on following directions. These are related limits and forms of thought without establishing that education itself produces isolation.
