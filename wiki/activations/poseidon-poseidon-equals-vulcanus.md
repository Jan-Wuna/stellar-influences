## Derived Synthesis

In `Poseidon/Poseidon = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the repeated pair through mental or spiritual power and the way it is experienced as self-regard. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ranges from honor, dignity, and self-confidence to pride and arrogance. This keeps confidence and overvaluation distinct without making either the inevitable result of intellectual influence.
