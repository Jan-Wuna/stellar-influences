## Derived Synthesis

In `Poseidon/Poseidon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the repeated Poseidon pair as purposeful mental production: forming a conviction, giving it demonstrable shape and establishing evidence. The same entry connects this work with teaching and leadership, and with the concrete acts of witnessing, testifying, testing and issuing a certificate or report. Its inclusion of mental compulsion keeps the reading from becoming an unqualified endorsement of conviction or leadership. This is the scope of the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), located at PDF p. 317.
