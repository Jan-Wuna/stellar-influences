## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Many crowded together in a narrow space, unable to unfold.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Restrictions and disturbances of success, success problems through depression, serious spread of depression, patient deepening of knowledge, major circulatory problems, concentration on knowledge of primordial substance, and extensive congestion disturbances

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: restricted scope and obstructed expansion.
- Psychology: patient study alongside the ABC entry's depression language.
- Body/health: ABC historically associates the formula with circulatory problems; this is not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: crowding prevents people from unfolding their possibilities.
- Events/manifestations: blocked success and sustained deepening of knowledge.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's congestion and circulatory language remains a historical association.

## Contradictions

Crowding and patient knowledge-building are different emphases, not opposing predictions. Neither entry establishes an inevitable outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through a conflict between scope and confinement: many people or possibilities have too little room to develop. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives physical crowding (PDF p. 229), while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the theme to obstructed success and patient deepening of knowledge (printed p. 365; PDF p. 181). Sustained study is therefore a distinct possibility within restriction. ABC's depression, congestion and circulatory language remains a historical association, not a diagnosis.
