## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To get into trouble through others. Another person is in trouble or in a forced position.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Restrictions of the partner in old age, serious depression of the partner, patient deepening in one place, patient concentration on the partner, thorough blockage of a place, circulatory problems of the partner, and deeper engagement with tasks concerning the surroundings

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: confinement involving another person or a place.
- Psychology: patience and concentrated attention to a partner.
- Body/health: ABC associates the partner with circulatory problems; this establishes no diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: another person's difficulty can become one's own constraint.
- Events/manifestations: a forced position, blocked place or sustained environmental task.
- Conflicts/notes: the partner may be the affected person, not the cause of the trouble.

## Contradictions

The entries allow both trouble through others and another person being in trouble. These are alternatives, not grounds for assigning blame.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = Asc`, Asc specifies the pair through a partner, other people or the immediate setting. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) distinguishes being constrained through someone else from encountering someone already in a forced position (PDF p. 229). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also permits patient concentration on that person or on tasks in one place (printed p. 364; PDF p. 181). Restriction and sustained attention thus belong together here, without making a partner inherently harmful. ABC's depression, circulation and age language remains attributed rather than universalized.
