## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Difficulties in the family. Separations and isolations in the family. Quarrels and long lasting estrangements in the family or in an association or company.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Developing deeper engagement with tasks, stabilizing a disturbed community, intensification of problems in a community, problems of a depressed community, disturbances in a community through restriction, and deep concentration on the whole

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: restricted cohesion within a family or group.
- Psychology: deep concentration on the whole; collective discouragement in ABC.
- Body/health: no bodily claim in these entries.
- Social/relationship: separation, isolation, quarrels and lasting estrangement.
- Events/manifestations: difficulties in a family, association or company; efforts to stabilize a disturbed community.
- Conflicts/notes: stabilization and estrangement remain distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes estrangement, while ABC also permits stabilization of a disturbed community. This extends the range without promising reconciliation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair as a difficulty of belonging and cohesion: members of a family, association or company may remain separated or estranged. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes the long duration of quarrels explicit (PDF p. 229). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds both intensified community problems and efforts to stabilize a disturbed collective through deeper engagement with its tasks (printed p. 365; PDF p. 181). This preserves a constructive possibility alongside prolonged division, without promising reconciliation.
