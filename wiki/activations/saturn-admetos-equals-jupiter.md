## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Modest fortune. Lucky in narrow circumstances. Happiness, resting of a matter. To find a way out of a misfortune.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Depression through money problems, restriction through financial losses, patient in-depth engagement with law, thorough concentration on law, deeper engagement with money problems and legal tasks, and beneficial restrictions in old age

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: modest relief within restriction, alongside financial difficulty.
- Psychology: distress over money in ABC; contentment in narrow circumstances in Witte.
- Body/health: no specific bodily claim; depression wording remains attributed.
- Social/relationship: legal tasks are present; relationship dynamics are not specified.
- Events/manifestations: money losses, legal concentration or finding a way out of misfortune.
- Conflicts/notes: fortunate restriction and loss-induced restriction are materially different possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes modest fortune and escape from misfortune; ABC emphasizes financial losses while also allowing beneficial restrictions. Neither emphasis cancels the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies how fortune, money and legal concerns meet narrow circumstances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) allows modest happiness and a way out of misfortune (PDF p. 229), whereas the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also describes financial loss and sustained attention to money or legal tasks (printed p. 364; PDF p. 181). The useful throughline is the limited scale of the circumstances, with relief possible inside them; the entries do not support turning every restriction into good fortune.
