## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Fall of an authority. Ruling personage in a tight spot.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special deepening of tasks, major depression-related disturbances, large losses through strikes, hard restrictions imposed by the state, intensification of problems for an authority, deeper engagement with state problems, and important restrictions in old age

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: authority under severe constraint.
- Psychology: deep attention to special tasks; ABC also uses depression language.
- Body/health: no specific bodily claim in these entries.
- Social/relationship: a ruler or authority may be restricted; the state may impose restrictions.
- Events/manifestations: fall from authority, strike losses and state problems.
- Conflicts/notes: authority may be either constrained or constraining.

## Contradictions

Restriction of an authority and restriction by the state place authority on different sides of the situation; both are retained without merging their roles.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair through authority and state affairs. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes an authority losing position or being trapped (PDF p. 229); the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also allows restrictions imposed by the state and deep attention to special or governmental problems (printed p. 365; PDF p. 181). Authority can therefore be either constrained or constraining, and greater concentration on the task does not guarantee restoration of power.
