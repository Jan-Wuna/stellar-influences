## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Hindrance of activities. Forced to quit working. Long lasting suppression. Death through crushing.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

End of loss-making work, restriction of work in old age, working through serious depression, patient deepening in work, hard earth work, concentration on work, stabilization of work problems, and disturbances in the work cycle

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: activity or work checked by sustained constraint.
- Psychology: patient concentration on work in ABC.
- Body/health: Witte includes death through crushing as a historical extreme, not a prediction.
- Social/relationship: suppression may force a person to stop working; no specific relationship is described.
- Events/manifestations: interrupted work, hard earth work and work-cycle disturbances.
- Conflicts/notes: patient work and forced cessation are alternatives within the entry's range.

## Contradictions

The entries include both sustained work and inability to continue. The formula alone does not decide which circumstance applies.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through blocked activity and the demands of work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes hindrance or enforced cessation (PDF p. 229), while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also permits patient concentration, hard earth work and attempts to stabilize work problems (printed p. 364; PDF p. 181). This joins endurance with the possibility of reaching a practical limit. Witte's fatal crushing association and ABC's reference to working through depression remain attributed historical claims, not predictions or clinical guidance.
