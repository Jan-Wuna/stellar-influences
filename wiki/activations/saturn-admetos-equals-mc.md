## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be hindered. To be tied to something. To be patient. The ability to wait. To be disinterested. To cross a valley. To be in a tight spot (a bottleneck).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

End of emotional strain, my thorough seriousness, my limitation in old age, my serious depression, my patience and perseverance, my deep concentration, my circulatory strain, emotional maturity through thoroughness, and intensification of emotional problems

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: personally experienced confinement and endurance.
- Psychology: seriousness, patience, perseverance and deep concentration.
- Body/health: ABC mentions circulatory strain; no medical inference is drawn.
- Social/relationship: being tied to something is stated; a particular relationship is not.
- Events/manifestations: waiting, a bottleneck or a period of limitation.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC allows both ending emotional strain and intensifying emotional problems.

## Contradictions

Patience and disinterest describe different responses to being held back. ABC's ending of emotional strain and intensification of emotional problems are alternatives, not a single inevitable sequence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = MC`, MC specifies the pair as one's own experience of being held, hindered or required to wait. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places patience beside disinterest and the image of a bottleneck (PDF p. 229). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) contributes seriousness, perseverance and deep concentration, allowing emotional maturity and an end to emotional strain as well as intensified problems (printed p. 364; PDF p. 181). Together these support sustained personal limitation and differing responses to it, without converting historical health associations into a diagnosis.
