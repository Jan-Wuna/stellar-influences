## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To think slowly deeply and logically. Sad and miserable thinking. Slowness and dullness of comprehension.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reports of circulatory strain, movement restriction in old age, serious conversations about depression, patient in-depth conversation, thorough concentration on youth, problematic restriction of youth, and suppressed media tasks

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: slow, concentrated or burdened thought and communication.
- Psychology: logical depth and sad thinking coexist in Witte's entry.
- Body/health: ABC mentions reports of circulatory stress and restricted movement in old age; these remain attributed associations.
- Social/relationship: serious conversations and limitations affecting youth.
- Events/manifestations: patient discussion and media-related tasks.
- Conflicts/notes: mental depth is not equivalent to impaired comprehension; both are listed possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte's depth of thought and dullness of comprehension are contrasting manifestations, not a single cognitive description. Suppressed media tasks extend the restrictive theme to communication work.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through thinking and conversation that proceed slowly or under a burden. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) permits both deep logical thought and difficulty comprehending (PDF p. 229); these should remain distinct possibilities. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the theme to patient discussion, serious talk about depression, concentration on youth and suppressed media tasks (printed p. 364; PDF p. 181). The common thread is sustained mental attention under restriction, with no basis for diagnosing an individual from the source's health language.
