## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Definitely broken off connections. To be without connections. To be alone.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Intensification of tasks within a connection, burdensome resistance within a connection, restricted connections in old age, serious depression in a relationship, patient deepening of a connection, and thorough concentration on a connection

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: connections narrowed, burdened or broken.
- Psychology: patient concentration on a connection; distress within a relationship in ABC.
- Body/health: no specific bodily claim is stated.
- Social/relationship: isolation, resistance within bonds and restricted contact.
- Events/manifestations: definite severance or sustained attention to relationship tasks.
- Conflicts/notes: an existing but constrained bond differs from complete disconnection.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes broken connections; ABC also permits work within a continuing connection. The latter is not evidence that severance must be repaired.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = Node`, Node specifies the pair through connections that become limited or difficult to sustain. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) reaches the extreme of definite severance and being alone (PDF p. 229). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also describes resistance and intensified tasks within an existing bond, where patient concentration remains possible (printed p. 364; PDF p. 181). The distinction between an absent connection and a burdensome continuing one must remain visible; neither entry makes reconciliation inevitable.
