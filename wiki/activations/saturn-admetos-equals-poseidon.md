## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Long duration of education and training. A stalemate of long duration in education and training, and interest in activities in such fields.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Consolidation of clarity through patience, thorough concentration on truth, disturbances through mental blockage, intensification of cultural tasks, reasonable restriction in old age, deeper engagement with ideal tasks, and truth about depression-related problems

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: prolonged or blocked learning and the patient pursuit of clarity.
- Psychology: concentration on truth; mental blockage as a separate possibility.
- Body/health: ABC mentions depression-related problems without a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: education, training and cultural tasks.
- Events/manifestations: long training, educational stalemate or deeper ideal work.
- Conflicts/notes: long duration alone does not distinguish development from stagnation.

## Contradictions

The entries retain both patient clarification and mental or educational blockage. These are contrasting possibilities, not interchangeable descriptions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through education, truth and cultural or ideal tasks that require sustained attention. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) distinguishes long training from an educational stalemate (PDF p. 229). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) likewise places patient clarification beside mental blockage, while permitting reasonable restriction and deeper engagement with ideals (printed p. 365; PDF p. 181). The throughline is sustained intellectual effort under limits, with no guarantee that duration itself produces understanding.
