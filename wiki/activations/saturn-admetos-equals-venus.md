## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To surmount or surpass difficulties easily. To be insensible. Not sensitive to bad manners. To be avoided and snubbed and taking it with indifference. Thick skinned.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Depression through love problems, strengthening of harmony in old age, patience and harmony in congestion, harmony loss through depression, consolidation of love problems, deepening in peace tasks, and preference for patience and perseverance

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: enduring difficulty with emotional insulation or patient harmony.
- Psychology: indifference to snubs, patience and perseverance.
- Body/health: no specific bodily claim is stated.
- Social/relationship: love problems, lost harmony, peace work and strengthening harmony.
- Events/manifestations: surmounting difficulties or remaining composed in congestion.
- Conflicts/notes: thick skin and relationship distress are contrasting possibilities.

## Contradictions

The entries describe a range of manifestations. Thick skin and relationship distress are contrasting possibilities; this is a qualification, not proof of agreement across sources.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = Venus`, Venus specifies the Saturn/Admetos pair through the way affection and harmony meet difficulty. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes taking snubs or poor manners with indifference and passing through obstacles easily (PDF p. 229). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also includes love-related distress and lost harmony, alongside patience, peace work and strengthened harmony (printed p. 364; PDF p. 181). Emotional insulation can therefore coexist in the entry's range with a patient effort toward harmony; neither is guaranteed by the formula.
