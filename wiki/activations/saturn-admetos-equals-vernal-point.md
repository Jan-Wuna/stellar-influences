## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

General shrinkage, hindrance and lack.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

restrictions and losses in public, thorough concentration on public life, public limitation of damage, condensation of problems in public life, congestion disturbances in public, and deepening into public duties

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: restriction and loss in public life.
- Psychology: thorough concentration on public concerns.
- Body/health: no bodily claim is stated.
- Social/relationship: public duties and collective hindrance.
- Events/manifestations: congestion, intensified problems and damage limitation.
- Conflicts/notes: public difficulty and attempts to contain damage are distinct.

## Contradictions

The entries describe a range of manifestations. Public difficulty and attempts to contain damage are distinct; this is a qualification, not proof of agreement across sources.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Saturn/Admetos pair through public circumstances. General shrinkage, hindrance and lack in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 229) become restrictions, losses and congestion in public life in the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 364; PDF p. 181). ABC also includes concentration on public duties and limitation of damage, so the reading retains a practical response to collective constraint rather than only a list of losses.
