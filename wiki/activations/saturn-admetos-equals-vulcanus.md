## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be heavily guarded, to feel limited and depressed. Long duration of privation of freedom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Restricted strength in old age, massive limitation of losses, massive congestion disturbances, suppressing severe pain, concentrated strengthening of force, problematic concentration of power, strong circulatory problems, and strong depression-related disturbances

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: force constrained or concentrated over a long period.
- Psychology: feeling limited and depressed in Witte's wording.
- Body/health: ABC's pain suppression and circulatory associations remain historical.
- Social/relationship: heavy guarding and deprivation of freedom.
- Events/manifestations: prolonged confinement, power concentration and loss limitation.
- Conflicts/notes: suppressed pain differs from simply experiencing severe pain.

## Contradictions

The entries describe a range of manifestations. Suppressed pain differs from simply experiencing severe pain; this is a qualification, not proof of agreement across sources.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Admetos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Saturn/Admetos pair through force held under severe constraint. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes heavy guarding and prolonged deprivation of freedom (PDF p. 229). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also includes concentrated strengthening and substantial limitation of losses, alongside problematic concentration of power (printed p. 365; PDF p. 181). These possibilities distinguish containment from an effective use of concentrated strength. Its pain, depression and circulation associations remain historical claims, not diagnoses.
