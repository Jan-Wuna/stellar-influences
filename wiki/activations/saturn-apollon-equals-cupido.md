## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 228.

Lectures. A skillful teacher. To proceed methodically in art. Many separate from a community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 363 (printed); PDF page: 180.

Expansion of shared problems, success of a community through patience, successful concentration on the whole, shared loss of freedom, painful experiences of a community, synthesis of old knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Methodical teaching or art and the shared tasks or difficulties of a community.
- Psychology: Concentration on the whole and patience in collective effort.
- Body/health: Painful experience is named without specifying a medical condition or establishing a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Community success, shared loss of freedom, or many leaving a community.
- Events/manifestations: Lectures, skilled teaching, methodical art, and synthesis of old knowledge.
- Conflicts/notes: Shared success and shared pain or restriction coexist in ABC's range.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Apollon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through methodical work within a community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives lectures, skilled teaching, and method in art, alongside many leaving a community. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds synthesis of old knowledge and shared concentration, while preserving common problems, pain, and loss of freedom beside patient success.
