## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 228.

Method in historical research. Odd or bad method. Obsolete system. Separation of many by ugly and vile means.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 363 (printed); PDF page: 180.

Old secret knowledge, experience of patience in need, dangerous expansion of problems, experiences of grief and pain, hidden problems of science, losses through trade crime, danger of losing freedom, increasing losses through crime.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Historical or concealed knowledge alongside defective methods and expanding difficulties.
- Psychology: Patience in need, grief, and painful experience.
- Body/health: Painful experience is named without specifying a medical condition or establishing a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Separation by harmful means and threatened freedom.
- Events/manifestations: Historical research, obsolete systems, hidden scientific problems, and trade crime.
- Conflicts/notes: Historical research and obsolete or harmful methods are separate associations, not equivalent judgments.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Apollon = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through historical or concealed knowledge and the difficulties of method. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes historical research, but separately names obsolete systems and separation by harmful means. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds patience in need, grief, hidden scientific problems, and losses through crime. Research into the past is not itself defined as defective.
