## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Apollon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through a successful method or a welcome separation in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) locates patient success within law and finance but also names expanding financial problems and extensive losses. The sources therefore support the value of method and experience without establishing a guaranteed favorable result.
