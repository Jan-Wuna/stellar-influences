### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 228.

Bad, confused method. To proceed without a method. To live under uncertain circumstances. Obliged to separate constantly.

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 363 (printed); PDF page: 180.

Old mystical knowledge, expansion of air/water problems, disappointing painful experience, success through sensitivity in old age, success through patience and sensitivity, problems of a future science, increased burdening of the air.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Apollon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through confused method and uncertain circumstances in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), which also names repeated separation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) retains patient sensitivity and mystical knowledge as another emphasis, alongside disappointing pain and expanding air or water problems. Those possibilities do not remove the source's uncertainty or guarantee that sensitivity brings success.
