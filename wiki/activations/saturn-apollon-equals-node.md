## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 228.

To give up the connections with many, to retire. Many have to interrupt their connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 362 (printed); PDF page: 180.

Expansion of a connection's problems, success in a mature connection, success through patience in a connection, successful connections in old age, serious connections to science, connection to old knowledge, considerable strain in a connection.

Translation and paragraph boundaries checked against the exact glyph row in the German scan.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Connections may be interrupted or sustained through mature patience.
- Psychology: Patience and considerable strain within a connection.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is supplied in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Giving up many contacts, retirement, mature relationships, and links to science.
- Events/manifestations: Interruption of connections or their success in old age.
- Conflicts/notes: Withdrawal and patient success are different relational possibilities in the entries.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Apollon = Node`, Node specifies the pair through the condition of connections. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) stresses withdrawing, retiring, or interrupting many contacts, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also allows mature connections to succeed through patience. Its serious links to science and old knowledge give the connection a concrete field of shared interest, with strain remaining part of the range.
