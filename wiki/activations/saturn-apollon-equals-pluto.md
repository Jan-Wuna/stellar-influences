## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Apollon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair through the development of a method or system in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds unfolding old knowledge, patient task development, and concentrated learning, while preserving growing scientific problems and pain. Development therefore includes both improved organization and new difficulty, with collective separation another stated expression.
