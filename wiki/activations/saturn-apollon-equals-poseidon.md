## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 228.

Instructional or educational method. To be educational through instruction. To be the educator or the student.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 363 (printed); PDF page: 180.

Old cultural knowledge, success through ideas in old age, successful concentration on truth, clarity about scientific tasks, concentrated spiritual knowledge, expanding cultural tasks, mature spiritual knowledge, loss of spiritual freedom.

- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is supplied in these entries.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Apollon = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through instruction and educational method, with teacher and student explicitly named in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds concentrated cultural or spiritual knowledge and clarity about scientific tasks, while also retaining the loss of intellectual or spiritual freedom. Methodical learning does not by itself establish freedom or truth.
