#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 362 (printed); PDF page: 180.

An old center of knowledge, expansion of personal problems, a man's success in old age, successful concentration on knowledge, human painful experience, personal success through patience, a day with personal problems, a center for knowledge of pain.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Apollon = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through personally demonstrating a method or being examined and checked in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places concentrated knowledge and patient personal success alongside expanding personal problems and painful experience.
