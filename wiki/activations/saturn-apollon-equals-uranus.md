#### Witte Entry

The own original method. Breaking new ground. To isolate oneself. Astrological method. Sudden separation of many.

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 363 (printed); PDF page: 180.

Tasks of technical science, success through patient reform, patient renewal of knowledge, concentration on new knowledge, new problems of science, sudden painful experiences, reform tasks of technology, increase of new duties.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Apollon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through an original method and breaking new ground in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), which includes astrological method and sudden separation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds patient reform, renewed knowledge, and technical-scientific tasks, while preserving new problems, duties, and sudden pain. Originality and patience are both explicit, rather than mutually exclusive.
