#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 362 (printed); PDF page: 180.

Seriousness and patience in love, expansion of harmony in old age, great grief in love, disturbances of harmony in the state, preference for old knowledge, an important task of love, increasing seriousness in love, increasing love problems.

- Core meaning: Methodical harmony, schooling, and serious or patient love.
- Psychology: Seriousness and patience in love; great grief and growing love problems also appear.

- Social/relationship: School friendships and important tasks of love.
- Events/manifestations: Singing lessons, harmony systems, harmony in old age, and disturbed state harmony.
- Conflicts/notes: The favorable harmony examples do not cancel ABC's grief or disharmony.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Apollon = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through learning and sustaining harmony. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives singing lessons, a harmony system, and friendships at school; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds patience and seriousness in love, together with grief and increasing love problems. Old knowledge and harmony in old age remain possible positive expressions without erasing the difficult ones.
