## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 87.

To be tied to sorrowful people. To be separated by a serious illness. Hostility.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 115 (printed); PDF page: 56.

An old mysterious place, an old historical place, tasks at a dangerous place, deprivation and patience for the environment, serious service to a partner, difficult environmental tasks, patient renunciation by the partner, disadvantages of a problematic environment.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sorrowful or difficult ties and demanding surroundings.
- Psychology: Sorrow, patience, deprivation, and renunciation.
- Body/health: Witte names separation through serious illness as a historical possibility, not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Hostility or attachment to sorrowful people; serious service within partnership.
- Events/manifestations: Historical places, dangerous locations, environmental disadvantages, and illness-related separation.
- Conflicts/notes: The old abandonment translation is corrected to renunciation; the illness example is historical.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Asc = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through sorrowful ties, hostility, and separation in difficult circumstances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes separation through serious illness as a historical extreme; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds demanding surroundings, patient renunciation, and serious service within partnership. Its old or historical place associations remain specific settings rather than causes of suffering.
