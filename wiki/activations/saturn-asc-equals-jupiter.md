## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Separation with fortunate ending. To be left by roommates. Change of residence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful separation from one place, financial problems with others, joy in an old place, money losses in the place, problematic environmental outcomes, legal problems with others, environmental success through patience, and satisfied with a serious partner

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Indifference, tendency to ignore or bypass difficulties, happy contentment in modest circumstances. Joy over a separation, a change of place or residence.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Enthusiasm is hampered by self-uncertainty. Your enthusiasm comes across as serious and sincere. A positive attitude toward working out the problems encountered when relating.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SATURN/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `300`

#### Munkasey Entry

Causes you to inflate perceptions of yourself easily, but are still reticent about sharing your views; a silent ego; your comprehension of large amounts of facts and data, especially about people or places, is enhanced.
