## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 87.

Separation with fortunate ending. To be left by roommates. Change of residence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 114 (printed); PDF page: 56.

Successful separation from a place, financial problems with others, joy in an old place, money losses at the place, problematic environmental outcomes, legal problems with others, environmental success through patience, satisfaction with a serious partner.

Translation and full row boundaries checked against the German activator glyph.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 260 (printed); PDF page: 262; entry code: 0920.

Indifference, tendency to ignore or bypass difficulties, happy contentment in modest circumstances. Joy over a separation, a change of place or residence.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 94 (exact entry text checked; chapter begins on PDF page 94).

Enthusiasm is hampered by self-uncertainty. Your enthusiasm comes across as serious and sincere. A positive attitude toward working out the problems encountered when relating.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SATURN/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `300`

#### Munkasey Entry

Causes you to inflate perceptions of yourself easily, but are still reticent about sharing your views; a silent ego; your comprehension of large amounts of facts and data, especially about people or places, is enhanced.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A welcome or fortunate separation can coexist with serious, restrained engagement with others.
- Psychology: Contentment, sincere enthusiasm, self-uncertainty, or a quietly inflated self-image.
- Body/health: No medical correspondence is supplied for this exact orientation.
- Social/relationship: Working positively on relational problems, or being left by roommates.
- Events/manifestations: Change of residence, patient environmental success, legal or financial problems.
- Conflicts/notes: Contentment and inflated self-perception are distinct emphases; enthusiasm is not uniformly confident.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Asc = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through finding a more workable relation to restrained circumstances. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin entries](#ebertin-entry) include a welcome separation or change of residence, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) retains sincere enthusiasm that can be checked by self-uncertainty. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) preserves patient success beside legal and financial problems.

[Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds comprehension of facts about people or places and a silent, potentially inflated self-image. That difference matters: contentment, optimism, and self-estimation need not all move together, and a favorable parting does not establish general financial or social success.
