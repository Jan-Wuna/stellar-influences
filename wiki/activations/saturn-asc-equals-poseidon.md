## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 87.

Mental separation. To be refused and separated mentally. To be or to become separated in matters of world-concept.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 115 (printed); PDF page: 56.

Old spirit of a place, burdensome truth for the partner, insight of an old partner, clarity through patience of the partner, problems of a cultural place, problematic separation from the partner, recognition of environmental tasks, and loss of environmental culture

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Separation over outlook, with burdensome truth or patient clarity in partnership.
- Psychology: Insight of an older partner and clarity through patience.
- Body/health: No medical correspondence is established in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Mental refusal, differences of worldview, and problematic partner separation.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural-place problems, recognized environmental tasks, and loss of local culture.
- Conflicts/notes: Mental separation coexists with insight and clarity; neither guarantees reconciliation.

## Contradictions

Mental separation coexists with insight and clarity; neither guarantees reconciliation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Asc = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through separation in thought or worldview in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a burdensome truth for a partner, insight, and clarity through patience, with cultural and environmental tasks as concrete settings. Insight remains possible even when differences of outlook or a problematic separation persist.
