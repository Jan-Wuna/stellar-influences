## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 87.

Sudden leave-taking. Sudden separation from others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 115 (printed); PDF page: 56.

Excitement through losses at the place, serious tensions with the partner, new partner tasks, sudden difficulties with the partner, sudden separation from others, problematic disturbances at the place, technical disturbances at the place, surprising environmental tasks.

Translation checked against the full exact glyph row in the German scan.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 260 (printed); PDF page: 262; entry code: 0921.

Self-willed and obstinate conduct in contact with others. An extraordinary or unusual dispute, enforced separation, running away.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF pages: 94–95 (exact entry text checked; chapter begins on PDF page 94).

You want others to take your uniqueness and originality seriously. The desire to be free of one’s personal limitations and inadequacies. At worst, you ignore them, but can only do this for so long. At best, you see how you really need them because they help you to grow, and commit yourself to working on them as a path toward liberation.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SATURN/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `300`

#### Munkasey Entry

You often surprise people with your astute perceptions because you seem to be quiet and introspective; you prefer to study others silently, and may use computers, applying astrological or statistical methods of analysis to people.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Contact changes abruptly, while originality presses against personal limitations.
- Psychology: Self-will, tension, wish to be taken seriously, and astute silent observation.
- Body/health: No medical correspondence is established in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Sudden or enforced separation, unusual disputes, and new partner tasks.
- Events/manifestations: Technical disturbances, new environmental tasks, and use of statistical or astrological analysis.
- Conflicts/notes: Working with limitations and ignoring them are explicit alternatives in Sandbach, not a guaranteed reform process.

## Contradictions

Working with limitations and ignoring them are explicit alternatives in Sandbach, not a guaranteed reform process.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Asc = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden changes in contact. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin entries](#ebertin-entry) include abrupt leave-taking, disputes, enforced separation, and running away; the restored [ABC row](#abc-entry) adds partner tension and technical or local disturbances.

[Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) gives a conditional choice between ignoring personal limitations and working with them toward freedom. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) supplies quiet, astute observation and computer-assisted analysis. This keeps originality, analysis, and disruptive separation distinct rather than making every abrupt change a constructive reform.
