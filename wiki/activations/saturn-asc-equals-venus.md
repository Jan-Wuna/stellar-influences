## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 87.

Love’s separation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 114 (printed); PDF page: 56.

Peace problems at the place, patience in love toward the partner, disturbed harmony with the partner, disturbed local peace, harmony with a serious partner, love problems with the partner, separation from a beautiful place, preference for an old place.

Translation checked against the full exact glyph row in the German scan.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 260 (printed); PDF page: 262; entry code: 0918.

An inhibited and depressed expression of feeling and love caused by influences within the environment. Voluntary separation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 94 (exact entry text checked; chapter begins on PDF page 94).

A love of being taken seriously. A beautiful way of presenting oneself as having depth and wisdom. One finds it easy to be taken seriously.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SATURN/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `300`

#### Munkasey Entry

Enhances the enjoyment received from being in your own mental space; the pleasure derived from listening to music or enjoying art by yourself; building appreciation within yourself about your abilities and skills.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Affection and pleasure are shaped by seriousness, privacy, or separation.
- Psychology: Love of being taken seriously, private enjoyment of art, and inhibited expression of love.
- Body/health: Ebertin describes inhibited or depressed feeling, without establishing a clinical disorder.
- Social/relationship: Voluntary or romantic separation, partner patience, and disturbed harmony.
- Events/manifestations: Music or art enjoyed alone; peace problems and preference for an old place.
- Conflicts/notes: Enjoyment of solitude differs from depressed affection; both are explicit source possibilities.

## Contradictions

Enjoyment of solitude differs from depressed affection; both are explicit source possibilities.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Asc = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through the meeting of affection with seriousness and private space. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) emphasizes the pleasure of being taken seriously, and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) describes enjoyment of music, art, and one's own abilities in solitude. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) retains patient love and harmony with a serious partner beside disturbed harmony.

The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin entries](#ebertin-entry) also preserve romantic or voluntary separation and inhibited expression of love under environmental pressure. Chosen private enjoyment therefore remains distinct from unwanted emotional restriction.
