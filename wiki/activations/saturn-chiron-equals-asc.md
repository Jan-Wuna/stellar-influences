### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 88 (exact entry checked; chapter begins on PDF page 87).

One’s emanates a deep, wise, profound aura that can help others to be more focused and clear. One’s personality exerts an organizing and stabilizing influence on all around one.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A personal presence that organizes, stabilizes, and clarifies.
- Psychology: Focus and clarity communicated through a profound or wise manner.
- Body/health: No bodily condition or clinical treatment is specified.
- Social/relationship: Others are helped to become clearer and more focused.
- Events/manifestations: An organizing influence on immediate surroundings; no particular event is named.
- Conflicts/notes: The aura language belongs to Sandbach's spiritual account, not a measured personal attribute.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Chiron = Asc`, Asc specifies the pair through the effect of one's personality on the immediate environment. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) describes a profound, wise presence that helps others focus and exerts an organizing, stabilizing influence. Its aura language remains part of that spiritual description.
