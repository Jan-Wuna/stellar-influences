## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 88 (exact entry checked; chapter begins on PDF page 87).

Your mission in life is to realize how your life is organized, to perceive its patterns at a deep level, and to understand the meaning and purpose of all hardships and restrictions in your life. The ability to bring a healing and spiritualizing sense of order to one’s career work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Understanding life patterns and bringing a spiritual sense of order to career work.
- Psychology: Reflection on the meaning of hardships and restrictions.
- Body/health: No bodily condition or clinical treatment is specified.
- Social/relationship: No specific relationship outcome is supplied.
- Events/manifestations: Career work organized through a healing or spiritualizing sense of order.
- Conflicts/notes: Meaning in every hardship is Sandbach's spiritual premise, not an established fact about suffering.

## Contradictions

Meaning in every hardship is Sandbach's spiritual premise, not an established fact about suffering.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Chiron = MC`, MC specifies the pair through recognizing the organization and patterns of one's life and career. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) interprets hardships through spiritual purpose and describes bringing a healing sense of order to work. That attribution preserves the source's premise without making it a universal explanation of suffering.
