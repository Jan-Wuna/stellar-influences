### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 88 (exact entry checked; chapter begins on PDF page 87).

To be able to communicate in a limited, careful way that is simple and pro foundly targets exactly what needs to be said for the purpose of healing.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Careful, simple communication directed precisely toward what needs saying.
- Psychology: Concentration and economy in expression.
- Body/health: No bodily condition or clinical treatment is specified.
- Social/relationship: Communication to others is named without a specific relationship outcome.
- Events/manifestations: Targeted speech for a healing purpose.
- Conflicts/notes: The focus is the choice of words, with no separate personality profile.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Chiron = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through carefully limited, simple speech that reaches exactly what needs to be said. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) gives this precision a healing purpose. Its brevity and focus supply the full supported meaning, without additional claims about the speaker's personality.
