## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 88 (exact entry checked; chapter begins on PDF page 87).

Seeking out connections with others who help you to be more aware of your limitations, fears and restrictions so that they can be healed.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Seeking connections that bring fears and limitations into awareness.
- Psychology: Greater awareness of one's own restrictions through others.
- Body/health: No bodily condition or clinical treatment is specified.
- Social/relationship: Connections chosen for their help in recognizing fears and limits.
- Events/manifestations: Seeking helpful contacts is the action explicitly named.
- Conflicts/notes: The entry identifies a shared purpose while leaving the form and duration of connections open.

## Contradictions

The entry identifies a shared purpose while leaving the form and duration of connections open.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Chiron = Node`, Node specifies the pair through seeking people who help one recognize limitations, fears, and restrictions. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) makes that increased awareness the route toward their healing. It describes the purpose of these connections without specifying their form or duration.
