## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Chiron = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through awareness of limitations and what the source says can be learned from them. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) frames those lessons in terms of spiritual reasons. Its brief statement supports that interpretive focus without supplying a wider personality or event profile.
