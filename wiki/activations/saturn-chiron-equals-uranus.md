### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A genius for having insights into the reasons and spiritual purposes behind all the restrictions and limitations one experiences. A unique gift for organizing things in a profoundly spiritual way, or a way that brings forth the deeper meaning of things

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Chiron = Uranus` gives flashes of insight into the purpose of restriction and a gift for organizing life in a spiritually revealing way. It can make blocked patterns suddenly intelligible; the caution is disruptive certainty or brilliance that is not grounded in practical care.
