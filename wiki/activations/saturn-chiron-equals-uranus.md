#### Sandbach Entry

A genius for having insights into the reasons and spiritual purposes behind all the restrictions and limitations one experiences. A unique gift for organizing things in a profoundly spiritual way, or a way that brings forth the deeper meaning of things

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Chiron = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through insight into the spiritual purposes attributed to restrictions. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) connects that insight with an unusual gift for organizing things so their deeper meaning becomes visible. It does not describe the timing or outward consequences of the insight.
