## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 224.

Not belonging to an association or community, to keep apart from others, not wanting any community with others, odd person, crank. Separation from others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 354 (printed); PDF page: 176.

Serious art at the place, shared partner tasks, shared concentration on the whole, disturbed community of a place, difficulties in the partnership community, separation from a local community, losses of another community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Distance from communal belonging and difficulty in local or partner associations.
- Psychology: Keeping apart or not wanting communal involvement.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is supplied in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Separation from others, local communities, or a partner community.
- Events/manifestations: Serious art at a place, shared partner tasks, and losses of another community.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's odd-person and crank language is a historical judgment, not a diagnostic label.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Cupido = Asc`, Asc specifies the pair through a person's relation to communal belonging. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes keeping apart or separating from others; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes shared partner tasks and serious art at a place, alongside disturbed local community and separation. Its losses of another community are a separate association, while Witte's pejorative labels remain attributed.
