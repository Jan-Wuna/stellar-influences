## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 224.

To refuse to join, which proves later to have been very fortunate. To luckily quit one organization to join another. Divorce associated with good fortune.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 354 (printed); PDF page: 176.

Tasks of a financial community, success through separation from a community, success through shared tasks, successful concentration on the whole, financial losses of a community, difficulties through a legal community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A fortunate refusal or separation, with success or losses in shared financial and legal tasks.
- Psychology: The entries emphasize outcomes rather than a detailed emotional profile.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is supplied in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Leaving one organization for another or a divorce associated with good fortune.
- Events/manifestations: Success through shared tasks and financial-community losses.
- Conflicts/notes: A fortunate departure in Witte does not cancel ABC's financial losses or legal difficulties.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Cupido = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through a refusal to join or a departure that proves fortunate in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also includes success through common tasks and concentration, but retains financial losses and difficulties involving a legal community. A beneficial change of association therefore remains one possibility rather than a guarantee of profit.
