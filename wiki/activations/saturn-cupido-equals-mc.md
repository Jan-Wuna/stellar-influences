## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 224.

Joy or sorrow through separation in married life or of associates or partners.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 354 (printed); PDF page: 176.

A disturbed community of souls, I mature within the community, my tasks for the community, my disturbed community, my concentration on the whole, my difficulties within the community, a community of souls in old age.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: The inner experience of communal tasks or separation, including either joy or sorrow.
- Psychology: Personal concentration, difficulty in community, and maturity through belonging.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is supplied in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Marriage, associates, partners, and a community of souls.
- Events/manifestations: Community tasks and inner fellowship in old age.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte explicitly allows joy as well as sorrow; difficulty is not the only emotional outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Cupido = MC`, MC specifies the pair through the personal experience of communal life and separation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly permits joy or sorrow when spouses, associates, or partners part. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds one's tasks and difficulties within a community, concentration on the whole, and maturation or inner fellowship. Communal seriousness does not determine the emotional result.
