### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 224.

Separation or divorce connected with obstacles. To separate for an indefinite time. Uncertainties of a divorce or separation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 355 (printed); PDF page: 176.

A community disturbed by lack of clarity, disappointment through shared losses, shared tasks at or on water, problematic dissolution of a community, gradually developing problems within a community, loss-making deception of a community.
