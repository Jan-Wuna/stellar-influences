## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Cupido = Node`, Node specifies the pair through the bonds that connect or separate people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives separation from parents or siblings; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also includes joining a separated community and connections formed through tasks or shared losses. Serious artistic connections are another named form of association, without making all communal bonds either lasting or broken.
