### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 224.

Days on which one feels antisocial and does better by oneself. To be separated from the family. Divorce of the father.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 354 (printed); PDF page: 176.

Serious art of a man, a community of older people, a concentrated men's community, personal tasks within the community, problems of an art center, separation from a community with a man, losses of a community of people.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal withdrawal from family or communal life and serious participation within it.
- Psychology: Feeling unsocial and sometimes doing better alone; concentration within community.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is supplied in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Older people's or men's communities, family separation, and the father's divorce.
- Events/manifestations: Personal communal tasks, serious art, and losses within a community of people.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Cupido = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through personal participation or withdrawal in communal life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes doing better alone, family separation, and the father's divorce; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds serious art, older people's community, and personal shared tasks. Its historical male communities remain source examples, with communal concentration and separation both represented.
