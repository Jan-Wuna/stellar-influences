## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 224.

To part suddenly from a community. To be suddenly connected with a community, but for the time being only. Sudden divorce.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 355 (printed); PDF page: 176.

Shared reform tasks, shared losses through reforms, a disturbed common rhythm, new serious art, a suddenly separated community, tensions through shared losses, a separation event within the community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Communal connections change suddenly, with reform tasks or disturbed collective rhythm.
- Psychology: Tension through shared losses and disruption of a common rhythm.
- Body/health: No specific medical correspondence is supplied in these exact entries.
- Social/relationship: Sudden departure, temporary joining, and sudden divorce.
- Events/manifestations: Shared reform tasks, losses through reform, and new serious art.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte allows sudden joining as well as sudden parting; reform is not guaranteed to preserve community.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Cupido = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through abrupt changes of association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes suddenly leaving, briefly joining, or divorcing; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds common reform tasks, disturbed shared rhythm, and tension through losses. New serious art is a separate specific association, while sudden connection and sudden separation remain different outcomes.
