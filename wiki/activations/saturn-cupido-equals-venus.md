## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Cupido = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through the pleasure and difficulty of shared affection or harmony. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives joy in temporary union and grief through divorce; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds serious love of art, shared peace tasks, and concentration on collective harmony. Disturbed love and common relationship problems remain part of the same range.
