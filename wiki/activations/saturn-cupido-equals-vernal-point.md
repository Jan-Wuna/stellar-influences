## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Cupido = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the pair through communal life in public. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes separation of common connections and divorce; the restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds serious public art, shared public tasks, and concentration on the public whole. Common difficulties and losses retain the strain within that public setting.
