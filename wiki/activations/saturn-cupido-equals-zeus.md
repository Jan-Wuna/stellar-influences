## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Cupido = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair through communal tasks and imposed separation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names a father-in-law's intervention or enforced separation in a family or company; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds serious artistic work, collective effort, and disrupted leadership. Shared fire, performance, and procreation problems remain attributed examples within that demanding setting.
