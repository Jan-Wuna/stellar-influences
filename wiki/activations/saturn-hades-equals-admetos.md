## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 225.

To get in a tight spot in a foreign country. To be exposed to hostilities for a long time. Great poverty and suppression.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 357 (printed); PDF page: 177.

Endurance and patience in need, danger of depression after separation, restrictions and severe deprivations, investigating past problems, dangerous concentration of problems, deficient endurance and concentration, deepening hidden problems.

Translation checked against the full exact German glyph row.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Prolonged constriction and deprivation, with endurance or investigation of hidden problems.
- Psychology: Patience in need, deficient concentration, and depression risk after separation.
- Body/health: ABC names depression risk after separation as historical doctrine, not a clinical finding.
- Social/relationship: Foreign-country difficulty, enduring hostility, poverty, and suppression.
- Events/manifestations: Investigating past problems and deepening hidden problems.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC retains patient endurance alongside deficient endurance; neither removes the severe restrictions.

## Contradictions

ABC retains patient endurance alongside deficient endurance; neither removes the severe restrictions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair through being confined by difficult circumstances over time. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives poverty, suppression, and prolonged hostility in a foreign country; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds deprivation, investigation of past problems, and hidden difficulties. Patience and endurance remain explicit beside deficient concentration and historical depression language, without promising release from the hardship.
