### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

#### ABC Entry

Past problems, dangerous disturbances of a place, severe deprivation of others, grief and pain of the partner, poor concentration of the partner, disadvantage by separation from the partner, and hidden environmental problems
