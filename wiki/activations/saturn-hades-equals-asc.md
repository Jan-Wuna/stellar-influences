## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 225.

To come into touch with the seriously sick. To be around suppressed, persecuted, refugee people. Living in a hostile environment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 356 (printed); PDF page: 177.

Partner problems from the past, dangerous disturbances of a place, severe deprivations for others, grief and pain of the partner, deficient concentration of the partner, disadvantages through separation from the partner, hidden environmental problems.

Translation checked against the full exact German glyph row.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Contact with suffering people and a hostile or troubled environment.
- Psychology: Partner grief, pain, and deficient concentration.
- Body/health: Contact with serious illness concerns the setting of encounters, not a diagnosis of the reader.
- Social/relationship: Contact with the ill, persecuted, suppressed, or displaced, and disadvantages through partner separation.
- Events/manifestations: Dangerous local disturbances and hidden environmental problems.
- Conflicts/notes: The suffering and displacement concern people's circumstances, not a negative judgment of their identities.

## Contradictions

The suffering and displacement concern people's circumstances, not a negative judgment of their identities.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Asc`, Asc specifies the pair through the people and conditions surrounding a person. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes contact with serious illness, persecution, suppression, and displacement within a hostile environment. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds partner problems from the past, deprivation, grief, and hidden environmental difficulty. These descriptions concern suffering circumstances and relationships, without making displaced or ill people inherently hostile.
