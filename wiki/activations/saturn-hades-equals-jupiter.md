## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 225.

Liver troubles. Tuberculosis of the lungs. Shortage of money. Severe money losses. Losses of material or other property. Destruction of valuables through enemies.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 356 (printed); PDF page: 177.

Burdening financial crime, joy over dangerous tasks, money losses due to criminality, profit and loss from the past (Karma), grief due to money losses, problematic legal disadvantages, and advantage due to secret tasks

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Financial or material losses and legal disadvantages, with secret tasks offering a different possibility.
- Psychology: Grief through money losses and joy in dangerous tasks.
- Body/health: Witte's liver troubles and pulmonary tuberculosis are attributed historical associations, not diagnoses.
- Social/relationship: Losses through crime or enemies and legal difficulty.
- Events/manifestations: Shortage of money, damaged valuables, and profit or loss attributed to the past.
- Conflicts/notes: The karma wording and historical illness list are source claims, not established financial or medical causation.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair chiefly through money, property, and law. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes shortage and severe material losses; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds financial crime and legal disadvantages but also advantage through secret tasks. Its karmic account of gain or loss and Witte's liver and lung disease associations remain attributed historical doctrine, without establishing financial outcomes or diagnoses.
