## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 225.

To have mighty enemies. Misery caused through power of the state. To make an enemy of the power of the state. Foreign supreme power. Occupying power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 357 (printed); PDF page: 177.

Lack of authority in old age, special tasks from the past, deprivation through the harshness of the state, dangerous losses of the state, disadvantages through loss of authority, state losses through crime, hidden problems of the state.

Translation checked against the full exact German glyph row.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: State power and authority are involved in hardship, enemies, or loss.
- Psychology: Disadvantages through lost authority rather than a detailed emotional profile.
- Body/health: The state-hardship entry supplies no specific medical correspondence.
- Social/relationship: Hostility from powerful authorities, foreign rule, or occupation.
- Events/manifestations: State losses through crime, harsh public demands, and hidden state problems.
- Conflicts/notes: The political examples are a range of source associations, not claims about a particular government.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair through the burdens of state power and authority. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes powerful enemies, foreign or occupying power, and misery caused by the state. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds harshness, loss of authority, and hidden or criminal losses of the state, preserving institutional as well as personal forms of hardship.
