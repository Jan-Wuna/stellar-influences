## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To have mighty enemies. Misery caused through power of the state. To make an enemy of the power of the state. Foreign supreme power. Occupying power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Deficiency of authoritat in old age, special tasks from the past, deprivation by the hard of the state, dangerous losses of the state, disadvantages due to loss of authoritat, state losses due to criminality, and hidden problems of the state
