## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 225.

The health suffers through the work. Death as a result of long illness or by a weapon. Sad death. To be exploited. Unfinished matters causes trouble.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 356 (printed); PDF page: 177.

Patient work in concealment, working on secret tasks, dangerous work tasks, disadvantages through loss of work, hidden work problems, losses through work-related crime, renunciation of hard work, separation from dirty work.

Translation checked against the full exact German glyph row.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Work becomes difficult, hazardous, exploitative, or unfinished.
- Psychology: Patience in concealed work and trouble through unfinished matters.
- Body/health: Witte's work-related illness and death by illness or weapon are historical extreme examples, not forecasts.
- Social/relationship: Exploitation, lost work, and separation from dirty work.
- Events/manifestations: Secret tasks, dangerous work, work-related crime, and renunciation of hard work.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's fatal examples are historical extremes and do not determine the outcome of work difficulties.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through the demands and dangers of work. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes patient hidden work and secret tasks alongside lost work, crime, and renunciation of hard work; the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) adds exploitation and trouble from unfinished matters. Its illness and death examples remain severe historical associations, not a forecast for a person facing difficult employment.
