## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 225.

Melancholic disposition. To be depressed through terrible events. To resign. To become seriously ill. Great poverty. To suffer losses through diseases and hostility.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 356 (printed); PDF page: 177.

My tasks from the past, my severe deprivations, my secret problems, my deficient concentration, my disadvantages through separation, my hidden pain, inner grief and pain, inner need in old age.

Translation checked against the full exact German glyph row.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal experience of deprivation, loss, past burdens, and inner distress.
- Psychology: Melancholy, resignation, deficient concentration, and grief.
- Body/health: Serious illness and depression are historical source claims, not clinical assessments.
- Social/relationship: Disadvantages through separation and losses through hostility.
- Events/manifestations: Personal past tasks, secret problems, poverty, and inner need in old age.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries describe severe distress without requiring every manifestation or establishing a clinical diagnosis.

## Contradictions

The entries describe severe distress without requiring every manifestation or establishing a clinical diagnosis.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = MC`, MC specifies the pair through the inward and personal burden of difficult circumstances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes melancholy, resignation, poverty, and losses through illness or hostility; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal deprivations, secret problems, past tasks, and impaired concentration. Grief and separation remain central, with the severe illness and depression language retained as historical attribution.
