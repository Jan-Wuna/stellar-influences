## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 225.

News of a serious sickness or of a severe loss. Death notice. News ofcrime or of other evil deeds. News of the death.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 356 (printed); PDF page: 177.

Reports about need in old age, talk about loss risk, dangerous tasks in youth, dangerous traffic losses, severe deprivations in youth, opinions about problems of the past, and hidden traffic problems

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Information and discussion concern serious illness, loss, crime, or dangerous problems.
- Psychology: Concern about loss and opinions on past problems.
- Body/health: Serious illness and death are the subject of news in Witte, not diagnoses.
- Social/relationship: News about others' illness or death; no specific relationship outcome is supplied.
- Events/manifestations: Death notices, transport losses, and dangerous tasks or deprivations in youth.
- Conflicts/notes: The medical and death material concerns the content of news, not a diagnosis of the recipient.

## Contradictions

The medical and death material concerns the content of news, not a diagnosis of the recipient.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through communicating about hardship. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) centers on news of serious illness, severe loss, death, or crime; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends this to discussions of risk, past problems, transport difficulties, and deprivations in youth. The severe content of a message does not itself establish an event or condition in the recipient's life.
