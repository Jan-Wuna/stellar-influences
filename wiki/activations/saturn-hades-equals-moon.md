## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 225.

Serious ailment of a woman. To become a widow through serious ailment and death of the husband. People under foreign rule. Persons or wife far away.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 356 (printed); PDF page: 177.

Dangerous problems for the woman, severe deprivation for the woman, grief and pain of the woman, poor concentration of the woman, disadvantages by separation from the woman, need of an old woman, and hidden feelings problems

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Hardship, separation, or distance experienced through women, a people, or emotional life.
- Psychology: Grief, hidden feeling problems, and deficient concentration.
- Body/health: The historical severe-illness and widowhood claims are not diagnoses or predictions.
- Social/relationship: Widowhood, foreign rule, and persons or a wife far away in the historical source.
- Events/manifestations: Deprivation, need in old age, and disadvantages through separation from a woman.
- Conflicts/notes: The gender, illness, and widowhood examples remain historical attributions rather than universal roles or forecasts.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through grief, distance, and deprivation involving women or a people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes foreign rule, a person far away, and severe illness or widowhood examples; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds hidden emotional problems and disadvantage through separation. The gendered and medical vocabulary preserves the historical source range without establishing a person's role, illness, or future bereavement.
