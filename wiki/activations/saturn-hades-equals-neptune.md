## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 225.

Severe loss. Dismal fate. Floods. Losses at sea. Slow decay of the body (cancer).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 357 (printed); PDF page: 177.

Dissolution of past problems, burdening through dirty water, dangerous problems in the air, severe deprivations in water, concentration on secrets of metaphysics, grief and pain through weakness, hidden losses in water.

Translation checked against the full exact German glyph row.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Loss, weakness, and difficult water or air conditions, with a separate dissolution of past problems.
- Psychology: Grief through weakness and concentration on metaphysical secrets.
- Body/health: Witte's cancer wording is historical medical doctrine, not an established association or diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: The brief entries supply no specific relationship outcome.
- Events/manifestations: Floods, losses at sea, dirty-water burdens, and concealed water losses.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC includes dissolution of past problems; the severe medical and maritime examples remain separate possibilities.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through loss and weakness in uncertain or watery conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names floods and losses at sea, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds dirty-water burdens, air problems, hidden water losses, and concentration on metaphysical secrets. ABC also retains dissolution of past problems; Witte's cancer language remains historical medical doctrine rather than a diagnosis.
