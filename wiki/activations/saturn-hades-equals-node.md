## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 225.

To be tied up to loathsome, distasteful things. To come into contact with seriously ill persons or corpses. Being made aware of unfinished things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 356 (printed); PDF page: 177.

Encountering dangerous problems, a disturbed connection to the past, severe deprivations within a connection, grief through loss of a connection, deficient concentration in encounters, losses through criminal connections.

Translation checked against the full exact German glyph row.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Connections bring awareness of unfinished, dangerous, or distressing circumstances.
- Psychology: Grief at losing a connection and deficient concentration during encounters.
- Body/health: Contact with the seriously ill or corpses concerns encounters, not a medical condition inferred for the reader.
- Social/relationship: Deprivation within a connection and contact with serious illness or death.
- Events/manifestations: Losses through criminal connections and a disturbed link to the past.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's loathsome vocabulary is an attributed evaluation of circumstances, not a judgment of ill people.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Node`, Node specifies the pair through involvement in distressing circumstances and unfinished matters. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes contact with serious illness or death; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds deprived relationships, grief at losing a connection, and losses through criminal connections. A disturbed link to the past and poor concentration in encounters further locate the burden in contact itself.
