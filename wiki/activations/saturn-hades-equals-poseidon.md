## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 225.

To have or to find enemies on the outlook of the world. To be mistreated because of world viewpoint or belief.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 357 (printed); PDF page: 177.

Dangerous intellectual or spiritual disturbances, intellectual or spiritual deficiency in old age, cultural losses from the past, grief through loss of reason, problems through dangerous ideas, deficient concentration on truth, losses through intellectual or spiritual deficiency.

Translation checked against the full exact German glyph row.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Hostility around belief or worldview and difficulty with truth, reason, or cultural inheritance.
- Psychology: Dangerous intellectual or spiritual disturbances, deficient concentration, and grief over lost reason.
- Body/health: Historical intellectual or spiritual deficiency language does not establish a medical diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Mistreatment or enemies arising from worldview or belief.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural losses from the past and problems through dangerous ideas.
- Conflicts/notes: The deficiency language is historical evaluation, not a diagnosis or proof that any particular belief is false.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through the conflict surrounding worldview and understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names enemies or mistreatment because of belief; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds cultural losses, dangerous ideas, deficient concentration on truth, and grief through lost reason. Its intellectual or spiritual deficiency language remains attributed historical vocabulary, without diagnosing a person or judging a particular faith.
