## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 225.

Surprising money losses. Sudden demise after severe sickness.

The scan prints two variant Uranus glyphs before this row; the former leading `<?)` was an OCR remnant of the second glyph.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 357 (printed); PDF page: 177.

Obstacles through disturbed rhythm, concentration on technical secrets, insufficient concentration on reforms, disadvantages through sudden separation, nervousness through lack of concentration, and tension from a burdensome past.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 357, PDF p. 177.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden disruption or separation intensifies a difficult, burdensome situation.
- Psychology: Nervousness, disrupted concentration, and tension from the past.
- Body/health: Witte names sudden death after severe illness as a historical extreme, not a forecast.
- Social/relationship: Sudden separation appears in the verified German ABC entry.
- Events/manifestations: Unexpected financial loss, disturbed rhythm, technical investigation, and difficulties with reform.
- Conflicts/notes: The old English cache confused separation with relief and technical secrets with technical features; the scan supports the revised translation.

## Contradictions

The ABC entry focuses on disruption and concentration, while Witte supplies severe losses. Their different scales do not make the extreme examples a necessary expression of the formula. Both Uranus markers and the complete Witte row have been checked against PDF p. 225.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden disruption: a broken rhythm or abrupt separation adds strain to an already burdensome situation. The verified [ABC entry, printed p. 357](#abc-entry) connects that strain with nervousness, difficulty concentrating on reform, technical secrets, and a burdensome past.

The [Witte entry, PDF p. 225](#witte-entry) gives unexpected financial loss and a severe illness-related death example. These preserve the entry's range without making a financial or medical prediction.
