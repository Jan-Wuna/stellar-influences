## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 225.

Serious abdominal diseases. Premature birth. Miscarriage.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Tasks of art in distress, patient service in love, dangerous problems in love, lack of harmony in old age, criminal burdens on love, disadvantages and problems in love, problematic renunciation of love, and disturbances through lovesickness.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 356, PDF p. 177.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Love or artistic work bears deprivation, distress, or difficult service.
- Psychology: Lovesickness and painful renunciation coexist with patience in love.
- Body/health: Witte lists abdominal illness, premature birth, and miscarriage as historical medical associations.
- Social/relationship: Troubled love, reduced harmony, and burdens affecting affection.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic tasks in distress and sustained service within a difficult relationship.
- Conflicts/notes: The scan says renunciation of love, not forgiveness; it also names art rather than a specific artist.

## Contradictions

Witte's medical list and the ABC entry's relationship and art themes occupy different domains. Neither establishes that relationship distress causes any of the named medical conditions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Venus`, Venus specifies the Saturn/Hades axis through love and art under difficult conditions. The verified [ABC entry, printed p. 356](#abc-entry) joins patient service with relationship problems, loss of harmony, renunciation, and lovesickness: affection or creative work continues in the presence of distress, rather than guaranteeing relief from it.

The [Witte entry, PDF p. 225](#witte-entry) separately lists abdominal and reproductive conditions. Those historical medical claims remain source examples; they do not support diagnosing a person or predicting a pregnancy outcome from this formula.
