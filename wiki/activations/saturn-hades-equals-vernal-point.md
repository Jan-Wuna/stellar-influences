## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 225.

Sad parting. Fugitives. Famine. Scarcity of employment. Damage through frost. Severe damage or plagues.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 356; PDF p. 177.

dangerous problems in public, concentration on public distress, sorrow and pain in public life, poor concentration on the public, public dirt problems, and hidden public problems

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Hardship and deprivation become public or collective concerns.
- Psychology: The ABC entry directs attention toward public distress and insufficient attention to it.
- Body/health: Famine and plagues belong to Witte's historical collective examples, not an individual diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Displacement, separation, and scarcity of employment affect groups.
- Events/manifestations: Public suffering, hidden public problems, frost damage, and severe shortages.
- Conflicts/notes: Public distress is the common scope; the entries do not say that every public problem is a disaster.

## Contradictions

The entries share a public scale but differ in specificity: Witte lists concrete crises, while the ABC entry also covers attention to public and hidden problems. This is a difference of emphasis, not a documented contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Saturn/Hades axis through hardship in public life. The [ABC entry, printed p. 356](#abc-entry) centers on public distress, sorrow, neglected concerns, and hidden problems; the organizing issue is how suffering reaches, or escapes, collective attention.

The [Witte entry, PDF p. 225](#witte-entry) makes that scale concrete through displacement, unemployment, famine, frost damage, and plagues. These are historical examples within a broad public theme, not a claim that the formula predicts any particular crisis.
