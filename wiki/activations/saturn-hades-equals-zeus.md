## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 225.

Inherited diseases. To work with great deficit. War losses or injuries.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 357; PDF p. 177.

Effortful secret tasks, beginning of dangerous disturbances, concentration on dangerous targets, grief and pain through fire, shaping secret tasks, poor concentration performance, hidden procreation problems, and fire damage losses

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Difficult or concealed tasks demand effort while carrying danger, deficit, or damage.
- Psychology: Concentration on dangerous goals and difficulty sustaining effective concentration.
- Body/health: Witte's inherited-disease and injury language and the ABC entry's procreation problems are historical associations.
- Social/relationship: Witte includes losses or injuries in war, rather than a general statement about every conflict.
- Events/manifestations: Work with deficits, secret tasks, dangerous beginnings, and fire damage.
- Conflicts/notes: Secret work and fire are explicit examples; the entry does not equate all purposeful work with danger.

## Contradictions

Witte's inherited and wartime conditions and the ABC entry's secret tasks or fire damage are distinct manifestations. Their coexistence does not establish a single causal chain.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Hades = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the Saturn/Hades axis through demanding work or goals pursued under deficit and danger. The [ABC entry, printed p. 357](#abc-entry) links secret tasks and dangerous targets with the difficulty of concentrating well, while its fire passages give a concrete form of loss and pain.

The [Witte entry, PDF p. 225](#witte-entry) adds work at a great deficit and wartime loss or injury. Its inherited-disease example and the ABC entry's procreation reference remain historical medical claims, not diagnoses or outcomes that follow automatically from a difficult task.
