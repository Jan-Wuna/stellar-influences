## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 227.

Unsociable family life. An art which is maintained by government subsidies or which is not very lucrative. To be hindered by the authority in one’s artistic endeavor.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Losses of a community's reputation, shared losses through the state, major community problems, concentration on a particular community, mature authority within a community, loss of shared independence, and important community tasks.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 361, PDF p. 179.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared independence, reputation, or artistic activity meets institutional restriction.
- Psychology: Concentrated attention to a particular community and mature authority within it.
- Body/health: No specific health claim appears in these entries.
- Social/relationship: Unsociable family life, group problems, and loss of shared independence.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic obstruction, government subsidy, weak artistic earnings, and collective losses through the state.
- Conflicts/notes: The German distinguishes major community problems from the problems of a necessarily large community.

## Contradictions

Government subsidy, poor earnings, and official obstruction are separate artistic examples in Witte. The ABC entry's mature authority is not a promise that a community escapes the listed losses.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Kronos = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the Saturn/Kronos axis through a community, family, or artistic undertaking whose independence and support are under pressure. The [Witte entry, PDF p. 227](#witte-entry) names withdrawn family life and art affected by subsidy, poor earnings, or official obstruction; the verified [ABC entry, printed p. 361](#abc-entry) extends the theme to shared losses and reputation.

The ABC passage also retains concentrated attention, mature authority, and important community tasks. Those responses belong alongside the limitations rather than replacing them with a uniformly negative picture of group life.
