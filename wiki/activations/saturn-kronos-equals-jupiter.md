## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 227.

Money losses through loans. Credit taker. A fortune is diminished or hindered by the authority.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Particular joy in old age, particular concentration on money, financial problems as a self-employed person, money losses through the state, happiness through particular patience, problems of the constitutional state, state legal problems, and important legal tasks.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 360, PDF p. 179.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Money and legal affairs are shaped by institutional limits and the demands of independence.
- Psychology: Attention to money and patience that may bring happiness.
- Body/health: Old age is a setting for joy in the ABC entry, not a health condition.
- Social/relationship: Credit relationships, self-employment, and dealings with the state.
- Events/manifestations: Loan losses, diminished fortunes, state-related financial problems, and legal responsibilities.
- Conflicts/notes: Joy and happiness remain alongside losses; the entry does not establish a uniform financial outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte concentrates on financial loss, while the ABC entry includes joy, patience, and legal tasks. These differ in emphasis rather than asserting mutually exclusive outcomes for the same case.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Kronos = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the Saturn/Kronos axis through finance and legal responsibilities under institutional constraint. The [Witte entry, PDF p. 227](#witte-entry) gives loan losses, borrowing, and wealth diminished by authority; the verified [ABC entry, printed p. 360](#abc-entry) adds financial problems in self-employment, state losses, and important legal tasks.

The same ABC passage includes joy in old age and happiness through patience. The synthesis therefore concerns the demands of managing money and law within limits, while preserving both the loss examples and the possibility of satisfaction.
