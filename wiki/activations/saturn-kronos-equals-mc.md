## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 227.

To be obstructed in independence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My disturbed self-awareness, my particular patience, my great pain, my concentration on the particular, my problems with the state, my losses through the state, loss of my independence, and my important tasks.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 360, PDF p. 179.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Authority-related constraints are experienced as personal loss of independence and responsibility.
- Psychology: Disturbed self-awareness, patience, and concentration on particular matters.
- Body/health: Great pain is named without identifying a bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: The person's relationship to the state is central.
- Events/manifestations: Obstructed independence, state-related losses, and important personal tasks.
- Conflicts/notes: The scan resolves the damaged opening as disturbed self-awareness; it is not a diagnosis.

## Contradictions

Witte's short statement of obstructed independence and the ABC entry's first-person descriptions are compatible in scope. They do not determine whether every important task is imposed by the state.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Kronos = MC`, MC specifies the Saturn/Kronos axis as a personally experienced restriction of independence. The [Witte entry, PDF p. 227](#witte-entry) states that constraint directly; the verified [ABC entry, printed p. 360](#abc-entry) gives its inner language through disturbed self-awareness, pain, patience, and concentration.

State-related problems and losses place that experience in an institutional setting, while important personal tasks remain in view. The passage supports a tension between autonomy and responsibility, without defining the person's whole identity or establishing a clinical condition.
