## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 227.

To occupy one’s thoughts with serious things. Enforced settlement. Receivership.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 360; PDF p. 179.

Reports of loss of reputation, special patience with youth, serious efforts of youth, concentration of thinking on the special, reports of state losses, talk about great pain, and important tasks of youth

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Serious thought and communication concern institutional losses, authority, and demanding tasks.
- Psychology: Concentrated thinking, patience with youth, and serious effort.
- Body/health: Talking about pain is present; a specific illness is not.
- Social/relationship: Reports about the state and responsibilities involving young people.
- Events/manifestations: Enforced settlements, receivership, reputation-loss reports, and important youth tasks.
- Conflicts/notes: Legal-administrative outcomes in Witte are distinct from the ABC entry's broader thinking and reporting themes.

## Contradictions

The entries share seriousness of thought and official concerns. Reports of losses do not establish that the speaker personally suffers the reported loss or enters receivership.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Kronos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the Saturn/Kronos axis through serious thinking, reports, and administrative communication. The [Witte entry, PDF p. 227](#witte-entry) joins grave concerns with enforced settlement and receivership; the [ABC entry, printed p. 360](#abc-entry) broadens the subject matter to reputation, state losses, and discussion of pain.

Patience with youth, young people's serious efforts, and important tasks involving them are also explicit. They preserve a practical field for concentrated thought without turning every serious conversation into a legal or financial crisis.
