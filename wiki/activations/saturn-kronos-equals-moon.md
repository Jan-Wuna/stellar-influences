## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 227.

Depressed by losses. Psychic depression through the hindrances of authority. Impoverished independent woman or wife of an impoverished independent man. Nation or people oppressed and hindered through the authorities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A woman's loss of reputation, tasks of the authorities of a people, an especially patient people, a woman's great pain, concentration on a particular people, loss of a people's independence, and important tasks of a woman.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 360, PDF p. 179.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Losses and official hindrances affect emotional life, women in the source's framing, or a people collectively.
- Psychology: Depression after loss, patience, and pain are explicitly named.
- Body/health: These passages do not establish a specific bodily condition; their psychological language is historical.
- Social/relationship: Impoverished independent women, spouses, and peoples constrained by authorities.
- Events/manifestations: Reputation loss, lost collective independence, and important tasks involving women or public authority.
- Conflicts/notes: The German says tasks of a woman, not gifts; the gendered examples remain source-specific.

## Contradictions

The entries connect individual and collective hardship, but neither says that every woman's experience or every national restriction is emotionally identical. Their gendered and clinical formulations are preserved as historical claims.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Kronos = Moon`, Moon specifies the Saturn/Kronos axis through the emotional and collective experience of loss or official restriction. The [Witte entry, PDF p. 227](#witte-entry) connects losses and authority's hindrances with depression, then moves to impoverished women and oppressed peoples; the verified [ABC entry, printed p. 360](#abc-entry) adds patience, reputation loss, and a people's lost independence.

Important tasks also remain in the ABC passage. Its specifically female examples and Witte's clinical vocabulary are historical source formulations, not universal statements about women or diagnoses derived from the formula.
