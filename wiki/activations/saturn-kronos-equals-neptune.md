## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 227.

Fraud through impoverished corporations or governments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Particular patience in the air, particular concentration on water, great uncertainty through pain, problematic dissolution of reputation, loss of independence through weakness, dissolution of a state involving losses, and an important task of metaphysics.

Translation and paragraph boundary checked against the German scan: printed p. 361, PDF p. 179. The following growth and development clauses belong to the separate Pluto paragraph.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Institutional weakness, loss of standing, or dissolution creates uncertainty and dependence.
- Psychology: Uncertainty through pain and the patience required in an unsettled setting.
- Body/health: The scan establishes weakness and pain, not the cached claim of lost health.
- Social/relationship: Fraud by impoverished organizations or governments; independence compromised by weakness.
- Events/manifestations: Dissolving reputation or state structures, air and water concerns, and metaphysical work.
- Conflicts/notes: The English cache reversed loss-making dissolution into lossless dissolution and absorbed part of the Pluto paragraph; both are corrected from the scan.

## Contradictions

Witte's fraud is a concrete institutional example, while the ABC entry ranges from uncertainty to air, water, and metaphysics. The latter themes do not establish fraud in every case.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Kronos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the Saturn/Kronos axis through uncertainty, weakened independence, and the dissolution of standing or institutions. The verified [ABC entry, printed p. 361](#abc-entry) connects pain and weakness with losses; the [Witte entry, PDF p. 227](#witte-entry) gives fraud by impoverished organizations or governments as one concrete manifestation.

The ABC passage also retains patience in the air, concentration on water, and a task of metaphysics. Those unusual domains remain distinct; the growth and development wording that had spilled across the Pluto boundary supplies no meaning for this orientation.
