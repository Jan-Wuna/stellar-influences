## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 227.

Measures of education taken by the state, to submit to such measures.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Serious cultural authority, intellectual authority in old age, disturbed culture within the state, recognizing great tasks, concentration on the authority of truth, a state's problems with truth, loss of intellectual independence, and important intellectual tasks.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 361, PDF p. 179. The German term translated here as intellectual can also carry a spiritual sense.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Education, culture, and truth are subject to authority and constraints on intellectual independence.
- Psychology: Recognizing large tasks and concentrating on the authority of truth.
- Body/health: No health condition is stated.
- Social/relationship: Cultural or intellectual authority, state education, and submission to official measures.
- Events/manifestations: Disturbed state culture, educational intervention, lost intellectual independence, and important intellectual work.
- Conflicts/notes: The scan says authority rather than authorship, and disturbed rather than merely local culture.

## Contradictions

Witte describes submission to state educational measures; the ABC entry also values the authority of truth. This creates a possible tension between official and intellectual authority, without specifying their agreement or conflict in every case.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Kronos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the Saturn/Kronos axis through education, culture, and intellectual authority. The [Witte entry, PDF p. 227](#witte-entry) places a person under state educational measures; the verified [ABC entry, printed p. 361](#abc-entry) adds the authority of truth, disturbed culture within the state, and loss of intellectual independence.

The organizing tension is between the demands of official structures and the responsibility to recognize and pursue important intellectual or spiritual tasks. The entries do not decide whether a particular official measure serves or obstructs that responsibility.
