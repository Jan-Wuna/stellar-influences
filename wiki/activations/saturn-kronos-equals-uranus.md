## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 227.

Unexpected losses in securities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Agitating problems within the state, concentration on particular technology, new tasks for the boss, nervousness through great pain, sudden loss of authority, surprising loss of independence, and important technical tasks.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 361, PDF p. 179.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Abrupt institutional or financial changes disturb authority and independence.
- Psychology: Nervousness through pain and concentration on particular technology.
- Body/health: Pain is stated without a specified condition.
- Social/relationship: A boss faces new tasks; authority or independence may be lost suddenly.
- Events/manifestations: Unexpected securities losses, state problems, and important technical responsibilities.
- Conflicts/notes: The German names loss of authority, not merely loss of an authorization.

## Contradictions

Witte's securities losses are narrower than the ABC entry's political and technical themes. Important new tasks do not establish that all sudden changes are financial losses.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Kronos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the Saturn/Kronos axis through sudden losses or disruptive new demands on authority. The [Witte entry, PDF p. 227](#witte-entry) supplies unexpected securities losses; the verified [ABC entry, printed p. 361](#abc-entry) extends the disruption to authority, independence, and problems within the state.

Concentration on technology and new or important technical tasks also belong to the passage. They describe a field in which the disturbance is encountered or addressed, without implying that technical work itself must produce loss.
