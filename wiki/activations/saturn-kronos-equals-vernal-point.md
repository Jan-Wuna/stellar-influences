## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 227.

Poor government structures. Shutdown of government structures in public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Losses of reputation in public, particular patience in public life, serious authority in public life, great pain in public, losses of the state in public life, and important tasks for the public.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 360, PDF p. 179. The opening concerns reputation, not a stated loss of human lives.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Official losses and responsibilities become visible in public life.
- Psychology: Publicly required patience and seriousness under painful conditions.
- Body/health: Public pain is mentioned without identifying a medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Authority and state structures face public scrutiny and reputation loss.
- Events/manifestations: Poor or closed government structures, state losses, and important public tasks.
- Conflicts/notes: The scan corrects the cached opening from human losses to reputation losses.

## Contradictions

The entries share an official public setting. Witte's shutdown example does not establish that every public task or reputation loss entails institutional closure.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Kronos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Saturn/Kronos axis through the public consequences of official difficulties. The [Witte entry, PDF p. 227](#witte-entry) describes poor or closed government structures; the verified [ABC entry, printed p. 360](#abc-entry) adds public reputation loss, state losses, pain, and the patience required in public life.

Important tasks for the public remain alongside those difficulties. The focus is the visible burden of authority and institutional responsibility, without turning the former mistranslation of reputation loss into a claim about deaths.
