## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: PDF p. 227.

To be put on probation by the authorities or leading persons. To be guarded. To suffer losses. Deprived of personal freedom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

An influence of particular patience, power problems within the state, an authority's loss of power, concentration of state power, severe loss of reputation, disturbances through state force, and separation from state power.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 361, PDF p. 179.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: State or leading power restricts freedom, or authority itself loses power and standing.
- Psychology: The ABC entry retains the influence of patience under pressure.
- Body/health: The entries establish no specific bodily condition.
- Social/relationship: Supervision by authorities or leading persons, and separation from state power.
- Events/manifestations: Probation, guarded status, losses, concentration of power, and disturbances through state force.
- Conflicts/notes: Being subjected to power and an authority losing power are different positions within the entry.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses restraint imposed on a person; the ABC entry also describes a weakened authority. These are different manifestations, not interchangeable claims about who holds power.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Kronos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Saturn/Kronos axis through the exercise and loss of institutional power. The [Witte entry, PDF p. 227](#witte-entry) locates a person under official supervision or deprived of freedom; the verified [ABC entry, printed p. 361](#abc-entry) adds concentrated state power, force, and loss of an authority's power or reputation.

The formula therefore preserves both pressure exerted by authority and instability within authority itself. Patience remains a named influence, but the passages do not establish a necessary political outcome or identify every subject as either ruler or prisoner.
