## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 66.

#### Witte Entry

To be a teacher, to instruct. Necessary to separate often. To separate from many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 77; PDF p. 37. Translation checked against the German row.

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of my tasks, experiences of a mature soul, my success through concentration, my loss of freedom, my successes through patience, my concentration on knowledge, my problematic experiences, and my successes in separation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: teaching and knowledge developed through concentrated, patient experience.
- psychology: concentration on knowledge and endurance through problematic experiences.
- body/health: Neither entry gives a specific bodily claim.
- social/relationship: instructing others and repeated separation from many people.
- events/manifestations: expanding tasks, teaching, successes through patience, and loss of freedom.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes successful separation, whereas Witte stresses its repeated necessity.

## Contradictions

- Teaching and successful concentration do not remove the entries' themes of restriction and separation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/MC = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the Saturn/MC axis through teaching, accumulated experience, and concentrated knowledge. [Witte](#witte-entry) names teaching and instruction alongside repeated separation from many people. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds expanding tasks and success through concentration and patience, while retaining loss of freedom and problematic experiences. Learning and instruction therefore coexist with limits and separations in this formula.
