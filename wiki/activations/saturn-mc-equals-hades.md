## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 66.

#### Witte Entry

Grief through sickness or other causes. To be out of work. To cause enemies because of one’s own character.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 77; PDF p. 37. Translation checked against the German row.

#### ABC Entry

Concentration on soul-grief, my patience in need, my hard past, my concentration on the hidden, my lack of seriousness, my disadvantages through inhibitions, my soul-related problems, and my dangerous pains.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: grief and hardship involving the past, hidden matters, or material need.
- psychology: concentration on grief, patience in need, inhibitions, and lack of seriousness in ABC.
- body/health: Witte names sickness as a cause of grief; ABC names dangerous pains without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: enemies attributed by Witte to one's own character.
- events/manifestations: unemployment, grief, disadvantages through inhibitions, and attention to the hidden.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's lack of seriousness must not be turned into lack of empathy.

## Contradictions

- Witte's character-related enmity and ABC's patient endurance are distinct aspects of the entry.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/MC = Hades`, Hades specifies the Saturn/MC axis through grief, need, and burdens associated with the past or what is hidden. [ABC](#abc-entry) combines patience in hardship and concentration on concealed matters with inhibitions and soul-related problems. [Witte](#witte-entry) adds grief through sickness or other causes, unemployment, and making enemies through one's own character. The entry thus includes personal conduct as well as difficult circumstances.
