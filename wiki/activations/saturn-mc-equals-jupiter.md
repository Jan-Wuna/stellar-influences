## Identity

- Formula: `Saturn/MC = Jupiter`
- Astronomicon formula: `W/MC = V`

## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A change of residence. Constraining happiness. Happy separation. To lose of burden.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Lost Soul Peace, Happiness of a mature soul, Inner Happiness by Empathy, My Success by Perseverance, My Financial Loss, My Steady Happiness, My Joy in Old Age, My Right problems, and Soul Joy despite Pain

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Plain and simple, modest, contented. Clinging to hopes or trying to look on the good side of things. Changing fortunes, change of abode or residence.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Enthusiasm for careful and patient progress. A willingness to learn more about the difficulties encountered in spiritual growth and how they may be solved. A strong motivation to be disciplined and patient in one’s career.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Activates others to give you counsel and advice about your self-imposed limitations; advice on how to grow as a person; becoming an avid reader and student; feeling happiness about life’s progress; growing social acumen.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/MC = Jupiter`: sober leadership, trustworthy judgment, and expansion that can hold its shape over time. Main caution: moralized ambition: the wish to succeed correctly can become rigid, self-righteous, or too fearful of risk.
