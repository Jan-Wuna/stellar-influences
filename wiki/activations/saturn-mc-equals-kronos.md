## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 66.

#### Witte Entry

A sense of responsibility towards the family. Tax worries.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 77; PDF p. 37. Translation checked against the German row.

#### ABC Entry

Authority of a mature soul, my special duties, my great endurance, my important tasks, my great patience, my concentration on the boss, my problems with the state, self-confidence of an old soul, and priority soul-related tasks.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: important duties and personal responsibility involving family or authority.
- psychology: endurance, patience, concentration, and old-soul self-confidence in ABC.
- body/health: Neither entry gives a bodily claim.
- social/relationship: responsibility toward family and attention to a boss.
- events/manifestations: tax worries, state-related problems, and priority tasks.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's authority of a mature soul is a source metaphor, not proof of official rank.

## Contradictions

- Family obligations and public authority are complementary contexts rather than interchangeable identities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/MC = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the Saturn/MC axis through weighty duties and responsibility. [Witte](#witte-entry) locates these in responsibility toward family and worries over taxes. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds important tasks, endurance, patience, attention to a superior, and problems with the state. Its mature-soul authority and self-confidence sit beside these obligations, giving the formula both a responsible and a burdened expression.
