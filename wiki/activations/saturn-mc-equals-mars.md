### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `MC + Saturn`
- Source page: `66`
#### Witte Entry

To work slowly. Not being able to work properly. To terminate or interrupt work. Sorrows brought on by one’s own actions. Suffering at work.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Saturn/Medium Coeli`
- Source page: `263`
- Entry: `0931`

#### Ebertin Entry

Lack of initiative and enterprise, tendency to be docile or to give in. Feeling defenseless, suffering under oppression, need to obediently accept one's destiny, enforced separation, mourning and bereavement.
