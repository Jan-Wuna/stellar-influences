#### Munkasey Entry

Recognition for prior work well down; an enhanced reputation for work completed earlier; strengthens the respect others have for your abilities; you find confirmation from experiences in life for your convictions and goals.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: recognition of completed work and confirmation of goals.
- psychology: greater confidence in convictions through lived experience.
- body/health: The entry gives no bodily or medical claim.
- social/relationship: increased respect from others for one's abilities.

- conflicts/notes: The source prints well down; this apparent typo is retained in the quotation and does not support a separate meaning.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/MC = MC`, the repeated MC specifies the Saturn/MC axis through recognition of work already completed. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) links an enhanced reputation and others' respect for one's abilities with experience that confirms convictions and goals. The emphasis is on acknowledgment of prior accomplishment and firmer direction.
