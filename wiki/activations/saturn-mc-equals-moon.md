## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To avoid or to deny public appearance. To separate from others. The frame of mind due to the separation from a woman.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My autgae as a woman, my stiff people, my women's problems, my feelings problems, my wife's duties, my people's problems, my mature women's soul, my wife's pain, the woman's emotional pain, and my mother's loss

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Sadness or melancholy, impressionable, a person easily depressed, inclination to fall into states of depression or psychoses. Suffering from members of the female sex.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One finds security in a settled and certain career, but this can change to insecu rity eventually if it is not the right career for you. One is very impressionable to the ideas of people in authority.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

A restrictive but nurturing parent; stifled in your childhood; a family which dominates your early life; subordinating yourself to the needs of the family; traditional values with ordinary or plain starts; strict family values or ethics.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/MC = Moon`: endurance, responsibility in domestic and public rhythms, and the capacity to carry long-term obligations without spectacle. Main caution: discouragement: sadness or fatigue can make career and purpose feel joyless or isolating.
