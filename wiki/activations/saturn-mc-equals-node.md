## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 66.

#### Witte Entry

Burdening relationships. To suffer. To be united by suffering. To say good-bye. Disconnecting contacts or relationships.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 76; PDF p. 37. Translation and activator boundary checked against the German row.

#### ABC Entry

Endurance in my connections, my patience in connections, my inhibitions in encounters, my duties in connections, my problematic connections, soul-related tasks in connections, and connection with mature souls.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: code `0936`, printed p. 264; PDF p. 266.

#### Ebertin Entry

Sympathy and compassion. A link with others through bad circumstances or mourning. People in mourning.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. 96.

#### Sandbach Entry

The need to seek out connections with others who can help one become more in tune with one’s purpose in life. To feel a lack of respect coming from the people or groups with whom one is connected.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SATURN/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `304`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased struggles to succeed in life; the importance of working with the people who can offer help or assistance; a need to work along with others and to share accomplishments and goals thru them; dependency on people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: relationships carrying duties, shared difficulties, or support for purpose.
- psychology: patience, compassion, felt disrespect, and dependency.
- body/health: The entries establish no specific bodily condition.
- social/relationship: bonds through suffering or mourning, helpful contacts, shared goals, and disconnection.
- events/manifestations: duties in connections, group difficulties, goodbyes, and assistance with goals.
- conflicts/notes: Supportive and burdening connections remain possible within this orientation.

## Contradictions

- Compassion and helpful bonds do not erase the entries' relationship burdens and separations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/MC = Node`, Node specifies the Saturn/MC axis through consequential bonds with others. [ABC](#abc-entry) emphasizes endurance, patience, duties, and problems in connections. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) add relationships united by suffering, compassion, mourning, and separation.

Connections can also support purpose. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes seeking people who help one become more attuned to life's purpose, while acknowledging a felt lack of respect from a group. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds helpful people and shared goals alongside struggles and dependency. The relationship may carry both support and burden.
