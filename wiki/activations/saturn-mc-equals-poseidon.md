## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 66.

#### Witte Entry

To be an educator. To cause hindrances on the mental plane.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 77; PDF p. 37. Translation checked against the German row.

#### ABC Entry

Insights about soul and spirit, clarity of an old soul, concentration on the life-element (`Lebens-Element`), my spiritual tasks, my concentration on spirit, a mature spirit-soul, disturbances between soul and spirit, and separation from the life-element.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: education and concentration on soul and spirit, with mental hindrances.
- psychology: clarity and concentration alongside disturbances between soul and spirit.
- body/health: The entries give no specific bodily claim; life-element is undefined here.
- social/relationship: Witte names the role of educator.
- events/manifestations: spiritual tasks, teaching, and hindrances on the mental plane.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's Lebens-Element is retained literally without supplying an unsupported definition.

## Contradictions

- Witte's education and ABC's spiritual vocabulary overlap in mental work but do not establish identical doctrine.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/MC = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the Saturn/MC axis through education and concentrated attention to soul and spirit. [Witte](#witte-entry) names an educator and hindrances on the mental plane. [ABC](#abc-entry) joins clarity and spiritual tasks to disturbances between soul and spirit. The range includes the work of understanding and teaching, together with obstacles to that work; ABC's undefined life-element remains a source term.
