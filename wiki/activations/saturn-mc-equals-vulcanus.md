## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 66.

#### Witte Entry

Being guarded. To be severely inhibited. To slow a situation down.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 77; PDF p. 37. Translation checked against the German row.

#### ABC Entry

Power of an old soul, my energy losses, my strength of patience, my enormous problems, my concentration-energy, my mighty endurance, my strong inhibitions, mighty soul-related problems, and soul-strength despite pains.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: powerful endurance under severe inhibition or restraint.
- psychology: patience and concentrated strength alongside major problems.
- body/health: ABC names energy losses and pains; these are attributed motifs without a specified diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Witte describes being guarded without identifying a relationship.
- events/manifestations: slowing a situation, enduring major problems, and strong inhibitions.
- conflicts/notes: ABC preserves both energy loss and strength despite pain; neither cancels the other.

## Contradictions

- Forceful endurance and severe restriction coexist in these entries.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/MC = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Saturn/MC axis through severe inhibition and the strength required to endure it. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes being guarded and slowing a situation down. [ABC](#abc-entry) places powerful patience, concentration, and endurance beside energy loss, enormous problems, and strong inhibitions. Strength here can be expressed as sustained restraint even when pain and difficulty remain.
