## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 226; PDF p. 222.

#### Witte Entry

Subversive deeds. Unclean methods. Cunning acts behind someone’s back. Chronic sickness of the masses.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 351; PDF p. 174. Activator and translation checked against the German row.

#### ABC Entry

Extensive insecurity through pains, successful intuition in old age, serious knowledge of metaphysics, concentration on unknown knowledge, problematic expectations of success, and creeping losses in trade.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: uncertain expectations and knowledge, or concealed methods and wider losses.
- psychology: insecurity, intuition in old age, and concentration on unfamiliar knowledge.
- body/health: Witte's chronic sickness of the masses is a historical medical attribution; ABC names pains.
- social/relationship: Witte describes acts carried out behind another person's back.
- events/manifestations: metaphysical study, problematic success expectations, creeping trade losses, and covert deeds.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's serious metaphysical knowledge and Witte's dishonest methods should not be collapsed into one moral judgment.

## Contradictions

- The two entries differ in focus: study and intuition do not by themselves imply covert wrongdoing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Neptune = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the Saturn/Neptune axis through uncertain knowledge, expectations, and dealings that can have a wider reach. [ABC](#abc-entry) gives room to successful intuition in old age and serious metaphysical study, while retaining insecurity, problematic expectations of success, and gradual trade losses. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes concealed or dishonest acts and chronic sickness among the masses. These are distinct supported expressions; metaphysical study is not itself evidence of deception.
