## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 226; PDF p. 222.

#### Witte Entry

Sickness in the family. Derangement of conjugal conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 351; PDF p. 174. Activator and translation checked against the German row.

#### ABC Entry

burdened future of the community, patience of a metaphysical community, shared concentration on metaphysics, shared air problems, problematic expectations of a community, and problems for the shared future

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: family or community life burdened by uncertainty and shared problems.
- psychology: patience and troubled expectations concerning a common future.
- body/health: Witte names family sickness as a historical attribution; ABC gives shared air problems without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: disturbed conjugal conditions and difficulties within a community.
- events/manifestations: a burdened collective future, common problems, and shared metaphysical concentration.
- conflicts/notes: A metaphysical community is ABC's specific example; it is not interchangeable with every family or marriage.

## Contradictions

- The entries use different scales of shared life, from marriage and family to community.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Neptune = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the Saturn/Neptune axis through uncertainty and strain shared by a family or community. [ABC](#abc-entry) describes a burdened collective future, problematic expectations, and common air problems, alongside patience and shared concentration within a metaphysical community. [Witte](#witte-entry) adds sickness in the family and disturbed conjugal conditions. Family health language remains an attributed historical claim, while the broader theme is difficulty affecting a shared life.
