## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Infection of the ancestors, of the father. Chronic symptoms of dissolution in the government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `351`; PDF page: `174`.

Particular weakness of concentration, disappointing losses of the state, disturbed independence through uncertainty, great uncertainty through pain, great losses through weakness, and creeping losses of the state.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: weakening authority and continuing losses within the state.
- psychology: ABC emphasizes poor concentration and insecurity affecting independence.
- body/health: Witte's ancestral or paternal infection and ABC's pain language remain attributed claims.
- social/relationship: the father, government, and state are the named social settings.
- events/manifestations: chronic governmental dissolution and gradual state losses.
- conflicts/notes: State losses and personal independence are distinct settings; the entry does not identify a cause of decline in every case.

## Contradictions

- The institutional decline themes overlap; the short entries do not establish a distinct contradiction or independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies the Saturn/Neptune axis as an erosion of authority or independence. [Witte](#witte-entry) places chronic dissolution in government; [ABC](#abc-entry) places insecurity and gradual losses at the level of the state. This is an institutional emphasis rather than a general statement about every leader. Witte's parallel reference to infection in the father or ancestry remains a historical medical claim, not an explanation of the governmental imagery.
