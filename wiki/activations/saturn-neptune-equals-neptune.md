## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies the Saturn/Neptune axis through loosening rigid ideas and offering others an imagined view of reality or the future. In the sole [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry), artistic escape and inspiration belong to this same movement beyond fixed structures. His qualification that drug-related awareness is illusive limits the reading: an alternative vision is presented, but the entry does not establish its truth or lasting value. This remains the repeated-Neptune orientation.
