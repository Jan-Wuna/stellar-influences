## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Chronic hindrances in work. Infection of the progeny.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 351, supplied PDF p. 174, Saturn + Neptun, zeus row (six clauses). Translation from the German scan:

Beginning weakness of concentration; problems through fire-water (alcohol); creeping losses of performance; losses through weak leadership; painful expectations concerning procreation; goal-directed concentration on metaphysics.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: work and directed performance subject to continuing hindrance.
- psychology: weakening concentration, with determined metaphysical concentration also named.
- body/health: Witte's infection/progeny and ABC's painful procreative expectations remain attributed.
- social/relationship: ABC links losses with weak leadership.
- events/manifestations: gradual performance losses and alcohol-related problems in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: Losses through weak leadership are an ABC example; metaphysical concentration is not stated to remedy them.

## Contradictions

- Directed concentration and declining performance are alternatives in ABC, not evidence that metaphysical focus remedies the hindrance.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies the Saturn/Neptune axis through impaired work or directed performance. [Witte](#witte-entry) names chronic obstacles at work; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds weakening concentration, gradual performance loss, and losses through weak leadership. Its explicit 'fire-water' reference means alcohol in this entry. Determined concentration on metaphysics is also listed, but no remedy is promised. The reproductive and infection language remains the authors' historical attribution.
