## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Enduring and close connections. Connections with a few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 217, supplied PDF p. 107, Node + Saturn, admetos row (six clauses). Translation from the German scan:

A connection disturbed by depression; restriction and disturbances of a connection; end of a problematic connection; consolidation of a relationship in old age; patience and endurance in a relationship; thorough attachment to a task.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: few close ties that endure or become restricted.
- psychology: ABC relates depression to disturbance in a connection.
- body/health: depression is named by ABC; no physical condition is specified.
- social/relationship: lasting closeness, patience, and consolidation in later life.
- events/manifestations: restriction or ending of a problematic bond.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also names thorough attachment to a task; neither depth nor longevity of a relationship is guaranteed.

## Contradictions

- Enduring closeness and the end of a troubled connection are alternatives, not contradictory descriptions of every bond.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies the Saturn/Node axis through the narrowing and consolidation of connections. [Witte](#witte-entry) names close, enduring ties with a few people; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds patience and later-life consolidation alongside restriction, disturbance, and the end of a problematic bond. ABC also names thorough attachment to a task, extending the theme beyond interpersonal bonds. The same focus on a limited connection can therefore describe depth or constraint. The entry does not establish that every restricted bond should endure.
