## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Many are inhibiting. Breaking off of connections with many. Many part with something.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 217, supplied PDF p. 107, Node + Saturn, apollon row (seven clauses). Translation from the German scan:

Success through patience in a connection; success of a serious relationship; a serious connection to science; expansion of the tasks in a connection; disturbed trade relationships; concentrated connections to the economy; many problems in a relationship.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: serious connections extended across many people or professional tasks.
- psychology: ABC allows success through patience despite numerous problems.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is specified.
- social/relationship: many contacts, scientific ties, and commercial relationships.
- events/manifestations: parting with many or expanding tasks within a connection.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes disturbed trade relationships alongside patient success; neither outcome is guaranteed.

## Contradictions

- Witte emphasizes inhibition and separation among many; ABC also allows patient success. These are different outcomes, not universal agreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies the Saturn/Node axis through the scale and scope of connections. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes inhibition or separation involving many people; [ABC](#abc-entry) locates serious ties in science and the economy, with expanding tasks and possible success through patience. The connection is sustained involvement across a wider network, which may burden participants or support achievement. The formula does not require either successful expansion or separation.
