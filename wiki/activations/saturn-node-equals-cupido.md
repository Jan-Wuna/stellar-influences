## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Separation of a marriage or union. Old or permanent connection to an association, to family.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 217, supplied PDF p. 107, Node + Saturn, Cupido row (six clauses). Translation from the German scan:

Serious encounters in a community; shared attachment to a task; problems through contacts with a community; connections in a divided community; connection to an old community; a community's relationships involving losses.

The opening is Ernste Begegnungen (serious encounters), confirmed at high resolution; the earlier Erste/first-encounters warning was mistaken.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: continuing or separating bonds within family, marriage, or community.
- psychology: no distinct inner state is described.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is specified.
- social/relationship: old associations, family ties, and shared attachment to a task.
- events/manifestations: marital separation or losses affecting community relations.
- conflicts/notes: The actual scan says Ernste Begegnungen (serious encounters); losses and divided belonging remain alternatives.

## Contradictions

- Permanent affiliation and separation appear together in Witte; they are alternatives within group belonging, not a single assured outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies the Saturn/Node axis through the durability and disruption of belonging to a family or group. [Witte](#witte-entry) places an old or permanent association beside separation of marriage or union; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a shared task and relationships within an old or divided community. The common setting is a bond embedded in a larger association. Its persistence and its possible loss both remain visible, rather than treating all group ties as either secure or broken.
