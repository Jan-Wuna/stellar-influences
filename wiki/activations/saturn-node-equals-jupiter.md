## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Quick and pleasant separations. The separation of a happy union or satisfaction through such.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Responsibilities of a legal entity, successful attachment to a task, money difficulties in a relationship, legal problems in a connection, and advantage by patience in a connection

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Fondness of solitude, happiness in seclusion, the experience of inner joy through sacrificing oneself for others. A fortunate separation from a partner or from a place of occupation, a change of position.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A desire to understand and learn from one’s connections through recognizing their spiritual aspects. An enthusiasm for having deep connections with other people and/or groups.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Generous assistance from others when you require it; fortunate support and meetings with friends who are able to provide you with the assistance you need (if you can recognize and accept same); sincere help in finding yourself.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Node = Jupiter`: reliable networks, respected partnerships, and growth that comes through serious but constructive association. Main caution: formalized optimism: alliances can become status-bound, moralized, or too cautious to evolve flexibly.
