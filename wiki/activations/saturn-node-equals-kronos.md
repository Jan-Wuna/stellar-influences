## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `145` (entry start).

Disagreeableness through the chief. Restriction through the state. To be separated by the government or some superior power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `217`; PDF page: `107`.

Special tasks of a connection, disturbed connection with authority, inhibited independence in a relationship, loss of independence in a connection, a connection with the state that brings losses, and important connections in old age.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Relationships constrained by authority or loss of independence.
- psychology: Feeling restricted within a bond.
- body/health: No bodily claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Trouble with a superior or the state; important ties in old age.
- events/manifestations: Government-imposed separation and tasks imposed on a connection.
- conflicts/notes: Restriction and mature importance are distinct possibilities, not universal outcomes.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe constrained relationships; ABC adds independence and connections in old age without directly disputing Witte.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Node = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the relationship through authority: a superior, government, or other power can impose restrictions or separation. The difficulty concerns who controls the bond and how much independence remains within it ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also includes important connections in old age and special tasks belonging to a relationship, so the entry is broader than conflict with officials alone.
