## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `145` (entry start).

Death in the family or near surroundings. One’s own conduct leads to separations or estrangements.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `216`; PDF page: `107`.

Create encounters in old age, serious activity in a connection, separation actions in a connection, contacts through activity in old age, binding separation actions, and lossful actions in connections

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: `257`; entry code: `0907`.

Desire to gain release from emotional depression, suffering through others. Enforced separation, bereavement within the family.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: `92` (chapter start; exact entry page unrecorded).

Action is carried out to maintain old ways of being. The need to actively confront one’s fear of change, and to take the initiative to enter into new experiences.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SATURN/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `296`

#### Munkasey Entry

Inner anger associated with being unable to find stability in life’s patterns; established athletic contests or sporting events; irritation for what others are not able to contribute to you; hastily rejecting assistance from others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action that maintains, strains, or severs established ties.
- psychology: Anger at instability and fear of change.
- body/health: Emotional depression is source language; no physical condition is established.
- social/relationship: Rejecting help, estrangement, or enforced separation.
- events/manifestations: Bereavement is a historical attribution; sporting contests are a separate Munkasey example.
- conflicts/notes: Active resistance to change and initiative toward new experience are alternatives.

## Contradictions

The entries vary between separation, maintenance of old patterns, and initiative toward change. These are different responses to strain rather than a demonstrated contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Node = Mars`, Mars makes the strain in a connection active: conduct can bring estrangement, anger can lead to rejected help, and separation can be enforced. The entries also describe acting to preserve familiar arrangements or taking the initiative to confront fear of change; action does not have a single direction here ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)). Munkasey's established sporting contests and ABC's activity and contacts in old age remain specific applications. References to death or bereavement belong to the historical entries and do not establish a prediction.
