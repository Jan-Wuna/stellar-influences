## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Death in the family or near surroundings. One’s own conduct leads to separations or estrangements.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Create encounters in old age, serious activity in a connection, separation actions in a connection, contacts through activity in old age, binding separation actions, and lossful actions in connections

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Desire to gain release from emotional depression, suffering through others. Enforced separation, bereavement within the family.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Action is carried out to maintain old ways of being. The need to actively confront one’s fear of change, and to take the initiative to enter into new experiences.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Inner anger associated with being unable to find stability in life’s patterns; established athletic contests or sporting events; irritation for what others are not able to contribute to you; hastily rejecting assistance from others.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Node = Mars`: disciplined collective effort, loyal struggle, and the ability to endure hard conditions with others. Main caution: combative attachment: resentment or defensive force can make relationships feel like contests or burdens.
