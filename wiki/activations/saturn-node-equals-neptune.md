## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `145` (entry start).

To be separated through sickness or falsehood. Dissolution of a disturbed connection.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `217`; PDF page: `107`.

Dissolution of a disturbed connection, concentration on a future connection, problems involving water, painful deception in a connection, separation of an unclear relationship, and uncertainty through the separation of a connection.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: `257`; entry code: `0910`.

The feeling of being neglected by others, being misunderstood or being lonely. Emotional suffering through falsehood, deception, fraud, separation, illness.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: `92` (chapter start; exact entry page unrecorded).

To be able to intuitively perceive the limitations one encounters when connecting. One’s ideal is to connect deeply to others. Confusion as to the problems encountered when one connects.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SATURN/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `296`

#### Munkasey Entry

Inspiration from others as a form of assistance toward finding your path or goals in life; deceit with friendship given; having to pay a price for help which is more than you expected; friends who deceive or confuse you.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertainty, deception, or dissolution within a troubled connection.
- psychology: Feeling neglected or misunderstood; intuitive awareness of relational limits.
- body/health: Illness is a historical reason for separation in the entries, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Confusing or deceptive friendship and unexpected costs of help.
- events/manifestations: Separation, dissolution of a disturbed bond, and problems involving water.
- conflicts/notes: Inspiration and deep connection remain possible alongside confusion; an uncertain bond is not necessarily deceptive.

## Contradictions

The entries include inspiration as well as deception; they do not demonstrate that every uncertain connection is false.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Node = Neptune`, Neptune specifies uncertainty within a strained bond: its limits may be hard to understand, help may conceal an unexpected cost, and deception or illness may separate people. Feeling neglected or misunderstood is part of that experience ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)). The entries also allow intuitive recognition of limits, a wish for deep connection, and inspiration received from others ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)). These possibilities do not erase the explicit dissolution and confusion motifs or turn historical illness references into medical conclusions.
