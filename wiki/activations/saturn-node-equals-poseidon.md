## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `145` (entry start).

Separation of unions or connections through mental differences. To deal with educational matters. Through such matters connected with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `217`; PDF page: `107`.

Tasks in a spiritual relationship, attachment to a spiritual task, insightful patient connection, serious attachment to ideas, clarity in a serious relationship, conveying cultural tasks, and painful truth in a connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections shaped by ideas, education and difficult truth.
- psychology: Insight, patience and serious attachment to ideas.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Educational concerns can unite people; differences of mind can separate them.
- events/manifestations: Educational and cultural work, spiritual tasks and separation through mental differences.
- conflicts/notes: Shared ideas and clarity do not exclude painful truth or disagreement.

## Contradictions

The entries describe both connection through shared concerns and separation through differences. These are conditional alternatives, not directly opposing claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Node = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies a connection through what people understand and believe: educational matters can bring them together, while differences of mind can separate them ([Witte](#witte-entry)). [ABC](#abc-entry) adds patient insight, spiritual or cultural tasks, and clarity within a serious relationship. The same bond may require facing a painful truth; seriousness of purpose does not ensure agreement.
