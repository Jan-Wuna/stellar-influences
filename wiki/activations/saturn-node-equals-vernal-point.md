## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `145` (entry start).

General separations. The loss of connection to public life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `216`; PDF page: `107`.

Burdensome encounters in public, disturbed connections in public life, separations of connections in public, connection with the public in old age, and losing connections to public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Disturbed or lost connections with public life.
- psychology: No specific inner state is described in these entries.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Burdensome public encounters and impaired public connections.
- events/manifestations: Separation from public life; ABC also includes public connection in old age.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's old-age connection broadens the range beyond loss; it does not guarantee continued involvement.

## Contradictions

Both entries include separation from public life. ABC also names connection with the public in old age, a different condition rather than an explicit denial of separation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Node = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the connection in its public setting: encounters may become burdensome, ties to public life disturbed, or involvement lost through separation ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also includes contact with the public in old age, so the entry spans both troubled participation and continuing connection. It supplies no specific psychological or health conclusion.
