## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `145` (entry start).

Waiting for an event. To feel depressed through separation from a union.. To be under supervision (control) or to supervise something.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `217`; PDF page: `107`.

Binding through strong concentration, energy losses in a relationship, the inhibiting influence of a connection, a severely disturbed relationship, strength through bonding to a task, separation of an influential connection, and connection through enormous tasks.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Concentration, control and the demanding influence of a connection.
- psychology: Waiting and distress through separation; strength found in commitment to a task.
- body/health: ABC names energy loss; Witte's depression wording supplies no medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Supervision, inhibited relationships and separation from an influential connection.
- events/manifestations: Waiting for an event, undertaking enormous tasks and exercising or receiving control.
- conflicts/notes: A demanding bond may strengthen task commitment or drain energy; these are distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

Strength through commitment and energy loss describe different effects of a demanding connection. Neither source makes one effect universal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Node = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the bond through concentrated demands and influence: a connection can inhibit or drain a person, while commitment to a substantial task can supply strength ([ABC](#abc-entry)). [Witte](#witte-entry) adds waiting, supervision and distress after separation. The common concern is how much force a connection exerts and how that force is experienced, whether as control, a difficult loss or the ability to carry a demanding task. The historical depression and energy language does not establish a health diagnosis.
