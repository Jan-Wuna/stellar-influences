## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `145` (entry start).

To take leave from the parents or from the husband. Forced separation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `217`; PDF page: `107`.

Efforts in a patient connection, beginning of a serious connection, problematic procreation connection, mature achievements of a connection, separation of a creative connection, and lossful goal striving in a relationship

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Effort, achievement and separation within a serious or creative connection.
- psychology: Patient effort and pursuit of goals appear; no fuller inner portrait is supplied.
- body/health: ABC's problematic procreation is a historical attribution, not a fertility diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Creative ties and separation from parents or a husband are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Beginning a serious connection, mature achievements and forced separation.
- conflicts/notes: Productive effort can coexist with loss; the entries do not identify a guaranteed result.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses separation; ABC also includes beginnings and mature achievements. These are different phases or possibilities, not an explicit disagreement about the same circumstances.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Node = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the connection through what it undertakes and produces: patient effort may form a serious bond and bring mature achievements, while striving can also incur losses or end a creative tie ([ABC](#abc-entry)). [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes taking leave and forced separation. Together the entries keep accomplishment and the cost of separation in view; the historical procreation and family examples remain particular attributions rather than assured outcomes.
