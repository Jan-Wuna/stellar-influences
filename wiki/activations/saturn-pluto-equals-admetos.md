## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `223` (entry start).

To get into a tight spot slowly. A slowly developing separation, which leads into an unmanageable uncompromising opposition. Separation for ever.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `353`; PDF page: `175`.

Blocked development in old age, developing firmness and concentration, disturbed circulatory development, thorough maturation, changes through severe restriction, changes involving loss through depression, and deepening of a painful development.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Gradual development constrained by increasing restriction or entrenched opposition.
- psychology: Concentration and firmness; ABC also names depression in its historical vocabulary.
- body/health: Disturbed circulatory development and deepening pain are historical ABC claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Slowly developing separation can harden into uncompromising opposition.
- events/manifestations: Blocked development, severe restriction and lasting separation.
- conflicts/notes: Thorough maturation remains in ABC alongside blockage; the entries give no assured outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes an increasingly unmanageable separation. ABC includes firmness and maturation as well as blocked or painful development; this broadens the range without directly disputing Witte's example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Pluto = Admetos`, Admetos specifies development that becomes tightly restricted: a separation can harden slowly into uncompromising opposition, while other changes are blocked or shaped by severe limits ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also retains firmness, concentration and thorough maturation, so restriction is accompanied by a narrower possibility of solid development. Its circulatory, pain and depression language belongs to the historical entry and does not establish a medical conclusion.
