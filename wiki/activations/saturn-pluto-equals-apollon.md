## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `223` (entry start).

Tiny beginning develops into something great. Method developed slowly. Building up a system.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `353`; PDF page: `175`.

Spread of painful development, development of experience in old age, successful development through patience, successful change of tasks, concentration on knowledge development, and problematic changes in the business sector

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Slow development that expands into a method, system or wider field.
- psychology: Patience and concentration on developing knowledge.
- body/health: Painful development is mentioned without a specific bodily condition.
- social/relationship: No distinct interpersonal claim appears in these entries.
- events/manifestations: Building a system, changing tasks successfully and changes in business.
- conflicts/notes: Growth and success coexist with the spread of painful developments; expansion is not necessarily beneficial.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes growth and method; ABC also includes painful development and problematic business changes. These qualify the range of expansion rather than directly denying the possibility of growth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Pluto = Apollon`, Apollon specifies development through its increasing extent and organization: a small beginning can grow into a system, with a method built gradually through patience and concentration ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). Experience, knowledge and successful changes of task belong to this process. ABC also includes the spread of painful developments and problematic business changes, so increasing scope does not by itself establish a favorable outcome.
