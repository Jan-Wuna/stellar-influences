## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `223` (entry start).

Others fit themselves, suit themselves, only very slowly. Slow separation from others. The beginning of a separation which lasts for some time. Relations to quite different persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `352`; PDF page: `175`.

Concentration on the development of a place, maturation with the partner, painful development with the partner, disturbances through a change of partner, change of environment in old age, a change of place involving loss, and changing tasks of the partner.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: `254`; entry code: `0900`.

Being placed in cumbersome and difficult circumstances. Separation, mourning and bereavement.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: `91` (chapter start; exact entry page unrecorded).

One may come across to others as a deeply sincere person, and/or one full of spiritual power. At worst, one who is strongly identified with having power and holding onto it tightly, and who may associate themselves with tradition, and do so in an extremely rigid manner.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SATURN/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `292`

#### Munkasey Entry

Concentration upon important matters which lie before you; assessments of how to work with those who have power or influence; an environment which gives you the impression of silent power or continuing influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult, gradual adjustment involving other people and one's environment.
- psychology: Sincerity or rigid identification with power; concentration on important matters.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is described in these entries.
- social/relationship: Slow separation, painful development with a partner and dealings with influential people.
- events/manifestations: Changes of partner or place, difficult circumstances and an environment of continuing influence.
- conflicts/notes: Spiritual power is Sandbach's framework; mourning and bereavement remain historical examples.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize slow adjustment, difficult circumstances and the impression of power in different ways. Sandbach's sincere and rigid presentations are alternatives, not a claim that every person displays both.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Pluto = Asc`, Asc specifies the development through encounters and surroundings: adjustment to other people may be slow, partnership changes painful, and separation prolonged ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). Influence is another part of that setting, from assessing how to work with powerful people to inhabiting an environment of silent, continuing power ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)). [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes how this can be perceived as sincerity or spiritual power, or at its worst as a rigid hold on power and tradition. The entries support this range of difficult adjustment and presentation, with [Ebertin's](#ebertin-entry) bereavement examples retained as historical attributions.
