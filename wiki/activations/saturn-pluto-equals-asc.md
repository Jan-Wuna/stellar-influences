## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others fit themselves, suit themselves, only very slowly. Slow separation from others. The beginning of a separation which lasts for some time. Relations to quite different persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

maturation development with the partner, painful development with the partner, changes in partners, changes in the environment in old age, change in places, and changing tasks of the partner

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Being placed in cumbersome and difficult circumstances. Separation, mourning and bereavement.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One may come across to others as a deeply sincere person, and/or one full of spiritual power. At worst, one who is strongly identified with having power and holding onto it tightly, and who may associate themselves with tradition, and do so in an extremely rigid manner.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SATURN/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `292`

#### Munkasey Entry

Concentration upon important matters which lie before you; assessments of how to work with those who have power or influence; an environment which gives you the impression of silent power or continuing influence.
