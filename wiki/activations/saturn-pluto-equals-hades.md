## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `223` (entry start).

Hindered development through malice or poor conditions. Increasing malice and villainess. Slow adaptation destroyed through enmity and vile actions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `353`; PDF page: `175`.

serious change due to deprivation, difficult development in old age, dangerous pain development, problematic development from the past, problems of hidden weakness, maturity development due to hardship, and growing losses due to criminality

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development obstructed or made costly by poor conditions, deprivation or harmful actions.
- psychology: Malice and enmity describe source-attributed conduct; no fixed personality diagnosis is established.
- body/health: ABC's pain and weakness language is historical and supplies no diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Hostility can destroy slow adaptation; criminality is one attributed source of loss.
- events/manifestations: Difficult development in old age, deprivation, losses and maturation through hardship.
- conflicts/notes: Hardship can accompany maturation as well as damage; the entries do not guarantee either outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses obstruction by conditions or hostile acts. ABC adds maturation through hardship alongside loss and difficulty, broadening the range without directly denying Witte's account.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Pluto = Hades`, Hades specifies development under damaging conditions: deprivation, hidden weakness or hostile acts can obstruct change and undo a slow adjustment ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also includes maturity gained through hardship, so difficulty can shape development as well as impair it. The entries give concrete possibilities of loss and difficult aging; their pain, weakness and criminality claims remain attributed source examples, with no basis for diagnosing a condition or assigning guilt.
