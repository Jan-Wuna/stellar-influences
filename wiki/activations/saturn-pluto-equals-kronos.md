## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `223` (entry start).

Personal experience through losses. You learn from your mistakes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `353`; PDF page: `175`.

Development of reputation and maturity, development of seriousness and self-confidence, patient development toward authority, great development of pain, problematic changes in the state, changes of tasks through independence, and growing state losses.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Experience and maturity developed through difficult change and loss.
- psychology: Learning from mistakes; growing seriousness and self-confidence.
- body/health: ABC names pain without a specific bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Reputation, authority and independence are affected by development.
- events/manifestations: Changed tasks, problematic state changes and losses at personal or state level.
- conflicts/notes: Patient growth toward authority is distinct from an assertion of authoritarian conduct.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes learning through loss; ABC extends development to reputation, authority and the state. These emphases are compatible without establishing that every loss produces maturity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Pluto = Kronos`, Kronos specifies development through experience, standing and authority. Loss and mistakes can become material for learning, while patience is involved in growing maturity, self-confidence and authority ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also places difficult change at the level of the state and links independence with changing tasks. The entry thus includes both personal maturation and costly changes in larger structures, without making hardship an assurance of growth.
