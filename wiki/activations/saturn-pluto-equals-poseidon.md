## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `223` (entry start).

Development of educational principles. Separation in educational matters, in matters of faith in matters of concept of life. Slow mental separation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `353`; PDF page: `175`.

Spiritual development in old age, spiritual developmental disruptions, developing spiritual maturity, alteration of cultural tasks, changes through patience and reason, changes through loss of truth, and growing mental disturbances.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Gradual change in principles, beliefs and spiritual or mental development.
- psychology: Patience and reason; disturbed development and loss of truth also appear.
- body/health: ABC's mental-disturbance wording is historical and gives no diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Separation over education, faith or one's conception of life.
- events/manifestations: Developing educational principles, changing cultural tasks and slow mental separation.
- conflicts/notes: Maturation and disruption are alternatives within the entries; neither is assured.

## Contradictions

Witte describes development and separation in matters of belief; ABC includes spiritual maturity and disturbance. These cover different trajectories without directly disagreeing about the same circumstances.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Pluto = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies development through principles and understanding: educational ideas, faith and one's conception of life can develop or separate slowly ([Witte](#witte-entry)). [ABC](#abc-entry) extends this to spiritual maturity and changing cultural tasks, with patience and reason supporting change. It also retains disturbed development and loss of truth. The central concern is alteration in what gives life intellectual or spiritual direction; the historical disturbance language establishes no clinical conclusion.
