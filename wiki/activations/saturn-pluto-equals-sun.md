## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Days without progress. Hindered physical development. The man who is slowly separated.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Gcdulds Development a Person, corperlichc Maturity Development, Human Maturity Development, personal development of age, personal loss through separation, problematic transformation of cincs Mans, and change through separation from the centre

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Physical toil and over-exertion, sparing no pains in one's work. Renunciation, privation, physical separation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A very rigid ego structure may encase the person, who will draw to himself circumstances which will tear this down and transform it so that he may reflect more of the Spiritual Light.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Dominating others thru a strong will and the determination to be better thru competent application; leadership based upon effectiveness of plans and thoroughness of preparation; important denials of extremes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Pluto = Sun`: formidable will, austere leadership, and the strength to remain purposeful under heavy strain. Main caution: hardness of identity: control and duty can become so total that spontaneity, trust, or flexibility are treated as weakness.
