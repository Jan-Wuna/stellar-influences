## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `223` (entry start).

Days without progress. Hindered physical development. The man who is slowly separated.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `352`; PDF page: `175`.

Development of patience in a person, physical maturation, developing human maturity, personal development in old age, personal loss through separation, problematic transformation of a man, and change through separation from the centre.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: `254`; entry code: `0891`.

Physical toil and over-exertion, sparing no pains in one's work. Renunciation, privation, physical separation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: `91` (chapter start; exact entry page unrecorded).

A very rigid ego structure may encase the person, who will draw to himself circumstances which will tear this down and transform it so that he may reflect more of the Spiritual Light.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SATURN/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `292`

#### Munkasey Entry

Dominating others thru a strong will and the determination to be better thru competent application; leadership based upon effectiveness of plans and thoroughness of preparation; important denials of extremes.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal development tested by slow progress, effort and separation.
- psychology: Patience, strong will and a potentially rigid sense of self.
- body/health: Physical toil, over-exertion and hindered physical development are historical source claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Dominance or leadership based on preparation; personal loss through separation.
- events/manifestations: Delayed progress, privation, maturation and consequential work plans.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's transformation of ego toward Spiritual Light is a spiritual interpretation; no result is assured.

## Contradictions

Slow or hindered development and mature, effective application describe different possibilities. Sandbach supplies a spiritual account of rigid identity being transformed, without directly disputing the other manifestations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Pluto = Sun`, Sun specifies development at the personal and bodily level: progress can be slow, work demanding and separation costly, while patience and maturity develop through the process ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry)). Strong will can be expressed through dominance or through leadership grounded in effective plans and thorough preparation ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)). [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes rigid identity being broken down in a spiritual transformation. These entries connect personal effort with difficult change, with bodily and spiritual claims remaining attributed rather than guaranteed.
