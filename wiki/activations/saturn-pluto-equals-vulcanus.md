## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `223` (entry start).

To slowly get into mighty dependence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `353`; PDF page: `175`.

Change of influence through separations, unfolding energy in old age, development of violence and hardness, disturbed development of strength, developing concentrated power, strong development of pain, severe disturbances of transformation, and a change of power involving loss.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development of powerful dependence or concentrated influence under difficult change.
- psychology: Hardness is attributed; no fuller inner response is specified.
- body/health: Energy, strength and pain are source language without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Dependence and influence can change through separation or shifts of power.
- events/manifestations: Concentration of power, disturbed development and costly changes of control.
- conflicts/notes: Violence and severe pain are historical examples; the entry does not make them inevitable.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes increasing dependence; ABC includes concentrated power as well as impaired strength and loss. These describe different positions within powerful change, without a direct disagreement established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Pluto = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies development through force and dependence: one can gradually come under a powerful influence, while separation or a change of power alters how that influence is held ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC includes concentrated power and energy developed in old age, but also impaired strength and severe disturbances of transformation. The common concern is the development and cost of power; violence and pain remain attributed possibilities rather than predictions.
