## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `223` (entry start).

One’s accomplishments increased only by difficulty and slowly.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `353`; PDF page: `175`.

Effortful development in old age, beginning development of pain, developing serious efforts, development of creative tasks, problematic change of leadership, developing mature achievements, disturbances at the beginning of a development, and growing losses through fire.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Accomplishment develops slowly through serious effort and difficulty.
- psychology: Serious effort is named; no fuller individual psychological portrait is supplied.
- body/health: ABC names beginning pain without a specific diagnosis.
- social/relationship: A problematic change of leadership is one explicit setting.
- events/manifestations: Creative tasks, mature achievements, disturbed beginnings and losses through fire.
- conflicts/notes: Achievement and loss remain different possibilities; slow progress is not proof of eventual success.

## Contradictions

Both entries link development with effort and difficulty. ABC adds particular disruptions and losses without directly denying the possibility of increasing accomplishment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Pluto = Zeus`, Zeus specifies development through accomplishment: performance increases slowly and with difficulty, while serious effort develops creative tasks and mature achievements ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). The process can be disturbed at its beginning or complicated by a change of leadership. ABC's pain and fire-loss examples preserve the costly side of development; they do not establish an inevitable event or bodily outcome.
