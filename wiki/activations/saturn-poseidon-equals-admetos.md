## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `231` (entry start).

End of training or education.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `369`; PDF page: `183`.

Enduring patience and reason, blockage through mental disturbances, an ending through reasonable separation, consolidation of spiritual maturity, thorough cultural tasks, clarity through thorough concentration, deeper involvement in spiritual tasks, and deeper involvement in cultural problems.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Completion or consolidation in education and spiritual or cultural tasks.
- psychology: Enduring patience, reason and thorough concentration.
- body/health: ABC's mental-disturbance wording is historical and supplies no diagnosis.
- social/relationship: A reasonable separation can bring an ending; no particular relationship is specified.
- events/manifestations: End of training, consolidated maturity and deeper engagement with cultural problems.
- conflicts/notes: Completion and deepening are distinct possibilities; blockage is also explicitly retained.

## Contradictions

Witte gives completion of education; ABC includes both an ending and a deepening of cultural or spiritual work. These are different applications without an explicit disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Poseidon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies educational and cultural work through completion and consolidation. Training can end, while maturity and understanding become firmer through patience, reason and thorough concentration ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also describes deeper involvement in spiritual tasks or cultural problems, alongside blockage and an ending reached through reasonable separation. The entries therefore allow both closure and sustained deepening; their historical mental-disturbance language establishes no diagnosis.
