## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `231` (entry start).

To be a model to others mentally. Another person is a model to us. To be mentally separated from others in one’s outlook on life, to be different.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `368`; PDF page: `183`.

patience and reason of the partner, spiritual tasks of a place, spiritual maturity of the partner, disturbed environmental culture, problems with the truth of the partner, recognize the difficulties of the other, environmental cultural duties, and rational separation from the partner

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Outlook, maturity and truth encountered through other people and one's setting.
- psychology: Patience, reason and recognizing another person's difficulties.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Being a mental model for another, following their example or separating over outlook.
- events/manifestations: Cultural tasks of a place and rational separation from a partner.
- conflicts/notes: Another person's example may inspire or differ from one's own outlook; no single relationship outcome is established.

## Contradictions

Witte describes mutual example and mental separation; ABC adds understanding and rational separation. These are different conditions within encounters, not direct disagreements.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Poseidon = Asc`, Asc specifies the outlook through another person: one can offer a mental example, take another as a model, or remain separated by a different conception of life ([Witte](#witte-entry)). [ABC](#abc-entry) adds patience, reason and recognizing the other's difficulties, while preserving problems with truth and rational separation from a partner. The setting matters too through its cultural tasks. The entries describe how understanding is tested in an encounter, with both example and distance possible.
