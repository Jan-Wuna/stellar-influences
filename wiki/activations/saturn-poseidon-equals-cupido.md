## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `231` (entry start).

Community education. Mental or world-philosophical differences in a community or family. Conduct, behavior or attitude of a community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `369`; PDF page: `183`.

Tasks of a spiritual community, development of a cultural community, a serious spiritual community, patience and reason in a community, a mentally disturbed community, shared spiritual tasks, and concentration on spiritual synthesis.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared education, cultural work and the outlook of a community.
- psychology: Patience and reason are named at the group level.
- body/health: ABC's disturbed-community wording is historical; it is not a clinical assessment.
- social/relationship: Shared spiritual tasks coexist with differences of worldview in a family or community.
- events/manifestations: Community education, cultural development and efforts toward spiritual synthesis.
- conflicts/notes: Common tasks do not establish common belief; differences within the group remain explicit.

## Contradictions

The entries include shared education and tasks alongside philosophical differences or disturbance. These are different conditions of community life rather than a direct disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Poseidon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies intellectual and spiritual concerns through a shared setting: a community educates its members, takes on cultural or spiritual tasks and develops a collective attitude ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). Patience and reason can support the attempt at synthesis, while differences of worldview can remain within the family or community. The common work therefore does not imply agreement; ABC's disturbed-community language remains a historical attribution.
