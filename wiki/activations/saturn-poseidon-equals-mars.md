## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `231` (entry start).

The model activity. Activity in educational matters. Hindrances, interruptions or disturbances in such a field.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `368`; PDF page: `183`.

Patience and reason in work, serious mental activity, working on mental tasks, actions of a disturbed mind, hard cultural work, concentrated cultural efforts, maturity through spiritual work, and reasonable efforts toward separation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Serious effort in educational, intellectual or cultural work.
- psychology: Patience, reason and concentration; ABC also attributes actions to a disturbed mind.
- body/health: Disturbed-mind wording is historical and establishes no diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Educational activity and reasonable efforts toward separation are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: Demanding cultural work, maturation through spiritual work and interruptions of educational activity.
- conflicts/notes: Purposeful work and disturbance are distinct possibilities; the entries do not identify every interruption as personal impairment.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses educational activity and its interruptions; ABC describes sustained effort as well as disturbed action. These are different conditions of work rather than a direct disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Poseidon = Mars`, Mars specifies intellectual and cultural concerns through active work: serious tasks require patience, reason and concentrated effort, and can contribute to maturity ([ABC](#abc-entry)). [Witte](#witte-entry) places this activity in education while retaining hindrances and interruptions. ABC also includes reasonable efforts toward separation, so action may concern ending an arrangement as well as carrying a task forward. Its disturbed-mind example remains a historical attribution, not an explanation of every difficulty.
