## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `231` (entry start).

Educational ideas. Thoughts about education or differences in world conception.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `368`; PDF page: `183`.

Spiritually mature youth, talk about patience and reason, discuss spiritual tasks, describe mental disturbances, hard truths in youth, clarity through concentrated conversations, problematic truths of the media, and reasonable conversations about separation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought and discussion about education, worldview and difficult truth.
- psychology: Mature understanding, patience, reason and concentrated attention in conversation.
- body/health: Mental disturbance is a topic described in ABC, not a diagnosis of the reader.
- social/relationship: Discussing spiritual tasks or separation and differences of worldview.
- events/manifestations: Educational ideas, hard truths encountered in youth and problems with media truth.
- conflicts/notes: Concentrated conversation can clarify; the presence of discussion does not settle whether a claim is true.

## Contradictions

Both entries concern ideas and their expression. ABC gives particular conversational and media settings without directly disputing Witte's educational emphasis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Poseidon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies educational and spiritual concerns through thought and conversation. Ideas about education and differences of worldview become subjects to examine, while patience and reason can help focused discussion bring clarity ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also includes hard truths in youth, problematic media claims and reasonable conversations about separation. The shared concern is working through difficult understanding in words, without assuming that every conversation resolves the underlying difference.
