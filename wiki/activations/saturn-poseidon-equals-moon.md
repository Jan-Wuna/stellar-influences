## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `231` (entry start).

Public matters of education. Persons who differentiate in their world concept.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `368`; PDF page: `183`.

Patience and reason of a woman, mental maturity of a woman, a mentally disturbed woman, emotional clarity through concentration, hard truth for the people, cultural tasks of a people, problems of a woman with truth, and reasonable separation from a woman.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Education, outlook and truth in public life and the source's gendered examples.
- psychology: Patience, reason and emotional clarity through concentration.
- body/health: ABC's disturbed-woman wording is historical and establishes no diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Different worldviews and reasonable separation from a woman are source examples.
- events/manifestations: Public education, cultural tasks of a people and difficult truths.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's female and collective examples remain attributed; they do not define every woman's experience.

## Contradictions

Witte gives public education and differing outlooks; ABC adds emotional clarity and gendered or collective truth problems. These are different applications without an explicit disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Poseidon = Moon`, Moon specifies educational and intellectual concerns through public understanding and the emotional response to truth. [Witte](#witte-entry) names public education and differences of worldview; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds cultural tasks of a people and hard truths, alongside clarity reached through concentration. ABC casts several examples in terms of women, including maturity, difficulty with truth and reasonable separation. Those gendered and mental-disturbance claims remain historical attributions, with no basis for a diagnosis or universal female interpretation.
