## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `231` (entry start).

Connections with educators. Separation of connections caused by differences in outlook on life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `368`; PDF page: `183`.

Tasks of a spiritual connection, serious insights in a relationship, spiritually mature relationship, disturbed spiritual connection, combining clarity with patience, clear tasks in a connection, cultural tasks that connect people, and reasonable separation of a connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections formed or tested through education, outlook and shared cultural tasks.
- psychology: Serious insight, patience and clarity within a bond.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Ties with educators, mature spiritual relationships and separation over worldview.
- events/manifestations: Shared tasks, disturbed spiritual connection and reasonable separation.
- conflicts/notes: Shared understanding can connect people without removing differences that lead to separation.

## Contradictions

The entries describe connection through education and shared tasks alongside separation through differences. These are conditional alternatives rather than direct disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Poseidon = Node`, Node specifies educational and spiritual concerns through the bond itself: educators, serious insights and shared cultural tasks can bring people into connection ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). Patience and clarity belong to the work of that relationship, but the connection may also be disturbed or end reasonably when outlooks differ. The exact entries therefore concern what understanding does within a tie, including both shared purpose and separation.
