## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `231` (entry start).

Days on which one has to pull oneself together. The man, who is a model student or model educator, who is outstanding or different. To show dignity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `368`; PDF page: `183`.

Tasks of a cultural centre, patience and reason in a person, a spiritually mature person, hard truth for a man, clarity through personal perseverance, personal spiritual success, personal problems with truth, and reasonable separation from a person.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal example, dignity and mature understanding sustained through effort.
- psychology: Patience, reason and perseverance toward clarity.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Model student or educator; reasonable separation is another source example.
- events/manifestations: Cultural-centre tasks, personal spiritual success and confronting difficult truth.
- conflicts/notes: Being outstanding or mature does not remove personal problems with truth; gendered examples remain attributed.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes self-composure and example; ABC gives perseverance, maturity and personal truth problems. These qualify the conditions of conduct without an explicit disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Poseidon = Sun`, Sun specifies educational and spiritual concerns in the person who carries them: self-composure, dignity and the example offered by a student or educator become central ([Witte](#witte-entry)). [ABC](#abc-entry) connects clarity with personal perseverance, patience and reason, while retaining difficult truth and personal problems with it. Cultural tasks and spiritual success are possible expressions, as is reasonable separation. The shared concern is how understanding is embodied in conduct, with the historical gendered examples left as particulars.
