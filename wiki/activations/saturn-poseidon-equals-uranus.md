## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `231` (entry start).

Surprising new knowledge in the field of education or leading others. To suddenly be shown as a model, which causes separation and hard feelings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `369`; PDF page: `183`.

Tasks of cultural renewal, excitement over hard truths, slow spiritual renewal, nervousness through problems with truth, recognizing new tasks, problematic cultural reform, and tensions through mental or spiritual problems.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: New educational understanding and difficult cultural or spiritual renewal.
- psychology: Excitement, nervousness and tension around hard truths.
- body/health: Nervousness is named without a specific medical condition.
- social/relationship: Being suddenly presented as a model can cause separation and hard feelings.
- events/manifestations: Surprising knowledge, new tasks and problematic cultural reform.
- conflicts/notes: Sudden recognition can coexist with slow renewal; innovation does not assure acceptance.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes sudden knowledge or exposure as a model, while ABC includes slow renewal and difficult reform. These concern different parts of change rather than directly opposing claims about its pace.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Poseidon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies education and outlook through a disruptive new recognition: knowledge can arrive unexpectedly, or a person can suddenly be held up as a model with difficult social consequences ([Witte](#witte-entry)). [ABC](#abc-entry) places excitement over hard truths beside nervousness, new tasks and problematic cultural reform. Recognition may be sudden even when spiritual renewal is slow. The entries connect fresh understanding with the tension of putting it into a cultural or educational setting.
