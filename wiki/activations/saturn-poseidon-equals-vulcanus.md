## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `231` (entry start).

The great model or example, an inspiration to others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `369`; PDF page: `183`.

tasks of a cultural power, influence through patience and reason, spiritual strength in old age, enormous spiritual duties, enormous problems with the truth, concentration on spiritual energies, concentrated power of the spirit, and rational separation from power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong cultural or spiritual influence expressed through example and concentrated purpose.
- psychology: Patience, reason and concentration on spiritual concerns.
- body/health: Spiritual strength is named without a bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: Inspiring others, exercising cultural power or reasonably separating from power.
- events/manifestations: Enormous spiritual duties and substantial problems with truth.
- conflicts/notes: Influence and inspiration do not establish correctness; truth problems remain explicit.

## Contradictions

Witte gives the inspiring example; ABC adds concentrated influence, difficult duties and separation from power. These are different applications rather than a direct disagreement.
<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Poseidon = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies educational and spiritual concerns through their force of influence: an example can inspire others, while patience, reason and concentrated purpose carry cultural power ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also retains enormous duties and problems with truth, and allows a rational separation from power. The impact of an example is therefore distinct from the truth of what it represents; strong influence does not settle that question.
