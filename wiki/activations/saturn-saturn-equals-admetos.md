## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the repeated Saturn pair through duration and constraint: bottlenecks, stoppage or compelled waiting demand patience and forbearance ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 220). The same entry includes overcoming a tight spot, leaving movement possible without assuring it. Its physical and depression terms remain historical attributions; the concrete valley example is retained without inventing a symbolic explanation.
