## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the repeated Saturn pair through system and scope: teaching, training and serious scientific work rely on method, with success linked to patience ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 220). The entry also names limits of expansion, trade restriction and loss of freedom, as well as separation from many. Organized growth and wider activity therefore appear together with their boundaries; the entry gives no rule for which result will prevail.
