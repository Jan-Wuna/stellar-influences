## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `219` (entry start).

Estrangement. To experience refusal. Given the cold shoulder. Separation from other persons. Other persons bidding farewell. To be alone. Serious acquaintance. Separations from the place or acquaintances.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Distance or separation in encounters and one's immediate setting.
- psychology: Being alone is named; a specific emotional response is not supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears in this entry.
- social/relationship: Refusal, estrangement, farewells and serious acquaintance.
- events/manifestations: Separation from people, acquaintances or a place.
- conflicts/notes: A serious acquaintance is distinct from refusal; the entry does not make every encounter rejecting.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes both serious acquaintance and forms of social distance. These are different possibilities, with no second source for comparison.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Asc`, Asc specifies the repeated Saturn pair through the encounter: refusal, estrangement or farewell puts distance between people, and separation can also concern a place or familiar acquaintances ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 219). Being alone is part of the range, but so is a serious acquaintance. The entry identifies the form of contact or separation without supplying its cause or a necessary emotional response.
