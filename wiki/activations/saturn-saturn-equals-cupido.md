## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the repeated Saturn pair in the structure of life together: associations may be old, lasting or consolidated, while shared tasks can also involve restriction, loss or separation ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 220). The settings range from family and marriage to firms and communities. Serious art and losses in the art trade are additional explicit applications. The entry keeps durability and dissolution side by side without making either the universal course of a group or bond.
