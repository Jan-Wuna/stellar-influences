## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `219` (entry start).

“Ripe Old Age”. Inconstant success. Successful separations. Separates from luck. Buildings. Change of residence. To have no luck. Money losses. Contentment in being alone. Separation from happiness or happy separation. Basic separation formula for birth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The uncertain relationship between separation and favorable circumstances.
- psychology: Contentment in being alone is the explicit inner experience.
- body/health: Ripe old age and a birth-separation formula are historical claims, not longevity or birth predictions.
- social/relationship: A separation can be happy or remove a person from happiness.
- events/manifestations: Inconstant success, financial loss, buildings and change of residence.
- conflicts/notes: The entry gives opposite possible outcomes without conditions that determine which applies.

## Contradictions

Happy separation and separation from happiness are explicitly different possibilities within one entry. Their presence does not establish a disagreement between sources or a rule selecting an outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the repeated Saturn pair through the gain or loss attached to separation: a parting can be fortunate, or it can mean losing happiness, luck or money. Contentment in being alone is a distinct favorable expression within that range ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 219). Buildings and a change of residence provide concrete settings. The source's old-age and birth-formula statements remain historical attributions; the entry gives no conditions that turn them into a prediction or settle its opposing outcomes.
