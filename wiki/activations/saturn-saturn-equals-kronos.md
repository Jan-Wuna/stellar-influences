## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `220` (entry start).

Old gentleman. High age. Late fulfillment. Long time or duration. Great hindrance. Ruling and independent persons impeded. Loss of sovereign persons. Losses suffered by the authorities. ## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Long hindrance or separation involving authority, independence and late fulfillment.
- psychology: No specific inner state is supplied in this entry.
- body/health: High age and an old gentleman are examples, not a medical or longevity conclusion.
- social/relationship: Authorities, ruling persons, the father, land ownership and urgent need of help.
- events/manifestations: Loss through or suffered by authorities, long separation and shutdown of government structures.
- conflicts/notes: Late fulfillment remains alongside great hindrance; authority may experience loss as well as impose separation.

## Contradictions

The entry places prolonged obstruction and late fulfillment in the same range. It gives no conditions selecting one and no second source for comparison.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the repeated Saturn pair through authority and extended duration: ruling or independent people can be impeded, and authorities may cause separation or suffer losses themselves ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 220). Long separation, late fulfillment and the shutdown of government structures keep delay and interruption central. The father, old gentleman, landowner and person urgently needing help remain particular source examples; none defines the formula's only social position or outcome.
