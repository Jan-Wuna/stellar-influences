#### Witte Entry

Periodic work. Interruption of work. Sickness. Acute sicknesses. Current affairs. Interruption. The act of separation. Forced separation. A general interruption. Activities leading to separation or which are periodically interrupted. End of action. The main formula for death.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action interrupted, ended or directed toward separation.
- psychology: No specific inner state is supplied in this entry.

- social/relationship: Forced separation and the act of separating are explicit possibilities.
- events/manifestations: Periodic work, interruption of work or current affairs and the end of action.
- conflicts/notes: The entry ranges from ordinary work interruption to severe historical claims without conditions that determine scale.

## Contradictions

The single entry consistently concerns interrupted action and separation. Different degrees of severity do not establish a contradiction or a rule selecting an outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Mars`, Mars specifies the repeated Saturn pair through what happens to action: work may proceed periodically, be interrupted or end, and activity may itself bring separation ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 219). Forced separation is one explicit expression within this pattern of interruption. The entry also contains sickness and a “main formula for death” assertion; these remain historical source doctrine and supply no basis for a medical or death prediction.
