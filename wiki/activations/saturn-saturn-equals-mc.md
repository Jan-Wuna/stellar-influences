## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `219` (entry start).

To feel a burden. Inhibition or loneliness. To take over a duty. Depression. Dejection. To separate. To feel deserted. Being ponderous. Personal sorrows. Mournful states or afflictions. Serious minded. Concentrated attitude. To be restrained. To slow down. Losing life goals. The moment (minute) of separation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal experience of burden, restraint, duty or separation.
- psychology: Loneliness, dejection, serious-minded concentration and loss of life goals.
- body/health: Depression and affliction are historical source language, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Feeling deserted and separating are explicit possibilities.
- events/manifestations: Taking over duty, slowing down and the moment of separation.
- conflicts/notes: Concentration and duty coexist with sorrow; seriousness does not by itself establish emotional suffering.

## Contradictions

This single entry spans responsible concentration and painful restraint. These are different experiences rather than an explicit contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = MC`, MC specifies the repeated Saturn pair as a personally felt weight: duty can be taken on seriously, while restraint, slowing down or separation can be experienced as burden, loneliness or sorrow ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 219). Concentrated attention and the loss of life goals occupy different parts of that range. The entry describes these experiences without establishing that duty must produce dejection, and its depression terminology is not a clinical diagnosis.
