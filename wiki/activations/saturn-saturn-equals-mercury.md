## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `219` (entry start).

Logic. Philosophical thinking. Competence to form an opinion, to pass judgement. Thoughts of separation. Parting to travel. To ponder. Deliberation. Impaired thinking. Serious conversations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Serious thought, deliberation and judgment, including thought about separation.
- psychology: Logic, philosophical reflection and pondering; impaired thinking is also named.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied; impaired thinking is not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Serious conversations and thoughts of parting.
- events/manifestations: Forming an opinion, passing judgment and departing to travel.
- conflicts/notes: Competent judgment and impaired thought are different possibilities; the entry supplies no condition selecting one.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes competent deliberation and impaired thinking. These are alternatives within its range rather than an established disagreement between sources.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the repeated Saturn pair through the work of thinking: logic, philosophical reflection and deliberation support forming an opinion or judgment, often in a serious conversation ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 219). Separation enters as something considered or enacted in departure for travel. The same entry also names impaired thinking, so seriousness of thought cannot by itself establish clarity or competence.
