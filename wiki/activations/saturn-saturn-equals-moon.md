## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `219` (entry start).

Restrain. Separate. Despair. Depressed mind. Old, single, separated or unhappy woman. Widow. The hours one has to be alone. Evening hours. To be separated from the public. The hour of separation. Separation of female persons. To avoid women. Discontentment trough separation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restrained feeling and discontent associated with separation or aloneness.
- psychology: Despair, a depressed mind and dissatisfaction through separation in the source's vocabulary.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified; depression wording is not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Separation from the public and historical female or widowhood examples.
- events/manifestations: Being alone, separation and evening hours are explicit source settings.
- conflicts/notes: The source's gendered examples do not define women or establish widowhood as an outcome.

## Contradictions

This single entry emphasizes restraint and separation with emotional distress. It supplies no contrasting source and no conditions establishing that every separation is distressing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Moon`, Moon specifies the repeated Saturn pair through the felt experience of separation: restraint, being alone and distance from public life are linked with discontent, despair or a depressed mind ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 219). Evening hours and female or widowhood examples are particular settings in the historical entry. They supply neither a universal account of women nor a diagnosis or timing rule for an individual's life.
