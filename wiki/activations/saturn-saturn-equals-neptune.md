## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `220` (entry start).

Habit. Things of long duration. Sickness. Treacherous diseases. Chronic ill health. Plague. Chronic conditions. Theft. Losses or separation through deception or fraud. Patience in an uncertain situation. Intuition in old age. Creeping losses. Problems through or with gas, air or water.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Long-lasting conditions marked by uncertainty, habit or gradual loss.
- psychology: Patience in uncertainty and intuition in old age are named experiences.
- body/health: Chronic illness, plague and treacherous disease are historical source claims, not diagnoses or forecasts.
- social/relationship: Loss or separation through deception, fraud or theft.
- events/manifestations: Creeping losses and problems involving gas, air or water.
- conflicts/notes: Intuition and patient endurance coexist with loss; uncertainty alone does not establish deception or illness.

## Contradictions

The single entry spans habitual duration, uncertain endurance and harmful losses. These are different applications, with no second source for comparison.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the repeated Saturn pair through uncertain persistence: a condition or habit lasts, patience is required and losses may develop gradually. Deception can also be an explicit cause of loss or separation in the entry ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 220). Intuition in old age and problems involving gas, air or water remain distinct examples. The chronic-illness and plague assertions belong to historical doctrine and do not establish a medical or event prediction.
