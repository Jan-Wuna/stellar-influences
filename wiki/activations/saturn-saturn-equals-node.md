## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `219` (entry start).

Breaking off of connections or other relationships. Difficulties in relationships or with contacts. To participate in separations. End of a connection. Long-term and serious connections. Separations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Serious or enduring connections and the difficulty of maintaining or ending them.
- psychology: No specific inner state is supplied in this entry.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears in this entry.
- social/relationship: Difficult contacts, long-term relationships and broken connections.
- events/manifestations: Participating in separation or ending a connection.
- conflicts/notes: Long-term seriousness is explicitly retained; the entry does not make separation inevitable.

## Contradictions

This single entry contains enduring bonds and separations as different conditions. It does not state that the same connection must exhibit both.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Node`, Node specifies the repeated Saturn pair through the connection itself: contact may be difficult, a relationship may be long-term and serious, or a tie may be broken off ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 219). Participation in separation and the end of a connection are explicit manifestations. Duration and seriousness remain distinct from the decision or event that ends a bond; no outcome is made universal.
