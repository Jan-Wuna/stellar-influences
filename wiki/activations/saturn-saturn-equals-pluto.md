## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `220` (entry start).

Hindered or slow development. Difficult to apply oneself. Differentiation. Slow, gradual separation or divorce. Hindrance and slowing up. Interruption of the development. Transformation through separation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development slowed, hindered or transformed through separation.
- psychology: Difficulty applying oneself is named without an explanation of its cause.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears in this entry.
- social/relationship: Gradual separation or divorce is an explicit example.
- events/manifestations: Interruption, slowing and differentiation within a process of change.
- conflicts/notes: Transformation is named, but the entry does not establish that it is beneficial or complete.

## Contradictions

The single entry consistently describes difficult or interrupted development. Transformation through separation gives a possible form of change rather than a conflicting claim of easy progress.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the repeated Saturn pair through the course of development: a process slows, encounters hindrance or is interrupted, and applying oneself can be difficult ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 220). Separation may unfold gradually and become the means by which circumstances transform or differentiate. The source names the process and its difficulty without promising that the resulting change is an improvement.
