## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the repeated Saturn pair through the organization and separation of thought: education, a model or instructions supply directions to follow, while worldview may be inhibited or mentally isolated ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 220). Separation can also be made for logical, spiritual or ideological reasons. The source therefore includes both a guiding pattern and distance created by an outlook, without judging whether the resulting belief is correct.
