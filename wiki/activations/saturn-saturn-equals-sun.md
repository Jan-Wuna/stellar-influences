## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `219` (entry start).

Old age. Old man. The serious man. Personal and physical hindrances and difficulties. Physical separations. To take leave. Susceptibility to disease and debility. The old body or object. The teacher. A day with hindrances and difficulties. (In the solar cycle: period of depression.)

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal or physical hindrance, seriousness and the source's images of age.
- psychology: Seriousness is explicit; the depression parenthesis is conditional on the source's solar-cycle setting.
- body/health: Disease susceptibility and debility are historical claims, not diagnoses or predictions.
- social/relationship: Taking leave and the teacher are explicit examples.
- events/manifestations: Physical separation, difficult days and an old body or object.
- conflicts/notes: Age and male examples remain source-specific; no timing method is developed from the parenthesis.

## Contradictions

The single entry describes difficulty through personal, bodily and concrete examples. It provides no contrasting source and no universal claim that age or seriousness entails illness.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Sun`, Sun specifies the repeated Saturn pair through the person or body facing hindrance: serious conduct, taking leave and physical separation sit beside images of age and an old object ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 219). The teacher is another explicit personal example. The entry's disease, debility and conditional solar-cycle depression claims remain historical attributions; they do not establish health or timing conclusions from the formula alone.
