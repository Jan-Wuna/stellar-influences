## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `219` (entry start).

Quarrels. Sudden separation. To part and die. To be separated suddenly. Surprising delay. Technical problems. Sudden disturbances. Disturbed rhythm. Sudden losses. Tension in old age. Technology in idle mode (standby, shut down). Outdated technology.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Abrupt interruption, separation or delay, including technical disruption.
- psychology: Quarrels and tension are named without a fuller inner explanation.
- body/health: Parting or death is historical source doctrine, not a medical prediction.
- social/relationship: Sudden separation and conflict are explicit possibilities.
- events/manifestations: Technical problems, disturbed rhythm, sudden losses and idle or outdated technology.
- conflicts/notes: Shutdown and delay need not imply a personal crisis; the entry spans several scales of interruption.

## Contradictions

This single entry consistently describes disturbance and interruption across social and technical settings. Different scales of severity do not establish an internal contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the repeated Saturn pair through abrupt interruption: separation, conflict or an unexpected delay breaks the expected rhythm ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 219). Technical faults, standby or shutdown and outdated equipment give the same pattern a concrete mechanical setting. The entry ranges from ordinary disruption to a historical death claim; it supplies no conditions that make the severe case an expected outcome.
