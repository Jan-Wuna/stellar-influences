## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `219` (entry start).

Interrupted harmony. Hindered inclinations. Passing affection. Love’s sorrow. Separation from love. Illegitimate birth. Inhibited in matters of affection. Limited peace. Serious, long lasting love.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affection and harmony constrained, interrupted or held seriously over time.
- psychology: Inhibited inclination, sorrow in love and passing affection.
- body/health: No bodily condition is established; the historical birth-status label is not a medical claim.
- social/relationship: Separation from love or a serious, lasting bond.
- events/manifestations: Interrupted harmony and limited peace; the source also includes a historical birth-status example.
- conflicts/notes: Passing affection and long-lasting love are distinct possibilities; the birth label carries no judgment of a child's worth.

## Contradictions

The single entry explicitly includes passing affection and lasting love. These are different conditions rather than a rule that every serious bond is interrupted.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Saturn = Venus`, Venus specifies the repeated Saturn pair through affection under limits: harmony can be interrupted, inclinations inhibited and love experienced through sorrow or separation. The same entry also names serious, long-lasting love ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF 219). Duration and difficulty therefore remain separate possibilities within the source range. Its historical birth-status label is preserved as source material and provides neither a prediction nor a judgment of a person's worth.
