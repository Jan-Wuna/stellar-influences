Separation, deceleration, parting or death in general. Inhibitions, pressures, problems, disturbances or losses in the general public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public experience of separation, delay, pressure and loss.
- psychology: The entry describes inhibiting conditions rather than a distinct personality.
- body/health: Death is named historically; no specific bodily mechanism is established.
- social/relationship: Difficulties are situated among the general public.
- events/manifestations: Parting, deceleration and public disturbances.
- conflicts/notes: One Witte entry; its range is broader than a prediction of death.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies the repeated Saturn/Saturn axis in public life: separation and slowing become pressures, disturbances or losses affecting the general public. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 219, entry start) includes parting and death in its range of possibilities; the passage does not make either the inevitable outcome of this formula.
