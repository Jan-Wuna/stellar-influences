## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Custody. To guard, to preserve, to banish, to fortify or to fix something. Mighty hindrance. Supervision. Captivity, imprisonment, jail. Vigilance. To be on guard. Limitation of the great forces or great power. The loss of power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Power held in check through custody, preservation or confinement.
- psychology: Vigilance and being on guard.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Supervision and custody place power within restrictions.
- events/manifestations: Fortification, imprisonment, mighty hindrance and loss of power.
- conflicts/notes: Guarding and imprisonment are distinct expressions of limitation, not interchangeable outcomes.

## Contradictions

The entry ranges from preservation to captivity; these are different uses of restriction rather than mutually exclusive claims.

## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Saturn/Saturn as power restricted or secured. The same boundary can preserve something through guarding and fortification, or deprive it of freedom through custody and imprisonment. Vigilance therefore connects the protective and coercive meanings of the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 220, entry start), whose final emphasis is the limitation or loss of great power.
