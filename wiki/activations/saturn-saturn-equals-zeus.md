## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Bequeathing. Perseverance. Oppression. Anxiety, anguish. Obstacles and hindrances in activity in work. To separate from or interrupt activities. Losses while producing. Breakdown of machines. Losses by fire in establishments. Processes without result. To separate from one’s work, or to separate from accomplishments. Hindered activity. To bring about separations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity and production obstructed, interrupted or lost.
- psychology: Perseverance accompanies anxiety, anguish and oppression.
- body/health: No distinct bodily claim is given.
- social/relationship: Separation from one's work or accomplishments.
- events/manifestations: Machine breakdown, fire losses and processes without results.
- conflicts/notes: Bequeathing is also listed but is not explained by the entry.

## Contradictions

A single entry juxtaposes persistence and obstruction; it does not claim that perseverance always overcomes the obstruction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Saturn/Saturn through work and production encountering obstacles. Perseverance operates amid anxiety and interruption, while the concrete examples are machinery breaking down, fire losses and effort yielding no result. Separation can mean leaving work or losing its accomplishments. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 220, entry start) also names bequeathing, without explaining that uncommon extension.
