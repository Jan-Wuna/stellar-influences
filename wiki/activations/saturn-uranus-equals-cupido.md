## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Quick termination of matrimony. Suspension of clubs and associations. Sudden disturbances in associations or groups.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Tasks of a technical community, disturbed shared rhythm, new losses for a community, a community unexpectedly separated, unrest and disturbances within a community, and losses for the community through reforms.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Established bonds or group cohesion disturbed abruptly.
- psychology: Collective unrest; no separate individual character description.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Marriage, associations and community ties undergo disruption or separation.
- events/manifestations: Suspension of organizations and losses accompanying reforms.
- conflicts/notes: Technical community tasks are also present; the formula is not limited to marriage.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in sudden disruption of collective ties; the technical and reform examples broaden the setting rather than contradict it.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Saturn/Uranus through a community or established bond whose continuity is interrupted. Marriage may end quickly, associations may be suspended, and a shared rhythm may be disturbed. Technical tasks and reform can also bring losses to the group. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 221, entry start) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 349; PDF p. 173) support this collective setting without making dissolution the only possible result.
