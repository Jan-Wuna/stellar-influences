## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden separating acts or actions. Sudden end through injury. To switch off technological devices.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Complaints in case of painful events, serious activity in technology, stratification rhythm, concentration on technical work, sudden cancellation activity, technical work, and surprising separation actions

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

An act of violence, occasional wrong use of extraordinary energy, undergoing great effort and toil. A violent or forced release from tension or strain, challenging others for a decisive contest or fight. Injury, accident, deprivation of freedom.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Actions that cause unexpected and negative reactions. The ability to act in a way that balances openness and freedom with caution. Anger caused by a negative reac tion to one’s own uniqueness or differences.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased irritation with changes which alter conservative traditions; impatience with radicals who insist upon new ways; adds to struggles which impose changes upon people who show loyalty to traditional methods.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Uranus = Mars`: decisive action under strain, technical courage, and the ability to force movement when systems are jammed. Main caution: rupture: impatience, irritation, or compressed aggression can trigger accidents, severance, or counterproductive conflict.
