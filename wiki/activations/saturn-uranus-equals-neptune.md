## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To suddenly be separated by untruth or falsehood. Separation by anesthesia.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting losses due to separation, concentration on the technology of the future, pliatory water problems, stress caused by unknown hazards, and technical future implications

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inability to face emotional stress, falsehood or malice caused through weakness. A resolve to resign oneself to the inevitable, abandonment of resistance, weakening strength, separation, mourning, bereavement.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The ability to subtly or even subliminally integrate the old with the new. One’s imagination may have a profound originality. You feel that your spiritual obligation is to offer something unique, distinctive, and/or original to the world, but you may have to do deep soul searching to come to an understanding of what is.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Helps weakens or mask changes designed to break traditional roles; the delusion that change will operate to improve upon the traditional methods; visions or insight on how to impose conversions in an unhurried manner.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Uranus = Neptune`: difficult adjustment, reform under confusing circumstances, and the need to recognize hidden drains before change can succeed. Main caution: disorientation: strain can become diffuse enough to sap nerve, judgment, and confidence all at once.
