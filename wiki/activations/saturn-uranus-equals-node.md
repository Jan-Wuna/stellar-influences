## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be present at separations. Connections torn apart.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

serious unrest in a relationship, sudden shocks in connections, combining technical tasks, surprising separation of a relationship, connection with new losses, and connection with a painful event

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inability to integrate oneself into a community, provocative conduct. Joint resistance to a common opponent, separation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The need to connect with people who will take your individuality and unique gifts seriously. The tendency to repress oneself and have a difficult time being open when connecting with others. In this lifetime you are learning to take your own uniqueness more seriously.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SATURN/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `284`

#### Munkasey Entry

Attracting people who help move traditionalists toward new ideas; locating associates who share similar views toward effecting needed changes or to hold down discontent; increased needs to rid groups of divisive radicals.

## Derived Synthesis
