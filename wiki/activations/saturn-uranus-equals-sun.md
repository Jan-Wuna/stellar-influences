## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Days of excitements, separations, obstacles. Sudden bodily separation. Overstrain. The man who is suddenly separated.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Old technical center, serious personal surprise, gcduld with raised people, stort rhythm us of a man, personal separation from a person, sudden personal disturbances, and new tasks of the man

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Physical exposure to severe tests of strength, power of resistance, rebellion, inflexibility. Separation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

You know it is important to find out who you really are, and what it is you really have to give, but doing so can take time and requires patience. Vitality harmed by repressing the urge to be free. You are able to bring to light your own unique vision of spirituality.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Finding an effective means of reconciling the forces for change with demands to uphold tradition; determination to introduce the new to those who remain conservative; prestige acquired thru invoking radical reforms.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Uranus = Sun`: serious independence, principled rebellion, and the strength to keep reform anchored in reality. Main caution: self-dividing tension: will can become strained, contradictory, or overly reactive when restraint and liberation both demand priority.
