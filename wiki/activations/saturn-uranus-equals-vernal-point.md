## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

General leave-taking. Sudden events with property losses.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Serious unrest in the public sphere, disturbed rhythm in public life, sudden loss in the public sphere, a painful event in public, technical disturbances in public, and surprising separation from the public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden separation and disturbance appear in public life.
- psychology: Serious public unrest rather than a distinct personal psychology.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Leave-taking or separation from the public.
- events/manifestations: Property losses, painful public events and technical disturbances.
- conflicts/notes: Public losses are examples, not an assertion that every event is disastrous.

## Contradictions

The entries place leave-taking and disturbance in public life; no explicit opposing claim appears.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Saturn/Uranus in the public sphere: a shared rhythm is disturbed, leave-taking becomes visible, and sudden events may involve losses of property. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 221, entry start) gives the general separation and material loss; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 348; PDF p. 173) adds unrest, technical disturbances and withdrawal from the public. Together they locate the disruption without fixing one inevitable event.
