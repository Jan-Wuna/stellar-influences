## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To abandon one’s freedom, to betake oneself surprisingly to a dependent state. Captivity, captured and held in confinement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Tasks of energy technology, violently disturbed rhythm, sudden loss of power, sudden losses through violence, tension through concentration of power, strong agitation over losses, disturbing influences of technology, and surprising separation from power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Freedom or power disrupted into dependence, confinement or loss.
- psychology: Strong agitation over losses and tension around concentrated power.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Dependence and captivity are Witte's examples.
- events/manifestations: Sudden power loss, violent disruption and energy-technology tasks.
- conflicts/notes: Confinement and loss of power remain possibilities rather than mandatory outcomes.

## Contradictions

ABC concerns power and disruptive forces while Witte stresses lost freedom; the settings differ without an explicit contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Saturn/Uranus through a sudden change in freedom or control. Power may be concentrated and strained, then lost, while the person becomes dependent or confined. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 221, entry start) supplies captivity as an extreme example; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 349; PDF p. 173) extends the field to violent disturbance and energy technology. Their common concern is a disruptive change in the ability to act freely.
