## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Losses through conflagrations or acts of war. Enforced separation connected with losses.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Beginning of painful events, beginning disturbances of rhythm, concentration on technical achievements, sudden losses through fire, sudden procreation problems, problematic nervousness among leaders, and state reform involving losses.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Productive or forceful activity interrupted by loss and enforced separation.
- psychology: Problematic nervousness among leaders is attributed by ABC.
- body/health: ABC names procreation problems; this is a historical source claim, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Separation can be imposed through war or other destructive events.
- events/manifestations: Fire losses, technical achievements and state reform with losses.
- conflicts/notes: Technical concentration does not erase the destructive possibilities.

## Contradictions

The entries share loss and interruption; technical achievement is an additional emphasis, not a guarantee against damage.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Saturn/Uranus where technical achievement or the start of an undertaking meets disruptive loss. Fire and warfare are the severe examples, with enforced separation tied to what is lost. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 221, entry start) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 349; PDF p. 173) ground this reading; ABC also names state reform and reproductive problems, which remain specific attributed extensions rather than universal results.
