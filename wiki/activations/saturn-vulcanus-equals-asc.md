## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Others must be attentive to their advantage. To be guarded. To be imprisoned.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influence of an old place, loss of influence among others, influence of patience through the partner, enormous concentration at the locality, concentration at a place of strength, powerful tasks concerning the environment, and disturbances through violence at a place.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Constraint and concentrated power encountered through others or a locality.
- psychology: Patience is an influence exerted by the partner in ABC.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Being guarded or imprisoned; others must attend to their advantage.
- events/manifestations: Concentration at a place of strength, demanding environmental tasks and local violent disturbance.
- conflicts/notes: Local concentration of power includes both influence through patience and disruption through violence.

## Contradictions

Guarding and imprisonment are Witte's examples; ABC situates influence and force locally without asserting that confinement always follows.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Saturn/Vulcanus in dealings with others and in a particular place. Concentrated power may be felt as guarding or confinement, while the partner's patience and the demands of the surroundings influence the situation. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 230, entry start) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 366; PDF p. 182) ground these interpersonal and local expressions, whose range also includes loss of influence among others and local disturbances through violence.
