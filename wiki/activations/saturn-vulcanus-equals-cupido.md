## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Connection with people who feel themselves hindered, imprisoned or oppressed. To be considerably impaired through family or community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Perseverance of a powerful community, loss of influence in of a community, energy and perseverance in of a community, common energy problems, enormous common tasks, hard and violence of a community, and loss of power of a community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Collective strength and endurance under heavy constraint.
- psychology: Perseverance within a powerful community.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Family or community can burden its members; connection with oppressed people.
- events/manifestations: Common energy problems, immense tasks and loss of group power.
- conflicts/notes: A community can sustain endurance and exert oppressive force.

## Contradictions

ABC's collective endurance and Witte's community impairment concern different uses or experiences of group power.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Saturn/Vulcanus through a community carrying or imposing great burdens. Shared energy and perseverance support immense tasks, yet the same group setting can hinder or oppress its members and lose influence or power. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 230, entry start) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 367; PDF p. 182) keep these sides together: collective strength is not automatically protection from collective constraint.
