## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To become imprisoned or dependent. The beginning of imprisonment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Burdensome development of power, developing influence in old age, unfolding of concentrated violence, immense developmental tasks, concentration on changes in energy, slow unfolding of strength, growing energy problems, and growing losses through violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A burdensome development of power or entry into dependence.
- psychology: Concentration on changes in energy; no separate character profile.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical diagnosis is given.
- social/relationship: Becoming dependent or imprisoned is Witte's severe example.
- events/manifestations: Slow unfolding of strength, growing energy problems and losses through violence.
- conflicts/notes: Development is described as burdensome; change is not necessarily improvement.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes the onset of confinement, while ABC describes a broader developing burden of power; these are different scales of manifestation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies Saturn/Vulcanus as a process in which power and constraint develop. Strength may unfold slowly, but energy problems and losses can grow as concentrated force becomes burdensome. The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 367; PDF p. 182) gives this continuing development; the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 230, entry start) gives entry into dependency or imprisonment as a severe concrete expression. The formula does not establish that every development ends in confinement.
