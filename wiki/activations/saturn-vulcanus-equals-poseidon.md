## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `230` (entry start).

Good perceptive faculty. To be mentally awake. Supervision and education with an outlook on life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `367`; PDF page: `182`.

Influential cultural tasks, concentration on spiritual energies, concentrated mental violence, power through clarity in old age, painful influence of truth, strength through patience and reason, disturbing mental influence, and loss of cultural influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong cultural or mental influence, with perception and clarity as explicit resources.
- psychology: Mental wakefulness, good perception, patience and reason.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied; spiritual-energy language is source doctrine.
- social/relationship: Supervision, education and cultural influence are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: Influential tasks, painful truth and loss or disturbance of influence.
- conflicts/notes: Clear perception and disturbing influence are distinct possibilities; strong impact does not establish truth.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes perceptive awareness and education; ABC also includes disturbing or lost influence. These describe different effects rather than directly opposing claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Vulcanus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies concentrated influence through perception and outlook: mental wakefulness supports supervision and education, while clarity, patience and reason can strengthen cultural influence ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also names painful truth, disturbing mental influence and loss of cultural influence. The concern is therefore both the clarity and the force of an idea's effect; the entry does not establish that a strong influence is necessarily sound or helpful.
