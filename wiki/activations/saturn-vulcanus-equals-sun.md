## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `230` (entry start).

The custodian, the watchman. Days on which one has to think of the bodily well being. The man who pays much attention to physical events or proceedings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `366`; PDF page: `182`.

Tasks of a centre of power, an influential centre for older people, energy problems of the body, influential personal tasks, personal loss of power, personal loss through violence, maturity through the influence of a person, and loss of bodily strength.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal responsibility for bodily matters and the exercise or loss of influence.
- psychology: Attention to physical events; no fuller inner portrait is supplied.
- body/health: Energy problems and loss of bodily strength are historical source claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Custodian, watchman and a person's formative influence are explicit examples.
- events/manifestations: Influential tasks, a centre for older people and personal loss of power.
- conflicts/notes: Guarding and influence coexist with vulnerability to loss; violence remains an attributed possibility.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses vigilance and bodily attention; ABC adds influential tasks and losses of power or strength. These are different settings rather than directly opposing claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Vulcanus = Sun`, Sun specifies concentrated responsibility at the personal and bodily level: the custodian or watchman attends closely to physical events and well-being ([Witte](#witte-entry)). [ABC](#abc-entry) adds influential personal tasks, the effect one person has on another's maturity, and the possibility of losing power or bodily strength. Its centres of power or care for older people are concrete settings. The entry's energy and violence claims remain historical attributions, with no medical or event outcome assured.
