## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `230` (entry start).

Sorrow through separation. Pain of separation. Strong patience in love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `366`; PDF page: `182`.

Disturbances of harmony through violence, patience in strong love, inhibited love influence, problematic artistic influence, problematic violence in love, mature love influence, lost love through violence, and preference for violence and harshness

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong feeling and patience in love, with painful separation or disturbed harmony.
- psychology: Patience and sorrow are explicit; ABC also attributes a preference for harshness or violence.
- body/health: Pain concerns separation; no bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Mature or inhibited affection and loss of love through violence are distinct source examples.
- events/manifestations: Separation, disturbed harmony and problematic artistic influence.
- conflicts/notes: Patience in love does not imply acceptance of violence; the severe claims remain attributed possibilities.

## Contradictions

Both entries include painful love and patience; ABC adds mature affection, artistic influence and violence. These extend the range without making one expression universal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Vulcanus = Venus`, Venus specifies concentrated feeling through the patience and cost of love: separation brings sorrow, while strong affection may be sustained patiently ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC distinguishes mature influence from inhibited affection and disturbed harmony, with artistic influence as an additional application. Its violence and harshness claims remain historical attributions; they do not turn patience into an obligation to endure harm or establish violence as an outcome of the formula.
