## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `230` (entry start).

To be forced to discontinue working. To work under supervision or under guard. Menial work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `367`; PDF page: `182`.

Beginning energy disturbance, energy-performance problems, patient power of leadership, disturbed influence of leadership, enormous leadership tasks, concentration of power in leadership, problematic striving for power, and severe fire damage.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Work and leadership under concentrated power, supervision or interrupted capacity.
- psychology: Patient leadership and problematic striving for power are explicit alternatives.
- body/health: Energy-performance problems are source language without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Working under guard or supervision; concentrated leadership authority.
- events/manifestations: Forced cessation of work, menial work, demanding leadership tasks and fire damage.
- conflicts/notes: Leadership and subordination are different positions; the entry does not determine which applies.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes supervised or stopped work, while ABC includes concentrated leadership and its disturbances. These are different positions within directed activity rather than a direct disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Vulcanus = Zeus`, Zeus specifies concentrated power through the direction of work: activity can be supervised, guarded or forcibly stopped, while leadership carries substantial tasks and can concentrate control ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC distinguishes patient leadership from disturbed influence and problematic striving for power, alongside impaired energy performance. The entries therefore concern both directing and being directed under demanding conditions; fire damage remains a particular historical example rather than an assured event.
