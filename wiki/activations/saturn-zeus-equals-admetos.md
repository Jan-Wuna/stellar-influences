## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `226` (entry start).

Complete standstill of activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `359`; PDF page: `178`.

Beginning depressive disturbances, blocked shaping of tasks, depression through losses by fire, restricted striving in old age, firmness through serious striving, thorough efforts at patience, problematic limits on achievement, and increasing leadership tasks.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity stopped or constrained by blocked tasks and limited achievement.
- psychology: Firmness, serious striving and efforts at patience; depression is historically attributed.
- body/health: Depressive disturbance is source language, not a diagnosis or a causal medical conclusion.
- social/relationship: Leadership tasks may increase while performance is limited.
- events/manifestations: Standstill, restricted striving and loss through fire in ABC's examples.
- conflicts/notes: Firmness and patience remain alongside blockage; the entries do not promise that effort restores activity.

## Contradictions

Witte gives complete standstill; ABC describes blocked or restricted activity with continuing striving and leadership tasks. These differ in degree and circumstance rather than directly disputing one another.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Zeus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies directed activity through stoppage and limits: tasks cannot readily take shape, achievement is constrained and striving may be restricted ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC nevertheless retains firmness, serious effort and deliberate patience, even as leadership duties increase. The tension is between continuing demand and limited ability to act; the historical depression and fire-loss claims do not establish an inevitable event or medical outcome.
