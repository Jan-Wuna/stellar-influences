## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `226` (entry start).

Independence in the method. New methods of teaching. New forms of expression. To follow new paths in research. Showing new goals for research.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `359`; PDF page: `178`.

Beginning disturbances of trade, successful efforts in old age, creative shaping of tasks, problematic expansion of goals, spread of fire involving loss, many difficulties at the start, growing leadership problems, and problems with the goals of science.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independent methods and wider goals, with difficulty in beginning or expanding activity.
- psychology: No specific inner state is supplied in these entries.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears in the verified entries.
- social/relationship: Teaching methods and leadership problems are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: New research paths, creative tasks, trade disturbance and costly spread of fire.
- conflicts/notes: Methodical independence and goal expansion can coexist with difficult starts; expansion does not assure success.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes new methods and research goals; ABC includes successful effort as well as problematic expansion and beginnings. These are different conditions, not a direct disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Zeus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies directed activity through method and wider purpose: independent teaching methods, new forms of expression and fresh research paths establish or reveal new goals ([Witte](#witte-entry)). [ABC](#abc-entry) adds creative shaping of tasks and successful effort, but also problematic expansion, difficult starts and growing leadership problems. The shared concern is how an undertaking finds a method and extends its aims, with trade and fire-loss examples retaining the concrete costs of disrupted expansion.
