## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `226` (entry start).

Machines damaged. To be endangered by fire. Fellowmen in a fire or in war.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `358`; PDF page: `178`.

Initial difficulties in the environment, task design for the environment, starting partner problems, loss of leadership at the place, design problems in the environment, performance problems of the partner, and losses through fire at the place.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficulty beginning or organizing activity in one's surroundings and partnerships.
- psychology: No specific inner state is supplied in these entries.
- body/health: Fire danger and war are historical examples, not diagnoses or forecasts.
- social/relationship: Partner performance, local leadership and other people caught in fire or war.
- events/manifestations: Starting difficulties, damaged machines, environmental tasks and fire losses.
- conflicts/notes: Everyday task or partner problems and severe fire/war examples are different scales of the source range.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses mechanical damage and severe environmental danger; ABC also gives ordinary starting, design and partner difficulties. These differ in scale without directly disputing one another.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Zeus = Asc`, Asc specifies directed activity through its setting and the people involved: a beginning may meet environmental difficulties, tasks may be hard to organize and a partner's performance or local leadership may be impaired ([ABC](#abc-entry)). [Witte](#witte-entry) gives more severe examples of damaged machines and people endangered by fire or war. The common concern is the condition under which activity takes place; the severe examples remain historical attributions rather than an inevitable outcome of ordinary difficulty.
