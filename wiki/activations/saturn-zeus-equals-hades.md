## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `226` (entry start).

To be hindered in work by lack and by poverty. Arson. Great losses in war.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `359`; PDF page: `178`.

Beginning dangerous disturbances, burdens through criminal leadership, mysterious shaping of tasks, sorrow through problematic goal-setting, disadvantages through initial difficulties, hidden procreation problems, and losses through fire-related criminality.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Work and aims obstructed by deprivation, harmful leadership or dangerous conditions.
- psychology: Sorrow through problematic goal-setting is the explicit inner response.
- body/health: Hidden procreation problems are a historical ABC claim, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Burdens attributed to criminal leadership; poverty impedes work.
- events/manifestations: Difficult beginnings, arson, war losses and obscure task-shaping.
- conflicts/notes: Crime and war are source examples without an identified person or event; no guilt or forecast follows.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize obstruction and loss, with ABC adding goal-setting and leadership problems. Their overlap does not establish independent corroboration or an unavoidable outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Zeus = Hades`, Hades specifies directed activity through the conditions that undermine it: lack or poverty hinders work, while difficult beginnings, problematic goals and harmful leadership add burdens or sorrow ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). Fire-related crime and war losses give severe concrete examples of damage. These remain historical source attributions, as do the hidden procreation problems; they establish neither a diagnosis nor wrongdoing or a particular event for any person.
