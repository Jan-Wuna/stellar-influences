## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `226` (entry start).

To cross the ideas of others successfully. Conversion. Change of opinion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `358`; PDF page: `178`.

Starting money problems, burdensome striving in legal matters, successful efforts in old age, generating financial difficulties, financial losses due to fire, profit and loss due to fire, problematic legal services, and problems of financial leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The results of directed effort, including changed opinion and difficult financial or legal outcomes.
- psychology: Conversion or change of opinion is Witte's explicit emphasis.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Successfully crossing another's ideas; financial leadership is another setting.
- events/manifestations: Money problems, successful effort in old age and profit or loss through fire.
- conflicts/notes: Success and financial difficulty remain distinct possibilities; these entries do not establish uniformly favorable results.

## Contradictions

Witte addresses changes of opinion; ABC addresses legal and financial striving. These are different settings rather than conflicting claims about the same outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Zeus = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies directed effort through its results. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes successfully crossing another's ideas, conversion and changed opinion. [ABC](#abc-entry) places effort in legal and financial settings, allowing success in old age while retaining burdensome striving, money problems and profit or loss through fire. These applications share concern with what an undertaking produces, but they should not be reduced to a promise of success or a single financial outcome.
