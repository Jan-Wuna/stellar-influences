## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `226` (entry start).

Periodically interrupted common efforts. Death or injuries through firearms, through fire. Sterilization.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `358`; PDF page: `178`.

Demanding work tasks, work performance in old age, beginning disturbances at work, effort toward mature achievements, serious leadership problems, patient procreative acts, hard work at the fire, and work achievements involving loss.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Demanding effort and performance interrupted or accompanied by loss.
- psychology: Patience and effort toward mature achievement are explicit; no fuller inner portrait is supplied.
- body/health: Procreation, sterilization and injuries are historical source claims, not diagnoses or predictions.
- social/relationship: Common efforts and leadership problems are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: Work disturbances, difficult fire-related work and costly achievements.
- conflicts/notes: Ordinary work disruption and severe firearms/fire claims are different scales of the source range.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe interrupted or difficult effort; ABC adds patient striving and mature achievement. The severe historical examples do not negate these less extreme applications.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Zeus = Mars`, Mars specifies directed activity through the work required to perform: tasks are demanding, leadership can be problematic and common efforts may be interrupted ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC retains patience and the pursuit of mature achievement, while also naming work that involves loss or fire. Witte's firearms, death and sterilization claims remain severe historical examples; they do not establish a medical or event prediction from the formula.
