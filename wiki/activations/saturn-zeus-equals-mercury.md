## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `226` (entry start).

To think or read about loss-making creative work, or fire damages. Enforced separation by others. Quick separation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `358`; PDF page: `178`.

Beginning speech disturbances, reports about efforts in old age, reports about losses through fire, serious striving among the young, conversations about shaping tasks, conversations about leadership problems, performance problems of the young, and problems of youth leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought and communication about effort, tasks and their difficulties or losses.
- psychology: Serious striving is named; no fuller inner portrait is supplied.
- body/health: Speech disturbance is historical source language, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Youth leadership, performance problems and enforced or quick separation.
- events/manifestations: Reports about fire loss or old-age effort and discussions of tasks or leadership.
- conflicts/notes: Reports concern difficulties; they do not establish that every communication causes loss or separation.

## Contradictions

Both entries concern difficult activity and its expression in thought or reports. Witte's separation examples and ABC's youth/leadership settings are distinct additions rather than direct disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Zeus = Mercury`, Mercury specifies directed activity through what is thought, read or discussed about it: creative work that brings loss, fire damage, difficult effort and the shaping of tasks become subjects of attention ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also emphasizes young people's striving, performance and leadership problems. Witte retains quick or enforced separation as a distinct manifestation. The historical speech-disturbance and fire-loss examples do not establish a diagnosis or event from the formula.
