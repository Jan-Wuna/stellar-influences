## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `226` (entry start).

Perseverance of the woman at work. Quick separation from women. The wife of the conscript. Stage fright. Losses through fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `358`; PDF page: `178`.

Initial difficulties for a people, efforts of a woman in old age, beginning emotional disturbances, performance difficulties of a woman, problematic goal-setting of a people, losses for a people by fire, and procreation problems of a woman.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Persevering effort and difficult performance in the source's female and collective examples.
- psychology: Stage fright and beginning emotional disturbance are explicit.
- body/health: Procreation problems are historical source claims, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Separation from women and the conscript's wife are particular source examples.
- events/manifestations: Initial collective difficulties, difficult goal-setting and fire losses.
- conflicts/notes: Female examples remain attributed; perseverance does not establish one outcome for women or a people.

## Contradictions

The entries combine perseverance with performance difficulties, separation and emotional strain. These are different conditions of effort, not an explicit disagreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Zeus = Moon`, Moon specifies directed effort through its emotional strain and the source's female or collective settings: perseverance at work can coexist with difficult performance, stage fright or troubled beginnings ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also names problematic goal-setting for a people; both entries retain fire losses. The conscript's wife, separation from women and procreation problems remain historical particulars rather than universal female meanings or predicted outcomes.
