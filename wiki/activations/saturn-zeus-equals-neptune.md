## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `226` (entry start).

Chronic hindrance, the fruits of toll are nil. Damage through burning gas. To be taken sick or to waste away through poison gas.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `359`; PDF page: `178`.

Beginning disturbances in water, disappointing shaping of tasks, performance problems in water, problematic weakness of leadership, mature performance in metaphysics, disturbing procreative weakness, unknown leadership problems, and losses through unclear goal-setting.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Effort hindered or unrewarded through unclear aims, weak leadership or difficult conditions.
- psychology: Disappointment is explicit; no fuller inner portrait is supplied.
- body/health: Procreative weakness and gas-related illness are historical source claims, not diagnoses or predictions.
- social/relationship: Leadership weakness and unrecognized leadership problems are ABC settings.
- events/manifestations: Unfruitful work, water-related performance problems, unclear goals and mature metaphysical work.
- conflicts/notes: Mature performance remains possible in ABC; the entries do not make all work futile.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes chronic hindrance and unfruitful effort; ABC includes mature metaphysical performance alongside disappointment. These concern different conditions rather than a universal claim about every undertaking.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Zeus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies directed effort through difficulty securing a clear or useful result: hindrance can persist, task-shaping disappoint and unclear aims or weak leadership contribute to loss ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also retains mature performance in metaphysics, so the entry does not reduce every application to futility. Its water, gas and procreative-weakness examples remain historical source attributions rather than a medical or event prediction.
