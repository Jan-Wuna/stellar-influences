## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `226` (entry start).

Work agreements where everybody works for himself. To be connected to fire losses.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: `358`; PDF page: `178`.

Serious procreative connection, a leadership problem in a relationship, patience in connection with leaders, performance problems in a connection, problematic goal-setting in a connection, and creative connections in old age.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Agreements and connections tested through leadership, performance and aims.
- psychology: Patience in dealings with leaders is the explicit inner stance.
- body/health: A procreative connection is named without a specific bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Work agreements with individual effort, creative bonds and leadership in a relationship.
- events/manifestations: Performance or goal-setting problems and connections to fire losses.
- conflicts/notes: Connection does not imply that everyone works jointly; Witte explicitly retains working for oneself.

## Contradictions

Witte's individually pursued work agreement and ABC's creative connection describe different arrangements. Leadership or performance difficulty does not directly contradict either one.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Saturn/Zeus = Node`, Node specifies directed activity through its agreement or bond: people can be connected while each works for themselves, and the relationship can face problems of leadership, performance or goal-setting ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)). ABC also retains patience with leaders and creative connections in old age. The shared concern is how effort is arranged between people, with procreative ties and fire-loss connections remaining particular source examples rather than assured outcomes.
