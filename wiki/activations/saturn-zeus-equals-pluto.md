## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 226 (one-based).

Slow development in activities, slow increase in accomplishments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 359; PDF page 178 (one-based).

Beginning of a disturbed development, beginning of growing endurance, shaping developmental tasks, developing serious striving, patient development of performance, concentration on growing achievement, increasing loss of performance, and a leadership change involving losses.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Slow development of activity and achievement, with endurance or declining performance.
- psychology: Serious striving, patience and concentration on increasing achievement.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: ABC includes a leadership change involving losses.
- events/manifestations: Gradual accomplishment, developmental tasks, disturbed development or growing performance losses.
- conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes slow increase; ABC retains both growing endurance and adverse development.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

The entries distinguish gradual progress from deterioration; slowness alone does not establish loss. ABC's community material in the earlier transcription belonged to the following Cupido row and has been replaced from the scan.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Saturn/Zeus = Pluto`, achievement develops slowly through serious, sustained effort. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a gradual increase in activity and accomplishment; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives that process both a constructive side—growing endurance, patient performance and concentration—and a difficult side, where development is disturbed, output declines or a leadership change brings losses. The formula therefore concerns how effort develops, without making slow progress itself a sign of failure.
