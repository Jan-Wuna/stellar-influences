## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 226 (one-based).

Hindrance, interruptions, or measures of force (coercion) in matters of education.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 359; PDF page 178 (one-based).

Serious striving for knowledge, spiritual efforts in old age, shaping intellectual tasks, intellectual leadership tasks, patient striving for truth, shaping problematic ideas, concentration on spiritual fire, and shaping cultural tasks.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Serious intellectual or cultural effort, alongside impediments to education.
- psychology: Patient pursuit of truth and concentration on ideas.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: Intellectual leadership; Witte includes coercion in educational matters.
- events/manifestations: Educational interruptions, cultural tasks and the development of difficult ideas.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's sustained striving does not erase Witte's obstructive or coercive possibilities.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte emphasizes educational constraints; ABC describes the intellectual work itself. ABC's previously omitted opening has been restored from the scan; the stray Witte suffix “Tan” was absent from the source.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Saturn/Zeus = Poseidon`, sustained effort takes an intellectual, educational or cultural form. [ABC](#abc-entry) describes patient pursuit of knowledge and truth, work on problematic ideas and intellectual leadership; [Witte](#witte-entry) places hindrance, interruption and coercion in education alongside that effort. The shared focus is the demanding work of developing or transmitting ideas, with constructive concentration and imposed restriction retained as distinct expressions.
