## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 226 (one-based).

Sudden enforced separation, to be exposed to pressure. Loss by fire or lightning.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 359; PDF page 178 (one-based).

Beginning of nervous pain, beginning technical disturbances, serious striving for reform, new leadership problems, sudden fire losses, problematic leadership goals, shaping technical tasks, and a reform beginning involving losses.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Effort or reform encounters abrupt pressure, technical disruption or forced separation.
- psychology: Serious reform aims coexist with problematic leadership goals.
- body/health: ABC's nervous pain is a historical source attribution, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Forced separation and new leadership problems.
- events/manifestations: Technical disturbances, a difficult reform launch and sudden fire or lightning losses.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes purposeful reform and technical tasks; sudden loss is one listed expression, not an assured outcome.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte stresses abrupt coercive events; ABC also describes the reform or technical work involved. Water, metaphysics and weakness clauses in the previous transcription belonged to the following Neptune row.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Saturn/Zeus = Uranus`, effort meets sudden pressure or disruption. [Witte](#witte-entry) names enforced separation and fire or lightning losses; [ABC](#abc-entry) places technical work and serious reform aims alongside disturbances, problematic leadership goals and a costly start to reform. This connects purposeful change with the strains of carrying it out, while keeping nervous pain and destructive events as attributed source examples rather than forecasts.
