## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 226 (one-based).

Obstacles in activities caused by love. Devoted activities with temporary interruptions. Forsaken after having been used sexually.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 358; PDF page 178 (one-based).

Initial difficulties in love, joy through creativity in old age, peace goals in old age, disturbed striving for harmony, harmonious shaping of tasks, problematic wishes of leaders, performance problems in love, and loss of love's fire.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affection and devoted activity encounter obstacles or interruptions.
- psychology: Striving for harmony, creative joy and peace aims coexist with frustrated desire.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: Difficult beginnings in love, loss of passion and Witte's abandonment after sexual use.
- events/manifestations: Interrupted devoted work, harmonious task design and creativity in old age.
- conflicts/notes: ABC retains joy and harmony; Witte's exploitation example does not characterize every relationship.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

The entries connect love with interrupted effort, while ABC also includes creative joy, peace and harmonious tasks. These constructive clauses qualify an exclusively negative reading.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Saturn/Zeus = Venus`, love and devotion become involved in the difficulty of sustaining activity. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes obstacles or temporary interruptions caused by love; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds difficult beginnings, disturbed harmony and loss of passion, but also harmonious tasks, peace aims and creative joy in old age. The formula accommodates devotion that remains productive through difficulty as well as its frustration; Witte's sexual exploitation example remains a particular source claim.
