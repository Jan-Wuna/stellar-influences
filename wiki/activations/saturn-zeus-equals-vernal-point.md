## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 226 (one-based).

General damages through steam power, and losses through fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 358; PDF page 178 (one-based).

Fire losses in public life, patient achievements in public life, inhibited creativity in public, performance problems in public, shaping public tasks, and public leadership problems.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public activity combines patient effort with inhibited performance or losses.
- psychology: Patience and constrained creativity are the explicit dispositions in ABC.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: Public tasks and leadership problems.
- events/manifestations: Public performance difficulties; Witte lists general steam-power damage and fire losses.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes patient achievement as well as inhibition; destructive examples are not forecasts.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

The entries overlap in fire losses. ABC extends the scope to public effort, creativity and leadership, so it does not support reducing the formula to physical damage alone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Saturn/Zeus = Vernal Point`, the effort and impediments described by the entries appear in public life. [ABC](#abc-entry) connects patient achievement and public tasks with inhibited creativity, performance difficulties and leadership problems. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies the narrower examples of general steam-power damage and fire losses, echoed by ABC's public fire losses. Public accomplishment under constraint is the broader reading; destructive events remain particular source examples.
