## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 120; PDF page 116 (one-based).

To refuse connections with others. No desire for connections. Homeland. Native place.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 168; PDF page 83 (one-based).

Other people are steadfast, oppressed people in the surroundings, restriction of one's personal environment, depression on the day at the place, a thorough personal partner, the partner's physical endurance, and a day of resistance at the place.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Steadfastness, restriction or refusal in one's immediate surroundings and connections.
- psychology: No desire for connections in Witte; ABC includes depressed or oppressed people.
- body/health: ABC names the partner's physical endurance without a specific bodily disorder.
- social/relationship: Refused contact, a thorough partner and steadfast people nearby.
- events/manifestations: Homeland or native place, restricted surroundings and local resistance.
- conflicts/notes: Steadfastness and unwanted contact are distinct possibilities; depression language remains attributed.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte stresses refusing connection and native place; ABC adds the qualities of nearby people and a partner. Firmness does not by itself establish rejection or depression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Admetos = Asc`, the entries place firmness and restriction in immediate relationships and surroundings. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes refusing contact or lacking the desire for it, together with homeland and native place. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds steadfast people, a thorough and physically enduring partner, restricted surroundings and local resistance. Its depressed or oppressed people are historical source examples; the common focus is the form that endurance or limitation takes around the person, without equating steadfastness with withdrawal.
