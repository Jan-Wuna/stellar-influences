## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 120; PDF page 116 (one-based).

To refuse physical connections with other people. The little exclusive community. Immovables.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 169; PDF page 83 (one-based).

An oppressed human community, a depressed human community, a confined community of people, a steadfast community of people, a community's day of resistance, and oppression of a men's community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A community marked by exclusivity, confinement, steadfastness or resistance.
- psychology: ABC includes a depressed community; this is historical collective language.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: Refused physical connections, a small exclusive group and collective oppression.
- events/manifestations: Community resistance and Witte's immovable property.
- conflicts/notes: A steadfast group need not be oppressed; the entries retain several conditions of collective life.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte describes exclusivity and refusal of physical connection; ABC adds imposed confinement and resistance. Voluntary exclusivity and oppression should not be treated as equivalent.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Admetos = Cupido`, the entries place firmness or restriction within a community. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a small exclusive group, refusal of physical connections and immovable property; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a steadfast or confined group, collective resistance and oppression. The connection is a community held apart or held firm, while chosen exclusivity, collective endurance and imposed oppression remain different situations. ABC's depressed and gendered community examples remain attributed particulars.
