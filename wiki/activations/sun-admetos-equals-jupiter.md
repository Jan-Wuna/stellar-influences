## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 120; PDF page 116 (one-based).

To be lucky or fortunate in the country.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 168; PDF page 83 (one-based).

Blocked money of a person, restriction of a successful person, successful personal deepening, financial limitation of a person, joy in physical endurance, basic human rights, and immersion in human rights.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Fortune or successful deepening alongside personal and financial restriction.
- psychology: Joy in endurance and successful personal deepening.
- body/health: Physical endurance is named without a specific bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Basic human rights and close attention to them.
- events/manifestations: Good fortune in the country, blocked money and financial limitation.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's favorable rural example does not cancel ABC's financial restrictions.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte gives a favorable setting; ABC includes both success and restriction in other settings. These are different possibilities, not a universal promise of prosperity or a universal denial of it.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Admetos = Jupiter`, benefit and restriction coexist in the entries. [Witte](#witte-entry) locates good fortune in the country; [ABC](#abc-entry) names successful personal deepening and joy in endurance, but also blocked money and limitations affecting a successful person. ABC's concern with basic human rights adds a legal theme. The formula therefore supports these particular forms of benefit, depth or constraint without making rural fortune a general promise of financial ease.
