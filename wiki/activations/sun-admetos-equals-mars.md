## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 120; PDF page 116 (one-based).

Activity within a limited scope or with a special subject. Tilling the soil. Agricultural work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 168; PDF page 83 (one-based).

Work with oppressed people, enduring activity of a man, restricted personal actions, thorough work by a person, personal immersion in work, heavy physical work, and the drive to act of a firmly established person.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Concentrated or enduring work within a limited scope.
- psychology: Thoroughness and immersion in work.
- body/health: Heavy physical work is named, without a specific bodily disorder.
- social/relationship: ABC includes work with oppressed people.
- events/manifestations: Specialized activity, agricultural work and restricted personal action.
- conflicts/notes: Restricted scope may describe specialization or constraint; it does not erase endurance or drive.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte's limited or specialized activity overlaps ABC's thorough immersion. Agricultural work and work with oppressed people are distinct examples, not interchangeable occupations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Admetos = Mars`, activity takes a concentrated, enduring or restricted form. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes work within a limited scope or special subject, including tilling the soil; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds thoroughness, immersion and heavy physical work while preserving restrictions on personal action. Endurance and drive remain capacities within that narrowed field. Work with oppressed people is a further explicit ABC setting, rather than a claim that all specialized work involves oppression.
