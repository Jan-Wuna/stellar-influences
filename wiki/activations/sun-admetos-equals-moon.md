## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 120; PDF page 116 (one-based).

Deficiency in the functioning of the glands. Death of a woman, the wife or the mother.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 168; PDF page 83 (one-based).

Blockage between woman and man, depressed people within a population, firmness between woman and man, a person's depressed emotional state, a woman's bodily circulation, a physically restricted woman, a woman's heavy body, and deep personal feelings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: ABC links emotional depth with firmness or blockage in its female and relational examples.
- psychology: Deep personal feelings and a depressed emotional state.
- body/health: Glandular deficiency, circulation, bodily restriction and death are historical source claims, not diagnoses or predictions.
- social/relationship: Firmness or blockage between woman and man; depressed people within a population.
- events/manifestations: The entries provide emotional, relational and bodily states rather than a general activity description.
- conflicts/notes: The previous “man of practical activity” synthesis had no support in either local entry.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte gives severe bodily and mortality examples; ABC also includes emotional depth and relational firmness. They should remain attributed and distinct rather than being merged into a single medical conclusion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Admetos = Moon`, [ABC](#abc-entry) describes emotional depth and firmness or blockage between woman and man, alongside depressed feeling and bodily restriction in its female examples. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies narrower historical claims about glandular deficiency and the death of a woman, wife or mother. The page supports those attributed emotional, relational and bodily themes; it supplies no basis for the former generic description of a practical person or for a diagnosis or mortality prediction.
