## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 120; PDF page 116 (one-based).

To come from confusion to clarity. The neglected or abandoned estate or soil. Wear and tear. Decay. Decomposition of the dead body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 169; PDF page 83 (one-based).

Depressed and disappointed person, bodily endurance in water, person in deep water, personal weakness through depressions, personal deepening in metaphysics, heavy body in the air, and uncertainty through staff reductions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Neglect, deterioration or uncertainty, with clarity and metaphysical depth also possible.
- psychology: Disappointment, depressed weakness and movement from confusion to clarity.
- body/health: Depression and decomposition language remain historical source claims, not diagnoses or predictions.
- social/relationship: ABC names uncertainty through staff reductions.
- events/manifestations: Neglected property, wear or decay; endurance in water, deep water and a heavy body in air.
- conflicts/notes: Clarity and deepening are retained; the former house-secrets synthesis was unsupported by these entries.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

The sources contain deterioration and weakness alongside clarity, endurance and metaphysical deepening. These separate possibilities should not be collapsed into inevitable decline.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Admetos = Neptune`, the entries connect uncertainty or deterioration with the possibility of clarity and depth. [Witte](#witte-entry) names neglected property, wear and decay but also coming from confusion to clarity; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disappointment, uncertainty through staff reductions, bodily endurance in water and metaphysical deepening. These constructive clauses qualify the difficult examples. Depression and decomposition remain historical source language, without supporting either a medical conclusion or the former unsourced house-secrets interpretation.
