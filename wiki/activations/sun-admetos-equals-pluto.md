## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 120; PDF page 116 (one-based).

Transformation, changes in the fertility of the soil. Decrease or change in the food intake.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 169; PDF page 83 (one-based).

Blocked personal development, restricted bodily growth, thorough personal transformation, developing physical endurance, changed bodily circulation, increasing human depression, and growing personal resistance.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Transformation or development, including blocked growth and increasing endurance or resistance.
- psychology: Thorough personal transformation; ABC also lists increasing depression.
- body/health: Growth, circulation and food intake are historical source themes, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: No particular relationship arrangement is established by these entries.
- events/manifestations: Changes in soil fertility and reduced or changed food intake; developing physical endurance.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's material changes and ABC's personal development are distinct settings; growth may be restricted or endurance developed.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries concern change, but Witte locates it in soil fertility and food intake while ABC adds personal and bodily development. Neither entry establishes separation or severance as the formula's core meaning.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Admetos = Pluto`, the entries concern transformation and the conditions of growth. [Witte](#witte-entry) names changes in soil fertility and food intake; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds blocked personal development, restricted bodily growth and thorough personal transformation, alongside developing endurance and increasing resistance. This preserves change that is constrained as well as capacities that develop within it. The bodily and depression clauses remain historical source attributions rather than medical conclusions.
