## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 120; PDF page 116 (one-based).

To go mentally to the bottom of a matter.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 169; PDF page 83 (one-based).

Blockage of mind and body, depressing human insights, restricted human culture, a mentally firmly established man, personal spiritual deepening, stabilization of mind and body, and a person's immersion in truth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thorough mental inquiry and personal deepening, with firmness or blockage of mind and body.
- psychology: Going to the bottom of a matter, stabilization and depressing insights.
- body/health: Mind-body stabilization or blockage is named without a specific bodily diagnosis.
- social/relationship: ABC includes restricted human culture.
- events/manifestations: Deep inquiry and immersion in truth; no particular external event is established.
- conflicts/notes: Blockage and stabilization remain distinct; depth is not equated with either one.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte's thorough inquiry overlaps ABC's deepening and immersion in truth. ABC also retains cultural restriction and mind-body blockage, qualifying an exclusively constructive account.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Admetos = Poseidon`, the entries emphasize going deeply into a matter or into truth. [Witte](#witte-entry) names thorough mental inquiry; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds spiritual deepening, mental firmness and stabilization of mind and body, alongside depressing insights, blockage and cultural restriction. The formula thus retains depth and stabilization as capacities while acknowledging that insight or restriction can be burdensome; it establishes no specific health condition.
