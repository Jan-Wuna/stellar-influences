## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 120; PDF page 116 (one-based).

To decline or to restrict physical love. Insensitive to pain. Love for the soil.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 168; PDF page 83 (one-based).

Enduring personal love, restriction of physical love, strengthening harmony within a person, strengthening love for a man, a peace blockage caused by a person, harmonious deepening of a person, and deep love for a man.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Enduring or deepened affection alongside restricted physical love.
- psychology: Strengthened harmony and harmonious personal deepening.
- body/health: Witte's insensitivity to pain is a historical attribution, not a medical conclusion.
- social/relationship: Enduring love, love for a man and a person blocking peace.
- events/manifestations: Declining physical love and love for soil are explicit Witte examples.
- conflicts/notes: Enduring love is not excessive love; restraint does not erase affection or harmony.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries include restriction of physical love; ABC also retains endurance, depth and harmony. Witte's pain-insensitivity clause does not establish bodily cramping or difficult release from pain.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Admetos = Venus`, affection may endure and deepen even where physical love is restricted. [ABC](#abc-entry) describes lasting love, strengthened harmony and harmonious personal depth, alongside a person blocking peace; [Witte](#witte-entry) adds declining physical love, love for soil and insensitivity to pain. The entries distinguish enduring attachment from bodily expression and retain both harmony and obstruction, with pain insensitivity kept as a particular source claim.
