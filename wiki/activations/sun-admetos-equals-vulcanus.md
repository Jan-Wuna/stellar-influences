## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 120; PDF page 116 (one-based).

Power through possessions of real estate. To be put under hard pressure. Death through crushing.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 169; PDF page 83 (one-based).

Blocked body energy, depression due to physical violence, influence of the Earth, restricted bodily strength, energy through personal firmness, enormous bodily blockage, bodily strength through endurance, and strong personal depression.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Power or pressure, with energy and bodily strength either sustained or blocked.
- psychology: Personal firmness; ABC also names strong depression and depression through violence.
- body/health: Bodily blockage and crushing death are historical source claims, not diagnoses or predictions.
- social/relationship: Power through real-estate ownership; exposure to pressure or violence.
- events/manifestations: Restricted strength, endurance-derived strength and Earth's influence.
- conflicts/notes: Possessing power and being under pressure are distinct positions; endurance does not establish invulnerability.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

The entries retain both power and constraint. ABC's energy through firmness and strength through endurance qualify its blocked-energy examples; Witte supplies a separate property setting.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Admetos = Vulcanus`, power and bodily force can be sustained through firmness or constrained by heavy pressure. [ABC](#abc-entry) places energy through personal firmness and strength through endurance alongside blocked energy, bodily restriction and violence; [Witte](#witte-entry) adds power from real-estate ownership and being placed under hard pressure. These distinguish exercising power from suffering it. Depression and crushing death remain historical source examples, rather than medical or mortality conclusions.
