## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 119; PDF page 115 (one-based).

Relations with successful people perhaps enjoying their protection. Acquaintance with many, with scientists and representatives of commerce and industry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 166; PDF page 82 (one-based).

Experienced man as partner, experience with other persons, successful people in the environment, freedom of the personal partner, body fullness of the partner, human masses in the environment, and personal environmental experiences.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A wide or successful human environment and experienced personal contacts.
- psychology: Personal experience is explicit; no fuller inner portrait is established.
- body/health: ABC names a partner's bodily fullness without a specific health claim.
- social/relationship: Experienced partners and acquaintances in science, commerce or industry; possible protection from successful people.
- events/manifestations: Many acquaintances, crowds nearby and experience of one's surroundings.
- conflicts/notes: Witte says protection is possible; neither entry promises success in every connection or diplomatic leadership.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

The entries overlap in successful people around the person. ABC's partner freedom and bodily fullness are additional particulars, not claims independently repeated by Witte.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Apollon = Asc`, the entries place success and experience in the people one meets or lives among. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes numerous acquaintances in science, commerce and industry, with possible protection from successful people; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds an experienced partner, successful people or crowds nearby and personal experience of the surroundings. Partner freedom and bodily fullness remain particular ABC meanings. The connection is a broad or accomplished human environment, with support kept conditional rather than guaranteed.
