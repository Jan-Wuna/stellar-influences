## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Success and expansion within a community or union.
- psychology: No specific inner disposition is established by these entries.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: Successful or scientific communities, with personal freedom within the group.
- events/manifestations: Collective expansion, personal success in a community and a famed artist.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's artist example is distinct from ABC's scientific community; fame does not establish universal popularity.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

The entries overlap in community success. ABC adds scale, science and personal freedom; Witte independently lists a famed artist.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Apollon = Cupido`, success takes a collective form. [Witte](#witte-entry) names a successful community or union; [ABC](#abc-entry) expands this to a growing community, successful people or scientists together, and personal success and freedom within that setting. The group can therefore be both the setting of accomplishment and a large gathering of people. Witte's famed artist remains a separate explicit expression, without making popularity a universal trait.
