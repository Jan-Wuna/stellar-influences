## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 119; PDF page 115 (one-based).

Glory and honor and success. Great financier. Richness. Croesus.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 166; PDF page 82 (one-based).

Experienced successful person, experienced financial man, successful commercial center, joy about personal success, person with a lot of money, personal legal knowledge, many happy people, and center of legal science

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Recognized success, prosperity and experience in finance or commerce.
- psychology: Joy in personal success and many happy people in ABC.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: Honored or successful people and financial expertise.
- events/manifestations: Wealth, a successful commercial center and legal knowledge or institutions.
- conflicts/notes: The favorable examples describe source meanings; they do not establish artistic success or guarantee wealth.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries emphasize success and substantial money. ABC adds experienced expertise and legal knowledge; these are further explicit settings rather than synonyms for wealth.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Apollon = Jupiter`, recognition and success have a prosperous, experienced expression. [Witte](#witte-entry) names glory, honor, wealth and a great financier; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds financial experience, a successful commercial center and joy in personal success. Its legal knowledge, legal-science center and happy people broaden the settings beyond money alone. The entries supply favorable meanings for this formula without guaranteeing a financial outcome.
