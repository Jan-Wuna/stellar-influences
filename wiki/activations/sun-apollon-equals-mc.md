## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 119; PDF page 115 (one-based).

To be successful. To achieve honors and dignities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 166; PDF page 82 (one-based).

My day off, my bodily fullness, my bodily experiences, my personal success, my experiences as a man, my personal freedom, my personal experience, and personal inner experiences.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personally experienced success, recognition and freedom.
- psychology: Personal and inner experience are explicit; no further character portrait is supplied.
- body/health: Bodily fullness and experience are named without a specific disorder.
- social/relationship: Honors and dignities describe recognition, without a particular relationship arrangement.
- events/manifestations: Personal success, a day off and achieved distinctions.
- conflicts/notes: Recognition and private freedom are distinct expressions; bodily fullness is not a medical conclusion.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte emphasizes achieved recognition; ABC adds the person's own experience, freedom and day off. These extend the scope beyond public honors without contradicting them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Apollon = MC`, success is described as one's own accomplishment and experience. [Witte](#witte-entry) names success, honors and dignities; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds personal freedom, a day off and bodily or inner experiences, including its gendered experience-as-a-man example. The formula thus includes recognized accomplishment and personally experienced latitude, while bodily fullness remains a separate source meaning rather than a health judgment.
