## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 119; PDF page 115 (one-based).

Successes in the public or in connection with women. The glory of a nation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 166; PDF page 82 (one-based).

Expansion of a people's body, a man's experience with a woman, success between man and woman, woman and man as scientists, a people's trade center, a woman's bodily fullness, and a woman's personal success.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public or collective success, with explicit female and relational examples.
- psychology: No specific inner disposition is established by these entries.
- body/health: ABC names a woman's bodily fullness without a specific disorder.
- social/relationship: Success involving women or between woman and man; both as scientists.
- events/manifestations: National glory, collective expansion, a people's trade center and personal success of a woman.
- conflicts/notes: The sources' gendered examples remain particular attributions; public success does not establish universal popularity.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte's public and national scope overlaps ABC's collective and commercial examples. ABC also retains personal female success and joint scientific activity, rather than a universal statement about ordinary life.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Apollon = Moon`, the entries place success in public, collective and female or relational settings. [Witte](#witte-entry) names public success, success connected with women and national glory; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a people's expansion and trade center, a woman's personal success and woman and man as scientists. The shared emphasis is accomplishment extending beyond an isolated individual, while the bodily-fullness and gendered examples remain source particulars rather than universal traits.
