## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 119; PDF page 115 (one-based).

Uncertain success or success in occult sciences. Make believe dignities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 167; PDF page 82 (one-based).

Experiences in the subtle body, an experienced person in metaphysics, successful expansion in the air, personal success through sensitivity, uncertainty through unfamiliar people, and many people by the water.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertain or apparent recognition, with success through sensitivity or in metaphysical subjects.
- psychology: Sensitivity and uncertainty through unfamiliar people.
- body/health: The subtle body is source doctrine; no specific medical condition is established.
- social/relationship: Unfamiliar people can be a setting of uncertainty; many people by water are also named.
- events/manifestations: Occult-science success, apparent dignities and successful expansion in air.
- conflicts/notes: Witte distinguishes uncertain success from occult success; neither makes every success deceptive.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte retains both uncertainty and success in occult sciences; ABC adds metaphysical experience and sensitivity. Its following Pluto development row has been excluded after scan verification.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Apollon = Neptune`, success may be uncertain or apparent, while the entries also allow accomplishment through sensitivity or metaphysical experience. [Witte](#witte-entry) distinguishes uncertain success, occult-science success and make-believe dignities; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds success through sensitivity, an experienced metaphysical person and subtle-body experience. Air expansion, unfamiliar people and gatherings by water are further explicit settings. The formula keeps those possibilities separate rather than treating every success as false or every subtle experience as proof of achievement.
