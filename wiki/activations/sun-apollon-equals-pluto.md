## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 119; PDF page 115 (one-based).

Success which develops slowly, one has to strive for or which is gained by working.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 167; PDF page 82 (one-based).

Successful personal development, unfolding personal freedom, development of personal knowledge, increasing personnel turnover, growing expansion of a center, and a scientific development center.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Success developed through work, personal growth or expanding knowledge.
- psychology: Personal knowledge develops; no fuller character portrait is supplied.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: Unfolding personal freedom and increasing personnel turnover.
- events/manifestations: Gradual earned success, expansion of a center and scientific development.
- conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes gradual effort; ABC does not make every personnel change a success.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

The entries overlap in developing success. ABC adds knowledge, freedom, organizational expansion and staffing change without specifying a dramatic conversion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Apollon = Pluto`, success is developed rather than merely bestowed. [Witte](#witte-entry) stresses slow accomplishment gained through striving or work; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful personal development, unfolding freedom and growing knowledge. Its expanding center, scientific development center and personnel turnover place change in an organizational setting as well. The shared concern is the development of accomplishment, without requiring a sudden or dramatic transformation.
