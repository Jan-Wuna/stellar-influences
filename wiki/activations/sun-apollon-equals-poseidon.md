## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 119; PDF page 115 (one-based).

Successes and experiences on the mental or spiritual plane. Success through like- minded persons, comrades or partners in fate.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 167; PDF page 82 (one-based).

Expansion of a cultural center, success through human reason, a free cultural center, human experiences of reason, people in Geistes-Wissenschaft, personal knowledge of the mind, and increasing personal clarity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental or spiritual success through reason, clarity and like-minded connections.
- psychology: Increasing clarity and experience of reason.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: Success through like-minded people, comrades or partners in fate.
- events/manifestations: Expansion or freedom of a cultural center and mental or spiritual experiences.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's Geistes-Wissenschaft is retained as its own term; no narrower discipline or leadership role is inferred.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte emphasizes mental or spiritual experience and helpful affinities; ABC adds reason, clarity and cultural institutions. Neither entry explicitly supplies love of truth or spiritual leadership for this orientation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Apollon = Poseidon`, success and experience take a mental, spiritual or cultural form. [Witte](#witte-entry) links them with like-minded people and comrades; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds success through reason, increasing clarity and the expansion or freedom of a cultural center. The connection is accomplishment supported by shared outlook or clearer understanding. ABC's Geistes-Wissenschaft remains an untranslated source term rather than grounds for assigning a specific discipline.
