## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 119; PDF page 115 (one-based).

Success in educational matters. Modest success in later part of life. Hindrance, decrease of success.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 167; PDF page 82 (one-based).

Success through personal perseverance, successful person in old age, loss of personal freedom, personal concentration on knowledge, multiplication of a man's tasks, loss of much money, and increasing personal problems.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Success through perseverance or education, qualified by hindrance, limitation or decline.
- psychology: Endurance and concentration on knowledge.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: Loss of personal freedom and increased tasks are explicit ABC conditions.
- events/manifestations: Educational or later-life success, decreased success and substantial money loss.
- conflicts/notes: Witte says modest later-life success; neither entry promises success in all labor.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries retain later-life success and limits on success. ABC's perseverance and knowledge focus are specific capacities, not guarantees against its losses or increasing problems.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Apollon = Saturn`, success is sustained through perseverance or education while remaining subject to limits. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes educational success and modest success later in life alongside hindrance or decline; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds endurance, concentration on knowledge and success in old age, but also lost freedom, more tasks, money loss and increasing problems. The entries therefore hold accomplishment and burden together, rather than promising that all hard work succeeds.
