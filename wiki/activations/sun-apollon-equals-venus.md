## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 119; PDF page 115 (one-based).

Successful in peace promotion. True friendship. Successes in art. To become known as a musician.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 166; PDF page 82 (one-based).

Successful man of the arts, love success of the man, personal freedom of the artist, personal love experiences, preference for persons of knowledge, a center for the study of art, and increasing human peace.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Success in art, peace promotion, friendship or love.
- psychology: Preference for knowledgeable people and personal love experience.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: True friendship, love success and an artist's personal freedom.
- events/manifestations: Recognition as a musician, successful artistic work, art study and increasing peace.
- conflicts/notes: Artistic recognition and personal freedom are separate meanings; the gendered love example remains attributed.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries emphasize art and peace. ABC adds freedom, love experience and knowledgeable people; Witte specifies friendship and recognition as a musician.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Apollon = Venus`, success is expressed through art and peaceful or affectionate relations. [Witte](#witte-entry) names peace promotion, true friendship, artistic success and recognition as a musician; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds love experience, an artist's freedom, knowledgeable people and a center for the study of art. The repeated art-and-peace themes give the formula a specific focus, while freedom and personal love success remain additional source meanings.
