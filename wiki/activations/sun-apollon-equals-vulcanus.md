#### ABC Entry

Expansion of masculine force, an energy center of science, a person's successful influence, the force of many people, personal abundance of power, success through bodily strength, and a person's growing power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Great success expressed through influence, collective force or bodily strength.
- psychology: No specific inner disposition is established by these entries.
- body/health: Bodily strength is named without a specific medical claim.
- social/relationship: Successful personal influence and the force of many people.
- events/manifestations: Growing power, expanded masculine force and a scientific energy center.
- conflicts/notes: ABC distinguishes force from power; success does not establish moral authority or mastery of every field.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte gives the superlative “greatest success”; ABC supplies concrete force, influence and energy settings. Its gendered example remains attributed rather than a universal claim about men.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Apollon = Vulcanus`, success is expressed with substantial strength, force or influence. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives the concise superlative “greatest success”; [ABC](#abc-entry) specifies successful influence, growing personal power, collective force and success through bodily strength, alongside a scientific energy center. These give the formula concrete forms of effective power without establishing universal mastery or judging how that power is used.
