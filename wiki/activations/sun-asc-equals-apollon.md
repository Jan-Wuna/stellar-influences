## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 80 (one-based).

Many acquaintances are formed in the field of science or commerce. To meet many people. Social gatherings with dignitaries and successful people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 101; PDF page 49 (one-based).

Expansion of a local center, an experienced personal partner, successful people in the surroundings, a local trade center, male trading partners, a personal partner's success, personal environmental experiences, and many people in the surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Numerous or accomplished contacts in an expanding local, scientific or commercial environment.
- psychology: Personal experience is explicit; no great-ideas character portrait is supplied.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: Experienced or successful partners, scientists, trading partners and dignitaries.
- events/manifestations: Many acquaintances, social gatherings and expansion of a local or trade center.
- conflicts/notes: Successful people and many contacts do not establish that every connection is happy.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries emphasize many people and scientific or commercial contacts. ABC adds local expansion, a partner's experience and a partner's success.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Asc = Apollon`, personal relations open onto a broad or accomplished human environment. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes many acquaintances in science or commerce and gatherings with dignitaries or successful people; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds an experienced partner, local commercial activity and an expanding center. A partner's success and personal experience of the surroundings remain explicit parts of that picture. The focus is the scale and quality of one's contacts, rather than an unsupported claim about great ideas or universal happiness.
