## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 23 (one-based), Sun/Asc, complete Chiron entry on this page.

Your energy helps others to become more aware of their own identity, and to realize their spiritual importance in the role they play in the world. The ability to bring a sense of meaning and purpose to relationships, and sometimes doing this in subtle or covert ways.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Helping others recognize identity and purpose through relationships.
- psychology: Awareness of one's identity and spiritual importance in one's worldly role.
- body/health: No bodily condition or treatment claim is supplied by this entry.
- social/relationship: Bringing meaning and purpose to relationships, sometimes subtly or covertly.
- events/manifestations: Interpersonal influence is described; no particular external event is specified.
- conflicts/notes: This single Sandbach entry does not establish perception of hidden purpose in everything.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Only one source entry is present. Its subtle or covert influence concerns meaning in relationships, not a general doctrine of hidden environmental purposes.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Asc = Chiron`, [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes an influence that helps other people recognize who they are and the importance of their role in the world. Bringing meaning and purpose to relationships is the practical expression of that influence, and the entry allows it to operate subtly or covertly. Its focus is this interpersonal recognition of identity and purpose; it supplies no bodily claim or broader assertion about hidden meaning in everything.
