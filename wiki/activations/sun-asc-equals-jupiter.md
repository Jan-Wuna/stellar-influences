## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Acquaintances that are very fortunate for the native. To enjoy advantages and support from relationships.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Financial center at the place, joy on the day at the place, joy with other persons, manly financial partner, glial people in the vicinity, personal partner rights, personal financial partner, personal environmental success, and right center at the place

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A good companion, gaining trust and confidence, a religious and social outlook. Joint success, public recognition.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An enthusiasm for engaging in meaningful interactions with others. The will ingness to learn and grow in terms of becoming more aware of one’s identity. Success through coming across to others as self-confident and purposeful.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

The generosity you show when relating to others; a need to give wisdom and advice to others when you meet them; realizing the authority of others in ways that make them aware that you appreciate them for who they are.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Asc = Jupiter`: charisma, popularity, and growth through direct contact with others and the local environment. Main caution: inflation: self-presentation can become excessive, entitled, or careless about limits.
