## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Co-workers. Social relations with male persons, soldiers and co-workers. Fights and brawls. To quarrel. Persons in the environment who are not under self control.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work with other people, work day at the place, actions of other persons, corporal work for the environment, personal work partners, personal partner activity, activity of other persons, and effective people in the vicinity

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

The fighting spirit, the power to establish oneself in the world, industry and diligence. Cooperation, association with others fighting for common interests. Quarrels.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One asserts oneself with a self-confident demeanor. Conflict created by coming on in an overly self-important way. To work on developing a meaningful way of present ing oneself outwardly.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `96`

#### Munkasey Entry

Enhances your personal levels of enthusiasm and energy; strengthens your personality by adding energy and power; indignation over how others accept you as a person; noisy clamor or welcoming shouts; an active daily lifestyle.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Action and assertion in direct relationships.
- psychology: Confidence, enthusiasm, fighting spirit, and possible indignation.
- body/health: Physical work and daily activity are mentioned; no specific diagnosis is given.
- social/relationship: Co-workers and people pursuing common interests.
- events/manifestations: Cooperation, work, quarrels, and potentially brawls.
- conflicts/notes: Constructive effort and conflict are both explicit possibilities; physical strain is not stated.

## Contradictions

Cooperation and quarrelling are alternative expressions in these entries, not mutually exclusive claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies the Sun/Asc axis as active engagement with others: work, self-assertion, and the defense of shared interests. The [ABC](#abc-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) entries connect co-workers and diligence with cooperation, while [Witte](#witte-entry) also gives quarrels and brawls. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) identifies a specific source of friction, an overly self-important manner; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds energy and an active daily life.
