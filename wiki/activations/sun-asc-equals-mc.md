## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Personal conduct toward other people. The impression made by the personal environment on the native.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 100; one-based PDF p. 49, complete MC row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Beseelter persönlicher Partner, beseelte Menschen in der Nähe, mein männlicher Partner, mein persönlicher Partner, meine persönliche Umwelt, Partner-Seele in der Nähe, Personen meiner Umwelt, persönliche beseelte Umwelt, Tag - Ort - Minute

Editorial translation: A soulful personal partner; soulful people nearby; my male partner; my personal partner; my personal surroundings; a partner's soul nearby; people in my surroundings; personal soulful surroundings; day – place – minute.

The source's soul-language and final day–place–minute phrase are retained without adding a mechanism or timing rule.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

The individual attitude to other persons, desire for soul unions, seeking of mental or intellectual contact, attainment of esteem and respect.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One work involves relating to other people in a meaningful and vitalizing manner. Coming across as self-confident has a big effect on one’s career. One sees that the way in which one presents oneself in one’s career is important, and can be used to enhance both the work and one’s place in the world.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `96`

#### Munkasey Entry

Defining a personal sense of style and self; becoming more at ease with who you are as a person and how you are able to grow and develop with your personality; personal expertise recognized and appreciated by others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Self-definition and personal conduct in relationships.
- psychology: Becoming at ease with oneself and seeking meaningful contact.
- body/health: No specific health claim is given in these activation entries.
- social/relationship: Personal partners, people nearby, mental contact, esteem and recognition of expertise are represented.
- events/manifestations: Career effects of self-presentation and appreciation by others occur alongside ABC's personal surroundings.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's soul-language adds a relational setting without establishing leadership or rigid self-importance.

## Contradictions

The entries connect personal attitude with contact and recognition, while Sandbach emphasizes career and ABC retains partners and the immediate surroundings. Those settings are not interchangeable, and ABC's soul-language does not establish a spiritual mechanism.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies the Sun/Asc axis through one's personal attitude, sense of self and recognized place among others. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) entries concern conduct, the impression made by one's surroundings and the search for meaningful contact and respect. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) keeps that contact concrete through one's personal partner and people nearby, using its own soul-language.

[Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) connects confident presentation with career, while [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) emphasizes ease with one's personality and appreciation of personal expertise. Personal relating and recognition therefore form the supported connection, without making a public leadership role necessary.
