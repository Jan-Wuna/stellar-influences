## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Fellow students, schoolmates or teachers. Relations to young people. Relationships with similar thinking people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Thoughts of other people, youth centre in the village, young person as a partner, opinions of people from the environment, news centre of a place, corporal movement in the village, person traffic in the village, and personal environment of youth

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

One's mental attitude, seeking mental or intellectual stimuli, critical attitude toward other people. Contact with young people, scientists or business people.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To communicate in a self-confident (egotistical) manner. To think much of the importance of how things appear. Cleverness in finding ways of drawing attention to oneself and/or making oneself seem important. A mind capable of making meaningful and purposeful use of opportunities.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `96`

#### Munkasey Entry

Discussions of the interpersonal roles you have with others when you meet together; a clever way of showing others that you are the person you are; information on personal needs and motivations for understanding others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Communication and intellectual contact in personal relationships.
- psychology: Seeking stimulation, forming critical judgments, and thinking about appearances.
- body/health: ABC mentions bodily movement locally; no specific health claim is given.
- social/relationship: Students, teachers, young people, scientists, and business contacts.
- events/manifestations: Discussions, exchanges of information, and purposeful use of opportunities.
- conflicts/notes: Attention-seeking is sourced; the former 'nervous egoism' and restlessness are not established.

## Contradictions

Intellectual exchange and concern with appearances are different emphases, with no direct contradiction stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies the Sun/Asc axis through thought, communication, and the way one makes oneself understood in encounters. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) entries name students, teachers, young people, and intellectual or business contacts. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) describes discussing interpersonal roles and understanding personal needs; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds both purposeful use of opportunities and the possibility of using clever presentation to attract importance.
