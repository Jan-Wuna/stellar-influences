## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Suddenly meeting acquaintances. Excitement and tension through other people. Sudden events caused by others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Agitated person in the near, dynamic personal partner, excited other persons, new people in the near, nervous man's partner, personal tensions in the surrounding area, reform in the local centre, and technical location centre

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

The desire to establish oneself in the world by violent means, agitated behavior. Sudden experiences with other persons.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Others see one as unusual, and confident in that distinctiveness. An unin hibited display of self-importance. To project one’s individuality in a conscious and confident manner. An original and/or unique way of coming across to other as self- important.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Sudden or unexpected ways of demonstrating who you are; a strong desire to have an unconventional appearance; adapting a new sense of what you are as a person; gaining insight and ideas just by being near other people.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Asc = Uranus`: originality, quick adaptation, and the power to awaken or unsettle others simply through presence. Main caution: instability: restlessness, contrariness, or abrupt self-assertion can disrupt relationships and continuity.
