## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To inspire and guide other people to a goal or objective. Interaction with creative and motivated persons. Ties also with military personnel. Fire alarm.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 101 / PDF page 49, Zeus glyph under Aszendent + Sonne. English translation checked against the complete row.

Efforts of other persons, leadership of a local center, creative people nearby, achievements of other persons, achievements of the personal partner, location and day of procreation, efforts in the personal surroundings, and creative male partner.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Goal-directed interaction and shared creative effort.
- psychology: Inspiring or guiding others toward an objective.
- body/health: ABC names procreation, but gives no medical condition or guaranteed conception.
- social/relationship: Creative, motivated people and partners engaged in achievement.
- events/manifestations: Leadership, joint effort, military contacts, and Witte's fire-alarm example.
- conflicts/notes: Creative partnership, procreation, military contact and a fire alarm remain distinct source examples, without requiring them to occur together.

## Contradictions

Achievement, military contact, and procreation are separate examples rather than mutually necessary expressions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies the Sun/Asc axis through directing people toward a goal and meeting others engaged in purposeful creation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes inspiration, guidance, and contact with motivated people; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) emphasizes effort, leadership, and a partner's achievements. Witte also lists military contacts and a fire alarm, while ABC separately includes procreation. These are distinct source examples, not predictions that must accompany productive relationships.
