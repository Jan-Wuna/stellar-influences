### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source: supplied Sandbach PDF p. 16, complete Moon entry under Sun/Chiron.

An instinct for knowing what is truly important, and the ability to find a way to reveal it. The ability to nurture others in exactly the ways that they need. To feel inse cure when not is following their true spiritual purpose.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Instinctive recognition of importance expressed through care.
- psychology: An intuitive sense of what is important and how to reveal it.
- body/health: Nurturing is mentioned; no bodily diagnosis or treatment is given.
- social/relationship: Care responsive to another person's needs.
- events/manifestations: Revealing what matters and offering responsive nurture.
- conflicts/notes: The final insecurity clause is damaged in the source and is excluded from interpretation.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies the Sun/Chiron axis through instinctive recognition of what matters and responsive care for others. The intact sentences of the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) connect perceiving importance with finding ways to reveal it, then with nurturing people according to their needs. The final sentence is grammatically damaged in the supplied PDF, so no confident condition linking insecurity to following or neglecting purpose is derived from it.
