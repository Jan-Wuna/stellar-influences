### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To seek out people that can help to get one more deeply in touch with one’s purpose in life. One is learning in this lifetime to find the hidden meanings that are ev erywhere around one. The key to evolution lies in progressively seeing more and more clearly the ways the ego retards personal growth.

## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies the Sun/Chiron axis through seeking people who help one recognize a deeper life purpose. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) links these contacts with learning to see hidden meanings and recognizing how ego patterns hinder personal growth. The direction of help matters here: the person seeks assistance in finding purpose; the entry does not expressly describe them as the helper of others.
