## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies the Sun/Chiron axis through frustration in the search for meaning and fear of releasing ego problems. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) also gives a developmental possibility: deeper confidence, grounded in what the author calls alignment with the Higher Self, can support the spiritual path. The difficulty and the possibility of confidence belong together; discipline or the deliberate stripping away of priorities is not stated here.
