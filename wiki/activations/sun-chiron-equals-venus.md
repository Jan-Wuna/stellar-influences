## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Healing the ego through love. To be able to appreciate the true spiritual purpose behind all things. To bring new and deeper meaning to relationships. The ability to ap preciate the ways in which egos have become damaged.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Love and appreciation that deepen relational meaning.
- psychology: Sensitivity to injured ego patterns and appreciation of purpose.
- body/health: Healing refers to the ego, not a bodily treatment.
- social/relationship: Love gives relationships new and deeper meaning.
- events/manifestations: Recognizing ego injury and bringing meaning into relationships.
- conflicts/notes: The spiritual-healing language is attributed; no cure is established.

## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies the Sun/Chiron axis through love and appreciation that give relationships deeper meaning. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) connects love with healing the ego and with understanding how egos have been hurt. Its organizing theme is appreciative attention to purpose and injury within relationships, rather than an unsourced opposition between spiritual love and physical attraction.
