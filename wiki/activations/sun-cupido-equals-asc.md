### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page: 111.

#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source page: 158 (printed); PDF page: 78.

#### ABC Entry

Other persons of a community, community day at the place, corporal partner community, artistic people in the environment, artist center at the place, men's community at the place, human environment community, and center of a local community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Community membership encountered through nearby people.
- Psychology: No distinct psychological trait is stated.
- Body/health: Physical companionship is named; no illness is described.
- Social/relationship: Bride or bridegroom; local artists and associations.
- Events/manifestations: A local community or art center brings people together.
- Conflicts/notes: The wedding role is one example within wider local association.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses wedding roles and associations; ABC extends the setting to the local community.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Sun/Cupido through the people and associations in one's immediate environment. A bride or bridegroom gives this relationship a personal form, while artists, physical companionship and a local community center broaden it to shared social surroundings. The focus is contact with a community through actual partners and neighbors, not an interchangeable reading of the other orientations. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
