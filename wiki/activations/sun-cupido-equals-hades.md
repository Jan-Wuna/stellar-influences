## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page: 111.

#### Witte Entry

Awkward or miserable wedding. Low company. Vulgar or old art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source page: 159 (printed); PDF page: 78.

#### ABC Entry

Deprivations of a person for the community, a mysterious community of persons, a shared personal past, a criminal community center, people of a dangerous community, and persons of a traditional community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Hardship or compromised circumstances in communal life.
- Psychology: Personal deprivation and a mysterious group atmosphere.
- Body/health: No specific medical statement appears.
- Social/relationship: An unhappy wedding, dangerous company or a traditional community.
- Events/manifestations: Difficult group involvement and art associated with age or vulgarity.
- Conflicts/notes: A shared past or tradition is not itself evidence of criminality.

## Contradictions

Hardship, danger and shared tradition are separate examples, not equivalent judgments.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Sun/Cupido through difficult belonging: an unhappy wedding, personal deprivation for a community, or involvement with dangerous company. The entries also reach backward through old art, shared personal history and traditional associations. Those historical forms should remain distinct from the explicitly criminal example; the passage does not make every community with a past disreputable. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
