## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page: 111.

#### Witte Entry

Fortunate marriage. Fortunate communities. Wealth acquired through art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source page: 158 (printed); PDF page: 78.

#### ABC Entry

Successful community of persons, finances of a community of persons, human joy in the community, persons of a legal community, personal artistic success, personal rights in the community, and center of a financial community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Good fortune and material success through shared life.
- Psychology: Joy in community participation.
- Body/health: No specific bodily claim is given.
- Social/relationship: Fortunate marriage, community and legal association.
- Events/manifestations: Art brings wealth; communal finances and personal success flourish.
- Conflicts/notes: The legal and financial examples extend the marital theme.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes fortunate marriage and artistic wealth; ABC adds communal finances and legal rights.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Sun/Cupido through fortunate participation in marriage, art or a wider community. Joy and prosperity reinforce one another in the entry: shared life can bring happiness, artistic work can bring wealth, and collective finances or legal associations can support personal success. These are the formula's stated possibilities, not a promise that membership guarantees prosperity. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
