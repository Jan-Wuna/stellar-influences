### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 115; PDF page 111 (one-based).

Thoughts of marriage. News of a marriage. Thoughts about art. Honeymoon trips. A person with an exclusive interest in local club life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 158; PDF page 78 (one-based).

Shared conversations among men, a community of young people, shared bodily movement, opinions of a men's community, a passenger-transport community, personal art of speech, and the center of a media community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought, news and conversation concerning marriage, art or group life.
- psychology: Interest in marriage, art or local clubs; no fuller character portrait is supplied.
- body/health: Shared bodily movement is named without a specific disorder.
- social/relationship: Group conversations, young people's communities and marital thoughts or news.
- events/manifestations: Honeymoon trips, media activity and a passenger-transport community.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's exclusive local-club interest is one example; group communication is not limited to family speech.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte emphasizes thoughts or news about marriage and art; ABC adds group communication, movement and media. These are different settings for shared human activity.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Cupido = Mercury`, participation in a relationship or group takes a communicative form. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes thoughts and news about marriage, thoughts about art and interest in club life; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds shared conversations, community opinions, the art of speech and a media-community center. Honeymoon travel, shared bodily movement and transport groups are further explicit settings. The formula therefore concerns how communal or marital life is thought about, reported or expressed, without reducing it to family membership alone.
