## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 115; PDF page 111 (one-based).

Unfortunate condition in married life. The deceived man. Dissolution of communities. Mediumistic artist. Many people without shelter.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 159; PDF page 78 (one-based).

disappointed human community, shared refinement of the body, personal sensitivity in the community, weak community of persons, uncertainty of a community of men, and center of a community at the water

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community or marriage affected by uncertainty, disappointment or dissolution, alongside sensitivity.
- psychology: Personal sensitivity within a group and collective disappointment.
- body/health: ABC's shared bodily refinement is source language, not a specific medical claim.
- social/relationship: Unfortunate married life, deception and weak or dissolving communities.
- events/manifestations: People without shelter, a mediumistic artist and a community center by water.
- conflicts/notes: Difficult communal conditions do not establish antisocial character or absence of family attachment.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte emphasizes adverse marital or collective conditions but also names a mediumistic artist. ABC adds sensitivity and bodily refinement, so the formula is not exhausted by dissolution alone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Cupido = Neptune`, the entries describe sensitivity and uncertainty within shared life. [ABC](#abc-entry) names personal sensitivity, collective disappointment and a weak or uncertain community; [Witte](#witte-entry) adds difficult married life, deception, dissolving communities and people without shelter. ABC's bodily refinement and waterside community center, and Witte's mediumistic artist, remain additional expressions. These concern conditions of communal life without establishing an antisocial disposition or a lack of family attachment.
