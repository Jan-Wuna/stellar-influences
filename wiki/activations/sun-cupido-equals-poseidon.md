## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Cupido = Poseidon`, community life takes an intellectual, cultural or spiritual form. [Witte](#witte-entry) names the spiritually minded artist and think-tank member; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds shared insight and clarity, art and cultural centers, and personal spiritualization within a group. The connection is participation in a community of thought or culture, with its clarity and spiritual development retained as source meanings rather than a claim that every such group is ideal.
