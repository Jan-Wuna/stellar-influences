## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 115; PDF page 111 (one-based).

An unfaithful husband. Divorce. Separation from the family. Separated from a personal association.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 159; PDF page 78 (one-based).

A serious community of people, shared loss on the day, a community of old people, a community of maturing people, a man's concentration on art, personal problems in the community, and a disturbed men's community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Serious or troubled shared life, with separation, maturation or concentrated artistic attention.
- psychology: Concentration on art is explicit; no general solitary disposition is established.
- body/health: Old or maturing people are named without a specific bodily disorder.
- social/relationship: Divorce, separation from family or association, and serious or disturbed communities.
- events/manifestations: Shared loss and personal problems within a group.
- conflicts/notes: Maturing community and concentrated art remain possible; infidelity is Witte's particular historical example.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte foregrounds separation; ABC also includes serious, old or maturing communities and concentration on art. These show shared life under demanding conditions rather than proving universal solitude.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Cupido = Saturn`, the entries describe the demands and possible limits of shared life. [Witte](#witte-entry) names divorce or separation from family and associations; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds shared loss, community problems and disturbance, but also serious or maturing groups and concentration on art. The formula therefore retains both separation and continued participation under weightier conditions. Witte's unfaithful husband is an attributed example, not a character judgment about every participant.
