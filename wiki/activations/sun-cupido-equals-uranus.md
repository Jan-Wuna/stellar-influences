## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden events, renewal or excitement within marriage, art and community life.
- psychology: Collective excitement and tension.
- body/health: Shared bodily rhythm is named without a specific disorder.
- social/relationship: Sudden marriage bonds, new communities and excited company.
- events/manifestations: Sudden art-world events, an eccentric artist and a technical-community center.
- conflicts/notes: The local entries do not explicitly supply divorce; renewal need not mean dissolution.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries emphasize excitement or sudden change in shared life. Witte supplies marriage and art settings; ABC adds renewal, bodily rhythm and technical community.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Cupido = Uranus`, shared life becomes dynamic, excited or suddenly changed. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a sudden marriage bond, excited company and unexpected art-world events; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds renewed or new communities, shared bodily rhythm and collective tension. The entries preserve both the formation of a bond and the unsettled energy of a group, with the eccentric artist and technical center as specific settings rather than grounds for assuming divorce.
