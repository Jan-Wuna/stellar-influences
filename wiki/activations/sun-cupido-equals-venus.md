## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 115; PDF page 111 (one-based).

A love match. Communion of love. Harmonious marriage or community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 158; PDF page 78 (one-based).

A community's peace center, shared joy on the day, harmony of a men's community, a harmonious community of people, a community's art center, a personal community of love, and a day of peace for a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Love, harmony and shared joy in marriage or community.
- psychology: Shared joy is explicit; no fuller character portrait is supplied.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: A love match, communion of love and harmonious shared life.
- events/manifestations: Community peace or art centers and a day of peace.
- conflicts/notes: The favorable source meanings describe possible forms of shared life, not a guarantee for every marriage.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

The entries overlap in harmony and love. ABC adds explicit peace, collective joy and an art center, extending the scope beyond family alone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Cupido = Venus`, shared life is expressed through affection, harmony and joy. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a love match and harmonious marriage or community; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds shared joy, peace and art centers, and a community of love. These overlapping themes connect intimate attachment with wider peaceful or artistic participation, while remaining meanings of the entry rather than a promise that every union will be harmonious.
