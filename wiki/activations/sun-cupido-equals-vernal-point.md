## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

The entries have distinct emphases: Witte's marital role and fortunate day, and ABC's public community settings. Neither should be used to infer an unlisted family or artist role in the surroundings.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Cupido = Vernal Point`, [ABC](#abc-entry) places communal life in public view: community days, art centers and public groups or centers are its concrete expressions. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives the distinct personal role of the bridegroom and the example of a lucky day. The page therefore retains public communal participation alongside that marital particular, without turning the favorable day into a timing rule or replacing the entries with generic family membership.
