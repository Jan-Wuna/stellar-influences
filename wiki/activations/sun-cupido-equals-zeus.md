## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 115; PDF page 111 (one-based).

Married life blessed with children. Creative associates. To be a leader in a society and to give it a direction.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 159; PDF page 78 (one-based).

A community's leadership center, a creative community of people, achievements of a men's community, personal effort within the community, personal efforts toward synthesis, and a creative art community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creative communal effort, leadership and direction.
- psychology: Creativity and personal effort are explicit capacities.
- body/health: Witte's children-in-marriage example is not a fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: Creative associates, leadership in a society and married life with children.
- events/manifestations: Collective achievements, a leadership center and a creative art community.
- conflicts/notes: The entry includes art and family settings, but does not reduce all communal leadership to a family creator.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries connect community with creativity and direction. ABC adds collective achievement and synthesis efforts; Witte adds the particular marriage-with-children example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Cupido = Zeus`, communal participation is directed toward creative work or a shared aim. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes creative associates and a leader giving a society direction; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a leadership center, collective achievements, personal effort and a creative art community. Witte's married life with children remains an additional historical example. The focus is the creative effort and direction of shared life, rather than family identity alone.
