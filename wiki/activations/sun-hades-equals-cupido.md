## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

Poor wedding. Joint experience of difficulties. Wedding of criminals. To use one’s artistic abilities when committing crimes, e.g. forgers of paper money.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 161; PDF page 79 (one-based).

A disadvantaged community of people, dangerous people in the community, a community of old people, a shortage of men in a community, a community of people in need, people in a traditional community, and a community's hidden center.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared difficulty, disadvantaged community life or concealed collective activity.
- psychology: No specific inner disposition is established by these entries.
- body/health: Old people and need are named without a specific bodily disorder.
- social/relationship: Difficult weddings, disadvantaged groups, a shortage of men and traditional communities.
- events/manifestations: A hidden community center and Witte's artistic ability used in crime.
- conflicts/notes: Old or traditional communities are not equated with criminal groups; family mourning is not explicitly supplied.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries concern shared difficulty. Witte gives criminal and artistic misuse examples; ABC also includes old people, shortage, need and tradition, which do not by themselves imply criminality.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Cupido`, difficulty is shared within a marriage or community. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a poor wedding and joint hardship, with criminal weddings or artistic skill used in crime as further examples; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds disadvantaged groups, people in need, old people, a shortage of men and a hidden communal center. Its traditional community is another distinct setting. These passages support collective hardship or concealment without assigning criminality to every disadvantaged or traditional group.
