## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

Action of the lungs or of the liver. Egotist. Rag-man. To make money through dirty work, waste material, rubbish.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 160; PDF page 79 (one-based).

Success through personal renunciation, a man's secret of success, a person's financial crime, joy through personal deprivation, personal financial distress, personal legal disadvantages, legal person in service, and a person's past happiness

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Gain or joy connected with deprivation, renunciation or work with discarded materials.
- psychology: Witte's egotist label is attributed; ABC includes joy through deprivation and past happiness.
- body/health: Witte's lung/liver statement is historical source language, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Legal disadvantage and service are explicit ABC settings.
- events/manifestations: Earning through dirty work or rubbish, financial distress, financial crime and a secret of success.
- conflicts/notes: Gain and deprivation coexist; the criminal and character examples do not define every instance.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte's earnings from discarded materials and ABC's success through renunciation provide specific gain-related meanings. ABC also retains distress and legal disadvantage; those are not erased by the success clauses.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Jupiter`, gain or satisfaction is linked with deprivation, renunciation or work others may discard. [Witte](#witte-entry) names earning through dirty work, waste or rubbish; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds success through renunciation and joy through deprivation, while retaining financial distress, legal disadvantage and financial crime as separate possibilities. Its past happiness and secret of success broaden the personal-history theme. Witte's organ statement and egotist label remain historical attributions rather than medical or character judgments.
