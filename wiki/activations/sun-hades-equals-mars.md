## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

Blood diseases. Occupation with waste material and garbage. To cause trouble. Ruffian. Inclined to homicide.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 160; PDF page 79 (one-based).

A man's labor service, difficult physical work, dangerous actions on the day, a person's secret activity, actions of a person from the past, criminal actions of a man, and a person renouncing work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Difficult, concealed or troublesome activity, including work with waste.
- psychology: Witte's ruffian and homicide-inclination labels remain attributed historical claims.
- body/health: Blood diseases are Witte's source attribution, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Labor service and trouble caused through action; no particular bond is specified.
- events/manifestations: Waste work, dangerous or secret activity, criminal actions and renouncing work.
- conflicts/notes: Ordinary difficult work is not equated with crime; tools and soil are not specified locally.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

The entries include work and adverse action but do not equate them. Witte's severe character and medical examples remain separate from ABC's labor-service or difficult-work settings.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Mars`, action is placed in difficult, concealed or troublesome conditions. [Witte](#witte-entry) names occupation with waste or garbage and causing trouble; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds labor service, difficult physical work, secret activity and renouncing work, alongside dangerous or criminal actions. These settings should remain distinct: difficult work does not establish criminal behavior. Witte's blood-disease and homicide-inclination statements remain historical source claims rather than medical or character conclusions.
