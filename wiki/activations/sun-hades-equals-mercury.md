## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

To think of sick and unhappy people. Diseases of the head or face. To be familiar with dirty matters. Opinions meeting with rejection and resistance. To appear repulsive. Messenger of bad news. To threaten. To instill fear.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 160; PDF page 79 (one-based).

Reports on disadvantaged persons, difficult corporal movement, criminal youth center, disadvantages for young people, news about people in need, personal transport service, personal renunciation thoughts, and daily news service

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought or communication concerning need, disadvantage or distress.
- psychology: Thinking of unhappy people and personal renunciation; Witte also names instilling fear.
- body/health: Head/face diseases and difficult bodily movement remain historical source attributions.
- social/relationship: Rejected opinions, threats and disadvantages affecting young people.
- events/manifestations: Reports about need, bad news, news or transport service and a criminal youth center.
- conflicts/notes: Reporting hardship is distinct from threatening people; the source's appearance judgment is not adopted as fact.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries concern thought or news about difficulty. Witte additionally names rejection and threatening speech; ABC includes service functions, which should not be equated with threats.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Mercury`, difficulty becomes a subject of thought, opinion or news. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes thinking of sick or unhappy people, rejected opinions and bad news, with threats and fear as further expressions; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds reports about disadvantaged people, youthful disadvantage, renunciation and news or transport service. The distinction is between communicating about hardship and using communication adversely. Bodily and appearance claims remain historical source attributions rather than diagnoses or judgments of the speaker.
