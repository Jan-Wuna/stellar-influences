## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

Anesthesia. Difficulties through falsehood. Death through attempts on one’s life. Drowned persons. To be unidentified after death.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 161; PDF page 79 (one-based).

An old and weak body, disappointing personal disadvantages, people in dirty air, disadvantages to a person through water, personal lack of air, a person's unclear past, hidden bodily weakness, and lack of water on the day.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Concealed weakness, unclear personal history or difficulty through falsehood and adverse conditions.
- psychology: Disappointment and an unclear past are named without a fuller character portrait.
- body/health: Weakness, anesthesia and lack of air are historical source claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Difficulty through falsehood; no particular bond is specified.
- events/manifestations: Dirty air, water disadvantage or shortage, and Witte's drowning/identity/mortality examples.
- conflicts/notes: Mortality claims remain attributed; Pluto's following development row has been excluded after scan inspection.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte emphasizes falsehood, anesthesia and severe mortality examples; ABC adds hidden weakness, personal history and air or water conditions. These do not establish a general diagnostic rule about concealed illness.
