## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

Sorrowful, dubious connections. Pursued by misfortune. Acquaintance with criminals.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 160; PDF page 79 (one-based).

Meeting with service personnel, relations with criminal persons, associated with people in need, disadvantages in a personal connection, connections of a person to the past, and hidden connection to a man

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections involving sorrow, disadvantage, need or concealment.
- psychology: Sorrow is named without a fuller character portrait.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is supplied by these entries.
- social/relationship: Service personnel, people in need, criminal acquaintances and a hidden connection to a man.
- events/manifestations: Disadvantage within a personal bond and ties to the past.
- conflicts/notes: Contact with need or service is not equated with criminality; sickness in unions is not explicitly supplied.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries include difficult or dubious connections. ABC adds service, need, the past and concealment, which should retain their separate meanings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Node`, disadvantage or sorrow is expressed through a connection. [Witte](#witte-entry) names sorrowful or dubious ties and criminal acquaintances; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds contacts with service personnel or people in need, personal disadvantage within a bond, ties to the past and a hidden connection to a man. The formula therefore includes burdensome, concealed or historically rooted relationships, while keeping service and need distinct from criminal association and supplying no specific illness claim.
