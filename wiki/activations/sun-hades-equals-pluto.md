## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

A sickness develops. Bodily effects develop. Tainted food. Things or matters which disappoint through poor quality.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 161; PDF page 79 (one-based).

Dangerous personal development, a person's sorrowful change, deficient bodily development, an adverse change involving the man, hidden personal unfolding, growing personal distress, and growing personal disadvantages.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Adverse or disappointing development, including hidden change and increasing disadvantage.
- psychology: Sorrow and personal distress accompany change in ABC.
- body/health: Developing sickness, bodily effects and deficient development remain historical source claims.
- social/relationship: An adverse change involving a man is named without a fuller relationship arrangement.
- events/manifestations: Tainted food, poor-quality matters and growing personal disadvantages.
- conflicts/notes: Neither source entry specifies surgery or a required intervention.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries describe adverse development or disappointing quality. ABC also retains hidden personal unfolding, without identifying a treatment or guaranteeing a particular event.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Pluto`, the entries describe development under adverse or disappointing conditions. [ABC](#abc-entry) names dangerous or sorrowful change, hidden personal unfolding and growing distress or disadvantage; [Witte](#witte-entry) adds poor quality, tainted food and developing bodily effects or sickness. The common focus is difficulty developing or becoming more consequential. Its bodily claims remain historical attributions and supply no basis for prescribing surgery or another intervention.
