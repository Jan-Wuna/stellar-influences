## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

A body without vitality, not fit to live. To separate, to seclude oneself for bodily reasons. Disagreeableness in the mental sphere.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 161; PDF page 79 (one-based).

Old mind in the body, insightful renunciation of the man, knowledge of an old man, mentally disadvantaged person, mental distress of people, lack of insight in the day, personal knowledge of the past, and past culture of man

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental or spiritual disadvantage and distress, alongside insight connected with age or the past.
- psychology: Mental distress, limited insight and insightful renunciation.
- body/health: Witte's vitality and viability wording remains a historical attribution, not a health or worth judgment.
- social/relationship: Seclusion for bodily reasons and renunciation of a man are particular examples.
- events/manifestations: Knowledge from the past and past human culture.
- conflicts/notes: Insight and limited insight coexist; no explicit “ugly truths” doctrine is supplied.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte emphasizes bodily seclusion and mental disagreeableness. ABC also retains insight, an old man's knowledge and past culture, so the account should preserve more than distress alone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Poseidon`, the entries concern disadvantage or distress in mental and spiritual life, with age and the past as additional sources of insight. [ABC](#abc-entry) retains knowledge of an old man, insight from the past and earlier culture alongside mental distress or limited understanding; [Witte](#witte-entry) adds mental disagreeableness and bodily reasons for seclusion. Witte's severe vitality wording remains an attributed historical claim, without supporting a judgment of bodily viability or a person's worth.
