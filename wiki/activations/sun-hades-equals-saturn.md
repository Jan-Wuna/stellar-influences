## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

Chronic diseases. Lingering illness. Grief and losses through rejection or oppression. To be banished. Prison.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 161; PDF page 79 (one-based).

A person maturing through deprivation, a patient old man, a man's concentration on the past, an old person's need, personal disadvantages in old age, a man's hard past, and losses through service personnel.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Deprivation, rejection or oppression, with patience and maturation through hardship.
- psychology: Patience and concentrated attention to the past.
- body/health: Chronic or lingering illness is Witte's historical attribution, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Rejection, oppression, banishment or prison; losses through service personnel.
- events/manifestations: Need and disadvantage in old age and a hard personal past.
- conflicts/notes: ABC names maturation through deprivation; this does not make hardship desirable or medically necessary.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte emphasizes prolonged illness and exclusion. ABC adds patience, maturation and personal history without denying the burdens of deprivation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Saturn`, the entries describe hardship that bears on personal endurance and one's relation to the past. [Witte](#witte-entry) names grief or loss through rejection and oppression, including banishment or prison; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds patience, maturation through deprivation, need in old age and concentration on a hard past. Witte's chronic-illness claims remain historical attributions. The formula includes the capacity to mature under difficulty without reducing every expression to ill health or endorsing the hardship itself.
