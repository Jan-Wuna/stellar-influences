### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 161; PDF page 79 (one-based).

A person's eventful past, an excited old man, a dangerous personal event, adverse bodily tensions, a person in technical service, sudden bodily deficiencies, and surprising disadvantage for a person.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden serious unpleasantness or surprising personal disadvantage.
- psychology: Excitement in an old man is a particular ABC example.
- body/health: Bodily tension or deficiency remains historical source language, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Technical service is named without a specific bond.
- events/manifestations: Dangerous personal events, surprising disadvantage and an eventful past.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's unpleasantness is broad; sickness is not established as the cause of every disturbance.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte gives a concise sudden-adversity statement. ABC adds bodily, service and personal-history examples rather than one uniform illness scenario.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Uranus`, disadvantage has a sudden or unsettling expression. [Witte](#witte-entry) names sudden grave unpleasantness; [ABC](#abc-entry) specifies surprising disadvantage, a dangerous personal event, excitement and bodily tension, alongside technical service and an eventful past. The entries therefore concern abrupt difficulty in several settings, without requiring sickness as its cause. Bodily deficiencies remain attributed source examples rather than a diagnosis.
