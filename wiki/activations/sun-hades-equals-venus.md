## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

Vulgar love. Dissolute in love. Skin diseases.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 160; PDF page 79 (one-based).

Harmonious old people, a person's harmonious past, bodily disadvantages through love, a man's love secret, an art center of the past, personal service in love, personal love sorrow, and renunciation of the man's love.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Love involving disadvantage, sorrow, service or renunciation, with harmony in age or the past also named.
- psychology: Love sorrow and harmonious personal history.
- body/health: Skin diseases and bodily disadvantages remain historical source claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Secret love, service in love and renunciation of a man's love.
- events/manifestations: Harmonious old people and an art center of the past.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's moral judgments remain attributed; ABC's harmony should not be erased by an exclusively unpleasant reading.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte supplies moral and medical judgments; ABC includes love sorrow but also harmony, service and past art. These distinct emphases do not justify treating every relationship as dissolute or unhappy.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Venus`, [ABC](#abc-entry) connects love with sorrow, service, secrecy or renunciation while also preserving harmonious old people and a harmonious past. Its art center of the past adds a separate cultural setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives moral judgments about love and a skin-disease claim, retained as historical source language. The formula therefore holds harmony and difficult love together, without making the medical or moral examples judgments about every relationship.
