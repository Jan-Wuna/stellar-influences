## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

People in deplorable conditions. Grief and difficulties in the public. People in rags. Mob.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 160; PDF page 79 (one-based).

Old people in public, people in public who are in need, disadvantages in public on the day, a man's public past, people in public service, and personal deprivation in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Need, deprivation or difficulty in public life, alongside public service and personal history.
- psychology: Public grief is named without a fuller inner portrait.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is supplied by these entries.
- social/relationship: Old people, people in need and public-service personnel.
- events/manifestations: Public difficulties, a man's public past and personal deprivation in public life.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's class labels remain attributed; the entries do not specify a dirty physical environment.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries concern public hardship. ABC also names old people, public service and a public past; these are distinct from Witte's evaluative crowd labels.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Vernal Point`, need or disadvantage appears in public life. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes public grief and difficult living conditions; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds public deprivation, people in need, old people and public service, with a man's public past as another setting. The shared emphasis is the public presence of hardship and service, while Witte's class-based labels remain historical source language rather than judgments adopted by the page.
