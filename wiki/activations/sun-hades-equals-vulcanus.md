## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

Deformation. Mutilation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 161; PDF page 79 (one-based).

A dangerous energy center, an endangered power center, enormous personal deprivations, a man's lack of strength, need through human violence, personal influence from the past, and hidden forces within a person.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Danger or deprivation involving force and power, alongside hidden or past influence.
- psychology: No specific inner disposition is established by these entries.
- body/health: Lack of strength and Witte's deformation/mutilation claims remain historical attributions.
- social/relationship: Need through human violence and personal influence from the past.
- events/manifestations: A dangerous energy center, an endangered power center and enormous deprivation.
- conflicts/notes: Endangered power is distinct from dangerous power; hidden forces do not establish a recovery claim.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Witte gives two severe bodily examples. ABC broadens the scope to violence, deprivation, threatened power and hidden influence; no particular disease is specified by either entry.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Vulcanus`, [ABC](#abc-entry) places need and deprivation in relation to force: violence can produce need, power can be endangered and strength can be lacking, while past influence and hidden forces remain additional meanings. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies deformation and mutilation as separate historical bodily claims. The formula therefore concerns force, vulnerability and severe deprivation in these entries, without establishing a particular disease or a medical outcome.
