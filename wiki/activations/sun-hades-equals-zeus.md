## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 116; PDF page 112 (one-based).

Fever. Grave injuries of the body through fire or firearms. To be born in poor circumstances. Criminal ancestors. Leader of criminals.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 161; PDF page 79 (one-based).

An old man's efforts, a dangerous leader, human need through fire, adverse personnel management, personal services, personal performance deficiency, a hidden leadership center, and renunciation of physical procreation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Effort, service or leadership in adverse, deprived or dangerous conditions.
- psychology: No specific inner disposition is established by these entries.
- body/health: Fever, fire injuries and renunciation of procreation remain historical source claims.
- social/relationship: Dangerous leadership, adverse staff management and Witte's criminal-ancestry examples.
- events/manifestations: Need through fire, poor birth circumstances, performance deficiency and a hidden leadership center.
- conflicts/notes: Personal service and old-age effort are not equated with criminal leadership; no event is predicted.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries include fire-related difficulty and leadership. ABC also retains personal services and an old man's efforts, which broaden the entry beyond destruction alone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Hades = Zeus`, effort and leadership are described within adverse or deprived conditions. [ABC](#abc-entry) names old-age effort, personal services, performance deficiency and harmful or hidden leadership, with need through fire as one concrete setting; [Witte](#witte-entry) adds poor birth circumstances and criminal leadership or ancestry, alongside fever and fire injuries. The service and effort meanings remain distinct from criminality, and the bodily or destructive examples remain historical attributions rather than forecasts.
