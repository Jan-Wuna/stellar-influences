## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 106 (one-based).

Good fortune through or in rest. Termination of luck.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 149; PDF page 73 (one-based).

A blocked money center, a depressed financial man, restriction of personal rights, success through physical endurance, a thorough legal person, good bodily circulation, restriction of personal success, and blocked personal finances.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Fortune in rest or through endurance, alongside blocked finances or restricted success.
- psychology: Thoroughness and ABC's depressed financial person.
- body/health: Physical endurance and good circulation are historical source claims, not a health assessment.
- social/relationship: Restriction of personal rights and a thorough legal person.
- events/manifestations: Blocked money or finances and Witte's termination of luck.
- conflicts/notes: Witte retains both fortune in rest and termination of luck; property or inheritance is not supplied locally.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

The entries include favorable and restrictive conditions. Rest and endurance are explicit routes to benefit; neither removes ABC's blocked-finance examples or Witte's termination clause.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Jupiter = Admetos`, benefit is described in conditions of rest, endurance or restriction. [Witte](#witte-entry) names fortune through rest but also termination of luck; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds success through physical endurance alongside restricted rights, blocked finances and limited personal success. Its thorough legal person and circulation clause are further particulars. The formula therefore preserves both a benefit within constrained activity and the possibility of curtailed advantage, without implying property or inheritance.
