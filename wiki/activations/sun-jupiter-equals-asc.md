## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Being fortunate with other people. To feel comfortable in the company of other people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success in the human environment, successful day at the place, money day for the partner, money centre at the place, happy person in the environment, personal partner, personal rights at the place, rights centre at the place, and advantages of other persons

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Devoting one's life to good objectives or social aspirations. Shared successes, a meeting or coming together with others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Coming across outwardly as having lots of self-confidence, which may only be a mask. The ability to find a successful way to present oneself to the world. The abil ity to capitalize on one’s outer appearance and demeanor.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `72`

#### Munkasey Entry

Focusing more closely the actions and intentions of others to see how they may contribute or detract from your aims and goals in life; becoming more introspective about your own efforts; concentration on your awareness.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Jupiter = Asc`: charisma, popularity, and opportunities that come through personal manner, visibility, and self-belief. Main caution: overstatement: vanity, excess, or taking up too much space can turn confidence into inflation.
