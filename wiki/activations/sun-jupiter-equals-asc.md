## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 106 (one-based).

Being fortunate with other people. To feel comfortable in the company of other people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 148; PDF page 73 (one-based).

Success in the personal environment, a successful day at the place, a money day for the partner, a local money center, happy people in the surroundings, personal happiness with a partner, personal rights at the place, a local legal center, and advantage for other people.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 91, coded entry 0240.

Devoting one's life to good objectives or social aspirations. Shared successes, a meeting or coming together with others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 14 (one-based), Sun/Jupiter Ascendant entry.

Coming across outwardly as having lots of self-confidence, which may only be a mask. The ability to find a successful way to present oneself to the world. The abil ity to capitalize on one’s outer appearance and demeanor.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `72`

#### Munkasey Entry

Focusing more closely the actions and intentions of others to see how they may contribute or detract from your aims and goals in life; becoming more introspective about your own efforts; concentration on your awareness.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Success, comfort and shared aims through contact with other people and effective self-presentation.
- psychology: Outward confidence may be a mask; introspection and attention to others' intentions are explicit.
- body/health: Appearance and demeanor are presentation themes, not a specific health claim.
- social/relationship: Shared success, partner happiness, social aspirations and comfort in company.
- events/manifestations: Meetings, successful presentation and local financial or legal settings.
- conflicts/notes: Apparent confidence need not reflect inner certainty; others may support or detract from one's aims.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in successful contact but distinguish social ease, outward presentation and reflective assessment of others. Sandbach's mask qualification and Munkasey's support-or-detraction distinction limit a uniformly confident reading.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Jupiter = Asc`, success is expressed through how one meets others and presents oneself. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) emphasize comfort in company, shared success and social aims; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives partner happiness and favorable local settings. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds successful self-presentation while warning that outward confidence may be a mask. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) supplies the reflective side: considering how others' actions support or detract from one's goals and examining one's own efforts. Social ease and effective presentation therefore coexist with a need to distinguish appearance from inner certainty and helpful contact from interference.
