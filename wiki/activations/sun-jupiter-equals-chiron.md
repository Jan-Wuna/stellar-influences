## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 13 (one-based), Sun/Jupiter Chiron entry.

Through exposure to your energy people automatically feel more selfconfi dence. You also have the effect of spiritualizing their ego, to the extent that they are ready for this. You can emanate a sense of life being meaningful and success being evitable, which can have a healing effect on others. The ability to heal those people who project egotism that is actually a cover-up for inner lack of self-confidence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: An influence that supports others' confidence and sense of meaning.
- psychology: Projected egotism may cover a lack of self-confidence.
- body/health: Sandbach's healing language is spiritual doctrine; no bodily condition or treatment is specified.
- social/relationship: Supporting others' confidence and spiritualizing ego to the extent they are ready.
- events/manifestations: Interpersonal influence is described; no particular external event is supplied.
- conflicts/notes: The PDF prints “success being evitable”; its intended sense is unclear and no inevitability of success is inferred.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Only one entry is present. Its readiness qualification limits the ego-spiritualization claim; the printed “evitable” wording is preserved without silent correction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Jupiter = Chiron`, [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes an interpersonal influence that strengthens confidence and conveys a sense that life is meaningful. Its specific focus is people whose projected egotism conceals insecurity, with spiritualization of ego qualified by their readiness. The entry calls this healing in its own spiritual framework; it provides no bodily treatment claim, and the ambiguous printed success clause does not establish that success is inevitable.
