## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 106 (one-based).

A happy husband. Wealth through marriage. Success through cooperation with others, sociability or business partners. Good fortune in art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 149; PDF page 73 (one-based).

successful community day, successful person in the community, shared legal center, happy human community, persons of a financial community, personal advantages in the community, and legal person for a community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Benefit, happiness or success through marriage, cooperation and community participation.
- psychology: Happiness is explicit; no fuller character portrait is supplied.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: A happy husband, business partners, sociability and communal advantage.
- events/manifestations: Wealth through marriage, artistic fortune and shared legal or financial settings.
- conflicts/notes: Favorable communal meanings do not establish a festive event or guarantee marital wealth.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries emphasize benefit within shared life. Witte adds cooperation and art; ABC specifies legal and financial communities and a person's advantage within them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Jupiter = Cupido`, benefit is connected with participation in shared life. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes happiness or wealth through marriage, success through cooperation and fortune in art; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds personal advantage within a happy or successful community and its legal or financial arrangements. The common focus is advantage gained or experienced together, with marriage, business cooperation and art retained as distinct settings rather than a single festive event.
