## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Facing obstacles while striving for success. One who becomes rich through theft. Handling obscure or sinister matters. Well-being despite bad surroundings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful past of a person, secret of a happy person, dangerous money center, money deficiency of a man, happiness through personal deprivations, criminal financial man, legal disadvantages of a man, and renunciation of personal joy

## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Jupiter = Hades`: good fortune despite adversity and success in difficult conditions.
- Practical expression: successful past of a person, secret of a happy person, dangerous money center, money deficiency of a man, and happiness through personal deprivations.
