## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 106 (one-based).

Facing obstacles while striving for success. One who becomes rich through theft. Handling obscure or sinister matters. Well-being despite bad surroundings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 149; PDF page 73 (one-based).

Successful past of a person, secret of a happy person, endangered money center, money deficiency of a man, happiness through personal deprivations, criminal financial man, legal disadvantages of a man, and renunciation of personal joy

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Benefit or well-being amid obstacles, deprivation or adverse surroundings.
- psychology: Happiness through deprivation and renunciation of personal joy are separate ABC expressions.
- body/health: General well-being is named without a specific health assessment.
- social/relationship: Legal disadvantage and obscure or criminal dealings are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: An endangered money center, lack of money, wealth through theft and a successful past.
- conflicts/notes: Success amid adversity does not mean adversity causes all success; criminal examples remain attributed particulars.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries retain advantage alongside difficulty. ABC's renounced joy and shortage of money prevent a uniformly prosperous reading, while Witte explicitly allows well-being despite bad surroundings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Jupiter = Hades`, advantage and adverse conditions coexist. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes striving against obstacles and well-being despite bad surroundings, alongside wealth through theft or obscure dealings; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds happiness through deprivation, a successful past, money shortage and legal disadvantage. These passages retain benefit without removing its difficult context. The criminal examples are particular source meanings, and the endangered money center or renounced joy should not be flattened into assured prosperity.
