## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 106 (one-based).

A good physician. Good news. Thoughts of success. Persevering speaker. Success through speaking, travel, writing commerce or trade.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 148; PDF page 73 (one-based).

A successful discussion on the day, a successful media person, financial discussions on the day, joy of young people, a successful young man, personal legal conversations, considering personal advantages, and a center for youth law.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 90, coded entry 0232.

Thought processes of a healthy person, successful thinking, good mode of expression, a mobile and healthy body, meditating or reflecting on health (medical doctor), healthy outlook on life. Success through thinking, speaking and acting. Good news, conclusion of a contract or of an agreement, recognition.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 13 (one-based), Sun/Jupiter Mercury entry.

Communicating in an excessively optimistic and confident way which may turn other people off. Success in communicating, and possible notoriety for it. A gen erous and magnanimous way of thinking. The ability to meaningfully synthesize and organize ideas.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `72`

#### Munkasey Entry

Cleverness, wit, and humor enhance your abilities; a fast mind able to react quickly to rapidly changing situations; getting ideas across to others in discussions or by their publication; thinking in religious or philosophical ways.

### Aren Ober - Midpoint Interpretation Simplified

- Source notation: `Mercury = Sun/Jupiter` / `Me-So/Ju`
- PDF page(s): `130`

#### Ober Entry

Ober gives mental success, ranging from a school diploma to a major business contract.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful thinking, expression and organization of ideas, with practical recognition or agreement.
- psychology: Wit, humor, quick responses, generosity and philosophical thought.
- body/health: Physician and healthy-body examples remain historical attributions, not a health assessment.
- social/relationship: Effective discussion and publication; excessive optimism or confidence may alienate others.
- events/manifestations: Good news, contracts, a diploma, media success and financial/legal discussions.
- conflicts/notes: Success and possible notoriety coexist; careless facts are not explicitly asserted by these entries.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in effective thought and expression. Sandbach also allows excessive confidence that puts people off; Munkasey's quick wit and idea transmission are capacities, not proof that every statement is sound.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Jupiter = Mercury`, benefit is expressed through thought, speech and the exchange of ideas. [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describe good news, effective discussion and practical success; [Ober](#ober-entry) gives a diploma or business contract as concrete examples. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds wit, quick responses and publication, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) emphasizes generous thought and meaningful organization of ideas. Sandbach also retains excessive optimism or confidence that alienates others, so communicative ability and its reception should remain distinct. Medical examples are source attributions rather than an assessment of health.
