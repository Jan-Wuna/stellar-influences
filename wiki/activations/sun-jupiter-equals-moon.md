## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 106 (one-based).

The bridegroom. A fortunate and happy husband. Lucky day for women or the people. The contented population. Fortunes through females or the public. At the right moment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 148; PDF page 73 (one-based).

A successful day for a people, a woman as a legal person, a woman in the money center, happiness between woman and man, a man's feeling of happiness, a man content with the woman, a woman content with the man, and the woman's personal happiness.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 90, coded entry 0231.

A healthy soul in a healthy body, harmonious expression of feeling. A happy hour, happy or wealthy person. Happy experiences in married life.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 13 (one-based), Sun/Jupiter Moon entry.

The emotional need to have big goals, and/or to be an important person. The emotional need for a meaningful and uplifting philosophy of life. To feel secure when one has many and/or large goals. To be very emotionally affected by one’s success or lack of it. If one is overly egocentric it can have a deleterious effect on one’s emotional connection to others. The ability to nurture and support the self-confidence and self- worth of others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `72`

#### Munkasey Entry

An interest in people; heightens needs to address the motives of others; added needs for approval from women or mother figures; trying different approaches rather than relying on one proven method to find well being.

### Aren Ober - Midpoint Interpretation Simplified

- Source notation: `Moon equals Sun/Jupiter` / `Mo = So/Ju`
- PDF page(s): `130`

#### Ober Entry

Ober gives a successful, happy feeling; success through a woman; habits bringing success; and success through the public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Felt happiness and shared success, with emotional investment in goals, meaning or approval.
- psychology: Security through meaningful aims, sensitivity to success and the capacity to nurture others' confidence.
- body/health: Ebertin's healthy-body/soul statement is historical attribution, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Marital contentment, public success and a need for approval from women or mother figures.
- events/manifestations: Happy experiences, successful habits and legal or financial roles in the gendered source examples.
- conflicts/notes: Egocentricity can harm emotional connection; complacency, indulgence or weakened discipline is not explicitly established here.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in happiness and successful shared life. Sandbach adds emotional dependence on meaningful goals or success and the effect of egocentricity; Munkasey adds approval needs and trying different approaches.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Jupiter = Moon`, success is felt emotionally and experienced with other people. [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) and [Ober](#ober-entry) give happiness, marital contentment or success through women and the public. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds the need for meaningful goals, strong emotional responses to success and the ability to support others' confidence; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds interest in people, approval needs and varied approaches to well-being. The entries therefore connect shared happiness with emotional investment in aims and relationships. Sandbach's warning that egocentricity can damage connection remains the specific limit, while gendered and health examples stay attributed.
