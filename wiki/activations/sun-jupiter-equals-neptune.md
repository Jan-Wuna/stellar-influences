## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 106 (one-based).

Passing happiness. Loss of money. Become poor. Unclear conditions. Uncertainty which limit the success. A successful swindler.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 149; PDF page 73 (one-based).

Success through personal sensitivity, joy through personal intuition, a man's expectations of happiness, personal joy in the air, personal legal uncertainty, the human right to water, and uncertainty in the money center.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 90, coded entry 0237.

A dissonant mood, feebleness, high sensitivity. A successful medium, the crook. Undermining of health, a material loss.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 13 (one-based), Sun/Jupiter Neptune entry.

Success in applying one’s intuition to the reaching of goals. The ability to intuitively sense how all of life is harmoniously linked together. The imagination has a grandiose and dramatic quality.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `72`

#### Munkasey Entry

A sense of the unreal; purposeful deceit about the nature of another’s intentions; grandiose plans for projects thought by others to be impractical; elements of doubt cloud your enthusiasm for life; an interest in illusions.

### Aren Ober - Midpoint Interpretation Simplified

- Source notation: `Neptune = Sun/Jupiter` / `Ne-So/Ju`
- PDF page(s): `131`

#### Ober Entry

Ober gives nebulous success or success without limits.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intuitive or nebulous success alongside uncertainty, passing happiness and possible material loss.
- psychology: Sensitivity, dramatic imagination, grandiose plans, doubt and interest in illusions.
- body/health: Ebertin's feebleness and undermined-health claims remain historical attributions.
- social/relationship: Purposeful deceit about intentions, legal uncertainty and the right to water.
- events/manifestations: Money loss, intuitive goal attainment and Ober's success without limits.
- conflicts/notes: Intuitive success does not erase uncertainty or deceit; charity and speculation are not explicitly supplied here.

## Contradictions

Sandbach and ABC include success through intuition or sensitivity, while Witte and Ebertin retain uncertainty and loss. Ober's “without limits” is one alternative; it should not override the adverse possibilities. Munkasey explicitly includes deceit and impractical plans.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Jupiter = Neptune`, success is linked with intuition and imagination but may be uncertain in its basis or result. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) and [ABC](#abc-entry) describe intuitive or sensitive approaches to goals, while [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) retain passing happiness, money loss and deceitful success. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds doubt, deliberate deception and plans others find impractical; [Ober](#ober-entry) preserves nebulous or unlimited success as alternatives. The formula therefore holds intuitive possibility beside uncertainty and loss, without treating imagination as proof of accomplishment. Health and mediumistic claims remain source attributions.
