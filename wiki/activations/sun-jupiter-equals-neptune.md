## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Passing happiness. Loss of money. Become poor. Unclear conditions. Uncertainty which limit the success. A successful swindler.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Success through personal finesse, personal joy in the air, and uncertainty in the Gcld center

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A dissonant mood, feebleness, high sensitivity. A successful medium, the crook. Undermining of health, a material loss.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Success in applying one’s intuition to the reaching of goals. The ability to intuitively sense how all of life is harmoniously linked together. The imagination has a grandiose and dramatic quality.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

A sense of the unreal; purposeful deceit about the nature of another’s intentions; grandiose plans for projects thought by others to be impractical; elements of doubt cloud your enthusiasm for life; an interest in illusions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Jupiter = Neptune`: inspired purpose, charitable feeling, and success through intuition, artistry, or devotion to something larger than the ego. Main caution: illusion: wishful expansion can become gullibility, speculation, or ungrounded confidence.
