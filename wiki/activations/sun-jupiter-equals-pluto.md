### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sun + Jupiter`

Good fortune and opportunity develops during a lifetime. The function of the organs is in good condition. Increase in health or good fortune.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Sun/Jupiter`
- Source page: `90`
- Entry: `0238`

#### Ebertin Entry

Pursuit of happiness and fortune. Expectation of good luck, acquiring of wealth, state of happiness.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Sun/Jupiter midpoint`
- Source page: `12`
- Activator: `Pluto`

#### Sandbach Entry

Attaining dramatic or extreme success. Obsessively thinking too big and/ or going too far. A relentless drive for recognition. Excesses eventually drain one’s vitality which then helps one to make needed changes and to put more energy into promoting one’s health on all levels.
