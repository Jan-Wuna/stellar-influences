## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Good fortune and opportunity develops during a lifetime. The function of the organs is in good condition. Increase in health or good fortune.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

development of personal advantages, successful personal development, financial change of a man, joy over personal transformation, luck through personal change, growing development of the finances, and growing human joy

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Pursuit of happiness and fortune. Expectation of good luck, acquiring of wealth, state of happiness.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Attaining dramatic or extreme success. Obsessively thinking too big and/ or going too far. A relentless drive for recognition. Excesses eventually drain one’s vitality which then helps one to make needed changes and to put more energy into promoting one’s health on all levels.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `72`

#### Munkasey Entry

Achieving the greatest of success with your efforts, or total and complete failure and despair; ideas that you can proceed in life despite the legality of your efforts; compulsive elements of destruction in your basic thinking.

### Aren Ober - Midpoint Interpretation Simplified

- Source notation: `Pluto = Sun/Jupiter` / `Pl-So/Ju`
- PDF page(s): `131`

#### Ober Entry

Ober gives fanatic success.
