## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 106 (one-based).

Good fortune and opportunity develops during a lifetime. The function of the organs is in good condition. Increase in health or good fortune.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 149; PDF page 73 (one-based).

development of personal advantages, successful personal development, financial change of a man, joy over personal transformation, luck through personal change, growing development of the finances, and growing human joy

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 90, coded entry 0238.

Pursuit of happiness and fortune. Expectation of good luck, acquiring of wealth, state of happiness.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 13 (one-based), Sun/Jupiter Pluto entry.

Attaining dramatic or extreme success. Obsessively thinking too big and/ or going too far. A relentless drive for recognition. Excesses eventually drain one’s vitality which then helps one to make needed changes and to put more energy into promoting one’s health on all levels.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `72`

#### Munkasey Entry

Achieving the greatest of success with your efforts, or total and complete failure and despair; ideas that you can proceed in life despite the legality of your efforts; compulsive elements of destruction in your basic thinking.

### Aren Ober - Midpoint Interpretation Simplified

- Source notation: `Pluto = Sun/Jupiter` / `Pl-So/Ju`
- PDF page(s): `131`

#### Ober Entry

Ober gives fanatic success.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Developing fortune or advantage, with intense pursuit and extremes of success or failure.
- psychology: Relentless recognition-seeking, oversized aims and compulsive destructive thinking are explicit alternatives.
- body/health: Organ-function, health-increase and depleted-vitality claims remain historical source attributions.
- social/relationship: Munkasey includes disregard for legality; no specific relationship arrangement is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Financial change, personal transformation, wealth acquisition and dramatic success or complete failure.
- conflicts/notes: The favorable development entries coexist with excess and failure; coercive leadership is not explicitly supplied.

## Contradictions

Witte, ABC and Ebertin emphasize developing advantage or fortune. Sandbach and Ober add extreme or fanatical pursuit, while Munkasey explicitly retains complete failure and despair as an alternative to greatest success. Those differences should not be flattened into assured growth.

## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Jupiter = Pluto`, the entries connect developing advantage with an intense pursuit of fortune or recognition. [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describe growing opportunity, personal or financial change and the pursuit of happiness; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) and [Ober](#ober-entry) add extreme or fanatical success. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) retains complete failure and despair, disregard for legality and destructive compulsion as difficult alternatives. The formula therefore concerns the development and intensity of the pursuit, without guaranteeing its outcome. Health and vitality statements remain attributed source claims.
