## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 106 (one-based).

Fortunate mental qualities. Great reason, good comprehension. Wisdom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 149; PDF page 73 (one-based).

A man's idea of success, insights of a financial center, joy through personal insight, joy in human culture, a happy spirit in the body, personal advantage through clarity, and truth about a legal center.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Benefit or joy through reason, comprehension, insight and clarity.
- psychology: Wisdom, good understanding and joy in culture.
- body/health: ABC's happy spirit in the body is doctrinal language, not a medical assessment.
- social/relationship: Truth about a legal center and insights of a financial center.
- events/manifestations: A success idea and advantage through clarity; no particular external event is established.
- conflicts/notes: ABC says happy spirit, not the prior ethnic mistranslation; inspiration alone does not capture the explicit reason/comprehension theme.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries emphasize favorable understanding. ABC adds joy, culture and financial/legal settings, extending Witte's concise mental-quality account without contradicting it.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Jupiter = Poseidon`, benefit is connected with understanding and clarity. [Witte](#witte-entry) names reason, comprehension and wisdom; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds joy through insight, advantage through clarity and appreciation of human culture. Its financial-center insights, truth about a legal center and idea of success give that understanding concrete settings, while the happy spirit in the body remains a separate source expression. The entry's emphasis is favorable insight and comprehension rather than inspiration alone.
