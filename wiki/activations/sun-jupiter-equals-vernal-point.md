## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 106 (one-based).

Fortunate day on earth. Success in general, in daily business and efforts. Payment day in the general public. Well-to-do people. One who is a friend of nature, who feels physically comfortable when surrounded by nature.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 148; PDF page 73 (one-based).

A successful person in public, a money center in public, human joy in public, a public financial man, a public legal center, personal joy in public life, and a legal person in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public success, joy and favorable daily activity.
- psychology: Joy and an affinity for nature are explicit source themes.
- body/health: Witte names bodily comfort in nature without a specific medical claim.
- social/relationship: Well-to-do people and public financial or legal roles.
- events/manifestations: Daily business success, public payment and money or legal centers.
- conflicts/notes: Fortunate-day language remains a source example, not a timing rule; comfort in nature is a distinct explicit meaning.

## Contradictions And Comparison Notes

Both entries include public benefit and financial settings. Witte also retains comfort in nature, which should not be lost in a purely institutional reading.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

For `Sun/Jupiter = Vernal Point`, success and joy take a public or everyday form. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes favorable business and effort, payment and well-to-do people; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful people, money and legal centers, and personal joy in public life. Witte also explicitly names bodily comfort when surrounded by nature. These settings retain both public benefit and natural comfort without turning the fortunate-day example into a timing rule.
