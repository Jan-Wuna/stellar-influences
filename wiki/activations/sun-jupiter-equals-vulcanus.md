## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Great health and virility of the body.

Source locator: PDF p. 106, Sun/Jupiter, Vulcanus entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influential legal center, financial power of a man, personal financial influence, a man's success in power, personal joy in power, personal advantages through strength, legal influence for a person, and the center of a financial power.

Source locator: printed p. 149, PDF p. 73, Sun/Jupiter, Vulcanus entry. Translation checked against the German row.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Benefit expressed through bodily strength or effective financial and legal power.
- psychology: ABC includes personal enjoyment of power and advantage through strength.
- body/health: Witte's health and virility statement is historical source language, not an assessment of an individual.
- social/relationship: ABC places influence in legal and financial relationships.
- events/manifestations: Financial influence, a center of financial power, and advantages gained through strength.
- conflicts/notes: Witte describes bodily vigor; ABC emphasizes institutional and personal power. These are distinct scopes, not independent confirmation of the same outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Jupiter = Vulcanus` brings benefit into the field of strength and effective influence. [Witte](#witte-entry) expresses this through a historical claim about bodily health and virility, while [ABC](#abc-entry) gives financial and legal forms: personal influence, advantages through strength, enjoyment of power, and a center of financial power. The common emphasis is potency supporting advantage; the bodily claim should remain separate from the financial and legal examples rather than becoming a promise of health or success.
