## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The fortune lies in production. Good inherited talents. The ability to procreate.

Source locator: PDF p. 106, Sun/Jupiter, Zeus entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful efforts of a man, successful personnel leadership, a person's financial achievements, shaping the law of persons, founding a legal center, personal success through setting goals, and personal success in procreation.

Source locator: printed p. 149, PDF p. 73, Sun/Jupiter, Zeus entry. Translation checked against the German row; Personen-Recht is retained in its legal sense as the law of persons.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Benefit through productive effort, talent, leadership and deliberate goals.
- psychology: ABC emphasizes purposeful effort and setting goals; Witte adds inherited talents.
- body/health: Procreation is an explicit historical source motif, not a fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: Personnel leadership and the organization of legal rights are ABC's social forms.
- events/manifestations: Production, financial achievements, founding a legal center and procreation.
- conflicts/notes: Both entries connect benefit with productive capacity; ABC's institutional examples extend beyond Witte's short talent and procreation statement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Jupiter = Zeus` locates benefit in productive capacity put to purposeful use. [Witte](#witte-entry) links production with inherited talents, while [ABC](#abc-entry) makes effort and goal setting concrete through personnel leadership, financial achievements and legal organization. These passages support a theme of creating results through directed ability. Their shared procreation motif remains a historical source claim, not a prediction about an individual's fertility.
