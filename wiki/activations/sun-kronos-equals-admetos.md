## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

End of a government or independence. Abdication of a ruler.

Source locator: printed p. 118, PDF p. 114, Sun/Kronos, Admetos entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special perseverance of a human being, blockade of a state center, depression of leading people, restriction of personal authority, end of male dominance, and important primal material in the body.

Source locator: printed p. 165, PDF p. 81, Sun/Kronos, Admetos entry. Translation checked against the German row; Urstoff is rendered broadly as primal material.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Authority or independence restricted, blocked or brought to an end.
- psychology: ABC includes both special perseverance and depression among leaders.
- body/health: ABC's primal-material clause names no substance or bodily condition; its depression wording is not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Limits on personal authority and the end of male dominance are distinct source examples.
- events/manifestations: A blocked state center, an ended government or independence, and a ruler's abdication.
- conflicts/notes: Perseverance remains present alongside termination; property ownership and land are not supplied by these entries.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Kronos = Admetos` concerns authority meeting a limit or an ending. [Witte](#witte-entry) makes this explicit through the end of government or independence and a ruler's abdication; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a blocked state center, restricted personal authority and ended male dominance. ABC also retains special perseverance and depressed leaders, so endurance and difficulty remain distinct possibilities within that restricted field. Its undefined bodily-material clause supports no specific health conclusion.
