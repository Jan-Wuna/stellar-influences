## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Scientific abilities. Authority for many. Success of officials.

Source locator: printed p. 118, PDF p. 114, Sun/Kronos, Apollon entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A particularly successful person, a successful statesman, a man's great success, personal prestige through knowledge, an independent scientific center, an important experience during the day, and a scientific authority figure.

Source locator: printed p. 165, PDF p. 81, Sun/Kronos, Apollon entry. Translation checked against the German row.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Knowledge or scientific ability expressed as prestige, authority and success.
- psychology: The entries name knowledge and experience without a fuller character portrait.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied by these entries.
- social/relationship: Authority for many people, scientific authority and successful officials or statesmen.
- events/manifestations: An independent scientific center, personal recognition through knowledge and important experience.
- conflicts/notes: The entries associate authority with knowledge and accomplishment; they do not state that deference to authority itself causes success.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Kronos = Apollon` joins authority with knowledge and recognized accomplishment. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes scientific ability, authority for many and successful officials; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds prestige through knowledge, a scientific authority figure and an independent scientific center. Its successful person or statesman and important experience extend that picture into personal and public recognition. The emphasis is authority expressed through ability and achievement, without assuming that authority alone guarantees success.
