## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To act independently. Power executed by authority. Police.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dominance of the male will, great personal work, acts of authority figures, a person acting self-confidently, a man's independent actions, superior human energy for action, and a statesman's activity.

Source locator: printed p. 164, PDF p. 81, Sun/Kronos, Mars entry. Translation checked against the German row.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Independent action and the practical exercise of authority.
- psychology: Self-confident action and dominance of will are explicit source themes.
- body/health: Energy for action is named without a specific health condition.
- social/relationship: Acts of authority figures, police and a statesman's activity.
- events/manifestations: Great personal work and independent actions are ABC's concrete forms.
- conflicts/notes: Confidence and independence are distinct from self-promotion; male-will and superiority wording remains attributed to the source.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Kronos = Mars` concerns authority put into action and the capacity to act independently. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes power exercised by authority and gives police as an example; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds acts of authority figures, substantial personal work and a statesman's activity. Its self-confident actor, independent actions and energy for action give a personal form to that practical authority. The shared emphasis is practical agency; the gendered dominance language remains a source particular.
