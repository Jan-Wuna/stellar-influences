## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Independence. Grandeur in one’s demeanor. High birth. Of royal birth.

Source locator: printed p. 118, PDF p. 114, Sun/Kronos, MC entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Dominance of the human soul, I am a self-confident person, my personal standing, my independent man, my physical superiority, my personal authority, and a person's inner greatness.

Source locator: printed p. 164, PDF p. 81, Sun/Kronos, MC entry. Translation checked against the German row.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal independence, authority and a sense or display of stature.
- psychology: Self-confidence, dominance of the soul and inner greatness are ABC's subjective forms.
- body/health: ABC names physical superiority, not moral superiority or a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Personal standing, an independent man and Witte's high or royal birth examples.
- events/manifestations: Grandeur of demeanor and a position of personal authority; no particular external event is supplied.
- conflicts/notes: Rank, birth and superiority are historical source categories, not evidence of inherent worth or actual lineage.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Kronos = MC` gives authority a personal and inwardly felt form. [Witte](#witte-entry) names independence and grandeur in demeanor; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds self-confidence, personal standing, personal authority and inner greatness. Its independent-man and bodily-superiority clauses are further source examples, while Witte's high or royal birth describes a historical rank motif. The emphasis is one's own stature or independence, without treating that symbolism as proof of lineage, moral superiority or an actual right to rule.
