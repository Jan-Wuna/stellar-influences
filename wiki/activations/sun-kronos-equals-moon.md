## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Noble descent. High birth. Wife of a prince. Leader of federations. Noble persons.

Source locator: printed p. 118, PDF p. 114, Sun/Kronos, Moon entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Authority figures for the people, a man's emotional dominance, a woman's bodily dominance, people of a great nation, personal authority as a woman, a man's independent woman, a woman's independent man, and an important day for the woman.

Source locator: printed p. 164, PDF p. 81, Sun/Kronos, Moon entry. Translation checked against the German row; the gendered relational wording is retained.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Authority or elevated standing in collective and gendered relational settings.
- psychology: ABC names emotional dominance without a broader personality claim.
- body/health: Bodily dominance is a source expression, not a health assessment.
- social/relationship: Authority as a woman, independent women and men, a prince's wife and leaders of federations.
- events/manifestations: Public authority, noble descent and an important day for a woman are source examples.
- conflicts/notes: Gender and class categories remain historically attributed; motherhood is not explicitly supplied by these entries.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Kronos = Moon` places authority within collective life and the sources' gendered relationships. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes elevated birth, a prince's wife and leadership of federations; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds authority figures for the people, authority as a woman and independent women and men in relation to one another. Its emotional and bodily dominance clauses are additional forms of precedence. These are historical descriptions of status and relationship, without establishing actual ancestry, motherhood or a person's entitlement to dominate others.
