## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Development of physical abilities, of one’s own authority. To become a leader. Changes or transformations in the leadership.

Source locator: printed p. 118, PDF p. 114, Sun/Kronos, Pluto entry. The preceding P is a Pluto display marker, not prose.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A man's upward development, special personal development, personal unfolding of self-confidence, developing personal standing, independent personal development, and changes in a person's standing.

Source locator: printed p. 165, PDF p. 81, Sun/Kronos, Pluto entry. Translation checked from the separate Pluto glyph through the final standing clause.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development or transformation of personal authority, ability and standing.
- psychology: Unfolding self-confidence and independent personal development.
- body/health: Witte names developing physical abilities without a specific health condition.
- social/relationship: Becoming a leader and changes in leadership or personal standing.
- events/manifestations: Upward development and shifts in leadership are source examples.
- conflicts/notes: Development is explicit; the entries do not state that coercion or power itself causes transformation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Kronos = Pluto` concerns authority and standing as they develop or change. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes developing abilities and one's own authority, becoming a leader and transformations in leadership; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds unfolding self-confidence, independent personal development and changing personal standing. The shared movement is from existing capacities or position toward a different expression of them. Upward development is one explicit form, while changes in leadership remain broader than a guarantee of promotion.
