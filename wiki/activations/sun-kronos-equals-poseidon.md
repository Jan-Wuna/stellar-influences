## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Capabilities in the mental plane. To be a spiritual leader. Nobility of the soul. Mental culture. Outstanding personality with high intellectual skills and insights.

Source locator: printed p. 118, PDF p. 114, Sun/Kronos, Poseidon entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A man's special insight, mind dominates the body, a great spiritual personality, a great cultural center, human superiority through truth, a self-confident cultured person, a rational authority figure, and a center of a spiritual hierarchy.

Source locator: printed p. 165, PDF p. 81, Sun/Kronos, Poseidon entry. Translation checked against the German row; geistig spans the mental and spiritual language retained here.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Intellectual or spiritual capacity expressed as leadership, culture and insight.
- psychology: Reason, self-confidence and nobility of soul are source themes.
- body/health: Mind dominating the body is attributed doctrine, not a bodily or therapeutic claim.
- social/relationship: Spiritual leadership, a rational authority figure and cultural or spiritual institutions.
- events/manifestations: A cultural center or center of a spiritual hierarchy; no particular event is specified.
- conflicts/notes: Truth and insight qualify authority in the entries; status itself does not establish truth or inherent personal superiority.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Kronos = Poseidon` gives leadership a mental, cultural or spiritual basis. [Witte](#witte-entry) joins spiritual leadership with intellectual skill, insight and mental culture; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds special insight, a rational authority figure and cultural or spiritual centers. The common emphasis is authority associated with understanding, rather than truth conferred by rank. Nobility, superiority and mind-over-body language remain attributed doctrine, without establishing inherent worth or a power to control health.
