## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be hindered in one’s abilities, or to be able to show them in later years, or not at all. Loss of independence. Separation through the power of the state. Difficulties of the government. Death of a superior.

Source locator: printed p. 118, PDF p. 114, Sun/Kronos, Saturn entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A respected person in old age, particular bodily problems, a disturbed authority figure, great bodily pain, concentration on special people, superiority of a concentrated person, and important tasks during the day.

Source locator: printed p. 165, PDF p. 81, Sun/Kronos, Saturn entry. Translation checked against the German row.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Authority, independence or ability hindered, delayed or lost, alongside concentrated effort.
- psychology: Concentration is explicit; ABC's disturbed-authority wording is not a diagnosis.
- body/health: Bodily problems and pain are historical source claims, not individual assessments.
- social/relationship: Respect in old age, lost independence and separation through state power.
- events/manifestations: Government difficulties, important tasks and Witte's death-of-a-superior example.
- conflicts/notes: Witte allows abilities to appear late or never; ABC retains respect and concentrated capacity, so burden alone is incomplete.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Kronos = Saturn` places authority and independence under restriction. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes abilities hindered or expressed only late, loss of independence and difficulties involving government; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a troubled authority figure and important tasks. ABC also preserves respect in old age and capacity associated with concentration, so limitation does not erase every competent or respected expression. Bodily pain and the death of a superior remain particular historical source claims, without providing a diagnosis, forecast or timing rule.
