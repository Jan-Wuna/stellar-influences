## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Excitement through independent persons. Sudden interference of the power of the state.

Source locator: printed p. 118, PDF p. 114, Sun/Kronos, Uranus entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Authority of a technical center, a special man of technology, great personal restlessness, an authority figure who provides impulses, directing bodily rhythm, and an independent technical center.

Source locator: printed p. 165, PDF p. 81, Sun/Kronos, Uranus entry. Translation checked against the German row.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Authority or independence expressed through sudden intervention, stimulation or technical direction.
- psychology: Personal restlessness and giving impulses are ABC themes, not a general impulsivity judgment.
- body/health: Directing bodily rhythm is a source expression without a diagnosis or treatment instruction.
- social/relationship: Excitement through independent people and an authority figure who supplies impulses.
- events/manifestations: Sudden state interference and authority or independence in a technical center.
- conflicts/notes: The entries do not supply unexpected promotion; technical direction and state intervention are distinct settings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Kronos = Uranus` connects authority with stimulation, unrest or sudden intervention. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives excitement through independent people and sudden interference by state power; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a technical center, an authority figure who provides impulses and personal restlessness. Directing bodily rhythm and an independent technical center are further specific forms. The formula therefore retains both outside intervention and independent technical direction, without turning suddenness into a promise of elevation or promotion.
