## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Leading or ruling personages.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 164, supplied PDF p. 81, Sonne + Kronos, Vernal Point row (six clauses). Translation from the German scan:

An authority figure in public life; a special man in public; a person's public reputation; a self-confident person in public; a statesman in public; an important day in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public prominence of a leading person.
- psychology: ABC names a self-confident person in public; no broader inner profile is specified.
- body/health: No body or health claim is stated.
- social/relationship: Authority appears in public life.
- events/manifestations: A ruler, public figure, or important public occasion.
- conflicts/notes: Public standing does not establish local circumstances.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction appears between Witte's leading figures and ABC's public-life examples. ABC also names public reputation and self-confident public presentation; neither entry establishes a particular office.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Kronos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Sun/Kronos theme of leadership through public visibility. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) identifies leading or ruling figures, and the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) places the person or statesman in public life, including an important public day. The supported throughline is authority seen in a public setting; the entries do not narrow it to a neighborhood or prove a particular office.
