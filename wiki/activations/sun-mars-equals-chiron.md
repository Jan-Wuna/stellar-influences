## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

You intuitively communicate to people the meaning and purpose inherent in the conflicts they are experiencing. Your energy encourages others to take steps toward their goals.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Helping others find purpose in conflict.
- psychology: Intuitive recognition and communication of meaning.
- body/health: No body or health claim is stated.
- social/relationship: Encouragement is directed toward other people.
- events/manifestations: Helping others take steps toward goals.
- conflicts/notes: The sole entry does not mention anger-clearing or spiritual assertion.

## Contradictions

Only Sandbach’s entry is present, so a cross-source contradiction cannot be assessed. It supports encouragement through understanding conflict, not the previous claims about clearing anger.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Mars = Chiron`, Chiron specifies the Sun/Mars theme of effort and conflict through helping another person recognize what their struggle means. The [Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry) connects intuitive communication of purpose with encouragement to take practical steps toward a goal. The supported emphasis is giving direction and impetus to others; this short entry does not establish a healing technique, an anger process, or a particular spiritual practice.
