## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A man who wants to marry. The marriage partner (husband). A man active in the arts or community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 147 / PDF page 72, Cupido glyph under Sonne + Mars. English translation checked against the complete seven-clause row.

Persons creating together, physical community work, male working community, people of a community for action, personal work for the community, daily work of a community, and center of a working community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal activity directed into a shared undertaking.
- psychology: Witte names a wish to marry.
- body/health: No health claim is stated.
- social/relationship: Marriage, community, and artistic participation in Witte.
- events/manifestations: Shared work or activity in a community.
- conflicts/notes: ABC describes shared work; Witte separately adds marriage, the husband and artistic participation.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. ABC's complete row develops collective work and a working center; Witte's marriage and artistic examples remain distinct additions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Mars = Cupido`, Cupido specifies Sun/Mars activity as participation in a shared undertaking. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) gives marriage, the husband’s role, and activity in art or community as examples; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) specifies creating together, physical and daily work for a community, and a working community's center. The throughline is personal effort expressed within a group or partnership, while the source’s husband example remains attributed rather than a rule about gender.
