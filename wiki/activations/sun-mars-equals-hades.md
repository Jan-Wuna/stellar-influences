## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Vulgar activity of a man. Working with garbage or evil smelling matter. Scuffle resulting in injuries. Personally involved in the adjustment of damages.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 147 / PDF page 72, Hades glyph under Sonne + Mars. English translation checked against the complete row.

A man's work service, difficult daily work, dangerous personal work, the man's criminal actions, personal activity in secret, dirty physical work, and renunciation of personal actions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal work carried out under difficult or unpleasant conditions.
- psychology: No separate psychological disposition is established.
- body/health: Witte names injuries in a scuffle; no diagnosis follows.
- social/relationship: Service, hidden activity, and damage adjustment are examples.
- events/manifestations: Dirty or hazardous labor and involvement in damages.
- conflicts/notes: Renunciation of personal actions is a separate ABC example; criminality and injury remain attributed source possibilities.

## Contradictions

There is no direct contradiction among difficult labor, hidden activity, and damage adjustment. Criminality and renunciation of personal actions are distinct attributed ABC examples, not inferences about every person.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Mars = Hades`, Hades specifies Sun/Mars activity through difficult, dirty, hazardous, or concealed work. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) includes handling refuse and personally dealing with damages, while the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds arduous work, activity in secret and renunciation of personal actions. The entries also contain injury and criminal-action examples, which remain attributed possibilities. They do not justify equating unpleasant work with wrongdoing.
