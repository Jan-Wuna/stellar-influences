## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The fiance. A man happy in his activity. The victorious one.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work pleasure of a man, successful work of a man, production pleasure of a man, personal work with money, personal success actions, legal activity of a man, activity in a financial center, and center for work law

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A positive outlook on life, optimism, striving for the attainment of power. The fiance, the civil servant. The act of taking possession, successful activity.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To have much enthusiasm for getting tasks accomplished. To overdo things, or to do too much. Confidence in one’s ability to get things done. Enthusiasm translated into accomplishment.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Increases your determination to develop physically; an awareness of the political strengths of others; inflating an already big ego; helps brings success during competitive activities; publicity about feats of courage or daring.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Mars = Jupiter`: success in enterprise, confident execution, and a natural tendency to win ground through energetic initiative. Main caution: overdoing: confidence in one's force can lead to excess effort, inflated risk-taking, or too much heat in pursuit of victory.
