## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Acquaintance with men, the husband or soldiers through work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Contact Centre for Work, Human Liaison Activity, Linked to Corporal Work, Wanting Personal Encounters, Personal Work Contacts, Connections of an Effective Man, and Centre for Work Achievement

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Cultivation of good fellowship, desire to succeed in teamwork, joint plans or undertakings. Confederation and association in working or fighting for certain objectives. Marriage community.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Wanting one’s long-range efforts to contribute to progress. Wanting to connect with people who one can work with toward meaningful goals. An egotistical way of asserting oneself can lead to negative results. One evolves through self-assertion and through being positively willful.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `68`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increases your need to work with others as a team during activities; meeting and working with others who encourage your to be more competitive; working to be part of a team which does gains renown for its physical feats.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Mars = Node`: successful teamwork, productive group effort, and evolution through self-assertion within relationship or community. Main caution: ego in collaboration: if assertion becomes too personal, the shared effort fractures instead of uniting.
