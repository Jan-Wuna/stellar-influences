## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Acquaintance with men, the husband or soldiers through work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 146 / PDF page 72, Node glyph under Sonne + Mars. English translation checked against the complete row.

Contact center for work, human activity of making connections, connected with physical work, wanting personal encounters, personal work contacts, connections of an energetic man, and center for job placement.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Cultivation of good fellowship, desire to succeed in teamwork, joint plans or undertakings. Confederation and association in working or fighting for certain objectives. Marriage community.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 12, complete Node entry under Planets activating the Sun/Mars midpoint.

Wanting one’s long-range efforts to contribute to progress. Wanting to connect with people who one can work with toward meaningful goals. An egotistical way of asserting oneself can lead to negative results. One evolves through self-assertion and through being positively willful.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `68`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increases your need to work with others as a team during activities; meeting and working with others who encourage your to be more competitive; working to be part of a team which does gains renown for its physical feats.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections formed through shared purposeful work.
- psychology: Desire for meaningful contribution and constructive assertion.
- body/health: Physical teamwork is named without medical detail.
- social/relationship: Fellowship, associations, and joint undertakings.
- events/manifestations: Working or competing as a team.
- conflicts/notes: Ego assertion can hinder shared aims in Sandbach.

## Contradictions

Cooperation and egotistical assertion are different ways of participating, not contradictory predictions. ABC's job-placement center is a distinct practical setting for work contacts.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Mars = Node`, Node specifies Sun/Mars activity through the people joined in its pursuit. The [Ebertin Entry](#ebertin-entry) emphasizes fellowship and association around an objective; the [Munkasey Entry](#munkasey-entry) makes a competitive team a concrete example. The [Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry) adds the wish to contribute through meaningful work, alongside the possibility that egotistical self-assertion produces poor results. The organizing theme is purposeful effort becoming a basis for connection and shared undertaking.
