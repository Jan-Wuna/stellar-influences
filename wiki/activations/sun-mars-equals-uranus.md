## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The violent man. To threaten or beat-up somebody. The man who suddenly becomes active. Being summoned for war (campaign).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

exciting workday, excited personal actions, actions of nervous persons, human reform actions, personal tensions at work, personal impulses of will, and technical work center

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Premature action or hastiness, impulsive behavior, tendency to do one's work in a state of excitement, overtaxing one's strength. Sudden events, adjustment to new conditions or circumstances, change of vocation, military call-up.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Getting things accomplished erratically or sporadically. Waiting for the inspira tion to come before one accomplishes something. A genius for finding ways to accom plish goals. The tendency to work hard on one’s own independent projects.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Toughens your resolution to be a bruising and non-stop competitor; adds to your indifference about the pain and struggle you endure during rigorous activities; unexpected problems or snags in your activities or conditioning.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Mars = Uranus`: technical ingenuity, bold initiative, and rapid adaptation when old methods are too slow. Main caution: instability: premature action, excitement, or sporadic effort can create accidents, reversals, or wasted force.
