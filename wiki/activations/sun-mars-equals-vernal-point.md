## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be active in public. To be busy. Workman. Soldier.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 146 / PDF page 72, Vernal Point glyph under Sonne + Mars. English translation checked against the complete six-clause row.

Acting persons in public life, human creation in public, public center for work, a man's publicity work, day of work in public, and daily publicity work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal activity carried into public life.
- psychology: No distinct psychological claim is stated.
- body/health: No body or health claim is stated.
- social/relationship: Public work and participation are central.
- events/manifestations: Being busy publicly; worker or soldier examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's publicity-work and public work-center examples do not establish an office or authority.

## Contradictions

The entries agree on public activity. ABC specifies public creation, a work center and publicity work, while Witte separately supplies worker and soldier examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Mars = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies Sun/Mars activity through its public setting. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) describes being publicly active and busy, with worker and soldier examples; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) specifies public creation, a work center and publicity work. The supported meaning is personal effort taking place in public life. The examples do not establish a particular public office or authority.
