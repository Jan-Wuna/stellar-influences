## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A man who is energetically active. The producer, manufacturer, fighter, sharpshooter.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 147 / PDF page 72, Zeus glyph under Sonne + Mars. English translation checked against the complete eight-clause row.

The man's work objectives, the man's leadership activity, personal shaping of work, personal work achievements, personal activity with fire, day of work with fire, the man's procreation efforts, and striving toward goals in personal work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Energetic effort aimed at production or a definite task.
- psychology: Work objectives and striving toward a goal in ABC.
- body/health: No distinct medical claim is stated.
- social/relationship: Producer, manufacturer, fighter, and sharpshooter in Witte.
- events/manifestations: Productive work, performance, and work with fire.
- conflicts/notes: Leadership, work with fire and procreation efforts are distinct source examples, without requiring a particular event.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction appears in the productive and goal-directed themes. ABC's leadership activity and procreation efforts are distinct examples of purposeful action.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Mars = Zeus`, Zeus specifies Sun/Mars activity as energetic effort directed toward making, performing, or attaining something. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) gives the producer and manufacturer alongside fighter and sharpshooter; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) names work objectives, leadership, performance, and work with fire. The throughline is focused production and purposeful action. ABC also names procreation efforts; this remains an attributed example rather than a promised outcome.
