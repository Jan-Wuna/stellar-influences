## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be loyal to the authorities with body and soul. A high personage.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 63, supplied PDF p. 30, Meridian + Sonne, Kronos row (eight clauses). Translation from the German scan:

Authority of my man; a special minute of the day; dominance of an ensouled person; my independent man; my physical superiority; personal reputation; the man's greatness of soul; superiority of my person.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal standing in relation to authority.
- psychology: Loyalty to authority with body and soul in Witte.
- body/health: ABC names physical superiority without a bodily measurement or health assessment.
- social/relationship: A high personage or relation to an authority figure.
- events/manifestations: Loyal service or occupying an elevated position.
- conflicts/notes: The actual row says independent man; superiority does not guarantee moral excellence.

## Contradictions

Loyalty to authority and being a high personage are distinct roles within Witte's entry. ABC adds independence, reputation, physical superiority and greatness of soul; these do not specify bodily measurements or make elevated standing a guarantee of moral excellence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/MC = Kronos`, Kronos specifies Sun/MC personal direction through authority and elevated standing. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) includes both wholehearted loyalty to authorities and the high personage; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds independence, personal reputation and superiority. The emphasis concerns how one's own stance relates to authority, whether as a loyal participant or as the person who holds it. ABC's physical superiority and greatness of soul remain broad source descriptions.
