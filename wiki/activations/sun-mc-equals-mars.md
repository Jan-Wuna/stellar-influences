## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

With body and soul at work. Going into action. Expression of will. Making a decision. To make a complete job of it.

Source locator: PDF p. 59, Meridian + Sun, Mars entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My husband's work, the treatment of a person's soul, my corporal actions, my personal will, my personal creativity, the emotional impulses of the man, the activity of body and soul, and the active soul of the man

Source locator: printed p. 62, PDF p. 30, Sun/MC, Mars entry. Menschen means a person in the treatment clause; the gendered Mann clauses remain distinct.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Strong individuality, energetic pursuit of one's objectives, urge to fulfil one's mission at all costs, making personal decisions. A powerful personality.

Source locator: printed p. 111, coded entry 0318.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One who is very conscious about the meaning and purpose of what one is doing, and who desires one’s actions to move one toward one’s spiritual goals. To feel con flicted about what is most important for one to accomplish. Negatively, an aggressive projection of one’s self-importance onto the world.

Source locator: PDF pp. 24–25, Sun/Midheaven, Mars entry begins at the foot of p. 24 and continues at the top of p. 25.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `100`

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds energy, activity, and impetus to your intentions to gain recognition in life; pushing ahead with your plans for beating competitors; showing initiative in situations where progress is too slow; organizing sports activity.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal purpose committed to decisive action.
- psychology: Strong will, individual decisions, and concern about priorities.
- body/health: Body-and-soul activity is stated without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Competition or an aggressive projection of self-importance.
- events/manifestations: Initiative, pursuing objectives, or organizing sport.
- conflicts/notes: Ebertin's at-all-costs mission and Sandbach's conflict over priorities or aggressive self-importance qualify the shared forceful action.

## Contradictions

The entries share purposeful action but allow conflict over what matters most. Sandbach’s aggressive self-importance is a qualification rather than a denial of Ebertin’s energetic individuality.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/MC = Mars`, Mars specifies Sun/MC personal purpose by putting it into action. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) joins body and soul at work with decisions and an expression of will; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds personal actions, will and creative work. The [Ebertin Entry](#ebertin-entry) emphasizes energetic pursuit of objectives, including an at-all-costs mission. The [Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry) asks whether action serves what is truly important, while allowing aggressive self-importance; the [Munkasey Entry](#munkasey-entry) adds initiative toward recognition and competition. Personal direction becomes a definite undertaking, with priorities and the force of self-assertion retained as qualifications.
