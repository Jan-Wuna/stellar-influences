## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be and remain adaptable and mutable with body and soul. Big changes in life. Change in one’s own physical perception.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

le, my personal development, my corporal transformation, seclic development of the man, changes in my body, and growing soul of the man

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A violent attainment of one's aims, desire to attain leadership by the use of force, realization of extraordinary and unusual plans, a tragic destiny.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An intense craving to be an important person, and/or to play a meaningful part in the world, with the potential of eventually becoming so burnt out on this that one’s spiritual path is finally made clear to one. Obsessively placing too much importance on one’s security. One feels tremendous inner pressure to attain mastery in one’s career and to follow one’s heart’s desire.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased opportunities for your personal and social contributions to be recognized more universally; important secrets which help you achieve fame; reduced personal honor even if you are exceptionally honest.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/MC = Pluto`: massive drive, the urge to play a meaningful role in the world, and the capacity to force extraordinary plans into reality. Main caution: compulsion: career, security, or destiny can become dominated by control struggles, tragic turns, or burnout.
