## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The body and soul are inhibited. Pessimist. Separation. Give up a particular objective. Sadness. Grief.

Source locator: PDF p. 59, Meridian + Sun, Saturn entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Old soul in the body, patient soul of the man, hardness of my husband, concentration on the minute of the day, my serious man, my bodily pain, minute of the day in the evening, maturing soul in the body, and separation of the soul from the body

Source locator: printed p. 63, PDF p. 30, Sun/MC, Saturn entry. Translation checked against the German row, including Härte and körperlichen Schmerzen.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A negative outlook on life, the negation of life, reserve, inhibitions. The tendency to withdraw or to retire, the mood of sadness. The necessity to make grave or difficult decisions.

Source locator: printed p. 111, coded entry 0320.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The slow attainment of recognition in one’s career. Feeling sensitive about one’s place in the world. A lack of self-confidence in one’s work or profession. One may feel uncomfortable receiving recognition for one’s work.

Source locator: PDF p. 25, Sun/Midheaven, complete Saturn entry.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `100`

#### Munkasey Entry

Working harder than others to ensure that your efforts are better planned and implemented; brings less public recognition but more internal satisfaction and pride about your capabilities; a retraction of prior praise or honors.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal direction inhibited or recognition delayed, with patient effort and inward satisfaction as alternatives.
- psychology: Reserve, sadness, low confidence and discomfort with recognition; pride in one's capabilities also remains possible.
- body/health: Bodily pain and inhibited body/soul language remain historical source claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Withdrawal, separation and reduced or retracted public recognition.
- events/manifestations: Difficult decisions, giving up an objective, careful implementation and slow career recognition.
- conflicts/notes: External recognition and internal satisfaction can diverge; soul/body separation supplies no death forecast or timing rule.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize inhibition, withdrawal and difficult choices. Sandbach allows recognition to arrive slowly but remain uncomfortable; Munkasey explicitly pairs less recognition with greater internal satisfaction. ABC's patience and maturing-soul language are further alternatives to a purely negative outlook.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/MC = Saturn` puts personal direction and recognition under conditions of inhibition, delay or withdrawal. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describe sadness, relinquished aims and difficult decisions; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds slow recognition and discomfort with one's professional standing. Yet [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) retains better planned work and internal satisfaction despite less public praise, while [ABC](#abc-entry) gives patience and maturation beside seriousness and pain. The entries distinguish the difficulty of receiving recognition from the value or satisfaction found in the work itself. Bodily and soul/body claims remain attributed doctrine, without a medical or fatal prediction.
