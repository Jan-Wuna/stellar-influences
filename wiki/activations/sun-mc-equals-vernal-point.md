## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The physical and personal attitude of the native toward the world in general. The telluric influence upon the body (earth influence).

Source locator: PDF p. 59, Meridian + Sun, Vernal Point entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

my man in public, my day in public, my person in public life, minute of the day in public, soul of the man in public life, and soulful people in public

Source locator: printed p. 62, PDF p. 30, Sun/MC, Vernal Point entry.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The personal, bodily and inward self in relation to the wider world or public life.
- psychology: One's attitude toward the world and inner life expressed publicly.
- body/health: Witte's telluric influence is an attributed earth-influence claim, not a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: One's person or man in public and soulful people in public life.
- events/manifestations: A public day or moment is an ABC example; no particular event is specified.
- conflicts/notes: Public participation does not establish prominence, and source minute/day language supplies no timing rule.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/MC = Vernal Point` places the person and the relation of body and inner life in the wider world. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a physical and personal attitude toward the world in general; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives one's person, day and inner life in public settings. The shared emphasis is the personal self participating in a public field, without requiring special prominence. Witte's separate earth-influence clause remains attributed doctrine, and ABC's day or minute examples do not define a timing method.
