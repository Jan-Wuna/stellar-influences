## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Union of the powers of body and soul.

Source locator: PDF p. 59, Meridian + Sun, Vulcanus entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influence of my man, influence of body and soul, energy of my man, strength of the human body, power of my man, my bodily influence, my bodily strength, personal inner influence, and the man's inner strength.

Source locator: printed p. 63, PDF p. 30, Sun/MC, Vulcanus entry. Translation checked against the German row; Kraft is strength, Macht is power, and seelisch refers to inner or soul life without specifying an extrasensory faculty.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Bodily and inward strength joined in personal influence.
- psychology: Inner strength and personal inward influence are explicit ABC forms.
- body/health: Bodily strength and energy are source themes without a specific health condition.
- social/relationship: The influence, energy or power of one's man is an attributed relationship example.
- events/manifestations: Personal and bodily influence; no particular public role or event is supplied.
- conflicts/notes: Strength and power remain distinct; soul language establishes neither a psychic faculty nor inevitable worldly impact.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/MC = Vulcanus` joins bodily strength and inner strength in the person. [Witte](#witte-entry) states their union directly; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives bodily energy, personal influence, a man's power and inward strength as particular forms. The emphasis is the relation between physical capacity and inner influence, without requiring a public leadership role or a specific worldly result. ABC's soul-related language does not itself name an extrasensory ability.
