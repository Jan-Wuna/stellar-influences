## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The creative drive of the body and soul. Courageous, energetic or wanting to lead. To have to interfere.

Source locator: PDF p. 59, Meridian + Sun, Zeus entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A man's fiery soul, shaping the inner being in a person, my fiery man, my bodily achievements, my bodily procreation, my bodily goals, a person's inner goals, and a man's inner leadership.

Source locator: printed p. 63, PDF p. 30, Sun/MC, complete Zeus glyph row. Its first three clauses were displaced into Hades; körperlich means bodily, and seelisch retains the inner/soul sense.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creative drive expressed through bodily effort, inner aims and a wish to lead.
- psychology: Courage, energy and a fiery inward disposition are source themes.
- body/health: Bodily achievement and procreation are historical source meanings, not fertility or health assessments.
- social/relationship: Wanting to lead or having to intervene; no specific public role is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Shaping the inner being and pursuing bodily or inward goals.
- conflicts/notes: The urge to create or lead is explicit; successful leadership or public accomplishment is not guaranteed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/MC = Zeus` gives the relation of body and inner life an active, creative direction. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes courage, energy, wanting to lead and having to intervene; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a fiery soul, shaping the inner being and pursuing bodily or inward goals. Their common emphasis is a drive to bring inner purpose into action, with achievement and leadership retained as source forms rather than assured outcomes. The bodily procreation clause remains historical source language, not a fertility prediction.
