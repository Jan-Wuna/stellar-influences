## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: `103`.

Success and experiences in the time of youth. Broad encompassing thinking. Far- reaching thoughts. To speak several languages. To talk much.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: printed `143` (PDF `70`).

Success of young people, talk about personal freedom, personal experiences in youth, personal experience reports, personal achievements in youth, a person who talks much, scientifically thinking people, and increasing passenger traffic

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: a broad reach of thought, language, and youthful experience.
- psychology: encompassing or scientific thinking and an interest in personal freedom.
- body/health: no specific health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: speaking several languages, talking much, and sharing experience.
- events/manifestations: success in youth, personal achievement, and increasing passenger traffic.
- conflicts/notes: the entries connect breadth and success but do not guarantee either.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict is evident: Witte stresses the breadth of thought and languages, while ABC adds freedom, scientific thought, and passenger traffic.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Mercury = Apollon`, Apollon specifies an expansive reach of thinking and communication: broad thoughts, several languages, much conversation, and accounts of personal experience. The entries connect this range with learning through youth and with youthful achievement; ABC also includes scientific thinking, discussion of freedom, and increasing passenger traffic. These are related manifestations of breadth, without establishing success in every instance. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF 103; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed 143.)
