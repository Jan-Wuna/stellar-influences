## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sickness in youth. Children’s diseases. Dirty thoughts and actions.

Source locator: PDF p. 103, Sun/Mercury, Hades entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Service to young people, an endangered personal youth, a person's dangerous thoughts, secret body language, criminal young people, lack of personal contact, a man's deficient speech, and lacking personal conversations.

Source locator: printed p. 143, PDF p. 70, Sun/Mercury, Hades entry. Translation checked against the German row; endangered youth and dangerous thoughts are separate clauses.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Youth, thought or communication in conditions of vulnerability, deficiency or service.
- psychology: Dangerous or concealed thought and expression are attributed examples, not a character assessment.
- body/health: Witte's sickness and childhood-disease claims remain historical source associations.
- social/relationship: Service to young people, lack of personal contact and missing conversations.
- events/manifestations: An endangered youth, deficient speech and secret body language are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Service and vulnerability differ from wrongdoing; criminal and moral labels establish neither diagnosis nor personal guilt.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Mercury = Hades` places youth, thought and communication in a difficult or vulnerable setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives historical sickness and morally charged thought/action examples; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds endangered youth, deficient speech, concealed body language and a lack of personal contact or conversation. ABC also explicitly includes service to young people, so difficulty may concern the people being helped as well as one's own experience. The health and criminal labels remain particular source associations, not diagnoses or judgments about an individual.
