#### Munkasey Entry

The various clever ways you place emphasis in your speech or writing; flamboyance with speaking gestures or delivery; any nuances of writing or speaking which add effective meaning to your communication abilities.

## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Mercury = Mercury` concerns the way expression gives a message its emphasis and meaning. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) connects clever stress in speech or writing with flamboyant gesture, delivery and finer expressive nuances. These forms work together as the manner through which an idea is conveyed, rather than supplying a separate event prediction. The entry's scope is expressive technique and its meaningful effect.
