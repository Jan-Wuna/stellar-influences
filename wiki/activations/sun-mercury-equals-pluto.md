## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A consciousness which is capable of development and transformation. Changeable thinking. The influence of the external motion on the internal development, the growth and the circulation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

developments, unfolding personal opinion, personal development of writing, changed personal traffic, growing media center, and transformation in personal thinking

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Making far-reaching plans. An orator exercising suggestive power.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s nervous system experiences stress due to too much intensity. The ten dency to obsess on certain ideas, and/or to be egotistical about one’s own thoughts. Until one arrives at a true sense of which ideas are most important there are nagging doubts and a driving urge to collect more information or to come to a resolution.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Important information that is transmitted world-wide, perhaps thru the use of satellites; concealing information that is important to others; theft or neglect of important communications devices or information.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Mercury = Pluto`: powerful planning, rhetorical influence, and a mind that keeps pressing until it reaches resolution or deeper truth. Main caution: overintensity: obsessive thought, nervous strain, and egotistical attachment to ideas can become consuming.
