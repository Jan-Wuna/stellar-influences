## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A consciousness which is capable of development and transformation. Changeable thinking. The influence of the external motion on the internal development, the growth and the circulation.

Source locator: PDF p. 103, Sun/Mercury, Pluto entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Unfolding of young people, development of the young body, conversations about bodily developments, unfolding personal opinion, personal development of writing, changed passenger traffic, a growing media center, and transformation in personal thinking.

Source locator: printed p. 143, PDF p. 70, Sun/Mercury, complete Pluto glyph row. The first three clauses were displaced into the preceding Neptune entry.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Making far-reaching plans. An orator exercising suggestive power.

Source locator: printed p. 82, coded entry 0202.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s nervous system experiences stress due to too much intensity. The ten dency to obsess on certain ideas, and/or to be egotistical about one’s own thoughts. Until one arrives at a true sense of which ideas are most important there are nagging doubts and a driving urge to collect more information or to come to a resolution.

Source locator: PDF p. 9, Sun/Mercury, complete Pluto entry.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MERCURY with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `60`

#### Munkasey Entry

Important information that is transmitted world-wide, perhaps thru the use of satellites; concealing information that is important to others; theft or neglect of important communications devices or information.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Developing or changing thought, influential planning and intense involvement with information.
- psychology: Obsession, doubt and a drive toward information or resolution coexist with changeable thinking.
- body/health: Growth/circulation and nervous-system stress are attributed source claims, not individual assessments.
- social/relationship: Suggestive oratory and withholding information important to others.
- events/manifestations: Writing or media development, worldwide transmission, and theft or neglect of information or devices.
- conflicts/notes: Intensity does not ensure truth or resolution; development, fixation and information loss remain distinct alternatives.

## Contradictions

Witte and ABC emphasize development and change, while Sandbach allows fixation, doubt and strain. Ebertin gives influential planning or speech; Munkasey retains both worldwide transmission and concealment, theft or neglect. These differences prevent a uniformly powerful or truth-seeking reading.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Mercury = Pluto` connects thought and communication with development, intensity and influence. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [ABC](#abc-entry) describe changing thought and unfolding capacities, while [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) adds far-reaching plans and suggestive oratory. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) retains obsession, doubt and pressure to gather information or reach resolution, with strain as a possible cost. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) extends the information theme to worldwide transmission as well as concealment, theft or neglect. The formula therefore preserves both developing communication and its pressured or obstructed forms, without making intensity a guarantee of truth or eventual resolution.
