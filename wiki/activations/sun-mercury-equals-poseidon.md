## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Spiritual and mental matters in youth. To concentrate on ideas and writings. Mental thinking and writing.

Source locator: PDF p. 103, Sun/Mercury, Poseidon entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

An insightful young man, a mental or spiritual youth center, intellectually rich personal conversations, conversations about body and mind, conversations about personal insights, clear body language, a rational personal opinion, and truth about young people.

Source locator: printed p. 143, PDF p. 70, Sun/Mercury, Poseidon entry. Translation checked against the German row; geistig retains its mental/spiritual range and vernünftig means rational.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Focused thought, writing and conversation concerned with insight and understanding.
- psychology: Concentration, insight and rational opinion are explicit themes.
- body/health: Clear body language and discussion of body and mind supply no health claim.
- social/relationship: Intellectually rich personal conversation and sharing insights.
- events/manifestations: Concentrating on ideas or writings and a mental/spiritual youth center.
- conflicts/notes: Truth is a particular source motif, not proof that every opinion or statement is correct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Mercury = Poseidon` focuses thought and communication on insight, understanding and ideas worth sustained attention. [Witte](#witte-entry) names concentration on ideas and writings in a mental or spiritual field; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds rich personal conversations, shared insights, clear body language and rational opinion. Its youth-center and insightful-young-man examples retain a youthful setting. The emphasis is clarity and considered understanding, while the truth motif remains a source meaning rather than a guarantee of anyone's truthfulness.
