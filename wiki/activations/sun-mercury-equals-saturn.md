## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Slowly running processes of consciousness. The faculty to become aware of something is slowed down. Serious practical thinking. Taking leave (parting). Travelling. The end of a journey. Arrival.

Source locator: PDF p. 103, Sun/Mercury, Saturn entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

burdened personal youth, serious personal conversations, disturbed passenger traffic, young and old persons, man thinking with concentration, personal thoughts of separation, mature thoughts of a person, and painful bodily movement

Source locator: printed p. 143, PDF p. 70, Sun/Mercury, Saturn entry. The scan reads gestörter Personen-Verkehr: disturbed passenger traffic.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inhibitions in thinking, pessimistic attitude to life, serious outlook on life, life experience. Thinking of separation, parting or farewell.

Source locator: printed p. 81, coded entry 0199.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A slow, careful, or patient way of expressing what one thinks is important. To be frustrated about others not seeing the importance of one’s ideas. The ability to figure out what needs to be said, and the ability to say it in a clear and simple way. A sensi tiveness to one’s ideas being ignored or slighted. As more and more time goes on one becomes clearer and clearer about which ideas are most important to the self.

Source locator: PDF p. 9, Sun/Mercury, complete Saturn entry.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MERCURY with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `60`

#### Munkasey Entry

Going directly to the heart of any matter in discussion; encourages effective lines of reasoning, clearly and simply stated; stifles conversation and thinking; a stuffed shirt; frustrating or dejecting discussions with other people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought and communication made slow, serious or concentrated, sometimes inhibited.
- psychology: Patient clarity and mature thought coexist with pessimism, frustration or sensitivity to being ignored.
- body/health: Slowed awareness and painful movement remain attributed descriptions, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Serious or dejecting discussions, stifled conversation and thoughts of separation.
- events/manifestations: Parting, travel, arrival and disturbed passenger traffic are source examples.
- conflicts/notes: Restraint may support clear, simple reasoning or obstruct expression; neither outcome must follow.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin retain slowness, seriousness and separation. Sandbach and Munkasey also allow clear, simple expression, while both preserve frustration or stifled exchange. Concentration and inhibition should therefore remain distinct possibilities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Mercury = Saturn` slows or concentrates thought and communication. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describe serious practical thinking, inhibition and thoughts of parting; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds concentrated or mature thought alongside serious conversation and disrupted movement. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) retain clear, simple reasoning, but also frustration, ignored ideas and stifled exchange. The formula holds careful expression beside difficulty expressing or receiving a message, without assuming that every restriction becomes clarity. Bodily statements remain historical source claims.
