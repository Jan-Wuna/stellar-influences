## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To think and speak by leaps and bounds. Inventor. Technician. Sudden thoughts. Sudden action of a person. An uproar of young people. Demonstrations.

Source locator: PDF p. 103, Sun/Mercury, Uranus entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

excitement about young people, dynamic young man, event in passenger traffic, excited personal conversation, nervously speaking person, personal reform conversations, exciting news of the day, and discussing the day's events

Source locator: printed p. 143, PDF p. 70, Sun/Mercury, Uranus entry. Personen-Verkehr is passenger traffic.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Incoherent and erratic thinking, sudden ideas, inventions, a flair for applied science and technology, organization and reforms. Adjustment to new circumstances in life.

Source locator: printed pp. 81–82, PDF pp. 83–84, coded entry 0200. The sentence continues at the top of printed p. 82.

#### Source Footnotes

The following are footnotes 3 and 4 attached to the preceding Jupiter (0198) and Saturn (0199) entries on printed p. 81. They are preserved as source commentary, not as part of the Uranus entry.

3. Under Sun/Jupiter = Mercury we find among other remarks: "meditating or reflecting on health (the medical doctor)." It has been found frequently that in the case of doctors who really have a calling for their profession that not only this factor is often present but also its variations: Sun = Mercury/Jupiter and Sun/Mercury = Jupiter. It has often been found that while a certain three factors may be important for a particular thing that it appears to be unimportant which of these factors stands at the middle of the other two.

4. Not only in the case of Sun/Mercury = Saturn but also with other combinations care should be taken not to make a definitely positive or definitely negative interpretation as both the positive and the negative could occur at the same time. One should always consider to what extent the particular individual can use a combination such as Saturn= Sun/Mercury. This combination is often found in the charts of philosophers who require to think deeply and seriously over difficult problems. This is a part of their life and work. In the case of others however the experiences which these combinations can bring with them may show themselves in a serious or even pessimistic view of life.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

An ability to bring to light ideas that others did not see or expect. An usual manner of expressing one’s own ideas. Other people may think that the importance you place on certain ideas is strange and/or unusual. To be highly independent and/or original in one’s thinking.

Source locator: PDF p. 9, Sun/Mercury, complete Uranus entry. The edition's “An usual” wording is retained.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MERCURY with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `60`

#### Munkasey Entry

Reasoning and thinking about informal or novel ways of communicating to others; electro-mechanical devices which enhance communications or store or transmit ideas well; upsetting discussions with others; contrary thoughts.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden, independent or novel thought and communication, with technical and reforming applications.
- psychology: Original ideas and rapid shifts coexist with erratic thought or contrary opinions.
- body/health: Nervous speech and excitement are source descriptions without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Surprising or upsetting discussion, youth demonstrations and unusual personal ideas.
- events/manifestations: Invention, communication devices, technical organization, reform and changed circumstances.
- conflicts/notes: Preceding-entry footnotes remain separate commentary; Ebertin's doctor-example permutation claim does not merge the canonical orientations.

## Contradictions

The entries retain both original or inventive thought and erratic or upsetting expression. Ebertin's source footnote3 discusses alternative orientations in a doctor example; this remains his particular observation, while this page keeps Sun/Mercury = Uranus distinct. Footnote4's positive/negative coexistence warning is preserved as commentary on the preceding Saturn entry.

## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Mercury = Uranus` gives thought and communication a sudden, inventive or independent expression. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes leaps of thought, invention and sudden action; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds excited conversation, reform discussions and striking news. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) retains applied science, organization and adjustment beside erratic thinking. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) emphasizes original ideas others may find unusual, while [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) gives novel communication methods and devices alongside contrary thoughts or upsetting exchanges. The common theme is a departure from ordinary expression, with originality and disruption remaining distinct possibilities.
