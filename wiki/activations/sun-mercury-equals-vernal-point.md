## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The thinking man in general. Motion on the day. Travelling and wandering.

Source locator: PDF p. 103, Sun/Mercury, Vernal Point entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

young people in public, body language in public life, public passenger traffic, public center of opinion, personal conversations in public, and daily conversations in public life

Source locator: printed p. 142, PDF p. 70, Sun/Mercury, Vernal Point entry. Translation checked against the German row; Personen-Verkehr is passenger traffic and Tages-Gespräche refers to daily conversations.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thought, communication and movement in the general or public field.
- psychology: Thinking is explicit; no detailed character portrait is supplied.
- body/health: Bodily movement and body language are named without a health claim.
- social/relationship: Public conversation, young people in public and a public opinion center.
- events/manifestations: Travel, wandering, daily movement and public passenger traffic.
- conflicts/notes: Public setting does not establish prominence or environmental causation; day language supplies no timing method.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Mercury = Vernal Point` places thinking, communication and movement in a general or public setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives the thinking person, travel and wandering; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds public conversation, opinion, body language and passenger traffic. These passages connect everyday mental and bodily activity with public participation, without stating that surroundings cause one's thoughts or that participation brings prominence. The daily-motion language remains a source example rather than a timing rule.
