## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A wide consciousness. Impressive thinking and speaking ability. Great agility of body. The momentum of a body in action. Speedy action.

Source locator: PDF p. 103, Sun/Mercury, Vulcanus entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influential personal opinion, an influential youth center, a person's strength of thought, force or violence of young people, vigorous young people, a person's thoughts of power, personal conversational influence, and heavy passenger traffic.

Source locator: printed p. 143, PDF p. 70, Sun/Mercury, Vulcanus entry. Translation checked against the German row; Gewalt retains its force/violence range, distinct from Kraft and Macht.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Force, capacity or momentum in thought, speech and bodily action.
- psychology: Strong thought, influential opinion and thoughts of power are source forms.
- body/health: Agility, bodily momentum and speed are named without a specific health claim.
- social/relationship: Conversational influence, an influential youth center and collective youthful force.
- events/manifestations: Vigorous action and heavy passenger traffic; no specific achievement is promised.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's force/violence clause is an attributed possibility, not a label for an individual's character.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Mercury = Vulcanus` expresses thought and communication with strength or influence, while retaining bodily force and momentum. [Witte](#witte-entry) joins impressive thinking and speaking with agility and speedy action; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds influential opinion, conversational influence, vigorous young people and heavy passenger traffic. Its thoughts-of-power and force-or-violence clauses preserve more difficult forms beside capacity and vigor. The common emphasis is force in mental or bodily activity, without equating that force with sound judgment, achievement or a violent character.
