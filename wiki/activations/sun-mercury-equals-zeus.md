## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Youthful leader. Improviser. Methodical, purposeful and commanding in thought. Making suggestions. Putting a plan into action.

Source locator: PDF p. 103, Sun/Mercury, Zeus entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

A leading opinion center, leadership of young people, founding a youth center, bodily achievements of youth, creative young people, a creative media center, a man's media goals, discussing personal goals, and creative personal conversations.

Source locator: printed p. 143, PDF p. 70, Sun/Mercury, Zeus entry. Translation checked against the complete German row, including Führung and Gründung.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Purposeful or creative thought and discussion carried into organized action.
- psychology: Methodical thinking and improvisation are both explicit Witte forms.
- body/health: Bodily achievements of youth are named without a health claim.
- social/relationship: Youthful leadership, guiding young people and making suggestions.
- events/manifestations: Founding a youth center, creative media activity and putting a plan into action.
- conflicts/notes: Commanding thought, method and improvisation remain distinct possibilities; leadership or a goal does not guarantee success.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Mercury = Zeus` brings purposeful thought and creative conversation toward action. [Witte](#witte-entry) connects suggestions, methodical or commanding thought and improvisation with putting a plan into practice; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives leadership of young people, founding a youth center and creative media work as concrete forms. Discussing personal goals links the communicative side with those undertakings. The shared emphasis is giving ideas a practical or organized expression, while method, improvisation and youthful creativity remain different ways of doing so.
