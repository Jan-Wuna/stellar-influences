## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be successful daily and hourly. To collect many differing experiences. A time for success. Day of glory for a people.

Source locator: PDF p. 101, Sun/Moon, Apollon entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of a man's feelings, a woman's experiences with a man, a man's experiences with a woman, success between woman and man, a woman's personal freedom, many people of a nation, and a man's increasing feelings.

Source locator: printed p. 139, PDF p. 68, Sun/Moon, Apollon entry. Translation checked against the German row; the final clause concerns Gefühle, feelings, rather than life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Breadth of experience and feeling, with success in shared or collective life.
- psychology: Expanding or increasing feelings are explicit ABC themes.
- body/health: No bodily condition or lifespan claim is supplied by these entries.
- social/relationship: Mutual experiences between woman and man, personal freedom and many people of a nation.
- events/manifestations: Varied experiences, success between partners and collective glory are source examples.
- conflicts/notes: Daily/hourly success remains Witte's association, not a timing rule; popularity is not explicitly supplied.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Moon = Apollon` broadens experience and feeling within shared life. [Witte](#witte-entry) connects success with gathering many differing experiences and a collective form of recognition; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds expanding feelings, mutual experiences between woman and man, shared success and a woman's personal freedom. These passages retain personal relationships and a wider field of people as distinct settings for breadth. Witte's daily/hourly language does not supply a timing rule, and increasing feelings establishes neither longer life nor assured popularity.
