## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The daily and hourly associations with other people. Friendship ties.

Source locator: PDF p. 101, Sun/Moon, Asc entry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

The man's other woman, woman and man as partners, a feelingful male partner, a person of feeling as partner, personal feelings toward a partner, people of the nation at the place, human feelings at the place, the man's hour at the place, and the hour of the day at the place.

Source locator: printed p. 138, PDF p. 68, Sun/Moon, Asc entry. Translation checked against the full nine-clause German row; am Ort means at the place, not necessarily a village, and Gemüts-Mensch describes a person of feeling.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A frank and open behavior toward other persons, desire to make new contacts, an association, a friendship, sociable, meetings with other persons.

Source locator: printed p. 79, coded entry 0192.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The ability to project much vitality through one’s personality. The need to feel good about oneself and to see one’s self as effective and viable. One yearns to be involved in meaningful relationships. The ability to throw oneself wholeheartedly into the affairs of the moment. The personality projects a completeness and aliveness that others can be very drawn to.

Source locator: PDF p. 8, Sun/Moon, complete Ascendant entry.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MOON with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `56`

#### Munkasey Entry

Focusing on the immediate needs of your home or family; noticing the roles people play in your life; an ability to know the thoughts of those close to you; your awareness of how you meet and accept others in your life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Feeling, vitality and shared life expressed in direct contact with others.
- psychology: Wanting to feel effective and viable, with awareness of one's acceptance of other people.
- body/health: Projected vitality is Sandbach's description of presence, not a medical assessment.
- social/relationship: Friendship, new contacts, meaningful relationships and feelings toward a partner.
- events/manifestations: Meetings, local associations and attention to home or family needs.
- conflicts/notes: The entries do not assert dependence on others' approval; Munkasey's thought-knowing claim remains attributed rather than established mind-reading.

## Contradictions

Witte, ABC and Ebertin emphasize direct association and feeling between people. Sandbach adds an alive, wholehearted presence and a need for meaningful contact; Munkasey emphasizes familiar roles, home and acceptance. Daily/hourly examples supply no timing rule or fixed relationship outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Moon = Asc` expresses feeling and shared life through immediate contact. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describe friendship, sociability and new meetings; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds feelings toward partners and people in a local setting. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) gives this contact a wholehearted, vital presence and a desire for meaningful relationships. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds attention to home, family and the roles of people close to oneself. The common emphasis is how one meets and accepts others, without an inferred dependence on their approval or a claim that another person's thoughts are directly knowable.
