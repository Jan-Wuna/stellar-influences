## Identity

- Formula: `Sun/Moon = Asc`
- Astronomicon formula: `S/R = Asc`
- Axis page: [Sun/Moon](../axes/sun-moon.md)
- Triad hub: [Sun Moon Asc](../triads/sun-moon-asc.md)

## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The daily and hourly associations with other people. Friendship ties.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Women of men, women and men as partners, full fledged partners, men and women as partners, men and women in the village, men in the village, and hours of the day in the village

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A frank and open behavior toward other persons, desire to make new contacts, an association, a friendship, sociable, meetings with other persons.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The ability to project much vitality through one’s personality. The need to feel good about oneself and to see one’s self as effective and viable. One yearns to be involved in meaningful relationships. The ability to throw oneself wholeheartedly into the affairs of the moment. The personality projects a completeness and aliveness that others can be very drawn to.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Focusing on the immediate needs of your home or family; noticing the roles people play in your life; an ability to know the thoughts of those close to you; your awareness of how you meet and accept others in your life.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Moon = Asc`: a relationally legible presence, partnership-minded self-expression, and the capacity to carry inner coherence outward into daily life. Main caution: overidentification with reflection: self-balance can become too dependent on how others receive, mirror, or complete the person.
