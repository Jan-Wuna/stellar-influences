## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 101, Cupido entry under Sun + Moon.

Legal bonds of marriage. Harmonious and sociable. Leisure hours of the day.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 139 / PDF page 68, Cupido glyph row under Sun + Moon. English translation checked against the scan.

A feelingful community of persons, shared bodily feelings, community between woman and man, a community center for women, people of a national community, human feelings of community, and a personal community of feeling.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared feeling expressed in community and social bonds.
- Psychology: Personal and collective feelings of belonging together.
- Body/health: ABC mentions shared bodily feelings; no health outcome is specified.
- Social/relationship: Community between woman and man, sociability and legal marriage bonds.
- Events/manifestations: A women's community center, communal life and shared leisure.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries overlap in association; ABC's national and gendered examples remain historically situated.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears in these brief entries. Witte emphasizes marriage and sociability; ABC extends the field to communal feeling and collective settings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Moon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the Sun/Moon pair through shared feeling that takes social form: a partnership, a community of persons or a common setting in which people feel connected. [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) makes both personal and collective feeling explicit, while [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) gives legal marriage, sociability and leisure as concrete expressions. The unifying theme is participation in a bond or community, with bodily feeling included among the experiences shared; these examples do not establish an inevitable marriage or a rule for timing events.
