## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 101, Hades entry under Sun + Moon.

Miserable married life, partnership or friendship. Sad hours. Daily dealing with unpleasant matters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 139 / PDF page 68, Hades glyph row under Sun + Moon. English translation checked against the scan.

A dangerous hour for the man, the man's lack of feeling, disadvantages through the man's mother, a personal partner from the past, day and hour of criminality, a person's hidden feelings, and renunciation of personal feelings.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Emotional privation and unpleasant experience in personal bonds.
- Psychology: Lack of feeling, hidden feelings and relinquishing personal feelings.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies sickness; ABC's feeling-deficiency clause is not a medical statement.
- Social/relationship: Sad or miserable partnership and friendship; ABC also names a partner from the past and disadvantage through a mother.
- Events/manifestations: Dealing with unpleasant matters; ABC includes historical danger and criminality examples.
- Conflicts/notes: These are source-attributed possibilities, without evidence for inevitable suffering, criminal character or event timing.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize difficult personal experience. ABC's emotional concealment, past partner and danger examples extend beyond Witte's description of unhappy bonds; they do not contradict it.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Moon = Hades`, Hades specifies the Sun/Moon pair through personal bonds burdened by sadness, unpleasant matters or a lack of accessible feeling. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) describes unhappy partnership and friendship; [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) adds feelings that remain hidden or are relinquished, alongside a partner from the past. The shared thread is difficulty sustaining a satisfying emotional connection. ABC's further danger and criminality examples remain attributed historical claims; they establish neither a diagnosis nor a forecast about a person or relationship.
