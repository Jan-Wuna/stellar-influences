#### Munkasey Entry

Increased needs to express care or bring relief to important people in your life; women who help you determine your life’s purpose; the watchfulness or protection you extend to your home, family, or values in life.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Moon = Moon`, the repeated Moon specifies the Sun/Moon pair through caring for and protecting what makes life personally meaningful. [Munkasey's entry](#munkasey-entry) joins relief for important people with watchfulness over home, family and values, and includes women who help clarify one's purpose. Care becomes a concrete expression of that purpose within the relationships and commitments named by this brief entry.
