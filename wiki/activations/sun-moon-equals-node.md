## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 101, Node entry under Sun + Moon.

Meeting with others. Effecting a friendship. Getting acquainted.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 138 / PDF page 68, Node glyph row under Sun + Moon. English translation checked against the scan.

Relationships with the man's mother, connected by personal feelings, an emotional connection to the man, connected with people of the nation, personal feelings in a relationship, personal connection to the woman, and connection between man and woman.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 79, entry 0191.

Associative and joint qualities, adaptation. Commencement of an association, a contact, an association for the purpose of marriage.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 8, Node entry under Sun/Moon.

The need to whole-heartedly embrace new experiences. Overattachment to the past depletes one’s vitality. The ability to involve oneself in one’s connections with others in a complete way and to derive a fulfilling sense of meaning and purpose from them.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MOON with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `56`

#### Munkasey Entry

Creating the methods or procedures others need for their personal fulfillment; making sexual appraisals of others; gathering cooperation from people or groups to help yourself with your aims and purposes.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal feelings expressed through contact, association and cooperation.
- psychology: Adaptation, openness to new experience and meaning gained through wholehearted involvement.
- body/health: Sandbach associates overattachment to the past with depleted vitality; no disorder is specified.
- social/relationship: Meeting others, forming friendship and emotional bonds; cooperation with people or groups.
- events/manifestations: Beginning an association, getting acquainted and gathering support for aims; Ebertin includes association toward marriage.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's practical cooperation and sexual appraisal differ in emphasis from emotional connection; the gendered examples are not universal requirements.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in connection and association. Sandbach adds a limit concerning attachment to the past, while Munkasey emphasizes practical cooperation and appraisal; these do not directly contradict the other passages.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Moon = Node`, Node specifies the Sun/Moon pair through personal feeling that becomes a connection with others. [ABC](#abc-entry) repeatedly names emotional bonds, while [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describe meeting, acquaintance and the beginning of association. Involvement can provide meaning and purpose in [Sandbach's entry](#sandbach-entry), and practical cooperation toward aims in [Munkasey's entry](#munkasey-entry). The movement is toward participating fully in a connection, with Sandbach qualifying it through openness to new experience rather than overattachment to the past. Marriage is one stated purpose for association, not a required result.
