## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 101, Pluto entry under Sun + Moon.

The changing course of the day. Daily absorption of food. Development and transformation through marriage.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 139 / PDF page 68, Pluto glyph row under Sun + Moon. English translation checked against the scan.

Developments between woman and man, unfolding of the man's feelings, transformation of personal feelings, change in personal feelings, growing personal feelings, a growing body of a people, transformations of the woman's body, and changing bodily feelings.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 78, entry 0190.

A soul torn by inner conflict, biased attitude or changed circumstances leads to critical phases of development in life or to separation from others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 8, Pluto entry under Sun/Moon.

The tendency to be obsessive in the use of one’s energy and to be drawn with great intensity toward certain activities which one may engage in compulsively. Drastic changes in one’s vitality level. One may experience great tension and conflict between one’s emotions and one’s conscious, objective self. The need to acknowledge one’s emotions and to be truthful about how one feels without allowing those emotions to overtake one. The need to find positive and productive outlets for one’s copious amounts of emotional energy.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MOON with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `56`

#### Munkasey Entry

Focusing your attention on your own plans or goals; secrecy about the care or concern you are willing to show to family; making startling new plans for enhancing the overall quality of your life; drastic measures to protect self.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Change in personal feelings, vitality and relationships, sometimes through intense inner conflict.
- psychology: Concentrated emotional energy, obsessive involvement or tension between feelings and the conscious self.
- body/health: Bodily feeling and vitality change; Witte includes absorption of food, without a specified medical outcome.
- social/relationship: Development through marriage, possible separation and secrecy about care for family.
- events/manifestations: Critical development, changed circumstances, new plans and drastic self-protection.
- conflicts/notes: Ordinary development and severe conflict form a range; no crisis or separation is inevitable, and the collective-body example remains ABC's wording.

## Contradictions

ABC and Witte include development without requiring crisis. Ebertin and Sandbach emphasize conflict, while Munkasey adds secrecy and drastic measures; the stronger examples qualify the range rather than establish one compulsory outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Moon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the Sun/Moon pair through changes in feeling and personal life that can become intensely absorbing. [ABC](#abc-entry) repeatedly names the unfolding, growth and transformation of feelings, while [Witte](#witte-entry) includes development through marriage. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) and [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) show the more difficult expression: inner conflict or emotional intensity that can overwhelm one's conscious direction. Sandbach's emphasis on acknowledging feelings and finding productive outlets connects that intensity with a possible constructive response. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds concentrated plans, concealed care and drastic self-protection. These passages support a range from development to critical change, without requiring a crisis or separation.
