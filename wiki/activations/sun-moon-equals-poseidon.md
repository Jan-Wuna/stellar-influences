## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 101, Poseidon entry under Sun + Moon.

Days and hours devoted to spiritual things. Intellectual friendships.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 139 / PDF page 68, Poseidon glyph row under Sun + Moon.

Ideas between woman and man, clarity of woman on the day, clarity between woman and man, culture centre of a people, personal insight of woman, hour of truth for man, day and hour of knowledge, and spiritualization of woman and man

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal bonds expressed through ideas, insight and spiritual or intellectual interests.
- Psychology: Clarity, personal insight and recognition of truth.
- Body/health: Neither entry gives a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Intellectual friendship and ideas or clarity shared between woman and man.
- Events/manifestations: Spiritual pursuits, cultural centers and experiences of insight.
- Conflicts/notes: Spiritualization and truth are the sources' interpretive language; gendered and day/hour examples supply no universal rule or event timing.

## Contradictions

The short entries overlap in intellectual and spiritual interests. ABC's clarity and cultural examples extend Witte's friendship and spiritual-pursuit emphasis without a direct conflict.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Moon = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the Sun/Moon pair through intellectual and spiritual exchange within personal life. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) names intellectual friendship and spiritual pursuits; [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) gives ideas, clarity and insight between woman and man, with a cultural center as a collective example. The connecting theme is a bond or shared setting in which understanding matters. Truth and spiritualization remain the sources' terms, rather than proof of privileged knowledge or a guaranteed spiritual attainment.
