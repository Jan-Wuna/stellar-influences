## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 101, Saturn entry under Sun + Moon.

Serious and difficult times. Hard times to marry. Obstacles to marriage. Sorrowful marital states. Divorce. Separation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 139 / PDF page 68, Saturn glyph row under Sun + Moon. English translation checked against the scan.

Tasks between man and woman, the man's disturbed feelings, disturbed bodily feeling, inhibitions between woman and man, human losses within a nation, personal feelings of pain, the man's problematic hour, and separation between man and woman.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 78, entry 0187.

Inner inhibitions or repressions, depression, loneliness, separation from the community, renunciation, difficulties in the relationship with the other sex, joint and shared suffering, illness, separation (divorce).

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 7, Saturn entry under Sun/Moon.

The tendency to conserve one’s energy and to desire fewer and deeper involvements. One is vitalized by simplicity, spirituality, and by being in an organized and meaningfully structured environment. One assimilates information coming from the subconscious slowly, and tends to delve deeply into ideas, for one is always searching for the deeper meanings inherent in everything. At worst one inhibits one energy by being afraid of making mistakes or of failing, and may tend to be overly cautious.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MOON with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `56`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased personal discipline and work habits; determination to motivate yourself to work consistently and steadily to achieve results; helps you mold your fate; increases restrictions on your ability to progress in life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restriction and seriousness in personal life, with discipline and depth as possible expressions.
- psychology: Inhibition, loneliness or fear of failure; conserving energy and seeking fewer, deeper involvements.
- body/health: Disturbed bodily feeling, pain and illness are attributed source claims; Sandbach also describes vitality through simplicity and structure.
- social/relationship: Tasks and inhibitions between partners, obstacles to marriage and separation from others.
- events/manifestations: Steady work and structured living; shared difficulty, divorce and human losses appear as historical examples.
- conflicts/notes: Sources range from meaningful restraint to severe suffering; neither illness, loss nor relationship failure is inevitable.

## Contradictions

Witte, ABC and Ebertin emphasize difficult bonds and suffering. Sandbach and Munkasey also describe depth, meaningful structure and disciplined work, while retaining caution or restriction. Organized restraint and painful restriction are distinct expressions within these entries.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Moon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the Sun/Moon pair through a serious or restricted experience of feeling, involvement and personal bonds. [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describe obstacles, inhibition and shared difficulty, sometimes extending to separation. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) gives a more selective expression: conserving energy, choosing fewer and deeper involvements, and finding vitality in simplicity and meaningful structure. That concentration connects with [Munkasey's](#munkasey-entry) steady work and discipline, while fear of failure or excessive caution can inhibit it. The range includes purposeful restraint and painful restriction; the illness, loss and divorce examples remain attributed possibilities, rather than diagnoses or required outcomes.
