## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `101`, `SUN + MOON`, Venus row.

A friendship of love. A marriage of love. Husband and wife in harmony. Peaceful hours.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `138`, PDF page `68`, `Sonne + Mond`, Venus row.

Source wording: "Freude am Tag in der Stunde, Harmonie der Körper-Gefühle, Kunst-Zentrum des Volkes, Liebe zwischen Frau und Mann, Schönheit des Frauen-Körpers, Liebes-Gefühle des Mannes, persönliche Liebe zur Frau, Tag und Stunde der Liebe, Zuneigung zur Mutter des Mannes".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Joy in the day and hour; harmony of bodily feelings; an artistic center of the people; love between woman and man; beauty of the woman's body; the man's feelings of love; personal love for the woman; day and hour of love; affection for the man's mother.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source reference: printed page `78`, PDF page `80`, entry `0184`, Venus on the Sun/Moon axis.

Harmonious coordination between spiritual and emotional as well as masculine and feminine dispositions, power of attraction between the sexes, artistic interests. Friendship between lovers, marriage of love, harmonious marriage.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source reference: PDF page `7`, Venus under `Planets activating the Sun/Moon midpoint`; the chapter begins on PDF page `6`.

To feel a full and complete love when one loves. To be around beauty feeds one energy. One needs to be appreciated as well as to appreciate if a health and vitality are to be maintained. Good relationships and fulfilling romance make one feel happy and complete in body and soul.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/MOON with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `56`, PDF page `57`, `Ven` row.

#### Munkasey Entry

Developing your skills in or appreciation for the fine arts or music; increased gratitude for the efforts of others; admiration from others for your talents; helps you become more calm and relaxation in your personal life.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Loving harmony, attraction, and appreciation connect inner coordination with affectionate companionship; artistic enjoyment is part of the same range.
- Psychology: Appreciating and being appreciated, gratitude for others' efforts, recognition of talents, artistic interests, calm, and felt completeness are explicit.
- Body/health: Sandbach connects appreciation and relationship fulfilment with vitality and completeness in body and soul. This is his interpretive claim, not an established medical effect. ABC also names bodily feeling and beauty without a clinical condition.
- Social/relationship: Loving friendship and marriage, affection, attraction, and peaceful companionship recur. ABC separately names personal love for a woman and affection for a man's mother; these are attributed historical examples.
- Events/manifestations: Peaceful hours, loving partnership, developing fine-art or musical skills, appreciation of beauty, gratitude, and admiration for talent are supported expressions.
- Conflicts/notes: These entries supply an affirmative range without establishing a universal outcome or an independently sourced negative profile. Historical gender categories remain attributed, and overlapping wording does not prove independent corroboration.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in affection, appreciation, harmony, and art without a direct conflict in the passages represented here. Munkasey's emphasis on arts, gratitude, and talent broadens the reading beyond romance; his omission of marriage is not a denial of the other entries. The old ABC phrases about "body flies," an "artificial center," and "shame" were translation/OCR errors, replaced from the checked Venus row.

Sandbach's "if a health" and Munkasey's "calm and relaxation" are awkward phrases present in the supplied PDFs, retained as written. The synthesis paraphrases their appreciation, vitality, and calm themes without reconstructing missing words or treating a health association as a medical finding.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Moon = Venus`, the activating Venus gives the [Sun/Moon relationship](../axes/sun-moon.md#derived-synthesis) the character of loving harmony and mutual appreciation. The entries connect coordination between inner dispositions with affection between people: attraction, loving friendship or marriage, and peaceful companionship. Artistic interests and enjoyment of beauty belong to this accord as well. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The meaning extends beyond romance into valuing what others do and feeling valued in return. Fine art, music, gratitude, and admiration for talents connect aesthetic pleasure with recognition; Sandbach describes the resulting experience as completeness in body and soul. His vitality language remains an attributed interpretation. This exact orientation supports a reading of affection, appreciation, and artistic fulfilment without guaranteeing a marriage or transferring its claims to the [other orientations](../triads/sun-moon-venus.md). ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))
