## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 101, Vernal Point entry under Sun + Moon.

Friendship with other people. The husband and wife. Acquaintances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 138 / PDF page 68, Vernal Point glyph row under Sun + Moon. English translation checked against the scan; the day-of-the-mothers clause is retained literally without identifying a holiday.

Woman and man in public life, the woman's body in public, people of the nation in public, the man's hour in public, the day of the mothers in public life, and day and hour in public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal association and the presence of people in public life.
- Psychology: No distinct inner disposition is specified by these brief entries.
- Body/health: ABC mentions a woman's body in public without a health claim.
- Social/relationship: Friendship, acquaintances, husband and wife; people encountered in public settings.
- Events/manifestations: Participation or presence in public life, including ABC's gendered day/hour examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte emphasizes association and ABC public settings; the examples do not establish fame, a named holiday or event timing.

## Contradictions

The entries give different levels of description: friendship and acquaintance in Witte, public life in ABC. Neither states a direct conflict with the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Moon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Sun/Moon pair through personal associations situated among other people and in public life. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) names friendship, acquaintances and husband and wife; [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) repeatedly places people and bodily presence in public settings. The shared field is social participation and encounter. Neither brief entry develops a separate psychological account of the person.
