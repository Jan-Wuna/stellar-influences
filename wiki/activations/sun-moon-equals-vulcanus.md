## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 101, Vulcanus entry under Sun + Moon.

Times when big things happen. Daily and hourly to exercise or experience executive power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 139 / PDF page 68, Vulcanus glyph row under Sun + Moon. English translation checked against the scan.

Energies between woman and man, influence of personal feelings, the man's strength of feeling, violence between man and woman, the woman's personal energy, personal feelings of power, the man's strong mother, and the woman's personal influence.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Strong personal feelings and energy expressed as influence or power.
- Psychology: Strength of feeling and personal feelings of power.
- Body/health: Personal energy is named without a specific bodily or medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Energies and influence between woman and man; ABC includes a strong mother and a violence example.
- Events/manifestations: Exercising or experiencing executive power and consequential events in Witte.
- Conflicts/notes: Emotional strength, personal influence, executive power and violence are distinct source examples; violence is neither a required outcome nor a justification.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes executive power and consequential events, while ABC gives emotional and relational forms of energy and influence. The violence clause is a more severe example within that range, not a synonym for every form of strength.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Moon = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Sun/Moon pair through feelings and personal energies that carry considerable influence. [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) joins strength of feeling with personal power and influence between people; [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) gives exercising or experiencing executive power as another expression. The connecting theme is the force with which personal life is felt or affected. ABC also names violence between man and woman, a distinct attributed example that cannot be assumed from emotional strength or influence alone.
