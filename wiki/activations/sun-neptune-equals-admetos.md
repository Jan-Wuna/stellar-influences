## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 113 / PDF page 109, Admetos entry under Sun + Neptune.

Frozen or chilled body. Coagulation of the blood. Stiffness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 155 / PDF page 76, Admetos glyph row under Sun + Neptune. English translation checked against the scan.

Dissolution of personal depressions, a constricted insecure person, strengthening of a person's sensitivity, poison in the body's circulation, a man in deep water, a creeping bodily blockage, and suppression of weak persons.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Constriction or stillness alongside sensitivity; ABC also describes relief from depression and strengthened sensitivity.
- Psychology: Insecurity under constraint, with dissolution of personal depressions given as a separate ABC possibility.
- Body/health: Witte's cold, blood coagulation and stiffness and ABC's poison/blockage claims remain historical medical doctrine, not diagnoses.
- Social/relationship: Suppression of weak persons is ABC's interpersonal example.
- Events/manifestations: A constricted person or a man in deep water; bodily stillness and blockage are source examples.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's relief and strengthening clauses differ from the restrictive examples; none establishes treatment, a medical outcome or a required event.

## Contradictions

Witte's short entry is entirely bodily and restrictive. ABC includes constriction and blockage but also dissolution of personal depressions and strengthening of sensitivity; those different possibilities cannot be reduced to one uniformly adverse outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Neptune = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the Sun/Neptune pair through sensitivity experienced with constriction or stillness. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) gives cold and stiffness as bodily examples; [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) includes a constrained, insecure person, blockage and suppression of weak persons. ABC also describes dissolution of personal depressions and strengthening of sensitivity, preserving a possibility of relief or consolidation within the entry's range. The bodily and depression-related claims remain attributed source doctrine, without establishing a diagnosis, treatment or forecast.
