## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The ability to sense the future. To feel future things. A talent and success in new upcoming future sciences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 155 / PDF page 76, Apollon glyph under Sonne + Neptun. English translation checked against the complete row.

Spreading uncertainty during the day, success through personal sensitivity, a man's success in the air, a person's poison trade, personal experiences of intuition, increase of water in the body, and center for future science.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: intuitive perception of what is developing, with success in future-oriented inquiry.
- psychology: sensing future possibilities and learning through personal intuition.
- body/health: ABC names increased water in the body; this remains an attributed bodily example, without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: no specific relationship pattern is established by these entries.
- events/manifestations: talent for emerging sciences; success through sensitivity; ABC also names spreading uncertainty.
- conflicts/notes: the ABC phrases about poison trade and increased bodily water are separate source associations, not explanations of scientific talent.

## Contradictions

- The entries give different applications without a direct contradiction. ABC's poison trade and increased bodily water remain distinct from sensitivity and future-oriented science; the row does not make them explanations of scientific talent.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies the Sun/Neptune axis as sensitivity that can become knowledge or success in an emerging field: the emphasis is on sensing what is coming and developing that perception into future-oriented inquiry. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) explicitly connects anticipation with talent in new sciences; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds success through sensitivity and personal intuitive experience. Uncertainty remains part of the range, but imagined honors or occult success are not established by these passages.
