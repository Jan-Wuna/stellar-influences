## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Your energy helps people to become more aware of subtleties, and to bring their intuitions to light. Through you others come to an awareness of the deeper and hidden meanings in their existence, and this heals them.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: helping others recognize subtle and hidden meaning in their lives.
- psychology: bringing intuitions into awareness rather than leaving them unrecognized.
- body/health: Sandbach calls this healing, but supplies no bodily condition or clinical claim.
- social/relationship: one's presence helps other people articulate their subtler perceptions.
- events/manifestations: recognition of deeper meaning through contact with the person described.
- conflicts/notes: this is one author's account; it does not state that identity must dissolve first.

## Contradictions

- The single entry supplies no opposing account to compare. Its healing language remains Sandbach's interpretation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Chiron specifies the Sun/Neptune axis as a capacity to help others bring subtle intuitions and hidden meanings into awareness. The emphasis is interpersonal: one's energy helps people understand their own existence more deeply. [Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry) calls that recognition healing; it does not require dissolution of identity or establish a medical effect.
