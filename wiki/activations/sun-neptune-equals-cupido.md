## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Dissatisfied relationship with the marriage partner. Terminating a membership of an association. Mediumistic artist. Sensibility for art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 155 / PDF page 76, Cupido glyph under Sonne + Neptun. English translation checked against the complete row.

Shared personal future, community of disappointed people, community center of metaphysics, community of persons in the air, personal disappointment in the community, and insecure community of persons.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sensitivity and uncertainty within shared life, alongside artistic receptivity.
- psychology: dissatisfaction with belonging; sensitivity to art.
- body/health: no bodily condition is established by these entries.
- social/relationship: dissatisfaction with a marriage partner, uncertain community ties, or disappointed members of a group.
- events/manifestations: ending an association membership; mediumistic art; a community centered on metaphysics or a shared future.
- conflicts/notes: artistic sensitivity and fragile belonging are separate applications; neither establishes an anti-social character.

## Contradictions

- No direct opposition is stated between the artistic and relationship meanings. ABC places personal disappointment and insecure community beside a shared future and a metaphysical center; the entry does not require all settings to occur together.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies the Sun/Neptune axis in shared life and art: sensitivity can find an artistic or metaphysical setting, while uncertainty or disappointment can make a partnership or association difficult to sustain. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) joins sensitivity to art with dissatisfaction in marriage and ending a membership; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds uncertain community ties and a shared future. These passages support fragile belonging, not a general anti-social disposition.
