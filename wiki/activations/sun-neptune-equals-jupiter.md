### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sun + Neptune`

#### Witte Entry

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Sun/Neptune`
- Source page: `99`
- Entry: `0271`

#### Ebertin Entry

Optimism in spite of little vitality. The capacity to enjoy pleasant things in life in spite of weakness or illness, achievement of success with little exertion.
