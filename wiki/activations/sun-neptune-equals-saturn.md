## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Lingering maladies. A body enfeebled by disease. Blood diseases. Poor states of health. Losses through deceit or weakness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 155 / PDF page 76, Saturn glyph under Sonne + Neptun. English translation checked against the complete row.

Disappointed old person, disturbed body in the air, concentration on the personal future, personal weakness through difficulties, uncertainty through separation from a person, and creeping bodily pains.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inhibitions through illness or physical debility, emotional affliction. Illness through mental and emotional suffering, impeded blood circulation, bad or unhealthy blood.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 19, complete Saturn entry under Planets activating the Sun/Neptune midpoint.

Being inhibited because one fears being hurt due to one’s sensitivities. Being cautious about revealing one’s sensitivities. Being oppressed by negative psychic energy and the need to learn how to protect oneself from it. Frustration due to having goals that are unrealistic or too idealistic to reach.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `84`

#### Munkasey Entry

Brings discipline to shaping reality thru your dreams; persisting in working toward your ideals and dreams; frustrations in attaining your goals without much long practice and work; the actualization of inner potential.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The restraint and sustained effort involved in working with sensitivity and ideals.
- psychology: Fear of being hurt may inhibit expression; discipline may help an ideal take practical form.
- body/health: Older illness, blood, and bodily-pain claims remain attributed historical associations.
- social/relationship: Caution about revealing sensitivity; ABC also names uncertainty after separation.
- events/manifestations: Long practice toward a dream, frustration with an unrealistic goal, or losses through weakness/deceit.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's disciplined realization must remain visible beside the predominantly inhibiting accounts.

## Contradictions

- The emphasis differs materially: Munkasey allows disciplined realization, while Sandbach emphasizes inhibition and unrealistic goals. Neither establishes a necessary outcome. ABC's disappointed older person, future concentration and separation uncertainty supply distinct settings rather than a single necessary outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies the Sun/Neptune axis through limits and the effort required to give an ideal practical form. Sensitivity may make a person cautious about exposure or frustrated by an unreachable aim, as [Sandbach Entry](#sandbach-entry) describes; [Munkasey Entry](#munkasey-entry) also allows persistence and long practice to actualize potential. The historical illness descriptions in [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin Entry](#ebertin-entry) should remain attributed rather than being made the cause or inevitable result of inhibition.
