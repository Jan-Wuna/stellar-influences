## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A day for fliers, inspiration, delicate health or sickness. Sensitive to weather. Uncertain times. To have a poor sense of direction.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

foreign people in public, body in the water of the public, body in the air of the world, unknown bodies in the air, unsettled people in public, and person disappointed by public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertainty and sensitivity appearing in public or shared circumstances.
- psychology: Poor sense of direction or disappointment with public life.
- body/health: Witte names delicate health and weather sensitivity as historical associations, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Unsettled people or unfamiliar people in public.
- events/manifestations: Aviation, bodies in air or water, uncertain times, and inspiration are the concrete source applications.
- conflicts/notes: These examples do not establish a general spiritual atmosphere.

## Contradictions

- The entries give public and environmental examples rather than directly conflicting claims. Their air/water wording remains literal source imagery; it need not be converted into psychological doctrine.

## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies the Sun/Neptune axis in public circumstances: uncertainty, sensitivity, and an unclear sense of direction move into the shared world. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) gives uncertain times, weather sensitivity, inspiration, and aviation; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) places unsettled or disappointed people in public life and retains concrete air/water settings. The common thread is exposed uncertainty rather than an assumed spiritual atmosphere.
