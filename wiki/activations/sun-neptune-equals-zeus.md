## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Unsuccessful undertakings. The vanquished. The loser. To transmit physical weakness by heredity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 155 / PDF page 76, Zeus glyph under Sonne + Neptun. English translation checked against the complete row.

Disappointing leadership of people, sensitive leaders, fiery body in the air, personal efforts in water, personal uncertainty in striving toward goals, personal striving toward the future, and a person's achievements in metaphysics.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Effort and direction exposed to uncertainty or unsuccessful undertakings.
- psychology: Insecurity while pursuing a goal; future-oriented striving.
- body/health: Witte's hereditary-weakness claim remains attributed, not a biological conclusion.
- social/relationship: ABC names disappointing leadership of people and sensitive leaders as distinct examples.
- events/manifestations: Unsuccessful undertakings alongside possible achievements in metaphysics.
- conflicts/notes: A fiery body is a concrete ABC phrase, not sufficient evidence for a general creative-inspiration theme.

## Contradictions

- Witte emphasizes failure, while ABC also names achievement in metaphysics; the conditions are not stated. ABC's leadership disappointment and sensitive leaders are distinct from its possible achievements; it gives no conditions that make one outcome necessary.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies the Sun/Neptune axis in undertaking and goal pursuit: effort may be uncertain, leadership disappointing, or the undertaking unsuccessful. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes defeat; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also allows future striving and achievement in metaphysics. That narrower possibility should stay visible without turning the whole formula into a promise of fiery creative inspiration.
