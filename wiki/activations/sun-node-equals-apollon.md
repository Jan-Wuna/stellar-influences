## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Close connected to successful people, dignitaries, scientists and commerce. Many possibilities to make personal connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 141, supplied PDF p. 69, Sonne + Node, Apollon row (six clauses). Translation from the German scan:

Expansion of personal relationships; experience in physical connections; successful personal connections; personal trade relationships; connections to a scientific center; many connections during the day.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Expansion and success through personal connections.
- psychology: Experience in making connections; no distinct inner motive is stated.
- body/health: ABC's bodily-connection phrase establishes no illness or health conclusion.
- social/relationship: Links to successful people, dignitaries, science, and commerce.
- events/manifestations: Many opportunities for contact, trade relationships, or a scientific center.
- conflicts/notes: The actual row names physical connections and connections to a scientific center; success is not guaranteed for every contact.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize a similar range without supplying a direct opposing claim. Their overlap alone does not prove independent corroboration. ABC explicitly names experience in physical connections and connections to a scientific center.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies the Sun/Node axis as widening personal connections that open opportunities in commerce, science, or influential circles. [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) names successful people, dignitaries, and many possibilities for contact; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds expanded relationships, trade links, and connection to a scientific center. Success is attached to this network of contacts, without establishing that every union will be helpful.
