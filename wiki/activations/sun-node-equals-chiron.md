### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 22, Chiron entry under Sun/Node.

People contacting your energy immediately have a sense about the purpose of the connections in their life, and an awareness of the direction in which they need to move. You instinctively help others to overcome their egoattachment to the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Contact that helps others recognize the purpose of their connections and their direction.
- Psychology: Overcoming ego-attachment to the past, in Sandbach's terms.
- Body/health: The entry gives no bodily condition or healing claim.
- Social/relationship: Instinctively helping others understand the connections in their lives.
- Events/manifestations: Encounters that clarify purpose and a possible direction forward.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Chiron`, Chiron specifies the Sun/Node pair through contact that clarifies the purpose and direction of other people's connections. [Sandbach's entry](#sandbach-entry) joins that awareness with help in overcoming attachment to the past: a relationship can become an occasion to recognize where to move next. The emphasis is on assisting another person's understanding and movement, within the author's claim about an instinctive interpersonal ability.
