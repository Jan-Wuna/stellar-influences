## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 102, Cupido entry under Sun + Nodes.

Social connection. A relationship that leads to marriage. Connected with art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 141 / PDF page 69, Cupido glyph row under Sun + Node. The last clause's duplicate preposition was corrected against the scan.

encounters in a men's community, personal connection to the community, persons in a contact community, day of shared connection, and connections in a community center

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal connections expressed in community and social bonds.
- Psychology: No distinct inner disposition is specified by the brief entries.
- Body/health: Neither entry gives a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Community encounters, social connection and a relationship leading to marriage.
- Events/manifestations: Connections in a community center, shared association and involvement with art.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's men's community is a specific example; marriage and art are Witte's additional forms, without a required outcome.

## Contradictions

ABC emphasizes communal settings, while Witte includes marriage and art. These are different forms of connection without a direct conflict in the entries.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the Sun/Node pair through personal ties that take a communal or social form. [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) repeatedly places encounters and connections within a community, including its shared center. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) extends that social connection to art and a relationship leading to marriage. The common meaning is participation in a bond or shared circle, with those particular settings and outcomes remaining examples rather than universal requirements.
