## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 102, Hades entry under Sun + Nodes.

Physically disgusting and repulsive connections. To be taken ill. Relationships with rabble. To be threatened with imprisonment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 141 / PDF page 69, Hades glyph row under Sun + Node. The damaged Y fragment was checked as Vergangenheit, the past.

Old personal connections, difficult body contact, criminal human ties, adverse corporal relationships, personal relationships with the past, hidden human ties, and renunciation of personal relationships

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Difficult, concealed or relinquished personal connections.
- Psychology: No detailed inner disposition is given; renunciation concerns withdrawing from personal relationships.
- Body/health: Difficult or adverse physical connections and Witte's illness claim remain attributed, not diagnostic.
- Social/relationship: Old ties, links with the past and hidden bonds; criminal ties are a separate ABC example.
- Events/manifestations: Renouncing relationships and difficult contact; Witte includes a threat of imprisonment.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's degrading class language and the illness/criminality examples are historical source claims, not judgments about a person or all past connections.

## Contradictions

Both entries include adverse contact, but ABC also names old or hidden bonds without explaining every circumstance. Witte's stronger aversion, illness and imprisonment examples do not make every old or concealed tie equivalent to those outcomes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Hades`, Hades specifies the Sun/Node pair through personal connections that may be difficult to maintain or approach, concealed, or relinquished. [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) places difficult bodily contact alongside hidden ties, links with the past and renunciation of relationships. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) gives more severe adverse examples. The shared theme is a troubled or restricted field of association; the historical illness, criminality, imprisonment and class judgments remain attributed source material, rather than a diagnosis or a judgment of someone's worth.
