## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 102, Jupiter entry under Sun + Nodes.

A fortunate physical union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 140 / PDF page 69, Jupiter glyph row under Sun + Node. English translation checked against the scan.

Successful personal connections, joy through bodily relationships, good fortune through human relationships, a happy relationship with men, joy in contact during the day, a personal legal connection, and connection to the financial center.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 105, entry 0295 under Sun/Dragon's Head.

Seeking public recognition. Generous, tactful, sociable, harmonious integration into the community. Successes in the community or in public. Good spiritual or mental contacts.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 22, Jupiter entry under Sun/Node.

Enthusiasm directed toward reaching goals mutually held with others. A desire to proliferate meaningful connections with other people and to use one’s connections to support the process of heightening awareness and reaching goals.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `92`

#### Munkasey Entry

Promises from influential people; the ambition you show when arranging alliances to important organizations; a genuine interest in people and their needs; publicity given to you involving important or newsworthy persons.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Favorable personal ties and cooperation toward shared goals.
- psychology: Enthusiasm, generosity, tact and a genuine interest in others, alongside ambition for recognition.
- body/health: Fortunate physical union and joy through bodily relationships; no specific health outcome is given.
- social/relationship: Sociability, community integration, meaningful connections and alliances with influential people or organizations.
- events/manifestations: Public recognition, promises, publicity and successful ties; ABC includes legal and financial connections.
- conflicts/notes: Public success and private enjoyment are distinct emphases; promises, recognition and success remain examples rather than guarantees.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in favorable connections. Ebertin emphasizes community and public recognition, Sandbach shared goals and awareness, and Munkasey influential alliances as well as interest in others; no direct disagreement is stated.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the Sun/Node pair through connections that bring enjoyment, cooperation and opportunities for shared success. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [ABC](#abc-entry) give favorable personal and physical ties, while [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) connects sociability and tact with integration into a community. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) makes enthusiasm for mutual goals central, and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) includes influential alliances, promises and publicity alongside genuine interest in others' needs. The common thread is a relationship or social circle supporting meaningful aims; its favorable examples do not guarantee recognition, financial benefit or fulfilled promises.
