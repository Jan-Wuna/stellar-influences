## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 102, Kronos entry under Sun + Nodes.

In touch with officials, superiors and capable people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 141 / PDF page 69, Kronos glyph row under Sun + Node. Selbständiger Personen is rendered broadly as independent persons, without requiring an occupation.

Special personal connection, relations with a state centre, connection of independent persons, connection with the centre of the hierarchy, priority personal connection, and important day of a connection

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal connections with authority, capability or a position of importance.
- Psychology: The entries give no explicit motive or attitude toward rank.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Officials, superiors, capable or independent people and state or hierarchical centers.
- Events/manifestations: An important or priority connection and contact with institutional authority.
- Conflicts/notes: Institutional standing and personal independence are different examples; neither guarantees promotion or establishes human superiority.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in contact with authority and people of standing. ABC also includes independent persons and a priority connection, without directly conflicting with Witte's short statement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the Sun/Node pair through connections marked by authority, capability or particular importance. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) names officials, superiors and capable people; [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) includes state and hierarchical centers, independent persons and a priority personal bond. These connections carry institutional or personal importance. The examples do not promise advancement or make institutional position a measure of personal worth.
