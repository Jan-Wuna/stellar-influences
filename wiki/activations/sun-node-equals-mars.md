## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 102, Mars entry under Sun + Nodes.

Physical sexual union. Contacts or ties through activity or work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 140 / PDF page 69, Mars glyph row under Sun + Node. English translation checked against the scan.

Actions for human connections, the man's work connections, work meetings during the day, arranging physical work, activating bodily relationships, establishing a connection between men, and creating personal connections.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 105, entry 0294 under Sun/Dragon's Head.

Desire to become a person of importance, for recognition and to establish oneself within a community. Associations based solely on physical attraction, cooperation and teamwork.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 22, Mars entry under Sun/Node.

Consciousness of others’ needs and purposes when one asserts oneself. One’s assertiveness comes off too others as egotistical. Taking action because one is aware of the importance of personal growth, but negatively one takes action with the motive of maintaining the status quo due to one’s ego attachment.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `92`

#### Munkasey Entry

An impatience to meet new friends and allies; the efforts you go thru to locate the kinds of people you need; expending energy to convey your importance to others; meeting effective competitors who can become allies.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Taking action to establish contacts and cooperate through work or shared activity.
- psychology: Initiative and impatience for allies; seeking importance, with assertion that can consider others or appear egotistical.
- body/health: Physical work, attraction and sexual union are examples; no health outcome is specified.
- social/relationship: Teamwork, work contacts, bodily relationships and competitors who can become allies.
- events/manifestations: Arranging work, seeking needed people and creating personal connections.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach distinguishes growth-oriented action from protecting the status quo through ego attachment; attraction, teamwork and recognition are different motives in the entries.

## Contradictions

Work and cooperation overlap across the entries. Sandbach adds awareness of others' purposes alongside an egotistical appearance or status-quo motive, while Munkasey permits a competitor to become an ally; these are different expressions of active association.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Mars`, Mars specifies the Sun/Node pair through action that makes a connection: working together, seeking allies or initiating bodily contact. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [ABC](#abc-entry) name ties through activity and work, while [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) joins teamwork with the wish to establish oneself in a community. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) gives the effort to find needed people, including competitors who become allies. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) supplies the important distinction in motive: assertion can recognize others' needs and personal growth, or serve an ego attachment to the status quo. The formula's active association therefore includes both cooperation and self-assertion, with sexual attraction as one source example.
