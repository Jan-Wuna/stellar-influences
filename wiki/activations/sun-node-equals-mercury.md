## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 102, Mercury entry under Sun + Nodes.

Thoughts and news concerning a personal union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 140 / PDF page 69, Mercury glyph row under Sun + Node. English translation checked against the scan.

Thoughts of bodily relationships, bodily contacts in youth, contacts through body language, connected to a media center, a news link with the man, passenger transport links, connection through conversations, and connection of young people.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 105, entry 0292 under Sun/Dragon's Head.

One's attitude to associations, desire to exchange ideas with other people. Business contacts, news, association with young people.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 21, Mercury entry under Sun/Node.

The need to communicate about purposes shared with other people. Being flexible and open to negotiation when it comes to goals held in common with others. Thinking that is not progressive because it places too much importance on the past, but conversely thinking that can be very progressive when it sees purpose as some thing which is growing and evolving.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `92`

#### Munkasey Entry

Generating ideas which influence others and simultaneously open previously closed doors for you; a gathering of rumors during meetings with friends or allies; groups which impose reporting obligations on you.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Ideas, news and conversation creating or shaping personal connections.
- psychology: Exchange and negotiation about common goals; thinking can remain attached to the past or allow purpose to evolve.
- body/health: Body language and bodily contact are named without a health outcome.
- social/relationship: Business contacts, young people, friends, allies and group reporting obligations.
- events/manifestations: News, influential ideas, rumors in meetings, media-center connections and passenger transport links.
- conflicts/notes: Rumor gathering and reporting are different forms of information exchange; Sandbach qualifies the interpretation through flexibility versus fixation on the past.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in communication and contacts. Sandbach distinguishes flexible negotiation from thinking fixed on the past; Munkasey adds rumors and reporting obligations to the otherwise broad exchange of ideas, without stating a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the Sun/Node pair through the exchange of ideas and information that creates or shapes a connection. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives thoughts and news about a personal union, [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) adds business contacts and exchange with others, and [ABC](#abc-entry) names conversation, body language and media links. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) includes ideas that open opportunities, as well as rumors and obligations to report within groups. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) makes the direction of that exchange important: common purposes can be negotiated flexibly and allowed to evolve, or held back by thinking that gives too much weight to the past.
