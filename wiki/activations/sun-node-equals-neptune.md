## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 102, Neptune entry under Sun + Nodes.

The cooling off of a relationship. Damage to the health through connections. To be susceptible to disease or poisonings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 141 / PDF page 69, Neptune glyph row under Sun + Node. English translation checked against the scan; feinstoffliche is retained in the source's subtle-body sense.

A disappointing connection with the man, sensitivity in a bodily connection, a subtle-body connection, contacts with unfamiliar persons, a personal connection to water, and an uncertain personal connection.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 105, entry 0298 under Sun/Dragon's Head.

Inability to adapt easily, tendency to be easily annoyed with others and quickly disappointed by others. Disappointment in associations.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 22, Neptune entry under Sun/Node.

To be unsure of one’s relationship to a group, or to other people in general. To be idealistic about the purpose and meaning of one’s connections with others. Clarity of intuition helps one to see the importance of encountering new experiences.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `92`

#### Munkasey Entry

An inability to decide which destiny in life to choose; a mystical leaning to measuring your progress in life; deception from allies you associate with; finding important secret societies; fantasies about public sexual activities.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertainty, idealism or disappointment within personal connections.
- psychology: Difficulty adapting or choosing direction, idealistic expectations and intuition in Sandbach's account.
- body/health: Witte's disease/poisoning claims and ABC's subtle-body language remain attributed doctrine, not diagnoses or mechanisms.
- social/relationship: Cooling bonds, disappointed association, uncertain group relationships and deception by allies.
- events/manifestations: Contacts with unfamiliar people or water; Munkasey includes secret societies and fantasies about public sexual activity.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also names sensitivity and Sandbach openness to new experience; the more adverse examples do not establish inevitable deception or illness.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize adverse connections and disappointment. ABC also names sensitivity, and Sandbach includes intuition and the value of new experience; these broaden the range without directly denying the difficult examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the Sun/Node pair through connections whose purpose or reliability can feel uncertain. [ABC](#abc-entry) joins sensitivity in contact with uncertain or disappointing bonds; [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes idealism about relationships alongside uncertainty about one's place with others. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) give cooling and disappointment as difficult expressions, while [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) includes indecision about direction and deception by allies. Sandbach also retains a role for intuition and new experience. The tension is between the meaning sought in a connection and what can be understood or relied upon; the health and subtle-body claims remain attributed, without establishing a diagnosis or required outcome.
