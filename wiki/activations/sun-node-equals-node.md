## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Node`, the repeated Node specifies the Sun/Node pair through reliance on friends and shared effort in pursuing goals. [Munkasey's entry](#munkasey-entry) presents cooperation as more effective than separate work, linking that realization with attention to one's personal direction.
