## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 102, Poseidon entry under Sun + Nodes.

Intellectual connections or unions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 141 / PDF page 69, Poseidon glyph row under Sun + Node. English translation checked against the scan; vernünftig means rational, while geistig retains an intellectual/spiritual range.

Relationships with rational people, intellectually rich personal connections, clear connection to the man, personal cultural connections, a day of insight in a relationship, connection to an insightful person, and connections to an intellectual/spiritual center.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Intellectual connection, clarity and insight in personal associations.
- Psychology: Understanding and insight within a bond; ABC names rational people as contacts.
- Body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Intellectually rich bonds, cultural connections and contact with insightful people.
- Events/manifestations: Connection to an intellectual/spiritual center and an experience of insight in a relationship.
- Conflicts/notes: The intellectual and spiritual range is retained without prescribing a belief system; the day example supplies no timing method.

## Contradictions

Witte's intellectual unions overlap with ABC's rational, insightful and cultural connections. ABC gives a broader intellectual/spiritual setting without a direct conflict.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the Sun/Node pair through connections shaped by understanding, ideas and insight. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) names intellectual unions; [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) gives rational or insightful people, intellectually rich personal bonds and cultural connections as concrete expressions. The shared quality is clarity and meaning in association, which can also take form around an intellectual or spiritual center. The entry leaves the particular beliefs and setting open.
