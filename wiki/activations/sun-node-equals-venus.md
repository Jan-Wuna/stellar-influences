## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 102, Venus entry under Sun + Nodes.

Physical love union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 140 / PDF page 69, Venus glyph row under Sun + Node. English translation checked against the scan.

Encounter with a handsome man, relations with artistic people, a harmonious human connection, a love connection to the man, personal love connections, a day of peaceful connection, and connection to an art center.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source locator: printed page 105 / PDF page 107, entry 0293 under Sun/Dragon's Head.

Interest in artistic performances. Desire for association with artists or people interested in art. Sociable. Beginning of a love relationship.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source locator: PDF page 22, Venus entry under Sun/Node.

An appreciation of the need to evolve one’s self-confidence and spiritualize one’s ego. A love of gaining recognition from other people. Complacency caused by connecting with other people who stroke one’s ego in a superficial way.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `92`

#### Munkasey Entry

Affectionate feelings toward those you have recently met; a love for meeting or associating with friends; the calming influences which friends bring; association with artistic individuals; meetings on issues of beautification.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affection, harmony and artistic interests expressed through personal connections.
- psychology: Enjoying recognition and developing self-confidence; superficial affirmation can foster complacency in Sandbach's account.
- body/health: Witte names physical love union without specifying a health outcome.
- social/relationship: Affectionate new contacts, calming friendships, artists and love relationships.
- events/manifestations: Beginning a love bond, attending to art or beautification and connecting with an art center.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's spiritualizing language remains attributed; recognition can support development or become superficial affirmation, without making romance inevitable.

## Contradictions

The affectionate and artistic emphasis overlaps across the entries. Sandbach adds a concern with self-confidence and the risk of complacency through superficial affirmation, qualifying the agreeable connections without denying them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Venus`, Venus specifies the Sun/Node pair through the enjoyment and affection found in a personal bond or shared artistic interest. [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) give love, harmony and association with artists as concrete forms. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds warmth toward new acquaintances, calming friendship and beautification. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) connects the wish for recognition with developing self-confidence, while warning that superficial affirmation can foster complacency. The range includes both affection that matters personally and pleasant recognition that may leave growth unchallenged, with a new love relationship remaining one possible example.
