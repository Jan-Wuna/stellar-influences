## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 102, Vernal Point entry under Sun + Nodes.

General unions or connections. Many connections in public. Meetings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 140 / PDF page 69, Vernal Point glyph row under Sun + Node.

human bonds in public life, relationships of persons in public life, personal connection to the public, day of the public connection, and connection to the public center

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Connections and meetings situated in public life.
- Psychology: No distinct inner disposition is specified by these brief entries.
- Body/health: Neither entry gives a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: General association, many public contacts and personal ties to the public.
- Events/manifestations: Meetings and connections with a public center.
- Conflicts/notes: The public setting is clear, while a specific institution or personal role is not; the day example supplies no timing method.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize public association. ABC gives a public center as one setting, without a direct conflict with Witte's general meetings and connections.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Sun/Node pair through association taking place in public life. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) gives meetings and many public connections; [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) repeatedly places human bonds and personal relationships in that setting, including a public center. The shared emphasis is participation in a wider field of contact. The brief entries leave the institution, personal role and inner experience unspecified.
