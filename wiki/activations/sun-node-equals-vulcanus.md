## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 102, Vulcanus entry under Sun + Nodes.

To have mighty and influential ties.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 141 / PDF page 69, Vulcanus glyph row under Sun + Node. English translation checked against the scan; Kraft is strength, distinct from the separate Gewalt clause.

Influential personal connection, conveying physical strength, physical violence in a connection, connected to a power center, personal transmission of energy, strong personal relationships, and connection to an energy center.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Powerful or influential ties and strong personal relationships.
- Psychology: No distinct inner disposition is specified by these brief entries.
- Body/health: ABC names physical strength and energy transmission as source language, without a medical claim or established mechanism.
- Social/relationship: Influential bonds and strong relationships; physical violence is a separate ABC example.
- Events/manifestations: Connection to power or energy centers and conveying strength.
- Conflicts/notes: Strength, influence and violence are distinct meanings; the violent example is neither an inevitable outcome nor a justification.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize strong, influential connections. ABC includes more specific strength, energy and violence examples without making them equivalent to every mighty tie in Witte.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Sun/Node pair through ties that carry strength or influence. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) names mighty and influential connections; [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) gives strong personal relationships, conveying physical strength and contact with power or energy centers. Its energy-transmission wording remains the source's description. ABC also includes physical violence in a connection, a distinct attributed example that cannot be inferred from a strong or influential bond alone.
