## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: PDF page 102, Zeus entry under Sun + Nodes.

Connections with creative or self-confident persons. Connected to the military. To come into physical contact with fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 141 / PDF page 69, Zeus glyph row under Sun + Node. English translation checked against the scan.

Beginning of a personal relationship, relationships with leading persons, shaping a human connection, creative personal connections, connected to a leadership center, and shaping personal connections.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Beginning and shaping personal connections, including creative or leadership contacts.
- Psychology: Creativity and self-confidence characterize contacts in Witte's entry; no broader inner profile is given.
- Body/health: Witte names physical contact with fire without specifying a health outcome.
- Social/relationship: New relationships and connections with creative, self-confident or leading people.
- Events/manifestations: Shaping bonds and connecting with a leadership center; military and fire contact are Witte's specific examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Creative and leadership ties differ from the military/fire examples; no particular occupation or hazardous event is required.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in creative connections, with ABC emphasizing active shaping and leadership. Witte's military and fire examples extend the field without defining every relationship in those terms.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Node = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the Sun/Node pair through the beginning and active shaping of a connection. [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) repeats creative relationship-building and includes leading people or a leadership center; [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) names creative and self-confident contacts. The common emphasis is a bond being initiated or given form. Witte also gives military affiliation and physical contact with fire as distinct source examples, without making either a required event or role.
