## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 114 / PDF page 110, Admetos entry under Sun + Pluto.

Deep reaching change in nutrition. Special food. Heaviness. Weight.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 157 / PDF page 77, Admetos glyph row under Sun + Pluto. English translation checked against the scan.

Blocked development of a man, a blocked development center, a person changed by depression, restricted bodily growth, thorough personal development, increasing personal oppression, and transformations through personal firmness.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Development that is blocked or restricted, alongside thorough change and personal firmness.
- Psychology: ABC names change through depression and firmness as different examples, without a general psychological profile.
- Body/health: Restricted growth, deep nutritional change, special food, heaviness and weight remain attributed source themes, not diagnoses or dietary advice.
- Social/relationship: ABC includes personal oppression without identifying its agent or a specific relationship.
- Events/manifestations: A blocked development center and substantial personal change.
- Conflicts/notes: Thorough development and firmness coexist with blockage and oppression; the bodily examples do not establish a medical outcome.

## Contradictions

ABC includes both blocked development and thorough development or transformation through firmness. Witte focuses on nutrition and weight; these different examples do not establish one uniform adverse outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Pluto = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the Sun/Pluto pair through development that can encounter limits or take a thorough, firm form. [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) places blocked or restricted growth beside thorough personal development and transformations through firmness, while also naming oppression and change through depression. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) gives a bodily register of deep nutritional change, special food, heaviness and weight. The range joins substantial change with constraint or consolidation; its depression, growth and nutrition claims remain attributed source material, without establishing a diagnosis, treatment or required outcome.
