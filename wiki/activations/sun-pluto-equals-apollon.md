## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source locator: printed page 114 / PDF page 110, Apollon entry under Sun + Pluto.

Growth in length. Unrestrained, prospering development. Glutton.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 157 / PDF page 77, Apollon glyph row under Sun + Pluto. English translation checked against the scan; Wissen vom Menschen concerns knowledge of the human being, and the centers are generic descriptions.

Scientific development center, successful personal development, developing personal freedom, personal transformation experiences, extensive personnel change, developing knowledge of the human being, and a center for economic development.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Broad or prospering development, extending into personal, scientific and economic fields.
- Psychology: Personal freedom and experiences of transformation; no detailed inner disposition is specified.
- Body/health: Witte names growth in length and a glutton label; these remain historical bodily examples, not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Extensive personnel change and collective development settings.
- Events/manifestations: Scientific or economic development centers, successful personal development and knowledge about the human being.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's excess example qualifies its prospering development; no particular institution, financial gain or bodily outcome is guaranteed.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize development. Witte uses bodily growth and excess, while ABC extends the field to personal freedom, personnel, science and economic development; these are different domains rather than a direct conflict.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Pluto = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the Sun/Pluto pair through development that gains breadth or room to unfold. [ABC's entry](#abc-entry) gives personal freedom, transformation experiences and successful development, with extensive personnel changes, scientific and economic centers, and knowledge about the human being as further examples. [Witte's entry](#witte-entry) uses physical growth and prospering development, with its glutton label marking a possible excess. The shared theme is an expanding developmental field, whose personal and collective forms remain distinct. These examples establish neither a particular institutional role nor a guaranteed financial or bodily outcome.
