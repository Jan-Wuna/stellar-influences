- Source heading: `SUN/PLUTO with Planets and Points`

#### Munkasey Entry

Concentrating on making changes thru applying your will; noticing the weakness of others and using this knowledge for personal advantage; efforts to rid your environment of corruption, or degrading or immoral practices.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal and environmental change interacts with forceful self-presentation and the will to reshape surroundings.
- psychology: Compelling purpose and the appearance of power can become overbearing, exaggerated or exploitative.
- body/health: Witte names environmental influence on physical development; no individual bodily outcome follows.
- social/relationship: Partner development and environmental influence coexist with suppressing others, experiencing ruthlessness or removing corruption.
- events/manifestations: Local personnel change, self-preservation crises and attempts to change one's surroundings are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Influence from the environment and force exerted upon it are distinct directions; appearing powerful is not proof of actual power.

## Contradictions

The accounts differ in emphasis rather than making exclusive claims: physical and environmental development is broader than ruthless assertion, while efforts to remove corruption remain distinct from exploiting weakness.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Pluto = Asc`, personal change is experienced through one's surroundings and the force one brings to encounters. Environmental habits and partnership can shape development, while an intense, compelling presence attracts attention and may seem more powerful than it is. Applying will to change conditions can involve confronting corruption, exploiting weakness or suppressing others; experiencing another person's ruthlessness preserves the opposite direction of that struggle. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey, p. 88](#munkasey-entry).)
