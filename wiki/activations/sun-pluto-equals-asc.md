## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The environmental influence on physical development. The lifestyle of the environment and its influences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

personal change on the spot, personal development with the partner, personal development of the partner, environmental developmental center, change of the personal environment, growing personal environment, and man capable of transformation as partner

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Ruthless establishment of one's own personality, suppression of the environment for the attainment of one's own aspirations. Experiencing the ruthlessness of others, the crisis of self-preservation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One comes across to others as a powerful person, or, negatively, overbear ing. Finding it easy to get the attention of other people. One who appears more power ful than they really are. The projection of an intense and compelling purposefulness.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `88`

#### Munkasey Entry

Concentrating on making changes thru applying your will; noticing the weakness of others and using this knowledge for personal advantage; efforts to rid your environment of corruption, or degrading or immoral practices.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Pluto = Asc`: force of character, intense self-projection, and the ability to shape circumstances through will and presence alone. Main caution: overbearing self-preservation: the native may suppress others, attract ruthless environments, or meet crisis with too much control and too little adaptability.
