## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Sun/Pluto midpoint`; one-based PDF pages `20–21` (exact Chiron entry checked in actual raw PDF text).

Exposure to your energy helps others to recognize their own power, as well as to realize the deeper meanings at work in life. A person who has the ability to heal the egos of others by intensifying their self-esteem issues until a transformation is created.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Another person's power and self-esteem become the focus of transformative influence.
- psychology: Recognizing one's power and deeper meaning is linked with intensifying self-esteem issues.
- body/health: Sandbach's ego-healing language supplies no physical mechanism or medical treatment.
- social/relationship: The entry concerns effects on others' egos and awareness of their own power.
- events/manifestations: Intensified self-esteem issues leading to transformation are the stated process.
- conflicts/notes: The claim is Sandbach's single spiritual interpretation; intensification is not a prescribed technique or guaranteed benefit.

## Contradictions

One source is represented. Its recognition-of-power and intensified-self-esteem themes belong to the same short passage and provide no inter-source comparison.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's `Sun/Pluto = Chiron`, transformative influence is directed toward other people's sense of power and self-worth. Recognizing their own power and life's deeper meanings is linked with self-esteem issues becoming intense enough to produce change. His language of healing the ego preserves that spiritual account without supplying a clinical method or guaranteeing that intensification benefits every person. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).)
