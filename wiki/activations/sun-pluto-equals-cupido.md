## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development occurs through communal life, shared bodily growth and collective nourishment.
- psychology: No separate psychological trait is supplied by these entries.
- body/health: Shared physical development and meals are named; these historical correspondences establish no individual health outcome.
- social/relationship: Communities of persons or men and public meal institutions provide collective settings.
- events/manifestations: Restaurants, canteens, people's kitchens and centers for bodily development are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: The entries center on communal development and nourishment; separation is not stated.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Communal meals and development of a community are different but compatible settings; neither supplies separation as a necessary theme.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Pluto = Cupido`, development takes communal forms: shared bodily growth and personal unfolding within a group accompany changes or growth in a community. Meals in restaurants, canteens, public institutions and people's kitchens give nourishment a collective setting, while a center for bodily development makes shared growth concrete. The source entries support development and sustenance within shared life. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
