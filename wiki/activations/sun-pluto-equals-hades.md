#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Pluto`; printed p. `157`; one-based PDF page `77` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Dangerous personal development, criminal development of a person, deficient bodily development, adverse personnel change, personal deprivations during the day, personal development service, and hidden changes in the body

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal or physical development takes deficient, deprived, dangerous or concealed forms.
- psychology: The source names criminal development as a severe example, without defining a universal disposition.
- body/health: Insufficient physical development, poor nutrition and hunger are attributed historical examples.
- social/relationship: Adverse personnel change and personal development service give social or institutional settings.
- events/manifestations: Inferior or wasted food, daily deprivation and hidden bodily changes are specific examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's mangelhafte means deficient, not man-made; severe criminal and medical examples remain source-attributed.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Hunger, inferior food, service and criminal development retain different settings rather than becoming one inevitable process of decay.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Pluto = Hades`, development is affected by deficiency or deprivation: insufficient bodily growth, inferior nutrition, hunger and wasted food give that difficulty concrete physical expressions. Adverse personnel change, daily deprivation and service connected with development extend the range into personal circumstances. Dangerous or criminal development and hidden bodily changes remain separate severe source examples, without making every change destructive or establishing a diagnosis. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
