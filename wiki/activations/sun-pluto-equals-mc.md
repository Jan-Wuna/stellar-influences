## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Pluto`; one-based PDF page `110` (oriented row checked against the scan).

One’s personal attitude toward nourishment and development of the body. The personal taste.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Pluto`; printed p. `156`; one-based PDF page `77` (oriented row checked against the scan).

My transformable man, my development as a human being, my bodily development, my personal evolution, my personal change, my changing men, personal development of the soul, transformation of a human being's soul.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: `Sun/Pluto`; printed p. `103`; entry code `0289` (supplied source-map locator; not visually verified).

The striving for power, consciousness of purpose or objective, the qualities of leadership. Vocational crises, the experience of physical interference or violence with psychical or emotional consequences.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Sun/Pluto midpoint`; one-based PDF page `21` (exact Midheaven entry checked in actual raw PDF text).

Power struggles in one’s career, or with one’s employer. An intense purposefulness when it comes to one’s life’s work. To have the reputation of being a powerful person. In one’s career work one may change the focus of what is usually considered important, and this may sometimes happen in a radical manner.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `88`

#### Munkasey Entry

Working to increase your influence in your community or or among your peers; pouring an excessive amount of your personal time or resources into achieving recognition and respectability; working thru past mistakes.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal development and concentrated purpose concern life work, reputation and one's own bodily attitudes.
- psychology: Leadership aims, personal evolution and the pursuit of recognition may demand excessive resources.
- body/health: Attitudes toward nourishment are explicit; Ebertin's physical violence with emotional consequences remains historical source material.
- social/relationship: Career or employer power struggles coexist with increasing influence among peers and working through mistakes.
- events/manifestations: Vocational crises, changed priorities in life work and personal bodily development are named.
- conflicts/notes: Personal development of the soul and my changing men are distinct ABC clauses; neither turns every career into tragedy.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Nourishment, first-person development and vocational power have different emphases; painful interference is a specific example, not the definition of purpose.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Pluto = MC`, development becomes one's own concern, from nourishment and bodily growth to personal evolution and spiritual change. Intense purpose can shape life work, a reputation for power and efforts to gain influence or recognition; it may also change what a profession treats as important. Employer conflicts, vocational crises and excessive investment of time or resources preserve difficult expressions alongside working through past mistakes. Ebertin's violence example remains a particular attributed possibility. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey, p. 88](#munkasey-entry).)
