### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Pluto`; one-based PDF page `110` (oriented row checked against the scan).

The glandular action. The circulation. The influence of the daily habits on the development of the body. The physical development of females.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Pluto`; printed p. `156`; one-based PDF page `77` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Evolution of man and woman, development of man by woman, development center of a people, corporal transformation of woman, personal development of woman, transformation of woman by man, and female personnel change

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: `Sun/Pluto`; printed p. `102`; one-based PDF page `104`; entry code `0279` (exact coded entry checked in actual PDF text).

Brutal suppression of feelings, irritability (illness of the female organism).

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Sun/Pluto midpoint`; one-based PDF page `20` (exact Moon entry checked in actual raw PDF text).

The emotional need to attain to ever deeper understanding of the meaning of life. Being vulnerable to the criticism and negative reactions of other people when one manifests one’s personal power. A tendency to not clearly see the power one has. Being impressionable to anything which carries a deep meaning. The emotional need to wield power.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `88`

#### Munkasey Entry

Determination to effect basic changes to the flow of life’s events; your efforts to bring help to those in need; fluctuations in your attempts to appear strong willed; basic ideas about your involvement with death or destruction.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Physical and personal development intersects with emotional power, vulnerability and meaning.
- psychology: A need for deeper understanding or influence coexists with criticism sensitivity, unclear awareness of power and suppressed feeling.
- body/health: Glandular action, circulation, habit-shaped development and female illness are attributed historical correspondences.
- social/relationship: Development between man and woman and help offered to people in need are specific social examples.
- events/manifestations: Changing life's events, female personnel changes and a people's development center are named.
- conflicts/notes: Suppression and irritability do not exhaust the entry; care and vulnerable searching remain present, with gendered examples attributed.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Emotional vulnerability, determination and repression describe different responses; physical and collective development retain their own source settings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Pluto = Moon`, development is both personal and emotionally consequential. The desire to understand deeper meaning or wield power can meet criticism sensitivity or difficulty recognizing one's own influence; suppression and irritability preserve a harsher response. Determination to change events and efforts to help people in need retain an active caring expression. Physical development, daily habits and development between men and women remain separate historical source themes rather than diagnoses or fixed gender roles. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey, p. 88](#munkasey-entry).)
