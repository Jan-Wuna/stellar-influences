## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Pluto`; one-based PDF page `110` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Initiation of physical connections. The physical change though connections.

Editorial note: "Though" is the printed wording. Reading it grammatically as "through connections" is an editorial normalization used in the paraphrase below.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Pluto`; printed p. `156`; one-based PDF page `77` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Development of a personal connection, personal development in a connection, communication of staff changes, growing personal contacts, changes in the corporal relationships, and change in a personal connection

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: `Sun/Pluto`; printed p. `103`; one-based PDF page `105`; entry code `0287` (exact coded entry checked in actual PDF text).

A lack of adaptability, fateful associations, separations.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Sun/Pluto midpoint`; one-based PDF page `21` (exact Node entry checked in actual raw PDF text).

The people in one’s life either encourage or try to undermine one’s personal power. The need to grow into a greater and more extensive use of one’s personal power. Ego defensiveness with this combination results in extremely retarded growth which can lead to drastic situations which are trying to help one to move forward.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `88`

#### Munkasey Entry

Attracting others into your life who can allow you to develop a stronger will and purpose; directing your dedication of purpose to that which companions feel is important; friends who help you channel your inner potential.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development takes place through connections that support, challenge or alter personal power.
- psychology: Stronger will and purpose can grow through companions, while ego defensiveness can obstruct growth.
- body/health: Witte names physical changes through connections without a specific illness.
- social/relationship: Friends can help channel potential or undermine power; lack of adaptability and separation remain difficult possibilities.
- events/manifestations: Initiating physical connections, growing contacts and personnel-change communication are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Fateful associations are Ebertin's term; the entry does not establish a separate karmic mechanism or inevitable crisis.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Supportive companionship and undermining relationships are different possibilities; physical connection and personal-power growth are distinct emphases.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Pluto = Node`, connections become a setting for development: companions can strengthen purpose, help channel potential or challenge one's power. Personal contacts and relationships themselves can grow or change, with physical change through connections retained in Witte. Defensiveness, poor adaptability and separations describe difficult possibilities alongside support, without turning every consequential alliance into a crisis. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey, p. 88](#munkasey-entry).)
