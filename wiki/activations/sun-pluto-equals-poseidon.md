## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal development takes mental, spiritual, cultural and refined bodily forms.
- psychology: Growing knowledge and personal clarity are explicit.
- body/health: Graceful body and spiritualization of the body are historical source descriptions, not medical mechanisms.
- social/relationship: Reasonable personnel change supplies one social example.
- events/manifestations: A spiritual development center, cultural unfolding and growth of mind and body are named.
- conflicts/notes: The entries support refinement and clarity without requiring a revelation or certifying a truth claim.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Bodily grace, cultural development and mental or spiritual growth are different examples of refinement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Pluto = Poseidon`, development is refined through mind, spirit and culture: growing knowledge and personal clarity accompany cultural unfolding and a spiritual development center. Delicate development and a graceful body retain the bodily expression, while reasonable personnel change provides a concrete social one. These entries connect refinement with growth without requiring every transformation to be revelatory. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
