## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Harmonious development. Development of taste. Harmonious physical changes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development center of the artist, peaceful change of personnel, harmonious change of the year, harmonious carperial development, corporal love unfold, developing the beauty of the carper, growing peace center, and preference for personal changes

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Creative force or powers, understanding of the platic arts. Emotional upsets, a tragic love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Battles of will in a love relationship — the two people may be at odds about what each one finds important in life. Being attracted to people with extreme self-con fidence, or who have a lack of confidence which they compensate for by attempting to dominate one. Intense willfulness when it comes to the fulfilling of one’s desires and getting what one wants.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Recognition of how to present beauty or adornment in intentional or basic ways; determination to show depths of feelings of love and affection toward others; the extremes you go thru to attract harmony or artistry to your life.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Pluto = Venus`: creative force, intense attraction, and the capacity to transform taste, affection, or artistic expression through depth and struggle. Main caution: power in love: desire can turn possessive, tragic, or locked into battles of will.
