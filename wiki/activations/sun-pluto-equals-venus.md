## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Pluto`; one-based PDF page `110` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Harmonious development. Development of taste. Harmonious physical changes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Pluto`; printed p. `156`; one-based PDF page `77` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Development center for art, peaceful personnel change, harmonious turn of the year, harmonious bodily development, unfolding physical love, developing bodily beauty, growing peace center, preference for personal changes.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: `Sun/Pluto`; printed p. `102`; one-based PDF page `104`; entry code `0281` (checked in actual text and scan; printed platic spelling retained).

Creative force or powers, understanding of the platic arts. Emotional upsets, a tragic love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Sun/Pluto midpoint`; one-based PDF page `20` (exact Venus entry checked in actual raw PDF text).

Battles of will in a love relationship — the two people may be at odds about what each one finds important in life. Being attracted to people with extreme self-con fidence, or who have a lack of confidence which they compensate for by attempting to dominate one. Intense willfulness when it comes to the fulfilling of one’s desires and getting what one wants.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `88`

#### Munkasey Entry

Recognition of how to present beauty or adornment in intentional or basic ways; determination to show depths of feelings of love and affection toward others; the extremes you go thru to attract harmony or artistry to your life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development concerns harmony, taste, beauty and intense creative or affectionate expression.
- psychology: Willfulness in desire and confidence can coexist with deep affection or attempts to dominate.
- body/health: Harmonious physical change, bodily beauty and physical love are named without medical conclusions.
- social/relationship: Love relationships may involve shared affection or battles over what matters in life.
- events/manifestations: Art-development centers, peaceful personnel change and emotional upsets or tragic love are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Harmonious growth and relationship conflict are distinct possibilities; Ebertin's printed platic wording is preserved.

## Contradictions

The entries include both harmonious development and emotional conflict, without requiring harmony to arise through struggle or making tragedy inevitable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Pluto = Venus`, development is directed toward harmony, taste and beauty, with physical love and bodily change among its concrete forms. Creative power and the determination to express deep affection can bring artistry into life, while intense desire may turn differences over priorities into a battle of wills. Ebertin's emotional upset and tragic-love examples preserve the difficult range beside harmonious development. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey, p. 88](#munkasey-entry).)
