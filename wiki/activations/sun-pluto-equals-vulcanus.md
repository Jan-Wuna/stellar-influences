#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Pluto`; printed p. `157`; one-based PDF page `77` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Influential personal change, powerful personal development, unfolding personal power, personal change of power, a man developing strength, growing bodily strength, growing physical violence, center for energy development.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development gains physical strength, power and influential personal change.
- psychology: No separate psychological disposition is supplied by these brief entries.
- body/health: Great bodily strength and increased food absorption are historical correspondences.
- social/relationship: Personal shifts of power and an influential changing person give social settings.
- events/manifestations: Energy-development centers, growing bodily force and physical violence are distinct examples.
- conflicts/notes: The restored violence clause remains separate from strength; forceful growth need not be described as devolution.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Physical strength, power change and bodily violence are different examples, without equating vigor with violence.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Pluto = Vulcanus`, development becomes powerful and physically strong: influential personal change and shifts of power accompany a man developing strength and growing bodily force. Witte links great strength with increased absorption of food, while an energy-development center gives development an institutional setting. ABC also names growing physical violence, which remains distinct from strength or nourishment. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
