## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development begins or advances through personal goals, leadership and bodily demands.
- psychology: Personal striving for goals is explicit.
- body/health: Puberty, reproductive development and greater food absorption under bodily demands are historical examples.
- social/relationship: Changes in personal leadership or the direction of people give social settings.
- events/manifestations: Growing bodily achievements and changes through purposeful striving are named.
- conflicts/notes: The scan concerns leadership changes, not filtration or transportation; reproductive examples do not establish fertility outcomes.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Bodily growth, leadership and personal goals retain their separate scopes.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Pluto = Zeus`, development gains a beginning and an aim: personal goals and purposeful striving accompany growing bodily achievements and changes in leadership. Increased food absorption in response to greater bodily demands, puberty and physical reproductive development preserve specific bodily examples. These developmental and directive forms give the orientation concrete expression without turning every change into a forceful struggle. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
