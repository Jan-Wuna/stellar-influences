## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Poseidon`; one-based PDF page `118` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Spiritual matter. Foundation or origin of spiritual ideas. To penetrate deeply into spiritual things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Poseidon`; printed p. `173`; one-based PDF page `85` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Blocked human culture, depression of a cultivated person, a person suppressing truth, restriction of personal culture, mentally blocked person, personal spiritual deepening, personal consolidation through truth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Spiritual inquiry deepens toward foundations while personal culture may be blocked or restricted.
- psychology: Penetrating spiritual matters coexists with mental blockage, depression and suppressing truth.
- body/health: ABC's depression is a historical psychological association; no clinical result is established.
- social/relationship: A person who suppresses truth is a particular source example, not a judgment on every reserved thinker.
- events/manifestations: Personal spiritual deepening and consolidation through truth are named.
- conflicts/notes: The actual row says truth-suppressing person, not devout person; depth and obstruction are different possibilities.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Foundational spiritual inquiry and restricted or blocked culture describe different expressions, without guaranteeing insight from difficulty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Poseidon = Admetos`, inquiry goes deeply into the foundations or origin of spiritual ideas. Personal spiritual deepening and consolidation through truth give that concentration a constructive form, while blocked culture, mental restriction and depression preserve difficult expressions. The source also names a person who suppresses truth; that particular example must remain distinct from devotion or depth of thought. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
