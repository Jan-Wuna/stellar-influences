Spiritual matter. Foundation or origin of spiritual ideas. To penetrate deeply into spiritual things.

Blocked human culture, depressions of a cultured person, the devout person, restriction of the person culture, mentally blocked person, personal spiritual deepening, and personal strengthening by truth
