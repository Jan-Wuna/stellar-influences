## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Spiritual or cultural understanding is shared through marriage and community.
- psychology: Personal clarity within a community and reasonable shared life are explicit.
- body/health: Shared physical culture is named without a medical claim.
- social/relationship: Ideological association, spiritually oriented matrimony and a community of persons provide social forms.
- events/manifestations: Cultural centers and participation in a spiritual or cultural community are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Ideological connection does not guarantee agreement, virtue or permanent harmony.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Marriage and cultural or spiritual fellowship are different forms of shared orientation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Poseidon = Cupido`, cultivation becomes shared life: spiritually oriented matrimony and ideological connection with a community join personal clarity to fellowship. A community can share physical culture, spiritual interests and cultural institutions, giving insight a collective setting. The entries describe what is shared without making the group's ideas automatically true or its relationships permanently harmonious. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
