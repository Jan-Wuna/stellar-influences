## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Poseidon`; one-based PDF page `118` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Dirty mentality, mental dirt. Obsolete philosophy of life. Disgusted by a philosophy of life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Poseidon`; printed p. `173`; one-based PDF page `85` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Dangerous ideas of a person, secret of a spiritualized person, mental criminality of a person, mental deficiency of a person, mental centre for criminality, criminal spirit of a community, and truth about a criminal person

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: An intellectual or spiritual outlook becomes suspect, concealed or repellent: an obsolete philosophy and disgust meet dangerous ideas and criminal misuse of thought. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: Disgust with a philosophy is distinct from the person who holds dangerous ideas; ABC also describes a spiritualized person's secret and a lack in the person's mental or spiritual life. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: The entries concern mentality and worldview; neither gives a specific bodily condition. ABC's mental deficiency is retained as historical wording rather than a clinical diagnosis. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: ABC extends the concern from one person's ideas to the criminal spirit of a community and a center associated with criminality. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: Possible source examples include encountering an outdated worldview, becoming disgusted with it, uncovering a person's secret, or learning the truth about a criminal person. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: Witte's moralized language and ABC's criminal examples remain attributed claims. They do not establish that a holder of this formula commits crime, nor do the entries equate spiritual suffering with criminality. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in a troubled or degraded mentality but differ in emphasis: Witte describes an obsolete worldview and disgust, while ABC adds secrecy and explicit criminal examples. No direct contradiction is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Hades` brings the person's ideas and spiritual outlook into contact with what is concealed, degrading or repellent. The difficulty can lie in an obsolete philosophy that provokes disgust, or in ideas that are dangerous and used for criminal ends. ABC carries that second possibility beyond an individual to a community or center, while also allowing the disclosure of truth about a criminal person. These are the sources' severe examples, not a warrant to infer criminal conduct from the formula. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
