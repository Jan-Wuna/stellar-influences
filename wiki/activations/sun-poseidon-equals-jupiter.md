## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Poseidon`; one-based PDF page `118` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Spiritualization of material things. Finding through wisdom the right harmony between matter and mind, between heavenly and earthly things. To have a remarkable sense of and for justice.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Poseidon`; printed p. `172`; one-based PDF page `85` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Success through personal reason, successful body culture, insights of a financial center, a human being's spiritual good fortune, clear finances of a man, clear human rights, cultural success of a person, personal legal insights.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Wisdom connects material life with mental or spiritual understanding; ABC gives success through reason and clarity in financial, legal and cultural matters. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: The psychological emphasis is sound understanding: finding a fitting harmony between matter and mind and bringing personal reason to practical concerns. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: ABC includes successful body culture. Witte's harmony between earthly and spiritual things is broader than a specific claim about health or cure. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: A strong sense of justice, clear human rights and personal legal insights connect the outlook to fair dealing and shared legal concerns. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: Source examples include cultural success, clear finances, insights within a financial center and success in cultivating the body. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: ABC's spiritual good fortune and practical success are examples, not a guarantee of wealth; Witte centers the quality of harmony and justice rather than a particular financial result. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte gives a broad harmony of material and spiritual life; ABC supplies financial, legal, bodily and cultural examples of personal understanding.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Jupiter` describes understanding that helps a person bring material and spiritual life into a workable harmony. Reason and a strong sense of justice give that outlook a practical direction: clearer finances, insight into rights or law, and cultural achievement are ABC's examples. The body also belongs to this pattern through successful body culture. The shared emphasis is on wisdom informing life, with spiritual good fortune and success remaining possibilities rather than assured outcomes. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
