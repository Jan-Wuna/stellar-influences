## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Poseidon`; one-based PDF page `118` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Mental, spiritual leader. Highest spiritualization of the body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Poseidon`; printed p. `173`; one-based PDF page `85` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Standing of an insightful man, dominance of truth in the human being, spiritually superior person, spiritual hierarchy of the human being, great human spirit, person with great clarity, personal culture in the state, personal insights about the state.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal clarity and spiritual cultivation assume an elevated or leading position, with ABC extending the theme to hierarchy and the state. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: ABC emphasizes the prominence of truth, great clarity and a highly developed spirit; these describe the source's ideal of intellectual or spiritual stature. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: Witte calls this the highest spiritualization of the body. That is spiritual language and gives no specific medical condition or measurable bodily superiority. [Witte](#witte-entry)
- social/relationship: A mental or spiritual leader, the standing of an insightful man and culture within the state place personal understanding in a recognized public or hierarchical setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: Possible expressions include spiritual leadership, recognition for insight, and personal understanding of the state. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: The sources' terms superior and highest express their hierarchy of spiritual attainment; the formula does not independently certify a person's truthfulness or right to govern. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte concentrates on spiritual leadership and the body; ABC adds stature, hierarchy and state-related culture or insight.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Kronos` gives personal insight a position of stature or leadership. The person may be recognized for clarity, embody a spiritual ideal, or direct understanding toward the state and its cultural life. Witte's spiritual leader and ABC's hierarchy belong to the same emphasis on elevated understanding, while the latter's language of truth and superiority remains a source ideal rather than proof that authority is justified. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
