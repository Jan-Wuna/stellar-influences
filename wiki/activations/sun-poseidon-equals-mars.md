## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Poseidon`; one-based PDF page `118` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Ethics and moral code of a person. The moral code of action. Proper conduct.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Poseidon`; printed p. `172`; one-based PDF page `85` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Actions of cultured persons, active mind in the body, work in a culture centre, activate a person's ideas, work with people with insight, personal culture work, personal will for truth, and a man's reasonable actions

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The person's spiritual or cultural understanding becomes a standard for action: proper conduct, work with insight and a will directed toward truth. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: The entries emphasize deliberate, ethically directed action rather than mere enthusiasm; ABC includes activating ideas and acting reasonably. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: ABC's active mind in the body links thought with embodied activity, but neither entry specifies illness or treatment. [ABC](#abc-entry) · [Witte](#witte-entry)
- social/relationship: Working with insightful people and the actions of cultivated persons place individual conduct within a cultural or cooperative setting. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: Work in a cultural center, cultural work, putting a person's ideas into action and pursuing truth are explicit examples. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: Witte's proper conduct is an ethical formulation, not evidence that every action under the formula is good; ABC emphasizes reasonable action and a will directed toward truth. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte foregrounds the moral code of action, while ABC describes cultural work and the practical activation of ideas.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Mars` turns insight into conduct. Ideas seek expression through work, cultural activity and cooperation with insightful people, while the person's will is directed toward truth. Witte gives this an explicit ethical frame: the issue is the moral code by which action proceeds. ABC's reasonable actions reinforce that connection between understanding and what a person does. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
