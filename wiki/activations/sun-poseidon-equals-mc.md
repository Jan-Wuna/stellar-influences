## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Poseidon`; one-based PDF page `118` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Mental and spiritual inclinations. To have a delicate body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Poseidon`; printed p. `172`; one-based PDF page `85` (oriented row checked against the scan).

My spiritual center (spirit + soul + body as a unity; personality point according to Carl Perch), my human insight, my human culture, my personal insights, my personal ideas, a body ensouled by spirit.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Spiritual or mental inclination is experienced personally, through one's own insight, ideas and cultural formation. ABC frames the spiritual center as a unity of spirit, soul and body. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: The first-person wording concerns personal understanding and inclinations rather than an externally assigned mission or public career. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: Witte explicitly includes a delicate body; ABC describes a body ensouled by spirit and preserves Carl Perch's personality-point formulation. Neither supplies a specific diagnosis. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: ABC's human culture is a personal orientation toward cultivation; the entries do not name a particular partner, family role or public office. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: Personal ideas, insights and cultural development are the stated expressions of this inwardly owned spiritual center. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: The unity of spirit, soul and body is attributed to ABC's Carl Perch parenthesis. It should remain distinct from Witte's brief statement about inclinations and bodily delicacy. [ABC](#abc-entry) · [Witte](#witte-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte names mental or spiritual inclinations and a delicate body; ABC offers a first-person formulation of personal insight and embodied spiritual unity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = MC` makes the spiritual outlook personal: these are my ideas, my insights and the culture I make part of myself. ABC gathers those concerns around a spiritual center understood, in its Carl Perch parenthesis, as spirit, soul and body in unity. Witte adds mental or spiritual inclination and a delicate body. Together the entries concern the person's inward relation to understanding and embodiment; they do not specify a public vocation. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
