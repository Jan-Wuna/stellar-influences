### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Poseidon`; one-based PDF page `118` (oriented row checked against the scan).

The mental agility. To perceive new ideas.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Poseidon`; printed p. `172`; one-based PDF page `85` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Report about a man's idea, insightful young person, a man's words rich in insight, conversations about personal insights, ideas of a young man, opinions about body culture, a man's cultural news, personal culture in the media.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental agility opens the person to new ideas and gives insight a communicable form. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: Perceiving ideas, forming personal insights and speaking with understanding are the central mental activities. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: Body culture appears as a subject of opinion or conversation, rather than a specified physical condition. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: Conversations, reports and media connect personal understanding with other people; ABC explicitly includes a young person and a young man. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: Reporting a man's idea, discussing insights, sharing cultural news and expressing culture through media are source examples. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: The entries describe insight and agility; neither guarantees that a person's communications are truthful or infallible. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte supplies the capacity to perceive ideas; ABC follows those ideas into speech, reporting and media.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Mercury` links quick understanding with the expression of ideas. What the person perceives can become words, conversations, a report or cultural news, with ABC giving particular examples involving young people and the media. Body culture is one possible topic of that exchange. The emphasis is the movement from insight to communication, rather than a guarantee that every statement is true. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
