## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Spiritual understanding is expressed through women, relations between man and woman, and the cultural life of a people. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: A sense for spiritual things and a woman's personal reason connect spiritual receptivity with insight and clarity. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: ABC specifically names a woman's body culture; Witte's spiritualized women is a spiritual characterization rather than a medical finding. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: Clarity between man and woman sits beside a broader collective setting: insightful people and the culture of a nation. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: A cultural center for the people, cultivated collective life and personal understanding in a woman are explicit examples. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: The historical gendered and national formulations are retained as source examples; they do not establish that insight belongs only to women or to a particular people. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte emphasizes women and spiritual interests; ABC adds male-female clarity and the culture of a people.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Moon` places insight within the life of women and the culture of a people. The personal expression is a sense for spiritual things, reason and cultivated bodily life; the relational expression is clarity between man and woman. ABC broadens this to insightful people and a cultural center serving the public. The entries connect personal cultivation with an explicitly collective cultural setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
