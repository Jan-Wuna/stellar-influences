sensitivity of an insightful person, person of a foreign culture, personal insights into subtle substance, spiritual intuition of a man, spiritualization of a person through metaphysics, and negation of the culture of persons

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The spiritual outlook can become confused and helpless, while ABC also allows sensitivity, intuition and metaphysical inquiry. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: Witte describes disconcerted or chaotic mentality; ABC's insightful sensitivity and spiritual intuition offer a different psychological possibility. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: ABC's subtle substance and metaphysics are source concepts; neither entry specifies a bodily illness or establishes a medical effect. [ABC](#abc-entry) · [Witte](#witte-entry)
- social/relationship: ABC includes a person of a foreign culture and negation of personal culture. These are distinct examples and are not equated with mental confusion. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: Possible expressions range from metaphysical study or intuition to disorientation and a denial of personal culture. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: The favorable intuitive examples and Witte's severe confusion must remain side by side; the entries give no rule for selecting one result or turning them into a fixed sequence. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

There is a material tension: Witte gives only confusion, chaos and mental helplessness, while ABC also includes insightful sensitivity, spiritual intuition and metaphysical spiritualization. ABC's foreign-culture example does not resolve or explain that tension.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Neptune` presents a tension in the person's relation to spiritual understanding. Sensitivity, intuition and metaphysical ideas may be prominent, yet Witte describes a mentality that is confused, helpless or disconcerted. ABC retains both spiritualization and a negation of personal culture, so a single uniformly uplifting interpretation would miss part of the evidence. Its separate example of a person from a foreign culture should remain separate from the adverse mental descriptions. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
