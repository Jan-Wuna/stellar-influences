## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Poseidon`; one-based PDF page `118` (oriented row checked against the scan).

To be connected with spiritual things. Connections with spiritualized man or spirits respectively.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Poseidon`; printed p. `172`; one-based PDF page `85` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Relationships with people with ideas, spiritual contacts of a person, contacts with insightful persons, clarity in personal connections, personal cultural connections, connections with cultured people, and connection among insightful people

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal understanding takes shape through connections with insightful, cultivated or spiritually oriented people. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: ABC emphasizes clarity within personal connections and relationships with people who have ideas. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: The entries describe contacts and spiritual interests rather than a bodily condition or health event. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: Shared ideas, cultural connections and association with spiritualized people form the central relationship pattern. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: Meeting insightful people, forming cultural links and becoming connected with spiritual things are explicit manifestations. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: Witte also says spirits, a source-specific spiritual claim; it is not evidence of verified spirit contact, and relational clarity is not an automatic guarantee of truthfulness. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. ABC gives human and cultural connections, while Witte additionally names spiritual things and spirits.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Node` locates personal cultivation in the people and ideas with which one connects. Insightful companions, shared cultural interests and clearer personal relationships are ABC's concrete expressions of the connection with spiritual things described by Witte. Witte's further reference to spirits remains a distinct spiritual claim rather than a necessary interpretation of ordinary human contacts. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
