## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Poseidon`; one-based PDF page `118` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Spiritualization in old age. To be brought to spiritualization through the force of destiny. Hindered, retarded spiritualization.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Poseidon`; printed p. `173`; one-based PDF page `85` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Burdening personal truth, knowledge of a man in old age, serious spiritualized man, cultural tasks of man, personal concentration on truth, personal cultural tasks, and problematic person culture

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Spiritual cultivation is serious, burdensome or delayed, and may be associated with old age or the pressure of destiny. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: Personal concentration on truth and a serious spiritualized person stand beside a truth experienced as a burden. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: Old age is explicitly named in both entries; neither gives a specific illness, and delayed spiritualization is not itself a medical diagnosis. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: Cultural tasks and problematic personal culture describe responsibilities or difficulties in the person's cultivated life. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: Insights in old age, taking up cultural tasks, and an obstructed or delayed spiritual development are the stated examples. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: The sources allow both spiritualization under pressure and its hindrance; they do not establish inevitable attainment or a specific emotional condition. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

There is an internal tension rather than a simple opposition between sources: Witte gives spiritualization through destiny alongside hindered spiritualization, and ABC joins concentration on truth to burden and problematic culture.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Saturn` makes personal understanding a serious task. Truth can weigh on the person, cultural responsibilities demand concentration, and insight may be associated with old age. Witte allows pressure from destiny to bring spiritualization, but also explicitly preserves hindrance and delay. The result is a demanding or obstructed path of cultivation, with neither failure nor spiritual achievement guaranteed. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
