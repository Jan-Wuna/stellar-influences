mental impulses of the person, clarity of the person for reforms, human cultural reform, personal spiritual renewal, personal insights about reform, reform of the culture of persons, and reasonable human technology

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental impulses and spiritual renewal are directed toward reform of personal or cultural life. Witte adds mental or spiritual visualization, bodily mental tension and an object with artificial intelligence. [ABC](#abc-entry) · [Witte](#witte-entry)
- psychology: ABC emphasizes clarity about reform, new spiritual impulses and personal reform insights. Witte explicitly names a mental or spiritual power to see and visualize. [ABC](#abc-entry) · [Witte](#witte-entry)
- body/health: Witte explicitly names mental tenseness of the body; neither entry supplies a particular illness or a treatment claim. [Witte](#witte-entry)
- social/relationship: Reform of human culture and of personal culture gives the renewal a collective as well as individual setting. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: Reform-oriented insight, spiritual renewal and reasonable human technology are ABC's examples; Witte also names artificial intelligence. [ABC](#abc-entry) · [Witte](#witte-entry)
- conflicts/notes: Witte's power to see and visualize is a mental or spiritual description; neither entry specifies precognition or a method for verifying predictions. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. ABC describes renewal and reform, while Witte adds mental or spiritual visualization, bodily tension and artificial intelligence.

## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Uranus` gives personal understanding an impulse toward renewal. Insight is applied to reform of human or personal culture, and ABC includes reasonable technology among its expressions. Witte adds bodily mental tension and a mental or spiritual power to see and visualize, beside the distinct example of an object with artificial intelligence. Reform, renewal and visualization are therefore supported themes, without a specific claim of precognition. [ABC](#abc-entry) · [Witte](#witte-entry)
