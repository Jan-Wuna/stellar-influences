## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Union of spirit and beauty in one body. To be very cultivated. To attract spiritual people and to harmonize with them. The religiously spiritualized man.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joy over personal insights, harmony of a spiritualized person, cultivation of human love, artificial and cultural center, beauty of body culture, preference for spirited persons, truth loving person, and spiritualized man of the artificial person
