## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Poseidon`; one-based PDF page `118` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Union of spirit and beauty in one body. To be very cultivated. To attract spiritual people and to harmonize with them. The religiously spiritualized man.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Poseidon`; printed p. `172`; one-based PDF page `85` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Joy in personal insights, harmony of a spiritualized person, cultivation of human love, art and cultural center, beauty of body culture, preference for intellectually lively people, truth-loving person, spiritualized man of art.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Spiritual understanding joins beauty, love and cultivation, with personal insight itself a source of joy. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: The person values insight, truth and intellectually lively company; ABC describes harmony within the spiritualized person. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: Witte unites spirit and beauty in one body, while ABC names beauty in body culture. Neither provides a medical condition. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: Cultivating human love and attracting or harmonizing with spiritually oriented people connect personal refinement with relationship. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: An art and cultural center, an artistic spiritualized man, religious cultivation and delight in insight are stated examples. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: The religiously spiritualized man is Witte's particular example, not a requirement that every artistic or affectionate expression be religious. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Both entries join cultivation and harmony; ABC broadens Witte's beauty and spiritual companionship with art, personal insight and love of truth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Venus` brings pleasure and affection into the person's spiritual or cultural life. Insight gives joy, beauty finds an embodied or artistic expression, and cultivation supports love and harmonious company. The setting can be an art and cultural center or a person's affinity with intellectually lively and spiritual people. Witte also preserves a specifically religious expression alongside these wider cultural possibilities. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
