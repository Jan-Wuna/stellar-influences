## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Poseidon`; one-based PDF page `118` (oriented row checked against the scan).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Poseidon`; printed p. `172`; one-based PDF page `85` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Spiritual center in public life, a man's idea in public life, body culture in public life, cultural center in public life, human clarity in public life, personal culture in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal ideas, spiritual cultivation and human clarity are expressed in public life rather than confined to a private setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: High-mindedness and clarity are the stated qualities; ABC places a man's idea within the public sphere. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: ABC includes body culture in public life, a cultural expression rather than a specific illness or health outcome. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: Public spiritual and cultural centers connect the person's cultivation with a wider social environment. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: A publicly expressed idea, a cultural center and public body culture are concrete examples of the shared spiritualization theme. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: Public visibility of cultivation does not independently establish the truth of a claim or the virtue of a public figure. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte gives high-minded men and spiritualization in general; ABC specifies several forms of cultivation and clarity in public life.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Vernal Point` brings spiritual and cultural cultivation into public life. A person's idea, human clarity, body culture or a spiritual center can become part of the wider social scene. Witte describes high-mindedness in general, while ABC gives the places and forms in which it may be publicly expressed. The connection is between personal cultivation and its public presence. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
