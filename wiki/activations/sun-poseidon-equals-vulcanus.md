## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Poseidon`; one-based PDF page `118` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Mental power or influence. The radiating power of the spiritualized body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Poseidon`; printed p. `173`; one-based PDF page `85` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Influence of personal insights, influential cultural centre, spiritual energy of man, mental force or violence within the human being (geistige Gewalt im Menschen), spiritual power center, power of personal truth, power through person culture, and man with cultural influence

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal insight and spiritual cultivation acquire power or influence, extending to a cultural center or a person with cultural standing. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: ABC includes spiritual energy, the power of personal truth and mental force or violence within the person; the forceful possibility is retained beside influence. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: Witte describes the radiating power of the spiritualized body, a spiritual formulation rather than a measured physical or medical effect. [Witte](#witte-entry)
- social/relationship: Influential cultural and spiritual centers, cultural influence and power through personal culture give the theme an outward social setting. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: A person's insights influencing others, a powerful cultural center and the expression of spiritual energy are explicit examples. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: ABC's geistige Gewalt can carry force or violence; its location is within the human being, and the entry does not specifically state physical assault on another person. [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte emphasizes mental influence and radiating power; ABC adds cultural centers, personal truth and the stronger possibility of mental force or violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Poseidon = Vulcanus` describes the force that personal understanding or cultivation can carry. Insight may influence others, a cultural center may hold power, and spiritual energy may be strongly expressed through the person. ABC also includes mental force or violence within the human being, so the pattern should retain the possibility of forcefulness alongside cultural influence. Witte's radiating spiritualized body is its particular image for this power. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
