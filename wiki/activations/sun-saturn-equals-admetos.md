## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `107` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Slow and ungainly body. A body very handicapped. Death in old age.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Saturn`; printed p. `151`; one-based PDF page `74` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Endurance of an old man, burdensome personal suppression, depression through bodily pain, constricted inhibited person, serious steadfast person, patience and endurance of a person, thorough personal maturity, severe bodily blockage.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Bodily restriction and personal inhibition are intensified, while patience, endurance and thorough maturity remain part of the same source range. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: ABC joins constriction, suppression and depression through pain with steadfastness and perseverance; it does not give only passive defeat. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: Witte names a slow, ungainly or severely handicapped body and death in old age; ABC adds pain and severe bodily blockage. These historical associations are not diagnoses or lifespan predictions. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: ABC's personal suppression and inhibition concern a person's restricted condition; the entries do not identify a particular relationship break or social cause. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: Enduring bodily pain, living with restriction, and developing patience or maturity are explicit examples; Witte's death in old age remains a severe attributed example. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: Endurance and maturity coexist with bodily incapacity and distress. They should not be presented as guaranteed compensation for hardship. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte foregrounds bodily impairment and death in old age; ABC adds a wider range of suppression, pain, steadfastness and maturity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Saturn = Admetos` describes a person meeting pronounced restriction. Bodily difficulty can slow or constrain life, while suppression and pain may deepen inhibition or depression. ABC also retains the capacity to endure: patience, steadfastness and thorough maturity belong to the same picture. Witte's severe bodily and death examples remain historical claims, not a prediction of a particular condition or lifespan. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
