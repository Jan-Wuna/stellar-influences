## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `107` (oriented row checked against the scan).

An old experienced man. Separation from many people. To learn from experience. The old teacher.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Saturn`; printed p. `151`; one-based PDF page `74` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Old experienced man, success of a serious person, personal trade losses, personal separation day, extensive personal tasks, many corporal problems, science of human pain, and increasing personal disturbances

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Experience accumulated through age, extensive tasks and separation is central; ABC includes both a serious person's success and losses or increasing difficulties. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: Learning from experience can produce the knowledgeable older teacher, while seriousness and expanding problems give the process weight. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: ABC names many bodily problems and the science of human pain; these are historical associations, not a diagnosis or proof of a medical specialty. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: Witte explicitly includes separation from many people and the old teacher, linking distance with the social transmission of experience. [Witte](#witte-entry)
- events/manifestations: Trade losses, a day of personal separation, extensive tasks and the success of a serious person are all explicit possibilities. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: Success is only one part of ABC's entry; it does not cancel the losses, separation or bodily problems retained by both sources. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte emphasizes age, teaching and separation from many people; ABC expands the range to success, trade losses, extensive tasks and bodily problems.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Saturn = Apollon` places experience and the demands of life on a broad scale. The older, experienced person may learn from what has happened and become a teacher, yet separation from many people and extensive personal tasks remain explicit. ABC permits the success of a serious person alongside trade losses, increasing disturbances and bodily problems. The interpretation therefore centers accumulated experience and extensive demands, with success and loss both retained. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
