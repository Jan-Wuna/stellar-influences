### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

An old experienced man. Separation from many people. To learn from experience. The old teacher.

Age of experienced man, success of a serious person, personal trade losses, personal separation day, extensive personal tasks, many corporal problems, science of human pain, and increasing personal disturbances
