## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `107` (oriented row checked against the scan).

The sickness of an older man. Older fragile body. Mourning, sorrowful end of life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Saturn`; printed p. `151`; one-based PDF page `74` (oriented row checked against the scan).

A man's more difficult tasks, patient person in service, an old man's secret, a body disturbed by deficiency, a man's dangerous hardness, criminally disturbed man (kriminell gestörter Mann), a person's criminal tasks, personal maturity through deprivation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Age, deprivation and difficult personal conditions are joined by serious bodily frailty and mourning; ABC also retains morally severe criminal examples. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: Patience in service and maturity through deprivation stand alongside dangerous hardness and a person's secret. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: Witte describes sickness in an older man, a fragile older body and a sorrowful end of life; ABC names bodily disturbance through deficiency. These are historical associations, not a personal prognosis. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: A person in service, an old man's secret and criminal tasks place the hardship in specific social situations rather than only private exhaustion. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: Difficult tasks, deprivation, illness in old age and mourning are explicit; criminality is a separate severe ABC possibility. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: Neither deficiency nor criminal disturbance means a dead body in the ABC row. Criminal examples remain attributed and do not establish that the person commits a crime. [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte concentrates on age, illness and mourning; ABC adds service, deprivation, secrecy, hardness and criminal examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Saturn = Hades` describes personal demands under conditions of deprivation or decline. Tasks become harder, bodily deficiency or old-age frailty can weigh on life, and Witte includes mourning and a sorrowful ending. ABC also retains patient service and maturity through deprivation, beside dangerous hardness, secrecy and criminal situations. These are distinct source possibilities; the bodily and criminal examples should not be converted into a diagnosis or accusation. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
