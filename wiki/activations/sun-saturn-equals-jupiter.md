- Source heading: `Sun/Saturn`
- Source page: `93`
- Entry: `0247`

#### Ebertin Entry

Phlegmatic temperament, propensity to corpulence, happiness in solitude. Good fortune of a sick, old or serious person. A fortunate separation from someone.
