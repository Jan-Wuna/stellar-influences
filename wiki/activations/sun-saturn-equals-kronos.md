## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `107` (oriented row checked against the scan).

The father. The older ruler (king). Pension from the state. Death or difficulties related to superiors.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Saturn`; printed p. `151`; one-based PDF page `74` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Tasks of a hierarchy center, great old man, hard statesman, personal difficulties with the state, maturing authority person, superiority of a concentrated man, loss of an authority person, and important personal tasks

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal duties and difficulties are connected with authority, hierarchy, the state and older governing figures. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- psychology: ABC gives the superiority of a concentrated man and a maturing authority figure, alongside a hard statesman; these describe different ways seriousness can inhabit rank. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- body/health: Witte names death in relation to superiors, while both entries include older men. These are historical examples and do not predict a particular death or medical condition. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: The father, an older ruler, superiors and the state are explicit authorities; difficulty or loss can occur through these relationships. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- events/manifestations: A state pension, important personal tasks, problems with the state and loss of an authority figure are stated manifestations. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
- conflicts/notes: Maturing authority and significant responsibility coexist with hardness, difficulty and loss; rank itself is not proof of wisdom or legitimacy. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is stated. Witte identifies paternal and state authorities and a pension; ABC extends the theme through hierarchy, concentration, maturity and difficult state relations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Saturn = Kronos` places the person's serious tasks within a hierarchy of authority. The father, older ruler, superior or state may structure those responsibilities, provide a pension, or become a source of difficulty and loss. ABC also allows an authority figure to mature and a concentrated person to hold a superior position. The pattern joins rank and duty with the burdens of dealing with those who hold power. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry)
