## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source passage: `Sun + Saturn`; one-based PDF page `107` (oriented row checked against the scan).

The own personal inhibitions are often processed by thinking, speaking or writing. Thoughts of separation. To go away.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source passage: `Sonne + Saturn`; printed p. `150`; one-based PDF page `74` (oriented row checked against the scan).

A man's serious thoughts, a person's hard words, personal speech disturbances, concentration on news, opinions of a matured person, personal problems in youth, pain during bodily movement.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source passage: `Sun/Saturn`; printed p. `93`; one-based PDF page `95`; entry code `0244` (oriented row checked against the scan).

Inhibited thinking, tendency to have grave or depressing thoughts, tendency to tackle serious problems. Thinking of separation, inhibition of speech.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source passage: `Planets activating the Sun/Saturn midpoint`; one-based PDF page `14` (exact activator entry checked in the raw PDF).

To communicate in a purposefully careful manner. A lack of confidence when communicating. Holding steadily to a definite purpose when communicating. A mind that is able to focus on goals and shut out everything else. Communication that is colored by ego-defensiveness.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `76`

#### Munkasey Entry

Adapting to circumstances despite continuing rigidity or resistance; deft thinking added a persona which is already highly disciplined; actively pursuing a need to obtain the best form of education or work credentials.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thinking and communication address personal inhibitions, serious problems and definite goals, with careful purpose alongside blocked or depressing thought. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry) · [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) · [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)
- psychology: Concentration can hold steadily to a goal or exclude everything else; low confidence and ego-defensiveness may color speech. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) · [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry)
- body/health: ABC names pain during bodily movement and speech disturbances. These historical examples do not establish a specific illness from the formula. [ABC](#abc-entry)
- social/relationship: Hard words, inhibited speech and thoughts of separation may complicate exchange, while thinking, speaking and writing can also process one's personal inhibitions. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry) · [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry)
- events/manifestations: Going away, concentrating on news, tackling serious problems and pursuing education or work credentials are stated possibilities. [Witte](#witte-entry) · [ABC](#abc-entry) · [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) · [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)
- conflicts/notes: Purposeful communication and adaptable thinking coexist with rigidity, depressing thought and inhibited speech; neither mental incapacity nor exceptional wisdom is inevitable. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) · [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) · [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)

## Contradictions

The sources allow productive use of thought to process inhibition as well as thought or speech that is inhibited. Sandbach's purposeful focus and Munkasey's adaptability coexist with defensive or rigid patterns.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Saturn = Mercury` concentrates the person's thinking on serious concerns. Careful, purposeful communication can hold a goal steadily in view, process personal inhibition or support education and credentials. The same concentration may narrow into depressing thought, low confidence, defensive speech or hard words; separation can become a topic or an action. The central tension is between thought used to work through limits and thought itself held back by them.
