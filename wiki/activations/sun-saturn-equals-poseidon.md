## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Old spiritualized man. An elderly or old educator.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Insightful concentrated person, serious spiritualized person, patience and reason of a person, spiritual maturity of man, hard personal truth, cultural tasks of a person, personal spiritual tasks, and personal clarity through patience

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: concentrated understanding, spiritual maturity, and clarity developed through patience.
- psychology: seriousness, reason, and willingness to face a difficult personal truth in ABC.
- body/health: neither entry states a bodily condition or medical implication.
- social/relationship: Witte gives an elderly educator; ABC adds personal cultural and intellectual or spiritual tasks.
- events/manifestations: patient clarification and work that requires mature understanding; no specific event is established.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's male and elderly figure is a source example, not a universal age or gender requirement.

## Contradictions

- No direct incompatibility is evident in these short entries. Witte presents a person, while ABC describes qualities and tasks; their overlap does not establish independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies the Sun/Saturn axis through patient understanding: concentration and seriousness become a way of gaining clarity and facing difficult personal truths. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 151; PDF p. 74) links this maturity with cultural and personal intellectual or spiritual tasks. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 107) gives an elderly educator as a concrete figure; that example does not require a particular age or gender, and neither entry establishes hardship as a necessary cause of wisdom.
