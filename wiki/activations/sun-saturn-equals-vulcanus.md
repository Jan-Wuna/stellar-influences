## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be bodily dependent. Important events fall in the later part of life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influence of an old man, concentrated body force, concentration on personal power, power problems of a person, personal energy loss, maturing influence of a man, severe disturbances of a person, and loss of body force

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: concentrated bodily force or personal influence, together with dependence and loss of energy.
- psychology: attention directed toward personal power; no more detailed inner motive is stated.
- body/health: bodily dependence, diminished energy, and loss of strength are source themes, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: the influence of an older man and problems involving personal power in ABC.
- events/manifestations: maturing influence and, in Witte, important events in later life; no specific age or event is supplied.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's “strong shocks” has been corrected to “severe disturbances” from the German scan, printed p. 151, PDF p. 74. Neither source establishes endurance as the sole expression.

## Contradictions

- Concentrated force and diminished strength coexist within ABC. Witte's bodily dependence and later-life emphasis do not specify which of these expressions must occur.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies the Sun/Saturn axis through the concentration and limits of personal force. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 151; PDF p. 74) brings attention to power and maturing influence, but also to energy loss and disturbances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 107) adds bodily dependence and important events in later life. Taken together, these passages concern the reach and restriction of strength or influence; they do not promise exceptional endurance or establish an age or medical outcome.
