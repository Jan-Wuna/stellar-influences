## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be all alone in this world. Arranged or ordered separations. To still have all the faculties at an advanced age. Ancestors. Forefathers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Serious creative man, disruptions to a man's performance, human maturity through goal striving, man's concentration on a goal, personal leadership tasks, problematic objective of a person, and goals of an old man

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: serious creative or purposeful effort, including difficulties with performance and goals.
- psychology: concentration on a goal and maturity through striving in ABC; Witte also includes solitude.
- body/health: Witte mentions retaining one's faculties at an advanced age; no general health conclusion follows.
- social/relationship: leadership tasks in ABC; arranged separations, ancestors, and forefathers in Witte.
- events/manifestations: problematic objectives and performance disruption; ordered separations are a distinct Witte example.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's “personal movement tasks” has been corrected to leadership tasks from printed p. 151, PDF p. 74. The male and older figures remain source examples.

## Contradictions

- ABC centers on purposeful activity, while Witte includes solitude, ordered separation, and ancestry. These themes are not direct opposites, but the entries do not explain a single cause connecting all of them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies the Sun/Saturn axis through serious creative effort and concentration on an objective. In the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 151; PDF p. 74), striving can develop maturity but can also meet performance difficulties or a problematic goal; leadership tasks are one concrete expression. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 107) adds solitude, ordered separation, retained faculties in old age, and ancestors. Those examples remain part of this orientation's range, without turning all purposeful effort into a story of isolation or inherited duty.
