#### Witte Entry

Perseverance. Heaviness. Weight. Objects. Ground. Soil. Real estate. Landscape. Homeland. That which is conservative. An object of raw material. An object under pressure. Body without motion. Body in repose. Standstill of the body. Day of death. Landed property.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The physical body or object is described through weight, pressure, endurance, and rest.
- psychology: Perseverance and a conservative orientation are named; practical selfhood is not stated.
- body/health: Witte lists bodily repose, standstill, and day of death as historical associations, without establishing a diagnosis or prediction.
- social/relationship: Homeland and landed property are settings; the entry gives no particular relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: Land, raw materials, objects under pressure, and a body at rest are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: One source joins bodily and material examples; these do not all describe the same event.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is represented; cross-source agreement or disagreement cannot be assessed.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the repeated Sun pair through the body's or object's weight, persistence, and reduced motion. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 100) connects this with pressure, repose, ground, raw materials, homeland, and landed property. Endurance and standstill therefore belong to the same recorded range, while the explicit death-day phrase remains an attributed source association rather than a prediction.
