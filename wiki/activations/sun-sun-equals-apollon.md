- Source heading: `SUN + SUN`

#### Witte Entry

Successes. Fame. Honors. A day of glory. Many people (bodies). Physical expansion and success. The successful man or body. The man who finds recognition. Days devoted to peaceful matters and days which bring success. Daily trade, commerce, business and science. (In the solar cycle: a period which concerns trade).

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The body or person appears in an expanded field of success, recognition, and many people.
- psychology: Finding recognition is named; a broad or expansive personality is not established.
- body/health: Witte mentions physical expansion but gives no disease or clinical explanation.
- social/relationship: Many people and a person receiving recognition provide the social setting.
- events/manifestations: Honors, peaceful or successful days, daily trade, commerce, business, and science are named.
- conflicts/notes: The solar-cycle parenthesis remains source wording; this page adds no timing method or guaranteed success.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the repeated Sun pair through physical expansion, many people, and a person finding recognition. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 100) places success and honors alongside peaceful matters and daily trade, business, or science. This gives recognition and a wider field of activity concrete settings; it does not establish fame as an inevitable outcome or turn the retained solar-cycle remark into a timing rule.
