## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Asc`, Asc specifies the repeated Sun pair through the body's relation to people and place. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 99) describes meeting or greeting acquaintances and bodily awareness of the immediate surroundings, making personal contact the concrete expression. Its remark about the quality of a day or year at a location remains source wording; it does not establish a forceful personality or a particular appearance.
