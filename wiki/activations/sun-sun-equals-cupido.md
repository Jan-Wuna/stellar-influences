## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the repeated Sun pair as a person among family or group members, or as bodies gathered together. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 100) makes this concrete through family meetings, weddings, club sessions, and someone spending much time with others. Artists, arts associations, and an art object extend the range beyond family life; the gendered marital roles remain attributed examples rather than requirements for belonging.
