### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + SUN`
- Source locator: PDF p. `100` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Diseased body. Deformed or mutilated body. To be oppressed or hindered in developing the body and personality. Pursued by misfortune. Carrier of secrets. Physical troubles. Old objects. The body’s susceptibility to sickness. Waste. Garbage day. (In the solar cycle: hard period or a period with an upcoming illness.)

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The body or object is associated with hindrance, physical difficulty, secrecy, or discarded material.
- psychology: Hindered personality development and carrying secrets are listed; a damaged core self is not established.
- body/health: Illness, bodily difference or injury, and susceptibility to sickness are historical Witte associations, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Oppression or hindrance in development is named without a particular relationship.
- events/manifestations: Old objects, waste, garbage day, and physical troubles are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: The solar-cycle illness parenthesis is retained as source wording, without forecasting illness or assigning moral value to bodily condition.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is represented; cross-source agreement or disagreement cannot be assessed.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Hades`, Hades specifies the repeated Sun pair through bodily difficulty or obstructed development, while the material examples include old objects and waste. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 100) also names secrecy and misfortune. Its medical and bodily descriptions remain historical source associations; they do not establish a person's condition, a damaged inner self, or an approaching illness.
