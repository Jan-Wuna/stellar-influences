## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + SUN`
- Source locator: PDF p. `99` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

The man. The husband. The active man. The soldier. The action of the living body. Work with physical strength. The working day. Men at work. Daily action. Handyman. Energy level of the body. (In the solar cycle: period concerning to work.)

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The living body acts and expends physical strength in work or daily activity.
- psychology: Activity is explicit, while assertiveness and a particular use of will are not separately stated.
- body/health: Physical strength and bodily energy level are named without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Man, husband, soldier, and working men are Witte's historically gendered roles.
- events/manifestations: Daily work, bodily action, and handyman activity are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: The solar-cycle work parenthesis remains source wording; occupations and male roles are examples rather than requirements.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is represented; cross-source agreement or disagreement cannot be assessed.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Mars`, Mars specifies the repeated Sun pair as the living body in action, using strength in work and everyday activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 99) links this with bodily energy, the working day, a handyman, and a soldier. These concrete activities explain the emphasis on action; the historical male roles and solar-cycle phrase remain attributed examples, without establishing a universally assertive personality.
