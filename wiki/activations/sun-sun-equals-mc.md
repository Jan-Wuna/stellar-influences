#### Witte Entry

Body and soul. The appearance of the “I”. The body’s capacity to react to the mind. The interaction between the body and the soul. One’s own living body. One’s life and goals of life. One moment (minute) at the day. Daily cycle.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = MC`, MC specifies the repeated Sun pair as one's own living body and its interaction with mind or soul. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 99) connects the appearance of the I with one's life and goals, placing bodily existence in a personal frame. The moment-of-day and daily-cycle phrases remain source details; this passage does not itself establish public visibility or a career direction.
