#### Witte Entry

Man and woman (husband and wife). Marriage, parents, friendships, partnerships of male and female. The day and the hour. The course of the day. The attitude of a man towards a woman and vice versa. Physical relations to female persons. The body of a female. The course of the day.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Moon`, Moon specifies the repeated Sun pair through bodily relationships and the partner or family roles named by Witte. The [entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 99) includes marriage, parents, friendships, mutual attitudes, and physical relations with women, alongside the course of the day. These are historical gendered examples and day-related associations; the passage does not itself supply an emotional character description or a universal model of partnership.
