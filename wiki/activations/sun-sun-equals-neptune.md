### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + SUN`
- Source locator: PDF p. `100` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Sensitive, delicate body or object. A body that is susceptible to disease or infection. Weak or physically defenseless. Infirmity. Poor blood. The gas balloon, airplane or ship. Uncertain times or days. Physical relaxation. Lack of live energy. Poor muscle tone of a body or decay. Feebleness or sickness. (In the solar cycle: an uncertain period.)

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The body or object is described through delicacy, weakness, relaxation, and uncertainty.
- psychology: Uncertainty is named, but dreams, ideals, and inspired selfhood are not.
- body/health: Susceptibility to illness, poor blood, low muscle tone, and feebleness are Witte's historical associations, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Physical defenselessness is listed without a specific interpersonal relationship.
- events/manifestations: Gas balloon, airplane, ship, physical relaxation, and uncertain days are distinct examples.
- conflicts/notes: Vehicles and medical phrases remain particular source associations; the solar-cycle remark supplies no new timing method.
