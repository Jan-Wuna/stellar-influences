## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Node`, Node specifies the repeated Sun pair through physical contact or union. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 99) extends that connection to the public and to being a witness, making contact a concrete occurrence rather than a theory of identity. The passage does not specify how intimate or enduring the connection is.
