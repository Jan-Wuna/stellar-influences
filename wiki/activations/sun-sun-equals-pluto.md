- Source heading: `SUN + SUN`

#### Witte Entry

Physical transformation, development and change. To grow. The circulation of the fluids in the body. The physical assimilation of nourishment. The regeneration capacity of the body. The ability of the body to transform itself. Changes in living conditions. The function of the internal organs. (In the solar cycle: period of changes, turnarounds or developments.)

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the repeated Sun pair through physical development and the body's capacity to change. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 100) brings growth, nourishment, fluid circulation, regeneration, and internal-organ function together with changes in living conditions. This is a bodily and circumstantial range in the source; it does not itself establish powerful identity change or an individual's medical condition.
