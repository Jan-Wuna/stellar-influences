#### Witte Entry

One’s own education and intellectual capacity. Spiritualization of the body. The astral body. The man who becomes spiritualized. The high educated personality. The deep thinker.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The person is described through education, intellectual capacity, and spiritualization.
- psychology: Deep thinking and one's own education are explicit.
- body/health: Spiritualization and astral body are Witte's esoteric terms, without a bodily health claim.
- social/relationship: An educated or spiritualized person is named, but no particular relationship.
- events/manifestations: Education and deep thought are the activities or states described.
- conflicts/notes: The entry places intellectual and spiritual terms together without defining astral body or establishing that education causes spiritual attainment.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is represented; cross-source agreement or disagreement cannot be assessed.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the repeated Sun pair through the educated or deep-thinking person and what Witte calls spiritualization of the body. The [entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 100) places intellectual capacity alongside the esoteric term astral body, which it does not define. These remain adjacent intellectual and spiritual descriptions; the passage supplies no necessary alignment with truth or ideals and no causal progression from education to spiritual attainment.
