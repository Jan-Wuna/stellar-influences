## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + SUN`
- Source locator: PDF p. `99` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Old age. Old man. The serious man. Personal and physical hindrances and difficulties. Physical separations. To take leave. Susceptibility to disease and debility. The old body or object. The teacher. A day with hindrances and difficulties. (In the solar cycle: period of depression.)

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The body or person is described through age, seriousness, hindrance, and separation.
- psychology: Seriousness is explicit; a general burdened identity is not.
- body/health: Susceptibility to illness and debility are historical source associations rather than assessments.
- social/relationship: The older person and teacher are roles; taking leave and physical separation are relational examples.
- events/manifestations: Old bodies or objects and a day marked by difficulties are listed.
- conflicts/notes: Age, teaching, and difficulty are separate associations; the solar-cycle depression phrase adds no diagnosis or timing method.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is represented; cross-source agreement or disagreement cannot be assessed.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the repeated Sun pair through an older or serious person, an old body or object, and personal or physical hindrances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 99) also names taking leave, separation, and the teacher. These keep age, difficulty, and a social role within the source's range, while its disease, debility, and solar-cycle depression wording remains attributed rather than an inevitable consequence of aging.
