## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the repeated Sun pair through bodily tension and sudden physical reactions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 99) links nervousness and restlessness with exciting events, while also listing accidents and the historical medical term apoplexy. Those adverse examples remain attributed possibilities in the source; the passage does not establish an accident or describe a psychological awakening.
