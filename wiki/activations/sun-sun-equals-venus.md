#### Witte Entry

Harmony. A peaceful day. Personal attraction. Magnetism. Beautiful body. The loving man. The time of love. The body’s capacity for love. (In the solar cycle: a peaceful period.)

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The body or person is described through harmony, attraction, and the capacity for love.
- psychology: A loving disposition is named; self-love and a theory of personal value are not.
- body/health: Beautiful body is an aesthetic source association, without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Personal attraction and the loving man are Witte's relational examples.
- events/manifestations: A peaceful day and time of love are listed.
- conflicts/notes: The historical male example and solar-cycle peaceful-period remark remain attributed rather than universal or predictive.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Venus`, Venus specifies the repeated Sun pair through personal attraction, bodily beauty, harmony, and the capacity for love. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 99) connects the loving person with peaceful circumstances, giving affection and attraction a physical and everyday expression. Its male-role and solar-cycle phrases remain source examples; this passage does not separately establish self-love or personal worth.
