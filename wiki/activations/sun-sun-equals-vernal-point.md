#### Witte Entry

The body or object in general. The body on this Earth. The body and everything pertaining to the body. The biological. Problems and matters of daily life. Many on a day. Persons in public life. The relationship of the general public to the body. Several people.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the repeated Sun pair as the body or person within general daily and public life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 99) includes ordinary bodily concerns, several people, and persons in public life, giving the physical theme a public context. The scope is broader than a private sense of self or a particular immediate relationship; the unexplained many-on-a-day phrase remains source wording.
