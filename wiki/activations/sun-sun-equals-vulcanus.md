Vitality. Physical strength. The power of an object or the body. The muscular power of the body. A large body. A powerful man. Day of a volcanic eruption.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the repeated Sun pair through vitality and the physical power of a body or object. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 100) makes this concrete as muscular strength, a large body, and a powerful person, while also naming a volcanic-eruption day. Those are bodily and material expressions of strength in the source; the unusual eruption example supplies neither a forecasting method nor a general theory of inner will.
