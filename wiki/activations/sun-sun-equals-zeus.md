#### Witte Entry

The burning body. A man with a goal. A born leader. Creative person. The procreative impulse. The rocket. Man’s procreative capacity. To be the leader. The physical fire. Inflammable objects. Exposed to physical compulsion. Obligated to act. Creative day.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: The body or person is described through purpose, creation, fire, or compelled action.
- psychology: A goal, creativity, and a disposition to lead are named.
- body/health: Procreative capacity and the burning body are historical source associations, without a medical assessment.
- social/relationship: Leadership and being exposed to physical compulsion are different roles.
- events/manifestations: Rocket, inflammable objects, creative day, and obligation to act are explicit examples.
- conflicts/notes: Creative purpose and compulsion are both retained; the fire examples are not reduced to a single psychological metaphor.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Sun = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the repeated Sun pair through purposeful or creative action, including the leader and procreative impulse. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 100) also gives physical fire, a rocket, inflammable objects, and action under compulsion. The range therefore includes both directed creation and being obliged to act; the bodily and fire examples remain source associations rather than proof of freely chosen will or an individual physical event.
