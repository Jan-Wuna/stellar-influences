## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`
- Source locator: printed p. `112`, PDF p. `108` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Grave, exciting disturbances. Sudden events with grave consequences. Sudden death through apoplexy of the heart or cerebral stroke.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `153`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

blocked personal rhythm, stimulation of the body's circulation, personal technical deepening, sudden bodily restriction, rotation of a technical body, surprising personal depression, and resistance against human reforms

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden tension or disturbance meets blocked rhythm and physical restriction.
- psychology: ABC names surprising depression and resistance to reforms; these are attributed descriptions.
- body/health: Circulation, bodily restriction, and Witte's death/apoplexy/stroke phrases remain historical associations, not diagnoses or forecasts.
- social/relationship: Resistance to human reforms is named without a specific relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: Rotation of a technical body, technical deepening, and sudden events with grave consequences are recorded.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's technical and circulatory examples remain alongside Witte's grave disturbances; neither source makes all expressions fatal.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies bodily tension and sudden disturbance through blockage or restriction. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 153) makes this concrete as a blocked personal rhythm, sudden bodily restriction, and technical rotation or deepening; it also names circulation and resistance to reforms. [Witte](#witte-entry) (printed p. 112) emphasizes sudden events with grave consequences. Its extreme medical and death examples remain attributed source possibilities, rather than the inevitable meaning of every disruption.
