## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`
- Source locator: printed p. `112`, PDF p. `108` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Surprising success. To be suddenly known to many people. Far extending tensions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `153`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

Extension of the personal rhythm, successful reform day, new knowledge for the human being, personal knowledge renewal, technical knowledge of a person, technical knowledge center, many personal tensions, and increasing personal tensions

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden success or recognition coexists with widening personal rhythm, knowledge, and tension.
- psychology: New knowledge and renewal of personal knowledge are ABC emphases.
- body/health: Personal tensions are named, without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Becoming suddenly known to many people is Witte's social example.
- events/manifestations: A successful reform day and a technical knowledge center are concrete manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: Success and increasing tensions are both preserved; expansion does not guarantee a favorable outcome.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies sudden events and tension through a wider field of recognition, knowledge, or personal rhythm. [Witte](#witte-entry) (printed p. 112) names surprising success and becoming known to many, while [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 153) adds new or renewed knowledge, technical expertise, and a successful reform day. Both entries also retain extending or increasing tensions, so the broadened activity need not be an uncomplicated breakthrough.
