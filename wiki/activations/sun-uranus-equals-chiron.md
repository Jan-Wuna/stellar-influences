#### Sandbach Entry

Your energy gives people the confidence to be themselves, and to follow their own individual and unique purpose in life. You see the special importance of each individual and help others to see how they are different from others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Encouragement helps others express their individual purpose and differences.

- body/health: No bodily condition or medical healing result is stated.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Chiron`, Chiron specifies individual purpose and uniqueness through encouragement of other people. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) (PDF p. 18) connects recognizing each person's special importance with giving them confidence to be themselves and follow their own purpose. The emphasis is on helping individuality become expressible, rather than a claim about sudden timing, hidden meaning, or a medical healing outcome.
