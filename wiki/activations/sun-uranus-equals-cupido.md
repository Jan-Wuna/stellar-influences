## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`

#### Witte Entry

A sudden decision to marry. Sudden events in an association. Social tension.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `153`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

dynamic community of men, shared bodily rhythm, shared event of the day, impulse giving community of persons, human reform community, new human community, and personal tensions in the community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden events or personal tension take a shared marital or group form.
- psychology: A group giving impulses is described; a general disruption of belonging is not.
- body/health: Shared bodily rhythm is named without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Marriage, associations, new communities, and reform groups are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: A sudden marriage decision and events within an association are Witte examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's male-community phrase is historical source wording; shared excitement and tension need not imply unstable family life.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Cupido`, Cupido specifies sudden events and personal excitation through a union or community. [Witte](#witte-entry) (printed p. 112) names a sudden decision to marry, events in an association, and social tension; [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 153) adds shared bodily rhythm, a common event, reform groups, and new communities. The shared setting can carry both impulse and tension, without making disrupted family belonging the necessary outcome.
