## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`
- Source locator: printed p. `112`, PDF p. `108` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Losses through disagreeable tension. A man energetic in misdeeds. Participants in murder affairs. To be a part of a misfortune. The front-line soldier.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `153`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous technical center, criminal event for a person, new personal disadvantages, hiding personal tensions, sudden disadvantage for the man, surprise by a person from the past, and restlessness due to bodily deficiencies

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden tension or events are associated with losses, disadvantage, or dangerous settings.
- psychology: Concealing personal tensions is explicit; no general malicious personality is established.
- body/health: ABC's restlessness due to bodily deficiencies remains historical source wording, without diagnosis.
- social/relationship: A person from the past, involvement in misfortune, and criminal-event settings are different examples.
- events/manifestations: Dangerous technical centers, losses, surprise, and Witte's front-line soldier are named.
- conflicts/notes: Thought, event, victimhood, and committing a misdeed must not be assumed equivalent from these associations.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Hades`, Hades specifies sudden events and tension through adverse or concealed circumstances. [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 153) names personal disadvantages, hidden tension, and a surprising person from the past, while [Witte](#witte-entry) (printed p. 112) emphasizes losses and involvement in misfortune. The dangerous, criminal, and front-line examples remain particular source associations; they do not identify an individual's conduct, and the bodily-deficiency wording supplies no diagnosis.
