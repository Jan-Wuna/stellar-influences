## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`
- Source locator: printed p. `112`, PDF p. `108` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Luck through sudden events or decisions. Advantageous decisions. A union or connection which cause happy results. A surprise full of joy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `152`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

Excitement in the financial center, dynamic financial man, successful reforms for the people, joy over new people, joyfully excited man, new law concerning persons, new unrest on the day, personal success impulses, and legal event during the day

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. `96`, PDF p. `98`, entry `0259` (raw scan checked).

#### Ebertin Entry

A successful reformer, inventor or technician. Good cognition and understanding. A pleasant surprise creating joy.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Sun/Uranus midpoint`
- Source locator: PDF p. `17` (complete Jupiter entry; raw scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

Enthusiasm for one’s own unique goals. An eagerness for making changes. One who is undaunted by sudden setbacks or sudden turns of events. A philosophy of life that is original, strange, and/or which is based on one’s own independent perceptions of life.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source locator: printed p. `80`, PDF p. `81` (Jupiter entry; raw scan checked).

#### Munkasey Entry

A lot of personal ambition; over-ambition; an optimistic view of what you are as a person and how you are unique; opinions formed about your personal ambitions; gains in your pursuit of personal expressions of freedom.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden decisions or changes have joyful, advantageous, ambitious, or reforming expressions.
- psychology: Enthusiasm for unique goals, optimism, independence of outlook, and over-ambition are named.
- body/health: No bodily condition or medical claim is stated in these entries.
- social/relationship: A connection producing happy results and joy over new people are recorded.
- events/manifestations: Successful reform, invention or technical work, financial excitement, and legal events are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's over-ambition remains a caution; reckless speculation or unstable gains are not stated.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies sudden events and independent purpose through joyful opportunity, favorable decisions, and enthusiasm for change. Successful reform or invention makes that constructive range concrete ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), printed p. 96), while personal ambition and confidence in unique goals support pursuing it ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 17; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 80). [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 152) adds financial, legal, and new-person settings. Over-ambition is explicitly recorded, but success is neither guaranteed nor automatically reduced to speculative excess.
