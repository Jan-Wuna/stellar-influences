## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`
- Source locator: printed p. `112`, PDF p. `108` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Sudden events taking place for the husband. Great excitement. Excitement through the father, superiors or authorities. To become a dictator.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `153`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

Special body tensions, dynamic man of the state, new head of personnel, leading man of technology, great tension center, personal excitement about the state, day of state reforms, and important body rhythm

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden events and excitement concern authority, state affairs, or leading technical roles.
- psychology: Excitement about the state is named without a general dictatorial personality claim.
- body/health: Special bodily tensions and an important bodily rhythm are ABC associations, without diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Husband, father, superiors, authorities, personnel chief, and technical leader are different roles.
- events/manifestations: A new personnel chief, state reforms, and excitement caused by authority are recorded.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's dictator example is one source possibility; encountering authority does not establish that one becomes it.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Kronos`, Kronos specifies sudden events and personal excitement through authority or an important organizational role. [Witte](#witte-entry) (printed p. 112) names events concerning the husband and excitement through the father, superiors, or authorities; [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 153) adds state reforms, a new personnel chief, and technical leadership. The range includes both events involving authorities and the source's more extreme dictator example, without making unexpected command the necessary outcome.
