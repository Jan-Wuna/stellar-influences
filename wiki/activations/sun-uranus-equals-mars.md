## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`
- Source locator: printed p. `112`, PDF p. `108` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Physically hasty work. To hurry too much. Spontaneous decisions. Excitements through acts of will. An accident. An injury to the body. Wounded body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `152`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

Treatment of bodily tensions, a dynamic and determined person, excited personal actions, new personal work, personal work rhythm, reform of work for people, a tense working day, and a person's restless activity.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. `96`, PDF p. `98`, entry `0258` (raw scan checked).

#### Ebertin Entry

A person prone to hasty physical action, precipitation and rash action. Injury, accident.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Sun/Uranus midpoint`
- Source locator: PDF p. `17` (complete Mars entry; raw scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

Asserting oneself in unexpected ways, or in a surprising manner. To be willing to work hard towards one own individual and unique goals and to pursue them in one’s own way. The confidence to work alone.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source locator: printed p. `80`, PDF p. `81` (Mars entry; raw scan checked).

#### Munkasey Entry

Gathering your courage to create new forms of self-expression; the energy and enthusiasm you place into expressing individuality; abrupt or crude expressions about who you are; unusual ways of competing with others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden excitation becomes bodily action, work, or an unusual expression of individuality.
- psychology: Determination, confidence to work alone, courage, and enthusiasm are represented.
- body/health: Injury and accidents are historical source possibilities; ABC also names treatment of bodily tension without an outcome.
- social/relationship: Unexpected assertion, abrupt self-expression, and unusual competition are named.
- events/manifestations: Spontaneous decisions, hasty work, new work, and reform of work are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: Independent effort and rash action are different possibilities; courage does not guarantee safe or effective action.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Mars`, Mars specifies sudden excitation through action and work. A person may pursue unique goals in an unexpected way, work independently, or put courage and energy into new self-expression ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 17; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 80). [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 152) adds work rhythm and reform, while [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) also record hurried or rash physical action. Their injury and accident examples remain attributed possibilities within this range, rather than an inevitable result of independence.
