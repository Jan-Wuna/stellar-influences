## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To experience a psychological shock. Easily excited) irritated. Sudden events.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

body and soul in unrest, my bodily tensions, my personal rhythm, my personal excitement, personal soulful impulses, a soulfully excited person, a soulful event of a person, and restless human soul

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

An excitable and restless person with far-reaching plans. Reformers, inventors, technicians. Experiencing the impact of sudden events or of emotional upheavals.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To have an unusual career, or to approach one’s career in a highly distinc tive fashion. Security derived from having the freedom to pursue one’s own chosen goals. The making of important and unusual contributions to one’s career.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Growth of character and self thru learning what it is that you want and how to achieve that as a person; taking the means to protect self or family from others who want to curtail your freedoms; happiness by being unique.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Uranus = MC`: public originality, reformist work, and the capacity to break stale structures in the outer life. Main caution: instability: abrupt shifts in direction or refusal of limits can fracture continuity, authority, or reputation.
