## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`
- Source locator: printed p. `112`, PDF p. `108` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

To experience a psychological shock. Easily excited, irritated. Sudden events.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `152`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

body and soul in unrest, my bodily tensions, my personal rhythm, my personal excitement, personal soulful impulses, a soulfully excited person, a soulful event of a person, and restless human soul

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. `97`, PDF p. `99`, entry `0265` (raw scan checked).

#### Ebertin Entry

An excitable and restless person with far-reaching plans. Reformers, inventors, technicians. Experiencing the impact of sudden events or of emotional upheavals.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Sun/Uranus midpoint`
- Source locator: PDF p. `18` (complete Midheaven entry; chapter starts on p. `17`; raw scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

To have an unusual career, or to approach one’s career in a highly distinctive fashion. Security derived from having the freedom to pursue one’s own chosen goals. The making of important and unusual contributions to one’s career.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source locator: printed p. `80`, PDF p. `81` (MC entry; raw scan checked).

#### Munkasey Entry

Growth of character and self thru learning what it is that you want and how to achieve that as a person; taking the means to protect self or family from others who want to curtail your freedoms; happiness by being unique.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden events and restlessness are personally experienced alongside distinctive goals and career choices.
- psychology: Excitability, far-reaching plans, knowledge of one's wants, and happiness in uniqueness are named.
- body/health: ABC bodily tension and Witte psychological shock are attributed descriptions, without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Protecting one's own or family's freedom from restriction is a Munkasey example.
- events/manifestations: Reform, invention, technical work, unusual career contributions, and emotional upheavals are recorded.
- conflicts/notes: Career freedom and personal agitation are separate emphases; broken reputation or inability to sustain direction is not stated.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = MC`, MC specifies sudden excitation as personal unrest and the impact of unexpected events ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 152; [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), printed p. 97). Far-reaching plans can also take an independent form: an unusual career or distinctive contribution offers security through freedom to pursue chosen goals ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 18). Learning what one wants and protecting personal or family freedom adds a practical expression of that independence ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 80). These emphases do not establish damage to reputation or an inevitable break with authority.
