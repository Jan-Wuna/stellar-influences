## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To comprehend fast. New spontaneous ideas. Vivid thinking. To give orders suddenly. To be excited while speaking or writing. A rescue team present at an accident. A day with surprising news or messages.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting daily news, aroused staff talk, discuss personal events, opinions on day events, new people in traffic, personal excitement in traffic, personal traffic event, and technical media center

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Presence of mind, quick and prudent acting, sudden flashes of cognition. A young reformer, making arrangements.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Sudden shifts of direction in one’s communicating. Cleverness in finding an unexpected purpose behind situations. The ability to think very swiftly and to receive sudden inspirations. Confident freedom and independence in communicating.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Talks with others about how to achieve freedom; nervousness when acting alone; clever ways to show that you are unique as an individual; observing how others are able to project their unique sense of personality.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Uranus = Mercury`: original ideas, scientific or technical intelligence, and the ability to think ahead of the immediate moment. Main caution: overstimulation: abrupt speech, scattered nerves, or contrarian thinking can disrupt clarity and cooperation.
