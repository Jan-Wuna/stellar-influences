## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`
- Source locator: printed p. `112`, PDF p. `108` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

The exciting course of the day. Sudden events that take place to females.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `152`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

Excited men of a people, dynamic man of the woman, excited people of a people, excitement of the woman about the man, personal excitement about women, tensions of the man with the woman, tension day for the woman, and unrest of a female person

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. `96`, PDF p. `98`, entry `0255` (raw scan checked).

#### Ebertin Entry

Emotional excitability, impulsiveness, rashness. Sudden experiences with the other sex, a nervous and agitated women.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Sun/Uranus midpoint`
- Source locator: PDF p. `17` (complete Moon entry; raw scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s daily life is cyclically affected by sudden or unusual shifts in awareness and/or direction. Sudden and unexpected displays of emotion. The emotional need to have others see that one is different and distinctive. The need to be able to freely express what you feels is most important to you.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source locator: printed p. `80`, PDF p. `81` (complete entry; raw scan checked).

#### Munkasey Entry

Unusual rhythms; sudden allergic reactions; individual displays of tenderness to others or family; arbitrary emotional outbursts; sensitivity to the freedoms that others demand; protecting others who want or need freedom.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden excitation appears in daily rhythms, emotional displays and encounters.
- psychology: Excitability, impulsiveness and the need to express one's distinctiveness are named.
- body/health: Sudden allergic reactions are a Munkasey example, without a diagnostic or predictive basis supplied here.
- social/relationship: Tenderness to family and protection of others' freedom accompany historical gendered encounter examples.
- events/manifestations: Unexpected emotion and changes in daily awareness or direction are explicit manifestations.
- conflicts/notes: The gendered descriptions belong to the cited authors; emotional freedom does not by itself establish domestic instability.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Moon`, Moon places sudden excitation in daily experience and emotional expression: awareness or direction may shift, feelings appear unexpectedly, and being seen as distinctive becomes an emotional need ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 17). [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) adds impulsiveness and rashness, while [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (printed p. 80) also includes tenderness and protecting others' freedom. Witte, ABC and Ebertin retain their historical gendered examples; Munkasey's allergic-reaction example remains an attributed possibility, not a diagnosis. The shared emphasis supports a range of emotional responses rather than an inevitable breakdown of home life.
