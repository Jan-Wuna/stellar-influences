## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The exciting course of the day. Sudden events that take place to females.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Excited men of a people, dynamic man of the woman, excited people of a people, excitement of the woman about the man, personal excitement about women, tensions of the man with the woman, tension day for the woman, and unrest of a female person

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Emotional excitability, impulsiveness, rashness. Sudden experiences with the other sex, a nervous and agitated women.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s daily life is cyclically affected by sudden or unusual shifts in awareness and/or direction. Sudden and unexpected displays of emotion. The emotional need to have others see that one is different and distinctive. The need to be able to freely ex press what you feels is most important to you.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Unusual rhythms; sudden allergic reactions; individual displays of tenderness to others or family; arbitrary emotional outbursts; sensitivity to the freedoms that others demand; protecting others who want or need freedom.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Uranus = Moon`: responsiveness to change, inventive instinct, and emotional renewal through movement, difference, or nonconformity. Main caution: instability: moods can swing sharply, and domestic or bodily rhythms may become erratic under strain.
