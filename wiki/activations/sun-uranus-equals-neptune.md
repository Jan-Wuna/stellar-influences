## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`
- Source locator: printed p. `112`, PDF p. `108` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Useless, erroneous excitement. Tension caused by uncertainties, confusion, lies or deceit. Airline accidents. Gas or water accidents. Events connected with death. Burials. Connections with dead people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `153`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

Excitation of an uncertain person, personal excitement about the future, sudden personal intuition, creeping bodily restlessness, a technical body in the air, surprising bodily weakness, and rejection of human reforms.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. `96`, PDF p. `98`, entry `0261` and its footnote `9` (raw scan checked).

#### Ebertin Entry

Inhibited urge to action, blind zeal can only be detrimental, unexpected events connected with water, navigation or flying. In isolated cases, disintegration of the body, death.

Source footnote `9` (attached to entry `0261`): Sun/Uranus = Neptune has been found frequently in the case of aircraft disasters and other catastrophes in the air.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Sun/Uranus midpoint`
- Source locator: PDF p. `18` (complete Neptune entry; chapter starts on p. `17`; raw scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

Confusion caused by sudden turns of events. One’s individuality and uniqueness may be very subtle, and might also be misunderstood by others. Intuitions which come suddenly to one, and which can quickly change the course of one’s life.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source locator: printed p. `80`, PDF p. `81` (complete entry; raw scan checked).

#### Munkasey Entry

Inspiration regarding your sense of self; dreams to help you leave unique marks in the world; visions of new trends which you can use to make your way in business or in life; neglect of self and/or your sense of individuality.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden excitation meets uncertainty, intuition and a subtle sense of individuality.
- psychology: Confusion, inhibited action, inspiration and possible neglect of self are distinct emphases.
- body/health: Bodily weakness and the authors' severe accident/death examples are attributed historical claims; Ebertin qualifies death with in isolated cases.
- social/relationship: Subtle individuality may be misunderstood by others.
- events/manifestations: Sudden intuitions, future-oriented visions, water/air events and rejection of reforms are named.
- conflicts/notes: Ebertin's aircraft-disaster footnote belongs to Neptune, not Node; no risk estimate or deterministic prediction is justified.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies sudden excitation through uncertainty and intuition. An unexpected event can confuse, yet a sudden intuition may also change one's direction, while individuality remains subtle or misunderstood ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 18; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 153). Inspiration and visions of new trends offer another expression, alongside possible neglect of self ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 80). Witte and Ebertin retain severe historical examples involving air, water and death; Ebertin's entry says 'in isolated cases,' and its footnote 9 supplies no basis for treating this formula as a prediction of catastrophe.
