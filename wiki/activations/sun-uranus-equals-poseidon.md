## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`
- Source locator: printed p. `112`, PDF p. `108` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

To be tense through a sudden realization. Propagandist. Phosphorescent body. The light-bearer. The transparent body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `153`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

Spiritual impulses of the man, new ideas of a person, dynamic spiritualized man, body and mind in excitement, human insight for reforms, new insights of man, surprising personal clarity, and restless mind in the body

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden realization and new ideas accompany mental or spiritual excitation.
- psychology: Surprising personal clarity and a restless mind are explicit ABC descriptions.
- body/health: Body and mind in excitement is stated; Witte's phosphorescent and transparent body images are not explained medically.
- social/relationship: Witte names a propagandist; ABC links human insight with reforms.
- events/manifestations: New ideas, insights and sudden realization are the concrete manifestations supplied.
- conflicts/notes: The language of light-bearing is retained as source wording; revelation of objectively verified truth is not established.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies excitation through realization, ideas and mental or spiritual impulses. A sudden realization may create tension ([Witte](#witte-entry), printed p. 112), while new ideas, insight for reforms and surprising personal clarity can emerge alongside a restless mind ([ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 153). These entries connect stimulation with understanding without guaranteeing that a revelation is true. Witte's propagandist and light-bearing images remain particular source descriptions rather than a universal vocation or a medical account.
