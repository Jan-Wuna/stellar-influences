## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`
- Source locator: printed p. `112`, PDF p. `108` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

Trying to rid oneself of something through physical efforts. To give birth. Sudden physical separations. To run away fast. Difficulties due to resistance. Painful and grievous surprises. Shaking with ague.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `153`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

New personal burdens, loss of people, personal rhythm in old age, suddenly separated man, problematic day event, surprising separation from man, reforms involving losses for people, and center for technical tasks

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. `96`, PDF p. `98`, entry `0260` (raw scan checked).

#### Ebertin Entry

Rebellion against limitation of freedom. Inhibitions and difficulties overcome through extraordinary efforts. Sudden separation, sudden loss, separation from another person carried out in haste.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Sun/Uranus midpoint`
- Source locator: PDF p. `18` (complete Saturn entry; chapter starts on p. `17`; raw scan checked).

#### Sandbach Entry

Shyness about displaying one’s uniqueness or differences. To be very sensitive about what places one apart from others. Over time one gradually gains more self-confidence concerning one’s individual differences from others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source locator: printed p. `80`, PDF p. `81` (complete entry; raw scan checked).

#### Munkasey Entry

Unusual forms of business activity which allow you to display your sense of individuality; working slowly but with determination toward making self unique as an individual; caution in the pursuit of personal freedom.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Individuality encounters limitation, resistance, separation or cautious determined effort.
- psychology: Shyness about differences and gradual confidence coexist with rebellion against restricted freedom.
- body/health: Birth, physical effort and shaking with ague are Witte's historical examples; no clinical prediction is supplied.
- social/relationship: Separation from others and sensitivity about standing apart are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Sudden loss, hasty separation, difficulties overcome by exceptional effort and unusual business activity are named.
- conflicts/notes: Gradual confidence and caution differ from sudden severance; successful modernization is not promised.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies excitation through resistance, limitation and separation. The entries include painful surprises and sudden loss, while Ebertin also allows difficulties to be overcome through extraordinary effort ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 153; [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), entry 0260). Individual difference can initially bring shyness, with confidence developing gradually ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), PDF p. 18). Slow, determined work and caution in pursuing freedom give this process a practical expression ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), printed p. 80). Witte's bodily examples remain attributed possibilities, and the formula does not guarantee either breakdown or successful reform.
