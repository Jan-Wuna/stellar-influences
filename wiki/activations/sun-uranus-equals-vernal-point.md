## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `SUN + URANUS`
- Source locator: printed p. `112`, PDF p. `108` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### Witte Entry

To be kept tense by general events. Several misfortunes. Lightening. Earthquakes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Sonne + Uranus`
- Source locator: printed p. `152`, PDF p. `75` (complete oriented entry; raw scan checked).

#### ABC Entry

A day of reform in public life, excited people in public, new people in public life, an eventful day in public, personal excitement in public, and a technical center in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: General events and public life give sudden excitation a collective setting.
- psychology: Being kept tense and public excitement are named.
- body/health: No clinical account is supplied; Witte's misfortune examples remain historical possibilities.
- social/relationship: New people and excited people in public are ABC examples.
- events/manifestations: Public reform, an eventful day and a technical center appear alongside Witte's earthquake example.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's printed Lightening is retained; the examples do not establish a forecast of public disaster.

## Contradictions

The entries give different manifestations of this orientation; no direct contradiction is established, and repeated themes alone do not prove independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Uranus = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point gives excitation a public setting. People may be excited by general events, while reform, new people and an eventful day bring change into public life ([ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 152; [Witte](#witte-entry), printed p. 112). Witte also names misfortunes and earthquakes. These are attributed examples within the entry, without establishing that a public reform or event must become a catastrophe.
