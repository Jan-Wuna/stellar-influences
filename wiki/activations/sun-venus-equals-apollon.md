## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A man in love with many women. Harmony and friendship with many people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 145, supplied PDF p. 71, Sonne + Venus, apollon row (eight clauses). Translation from the German scan:

Expansion of personal affection; successful personal love; physical experience of love; a person in the art trade; personal knowledge of art; a day of experiencing love; many beautiful people; a scientific center of peace.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Affection extends into friendship with many people.
- psychology: A broad capacity for affection is explicit.
- body/health: No health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Harmony and friendship reach a wide circle.
- events/manifestations: Successful love and an expanding circle of contacts are named.
- conflicts/notes: The gendered many-women example is a historical formulation, not a universal pattern.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC includes bodily love experience, art and beauty beside widened affection; these examples do not make every friendship romantic.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Sun/Venus through the widening of affection into harmony and friendship with many people. Successful personal love accompanies this broader social reach; the entry supports an expansive circle of affection without making every relationship romantic. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
