## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A man in love, who’s inclined to marry. The formula for a man on his wedding day. A beautiful, artfully shaped body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 145, supplied PDF p. 71, Sonne + Venus, cupido row (seven clauses). Translation from the German scan:

A peaceful community of persons; a harmonious human community; a community's harmonious day; a community's art center; a man's art of love; personal joy in the community; a personal community of love.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Affection takes communal or marital form.
- psychology: Joy is experienced in belonging to a community.
- body/health: Witte includes bodily beauty, not a health diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Marriage and an affectionate community are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Witte names a man's wedding day as a historical example.
- conflicts/notes: The male wedding formula is attributed to Witte and does not establish a universal event prediction.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC's art center and art of love are distinct phrases within its communal affection examples; Witte's wedding day remains a historical example rather than a prediction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Sun/Venus as love taking a shared form: marriage, an affectionate community, or joy within a group. Witte's wedding-day example gives this union a concrete historical expression; his separate image of a beautifully shaped body should remain distinct from the relational theme. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
