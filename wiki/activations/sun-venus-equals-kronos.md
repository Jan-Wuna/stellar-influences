## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Perfect lover. A Womanizer. Exceptional physical charisma.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 145, supplied PDF p. 71, Sonne + Venus, kronos row (seven clauses). Translation from the German scan:

Reputation of a beautiful person; special human affection; the state's center of peace; great personal love; a self-confident peaceful man; a handsome man's self-confidence; a man's superiority through harmony.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Affection is expressed as exceptional charisma or distinguished love.
- psychology: ABC names confidence associated with harmony.
- body/health: Witte describes physical charisma, not illness.
- social/relationship: Witte's lover and womanizer are attributed gendered examples.
- events/manifestations: ABC includes a state peace center and exceptional personal affection.
- conflicts/notes: Charisma and womanizing are different expressions; neither establishes moral superiority.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC's beauty and self-confidence concern appearance and harmony; Witte's charisma and womanizing examples do not establish moral superiority.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Sun/Venus through exceptional attraction and conspicuous personal affection. Witte's physical charisma and ABC's confidence through harmony support a reading of love that stands out; Witte's gendered lover and womanizer examples describe possible expressions rather than a universal character judgment. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
