## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: PDF page `104`, `SUN + VENUS`, Moon row.

Beautiful body of a woman. To be in love. Expressions of love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source reference: printed page `144`, PDF page `71`, `Sonne + Venus`, Moon row.

Source wording: "Freude-Gefühl am Tage, friedlicher Mann der Frau, gefühlvolle körperliche Liebe, Harmonie und Liebe zur Frau, harmonischer Tag für die Frau, persönliche Harmonie der Frau, schöne Frau des Mannes, schöner Frauen-Körper, schönste Frau des Jahres".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A feeling of joy during the day; the woman's peaceful man; emotionally felt bodily love; harmony and love toward the woman; a harmonious day for the woman; the woman's personal harmony; the man's beautiful woman; a beautiful female body; the most beautiful woman of the year.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Source reference: printed page `84`, PDF page `86`, entry `0207`, Moon on the Sun/Venus axis.

Strong feeling of love, attractive personality, demonstration of love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Source reference: PDF page `10`, Moon under `Planets activating the Sun/Venus midpoint`.

The feeling that one needs to be surrounded by beauty and that one actually feeds on it as if it were food. Beauty creates a sense of security. One who is very im pressionable to beauty in the arts, and all other forms. One feels emotionally fulfilled by a meaningful love relationship.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/VENUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: printed `64`, PDF page `65`, Moon row.

#### Munkasey Entry

Enhancing the care and sensitivity you bring to the affection and lovingness you give to others; pleasures you derive from mothering; hesitations or inconsistencies you show with your appreciation or gratitude.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).

- core meaning: Affection and beauty as sources of emotional fulfilment, with care and sensitivity in their expression.
- psychology: Beauty is experienced as nourishment and security.
- body/health: Witte and ABC describe female bodily beauty in historical gendered language; these are aesthetic statements, not medical findings.
- social/relationship: Care, sensitivity, and meaningful love relationships are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Expressions of love and the pleasure of mothering are named.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's hesitation or inconsistent gratitude qualifies the warm expression.

## Contradictions

Munkasey's hesitation or inconsistent gratitude qualifies the warm expression: feeling affection and expressing appreciation evenly are distinct. This is a difference of emphasis, not a denial of the other entries' affection and beauty themes. The [ABC Moon row](#abc-entry) has been repaired from printed page `144`; its repeated beauty terms were previously mistranslated as "gentle woman." Its gendered examples remain attributed to that source. Sandbach's split "im pressionable" is present in the supplied PDF and is retained as written.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Sun/Venus = Moon`, the love, beauty, and personal attraction of the [Sun/Venus axis](../axes/sun-venus.md#derived-synthesis) become experiences of emotional nourishment and security. Being in love, expressing affection, and responding to beauty connect attraction with felt fulfilment; Sandbach's image of feeding on beauty makes this relationship explicit. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry))

Care and sensitivity give affection a nurturing expression, including Munkasey's pleasure in mothering. His hesitation or inconsistent gratitude also shows that a strong feeling need not be expressed evenly. ABC retains bodily beauty and harmony in its historical woman/man vocabulary; these are this orientation's source examples, without supplying a health judgment or transferring them to a sibling formula. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
