## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Flirtations. Love affairs. Celebrating festivities. Attending shows. Getting connections is easy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Harmonic connection to man, corporal love connection, love connection on the day, personal harmonic connection, gentle encounter people, day of harmonious contacts, meetings of schaner people, and connected by human affection

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

The desire to come together with lovable and kind-hearted people. Association with art lovers. Artistic events.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The need to share love with other people. A love of connecting with others. Negative karma returning from selfishness and/or egotism. The need to evolve one’s positive self-love and one’s ability to see what is most important in life.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/VENUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `64`

#### Munkasey Entry

Helps you meet others who teach you how to develop your basic ways of showing devotion and lovingness; meeting groups which teach you the meaning of how to express your inner felt affections for another person.
