## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Idealistic love. The body which turns from earthly love. The deep thinker for beautiful, peaceful or harmony. The spiritual man.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 145, supplied PDF p. 71, Sonne + Venus, poseidon row (seven clauses). Translation from the German scan:

Insight of a peaceful person; a person's harmony and clarity; a man's artistic idea; personal insight into love; beauty of body and mind/spirit; preference for physical culture; a center for peace and culture.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Love is expressed as an ideal of beauty, peace, and clarity.
- psychology: Reflection and awareness of love are explicit.
- body/health: Witte's withdrawal from earthly love is historical symbolism, not a medical claim.
- social/relationship: Affection may take an idealistic rather than earthly form.
- events/manifestations: ABC includes a center for peace and culture.
- conflicts/notes: The entries do not establish a universal rejection of physical relationships.

## Contradictions

The entries do not establish a universal rejection of physical relationships. ABC explicitly joins artistic ideas, insight into love and beauty of body and mind/spirit.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Sun/Venus as idealistic love and reflection on beauty, peace, and harmony. ABC adds personal awareness and clarity; Witte's turning from earthly love gives one possible spiritual expression, rather than defining every expression of the formula. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
