## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The wish to have descendants. Preparations for obtaining descendants.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 145, supplied PDF p. 71, Sonne + Venus, Zeus row (eight clauses). Translation from the German scan:

A leading art center; shaping a harmonious day; harmonious physical procreation; bodily fire of love; a man's artistic creation; achievements of an artistic person; personal striving toward harmony; a day of love-procreation.

The final compound is Liebes-Zeugung; it is retained without specifying a reproductive mechanism or predicting an event.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Affection is directed toward procreation, artistic creation and harmony.
- psychology: Desire for descendants and striving toward harmony are explicit.
- body/health: Physical procreation and bodily love-fire remain source motifs without a fertility guarantee.
- social/relationship: Parenthood is Witte's stated aim; ABC adds artistic persons and a leading art center.
- events/manifestations: Preparation for descendants, artistic achievements and shaping a harmonious day are examples.
- conflicts/notes: The artistic and procreative examples remain distinct; Liebes-Zeugung does not establish a specific event.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte concentrates on wanting descendants; ABC also includes art, harmonious creation and bodily love-fire. These different examples do not establish a fertility guarantee.

## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Sun/Venus as affection directed into creation. [Witte](#witte-entry) concentrates on the wish for descendants and preparations toward it; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds artistic creation, an art center, personal striving toward harmony and shaping a harmonious day. The common thread is love taking a creative or procreative direction, while the artistic and reproductive examples remain distinct and establish no guaranteed event.
