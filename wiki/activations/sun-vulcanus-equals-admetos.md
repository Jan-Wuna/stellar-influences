## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Physical limitations. Diminished body weight.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 171 / PDF page 84, Admetos glyph under Sonne + Vulkanus. English translation checked against the complete row.

Blocked personal energy, depressions through physical violence, end of a man's power, restricted bodily strength, consolidation of personal power, violent personal suppression, and severe depressions of a person.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Personal energy and bodily force encounter limits.
- psychology: ABC names depressive experience in connection with violence.
- body/health: Witte's diminished body weight and ABC's depression are attributed historical claims.
- social/relationship: ABC includes the ending of a man's power.
- events/manifestations: Restricted strength and the ending or consolidation of power are named.
- conflicts/notes: Consolidating power differs from blocked energy; neither cancels the other.

## Contradictions

Consolidating power differs from blocked energy; neither cancels the other. [ABC](#abc-entry) separately names violent suppression and depressions through physical violence; these remain attributed historical examples rather than clinical conclusions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Sun/Vulcanus through limits placed on bodily force and personal power. Witte gives physical limitation and diminished body weight as historical examples, while ABC includes blocked energy and loss of power. Its separate strengthening-of-power clause remains an alternative to restriction. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
