## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Necessity to apply physical strength. To deal with people of great physical strength.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 170 / PDF page 84, Asc glyph under Sonne + Vulkanus. English translation checked against the complete nine-clause row.

Influence of other persons, influential place at the location, energy center in the surroundings, violent person at the location, partner's bodily strength, human strength for the surroundings, personal energy for the surroundings, the partner's personal power, and day of violence at the location.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Physical strength is encountered or required in dealings with others.
- psychology: A separate inner disposition is not specified.
- body/health: Physical strength is explicit; no health outcome is established.
- social/relationship: Strong people and a partner's personal power are named.
- events/manifestations: Applying strength and local concentrations of energy are examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes violence as a possibility, not as the only expression.

## Contradictions

ABC includes a violent person and a day of violence beside human strength, influence and energy for the surroundings; violence is a distinct possibility rather than the only expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Sun/Vulcanus through encounters in which physical strength must be used or strong people must be dealt with. ABC places influence and personal power in the partner or local environment; its violence examples remain distinct possibilities rather than a definition of every contact. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
