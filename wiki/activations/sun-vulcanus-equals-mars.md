## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To employ physical strength at work. Work which requires great physical strength.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 170 / PDF page 84, Mars glyph under Sonne + Vulkanus. English translation checked against the complete eight-clause row.

Influence of human will, influential personal activity, energetic physical actions, the man's violent acts, activating bodily strength, physical violent activity, human working strength, and activity in a power center.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Physical strength is put to work.
- psychology: No separate emotional disposition is stated.
- body/health: Great bodily exertion is named, without a clinical outcome.
- social/relationship: ABC includes human labor and force used in a power center.
- events/manifestations: Work demanding great strength is the clearest example.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's violence clauses are alternatives to labor, not an inevitable result.

## Contradictions

ABC's violence clauses are alternatives to labor, not an inevitable result. The complete row also names human will, physical action and working strength.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Sun/Vulcanus as strength actively employed, especially in work that requires considerable physical effort. ABC broadens this to influential activity and human working strength, while its violent examples mark another possible use of force rather than changing all exertion into aggression. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
