## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Feeling of power through one’s own physical strength.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 170 / PDF page 84, MC glyph under Sonne + Vulkanus. English translation checked against the complete ten-clause row; Seele/seelisch is rendered as soul/of the soul without an added mechanism.

Influence of body and soul, power of the human soul, my energy center, my strong man, my bodily strength, my personal power, personal influence of the soul, psychological energy of a person, force/violence of the soul during the day, and a person's strength of soul.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Power is felt through one's own strength.
- psychology: Personal power, soul and psychological energy are explicit source descriptions.
- body/health: Witte relates the feeling of power to physical strength.
- social/relationship: ABC names personal influence of the soul, without specifying a relationship event.
- events/manifestations: An energy center and one's strong man are examples.
- conflicts/notes: The entry supports an inward feeling of power more directly than worldly leadership.

## Contradictions

The entry supports an inward feeling of power more directly than worldly leadership. ABC's personal power, bodily strength and soul descriptions remain distinct, with no mechanism supplied.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Sun/Vulcanus as the felt experience of one's own power, grounded in physical strength. ABC extends that personal experience toward influence of body and soul; it does not by itself establish a public vocation or leadership role. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
