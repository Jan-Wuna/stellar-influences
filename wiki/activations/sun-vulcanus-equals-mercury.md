## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Force is expressed through thought and emphatic speech.
- psychology: Strong nerves and thought power are named.
- body/health: Witte's strong-nerves phrase is historical doctrine, not a clinical finding.
- social/relationship: Young people and the influence of media appear in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Emphatic words, running, and daily media influence are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Violence in news and violent young people are specific ABC examples, not a universal temperament.

## Contradictions

Violence in news and violent young people are specific ABC examples, not a universal temperament. No direct contradiction is established within the available entry.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies Sun/Vulcanus through force in words, thought, and communication. Witte's emphatic speech meets ABC's thought power and media influence, while the unusual example of a runner gives a separate bodily expression. ABC's violent examples should remain particular manifestations rather than a definition of forceful thought. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
