## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Well functioning glands. The physical strength of a woman. To demonstrate or apply physical strength in public. Persons with executive power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 170 / PDF page 84, complete Moon glyph row under Sonne + Vulkanus; the violence clause was checked directly.

Influence between woman and man, influence of the woman's body, influential hour of the day, emotional influence of the man, violence by the man against the woman, power center of a people, personal influence of the woman, and strong man of a woman

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Bodily power becomes visible in public or between people.
- psychology: ABC includes emotional influence.
- body/health: Witte's well-functioning-glands assertion is attributed historical doctrine.
- social/relationship: Witte names a woman's strength; ABC adds influence between woman and man.
- events/manifestations: Public application of strength and executive power are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered examples do not establish fixed traits or a necessary pattern of conflict.

## Contradictions

Gendered examples do not establish fixed traits or a necessary pattern of conflict. ABC explicitly names violence by the man against the woman; this remains an attributed historical example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Sun/Vulcanus through strength expressed in public or interpersonal life. Witte names the public demonstration of physical strength and executive power, while ABC adds emotional and personal influence between people. ABC's gendered violence example and Witte's glandular statements remain historical examples, not universal traits or health assessments. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
