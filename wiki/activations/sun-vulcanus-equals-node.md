## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Connection with a strong, muscular man. To suffer from other persons. Wish for power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 170 / PDF page 84, complete Node glyph row under Sonne + Vulkanus.

Encounter with powerful people, relationships with influential people, contacts with strong men, connected with violent people, connection to an energy center, connection to powerful people, and linking with violence during the day

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Contacts bring one into relation with strength and power.
- psychology: Witte includes a wish for power.
- body/health: A strong muscular man is a historical example, not a health outcome.
- social/relationship: Influential or violent people and suffering through others are named.
- events/manifestations: Meetings with powerful people and links to energy centers are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Connections with strength can be desirable or harmful; mutual support is not guaranteed.

## Contradictions

Connections with strength can be desirable or harmful; mutual support is not guaranteed. ABC's linking with violence during the day remains a distinct attributed example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Sun/Vulcanus through connections with strong or influential people and centers of power. Witte includes both the wish for power and suffering through other people, so the relationship may provide access to strength or expose one to its harmful use. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
