## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Rapid development and transformation of physical strength through exercise. To bring physical strength into play.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 171 / PDF page 84, complete seven-clause Pluto glyph row under Sonne + Vulkanus.

Development of an energy center, developing bodily strength, developing personal violence, changes through influential persons, transformation of human energy, growing human violence, and changing personal influence.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Strength develops and is brought into use.
- psychology: A separate psychological motive is not established.
- body/health: Witte explicitly connects development of strength with exercise.
- social/relationship: ABC includes changing personal influence.
- events/manifestations: Rapid transformation of physical strength is the clearest example.
- conflicts/notes: Developing strength, violence and influence are distinct source examples, without a necessary causal link.

## Contradictions

Witte's exercise and ABC's development of strength overlap. ABC separately names developing violence and changing influence; neither is stated as a necessary result of physical exercise.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies Sun/Vulcanus through the development and active use of physical strength. Witte makes exercise the concrete means of rapid change; ABC's complete row adds development of an energy center and changes in strength, human energy and influence. Its separate violence clauses are not consequences inferred from exercise. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
