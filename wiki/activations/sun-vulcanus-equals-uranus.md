## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Body with powerful tension. To start using the physical strength with a jerk. Electricity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source locator: printed page 171 / PDF page 84, complete seven-clause Uranus glyph row under Sonne + Vulkanus.

Excitement through a violent man, a person's energy impulses, influential man in technology, powerful bodily tension, new energy for the human being, strong personal impulses, and technical power center.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Force is released suddenly from a state of tension.
- psychology: Strong impulses and excitement are explicit.
- body/health: Bodily tension and abrupt exertion are source motifs, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: ABC includes excitement through a violent man as one setting.
- events/manifestations: Electricity and technical energy centers are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: Sudden application of force does not alone establish aggression.

## Contradictions

Sudden application of force does not alone establish aggression. ABC's powerful bodily tension and technical power center remain distinct from its violent-man example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies Sun/Vulcanus as power held in tension and released abruptly. Witte's sudden bodily exertion and electricity meet ABC's energy impulses and technical centers; excitement through a violent person remains a particular interpersonal example. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
