## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Physical power. Persons with great physical strength in general. The great powers of the earths body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

influential person of the public, energy center in public, violent man in public life, public influence on the day, public power center, person of public power, and day of violence in public

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Physical power appears on a public or earthly scale.
- psychology: No distinct inner disposition is supplied.
- body/health: Great physical strength is named without a clinical conclusion.
- social/relationship: Publicly influential people and public power centers are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Witte additionally names the great powers of the earth's body.
- conflicts/notes: Public violence is ABC's specific possibility, not the whole meaning.

## Contradictions

Public violence is ABC's specific possibility, not the whole meaning. No direct contradiction is established within the available entry.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Sun/Vulcanus as physical power in the wider world: strong people, public influence, and centers of power. Witte's great powers of the earth's body add a broader earthly expression, while ABC's violence in public remains one possible manifestation. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
