## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Enormous endeavor and physical accomplishment. Products generated through physical strength.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Efforts of an energetic human being, influence through personal goal seeking, energetic personal guidance, fire violence in the day, generation of corporal violence, achievements of an energy center, human leadership force, and power of fire

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Strength is directed into endeavor and production.
- psychology: Energetic pursuit of personal aims is explicit.
- body/health: Physical accomplishment is named without a disease claim.
- social/relationship: ABC includes personal guidance and human leadership force.
- events/manifestations: Products generated through strength and power over fire are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Creative accomplishment and destructive force are distinct possibilities in ABC.

## Contradictions

Creative accomplishment and destructive force are distinct possibilities in ABC. [ABC](#abc-entry): ABC "fire violence" and "generation of corporal violence" are awkward renderings; no specific destructive event is inferred.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Sun/Vulcanus as strength directed toward effort, accomplishment, and a result. Witte emphasizes products generated through physical exertion; ABC adds energetic goal pursuit and guidance, alongside the separate possibilities of fire and bodily violence. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
