## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

As a leader making one’s way against strong resistance.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 163, supplied PDF p. 80, Sonne + Zeus, admetos row (seven clauses). Translation from the German scan:

Enduring physical achievements; end of the man's procreative capacity; restricted personal creativity; restriction of physical performance; a blockade of fire during the day; a thorough creative person; suppression of personal efforts.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Leadership or creative effort meets resistance.
- psychology: Thorough creativity and restricted personal creativity both appear in ABC.
- body/health: ABC's ending of male procreative ability is an attributed historical claim.
- social/relationship: Witte places the person in a leadership role opposed by resistance.
- events/manifestations: Restricted performance and reduced effort are named.
- conflicts/notes: Making one's way through resistance differs from unrestricted success.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC combines enduring achievement and thorough creativity with restrictions and suppression; the procreative-capacity statement remains historical source doctrine rather than a diagnosis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Sun/Zeus as creative or leading effort meeting strong resistance. Witte still allows the leader to make a way through it, while ABC names restrictions on creativity and bodily performance. The emphasis is on effort under obstruction, with success dependent on overcoming resistance. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
