## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Fortunate personal undertakings. The successful leader. Physical intercourse with consequences (procreation).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 162, supplied PDF p. 80, Sonne + Zeus, jupiter row (seven clauses). Translation from the German scan:

Efforts of a person in a legal context; successful personal striving toward goals; a leading man of finance; founding a financial center; personal shaping of legal matters; advantages through personal efforts; human joy in procreation.

Rechts-Person and Rechts-Gestaltung are brief legal-context phrases; this row does not settle an exact role or corporate entity status.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Personal undertaking and leadership have favorable outcomes.
- psychology: Successful pursuit of personal aims is explicit.
- body/health: Witte includes procreation following intercourse as historical doctrine.
- social/relationship: Leadership and a person in a legal context are named.
- events/manifestations: Benefits from personal effort are a shared practical theme.
- conflicts/notes: A favorable undertaking is a source possibility, not a promised outcome.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC's legal and financial examples do not identify an exact legal role or entity status, and favorable undertakings remain possibilities rather than promised results.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Sun/Zeus through fortunate undertakings and successful direction of effort. Personal goal pursuit can bring practical benefit, and Witte gives the successful leader as a clear example. His separate procreation statement remains a historical reproductive claim rather than a guarantee. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
