## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The urge to create or to do something. To lead in a fight and win. A firecracker. To be an incendiary.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 162, supplied PDF p. 80, Sonne + Zeus, mars row (eight clauses). Translation from the German scan:

A man's work with fire; a person's beginning of work; the man's fiery actions; human will to perform; personal will to perform; a leader's vigor; activity in a leadership center; the man's act of procreation.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Creative intention becomes an urge to act.
- psychology: The desire to create or do something is explicit.
- body/health: ABC includes procreative action as historical doctrine.
- social/relationship: Witte names leading a fight and winning.
- events/manifestations: Work with fire, a firecracker, and an incendiary are concrete examples.
- conflicts/notes: Constructive action and incendiary action are different expressions.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC explicitly places vigor and activity in a leadership setting; productive, combative and incendiary examples remain distinct alternatives.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Sun/Zeus as the urge to turn creative intention into action. Work, energetic performance, and leading a fight are source expressions; Witte also gives firecracker and incendiary examples, so the active impulse includes both productive and destructive possibilities. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
