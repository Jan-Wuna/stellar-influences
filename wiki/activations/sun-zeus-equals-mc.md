## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be fit to be a leader. To display might. To be creatively active. To perceive a conflagration. A dynamic personality.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Source: ABC printed p. 162, supplied PDF p. 80, Sonne + Zeus, mc row (nine clauses). Translation from the German scan:

A fiery soul in the body; I produce a body; a person's inner fire; my leadership center; my performance center; my personal goal; my daily goal; my personal efforts; my personal achievements.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Creative activity and leadership become personal capacities.
- psychology: Witte explicitly describes a dynamic personality.
- body/health: ABC names a fiery soul in the body and producing a body without a medical claim.
- social/relationship: Fitness to lead and a display of might are named.
- events/manifestations: Creative activity and perceiving a conflagration are Witte's examples.
- conflicts/notes: The entry does not require a public career interpretation.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC's bodily and inner-fire imagery accompanies personal aims and achievement without specifying a physiological mechanism or requiring a public career.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Sun/Zeus as personal capacity for creative activity and leadership, expressed in Witte's dynamic personality and display of might. ABC's personal aims, daily goal, efforts and achievements give the effort a personal direction; its inner fire and fiery soul in the body remain source imagery. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
