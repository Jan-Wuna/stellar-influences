## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 113.

To be forced or to feel oneself pressed to increase efficiency. Education for leadership.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 163 (printed); PDF page: 80.

Development of a leader, founding a development centre, development of fire during the day, personal creative unfolding, a man's change through procreation, growing personal achievements, and changes in a creative man.

Translation checked against the German scan, PDF p. 80; the original gendered associations are retained.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development under pressure to increase achievement and learn leadership.
- psychology: Feeling pressed to become more effective.
- body/health: ABC includes procreation in its historical male wording; no diagnosis is established.
- social/relationship: Leadership education and personal development.
- events/manifestations: Growing achievement, creative change, and founding a development centre.
- conflicts/notes: The older childcare wording mistranslated Führungs-Person; the scan supports leader.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in the meanings summarized here; their distinct examples do not establish a direct contradiction or independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Zeus = Pluto` describes creative development that increases a person's effectiveness and capacity to lead. The pressure to improve and education for leadership give a direction to the growth, rather than change alone being the central meaning. ABC adds growing achievement and founding a development centre; its procreation language is historical and gendered. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 113; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 163, PDF p. 80.)
