## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 113.

Parting from the father. Fanatics with perseverance. A leader suffering heavy losses. Losses by fire. Destroyed by fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 163 (printed); PDF page: 80.

A leader's tasks, the beginning of bodily problems, a person's losses through fire, leading older people, patient leadership of people, concentration on personal goals, a creative older man, and personal efforts in old age.

Translation checked against the German scan, PDF p. 80; the original gendered associations are retained.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Leadership and creative effort under duties, losses, or age-related limits.
- psychology: Perseverance, patience, and concentration; Witte also names fanaticism.
- body/health: ABC names bodily problems and effort in old age, as historical source associations.
- social/relationship: Parting from the father and leading older people.
- events/manifestations: Leadership losses, fire losses, and persistent personal effort.
- conflicts/notes: Duty and patient concentration coexist with destructive loss; these are different expressions, not one inevitable outcome.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in the meanings summarized here; their distinct examples do not establish a direct contradiction or independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Zeus = Saturn` places creative leadership under the demands of endurance, responsibility, and limitation. Patient work toward personal goals belongs alongside the harsher examples of separation and losses, so the formula cannot be reduced to an inability to continue. The references to bodily problems, older people, and male creativity remain historical source associations. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 113; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 163, PDF p. 80.)
