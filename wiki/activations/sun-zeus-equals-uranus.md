## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 113.

To back others suddenly. To usurp leadership. The suddenly procreating man. Danger of burns. Explosions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 163 (printed); PDF page: 80.

Beginning of bodily renewal, a dynamic leader, an excited fiery person, a creative person in technology, a nervous creative man, a new leader, a new goal for the day, a technical performance centre, and surprising procreation during the day.

Translation checked against the German scan, PDF p. 80; the original gendered associations are retained.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Abrupt creative action, new leadership, and technical initiative.
- psychology: Excitement and nervousness around creative action.
- body/health: Burns and explosions appear in Witte; bodily renewal and procreation in ABC are historical associations.
- social/relationship: Sudden backing of others, usurping leadership, or a new leader.
- events/manifestations: Technical achievement, a new daily goal, and sudden action.
- conflicts/notes: The entry ranges from productive initiative to hazardous disruption; no event follows inevitably.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in the meanings summarized here; their distinct examples do not establish a direct contradiction or independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Zeus = Uranus` gives creative action and leadership a sudden, excited, or technically inventive expression. A new leader or goal and abrupt support of others share the theme of initiative arriving quickly; usurping leadership, burns, and explosions mark its more disruptive examples. The bodily and procreative wording is attributed historical doctrine, not a prediction of health or fertility. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 113; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 163, PDF p. 80.)
