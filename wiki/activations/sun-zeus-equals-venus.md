## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 113.

To be fit for propagation (male, female). To create with devotion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 162 (printed); PDF page: 80.

A man's fiery love, a celebratory fire during the day, a harmonious beginning to the day, a centre for artistic achievement, personal procreation through love, personal striving for harmony, a person's striving for beauty, and a creative person in the arts.

Translation checked against the German scan, PDF p. 80; the original gendered associations are retained.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creation with devotion, affection, harmony, and beauty.
- psychology: Devotion and striving for harmony or beauty.
- body/health: Witte's reproductive fitness and ABC's procreation language are historical claims, not fertility assessments.
- social/relationship: Fiery love and devoted creative expression.
- events/manifestations: Artistic creation, a harmonious beginning, and celebration.
- conflicts/notes: The scan corrects love witness, harmony strike, and gentleness stretching; the sources retain historical gender wording.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in the meanings summarized here; their distinct examples do not establish a direct contradiction or independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Zeus = Venus` presents creative activity as something pursued with love and devotion. Striving for harmony and beauty connects the artistic examples with the affectionate ones, rather than making this merely a pursuit of pleasure. Both entries also use historical procreation language, which should remain attributed and cannot establish fertility. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 113; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 162, PDF p. 80.)
