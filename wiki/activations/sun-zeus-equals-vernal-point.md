## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creative leadership and achievement in public life.
- psychology: No separate psychological description is supplied.
- body/health: No separate health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public leadership; Witte adds military rank and the general staff.
- events/manifestations: Public creative work, performance centres, and fire imagery.
- conflicts/notes: Military status is one source example, not a requirement for this formula.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in the meanings summarized here; their distinct examples do not establish a direct contradiction or independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Zeus = Vernal Point` brings creative work and leadership into public life. The shared thread is visible activity by a leader or creative person, extending to public performance centres; military rank and the general staff are narrower examples within that range. ABC also retains fire as a public manifestation. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 113; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 162, PDF p. 80.)
