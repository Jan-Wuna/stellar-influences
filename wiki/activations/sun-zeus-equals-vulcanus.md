## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 113.

Mighty leadership. To command great energies.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 163 (printed); PDF page: 80.

Influence through personal striving for a goal, an influential leadership centre, fire power during the day, a man's strenuous exertions, the strength of a creative person, a man's creative energy, strong leadership of people, and a man's procreative power.

Translation checked against the German scan, PDF p. 80; the original gendered associations are retained.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Command of great energy and powerful creative leadership.
- psychology: Strong personal striving and strenuous effort.
- body/health: Procreative power occurs in ABC's historical male wording; no health assessment follows.
- social/relationship: Influence and strong leadership of people.
- events/manifestations: Commanding energies and forceful creative achievement.
- conflicts/notes: Strong person welfare mistranslated Personen-Führung; the scan supports leadership of people.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in the meanings summarized here; their distinct examples do not establish a direct contradiction or independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Zeus = Vulcanus` describes creative leadership with the strength to command considerable energy. Personal striving becomes influential action or an influential centre, while strenuous effort supplies the physical side of that emphasis. ABC's male and procreative wording remains historical source language, and the entry does not make forcefulness inherently beneficial. ([Witte](#witte-entry), PDF p. 113; [ABC](#abc-entry), printed p. 163, PDF p. 80.)
