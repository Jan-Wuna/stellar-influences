## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 241.

#### Witte Entry

Surprisingly sudden great need and want. Sudden grave illness caused by lack and privation. Grave stomach and intestinal ailments. To detect and uncover secrets.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 385; PDF p. 191.

#### ABC Entry

A dangerous blockage event, deficient circulation or cycle dynamics, disadvantages through technical restrictions, sudden grief through depression, sudden criminal suppression, sudden deepening into the past, surprising consolidation through renunciation.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Need, privation and dangerous blockage, alongside uncovering what was hidden.
- psychology: ABC names grief and depression; the excerpts do not supply a broader personality account.
- body/health: Witte's serious stomach and intestinal ailments and illness through privation remain historical source associations.
- social/relationship: ABC includes criminal suppression; no particular intimate bond is specified.
- events/manifestations: Technical restrictions, looking into the past, uncovering secrets and consolidation through renunciation.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's Kreislauf wording can concern circulation or a cycle; it does not establish a specific circulatory diagnosis.

## Contradictions

The entries share hardship and restriction, but also retain disclosure of secrets and consolidation through renunciation. These latter claims should not be reduced to illness. The short excerpts do not establish a causal link between every technical restriction and bodily harm.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Admetos = Hades`, Hades specifies interruption through deprivation, dangerous blockage or a restricted condition. [Witte](#witte-entry) adds uncovering secrets; [ABC](#abc-entry) includes looking into the past and consolidation through renunciation, so the range also contains discovery and a firmer condition after giving something up. Witte's illness and digestive-ailment claims and ABC's depression wording remain attributed source material, without establishing a diagnosis.
