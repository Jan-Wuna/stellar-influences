## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 241.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden change in the authority. To get a different government. To be transferred to different responsibilities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 385; PDF p. 191.

#### ABC Entry

Directing impulses through psychic energy, excitement through suppression by authorities, new state restrictions, new impulses of concentration or compression, independent rotational technology, an independent technical cycle, unrest in the state through restrictions.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: A change of authority or responsibility, with state restrictions and independent technical operation.
- psychology: Excitement and unrest in response to suppression or restriction.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Government, authorities and assigned responsibilities are explicit social settings.
- events/manifestations: Governmental change or transfer of responsibilities; independent rotational technology and a technical cycle.
- conflicts/notes: Suppression by authorities is the source phrase; it does not establish the character of every authority.

## Contradictions

Witte describes changes in who governs or carries responsibility. ABC gives restriction and unrest, but also independent technical systems. The entries do not state that every governmental change is suppressive or that a technical cycle causes political unrest.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Admetos = Kronos`, Kronos specifies interruption through authority, responsibility and control. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a sudden change of government or a transfer to different responsibilities. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds suppression by authorities and unrest through restrictions, while also naming directing impulses and independently operating rotational technology or a technical cycle. These are distinct applications of changed or constrained control, without a necessary political outcome.
