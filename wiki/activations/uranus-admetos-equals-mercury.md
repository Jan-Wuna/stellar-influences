## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 241.

#### Witte Entry

The thinking is clouded by grave enmity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 384; PDF p. 191.

#### ABC Entry

Reports about rotational technology, concern caused by depressed young people, blocked media technology, a dynamic cycle of movement, restricted impulses of movement, thorough thoughts about technology, nervousness in a traffic jam, a sudden traffic blockage.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Thinking, movement or communication under hostility or restriction.
- psychology: Thought clouded by grave enmity; concern and nervousness appear in ABC.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is supplied; restricted movement is not developed into a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Grave enmity and concern involving depressed young people are the stated interpersonal settings.
- events/manifestations: Reports on rotational technology, blocked media and traffic congestion.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's technical and traffic meanings remain literal alternatives, without a supplied causal link to enmity.

## Contradictions

Witte's enmity is a condition affecting thought. ABC includes concern, restricted movement and literal technical or traffic blockage, without making hostility the cause of every delay. Thorough technical thought and a dynamic movement cycle remain beside the restrictions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Admetos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies interruption through thought, communication and movement. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes thinking clouded by grave enmity; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds concern, restricted movement, blocked media and traffic congestion, while retaining thorough technical thought and reports on rotational technology. The shared thread is an intellectual or communicative process encountering constraint, with literal technical settings preserved.
