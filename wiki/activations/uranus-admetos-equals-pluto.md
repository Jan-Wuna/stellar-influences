## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 241.

#### Witte Entry

The beginning of a development which finally leads to clarity and a clear cut solution. Sudden drastic change.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 385; PDF p. 191.

#### ABC Entry

A disquieting change in circulation or a cycle, dynamic development of circulation or a cycle, restrictive technical development, the end of an exciting change, consolidation of a new development, thorough development of reform, deepening into new developments.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: A drastic change or development that may reach clarity and consolidation.
- psychology: ABC describes a disquieting change; Witte allows clarity and a clear-cut solution.
- body/health: The circulation/cycle wording is ambiguous and establishes no specific bodily condition.
- social/relationship: No particular relationship is specified.
- events/manifestations: Restrictive technical development, reform and consolidation, as well as the end of an exciting change.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's restrictive technical development was formerly replaced by a repeated circulation phrase.

## Contradictions

Witte describes a development eventually reaching clarity. ABC also retains restriction, consolidation and an ending, without saying that every development ends successfully. Its circulation/cycle expressions remain distinct from the explicitly technical clause.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Admetos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies an abrupt interruption as a development or drastic change. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes an opening that eventually reaches clarity and a clear-cut solution. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds restrictive technical development, reform, consolidation and deeper engagement with new developments, while also allowing an exciting change to end. Clarity is one stated outcome, alongside restrictions that the source does not erase.
