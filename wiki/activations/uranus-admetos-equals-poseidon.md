## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 241.

#### Witte Entry

To suddenly gain clarity of a matter through investigation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 385; PDF p. 191.

#### ABC Entry

Restriction of new ideas, intellectual renewal through psychic energy, thorough cultural renewal, sudden restriction of truth, surprising thorough clarity, surprising investigation into truth.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Investigation can bring sudden clarity, while ideas or truth may also be restricted.
- psychology: The focus is intellectual clarification and renewal, without a wider personality claim.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Restrictions of ideas and truth appear without an identified authority or relationship.
- events/manifestations: Cultural renewal, intellectual renewal and investigation into truth.
- conflicts/notes: ABC retains both restriction and clarity; renewal is not the former emanation and investigation is not enlargement.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes the clarity gained through investigation. ABC includes that possibility alongside restrictions on truth and ideas. These are different conditions of inquiry; the excerpts do not establish that every restriction ends in revelation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Admetos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies interruption or consolidation through investigation of truth. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes a matter suddenly becoming clear through inquiry. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds intellectual and cultural renewal, but keeps restrictions on ideas and truth beside thorough clarity. The supported thread is inquiry that can deepen understanding, without making clarity inevitable whenever thought is constrained.
