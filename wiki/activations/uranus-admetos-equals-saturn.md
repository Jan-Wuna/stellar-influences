## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 241.

#### Witte Entry

Enmities which start suddenly and are of long duration. Hindrances and obstacles through the same. Sudden coming of death.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 385; PDF p. 191.

#### ABC Entry

Depression through sudden separation, restrictive impulses in old age, excitement over thoroughgoing losses, firmness through renewed patience, new losses through a strike, sudden disturbances of circulation or a cycle, deepening into technical tasks.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Sudden separation or hostility with lasting hindrances, alongside patience and technical concentration.
- psychology: Depression after separation, excitement over loss and firmness through renewed patience.
- body/health: Witte's death association remains historical; ABC's depression and circulation/cycle terms do not establish a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Suddenly beginning, long-lasting enmity and separation are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Losses through a strike, sudden circulation/cycle disturbance and deepening into technical tasks.
- conflicts/notes: Renewed patience is a distinct stabilizing claim, rather than the former patient-emulation wording.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes enduring enmity, obstacles and sudden death. ABC adds loss and depression but also firmness through renewed patience and deeper technical engagement. The stabilizing claims do not explain away the severe historical claim or establish a mortality prediction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Admetos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies interruption through separation or an obstacle that persists. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes suddenly arising, long-lasting enmity. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds losses, restriction and depression after separation, but also firmness through renewed patience and deeper work on technical tasks. Witte's sudden-death association remains attributed historical wording, without determining a personal outcome.
