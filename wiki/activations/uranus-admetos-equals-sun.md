## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 241.

#### Witte Entry

Days of physical menace. Sudden physical violent commotions and explosions where the life is endangered. Earthquake, broken surface of the earth newly ploughed land, plowed fields. The man who destroys, breaks down, turns something upside down or who plows the land.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 384; PDF p. 191.

#### ABC Entry

Excitement of a depressed person, restriction of personal impulses, physical restriction through nervousness, sudden depression of a man, personal deepening into technology, surprising personal consolidation.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Personal or bodily restriction, with a separate possibility of deeper technical engagement and consolidation.
- psychology: Nervousness, depressed feeling and restricted impulses appear in ABC.
- body/health: Witte describes life-endangering physical violence; ABC associates physical restriction with nervousness. These remain attributed claims.
- social/relationship: Both entries use historical male examples without limiting the formula to men.
- events/manifestations: Witte includes ploughed land and earth turned over as well as violent commotion; ABC adds technical deepening.
- conflicts/notes: The six-clause ABC source passage replaces a dash-only cached entry; bodily danger is not its only supported emphasis.

## Contradictions

Witte's physical-danger examples differ sharply from ABC's depressed or restricted impulses and personal consolidation. Witte also includes literal ploughing and turned earth, which must not be flattened into violence. Neither excerpt supplies a diagnosis or a criterion for selecting an event.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Admetos = Sun`, Sun specifies interruption in personal or bodily experience. [ABC](#abc-entry) describes restricted impulses and nervousness, while also allowing deeper involvement in technology and unexpected personal consolidation. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives severe physical commotion and danger, but also literal ploughing or turning earth over. Those concrete and historical examples remain visible without making bodily harm inevitable or treating the male examples as universal.
