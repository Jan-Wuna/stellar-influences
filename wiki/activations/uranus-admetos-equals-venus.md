## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 241.

#### Witte Entry

To wrong or to injure others or to be wronged. Acts or actions which are very much against tact.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 384; PDF p. 191.

#### ABC Entry

A blocked peace event, restricted artistic impulses, deepening new love, a sudden end to harmony, sudden concentration of harmony, surprising consolidation of peace, a preference for deepening into technology.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Harmony or peaceful relations can be injured, blocked, deepened or consolidated.
- psychology: A preference for deeper technical engagement is stated; no broader temperament is supplied.
- body/health: Witte's injuring-others wording is not developed into a specific medical condition.
- social/relationship: Being wronged, tactless acts, deeper new love and strengthened peace remain distinct possibilities.
- events/manifestations: Restricted art, an end to harmony or a surprising consolidation of peace.
- conflicts/notes: The source includes both disrupted and intensified harmony; neither establishes a uniformly adverse love outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes wrongdoing, injury and lack of tact. ABC also gives blocked peace and an end to harmony, but retains deepening love, concentrated harmony and strengthened peace. This material range is preserved without making harm the necessary route to affection.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Admetos = Venus`, Venus specifies interruption or consolidation in harmony, affection and art. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes tactless acts and doing or suffering wrong. [ABC](#abc-entry) preserves restricted artistic impulses and a sudden end to harmony, yet also allows new love to deepen and peace to become firmer. Its separate preference for deeper technical engagement remains another stated expression, without a causal story linking it to relationship harm.
