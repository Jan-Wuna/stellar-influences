## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 241.

#### Witte Entry

To be separated from the general public through such violent events. The general public is under the impression of violent commotions or events.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 384; PDF p. 191.

#### ABC Entry

A blockage event in public, a new circulation or cycle in public life, restrictive impulses in public life, thorough reform in public, a technical blockage in public, public unrest through a strike.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Public interruption or restriction, with a distinct possibility of thorough reform.
- psychology: The public is affected by commotion in Witte; ABC includes unrest.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Separation from the general public and collective unrest are explicit.
- events/manifestations: A strike, technical blockage, public reform and a new circulation or cycle in public life.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's six-clause public passage replaces the former symbol-only cache; reform remains alongside disruption.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes separation and the public impression of violent events. ABC includes public blockage and unrest through a strike, but also thorough reform and a new public cycle. The entries give no named political event or inevitable violent outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Admetos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies interruption on a public scale. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes separation from the public or a public strongly affected by violent commotion. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds technical blockage, restriction and unrest through a strike, while retaining public reform and a new circulation or cycle in public life. Collective disruption and reform remain distinct possibilities within the same public setting.
