## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 241.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden end, or beginning of grave suppression. To fight suppression. To fight for freedom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 385; PDF p. 191.

#### ABC Entry

Dynamic power of psychic energy, influential renewal of knowledge, the end of a new power, successful power of technology, strength through new knowledge, sudden spread of force or violence, technical energy science.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: A sudden change in suppression or power, including resistance and the use of knowledge or technology.
- psychology: Fighting suppression is stated; no broader psychological profile is supplied.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is given.
- social/relationship: Suppression and fighting for freedom are explicit social claims.
- events/manifestations: The beginning or end of suppression, the end of a new power, and technical energy science.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes technical success and knowledge as well as force/violence; its science term is not energy consciousness.

## Contradictions

Witte allows suppression to begin or end and includes resistance to it. ABC adds power through knowledge and technology as well as the end of a new power and the spread of force or violence. These are different power conditions, not a guaranteed liberation narrative.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Admetos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies interruption through a change in the exercise of power. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes severe suppression starting or ending and the fight against it. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds the end of a new power, strength through knowledge and successful technical power, alongside a sudden spread of force or violence. Technical energy science remains a concrete field in the entry; neither freedom nor suppression is the sole necessary outcome.
