## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 241.

#### Witte Entry

Surprising start of a difficult task. To enforce the start of work in the face of grave obstacles.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 385; PDF p. 191.

#### ABC Entry

Enduring technical creativity, a restricted new beginning, thorough leadership impulses, thorough technical achievements, reshaping through psychic energy, a new thorough setting of aims, suddenly beginning depression.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Beginning difficult work despite restriction, with sustained technical creativity.
- psychology: Endurance and sudden depression are distinct ABC associations.
- body/health: Depression wording remains attributed without establishing a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Leadership impulses are named, without a specified leader or relationship.
- events/manifestations: Enforcing the start of an obstructed task, technical achievement and new aims.
- conflicts/notes: The full seven-clause ABC row extends beyond the former two-clause cache; it includes both achievement and difficulty.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes forcing a difficult task to begin. ABC adds endurance, technical achievement, leadership and new aims, but also a restricted beginning and suddenly arising depression. The entries do not promise that effort necessarily removes the restrictions or depression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Admetos = Zeus`, Zeus specifies interruption through the effort to begin or shape work despite obstacles. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes enforcing the start of a difficult task. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds enduring technical creativity, thorough achievement, leadership impulses and new aims, while preserving a restricted beginning and sudden depression. The theme is a constrained undertaking that may still be shaped deliberately, without guaranteeing its outcome.
