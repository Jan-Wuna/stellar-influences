## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 240.

#### Witte Entry

Others are under tension, in cheerful excitement and expectation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 382; PDF p. 190.

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of local tensions, successful technology nearby, new partner experiences, new environmental knowledge, sudden environmental success, technical environmental experiences, surprising experiences with the partner, and restless trade on site

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Expansion of local experiences and tension through others.
- psychology: Cheerful excitement, expectancy and tension are explicit.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: New or surprising experiences with a partner.
- events/manifestations: Local technical success, new environmental knowledge and restless trade.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's cheerful expectation and ABC's growing tensions can coexist.

## Contradictions

The entries place excitement and tension among other people or in the immediate surroundings. ABC adds local technology, trade and partner experience; these do not guarantee that every anticipated event succeeds.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Apollon = Asc`, Asc specifies expanding experience through other people and the immediate surroundings. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes others' cheerful excitement and expectation. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds new experiences with a partner, technical success nearby and new environmental knowledge, while retaining growing local tensions and restless trade. Shared anticipation is therefore accompanied by both opportunity and unrest.
