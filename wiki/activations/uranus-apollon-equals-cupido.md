## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 240.

#### Witte Entry

A community, family or partners are under tension. Sudden expansion in art. To create something new in science or art. Astrological association.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 383; PDF p. 190.

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of tensions in the community, renewal of science as a whole, shared impulses toward freedom, renewing shared experiences, new scientific communities, new technical knowledge, technical successes for the whole.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Shared tension and innovation in a family, community or association.
- psychology: Tension is stated without a fuller personality account.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Family, partners, scientific communities and an astrological association are explicit.
- events/manifestations: New art or science, renewed shared experience and technical success for a wider whole.
- conflicts/notes: The source's scientific renewal and shared freedom impulses are distinct from a guaranteed harmonious group.

## Contradictions

Witte links community tension with new work in art or science. ABC adds scientific renewal and shared freedom impulses. Their constructive examples do not remove the explicitly expanding tensions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Apollon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies expansion and discovery as a shared undertaking. [Witte](#witte-entry) places family or community tension beside creating something new in art or science and an astrological association. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds renewed shared experiences, new scientific communities and technical success for the whole. Innovation is collective here, with tension remaining part of the supported range.
