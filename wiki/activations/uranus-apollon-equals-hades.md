## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 240.

#### Witte Entry

Tenseness and excitement through base actions or vile acts. Degradation of a new science. Ancient or historical scientific study. Hamburg School of Astrology.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 383; PDF p. 190.

#### ABC Entry

Extension of dangerous tensions, lack of impulses of science, new knowledge of the past, sudden expansion of danger, technical successes of the past, surprising experiences in need, and hidden freedom pulses

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Tension around harmful acts or danger, with a separate historical-study theme.
- psychology: Excitement and tension through base acts; experiences in need in ABC.
- body/health: The excerpt names danger without a specific bodily diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Witte names degradation of a new science and the Hamburg School of Astrology.
- events/manifestations: Historical scientific study, knowledge of the past and its technical successes.
- conflicts/notes: Historical inquiry and harmful conduct are separate claims; no causal link is supplied.

## Contradictions

Witte juxtaposes base acts, degradation of new science, historical study and the Hamburg School without explaining their relationship. ABC likewise combines danger with knowledge of the past. The sources do not make a named school inherently harmful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Apollon = Hades`, Hades specifies expanding experience through difficult material or the past. [Witte](#witte-entry) includes tension through base acts, but separately names ancient or historical scientific study and the Hamburg School of Astrology. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds past knowledge and technical successes beside danger and experiences in need. These branches remain distinct; historical inquiry is not evidence of wrongdoing.
