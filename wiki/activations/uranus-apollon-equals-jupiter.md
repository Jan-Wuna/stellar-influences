## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 240.

#### Witte Entry

To be happily tense and excited. Happy excitement through successes in astrology.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 382; PDF p. 190.

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of the technical sciences, success through new knowledge, joy over new knowledge, nervousness in the money business, new commercial law, sudden legal success, surprising commercial advantages, advantages through technical experience.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Happy success through new knowledge, technical experience or legal and commercial affairs.
- psychology: Joy and happy excitement, with nervousness in the money business.
- body/health: Nervousness is an attributed experience, without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Commercial law and legal success supply the social setting.
- events/manifestations: Expansion of technical sciences and commercial advantages; Witte specifically names astrological success.
- conflicts/notes: Advantages are source claims rather than an assurance of money or success.

## Contradictions

Both entries favor success and excitement, while ABC retains money-related nervousness. Witte's astrological example is narrower than ABC's technical, legal and commercial range; neither guarantees a result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Apollon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies expansion of experience through success and the joy of learning. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes happy excitement, particularly through success in astrology. [ABC](#abc-entry) broadens this to technical sciences, new knowledge and legal or commercial advantages, while retaining nervousness in the money business. Knowledge and experience support the favorable theme without establishing a guaranteed gain.
