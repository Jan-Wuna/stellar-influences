## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 240.

#### Witte Entry

Tense scientific, peaceful or commercial activity. Astrological activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 382; PDF p. 190.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting peace work, successful reform activity, successful technical activity, a successful work rhythm, renewal of commercial activity, creating new knowledge, surprising success at work, increased tensions at work.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Active scientific, peaceful, commercial or technical work.
- psychology: Excitement and work tension are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Peace work and commercial activity supply collective settings.
- events/manifestations: Reform, renewed trade, technical achievement and creating knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: Successful work and increased tension are both stated; success need not mean ease.

## Contradictions

Witte describes tense work, including astrology. ABC supplies successful reforms and technical activity but also increased tension at work. These are compatible emphases without establishing a necessary profession or result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Apollon = Mars`, Mars specifies expansion and knowledge through active work. [Witte](#witte-entry) names scientific, peaceful, commercial and astrological activity. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds reform, technical work, renewed commerce and creating new knowledge. Successful rhythm and surprising achievement remain accompanied by tension, so productive activity is supported without an assumption of effortless success.
