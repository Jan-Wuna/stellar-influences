## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 240.

#### Witte Entry

To cause tenseness of many. To be under tension with many. To look for coming experiences with great interest and tenseness. Interest in new things. To suddenly gain experiences. To be an astrologer.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 382; PDF p. 190.

#### ABC Entry

Successful emotional impulses, a great emotional surprise, my new knowledge, my sudden success, my sudden freedom, my technical experiences, my increasing excitement, new knowledge of the soul, increasing emotional tensions.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Personal openness to new experience, knowledge and freedom amid collective tension.
- psychology: Interest, anticipation, surprise and increasing emotional excitement.
- body/health: Emotional tension is stated without a specific medical condition.
- social/relationship: Being tense with many people or causing their tension.
- events/manifestations: Sudden experience or success, technical experience and Witte's astrologer example.
- conflicts/notes: Great emotional surprise replaces the former claim of psychic superiority.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes interest and collective expectation. ABC adds emotional surprise, knowledge, freedom and success, while retaining increasing tension. These claims do not establish superiority over other people.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Apollon = MC`, MC specifies expanding experience as a personal encounter with something new. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes intense interest, anticipation and sharing or causing the tension of many people. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds emotional surprise, new knowledge, technical experience and sudden freedom or success. Personal excitement can grow with the opportunity; it is not a claim of psychic superiority.
