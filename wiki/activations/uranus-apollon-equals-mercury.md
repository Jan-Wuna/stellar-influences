## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 240.

#### Witte Entry

Astrological thinking. Interesting conversation. Fascinating story teller. Talent of narrating.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 382; PDF p. 190.

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of transport technology, reports about new knowledge, a successful rhythm of movement, new thoughts in science, surprising news of success, restless trade reports, increasing tensions with young people.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: New knowledge conveyed through engaging thought, reports and conversation.
- psychology: Fascinating narration and new scientific ideas.
- body/health: A rhythm of movement is mentioned without a bodily diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Conversation and increasing tension with young people.
- events/manifestations: Transport technology, trade reports and unexpected news of success.
- conflicts/notes: Astrological thought is Witte's named field; technical reports broaden the range without replacing it.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes conversation and narrative talent. ABC adds transport, science and trade reports, including unrest and tension with young people. Interesting communication is not necessarily free of strain.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Apollon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies expansion of experience through thought and communication. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives interesting conversation, storytelling and astrological thinking. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds reports of new knowledge, scientific ideas and unexpected success news, with transport technology as a concrete field. Restless trade reports and tension with young people remain beside the engaging exchange.
