## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 240.

#### Witte Entry

Persons who are high-strung and tense in expectation of an event. People and the public excited and under tension. Female engineer or astrologer.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 382; PDF p. 190.

#### ABC Entry

Excited masses of people, impulses toward freedom in a people, new knowledge for a woman, sudden experiences of a woman, technical experiences of a people, surprising success of a woman, much unrest through feelings, increasing emotional excitement.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Individual and public excitement in expectation of new experience.
- psychology: Being high-strung, anticipation and increasing emotional arousal.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: The public and historical female engineer/astrologer examples are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Technical experience, new knowledge and surprising success among gendered examples.
- conflicts/notes: Gefühle means feelings; the source does not mention refugees in this row.

## Contradictions

Witte and ABC both describe excited people and expectation, with ABC also retaining unrest through feeling. Their explicitly gendered examples remain historical associations, without making a profession or experience universal to women.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Apollon = Moon`, Moon specifies expanding experience through excited feeling and public anticipation. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes people tense before an event and names a female engineer or astrologer. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds new knowledge, technical experiences and surprising success, while retaining unrest and increasing emotional excitement. Its examples concerning women remain attributed historical formulations; the unrest clause concerns feelings, not refugees.
