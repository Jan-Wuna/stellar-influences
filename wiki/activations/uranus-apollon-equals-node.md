## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 240.

#### Witte Entry

To be connected with people who are under tense excitement and expectation. Sudden expansion of connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 382; PDF p. 190.

#### ABC Entry

Successful connections with technology, new trade connections, new connections in science, a sudden successful encounter, restless trade connections, a connection with new knowledge, increasing tensions in connections.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Connections expand through technology, trade, science or a new encounter.
- psychology: Shared excitement and tense expectation.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: New or widening connections and contact with expectant people.
- events/manifestations: Successful encounters, technical contacts and new commercial or scientific links.
- conflicts/notes: Success and increasing tension coexist; the quality or duration of a connection is not determined.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes links with excited, expectant people and sudden expansion. ABC adds technical, scientific and trade settings, retaining restless contacts and increasing tension rather than uniformly easy associations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Apollon = Node`, Node specifies expanding experience through connections. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes suddenly widening links with people under excited expectation. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds successful encounters and new connections in technology, trade, science and knowledge. Their spread can carry restlessness or rising tension as well as success, without determining the bond's permanence.
