## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 240.

#### Witte Entry

The generality, in expectation of coming things, waits with tenseness for the decisions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 382; PDF p. 190.

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of tension in public, technical successes in public, new knowledge for the general public, sudden experiences in public life, unrest in public trade, economic renewal in public life.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Public expectation and expanding tension around coming developments.
- psychology: Collective anticipation of decisions.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: The general public and public trade are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Public technical success, new knowledge and economic renewal.
- conflicts/notes: Economic renewal is the source phrase; it does not by itself establish recovery from a prior downturn.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes tense public waiting for decisions. ABC adds technical success and economic renewal alongside trade unrest. Their conjunction does not establish a named public event or a uniformly favorable economy.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Apollon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies expanding experience on a public scale. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes collective tension while decisions are awaited. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds new knowledge, public technical success and economic renewal, while preserving unrest in public trade. The public can face opportunity and increasing tension together, without a predetermined political or economic result.
