## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 240.

#### Witte Entry

To lead and direct energies. To create tension. Aimed and directed scientific problems.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 383; PDF p. 190.

#### ABC Entry

Beginning expansion of trade, dynamic leadership authority, experienced technical leadership, successful technical goal-setting, shaping a new science, new creative knowledge, sudden spread of fire, technical successes in knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Directed energies and technical or scientific aims.
- psychology: Intentional direction and creating tension.
- body/health: No particular bodily condition is given.
- social/relationship: Leadership authority and trade are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Technical goal-setting, shaping a science and fire spreading.
- conflicts/notes: Leadership replaces the former authorship/filtration corruption; literal fire remains a separate example.

## Contradictions

Witte's directed energies and scientific problems correspond to ABC's leadership and goal-setting. ABC also retains literal fire spreading; the entries do not establish that deliberate scientific work must be destructive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Apollon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies expansion through direction: energy is led toward a scientific or technical aim. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes directed problems and created tension. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds experienced technical leadership, new creative knowledge and shaping a new science, with trade expansion and the spread of fire as further examples. A directed undertaking is supported without a guaranteed achievement or destructive outcome.
