## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 88.

#### Witte Entry

Others show great interest for the native. Acquaintances who are interested in astrology. Astrological customers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 117; PDF p. 57.

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of technology at the location, impulses toward success from the partner, successful environmental technology, trade events with others, a place of technical knowledge, surprising experiences with the partner, increased unrest at the location, scientific reform of the environment.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Interest from others and expanding technical or commercial experience nearby.
- psychology: Others show interest; local unrest may increase.
- body/health: No bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Interested acquaintances, astrological customers and partner experiences.
- events/manifestations: Technical expansion, environmental technology and scientific environmental reform.
- conflicts/notes: Increased unrest is the source phrase, rather than a claim that unrest is nurtured.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes others' interest and astrological contacts. ABC extends the setting to technology, trade and environmental reform, while preserving unrest. Increased contact need not be either uniformly calm or uniformly disruptive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Asc = Apollon`, Apollon specifies unexpected interaction through others' interest and a wider field of local experience. [Witte](#witte-entry) names interested acquaintances and astrological customers. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds technical knowledge, partner experiences, trade and scientific reform of the environment. Technical opportunity and a wider reach coexist with increased local unrest.
