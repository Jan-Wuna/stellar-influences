### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/ASCENDANT With Itself`

#### Munkasey Entry

Focusing on your need for relating to others, or for understanding how you can exist with others even though their ways of doing things are very different from yours; impacts your ability to find any personal privacy.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Asc = Asc`, Asc specifies interaction with others through the practical question of coexistence: how to relate when other people's ways differ greatly from one's own. The sole [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry) also connects this focus with the ability to find personal privacy. Contact and private space remain the linked concerns; the short passage does not supply a wider personality, health or event claim.
