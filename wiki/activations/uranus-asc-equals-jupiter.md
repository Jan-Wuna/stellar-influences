## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 88.

#### Witte Entry

Happiness (good fortune) with or of other people. Financial advantage through others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 116; PDF p. 57.

#### ABC Entry

Excitement at a place of money, successful events in the surroundings, successful environmental technology, a joyfully excited partner, a joyful surprise through the partner, impulses of good fortune at the location, sudden good fortune at the location, tensions at a place of law.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 275; PDF p. 277; entry code `0980`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Ability to quickly come in contact with others and to show one's joy about it, fortunate rearrangement of the environment, optimistic attitude. Successful cooperation or teamwork.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. 108 (exact Jupiter entry; chapter starts on PDF p. 107).

#### Sandbach Entry

To express one’s enthusiasm in an uninhibited, erratic and/or impulsive fashion. One who is self-taught, and/or has one’s own way of learning. To be excessive as a means of asserting one’s independence.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `320`

#### Munkasey Entry

A fortunate set of circumstances where others appreciate your innovation and uniqueness; conceiving new approaches to solving social or legal problems; creates new opinions which are contrary to accepted social norms.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).

- core meaning: Fortunate contacts, cooperation and appreciation of unusual approaches.
- psychology: Optimism and joy coexist with uninhibited or excessive enthusiasm.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Teamwork, financial advantage through others and appreciation of individuality.
- events/manifestations: Environmental rearrangement, technical success and new social or legal solutions.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's excess and erratic expression qualify the favorable contact theme; novelty is not an assurance of success.

## Contradictions

Witte and Ebertin emphasize good fortune through others and cooperation, while Sandbach includes erratic enthusiasm and excess used to assert independence. Munkasey adds appreciation of uniqueness and opinions contrary to social norms. These emphases retain both cooperation and nonconformity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Asc = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies unexpected contact through optimism, cooperation and favorable responses from others. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) support fortunate encounters or teamwork; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds partner joy and successful local or technical events. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) extends this to appreciation of innovation and new social or legal approaches. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) preserves a qualification: enthusiasm may be erratic or excessive when independence is asserted, so favorable exchange does not require uniform conformity or moderation.
