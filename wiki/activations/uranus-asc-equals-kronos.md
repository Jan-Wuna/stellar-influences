## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 88.

#### Witte Entry

To be implicated in an event by the state. The execution of orders.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 117; PDF p. 57.

#### ABC Entry

A special event at the location, great tensions with the partner, sudden superiority of the partner, reform of the environmental authority, state environmental technology, a state event at the location, surprising independence of the partner, an important place of technology.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Local events shaped by state action, authority or a partner's position.
- psychology: Great tension with a partner.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Orders, the state and a partner's superiority or independence are explicit.
- events/manifestations: State events, reform of an environmental authority and technical locations.
- conflicts/notes: Surprising independence replaces the former ubiquitous-independence wording.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes state implication and executing orders. ABC adds local administration, technical settings and a partner's independence or superiority. These are distinct authority relations rather than proof of a uniformly dominant partner.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Asc = Kronos`, Kronos specifies unexpected events involving others through authority and responsibility. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes state involvement and the execution of orders. [ABC](#abc-entry) places state or administrative events in a local setting, while also retaining a partner's sudden superiority, surprising independence and considerable tension. Neither state involvement nor a partner's position establishes one necessary outcome.
