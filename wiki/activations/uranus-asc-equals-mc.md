## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be excited by or over other people. Psychological influence due a restless environment.

#### ABC Entry

Achieving minute in the environment, troubled partncr seclc, dynamic partner schele, my nervousness in the environment, my restless partner, mental arousal in the place, rhythm rneins partners, scelic partner impulses, and mental tensions in one place

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A restless character changing his or her objectives in life and constantly searching for new stimuli and contacts. Excitement, making changes, cooperation in the technological and industrial sphere.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s spiritual path involves overcoming the restrictions of labels, and creatively and experimentally playing with identities. One's career involves a unique or distinctive way of doing things.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

A draw to using astrology for understanding self or others; fulfillment of your aims even though unexpected changes occurred; agitation for an increase in safety and protection for all; experience prevails over disruption.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Uranus/Asc = MC`: visible originality, mission driven by freedom, and the capacity to embody a new direction before it is socially validated. Main caution: unstable alignment: abrupt personal shifts can unsettle career continuity and public standing.
