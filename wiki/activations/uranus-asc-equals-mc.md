## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 88.

#### Witte Entry

To be excited by or over other people. Psychological influence due a restless environment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 116; PDF p. 57.

#### ABC Entry

An exciting minute in the surroundings, a partner's excited soul, a partner's dynamic soul, my nervousness in the surroundings, my restless partner, psychological excitement at the location, my partner's rhythm, emotional partner impulses, emotional tensions at a location.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 276; PDF p. 278; entry code `0985`.

#### Ebertin Entry

A restless character changing his or her objectives in life and constantly searching for new stimuli and contacts. Excitement, making changes, cooperation in the technological and industrial sphere.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. 108 (exact entry; chapter starts on PDF p. 107).

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s spiritual path involves overcoming the restrictions of labels, and creatively and experimentally playing with identities. One's career involves a unique or distinctive way of doing things.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `320`

#### Munkasey Entry

A draw to using astrology for understanding self or others; fulfillment of your aims even though unexpected changes occurred; agitation for an increase in safety and protection for all; experience prevails over disruption.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 88), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 116; PDF p. 57), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) (p. 276, entry 0985), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) (exact PDF p. 108), and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (p. 320).

- core meaning: Encounters with a restless environment affect personal aims and identity.
- psychology: Excitability, a search for new stimuli, and experiments with identity are explicit.
- body/health: Nervousness is named in ABC; no specific physical condition is established.
- social/relationship: Other people can excite or unsettle the person and introduce new contacts.
- events/manifestations: Changing objectives, distinctive work, technical cooperation, and fulfilling aims despite changes are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Munkasey's experience prevailing over disruption qualifies the emphasis on restlessness.

## Contradictions

Changing objectives and fulfilling an aim despite changes describe different responses to disruption. ABC's complete nine-clause entry describes personal nervousness, a partner's inner state and rhythm, and local emotional tensions; the former damaged fragments have been resolved against the scan.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Uranus/Asc through the effect of people and surroundings on personal direction: a restless environment stimulates changing objectives, new contacts, and experiments with identity. A distinctive way of working can grow from this search, while experience can also help a person fulfill aims through unexpected changes. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).)
