## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 88.

#### Witte Entry

Dealing with enlightenment and propaganda.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 117; PDF p. 57.

#### ABC Entry

Dynamic partner ideas, mental tensions with the partner, clear partner impulses, clarity for environmental technology, a cultural event at the location, new partner culture, surprising insights of the partner, rational technology at the location, truth about environmental technology.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 88) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 117; PDF p. 57).

- core meaning: Encounters involve enlightenment, propaganda, ideas, and sudden insight.
- psychology: Mental tension and surprising insight are both named in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: A partner's ideas and impulses make the encounter intellectually or culturally active.
- events/manifestations: Cultural events and discussion of environmental technology are explicit settings.
- conflicts/notes: Enlightenment and propaganda are both preserved; the entry does not establish that a persuasive message is true.

## Contradictions

Witte's enlightenment and propaganda describe different possible uses of ideas. ABC names clarity, rational technology and truth as themes; these do not establish that any particular partner's insight or persuasive message is reliable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Uranus/Asc through ideas encountered with other people: enlightenment or propaganda, mental tension with a partner, and surprising insights can give an event its intellectual character. Cultural encounters and questions of clarity or truth in environmental technology are further settings named in ABC; the wording does not make every such message reliable. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
