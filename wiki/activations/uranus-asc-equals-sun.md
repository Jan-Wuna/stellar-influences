## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 88.

#### Witte Entry

Exciting events with others. To be menaced by others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 116; PDF p. 57.

#### ABC Entry

Events with other people, a new man as partner, physical restlessness of the partner, the place and day of an event, personal excitement in the surroundings, personal events with others, tensions with a male partner, a technical center at the location.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 275; PDF p. 277; entry code `0975`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Physical unrest, mobility, nervousness. A restless colleague or partner. An environment full of danger. Cooperation in the sphere of technology or industry.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. 107–108 (exact Sun entry spans the page break).

#### Sandbach Entry

Unusual and/or unique means of going about the fulfilling of one’s goals. A person who feels it is important to be themselves and to live under as few restraints as possible.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `320`

#### Munkasey Entry

Emphasizes your ability to be a unique person; brings notoriety for achievements or inventions in electro-mechanical fields; friendship with leaders and important persons; prompts you to try to suppress unpredictable urges.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 88), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 116; PDF p. 57), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) (p. 275, entry 0975), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) (exact PDF pp. 107–108), and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (p. 320).

- core meaning: Personal individuality is expressed in exciting encounters and unusual ways of pursuing goals.
- psychology: A wish for few restraints accompanies restlessness and attempts to suppress unpredictable urges.
- body/health: Ebertin names physical unrest and nervousness; these are attributed correspondences, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: A restless partner, technical cooperation, and friendships with leaders are named.
- events/manifestations: Exciting events and recognition for electromechanical inventions or achievements are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte and Ebertin's danger/menace claims remain distinct from the achievement and self-expression themes.

## Contradictions

Personal freedom, cooperation and danger in the environment are different possibilities. ABC's first clause concerns other people, while its later male-partner examples remain historical source wording; they do not limit every encounter in this formula to men.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Uranus/Asc through a person's direct involvement in exciting encounters and the wish to pursue goals in an individual way. Technical cooperation and recognition for unusual inventions are supported expressions of this involvement, alongside restlessness or efforts to contain unpredictable urges. ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).) Witte and Ebertin also describe menace or danger from the environment; that separate historical theme is not implied by every instance of originality. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry).)
