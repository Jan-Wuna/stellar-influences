## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 88.

#### Witte Entry

To fall in love suddenly.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 116; PDF p. 57.

#### ABC Entry

Harmonic partner impulses, art event on the spot, love surprise on the spot, new love partner, beauty impulses for the environment, tensions with the love partner, unrest in a place of peace, restless art place, and preference for environmental technology

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 275; PDF p. 277; entry code `0978`.

#### Ebertin Entry

The response of feelings to environmental influences, desire to beautify one's environment as quickly as possible. Communication of one's feelings to others, tendency to take the line of least resistance with regard to the growth of an attachment, a love union.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. 108 (exact Venus entry; chapter starts on PDF p. 107).

#### Sandbach Entry

One is admired or found attractive due to one’s unique or unusual personality. A love of relationships that are unusual, different, or full of surprises. One finds beauty in anything that is unusual or experimental. A love of relating in an unpredictable manner.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `320`

#### Munkasey Entry

Softens the impact of a harsher side of self which can be very disruptive to others you meet; brings an appreciation of the individuality in others; study and observation of what makes a good social relationship between others.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 88), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 116; PDF p. 57), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) (p. 275, entry 0978), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) (exact PDF p. 108), and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (p. 320).

- core meaning: Sudden attraction and appreciation of individuality enliven relationships.
- psychology: Feelings respond quickly to surroundings and find beauty in unusual or experimental things.
- body/health: No bodily condition is described.
- social/relationship: Attraction to an unusual personality and a softer impact on others are supported.
- events/manifestations: Falling in love, a love union, art events, and beautifying surroundings are examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's partner tensions coexist with harmonious impulses; the entries do not establish inevitable instability of attachment.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Surprise and unpredictability can be attractive while individual encounters still contain tension. The stated possibilities do not establish how long an attachment will last.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies Uranus/Asc through attraction to individuality: a sudden love contact or an unusual personality can make a relationship appealing, and beauty may be found in experimental forms. Appreciation of another person's distinctiveness can also soften a disruptive manner and help feelings be communicated. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).) ABC retains both harmonious partner impulses and tensions; neither establishes the duration of the attachment. ([ABC](#abc-entry).)
