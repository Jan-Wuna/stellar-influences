## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 88.

#### Witte Entry

General meeting for a special reason or occasion. To be confronted with natural powers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 116; PDF p. 57.

#### ABC Entry

Events with others in public, excited partner in public, new public partners, public place of technology, public environmental technology, public environmental event, and tensions at a public place

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 88) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 116; PDF p. 57).

- core meaning: A special encounter or event takes place in a public setting.
- psychology: ABC names excitement and tension without describing a stable personality trait.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Meetings, public partners, and events with other people are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Public technology settings and a meeting for a special occasion are examples; Witte also names natural powers.
- conflicts/notes: Confrontation with natural powers is a separate possibility, not a definition of every public meeting.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The public encounter theme and Witte's confrontation with natural powers concern different settings or manifestations; the short entries do not specify their circumstances.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Uranus/Asc as an encounter visible in public: a meeting for a special occasion, events with other people, or excitement and tension around public technology. Witte also names confrontation with natural powers; that distinct possibility broadens the setting without making every public gathering a dangerous event. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
