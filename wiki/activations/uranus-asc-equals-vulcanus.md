## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 88.

#### Witte Entry

Intensified excitement, through other people, in the home or at a certain location.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 117; PDF p. 57.

#### ABC Entry

Unsettling environmental impact, energy of a dynamic partner, local violence event, technical environmental energy, local power stresses, new partner influence, strongly excited partner, and strong tensions in the environment

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 88) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 117; PDF p. 57).

- core meaning: Other people or a particular place become the focus of intensified excitement and influence.
- psychology: Strong arousal and an unsettling impression are explicit.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is described; ABC includes a local violent event among its correspondences.
- social/relationship: A dynamic partner's energy or influence can create strong tensions.
- events/manifestations: Excitement in the home, technical energy, and local power tensions are examples.
- conflicts/notes: The range includes energetic influence and violence; one does not establish the other.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. ABC extends the intensity theme into technical energy and power tensions; its violent-event example remains an attributed possibility rather than a universal outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Uranus/Asc through the strength of an encounter's impact: other people, especially a dynamic partner, can intensify excitement or tension at home or in a particular place. Technical energy and local power tensions are additional settings in ABC, which also gives a violent event as a stronger possible manifestation. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
