## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 88.

#### Witte Entry

To be under the compulsion of others. To make fire in the surrounding.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 117; PDF p. 57.

#### ABC Entry

Strenuous environmental technology, beginning environmental reform, excited local leadership, a fire event in the environment, the partner's leadership achievements, sudden efforts at a location, surprising tensions with the partner, unrest through fire at the location.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 88) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 117; PDF p. 57).

- core meaning: Compulsion, strenuous effort, or fire gives an encounter its forceful character.
- psychology: The entries name pressure and unrest without describing a stable personal disposition.
- body/health: No bodily condition is supplied; fire is an event setting rather than a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Being under others' compulsion, local leadership, a partner's leadership achievements and surprising partner tensions are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Making fire, environmental reform, and effortful technical work are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Local leadership and a partner's leadership achievements are explicit; the row does not describe running services.

## Contradictions

Compulsion by others and exertion around fire or technical work are different manifestations. ABC additionally names local leadership and a partner's leadership achievements, alongside effort and tension; none establishes a fixed personal disposition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Asc = Zeus`, Zeus specifies interaction through compelled or strenuous activity in one's surroundings. Other people may exert pressure, while technical work, reform or fire demands effort and produces unrest. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies compulsion and making fire; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds local leadership and a partner's leadership achievements alongside surprising tension. These remain distinct expressions rather than a fixed trait.
