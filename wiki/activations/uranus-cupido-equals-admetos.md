## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

To suddenly withdraw from a community. Great loss of persons in a community or in families.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 375; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

enduring tensions in of a community, restricted technical community, excitement of a blocked community, shared deepening into technology, suddenly depressed community, and surprising suppression of a community

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 375; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: A community's sudden change becomes withdrawal, loss, restriction, or sustained tension.
- psychology: ABC describes a community as depressed; it does not establish an individual's diagnosis.
- body/health: Witte's great loss of persons is unspecified and does not by itself identify a cause or bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Withdrawal, blocked participation, or suppression affect communal or family life.
- events/manifestations: Shared concentration on technology is another named expression alongside community restriction.
- conflicts/notes: A group deepening its technical work is not equivalent to a group losing members.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The entries name different forms of contraction or concentration in a community; the loss of persons has no stated cause and must not be expanded into one.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Uranus/Cupido as a community facing restriction or withdrawal: an abrupt departure, loss of members, or suppression can leave sustained tension in the group. ABC also names shared deepening into technology, a narrower concentration of communal activity that should remain distinct from loss or exclusion. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).)
