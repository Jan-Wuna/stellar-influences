Dynamic trading community, success through technical knowledge, a successful technical community, a successful art event, shared new experiences, new artistic successes, new knowledge for the community, increasing impulses toward the whole.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 375; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: Collective expansion takes the form of large gatherings, shared experience or technical and artistic success.
- psychology: New experience and knowledge concern the group; no individual temperament is specified.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied by these entries.
- social/relationship: Mass weddings, an astrology club and a mass meeting are explicit forms of association.
- events/manifestations: Technical knowledge supports success; artistic events and new art are further examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's mass gatherings and ABC's successful communal work are separate branches, without a guarantee of success.

## Contradictions

Mass marriage, an astrology club and technical success are different expressions of collective activity. None establishes that every group is large or successful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Uranus/Cupido through collective participation and the widening of shared activity. A mass meeting, astrology club or many sudden wedding decisions gives this a social form in [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry) gives it a practical form through technical knowledge, successful art and new experiences shared by the community. These branches connect growth in participation with growth in what the group knows or accomplishes, without making success inevitable.
