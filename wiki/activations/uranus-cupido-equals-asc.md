## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden weddings of others. To find oneself suddenly in a different company.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 374; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

Shared partner rhythm, an art event at the location, a tense partner community, the partner's artistic impulses, art and technology at the location, tensions in a community at the location, a local technical community, environmental impulses of a community.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 374; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: Unexpected communal changes are encountered through other people or at a particular location.
- psychology: Partner and community tension is named, without establishing a fixed temperament.
- body/health: The entries give no specific bodily or medical correspondence.
- social/relationship: Another person's wedding or suddenly finding oneself in different company changes one's social setting.
- events/manifestations: Local art events, art joined with technology and a technical community are explicit settings.
- conflicts/notes: Shared rhythm and tension are alternative qualities of participation; neither describes every group.

## Contradictions

Sudden changes of company and tension within an existing community are different situations. The artistic and technical examples do not imply a wedding.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Uranus/Cupido through the people and setting immediately encountered. Others may marry suddenly, or one may unexpectedly find oneself in different company, while [ABC](#abc-entry) places art, technology and shared rhythm in a local partner or community setting. Tension can accompany that participation without defining every encounter. The emphasis remains on a change in one's social surroundings, as described by [Witte](#witte-entry), rather than an assumed personal marriage.
