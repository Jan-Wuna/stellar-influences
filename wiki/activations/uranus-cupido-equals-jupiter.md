## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden marriage contracted happily. Happy times in married life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 374; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

Success of a technical community, a successful art event, an excited legal community, shared excitement over money, impulses of a legal community, reform successes of a community, unrest in a financial community, advantage for art and technology.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 374; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: Communal change can bring happiness, artistic or technical success, and advantages.
- psychology: Excitement over money is shared; the entries do not establish a general optimistic temperament.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Witte describes a happily contracted sudden marriage and happy married life.
- events/manifestations: Legal-community impulses, successful reform, art events and financial-community unrest are named.
- conflicts/notes: Financial unrest remains alongside success; the formula does not guarantee wealth or universal harmony.

## Contradictions

Happy marriage and successful collective work are compatible but distinct. ABC's financial unrest qualifies any reading of uniformly fortunate communal affairs.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Uranus/Cupido through a welcome communal development: a sudden marriage may be happy, while a group may succeed in technical work, art or reform. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).) The same ABC entry also names shared excitement over money and unrest in a financial community. The synthesis therefore concerns shared advantage or happiness with an active financial or legal setting, without promising that every collective change brings gain.
