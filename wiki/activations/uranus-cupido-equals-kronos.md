## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Marriage accomplished through the power of the state. To enter an association by the order of the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 375; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

Special tensions in the community, dynamics of a hierarchical community, a major art event, a hierarchical technical community, new dominant art, a restless state community, a state reform community.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 375; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: Communal change is organized through state power or a hierarchy.
- psychology: Tension and restlessness describe the collective setting rather than an individual personality.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Marriage through state power and entry into an association by state order are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Hierarchical technical groups, state reform and a major artistic event are examples.
- conflicts/notes: State authority, artistic prominence and communal tension are different themes; authority does not establish consent or harmony.

## Contradictions

The entries do not establish a direct contradiction. They distinguish formal state intervention from hierarchy or dominant art within a community.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Uranus/Cupido through the authority under which people come together. [Witte](#witte-entry) names marriage or association through the power or order of the state; [ABC](#abc-entry) describes hierarchical technical groups, state reform and communal tension. A major art event or newly dominant art gives this a further cultural expression. The organizing role of authority is shared across these examples, while the entries leave the community's response open.
