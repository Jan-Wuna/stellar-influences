### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 374; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

Efforts toward new art, dynamic communal work, creating a shared rhythm, impulses for artistic work, artistic activity in technology, tensions in a work community, creating a shared rhythm, a technical work community.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 374; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: Shared work, artistic effort and establishing a common rhythm activate communal life.
- psychology: Effort and tension are explicit; the entries do not establish a universal aggressive disposition.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Witte names sudden unpleasant scenes in married life; ABC includes tensions among coworkers.
- events/manifestations: New artistic work and art within technical activity are named expressions.
- conflicts/notes: The repeated shared-rhythm phrase is retained as printed; cooperative work and marital conflict remain distinct.

## Contradictions

Witte's unpleasant marital scene and ABC's constructive communal work differ in emphasis. Group effort is not evidence that conflict must follow.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Uranus/Cupido as active work within a shared undertaking: people put effort into new art, create a common rhythm or combine artistic and technical activity. [ABC](#abc-entry) includes tensions within that work community, so joint effort can involve friction. [Witte](#witte-entry) separately describes sudden unpleasant scenes in marriage. These are different expressions of activity in shared life, rather than a required progression from cooperation to quarrel.
