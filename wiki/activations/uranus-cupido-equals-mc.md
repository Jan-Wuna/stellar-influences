### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 374; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

A soulful art event, an excited community of souls, shared soul impulses, my impulses toward the whole, my new community, my new art, my technical community, a new community of souls, soulful artistic impulses.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 374; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: The person becomes directly involved in a new union, community or artistic undertaking.
- psychology: Soulful artistic impulses and a community of souls give participation an inner, emotional emphasis.
- body/health: No specific bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Witte names sudden marriage or betrothal; ABC names one's new community.
- events/manifestations: One's own art and technical community are explicit fields of involvement.
- conflicts/notes: The soul language remains source wording; it does not establish a spiritual status or require a marriage.

## Contradictions

Sudden betrothal and involvement in art or a technical community are distinct possibilities. ABC's soulful language is not a medical or metaphysical conclusion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Uranus/Cupido through personal involvement in a shared life or undertaking. Sudden marriage or betrothal is [Witte's](#witte-entry) example; [ABC](#abc-entry) also names one's new community, art and technical association. Its repeated soul language emphasizes inward participation and artistic impulse. Together these passages describe a communal change that becomes personally meaningful, while leaving its form open between intimate union, art and technical work.
