## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Being congratulated. Telegrams, emails of congratulations. To converse about new things in art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 374; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

A report on an art event, a dynamic youth community, an excited transport community, shared experiences in youth, new news from a community, a technical media community, discussing technical synthesis.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 374; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: Communication circulates congratulations, artistic news and discussion within shared activity.
- psychology: The entries describe discussion and shared experience rather than a fixed mental trait.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Congratulations and shared experiences in youth connect participants.
- events/manifestations: Art reporting, a transport community, technical media and discussion of technical synthesis are named.
- conflicts/notes: Congratulation, youthful experience and technical discussion remain separate examples; no event is guaranteed.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in subject matter rather than contradicting one another: a congratulatory message and discussion of art or technical synthesis serve different communal occasions.

## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies Uranus/Cupido through the messages and conversations around shared events. Congratulations mark a communal occasion, while reports about art and discussion of technical synthesis carry new material through a group. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).) Youthful shared experiences, transport and technical media add further settings. The common thread is communication within communal activity, without assuming that every message announces a marriage.
