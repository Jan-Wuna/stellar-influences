## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Women who are married quickly. Persons who decide quickly to enter a certain association or company. Suddenly forming a company in the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 374; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

An excited community of a people, emotional impulses of a community, artistic impulses of a woman, a nervous women's community, new folk art, a new feeling of community, women in a technical community, a folk-art event.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 374; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: Sudden association is accompanied by shared feeling, folk art or activity involving women.
- psychology: Collective excitement, nervousness and a new feeling of community are named.
- body/health: Nervousness is attributed to a group; no individual diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Witte names women marrying quickly and people deciding quickly to enter an association.
- events/manifestations: Public company formation, folk-art events and women in a technical community are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Female-person and people's-community examples retain their historical scope without defining every possible participant.

## Contradictions

Quick entry into an association and a new feeling of belonging are compatible but different claims. The female examples do not limit all forms of this activation to women.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Uranus/Cupido through the feeling accompanying a sudden association. People may quickly enter a group, while [ABC](#abc-entry) describes collective excitement, nervousness or a new feeling of community. Folk art and women's participation give that feeling concrete cultural and social settings. [Witte's](#witte-entry) quickly married women are a narrower historical example alongside public company formation; they do not define the full scope of the formula.
