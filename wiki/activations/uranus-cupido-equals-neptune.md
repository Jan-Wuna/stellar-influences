## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Marriage separated by death. Signs of dissolution in a community. Dissolution of a revolutionary union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 375; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

A dynamic community of the future, subtle-material impulses in a community, shared air technology, artistic water technology, sudden dissolution of a community, weakness of a nervous community, unknown tensions in a community.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 375; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: A community's cohesion becomes uncertain, weakens or dissolves.
- psychology: Unknown tensions and collective nervousness are named; no individual disorder is established.
- body/health: Witte's separation through death is a historical correspondence, not a medical forecast.
- social/relationship: Marriage, a community and a revolutionary union are each named as settings of dissolution.
- events/manifestations: ABC separately includes a future community, subtle-material impulses, shared air technology and artistic water technology.
- conflicts/notes: Technical and subtle-material themes are separate from the historical claims about death or dissolution.

## Contradictions

Witte's separation through death is more specific and severe than ABC's weakness or uncertain tension. Its technical examples remain separate from dissolution.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Uranus/Cupido through uncertainty about a community's cohesion: unknown tensions, weakness or sudden dissolution may affect the union. [Witte](#witte-entry) also names marriage separated by death, a stronger historical claim that should remain attributed and conditional. [ABC](#abc-entry) supplies separate future-oriented, subtle-material, air-technology and artistic water-technology themes. These broaden the possible communal setting without making a technical project a cause of loss.
