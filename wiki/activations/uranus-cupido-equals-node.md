## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Connection leading to quick marriage. To decide quickly upon a union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 374; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

An encounter through a shared event, relationships with new art, dynamic connections in a community, shared impulses of a connection, joining art with technology, a connection to a technical community.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 374; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: A shared event or new art creates a connection that may develop into a union.
- psychology: Shared impulses are named, without establishing a fixed personal temperament.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Witte describes a connection leading to quick marriage or a quick decision on union.
- events/manifestations: A shared event, contact with a technical community and joining art with technology are examples.
- conflicts/notes: A connection to art or technology is not by itself a promise of marriage.

## Contradictions

Rapid union and connections formed through collective artistic or technical activity describe different degrees and forms of association.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Uranus/Cupido through the connections formed around a shared event. A quick decision to unite or marry is [Witte's](#witte-entry) intimate expression; [ABC](#abc-entry) also describes relationships with new art and a technical community. Joining art with technology gives this connecting activity a practical form. The common theme is a link becoming active within communal life, without requiring every shared event to produce marriage.
