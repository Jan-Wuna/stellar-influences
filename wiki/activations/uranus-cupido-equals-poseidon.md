## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Founding, establishing or entering an association which pursues its goal in philosophy, propaganda or culture.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 375; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

A dynamic cultural community, spiritual renewal of a community, a shared cultural event, clarity in a spiritual community, new spiritual art, surprising truth for a community, rational impulses toward the whole.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 375; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: Association forms around philosophy, propaganda, culture or spiritual renewal.
- psychology: Clarity, surprising truth and rational impulses are named themes, without proving any belief true.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Founding, establishing or entering a purpose-driven association is explicit in Witte.
- events/manifestations: A shared cultural event and new spiritual art give the association concrete activities.
- conflicts/notes: Propaganda remains beside philosophy and culture; spiritual renewal does not establish moral superiority or reliable insight.

## Contradictions

Clarity, truth and propaganda are different possible uses of shared ideas. Their presence as themes does not validate a particular message.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Uranus/Cupido through people coming together around ideas and culture. [Witte](#witte-entry) names founding or joining an association devoted to philosophy, propaganda or culture; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds spiritual renewal, shared cultural events and new spiritual art. Clarity or a surprising truth can animate this collective purpose, but the source's inclusion of propaganda keeps the reliability of any particular message open.
