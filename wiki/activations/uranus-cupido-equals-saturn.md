## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Suddenly separated marriage or union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 375; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

Shared technical tasks, shared losses through technology, new tasks in art, sudden losses of a community, a separation event in a community, disturbances and tensions in a community.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 375; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: Shared life faces separation, loss, disturbance or new collective tasks.
- psychology: Tension describes the community; no individual temperament or disorder is specified.
- body/health: The losses are unspecified and do not establish death or a bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Witte describes a suddenly separated marriage or union.
- events/manifestations: Shared technical tasks, losses through technology and new artistic tasks are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Working through collective tasks and losing a union are alternative situations, not an inevitable sequence.

## Contradictions

Witte concentrates on sudden separation while ABC also names continuing shared technical or artistic tasks. Loss is not given a universal cause.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Uranus/Cupido through a disruption in shared life: a union may separate suddenly, or a community may face loss and tension. [ABC](#abc-entry) also names shared technical tasks and new tasks in art, so communal responsibility remains part of the picture alongside the separation emphasized by [Witte](#witte-entry). Losses through technology are a specific source example, not a reason to assume that every joint technical task ends in loss.
