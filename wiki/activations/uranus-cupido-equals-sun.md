## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Quick decision to marry. Sudden events in married life. Exciting days or events for a community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 374; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

A dynamic art center, an excited community of persons, impulses of a men's community, an art event on the day, personal artistic impulses, a shared bodily rhythm, a technical synthesis center, a center for new art.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 374; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: Sudden decisions or exciting communal events take a personal and concrete form.
- psychology: Personal artistic impulses are named; no general temperament is established.
- body/health: ABC's shared bodily rhythm is retained without expansion into a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Witte names a quick decision to marry and sudden events in married life.
- events/manifestations: An art center, an artistic event and a technical synthesis center are examples.
- conflicts/notes: The men's-community example is source-specific; it does not restrict all communal participation to men.

## Contradictions

A personal marriage decision and a community's artistic or technical event are distinct expressions. Shared bodily rhythm remains unspecified.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Uranus/Cupido through direct participation in an exciting shared event. A quick marriage decision or sudden event in married life is [Witte's](#witte-entry) example; [ABC](#abc-entry) also places personal artistic impulses within an art center, a technical synthesis center or a shared bodily rhythm. These passages make communal change concrete in persons and activities, while the historical men's-community example remains one narrower setting.
