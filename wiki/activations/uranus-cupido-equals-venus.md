## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden expressions of love in married life. Sudden harmonious partnership.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 374; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

A dynamic art community, a shared art event, a shared experience of love, a shared preference for surprises, impulses toward harmony in a community, sudden peace in a community, a surprising love community.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 374; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: A communal surprise can bring love, harmony or peace.
- psychology: A shared liking for surprises accompanies artistic and affectionate impulses.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Sudden expressions of love in marriage and a harmonious partnership are explicit.
- events/manifestations: Shared art events, shared love experiences and sudden communal peace are named.
- conflicts/notes: Harmony is the theme of these entries, without establishing permanent agreement or a guaranteed union.

## Contradictions

A surprising love relationship, an artistic community and sudden peace are distinct expressions of harmony. None establishes how long the harmony lasts.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies Uranus/Cupido through a welcome surprise in shared life: affection can emerge suddenly, or a community can find harmony and peace. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives this an intimate form in married love and harmonious partnership; [ABC](#abc-entry) joins it to art, a shared love experience and a liking for surprises. The overlap is affectionate or artistic participation that brings people together, without promising that the harmony will be permanent.
