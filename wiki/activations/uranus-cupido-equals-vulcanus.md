## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Community or married life is jarred or jolted.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 375; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

Influential impulses of a community, the power of a technical community, new strength in a community, tensions in a power community, surprising force or violence of a community, unrest in a powerful community.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 375; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: Communal power or strength produces a jolt, tension or unrest.
- psychology: The entries describe collective force and disturbance rather than a fixed individual character.
- body/health: No bodily injury or diagnosis is specifically supplied.
- social/relationship: Witte describes community or married life being jarred or jolted.
- events/manifestations: A technical community's power, new collective strength and surprising force or violence are named.
- conflicts/notes: Gewalt retains force and violence as possible senses; neither every jolt nor every powerful group is violent.

## Contradictions

Influence, new strength, collective violence and a jolt in married life are different expressions. The source does not make them equivalent or inevitable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Uranus/Cupido through the force of a shared undertaking. A technical community may exert power or gain new strength, while tensions and unrest can accompany that power. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes the effect as community or married life being jolted; [ABC](#abc-entry) also allows a surprising expression of force or violence. That stronger possibility stays distinct from ordinary collective influence.
