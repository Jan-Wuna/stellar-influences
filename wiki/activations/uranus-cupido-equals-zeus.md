## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 236.

#### Witte Entry

Serious marital row. Steadfastness. Strong resistance. Fire in large establishments caused by explosions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 375; PDF p. 186.

#### ABC Entry

A dynamic leadership community, fiery communal impulses, shared technical leadership, shared procreative impulses, creative artistic impulses, new shared goals, a new artistic direction, a technical achievement community, shaping technical synthesis.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 236) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 375; PDF p. 186).

- core meaning: A community directs energy toward shared goals, creation or strong resistance.
- psychology: Steadfastness and resistance coexist with fiery communal impulses.
- body/health: ABC names procreative impulses without establishing fertility or a medical outcome.
- social/relationship: Witte includes a serious marital quarrel; ABC includes shared technical leadership.
- events/manifestations: Artistic direction, technical achievement and synthesis are explicit; Witte separately names explosion-caused fire in large establishments.
- conflicts/notes: Creative cooperation and severe quarrel or fire are distinct source examples, not one expected chain of events.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses resistance, marital conflict and explosion-caused fire, while ABC also describes purposeful creative or technical cooperation. Neither branch cancels the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Uranus/Cupido as a community concentrating energy on shared goals. Technical leadership, artistic creation and new directions give that energy a constructive form in [ABC](#abc-entry), alongside its procreative language. [Witte](#witte-entry) adds steadfast resistance, serious marital conflict and the separate example of fire caused by explosions in large establishments. The synthesis holds purposeful communal effort beside these more disruptive expressions without treating either as inevitable.
