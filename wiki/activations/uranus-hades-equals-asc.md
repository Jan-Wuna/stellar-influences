## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

To have to deal with low class people. Obliged to make oneself responsible for ugly deeds of others. Assassination of well- known persons. Others commit wicked deeds, the native has to set matters straight.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 376; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous events at the location, disadvantages through technology at the location, adverse partner tensions, new privations for the partner, the partner's sudden renunciation, technical deficiency at the location, restless service to the partner, outdated environmental technology.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 376; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Difficult or dangerous circumstances are encountered through other people or a particular place.
- psychology: Adverse partner tension and restless service are named, without establishing character.
- body/health: Witte's assassination example is a severe historical event correspondence, not a health forecast.
- social/relationship: Responsibility for setting right others' harmful deeds is explicit in Witte.
- events/manifestations: Local technical deficiency, outdated environmental technology and a partner's privations or renunciation are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's low-class label is historical evaluative language, not an equation of social rank with wrongdoing.

## Contradictions

Setting matters right after another person's acts differs from causing them. Witte's class label and assassination example remain attributed, while ABC also describes technical and partner difficulties.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Uranus/Hades through difficulties in one's immediate surroundings or dealings with others. A partner's privations, local technical deficiencies and outdated environmental technology make this concrete in [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry) also describes having to set matters right after others' harmful acts. Its class-based label and assassination example retain their historical attribution. Neither supplies a general judgment of the people encountered or a necessary violent outcome.
