## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden impoverishment of families or associations. Social ties to impoverished persons. To die in company of others. Sudden tragedy at a celebration.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 377; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Danger to a technical community, shared excitement over privations, shared impulses from the past, sudden danger to a community, a technical community formed by need, surprising shared privations.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 377; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Distress, deprivation or danger is shared by a family, association or technical community.
- psychology: Shared excitement over privation is explicit; no individual disorder is established.
- body/health: Witte's death in company is a historical correspondence, not a medical forecast.
- social/relationship: Sudden impoverishment and ties to impoverished persons are explicit social themes.
- events/manifestations: A technical community formed by need, shared past impulses and tragedy at a celebration are distinct examples.
- conflicts/notes: Shared need, technical work and communal tragedy remain distinct; deprivation does not make tragedy inevitable.

## Contradictions

A community formed through need, sudden impoverishment and tragedy at a celebration are different situations. Their shared social setting does not make one an explanation of another.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Uranus/Hades as a difficulty shared by a family or association: impoverishment, privation or danger affects people together. [ABC](#abc-entry) includes technical communities and shared impulses from the past, alongside a community formed by need. [Witte](#witte-entry) also names death in company or tragedy at a celebration, stronger historical examples that remain distinct from a community formed by need or shared deprivation.
