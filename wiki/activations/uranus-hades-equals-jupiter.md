## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

To be lucky in dangerous things or assignments. To avoid or to escape danger. Quick death by murder or accident.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 376; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Success through sudden renunciation, excitement over dirty money, joy in old technology, inadequate legal impulses, a new shortage of money, sudden joy in danger, sudden good fortune in need, unrest through financial disadvantages, hidden financial tensions.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 376; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Good fortune or success can arise amid danger, need or renunciation.
- psychology: Joy in danger and enjoyment of old technology are named, without establishing a universal risk-seeking temperament.
- body/health: Witte also lists quick death by murder or accident; it remains a historical possibility, not a medical forecast.
- social/relationship: Financial disadvantages and hidden financial tension may affect affairs with others.
- events/manifestations: Escape from danger, success through renunciation and new money shortage are explicit alternatives.
- conflicts/notes: Witte places escape and fatal danger together; Jupiter does not guarantee protection.

## Contradictions

Fortunate escape and quick death are materially different outcomes within Witte's entry. ABC likewise places success and joy beside financial lack; no single outcome is established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Uranus/Hades through the possibility of advantage amid difficulty: danger may be escaped, or renunciation may bring success. [ABC](#abc-entry) also names joy in old technology and good fortune in need, alongside financial shortage and hidden tensions. [Witte](#witte-entry) retains the opposing severe possibility of quick death by murder or accident. The source range therefore allows relief or advantage without making protection certain.
