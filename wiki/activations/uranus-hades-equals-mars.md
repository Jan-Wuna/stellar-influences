## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden mean deeds. Sudden damage or death by weapons or other objects. Attempted murder. Death through injuries.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 376; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Excitement over dirty work, efforts concerning technology of the past, dangerous surprises at work, sudden renunciation of work, tensions over lack of work, working on technical secrets, unrest through defective work.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 376; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Work or action encounters danger, deficiency, dirty work or technical material from the past.
- psychology: Agitation and tension over work are named, without establishing violent intent.
- body/health: Witte includes injury-caused death and harm through weapons as severe historical correspondences.
- social/relationship: The entries leave the person's role open between harmful action, exposure to harm and difficult work.
- events/manifestations: Lack of work, renouncing work, old technology and working on technical secrets are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Technical effort and deficient work remain distinct from attempted murder; the latter is not a characterization of the person.

## Contradictions

Witte's weapon and injury claims are more severe than ABC's work-related difficulties. The entries do not establish a progression from technical deficiency to violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Uranus/Hades through difficult or hazardous work and action. [ABC](#abc-entry) describes dirty work, technical secrets, old technology and tension over missing or defective work. [Witte](#witte-entry) adds severe examples of harmful deeds, weapons and fatal injuries. These branches hold ordinary technical difficulty beside exposure to or commission of harm, without assigning a violent intention or forecasting an injury.
