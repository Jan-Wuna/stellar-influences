## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

To suffer from mean and vile acts. To suddenly experience loathsomeness and disgust. To be the cause of death. In danger of being murdered.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 376; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Agitating sorrow of the soul, impulses from my past, my exciting secret, my sorrow with technology, my sudden renunciation, my technical secret, my new disadvantages, sudden sorrow of the soul, hidden emotional tensions.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 376; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Distress, secrets or renunciation become personally experienced.
- psychology: Sorrow, disgust and hidden emotional tension are explicit, without establishing a diagnosis.
- body/health: Witte includes being a cause of death or being in danger of murder as separate severe historical claims.
- social/relationship: The person may suffer others' harmful acts; that role differs from causing harm.
- events/manifestations: Personal past impulses, technical secrets and new disadvantages are named.
- conflicts/notes: Suffering harmful acts and being their cause are distinct roles; the source does not assign one to every instance.

## Contradictions

Witte explicitly allows both suffering harmful acts and causing death. Those roles must remain distinct; ABC's personal sorrow or secrets do not establish either.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Uranus/Hades through personally felt distress: sorrow, an unsettling secret or renunciation gives the difficult event an inward significance. [ABC](#abc-entry) includes one's past and technical concerns in that experience. [Witte](#witte-entry) separately allows suffering harmful acts, feeling disgust, causing death or facing murderous danger. Those sharply different roles remain attributed possibilities; personal sorrow alone does not select one of them.
