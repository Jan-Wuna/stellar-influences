## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

To be helpless or defenseless towards mean attacks. To perish by water. Murder through treacheries.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 377; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Disadvantages through surprising weakness, sudden distress in water, sudden lack of air, tensions through lack of water, surprising danger through water, uncertainty through hidden impulses, refined technical service.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 377; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Weakness, hidden impulses or water-related distress leave a person vulnerable.
- psychology: Uncertainty and defenselessness are named, without establishing a psychological diagnosis.
- body/health: Lack of air and death by water are source correspondences, not a medical forecast.
- social/relationship: Witte describes helplessness before attacks and treachery; no perpetrator role is assigned.
- events/manifestations: Water shortage, refined technical service and sudden distress in water are explicit alternatives.
- conflicts/notes: Refined technical service is a separate nonviolent expression and does not imply drowning or attack.

## Contradictions

Water shortage, helplessness before harmful attacks and refined technical service are different settings. Witte's fatal water example remains more severe than ABC's uncertainty or deficiency.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Uranus/Hades through vulnerability: surprising weakness or hidden impulses can leave circumstances uncertain, and water or air deficiency supplies concrete examples in [ABC](#abc-entry). [Witte](#witte-entry) describes defenselessness against attacks and the stronger historical possibilities of death by water or treachery. Refined technical service remains a separate practical meaning. The overlap concerns reduced security without making any particular danger or medical outcome inevitable.
