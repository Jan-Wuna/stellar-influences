## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

To be suddenly connected with ugly and cunning treachery or a surprisingly setting in need and distress. Connections with murder affairs.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 376; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous events in a connection, disadvantages through technical connections, sudden distress in a connection, sudden renunciation of a connection, a surprising connection with the past, hidden tension in a relationship.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 376; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: A connection becomes involved with distress, danger, renunciation or a hidden tension.
- psychology: The entries describe relational tension without establishing an individual temperament.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is supplied; murder affairs remain an attributed severe event example.
- social/relationship: Witte names sudden involvement with treachery or need, not necessarily responsibility for either.
- events/manifestations: Technical connections, a surprising link to the past and renouncing a connection are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Being connected with a harmful affair does not establish guilt or identify a perpetrator.

## Contradictions

Involvement with treachery, a technical disadvantage and renunciation of a relationship describe different kinds of connection. The source does not assign wrongdoing to every participant.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Uranus/Hades through a connection that brings distress or hidden difficulty. Technical links, a surprising link with the past or sudden renunciation of a relationship make the range concrete in [ABC](#abc-entry). [Witte](#witte-entry) also allows involvement with treachery or murder affairs, severe historical examples that do not by themselves assign guilt. The shared theme is the difficult connection and its consequences, with the person's role left open.
