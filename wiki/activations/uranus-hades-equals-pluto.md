## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

To be the cause or the instigator of mean acts. Denial of changes we hope or wish for.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 377; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Dynamic development from the past, dangerous technical development, deficient technical unfolding, a sudden dangerous change, transformation through sudden renunciation, growing excitement over criminality.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 377; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Change develops from the past through technical difficulty, danger or renunciation.
- psychology: Witte describes frustrated wished-for change; ABC names growing agitation over criminality.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Witte includes instigating harmful acts as one explicit role, without assigning it to every person.
- events/manifestations: Dangerous or deficient technical development and transformation through renunciation are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Being an instigator, experiencing denied change and working with deficient technology are distinct situations.

## Contradictions

Witte includes both active instigation and denial of hoped-for change. ABC's technical development and agitation about criminality do not establish either role in a particular case.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies Uranus/Hades through a difficult process of change: past conditions develop, technical unfolding may be deficient, or sudden renunciation brings transformation. [ABC](#abc-entry) also names growing agitation about criminality. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives two different personal branches, instigating harmful acts and being denied a hoped-for change. Those roles remain distinct from technical difficulty, so the formula alone does not identify a perpetrator.
