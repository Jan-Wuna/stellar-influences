## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

To see through ugly acts. Detection of mean acts, e.g. murder.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 377; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Excitement about culture deficiency, clarity about hidden technology, lack of ideas in technology, cultural impulses from the past, sudden clarity about the past, tensions over sudden truth, and unrest by renunciation of clarity

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 377; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Clarity exposes hidden acts, technology or material from the past.
- psychology: Sudden truth can produce tension, while renouncing clarity produces unrest.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Detection of harmful acts is explicit in Witte; detection differs from participation.
- events/manifestations: Hidden technology, cultural impulses from the past and a shortage of technical ideas are named.
- conflicts/notes: The murder example is a possible subject of detection, not an accusation or a general predictive ability.

## Contradictions

The sources share an emphasis on seeing what was hidden. Cultural lack and technical ideas broaden this beyond the detection of crime.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Uranus/Hades through clarity about what was hidden. [Witte](#witte-entry) names seeing through harmful acts; [ABC](#abc-entry) also makes hidden technology, past cultural impulses and sudden understanding of the past explicit subjects. Truth can create tension, while giving up clarity brings unrest. The emphasis is on disclosure or understanding, with murder retained as a severe source example of what might be detected.
