## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

Separation or losses through murder, ugly and mean cunning.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 377; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Excitement over privations and losses, concentration on technical deficiencies, new problems from the past, an adverse separation event, sorrow through sudden losses, a sudden lack of patience, hidden technical disturbances.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 377; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Loss, separation or technical deficiency requires concentration and brings distress.
- psychology: Sorrow, excitement over loss and a sudden lack of patience are explicit.
- body/health: No diagnosis is supplied; Witte's murder-related loss is a historical event claim.
- social/relationship: Separation may be harmful; Witte also associates losses with deceitful acts.
- events/manifestations: Problems from the past and hidden technical disturbances are separate practical examples.
- conflicts/notes: Technical deficiency and impatience do not establish murder or intentional deceit.

## Contradictions

Witte's loss through murder or cunning is more specific than ABC's general loss and separation. The technical and past-problem themes remain independent meanings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Uranus/Hades through deprivation or loss that brings sorrow and separation. [ABC](#abc-entry) joins this to concentration on technical deficiencies, hidden disturbances and problems returning from the past; a sudden lack of patience gives the experience an emotional edge. [Witte](#witte-entry) separately names loss through murder or deceitful acts. The stronger historical examples stay distinct from ordinary technical difficulty or grief.
