## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

Days on which danger lurks. The man who is suddenly exposed to influences which are injurious to health. To perish by an unusual death. Murder on the day.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 376; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Danger to a technical center, a person's dangerous experience, personal impulses from the past, personal agitation over criminality, sudden danger to a man, surprising personal renunciation, a hidden center of tension.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 376; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Danger or hidden tension becomes a person's direct experience.
- psychology: Personal agitation over criminality and impulses from the past are named.
- body/health: Witte names health-injurious influences and unusual death as historical correspondences, not a diagnosis or forecast.
- social/relationship: The male-person example retains its historical gendered scope.
- events/manifestations: A technical center in danger, personal renunciation and a hidden center of tension are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Agitation over criminality does not establish criminal conduct; source day language is not a timing rule.

## Contradictions

ABC's personal agitation and renunciation do not establish the fatal events in Witte. Health and male-person wording remain attributed historical examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Uranus/Hades through a person directly encountering danger, agitation or renunciation. [ABC](#abc-entry) includes past impulses and a technical center under threat, while [Witte](#witte-entry) names exposure to health-injurious influences and severe death-related examples. These remain historical correspondences rather than a diagnosis or forecast. Personal concern about criminality is one stated response, without implying participation in a crime.
