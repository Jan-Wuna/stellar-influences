## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

Sadism. Murder of female persons. Lustful murder. Murder preceded by rape.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 376; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting service in love, a dangerous event in love, harmonious impulses from the past, a sudden lack of harmony, sudden renunciation of love, surprising danger to love, hidden tensions in love.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 376; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Love or harmony encounters hidden tension, danger or renunciation.
- psychology: Service in love and harmonious past impulses coexist with a lack of harmony.
- body/health: Witte's sadism, rape and murder terms are historical source claims, not a diagnosis or characterization of a person.
- social/relationship: ABC describes difficulties within love; it does not equate ordinary tension with sexual violence.
- events/manifestations: Sudden renunciation and surprising danger to love are explicit alongside past harmony.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's severe sexual-violence examples differ materially from ABC's broader relationship difficulties.

## Contradictions

The sources differ substantially in severity. Witte's sadism, rape and murder claims must remain attributed and cannot be inferred from a lack of harmony or renunciation of love.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies Uranus/Hades through difficulty in love: service, hidden tension, a loss of harmony or renunciation appears beside harmonious impulses from the past in [ABC](#abc-entry). [Witte](#witte-entry) gives a much more severe range involving sadism and sexual violence culminating in murder. That historical branch remains explicit and separate; the broader relationship themes do not establish violent character or predict such an event.
