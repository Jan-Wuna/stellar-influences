## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

Unexpected events are taking a toll of life. Sudden murder.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 376; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

dangerous events in public, public excitement about hardships, sudden distress in public, impulses from the past in public life, technical disadvantages in public life, and outdated technology in public

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 376; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Danger, hardship or technical disadvantage enters public life.
- psychology: Public agitation over difficulties is named, without defining a person's temperament.
- body/health: Witte's toll of life and sudden murder are historical event claims, not a prognosis.
- social/relationship: The setting is public or communal; the entries do not identify an offender.
- events/manifestations: Outdated public technology and impulses from the past remain concrete nonviolent meanings.
- conflicts/notes: Public technical shortcomings are not equivalent to loss of life; the severe source examples remain conditional.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses fatal unexpected events while ABC also describes public hardship and obsolete technology. These are different degrees and forms of public difficulty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Uranus/Hades through difficulty appearing in public life: hardship, dangerous events or technical disadvantages draw communal attention. [ABC](#abc-entry) includes obsolete technology and past impulses in that public field. [Witte](#witte-entry) separately names unexpected loss of life and sudden murder. The wider theme of public distress therefore includes concrete technical problems without making the severe historical examples the expected outcome.
