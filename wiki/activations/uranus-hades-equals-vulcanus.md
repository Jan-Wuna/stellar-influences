## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

To be suddenly in great danger. Great danger of war. Cases of murder.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 377; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous influence of technology, sudden strength to endure privation, powerful hidden tensions, sudden renunciation of power, surprising disadvantages through force or violence, a surprising lack of strength, outdated technical energy.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 377; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Power or technical energy becomes dangerous, deficient or subject to renunciation.
- psychology: Powerful hidden tensions coexist with strength to endure privation.
- body/health: No diagnosis is supplied; Witte's murder and war claims are historical event possibilities.
- social/relationship: Force or violence can bring disadvantages, without identifying the person's role.
- events/manifestations: Outdated technical energy, relinquishing power and surprising lack of strength are explicit.
- conflicts/notes: Strength for privation and loss of strength are distinct possibilities; power is not always accumulated.

## Contradictions

ABC includes both capacity to endure lack and sudden weakness or renunciation of power. Witte's great danger, war and murder do not establish that any one technical problem becomes violent.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Uranus/Hades through the limits or dangers of power. [ABC](#abc-entry) names hidden tensions, harmful technical influence and disadvantages through force or violence, but also strength to endure privation and sudden renunciation of power. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies the severe historical examples of great danger, war and murder. The range includes both the exercise and the loss or relinquishment of strength without fixing the person's role.
