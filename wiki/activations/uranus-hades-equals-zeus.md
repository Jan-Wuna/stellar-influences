## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 237.

#### Witte Entry

Abortion. Well planned and carried out mean acts. Sudden misfortune through fire. Death through fire, through war. Murder by firearms or weapons of war.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 377; PDF p. 187.

#### ABC Entry

Beginning hidden tensions, experiencing fire danger, shared efforts in need, a technical achievement community, a surprising lack of goal-directed striving, unrest over deficient leadership, outdated technical performance.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 237) and [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 377; PDF p. 187).

- core meaning: Effort and direction meet hidden tension, need, fire danger or deficient leadership.
- psychology: A lack of goal-directed striving is named alongside planned action and shared effort.
- body/health: Witte's abortion and death claims remain historical correspondences, not reproductive or medical forecasts.
- social/relationship: Shared efforts in need and a technical achievement community are constructive collective themes.
- events/manifestations: Deficient leadership, obsolete technical performance, fire and war are distinct examples.
- conflicts/notes: Deliberately harmful acts are a separate Witte branch, not the necessary meaning of technical effort.

## Contradictions

Witte's planned harmful acts and severe fire or war claims differ from ABC's shared effort and inadequate leadership. Its abortion correspondence is preserved without causal or medical inference.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Uranus/Hades through effort under difficult conditions: people may work together in need, while deficient leadership or a lack of direction produces unrest. [ABC](#abc-entry) also names fire danger and outdated technical performance. [Witte](#witte-entry) adds planned harmful acts and severe fire, war and abortion correspondences. These remain distinct historical branches alongside shared technical effort, without establishing intent, a medical outcome or an inevitable disaster.
