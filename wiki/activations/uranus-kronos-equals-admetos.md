## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

To be torn from something through peremptory words. Sudden end of a power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 381; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

Special deepening into technology, impulses to block the chief, consolidation of important reforms, great tensions through depression, sudden consolidation of independence, surprising restriction by the superior, resistance to state reforms.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 381; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Abrupt termination or restriction of authority. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Resistance to a superior and tension around constraint. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: The ABC entry names depression; this remains an attributed historical association, not a diagnosis. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Commands, superiors, and resistance to state reforms. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Severance, the end of a power, and technical consolidation. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Authority may end, restrict another person, or meet resistance; consolidation of independence is another stated possibility. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

A sudden end of power and consolidation of independence or reforms are different possible developments. Depression remains an attributed historical association, without establishing a diagnosis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies the Uranus/Kronos axis through interruption and resistance to authority. Abrupt commands can sever an attachment or bring a power to an end; the technical and institutional setting also includes blocked initiatives and the consolidation of reforms. The common thread is a sudden encounter between an assertion of authority and what resists or fixes it in place, rather than a promise that every reform fails. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
