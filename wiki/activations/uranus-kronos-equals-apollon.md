## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden expansion of a power. Decrees of the government on astrology and future sciences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 381; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of unrest in the state, special surprises for science, successful renewal of authority, great expansion of technology, new standing for science, reform experiences of the state, important renewal of knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 381; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Expansion of power and technical or scientific activity. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: The entries describe collective unrest rather than a detailed individual psychology. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Governmental relations with scientific activity. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Decrees, technological expansion, and changed scientific reputation. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Renewal of authority and knowledge is explicit, alongside unrest; expansion does not guarantee a favorable institutional outcome. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Expansion and renewal of authority coexist with unrest in the state. Governmental decrees about astrology or future sciences do not establish the quality or success of the science.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Uranus/Kronos through expansion and renewal of authority, technology, and scientific recognition. Sudden enlargement can take an official form, such as decrees concerning astrology or future sciences, while the wider technical and scientific field can also become a setting for unrest. Expansion and improved standing therefore coexist with disruption; the entry does not identify one inevitable outcome. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
