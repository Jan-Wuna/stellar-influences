## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

Great excitement caused by the native. To be detained by the power of the state, or to perish by it.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 380; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

special technical place, special surprises at a place, great tensions in the environment, nervousness through the dominance of the partner, new self confidence of the partner, state event at a place, and priority of technology in the environment

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 380; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: A tense local encounter with authority or technical prominence. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Nervousness in response to a dominant partner. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: The Witte entry's fatal outcome is an attributed extreme, not a medical prediction. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Partner dominance and direct contact with state power. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Local surprises, state events, and detention in the Witte entry. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: The entries range from environmental tension to severe coercion; this is a difference of scale, not a demonstrated contradiction. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

The entries range from local partner or technical tension to severe state coercion. These different scales do not make detention or death the expected outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Uranus/Kronos through the immediate environment and encounters with power. Excitement can be generated by the person or by the surrounding place, and dominance within a partnership can make that environment tense. The Witte entry extends this encounter to detention and lethal state power; those severe examples belong to the source's range and do not establish the expected result of the formula. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
