Money power, trusts, corporations. An association sponsored and subsidized by the state. Art activities protected by government. Awakening of a nation.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 381; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Collective organization and power under state or corporate authority. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Community nervousness and renewed collective confidence. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Associations, corporations, and state-supported artistic groups. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Subsidized activity, shared reform, and important collective events. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: State sponsorship and community tension are compatible possibilities; the entries do not establish that sponsorship removes conflict. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

State sponsorship and community tension are compatible possibilities; sponsorship does not establish agreement or remove collective unrest.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Uranus/Kronos through organized communities and their relation to authority. Corporate or financial power, state support for associations and art, and the awakening of a nation sit alongside shared technical direction and reform. The unifying concern is collective organization under a strong or changing authority, with both institutional backing and tension within the community represented. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
