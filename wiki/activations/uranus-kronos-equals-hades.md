## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

Mean acts by rulers. Death and destruction through ruling powers or governments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 381; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

Excitement over criminality in the state, dangerous surprises for the chief, special disadvantages of technology, agitation over great deficiencies, inadequate impulses of superiors, a sudden lack of authority, hidden unrest among the authorities.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 381; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Harmful rule and deficiencies in authority or technology. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Hidden unrest and agitation over serious deficiencies. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: Death in the Witte entry is an attributed political extreme, not a health diagnosis. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Rulers, superiors, and criminality within the state. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Destructive government action and dangerous surprises for a superior. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Deficient authority and destructive exercise of authority are distinct forms of difficulty; neither defines every technical disadvantage. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Harmful governmental action is the stronger Witte branch. Technical deficiencies, deficient authority and concealed unrest in ABC do not establish a political classification or particular criminal act.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Uranus/Kronos through destructive or deficient uses of authority. The Witte entry gives the severe political form as harmful acts by rulers and destruction through governments; the ABC entry adds criminality, shortcomings in technology, and concealed unrest among authorities. These motifs concern the damage or inadequacy surrounding power, without making every technical disadvantage equivalent to political violence. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
