### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 380; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

Excitement over the direction of finances, an especially joyful surprise, a successful state event, successful direction of technology, major financial reform, great legal tension, surprising joy of the superior, priority success of technology.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 380; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Official financial decisions and successful technical administration. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Joyful surprise and excitement about financial direction. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: State administration, superiors, and legal tensions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Financial decrees and successful state or technical events. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Major financial reform and legal tension remain beside successful administration and joy. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Successful financial or technical direction coexists with legal tension. A joyful surprise or a financial decree does not establish guaranteed financial gain.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Uranus/Kronos through financial administration and the prospects of official or technical success. Decrees concerning finances provide the clearest institutional expression, while the ABC entry also allows joyful surprise and successful management of technology. Legal tension remains in that same entry, so success here does not imply the absence of conflict around financial direction. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
