## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

To act with great concentration and firm authority. Sudden acts of despotism of government. Suppression of uproar.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 380; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

A special work rhythm, great tensions at work, sudden important work, independent technical work, tense work for the state, surprising actions of the superior, important technical work.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 380; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Concentrated action under strong authority. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Firmness, concentration and work tension are named. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Employers, superiors, and government power over unrest. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Sudden important work, technical tasks, and suppression of uproar. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Independent work and action under firm authority are distinct expressions, while coercive suppression remains a separate Witte example. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Independent technical work and firm concentration differ from government despotism or suppression. The source does not assign one employment arrangement or coercive role to every instance.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Uranus/Kronos through decisive work and concentrated action. Authority becomes something exercised in a task or imposed through sudden government action, while technical work and unexpected actions by a superior carry the same pressure into employment. Firm concentration and coercive suppression are distinct expressions in the entries; the latter must not be treated as the necessary meaning of effective action. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
