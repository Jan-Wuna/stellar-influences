## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

To be tense and loaded with energy and strength. Tyrant. Despot in business. Dictator. To be subject to someone’s power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 380; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

A special experience of the soul, great emotional tension, inner unrest in the state, my special rhythm, my new self-confidence, my great surprise, my sudden independence, sudden greatness of soul, important soul impulses.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 380; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Personal tension and inner experience accompany an encounter with power or independence.
- psychology: Energy, emotional tension, new confidence and important soul impulses are named.
- body/health: Energy and tension are descriptive source terms, without establishing a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Witte includes both business despotism and subjection to another's power.
- events/manifestations: Great surprise, a personal rhythm and sudden independence are explicit in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: Exercising control, being subject to power and gaining independence remain distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte describes both exercising despotic power and being subject to another's power. ABC adds personal independence, confidence and inner experience; these do not determine which role a person occupies.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Uranus/Kronos as a personal encounter with concentrated power and inner tension. [Witte](#witte-entry) allows both exercising despotic authority and being subject to someone else's power. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds surprise, new confidence and sudden independence, expressed through its language of soul experience and inner unrest. The person's position within power can therefore vary; neither confidence nor tension alone selects the ruling or subordinate role.
