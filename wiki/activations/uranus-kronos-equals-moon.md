## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

Despotic or ruling women. Persons who can decree. Power of the people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 380; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

An exciting hour for the chief, special unrest of the woman, great tensions with a woman, new authority for the people, self-confident impulses of the woman, a technically superior people, restless people in the state, important emotional impulses.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 380; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Authority expressed through people and emotionally charged relations. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Unrest and tension around a ruler or superior. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Ruling women and popular power are historical source examples. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Decrees, a restless population, and a charged moment for a superior. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Technical superiority, popular power and ruling women are distinct source examples; the gendered language is not a universal rule. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Ruling women and the power of the people are different positions of authority. Gendered examples remain historical source wording, without defining a universal female disposition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Uranus/Kronos through public or personal feeling around authority. The entries place power both in rulers and in the people, with unrest and tension surrounding those relations. Witte's examples explicitly assign ruling roles to women; that historical gender framing is retained as attribution rather than treated as a universal property of women. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
