## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

Decrees which are not effective. Destruction by ruling powers. Bad weather. Turbulent sea.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 381; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

Excitement over weak authority, a special surprise involving water, disappointing state reforms, great excitement through water, surprising standing for metaphysics, unrest through weak governing authority, priority air technology, important water technology.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 381; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Ineffective authority and turbulent environmental conditions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Restlessness in relation to weak authority. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Ineffective rulings and destructive exercises of power. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Failed decrees, bad weather and turbulent sea coexist with air/water technology and metaphysical standing. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Ineffective decrees and destruction by authority remain distinct from water or air technology and metaphysical standing. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Weak or ineffective authority, destructive power and turbulent water are different source branches. Technical water or air themes and metaphysical standing do not establish a causal relation between political weakness and weather.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Uranus/Kronos through authority that fails to take effect or becomes destructive. Ineffective decrees and weak authority form one institutional line, while bad weather and turbulent water form a separate environmental expression in the entries. ABC also names air and water technology and the surprising standing of metaphysics. The institutional and environmental examples share instability but should not be collapsed into a claim that political weakness causes a natural event. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
