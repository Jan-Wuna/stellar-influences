## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 380; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Connections shaped by authority and important events. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Tension or unrest within an important connection. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Links with the state, a superior, or technical activity. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Intervention by ruling power and new institutional connections. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: The entries do not determine whether intervention assists or constrains a connection. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

The entries do not determine whether intervention assists or constrains a connection. Technical, state and superior-related links remain distinct settings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Uranus/Kronos through connections affected by important events or ruling power. The Witte entry concentrates this in intervention by authority; the ABC entry supplies links with technology, the state, and a superior. The relationship itself is therefore the setting in which sudden institutional pressure or a new connection becomes significant. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
