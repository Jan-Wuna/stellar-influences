## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

Suddenly arising capabilities. Governmental orders, which are withdrawn. Sudden change in authority.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 381; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

Development of great tensions, development of important events, great technical evolution, sudden unfolding as chief, surprising development of self-confidence, unrest through state changes, transformations through new superiors.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 381; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Sudden change in authority and developing capability. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Developing self-confidence coexists with unrest around state changes. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Changing leadership and the position of a chief. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Withdrawn government orders and newly arising capabilities. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Development of self-confidence is explicit, alongside growing tension and changes imposed by new superiors. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

New capabilities, withdrawn orders, changed superiors and developing self-confidence are different changes in authority. They do not all strengthen the same person.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies Uranus/Kronos through abrupt changes in authority and capability. The Witte entry includes newly arising capabilities, withdrawn orders, and a sudden alteration of authority; the ABC entry also names development into a chief's role and unrest during state changes. The emphasis is on power changing its form or holder, without assuming that every change strengthens the person involved. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
