## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

Decrees and statements of the government about spiritual matters or education.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 381; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

Dynamic intellectual or spiritual authority, great tensions through truth, idea impulses of an authority, clear technical superiority, a cultural event of the state, new self-confidence through insights, rational impulses of the chief, important intellectual or spiritual reform.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 381; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Authority directing education, spiritual matters, or culture. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Tension around truth and confidence associated with knowledge. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Government involvement in education and cultural life. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Official statements, cultural events, and spiritual reform. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Authority acting through ideas does not establish possession of truth or freedom from tension. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Government direction of education or spiritual matters coexists with tension around truth. Intellectual authority and rational impulses remain source themes, not proof that an authority's claims are true.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Uranus/Kronos through official direction of spiritual, educational, or cultural matters. Government decrees and statements are the clearest expression, while the ABC entry adds tension around truth, technical superiority, and confidence through knowledge. The shared concern is authority acting through ideas or cultural direction; it does not establish that such authority possesses the truth it invokes. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
