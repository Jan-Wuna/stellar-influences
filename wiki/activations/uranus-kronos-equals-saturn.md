## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

Losses caused by the ruling powers, by powers suddenly breaking forth. To separate or rid oneself of something with help from the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 381; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

Burdensome state reforms, special losses in technology, dominant reform problems, great tensions among the authorities, concentration on an important event, new self-confidence in old age, problematic excitement of the chief, important reform tasks.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 381; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Loss or separation involving sudden institutional power. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Concentration on an important event and tension around reform. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: The ABC mention of old age is a context for self-awareness, not a disease claim. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Relations with ruling powers and a troubled superior. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Technical losses, reform problems, and separation with state help. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Loss imposed by power and separation supported by the state are different roles for authority. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Ruling power may cause losses, while state help may support separation. Burdensome reforms and important tasks do not establish a particular legal remedy.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Uranus/Kronos through losses, burdens, and separation in relation to authority. Sudden power can cause loss, yet state assistance can also enable someone to leave or relinquish something. The technical and administrative examples add reform difficulties and tension among authorities, so restriction and supported separation remain distinct possibilities within the same formula. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
