## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

To express oneself fiercely. Despots. Enraged over government rulings. Days of great excitement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 380; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

An authority figure in technology, great bodily tensions, new self-confidence of the man, the day of a special event, a technical direction center, unrest in a state center, an important personal event, a center for special technology.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 380; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Forceful personal expression amid authority and technical direction. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Anger at rulings and heightened personal tension. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: ABC associates the formula with bodily tension; this is not a medical diagnosis. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Personal encounters with government rulings. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Days of great excitement and important personal or technical events. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: The male confidence example remains source-specific; bodily tension does not establish a medical condition. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Fierce expression and anger over rulings coexist with technical authority and personal events. The male example is historical attribution, without restricting the whole formula to men.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Uranus/Kronos through a forceful personal expression of authority and the tension surrounding it. The Witte entry includes fierce self-expression and anger at government rulings, while the ABC entry places important personal events and technical direction in the same field. The bodily tension named by ABC remains an attributed association, and the historically male wording does not restrict the whole formula to men. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
