## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

To enjoy suddenly great popularity. Ruler (female) in love. Love quarrels.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 380; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

Special love excitement, a special art event, dominance of a new love, a peaceful state event, great love tension, a great preference for technology, a harmonious state event, new self-confidence through love, an important peace event.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 380; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Sudden popularity and intense love or artistic events. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Love excitement, tension, and confidence associated with affection. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Love quarrels, new attachments, and public popularity. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Artistic events and peaceful or harmonious state occasions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Witte's “Ruler (female) in love” is a historical gendered example; peaceful events and love quarrels are differing contexts, not contradictory predictions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

The female ruler in love is a historical gendered example. Public peace, popularity and private love quarrels are different contexts, without a guaranteed harmonious outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies Uranus/Kronos through a sudden rise in affection, popularity, or artistic prominence, with tension still possible in love. The entries also allow peaceful state events and increased confidence through love. These harmonious expressions sit beside quarrels and an intense new attachment, so popularity or official harmony does not settle the condition of a private relationship. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
