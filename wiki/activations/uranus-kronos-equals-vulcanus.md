## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 239.

#### Witte Entry

Suddenly appearing great power. Mighty revolt or resistance against ruling power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 381; PDF p. 189.

#### ABC Entry

Special tensions through force or violence, dominant power impulses, dynamic state power, an influential important event, power tensions among superiors, strong impulses through independence, unrest through state force or violence, important energy reform.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 239), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 381; PDF p. 189).

- core meaning: Sudden great power and resistance to ruling authority. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Tension among those who exercise power. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: Violence is an attributed event context, not a diagnosis. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Superiors, state power, and opposition to it. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Powerful revolt, resistance, and unrest through state violence. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Power may be exercised or resisted; independence and energy reform remain explicit additional themes. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Suddenly appearing power and resistance to ruling power are opposing positions within the source. Energy reform is an additional technical theme and does not identify a profession.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Uranus/Kronos through the sudden appearance or contest of great power. Authority can become forceful, or meet powerful revolt and resistance; the ABC entry also describes tension among superiors and unrest through state violence. The organizing issue is the intensity of the struggle over power, rather than an assumption that the person always represents the ruling side. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
