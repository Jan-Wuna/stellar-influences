## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 67.

#### Witte Entry

Very difficult to appease. To tear up, to break up, to interrupt something suddenly. To scatter shreds around.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 79; PDF p. 38.

#### ABC Entry

An exciting minute of a blockade, thorough soul impulses, my blocked rhythm, my unrest through depression, sudden emotional depression, my restless cycle or circulation, my deepening into technology, condensation of emotional tensions.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 67), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 79; PDF p. 38).

- core meaning: Blocked personal momentum and sudden interruption. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Difficulty settling agitation and concentrated emotional tension. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: ABC names depression; its wording does not establish a clinical condition. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: The entries do not specify a partner or a defined social role. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Tearing, breaking, or abruptly interrupting something. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Kreislauf can mean cycle or circulation; the source does not identify a particular bodily condition. Depression remains an attributed historical association.

## Contradictions

Abrupt interruption, blocked rhythm and concentrated tension coexist with deepening into technology. The source's Kreislauf retains a cycle/circulation ambiguity; no precise bodily cycle or circulatory diagnosis is established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Uranus/MC through a personal state of blocked momentum and abrupt interruption. Difficulty being appeased can find an outward expression in tearing or breaking something up, while the ABC entry names emotional tension and deepening into technology. Its depression language remains an attributed historical association; neither that language nor the disruptive examples establish a diagnosis or an inevitable act. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
