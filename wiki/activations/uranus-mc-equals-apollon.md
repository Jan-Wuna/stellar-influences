## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 67.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden expansion. Sudden unexpected success. Sudden astrological success.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 79; PDF p. 38.

#### ABC Entry

Experiences of an exciting minute, dynamic soul experiences, my success in technology, my successful rhythm, my experiences in technology, my technical knowledge, my surprising success, and new experiences of the soul

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 67), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 79; PDF p. 38).

- core meaning: Sudden expansion and personally experienced success. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Excitement and new inward experience accompany success. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: The entries emphasize personal experience rather than a defined relationship. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Unexpected success in technical or astrological activity. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Personal technical or astrological success is a source possibility, without a guaranteed career result or measurable rhythm.

## Contradictions

Sudden expansion and technical or astrological success are compatible but distinct examples. The successful rhythm is source wording without a specified measurable process.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Uranus/MC through sudden personal expansion and success. Technical knowledge and experience give this success a concrete field in the ABC entry, while Witte explicitly includes unexpected success in astrology. The common emphasis is an opening that changes one's experience quickly, without specifying a guaranteed career result. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
