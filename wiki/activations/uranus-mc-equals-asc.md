## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be upset about others. Moment of an accident at a certain location.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Achieving minutes in the environment, the Paiiner soul, dynamic partner soul, my restless partner, my nervousness in the environment, my unrest in one place, mental arousal in the place, mental partner impulses, and mental tensions in one place

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inclination to behave in a restless and hasty manner in the presence of others, tendency to easily fall into a state of excitement. Sudden experiences, sudden adjustment to new circumstances or conditions in life.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To present oneself to the world in an independent and dignified manner. The need to share one's original insights with others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Concentrating on your projects and become a good judge of the people you meet; increases your reputation for being a personally independent and freedom loving person who needs much recognition and personal space.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Uranus/MC = Asc`: charismatic originality, rapid changes in stance or status, and the ability to signal a new direction through sheer personal presence. Main caution: instability: abrupt conduct or nervous tension can unsettle both close relationships and public continuity.
