## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 67.

#### Witte Entry

To be upset about others. Moment of an accident at a certain location.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 78; PDF p. 38.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting minutes in the surroundings, a partner's excited soul, a partner's dynamic soul, my restless partner, my nervousness in the surroundings, my unrest at a location, psychological excitement at the location, emotional partner impulses, emotional tensions at a location.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 279; PDF p. 281; entry code `0997`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Inclination to behave in a restless and hasty manner in the presence of others, tendency to easily fall into a state of excitement. Sudden experiences, sudden adjustment to new circumstances or conditions in life.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. 109 (exact entry; chapter starts on PDF p. 108).

#### Sandbach Entry

To present oneself to the world in an independent and dignified manner. The need to share one's original insights with others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `324`

#### Munkasey Entry

Concentrating on your projects and become a good judge of the people you meet; increases your reputation for being a personally independent and freedom loving person who needs much recognition and personal space.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 67), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 78; PDF p. 38), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) (printed p. 279; PDF p. 281; entry 0997), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) (exact PDF p. 109), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (printed p. 324).

- core meaning: Independent presentation and rapid adjustment in encounters. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- psychology: Excitability, haste, and a need for recognition and personal space. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- body/health: Witte names an accident; no specific bodily disorder is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- social/relationship: Sharing original insights and judging people encountered in one's projects. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Sudden experiences and adjustment to new conditions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Independent presentation and restless or hasty response are different emphases; the accident example does not define every encounter.

## Contradictions

Dignified independence, original insight and a need for space coexist with haste and upset in encounters. Witte's accident example is more specific and severe than the general adjustment described by Ebertin.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Uranus/MC through the way personal independence meets other people and a particular environment. Original insight can be shared with dignity and a strong need for space, yet encounters can also produce haste, upset, and rapid adjustment to changed circumstances. Recognition and independent presentation therefore coexist with sensitivity to the people one meets; Witte's accident example remains a severe possibility in that source, not the summary of every encounter. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
