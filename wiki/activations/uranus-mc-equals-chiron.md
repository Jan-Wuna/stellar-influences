#### Sandbach Entry

Your vibration helps others to find their own unique path to God, and to be more uninhibited about opening up to spiritual messages. You also have the innate ability to heal feelings of social alienation, and to help others see the value of their own personal uniqueness to the world

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) (exact PDF p. 109).

- core meaning: Support for uniqueness within spiritual and social life. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- psychology: Greater openness to spiritual messages and acceptance of difference. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- body/health: Sandbach's healing language concerns social alienation, not a diagnosed bodily condition. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- social/relationship: Helping others feel the value of their distinct contribution. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Encouragement of an individual spiritual path. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach is represented; the entry does not supply a method or an independently corroborated healing claim. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)

## Contradictions

Only Sandbach is represented. Its spiritual and relational healing language does not supply a medical method or independent corroboration of a healing power.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Chiron specifies Uranus/MC through helping others value their own distinctive path and place in the world. In Sandbach's spiritual vocabulary, a person's presence encourages openness to spiritual messages and a unique path to God; the related social task is easing alienation by recognizing personal uniqueness. This is the single entry's spiritual and relational claim, not evidence of a medical healing power. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
