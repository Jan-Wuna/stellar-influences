## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 67.

#### Witte Entry

Stimulation, excitement and tension in social life. Marrying quickly. Wedding joys.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 79; PDF p. 38.

#### ABC Entry

An exciting minute of a community, soulful artistic impulses, a dynamic community of souls, my impulses for the community, tensions in a community of souls, shared soul impulses, a restless community of souls.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 67), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 79; PDF p. 38).

- core meaning: Personal stimulation expressed in a social or communal setting. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Excitement and tension within a shared emotional life. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Community involvement, social stimulation, and marriage. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Rapid marriage and artistic or communal impulses. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Wedding joy and communal tension coexist; the entries do not establish lasting harmony or an inevitable marriage.

## Contradictions

Rapid marriage and wedding joys are specific Witte examples. Artistic and emotional participation in a community may also carry tension; neither determines how long a union or harmony lasts.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Uranus/MC through personally felt stimulation and tension in community or social life. The shared setting can carry artistic impulses and collective excitement, and Witte gives marrying quickly as one concrete manifestation. The entries connect an inner surge with participation in a group; they do not establish whether that excitement becomes durable harmony. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
