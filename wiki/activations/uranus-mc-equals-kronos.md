## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 67.

#### Witte Entry

To issue a sudden order. Tension caused by a sudden test of strength.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 79; PDF p. 38.

#### ABC Entry

Technology dominates with me, an especially exciting minute, great emotional arousal, I am a technical authority, inner unrest in the state, my special nervousness, my great tension, my independent rhythm, important soul impulses.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 67), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 79; PDF p. 38).

- core meaning: Personal authority expressed abruptly or tested under pressure. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Emotional arousal, nervousness, and tension. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Issuing orders and encountering a test of strength. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: A sudden command and prominent involvement with technology. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Technical authority, independent rhythm and a test of strength are distinct themes; independence does not establish selfishness.

## Contradictions

Issuing a sudden order and meeting a test of strength describe different positions around authority. The independent rhythm and technical prominence named by ABC do not determine which position applies.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Uranus/MC through a personal surge of authority or a sudden test of strength. The clearest expression is issuing an unexpected order, with the resulting tension placed beside technical prominence and inner unrest in the ABC entry. The evidence supports heightened pressure around command and capability, without establishing selfishness as a trait. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
