## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Uranus/MC by emphasizing the personal need for recognition of ideas and contributions. In the sole Munkasey entry, the need for recognition appears alongside working through consequences and resolving outstanding issues, with rewards for scientific work as a concrete possibility. The repeated MC keeps the emphasis on one's own contribution and its consequences; it does not add a distinct third-factor doctrine. ([Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
