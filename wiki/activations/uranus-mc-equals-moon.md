## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 67.

#### Witte Entry

To be in a bad humor on account of a woman or the people. To enact a scene. Labor pains.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 78; PDF p. 38.

#### ABC Entry

An exciting hour for the woman, a woman's dynamic soul, I excite my wife, my feeling for rhythm, my excited wife, my excitement through the woman, my nervous disposition, my people are excited, a psychologically excited woman, a restless soul of a people.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 278; PDF p. 280; entry code `0988`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Irritable, frequent change of mood, quick temper, tendency to become easily upset. Extraordinary state of excitement or irritability of a woman, such as during pregnancy or just before delivery.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. 109 (exact entry; chapter starts on PDF p. 108).

#### Sandbach Entry

To over-exaggerate any career upsets. To feel vulnerable about the world’s reactions to your being different or unusual. The emotional need to be respected for being one’s own unique self.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `324`

#### Munkasey Entry

Care and concern about the impact your ideas may have; added inner uncertainty about paths to success; vacillating on how to proceed with plans; impels you to demand more isolation and personal freedom from people.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 67), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 78; PDF p. 38), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) (printed p. 278; PDF p. 280; entry 0988), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) (exact PDF p. 109), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (printed p. 324).

- core meaning: Emotional sensitivity around independence and recognition. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- psychology: Irritability, vulnerability, vacillation, and uncertainty about success. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- body/health: Labor pains and pregnancy-related excitement are historical Witte/Ebertin examples, not medical predictions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- social/relationship: A need for respect alongside demands for personal freedom or isolation. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Career upsets and changes in how plans are pursued. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Gendered and pregnancy-related examples remain historical attributions, while vulnerability and a wish for respect are broader psychological themes.

## Contradictions

The older female and pregnancy examples differ in scope from concern about recognition and independence. Neither group defines all people represented by the formula or establishes a medical outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Uranus/MC through emotional sensitivity to one's independent course and to other people's reactions. Concern about how ideas will be received can bring uncertainty, changes of mood, or a wish for more freedom and isolation. The older entries also describe irritability involving women and labor or pregnancy; those gendered and bodily examples remain attributed and do not define all people carrying the formula. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
