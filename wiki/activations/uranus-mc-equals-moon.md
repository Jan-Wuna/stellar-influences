## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be in a bad humor on account of a woman or the people. To enact a scene. Labor pains.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting hour for the woman, dynamic woman soul, I excite my wife, my rhythm gesture, my excited wife, my excitement through the woman, my nervous gemtite, my people is excited, mentally excited woman, and restless people soul

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Irritable, frequent change of mood, quick temper, tendency to become easily upset. Extraordinary state of excitement or irritability of a woman, such as during pregnancy or just before delivery.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To over-exaggerate any career upsets. To feel vulnerable about the world’s reactions to your being different or unusual. The emotional need to be respected for being one’s own unique self.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Care and concern about the impact your ideas may have; added inner uncertainty about paths to success; vacillating on how to proceed with plans; impels you to demand more isolation and personal freedom from people.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Uranus/MC = Moon`: intuitive course correction, emotional responsiveness to change, and the capacity to feel when a life path has become stale. Main caution: instability: mood swings or domestic upheaval can translate directly into erratic decisions about status or vocation.
