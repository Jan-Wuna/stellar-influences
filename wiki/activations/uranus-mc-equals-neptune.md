## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 67.

#### Witte Entry

To excite oneself in vain. Much noise about nothing or over something unintelligible. Suddenly confused.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 79; PDF p. 38.

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of emotional tension, an exciting minute in water, sensitive soul impulses, my nervousness in the air, my surprising weakness, weak soul impulses, producing emotional tensions, sensitivity of an excited soul.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 278; PDF p. 280; entry code `0994`.

#### Ebertin Entry

A passive emotional life, powerlessness, inability to obtain recognition. Suppressed anger, a paralyzed will.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. 109 (exact Neptune entry; chapter starts on PDF p. 108).

#### Sandbach Entry

To have visions about innovations that need to be carried out in the world. A refined way of expressing one’s creativity, individuality and uniqueness to the world. To feel uncertain about pursuing one’s own individual and independent way in life.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `324`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased native creativity and desire to produce results which are new or different; added indifference about how these effects are introduced or presented; enlightened creativity by using modern forms of expression.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creative individuality and sensitivity coexist with uncertainty about effective expression ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- psychology: Confusion, suppressed anger, powerlessness and hesitation contrast with refined creativity and visions of innovation ([Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- body/health: ABC names weakness and sensitivity; Ebertin's “paralyzed will” is psychological wording here, without a neurological diagnosis ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry)).
- social/relationship: Difficulty obtaining recognition and indifference to how creative work is presented ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
- events/manifestations: Innovative expression, excitement that achieves little, and the production or dissolution of emotional tension; ABC also retains literal water and air images ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- conflicts/notes: Creative vision and ineffective excitement are different claims; neither guarantees the other will prevail. The repaired ABC row ends before Pluto's dynamic-development entry.

## Contradictions

The entries retain a material tension between refined creative expression and powerlessness or confusion. ABC adds both dissolving and producing emotional tension; it does not resolve that contrast. Its former bullet-only translation has been replaced from the eight Neptune clauses on printed p. 79 (PDF p. 38), excluding the following Pluto row. Ebertin's final punctuation is a period on the actual printed p. 278 scan (PDF p. 280), despite the extracted comma. Sandbach's exact Neptune entry is on PDF p. 109.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/MC = Neptune`, Neptune specifies personal excitation through sensitivity and uncertainty about translating a distinctive inner vision into visible action. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) describe refined creativity and innovative forms of expression; [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) also retain confusion, ineffective excitement and difficulty obtaining recognition. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds sensitive or weak impulses and emotional tension that may be produced or dissolved. Creative possibility and powerlessness remain side by side: vision does not automatically overcome hesitation.
