## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden development and changes. To adapt or adjust oneself to something by leaps and bounds. To be pushed back and forth by one’s impulses.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

my troubled development, minute of exciting changes, developing scelic tensions, changing mental tensions, and growing mental unrest

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Restless and energetic pursuit of one's objectives, fanatic ambition. A nervous breakdown caused through overwork.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A driving urge to be free and open in a highly overt fashion. One is intensely compelled to set up one’s own rules, and arrive at one’s own ethics and philosophy of life. The potential of drastic rebellion against authority figures and the status quo.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `324`

#### Munkasey Entry

Helps either bring you world-wide fame and recognition for your efforts, or drop you alone and unnoticed to the depths of despair; adds to the personal intensity you put into your work efforts; may bring disgrace at work.
