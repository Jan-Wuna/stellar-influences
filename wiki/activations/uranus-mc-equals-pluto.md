### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `MC + Uranus`
- Source page: `67`
#### Witte Entry

Sudden development and changes. To adapt or adjust oneself to something by leaps and bounds. To be pushed back and forth by one’s impulses.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Uranus/Medium Coeli`
- Source page: `278`
- Entry: `0995`

#### Ebertin Entry

Restless and energetic pursuit of one's objectives, fanatic ambition. A nervous breakdown caused through overwork.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Uranus/Midheaven midpoint`
- Source page: `108`
- Activator: `Pluto`

#### Sandbach Entry

A driving urge to be free and open in a highly overt fashion. One is intensely compelled to set up one’s own rules, and arrive at one’s own ethics and philosophy of life. The potential of drastic rebellion against authority figures and the status quo.
