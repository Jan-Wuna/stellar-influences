## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 67.

#### Witte Entry

To look through to the bottom of something. To make something lucid. To know the truth. To generate propaganda.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 79; PDF p. 38.

#### ABC Entry

An exciting minute of truth, a dynamic mind-soul, cultural impulses for the soul, my dynamic mind, my intellectual or spiritual tension, my new ideas, my cultural impulses, my sudden clarity, my surprising truth, unrest for soul and mind.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 67), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 79; PDF p. 38).

- core meaning: Personal clarification and the expression of ideas. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Intellectual or spiritual tension alongside sudden clarity. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Making matters lucid for others, including the source's propaganda example. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: New ideas and attempts to disclose or communicate truth. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Clarity, a surprising truth and propaganda are different source themes; none validates a particular message.

## Contradictions

Knowing or presenting truth and generating propaganda are both named by Witte. ABC's sudden clarity and cultural impulses do not prove a claim true or establish wisdom.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Uranus/MC through sudden clarity and the urge to make something understandable. Investigating to the bottom of a matter connects personal intellectual tension with new ideas and cultural impulses. Witte also includes generating propaganda, so the experience or assertion of clarity cannot by itself be treated as proof that a message is true. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
