## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To rid oneself of something. To kick someone out. To be quarrelsome. Excitement through separation, losses or resistance.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting star minute, impulses of a mature soul, my sudden pain, my concentrated tension, my sudden separation, my my rhythm cancellations, my technical tasks, my surprising losses, and my closing seclen impulses

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Frigidity, narrow-mindedness, egoism. Regaining reality and coming down to earth, desire to part quickly, saying good-bye quickly, losing one's job or career position.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A fear of allowing the world to see one’s individuality and uniqueness. The tendency to repress urges to rebel against authority. After much soul searching one gradually finds one’s own individual path in life.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `324`

#### Munkasey Entry

Adds to your need to do your tasks alone and without interference or relief from others; lessens your rewards for tasks done, but increases your reputation as a solid and reliable worker; helps bring new pitfalls while working.
