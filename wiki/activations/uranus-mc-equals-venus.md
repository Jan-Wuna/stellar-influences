## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Longing for love. To become daring. Tension through love, pleasure or diversion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Blessed love event, soul aroused by love, harmonious love impulses, my artistic impulses, exciting love minute, and surprising soul peace

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Quick response to sensory stimulation, ability to arouse physical desire in others, promiscuity in the expression of love. Sudden desire, procreation, birth.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

In love relationships you want others to respect your freedom, independence, and uniqueness. To appreciate any new and/or innovative ways of expressing spiritual energy. Art that challenges authority.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased creativity and imagination in areas like music or the arts; experimenting with synthetic forms of art or music; you consider using machines to assist you in expressing your ideas or thoughts in music, art, or love.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Uranus/MC = Venus`: creative reform, appealing unconventionality, and the ability to make change socially attractive rather than merely disruptive. Main caution: inconsistency in values: novelty and pleasure can destabilize commitments, alliances, or long-term direction.
