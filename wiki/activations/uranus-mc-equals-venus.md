## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 67.

#### Witte Entry

Longing for love. To become daring. Tension through love, pleasure or diversion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 78; PDF p. 38.

#### ABC Entry

A soulful event in love, a soul excited by love, harmonious love impulses, my artistic impulses, my preference for technology, an exciting minute in love, surprising peace of soul.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 278; PDF p. 280; entry code `0990`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Quick response to sensory stimulation, ability to arouse physical desire in others, promiscuity in the expression of love. Sudden desire, procreation, birth.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. 109 (exact entry; chapter starts on PDF p. 108).

#### Sandbach Entry

In love relationships you want others to respect your freedom, independence, and uniqueness. To appreciate any new and/or innovative ways of expressing spiritual energy. Art that challenges authority.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `324`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased creativity and imagination in areas like music or the arts; experimenting with synthetic forms of art or music; you consider using machines to assist you in expressing your ideas or thoughts in music, art, or love.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 67), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 78; PDF p. 38), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) (printed p. 278; PDF p. 280; entry 0990), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) (exact PDF p. 109), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) (printed p. 324).

- core meaning: Distinctive expression through love and the arts. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- psychology: Longing, responsiveness to stimulation, and artistic imagination. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- body/health: Ebertin's procreation and birth examples remain attributed, not reproductive predictions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- social/relationship: Respect for freedom and uniqueness in love relationships. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Daring affection, innovative art, and machine-assisted expression. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Ebertin's “promiscuity” is a historical evaluative label; freedom, tension, and peace do not establish one universal relationship pattern. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)

## Contradictions

Freedom and inventive expression coexist with love tension or unexpected peace. Ebertin's evaluative promiscuity label and reproductive examples remain attributed, without defining a universal relationship pattern or outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Venus specifies Uranus/MC through love, sensory responsiveness, and inventive artistic expression. Longing and sudden desire coexist with a need for freedom and respect for uniqueness within relationships. Technical or synthetic means can assist expression in music, art, or love, while the entries range from tension to unexpected peace; they do not decide the stability of a particular commitment. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
