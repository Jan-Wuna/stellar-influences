## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 67.

#### Witte Entry

General excitement. Mental tension all around. To give or bring something to the world.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 78; PDF p. 38.

#### ABC Entry

An exciting minute in public, my nervousness in public life, my rhythm in public, soul impulses in public life, an emotionally excited public, emotional tensions in public.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 67), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 78; PDF p. 38).

- core meaning: Personal excitement and expression within public life. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Public nervousness and shared mental tension. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: The individual and an emotionally aroused public. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Bringing something into the world amid general excitement. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: The entries leave the object and outcome of public expression unspecified. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Personal excitement and an aroused public coexist in the entries. What is brought into the world and how it is received remain unspecified.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Uranus/MC through personal excitement entering public life. Nervousness and inner impulses become part of a wider field of tension, while Witte also allows bringing something into the world. The entries support public expression under excitement; they do not identify what is introduced or establish that public tension must produce a recognized achievement. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
