## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 67.

#### Witte Entry

Irritable character. Suddenly using force.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 79; PDF p. 38.

#### ABC Entry

Dynamic force of the soul, influence of a dynamic soul, influence by psychic impulses, enormous psychic tensions, powerful psychic arousal, my enormous restlessness, new psychic energy, and strong emotional restlessness

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 67), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 79; PDF p. 38).

- core meaning: Intense personal arousal with forceful outward expression. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Irritability, powerful agitation, and emotional restlessness. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Emotional influence on others and the possibility of sudden force. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: A rapid forceful reaction or a surge of energy. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: “Psychic” translates inward or psychological language here; it does not establish a paranormal capacity. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Inward force, emotional influence and sudden use of force are distinct expressions. The psychological wording does not establish a paranormal capacity or an inevitable violent action.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Uranus/MC through an unusually forceful personal arousal. The ABC entry emphasizes emotional influence, new energy, and intense restlessness, while Witte gives irritability and the sudden use of force as outward expressions. The link is between inward pressure and its forceful expression; the entries do not establish a psychic power or an inevitable violent act. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
