## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 67.

#### Witte Entry

To be surprised by one’s own decisions. To have and pursue a direction or goal unerringly. To reach one’s goal. Decidedly self-willed. To enjoy fires. To set a fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 79; PDF p. 38.

#### ABC Entry

Beginning emotional tensions, a dynamic creative soul, fiery soul impulses, I generate nervousness, my strenuous rhythm, my dynamic goal-setting, my technical achievements, a minute of exciting efforts, generating emotional impulses.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 67), [ABC](#abc-entry) (printed p. 79; PDF p. 38).

- core meaning: Abrupt personal decision followed by determined pursuit of a goal. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Self-will, creative effort, and emotional tension. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: The entries emphasize personal direction rather than a defined relationship. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Reaching a goal, technical achievement, and Witte's fire examples. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Creative effort and self-willed pursuit of a goal coexist with the separate source fire examples; neither makes fire-setting inevitable.

## Contradictions

The source describes both directed creative effort and emotional tension. Witte's enjoyment of fire or setting a fire remains a separate severe possibility, not the necessary consequence of pursuing a goal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Uranus/MC through a sudden personal decision that becomes a strongly pursued goal. Technical achievement and creative effort give that direction practical expression, while the person may be surprised by the strength or abruptness of the decision. Witte's examples involving enjoyment of fire and setting a fire remain separate, severe source manifestations rather than a necessary consequence of goal-directed action. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
