## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 234, `Uranus + Neptune`, Admetos entry.

#### Witte Entry

Prospecting for hidden earth treasures. To drill for them. Revolution on a small scale. Suppression of a revolt.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 371 (PDF p. 184), `Uranus + Neptun`, Admetos entry.

#### ABC Entry

Restricted technical sensitivity, thorough water technology, sudden blockage through water, technology in deep water, technical compression of air, surprising dissolution of a barrier, and suppression of subtle-material impulses.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restriction and access to hidden or difficult material. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: The entries do not supply a detailed personal psychology. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Suppression of a revolt is Witte's collective example. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Prospecting, drilling, deep-water technology, and a barrier's release. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Technical restriction and the dissolution of a barrier are alternatives; prospecting and suppression of revolt remain separate examples. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

The technical examples concern sensitivity, water, air compression and barriers; Witte separately gives prospecting and political suppression. These are different manifestations, with no direct contradiction established.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Uranus/Neptune through restricted sensitivity, hidden resources, and barriers that can either hold or dissolve. Prospecting and drilling give this a concrete material setting, while deep-water technology and air compression retain the technical range. A small revolution or its suppression forms a separate political example; the entries do not combine these settings into one event. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
