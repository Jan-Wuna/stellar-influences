## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 234, `Uranus + Neptune`, Apollon entry.

#### Witte Entry

Many do not know what they want. Occult science. Crisis and revolution in science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 371 (PDF p. 184), `Uranus + Neptun`, Apollon entry.

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of air technology, success technology of the future, excitement about future knowledge, subtle knowledge impulses, new future science, creeping unrest in trade, unrest through much water, and increasing refinement of technology

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Expanding knowledge and technology amid uncertainty. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Excitement about future knowledge and uncertainty about aims. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Scientific communities and unrest in trade. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Crisis or revolution in science and expansion of air technology. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: The entries juxtapose future-oriented success and uncertainty; they do not establish a contradiction or a guaranteed scientific advance. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Future-oriented success and increasing technical refinement coexist with uncertainty and crisis in science. The entries do not guarantee a scientific advance or establish a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Uranus/Neptune through the widening of uncertain or emerging fields of knowledge. Occult science and crisis within science appear beside future technology and increasing technical refinement. The shared theme is expansion into what is not yet settled, with excitement about knowledge and unrest in trade both represented; the entries do not establish that technological expansion resolves the uncertainty. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
