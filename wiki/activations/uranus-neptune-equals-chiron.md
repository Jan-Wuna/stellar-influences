### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. 104–105, `Planets activating the Uranus/Neptune midpoint`, Chiron entry (chapter begins on PDF p. 104).

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s vibration brings a subtle cosmic element to everything one touches and every person one meets. Healing others through making them aware that they are much greater, more powerful, and wiser than any and all of their problems.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A wider spiritual perspective on personal difficulties. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- psychology: Recognition of capacities that exceed one's current problems. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- body/health: Sandbach's healing language does not supply a medical treatment. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- social/relationship: Encouraging the people one encounters. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- events/manifestations: A shift in how another person understands their difficulties. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach is represented; its cosmic vocabulary is a spiritual claim, not independent proof of healing. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)

## Contradictions

Only Sandbach is represented. Its cosmic and healing language describes a spiritual claim; the entry supplies no bodily treatment or independently established healing effect.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Chiron specifies Uranus/Neptune through encouragement that puts a person's troubles within a wider spiritual perspective. In Sandbach's sole entry, the subtle quality of one's presence helps others recognize capacities greater than their current problems. Its healing language concerns an enlarged sense of wisdom and power; the entry supplies no bodily treatment or independently established therapeutic effect. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
