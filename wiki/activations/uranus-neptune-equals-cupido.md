## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 234, `Uranus + Neptune`, Cupido entry.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden marriage with difficulties. Death in the family. Signs of dissolution in a community or family. Sudden bewilderment in a community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 371 (PDF p. 184), `Uranus + Neptun`, Cupido entry.

#### ABC Entry

A community disappointed by technology, an excited community in the air, a shared event in water, shared technical sensitivity, a metaphysical community experience, a technical community in the air, and sudden weakness in the community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Community vulnerability and uncertainty in shared life. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Sudden bewilderment within a group. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: Witte names family death as a historical event association, not a medical prediction. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Marriage, family, and shared technical or spiritual interests. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Difficult sudden marriage, communal dissolution, and shared water events. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Shared technical and metaphysical experiences coexist with vulnerability; Witte's dissolution and family death examples are not universal outcomes. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Communal technical or metaphysical experience does not itself imply dissolution. Witte's family death and difficult marriage remain distinct severe historical associations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Uranus/Neptune through vulnerability and bewilderment within a community or family. Disappointment through technology and sudden communal weakness accompany shared experiences around water, technical sensitivity and metaphysical interests. Difficult sudden marriage and signs of dissolution extend this uncertainty into shared life; death in the family remains an attributed severe example rather than the outcome of every communal experience. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
