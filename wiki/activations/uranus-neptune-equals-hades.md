## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 234, `Uranus + Neptune`, Hades entry.

#### Witte Entry

To see through treachery (with strong Uranus) or not see through treachery (with strong Neptune). To have to do with corpses as a profession.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 371 (PDF p. 184), `Uranus + Neptun`, Hades entry.

#### ABC Entry

Disappointment through a criminal event, excitement over a lack of air, subtle-material impulses from the past, a dangerous event in water, sudden premonitions from the past, surprising weakness in the air, and unknown technical disadvantages.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Recognition or concealment of harm and disadvantage. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Awareness of treachery is conditional in the Witte entry. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: Witte's occupational contact with corpses is not a diagnosis. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Treachery and the source's profession-related example. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Danger in water and unknown technical disadvantages. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Seeing through treachery or failing to do so are explicitly conditional alternatives, not a direct contradiction. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Witte explicitly gives seeing through treachery with strong Uranus and failing to see through it with strong Neptune. These are conditional alternatives; occupational contact with corpses, water danger and technical disadvantage remain separate examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Uranus/Neptune through concealed harm and uncertain recognition of it. Seeing through treachery is explicitly conditional on strong Uranus, while failing to see through it is conditional on strong Neptune. Disappointment through a criminal event, danger in water and unknown technical disadvantages give concrete forms of harm; premonitions from the past and occupational contact with corpses remain distinct source examples. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
