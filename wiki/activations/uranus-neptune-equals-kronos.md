## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 234, `Uranus + Neptune`, Kronos entry.

#### Witte Entry

Gifted clairvoyant. Leading persons do not know what they want. Probate courts. Human losses of the government. Death among the authorities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 371 (PDF p. 184), `Uranus + Neptun`, Kronos entry.

#### ABC Entry

Special metaphysical experience, particularly restless water, great tension in the air, great technical sensitivity, guidance through subtle-material impulses, creeping unrest in the state, state water technology, and important metaphysical experience.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Authority and state affairs under uncertain or subtle influences. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Indecision among leading people. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: Deaths among authorities remain a historical source example, not a medical prediction. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Leading persons, government, and probate courts. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: State water technology, unrest, and estate-related institutions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Clairvoyance, institutional uncertainty, and environmental examples are separate source manifestations; no causal link between them is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Clairvoyance, uncertain leadership, institutional losses and technical or environmental concerns are different manifestations. The entries establish no causal link among them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Uranus/Neptune through authority faced with uncertain aims and subtle or technical concerns. Witte includes indecisive leaders and probate courts, while ABC places state water technology beside unrest in the state. The source's gifted clairvoyant is a further attributed spiritual example; it does not make uncertain leadership evidence of clairvoyance or turn deaths among authorities into an expected outcome. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
