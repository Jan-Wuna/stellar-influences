## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 234, `Uranus + Neptune`, Poseidon entry.

#### Witte Entry

To gain insight and clarity in occult connections. To clear occult matters and to demonstrate the connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 371 (PDF p. 184), `Uranus + Neptun`, Poseidon entry.

#### ABC Entry

Fine mental impulses, clear future impulses, clarity through sudden intuition, new water technology, sudden clarity through metaphysics, surprising mental weaknesses, refinement of technical ideas, and water culture and technology

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Clarification of subtle connections and technical ideas. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Sudden intuition alongside possible mental weakness. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: ABC's mental-weakness wording is not a medical diagnosis. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Demonstrating connections so that others can understand them. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Clarifying occult matters and developing water technology. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Insight and mental weakness are different expressions in the entries; no direct contradiction or objective proof of occult connections is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Clarity through intuition and metaphysics coexists with unexpected mental weakness. These are different expressions, without establishing uninterrupted clarity or objective proof of the occult connections described.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Uranus/Neptune through clarifying subtle or occult connections and making their relationships intelligible. Sudden intuition and refinement of technical ideas are usable expressions of that search for clarity. ABC also includes unexpected mental weakness, so the experience of insight does not establish uninterrupted clarity or independently verify the occult claims being explained. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
