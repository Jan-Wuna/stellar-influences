## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 234, `Uranus + Neptune`, Vulcanus entry.

#### Witte Entry

Mighty disappointment, suddenly checkmated. The beginning of a large revolution.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 371 (PDF p. 184), `Uranus + Neptun`, Vulcanus entry.

#### ABC Entry

Subtle-material energy impulses, enormous nervousness through uncertainty, new energies from water, sudden force of water, strong subtle-material impulses, tension through weakness of power, technical air power, surprising dissolution of power, and water-power technology.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Power under uncertain conditions and sudden obstruction. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Strong nervousness and disappointment. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: A large revolution is Witte's collective manifestation. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Being checkmated, powerful water events, and water-energy technology. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Power and its weakness or dissolution are alternatives; dissolution is not a successful solution. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Force, nervousness and weakness or dissolution of power coexist. Witte's sudden checkmate and beginning of a large revolution remain alternative severe manifestations, without equating personal disappointment with collective uprising.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Uranus/Neptune through a sudden confrontation with the limits or release of power. Witte describes severe disappointment, being checkmated, and the start of a large revolution; ABC places strong nervousness and forceful water or energy events beside this range. The shared emphasis is intensity under uncertainty, without equating every disappointment with a collective uprising. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
