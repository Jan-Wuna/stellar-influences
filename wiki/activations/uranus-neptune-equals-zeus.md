## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 234, `Uranus + Neptune`, Zeus entry.

#### Witte Entry

To die through fire or automobile. Gas explosion. A revolution which develops successfully.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 371 (PDF p. 184), `Uranus + Neptun`, Zeus entry.

#### ABC Entry

Fiery subtle impulses, creativeness in air technology, new objectives for the future, sudden fire through gas, technical performance in water, surprising performance weakness, refined technical performance, and shaping future technology

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed technical activity and revolutionary development. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Future-oriented creativity; further personal psychology is sparse. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: Witte's fatal fire or automobile examples are historical associations, not predictions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: A revolution that develops successfully in Witte's account. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Gas explosion, new technical goals, and performance involving water or air technology. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Technical achievement and performance weakness remain alternatives; repeated fire wording does not prove independent corroboration. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Technical achievement and performance weakness remain alternatives. Repeated fire wording does not establish independent corroboration or turn Witte's fatal examples into an expected outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Uranus/Neptune through directed technical or revolutionary activity whose outcomes can be sharply unequal. New future goals and refined performance sit beside sudden weakness in performance, while Witte includes a successfully developing revolution. Fire and gas explosion form a separate severe material line; fatal fire or automobile examples remain attributed rather than expected outcomes of creative work. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
