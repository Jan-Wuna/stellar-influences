## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 146, `NODES + URANUS`, Admetos entry.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden breaking off of connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 219 (PDF p. 108), Node/Uranus spread, Admetos entry.

#### ABC Entry

Endurance in a tense relationship, limiting impulses in a relationship, ending a restless connection, strengthening a dynamic relationship, sudden limitations in a connection, and restlessness in a connection through depression

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restriction, endurance, or abrupt ending within a connection. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Tension and persistence in a relationship. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: ABC's depression wording remains attributed, not a diagnosis. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: A relationship may be limited, strengthened, or broken off. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Sudden limitations or the end of a restless bond. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Strengthening and severance are alternative expressions, not proof that every constrained connection ends. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Strengthening and severance are alternative expressions, not proof that every constrained connection ends.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Uranus/Node through limits placed on a tense or rapidly changing connection. Sudden severance is Witte's concise expression, while ABC also allows endurance and the strengthening of a dynamic relationship. The distinction matters: pressure can end a bond or consolidate it, and the entries do not decide between those outcomes. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
