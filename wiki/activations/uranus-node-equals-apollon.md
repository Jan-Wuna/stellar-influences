## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 146, `NODES + URANUS`, Apollon entry.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden business connections or with scientists. A lot of tension in a relationship.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 219 (PDF p. 108), Node/Uranus spread, Apollon entry.

#### ABC Entry

Success through dynamic relationships, conveying impulses toward success, new connections to knowledge, technical trade relations, new connections to the economy, sudden expansion of a connection, and much tension in a relationship.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Expanding contacts in business, science, and knowledge. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Tension can accompany an otherwise successful relationship. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Scientific contacts, trade relations, and new economic connections. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Sudden business acquaintance and expansion of a connection. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Success and relationship tension coexist; the entries do not establish that a useful connection is harmonious. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Success and relationship tension coexist; the entries do not establish that a useful connection is harmonious.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Uranus/Node through sudden expansion of contacts in business, science, or knowledge. Connections can bring success and new economic or technical opportunities, yet considerable tension can remain within the relationship. The formula therefore describes the widening and usefulness of a bond without equating expansion with ease. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
