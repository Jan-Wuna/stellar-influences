### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. 107, `Planets activating the Uranus/Node midpoint`, Chiron entry (chapter begins on PDF p. 106).

#### Sandbach Entry

Your vibration helps others to free themselves from the past as well as to see the past in a new way. You also help others to be more open when they connect with others, and to seek connections that fulfill their own unique needs.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Freedom from past limits through a changed approach to connection. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- psychology: Reconsidering the past and becoming more open. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- social/relationship: Seeking relationships that meet individual needs. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Helping another person view the past differently. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach is represented; its “vibration” vocabulary describes a spiritual interpretation, not an independently established mechanism. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)

## Contradictions

Only Sandbach is represented; its “vibration” vocabulary describes a spiritual interpretation, not an independently established mechanism.

## Derived Synthesis

Chiron specifies Uranus/Node through helping others loosen their ties to the past and become more open in present relationships. Sandbach joins a new view of what has happened with the search for connections that suit a person's unique needs. The single entry describes relational encouragement; it does not supply a medical healing claim or a particular method. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
