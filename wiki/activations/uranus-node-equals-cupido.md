## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 146, `NODES + URANUS`, Cupido entry.

#### Witte Entry

Connection to a quick marriage. Surprisingly connected with one’s own or another family. To have family visitors, or to be a visitor oneself.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 219 (PDF p. 108), Node/Uranus spread, Cupido entry.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting contacts with a community, encounters in a dynamic community, eventful relationships of a community, connected together in rhythm, new contacts in a community, and sudden connections with a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden family or community connection. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Excitement in communal encounters; further psychology is sparse. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Marriage, family visitors, and community membership. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Quick marriage, visits, and new group contacts. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Rhythm is retained as a shared quality; it does not establish a special practice or the duration of a new connection. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Family visits, quick marriage and community contact are different forms of joining or meeting. The shared-rhythm wording remains broad and supplies no special practice or duration of the connection.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Uranus/Node through sudden entry into a family or community. A quick marriage, unexpected family connection, or visit gives the bond a concrete social form, while ABC places new contacts within an active communal life. These are examples of joining and meeting; they do not establish how long the new connection lasts. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
