## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 146, `NODES + URANUS`, Hades entry.

#### Witte Entry

To have trouble with unpleasant matters suddenly. To be entangled in murder affairs. Lack of dynamism in a relationship.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 219 (PDF p. 108), Node/Uranus spread, Hades entry.

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous events in a connection, dangerous tensions in a connection, criminal connection to technology, lack of dynamics in a relationship, disadvantages due to nervousness in a connection, and connections from a troubled past

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Troublesome connections and reduced relational dynamism. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Nervousness and dangerous tension within a bond. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Connections affected by unpleasant matters or a troubled past. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Dangerous events and severe criminal entanglements in the sources. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: The entries' severe examples do not establish the conduct or character of any particular person. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

The entries' severe examples do not establish the conduct or character of any particular person.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Uranus/Node through troublesome or dangerous connections whose energy may be diminished. Sudden entanglement in unpleasant matters sits beside nervous disadvantage and ties to a troubled past. Witte's murder-affair example and ABC's criminal technology connection remain severe attributed manifestations; a difficult relationship alone does not establish criminal conduct. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
