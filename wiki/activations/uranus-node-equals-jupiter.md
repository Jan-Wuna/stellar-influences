## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 146, `NODES + URANUS`, Jupiter entry.

#### Witte Entry

To enjoy advantages. To come into money suddenly through acquaintances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 218 (PDF p. 108), Node/Uranus spread, Jupiter entry.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting financial relationship, successful connection to technology, new financial connections, sudden joy in a connection, tensions through legal mediation, linkage with financial reform, and advantages through new connections.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. 272 (PDF p. 274), code `0968`.

#### Ebertin Entry

A great liking for social functions and entertainment, tendency to seek advantages with the help of other people. Sharing happy experiences in life with others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. 107, `Planets activating the Uranus/Node midpoint`, Jupiter entry (chapter begins on PDF p. 106).

#### Sandbach Entry

Success through freely connecting with other people and through bringing new ideas into the world.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `316`

#### Munkasey Entry

Brings fortunate connections and ties which help others accept your ideas or insights; introducing new scientific or astrological theories; the publication of ideas or theories in respected journals; the mixing of sex and politics.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Benefits and shared joy through new connections. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- psychology: Pleasure in social life and the pursuit of advantage. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- social/relationship: Helpful acquaintances and acceptance of ideas by others. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Sudden money through contacts and publication of theories. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Advantages and shared happiness do not exclude legal tension or self-interest; Munkasey's sex-and-politics example remains source-specific. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)

## Contradictions

Shared happiness and benefits through contacts coexist with tensions through legal mediation and the pursuit of advantage. Munkasey's sex-and-politics example remains source-specific, without defining every helpful connection.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Uranus/Node through advantages and shared happiness arriving by way of contacts. Acquaintances may bring money, while freely exchanged ideas can find acceptance, publication, or scientific discussion. The sources also retain the pursuit of advantage and ABC's tensions through legal mediation, so the usefulness of a connection should not be mistaken for an absence of self-interest or difficulty. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
