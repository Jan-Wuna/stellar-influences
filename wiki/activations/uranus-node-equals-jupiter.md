## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To enjoy advantages. To come into money suddenly through acquaintances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting financial relationship, successful connection to technology, new financial connections, sudden joy in one connection, tensions caused by accounts mismatch, tangle with financial reform, and benefits from new connections

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A great liking for social functions and entertainment, tendency to seek advantages with the help of other people. Sharing happy experiences in life with others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Success through freely connecting with other people and through bringing new ideas into the world.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Brings fortunate connections and ties which help others accept your ideas or insights; introducing new scientific or astrological theories; the publication of ideas or theories in respected journals; the mixing of sex and politics.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Uranus/Node = Jupiter`: benefic networks, adventurous social expansion, and the ability to benefit from bold collective change. Main caution: unstable optimism: excitement about people or causes can outpace realism and produce scattered loyalties.
