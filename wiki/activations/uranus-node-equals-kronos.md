## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 146, `NODES + URANUS`, Kronos entry.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden connections with the power of the state. Independence in new contacts. Special technological relations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 219 (PDF p. 108), Node/Uranus spread, Kronos entry.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting connection with an authority, dynamic connection with the boss, great excitement in a connection, independence in new contacts, tense relationships with authorities, restless connections in the state, and important connections to technology.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: New contacts involving state power or technical authority. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Self-reliance and heightened excitement in connections. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Relations with authorities, a boss, or the state. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Sudden official contacts and specialized technological relations. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Independence and tense relations with authorities coexist; the entries do not decide whether official contact supports or restricts. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Official or technical contacts coexist with independence, excitement and tension toward authorities. These different emphases do not establish whether a given contact is supportive or restrictive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Uranus/Node through sudden connections with authority, a superior, or specialized technology. Independence and self-reliance in new contacts stand alongside excitement and tense state relations. The governing issue is access to power while retaining one's independence; the entries do not decide whether the connection is supportive or restrictive. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
