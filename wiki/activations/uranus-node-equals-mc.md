## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 146, `NODES + URANUS`, MC entry.

#### Witte Entry

The desire for innovations. To be connected with new things. Emotional excitement during contacts. Moment (minute) of a sudden contact.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 218 (PDF p. 108), Node/Uranus spread, MC entry.

#### ABC Entry

My excitement during encounters, my new contacts, my connections to technology, my unrest during contacts, the minute of an excited encounter, emotional unrest during encounters, and soul impulses in a connection.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. 273 (PDF p. 275), code `0973`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Fondness of change and variety, tendency to seek stimulating ideas from other people. Sudden recognition of a joint goal, desire to bring plans to fruition together with others.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. 107, `Planets activating the Uranus/Node midpoint`, Midheaven entry (chapter begins on PDF p. 106).

#### Sandbach Entry

Your dharma in this lifetime is to help others to free themselves up to grow, to release themselves from limiting ties of the past, and to follow their own unique destiny.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `316`

#### Munkasey Entry

The popularity and acclaim you achieve due to the acceptance of your ideas and theories; personal assurances you receive from those who share your destiny about the help and assistance you will receive from them.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal involvement in new contacts and shared objectives. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- psychology: Fondness for variety and stimulation from others. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- social/relationship: Joint plans, encouragement, and help in loosening past ties. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Sudden contacts, recognition of a common goal, and acceptance of ideas. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Collaboration and spiritual purpose remain distinct; Sandbach's dharma does not independently establish destiny or acclaim. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)

## Contradictions

Personally felt excitement and shared aims coexist with support or recognition from others. Sandbach's dharma is its spiritual account of a life purpose, not an independently verified destiny or guarantee of acclaim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Uranus/Node through personally felt excitement in new contacts and the recognition of a shared goal. Other people supply stimulating ideas, support, or acceptance of one's work, while Sandbach gives helping others release limiting past ties as a life-purpose interpretation. That spiritual purpose and the practical experience of collaboration remain distinguishable; neither guarantees acclaim. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
