## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 146, `NODES + URANUS`, Poseidon entry.

#### Witte Entry

Connections through common mental efforts. Connections through common search for truth and understanding. Wanting to clear up something together with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 219 (PDF p. 108), Node/Uranus spread, Poseidon entry.

#### ABC Entry

Dynamism of a thoughtful relationship, connections for intellectual reform, clear impulses in a connection, mediating cultural events, new cultural connections, connections between mind and technology, and unrest through truth in a connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connection through joint inquiry and clarification. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: A wish for understanding alongside unrest prompted by truth. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Common mental effort and new cultural connections. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Investigating or clarifying a matter together. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: A shared search and unrest over truth can coexist; inquiry does not establish that the sought conclusion is already known. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Shared inquiry, culture and intellectual reform coexist with unrest prompted by truth. The entries describe a search and a wish to clarify together, without establishing that participants already possess the truth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Uranus/Node through a shared search for truth and understanding. Connections form around mental effort, cultural interests, and the wish to clarify something together, while truth can also bring unrest within the relationship. The entries support an active intellectual bond without establishing that its participants already possess the truth they seek. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
