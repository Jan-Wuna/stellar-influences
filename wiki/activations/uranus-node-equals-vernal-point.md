## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 146, `NODES + URANUS`, Vernal Point entry.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden events. Exciting connections in the general public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 218 (PDF p. 108), Node/Uranus spread, Vernal Point entry.

#### ABC Entry

exciting connections in public life, sudden encounters in public, mediation of tension through the public, restless connections in public life, and connections to technology in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden publicly visible connections. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Restlessness and excitement in public relations. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Encounters within the general public. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Sudden public events and public technical connections. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: ABC “mediation of tension” does not establish whether tension is transmitted or resolved; no specific mediation procedure is inferred. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

ABC “mediation of tension” does not establish whether tension is transmitted or resolved; no specific mediation procedure is inferred.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Uranus/Node through sudden encounters and stimulating connections in public life. Technical contacts and tension involving the wider public give the relational excitement a collective setting. The entries identify this public sphere without naming its participants, cause, or lasting result. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
