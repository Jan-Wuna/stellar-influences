## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 146, `NODES + URANUS`, Vulcanus entry.

#### Witte Entry

Suddenly connected with mighty influential people or with superiors. Examiners.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 219 (PDF p. 108), Node/Uranus spread, Vulcanus entry.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting influence of a connection, energy impulses in a relationship, great nervousness in a relationship, powerful tensions in a connection, reform of power connections, and connection through enormous events.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden contact with strong influence or superior power. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Great nervousness and powerful relational tension. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Influential people, superiors, and examiners. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: A connection formed through a major event. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Powerful influence and nervous tension coexist; a contact's effect is not determined by the entry. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Influence, powerful tension and reform of power connections coexist with sudden contact with superiors or examiners. The entries do not determine whether that influence assists, tests or constrains.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Uranus/Node through a sudden connection with powerful or influential people. The contact can involve superiors or examiners, while ABC emphasizes nervousness and powerful tension within the bond. The intensity of the connection is clear, but the entries do not decide whether influence assists, tests, or constrains the person. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
