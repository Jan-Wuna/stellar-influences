## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 146, `NODES + URANUS`, Zeus entry.

#### Witte Entry

To be forced into a union, or to get your way by force. To be visited by fire or explosion. To expect progeny. Tensions in a creative connection.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 219 (PDF p. 108), Node/Uranus spread, Zeus entry.

#### ABC Entry

Efforts of a dynamic connection, beginning of a new connection, achievements in a dynamic connection, nervous striving toward a goal in a relationship, a disturbed procreative connection, suddenly connected with fire, and tensions in a creative connection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Forceful formation of a connection and effort toward a goal. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Nervous striving and tension in creative participation. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: Expecting progeny and a disturbed procreative connection are distinct historical examples, not reproductive predictions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: A forced union or a dynamic creative relationship. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: New connections, achievement, fire, and explosion in the sources. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Expected progeny and disturbed procreative connection remain distinct historical examples, not pregnancy or infertility predictions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Creative effort and achievement coexist with force or tension. Witte's expecting progeny and ABC's disturbed procreative connection are different historical reproductive examples, without establishing pregnancy, infertility or a bodily diagnosis; fire and explosion remain separate possibilities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Uranus/Node through effort, force, and tension in forming a connection or pursuing a shared goal. Creative achievement and a new union sit beside being compelled into a union or forcing one's way. Witte also names fire, explosion, and expecting progeny as concrete manifestations; these remain separate attributed possibilities rather than the inevitable result of a tense relationship. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
