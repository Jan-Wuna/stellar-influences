## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 235, `URANUS + PLUTO`, Admetos entry.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden commencing of inner development and transformation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 373 (PDF p. 185), Uranus/Pluto spread, Admetos entry.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting changes through restrictions, blocked technical developments, sudden suppression of a development, tensions through growing restrictions, surprising thorough changes, and deepening into new developments.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Inner transformation amid restricted or blocked development. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Deepening into new developments, with tension as restrictions grow. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is supplied. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Restrictions are described without specifying a particular relationship or group. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Sudden suppression or blockage of development and surprising thorough change. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Beginning inner transformation and blocked external development are different possibilities; no inevitable breakthrough is established. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Witte's sudden inner transformation and ABC's restriction, suppression and deepening are different emphases. The entries do not establish whether a blocked development will resume.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies Uranus/Pluto through transformation that deepens while development encounters limits. Sudden inner change can coexist with blocked technical development, suppression and growing tension from restrictions. The surprising thoroughness of a change is therefore distinct from easy outward progress; the entries do not decide whether a particular barrier will be overcome. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
