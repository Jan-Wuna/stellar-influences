### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden changes of the place, the view of the place, as for instance scenic view obstructed through new buildings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting changes at the site, dynamic developments with the partner, sudden change of location, rhythmic changes in the environment, technical changes at the site, iibcrra-;chender partner changes, and growing tensions at the site

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Being placed in an unusual or restless environment. Applications of force, an accident.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One has an inherent way of surprising other people, and/or of catching them off guard. A person who cannot be categorized, and who is unpredictable, often in the extreme. One identifies with the role of being a bringer of radical change and/or innovation.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

An enhanced ability to focus on the kind of world which must grow out of today into tomorrow; allows you to read others and their restlessness with the status quo; an increased need to appear unique as an individual.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Uranus/Pluto = Asc`: catalytic individuality, rebellious magnetism, and the ability to force personal reinvention or environmental upheaval quickly. Main caution: destabilizing extremity: self-presentation can become too shocking, volatile, or destructive to sustain workable contact.
