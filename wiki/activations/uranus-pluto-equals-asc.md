## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 235, `URANUS + PLUTO`, Asc entry.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden changes of the place, the view of the place, as for instance scenic view obstructed through new buildings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 372 (PDF p. 185), Uranus/Pluto spread, Asc entry.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting changes at the site, dynamic developments with the partner, sudden change of location, rhythmic changes in the environment, technical changes at the site, surprising change of partner, and growing tensions at the site.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. 270 (PDF p. 272), code `0960`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Being placed in an unusual or restless environment. Applications of force, an accident.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. 106, `Planets activating the Uranus/Pluto midpoint`, Ascendant entry (chapter begins on PDF p. 105).

#### Sandbach Entry

One has an inherent way of surprising other people, and/or of catching them off guard. A person who cannot be categorized, and who is unpredictable, often in the extreme. One identifies with the role of being a bringer of radical change and/or innovation.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `312`

#### Munkasey Entry

An enhanced ability to focus on the kind of world which must grow out of today into tomorrow; allows you to read others and their restlessness with the status quo; an increased need to appear unique as an individual.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden environmental or relational change and distinctive personal response. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- psychology: Unpredictability, identification with innovation and attention to others' restlessness. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- body/health: Ebertin's accident and applications of force remain attributed event associations. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- social/relationship: Dynamic partner developments and a wish to appear unique. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Changes of location, obstructed views through buildings and local technical change. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Physical changes of place and a radical personal style are separate manifestations; unpredictability does not establish destructive behavior. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)

## Contradictions

Witte's altered place or view, ABC's partner and local changes, and Sandbach's unpredictable identity are different emphases. Ebertin's force and accident examples remain attributed, without establishing an inevitable outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Uranus/Pluto through a changing environment and a distinctive response to other people. A move, new buildings that obstruct a view, or technical changes at a site give sudden change a literal setting; partner developments and growing local tension add a relational dimension. Identifying with innovation or surprising others is another expression of this range, while force and accident remain attributed severe possibilities. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
