## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. 106, `Planets activating the Uranus/Pluto midpoint`, Chiron entry (chapter begins on PDF p. 105).

#### Sandbach Entry

One’s vibration helps others to open up and to be more free, and even eventu ally to instigate profound changes in one’s life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Encouraging openness and greater freedom that can lead to profound change. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- psychology: Opening up and loosening personal constraint. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- body/health: No bodily or medical treatment is supplied. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- social/relationship: One's presence is described as helping others become freer. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Profound change can follow the openness described. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Only Sandbach is represented; vibration is spiritual vocabulary without an independently established mechanism. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)

## Contradictions

The single entry links openness, freedom and eventual profound change. It supplies no specific method or medical healing claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Chiron specifies Uranus/Pluto through helping others open up and become freer, with profound change as a possible extension of that openness. Sandbach describes this through the spiritual vocabulary of one's vibration. The single entry offers a relational theme without specifying a method, a medical effect, or the outcome of a particular change. ([Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).)
