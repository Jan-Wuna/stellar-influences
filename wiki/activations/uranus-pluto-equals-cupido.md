## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 235, `URANUS + PLUTO`, Cupido entry.

#### Witte Entry

Surprising changes in the family through marriages, births or deaths.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 373 (PDF p. 185), Uranus/Pluto spread, Cupido entry.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting changes in a community, dynamic change in art, new development in art, sudden transformations in a community, a changed shared rhythm, and growing tensions in a community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Transformation of family or communal life and artistic expression. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Communal excitement and growing tension; individual psychology is sparse. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: Births and deaths are Witte's historical family-event examples, not medical predictions. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Family changes through marriage and changing communal patterns. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: New art developments, sudden community changes and changed shared rhythm. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Family, community and art are distinct settings; growing tension does not make every change a loss. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Witte's family changes through marriage, birth or death and ABC's community and art changes are different settings. They do not establish that artistic change causes family events or that all communal change is harmful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Uranus/Pluto through sudden changes in shared life and expression. Marriage, birth or death can change a family, while community transformations alter a shared rhythm and may bring growing tension. Dynamic changes and new developments in art extend the same communal range without implying that every artistic or social change entails a family loss. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
