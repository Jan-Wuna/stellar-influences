## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 235, `URANUS + PLUTO`, Hades entry.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden very unpleasant changes. Changes which are very unwillingly accepted.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 373 (PDF p. 185), Uranus/Pluto spread, Hades entry.

#### ABC Entry

Sorrow through growing tensions, deficient development of technology, disadvantageous technical unfolding, sudden dangerous development, surprising criminal unfolding, hidden technical development, and renunciation of technical developments.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Unwelcome change and disadvantageous or concealed development. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Sorrow under growing tension and reluctance to accept change. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is supplied. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Changes can be accepted very unwillingly; criminal development remains a severe attributed example. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Deficient, dangerous or hidden technical development and giving up technical developments. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Hidden or deficient development does not by itself establish criminal activity; the entries do not provide a specific incident. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Witte's unpleasant and unwillingly accepted change aligns in emphasis with ABC's sorrow and disadvantage. Dangerous and criminal development remain separate severe examples; hidden technical work alone does not establish wrongdoing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Uranus/Pluto through changes experienced as unwelcome, deficient or disadvantageous. Growing tension can bring sorrow, while technical development may be hidden, become dangerous, or be relinquished. The source also names criminal unfolding as a severe possibility; secrecy or resistance to change alone does not establish criminal conduct. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
