## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 235, `URANUS + PLUTO`, Kronos entry.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden development of leadership. To make oneself a leader. Sudden changes in the power of the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 373 (PDF p. 185), Uranus/Pluto spread, Kronos entry.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting changes in the state, special exciting developments, large technical developments, restless developments in the state, state reform development, growing superiority of technology, and important changes through reforms.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development of leadership and changes in state power or technology. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- psychology: Making oneself a leader; further personal psychology is sparse. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is supplied. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- social/relationship: Leadership, the state and state reform. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Large technical developments and sudden changes in state power. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Growing technical superiority is source wording, not proof that every new technology is better or that leadership is beneficial. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)

## Contradictions

Leadership and state power provide Witte's main setting; ABC adds technical development and restless state reform. The entries do not settle the political value or success of these changes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Uranus/Pluto through sudden development of leadership and changes in state power. Reform and large technical developments extend this institutional setting, with restlessness in the state and growing technical superiority among ABC's expressions. Becoming a leader and directing significant change are represented, without establishing that a particular leader or reform is beneficial. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry).)
