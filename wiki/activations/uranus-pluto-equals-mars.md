## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: PDF p. 235, `URANUS + PLUTO`, Mars entry.

#### Witte Entry

Sudden change in activity. To carry out activity after a quickly set up plan.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source reference: printed p. 372 (PDF p. 185), Uranus/Pluto spread, Mars entry.

#### ABC Entry

Work rhythm change, dynamic development impulses, development of new activity, sudden change of work, development of reform activity, technical development activity, and change of work through technology

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source reference: printed p. 269 (PDF p. 271), code `0955`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Fanaticism, act of violence, mania of destruction, a state of precipitation. "The stage of bending or breaking." Injury, accident.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source reference: PDF p. 105–106, `Planets activating the Uranus/Pluto midpoint`, Mars entry (chapter begins on PDF p. 105).

#### Sandbach Entry

To assert oneself in extremely uninhibited fashion. To engage in extreme rebellion that is potentially highly destructive. Working hard to effect radical changes.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `312`

#### Munkasey Entry

You can exhibit many forms of anger at others, and enjoy devising loud or disruptive activities to purposely make others feel uncomfortable; you can be impulsive, headstrong, or demanding when forcing your ways and ideas.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Forceful action and rapid change of work or activity. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- psychology: Uninhibited self-assertion, anger, impulsiveness and potentially destructive rebellion. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- body/health: Ebertin's injury and accident remain attributed severe associations. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- social/relationship: Demanding or disruptive behavior toward others in Munkasey's account. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- events/manifestations: Quickly planned action, changed work rhythms and reform or technical work. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
- conflicts/notes: Technical work and hard effort coexist with violent or destructive possibilities; the latter are not inevitable outcomes. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)

## Contradictions

Witte and ABC describe changed activity and technical or reform work, while Ebertin, Sandbach and Munkasey retain force, anger and destructive extremes. These different expressions do not make every energetic change violent.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Uranus/Pluto through forceful action that changes work, methods or activity quickly. A rapidly formed plan and hard effort can advance reform or technical development, while uninhibited assertion can also become anger, coercive demands or destructive rebellion. Injury and accident remain attributed severe examples; the entries do not make them the necessary outcome of decisive action. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-entry); [Ebertin entry](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry).)
