## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Actions of the glands change suddenly. Sudden changes in the public for several persons, especially females.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting changes of a people, excited feelings development, sudden change of a woman, rhythm change of a woman, technical development of a people, transformation of feelings impulses, and changing feelings impulses

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Intuition, restlessness, daring and audacious, ambition, determination. Bringing about changes by force.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Feelings which sometimes come forth with extreme openness and freedom. The emotional need to be oneself, no matter what the cost, and to make change happen. An extreme need to overcome the impressions that others make on one.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

A sensitivity and awareness of the needs of others, despite desires to work alone; your insight into tradition allows you to introduce new and unique ways for representing old methods in newly devised explanations.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Uranus/Pluto = Moon`: radical emotional awakening, intense instinctive change, and the ability to feel collective rupture personally. Main caution: nervous or emotional extremity: the inner life can become too unstable, compulsive, or crisis-driven to rest safely.
