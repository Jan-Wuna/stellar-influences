## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 235 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Sudden commencing of development which cannot be judged as to duration, direction and process. The step into the unknown.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 373; PDF p. 185.

#### ABC Entry

Subtle impulses of transformation, a creeping change in the air, a surprising change in the air, unrest through creeping changes, growing excitement through water, change in water through technology.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source locator: printed p. 269, entry 0958.

#### Ebertin Entry

Sympathetic understanding of other people, knowledge of human nature, deep study of a subject of special interest, the inclination to delve into the supernatural realms. Insecurity and uncertainty, unfulfilled wishes, fatigue, nervous diseases (neuroses).

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source locator: PDF p. 105 (chapter start only; exact entry page not established).

#### Sandbach Entry

To be able to effect a profound revolution in a subtle, hidden, and/or unseen way. To harbor extremely strange ideals and imaginings. Extreme changes which create chaos and/or confusion.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `URANUS/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `312`

#### Munkasey Entry

You spend much time with your own dreams of what reality is and how the non-physical and physical worlds operate; you enjoy astrology and the occult; you desire to use computers and new-age sciences to assist you.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry), [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).

- core meaning: Change whose direction and duration remain unknown.
- psychology: Unusual ideals, close study and interest in unseen realities.
- body/health: Ebertin historically associates fatigue and neuroses; these are attributed claims.
- social/relationship: Ebertin includes sympathetic understanding of others.
- events/manifestations: Hidden revolution, confusion or uncertain change; ABC separately names changes in air and water through technology.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's six-clause Neptune row ends with water changed through technology; the following “see above” belongs to Pluto.

## Contradictions

The entries describe uncertain or subtle change across different subjects. ABC's actual Neptune row supplies six clauses about subtle transformation, creeping changes, air and water. The old one-line water translation lost most of that passage and absorbed Pluto's “see above”; the row has been restored from printed p. 373 (PDF p. 185).

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Pluto = Neptune`, Neptune specifies sudden development through uncertainty about its course and through subtle or unseen forms of change. Insight into people, sustained investigation and unusual ideals coexist with confusion or unfulfilled wishes in [Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) and [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry). [ABC](#abc-entry) adds subtle transformation and creeping changes, including literal air and water images; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) locates inquiry in physical and nonphysical realities and new sciences.
