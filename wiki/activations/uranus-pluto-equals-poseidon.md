## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 235 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To gain surprising survey, insight and clarity in mental or spiritual matters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 373; PDF p. 185.

#### ABC Entry

Intellectual development impulses, developing cultural reform, new intellectual development, surprising unfolding of truth, change through spiritual impulses, rational development of reform, growing mental tensions, transformation through cultural events.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Unexpected clarity and perspective within intellectual or spiritual development.
- psychology: Insight alongside growing mental tension.
- body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Cultural reform and cultural events are ABC's stated collective settings.
- events/manifestations: A surprising overview, unfolding truth and rational development of reform.
- conflicts/notes: All eight ABC clauses belong to Poseidon; its opening was formerly absorbed into Vulcanus.

## Contradictions

Clarity in Witte and growing mental tension in ABC remain different possible aspects of development. ABC also supplies intellectual impulses, cultural reform and the unfolding of truth. The complete eight-clause row has been restored from printed p. 373 (PDF p. 185), including “vernünftige Reform-Entwicklung”, rather than the former fragment “form development”.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Pluto = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies sudden development as an unexpected gain in mental or spiritual perspective. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes overview, insight and clarity. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds intellectual development, cultural reform and surprising unfolding of truth, while retaining growing mental tension and transformation through cultural events. A broader perspective can emerge without implying that every change resolves the tension.
