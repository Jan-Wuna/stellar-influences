## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Abrupt disruption of hitherto development. Separation that cause suddenly changes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

growing unrest due to losses and changing problems due to nervousness see above

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

One-sidedness, self-will, egoism, stubbornness, hatred. Exposure to extremely great pressure by others, separation caused through major force.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To have an intense desire for change, innovation and even revolution, but to either be afraid of this, and/or careful, cautious, and patient about engaging in it.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Diminishes your needs to appear rebellious, but does not dispel them; your ability to persist despite obstacles on your path toward reform is enhanced; you have many desires to be alone, and ask others to respect your solitude.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Uranus/Pluto = Saturn`: hard reform, crisis endurance, and the ability to restructure what is decaying under immense pressure. Main caution: rupture under compression: tension can become brutal, repressive, or catastrophic when breakthrough and control deadlock each other.
