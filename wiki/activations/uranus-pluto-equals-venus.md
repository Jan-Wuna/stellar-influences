## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Affections and friendships which start with surprise.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Harmonious impulses transform, sudden harmonious development, new artificial developments, sudden change of affection, surprising peace development, growing harmony impulses, and changing love tensions

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Creative power, desire to bring the new in art, constant longing for the new. The conquest of love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To find beauty in that which is extremely unusual, strange, and/or radically experimental. One’s love relationships may go through extreme changes. One is at tracted to eccentric or intensely unique people.
