## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Sudden changed conditions in public life.
- psychology: ABC describes public unrest and growing tension.
- body/health: No health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: The public collectively adapts to changed circumstances.
- events/manifestations: Public reforms and technical changes in public life.
- conflicts/notes: Public scope does not specify a particular political event.

## Contradictions

The excerpts share a public-change theme but give no named event or guaranteed outcome. ABC's unrest is a possible expression, not a statement that every reform produces disorder.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Pluto = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point places sudden development in public life: changed conditions require collective adaptation. [Witte](#witte-entry) states that demand directly; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds reforms, technical change, unrest and growing tension. Together they describe the social reach of change without identifying a specific event or fixing its outcome.
