## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 243 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To explain, to clear up, to enlighten together with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 389; PDF p. 193.

#### ABC Entry

Disquieting truth for a community, a dynamic intellectual community, intellectual renewal within a community, a shared cultural event, new shared ideas, sudden clarity within a community, a community of technical ideas.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Explaining and clarifying together, with intellectual renewal within a community.
- psychology: Shared ideas and sudden clarity; a truth can also disturb the group.
- body/health: Neither entry specifies a bodily condition.
- social/relationship: Collaborative enlightenment and a community formed around intellectual or technical ideas.
- events/manifestations: Shared cultural events, new common ideas and collective clarification.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes disquieting truth alongside renewal; Witte emphasizes the joint act of explaining.

## Contradictions

The entries share collective clarification as a theme. ABC also allows truth to be disquieting for a community; this qualifies the shared activity without contradicting Witte's act of explaining. The former policy-and-structural-funds translation has been replaced from the seven clauses of the Cupido row on printed p. 389 (PDF p. 193).

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Poseidon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies mental illumination as a shared activity: people explain, clarify or seek understanding together. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies the joint act of enlightenment, while [ABC](#abc-entry) extends it to intellectual renewal, new common ideas and shared cultural events. A community may gain sudden clarity or organize around technical ideas, although the truth it encounters can also be disquieting.
