## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 243 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To bring light and clarity into an evil and dark or obscure matter. To make things of the past come to life again.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 389; PDF p. 193.

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous technical ideas, spiritual impulses from the past, a deficient cultural event, sudden clarity in distress, exciting criminal insights, surprising mental distress, hidden cultural renewal, forgoing new ideas.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Clarifying an obscure matter or reviving something from the past.
- psychology: Insight in difficult circumstances; ABC also mentions mental distress.
- body/health: Mental distress is ABC's wording, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: The excerpts do not define a particular relationship.
- events/manifestations: Past matters come to life; obscure or troubling subjects become clearer.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes dangerous ideas and forgoing new ideas alongside the possibility of clarification.

## Contradictions

Illumination and distress are different possible aspects of encountering difficult material, not a demonstrated contradiction. ABC also retains dangerous technical ideas, criminal insights and forgoing new ideas; Witte emphasizes clarification and reviving the past.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Poseidon = Hades`, Hades specifies mental illumination through difficult, obscure or past material. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes bringing clarity to a dark matter and making the past vivid again; [ABC](#abc-entry) also allows sudden clarity in need and mental distress. The shared focus is what becomes visible, while the emotional result remains variable.
