## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 243 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To be mentally alive and alert. Mental preparedness and tenseness. To strive for light, knowledge and truth.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 388; PDF p. 193.

#### ABC Entry

An excited mind-soul, my restless mind, my spiritual renewal, my spiritual impulses, my cultural renewal, my new ideas, my sudden clarity, a new culture of the soul, emotional arousal through truth.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Personal mental alertness and the search for truth.
- psychology: Preparedness, tension and a restless mind.
- body/health: The excerpts give no distinct bodily condition.
- social/relationship: No particular relationship is established.
- events/manifestations: New ideas and sudden personal clarity.
- conflicts/notes: The compound mind-soul and first-person wording belong to ABC's source vocabulary.

## Contradictions

Witte's alertness and tension accompany ABC's personal renewal, restlessness and arousal through truth. These are compatible emphases, without establishing a medical condition. The formerly damaged ABC opening and closing have been checked against printed p. 388 (PDF p. 193).

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Poseidon = MC`, MC specifies mental or spiritual stimulation as personal alertness: being prepared, feeling tension and actively seeking knowledge or truth. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies this clear account. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds personal spiritual and cultural renewal, a restless mind, new ideas and sudden clarity; truth itself can arouse the emotions.
