## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 243 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To become tense as the result of new ideas. Visionary recognition. To send or receive a sign of life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 388; PDF p. 193.

#### ABC Entry

Reports about cultural renewal, intellectual impulses from young people, conversations about intellectual renewal, ideas for transport technology, new insights of young people, sudden clarity of thought, news about cultural events, surprising truths in the media.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: New ideas becoming thought, recognition or communication.
- psychology: Visionary recognition and tension from ideas.
- body/health: No separate bodily condition is given.
- social/relationship: Sending or receiving a sign of life.
- events/manifestations: News about cultural events and sudden clarity of thought.
- conflicts/notes: ABC specifies conversations and reports about renewal, rather than a separate doctrine of emanation.

## Contradictions

The tension of new ideas and clarity of thought can occur together; the excerpts do not establish a contradiction. ABC's reports, conversations, youth and media supply communication settings, while Witte also names visionary recognition and signs of life.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Poseidon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies mental stimulation through ideas and messages. New understanding may feel tense or visionary, while communication carries it between people; [Witte](#witte-entry) even includes sending or receiving a sign of life. [ABC](#abc-entry) broadens the communication setting to news, media and cultural events, with sudden clarity of thought as a related expression.
