## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 243 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Hours of mental tenseness and alertness. Persons who are mentally alert. Persons who are possessed with an idea. Public propaganda. Propaganda for political elections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 388; PDF p. 193.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting truth for a woman, spiritual impulses for a woman, a cultural event for the people, cultural renewal of a people, new ideas for a woman, new ideas for a people, sudden clarity about a woman, a spiritually excited woman, surprising clarity of feeling.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Mental alertness and public excitement around ideas.
- psychology: Tension or being preoccupied with an idea.
- body/health: No specific bodily condition is established.
- social/relationship: Witte includes public and election propaganda; ABC uses historical gendered examples.
- events/manifestations: Arousing attention to ideas in a public or cultural setting.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's gendered vocabulary is source-specific, not universal.

## Contradictions

Witte's alertness and propaganda differ in scope from ABC's gendered and cultural examples. The sources' references to women and a people remain historical formulations; they are not universal traits. The ABC closing phrase is clarity of feeling, verified on printed p. 388 (PDF p. 193).

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Poseidon = Moon`, Moon specifies mental stimulation as alertness, tension and an idea's hold on attention. [Witte](#witte-entry) carries this into public propaganda and elections. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds cultural events and new ideas for a people, alongside explicitly gendered historical examples; those examples do not establish a trait shared by all women.
