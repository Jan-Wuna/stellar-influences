## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 243 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Not recognizing the truth. To deceive oneself. To twist and spin the truth around. To make believe or deceive. To be deceived.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 389; PDF p. 193.

#### ABC Entry

Subtle spiritual impulses, intellectual renewal of metaphysics, denial of a cultural renewal, new insights in metaphysics, new ideas for the future, new cultural expectations, surprising clarity through intuition, refinement through new insights.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Obscured or distorted truth in Witte; subtle spiritual impulses and intuitive clarity in ABC.
- psychology: Self-deception or failure to recognize truth contrasts with intuitive insight and new expectations.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Being deceived or conveying a deceptive account.
- events/manifestations: Distortion of a matter that calls for clarity, or new metaphysical insights and ideas for the future.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's deception and ABC's intuitive clarity remain distinct possibilities; ABC also names denial of cultural renewal.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes deception, whereas ABC includes intuitive clarity and metaphysical insight. This is a material difference: neither claim cancels the other or makes intuition a guarantee of truth. The ABC Neptune row has eight clauses ending “Verfeinerung durch neue Einsichten”; the following reform-development and evolutionary-insight clauses belong to Pluto and have been removed from this entry after scan inspection of printed p. 389 (PDF p. 193).

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Poseidon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the search for truth through a tension between subtle insight and an unreliable grasp of truth. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes missing, distorting or concealing truth, including self-deception. [ABC](#abc-entry) describes spiritual impulses, new metaphysical insights and surprising clarity through intuition, while also allowing denial of cultural renewal. The page retains insight and deception as distinct claims; an intuitive impression does not establish its own truth.
