## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 243 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To be connected with representatives of an idea. To have dealings with propagandists.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 388; PDF p. 193.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting truth in a connection, spiritual impulses in a relationship, new insights in a connection, new cultural connections, sudden clarity in a connection, linking technical ideas, a surprising spiritual connection.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Connections formed around an idea.
- psychology: Insight or spiritual impulses experienced in a relationship.
- body/health: No health claim is stated.
- social/relationship: Association with representatives of ideas or propagandists.
- events/manifestations: New cultural connections and the linking of technical ideas.
- conflicts/notes: An association's existence does not establish the truth of its ideas.

## Contradictions

ABC's relational clarity and Witte's propaganda contacts are not contradictory: they describe the content and carriers of ideas from different angles. Neither guarantees that an associated person's claim is true.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Poseidon = Node`, Node specifies mental or spiritual stimulation through connections with people who carry an idea. [Witte](#witte-entry) includes representatives and propagandists; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds shared insights, cultural connections and technical ideas brought together. The organizing theme is an association shaped by ideas, whose truth still requires a separate judgment.
