## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 243 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To create light, to enlighten, to back truth with clarity. To awake interest, to give mental stimulant. To bring to life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 389; PDF p. 193.

#### ABC Entry

Intellectual reform and development, new insights into evolution, tensions through the unfolding of ideas, developing surprising insight, changes through cultural renewal, changes through new ideas, growing cultural reform.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Ideas developing into clearer understanding and renewed interest.
- psychology: Interest awakened by mental stimulation, surprising insights and tension as ideas unfold.
- body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Witte describes enlightening or stimulating others.
- events/manifestations: Changes through new ideas and growing cultural reform.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes tensions in the unfolding of ideas alongside cultural reform; Witte emphasizes illumination and awakened interest.

## Contradictions

The entries share an ideas-and-development emphasis, without proving independent corroboration. ABC also names tensions as ideas unfold. Scan inspection of printed p. 389 (PDF p. 193) restores the complete seven-clause Pluto row beginning “Geistige Reform-Entwicklung”; its opening was formerly absorbed into Neptune, and “cultural emanation” has been corrected to cultural renewal.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Poseidon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies mental illumination as an unfolding process: interest is awakened, ideas develop and clearer understanding can bring a matter to life. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies the act of enlightening and stimulating; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds intellectual reform, new insights into evolution and change through cultural renewal or new ideas. This development can carry tension as ideas unfold, alongside the possibility of surprising insight and growing cultural reform.
