## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 243 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Expiring life or light. To give up the mental soul, to breathe ones last. Death occurs.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 389; PDF p. 193.

#### ABC Entry

Tasks of cultural renewal, a serious cultural event, intellectual tasks of technology, disturbed mental concentration, sudden clarity through concentration, problems of spiritual renewal, surprising problems with truth.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: A severe ending in Witte; concentration and difficulties with truth in ABC.
- psychology: ABC includes disturbed concentration, clarity through concentration and problems with truth.
- body/health: Witte's historical entry explicitly associates dying; it is not a mortality prediction.
- social/relationship: The entries do not define a relationship.
- events/manifestations: Witte gives extinction of life or light; ABC gives cultural and technical tasks.
- conflicts/notes: The sources' very different scopes must remain visible.

## Contradictions

Witte's literal dying language and ABC's concentration and cultural tasks are substantially different claims; the excerpts do not explain their relationship. ABC includes both disturbed concentration and clarity through concentration. Its seven clauses have been checked against printed p. 389 (PDF p. 193).

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Poseidon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies a severe limit in [Witte's historical entry](#witte-entry), which explicitly associates the formula with expiring life or light and dying. [ABC](#abc-entry) instead describes cultural and technical tasks, disturbed concentration, clarity through concentration and difficulties concerning truth. These cannot be reduced to one confident outcome: the bodily claim remains attributed to Witte, and neither excerpt explains the divergence.
