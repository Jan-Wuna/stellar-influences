## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 243 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Days of mental tension and tenseness. To be excited or provoked through opinions, ideas or thoughts. The man who appears as the bearer of an idea, who represents an idea, or conviction. To incite propaganda.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 388; PDF p. 193.

#### ABC Entry

A dynamic mind in the body, impulses of the mental body, a new spiritual center, new ideas of a man, personal spiritual renewal, a personal cultural event, a spiritually excited body, surprising clarity for a person.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: A person representing an idea amid mental excitement.
- psychology: Provocation or tension through opinions and convictions.
- body/health: ABC uses body-and-spirit language without establishing a medical condition.
- social/relationship: Witte includes acting as an idea's representative and inciting propaganda.
- events/manifestations: Personal cultural activity and ideas put forward by a person.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered examples belong to the sources' historical vocabulary.

## Contradictions

The sources' personal and bodily language does not make their claims universal or exclusively male. ABC's renewal wording and final clause, surprising clarity for a person, have been restored from printed p. 388 (PDF p. 193).

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Poseidon = Sun`, Sun specifies mental stimulation through a person who bears or represents an idea. Opinions can excite or provoke, and conviction may become public advocacy or propaganda in [Witte](#witte-entry). [ABC](#abc-entry) adds personal cultural activity and bodily language; its references to a man remain historical examples rather than a restriction on who can express the formula.
