## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 243 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Forceful insight and clear sight. To be a great mental stimulant. To create mental tenseness. To draw mighty attention.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 389; PDF p. 193.

#### ABC Entry

Dynamic spiritual power, the influence of technical ideas, the influence of culture and technology, influential new ideas, spiritual energy impulses, powerful cultural renewal, nervousness through mental violence, new spiritual energies, surprising power of insight.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Forceful insight and ideas that draw strong attention.
- psychology: Mental stimulation and tension.
- body/health: ABC mentions nervousness through mental violence; this remains attributed wording.
- social/relationship: Influencing others through technical or cultural ideas.
- events/manifestations: A powerful stimulus or striking recognition.
- conflicts/notes: Force of impact does not establish accuracy.

## Contradictions

The entries allow insight and mental tension together. ABC adds cultural renewal and nervousness through mental violence. These remain distinct source claims; force of influence does not establish that an idea is true.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Poseidon = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies mental illumination through its force: an insight draws strong attention or becomes a powerful stimulus to others. [Witte](#witte-entry) includes both clear sight and mental tension; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds the influence of technical and cultural ideas. The intensity of an idea's impact is distinct from evidence that it is correct.
