## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 243 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To enforce or insist upon a clarification or a clear cut statement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 389; PDF p. 193.

#### ABC Entry

Beginning a cultural renewal, renewal of spiritual leadership, shaping spiritual renewal, fiery spiritual impulses, spiritual leadership impulses, beginning spiritual reforms, shaping cultural events, creating new ideas, a suddenly clear objective.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Insistence on a clear explanation or statement.
- psychology: Directed intent to obtain clarification.
- body/health: No bodily claim is established.
- social/relationship: Pressing for a definite statement from those involved.
- events/manifestations: ABC includes creating new ideas and setting clear objectives.
- conflicts/notes: The leadership and reform vocabulary belongs to ABC; Witte's narrower wording concerns clarification.

## Contradictions

Witte's demand for clarity and ABC's clear objective are compatible emphases. ABC additionally specifies beginning cultural and spiritual reforms and renewing spiritual leadership. The former filthyness wording was a mistranslation of leadership, verified on printed p. 389 (PDF p. 193).

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Poseidon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies mental illumination as a demand for clarity: a matter is pressed toward a definite explanation or a clear statement. [Witte](#witte-entry) establishes that directed insistence, while [ABC](#abc-entry) adds new ideas and sudden clear objectives. ABC also locates that direction in cultural renewal, spiritual leadership and the beginning of reforms.
