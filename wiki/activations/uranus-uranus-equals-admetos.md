#### Witte Entry

Sudden rupture-like separation. Shock commotion. Destruction through convulsive natural events. Earthquake. To break off. To break up. To break down. To tear up. Rags. Homicide. Accidents. Fissures, cracks, shreds. Separations by sudden breaks.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry).

- core meaning: Sudden separation by rupture or breaking apart.
- psychology: Shock and commotion accompany the rupture imagery.
- body/health: Witte historically includes accidents and homicide among its severe examples.
- social/relationship: A connection can end through an abrupt break.
- events/manifestations: Cracks, shreds and earthquakes are examples in the entry.
- conflicts/notes: Physical breakage and separation are linked imagery, not a prediction of disaster.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is present. Its examples span objects, relationships and violent natural or human events; the excerpt does not state that all occur together or identify which applies.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies sudden disturbance through rupture: something breaks, separates or comes apart abruptly. [Witte](#witte-entry) moves from cracks and shreds to severed connections and severe historical examples such as earthquakes and accidents. The unifying image is a sudden break; no particular catastrophe follows necessarily from the formula.
