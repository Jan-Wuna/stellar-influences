## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies sudden developments through expansion and discovery. [Witte](#witte-entry) associates advances in science, measurement, technology and trade with surprising success, while also describing many people held in excitement or tension. The emphasis is a leap in scope or knowledge, with mathematics and astrology among the source's specific examples.
