#### Witte Entry

Excitement, irritation or accidents caused by other people. Sudden acquaintances. Sudden events of other peoples. Tension and excitement by others. Reunions for a special reason. Sudden appearance. Location of an accident. Location for mathematics, technology or inventions. Engineers, restless or excited people in the immediate surroundings. Environmental technology.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry).

- core meaning: Sudden events mediated by others or the immediate surroundings.
- psychology: Irritation, excitement and tension caused by others.
- body/health: Witte includes accidents caused by others, without establishing a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Unexpected acquaintances and reunions.
- events/manifestations: Technical surroundings, engineers and places associated with inventions or accidents.
- conflicts/notes: The entry's technical setting is distinct from its interpersonal disturbance examples.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is represented. Unexpected social encounters, technical environments and accidents are alternative emphases; the excerpt does not make one the cause of all the others.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Asc`, Asc specifies sudden disturbance or surprise through other people and the immediate environment. [Witte](#witte-entry) includes new acquaintances, unexpected reunions and excitement caused by others. It also identifies engineering, invention and environmental technology as settings, preserving a practical technical dimension alongside the more disruptive examples.
