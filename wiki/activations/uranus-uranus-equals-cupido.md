#### Witte Entry

Sudden marriage. Sudden wedding. Sudden event in the family. Wedding celebrations. Surprises, unexpected events in married life, partnership. To be united or reunited with the family by surprise. Unforeseen event during the wedding. Sudden innovations in art. Associations, groups or companies in the fields of innovation, technology or energy.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Cupido`, Cupido specifies sudden developments within a shared bond or organized activity. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives surprise marriages, family reunions and unexpected partnership events, then extends the theme to artistic innovation and groups working in technology or energy. The connection is a shared setting changed or enlivened by something unforeseen.
