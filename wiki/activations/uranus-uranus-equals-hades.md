## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Hades`, Hades specifies sudden disturbance in [Witte](#witte-entry) through damage, mean acts and the disgust they can provoke. Its extreme historical examples include accidents, atrocities and sudden death, which remain source claims rather than forecasts. The separate reference to old technology or science is retained as another application, without inventing a causal connection to those violent examples.
