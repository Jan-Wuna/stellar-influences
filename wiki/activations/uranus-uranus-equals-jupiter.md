#### Witte Entry

Material success. Sudden luck. Sudden misfortune, if Jupiter posited in poor pictures. Sudden strokes of good luck. Fortunate events. Happy surprises. Success in technology. Money though technology. Electronic money. Digital currency. New money. Monetary reform.

## Contradictions

Good fortune and the conditional misfortune clause are not a contradiction: Witte qualifies the favorable reading by the surrounding planetary pictures. The excerpt does not supply a financial prediction or an assessment of those additional pictures.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies sudden developments as material opportunity, a happy surprise or success associated with technology. [Witte](#witte-entry) includes electronic money, digital currency and monetary reform, but explicitly qualifies its favorable account with possible misfortune when Jupiter occurs in poor pictures. The source therefore supports a conditional range, not an assurance of gain.
