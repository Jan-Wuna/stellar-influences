#### Witte Entry

Sudden event. Exciting action. Energetic act. Fighting. Wounds. Bloody injury. Surgical procedure. Over impulsive or sudden action. Surprising action. Unexpected or new work. Impulsive movements or action. Actions in the field of technology or mathematics.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry).

- core meaning: Sudden energetic or impulsive action.
- psychology: An urge to act quickly or unexpectedly.
- body/health: Witte historically includes wounds, bloody injury and surgery.
- social/relationship: Fighting is one of the entry's interpersonal examples.
- events/manifestations: Unexpected work and technical or mathematical action.
- conflicts/notes: Constructive work and severe injury are alternatives, not a required sequence.

## Contradictions

The entry ranges from new work to fighting and injury. This is a range within one source, without a second account for comparison; action is not automatically violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Mars`, Mars specifies sudden disturbance through action: a quick movement, energetic intervention or unexpected piece of work. [Witte](#witte-entry) places technical and mathematical activity alongside impulsive acts and fighting. Its historical injury and surgery examples remain attributed possibilities, so the shared theme of sudden action does not by itself determine a harmful outcome.
