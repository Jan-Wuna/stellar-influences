#### Witte Entry

Nerves. Practical thinking. Logic. Good mathematician. Revolutionary thoughts. Original. Rhythm. Telegrams. New information. Sudden inspiration. Surprising mails. Sudden news or information. Excited way of speaking. Technological mindset. The main axis for astrologers, astronomers, technicians or mathematicians.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Mercury`, Mercury specifies sudden stimulation through thought and communication. [Witte](#witte-entry) combines practical logic and mathematics with original ideas, unexpected messages and excited speech. Technology, astronomy and astrology are named fields of application; the organizing link is quick information or insight put into an intellectual form.
