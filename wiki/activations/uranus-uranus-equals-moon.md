## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry).

- core meaning: Aroused attention and bodily or mental restlessness.
- psychology: Excitement, interest and ambition.
- body/health: Witte associates restlessness and nervousness without a specific diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Collective uproar appears alongside explicitly gendered historical examples.
- events/manifestations: An eventful hour or public excitement.
- conflicts/notes: The source's claims about women remain attributed, not universal.

## Contradictions

One Witte entry moves between individual and collective excitement. Its gendered examples are historical source vocabulary, not evidence that excitement or ambition belongs exclusively to women.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Moon`, Moon specifies sudden stimulation through aroused attention, excitement and restlessness of body or mind. [Witte](#witte-entry) extends this from a person's heightened interest to public uproar. The entry also uses gendered examples of energetic or nervous women; these remain attributed historical illustrations rather than general claims about women.
