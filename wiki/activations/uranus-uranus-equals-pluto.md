Mutations. Jumps. To leap. Explosions. Changes and transformations which start suddenly. The development starts with a shock. Inventions or changes in the field of technology. Surprising, unexpected turnaround or development.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is represented. Jumps, explosions and invention are different examples of abrupt development; their inclusion does not establish that every transformation is damaging.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies sudden disturbance as the start of a development or transformation. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes a leap, shock or unexpected turnaround, with inventions and technical changes as concrete examples. What unifies the entry is an abrupt beginning to change, rather than a guaranteed destructive result.
