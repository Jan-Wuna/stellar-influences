#### Witte Entry

Mentally alert. Signs of life. To be lively or to be alive. Mental tension. Truth. The light bearer. Phosphorus. Enlightenment. Propaganda. Moving pictures. Television. Illumination. Light. Elucidation. Clarification, clear up. Clarity. Proof. Revelation.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry).

- core meaning: Sudden alertness, illumination or clarification.
- psychology: Mental liveliness and tension.
- body/health: Signs of life are named, without a medical diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Enlightenment and propaganda appear as forms of influence.
- events/manifestations: Moving pictures, television and literal light accompany the clarity motif.
- conflicts/notes: Phosphorus is a named source example; no chemical doctrine is inferred.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is represented. Truth and propaganda, and literal light and mental illumination, occupy the same list without an explanation of their relationship; they must not be treated as identical.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies sudden stimulation through alertness and illumination: something is clarified, revealed or made conspicuous. [Witte](#witte-entry) places mental insight beside literal light, phosphorus, moving pictures and television. Its inclusion of propaganda keeps communication distinct from proof, even though proof and truth are also named.
