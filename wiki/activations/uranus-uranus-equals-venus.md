#### Witte Entry

Refined sensuality. To be choosy in love. Sudden romantic love. To give birth to a child. Sudden affection or love. Desired event. Sudden expression of love. To be the surprised object of love and affection.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry).

- core meaning: Sudden affection, romantic interest or a desired event.
- psychology: Refined sensuality and selectiveness in love.
- body/health: Witte includes childbirth as an attributed bodily event.
- social/relationship: Giving or unexpectedly receiving affection.
- events/manifestations: A sudden expression of love or welcome development.
- conflicts/notes: Childbirth is one listed example, not an inevitable result of attraction.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is represented. Selectiveness and sudden attraction can coexist; the entry's childbirth example does not establish that every affectionate encounter leads to pregnancy or birth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Venus`, Venus specifies sudden excitement through affection: love can arise unexpectedly, be expressed abruptly or come as a surprise to its recipient. [Witte](#witte-entry) combines this with refined sensuality and selectiveness. Childbirth remains one of its explicitly attributed examples, while the general reading centers on affection or a desired event arriving unexpectedly.
