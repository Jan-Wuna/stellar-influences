## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies sudden disturbance on a public scale. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes quickly arriving events, renewal and reform accompanied by collective excitement or tension. Environmental technology and earth science are additional named fields; the entry does not determine whether a public development is constructive or disruptive.
