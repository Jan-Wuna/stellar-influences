Sudden appearance of forces. Thunderbolt. Explosion. Mighty tension. Great display of strength. Engine power. Electric energies. Electrical high tension. Lightnings. Fires. New kind of energy. Revolutionary forces.

## Comparative Schema

## Contradictions

One Witte entry groups natural forces, machinery and revolutionary force. Their common intensity does not make every display of power destructive or establish a specific event.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Uranus = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies sudden developments through an intense release or display of force. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives engine power, electrical energy, lightning and explosions as concrete expressions, with great tension linking them. New energy and revolutionary forces broaden the setting while retaining the theme of power becoming suddenly evident.
