## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 242 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Useless battles. Sudden great defeat and suppression. Sudden grave misfortune. Great ruins.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 387; PDF p. 192.

#### ABC Entry

Depression through sudden violence, restrictive influence of technology, thorough renewal of power, mighty tensions through blockage, sudden force or violence of blockage, technical concentration of energy, technical rotational force, deepening into new energies.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Force meeting defeat or blockage, alongside concentrated energy and renewal of power.
- psychology: ABC associates sudden violence with depression.
- body/health: ABC's depression wording remains attributed rather than diagnostic.
- social/relationship: Suppression and futile battles in Witte.
- events/manifestations: Ruins and blocked force in Witte; concentrated technical energy, rotational force and renewal of power in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's concentration and renewal of power cannot all be reduced to Witte's defeat.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes defeat and suppression, whereas ABC also retains thorough renewal of power, technical rotational force and deepening into new energies. The latter are not all descriptions of futile effort. The former “grim powerMeuration” has been corrected from “gründliche Macht-Erneuerung” on printed p. 387 (PDF p. 192); the sources do not explain how their differing outcomes relate.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Vulcanus = Admetos`, Admetos specifies sudden force through obstruction or concentration. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes useless battles, suppression and ruins; [ABC](#abc-entry) also names tension through blockage, but adds thorough renewal of power, concentrated technical energy and rotational force. Force may fail to gain ground, while the technical and renewal claims preserve a different possibility that cannot be reduced to defeat.
