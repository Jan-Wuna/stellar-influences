## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 242 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

Many are waiting expectantly, they are longing for a change, waiting for a change in existing conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 387; PDF p. 192.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting expansion of force or violence, influence of new knowledge, success through technical energies, mighty tensions in trade, a sudden experience of force or violence, strong impulses of knowledge, knowledge of new energies, surprising power of freedom, extensive new influence.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Collective expectation of a substantial change.
- psychology: Longing and expectancy shared by many.
- body/health: No health condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Many people await altered conditions; ABC adds trade and wider influence.
- events/manifestations: Technical success and knowledge concerning new energies in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's “Gewalt” retains a force-or-violence range, rather than establishing violence as the only reading.

## Contradictions

Witte describes anticipation, while ABC lists knowledge, trade tensions and violence. These are different emphases, not an established sequence from expectation to violence; the broad German “Gewalt” wording requires care.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Vulcanus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies sudden force through a wider field of shared expectation and influence. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes many people longing for changed conditions; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds technical success, new knowledge and tensions in trade. The entries preserve both anticipation and a possible large reach, without showing that an awaited change must succeed or turn violent.
