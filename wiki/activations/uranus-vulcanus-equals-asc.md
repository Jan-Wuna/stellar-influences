## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: PDF p. 242 (entry start).

#### Witte Entry

To be suddenly exposed to violent acts of others or with others. To live in a dangerous environment. Danger through current energies, through technical medium.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source locator: printed p. 386; PDF p. 192.

#### ABC Entry

Exciting force or violence at the location, mighty tensions with the partner, technical influence at the location, renewal of power at the location, new environmental energies, strong impulses from the partner, technical energy in the environment, surprising influence from the partner.

## Comparative Schema

Evidence: [Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry).

- core meaning: Sudden force encountered through others or one's environment.
- psychology: ABC includes powerful tension with a partner.
- body/health: Witte historically describes exposure to violence and technical danger.
- social/relationship: Acts by or with others and forceful partner influence.
- events/manifestations: Danger involving current energies or technical media in a place.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also names local renewal of power; its force-or-violence vocabulary does not make every influence violent.

## Contradictions

Both entries place force in a local or interpersonal setting, without establishing independent corroboration. ABC also includes renewal of power at the location, corrected from the former “power eminence” after checking “Macht-Erneuerung am Ort” on printed p. 386 (PDF p. 192). This broader range does not make a partner's influence necessarily dangerous.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Uranus/Vulcanus = Asc`, Asc specifies sudden force as something encountered with other people or in the immediate surroundings. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes exposure to violent acts and technical or electrical dangers; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds tension and strong influence involving a partner, as well as local renewal of power. These remain situational source possibilities, not a claim that a partner or environment is necessarily dangerous.
