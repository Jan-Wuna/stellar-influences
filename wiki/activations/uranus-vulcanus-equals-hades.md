## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 242.

To be exposed and subjected to great baseness and vile acts. Sudden great dangers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 387 (printed); PDF page: 192.

Energy renewal by renunciation, excitement about hidden energy, dangerous tensions by violence, adverse influence of technology, sudden dangerous influence, strong hidden impulses, unrest by criminal violence, and renunciation of technical energies

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Danger from hidden or destructive forces.
- Psychology: Strong concealed impulses and alarm.
- Body/health: Exposure to danger is stated; no diagnosis is established.
- Social/relationship: Vile acts and criminal violence.
- Events/manifestations: Harmful technical influence; relinquishing technical energies.
- Conflicts/notes: Renewal through renunciation coexists with danger; neither exhausts the entry.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Renewal through renunciation coexists with danger; neither exhausts the entry. The entries' different examples do not by themselves establish a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Uranus/Vulcanus as power encountered through concealed impulses, harmful actions, or dangerous technical influence. The suddenness can make an already threatening situation acute. Alongside this destructive range, ABC includes renewal through renunciation and giving up technical energies, so the entry also allows withdrawal from the source of danger. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
