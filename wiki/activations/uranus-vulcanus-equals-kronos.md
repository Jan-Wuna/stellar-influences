## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 242.

Surprisingly sudden great might and independence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 387 (printed); PDF page: 192.

Exciting state violence, dominant influence of technology, influence of a new hierarchy, renewal of state power, great technical force, great influence of technology, powerful tensions in the state, new important energies, and Surprising strength of the state

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden might, independence, and state power.
- Psychology: A strong experience of independence.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: State authority, hierarchy, and tensions within the state.
- Events/manifestations: Dominant technical influence and renewed state power.
- Conflicts/notes: State strength is not necessarily peaceful; the entry also includes state violence.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

State strength is not necessarily peaceful; the entry also includes state violence. The entries' different examples do not by themselves establish a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Uranus/Vulcanus as a sudden assertion of might and independence, with ABC locating much of that force in government and hierarchy. Technology can become a dominant instrument of state power. The range includes renewed authority and surprising strength as well as state tension or violence, rather than a simple promise of successful leadership. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
