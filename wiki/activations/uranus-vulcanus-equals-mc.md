## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 242.

To express oneself in an explosive way. To concentrate all power on one point. To be exposed to suddenly breaking out forces of nature. A bellicose character. Sudden mighty display of power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 386 (printed); PDF page: 192.

Renewal of my energy, powerful emotional arousal, my inner influence, my technical influence, my dynamic force, my force impulses, new inner strength, a strong inner impulse, and technical energies for me.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: An explosive personal concentration of power.
- Psychology: Emotional arousal, forceful self-expression, and a bellicose tendency.
- Body/health: Exposure to forces of nature is stated; no clinical claim.
- Social/relationship: Personal influence rather than a defined relationship pattern.
- Events/manifestations: A sudden display of power or renewed personal energy.
- Conflicts/notes: A bellicose character is one source expression, not a fixed identity.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

A bellicose character is one source expression, not a fixed identity. The entries' different examples do not by themselves establish a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Uranus/Vulcanus as energy experienced and expressed personally. Explosive self-expression and intense emotional arousal can concentrate power on one point; technical influence and renewed inner strength are part of the same range. Witte also includes exposure to erupting natural forces, so the person may encounter the force as well as exert it. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
