## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 242.

Actions dictated in a great hurry. To act quickly and forced to make a quick decision. News which creates sudden excitement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 386 (printed); PDF page: 192.

Reports about sudden violence, dynamic powerful thinking, influence of technical media, influential language about technology, language about power renewal, strong traffic impulses, and talk about influential connections

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Urgent decisions and forceful communication.
- Psychology: Dynamic thinking under pressure to decide quickly.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Influential discussions and connections.
- Events/manifestations: Exciting news, technical media, and strong traffic impulses.
- Conflicts/notes: Reports of violence do not identify the speaker as its perpetrator.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Reports of violence do not identify the speaker as its perpetrator. The entries' different examples do not by themselves establish a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies Uranus/Vulcanus through thought and communication accelerated by powerful events. News can produce sudden excitement and force an immediate decision; technical media and influential discussions give that pressure a concrete channel. ABC also includes reports about violence, which describes the content of communication rather than proving violent intent. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
