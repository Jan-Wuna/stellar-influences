## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 242.

Sudden surprising powerlessness, disappointment, and to be forced out of activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 387 (printed); PDF page: 192.

Excitement by water violence, dynamic water force, influential air technology, new influence of metaphysics, new water energies, strong subtle impulses, technical energy in the air, surprising dissolution of power, and uncertain renewal of power.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Power dissolves or operates through water, air, and subtle influences.
- Psychology: Surprising powerlessness and disappointment.
- Body/health: No specific bodily diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Forced withdrawal from activity rather than a defined relationship pattern.
- Events/manifestations: Water power, air technology, and uncertain renewal of power.
- Conflicts/notes: The restored ABC boundary excludes Pluto's development paragraph.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

The restored ABC boundary excludes Pluto's development paragraph. The entries' different examples do not by themselves establish a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Uranus/Vulcanus through a force that can suddenly fail or dissolve, leaving disappointment and enforced inactivity. ABC also gives concrete water-power and air-technology expressions, alongside metaphysical and subtle impulses. These extend the field beyond personal powerlessness without cancelling it; the development of new power belongs to Pluto's separate row. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
