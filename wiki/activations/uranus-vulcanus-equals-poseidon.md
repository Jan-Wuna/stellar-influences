## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 242.

To suddenly gain great insight and to arrive at clear results. To suddenly exercise mental power and influence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 387 (printed); PDF page: 192.

Influence of new ideas, restricted spiritual force, spiritual energy impulses, clarity about new energies, powerful renewal of culture, new spiritual energies, sudden mental violence, strong spiritual impulses, and surprising force of truth

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden insight and the force of ideas or truth.
- Psychology: Clarity, mental influence, and spiritual impulses.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Cultural renewal and intellectual influence.
- Events/manifestations: Clear results and the arrival of new ideas.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC includes restricted mental force as well as new spiritual energies.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

ABC includes restricted mental force as well as new spiritual energies. The entries' different examples do not by themselves establish a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Uranus/Vulcanus through a sudden concentration of mental or spiritual force: insight becomes clear results and ideas acquire influence. ABC broadens this into cultural renewal and the surprising force of truth, while also allowing restricted mental power. The entries describe the impact and limits of ideas rather than guaranteeing that every forceful conviction is true. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
