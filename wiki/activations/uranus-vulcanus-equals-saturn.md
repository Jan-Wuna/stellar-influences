## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 242.

To suddenly feel oneself extremely hindered. To suddenly experience the bitter taste of dependency.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 387 (printed); PDF page: 192.

Excitement over huge losses, persistent dynamic force, disturbed technical influence, enormous technical tasks, power losses in technology, slow power renewal, powerful tensions in old age, new influence in old age, and sudden losses by violence

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Power abruptly hindered, depleted, or made dependent.
- Psychology: A bitter experience of dependency; persistence under strain.
- Body/health: Age-related tensions are ABC's historical association, not a prognosis.
- Social/relationship: Dependency and losses rather than a defined relationship pattern.
- Events/manifestations: Disturbed technical influence, demanding tasks, and slow renewal.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC retains endurance and new influence in age alongside losses.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

ABC retains endurance and new influence in age alongside losses. The entries' different examples do not by themselves establish a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Uranus/Vulcanus as the experience of powerful forces meeting hindrance or loss. Technical energy may be disturbed or depleted, and dependency can become suddenly apparent. ABC also retains persistent dynamic effort, large technical tasks, and slow renewal, so the formula includes continuing under constraint rather than only abrupt defeat. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
