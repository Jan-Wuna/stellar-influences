## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 242.

Days of great menace. The body is suddenly exposed to violent acts. Grave danger to the body. The man who handles great energies.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 386 (printed); PDF page: 192.

Renewal of personal power, powerful personal tensions, renewal of personal power, sudden personal influence, strong personal impulses, strong restlessness center, technical energy center, and surprising corporal violence

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal and bodily exposure to great energy.
- Psychology: Powerful personal impulses and tension.
- Body/health: Witte's bodily danger is historical doctrine, not a forecast or diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Personal influence rather than a defined relationship pattern.
- Events/manifestations: Handling great energies and renewed personal power.
- Conflicts/notes: The source's man-language does not restrict this formula to men.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

The source's man-language does not restrict this formula to men. The entries' different examples do not by themselves establish a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Uranus/Vulcanus through the person who handles or is exposed to great energy. Strong impulses and renewed personal power sit beside bodily tension and Witte's warning of violent danger. This connects agency with exposure: the source does not say that strength makes someone immune to the forces involved, and its danger language is not an individual prediction. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
