## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 242.

To be connected with violent acts in public. Kinetic powers. Electric current.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 386 (printed); PDF page: 192.

Public violence, public tension, new energies for the public Life, influence of technology for the public, strong public impulses, and technical energy for the public

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden forces entering public life.
- Psychology: Strong impulses from the public; no detailed individual psychology.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Public tension and public violence.
- Events/manifestations: Electrical current, kinetic power, and technology serving public life.
- Conflicts/notes: Public manifestation does not establish one individual's conduct.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Public manifestation does not establish one individual's conduct. The entries' different examples do not by themselves establish a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Uranus/Vulcanus by placing the emergence of force in public life. Electrical and technical energies can enter shared use, while strong public impulses and tensions may become violent acts. The entry concerns the public reach of these forces rather than a detailed private character portrait. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
