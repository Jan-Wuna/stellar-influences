## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 242.

To accomplish the purpose. Great energies directed towards one point. Mighty stored up energies.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 387 (printed); PDF page: 192.

Exciting Fire Force, Dynamic Leadership Force, Renewal of Leadership Power, Huge Technical Effort, Powerful New Beginning, New Fiery Energies, Sudden Fire Power, Technical Power, and Surprising Force Generation

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Concentrated energy serving a purpose or new beginning.
- Psychology: Directed effort and leadership.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Leadership power rather than a defined relationship pattern.
- Events/manifestations: Stored energy, fire power, technical effort, and energy generation.
- Conflicts/notes: Purposeful concentration does not imply that the purpose succeeds harmlessly.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Purposeful concentration does not imply that the purpose succeeds harmlessly. The entries' different examples do not by themselves establish a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Uranus/Vulcanus as great energy gathered and directed toward a purpose. Stored power becomes technical performance, leadership effort, or a forceful new beginning; ABC also makes fire and energy generation explicit. The organizing theme is concentrated productive force, with sudden fire violence retained among its possible expressions. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
