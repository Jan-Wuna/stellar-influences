## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 238.

Expansion of a power. Forces in expansion. Spreading war.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 379 (printed); PDF page: 188.

Efforts for new knowledge, beginning expansion of unrest, successful dynamic leadership, successful technical achievements, re design of knowledge, new experiences of science, creative knowledge impulses, and growing reform struggles

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Creative force expands.
- Psychology: Seeking new knowledge.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Leadership, science, and reform.
- Events/manifestations: Technical achievement; spreading war.
- Conflicts/notes: Expansion includes unrest and war, not only success.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Expansion includes unrest and war, not only success.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Uranus/Zeus through the widening reach of directed force. Technical achievement, scientific experience, and new knowledge share that expansive movement with spreading unrest or war. The entries support both productive extension and the extension of conflict. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
