## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 238.

To be under the will power of others. Subjugated by the arbitrariness of others. Losses through flashes of lightning.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 378 (printed); PDF page: 188.

dynamic leadership of a place, new start with the partner, new efforts for the environment, sudden fire at the place, creative partner impulses, technical services in a place, and restless start of a partnership

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Directed force encountered through others or place.
- Psychology: Subjection to another's will.
- Body/health: No diagnosis; lightning losses are stated.
- Social/relationship: A restless new partnership.
- Events/manifestations: Local fire and technical achievement.
- Conflicts/notes: Creative partnership and domination are alternatives.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Creative partnership and domination are alternatives.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Uranus/Zeus through the immediate setting and other people. A new partnership or local technical effort can initiate a change, while Witte emphasizes being subjected to someone else's arbitrary will. Fire and lightning also make the locality a place of exposure. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
