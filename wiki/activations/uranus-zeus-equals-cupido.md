## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 238.

Procreation in a marriage union. To force one’s will on a community, to dominate and to form it. Many gun shots at once or machine-gun fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 379 (printed); PDF page: 188.

Starting unrest in of a community, dynamic leadership community, joint technical efforts, joint re start, joint procreation event, new direction for of a community, and reform struggles of a community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Collective creation and imposed direction.
- Psychology: Will exercised over a group.
- Body/health: Historical procreation claim; no sex or health prediction.
- Social/relationship: Marriage and shared community effort.
- Events/manifestations: Reform, joint new beginning, and gunfire.
- Conflicts/notes: Shared creation does not imply consensual direction.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Shared creation does not imply consensual direction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Uranus/Zeus through a community or marriage brought into a forceful process of creation. Shared work and a new collective direction sit beside domination of the group. Witte's procreation and mass gunfire examples retain the formula's unusually wide, historically stated range. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
