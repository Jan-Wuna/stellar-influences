## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 238.

Forces bringing destruction. Destruction through power of nature such as thundershowers, storms at sea, earthquakes, eruption of volcanoes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 379 (printed); PDF page: 188.

Excitement about criminal leadership, difficult new beginning, danger of sudden fire, inadequate design impulses, disadvantages due to technical creativity, restlessness due to fire crime, and creative impulses from the past

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Destructive force and a difficult beginning.
- Psychology: Disturbance around criminal leadership.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Criminal leadership and creative impulses from the past.
- Events/manifestations: Fire crime; destructive natural forces.
- Conflicts/notes: Technical creativity can also bring disadvantages.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Technical creativity can also bring disadvantages.

## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Uranus/Zeus through directed or erupting force that brings destruction. Witte gives storms, earthquakes, and volcanic eruption; ABC adds criminal leadership, fire crime, and a difficult new start. Creative impulses from the past remain a quieter possibility within this otherwise hazardous range. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
