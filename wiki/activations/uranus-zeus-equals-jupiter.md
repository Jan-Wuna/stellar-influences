## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Uranus/Zeus through force that benefits a new undertaking. Technical achievement, law, financial leadership, and the production of money give ABC's favorable beginning concrete settings. Witte's tropical rainstorms preserve a natural-force expression alongside these human activities. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
