## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 238.

To be leading and a model in creative work. To be a master in creation. Forces aroused by the state. Higher powers. Wars.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 379 (printed); PDF page: 188.

Beginning of great unrest, special technical achievements, dynamic state leadership, new state direction, sudden major fire, creative impulses of the boss, independent rhythmic design, self conscious reform struggles, and important new beginnings

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Creative mastery and state-directed force.
- Psychology: Self-assured reform and independent shaping.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Leadership, state direction, and the boss.
- Events/manifestations: Technical accomplishment, war, and major fire.
- Conflicts/notes: Creative mastery and coercive state force remain distinct possibilities.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Creative mastery and coercive state force remain distinct possibilities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Uranus/Zeus through mastery in creative work and force organized by authority. State direction and the impulses of a leader can support technical accomplishment or reform, while Witte also includes wars. The organizing capacity is strong; its purpose is not automatically benign. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
