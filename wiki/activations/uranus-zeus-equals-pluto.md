#### Witte Entry

Beginning of a new development, beginning of a restless change, dynamic development of performance, creative technical changes, developing new goals, creative rhythm change, surprising changes in leadership, and growing reform design

## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies Uranus/Zeus through the development of will and productive capacity. Inner or outer forces can make production rise abruptly, while new goals and technical change give that growth direction. ABC also includes a restless change and unexpected changes in leadership, so development need not be orderly. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
