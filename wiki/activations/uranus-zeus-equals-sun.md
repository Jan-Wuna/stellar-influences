## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 238.

The violent person. Days of great accomplishment. The man who concentrates and directs his thoughts and energies. Sudden strong explosions on the day.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 378 (printed); PDF page: 188.

Fiery impulses of a man, design pulses of a man, personal leadership impulses, personal new beginning, personal procreation event, restless leadership center, technical performance center, and surprising corporal effort

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal concentration of will and energy.
- Psychology: Directed thought and fiery impulses.
- Body/health: Bodily effort; historical procreation language.
- Social/relationship: Personal leadership rather than a defined relationship pattern.
- Events/manifestations: Great accomplishment or strong explosions.
- Conflicts/notes: The historical violent-person label is not a fixed identity.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

The historical violent-person label is not a fixed identity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Uranus/Zeus through a person concentrating thought and energy into action. Great accomplishment and a new beginning share that force with bodily effort and the source's violent possibilities. Historical man-language and procreation associations remain attributed; they do not establish an inevitable character or outcome. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
