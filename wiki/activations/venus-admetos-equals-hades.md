## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 187.

Unable to true and deep love. Lack of carefulness and cleanness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 293 (printed); PDF page: 145.
- Translation and activator boundary checked against the scanned row.

Depressions through crime connected with love, restrictions through sorrow in love, the end of a dangerous love, thorough renunciation of love, deepening of an old love, immersion in art of the past, and deepening a preference for the past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Love narrowed by sorrow, danger, or renunciation.
- Psychology: Sorrow and a turn toward old attachments.
- Body/health: Depression and cleanliness language are attributed historical claims.
- Social/relationship: Dangerous love may end; an old love may deepen.
- Events/manifestations: Immersion in art of the past.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's inability-to-love claim is stronger than ABC's continuing old affection.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Witte's inability-to-love claim is stronger than ABC's continuing old affection.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Venus/Admetos through affection burdened by sorrow or danger. Renunciation and the end of a dangerous love can narrow the bond, while ABC still allows deepening an old love or interest in past art. Witte's categorical inability-to-love statement remains a distinct, stronger claim rather than a universal verdict. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
