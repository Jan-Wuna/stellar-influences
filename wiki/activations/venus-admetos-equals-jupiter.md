## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 187.

Happy in a close circle, or resting deeply absorbed in thoughts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 292 (printed); PDF page: 145.
- Translation and activator boundary checked against the scanned row.

The end of joy in love, restriction of financial wishes, a restricted right to peace, success through enduring love, consolidation of happiness in love, joy in the circulation of money, success in peace through endurance, deepening into the art of law, and deep joy in love.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Quiet happiness and love sustained by endurance.
- Psychology: Absorbed rest and deep joy.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Happiness in a close circle; consolidated love.
- Events/manifestations: Money circulation, financial limits, and peace through endurance.
- Conflicts/notes: The end of joy and deep joy are both present in ABC.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

The end of joy and deep joy are both present in ABC.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Venus/Admetos through contentment that can be quiet, close, and deeply absorbed. Enduring love may consolidate happiness, while ABC also includes limited financial wishes and the end of joy in love. The range supports happiness within limits without promising that all restriction becomes satisfying. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
