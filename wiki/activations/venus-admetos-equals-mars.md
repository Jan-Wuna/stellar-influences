## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 187.

Permanence, firmness and faithfulness in attitude. Connected with few through activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 292 (printed); PDF page: 145.
- Translation and activator boundary checked against the scanned row.

An enduring will for peace, blocked harmonious actions, wanting to consolidate love, joy in energetic resistance, thorough efforts in love, harmonious deepening into work, deepening artistic activity, and a preference for steady work.

## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Venus/Admetos through firmness of attitude enacted in work and effort. A few people may be connected through activity, while artistic immersion and the wish to consolidate love give perseverance a purpose. Energetic resistance and blocked harmonious actions preserve the possibility that effort meets obstruction. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
