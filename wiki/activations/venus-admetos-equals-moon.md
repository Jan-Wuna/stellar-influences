## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 187.

Peace of mind. Women who love deep and unselfishly. Small harmonious circle of women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 292 (printed); PDF page: 145.
- Translation and activator boundary checked against the scanned row.

Restriction of a peaceful people, depressions of a beautiful woman, consolidation of a woman through art, thorough folk art, loving steady women, deepening love for a woman, deep love of a woman, and suppression of the women of a people.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Peaceful feeling in a small affectionate circle.
- Psychology: Peace of mind and deep affection.
- Body/health: ABC's depression language remains historical attribution.
- Social/relationship: Historical women-language; deep unselfish love.
- Events/manifestations: Art, folk art, and restriction of a peaceful people.
- Conflicts/notes: Women's suppression is a source claim, not an inherent female condition.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Women's suppression is a source claim, not an inherent female condition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Venus/Admetos through the possibility of peace of mind and deep, unselfish attachment within a small circle. ABC adds art and collective restriction, while retaining depression and suppression of women as historical claims. The gendered examples are preserved without turning them into general facts about women. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
