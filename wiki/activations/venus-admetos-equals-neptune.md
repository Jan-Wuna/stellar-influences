## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 187.

Dissolution of a deep love. Uncertain love. Disappointment through carelessness. Perversions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 293 (printed); PDF page: 145.
- Translation and activator boundary checked against the scanned row.

Limitation of a joyful expectation, a disappointing blockage of peace, consolidation of an uncertain love, a thorough preference for new things, deep disappointment in love, immersion in metaphysical art, a preference for deep water, and a preference for securing the future.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Deep love becomes uncertain or disappointed.
- Psychology: Limited expectation and deep disappointment.
- Body/health: Witte's “Perversions” is a historical label, not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: An uncertain love may consolidate or dissolve.
- Events/manifestations: Metaphysical art, deep water, and securing the future.
- Conflicts/notes: The row allows consolidation despite uncertainty; dissolution is not inevitable.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Venus/Admetos through uncertainty within a deep attachment. A bond may consolidate while remaining insecure, or dissolve through disappointment and carelessness. ABC's metaphysical art, deep water, and concern for securing the future widen the range beyond relationship loss; Witte's historical sexual label remains attributed. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
