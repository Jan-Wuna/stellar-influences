## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 187.

Harmoniously connected with few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 292 (printed); PDF page: 145.
- Translation and activator boundary checked against the scanned row.

An enduring love connection, the end of a love connection, firmly connected with art, restricted connections with art, thorough connections with art, deepening harmonious relationships, and a preference for firm connections.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Few durable harmonious connections.
- Psychology: Preference for firm bonds.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Enduring or ended love connections.
- Events/manifestations: Firm or restricted connections with art.
- Conflicts/notes: Duration and an ending are both stated, without deciding one outcome.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Duration and an ending are both stated, without deciding one outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Venus/Admetos through a small number of firm, harmonious connections. Love may endure and deepen, yet the same entry also allows a connection to end. ABC's links with art give this selectivity a further setting beyond personal partnership. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
