## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies Venus/Admetos through an interruption of repose. Joy may be suddenly blocked and restricted love become tense, yet ABC also includes new deep affection and dynamic immersion in art. The surprise can therefore disturb stillness without determining that the attachment must end. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
