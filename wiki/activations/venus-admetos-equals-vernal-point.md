## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 187.

Harmonious community in close circle. Meditation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 292 (printed); PDF page: 145.
- Translation and activator boundary checked against the scanned row.

Depressing art in public, the end of peace in public life, consolidation of art in public life, deepening harmony in public, artistic creation in public, and a sympathy strike in public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A close harmonious circle in public life.
- Psychology: Meditation and deepening harmony.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Small community; public sympathy strike.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic creation or an end of public peace.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Public harmony and public disruption are both retained.

## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Venus/Admetos through the presence of a close, harmonious circle within public life. Meditation and artistic creation connect inward depth with shared expression. ABC also includes depressing public art, a sympathy strike, and an end of peace, so the public setting does not ensure agreement. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
