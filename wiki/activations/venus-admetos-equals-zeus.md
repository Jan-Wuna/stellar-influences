- **social/relationship:** Enduring effort can initiate deep love or support peace. [Sources](#source-entries).

## Contradictions

Witte's wholehearted effort and ABC's enduring love, peace efforts, and lasting artistic achievements support a shared emphasis on sustained commitment. The former apparent difference concerning state authority came from a paragraph assigned to the wrong activator; the scanned paragraph belongs to Kronos.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Admetos = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the Venus/Admetos axis through wholehearted effort sustained by affection for a cause. [Witte](#witte-entry) makes the connection concrete: love for the undertaking helps one overcome a difficult beginning. [ABC](#abc-entry) extends the theme into enduring efforts in love, peace work, and the creation of an artistic direction. Concentration becomes sustained commitment whose stated aim is lasting achievement.
