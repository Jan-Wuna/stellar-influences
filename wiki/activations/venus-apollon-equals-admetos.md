## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 186, Venus/Apollon, Admetos row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

A few or no friends. Failure and difficulties in art and science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 291; one-based PDF p. 144, Venus/Apollon, Admetos row; complete row and neighboring glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Enduring love of freedom, blocked success in peace, restricted science of peace, the end of an experience of love, successful deepening in art, thorough knowledge of art, increasing generation of harmony, and increasing suppression of love.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Friendship, art and science meet limitation, with concentrated artistic study as an alternative. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **psychology:** Persistence in love of freedom and deepening in art are ABC themes. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **body/health:** Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **social/relationship:** Witte names few or no friends; ABC includes the end of a love experience and increasing suppression of love. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **events/manifestations:** Difficulties in art and science coexist with successful deepening in art, thorough art knowledge, blocked peace success and restricted peace science. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **conflicts/notes:** Zunehmende Unterdrückung der Liebe is increasing suppression of love, not increasing support; failure and successful deepening remain distinct alternatives. [Sources](#source-entries).

## Contradictions

Witte's failure language and ABC's successful artistic deepening are materially different alternatives. ABC also retains restricted peace science, increasing generation of harmony and increasing suppression of love; these clauses do not erase one another or make limitation uniformly beneficial.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Admetos`, the axis's social and cultural range is narrowed or deepened. [Witte](#witte-entry) names few or no friends and difficulties in art or science. [ABC](#abc-entry) includes blocked peace success, restricted peace science, an ending love experience and increasing suppression of love, alongside successful deepening in art and thorough art knowledge. Limitation can therefore coexist with concentrated cultural depth, but the source's failure and suppression claims remain explicit rather than being converted into support.
