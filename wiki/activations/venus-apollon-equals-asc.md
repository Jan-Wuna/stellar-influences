## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 186, Venus/Apollon, Asc row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

To be around faithful and reliable people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 290; one-based PDF p. 144, Venus/Apollon, Asc row; complete row and neighboring glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: An experienced partner in love, joy in experiences of the surroundings, spreading harmony in the surroundings, others' knowledge of art, art trade at the place, successful love of the partner, a place for the study of art, much harmony in the surroundings, and the partner's increasing love.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Affection and harmony take shape through reliable people in the surrounding environment. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **psychology:** Enjoyment of shared experience appears in ABC; Witte centers trustworthiness. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **body/health:** No bodily or health condition is established. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **social/relationship:** Faithful company, a successful loving partnership and increasing partner affection are stated. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **events/manifestations:** Spreading harmony, art trade at a place, others' art knowledge and a place for the study of art are stated environmental manifestations. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **conflicts/notes:** Kunst-Wissen and Kunst-Handel mean knowledge of art and art trade; the complete nine-clause row also includes Ort der Kunst-Wissenschaft. [Sources](#source-entries).

## Contradictions

Reliable company, successful partner love and harmony in the surroundings are compatible emphases. Art trade and the study of art supply further settings, without proving that all surrounding relationships are faithful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Asc`, affection and harmony are expressed through the people and places around a person. [Witte](#witte-entry) foregrounds faithful, reliable company; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds an experienced loving partner, increasing partner affection and joy in experiences of the surroundings. Spreading harmony can also take a cultural setting in others' art knowledge, local art trade and a place for the study of art. These are stated possibilities rather than a guarantee about every surrounding relationship.
