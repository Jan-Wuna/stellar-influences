## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Feeling of happiness in or through the family. Social intercourse with friends, devoting time and effort to art and science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Achievements of the female principle in society, successful artistic community, peaceful expansion of a community, common love for freedom, harmony in the scientific community, and goodwill of the scientific community

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Harmony is experienced through family, friendship and artistic or scientific community. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **psychology:** Happiness and goodwill accompany participation in shared interests. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **body/health:** Neither entry gives a bodily or health meaning. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **social/relationship:** Family happiness and social contact with friends are explicit in Witte. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **events/manifestations:** Devoting time to art or science and peaceful community expansion are stated manifestations. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **conflicts/notes:** ABC's “female principle” is historical source terminology, not a universal gender claim. [Sources](#source-entries).

## Contradictions

Family happiness and collective artistic or scientific success are compatible emphases, not evidence that every community is harmonious. ABC's “female principle” remains attributed historical terminology; the entry does not justify assigning these outcomes by sex.

Source locators from the evidence cache: ABC printed p. 291 (PDF p. 144); Witte PDF p. 186 (entry start). These passages have not been visually verified.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the Venus/Apollon axis as harmony belonging to a family or community. [Witte](#witte-entry) joins happiness through family with friendship and time devoted to art or science; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds artistic cooperation, peaceful expansion and goodwill in a scientific community. What unifies these settings is pleasure and belonging developed through shared cultural or intellectual activity.
