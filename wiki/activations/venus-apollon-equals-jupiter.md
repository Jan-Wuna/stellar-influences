## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Much happiness and joy through friendship, science, art or economic enterprises.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Extensive love pleasure, successful right art, successful peace right, joy in law science, love happiness experience, love for money trade, preference for right knowledge, preference for vie) money, and increasing finances through love

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Friendship, art, science and enterprise become sources of happiness. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **psychology:** Joy and pleasure are the main explicit inner responses. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **body/health:** No bodily or medical claim appears. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **social/relationship:** Friendship and happiness in love are supported themes. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **events/manifestations:** Witte names economic enterprises; ABC adds finances through love and interest in law or money trade. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **conflicts/notes:** The legal and financial expressions are alternatives, not promises of profit. [Sources](#source-entries).

## Contradictions

The entries share a favorable emphasis without establishing that all listed ventures succeed. ABC's “successful right art”, “successful peace right” and “vie) money” require translation/OCR review; they are retained and excluded from the interpretation. The former synthesis's “oy” typo is removed with the rewritten prose.

Source locators from the evidence cache: ABC printed p. 290 (PDF p. 144); Witte PDF p. 186 (entry start). These passages have not been visually verified.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the Venus/Apollon axis through the enjoyment and benefit found in friendship, art, science or economic enterprise. [Witte](#witte-entry) explicitly connects these activities with happiness; the intelligible parts of [ABC](#abc-entry) add pleasure in love and financial themes. The emphasis is an enlarged field of satisfying associations and interests, with financial gain remaining one source-listed possibility rather than a guaranteed result.
