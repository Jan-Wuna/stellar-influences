## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 186, Venus/Apollon, Kronos row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Friendship with superiors and with people working in the government. Outstanding in art and science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 291; one-based PDF p. 144, Venus/Apollon, Kronos row; complete row and neighboring glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Special knowledge of art, success through peace with hierarchy, successful independence in love, successful dominance of harmony, great experiences in love, independent art trade, state success in peace, and experiencing the boss's goodwill.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Friendship and cultural achievement connect with authority and distinction. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **psychology:** Special art knowledge and independence in love are explicit ABC themes. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **body/health:** No bodily or health claim is supplied. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **social/relationship:** Friendship with superiors, government workers and a benevolent boss are explicit settings. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **events/manifestations:** Outstanding art or science, independent art trade and state success in peace are source-listed possibilities. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **conflicts/notes:** Selbständigkeit means independence; hierarchical favor and independent activity remain distinct expressions. [Sources](#source-entries).

## Contradictions

Authority, independence and harmony are compatible possibilities. The source explicitly preserves independence in love and art trade alongside hierarchy and state success; it does not promise an official position or favor.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Kronos`, cultural distinction and friendship enter relationships with authority. [Witte](#witte-entry) names superiors, government workers and outstanding art or science; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds special art knowledge, peace with hierarchy, state peace success and a boss's goodwill. Independence in love and independent art trade remain explicit, so this range includes autonomous activity alongside authoritative settings without guaranteeing status or official favor.
