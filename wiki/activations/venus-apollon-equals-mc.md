## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 186, Venus/Apollon, MC row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Peaceful thinking. To be faithful and reliable. The own disposition and inclination toward faithfulness. Affection to the native is shown by many. Dependable.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 290; one-based PDF p. 144, Venus/Apollon, MC row; complete row and neighboring glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Expansion of my harmony, successful harmony of the soul, I love freedom, my success in love, my knowledge of art, my artistic success, my love of abundance, my preference for knowledge, and much harmony in the inner being.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Harmony and faithfulness are described as personal dispositions. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **psychology:** Peaceful thinking, reliability, inner harmony and love of freedom, abundance and knowledge. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **body/health:** No bodily or medical meaning is established. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **social/relationship:** Witte adds affection shown by many, alongside one's own faithfulness. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **events/manifestations:** Personal success in love and art, knowledge of art and expansion of one's harmony. [Sources](#source-entries).
- **conflicts/notes:** Fülle means abundance; the complete nine-clause row retains successful harmony of the soul and much harmony in the inner being. [Sources](#source-entries).

## Contradictions

Witte's faithful disposition and ABC's inner harmony, freedom and abundance are different personal emphases. The entries do not say that reliability necessarily earns affection or success.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = MC`, the axis is experienced as an inward disposition toward peaceful thought, reliable affection and harmony. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes faithfulness and affection shown by many; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds expansion of one's harmony and successful harmony of the soul, together with love of freedom, abundance and knowledge. Artistic knowledge and success give these interests a cultural expression. Inner harmony and receiving affection remain related possibilities rather than a guaranteed exchange.
