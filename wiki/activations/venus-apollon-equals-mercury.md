## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 186, Venus/Apollon, Mercury row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

To be known to many, to speak to many, to be acquainted with many. Acquaintances with scientists of languages or people who speak foreign languages.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 290; one-based PDF p. 144, Venus/Apollon, Mercury row; complete row and neighboring glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Success through harmonious conversations, expanding peace talks, news about successes in peace, reporting experiences of love, speaking much about love, preference for media studies, and scientific reports about love.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Speech widens a harmonious social or intellectual circle.
- **psychology:** Harmonious conversation, reporting love experiences and preference for media studies.
- **body/health:** No bodily claim is supplied.
- **social/relationship:** Witte names many acquaintances, including people working with languages.
- **events/manifestations:** Speaking to many, peace talks, news of peace success and scientific reports about love.
- **conflicts/notes:** Friedens-Gespräche means peace talks and viel means much; Medien-Wissenschaft means media studies.

## Contradictions

Many acquaintances, languages and harmonious communication are compatible emphases. The source's success language does not establish that every conversation succeeds or makes peace.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Mercury`, speech and knowledge widen the axis's social and cultural circle. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes being known to many and knowing people who study or speak languages. [ABC](#abc-entry) specifies harmonious conversations, expanding peace talks and speaking or reporting about love, alongside media studies and scientific reports about love. These give communication several distinct contents without promising that all communication is harmonious.
