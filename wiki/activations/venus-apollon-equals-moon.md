## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 186, Venus/Apollon, Moon row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Many good friends. People, who busy themselves with art and science. Participation of the public or of women in art and science. To spend many hours with art or science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 290; one-based PDF p. 144, Venus/Apollon, Moon row; complete row and neighboring glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A successful female artist, a successful hour of love, a successful beautiful woman, a woman's love of freedom, a woman's success in art, a woman's success in love, a woman's experiences of love, loving many women, and preference for economics or the national economy.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Friendship accompanies participation in art or science.
- **psychology:** Witte describes sustained cultural involvement.
- **body/health:** Neither entry establishes a medical condition.
- **social/relationship:** Many good friends and public participation are explicit.
- **events/manifestations:** Hours devoted to art or science, women's artistic and love success, and ABC's preference for economics or the national economy.
- **conflicts/notes:** Schöne Frau means beautiful woman and Kunst-Erfolg is success in art. Volks-Wirtschaft can mean economics or the national economy; no economic system is identified.

## Contradictions

Witte's public cultural participation and ABC's women's love or artistic experiences differ in scale. Their gendered examples remain attributed and do not establish fixed traits or limit the formula to women.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Moon`, participation in art or science is linked with friendship and shared cultural life. [Witte](#witte-entry) includes many good friends, public or women's participation and many hours spent with these interests. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a successful female artist, a woman's artistic and love experiences, beauty and love of freedom. Its loving-many-women and economics or national-economy clauses retain further specific associations. The gendered examples remain source claims rather than universal traits of women.
