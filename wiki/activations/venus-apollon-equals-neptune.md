## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 186, Venus/Apollon, Neptune row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Falsehood and unfaithfulness. Dissolution of friendship with many. Hopeless love for art and science, especially those in a state of development. Fanciful love for these things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 291; one-based PDF p. 144, Venus/Apollon, Neptune row; complete row and neighboring glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A disappointing experience of peace, success with metaphysical art, inclination toward new knowledge, uncertain success in peace, many disappointments in love, preference for much water, preference for knowledge of the future, and increasing uncertainty in love.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Friendship and cultural affection encounter uncertainty or dissolution.
- **psychology:** Fanciful or hopeless love for art and science in Witte; inclination toward new knowledge and knowledge of the future in ABC.
- **body/health:** No bodily meaning is established.
- **social/relationship:** Unfaithfulness and dissolution of friendships in Witte; many disappointments and increasing uncertainty in love in ABC.
- **events/manifestations:** Metaphysical art, disappointing or uncertain peace experience, and preference for much water.
- **conflicts/notes:** The Neptune row ends increasing uncertainty in love; extensive artistic development starts the following Pluto row and is excluded.

## Contradictions

Dissolved friendships and disappointment coexist with ABC's success in metaphysical art and interest in new or future knowledge. These are different expressions; the removed Pluto development paragraph cannot reconcile or expand them.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Neptune`, affection and social or cultural ties encounter uncertainty, disappointment or dissolution. [Witte](#witte-entry) names unfaithfulness, dissolved friendships and fanciful or hopeless love for developing art and science; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds many disappointments and increasing uncertainty in love. ABC also allows success with metaphysical art, interest in new or future knowledge and preference for much water. Those specific associations preserve a wider range without turning every cultural interest into deception or importing the following Pluto development row.
