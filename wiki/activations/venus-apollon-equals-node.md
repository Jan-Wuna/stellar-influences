## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 186, Venus/Apollon, Node row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Comradeship. Connections with artists or scientists. To be tied to many harmoniously. Esprit de corps among colleagues.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 290; one-based PDF p. 144, Venus/Apollon, Node row; complete row and neighboring glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Expansion of harmonious relationships, success in love connections, a successful connection to art, connection with the art trade, combining art and knowledge, preference for trade connections, and increasing joy in connections.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Comradeship connects people through culture or knowledge.
- **psychology:** Increasing joy in connections appears in ABC.
- **body/health:** No bodily meaning is supplied.
- **social/relationship:** Witte names colleagues, artists and scientists.
- **events/manifestations:** Expanding harmonious relationships, connections to art and art trade, and combining art with knowledge.
- **conflicts/notes:** Kunst-Handel means art trade; this row supplies no criminal or illicit-trade claim.

## Contradictions

Comradeship, culture, knowledge and trade connections are compatible social settings. The source does not say every connection brings success or requires a commercial purpose.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Node`, shared cultural or intellectual interests form connections and comradeship. [Witte](#witte-entry) names harmonious ties with artists, scientists and colleagues; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds expanding relationships, successful love connections and increasing joy in connections. Art trade and the combination of art with knowledge give some of those connections specific fields, without establishing any illicit activity.
