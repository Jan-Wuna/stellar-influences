## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 186, Venus/Apollon, Pluto row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Pleasant development for many. Development of friendship and affection. To develop with many others love for the arts, sciences or economics.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 291; one-based PDF p. 144, Venus/Apollon, Pluto row; complete row and neighboring glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Extensive artistic development, successful development of peace, successful transformation of the feminine principle, successful changes in art, harmonious growth in trade, unfolding knowledge of art, and preference for developments in the economy.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Friendship and shared cultural interests develop.
- **psychology:** Witte describes growing love for art, science or economics.
- **body/health:** No bodily condition is given.
- **social/relationship:** Witte explicitly describes developing friendship with many others.
- **events/manifestations:** Artistic and peace development, unfolding art knowledge, harmonious growth in trade and developments in the economy.
- **conflicts/notes:** Witte's leading P was an alternative Pluto glyph, not prose. ABC's complete seven clauses retain the transformation of the feminine principle as attributed symbolism.

## Contradictions

Development of friendship, affection and cultural interests is compatible with ABC's art, peace and economic developments. Successful development remains a stated possibility rather than a guarantee, and the feminine principle remains attributed symbolism.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Pluto`, friendship and shared affection for art, science or economics develop and change. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes pleasant development involving many people; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives extensive artistic development, unfolding art knowledge, successful peace development and harmonious trade growth as specific settings. Its transformation of the feminine principle remains source symbolism. Together these describe growing cultural or social participation without guaranteeing success at every stage.
