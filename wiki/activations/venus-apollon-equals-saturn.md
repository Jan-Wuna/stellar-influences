## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 186, Venus/Apollon, Saturn row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Interrupted art activity, for a short time. Temporary interruption of of tuition. Temporary separation from many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 291; one-based PDF p. 144, Venus/Apollon, Saturn row; complete row and neighboring glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Old knowledge of art, widespread problems of harmony, increased disturbances of peace, loss-making art trade, much harmony in old age, preference for tasks of science, growing enjoyment of concentration, and increasing financial losses.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Cultural activity encounters interruption or separation.
- **psychology:** ABC includes growing enjoyment of concentration as an alternative.
- **body/health:** No bodily condition is supplied.
- **social/relationship:** Witte specifies temporary separation from many.
- **events/manifestations:** Temporary interruption of art activity or tuition; disturbances of peace, loss-making art trade and financial losses alongside harmony in old age and enjoyment of concentration.
- **conflicts/notes:** Witte's repeated of of is present in the scan and retained as printed; its interruptions remain explicitly temporary. ABC distinguishes art knowledge from art trade.

## Contradictions

Witte's interruptions are explicitly temporary. ABC includes old art knowledge, peace disturbances and loss-making art trade, alongside harmony in old age and enjoyment of concentration; these alternatives do not establish permanent isolation or inevitable loss.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Saturn`, cultural participation or contact with others is interrupted or reduced. [Witte](#witte-entry) repeatedly qualifies interrupted art, tuition and separation as temporary. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds widespread harmony problems, disturbances of peace and losses in art trade or finance, while also preserving old art knowledge, harmony in old age and growing enjoyment of concentration. The range therefore includes adverse interruptions and more focused attention without making either a permanent condition.
