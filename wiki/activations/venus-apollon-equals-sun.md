## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Peaceful and friendly manners. To incline to peace. To make peace. A peaceful man.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful artist, Successful Peace Center, Man's Love of Freedom, Man's Experience of Love, Personal Love Success, Personal Success through Harmony, Preference for People of Science, and Center for Arts Science

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** A friendly personal manner inclines toward peace.
- **psychology:** Peaceful inclination and love of freedom appear in the sources.
- **body/health:** No bodily condition is asserted.
- **social/relationship:** ABC adds personal success in love.
- **events/manifestations:** Peacemaking and artistic success are stated.
- **conflicts/notes:** Masculine historical examples are not exclusive gender rules.

## Contradictions

Witte's peaceful character and ABC's success-oriented examples do not directly contradict one another. “Man” and “man's” are historical wording here and do not justify assigning the meaning exclusively by sex.

Source locators from the evidence cache: ABC printed p. 290 (PDF p. 144); Witte PDF p. 186 (entry start). These passages have not been visually verified.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Sun`, Sun specifies the Venus/Apollon axis through a person's friendly and peace-seeking manner. [Witte](#witte-entry) makes peacemaking an active expression of that disposition; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds personal success through harmony and success in art or love. The themes meet where agreeable conduct becomes a personal contribution to a cultural or social setting.
