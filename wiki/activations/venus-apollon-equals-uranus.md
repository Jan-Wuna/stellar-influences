## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 186, Venus/Apollon, Uranus row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Burning love for art and science, especially for astrology.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 291; one-based PDF p. 144, Venus/Apollon, Uranus row; complete row and neighboring glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Exciting experiences of love, successful artistic technique, a successful artistic event, dynamic art trade, harmony through new knowledge, harmonious impulses of knowledge, increased peace impulses, and preference for new knowledge.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Cultural enthusiasm meets new knowledge and exciting experience.
- **psychology:** Witte names burning love for art, science and astrology.
- **body/health:** Neither entry provides a health claim.
- **social/relationship:** ABC adds exciting love experiences, without a complete relationship profile.
- **events/manifestations:** Artistic technique, an artistic event, dynamic art trade, harmony through new knowledge and increased peace impulses.
- **conflicts/notes:** The complete row distinguishes Kunst-Technik, Kunst-Ereignis and Kunst-Handel; astrology remains Witte's explicit minority emphasis.

## Contradictions

Burning cultural or intellectual enthusiasm and exciting love experiences are different settings. Artistic technique, events and trade remain distinct ABC expressions, and Witte's special mention of astrology is preserved.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Uranus`, cultural or intellectual interests are animated by excitement and new knowledge. [Witte](#witte-entry) names burning love for art and science, especially astrology; [ABC](#abc-entry) gives exciting love experiences, artistic technique, an artistic event and dynamic art trade as concrete expressions. Harmony through new knowledge and increased peace impulses extend the range beyond excitement alone without making every novelty beneficial.
