## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 186, Venus/Apollon, Vernal Point row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Many friendships. General harmony with many. The general attitude toward peace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 290; one-based PDF p. 144, Venus/Apollon, Vernal Point row; complete row and neighboring glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Spreading harmony in public, artistic successes in public, art and commerce in public life, knowledge of art in public, much beauty in public life, and increasing harmony in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Friendship and harmony extend into general or public life.
- **psychology:** Witte gives a general attitude toward peace.
- **body/health:** No bodily claim appears.
- **social/relationship:** Many friendships and harmony with many are explicit.
- **events/manifestations:** Public artistic success, art and commerce, art knowledge, beauty and increasing harmony.
- **conflicts/notes:** The complete six-clause ABC row includes public artistic success and art knowledge; viel means much, without an omitted or damaged quantity clause.

## Contradictions

General friendships, peace and public cultural activity are compatible emphases. Public scope does not establish a private partnership or imply that every public activity is harmonious.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Vernal Point`, friendship and harmony extend into public relations. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes many friendships, harmony with many and a general attitude toward peace; [ABC](#abc-entry) specifies public artistic success, art and commerce, knowledge of art and beauty in public life. The supported range is friendly or cultural influence at a public scale, without turning it into a claim about every private relationship.
