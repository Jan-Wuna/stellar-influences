## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Leader in the fields of art and science. Artistic and scientific creations. Treading new paths in art or science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Spreading of artistic achievements, successful peace efforts, successful procreation in love, successful beauty design, creative arts trade, spreading of an artistic direction, preference for increasing achievements, and increasing striving in love

- Source page: 291 (printed; PDF p. 144). The Zeus row reads “Liebes-Zeugung”, “Kunst-Richtung” and “Liebes-Streben”; the earlier love-denial, artificial-direction and love-strike translations were incorrect.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Cultural interest takes form through creative work and leadership.
- **psychology:** ABC includes a preference for increasing achievement.
- **body/health:** Neither entry establishes a bodily meaning.
- **social/relationship:** The entry concerns creative participation rather than a complete relationship profile.
- **events/manifestations:** Witte names new paths and creations in art or science.
- **conflicts/notes:** ABC's procreation phrase is historical source symbolism, not a fertility prediction.

## Contradictions

The entries share creative achievement; ABC also applies production and striving to love and peace. These are differences of application rather than a direct disagreement.

The ABC row and glyph were checked against PDF p. 144. Witte's evidence-cache locator is PDF p. 186 (entry start).

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Apollon = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the Venus/Apollon axis through creative achievement and the initiative to open a new path. [Witte](#witte-entry) connects leadership in art or science with the making of new work; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds the spread of artistic achievements, beauty design and peace efforts. Shared cultural interest becomes something deliberately produced or advanced.
