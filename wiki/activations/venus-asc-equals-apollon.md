## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Good standing in relationships with friends. To be loved by many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Extension of peace in the place, freedom and peace in the place, love of freedom of the partner, successful art in the place, love experiences of the partner, preference for trade in the place, art and science in the environment, and increasing joy in the place

- Source page: 109 (printed; PDF p. 53). “Erfolgreiche Kunst” and “Kunst und Wissenschaft” refer to art, not an individual artist.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Affection and good standing extend across a wider local circle.
- **psychology:** ABC includes increasing joy and a partner's love of freedom.
- **body/health:** Neither entry establishes a health condition.
- **social/relationship:** Witte explicitly describes being loved by many.
- **events/manifestations:** Local peace, trade, art and science are settings in ABC.
- **conflicts/notes:** Widened goodwill does not prove every relationship is close.

## Contradictions

Many friendships and local cultural activity are compatible emphases. Being loved by many does not establish equal intimacy in every relationship.

The ABC row and glyph were checked against PDF p. 53. Witte's evidence-cache locator is PDF p. 84 (entry start).

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Asc = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the Venus/Asc axis through an expanded circle of friendly relations. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes good standing with friends and being loved by many; [ABC](#abc-entry) places peace, joy and cultural or commercial activity in the surrounding locality. The core is affection that extends beyond a single tie into a congenial social environment.
