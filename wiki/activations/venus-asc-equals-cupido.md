#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 109 (printed); PDF page: 53; scanned activator row checked.

The joy of a community in the place, peace in the common environment, peaceful local community, harmony in the community with other, harmonious partner community, harmonious community in the near, and art community in the locality

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Affection is situated in family, partnership and local community.
- **psychology:** ABC describes joy in shared surroundings.
- **body/health:** No bodily meaning is asserted.
- **social/relationship:** Witte names social acquaintances, marriage interest and beloved family nearby.
- **events/manifestations:** A peaceful local community is ABC's concrete setting.
- **conflicts/notes:** Family intimacy and local artistic community are complementary settings.

The categories follow the local source entries: [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry).

## Contradictions

Family intimacy and local artistic community are complementary settings. The scanned ABC row says an art community, rather than the former cached “artificial community.”

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Asc = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the Venus/Asc axis through the shared life of a family, partnership or local community. [Witte](#witte-entry) links social acquaintances and interest in marriage with nearby beloved family members; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds joy and harmony in common surroundings. The unifying meaning is affection experienced as belonging to a circle that shares everyday life.
