## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 84; scanned activator row checked.

Difficulties or restrictions in love or other pleasant connections. To be the object of mercenary or illicit love. Servants and people socially below the native show their affection.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 109 (printed); PDF page: 53; scanned activator row checked.

Danger for the harmony in the environment, harmony deficiency with others, art from places of the past, criminal love involving a partner, love deficiency in the place, love partner from the past, hidden love to the other, and renunciation of partner love

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Affection and agreeable connections encounter restriction or deficit.
- **psychology:** ABC adds hidden love and renunciation, without a complete psychological account.
- **body/health:** No medical condition is established.
- **social/relationship:** Witte includes mercenary or illicit love and historically classed service relationships.
- **events/manifestations:** ABC names a past partner and diminished harmony in the surroundings.
- **conflicts/notes:** Restrictions and hidden or illicit affection remain adverse possibilities; the class language belongs to Witte's historical wording and does not establish anyone's social inferiority.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Asc = Hades`, Hades specifies the Venus/Asc axis through difficulty in receiving or sustaining agreeable contact. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes restrictions in love and the possibility of mercenary or illicit affection; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a lack of harmony, hidden love, renunciation and a partner from the past. These are distinct ways affection can be constrained or complicated, without making any person's social position evidence of illicit conduct.
