## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 84; scanned activator row checked.

To be the object of love’s happiness. Fortunate and agreeable experiences with others. Nice conversations with others. Petting.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 108 (printed); PDF page: 53; scanned activator row checked.

Joy about peace in the environment, money for art in the locality, happiness and harmony in the environment, harmony through money of the partner, love happiness with the partner, artistic success in the locality, legal advantages of the other, and preference for law in the environment

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 196 (printed), entry `0667`.

A sense of beauty and art, appreciation of luxury, sociable. An affectionate relationship with other persons, happiness in love. A wealth of art.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 65 (exact passage checked in the raw PDF text).

To be generous in the display of affections. An eagerness to put others at ease and to cause them to like you.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `216`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased desire to notice fashion and style changes in society; an added ability to be recognized for your talents; expanded ideas or artistic qualities to present new social changes or directions to people or the world in general.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Warm social presentation meets affection, enjoyment and recognition.
- **psychology:** Sandbach names generous affection and eagerness to put others at ease.
- **body/health:** The entries establish no bodily condition.
- **social/relationship:** Witte and Ebertin emphasize agreeable encounters and happiness in love.
- **events/manifestations:** Munkasey adds recognition of talents and presenting artistic or social change.
- **conflicts/notes:** Affectionate contact, luxury, financial support for art, law, and public recognition differ in setting.

The categories follow the local source entries: [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).

## Contradictions

Affectionate contact, luxury, financial support for art, law, and public recognition differ in setting. The entries support enjoyment and recognition without determining one outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Asc = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the Venus/Asc axis through generous affection and an enjoyable social presence. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describe agreeable contact, sociability and happiness in love, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) gives the interpersonal action: showing affection and putting others at ease. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) extends this outward presentation to recognized talents, style and artistic ideas offered to a wider audience. The throughline is warmth and aesthetic interest becoming a means of connection and visibility.
