## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 84; scanned activator row checked.

To be desired as husband, as head of the family. To be liked and loved by superiors. To gain advancement through good acquaintances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 109 (printed); PDF page: 53; scanned activator row checked.

Respected place of art, special harmony in the environment, special love for the place, peaceful partner of the state, great joy at the place, harmony with the hierarchy in the environment, harmonious local direction, and independent artist as partner

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Affection and social standing connect with leadership or authority.
- **psychology:** ABC includes special joy and affection for a place.
- **body/health:** No health claim is supplied.
- **social/relationship:** Witte names favor from superiors and desirability in a family leadership role.
- **events/manifestations:** Advancement through acquaintances is explicit in Witte.
- **conflicts/notes:** Family responsibility, favor from superiors, and harmonious local direction are distinct authority settings.

The categories follow the local source entries: [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry).

## Contradictions

Family responsibility, favor from superiors, and harmonious local direction are distinct authority settings. Witte's husband/head-of-family wording remains historical, not a universal gender prescription.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Asc = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the Venus/Asc axis through affection connected with standing and responsibility. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes being liked by superiors and gaining advancement through acquaintances; his family-leadership example places desirability in a responsible role. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds harmony with hierarchy in the environment. The emphasis is favorable personal relations where authority or social position matters.
