## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 84; scanned activator row checked.

Spiritual harmony with others. Soulmates in harmony. Trains of thoughts about the beautiful appearance of the acquainted person.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 109 (printed); PDF page: 53; scanned activator row checked.

Peace and culture in the place, harmony and clarity in the environment, harmonious spirit in the place, art and culture of a place, place of spiritual arts, beauty of the local culture, true love for the partner, and the partner's love of truth

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Harmony is connected with a shared spiritual or cultural outlook.
- **psychology:** Witte includes thoughts about an acquaintance's beauty.
- **body/health:** No bodily condition is supplied.
- **social/relationship:** Witte names spiritual harmony and soulmates; ABC adds true partner love.
- **events/manifestations:** Peace, culture and spiritual art are settings in ABC.
- **conflicts/notes:** Spiritual affinity, aesthetic thought, and local culture are related emphases.

The categories follow the local source entries: [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry).

## Contradictions

Spiritual affinity, aesthetic thought, and local culture are related emphases. Soulmate language remains the source's description rather than proof that a particular relationship is destined.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Asc = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the Venus/Asc axis through affinity that is experienced as spiritual harmony. [Witte](#witte-entry) names harmonious soulmates and appreciation of an acquaintance's appearance; [ABC](#abc-entry) places clarity, spiritual art and true partner love within a cultural environment. The throughline is affection joined to shared outlook and perceived beauty, without a promise that a particular connection is a destined soulmate bond.
