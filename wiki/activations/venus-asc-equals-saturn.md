## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 84; scanned activator row checked.

To be deserted. Disturbances and interruptions in love-affairs.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 109 (printed); PDF page: 53; scanned activator row checked.

Older love partner, disturbed harmony in the environment, harmony through patience with the partner, artistic tasks for a place, love pain by the partner, concentration on local art, maturing partner love, and loss of the partner's love

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 196 (printed), entry `0668`.

Inhibitions, shyness, love of solitude. Difficulties, estrangement, termination of a relationship.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 65 (exact passage checked in the raw PDF text).

To be inhibited and/or fearful about expressing one’s love. To feel that one is not attractive or is unappreciated. Having difficulty in letting go of a relationship. To be constant, deep, and profound in the expression of one’s affection.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `216`

#### Munkasey Entry

Withdrawing from others and being alone with self; finding that you will not be hurried or pushed by others; new barriers to the way you want life to go; not getting your way with others; shorter periods with charming people.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Affection meets barriers, withdrawal or the need for patience.
- **psychology:** Ebertin names shyness and solitude; Sandbach adds fear of expressing love.
- **body/health:** No bodily condition is established.
- **social/relationship:** Estrangement and loss coexist with Sandbach's constant, deep affection and ABC's maturing love.
- **events/manifestations:** Interruptions, shorter contact and relationship termination are stated alternatives.
- **conflicts/notes:** Constancy and maturing affection coexist with estrangement or loss.

The categories follow the local source entries: [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).

## Contradictions

Constancy and maturing affection coexist with estrangement or loss. The scanned ABC row concerns disturbed harmony and concentration on art, while patience remains explicit.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Asc = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the Venus/Asc axis through difficulty in expressing or maintaining affectionate contact. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes desertion and interruptions; [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) adds shyness, estrangement and possible termination. Yet [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) also allows constancy and depth of affection, while [ABC](#abc-entry) names patience and maturing partner love. The relationship between these meanings is affection held under constraint: it may persist with depth or be experienced through withdrawal and loss.
