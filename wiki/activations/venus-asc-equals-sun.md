## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 84; scanned activator row checked.

Love of others bestowed upon the body. Harmony in personal connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 108 (printed); PDF page: 53; scanned activator row checked.

The man as a harmonious partner, joy with other persons, harmony centre at the place, artist centre at the place, place of personal peace, personal love partner, personal preference for a place, and preference for a person of the environment

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 196 (printed), entry `0663`.

A harmonious disposition coupled with a beautiful physique, graceful, a sense of art, an appreciation of beauty. The ability to create an impression through physical beauty. Love affair.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 65 (exact passage checked in the raw PDF text).

One feels it is important to get along with others, be a peacemaker, and project a loving disposition. Egotism expressing as selfishness.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/ASCENDANT with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `216`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increases the sincerity and appearance of your charm and manners as you meet others; impels you to maintain your appearance, and to appear as a loving and kind person to all you meet; you become famous for your style.

## Comparative Schema

- **core meaning:** Affection is visible in personal conduct and physical presentation.
- **psychology:** Sandbach pairs a wish to get along with a separate selfish expression.
- **body/health:** Ebertin's beautiful-physique association is historical interpretive language, not a bodily assessment.
- **social/relationship:** Witte names harmonious personal connections and affection toward the body.
- **events/manifestations:** Munkasey emphasizes maintaining appearance and becoming known for style.
- **conflicts/notes:** Sandbach gives both peacemaking and selfish egotism.

The categories follow the local source entries: [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry).

## Contradictions

Sandbach gives both peacemaking and selfish egotism. Ebertin's physique wording and ABC's masculine example remain attributed, rather than universal bodily or gender claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Asc = Sun`, Sun specifies the Venus/Asc axis through the person's visible expression of affection. [Witte](#witte-entry) joins personal harmony with love directed toward the body; [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) associates grace and beauty with the impression made on others. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds attention to manners and appearance, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) gives both peacemaking and an alternative of selfish egotism. An agreeable outward manner is central, but its relational use may be considerate or self-centered.
