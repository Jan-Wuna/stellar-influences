## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 84; scanned activator row checked.

To make nice acquaintances on the way. General likes of the native.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 108 (printed); PDF page: 53; scanned activator row checked.

Joy at a public place, harmony with others in public, artists in the environment of the public, place of art in public, public place of peace, beautiful public place, and preference for the public environment

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Pleasant contact in public places.
- Psychology: Enjoyment of shared surroundings.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Nice acquaintances encountered along the way.
- Events/manifestations: Public art, beauty, and peaceful places.
- Conflicts/notes: Public enjoyment and pleasant acquaintances are the stated range.

The categories follow the local source entries: [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry).

## Contradictions

Public enjoyment and pleasant acquaintances are the stated range. The entries do not establish a private relationship outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Venus/Asc through agreeable encounters in public life. Nice acquaintances met along the way belong with joy in public places, accessible art, and harmonious surroundings. The common setting is the shared environment where affection and beauty become part of ordinary social contact. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
