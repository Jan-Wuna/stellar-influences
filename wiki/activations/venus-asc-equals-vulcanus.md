## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Venus/Asc through the strength of affection received from others and the influence a close environment can carry. Partner love, the power of shared joy, and influential local art connect strong attachment with the atmosphere around it. Witte's accompanying proverb emphasizes one's company without establishing a fixed rule about character. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
