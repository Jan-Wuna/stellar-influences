## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Venus/Chiron through active cooperation that does not require everyone to want the same thing. Sandbach joins respect for different outlooks with a conditional view of conflict as constructive when seen clearly. The phrase “confront contacts” is retained from the raw PDF text; its uncertain wording is not silently rewritten. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).)
