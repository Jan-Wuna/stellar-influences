### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 60 (exact entry passage checked in the raw PDF text).

Helping others to see the true value of all that is around them is central to one’s life mission. The ability to be receptive to the love that is everywhere around one.

## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Venus/Chiron through a central commitment to helping others recognize value. Receptivity matters alongside that outward purpose: the person is also able to receive love from their surroundings. The entry describes a meaningful orientation of life without identifying a particular profession or guaranteed public result. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).)
