## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 59 (exact entry passage checked in the raw PDF text).

The ability to feel love everywhere and in everything and to contact an underly ing sense of peace and fulfillment even in the midst of the most difficult of emotions.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Love and peace remain accessible amid difficult feelings.
- Psychology: Underlying fulfillment alongside emotional difficulty.
- Body/health: No medical claim; difficulty is emotional here.
- Social/relationship: A felt sense of love, without a specific relationship pattern.
- Events/manifestations: An inner experience rather than a specified external event.
- Conflicts/notes: Peace coexists with difficult emotions; their disappearance is not stated.

These categories follow the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).

## Contradictions

Peace coexists with difficult emotions; their disappearance is not stated. No second source is represented for comparison.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Venus/Chiron through an ability to feel love and reach an underlying sense of peace even during difficult emotions. Fulfillment is not presented as the absence of distress: it can be contacted within it. The brief entry establishes this emotional possibility without describing a method or predicting external circumstances. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).)
