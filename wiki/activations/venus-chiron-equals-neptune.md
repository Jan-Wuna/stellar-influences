## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 59 (exact entry passage checked in the raw PDF text).

To intuitively perceive the hidden beauty that is everywhere around one, and to be healed by this perception, as well as being able to potentially use it to foster the healing of others.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Hidden beauty is perceived intuitively.
- Psychology: Intuitive appreciation and a healing response.
- Body/health: Healing is spiritual language; no medical treatment is established.
- Social/relationship: Perception may also help others.
- Events/manifestations: Appreciation used to support another's healing.
- Conflicts/notes: Helping others is explicitly potential, not guaranteed.

These categories follow the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).

## Contradictions

Helping others is explicitly potential, not guaranteed. No second source is represented for comparison.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Venus/Chiron through intuition that recognizes beauty others may overlook. Sandbach describes that perception as healing for the person and potentially helpful to others. The claim remains a spiritual account of appreciation; the entry establishes neither a clinical effect nor certainty that another person will be helped. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).)
