## Derived Synthesis

Pluto specifies Venus/Chiron through the intensity of a search for true love. The persistent call of beauty and the burning desire are connected aspects of that search. Sandbach describes its strength and direction without establishing that it results in a relationship or giving a separate account of obstacles. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).)
