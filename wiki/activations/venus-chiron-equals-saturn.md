## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 59 (exact entry passage checked in the raw PDF text).

Difficulties encountered in love, as well as lack of gratification in life and lack of prosperity encourage one to recognize the deeper value of all one’s experiences, and eventually to come to see that one is truly rich.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Difficulty opens a deeper sense of value.
- Psychology: Reappraising experience amid dissatisfaction.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Difficulties in love are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Lack of prosperity and gratification.
- Conflicts/notes: Recognizing richness does not mean material deprivation disappears.

These categories follow the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).

## Contradictions

Recognizing richness does not mean material deprivation disappears. No second source is represented for comparison.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Venus/Chiron through disappointment that encourages a reassessment of value. Difficult love, limited gratification, and lack of prosperity can lead toward recognizing richness in experience itself. The source describes a possible change of understanding rather than promising material improvement or requiring suffering as a virtue. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).)
