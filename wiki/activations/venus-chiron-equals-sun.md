## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 59 (exact entry passage checked in the raw PDF text).

One’s presence has a centering and calming effect on others. One brings to light hidden values which were possibly unsuspected by others, as well as the deeper val ues of everything. The overall effect of this is to promote the realization that we live in a rich universe of endless treasures.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A calming presence reveals unsuspected worth.
- Psychology: Recognizing depth and richness.
- Body/health: Calming is described; no medical effect is established.
- Social/relationship: Helping others recognize hidden values.
- Events/manifestations: Bringing overlooked worth to light.
- Conflicts/notes: The universe's endless treasures is the source's spiritual image.

These categories follow the [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry).

## Contradictions

The universe's endless treasures is the source's spiritual image. No second source is represented for comparison.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Sun specifies Venus/Chiron through a presence that centers others while bringing unsuspected value to light. Calm and appreciation are part of one movement: recognition of deeper worth promotes a sense of living in a rich universe. The entry describes that interpersonal effect without defining a particular external event. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry).)
