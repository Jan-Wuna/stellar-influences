## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 182; scanned activator row checked.

Much luck in marriage or society. Harmonious association with many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 283 (printed); PDF page: 140; scanned activator row checked.

Success of a community through harmony, a free community of artists, shared successes in peace, shared artistic successes, shared experiences of love, a community of art dealers, a preference for the synthesis of knowledge, and an increasing preference for the whole.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Harmony expands association and shared success.
- Psychology: Appreciation of knowledge and the whole.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Harmonious association with many; free artists' community.
- Events/manifestations: Art dealing and shared success in art or peace.
- Conflicts/notes: Success is stated as a possibility, not a guarantee.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Success is stated as a possibility, not a guarantee.

## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Venus/Cupido through harmonious association reaching many people. Shared artistic and peace successes connect a wider social circle with common achievement. ABC's art dealers and synthesis of knowledge preserve commercial and intellectual settings alongside marriage and pleasant society. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
