## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 182; scanned activator row checked.

Marital happiness spoiled by poverty. Harmony and happiness under otherwise miserable conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 283 (printed); PDF page: 140; scanned activator row checked.

A troubled community of love, services of love for a community, shared danger to love, a criminal community of love, deficient harmony in a community, renunciation of peace in the community, and a preference for a shared past.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared love under poverty or other strain.
- Psychology: Troubled common feeling and attachment to the past.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Harmony amid miserable circumstances; dangerous ties.
- Events/manifestations: Service to a community and shared danger.
- Conflicts/notes: Poverty can spoil happiness, yet Witte also allows harmony within hardship.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Poverty can spoil happiness, yet Witte also allows harmony within hardship.

## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Venus/Cupido through a community or marriage living under strain. Poverty may spoil happiness, but Witte also preserves harmony despite miserable conditions. ABC extends the range to service, shared danger, and deficient peace, without making hardship itself proof that affection has ended. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
