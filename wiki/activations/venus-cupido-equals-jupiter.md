## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 182; scanned activator row checked.

Great happiness in married life. To be happy in pleasant company.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 282 (printed); PDF page: 140; scanned activator row checked.

A successful legal community, joy in the beauty of art, success in peace for a community, shared artistic success, shared joy in love, a shared preference for money, and legal advantages for a community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Joy and benefit experienced together.
- Psychology: Enjoyment of love and artistic beauty.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Happiness in marriage and pleasant company.
- Events/manifestations: Shared artistic, legal, financial, and peace advantages.
- Conflicts/notes: Shared enjoyment does not establish universal marital happiness.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Shared enjoyment does not establish universal marital happiness.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Venus/Cupido through happiness that is shared in marriage, company, or a community. Artistic beauty, successful peace efforts, and legal advantages give the pleasure concrete forms. ABC's shared interest in money also places benefit within collective practical concerns. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
