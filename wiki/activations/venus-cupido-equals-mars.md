## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 182; scanned activator row checked.

Happy intercourse in married life. To come to intimate acquaintances through pleasant company.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 282 (printed); PDF page: 140; scanned activator row checked.

A working community in art, creating harmony with the whole, a peaceful working community, shared love of art, actions of a community of love, a preference for community work, and a preference for artistic work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared affection enacted in work and intimacy.
- Psychology: The wish to create harmony with the whole.
- Body/health: Sexual intercourse is a source expression, not a health claim.
- Social/relationship: Intimate acquaintances through pleasant company.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic collaboration and peaceful community work.
- Conflicts/notes: The work and sexual meanings are separate supported expressions.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

The work and sexual meanings are separate supported expressions.

## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies Venus/Cupido through people acting together in affection. Artistic collaboration and peaceful community work give shared harmony an active form. Witte separately includes happy marital intercourse and intimate acquaintances through company, retaining an intimate range alongside the collective work. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
