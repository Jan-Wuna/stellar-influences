### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 182; scanned activator row checked.

To be endowed with much love in married life. To be in harmonious company or society. The love and affection to the native from community.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 282 (printed); PDF page: 140; scanned activator row checked.

Soulful art of peace, a soulful community of love, I love art, my peaceful community, my community with artists, beautiful soulful art, inner harmony in a community, and a preference for a soulful community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared affection experienced personally.
- Psychology: Inner harmony and love of art.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Receiving love from marriage or community.
- Events/manifestations: A personally meaningful peaceful artistic circle.
- Conflicts/notes: Receiving affection is emphasized, not a defined public role.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Venus/Cupido through the person's experience of being loved within a marriage or community. ABC connects that belonging with inner harmony, soulful art, and a personally valued artists' circle. The emphasis is how shared affection is experienced, without establishing a particular vocation. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
