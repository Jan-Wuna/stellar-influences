## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Venus/Cupido through a shared bond whose harmony may be subtle, secret, or insecure. Sensitivity can support a loving community, while misunderstood love and dissolution describe the adverse range. ABC's metaphysical interest offers a further collective setting without cancelling the possibility of loss. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
