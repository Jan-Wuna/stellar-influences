## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 182; scanned activator row checked.

Separated marital happiness. Passing harmonious ties with others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 283 (printed); PDF page: 140; scanned activator row checked.

Tasks of an artists' community, problems of harmony within a community, harmonious community in old age, a maturing community of love, separation of a community of love, loss of a shared love, and a preference for serious art.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared harmony meets tasks, maturity, or separation.
- Psychology: Serious artistic interest.
- Body/health: Old-age community is a historical association, not a prognosis.
- Social/relationship: Maturing love or passing and separated ties.
- Events/manifestations: Artists' communal tasks and loss of shared love.
- Conflicts/notes: Maturity and separation remain alternatives within the range.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Maturity and separation remain alternatives within the range.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Venus/Cupido through the demands and limits of maintaining shared harmony. An artists' community may have tasks to carry, and a love community may mature with age. Passing ties, separation, and loss remain equally explicit, so endurance is a possibility rather than an assured result. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
