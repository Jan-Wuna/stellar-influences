## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies Venus/Cupido through affection that brings a sudden change in shared life. Unexpected pleasant company and marital happiness sit beside common love impulses that stir tension or unrest. Dynamic artists' circles, rhythmic art, and technical community give that movement explicit collective forms. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
