## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 182; scanned activator row checked.

Conclusion of a marriage, of a sex relationship. Marital happiness through the blessing of children.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 283 (printed); PDF page: 140; scanned activator row checked.

Efforts of a peace community, a fiery community of love, a shared preference for achievements, a harmonious community of performance, a creative art community, achievements of a harmonious community, and a procreative community of love.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared affection becomes creative achievement.
- Psychology: Fiery commitment to common aims.
- Body/health: Historical children and procreation claims; no pregnancy prediction.
- Social/relationship: Marriage or sexual bond; creative community.
- Events/manifestations: Peace efforts and artistic performance.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's wording “Conclusion of a marriage” is preserved alongside its reference to children.

## Derived Synthesis

Zeus specifies Venus/Cupido through shared affection directed into creation and achievement. A harmonious working or artistic circle and efforts for peace give the common aim concrete forms. Witte's marriage, sexual relationship, and happiness through children remain historical source associations, without predicting a reproductive event. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
