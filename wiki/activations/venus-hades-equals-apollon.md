### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 183; scanned activator row checked.

Friendship with low class people, fellow workmen, colleagues. Acquaintances among prostitutes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 285 (printed); PDF page: 141; scanned activator row checked.

Old knowledge of art, success in harmony through deprivation, sorrowful experiences of love, deficient successes in peace, trade in art from the past, a preference for secret knowledge, and increasing sorrow in love.

## Derived Synthesis

Apollon specifies Venus/Hades through associations and knowledge linked with service, sorrow, or the past. Friendship among colleagues and trade in old art give those themes concrete settings. Success through deprivation remains possible beside increasing love-sorrow; Witte's social rankings do not establish anyone's worth. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
