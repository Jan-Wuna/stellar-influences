## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 183; scanned activator row checked.

Deal with people with vulgar manners. Tied to others through low love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 284 (printed); PDF page: 141; scanned activator row checked.

An old place of art, danger to peace in the environment, peaceful service to the environment, a dangerous preference of the partner, a mysterious artistic environment, lack of harmony in the locality, secret love for another, and lack of harmony with the partner.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Troubled affection encountered through partner and surroundings.
- Psychology: Secrets and a partner's dangerous preferences.
- Body/health: No clinical claim; the corrected ABC setting is artistic.
- Social/relationship: Hidden love and impaired partner harmony.
- Events/manifestations: Old art places and service to the surroundings.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's moral vocabulary is attributed, not a judgment of a person.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Venus/Hades through people and places where affection is secret or harmony is lacking. A partner's dangerous preferences and Witte's disparaging relationship language describe difficult contact. An old art setting and peaceful service to the surroundings remain distinct possibilities within the same entry. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
