## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 183; scanned activator row checked.

Pleasant and happy service relations. Income from prostitution.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 284 (printed); PDF page: 141; scanned activator row checked.

Success in love through deprivation, successful service to peace, joy in service in love, dangerous financial wishes, endangered success in peace, harmony despite lack of money, a preference for financial crime, and advantage through service in love.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Benefit or joy within service and deprivation.
- Psychology: Joy in serving love; risky financial wishes.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Pleasant service relations and harmony despite financial lack.
- Events/manifestations: Peace work, income, and financial crime among the stated possibilities.
- Conflicts/notes: Financial deprivation and benefit coexist; the crime claim is not a character diagnosis.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Financial deprivation and benefit coexist; the crime claim is not a character diagnosis.

## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Venus/Hades through enjoyment or advantage that can exist within service and deprivation. Harmony despite lack of money and successful peace work preserve positive meanings alongside dangerous financial wishes. Witte's income-from-prostitution example and ABC's financial crime remain distinct source statements, not assumptions about someone's livelihood. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
