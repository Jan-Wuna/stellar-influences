## Derived Synthesis

Kronos specifies Venus/Hades through service organized or supervised by authority. State employment and official control of service or sexual commerce are Witte's institutional examples; ABC also includes special peace service and dangers to state peace. Past art and great historical figures preserve a cultural range beyond regulation. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
