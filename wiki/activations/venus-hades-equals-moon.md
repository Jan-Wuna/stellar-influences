## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 183; scanned activator row checked.

Submissive persons. Low, servile people. Purchasable girls. Elderly maternal female.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 284 (printed); PDF page: 141; scanned activator row checked.

Old folk art, danger to the woman through love, dangerous love involving women, concealing feelings of love, the woman's sorrow in love, distress of a peace-loving people, a woman's lack of harmony, transitory female beauty, and a woman's renunciation of love.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Hidden feeling and sorrow within gendered or collective settings.
- Psychology: Concealing affection and renouncing love.
- Body/health: Aging beauty is described; no bodily disorder is stated.
- Social/relationship: Historical submissive and sexual-role language; an elderly maternal figure.
- Events/manifestations: Old folk art and distress of a peace-loving people.
- Conflicts/notes: The sources' social and gender classifications remain historical, not universal.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

The sources' social and gender classifications remain historical, not universal.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Venus/Hades through hidden affection, sorrow, and the passing of beauty. Old folk art and the distress of a peace-loving people widen the range beyond personal love. Witte's submissive and maternal figures and ABC's woman-language remain historical examples, without defining all women or their social position. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
