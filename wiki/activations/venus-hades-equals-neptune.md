## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Secret love or service becomes uncertain or dissolves.
- Psychology: Sorrow through uncertainty; sensitivity in old love.
- Body/health: Witte's infection and sexual labels are historical claims, not diagnoses.
- Social/relationship: Unreliable service relationships and dissolving hidden love.
- Events/manifestations: Danger to future peace; metaphysics of the past.
- Conflicts/notes: Pluto's development paragraph is excluded from the restored Neptune row.

## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies Venus/Hades through affection or service whose reliability is uncertain. A hidden love or service relationship may dissolve, while sensitivity in an old attachment remains possible. ABC adds concern for future peace and past metaphysics; Witte's medical and sexual classifications stay attributed rather than becoming clinical conclusions. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
