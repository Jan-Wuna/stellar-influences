#### Witte Entry

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 284 (printed); PDF page: 141; scanned activator row checked.

Encounter with the beauty of age, dangerous love connections, love relationships from the past, insufficient peace connections, hidden peace contacts, connection with art of the past, and a preference for secret connections.

## Derived Synthesis

Node specifies Venus/Hades through the ties that connect someone with service, past affection, or hidden contacts. Some are described as dangerous, while beauty in age and art of the past offer other reasons for connection. Witte's historical class and prostitution categories do not determine the value or conduct of particular associates. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
