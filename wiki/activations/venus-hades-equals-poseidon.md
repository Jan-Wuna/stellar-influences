## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 183; scanned activator row checked.

Submissive mental attitude. To be a servant of a worldview.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 285 (printed); PDF page: 141; scanned activator row checked.

Service to peace and culture, insightful service in love, knowledge about art crime, lack of peace and culture, the past of art and culture, lack of spiritual harmony, lack of love for truth, and hidden beauty of the spirit.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Service to ideas, peace, and culture.
- Psychology: Mental submission or insightful service.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Serving a worldview.
- Events/manifestations: Art-crime knowledge and hidden spiritual beauty.
- Conflicts/notes: Insightful service coexists with deficient truth-seeking or spiritual harmony.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Insightful service coexists with deficient truth-seeking or spiritual harmony.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Poseidon specifies Venus/Hades through service to a worldview or cultural purpose. Insightful loving service and care for peace stand beside mental submission, lack of spiritual harmony, or deficient love of truth. Knowledge of art crime and hidden spiritual beauty retain the entry's uncommon cultural meanings. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
