## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 183; scanned activator row checked.

Shirking. To shirk a service. Bad rendering of service. To quit the service. Passing connections with persons of vulgar standards or with lower services. Dirty illicit union.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 285 (printed); PDF page: 141; scanned activator row checked.

A dangerous task for peace, disturbed service to peace, sorrow through problems of harmony, lack of harmony in old age, maturing sorrow in love, separation from an old love, hidden beauty in old age, and a preference for dangerous tasks.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Service interrupted or relinquished; old love separated.
- Psychology: Sorrow deepens through harmony problems.
- Body/health: Age-related lack of harmony is a source association, not a prognosis.
- Social/relationship: Passing ties and separation from an old love.
- Events/manifestations: Disturbed peace service and dangerous tasks.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's shirking language does not explain every withdrawal from service.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Contradictions

Witte's shirking language does not explain every withdrawal from service.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Venus/Hades through limits or endings in service and affection. Quitting or poorly rendering service connects with disturbed peace work, while separation from an old love brings the theme into relationship. Hidden beauty in age remains alongside sorrow, so withdrawal does not exhaust the entry's range. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
