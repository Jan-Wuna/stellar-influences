## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 183; scanned activator row checked.

Quickly ready for service. Excitement through service relation. Sudden meanness or dirty tricks of common people. Quarrels and fights among prostitutes, or for love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 285 (printed); PDF page: 141; scanned activator row checked.

A dangerous love event, secret impulses of love, artists' technique from the past, sudden renunciation of love, tensions through sorrow in love, unrest through endangerment of peace, and unrest through past love.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Service or troubled love provokes sudden unrest.
- Psychology: Secret impulses and abrupt renunciation.
- Body/health: No specific health claim.
- Social/relationship: Excitement, quarrels, and tensions through past love.
- Events/manifestations: Quick readiness for service; artists' techniques from the past.
- Conflicts/notes: Quick service and conflict are separate expressions, not a single fixed temperament.

The categories follow the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry).

## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies Venus/Hades through sudden readiness or disturbance in service and love. Secret impulses, abrupt renunciation, and unrest around an old attachment connect the personal range with Witte's quarrels and service excitement. Artists' techniques from the past remain an additional, explicitly cultural expression. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
