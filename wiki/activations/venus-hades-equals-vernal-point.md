## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point specifies Venus/Hades through the public presence of service, difficult affection, and beauty with a past. Threats to peace and deficient public harmony coexist with old art and hidden beauty in shared life. Witte's general servitude and prostitution statements remain historical public categories rather than individual accusations. ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)
