## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Jupiter`
- Source reference: PDF page `177` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To have a few good friends. Luck through a few or with a few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Jupiter`
- Source reference: printed page `273`; PDF page `135` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Enduring preference for justice, limitation of financial wishes, lasting advantages through peace, blocked success in peace, restricted happiness in love, success through deepening harmony, strengthening the right to peace, and joy in the circulation of money.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Happiness sustained within a small circle, alongside restricted desires or blocked fulfillment.
- Psychology: Preference for a few enduring friendships; satisfaction through deepening harmony and limiting financial wishes.
- Body/health: No specific bodily or health claim occurs in either activation entry.
- Social/relationship: Good friends and luck through a few people; love may also feel restricted.
- Events/manifestations: Steady benefits from peace, a firmer right to peace, or blocked peace efforts; enjoyment of money circulating.
- Conflicts/notes: A small circle can support happiness without removing the ABC entry's restrictions.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Jupiter = Admetos` locates happiness in a few valued relationships and in harmony that becomes deeper and more lasting. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes good friends and good fortune through a small circle; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds enduring concern for justice, limited financial wishes, and steady benefits from peace. Its blocked peace efforts and restricted happiness in love remain part of the range: depth and continuity can provide satisfaction, while fulfillment may also be constrained.
