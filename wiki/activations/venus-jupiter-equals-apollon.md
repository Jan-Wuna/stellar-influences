## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Jupiter`
- Source reference: PDF page `177` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Many good friends and fortune through them. Luck and happiness through and with many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Jupiter`
- Source reference: printed page `273`; PDF page `135` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Success in art and science, successful expansion of peace, successful knowledge of art, joy over legal successes, experiencing happiness in love, unlimited love of money, much money through love, preference for money trading, and increasing financial wishes.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Happiness and opportunity extending through many friends, knowledge, art, and exchange.
- Psychology: Broadening interests and financial wishes; pleasure in learning about art and experiencing love.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a body or health delineation.
- Social/relationship: Many good friends and happiness shared with or obtained through many people.
- Events/manifestations: Success in art and science, expansion of peace, legal successes, and money trading.
- Conflicts/notes: The source's expanding financial wishes and unlimited love of money qualify its favorable language.

## Contradictions

- The source's expanding financial wishes and unlimited love of money qualify its favorable language.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Jupiter = Apollon` broadens happiness through many people and through the circulation of knowledge and resources. [Witte](#witte-entry) stresses numerous good friends and fortune through them; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends this outward movement to art, science, knowledge of art, legal success, and an expansion of peace. Financial opportunity belongs to the same range, together with growing financial wishes and an unlimited love of money. The entry therefore supports breadth of enjoyment and exchange without establishing a limit to appetite or guaranteeing gain.
