## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Jupiter`
- Source reference: PDF page `177` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

A person bewitching by his or her appearance. Goodwill and fortune through authorities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Jupiter`
- Source reference: printed page `273`; PDF page `135` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Respected artistic successes, prestige and success through beauty, special happiness in love, financial wishes of the state, joy over financial independence, great preference for money, great happiness in love, and important success in peace.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Prestige through beauty or artistic achievement, and favorable relations with authority.
- Psychology: Pleasure in recognition, financial independence, and happiness in love.
- Body/health: Appearance is described as captivating; no medical implication is stated.
- Social/relationship: Goodwill and fortune through authorities; special or considerable happiness in love.
- Events/manifestations: Respected artistic success, state financial wishes, and important peace achievements.
- Conflicts/notes: Authority, prestige, and financial independence are source themes, without a promise of office or wealth.

## Contradictions

- Authority, prestige, and financial independence are source themes, without a promise of office or wealth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Jupiter = Kronos` joins the appeal of beauty with standing, recognition, and support from authority. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a captivating appearance and goodwill through authorities; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops prestige through art or beauty, joy in financial independence, and important success in peace. Special happiness in love stands beside these public and financial concerns. The sources connect enjoyment with esteem and influence, while leaving the specific form of recognition or support open.
