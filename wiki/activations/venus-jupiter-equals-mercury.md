## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Thoughts of happy love. Joy through confiding in friends.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joy of a young love, thoughts about the happiness of love, talk about legal successes, harmonious talk about success, artificial success in speech, love joy in youth, news about peace successes, and talk about love pleasure

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Mirth, a love of entertainment, optimism, a sense of humor. Happiness in thought, the tendency to think of future happiness in love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To be alluring, seductive or flirtatious when communicating. To give high praise to something. Ideas of beauty and value come to one easily.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `192`

#### Munkasey Entry

Emphasizes your education in areas of philosophy, religion, ethics, etc.; your investigation of new systems of thought in economics, culture, religion, markets, etc.; increased ability to speak or write on similar subject areas.
