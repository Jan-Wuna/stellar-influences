## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Jupiter`
- Source reference: PDF page `177` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Joyous love of a woman. The great peace of the people. Hour of raptured happiness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Jupiter`
- Source reference: printed page `272`; PDF page `135` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

A successful beautiful woman, successful folk art, financial wishes of the woman, feelings of joy through love, feelings of happiness through harmony, artistic successes of the woman, happiness in love of a woman, wealth of a beautiful woman, and advantage through peace with the woman.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Venus/Jupiter`
- Source reference: printed page `179`, coded entry `0592`.

#### Ebertin Entry

A good-natured disposition, affectionate and cordial manner, charm. A woman permeated by the joy of love (bride or mother).

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Venus/Jupiter midpoint`
- Source reference: PDF page `56` (one-based; exact activation entry).

#### Sandbach Entry

Emotion expressed freely, easily, and generously. A lavishly affectionate disposi tion. Emotional insecurity causes one to be self-indulgent.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `192`

#### Munkasey Entry

Fundamental fears about the current social philosophy and its effect on home or the family; conflicting moods and sentiments which lead to errors in the judgment of values; arbitrary quality or evaluation methods.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Freely expressed affection and happiness, with emotional insecurity or conflicting feelings as an adverse range.
- Psychology: Generous feeling and charm; insecurity can prompt indulgence, while conflicting moods can distort judgments of value.
- Body/health: Ebertin's bride and mother imagery is historical relational language, not a medical or reproductive prediction.
- Social/relationship: Warm affection in close relationships, alongside concern about the effect of social ideas on home or family.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic success, folk art, and feelings of happiness through love or harmony in the ABC entry.
- Conflicts/notes: The historical sources use gendered woman/bride/mother language; Munkasey's fears and poor judgments remain a distinct adverse emphasis.

## Contradictions

- The historical sources use gendered woman/bride/mother language; Munkasey's fears and poor judgments remain a distinct adverse emphasis.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Jupiter = Moon` expresses happiness through feeling, affection, and the ease with which warmth reaches others. [Witte](#witte-entry), the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) use historical images of a happy woman, bride, or mother, while also describing harmony, charm, and artistic success. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) gives the emotional generosity an adverse condition: insecurity can turn it toward self-indulgence. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) is more cautionary, describing fears about social ideas affecting home and family, and moods that impair judgments of value. These concerns qualify the favorable pattern without reducing all affection to insecurity or treating the gendered images as requirements.
