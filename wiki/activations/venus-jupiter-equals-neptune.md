## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Love and affection with obstacles. Hopelessness of love’s happiness. Deception in the bliss of love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exchanged peace joy, joy over harmony of water, joy and harmony in the air, joyful love expectation, insecure love happiness, and refined love happiness

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Negligence, lack of correct behavior, falsehood. Hopelessness, a disharmonious or destructive relationship.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One fantasizes about luxuries and grandiosity. A lush, rich imagination that revels in the warmth of love. To radiate an ethereal beauty. To be impractical where lux uries are concerned. Overindulgence leads to a dissipation of one’s energies.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Aids the inflation or expansion of any current process; weakens any limitations which are imposed; expands illusions about your welfare or health in general; added confusion as a factor to delaying your progress anywhere.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Jupiter = Neptune`: inspired devotion, artistic richness, and the ability to enchant through kindness, beauty, and hope. Main caution: unreal sweetness: love and pleasure can become too idealized, escapist, or vulnerable to disappointment and excess.
