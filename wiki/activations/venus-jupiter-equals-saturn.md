## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Separated love. Slow ripening happiness in love. Doubtful happiness in love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 273; one-based PDF p. 135, Venus/Jupiter, Saturn row; complete actual row and adjacent glyphs checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: An old love that brings happiness, success in artistic tasks, disturbances of success through love, financial difficulties in peace, disturbed success in peace, disturbed harmony in old age, happiness through separation in love, and happiness and harmony in old age.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inhibitions in love life, lack of adaptability. Trouble and separation in love, the state of sobering down again, destroyed hopes.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To be suspicious about people who reach out to you. To desire luxuries but to hold oneself back from obtaining them. It may take one a long time to open up to oth ers, but when one finally does, there can be a warm, generous sharing.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/JUPITER with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `192`

#### Munkasey Entry

Increased caution or care about opinions or advice given to others; emphasizes the seriousness behind your intentions and principles; guarded statements about your stand on issues; an interest in business methods or cycles.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Slow or obstructed happiness in love ([Witte](#witte-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry)).
- psychology: Suspicion, inhibition, and restraint around luxuries or emotional opening ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- body/health: No specific bodily or health claim appears in these entries.
- social/relationship: Separation or a gradual movement toward generous sharing ([Witte](#witte-entry); [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry)).
- events/manifestations: Trouble in love, sobering expectations, and guarded advice ([Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry)).
- conflicts/notes: ABC retains both disturbed and happy old-age conditions; neither is an unconditional outcome.

## Contradictions

Separation, obstruction and happiness are distinct possibilities. ABC explicitly retains artistic tasks, disturbances of success, financial difficulties and both disturbed and happy old-age conditions; positive clauses do not erase the adverse range.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies Venus/Jupiter through happiness in love that is delayed, questioned, inhibited, or separated. Caution is central: opening to another person may be slow, although eventual sharing can be warm and generous. [Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), and [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) support this range. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) carries the same guarded quality into advice, public statements, and serious principles; the entries do not establish durable prosperity.

ABC's artistic tasks, disturbances of success through love and both disturbed and happy old-age conditions retain the full [source range](#abc-entry).
