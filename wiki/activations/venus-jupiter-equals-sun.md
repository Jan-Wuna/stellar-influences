## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Joyous love of a man. Love’s happiness on the day.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Successful art, Financial wishes of the man, Happiness through the love of the man, corperlichc's love happiness, artistic success of the man, love joy of the man, personal happiness through harmony, and right success of the man

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A warm heart, kindness, good will toward others, a healthy body. A man permeated by the joy of love, a happy man (bridegroom).

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One feels it is important to be generous and loving. One’s self-esteem is based on enthusiastically loving others, or negatively on one’s superficial beauty, having a lot of money, amassing treasures, etc.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Understanding the importance of social activities with new manners and politeness; increased feelings of luck and pleasure from taking chances; added importance given to politics, social opinion, and expectations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Jupiter = Sun`: radiant kindness, generous self-expression, and the ability to lead through encouragement, beauty, and good will. Main caution: self-esteem through abundance: identity can become too invested in approval, display, or the need to feel exceptionally fortunate and adored.
