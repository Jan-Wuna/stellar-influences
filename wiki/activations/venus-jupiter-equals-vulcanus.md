## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Great longing for happiness. Great optimism.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Influential financial desires, successful peace power, successful art influence, harmonizing influence of money, peaceful right power, enormous artistic success, powerful love happiness, and advantage through the power of beauty

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong longing for happiness and great optimism ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: Intense financial wishes and optimistic expectation ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- body/health: No bodily or health manifestation is stated.
- social/relationship: Powerful happiness in love and harmonizing influence ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- events/manifestations: Artistic success and advantage through beauty ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- conflicts/notes: The entry's power language is applied to happiness, money, art, peace, and law; it does not establish domination.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in specificity rather than contradicting one another. ABC's compressed legal-power wording is retained without further interpretation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies Venus/Jupiter through a powerful desire for happiness and an expansive optimism. [Witte](#witte-entry) makes that longing the center. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives it concrete expressions in financial wishes, artistic success, money's harmonizing influence, and powerful happiness in love. The shared point is the strength of the desire or influence, rather than a guarantee that every wish succeeds.
