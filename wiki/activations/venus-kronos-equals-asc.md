## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Acquaintances with charitable people. To receive presents.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Kronos`
- Source reference: printed page `288`; PDF page `143` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Special harmony with others, dominance of the partner through beauty, harmony with independent partners, artistic authority at the place, a place for special art, a state partner for peace, preference for respected partners, and the authority's wishes concerning the environment.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Encounters with charitable people and receiving presents ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: A preference for respected partners is stated ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- body/health: No bodily or health manifestation is given.
- social/relationship: Charitable acquaintances and harmony with others ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- events/manifestations: Receiving presents ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- conflicts/notes: ABC's authority, partner, and locality examples extend beyond Witte's charitable encounters.

## Contradictions

The entries do not directly conflict. ABC's independent partners, artistic setting, and public authority are distinct examples rather than equivalents of receiving presents.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies Venus/Kronos through encounters shaped by kindness, standing, or authority. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives charitable acquaintances and presents; [ABC](#abc-entry) adds harmony with independent or respected partners, artistic authority in a place, and a state partner for peace. The common field is contact with people or settings that confer favor or recognized standing, while ABC also retains dominance through beauty and an authority's environmental wishes.
