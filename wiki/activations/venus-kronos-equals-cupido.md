## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

possess the great talent to give someone joy. To endow arts. Patron of arts. Clubs or associations favored by the state.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Special harmony of a community, dominance of a art community, common love for the state, great preference for the whole, Hierarchic in a peace community, and harmony in a state community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Artistic patronage and favored associations ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: A capacity to give joy and a preference for the whole ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- body/health: No bodily or health assertion appears.
- social/relationship: Community harmony and associations receiving support ([ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry)).
- events/manifestations: Endowment of the arts and state favor toward clubs ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- conflicts/notes: The community's hierarchy or dominance is ABC's additional emphasis.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis between patronage and community structure; neither establishes an unconditional outcome or independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Cupido specifies Venus/Kronos through the support and harmony of groups, especially where art and public patronage meet. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes endowing the arts, patrons, and associations favored by the state, alongside a talent for giving joy. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places harmony and a concern for the whole within communities and state structures. This is a collective setting for favor and artistic support, not a claim that every group is harmonious.
