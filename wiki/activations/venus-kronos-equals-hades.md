## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sympathy with poverty, with the sick and suffering.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Kronos`
- Source reference: printed page `289`; PDF page `143` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Special sorrow in love, danger to peace for the state, lack of harmony in old age, artistic greatness from the past, poor reputation of art, loving a hidden authority, preference for great danger, and important service in love.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sympathy toward poverty and suffering ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: Compassion is explicit; other internal motives are not developed ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- body/health: Sick people are objects of sympathy in Witte, not a health prediction.
- social/relationship: Concern for people in need; ABC also mentions deficient harmony in old age.
- events/manifestations: Charitable concern is stated without a specific event in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: Compassion in Witte coexists with ABC's sorrow, danger, and impaired harmony; these are distinct source emphases.

## Contradictions

Witte describes sympathy toward people in need; ABC includes sorrow and danger alongside past artistic greatness and a hidden authority. Compassion does not remove those adverse possibilities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies Venus/Kronos through benevolence directed toward suffering or through difficulty affecting love and standing. [Witte](#witte-entry) names sympathy for poverty, sickness, and suffering. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds sorrow in love, deficient harmony in old age, danger to state peace, and poor reputation of art, while retaining past artistic greatness and love for a hidden authority. This preserves both compassionate response and adverse circumstances without turning sickness into a diagnosis.
