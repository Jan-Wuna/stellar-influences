## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Gifts through charitable persons. Endowments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Kronos`
- Source reference: printed page `288`; PDF page `143` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Special joy in love, successful artistic authority, the state's success in peace, a peaceful constitutional state, great legal skill, love of a legal authority, the state's love of law, independent legal success, and a priority love of money.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Gifts and endowments through charitable people ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: Joy in love and a preference for money ([ABC](#abc-entry)).
- body/health: No bodily or health manifestation is stated.
- social/relationship: Benefactors supply gifts or support ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- events/manifestations: Gifts, endowments, and artistic success ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- conflicts/notes: ABC adds specific legal, artistic-authority, state-peace, and financial examples; Witte's endowments do not guarantee legal success.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is shown. Charitable gifts and success in law or state peace describe different forms of benefit.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies Venus/Kronos through favor that yields a benefit or successful standing. [Witte](#witte-entry) names gifts and endowments from charitable people. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds joy in love, artistic authority, success in peace, and legal skill or independent legal success, alongside a priority love of money. These are particular routes to benefit, rather than a promise that every charitable encounter brings official success.
