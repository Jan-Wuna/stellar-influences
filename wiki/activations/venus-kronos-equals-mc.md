## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Take part in charitable activities. Charitably disposed. Receive benefits.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Kronos`
- Source reference: printed page `288`; PDF page `143` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Dominance of inner peace, great harmony of the soul, harmony is important to me, I love independence, my special preference, my artistic reputation, my love for hierarchy, my wishes to the state, and priority for my art.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal participation in charity and receiving benefits ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: Charitable disposition and the importance of harmony ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- body/health: No bodily or health manifestation appears.
- social/relationship: Taking part in benevolent activity and receiving help ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- events/manifestations: Charitable activities and artistic reputation ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- conflicts/notes: ABC's first-person clauses specify inner peace, independence, artistic standing, and wishes addressed to the state.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Personal charity, inner harmony, and artistic independence are related possibilities with different objects.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

MC specifies Venus/Kronos through one's own investment in benevolence, harmony, or recognized standing. [Witte](#witte-entry) names charitable disposition and participation, including receiving benefits. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds inner peace, love of independence, artistic reputation and priority for one's art, alongside affinity with hierarchy and wishes addressed to the state. These locate the concern in personal values and participation, without making charity and official recognition identical.
