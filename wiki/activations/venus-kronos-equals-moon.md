## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sacrificing, benevolent woman. Philanthropists (female).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Kronos`
- Source reference: printed page `288`; PDF page `143` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

A woman's respected art, special love for a woman, dominance of a beautiful woman, a dominant feeling of harmony, a woman's great love, great folk art, state peace for a people, priority feelings of love, and an important hour of harmony.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Benevolence and philanthropy in Witte's female examples ([Witte](#witte-entry)).
- psychology: Self-sacrifice and strongly felt harmony ([Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry)).
- body/health: No bodily or health manifestation is given.
- social/relationship: Affection involving a woman and female philanthropy are source-specific examples.
- events/manifestations: Philanthropic activity is named; ABC also refers to a harmonious hour.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered examples are retained as historical source claims.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Witte's female philanthropy and ABC's female, artistic, and public-peace examples are distinct expressions of benevolence or valued harmony.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Moon specifies Venus/Kronos through strongly valued harmony and benevolence, expressed partly in the sources' female examples. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes a self-sacrificing woman or female philanthropist. [ABC](#abc-entry) adds a woman's respected art and love, a beautiful woman's dominance, folk art, and state peace for a people. The gendered framing remains historical source language, while the artistic and public examples broaden the field beyond private affection.
