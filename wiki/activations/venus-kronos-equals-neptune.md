- Source heading: `Venus + Kronos`

#### Witte Entry

Predestined nurse. To be sympathetic. Not kept a promise of help.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Kronos`
- Source reference: printed page `289`; PDF page `143` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a great love, special sensitivity in love, great disappointment in love, great metaphysical art, great preference for water, uncertain state peace, denying the priority of the female principle, and a future peace state.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sympathetic care and great sensitivity, with uncertain help or disappointed love.
- Psychology: Sympathy and sensitivity in love; disappointment can accompany the dissolution of a great love.
- Body/health: Nursing is Witte's historical vocational example; no illness is diagnosed.
- Social/relationship: Promises of help may fail to be kept, and great love may dissolve.
- Events/manifestations: Metaphysical art, preference for water, uncertain state peace, or the idea of a future peace state.
- Conflicts/notes: The ABC's denial of priority to the female principle is historical gendered doctrine; sympathy and failed help remain distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

- The ABC's denial of priority to the female principle is historical gendered doctrine; sympathy and failed help remain distinct possibilities.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Kronos = Neptune` gives benevolence a sympathetic, sensitive expression while leaving the fulfillment of help uncertain. [Witte](#witte-entry) names sympathy, nursing, and a promise of assistance that is not kept. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) carries the uncertainty into great love through disappointment or dissolution, alongside special sensitivity and metaphysical art. Its public range includes insecure state peace and a future peace state. These combine care and aspiration with the possibility that affection or promised support does not materialize; the gendered clause about the female principle remains the source's historical framing.
