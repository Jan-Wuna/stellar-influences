- Source heading: `Venus + Kronos`

#### Witte Entry

Connections with a benefactor or a sponsor.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Kronos`
- Source reference: printed page `288`; PDF page `143` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

An especially harmonious connection, a dominant love connection, peace connections of the state, united in great love, connections of the state with art, connection with great art, and preference for important connections.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Support and significant affection through connections with benefactors, sponsors, or institutions.
- Psychology: Preference for important connections and especially harmonious bonds.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- Social/relationship: A benefactor or sponsor; being united in great love.
- Events/manifestations: State connections concerning peace or art, and connection with great art.
- Conflicts/notes: Sponsorship and important connections are explicit, but influence alone does not establish benevolent conduct.

## Contradictions

- Sponsorship and important connections are explicit, but influence alone does not establish benevolent conduct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Kronos = Node` locates benevolence in a relationship: [Witte](#witte-entry) names a benefactor or sponsor who provides support. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds especially harmonious bonds, union in great love, and preference for important connections. It also extends the setting to the state's links with peace and art, and connection with great art itself. Personal support, significant affection, and institutional or cultural links therefore belong to the range, without assuming that every influential connection provides help.
