## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Developing benevolence, affection, artistic authority, or public harmony.
- Psychology: A growing preference for hierarchy is named by the ABC; no further inner mechanism is stated.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- Social/relationship: Increasing goodwill from a boss, chief, government, or superior; development of a great love.
- Events/manifestations: Harmonious and peaceful development, artistic authority unfolding, or independent art taking shape.
- Conflicts/notes: The ABC's priority development of the female principle retains historical gendered framing.

## Contradictions

- The ABC's priority development of the female principle retains historical gendered framing.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Kronos = Pluto` describes benevolence or affection that grows and takes shape. [Witte](#witte-entry) places this in increasing goodwill from a boss, government, or superior, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also names the development of great love and especially harmonious development. Artistic authority, independent art, and peaceful development in the state broaden the setting beyond personal patronage. Developing attachment to hierarchy is another explicit possibility; it does not establish that authority will invariably be generous. The source's gendered developmental clause remains attributed to its historical framework.
