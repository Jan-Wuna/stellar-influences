### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Kronos`
- Source reference: printed page `289`; PDF page `143` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Losses of reputation through love, respected beauty in old age, special harmony in old age, particular pain in love, disturbances of peace in the state, great peace problems, great loss for art, state peace tasks, and losses of sympathy for the state.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Withheld support, loss of esteem, or difficult peace efforts, alongside harmony in old age.
- Psychology: Pain in love is explicit; the entries supply no developed psychological mechanism.
- Body/health: Old age is a setting for respected beauty and harmony, not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Refusal of endowments or scholarships; losses of reputation or sympathy.
- Events/manifestations: Disturbances and tasks concerning state peace, or a great loss for art.
- Conflicts/notes: Old-age harmony and respected beauty remain favorable possibilities beside refusal, pain, and public difficulties.

## Contradictions

- Old-age harmony and respected beauty remain favorable possibilities beside refusal, pain, and public difficulties.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Kronos = Saturn` can express benevolence as support that is refused: [Witte](#witte-entry) names endowments and scholarships. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds pain in love, losses of reputation or sympathy, disturbances of state peace, and loss for art. Its respected beauty and special harmony in old age preserve a favorable range alongside those difficulties. Withholding, loss, and later harmony are separate possibilities, so none should be made an inevitable result.
