## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden favor or an important event in affection, art, or public harmony.
- Psychology: Dominant tension in love and abrupt great affection are named without a further psychological mechanism.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- Social/relationship: Sudden great love or an important love event; unexpected generosity.
- Events/manifestations: Large presents, gifts, or legacies; artistic events and impulses from state or hierarchy.
- Conflicts/notes: Gifts and legacies are examples; the ABC also retains tension in love and dynamic artistic authority.

## Contradictions

- Gifts and legacies are examples; the ABC also retains tension in love and dynamic artistic authority.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Kronos = Uranus` brings benevolence or affection into a sudden event. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes unexpected gifts, large presents, and legacies. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds sudden great love and important events in love or art, with dynamic artistic authority and impulses toward state peace or harmony. Tension in love remains part of that account. The distinctive thread is abrupt favor or emotional significance, while the entries leave the giver and the particular outcome open.
