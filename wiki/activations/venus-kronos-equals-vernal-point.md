## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Benevolence, beauty, and cultural esteem expressed publicly.
- Psychology: A strong preference for public life is explicit; no detailed inner psychology is stated.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- Social/relationship: General charity, public harmony, and state goodwill in public.
- Events/manifestations: Public standing of art, artistic authority, and prominence of beauty.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries describe public expressions, without establishing how a particular public responds.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Kronos = Vernal Point` brings benevolence and cultural standing into public life. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives the compact meaning of general charitable activity. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds public harmony, state goodwill, esteem for art, and artistic authority, together with a strong preference for public life and the prominence of beauty. Visible charity and cultural distinction are thus parallel expressions of this orientation; public visibility alone does not establish a particular reception.
