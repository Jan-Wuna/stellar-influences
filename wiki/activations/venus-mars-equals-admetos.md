## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: PDF page `176` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To refuse, to reject, to suppress or mortify the sexual urge. Small circle of relatives. A few intimate friends.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: printed page `271`; PDF page `134` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Enduring preference for work, blocked peace work, restricted will for harmony, restriction of acts of love, consolidation of peace activity, thorough artistic activity, suppressed impulses of love, and deepening of peace work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Restricted sexual expression and close ties within a small circle.
- Psychology: Refusal or suppression of sexual urges; enduring preference for work.
- Body/health: Sexual restraint is described; neither entry establishes a clinical condition.
- Social/relationship: Few intimate friends or relatives and restricted acts of love.
- Events/manifestations: Blocked or consolidated peace work, deepened efforts, and thorough artistic activity.
- Conflicts/notes: Restriction and concentration are distinct possibilities; suppressed impulses do not establish a disorder.

## Contradictions

- Restriction and concentration are distinct possibilities; suppressed impulses do not establish a disorder.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Mars = Admetos` narrows the field of intimate expression or concentrates devoted activity. [Witte](#witte-entry) names refusal or suppression of the sexual urge and a small circle of relatives and intimate friends. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) carries restriction into acts of love and peace work, while also allowing peace activity to consolidate or deepen and artistic activity to become thorough. Enduring commitment to work and blocked expression therefore remain separate possibilities within this orientation.
