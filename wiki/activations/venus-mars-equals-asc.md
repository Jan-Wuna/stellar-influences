## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Personal sexual intercourse. Acquaintance with relatives or intimate friends.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work joy with a partner, happy creation in the place, peace will of the partner, harmonious environmental activity, artistic activity in the place, love actions with the partner, partnership peace work, and gentle work place

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A lively expression of feelings, an affectionate nature. The revelation 31 Venus/Mars = Sun is often operative as a direction in connection with birth. of love, a physical union.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A person with a lot of verve. One who is attractive and/or seductive. Being efficient gives one a sense of identity. A person who plays the role of a facilitator, or of one who has the capacity to get things done. One projects a strong sexuality.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `188`

#### Munkasey Entry

Focusing on activities which bring relaxation and pleasure; any awareness of your effects on others and how you use this to heighten pleasure in people; disputes about pretenses which bring enjoyment to you.
