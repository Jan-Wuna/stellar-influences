## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: PDF page `176` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Marital sex intercourse. Sexual intercourse leading to marriage. Social relations with relatives or intimate friends.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: printed page `271`; PDF page `134` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

A community's joy in work, a peaceful working community, shared artistic activity, a shared will for love, harmonious community work, harmonious creation in the community, and synthesis between the female and male principles.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Intimate and productive activity within marriage, family, or a community.
- Psychology: Shared intent in love and enjoyment of communal work.
- Body/health: Intercourse is described without a health condition.
- Social/relationship: Marriage, relatives, intimate friends, and a cooperative working group.
- Events/manifestations: Marriage-related intercourse and harmonious shared artistic creation.
- Conflicts/notes: The synthesis of female and male principles is the ABC's historical framing.

## Contradictions

- The synthesis of female and male principles is the ABC's historical framing.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Mars = Cupido` places intimate and creative activity within a partnership or group. [Witte](#witte-entry) names marital intercourse, intercourse leading to marriage, and social relations with relatives or intimate friends. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds shared artistic activity, pleasure in work, and harmonious creation in a community. The common thread is affection or productive action carried by an intimate or cooperative unit; the source's female-and-male principle language remains its own historical framing.
