## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: PDF page `176` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Illicit intercourse. Misfortune and unpleasantness with or through intimate acquaintances or relatives. Intemperance, gluttony.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: printed page `271`; PDF page `134` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Dangerous activity in love, endangered efforts for peace, artistic activity from the past, deprivation of impulses of love, criminal work by a community, lack of will for love, disadvantages through activity in love, and deficient will for harmony.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Difficult intimate activity, deprived affection, or adverse close relationships.
- Psychology: Intemperance and a deficient will for love or harmony are source themes.
- Body/health: Witte names gluttony; no clinical diagnosis is established.
- Social/relationship: Unpleasantness through intimate acquaintances or relatives and dangerous love activity.
- Events/manifestations: Endangered peace efforts, artistic activity from the past, and criminal community work in the ABC.
- Conflicts/notes: Historical moral and criminal terms remain attributed source language, without assigning guilt or immorality.

## Contradictions

- Historical moral and criminal terms remain attributed source language, without assigning guilt or immorality.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Mars = Hades` describes difficulty around intimate activity and close relationships. [Witte](#witte-entry) names unpleasantness through relatives or intimate acquaintances, intemperance, and gluttony, while using the historical moral label 'illicit' for intercourse. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds endangered peace efforts, deprivation of loving impulses, and deficient willingness for love or harmony. Artistic activity from the past supplies a separate expression within this adverse range. Its criminal community-work clause remains a source assertion, without establishing anyone's guilt or character.
