- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`

Woman dominated by love. Relationships or close intimate acquaintance with ruling persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: printed page `271`; PDF page `134` (one-based).

Respected artistic activity, a special will for harmony, special artistic activity, efforts for peace by the state, the boss's will for harmony, self-confident artistic work, state peace work, and preference for state work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Intimate or active relationships involving ruling people, artistic standing, or state work.
- Psychology: A special will for harmony and confidence in artistic work.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry specifies a bodily or health condition.
- Social/relationship: Close acquaintance with ruling people; a boss's will for harmony.
- Events/manifestations: Respected artistic activity, self-confident art, and state peace work.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's woman dominated by love is historical gendered language; it is separate from relations with authority.

## Contradictions

- Witte's woman dominated by love is historical gendered language; it is separate from relations with authority.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Mars = Kronos` places intimate or devoted activity in relation to authority and standing. [Witte](#witte-entry) names close acquaintance with ruling people, alongside the historically gendered image of a woman dominated by love. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds respected and self-confident artistic work, a boss's will for harmony, and state efforts for peace. The personal and public expressions share active affection or harmony under a theme of prominence, without establishing that someone must dominate or be dominated.
