## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: PDF page `176` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The end of intimate relations or the relation to relatives. Sexual intercourse connected with infection. Denial of sexual intercourse.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: printed page `271`; PDF page `134` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Expecting joy in work, preferring work in the air, joy in working with water, peace work for the future, harmonious activity in water, love of metaphysical work, weak activity in love, and refinement of activity in love.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Venus/Mars`
- Source reference: printed page `176`, coded entry `0585`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Pathological or abnormal craving in sex relationship, negation of the love urge, inclination to perversity. Disappointment in sex relationships. Appendicitis.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Venus/Mars midpoint`
- Source reference: PDF page `55` (one-based; exact activation entry).

#### Sandbach Entry

Subtlety and refinement in the expression of passion. An imaginative, gothic, or romantic love nature. Lack of efficiency due to confusion, or, when one develops one’s psychic sense, the ability to enlist one’s intuition in the successful accomplish ment of tasks.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `188`

#### Munkasey Entry

Added need for escape during passionate moments; the use of drugs to heighten sexual response; weakness shown in a beauty competition; allows subtle or more inner delights from activities which also bring pleasure.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Refined or imagined passion, with confusion, disappointed intimacy, or interrupted contact.
- Psychology: Romantic imagination and subtlety; confusion or escapism can reduce effectiveness.
- Body/health: Witte's infection and Ebertin's appendicitis are historical medical assertions; Munkasey's drug-use clause is descriptive source material.
- Social/relationship: Intimate relations may end or be denied; passion may also be subtly expressed.
- Events/manifestations: Intuitive accomplishment, metaphysical work, or activity involving water or air in the ABC.
- Conflicts/notes: Constructive imagination coexists with strongly adverse historical descriptions; pathologizing sexual vocabulary does not establish a diagnosis.

## Contradictions

- Constructive imagination coexists with strongly adverse historical descriptions; pathologizing sexual vocabulary does not establish a diagnosis.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Mars = Neptune` makes passion subtle or imaginative while leaving its expression and practical direction uncertain. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) allows refined romance and intuitive accomplishment, alongside confusion that impairs efficiency. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds metaphysical work, weak or refined activity in love, and work involving water or air. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) emphasize denied or ended intimacy and disappointment more strongly. Their infection, appendicitis, and pathologizing sexual claims remain historical assertions; [Munkasey's](#munkasey-entry) escape and drug-use clauses likewise describe his source range, not a recommended practice. The constructive subtlety and adverse loss of direction should both remain visible.
