## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: PDF page `176` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

The connection with blood relatives or intimate friends. Sexual connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: printed page `270`; PDF page `134` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Working pleasure in a connection, creating peaceful connections, activating harmony in a relationship, wanting harmonious connections, harmonious actions in connections, and connection between the female and the manly principle

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Venus/Mars`
- Source reference: printed page `176`, coded entry `0587`.

#### Ebertin Entry

An extremely magnetic personality with regard to sexual attraction. Sex unions.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Venus/Mars midpoint`
- Source reference: PDF page `56` (one-based; exact activation entry).

#### Sandbach Entry

The desire to connect with people who will help one get things done, and/ or to bring efficiency to groups. One needs to direct one’s ability to be efficient toward spiri tually evolving rather than only maintaining the status quo.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `188`

#### Munkasey Entry

Meeting people who bring pleasure and enjoyment to your activities; associations with others who enjoy similar forms of music or performing arts; a feeling that it is your destiny to enjoy reckless activities.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Connections formed through intimacy, shared pleasure, or effective joint action.
- Psychology: Desire for useful cooperation and enjoyment of shared activities.
- Body/health: Sexual attraction and unions are explicit; no medical outcome is stated.
- Social/relationship: Blood relatives, intimate friends, sexual connections, or companions with similar musical and performing interests.
- Events/manifestations: Bringing efficiency to groups and forming peaceful or harmonious connections.
- Conflicts/notes: Sandbach distinguishes spiritual development from merely maintaining the status quo; Munkasey's destiny language is an attributed feeling.

## Contradictions

- Sandbach distinguishes spiritual development from merely maintaining the status quo; Munkasey's destiny language is an attributed feeling.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Mars = Node` places affection and activity in a connection with other people. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) emphasize relatives, intimate friends, sexual attraction, and unions. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the relation to peaceful connections and harmonious joint action, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) seeks people who help accomplish things and bring efficiency to groups. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds companionship through pleasure, music, and performing arts, as well as a felt destiny toward reckless enjoyment. Shared activity can thus connect people in several ways; Sandbach's spiritual-development aim and Munkasey's risk-related feeling remain distinct emphases.
