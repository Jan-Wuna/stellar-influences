## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The connection with blood relatives or intimate friends. Sexual connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Working pleasure in a connection, creating peaceful connections, activating harmony in a relationship, wanting harmonious connections, harmonious actions in connections, and connection between the female and the manly principle

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

An extremely magnetic personality with regard to sexual attraction. Sex unions.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The desire to connect with people who will help one get things done, and/ or to bring efficiency to groups. One needs to direct one’s ability to be efficient toward spiri tually evolving rather than only maintaining the status quo.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `188`

#### Munkasey Entry

Meeting people who bring pleasure and enjoyment to your activities; associations with others who enjoy similar forms of music or performing arts; a feeling that it is your destiny to enjoy reckless activities.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Mars = Node`: romantic opportunity, social magnetism, and the ability to build lively connection through mutual desire and shared action. Main caution: entangled chemistry: associations can become flirtatious, competitive, or too charged to remain simple and cooperative.
