## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: PDF page `176` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Platonic love. Devotion to mental matters. Spiritualized relatives or friends.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: printed page `271`; PDF page `134` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Work for peace and culture, effort toward love of truth, harmony and clarity at work, joy through intellectual work, harmonious intellectual work, artistic and cultural activity, beauty of intellectual work, and spiritual peace work.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Devoted activity directed toward truth, culture, peace, or spiritual concerns.
- Psychology: Love of truth, intellectual clarity, and joy in spiritual or intellectual work.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Platonic love and spiritually oriented relatives or friends.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic and cultural activity, intellectual work, and spiritual peace work.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries emphasize nonsexual affection and mental devotion without denying other orientations' separate meanings.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Mars = Poseidon` directs affection and devoted action toward mental or spiritual aims. [Witte](#witte-entry) names platonic love, devotion to mental matters, and spiritually oriented relatives or friends. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives that devotion an active range in effort toward truth, intellectual clarity, art, culture, and work for peace. Joy in thoughtful or spiritual work connects the inner ideal with what is done, while the activation entries supply no separate medical claim.
