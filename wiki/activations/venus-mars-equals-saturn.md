## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Venereal diseases. Severance of the sexual intercourse. Older relatives or close friends. Separation from them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Activate old love, peaceful patient creation, urban work peace, harmony through concentrated work, harmonious activity in old age, artistic work tasks, concentration on artificial work, problematic peace work, and loss of work pleasure

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inhibitions in love life, inclination to adultery, abnormal and pathological sex-expression. A separation in love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Efficiency carried out slowly and carefully or gradually. To feel a lack of romance in one’s life. To desire serious romance. A great ability to focus and to shut others things out when accomplishing something. A feeling that one is unattractive.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/MARS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `188`

#### Munkasey Entry

Inhibits any direct pleasure or enjoyment derived from the opposite sex; the respect or admiration you gain from exercise; a conservative side to your displays of affection or admiration; a diminished attraction for competing.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Mars = Saturn`: committed love, controlled creative drive, and the ability to make desire endure rather than burn out quickly. Main caution: inhibited heat: passion can become resentful, blocked, or too burdened by caution and duty to remain spontaneous.
