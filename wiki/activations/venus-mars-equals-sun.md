## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sexual love of the body. Determination to marriage. Procreation. To be bodily together with intimate friends or blood relatives. Male relatives.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work in the arts centre, harmonious work of the man, corporal love acts, activate body love, artistic activity of the man, personal peace work, personal love acts, and human preference for work

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

A strongly physical love, artistic power of creation. The urge to establish an union between the sexes, marriage, procreation.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The potential of being a master of efficiency. Goals reached through gradual and daily accomplishment. One feels that romance is important and feels vitalized by it. One’s ego and self-esteem are dependent on one’s ability to get things accomplished.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Invigorates and enlivens you to derive pleasure from your activities; activates your need for approval after athletic activities; mastery of graceful movement and motion, and a tendency to be proud of activities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Mars = Sun`: radiant magnetism, romantic courage, and the ability to lead through warmth joined to initiative. Main caution: ego fused with desire: self-expression can become too self-centered, dramatic, or driven by conquest and validation.
