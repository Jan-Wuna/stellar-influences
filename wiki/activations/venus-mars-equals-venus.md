- Source heading: `VENUS/MARS With Itself`

#### Munkasey Entry

Deriving enjoyment from exercise or movement; luring or charming others as a form of sexual arousal; immediate attractiveness; motion or movement in art or music; the tranquility and inner peace which follows exertion.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Mars = Venus` finds enjoyment in movement and in the immediate appeal of another person. [Munkasey's repeated-pair entry](#munkasey-entry) connects exercise, motion in art or music, and sexual charm, then adds tranquility and inner peace after exertion. The activity can therefore be pleasurable in itself or prepare the way for calm. The source supplies these distinct bodily, artistic, and interpersonal expressions without specifying a medical result.
