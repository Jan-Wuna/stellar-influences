## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: PDF page `176` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

A free (liberal) concept (or idea) of sexual love. Intimate friends. Blood relatives.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: printed page `270`; PDF page `134` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

joy in work in public life, peace work in public, harmonious efforts in public, public artistic activity, public will for peace, and beautification work in public life

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Publicly expressed affection, harmonious action, and work for peace or beauty.
- Psychology: A liberal conception of sexual love is Witte's stated outlook.
- Body/health: Sexual love is mentioned without a bodily or health condition.
- Social/relationship: Intimate friends and blood relatives remain part of Witte's range.
- Events/manifestations: Public artistic activity, beautification, peaceful work, and public willingness for peace.
- Conflicts/notes: The public work themes coexist with Witte's intimate and kinship examples; neither replaces the other.

## Contradictions

- The public work themes coexist with Witte's intimate and kinship examples; neither replaces the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Mars = Vernal Point` gives affectionate or harmonious activity a public expression. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places joy in work, peace efforts, art, and beautification in public life. [Witte](#witte-entry) supplies a different but compatible range in a liberal conception of sexual love, intimate friends, and blood relatives. Public creative or peaceful activity and the outlook brought to intimate life remain distinct applications of this exact formula.
