## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`

#### Witte Entry

Passionate sexual love. Influential relatives or intimate friends.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: printed page `271`; PDF page `134` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Influence of joy in creating, influential artistic activity, peaceful working strength, harmonious energies in action, immense artistic work, powerful will for love, masculine strength in the woman, strong joy in work, and female influence in the man.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Powerful passion and influential or forceful devoted activity.
- Psychology: Strong will for love and joy in creating or working.
- Body/health: Passionate sexual love is described without a medical implication.
- Social/relationship: Influential relatives or intimate friends.
- Events/manifestations: Influential artistic activity, immense artistic work, peaceful working strength, and harmonious energies in action.
- Conflicts/notes: The ABC's masculine strength in women and female influence in men are historical gendered formulations.

## Contradictions

- The ABC's masculine strength in women and female influence in men are historical gendered formulations.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Mars = Vulcanus` gives passion and devoted activity strength or influence. [Witte](#witte-entry) joins passionate sexual love to influential relatives and intimate friends. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) carries that force into artistic activity, joy in creating, peaceful work, and harmonious energies put into action. Its masculine and feminine formulations retain the source's historical gendered language. Powerful affection, influential ties, and substantial creative work belong to the range without fixing how that influence will be used.
