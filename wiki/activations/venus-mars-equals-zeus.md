## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: PDF page `176` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Procreative coition in marital life. Intimate acquaintance or relationship with leading people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Mars`
- Source reference: printed page `271`; PDF page `134` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Strenuous efforts for peace, peaceful work goals, wanting achievements in peace, harmonious leadership work, artistic work achievements, creative joy in work, creative artistic activity, and will for procreation in love.

- Core meaning: Purposeful intimate or creative activity directed toward achievement.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Mars = Zeus` directs affectionate or creative action toward a result. [Witte](#witte-entry) names procreative marital intercourse and intimate relationships with leading people. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends purposeful activity to peaceful work goals, harmonious leadership, artistic achievement, and creative joy in work. Its will for procreation belongs beside these other acts of creation as an attributed historical possibility. The common thread is devoted effort given direction, without a specific reproductive or public achievement being determined.
