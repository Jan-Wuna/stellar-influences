## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: PDF page `63` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To seek friendship. Peaceful inclinations. To be inclined toward art and science. Longing for religion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: printed page `71`; PDF page `34` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of inner peace, the abundance of my wishes, I love freedom, my successes in art, my experiences in love, my unlimited love, my preference for knowledge, and increasing peace of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Expanding inner peace and affection through friendship, knowledge, art, or religion.
- Psychology: Peaceful inclinations, love of freedom, abundant wishes, and interest in knowledge.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- Social/relationship: Seeking friendship and experiencing broad or unlimited affection.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic success, scientific interests, religious longing, and increasing inner peace.
- Conflicts/notes: The abundance of wishes does not itself establish fulfillment.

## Contradictions

- The abundance of wishes does not itself establish fulfillment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/MC = Apollon` opens personal affection and inner peace toward a wider field of interests and relationships. [Witte](#witte-entry) joins seeking friendship to art, science, and longing for religion. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds freedom, knowledge, experiences in love, and artistic success, with abundant wishes and increasing peace of the soul. The shared movement is toward broader connection and understanding, while the scope of desire remains distinct from its fulfillment.
