#### Sandbach Entry

Your vibration helps others to experience the most exalted and spiritual aspect of the beauty of everything around them. You also have the ability to help them appre ciate the foundation of their life, that is, all that creates security for them, as well as to recognize and be at peace with whatever their heart’s desire is.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/MC = Chiron` describes helping others perceive beauty as spiritually meaningful and appreciate what gives their lives security. [Sandbach's entry](#sandbach-entry) connects that appreciation with recognizing the heart's desire and being at peace with it. The emphasis is on helping someone value both the surroundings and foundations of life. Its language of vibration and spiritual beauty remains a single-source interpretation, with no separate medical claim.
