### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: printed page `71`; PDF page `34` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

A soulful art community, a peaceful community of souls, a harmonious community of souls, I love synthesis, inner peace in the community, my harmonious community, my art for the community, and my community of love.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal affection and inner harmony experienced within a community or union.
- Psychology: Love of synthesis and a harmonious community of souls.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- Social/relationship: Betrothal, inclination toward marriage, social life, and a community of love.
- Events/manifestations: Art made for a community and inner peace in shared life.
- Conflicts/notes: Marriage is one expression; the ABC also explicitly includes artistic and soulful community.

## Contradictions

- Marriage is one expression; the ABC also explicitly includes artistic and soulful community.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/MC = Cupido` gives personal affection a shared home in a union or community. [Witte](#witte-entry) names betrothal, inclination toward marriage, and social life. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens this belonging through a peaceful or harmonious community of souls, shared inner peace, and art offered to the community. Emotional union and artistic contribution are therefore related ways of finding harmony with others, without reducing every communal expression to marriage.
