## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: PDF page `63` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Preference for base love. Attracted to base love or immorality.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: printed page `71`; PDF page `34` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Danger to my harmony, endangered peace of the soul, deficient peace of the soul, my lack of love, my deficient harmony, hiding my love, my past love, my renunciation of love, and my preference for criminality.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Deficient, endangered, hidden, or renounced affection and inner harmony.
- Psychology: Lack of love or peace and concealment of affection.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Past love, renunciation, and difficulty in personal harmony.
- Events/manifestations: Hiding love or experiencing its absence; the ABC includes preference for criminality.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's moral labels and the ABC's criminal clause are historical assertions, not judgments of a person.

## Contradictions

- Witte's moral labels and the ABC's criminal clause are historical assertions, not judgments of a person.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/MC = Hades` describes personal affection under conditions of lack, danger, concealment, or renunciation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) holds hidden or past love beside deficient inner peace and harmony. [Witte](#witte-entry) uses the historical moral language of 'base love' and 'immorality,' while the ABC also includes criminality. Those severe labels remain attributed source claims; they do not establish conduct or character. The more broadly supported theme is affection or inner harmony that is troubled, unavailable, or kept out of view.
