## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The sensation of happiness of love. Lucky wishes. Fulfilment of wishes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Joy over the beauty of a soul, money for my kiss, happiness through soul harmony, I love success, my joy of peace, my happy wife, my pleasure success, my love happiness, my legal advantages, my fondness for right, and my fondness for money

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Rich expression of feeling, desire to give a lot of love, healthy and confident attitude with regard to love life, ability to easily gain the affection of others. A popular and happy person.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To be eager to be a channel of God’s love. An enthusiastic appreciation of that which one feels to be of the highest value. To be enthusiastic about giving something of value to the world. To grow in terms of your appreciation of what you have to give to the world.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `220`

#### Munkasey Entry

Added enjoyment received from travel or religious experiences; increased understanding of the loving intentions received for your efforts; expanded appreciation for beauty or love in life; adding love to one’s philosophy.
