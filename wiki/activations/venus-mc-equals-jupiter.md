## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `MC + Venus`
- Source page: `63`
#### Witte Entry

The sensation of happiness of love. Lucky wishes. Fulfilment of wishes.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Venus/Medium Coeli`
- Source page: `199`
- Entry: `0679`

#### Ebertin Entry

Rich expression of feeling, desire to give a lot of love, healthy and confident attitude with regard to love life, ability to easily gain the affection of others. A popular and happy person.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Venus/Midheaven midpoint`
- Source page: `66`
- Activator: `Jupiter`

#### Sandbach Entry

To be eager to be a channel of God’s love. An enthusiastic appreciation of that which one feels to be of the highest value. To be enthusiastic about giving something of value to the world. To grow in terms of your appreciation of what you have to give to the world.
