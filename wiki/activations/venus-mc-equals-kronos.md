## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: PDF page `63` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

An idealistic point of view concerning love. One’s love for father, for superiors or for persons of importance. Extraordinary attitude to love, peace or harmony.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: printed page `71`; PDF page `34` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Special harmony of the soul, harmony is important to me, greatness and beauty of a soul, my esteem in art, my goodwill toward the state, my harmony with the boss, my artistic independence, and self-confidence through inner peace.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: An elevated ideal of love, artistic standing, and harmony with authority.
- Psychology: Importance placed on harmony and self-confidence through inner peace.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or health claim.
- Social/relationship: Love for father, superiors, or important people; harmony with a boss.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic independence, esteem in art, and goodwill toward the state.
- Conflicts/notes: The sources' idealistic outlook does not establish anyone's moral superiority.

## Contradictions

- The sources' idealistic outlook does not establish anyone's moral superiority.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/MC = Kronos` gives personal love or harmony an elevated ideal and a relation to authority. [Witte](#witte-entry) names affection for a father, superiors, or important people, together with an extraordinary attitude toward love and peace. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds artistic standing and independence, harmony with a boss, and self-confidence through inner peace. Distinction can thus concern the quality one seeks in affection, the people admired, or the place of art, while the ideal does not itself establish conduct.
