## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: PDF page `63` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Preference for females persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: printed page `70`; PDF page `34` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Soulful art of the woman, my wife's joy, the art of my people, my wife's love, I feel love, I love my wife, my peaceful wife, my folk art, my preference for the people, my feeling for harmony, and my wife's wishes.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Venus/MC`
- Source reference: printed page `199`, coded entry `0676`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Deep feelings of love, a warm-hearted and cordial disposition, a motherly nature, typically female qualities. The deep attachment of a wife or of a mother.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Venus/Midheaven midpoint`
- Source reference: PDF page `66` (one-based; exact activation entry).

#### Sandbach Entry

The emotional need to have a parent figure who values one, or to play the role of a parent who teaches the appreciation of life and values.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `220`

#### Munkasey Entry

Separating nurturing needs from love or appreciation needs; becoming a more complete person as you grow from your family origins to your present position in life; coping with changes in the respect received from others.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Warm attachment and the need to value, nurture, or be valued.
- Psychology: Distinguishing nurturing needs from appreciation and learning from family origins.
- Body/health: Maternal and female qualities are historical relational language, not a medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Affection toward women or a wife, motherly attachment, and valuing parental figures.
- Events/manifestations: Teaching appreciation of life and adjusting to changes in respect received.
- Conflicts/notes: Historical gendered examples coexist with broader needs for nurturing, love, and appreciation.

## Contradictions

- Historical gendered examples coexist with broader needs for nurturing, love, and appreciation.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/MC = Moon` locates personal affection in warmth, care, and the wish to be valued. [Witte](#witte-entry), the [ABC entry](#abc-entry), and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) use historical images of women, wives, or motherly attachment, with art and harmony also explicit. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes needing a valuing parent figure or becoming a parent who teaches appreciation of life. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) distinguishes nurturing needs from love and appreciation, and follows development from family origins into present life. These sources support caring attachment without making their gendered examples universal.
