## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Giving affection to another. Receptive to establishing relations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

I love relationships, my love connection, harmonious soul connection, I love connections, my harmonious connections, my connections to the artificial, soul harmony in a relationship, and encounters of the soul love

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

affectionate attitude toward other people. Deep bonds of love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To seek out groups and individuals that will help one open up to love and grow in one’s ability to give and receive it. In this lifetime one is learning how to appreciate spir ituality more.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Association with others in life who are able to teach you about love and the different types of beauty you encounter; familiarity with notable persons in the arts; the connections you develop to famous people in music or the arts.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/MC = Node`: public popularity, relationship-based opportunity, and outer success that grows through supportive networks. Main caution: approval-driven direction: alliances can become too central to purpose, weakening independence or discernment.
