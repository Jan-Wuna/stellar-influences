## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: PDF page `63` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To revere and idolize something. Strong spiritual inclinations. Love which turns away from the worldly. Moment of calming of the ocean.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: printed page `71`; PDF page `34` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

My joy in insight, my love of the spiritual principle, my artistic ideas, I love truth, inner harmony and clarity, a harmonious spirit-soul, my true love, my art is a cultural asset, and true peace of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Reverence, spiritual affection, truth, and inner clarity.
- Psychology: Strong spiritual inclinations and joy in insight.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Love that turns away from worldly concerns or reveres an ideal.
- Events/manifestations: Art as a cultural asset and true inner peace; Witte also gives calming of the ocean.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's ocean image is a separate source manifestation; reverence does not establish the truth of an ideal.

## Contradictions

- Witte's ocean image is a separate source manifestation; reverence does not establish the truth of an ideal.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/MC = Poseidon` gives personal affection a reverent or spiritual direction. [Witte](#witte-entry) describes idolizing something and love turning away from worldly concerns. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) joins this orientation to insight, truth, clarity, and art as a cultural asset. The connected inner theme is affection guided by an ideal and a search for peace. Witte's moment of a calming ocean remains a separate concrete image, without becoming a prediction or replacing the spiritual range.
