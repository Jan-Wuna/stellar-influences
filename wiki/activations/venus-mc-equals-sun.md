## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: PDF page `63` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Self-love. Vanity. Being in harmony with one’s own body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: printed page `70`; PDF page `34` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Soulful art of a man, I love people, my artistic center, my love for the man, my personal art, my personal preference, my preference for a man, personal harmony of the soul, and a center for inner peace.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Venus/MC`
- Source reference: printed page `199`, coded entry `0675`.

#### Ebertin Entry

Self-love, vanity and conceit, desire to make oneself beautiful and to get or to catch the limelight, expectation of admiration from other people. The bliss of being loved by another person.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source heading: `Planets activating the Venus/Midheaven midpoint`
- Source reference: PDF page `66` (one-based; exact activation entry).

#### Sandbach Entry

To desire attention for one’s value, which could be anything from wanting others to admire one’s appearance to wanting others to appreciate what one does in one’s ca reer. To feel that it is important to play the role of loving parent to other people.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/MIDHEAVEN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `220`

#### Munkasey Entry

Approval received from others for appreciation given; an important and significant artistic or financial event; learning to integrate and appreciate the role that love can play in life; working to integrate love as a personal trait.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Self-love and the wish for appreciation, together with receiving or integrating love.
- Psychology: Vanity, admiration-seeking, pride in appearance, and valuing one's contributions.
- Body/health: Harmony with one's body and care for appearance are described without a medical claim.
- Social/relationship: The bliss of being loved, approval for appreciation given, and a loving parental role.
- Events/manifestations: Personal artistic work or an important artistic or financial event.
- Conflicts/notes: Admiration-seeking and the integration of love are distinct expressions; neither establishes a person's character.

## Contradictions

- Admiration-seeking and the integration of love are distinct expressions; neither establishes a person's character.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/MC = Sun` makes personal value and affection part of the sense of self. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) describe self-love, vanity, and the wish for admiration, while also allowing bodily harmony and the bliss of being loved. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes personal art and inner harmony. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) extends the wish to be valued from appearance to career contributions and a loving parental role. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds approval for appreciation given and learning to integrate love as a personal trait. Seeking admiration and becoming more able to give or receive affection remain separate parts of the range.
