## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: PDF page `63` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

To be popular. To show preferences in general. To send much love to the general public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: printed page `70`; PDF page `34` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Blissful harmony in public, my joy in public life, my preference for the public, I love public life, my joy in public life, my art in public, and my wishes for the public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal affection and enjoyment expressed toward public life.
- Psychology: Pleasure in public involvement and openly showing preferences.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Affection directed toward the public and Witte's popularity.
- Events/manifestations: Art in public, blissful public harmony, and wishes for the public.
- Conflicts/notes: Sending affection outward is distinct from receiving approval; popularity is a source possibility.

## Contradictions

- Sending affection outward is distinct from receiving approval; popularity is a source possibility.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/MC = Vernal Point` directs personal affection and preferences into public life. [Witte](#witte-entry) names popularity, showing preferences, and sending love to the public. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds enjoyment of public life, art in public, and wishes for the public's benefit. The orientation therefore includes outwardly shared affection and aesthetic expression, while giving that affection does not by itself settle how it will be received.
