## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: PDF page `63` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

One’s own love increases to passion. To personally desire something.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: printed page `71`; PDF page `34` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Blissful influence of art, harmonious influence of the soul, I am strong in love, my power in love, strength and beauty of a soul, powerful absorption in art, power of a peaceful soul, and harmonizing my energies.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal love strengthened into passion and artistic absorption.
- Psychology: Strong desire and powerful involvement in love or art.
- Body/health: The activation entries supply no specific bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Personal affection intensifies to passion.
- Events/manifestations: Harmonizing energies, the influence of art, and strength in a peaceful soul.
- Conflicts/notes: The strength of desire or influence does not establish its reception by another person.

## Contradictions

- The strength of desire or influence does not establish its reception by another person.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/MC = Vulcanus` strengthens personal affection into passion or intense desire. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives this direct emotional meaning. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens the strength into powerful absorption in art, harmonious influence, and the power of a peaceful soul. Personal feeling and artistic involvement share intensity, with harmonizing one's energies as another explicit possibility. The entries leave the use and reception of that strength open.
