## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: PDF page `63` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Wishing to be desired. The sower. To wish for progeny.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Meridian + Venus`
- Source reference: printed page `71`; PDF page `34` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Beginning harmony of the soul, I generate love, I love setting goals, inner peace through achievement, my fiery love, my harmonious leadership, my creative wife, my efforts in love, my creative art, and my joy in achievement.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Generating affection and directing creative aims toward achievement.
- Psychology: Wishing to be desired, liking goals, and finding joy in achievement.
- Body/health: Witte's wish for progeny is a desire, not a reproductive outcome.
- Social/relationship: Fiery love, efforts in love, and the source's creative-wife image.
- Events/manifestations: Creative art, harmonious leadership, and inner peace through accomplishment.
- Conflicts/notes: The reproductive and gendered examples remain attributed alongside broader artistic or purposeful creation.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/MC = Zeus` gives personal affection a creative or goal-directed form. [Witte](#witte-entry) names wishing to be desired and wishing for progeny. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also speaks of generating love, setting goals, harmonious leadership, and inner peace through achievement. Its creative art and joy in accomplishment extend the theme beyond the reproductive image. The connecting movement is a wish made active or productive, without determining whether a particular intimate or creative outcome follows.
