## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Neptune`
- Source reference: PDF page `180` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Completely cold in matters of love. Disharmony with a few.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Neptune`
- Source reference: printed page `279`; PDF page `138` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of a frozen love, depressing disappointment in love, deepening peace through sensitivity, thoroughgoing preference for metaphysics, circulatory weakness through love, suppression of metaphysical art, and resistance to preference for the subtle realm.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Cold or frozen affection, disappointment, and restricted response to subtle feeling.
- Psychology: Depressing disappointment and thorough involvement in metaphysics.
- Body/health: The ABC's circulatory weakness through love remains a historical medical assertion.
- Social/relationship: Coldness in love and disharmony with a few people.
- Events/manifestations: Dissolving frozen love, suppressing metaphysical art, or deepening peace through sensitivity.
- Conflicts/notes: Deepened peace and frozen affection are distinct possibilities; the circulation clause is not a diagnosis.

## Contradictions

- Deepened peace and frozen affection are distinct possibilities; the circulation clause is not a diagnosis.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Neptune = Admetos` describes affection that has become cold or frozen, together with the disappointment around it. [Witte](#witte-entry) names coldness in love and disharmony with a few people. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also allows a frozen love to dissolve and peace to deepen through sensitivity, while naming resistance to subtle interests and suppressed metaphysical art. These keep deepening and restriction distinct. The source's circulatory claim remains historical, without establishing a bodily condition.
