## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Neptune = Chiron` describes helping others reach subtler dimensions of their capacity to love. [Sandbach's single entry](#sandbach-entry) connects intuitive understanding with seeing through romantic illusion into what he calls Divine Love. The emphasis is a refinement of perception and affection through spiritual understanding. His theological and vibration language remains attributed to this source, without supplying a clinical healing claim.
