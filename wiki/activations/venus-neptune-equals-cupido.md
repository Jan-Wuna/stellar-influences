Misunderstanding in marital life. Unhappy matrimony. Dissolution of a harmonious association.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Neptune`
- Source reference: printed page `279`; PDF page `138` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

A peaceful community by the water, a secret community of love, harmony in a sensitive community, unclear wishes in a community, uncertain peace for a community, and a community's preference for metaphysics.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A sensitive or secret community of love whose harmony may be uncertain.
- Psychology: Unclear communal wishes and interest in metaphysics.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Misunderstanding in marriage, unhappy matrimony, or dissolution of an association.
- Events/manifestations: A peaceful community by water or harmony through shared sensitivity.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Neptune = Cupido` brings idealized, sensitive, or hidden affection into a shared union. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) allows a peaceful community by water and harmony within a sensitive group, alongside unclear wishes and insecure peace. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes misunderstanding in marriage or dissolution of a harmonious association. A shared subtle or metaphysical interest can therefore belong to the same range as uncertain agreement; the presence of a community does not settle the quality of its bond.
