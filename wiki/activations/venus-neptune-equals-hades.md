## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Prolonged sorrow, privation, or disappointed service in love.
- Psychology: Lack of sensitivity in love and dangerous future wishes in the ABC.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a specific medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Secret love or service associated with deprivation and disadvantage.
- Events/manifestations: Chronic love sorrow, uncertain peace, or disappointing care.
- Conflicts/notes: Chronic sorrow and privation form the shared adverse range; hidden service is a separate clause.

## Contradictions

- Chronic sorrow and privation form the shared adverse range; hidden service is a separate clause.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Neptune = Hades` gives disappointed or hidden love an enduring burden. [Witte](#witte-entry) names chronic sorrow in love. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops this through privations connected with secret love, disappointing service, and insufficient sensitivity, while also retaining secret service as a separate clause. Its dangerous future wishes and uncertainty around peace extend the adverse range. The connected theme is affection or care under conditions of deprivation, without inventing a specific event from those phrases.
