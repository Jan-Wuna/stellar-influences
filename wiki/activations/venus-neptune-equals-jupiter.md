## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Happy enthusiasm of love. Temporary happiness in an unfortunate love affair. Only for hours united in happy love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exchangeable right peace, joy and success in the water, money problems through love, love success through fine felt, love GIOck at the water, exchangeable financial desires, uncertain peace success, and advantage through love for metaphysics

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Rich imagination, romantic reverie, love of comfort, receptive to beauty and art, tendency to revel in illusions. Unreal or apparent happiness in love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To exuberantly give of oneself in a relationship, possibly excessively. The ten dency to be overly idealistic about love. To go too far in one’s idealization of the loved one.

- Source heading: `VENUS/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`

Opens you to good advice on how to use makeup or adornments to increase your appearance; excessive use of deceptive practices in financial or artistic endeavors; indulging in religious deceptions as a form of holiness.
