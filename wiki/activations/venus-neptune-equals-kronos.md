## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Neptune`
- Source reference: PDF page `180` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Sanitariums for blood ailment or for hysterical females. Falsehood or insincere affection of leading people or representatives of the government.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Neptune`
- Source reference: printed page `279`; PDF page `138` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Joy and self-confidence through metaphysics, the state's expectation of peace, authority weakened through love, great preference for water, secret love for the boss, artistic superiority through intuition, and independent metaphysical art.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Intuitive or metaphysical distinction alongside uncertain sincerity in authority.
- Psychology: Confidence through metaphysics and authority weakened through love.
- Body/health: Witte's institutional blood-ailment and gendered hysteria examples remain historical medical language.
- Social/relationship: Secret love for a boss or insincere affection from leading people.
- Events/manifestations: Independent metaphysical art, intuitive artistic standing, and state expectations of peace.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's medical and gender labels do not establish diagnosis; artistic distinction and insincerity are separate possibilities.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Neptune = Kronos` places refined or hidden affection in relation to authority. [Witte](#witte-entry) emphasizes insincerity from leaders or government representatives, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes secret love for a boss and authority weakened through love. It also gives intuition a constructive artistic range and names confidence through metaphysics. Witte's sanitarium examples retain historical medical and gendered language, without defining an individual's health. The artistic and metaphysical possibilities coexist with uncertainty about the sincerity or strength of authority.
