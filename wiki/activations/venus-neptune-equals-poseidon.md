## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Neptune`
- Source reference: PDF page `180` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Supersensitive, delicate. Dissolution of a harmony by influences which one is unable to control. Sensitive to radiation. Dowser.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Neptune`
- Source reference: printed page `279`; PDF page `138` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

An artistic idea of the future, harmony and clarity through metaphysics, foreign art and culture, love of clear water, weak love of truth, unclear ideas of peace, uncertain cultural peace, refinement of peace culture, and refined spiritual art.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Extreme sensitivity and spiritual or metaphysical refinement, with uncertain harmony.
- Psychology: Delicacy, search for clarity through metaphysics, or weak attachment to truth.
- Body/health: Radiation sensitivity and dowsing are Witte's historical assertions, not established medical effects.
- Social/relationship: Harmony may dissolve under influences outside one's control.
- Events/manifestations: Foreign art and culture, refined spiritual art, clear water, and uncertain cultural peace.
- Conflicts/notes: Spiritual refinement coexists with unclear peace ideas and weak love of truth; reverence does not secure clarity.

## Contradictions

- Spiritual refinement coexists with unclear peace ideas and weak love of truth; reverence does not secure clarity.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Neptune = Poseidon` describes delicacy and a search for refined or spiritual harmony. [Witte](#witte-entry) also allows that harmony to dissolve under influences beyond one's control. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds clarity through metaphysics, foreign art and culture, and refined spiritual art, alongside unclear peace ideas and weak love of truth. Refinement and uncertainty therefore remain together. Witte's radiation and dowsing clauses are retained as historical source assertions, without converting them into verified bodily effects.
