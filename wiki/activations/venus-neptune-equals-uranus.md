## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 180; activator glyph and paragraph checked against the scan.

Homesickness. Love’s longing. Sudden misfortune in love. Death of love partner. Suicide as a result of love. Reminder. To be reminded.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 279 (printed); PDF page: 138; Uranus row checked against the scan.

Dynamic art of the future, harmonious technique with water, sudden love for metaphysics, sudden disappointment in love, creeping tensions in love, uncertain peace event, restlessness through insecure peace, and preference for events in the air

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 188 (printed), entry `0633`.

Strong longing for love, impressionable or an easy susceptibility to external impressions through other persons or environment, prone to seductive influences, stepping-up of impulses in the expression of love. Giving way to despair, periodic extravagance, sudden separation in love, perversion.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: PDF page `62` (one-based; exact activation entry personally read).

To freely and openly expressing one’s romantic idealism and exalted love. One has a unique ability to intuit just how to love someone and then do it. Romantic fan tasies that are inspiring and original. The need to free oneself from love addiction.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/NEPTUNE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `204`

#### Munkasey Entry

Producing unusual methods to convince others about what is real and what is not real; extremes you are willing to try to inspire appreciation in others for artistic or musical creations; the politics of deceit and vanity.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Romantic longing and imagination acquire a sudden, original, or disruptive expression.
- Psychology: Receptivity, intense impulses, original fantasy, and the need to free oneself from love addiction.
- Body/health: Witte's partner-death and suicide claims and Ebertin's “perversion” label remain historical source statements, not diagnoses or predictions.
- Social/relationship: Intuitive, freely expressed affection coexists with susceptibility to seduction, disappointment, and sudden separation.
- Events/manifestations: Unusual artistic persuasion, water technology, uncertain peace, and reminders are explicitly represented.
- Conflicts/notes: Sandbach's intuitive loving capacity differs materially from the older emphasis on misfortune; Munkasey also adds deceit and vanity.

## Contradictions

The entries share heightened romantic receptivity but develop it differently. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) gives original, intuitively responsive love and release from addiction; [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) emphasize longing, susceptibility, and severe losses. These are retained as differing possibilities rather than treated as either a guaranteed gift or inevitable harm. The scanned ABC row reads disappointment in love, not the former cached “love exchange.”

## Derived Synthesis

Uranus specifies Venus/Neptune through romantic imagination that becomes strikingly original, freely expressed, or suddenly disruptive. Intuition may suggest an unusual way to love someone or create an inspiring fantasy, while heightened impressionability can also intensify seduction, extravagance, disappointment, or abrupt separation. The need to free oneself from love addiction belongs to this same range of imagination and attachment. ([Sandbach](#sandbach-entry); [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).)

The artistic range includes unusual methods of winning appreciation and persuading others about reality; deceit and vanity remain possible uses of that inventiveness. Water technology and uncertain peace extend ABC beyond private romance. Witte also explicitly includes homesickness, reminders, and being reminded; its catastrophic love-loss claims remain attributed historical doctrine, without becoming individual forecasts. ([Munkasey](#munkasey-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry); [Witte](#witte-entry).)
