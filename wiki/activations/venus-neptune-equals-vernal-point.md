## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Neptune = Vernal Point` gives uncertain or hidden affection a public setting. [Witte](#witte-entry) names aversion to love and deceit in public, using prudishness as a historical characterization. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds secret love, unclear wishes, disappointing art, and insecure public peace. Its water art and wishes for the future remain separate creative or aspirational expressions. The shared public setting does not make every ideal or artistic work deceptive.
