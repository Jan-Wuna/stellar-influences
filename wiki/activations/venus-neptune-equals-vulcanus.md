Influence through artistic intuition, subtle harmonious influence, joy over the strength of water, harmonizing water power, weakened power for peace, artistic influence through metaphysics, weakening of the power of love, and preference for strong wind.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Strong affection that is concealed or simulated, with subtle artistic influence.
- Psychology: Powerful but obscured feeling or a weakening of love's force.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Hidden mighty affection or its simulation.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic intuition, metaphysical influence, water power, and preference for strong wind.
- Conflicts/notes: Hidden feeling and simulated feeling are distinct; strength does not establish sincerity.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Neptune = Vulcanus` gives concealed or simulated affection considerable force. [Witte](#witte-entry) explicitly preserves both hidden feeling and its imitation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds subtle harmonious influence through artistic intuition or metaphysics, while also naming weakened power in peace or love. Water power and strong wind remain literal source examples. The orientation therefore concerns intensity and influence under uncertain visibility, without resolving whether the feeling is sincere.
