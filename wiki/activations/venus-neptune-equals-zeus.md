## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Neptune`
- Source reference: PDF page `180` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Inherited blood diseases. A bleeder. The generative capability ends.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Neptune`
- Source reference: printed page `279`; PDF page `138` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Beginning of an uncertain peace, beginning disappointment in love, disappointed striving in love, fiery expectation of love, joy through efforts in metaphysics, artistic achievements in the air, creative art in water, and generating future wishes.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Fiery romantic expectation and creative effort, with disappointed striving or insecure beginnings.
- Psychology: Expectation of love and effort toward metaphysical aims.
- Body/health: Witte's inherited blood disease, bleeding, and end of generative capacity are historical medical assertions.
- Social/relationship: Beginning disappointment in love or striving that does not find its desired result.
- Events/manifestations: Creative art involving water or air, metaphysical effort, and generating wishes for the future.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's severe medical claims and the ABC's artistic or aspirational clauses are distinct source ranges.

## Contradictions

- Witte's severe medical claims and the ABC's artistic or aspirational clauses are distinct source ranges.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Neptune = Zeus` directs romantic expectation or subtle creative interests toward an attempted result. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) names fiery expectation, effort in metaphysics, and art involving water or air, alongside insecure peace and disappointed striving in love. [Witte](#witte-entry) gives a separate severe historical range in inherited blood disease, bleeding, and an end to generative capacity. Those medical assertions remain attributed and do not establish health or reproductive outcomes. The creative expectations and the older bodily claims should not be collapsed into one causal story.
