## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Nodes + Venus`
- Source reference: PDF page `142` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Difficulties or obstacles in getting acquainted. Few or no love connections. Held back or refusal in love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Mondknoten + Venus`
- Source reference: printed page `211`; PDF page `104` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Breaking off a love relationship, a depressing love connection, restricted encounters with art, strengthening peaceful contacts, a consolidated love connection, thorough relations with art, and suppressed love within a connection.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Restricted or obstructed loving contact, with consolidation as a distinct possibility.
- Psychology: Being held back, refusal, or suppressed affection.
- Body/health: The activation entries supply no specific bodily or medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Few or no love connections, breaking off a relationship, or a bond that becomes consolidated.
- Events/manifestations: Obstacles in acquaintance, restricted encounters with art, and strengthening peaceful contacts.
- Conflicts/notes: Ending or blocking a relationship and strengthening it are separate source possibilities.

## Contradictions

- Ending or blocking a relationship and strengthening it are separate source possibilities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Node = Admetos` narrows or obstructs the formation and expression of a loving bond. [Witte](#witte-entry) names difficulties in acquaintance, few or no love connections, and refusal or being held back. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also allows a connection to consolidate and peaceful contacts to strengthen, beside breaking off a relationship or suppressing affection. Its thorough relations with art extend concentration beyond intimacy. Restriction and consolidation therefore remain distinct ways this orientation can develop.
