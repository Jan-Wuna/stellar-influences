## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Nodes + Venus`
- Source reference: PDF page `142` (one-based).

#### Witte Entry

Many love connections. Peaceful or religious connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Mondknoten + Venus`
- Source reference: printed page `211`; PDF page `104` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Expansion of harmonious relationships, success of a harmonious connection, successful love relationships, peaceful trade connections, conveying artistic experience, many harmonious contacts, and a benevolent encounter with science.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Many loving or harmonious connections and the expansion of shared interests.
- Psychology: Openness to art, science, peaceful contact, or religion.
- Body/health: Neither activation entry supplies a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Numerous love connections and a successful harmonious union.
- Events/manifestations: Peaceful trade relations, conveying artistic experience, and contact with science.
- Conflicts/notes: Breadth of connections does not establish that every relationship will be lasting or successful.

## Contradictions

- Breadth of connections does not establish that every relationship will be lasting or successful.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Node = Apollon` widens the circle of affectionate or harmonious contact. [Witte](#witte-entry) names many love connections and peaceful or religious connections. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful relationships, peaceful trade, and the sharing of artistic experience, with science as another field of encounter. The distinctive range is breadth joined to shared appreciation and exchange, while many connections and the success of a particular bond remain separate possibilities.
