#### Sandbach Entry

Your vibration helps others to appreciate the way they are connected to everything, and also to connect in a more loving and relaxed way. You help them to appreciate the value of their past connections, as well as to appreciate what they most need for their own personal growth.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Node = Chiron` describes helping others appreciate how they are connected and approach those ties with greater love and ease. [Sandbach's entry](#sandbach-entry) links recognition of past connections with understanding what is needed for personal growth. The emphasis is on valuing relationship as part of development, rather than assigning every past bond the same value. His spiritual language of vibration supplies no separate clinical claim.
