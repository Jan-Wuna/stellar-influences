## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Tainted love affairs. Vulgar love unions. Purchasable love. Protection of prostitutes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Disadvantaged love contacts, love service in a connection, connection of crime and love, connections from the past, hidden love connection, renunciation of love in a connection, and preference for dangerous connections

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Love contacts marked by disadvantage, secrecy, commerce, or danger.
- psychology: The ABC entry includes attraction to dangerous connections and renunciation of love within a connection.
- body/health: No specific bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Hidden relationships, ties from the past, and paid sexual relationships appear in the entries.
- events/manifestations: The sources mention criminal involvement and protection of prostitutes; these are attributed historical examples.
- conflicts/notes: The Witte entry uses moral judgments that should not become judgments about a person.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in troubled or concealed love contacts. Their different examples do not establish a contradiction, and Witte's evaluative vocabulary is not a neutral description of every relationship.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Node = Hades`, Hades specifies the Venus/Node connection through disadvantage, concealment, or an exchange that complicates affection. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends this to ties from the past, renunciation of love within a relationship, and attraction to dangerous contacts. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives paid sexual relationships and protection of prostitutes as historically worded examples; its moral labels need not be adopted as the interpretation.
