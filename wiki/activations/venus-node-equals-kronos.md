## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Civil wedding. Agreeable connections with independent, leading people. Profit through them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 211; one-based PDF p. 104, Venus/Node, Kronos row; complete actual row and adjacent glyphs checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Special connections to art, peace mediation by the state, a great love relationship, harmonious contact with authority, independent connections to art, priority given to beauty in a connection, and a benevolent connection to the state.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Love and agreeable contacts connected with leadership, authority, or formal recognition.
- psychology: The entries emphasize harmonious contact rather than a defined inner disposition.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Civil marriage and agreeable connections with independent or leading people.
- events/manifestations: A civil wedding, benefit through influential contacts, and state peace mediation are source examples.
- conflicts/notes: Kunst means art; independent art connections and the priority of beauty are explicit alongside authority and the state.

## Contradictions

The clear passages emphasize formal or authoritative connections without a direct contradiction. Their narrower examples do not establish that every such connection is beneficial.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Node = Kronos`, the connection concerns formal standing or contact with people who lead. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives civil marriage and agreeable connections with independent or leading people, including benefit through them. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds authority and state peace mediation, together with special or independent connections to art and priority given to beauty in a connection. These remain specific possibilities rather than a promise that all authoritative connections are beneficial.
