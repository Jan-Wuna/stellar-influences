## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be in love. To be the object or affections. Harmonious connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Immediate contact joy, my love relations, my connections to the arts, my preference for connections, and psychic desire intermediation

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

An individual attitude to love unions or the arts. Deep feelings of love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The highest that one can attain to is to connect with others in a loving way. The channeling of Divine Love to all people.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `212`

#### Munkasey Entry

Learning from your interactions with others and growing from this; recognition of your accomplishments by friends; the conclusions and experiences you gain from working with others; a family life rich in values or wealth.
