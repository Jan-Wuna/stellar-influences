## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be in love. To be the object or affections. Harmonious connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 210; one-based PDF p. 104, Venus/Node, MC row; complete actual row and adjacent glyphs checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Soulful encounters in love, harmony of an emotional bond, a minute of harmonious connection, my joy in contact, my love relationships, my connections to art, my preference for connections, and emotional mediation of wishes.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

An individual attitude to love unions or the arts. Deep feelings of love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

The highest that one can attain to is to connect with others in a loving way. The channeling of Divine Love to all people.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `212`

#### Munkasey Entry

Learning from your interactions with others and growing from this; recognition of your accomplishments by friends; the conclusions and experiences you gain from working with others; a family life rich in values or wealth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A personally felt involvement in love and harmonious connections.
- psychology: Deep love, an individual attitude to unions and art, and learning through interaction.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Being loved, working with others, friendship, and valued family life.
- events/manifestations: Recognition by friends and conclusions gained through collaboration.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's minute of harmonious connection and emotional mediation of wishes remain literal brief claims; no timing model or mechanism is established.

## Contradictions

Personal affection, learning through others, and Sandbach's ideal of Divine Love differ in scope without directly contradicting one another.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Node = MC`, MC specifies the Venus/Node connection as personally meaningful involvement in love. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin entries](#ebertin-entry) center being loved, harmonious ties, and deep individual feeling. The [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry) adds learning through cooperation and recognition from friends, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) frames loving connection as a highest aim. These are supported personal and relational meanings; the passages do not establish a particular career or public image.

The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also names soulful encounters in love, harmony of an emotional bond, a minute of harmonious connection and emotional mediation of wishes, without specifying a timing model or mechanism.
