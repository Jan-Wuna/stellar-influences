## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Pleasant love ties. Getting acquainted. Love messages. Love letters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 210; one-based PDF p. 104, Venus/Node, Mercury row; complete actual row and adjacent glyphs checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Describing encounters with artists, mediating peace talks, peace contacts of young people, joy in contact through media, love connections in youth, connections with female youth, and preference for media connections.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Desire to discuss love problems with others and to make contact with others. Union through thoughts of love, association with another person through common interest or through love. The person who transmits a love letter, the sender of a love letter.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To communicate in a way that is both loving and connected. To think in terms of how to bring greater peace, harmony, and joy to those with whom one is connected. A mind wanting to share its insights with others and to have them be appreciated.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/NODE with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `212`

#### Munkasey Entry

The presentation and acceptance of ideas at meetings; an adaptability to alter responses and respond more quickly to the needs of others; a quickness of mind and an ability to read the motives and intentions of others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Love and connection expressed through communication.
- psychology: A wish to discuss love, share insights, and respond to others' needs.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Making acquaintances and forming bonds through common interests or loving thought.
- events/manifestations: Love letters, messages, meetings, and presentation of ideas.
- conflicts/notes: Humor, evasiveness, and dishonesty in the former synthesis were not stated in these entries.

## Contradictions

The entries connect communication with affection or responsive exchange; no direct contradiction is apparent within these passages.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Node = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the Venus/Node connection through messages, discussion, and shared thought. The [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin entries](#ebertin-entry) make this concrete as getting acquainted, discussing love problems, and sending love letters. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) adds communication intended to bring peace and appreciation, while [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) emphasizes presenting ideas and adapting responses to other people. The unifying theme is using communication to establish or sustain a valued connection.

ABC specifically includes encounters with artists, mediation of peace talks and media connections, while retaining its historical youth examples ([ABC entry](#abc-entry)).
