## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Pleasant love ties. Getting acquainted. Love messages. Love letters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

To describe encounters with art, to convey peace languages, peace contacts of the youth, contact joy of the media, love connections in the youth, connections to the female youth, and preference for media connections

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Desire to discuss love problems with others and to make contact with others. Union through thoughts of love, association with another person through common interest or through love. The person who transmits a love letter, the sender of a love letter.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To communicate in a way that is both loving and connected. To think in terms of how to bring greater peace, harmony, and joy to those with whom one is connected. A mind wanting to share its insights with others and to have them be appreciated.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

The presentation and acceptance of ideas at meetings; an adaptability to alter responses and respond more quickly to the needs of others; a quickness of mind and an ability to read the motives and intentions of others.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Node = Mercury`: diplomatic speech, social intelligence, and bonds built through tactful exchange, humor, or artistic discussion. Main caution: pleasing talk without depth: charm in language can evade necessary honesty or hard distinctions.
