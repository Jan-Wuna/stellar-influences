## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Changes or transformations in love relations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development of links with the artist, Fricdens contact group, development of contact joy, changes in harmony, growing harmony in connection with each other, changing peace contacts, and developing benevolence in connections

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Shaping of destiny through love unions, an inseparable union.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

A intense attraction to certain people or groups that one intuitive knows will help one to evolve. Personal transformation that comes about through allowing one self to experience the depth of love, and to allow that love to bring growth to one’s consciousness.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Intensity by artists or financiers; the power that you or others bring or show to others; troubles which arise because others want the power without wanting to do the work required; intensifies attitudes toward others.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Node = Pluto`: compelling rapport, profound artistic or relational influence, and ties that alter the native's social field deeply. Main caution: possessive alliance: attraction and cooperation can become controlling, obsessive, or difficult to leave cleanly.
