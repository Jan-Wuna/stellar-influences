## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Mental or spiritual kind of connection or ties.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 211; one-based PDF p. 104, Venus/Node, Poseidon row; complete actual row and adjacent glyphs checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Insights from the harmony of a connection, a peaceful spiritual connection, harmonious cultural connections, clarity in a love relationship, connection to truth through love, and combining art and culture.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental or spiritual ties, with clarity in loving connection.
- psychology: Insight into a relationship and a connection between love and truth.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Peaceful spiritual and cultural connections.
- events/manifestations: Clarity in a love relationship and harmony in cultural contacts.
- conflicts/notes: These entries concern the quality of connection; they do not establish a specific event outcome.

## Contradictions

The short Witte statement and ABC's elaboration on clarity and culture are compatible in emphasis. Their brevity limits any stronger conclusion.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Node = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the Venus/Node connection as mental or spiritual rapport. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states that form of tie directly; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds peaceful spiritual connection, clarity in love, and insight arising from harmony. The organizing theme is a relationship valued for shared understanding or truth, with cultural connection as another stated expression.

ABC's final clause explicitly combines art and culture, rather than naming a particular artist ([ABC entry](#abc-entry)).
