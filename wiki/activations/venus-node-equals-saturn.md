## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Short pleasant union. Sadness and disturbance in an agreeable connection. Separation of a flirtation. Also of other persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Distressed love connection, harmonious connections in old age, harmonious mediation as task, contact joy in old age, problematic peace contacts, painful love connection, and separation of a harmonious relationship

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Inhibitions, shyness. A renewed awakening to reality, awakening from a love dream, difficulties or worries in a love union.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Fear or shyness about sharing one’s love and values with others. Difficulty in feeling relaxed and at ease when connecting with others. To find it hard to let go of old value and adopt new ones.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

You temper your ideas and thoughts so that they are shorter; others judge you to be more organized than most; frustrations encountered thru others which continue; ties to older people who have the resources you need.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Node = Saturn`: dependable affection, mature alliance, and relationships that prove their worth over time. Main caution: inhibited warmth: fear of loss or rejection can make closeness careful, formal, or harder to relax into.
