## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden love union. Spontaneous excitement in an agreeable relationships.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Exciting encounter with the artist, encounter with new artist, harmonious impulses in relationships, harmonious connection to technology, new love connection, tensions in a harmonious connection, and mediation of peace contacts

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Ability to easily make contacts and to like people. Lovesick, a sudden love union of short duration.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To be free and uninhibited in sharing one’s love with others. A unique gift for being at ease when connecting. A love connection that manifests suddenly

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Disruptions which occur suddenly at meetings you attend; unsettling news received or presented from friends; pleasant meetings among those who share astrological or computer knowledge; polite indifference to ideas.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Node = Uranus`: stimulating friendships, unconventional love, and social openings that arrive suddenly and alter the field. Main caution: instability in rapport: novelty and freedom can outrun steadiness, making attachment vivid but erratic.
