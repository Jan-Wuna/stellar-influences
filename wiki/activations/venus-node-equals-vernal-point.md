## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Love ties in general. Pleasant connections with many persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

joy through relationships in public life, peace contacts in public, artistic connections in public life, joy of contact in public life, and mediation of wishes in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Pleasant or loving ties situated among many people or in public life.
- psychology: Joy in contact is stated; no detailed inner disposition is given.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Agreeable connections with many persons.
- events/manifestations: Public peace contacts, artistic connections, and mediation of wishes.
- conflicts/notes: The public setting does not itself establish fame, status, or a particular profession.

## Contradictions

General love ties and public contacts differ in scope without a direct contradiction. The entries give no contrary case on this page.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Node = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the Venus/Node connection in a public or broadly social setting. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives pleasant connections with many people; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds enjoyment of public relationships, peace contacts, and artistic connections. The common thread is valued association extending into public life, with mediation of wishes another stated expression.
