## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Longing for harmony with a soul equally attuned. Harmony of the soul with others. Passionate in love affairs.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

energetic bond through love, influential connections to art, violence in a love relationship, force of a love relationship, powerful peace contacts, strength of a harmonious connection, and benevolent influence of a connection

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strength, intensity, or influence within a loving connection.
- psychology: Longing for a similarly attuned person and passion in love affairs.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Soul harmony, energetic love bonds, and the ABC possibility of violence.
- events/manifestations: Influential artistic contacts and powerful peace contacts are stated examples.
- conflicts/notes: Strength and benevolent influence must remain distinct from the explicitly stated possibility of violence.

## Contradictions

Harmony and violence are sharply different expressions in the entries, not interchangeable meanings. Neither is established as an inevitable outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Node = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the Venus/Node connection through its strength and intensity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) centers passionate love and longing for someone similarly attuned, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes the power of a loving bond, benevolent influence, and influential artistic or peace contacts. ABC also explicitly includes violence in a love relationship; that adverse possibility must remain separate from the desired harmony rather than being softened into intensity alone.
