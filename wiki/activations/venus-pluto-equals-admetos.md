## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Favor and preferment are the exception. Pleasant development for a few. Experience a profound change in politeness and consideration.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Pluto`
- Source reference: printed page `281`; PDF page `139` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Blocked development of harmony, restricted development of love, depressing change in love, growing deepening of peace, thorough development in art, a deep preference for transformation, and circulatory changes through love.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restricted or uneven development of love and harmony.
- psychology: Depressing change in love and a profound change in consideration are stated.
- body/health: ABC mentions circulatory changes through love; this is an attributed historical claim, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Pleasant development is described as available to a few rather than generally shared.
- events/manifestations: Blocked harmony, restricted love development, and exceptional favor.
- conflicts/notes: ABC includes deepening of peace and thorough artistic development alongside blocked harmony and restricted love; its circulatory clause remains an attributed historical claim.

## Contradictions

Witte allows pleasant development for a few while ABC emphasizes blockage. These are limited and adverse possibilities, not a clear contradiction about every case.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Pluto = Admetos`, Admetos specifies Venus/Pluto development as restricted, concentrated, or unevenly available. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places blocked harmony and restricted love beside deepening of peace and thorough development in art. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) allows pleasant development for a few and a profound change in politeness or consideration. Together these passages support constrained or deepened change, with benefit described as exceptional rather than generally shared.
