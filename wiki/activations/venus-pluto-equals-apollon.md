## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The pleasant development of many. Great optimism.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development of arts and science, successful development of harmony, preference for transformational knowledge, growing peace success, growth through love experiences, knowledge about love develop, and increasing love development

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Expanding development of love, harmony, art, and knowledge.
- psychology: Great optimism and growth through love experiences.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Pleasant development involving many people.
- events/manifestations: Development of arts and science, successful harmony, and peace success.
- conflicts/notes: Knowledge about love and transformation are specific ABC themes; they do not define a technical method.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize favorable development on different scales, with no direct conflict apparent in these passages.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Pluto = Apollon`, Apollon specifies Venus/Pluto development as growth that can extend to many people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) combines pleasant development with optimism; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives arts and science, successful harmony, and growth through love experiences as concrete forms. Developing knowledge about love or transformation belongs to the same pattern of widening experience and understanding.
