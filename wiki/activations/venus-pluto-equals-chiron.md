## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

Your vibration helps others to attune to the tremendous power of love, and to let go of their fear of it. You help them to recognize what it is that they most deeply want and are attracted to.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Helping others approach the power of love and recognize deep attraction.
- psychology: Release of fear of love and recognition of what is deeply wanted.
- body/health: No bodily condition or treatment is stated.
- social/relationship: An assisting influence on another person's relation to love.
- events/manifestations: The passage describes interpersonal help, without a specific external event.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach's 'vibration' is his own explanatory language; this single entry does not establish a mechanism.

## Contradictions

Only one passage is present. Its release-of-fear and recognition-of-desire themes are complementary; comparison with other sources is unavailable.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Pluto = Chiron`, Chiron specifies the Venus/Pluto power of love as an influence that helps another person face it with less fear. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) connects that help with recognizing what one most deeply wants and finds attractive. This is a specific interpersonal role in Sandbach's language of 'vibration'; the passage does not supply a medical claim or explain how such an influence operates.
