## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Happy matrimony. Desired family increase. Harmonious development of an association. Mutual consideration in marriage.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development of a community of peace, peaceful transformation of a community, joint development of the arts, common preference, common transformation of love, harmony development in of a community, and growing love in the community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development of love and harmony within marriage, family, or a community.
- psychology: Mutual consideration is stated; no further inner disposition is specified.
- body/health: Desired family increase is a source example, not a prediction of fertility.
- social/relationship: Happy matrimony, associations, and growing communal love.
- events/manifestations: Family increase, peaceful community change, and joint artistic development.
- conflicts/notes: The favorable wording describes possibilities within these entries, not guaranteed marriage outcomes.

## Contradictions

Marriage and wider community development differ in scale rather than contradicting one another. The entries provide no adverse comparison here.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Pluto = Cupido`, Cupido specifies Venus/Pluto development through a shared life or association. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives happy marriage, desired family increase, and mutual consideration; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the setting to peaceful community change, joint artistic development, and growing love within a group. The unifying theme is development that is shared, with harmony supported through participation rather than described only as an individual feeling.
