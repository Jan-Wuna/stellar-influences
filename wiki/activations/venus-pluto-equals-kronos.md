## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The master, the superior, the employer (boss), the authority wink (connive) at someone. To stretch a point. Pleasant change in the ruling body or authorities. To be favored.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Authoritate development in love, special developments in the arts, self awareness through growing joy, state peace developments, preference for the boss to develop, growing prestige of the arts, and growing artistic superiority

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Development involving favor, authority, prestige, or superior standing.
- psychology: Growing joy and self-awareness appear in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Favor or leniency from a superior and changes in authorities.
- events/manifestations: Pleasant change in a ruling body, artistic prestige, and state peace developments.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's connivance/leniency need not be treated as unqualified ethical approval.

## Contradictions

Authority's favor and artistic or state development are different applications without a direct contradiction. The sources do not establish that favor is always deserved.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Pluto = Kronos`, Kronos specifies Venus/Pluto development through authority or standing. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes being favored, a superior stretching a point, and pleasant change among those in charge. The clear portions of the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extend development to artistic prestige and state peace efforts. The shared pattern is favorable change connected with authority or recognized standing, while the source's leniency or connivance remains a particular form of favor.
