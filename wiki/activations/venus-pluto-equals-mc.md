## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The own harmonious development. Development of own wishes and hopes. Ready to considerate.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

I love evolution, my peaceful development, my development in love, my fondness for change, my growing frcude, and growing soul harmony

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Ego-conscious direction of the sexual urges, strong feeling of love, artistic creation. Deep feelings of love (karmic union).

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

One's highest attainment in this life has to do with transmuting the lesser manifestations of love into higher ones. One may be intensely loved by the public or by people at large. Career decisions that are difficult to make.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/PLUTO with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `208`

#### Munkasey Entry

Growing thru destructive tendencies and overcoming obsessive habits which do little for your personal standing; creating some protection for self or family against large destructive natural or man-made forces.
