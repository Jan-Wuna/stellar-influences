## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be prepared in thought for a pleasant change. New acquaintances prepare the way for a pleasant change. To realize the pleasant change of things. Politeness facilitates acquaintance.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development of a young love, peaceful development of youth, language about harmonious developments, developing harmonious thoughts, developing youth beauty, fondness for developmental language, and growing love for youth

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

Artistic talent, an outsider in the arts, desire to solve problems of love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To communicate with a compelling seductiveness. A mind which inevitably falls madly in love with certain subjects.
