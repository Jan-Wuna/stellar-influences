## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Harmonious development of females. Harmonious development in the public or with female persons. Harmonious function of the glands.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

The development of feelings of love, the development of a people in peace, harmony through the change of women, the artistic development of a woman, the development of love for women, the change of affection for women, the growth of love for the people, and the development of benevolent feelings

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

An overly intense emotional life, strong procreative power. Motherhood.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

To be strongly impressionable to intense beauty. The emotional need for intense relationships, connections, and romances. To fall easily under the sway of the charms and allurements of another person.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

#### Munkasey Entry

Amplifies your urges to destroy; taking pleasure in the arbitrary use of punishment; commonplace items used for waste receptacles; a need to glamorize self by creating glitzy gaudiness; popular forms of luxury.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Pluto = Moon`: profound attachment, deep emotional honesty, and the capacity to heal or bond through total feeling. Main caution: moods can become possessive, jealous, or compulsive, making closeness feel all-or-nothing.
