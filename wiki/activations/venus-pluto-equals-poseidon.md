## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Harmonious change or development in the philosophical, or political attitude. Consideration for the world view.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Pluto`
- Source reference: printed page `281`; PDF page `139` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Development of peace through culture, unfolding artistic ideas, development of art and culture, growing peace ideas, spiritual unfolding of love, developing spiritual art, growing harmony through clarity, and unfolding truth in love.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Harmonious change in outlook, with culture, peace, and clarity.
- psychology: Development in philosophical or political attitude and spiritual love.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Consideration for another worldview is stated in Witte.
- events/manifestations: Peace through culture, growing peace ideas, and harmony through clarity.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's spiritual art and truth in love remain its interpretive language; neither establishes a demonstrated mechanism.

## Contradictions

Changes in worldview and artistic, cultural, or spiritual development are compatible emphases. The entries do not supply a contrary outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Pluto = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies Venus/Pluto development through outlook and understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes harmonious change in philosophical or political attitude and consideration for a worldview. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds peace through culture, artistic ideas and spiritual art, spiritual unfolding of love, and growing harmony through clarity or truth in love. The common theme is a change in how love or harmony is understood and expressed through ideas, art, or culture.
