## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 189 (one-based).

Atheist or deep religious contemplation. To reject matters of faith. Anti- religious. Dislike and antipathy of a particular religion or philosophy of life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 297; PDF page: 147 (one-based spread).

enduring love for reason, limited cultural pleasure, restricted reason in love, strengthening of spiritual peace, mentally restricted art, profound clarity in love, deep love for truth, and deepening of spiritual peace

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restriction or depth in matters of faith, truth, and spiritual harmony.
- psychology: Rejection of a belief and deep religious contemplation are both explicit Witte possibilities.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Dislike of a particular religion or philosophy is stated; no general relationship outcome is given.
- events/manifestations: Limited cultural pleasure and deepening spiritual peace occur in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: Atheism, anti-religion, and contemplation remain distinct possibilities. ABC's reason and art phrases are translated from the scanned German entry.

## Contradictions

Witte explicitly allows both rejection of faith and deep contemplation. ABC's deep peace and truth coexist with restriction; these are alternatives, not proof that all rejection conceals religious belief.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Poseidon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the Venus/Poseidon theme through limitation or depth in one's relation to faith and truth. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly includes rejection of religion as well as deep religious contemplation. Clear phrases in the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) add limited cultural pleasure, profound clarity in love, deep love of truth, and deepening spiritual peace. The reading must retain rejection and contemplation as distinct possibilities, without treating either as a judgment of a person's worth.
