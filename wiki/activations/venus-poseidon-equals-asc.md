## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 189 (one-based).

Acquaintance with priests, preachers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 296; PDF page: 147 (one-based spread).

Harmony and clarity with the partner, spiritual love for the partner, art and culture in the environment, spiritual art at the location, spiritualized artistic environment, reasonable love partner, and truth love of the partner

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Spiritual or truth-related harmony encountered through a partner or surroundings.
- psychology: Clarity with a partner is stated; no fuller inner disposition is described.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Acquaintance with priests/preachers and spiritual love for a partner.
- events/manifestations: An environment characterized by spiritualized artistic activity in the clear ABC wording.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's partner is described as reasonable; the scanned German does not support the former translation 'strong love partner'.

## Contradictions

Religious acquaintances and partner/environmental harmony differ in application rather than directly conflicting. The entries do not identify every partner as a religious figure.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Poseidon = Asc`, Asc specifies the Venus/Poseidon theme in direct contact with people or the surrounding environment. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives acquaintance with priests or preachers as a concrete religious example. The clear partner clauses of the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) add harmony, clarity, spiritual love, and a partner's love of truth. These connect the theme with lived encounters, without making religious acquaintance a requirement of every relationship.
