## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 189 (one-based).

Religious activities. Religious zeal. Religious deeds. To act according to one’s beliefs, faith or world conception.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 296; PDF page: 147 (one-based spread).

Prefer mental activity, harmonious mental work, work on artistic ideas, desire peace and culture, clear efforts for peace, art and cultural creation, reasonable actions in love, and preference for cultural work

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Putting belief, spiritual harmony, and cultural ideas into action.
- psychology: Religious zeal and preference for mental or cultural work.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Actions in love guided by reason; efforts for peace.
- events/manifestations: Religious deeds and artistic or cultural creation.
- conflicts/notes: The sources name both religious and cultural action; neither requires the other in every case.

## Contradictions

Religious zeal and harmonious cultural work differ in emphasis without a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Poseidon = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair by acting on beliefs and cultural values. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes this explicit in religious activities, zeal, and deeds according to one's faith or worldview. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens the work to artistic ideas, cultural creation, and efforts for peace, including reasonable actions in love. The common thread is an ideal expressed through purposeful activity, rather than belief remaining only an inward preference.
