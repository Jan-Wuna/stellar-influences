## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 189 (one-based).

Acquaintanceship with faithful, religious people. Talks about religion and world conception.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 296; PDF page: 147 (one-based spread).

Youthful love rich in understanding, thoughts about the beauty of the mind, harmony through clear thinking, harmonious conversations about ethics, conversations about culture and art, arts and culture of youth, preference for spiritual conversations, and reasonable thoughts about harmony

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Thinking and conversation about faith, ethics, beauty, and harmony.
- psychology: Clarity of thought and reflection on spiritual beauty.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Acquaintance with religious people; conversations about ethics and worldview.
- events/manifestations: Youthful love and interest in art and culture are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: Conversations are not a special artificial language; reasonable does not mean indomitable.

## Contradictions

Religious acquaintances and cultural or ethical thought are distinct emphases without a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Poseidon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through clear thought and exchange about belief and cultural meaning. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names religious acquaintances and talks about religion or worldview. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds ethical, artistic, and spiritual conversation, reflection on beauty of mind, and harmony through clear thinking. Its youthful-love example belongs to that reflective setting; the entry does not equate every such conversation with romance.
