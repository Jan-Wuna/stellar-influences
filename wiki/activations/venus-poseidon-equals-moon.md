## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 189 (one-based).

Strongly and firmly believing persons. The religious and undaunted woman and populace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 296; PDF page: 147 (one-based spread).

Peace culture of a people, spirited beautiful woman, clear feelings of love, clarity in love for a woman, arts and culture of a people, beauty of a people's culture, spiritualized love for a woman, preference for clarity of feelings, and a woman's love of truth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Firm belief and spiritual or cultural feeling in people.
- psychology: Clarity in feelings of love and steadfast belief.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Love for a woman and the beliefs of a populace are source examples.
- events/manifestations: A people's peace culture, art, and cultural beauty.
- conflicts/notes: The gendered examples remain historical source language, not universal female traits.

## Contradictions

The entries share collective and personal expressions of belief and feeling. Firm conviction and emotional clarity are emphases rather than guarantees.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Poseidon = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through felt conviction and the cultural life of a people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names firm believers and an undaunted populace; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) joins clear love feelings with peace culture, art, and cultural beauty. Both use women as specific historical examples, which should not be turned into a general rule about women.
