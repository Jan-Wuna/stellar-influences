## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 189 (one-based).

To be connected with matters of belief, philosophy, religion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 296; PDF page: 147 (one-based spread).

Pleasure in clear relationships, preference for spiritual connections, harmonious cultural relationships, harmonious connection of ideas, clarity in a love connection, connecting art and culture, spiritual encounter in love, and connection with the beauty of culture

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections formed around belief, ideas, culture, and spiritual love.
- psychology: Preference for clarity and spiritual connections.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Clear love connections and harmonious cultural relationships.
- events/manifestations: Encounters through belief, philosophy, art, or cultural beauty.
- conflicts/notes: The entries do not establish a particular group's doctrine or every connection's permanence.

## Contradictions

Religious connection and cultural or loving connection are different settings without a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Poseidon = Node`, Node specifies the pair through connections that carry belief, ideas, or cultural meaning. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names involvement with belief, philosophy, and religion; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds clear relationships, spiritual encounters in love, and the joining of art and culture. The shared motif is meaningful connection, without evidence here about its duration or the correctness of the beliefs involved.
