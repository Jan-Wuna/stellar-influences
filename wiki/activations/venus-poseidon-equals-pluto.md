## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 189 (one-based).

Religion and world conception in a state of development.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 297; PDF page: 147 (one-based spread).

Development of spiritual art, harmonious cultural unfolding, clear developments toward peace, unfolding spiritual love, reasonable development of the female principle, preference for spiritual developments, growing spiritual harmony, and developing love of truth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Belief, culture, and spiritual love in development.
- psychology: Growing spiritual harmony and love of truth.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Spiritual love unfolds; no concrete relationship outcome is given.
- events/manifestations: Developing religion or worldview, spiritual art, and culture.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's female principle is retained as its terminology; no universal gender claim follows.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe development. Neither says that every change in belief or culture is necessarily beneficial.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Poseidon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair as a process of development. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places religion and worldview in a state of change; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) supplies spiritual art, cultural unfolding, developing love of truth, and growing spiritual harmony. This supports an evolving expression of belief and cultural meaning, while the direction and concrete outcome remain dependent on the stated example.
