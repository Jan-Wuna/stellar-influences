## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 189 (one-based).

To be hindered, wronged, separated or persecuted because of faith, religion or world conception.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 297; PDF page: 147 (one-based spread).

Old art and culture, joy in cultural tasks, disturbed peace idea, spiritual harmony in old age, clarity in love through patience, concentration on peace ideas, maturity through true love, painful love of truth, and preference for spiritual tasks

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Limits or suffering in belief alongside patient spiritual or cultural work.
- psychology: Patience, concentration, maturity, and painful love of truth.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Separation or persecution because of faith is explicitly named.
- events/manifestations: Cultural tasks, old art, and spiritual harmony in old age.
- conflicts/notes: Hindrance and mature harmony are alternatives; suffering is not established as a necessary route to wisdom.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses obstruction or persecution; ABC also describes patient clarity and harmony in old age. The favorable strand does not erase persecution.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Poseidon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through limits and sustained engagement with belief or culture. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names hindrance, separation, and persecution because of a worldview. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds disturbed peace ideas and painful love of truth, but also patience, cultural tasks, maturity, and spiritual harmony in old age. These preserve both strain and constructive endurance without making either inevitable.
