## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 189 (one-based).

The believing or faithful man. The priest or preacher. Representative of a religion, of a faith, or philosophy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 296; PDF page: 147 (one-based spread).

Mind and body in harmony, spiritual peace center, harmony in the spiritual center, art and cultural center, love of a reasonable man, personal love for an idea, personal love culture, and a man's love of truth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal embodiment or representation of belief and spiritual harmony.
- psychology: Personal love for an idea and love of truth.
- body/health: Mind and body in harmony is stated without a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: A priest, preacher, or representative of a faith is Witte's concrete example.
- events/manifestations: Spiritual, artistic, or cultural centers are ABC examples.
- conflicts/notes: The man-centered language is historical source framing; no universal male trait is established.

## Contradictions

Representing a religion and personally loving an idea differ in setting without a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Poseidon = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through a person who embodies or represents belief and cultural meaning. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives the faithful man, priest, or preacher as examples; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects personal love of an idea and truth with mind-body harmony and spiritual or cultural centers. The source's male examples remain attributed rather than defining who can hold those roles.
