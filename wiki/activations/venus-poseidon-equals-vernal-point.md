#### Witte Entry

The general faith. The general world conception.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 296; PDF page: 147 (one-based spread).

Peace culture in public, spiritual harmony in public, art and culture in public life, beauty of culture in public, wishes guided by reason in public life, and public love of truth

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Poseidon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the pair in public or collective life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names general faith and worldview; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives that setting concrete form in public peace culture, spiritual harmony, art, and love of truth. This describes publicly shared meaning without establishing unanimous belief or the authority of a particular creed.
