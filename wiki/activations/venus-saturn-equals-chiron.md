## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF chapter start: 57 (one-based; entry-specific page not established).

Your very vibration helps others to contact the deepest and most profound aspects of love, and to realize the ways they have cut themselves off from love, as well as the fears that caused them to do this. You also help others to realize the beauty of their problems and limitations, and to appreciate how these difficulties are there to help them grow.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Helping others recognize depth in love and the limits they place around it.
- psychology: Recognition of fear and self-exclusion from love.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Assisting others to see beauty in their difficulties and limitations.
- events/manifestations: No concrete event is specified beyond this interpersonal influence.
- conflicts/notes: The account is Sandbach's spiritual interpretation; vibration is not given a physical mechanism.

## Contradictions

Only one entry is present. Its claim that difficulties help growth is the source's interpretive position, not independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Saturn = Chiron`, Chiron specifies the pair through helping others recognize both the depth of love and the fears that have closed them to it. The [Sandbach entry](#sandbach-entry) connects this influence with appreciation of problems and limitations as opportunities for growth. This is a spiritual account of interpersonal help; the entry supplies neither a bodily mechanism for vibration nor a guarantee that every difficulty produces growth.
