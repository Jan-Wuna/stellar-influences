## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 178 (one-based).

Flirtations. Hindrances and obstacles in love. Illicit intercourse. Illegitimate birth. He or she being forsaken.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Saturn`
- Source reference: printed page `274`; PDF page `136` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Disturbed inner peace, I mature through love, I love perseverance, I disturb harmony, my harmony is disturbed, my artistic seriousness, my problems in love, my beauty in old age, and my preference for hardness.

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 183; entry code: 0613.

Emotional inhibitions or inhibited desires, the state of being unsatisfied in love, irritability, jealousy, reserve, urge to seek solitude. Separation in love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 58 (one-based; exact activator paragraph and adjacent boundary visually checked).

The desire to share spiritual love with the world. Difficulties in love grow one spiritually. A career combining practicality with beauty. One receives a lack of appreciation for one’s career work, or that appreciation comes later in life. A deep and enduring love is what brings security to one’s life.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `196`

#### Munkasey Entry

Maturity and understanding of how you can relax amidst the demands of a pressure building life-style; enhanced needs to be noticed for your enduring financial contributions to important causes or efforts.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal inhibition or difficult love, with possible maturity and enduring security.
- psychology: Dissatisfaction, irritability, jealousy, reserve, and an urge for solitude.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Obstacles, being forsaken, separation, or deep enduring love.
- events/manifestations: A practical and beautiful career, delayed appreciation, and recognition for financial contributions.
- conflicts/notes: Illicit and illegitimate are historical source labels; ABC distinguishes maturing through love from disturbing harmony.

## Contradictions

Separation and enduring love are different possibilities. Sandbach's career appreciation may be absent or delayed; Munkasey's wish for recognition does not guarantee recognition.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Saturn = MC`, MC specifies the pair as a personally felt problem of love, security, and appreciation. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) emphasize obstacles, inhibition, being forsaken, or separation. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) also allows deep enduring love and a career combining practicality with beauty, though appreciation may come late or be absent. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds maturity under pressure and a wish for lasting contributions to be noticed. The clear [ABC wording](#abc-entry) retains love problems and perseverance; it does not establish assured reputation or success.
