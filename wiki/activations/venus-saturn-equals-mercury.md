## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 178 (one-based).

To think of hindrances in love. Thoughts of parting (divorce). Separation from acquaintances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 274; PDF page: 136 (one-based spread).

Reports on art successes, serious thoughts on art, speech about love as a task, artistic maturity in speech, discussing love problems, separation of love in youth, problematic peace language, and preference for serious language

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 182; entry code: 0605.

Sober thinking, visualization of stark reality, narrowminded or selfish in love. Considering separation in love or in marriage.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF chapter start: 57 (one-based; entry-specific page not established).

To communicate in a beautifully simple way. A mind that appreciates pro found ideas. To think about the problems of relating and romance.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `196`

#### Munkasey Entry

Studying the effects of beauty and charm, and how to enhance these qualities within you; delayed enjoyment from your experiences with education; creating lasting and enjoyable communications; messages of lasting love.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Serious thought and communication about love, art, and separation.
- psychology: Sober realism, appreciation of profound ideas, or narrowness in love.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Thoughts of divorce or parting; messages of lasting love.
- events/manifestations: Artistic discussion, delayed educational enjoyment, and lasting communications.
- conflicts/notes: Simple or sober expression is not necessarily diplomatic or warm.

## Contradictions

Contemplating separation and communicating lasting love are different possibilities. The sources do not reduce sober thinking to either one.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Saturn = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through serious thought and communication about relating. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) name hindrances and thoughts of separation, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds simple expression and appreciation of profound ideas. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places that seriousness in art and discussion of love as a task. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) also allows lasting messages of love and delayed enjoyment of education, so restraint in expression need not mean a wish to part.
