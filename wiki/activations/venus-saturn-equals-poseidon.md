## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 178 (one-based).

Disturbed harmony in dealing with wisdom. Passing harmony or disturbed harmony among educators or between educators and students.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 275; PDF page: 136 (one-based spread).

Tasks in art and culture, joy in serious insights, recognizing peace tasks, patience and reason in love, spiritual harmony in old age, disturbed peace idea, disturbed love of truth, problematic art idea, and maturing harmony and clarity

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Serious engagement with wisdom, education, peace, and culture.
- psychology: Patience, reason in love, serious insight, and maturing clarity.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Passing or disturbed harmony among educators or between educators and students.
- events/manifestations: Cultural tasks and spiritual harmony in old age.
- conflicts/notes: The scan supports disturbed rather than dead or lost, and reason rather than courage.

## Contradictions

Disturbed educational harmony and maturing clarity are different possibilities. The favorable strand does not make conflict evidence of wisdom.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Saturn = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair in relation to wisdom, education, and cultural meaning. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names disturbed harmony among educators or with students. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) retains disturbed peace and love of truth, but also patience, reason, serious insight, and maturing harmony. The reading therefore allows difficult exchange around ideas alongside thoughtful cultural work and clarity developed over time.
