## Source Entries

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/SATURN With Itself`
- Source page: `198`

#### Munkasey Entry

An increased tendency to feel restricted or curtailed by the commitments you have made to console or provide for others; increased realism about the status of affections and appreciation received; smaller payments for artists.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Commitments felt as restrictions, with a realistic appraisal of affection.
- psychology: Feeling curtailed by obligations and recognizing what appreciation is received.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Commitments to console or provide for others may feel burdensome.
- events/manifestations: Smaller payments for artists.
- conflicts/notes: A single entry states restriction and realism; it does not establish the value or duration of the commitment.

## Contradictions

Restriction and increased realism are different aspects of this one entry, not conflicting testimony from separate sources.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Saturn = Saturn`, the repeated Saturn specifies the pair through the cost and limits of commitments. The [Munkasey entry](#munkasey-entry) links feeling curtailed while providing for or consoling others with a more realistic view of affection and appreciation received. Smaller payments for artists give a concrete material example of reduced reward; the entry does not say that care for others is inherently unrewarding.
