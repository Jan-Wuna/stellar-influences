## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 178 (one-based).

Transiently united or separated. Grass widower. Widower.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 274; PDF page: 136 (one-based spread).

Inhibited personal love, disturbances of a man's harmony, bodily love problems, artistic maturity of a man, love of an older man, personal harmony in old age, problematic love of a man, and preference for a center for older people

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 182; entry code: 0603.

Unsatisfied desire in love, inhibitions in sex expression, an unhealthy expression of the sex urge, weak powers of procreation. A lonely life, separation in love or marriage. Organic troubles.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 57 (one-based; exact activator paragraph and adjacent boundary visually checked).

One feels it is important to be sincere in one’s values, and is looking for mean ingful and enduring love. To be aware of hardships and problems in all relationships, including romantic ones.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/SATURN with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `196`

#### Munkasey Entry

Brings distinguished, older people who are serious artists into your life; your tastes remain simple but you still enjoy extravagant displays of beauty; brings honors in later life from your artistic or musical skills or disciplines.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personally experienced limits in love alongside sincere values and mature artistic expression.
- psychology: Unsatisfied desire and awareness of relationship hardships.
- body/health: Ebertin's sexual, procreative, and organic-trouble statements are historical claims, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Temporary union, separation, loneliness, and a search for enduring love.
- events/manifestations: Older artistic contacts and honors later in life through artistic or musical skill.
- conflicts/notes: Source examples are male-centered; the scanned Alten-Zentrum is a center for older people.

## Contradictions

Wanting enduring love does not establish obtaining it. Later artistic honor and relationship hardship concern different parts of the entries.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Saturn = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through personally experienced limits in love and an interest in sincerity and endurance. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) name separation and loneliness, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes meaningful values and a search for enduring love. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) also allows harmony in old age, and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds older artistic contacts and later honors through skill. The bodily and procreative claims remain attributed historical doctrine, while these male-centered examples do not define universal traits.
