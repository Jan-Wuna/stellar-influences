## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 178 (one-based).

Disharmonies in an otherwise great love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 275; PDF page: 136 (one-based spread).

Inhibited power of love, disturbed peace power, enormous artistic tasks, harmonizing influence through seriousness, love of concentrating forces, love's influence in separation, powerful peace problems, and the maturing influence of art

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong love or influence encountering disharmony and inhibition.
- psychology: Seriousness can have a harmonizing influence.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Disharmonies in an otherwise great love; love's influence in separation.
- events/manifestations: Large artistic tasks and powerful peace problems.
- conflicts/notes: Strength of love and harmony are not equivalent in the stated evidence.

## Contradictions

Witte explicitly retains great love despite disharmony. ABC's harmonizing seriousness is another possibility rather than assurance that strength resolves difficulty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Saturn = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through strong love or influence meeting restriction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly allows disharmony within an otherwise great love. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds inhibited love power and powerful peace problems, but also harmonizing seriousness and art's maturing influence. The common point is the coexistence of strength and difficulty, with concentrated effort or artistic work among the possible expressions.
