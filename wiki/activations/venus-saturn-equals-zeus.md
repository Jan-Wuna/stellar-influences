## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 178 (one-based).

Forced separation of love. Illegitimate procreation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 275; PDF page: 136 (one-based spread).

Beginning losses of harmony, fiery love in old age, achievements disturbed through love, concentration on harmonious leadership, creative artistic tasks, problematic procreation in love, mature art creations, and creative peace tasks

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed or creative activity amid loss of harmony and separation.
- psychology: Concentration on harmonious leadership.
- body/health: Procreation is source doctrine; no medical mechanism or diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Forced separation, problematic procreation, or fiery love in old age.
- events/manifestations: Creative art and peace tasks; achievements disturbed through love.
- conflicts/notes: Illegitimate is Witte's historical label; Liebes-Zeugung means procreation, not denial.

## Contradictions

Forced separation and mature creative activity are distinct manifestations. The constructive strand does not cancel losses of harmony.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Saturn = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair through directed and creative activity under relational strain. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names forced separation and uses the historical label illegitimate procreation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) combines losses of harmony and disturbed achievements with harmonious leadership, mature art, creative peace tasks, and fiery love in old age. These preserve both disruption and creation without recasting the source's procreation phrase as denial.
