## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 179 (one-based).

Sudden harmony and friendship with many.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 277; PDF page: 137 (one-based spread).

Successful peace event, art and technology in science, new knowledge of art, new knowledge about love, sudden expansion of harmony, exciting love experiences, much excitement in love, and preference for new knowledge

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden expansion of friendship, harmony, or knowledge.
- psychology: Interest in new knowledge and excitement in love.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Sudden harmony and friendship with many.
- events/manifestations: Peace events, art and technology in science, and new artistic knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: New knowledge of art does not mean artificial knowledge.

## Contradictions

Both entries describe widening harmony; ABC also names excitement, without specifying the duration of the friendships.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through widening contact and discovery. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names sudden harmony and friendship with many. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects that expansion with new knowledge of love and art, science involving art and technology, and exciting love experiences. The interpretation centers on broader participation and discovery without establishing how long the harmony lasts.
