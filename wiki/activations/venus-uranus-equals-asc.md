## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 179 (one-based).

Flirtations. Betrothal or marriage. Excitement about the love of others. Favors for others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 276; PDF page: 137 (one-based spread).

Exciting beauty as a partner, dynamic love partner, peace event at the location, tense peace in the environment, harmonious technical environment, new love for the partner, place for art events, and preference for tensions in the surroundings

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source reference: printed pp. 185–186; one-based PDF pp. 187–188, coded entry 0624. Footnote 34 belongs to the separate Moon entry 0616 and is excluded from the Asc paragraph.

Demonstration of love in the presence of others. A sudden meeting or contact, a hasty engagement.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 61, Venus/Uranus, Ascendant paragraph.

To identify with that which is strange and beautiful. An unusually compelling personality. The ability to find unique ways of sharing with others, or unexpected points in common. To freely express love toward other people.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `200`

#### Munkasey Entry

Concentrating on innovative themes in music, art or finance; a heightened show of affection to those who have unusual talents in artistic fields; the unusual delights you receive from enjoying your personal space.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Unexpected attraction and free or unusual sharing in encounters and surroundings.
- psychology: Identification with strange beauty and unexpected points in common.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied by the Asc entries; the removed conception/birth footnote belongs to Ebertin's separate Moon entry.
- social/relationship: Flirtation, engagement or marriage, free expression of love, and affection for unusual talents.
- events/manifestations: Sudden contact, innovative art or finance, and unusual enjoyment of personal space.
- conflicts/notes: The scanned ABC says harmonious technical environment, not Hamburg. Ebertin's Asc sentence continues across printed pp. 185–186; footnote 34 is attached to Moon 0616 and supplies no Asc claim.

## Contradictions

Hasty engagement and free expression do not establish stable or unstable closeness. Personal space is another stated source of enjoyment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Asc`, Asc specifies the pair through encounters, attraction, and the surroundings. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) name flirtation, sudden contact, or engagement. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) adds unusual ways of sharing and unexpected common ground, while [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) links affection to unusual artistic talents and enjoyment of personal space. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes a dynamic partner and harmonious technical surroundings. Ebertin's conception/birth footnote belongs to the separate Moon entry and contributes no Asc meaning.
