## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 179 (one-based).

Sudden love with quick marriage. Surprises that occur in an association.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 277; PDF page: 137 (one-based spread).

Tensions in a love community, peaceful events in a community, shared peace reform, shared artistic experiences, harmony in a dynamic community, and harmonious rhythm in a community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Surprise, change, and dynamic harmony within an association.
- psychology: No distinct personal disposition is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Sudden love with quick marriage; tension within a love community.
- events/manifestations: Shared art, peace reform, and community events.
- conflicts/notes: The scan reads peaceful events, not fraternal achievements.

## Contradictions

Tensions and harmonious rhythm are both explicit communal possibilities. Quick marriage does not establish its duration or quality.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through surprise and dynamic activity in a union or community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names sudden love, quick marriage, and surprises in an association. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) carries the communal setting into shared art, peace reform, tension, and harmonious rhythm. The common setting is collective experience, with tension and harmony retained as alternatives.
