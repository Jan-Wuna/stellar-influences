## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 179 (one-based).

Manifestation of base sensuality. Perversities. Surprisingly being connected with persons of lower kind. Harmony with servants.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 277; PDF page: 137 (one-based spread).

Dangerous love experience, artistic impulses from the past, sudden danger to peace, lack of tension in love, disadvantages through new love, sudden love sorrow, exciting service in love, and unrest through criminal wishes

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Disadvantage, danger, or sorrow in an unexpected love experience.
- psychology: Unrest, lack of tension in love, or sudden sorrow.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Disadvantages through new love; harmony with servants is a separate historical example.
- events/manifestations: Sudden danger to peace and artistic impulses from the past.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's class and sexual judgments remain attributed and are not standards for judging people.

## Contradictions

Harmony with servants is not evidence of the adverse sexual or criminal claims. The past artistic strand is another application.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through sudden difficulty, disadvantage, or sorrow in love. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) names dangerous experience, new love bringing disadvantage, and sudden love sorrow, while also retaining art impulses from the past. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes harmony with servants alongside class and sexual judgments that remain historical source language. These different examples should not be turned into a judgment that a person's social position implies danger or wrongdoing.
