## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 179 (one-based).

To experience sudden goodwill from superiors, leading persons or authorities. Author of erotic stories or songs.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 277; PDF page: 137 (one-based spread).

Special love experience, dynamic artistic authority, great love of technology, love for the new boss, new great love, tensions through artistic superiority, state peace impulses, state art event, and important artistic impulses

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden favor, love, or artistic activity in relation to authority.
- psychology: Love of technology; no fuller disposition is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Goodwill from superiors and love for a new boss.
- events/manifestations: Erotic stories or songs, state art events, and important artistic impulses.
- conflicts/notes: Goodwill and artistic superiority are source examples, not proof of personal worth.

## Contradictions

Goodwill from leaders and tension through artistic superiority are different possibilities. Authorship of erotic material is a separate listed expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair in contact with authority or notable artistic activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names sudden goodwill from superiors and the author of erotic stories or songs. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds dynamic artistic authority, love for a new boss, technological interest, and state artistic or peace events. These link sudden favor and creative distinction while retaining tension through artistic superiority as another possibility.
