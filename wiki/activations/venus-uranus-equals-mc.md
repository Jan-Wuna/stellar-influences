## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 179 (one-based).

One’s own refined feeling of love. To be suddenly in love. Easily obtain favors.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 276; PDF page: 137 (one-based spread).

Soulful new art, peaceful impulses of the soul, I experience love, my harmonious impulses, I love excitement, my art is dynamic, my tension in love, my new love, my preference for rhythm, and new harmony of the soul

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 186; entry code: 0625.

A high degree of excitability in love life, quickly getting to like someone, inclination to experience the adventures of love. Engagement.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF chapter start: 60 (one-based; entry-specific page not established).

Spiritually one is manifesting the ability to love all things for their beautiful uniqueness, and to find beauty in the most strange and unexpected places. One’s ca reer may involve artistic experimentation.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `200`

#### Munkasey Entry

Developing an appreciation for the unusual in the worlds of music, art, and finance; family origins rooted in the pleasures of being a rebel; helps to focus on and modernize your personal stylistic tastes as you mature through life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personally experienced sudden love and appreciation of unusual beauty.
- psychology: Excitability, quickly liking someone, and enjoyment of uniqueness.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Sudden love, engagement, and easily obtained favors.
- events/manifestations: Artistic experimentation, changing personal taste, and unusual music, art, or finance.
- conflicts/notes: The entries do not establish career collapse, public scandal, or a particular political outlook.

## Contradictions

Personal excitement and artistic experimentation share novelty but do not imply the same outward event. No direct source disagreement is stated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = MC`, MC specifies the pair through one's own sudden attraction and changing sense of beauty. [Witte](#witte-entry) and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) name suddenly falling in love or engagement, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) joins personal excitement, rhythm, and new inward harmony. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) places beauty in unexpected things and allows artistic experimentation in a career; [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds maturing personal style and appreciation of unusual art or finance. The evidence does not supply the old summary's public reversals.
