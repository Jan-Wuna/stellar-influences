## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 179 (one-based).

A woman fastidious in love. Labor-pains. Sudden surprising harmony with the public or female persons. Social dances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 276; PDF page: 137 (one-based spread).

Exciting beauty of a woman, eventful love of a woman, emotionally full tension in love, a woman's love experience, a woman's new love, sudden feelings of love, rhythmic art of a woman, rhythmic folk art, and benevolent impulses of feeling

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 185; entry code: 0616.

Sudden excitability in love life (sudden emotion, hysterical outbursts), convulsions. Birth.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF chapter start: 60 (one-based; entry-specific page not established).

Feelings of love and attraction that may suddenly arise and also disappear quickly. The emotional need to pursue one’s own values freely and independently. To be impressionable to unusual beauty. The emotional need to be able to share freely and openly in a relationship.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `200`

#### Munkasey Entry

Unusual forms of beauty and art shown with compassion and sensitivity; appreciation of novel art forms created by ordinary people; uncertainty over how to present innovative charm or affection; novel forms of romance.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden feeling and sensitivity to unusual love or beauty.
- psychology: Attraction may arise and vanish quickly; a need for free sharing.
- body/health: Labor pains, convulsions, and birth are attributed historical associations, not diagnoses or predictions.
- social/relationship: Surprising harmony with the public or women; uncertainty in expressing innovative affection.
- events/manifestations: Social dances, rhythmic folk art, and novel romance.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered and hysterical-outburst language remains historical source wording.

## Contradictions

Free affection and uncertainty about presenting it are distinct possibilities. Sudden feelings do not establish lasting attachment or its absence.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through sudden feeling and responsiveness to unusual beauty. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) describes attraction that may arise and disappear quickly and a need for free sharing. [Witte](#witte-entry) and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) place harmony in public contact, dance, and rhythmic art. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds sensitivity and uncertainty in presenting affection. The women's examples and bodily associations in Witte and [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) remain historical doctrine, not a diagnosis or a prediction.
