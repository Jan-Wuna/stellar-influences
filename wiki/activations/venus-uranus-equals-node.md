## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 179 (one-based).

To enter into love adventures. Harmonious unions made by surprise.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 276; PDF page: 137 (one-based spread).

Tense peace contacts, eventful love connection, connection with an art event, sudden harmony in a relationship, connections with rhythmic art, restless love connection, and preference for new connections

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 185; entry code: 0623.

Intensification of feeling and instinct leads to new associations.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF chapter start: 60 (one-based; entry-specific page not established).

The need to evolve by appreciating more fully one’s own individual uniqueness and the special gifts one has. To be able to be uninhibited in the expression of love supports one’s spiritual evolution. Seeking out groups in which can be valued as a spe cial person.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `200`

#### Munkasey Entry

Connections to others who have innovative and unusual forms of music or art they are developing; presentations showing unusual forms of entertainment; attention getting methods which use disruptive forms of affection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: New associations formed through intensified feeling and unusual creative interests.
- psychology: Appreciating one's uniqueness and seeking a group that values it.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Love adventures and harmonious unions made by surprise.
- events/manifestations: Art or entertainment contacts and attention-seeking disruptive affection.
- conflicts/notes: Restlessness is stated, but the sources do not establish the duration of all ties.

## Contradictions

Surprising harmony and disruptive affection are separate expressions. A group valuing uniqueness is Sandbach's sought setting, not a guaranteed result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Node`, Node specifies the pair through new associations and unexpected connections. [Witte](#witte-entry) names love adventures and surprising harmony; [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) links new associations to intensified feeling. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) and [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) add art and entertainment contacts, including restless or disruptive affection. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) supplies the desire to be valued for one's uniqueness. These describe both attraction into a circle and the varied ways affection is shown within it.
