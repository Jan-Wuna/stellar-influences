## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 179 (one-based).

Harmonious development which begins suddenly.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 277; PDF page: 137 (one-based spread).

Dynamic unfolding of love, development of a new love, developing harmonious rhythms, harmonious technical development, new artistic developments, preference for technical evolution, growing peace reform, and change through peace events

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 185; entry code: 0622.

high degree of excitability. Blind love.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF chapter start: 60 (one-based; entry-specific page not established).

An obsessive attraction to the unusual, strange, or bizarre. To feel compelled to be totally open in the display of affection. One need to avoid using one’s own unique or strange attractiveness to gain power over others.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `200`

#### Munkasey Entry

Accusations, attack, or disruptions when you seek enjoyment or satisfaction; obsession with unusual forms of pleasing others; fanatical appreciation shown for haphazardly offered forms of enjoyment or affection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden development intensified into compelling attraction or appreciation.
- psychology: High excitability, blind love, and obsession with unusual beauty.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Compelled openness, power through attractiveness, or obsessive pleasing.
- events/manifestations: Harmonious development, technical or artistic change, and disruption when seeking enjoyment.
- conflicts/notes: Sudden harmonious growth and the adverse power strand remain distinct.

## Contradictions

Witte and ABC emphasize development; Sandbach and Munkasey emphasize compulsion and power or disruption. Development must not be assumed beneficial in every case.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair through intensified development and attraction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names harmonious growth beginning suddenly, and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds unfolding love, art, and technical change. [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry) retains blind love, while [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) warns about obsession and using attractiveness for power. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) adds disruption and compulsive appreciation. The development strand therefore remains alongside the possibility that intensity overwhelms judgment or freedom.
