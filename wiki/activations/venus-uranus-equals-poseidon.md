## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 179 (one-based).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 277; PDF page: 137 (one-based spread).

Dynamic peace idea, peace culture and technology, impulses toward spiritual harmony, harmonious impulses of reason, new art idea, sudden harmony and clarity, art, culture and technology, tensions through love of truth, and surprising clarity in love

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Unexpected spiritual or philosophical harmony and clarity.
- psychology: Reasoned impulses and tension through love of truth.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Surprising harmony with others in spirit or worldview.
- events/manifestations: Peace ideas, new art, culture, and technology.
- conflicts/notes: Sudden clarity does not establish the truth of a particular worldview.

## Contradictions

Harmony and tension through love of truth are different listed possibilities, with no direct disagreement between the entries.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through surprising clarity and shared spiritual or philosophical understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names sudden harmony with others in that realm; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds peaceful ideas, reason, new art, and technology. Love of truth may also create tension, so shared insight is a possibility rather than proof that all disagreement has ended.
