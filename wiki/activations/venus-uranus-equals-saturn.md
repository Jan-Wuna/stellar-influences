## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 179 (one-based).

Sudden interrupted or separated love union. One’s tend to break up.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 277; PDF page: 137 (one-based spread).

Burdened peace reform, disturbed love event, love impulses in old age, new problems in love, sudden separation in love, sudden harmony problems, rhythmic artistic tasks, and tensions through disturbed peace

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Ebertin Entry

- Source page: 185; entry code: 0620.

Controlled sensuality, suppressed tensions in love life, passing sexual and emotional inhibitions. Awakening from love illusions, estrangement, separation. (Difficult birth.)

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Sandbach Entry

- PDF page: 60 (one-based; exact activator paragraph and adjacent boundary visually checked).

Shyness about expressing one’s own individual values. A fear of being open when relating to people. To love the unusual, but to fear being criticized or looked down on for it. Having to know someone a long time before one becomes open in relating.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `VENUS/URANUS with Planets and Points`
- Source page: `200`

#### Munkasey Entry

Moderation shown when demonstrating unusual methods of beautifying or embellishing self; caution taken with innovative forms of gratification; perpetuating new forms of expression involving art, finance, or music.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restriction or interruption in unusual love or creative expression.
- psychology: Suppressed tension, shyness, fear of criticism, and cautious self-expression.
- body/health: Ebertin's parenthesized difficult birth is historical doctrine, not a medical prediction.
- social/relationship: Sudden separation, estrangement, or needing long acquaintance before openness.
- events/manifestations: Moderate innovation and sustaining new forms in art, finance, or music.
- conflicts/notes: Actual Witte PDF179 itself prints One's tend to break up; the grammatical defect is retained, with no missing subject reconstructed.

## Contradictions

Sudden separation and cautious preservation of new expression are different outcomes. The evidence does not establish mature commitment as the default.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through limits on openness or an interruption of love. [Witte](#witte-entry), [Ebertin](#ebertin-entry), and the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) name separation, estrangement, and suppressed tension. [Sandbach](#sandbach-entry) explains another form of restraint: fear of criticism and a long time needed before sharing freely. [Munkasey](#munkasey-entry) also allows moderation that preserves new artistic or musical expression. These retain both inhibited relating and cautious continuity without promising an enduring bond.
