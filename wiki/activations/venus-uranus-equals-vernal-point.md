## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 179, Vernal Point row under `Venus + Uranus`.

Mating time. Rutting season.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 276; one-based PDF p. 137, Vernal Point row under `Venus + Uranus`.

> Aufregende Schönheit in der Öffentlichkeit, dynamische Kunst in der Öffentlichkeit, harmonische Spannungen im öffentlichen Leben, Kunst-Ereignis in der Öffentlichkeit, plötzlicher Frieden in der Öffentlichkeit, Vorliebe für öffentliche Spannungen

Editorial translation: Exciting beauty in public; dynamic art in public; harmonious tensions in public life; an art event in public; sudden peace in public; a preference for public tensions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public expressions of exciting beauty, dynamic art and harmony under tension in ABC; a mating-season image in Witte.
- psychology: ABC names a preference for public tensions; it does not give a complete psychological profile.
- body/health: Witte names mating activity without a medical conclusion.
- social/relationship: Public harmony, sudden peace and tensions in public life; Witte's separate reproductive image remains attributed to that source.
- events/manifestations: A public art event, dynamic art and sudden public peace in ABC; mating activity in Witte.
- conflicts/notes: The two entries differ materially in scope. ABC's public art and peace wording does not explain Witte's reproductive example.

## Contradictions

ABC emphasizes beauty, art, peace and harmony in public life, including tension and a preference for it. Witte supplies a reproductive image. These are distinct expressions in the exact oriented entries; neither passage establishes that public art or tension leads to mating activity.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Vernal Point`, the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places the pair's exciting beauty and dynamic art in public life. Art events, harmonious tension and sudden peace connect attraction and harmony with an eventful public setting. Its preference for public tensions keeps tension within the supported range rather than reducing the formula to effortless agreement.

The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives a separate, narrow reproductive image: “mating time” and “rutting season.” Its scope remains distinct from ABC's public art and peace themes; neither entry predicts a particular encounter or supplies a general psychological profile.
