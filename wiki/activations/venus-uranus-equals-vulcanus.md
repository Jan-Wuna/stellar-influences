## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden passionate love. Suddenly appearing powerful unity or enthusiasm, power and influence through concord. Be mightily in love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Uranus`
- Source reference: printed page `277`; PDF page `137` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Dynamic strength of love, influential peace reform, influence of a new love, an enormous artistic event, a powerful peace event, a mighty art event, sudden violence in love, loving the tension of forces, and preference for technical energies.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sudden affection or concord taking on force and influence.
- psychology: powerful enthusiasm and passionate involvement.
- body/health: passion is described without a specific health claim.
- social/relationship: strong unity and influence through concord.
- events/manifestations: a new love, influential peace reform, or a large artistic event.
- conflicts/notes: ABC explicitly includes sudden Liebes-Gewalt, rendered as violence in love; it is a particular adverse possibility, not a universal claim.

## Contradictions

Passion, concord, and artistic influence coexist with ABC's sudden violence in love. Witte's power through concord and ABC's violent possibility must remain distinct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus gives sudden love or concord a powerful presence: affection can arrive with intense enthusiasm, while unity becomes a means of influence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 179, joins passion with power through concord. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 277 (PDF p. 137), extends this force into peace reform and substantial artistic events but also names sudden violence in love. The favorable and adverse expressions therefore remain visible within the same intense range.
