## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To procreate in love’s ecstasy. Joy out of installing machines. Comfort through machines and technical installations, to produce harmony in this manner.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Uranus`
- Source reference: printed page `277`; PDF page `137` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Dynamic artistic creations, a fiery experience of love, harmonious leadership technique, creative artistic impulses, impulses toward an artistic direction, new aims in art, sudden procreation in love, and creative impulses for peace.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: creative or productive expression of excitement and harmony.
- psychology: fiery involvement and pleasure in making something work.
- body/health: Witte names procreation in ecstasy; no fertility prognosis is established.
- social/relationship: an intense love experience is one expression.
- events/manifestations: artistic creation and direction, or comfort produced by machines and installations.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's sudden procreation in love agrees with Witte's procreation example; neither establishes a reproductive prediction.

## Contradictions

The procreation and artistic-creation examples overlap. Witte's comfort through machines is an additional productive setting, without a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Uranus = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair's excitement and harmony through creation or productive activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 179, links ecstatic procreation with the less obvious pleasure of installing machines that produce comfort and harmony. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 277 (PDF p. 137), adds sudden procreation in love, creative art, new artistic aims, and harmonious leadership technique. Together these support making or generating something through enthusiasm, with reproductive examples retained as source doctrine.
