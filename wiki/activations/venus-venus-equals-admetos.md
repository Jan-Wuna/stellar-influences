## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Connected in love and friendship with a few. Picky, but true and deep in love and friendship. Unselfish. End of a love. Meditation, turning inwards. Death of female persons. Repressed affections. Frigidity in love. Heartlessness. Death of love. Without sensation. Limited feeling.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: selective, inward, or restricted affection.
- psychology: devotion to a few, meditation, or repressed feeling.
- body/health: historical frigidity and death language is attributed, not a diagnosis or prediction.
- social/relationship: deep loyalty to a small circle, or the ending of love.
- events/manifestations: withdrawal, meditation, and loss appear beside fidelity.
- conflicts/notes: The historical death and frigidity language remains attributed; Heartlessness is printed clearly in the source.

## Contradictions

The entry juxtaposes deep fidelity with restricted or extinguished affection. These are alternative expressions, not proof that devotion necessarily ends in loss.

## Derived Synthesis

Admetos specifies `Venus/Venus` through concentration and limitation of affection: love may be selective, deep, and unselfish toward a few, or it may turn inward and become repressed or limited. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 175, also names endings; its historical death and frigidity language remains attributed and does not justify predicting a death or diagnosing a person.
