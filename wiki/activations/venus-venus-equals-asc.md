## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Lovable acquaintances. To be treated well by others. Others who long for the native. Personal love connections and love unions. To show evidence of love. Nice appearance. Peace in the immediate vicinity. Harmonious relations to the place. Harmonious surroundings. Peaceful place.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: affection and harmony encountered through people and surroundings.
- psychology: a wish or occasion to show love.
- body/health: appearance is mentioned without a health claim.
- social/relationship: kind treatment, personal love connections, and unions.
- events/manifestations: pleasant appearance and a peaceful immediate environment.
- conflicts/notes: the entry does not specify whether harmony is enduring.

## Contradictions

The entry consistently concerns agreeable contact and surroundings; its single-source scope does not establish independent agreement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies `Venus/Venus` through the immediate encounter: being treated kindly, showing love, or forming personal love connections. In the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 174, the same agreeable quality belongs to appearance and to a peaceful place or surrounding environment. This joins interpersonal warmth with local harmony without promising a permanent relationship.
