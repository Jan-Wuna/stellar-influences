## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Lover’s bliss. Rapture of joy. Goldsmiths. Well-balanced finances. Plump woman. Happiness in love. To enjoy money. Legal peace and justice.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: enjoyment and balance in love, money, and justice.
- psychology: rapture and happiness.
- body/health: “Plump woman” is a historical bodily image, not a health inference.
- social/relationship: happiness in love and legal peace.
- events/manifestations: enjoying money, balanced finances, and goldsmithing.
- conflicts/notes: financial balance is a named expression, not a promise of wealth.

## Contradictions

Joy, financial balance, and legal peace are compatible expressions in one entry; there is no second source for comparison.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter specifies `Venus/Venus` through enjoyment that can be emotional, material, or social: lovers' happiness, pleasure in money, and balanced finances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 174, also names goldsmiths and legal peace or justice, extending pleasure toward valued objects and orderly relations. Its gendered body image does not support a health conclusion.
