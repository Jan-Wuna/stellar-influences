## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

The blood-relationship. Intimate friendship. Sex love. To act with devotion, out of love for the cause. To become intimate. Sex activity. Joy of action. Acting like a maternal female. Passionate activity. Kinship.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: affection expressed through intimacy, kinship, and devoted action.
- psychology: joy in acting for something loved.
- body/health: sexual activity is named, without a medical claim.
- social/relationship: intimate friendship, sexual love, and kinship.
- events/manifestations: passionate activity and devotion to a cause.
- conflicts/notes: the maternal gender image is source wording, not a universal role.

## Contradictions

Kinship, sexuality, and devotion are different forms of active attachment within one entry; they are not interchangeable relationships.

## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies `Venus/Venus` through active attachment: becoming intimate, sexual expression, and acting with devotion to a loved cause. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 174, also places kinship and blood relationship beside friendship. The common thread is affection expressed in involvement and action, while the entry keeps these different kinds of bond distinct.
