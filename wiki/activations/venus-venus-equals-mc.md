#### Witte Entry

One’s own personal affections. Love sentiments. One’s wishes and hopes. One’s own personal attraction. To have the power of attraction. To be attractive. To be fond of something. Peaceful minded. Feeling balanced. Sense of attraction. Sympathies.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: personal affection, attraction, and inner balance.
- psychology: wishes, hopes, sympathies, and a peaceful mind.
- body/health: attractiveness is named without a medical condition.
- social/relationship: one's own affection and power of attraction.
- events/manifestations: fondness and a sense of balance.
- conflicts/notes: the entry concerns personal experience, not professional status.

## Contradictions

The entry's affection, attraction, and balance are compatible; it supplies no competing interpretation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Venus = MC`, MC specifies the pair as one's own felt preferences and attractions: love sentiments, hopes, sympathies, and fondness become personal experiences. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 174, joins the capacity to attract with peaceful-mindedness and balance. This supports an inward account of affection rather than importing an occupational meaning of MC.
