## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Venus = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through thinking about love and art, meeting others, and expressing taste in arrangements or beautification. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 174, links acquaintanceship with rhythm and aesthetic judgment, while also naming the salesman. That role remains a concrete association, without making all pleasant contact a transaction.
