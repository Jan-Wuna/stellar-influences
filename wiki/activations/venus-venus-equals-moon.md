## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

The feeling. The love. The loving woman. Hours of love. Pregnancy. Motherhood. Peaceful hour. The love or affection of women. The love of a people. The people’s peace or harmony.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: felt affection and peaceful experience at personal or collective scale.
- psychology: loving feeling and a peaceful hour.
- body/health: pregnancy and motherhood are source associations, not a prognosis.
- social/relationship: affection of women and love of a people are listed.
- events/manifestations: hours of love and communal peace or harmony.
- conflicts/notes: gendered and reproductive images remain attributed.

## Contradictions

The entry moves between individual feeling and a people's harmony; these scales differ without contradicting one another.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Venus = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through the lived feeling of affection: hours of love and peaceful experience can concern a person or a wider people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 174, also names pregnancy, motherhood, and women's affection. Those are its historical images, not grounds for predicting pregnancy or assigning a universal gender role.
