## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Rapture. Sensitive, renouncing, false or unhappy love. Deceptive hopes. Hidden and secret feelings, sensations or sentiments. Wrong sex instinct. Renouncing love. Misfortune in love. Untrue love. Abnormality. Secret love. Disappointment in love. To be deceived in one’s affections.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: sensitive or hidden affection vulnerable to deception and disappointment.
- psychology: rapture, renunciation, and deceptive hopes.
- body/health: historical “abnormality” and sexual labels are not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: secret, unhappy, or untrue love.
- events/manifestations: disappointment or being deceived in affection.
- conflicts/notes: stigmatizing source terminology remains attributed.

## Contradictions

Rapture and disappointment are both supplied by the one entry. The text does not establish that one must lead to the other.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Venus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through sensitive, concealed, or renouncing affection in which hopes may prove deceptive. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 175, holds rapture beside secret feelings, disappointment, and untrue love; the useful connection is between intense feeling and uncertain trust. Its historical sexual and “abnormality” labels do not establish a medical or moral verdict.
