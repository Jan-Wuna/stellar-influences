## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Harmonious development or transformation. Development of a wish, of a hope. Beginning and harmonious development of love and affection. To give in. Consideration. Pleasant change. Thinking of the well-being of others. Glandular development.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: affection and hopes developing through considerate change.
- psychology: consideration and concern for others' well-being.
- body/health: glandular development is a historical source association only.
- social/relationship: the beginning and growth of love, and willingness to give in.
- events/manifestations: pleasant change and development of wishes.
- conflicts/notes: the text does not make growth depend on domination or crisis.

## Contradictions

The single entry's harmonious change and consideration are compatible; no competing source is present.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Venus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair as affection in development: wishes and hopes take shape, love begins or grows, and change can be pleasant. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 175, connects this process with consideration, yielding, and concern for others' well-being. Its separate glandular association remains attributed rather than serving as a health inference.
