## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Inclinations for mental or spiritual things. Platonic love. Faith. Worldview. Spiritualized love. Love related to a worldwide concept. To be in harmony with the infinite. Idealization of love and harmony. Mental harmony. Peaceful ocean.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: love and harmony understood through ideals, faith, or worldview.
- psychology: spiritual inclination and idealization.
- body/health: no bodily or health condition is described.
- social/relationship: platonic or spiritualized love.
- events/manifestations: mental harmony and a worldwide conception of love.
- conflicts/notes: “peaceful ocean” remains a separate concrete image.

## Contradictions

Platonic love, faith, and mental harmony are complementary themes in one entry; the ocean image is not evidence of a causal relation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Venus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through mental and spiritual orientation: affection may be platonic, shaped by faith or worldview, or idealized as harmony with something wider. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 175, extends that scope to a worldwide conception and the infinite. It also names a peaceful ocean, a separate image that should not be forced into a psychological claim.
