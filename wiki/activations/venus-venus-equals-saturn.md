## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Interrupted harmony. Hindered inclinations. Passing affection. Love’s sorrow. Separation from love. Illegitimate birth. Inhibited in matters of affection. Limited peace. Serious, long lasting love.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: affection under limits, including both separation and endurance.
- psychology: inhibition, sorrow, or seriousness in love.
- body/health: the birth-status phrase is historical social wording, not a health prediction.
- social/relationship: passing affection, separation, or long-lasting love.
- events/manifestations: hindered inclinations and limited peace.
- conflicts/notes: the entry does not equate every limitation with loss.

## Contradictions

Passing affection and serious lasting love are distinct possibilities in the same entry. Their coexistence prevents reducing the formula to separation alone.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Venus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through limits on affection and harmony: inclinations can be hindered, peace interrupted, or love touched by sorrow and separation. Yet the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 174, also explicitly includes serious, long-lasting love. The interpretation must retain that endurance alongside the possibility of interruption rather than turning restriction into an assured ending.
