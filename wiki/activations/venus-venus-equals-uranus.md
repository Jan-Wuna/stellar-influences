## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Refined sensuality. To be choosy in love. Sudden romantic love. To give birth to a child. Sudden affection or love. Desired event. Sudden expression of love. To be the surprised object of love and affection.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: selective sensuality and sudden affection.
- psychology: choosiness and surprise in love.
- body/health: childbirth is a source association, not a reproductive prognosis.
- social/relationship: sudden romance or becoming the unexpected object of love.
- events/manifestations: a desired event or sudden expression of affection.
- conflicts/notes: the source does not state that surprise implies instability.

## Contradictions

Refined selectivity and sudden love describe different aspects of attraction; no direct contradiction is established.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Venus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through a sudden expression or arrival of affection: a person may be surprised by being loved, or experience an unexpected romance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 174, retains choosiness and refined sensuality alongside that surprise. Childbirth is also listed, but the entry supplies no basis for a pregnancy forecast.
