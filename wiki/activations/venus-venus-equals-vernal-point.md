## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Venus = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the pair through a public field of affection: goodwill and love can reach many people, while peace, harmony, and art appear in public life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 174, establishes that broad scope without specifying one person's private relationship or the duration of public harmony.
