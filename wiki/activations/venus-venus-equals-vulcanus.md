## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

The biological urge of man. Great power of attraction. Passion. Mighty love. Ardent desire. Bold passion. Passionate action. Longing. Overblown love.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: powerful attraction and intensified desire.
- psychology: longing, ardor, and possible excess.
- body/health: biological urge is historical source wording, not a clinical explanation.
- social/relationship: mighty love and strong attraction.
- events/manifestations: bold or passionate action.
- conflicts/notes: “overblown love” keeps excess within the entry's range.

## Contradictions

Strong attraction and overblown love mark a range of intensity, not two incompatible claims.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Venus = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through the force of attraction: longing and ardent desire can become bold passion or passionate action. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 175, also includes “overblown love,” so intensity can exceed measure rather than always improve a bond. Its biological and gendered wording remains an attributed image.
