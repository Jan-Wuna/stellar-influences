## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Devoted activity. Seed. Sperm. Procreation out of love. Increase. To seed. To procreate. To produce love. To force peace. Creative in art.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: affection expressed in creation, generation, and purposeful activity.
- psychology: devotion to making or producing.
- body/health: seed, sperm, and procreation are source images, not a fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: procreation out of love and producing love.
- events/manifestations: artistic creation, increase, and forcing peace.
- conflicts/notes: forcing peace is coercive wording and must not be recast as effortless harmony.

## Contradictions

Devotion and “to force peace” differ in tone; the entry does not explain their relation or make coercion a necessary expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Venus = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair through devoted, generative activity: producing love or art, seeding, and procreation belong to the [Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 175. These concrete forms of making and increase explain its productive emphasis. The separate phrase “to force peace” retains a coercive edge; it should not be silently softened into voluntary concord.
