## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 188, Venus + Vulcanus, Asc row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

To be powerfully attracted by others or to attract others powerfully.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 294; one-based PDF p. 146, Venus/Vulcanus, Asc row; complete row and adjacent glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Harmonious partner energies, artistic influence at the place, the partner's influence in love, force or violence in love by others, powerful love for the partner, strong harmony in the surroundings, strength of the feminine principle in the surroundings, and the partner's preference for force or violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful reciprocal attraction and love influence in partnerships and surroundings.
- psychology: Strong attraction toward another person.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Being strongly attracted or attracting others; ABC also retains others' force or violence in love.
- events/manifestations: Artistic influence at a place, harmony in the surroundings and the source's feminine-principle imagery.
- conflicts/notes: Gewalt permits force/power or violence; the row does not select one expression for every partnership. The feminine principle is source-native gendered symbolism.

## Contradictions

Reciprocal attraction and harmonious surroundings coexist with ABC's force-or-violence clauses. Strong attraction alone does not establish either harmony or coercion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Vulcanus = Asc`, attraction and influence take shape through another person and the surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes the direction reciprocal: one may strongly attract others or be strongly drawn to them. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends this into powerful love for a partner, harmonious partner energies, artistic influence at a place and strong harmony around it. It also explicitly retains force or violence in love by others and a partner's preference for force or violence. Those clauses preserve a possible tension within strong influence; they do not establish coercion in every encounter. ABC's feminine principle remains attributed symbolism.
