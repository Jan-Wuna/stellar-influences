## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 188, Venus + Vulcanus, Hades row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

To attend to duty with love and care. To like old things or antiques. To be attracted to dirty things or matters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 295; one-based PDF p. 146, Venus/Vulcanus, Hades row; complete row and adjacent glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Contamination of the power of love, love endangered by violence, influence in love from the past, great sorrow in love, criminal force or violence in love, harmful influence in love, hidden influence in love, and renunciation of violence in love.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong affection or attraction involving care, the past, or harmful and concealed influence.
- psychology: Love and care in attending to duty; sorrow in love in ABC.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Love endangered by violence, harmful or hidden influence, and renunciation of violence.
- events/manifestations: Caring duty, attraction to old things or antiques, and Witte's attributed dirty-things image.
- conflicts/notes: The former family/art paragraph belongs to Cupido. Gewaltiger Liebes-Kummer means great sorrow in love; criminal force or violence remains a separate source clause.

## Contradictions

Caring duty and attraction to the past differ from ABC's harmful-love possibilities. Neither source makes care or interest in antiques evidence of criminality or violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Vulcanus = Hades`, strong affection and attraction can concern care, the past, or love under harmful conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives caring attention to duty and attraction to old things or antiques, also retaining its historical dirty-things wording. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds the past's influence in love, great sorrow, hidden or harmful influence and love endangered by violence. Its renunciation-of-violence clause remains explicit. These preserve a range from care and old attachments to damaging influence, without making one imply the other.
