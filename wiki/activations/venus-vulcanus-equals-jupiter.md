## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 188, Venus + Vulcanus, Jupiter row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Joy through the power of attraction which one wields. Great happiness of love. Optimism caused by luck and success. The joy and happiness of a reunion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 294; one-based PDF p. 146, Venus/Vulcanus, Jupiter row; complete row and adjacent glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Successful influence of the feminine principle, successful power of peace, powerful happiness in love, preference for the power of money, strong success in art, preference for legal power, and beneficial influence through beauty.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Joy and success associated with powerful attraction, love and peaceful influence.
- psychology: Optimism, happiness in love and pleasure in reunion.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- social/relationship: Happiness in love and reunion.
- events/manifestations: Artistic success, beneficial influence through beauty, power of money and legal power.
- conflicts/notes: Rechts-Macht means legal power; the brief row identifies no particular legal office or outcome. ABC's feminine principle remains attributed symbolism.

## Contradictions

Happiness in love and successful influence overlap, while money, art and legal power extend the range. No clause promises that every reunion brings wealth or legal success.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Vulcanus = Jupiter`, effective attraction is associated with joy, happiness in love and optimism. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives reunion as one concrete expression; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends successful influence through beauty, art and peace. Its preferences for the power of money and legal power preserve material and legal interests without guaranteeing wealth, authority or a favorable judgment. The source's feminine principle remains an attributed symbolic category.
