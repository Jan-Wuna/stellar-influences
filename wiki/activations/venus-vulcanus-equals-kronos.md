## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 188, Venus + Vulcanus, Kronos row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

To be favored or preferred by the state or superiors. Great diligence and conscientiousness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 295; one-based PDF p. 146, Venus/Vulcanus, Kronos row; complete row and adjacent glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Special preference for power, special artistic influence, dominant influence in love, peaceful influence of hierarchy, great love energy, independent power of love, state power of peace, and strong preference for the boss.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Love or harmonious influence in settings of authority, hierarchy and official favor.
- psychology: Diligence, conscientiousness, preference for power or attraction to a superior.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied by the correctly mapped rows.
- social/relationship: Dominant influence in love, independent love-power and preference for a boss.
- events/manifestations: Favor from the state or superiors, artistic influence and state or hierarchical peace-power.
- conflicts/notes: The former bodily self-control/autogenic-training paragraph belongs to Zeus. Selbständige Liebes-Kraft means independent power of love.

## Contradictions

Official favor and hierarchical influence are compatible settings, while independent love-power remains explicit in ABC. The entries do not say that affection earns favor or that every relationship is hierarchical.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Vulcanus = Kronos`, attraction and love-power enter settings of authority and preference. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names favor from the state or superiors together with diligence and conscientiousness. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the range to special artistic influence, dominant influence in love, peaceful hierarchy and state peace-power, while also preserving independent love-power. These describe possible expressions of influence and preference rather than a guarantee of official favor.
