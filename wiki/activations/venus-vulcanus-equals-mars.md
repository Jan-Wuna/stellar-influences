## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 188, Venus + Vulcanus, Mars row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

To carry out one’s activities (actions or work) by inner compulsion. To arrive at intimate relationship through one’s activities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 294; one-based PDF p. 146, Venus/Vulcanus, Mars row; complete row and adjacent glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Influence through harmonious actions, joy and influence through work, forceful or violent activity in love, harmonious work-power, influential artistic activity, powerful efforts in love, a strong will for peace, and preference for strenuous work.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful affection or influence expressed through action and work.
- psychology: Inner compulsion, determined effort and a strong will for peace.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is given.
- social/relationship: Activities can lead to intimacy in Witte; ABC also retains forceful or violent activity in love.
- events/manifestations: Harmonious action and work-power, influential artistic activity, efforts in love and strenuous work.
- conflicts/notes: Liebes-Bemühungen means efforts in love, not commitments. Gewalt-Tätigkeit permits forceful or violent activity; the row does not make every action violent.

## Contradictions

Harmonious work, artistic activity and peace effort coexist with the force-or-violence clause. They are distinct possibilities rather than a single necessary sequence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Vulcanus = Mars`, the pair's force becomes action and effort. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names activity driven by inner compulsion and intimacy reached through one's activities. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives that effort harmonious, artistic and practical expressions: influence through work, powerful efforts in love, a will for peace and preference for strenuous work. Its forceful-or-violent activity in love remains explicit as a separate possibility; inner drive does not by itself establish that expression.
