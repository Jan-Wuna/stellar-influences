## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 188, Venus + Vulcanus, MC row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

A passionate disposition. To be compelled and motivated by an inner force.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 294; one-based PDF p. 146, Venus/Vulcanus, MC row; complete row and adjacent glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: I influence art, my consolidation through harmony, my harmonious influence, my power of love, my strength in art, my love is strong, emotional power of joy, emotional force or violence in love, emotional power of love, and strong harmony of the soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Passion and love experienced as a personal inner force.
- psychology: Inward motivation, strong love and harmony of the soul.
- body/health: No health outcome follows from the emotional wording.
- social/relationship: One's harmonious influence and strong love; ABC also names emotional force or violence in love.
- events/manifestations: Personal artistic influence and strength in art.
- conflicts/notes: Seelisch concerns the soul or emotional life here; the passage supplies no paranormal mechanism. The Gewalt clause retains force or violence as a range.

## Contradictions

Inner motivation, art, joy and harmony coexist with ABC's force-or-violence wording. Passion does not establish either a harmful emotional expression or a medical condition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Vulcanus = MC`, intense affection is experienced as an inward, personally motivating force. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names passion and compulsion from within; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects one's strong love with artistic influence, strength in art and consolidation through harmony. Its emotional joy, love-power and harmony of the soul deepen that inward range, while emotional force or violence in love remains a distinct possibility. The row does not identify a clinical or paranormal mechanism.
