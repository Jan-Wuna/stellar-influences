## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 188, Venus + Vulcanus, Moon row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

The passionate, loving wife. Persons who are passionate in love. The population that is driven to something by an inner urge.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 294; one-based PDF p. 146, Venus/Vulcanus, Moon row; complete row and adjacent glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Influential art by a woman, peaceful power of women, harmonious influence of the people, power of a peaceful people, power of a beautiful woman, loving strong women, a woman's strong love, influencing the art of the people, and feminine power of love.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong affection and harmonious influence at individual and collective scales.
- psychology: Passionate love or a population's inner urge.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: The sources' wife, women and feminine-love examples remain attributed historical imagery.
- events/manifestations: Influential art by a woman, the power of beauty, the art of the people and peaceful collective influence.
- conflicts/notes: Schönen Frau means beautiful woman, not gentle woman. Volks-Kunst means the art of the people; it does not establish a modern genre.

## Contradictions

Passionate individuals and a peaceful people's influence differ in scale. The gendered examples do not establish that only women express the formula or that a population acts uniformly.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Vulcanus = Moon`, powerful affection and harmonious influence can appear through a person's love or a collective disposition. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places passionate love beside a population moved by an inner urge. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds peaceful collective power and artistic influence, including art by a woman and the art of the people. Its beautiful-woman and strong-women clauses preserve the source's gendered and aesthetic examples without making them universal conditions.
