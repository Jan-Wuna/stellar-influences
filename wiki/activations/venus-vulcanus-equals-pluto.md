## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 188, Venus + Vulcanus, Pluto row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

To push something forward with fervor. Longing and desiring changes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 295; one-based PDF p. 146, Venus/Vulcanus, Pluto row; complete row and adjacent glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Influential developments in peace, influential unfolding of harmony, developing the power of love, powerful developments in art, strong transformation of the feminine principle, developing strong affection, and preference for influential developments.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful affection and harmonious influence developing through change.
- psychology: Fervor and longing for change.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied by the correctly mapped rows.
- social/relationship: Developing strong affection and love-power.
- events/manifestations: Developments in peace, unfolding harmony, artistic developments and the source's transformation of the feminine principle.
- conflicts/notes: The former pretended/passing-affection paragraph belongs to Neptune. ABC's complete seven clauses concern influential developments, not individual developments or solidarity.

## Contradictions

Fervor for change and developing love, art or harmony are compatible emphases. No clause establishes that every change succeeds or that it involves deception.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Vulcanus = Pluto`, attraction and affection acquire a developmental direction: something is pushed forward with fervor, while change is strongly desired in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) specifies developments in love-power, strong affection, art, peace and harmony. Its transformation of the feminine principle remains an attributed symbolic image. These give the desire for change a stated range without promising a favorable result; the pretended-affection paragraph belongs to Neptune.
