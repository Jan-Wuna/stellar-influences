## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 188, Venus + Vulcanus, Poseidon row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

To be strongly attracted to mental or spiritual things. Strong philosophical inclinations and convictions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 295; one-based PDF p. 146, Venus/Vulcanus, Poseidon row; complete row and adjacent glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Influence of peaceful ideas, mental or spiritual force or violence in love, a powerful culture of peace, power through true love, power of art and culture, love of mental or spiritual power, powerful love of truth, spiritual power of love, strong spiritual harmony, and reasonable artistic influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong attraction toward truth, ideas and mental or spiritual concerns.
- psychology: Philosophical inclinations, convictions and love of truth.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is given.
- social/relationship: True love, spiritual harmony and the spiritual power of love.
- events/manifestations: Peaceful ideas, a culture of peace, power of art and culture, and reasonable artistic influence.
- conflicts/notes: The former rejection/hindrance paragraph belongs to Admetos. Geistige Gewalt retains mental/spiritual force or violence; the row does not settle a single concrete expression.

## Contradictions

Spiritual attraction, truth, art and peace are compatible themes, but ABC also retains mental or spiritual force or violence in love. That clause must not disappear into an exclusively harmonious reading.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Vulcanus = Poseidon`, strong attraction is directed toward ideas, truth and mental or spiritual concerns. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes philosophical inclination and conviction explicit; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds peaceful ideas, a culture of peace, true love, spiritual harmony and the power of art and culture. Its reasonable artistic influence extends the range into art. ABC also names mental or spiritual force or violence in love, whose exact expression remains contextual; strong conviction alone does not establish coercion or harmony.
