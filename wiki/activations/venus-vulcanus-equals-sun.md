## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 188, Venus + Vulcanus, Sun row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Powerful physical longing for love. Passionate bodily love. The man who is helplessly in love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 294; one-based PDF p. 146, Venus/Vulcanus, Sun row; complete row and adjacent glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: A harmonious energy center, a person's power of love, powerful bodily love, masculine influence in love, personal artistic influence, personal strength through harmony, strong personal love, a strong peace center, and a man's preference for force or violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful affection experienced personally and bodily.
- psychology: Longing and the source's description of being helplessly in love.
- body/health: Bodily love is explicit, without a clinical claim.
- social/relationship: Strong personal love; masculine and man examples remain attributed historical wording.
- events/manifestations: Personal artistic influence, strength through harmony and a strong peace center.
- conflicts/notes: Kunst-Einfluß is artistic influence. The man's preference for Gewalt retains force or violence as a separate clause and is not an implication of bodily passion.

## Contradictions

Personal intensity, art and harmony coexist with the historical male force-or-violence clause. Neither source makes bodily longing evidence of violence or confines the formula to men.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Vulcanus = Sun`, the pair's force is experienced personally and physically as longing and passionate bodily love, the emphasis of the [Witte entry](#witte-entry). The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects strong personal love with strength through harmony, personal artistic influence and a harmonious energy or peace center. Its masculine examples and man's preference for force or violence remain attributed source claims. They neither restrict the formula to men nor make violence follow from strong affection.
