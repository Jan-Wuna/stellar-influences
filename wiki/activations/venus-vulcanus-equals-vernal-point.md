## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 188, Venus + Vulcanus, Vernal Point row; actual glyph and neighboring rows checked.

Something that is mostly desired by the general public. The general longing.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 294; one-based PDF p. 146, Venus/Vulcanus, Vernal Point row; complete row and adjacent glyph boundaries checked.

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: The power of beauty in public, force or violence and public love, influence of harmony in public life, artistic influence in public, public power of peace, and strengthening of the feminine principle in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public longing and the influence of beauty, love, harmony or art.
- psychology: General public desire.
- body/health: No bodily or medical condition is supplied.
- social/relationship: Love and harmony at a public scale; force or violence is also explicit in ABC.
- events/manifestations: Public artistic influence, peace-power and the source's feminine-principle image.
- conflicts/notes: Gewalt means force/power or violence, not administration. Weiblichen Prinzips is the feminine principle, not a common principle; its symbolism remains attributed.

## Contradictions

Public longing, beauty and harmony coexist with the force-or-violence clause. The entries do not say that everything widely desired is peaceful or beneficial.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Vulcanus = Vernal Point`, the pair's attraction is expressed through public desire and influence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names something widely desired and general longing; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives public beauty, love, harmony, art and peace-power as concrete expressions. Its strengthening of the feminine principle remains attributed symbolism. ABC also retains force or violence alongside public love, so public appeal alone does not establish a peaceful outcome.
