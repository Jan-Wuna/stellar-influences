## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 184 (one-based; scanned row checked).

To be impregnated. Others are forced to settle peacefully, to make peace, or the native is forced to make peace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 286; PDF page: 142 (one-based spread).

Fiery love partner, peace efforts in the environment, striving for harmony with the partner, harmonious goals with others, artistic achievements in the surroundings, a place for artistic creation, and the partner's preference for achievement

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed love, artistic effort, or imposed peace in direct relationships.
- psychology: A partner's preference for achievement; no broader inner disposition is supplied.
- body/health: Witte's impregnation statement is historical doctrine without a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: Fiery partnership, shared goals, or being forced to settle peacefully.
- events/manifestations: Artistic creation in the surroundings and efforts for peace.
- conflicts/notes: Harmonie-Streben means striving for harmony, not strife; forced settlement remains distinct from willing cooperation.

## Contradictions

Witte's forced peace and ABC's shared harmony goals differ materially in consent and setting. They must not be collapsed into an uncomplicated cooperative result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Zeus = Asc`, Asc specifies the pair through direct partners and shared surroundings. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes a fiery partner, striving for harmony, shared goals, and artistic achievement. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) adds a different possibility: others or the person being forced to settle peacefully. It also names impregnation as historical doctrine. These preserve willing shared effort and imposed settlement as distinct manifestations rather than treating all peace-making as harmonious consent.
