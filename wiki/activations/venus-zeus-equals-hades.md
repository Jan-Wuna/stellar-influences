## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 184 (one-based; scanned row checked).

Sorrows through adverse circumstances in service relations. Grief through procreation. Illicit procreation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 287; PDF page: 142 (one-based spread).

Dangerous preference for fire, harmonious service, artistic creations from the past, achievements from a harmonious past, inadequate peace efforts, disadvantage through peace efforts, and renunciation of procreation in love

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sorrow or disadvantage in devoted service, peace efforts, or procreation.
- psychology: Grief is explicitly named; no broader disposition is supplied.
- body/health: Procreative associations remain historical doctrine, not a medical prediction.
- social/relationship: Adverse circumstances in service relations or renunciation of procreation.
- events/manifestations: Past artistic creations, inadequate peace efforts, and a dangerous preference for fire.
- conflicts/notes: Illicit is Witte's historical label; ABC's final clause concerns procreation, not denial.

## Contradictions

Harmonious service and adverse service circumstances are alternatives. Past artistic achievement is not itself an adverse claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Zeus = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through adverse conditions around service and creation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names sorrow in service relations and grief through procreation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds disadvantage in peace efforts and renunciation of procreation, but also harmonious service and artistic creations from the past. These retain both useful work and its possible burdens without treating the source's historical label illicit as a judgment of a person.
