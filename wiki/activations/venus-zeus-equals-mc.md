## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 184 (one-based; scanned row checked).

Personal creation with devotion. The progenitor. The carrier of seed, the sower.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 286; PDF page: 142 (one-based spread).

Soulful artistic creation, soulful procreation in love, joy in the minute of procreation, I strive for harmony, joy of a creative soul, my love fire, my joy in fire, my peaceful goals, my achievements in art, and striving for harmony of the soul

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal devoted creation and inward striving for harmony.
- psychology: Joy in creative activity and peaceful goals.
- body/health: Procreation and seed imagery are historical source doctrine, not medical prediction.
- social/relationship: Love's fire is named without a detailed partnership outcome.
- events/manifestations: Artistic achievement and personal creative effort.
- conflicts/notes: The scan says peaceful goals, not final goals; seed imagery is retained as source language.

## Contradictions

The entries connect personal creation with harmony and procreation at different levels. They do not establish one required form of creative work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Zeus = MC`, MC specifies the pair as personal devoted creation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names the progenitor and sower, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places creative joy, love's fire, peaceful goals, and artistic achievement in the first person. The organizing meaning is one's own creative involvement and striving for harmony; the procreative imagery remains a specific historical application rather than the definition of all creation.
