## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 184 (one-based; scanned row checked).

Pregnancy, gestation. Nations forced to make peace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 286; PDF page: 142 (one-based spread).

Beginning of a woman's love, fiery feelings of love, emotional striving for harmony, a woman's creative art, a woman's love fire, peace efforts of a people, achievements in folk art, and a woman's procreation in love

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Felt or collective devoted creation and harmony-seeking.
- psychology: Fiery feelings of love and emotional striving for harmony.
- body/health: Pregnancy, gestation, and procreation remain historical source associations.
- social/relationship: Beginning love is a source example; nations forced to make peace is a distinct collective claim.
- events/manifestations: Folk art and the peace efforts of a people.
- conflicts/notes: The women-centered examples are attributed; forced peace is not equivalent to willing harmony.

## Contradictions

Emotional harmony-seeking and nations forced to make peace differ materially in scale and consent. They should not be merged into one simple relationship meaning.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Zeus = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through feeling and the collective life of a people. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) joins fiery love feelings, artistic creation, folk art, and peace efforts. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names pregnancy and gestation, but also nations forced to make peace. Those bodily and political examples remain distinct historical claims; the shared theme of creation or harmony does not make imposed peace consensual or the female examples universal.
