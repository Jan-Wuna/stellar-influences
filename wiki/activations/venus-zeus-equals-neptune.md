## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 184 (one-based; scanned row checked).

No ambition or desire. Efforts of love are in vain, wasted. Sterility.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 287; PDF page: 142 (one-based spread).

Dissolution of a beginning love, beginning pleasure in metaphysics, sensitive procreation in love, peace goals for the future, intuitive artistic achievements, metaphysical artistic creation, unclear artistic direction, and uncertain beginning of peace

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creation or beginning love marked by dissolution, uncertainty, or intuitive expression.
- psychology: Lack of ambition or desire; interest in metaphysics.
- body/health: Sterility and sensitive procreation are historical source doctrine, not diagnoses.
- social/relationship: Dissolution of beginning love and efforts of love described as wasted.
- events/manifestations: Intuitive art, unclear artistic direction, and uncertain peace beginnings.
- conflicts/notes: The scan restores metaphysical artistic creation and procreation, not witnessing.

## Contradictions

Witte's lack of desire and ABC's intuitive creativity are distinct possibilities. Sterility and procreative wording are historical alternatives that cannot supply a medical conclusion.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Zeus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through uncertain or dissolving beginnings, with a separate intuitive creative strand. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names lack of desire and wasted efforts of love. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds dissolution of beginning love and unclear artistic direction, but also metaphysical interest and intuitive artistic work. These retain both frustrated effort and subtle creation, while the reproductive claims remain attributed historical doctrine.
