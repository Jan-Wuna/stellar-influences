## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 184 (one-based; scanned row checked).

Increased activities. Increasing augmentation. Arising seed. Growing number of births.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 287; PDF page: 142 (one-based spread).

Beginning of harmonious development, developing peace efforts, unfolding creative art, developing creativity in love, creative artistic development, growing joy in fire, growing artistic achievements, and changing artistic creation

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Increase and unfolding in activity, love, art, or peace efforts.
- psychology: Growing joy in fire is listed without a broader disposition.
- body/health: Seed and growing birth numbers are historical source doctrine, not medical predictions.
- social/relationship: Developing creativity in love.
- events/manifestations: Increased activity, artistic achievement, and evolving peace efforts.
- conflicts/notes: The scan says changing artistic creation; no universal direction or beneficial outcome is established.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize growth at different levels. Increase in activity or births is not identical with improvement in every relationship.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Zeus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair as increase and unfolding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names increased activity and growing birth numbers, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends development into love, art, and peace efforts. Together they support growing creative involvement and changing forms of achievement, without making quantity of activity a measure of its quality or treating the historical reproductive examples as predictions.
