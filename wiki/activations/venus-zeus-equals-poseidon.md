## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 184 (one-based; scanned row checked).

The spiritual or mental power. To proclaim new philosophical or ideas of a worldview.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 287; PDF page: 142 (one-based spread).

Efforts for peace and culture, beginning clarity about love, leading in arts and culture, leading peace idea, artistic culture design, love for spiritual guidance, beauty of a creative spirit, and creative spiritual arts

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Devoted creation expressed through spiritual ideas and cultural direction.
- psychology: Beginning clarity about love and appreciation of a creative spirit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Love of spiritual guidance is stated without a concrete relationship outcome.
- events/manifestations: Proclaiming philosophical ideas; leadership in art, culture, or peace efforts.
- conflicts/notes: The entry does not establish the truth of a proclaimed worldview.

## Contradictions

The entries share an emphasis on spiritual or cultural creation, without a direct disagreement. Leadership does not itself validate an idea.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Zeus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through spiritual thought and cultural direction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names mental power and proclaiming philosophical or worldview ideas. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives that activity a creative form in art, culture, peace efforts, and growing clarity about love. The shared meaning is ideas brought into expression and direction, without evidence that their force or prominence proves their truth.
