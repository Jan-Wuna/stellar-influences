## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 184 (one-based; scanned row checked).

To separate from a devoted activity. Passing, temporary devoted activity. Loss of a blood relative.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 287; PDF page: 142 (one-based spread).

Beginning harmony problems, serious artistic efforts, serious peace goals, shaping peace tasks, inhibited efforts in love, disturbed striving for peace, shaping harmony in old age, love fire in old age, and problematic artistic achievement

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Devoted activity limited, interrupted, or made temporary.
- psychology: Serious effort and inhibited striving in love.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Separation from a devoted activity; loss of a blood relative is also listed.
- events/manifestations: Serious art or peace tasks and harmony or love's fire in old age.
- conflicts/notes: Loss of a relative is a historical source association, not a prediction.

## Contradictions

Temporary devoted activity and serious sustained effort are different possibilities. Harmony in old age remains alongside interruption rather than cancelling it.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Zeus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the pair through limitation or interruption of devoted effort. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names separation from an activity and temporary involvement. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects inhibition and harmony problems with serious artistic or peace tasks, while retaining harmony and love's fire in old age. The reading therefore includes both constrained effort and mature creation; Witte's loss-of-relative example remains an attributed historical association.
