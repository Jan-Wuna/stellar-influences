## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 184 (one-based; scanned row checked).

Physical work done lovingly. Generative qualities and abilities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 286; PDF page: 142 (one-based spread).

Beginning of personal love, a man's striving for peace, bodily procreation in love, personal striving for harmony, personal love goals, personal preference for fire, bodily beauty as a goal, and preference for bodily achievement

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal embodied creation and work done with love.
- psychology: Personal love goals and striving for harmony.
- body/health: Generative abilities and bodily procreation are historical source claims.
- social/relationship: Beginning personal love.
- events/manifestations: Lovingly done physical work, bodily achievement, and beauty as a goal.
- conflicts/notes: Male-centered source examples do not establish universal traits; Streben is striving, not a strike.

## Contradictions

Physical work and procreation are different applications of creation. The entries do not make bodily beauty a measure of human worth.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Zeus = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through personal and bodily creative effort. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins lovingly done physical work with generative abilities. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal love goals, harmony-seeking, bodily achievement, and beauty as an aim. These describe a person's involvement in creation, with reproductive and male-centered language retained as historical source applications rather than a norm for identity or appearance.
