## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 184 (one-based; scanned row checked).

Burning desire. Unexpected procreation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 287; PDF page: 142 (one-based spread).

Exciting love event, beginning of tension in love, beginning of new love, dynamic artistic direction, dynamic striving for harmony, joy in technical achievements, leading art event, creative artistic impulses, and surprising procreation in love

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden desire and the beginning of love or creative impulses.
- psychology: Burning desire, excitement, and tension.
- body/health: Unexpected procreation is historical source doctrine, not a medical prediction.
- social/relationship: Beginning new love.
- events/manifestations: Artistic direction, creative impulses, and joy in technical achievement.
- conflicts/notes: Surprising procreation does not mean testimony; harmony-seeking does not mean strife.

## Contradictions

Love tension and dynamic harmony-seeking are both stated, without a direct contradiction. A sudden beginning does not establish its duration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Zeus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden desire and creative beginnings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names burning desire and unexpected procreation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds new love, tension, dynamic artistic direction, and technical achievement. The common thread is an impulse becoming active or visible, while the bodily example remains historical doctrine and the entries give no assurance about how long a new undertaking lasts.
