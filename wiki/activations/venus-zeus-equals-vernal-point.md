## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 184 (one-based; scanned row checked).

General procreation, propagation. Devoted activities in general. Semen. Seed.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 286; PDF page: 142 (one-based spread).

Joyful fire in public, peace efforts in public, striving for harmony in public, public artistic creation, artistic achievements in public, and artistic creations in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: General devoted activity and public creative or peace efforts.
- psychology: No distinct personal disposition is supplied.
- body/health: Procreation, semen, and seed are historical source terms without a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: The entries concern general or public activity rather than a detailed partnership pattern.
- events/manifestations: Public art, peace efforts, harmony-seeking, and joyful fire.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's former single location phrase omitted the scanned public-art and peace clauses.

## Contradictions

The sources describe general creation at different scales. The public art strand must not be reduced to the reproductive examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Venus/Zeus = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the pair in general or public activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names devoted activity and propagation, using seed and procreative imagery. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) supplies the public setting in art, peace efforts, and striving for harmony. The combined reading centers on creation and devoted effort made public, while keeping the reproductive terms as one historical application.
