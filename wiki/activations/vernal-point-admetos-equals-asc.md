## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 53 (one-based).

Connections and ties to home. Strong resistance in the environment. Strong resistance from another person nearby.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 54; PDF page: 26 (one-based spread).

Depressed partner in public, restricted public surroundings, consolidation of the partner's position in public life, public blockade at the location, suppression in the public surroundings, and public resistance at the location

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public restriction or resistance encountered in nearby people and surroundings.
- psychology: ABC names a depressed partner as source wording, not a clinical assessment.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Ties to home and resistance from another person nearby.
- events/manifestations: Local blockade and restriction of the public environment.
- conflicts/notes: Consolidation of a partner's public position differs from environmental suppression.

## Contradictions

Ties to home, nearby resistance, and a consolidated public position are different applications. They do not make a partner inherently obstructive.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Admetos = Asc`, Asc specifies the pair through nearby people and the public surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names ties to home and strong resistance in the environment or from another person. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds local blockade and suppression, while also allowing a partner's public position to become more established. The interpretation centers on how restriction or consolidation is encountered directly, without assigning every obstacle to the partner.
