## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 53 (one-based).

The beginning or the end of a disagreeable situation. Gaining new raw materials from junk or from coal. Gaining raw materials from the earth.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 55; PDF page: 26 (one-based spread).

Restrictions and deprivations in public, criminal suppression in public, grief from the past in public life, disadvantages through a blocked public sphere, and hidden resistance in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Adverse public conditions or a difficult situation beginning or ending.
- psychology: Grief from the past is stated without a fuller disposition.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Deprivation and hidden public resistance.
- events/manifestations: Recovering raw materials from junk, coal, or the earth.
- conflicts/notes: Criminal suppression is the source's description; no specific actor is established.

## Contradictions

The beginning or end of a disagreeable situation leaves the direction open. Resource recovery is a separate material application, not proof that deprivation is beneficial.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Admetos = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through adverse conditions and what can be recovered from them. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) emphasizes public deprivation, suppression, and hidden resistance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) also allows the beginning or end of a disagreeable situation, and gives the concrete material examples of raw materials recovered from junk, coal, or the earth. These retain social hardship and material recovery as distinct expressions.
