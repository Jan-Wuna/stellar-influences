## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 53 (one-based).

The overcoming of difficulties in general. Moderate luck. Fortunate with property. Fortunate in retirement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 54; PDF page: 26 (one-based spread).

Blocked public funds, restricted finances in public life, successful public resistance, joy through consolidation in public life, public circulation of money, and stabilization of public law

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Overcoming difficulties or consolidating public affairs within financial limits.
- psychology: Joy through consolidation.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: No detailed personal relationship pattern is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Property or retirement fortune, blocked funds, public resistance, and stabilized public law.
- conflicts/notes: Moderate luck is explicit; public money circulation is not a guaranteed financial gain.

## Contradictions

Blocked funds and fortunate property or retirement are different possibilities. Public success is not equivalent to unlimited finances.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Admetos = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through moderate fortune or successful consolidation amid limits. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names overcoming difficulties and favorable property or retirement circumstances. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) retains blocked public funds and restricted finances, but also successful resistance and stabilization of public law. The shared meaning is a bounded favorable outcome or firmer position, without erasing the financial restrictions.
