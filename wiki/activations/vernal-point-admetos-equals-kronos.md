## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 53 (one-based).

Great cracking, dividing, splitting. An authority for a few people. Deaths or difficulties under ruling persons. Lowness, low ground or a valley.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 55; PDF page: 26 (one-based spread).

Restriction of public independence, the public suppressed by the state, established standing in public life, thoroughgoing public authority, public resistance against the state, and important restrictions in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Restriction, division, or consolidation in relation to public authority.
- psychology: No distinct personal disposition is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily mechanism is supplied.
- social/relationship: Authority over few people, state suppression, or public resistance to the state.
- events/manifestations: Cracking or splitting, difficulties among rulers, and low terrain or valleys.
- conflicts/notes: Witte also lists deaths among ruling persons as historical doctrine; landscape imagery remains distinct from politics.

## Contradictions

Established public standing and state suppression are different possibilities. The authority and terrain examples cannot be treated as a single literal event.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Admetos = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair in the field of authority. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes restricted independence, state suppression, established public standing, and resistance to the state. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) adds authority over few people and difficulties among rulers, alongside literal cracking and low ground or valleys. These retain constrained power and physical division as separate applications; the historical death association is not an inevitable outcome.
