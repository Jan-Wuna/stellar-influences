## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 53 (one-based).

Fighting resistance. Homicide.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 54; PDF page: 26 (one-based spread).

Persistent public activity, restricted public work, consolidation of public work, thorough creation in public, public resistance work, and suppression of the public will

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity that confronts public resistance or works under restriction.
- psychology: Persistence is named without a fuller personal disposition.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public resistance or suppression of the public will.
- events/manifestations: Restricted or consolidated public work; Witte also lists homicide.
- conflicts/notes: The scan reverses the former promotion wording: Unterdruckung is suppression; homicide remains an attributed historical association.

## Contradictions

Fighting resistance and suppressing the public will concern different directions of action. The entries do not identify one actor or establish inevitable violence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Admetos = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through work and action around resistance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names fighting resistance; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds persistent but restricted public work, consolidation, and resistance activity. It also explicitly names suppression of the public will, a different direction from resisting suppression. Witte's homicide example remains historical doctrine and does not establish that every obstruction leads to violence.
