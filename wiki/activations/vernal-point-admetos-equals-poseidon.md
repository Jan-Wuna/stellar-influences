## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 53 (one-based).

General education, culture and training. General mental obstacles.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 55; PDF page: 26 (one-based spread).

Insight into the public circulation, strengthening the truth in the public, mental resistance in the public, clarity in the public life through perseverance, public culture manifestation, and resistance to the public reason

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public education, truth, and culture meeting mental resistance or gaining clarity through perseverance.
- psychology: Insight and mental resistance.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: General education and training rather than a detailed partnership pattern.
- events/manifestations: Public cultural expression and firmer public standing for truth.
- conflicts/notes: Resistance to public reason and strengthening truth are different possibilities; no worldview is certified.

## Contradictions

Mental obstacles and clarity through perseverance are different possible expressions. Publicly established truth is source terminology, not independent verification.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Admetos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through public knowledge and culture under resistance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names education, training, and mental obstacles. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds insight, public cultural expression, and clarity through perseverance, while retaining resistance to public reason. These connect the difficulty of understanding or communicating with the possibility of firmer clarity, without validating a particular public doctrine.
