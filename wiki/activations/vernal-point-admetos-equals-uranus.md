## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 53 (one-based).

Sudden heavy depression. Excitement, aggravations through obstacles of a turbulent nature. Wreckage, broken pieces and rags. Surprised by the sudden death of another persons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 55; PDF page: 26 (one-based spread).

blocked public rhythm, restricting public impulses, new restrictions in public, suddenly blocked public life, rotational technology in public, and unrest through public resistance

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden public blockage, turbulent resistance, or disruption.
- psychology: Excitement, aggravation, and sudden heavy depression as source language.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Unrest through public resistance.
- events/manifestations: Wreckage, new restrictions, blocked rhythm, and rotational technology; Witte also names surprise at another's death.
- conflicts/notes: Rotational technology and the death example are separate source associations, not a single predicted event.

## Contradictions

The entries share abrupt obstruction while retaining unrelated material examples. They do not establish that every public blockage causes wreckage or death.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Admetos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through abrupt or turbulent obstruction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names excitement and aggravation through obstacles, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places sudden blockage, disrupted rhythm, and unrest in public life. Wreckage and rotational technology are concrete material examples in different entries. The surprise-at-death association remains historical source doctrine rather than an inevitable consequence of public resistance.
