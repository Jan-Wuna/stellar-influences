## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 53 (one-based).

To be without feelings and insensitive.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 54; PDF page: 26 (one-based spread).

Restriction of joy in public, end of public peace, consolidation of love in public life, depressions in love in public life, consolidation of public peace, and harmonious public circulation

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Feeling, joy, love, or peace affected by public restriction or consolidation.
- psychology: Insensitivity and depression in love are attributed source expressions.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Love in public life may be consolidated or constrained.
- events/manifestations: End of public peace or consolidation of peace.
- conflicts/notes: The page includes contrary possibilities; insensitivity is not a universal character judgment.

## Contradictions

The end and consolidation of peace are explicit alternatives. Witte's lack of feeling differs from ABC's firming of love and cannot define the whole range.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Admetos = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through public joy, love, and peace under limits. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names absence of feeling or insensitivity, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes restricted joy and depression in love but also consolidated love and peace. These preserve emotional constriction and firmer harmony as alternatives, without treating the adverse wording as a complete judgment of a person's capacity to love.
