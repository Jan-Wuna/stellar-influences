## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 53 (one-based).

Obstacles through the present powers. To bundle or to compress something.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 55; PDF page: 26 (one-based spread).

Energy circle in the public, strengthening the public power, strengthening the influence in the public life, restrictions on violence in the public, public power limitation, and resistance to public power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public power concentrated, constrained, or resisted.
- psychology: No distinct personal disposition is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Obstacles through existing powers and resistance to public power.
- events/manifestations: Bundling or compression, stronger public influence, and restrictions on force or violence.
- conflicts/notes: Strengthening power and limiting power are different possibilities.

## Contradictions

ABC explicitly permits consolidation and limitation of power. The entries leave open which side of a public conflict holds or resists it.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Admetos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through concentrated power and its limits. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names obstacles from existing powers and literal bundling or compression. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds consolidated public influence, limits on power or violence, and resistance to that power. The common theme is concentration or constraint, while the direction of power remains an open distinction in the source examples.
