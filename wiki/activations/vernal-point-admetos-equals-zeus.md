## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 53 (one-based).

To start to tackle a difficult job. Fire in the earth such as lava.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 55; PDF page: 26 (one-based spread).

Persistent resistance in public, blocked efforts in public life, a restricted public beginning, thorough public goal-striving, suppressed public leadership, and suppression by public leadership

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Beginning difficult work or directed public effort under resistance.
- psychology: Persistence and thorough goal-striving.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Leadership may be suppressed or may itself suppress.
- events/manifestations: Blocked public efforts and restricted beginnings; Witte also names lava.
- conflicts/notes: The scan says suppression by leadership, not public support; lava is a separate physical example.

## Contradictions

Suppressed leadership and suppression by leadership reverse the direction of action. Both must remain visible rather than assigning one role to all leaders.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Admetos = Zeus`, Zeus specifies the pair through directed effort confronting a difficult beginning. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names tackling a difficult job and gives lava as a separate physical image. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds persistent resistance, blocked efforts, and thorough goals, while distinguishing leadership being suppressed from leadership suppressing others. The organizing meaning is effort under resistance, with the actor and direction of suppression kept explicit.
