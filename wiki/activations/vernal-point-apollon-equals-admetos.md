## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 52 (one-based).

A general standstill, stagnation, stoppage or shut down in economy, commerce, trades or science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 53; PDF page: 25 (one-based spread).

Blocked public freedom, experience of depression in public life, restrictions on public success, strengthening of public freedom, trade blockade in public life, and suppression of freedom in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public expansion, commerce, or science brought to standstill or limited.
- psychology: ABC's depression experience remains attributed wording.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public freedom may be blocked, suppressed, or consolidated.
- events/manifestations: Economic shutdown, restricted success, and trade blockade.
- conflicts/notes: Consolidation of freedom is a distinct possibility within the restrictive range.

## Contradictions

Suppression and strengthening of public freedom are explicit alternatives. Consolidation does not establish the absence of economic stagnation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Apollon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair through limits on public scope and exchange. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names stoppage in commerce, economy, trades, or science. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends that restriction to public success and freedom, but also allows freedom to become consolidated. The common meaning is expansion meeting a boundary, with blockage and firmer establishment kept as different possibilities.
