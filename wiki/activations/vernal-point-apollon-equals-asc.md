## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 52 (one-based).

Get to know many places. To know many people. To have many customers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 52; PDF page: 25 (one-based spread).

Expansion of a place in public, success of the partner in public, experiences with the partner in public life, public trade in the place, public environmental experiences, and growing world economy

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Broad public contact through places, people, partners, and customers.
- psychology: No distinct personal disposition is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Knowing many people or customers; a partner's public success.
- events/manifestations: Local trade, a place expanding, and public environmental experience.
- conflicts/notes: Public experience with a partner does not establish intimacy or the duration of the relationship.

## Contradictions

The entries share wider contact without establishing intimacy, duration, or a guaranteed commercial result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Apollon = Asc`, Asc specifies the pair in direct contact with people and places. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives knowing many places, people, and customers as concrete examples. Clear [ABC clauses](#abc-entry) add a partner's public success, local trade, and experience of the public surroundings. The integrated meaning is breadth of contact in a public or commercial setting, without assuming that numerous acquaintances are close relationships.
