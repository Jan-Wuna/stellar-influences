## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 53; PDF page: 25 (one-based spread).

expansion of public service, endangered freedom in public life, secret trade in public, public experiences from the past, hidden knowledge in public life, and economic criminality in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public knowledge or trade connected with deficiencies, the past, or concealment.
- psychology: No distinct personal disposition is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public service expands; freedom may be endangered.
- events/manifestations: Junk or waste dealing, historical study, secret trade, and economic criminality.
- conflicts/notes: Historical study, waste work, and public service are not themselves claims of criminality.

## Contradictions

The entries juxtapose service, historical knowledge, and adverse concealment. These must remain separate rather than treating every past-related trade as deficient.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Apollon = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through deficiencies, concealed activity, or knowledge of the past. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names widespread deficiencies, waste dealers, and historical study. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds hidden knowledge and secret trade, but also expanded public service and experience from the past. The synthesis retains the adverse and useful applications separately; neither historical work nor public service implies economic wrongdoing.
