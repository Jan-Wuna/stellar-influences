## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 52 (one-based).

Several fortunate happenings at the same time. Extensive fortune. Science of money. Large amounts of money. Great financial institutions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 52; PDF page: 25 (one-based spread).

Successful expansion in the public life, financial experience in the public, happy experiences in the public life, public financial success, public law science, and legal success in the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Extensive public fortune or successful expansion, especially financial or legal.
- psychology: Happy public experiences are named without a fuller personal disposition.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: No detailed personal relationship pattern is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Concurrent fortunate events, large sums, financial institutions, and public legal success.
- conflicts/notes: These are attributed associations, not an assurance of profit or legal victory.

## Contradictions

The entries share a favorable public emphasis without specifying conditions, amounts, or which event occurs.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Apollon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through extensive fortune and public success. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes the scale concrete in several fortunate events, large sums, and financial institutions. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful expansion, happy public experience, and financial or legal success. Together these describe a favorable public or institutional range, without providing a promise of a particular monetary or legal outcome.
