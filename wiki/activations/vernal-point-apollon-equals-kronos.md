## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 52 (one-based).

One who is a scientific authority. Great masses of people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 53; PDF page: 25 (one-based spread).

Expansion of the state in public, freedom and self governing in public life, hierarchic of the world economy, self governing public trade, state successes in public, and increasing freedom in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public scope and scientific or state authority.
- psychology: No distinct personal disposition is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Scientific authority and large masses of people.
- events/manifestations: State expansion, economic hierarchy, public trade, and increasing freedom.
- conflicts/notes: Authority and self-governing activity are both stated; neither establishes a particular political system.

## Contradictions

State expansion, hierarchy, and increasing freedom are different applications. Their coexistence in the entry does not prove that hierarchy always expands freedom.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Apollon = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair through authority acting at public scale. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names a scientific authority and large masses of people. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends that scale to the state, economic hierarchy, and public trade, while retaining self-governing activity and increasing freedom. These keep institutional reach and independence visible as distinct possibilities rather than validating one political arrangement.
