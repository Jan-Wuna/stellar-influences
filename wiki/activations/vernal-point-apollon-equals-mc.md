## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 52 (one-based).

To possess a broad mental horizon. A wholesale merchant. One who longs for distant places. The scientist.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 52; PDF page: 25 (one-based spread).

My success in the public life, my knowledge of the public, my expansion in the public life, my public experience, my freedom in the public life, spiritual freedom in the public sphere, and increasing public feeling

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personally broad horizons and participation in public knowledge or commerce.
- psychology: Longing for distant places and a broad mental horizon.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: No detailed partnership pattern is supplied.
- events/manifestations: Wholesale trade, scientific work, public experience, and personal freedom.
- conflicts/notes: The entries do not establish that every broad interest becomes a profession.

## Contradictions

Scientific work, trade, and distant places are different expressions of breadth. Public success is one named possibility, not proof of a particular vocation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Apollon = MC`, MC specifies the pair through one's own breadth of outlook and public involvement. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names a broad mental horizon, longing for distant places, the wholesale merchant, and the scientist. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives a personal voice to public knowledge, experience, expansion, and freedom. The organizing meaning is a life or perspective reaching beyond a narrow setting, without requiring one specific profession.
