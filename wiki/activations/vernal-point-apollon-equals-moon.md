## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 52 (one-based).

To have a cheerful or a peaceful mind. Many women. Masses of people. Folklore.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 52; PDF page: 25 (one-based spread).

Experiences of a woman in public, successful woman in public, freedom of women in public Life, freedom of a people in the world, public People's economy, and hour of success in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Collective experience, public freedom, and cheerful or peaceful feeling.
- psychology: A cheerful or peaceful mind.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Women and masses of people in public life.
- events/manifestations: Folklore, a people's economy, and public success.
- conflicts/notes: Women-centered examples remain source framing, not universal female qualities.

## Contradictions

Personal peace of mind and collective public freedom are different scales of application. The entries do not establish that one guarantees the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Apollon = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through people, public experience, and collective culture. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects a cheerful or peaceful mind with masses of people and folklore. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds public success and freedom for women or a people. These retain both feeling and public participation, while the gendered examples remain specific historical framing rather than universal traits.
