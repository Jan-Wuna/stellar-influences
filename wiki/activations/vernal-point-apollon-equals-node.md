## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 52; PDF page: 25 (one-based spread).

Expansion of public relations, successfully connected in public, public trade links, many connections in public, world economic links, and scientific links in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Many public connections in commerce or science.
- psychology: No distinct personal disposition is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Numerous scientific, trading, or world-economic links.
- events/manifestations: Expansion of public relations and networks.
- conflicts/notes: The entries establish breadth of connection, not the closeness or duration of every tie.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize the same broad associative setting without specifying one group or outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Apollon = Node`, Node specifies the pair through many public connections. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) locates those ties in science and commerce; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends them to public relations, trade, and the world economy. The coherent meaning is an extensive network of exchange or shared work, without evidence here that every connection is intimate, durable, or equally successful.
