## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 52 (one-based).

Comprehensive transformations. Development of commerce and science. Changes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 53; PDF page: 25 (one-based spread).

Unfolding of public trade, successful developments in public life, changes in public trade, growing knowledge in public, changes in public freedom, and changing public successes

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Comprehensive change in public trade, science, knowledge, or freedom.
- psychology: No distinct personal disposition is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Changes in public freedom rather than a detailed relationship pattern.
- events/manifestations: Commercial development, growing public knowledge, and changing successes.
- conflicts/notes: The stray R in Witte was an alternative Pluto glyph, removed after scan inspection.

## Contradictions

Success and changing success are compatible with an evolving public situation. The entries do not establish uninterrupted progress.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Apollon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair through comprehensive public development and change. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names commerce and science; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) makes that change specific in trade, growing knowledge, public freedom, and varying success. The organizing meaning is a broad field of exchange being transformed, without assuming that every stage is equally favorable.
