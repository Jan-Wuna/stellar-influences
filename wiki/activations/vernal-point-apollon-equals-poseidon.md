## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 52 (one-based).

Spreading extensive ideas to the world. Knowledge and education about public economy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 53; PDF page: 25 (one-based spread).

Success in the public through truth, public culture success, mind science in the public, intellectual freedom in the public, culture science in the public life, and truth experiences in the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Ideas, education, culture, and public economic knowledge spread widely.
- psychology: Intellectual freedom and experience of truth.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public sharing of knowledge rather than a detailed intimate pattern.
- events/manifestations: World-facing ideas, cultural study, and education about public economy.
- conflicts/notes: Geistes-Wissenschaft is rendered mind science in ABC; no additional technical meaning is inferred.

## Contradictions

The entries share broad intellectual dissemination without a direct disagreement. Public success through truth remains source doctrine, not independent verification.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Apollon = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through widely shared ideas and education. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names ideas spread to the world and knowledge of public economy. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds cultural study, intellectual freedom, and public experience of truth. The integrated meaning is understanding made broadly available, with success and truth kept as the source's stated possibilities rather than certification of a particular doctrine.
