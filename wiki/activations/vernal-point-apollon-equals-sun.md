- psychology: No distinct personal disposition is supplied.

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Apollon = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through personal public success or a focal place of commerce. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names success and an honorary position. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the theme to a growing local center, business, and the world economy, while retaining personal freedom. The organizing meaning is public prominence or effective participation, without evidence here about the means or duration of that success.
