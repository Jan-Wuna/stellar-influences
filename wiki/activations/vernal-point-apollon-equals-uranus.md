## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 52 (one-based).

Sudden expansion. Extended reforms. A public event that causes mass excitement. New branches of science. Revolution in science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 53; PDF page: 25 (one-based spread).

Dynamic world economy, successful public event, new knowledge for the public, expansion of public tension, public technical successes, technical success in public, and increasing reforms in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden public expansion, reform, or scientific innovation.
- psychology: Mass excitement is named.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: A public event affecting many people.
- events/manifestations: New scientific branches, technical success, economic activity, and increasing reforms.
- conflicts/notes: The scan says increasing reforms, not increasing awareness; excitement does not establish approval.

## Contradictions

Successful events and expanding tension can coexist as different public responses. Innovation is not automatically universally welcomed.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Apollon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through sudden public expansion and reform. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names mass excitement, new scientific branches, and revolution in science. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds dynamic economic activity, technical successes, and new public knowledge, while retaining expanding tension. The shared meaning is a broad field changing rapidly, with public excitement left distinct from unanimous support.
