## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 36 (one-based).

To be settled. Low or deeply situated places. Valleys. Mines.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 21; PDF page: 9 (one-based spread).

Blocked environment in public, depression of the partner in public, restriction of others in public life, restrictions in public environment, public congestion on the spot, and public strike on the spot

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public surroundings that are settled, low-lying, or blocked.
- psychology: A partner's depression is attributed source language.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Restriction of others or a partner in public life.
- events/manifestations: Valleys, mines, local congestion, and a public strike.
- conflicts/notes: Low places are literal geographical examples; a settled place is not necessarily blocked.

## Contradictions

Being settled and being blocked are different conditions. The entries do not explain a causal relation between low terrain and a partner's distress.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Asc = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair through the condition of a public place or its nearby people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names being settled and low or deep places such as valleys and mines. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) instead emphasizes blocked surroundings, restrictions, congestion, and a local public strike. These retain geographical depth and social obstruction as distinct expressions rather than treating one as the cause of the other.
