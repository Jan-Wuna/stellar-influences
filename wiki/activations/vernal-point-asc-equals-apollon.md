## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 36 (one-based).

Many meetings. Many customers. Many people. A crowd. Distant places. Much frequented places.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 21; PDF page: 9 (one-based spread).

Expansion of trade in public, expansion of a public place, freedom for others in public, trade partners in public, partner experiences in public life, and environmental success in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A public place or contact circle growing in reach and number.
- psychology: No distinct personal disposition is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Many meetings, people, customers, and trading partners.
- events/manifestations: Crowded or much-frequented places, distant places, and expanding public trade.
- conflicts/notes: Numerous contacts do not establish intimacy or the duration of a partnership.

## Contradictions

The entries emphasize broader contact and place use without an explicit disagreement. Distant and crowded places are alternatives, not one required location.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Asc = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through more people, contacts, or public reach. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names meetings, customers, crowds, and much-frequented or distant places. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds expanding trade, a larger public place, and partner experience. The organizing theme is breadth of public encounter, without assuming that a crowd is a close community or that every trade contact is permanent.
