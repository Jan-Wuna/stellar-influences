## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 36 (one-based).

Family celebration. Sociableness. Brought together by art. To attend a concert. Inhabited country or area. Places. Apartments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 21; PDF page: 9 (one-based spread).

Other communities in public, shared public surroundings, art at a public place, public environmental communities, a local community in public, and partner communities for the surroundings

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: People gathered through family, community, art, or shared surroundings.
- psychology: Sociableness is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Family celebrations, partner communities, and gathering through art.
- events/manifestations: Concerts, inhabited areas, places, and apartments.
- conflicts/notes: The scan says art at a public place, not private public place.

## Contradictions

Family gathering and broader environmental communities are different scales of association, with no explicit contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Asc = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through people sharing a place or communal activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names family celebration, sociableness, concerts, and inhabited places or apartments. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds local and partner communities and art in a public place. The common meaning is a setting that brings people together, without requiring every gathering to be a family or every inhabited place to host a performance.
