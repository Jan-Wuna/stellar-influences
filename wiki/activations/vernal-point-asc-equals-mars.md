## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To do something with others. Work places. Workshops. Training camps.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Widder + Aszendent`
- Source reference: printed page `20`; PDF page `9` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Activities with others in public, work partners in public, work at a public place, partner activity in public, and creating in the public surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared activity and work in public surroundings.
- Psychology: The entries describe doing rather than an inner disposition.
- Body/health: Training camps are settings, not a stated health condition.
- Social/relationship: Working and acting with partners or other people.
- Events/manifestations: Workplaces, workshops, training camps, and work at public places.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's Umwelt-Schaffen concerns activity or creation in the surroundings; it does not specify a profession.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears; one entry names workplaces while the other emphasizes activities and partners in public.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Mars specifies `Vernal Point/Asc` as doing or working with others in public surroundings. The shared activity takes concrete form in workplaces, workshops, training camps, and public work locations. The entries support cooperation in activity without specifying its success, conflict, or a particular profession. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 36; [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 20 / PDF p. 9.)
