## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be deceived by many. Unknown areas. Errors in finding the right location or right direction. No sense of direction. To loose one’s way. Unexplored territories.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Widder + Aszendent`
- Source reference: printed page `21`; PDF page `9` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

A disappointed partner in public, a false partner in public life, strangers in the public surroundings, a weak partner in public, an insecure public place, and water in the public surroundings.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Uncertain or unfamiliar public places and contacts.
- Psychology: Disorientation, deception, or disappointment.
- Body/health: A "weak partner" is not a defined medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Disappointing or false partners; unfamiliar people in public surroundings.
- Events/manifestations: Losing one's way, unexplored areas, insecure places, and water in public surroundings.
- Conflicts/notes: German Fremde can denote strangers or foreign persons; the passage does not determine nationality or make unfamiliar people deceptive.

## Contradictions

The entries share uncertainty but give different examples. Water and unfamiliarity do not establish deception in every public encounter.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `Vernal Point/Asc` through uncertainty about public surroundings or the people met there. Losing one's way in an unfamiliar location parallels the entries' disappointing or unreliable contacts, while insecure places and water supply additional environmental examples. The wording does not justify treating strangers as inherently deceptive. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 36; [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 21 / PDF p. 9.)
