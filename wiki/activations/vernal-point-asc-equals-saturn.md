## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To be alone. Lonely. To take leave of many people. Fields. Villages.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Widder + Aszendent`
- Source reference: printed page `21`; PDF page `9` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Burdens in the public surroundings, patience with the partner in public, public tasks concerning the surroundings, partner problems in public life, disturbances at a public place, and others' losses in public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Separation or burdens within public surroundings.
- Psychology: Loneliness and patience with a public partner.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Taking leave, partner problems, and others' losses.
- Events/manifestations: Fields, villages, environmental tasks, and disturbances at a public place.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC separates patience with a partner from public environmental tasks and says disturbances rather than shocks.

## Contradictions

Loneliness and public duties are different possibilities, not a direct conflict. Rural settings alone do not establish loneliness.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Saturn specifies `Vernal Point/Asc` through separation from company or burdens in the public environment. Taking leave and loneliness stand alongside patience with partners, public tasks, disturbances, and losses affecting others. Fields and villages remain concrete settings in the Witte entry; they do not themselves imply an adverse experience. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 36; [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 21 / PDF p. 9.)
