## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Industrial country. To be connected with others through machine work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Widder + Aszendent`
- Source reference: printed page `21`; PDF page `9` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

An influential partner in public, violence against others in public, others' power in public, a place of strength in public, a strong partner in public life, and influence of the surroundings in public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public surroundings shaped by power or machine work.
- Psychology: No inner response is specified.
- Body/health: Violence is named, but no injury or diagnosis is specified.
- Social/relationship: Strong or influential partners and connections through machine work.
- Events/manifestations: Industrial country, places of power, and public violence as one adverse example.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's influential partner and public violence are separate examples; influence does not itself establish violence.

## Contradictions

Industrial cooperation and public violence are distinct examples; neither cancels the other or makes violence inevitable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vulcanus specifies `Vernal Point/Asc` through powerful public contacts or environments, with industrial settings and machine work providing a concrete form of connection with others. The German ABC entry adds influential or strong partners and, separately, violence against others in public. Power is the shared thread; violence remains one attributed adverse possibility. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 36; [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 21 / PDF p. 9.)
