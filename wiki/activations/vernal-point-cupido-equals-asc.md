## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A partner within a public or local community.
- Psychology: No inner disposition is specified.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Partnership, association, or company membership.
- Events/manifestations: Participation in a local community is the available setting.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC distinguishes local, artistic, partner, and environmental communities without specifying their purpose or success.

## Contradictions

The local, artistic, and partner communities in ABC are compatible with Witte's participant in a partnership, association, or company; neither establishes a particular outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

Asc specifies `Vernal Point/Cupido` through the participant and immediate setting of public group life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names a partner in a partnership, association, or company. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds communities situated at a place and artistic, partner, or environmental communities. Together these identify membership and its setting without prescribing the group's purpose or success.
