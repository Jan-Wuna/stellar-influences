## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Collapse of corporations. Business depressions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Widder + Cupido`
- Source reference: printed page `45`; PDF page `21` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Disadvantaged communities in public, service communities in public life, shared deprivation in public, criminality in a public community, communities of need in public life, and public art criminality.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public community affected by failure, deprivation, service, or criminality.
- Psychology: No personal mental state is specified.
- Body/health: "Business depressions" describes economic conditions, not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Corporations are the Witte collective example.
- Events/manifestations: Corporate collapse, business depression, shared deprivation, and criminality affecting a community or art.
- Conflicts/notes: Business depression is economic language; ABC's criminality and deprivation are particular public-community examples.

## Contradictions

Witte's corporate collapse and ABC's disadvantage or deprivation overlap in their adverse range. ABC also names service communities; service itself is not a claim of criminality.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Hades specifies `Vernal Point/Cupido` through public collective life under adverse conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supplies corporate collapse and business depression. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) broadens the range to disadvantaged or service communities, shared deprivation, communities of need, and criminality in public community or art. These preserve economic failure and need alongside specific criminal examples, without making all service associations criminal.
