## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public association through communication or ideas.
- Psychology: Artistic thoughts and opinions.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Founding an association; youth and media communities.
- Events/manifestations: Public group conversations and transport communities.

## Contradictions

No direct conflict appears; the founder and artistic thoughts are Witte specifics, while the ABC describes communicative communities.

## Derived Synthesis

Mercury specifies `Vernal Point/Cupido` through ideas and communication within public groups: shared language, media, conversation, and artistic thought or opinion. The founder of an association is a particular Witte example; youth and transport communities widen the ABC settings without assigning the founder's role to everyone involved. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 48; [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 44 / PDF p. 21.)
