## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Collapse of a corporation. Fraudulent banks or other institutions. Suppressed organizations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Widder + Cupido`
- Source reference: printed page `45`; PDF page `21` (one-based).

#### ABC Entry

Dissolution of public communities, foreign or unfamiliar communities in public, shared public life by water, a shared future in public, a weak public community, and an uncertain community in public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Uncertain, weakened, or dissolving public community.
- Psychology: Uncertainty is named at community level.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Suppressed organizations and unfamiliar communities.
- Events/manifestations: Corporate collapse, institutional fraud, dissolution, and shared life by water.
- Conflicts/notes: Future and waterside settings do not themselves imply fraud; German "fremde" need not specify nationality.

## Contradictions

The adverse institutional examples coexist with broader ABC settings; life by water and a shared future are not contrary claims about a corporation's condition.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Neptune specifies `Vernal Point/Cupido` through public groups whose cohesion or reliability is uncertain: weakened or dissolving communities, suppressed organizations, and institutions affected by fraud or collapse. The ABC also supplies shared life by water and a shared future as separate possibilities; those settings should not inherit the adverse institutional judgment automatically. ([Witte entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 48; [ABC entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 45 / PDF p. 21.)
