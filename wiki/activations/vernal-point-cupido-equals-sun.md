### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Art center in public, men's community in public life, public art community, people of the arts in public life, community of people in public, personal art in public.

- Source reference: printed p. 44; PDF p. 21. Translation checked against the Sun row on the scan.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A person occupying a place within a public association or artistic community.
- psychology: Being welcome is stated without a fuller disposition.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Companions, associates, a welcome guest, and men's or artistic communities.
- events/manifestations: A corporation director, an art center, and personal art in public.
- conflicts/notes: The restored ABC row supplies reliable artistic detail; its male example remains attributed.

## Contradictions

Companionship, directing a corporation, and an artistic community are different roles or settings rather than conflicting claims.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Cupido = Sun`, Sun specifies the pair through a person's place in public collective life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ranges from companions and a welcome guest to a corporation's director. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds art centers, artistic communities, and personal art in public. These connect belonging with a visible personal role, while leaving companionship and institutional direction as distinct possibilities.
