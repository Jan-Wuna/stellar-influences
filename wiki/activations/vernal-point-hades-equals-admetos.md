## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 49 (one-based).

Lack of raw material. Junk materials and waste.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 47; PDF page: 22 (one-based spread).

Restrictions and deprivations in public, difficult public circulation, dangerous public strike, thoroughgoing shortage in public life, public deepening into the past, and suppression of criminality in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public scarcity, restriction, and deprivation.
- psychology: No personal mental state is specified.
- body/health: Public circulation is process wording here, not a bodily diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Public restriction and a dangerous strike.
- events/manifestations: Raw-material shortage, scrap, waste, and engagement with the past.
- conflicts/notes: Suppression of criminality is a separate source possibility without an explained link to shortage.

## Contradictions

Scarcity and crime suppression are distinct possibilities. The entries establish no causal rise or fall in crime when raw materials are lacking.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Hades = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair through scarcity and restriction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes this concrete in raw-material shortage, scrap, and waste. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds public deprivation, interruption, strike, and deep engagement with the past. Its crime-suppression example remains separate from material shortage; the entries give no single mechanism connecting all these applications.
