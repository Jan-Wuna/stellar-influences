## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 49 (one-based).

Widespread poverty. Far expanded coal fields. Coal-trading.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 47; PDF page: 22 (one-based spread).

Experiences in public service, past successes in public, lack of success in public life, lack of freedom in public, economic criminality in public life, and increasing public danger

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Widespread public or economic deprivation and experience of service.
- psychology: No individual inner response is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public service and restricted freedom.
- events/manifestations: Poverty, extensive coalfields, coal trading, and increasing public danger.
- conflicts/notes: Past successes coexist with lack of success; coal trading does not itself imply crime.

## Contradictions

Past success and lack of success need not concern the same occasion. Material trade and adverse social conditions remain different applications.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Hades = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair through the breadth of public deprivation or economic activity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names widespread poverty and extensive coalfields or coal trading. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds service, restricted freedom, economic crime, and growing danger, while retaining past successes as another possibility. These describe several public settings without making coal trade or service inherently criminal.
