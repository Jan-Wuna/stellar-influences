## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 49 (one-based).

To be impoverished by the general public. To be a physician of the general health system.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 46; PDF page: 22 (one-based spread).

Deficiencies in the public surroundings, danger in the public environment, public environmental service, public renunciation of a partner, public place of tradition, environmental criminality in public, and polluted public surroundings

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Direct dealings with public hardship, service, or a disadvantaged environment.
- psychology: No inner response is specified.
- body/health: A public-health physician is a professional role, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Public impoverishment or renunciation of a partner.
- events/manifestations: Environmental service, pollution, danger, and a traditional place.
- conflicts/notes: The unreadable bullet-only ABC cache has been restored from its scanned Asc row.

## Contradictions

Service, pollution, and public impoverishment are different relations to public need. The entries do not establish that service work causes hardship.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Hades = Asc`, Asc specifies the pair through nearby people and surroundings affected by public need. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names impoverishment through the public or work as a physician in its health system. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds environmental deficiencies, danger, pollution, service, and a traditional place. The organizing theme is direct contact with disadvantage or its service setting, while the professional example remains distinct from a diagnosis.
