## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 49 (one-based).

Great general want. Great depression. General worries through leading forces. Coal mining.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 47; PDF page: 22 (one-based spread).

Lack of public authority, deficiencies of public agencies in public life, great public danger, public disadvantage through the state, independent public service, state criminality in public, and important public renunciation

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public hardship, deficient authority, or worries through those in power.
- psychology: Great depression and worry are source language without an individual diagnosis.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Deficient agencies, state disadvantage, or independent public service.
- events/manifestations: Great general want, coal mining, danger, and state criminality.
- conflicts/notes: Independent service does not mean self-evident service; coal mining remains a distinct material application.

## Contradictions

Service and state criminality are separate possibilities. The entries do not classify every state function or mining activity as harmful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Hades = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair through authority's relation to public need. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names general want and worries through leading forces, with coal mining as another material setting. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds deficient public agencies, state disadvantage, and danger, but also independent public service. These preserve a service role alongside failures or abuses of authority without making them equivalent.
