## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 49 (one-based).

Work scarcity. Faulty, vulgar actions. Carelessness. Brutalities. To work in the general health system. *

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 46; PDF page: 22 (one-based spread).

Work crime in public life, lack in public life, dangerous acts in public, criminal activity in public, dirty work in public, and hidden work in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Work and action around public scarcity, service, or harmful conduct.
- psychology: Carelessness is named; vulgar is Witte's evaluative language.
- body/health: Health-system work is a service role, not a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Brutality and public crime are adverse possibilities.
- events/manifestations: Work scarcity, dangerous actions, dirty work, or hidden work.
- conflicts/notes: Service work must remain distinct from criminal or careless action; Witte's trailing asterisk is unexplained.

## Contradictions

Work in the health system and criminal activity are separate applications. The entry does not make service work inherently harmful.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Hades = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through work and public action. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ranges from work scarcity and careless or brutal action to health-system service. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds dangerous, dirty, hidden, or criminal work. The organizing theme is activity around public need or disadvantage, with constructive service kept distinct from the adverse actions rather than judged by association.
