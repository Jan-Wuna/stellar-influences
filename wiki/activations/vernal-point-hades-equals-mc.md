## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 49 (one-based).

To be influenced, personally or in ones own profession, by general poverty or sad things. A service oriented profession.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 46; PDF page: 22 (one-based spread).

My service in public, my lack in public, my renunciation of publicity, my disadvantage in public, my public past, and inner danger in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal or professional involvement with public hardship and service.
- psychology: Inner danger is ABC's source phrase, without a clinical meaning.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Service to the public or withdrawal from publicity.
- events/manifestations: A service-oriented profession affected by general poverty or sad circumstances.
- conflicts/notes: The scan says inner danger, not spiritual experience.

## Contradictions

Service and withdrawal from public life are different possibilities. The entries do not establish that one profession must bring deprivation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Hades = MC`, MC specifies the pair as one's own encounter with public hardship. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places poverty or sad circumstances within personal or professional life, including a service-oriented profession. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal lack, disadvantage, public history, or renunciation of publicity. Its inner-danger phrase remains source language; the unifying reading is personal involvement in need or service, without a clinical conclusion.
