## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 49 (one-based).

Depressed mind. Poor, base people or women. Miners. Losses of the people. Pessimistic emotions or feelings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 46; PDF page: 22 (one-based spread).

Difficult public service, danger to women in public, lack of feeling in public life, distress of mothers in the world, distress of a people in the world, a time of public danger, and female criminality in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Collective distress, pessimistic feeling, or difficult public service.
- psychology: Depressed mind, pessimism, and lack of feeling are source descriptions.
- body/health: No bodily diagnosis is supplied.
- social/relationship: Distress affecting women, mothers, or a people; miners are another source group.
- events/manifestations: Public losses, danger, and service burdens.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's base-people label and gendered crime language remain historical judgments, not universal traits.

## Contradictions

People in need, miners, and criminal conduct are different source categories. Poverty or gender does not establish wrongdoing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Hades = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through public feeling and the hardships of a people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names pessimism, losses, and miners; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds difficult service, distress of mothers or a people, and public danger. These join collective need with its emotional burden. The class and gender judgments remain attributed source language, separate from the question of who experiences hardship.
