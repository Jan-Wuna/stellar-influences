## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 49 (one-based).

Increasing poverty and misfortune or change from bad to good. Increase or decrease of coal production.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 47; PDF page: 22 (one-based spread).

Developments for the public from the past, dangerous developments in the public life, hidden changes in the public life, growing crime in the public life, and growing public danger

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public hardship or inherited conditions undergoing change.
- psychology: No distinct personal disposition is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Poverty and misfortune may increase or change for the better.
- events/manifestations: Hidden or dangerous developments and increases or decreases in coal production.
- conflicts/notes: Direction remains explicitly open in Witte; ABC's adverse growth does not exhaust the range.

## Contradictions

Witte permits change from bad to good as well as worsening. The coal-production example likewise allows increase or decrease.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Hades = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair through change in public conditions linked to need or the past. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) emphasizes hidden or dangerous developments and growing crime or danger. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) explicitly preserves both increasing hardship and a turn from bad to good, with changing coal production as a material example. The synthesis must therefore keep the direction of change open.
