## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 49 (one-based).

Lack of public education. Mental poverty. Ignorance.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 47; PDF page: 22 (one-based spread).

Dangerous findings in public, mental pollution of public life, lack of ideas in public, clarity about public criminality, cultural crime in public, and lack of culture in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public knowledge or culture affected by deficiency, harmful ideas, or insight into crime.
- psychology: Ignorance and lack of ideas are attributed source descriptions.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Lack of public education and culture.
- events/manifestations: Dangerous findings, clarity about crime, and cultural wrongdoing.
- conflicts/notes: Mental pollution is source imagery; it does not diagnose people or certify one doctrine.

## Contradictions

Lack of knowledge and clarity about crime are different possibilities. The entries do not establish that all difficult knowledge is harmful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Hades = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the pair through the condition of public knowledge and culture. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names educational lack and ignorance; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds deficient ideas and dangerous findings, but also clarity about public crime. These retain impoverished understanding and revealing insight as different possibilities, without adopting mental-pollution imagery as a diagnosis or ideological judgment.
