## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 49 (one-based).

Negligence resulting in loss of life.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 47; PDF page: 22 (one-based spread).

Dangerous public tensions, a dangerous public event, disadvantageous public reforms, new disadvantages in public life, hidden public unrest, and technical deficiencies in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden or dangerous public events, technical deficiencies, and unrest.
- psychology: Public tension and hidden unrest.
- body/health: Witte's negligence with loss of life is historical doctrine, not a forecast for an individual.
- social/relationship: Public reforms may have adverse effects.
- events/manifestations: Dangerous events and technical shortcomings.
- conflicts/notes: The source does not establish a particular failure mechanism or identify who is negligent.

## Contradictions

Negligence and adverse reform are distinct possible settings. The entries do not say that every technical deficiency causes death.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Hades = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the pair through dangerous public events or disruption. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) names tension, adverse reform, hidden unrest, and technical deficiencies. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives negligence resulting in loss of life as a severe historical example. This supports attention to public disruption within the source's range without converting each technical problem into a fatal prediction.
