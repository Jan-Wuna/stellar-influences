## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 49 (one-based).

Ugliness. Dangerous plagues. Capital crimes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 47; PDF page: 22 (one-based spread).

Energy shortage in public, huge disadvantages in public, violence crime in public life, grief by public violence, power of public service, lack of public influence, and hidden public power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public deprivation or danger expressed through power and its absence.
- psychology: Grief under public violence is explicit.
- body/health: Witte names dangerous plagues; this is a source claim, without a diagnosis.
- social/relationship: Public service may wield power while the public lacks influence.
- events/manifestations: Violent crime, energy shortage, and concealed public power are distinct examples.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's ugliness is evaluative; powerful service and powerless public are different situations.

## Contradictions

The entry includes both the power of public service and a lack of public influence. These concern different holders of power and need not describe the same situation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Hades = Vulcanus`, public deficiency becomes a question of force: too little energy or influence, or destructive power imposed on others. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects shortages and major disadvantages with grief through public violence, while also naming powerful service and concealed power. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) concentrates on severe adverse examples, including plagues and capital crimes. These are source possibilities rather than a judgment that every form of public power is criminal.
