## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 43 (one-based).

Would-be fortune. Squandering of substance. Losses. Extravagance.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 35; PDF page: 16 (one-based spread).

Expectations of success in public, counterfeit money in public, joy in public water, financial weakness in public, air law for the public, public legal uncertainty, public water law, and weak public law

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertain or apparent public prosperity, with financial and legal weakness.
- psychology: Expectation of success contrasts with Witte's would-be fortune.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public rights and resources may lack security.
- events/manifestations: Counterfeit money, extravagance, losses, and air or water law are examples.
- conflicts/notes: Joy in water is a separate favorable example; no inevitable loss is inferred.

## Contradictions

Expectations and enjoyment coexist with weak finances and legal uncertainty. The entries distinguish hoped-for fortune from secure material results.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Jupiter = Neptune`, public success is expected or appears possible while its material and legal basis may be weak. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes would-be fortune, extravagance, and losses. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives financial weakness, counterfeit money, and uncertain rights as public examples, while retaining enjoyment of water and air or water law as distinct settings. The central tension is between anticipated benefit and dependable substance.
