## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 43 (one-based).

Fortunate development in the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 35; PDF page: 16 (one-based spread).

Successful developments in the public sphere, growing money weakness in the public sphere, changes in the public sphere Law, favourable development in the public sphere, and changing happiness in the public sphere Life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Changing public prosperity, including favorable development and financial weakness.
- psychology: Changing happiness is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Changes in public law affect the collective setting.
- events/manifestations: Successful developments and growing financial weakness are both named.
- conflicts/notes: Witte stresses fortunate development; ABC retains an adverse financial alternative.

## Contradictions

Favorable public development and growing financial weakness are an explicit difference in emphasis. They may coexist in different areas and should not be collapsed into guaranteed growth.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Jupiter = Pluto`, public fortune is understood as something developing and changing. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) concentrates on fortunate development. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) supports successful or advantageous change but also includes growing financial weakness, changes in public law, and fluctuating happiness. The synthesis preserves development as the shared theme while leaving its financial and legal consequences open.
