## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 43 (one-based).

The love-happiness of women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 34; PDF page: 16 (one-based spread).

Successful beautification of public life, joy in public beauty, success in public peace, harmony and joy in public life, artistic successes in public, and a preference for public law

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public benefit through beauty, art, peace, and harmony.
- psychology: Joy in beauty and a preference for public law are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Witte gives women's happiness in love as an attributed example.
- events/manifestations: Public beautification, artistic success, and successful peace efforts.
- conflicts/notes: Scans restore love, beautification, and art in place of Jove, saving, and artificial success.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The gendered love example is narrower than the public art and peace themes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Jupiter = Venus`, public advantage is expressed as a more beautiful, harmonious, or peaceful shared life. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) joins public beautification and artistic achievement with joy and successful peace efforts. The corrected [Witte entry](#witte-entry) adds women's happiness in love as a specific source example. The interpretation connects aesthetic and relational satisfaction without reducing public harmony to that gendered example.
