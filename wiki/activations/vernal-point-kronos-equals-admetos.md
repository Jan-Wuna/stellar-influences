## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 51 (one-based).

One who is an authority for a few. The overthrow of a government. Mountains. Great altitudes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 51; PDF page: 24 (one-based spread).

Limitation of authority in public life, blocked governing authority in public, restrictions by the state in public, firmness of the state in public, consolidation of independence in public, and a major public strike

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public authority limited, resisted, or consolidated.
- psychology: No distinct psychological claim is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Authority for a few, blocked government, and public strikes.
- events/manifestations: Government overthrow, state restrictions, mountains, and great altitudes.
- conflicts/notes: Firm statehood and blocked authority are distinct possibilities; independence is broader than self-governance.

## Contradictions

The source includes both firmness of the state and its overthrow or blockage. These are opposing outcomes within the theme of bounded authority and should remain separate.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Kronos = Admetos`, public authority is defined by its limits and its capacity to hold firm. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places restricted or blocked government beside consolidated independence and a firm state, with a major strike as an example. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) adds authority for a few and government overthrow, as well as mountains and high altitude. The political reading preserves stability and obstruction as alternatives; the geographic examples remain distinct.
