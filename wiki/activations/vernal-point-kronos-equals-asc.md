## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Kronos = Asc`, public authority is encountered in one's surroundings and relationships. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names independent acquaintances and national territory. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops this through a partner's authority or independence and through prominent public or state places. The shared focus is the presence of authority in the people and spaces around an individual.
