## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Kronos = Cupido`, authority takes a collective or artistic form. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names a manager, public recognition as a master of arts, and leadership of an association. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds artistic authority, hierarchical or independent communities, and a community of states. The reading joins recognized mastery with the organization of a larger body.
