#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 50; PDF page: 24 (one-based spread).

Special good fortune in public, successful authority in public life, financial greatness in public, great joy in public, public legal authority, public successes of the state, and a state governed by law in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful public authority and substantial financial or civic advantage.
- psychology: Great public joy is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Successful leadership and state achievement.
- events/manifestations: Good income, banks, financial transactions, and legal authority.
- conflicts/notes: Financial greatness is qualitative; the entry supplies no amount or financial advice.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Personal income, banking, and state success are different examples of favorable public authority.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Kronos = Jupiter`, authority is specified through success and material or legal advantage. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names a successful leader, good income, banks, and financial dealings. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places successful authority alongside financial scale, public joy, state achievements, and government under law. The emphasis is the favorable capacity and standing of public authority, rather than a promise attached to any particular office.
