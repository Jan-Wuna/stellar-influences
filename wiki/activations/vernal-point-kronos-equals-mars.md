## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 51 (one-based).

Leadership. Great deeds of leading persons or the state. Great deeds in general. Mining and metallurgical industry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 50; PDF page: 24 (one-based spread).

An agency for public relations work, public relations work of the state, independent activity in public, state actions in public, energetic public authority, and important work in public

## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Kronos = Mars`, public authority acts through work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes leadership, major deeds, and mining or metallurgical industry. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds state action, public relations agencies, independent activity, and energetic authority. The connected theme is visible, consequential activity performed by, or in the sphere of, public leadership.
