### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

My chief in public life, my public reputation, my state in public, my dominance in public, my independence in public life, and my self-confidence in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal experience of public authority, independence, and standing.
- psychology: Public self-confidence is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: One's chief and relationship to the state.
- events/manifestations: Extensive power, public reputation, and personal dominance.
- conflicts/notes: The damaged ABC entry omitted dominance and self-confidence; restored from the scan.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Having a chief and being personally independent describe different relations within public authority.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Kronos = MC`, public authority is approached from the personal standpoint: one's chief, reputation, independence, and power. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes extensive power and strong independence. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives the fuller range of public dominance, state affiliation, and self-confidence. The interpretation concerns how authority is personally held or experienced without adding a career or timing layer.
