## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 51 (one-based).

A bad leader. Loss of authority. Loss of power. A government that is not officially recognized.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 51; PDF page: 24 (one-based spread).

Dissolution of public independence, weakness of authority in public, great uncertainty in public life, a public water authority, weak public reputation, and an unknown hierarchy in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public authority weakened, unrecognized, or uncertain.
- psychology: Great uncertainty is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: An unrecognized government and unknown hierarchy.
- events/manifestations: Loss of power, dissolution of independence, and a public water authority.
- conflicts/notes: The water-authority example is administrative and does not itself imply failed leadership.

## Contradictions

The water authority is a concrete administrative example beside adverse descriptions of authority. It should not be automatically classified as weak or illegitimate.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Kronos = Neptune`, the central public question is whether authority has substance, recognition, and independence. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names poor leadership, lost power, and an officially unrecognized government. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds uncertainty, weak reputation, and an unknown hierarchy. Its water-authority example supplies a separate administrative setting, while the main interpretive emphasis remains insecure public standing.
