## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Kronos = Node`, public authority is organized through links between people and institutions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names ruling-circle contacts and international contact between government leaders. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds public hierarchy, an association's reputation, state connections, and important meetings. The reading centers on associations that carry public standing or provide access to authority.
