### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 51 (one-based).

Large agricultural estates. Forestry. Separation and prohibition ordered by the state. Authorities issue orders to evacuate, a curfew or a shut down.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 51; PDF page: 24 (one-based spread).

Hard authority in public, public problems of the state, public reputation in old age, state-imposed burdens on the public, important tasks for the public, state losses in public, and loss of public reputation

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public authority exercised through limits, obligations, and burdens.
- psychology: No distinct psychological claim is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: State prohibitions and orders affecting the public.
- events/manifestations: Evacuations, curfews, shutdowns, forestry, and large agricultural estates.
- conflicts/notes: Public reputation in old age is retained beside lost reputation; authority replaces erroneous authorship.

## Contradictions

Reputation in old age and loss of reputation are separate possibilities. Agricultural or forestry estates are concrete examples distinct from coercive state orders.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Kronos = Saturn`, public authority appears through boundaries, difficult tasks, and imposed obligations. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives prohibitions, evacuations, curfews, shutdowns, and large agricultural or forestry holdings. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) emphasizes hard authority, state problems, public burdens, and losses, while retaining reputation in old age. The reading concerns the restrictive or weight-bearing side of authority without assigning every example the same outcome.
