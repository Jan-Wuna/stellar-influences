#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 51; PDF page: 24 (one-based spread).

A special public event, dominance of technology in public, new authority in public life, excitement of the public hierarchy, public unrest in the state, state reforms in public, and a state event in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public authority disrupted or renewed by events, technology, and reform.
- psychology: Extensive excitement or tension is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Sudden events in ruling circles and an excited public hierarchy.
- events/manifestations: Technical establishments, new authority, unrest, and state reforms.
- conflicts/notes: New authority replaces erroneous authorship; reform is not automatically favorable or harmful.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Technological establishments, reform, and unrest are different expressions of abrupt movement in public authority.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Kronos = Uranus`, public authority is drawn into sudden events and technical or political change. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names tension, events in ruling circles, and technical establishments. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds new authority, an excited hierarchy, unrest, and state reforms. The synthesis centers on an altered public order, leaving the result of the change unspecified.
