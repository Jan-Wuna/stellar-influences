## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 51 (one-based).

Great philanthropic. The peace movement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 50; PDF page: 24 (one-based spread).

Public esteem for beauty, special art in public, artistic authority in public life, harmony between the state and public, a preference for public authorities, and precedence of beauty in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public authority and standing expressed through beauty, art, and harmony.
- psychology: A preference for public authorities is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Harmony between the state and public; Witte's peace movement.
- events/manifestations: Esteemed beauty, special art, and artistic authority.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's philanthropic phrase is evaluative; art and authority replace mistranslated artist wording.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Esteem for authority and a peace movement are distinct associations, without implying that all authority is peaceful.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Kronos = Venus`, public standing is related to beauty, artistic mastery, and harmonious relations. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) names esteemed beauty, artistic authority, and harmony between state and public. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) adds a philanthropic emphasis and the peace movement. The reading connects recognized cultural value with a conciliatory public role, while keeping admiration for authorities as a separate source possibility.
