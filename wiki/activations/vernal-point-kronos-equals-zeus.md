## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Kronos = Zeus`, public authority is expressed through focused leadership and substantial effort. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names outstanding accomplishments and achieving something great. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) specifies state efforts, dominant leadership, special goals, and public achievements. Both entries also name a large fire, retained as a separate concrete manifestation rather than a metaphor imposed on every achievement.
