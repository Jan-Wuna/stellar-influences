## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 42 (one-based).

Working on special subjects. Limited field of activity. Oppression and terror.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 33; PDF page: 15 (one-based spread).

Blocked activities in public, depressing work in public, restriction of work for the public, thorough public relations work, a public labor strike, and suppression of the public will

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public action narrowed, blocked, or concentrated in a special field.
- psychology: Depressing work is explicit; it is not a clinical diagnosis.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Labor strikes, oppression, and suppression of public will.
- events/manifestations: Specialist work, limited activity, and thorough public relations work.
- conflicts/notes: Thorough work is retained alongside blocked activity; it is not another phrase for failure.

## Contradictions

Specialized and thorough work can be productive within a limited field, whereas strikes, oppression, and blockage describe obstruction. Both directions remain explicit.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mars = Admetos`, public activity encounters a narrow field or a firm limit. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) places specialist work beside limited activity, oppression, and terror. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds blocked or depressing work, strikes, and suppression of public will, while retaining thorough public relations work. The synthesis distinguishes concentrated effort from prevented action rather than treating every limitation as defeat.
