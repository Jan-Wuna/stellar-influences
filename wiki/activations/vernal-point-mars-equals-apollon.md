## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 42 (one-based).

Undertakings in commerce and industry. Scientific work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 33; PDF page: 15 (one-based spread).

Expansion of public work, peace activity in public, commercial activity in public life, economic activity in public life, creating knowledge for the public, and increasing public relations work

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public activity expanding through commerce, industry, knowledge, or peace work.
- psychology: No distinct psychological claim is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Work contributes to a wider public.
- events/manifestations: Commercial and scientific undertakings, economic activity, and public relations.
- conflicts/notes: Increasing public relations work is more specific than the former generic public work clause.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Scientific, commercial, and peace activities are separate settings for expanded work.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mars = Apollon`, public work extends its reach through trade, industry, and knowledge. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names commercial or industrial undertakings and scientific work. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds economic and peace activity, creating knowledge for the public, and increasing public relations work. The common theme is activity serving a broader field of exchange and participation.
