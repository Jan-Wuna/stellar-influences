## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 42 (one-based).

To act faultily. Faulty work. Archaeological work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 33; PDF page: 15 (one-based spread).

Work in public service, dangerous activity in public life, impulses from the past in public, criminal actions in public, deficient work in public life, and hidden activity in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public work involving defects, danger, concealment, or the past.
- psychology: No distinct psychological claim is supplied.
- body/health: Dangerous activity is named, without a specific health claim.
- social/relationship: Public service is retained as a separate form of work.
- events/manifestations: Faulty work, archaeology, concealed activity, and criminal acts.
- conflicts/notes: Archaeology and public service are not equated with criminality.

## Contradictions

Useful work on the past and public service coexist with faulty or criminal action. These must remain separate source examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mars = Hades`, public activity meets what is defective, dangerous, hidden, or left from the past. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) pairs faulty action with archaeological work. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds service, past impulses, concealed activity, and explicitly criminal acts. The connected reading preserves work with difficult or historical material without making that work itself culpable.
