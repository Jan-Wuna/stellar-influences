#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 32; PDF page: 15 (one-based spread).

Conversations about public actions, youth work in public life, public conversations about art, media work in public life, public news activity, the public will of youth, and activity in public transport

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public activity expressed through thought, communication, and transport.
- psychology: Thinking and the power of thought are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Youth work and conversations about public action or art.
- events/manifestations: Media, news, creative action, and public transport.
- conflicts/notes: Conversations about art replaces the imprecise artistic-language rendering.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Transport, media, and artistic conversations are distinct channels of public activity.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mars = Mercury`, thought and communication become active contributions to public life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins thinking and its power with energetic creation. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives conversations about public action and art, youth work, media, news, and transport. The shared emphasis is the work of communicating, moving, or organizing ideas in the public sphere.
