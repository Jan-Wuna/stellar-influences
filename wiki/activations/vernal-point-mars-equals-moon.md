## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 42 (one-based).

Deeds or activities of a woman or the public. Warring peoples.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 32; PDF page: 15 (one-based spread).

Work hours in public, feelings acts in public life, acting women in public, life of the people in public, public women activities, public will of women, and people will in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public activity expressed through collective life, feeling, and the source's examples of women.
- psychology: Actions arising from feeling and collective will are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Women's public activity and the will of a people.
- events/manifestations: Work hours, public deeds, and warring peoples.
- conflicts/notes: Warring peoples is an adverse example; gendered associations remain attributed source doctrine.

## Contradictions

The neutral activity of women or the public is broader than the warfare example. Public action is not reduced to conflict.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mars = Moon`, public action is carried by people, feelings, and collective will. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names the activity of a woman or the public and adds warring peoples as an adverse example. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops emotional action, women's public work, and a people's will. The synthesis preserves ordinary participation alongside conflict and attributes the gendered examples to the sources.
