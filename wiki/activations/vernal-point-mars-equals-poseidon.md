## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The general morality. Mental activity. Philosophical activity or labor.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `33`, one-based PDF p. `15`, complete Poseidon row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Arbeit in der Welt des Geistes, geistige Tatkraft für das öffentliche Leben, Klarheit des öffentlichen Willens, Kultur-Schaffen in der Öffentlichkeit, vernünftige Öffentlichkeits-Arbeit, Wahrheit schaffen für die Öffentlichkeit

Editorial translation: Work in the world of the mind; intellectual drive for public life; clarity of public will; cultural creation in public; rational public work; creating truth for the public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public work directed toward intellectual, philosophical, or moral purposes.
- Psychology: Clarity of public intention and mental effort.
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Cultural work and making truth available to the public.
- Events/manifestations: Philosophical labor and public intellectual activity.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC names rational public work; it does not establish a botanical meaning or specify a single moral doctrine.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in intellectual work but differ in emphasis: morality and philosophy in Witte, public clarity and cultural production in ABC. This is no direct contradiction. The scanned ABC row names rational public work alongside clarity and cultural creation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mars = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies public activity as work of the mind: effort is directed toward philosophical thought, moral questions, cultural creation, and clearer public understanding. The connection is the purpose of the work, rather than activity alone, supported by the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 42) and the complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 33; PDF p. 15).
