## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A day of public work. Special work. The man's activities or his labors. A fight or battle action.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Work man in public, manly people in public, personal creation for the public, creating people in public life, day of work in public, and center for public work

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A person's labor becomes a visible part of public activity.
- Psychology: Personal effort is indicated; neither entry develops motives or character.
- Body/health: No medical claim is supplied; Witte's battle example does not establish injury.
- Social/relationship: Workers and people creating for public life are foregrounded.
- Events/manifestations: A day of public work, special labor, or a fight or battle action.
- Conflicts/notes: The sources' male wording describes their historical examples, not a rule excluding other people.

## Contradictions

The entries converge on personal labor in public. Witte adds combat, while ABC emphasizes public creation; these are different manifestations rather than mutually exclusive claims. The awkward ABC translation should not turn "manly" into a personality judgment.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mars = Sun`, the Sun brings the person doing the work to the foreground of public activity. A workday, special task, or public act becomes identifiable with someone's own labor; Witte also gives fighting or battle as an example. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 42) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 32; PDF p. 15) support this personal focus, while their male examples remain historically attributed rather than a universal gender restriction.
