## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- Source reference: one-based PDF p. 42, Uranus row under `Aries + Mars`.

Sudden action. Injury.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. 33; one-based PDF p. 15, complete Uranus row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Aufregende Aktionen in der Öffentlichkeit, erregter öffentlicher Wille, neue Impulse für die Öffentlichkeit, Reform-Schaffen im öffentlichen Leben, Spannungs-Handlungen in der Öffentlichkeit, technische Tätigkeit im öffentlichen Leben

Editorial translation: Exciting actions in public; excited public will; new impulses for the public; reform work in public life; actions under tension in public; technical activity in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden public action, excitement and new impulses in the wider world. [Witte](#witte-entry); [ABC](#abc-entry).
- psychology: ABC names excited public will; neither entry establishes an individual's motive or permanent temperament.
- body/health: Witte lists injury without specifying type, severity or likelihood.
- social/relationship: ABC places action in public life rather than identifying a particular personal relationship.
- events/manifestations: Reform work, technical activity and public actions under tension broaden Witte's brief sudden-action example.
- conflicts/notes: Excitement or tension does not by itself establish conflict or a causal route from haste to injury.

## Contradictions

ABC's public reform, technical work and tension describe settings beyond Witte's two brief terms. They do not establish that sudden action necessarily causes injury or that public tension must become conflict.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mars = Uranus`, Uranus specifies activity in the wider world through sudden action, public excitement and new impulses. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) makes that public setting concrete through reform work, technical activity and actions under tension. These expressions connect the pair's activity with an excited or changing public situation.

The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) separately lists sudden action and injury. Injury remains an attributed historical example; neither entry identifies an inevitable event or establishes that haste causes harm.
