## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

One’s unchanging attitude while facing the public. Hard to influence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `19`, one-based PDF p. `8`, complete Admetos row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Mein öffentlicher Widerstand, meine Beschränkungen im öffentlichen Leben, meine Festigung in der Öffentlichkeit, meine Freiheit in der Öffentlichkeit, seelische Blockade in der Öffentlichkeit, seelische Unterdrückung im öffentlichen Leben

Editorial translation: My public resistance; my restrictions in public life; my consolidation in public; my freedom in public; inner blockade in public; inner oppression in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A fixed personal stance toward public life.
- Psychology: Resistance to influence, inner blockage, and consolidation.
- Body/health: ABC names inner oppression and blockade, without supplying a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Holding one's position in the face of public pressure or limitation.
- Events/manifestations: Public resistance, restriction, and also ABC's stated freedom.
- Conflicts/notes: The source's inner-life terms do not establish a clinical condition; restriction and freedom remain separate expressions.

## Contradictions

ABC includes both limitations and freedom, without specifying the conditions separating them. They remain a tension in its range. Witte's resistance to influence supports a fixed stance, not a resolution of that tension.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/MC = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the person's public stance as difficult to shift. That firmness can appear as resistance or consolidation, while the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 19; PDF p. 8) also describes restrictions and inner blockage. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 35) supports constancy in facing the public; ABC's separate freedom motif prevents reducing the whole entry to confinement alone.
