## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Life’s experiences (external). Aspiring. The will to expand. Ones general attitude to religions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

My success in public, my trade in public, my knowledge of the public, my expansion in public, experiences in public life, and mental experiences in public life

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Expansion of one's participation in public life through experience.
- Psychology: Aspiration, the will to expand, and inward responses to public experience.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim appears.
- Social/relationship: Public trade, knowledge of the public, and attitudes toward religions.
- Events/manifestations: Public success, commercial activity, and widening experience.
- Conflicts/notes: Success is one listed manifestation, not a promised result of aspiration.

## Contradictions

The entries share experience and expansion. Witte's religious attitude and ABC's trade and knowledge broaden the range without directly contradicting each other; the entries do not establish independent confirmation of every example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/MC = Apollon`, Apollon specifies one's public orientation through aspiration and expanding experience. The person seeks a wider field of involvement, knowledge, or trade; success is one named expression rather than an assured outcome. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 35) also includes attitudes to religions, while the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 19; PDF p. 8) locates experience and expansion explicitly in public life.
