## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To make friendships which influence the profession. Meetings outside the home. At this moment and place in the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `18`, one-based PDF p. `8`, complete Asc row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Beseelte Umwelt in der Öffentlichkeit, meine Umwelt ist die Öffentlichkeit, mein Partner im öffentlichen Leben, mein Platz in der Öffentlichkeit, Öffentlichkeit meines Partners, andere in meiner Welt, ich in der Welt anderer

Editorial translation: Soulful surroundings in public; my surroundings are the public; my partner in public life; my place in public; my partner's public sphere; others in my world; I in the world of others.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: One's public place is defined through encounters and surroundings.
- Psychology: The entries describe participation, not a developed inner disposition.
- Body/health: No medical claim is supplied.
- Social/relationship: Professional friendships, partners in public life, and reciprocal entry into others' worlds.
- Events/manifestations: Meetings outside the home and being present at a public place.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's soulful surroundings retains its inner-life vocabulary without specifying how the surroundings are animated.

## Contradictions

Public meetings in Witte and public surroundings and partners in ABC are compatible emphases. The entries do not equate every public encounter with a professional friendship. ABC adds a soulful quality to the public surroundings without explaining its mechanism.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/MC = Asc`, Asc specifies one's public position through the people and surroundings encountered there. The emphasis is on being present with others: meetings outside the home, partnerships in public life, and friendships that can affect the profession. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 35) and the complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 18; PDF p. 8) support this reciprocal meeting of one's own world and other people's worlds.
