## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

A sense of sociability. To look and to be social. To have dealings with the arts, associations or groups.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `19`, one-based PDF p. `8`, complete Cupido row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Beseelte Welt-Gemeinschaft, beseelte Gemeinschaft im öffentlichen Leben, meine Gemeinschaft in der Öffentlichkeit, meine Kunst in der Öffentlichkeit, Minute der Gemeinschaft in der Öffentlichkeit, öffentliche Seelen-Gemeinschaft

Editorial translation: A soulful world community; soulful community in public life; my community in public; my art in public; a minute of community in public; a public community of souls.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal public life takes shape through sociability, community, and art.
- Psychology: A sense of sociability and connection with a community.
- Body/health: No bodily claim is present.
- Social/relationship: Associations, groups, and one's public community.
- Events/manifestations: Dealings with art and collective social activity.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC uses soul/community language and a literal minute-of-community phrase without explaining the latter; no timing technique follows.

## Contradictions

The complete entries share social and artistic involvement. ABC adds soul/community language and a minute-of-community expression whose significance is not explained.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/MC = Cupido`, Cupido specifies one's public involvement through belonging and sociability. Public life is encountered in groups, associations, and artistic dealings, making community and art part of one's own participation. This reading follows the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 35) and the community and art clauses of the complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 19; PDF p. 8), while leaving its unexplained minute-of-community expression as a specific source phrase.
