## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Being together officially. To appear leading. Strive for public leadership.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `19`, one-based PDF p. `8`, complete Kronos row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Das innere Wesen des Staates in der Öffentlichkeit, große Seele in der Öffentlichkeit, mein Ansehen in der Welt, meine besondere Welt, meine Selbständigkeit im öffentlichen Leben, wichtige Minute in der Öffentlichkeit

Editorial translation: The inner nature of the state in public; a great soul in public; my reputation in the world; my special world; my independence in public life; an important minute in public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal standing in official or leading public roles.
- Psychology: Striving for leadership, public reputation and ABC's independence in public life.
- Body/health: Neither entry gives a bodily claim.
- Social/relationship: Official association and a leading appearance among others.
- Events/manifestations: Public leadership, reputation, and ABC's state-related motif.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC names independence, not self-pity; its abstract state/soul and important-minute language is not explained further.

## Contradictions

The entries share prominence and standing. ABC adds independence; its abstract state and soul language does not define every form of leadership.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/MC = Kronos`, Kronos specifies one's public position through official standing or leadership: appearing to lead and seeking such a role connect with reputation in the world. This follows the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 35) and the reputation and independence clauses of the complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 19; PDF p. 8). ABC retains an additional important-minute expression without specifying a timing method.
