## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The emotional attitude toward publicity or women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `18`, one-based PDF p. `8`, complete Moon row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Beseelte Frau im öffentlichen Leben, beseelte Gefühle in der Öffentlichkeit, beseelte Stunde in der Öffentlichkeit, meine Frau in der Öffentlichkeit, meine Gefühle in der Öffentlichkeit, ich als Frau in der Öffentlichkeit

Editorial translation: A soulful woman in public life; soulful feelings in public; a soulful hour in public; my wife in public; my feelings in public; I as a woman in public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: One's emotional relation to public life.
- Psychology: Emotional attitude toward publicity or women in Witte.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical conclusion is supplied.
- Social/relationship: ABC names a wife and a woman speaking of her own public life.
- Events/manifestations: Public presence of those women and the person's feelings; ABC also names a soulful hour without supplying timing conditions.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC names feelings and soulful qualities, not animals, flowers or blessings; its gendered examples remain attributed rather than universal.

## Contradictions

Witte's emotional focus and ABC's female examples are compatible but do not support a universal account of women. ABC explicitly names soulful feelings and my feelings alongside its female examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/MC = Moon`, the Moon specifies one's public orientation through emotional attitude. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 35) supplies this directly; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 18; PDF p. 8) gives a wife or a woman in public life as particular examples. Those examples remain attributed and do not make emotion exclusive to women; ABC also names the person's own feelings and soulful feelings in public.
