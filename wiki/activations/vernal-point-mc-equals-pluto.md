## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The development of one’s own attitude toward the world in general.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `19`, one-based PDF p. `8`, complete Pluto row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Entwicklung in einer beseelten Öffentlichkeit, ich verändere die Öffentlichkeit, meine Entwicklung in der Öffentlichkeit, mein Wechsel in die Öffentlichkeit, seelische Entfaltung in der Öffentlichkeit, seelische Wandlungen im öffentlichen Leben

Editorial translation: Development within a soulful public sphere; I change the public sphere; my development in public; my transition into public life; inner unfolding in public; inner transformations in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Development of one's attitude and involvement toward the public world.
- Psychology: A changing personal attitude toward the world.
- Body/health: No bodily claim is made.
- Social/relationship: ABC also gives the person an active role in changing the public.
- Events/manifestations: Personal development, transition into public life and inner unfolding or transformation, without a specified outcome.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC distinguishes changing the public sphere from personal development and inner transformation; it does not name changing the public mind.

## Contradictions

Witte describes change in one's attitude, ABC also change directed toward the public. These are distinct directions of change, not equivalent or mutually exclusive claims. The complete ABC row also names personal development, transition into public life and inner transformations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/MC = Pluto`, Pluto specifies one's public orientation as developing rather than fixed. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 35) concerns the evolution of one's attitude toward the world; the "I change the public sphere" clause of the complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 19; PDF p. 8) also gives change an outward direction. Neither establishes the means, scale, or success of such influence.
