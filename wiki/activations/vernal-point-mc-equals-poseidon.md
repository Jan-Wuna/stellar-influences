## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Strive for public education. Knowledge about the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Inspired public culture, spirit soul of the world, my insights about the public, my spiritual world, my ideas for the public, my clarity in public life, and minute of truth in public

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal understanding directed toward public knowledge and culture.
- Psychology: Insight, ideas, and clarity concerning public life.
- Body/health: No medical claim is given.
- Social/relationship: Striving for public education.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural participation and a moment of truth in public.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's spirit-soul language is not accompanied by a defined metaphysical mechanism.

## Contradictions

Education and knowledge in Witte are compatible with ABC's insight and clarity. ABC's spiritual language is an additional emphasis, not proof that all public ideas are true.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/MC = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies one's public orientation through understanding: knowledge of public life becomes a basis for education, ideas, and cultural participation. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 35) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 19; PDF p. 8) connect public education with personal insight and clarity, while leaving the content and truth of particular ideas unspecified.
