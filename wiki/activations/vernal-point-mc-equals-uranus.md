## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To experience something with others. To show oneself eccentric. To be a revolutionary. Excitement in public. To be an environmental engineer.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `19`, one-based PDF p. `8`, complete Uranus row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Beseelter Rhythmus in der Öffentlichkeit, meine Reformen im öffentlichen Leben, meine Unruhe im öffentlichen Leben, Minute der Aufregung in der Öffentlichkeit, seelische Erregung in der Öffentlichkeit, seelische Impulse der Öffentlichkeit

Editorial translation: A soulful rhythm in public; my reforms in public life; my unrest in public life; a minute of excitement in public; inner excitement in public; inner impulses of the public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public involvement expressed through excitement or an unconventional presence.
- Psychology: Eccentric self-presentation and public excitement in Witte; ABC adds personal unrest and inner excitement.
- Body/health: No particular medical condition is stated.
- Social/relationship: Sharing an experience with others.
- Events/manifestations: Reforms in public life, revolutionary activity and Witte's environmental-engineer example.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC names reforms, unrest, a soulful rhythm and a minute of excitement; it does not identify a publication event or a timing method.

## Contradictions

Witte's varied examples are not stated as necessary companions. ABC overlaps in excitement and adds public reforms and inner impulses; neither entry makes these varied examples necessary companions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/MC = Uranus`, Uranus specifies one's public presence through excitement and an unconventional or revolutionary expression. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 35) also includes experiencing something with others and the specific occupation of environmental engineer, without explaining how those examples are linked. The complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 19; PDF p. 8) adds the person's reforms and unrest, a soulful public rhythm and inner excitement or impulses; its minute-of-excitement phrase does not establish timing conditions.
