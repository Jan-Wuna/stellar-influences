## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To make-up oneself for others. Being liked. Looking attractive to the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `18`, one-based PDF p. `8`, complete Venus row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Durch Kunst beseelte Öffentlichkeit, mein Frieden mit der Öffentlichkeit, meine Freude im öffentlichen Leben, meine Vorliebe für die Öffentlichkeit, meine Wünsche für die Öffentlichkeit, Seelen-Harmonie im öffentlichen Leben

Editorial translation: The public sphere made soulful through art; my peace with the public; my joy in public life; my preference for public life; my wishes for the public; harmony of soul in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: An agreeable or attractive personal relationship with the public.
- Psychology: Joy, fondness, and wishes concerning public life.
- Body/health: Making oneself attractive is described, not a medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Being liked and being at peace with the public.
- Events/manifestations: Preparing one's appearance for others, enjoying public participation and art giving the public sphere a soulful quality.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC names art giving the public sphere a soulful quality and harmony of soul; neither entry guarantees public approval.

## Contradictions

Witte's public attractiveness and ABC's enjoyment of public life describe outward reception and inward response. They are compatible without implying that one guarantees the other.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/MC = Venus`, Venus specifies one's public involvement through attraction and pleasure: attention to appearance accompanies being liked, while public life itself can be enjoyed. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 35) and the peace, joy, art and preference clauses of the complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 18; PDF p. 8) support an agreeable relation with the public, rather than a promise of approval.
