## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To execute power in general.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

my power for the public, my strength in public life, minute of violence in public, and mental violence in public life

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personally exercising power in public life.
- Psychology: ABC includes mental violence; no motive or personality diagnosis is given.
- Body/health: Strength is named without a health condition.
- Social/relationship: Power used for the public and potentially coercive public dealings.
- Events/manifestations: Public strength and a moment of violence in ABC.
- Conflicts/notes: Power and violence are related source motifs, not interchangeable definitions.

## Contradictions

Witte's general exercise of power is broader than ABC's violent examples. This is a difference of scope, not a requirement that power be violent or beneficial.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/MC = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies one's public participation through the exercise of power and strength. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 35) gives the broad action; the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 19; PDF p. 8) places that strength in public life and also names physical or mental violence. Those severe examples remain within the source's range without defining every exercise of public power.
