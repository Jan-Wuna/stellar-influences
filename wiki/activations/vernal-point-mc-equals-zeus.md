## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To stick together (people). Comradeship. To be a leader. The end at which a person aims.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `19`, one-based PDF p. `8`, complete Zeus row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Beseelte Führung in der Öffentlichkeit, mein Start ins öffentliche Leben, mein Zielstreben in der Öffentlichkeit, meine Leistungen in der Öffentlichkeit, meine Öffentlichkeits-Gestaltung, Minute des Feuers in der Öffentlichkeit

Editorial translation: Soulful leadership in public; my start in public life; my striving toward goals in public; my achievements in public; my shaping of public life; a minute of fire in public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Personal public involvement organized around an aim.
- Psychology: Purpose and striving toward a goal.
- Body/health: The fire example supplies no medical conclusion.
- Social/relationship: Comradeship, sticking together, and leadership.
- Events/manifestations: A public start, achievement, shaping public activity, and ABC's fire motif.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC names soulful leadership and a minute of fire; it gives no rule that all leadership involves fire or timing method for that expression.

## Contradictions

The entries share aims and purposeful public involvement. Witte's comradeship adds a collective dimension; ABC's fire is a specific manifestation, not a claim that all leadership involves fire.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/MC = Zeus`, Zeus specifies one's public orientation through a goal pursued with others. Leadership and comradeship concern how people hold together around an aim, while public beginnings and achievements give that aim an outward form. This follows the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 35) and the leadership, goal and achievement clauses of the complete [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 19; PDF p. 8); the source's soulful leadership and specific fire expression remain distinct.
