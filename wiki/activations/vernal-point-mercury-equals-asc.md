## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mercury = Asc`, public thought is encountered through the immediate environment and other people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states the theme as contact with public opinion. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) supplies media, local and environmental news, young people in public places, and partners in transport. The coherent focus is the interpersonal and local setting in which public information is exchanged.
