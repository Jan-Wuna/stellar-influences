## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 40 (one-based).

General topics of conversation. Talkativeness. Masses indulging themselves in conversation. Expectancies.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 28; PDF page: 13 (one-based spread).

Successful public speaking, financial discussions in public life, joy about public news, media successes in public, public success of youth, and legal opinions in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public conversation associated with success, favorable news, and expectations.
- psychology: Talkativeness, expectancy, and joy about news are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Many people engaging in conversation and successful youth.
- events/manifestations: Financial discussion, media success, and legal opinion.
- conflicts/notes: Expectation is not proof that a favorable outcome has occurred.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Conversation and expectation are broader than ABC's explicitly successful examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mercury = Jupiter`, public thought becomes active conversation, often carrying expectation or welcome news. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes talkativeness and the many people engaged in it. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful discussion, financial and legal topics, media achievement, and joy about public news. The shared emphasis is favorable exchange and anticipation, with the outcome of any expectation left open.
