## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 40 (one-based).

To be interested in general opinion. To be influenced by public opinion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 28; PDF page: 13 (one-based spread).

Inwardly animated youth in public, inwardly animated public opinion, public conversations about me, my thoughts about the public, my public conversations, and my youth in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal involvement in public thought, speech, and opinion.
- psychology: Interest in and influence by public opinion; ABC's inward animation.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public discussion about oneself and one's own public conversations.
- events/manifestations: Personal thoughts and youth situated in public life.
- conflicts/notes: Beseelt means inwardly animated or ensouled; it does not supply the former secret-opinion claim.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Being influenced by public opinion and contributing one's own speech describe different directions of involvement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mercury = MC`, public opinion becomes personally significant. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes interest in general opinion and being influenced by it. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places one's thoughts and conversations alongside public discussion about oneself, using inwardly animated language for youth and opinion. The reading links personal expression with receptivity to the public atmosphere; it adds no secrecy claim.
