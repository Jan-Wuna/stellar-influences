## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 40 (one-based).

Confusion in general. Ideas floating in the air which are mostly unrealizable. General bewilderment. Talks, opinions, news which creates more confusion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 29; PDF page: 13 (one-based spread).

Disappointed youth in public life, foreign languages in public, public air traffic, public water reports, insecure youth in public life, and insecurity through public media

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public ideas and news that lack clarity or dependable realization.
- psychology: Confusion, bewilderment, disappointment, and insecurity are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Insecure or disappointed youth and uncertainty through media.
- events/manifestations: Foreign languages, air traffic, and water reports are concrete settings.
- conflicts/notes: Foreign languages and transport are not inherently confusing or deceptive.

## Contradictions

The adverse communication theme does not make the neutral language and transport examples inherently harmful. The sources describe multiple possibilities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mercury = Neptune`, public information can become difficult to understand or realize. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes bewilderment, impractical ideas, and news that adds confusion. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops disappointment and insecurity among youth and through media, while separately naming foreign languages, air traffic, and water reports. The synthesis keeps uncertainty central without treating every unfamiliar language or transport setting as deceptive.
