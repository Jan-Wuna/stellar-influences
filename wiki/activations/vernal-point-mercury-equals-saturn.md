## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 40 (one-based).

Beginning of a journey. General thoughts of separations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 29; PDF page: 13 (one-based spread).

Media tasks in public, news about losses in public life, youth and age in public life, difficult youth in public, losses in public traffic, and public thoughts of separation

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public communication concerned with separation, loss, and age.
- psychology: Thoughts of separation are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Youth and age, including difficult youth, in public life.
- events/manifestations: Beginning a journey, media tasks, and news of losses.
- conflicts/notes: Journey beginnings need not imply bereavement or a specific loss.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The start of a journey is broader than the source's adverse loss examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Mercury = Saturn`, public thought and news concern departures, limits, and losses. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names beginning a journey and thoughts of separation. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds media tasks, news of loss, difficulties of youth, age, and losses in traffic. The synthesis preserves a separation theme without assigning every departure an adverse outcome.
