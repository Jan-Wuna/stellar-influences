## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 38 (one-based).

Anthropology. Ethnography. Many women or nations. Masses of people. Women related sciences. Religious women in public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 25; PDF page: 11 (one-based spread).

An experienced woman in public, experiences with women in public, success of the mothers of the world, a successful woman in public life, joy in women's world, trade in women in public, and many women in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public collective life extended through experience, numbers, and fields concerning women.
- psychology: Experience and joy are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Many women or nations, mothers, and masses of people.
- events/manifestations: Anthropology, ethnography, women-related sciences, and religious women.
- conflicts/notes: Frauen-Handel is retained literally as trade in women; its context is not clarified by the entry.

## Contradictions

Scientific and religious examples coexist with success and the ambiguous trade-in-women compound. They must not be merged into a uniformly favorable reading.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Moon = Apollon`, the public theme broadens through many people, accumulated experience, and the sources' fields concerning women. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names anthropology, ethnography, crowds, sciences, and religious women. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds experience, success, mothers, and joy, while retaining its trade-in-women wording without resolving that compound beyond the evidence. The synthesis preserves breadth and experience without flattening every example into benefit.
