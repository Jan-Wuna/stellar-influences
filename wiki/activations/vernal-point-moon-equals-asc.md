## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Moon = Asc`, public life is encountered through others and the places where people meet. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives acquaintances with women, meetings elsewhere, and participation in a course. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds women as partners, the public environment, and places for a people's public life. The common focus is the social and spatial setting of participation, with gendered examples attributed to the sources.
