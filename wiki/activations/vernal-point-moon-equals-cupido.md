## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 38 (one-based).

The bride. The housewife. The female artist. Union of peoples.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 25; PDF page: 11 (one-based spread).

Women's communities in public, shared hours in public, a community of the mothers of the world, public community feeling, communities of a people in public, and public folk art

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public collective life taking a communal or artistic form.
- psychology: Community feeling is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Women's and mothers' communities and the union of peoples.
- events/manifestations: Shared hours, folk art, and Witte's bride, housewife, or female artist.
- conflicts/notes: Mothers is plural in the scan; folk art is not a mystical Mother-of-the-World doctrine.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Marriage and household roles are specific examples within a wider collective and artistic scope.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Moon = Cupido`, public feeling becomes a sense of belonging to a community. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) names women's and mothers' communities, shared hours, a people's communities, and folk art. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) adds the bride, housewife, female artist, and union of peoples. The synthesis centers on communal life and its cultural expression while preserving the sources' gendered roles as examples.
