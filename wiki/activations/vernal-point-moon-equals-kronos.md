## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 38 (one-based).

Female leadership. The people representing the authorities. Officials. Great peoples.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 25; PDF page: 11 (one-based spread).

Women's standing in public life, authority of the mothers of the world, authority of a people in public, women of the state in public, independent women in public, and a superior woman in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public collective life represented through leadership, standing, and authority.
- psychology: No distinct psychological claim is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Women leaders, mothers, officials, and representatives of authority.
- events/manifestations: Public independence and the authority of a people.
- conflicts/notes: Superior is the source's comparative description, not a universal hierarchy of human worth.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Authority of a people and individual women's leadership remain distinct scales.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Moon = Kronos`, public life is specified through recognized leadership and standing. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names female leadership, officials, and people representing authority. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds mothers' authority, the authority of a people, women connected with the state, and independence. The coherent emphasis is public representation and rank, with the sources' gendered examples explicitly retained.
