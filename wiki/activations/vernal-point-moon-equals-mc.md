## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 38 (one-based).

To be influenced by the public or women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 24; PDF page: 11 (one-based spread).

My wife's world, a feelingful soul in public, myself as a woman in public life, my people in the world, my wife in public, my feelings in public, and my mother in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal involvement in public feeling and the source's relationships with women.
- psychology: One's public feelings and a feelingful soul are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Influence by women or the public; wife, mother, and one's people.
- events/manifestations: Personal participation in public life.
- conflicts/notes: Feelingful soul replaces fleeting soul; one's feelings replaces the former generic life wording.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The first-person female standpoint and relationships with wife or mother are alternative examples, not a required personal identity.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Moon = MC`, public feeling becomes personally experienced. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names influence by the public or women. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) supplies one's feelings, wife, mother, people, and a first-person female standpoint. The connected reading concerns personal emotional participation in public life, with those gendered and familial examples retained as source possibilities.
