## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 38 (one-based).

Union of women and nations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 24; PDF page: 11 (one-based spread).

Women's public connections, feelingful public connections, an hour of public encounter, connection among the women of the world, connection with a people's public life, and connection with women's world

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public collective life joined through associations and emotional connections.
- psychology: Feelingful connections are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Union of women or nations and connection to a people's life.
- events/manifestations: Public encounters and women's associations.
- conflicts/notes: Feelingful does not by itself establish friendship; no timing method follows from hour of encounter.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Emotional connection and national union are different scales of association.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Moon = Node`, public feeling is expressed through connection. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names unions of women and nations. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops emotional links, public encounters, and association with a people's life or women's world. The shared emphasis is participation through relationships, without assuming that every emotionally significant link is friendly.
