## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 38 (one-based).

Sensitivity of the body. A man’s woman. A women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 24; PDF page: 11 (one-based spread).

Woman and man in public, a feelingful person in public, a woman's personal world, a man's hour in public, women's day in public life, day and hour in public, and female persons in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public collective feeling embodied in persons and gendered relationships.
- psychology: A feelingful person is explicit.
- body/health: Sensitivity of the body is Witte's source association.
- social/relationship: Women and men, and the source's man's-woman relationship.
- events/manifestations: Personal public life and day or hour examples.
- conflicts/notes: Feelingful person replaces fictitious person; Witte's final grammatical fragment is preserved without extra inference.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Gendered relationship examples and bodily sensitivity are distinct associations.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Moon = Sun`, public feeling is brought to the level of concrete persons and bodily sensitivity. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supplies bodily sensitivity and gendered relationship language. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a feelingful person, women and men in public, and a woman's personal world. Its day and hour examples remain source terms without introducing a timing technique.
