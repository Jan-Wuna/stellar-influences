## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 38 (one-based).

Irritability. Neurotic woman (intensely excited). A nervous stomach. Uproar of the population.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 25; PDF page: 11 (one-based spread).

An eventful public hour, excited women in public, nervousness in a people's public life, a public event for a people, reform-minded women in public, and an hour of public unrest

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public feeling stirred by events, nervous excitement, or unrest.
- psychology: Irritability and nervousness are explicit.
- body/health: Witte names a nervous stomach as an attributed bodily association.
- social/relationship: Excited or reform-minded women and an aroused population.
- events/manifestations: Collective uproar and eventful public moments.
- conflicts/notes: Witte's neurotic wording is historical source language, not a diagnosis.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Reform-minded participation and nervous uproar are different forms of public excitement.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Moon = Uranus`, public feeling is stirred into nervous or eventful movement. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names irritability, intense excitement, a nervous stomach, and population uproar. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds public events, collective nervousness, reform-minded women, and unrest. The shared emphasis is heightened responsiveness in public life, with bodily and gendered descriptions kept as attributed source associations.
