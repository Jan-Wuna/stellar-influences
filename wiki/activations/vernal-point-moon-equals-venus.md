## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 38 (one-based).

The love of women. Motherhood.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 24; PDF page: 11 (one-based spread).

Joy for women in public, peace in a people's public life, a people's public feelings, artistic women in public life, beautiful women in public, and an hour of public harmony

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public feeling shaped by love, joy, peace, and beauty.
- psychology: A people's feelings and harmony are explicit.
- body/health: Motherhood is named without a clinical claim.
- social/relationship: Love involving women and the public life of a people.
- events/manifestations: Artistic women, beauty, and public peace.
- conflicts/notes: Beautiful replaces gentle; the omitted collective-feelings clause is restored.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Motherhood and artistic or public harmony examples are separate possibilities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Moon = Venus`, public feeling is specified through affection, joy, and harmony. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names the love of women and motherhood. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds collective feeling, peace, artistic women, and beauty in public life. The synthesis connects personal affection with a harmonious public atmosphere while retaining the sources' gendered examples as particular forms.
