## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 38 (one-based).

Attracting attention. Influence of females. Persons with executive power. Powerful and mighty peoples. People who are admired.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 25; PDF page: 11 (one-based spread).

Women's influence in public, women's power in public life, emotional influence from the public, violence against women in public, power of the mothers of the world, and an hour of public violence

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public feeling and collective life expressed through influence and power.
- psychology: Emotional influence from the public is explicit.
- body/health: Violence against women is a source possibility, without clinical detail.
- social/relationship: Women or mothers holding power and persons with executive power.
- events/manifestations: Attention, admiration, powerful peoples, and public violence.
- conflicts/notes: The former freedom clause lacked scanned support; influence and violence remain distinct.

## Contradictions

Admired or executive power and violence against women place people in different relations to power. The interpretation preserves both agency and vulnerability.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Moon = Vulcanus`, public life is charged with influence and power. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes attention, admiration, executive capacity, and powerful peoples. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds women's or mothers' power and emotional influence, but also violence against women. The synthesis retains both the exercise of power and exposure to it, without treating admiration as protection from harm.
