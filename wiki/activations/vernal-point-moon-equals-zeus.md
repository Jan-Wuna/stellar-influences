## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 38 (one-based).

Expectant mother. Arming or rearming people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 25; PDF page: 11 (one-based spread).

Fiery public feelings, a leading woman in public life, a people's public achievements, a creative woman in public, goals of the mothers of the world, and a people's goals in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public feeling directed into creation, leadership, and collective goals.
- psychology: Fiery feelings and goals are explicit.
- body/health: Witte names an expectant mother as a source association.
- social/relationship: Leading or creative women and mothers' aims.
- events/manifestations: Collective achievements and arming or rearming people.
- conflicts/notes: Creative or maternal examples are not merged with militarization.

## Contradictions

An expectant mother, creative leadership, and rearmament are distinct manifestations. The sources do not state that one causes another.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Moon = Zeus`, public feeling is directed toward creation and a collective aim. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) names fiery feeling, a leading or creative woman, mothers' goals, and a people's achievements. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supplies expectant motherhood and arming or rearming people as separate concrete examples. The coherent theme is directed collective energy, with those very different outcomes kept apart.
