## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 46 (one-based).

Far spread chaos. Corruption. Dissolutions. Disappointment of the masses. Chemical sciences.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 41; PDF page: 19 (one-based spread).

Expansion of the public Uncertainty, public Water trade, water masses in the public, knowledge weakness in the public Life, increasing uncertainty in the public Life, and future knowledge for the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Uncertainty or dissolution spread across a broad public field.
- psychology: Mass disappointment and increasing uncertainty are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Disappointment affecting masses.
- events/manifestations: Water commerce, large quantities of water, chemical sciences, and future knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: Chemical science and water trade are neutral fields distinct from corruption.

## Contradictions

Widespread uncertainty and future knowledge coexist. The technical and commercial examples do not establish corruption or failed knowledge in every case.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Neptune = Apollon`, public uncertainty or dissolution takes on a wide scale. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names spreading chaos, corruption, mass disappointment, and chemical sciences. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds growing uncertainty and weak knowledge, alongside water trade, water masses, and knowledge of the future. The reading preserves the collective scale while keeping neutral technical fields separate from adverse outcomes.
