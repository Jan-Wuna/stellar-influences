## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 46 (one-based).

To be led astray by others. To be deceived with others or through others. Avoiding others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 40; PDF page: 19 (one-based spread).

weak public presence at a place, sensitivity in the public environment, unknown public environment, unknown persons in public, uncertainty in the public environment, and water for the public at a place

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public uncertainty and sensitivity encountered through people and surroundings.
- psychology: Sensitivity and uncertainty in the environment are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Being misled or avoiding others; unknown public persons.
- events/manifestations: An unfamiliar public environment and local provision of water.
- conflicts/notes: Unfamiliar persons are not automatically deceptive.

## Contradictions

The neutral presence of unfamiliar people or water provision is broader than Witte's deception examples. These should remain distinct.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Neptune = Asc`, public uncertainty is encountered through others and the immediate environment. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names being misled, deceived with or through others, and avoidance. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds sensitivity, unfamiliar surroundings or persons, weak public presence, and water at a place. The synthesis centers on uncertain contact while leaving unfamiliarity and deception as different conditions.
