## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 46 (one-based).

Precarious marital conditions. Dormant art. Dissolution of unions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 41; PDF page: 19 (one-based spread).

A shared future in public life, communities in public waters, public expectations of art, weak communities in public, unknown communities in public, and water for the public community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public uncertainty or unrealized possibility affecting communities and art.
- psychology: Expectations concerning art and a shared future are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Precarious marriage, weak communities, and dissolved unions.
- events/manifestations: Dormant art, water communities, and communal water provision.
- conflicts/notes: Unknown communities is not the same as socially unrecognized communities.

## Contradictions

A shared future and artistic expectations coexist with precarious or dissolving associations. The sources do not establish whether those expectations will be fulfilled.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Neptune = Cupido`, public uncertainty takes a communal or artistic form. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names precarious marriage, dormant art, and dissolution of unions. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds a shared future, artistic expectations, weak or unknown communities, and communal water settings. The common theme is collective life whose substance or realization may remain unsettled.
