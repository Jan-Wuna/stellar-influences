## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 46 (one-based).

Sorrow and discomfort through uncertainties. Serious blood ailments. Lack of water. Rough or stormy weather. Water catastrophes, floods.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 41; PDF page: 19 (one-based spread).

Deprivation and disappointment in public life, danger to the public's future, dangerous water in public, deficient air in public, public water shortage, and public insecurity through crime

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public uncertainty intensified by deprivation, danger, or deficient resources.
- psychology: Sorrow, discomfort, and disappointment are explicit.
- body/health: Witte names serious blood ailments as an attributed source association.
- social/relationship: Public insecurity through crime.
- events/manifestations: Water shortage, dangerous water, storms, floods, and deficient air.
- conflicts/notes: Deprivation and disappointment replace the former discharges and exchanges wording.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Bodily illness, crime, and environmental hazards are separate source examples, not a causal chain.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Neptune = Hades`, public uncertainty is joined by deprivation or danger. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects sorrow with uncertain conditions and names water shortage, storms, floods, and blood ailments. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds disappointment, deficient air, crime-related insecurity, and danger to the public future. The synthesis preserves concrete environmental and bodily associations without asserting that they cause one another.
