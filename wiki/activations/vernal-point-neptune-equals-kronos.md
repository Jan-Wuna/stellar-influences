## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 46 (one-based).

Leading persons disappoint the general public. Diplomatic entanglements.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 41; PDF page: 19 (one-based spread).

Weakness of authority in public, special water in public, great uncertainty in public life, weak public reputation, an unknown quantity in public life, and insecure governing authority in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public uncertainty focused on leadership, authority, and reputation.
- psychology: Great uncertainty and disappointment in leaders are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Insecure governing authority and diplomatic relations.
- events/manifestations: Diplomatic entanglements and special water.
- conflicts/notes: Unknown quantity is the scanned expression; it supplies no hidden identity or magnitude.

## Contradictions

Special water is a separate concrete example and is not itself a statement about failed authority. The institutional emphasis remains insecure standing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Neptune = Kronos`, uncertainty is concentrated in public leadership and standing. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names leaders disappointing the public and diplomatic entanglements. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds weak authority, uncertain government, diminished reputation, and an unknown quantity. Its water example remains separate from the central question of whether public authority is dependable.
