## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 46 (one-based).

Disturbance in a sex union. Infections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 40; PDF page: 19 (one-based spread).

Work on, with, or in public water, disappointments in public relations work, public efforts concerning water, public activity in the air, uncertain work in public, and future work for the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public uncertainty expressed through work, effort, and activity.
- psychology: Disappointment in public relations work is explicit.
- body/health: Witte names infections as an attributed association.
- social/relationship: Disturbance in a sexual union is a separate Witte example.
- events/manifestations: Water or air work and uncertain public activity.
- conflicts/notes: The former exchanges wording meant disappointments; practical work is not equated with infection.

## Contradictions

The sexual and infection examples differ from ABC's public-work scope. They are preserved side by side without an invented causal link.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Neptune = Mars`, uncertainty is expressed through effort and activity. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) names disappointing or uncertain public work, water-related effort, air activity, and future work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) instead gives disturbance in a sexual union and infections. The synthesis preserves this difference in scope and avoids making ordinary water or public work a bodily diagnosis.
