## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 46 (one-based).

To be deceived or disappointed by the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 40; PDF page: 19 (one-based spread).

My disappointments in public life, my unknown public sphere, my uncertainty in public, my weakness in public life, sensitivity in public life, and inner fine sensitivity in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personally experienced public uncertainty, disappointment, and sensitivity.
- psychology: Personal insecurity and fine inner sensitivity are explicit.
- body/health: Weakness is source language without clinical detail.
- social/relationship: Being deceived or disappointed by the public.
- events/manifestations: An unknown personal public sphere.
- conflicts/notes: Inner sensitivity does not by itself establish a spiritual doctrine.

## Contradictions

Sensitivity and disappointment are different aspects of public experience. The source does not state that sensitivity necessarily causes deception.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Neptune = MC`, public uncertainty is experienced personally. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names deception or disappointment by the public. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds one's unknown public sphere, uncertainty, weakness, and fine inner sensitivity. The reading connects a vulnerable or subtle personal response with unsettled public conditions without making every sensitive experience deceptive.
