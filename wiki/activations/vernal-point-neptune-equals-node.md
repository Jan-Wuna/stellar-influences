## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 46 (one-based).

Disappointment or disagreeable things through connections. General dissolution of connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 40; PDF page: 19 (one-based spread).

Weak connections in public life, uncertain public connections, connection with an unsettled public, connection with water in public, and public water connections

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public uncertainty affecting connections and associations.
- psychology: Disappointment through connections is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Weak or dissolving links and contact with an unsettled public.
- events/manifestations: Public water connections.
- conflicts/notes: Connections are broader than professional public relations; the omitted unsettled-public clause is restored.

## Contradictions

Water connections are a separate concrete setting and need not be weak or dissolving. The adverse relational possibilities remain explicit.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Neptune = Node`, public uncertainty appears in the quality and durability of connections. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names disappointment or disagreeable experiences through links and their dissolution. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds weak or uncertain connections and contact with an unsettled public, alongside water connections. The reading preserves fragile association as the main theme without making every water link adverse.
