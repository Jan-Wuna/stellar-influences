## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 46 (one-based).

The end of a romantic affair, of a beautiful dream. Losses through fraud. Pestilential diseases.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 41; PDF page: 19 (one-based spread).

Dissolution of public problems, patience and fine sensitivity in public life, gradual public losses, unknown problems in public life, public water problems, and future tasks for the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public uncertainty involving endings, losses, tasks, or the resolution of problems.
- psychology: Patience, fine sensitivity, and the ending of a beautiful dream are explicit.
- body/health: Witte names pestilential diseases as an attributed source association.
- social/relationship: The end of a romantic affair is a specific relational example.
- events/manifestations: Fraud losses, gradual losses, water problems, and future tasks.
- conflicts/notes: The scanned Saturn paragraph replaces the Uranus row formerly assigned here.

## Contradictions

Problem resolution and patient sensitivity coexist with loss, endings, and unknown problems. The corrected source does not reduce the formula to agitation.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Neptune = Saturn`, uncertainty is brought into contact with endings, losses, and tasks that must be borne or resolved. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names an ended affair or dream, fraud losses, and pestilential diseases. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds patience, sensitivity, problem resolution, gradual losses, and future tasks. The synthesis retains possible resolution alongside difficulty; the misplaced Uranus agitation row is excluded.
