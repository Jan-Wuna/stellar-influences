## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 46 (one-based).

Great or severe disappointments.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 41; PDF page: 19 (one-based spread).

Dissolution of public power, public air power, strong public disappointment, unknown public influence, insecurity through violence in public life, and future energy for the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public uncertainty involving power, influence, and severe disappointment.
- psychology: Great or severe disappointment is the repeated emphasis.
- body/health: Violence is named without a clinical claim.
- social/relationship: Unknown influence and insecurity under violence.
- events/manifestations: Dissolving power, air power, and future public energy.
- conflicts/notes: Strong disappointment replaces exchange; future energy supplies no specified technology.

## Contradictions

Air power and future energy are concrete possibilities distinct from dissolution or violence. Public power is not assigned one inevitable result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Neptune = Vulcanus`, public uncertainty concerns the strength and dependability of power. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) concentrates on severe disappointment. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds dissolving power, unknown influence, and insecurity through violence, with air power and future energy as separate settings. The shared emphasis is powerful disappointment or uncertain influence, without collapsing every use of power into failure.
