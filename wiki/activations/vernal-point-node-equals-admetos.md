## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 39 (one-based).

Permanent connection. End or finish of a connection. Restriction of the possibility to establish connections.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 27; PDF page: 12 (one-based spread).

Consolidating relationships with the public, blocked public connections, restriction of public connections, firm connections with the public, and thorough contacts with the public

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Node = Admetos`, public connections acquire a firm boundary. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives both permanent links and endings, alongside restricted opportunities to connect. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) develops consolidation, thorough contact, blockage, and limitation. The coherent theme is the fixing or bounding of association, which may preserve a link or prevent it.
