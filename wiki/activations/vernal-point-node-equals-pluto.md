## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 39 (one-based).

Many join together to change something. Transformation in the connections with many people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 27; PDF page: 12 (one-based spread).

Developing relationships with the public, development of public relationships, change in relationships with the public, growing connections with the public, and changeable contacts with the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public connections developed or transformed through collective participation.
- psychology: No distinct psychological claim is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Many people joining to change something.
- events/manifestations: Growing links and changeable contacts.
- conflicts/notes: Change is explicit without a guaranteed beneficial direction.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Growth of connections and variability of contacts describe different aspects of change.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Node = Pluto`, public association develops and changes. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes many people joining to alter something and the transformation of their connections. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds growing relationships, unfolding links, and changeable contacts. The shared theme is collective participation that reshapes both its object and the network involved.
