## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 39 (one-based).

Bodily associated with many people. Daily contacts in general.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 26; PDF page: 12 (one-based spread).

Meeting Centre in the public, men bindings in the public life, human encounters in the public, personal relations in the public life, connection of persons in the public, and day public encounters

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public connections embodied in personal and daily encounters.
- psychology: No distinct psychological claim is supplied.
- body/health: Bodily association is named without a health claim.
- social/relationship: Personal relations, human encounters, and bonds among men.
- events/manifestations: Meeting centers and days of public encounter.
- conflicts/notes: Bodily association does not by itself supply a sexual or medical interpretation.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Meeting centers and personal relations are different concrete forms of public contact.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Node = Sun`, public association takes a concrete personal form. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names bodily association with many people and daily contacts. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds meeting centers, human encounters, personal relationships, and connections among persons. The common emphasis is actual presence and contact within public life.
