## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 39 (one-based).

Quick affection for someone. Love or harmonic union with many people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 26; PDF page: 12 (one-based spread).

Encountering beauty in public, public peace contacts, harmonious public connections, art connections in public life, wishing for connections with the public, and a preference for public connections

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public association shaped by affection, harmony, art, and peace.
- psychology: Quick affection and desire for connections are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Love or harmonious union with many people.
- events/manifestations: Peace contacts and encounters with beauty.
- conflicts/notes: Desire for connection is explicit; it does not prove the desired relationship exists.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Wishing for a connection and having a harmonious union are distinct states.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Node = Venus`, public connections are sought or experienced through affection and harmony. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names quick affection and loving or harmonious union with many people. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds beauty, art, peace contacts, and a wish for public connection. The synthesis joins sociable attraction with cultural or conciliatory links while distinguishing desire from an established relationship.
