## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 47 (one-based).

Change of circumstances from bottom to top. Deep reaching transformation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 43; PDF page: 20 (one-based spread).

Blocked developments in the public, depression through public changes, strike developments in the public, profound changes in the public, and growing limitations in the public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Deep public transformation confronting blockage or limitation.
- psychology: Depression through public change is explicit source language.
- body/health: No clinical or bodily detail is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public changes and strike developments affect collective life.
- events/manifestations: Blocked development, growing limits, and changes from bottom to top.
- conflicts/notes: Depth of transformation is distinct from its success or failure.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes deep transformation, while ABC also gives blocked development and expanding limits. Deep change does not necessarily mean free expansion.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Pluto = Admetos`, public change reaches deeply into established circumstances. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes transformation from bottom to top. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds blockage, strikes, depression through change, and growing limits. The synthesis holds fundamental transformation together with the constraints that can obstruct or define its course.
