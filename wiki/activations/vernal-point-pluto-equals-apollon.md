## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 47 (one-based).

Peaceful development. Growth in science, commerce and industry. Changes or developments in the field of religion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 43; PDF page: 20 (one-based spread).

Expansion of public changes, successful public development, public trade development, growing experience in public life, development of public knowledge, and increasing public developments

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public development expanding through science, trade, industry, and knowledge.
- psychology: Growing experience is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public growth across commercial, scientific, or religious fields.
- events/manifestations: Peaceful development, trade growth, and expanding knowledge.
- conflicts/notes: Changes replaces the erroneous differences wording; religious change is not independently assigned success.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Successful development is explicit, but not every separately listed religious or commercial change is guaranteed to prosper.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Pluto = Apollon`, public development widens its field. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names peaceful growth in science, commerce, and industry, with changes in religion. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds expanding changes, trade development, accumulated experience, and growing knowledge. The coherent emphasis is development spreading through public institutions and fields of exchange.
