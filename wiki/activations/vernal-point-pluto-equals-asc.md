## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Pluto = Asc`, public development becomes visible in the immediate environment and in other people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states that general changes influence the native's surroundings. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds local growth, development of others, and changes with a partner. The shared focus is the interpersonal and local expression of a wider public transformation.
