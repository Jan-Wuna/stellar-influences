## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Growth of families and communities. Engagement. Marriage. Births. Tradition. Inheritance. Changes in the perception of the arts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `43`, one-based PDF p. `20`, complete Cupido row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Entwicklungs-Gemeinschaft für die Öffentlichkeit, gemeinsame Entwicklungen im öffentlichen Leben, Gemeinschaft verändert die Öffentlichkeit, Kunst-Entfaltung in der Öffentlichkeit, Wandlungen in öffentlichen Gemeinschaften

Editorial translation: Development community for the public; joint developments in public life; a community changes public life; artistic unfolding in public; changes within public communities.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Growth and change in families, communities, and public cultural life.
- Psychology: Changes in the perception of art are named; a general personality description is not supplied.
- Body/health: Births appear among family events, without a medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Engagement, marriage, tradition, and inheritance connect development with continuing communal ties.
- Events/manifestations: Family growth, joint public developments, and artistic development are the concrete settings.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC explicitly names a community changing public life, alongside joint development and artistic unfolding.

## Contradictions

The entries differ in emphasis between family continuity and public communal development; neither explicitly negates the other. Their overlap does not establish independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Pluto = Cupido`, Cupido specifies public development through families, communities, and the arts. Growth takes a social form: engagement, marriage, births, tradition, and inheritance describe continuity within groups, while joint developments and artistic expression place change in public communal life. This joins the concrete family and art themes in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) with the public-community setting of the [ABC entry](#abc-entry); the community can also participate in changing public life.
