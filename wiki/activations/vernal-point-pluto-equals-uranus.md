## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Sudden developments. Technical development or change.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

dynamic developments in public, public reform developments, technical changes in public life, surprising changes in public, and growing tensions in public life

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public development taking a sudden, surprising, or technical form.
- Psychology: Surprise is named; a general psychological disposition is not supplied.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- Social/relationship: Reform and growing tensions are placed in public life.
- Events/manifestations: Technical development, public reform, and sudden changes are concrete examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Growing tension accompanies the ABC account but is not said to result from every technical change.

## Contradictions

Suddenness and technical change overlap across the entries; reform and tension add scope. No explicit opposed proposition is present.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Pluto = Uranus`, Uranus specifies public development through sudden and technical changes. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names those two features directly; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places dynamic change, reform, surprise, and growing tensions in public life. The connected picture is abrupt or technically driven public development, with tension a possible accompaniment rather than a necessary outcome.
