## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Peaceful, harmonious developments or pleasant transformations.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

unfolding public joy, development of beauty in public life, harmonydevelopment in public, public peace development, change of the arts in public life, and growing wishes in public life

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Peaceful, harmonious, or pleasant public development.
- Psychology: Public joy and growing wishes are explicitly named.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim appears.
- Social/relationship: Peace and harmony are described in public life.
- Events/manifestations: Development of beauty and changes in the arts are concrete manifestations.
- Conflicts/notes: Growing wishes and pleasant transformations are both named, without an explicit causal link.

## Contradictions

The entries overlap in harmonious development. The ABC examples add public joy, beauty, and art without establishing that every wish is fulfilled.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Pluto = Venus`, Venus specifies public development through pleasant and harmonious change. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supplies the peaceful quality; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives it public expression in joy, beauty, peace, and the arts, alongside growing wishes. The shared theme is development experienced as welcome or aesthetically pleasing, with those wishes remaining desires rather than promised outcomes.
