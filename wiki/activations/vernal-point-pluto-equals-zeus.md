#### Witte Entry

The increase in performance. New weapons.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `43`, one-based PDF p. `20`, complete Zeus row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Beginnende Veränderungen in der Öffentlichkeit, Führungs-Wechsel in der Öffentlichkeit, kreative Veränderungen im öffentlichen Leben, Richtungs-Wechsel in der Öffentlichkeit, wachsende Leistungen in der Öffentlichkeit, zielstrebige Entwicklung im öffentlichen Leben

Editorial translation: Beginning changes in public; changes of leadership in public; creative changes in public life; changes of direction in public; growing achievements in public; purposeful development in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public development expressed through increasing performance and purposeful or creative change.
- Psychology: Determined development is explicit; a broader temperament is not supplied.
- Body/health: No specific bodily or medical condition is stated.
- Social/relationship: Performance and changes of direction are situated in public life.
- Events/manifestations: New weapons are a specific Witte example alongside increasing performance.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC names changes of leadership and direction; these are distinct from its creative changes and increasing achievements.

## Contradictions

Increasing performance overlaps across the entries. New weapons are an additional example, not evidence that every creative or purposeful development concerns weapons.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Pluto = Zeus`, Zeus specifies public development through increasing performance and changes that are creative or directed toward a goal. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) adds new weapons as a concrete manifestation, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes beginning changes, changes of leadership and direction, and purposeful development.
