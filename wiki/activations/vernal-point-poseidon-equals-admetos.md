## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The education system. The condition of education and culture in general.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `59`, one-based PDF p. `28`, complete Admetos row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Einschränkung der öffentlichen Kultur, Festigung von Ideen in der Öffentlichkeit, geistige Manifestation im öffentlichen Leben, geistige Vertiefung in der Öffentlichkeit, Unterdrückung der Wahrheit im öffentlichen Leben, zunehmende Vernunft im öffentlichen Leben

Editorial translation: Restriction of public culture; consolidation of ideas in public; spiritual manifestation in public life; spiritual deepening in public; suppression of truth in public life; increasing reason in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: The condition of public education and culture, including limitation and consolidation of ideas.
- Psychology: Spiritual deepening and strengthening ideas are named.
- Body/health: No specific bodily or medical theme is supplied.
- Social/relationship: The education system and public culture are the explicit collective settings.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural limitation and spiritual manifestation are named forms.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC names suppression of truth as well as increasing reason; these remain distinct possibilities within public intellectual life.

## Contradictions

Cultural limitation and spiritual deepening can describe different aspects of the same field. Suppression of truth and increasing reason remain a tension in the source's range; no conditions are supplied to reconcile them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies public intellectual and spiritual life through the condition of education and culture. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) identifies that institutional field, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds limitation of culture, consolidation of ideas, spiritual deepening, suppression of truth, and increasing reason. The interpretation preserves both constraint and consolidation rather than treating this as an unqualified account of cultural progress.
