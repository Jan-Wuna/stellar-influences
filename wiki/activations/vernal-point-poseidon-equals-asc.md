## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

To deal with spiritualized men. Persons in the immediate environment who have special skills of knowledge.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `58`, one-based PDF p. `28`, complete Asc row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Klarheit mit dem Partner in der Öffentlichkeit, öffentliche Umwelt-Ideen, öffentlicher Kultur-Ort, Ort für öffentliche Wahrheit, Orts-Geist im Umfeld, Umwelt-Kultur im öffentlichen Leben, vernünftiger Partner im öffentlichen Leben

Editorial translation: Clarity with the partner in public; public ideas about one's surroundings; a public cultural place; a place for public truth; spirit of place in one's surroundings; culture of the surroundings in public life; a reasonable partner in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public intellectual or spiritual life encountered through people and the immediate environment.
- Psychology: Knowledge, clarity with a partner, and a reasonable partner are named; these do not establish a complete personality assessment.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is stated.
- Social/relationship: Witte describes nearby people with special knowledge; ABC names partners and cultural places.
- Events/manifestations: Encounters with knowledgeable people and public cultural settings are concrete manifestations.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's partner and surroundings clauses describe particular relations and cultural settings rather than a general personality judgment.

## Contradictions

The entries concern people and places in the surroundings without explicit conflict. Knowledgeable people, a reasonable partner, and cultural places are distinct expressions.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Asc`, Asc specifies public intellectual and spiritual life through nearby people and places. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes dealings with people who have special knowledge; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds clarity with a partner, a reasonable partner, public cultural places, and the surrounding cultural environment. These connect encounters with knowledge and culture to one's relationships and surroundings.
