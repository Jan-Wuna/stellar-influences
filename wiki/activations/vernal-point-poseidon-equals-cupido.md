## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The refined mental attitude in marriage, community or art. Spiritual communities. Societies based on a common perception of the world.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `59`, one-based PDF p. `28`, complete Cupido row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Geistige Gemeinschaft in der Öffentlichkeit, geistige Synthese in der Öffentlichkeit, gemeinsame Kultur im öffentlichen Leben, öffentliche Kunst und Kultur, vernünftige Gemeinschaft in der Öffentlichkeit, Welt-Kultur-Gemeinschaft

Editorial translation: Intellectual community in public; intellectual synthesis in public; shared culture in public life; public art and culture; a reasonable community in public; a world-cultural community.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared intellectual or spiritual culture expressed in community and art.
- Psychology: A refined mental attitude and shared perception of the world are explicit source themes.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical interpretation is supplied.
- Social/relationship: Marriage, spiritual communities, and societies with a common worldview are named.
- Events/manifestations: Public art, culture, and intellectual synthesis provide concrete forms.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC names a reasonable community alongside intellectual synthesis and shared culture; shared understanding does not establish agreement on every idea.

## Contradictions

The entries differ between particular relationships and wider public culture without explicit opposition. A shared worldview is not proof of agreement on every idea.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies public intellectual and spiritual life through shared culture and community. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links marriage, art, and spiritual societies with a common perception of the world; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends this into public art, shared culture, intellectual synthesis, and a reasonable community. The connecting theme is a community formed or sustained through shared cultural and spiritual understanding.
