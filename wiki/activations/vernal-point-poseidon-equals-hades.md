## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

The spirit of antiquity or the past. Mental baseness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Development of dangerous ideas, criminal ideas in public, cultural past in public, lack of truth in public life, hidden truth in public, and past world cultures

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public ideas and culture connected either with the past or with degraded and concealed content.
- Psychology: 'Mental baseness' is the historical Witte judgment, not a general personal diagnosis.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- Social/relationship: Past world cultures and ideas circulating publicly are named settings.
- Events/manifestations: Dangerous or criminal ideas, lack of truth, and hidden truth are explicit examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Antiquity and harmful ideas are separate strands; the entries do not say that past cultures are inherently base or criminal.

## Contradictions

The past-oriented and negative moral strands are not explicit opposites, but the entries do not establish a causal relationship between them. Hidden truth and lack of truth also remain distinct claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Hades`, Hades specifies public intellectual and cultural life through two retained strands: the presence of antiquity or past cultures, and ideas described as base, dangerous, criminal, or lacking truth. These appear in the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) and [ABC entry](#abc-entry). The synthesis keeps the strands beside one another without equating historical culture with harmful ideas or hidden truth with falsehood.
