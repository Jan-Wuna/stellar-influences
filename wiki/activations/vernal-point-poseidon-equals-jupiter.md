## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 55 (one-based).

Fortunate understanding, reason, wisdom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 58; PDF page: 28 (one-based spread).

Successful ideas in public life, intellectual successes in public, cultural success in public life, cultural law in public, public money culture, public law clarity, and truth about public finances

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Successful public ideas, understanding, and cultural activity.
- Psychology: Understanding, reason, and wisdom are explicit Witte themes.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is supplied.
- Social/relationship: Public cultural and intellectual life is the stated setting.
- Events/manifestations: Intellectual or cultural success, legal clarity, and truth about public finances are named.
- Conflicts/notes: The financial and legal examples concern public affairs; they do not promise individual wealth.

## Contradictions

The entries share favorable intellectual themes while supplying different examples. They do not establish that every successful idea is wise or true.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies public intellectual and cultural life through successful ideas and favorable understanding. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes reason and wisdom; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) makes intellectual and cultural successes concrete and adds legal clarity and truth about public finances. The common thread is beneficial understanding in public affairs, with financial and legal matters particular examples.
