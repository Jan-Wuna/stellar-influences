## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 55 (one-based).

Mental or intellectual leadership. Spiritual standard of the time.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 59; PDF page: 28 (one-based spread).

Authority of truth in public, intellectual authority in public life, intellectual hierarchy in the world, great public clarity, state reason in public life, and important public ideas

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public intellectual or spiritual life expressed through leadership and authority.
- psychology: Important ideas, clarity, and reason are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Intellectual hierarchy and leadership.
- events/manifestations: The spiritual standard of the time and state reason.
- conflicts/notes: State reason replaces state authority; authority does not establish truth.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Leadership and clarity are different emphases, and the source's authority-of-truth phrase does not prove every authoritative idea true.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Kronos`, public intellectual and spiritual life is specified through leadership and standing. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names intellectual leadership and the spiritual standard of the time. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds important ideas, hierarchy, clarity, and reason in state affairs. The synthesis connects recognized intellectual authority with a public standard of understanding while keeping rank and truth distinct.
