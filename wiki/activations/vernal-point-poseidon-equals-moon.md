### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 55 (one-based).

Mentally high ranking people. Mediums. Spiritually attuned women.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 58; PDF page: 28 (one-based spread).

Insights of the mothers of this world, women's culture in public life, feelingful ideas in public life, public clarity through women, cultural peoples of the world, a spiritually attuned woman in public, and a reasonable woman in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public intellectual and cultural life expressed through people and the sources' examples of women.
- psychology: Feelingful ideas, insight, reason, and spiritual attunement.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Mothers' insights, women's culture, and cultural peoples.
- events/manifestations: People of intellectual standing and Witte's mediums.
- conflicts/notes: Mediums and mental rank are attributed source categories; repaired ABC clauses supply no verified supernatural capacity.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The historical gendered and medium examples are particular categories rather than universal personal traits.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Moon`, public intellectual and spiritual life is expressed through the people who participate in it. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names intellectual standing, mediums, and spiritually attuned women. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds mothers' insights, women's culture, feelingful ideas, clarity, and reason. The synthesis retains cultural participation and understanding while treating mediumship and gendered examples as attributed source doctrine.
