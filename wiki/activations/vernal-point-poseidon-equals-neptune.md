## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 55 (one-based).

Refined mental motions. Despondency. Deterioration of a philosophy or belief.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 59; PDF page: 28 (one-based spread).

Disappointing truth in public life, foreign ideas in public life, clear public air, weak public culture, refined spiritualization in public life, and future ideas in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public intellectual or spiritual life refined, uncertain, or weakened.
- psychology: Despondency and deterioration of belief are explicit.
- body/health: Despondency is source language without clinical detail.
- social/relationship: Foreign ideas and weak public culture are separate examples.
- events/manifestations: Disappointing truth, refined spiritualization, clear air, and future ideas.
- conflicts/notes: The scan restores disappointing truth and removes the opening of Pluto's paragraph.

## Contradictions

Refinement and weakened belief are different possibilities. Foreign ideas are not identified as the cause of deterioration, and clear air is a separate concrete example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Neptune`, public intellectual and spiritual life combines subtle refinement with the possibility of disappointment or weakened belief. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names refined mental activity, despondency, and deterioration of philosophy. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds disappointing truth and weak culture while retaining refined spiritualization, foreign ideas, clear air, and future ideas. The synthesis preserves those differences and excludes the development clauses belonging to Pluto.
