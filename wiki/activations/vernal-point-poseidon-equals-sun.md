## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 55 (one-based).

Turning ideas into reality. Inspiration and ideas about a physical form or an object. The ideas and mindset of the current generation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 58; PDF page: 28 (one-based spread).

An intellectual center in public, a man's public ideas, physical culture in public life, personal clarity in public life, reasonable people in public, and a world-culture center

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public ideas embodied in persons, centers, and physical forms.
- psychology: Inspiration, personal clarity, reason, and a generation's mindset.
- body/health: Physical culture is explicit; no disease or clinical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: A man's ideas and reasonable people are particular examples.
- events/manifestations: Turning ideas into reality and cultural or intellectual centers.
- conflicts/notes: Scanned Körper-Kultur and vernünftige Menschen restore physical culture and reasonable people, excluding corrupt indomitable wording.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Physical culture and intellectual centers are separate embodiments of the idea-to-form theme.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Sun`, public ideas are given concrete form. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names turning ideas into reality, inspiration about physical objects, and a generation's mindset. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal clarity, reasonable people, physical culture, and intellectual or cultural centers. The synthesis connects thought with its human and material expression; the former corrupt wording contributes no doctrine.
