## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public understanding stirred by sudden perception, reform, or cultural events.
- psychology: Enlightenment, new insight, and mental tension are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public culture encounters technology and reform.
- events/manifestations: Exciting truth, cultural reform, and public cultural events.
- conflicts/notes: Sudden perception is an attributed source theme; no special ability is independently verified.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Insight and mental tension are different possible responses to public intellectual change.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Uranus`, public intellectual life is enlivened by sudden perception and change. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names enlightenment, sudden understanding, and truth. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds new insights, mental tension, cultural reform, and the meeting of culture with technology. The shared theme is a fresh or disruptive opening in public understanding, without assigning every reform a favorable result.
