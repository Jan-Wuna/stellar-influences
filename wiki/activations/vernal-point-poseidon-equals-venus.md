## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 55 (one-based).

Peaceful attitude of the general public through knowledge. Spiritual attitude. Referring to philosophy affairs and matters of belief.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 58; PDF page: 28 (one-based spread).

Pleasure about reason in public, Peace culture in public Life, spiritual harmony in public Life, arts and culture in public, beauty of culture in public Life, and preference for public Ideas

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public understanding expressed through peace, harmony, beauty, and culture.
- psychology: Joy in reason and a spiritual attitude are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: A peaceful public attitude informed by knowledge.
- events/manifestations: Art, cultural beauty, philosophy, and belief.
- conflicts/notes: Knowledge and peace are linked by the source, without a claim that every belief is peaceful.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Philosophy, belief, art, and public harmony are related fields rather than identical outcomes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Venus`, public understanding is expressed through harmony and cultural value. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects knowledge with a peaceful public attitude and matters of philosophy or belief. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds joy in reason, peace culture, spiritual harmony, art, and beauty. The coherent reading joins intellectual or spiritual orientation with an aesthetic and conciliatory public expression.
