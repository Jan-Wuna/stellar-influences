## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 55 (one-based).

Mental activity. Mental leadership. Proofs. Flash of inspiration.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 59; PDF page: 28 (one-based spread).

Fiery spirit in public, leading public ideas, intellectual leadership in public, intellectual goal-striving in public life, clear public direction, cultural creation in public, and public testimony to truth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public intellectual activity directed toward leadership, creation, and a clear aim.
- psychology: Inspiration, mental activity, and purposeful striving are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Intellectual leadership and leading ideas.
- events/manifestations: Proofs, cultural creation, and testimony to truth.
- conflicts/notes: Leadership replaces movement; testimony to truth is a source theme, not proof of every claimant's correctness.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Inspiration, proof, and testimony are distinct forms of intellectual activity and do not automatically validate one another.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Poseidon = Zeus`, public thought is active, directed, and creative. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins mental leadership with proofs and a flash of inspiration. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds leading ideas, intellectual goals, clear direction, cultural creation, and testimony to truth. The coherent emphasis is ideas organized toward expression or demonstration, with truth remaining a source claim to be assessed.
