## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 44 (one-based).

Restrictions in commerce. Separation of the masses. Mass separations. Matters of education. The teacher.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 37; PDF page: 17 (one-based spread).

Expanding public problems, successful concentration on the public life, trade blockades in the public, public tasks of science, extensive losses in the public life, and increasing burdens on the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public burdens or limitations extending across commerce, education, and science.
- psychology: Successful concentration on public life is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Mass separation and teachers are particular source examples.
- events/manifestations: Trade restrictions, scientific tasks, extensive losses, and growing burdens.
- conflicts/notes: Education and successful concentration coexist with adverse collective conditions.

## Contradictions

Witte's education and ABC's successful concentration are not equivalent to loss or blockage. The sources preserve constructive focus within a difficult public field.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Saturn = Apollon`, public difficulty takes on breadth through commerce, collective separation, or demanding intellectual work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names commercial restriction, mass separation, education, and the teacher. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds expanding problems and losses, but also successful concentration and scientific tasks. The common theme is the wide reach of obligations and limits, with disciplined work retained as a constructive expression.
