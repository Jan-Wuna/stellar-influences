## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 44 (one-based).

Separation from other people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 36; PDF page: 17 (one-based spread).

A disturbed public environment, public tasks of a place, partner problems in public, disturbances in a public place, public environmental burdens, and loss of a partner in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public burdens encountered through partners, other people, and places.
- psychology: No distinct psychological claim is supplied.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Separation from others and partner difficulties or loss.
- events/manifestations: Local public tasks, environmental burdens, and disturbances.
- conflicts/notes: Partner loss does not specify death or the form of separation.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Ordinary local tasks and environmental burdens are broader than actual separation or loss.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Saturn = Asc`, public burdens become concrete in relationships and surroundings. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names separation from other people. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds partner problems or loss, disturbed public places, environmental burdens, and local tasks. The coherent focus is a difficult or constrained encounter with others and the public environment, without specifying the form of a loss.
