## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public burdens imposed, managed, or experienced through authority.
- psychology: Major inhibitions are explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Hindrances through officials or superiors.
- events/manifestations: Lockouts, territorial losses, state tasks, and priority problems.
- conflicts/notes: Burdened authority replaces exhausted authority; priority problems were omitted.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Authority bearing a burden and authority imposing a hindrance describe different positions within the institutional theme.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Saturn = Kronos`, public difficulty is specified through authority and the state. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names official hindrances, lockouts, and territorial losses. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds burdened authority, major inhibitions, state tasks, and priority problems. The synthesis connects institutional responsibility with the limits it bears or imposes on public life.
