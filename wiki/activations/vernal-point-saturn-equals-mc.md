## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 44 (one-based).

Difficult to contact. Difficult to adjust oneself to others. To be deserted.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 36; PDF page: 17 (one-based spread).

My public tasks, my burden in public life, my public problems, my inhibitions in public life, my public losses, and inner strain through the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal experience of public tasks, burdens, inhibition, and loss.
- psychology: Inner strain and difficulty adjusting to others are explicit.
- body/health: No clinical illness is stated in the corrected ABC row.
- social/relationship: Difficulty making contact and being deserted.
- events/manifestations: One's public obligations and losses.
- conflicts/notes: Inner strain replaces an unsupported mental-illness claim; burden replaces profession.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Personal tasks and inner strain are broader than social desertion, and the source does not establish a psychiatric diagnosis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Saturn = MC`, public burdens are experienced personally. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names difficulty with contact or adjustment and being deserted. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) supplies one's tasks, problems, inhibitions, losses, and inner strain through the public. The coherent reading joins constrained participation with the weight of public demands; the former mental-illness wording was not supported by the scanned row.
