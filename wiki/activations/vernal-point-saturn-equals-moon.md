## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 44 (one-based).

To take leave of a woman. The parting hours.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 36; PDF page: 17 (one-based spread).

Stressing a people in public, burdening a woman in public, inhibiting women in public life, public tasks of a woman, problems of a woman in public, and lossful hour in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public burdens experienced collectively or through the source's examples of women.
- psychology: Inhibition is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Parting from a woman and burdens borne by a woman or people.
- events/manifestations: Women's public tasks and a loss-bearing public hour.
- conflicts/notes: Parting does not by itself specify death or the cause of separation.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Women's tasks and collective burdens are broader than the specific parting example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Saturn = Moon`, public difficulty is felt through collective burdens and particular women. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names taking leave of a woman and parting hours. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds burdens on a people or woman, inhibition, public tasks, problems, and loss. The synthesis preserves constrained or sorrowful public participation while leaving the cause and form of a parting unspecified.
