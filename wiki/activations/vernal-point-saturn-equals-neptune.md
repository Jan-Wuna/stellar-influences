## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 44 (one-based).

Separation through sickness. Suspected infection. Quarantine. To end a state. To reject something definitively.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 37; PDF page: 17 (one-based spread).

Dissolution of problems in public life, serious weakness in public, air burdens in public life, public water problems, public tasks of the future, and insecurity through public losses

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public burdens dissolving or becoming uncertain, with separation and weakness.
- psychology: Uncertainty through public loss is explicit.
- body/health: Witte names sickness, suspected infection, and quarantine as source associations.
- social/relationship: Separation and definitive rejection.
- events/manifestations: Resolution of public problems, air burdens, water problems, and future tasks.
- conflicts/notes: Problem resolution is retained alongside weakness; no illness is diagnosed.

## Contradictions

Dissolution of problems can indicate relief, while weakness and loss-related uncertainty remain adverse possibilities. The source does not reduce dissolution to one outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Saturn = Neptune`, public difficulty meets dissolution, uncertainty, or separation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names sickness-related separation, quarantine, ending a state, and definitive rejection. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds resolution of problems, serious weakness, air or water burdens, and uncertainty through losses. The synthesis retains relief and vulnerability as different possibilities within an unsettled public situation.
