## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 44 (one-based).

To bid farewell. A day of separation. Dying.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 36; PDF page: 17 (one-based spread).

Older people in public, a serious person in public, bodily problems in public, personal public loss, a loss-bearing public day, and a center for public tasks

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public burdens embodied in personal loss, seriousness, and bodily difficulty.
- psychology: A serious person is explicit.
- body/health: Bodily problems and Witte's dying are attributed source associations.
- social/relationship: Older persons and farewell.
- events/manifestations: A day of separation or loss and a center for public tasks.
- conflicts/notes: Older age and seriousness do not themselves establish dying or illness.

## Contradictions

Ordinary age and seriousness remain distinct from bodily problems or dying. The severe example is not inferred from every farewell.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Saturn = Sun`, public difficulty takes a concrete personal and bodily form. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names farewell, separation, and dying. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds serious or older people, bodily problems, personal loss, and public tasks. The reading preserves the source's severe possibilities while distinguishing them from ordinary age, seriousness, or parting.
