### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Serious tensions in public, new burdens for the public, sudden separation from public life, difficult reforms in public, disturbances and unrest in public life, and technical losses in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public burdens intensified by sudden separation, tension, or disturbance.
- psychology: Serious tension and unrest are explicit.
- body/health: Sudden death is a severe Witte source example.
- social/relationship: Abrupt parting from another person or public life.
- events/manifestations: New burdens, difficult reforms, technical losses, and disturbances.
- conflicts/notes: The stray alternative Uranus glyph was removed against the scan; no severity threshold or timing method is supplied.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Sudden death is one extreme example among sudden separations and public disruptions, not their necessary outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Saturn = Uranus`, public difficulty becomes abrupt and tension-filled. The corrected [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names sudden parting and sudden death as distinct source examples. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds new burdens, separation from public life, difficult reforms, unrest, and technical losses. The synthesis centers on sudden disruption under pressure, without inventing conditions that would select the most severe outcome.
