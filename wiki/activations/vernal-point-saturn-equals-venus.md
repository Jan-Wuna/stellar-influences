## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 44 (one-based).

Separation of love. Separation from the mother. Liking separation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 36; PDF page: 17 (one-based spread).

Serious art in public, peace problems in public life, disturbed public harmony, loss of harmony in public life, an artistic task for the public, and public sorrow in love

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public burdens affecting affection, peace, harmony, and art.
- psychology: Sorrow in love and liking separation are distinct source possibilities.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Separation in love or from the mother.
- events/manifestations: Serious art, artistic tasks, and disturbed peace.
- conflicts/notes: Sorrow in love replaces love chamber; disturbed public harmony replaces local harmony.

## Contradictions

Witte's liking separation differs from sorrow in love. Both responses are retained rather than assuming that every separation is unwanted.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Saturn = Venus`, public difficulty concerns affection, harmony, and cultural work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names separation in love or from the mother, while also allowing a liking for separation. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds love sorrow, peace problems, disturbed harmony, serious art, and artistic tasks. The synthesis preserves emotional loss and chosen distance alongside demanding cultural expression.
