## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 44 (one-based).

Estate or home lost by fire. To work under heavy restrictions and disagreeableness.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 37; PDF page: 17 (one-based spread).

Demanding tasks for the public, burdened public leadership, burdens on the public through leadership, public fire damage, concentration on efforts for the public, and public leadership tasks

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public burdens expressed through demanding effort, leadership, and fire-related loss.
- psychology: Concentration on effort is explicit.
- body/health: No bodily or health claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Leadership bearing burdens and leadership burdening the public.
- events/manifestations: Restricted work, demanding tasks, and loss of home or estate by fire.
- conflicts/notes: Leadership replaces filtration and running; the two directions of burden remain distinct.

## Contradictions

Leadership may carry a burden or impose one. Fire damage is a separate concrete example and does not define all demanding work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Saturn = Zeus`, public difficulty is expressed through effort and leadership under restriction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names heavily constrained work and a home or estate lost to fire. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds demanding tasks, burdened leadership, burdens imposed through leadership, and focused public effort. The synthesis preserves both responsibility under pressure and its possible cost to the public.
