## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Sun = Apollon`, public personal life acquires breadth and recognition. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names honors, dignities, and religious days. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds experience with public figures, successful people, trade, and knowledge centers. The coherent emphasis is a person or center participating in an expanded public field.
