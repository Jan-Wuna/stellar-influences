## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 37 (one-based).

Bodily contact with other people, or in the place. Personally connected with others in public. Many acquaintances through or while taking care of daily chores.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 22; PDF page: 10 (one-based spread).

Other persons in public, the man as a partner in public life, the world of other men, public local center, place for persons of public life, persons of public life at a place, and center of a public place

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public personal life expressed through contact with other people and places.
- psychology: No distinct psychological claim is supplied.
- body/health: Bodily contact is named without a health claim.
- social/relationship: Partners, acquaintances, and connections during daily chores.
- events/manifestations: Public places, local centers, and the setting of public persons.
- conflicts/notes: Bodily contact does not by itself imply a sexual interpretation.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Daily acquaintances and a public center are different scales of contact.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Sun = Asc`, public personal life takes shape through concrete encounters. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names bodily or local contact and acquaintances made during daily chores. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds partners, other public persons, local centers, and public places. The common focus is the people and surroundings that give public participation a tangible setting.
