## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 37 (one-based).

Bodily contact with the general public, or in the moment. Personal connection with things and matters: of the daily life or at a moment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 22; PDF page: 10 (one-based spread).

Inwardly animated people in public, an inwardly animated public center, my husband's world, my husband in public, my person in public life, and my public center

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal involvement in public bodily presence and daily life.
- psychology: ABC's inward animation gives a personal inner register.
- body/health: Bodily contact is named without a health claim.
- social/relationship: One's person or husband in public life.
- events/manifestations: A personally significant public center and daily matters.
- conflicts/notes: The scan restores the omitted opening and husband-in-public clause; no timing layer is added.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. The husband's-world example and one's own public person are alternative personal standpoints.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Sun = MC`, public personal and bodily life is experienced from one's own standpoint. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names bodily contact with the public and personal involvement in daily matters. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds one's person or husband, a public center, and inwardly animated public presence. The synthesis connects actual participation with its personal significance without requiring a particular marital identity.
