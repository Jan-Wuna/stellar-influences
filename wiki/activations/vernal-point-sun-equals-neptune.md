## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 37 (one-based).

Frail and delicate bodies. Hindered in activity. Many deceive themselves or are deceived. Physical breakdown. Negative pole of the body. Relaxation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 23; PDF page: 10 (one-based spread).

Dissolutions in a man's world, finely sensitive people in public life, foreign people in public, personal disappointments in public life, personal uncertainty in public, and a day of public insecurity

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public personal life weakened, sensitized, uncertain, or dissolving.
- psychology: Self-deception, disappointment, and personal insecurity are explicit.
- body/health: Witte names frailty, physical breakdown, and relaxation as attributed associations.
- social/relationship: Sensitive or foreign people are separate examples.
- events/manifestations: Hindered activity and dissolution in personal public life.
- conflicts/notes: Negative pole of the body is unexplained; relaxation is retained beside breakdown.

## Contradictions

Relaxation and fine sensitivity coexist with weakness and deception. Foreign people are not identified as causes of harm, and the unexplained bodily phrase adds no mechanism.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Sun = Neptune`, public personal life is considered through sensitivity and uncertain substance. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names frailty, hindered activity, deception, breakdown, and relaxation. The restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds dissolution, personal disappointment, and insecurity, while retaining finely sensitive or foreign people as separate examples. The synthesis preserves receptivity and relaxation alongside adverse possibilities and leaves the unexplained negative-pole phrase unexpanded.
