Development of a man in the public sphere, development of a centre for the public sphere, change of people in the public sphere, personal development in the public sphere, and change in the world of man

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public personal or bodily life developing and changing.
- psychology: No distinct psychological claim is supplied.
- body/health: Metabolism and bodily growth are Witte source associations.
- social/relationship: Changes of public persons and personal development.
- events/manifestations: Food products, daily changes, and development of a public center.
- conflicts/notes: The bodily and civic registers remain parallel; no causal mechanism joins them.

## Contradictions

No direct contradiction is established. Internal bodily processes and changes of public figures are different scales of development.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Sun = Pluto`, public personal and bodily life is treated as developing rather than fixed. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names metabolism, growth, food products, and daily changes. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal development, changing public persons, and development of a public center. The shared theme is continuing transformation, while the bodily and civic examples remain distinct.
