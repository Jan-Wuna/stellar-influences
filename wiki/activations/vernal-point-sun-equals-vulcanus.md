## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 37 (one-based).

Strength of the body. Power achieved with the body. Eruptions during the day.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 23; PDF page: 10 (one-based spread).

An influential man in public, an energy center in public, violence against persons in public, personal power in public, a day of violence in public, and a center of public power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Bodily strength and personal power in public.
- psychology: The entries emphasize force rather than a distinct mental disposition.
- body/health: Witte explicitly names bodily strength and power achieved through the body.
- social/relationship: Influential people and power exercised upon persons.
- events/manifestations: Public energy or power centers; eruptions and violence are separate source possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: Strength and influence do not make the violent examples inevitable.

## Contradictions

The sources share strength and power. ABC explicitly includes violence; Witte also gives bodily capacity, so the harmful cases should not absorb the whole interpretation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Sun = Vulcanus` brings bodily strength and personal influence into the public sphere. Witte stresses power achieved through the body; ABC places powerful people, energy centers and centers of public power in that setting. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The same entries also contain eruptive or violent manifestations: Witte names eruptions during the day, and ABC violence against persons and days of public violence. These are adverse possibilities alongside strength and influence, not an inevitable outcome. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
