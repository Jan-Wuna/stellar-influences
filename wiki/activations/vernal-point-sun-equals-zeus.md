## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 37 (one-based).

To master the body. Capability to lead. Person who lead others. Days with fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 23; PDF page: 10 (one-based spread).

Leadership person for the public, creative people of the public life, achievements of the man in the public, public fire center, public performance center, and personal goal striving in the public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal mastery, leadership and public achievement.
- psychology: Goal striving and direction of effort.
- body/health: Witte names mastery of the body.
- social/relationship: People who lead others and creative public figures.
- events/manifestations: Public achievements, performance centers and fire centers.
- conflicts/notes: Fire and leadership are parallel examples; neither establishes an inevitable event.

## Contradictions

The sources agree on personal leadership. ABC expands it into public achievement and goal striving, while both retain a separate fire association.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Sun = Zeus` connects personal command with visible leadership and achievement. Witte begins with mastering the body and leading others; ABC develops creative public figures, personal goal striving and achievements in public life. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Public performance centers provide one setting for directed accomplishment. Fire centers and days with fire are another explicit source branch and should remain distinct from the leadership interpretation. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
