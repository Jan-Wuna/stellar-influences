## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 45 (one-based).

Sudden experiences with other persons. Excitement in the place.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 38; PDF page: 18 (one-based spread).

Excited public activity in a place, reforms in the public life of a place, impulses from the public surroundings, public environmental reforms, partner impulses in public, and tensions in the public environment.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden experiences and reform in one’s public surroundings.
- psychology: Local excitement and tension.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Unexpected encounters and impulses involving partners or other people.
- events/manifestations: Local reforms and changes in the public environment.
- conflicts/notes: The setting is the surrounding public world; the entries do not establish a personal character trait.

## Contradictions

Both entries locate excitement among other people or in a place. ABC adds reform and environmental change without contradicting Witte’s sudden encounters.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Uranus = Asc` places sudden encounters in an excited local setting. Witte names experiences with other people and excitement in the place; ABC shows impulses from the surrounding public environment, including partners, reform and tension. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The formula can therefore describe public renewal as it reaches a particular place and its relationships. Environmental reforms are an explicit example, while the interpersonal branch concerns unexpected experiences rather than a fixed personality description. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
