## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 45 (one-based).

Sorrow through a sudden event. Serious accidents. Attack. Murders.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 39; PDF page: 18 (one-based spread).

Deprivation through public reforms, a dangerous event in public, grief and unrest in public, innovations in public service, tensions caused by crime in public, and technical deficiencies in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public disruption involving deprivation, danger or sorrow.
- psychology: Grief and unrest following sudden events.
- body/health: Serious accidents are Witte’s historical bodily-risk association.
- social/relationship: Public crime and the consequences of reform for people.
- events/manifestations: Dangerous events, technical deficiencies and public-service innovations.
- conflicts/notes: The violent examples are source possibilities, not predictions; public-service innovation is a separate, less severe branch.

## Contradictions

Both emphasize disruption and distress. ABC also names public-service innovation, which cannot be reduced to Witte’s accidents, attacks and murders.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Uranus = Hades` concentrates on the difficult consequences of sudden public events. Witte gives sorrow, serious accidents and violent attacks; ABC links public danger and unrest with deprivation through reform, crime and technical deficiencies. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

These severe examples remain historical source associations rather than predictions about an individual. ABC also includes innovation in public service, a separate organizational expression whose outcome is not specified. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
