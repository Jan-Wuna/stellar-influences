## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 38; PDF page: 18 (one-based spread).

Successful technology in public, joy about reforms in public life, joyful tension in public, public financial reform, public legal event, public legal reform, and beneficial impulses for the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden benefit and successful public reform.
- psychology: Joy and welcome excitement around change.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Beneficial impulses reach the public.
- events/manifestations: Property gains, successful technology and financial or legal reforms.
- conflicts/notes: The entries describe favorable possibilities without guaranteeing a gain.

## Contradictions

Witte’s sudden luck and property increase complement ABC’s public technical, financial and legal successes.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Uranus = Jupiter` connects sudden change with benefit. Witte’s luck and increase in property become a wider public pattern in ABC: successful technology, welcome reforms and advantageous impulses accompanied by joy and positive tension. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Financial and legal reform are concrete examples of this favorable renewal. The entries support an opportunity or beneficial event, while giving no basis to promise a specific material gain. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
