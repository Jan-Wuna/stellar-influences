## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 45 (one-based).

Surprising ordinances of the state. Events in a state or nation. Coup d etat.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 39; PDF page: 18 (one-based spread).

special excitement in public, dominance of technology in public life, major tensions in public life, authoritative impulses in public, state reforms for the public, and important renewals in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden public change involving authority or the state.
- psychology: Excitement and major public tension.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Authorities initiate impulses and state reforms.
- events/manifestations: Unexpected ordinances, national events, technical dominance; Witte also names a coup.
- conflicts/notes: Routine reform and a coup are different degrees of upheaval.

## Contradictions

The sources agree on authoritative public change. Witte includes a coup, while ABC also allows important renewal and state reform without that extreme.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Uranus = Kronos` brings sudden public change into the sphere of authority. Witte names surprising state ordinances and national events; ABC develops authorities giving impulses, state reforms and important renewal amid public excitement and tension. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Technical dominance is one specific public setting. Witte’s coup is a more extreme political possibility and should remain distinguishable from the broader reform and administrative examples. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
