## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 45 (one-based).

Heedless or hasty actions. Accident or attempt upon a person’s life. Working with environmental technology.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 38; PDF page: 18 (one-based spread).

Exciting actions in public, new work in public, public reform work, reforms in public-relations work, activity to renew public life, and technical creation in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden public action, technical work and renewal.
- psychology: Haste or heedlessness in Witte; excitement in ABC.
- body/health: Witte includes accidents and attempts upon a person’s life as historical risk examples.
- social/relationship: Work directed toward public life and public relations.
- events/manifestations: New work, reform activity, environmental technology and technical creation.
- conflicts/notes: Constructive technical work and harmful haste are distinct branches.

## Contradictions

Both include technical activity. ABC emphasizes reform and new work, while Witte also stresses haste and severe harm.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Uranus = Mars` describes public excitement becoming action. ABC gives new work, reform work and technical creation, while Witte’s environmental technology supplies a closely related practical setting. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))

Witte also identifies a hazardous branch in heedless or hasty action, accidents and attempts upon a person’s life. Those historical examples should remain separate from the constructive work of renewing public life. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
