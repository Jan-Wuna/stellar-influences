## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 45 (one-based).

Sudden ideas, thoughts. Telegraphic news. Breaking news.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 38; PDF page: 18 (one-based spread).

Exciting news for the public, reports about renewals in public life, excited youth in public, excitement about youth in public, public traffic rhythm, and tense conversations in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden news, ideas and public communication.
- psychology: Quick thoughts and excitement.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Youth and tense public conversations.
- events/manifestations: Breaking news, reports on renewal and public traffic rhythms.
- conflicts/notes: Telegraphic news is Witte’s historical medium example.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize sudden or exciting communication. ABC adds youth and traffic as specific public settings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Uranus = Mercury` connects sudden ideas with news moving through public life. Witte’s telegraphic and breaking news meet ABC’s exciting reports about renewal, making communication itself part of the public excitement. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Tense conversations, excited youth and public responses to youth provide interpersonal examples. ABC also names traffic rhythms, a separate movement-related expression that should not be reduced to verbal communication alone. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
