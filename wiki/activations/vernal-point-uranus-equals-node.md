## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 45 (one-based).

Experiencing or sharing the experience of a sudden event. To bear witness. Suddenly to witness something.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 38; PDF page: 18 (one-based spread).

Excitement in public connections, renewal of connections in public life, tension-filled connections in public, connection to public events, and connection to technology in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections through sudden public experiences.
- psychology: Excitement and tension within connections.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Sharing an event and bearing witness.
- events/manifestations: Renewed public connections and links to events or technology.
- conflicts/notes: Connections are broader than professional public relations.

## Contradictions

Witte’s shared experience and witness role complement ABC’s excited or renewed public connections.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Uranus = Node` connects people through a sudden public experience. Witte emphasizes sharing the event or unexpectedly witnessing it; ABC describes the resulting field of excited, renewed or tension-filled connections. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Links to public events and technology make that participation concrete. The source supports involvement and witnessing, without specifying agreement among everyone connected or a professional communications role. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
