## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 45 (one-based).

Sudden appearance of a new idea. Propaganda. Inventor for environmental technology.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 39; PDF page: 18 (one-based spread).

Insight for public reforms, intellectual renewal in public life, a cultural event in public, clarity through public reforms, cultural reform in public life, new truths for the public, and public cultural renewal.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: New ideas and insight enter public reform.
- psychology: Intellectual renewal and clarity.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Ideas and new truths are addressed to the public.
- events/manifestations: Cultural events, cultural reform and environmental invention.
- conflicts/notes: Witte’s propaganda is not equivalent to ABC’s truth claims.

## Contradictions

Both support new public ideas. Witte includes propaganda, whereas ABC stresses clarity and truth; retain that difference rather than assuming all persuasion is truthful.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Uranus = Poseidon` connects the sudden appearance of an idea with public intellectual and cultural renewal. ABC develops insight for reform, clarity, cultural events and new truths; Witte adds invention in environmental technology as a practical field for the new idea. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))

Witte also names propaganda. That public use of ideas should remain distinguishable from ABC’s emphasis on truth and insight, since the entries do not establish the truthfulness of every message. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
