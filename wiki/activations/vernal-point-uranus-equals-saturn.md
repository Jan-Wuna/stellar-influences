## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 45 (one-based).

Sudden events connected with separation. Sudden restrictions of the public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 39; PDF page: 18 (one-based spread).

Burdensome impulses in public life, concentration on public reforms, public reform tasks, sudden separations in public, technical problems in public, and surprising losses in public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden public restriction and burdensome reform.
- psychology: Concentration under pressure.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Sudden separation and limits affecting the public.
- events/manifestations: Reform tasks, technical problems and surprising losses.
- conflicts/notes: Focused reform work and involuntary loss are different expressions.

## Contradictions

Both entries stress restriction and separation. ABC adds concentrated reform tasks, giving a work-oriented branch alongside loss.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Uranus = Saturn` places sudden change under conditions of restriction and burden. Witte names separation and limits imposed on the public; ABC adds technical problems, surprising losses and demanding impulses in public life. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC’s concentration on reform and reform tasks gives a practical expression in sustained attention to difficult public changes. It does not remove the separate source possibilities of abrupt separation or loss. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
