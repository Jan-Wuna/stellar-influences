## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 45 (one-based).

Nervous. Body under tension. Sudden event on the day. Sudden event that affects the body.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 38; PDF page: 18 (one-based spread).

Agitated persons in public, tense people in public life, men of technology in public, personal impulses from the public, a day of public reforms, and a center for renewal of public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public excitement directly involves a person or body.
- psychology: Nervousness, tension and personal impulses.
- body/health: Witte names bodily tension and an event affecting the body.
- social/relationship: Agitated people and public technical figures.
- events/manifestations: Sudden daily events, reform days and centers of public renewal.
- conflicts/notes: The bodily language does not specify an illness or injury.

## Contradictions

Both describe personal involvement in public excitement. Witte stresses the body; ABC stresses people, technology and reform.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Uranus = Sun` makes public excitement immediate and personal. Witte describes nervousness, bodily tension and a sudden event affecting the body; ABC gives agitated or tense people and impulses coming from public life. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Technical figures, reform days and centers of public renewal supply concrete settings. The bodily association remains specific to Witte’s wording and does not itself identify an illness or injury. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
