## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 45 (one-based).

Sudden expressions of love. Sudden peace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 38; PDF page: 18 (one-based spread).

Joy in public rhythm, harmonious impulses from the public, harmonious rhythms in public, new love in public, new art in public, and an art event in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden affection, peace and public harmony.
- psychology: Joy in rhythm and harmonious impulses.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: New love and sudden expressions of affection.
- events/manifestations: Peace, new art and public art events.
- conflicts/notes: The entries allow different relational and artistic expressions.

## Contradictions

Witte’s sudden love and peace agree with ABC’s harmony and new love. ABC adds rhythm and art as public settings.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Uranus = Venus` turns sudden public change toward affection and harmony. Witte names unexpected expressions of love and sudden peace; ABC develops new love, harmonious impulses and pleasure in public rhythm. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

New art and public art events provide a cultural expression of the same welcome renewal. These examples sit alongside the relational branch rather than implying that every artistic event brings a love relationship. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
