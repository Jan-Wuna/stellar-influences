## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 41 (one-based).

To be loved a great deal by others.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 30; PDF page: 14 (one-based spread).

Joy in a public place, peaceful surroundings in public, public harmony with a partner, a public art venue, a place of harmony in public, a beautiful place in the surroundings, and beautification of the public environment.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affection and harmony in the surrounding public world.
- psychology: Joy in a place and appreciation of beauty.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Being loved by others and harmony with a partner.
- events/manifestations: Art venues, harmonious places and public beautification.
- conflicts/notes: The place-related examples are broader than a private relationship.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes affection received from others. ABC supplies peaceful relationships and attractive surroundings as compatible local expressions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Venus = Asc` connects affection from other people with a harmonious public environment. Witte emphasizes being loved; ABC places joy, peaceful surroundings and harmony with a partner in a particular public setting. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Art venues, beautiful places and beautification of the public environment extend that harmony into the surroundings themselves. The formula therefore has both interpersonal and place-related expressions. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
