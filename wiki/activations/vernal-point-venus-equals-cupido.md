## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 41 (one-based).

Marital matters for female persons. A marriage based on love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 31; PDF page: 14 (one-based spread).

Shared joy in public, peaceful community in public, harmony in a public community, public artistic community, and a community's preference for the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Shared public joy and harmonious union.
- psychology: Enjoyment of communal life.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Peaceful communities and marriage based on love.
- events/manifestations: Artistic communities and public communal harmony.
- conflicts/notes: Witte’s women’s marital matters is historical gendered scope, not a universal assignment.

## Contradictions

Witte gives marriage and ABC gives communities. Both emphasize affectionate union, with different social scales.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Venus = Cupido` connects enjoyment with belonging together. ABC describes shared joy, peaceful communities and harmony within public groups; Witte gives marriage based on love as a specific personal union. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))

Artistic communities supply a cultural setting for that shared satisfaction. Witte’s separate reference to women’s marital matters retains its historical gendered scope rather than defining every expression of the formula. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
