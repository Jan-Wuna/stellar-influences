## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 41 (one-based).

Bliss. Happiness through general peace.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 30; PDF page: 14 (one-based spread).

Joy in the beauty of the world, success in art in public, success in love in public life, public success for peace, legal peace in public, legal art in public life, and a liking for public finances.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Happiness through beauty, love and public peace.
- psychology: Bliss and appreciation of the world’s beauty.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Success in love and harmonious public relations.
- events/manifestations: Artistic success, peace successes, legal peace and public finances.
- conflicts/notes: Rechts-Frieden and Rechts-Kunst concern law; the precise scope of “legal art” is unspecified.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize happiness and peace. ABC adds art, love and legal or financial examples, rather than a political “right wing” meaning.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Venus = Jupiter` connects happiness with beauty and peaceful public life. Witte gives bliss through general peace; ABC develops joy in the world’s beauty and success in art, love and efforts toward peace. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also includes legal peace and an interest in public finances. Its “legal art” remains a literal source phrase whose particular practice is unspecified; the German law-related terms do not support the former political “right wing” reading. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
