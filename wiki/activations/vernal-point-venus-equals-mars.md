## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 41 (one-based).

Mating time. Love in general. Relatives on the side of the mother.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 30; PDF page: 14 (one-based spread).

Work peace in public Life, joy in public Work, creating peace in public, harmonious actions in public Life, artistic activity for the public, and preference for public Work

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affection expressed through harmonious action and public work.
- psychology: Joy in work and preference for public activity.
- body/health: Witte gives mating time as a historical bodily association.
- social/relationship: Love and maternal relatives in Witte’s entry.
- events/manifestations: Peacemaking, harmonious actions and artistic work for the public.
- conflicts/notes: The family and mating examples are separate from ABC’s work setting.

## Contradictions

ABC emphasizes public work and peacemaking; Witte emphasizes love, mating and maternal kin. Affection connects part of the material, but the family reference remains a separate example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Venus = Mars` gives harmony an active public expression. ABC describes pleasure in public work, harmonious action, peacemaking and artistic activity for the public; Witte’s love association supplies a related personal branch. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))

Witte separately includes mating time and relatives on the mother’s side. The local entries do not explain those examples through public work, so they remain distinct source associations. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
