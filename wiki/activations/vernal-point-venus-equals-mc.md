## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 41 (one-based).

Ones power of attraction. The native is beloved. The personal attitude of love, peace or freedom towards the general public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 30; PDF page: 14 (one-based spread).

Soulful art in public, harmony of souls in public life, my joy in public, my harmony in public life, my art in public, and my liking for public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal attraction and inwardly felt public harmony.
- psychology: Joy, love, peace and an affinity for public life.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Being beloved and harmony between people.
- events/manifestations: Personal artistic expression and soulful public art.
- conflicts/notes: ABC speaks of art, not an animated artist.

## Contradictions

The entries agree on personal affection and harmony. ABC adds inwardly animated art, while Witte also names an attitude of freedom toward the public.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Venus = MC` connects personal attraction with an inwardly felt harmony in public life. Witte describes being beloved and approaching the public with love, peace or freedom; ABC adds one’s own joy and affinity for public life. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC’s soulful art and personal artistic expression give that inner accord a creative form. Its harmony of souls concerns shared feeling rather than a claim about a particular occupation or social position. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
