## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 41 (one-based).

Beauty of thought. Well-thought-of. Making new acquaintances through general popularity. An appreciation for art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 30; PDF page: 14 (one-based spread).

Reports about the beauty of the world, peace conversations in public, peaceful youth of the world, harmonious thoughts in public life, youthful love in public, media art in public, and youth’s liking for the public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Agreeable thought and communication about beauty or peace.
- psychology: Harmonious ideas and appreciation of art.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Popularity, new acquaintances and youthful love.
- events/manifestations: Peace conversations, beauty reports and media art.
- conflicts/notes: Youth and art are explicit examples rather than general traits of everyone involved.

## Contradictions

Both entries connect harmonious thought and art. Witte adds social popularity; ABC adds peace conversations, youth and media.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Venus = Mercury` connects harmonious thinking with favorable public communication. Witte’s beauty of thought and appreciation of art meet ABC’s reports on beauty, peace conversations and media art. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Being well regarded and making acquaintances through popularity give the interpersonal expression. ABC’s peaceful youth and youthful love add specific social examples without making them the formula’s only setting. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
