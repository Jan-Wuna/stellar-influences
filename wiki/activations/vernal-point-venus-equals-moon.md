## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 41 (one-based).

A woman’s love. To be loved by the public. The people’s love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 30; PDF page: 14 (one-based spread).

Joy for a people in public, a peaceful people in public, a feeling of joy in public life, a harmonious woman in public life, public peace for a people, beautiful women in public, and an hour of public joy.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public affection and shared joy.
- psychology: A collective feeling of happiness and harmony.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: A woman’s love and affection from the public.
- events/manifestations: Peace for a people and moments of public joy.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered examples remain source examples rather than fixed assumptions about women.

## Contradictions

Witte’s public love and ABC’s collective joy and peace reinforce one another; ABC also gives aesthetic and gendered examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Venus = Moon` connects affection with the feelings of a public or people. Witte names being loved by the public; ABC develops a peaceful people, shared joy and public peace, including particular moments of collective happiness. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

A woman’s love, a harmonious woman and beautiful women are the entries’ specific gendered examples. They sit within the broader theme of affection and collective enjoyment rather than defining women generally. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
