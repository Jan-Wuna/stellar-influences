## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 41 (one-based).

Falsehood. To pretend love. Make believe love. Unrequited love.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 31; PDF page: 14 (one-based spread).

Fine sensitivity and harmony in public, unfamiliar or foreign art in public, joyful expectations in public, unclear wishes in public life, uncertain peace in public, and the future beauty of the world.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sensitive public harmony mixed with uncertain affection or expectations.
- psychology: Fine feeling, hopeful expectation and unclear wishes.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Witte gives pretended or unrequited love.
- events/manifestations: Uncertain peace, unfamiliar art and anticipated beauty.
- conflicts/notes: ABC’s hopeful sensitivity differs from Witte’s emphasis on falsehood.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses false and unrequited love. ABC includes uncertainty but also sensitivity, joyful expectation and future beauty, so its entry is not wholly deceptive or adverse.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Venus = Neptune` places harmony and affection in a field of sensitivity and uncertain expectation. ABC gives fine feeling, joyful hopes and unclear wishes, with uncertain public peace and unfamiliar art as concrete settings. ([ABC](#abc-entry))

Witte’s pretended and unrequited love supplies a more difficult relational branch. Its falsehood should remain distinguishable from ABC’s hopeful expectations and future beauty, since uncertainty alone does not establish deception. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
