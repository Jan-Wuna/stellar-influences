## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

General harmonious development.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `31`, one-based PDF p. `14`, complete Pluto row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Freude-Entfaltung in der Öffentlichkeit, harmonische Entwicklung in der Öffentlichkeit, Kunst-Entwicklung im öffentlichen Leben, wachsender Friede in der Öffentlichkeit, wachsende Vorliebe für die Öffentlichkeit, Wandlung zum öffentlichen Frieden

Editorial translation: Unfolding of joy in public; harmonious development in public; development of art in public life; growing peace in public; growing preference for public life; change toward public peace.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: harmonious development becomes visible in public life.
- psychology: growing joy and a growing preference for public involvement appear in the ABC entry.
- body/health: neither entry specifies a bodily condition or health outcome.
- social/relationship: the public setting concerns peace and shared harmony; a private relationship is not specified.
- events/manifestations: developing public art, increasing peace, and a change toward public peace are named possibilities.
- conflicts/notes: ABC names development of art alongside public harmony and peace; no timetable or guarantee of progress is supplied.

## Contradictions

- The entries differ in specificity: Witte gives general harmonious development, while ABC places it in public life. Their overlap does not establish independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Venus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies public harmony through development: joy, peace, and artistic life take shape or grow. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) supplies the broad theme of harmonious development (PDF p. 41); the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives its public setting and a movement toward public peace (printed p. 31; PDF p. 14). These are possible forms of the picture, without a stated timetable or guarantee of progress.
