## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Insights for a peaceful world. Calm ocean. Non dangerous water masses.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Peace ideas in public, spiritual harmony in public life, cultural peace in public, cultural beauty in public, arts and culture in public, and preference for the truth in public life

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: public harmony takes the form of peace ideas, cultural beauty, and spiritual accord.
- psychology: a preference for truth in public life is explicit in ABC.
- body/health: neither entry states a bodily or medical implication.
- social/relationship: the emphasis is public and cultural peace, rather than a specified private relationship.
- events/manifestations: public art and culture are named; Witte also gives calm ocean and nondangerous water masses.
- conflicts/notes: the water imagery is a separate source manifestation, not evidence that an actual body of water is safe.

## Contradictions

- The cultural and spiritual emphasis in ABC does not contradict Witte's peaceful-world theme. Witte's additional water imagery has no matching explanation in ABC.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Venus = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies public harmony through ideas of peace, spiritual accord, and cultural expression. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects this with public art, beauty, and a preference for truth (printed p. 31; PDF p. 14), giving substance to the [Witte entry's](#witte-entry) insights for a peaceful world (PDF p. 41). Witte also names calm ocean and nondangerous water masses; that distinct image remains a source-specific possibility rather than a practical assessment of water safety.
