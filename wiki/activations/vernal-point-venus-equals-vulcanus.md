## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Strong force of attraction. General longing of something. General passion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Violent love in public, power and beauty in public, artistic influence in public, power of joy in public life, strong harmony in public life, and preference for public power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: attraction, passion, and public harmony acquire force and influence.
- psychology: strong longing and general passion are explicit in Witte.
- body/health: neither entry describes a health condition or bodily mechanism.
- social/relationship: forceful or violent love is one ABC formulation; it does not make coercion a necessary expression.
- events/manifestations: public artistic influence, power joined with beauty, and strong public harmony are named.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's public power and Witte's attraction share intensity, but the entries do not explain how to distinguish constructive influence from violence.

## Contradictions

- Strong harmony and violent love are distinct possibilities within the evidence. The page does not resolve them into one uniformly favorable or harmful outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Venus = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies public harmony and attraction through strength: longing and passion can accompany powerful artistic influence or an emphatic presence of beauty and joy in public life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) stresses attraction and general passion (PDF p. 41); the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds both strong harmony and violent love (printed p. 31; PDF p. 14). That range supports intensity, without treating violence as inevitable or equating force with harmony.
