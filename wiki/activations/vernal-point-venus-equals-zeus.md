## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

General procreative frenzy. Fortunate sowing time. The good sower.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source reference: printed p. `31`, one-based PDF p. `14`, complete Zeus row.

German passage transcribed from the scan:

> Friedens-Anstrengungen in der Öffentlichkeit, Führungs-Freude im öffentlichen Leben, Kunst-Leistungen in der Öffentlichkeit, öffentliches Friedens-Feuer, Schönheit der Schöpfung im öffentlichen Leben, Vorliebe für öffentliche Ziele

Editorial translation: Peace efforts in public; enjoyment of leadership in public life; artistic achievements in public; a public peace fire; beauty of creation in public life; preference for public goals.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: public peace and beauty are pursued through effort, achievement, and creation.
- psychology: the scan names enjoyment of leadership and a preference for public goals.
- body/health: Witte's procreative wording is not accompanied by a medical or fertility claim.
- social/relationship: peace efforts concern public life; no particular private partnership is specified.
- events/manifestations: artistic achievements, peace efforts, creation, and favorable sowing are the entry's distinct examples.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's public peace fire remains a literal source example whose form or mechanism is not explained.

## Contradictions

- Witte's procreation and sowing examples differ from ABC's public peace and art. The evidence does not make either setting mandatory or establish a causal connection between them.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Venus = Zeus`, Zeus specifies public harmony through directed effort and creation. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives peace efforts, artistic achievements, enjoyment of leadership, and public goals (printed p. 31; PDF p. 14). The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) instead expresses the productive theme through procreation and favorable sowing (PDF p. 41). The connection is activity that brings something into being, while the public, artistic, agricultural, and procreative examples remain distinct possibilities rather than promised results.
