#### Witte Entry

To spread out. To extend on Earth. Quantities. Commerce. Sciences. Trade. Glory. General recognition, honors, success. General prosperity in commerce, trades and science. The global economy. Freedom and peace in general public. World religions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: public and terrestrial affairs extend in scale, quantity, and reach.
- psychology: recognition, honors, and success are named; the entry does not specify an inner motive.
- body/health: no bodily condition or medical meaning is given.

- events/manifestations: commerce, trade, science, the global economy, prosperity, and public recognition are explicit fields.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Vernal Point = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the repeated public-world pair through extension and scale. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) applies this to quantities, trade, science, and the global economy, then includes prosperity, honors, general freedom and peace, and world religions (PDF p. 34). Expansion in public reach unifies the entry, while these different fields remain possible expressions rather than one required sequence or a guarantee of success.
