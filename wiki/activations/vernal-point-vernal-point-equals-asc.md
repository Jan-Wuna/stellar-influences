## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Vernal Point = Asc`, Asc specifies the repeated public-world pair through the immediate place where encounters occur. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins meetings and acquaintances with the relationship between a location, its surroundings, and the wider public (PDF p. 33).
