## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Vernal Point = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the repeated public-world pair through favorable circumstances in general life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects success and enrichment with contentment and happiness, while extending law to international justice and jurisdiction (PDF p. 33). Material prosperity, emotional satisfaction, and legal matters are separate parts of its range; the entry does not guarantee that all occur together.
