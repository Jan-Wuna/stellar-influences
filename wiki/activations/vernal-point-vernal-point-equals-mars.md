## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Labor, the forces of nature. Injuries. Natures power in a general sense. Work and activity in public life. Working energy. Energy in general. The world of labor. To show courage.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: public life is specified through work, activity, and force.
- psychology: showing courage is explicit; the entry gives no fuller personality profile.
- body/health: injuries are a historical source association, without a cause, diagnosis, or prognosis.
- social/relationship: the world of labor and activity in public life provide the collective setting.
- events/manifestations: labor, working energy, and the forces of nature are named.
- conflicts/notes: natural forces, work, courage, and injuries are listed together without a stated causal sequence.

## Contradictions

- The single entry includes several forms of energy and action. It does not establish that work or courage necessarily produces injury.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Vernal Point = Mars`, Mars specifies the repeated public-world pair through active force: work, labor, and energy expressed in public life. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) also includes natural forces and showing courage (PDF p. 33), extending the theme beyond employment alone. Injuries are another named manifestation, but the entry gives no basis for treating them as a necessary result of activity or courage.
