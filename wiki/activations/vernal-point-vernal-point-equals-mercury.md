## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Vernal Point = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the repeated public-world pair through the circulation of thought and information. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names general opinion, news, media, and information in public life, with youth and general motion as further associations (PDF p. 33). The formula therefore concerns a public field of ideas and reports; this entry does not establish what people think or which information is accurate.
