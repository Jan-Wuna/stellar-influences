## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vernal Point = Zeus` connects the urge to activity with forces at work in the world. Controlled fire, steam machinery and railroads give concrete examples of force directed into operation; conflagrations and war show destructive expressions. ([Witte](#witte-entry))

The entry separately names natural laws, natural forces and procreation. It does not explain a mechanism joining those broad associations or require activity to take a military form. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
