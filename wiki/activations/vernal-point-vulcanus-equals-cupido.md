## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 54 (one-based).

Power pressure exerted by an association, community or political party.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 57; PDF page: 27 (one-based spread).

An energy community in public, shared power in public life, a community of force or violence in public, the influence of art in public life, a synthesis of power in the world, and a powerful world community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Power pooled and exerted through communities.
- psychology: The entries emphasize collective pressure rather than a private disposition.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Associations, communities and political parties.
- events/manifestations: Shared power, influential art and powerful world communities.
- conflicts/notes: The force community can have a coercive sense; art influence is a distinct example.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses pressure exerted by organized groups. ABC includes shared power and artistic influence, allowing broader communal expressions.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Cupido` describes power organized through a group. Witte names pressure from an association, community or political party; ABC expands this into shared public power and powerful world communities. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The community-of-force wording retains a potentially coercive branch. Art’s public influence supplies a separate cultural expression, showing that collective influence is not confined to political pressure. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
