## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 54 (one-based).

Powerful meanness, lowness and vulgarity. The lack of energy. Dead volcano.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 57; PDF page: 27 (one-based spread).

Influence of the past in public life, danger of violence in public life, criminal forces in public, crime involving power in public life, renunciation of power in public, and disadvantageous energies in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Adverse force, diminished energy or relinquished power.
- psychology: Witte’s meanness and vulgarity are historical judgments.
- body/health: Lack of energy is named without a medical explanation.
- social/relationship: Criminal forces and renunciation of public power.
- events/manifestations: Danger of violence, influence of the past and a dead volcano.
- conflicts/notes: Renunciation of power was mistranslated as abuse; the scan confirms relinquishment.

## Contradictions

Both include adverse force. Witte additionally gives lack of energy and a dead volcano; ABC gives the past’s influence and renunciation, so the whole entry cannot be reduced to abuse.

## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Hades` includes harmful public force in ABC’s danger of violence and criminal power. Witte’s meanness and vulgarity are historical evaluative language attached to a related adverse branch. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))

Reduced or relinquished power is a distinct expression: Witte names lack of energy and a dead volcano, while ABC explicitly gives renunciation of power. Its influence of the past remains another public example, not proof of criminal conduct. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
