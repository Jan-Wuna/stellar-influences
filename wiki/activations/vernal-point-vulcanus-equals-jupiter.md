## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 54 (one-based).

Great fortune. Power through possessions. Financial power. Powerful justice.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 56; PDF page: 27 (one-based spread).

Energy advantages in public life, successful power in public, financial strength in public, strength through joy in public, the power of money in public life, public financial power, and legal force in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Financial strength and effective public power.
- psychology: Strength through joy.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Influence supported by possessions, money or justice.
- events/manifestations: Great fortune, financial power and legal force.
- conflicts/notes: Legal force and justice are source associations, not guarantees of a favorable legal outcome.

## Contradictions

Both emphasize financial power and favorable strength. ABC adds energy advantages and strength through joy to Witte’s fortune and justice.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Jupiter` connects public power with resources and favorable effectiveness. Witte gives fortune and influence through possessions; ABC develops financial strength, the power of money and energy advantages in public life. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Strength through joy supplies a subjective branch, while powerful justice and legal force provide institutional examples. The source material describes possible forms of strength without promising wealth or a particular legal result. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
