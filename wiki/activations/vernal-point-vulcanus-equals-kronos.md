## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 54 (one-based).

The great powers. Using the influence of one’s special position. Exercising executive power of a state or country.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 57; PDF page: 27 (one-based spread).

The prestige of public power, special energies in public, influential authority in public life, great public force or violence, great public influence, the power of hierarchies in the world, and public state power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public power exercised from an authoritative position.
- psychology: The entries emphasize authority rather than a distinct mental disposition.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Great powers, hierarchies and influential authorities.
- events/manifestations: Executive state power, prestige and substantial public influence.
- conflicts/notes: Authority and great force do not establish the legitimacy of any particular use.

## Contradictions

Both entries emphasize major public authority. ABC adds prestige and force to Witte’s executive and positional power.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Kronos` connects public strength with authority and position. Witte describes great powers and the exercise of executive state power; ABC develops influential authorities, hierarchies and the prestige of public power. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Special energies and great public influence show the reach of that position. ABC also retains force or violence as a possible expression, so the description of authority does not itself establish a benevolent use of power. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
