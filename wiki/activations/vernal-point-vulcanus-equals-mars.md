## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 54 (one-based).

To be forced. To be under the pressure of a powerful force. To work with great energies. Force of arms.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 56; PDF page: 27 (one-based spread).

Actions of world power, work with public energies, influential public activity, public energies in action, violent activity in public life, strenuous work in public, and strength of will in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Great public force put into action or work.
- psychology: Strength of will and pressure.
- body/health: Strenuous work is explicit; no medical condition is specified.
- social/relationship: Coercion, force of arms and actions of world power.
- events/manifestations: Influential activity and work with great energies.
- conflicts/notes: Working forcefully and being forced are different positions within the entries.

## Contradictions

Both include great energies in action. Witte highlights coercion and arms, while ABC also includes influential work and strength of will.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Mars` puts substantial public energy into activity. Both entries name work with great energies; ABC adds strenuous work, influential public action and strength of will. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

A more coercive branch appears in Witte’s being forced, pressure from a powerful force and force of arms, alongside ABC’s violent activity. The entries distinguish applying energy in work from having power imposed upon oneself. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
