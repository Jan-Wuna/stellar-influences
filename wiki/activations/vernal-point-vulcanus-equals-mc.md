## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 54 (one-based).

One’s own authority and power. To deal with forces. To appear influential or powerful.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 56; PDF page: 27 (one-based spread).

My influence in public life, my energies in public, my power in public, my strength in public life, inner force or psychological violence in public, and inner strength in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: One’s own public power and influence.
- psychology: Inner strength and force; the source also admits psychological violence.
- body/health: Energy and strength are named without a medical explanation.
- social/relationship: Appearing influential or powerful.
- events/manifestations: Dealing with forces and exercising personal public authority.
- conflicts/notes: ABC’s seelische Gewalt has a force-or-violence range; no psychiatric label follows.

## Contradictions

Witte and ABC agree on personal power. ABC adds energy and inner strength, including an ambiguous force-or-violence phrase rather than a diagnosis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vulcanus = MC` brings public power into one’s own experience and expression. Witte describes personal authority, dealing with forces and appearing influential; ABC adds one’s energies, strength and power in public life. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC’s inner strength supports a subjective capacity to sustain that presence. Its separate inner-force or psychological-violence wording remains broad and does not establish a medical condition or specify the direction of any coercion. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
