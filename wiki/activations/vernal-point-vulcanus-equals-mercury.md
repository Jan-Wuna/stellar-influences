## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 54 (one-based).

To have an influence through thinking, writing or speaking. Power of thinking. Power of language. Buoyancy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 56; PDF page: 27 (one-based spread).

Influence of Youth in Public, Youth Violence in Public Life, Power of Thought in Public Life, Power of Language in Public, News Influence in Public Life, and Public Media Influence

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public influence through thought, language and media.
- psychology: Strength of thought; Witte also names buoyancy.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Youth’s influence and a separate youth-violence branch.
- events/manifestations: Influential writing, speaking, news and media.
- conflicts/notes: Buoyancy is a standalone source term without a supplied mechanism.

## Contradictions

Both connect thought and language with influence. ABC adds media and youth violence; that adverse example does not define all communication.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Mercury` locates public power in thinking and communication. Witte’s influence through writing and speaking meets ABC’s power of thought and language, news influence and public media influence. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also gives youth’s influence and youth violence as separate social examples. Witte’s isolated “buoyancy” remains a source term without enough explanation here to establish a physical process. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
