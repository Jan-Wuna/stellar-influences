## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 54 (one-based).

Women with physical strength. Influential women. A violent people. To cause excitement in public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 56; PDF page: 27 (one-based spread).

A woman’s influence in public life, the energies of a people for the world, women’s power in public life, emotional influence from the public, violence against a woman in public, the strength of a people in the world, and a strong woman in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Women’s or collective strength and public emotional influence.
- psychology: Public excitement and emotional influence.
- body/health: Witte explicitly names women’s physical strength.
- social/relationship: Influential women and the power of a people.
- events/manifestations: Collective energy, public excitement and a separate violence branch.
- conflicts/notes: Strength, violence by a people and violence against a woman are different source roles.

## Contradictions

Both describe women’s and collective strength. Witte also gives a violent people, while ABC explicitly gives violence against a woman; those positions must not be conflated.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Moon` connects public influence with women and collective strength. Witte names physically strong and influential women; ABC adds women’s public power, the energy of a people and emotional influence coming from the public. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The violence examples have different roles: Witte names a violent people, while ABC names violence against a woman. They remain distinct historical possibilities alongside strength and public excitement, without assigning violence to women or a whole people generally. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
