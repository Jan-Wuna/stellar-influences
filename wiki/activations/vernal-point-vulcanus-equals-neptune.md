## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 54 (one-based).

Power felt and sensed, but not seen or heard. Power that lies in deception. Powerlessness. Pretending. Powers of wind or water.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 57; PDF page: 27 (one-based spread).

Dissolution of public power, subtle-material energy in public life, weakness of power in public, unknown power in public, pretence of power in public life, and influence on the public’s future.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public power becomes hidden, weakened or pretended.
- psychology: Sensing influence without clear outward signs.
- body/health: “Subtle-material energy” is source doctrine without a physiological explanation.
- social/relationship: Deceptive or unknown public power.
- events/manifestations: Dissolving authority, powerlessness and forces of wind or water.
- conflicts/notes: Hidden power, pretended power and absent power are distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

Both include deception and weakness. Witte also names wind and water, while ABC adds subtle-material energy and future influence without explaining their mechanisms.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Neptune` describes power whose presence or effectiveness is uncertain. Witte gives force sensed without being seen or heard; ABC adds unknown power and weakening or dissolving public power. Both also explicitly include pretence or deception. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Wind and water provide Witte’s separate natural-force examples. ABC’s subtle-material energy remains attributed doctrine, and its influence on the public’s future supplies no further mechanism or definite outcome. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
