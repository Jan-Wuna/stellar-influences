## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 54 (one-based).

Influential connections in general. Associated with violent acts or actions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 56; PDF page: 27 (one-based spread).

Encounter with public force or authority, influential connections in the world, influential contacts with the public, energy mediation for the public, a public energy network, and connection to public power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections involving public power or energy.
- psychology: The entries describe influential involvement rather than a distinct mental trait.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Influential contacts and association with violent acts.
- events/manifestations: Encounters with authority, energy mediation and energy networks.
- conflicts/notes: Association with force does not by itself identify someone as its perpetrator.

## Contradictions

Both emphasize influential connections. Witte adds association with violence, while ABC also includes public power and energy networks.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Node` connects people or networks with public power. Witte’s influential connections meet ABC’s influential contacts, encounters with public force or authority and links to public power. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Energy mediation and public energy networks give practical organizational examples. Witte also names association with violent actions, but the wording does not establish who performs them or make every influential connection violent. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
