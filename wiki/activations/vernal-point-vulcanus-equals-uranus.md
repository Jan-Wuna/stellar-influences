## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 54 (one-based).

A suddenly appearing force. Explosive power. Combustion in a motor.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 57; PDF page: 27 (one-based spread).

Energy reforms in public, an influential world event, a public event involving force or violence, public power tensions, new energy in public life, technical energies in public, and unrest through public force or violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Suddenly activated force and public energy change.
- psychology: Unrest and power tension.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: An influential world event and public force.
- events/manifestations: Explosive power, motor combustion, energy reforms and technical energies.
- conflicts/notes: Constructive technical energy and violent unrest are separate expressions.

## Contradictions

Both emphasize force and technical energy. Witte gives motor combustion, while ABC adds public reform, power tension and unrest.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Uranus` brings public force into sudden activity. Witte’s explosive power and motor combustion supply technical examples; ABC connects energy reforms and new technical energies with influential world events. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Public power tensions and unrest through force or violence form a more disruptive branch. The entries do not make every technical energy development an act of violence. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
