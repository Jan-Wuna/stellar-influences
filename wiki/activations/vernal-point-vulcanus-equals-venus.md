## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 54 (one-based).

Great power of attraction. Magnetism. Strenuous peace efforts in general

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 56; PDF page: 27 (one-based spread).

The influence of beauty in public life, peace power in public, harmonious public influence, the influence of art in public life, love’s influence in public life, and the force or violence of love in public.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong attraction and the public influence of beauty or love.
- psychology: Harmonious influence and attraction.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Love’s influence and strenuous peace efforts.
- events/manifestations: Public art, beauty and power applied toward peace.
- conflicts/notes: Magnetism is Witte’s term; ABC’s Liebes-Gewalt leaves a force-or-violence ambiguity.

## Contradictions

Both include powerful attraction or harmonious influence. ABC’s force-of-love compound can also carry a coercive sense, which should not be silently softened.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Venus` connects attraction with strong public influence. Witte’s power of attraction and strenuous peace efforts meet ABC’s influence of beauty, art and love, alongside harmonious influence and power directed toward peace. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Witte’s “magnetism” is retained as his attraction term without a physical mechanism. ABC’s force-or-violence-of-love phrase remains ambiguous and should not be turned into either a promise of harmony or an allegation of abuse. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
