## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 50 (one-based).

A difficult problem is attacked. Eruption of the earth. Earthquake.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 49; PDF page: 23 (one-based spread).

Endurance of public leadership, blocked public leadership, containment of public fire, thorough efforts in public life, consolidation of public goals, restriction of public achievement, and stabilization of public leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Directed effort faces obstruction or gains stability.
- psychology: Endurance and thorough effort.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Blocked or stabilized public leadership.
- events/manifestations: Difficult problems, consolidated goals, restricted achievements; earth eruptions and earthquakes are separate.
- conflicts/notes: The geophysical examples are not explained as effects of leadership.

## Contradictions

ABC concentrates on leadership, effort and limits. Witte adds attacking a difficult problem and separate earth-eruption or earthquake examples.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Zeus = Admetos` describes directed effort meeting something difficult or resistant. Witte gives tackling a difficult problem; ABC develops endurance, thorough work and consolidated goals alongside blocked leadership and restricted public achievement. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Stabilized leadership and fire containment give more constructive forms of limitation. Witte’s earth eruption and earthquake are separate source associations without a supplied link to those public tasks. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
