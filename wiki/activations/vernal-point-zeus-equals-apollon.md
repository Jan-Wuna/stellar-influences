## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 50 (one-based).

Inevitably spreading. Expansion. Natural science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 49; PDF page: 23 (one-based spread).

Successful public leadership, spread of fire in public, free shaping of the public sphere, public scientific efforts, trade efforts in public, and extensive public goals.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public activity expands through leadership, science and trade.
- psychology: Direction toward extensive goals.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Successful public leadership.
- events/manifestations: Scientific and commercial efforts, expansion and spreading fire.
- conflicts/notes: Witte’s “inevitably” belongs to his wording, not a guaranteed forecast.

## Contradictions

Both emphasize expansion and science. ABC adds successful leadership and trade, alongside a separate spread-of-fire example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Zeus = Apollon` extends directed public activity into a wider field. Witte names expansion and natural science; ABC supplies successful leadership, scientific and trade efforts, and extensive public goals. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Free shaping of the public sphere is a constructive expression of this scope. Spreading fire is another explicit branch, which should not be made the necessary result of public success or expansion. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
