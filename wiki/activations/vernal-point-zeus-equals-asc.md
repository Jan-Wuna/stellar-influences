## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 50 (one-based).

Acquaintance with people who have the authority to command.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 48; PDF page: 23 (one-based spread).

Others shape the surroundings, a leading place in public, a creative partner in public life, a partner’s achievements in public, shaping a public place, public environmental achievements, and public fire in the place.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public direction is encountered through others or a place.
- psychology: No distinct psychological state is specified.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: People with command authority and creative partners.
- events/manifestations: Partner achievements, shaping places and local public fire.
- conflicts/notes: The commanding and creative examples need not concern the same person.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes commanding people. ABC also gives creative partners and the shaping of surroundings, broadening the interpersonal field.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Zeus = Asc` places public direction in the people and surroundings one meets. Witte gives acquaintance with people authorized to command; ABC adds creative partners and their achievements, with others shaping the environment. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Shaping a public place and environmental achievements provide concrete local expressions. Public fire in the place remains a separate source example rather than a necessary consequence of another person’s leadership. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
