## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 50 (one-based).

A general increase in the number of marriages. An organization with a goal such as trade-unions or political parties.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 49; PDF page: 23 (one-based spread).

Joint efforts in public, a shared direction in public life, common public goals, a leading public community, artistic shaping of the public sphere, and a public community of achievement.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Organized public effort toward shared goals.
- psychology: A shared direction and purpose.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Goal-oriented associations, trade unions and political parties.
- events/manifestations: Communal achievements, artistic public shaping and an increase in marriages.
- conflicts/notes: The marriage increase is Witte’s separate collective association.

## Contradictions

The sources agree on purposeful organization. Witte also names increasing marriages; ABC adds artistic and achievement-oriented communities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Zeus = Cupido` organizes public effort around a shared direction. Witte’s goal-oriented associations, trade unions and political parties meet ABC’s common goals, joint efforts and leading communities. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Communities of achievement and artistic shaping of the public sphere give practical expressions of that coordination. Witte’s general increase in marriages remains a separate collective association, not a necessary result of organized public work. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
