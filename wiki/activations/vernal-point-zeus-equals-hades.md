## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 50 (one-based).

Meanness and dirty tricks prevent one from reaching a goal. Devastation by open fires, e.g. forest fires. Fires started by lightning.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 49; PDF page: 23 (one-based spread).

fire criminality in public, leadership in public service, dangerous goals in public, criminal leadership in public life, deficient efforts in public, and public achievements from the past

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Public goals or achievements meet damaging interference.
- psychology: Witte gives meanness and dirty tricks as historical judgments about obstruction.
- body/health: No specific bodily or health condition is named.
- social/relationship: Criminal leadership and leadership in public service are distinct examples.
- events/manifestations: Dangerous goals, deficient efforts, past achievements and devastating fire.
- conflicts/notes: Public-service leadership is not inherently criminal; the source lists it separately.

## Contradictions

Both include damage around goals and fire. ABC also names public-service leadership and achievements from the past, which cannot be reduced to wrongdoing.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vernal Point/Zeus = Hades` includes public effort obstructed or made harmful. Witte describes dirty tricks preventing a goal; ABC gives dangerous goals, criminal leadership and deficient efforts. Their fire branch includes fire-related crime, open-fire devastation and lightning fires. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC separately names leadership in public service and achievements from the past. Those examples should retain their own scope without implying that public service or inherited accomplishments are criminal. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
