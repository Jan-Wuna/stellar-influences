## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Laws. One who follows orders. To learn the manipulation of motors. A general urge for mental or spiritual work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

leadership talks in public, talks with the public leadership, youth leadership in public, achievements of youth in public, news about public achievements, public traffic goals, and language achievements in public

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Mental, verbal, and instructional activity connected with public direction and achievement.
- Psychology: An urge for mental or spiritual work and a disposition to follow orders are stated.
- Body/health: No health condition is described.
- Social/relationship: Public leadership conversations, youth leadership, and communication about public achievements are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Laws, motor-operation training, public traffic goals, news, and language achievements are distinct examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Learning motors is a specific technical application; it does not turn all the communication examples into machinery work.

## Contradictions

Following orders and discussing leadership describe different positions within organized activity. These entries establish no direct contradiction and do not establish independent corroboration for every example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Zeus = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the pair through the mental and communicative work that directs public activity: conversations about leadership, news of achievements, youth leadership, and language performance. Laws, obedience to orders, and learning to operate motors give the same orientation an organizational or technical expression. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 50) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 48; PDF p. 23) support this range; neither makes every example a necessary part of one event.
