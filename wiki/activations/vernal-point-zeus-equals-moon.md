## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Females who are eager to accomplish great feats.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Efforts of a people in public, a leading woman in public, shaping public life with feeling, shaping public life by a woman, public objectives of a people, and a creative woman in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public creative achievement described through women or the efforts of a people.
- Psychology: Eagerness for accomplishment belongs to Witte's explicitly gendered example.
- Body/health: No medical claim is supplied.
- Social/relationship: A woman shaping public life and a people's public objectives are the named social settings.
- Events/manifestations: Public leadership, creative work, and collective efforts are examples, without a specific predicted event.
- Conflicts/notes: The historical female attribution stays attributed; the repaired ABC feeling phrase does not promise fortunate results.

## Contradictions

The collective and individual examples broaden the field of public achievement rather than contradict each other. The ABC phrase formerly rendered "felicitous public design" was checked against printed p. 48 (PDF p. 23) and corrected to shaping public life with feeling.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Zeus = Moon`, Moon specifies the pair through public accomplishment associated, in these historical entries, with women and with the efforts or objectives of a people. Witte's eager achiever and ABC's publicly creative or leading woman connect personal accomplishment with shaping public life; ABC also gives that shaping an explicitly feelingful quality. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 50) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 48; PDF p. 23) support these as attributed examples, not universal statements about women or promises of success.
