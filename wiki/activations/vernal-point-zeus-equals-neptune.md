#### Witte Entry

Rejecting a compulsion. Wasted energies. Heat vapors. Combustible gas. Fire fighting institutions.

#### ABC Entry

efforts for public waters, disappointing public leadership, unknown leadership in public life, unclear goals in public, uncertain public leadership, and weak public performance

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public effort whose direction or effectiveness is uncertain, alongside literal vapor, gas, and water applications.
- Psychology: Rejecting compulsion and disappointment are named; no comprehensive character profile follows.
- Body/health: Heat vapor and combustible gas are physical examples, not medical diagnoses.
- Social/relationship: Uncertain or disappointing public leadership and unclear public goals are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Wasted effort, firefighting institutions, and work concerning public waters are distinct manifestations.
- Conflicts/notes: The practical water and fire examples should not be reduced to a judgment that all public work fails.

## Contradictions

The entries combine uncertain performance with concrete institutions and materials. This variety does not establish a contradiction or make the unfavorable examples inevitable.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Zeus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair as effort whose direction, leadership, or effective result can be difficult to establish: goals are unclear, leadership uncertain or disappointing, and energy may be wasted. A separate literal strand concerns heat vapor, combustible gas, firefighting, and public waters. Both strands are preserved in the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 50) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 49; PDF p. 23); the material examples do not by themselves imply failed leadership.
