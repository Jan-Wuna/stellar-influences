## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Natural selection. To produce something beautiful for the general public.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Beginning love in public, fiery love in public life, celebratory fire in public, peace efforts in public, artistic creation for the public, and striving for harmony in public life.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public creation of beauty and publicly expressed love or peace efforts.
- Psychology: Fiery love and efforts toward harmony are named, without a general personality claim.
- Body/health: "Natural selection" is an unexplained source phrase; no medical or biological mechanism is established.
- Social/relationship: Beginning love in public and public peace efforts are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Producing something beautiful for the public, artistic creation, and celebratory fire are concrete applications.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's art, celebratory fire, and harmony wording was repaired against the German scan.

## Contradictions

The beauty and love examples can coexist with peace efforts. Witte's "Natural selection" remains bounded by its unexplained wording. ABC's former "artificial design", "joy fire", and "harmony struggles" were corrected against printed p. 48 (PDF p. 23).

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Zeus = Venus`, Venus specifies the pair through creating beauty for public enjoyment and expressing love, peace efforts, or a wish for harmony in public life. Artistic creation and celebratory fire give that public expression a concrete form. This connects the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 50) with the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 48; PDF p. 23), without expanding Witte's unexplained "Natural selection" into a biological doctrine.
