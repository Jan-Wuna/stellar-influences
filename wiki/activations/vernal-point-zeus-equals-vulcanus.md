## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Great natural forces or machine forces. To utilize natural forces.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Emerging public force, fire power in public, leadership influence in public life, feats of strength in public, capacity for achievement in public, public striving for power, and striving for public power.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: The use of great natural or mechanical force, with public power among the ABC themes.
- Psychology: Aspiration for public power is explicit; motives for that aspiration are not supplied.
- Body/health: Force and fire are physical examples, not diagnoses or claims of bodily strength.
- Social/relationship: Public leadership influence and striving for public power are named; these do not establish violent action.
- Events/manifestations: Harnessing natural forces, machine forces, and fire power are concrete applications.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's leadership and striving wording was repaired from the scan; German Gewalt can denote force or violence, so the emerging-force phrase remains broad.

## Contradictions

Harnessing force and aspiring to public power are different scales of application. ABC's "Fire influence/3" and "Public Power strikes" were corrected against printed p. 49 (PDF p. 23) to leadership influence and striving for power. The broader force/violence range of Gewalt is not narrowed to an inevitable violent event.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vernal Point/Zeus = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the pair through the use of substantial force: natural forces and machinery are harnessed, while public work can display fire power, feats of strength, leadership influence, or striving for power. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 50) supports the material expression, and the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 49; PDF p. 23) supplies the public setting. Public force and ambition do not by themselves establish violent action.
