## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

To enjoy esteem and respect in a small circle.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 479; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

Blocked mental energy, depression through mental violence, curtailment of mental violence. Condensing mental energies, thorough influence of an idea, thorough cultural influence, reasonable consolidation of power, and resistance against mental violence.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Concentrated mental influence and respect within a small circle, with blockage or resistance as additional possibilities.
- Psychology: ABC names blocked energy and depression through mental violence; these remain historical source claims.
- Body/health: No diagnostic inference follows from the historical depression wording.
- Social/relationship: Esteem in a small circle and cultural influence are named; resistance to mental violence is also explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Consolidating power, concentrating mental energies, and curtailing coercive influence are named applications.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's corrupt thoroughness and reasonable-consolidation phrases were repaired from the German scan.

## Contradictions

Esteem, consolidation, blockage, and resistance are different possible expressions, not a promise that respect requires depression or coercion. The ABC translation repair was checked at printed p. 479 (PDF p. 238).

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies the pair through concentrated or restricted influence. Respect may be established within a small circle, while mental energies are condensed, an idea or culture exerts a thorough influence, and power is consolidated; a difficult expression is blockage or resistance to mental violence. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 315) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 479; PDF p. 238) support those distinct possibilities. ABC's depression language remains attributed and does not establish a diagnosis or a necessary result of concentration.
