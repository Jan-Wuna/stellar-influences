## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

Considered by many as a person of honour and esteem. Success and honour, because one enjoys high esteem.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 479; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

expansion of a spiritual influence, influence of spiritual freedom, successful influence of an idea, successful cultural influence, extensive spiritual energy, increase of spiritual energy, and reasonable experiences of power

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Expanding intellectual or spiritual influence and the esteem that can accompany it.
- Psychology: Spiritual freedom and extensive mental energy are named; no fixed temperament is supplied.
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Being held in esteem by many and successfully influencing culture are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: Expansion of influence, successful ideas, and honor arising through esteem are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's corrupted opening was restored to "Considered" after checking the scan.

## Contradictions

ABC's expansion of influence and Witte's esteem-based success are compatible emphases. Their overlap does not establish independent corroboration or guarantee that influence brings honor.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Apollon`, Apollon specifies the pair as intellectual or spiritual influence extending in reach and finding successful cultural expression. Witte connects honor and success with being esteemed by many, while ABC emphasizes expanding influence, freedom, and the successful effect of an idea. Together the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 315) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 479; PDF p. 238) support a reading of influence gaining breadth and recognition, without making that recognition inevitable.
