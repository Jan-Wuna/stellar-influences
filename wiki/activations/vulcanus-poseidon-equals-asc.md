## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

Mutual mighty mental influences. To impress others or to be impressed.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 478; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

influential cultural place, influential environmental insights, power of insight of others, spiritual energy of the partner, spiritual force of a place, cultural influence of a place, influence the culture of the environment, and reasonable influence of the partner

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Strong intellectual or spiritual influence exchanged with other people and one's surroundings.
- Psychology: Impressing others and being impressed are both explicit, preserving the reciprocal direction.
- Body/health: No medical condition is stated.
- Social/relationship: The partner's insight and spiritual energy are named, alongside mutual mental influence.
- Events/manifestations: A culturally influential place, insights from the environment, and influencing local culture are concrete settings.
- Conflicts/notes: Influence can be received as well as exercised; the entry does not establish dominance by one party.

## Contradictions

Mutual influence and the influence of a place are complementary applications. Neither proves that the partner and the environment exert the same influence in a given case.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Asc`, Asc specifies the pair through encounters with other people and places: one can make a strong mental impression, receive such an impression, or participate in an influential cultural environment. The partner's insight and spiritual energy place the emphasis on exchanged influence rather than an isolated powerful mind. See the [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 315) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 478; PDF p. 238). The passages establish reciprocity and setting, not a necessary hierarchy between people.
