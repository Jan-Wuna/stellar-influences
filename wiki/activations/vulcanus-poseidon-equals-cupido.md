## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

The honor of a community, family, or social stratum. The pride and self-esteem of a class of people.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 479; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

Influential spiritual community, influence of a community through clarity, insight of an energy community, violence of an idea community, power of spiritual synthesis, powerful spiritual community, and strong cultural community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared honor, self-esteem, and intellectual or spiritual influence within a community.
- Psychology: Collective pride and self-esteem are explicit, rather than an individual personality description.
- Body/health: No health claim is supplied.
- Social/relationship: Family, community, and social stratum are named; ABC adds spiritual and cultural communities.
- Events/manifestations: A community influencing through clarity and the power of spiritual synthesis are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The scan supports "Influential", repairing "Influenced". ABC's Gewalt wording has a force/violence range and does not make every community coercive.

## Contradictions

Collective dignity and collective force are different possibilities. The unfavorable force/violence wording is retained alongside the positive community examples rather than absorbed into a wholly favorable reading.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies the pair through a group's shared dignity and influence. Family, community, or social class can carry pride and self-esteem, while a spiritual or cultural community can influence through clarity and shared synthesis. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 315) and [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 479; PDF p. 238) support this collective expression; ABC also preserves a potentially coercive use of communal force, so cohesion is not automatically benign.
