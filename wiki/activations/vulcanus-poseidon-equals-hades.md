## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

Lack of self-confidence, pride, honor and dignity. That which is despised, undignified or dishonorable.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 479; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

Influential idea from the past, dangerous mental violence, criminal mental community, cultural influence from the past, lack of spiritual energy, adverse cultural influence, strong cultural crime, and hidden spiritual power

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Diminished dignity or mental energy, with concealed, adverse, or inherited cultural influence among the examples.
- Psychology: Lack of self-confidence and pride is explicit; it is an attributed description, not a diagnosis.
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a medical condition.
- Social/relationship: Being despised and involvement with a criminal mental community are source examples, not judgments about a person from the formula alone.
- Events/manifestations: Ideas and cultural influence from the past, hidden spiritual power, and harmful cultural influence are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Past influence is not inherently criminal; the entry lists these separately.

## Contradictions

The loss-of-dignity emphasis and the hidden/past-influence emphasis are distinct rather than directly contradictory. ABC's dangerous and criminal examples must not be extended to every inherited idea or private influence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Hades`, Hades specifies the pair through impaired dignity or depleted mental energy and through forms of influence that may be hidden, inherited from the past, or adverse. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes lack of self-confidence and social esteem (PDF p. 315); the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) adds hidden power and potentially harmful cultural or mental influence (printed p. 479; PDF p. 238). The past-oriented examples remain distinct from the criminal ones: age or concealment alone does not establish harmful intent.
