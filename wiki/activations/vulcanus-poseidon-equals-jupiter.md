## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

Happiness and joy over honour and dignity that one possesses. Happiness and joy at the prestige one enjoys.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 478; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

influential financial idea, influential money culture, successful cultural influence, insights into the power of money, power through legal clarity, strong spiritual success, reasonable legal influence, truth through legal power, and contented cultural power

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Satisfaction in dignity and prestige, with successful cultural, financial, or legal influence as applications.
- Psychology: Happiness and joy in one's honor or standing are explicit.
- Body/health: No medical claim is given.
- Social/relationship: Prestige, cultural influence, and the power of money or law define social settings, without an intimate relationship claim.
- Events/manifestations: Influential financial ideas, legal clarity, and successful spiritual or cultural activity are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Legal clarity and prestige are source motifs, not proof of legal correctness or moral superiority.

## Contradictions

Personal joy in esteem and institutional forms of influence are compatible but not identical. The entries do not establish that money, law, and honor necessarily accompany one another.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the pair through successful or satisfying influence: honor and dignity are enjoyed, and ideas can become effective in culture, finance, or law. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) supplies the joy in prestige (PDF p. 315), while the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) names financial ideas, insight into money's power, and influence through legal clarity (printed p. 478; PDF p. 238). These are related expressions of valued influence, not a guarantee of wealth, favorable legal outcomes, or deserved status.
