## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

Great self-confidence. Great honor and esteem. To be conscious of one’s own value. Dignitaries.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 479; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

Special power of truth, influence of the spiritual hierarchy, a dominant influential idea, great spiritual energy, state cultural influence, strong intellectual authority, superior spiritual power, reasonable influence of the state, and important cultural influence.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Conscious personal worth and high esteem, expressed through intellectual, spiritual, or state authority.
- Psychology: Great self-confidence and awareness of one's own value are explicit.
- Body/health: No medical condition is given.
- Social/relationship: Dignitaries, spiritual hierarchy, intellectual authority, and the state's cultural influence are named.
- Events/manifestations: A dominant idea and influential cultural or state activity are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: The German scan supports superior power, not "illegitimate" power, and reasonable rather than merely powerful state influence.

## Contradictions

Individual esteem and institutional authority are different scales, not a contradiction. The ABC English was repaired against printed p. 479 (PDF p. 238); the original translation's illegitimacy claim is unsupported by that passage.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the pair through elevated standing and authoritative influence. Awareness of one's worth and the esteem accorded to dignitaries connect with intellectual authority, spiritual hierarchy, and state cultural influence. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) (PDF p. 315) and scan-checked [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) (printed p. 479; PDF p. 238) support these forms of rank and influence; they do not establish that authority is morally right or that high standing follows automatically from confidence.
