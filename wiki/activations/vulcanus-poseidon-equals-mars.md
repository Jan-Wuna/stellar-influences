## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

Tremendous mental activity. Bold, honorable and dignified action. To act as a gentleman.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 478; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

efforts toward cultural influence, influential impulses of ideas, influential cultural creation, influence of mental activity, tremendous spiritual activities, powerful cultural activity, strong efforts toward clarity, and strong spiritual impulses

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Vigorous mental activity expressed through action, cultural creation, and efforts toward clarity.
- Psychology: Boldness and strong intellectual or spiritual impulses are explicit.
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a medical claim or equates mental activity with bodily stamina.
- Social/relationship: Witte describes honorable, dignified conduct; ABC names efforts to exert cultural influence.
- Events/manifestations: Influential creative work, strong mental activity, and pursuing clarity are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's "gentleman" is historical gendered wording for its conduct example, not a sex-specific rule.

## Contradictions

Witte's ethical description of action and ABC's energetic cultural work differ in emphasis. No passage establishes that vigorous cultural activity is necessarily honorable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Mars`, Mars specifies the pair through ideas becoming vigorous activity: strong mental impulses are put into cultural creation, influence, and efforts toward clarity. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) also characterizes action as bold, honorable, and dignified (PDF p. 315), while the [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) emphasizes the energy and effect of the work (printed p. 478; PDF p. 238). Those ethical qualities remain attributed examples rather than conclusions that can be inferred from intensity alone.
