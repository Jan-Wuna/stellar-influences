## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

Degradation. Without respect or esteem. Disrespect or threat to prestige and honor. Degradation of respect and dignity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 479; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

insights into a future energy, subtle feeling through strong spiritualization, subtle spiritual energy, foreign spiritual influence, force of clear water, weak influence of an idea, weak cultural influence, and refined spiritual energy

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Weakened esteem or cultural influence, with subtle and refined spiritual experience as a separate ABC emphasis.
- Psychology: Subtle feeling through spiritualization is named; humiliation is not supplied as a universal response.
- Body/health: The force of clear water is a literal example, not a medical claim.
- Social/relationship: Disrespect and threatened prestige appear in Witte; ABC names foreign spiritual influence and weak cultural influence.
- Events/manifestations: Diminished standing, weak influence of an idea, refined spiritual energy, and insight into a future energy are examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Three development clauses belonging to Pluto were removed after checking the original table boundary.

## Contradictions

Witte's degradation emphasis is not equivalent to ABC's subtle or refined spiritual energy; only the weakening-of-influence theme overlaps. The German scan at printed p. 479 (PDF p. 238) confirms that "unfolding of spiritual force", "development of influential ideas", and "influential spiritual development" belong to the Pluto row, not this one.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Vulcanus/Poseidon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the pair through influence or esteem that may weaken: an idea has little effect, cultural influence declines, or prestige and dignity are threatened. The [Witte Entry](#witte-entry) makes the loss of respect explicit (PDF p. 315). The [ABC Entry](#abc-entry) also offers a distinct, less negative strand of subtle feeling, refined spiritual energy, foreign influence, and clear water (printed p. 479; PDF p. 238). Those meanings are retained without treating refinement as degradation or borrowing the adjacent Pluto development clauses.
