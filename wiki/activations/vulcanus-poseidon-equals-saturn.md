## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

Humiliation, to be depreciated. Insulted sense of honor. Loss of prestige and dignity. Honor, prestige and dignity must be very carefully guarded. To overcome inhibitions on a spiritual level or through spiritual forces.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 479; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

Spiritual strength in old age, tremendous mental or spiritual problems, inhibitions through mental or spiritual violence, concentration on mental or spiritual energies, power through patience and reason, major cultural tasks, and serious problems with truth.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Dignity or mental influence encounters difficulty and demands careful preservation.
- psychology: Humiliation, inhibition, concentration, patience and reason.
- body/health: Strength in old age is an attributed source association, not a health forecast.
- social/relationship: Threatened prestige and an injured sense of honor.
- events/manifestations: Major cultural tasks and efforts to overcome inhibition.
- conflicts/notes: The source gives reason, not courage, as a companion to patience.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes loss of dignity and overcoming inhibition. ABC adds concentrated energy and patience with reason, preserving an active response alongside difficulty.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Poseidon = Saturn` places dignity and mental influence under strain. Witte gives humiliation, injured honor and lost prestige; ABC adds inhibition, major mental or spiritual problems and demanding cultural tasks. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Carefully guarding dignity, concentrating energy and gaining power through patience and reason describe the response to these difficulties. Strength in old age and overcoming inhibition remain attributed possibilities rather than promised outcomes. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
