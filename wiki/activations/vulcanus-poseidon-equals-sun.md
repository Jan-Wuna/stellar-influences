## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

The strong aura. Days on which one is under the influence of a great idea. The man who exercises great mental influence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 478; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

energy of body and spirit, influential insight in the day, insight into bodily energy, influential idea of a man, spiritual energy of a person, spiritual center of power, personal cultural influence, and reasonable personal influence

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal presence and mental influence centered on an idea.
- psychology: Being strongly affected by an idea or insight.
- body/health: ABC names body-and-spirit energy without a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: A person exercising substantial mental or cultural influence.
- events/manifestations: Influential insights, personal cultural effects and a spiritual center of power.
- conflicts/notes: Witte’s aura is attributed spiritual vocabulary, not a verified physical field.

## Contradictions

Both emphasize personal mental influence. ABC adds body-and-spirit energy and insight; Witte adds the source term aura.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Poseidon = Sun` makes the influence of an idea personal. Witte describes days under the sway of a great idea and a person exercising mental influence; ABC develops influential insight, personal cultural influence and reasonable personal influence. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC’s body-and-spirit energy and Witte’s strong aura belong to their spiritual vocabulary. They support the source’s theme of a forceful personal presence without establishing a physiological mechanism. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
