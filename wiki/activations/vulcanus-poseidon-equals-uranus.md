## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

To exercise a surprising mental influence. Suddenly come to dignity, honor or esteem.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 479; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

Excitement about mental force or violence, an influential cultural event, insightful energy reform, mental energy impulses, mental reform strength, tremendous mental tensions, new cultural influence, strong mental dynamics, and reasonable energy technology.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Sudden mental influence or recognition.
- psychology: Strong impulses, excitement and mental tension.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Unexpected dignity, honor or esteem.
- events/manifestations: Cultural events, new influence, reform and reasonable energy technology.
- conflicts/notes: Forceful or coercive influence is separate from favorable recognition.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses surprising influence and esteem. ABC adds cultural reform and tension, including concern about mental violence; honor is not guaranteed by intensity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Poseidon = Uranus` gives mental or cultural influence a sudden, dynamic expression. Witte names surprising influence and abrupt recognition; ABC supplies cultural events, new influence, reform impulses and strong mental tension. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also includes excitement about mental force or violence and reasonable energy technology. These remain different applications, so a sudden influence need not be honorable, coercive or technical in every case. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
