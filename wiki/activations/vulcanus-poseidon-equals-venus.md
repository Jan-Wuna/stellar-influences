## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

Faith, decency, honor, dignity and moral values from a philosophical point of view.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 478; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

Influence of a peaceful culture, influence of arts and culture, influential peace idea, spiritual influence of love, spiritual peace energy, harmony with a spiritual power, strong through love of truth, and 'preference for the power of truth

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Values, peace and love shape mental or cultural influence.
- psychology: Love of truth and harmony with a spiritual power.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Peaceful culture and philosophical ideas of honor or decency.
- events/manifestations: Artistic influence and influential peace ideas.
- conflicts/notes: Moral terms describe the sources’ values without certifying anyone’s conduct.

## Contradictions

The sources agree on values and constructive influence. Witte frames them philosophically; ABC develops peace, art, culture and love of truth.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Poseidon = Venus` connects mental influence with values and harmony. Witte gives a philosophical concern with faith, decency, honor and dignity; ABC shows peace ideas, art and culture carrying a related influence. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Love’s spiritual influence and strength through love of truth give the inner emphasis. These are attributed value orientations rather than proof that a particular influential person embodies them. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
