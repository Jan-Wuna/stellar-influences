## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

The general elevated state of mind, ruling ideas, affecting everybody.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 478; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

influential idea in public, spiritual energy in public life, spiritual force in public, cultural influence in public, reasonable influence in public, and important influence from the public

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Ideas and mental or cultural influence reach the general public.
- psychology: A shared elevated state of mind and ruling ideas.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Influence can come from the public as well as act upon it.
- events/manifestations: Public cultural effects and important public ideas.
- conflicts/notes: Witte’s “everybody” is his broad formulation, not evidence of universal agreement.

## Contradictions

The entries share public mental influence. Witte emphasizes ruling ideas, while ABC also explicitly allows influence received from the public.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Poseidon = Vernal Point` gives ideas and mental strength a public reach. Witte describes an elevated general state of mind and ruling ideas; ABC supplies influential public ideas, cultural influence and reasonable influence in public life. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

ABC also names important influence coming from the public, preserving a reciprocal direction. The broad collective language does not establish that every person shares the same idea or response. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
