## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 315 (one-based).

To practice self-control and discipline. To show pride, honor, dignity. To be an example of pride, honor, dignity. Military pride.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 479; PDF page: 238 (one-based spread).

Influence of intellectual or spiritual leadership, influence of a creative idea, generation of mental force or violence, leading cultural power, generating mental or spiritual energies, clear energy goals, clear leadership influence, forceful testimony to truth, and intellectual or spiritual leadership power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental or cultural power is directed through leadership and discipline.
- psychology: Self-control, pride and clear goals.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Exemplary honor, leadership and military pride in Witte.
- events/manifestations: Creative ideas, cultural leadership and forceful testimony to truth.
- conflicts/notes: Discipline and proclaimed truth do not certify moral correctness; ABC also allows coercive force.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes self-control and honor. ABC emphasizes leadership and creative influence, with a mental-force or violence branch that keeps the reading from being wholly favorable.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Poseidon = Zeus` directs mental or cultural power toward a clear goal. Witte gives self-control and discipline; ABC develops creative ideas, cultural leadership, clear leadership influence and forceful testimony to truth. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Witte’s exemplary honor and military pride remain attributed conduct examples. ABC also names generating mental force or violence, so clarity and discipline should not be assumed to make every use of influence benign. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
