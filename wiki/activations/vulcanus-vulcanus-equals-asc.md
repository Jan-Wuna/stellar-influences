## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 313 (one-based).

Others are superior. To experience force from others. Powerful, strong or forceful personalities in the close environment. Others act violently. Surroundings occupied by great energies or powerful influences, whether natural or biological.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong influence or force is encountered in the close environment.
- psychology: The entry concerns outside pressure rather than a fixed inner trait.
- body/health: Natural or biological influence is named without a particular medical condition.
- social/relationship: Powerful people, others’ superiority and violent actions by others.
- events/manifestations: Surroundings occupied by great energies.
- conflicts/notes: One source only; strength and experienced violence are distinct degrees of force.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is present. Powerful surroundings need not always involve the separate violent-action example.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Vulcanus = Asc` places strong force in the people and surroundings one encounters. Witte describes forceful personalities, others’ superiority and receiving force from others, extending the setting to natural or biological influences. Violence by others is a distinct adverse branch within that broader field of powerful surroundings. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
