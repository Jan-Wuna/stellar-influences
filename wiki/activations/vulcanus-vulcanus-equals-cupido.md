#### Witte Entry

Mighty artistic abilities and talent. Power and influence in or through a community, an association, or also of a political party. Great organization. The powerful community. Combined or collective power. Influence of a kind of art. Might through unity. Great art.

## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Vulcanus = Cupido` gathers power through unity. Witte’s associations, political parties and large organizations show how combined strength can become influential, while great artistic talent and the influence of an art form give a cultural expression. The shared theme is capacity enlarged through a community or artistic medium. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
