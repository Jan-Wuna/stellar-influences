#### Witte Entry

Optimism. Confidence. Huge luck. Success by force. The power of money and finances. Very influential financial power. Benefice energy. Benevolent power. Successful power. The great optimist. Excellent credit.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Successful or benevolent power backed by financial resources.
- psychology: Optimism and confidence.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: Influential financial power and excellent credit.
- events/manifestations: Luck, success by force and the power of money.
- conflicts/notes: One source only; confidence and resources do not guarantee success.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is present. Benevolent power and success by force are different emphases within the entry.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Vulcanus = Jupiter` connects strength with confidence and favorable results. Witte gives optimism, luck and successful or benevolent power, then makes the material setting concrete through money, financial influence and excellent credit. Success by force is also explicit, so favorable results should not be taken as proof that every use of power is benevolent. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
