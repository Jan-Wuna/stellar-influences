## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Vulcanus = Kronos` concentrates force in a commanding or authoritative position. Witte’s top authority, referee and statesman show different roles for decision-making power; supreme executive authority and a great nation extend the scale to the state. The separate despot example retains the possibility of oppressive authority without defining every powerful office that way. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
