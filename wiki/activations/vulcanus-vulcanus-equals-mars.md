#### Witte Entry

Great effort. Exercise. Mighty activity. Great power. Acts of violence. Mechanical energies. Unleash power. Energetic forces in action. Muscular strength. Working in the field of high energies.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Great energy released in action or work.
- psychology: Effort and energetic activity.
- body/health: Exercise and muscular strength are explicit without a medical conclusion.
- social/relationship: Acts of violence form the adverse social branch.
- events/manifestations: Mechanical energy and work in high-energy fields.
- conflicts/notes: One source only; strenuous work and violence are distinct uses of force.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is present. Its bodily, mechanical and violent examples share active force but have different consequences.

## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Vulcanus = Mars` puts substantial force into action. Great effort, exercise and muscular strength describe bodily application, while mechanical energies and work in high-energy fields give technical settings. Witte also names violent acts as a separate adverse use of released power; intensity alone does not identify which expression occurs. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
