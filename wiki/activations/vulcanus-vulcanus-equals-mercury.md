## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 313 (one-based).

Mighty exerted thinking. The force of thought. The power of thoughts. Great speed. Mighty and fast motion. Sports that require speed. The dominant mind. Strong opinion. Very important news, ideas, plans or messages. Forceful words.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Forceful thought, communication and rapid movement.
- psychology: Strong opinions, exerted thinking and a dominant mind.
- body/health: Speed-based sport is explicit without a medical claim.
- social/relationship: Forceful words and consequential messages.
- events/manifestations: Important news, plans, ideas and fast motion.
- conflicts/notes: One source only; mental force and physical speed are parallel examples.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is present. Thought, communication and speed share intensity but need not occur together.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Vulcanus = Mercury` gives thought and communication considerable force. Witte connects exerted thinking and strong opinion with important ideas, plans, news and forceful words. Fast movement and sports requiring speed provide a separate bodily or practical expression of intensity, without making physical speed evidence of intellectual dominance. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
