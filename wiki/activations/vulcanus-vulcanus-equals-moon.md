## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Collective or women’s influence and conscious power.
- psychology: Mighty feelings, a powerful mind and awareness of power.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: Influential women, public figures and the power of a people.
- events/manifestations: Public sensation, executive power and national might.
- conflicts/notes: One source only; gendered and national superiority are historical descriptions, not general facts.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is present. The mental, gendered and national examples are different scales of influence.

## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Vulcanus = Moon` connects strong feeling and awareness of power with public influence. Witte gives influential women, commanding public figures and a powerful people or nation, alongside causing a sensation. His language of superiority remains historical attribution rather than a factual ranking of women or peoples. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
