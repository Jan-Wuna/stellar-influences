## Contradictions

## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Vulcanus = Node` gathers force in a connection. Witte’s stable tie and influential association describe a bond with weight and persistence, while eagerness and applied force show active involvement in it. The commanding-relationship example retains a power dimension without establishing that all strong ties are either coercive or equal. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
