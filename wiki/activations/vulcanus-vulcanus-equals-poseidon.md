## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 314 (one-based).

State of mind. Sense of honor. Self-esteem. Self-confidence. Pride. Dignity. Arrogance. The mental influence, power and forces. The powerful mind. The spiritual power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Mental power and a strong sense of personal worth.
- psychology: Self-esteem, confidence, pride and arrogance.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: Honor, dignity and mental influence.
- events/manifestations: Intellectual or spiritual power expressed through a strong mind.
- conflicts/notes: One source only; confidence and arrogance preserve favorable and adverse possibilities.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is present. Dignity and arrogance are different expressions of a strong self-concept.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Vulcanus = Poseidon` places power in the mind and in one’s sense of worth. Witte links mental influence with honor, dignity, self-esteem and confidence, while also naming pride and arrogance. The same strong self-concept can therefore have different social qualities; mental force alone does not establish insight or deserved esteem. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
