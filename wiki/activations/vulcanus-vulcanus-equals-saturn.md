#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 313 (one-based).

Custody. To guard, to preserve, to banish, to fortify or to fix something. Mighty hindrance. Supervision. Captivity, imprisonment, jail. Vigilance. To be on guard. Limitation of the great forces or great power. The loss of power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Power is guarded, contained, restricted or lost.
- psychology: Vigilance and being on guard.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim.
- social/relationship: Custody, supervision and captivity.
- events/manifestations: Fortification, preservation, imprisonment and loss of power.
- conflicts/notes: One source only; protective containment and coercive confinement have different consequences.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is present. Guarding or preserving and imprisonment are distinct forms of limiting force.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Vulcanus = Saturn` puts strong force under limits. Witte gives guarding, preservation and supervision as protective or administrative forms, while captivity, imprisonment and mighty hindrance describe imposed restriction. Loss of power is another explicit possibility, so maintaining control and having control removed should remain distinct. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
