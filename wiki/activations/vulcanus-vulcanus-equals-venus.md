## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 313 (one-based).

The biological urge of man. Great power of attraction. Passion. Mighty love. Ardent desire. Bold passion. Passionate action. Longing. Overblown love.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful attraction and intense love or desire.
- psychology: Passion, longing and ardent desire.
- body/health: A biological urge is attributed without a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: Mighty love and overblown attachment.
- events/manifestations: Passionate action and bold expressions of desire.
- conflicts/notes: One source only; intensity includes excess and does not establish reciprocity.

## Contradictions

Only Witte is present. Strong love and overblown love preserve different degrees of the same intensity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Vulcanus = Venus` intensifies attraction into passion and strong desire. Witte connects mighty love and longing with bold, passionate action, while “overblown love” retains the possibility of excess. The biological-urge wording remains historical attribution and does not establish reciprocity, compatibility or a physiological mechanism. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
