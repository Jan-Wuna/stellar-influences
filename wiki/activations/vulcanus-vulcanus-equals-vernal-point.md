## Derived Synthesis

`Vulcanus/Vulcanus = Vernal Point` brings great force into the general public and earthly field. Witte’s power, might and extraordinary energies describe that broad scope, including energies of Earth itself. Public violence is an explicit adverse expression alongside natural forces, without a supplied causal link between them. ([Witte](#witte-entry))
