## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 294 (one-based).

Large changes of conditions. Many are forced to begin anew.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 455; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Blocked trade efforts, restriction of commercial achievements, experience with fiery primordial material, containing the spread of fire, deepening leadership experience, thorough scientific goal striving, and increasingly blocked leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Wide changes or commercial effort encounter restriction and renewed beginnings.
- psychology: Experience deepens through thorough goal striving.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Many people must begin anew; leadership can be increasingly blocked.
- events/manifestations: Restricted trade, scientific effort and containing fire spread.
- conflicts/notes: Witte stresses a broad restart; ABC gives both obstruction and deeper experience.

## Contradictions

Witte names large changes requiring many to restart. ABC instead details blocked work and leadership alongside containment and deepening experience; no single causal sequence is supplied.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Zeus/Admetos = Apollon` places wider activity under conditions of restriction or major change. Witte describes many people being forced to begin anew; ABC gives blocked trade efforts, restricted commercial achievements and increasingly blocked leadership. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Deepened leadership experience and thorough scientific goal striving provide a more sustained response. Containing spreading fire and experience with fiery primordial material remain specific practical examples rather than explanations of every restart. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
