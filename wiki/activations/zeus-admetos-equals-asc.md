## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

To come into changed states through others or with others, and have to solve new tasks with them. To solve problematic tasks with other people. Facing and overcoming environmental problems.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 454; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Sustained efforts with others, beginning depression of the partner, blocked local leadership, fire containment at the place, consolidated local leadership, thorough efforts of the partner, restrictions on the partner's performance, and resistance to local leadership.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 454 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: New conditions and difficult tasks shared with other people.
- Psychology: The ABC entry associates the partner with strain and depression; Witte emphasizes tackling the problem.
- Body/health: No physical condition is specified; the partner's depression is a historical source association, not a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Cooperation with others is central, alongside limits on a partner and resistance to local leadership.
- Events/manifestations: Joint problem solving, environmental difficulties, and, in ABC, local fire containment.
- Conflicts/notes: Constructive cooperation and obstructed leadership are alternatives within the entries, not a promised sequence.

## Contradictions

The scan supports local leadership and fire containment. Witte stresses solving shared problems, while ABC also records obstruction and the partner's depression; these different possibilities do not establish a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Asc`, Asc specifies the Zeus/Admetos task of beginning under difficult conditions through other people and the immediate environment. Changed circumstances create problems that have to be worked through together. The ABC examples add persistent joint effort, limits on a partner's performance, and local leadership that may become consolidated or meet resistance. Fire containment is a concrete local example, rather than a general prediction. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 454, PDF p. 226.)
