#### Witte Entry

Changes of conditions in the family as well as socially; Changes in the general social forms or manners.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 455; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

German: Blockierte Führungs-Gemeinschaft, deprimierte Leistungs-Gemeinschaft, Einschränkung gemeinsamer Anstrengungen, Festigung durch gemeinsame Zielsetzung, gemeinsame Feuer-Eindämmung, gründliche Leistungs-Gemeinschaft.

English translation: Blocked leadership community, depressed achievement community, restriction of joint efforts, consolidation through a common objective, joint fire containment, and a thorough achievement community.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 455 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Collective conditions change while common achievement is either constrained or consolidated.
- Psychology: ABC describes a depressed achievement community; neither entry establishes an individual psychological profile.
- Body/health: No bodily condition or medical mechanism is specified.
- Social/relationship: Family, social forms, collective leadership, and a shared objective provide the setting.
- Events/manifestations: Changes in family or social conditions, restricted common effort, and collective fire containment.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's social change and ABC's impeded or consolidated group effort are compatible emphases; no necessary outcome is given.

## Contradictions

The German scan distinguishes a leadership community from an achievement community. The positive and obstructed collective possibilities should remain side by side; neither entry guarantees cohesion.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Cupido`, Cupido places difficult beginnings and changing conditions in family and collective life. A group must find a workable form for its shared effort: that effort may be blocked or dispirited, but a common objective can also consolidate it. Changes in social manners and joint fire containment show the range from social organization to a concrete common task. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 455, PDF p. 226.)
