## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

To find the existing conditions repulsive and disgusting. Forced to be part of creating disgusting things.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 455; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Beginning circulatory danger, restriction of criminal creativity, containing fire danger, danger from fiery primordial material, dangerous suppression of leadership, restriction of performance through depression, and resistance to criminal leadership.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 455 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Repellent conditions and dangerous or compromised activity make the difficult situation harder to resolve.
- Psychology: Witte describes disgust at existing conditions; ABC adds depression as a limit on performance.
- Body/health: ABC's circulatory danger is a historical medical association, not a clinical conclusion.
- Social/relationship: Forced participation and resistance to criminal leadership are explicit source examples.
- Events/manifestations: Containing fire danger, hazardous fiery material, and suppression of leadership.
- Conflicts/notes: The repaired Witte wording concerns creating disgusting things; it does not identify a particular act or crime.

## Contradictions

Witte PDF p. 293 reads 'Forced to be part of creating disgusting things.' The ABC scan supports containing fire danger and suppression of leadership. Its depression and circulatory-danger phrases remain attributed historical claims.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Hades`, Hades specifies the difficult Zeus/Admetos situation through repellent conditions, compromised participation, and hazards that have to be contained. One may dislike the conditions yet be forced to help create them. The ABC examples develop a related tension between dangerous or criminal leadership and attempts to restrict or resist it; fire danger is one concrete manifestation. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 455, PDF p. 226.)
