## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

Happy and contented activity under different conditions. Satisfactory new situation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 454; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Beginning financial restrictions, blocked financial leadership, success through effort and perseverance, successful fire containment, successful resistance to the state, thorough legal efforts, and a creative circulation of money.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 454 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A new situation may become satisfactory through persistent effort despite financial restrictions.
- Psychology: Witte gives happiness and contentment in activity under changed conditions.
- Body/health: No bodily condition is specified; the circulation mentioned in ABC is explicitly of money.
- Social/relationship: Financial management, legal effort, and resistance to the state are named contexts.
- Events/manifestations: Restricted finances, success through perseverance, and successful fire containment.
- Conflicts/notes: Satisfaction and financial restriction are distinct possibilities; successful effort does not imply unrestricted resources.

## Contradictions

The German scan confirms fire containment, not fire damage. Witte's satisfactory situation and ABC's restricted finances are different possible emphases; no single financial outcome follows.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the difficult new situation through the possibility of satisfaction and success achieved by perseverance. Changed conditions can provide contented activity without removing every constraint: the ABC entry also names financial restrictions and blocked financial leadership. Legal efforts, money circulation, and successful fire containment keep this success tied to concrete work rather than effortless good fortune. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 454, PDF p. 226.)
