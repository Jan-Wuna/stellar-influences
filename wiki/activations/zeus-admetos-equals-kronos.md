### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 455; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Beginning major depressions, exceptional efforts and perseverance, blocked state leadership, a consolidated leadership hierarchy, thorough efforts by the state, self-confident consolidated leadership, and important circulatory efforts.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 455 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A large undertaking or government action reshapes conditions and demands sustained effort.
- Psychology: ABC names major depressions and self-confident leadership as different possibilities.
- Body/health: ABC's circulatory-effort phrase is a historical association without a defined medical condition.
- Social/relationship: State leadership, hierarchy, and government initiative are the explicit institutional settings.
- Events/manifestations: Starting something great, government-driven change, or blocked state leadership.
- Conflicts/notes: Consolidated hierarchy and blocked leadership are alternatives; the entry does not identify a government or policy.

## Contradictions

The scan resolves the garbled leadership wording. The entries provide no specific political event; ABC's major-depression and circulatory-effort associations remain historical and unquantified.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the difficult beginning through a major undertaking or government-led change. The task acquires institutional scale: exceptional effort and perseverance may consolidate a leadership hierarchy, while state leadership may also remain blocked. This is a statement about the burden and organization of the undertaking, with both effective and impaired leadership retained. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 294; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 455, PDF p. 226.)
