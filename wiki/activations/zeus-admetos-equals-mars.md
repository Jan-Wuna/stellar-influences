## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

start all over again to revolt against a hated force. To begin anew on a limited scope. To provide for more room, air and light through energetic action.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 454; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Strenuous sustained efforts, shaping the work cycle, beginning thorough work, blocked leadership activity, restricted work performance, thorough efforts of will, deepening in creative work, and resistance activity against leadership.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 454 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Energetic action begins work again within limits or struggles against an obstructing force.
- Psychology: Effort of will and persistence are explicit; no stable personality type is established.
- Body/health: The entries concern effort and work performance, without naming an illness.
- Social/relationship: Resistance to a hated force or to leadership is named, alongside blocked leadership activity.
- Events/manifestations: Restarting work on a limited scale and creating more room, air, and light through action.
- Conflicts/notes: The same entry includes constructive work and resistance; it does not require violence.

## Contradictions

The German scan supports leadership activity, not training. Witte's revolt and ABC's sustained work describe different forms of action; neither establishes a necessary violent event.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Mars`, Mars specifies a difficult beginning as active work: starting again, applying sustained will, and making space for the task through energetic action. Limits on performance and blocked leadership do not erase the possibility of deepening creative work. Resistance to an obstructing force is another expression of the same effort to act under constraint. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 454, PDF p. 226.)
