## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

Ready to start, willingness to begin, ability to proceed. To give something shape, image and expression. To feel a new task as a burden.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 454; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Beginning psychological suppression, limitation of my goals, restricted psychological creativity, consolidated psychological leadership, my creative cycle, my difficult start, my sustained efforts, and my beginning depressions.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 454 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: The difficult beginning becomes one's own task of giving something form.
- Psychology: Readiness and willingness coexist with burden, limited goals, and ABC's depression language.
- Body/health: No physical illness is specified; psychological suppression and depression remain the source's historical associations.
- Social/relationship: No particular relationship or institution is identified; the emphasis is explicitly personal.
- Events/manifestations: Beginning a new task, proceeding with it, and giving it shape, image, and expression.
- Conflicts/notes: Willingness to proceed and feeling burdened may coexist; the entry does not establish incapacity.

## Contradictions

The German scan reads beginning psychological suppression and sustained efforts, rather than the garbled English opening. Depression is source language and does not establish a diagnosis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = MC`, MC specifies the difficult beginning as a personally felt task. There is readiness to proceed and give something a shape or expression, yet the new responsibility can feel burdensome. The ABC entry places limits on one's goals and creativity alongside sustained personal effort, preserving the tension between willingness and difficulty rather than reducing the formula to either one. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 454, PDF p. 226.)
