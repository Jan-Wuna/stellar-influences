## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

To collect sketches for a new work, to concentrate ones thoughts on them. The beginning of preparation or the beginning of finishing the work.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 454; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Taxing traffic congestion, beginning of a news blockade, reports about fiery primordial material, consolidation of leadership through discussions, discussions about beginning depressions, thorough efforts in youth, and shaping traffic circulation.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 454 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Concentrated thought and preparation give a new piece of work its initial form.
- Psychology: Attention is directed toward sketches and planning; ABC also names discussions about depression.
- Body/health: Depression occurs as a topic of discussion, not as an established condition of the subject.
- Social/relationship: Discussion can consolidate leadership; a news blockade can restrict communication.
- Events/manifestations: Collecting sketches, preparing or beginning to finish work, traffic congestion, and reports on fiery material.
- Conflicts/notes: Traffic and communication examples extend the work-planning theme without establishing a general communication failure.

## Contradictions

The scan confirms traffic congestion and leadership through discussions. ABC's depression phrase concerns conversation about it; it should not become a diagnosis in synthesis.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the difficult beginning through thought, sketches, discussion, and preparation. Concentrating on the shape of new work can be the start of either preparing it or bringing it toward completion. The ABC examples extend this to organizing traffic and communication, where congestion or a news blockade restricts flow while discussion may consolidate leadership. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 454, PDF p. 226.)
