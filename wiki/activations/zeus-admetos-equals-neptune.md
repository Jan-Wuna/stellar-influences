## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

Not a chance to start anything worth while. Slow and sneaking change of conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 455; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Effort and perseverance in water, beginning dissolution of a blockade, beginning circulatory weakness, blockage through fire-water (alcohol), creation of a water cycle, fiery liquid primordial material, and depression through unclear goals.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 455 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A worthwhile start is obstructed while change proceeds slowly or a blockage begins to dissolve.
- Psychology: ABC links depression to unclear goals; this is a historical association, not a diagnosis.
- Body/health: ABC names circulatory weakness and alcohol-related blockage without establishing a clinical mechanism.
- Social/relationship: No specific relationship is established; the stated obstacles concern starting and unclear objectives.
- Events/manifestations: Slow change of conditions, dissolution of a blockade, work in water, and a water cycle.
- Conflicts/notes: The ABC entry ends at unclear goals; subsequent development wording belongs to Pluto and is excluded.

## Contradictions

Witte PDF p. 293 supplies the missing word 'conditions'. The German scan ends Neptune at 'Depression durch unklare Zielsetzung'; the subsequent development phrases belong to Pluto and have been removed from this source subentry.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Neptune`, Neptune specifies the difficult beginning through uncertainty and weakened opportunity: the start appears unpromising while conditions change slowly. The ABC entry nevertheless allows a blockage to begin dissolving, so obstruction and release remain possible directions rather than a guaranteed failure. Its water-related examples are concrete source associations and should not be turned into a general medical or event forecast. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 455, PDF p. 226.)
