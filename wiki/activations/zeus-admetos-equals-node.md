## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

To be called upon to solve new problems. To be part of solving problems. To be involved in changed conditions. To be, in part, the cause of changed conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 454; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Combining effort with perseverance, blocked connection to leadership, restricted creativity within a connection, a firm connection among leaders, a consolidated connection to leaders, and thorough striving within a connection.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 454 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Participation or connection draws one into solving problems and changing conditions.
- Psychology: Sustained effort is named; no individual psychological profile is established.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Connections among or to leaders may be firm or blocked; collaboration can restrict creativity.
- Events/manifestations: Being called on to solve problems, contributing to change, or helping cause it.
- Conflicts/notes: Connection is not automatically supportive: involvement may bring responsibility and restriction.

## Contradictions

The scan confirms connections among and to leaders. Firm and blocked connections are retained as alternatives; they are not evidence that every association succeeds.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Node`, Node specifies difficult beginnings through involvement with others and participation in a changing situation. One may be called upon to solve a problem, help bring about the change, or share responsibility for it. The ABC examples make the quality of the connection consequential: links to leaders can be established or blocked, while effort and perseverance must be joined within the relationship. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 454, PDF p. 226.)
