## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

The beginning of a change of conditions. Things keep going. Several changes before a definite settlement.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 455; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Developing effort and perseverance, beginning a depressive development, restriction of creative development, developing thorough efforts, suppression of purposeful development, and growing resistance to leadership.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 455 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A change begins but passes through further changes before reaching a settlement.
- Psychology: Persistent effort and ABC's depressive-development phrase are named without a psychological diagnosis.
- Body/health: No physical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: ABC adds growing resistance to leadership and suppression of purposeful development.
- Events/manifestations: Continuing change, repeated revisions before settlement, and restricted creative development.
- Conflicts/notes: The scan restores ABC's missing beginning and suppression wording; development is not automatically improvement.

## Contradictions

The German scan restores the entry from 'Anstrengungen und Ausdauer entwickeln' through growing resistance to leadership. Its 'Unterdrückung' means suppression, so the earlier 'promotion' reading is excluded.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the difficult beginning as a process that continues to change before it settles. Starting is only one stage: perseverance and further effort are needed while creative or purposeful development may be restricted. The ABC example of growing resistance to leadership adds a social obstacle to the continuing process, rather than making every change a constructive advance. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 455, PDF p. 226.)
