## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 294 (one-based).

To start something new in intellectual respect. To demonstrate ones abilities and development in a test. To meet with changed conditions in the intellectual and ideological respect.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 455; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Sustained intellectual or spiritual efforts, beginning intellectual or spiritual deepening, blocked insight of leadership, consolidated intellectual or spiritual leadership, thorough cultural striving, consolidated intellectual or spiritual objectives, suppressed testimony to truth, and deepening in creative ideas.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 455 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A new intellectual or ideological task requires sustained thought and demonstrated ability.
- Psychology: Deepening in ideas and holding a clear objective are explicit; no personality type is given.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Leadership may consolidate or lack insight, while testimony to truth may be suppressed.
- Events/manifestations: Beginning intellectual work, facing changed ideological conditions, and demonstrating ability in a test.
- Conflicts/notes: The source's geistig spans intellectual and spiritual language; blocked insight is not filtration.

## Contradictions

The scan supports blocked insight of leadership, thorough cultural striving, and suppressed testimony to truth. Neither entry establishes a particular doctrine or guarantees that a test will be passed.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies the difficult beginning through ideas, intellectual work, and ideological conditions. A new task calls for sustained deepening and may require demonstrating one's ability in a test. The ABC entry adds cultural striving and clear intellectual or spiritual objectives, while also allowing leadership's insight or testimony to truth to be obstructed. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 294; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 455, PDF p. 226.)
