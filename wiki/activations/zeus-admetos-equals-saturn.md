## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

To be separated from circumstances one is accustomed to and finding the new ones unpleasant. To be in an environment where one feels hampered and depressed.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 455; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Taxing circulatory disturbance, beginning depressive disturbances, restricted powers of concentration, restriction through fire losses, thorough concentration, resistance to the state involving losses, and deepening in creative tasks.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 455 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Separation from familiar circumstances makes the new situation feel restrictive and unpleasant.
- Psychology: Feeling hampered or depressed coexists with the possibility of thorough concentration on creative tasks.
- Body/health: ABC's circulatory and depressive disturbances are historical associations, not diagnoses.
- Social/relationship: Separation and loss-bearing resistance to the state are explicit examples.
- Events/manifestations: Entering an unfamiliar environment, restricted concentration, and losses through fire.
- Conflicts/notes: Restriction through fire losses is different from preventing fire loss; the scan resolves the earlier reversal.

## Contradictions

The scan gives circulatory disturbance and restriction through fire losses. The sources' depressed language remains historical; sustained concentration is an alternative response, not a clinical remedy.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Saturn`, Saturn specifies the difficult new situation through separation from familiar conditions and a felt lack of room to proceed. The unfamiliar environment may feel unpleasant, hampering, or depressing. The ABC entry also preserves a focused response: thorough concentration and deepening in creative tasks remain possible even where performance is restricted or resistance brings losses. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 455, PDF p. 226.)
