## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

To be adaptable. Days on which the surroundings show a completely new face. The man who faces entirely new conditions and completely changed surroundings, who has to cope with new things and implements.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 454; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Blocked physical performance, restricted creativity of a person, a consolidated leader, thorough personal efforts, sustained physical efforts, a creative personal cycle, and a center for thorough achievement.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 454 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A person must adapt to entirely changed conditions and unfamiliar practical demands.
- Psychology: Adaptability is explicit, alongside limited personal creativity and sustained effort.
- Body/health: ABC names blocked physical performance and sustained bodily effort, without specifying an illness.
- Social/relationship: A consolidated leader is one ABC example; no particular relationship is established.
- Events/manifestations: Coping with new surroundings, new things, and implements through personal effort.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's 'man' is historical wording; the entry does not warrant a universal gender trait.

## Contradictions

The scan confirms thorough personal efforts and sustained physical effort. Performance limits do not establish disease, and Witte's gendered phrasing remains historical.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Sun`, Sun specifies the difficult beginning through a person's practical adaptation to a changed environment. New surroundings and unfamiliar things require direct effort, while physical performance or creativity may initially be limited. The emphasis is on coping with what is newly encountered; a consolidated leader is one ABC example, not an assurance of authority or success. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 454, PDF p. 226.)
