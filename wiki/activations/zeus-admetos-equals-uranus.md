## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

To suddenly start and finish a job. Surprising changes of circumstances and conditions.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 455; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Exciting fire blockade, consolidated new leadership, thorough technical efforts, suddenly restricted creativity, suddenly blocked leadership, surprising onset of depression, deepening in new goals, and suppressed leadership reform.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 455 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A difficult beginning or change becomes abrupt and surprising.
- Psychology: ABC names the surprising onset of depression; neither entry establishes a stable mental condition.
- Body/health: No physical illness is specified.
- Social/relationship: New leadership may consolidate, be blocked, or have its reform suppressed.
- Events/manifestations: Suddenly starting and finishing a job, technical effort, and unexpected changes of conditions.
- Conflicts/notes: Sudden completion and sudden obstruction are alternative expressions; speed is not guaranteed.

## Contradictions

The scan distinguishes consolidated new leadership, suddenly blocked leadership, and suppressed leadership reform. These are alternative outcomes; depression is an attributed source association.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the difficult beginning through a sudden shift in work or circumstances. A job may be begun and finished abruptly, but the ABC entry also allows creativity or leadership to become suddenly blocked. Technical effort and commitment to new goals give this surprise a practical setting without making every abrupt change productive. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 455, PDF p. 226.)
