## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

New problems, which are gladly tackled. Pleasant new surroundings where one falls into a soft berth.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 454; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Blocked peace efforts, the end of a passionate love, restricted efforts in love, consolidation of a passionate love, joy over fire containment, thorough peace efforts, thorough shaping of harmony, and deepening in creative art.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 454 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: New problems may be welcomed, while love, peace, and harmony require effort under constraint.
- Psychology: Glad engagement and pleasure in new surroundings are explicit; ABC also allows disappointment in love.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Passionate love may end or consolidate; peace efforts may be blocked or pursued thoroughly.
- Events/manifestations: Finding pleasant new surroundings, working for harmony, creative art, and joy over containing fire.
- Conflicts/notes: End and consolidation of love remain distinct possibilities; neither is predicted for a particular relationship.

## Contradictions

The scan supports joy over fire containment, thorough peace efforts, and shaping harmony. An ending and a consolidation of love are alternative entries, not a single sequence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Venus`, Venus specifies the difficult beginning through the pleasure of taking up a new task and finding agreeable surroundings. The ABC entry adds the work needed to establish peace, harmony, or a passionate attachment under limits. Love may consolidate or end, and peace efforts may encounter blockage; the pleasant response does not erase these different possibilities. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 454, PDF p. 226.)
