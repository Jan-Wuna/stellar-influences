## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 293 (one-based).

To face new problems in general. A new beginning, new environment, new circumstances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 454; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Effort and perseverance in public, leadership blocked by the public, restricted performance for the public, fire containment in public, consolidated public leadership, and a creative cycle in the public sphere.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 454 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A new environment and new problems take a general or public form.
- Psychology: Persistent effort is named, without a personal psychological profile.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Public leadership may be consolidated or obstructed by the public.
- Events/manifestations: Public beginnings, altered circumstances, restricted public performance, and public fire containment.
- Conflicts/notes: The public dimension is explicit in ABC; the entry does not identify a specific historical event.

## Contradictions

The German scan resolves the public-leadership and fire-containment wording. The public examples specify the setting without supplying a date, scale, or guaranteed outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the difficult beginning in general circumstances and public life. New problems and a changed environment call for public effort and perseverance, while leadership may either consolidate or meet public obstruction. The ABC examples keep the task concrete through performance for the public and fire containment rather than promising a particular public event. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 293; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 454, PDF p. 226.)
