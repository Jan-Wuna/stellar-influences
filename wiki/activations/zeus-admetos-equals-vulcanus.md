## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 294 (one-based).

Abrupt beginning of new work. To start with a mighty effort. Great forces are concentrated toward one goal. Great difficulties in the initial state. Long held- back forces release themselves violently. Volcanic eruption.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 455; PDF page: 226 (one-based spread).

Beginning severe depressions, a depressive influence of leadership, containment of fire's force, restricted energy output, consolidated leadership strength, consolidation of power objectives, creative circulatory energy, and resistance to coercive leadership.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 455 (PDF p. 226).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: A difficult start concentrates great force toward one goal and may release long-restrained energy abruptly.
- Psychology: ABC adds severe-depression language and a depressive leadership influence, as historical associations.
- Body/health: Energy output and circulatory energy are source terms without a specified medical mechanism.
- Social/relationship: Leadership strength, power objectives, and resistance to coercive leadership are explicit.
- Events/manifestations: An abrupt start of work, a mighty initial effort, violent release, and the source example of volcanic eruption.
- Conflicts/notes: The eruption is a source example, not an event forecast; blocked output and strong release are alternative expressions.

## Contradictions

The scan supports a depressive influence of leadership and resistance to coercive leadership. Historical depression and circulatory-energy claims remain attributed and do not establish a diagnosis or mechanism.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Admetos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the difficult beginning through a concentration of force. Starting new work may demand a mighty effort, and energy held back for a long time may be released abruptly or violently. The ABC entry places this tension in restricted output, consolidated leadership strength, and resistance to coercive leadership; Witte's volcanic eruption is a concrete extreme example. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 294; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 455, PDF p. 226.)
