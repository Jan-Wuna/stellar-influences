## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 292 (one-based).

To terminate an apprenticeship, a study or research work. Deep research work in a narrowed field. Suspended economics.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 453; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Blocked trade efforts, restriction of commercial performance, restricted creativity in science, experiences with fiery primordial material, containing the spread of fire, deepening leadership experience, and thorough scientific striving toward goals.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 453 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Research or practical enterprise narrows, stops, or encounters limits.
- Psychology: Deep concentration in a restricted field is explicit, without a personality or diagnosis.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Commercial performance and leadership experience are named settings.
- Events/manifestations: Ending an apprenticeship, study, or research project; deep work in a narrow field; economic suspension.
- Conflicts/notes: Termination and depth are distinct possibilities; an ending is not necessarily failure.

## Contradictions

The scan confirms restricted scientific creativity, leadership experience, and containing fire spread. Completion, suspension, and narrow-field depth should not be collapsed into one outcome.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies research, learning, and practical enterprise through a limit or narrowing of scope. A study or apprenticeship may end, but the same orientation also supports deep research in a restricted field. The ABC entry adds constrained trade and scientific creativity alongside deepened leadership experience: work can become more focused even when its expansion is blocked. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 292; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 453, PDF p. 225.)
