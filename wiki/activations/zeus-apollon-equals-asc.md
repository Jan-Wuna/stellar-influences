## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

To receive technical and practical training with or through others. Handling of fireworks, machines, apparatus or other technical things. Technical schools for arms and war.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 452; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Taxing experiences of the environment, successful guidance of a partner, spread of fire at the place, a place for commercial performance, creative experiences with a partner, independent goals of the partner, extensive environmental objectives, and increased shaping of the environment.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 452 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Practical and technical experience is acquired through other people and the immediate setting.
- Psychology: The environment can demand effort; no distinct psychological condition is stated.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Partners, practical teachers, and technical schools provide the relational setting.
- Events/manifestations: Technical training, handling machines or fireworks, commercial work at a place, and local fire spread.
- Conflicts/notes: Military technical schools are one Witte example; partner guidance is not transportation.

## Contradictions

The German scan supports partner guidance and independent partner goals. Military training is an example within Witte's technical range, not a necessary vocation or event.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Asc`, Asc specifies practical learning and technical enterprise through other people and the immediate environment. Training with others and handling equipment make knowledge usable. The ABC entry adds a partner's guidance and independent goals, plus active shaping of the surroundings; the military-school and local-fire examples remain concrete settings within this wider practical theme. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 452, PDF p. 225.)
