## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

Forming of great companies in science, commerce, trades or industry. Corporations, economic associations and study groups.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 453; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Success through joint efforts, successful objectives of a community, joint efforts for knowledge, shared leadership experience, achievements of a commercial community, and objectives of a trading community.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 453 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Learning, research, and economic activity take organized collective form.
- Psychology: Shared pursuit of knowledge is explicit; no individual psychological profile is given.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Corporations, economic associations, commercial communities, and study groups are central.
- Events/manifestations: Forming companies and pursuing successful shared objectives.
- Conflicts/notes: The sources describe collective effort and success, without guaranteeing a particular organization's result.

## Contradictions

The scan confirms shared leadership experience and distinguishes commercial performance from trading objectives. The entries emphasize organized cooperation without establishing an inevitable success.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies practical knowledge and enterprise through a community or organized group. Research, commerce, and industry become shared endeavors, with corporations, associations, and study groups as explicit examples. Success is linked to common effort, shared leadership experience, and a collective objective rather than to isolated individual achievement. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 453, PDF p. 225.)
