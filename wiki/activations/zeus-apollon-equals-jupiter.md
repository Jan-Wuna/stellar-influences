## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

Happiness and success through learning, studying and searching. Luck and success in the arts and works of peace like science, commerce, trades or industry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 452; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Efforts in legal science, experienced legal guidance, successful financial efforts, successful legal performance, joy over successful procreation, leading money trading, and creative legal knowledge.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 452 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Study and practical enterprise are associated with satisfaction and successful achievement.
- Psychology: Happiness and joy in learning or successful work are explicit.
- Body/health: ABC's successful-procreation example is historical doctrine, not a fertility assessment.
- Social/relationship: Law, finance, commerce, and peaceful industry are named contexts.
- Events/manifestations: Success through study and research, legal work, and financial effort.
- Conflicts/notes: The success wording belongs to the sources and supplies no guarantee for a particular investment or project.

## Contradictions

The German scan confirms legal guidance and joy over successful procreation. Neither entry establishes a financial forecast or a medical conclusion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies learning and practical enterprise through happiness in study and successful application. Knowledge becomes useful in peaceful arts, commerce, industry, and the ABC examples of law and finance. The organizing theme is rewarding achievement through inquiry and effort, with the source's procreation example retained as a separate historical association rather than a general promise. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 452, PDF p. 225.)
