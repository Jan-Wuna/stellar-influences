## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 292 (one-based).

To be outstanding through one’s eagerness for knowledge, study and research. To outdo or leave others behind, to be without competition. To surpass the competitors in science or economy.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 453; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Spread of a major fire, special trading objectives, successful creativity of authorities, great richness of creation, great scientific achievement, independent leadership experience, state leadership successes, and multiplication of exceptional efforts.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 453 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Knowledge, research, and enterprise acquire distinction, authority, or exceptional scale.
- Psychology: Eagerness for knowledge and research is explicit; no personality diagnosis is offered.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Authorities, state leadership, and scientific or economic competitors provide the context.
- Events/manifestations: Outstanding achievement, surpassing competitors, independent leadership experience, and the spread of a major fire.
- Conflicts/notes: ABC's fire and creative-abundance phrases were missing in the old translation; distinction does not guarantee superiority in every field.

## Contradictions

The German scan restores the spread of a major fire and great creative richness, and identifies independent leadership experience. Witte's competitive distinction and ABC's state examples are different settings, not identical claims.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Kronos`, Kronos specifies study and enterprise through distinction and exceptional accomplishment. Eagerness for knowledge can lead to work that surpasses competitors, while the ABC entry places creativity and achievement in official, state, or independent leadership settings. Its major-fire example preserves the source's range beyond professional excellence and does not follow as a necessary event. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 292; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 453, PDF p. 225.)
