## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

To industriously learn, study or research. To put the theory into practice with energy. To occupy one’s mind with military problems. Military science.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 452; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Strenuous scientific work, successful leadership work, successful handling of fire, successful efforts at procreation, creative peace efforts, creative scientific activity, and purposeful efforts to gain knowledge.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 452 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Learning and research are actively worked at and put into practice.
- Psychology: Industry, energy, and purposeful pursuit of knowledge are explicit.
- Body/health: ABC's efforts at procreation are a historical association without a fertility conclusion.
- Social/relationship: Leadership work and peace efforts coexist with Witte's military-science examples.
- Events/manifestations: Scientific work, practical application of theory, handling fire, and studying military problems.
- Conflicts/notes: Peaceful and military applications remain distinct examples; neither is the sole meaning.

## Contradictions

The German scan confirms leadership work, efforts at procreation, peace efforts, and purposeful pursuit of knowledge. Peaceful work and military science are different applications, not a contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Mars`, Mars specifies learning and practical research through energetic work. The emphasis falls on industrious study and putting theory into use, with purposeful scientific effort and leadership work as concrete expressions. Peace efforts and military science show different applications of that work; successful handling of fire is another source example rather than a required event. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 452, PDF p. 225.)
