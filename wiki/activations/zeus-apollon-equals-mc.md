## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

Gifted hands and nimble fingers. To be practical. To solve problems in a practical manner rather than by making a study of them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 452; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Successful psychological guidance, my success in procreation, my successful objectives, my leadership experience, my commercial achievements, my trading objectives, my scientific achievement, and my increased efforts.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 452 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Practical knowledge becomes one's own skill and capacity to solve problems.
- Psychology: Practical orientation, successful objectives, and increased personal effort are explicit.
- Body/health: Witte names gifted hands and nimble fingers; ABC's procreation example is not a clinical assessment.
- Social/relationship: Personal leadership experience and commercial goals are named without a specific relationship.
- Events/manifestations: Practical problem solving and personal commercial or scientific achievement.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's preference for practical solutions does not deny the value of study or establish a cognitive type.

## Contradictions

The German scan distinguishes psychological guidance from leadership experience; the former English 'training' wording is corrected. The procreation example remains source-specific and does not establish fertility.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = MC`, MC specifies practical learning as one's own ability to handle a problem. Witte's gifted hands and nimble fingers make the emphasis concrete: the person solves through practical action. The ABC entry broadens that personal application to leadership experience, commercial objectives, and scientific achievement, while retaining increased effort as part of the picture. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 452, PDF p. 225.)
