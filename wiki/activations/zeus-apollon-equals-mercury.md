## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

Thoughts are concentrated on the practical. The thoughts are concentrated on life's necessities. Induction orders of the armed forces.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 452; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Reports on scientific achievements, experienced shaping of news, successful guidance of young people, successful conduct of discussions, successful media performance, scientific direction of thought, and scientific shaping of traffic.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 452 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Practical knowledge is organized through thought, reports, and communication.
- Psychology: Thought concentrates on practical matters and life's necessities.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Guiding young people, conducting discussions, and media work are explicit settings.
- Events/manifestations: Scientific reporting, news production, traffic organization, and military induction orders.
- Conflicts/notes: The military order is one communication example; the entry does not establish a universal military context.

## Contradictions

The scans confirm conduct of discussions and life's necessities. Scientific reporting and military induction orders are separate manifestations rather than a single required setting.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies practical knowledge through concentrated thought and its communication. Attention turns to what is needed in daily life, while the ABC entry develops reports, news, discussions, media, and the organization of traffic. The shared thread is giving usable knowledge a directed form; Witte's induction order is one concrete administrative example. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 452, PDF p. 225.)
