## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

Persons with practical experience. To demonstrate practice in public. Armed people, whose units are well trained and under efficient leadership, whose abilities are practically tested.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 452; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Successful leadership of a woman, successful achievements of a woman, passionate emotional experiences, freedom objectives of a people, leadership experiences of a woman, commercial achievements of a people, trading objectives of a people, and creative emotional knowledge.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 452 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Practical experience is demonstrated through people, public performance, and collective achievement.
- Psychology: ABC adds passionate emotional experience and creative emotional knowledge.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Women, a people, public demonstrations, and well-trained armed units are explicit historical examples.
- Events/manifestations: Publicly demonstrating practiced abilities and pursuing commercial, leadership, or collective freedom objectives.
- Conflicts/notes: The gendered examples remain source-attributed; well-trained units do not imply every manifestation is military.

## Contradictions

The German scan reads Gefühls-Erfahrungen and Gefühls-Wissen, meaning emotional experiences and knowledge. The gendered and military examples remain attributed and bounded.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Moon`, Moon specifies practical experience through people and its public demonstration. Abilities are tested in use, whether in Witte's trained units or ABC's examples of a woman's leadership and a people's commercial goals. Passionate emotional experience and creative emotional knowledge add an affective dimension; these historical examples do not define all women or every public activity. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 452, PDF p. 225.)
