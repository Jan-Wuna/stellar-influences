## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

Business man engaged in daring enterprises and speculations. Research in a new field, daring to explore unknown territory.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 453; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Beginning increased uncertainty, successful achievements in water, successful achievements in metaphysics, leadership experience in the air, creeping spread of fire, future scientific objectives, and shaping future knowledge.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 453 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Inquiry and enterprise enter uncertain, unfamiliar, or speculative territory.
- Psychology: Daring exploration and increased uncertainty are explicit, without a diagnosis.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Business enterprise is named; no specific personal relationship is established.
- Events/manifestations: Speculative ventures, research in a new field, achievements in water and metaphysics, and creeping fire spread.
- Conflicts/notes: The restored ABC boundary ends at shaping future knowledge; development phrases belong to Pluto.

## Contradictions

The German scan ends Neptune at 'Zukunfts-Wissen gestalten'. The subsequent performance and trading-development phrases belong to Pluto and have been removed from this entry.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies research and enterprise through the unknown. Daring exploration can open a new field while increasing uncertainty, and the ABC entry adds future scientific objectives and knowledge still being shaped. Achievements in water and metaphysics are explicit examples; creeping fire spread is a different possible manifestation, not evidence that every venture fails or succeeds. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 453, PDF p. 225.)
