## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

To be jointly trained with arms, machines, apparatus and other technical implements. Joint peaceful activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 452; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Meeting of leading scientists, beginning of a successful encounter, successful efforts for contacts, many creative contacts, targeted scientific encounters, and increasing efforts of a connection

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Practical knowledge and technical learning develop through contact and joint activity.
- Psychology: The entries emphasize purposeful engagement, without an individual psychological profile.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Creative contacts, meetings of leading scientists, and shared technical training are central.
- Events/manifestations: Scientific meetings, joint training with machines or arms, and peaceful cooperative activity.
- Conflicts/notes: Arms training and peaceful activity are both retained; connection does not imply only one application.

## Contradictions

The German scan confirms the existing ABC entry. Joint arms training and peaceful activity are different possibilities within Witte, not competing orientations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Node`, Node specifies practical learning and enterprise through contact and joint activity. People meet to exchange knowledge, train together, or pursue a scientific purpose; effort to establish contacts is itself part of the entry. The range includes technical or arms training as well as peaceful cooperation, so the organizing theme is shared practical engagement. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 452, PDF p. 225.)
