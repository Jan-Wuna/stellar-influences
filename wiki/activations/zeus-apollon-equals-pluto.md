## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

To develop research abilities from a very small beginning. To show increased eagerness to learn and study. A very large business or firm which had a very small start.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 453; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Successful unfolding of performance, developing trading objectives, scientific development objectives, development of commercial performance, extensive striving for transformation, growing leadership experience, and increasing spread of fire.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 453 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Practical knowledge and enterprise grow from a small start into broader accomplishment.
- Psychology: Increasing eagerness to learn and study is explicit.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Growth of a firm and expanding leadership experience are named contexts.
- Events/manifestations: Developing research ability, commercial performance, trading objectives, and spreading fire.
- Conflicts/notes: Growth is a source theme, not a guarantee that every small business becomes large.

## Contradictions

The German scan restores Pluto from 'Erfolgreiche Leistungs-Entfaltung' onward. Development and increasing fire spread are distinct examples; expansion should not be treated as uniformly beneficial.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies learning and enterprise as an unfolding process. Research ability, eagerness to study, or a business can grow from a very small beginning. The ABC entry adds developing commercial performance, trading objectives, and leadership experience, while its spreading-fire example shows that increase itself is not always beneficial. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 453, PDF p. 225.)
