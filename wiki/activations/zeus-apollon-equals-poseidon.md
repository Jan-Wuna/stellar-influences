## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 292 (one-based).

Learning, studying and research work in the spiritual and mental realm. People of the same caliber are associates. In the economic respect persons are equal. Members of a guild such as craft guild. A party-spirit.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 453; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

German: Anstrengungen um Wissen und Wahrheit, beginnende Vernunft-Erfolge, Erfahrungen eines feurigen Geistes, erfolgreiche Kreation einer Idee, erfolgreiche Wahrheits-Bezeugung, Wissen um geistiges Feuer, wissenschaftliche Kultur-Leistung.

English translation: Efforts toward knowledge and truth, beginning successes of reason, experiences of a fiery mind, successful creation of an idea, successful testimony to truth, knowledge of spiritual fire, and scientific cultural achievement.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 453 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Learning and research turn toward intellectual or spiritual knowledge and truth.
- Psychology: Reason, a fiery mind, and creating an idea are explicit motifs.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Peers, economic equals, guild members, and party spirit are Witte's association examples.
- Events/manifestations: Research in the mental or spiritual realm, creating an idea, and testimony to truth.
- Conflicts/notes: Truth testimony is not sacrifice; peer association does not establish the truth of a doctrine.

## Contradictions

The German scan supports successful creation of an idea and testimony to truth. Witte's party spirit and ABC's truth-seeking are different aspects, not proof of doctrinal correctness.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Poseidon`, Poseidon specifies learning and research through intellectual or spiritual knowledge and the pursuit of truth. An idea is formed and brought into expression, with reason and testimony as explicit ABC themes. Witte also places this work among peers or guild-like associates, linking the inquiry to a community of comparable standing without making their shared conviction proof of truth. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 292; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 453, PDF p. 225.)
