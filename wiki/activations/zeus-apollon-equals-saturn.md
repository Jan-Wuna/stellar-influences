## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

Slow mentality. Hindrances in learning, studying or research. Losses in trade, commerce or industry. Severed commercial connections. Business liquidation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 453; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Successful objectives in old age, leadership experience through perseverance, shaping scientific tasks, concentration on scientific objectives, mature scientific achievement, and extensive losses through fire.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 453 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Learning and enterprise meet hindrance or loss, while focused, mature work remains another possibility.
- Psychology: Witte's 'slow mentality' is historical wording; ABC emphasizes concentration and perseverance.
- Body/health: No medical condition is established by the historical 'slow mentality' phrase.
- Social/relationship: Severed commercial connections and business liquidation are explicit Witte examples.
- Events/manifestations: Hindered study, trade losses, mature scientific achievements, and extensive fire losses.
- Conflicts/notes: The sources differ materially in emphasis: impairment and loss coexist with late or mature achievement.

## Contradictions

The scan confirms leadership experience through perseverance. Witte emphasizes hindrance and loss; ABC also names success in old age and mature achievement. This is a substantive difference of emphasis, without a required sequence.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies learning and enterprise through delay, restriction, or separation. Study may be hindered and commercial links may be severed, with liquidation as an extreme business example. The ABC entry also allows concentration, perseverance, and mature scientific achievement, so the reading must retain disciplined accomplishment alongside loss rather than equating the orientation with incapacity. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 453, PDF p. 225.)
