## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

The man who has practical experience.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 452; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Successful objectives of a man, successful physical performance, personal leadership experience, science serving physical performance, a center for commercial achievement, procreative experiences of a man, and increasing efforts of a man.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 452 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Practical knowledge is embodied in a person's experience and achievement.
- Psychology: Successful objectives and increasing personal effort are explicit, without a personality profile.
- Body/health: ABC names physical performance and procreative experience as historical associations, without diagnosing ability or fertility.
- Social/relationship: Personal leadership and a center for commercial achievement are named examples.
- Events/manifestations: Practical experience, physical accomplishment, and increasing effort.
- Conflicts/notes: The sources use male examples; they do not establish universal male traits.

## Contradictions

The German scan confirms personal leadership experience and physical performance. Gendered and procreative examples remain attributed; neither source establishes a medical conclusion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Sun`, Sun specifies practical learning through the experienced person. Witte's concise entry centers on having practical experience, while ABC makes this concrete through objectives, physical accomplishment, personal leadership, and increasing effort. Its male and procreative examples remain the historical source's stated range, not a general claim about gender or fertility. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 452, PDF p. 225.)
