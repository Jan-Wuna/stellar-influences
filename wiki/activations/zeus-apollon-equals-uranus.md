## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

Quickly consummated business deals. Talent for construction. To be suddenly tied to technical matters or things. To study and search technical problems. Technical designs. Engineering drawings.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 453; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Beginning new experiences, successful technical achievements, new leadership experiences, shaping new knowledge, new scientific objectives, sudden spread of fire, surprising leadership experiences, and much excitement through fire.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 453 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Practical inquiry and enterprise develop through sudden technical engagement and new experience.
- Psychology: Surprise and excitement are explicit, without a clinical condition.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Business deals and new or surprising leadership experience are named contexts.
- Events/manifestations: Rapid business completion, technical design, engineering drawings, and sudden fire spread.
- Conflicts/notes: Technical talent and fire spread are separate examples; novelty does not guarantee benefit.

## Contradictions

The German scan confirms new and surprising leadership experience and 'viel' (much) excitement through fire. Technical success and hazardous fire spread remain separate possible manifestations.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies practical inquiry through sudden engagement with technical problems and new experience. Construction, engineering drawings, and quickly concluded business are concrete applications. The ABC entry adds new scientific goals and surprising leadership experience, while sudden fire spread preserves a hazardous possibility alongside technical accomplishment. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 453, PDF p. 225.)
