## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

All peaceful efforts are prosperous. Joy through artistic and scientific activity.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 452; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Expansion of creative harmony, exceptional peace efforts, a successful beginning in love, successful striving for harmony, successful artistic creation, successful procreation through love, passionate experiences of love, and creative art scholarship.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 452 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Practical creative work is associated with pleasure, harmony, and peaceful accomplishment.
- Psychology: Joy through artistic and scientific activity is explicit.
- Body/health: ABC's procreation-through-love example is a historical association, not a fertility prediction.
- Social/relationship: Love, harmony, and peace efforts provide the relational setting.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic creation, scientific activity, and successful beginnings in love.
- Conflicts/notes: Witte's sweeping prosperity wording is attributed source language; it does not guarantee every peaceful project succeeds.

## Contradictions

The German scan supports striving for harmony and procreation through love. Witte's broad prosperity claim remains attributed rather than expanded into a guaranteed result.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Venus`, Venus specifies practical creativity and inquiry through joy, harmony, and peaceful accomplishment. Artistic and scientific activity become satisfying pursuits, while ABC adds creating art, striving for peace, and a successful beginning in love. The sources' success language remains a stated possibility; the procreative example should not become a medical promise. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 452, PDF p. 225.)
