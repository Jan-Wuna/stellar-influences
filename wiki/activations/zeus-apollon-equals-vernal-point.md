## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 291 (one-based).

The people in general try to find a solution to present problems by practical application. General problems that demand a solution.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 452; PDF page: 225 (one-based spread).

Successful public efforts, spread of fire in public, leadership experience in public, successful performance in public life, public scientific efforts, and increasing public efforts.

Translation checked against the German scan: printed p. 452 (PDF p. 225).

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Practical knowledge is applied to problems that affect the general public.
- Psychology: The entries stress efforts and problem solving, without an individual psychological profile.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical condition is specified.
- Social/relationship: Public life, public leadership, and scientific effort in public are explicit settings.
- Events/manifestations: Practical solutions to general problems, public achievement, and public fire spread.
- Conflicts/notes: The scan restores leadership experience missing from the old English list; the fire example is separate from successful problem solving.

## Contradictions

The German scan restores public leadership experience, successful public performance, and increasing effort. Successful work and fire spread remain distinct possibilities.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Apollon = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies practical inquiry through general problems and public life. People seek usable solutions rather than leaving the problem at the level of study. The ABC entry adds public achievement, leadership experience, and increasing scientific effort, while public fire spread remains a concrete hazardous example rather than the necessary result of that effort. ([Witte Entry](#witte-entry), PDF p. 291; [ABC Entry](#abc-entry), printed p. 452, PDF p. 225.)
