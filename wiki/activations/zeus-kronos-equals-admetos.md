#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 290 (one-based).

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 451; PDF page: 224 (one-based spread).

Beginning a special deepening, blocked goal striving of a hierarchy, restricted state leadership, restriction of exceptional efforts, consolidated state leadership, containing a major fire, and resistance to state leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Exceptional creation or leadership is narrowed, restricted or consolidated.
- psychology: Special deepening and focused accomplishment.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Competition among a few, a special commission and resistance to state leadership.
- events/manifestations: Distinctive accomplishments, limited-scope creation and major-fire containment.
- conflicts/notes: Witte’s odd-character description is historical evaluative wording.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes distinction within a narrow field. ABC gives restriction and consolidation of leadership, allowing difficulty alongside special accomplishment.

## Derived Synthesis

`Zeus/Kronos = Admetos` concentrates exceptional effort within a limited scope. Witte gives creation on a small scale, competition among a few and a special commission; ABC adds special deepening and leadership that may consolidate or be restricted. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Blocked hierarchical goals and resistance to state leadership preserve the obstructed branch. Major-fire containment is a separate practical example; Witte’s odd-character wording remains his judgment about behavior rather than a diagnosis. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
