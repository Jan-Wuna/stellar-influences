## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 290 (one-based).

Eager to learn, to gain knowledge. Many are in competition. Great enterprises and efforts in science, commerce, trades and industry. Expansion of armament or war.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 451; PDF page: 224 (one-based spread).

Prestige through scientific achievements, spread of a major fire, special leadership experience, experienced state leadership, a successful leadership hierarchy, successful state objectives, great scientific achievements, and state leadership successes.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Large enterprises and leadership gain scope or distinction.
- psychology: Eagerness to learn and gain knowledge.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Competition among many and state leadership.
- events/manifestations: Scientific, commercial or industrial effort; expanded armament and major-fire spread are separate.
- conflicts/notes: Scientific prestige and military expansion are different source applications.

## Contradictions

The sources share large-scale achievement and knowledge. Witte also gives expanding armament or war, while ABC adds state leadership and major fire.

## Derived Synthesis

`Zeus/Kronos = Apollon` expands exceptional effort into a wider competitive or institutional field. Witte’s eagerness for knowledge and large scientific or commercial enterprises meet ABC’s scientific prestige, leadership experience and successful state objectives. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Expansion of armament or war and spread of a major fire remain separate, more hazardous examples. They should not become necessary outcomes of scientific distinction or large enterprise. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
