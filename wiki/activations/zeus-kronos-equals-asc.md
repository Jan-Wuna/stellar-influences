## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 289 (one-based).

To be connected with great producers. Connection with military leaders. Others take (seize) the lead.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 450; PDF page: 224 (one-based spread).

State efforts for the surroundings, special spatial design, self-confident leadership of a place, independent objectives of a partner, independent shaping of the surroundings, state environmental achievements, superior local leadership, and important environmental objectives.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Leadership and major production are encountered through others and place.
- psychology: Self-confident local leadership is ABC’s explicit disposition.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Producers, military leaders and partners with independent goals.
- events/manifestations: Spatial design, state environmental efforts and others taking the lead.
- conflicts/notes: Independence and leadership were mistranslated as self-sufficiency.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses connections with major producers or military leaders. ABC adds independent environmental shaping and local leadership without requiring a military setting.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Zeus/Kronos = Asc` places leadership in the people and surroundings one encounters. Witte gives connections with major producers and military leaders, including others taking the lead; ABC adds confident local leadership and a partner’s independent objectives. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

Spatial design and state efforts affecting the environment give practical settings for that leadership. Independent shaping of the surroundings broadens the field beyond Witte’s military examples. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
