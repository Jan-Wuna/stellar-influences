## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 289 (one-based).

Firedamp explosion. Big gas explosion. To extinguish a conflagration with water. Defeats. Defeated war leaders. A lost battle.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 451; PDF page: 224 (one-based spread).

Special creativity in metaphysics, special expectations of achievement, subtle-material leadership authority, a major fire through gas, great achievements through fine sensitivity, weak state leadership, and future objectives of the state.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Leadership may weaken, while subtle creativity or fire-related events form separate branches.
- psychology: Fine sensitivity and expectations of achievement.
- body/health: Subtle-material authority is attributed doctrine without a medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: Weak state leadership and defeated military leaders.
- events/manifestations: Gas explosions, extinguishing fire with water and metaphysical creativity.
- conflicts/notes: The Witte opening belonged to Uranus; ABC development clauses belong to Pluto.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes gas, water and defeat. ABC also gives weak leadership but adds sensitivity and metaphysical creativity, which should not be reduced to failure.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Zeus/Kronos = Neptune` includes weakened leadership in ABC and military defeat in Witte. The sources also share gas-related fire examples, with Witte separately naming extinguishing a conflagration with water. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))

ABC’s fine sensitivity, metaphysical creativity and future state objectives give a different branch. Its subtle-material authority remains source doctrine; these examples do not establish that sensitivity or metaphysical work produces defeat. ([ABC](#abc-entry))
