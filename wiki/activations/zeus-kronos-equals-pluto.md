## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 289 (one-based).

The beginning and the development of an expanded work. On the way to great accomplishment. Expansion of a great war.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 451; PDF page: 224 (one-based spread).

Developing special creativity, unfolding of a major fire, a change of leadership in the state, developing great achievements, independent developmental striving, change in the reputation of leadership, growing achievements of authorities, and important evolutionary objectives.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Major work, creativity and leadership develop or change.
- psychology: Independent striving toward development.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: Changes in state leadership or its reputation.
- events/manifestations: Growing achievements, expanded work, unfolding fire and expansion of war.
- conflicts/notes: Development is not necessarily improvement; the scan restores the opening split into Neptune.

## Contradictions

Both describe expanding work and achievement. Witte adds war expansion, while ABC adds leadership change and major-fire development.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Zeus/Kronos = Pluto` develops exceptional effort into larger work and changing leadership. Witte describes progress toward great accomplishment; ABC gives special creativity unfolding, growing achievements of authorities and changes in state leadership or its reputation. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

A major fire unfolding and a great war expanding are separate source examples of increase. Their presence keeps development from being read as automatic improvement or benefit. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
