## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 290 (one-based).

Great mental struggles. A struggle for one’s convictions. To back one’s convictions or way of seeing the world and life. To be tested to the utmost.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 451; PDF page: 224 (one-based spread).

Special intellectual or spiritual creativity, insightful state leadership, a leading cultural state, great cultural achievements, direction of intellectual or spiritual fire, state cultural efforts, priority pursuit of truth, superior intellectual or spiritual leadership, and objectives of the intellectual or spiritual hierarchy.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Convictions and intellectual leadership undergo demanding tests.
- psychology: Mental struggle and commitment to one’s worldview.
- body/health: No explicit bodily or health claim in these entries.
- social/relationship: State cultural leadership and intellectual or spiritual hierarchies.
- events/manifestations: Cultural achievement, creative ideas and pursuit of truth.
- conflicts/notes: Standing by a conviction does not prove its truth.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes struggle and testing of convictions. ABC emphasizes cultural creativity and insightful leadership; the two describe different conditions of intellectual work.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Zeus/Kronos = Poseidon` directs exceptional effort into convictions and intellectual or cultural leadership. Witte stresses standing by one’s worldview under severe testing; ABC adds creative thought, cultural achievements, insightful state leadership and a priority pursuit of truth. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))

The sources therefore retain both demanding struggle and constructive leadership. Their truth and superiority language remains attributed, without proving the correctness of a particular conviction or hierarchy. ([Witte](#witte-entry), [ABC](#abc-entry))
