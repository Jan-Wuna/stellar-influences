## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 289 (one-based).

A fighter by nature. The man who demands and inclines to aggressive action. War chief. Commanding Officer.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 450; PDF page: 224 (one-based spread).

A man beginning independence, special personal creativity, a major fire during the day, great human achievement, male leadership dominance, a state leadership center, superior physical achievements, and important personal objectives.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal initiative and leadership expressed through major achievement.
- psychology: A fighting or aggressive disposition is Witte’s historical characterization.
- body/health: ABC names physical achievements without a medical claim.
- social/relationship: A commanding officer and leadership dominance.
- events/manifestations: Personal creative effort, independent beginnings, state leadership centers and major fire.
- conflicts/notes: The male and military examples do not establish universal gender traits.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes aggressive command. ABC adds personal creativity, independence and physical achievement, allowing a broader practical expression.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Zeus/Kronos = Sun` gives exceptional effort a personal center. ABC describes independence beginning, special creativity, important objectives and substantial physical or human achievement; Witte gives a fighter or commanding officer as a more aggressive leadership example. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))

State leadership centers and a major fire during the day are additional concrete settings. The gendered and military language remains historical attribution rather than a universal trait or an event prediction. ([ABC](#abc-entry), [Witte](#witte-entry))
