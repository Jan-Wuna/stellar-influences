## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 289, Zeus + Kronos, Venus row; checked against the scan.

The creative ability of the womb. Love's labor. Expansive peaceful work. To experience friendship and affection through great accomplishment. Diplomat. Peaceful military power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 450 (PDF p. 224), Zeus + Kronos, Venus row; English translation checked against the German scan.

Demanding statecraft, special achievements through beauty, peace efforts by leaders, a leading authority in art, great artistic creativity, and benevolent leadership by the boss.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Creative achievement and leadership expressed through beauty, affection, or peace.
- Psychology: Benevolent leadership and creative engagement, within the ABC entry's limited wording.
- Body/health: Witte's reference to the womb is a historical reproductive association, not a fertility assessment.
- Social/relationship: Friendship and affection through accomplishment, diplomacy, and leaders' peace efforts.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic achievement, peaceful work, statecraft, and peaceful military power are the stated settings.
- Conflicts/notes: The entries place authority in a peaceful or artistic role; they do not establish that all leadership here is benevolent.

## Contradictions

The former Witte passage belongs to Mercury on the scanned page. Displaced transcription, retained for traceability only: "The capabilities of the brain. Great mental creations and projects. Able to lead and command."

The ABC scan supports statecraft, leaders' peace efforts, and great artistic creativity; "state art", "peace efforts leading", and "groll artistic creativity" were damaged English renderings. No direct contradiction is established by the corrected passages.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Kronos = Venus`, Venus specifies Zeus/Kronos through the ability to direct creative work toward beauty, affection, and peaceful accomplishment. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links great accomplishment with friendship, diplomacy, and peaceful work; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends that emphasis to artistic authority, statecraft, and benevolent leadership. The shared thread is achievement whose authority works through creative or conciliatory means.

Witte also supplies a reproductive image of the womb. That remains an attributed historical association, not a medical conclusion, and it should not replace the wider artistic and social meanings stated in the entries.
