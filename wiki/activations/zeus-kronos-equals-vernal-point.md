## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 289, Zeus + Kronos, Vernal Point row; checked against the scan.

The general energetic will in pursuit of a goal. Big fires. Wars. Deciding battles.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 450 (PDF p. 224), Zeus + Kronos, Vernal Point row; English translation checked against the German scan.

Efforts by public authorities in the public sphere, large fire in public, great public achievements, independent striving in public life, state achievements for the public, and state leadership in public.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Energetic pursuit of goals expressed in public or state affairs.
- Psychology: Goal-directed will and independent striving in public life; no fuller personal psychology is supplied.
- Body/health: These entries contain no bodily or medical description.
- Social/relationship: Public authorities, state leadership, and achievements for the public are the specified relationships.
- Events/manifestations: Public achievements and efforts, large fires, wars, and decisive battles are stated examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Public accomplishment and destructive collective events coexist in the evidence; neither is an inevitable outcome.

## Contradictions

The ABC entry had been reduced to a dash-only translation artifact. Its German row on printed p. 450 supplies the restored public and state meanings above. Witte's military emphasis and ABC's public-administration emphasis are differences of scope, not a demonstrated contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Kronos = Vernal Point`, the Vernal Point specifies Zeus/Kronos through goal-directed effort in public life. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) places leadership and major achievements in the public sphere, while the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes a general energetic will and gives war, decisive battle, and large fire as collective manifestations. Publicly consequential effort unifies the entries without reducing their range to conflict alone.

These are broad collective images, not a forecast of a particular public event. The entries give no basis for adding bodily, intimate relationship, or detailed individual character claims.
