## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 290, Zeus + Kronos, Vulcanus row; checked against the scan.

The use of much violent force. Blind force. Tremendous powers are engaged. The outbreak of a great war. Great powers locked in battle.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 451 (PDF p. 224), Zeus + Kronos, Vulcanus row; English translation checked against the German scan.

Special energy production, dominant leadership strength, an influential hierarchy of achievement, mighty achievements of the state, great force of fire, great exertions, leaders' power goals, state leadership power, and important energy performance.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Concentrated power in leadership, state achievement, and the use of force.
- Psychology: Forceful aims and dominance are present; Witte's blind force is an attributed extreme rather than a complete personality description.
- Body/health: No medical or bodily assessment is supplied.
- Social/relationship: Power hierarchies, state leadership, and great powers in conflict are the stated collective settings.
- Events/manifestations: Energy production, major state achievements, fire, and great war are source examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Effective power and violent excess share the page; the entries do not specify when one becomes the other.

## Contradictions

The ABC scan supports leadership strength and an influential achievement hierarchy, replacing the damaged "filtration force" and "one flinch power hierarchy". Witte foregrounds destructive force, while ABC also includes productive energy and state achievement; this is a difference of emphasis, not proof of disagreement or independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Kronos = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies Zeus/Kronos through the scale and concentration of power brought to leadership and achievement. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) includes energy production, major state efforts, and influential hierarchies; the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) stresses the same magnitude in its extreme form of blind violence and great powers at war. The organizing theme is force deployed through authority, ranging from formidable production to destructive conflict.

The severe examples remain source possibilities, not an inevitable event or a diagnosis of an individual. Neither entry supplies a medical interpretation or an intimate relationship account.
