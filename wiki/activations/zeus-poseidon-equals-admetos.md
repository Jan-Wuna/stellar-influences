## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Admetos row; checked against the scan.

A creative mind that can see very deeply. To be noticed in a small circle right at the start for accomplishment.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 459 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Admetos row; checked against the German scan.

Limitation of a creative spirit, striving to consolidate truth, consolidating clear goals, spiritual shaping of primordial matter, producing spiritual condensation, thorough spiritual effort, suppressed striving for truth, and deepening into cultural goals.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Depth and concentration of creative thought within a limited field.
- Psychology: Thorough effort and insight coexist with restriction or suppressed truth-seeking.
- Body/health: No medical description is supplied; spiritual condensation is the source's wording.
- Social/relationship: Recognition within a small circle rather than broad public influence.
- Events/manifestations: Early recognition for accomplishment, consolidation of aims, and deep engagement with cultural goals.
- Conflicts/notes: The expressions about primordial matter and condensation are not defined here and do not establish a physical theory.

## Contradictions

The German scan corrects "grinning spiritual effort" to thorough spiritual effort and restores suppressed striving for truth. Depth and restriction coexist; this is not a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Admetos`, Admetos specifies Zeus/Poseidon through concentrated creative thought that reaches deeply within a narrow field. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins insight to early recognition in a small circle; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects consolidation of truth and aims with thorough effort, restriction, and suppressed truth-seeking. Depth is the common thread, with both accomplishment and constraint in the evidence.

ABC's spiritual condensation and primordial matter remain attributed images whose mechanism this entry does not explain.
