## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Apollon row; checked against the scan.

To be a leader for many, especially for coworkers. Creative accomplishment in science and commerce.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 459 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Apollon row; checked against the German scan.

Efforts in the study of mind and spirit, beginning spiritual experiences, successful spiritual leadership, successful cultural scholarship, spiritual aims of science, clear commercial goals, creative knowledge of spirit, and increasing efforts of reason.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Creative intellectual achievement reaching coworkers, science, or commerce.
- Psychology: Increasing experience, knowledge, and efforts of reason.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is stated.
- Social/relationship: Leadership for many, especially coworkers, alongside commercial aims.
- Events/manifestations: Scientific and commercial accomplishment, cultural scholarship, and spiritual experience.
- Conflicts/notes: The spiritual vocabulary does not identify a particular modern discipline or prove a belief.

## Contradictions

The German scan resolves "spiritual filtration" as spiritual leadership and clarifies the compound about study of mind and spirit. The entries offer different fields of achievement without a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Apollon`, Apollon specifies Zeus/Poseidon through creative knowledge put to work across a wider circle. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names leadership among coworkers and achievement in science and commerce; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds experience, cultural scholarship, and growing efforts of reason. Intellectual production thus has both a collective setting and practical commercial aims.

The spiritual vocabulary remains part of ABC's range, without identifying a specific discipline or verifying the ideas being advanced.
