## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Cupido row; checked against the scan.

Mental attitude or behavior of a society or a family. To hold or to take the leadership in some field of art. Creative mental accomplishment in the field of art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 459 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Cupido row; checked against the German scan.

fiery spiritual community, spiritual leadership community, spiritual coming together, shared cultural formation, shared attestation of truth, clear goals of a community, cultural performance community, and creative spiritual synthesis

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Shared creative outlook and leadership within a family, society, or cultural community.
- Psychology: Collective attitude and a synthesis of ideas.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- Social/relationship: Family, society, common goals, and joint testimony.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic leadership, creative accomplishment, and common cultural work.
- Conflicts/notes: Shared conviction does not establish unanimity or prove a community's beliefs.

## Contradictions

The scanned Witte Cupido row is the family, society, and art passage formerly attached to Hades. The former Cupido passage belongs to Pluto. Displaced transcription, retained for traceability only: "Development of mental leadership. Development of one’s own mental conviction." No direct contradiction is established by the corrected entries.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Cupido`, Cupido specifies Zeus/Poseidon through a shared creative outlook expressed by a family, society, or cultural group. The corrected [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names collective attitude and artistic leadership; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds common goals, joint cultural work, and synthesis. Thought becomes a community's creative undertaking.

This supports collective expression, without implying universal agreement or treating shared testimony as proof.
