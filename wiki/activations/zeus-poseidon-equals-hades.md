## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Hades row; checked against the scan.

Ugly mental attitude and behavior. Miserable disposition. Lack of character. Poor report card.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 459 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Hades row; checked against the German scan.

Beginning clarity about the past, service in spiritual leadership, criminal spiritual leadership, cultural achievements of the past, lack of spiritual leadership, lack of clear goals, inadequate striving for truth, and hidden spiritual striving toward goals.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Difficulty or deficiency in creative thought and leadership, alongside engagement with the past.
- Psychology: Witte's negative judgments of attitude and character remain attributed evaluations.
- Body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Deficient or criminal leadership and hidden spiritual striving are ABC's wording, not a judgment about a named person.
- Events/manifestations: A poor report card in Witte; past cultural achievements and emerging historical clarity in ABC.
- Conflicts/notes: Historical achievement and present deficiency are distinct motifs; moral labels do not justify a universal character verdict.

## Contradictions

The scanned Witte Hades row starts at "Ugly mental attitude". Its former opening belongs to Cupido. Displaced transcription, retained for traceability only: "Mental attitude or behavior of a society or a family. To hold or to take the leadership in some field of art. Creative mental accomplishment in the field of art." ABC also mistranslated leadership as "filtration" and truth-seeking as "striving for prosperity"; the scan supports the repairs.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Hades`, Hades specifies Zeus/Poseidon through difficulty in giving thought and conviction a clear or adequate direction. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) names deficient leadership, unclear aims, and inadequate truth-seeking, but also past cultural achievement and emerging clarity about it. The corrected [Witte entry](#witte-entry) adds harsh assessments of attitude and a poor report card.

Those remain historical source judgments, not grounds to call someone criminal or lacking in character. The cultural and historical motifs also belong to the page's range.
