## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Jupiter row; checked against the scan.

The successful proof. To prove the correctness of something. Joy and happiness over the correctness of one’s own opinion, conviction, reason and wisdom.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 458 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Jupiter row; checked against the German scan.

Beginning legal clarity, insights in financial management, successful spiritual leadership, successful cultural work, financial efforts for an idea, joy over spiritual striving toward goals, money for spiritual goals, and clear legal goals.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Successful demonstration or support for an idea and its aims.
- Psychology: Joy over a convincing result or confidence in one's reason and wisdom.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is stated.
- Social/relationship: Leadership, cultural work, financial management, and legal aims.
- Events/manifestations: Successful proof, financial effort for ideas, and emerging legal clarity.
- Conflicts/notes: The source image of successful proof does not verify any particular opinion.

## Contradictions

The scan corrects "financial support" to financial management and "mental filtration" to spiritual leadership. Witte stresses proof while ABC adds legal, financial, and cultural settings; no direct contradiction is established.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies Zeus/Poseidon through successful advancement or demonstration of an idea. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects proof with satisfaction in one's conviction and reason; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds successful cultural leadership, financial effort for ideas, and legal clarity. An idea finding effective expression or support is the connecting theme.

The formula itself does not verify an opinion, determine a legal outcome, or guarantee financial backing.
