## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Mercury row; checked against the scan.

Ideas, thoughts, and opinions about mental deportment, attitude and behavior.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 458 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Mercury row; checked against the German scan.

Leading cultural language, thoughts about spiritual fire, spiritual leadership of youth, conversations about spiritual leadership, the spiritual striving of youth toward goals, clear direction of thought, cultural achievements of the media, shaping a culture of speech, and reasonable goals for traffic or communication.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Thought and communication about creative mentality, guidance, and conduct.
- Psychology: Ideas about attitude and behavior, with clear direction of thought.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is stated.
- Social/relationship: Youth, leadership conversations, media, and speech culture.
- Events/manifestations: Cultural communication, directing thought, and aims for traffic or communication.
- Conflicts/notes: Verkehr can concern traffic or communication; neither supports the former poverty claim.

## Contradictions

The scan resolves "Fundende culture Sprache" and "mental Fundung". In "impoverished traffic objectives", the German adjective is vernünftige, meaning reasonable. The entry does not narrow Verkehr to one setting, so the translation preserves its traffic or communication range.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Mercury`, Mercury specifies Zeus/Poseidon through the language used to express creative thought and guide conduct. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) concerns ideas about attitude and behavior; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds youth, leadership conversations, media, and the shaping of speech. Conviction is articulated, discussed, and directed through communication.

ABC also includes reasonable aims for Verkehr. Its broad traffic or communication setting does not justify inventing a particular transport event or relationship.
