## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Neptune row; checked against the scan.

Rejection of mental compulsion. Hidden mental leadership. The invisible hand. To lead from the background or secretly. False witnesses or faked proofs.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 459 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Neptune row; checked against the German scan.

Disappointing testimony to truth, production of clear water, subtle spiritual leadership, foreign spiritual leadership, future spiritual goals, clear goals in the air, producing clear water, and unknown spiritual leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Creative mentality expressed through hidden, uncertain, or disappointing guidance and testimony.
- Psychology: Rejection of mental compulsion and uncertainty about the source of guidance.
- Body/health: No health claim is given; clear water is a literal source example.
- Social/relationship: Secret or background leadership, foreign guidance, and false witnesses in Witte.
- Events/manifestations: Faked proofs, disappointing testimony, clear-water production, and future aims.
- Conflicts/notes: The clear-water motif is distinct from the testimony problem; the entry gives no mechanism connecting them.

## Contradictions

The Witte scan shows that its Neptune row continues through the former Pluto passage, now restored here. ABC had merged the first three Pluto phrases into Neptune and mistranslated disappointing testimony as "Exchangeable Truth Proof"; the scan establishes the boundary and corrected meaning. The removed development phrases belong to the Pluto row, not Neptune.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Neptune`, Neptune specifies Zeus/Poseidon through guidance and testimony whose source or reliability can be unclear. The complete [Witte entry](#witte-entry) includes hidden leadership, rejection of compulsion, and false witnesses or proofs; the corrected [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds disappointing testimony and unknown or foreign guidance. The connecting theme is difficulty locating or trusting the direction given to creative thought.

ABC separately names production of clear water and future aims. Those uncommon examples remain part of its range, without making every instance deceptive or supplying a mechanism that joins all motifs.
