## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Node row; checked against the scan.

To have to issue a document. To find the right connection.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 458 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Node row; checked against the German scan.

Encounters with creative ideas, conveying spiritual goals, the spiritual striving of a connection toward goals, shaping cultural connections, clear contact goals, a connection to spiritual leadership, and conveying cultural achievements.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Connections that convey ideas, goals, or cultural accomplishments.
- Psychology: Clear aims for contact; the entry supplies no fuller personality account.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim is stated.
- Social/relationship: Cultural contacts, connections to leadership, and finding the right connection.
- Events/manifestations: Issuing a document and communicating spiritual goals or achievements.
- Conflicts/notes: Issuing a document is Witte's specific example, not a guarantee of official certification.

## Contradictions

The scan resolves the translation fragments "Y commitment" as connection and restores the active conveying of goals and cultural achievement. The documentary and cultural meanings are distinct settings without a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Node`, Node specifies Zeus/Poseidon through connections that carry creative ideas, cultural work, and shared aims. The [ABC entry](#abc-entry) describes encounters, contact goals, and links to spiritual leadership; the [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives the concrete acts of finding a connection and issuing a document. The shared emphasis is making a link through which thought or testimony can be conveyed.

The brief entries do not determine the nature of a particular relationship or promise that a document will be officially certified.
