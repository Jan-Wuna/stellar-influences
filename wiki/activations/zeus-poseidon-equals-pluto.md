#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Pluto row; checked against the scan.

Development of mental leadership. Development of one’s own mental conviction.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 459 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Pluto row; checked against the German scan.

Developing spiritual striving toward goals, clear development of leadership, clear transformation goals, shaping cultural development, achievements of cultural evolution, developing creative ideas, growing spiritual leadership, and a change of spiritual leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Development of conviction, creative ideas, and intellectual leadership.
- Psychology: Growth of one's own mental conviction.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is supplied.
- Social/relationship: Growing or changing spiritual leadership and cultural development.
- Events/manifestations: Transformation goals and the development of creative ideas or cultural work.
- Conflicts/notes: Development does not itself establish improvement or the truth of a conviction.

## Contradictions

The scanned Witte Pluto row is the development passage formerly attached to Cupido. Its former passage belongs to Neptune. Displaced transcription, retained for traceability only: "To lead from the background or secretly. False witnesses or faked proofs." The ABC scan also restores three opening development phrases formerly merged into Neptune and corrects "filtration" to leadership.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Pluto`, Pluto specifies Zeus/Poseidon through the development of creative thought and the leadership that expresses it. The corrected [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names growth of mental leadership and personal conviction; the restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects that process with transformation aims, new ideas, cultural development, and changes of leadership. The theme is thought and conviction taking a developing form.

The entries describe development, without proving that every change is an improvement or that growing conviction is correct.
