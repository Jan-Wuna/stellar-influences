## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Saturn row; checked against the scan.

Passing interruption in schooling or training. To take over or to give to others, for a short time, the leadership in educational matters.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 459 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Saturn row; checked against the German scan.

Effortful mental concentration, stressful truth witnessing, patient mental goal striving, clear goals in old age, concentration on spiritual guidance, problematic truth witnessing, and lossful culture design

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Concentrated intellectual effort under delay, burden, or temporary interruption.
- Psychology: Patient striving, demanding concentration, and burdensome testimony.
- Body/health: Old age is an ABC setting, not a disease or diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: Temporary transfer of leadership in education and concentration on spiritual guidance.
- Events/manifestations: An interruption in schooling, problematic testimony, and cultural work involving loss.
- Conflicts/notes: A passing educational interruption is not a claim of permanent failure.

## Contradictions

Witte describes a passing interruption or temporary transfer of educational leadership; ABC adds concentration, burden, old age, and loss. These are differences of scale and setting without a direct contradiction.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Saturn`, Saturn specifies Zeus/Poseidon through sustained intellectual effort when expression is burdened or interrupted. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) locates this in schooling and a temporary transfer of educational leadership; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds patient goal-seeking, demanding concentration, and difficult testimony. The connecting theme is maintaining direction through limits or delay.

The evidence does not turn a passing interruption into permanent failure, and ABC's old-age setting is not a medical claim.
