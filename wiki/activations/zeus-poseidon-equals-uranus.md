## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Uranus row; checked against the scan.

To suddenly take up or to receive a document, certificate or license. By surprise to prove, to witness or clarify something.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 459 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Uranus row; checked against the German scan.

Dynamic technical achievements, arousing truth testimony, spiritual direction impulses, shaping a cultural event, clear cultural goals, new clear goals, new cultural achievements, suddenly beginning knowledge, and restless fiery spirit

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Sudden clarification, testimony, or a fresh intellectual direction.
- Psychology: A restless fiery spirit and sudden insight in ABC's wording.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is stated.
- Social/relationship: Cultural goals and events provide the collective setting.
- Events/manifestations: Unexpected documents, certificates or licenses, sudden proof, and dynamic technical achievement.
- Conflicts/notes: A source image of sudden proof does not verify a particular claim.

## Contradictions

Witte stresses unexpected documents and testimony, while ABC includes technical achievement and fresh cultural aims. These are different manifestations, not interchangeable activation orientations or proof of independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Uranus`, Uranus specifies Zeus/Poseidon through a sudden move toward clarification or a fresh direction of thought. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes unexpected documents and surprising acts of proof or witnessing; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) joins sudden insight to technical achievement, new aims, and a restless creative spirit. The theme is an abrupt expression or redirection of conviction.

These examples neither verify the content of a claim nor guarantee a particular document or cultural event.
