## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Venus row; checked against the scan.

To stand firmly with religious and worldly convictions. To testify to them.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 458 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Venus row; checked against the German scan.

Beginning clarity in love, joyful cultural creation, harmonious spiritual striving toward goals, clear peace goals, love of spiritual leadership, artistic and cultural creation, beauty in cultural achievement, and reasonable efforts for peace.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Conviction expressed through harmony, beauty, love, or peace.
- Psychology: Firm religious or worldly conviction alongside harmonious spiritual aims.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is given.
- Social/relationship: Love, peace efforts, and testimony to shared or personal convictions.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic and cultural creation, beauty, and declaration of belief.
- Conflicts/notes: A firmly held conviction is not evidence of its truth or of guaranteed harmony.

## Contradictions

The scan corrects "artificial" to artistic and "powerful peace efforts" to reasonable peace efforts. Witte emphasizes firm testimony, while ABC gives love, art, and peace as settings without a direct contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Venus`, Venus specifies Zeus/Poseidon through creative conviction expressed in love, beauty, and peace. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes standing by and testifying to religious or worldly beliefs; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives that directed thought a harmonious artistic and cultural setting. The entries connect committed belief with forms of expression that seek beauty or concord.

Firm testimony does not prove a belief true, and the harmonious examples do not guarantee agreement in a particular relationship.
