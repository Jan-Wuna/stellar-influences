## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Vernal Point row; checked against the scan.

Mental disposition. To be under a general mental leadership. To be mentally influenced and impressed by others. The generality must witness something, must prove something or must show its conviction.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 458 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Vernal Point row; checked against the German scan.

insight of public leadership, spiritual striving toward goals in public, clear goals in public life, cultural formation in public, public attestation of truth, and creative reason in public life

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Creative thought, guidance, and testimony in public life.
- Psychology: Public conviction and receptivity to the influence of others.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical claim is stated.
- Social/relationship: Public leadership, public cultural activity, and collective witnessing.
- Events/manifestations: Public testimony, cultural creation, and the declaration or demonstration of conviction.
- Conflicts/notes: Public influence and clarity do not establish that a collective belief is true.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes public influence and the need to witness or prove; ABC emphasizes insight and cultural goals in public life. These are compatible emphases, without establishing independent corroboration.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Vernal Point`, the Vernal Point specifies Zeus/Poseidon through the public expression of thought, cultural aims, and conviction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes the public being influenced and called to witness or demonstrate its views; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds public leadership, cultural creation, and testimony. The connecting theme is conviction becoming a matter of public expression and influence.

The entries do not determine the correctness of a public belief or identify a particular event.
