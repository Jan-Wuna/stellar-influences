## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

Reference: PDF p. 296, Zeus + Poseidon, Vulcanus row; checked against the scan.

Mighty mental work. A very great mental leader. Tremendous mental compulsion, overwhelming evidence.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

Reference: printed p. 459 (PDF p. 228), Zeus + Poseidon, Vulcanus row; checked against the German scan.

Influential spiritual leadership, influential cultural achievements, fiery spiritual energy, spiritual leadership strength, spiritual shaping power, tremendous cultural efforts, creative cultural power, and truth as a creative force.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Powerful intellectual work, leadership, and cultural influence.
- Psychology: Strong conviction or mental compulsion in Witte's wording.
- Body/health: No bodily or clinical meaning is stated.
- Social/relationship: A major intellectual leader and influential cultural work.
- Events/manifestations: Large mental undertakings, cultural efforts, and overwhelming evidence in the source.
- Conflicts/notes: The source's image of overwhelming evidence is not independent verification of a claim.

## Contradictions

The ABC scan corrects repeated "filtration" renderings to leadership and leadership strength. Both entries describe magnitude of intellectual influence; that overlap does not demonstrate independent corroboration.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Poseidon = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies Zeus/Poseidon through the power of creative thought and the influence it exerts. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ranges from major intellectual work and leadership to compulsion; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds substantial cultural effort and truth as creative force. The shared theme is thought gaining concentrated capacity to direct or shape.

The page preserves the tension between accomplishment and pressure without treating mental compulsion as a diagnosis or the formula as proof of a claim.
