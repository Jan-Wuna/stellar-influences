## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

Mighty expansion and success of peaceful efforts. Successful efforts in science, commerce, trades and industry.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 457; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Expansion of a fiery force, influential scientific achievements, successful energy output, successful leadership power, successful exertion of strength, experiences of power through leadership, strong leadership success, and extensive generation of force or violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Expanding achievement and effective leadership across science, commerce and industry.
- psychology: Experience of power through leading, rather than a fuller personality description.
- body/health: No bodily or medical account is stated.
- social/relationship: Leadership and scientific, commercial, trade and industrial work.
- events/manifestations: Successful effort, increased output and expansion of a fiery force.
- conflicts/notes: Witte calls the effort peaceful; ABC's force/violence clause leaves a broader and potentially coercive branch.

## Contradictions

Witte explicitly names peaceful effort. ABC ends with umfangreiche Gewalt-Erzeugung, whose force/violence sense is not narrowed in the paragraph. That potentially coercive branch must remain beside the productive examples. The Witte scan removes the stray "“a" before commerce.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Apollon`, Apollon specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through powerful effort that expands into substantial achievement. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) locates peaceful success in science, commerce, trades and industry; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds output, leadership success and experience of power. Effective direction and expansion join the practical fields named by the entries.

ABC also includes generation of force or violence. Its sense remains open here, so the peaceful wording cannot be extended to every manifestation.
