## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

To concentrate, to center all efforts for one purpose in cooperation with others. To direct the efforts of others to one goal.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 456; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Influential local leadership, the power of fire at a place, influence of force or violence at a place, enormous efforts by partners, a partner's power goal, powerful achievements in the surroundings, the creative strength of a place, strong local leadership, and a partner's procreative power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Concentrating shared effort around one aim in a partnership or local setting.
- psychology: Purposeful direction of effort; no fuller personality account is supplied.
- body/health: ABC attributes procreative power to a partner without establishing fertility or a bodily outcome.
- social/relationship: Cooperation, partners and local leadership.
- events/manifestations: Directing others toward a goal, local achievements, and the power of fire at a place.
- conflicts/notes: Cooperation and potentially coercive local influence are distinct possibilities.

## Contradictions

Witte describes cooperation and common direction. ABC also includes local force or violence, so a common aim does not establish consent. The raw scan supports local fire power rather than "feucr administration", creative strength of a place rather than local authority, and a partner's procreative power.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Asc`, Asc specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through the concentration of effort among partners and people in a particular place. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) describes cooperation and directing others toward one purpose; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds strong local leadership, a partner's power aims and substantial achievement in the surroundings. Shared direction is the central theme, with the local setting giving it a concrete field.

ABC also names fire, force or violence, and a partner's procreative power. These remain distinct source examples; cooperation alone cannot resolve coercion, and procreative language does not predict fertility.
