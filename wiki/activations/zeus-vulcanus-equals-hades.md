## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

Great hate and enmity. Mighty efforts under difficult circumstances and ugly conditions, filth and disorder. Churned earth.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 457; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Energy service, leadership strength from the past, dangerous violent exertion, criminal generation of violence, lack of shaping power, deficient procreative power, disadvantageous energy output, severe fire danger, and hidden leadership influence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful effort under adverse conditions, with dangerous or deficient uses of energy.
- psychology: Witte names hatred and enmity as attributed source descriptions.
- body/health: ABC's deficient procreative power is a historical claim without a diagnosis or fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: Past or hidden leadership influence and criminal violence appear among the examples.
- events/manifestations: Difficult work, disorder, churned earth and fire danger.
- conflicts/notes: The examples include both service and harmful exertion; they do not establish criminality in a person.

## Contradictions

The raw ABC paragraph resolves "Energy Service Service" as energy service and the trailing "Influence/3" as influence. Witte's adverse working conditions and ABC's dangerous or deficient exertion overlap without making every form of service harmful. Leadership from the past is a separate temporal description, not a timing model.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Hades`, Hades specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through substantial effort constrained by difficult, disordered or damaging conditions. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names enmity and work amid filth and disorder, while the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds dangerous violence, reduced capacity and fire danger, alongside service and past or hidden leadership. The common concern is the burden or harmful use of power, with several distinct settings retained.

Criminal and procreative wording remains attributed to ABC; it supplies neither a verdict about a person nor a health diagnosis.
