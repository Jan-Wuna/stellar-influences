## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

Mighty successful efforts. Important increase of fortune through luck or the own efforts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 456; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

influential financial leadership, successful energy achievements, joy about leadership influence, tremendous financial achievements, tremendous legal success, powerful financial efforts, strong legal efforts, and creative financial power

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Powerful effort associated with success and increased fortune.
- psychology: Joy in leadership influence, as ABC describes it.
- body/health: Neither entry supplies a bodily or medical meaning.
- social/relationship: Financial leadership and legal efforts provide specific fields.
- events/manifestations: Substantial financial achievement, legal success, and gains through luck or effort.
- conflicts/notes: These source examples do not guarantee wealth, legal success or a return on effort.

## Contradictions

Witte allows both luck and personal effort in increased fortune; ABC details financial leadership and legal accomplishment. These are compatible examples, without establishing the cause of any actual outcome.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through forceful effort that achieves substantial results. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links success and increased fortune with either luck or one's own exertion; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives financial leadership, legal effort and financial achievement as settings. The unifying theme is effective power expressed in gains and satisfaction with influence.

The entries describe possible results without guaranteeing a financial gain or legal outcome.
