## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

To gain authority through great accomplishments. Great powers locked in battle. Great efforts of a government. Great power efforts, Taking measures to use force.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 457; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Special energy output, influence through creative independence, leadership influence of a hierarchy, enormous state efforts, superior fiery energy, state leadership influence, strong leadership authority, and important leadership strength.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Authority and government exercise power through major accomplishment and effort.
- psychology: Creative independence contributes influence in ABC's wording.
- body/health: No bodily or medical claim is supplied.
- social/relationship: Hierarchy, government and great powers are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: Gaining authority, major state undertakings, battle and measures to use force.
- conflicts/notes: Accomplishment and coercion coexist; authority does not establish legitimacy.

## Contradictions

Witte includes powers in battle and measures to use force. ABC emphasizes independence, hierarchy and state leadership. Institutional achievement cannot erase the coercive branch. The scan supports creative independence rather than "self governingness" and output rather than the narrower services.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Kronos`, Kronos specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through power exercised at the level of authority, hierarchy or government. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ties authority to great accomplishment but also names battle and preparations to use force; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds major state effort and creative independence. Substantial capacity becomes institutional direction.

The evidence leaves productive achievement and coercive action side by side. It does not establish the legitimacy or wisdom of the authority involved.
