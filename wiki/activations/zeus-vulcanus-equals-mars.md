## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

Activity in connection with machines or mechanical contraptions. Mechanical horse power.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 456; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Effortful force work, work with fiery energies, violence acts of leadership, actions of leadership power, powerful labor performances, powerful leadership activity, effective energy generation, and determined power acts

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Power expressed directly in demanding work and purposeful action.
- psychology: Determined action is named; no fuller personality account is given.
- body/health: No bodily or medical account is supplied.
- social/relationship: Leadership acts and the exercise of leadership power.
- events/manifestations: Work with machinery, mechanical horsepower, energy generation and fiery energies.
- conflicts/notes: ABC also names violent acts of leadership; machinery work alone does not imply violence.

## Contradictions

Witte gives mechanical work and horsepower as concrete manifestations. ABC extends the range to leadership actions, including violent acts. These are distinct branches, rather than a claim that all energetic work is violent.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Mars`, Mars specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through power put directly into work. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives machinery and mechanical horsepower; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) joins demanding labor, energy generation and determined leadership action. The connecting theme is the practical exertion of force toward an aim.

ABC includes violent acts within that range. This remains a particular source branch, without turning every instance of mechanical or energetic work into violence.
