## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

Mighty urge for activities. The strong-willed man who accomplishes things like a machine.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 456; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Influence of a creative soul, fiery strength of soul, my fiery energy, my leadership influence, my enormous achievements, my exertions of strength, minute of fire power, exertion of strength of soul, and strong leadership of soul.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal will and inner drive directed into substantial activity.
- psychology: A strong urge to act, leadership influence and strength of soul.
- body/health: Soul and fire are source images; no physiological account is supplied.
- social/relationship: Personal leadership influence is ABC's specific social expression.
- events/manifestations: Major achievements and exertion; Witte compares accomplishment to a machine.
- conflicts/notes: The machine comparison and male wording remain attributed; minute of fire power is unexplained.

## Contradictions

The ABC scan restores omitted personal leadership influence and strong leadership of soul, and resolves the damaged creative-soul and achievement phrases. Its literal Minute der Feuer-Kraft is not explained. Witte's machine comparison describes activity rather than a bodily mechanism.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = MC`, MC specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through personally owned drive, effort and accomplishment. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) pictures a strong-willed person acting with machine-like productivity; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives that drive the language of creative soul, personal energy and leadership influence. The common theme is concentrated inner impetus expressed as substantial work.

The source's male example, soul imagery and unexplained minute of fire power do not establish a universal gender trait, physiology or a timing rule.
