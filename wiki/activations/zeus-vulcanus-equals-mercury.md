## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

To concentrate on energetic work. Machines, power or things in fast motion. To use cutting words, to make bold statements. The direction of motion.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 456; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Demanding influence of youth, influential leadership of youth, influential linguistic creations, influential shaping of traffic or communication, conversations about power goals, shaping power of thought, enormous media achievements, and news about the power of fire.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Energy directed through thought, language and movement.
- psychology: Concentration on work and bold or cutting expression.
- body/health: No bodily or medical account is stated.
- social/relationship: Youth leadership, media and conversations about power aims.
- events/manifestations: Machines or fast motion, linguistic creation and news about fire power.
- conflicts/notes: Verkehr retains a traffic or communication range; the entry does not identify a specific event.

## Contradictions

The scan supports Sprach-Schöpfungen as linguistic creations, replacing "language recordings". Witte joins machinery and motion with cutting words; ABC adds youth, media and thought. These are different channels for directed energy. The stray opening quotation before The direction of motion is absent from the scan.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Mercury`, Mercury specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through the direction of energy in thought, speech and motion. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects concentrated work, machines and fast movement with bold or cutting statements; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds linguistic creation, youth leadership, media and discussion of power goals. Focused thought and communication provide direction to active power.

ABC also names Verkehr, which can concern traffic or communication. Its breadth and the fire-news example do not establish a particular transport or fire event.
