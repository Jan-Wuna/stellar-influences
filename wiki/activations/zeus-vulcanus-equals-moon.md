## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

Persons, the public, a people or women who concentrate all their efforts in one direction.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 456; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Influence of a strong people, influence of fiery feelings, energy of a fiery woman, a woman's leadership influence, generating emotional strength, enormous achievements of a people, exertions of strength by a woman, and powerful leadership of a people.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: People concentrate substantial effort in a common direction.
- psychology: Fiery feelings and generated emotional strength are ABC's specific emotional motifs.
- body/health: No bodily or medical account is supplied.
- social/relationship: The public, a people and women appear as historical source examples.
- events/manifestations: Collective accomplishment and strong leadership.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered examples remain attributed; they do not establish a universal female trait.

## Contradictions

The ABC scan restores feelings, emotional strength, achievements of a people and a woman's exertion, replacing the repeated generic woman-influence phrases. Both entries give collective and gendered examples, without identifying those examples as universal traits.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Moon`, Moon specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through people directing substantial effort toward one purpose. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) emphasizes concentrated public or collective activity; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds emotional strength, leadership and large achievements, with women among its examples. The connecting theme is a group's directed capacity, including the feelings and exertion that accompany it.

The historical gendered wording remains attributed to the entries and supplies no universal psychological or bodily claim.
