## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

Not to find any use for one’s energies. Not able or not obliged to do anything. Wasted energies. Useless combustion of gas.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 457; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Beginning energy weakness, influence of fire-water (alcohol), energy output of water, subtle leadership influence, leading air power, weak leadership influence, strong leadership in the air, and unknown leadership power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Power lacks a usable outlet or becomes weak, subtle or difficult to locate.
- psychology: Inability or lack of obligation to act is Witte's description; no fuller personality account is given.
- body/health: ABC names alcohol as fire-water, without a medical assessment.
- social/relationship: Leadership influence may be weak, unknown or associated with air.
- events/manifestations: Wasted energy and useless gas combustion in Witte; water output and air power in ABC.
- conflicts/notes: Water and air examples do not all mean wasted effort; the entries retain different settings.

## Contradictions

The ABC scan ends Neptune at unknown leadership power. The following energy-development, fire-power and leadership-strength phrases begin Pluto. It also restores the omitted weak leadership influence and corrects water output, rather than "energic lighting". The water and air motifs are not explained by Witte's inactivity examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Neptune`, Neptune specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through difficulty putting power to use or identifying where direction comes from. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names unused or wasted energy; the corrected [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds weakening, subtle influence and unknown leadership. Ineffective or elusive direction connects these particular motifs.

ABC separately includes alcohol, the energy output of water and air power, including strong leadership in the air. Those examples remain within its range and cannot all be reduced to failure or to a medical condition.
