## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

To be exposed to the effects of great strength or power. To be connected with military things, machines, arms, or other implements.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 456; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Beginning a strong connection, an influential connection to leadership, conveying energy output, enormous efforts within connections, a strong procreative connection, a connection to fiery energies, and mediation of leadership strength.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections bring people into contact with substantial power and directed energy.
- psychology: Exposure to power is stated; a fuller personality account is absent.
- body/health: ABC names a procreative connection without establishing fertility.
- social/relationship: Leadership links and demanding connections are explicit settings.
- events/manifestations: Contact with military things, machinery, arms or implements; conveying energy output.
- conflicts/notes: A military connection does not establish an act of violence, and a strong link does not establish consent.

## Contradictions

The ABC scan resolves "energy performance venous" as conveying energy output. Witte names exposure to power and equipment, while ABC emphasizes connections and mediation. These are related settings rather than a prediction of a particular military or reproductive event.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Node`, Node specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through connections that expose people to power or convey it onward. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives military equipment, machinery and implements; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds leadership links, large efforts within connections and mediation of strength. A connection serves as the point of contact with directed force.

ABC's procreative example stays attributed, and Witte's military setting does not establish a specific act of violence.
