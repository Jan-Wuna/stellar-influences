## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

The rise of great works (projects). The development of natural laws. Great transformations accomplished through natural powers.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 457; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Demanding energy development, unfolding fire power, developing leadership strength, greatly growing efforts, change of leadership power, growing energy output, growing power influence, and purposeful development of power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Large works and power develop through growing effort and transformation.
- psychology: Purposeful development is stated, without a fuller personality account.
- body/health: No bodily or medical account is given.
- social/relationship: Leadership power and its influence can grow or change.
- events/manifestations: Major projects, increasing energy output and transformations through natural powers.
- conflicts/notes: The phrase development of natural laws is Witte's wording, not scientific verification.

## Contradictions

The ABC scan restores the three opening clauses formerly absorbed into Neptune and joins the broken gewal-tig word to growing efforts. Leadership power replaces the incorrect "power of movement". Witte's natural-law expression and ABC's leadership development remain different settings.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through power taking shape in a developing undertaking. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names the rise of great projects and transformations through natural forces; the restored [ABC entry](#abc-entry) connects increasing effort and output with the growth or change of leadership power. Development and large-scale transformation give directed energy an expanding form.

Witte's reference to natural laws remains a source expression. It supplies no verified physical mechanism, and growth does not itself establish improvement.
