## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

Periodic work and great accomplishment. Great efforts with interruptions, changes and hindrances.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 457; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Demanding power problems, sustained energy output, influential leadership in old age, enormous concentration, hard exertions of strength, problems of leadership strength, problematic power goals, and losses through fire power.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Substantial work continues through interruptions, obstacles and demanding concentration.
- psychology: Endurance and concentration appear alongside difficult power goals.
- body/health: Old age is an ABC setting, without a disease claim.
- social/relationship: Leadership faces problems and may remain influential in old age.
- events/manifestations: Periodic work, major accomplishment, changes, hindrances and losses through fire.
- conflicts/notes: Interrupted work and sustained output coexist; difficulty does not establish total failure.

## Contradictions

The ABC scan reads ausdauernde as sustained rather than "exhausting" and Führungs-Kraft as leadership strength rather than "speeding force". Witte's interrupted work can still yield great accomplishment; the entries do not make hindrance synonymous with failure.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through power applied under limits that require endurance and concentration. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) pairs great accomplishment with periodic work, interruptions and hindrances; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds sustained output, hard exertion and leadership problems. Continued effort through difficult conditions is the common thread.

ABC also names losses through fire and leadership in old age. These remain separate examples, without making every difficulty a failure or old age a medical condition.
