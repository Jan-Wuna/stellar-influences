## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

The man who works hard at machines and apparatus, who adjusts and serves industrial machines. The man who directs natural forces.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 456; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Energy of a fiery man, an influential leadership person, an influential power center, a fiery energy center, fiery bodily energy, enormous bodily achievement, bodily procreative power, personal energy output, and strong personal leadership.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A person directs substantial energy into work and leadership.
- psychology: A purposeful personal direction is evident; a fuller personality account is absent.
- body/health: ABC attributes bodily and procreative power without establishing health or fertility.
- social/relationship: An influential leader or power center.
- events/manifestations: Working with, adjusting and servicing industrial machines; directing natural forces.
- conflicts/notes: The male and bodily examples remain source claims, not universal traits.

## Contradictions

The ABC scan resolves "lcurig" and "four" as fiery, "body person" as leadership person, and "encrusting power" as personal energy output. Witte emphasizes work with machinery; ABC names bodily power and leadership. Their different examples do not establish a medical mechanism.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Sun`, Sun specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through a person putting strong energy into work and direction. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives industrial machinery and the control of natural forces; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds personal output, leadership and bodily energy. Substantial capacity is expressed through a person's practical activity and influence.

The male and procreative examples remain attributed. They do not establish health, fertility or a universal male trait.
