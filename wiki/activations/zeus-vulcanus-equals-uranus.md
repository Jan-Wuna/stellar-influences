## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

To suddenly get busy. To apply force. Energy that is expended by jerking motions. Explosion-like appearing of great forces. Electric or atomic energies.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 457; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

Exciting fiery energies, dynamic fire power, influential leadership reform, renewal of the capacity for achievement, enormous technical achievement, powerful leadership impulses, new leadership strength, sudden fire power, and a surprising onset of force or violence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Power is released suddenly or redirected through technical change and leadership reform.
- psychology: Excitement and sudden impulses appear in ABC's wording.
- body/health: Jerking motions describe expended energy, without a clinical account.
- social/relationship: Leadership reform and new leadership strength.
- events/manifestations: Abrupt activity, explosion-like force and electric or atomic energy; technical achievement.
- conflicts/notes: These examples do not predict an explosion or a particular violent act.

## Contradictions

The Witte scan restores the break between great forces and electric or atomic energies. ABC's Leistungs-Kraft is capacity for achievement, replacing "power power". Its final Gewalt can carry a force or violence sense, which the paragraph does not narrow.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through a sudden release or redirection of substantial power. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names abrupt action, jerking motion and explosion-like force; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) adds technical achievement, renewed capacity and leadership reform. A rapid change in how energy acts is the connecting theme.

Electric, atomic and fire imagery remains in the source range. It does not establish a particular accident or violent event.
