## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

Great longing for love. Mighty will for procreation.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 456; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

fiery influence of love, shaping power of love, tremendous peace achievements, harmonious leadership power, powerful artistic striving, creative influence of beauty, creative artistic influence, and benevolence of an influential leadership

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Strong creative effort is directed toward love, art or harmony.
- psychology: Great longing for love and artistic striving.
- body/health: Witte names a will for procreation, without establishing fertility.
- social/relationship: Harmonious leadership, peace achievement and benevolent influence.
- events/manifestations: Artistic creation and the shaping influence of beauty and love.
- conflicts/notes: The will for procreation does not establish an actual reproductive outcome.

## Contradictions

Witte emphasizes longing and procreative will, while ABC adds peace, artistic work and leadership. These are different expressions of directed creative effort, without a direct contradiction or proof that a specific relationship is harmonious.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Venus`, Venus specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through strong creative power directed toward love, beauty and harmony. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names longing and the will for procreation; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) extends the range to artistic striving, peace achievement and benevolent leadership. Desire and creative influence give substantial effort an affectionate or artistic aim.

These are source possibilities, without a promise of fertility, agreement or a particular relationship outcome.
