## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 295 (one-based).

Great urge to create or accomplish something, in general.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Entry

- Source page: 456; PDF page: 227 (one-based spread).

fire force in public, leadership influence in public life, production of force in public, tremendous achievement in public life, exertion of force in public, public energy achievements, and strong public leadership

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A strong urge to create or accomplish takes a public form.
- psychology: The urge to act is named; a fuller personality account is absent.
- body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is supplied.
- social/relationship: Public leadership and visible exertion.
- events/manifestations: Large public achievements, energy output and the public force of fire.
- conflicts/notes: ABC's public force wording permits coercive possibilities without identifying a specific event.

## Contradictions

Witte states a general creative urge; ABC locates effort, leadership and power in public life. Its Gewalt-Erzeugung does not by itself settle whether force or violence is intended. General accomplishment cannot erase that ambiguity.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Vulcanus = Vernal Point`, the Vernal Point specifies Zeus/Vulcanus through the public expression of powerful creative effort. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) states an urge to accomplish; the [ABC entry](#abc-entry) gives that urge a public field of leadership, exertion and substantial achievement. Directed capacity becomes visible in collective life.

ABC also names fire and generation of force in public. Its force/violence range does not establish the character or occurrence of a particular public event.
