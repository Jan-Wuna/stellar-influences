## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 287 (one-based), Asc glyph row.

Others are near. Things or matters which are in the immediate environment of the native. To work with others, being together with others. To be under the pressure by others. Working activities in a manufacturing plant. To have something to do with fire or machines. To be the object of disputes. To be threatened. Creating with others.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creative work is shaped by others and the immediate surroundings.
- psychology: Pressure, threats and disputes appear alongside collaboration.
- body/health: No bodily or medical account is supplied.
- social/relationship: Working or creating with others, including under their pressure.
- events/manifestations: Manufacturing work and dealings with fire or machinery.
- conflicts/notes: Being together does not establish cooperation free of conflict.

## Contradictions

This single entry includes both joint creation and pressure, disputes or threats. Those alternatives remain side by side; the page supplies no second source with which to establish a doctrinal disagreement.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Asc`, Asc specifies the repeated Zeus pair through work and creation in the immediate company of others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gives manufacturing, fire and machinery as settings, while describing both collaboration and pressure. The surroundings and the people within them become the concrete field of creative activity.

The same paragraph includes disputes and threats, so proximity or shared work does not establish a harmonious relationship.
