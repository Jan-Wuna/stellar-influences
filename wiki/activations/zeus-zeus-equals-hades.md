## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Activity pauses, loses direction or meets destructive conditions.
- psychology: Lack of purpose, hatred and hostile behavior are attributed source descriptions.
- body/health: Burns are historical source examples; paralyzed and cramp are explicitly qualified as restrictions of action.
- social/relationship: Strikes, lock-outs and war atrocities are among the adverse collective settings.
- events/manifestations: Recreation and vacation coexist with industrial interruption, lack of coal and destructive fire.
- conflicts/notes: Rest and destructive action are separate possibilities; neither diagnoses a person or predicts a disaster.

## Contradictions

The baseline paragraph about starting anew and growing duties belongs to Pluto. The raw Hades row includes both recreation and forced inactivity, followed by destructive industrial, fire and war examples. Those branches cannot be flattened into either harmless rest or inevitable harm.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Hades`, Hades specifies the repeated Zeus pair through activity that pauses, loses its aim or is obstructed. The corrected [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ranges from relaxation and vacation to enforced inactivity, strikes, lock-outs and lack of coal. Its explicit qualifications make paralysis and cramp descriptions of constrained action here.

The paragraph separately includes destructive fire, burns, hatred and war atrocities. These remain attributed examples within a broad range, without turning rest into wrongdoing or predicting injury or disaster.
