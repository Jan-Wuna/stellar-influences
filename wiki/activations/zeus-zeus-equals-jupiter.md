## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 287 (one-based), Jupiter glyph row.

Successful creation. Fortunate activity. To bring good luck and benefits. Beneficial acts. To be compelled to give. Time of fortunate activity. To be fortunate in connection with machines. To produce something. Money through creativity. Successful procreation.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creative activity is described as successful, fortunate or beneficial.
- psychology: No fuller personality account is supplied.
- body/health: Successful procreation is an attributed source example, without a fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: Beneficial acts and being compelled to give.
- events/manifestations: Production, gains through creativity and fortunate dealings with machines.
- conflicts/notes: Benefit and compelled giving coexist; favorable wording does not guarantee an outcome.

## Contradictions

The baseline devoted-activity, seed and artistic-creation paragraph belongs to Venus. The Jupiter glyph row concerns successful creation and benefits, including compelled giving. That qualification remains alongside the favorable language.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Jupiter`, Jupiter specifies the repeated Zeus pair through creative activity associated with benefit and success. The corrected [Witte entry](#witte-entry) joins production and gains through creativity with fortunate dealings in machines. Making something is presented as a source of useful or favorable results.

The entry also names compelled giving and successful procreation. These remain particular source examples, without guaranteeing money, fertility or an effortless benefit.
