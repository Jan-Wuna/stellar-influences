## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 288 (one-based), Kronos glyph row.

Great and energetic activities. Energetic leadership. To assert oneself at all costs. The machine industry. Conflagrations. Warfare. Armament. The great commander-in-chief. To put oneself in the right light. To play the overlord. To lord it over others. Tremendous activity. Intervention of the authorities. Fire protection. Governmental fire insurance. Achieving a goal through competence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Large activity combines leadership, authority and the pursuit of a goal.
- psychology: Assertiveness can extend to domination and self-display.
- body/health: No bodily or medical account is supplied.
- social/relationship: Authorities, military command and government intervention.
- events/manifestations: Machine industry, armament, conflagrations, fire protection and governmental fire insurance.
- conflicts/notes: Competence and protection coexist with domination and warfare; authority is not thereby legitimized.

## Contradictions

The baseline foundation-and-marriage paragraph belongs to Cupido. The actual Kronos glyph row concerns energetic leadership, authority and major industrial or military activity. Its constructive competence and protection do not cancel the domination and warfare examples.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Kronos`, Kronos specifies the repeated Zeus pair through energetic activity organized around leadership and authority. The corrected [Witte entry](#witte-entry) ranges from competent attainment and fire protection to government intervention, industry and military command. Great effort is expressed through the capacity to direct or intervene.

The same paragraph includes self-display, domination and warfare. Those possibilities remain beside competence and protection, without establishing that authority is legitimate or beneficial.
