## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 287 (one-based), MC glyph row.

One’ own creative abilities. The thirst for action. The urge to work. Passion for work. The urge to accomplish great feats. Leadership. The want to lead. Being goal-oriented. Consciously controlled energy. The urge to create something. To assert oneself successfully. The moment (minute) of creation or fathering.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal creative ability is directed toward work, leadership and accomplishment.
- psychology: Passion for work, conscious control and the urge to lead or assert oneself.
- body/health: Fathering is a source example, without a fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: Leadership and successful self-assertion.
- events/manifestations: Creating something and pursuing major feats; a moment of creation or fathering is also named.
- conflicts/notes: The entry gives no timing procedure or guarantee of achievement.

## Contradictions

This single paragraph connects personal ability and consciously controlled energy with a strong urge to work and lead. It supplies no competing account. Its moment-of-creation phrase is retained without constructing a timing model.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = MC`, MC specifies the repeated Zeus pair through one's own creative ability consciously directed toward a goal. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links passion for work, leadership and the urge for major accomplishment with controlled energy and self-assertion. Personal drive is organized into an effort to create something.

The named moment of creation or fathering remains a source example. The paragraph supplies neither a timing procedure nor a guarantee of achievement or fertility.
