## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

- PDF page: 287 (one-based), Mercury glyph row.

Creative thinking. To command. To threaten. Dictatorial. Inventions. Thinking that aims at something. Creative inventions. An order that was obeyed. Single-minded thinking. To issue decrees or laws. To pass sentence.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Purposeful creative thought is expressed in invention or command.
- psychology: Single-minded thought can accompany threatening or dictatorial expression.
- body/health: No bodily or medical account is stated.
- social/relationship: Orders, decrees, laws and passing sentence.
- events/manifestations: Creative inventions and an order being obeyed.
- conflicts/notes: The entry retains invention and coercive command as distinct possibilities; it does not legitimate an order.

## Contradictions

The baseline union-and-fire passage is an incomplete tail of the Node row. The Mercury glyph row concerns creative thinking, invention and command. Its coercive expressions remain attributed alongside invention rather than being recast as neutral leadership.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Mercury`, Mercury specifies the repeated Zeus pair through thought directed toward an intended result. The corrected [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names creative invention and single-minded thinking, but also commands, decrees, threats and judgment. Thought becomes either an invention or a directive intended to produce action.

The coercive branch remains explicit. The paragraph does not establish the legitimacy of a command or diagnose a person's character.
