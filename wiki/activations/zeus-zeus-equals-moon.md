#### Witte Entry

A creative mind. The hours of creative work. Discipline. Hot blooded woman. The martial nation. A woman who has children. Procreative faculty of female person. Institutions of general benefit created by the state. Inflammable. A people lined up.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Creative work is organized through discipline and collective activity.
- psychology: A creative mind and discipline; hot-blooded woman is historical source wording.
- body/health: Female procreative capacity is an attributed claim, not a fertility assessment.
- social/relationship: A people, martial nation and state-created institutions of general benefit.
- events/manifestations: Creative work, organized people and public-benefit institutions.
- conflicts/notes: Gendered and inflammable wording is not a universal trait or a prediction of harm.

## Contradictions

The paragraph places disciplined creative work beside gendered procreative examples, a martial nation and public-benefit institutions. These are distinct examples from one source, not proof that all collective activity is military. The object of the bare word Inflammable is unspecified.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Moon`, Moon specifies the repeated Zeus pair through creative activity disciplined in time and organized among people. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) names a creative mind and hours of work, then gives a people lined up, a martial nation and state-created institutions of general benefit as collective examples. Organized effort connects these themes without reducing every group to a military one.

Its gendered and procreative statements remain historical source claims. The bare word Inflammable has no stated object and supplies no diagnosis or prediction.
