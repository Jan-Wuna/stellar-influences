## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Node`, Node specifies creative accomplishment through selecting connections and joining with others. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes the relationship instrumental to making or doing something together: it includes procreative union, shared work, and contacts concerned with fire, firearms, stoves, or machines. Curiosity provides one stated impulse for seeking that connection.

The [Zeus/Zeus pair summary](../axes/zeus-zeus.md#pair-summary) supplies the underlying creation and controlled activity; this activation locates it in association and selection. The brief verbs about election do not establish a particular political office or outcome, and the procreative wording gives no medical conclusion.
