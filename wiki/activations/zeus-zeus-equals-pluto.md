## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Pluto`, Pluto specifies the pair's creative accomplishment as something beginning, developing, and demanding more effort. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) repeatedly joins an increase of creative power and accomplishment with increased duties and being duty bound. Development therefore carries a practical demand to sustain and extend the work, rather than describing change in the abstract.

The [pair summary](../axes/zeus-zeus.md#pair-summary) grounds the emphasis on purposeful creation. The exact activation also names transformation in the immediate vicinity, but supplies no scale, cause, timing, or promised result for a particular undertaking.
