## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Saturn`, Saturn specifies purposeful creation through the difficulty of continuing or completing it. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) gathers interruption, separation from work or accomplishments, failed processes, and losses during production around the [pair's controlled activity](../axes/zeus-zeus.md#pair-summary). Machine breakdown and fire loss give concrete examples of that obstruction.

Perseverance remains part of the same entry, so the interpretation includes sustaining effort under pressure as well as the possibility of frustrated work. Anxiety and anguish describe the source's associated strain; they establish no clinical diagnosis. Bequeathing is an additional stated meaning whose connection to the rest is not explained.
