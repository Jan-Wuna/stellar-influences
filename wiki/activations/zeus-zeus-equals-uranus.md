## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Uranus`, Uranus specifies the [pair's controlled energy](../axes/zeus-zeus.md#pair-summary) through sudden discharge or ignition. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) connects will and surprising acts with fire, firearms, lightning, explosions, electric motors, and dynamos. These examples put the emphasis on force becoming active abruptly, whether organized in machinery or expressed as a disruptive outbreak.

The entry includes both controlled forces and acts that cannot be suppressed, so one should retain that tension rather than assume reliable control. Fatherhood is also explicitly listed but not explained by the surrounding fire imagery. No particular accident, war, or family event follows from the entry alone.
