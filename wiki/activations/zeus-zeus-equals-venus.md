## Source Entries

#### Witte Entry

Source reference: *Rules for Planetary Pictures*, `Zeus + Zeus`, Venus row, PDF page 287 (one-based; visually checked).

Devoted activity. Seed. Sperm. Procreation out of love. Increase. To seed. To procreate. To produce love. To force peace. Creative in art.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Devotion gives creative activity an affectionate, artistic, or procreative expression, with increase as an explicit theme.
- Psychology: Devoted activity and producing love are stated; the passage does not describe a full emotional temperament.
- Body/health: Seed, sperm, and procreation are historical source imagery, not a fertility test or a medical finding.
- Social/relationship: Procreation out of love and producing love are joined by forcing peace, which introduces pressure rather than assured harmony.
- Events/manifestations: Artistic creation, seeding, increase, procreation, and an effort to force peace are named.
- Conflicts/notes: Love and devotion coexist with coercive peace. The earlier relationship-and-curiosity text is part of the Node row, as confirmed in the scan.

## Contradictions

Forcing peace sits uneasily beside love and devotion, but the source supplies no further explanation. This is a retained tension within one entry; there is no independent comparison here.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Venus`, Venus specifies creative activity through devotion, love, artistic work, and the source's imagery of seeding and procreation. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) links the [pair's creation theme](../axes/zeus-zeus.md#pair-summary) to bringing love or increase into expression, rather than simply making contact with someone.

The phrase about forcing peace keeps pressure within the interpretation: a desired affectionate or peaceful result does not make the means harmonious. The reproductive terms remain historical source associations and establish no medical or fertility outcome for a person.
