## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Vernal Point`, Vernal Point specifies the [pair's creation and controlled energy](../axes/zeus-zeus.md#pair-summary) at a general or collective scale. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) unites natural forces, a general urge to activity, fire, steam machinery, and railroads: energy is made active and can be organized into material systems.

The same row also names conflagrations, wars, and war powers, so controlled use and destructive expression both belong to its scope. It offers a range of manifestations rather than a claim that a particular public event or personal circumstance must occur.
