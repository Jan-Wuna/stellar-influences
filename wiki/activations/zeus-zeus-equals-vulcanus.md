Mighty creations. Machine power. Mighty controlled and adjusted power. Horsepower. Natural forces. The force of external circumstances. Great wars. Loaded with an urge to be active. ## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Creative force is concentrated, organized, and applied with great power, including machinery and electrical energy.
- Psychology: An intense urge to be active is explicit; the entry does not establish an individual's stable temperament.
- Body/health: No bodily or medical meaning is given.
- Social/relationship: External circumstances can exert force, and great wars are listed; there is no specific partnership or family claim.
- Events/manifestations: Mighty creations, machine power, horsepower, electrical energies, wars, and extraordinary eruptions or explosions are stated examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Controlled and adjusted power appears with extreme eruptions; scale does not ensure control. The former research passage belongs to the Apollon row and was replaced from the scan.

## Contradictions

The entry retains organized force and extraordinary eruptions as different expressions of great power. With one source only, their coexistence is not evidence of independent agreement or contradiction.

## Derived Synthesis

In `Zeus/Zeus = Vulcanus`, Vulcanus specifies the [pair's directed creative energy](../axes/zeus-zeus.md#pair-summary) as concentrated power: all forces are brought to one point. The [Witte entry](#witte-entry) makes this concrete through mighty creations, horsepower, machinery, organized forces, and electrical energy. An urge to act and the pressure of external circumstances are both included.

Great wars and extraordinary eruptions or explosions extend the same theme into extreme manifestations. The source therefore supports the intensity and concentration of applied force, while giving no guarantee that a large effort will stay controlled or achieve a particular result.
