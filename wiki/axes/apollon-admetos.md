## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page: `306` (exact bold pair summary checked against actual scan; no printed folio visible).

#### Pair Summary

The feeling of satisfaction. Contentment. Satiety. To be saturated. Stagnation or standstill in trade, handicraft, commerce, industry or science. Science in a small circle, for only a few. Exclusive scientific circle. The few, the small circle. The exclusive circle.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `468`; PDF page: `233` (bold pair summary checked against actual scan).

#### ABC Pair Summary

German pair summary (transcribed from printed page `468`; PDF page `233`):

> Blockierte Wissenschaft, Erfolg durch Ausdauer, Festigung des Wissens, Freiheits-Einschränkung, gründliche Erfahrung, Handels-Beschränkung, viel Depressionen, Wissen vertiefen, Wissen unterdrücken, zunehmender Widerstand

Editorial translation from the German passage: Blocked science; success through endurance; consolidation of knowledge; restriction of freedom; thorough experience; restriction of trade; much depression; deepening knowledge; suppressing knowledge; increasing resistance.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Knowledge, trade, and wider activity become restricted, concentrated, or saturated in the [Pair Summary](#pair-summary) and [ABC Pair Summary](#abc-pair-summary).
- Psychology: Satisfaction, contentment, and satiety are explicit in the [Pair Summary](#pair-summary), alongside the limiting conditions listed elsewhere in the entry.
- Body/health: Satiety and saturation are named without a clinical account. ABC's "much depression" remains an attributed historical keyword rather than a diagnosis.
- Social/relationship: The few, small circles, and exclusive scientific circles are explicit in the [Pair Summary](#pair-summary); a general relationship style is not developed.
- Events/manifestations: Standstill in commerce, industry, handicraft, or science can coexist with consolidation and deepening of knowledge or success through perseverance in the [ABC Pair Summary](#abc-pair-summary).
- Conflicts/notes: Contentment and constraint are different emphases. ABC's thorough experience and knowledge deepening can coexist with restricted trade, suppressed knowledge and increasing resistance.

## Contradictions

The entries juxtapose satisfaction with restricted activity, while ABC also allows success through perseverance. Nothing here establishes that satisfaction must cause stagnation or that restriction always prevents achievement.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Apollon/Admetos` brings knowledge and commercial activity into a limited or concentrated field. The [Pair Summary](#pair-summary) places science within a small, exclusive circle and names standstill in trade, industry, and handicraft; it also gives satisfaction, contentment, and saturation their own place. The [ABC Pair Summary](#abc-pair-summary) adds restricted freedom or trade alongside deepened and consolidated knowledge and success through perseverance. The shared theme is a field that is narrowed, held, or brought to a point of sufficiency, with room for achievement within limits as well as for blockage.
