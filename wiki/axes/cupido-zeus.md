## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Zeus`: organized foundation: the creation of new societies, corporations, marriages, artistic forms, and coordinated bodies formed around a focused intention or activating force.
- Chart expression: communities being started, mobilized, or actively shaped rather than simply inherited. It can show founding energy, artistic invention, military or strategic organization, and the drive to turn collective intention into a concrete structure. Liabilities: premature formation, forced cohesion, destructive assertion, and the use of communal bond as an instrument of ambition, conflict, or ideological push.
