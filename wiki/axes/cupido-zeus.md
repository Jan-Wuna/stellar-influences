## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Pair Summary

Foundation of a corporation. Foundation of a new society. Marriage. Artistic creation. Military organization. Artistic activity. Appearance of a new art form. Matrimonial agent. Creator in art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Pair Summary

Leadership of the community, a leadership community, shared efforts, a shared direction, founding community, achievement oriented community, procreative community, and goal directed community

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Founding and directing a community through shared effort and a common aim ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [ABC Pair Summary](#abc-pair-summary)).
- Psychology: Goal-directed effort is named without an account of individual motives or temperament.
- Body/health: "Procreative community" is a source phrase, not a physiological or fertility finding.
- Social/relationship: Marriage, a new society, joint leadership, and communal achievement are explicit forms of association.
- Events/manifestations: Founding a corporation, military organization, artistic activity, and the appearance of a new art form are specific examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Founding, leadership, and coordinated effort overlap in the readable summaries; the quality of leadership remains unspecified. Witte PDF p. 268 is a chapter-start locator; ABC printed pp. 426-427 is a pair-spread locator.

## Derived Synthesis

`Cupido/Zeus` describes association taking shape through purposeful creation and shared direction. Founding a society or corporation, marriage, and military organization are concrete examples; the ABC summary connects them with leadership, common effort, and a common aim ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [ABC Pair Summary](#abc-pair-summary)).

Artistic activity and the appearance of a new art form extend this theme beyond formal organizations. The evidence supports founding and creating together, while leaving the quality of leadership and the group's motives unspecified ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary)).
