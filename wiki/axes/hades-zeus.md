## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Interrupted or absent purposeful activity, with a separate destructive range involving fire and force ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary)).
- Psychology: Lack of aim, idleness, hate, relaxation, and recreation are distinct examples whose relationship is not explained.
- Body/health: Burns are a historical source example. "Paralyzed" is qualified as inability to act, and "cramp" is explained metaphorically; no neurological diagnosis is established.
- Social/relationship: Strikes, lockouts, and war atrocities place part of the range in collective or industrial settings.
- Events/manifestations: Vacation, compelled inactivity, coal shortage, industrial stoppage, destructive fire, and coal fire are explicit examples.
- Conflicts/notes: Ordinary rest, constrained inactivity, dangerous aims, fire-related crime, and renunciation of procreation remain distinct source expressions; the pair lists do not explain their relationship.

## Derived Synthesis

`Hades/Zeus` concerns activity that is absent, prevented, or destructive. Inactivity ranges from relaxation and vacation to being compelled to stop or unable to pursue an aim. Industry, coal shortage, strikes, and lockouts give the interrupted-action theme concrete settings ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary)).

A separate group of examples concerns destructive fire, burns, hate, and war atrocities. The relationship between ordinary rest, constrained inactivity, and destruction is left unspecified. The ABC themes of past achievement, deficient performance, lack of leadership or purpose, and hidden leadership add emphasis, while fire-related crime and renunciation of procreation remain distinct expressions ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [ABC Pair Summary](#abc-pair-summary)).
