## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Pair Summary

Relaxation. Recreation. Vacation. Idleness and vice. No pursuing of one’s aim, lack of purposeful behavior. Prevented to act, paralyzed (in respect to action), compelled to be inactive. Cramp (to restrain or confine the action of, as with a cramp). Vileness, mean trick. Industry. Lack of coal. Strike. Lock-out. Destructive fire. Grave viciousness. Burns. War atrocities. Outbreak of hate. Burning hate. To cause a disaster. Incendiary. Coal fire.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Pair Summary

Fire criminality, leadership service, senior management, past achievements, performance deficiency, hidden guidance, and procreation waiver

## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Zeus`: blocked or corrupted action: idleness, mean tricks, strikes, lockouts, destructive fire, war atrocity, burning hate, and force that either cannot act cleanly or acts destructively.
- Chart expression: energy and will becoming degraded, hateful, or misdirected. It can describe industry under failure or shortage, rest and inactivity turning into vice, or aimed effort erupting as fire, sabotage, cruelty, and disaster rather than constructive achievement.
