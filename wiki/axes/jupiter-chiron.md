## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Principle

Instinctively knowing how to uplift others and how to dispel depression. En couraging people to proceed along their spiritual path even through difficult situations and disheartening conditions. Intuitively knowing how to give someone just the sort of encouragement that they need. Ability to instinctively synthesize and harmonize ideas and to perceive the hidden connections between different events, energies, etc. Ability to make others feel included and cared for, so that no one is left out or left behind. Abil ity to help others find prosperity and to tap in to the abundance of the universe. The healing power of excess — taking something to its ultimate extreme is a means of bringing it into a higher awareness and finer balance. James Joyce had Chiron con junct Jupiter.

#### Process

The healing of all schisms and boundaries in the process of creating complete harmony and synthesis of energies. The ability to create enthusiasm and positivity in others through assisting in their reconnection to spirit.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Chiron`: uplifting wisdom: helping others beyond discouragement, restoring hope, harmonizing what has been split, and guiding people toward abundance and spiritual reconnection.
- Chart expression: a healing force that works through generosity, inclusion, and synthesis. It can show the instinct to know exactly how to encourage, how to integrate conflicting energies, and how to make others feel cared for and not left behind. Its shadow can be overextension or trying to heal everything at once through sheer positivity.
