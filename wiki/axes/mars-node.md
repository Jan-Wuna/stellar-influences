### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `NODES + MARS`
- PDF page: `143`

#### Pair Summary

Working community. Concerted action. Betrothal. Sexual union. Erotic unions. Common action. Connected through work or activities. Joint activities.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `212`; PDF page: `105` (bold pair summary checked against actual scan).

#### ABC Pair Summary

German pair summary (transcribed from printed page `212`; PDF page `105`):

> Arbeits-Treffen, Arbeits-Vermittlung, Beziehungen aktivieren, Kontakte wollen, tatkräftige Beziehungen, Verbindungen schaffen, Verbindungs-Impulse, Vermittlungs-Tätigkeit

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Principle

Collaboration and cooperation, union based on physical attraction alone.

#### Psychological Correspondence

+ Desire to cooperate with others, comradeship and team spirit, a social conscience, desire for progeny. - Disharmonious collaboration, desire to quarrel, lack of good fellowship. C Lack of adaptability, desire to quarrel.

#### Biological Correspondence

"The activity of the astral body." A disturbance of the great life rhythms: precocity, disturbances during the climacteric years, eunuchs and eunuch-like behavior.

#### Sociological Correspondence

Associations of people working together to a common end or purpose.

#### Probable Manifestations

+ Attainment of joint or shared successes, entering into union with others. - Quarrels and disputes within communities, organizations and associations, an interruption of associations. Mars/Dragon's Head o7&

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Principle

To do things with other people and/or with group. Forming connections with people with whom one ends up having conflicts. To evolve in this lifetime a greater effectiveness in doing things, as well as releasing anger and hostility so that one is freer to act. To need to find new ways of doing things and to overcome old, worn-out and ul timately inefficient ways of doing things.

#### Process

The taking of initiative to bring needed changes into one’s life, and to evolve. The active breaking of ties with the past as well as the active seeking of new experi ences and the forming of new connections.

### Robert Hand - Horoscope Symbols

#### Pair Delineation

Involvement with workers in a common activity, either cooperative or conflicting. Involvement in sports.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MARS/NODE`
- Source page: `243`

#### Basic Ideas

The activity and emphasis you place on finding new friends or people who can help you achieve your aims in life, your insistence on having your way during meetings, and your haste and impatience with delaying tactics and bureaucracies are all found as part of this pattern. The noisy and persistent criticism you attract, the initiatives you take with any potential offered you, and the trouble and reversals you face from friends are also found here. Insistence and clamor unite with the potential or encouragement you receive thru others in a unique combination of powers which induce you to activate new friendships and associations to help you with your work and its recognition or correction. Your progress due to help or advice from others, arguments which force you to retreat and then attack, and your battles against forces which divide are found within these themes.

#### In Your Personal Life

- Thesis: Your ability to control and bend the will of others so that they work for your ideas; your persistence in following thru and pursuing your ideas during meetings; your haste to find new friends who can help you.
- Anti: Your impatience with bureaucracy and delays; your need to forge ahead with your ideas; the friends you call on for help and the stimulation of ideas which you have; your irritation with criticism received.

#### In Your Relationships

- Thesis: Enhanced needs for having the dominant sexual position in a relationship; increased drive to find new friends after you have discarded old companions; your physical strength and sexual drive in love.
- Anti: Arguments because neither person wishes to consider another’s point of view; your irritation and insistence on having only your way in a relationship; the criticism you give another because they are not you.

#### With Body or Mind

Tears and rips of the muscles or ligaments which hold any body tubes, like the intestines; energy levels which are affected by stress induced from others.

#### In Politics or Business

- Thesis: An ability to attract or to sell military might from others; the use of mercenaries; allies who send military help in times of need; energy or military hardware which is provided for by friends or treaty.
- Anti: The dispersal of military weapons or resources without safeguards or intention from others that they will pay for these; aggressive actions against the nation by hostile countries; treaties broken by war-like acts.

### Hajo Banzhaf and Anna Haebler - Keywords for Astrology

- Source section: `Mars-North Node`
- PDF page(s): `324`

#### Banzhaf/Haebler Aspect Keywords

- harmony: Bold, aggressive basic attitude that is confident of victory in mastering the true tasks in life. Works actively, hard, and successfully on one's goals. When it is important, one will fight for self and one's cause in a committed manner.
- discord: Great inner tensions and frustrations because the fighting strength, the will to conquer, as well as the driving force and the sexual desire distract from one's life priorities instead of striving toward them. Danger of creating artificial foe images and tendency toward disproportionate actions and overreactions as a result of suppressed aggressions.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Connections formed or exercised through common action; cooperation and conflict both belong to the [Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [Principle](#principle), and [Pair Delineation](#pair-delineation).
- psychology: Comradeship, team spirit, and a social conscience coexist with insistence, impatience, quarrels, and the need to change ineffective ways of doing things.
- body/health: Ebertin's "astral body," climacteric, and eunuch-related statements and Munkasey's muscle/ligament claims are attributed historical correspondences, not medical or gender conclusions about a person.
- social/relationship: Coworkers, associations, new friendships, physical attraction, and sexual union are represented. Attraction alone is one stated kind of union, not the definition of every bond on the page.
- events/manifestations: Shared success, entering or interrupting an association, joint sport, meetings, and disagreements over collective action are explicit. Military assistance and treaties are Munkasey's institutional examples.
- conflicts/notes: Banzhaf/Haebler's North Node discussion adds action in relation to life priorities; it does not replace the other entries' concrete associations. ABC explicitly names activating relationships, creating connections, meetings and mediation.

## Contradictions

- Cooperative work, physical attraction, and conflict within associations describe different forms of connection. The entries do not establish that every union rests on attraction alone or that every shared activity becomes a dispute.
- ABC's damaged English wording is preserved without confident expansion.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Mars/Node` describes doing things with other people and forming bonds through the activity itself. Work, sport, shared aims, and physical attraction bring people together in the [Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [Principle](#principle), and [Pair Delineation](#pair-delineation). Team spirit and a wish to cooperate can make that connection productive, while competing wills and lack of adaptability can interrupt it.

Taking initiative in a group also exposes one's pace and expectations to other people. Meetings, criticism, bureaucratic delays, and the search for helpful friends make this concrete in [Basic Ideas](#basic-ideas); mutual attraction and insistence on having one's own way give it an intimate expression in [In Your Relationships](#in-your-relationships). The [Process](#process) adds the possibility of leaving ineffective patterns behind and forming new connections, so conflict is part of the range rather than the only outcome.
