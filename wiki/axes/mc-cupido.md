## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- PDF page: `70`

#### Pair Summary

Family and community minded. Enjoying art. Artistic taste. One’s attitude toward the community, the family and the arts.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed source page: `84`; PDF page: `41` (bold pair summary checked against actual scan).

#### ABC Pair Summary

German pair summary (transcribed from printed page `84`; PDF page `41`):

> Beseelte Gemeinschaft, beseelte Kunst, Gemeinschaft der Seelen, ich in der Gemeinschaft, innere Gemeinschaft, meine Gemeinschaft, meine Kunst, meine Synthese, seelische Gemeinschaft

Editorial translation from the German passage: Ensouled community, ensouled art, community of souls, I in the community, inner community, my community, my art, my synthesis, psychological or soul community.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: A personal orientation toward family, community, and art ([pair summary](#pair-summary)).
- psychology: Enjoyment of art and aesthetic taste accompany a sense of belonging ([pair summary](#pair-summary), [ABC summary](#abc-pair-summary)).
- body/health: The brief pair entries provide no bodily or medical correspondence.
- social/relationship: Family feeling and one's attitude toward communal life are explicit ([pair summary](#pair-summary)).
- events/manifestations: Artistic enjoyment and participation in community are supported settings, rather than a promised event ([pair summary](#pair-summary)).
- conflicts/notes: ABC's beseelt and seelisch concern soul or inner life; the list names art and community without a Holy Spirit doctrine.

## Contradictions

The two pair summaries connect personal belonging with community and art. ABC's ensouled art and community of souls are source-native expressions of inner life; they do not introduce a distinct Holy Spirit claim.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`MC/Cupido` concerns one's personal relationship to belonging and shared culture: feeling part of a family or community, enjoying art, and forming an aesthetic taste. The [pair summary](#pair-summary) links these interests through the individual's attitude toward communal and artistic life. The [ABC summary](#abc-pair-summary) reinforces this personal and inward connection through ensouled art, community of souls, and one's own art and synthesis.
