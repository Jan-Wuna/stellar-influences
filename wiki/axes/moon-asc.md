## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `ASCENDANT + MOON`
- PDF page (one-based): `81` (exact pair passage checked in the raw PDF text).

#### Pair Summary

Acquaintances with female persons. The people of the closer surroundings. To be brought into public by others. Female acquaintances. Women in the environment. Quality of the hour at this place.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed pages: `102–103`; PDF page (one-based): `50` (bold pair summary checked against the scan).

#### ABC Pair Summary

Other women, other peoples, woman as partner, an emotionally responsive environment, an emotionally responsive partner, near the mother, place of the woman, mother of others, hour at the place, environment of the mother.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Principle

Being impressionable to the energies of other people. Difficulty in being ob jective about oneself. One’s moods strongly affect one’s identity. The need for intimate relationships and for a sense of security when one relates. Often, though, a feeling of insecurity when one interacts with others. The need to stay in the moment when relating.

#### Process

The ability to become more and more aware of all one’s emotions until one no longer has any repressed feelings, and then eventually to become free of identifying with any of one’s emotions so that one may experience the eternal nature of the self.

### Robert Hand - Horoscope Symbols

#### Pair Delineation

Close relationships with women, close emotional relationships, close relationships with people whom one has known for a long time. Habits in relationships. Domestic relationships. Expression of emotions to or from others in the immediate environment.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `MOON/ASCENDANT`
- Source page: `139`

#### Basic Ideas

This combination represents your potential as a protective and sensitive person aware of their immediate surroundings and the roles different people play among each other. It mirrors your perception of the people’s personal styles, and fosters a sensitivity to the dispositions people have. A sentimental inner focus may cause you to nurture and cherish the attention you receive. You may receive subtle impressions from others, become more aware of the inner and personal fears from elements or people around you, have others see you as a somewhat fickle person, or show an inner restlessness which is activated by changes or alterations in your daily routine. An element of moodiness may affect the way you approach life, and you may vacillate as you try to address the cares and concerns of others. Your intuition and non-sensory hunches are accented, and you may learn to pay attention to them.

#### In Your Personal Life

- Thesis: A sensitive and caring person who is quick to notice the needs and care demanded by others; sensitivity to the reality of another’s psychological space; you cherish the attention you receive and are sensitive to others.
- Anti: Others get the impression that you do not give the compassion you should; changing or altering your views of others more than is needed; secret and irrational fears brought into focus; personal insecurity noted.

#### In Your Relationships

- Thesis: You become more aware of your partner’s moods and need for personal space; you seek out others who are receptive to the attention and personal concern you give; attention to your partner’s demands.
- Anti: Moody indecision about relationship choices available to you; a crabby crankiness to your general disposition which makes it difficult for others to give you the kind of comfort and support you wish to have.

#### With Body or Mind

Sinus sensitivities or infections; emotions held in check due to social or environmental pressures; sensitivity to dust or other allergens; emotional states.

#### In Politics or Business

- Thesis: How others view the disposition of the population; the effect that the women have in forming opinion or determining priorities; production goals met by workers; emphasis on immediate needs and quotas.
- Anti: Causes for concern raised on behalf of the common people; an environmental study which affects the welfare of all; problems with the production of goods as seen by allies; raising of environmental causes.

### Hajo Banzhaf and Anna Haebler - Keywords for Astrology

- Source section: `Moon-Ascendant`
- PDF page(s): `300`

#### Banzhaf/Haebler Aspect Keywords

- harmony: The Obliging Individual. Sensitive, imaginative, impressionable, cautious, usually reacts, tends to have a feminine form of expression. Willingness to adapt and be compliant.
- discord: The Touchy Person. Difficulties in showing feelings and putting one's own wishes into practice. Emotionally blocked in the manner of expression. At the same time, moody and touchy toward others. Tends to get involved in misunderstandings.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Emotional exchange with nearby people and surroundings, especially familiar, domestic or female relationships.
- psychology: Receptivity, care and sensitivity to moods range into insecurity, touchiness and identifying too strongly with passing feelings.
- body/health: Munkasey alone here names sinus, dust and allergy sensitivities; these remain attributed associations, not diagnoses from the pair.
- social/relationship: Familiar ties, emotional habits and a need for security shape contact; sensitivity to another's mood or space can help or unsettle relating.
- events/manifestations: Female acquaintances, being brought into public by others, local emotional atmosphere and collective needs appear alongside private domestic exchange.
- conflicts/notes: Sandbach develops emotional awareness toward freedom from emotional identification; Banzhaf/Haebler distinguish compliant from blocked expression. Adjacent Moon/MC text was removed from their entry after scan-confirming the boundary.

## Derived Synthesis

`Moon/Asc` concerns the exchange of feeling with the people and atmosphere nearest at hand. Contact can be familiar, domestic and protective, shaped by habits of responding and a need to feel secure. [Hand](#robert-hand---horoscope-symbols) emphasizes longstanding emotional relationships and expression to or from the immediate environment; [Munkasey](#michael-munkasey---midpoints-unleashing-the-power-of-the-planets) connects care and receptivity with awareness of other people's moods and psychological space. The same impressionability can become insecurity, moodiness or a reaction that makes it hard to see oneself objectively. [Sandbach](#john-sandbach---midpoints-a-kabbalistic-compendium-of-meanings-for-astrological-midpoints) develops this tension as a process of recognizing emotions without making each passing feeling one's identity.

The [Witte](#alfred-witte-ludwig-rudolph--hermann-lefeldt---rules-for-planetary-pictures) and [ABC](#udo-rudolph---abc-for-planetary-pictures) summaries retain concrete female acquaintances, partners, mothers and the quality of a particular place or hour. Those motifs give the pair local and public expressions as well as private ones, including being brought into public by others. [Banzhaf and Haebler](#hajo-banzhaf-and-anna-haebler---keywords-for-astrology) distinguish a responsive, accommodating manner from blocked expression and touchiness; accommodation is not guaranteed. Munkasey's bodily associations remain specific to his entry. Neither the gendered examples nor the pair as a whole determines a relationship role or medical condition; particular closing factors have separate [activation pages](#related-activations).
