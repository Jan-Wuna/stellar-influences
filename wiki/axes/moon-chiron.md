## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

#### Principle

Allowing oneself to be guided by instinct, or, when one does not do this, suffering due to ones inability to act on one’s instincts. Transcending the dramas of family and learning to love family without being controlled or negatively influenced by them. The quest for and finding of the spiritual family. Letting go of the past. Seeing the past in a spiritual light, which helps to release regrets. Because one has difficulty in finding security in outer and material things, one is guided toward finding security in identification with the greater self and the protection coming from higher forces. The ability to move through emotions, especially those that have become stagnant and fixated. The Chiron/Moon combination can mean the clearing of the emotional body, and the exorcising of the ghosts of the past. Learning to find security in fluidity rather than rigidity.

#### Process

The cleansing of emotions through allowing feelings to flow freely.

## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Chiron` is the axis of instinctive emotional healing, family memory, the search for spiritual family, and the cleansing of the emotional body by letting feelings move.
- Constructively it helps the native trust instinct, release regret, see the past in a spiritual light, and find security through fluidity rather than rigid attachment.
- The shadow is suffering from ignored instincts, stagnant feeling, family drama that controls the present, or the inability to feel safe in outer or material supports.
- Activations show what helps the clearing process: Mars acts, Mercury names it, Venus softens it, Saturn slows it, and the angles bring it into lived contact.
