## Source Entries

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Principle

The supernatural.

#### Psychological Correspondence

+ An intensified and purified soul life, high degree of sensitivity, highly active imagination and fantasy, illusions, clairvoyant visions and second sight, inclination to delve into unusual and supernatural phenomena, love of mysticism, self-knowledge (by inner realization of truth). - Confused and hazy interpretation of soul experiences, a chimera or whim, peculiar states of soul experience, juggling or illusory tricks, falsehood, fraud. C Tendency to concentrate on peculiar or strange aims and ideas, wavering between fantastic and mystical inclination, presentiments.

#### Biological Correspondence

The treacherously painless stage of disease. Lack of incentive or willpower to maintain health. ("Throwing up the game or the sponge.") Disturbances or troubles of slow or lingering development. Gradual commencement of a therapy.

#### Sociological Correspondence

Peculiar people, mystics, mediums, occultists, people engaged in psychical research.

#### Probable Manifestations

+ Tendency to occupy oneself with unusual problems, pursuit of peculiar hobbies and aspirations, a progressive, spiritual evolution. - Peculiar states of soul experience, pursuit of fantastic ideas, self-torment, manias, craving for alcohol, nicotine, etc. Obsession, confusion, grievous loss.
