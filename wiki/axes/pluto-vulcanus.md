## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Pair Summary

Mighty changes or transformations that occurs with great speed. Unhealthy, rushed development. The changes (growth) inside the greenhouse. Quick transformation. Mighty change in circumstances. Transformations and developments which are too rapid, too swift in their course.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Pair Summary

Influenced changes, energy development, energy transformation, development of violence, violent change, change of power, powerful development 1 Developing strong, growing force, and changing influence
