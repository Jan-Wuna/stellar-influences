## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: mental and spiritual activity expressed through ideas, cognition, understanding, and worldview ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary)).
- psychology: deep thinking and mental power are explicit, but the entry does not establish a personality profile or an emotional liability.
- body/health: no bodily function or medical condition is given; “water masses” and “floods” are not identified as bodily metaphors.
- social/relationship: education and culture are named settings; interpersonal relationships are not directly delineated.
- events/manifestations: water masses and floods are explicit, uncommon physical associations alongside the intellectual vocabulary; their conditions are not explained.
- conflicts/notes: this is one repeated-factor source entry. It does not explain how its water associations relate to its mental/spiritual terms, and that gap should not be filled by invention.

## Derived Synthesis

`Poseidon/Poseidon` gathers the [source's mental and spiritual vocabulary](#pair-summary): ideas become objects of cognition, understanding, and deep thought, with education, culture, and worldview as named settings. This repeated-factor entry centers on the work and content of understanding without specifying a personality type, a superior level of knowledge, or a universal spiritual outcome.

The same summary explicitly names water masses and floods. Those uncommon physical associations must remain visible; the entry gives no reason to convert them into metaphors for thought or to predict a flood. It supplies no medical delineation or generic constructive/shadow pattern, and the linked activations retain their own third-factor meanings.
