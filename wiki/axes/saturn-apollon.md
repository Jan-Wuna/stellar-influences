## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: methodical learning and organization alongside limits to expansion ([pair summary](#pair-summary)).
- psychology: patient concentration, increasing seriousness, and mature knowledge; success is associated with patience rather than speed.
- body/health: ABC names experience of pain; neither pair entry establishes a specific bodily condition or medical mechanism.
- social/relationship: teacher–student relations, training, and separation from many people.
- events/manifestations: scientific teaching, trade restrictions, loss of freedom, and problems of science or expansion.
- conflicts/notes: The complete ABC scan distinguishes loss of freedom, trade losses, expansion of problems, problems of science, and experience of pain; these are separate clauses rather than a single causal claim.

## Derived Synthesis

`Saturn/Apollon` joins the patient acquisition and ordering of knowledge with the experience of limits. Systems, method, education, scientific teaching, and training give the [pair summary](#pair-summary) a concrete organizing theme; the [ABC entry's](#abc-pair-summary) references to concentration, mature knowledge, and success through patience extend that theme into learning over time.

The same pair includes restricted trade, constrained expansion, loss of freedom, and separation from many people. These are part of its range, not evidence that every sustained course of study ends in loss or success. ABC also preserves old knowledge and experience of pain as specific source motifs; particular third-factor outcomes remain on their activation pages.
