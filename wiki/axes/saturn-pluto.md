### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Principle

Hard labor, cruelty.

#### Psychological Correspondence

+ Tenacity and toughness, endurance, capability to make record efforts of the highest possible order, ability to perform the roost difficult work with extreme self-discipline, self-denial and renunciation. Magicians and adepts. - A hard and unfeeling disposition, cold-hearted, severe, tendency to violence, fanatical adherence to one's principles once they have been adopted. A martyr. C Violence. (Mass murderer.)

#### Biological Correspondence

Organic underdevelopment (absence of testicle descensus, infantile organs, organic hardening or calcification processes). The following medical observation has been found: In the case of a lithopaedion (i.e., dried up or calcified foetus within the maternal body) the mother had the following mid-point configurations: Saturn/Pluto = Mars/Jupiter = Sun/MC, according to the practitioner, Dr. M.

#### Sociological Correspondence

Deeply searching scientists, reactionaries, violent people.
