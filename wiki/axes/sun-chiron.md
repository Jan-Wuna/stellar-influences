## Source Entries

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source page: PDF pp. 15–16.
- Editorial note: actual internal word splits are joined for reading. The printed form `ones importance` is retained.

#### Principle

Letting go of all things of lesser importance and focusing on what is most important in spiritual terms. Ability to clearly differentiate between what is truly important to oneself in terms of one’s personal evolution and what the world/culture/social milieu deems important. The process of ceasing to base one’s self-worth on outer or superficial criteria and to perceive ones importance as a being assisting in world change and the spiritual process of others. Allowing the deeper meanings of things to emerge by not placing the projections of one’s ego on them. One has the ability to assist others in the discovery of their life’s meaning and purpose. Bringing to light those ideas and energies which are exactly what is needed to heal those around one, and often doing this without consciously knowing it.

#### Process

The opening up to deeper and more expanded realizations of the meaning and purpose of everything one encounters. The healing of the ego of both oneself and others through clearing away what is meaningless so that the true meaning and purpose behind reality can be clearly perceived.

## Comparative Schema

## Derived Synthesis

In Sandbach's account, `Sun/Chiron` centers on discovering what gives life meaning after superficial measures of importance fall away. The [Principle](#principle) connects this with releasing social judgments of worth and reducing the ego's projections; the [Process](#process) describes clearing away what is meaningless so that purpose can become more apparent. The emphasis is a change in how value is recognized, rather than a promise of status or recognition.

Helping others discover their purpose, including assistance offered without consciously recognizing it, is an explicit part of this spiritual account. Its language of healing the ego and bringing forward healing energies remains attributed to Sandbach; it establishes neither a bodily diagnosis nor a clinical result. The source does not supply a general negative counterpart, and the linked activations require their own oriented evidence.
