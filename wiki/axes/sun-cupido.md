## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Pair Summary

- Source page: PDF p. 111.

Member of family, association, community or a group. Brothers. The bridegroom. The married man. Father-in-law. The artist or association of arts. A community of bodies. Family meetings. The family man. The wedding day. Meeting days. Club session. The man who spends much time in the company of others. The social. An object of art.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Pair Summary

- Source page: printed pp. 158–159; PDF p. 78.

Center of community, bodily community, center of art, man of art, community of men, the person as a whole, human community, community of persons, and day of community

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal belonging in a family, association, community, or artistic circle; both entries place the person within a larger human whole. ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [ABC Pair Summary](#abc-pair-summary))
- psychology: A social orientation and spending time in company are explicit; the entries do not establish a separate pattern of inner conflict. ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary))
- body/health: A community of bodies or bodily togetherness is named, without a specific illness or health claim. ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [ABC Pair Summary](#abc-pair-summary))
- social/relationship: Family membership, siblings, marriage, and associations are prominent. Bridegroom, married man, and father-in-law are roles in the source's gendered vocabulary. ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary))
- events/manifestations: Weddings, family meetings, club sessions, artistic associations, and objects of art give the pair a concrete social and cultural range. ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary))
- conflicts/notes: The entries overlap around community and art without supplying a negative counterpart. A gathering or role does not specify an activation by a third factor.

## Contradictions

The two summaries share a community theme, with Witte's entry giving more concrete family roles, gatherings, and art objects. Their overlap does not establish independent corroboration, and neither entry sets out an opposing interpretation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Cupido` concerns the person as a participant in a human whole: family member, partner, club member, or person connected with art. Belonging is concrete here, expressed through bodily company, shared gatherings, and the social roles through which people recognize one another. The artistic range includes both the artist or association of arts and an actual art object; it is broader than family life alone. ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [ABC Pair Summary](#abc-pair-summary))

The pair therefore supports a reading of personal life organized around community and cultural participation. It does not, in these entries, establish a particular conflict, illness, or adverse outcome. The older male family-role labels remain source examples rather than limits on who can express the pair, and the listed gatherings do not establish a timing method or the meaning of a third-factor activation. ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary))
