Physical transformation, development and change. To grow. The circulation of the fluids in the body. The physical assimilation of nourishment. The regeneration capacity of the body. ### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source page: printed p. 101; PDF p. 103.

#### Principle

Striving for power, craving for rulership, power of attainment.

#### Psychological Correspondence

+ Striving for power by means of physical strength or mental energy, creative power, consciousness of aim or objective, an appreciation of innovations, qualities of leadership. - Craving for rulership, fanatical aspirations or tendencies, over-estimation of self, arrogance. C Desire to exercise power, craving for rulership and disadvantages through the former.

#### Biological Correspondence

Regeneration of the cells. Swellings.

#### Sociological Correspondence

Pioneers, fighters, persons accustomed to rule others (martyrs of their own ideas).

#### Probable Manifestations

+ Sudden advancement in life, ability to establish and consolidate one's own position as a leader, realization of new ideas. - Physical suffering, martyrdom. Danger to life, separation by providence.

### John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints

- Source page: PDF p. 20; complete Principle and Process. The actual internal split `block ing` is joined editorially.

#### Principle

A relentless craving for discovering the deepest meanings in life. Striving for personal power, be it mental, physical, or spiritual. Obsessiveness where goals are concerned. The transformation of the ego into a higher spiritual state, so that it may reflect the Divine Light. At worst, an intense desire for objectivity results in emotional repression.

#### Process

The intensification of ego pressures to the point that their limiting and blocking qualities are eradicated so that the Spiritual Light may shine forth. Discovery of the true meaning of life beyond all limited, materialistic and outward considerations.

### Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets

- Source heading: `SUN/PLUTO`
- Source page: `87`; PDF page `88`, complete pair entry. The printed compound `self-corruption` is restored from the line break.

#### Basic Ideas

Your fundamental urge to exert control over all situations, your capacity or determination to defeat or ally with corrupt forces as they rise around you, and the mastery you show against self-corruption are prominent with this combination. Your commitment to concentrating yourself into the essence of what you are as a person, the secret motivations you rally your inner strength around, and the obsession you have with increasing your self-confidence are also here. You appear as a person who has a deep and formidable willpower, and one who is not easily swayed from important decisions about where your life is going. You may appear to others to have an easygoing outer facade, but inwardly you are a tower of strength and determination. The strength and resolution you bring thru these motifs to effect what you want in life is immense. You do not give up easily against anything.

#### In Your Personal Life

- Thesis: The force of will and determination you bring toward achieving your objectives; the unswerving reliability you offer to others; keeping secrets under all situations; the success you achieve using threats.
- Anti: Ruthless pursuit of your goals; focusing on activities which enable the accumulation of power; punishment for those who cannot perform their tasks; squandering your will by giving up on your principles.

#### In Your Relationships

- Thesis: You need to express your deeply felt emotions to a partner who also responds in kind; a deep need for truth between people; expressions of loyalty or respect and how these are reciprocated by your partner.
- Anti: Deep feelings of hurt when another has betrayed your love or trust; an inability to find another who can respond with intense emotions; your need to dominate another person thru the use of love or sex.

#### With Body or Mind

The role of the colon in maintaining your overall health; susceptibility to any infections caused by toxic conditions in the body or environment.

#### In Politics or Business

- Thesis: Changes of policy or direction from the leadership; new leadership and the changing of policy and direction; the governing authority used to control threatened dissent or subversion despite its cause or sources.
- Anti: Accumulation of an excessive amount of armed strength; the use of secret police to exert authority and control over others; the influence of criminal organizations; extremes of corruption which waste resources.

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Personal transformation and regeneration together with the will to exert power or bring about substantial change. ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [Ebertin](#principle), [Hand](#pair-delineation))
- psychology: Concentration, deep self-examination, and determination can become obsessive goals, emotional repression, or an uncompromising need for control. ([Falis](#falis-entry), [Sandbach](#john-sandbach---midpoints-a-kabbalistic-compendium-of-meanings-for-astrological-midpoints), [Munkasey, source p. 87](#basic-ideas))
- body/health: Growth and physical transformation appear in the pair summaries. Witte's organ and nourishment claims, Ebertin's cell-regeneration and swelling claims, and Munkasey's colon and infection claims remain historical correspondences. ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [Ebertin](#biological-correspondence), [Munkasey](#with-body-or-mind))
- social/relationship: Leadership, powerful alliances, loyalty, and emotional truth coexist with coercion, betrayal, and attempts to dominate through intimacy. ([Ebertin](#sociological-correspondence), [Falis](#falis-entry), [Munkasey](#in-your-relationships))
- events/manifestations: Changed living conditions, personnel changes, realization of new ideas, consolidated leadership, and policy changes are represented alongside destructive control and corruption. ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [ABC summary](#abc-pair-summary), [Ebertin](#probable-manifestations), [Munkasey](#in-politics-or-business))
- conflicts/notes: Physical development and the pursuit of power are different emphases within the admitted range. Munkasey's thesis includes success through threats, and Banzhaf/Haebler's harmony permits constructive or destructive power; favourable labels do not make coercion benign. ([Munkasey](#in-your-personal-life), [Banzhaf/Haebler, PDF pp. 287–288](#banzhafhaebler-aspect-keywords))

## Contradictions

Witte and the ABC emphasize development and physical change, while several other entries give the pursuit and experience of power greater weight. These emphases should remain visible rather than making every development an ordeal. Renewal and destruction both belong to Hand's delineation; Munkasey's thesis/anti and Banzhaf/Haebler's harmony/discord headings are source categories rather than simple ethical verdicts. ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [ABC Pair Summary](#abc-pair-summary), [Hand](#pair-delineation), [Munkasey](#in-your-personal-life), [Banzhaf/Haebler](#banzhafhaebler-aspect-keywords))

## Derived Synthesis

`Sun/Pluto` concerns the power to transform oneself or one's circumstances and the experience of forces that reshape personal life. Growth, physical development, and changed living conditions belong to the range alongside concentrated will, leadership, and the search for deeper meaning. Determination can sustain self-examination and renewal, while an intense purpose can make a person unusually effective or difficult to sway. ([Pair Summary](#pair-summary), [ABC Pair Summary](#abc-pair-summary), [Hand](#pair-delineation), [Munkasey, source p. 87](#basic-ideas))

The same concentration can harden into obsession, secrecy, ruthless pursuit of an aim, or the need to keep control of another person. Emotional truth and loyalty are also present, especially where intimate feelings can be met and reciprocated rather than used as instruments of power. The pair therefore spans regeneration, development, and destructive control; favourable source labels do not make every effective use of power beneficial. Crisis and bodily danger are attributed possibilities in particular entries, not necessary outcomes or medical conclusions. ([Falis](#falis-entry), [Sandbach](#john-sandbach---midpoints-a-kabbalistic-compendium-of-meanings-for-astrological-midpoints), [Munkasey's relationship entries](#in-your-relationships), [Banzhaf/Haebler, PDF pp. 287–288](#banzhafhaebler-aspect-keywords))
