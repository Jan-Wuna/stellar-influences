## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Pair Summary

- PDF page: 185.

Main formula for the great helping hand. Charitable love. Benevolence. High feelings, great love and capability. Affection. Fondness. Welfare departments. Help through the state. Mother-love. Unselfish love. Charity. Alms. Art critic. Ordered beautifications. Decorations with flags.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `Venus + Kronos`
- Printed pages: `288-289`; PDF page: `143` (pair spread).
- German pair summary checked; its existing translation is retained.

#### ABC Pair Summary

Special harmony, dominance of beauty, great joy, great love, artistic authority, love of hierarchy, state peace, priority of the female principle, and benevolence of the boss

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Affection and beauty expressed through benevolence, distinction or authority ([pair summary](#pair-summary), [ABC summary](#abc-pair-summary)).
- psychology: High feeling, unselfish affection and great love; Udo also names love of hierarchy and the prominence of beauty.
- body/health: Neither pair summary supplies a bodily or medical claim.
- social/relationship: Charity and helping others extend from personal affection to welfare departments, state assistance and a superior's goodwill.
- events/manifestations: Welfare, alms, state peace, artistic authority, art criticism and officially ordered beautification or flag decoration.
- conflicts/notes: Charitable help and hierarchy are both explicit emphases; authority does not by itself establish benevolent conduct. "Mother-love" and the "female principle" retain the sources' gendered framing.

## Derived Synthesis

`Venus/Kronos` gives affection a helping or elevating role. Great love, benevolence and unselfish care can extend into charity, welfare and assistance through the state. The pair therefore includes both personal warmth and help made effective through a position or institution. Witte's mother-love and Udo's priority of the female principle retain the entries' explicit gendered emphasis ([pair summary](#pair-summary), [ABC summary](#abc-pair-summary)).

Beauty also acquires standing: artistic authority, art criticism and ordered beautification sit beside the prominence of beauty and a superior's goodwill; state peace is another explicit public expression. Udo's love of hierarchy and Witte's charitable love are distinct emphases within this range; the pair entries do not establish that status invariably brings generosity ([pair summary](#pair-summary), [ABC summary](#abc-pair-summary)). Each closing factor requires its own oriented activation.
