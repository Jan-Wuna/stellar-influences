## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

- Source heading: `ARIES + HADES`
- PDF page: `49`

#### Pair Summary

General poverty, misfortune, tribulations, plagues. Coal mines. Worldwide disease. Diseases in general. Something is hidden for general public. Contaminated areas of the earth. Public service. General health system. Catastrophes.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

- Printed pages: `46–47`; PDF page: `22`

#### ABC Pair Summary

Deficiencies in public, disadvantages in public life, public danger, public crime, public service, public renunciation, hidden in public, a polluted public sphere.

German pair summary checked: “Mangel in der Öffentlichkeit, Nachteile im öffentlichen Leben, öffentliche Gefahr, öffentliche Kriminalität, öffentlicher Dienst, öffentlicher Verzicht, verborgen in der Öffentlichkeit, verschmutzte Öffentlichkeit”.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Public deprivation, danger, contamination, and hidden conditions recur, with public crime and renunciation also named by ABC.
- Psychology: These entries concern collective conditions and give no specific individual psychological profile.
- Body/health: Witte's pair summary historically associates the pair with plagues, worldwide disease, and the general health system. These are attributed associations, not a diagnosis or forecast.
- Social/relationship: Public service, disadvantage, renunciation, and what is hidden from the general public place the pair within social conditions and provision.
- Events/manifestations: Poverty, contaminated areas, catastrophes, and coal mines are explicit examples in the pair summary.
- Conflicts/notes: Public service and the health system are distinct applications beside crime, deprivation or contamination; they do not imply that service is harmful. These public motifs are not personal accusations or disaster predictions.

## Contradictions

Public service and the health system coexist with deprivation and contamination. They are distinct applications, not proof that every service is harmful or that every instance produces a catastrophe; ABC also names public crime and renunciation.

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

Vernal Point/Hades brings attention to deprivation, contamination, and hidden difficulties in the public sphere. Poverty, disadvantage, and things concealed from general view connect the entries; public danger, crime and renunciation extend that range to threatening conditions and giving something up. The scale is collective, so these motifs do not amount to an accusation or a complete account of an individual's character ([Witte](#pair-summary), [ABC](#abc-pair-summary)).

The pair summary also names public service, the general health system, and coal mines, preserving institutional and material applications beyond misfortune alone. Its disease and catastrophe language remains an attributed historical association; the pair does not by itself diagnose illness or establish that a particular disaster will occur.
