## Source Entries

### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Pair Summary

Source reference: PDF page `291`, chapter heading `ZEUS + APOLLON`.

To do research. To experiment. To learn, to study, to collect experience. Natural science. Creative art and science. Modern science. Trials. Practical research. Trade with machines.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Pair Summary

Source reference: printed page `452`, PDF page `225`, bold pair summary.

Source wording: "Erfolgreiche Zielsetzung, Feuer-Ausbreitung, Führungs-Erfahrungen, gewerbliche Leistungen, Handels-Ziele, Leistungs-Erfolge, Massen-Erzeugung, schöpferisches Wissen, Zeugungs-Erfolge, Ziele der Wissenschaft, zunehmende Anstrengungen".

Editorial translation, checked against the scan: Successful goal-setting; spread of fire; leadership experience; commercial or industrial achievements; trade goals; successful performance; mass production; creative knowledge; procreative success; scientific goals; increasing efforts.

## Comparative Schema

- Core meaning: Research, experimentation, learning, and the application of knowledge in creative or practical work.
- Psychology: Studying, collecting experience, and testing through trials; the entries do not establish a general personality type.
- Body/health: ABC names procreative success as a source meaning, but gives no bodily condition or clinical assessment.
- Social/relationship: Commercial and trade activity is represented, especially trade with machines; no private relationship pattern is specified.
- Events/manifestations: Practical research, natural and modern science, creative art, machine trade, mass production, and the ABC entry's fire propagation.
- Conflicts/notes: Productive or successful results are part of ABC's vocabulary, not a guarantee for every experiment. Leadership experience and commercial achievement replace the damaged cached phrases about filtration and investigations.

## Contradictions

Witte centers on inquiry and experience, while ABC extends into production and commercial or scientific aims. That is a difference of emphasis, not an explicit contradiction. The ABC summary is now a labeled translation checked against printed page `452`; its opening concerns successful goal-setting, not "abundant circling".

<a id="derived-synthesis"></a>
## Derived Synthesis

`Zeus/Apollon` links learning and experimentation with creative or practical results. The [pair summary](#pair-summary) emphasizes research, trials, study, and accumulated experience, including natural and modern science, creative art, and trade with machines. The [ABC pair summary](#abc-pair-summary) extends the productive range into scientific and trade goals, leadership experience, creative knowledge, successful performance, and mass production.

This is a pair about developing and applying knowledge, with both inquiry and production represented. ABC also preserves the spread of fire and procreative success as additional meanings; neither should be silently converted into a generic success claim. The entries describe successful expressions without promising that every trial will succeed.
