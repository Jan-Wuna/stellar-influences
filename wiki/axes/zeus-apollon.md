### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Pair Summary

To do research. To experiment. To learn, to study, to collect experience. Natural science. Creative art and science. Modern science. Trials. Practical research. Trade with machines.

### Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures

#### ABC Pair Summary

## Derived Synthesis

- This axis combines purposeful action with Apollonian breadth, yielding applied research, experimentation, and organized learning, especially where technical skill, science, or machines are involved.
- Constructively this axis supports coherent integration; the shadow is overreach, rigidity, or internal split that undermines follow-through.
