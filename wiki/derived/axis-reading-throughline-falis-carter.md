## Analysis

### The Dual Reading Surface

Within the four inspected examples, two labels describe the presentation. Falis supplies associative reach. Carter supplies tonal discrimination. Reach means the range of possible images placed beside one another; tonal discrimination means explaining how expressions may differ with aspect family and other conditions. This is an editorial way to read the preserved entries, not a ranking of their authority.

### What Each Source Adds

On [Mercury/Venus](../axes/mercury-venus.md#michelle-falis---planet-combinations-astrological-brainstorms), Falis ranges from pleasing speech to flattery and manipulation. [Carter's entry](../axes/mercury-venus.md#charles-carter---the-astrological-aspects) also discusses charm, but qualifies its force and distinguishes the contacts he considers. On [Venus/Neptune](../axes/venus-neptune.md#charles-carter---the-astrological-aspects), Carter allows artistic achievement under both harmonious and inharmonious aspects. His distinctions therefore cannot be reduced to a simple good/bad scale.

## Contradictions and Notes

- Carter is an aspect source placed beside midpoint material by editorial normalization. An unordered axis identity does not establish which of his aspect conditions applies.
- The inspected examples show overlapping themes and different ways of presenting them. They do not establish agreement on every claim or across all shared axes.

## Derived Synthesis

Read the shared entries by finding the pair's recurring theme, then keeping the range of expressions attached to the conditions actually stated. For example, both [Falis](../axes/sun-moon.md#michelle-falis---planet-combinations-astrological-brainstorms) and [Carter](../axes/sun-moon.md#charles-carter---the-astrological-aspects) describe Sun/Moon through coherence or division between parts of the self. Their overlap supports that organizing theme; it does not select a particular outcome for every Sun/Moon picture.

Falis's image clusters can make the range concrete, while Carter's argument can show why a qualification matters. Use that combination to write a connected delineation, without treating every image as simultaneous or transferring an aspect-specific statement to a midpoint automatically. This conclusion is bounded to the four inspected examples and the source roles documented in their catalogs.
