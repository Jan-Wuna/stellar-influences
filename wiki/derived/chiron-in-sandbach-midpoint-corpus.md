### How Chiron Behaves In This Corpus

The inspected axes connect healing with a change in what defines the self. [Sun/Chiron](../axes/sun-chiron.md#john-sandbach---midpoints-a-kabbalistic-compendium-of-meanings-for-astrological-midpoints) describes releasing superficial measures of worth to discover meaning and spiritual purpose. [Chiron/Asc](../axes/chiron-asc.md#john-sandbach---midpoints-a-kabbalistic-compendium-of-meanings-for-astrological-midpoints) emphasizes confidence grounded in the spiritual self, freedom from others' limiting definitions, and connection with spirit guides, nature spirits, and angels. Those are Sandbach's spiritual claims, not clinical findings.

The exact orientation still matters. In [Sun/Chiron = Mars](../activations/sun-chiron-equals-mars.md#john-sandbach---midpoints-a-kabbalistic-compendium-of-meanings-for-astrological-midpoints), Mars specifies this pair through clearing anger by understanding its meaning and asserting oneself toward spiritual purpose. That passage supports this activation; it does not supply a generic meaning for every formula containing Chiron.
