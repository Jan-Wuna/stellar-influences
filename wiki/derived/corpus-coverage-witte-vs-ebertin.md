## Analysis

### Witte Coverage

- Witte contributes `22` factor chapters: Vernal Point, the angles, the classical planets, Node, and the transneptunians.
- The current ingest exposes `253` axis pages, `5082` activation pages, and `1540` triad hubs from that source.
- In practice, Witte behaves like the wide combinatorial framework in the current wiki.

### Ebertin Coverage

- Ebertin contributes `13` factor chapters: Sun through Pluto plus Node, Asc, and MC.
- The current ingest exposes `76` axis pages, `836` activation pages, and `286` triad hubs from that source.
- Ebertin's admitted factor inventory is narrower. These totals describe the ingest; they do not prove that every individual absence within that inventory is source silence.

### What The Coverage Difference Means

- The Ebertin catalog does not include `Vernal Point`, `Cupido`, `Hades`, `Zeus`, `Kronos`, `Apollon`, `Admetos`, `Vulcanus`, or `Poseidon` in its admitted factor inventory. Their absence from Ebertin subentries is therefore a documented scope boundary of this ingest.
- The shared zone between both corpora is the `13`-factor overlap now surfaced on the canonical factor pages.
- The live wiki therefore mixes a broad Hamburg corpus with a narrower Cosmobiological corpus. Raw page counts should not be mistaken for doctrinal priority or evidential superiority.

### Editorial Consequence

- Comparative work should focus first on the overlap zone where both sources actually speak.
- Within this two-source comparison, a formula supported only by Witte must retain that attribution. Other sources may already contribute to the same canonical page; this note does not classify the whole wiki as Witte-only outside Ebertin's inventory.
- For an answer about `A/B = C`, retrieve the exact activation first. An axis count or a triad hub cannot establish that orientation's interpretation or source coverage.

## Contradictions and Notes

- Corpus breadth and doctrinal precision are different questions.
- Source membership counts include structural pages where present; a count alone does not establish text-bearing evidence. Witte's repeated-pair activations do not generate triad hubs, so activation and hub totals also measure different structures.
- Matching catalog and manifest totals does not verify the attribution or formula assignment of every parsed entry. A disputed assignment needs its own source-passage check.
- For a missing formula inside the shared inventory, inspect the canonical page and catalog before deciding whether there is source silence, an intentional omission, or an extraction problem. This note is not a page-by-page completeness audit.
