## Analysis

### Shared Zone

The source catalogs and query manifest identify `36` shared axes, checked on `2026-10-15`. The overlap is axis-only: Falis supplies midpoint pair brainstorms, while Carter's aspect material is normalized into the same unordered pair pages. Neither ingest supplies oriented activation meanings. The four examples below illustrate presentation and thematic overlap; they are not a complete textual comparison of all `36` pairs.

### Difference In Method

- Falis writes in brainstorm clusters. Her axis pages read like rapid associative expansion, with many short possibilities placed side by side.
- Carter writes longer aspect arguments, preserving pair overviews where present, aspect-family sections, qualifications, and examples. Those aspect conditions remain part of each claim after placement on an axis page.
- The useful comparison is between a field of possible images and an argument about conditional expression. It does not authorize choosing one of Carter's aspect families from midpoint identity alone.

### Repeating Convergences

- On Sun/Moon, Falis's contrasts between moods and purpose overlap with Carter's account of coordination or division between private and active life. Carter separately qualifies harmonious, conjunction, and inharmonious contacts.
- On Mercury/Venus, pleasing expression and charm recur in both entries. Falis also includes flattery and manipulation; Carter qualifies the strength of the contacts and does not infer genius from the pair alone.
- On Mars/Jupiter, enthusiasm and action driven by conviction recur. Both also admit excess, but Carter's severe judgment of inharmonious contacts belongs to that aspect section, not to every midpoint picture with the pair.
- On Venus/Neptune, both describe artistic sensitivity and idealization. Carter explicitly allows artistic achievement under inharmonious contacts, so a simple harmonious-good/inharmonious-bad summary would misstate his argument.

These examples justify comparing the entries around shared pair themes while retaining their different conditions. ## Contradictions and Notes

The examples establish overlap and differences of emphasis, not agreement on every proposition. Carter's historical health and gender claims remain attributed in their source entries; this comparison does not turn them into diagnoses or universal facts. Nor does it establish that the sources independently corroborate each other.
