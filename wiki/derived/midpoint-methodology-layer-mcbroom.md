## Derived Synthesis

McBroom treats midpoint analysis as a connected interpretation of the focal point and its pair. His discussion moves from terminology and organization to keywords, possible ranges of expression, and integration in a worked example. The [verified passages](#verified-method-passages) support that account; the contents list merely locates additional topics. His invitation to formulate interpretations from keywords describes his method, while canonical wiki entries remain bounded to the meanings actually admitted from sources.
