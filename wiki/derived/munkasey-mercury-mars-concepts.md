## Notes On Use

These are prompts from one source, not independent corroborating claims. The list supplies no activation factor after `=` and does not establish that its images apply together.

The verbs matter: "Talks of Anger", "Questions Urgency" and "Swift to Anger" do not make the same claim. Speaking about, questioning or observing aggression must remain distinct from acting aggressively. Prompts such as "Educated in Athletics" and "An Ambidextrous Fighter" retain their specific meanings within the list.
