## Notes On Use

The [Source Concepts](#source-concepts) are unranked prompts from one author. They remain outside the comparative schema and do not, by themselves, establish a third-factor activation. Use the axis page's [Related Activations](../axes/moon-asc.md#related-activations) to find the exact formula and orientation.

The list includes both "Committed to Comforting Others" and "Ineffective Personal Responses", alongside images such as "A Cultural Environment". These remain separate prompts rather than a single verdict about a person's character or surroundings.
