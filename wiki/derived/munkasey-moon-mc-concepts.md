## Notes On Use

The [Source Concepts](#source-concepts) are unranked prompts from one author. They remain outside the comparative schema and do not, by themselves, establish a third-factor activation. Use the axis page's [Related Activations](../axes/moon-mc.md#related-activations) to find the exact formula and orientation.

"Family Roots", "Projection into the Public", and "The Expert who Changes Career" show the list's range across origins, public presence, and work. Its separate prompts do not establish a person's family history, competence, or career outcome.
