The [Source Concepts](#source-concepts) are unranked prompts from one author. They remain outside the comparative schema and do not, by themselves, establish a third-factor activation. "A Visionary with Expertise", "Deceived by Credentials", and "Matures Despite Inner Confusion" are separate prompts. The list does not establish a single outcome for learning, work, or personal development.
