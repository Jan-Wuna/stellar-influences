## Notes On Use

These are Munkasey's concept prompts for the pair. The [source list](#source-concepts) includes “Eliminates Unwanted Attitudes” alongside “Destructive Personal Style” (p. `349`), without stating how to choose between such images. Their different emphases should remain visible.

The list supplies vocabulary rather than a comparative delineation or an interpretation of an added activating factor. Evaluative wording such as “Abnormal Characteristics” remains attributed to Munkasey and does not establish a diagnosis.
