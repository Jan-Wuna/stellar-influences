## Notes On Use

These are Munkasey's concept prompts for the pair. The [source list](#source-concepts) includes both “Renews Acquaintances” and “Terminates Close Ties” (p. `345`), without stating when either applies. Their different directions should remain visible.

The list supplies images and vocabulary rather than a comparative delineation or an interpretation of an added activating factor. Its evaluative sexual language belongs to the source; these prompts do not establish facts about a person's conduct or character.
