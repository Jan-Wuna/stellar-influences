## Notes On Use

These are Munkasey's concept prompts for the pair. The [source list](#source-concepts) includes both “Continues with Originality” and “Banishes Originality” (p. `285`), without stating when either applies. Their different directions should remain visible.

The list supplies images and vocabulary rather than a comparative delineation or an interpretation of an added activating factor. Uncommon images such as “Dependable Astrologers” and “Precise Computers” remain part of the source list without establishing a vocation or ability.
