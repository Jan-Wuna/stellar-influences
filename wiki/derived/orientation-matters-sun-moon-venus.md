## Analysis

### The Three Orientations

- `Sun/Moon = Venus`: Witte names friendship and marriage of love, harmony between husband and wife, and peaceful hours.
- `Sun/Venus = Moon`: Witte names the beautiful body of a woman, being in love, and expressions of love.
- `Moon/Venus = Sun`: Witte names a woman's love for a man, a man favored by women, the mother, recurring harmonious hours, and "A feminine man."

These are the source's historical gender categories. Keeping them attributed preserves the difference between the entries without making them universal descriptions of bodies, relationships, or gender.

### Why The Orientation Cannot Be Collapsed

All three formulas contain `Sun`, `Moon`, and `Venus`, but each places a different pair before `=` and a different factor after it. The entries demonstrate why that distinction matters: marriage and peaceful companionship in the first entry do not replace bodily expressions of love in the second, or the maternal and gendered figures in the third. This conclusion comes from comparing the actual entries, not from combining generic factor meanings.

An alias that merged the three formulas would obscure which claim Witte attaches to which orientation. Reversing the two members of an unordered pair is allowed; moving a member across `=` changes the activation identity.

Distinct entries under different formulas are not, by themselves, contradictions.

## Contradictions and Notes

- The three formulas share relationship imagery but are not interchangeable; this note does not establish that their separate manifestations occur together.
- This triad is a clean demonstration of why the repo keeps activation identity oriented while keeping triad hubs unordered and structural.

## Derived Synthesis

The common thread is love and harmonious experience, but the three Witte entries attach it to different situations and figures. Preserve that overlap without transferring a marriage, maternal, or bodily claim to a sibling formula. The triad records membership; the exact activation page supplies the interpretation. This three-entry example supports that reading rule, not a merged doctrine for every combination of these factors.
