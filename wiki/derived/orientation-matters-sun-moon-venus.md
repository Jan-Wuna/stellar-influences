## Analysis

### The Three Orientations

- `Sun/Moon = Venus`: the relationship field of man and woman resolves into harmony, love, marriage, and peaceful shared experience.
- `Sun/Venus = Moon`: the field of body and love resolves into the woman, being in love, and bodily or affective expression of love.
- `Moon/Venus = Sun`: the field of woman and love resolves into the man, maternal expression, being favored by women, and a feminized solar presentation.

### Why The Orientation Cannot Be Collapsed

- The triad-set stays the same: `Sun`, `Moon`, `Venus`.
- The activated factor changes what is brought into manifestation. That shift changes the doctrinal claim.
- `Sun/Moon = Venus` is about relational harmony.
- `Sun/Venus = Moon` is about love taking feminine or bodily-emotional expression.
- `Moon/Venus = Sun` is about feminine love resolving into a solar figure or masculine focus.
- Because the activated factor changes the meaning, an alias that flattens these pages into one entry would destroy the core Hamburg rule that orientation matters.

### How To Read The Triad Hub

- The triad hub should answer only one question: which three orientation pages belong to the same unordered factor set?
- The triad hub should not produce a merged interpretation.
- Distinct meanings across the three pages are not contradictions. They are the expected result of changing the activated factor.

## Contradictions and Notes

- The three formulas are complementary but not interchangeable.
- This triad is a clean demonstration of why the repo keeps activation identity oriented while keeping triad hubs unordered and structural.
