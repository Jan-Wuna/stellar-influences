## Analysis

### The Stable Reading Rule

For the shared love-and-appreciation motif in `Sun/Moon = Venus`, two shorthand sentences describe a useful reading arrangement: Witte supplies the compressed outcome-key. Sandbach supplies the process expansion. This describes the overlap in that example, not a general equivalence between their other entries.

Keep `A/B = C` fixed while comparing entries. Witte's brevity and Sandbach's more discursive treatment can help the reader, but length and style do not establish equivalence. The pair and the factor after `=` are part of the claim.

### What Each Source Adds

For `Sun/Moon = Venus`, Witte's loving friendship, marriage, and peaceful hours overlap with Sandbach's account of appreciation, beauty, and fulfilling romance. Sandbach develops the felt experience of completeness; this does not turn his language about vitality into a medical finding.

The other examples set limits on that overlap. At `Sun/Mercury = Moon`, Witte names a young partner and connections with women, while Sandbach describes emotional investment in ideas, impressionability, and independent judgment. At `Jupiter/Node = Neptune`, Witte names cooling friendship, money-related deception, and insecure legal mediation; Sandbach describes subtle connections and confusion from too much relational input. Neither Sandbach entry independently confirms all of Witte's concrete situations. Calling each merely an expansion of the other would erase material differences.

### Why The Orientation Boundary Must Stay Hard

`Sun/Moon = Venus` remains distinct from `Sun/Venus = Moon` and `Moon/Venus = Sun`. The triad hub locates those siblings; it does not authorize transferring a source's claim between them. Compare each exact formula with itself across sources before considering shared themes.

### How To Use This During Retrieval

Start with the exact activation page's `Derived Synthesis`, then inspect the linked source entries when a difference matters. Synthesize demonstrated overlap first; retain source attribution for a concrete event or psychological process that only one entry supplies. Use the triad hub only for sibling lookup. A fixed source-reading order is unnecessary.

## Contradictions and Notes

- Different emphases are not automatically contradictions, but they are also not proof that one source explains or corroborates the other.
- This note does not authorize merging sibling orientations of the same triad.
- Sandbach's PDF locators above identify chapter starts, not verified pages for each activation paragraph. No visual source check is claimed here.

## Derived Synthesis

Read the entries as two contributions to the same exact formula, with overlap assessed rather than assumed. The love and appreciation motif in `Sun/Moon = Venus` supports a connected explanation. The thinking and relationship-uncertainty examples require more attribution because their concrete claims differ. This method preserves a useful synthesis while keeping distinct situations, historical gender language, and inner processes traceable to the entry that supplies them.
