### Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures

#### Witte Factor Entry

## Comparative Schema

- core meaning: Resistance, standstill, compression, and containment connect obstruction with consolidation and stabilization. ([Rules factor entry](#witte-factor-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-keyword-entry))
- psychology: Inhibition, suppression, and depression are listed as keywords; the entries do not develop a psychological mechanism or a clinical assessment. ([Rules factor entry](#witte-factor-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-keyword-entry))
- body/health: Congestion and death occur in the source wording, but no anatomy, diagnosis, or inevitable bodily outcome is established. ([ABC entry](#abc-keyword-entry); [Rules factor entry](#witte-factor-entry))
- social/relationship: Separation, strike, and blockade describe interrupted connection or activity; no specific relationship scenario is supplied. ([Rules factor entry](#witte-factor-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-keyword-entry))
- events/manifestations: Hindrance, standstill, and end coexist with consolidation and stabilization; raw material, rotation, circulation, contraction, and aggregation state add a material-process range. ([Rules factor entry](#witte-factor-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-keyword-entry))
- conflicts/notes: Restriction and stabilization are different emphases within these entries, not an explicit contradiction. Their short keyword form does not establish when either expression applies.

## Derived Synthesis

Admetos describes restriction that can obstruct movement or consolidate a condition. Resistance, inhibition, standstill, and containment join reduction, compression, and contraction; consolidation and stabilization show the same range reaching a settled or firm state. The entries therefore support both blockage and stabilization without specifying which will prevail. ([Rules factor entry, PDF p. 29](#witte-factor-entry); [ABC entry, printed p. 17 / PDF p. 7](#abc-keyword-entry))

Its scope also includes first origin and primary or raw material, together with rotation, circulation, the raw-material cycle, and state of aggregation. These give the factor a material and process dimension alongside separation, end, and death. The severe terms, including depression and congestion, remain source keywords rather than diagnoses or predictions of an inevitable outcome. ([Rules factor entry](#witte-factor-entry); [ABC entry](#abc-keyword-entry))
