- Source factor chapter page: `27`

### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `The Ascendant`
- Source page: `71`

#### Principle

The personality. (Environment)

#### Psychological Correspondence

The psychological coloring of the Ascendant depends upon its zodiacal sign.

#### Biological Correspondence

The moment of birth. The reaction to the world outside or the environment in general.

#### Sociological Correspondence

The ego in relationship with other people, particularly with persons of the environment.

#### Position in Houses and Signs

- Aries (`0157`, page `71`): A restless environment with energetic or industrious people. The power to obtain and to maintain one's place in the world, self-confidence, ambition, conscious concentration upon a desired aim or objective. Impulsiveness, a passionate mode of expression. Fanaticism, ruthlessness, rashness, quarrelsome.
- Taurus (`0015`, page `45`): A beautiful and quiet environment, real estate and land ownership, secure circumstances. Steadfastness, firmness, domesticity. Paying attention to the practicalities of life, ability to create a homely atmosphere, love of pompous display. Egotism, craving for enjoyment and indulgence, obstinacy, avarice.
- Gemini (`0159`, page `71`): A lively and versatile environment with many social contacts. An obliging manner, quick response to influences from the environment, adaptable, good comprehension, talkative. Superficial, inconstant, boastful.
- Cancer (`0160`, page `71`): Dependence on moods, a rich home life within the family circle, moist and humid regions, simple and humble circumstances. A caring, sympathetic and dedicated nature, impressionable, desire to lean on other people, industrious. Moody, inability to stand on one's own feet, hyper-sensitive.
- Leo (`0161`, page `72`): An impressive personality, a luxurious environment. An authoritative, self-confident and dignified manner, generosity, justice and fair play, open-minded and frank, love of splendor, the joy of living. Haughtiness, craving for adventure, self-glorification.
- Virgo (`0162`, page `72`): Stable conditions and circumstances. Reserve, a critical attitude, caution, observation, thoroughness, care and attention, simplicity, tidiness. Fussiness, prejudice. Harmonious establishment of the home. An affectionate nature, liveliness, good manners, desire to lean on someone. An easygoing and playful nature, aversion to hard or dirty work, vanity, craving for applause.
- Libra (`0163`, page `72`): Harmonious establishment of the home. An affectionate nature, liveliness, good manners, the desire to lean on somebody. An easygoing and playful nature, aversion to hard or dirty work, vanity, the craving for applause.
- Scorpio (`0164`, page `72`): A disharmonious and aggressive environment, humid regions. Reserve, caution, readiness for action, the power of quick decisions, zeal, industry. The preservation of the species, passion, violence, sudden wrath, fanaticism.
- Sagittarius (`0165`, page `72`): A social or religious person with great and far-reaching aims. Liveliness, dependence on moods, enthusiastic, sociable, love of nature, sport and outdoor activity. Desire for recognition, love of adventure, sentimental.
- Capricorn (`0166`, page `72`): A purposeful attitude and the tendency to act according to expediency. Self-willed, tenacity, industry, self-control, concentration, diplomacy, a responsible attitude. "Actions say more than words." Inhibitions, shyness, egotism.
- Aquarius (`0167`, page `73`): An environment keyed to reform ideas. One's own ideas, knowledge of human nature, desire for innovations, far-reaching interests, adaptable, intense soul life. Changes of mood, small measure of endurance, changing objectives.
- Pisces (`0168`, page `73`): A peculiar, lonely and simple person. Reserve, inhibition, timidity, a passive and inactive nature, abundance of feeling, the tendency to become depressed. Instability, vagueness, comfort and laziness.

## Contradictions and Framework Notes

- Witte emphasizes surroundings, acquaintances, and outward appearance. Ebertin frames the same factor as ego in relationship with environment and other people.
- This page preserves the Hamburg and Cosmobiological factor chapters side by side instead of treating one as a gloss on the other.

## Derived Synthesis

- Taken together, Asc works as the lived interface between the self and its immediate environment, contacts, and appearance.
