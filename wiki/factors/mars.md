### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Principle

Energy.

#### Psychological Correspondence

+ Willpower, active energy, courage, determination, enjoyment of work and fighting, the urge to do something. - Waste of energy, enjoyment of destruction, impulsiveness, ruthlessness, brutality. Weakness, lack of energy.

#### Biological Correspondence

The heat of the body, muscles, sex functions.

#### Sociological Correspondence

Fighters, bearers of arms, athletes, mechanics, technicians, craftsmen, surgeons.

#### Position in Houses and Signs

- Aries (`0066`, page `55`): Fighting spirit, urge to do something, ambition, love of independence. Irascibility, destructive urge, impatience, waste of energy. Blind zeal is harmful! Danger of head injuries, many quarrels.
- Taurus (`0067`, page `55`): Great capacity for work, endurance, practical gifts or abilities, urge to acquire possessions. Obstinate, intractable, impulsive, vindictive.
- Gemini (`0068`, page `55`): Mobility, readiness for action, versatile gifts, a ready wit, biting criticism. The tendency to scatter one's energies. Restless, quarrelsome.
- Cancer (`0069`, page `55`): An intense emotional life, subject to moods, impulsive, little perseverance. Lack of self-control, moodiness, actions governed by instinct.
- Leo (`0070`, page `55`): Formative powers, acting with great self-assurance, great self-confidence, spirit of enterprise, working zeal, striving for possessions, responsibility. The inclination to speculate or to gamble, too much love of self, egoism, unbridled passion.
- Virgo (`0071`, page `56`): Love of tidiness, endurance in detail work and scientific activity, methodical work, exploitation of every situation, adaptation, the urge to criticize, irritability, nervousness.
- Libra (`0072`, page `56`): The urge to associate, a friendly and cordial manner assists in attaining success. Enthusiasm, love of teamwork. Dependence on feeling and mood as well as too much frankness lead to differences.
- Scorpio (`0073`, page `56`): The survival instinct, ruthless use of one's energies, ambition, consciousness of goal, a sense of criticism. Dissipation of energy, the urge to criticize and to destroy wilfully, craving for power and authority, selfishness. Incompatibility.
- Sagittarius (`0074`, page `56`): The need to convince others of one's own ideas. Love of contest and of sport, the display of enthusiasm in one's work, mobility, frankness, candor. Rashness, love of adventure, the tendency to be extravagant.
- Capricorn (`0075`, page `56`): Ambition, working zeal, endurance, a sense of reality, sober deliberation, the will to attain success, independence. Over-estimation of self, defiance, obstinacy, egoism.
- Aquarius (`0076`, page `56`): Acting with deliberation, love of freedom, utilization of new working methods, the urge to reform, teamwork, organization, independence. The spirit of contradiction, inconstancy, superficiality.
- Pisces (`0077`, page `56`): Wait and see attitude in hoping and acting, to work silently, a struggle for recognition, social activity. Interest in the occult (borderline) sciences. The inclination to join secret societies, craving for alcohol, nicotine, etc. Lack of self-control and unreliability in love relationships.
