### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Principle

Intellect, mediation.

#### Psychological Correspondence

+ Good grasp or understanding of a subject, sound judgement, critical ability, dexterity in expression and writing, mediation, diplomacy, general intellectual abilities, analysis. - A lack of understanding and of objective criticism, tendency to diffuse one's energy into too many channels, inhibitions in speech and writing, over-development or weakness of intellect.

#### Biological Correspondence

The motor nerves (speech and hearing organs).

#### Sociological Correspondence

Intellectual workers, trades-people, agents or mediators.

#### Position in Houses and Signs

- Aries (`0040`, page `50`): Mental activity, constructive intellect, creative thinking, keen powers of observation, criticism, enthusiasm, quickness at repartee in speech and writing, rashness, exaggeration, mental overwork. Nervous.
- Taurus (`0041`, page `50`): Logical thinking, formal thinking, logic, endurance and patience in relation to mental activity, a sense of form, deliberation. One-sided views. Hoarseness caused by too much talking.
- Gemini (`0042`, page `50`): Versatile thinking, love of variety and change, adaptable, business talent. Lack of constancy, honesty, tidiness. Active or lively correspondence.
- Cancer (`0043`, page `50`): Perception, interrelationship between thinking and feeling, good memory, tendency to become deeply immersed in something, positive or constructive criticism, one's own individual methods of work, a sense of harmony, art interests.
- Leo (`0044`, page `51`): Creative thinking, power of enthusiasm, wealth of plans, organizing ability, prudence, far-sightedness, speculation, desire for possessions. Intellectual leaders, organizers, speculators.
- Virgo (`0045`, page `51`): Thirst for knowledge, practical thinking, patience, well-grounded expert knowledge and specialized skill, little ability to comprehend the whole. Tidiness, care and attention, criticism. Nervous disturbances of the digestive tract owing to a sedentary life. Good collaboration and cooperation, teamwork.
- Libra (`0046`, page `51`): Creative thinking within patterns already in existence, teamwork, adaptable, good powers of comparison, sense of justice, sense of form and beauty, good manners, tactful mediation.
- Scorpio (`0047`, page `51`): Sharp criticism, fanaticism, endurance and perseverance in the pursuit of difficult problems and in the solution of arduous tasks, practical ability. Sarcasm, skepticism, cunning and craft. Lively disputes, arguments, fights.
- Sagittarius (`0048`, page `51`): Deep thinking coupled with social or religious tendencies, the philosopher, manifold interests, a striving for justice and truth, occupation with the problems of life. The danger of scattering one's energies. The tendency to evade difficult tasks or to take the line of least resistance.
- Capricorn (`0049`, page `51`): Concentration, thoroughness, endurance, patience, consciousness of aim or objective, logic, tendency to view life seriously, solution of difficult problems. Melancholy, cunning, crafty. Success through one's own merit, economy, reserve. Ambition is often the driving force behind all activity.
- Aquarius (`0050`, page `51`): Progressive thinking, interest in inventions and reforms, a wealth of plans, quick grasp of a situation. Individual and original judgement, creative thinking, enthusiasm. Utopian ideas, tendency to revel, desire to work alone. Organizers, reformers, inventors.
- Pisces (`0051`, page `52`): A person whose thoughts are easily influenced by others, receptive to the thoughts of others, ability to reproduce, fantasy and imagination. Planning without energy of action, philosophical ideas.
