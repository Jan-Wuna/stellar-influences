### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Neptune`
- Source page: `64`

#### Principle

Receptive, impressionable, sympathetic understanding of people.

#### Psychological Correspondence

+ Receptive, fantasy or imagination, sensitive, contemplation, sympathetic understanding or compassionate sensitivity, dreamy nature, inclination to mysticism, mediumship, - Impressionable, sensitive, vagueness or lack of clarity, confusion, fanciful notions, deception, lack of planning ability, a lie or a fraud.

The pineal gland, solar plexus, aura slackness or laxity, paralysis.

## Contradictions and Framework Notes

- Both sources agree on uncertainty, deception, and unstable states. Ebertin adds more explicit sympathy, receptivity, and mediumistic framing.
- This page preserves the Hamburg and Cosmobiological factor chapters side by side instead of treating one as a gloss on the other.

## Derived Synthesis

- Taken together, Neptune works as porousness: inspiration and sympathy on one side, confusion and dissolution on the other.
