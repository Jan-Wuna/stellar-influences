### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Principle

Association or alliance.

#### Psychological Correspondence

+ Adaptable, urge to unite with others, sociable, good fellowship, obliging manner. - Lack of adaptability, incompatible, unsociable, antisocial conduct.

#### Biological Correspondence

The power body or the astral body, the subconscious memory.

#### Sociological Correspondence

Meetings, alliances, societies, contacts, blood ties, kindred and related people.

#### Position in Houses and Signs

- Aries (`0144`, page `69`): The urge to associate, striving for leadership in communities, cultivation of personal associations. The wish to rule other people.
- Taurus (`0145`, page `69`): Permanent unions, consolidation and preservation of alliances or associations, fostering and developing of communal establishments. The striving for personal advantages through others.
- Gemini (`0146`, page `69`): Many-sided associations and also the seeking of stimulating ideas through the former, the cultivation of contact with relatives and neighbors. Superficial associations of short duration.
- Cancer (`0147`, page `69`): Maintaining contact with one's parents and one's native country, soul associations. Disagreements with relatives.
- Leo (`1014`, page `284`): The tendency to like or to prefer big parties, festivities and entertainments. The ability to become popular in society, organization. Wastefulness, love of pomp, speculations with other people.
- Virgo (`0149`, page `70`): Working or scientific associations, connection with teaching or research institutes. Disagreements through uncalled-for criticism or nagging.
- Libra (`0150`, page `70`): Special aptitude for teamwork in connection with the welfare of the public. Friendship. A communal and social sense, sociable, the harmonious arrangement of sociable meetings and entertainments.
- Scorpio (`0151`, page `70`): An association of people fighting for identical aims, cooperative effort, association through force of circumstances. Undermining forces in teamwork.
- Sagittarius (`0152`, page `70`): Associations with kindred souls, aptitude for teamwork in the spheres of the law, the administration and within societies upholding a particular outlook on life. Utopian plans advocated by a group.
- Capricorn (`0153`, page `70`): Striving for the establishment of suitable associations pursuing clear and definite objectives, a sense of responsibility with regard to others. Social climbing and egotism within a community.
- Aquarius (`0154`, page `70`): Stimulating and friendly associations with many-sided or widely varying interests and reformatory objectives, love of social contacts. The constant desire for innovations or new arrangements within an association of people.
- Pisces (`0155`, page `70`): The inclination to have secret associations and meetings, philosophical interests in common with other people, a community of persons holding the same beliefs. Associations which harm the community at large.
