### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

#### Principle

Inhibition, concentration.

#### Psychological Correspondence

+ Concentration, consolidation, perseverance, seriousness, the ability to leam from experience, economy. - Inhibition, melancholy, reserved and taciturn, increasing loneliness, isolation, eccentricity, distrust, stinginess, lack of adaptability.

#### Biological Correspondence

The bony structure, the process of hardening up, stone-formation, the loss of organs. Old age.

#### Sociological Correspondence

Hard working, inhibited or sad people. Agriculture, mining, real estate.

- Aries (`0092`, page `59`): Ambition, diligence and industry, endurance, obstinacy, self-restraint, modesty, serious mode of working. The desire to rule other people, self-will, defiance, the tendency to go one's way alone, selfishness.
- Taurus (`0093`, page `59`): Perseverance, enduring energy, the application of method, economy, urge to acquire possessions or property. A conservative outlook, inertia, inhibitions make advancement in the world difficult.

- Cancer (`0095`, page `59`): Reserve, controlled emotional life, love of independence, difficult contact with family members. Hypersensitive, discontented. Modest or aggravating circumstances are due to family ties.
- Leo (`0096`, page `60`): Reliability, loyalty, simplicity, inhibitions regarding social contacts, dislike of formalities, shyness, caution, diplomacy, inhibited sex expression.
- Virgo (`0097`, page `60`): Care and attention, meticulous, correct, serious, thorough, responsible, discrete, a sense of criticism. A nagging nature, the desire to act alone and not in unison with others, the misunderstood person.

- Scorpio (`0099`, page `60`): Obstinate, serious outlook on life, urge to probe or to delve deeply into difficult problems, tendency to occupy one's mind with metaphysical spheres of knowledge, skill, endurance. Melancholy, transformations. The experience of spiritual rebirth.
