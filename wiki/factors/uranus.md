### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Uranus`
- Source page: `61`

#### Principle

Suddenness, revolution, change.

#### Psychological Correspondence

+ Peculiarity, independence, love of freedom, independent action and ideas, enthusiasm for every thing that is new or modem, agility or motility, good powers of perception, intuition allied with objective judgement, easy excitability. A sense of rhythm. - An obstinate nature, lack of adaptability, rebellion, revolutionary tendencies, an excitable nature, impulsive actions, strong emotional tensions, passion for innovation, changeable character.

#### Biological Correspondence

Rhythm, connections with the nervous system, the meninges or membranes of the brain, the pituitary gland (hypophysis cerebri) and the spinal marrow.

## Contradictions and Framework Notes

- Both sources agree on suddenness, excitement, change, and technical or reforming tendencies. Ebertin articulates freedom, intuition, and revolution more fully.
- This page preserves the Hamburg and Cosmobiological factor chapters side by side instead of treating one as a gloss on the other.

## Derived Synthesis

- Taken together, Uranus works as disruptive awakening: sudden change, excitation, innovation, and the push toward reform.
