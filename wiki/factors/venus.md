### Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences

- Source heading: `Venus`
- Source page: `53`

#### Principle

Love, art.

#### Psychological Correspondence

+ Physical attraction, feeling, a sense of harmony, beauty and art, a positive outlook or attitude toward life. - Sexual aberration, sentimentality, a want of taste, heedlessness, vice.

Glandular products (kidneys, veins).

## Contradictions and Framework Notes

- Both sources converge on love, art, harmony, and attraction. Ebertin expands the same field into stronger moral, social, and psychological distinctions.
- This page preserves the Hamburg and Cosmobiological factor chapters side by side instead of treating one as a gloss on the other.

## Derived Synthesis

- Taken together, Venus works as attraction, harmony, pleasure, and aesthetic bonding in both personal and social form.
