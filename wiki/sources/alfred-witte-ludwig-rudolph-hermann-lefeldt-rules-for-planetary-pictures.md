## Bibliographic Metadata

- Authors: Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph, Hermann Lefeldt
- Title: *Rules for Planetary Pictures*
- Vault file: `Stellar Influences Vault/planetary expressions - Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures 2020.pdf`

The unnumbered title and copyright leaves (one-based PDF pp. 1–2) credit Ludwig Rudolph and Hermann Lefeldt, identify Witte-Verlag, Hamburg, and explicitly state **Revised Edition 2020**. PDF p. 2 names Curt Knupfer as English translator and Michael Feist for the revision, preface and introduction; it lists ISBNs 978-3-920807-46-1 and 978-3-920807-47-8. PDF p. 3 explains the canonical Witte attribution: the book is based on Alfred Witte's teachings, collected and completed by Rudolph and later expanded by Lefeldt.
