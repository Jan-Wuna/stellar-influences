## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Admetos = MC`: settling down deeply, sticking to given ties, and occupying a special position that others notice from below.
- `Asc/MC = Admetos`: fixed bonds, settled vocational ties, and relationships or roles that become firmly established, enduring, and difficult to dislodge.
- `MC/Admetos = Asc`: brings the axis into contact with others as emotional hardness and unyielding self-containment.
