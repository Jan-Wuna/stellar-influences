## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Cupido = MC`: identification with parental home, domestic values, and personal orientation toward residence and communal life.
- `Asc/MC = Cupido`: active participation in society, marriage, and professional association as the setting in which the personality finds role, belonging, and direction.
- `MC/Cupido = Asc`: brings the axis into immediate relationship through companionship and artistic association.
