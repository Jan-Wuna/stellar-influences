## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/MC = Poseidon`: agreement with others at the level of principles, ideas, insight, or shared understanding.
- `Asc/Poseidon = MC`: mental or spiritual exchange with others and a public or personal path shaped by subtle influence coming through contact.
- `MC/Poseidon = Asc`: brings the axis into relation through shared ideas and mental influence.
