## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/MC = Zeus`: inciting, motivating, or aiming others toward a shared objective, with personal and public life shaped by purposeful drive.
- `Asc/Zeus = MC`: being incited by others toward a deed or a concrete turn in direction.
- `MC/Zeus = Asc`: brings the axis out through teaching, instruction, and direct personal influence.
