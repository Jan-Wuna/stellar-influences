## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Poseidon = Zeus`: being guided by others toward a definite spiritual or intellectual aim.
- `Asc/Zeus = Poseidon`: being forced into a mental attitude or led by others in thought, belief, or spiritual view.
- `Zeus/Poseidon = Asc`: To be mentally influenced and impressed by others. To be under the mental guidance of others. Others show their conviction and mental attitude. They are under mental compulsion.
