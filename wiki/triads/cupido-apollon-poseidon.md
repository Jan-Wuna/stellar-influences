## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Poseidon = Cupido`: congenial culture: a group united by similar mentality, aesthetic principle, or social formation.
- `Cupido/Apollon = Poseidon`: artistic or scientific union built on common faith or common intellectual outlook.
- `Cupido/Poseidon = Apollon`: the spread of a spiritual cult or the gathering together of many people under the same faith or worldview.
