## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Vulcanus = Jupiter`: others using their power for the native's good and spreading confidence, optimism, or generous strength.
- `Jupiter/Asc = Vulcanus`: luck through mighty enterprises or through energetic, enterprising, and cheerful people.
- `Jupiter/Vulcanus = Asc`: a presence that gives others courage, confidence, and trust, while also showing how strongly the environment can shape or impress the native.
