## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Vulcanus = Jupiter`: mighty luck for the family, great artistic success, happiness through art, and strong financial power or high finance.
- `Jupiter/Cupido = Vulcanus`: power or strong impact within a community achieved through artistic or collective ability.
- `Jupiter/Vulcanus = Cupido`: great joy through marriage, household, culture, or a close-knit group that carries material or emotional strength.
