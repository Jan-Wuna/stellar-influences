## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Zeus = Jupiter`: successful artistic creation and the formation of banks or other financial institutions.
- `Jupiter/Cupido = Zeus`: a happy family blessed with children, successful work in family or community, success in good firms, and organized group action.
- `Jupiter/Zeus = Cupido`: successful collective activity, a lucky start in a company, and productive creation strengthened by belonging and shared purpose.
