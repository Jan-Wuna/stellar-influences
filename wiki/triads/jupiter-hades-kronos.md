## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Kronos = Jupiter`: escape from mean plots, but also finance scandals, bank collapse, and inflation.
- `Jupiter/Hades = Kronos`: a façade of refinement amid poverty, impoverished government, losses through authorities, hidden officials, and welfare administration.
- `Jupiter/Kronos = Hades`: accomplishing much with little, money made from things taken out of the earth, and gain through mining or low-material sources.
