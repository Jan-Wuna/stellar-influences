## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Poseidon = Jupiter`: happiness through mental separateness, but also money losses caused by lack of understanding.
- `Jupiter/Hades = Poseidon`: depression, sorrow over wrong suffered, or being forced to endure injustice.
- `Jupiter/Poseidon = Hades`: unpleasantness and difficulty arising from missing intelligence, judgment, or clear thought.
