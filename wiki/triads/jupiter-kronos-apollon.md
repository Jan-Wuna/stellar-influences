## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Apollon = Kronos`: fortunate command in science or business, industry leadership, and bank direction.
- `Jupiter/Kronos = Apollon`: luck for many, prospering developments, and monetary income through commerce or industrial growth.
- `Kronos/Apollon = Jupiter`: joy through leadership, prosperous science or commerce, and success in directing an important trade or knowledge field.
