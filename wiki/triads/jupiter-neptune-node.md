## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Neptune = Node`: connection based on shared hopes, ideals, or fantasies, and the tendency to form ties with naive, speculative, or highly imaginative people.
- `Jupiter/Node = Neptune`: idealized connection, subtle or unspoken bonds, and hope invested in partnership, but with real risk of instability, disappointment, or confused alliance.
- `Neptune/Node = Jupiter`: idealistic fellowship, with bonds shaped by faith, hope, compassion, and the wish to belong to something spiritually or morally larger.
