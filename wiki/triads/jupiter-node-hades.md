## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Hades = Node`: involvement in money troubles and connection with people who have hard luck.
- `Jupiter/Node = Hades`: good relations with single women or with persons of lower social standing.
- `Node/Hades = Jupiter`: To be prompted or to prompt others to misappropriate or to extort something. Unpleasantness in money affairs. To be a witness to thefts.
