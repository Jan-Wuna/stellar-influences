## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/MC = Node`: fortunate association, helpful contacts, and growth in status or purpose through congenial alliances, mentors, or encouraging social networks.
- `Jupiter/Node = MC`: popularity, tact, and life development through advantageous associations, common interests, and socially constructive bonds.
- `Node/MC = Jupiter`: a public path advanced through generous alliances, helpful networks, and associations that open the field of vocation, status, or collective purpose.
