## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Node = Poseidon`: fortunate spiritual relations and connection with prudent, wise people.
- `Jupiter/Poseidon = Node`: ties with intelligent and reasonable people and happiness through intellectual or spiritual relationships.
- `Node/Poseidon = Jupiter`: Aligned with reasonable and prudent people. Connected with wise and philosophically educated people.
