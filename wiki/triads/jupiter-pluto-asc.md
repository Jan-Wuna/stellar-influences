## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Asc = Pluto`: amplified influence, recognition, and transformative expansion of personal presence, often through strong impact on other people.
- `Jupiter/Pluto = Asc`: a large presence, strategic self-advancement, and the ability to impress the environment with force, enthusiasm, and organizing talent.
- `Pluto/Asc = Jupiter`: a strong, influential presence, with personal magnetism amplified into leadership, persuasive force, and the urge to enlarge one's impact on others and the environment.
