## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Node = Pluto`: powerful networking, transformative alliances, and the urge to widen influence through many consequential contacts.
- `Jupiter/Pluto = Node`: far-reaching alliance, collective ambition, and success through groups, networks, or political and managerial connection.
- `Pluto/Node = Jupiter`: influential alliance, with powerful associations enlarged into social reach, institutional influence, or shared ambitions that alter life on a bigger stage.
