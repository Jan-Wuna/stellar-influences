## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Pluto = Poseidon`: successful development in education and happy spiritual growth.
- `Jupiter/Poseidon = Pluto`: the happy growth of reason, intelligence, wisdom, and conscience.
- `Pluto/Poseidon = Jupiter`: This activation emphasizes reason an good judgement lead to a very fortunate change or transformation in the spiritual attitude as well as in the attitude towards the world.
