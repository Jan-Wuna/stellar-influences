## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Poseidon = Saturn`: having to switch off reason for a time, dealing with people whose reason must first mature, and educating others toward intelligence.
- `Jupiter/Saturn = Poseidon`: inner ripening and, in the source, university buildings.
