## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Saturn = Zeus`: taking possession of an inheritance, postponement of creative work, burning buildings, and provision for parents through estate transfer.
- `Jupiter/Zeus = Saturn`: hindrance to happy work, blocked productivity, or detachment from what has been built or achieved.
