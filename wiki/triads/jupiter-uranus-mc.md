## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter, Uranus and MC form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

The [Jupiter/Uranus = MC](../activations/jupiter-uranus-equals-mc.md#contradictions) page flags “Frcudigcs Ercignis”, “Scre” and “technical anticipation”; [Uranus/MC = Jupiter](../activations/uranus-mc-equals-jupiter.md#contradictions) retains “psychic glick impulses”. These subentries remain represented with wording limits.
