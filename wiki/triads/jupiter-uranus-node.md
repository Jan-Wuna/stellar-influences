## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Node = Uranus`: witty, unusual, or technically flavored connection, and the rapid arrival of lucky new contacts or ventures.
- `Jupiter/Uranus = Node`: new or happy association with others, especially where freedom, experimentation, or shared optimism are central.
- `Uranus/Node = Jupiter`: opportunity through unusual people, with growth arriving through progressive circles, reformist groups, or sudden openings in relationship.
