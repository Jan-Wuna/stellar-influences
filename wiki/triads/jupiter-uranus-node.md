## Derived Synthesis

Jupiter, Uranus and Node form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

Aren Ober is represented specifically for [Jupiter/Uranus = Node](../activations/jupiter-uranus-equals-node.md#ober-entry), not for all three orientations. That activation's [Ebertin entry](../activations/jupiter-uranus-equals-node.md#ebertin-entry) retains the scan-confirmed printed typo `w'th`.
