## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Poseidon = Uranus`: wisdom and insight arriving by surprise.
- `Jupiter/Uranus = Poseidon`: good fortune on the mental or spiritual plane through intelligence, reason, wisdom, and one's philosophy of life.
