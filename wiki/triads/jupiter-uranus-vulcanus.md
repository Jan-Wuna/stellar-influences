## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Uranus = Vulcanus`: might joined with sudden luck and optimism born of abrupt good fortune.
- `Jupiter/Vulcanus = Uranus`: abrupt good fortune, a rapid surge of optimism, and breakthroughs that arrive with unusual speed or force.
