## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Vulcanus = Zeus`: great happiness through successful production, initiative, or the effective release of will into concrete results.
- `Jupiter/Zeus = Vulcanus`: optimistic accomplishment, seeing the fruits of one's labor, and increased fortune through powerful, successful work.
- `Zeus/Vulcanus = Jupiter`: Mighty successful efforts. Important increase of fortune through luck or the own efforts.
