## Derived Synthesis

- `Admetos/Vulcanus = Mars`: blocked labor, battle under heavy pressure, and the enforced stoppage or surrender of action.
- `Mars/Admetos = Vulcanus`: mighty suppression and terrorization, where pressure becomes massive and hard to resist.
- `Mars/Vulcanus = Admetos`: brings immense force to a halt, exhaustion, or a hard stop in struggle.
