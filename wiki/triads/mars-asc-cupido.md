## Derived Synthesis

Mars, Asc and Cupido form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

The [Asc/Cupido = Mars ABC Entry](../activations/asc-cupido-equals-mars.md#abc-entry) is on printed page 122, PDF page 60. The former hub reference to page 121 pointed to the preceding page; the Mars glyph row is on the left page of the retained scan.
