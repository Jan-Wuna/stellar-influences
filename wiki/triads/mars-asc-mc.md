## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/MC = Mars`: effort, initiative, teamwork, and practical drive as the force that advances the personality and pushes life circumstances forward.
- `Mars/Asc = MC`: a fighter's stance in life, vigorous collaboration, and a drive to make common cause through visible effort or shared tasks.
- `Mars/MC = Asc`: a confident, active, and respected style that naturally takes initiative and often assumes leadership in immediate circumstances.
