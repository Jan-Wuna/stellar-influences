## Derived Synthesis

Mars, Asc and Poseidon form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

The [Witte entry for Mars/Asc = Poseidon](../activations/mars-asc-equals-poseidon.md#witte-entry) retains the unclear phrase “To experience comprehensive through others.” The linked activation marks this wording as unclear; it does not justify an inferred completion or a shared interpretation for the three orientations.
