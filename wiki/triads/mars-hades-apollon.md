## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Apollon = Mars`: harsh acts, trade in old goods, work in antiquity or historical science, and active involvement in occult or hidden disciplines.
- `Mars/Apollon = Hades`: scientific work on old subjects, trade in antiques, and engagement with historical or ancient records.
- `Mars/Hades = Apollon`: contact with degraded groups, serving classes, soldiers, and large-scale trade or historical work carried on under inferior conditions.
