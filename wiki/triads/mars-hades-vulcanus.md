## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Vulcanus = Mars`: repulsiveness in love and weakness or impairment in sexual force.
- `Mars/Hades = Vulcanus`: warlike activity, perilous missions, and degraded action backed by overwhelming strength.
- `Mars/Vulcanus = Hades`: turns strong force dangerous, warlike, and destructive in crude or geological ways.
