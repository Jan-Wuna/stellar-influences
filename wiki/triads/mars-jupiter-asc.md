## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Asc = Mars`: initiative, proposals, engagement, and active cooperation as the force that enlarges relationships and opens practical opportunity.
- `Mars/Asc = Jupiter`: confident teamwork, effective leadership, and gains or fortunate unions that come through direct action with others.
- `Mars/Jupiter = Asc`: an energetic, upbeat, and capable presence that naturally tries to improve situations and mobilize others.
