## Derived Synthesis

Mars, Jupiter and Neptune form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

The [ABC entry for Jupiter/Neptune = Mars](../activations/jupiter-neptune-equals-mars.md#abc-entry) retains “joy in rhythmic credence”, which the activation identifies as an unreliable translation. Its paragraph is represented without treating that fragment as a confirmed meaning. The [Sandbach entry for Mars/Jupiter = Neptune](../activations/mars-jupiter-equals-neptune.md#sandbach-entry) reports a direct PDF-page check.
