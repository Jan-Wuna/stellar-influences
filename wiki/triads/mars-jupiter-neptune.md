## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Neptune = Mars`: motivated idealism, action driven by hope or imagination, and the urge to assert beliefs or plans that may not be grounded in practical reality.
- `Mars/Jupiter = Neptune`: enthusiastic effort on behalf of ideals or promises, but with a real risk that plans outrun reality and success becomes blurred, delayed, or lost.
- `Mars/Neptune = Jupiter`: hopeful or spiritualized effort that can produce subtle guidance or a fortunate turn after disappointment, yet still operates through weakened vitality or unstable circumstances.
