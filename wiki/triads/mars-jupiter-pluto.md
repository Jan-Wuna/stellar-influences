## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Pluto = Mars`: relentless drive, successful execution of large plans, and enthusiasm strong enough to mobilize both self and others.
- `Mars/Jupiter = Pluto`: extraordinary ambition, creative force, and the capacity to throw major power behind a project, campaign, or developmental push.
- `Mars/Pluto = Jupiter`: relentless work directed toward large objectives, with unusual success possible when force is disciplined toward a clear aim.
