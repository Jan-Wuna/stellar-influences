## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Vulcanus = Mars`: political action, fighting for or against a regime, and direct exercise of state force.
- `Mars/Kronos = Vulcanus`: arms the axis with brute force and coercive strength.
- `Mars/Vulcanus = Kronos`: channels exceptional force into state-controlled mobilization and official direction of labor.
