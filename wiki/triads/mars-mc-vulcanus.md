## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Vulcanus = Mars`: energizes the axis into intense willpower and urgent action.
- `Mars/MC = Vulcanus`: intensifies vocational action into personal force, energetic dominance, and mighty will.
- `Mars/Vulcanus = MC`: internalizes the axis as courage, readiness for conflict, and the capacity for powerful achievement.
