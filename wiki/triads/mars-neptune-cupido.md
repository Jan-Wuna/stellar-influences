## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Cupido = Neptune`: dissolves Mars/Cupido through fading intention and the breakdown of joint effort.
- `Mars/Neptune = Cupido`: dissolves family or communal bonds through confusion, disappointment, and destructive misunderstanding.
- `Neptune/Cupido = Mars`: This activation forces conflict and assertion into a vulnerable family or group field, showing quarrels, bodily strain, mistrust, or anger that break through enmeshment, passivity, or disappointed attachment. It can mark the point where blurred domestic or relational dynamics demand decisive action.
