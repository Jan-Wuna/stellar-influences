## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Neptune = Zeus`: turns confused or weakened force explosive, fiery, and technically dangerous.
- `Mars/Zeus = Neptune`: dissolves directed force through deprivation, smoke, concealment, and denial of support.
- `Neptune/Zeus = Mars`: Productive force is generated through volatile or combustible means, emphasizing energy derived from burning gas, liquid fuel, or similar fire-driven processes.
