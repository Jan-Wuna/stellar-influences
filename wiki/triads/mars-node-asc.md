## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Asc = Node`: active connection, teamwork, physical attraction, and relationships formed around shared effort, shared momentum, or the need to get something done together.
- `Mars/Node = Asc`: vigorous fellowship, energetic personal ties, and the tendency to meet others through work, shared effort, or strong physical involvement.
- `Node/Asc = Mars`: an identity shaped by active contact, with close associations marked by urgency, assertion, competition, or the need to fight for one's place within the immediate social field.
