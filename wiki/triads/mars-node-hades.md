## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Hades = Node`: connection that blocks success and links one to ugly affairs, violence, or murder.
- `Mars/Node = Hades`: degrades common effort into unpleasantness, vice, or harmful collective activity.
- `Node/Hades = Mars`: To call together or be together for the purpose of committing mean acts.
