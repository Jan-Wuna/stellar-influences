## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/MC = Node`: leadership within groups, command in working life, and the drive to develop effectiveness by acting with or through other people.
- `Mars/Node = MC`: purposeful collaboration, the integration of individuality into community work, and a path that develops through constructive partnership and shared problem-solving.
- `Node/MC = Mars`: a public direction shaped by active, sometimes pressured alliances, with shared aims pushed forward through effort, competition, or assertive group dynamics.
