## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Kronos = Pluto`: deepens executive force into large plans, strategic development, and transformative uses of power.
- `Mars/Pluto = Kronos`: raises transformative planning into government, large leadership, and divided or distributed state power.
- `Pluto/Kronos = Mars`: This activation emphasizes disciplined action and testing; Work drives advancement or proves ability.
