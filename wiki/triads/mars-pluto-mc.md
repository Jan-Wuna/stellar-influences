## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/MC = Pluto`: an ambitious push toward major achievement, with the will to take on difficult tasks and reshape circumstances through concentrated effort.
- `Mars/Pluto = MC`: extreme career drive, powerful organizing capacity, and the will to push advancement through difficult, dangerous, or deeply transformative circumstances.
- `Pluto/MC = Mars`: ruthless ambition in motion, with vocation, public conflict, and transformative pressure driven by strong will and uncompromising effort.
