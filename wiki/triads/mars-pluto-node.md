## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Node = Pluto`: an urgent need to make associations productive, often by confronting resentment, clearing hostilities, and pushing cooperation through difficult change.
- `Mars/Pluto = Node`: intense cooperation, the ability to spur others toward major results, and shared work that demands high effort and strong will.
- `Pluto/Node = Mars`: a high-pressure alliance field, with relationships and group ties shaped by struggle, confrontation, and the need to push through intense social circumstances.
