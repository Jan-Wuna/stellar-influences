## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Pluto = Saturn`: disciplined pressure, long-range effort, and the refusal to quit even when plans are slowed, hindered, or burdened by severe resistance.
- `Mars/Saturn = Pluto`: blocked energy building to a point where drastic change, rage, or forced restructuring becomes unavoidable.
- `Saturn/Pluto = Mars`: compressed power in action, with will applied through grit, survival instinct, and the readiness to work through harsh resistance.
