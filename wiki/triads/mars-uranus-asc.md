## Derived Synthesis

Mars, Uranus and Asc form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.
