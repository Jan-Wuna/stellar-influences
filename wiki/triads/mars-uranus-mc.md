## Derived Synthesis

Mars, Uranus and MC form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

Printed-wording limitation: The [Sandbach entry for Mars/MC = Uranus](../activations/mars-mc-equals-uranus.md#sandbach-entry) prints “One need” and “even usual” on PDF77; the latter adjective remains unclear. Mars/Uranus = MC's former ABC transcription damage has been repaired.
