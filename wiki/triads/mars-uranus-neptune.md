## Derived Synthesis

Mars, Uranus and Neptune form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

Printed-wording limitation: The [Mars/Uranus = Neptune Sandbach entry](../activations/mars-uranus-equals-neptune.md#sandbach-entry) actually prints “actions that are confusion or misconstrued” on PDF71; its awkward wording is preserved.
