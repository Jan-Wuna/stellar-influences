## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Admetos = Zeus`: moving old things out of the way, destruction used to prepare for something new, and becoming the cause of removal or wreckage.
- `Mars/Zeus = Admetos`: compresses directed effort into blocked or difficult working conditions.
- `Zeus/Admetos = Mars`: start all over again to revolt against a hated force. To begin anew on a limited scope. To provide for more room, air and light through energetic action.
