## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Apollon = Zeus`: scientific inventions, technicians, and the making of technical products.
- `Mars/Zeus = Apollon`: extends directed force into wide-ranging work, science, and large operational measures.
- `Zeus/Apollon = Mars`: To industriously learn, study or research. To put the theory into practice with energy. To occupy one’s mind with military problems. Military science.
