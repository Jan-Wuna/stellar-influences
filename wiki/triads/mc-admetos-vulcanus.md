## Derived Synthesis

- `Admetos/Vulcanus = MC`: great perseverance, unbending character, and the demand to remain firm under harsh treatment by fate.
- `MC/Admetos = Vulcanus`: massively strengthens the axis through exceptional endurance and resistance.
- `MC/Vulcanus = Admetos`: compresses the axis into suppression, subjugation, and failure under force.
