- `Cupido/Poseidon = MC`: seeking mental or spiritual community and being drawn toward cultural, philosophical, or educational belonging.
- `MC/Cupido = Poseidon`: spiritualizes the axis through gifted circles and ideal communities.
- `MC/Poseidon = Cupido`: socializes the axis through comprehensive understanding of marriage, art, and community.
