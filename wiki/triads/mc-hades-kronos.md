## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Kronos = MC`: suffering from great meanness, committing it, or losing profession and business through mismanagement.
- `MC/Hades = Kronos`: raises the axis into conflict with authority and governmental interference.
- `MC/Kronos = Hades`: lowers the axis into sorrow, obstacles, and authority tied to heavy or dirty material realities.
