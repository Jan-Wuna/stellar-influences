## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Poseidon = MC`: mental or spiritual poverty, defect, oddness, and also representing secret science or hidden wisdom.
- `MC/Hades = Poseidon`: spiritualizes the axis into secret knowledge and odd or damaged mentality.
- `MC/Poseidon = Hades`: lowers the axis into past-bound thinking and degraded mentality.
