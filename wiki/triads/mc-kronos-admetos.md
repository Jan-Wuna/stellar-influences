## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Admetos = MC`: being known for peculiarity, difference, or a remarkable position that stands out even among many people.
- `MC/Admetos = Kronos`: raises the axis into fixed authority and unwavering command.
- `MC/Kronos = Admetos`: narrows the axis through unrecognized ability and reduced authority.
