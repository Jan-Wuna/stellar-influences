## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Zeus = Mercury`: having one's way shown by others, being compelled into a fixed line of thought, or much discussion around work, tools, or machines.
- `Mercury/Asc = Zeus`: directs the Mercury/Asc axis into command, obedience, and technical discussion.
- `Mercury/Zeus = Asc`: To be asked. To ask others. A request that is hard to refuse.
