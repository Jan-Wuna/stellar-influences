## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Apollon = Mercury`: commercial conversation, social entertainment with many, and lively thought around art and science.
- `Mercury/Apollon = Cupido`: socializes the axis through artistic and scientific discussion in community.
- `Mercury/Cupido = Apollon`: enlarges the axis into big gatherings, broad entertainment, and literary spread.
