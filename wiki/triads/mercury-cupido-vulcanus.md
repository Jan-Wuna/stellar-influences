## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Vulcanus = Mercury`: powerful artistic ideas, sketches, plans, and mental projects aimed at major creation.
- `Mercury/Cupido = Vulcanus`: strengthens the axis into forceful communal speech and heated group discussion.
- `Mercury/Vulcanus = Cupido`: The thoughts are concentrated on the family, the arts or a big organization.
