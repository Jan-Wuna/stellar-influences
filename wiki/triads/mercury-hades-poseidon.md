## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Poseidon = Mercury`: unusual ideas, fixation on them, and immersion in strange or uncommon lines of thinking.
- `Mercury/Hades = Poseidon`: exposes the axis by making ignorance and falsity unmistakable.
- `Mercury/Poseidon = Hades`: To meet with stupidity.
