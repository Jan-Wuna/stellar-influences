## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Zeus = Mercury`: scoffing, hateful thinking, defamation, denunciation, lying propaganda, and fire or arson themes.
- `Mercury/Hades = Zeus`: drives the axis into aggressive mockery, threats, and distorted invention.
- `Mercury/Zeus = Hades`: Vile demands or requests. Orders and rules born out of villainy or baseness. Instigation to robbery, murder or theft.
