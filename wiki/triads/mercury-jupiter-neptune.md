## Derived Synthesis

Mercury, Jupiter and Neptune form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

Reinhold Ebertin's *The Combination of Stellar Influences* is represented on the other two orientations, but the linked Mercury/Jupiter = Neptune page has no Ebertin source subentry. Its other four source entries remain available; this is a source-specific coverage gap, not a missing activation.
