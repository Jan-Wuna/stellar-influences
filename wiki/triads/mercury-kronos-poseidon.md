## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Poseidon = Mercury`: high ideals, spiritualized thinking, and correspondence or discussion centered on cultural or high-minded concerns.
- `Mercury/Kronos = Poseidon`: clarifies the axis into high-minded ideas recognized by authority.
- `Mercury/Poseidon = Kronos`: Great ideas. High ideals. Leading thinker of a philosophy.
