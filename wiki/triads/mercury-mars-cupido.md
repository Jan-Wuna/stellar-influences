## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Cupido = Mercury`: verbalizes Mars/Cupido through debate, criticism, and planning around marriage or cooperation.
- `Mercury/Cupido = Mars`: activates the axis into making, crafting, and executing beauty.
- `Mercury/Mars = Cupido`: socializes the axis through lectures, consultations, and organized debate.
