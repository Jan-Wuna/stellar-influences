## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Jupiter = Mercury`: confident thought, successful speaking, and an abundance of plans or arguments directed toward constructive results.
- `Mercury/Jupiter = Mars`: big-picture thinking put into motion, with ideas expressed through force, initiative, and the urge to persuade actively.
- `Mercury/Mars = Jupiter`: energetic but beneficial thinking, persuasive criticism, and debate used to solve problems, enlarge opportunities, or move plans forward.
