## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Node = Mercury`: thought and speech directed toward collaboration, alliances, and collective action, often with words that stimulate or provoke movement within groups.
- `Mercury/Mars = Node`: connection through debate, teamwork under pressure, and relationships formed around verbal candor, challenge, and mental excitement.
- `Mercury/Node = Mars`: a charged relational mind, with networks shaped by argument, initiative, strategy, and the need to act on ideas together.
