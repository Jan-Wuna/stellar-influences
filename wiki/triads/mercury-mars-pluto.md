## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Pluto = Mercury`: forceful thinking, argumentative penetration, and the drive to make plans or ideas prevail at any cost.
- `Mercury/Mars = Pluto`: strategic verbal force, transformative or fanatical criticism, and the drive to make ideas prevail through pressure, persistence, or sheer intensity.
- `Mercury/Pluto = Mars`: cutting intelligence in action, with ideas used strategically, aggressively, and with decisive impact.
