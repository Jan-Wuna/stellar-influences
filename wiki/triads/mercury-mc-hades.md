## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Hades = Mercury`: mentalizes the axis into heavy thought, bad intentions, and problematic planning.
- `Mercury/Hades = MC`: internalizes the axis as concealed pessimism and rigid mental posture.
- `Mercury/MC = Hades`: darkens the axis through contact with atrocity, waste, disease, and history.
