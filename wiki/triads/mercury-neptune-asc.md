## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Asc = Neptune`: subtle, impressionable, or elusive social communication that may express intuition and imaginative linkage but can just as easily drift into misunderstanding, projection, or deception.
- `Mercury/Neptune = Asc`: a sensitive, elusive, or imaginative presence, with thought and communication expressed through atmosphere, suggestion, and subtle perception.
- `Neptune/Asc = Mercury`: an impressionable mind and suggestive presence, with identity shaped by intuition, subtle speech, imagination, and hard-to-pin-down perceptions of self and others.
