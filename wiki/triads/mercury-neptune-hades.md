## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Hades = Neptune`: confuses the axis further through deception and uncertainty.
- `Mercury/Neptune = Hades`: degrades the axis into murky obstruction and dirty plotting.
- `Neptune/Hades = Mercury`: This activation mentalizes the axis through confusion, loss of concentration, and impaired coherence, showing thought that becomes disordered, obsessive, or overtaken by decaying and unstable themes.
