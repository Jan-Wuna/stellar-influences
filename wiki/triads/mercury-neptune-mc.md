## Derived Synthesis

Mercury, Neptune and MC form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

The [Neptune/MC = Mercury ABC entry](../activations/neptune-mc-equals-mercury.md#abc-entry) preserves the German term Verkehr. Its sense between traffic and social interaction remains unresolved in the source-level translation, so the activation excludes that clause from confident synthesis.
