## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/MC = Neptune`: imaginative or intuitive self-direction, contemplation, and receptivity to subtle impressions, but also the risk that one's aims or public message become vague, misunderstood, or unrealistic.
- `Mercury/Neptune = MC`: a public path shaped by imagination, subtle communication, and the need to express vision, symbolism, or compassion visibly.
- `Neptune/MC = Mercury`: a vocation shaped by imagination, symbolism, and subtle messaging, with public identity built through ideas, stories, impressions, or elusive plans.
