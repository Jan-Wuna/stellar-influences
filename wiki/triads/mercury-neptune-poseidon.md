## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Neptune = Poseidon`: spiritualizes the axis into high-minded intuition and idea-search.
- `Mercury/Poseidon = Neptune`: Development of spiritual or mental thinking Transformation of one’s ideas or ideals.
- `Neptune/Poseidon = Mercury`: This activation mentalizes refined and subtle concerns through intuitive thought and debate, while making wrong insights or misleading information especially easy to absorb.
