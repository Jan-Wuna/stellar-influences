## Derived Synthesis

Mercury, Node and Asc form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

John Sandbach's compendium is represented on Mercury/Node = Asc and Node/Asc = Mercury, but the [Mercury/Asc = Node activation](../activations/mercury-asc-equals-node.md#source-entries) has no Sandbach source subentry. Its frontmatter declaration alone does not supply that missing paragraph.
