## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Pluto = Zeus`: To exert oneself. To recognize one’s own greater accomplishment.
- `Mercury/Zeus = Pluto`: Development of creative thinking. To learn to command and to obey.
- `Pluto/Zeus = Mercury`: This activation emphasizes to be at work with one’s thoughts; To direct one’s thoughts to one goal.
