## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Asc = Saturn`: inhibited exchange, careful or sorrowful discussion, and the experience of separation, refusal, or farewell through words and social contact.
- `Mercury/Saturn = Asc`: a serious, reserved presence, with careful speech, disciplined thought, and realism made visible in conduct and first impressions.
- `Saturn/Asc = Mercury`: cautious self-expression, with presence shaped by measured speech, realism, and a tendency to think before revealing oneself.
