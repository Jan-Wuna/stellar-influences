## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Neptune = Saturn`: imagination under restraint, with thought shaped by seriousness, caution, and the effort to make subtle perception workable.
- `Mercury/Saturn = Neptune`: careful thought strained by uncertainty, with the mind trying to give form, structure, or realism to what is vague, symbolic, or hard to define.
- `Saturn/Neptune = Mercury`: sober but uncertain thinking, with judgment shaped by realism, worry, subtle perception, and difficulty separating fact from fear or atmosphere.
