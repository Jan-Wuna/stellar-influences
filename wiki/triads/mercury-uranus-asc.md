## Derived Synthesis

Mercury, Uranus and Asc form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

The [Mercury/Uranus = Asc ABC subentry](../activations/mercury-uranus-equals-asc.md#abc-entry) contains bullet separators only. Its heading is retained, but no ABC delineation is currently represented. The other four sources and sibling formulas do not supply that missing paragraph.
