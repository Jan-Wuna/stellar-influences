## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/MC = Uranus`: sudden redirection of thought, originality in life aims, and a public or vocational role shaped by unexpected insights, rebellion, or mental shock.
- `Mercury/Uranus = MC`: publicly original intelligence, with innovation, technical skill, and unusual thinking shaping vocation and social impact.
- `Uranus/MC = Mercury`: inventive career thinking, sudden ideas about direction, and public expression shaped by originality, speed, and unconventional judgment.
