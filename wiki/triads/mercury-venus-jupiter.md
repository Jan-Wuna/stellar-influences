## Derived Synthesis

Mercury, Venus and Jupiter form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

Reinhold Ebertin's work has actual source subentries on Mercury/Venus = Jupiter and Venus/Jupiter = Mercury. The [Mercury/Jupiter = Venus activation](../activations/mercury-jupiter-equals-venus.md#source-entries) has no Ebertin subentry; its other four represented works do not supply one.
