## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Kronos = Venus`: softens the axis into refined relationships, artistic literature, and pleasant expert ties.
- `Mercury/Venus = Kronos`: High social connections. Promotion through acquaintance. Polite. Artistic taste of highest degree. Distinguished forms of behavior. Art critic.
- `Venus/Kronos = Mercury`: charitable thoughts.
