## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/MC = Venus`: a reflective outlook shaped by love, beauty, sympathy, and the wish to harmonize ideas, values, and public role.
- `Mercury/Venus = MC`: graceful public intelligence, with diplomacy, aesthetic judgment, and pleasing communication shaping vocation and social standing.
- `Venus/MC = Mercury`: persuasive public expression, with vocation shaped by tact, artistic speech, social intelligence, and the ability to communicate value attractively.
