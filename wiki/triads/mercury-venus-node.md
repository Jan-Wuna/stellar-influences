## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Node = Venus`: charming connection, with relationships built through conversation, courtesy, and the wish to keep communication harmonious and mutually pleasing.
- `Mercury/Venus = Node`: sociable harmony, with relationships built through conversation, courtesy, and the wish to keep contact pleasing and cooperative.
- `Venus/Node = Mercury`: agreeable communication, with friendship and attraction shaped by conversation, shared ideas, and the ability to keep interaction smooth and reciprocal.
