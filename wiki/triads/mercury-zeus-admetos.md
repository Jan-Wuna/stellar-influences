## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Admetos = Zeus`: directs the axis toward leadership problems, planning, and difficult beginnings.
- `Mercury/Zeus = Admetos`: Thoughts about a hard beginning. Orders for few.
- `Zeus/Admetos = Mercury`: To collect sketches for a new work, to concentrate ones thoughts on them. The beginning of preparation or the beginning of finishing the work.
