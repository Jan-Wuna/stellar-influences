## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Vulcanus = Moon`: prosperous public life, successful people or populations, and growth of collective well-being under strong expansion.
- `Moon/Apollon = Vulcanus`: To see efforts rewarded. To witness many hours of success. Might and power of masses in motion. Great success of nations. To spend many hours with mental themes. Exchange of higher and finer.
- `Moon/Vulcanus = Apollon`: Being noted or envied on account of one’s own wealth. Spreading the influence. Benevolence and amenities through officials of the public power. Successfully influencing the public.
