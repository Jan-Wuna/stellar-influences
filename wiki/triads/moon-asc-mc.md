## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/MC = Moon`: a personality and life path shaped by mood, receptivity, care, and the need for cordial understanding with others.
- `Moon/Asc = MC`: a visibly feeling-based path, with emotional presence and personal responsiveness tied closely to outer role and life course.
- `Moon/MC = Asc`: a visibly feeling-based presence, with public purpose and emotional responsiveness expressed directly through manner, appearance, and close contact.
