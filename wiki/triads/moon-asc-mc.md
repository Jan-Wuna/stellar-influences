## Derived Synthesis

Moon, Asc and MC form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

Reinhold Ebertin's work has source subentries on Asc/MC = Moon and Moon/MC = Asc. The [Moon/Asc = MC activation](../activations/moon-asc-equals-mc.md#source-entries) has no Ebertin subentry; its four represented works do not fill that source-specific gap.
