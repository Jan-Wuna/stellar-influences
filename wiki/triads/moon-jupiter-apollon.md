## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Apollon = Moon`: successful businesswomen or successful people in commerce or science, along with many cheerful people.
- `Moon/Apollon = Jupiter`: Many happy hours. Many happy hours spent together with other people. Well being of nations. Prosperity.
- `Moon/Jupiter = Apollon`: This activation expands `Moon/Jupiter` outward into shared joy, public blessing, and emotional abundance that reaches many people. It points to good feeling that spreads through groups, families, or wider communities, so private contentment becomes generosity, popularity, or collective uplift.
