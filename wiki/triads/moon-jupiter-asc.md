## Derived Synthesis

Moon, Jupiter and Asc form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

Ebertin is represented on Jupiter/Asc = Moon and Moon/Jupiter = Asc, but [Moon/Asc = Jupiter](../activations/moon-asc-equals-jupiter.md#source-entries) has no Ebertin subentry. The Sandbach locators for Jupiter/Asc = Moon and Moon/Asc = Jupiter remain chapter starts; Moon/Jupiter = Asc now has its owner's exact PDF30 text check.
