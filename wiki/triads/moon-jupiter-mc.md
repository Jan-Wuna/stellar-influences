## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/MC = Moon`: cheerful feeling, emotional confidence in one's path, and a warm, supportive bond between aspiration, temperament, and social or feminine response.
- `Moon/Jupiter = MC`: a benevolent public path, with emotional generosity, support, and hope strongly shaping career and visible purpose.
- `Moon/MC = Jupiter`: a benevolent outer role, with vocation and status shaped by generosity, support, and a visibly encouraging temperament.
