## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Node = Moon`: supportive belonging, emotional approval through connection, and happiness that grows through nurturing bonds, especially with women or the public.
- `Moon/Jupiter = Node`: generous connection, with belonging shaped by goodwill, support, and emotionally affirming social exchange.
- `Moon/Node = Jupiter`: broad-hearted connection, with ties shaped by encouragement, hospitality, and shared emotional confidence.
