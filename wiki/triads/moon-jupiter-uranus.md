## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Uranus = Moon`: emotional uplift, sudden happiness, intuitive optimism, and the capacity to feel opportunity or possibility very quickly.
- `Moon/Jupiter = Uranus`: an expansive but changeable emotional field, with goodwill expressed through freedom, surprise, and openness to new possibility.
- `Moon/Uranus = Jupiter`: expansive but unconventional feeling, with care and belonging shaped by optimism, surprise, and openness to new possibilities.
