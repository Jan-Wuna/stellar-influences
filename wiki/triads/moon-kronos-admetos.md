## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Admetos = Moon`: misunderstood or peculiar women and people whose special public position makes their behavior seem different from the norm.
- `Moon/Admetos = Kronos`: Offices. Red tape. Bureaucracy. Narrow minded bureaucratic attitude. A crowded great nation. A great nation without political influence. A great nation that has lost its independence and lives in the past.
- `Moon/Kronos = Admetos`: Decreased rendering of service. Reduction of officials.
