## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Apollon = Moon`: people active in trade, commerce, or science and the movement of business and traffic through the collective field.
- `Moon/Apollon = Mars`: Busy for many hours in trade, industry, or science.
- `Moon/Mars = Apollon`: This activation expands `Moon/Mars` through productivity, improvement, and wider application of effort. It points to longer working hours, more opportunities to act, and practical enhancement of marriage or home life, so instinctive drive becomes constructive, restorative, and more broadly useful.
