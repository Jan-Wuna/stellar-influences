## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Asc = Moon`: action driven by mood, irritation, or instinct, often bringing visible emotional friction or quarrels into everyday life and close contact with others.
- `Moon/Asc = Mars`: a quickly responsive presence, with feelings expressed through action, defense, and immediate bodily or emotional reaction.
- `Moon/Mars = Asc`: a reactive, energetic presence, with feeling expressed quickly through body, manner, and instinctive responses to what is happening now.
