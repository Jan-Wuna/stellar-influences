## Derived Synthesis

Moon, Mars and Asc form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

Ebertin is represented on Mars/Asc = Moon and Moon/Mars = Asc. The [Moon/Asc = Mars activation](../activations/moon-asc-equals-mars.md#source-entries) has no Ebertin subentry; its other four represented works do not supply that missing paragraph.
