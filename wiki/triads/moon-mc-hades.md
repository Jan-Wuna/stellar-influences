## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Hades = Moon`: emotionalizes the axis through worry about women and grief in public feeling.
- `Moon/Hades = MC`: Defamation through malicious women. To be enmeshed by bad women. Fiancee of low standards. Slandered, worried. Moment of loneliness or difficult circumstances for females. Tragically moment for the public.
- `Moon/MC = Hades`: This activation degrades `Moon/MC` into troubled feeling, low mental tone, and painful entanglement with difficult women or unhealthy emotional environments. It can show a life direction burdened by humiliation, emotional coarseness, or private distress that leaks into the public self.
