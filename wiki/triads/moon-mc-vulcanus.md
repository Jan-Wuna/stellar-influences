## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Vulcanus = Moon`: emotionalizes the axis through strong influence over women and the public.
- `Moon/MC = Vulcanus`: This activation intensifies `Moon/MC` with mental force, strong public ties, and emotionally consequential influence. It can show someone whose inner life is powerfully affected by women or the public, making the personal mind and feeling nature unusually forceful, impactful, and difficult to separate from collective pressures.
- `Moon/Vulcanus = MC`: General great events. Personage of great public might. Mighty nations.
