## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/MC = Moon`: a subjective, impressionable outlook in which life direction, opinion, and self-knowledge are strongly colored by moods, memories, and the need for emotional grounding.
- `Moon/MC = Mercury`: emotionally intelligent public expression, with vocation shaped by communication, responsiveness, and the ability to translate feeling into message or guidance.
- `Moon/Mercury = MC`: a public role shaped by responsive communication, emotional intelligence, and the ability to think and speak from lived human feeling.
