## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Pluto = Moon`: emotionally charged thought, with deep impressions, penetrating sensitivity, and mentally intense responses to what is felt or remembered.
- `Moon/Mercury = Pluto`: perceptive but heavy communication, with mood and mind fused around depth, secrecy, and transformative insight.
- `Moon/Pluto = Mercury`: penetrating feeling-thought, with emotional memory and instinct expressed through probing language, perception, and psychological depth.
