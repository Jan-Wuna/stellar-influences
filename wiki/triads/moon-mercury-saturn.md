## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Saturn = Moon`: restrained emotional thinking, with quiet reflection, careful memory, and a tendency to process experience soberly and inwardly.
- `Moon/Mercury = Saturn`: sober feeling-thinking, with communication shaped by reserve, memory, responsibility, and the need to weigh emotional consequences carefully.
- `Moon/Saturn = Mercury`: sober emotional thinking, with feeling translated into careful judgment, serious memory, and cautious expression.
