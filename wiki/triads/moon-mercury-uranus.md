## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Uranus = Moon`: emotionally electrified thought, with rapid impressions, intuitive flashes, and unstable but vivid mental responsiveness.
- `Moon/Mercury = Uranus`: an unusual mental-emotional field, with intuition and speech moving through flashes, surprises, and unstable impressions.
- `Moon/Uranus = Mercury`: quick emotional intelligence, with feeling and thought moving through flashes, sudden perception, and unconventional response.
