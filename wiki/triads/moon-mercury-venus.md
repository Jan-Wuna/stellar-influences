## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Venus = Moon`: affectionate thought, with tact, tenderness, and socially pleasing expression shaped by sensitivity, habit, and the need for emotional ease.
- `Moon/Mercury = Venus`: pleasant emotional communication, with thought and feeling linked through diplomacy, affection, and the wish to keep exchange harmonious.
- `Moon/Venus = Mercury`: channels the tender, receptive love of the Moon/Venus axis through Mercury, giving affection language, tact, and responsive thought. It points to emotional grace expressed through conversation, listening, and the ability to say what soothes or connects.
