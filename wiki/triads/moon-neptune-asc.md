## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Asc = Neptune`: a soft, elusive presence, with feelings and identity shaped by atmosphere, dream, longing, and subtle environmental influence.
- `Moon/Neptune = Asc`: a soft, porous, and highly sensitive presence, with feeling expressed through atmosphere, longing, and subtle emotional permeability.
- `Neptune/Asc = Moon`: a highly impressionable personality, with mood, sympathy, and unconscious response patterns strongly shaping how the person appears, bonds, and reacts.
