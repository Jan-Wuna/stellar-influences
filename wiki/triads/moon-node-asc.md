## Derived Synthesis

Moon, Node and Asc form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

[Moon/Asc = Node](../activations/moon-asc-equals-node.md#source-entries) has three actual subentries: Witte, ABC and Munkasey. The complete Sandbach Moon/Asc chapter on PDF pp. 39–40 omits Node; its unsupported metadata declaration has been removed. Ebertin also has no subentry there. Both works are represented on the siblings only.
