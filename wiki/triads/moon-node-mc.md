## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/MC = Node`: a relational life direction, with status and purpose shaped by emotional bonds, group connection, and the wish to belong visibly.
- `Moon/Node = MC`: a communal outer path, with career and purpose shaped by family feeling, group belonging, and emotionally resonant public ties.
- `Node/MC = Moon`: a public path shaped by family bonds, communal feeling, and the need to find direction through emotionally meaningful association.
