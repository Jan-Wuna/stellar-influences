## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Node = Zeus`: This activation energizes `Moon/Node` through active contact, decisive pursuit, and vigorously formed connection with women or groups. It makes alliance more dynamic and intentional, so emotional ties are pursued, mobilized, or put to work.
- `Moon/Zeus = Node`: Connections with drivers, stokers, soldiers. Contact with a procreative woman.
- `Node/Zeus = Moon`: To be a candidate. The elected one (female). The people elected or drafted to serve in army.
