## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Apollon = Pluto`: E Changes in hourly schedule. Changes or transformations in public develop from small beginnings. Changes or transformations in the masses of people, or in the structure of nations.
- `Moon/Pluto = Apollon`: This activation expands `Moon/Pluto` into changes that affect many people at once, especially in schedules, rhythms, or collective timing. It points to transformation distributed across a wider social field, where emotional or public adjustments ripple outward.
- `Pluto/Apollon = Moon`: This activation emphasizes precedent cases; First-time events in public; Events to which later on reference is made.
