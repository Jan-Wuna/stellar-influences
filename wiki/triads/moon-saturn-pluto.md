## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Pluto = Saturn`: severe emotional endurance, with depth and attachment shaped by caution, deprivation, and the need to survive inwardly under pressure.
- `Moon/Saturn = Pluto`: concentrated inner pressure, with habit, attachment, and vulnerability shaped by fear, control, and the need to withstand intensity.
- `Saturn/Pluto = Moon`: emotional compression, with feeling shaped by loss, endurance, and the need to survive inwardly under intense conditions.
