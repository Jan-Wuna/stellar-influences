## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Saturn = Uranus`: tension between safety and freedom, with feeling shaped by both reserve and sudden disruptive change.
- `Moon/Uranus = Saturn`: unstable restraint, with feeling shaped by the clash between freedom and security, disruption and control.
- `Saturn/Uranus = Moon`: emotional strain under restraint, with the need for freedom clashing against habit, security, or bodily sensitivity.
