## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/MC = Uranus`: an unconventional outer path, with vocation and status shaped by sensitivity to change, sudden insight, and unstable emotional climates.
- `Moon/Uranus = MC`: an unconventional emotional path, with outer role shaped by sudden shifts, intuitive change, and the need for freedom from confining patterns.
- `Uranus/MC = Moon`: inner restlessness around purpose, fluctuating public circumstances, and a strong need for freedom in the outer direction of life.
