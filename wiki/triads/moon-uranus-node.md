## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Node = Uranus`: unusual connection, with bonds shaped by rapid shifts, freedom needs, and fluctuating communal feeling.
- `Moon/Uranus = Node`: changeable connection, with family or group feeling shaped by freedom needs, sudden closeness, and unconventional attachment.
- `Uranus/Node = Moon`: emotional unrest in relationship, with social life shaped by changeable closeness, sudden attachment, and fluctuating communal atmospheres.
