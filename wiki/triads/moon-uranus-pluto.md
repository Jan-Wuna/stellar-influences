## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Pluto = Uranus`: intense feeling under abrupt change, with instinct and attachment shaped by rupture, awakening, and high-voltage inner pressure.
- `Moon/Uranus = Pluto`: a highly charged instinctive field, with attachment and habit shaped by rupture, pressure, and sudden catharsis.
- `Uranus/Pluto = Moon`: emotionally volatile transformation, with deep pressure and sudden change moving through mood, family imprint, and unconscious reaction patterns.
