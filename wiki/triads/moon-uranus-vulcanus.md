## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Uranus = Vulcanus`: This activation intensifies `Moon/Uranus` into mighty events, powerful excitement, and large-scale emotional or public shock. It favors upheaval with force behind it, making instinctive unrest feel overwhelming, consequential, and difficult to dissipate.
- `Moon/Vulcanus = Uranus`: To separate in an ugly or noisy manner. Unpleasant strike. To be separated in a loud, showy way. To lose great influence over females or the public. To do watch-duty.
