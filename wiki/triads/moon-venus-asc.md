## Derived Synthesis

Moon, Venus and Asc form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

[Moon/Asc = Venus](../activations/moon-asc-equals-venus.md#source-entries) has no Ebertin subentry. Ebertin is represented only on Moon/Venus = Asc and Venus/Asc = Moon; their coverage does not establish evidence for the missing orientation.
