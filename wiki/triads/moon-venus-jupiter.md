## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Jupiter = Venus`: abundant warmth, with emotional generosity expressed through kindness, beauty, comfort, and pleasing relationship.
- `Moon/Venus = Jupiter`: channels the tender, receptive love of the Moon/Venus axis through Jupiter, enlarging affection into generosity, emotional abundance, and supportive warmth. It points to comfort and love becoming expansive, hopeful, and nourishing on a broad scale.
- `Venus/Jupiter = Moon`: warm emotional abundance, with affection, pleasure, and generosity expressed through nurture, domestic ease, and the desire to create happiness around oneself.
