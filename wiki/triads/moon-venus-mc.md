## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/MC = Venus`: a caring and attractive public role, with vocation shaped by harmony, receptivity, and the wish to create beauty or comfort in the outer life.
- `Moon/Venus = MC`: channels the tender, receptive love of the Moon/Venus axis through the MC, making care, beauty, and harmony part of life direction and public role. It points to vocation shaped by emotional grace, support, and the wish to create a pleasing outer environment.
- `Venus/MC = Moon`: an emotionally receptive public role, with direction shaped by care, comfort, rapport, and the wish to be appreciated for warmth and harmony.
