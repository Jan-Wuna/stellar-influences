## Derived Synthesis

Moon, Venus and MC form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

The [Moon/Venus = MC Ebertin entry](../activations/moon-venus-equals-mc.md#ebertin-entry) explicitly separates footnote20, attached to Pluto0346, from MC0349; the footnote does not supply an MC claim.
