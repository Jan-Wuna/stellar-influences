## Derived Synthesis

Moon, Venus and Neptune form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

[Venus/Neptune = Moon](../activations/venus-neptune-equals-moon.md#source-entries) has four actual source subentries. The complete Sandbach Venus/Neptune chapter on PDF pp. 61–62 omits Moon; its unsupported metadata declaration has been removed. The siblings cannot supply this missing orientation.
