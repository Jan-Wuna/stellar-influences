## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Node = Venus`: gentle belonging, with ties shaped by tenderness, shared pleasure, and the wish to create emotional safety through warmth and harmony.
- `Moon/Venus = Node`: channels the tender, receptive love of the Moon/Venus axis through the Node, making affection social, familiar, and shared. It points to emotional belonging built through kindness, comfort, and the instinct to create harmony in bonds and community.
- `Venus/Node = Moon`: affectionate closeness, emotional rapport, and social bonds shaped by comfort, tenderness, and the wish to belong harmoniously.
