## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Admetos = Zeus`: To feel depressed at work. To work under pressure. To make up one’s mind.
- `Moon/Zeus = Admetos`: Decrease in working hours. Shrinking of accomplishment.
- `Zeus/Admetos = Moon`: Persons, the public or women who face new problems or surroundings.
