## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Admetos = Neptune`: confusion about place, hidden inferiority, and the weakening or surrender of residence or foothold.
- `Neptune/Admetos = Asc`: This activation externalizes `Neptune/Admetos` through environmental and interpersonal change. It shows altered living conditions being experienced through others, local surroundings, or economic shifts, so hardening or decline becomes concretely relational and situational.
- `Neptune/Asc = Admetos`: This activation compresses relational uncertainty into vigilance and guardedness, showing a need to narrow exposure so deception, fraud, or manipulation cannot easily take hold.
