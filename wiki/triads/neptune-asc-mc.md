## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/MC = Neptune`: sensitivity, imagination, psychic or subtle receptivity, and spiritualized self-concept, but also uncertainty, feigning, and disappointment in relationships or circumstances.
- `Neptune/Asc = MC`: a visibly impressionable identity, with vocation and status shaped by ideals, subtle influence, uncertainty, or the search for a more meaningful self-definition.
- `Neptune/MC = Asc`: a visibly idealistic or uncertain identity, with vocation, purpose, and public image strongly shaping how the person appears and how others read them.
