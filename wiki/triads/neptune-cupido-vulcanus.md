## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Vulcanus = Neptune`: the beginning of dissolution in a family or association and pretended belonging to a mighty group.
- `Neptune/Cupido = Vulcanus`: This activation intensifies group and family breakup through force, showing separation imposed with pressure, compulsion, or overpowering circumstances rather than gradual drifting apart.
- `Neptune/Vulcanus = Cupido`: This activation brings unstable hidden pressure into partnership and group bonds. It often points to unions that form under strain, lose coherence quickly, or dissolve once the underlying weakness or deception becomes impossible to ignore.
