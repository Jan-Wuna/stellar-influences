## Derived Synthesis

- `Kronos/Vulcanus = Neptune`: a great power unable to continue, the breakdown of a nation-state's force, and the fading of concentrated authority.
- `Neptune/Kronos = Vulcanus`: This activation intensifies compromised authority into powerful deception, combining great-power diplomacy with the forceful consequences of incompetent or misleading leadership.
- `Neptune/Vulcanus = Kronos`: A power structure or authority is portrayed as losing effective command, with weakness, failure of leadership, or governmental incapacity brought to the surface.
