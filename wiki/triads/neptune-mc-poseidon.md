## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Poseidon = Neptune`: softens the axis into delicacy, enthusiasm, and non-material orientation.
- `Neptune/MC = Poseidon`: This activation spiritualizes uncertainty in a distorted way, producing bluffing, simulated intelligence, or the appearance of insight without real substance behind it.
- `Neptune/Poseidon = MC`: This activation internalizes the axis through introspection and self-reflection, but ties self-knowledge to the risk of adopting a misleading philosophy or being seen as aligned with it.
