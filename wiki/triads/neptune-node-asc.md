## Derived Synthesis

Neptune, Node and Asc form one unordered lookup set. Each formula above gives a different midpoint pair and activator. Follow the exact formula for its interpretation and source entries, its base axis for shared pair meaning, and its factor links for standalone evidence.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Different wording across these formulas does not itself establish a contradiction: each formula makes a distinct claim.

The [Neptune/Node = Asc ABC heading](../activations/neptune-node-equals-asc.md#abc-entry) contains only bullet/dot separators; no ABC delineation is represented there. Its [Witte entry](../activations/neptune-node-equals-asc.md#witte-entry) separately retains “odters”. Other works and sibling formulas cannot supply the absent ABC paragraph.
