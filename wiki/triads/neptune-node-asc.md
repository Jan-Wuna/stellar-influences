## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Asc = Node`: a relationally impressionable presence, with identity shaped by subtle bonds, idealized encounters, and the tendency to absorb the tone of relationships and social environments.
- `Neptune/Node = Asc`: an identity shaped by subtle bonds, porous relating, and the tendency to meet others through sympathy, impressionability, or invisible expectations.
- `Node/Asc = Neptune`: a highly impressionable social identity, with close relationships and the immediate environment shaping the self through empathy, projection, and subtle emotional atmosphere.
