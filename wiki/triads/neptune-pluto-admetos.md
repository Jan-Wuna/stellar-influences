## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Admetos = Pluto`: This activation transforms `Neptune/Admetos` almost imperceptibly at first, beginning a deep reworking of life conditions beneath the surface. It points to slow, subtle mutation that eventually alters the form of being more completely than first appears.
- `Neptune/Pluto = Admetos`: This activation compresses hidden transformation into profound, low-visibility change, where dissolution works at depth and gradually reshapes conditions from below the surface.
- `Pluto/Admetos = Neptune`: This activation emphasizes one experiences deep changes in the near future; Coming events develop below the surface and can be noticed in small symptoms.
