## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/MC = Pluto`: consequential but opaque ambition, with vocation shaped by fascination, secret motives, psychological pressure, and the desire to transform life through an all-consuming ideal.
- `Neptune/Pluto = MC`: a vocation shaped by hidden transformation, spiritual or psychological depth, and callings that work through invisible but consequential processes.
- `Pluto/MC = Neptune`: a mysterious but consequential vocation, with public fate shaped by invisible forces, symbolic power, and deep transformation operating behind the scenes.
