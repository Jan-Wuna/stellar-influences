## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Apollon = Zeus`: This activation energizes `Neptune/Apollon` through active but wasted effort in uncertain, occult, or speculative work. It turns the axis toward production and experimentation, though the output often disperses into futility or invisible labor.
- `Neptune/Zeus = Apollon`: Imagination and directed force expand into specialized production, especially inventive work or large-scale dealings with fuels, gas, or similar materials.
- `Zeus/Apollon = Neptune`: Business man engaged in daring enterprises and speculations. Research in a new field, daring to explore unknown territory.
