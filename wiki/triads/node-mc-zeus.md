## Derived Synthesis

- `MC/Zeus = Node`: socializes the axis into teamwork and shared activity.
- `Node/MC = Zeus`: To be incited. To be aroused mentally. Want to achieve the personal goal in a relationship.
- `Node/Zeus = MC`: To be elected or selected. The driver. Wishing to produce something with several others. To force oneself or to submit to force.
