## Derived Synthesis

- `Node/Admetos = Zeus`: Start work with a few. Difficult beginning of work with others.
- `Node/Zeus = Admetos`: A work-community fighting against odds, great obstacles and opposition. To have to overcome all obstacles at the beginning.
- `Zeus/Admetos = Node`: To be called upon to solve new problems. To be part of solving problems. To be involved in changed conditions. To be, in part, the cause of changed conditions.
