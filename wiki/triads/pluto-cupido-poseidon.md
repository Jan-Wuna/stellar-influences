## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Poseidon = Pluto`: the establishment, evolution, and transformation of a spiritual, cultural, or philosophical community.
- `Pluto/Cupido = Poseidon`: This activation emphasizes building or developing of a philosophical society.
- `Pluto/Poseidon = Cupido`: This activation emphasizes to develop in cooperation with others a different outlook on life, a political doctrine or philosophy.
