## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/MC = Saturn`: inhibition, burden, separation, and slow structural development as the force that tests the personality and its path.
- `Saturn/Asc = MC`: a grave personal bearing tied closely to public duty, with self-presentation shaped by responsibility, reputation, and the long work of self-definition.
- `Saturn/MC = Asc`: a visibly serious bearing, with life direction and public responsibility expressed through reserve, caution, and a controlled personal manner.
