## Derived Synthesis

- `Chiron/Neptune = Saturn`: blocked receptivity, fear of the invisible, and healing through patient trust in subtle guidance. Main caution: allowing fear, skepticism, or heaviness to shut down a needed attunement.
- `Saturn/Chiron = Neptune` intuits the meaning behind restriction, fear, and the effort to overcome limits. It can soften Saturn/Chiron through spiritual perception; the caution is confusion or spiritual bypass when concrete limits still need direct handling.
- `Saturn/Neptune = Chiron` heals by revealing the gap between ideals and reality and by helping fears come into view for release. It is useful for spiritualizing disappointment without denying it; the caution is to keep compassion connected to practical boundaries.
