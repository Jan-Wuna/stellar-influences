- `Neptune/Admetos = Saturn`: This activation slows `Neptune/Admetos` into preservation, endurance, or very gradual transition. It suggests conditions that change only reluctantly, where decay and hardening are stabilized, prolonged, or carefully managed rather than rapidly overturned.
