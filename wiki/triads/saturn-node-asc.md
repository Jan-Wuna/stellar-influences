## Derived Synthesis

- `Node/Asc = Saturn`: a careful, socially burdened identity, with close relationships shaping the self through responsibility, caution, obligation, or inhibited contact.
- `Saturn/Asc = Node`: a guarded social presence, with ties formed under duty, caution, or the sense that closeness must be earned slowly.
- `Saturn/Node = Asc`: a guarded social presence, with identity shaped by duty to others, cautious contact, and ties that feel serious from the outset.
