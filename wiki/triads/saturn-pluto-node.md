## Derived Synthesis

- `Pluto/Node = Saturn`: a burdensome but consequential alliance field, with relationships shaped by obligation, loss, survival, and the weight of long-term collective pressure.
- `Saturn/Node = Pluto`: serious relationship under compulsion, with bonds shaped by control, survival, or high-stakes institutional conditions.
- `Saturn/Pluto = Node`: weighty association, with relationships shaped by pressure, obligation, power imbalances, or the need to endure serious shared conditions.
