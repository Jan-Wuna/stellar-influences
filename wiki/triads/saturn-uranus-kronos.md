## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Kronos = Uranus`: unexpected losses in securities.
- `Saturn/Uranus = Kronos`: losses of the father or chief, intervention by the power of the state, separation through the state.
- `Uranus/Kronos = Saturn`: Losses caused by the ruling powers, by powers suddenly breaking forth. To separate or rid oneself of something with help from the state.
