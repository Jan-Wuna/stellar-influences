## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Node = Uranus`: unstable alliance, with relationships strained by the clash between obligation and freedom.
- `Saturn/Uranus = Node`: tense association, with ties formed around pressure, reform, separation, or the need to renegotiate rigid social patterns.
- `Uranus/Node = Saturn`: tension between freedom and obligation in relationship, with associations tested by structure, distance, or the need to reform slowly.
