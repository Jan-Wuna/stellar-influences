## Derived Synthesis

- `Admetos/Poseidon = Sun`: the individual undergoing schooling, apprenticeship, or disciplined formation so that education becomes part of self-development.
- `Sun/Admetos = Poseidon`: This formula emphasizes to go mentally to the bottom of a matter.
- `Sun/Poseidon = Admetos`: This formula emphasizes deep thought and mentally getting to the bottom of things.
