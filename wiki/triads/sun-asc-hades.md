## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Hades = Sun`: the self coming physically into contact with dirt, contamination, or harm through other people or the immediate surroundings.
- `Sun/Asc = Hades`: This formula emphasizes weak or feeble at the time and a sufferer.
- `Sun/Hades = Asc`: This formula emphasizes sickness in the environment and unsympathetic behavior.
