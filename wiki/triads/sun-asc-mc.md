## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/MC = Sun`: conscious participation, cooperation, vitality, and the need to align personality, life role, and relationship stance around a recognizable center of purpose.
- `Sun/Asc = MC`: public visibility, strong self-definition around purpose, and a close link between personal image and the course one tries to establish in the world.
- `Sun/MC = Asc`: visible authority, a strong sense of personal mission, and a manner that naturally presents itself as important, formative, or central.
