## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Kronos = Sun`: going to the registry office, rejoining family or former union, and living as a recognized professional artist.
- `Sun/Cupido = Kronos`: This formula emphasizes artist marriage and marriage of rank.
- `Sun/Kronos = Cupido`: This formula emphasizes head of the family and family of rank.
