## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Node = Sun`: good fellowship, social justice, and conscious growth through positive, visible alliance.
- `Sun/Jupiter = Node`: popularity, helpful associations, and opportunities that arise through cooperation, patronage, or the goodwill of others.
- `Sun/Node = Jupiter`: recognition through community, supportive alliances, and public or group contact that opens opportunities and strengthens confidence.
