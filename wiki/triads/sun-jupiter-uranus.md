## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Uranus = Sun`: inventive confidence, a lively pursuit of unique goals, and the sense that personal growth comes through freedom, originality, and timely lucky breaks.
- `Sun/Jupiter = Uranus`: sudden opportunity, inventive success, and expansion through independence, unusual timing, or bold departure from convention.
- `Sun/Uranus = Jupiter`: breakthrough success, confidence in unconventional paths, and expansion through innovation, risk, or timely revolt against limits.
