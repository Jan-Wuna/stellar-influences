## Source Coverage

Each linked activation currently has entries from all five sources below. This lists canonical source coverage, not the full contents of the works. Munkasey's retained locators are printed entry pages; other entries are linked without inventing missing page numbers.

- `Mars/Neptune = Sun`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/mars-neptune-equals-sun.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/mars-neptune-equals-sun.md#abc-entry); [Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences](../activations/mars-neptune-equals-sun.md#ebertin-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/mars-neptune-equals-sun.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/mars-neptune-equals-sun.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `236`.
- `Sun/Mars = Neptune`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-mars-equals-neptune.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-mars-equals-neptune.md#abc-entry); [Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences](../activations/sun-mars-equals-neptune.md#ebertin-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/sun-mars-equals-neptune.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/sun-mars-equals-neptune.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `68`.
- `Sun/Neptune = Mars`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-neptune-equals-mars.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-neptune-equals-mars.md#abc-entry); [Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences](../activations/sun-neptune-equals-mars.md#ebertin-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/sun-neptune-equals-mars.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/sun-neptune-equals-mars.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `84`.

For `Mars/Neptune = Sun`, the current entry cites Witte one-based PDF entry-start p. `195`, ABC printed p. `304`, Ebertin printed p. `211`, entry `0723`, Sandbach one-based PDF chapter-start p. `72`, and Munkasey printed p. `236`; the Sandbach number is not an exact formula locator.

Coverage limitations: `Mars/Neptune = Sun` reports damaged ABC water wording and an omitted opening metaphysics clause. `Sun/Mars = Neptune` qualifies an appended Ebertin example about the separate Uranus orientation. `Sun/Neptune = Mars` qualifies damaged ABC dissolution/physical-activity wording (printed p. `154`; PDF p. `76`).

## Contradictions Across Orientations

The formulas have distinct base pairs and activators. Their different meanings are not contradictions merely because the same three factors recur. This structural hub makes no cross-orientation doctrinal comparison; the activation pages retain their own source differences and qualifications.
