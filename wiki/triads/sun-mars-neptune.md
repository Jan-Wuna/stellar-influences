## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Neptune = Sun`: a weakened or porous core drive, with identity and health easily drained by subtle pressures, sacrifice, or unclear action, yet also capable of intuitive or compassionate response.
- `Sun/Mars = Neptune`: drained or undermined action, loss of force through fraud, fatigue, illness, or misdirection, and the feeling of being unable to get things done in a straightforward way.
- `Sun/Neptune = Mars`: effort directed toward idealistic or subtle aims, with action that may be compassionate and sacrificial or else ineffective, self-defeating, and physically depleted.
