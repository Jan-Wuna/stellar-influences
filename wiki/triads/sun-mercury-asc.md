## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Asc = Sun`: a strong need to define oneself through exchange of ideas, conversation, and active engagement with one's environment.
- `Sun/Asc = Mercury`: lively self-articulation, quick personal responsiveness, and identity expressed through speech, thought, movement, and exchange with the immediate environment.
- `Sun/Mercury = Asc`: a distinctly personal communication style, strong awareness of surroundings, and identity shaped by exchange of ideas, correspondence, and negotiation.
