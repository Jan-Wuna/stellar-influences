## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Jupiter = Sun`: a visible intellectual worldview, with self-expression shaped by teaching, judgment, and the need to communicate meaning confidently.
- `Sun/Jupiter = Mercury`: broad thinking, persuasive speech, and judgment shaped by optimism, confidence, teaching, publishing, or the wish to see the larger pattern.
- `Sun/Mercury = Jupiter`: optimistic intelligence, persuasive speech, and the ability to organize ideas broadly and express them in a way that gains protection, sponsorship, or opportunity.
