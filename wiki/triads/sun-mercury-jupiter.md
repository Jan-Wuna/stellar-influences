## Source Coverage

The list below follows the source entries actually present on each linked activation. Source coverage differs by orientation. This lists canonical source coverage, not the full contents of the works. Munkasey's retained locators are printed entry pages; other entries are linked without inventing missing page numbers.

- `Mercury/Jupiter = Sun`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/mercury-jupiter-equals-sun.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/mercury-jupiter-equals-sun.md#abc-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/mercury-jupiter-equals-sun.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/mercury-jupiter-equals-sun.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `156`.
- `Sun/Jupiter = Mercury`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-jupiter-equals-mercury.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-jupiter-equals-mercury.md#abc-entry); [Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences](../activations/sun-jupiter-equals-mercury.md#ebertin-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/sun-jupiter-equals-mercury.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/sun-jupiter-equals-mercury.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `72`.
- `Sun/Mercury = Jupiter`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-mercury-equals-jupiter.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-mercury-equals-jupiter.md#abc-entry); [Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences](../activations/sun-mercury-equals-jupiter.md#ebertin-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/sun-mercury-equals-jupiter.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/sun-mercury-equals-jupiter.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `60`.

`Mercury/Jupiter = Sun` currently has no Ebertin entry; this describes the canonical page, not an asserted omission in Ebertin's full work. `Sun/Jupiter = Mercury` also has [Aren Ober - Midpoint Interpretation Simplified](../activations/sun-jupiter-equals-mercury.md#ober-entry), one-based PDF p. `130`. Ober is not represented on the other two linked orientations.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

The formulas have distinct base pairs and activators. Their different meanings are not contradictions merely because the same three factors recur. This structural hub makes no cross-orientation doctrinal comparison; the activation pages retain their own source differences and qualifications.
