## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Saturn = Sun`: conscientious selfhood, with serious judgment, mental endurance, and disciplined thought woven into central aims and character.
- `Sun/Mercury = Saturn`: serious, slow, and carefully structured thought, often shaped by life experience, parting, travel, or the need to say only what can withstand scrutiny.
- `Sun/Saturn = Mercury`: sober judgment, concentration, cautious planning, and a serious cast of mind that thinks in terms of structure, limits, and consequences.
