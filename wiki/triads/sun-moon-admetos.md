## Contradictions Across Orientations

The [Moon/Admetos = Sun Witte entry](../activations/moon-admetos-equals-sun.md#witte-entry) retains historical death and timing assertions. The activation's source notes keep those claims attributed and do not establish a predictive timing method.

Different orientations are distinct formulas, so differences between their entries do not establish contradictions. This hub performs no cross-orientation doctrinal comparison; consult the individual activation pages for their source differences and limits.
