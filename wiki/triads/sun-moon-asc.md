## Source Coverage

Sun/Asc = Moon and Sun/Moon = Asc each have five source entries; Moon/Asc = Sun has four, with no Ebertin entry in the canonical page. This lists canonical source coverage, not the full contents of the works. Munkasey's retained locators are printed entry pages; other entries are linked without inventing missing page numbers.

- `Moon/Asc = Sun`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/moon-asc-equals-sun.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/moon-asc-equals-sun.md#abc-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/moon-asc-equals-sun.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/moon-asc-equals-sun.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `140`.
- `Sun/Asc = Moon`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-asc-equals-moon.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-asc-equals-moon.md#abc-entry); [Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences](../activations/sun-asc-equals-moon.md#ebertin-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/sun-asc-equals-moon.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/sun-asc-equals-moon.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `96`.
- `Sun/Moon = Asc`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-moon-equals-asc.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-moon-equals-asc.md#abc-entry); [Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences](../activations/sun-moon-equals-asc.md#ebertin-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/sun-moon-equals-asc.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/sun-moon-equals-asc.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `56`.

Coverage limitation: Moon/Asc = Sun has no canonical Ebertin entry; this is an observed wiki coverage gap, not a claim about the complete work.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

The formulas have distinct base pairs and activators. Their different meanings are not contradictions merely because the same three factors recur. This structural hub makes no cross-orientation doctrinal comparison; the activation pages retain their own source differences and qualifications.
