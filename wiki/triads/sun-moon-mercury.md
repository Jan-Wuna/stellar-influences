## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Mercury = Sun`: an expressive and emotionally aware selfhood, with identity shaped by thought, speech, and the ability to verbalize inner life directly.
- `Sun/Mercury = Moon`: thinking with the heart, a strong need to form one's own conclusions, and communication shaped by feeling, vulnerability, and the desire for inner reassurance.
- `Sun/Moon = Mercury`: communication as the unifying function between feeling and will, often showing shared ideas, intimate conversation, or the wish to think through partnership and life-balance.
