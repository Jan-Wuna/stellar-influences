## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/MC = Sun`: a selfhood strongly tied to vision, calling, and idealized life direction, with public purpose shaping the core sense of who one is.
- `Sun/MC = Neptune`: uncertainty about vocation or mission, a longing for more spiritual meaning in life direction, and difficulty keeping objectives clear and grounded.
- `Sun/Neptune = MC`: a life path shaped by imagination, psychic sensitivity, sacrifice, or subtle insight, with public purpose often tied to art, healing, symbolism, or invisible influences.
