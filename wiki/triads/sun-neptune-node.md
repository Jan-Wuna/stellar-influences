## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Node = Sun`: a selfhood strongly affected by subtle bonds, with energy and purpose shaped by idealized, spiritual, or deceptive relationships and collective atmospheres.
- `Sun/Neptune = Node`: connection with weak, sensitive, sick, draining, or spiritually charged people, and relationships that either uplift through subtle sympathy or exhaust through porous boundaries and wishful attachment.
- `Sun/Node = Neptune`: association colored by intuition, longing, sacrifice, or vagueness, with group ties that can feel inspiring but also disappointing, draining, or hard to define.
