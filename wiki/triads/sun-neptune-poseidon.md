## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Poseidon = Sun`: This activation personalizes spiritual receptivity and reflective insight, making identity more porous, introspective, and oriented toward psychology, devotion, or refined study. It can mark genuine self-knowledge, but also the danger of embodying false doctrine or sacrificing clarity to an idealized worldview.
- `Sun/Neptune = Poseidon`: This formula emphasizes spiritual truth, vision, and inspired inner guidance.
- `Sun/Poseidon = Neptune`: This activation dissolves mental clarity into confusion and helplessness. It can show disordered thought, spiritual disorientation, or chaos in the guiding idea that normally gives the self coherence and direction.
