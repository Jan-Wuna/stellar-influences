## Derived Synthesis

- `Node/Poseidon = Sun`: Being bodily together with others of the same mind or mentality. Bodily contact with people of high mentality.
- `Sun/Node = Poseidon`: This formula emphasizes spiritual association and truthful or idealistic bonds.
- `Sun/Poseidon = Node`: This formula emphasizes truthful association and spiritual or idealistic connection.
