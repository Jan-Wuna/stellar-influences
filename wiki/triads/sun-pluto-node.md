## Derived Synthesis

- `Pluto/Node = Sun`: a selfhood empowered or altered through powerful associations, with purpose and visibility shaped by consequential bonds and collective leverage.
- `Sun/Node = Pluto`: the urge to influence others strongly, reshape alliances, and bind one's identity to mass movements, deep loyalties, or decisive changes in relationship patterns.
- `Sun/Pluto = Node`: fateful association, transformative ties, and contact with people who either strengthen or undermine the native's personal power and growth.
