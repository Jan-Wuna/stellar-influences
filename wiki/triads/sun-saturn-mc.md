## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/MC = Sun`: a serious life mission, with self-definition strongly tied to duty, endurance, and the desire to earn recognition through tested achievement.
- `Sun/MC = Saturn`: slow recognition, serious decisions, and a life path tested by sadness, inhibition, or the need to mature through difficulty.
- `Sun/Saturn = MC`: a serious public role, responsibility in career matters, and a path shaped by duty, endurance, and the need to build recognition slowly.
