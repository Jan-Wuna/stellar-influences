## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Node = Sun`: a serious relational identity, with self-expression shaped by loyalty, duty, and the need to carry responsibilities toward others visibly.
- `Sun/Node = Saturn`: reserve in association, difficult public contact, and the experience that ties, groups, or partnerships require patience, endurance, and realism.
- `Sun/Saturn = Node`: serious ties, duty in relationships, and the sense that belonging must be earned through patience, loyalty, and realism.
