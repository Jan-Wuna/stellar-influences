## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Neptune = Uranus`: abrupt intuitive awakening, nervous upset, or unforeseen weakness that breaks continuity and makes the subtle or hidden dramatically visible.
- `Sun/Uranus = Neptune`: inspired originality, unusual intuition, and identity touched by dream, idealism, or elusive impressions that do not fit ordinary logic.
- `Uranus/Neptune = Sun`: a visionary individuality, with self-expression shaped by unusual ideals, subtle perception, and the urge to live from a different imaginative or spiritual center.
