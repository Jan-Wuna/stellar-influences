## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Node = Uranus`: disruptive association, abrupt encounters, and alliances that awaken change or tension around collective direction.
- `Sun/Uranus = Node`: unconventional associations, reform-minded networks, and identity sharpened through encounters that awaken independence or disrupt familiar social patterns.
- `Uranus/Node = Sun`: a socially awakening identity, with self-expression shaped by unusual alliances, progressive causes, and the urge to connect through difference rather than conformity.
