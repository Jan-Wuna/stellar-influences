## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Pluto = Uranus`: obsessive innovation, sudden life reorientation, and a forceful will that breaks old conditions in order to establish something radically new.
- `Sun/Uranus = Pluto`: radical transformation, pressure to break old forms, and the capacity to wield disruptive power on a deep or collective scale.
- `Uranus/Pluto = Sun`: a selfhood built for radical change, with will and life direction shaped by breakthrough, uncompromising transformation, and the drive to overturn what is stale.
