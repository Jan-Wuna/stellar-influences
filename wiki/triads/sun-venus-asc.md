## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Asc = Venus`: charm in self-presentation, likability in direct contact, and identity expressed through attractiveness, tact, and the wish to create pleasant exchange.
- `Sun/Venus = Asc`: visible charm, with love, beauty, and social grace expressed directly through presence, manner, and close contact.
- `Venus/Asc = Sun`: visible charm, with self-expression shaped by affection, beauty, and the wish to be recognized as warm, appealing, and socially graceful.
