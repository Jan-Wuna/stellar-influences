## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Hades = Venus`: This formula emphasizes painful love and unpleasant relationships.
- `Sun/Venus = Hades`: This formula emphasizes painful affection and difficulty in love or comfort.
- `Venus/Hades = Sun`: to find oneself in a low position and dependence.
