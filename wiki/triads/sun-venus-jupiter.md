## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Jupiter = Venus`: charm, social advantage, artistic enjoyment, and growth that comes through likability, diplomacy, or benefic support.
- `Sun/Venus = Jupiter`: broad warmth, social favor, and a confident expression of love, value, and harmony.
- `Venus/Jupiter = Sun`: a warm-hearted selfhood, with love, generosity, and social grace woven into confidence, visibility, and central purpose.
