## Source Coverage

All three orientations have the five shared sources below; additional coverage is named only on its actual orientation. This lists canonical source coverage, not the full contents of the works. Munkasey's retained locators are printed entry pages; other entries are linked without inventing missing page numbers.

- `Sun/Venus = Jupiter`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-venus-equals-jupiter.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/sun-venus-equals-jupiter.md#abc-entry); [Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences](../activations/sun-venus-equals-jupiter.md#ebertin-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/sun-venus-equals-jupiter.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/sun-venus-equals-jupiter.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `64`.
- `Venus/Jupiter = Sun`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/venus-jupiter-equals-sun.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/venus-jupiter-equals-sun.md#abc-entry); [Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences](../activations/venus-jupiter-equals-sun.md#ebertin-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/venus-jupiter-equals-sun.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/venus-jupiter-equals-sun.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `192`.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

The formulas have distinct base pairs and activators. Their different meanings are not contradictions merely because the same three factors recur. This structural hub makes no cross-orientation doctrinal comparison; the activation pages retain their own source differences and qualifications.
