## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/MC = Venus`: a life direction supported by love, aesthetics, peace-making, and the wish to contribute something pleasing or meaningful to the world.
- `Sun/Venus = MC`: a public expression of affection, beauty, and value, with life purpose strongly shaped by harmony, artistry, and the wish to be appreciated.
- `Venus/MC = Sun`: a visibly gracious sense of purpose, with self-expression shaped by beauty, affection, appreciation, and the wish to be known for harmony and value.
