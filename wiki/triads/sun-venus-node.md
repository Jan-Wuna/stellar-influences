## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Node = Venus`: love union, sociable recognition, and association formed through art, charm, attraction, or the wish to be appreciated by others.
- `Sun/Venus = Node`: affectionate connection as the vehicle for self-expression, with social bonds helping love and worth become visible and meaningful.
- `Venus/Node = Sun`: a relationally expressive identity, with warmth, affection, and social appreciation central to how the self shines and connects.
