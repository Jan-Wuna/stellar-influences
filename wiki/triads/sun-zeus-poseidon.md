## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Poseidon = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes inspired creation and fiery pursuit of ideals.
- `Sun/Zeus = Poseidon`: This formula emphasizes inspired creation and fiery pursuit of ideals.
- `Zeus/Poseidon = Sun`: Days of good mental creating. The man who mentally becomes independent, who must show his conviction and declare himself.
