## Derived Synthesis

- `Sun/Vulcanus = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes forceful creation and powerful active drive.
- `Sun/Zeus = Vulcanus`: This formula emphasizes forceful creation and powerful active drive.
- `Zeus/Vulcanus = Sun`: The man who works hard at machines and apparatus, who adjusts and serves industrial machines. The man who directs natural forces.
