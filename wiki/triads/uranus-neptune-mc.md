## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/MC = Uranus`: an unstable but visionary vocation, with life direction shaped by sudden inspiration, unconventional ideals, and the urge to break from ordinary social definitions of purpose.
- `Uranus/MC = Neptune`: an inspired but elusive public path, with life direction shaped by ideals, intuition, and disruptions that are hard to define clearly.
- `Uranus/Neptune = MC`: a visionary but unstable public path, with outer life shaped by unusual ideals, spiritualized change, and abrupt shifts in purpose or role.
