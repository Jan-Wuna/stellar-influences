## Derived Synthesis

- `Node/MC = Uranus`: a public path shaped by unconventional alliances, abrupt changes in association, and the need to grow through freer, less predictable forms of belonging.
- `Uranus/MC = Node`: unconventional alliances, reform-minded associations, and a career or mission advanced through unusual people and collective change.
- `Uranus/Node = MC`: a change-oriented social path, with public direction shaped by unusual alliances, reformist groups, and abrupt shifts in collective standing.
