## Derived Synthesis

- `Pluto/MC = Uranus`: a radically transformative life path, with public purpose shaped by breaks, shocks, and the need to overthrow what can no longer hold.
- `Uranus/MC = Pluto`: revolutionary ambition, disruptive influence, and the power to break old outer structures under concentrated force.
- `Uranus/Pluto = MC`: a destiny shaped by upheaval, with vocation and reputation transformed through radical turns, breakdowns, and uncompromising change.
