## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Cupido = Venus`: love leading to marriage, harmonious community, and an impulse to beautify and keep the home well.
- `Venus/Asc = Cupido`: social acquaintances.
- `Venus/Cupido = Asc`: to be desired for marriage by many.
