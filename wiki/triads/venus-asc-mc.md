## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/MC = Venus`: beauty, agreement, affection, and relational ease as the factor that softens personality, supports cooperation, and makes one's path more pleasant or attractive.
- `Venus/Asc = MC`: a graceful outer image, with personal charm and attractiveness strongly tied to life direction, reputation, and the wish to be valued visibly.
- `Venus/MC = Asc`: a graceful public presence, with identity expressed through charm, taste, social intelligence, and a visible desire to create harmony around oneself.
