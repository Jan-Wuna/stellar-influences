## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Poseidon = Venus`: religious or spiritually congenial acquaintances and a preference for people of refined belief or gentle moral inclination.
- `Venus/Asc = Poseidon`: spiritual harmony with others.
- `Venus/Poseidon = Asc`: acquaintance with priests, preachers.
