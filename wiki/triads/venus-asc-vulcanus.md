## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Vulcanus = Venus`: enjoying broad favor with others and feeling well within a strong environment.
- `Venus/Asc = Vulcanus`: to be greatly loved by others.
- `Venus/Vulcanus = Asc`: to be powerfully attracted by others or to attract others powerfully.
