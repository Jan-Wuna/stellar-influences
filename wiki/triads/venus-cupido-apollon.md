## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Apollon = Venus`: pleasant union with many, familiar harmony through art or science, and specifically vocal or singing art.
- `Venus/Apollon = Cupido`: feeling of happiness in or through the family.
- `Venus/Cupido = Apollon`: much luck in marriage or society.
