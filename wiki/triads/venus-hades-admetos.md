## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Admetos = Venus`: love obstructed by scarcity, nostalgia, and desire constrained by duty or depleted conditions.
- `Venus/Admetos = Hades`: unable to true and deep love.
- `Venus/Hades = Admetos`: lack of servants.
