## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Asc = Venus`: affection, social appreciation, beauty, and happy union as the factor that makes the native's style especially attractive, well-received, and supported by others.
- `Venus/Asc = Jupiter`: an attractive, generous personal presence, with harmony and likability opening doors through goodwill and visible grace.
- `Venus/Jupiter = Asc`: a warm, generous presence, with charm, goodwill, and social abundance made visible in conduct, appearance, and close contact.
