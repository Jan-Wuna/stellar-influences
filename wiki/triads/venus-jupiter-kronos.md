## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Kronos = Venus`: being able to inflame with love and being favored by the authorities.
- `Venus/Jupiter = Kronos`: a person bewitching by his or her appearance.
- `Venus/Kronos = Jupiter`: gifts through charitable persons.
