## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Vulcanus = Venus`: great fortunate love, powerful affection, and happiness through relationship, attraction, or enjoyment.
- `Venus/Jupiter = Vulcanus`: great longing for happiness.
- `Venus/Vulcanus = Jupiter`: joy through the power of attraction which one wields.
