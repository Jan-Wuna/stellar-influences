## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Hades = Venus`: closeness formed through serving conditions, but also damaged affection, assault, reproductive harm, or ties that carry injury.
- `Venus/Hades = Mars`: activities of servants.
- `Venus/Mars = Hades`: illicit intercourse.
