## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Pluto = Venus`: passionate attraction, intense pleasure-seeking, and relationships charged with force, excitement, and the urge to direct how love unfolds.
- `Venus/Mars = Pluto`: obsessive passion, with attraction and pursuit driven into stronger desire, powerful bonding, and transformative erotic or creative force.
- `Venus/Pluto = Mars`: compelling attraction, transformative love, and deep value intensity made urgent, erotic, and forceful.
