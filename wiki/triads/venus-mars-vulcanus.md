## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Vulcanus = Venus`: makes love, attraction, and intimacy intensely passionate and forceful.
- `Venus/Mars = Vulcanus`: passionate sexual love.
- `Venus/Vulcanus = Mars`: to carry out one’s activities (actions or work) by inner compulsion.
