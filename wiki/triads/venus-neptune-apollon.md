## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Apollon = Venus`: This activation socializes `Neptune/Apollon` through deceptive affections, feigned friendship, and unstable sympathies. It shows broad illusion entering the field of love and value, where attraction easily shifts or proves less sincere than it first appears.
- `Venus/Apollon = Neptune`: falsehood and unfaithfulness.
- `Venus/Neptune = Apollon`: false friendship of many, or dissolution of friendship with many.
