## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Pluto = Venus`: seductive depth, with love, taste, and sympathy colored by hidden transformation, subtle longing, and intense but elusive emotional or artistic magnetism.
- `Venus/Neptune = Pluto`: magnetic romantic or artistic intensity, with love and value shaped by depth, sacrifice, obsession, or redemptive desire.
- `Venus/Pluto = Neptune`: compelling attraction, transformative love, and deep value intensity made seductive, mystical, and hard to define.
