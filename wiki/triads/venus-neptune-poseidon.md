## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Poseidon = Venus`: This activation harmonizes refined feeling with subtle, mental, or occult perception. It often shows pleasure in delicate impressions, aesthetic sensitivity, affectionate spiritual rapport, or a tendency to enjoy one's own reflected image and ideals.
- `Venus/Neptune = Poseidon`: supersensitive, delicate.
- `Venus/Poseidon = Neptune`: religious revelling.
