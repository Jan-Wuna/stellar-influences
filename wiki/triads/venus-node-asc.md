## Derived Synthesis

- `Node/Asc = Venus`: a likable, cooperative identity, with personality shaped by harmony, friendship, attractiveness, and the wish to create pleasing relations in the immediate field of contact.
- `Venus/Asc = Node`: attractive rapport, with identity shaped by friendship, affection, and the ability to create harmonious contact quickly.
- `Venus/Node = Asc`: a pleasant, socially magnetic presence, with identity expressed through charm, warmth, and the ability to create easy rapport in direct contact.
