## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Asc = Venus`: reserved charm, with warmth expressed carefully, loyally, and often more through reliability than overt display.
- `Venus/Asc = Saturn`: reserved charm, with personal grace shaped by loyalty, discipline, and the need to protect affection rather than display it freely.
- `Venus/Saturn = Asc`: restrained affection, serious love, and value tested through time, duty, and limitation made visible as reserve, selectivity, and measured grace.
