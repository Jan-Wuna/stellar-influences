## Derived Synthesis

- `Saturn/Pluto = Venus`: serious, controlled attachment, with love, money, or pleasure shaped by pressure, restraint, loyalty, and fear of loss.
- `Venus/Pluto = Saturn`: compelling attraction, transformative love, and deep value intensity made severe, enduring, and difficult to escape.
- `Venus/Saturn = Pluto`: restrained affection, serious love, and value tested through time, duty, and limitation made exacting, deep, and hard to soften.
