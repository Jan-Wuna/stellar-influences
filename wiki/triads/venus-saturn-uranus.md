## Source Coverage

Each linked activation currently has entries from all five sources below. This lists canonical source coverage, not the full contents of the works. Munkasey's retained locators are printed entry pages; other entries are linked without inventing missing page numbers.

- `Saturn/Uranus = Venus`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/saturn-uranus-equals-venus.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/saturn-uranus-equals-venus.md#abc-entry); [Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences](../activations/saturn-uranus-equals-venus.md#ebertin-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/saturn-uranus-equals-venus.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/saturn-uranus-equals-venus.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `284`.
- `Venus/Saturn = Uranus`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/venus-saturn-equals-uranus.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/venus-saturn-equals-uranus.md#abc-entry); [Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences](../activations/venus-saturn-equals-uranus.md#ebertin-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/venus-saturn-equals-uranus.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/venus-saturn-equals-uranus.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `196`.
- `Venus/Uranus = Saturn`: [Alfred Witte, Ludwig Rudolph & Hermann Lefeldt - Rules for Planetary Pictures](../activations/venus-uranus-equals-saturn.md#witte-entry); [Udo Rudolph - ABC for Planetary Pictures](../activations/venus-uranus-equals-saturn.md#abc-entry); [Reinhold Ebertin - The Combination of Stellar Influences](../activations/venus-uranus-equals-saturn.md#ebertin-entry); [John Sandbach - Midpoints: A Kabbalistic Compendium of Meanings for Astrological Midpoints](../activations/venus-uranus-equals-saturn.md#sandbach-entry); [Michael Munkasey - Midpoints: Unleashing the Power of the Planets](../activations/venus-uranus-equals-saturn.md#munkasey-entry), printed p. `200`.

Printed-wording and locator limits: Venus/Uranus = Saturn's Witte entry itself reads “One’s tend to break up”; the missing subject is not reconstructed. Its Sandbach entry is now located exactly on PDF p. 60. Venus/Saturn = Uranus retains a chapter-start-only Sandbach reference at PDF p. 57, and Saturn/Uranus = Venus has no recorded exact Sandbach page.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

The formulas have distinct base pairs and activators. Their different meanings are not contradictions merely because the same three factors recur. This structural hub makes no cross-orientation doctrinal comparison; the activation pages retain their own source differences and qualifications.
