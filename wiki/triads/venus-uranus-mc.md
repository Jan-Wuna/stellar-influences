## Derived Synthesis

- `Uranus/MC = Venus`: a distinctive public style, unusual artistic or relational direction, and opportunities that come through charm paired with originality.
- `Venus/MC = Uranus`: a distinctive vocation, with status shaped by originality, artistic innovation, or relational forms that break convention.
- `Venus/Uranus = MC`: electric attraction, unconventional love, and liberating value shifts made innovative, public-facing, and unconventional.
