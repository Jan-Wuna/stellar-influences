## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Poseidon = Zeus`: the sower of religion.
- `Venus/Zeus = Poseidon`: the spiritual or mental power.
- `Zeus/Poseidon = Venus`: To stand firmly with religious and worldly convictions. To testify to them.
