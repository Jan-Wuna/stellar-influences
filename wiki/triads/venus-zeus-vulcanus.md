## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Vulcanus = Zeus`: to attend to duty with love and care.
- `Venus/Zeus = Vulcanus`: passionate love desire.
- `Zeus/Vulcanus = Venus`: Great longing for love. Mighty will for procreation.
