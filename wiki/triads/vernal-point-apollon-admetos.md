## Derived Synthesis

- `Apollon/Admetos = Vernal Point`: general tranquility and a wider atmosphere of contentment, quiet, or carefree sufficiency.
- `Vernal Point/Admetos = Apollon`: This formula emphasizes limited success, general difficulties in commerce and trade, finding extensive raw materials, and dying of the masses.
- `Vernal Point/Apollon = Admetos`: This formula emphasizes a general standstill, stagnation, stoppage or shut down in economy, commerce, trades or science.
