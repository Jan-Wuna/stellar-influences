## Derived Synthesis

- `Asc/Kronos = Vernal Point`: public service, public tasks, broad official command, or being subject to visible collective authority.
- `Vernal Point/Asc = Kronos`: This formula emphasizes to plan something important with others, metropolitan areas, capitals with governments, and highly situated places.
- `Vernal Point/Kronos = Asc`: This formula emphasizes acquaintances with independent persons and national territory.
