## Derived Synthesis

- `Cupido/Apollon = Vernal Point`: general peaceful efforts and becoming broadly known through artistic or scientific association.
- `Vernal Point/Apollon = Cupido`: This formula emphasizes extensive efforts for peace, societies for peace, expansion of organizations or companies, and the science of the arts.
- `Vernal Point/Cupido = Apollon`: This formula emphasizes associations, associations in science, commerce, industry or art, and a religious community in general.
