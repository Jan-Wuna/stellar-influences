## Derived Synthesis

- `Hades/Zeus = Vernal Point`: destructive fire affecting many, dirty tricks, strikes, lockouts, lack of coal, industry in general, and broad outbreaks of hate.
- `Vernal Point/Hades = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes coal fire (serviceable), incinerators, crematoriums, and unemployment.
- `Vernal Point/Zeus = Hades`: This formula emphasizes meanness and dirty tricks prevent one from reaching a goal, devastation by open fires, e.g, forest fires, and fires started by lightning.
