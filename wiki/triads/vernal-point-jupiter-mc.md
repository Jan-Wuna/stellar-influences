## Derived Synthesis

Use the orientation whose base pair and activating factor match the requested formula. `Jupiter/MC = Vernal Point` activates `Jupiter/MC` with `Vernal Point`; it is distinct from `Vernal Point/Jupiter = MC` and `Vernal Point/MC = Jupiter`. The activation links supply their separate interpretations, while the axis links supply pair context. This hub does not merge those interpretations.

## Contradictions Across Orientations

Source scope: Jupiter/MC = Vernal Point retains ABC's public-right phrase literally; it specifies no particular entitlement or legal context.

No identity conflict was found between the listed formulas and their linked activation metadata. This structural check does not establish agreement between source interpretations. Changing the base pair and activator produces a different formula, so differences between these orientations are not by themselves contradictions.
