## Derived Synthesis

- `Jupiter/Zeus = Vernal Point`: general prosperous work, successful accomplishment, and products or creations that become broadly visible or relevant.
- `Vernal Point/Jupiter = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes fortunate creation and the happiness in one's work.
- `Vernal Point/Zeus = Jupiter`: This formula emphasizes successful work projects in general, to produce coins, and to create something valuable.
