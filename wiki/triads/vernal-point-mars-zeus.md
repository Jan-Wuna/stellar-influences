## Derived Synthesis

- `Mars/Zeus = Vernal Point`: externalizes the axis into visible creative activity and public fire danger.
- `Vernal Point/Mars = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes fruitful deeds, directed energies, combustion, and machinery power.
- `Vernal Point/Zeus = Mars`: This formula emphasizes general energetic work, ready to use weapons, weapons of war, war turmoil, and the flame-thrower.
