## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Cupido = Vernal Point`: projects the axis into general social enjoyment and artistic sensibility.
- `Vernal Point/Cupido = Mercury`: This formula emphasizes the founder of an association and artistic thoughts.
- `Vernal Point/Mercury = Cupido`: This formula emphasizes general opinion about marriage, predominant tendency in art, and opinions of associations or unions.
