## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Venus = Vernal Point`: Acquaintance in the public. To become acquainted with many. Beautiful appearance of the creatures. The fashions.
- `Vernal Point/Mercury = Venus`: This formula emphasizes general conception of beauty, acquaintances in public, conformity of the public opinion, to adorn oneself, and to dress-up.
- `Vernal Point/Venus = Mercury`: This formula emphasizes beauty of thought, well-thought-of, making new acquaintances through general popularity, and an appreciation for art.
