## Derived Synthesis

- `Mercury/Zeus = Vernal Point`: Edicts, laws, decrees in general.
- `Vernal Point/Mercury = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes constructive thinking of the general public, general thinking under compulsion, and general thinking is guided.
- `Vernal Point/Zeus = Mercury`: This formula emphasizes laws, one who follows orders, to learn the manipulation of motors, and a general urge for mental or spiritual work.
