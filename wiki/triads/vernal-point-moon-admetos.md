## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Admetos = Vernal Point`: Crowded people. Dying or dead nations.
- `Vernal Point/Admetos = Moon`: This formula emphasizes stiff resistance as regards the public, people or women, death of the mother, to be present at a death, and many deaths among the population.
- `Vernal Point/Moon = Admetos`: This formula emphasizes death of many female persons and the death of a nation.
