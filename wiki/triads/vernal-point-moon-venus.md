## Derived Synthesis

- `Moon/Venus = Vernal Point`: This activation externalizes `Moon/Venus` into general motherhood and visible nurturing presence. It points to the loving, containing, and maternal side of feeling becoming an openly recognizable social fact.
- `Vernal Point/Moon = Venus`: This formula emphasizes the love of women and motherhood.
- `Vernal Point/Venus = Moon`: This formula emphasizes a woman’s love, to be loved by the public, and the people’s love.
