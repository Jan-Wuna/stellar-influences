## Derived Synthesis

- `Neptune/Node = Vernal Point`: This activation externalizes dissolution as a general condition, making separation and loosening of bonds a widespread or collective experience rather than a private one.
- `Vernal Point/Neptune = Node`: This formula emphasizes disappointment or disagreeable things through connections and general dissolution of connections.
- `Vernal Point/Node = Neptune`: This formula emphasizes deception in general relationships, dissolving connections to many people, and avoiding people.
