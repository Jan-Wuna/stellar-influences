## Derived Synthesis

- `Vernal Point/MC = Saturn`: This formula emphasizes to be mournful with others, to appear repulsive or clumsy, to have losses, and to feel abandoned or lonely.
- `Vernal Point/Saturn = MC`: This formula emphasizes difficult to contact, difficult to adjust oneself to others, and to be deserted.
