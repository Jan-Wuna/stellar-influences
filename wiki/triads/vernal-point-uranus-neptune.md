## Derived Synthesis

- `Vernal Point/Neptune = Uranus`: This formula emphasizes sudden losses of a general character and from the beyond to here.
- `Vernal Point/Uranus = Neptune`: This formula emphasizes from here to the beyond, to make connection with the dead, and to die.
