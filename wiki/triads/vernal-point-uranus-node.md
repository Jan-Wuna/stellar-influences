## Derived Synthesis

- `Vernal Point/Node = Uranus`: This formula emphasizes gatherings, sudden meetings, and excitement with or through many people.
- `Vernal Point/Uranus = Node`: This formula emphasizes experiencing or sharing the experience of a sudden event, to bear witness, and suddenly to witness something.
