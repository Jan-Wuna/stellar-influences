## Derived Synthesis

- `Vernal Point/Uranus = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes thunderstorms, danger by fire, and explosions.
- `Vernal Point/Zeus = Uranus`: This formula emphasizes & Spontaneous work in general, manufacturing explosives, fire spitting craters, and lightening.
