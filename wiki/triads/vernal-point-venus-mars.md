## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Mars = Vernal Point`: a free (liberal) concept (or idea) of sexual love.
- `Vernal Point/Mars = Venus`: This formula emphasizes close acquaintances, relatives on the father's side, and deeds born out of love.
- `Vernal Point/Venus = Mars`: This formula emphasizes mating time, love in general, and relatives on the side of the mother.
