## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Node = Vernal Point`: love ties in general.
- `Vernal Point/Node = Venus`: This formula emphasizes quick affection for someone and love or harmonic union with many people.
- `Vernal Point/Venus = Node`: This formula emphasizes love ties in general and contacts which are pleasant in the public.
