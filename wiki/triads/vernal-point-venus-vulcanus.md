## Derived Synthesis

- `Venus/Vulcanus = Vernal Point`: something that is mostly desired by the general public.
- `Vernal Point/Venus = Vulcanus`: This formula emphasizes strong force of attraction, general longing of something, and general passion.
- `Vernal Point/Vulcanus = Venus`: This formula emphasizes great power of attraction, magnetism, and strenuous peace efforts in general.
