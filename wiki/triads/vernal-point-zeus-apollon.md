## Derived Synthesis

- `Vernal Point/Apollon = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes mechanical engineering, technical institute, and the general creativity in sciences.
- `Vernal Point/Zeus = Apollon`: This formula emphasizes inevitably spreading, expansion, and natural science.
- `Zeus/Apollon = Vernal Point`: The people in general try to find a solution to present problems by practical application. General problems that demand a solution.
