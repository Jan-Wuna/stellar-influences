## Derived Synthesis

- `Vernal Point/Kronos = Zeus`: This formula emphasizes outstanding accomplishments, to achieve something great, and a large fire.
- `Vernal Point/Zeus = Kronos`: This formula emphasizes creation in the public, either independently or in a leading position, wars brought about by the government, and war apparatus.
- `Zeus/Kronos = Vernal Point`: The general energetic will in pursuit of a goal. Big fires. Wars. Deciding battles.
